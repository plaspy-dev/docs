# GalileoSky

GalileoSky produces a line of GPS tracking devices used for vehicle and asset monitoring. On this page you will find information about how GalileoSky devices work with Plaspy, common features to expect, typical use cases, and guidance for choosing the right device for your needs. Use the model catalog below to explore all GalileoSky devices that are compatible with Plaspy.

## How GalileoSky devices work with Plaspy

GalileoSky trackers collect location and telemetry data and can be configured to forward that data to third party platforms like Plaspy. Plaspy receives and interprets the tracking data, displaying positions, historical routes, alerts, and diagnostics in a unified dashboard. Device-level configuration and connection steps vary by model; consult the specific device documentation for details on network settings and protocol options. Plaspy provides a consolidated view and tools to manage GalileoSky devices alongside other fleets and tracker brands.

## Common features found in GalileoSky devices

While features vary across models, GalileoSky devices commonly include:
- Real time location reporting and position history
- Configurable reporting intervals to balance accuracy and battery life
- Alerting for movement, geofence entries and exits, and tamper or power events
- Support for battery powered and wired power configurations
- Durable enclosures suitable for vehicles and field equipment
- Connectivity options for cellular networks and optional external antennas
These general capabilities make GalileoSky devices suitable for a broad range of tracking scenarios. For model specific specifications, view the device entries in the catalog above.

## Typical use cases for GalileoSky GPS trackers

GalileoSky devices are often used for:
- Fleet management and route monitoring for light and heavy vehicles
- Theft prevention and recovery for cars, motorcycles, and equipment
- Rental fleet tracking for usage and billing reconciliation
- Remote asset monitoring for trailers, containers, and construction machinery
- Operational oversight where location, uptime, and movement history are required
Plaspy helps you operationalize these use cases by centralizing alerts, reports, and location-based automation.

## Help finding the right GalileoSky device

When selecting a GalileoSky model consider:
- Power source and installation method required for your vehicle or asset
- Expected reporting frequency and battery life needs for remote assets
- Required I/O and sensor compatibility for external inputs or CAN data
- Physical form factor and environmental protection for field conditions
- Connectivity and roaming coverage for geographic requirements
