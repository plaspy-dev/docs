# GalileoSky - GALILEOSKY Lite v1.8.5

![GALILEOSKY Lite v1.8.5](./logo.jpg)

The GALILEOSKY Lite v1.8.5 GPS tracker is a powerful device that accurately determines the coordinates of a moving vehicle and saves time data, routes, and sensor information in the form of points with geographic coordinates. These data are then transmitted to a server for processing by a dispatch center. This GPS tracker is designed to provide organizations with significant cost savings through fuel economy, reduced transport mileage, and improved driver discipline. It not only tracks vehicle misuse but also helps prevent vehicle theft by regularly sending location data. In case of an emergency, the driver can easily send an alarm by pressing a special button.

The GALILEOSKY Lite v1.8.5 GPS tracker offers a range of features to ensure efficient vehicle tracking and monitoring. It provides detailed track drawings without breaks, even in the absence of communication. The device has two full-function analog/digital/frequency/pulse inputs, allowing for versatile sensor installations. It also features a built-in accelerometer to filter false origins and a built-in thermometer for temperature diagnosis. The GPS tracker supports the "EcoDrive" function, which helps promote economical driving by monitoring sudden acceleration, braking, and bumps on the road. Configuration and diagnostics can be done remotely via GPRS through the Configurator without disconnecting from the server data monitoring. The device also has a built-in non-volatile memory to save up to 58,000 points and flexible settings for "Sleeping mode" in the parking lot.

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the GALILEOSKY Lite v1.8.5 GPS tracker is ideal for tracking the movement and character of vehicles. It provides accurate information such as coordinates, speed, direction of movement, violations \(speeding\), vibration level, fuel control, temperature inside the device, and status of inputs and outputs. The GPS tracker supports operation and settings via SMS and remote firmware updates via GPRS. It has a USB 2.0 interface for easy connectivity to a PC and uses external antennas for GPS and GSM signals. The device operates on a supply voltage of 10 to 30 volts, making it compatible with a wide range of vehicles.
