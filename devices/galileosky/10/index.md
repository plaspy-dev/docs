# GalileoSky - 10

![10](./logo..png)

The GalileoSky 10 is a compact GPS/GLONASS terminal designed for monitoring, control, and management of vehicles and mobile assets. It supports direct connection and control of external sensors and is notable for its ability to process low level analog signals without requiring an additional external resistor. The device also reads data from two CAN buses simultaneously and can interpret a large set of J1939 parameters, making it suitable for projects that need both location and vehicle data in one package.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GalileoSky 10 can feed its location, sensor inputs, and decoded CAN information into the Plaspy platform to support fleet oversight and operational reporting. Integrating this tracker with Plaspy makes it possible to consolidate position tracking and vehicle condition data in a single fleet management view, helping teams monitor assets, respond to events, and analyze historical activity with more context.

## Key Highlights

- GPS and GLONASS tracking for reliable positioning
- Direct sensor connectivity and control for flexible telemetry
- Processes low level analog signals without extra external resistor
- Dual CAN bus monitoring with decoding of J1939 parameters
- Ability to access a large set of vehicle parameters for diagnostics
- Compact enclosure and four mounting points for discreet installation
- Designed for combined location and asset condition monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GalileoSky 10 supplies location updates, sensor readings, and decoded vehicle bus data that Plaspy uses for real time visibility and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests the telemetry and makes it available across dashboards, alerts, and reports to support operational decisions.

- Real time location and status visibility for each tracked asset in Plaspy
- Sensor inputs mapped to Plaspy telemetry fields for live monitoring and thresholds
- Decoded CAN and J1939 parameters available for vehicle condition insight within Plaspy
- Event and alert generation based on sensor or vehicle parameter changes
- Historical playback and reporting combining location, sensor, and CAN data

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking with integrated vehicle data for diagnostics and maintenance planning
- Remote monitoring of sensor-equipped assets where analog measurements are required
- Operational oversight for mixed fleets needing compact and discreet tracking devices
- Collecting detailed vehicle bus parameters to support condition based monitoring
- Consolidating position and asset telemetry into a single fleet management workflow

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GalileoSky 10 is a practical option for organizations that need both accurate positioning and deeper telemetry from vehicle systems. Its ability to process low level analog signals and to read two CAN buses makes it useful where external signal conditioning or multiple bus sources would otherwise complicate installations. Paired with Plaspy, those capabilities translate into richer data for monitoring, alerts, and reporting without multiplying data sources.

Because the device is compact and built for vehicle and asset monitoring, it can suit a range of deployments from light commercial fleets to specialized equipment tracking. If your operations benefit from combined location, sensor, and vehicle bus data in one platform, using the GalileoSky 10 with Plaspy can simplify data collection and provide more actionable insights.
