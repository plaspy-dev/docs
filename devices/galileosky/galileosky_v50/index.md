# GalileoSky - GALILEOSKY V5.0

![GALILEOSKY V5.0](./logo.jpg)

The GALILEOSKY V5.0 from GalileoSky is a flagship GPS tracker that offers a high degree of reliability and rich functionality. It is designed to be versatile and can be integrated into a variety of industry-specific solutions by connecting up to 17 digital sensors and a large number of external devices. This tracker is perfect for businesses that require detailed monitoring and control over their assets.

One of the standout features of the GALILEOSKY V5.0 is its ability to have two SIM cards, allowing for increased connectivity and flexibility. It also complies with the quality standards of the European Union, ensuring that it meets the highest industry standards. With the ability to connect up to 16 digital devices under test \(DUT\), this tracker offers extensive monitoring capabilities.

Other notable features of the GALILEOSKY V5.0 include detailed drawing angles for accurate tracking, the ability to switch between SIM cards for different operators, and the option to specify preferred networks for each SIM card. It also offers custom settings for international data roaming, reducing costs for GPRS traffic. Additionally, this tracker supports on-line monitoring, off-line monitoring, and "stealth mode" for flexible and discreet monitoring options.

The GALILEOSKY V5.0 is a powerful and reliable GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features and capabilities. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or personnel, this tracker is a versatile solution that can meet your needs.
