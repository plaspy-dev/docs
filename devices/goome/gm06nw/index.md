# Goome - GM06NW

![GM06NW](./logo.jpg)

The Goome GM06NW is a functional GPS tracking device that combines GSM and GPS technology to provide accurate and reliable tracking. With a built-in antenna and sensors, this device offers ACC detection, vibration alarm, and fuel/electricity cut-off functions. Additionally, when connected with an SOS button and microphone, the user can send SOS information to predefined numbers in dangerous situations, and their family can listen to what is happening around. Its wide input voltage range makes it suitable for use in cars, trucks, taxis, and motorcycles.

The Goome GM06NW offers a range of outstanding features that make it an ideal choice for vehicle tracking. These features include:

- Real-time tracking: With the integrated GPS technology, this device allows for real-time tracking of your vehicle's location, providing you with peace of mind and security.
- Geo-fence: You can define a specific geographical zone and receive notifications when your vehicle enters or exits that zone. This is especially useful for monitoring the use of commercial vehicles or keeping track of the location of your loved ones.
- Tracking playback: You can access the tracking history and replay the routes taken by your vehicle over a specific period of time. This is useful for analyzing driving patterns or verifying the location of a vehicle at a certain time.
- High-sensitivity chip: The GM06NW uses a high-sensitivity chip to ensure optimal accuracy and reliability in GPS signal reception.
- Remote power/electricity cut-off: With this feature, you can remotely cut off the power or electricity supply to your vehicle in case of theft or unauthorized use.
- Small size: The GM06NW has a compact and discreet design that can be easily installed in any vehicle without drawing attention.
- Vibration alarm: If unusual vibration is detected in your vehicle, the GM06NW will activate an alarm to alert you of possible theft attempts or damage.

In summary, the Goome GM06NW is a reliable and functional GPS tracking device that offers a wide range of features to ensure the safety and control of your vehicle. Whether you need to monitor your fleet of commercial vehicles or keep your loved ones safe, the GM06NW is a reliable and effective choice.
