# Goome - U9

![U9](./logo.jpg)

The Goome U9 is a high-end GPS tracking device that utilizes u-blox 7 and 4th generation GPS technology, making it a reliable and accurate tracking solution. It supports GPS, LBS, and AGPS location modes, ensuring that you can always get the most precise location information. In situations where the GPS signal is poor, the U9 automatically activates the LBS location mode to provide an alternative means of tracking. This feature ensures that you can always keep an eye on your assets, even in challenging environments.

One of the standout features of the Goome U9 is its small size. Despite its compact design, it doesn't compromise on quality or functionality. This makes it a popular choice among clients who value both performance and convenience. Additionally, the U9 can be connected to a relay, allowing you to remotely shut down the vehicle's engine if necessary. This feature provides an extra layer of security and control over your assets.

The Goome U9 offers a range of impressive features that make it a versatile and reliable GPS tracking device. Some of its key features include real-time tracking, user-defined geo-fencing, trace playback, remotely voice monitoring, an SOS button for emergencies, cut wire alarm, remotely cutoff petrol/electricity, and vibration alarm. These features ensure that you have complete control over your assets and can respond quickly to any unexpected events or emergencies.

With its advanced technology, compact size, and comprehensive features, the Goome U9 is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a high-quality GPS tracking device. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or even loved ones, the U9 provides the reliability and functionality you need to stay connected and in control.
