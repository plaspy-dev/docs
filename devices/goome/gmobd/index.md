# Goome - GMOBD

![GMOBD](./logo.jpg)

The Goome GMOBD is an OBD2 based GPS tracker that combines a built in GSM module with BDS GPS positioning. Designed for vehicle monitoring, it offers features such as anti theft, remote listening, alarms, real time tracking, position reporting, geo fencing, and trace playback. The device is compact, includes a backup battery for continued operation when vehicle power is lost, and is described as suitable for a range of vehicle types that accept a 16 pin OBD interface.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GMOBD can feed live location and event data into the Plaspy platform for visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can use the tracker's real time tracking, geofence events, playback history, and power off alerts to support fleet monitoring, dispatching, and vehicle security workflows, making the GMOBD a practical option for organizations that require plug in style tracking integrated with a fleet management system.

## Key Highlights

- OBD2 form factor designed to work with standard 16 pin OBD interfaces for straightforward vehicle connection.
- Real time location tracking for continuous vehicle visibility.
- Geofencing and trace playback support for boundary alerts and route review.
- Power off alarm and backup battery to help maintain tracking continuity when vehicle power is interrupted.
- Built in GSM and BDS GPS modules for position reporting and communications.
- Anti theft features plus vibration alarm and remote listening for added security.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Goome GMOBD provides vehicle location and event data to the Plaspy platform where it can be visualized, monitored, and reported. Plaspy ingests the tracker's status and location updates so fleet managers can keep an operational view of vehicles and respond to alerts.

- Live map visualization of vehicle positions for real time monitoring.
- Geofence configuration and alerts within Plaspy to notify on entry and exit events.
- Historical route playback for trip review and incident analysis.
- Event alerts for power loss, vibration, or configured alarms routed into Plaspy workflows.
- Fleet level dashboards and reporting to summarize activity across multiple GMOBD devices.

## Typical Use Cases

- Car rental fleets that need plug in tracking and quick redeployment between vehicles.
- Small fleet management for delivery, service, or local transport operations.
- Dispatching systems that require live vehicle locations and route playback.
- Vehicle security and anti theft monitoring with power off and vibration alerts.
- Situations where a compact, OBD2 style tracker with backup battery is preferred for minimal vehicle modification.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Goome GMOBD is a practical choice for organizations that prefer an OBD2 plug in tracker with a compact footprint and core vehicle monitoring features. Its real time tracking, geofence support, and backup power make it suitable for fleets and rental operators who need straightforward position visibility and basic security alerts. Integrated into Plaspy, the GMOBD can contribute to improved dispatch decisions, simplified monitoring, and searchable trip history without requiring specialized installation.
