# Laipac - S911 Bracelet HC

![S911 Bracelet HC](./logo.jpg)

The Laipac S911 Bracelet Locator HC is a GPS tracker specifically designed for healthcare applications, providing the location of patients and elderly care. This device features a high sensitivity GPS receiver with 2-way voice communication, allowing for immediate assistance in emergency situations. It is equipped with an SOS emergency button, G-sensor to detect falls, GeoFence protection, and quick dial for easy communication. The S911 Bracelet Locator HC is ideal for locating patients with Alzheimer's, Dementia, Autism, and for general elderly care.

One of the standout features of the S911 Bracelet Locator HC is its real-time tracking capability. The device can pinpoint the exact location of the person wearing the bracelet, allowing caretakers or call centers to quickly respond in case of emergencies or when the person is unable to communicate. The location data is transmitted in real-time to a secure website, where it can be viewed and tracked. Additionally, the device features tamper detection, sending a notification if the band is cut or the clasp is opened.

The S911 Bracelet Locator HC is packed with useful features such as GSM/GPRS for worldwide coverage, an LCD display for easy monitoring of GPS and GSM status, AGPS for faster GPS fix, and a built-in speaker and microphone. It also has a micro USB port for configuration and power charging, making it convenient to use. With its durable design and IP67 rating, the S911 Bracelet Locator HC is built to withstand everyday use by patients. Overall, this GPS tracker is an excellent choice for healthcare applications, providing peace of mind for caretakers and ensuring the safety of patients.

### Key Features:

- GSM/GPRS for worldwide coverage
- LCD Display for Time, GPS Status, GSM Status, and Battery Level
- AGPS for obtaining a GPS fix faster
- Built-in speaker and microphone
- Micro USB port for configuration and power charging
- Geo-Fence setting with in/out alert
- Tamper detection
- Built-in Smart logger with thousands of waypoints
- 3 Axial Accelerometer to report impact, accident, crash, and falling
- Real-time position updates based on time and distance intervals
- Mileage report and over-speed alert
- Phonebook & Fast dial feature
- IP67 rating for durability
