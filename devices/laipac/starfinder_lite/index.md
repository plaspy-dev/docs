# Laipac - StarFinder LITE

<div className="device-logo">
![StarFinder LITE](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Laipac StarFinder LITE is a compact, real time vehicle tracking device designed to provide accurate position and timing information for mobile assets. Using GPS and cellular connectivity, the unit offers core tracking features such as speed, position, heading, mileage, geofence alerts, tow and collision alerts, a panic button with voice capability, and a built in data logger for long term waypoint collection. The device is positioned for both private and commercial vehicle tracking where continuous visibility and periodic reporting are required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the StarFinder LITE can feed location and alert data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Plaspy can use the tracker data to present real time locations, historical routes from the data logger, geofence events, and alert notifications alongside other vehicles and assets managed in a single platform. That compatibility makes the StarFinder LITE a practical option for teams that want hardware capability combined with Plaspy software visibility and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Compact real time vehicle tracking with GPS and cellular connectivity for location updates
- Built in data logger capable of storing a large number of waypoints for route history
- Alerts for over speed, tow events, collision detection, panic, and main power disconnection
- Geofence support for virtual perimeter monitoring and event notifications
- Remote control features for vehicle functions and reported vehicle status signals
- Speaker and microphone jacks to support voice calls from the device when needed

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the StarFinder LITE supplies location updates, event alerts, and logged route data so fleet managers can monitor assets in one place. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and makes it actionable through dashboards, alerts, and reports.

- Real time location visibility within Plaspy dashboards to track vehicles on a map
- Alert ingestion for speed, tow, collision, panic, and power events with configurable notifications
- Historical route and waypoint reporting using the device data logger for post trip review
- Geofence management and event reporting to identify perimeter breaches and arrivals
- Centralized fleet views that combine StarFinder LITE devices with other compatible trackers for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium vehicle fleets requiring real time tracking and reporting
- Taxi and ride services that need location visibility, trip history, and alerting
- Emergency vehicles and ambulance monitoring where timely location and alerts matter
- Construction vehicle and equipment tracking to maintain site visibility and asset control
- School bus monitoring and other transport services that benefit from geofence and alert features
- Personal or business users who require a reliable data logger for vehicle route history

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The StarFinder LITE provides a balanced set of tracking features suited to organizations that need dependable location information, event alerts, and route history without excessive complexity. Its built in data logger and support for geofences and multiple alert types make it useful for both continuous monitoring and post trip analysis. Paired with Plaspy, those capabilities become easier to manage at scale, with consolidated dashboards and reporting across an entire fleet.

For teams evaluating devices, the combination of Laipac hardware and Plaspy software offers a practical path from device data to operational insight.
