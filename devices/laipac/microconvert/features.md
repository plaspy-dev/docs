# Laipac - MicroConvert Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the Laipac MicroConvert (aka MicroCovert) GPS tracker and how its documented capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing features, practical value for asset tracking, and what to expect when integrating the device into Plaspy for monitoring and recovery workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional radio configuration, and manufacturer implementation. Users should treat the items below as a feature summary based on the available device description and verify specifics against the manufacturer documentation when needed.

## Feature Overview

The Laipac MicroConvert is designed for covert tracking of non-powered assets. It combines rugged, weather resistant packaging with magnetic mounting and long battery runtime options to enable long-term location monitoring for off-grid equipment and trailers.

- Rugged, waterproof and dust resistant enclosure for outdoor installation and protection.
- Magnetic mounting that allows quick attachment to metal assets without drilling or wiring.
- Dual battery options with significantly different runtimes to suit short term or extended monitoring needs.
- GPS plus cellular reporting through GSM GPRS for position updates and recovery support.
- Movement sensing and geofence capability to detect unauthorized or unexpected asset movement.
- Sleep and power saving modes to extend on-board battery life between reporting events.

## Core Features of Laipac - MicroConvert

- GPS position reporting with assisted GPS support as described in the manufacturer materials.
- GSM and GPRS cellular connectivity for sending location reports to a tracking platform.
- Geo-fence capability with configurable in and out alerts.
- Onboard movement sensing via a 3-axis accelerometer to report motion and impacts.
- Sleep mode and power management features to prolong battery life between transmissions.
- Two industrial Li‑Ion battery capacities available for different deployment durations.
- Durable, crush resistant enclosure with magnetic mounting for non-powered asset attachment.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and process location and event reports from compatible Laipac MicroConvert devices, making the device capabilities visible within the Plaspy platform for monitoring and response.

- Real-time or periodic location updates appear in Plaspy as position points on the map for tracking asset movement.
- Movement and geofence events can be surfaced in Plaspy as alerts or notifications for operational follow up.
- Long battery runtime modes help reduce the frequency of reports, which Plaspy can display as history or intermittent telemetry.
- Rugged magnetic mounting simplifies installation workflows tracked and documented in Plaspy for asset inventories.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols, enabling easier onboarding of compatible devices.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert or discreet tracking of trailers and unpowered equipment on job sites.
- Long term location monitoring for stored construction materials and rented tools.
- Asset recovery workflows for high value portable equipment.
- Audit and inventory visibility for assets that are infrequently moved.
- Theft detection and alerting when an asset leaves a specified area.
- Fleet support for non-powered assets where wiring is impractical or not allowed.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Battery runtimes quoted by the manufacturer depend on reporting intervals, device settings, and environmental conditions.
- Some features may vary between hardware revisions or regional variants of the device.
- Firmware version can affect available configuration options and event behavior.
- Installation type and placement (for example magnetic mount surface quality) can affect reception and sensing performance.
