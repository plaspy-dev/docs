# Laipac

Laipac is a manufacturer of GPS tracking devices used for vehicle and asset tracking across a variety of industries. Plaspy users can monitor Laipac hardware through the Plaspy platform to collect location, movement, and status data. This page explains how Laipac devices integrate with Plaspy, common features you can expect, and guidance for choosing the right tracker for your use case.

## How Laipac devices work with Plaspy

Laipac trackers send positional and telemetry data over cellular networks to a server endpoint. Plaspy ingests that data and exposes it through maps, live tracking, alerts, reports, and integrations. To connect a Laipac device with Plaspy you typically register the device in the Plaspy dashboard and ensure its reporting parameters are configured to send data to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy processes incoming messages, decodes standard GPS payloads, and associates them with the correct vehicle or asset profile.

This brand page and the Plaspy documentation do not replace device configuration guides. For device setup details consult the manufacturer documentation and use the Plaspy device pages for platform specific settings.

## Common features found in Laipac devices

Although specific capabilities vary by model, Laipac trackers commonly offer features useful for Plaspy customers:

- GPS location reporting for real time and historical tracking
- Cellular connectivity for remote location updates
- Geofence support to detect entry and exit events
- Motion and tamper detection to identify activity or interference
- Configurable reporting intervals and sleep modes for battery management
- Auxiliary inputs for sensors such as ignition, door, or fuel monitoring

## Typical use cases for Laipac GPS trackers

Laipac devices are used in scenarios where reliable location and status information is required:

- Fleet vehicle tracking to monitor routes, driver behavior, and vehicle utilization
- Mobile asset tracking for trailers, equipment, and rented items
- Field service operations to coordinate crews and verify work locations
- Security and recovery applications to assist with theft response
- Remote monitoring for long duration assets that require periodic reporting

Plaspy provides the platform tools to turn raw device data into operational insights such as route playback, geofence alerts, and utilization reports.
