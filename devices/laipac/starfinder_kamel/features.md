# Laipac - Starfinder KAMEL Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Laipac Starfinder KAMEL and explains how its published capabilities are used when the device is connected to Plaspy. It focuses on practical, user-facing functions such as long-term asset monitoring, geofence alerts, movement detection, and mounting options that matter for fleet and asset workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can depend on device firmware version, hardware revision, regional variants, and how the unit is installed. Use this page as a high level reference for Plaspy compatibility and consult the manufacturer documentation for the most current device-specific details.

## Feature Overview

The Starfinder KAMEL is designed for industrial asset tracking where long battery life, rugged enclosure, and flexible mounting are priorities. It reports location on configurable intervals and supports a set of operational alerts that help with monitoring and recovery.

- Long runtime operation suitable for non powered and powered assets including trailers, machinery, and watercraft
- Rechargeable Li‑Ion polymer battery with extended runtime allowing multi‑month deployments on typical reporting schedules
- Geo‑Fence capability with in and out of fence alerts to notify you of boundary events
- Motion detection and 3‑axis accelerometer to report movement and impacts for event awareness
- Over‑speed alerts and mileage reporting to support usage monitoring
- IP67 rated rugged housing with permanent and strong magnet mounting options for flexible installation

## Core Features of Laipac - Starfinder KAMEL

- Battery powered design intended for long unattended deployments, with a rechargeable 18Ah Li‑Ion polymer battery
- SiRF IV GPS receiver with AGPS support for consistent satellite positioning
- GSM/GPRS cellular connectivity for broad area reporting
- Geo‑fence support including in and out notifications
- 3‑axial accelerometer for movement and impact detection
- Configurable sleep mode to extend battery life between reports
- Real‑time position updates configurable by time and distance intervals
- Rugged IP67 enclosure with both permanent screw mounting and rare earth magnet mounting (80 lb force)

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy accepts location and status reports from compatible trackers and presents them in dashboards, maps, and event lists for operational visibility. When a Starfinder KAMEL is connected to Plaspy it can deliver position, movement events, and alert notifications that Plaspy surfaces for monitoring and historical review.

- Plaspy displays reported positions on maps and in timeline views for asset location history
- Geo‑fence in/out events and curfew alerts show as configurable notifications within Plaspy
- Motion and accelerometer based events are available as movement or impact alerts for operational oversight
- Mileage and over‑speed events reported by the device can be used for utilization and compliance tracking
- Devices report to Plaspy servers and Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol for seamless onboarding; the Starfinder KAMEL can be used with standard UDP or TCP reporting modes supported by Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers, containers, and seasonal equipment where battery life and ruggedness are critical
- Monitoring construction machinery and site assets for unauthorized movement or location changes
- Watercraft location reporting and recovery support with waterproof housing
- Fleet asset utilization reporting using mileage and over‑speed notifications
- Stationary equipment protection using geo‑fence and curfew alerts to detect unexpected moves
- Recovery and theft deterrence for valuable non powered assets using magnet or permanent mounts depending on installation needs

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions can change how frequently alerts are generated and which settings are available; check device firmware notes for details
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may alter cellular band support or enclosure labeling; confirm the unit you receive matches your deployment requirements
- Installation method affects behavior and detection; magnet mount and permanent mount options have different mechanical considerations
- Some advanced reporting intervals and alert thresholds are configurable and may depend on how the device is provisioned by the installer
- Manufacturer documentation is the authoritative source for latest specifications and supported configurations

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the Starfinder KAMEL with Plaspy gives organizations a single place to view location history, receive geofence and motion alerts, and review utilization metrics for tracked assets. Plaspy aggregates incoming events into dashboards and notifications so teams can take action quickly when an asset moves unexpectedly or when usage exceeds defined limits.
