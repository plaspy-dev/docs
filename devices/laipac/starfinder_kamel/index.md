# Laipac - Starfinder KAMEL

<div className="device-logo">
![Starfinder KAMEL](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Laipac Starfinder KAMEL is a versatile GPS tracking device built to monitor, track, and recover both non powered and powered assets. Designed for industrial and field applications, it covers vehicles, watercraft, trailers, construction machinery, and stationary equipment. The device includes an industrial grade rechargeable battery rated at 18Ah and is advertised to support extended operation periods of up to four months on a full charge, making it suitable for long duration deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Starfinder KAMEL can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can use the device s continuous positioning reports, geo fence events, motion alerts, and over speed notifications to support fleet monitoring, asset recovery, and routine reporting. That compatibility makes the Starfinder KAMEL a practical option for organizations that want rugged hardware paired with a modern tracking platform.

## Key Highlights

- Suitable for powered and non powered assets including vehicles trailers watercraft and heavy equipment
- Long lasting rechargeable 18Ah battery with advertised operation up to four months on a full charge
- Continuous 24 7 positioning reports to support location visibility and recovery workflows
- Geo fence capability with in and out alerts plus curfew and over speed notifications
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure that is shockproof dustproof and waterproof for industrial use
- Flexible mounting options with permanent screw mount or strong 80 lb rare earth magnet attachments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the Starfinder KAMEL delivers regular position updates and event notifications so operators can monitor assets from a single platform. Plaspy collects and processes those updates to provide live visibility historical playback and configurable alerts.

- Live location tracking and map display for real time monitoring of assets
- Geo fence events and curfew alerts routed into Plaspy for immediate notifications
- Motion and movement reports used to detect use periods and inactivity in Plaspy dashboards
- Overspeed and mileage reports available for operational review and basic fleet oversight
- Historical position logs and reporting to support audits recovery and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring trailers and unpowered assets during storage and transit
- Tracking construction machinery and site equipment between job sites
- Securing watercraft and marine equipment while docked or at sea
- Managing vehicle fleets where long battery autonomy reduces maintenance visits
- Tracking rented assets and equipment to support recovery and usage billing

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Starfinder KAMEL combines rugged construction with long battery autonomy and a practical feature set for asset protection and fleet oversight. Its ability to report position continuously and generate geo fence and motion alerts makes it a good match for organizations that need reliable location data without constant power availability.

Paired with Plaspy the device becomes part of a centralized tracking environment where alerts reporting and location history are accessible to operators and managers. For teams that prioritize long deployment intervals ruggedness and basic event alerts, the Starfinder KAMEL represents a sensible hardware choice to use with Plaspy.
