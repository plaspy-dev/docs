# Laipac - Kamel S

<div className="device-logo">
![Kamel S](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Kamel S is a compact, self-powered GPS tracker designed for covert, long-duration monitoring of both powered and non-powered assets. Built for outdoor use, it combines GNSS positioning and 4G LTE connectivity with a high-capacity Li‑Ion polymer battery and an IP68-rated enclosure to deliver reliable location visibility for trailers, containers, construction equipment and rented machinery.

As a Plaspy compatible asset locator, the Kamel S brings steady location feeds and essential motion telemetry into a centralized fleet platform. Its configurable reporting and event detection make it a practical choice for fleet managers who want low-maintenance tracking, actionable alerts and consolidated reporting inside Plaspy without changing asset workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for centralized real-time tracking and fleet oversight
- 4G LTE connectivity combined with GNSS positioning for continuous location updates
- Long-life Li‑Ion polymer battery for extended operation of non-powered assets
- IP68 water resistance and rugged enclosure for harsh outdoor environments
- Magnet-mount and permanent-mount deployment options for covert or long-term placement
- Integrated motion and G-sensor detection for movement, tow and event-based alerts
- Configurable reporting by time interval and distance travelled to support mileage records

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Kamel S streams GNSS locations and motion telemetry to the Plaspy platform for near real-time visibility. Plaspy ingests the device reports, converts sensor events into alerts, and centralizes devices, groups and historical routes so teams can monitor assets, enforce geofences and act on exceptions from a single dashboard.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates for continuous situational awareness
- Event alerts for towing, geofence violations and other motion based triggers
- Configurable mileage and distance-travelled reporting to support utilization and billing
- Consolidation of Kamel S location feeds with group management and historical routes
- Centralized notifications and reporting to streamline operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert asset recovery and anti-theft monitoring for trailers and towed equipment
- Construction site equipment tracking where long battery life and rugged design are needed
- Rental and leased equipment utilization tracking and distance based billing
- Fast deployment monitoring for containers and intermittently used assets
- General long-duration protection for remote or non-powered stock

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Kamel S is a practical choice for organizations that need discreet, low-maintenance tracking paired with a centralized fleet platform. Its balance of long battery life, rugged protection and flexible mounting options fits use cases where ongoing physical access to assets is limited or where covert placement is preferred. Integrating the Kamel S with Plaspy brings those location and event feeds into a single operational view, reducing the effort required to monitor distributed assets and respond to incidents.
