# Laipac - Kamel S

![Kamel S](./tracker.png)

The Kamel S is a compact, self-powered GPS tracker engineered for covert, long-life monitoring of powered and non-powered assets. Designed for 4G LTE connectivity and GNSS positioning, the Kamel S delivers reliable real-time tracking for construction equipment, trailers, containers and rented machinery. As a Plaspy compatible asset locator, it brings long-duration location visibility and essential telemetry into one platform for fleet managers who need secure, low-maintenance tracking with configurable alerts.

Built to operate in harsh outdoor conditions, the Kamel S pairs a high-capacity Li‑Ion polymer battery and IP68 water resistance with integrated motion and G‑sensor detection. Its permanent- or magnet-mount options make installation flexible — permanent mounting for up to one year of operation life, or a no-installation magnet mount for covert placements. When integrated into Plaspy, the Kamel S provides continuous location feeds, event alerts and mileage reports to streamline fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and asset utilization workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for consistent real-time tracking and centralized fleet management.
- 4G LTE connectivity with GNSS positioning to provide 24/7/365 location updates and telemetry.
- Two deployment options — permanent mount \(up to one-year operation life\) or magnet mount for covert placement.
- Long-life Li‑Ion polymer battery engineered for extended operation of non-powered assets.
- IP68 water resistance and rugged design for harsh outdoor environments and construction sites.
- Integrated motion and G-sensor detection for tow, geofence violations and excessive-speed alerts.
- Configurable reporting \(time-interval and distance-travelled\) for accurate mileage and utilization records.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated as a Plaspy compatible device, the Kamel S streams GNSS locations and motion telemetry to Plaspy’s platform in near real time. Plaspy ingests the device’s configurable reports \(time-interval and distance-based\), converts sensor events into alerts, and centralizes device groups, maps and historical routes. This allows fleet and asset managers to track individual units or groups, set geofences, and receive immediate notifications when a device moves outside authorized zones.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via 4G LTE for continuous situational awareness.
- Towing and geofence violation alerts based on integrated motion and G-sensor detection.
- Configurable mileage and distance-travelled reports to support utilization and maintenance schedules.
- Excessive-speed alerts to help enforce safe operation and detect potential misuse.
- Ability to consolidate Kamel S data with additional vehicle channels in Plaspy — for example, ignition, fuel monitoring, immobilizer status or Bluetooth sensors if those inputs are available from the vehicle or supplementary devices.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the Kamel S as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivers a pragmatic balance of covert installation, long battery life and rugged reliability.
