# Laipac - Kamel S Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Laipac Kamel S and explains how its capabilities are presented when used with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing functionality and practical uses such as covert long life monitoring, GNSS location reporting, motion based event detection, and centralized visibility inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability, behavior, and operational limits can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and how the manufacturer configures each unit. Where applicable this page notes practical considerations such as mounting options, battery life dependency on reporting configuration, and the importance of checking manufacturer documentation for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The Kamel S is a compact, self powered GPS tracker built for long duration monitoring of both powered and non powered assets. It combines GNSS positioning and 4G LTE connectivity with a rugged, weather resistant enclosure and motion sensing to deliver continuous location visibility and event driven alerts.

- Covert form factor with magnet mount or permanent mount options for flexible installation.
- Long life Li‑Ion polymer battery designed for extended operation on non powered assets.
- GNSS positioning and 4G LTE connectivity for continuous location reporting and telemetry.
- IP68 rated enclosure to withstand harsh outdoor conditions on construction sites and equipment.
- Integrated motion and G sensor detection to trigger tow, movement, or related alerts.
- Configurable reporting on time interval and distance travelled to support mileage and utilization records.

## Core Features of Laipac - Kamel S

- GNSS positioning for accurate location fixes suitable for asset tracking workflows.
- 4G LTE cellular connectivity to deliver regular location and telemetry updates.
- High capacity Li‑Ion polymer battery engineered for multi month operation depending on configuration.
- IP68 water resistant housing for outdoor and construction site deployments.
- Integrated motion detection and G sensor to detect towing, movement, and sudden motion events.
- Two mounting choices: magnet mount for covert or permanent mount for longer service life.
- Configurable reporting modes including time based and distance based transmissions for mileage and utilization tracking.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a Kamel S is integrated into Plaspy, its location and motion reports are ingested into the platform so fleet and asset managers can monitor devices, receive alerts, and review historical routes. Plaspy centralizes device data, converts sensor inputs into meaningful events, and lets teams apply reporting and notification rules across groups of devices.

- Continuous location feeds appear on Plaspy maps for real time situational awareness and historical route review.
- Motion and G sensor events are translated into alerts such as towing or unauthorized movement notifications.
- Time interval and distance based reports are used to generate mileage and utilization records inside Plaspy.
- Grouping and rule based notifications let operations manage multiple Kamel S devices consistently.
- Plaspy can consolidate Kamel S location and motion data with other vehicle channels or supplementary sensors when those inputs are available from the vehicle or additional devices.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert anti theft monitoring for trailers, containers, and high value portable assets.
- Construction equipment tracking where assets are off grid and exposed to harsh environments.
- Rental and leased machinery utilization and mileage reporting for billing and maintenance planning.
- Trailer and container logistics for rapid deployment using magnet mount placement.
- Long duration monitoring of non powered stock and remote assets with minimal maintenance overhead.
