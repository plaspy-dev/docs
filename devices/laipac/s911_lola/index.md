# Laipac - S911 Lola

![S911 Lola](./logo.jpg)

The S911 Lola by Laipac is a mobile Personal Emergency Response System \(mPERS\) that combines GPS tracking technology with cellular phone capabilities to provide instant two-way communication and location tracking. This small and portable GPS locator is designed to address emergency situations where assistance may not be readily available or when the user is at risk due to being alone. It is suitable for a variety of applications, including people with disabilities, lone workers, students on campus, teenage drivers, recreational activities in remote areas, child safety, and personal asset tracking.

The S911 Lola is similar in size to a car's remote starter or door opener, making it easy to carry. It can be attached to a keychain, carried in a pocket or backpack, or worn on a belt, lanyard, or wrist strap with optional accessories. The device is equipped with an SOS panic button that, when pressed for 3 seconds, sends a text message and system alert to a predetermined emergency contact number. It provides the caller ID, location coordinates, and time and date of the panic alert. The SOS panic button also opens two-way cellular communication through a speakerphone, allowing the user to communicate with the emergency contact even if they are unable to speak or communicate verbally.

The S911 Lola offers a range of features to enhance safety and security, including AGPS receiver for outdoor and indoor applications, real-time position reporting, geofence alerts, fall alerts with false alarm cancellation, over speed alerts, and audible medication reminders. It has a battery life of up to 30 hours per charge and offers standby, sleep, and asset tracking modes for extended usage. The device can also be monitored in real-time on LocationNow.com, providing peace of mind and protection wherever you go.
