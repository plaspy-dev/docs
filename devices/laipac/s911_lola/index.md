# Laipac - S911 Lola

![S911 Lola](./logo.jpg)

The Laipac S911 Lola is a mobile personal emergency response system (mPERS) that combines GPS location tracking with cellular two way communication. Designed as a small, portable locator roughly the size of a car remote, the S911 Lola offers an SOS panic button that sends alerts to a designated contact, provides location coordinates and time of the event, and opens two way voice communication for immediate assistance. Its compact form factor and multiple carrying options make it easy to keep on a keychain, lanyard, belt, or in a pocket.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S911 Lola can be integrated into Plaspy's tracking and monitoring platform to provide centralized visibility and alert handling. When paired with Plaspy, location reports and incident alerts from the device can be displayed on live maps, routed to operators, and included in historical reports. This makes the S911 Lola a practical choice for organizations and individuals who need personal safety monitoring alongside fleet or asset oversight within Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Mobile personal emergency response system with GPS location and two way voice communication
- SOS panic button that sends alert details and opens two way calling to an emergency contact
- Compact and portable design suitable for keychain or wearable carry
- Assisted GPS receiver for improved outdoor and indoor location reporting
- Built in alerts such as geofence, fall detection, and over speed to enhance situational awareness
- Long battery runtime and multiple operation modes for standby and asset tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

The S911 Lola can feed its position and alert messages into Plaspy so administrators and monitoring staff can see events in context and respond efficiently. Plaspy collects and visualizes location updates, preserves event history, and provides tools for configuring alert handling and notifications.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring
- SOS and alert notifications presented to operators for rapid response coordination
- Geofence and event alerts shown alongside historical movement and timestamps
- Consolidated reporting of incidents and position history for review and compliance
- Use of Plaspy dashboards to monitor multiple devices including personal trackers and fleet assets

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for people with disabilities or at risk when alone
- Lone worker protection and check in monitoring in non staffed environments
- Campus safety for students and staff with quick alerting and location sharing
- Recreational tracking for users in remote areas who may need emergency assistance
- Asset tracking of high value portable items or equipment that benefit from location alerts

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The S911 Lola pairs compact personal safety features with basic location reporting, making it a sensible option for organizations that need a lightweight, wearable device for personnel or assets. When used with Plaspy, the device's alerts and location updates can be incorporated into broader operational views, enabling centralized monitoring, faster incident response, and consolidated reporting across devices.

Because the S911 Lola focuses on immediate personal alerts and two way communication, it complements Plaspy's platform capabilities by bringing direct user initiated alerts into a system designed for visibility and oversight. For operations that mix personal safety devices with vehicle or asset tracking, the combination offers a practical way to manage different device types from a single platform.
