# Laipac - S911 Lola Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Laipac S911 Lola and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about the device as a mobile personal emergency response system and how core functions such as location reporting and panic alerts can be surfaced in Plaspy for monitoring and operational awareness.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, accessory configuration, and how the device is installed and provisioned. For the most current device specific details consult official Laipac documentation and the manufacturer resources.

## Feature Overview

The S911 Lola is a compact mPERS device that combines GPS location reporting with cellular two way communication and dedicated alert functions. It is designed to be carried or worn, with an SOS panic button, location reporting, and several alert modes to support personal safety and simple asset tracking.

- Portable form factor for keychain or lanyard carry, suited to personal safety use
- SOS panic button that sends an alert with caller ID, coordinates, and time stamp
- Two way cellular voice through the device speakerphone for direct communication in emergencies
- Assisted GPS for improved outdoor and some indoor location performance
- Alert types including geofence, fall alerts with false alarm cancellation, and over speed notifications
- Multiple operating modes for longer use such as standby, sleep, and asset tracking

## Core Features of Laipac - S911 Lola

- Mobile Personal Emergency Response System with dedicated SOS panic function
- Real time position reporting using AGPS to provide location coordinates
- Two way cellular voice connectivity via speakerphone for direct contact with emergency contacts
- Geofence alerts to signal entry or exit of predefined areas
- Fall alert capability with false alarm cancellation logic
- Over speed alerting to indicate potential high speed movement
- Audible medication reminders for scheduled prompts
- Multiple usage modes including standby sleep and asset tracking to extend operational time

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present the device events and location information reported by the S911 Lola, helping teams keep track of people or assets and respond to incidents more quickly. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols so reported events integrate into the platform without requiring protocol selection by the user.

- Real time locations are shown on Plaspy maps to provide visibility into current device position
- SOS panic events are recorded with the associated coordinates and timestamp so responders can act on the alert
- Geofence triggers appear as events in Plaspy so you can review entry and exit activity
- Fall alerts submitted by the device are surfaced to operations staff for review
- Device mode changes such as sleep or asset tracking appear as status indicators within the device record
- Event history and timestamps are retained for later review and audit

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety for people with mobility or health concerns who need an easy emergency call option
- Lone worker monitoring where quick location and alerting improves response
- Student and campus safety for staff and students who require a compact emergency device
- Teen driver oversight and peace of mind for guardians
- Recreational safety when participants are active in remote areas
- Lightweight asset tracking for portable items that need occasional monitoring

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features and alert behaviors depend on device firmware and may change with updates
- Two way voice functionality requires an active cellular service plan and proper provisioning
- Fall alert sensitivity and false alarm cancellation may vary between firmware versions and setup
- Regional hardware variants or network compatibility can affect cellular performance and coverage
- Optional accessories such as straps or clips may change how the device is carried and how reliably alerts are triggered
