# Laipac - Kamel S - NA

<div className="device-logo">
![Kamel S - NA](./logo..png)
</div>

The Laipac Kamel S - NA is a compact, versatile GPS tracker designed to improve visibility and management of portable and tracked assets. It uses 4G LTE connectivity combined with GNSS positioning to provide real-time location updates, and includes built-in features such as Time Interval and Distance Travelled reporting, Tow Alerts, Over Speeding Alerts, and Geofence Violation alerts. The unit is IP68 water resistant and designed for quick deployment without installation, making it suitable for a wide range of asset tracking scenarios.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the Kamel S - NA can feed location and event data into Plaspy’s fleet and asset management tools to support monitoring, reporting, and alerting workflows. Plaspy users can leverage the tracker’s reporting and alert features to improve utilization insights, enforce operational policies, and receive timely notifications about asset movement or boundary events, all within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- 4G LTE connectivity with GNSS for real time location tracking
- Time Interval and Distance Travelled reports to monitor usage patterns
- Tow Alerts, Over Speeding Alerts, and Geofence Violation alerts for timely notifications
- No installation required for quick attachment and redeployment
- IP68 water resistance for use in challenging outdoor environments
- Long lasting Li Ion polymer battery for extended field operation
- Compatible with monitoring platforms including integration with Plaspy for centralized management

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected into Plaspy, the Kamel S - NA provides continuous position and event data that Plaspy presents in dashboards, maps, and reports to give teams operational oversight. Plaspy ingests alerts and reporting data from the device so users can centralize monitoring across mixed fleets and asset types.

- Real time location visibility on Plaspy maps for tracked assets
- Scheduled and on demand interval and distance reports to assess utilization
- Immediate notifications in Plaspy for tow, over speed, and geofence events
- Historical event logs and playback for investigations and operational review
- Fleet level monitoring and grouping to manage multiple Kamel S units together
- Customizable alert rules and reporting cadence within Plaspy for operational needs

- Monitoring portable assets and inventory moved between sites
- Temporary deployments where quick attach trackers are preferred over permanent installs
