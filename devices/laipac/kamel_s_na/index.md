# Laipac - Kamel S - NA

![Kamel S - NA](./logo..png)

The Laipac Kamel S - NA is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features to help improve the utilization and management of your assets. With its 4G LTE connectivity and GNSS capabilities, you can track the real-time location of your assets with accuracy and reliability. Whether you need to monitor your stock, rented equipment, or acquired assets, the Kamel S is designed to provide you with the information you need to make informed decisions.

One of the standout features of the Kamel S is its ability to generate Time Interval & Distance Travelled reports. This allows you to keep track of how far your assets have traveled over a specific time period, giving you valuable insights into their usage patterns. Additionally, the Kamel S offers Tow Alerts, Over Speeding Alerts, and Geofence Violation alerts, ensuring that you are promptly notified of any unauthorized or unusual activity.

With the Kamel S, installation is a breeze as it requires no installation. Simply attach the tracker to your asset and you're good to go. The tracker is also IP68 water-resistant, providing protection against water damage in various environments. Its long-lasting Li-Ion polymer battery ensures that you can rely on the Kamel S for extended periods without the need for frequent recharging.

When paired with Laipac's LocationNow.com monitoring platform, the Kamel S offers even more functionality. You can customize regular reports and alerts, view real-time positions, log pertinent vehicle data, and receive alerts when vehicles have exited or entered predefined zones. This comprehensive solution allows you to effectively manage and track your assets, giving you peace of mind and improved operational efficiency.

### Key Features:

- 4G LTE with GNSS for accurate and reliable tracking
- Time Interval & Distance Travelled reports for monitoring asset usage
- Tow Alerts, Over Speeding Alerts, and Geofence Violation alerts for prompt notifications
- No installation required for easy setup
- IP68 water resistance for protection against water damage
- Long-lasting Li-Ion polymer battery for extended usage
- Integration with LocationNow.com monitoring platform for advanced features
