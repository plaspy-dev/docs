# Laipac - S911 LOLA S

![S911 LOLA S](./tracker.png)

The S911 LOLA S is a compact mobile Personal Emergency Response System \(mPERS\) designed for real-time personal safety and remote monitoring. Plaspy compatible and built for dependable 4G LTE GNSS reporting, the Lola S delivers continuous real-time tracking, a dedicated SOS button and two-way voice so caregivers, dispatchers or monitoring centers can locate and speak with a user instantly during an emergency. Its combination of fall detection, automatic check-ins and geofence alerts makes the Lola S a practical GPS tracker option for families and organizations that need fast, reliable situational awareness.

Designed to work with managed monitoring platforms such as Laipac’s LocationNow and compatible with Plaspy deployments, the LOLA S brings mPERS functionality into larger safety and telemetry ecosystems. Whether deployed for elderly care, lone-worker protection, campus security or VIP protection, this compact device provides the core safety features organizations expect from a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker while offering accessory options to adapt to a variety of wear-and-carry needs.

## Key Highlights

- Real-time location reporting on 4G LTE with integrated GNSS — accurate GPS tracking for immediate response.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button and immediate SOS calling to monitoring centers or caregivers.
- Two-way voice communication for direct contact between the user and a monitoring dispatcher or responder.
- Automatic fall detection \(man-down\) using a G-force sensor to trigger alerts when a fall is detected.
- Automatic check-in alerts and medicine reminders to support daily care routines and compliance.
- Geofence creation and geofence-violation alerts to monitor safe zones and respond to unexpected exits.
- Accessory ecosystem \(wrist straps, belt cases, lanyards and more\) to suit personal, occupational or pet applications.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the S911 LOLA S becomes a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that feeds real-time position, status and event data into your existing monitoring workflow. Plaspy can ingest the device’s GNSS coordinates, SOS triggers, fall events and check-ins to provide consolidated dashboards, alerting rules and historical reports. This lets operations teams and caregivers view live locations, receive instant alarms and manage responses from the same Plaspy console used for other telemetry and fleet assets.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GNSS position and timestamped updates for accurate tracking.
- SOS emergency and two-way voice: immediate alarm events plus voice channel for assessment and reassurance.
- Fall detection and stored G-force data: automated man-down alerts and event logs to support incident review.
- Geofence violation alerts and check-in reminders: configurable zones and scheduled notifications for preventive care.
- Care and monitoring metadata: medicine reminders, event notes and status flags available to Plaspy for logging and reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The S911 LOLA S is designed for organizations and families that prioritize quick response, continuous supervision and ease of integration. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the Lola S offers clear operational value: reliable real-time tracking, immediate SOS and fall detection events, and two-way voice to triage incidents faster. While the Lola S is focused on personal safety rather than vehicle telematics features such as fuel monitoring, ignition control or immobilizer functions, its telemetry and alert streams can be consolidated alongside fleet devices in Plaspy dashboards so teams maintain a single pane of glass for operational awareness.

Choosing the Lola S with Plaspy compatibility delivers turnkey safety monitoring that scales from individual users to managed programs. The accessory options and clinical- and security-oriented features \(medicine reminders, geofencing, fall logging\) make it straightforward to deploy for elderly care, lone-worker programs and campus safety initiatives. For organizations that already use Plaspy for fleet management and telemetry, adding mPERS devices like the LOLA S expands coverage across people and assets without fragmenting alerting or reporting workflows.
