# Laipac - StarFinder AIRE Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused description of the Laipac StarFinder AIRE and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It highlights the device's practical functions, typical operational value, and what to expect when integrating the tracker into Plaspy for location monitoring and alerting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Wherever possible this page reflects the device description from Laipac, but users should confirm current, device-specific details with the manufacturer and installation notes.

## Feature Overview

The StarFinder AIRE is a compact GPS tracker designed for small vehicles and assets, offering location reporting, movement alerts, and remote configuration options. It emphasizes straightforward installation, real-time tracking, and a durable enclosure for outdoor use.

- Real-time position updates based on configurable time and distance intervals for timely location visibility.
- Geo-fence capability with in/out alerts to notify when an asset crosses defined boundaries.
- Motion detection and G-sensor alerts that report movement or impacts to support theft detection or misuse monitoring.
- Over-speed and mileage reporting to help monitor usage patterns for vehicles and rental equipment.
- Internal relays and multiple I/O ports for basic output control and status inputs from the tracked unit.

## Core Features of Laipac - StarFinder AIRE

- GSM/GPRS cellular connectivity for wide area coverage and online reporting.
- GPS 48 channel receiver with satellite augmentation support for improved position accuracy.
- Geo-fence alerts with in and out notification options.
- Built-in G-sensor to detect movement and impacts, enabling lift and motion alerts.
- Configurable real-time tracking intervals tied to time and distance thresholds.
- Mileage reporting and over-speed alert functionality for usage oversight.
- Main power disconnect alert plus a rechargeable internal backup battery for continued reporting on power loss.
- IP67 rated enclosure for water, dust, and shock resistance suitable for exposed installations.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and interprets standard location and event messages from compatible trackers such as the StarFinder AIRE, allowing teams to view position history and alerts in one platform. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols to simplify configuration and reporting.

- Position updates appear on Plaspy maps and history views, showing movement over time.
- Geo-fence events are recorded as boundary crossing alerts and can generate notifications inside Plaspy.
- Motion and G-sensor events translate to activity or tamper alerts that help surface potential theft or impact incidents.
- Power disconnect and backup battery events show as device status changes so operators can react to offline or powered down devices.
- Usage reports such as mileage and overspeed events are available to support fleet oversight and operational analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking small vehicles such as motorbikes, scooters, and ATVs for route and status visibility.
- Monitoring boats and other recreational craft where waterproof and shockproof design is required.
- Protecting high-value or high-risk assets such as standalone ATMs or remote equipment installations.
- Rental machine monitoring to capture working hours, mileage, and movement patterns.
- Theft detection workflows using movement, lift, and power disconnect alerts to trigger rapid response.
