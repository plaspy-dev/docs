# Laipac - Kamel S - E Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Laipac Kamel S - E tracker and explains how those features are applied when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive descriptions of capabilities such as location tracking, reporting, alerts, and the typical operational value these features deliver when monitored through Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior for any individual Kamel S - E unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. For device specific parameters, firmware notes, and the most recent technical details, consult Laipac documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Laipac Kamel S - E is a portable asset tracker that combines cellular 4G LTE connectivity with GNSS positioning to provide up to date location visibility and event reporting. Its core design targets asset utilization monitoring and alerting for assets that are moved, rented, or otherwise deployed outside fixed locations.

- Real time location updates via 4G LTE and GNSS for persistent position awareness
- Time Interval and Distance Travelled reports to help quantify usage and movement
- Tow Alerts to notify when an asset is moved unexpectedly
- Over Speeding alerts and Geofence Violation alerts for situational awareness
- No installation required for quick deployment and IP68 water resistance for outdoor durability

## Core Features of Laipac - Kamel S - E

- 4G LTE connectivity combined with GNSS positioning for location fixes
- Time Interval reporting to view how long an asset has been in use
- Distance Travelled reporting to capture movement totals over reporting periods
- Tow Alerts to detect unauthorized or unexpected movement
- Over Speeding Alerts to flag instances of excess speed when supported by the device
- Geofence Violation Alerts to notify entry or exit from predefined zones
- Portable form factor that requires no permanent installation
- IP68 rating for water and dust resistance in challenging environments

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Kamel S - E reports its location and configured events into the platform so administrators can monitor assets in a single operational view. Plaspy automatically recognizes compatible tracker protocols and surfaces location and event data for visualization and alerting.

- Real time position updates appear on the Plaspy map for continuous visibility
- Time Interval and Distance Travelled summaries can be reviewed in Plaspy reporting tools
- Tow, Over Speeding, and Geofence alerts can be configured to generate notifications inside Plaspy
- Device status and recent activity are available in the Plaspy device list for quick oversight
- Plaspy supports customized reporting schedules and alert routing to match operational workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking rented equipment to measure usage hours and distance for billing or maintenance
- Monitoring portable assets stored outdoors or moved between sites where IP68 protection is beneficial
- Detecting unauthorized movement of valuable inventory with Tow Alerts
- Using geofence alerts to manage entry and exit of assets from defined zones
- Producing regular distance and interval reports to support preventive maintenance planning
- Providing operational oversight for fleets of portable devices without permanent installation
