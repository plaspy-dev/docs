# NVS - Navitrek UM-02

<div className="device-logo">
![Navitrek UM-02](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Navitrek UM-02 is an autonomous search device designed to locate objects and transmit location information to a control center using GPRS and SMS channels. It combines a compact form factor with positioning functionality, making it suitable for tracking and monitoring vehicles, assets, or individuals where discreet, reliable reporting is required. The device description highlights a domestic certified GLONASS/GPS receiver series NV08C from ZAO KB NAVIS, which contributes to improved positioning reliability.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Navitrek UM-02 can be integrated into a fleet and asset monitoring workflow to provide centralized visibility. Plaspy can receive the location updates and present them in maps, historical tracks, and operational reports, allowing organizations to use the UM-02 for routine monitoring, alerts, and oversight without replacing existing processes. This compatibility makes the Navitrek UM-02 a practical choice for users who want straightforward tracking capability combined with Plaspy platform tools.

- Autonomous search and tracking device intended to locate objects and report locations

- Uses the NV08C GLONASS GPS receiver from ZAO KB NAVIS for enhanced positioning reliability

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location reports from compatible trackers like the Navitrek UM-02 and converts them into actionable visibility for operations teams. Once connected, Plaspy can use incoming position updates to build live and historical context for each device and offer tools for monitoring and reporting.

- Map visualization of current device location and recent movement history
- Live position updates and status visibility for fleet and asset monitoring
- Configurable alerts and notifications based on movement or reporting patterns
- Historical tracks and exportable reports for analysis and accountability
- Centralized device management and overview to support operational decisions

## Typical Use Cases

- Asset tracking for high value or movable inventory management
- Personnel location monitoring for lone worker oversight and safety checks

- Rental equipment tracking and return verification
