# NVS

NVS produces GPS tracking hardware used in vehicle and asset monitoring. On this page you will find clear guidance about how NVS devices work with Plaspy and where to go next to connect a device to your tracking platform. The Brand Catalog below lists all NVS models that are compatible with Plaspy so you can pick a device that fits your fleet or asset requirements.

## How NVS devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and processes location and telemetry data sent by NVS trackers using industry standard protocols. Once an NVS device is configured to forward data to Plaspy, the platform decodes incoming messages, displays real time locations on maps, logs historical tracks, and applies rules and alerts based on your settings. This page is intended to help you confirm compatibility and find the right device in the catalog; device specific setup instructions live on each model page.

## Common features found in NVS devices

NVS trackers commonly include features relevant to vehicle and asset monitoring. While specific capabilities vary across models, typical features you will encounter include:
- GPS based location reporting for precise tracking
- Cellular connectivity for data transmission to cloud platforms
- Motion and ignition detection for trip and idle monitoring
- Digital and analog inputs for external sensors and events
- Low power modes for long term asset tracking

For exact capabilities and configuration steps consult the device page in the catalog above rather than this brand overview.

## Typical use cases for NVS GPS trackers

NVS devices are suited to a range of tracking scenarios. Some representative use cases include:
- Fleet vehicle tracking for route monitoring and driver oversight
- Asset protection and recovery for trailers and mobile equipment
- Equipment telemetry for usage based maintenance planning
- Non powered asset surveillance using long battery life modes

Plaspy helps turn the raw device data into actionable insights for logistics, field service, construction, and rental operations.

## Why choose Plaspy for NVS device monitoring

Plaspy is designed to accept and process data from a wide ecosystem of GPS hardware including NVS trackers. Key benefits of using Plaspy with NVS devices:
- Unified monitoring across mixed fleets regardless of device brand
- Flexible alerting and reporting to support operational workflows
- Map based visualization and historical replay for incident review
- API access for integration with existing back office systems

Plaspy focuses on platform reliability and usability so you can concentrate on managing assets rather than handling data formats.

## Help finding the right NVS device

The interactive Brand Catalog above surfaces the NVS models that are compatible with Plaspy. When choosing a tracker, consider:
- Connectivity needs such as cellular bands and roaming support
- Power options including hardwired installation or battery operation
- Required inputs and outputs for sensors you plan to use
- Anticipated reporting frequency and battery life trade offs

If you are unsure which device suits your project, review the model pages in the catalog and then consult Plaspy documentation or your hardware vendor for procurement advice.
