# NVS - SN-5001

![SN-5001](./logo.jpg)

The NVS SN-5001 is a compact and reliable navigation device that combines the power of GLONASS and GPS satellite systems to accurately determine your current location. With this device, you can easily transmit your location information to a monitoring center, allowing for real-time tracking and monitoring. Additionally, the SN-5001 is equipped with the capability to receive incoming and outgoing phone calls to programmed numbers, providing an added layer of communication and security.

One of the standout features of the NVS SN-5001 is its ability to send an alarm signal to the monitoring center in case of an emergency. This ensures that help can be dispatched quickly and efficiently, providing peace of mind for both individuals and organizations. The device also offers a range of customizable settings, allowing users to tailor the device to their specific needs and preferences.

With its compact size and advanced features, the NVS SN-5001 is an ideal choice for a wide range of applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, monitor assets, or ensure the safety of individuals, this navigation device offers the reliability and functionality you need. Trust in the NVS SN-5001 to provide accurate location information and seamless communication, making it an invaluable tool for any tracking and monitoring needs.
