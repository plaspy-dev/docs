# ThingSys - TS-P4B

![TS-P4B](./tracker.jpg)

The TS-P4B is a magnetic, large battery GPS tracker designed for long-duration and discreet vehicle and asset tracking. It combines a high-capacity rechargeable battery with a robust positioning chipset and energy saving modes to support extended standby and infrequent maintenance. Built to attach securely to metal surfaces, the unit is suited for applications where battery life, secure mounting and reliable location visibility are primary requirements.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TS-P4B can feed continuous location and telemetry into Plaspy’s fleet and asset management platform. That compatibility makes it a practical choice for operations that need persistent tracking, event alerts and historical route data without daily intervention, allowing Plaspy users to monitor assets, schedule maintenance and react to motion or low-battery events from a centralized dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- High capacity battery options for extended standby and reduced maintenance intervals
- Magnetic mounting and waterproofing for discreet and secure attachment to vehicles and containers
- u-blox based GNSS positioning with assisted fixes for reliable location reporting
- 3D accelerometer and light sensor for motion-triggered updates and tamper awareness
- Remote voice monitoring and SMS reporting to add situational context during events
- Low standby current and wide operating temperature range for demanding field use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TS-P4B supplies periodic and event-driven location and status data that Plaspy uses for live tracking, history playback and alerting. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and makes it available across map views, reports and notification workflows so operators can maintain visibility and respond to predefined conditions.

- Live position updates and historical route playback within Plaspy for operational oversight
- Motion and tamper events from the accelerometer routed as alerts or used to trigger virtual ignition or movement rules
- Battery level and low-battery notifications feeding Plaspy workflows to support proactive maintenance
- Light-sensor and remote voice monitoring used as supplementary context for security or recovery checks
- SMS or platform alerts from the device complement Plaspy notifications to ensure timely awareness

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for rental cars, buses and trucks where discreet long-duration tracking reduces downtime
- Asset and container tracking in logistics where secure mounting and waterproofing are important
- Anti-theft monitoring and recovery with motion alerts, low-battery warnings and remote voice checks
- Long-term surveillance or monitoring where infrequent maintenance is required
- Remote equipment monitoring at sites without reliable wired power using battery-backed operation

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

For teams that prioritize endurance, secure attachment and dependable positioning, the TS-P4B presents a balanced option for Plaspy deployments. Its large battery capacities and energy-saving behaviors lower the frequency of service calls, while integrated motion sensing and reporting provide meaningful events that Plaspy can convert into alerts and operational insights.

If you want a discreet, long-running tracker that pairs with Plaspy for centralized monitoring, the TS-P4B is a practical choice for many fleet and asset tracking scenarios.
