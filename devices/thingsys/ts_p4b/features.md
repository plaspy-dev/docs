# ThingSys - TS-P4B Features

This page provides a public, feature-oriented overview of the ThingSys TS-P4B magnetic GPS tracker and what to expect when using the device with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker’s practical capabilities and how those capabilities surface in Plaspy for real-time location, event visibility, and routine monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. For device-specific configuration, firmware behavior, and the latest specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The TS-P4B is a magnetic, large-battery GPS tracker designed for long-duration and discreet vehicle or asset deployments. It focuses on endurance, secure mounting, and reliable location reporting over cellular networks while offering motion sensing and remote monitoring features that Plaspy can surface for operational use.

- Long standby life with high-capacity rechargeable battery options for reduced maintenance intervals.
- Magnetic mounting and IP-rated enclosure for secure, discreet attachment on vehicles and containers.
- GNSS positioning with u-blox chipset and A‑GPS assistance for fast fixes and reliable location reporting.
- 3D accelerometer and light sensor for motion-triggered updates, tamper awareness, and power saving modes.
- Remote monitoring and alerting via SMS and online reporting, plus remote voice monitoring for situational checks.

## Core Features of ThingSys - TS-P4B

- High-capacity rechargeable battery options including 10000 mAh and a 5000 mAh variant for extended standby.
- u-blox GNSS positioning with A‑GPS assistance providing typical position accuracy in the neighborhood of 10 meters.
- 2G quad-band cellular connectivity for telemetry and reporting across widely available networks.
- 3D accelerometer for motion detection and related event generation.
- Light sensor to support tamper indication and context-aware reporting.
- Low-battery alerting and energy-saving sleep modes to extend deployment life.
- Strong internal magnets and a rugged, IP-rated housing suitable for outdoor and vehicle use.
- Remote voice monitoring and SMS-based remote management for additional situational awareness.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TS-P4B reports location and telemetry, Plaspy ingests that data to provide live tracking, historical playback, and event-driven notifications. Plaspy’s platform makes the tracker’s position, motion state, and battery status visible to operators and can route alerts into workflows used for fleet and asset oversight.

- Real-time position updates and historical route playback based on GNSS fixes reported by the device.
- Motion and tamper events from the accelerometer and light sensor appear as actionable events in Plaspy dashboards.
- Low-battery notifications and battery level telemetry surface in Plaspy to support scheduled maintenance.
- Remote voice monitoring or SMS alerts can be recorded in Plaspy activity logs as additional context for security checks.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and maps incoming telemetry into the interface for monitoring and alerting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long-duration vehicle tracking for fleets where infrequent maintenance and discreet mounting are priorities.
- Container and asset monitoring in logistics applications that require rugged mounting and environmental protection.
- Anti-theft monitoring with motion detection, low-battery alarms, and remote voice checks to assist recovery workflows.
- Covert or low-profile deployments that benefit from strong magnets and extended battery life.
- Remote site equipment tracking where wired power is unavailable and battery-backed operation is necessary.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions can enable, disable, or modify behavior of motion reporting, power modes, and telemetry intervals; check firmware details for exact behavior.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants (for example cellular radio region support) may change connectivity characteristics.
- Installation method and mounting location affect GPS reception, magnetic attachment security, and sensor responsiveness.
- 2G network availability varies by region and operator; verify cellular coverage and regulatory status where the device will operate.
- Manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes are the authoritative source for the latest specifications and supported features.
