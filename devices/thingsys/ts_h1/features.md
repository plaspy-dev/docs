# ThingSys - TS-H1 Features

This page provides a public, high level summary of the ThingSys TS-H1 GPS tracker features as they relate to use with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about what the device can do, how those capabilities are presented in a fleet or asset monitoring context, and what users can expect when adding TS-H1 devices into Plaspy-based monitoring workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where the TS-H1 offers optional or configurable capabilities, users should confirm which options are present on a given unit and review the manufacturer documentation for the most current device details.

## Feature Overview

The TS-H1 is presented as a compact, rugged tracker with voice call capability, location reporting, and device management features suitable for monitoring people or assets. Its mix of positioning modes and operational safeguards aim to provide continuous location awareness and basic remote control and maintenance.

- Two way dial and answer phone call capability to enable voice communication with the device wearer
- GPS positioning that can return location messages including a Google Maps link for quick viewing
- Location fallback via LBS for improved reporting in areas with poor satellite reception
- Tamper alert to notify a guardian or manager if the device is interfered with
- Reminder and alarm functions for scheduled notifications
- IP67 waterproof rating and built in battery for durable field use

## Core Features of ThingSys - TS-H1

- Dial and answer phone calls for direct voice contact between a guardian and the device wearer
- GPS positioning with location reports that include a Google Maps link for quick map reference
- Location Based Service (LBS) fallback to save and report coarse location in blind spots
- Tamper alert to notify a guardian of potential device interference
- Reminder and alarm functions for scheduled prompts or alerts
- Remote software upgrading plus remote maintenance and control capabilities
- Self diagnostics for operating system and SIM protection to surface device health issues
- IP67 ingress protection rating and an integrated battery for reliable outdoor operation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides a single platform view where TS-H1 devices can be registered and monitored alongside other compatible trackers. When a TS-H1 reports location or events, Plaspy surfaces that information in device lists, maps, and event logs so administrators and guardians can maintain situational awareness.

- Location reports from the TS-H1 are displayed on Plaspy maps, making it easy to follow device position and history
- Tamper alerts, reminder triggers, and self diagnostic events are logged as device events for audit and response
- LBS based position updates provide coarse location visibility in areas where GPS is limited and those updates appear in the device timeline
- Remote firmware upgrade and maintenance states reported by the device can be tracked in Plaspy device status views
- Call and voice related events from the unit are recorded as part of the device activity history for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Guardian monitoring for children or vulnerable adults where voice contact and location links are useful
- Asset tracking for equipment that requires durable, waterproof housing and regular location checks
- Field personnel check in and reminder services using scheduled alarms and voice contact
- Coverage in mixed reception areas where LBS helps maintain some location visibility
- Remote device maintenance workflows that leverage over the air software updates and self diagnostics

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some TS-H1 capabilities may be optional or depend on the device firmware and hardware revision installed by the manufacturer
- LBS accuracy and GPS fix times can vary by environment, installation, and regional satellite availability
- Optional navigation systems such as BDS or assisted GPS support depend on the specific product variant and regional configuration
- Physical installation, SIM provisioning, and local radio frequency bands can affect connectivity and reporting behavior
- Always consult the device serial specific documentation for precise behavior before relying on a particular function for critical operations
