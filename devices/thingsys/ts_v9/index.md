# ThingSys - TS-V9

<div className="device-logo">
![TS-V9](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TS-V9 is a professional vehicle GPS tracker from ThingSys designed for rugged, vehicle focused deployments. It targets fleet management, logistics, rental fleets, buses and other specialized vehicle applications. The device combines multi generation cellular connectivity with a Ublox7 Compass GNSS receiver and a set of vehicle telemetry and security features to deliver continuous position reporting and operational data suitable for professional installations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TS-V9 can feed location, alerts and vehicle telemetry into the Plaspy platform to support live tracking, historical reports and automated notifications. Its combination of platform reporting and SMS based control gives organizations flexible integration paths when adding the TS-V9 to a Plaspy powered fleet monitoring solution.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for professional vehicle use with rugged protections and backup power for resilience during power loss.
- Multi generation cellular connectivity for platform reporting and direct SMS or phone control where available.
- Ublox7 Compass GNSS performance for dependable positioning with manufacturer provided accuracy guidance.
- Vehicle telemetry support including ignition detection, mileage statistics, overspeed alerts and panic alarm.
- Remote control features for immobilizer and circuit control to support anti theft and emergency response workflows.
- Professional I O and bus interfaces plus support for optional fuel sensing and RFID integration for extended telemetry.
- OTA firmware update support and remote configuration options to simplify device maintenance over time.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TS-V9 transmits position and vehicle telemetry over cellular links or via SMS based commands, allowing Plaspy to ingest those data streams and present them within map views, reports and alerting workflows. Once connected to Plaspy, the device enables operational visibility and automated handling of common fleet events.

- Live map positions and regular location updates for fleet visibility and dispatch support.
- Ignition and mileage data available to Plaspy for utilization reporting and maintenance planning.
- Fuel level and consumption insights when OEM fuel sensors are connected, feeding into Plaspy dashboards.
- Remote immobilizer and circuit control actions coordinated through Plaspy notifications and operator workflows.
- Automatic delivery of panic, overspeed and geo fence alerts into Plaspy for fast operator response and logging.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking for visibility, route verification and utilization analysis.
- Rental vehicle monitoring and recovery support using immobilizer and alerting features.
- Bus and specialized vehicle telemetry where integration with onboard systems is required.
- Anti theft response and remote immobilization for high value or high risk vehicles.
- Fuel monitoring and efficiency programs when paired with compatible fuel sensors.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TS-V9 is suited to organizations that need a hardened vehicle tracking core with a broad feature set for fleet telemetry and security. Its hardware protections, multi generation cellular reporting and GNSS positioning make it a practical choice for deployments where consistent location updates and vehicle data are needed. For teams using Plaspy, the TS-V9 provides the telemetry inputs and remote control capabilities that enable dispatch, alerting and maintenance workflows within the Plaspy platform.

While the TS-V9 offers many capabilities for professional installations, specific configuration choices and optional sensor integrations will depend on each fleet's operational needs. Plaspy can ingest the device data and present it alongside other fleet sources to provide a unified operational view.
