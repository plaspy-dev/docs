# ThingSys - J16W

![J16W](./tracker.jpg)

The J16W is a rugged IP67 rated GPS tracker purpose built for vehicle installations. Compact and lightweight, the device pairs a 4G LTE CAT1 modem with 2G fallback and a high sensitivity GNSS receiver to provide consistent positioning and telemetry in demanding environments. The J16W family includes a base unit with ACC and relay control and a J16Wm variant that adds microphone and SOS functionality for emergency workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the J16W is designed to integrate vehicle location, ignition state, and control events into Plaspy dashboards and alerts. Its offline buffering and power resilience help maintain continuous reporting to Plaspy, making the J16W suitable for fleet monitoring, route visibility, anti theft operations, and incident handling when paired with Plaspy fleet workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Built for vehicles with IP67 rated enclosure for protection in harsh conditions.
- Cellular connectivity with 4G LTE and 2G fallback for broad coverage and reliable reporting.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver supporting GPS and BeiDou for accurate vehicle location.
- Compact host size and light weight for discreet installation in fleet assets.
- Wide operating voltage tolerance and a built in backup battery to preserve reporting during power interruptions.
- Dedicated vehicle inputs and relay output for ignition detection and controlled cut or restore actions.
- Offline data storage and retransmit capability to preserve telemetry when connectivity is intermittent.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the J16W transmits GNSS position and vehicle telemetry so Plaspy can present live tracking, alerts, and historical playback. Plaspy ingests status flags and I O events from the device to support operational oversight, automated alerts, and reporting across fleet assets.

- Real time location updates and route playback for operational visibility and monitoring.
- Ignition and ACC detection that can trigger Plaspy alerts, reports, or usage summaries.
- Relay driven cut and restore control usable in vehicle immobilizer and anti theft routines managed inside Plaspy.
- Offline point storage with retransmission so Plaspy retains a continuous telemetry record after network restoration.
- J16Wm variant support for SOS and microphone events so incident workflows in Plaspy can include emergency signalling and voice context.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for buses trucks and service vehicles requiring continuous location and status visibility.
- Vehicle security and anti theft operations using ignition monitoring and relay based immobilizer control.
- Remote incident response where SOS signalling and audio context are routed into operator workflows.
- Small vehicle and motorcycle tracking where compact size and wide voltage support are beneficial.
- Operational workflows that rely on offline buffering and later telemetry reconciliation for reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The J16W is a practical option for organizations that need a rugged vehicle tracker that feeds reliable position and status data into Plaspy. Its durable enclosure, broad cellular coverage, and onboard buffering reduce data gaps and support continuous monitoring across diverse vehicle types. Variants with SOS and microphone support add useful incident handling capabilities when combined with Plaspy incident and alerting features.
