# ThingSys - TS-V7

<div className="device-logo">
![TS-V7](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The TS-V7 Professional Vehicle GPS Tracker from ThingSys is a compact tracker designed for vehicle safety, security, and fleet management. It provides positioning and tracking information over 2G 3G and 4G networks and can be monitored through an online platform or a smartphone. Key features described by the manufacturer include an emergency alarm button with alert dispatch to authorized numbers, remote control of fuel and vehicle circuit, remote audio monitoring by call, movement and overspeed alerts, geo fencing, mileage statistics, ignition detection, and power cutoff.

As a Plaspy compatible device the TS-V7 can feed real time location and event data into Plaspy for consolidated fleet oversight. Plaspy users can bring TS-V7 data into maps dashboards and reporting within the platform to improve visibility across vehicles and assets. Because the TS-V7 also supports additional accessories such as fuel sensors temperature sensors LED screens and RFID readers it can be used with Plaspy to augment operational telematics and reporting where those inputs are available.

## Key Highlights

- Multi generation cellular connectivity for remote vehicle tracking and location updates
- Emergency alarm with alert distribution to pre authorized contacts for faster response
- Remote audio monitoring capability for situational awareness around the vehicle
- Centralized features such as geofence alerts overspeed notifications and mileage logging
- Remote control of fuel circuit and vehicle power functions to support operational management
- Expandable with accessories like fuel and temperature sensors and RFID reader integration

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the TS-V7 delivers position and event information into the Plaspy platform so operations teams can monitor vehicles in a single interface. Plaspy aggregates the tracker data and makes it actionable alongside other fleet inputs.

- Live and historical location display for route review and playback
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy for overspeed geofence breaches and emergency alarms
- Fleet level dashboards showing mileage summaries and event counts for operational oversight
- consolidated monitoring for remote audio triggered events and alarm conditions
- Use of accessory inputs reported by the tracker to enhance reporting and asset management

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operators tracking vehicle location and mileage for scheduling and dispatch
- Security use cases where emergency alarm and remote audio monitoring increase situational awareness
- Fuel management scenarios using remote fuel control and compatible fuel sensors for oversight
- Rental or shared vehicle programs needing geofence and ignition detection for policy enforcement
- Field service or delivery operations requiring consolidated tracking and event reporting
