# ThingSys - TS-V7

![TS-V7](./Logo.jpg)

The TS-V7 Professional Vehicle GPS Tracker from ThingSys is a versatile and reliable tracking device that offers a wide range of features to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle. With support for 2G/3G/4G networks, this tracker provides accurate positioning and tracking information through an online platform or your smartphone. You have the flexibility to choose the positioning platform that suits your needs best.

In case of emergencies, the TS-V7 has a built-in emergency alarm that can be triggered with the push of a button. This alarm will send alerts to authorized numbers, ensuring that help can be dispatched quickly. Additionally, you can remotely control the fuel and circuit of your vehicle using the unit, allowing you to manage your vehicle's power and fuel consumption efficiently.

One of the standout features of the TS-V7 is its remote monitoring capability. By calling the phone number associated with the unit, you can listen to the sound within a 5-meter radius of the vehicle. This feature provides an extra layer of security and allows you to monitor the vehicle's surroundings in real-time.

The TS-V7 also offers various alert functions, including movement alerts and overspeed alerts. You can set up a geo-fence to restrict the vehicle's movements within a specific area, and the unit will send alerts if the vehicle goes beyond the designated boundaries. Additionally, the tracker supports mileage statistics, ignition detection, power cutoff, and alarm functions, making it a comprehensive solution for vehicle tracking and management.

With its wide range of features and compatibility with additional accessories such as LED advertisement screens, fuel sensors, temperature sensors, and RFID card readers, the TS-V7 Professional Vehicle GPS Tracker is a reliable and versatile choice for fleet management, personal vehicle tracking, and security applications.
