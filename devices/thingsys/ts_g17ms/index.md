# ThingSys - TS-G17Ms

<div className="device-logo">
![TS-G17Ms](./tracker.png)
</div>

The TS-G17Ms from ThingSys is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for dependable real-time tracking and practical vehicle management. Designed for motorcycles, cars and light commercial vehicles, the unit pairs a high sensitivity GPS chipset with flexible reporting modes so fleet managers and vehicle owners can monitor location, alarms and basic telemetry through the Plaspy platform.

As a device that supports SOS alerts, vibration and overspeed alarms, ACC ignition detection and remote fuel or power cut via an included relay, the TS-G17Ms fits naturally into Plaspy workflows for monitoring security and operations. Its discreet form factor, broad vehicle voltage support and low power modes make it a sensible choice where anti theft protection, route monitoring and fast recovery are priorities when integrated into Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Confirmed compatibility with Plaspy for centralized real time tracking and fleet oversight.
- High sensitivity GPS chipset that helps with fast satellite acquisition and reliable fixes in challenging environments.
- Supports both continuous GPRS trace tracking and SMS based polling for flexible reporting.
- Built in security features including SOS alert, vibration and overspeed alarms and ACC ignition detection.
- Remote immobilizer function via included relay to enable fuel or power cut for anti theft response.
- Wide operating voltage range and low power sleep modes suitable for a variety of vehicle installations.
- Compact and discreet form factor suitable for motorcycles cars and commercial vehicles.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once connected to Plaspy the TS-G17Ms sends location and event data to the platform so operators can view vehicle positions, alarms and key status updates in near real time. Plaspy displays incoming traces and alarm events on dashboards and can route notifications and reports to operational teams according to configured rules.

- Real time location updates through GPRS trace tracking and ad hoc status queries via SMS polling.
- Alarm visibility for SOS events overspeed and vibration alerts on Plaspy dashboards with notification options.
- ACC ignition status provided to Plaspy for start stop analytics and driver behavior insights.
- Remote immobilizer actions using the device relay can be coordinated through Plaspy incident workflows.
- Device events can trigger geofence alerts scheduled reports and actionable notifications for fleet teams.
