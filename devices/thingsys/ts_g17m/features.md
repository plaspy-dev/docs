# ThingSys - TS-G17M Features

This page covers the public feature context for using the ThingSys TS-G17M tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the observable capabilities and practical functions you can expect when integrating the device into the Plaspy fleet and asset management platform, including hybrid positioning, alarm types, relay control and the general telemetry that flows into Plaspy for monitoring and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration steps, the latest firmware behavior and detailed technical specifications consult the official ThingSys documentation and release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The TS-G17M is a compact tracker designed for motorcycles and small vehicles where space and discreet installation matter. It focuses on reliable real time tracking over cellular networks, a set of vehicle oriented alarms and remote control options that are useful for anti theft and operational monitoring in a Plaspy managed environment.

- Real time tracking using GSM GPRS with hybrid positioning options for improved coverage in varied environments.
- Remote relay control for engine or fuel cut off to support immobilizer style interventions.
- Vehicle telemetry including ACC ignition status, low battery and power off alerts for proactive fleet operations.
- Multiple alarm modes including SOS panic button, vibration and tilt detection plus a built in microphone for voice monitoring.
- Long term route playback capability for investigations and compliance with up to 365 days of history.
- Compact form factor suited to motorcycle and small vehicle installations where discreet mounting is preferred.

## Core Features of ThingSys - TS-G17M

- GSM GPRS connectivity for continuous tracking and telemetry reporting.
- Hybrid positioning that combines GPS with assisted methods such as LBS and AGPS for improved location availability.
- Remote relay control enabling engine or fuel cut off for immobilization.
- ACC detection to report ignition on and off events for trip and usage visibility.
- SOS panic button for immediate alerting to designated responders or platforms.
- Built in microphone to support remote voice monitoring during incidents.
- Vibration and tilt alarms for tamper and movement detection.
- Power off and low battery alerts along with long duration historical route playback.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TS-G17M is paired with Plaspy the device's location and telemetry are presented in a single operational view so teams can monitor assets, receive alarms and act on remote commands. Plaspy ingests position updates and event messages, turning them into map markers, timeline entries and alert notifications for operators.

- Live location and status updates displayed on Plaspy maps and fleet dashboards.
- Alarm events such as SOS, vibration, tilt, power off and low battery delivered to Plaspy alert streams.
- Ignition and ACC state changes reflected in trip summaries and start stop reports inside Plaspy.
- Remote relay commands initiated from Plaspy are sent back to the device to perform immobilizer actions when supported.
- Historical route playback from the device appears in Plaspy timelines for investigation and compliance reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle and light vehicle courier fleets that need compact trackers with ignition reporting and remote control.
- Anti theft installations where vibration detection, tilt alarms and remote relay immobilization aid recovery.
- Driver safety and incident response using SOS alerts and voice monitoring to assess emergencies.
- Preventive telemetry monitoring for low battery and power loss to reduce unexpected downtime.
- Compliance and evidence logging using long duration route history for audits and investigations.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Regional variants or cellular band support may influence connectivity performance in certain markets.
