# ThingSys - YTWL_CA100F

![YTWL_CA100F](./Logo.jpg)

The YTWL\_CA100F Speed Limiter for Ethiopia is an intelligent terminal equipment designed to provide precise speed limiting for vehicles. It is not only a speed limiter but also a professional multifunctional vehicle GPS tracker. This product features an ARM intelligent chip, ensuring high speed control precision and reliable performance. It does not affect the vehicle's throttle start or power torque, providing stable and reliable speed limiting without stalling the vehicle. The YTWL\_CA100F allows for free movement of the throttle pedal within the specified speed range.

In addition to its speed limiting capabilities, the YTWL\_CA100F also offers a wealth of interfaces, allowing it to work seamlessly with various external equipment. It boasts high compatibility and performance, making it a versatile and reliable choice for vehicle tracking and speed control.
