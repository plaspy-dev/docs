# ThingSys - TS-V1

![TS-V1](./tracker.jpg)

The TS-V1 is a compact, hardwired GPS tracker designed for professional vehicle tracking and fleet management. Built for installation in commercial vehicles, construction machines, rental cars, and private vehicles, the TS-V1 provides real time location reporting, geo fencing, mileage statistics, and remote control features such as fuel or power cut off and SOS alarm signaling. Its rugged design, broad operating voltage range, and removable backup battery help keep units online during demanding operations.

This tracker is listed as Plaspy compatible out of the box, making it straightforward to add the TS-V1 to a Plaspy deployment for centralized monitoring. The device supports platform based command and control and can be pointed to a fleet platform for consistent upload of position and event data, so Plaspy can handle visualization, alerts, geo fence management, and historical reporting for your vehicles.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into centralized fleet dashboards and alerting workflows.
- Real time tracking with geo fencing and historical playback for operational visibility and incident review.
- Remote control and anti theft features including SOS alarm and remote fuel or power cut off outputs.
- Fleet oriented telemetry such as mileage reporting and ignition status monitoring to support reporting and operations.
- Rugged design with wide operating voltage range and removable backup battery for continued service in harsh conditions.
- Flexible inputs and optional sensor compatibility to extend telemetry where needed.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TS-V1 uploads position and event data to the platform so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in a single interface. Plaspy receives location updates and device events, applies geo fence rules and alerting, and stores historical records for reporting and analysis.

- Live location updates and history playback on Plaspy maps for dispatcher and manager visibility.
- Event and status monitoring such as ignition or engine on/off events and mileage calculations for operational reporting.
- Fuel level and analog input monitoring forwarded to Plaspy to support consumption reports and alerts.
- Remote immobilization and control outputs accessible via Plaspy or device commands for theft response and remote interventions.
- SOS alerts, movement alerts, over speed and geo fence breaches forwarded to Plaspy so operators receive timely notifications.
- Centralized device configuration and event routing through Plaspy to standardize fleet workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking for route oversight, mileage tracking, and driver behavior monitoring.
- Anti theft response and recovery with SOS alerts and remote cut off for immobilization when necessary.
- Construction and heavy equipment monitoring where rugged operation and wide voltage support are needed.
- Rental and shared vehicle management using geo fences and movement alerts to enforce usage policies.
- Remote telemetry and vehicle health reporting to consolidate telemetry into Plaspy reports and dashboards.
