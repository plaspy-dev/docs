# ThingSys - TS-P4D

![TS-P4D](./logo..png)

The ThingSys TS-P4D is a compact magnetic GPS tracker designed for extended deployments. It supports both 3G and 4G networks and ships with large removable battery options up to 8000 mAh, making it suitable for prolonged tracking of vehicles, assets, and containers. The unit uses a Ublox 7 GPS receiver and offers high sensitivity and practical position accuracy, together with features such as geofencing, SOS alerts, remote voice monitoring, low battery warnings, and movement detection via a 3D accelerator.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TS-P4D can feed reliable location and alert data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its long battery life and small form factor make it a practical choice for deployments where regular power access is limited and discreet placement is required. Organizations using Plaspy can add the TS-P4D to their device roster to extend coverage for rental fleets, asset pools, and container operations without sacrificing visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Long run time with large removable battery options for extended field use
- Compact magnetic housing and lightweight design for discreet placement
- High sensitivity GPS receiver for reliable position reporting and quick reacquisition
- Support for 3G and 4G networks for timely transmission of location and alerts
- Built in geofence, SOS hijack alarms, and low battery notifications for operational safety
- Remote voice monitoring and movement detection to enhance situational awareness
- Designed to operate across a wide temperature range for outdoor and transport use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TS-P4D provides location and event data that Plaspy ingests, displays, and archives so teams can monitor assets and respond to alerts from a single platform. Plaspy can be used to organize TS-P4D units within fleets, set up alerting rules, and review historical movements for operational analysis.

- Real time location visualization and map tracking within the Plaspy interface
- Geofence management with entry and exit alerts routed through Plaspy notifications
- SOS and hijack alarm events forwarded to operators for immediate attention
- Low battery and status alerts visible in Plaspy to support device maintenance
- Historical playback and reporting for route review and compliance checks
- Aggregated device lists and grouping for fleet level oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle rental units that need discreet and long duration tracking
- Fleet management for light commercial vehicles and mobile assets
- Asset tracking for equipment and containers in transit or storage
- Temporary deployments where extended battery life reduces maintenance visits
- Remote equipment monitoring when wired power is unavailable
