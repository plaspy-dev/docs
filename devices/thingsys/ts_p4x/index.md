# ThingSys - TS-P4X

![TS-P4X](./tracker.jpg)

The TS-P4X is a heavy duty magnetic GPS tracker designed for long term deployments on cargo containers, trailers and other long haul transportation assets. Built for harsh outdoor conditions, the device combines a high capacity 15,000 mAh rechargeable backup battery with strong integrated magnets and multi generation cellular options to deliver reliable location tracking and extended field operation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TS-P4X can report positional fixes, alarm events and stored records into the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility and fleet oversight. Its blind area buffering, configurable geofences and remote firmware management make it a practical choice when you need remote monitoring and continuity of tracking data within Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward ingestion into fleet dashboards and reporting workflows
- Long duration operation from a 15,000 mAh rechargeable backup battery suitable for unpowered assets
- Durable magnetic mounting and compact form factor for secure attachment to container exteriors or chassis
- Multi generation cellular support with GPRS data transmission for consistent low bandwidth telemetry
- Large blind area buffer that retains up to 20,000 records for continuity during connectivity gaps
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware upgrade capability to reduce on site maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TS-P4X transmits location updates and device telemetry to cloud endpoints accepted by Plaspy, where those streams are ingested and surfaced for live monitoring, alerting and historical playback. Plaspy uses the device events and buffered records to preserve continuity and to trigger operational responses when conditions require attention.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and history playback
- Alarm and status events, such as lock state changes and tamper alerts, transmitted as discrete events for anti theft workflows
- Geofence support with configurable perimeters that generate entry and exit alerts inside Plaspy
- Blind area data forwarding so stored records are uploaded when connectivity is restored to maintain history
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware upgrades to keep devices current without physical intervention
- Integration with broader telemetry pipelines so Plaspy can correlate TS-P4X location streams with other operational inputs when those signals are available

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term container monitoring across intermodal movements where external power is unavailable
- Trailer and cargo anti theft protection using tamper and lock state reporting plus access control
- Fleet management for non powered assets where blind area buffering preserves location history
- Logistics visibility for long haul deployments that require rugged mounting and extended battery life
- Operational telemetry aggregation to support centralized reporting and incident investigation

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TS-P4X is purpose built for heavy duty, long duration tracking where durability, battery capacity and secure mounting are priorities. Its combination of large blind area storage, configurable geofences and remote management makes it easier to deploy and maintain large fleets of unmanned assets when paired with a platform like Plaspy.
