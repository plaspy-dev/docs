# ThingSys - TS-G17W

![TS-G17W](./tracker.jpg)

The TS-G17W is a compact, IP67 waterproof 4G vehicle GPS tracker engineered for reliable, real-time tracking in demanding environments. Designed for worldwide deployment with 4G LTE‑FDD and 2G GSM fallback \(banding dependent on country\), the TS-G17W delivers stable connectivity for fleet management, anti-theft protection and remote telemetry. Its small form factor and rugged housing make it ideal for discreet installation on cars, trucks, buses and other assets where durability and weather protection are essential.

The TS-G17W is Plaspy compatible and integrates smoothly into Plaspy-based monitoring workflows for live tracking, alerts and remote commands. With a high-sensitivity 7670SA GNSS chipset \(chipset may vary by band\), typical positioning accuracy of approximately 5 meters and excellent weak‑signal performance, the device supports precise location reporting, fast TTFF and continuous reporting during brief power interruptions thanks to a built-in 3.7 V Li‑ion backup battery.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for seamless real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- 4G LTE‑FDD with 2G GSM fallback \(banding depends on region\) for worldwide connectivity.
- High-sensitivity GNSS \(7670SA, depending on band\) with ~5 m positioning accuracy and -159 dBm sensitivity.
- Vehicle-oriented I/O: ACC ignition detection and optional external relay for remote cut-off \(immobilizer‑style control\).
- IP67 waterproof rating and wide voltage tolerance \(9–80 V\) — built for harsh environments and varied vehicle electrical systems.
- Low energy consumption and fast TTFF for quick lock times: cold 35–80 s, warm ~35 s, hot ~1 s.
- Multiple alert types \(over-speed, vibration, configurable rules\) plus SMS polling and GPRS trace modes for flexible telemetry.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TS-G17W becomes a turnkey data source for real-time location, alarms and basic vehicle telemetry. Plaspy ingests the device’s GPRS or SMS reports and presents them as live location points, alert notifications and historical tracks. Integration is straightforward: the tracker’s server protocol and trace modes align with common Plaspy ingestion methods, enabling rapid onboarding of vehicles into dashboards, geofences, and automated reporting.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates pushed to Plaspy via GPRS \(or polled via SMS where data coverage is limited\).
- Ignition \(ACC\) detection and alarm events \(over-speed, vibration\) reported to Plaspy for automated rules and alerts.
- Telemetry support suitable for fleet management dashboards and workflows such as route replay and basic sensor data.
- Remote immobilizer capability via optional external relay \(cut-off commands routed through Plaspy for anti-theft recovery\).
- Plaspy can correlate TS-G17W data with additional sensor feeds \(fuel monitoring systems, Bluetooth sensors/beacons managed by the platform\) to provide a unified view of vehicle health and cargo conditions.

- Fleet management — live vehicle tracking, route monitoring and driver event alerts through Plaspy dashboards.
- Vehicle anti-theft and recovery — remote cut-off via optional relay and alarm notifications for rapid response.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the TS-G17W for Plaspy deployments offers a balance of rugged reliability and vehicle-centric features that matter for fleet management and anti-theft use cases. Its 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback, high-sensitivity GNSS and fast TTFF ensure consistent, real-time tracking even in challenging signal conditions. The IP67 housing and broad voltage input make it suitable for diverse vehicle fleets and exposed assets, while the built‑in backup battery and low power design protect continuity of reporting during short power interruptions.

For Plaspy users, the TS-G17W provides dependable location and alarm events \(ignition, over-speed, vibration\) that feed directly into Plaspy’s rules engine, geofencing and reporting. The optional external relay for remote cut-off offers an immobilizer‑style response for theft mitigation, and SMS polling/GPRS trace modes give flexible telemetry options where coverage varies. Together, the TS-G17W and Plaspy deliver a practical, scalable solution for operators who need reliable real-time tracking, telemetry integration, and straightforward fleet management without sacrificing ruggedness or ease of installation.
