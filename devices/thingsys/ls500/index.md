# ThingSys - LS500

![LS500](./Logo.jpg)

The LS500 GPS Speed Limiter from ThingSys is a versatile and intelligent terminal equipment designed to provide precise speed limiting for vehicles. It not only functions as a speed limiter but also as a professional multifunctional vehicle GPS tracker. With its high-speed control precision and reliable performance, the LS500 ensures that the vehicle's throttle pedal remains free to move within the right speed range, without stalling or affecting the power torque during speed limiting.

This GPS speed limiter adopts an ARM intelligent chip and features a wealth of interfaces, allowing it to work seamlessly with various external equipment. Its high compatibility and performance make it a versatile solution for different vehicle tracking and speed control needs. The LS500 is equipped with a U-blox GPS chip and a GSM/GPRS module \(SIM800C\), ensuring accurate positioning and reliable communication over the GSM/GPRS/GPS network. It also features a Bluetooth module \(BT360I\) for additional connectivity options.

With its compact dimensions of 117\*92\*31mm and a weight of 280g, the LS500 is easy to install and integrate into different vehicle types. It operates within a wide voltage range of DC 9V-36V and is powered by a rechargeable 3.7V/1000mAh Li-ion battery \(customizable\). The device is designed to withstand harsh conditions, with an operating temperature range of -20°C to +70°C and a storage temperature range of -40°C to +85°C. Its strengthened anti-jamming capabilities ensure reliable performance even in challenging environments.

Overall, the LS500 GPS Speed Limiter from ThingSys is a reliable and versatile solution for precise speed limiting and vehicle tracking. Its high-speed control precision, compatibility with external equipment, and robust design make it suitable for various applications in Ethiopia and beyond.
