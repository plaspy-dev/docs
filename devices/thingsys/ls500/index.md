# ThingSys - LS500

![LS500](./Logo.jpg)

The ThingSys LS500 is a GPS Speed Limiter and multifunctional vehicle tracking terminal designed for precise speed control and continuous location monitoring. Built around an ARM intelligent chip and equipped with a U-blox GPS receiver, GSM GPRS communication, and a Bluetooth module, the LS500 combines speed limiting functionality with tracking features in a compact package. Its design emphasizes control precision and operational reliability while allowing the vehicle throttle to operate within the permitted speed range without compromising power torque.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LS500 can feed vehicle location and speed related information into Plaspy for centralized fleet oversight. Organizations that need both enforced speed limits and position visibility can use the LS500 together with Plaspy to consolidate tracking, monitoring, alerts, and reporting into a single platform. The device's compatibility and rugged specifications make it a suitable option for fleet operators seeking integrated speed control and tracking solutions.

## Key Highlights

- Dual purpose device combining speed limiting and GPS tracking in a single terminal
- High speed control precision enabled by an ARM based control chip
- U-blox GPS receiver for accurate positioning and reliable location data
- GSM GPRS communication module for ongoing data transmission
- Built in Bluetooth for supplementary connectivity options
- Compact and robust design with wide operating voltage and temperature ranges
- Enhanced anti jamming capability for dependable performance in challenging environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the LS500 supplies location and speed related data that Plaspy uses to present maps, alerts, and operational reports. Plaspy ingests the device data to give fleet managers real time visibility and historical insights without requiring changes to the vehicle behavior beyond the LS500s native speed limiting function.

- Real time location tracking on Plaspy maps for individual vehicles and fleets
- Speed and movement visibility to support compliance monitoring and alerts
- Event reporting and historical playback for route analysis and incident review
- Centralized fleet dashboards combining LS500 data with other assets managed in Plaspy
- Configurable notifications to highlight speed limit events or deviations

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring delivery and logistics vehicles for route and speed compliance
- Managing municipal or regional transport where controlled speeds are required
