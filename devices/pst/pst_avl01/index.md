# PST - PST-AVL01

![PST-AVL01](./logo.jpg)

The PST-AVL01 is a GPS GSM GPRS tracker designed for real time vehicle tracking and fleet management. Built around high sensitivity GPS and reliable GPRS connectivity, it delivers continuous location updates and stable performance suitable for single vehicles or larger fleets. The device is described as compact and durable, with features commonly used in vehicle tracking such as geo fencing, remote immobilization, and SOS alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PST-AVL01 can feed its location and alert data into the Plaspy platform to provide consolidated visibility across your fleet. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s real time position updates and event notifications can be used in dashboards, map views, and routine reporting to support operational oversight, security monitoring, and fleet optimization.

## Key Highlights

- Real time GPS tracking for up to date vehicle location information
- GSM and GPRS connectivity for continuous data transmission
- Superior GPS and GPRS modules aimed at good sensitivity and stable performance
- Compact and durable form factor suitable for everyday vehicle use
- Built in features such as geo fencing to detect area entry and exit
- Remote immobilization and SOS alert capability for security and emergency response

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event data from compatible trackers like the PST-AVL01 and presents that information through fleet dashboards, maps, and alert workflows. Integration focuses on delivering clear visibility into vehicle positions and important events so fleet managers can act on timely information.

- Live vehicle location displayed on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness
- Geofence events routed to Plaspy for automated alerts and entry exit reporting
- SOS alerts surfaced within Plaspy so operators can respond to emergency signals
- Historical tracking and route playback available for reporting and post event review
- Fleet level monitoring and status views to help prioritize dispatch and maintenance
- Where remote commands are supported by the tracker and deployment, immobilization actions can be coordinated through Plaspy interfaces

## Typical Use Cases

- Day to day fleet tracking and centralized operational oversight
- Route monitoring and historical route analysis for deliveries and service vehicles
- Security and recovery workflows using SOS alerts and immobilization features
- Rental or leased vehicle monitoring for improved asset control
- Field service vehicle coordination and time on site tracking for operational efficiency

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PST-AVL01 combines practical tracking functions with a compact design, making it a sensible choice for organizations that need reliable position updates and standard security features. When used with Plaspy, the tracker’s data can be consolidated into a single platform for simplified monitoring, alerting, and reporting across an entire fleet.

For organizations seeking straightforward vehicle visibility and core fleet controls, the PST-AVL01 paired with Plaspy offers a balanced solution that emphasizes dependable tracking and usable event features without unnecessary complexity.
