# PST - PST-AVL01

![PST-AVL01](./logo.jpg)

The PST-AVL01 is a cutting-edge GPS/GSM/GPRS tracker designed specifically for real-time vehicle tracking and fleet management. With superior GPS and GPRS modules, this tracker offers excellent sensitivity and stable performance, ensuring accurate and reliable tracking data.

Whether you need to monitor a single vehicle or an entire fleet, the PST-AVL01 is the perfect solution. It provides real-time tracking information, allowing you to keep a close eye on your vehicles' whereabouts at all times. This can be invaluable for businesses looking to optimize their operations, improve efficiency, and enhance overall fleet management.

With its compact and durable design, the PST-AVL01 is built to withstand the rigors of daily use in a vehicle. It is easy to install and can be discreetly placed in a variety of locations within the vehicle. Additionally, it offers a range of advanced features, including geo-fencing, remote immobilization, and SOS alerts, providing added security and peace of mind.

Key Features:

- Real-time GPS tracking for accurate and up-to-date location information
- GSM/GPRS connectivity for seamless communication
- Superior GPS and GPRS modules for excellent sensitivity and stable performance
- Compact and durable design for easy installation and long-lasting use
- Advanced features such as geo-fencing, remote immobilization, and SOS alerts
