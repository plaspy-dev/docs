# PST - AVL-011

![AVL-011](./logo.jpg)

The PST AVL-011 is a powerful GPS tracker designed to provide advanced anti-theft features for your car, vehicle, or truck. With its cutting-edge technology, this tracker allows you to remotely cut off the power supply or fuel of your vehicle if necessary, giving you an extra layer of security and control. This feature can be extremely useful in case of theft or unauthorized use of your vehicle, allowing you to immobilize it and prevent further damage or loss.

One of the standout features of the AVL-011 is its ability to remain anonymous when installed in a secret place. This means that even if someone gains access to your vehicle, they won't be able to detect the presence of the tracker, ensuring that it remains safe and hidden. This stealthy design adds an extra level of security, giving you peace of mind knowing that your vehicle is protected without drawing attention to itself.

In addition to its anti-theft capabilities, the AVL-011 also offers SOS emergency functionality. In case of an emergency, you can activate the SOS feature, which will allow you to monitor the voice around your car, vehicle, or truck. This can be crucial in situations where immediate assistance is needed, as you can listen in and assess the situation without alerting others that the conversation is being monitored.

Overall, the PST AVL-011 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that provides advanced anti-theft measures for your vehicle. With its ability to remotely cut off power or fuel, remain anonymous when installed, and offer SOS emergency functionality, this tracker offers a comprehensive solution to protect your valuable assets.
