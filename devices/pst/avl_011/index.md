# PST - AVL-011

<div className="device-logo">
![AVL-011](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The PST AVL-011 is a vehicle GPS tracker designed to provide advanced anti theft protection for cars, vans, and trucks. It combines location tracking with remote immobilization capabilities, allowing operators to interrupt a vehicle's power or fuel supply when needed. The device also offers SOS emergency functionality and can be installed in a discreet location to remain hidden from casual inspection.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AVL-011 can be used alongside Plaspy's fleet and asset monitoring platform to bring anti theft features into a centralized tracking workflow. Plaspy users can view location information, receive alerts, and incorporate the AVL-011's emergency and immobilization features into broader operational oversight without relying on product specifics beyond what the tracker provides.

## Key Highlights

- Remote immobilization capability to cut off power or fuel for vehicle security and theft response.
- Stealthy installation design that can be placed discreetly for covert monitoring.
- SOS emergency function with the ability to monitor ambient audio around the vehicle.
- Designed for use in cars, trucks, and similar vehicles where anti theft measures are required.
- Complementary to fleet monitoring workflows when paired with a compatible tracking platform.
- Practical value for operators who need quick intervention and remote control in loss events.
