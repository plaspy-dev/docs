# PST

PST is a manufacturer whose range of GPS tracking devices is used across fleet management, asset tracking, and mobile workforce applications. This page explains how PST devices can integrate with Plaspy, what to expect from typical PST hardware, and how to choose the right device for your monitoring needs. Use the device catalog below to view the PST models compatible with Plaspy and jump to device specific documentation when needed.

## How PST devices work with Plaspy

PST devices that are compatible with Plaspy can send location and telemetry to the Plaspy platform after basic configuration. In most deployments you will configure the tracker with the platform server address and credentials provided by Plaspy or your integration guide. Once connected, Plaspy receives position fixes, status messages, and alerts from the device so you can visualize and manage devices in a single interface.

For device specific setup instructions and recommended settings, open the model details in the catalog above or consult the PST hardware documentation and your Plaspy onboarding resources.

## Common features found in PST devices

PST devices generally provide the standard feature set expected in modern GPS trackers. Common features include:
- Real time GPS location reporting and position history
- Movement detection and status reporting
- Battery and power source monitoring for portable and hardwired units
- Inputs and outputs for external sensors, ignition detection, or relay control
- Alarm functions such as SOS, geofence and tamper alerts
- Configurable reporting intervals and low power modes for longer battery life

The exact feature set varies by model. Refer to the catalog to compare devices and to the device pages for detailed capabilities.

## Typical use cases for PST GPS trackers

PST devices are used across a variety of scenarios including:
- Fleet tracking and route visibility for light and heavy vehicles
- Asset monitoring for trailers, containers, and portable equipment
- Rental and shared vehicle tracking for utilization and theft prevention
- Field workforce location and safety monitoring
- Long term location logging for compliance and analytics

Plaspy lets you centralize these use cases in one platform to combine mapping, alerts, reporting, and integrations.

## Help finding the right PST device

Choosing the right PST device depends on use case and installation:
- Determine power and mounting needs: hardwired unit for vehicles or battery powered for assets
- Check connectivity and reporting requirements: reporting frequency and network coverage
- Evaluate required inputs and outputs for sensors, ignition, or immobilization
- Consider enclosure and environmental ratings if devices will be exposed to weather or vibration

Use the catalog above to review available PST devices. For hands on guidance, consult your PST vendor or contact Plaspy for recommendations that match your deployment.
