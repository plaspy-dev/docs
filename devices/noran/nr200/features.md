# Noran - NR200 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Noran NR200 Magnetic Wireless GPS Tracker and explains how the device integrates with Plaspy for fleet and asset tracking. It summarizes practical capabilities you can expect when using the NR200 with Plaspy for real time monitoring, route history, and alarm delivery across web and mobile interfaces.

Exact feature availability may vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, regional cellular configuration, and how the manufacturer implements specific functions. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware behavior please consult the official Noran documentation.

## Feature Overview

The NR200 is a compact, battery powered, magnetic GPS tracker designed for non invasive mounting and long standby operation. Paired with Plaspy, it is intended for scenarios that require discreet long term tracking, recovery support, and remote asset oversight without permanent wiring.

- Magnetic, fully wireless installation for covert attachment to metal surfaces
- Long battery life from a 5000 mAh battery with configurable sleep modes for up to 30 days standby
- Real time tracking and route history with playback and mileage statistics
- Double positioning using GPS plus A GPS LBS to improve location reliability in weak signal areas
- Alerts and telemetry support including geo fence alarms, overspeed alerts, and history playback
- Audio monitoring via remote call in to help with situational awareness during recovery operations

## Core Features of Noran - NR200

- Compact magnetic housing for quick, tool free installation on vehicles and assets
- 5000 mAh rechargeable battery with configurable sleep modes for extended deployments
- Real time location reporting with route history and up to 90 days playback as supported by the device and platform
- Double positioning using GPS combined with A GPS and cellular LBS for better fixes in urban or marginal environments
- Alerts for geo fence events, overspeed, and mileage statistics for operational oversight
- Communication over standard TCP UDP and HTTP protocols with SMS support for alternate reporting
- Audio monitoring via the device remote call feature for on demand listening during incidents
- Form factor and weight suitable for trailers, containers, equipment, and covert vehicle placement

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the NR200 is used with Plaspy, the device transmits position and telemetry that Plaspy ingests to present unified tracking and alerting alongside other fleet assets. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and surfaces location, history, and alarm data in dashboards and notification workflows.

- Real time location updates and telemetry appear on Plaspy dashboards for live monitoring
- Geo fence and overspeed alerts generated by the NR200 are forwarded to Plaspy for operator notification and event handling
- Route history and mileage statistics are available inside Plaspy for operational review and playback
- Audio monitoring events from the device can be associated with Plaspy alerts and investigation workflows
- Plaspy can correlate NR200 position and telemetry with other fleet signals when those inputs exist in the overall telematics setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle recovery and anti theft monitoring where a magnetic non invasive installation reduces detection risk
- Tracking trailers and shipping containers on long journeys or in storage where external power is not available
- Equipment and asset oversight on job sites for remote location monitoring and route history review
- Temporary fleet deployments that require quick installation and Plaspy compatible real time tracking
- Situational monitoring and investigations using route playback and audio monitoring to reconstruct events
- Short term or rental vehicle tracking where non permanent mounting is preferred

## Feature Availability Notes

- Battery life depends on reporting frequency, configured sleep modes, and cellular signal conditions in the deployment area
- Firmware versions and hardware revisions can change exact behavior for alerts, reporting intervals, and audio features
- Regional cellular band support and carrier SIM configuration may affect connectivity and reporting performance
- History retention and export capabilities can depend on both device settings and the Plaspy platform configuration
- Always verify specific feature availability and regulatory considerations for audio monitoring in your region with the manufacturer
