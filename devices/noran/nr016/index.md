# Noran - NR016

<div className="device-logo">
![NR016](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The NR016 is a car alarm GPS tracker designed for vehicle security and fleet telematics. From an established hardware family, it combines GPS satellite positioning with GSM GPRS communications and 3G support to provide continuous location updates, SOS alerts, remote immobilization and multi input telemetry. The unit targets cars, taxis, buses and commercial fleets that require practical anti theft protection alongside routine tracking functions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the NR016 feeds position, alarm events and sensor telemetry into centralized fleet management workflows. Plaspy can ingest the NR016 data streams to display live maps, trigger alerts, and produce historical reports, making the device a relevant choice for operators who want integrated monitoring and recovery tools without replacing an existing telematics platform.

## Key Highlights

- Vehicle focused tracking and anti theft functionality suited to cars and commercial fleets
- Real time location updates using GPS with mobile base station hybrid locating for consistent positions
- SOS alarm, configurable geo fencing and alarm event reporting for rapid notifications
- Remote immobilization via relay outputs to assist in theft recovery and fleet control
- Multi input I O for ignition and door trunk bonnet status plus relay control for peripherals
- Built in vibration detection and included accessories such as microphone and SOS button for voice monitoring and tamper alerts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected in a vehicle, the NR016 transmits location and event data over mobile networks to Plaspy. The platform receives GPS coordinates, hybrid locating information and digital or analog telemetry from the device, then consolidates those streams into dashboards, alerts and reports for operational oversight and security response.

- Live location and telemetry updates forwarded to Plaspy dashboards and maps
- Ignition, door, trunk and bonnet status events logged for trip and security analysis
- Remote cut off and relay commands available through platform controls for immobilization
- SOS alarms and voice monitoring alerts routed into Plaspy incident workflows for verification
- Vibration and low battery alarms sent to Plaspy to support tamper detection and maintenance planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for taxis, shuttle buses and delivery vehicles with continuous tracking and route history
- Vehicle anti theft systems that combine SOS alerts and remote immobilization to aid recovery
- Telematics and driver behavior monitoring using ignition and door event logs for compliance
- Remote monitoring of high value vehicles or assets with vibration detection and geo fencing
- Integrated emergency response where SOS and voice monitoring support rapid incident verification

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The NR016 offers a balance of anti theft controls and telematics inputs that make it a practical option for organizations using Plaspy. Its multi input I O and relay outputs allow common vehicle signals and control actions to be reported and managed from a single platform, and built in sensors help reduce false alarms and speed incident detection. Plaspy turns device signals into actionable visibility through configurable alerts, live maps and historical reporting, supporting faster recovery and clearer operational insight.
