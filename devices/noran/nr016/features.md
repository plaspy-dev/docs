# Noran - NR016 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Noran NR016 Car Alarm GPS Tracker and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for centralized vehicle tracking and security monitoring. It summarizes the practical, non-sensitive functionality that operators and installers commonly use when integrating the NR016 with Plaspy, including real-time location, alarm events, and multi-input telemetry.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, specific installation wiring, and manufacturer implementation. Some items listed here are described in manufacturer materials and installer guides; for the most current device details, consult official Noran documentation and the device packaging.

## Feature Overview

The NR016 is a vehicle-focused tracker designed for anti-theft protection and fleet telematics. It combines satellite positioning with mobile network locating to provide continuous tracking and alarm reporting suitable for cars, taxis, buses, and light commercial fleets.

- Real-time vehicle tracking using GPS plus mobile base-station hybrid locating for consistent position updates.
- Vehicle alarm and security events such as SOS alerts, overspeed alerts, and geo-fencing notifications.
- Remote immobilization and cut-off capability to support theft recovery and centralized fleet control.
- Multi-input telemetry for ignition (ACC), door, trunk and bonnet status reporting to monitor vehicle state.
- Vibration and shock detection for tamper alerts, plus optional voice monitoring for incident verification.
- Built-in backup battery and included accessories to support continued operation and streamlined installation.

## Core Features of Noran - NR016

- GPS satellite positioning combined with mobile base-station hybrid locating for resilient location fixes.
- GSM/GPRS and 3G cellular communications for reporting position and events to a tracking platform.
- SOS alarm and configurable event alerts for rapid notification of critical incidents.
- Remote immobilization and relay control to disable fuel or power circuits when required.
- Comprehensive I/O with multiple digital and analog inputs for ACC, door, trunk and bonnet monitoring.
- Vibration and shock detection to detect tampering or unauthorized movement.
- Integrated microphone and support for remote voice monitoring; accessories such as SOS button and relay are commonly included.
- Built-in backup battery to maintain basic reporting if main vehicle power is removed.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the NR016 feeds location and event data into centralized dashboards and alerting systems so teams can monitor fleets and respond to security incidents.

- Live position updates and hybrid locating data appear on Plaspy maps and tracking views for continuous visibility.
- Alarm events such as SOS, vibration, overspeed, and low battery are forwarded into Plaspy alert streams for timely notification.
- Digital and analog telemetry (ignition, door, trunk, bonnet status) is recorded to support trip logging and security event history.
- Remote immobilization and relay-based cut-off can be managed as platform-level events or recorded in reports when device and installation support remote commands.
- Voice monitoring or listen-in events and SOS triggers can be associated with specific incidents inside Plaspy for verification and response workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for taxis, shuttles, buses, and delivery vehicles with live location and history.
- Vehicle anti-theft monitoring with SOS alerts and remote immobilization for recovery assistance.
- Operational oversight of ignition cycles and door/trunk activity to improve safety and compliance.
- Protection of high-value vehicles and assets using vibration detection and geo-fencing.
- Incident verification and emergency response using SOS button alerts and voice monitoring.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Functionality depends on installed firmware and hardware revision; some features may be optional or require specific accessory wiring.
- Installation choices affect what telemetry is available; ACC, door, trunk, and bonnet reporting require correct input wiring at install.
- Regional cellular band support and 3G availability vary by market and service provider.
- Some accessories such as external vibration sensors, SOS buttons, or relays may be supplied in certain bundles and optional in others.
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols, but configuration and verification in the platform are recommended after installation.
