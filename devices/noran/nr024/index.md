# Noran - NR024

![NR024](./logo.jpg)

The Noran NR024 is an advanced GPS fleet tracker designed for vehicles. With its powerful features and capabilities, it is an ideal solution for fleet management and vehicle tracking. One of the standout features of the NR024 is its ability to upload images and fuel curve to a GIS real-time vehicle tracking system. This allows for a more comprehensive and detailed monitoring of the vehicle's activities.

In addition to its GPS tracking capabilities, the NR024 also offers GSM \(GPRS\) wireless communication, enabling seamless communication between the tracker and the tracking system. It also comes equipped with a digital camera, allowing for the capture of images. This can be particularly useful for verifying the vehicle's location or capturing evidence in case of an incident.

The NR024 also offers fuel monitoring and audio monitoring functionalities, providing valuable insights into the vehicle's fuel consumption and allowing for remote audio monitoring. It also features engine remote control, allowing for remote control of the vehicle's engine. This can be useful for immobilizing the vehicle in case of theft or unauthorized use.

Other notable features of the NR024 include GPS tracking and navigation, A-GPS \(LBS\) for improved location accuracy, real-time upload of location information, geo-fencing, over-speed alert, SOS function, and vehicle status monitoring. With its wide range of features and capabilities, the Noran NR024 is a reliable and versatile GPS fleet tracker that can greatly enhance fleet management and vehicle tracking operations.

### Key Features:

- GPS Tracking & GPS Navigation
- GSM \(GPRS\) Wireless Communication
- Take Photo by digital camera
- Fuel Monitoring & Audio Monitoring
- Engine remote control
- Located by GPS & A-GPS \(LBS--Location Based Service\)
- Real-time upload location information via time/distance interval
- Geo-fence, Over-speed Alert & SOS function
- Vehicle Status Monitoring \(Position, ACC, Speed etc.\)

### Technical Specifications:

- GPS module: SiRF Star3
- GSM module: 850/900/1800/1900Mhz and 2100MHz\(3G\)
- Working Voltage: DC 6.3V-40V
- Working current: 60mA-150mA
- Working temperature: -40℃-85℃
- Humidity: 10%-90%
- Digital camera: infrared 150w pixel
- Backup battery: 400mA
- A/D Port: 1
- General I/O Port: 3
- RS232 Extended: 2
