# Noran - NR024

![NR024](./logo.jpg)

The Noran NR024 is an advanced vehicle GPS tracker designed for fleet use. It combines satellite based positioning with GSM wireless communication and an integrated digital camera to provide continuous location reporting together with image capture. The device also includes functional features such as fuel monitoring, audio monitoring, geo fencing, over speed alerts, SOS, and engine remote control, which together support a wide range of fleet management needs.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the NR024 can feed location, status, and media information into a centralized tracking platform for real time oversight. Its ability to upload images and fuel curve data to a GIS style tracking system makes it particularly useful for operators who need richer context in addition to position data. When used with Plaspy, the NR024 becomes part of a broader solution for visibility, reporting, and operational control.

## Key Highlights

- Vehicle oriented GPS tracker with built in digital camera for photo capture and evidence collection
- Supports wireless data upload so location and status can be reported to a remote tracking platform
- Fuel monitoring capability to help track consumption and operational costs
- Engine remote control feature for remote immobilization or control when supported by system policies
- Standard fleet alerts including geo fence triggers, over speed warnings, and SOS signaling
- Audio monitoring and vehicle status reporting to add operational context beyond pure location

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the NR024 streams location and event data into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in a single interface. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it alongside maps, alerts, and historical records.

- Real time vehicle positioning visible on Plaspy maps for active fleet monitoring
- Photo uploads appear in Plaspy to help verify incidents, deliveries, or location context
- Fuel monitoring data integrated into Plaspy reports to support cost analysis and efficiency tracking
- Alerts from the NR024 such as geo fence breaches, over speed events, and SOS are routed to Plaspy notification workflows
- Engine control events and status updates recorded in Plaspy for operational oversight and incident response

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet managers who need combined location and photo evidence for deliveries or incident review
- Operators tracking fuel usage across vehicles to identify inefficiencies or unexpected consumption
- Organizations requiring geo fence enforcement and route compliance monitoring
- Situations where remote engine control is desirable for theft response or unauthorized use prevention
- Services that need integrated audio or status monitoring alongside standard GPS tracking
