# NR108 GPS Tracker

The NR108 is a compact GPS tracker engineered for discreet vehicle installation and accurate fuel monitoring. Designed for motorcycles and other small vehicles, the NR108 combines internal GPS and GSM antennas in a small housing to deliver Plaspy compatible real-time tracking, reliable telemetry, and anti-theft functionality without bulky hardware.

The NR108 reports location via SMS and Internet, with LBS fallback when GPS reception is limited. Built-in power saving sleep mode and an internal backup battery ensure continued tracking and power-cut alerts. When paired with Plaspy, the NR108 delivers streamlined fleet management, theft prevention, and fuel monitoring capabilities for businesses that require compact, low-data, high-reliability tracking.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device: Integrates with Plaspy for dashboards, alerts, and reporting.
- Real-time tracking via SMS and Internet with LBS fallback for low-GPS areas.
- Fuel monitoring and fuel consumption reporting to support telemetry and cost control.
- Anti-theft features including overspeed alarm, geo-fence, SOS alarm, and remote engine cut-off \(immobilizer via relay\).
- Built-in backup battery and power-cut reporting to maintain visibility during power interruptions.
- Low GPRS data usage: compact 34-byte position packets and optional internal flash logging when GPRS is unavailable.
- Compact, discreet form factor ideal for motorcycles and concealed installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the NR108 supplies continuous location and status updates that feed Plaspy’s real-time tracking engine, alerting system, and reporting modules. The tracker’s compact telemetry packets and optional local logging are optimized to keep GPRS costs low while preserving historical position data for later upload. Plaspy can use the NR108’s alarms and fuel telemetry to trigger notifications, generate usage reports, and automate fleet workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy via Internet \(GPRS\) or SMS where configured.
- Fuel level and fuel consumption data for telemetry and fuel monitoring reports.
- Overspeed, geo-fence, and SOS alarms to create immediate Plaspy alerts and escalation rules.
- Remote engine cut-off control \(immobilizer\) through the included relay, usable from Plaspy’s control interface when configured and authorized.
- Power-cut detection and backup battery reporting so Plaspy shows continuity of tracking during power interruptions.
- Logged positions stored in internal flash memory and uploaded to Plaspy when GPRS service resumes.
