# NR028 Navigation GPS Tracker

The NR028 Navigation GPS Tracker is a vehicle-mounted telematics device designed for fleet management, vehicle security and in-cab navigation. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the NR028 combines GPS satellite positioning with a built-in digital camera and a touchscreen navigation display to deliver reliable real-time tracking, driver communication and multimedia navigation for operators who need a single integrated solution.

The unit supports real-time tracking via SMS and Internet with LBS fallback, two-way voice calling, text messaging and remote command functions such as engine cut-off and parameter configuration \(GPRS, IP, APN\). Its Windows CE 6.0 platform, Sirf Atlas 600 MHz processor and 4 GB of storage make it suitable for dispatch operations, telemetry-driven reporting and anti-theft workflows when paired with Plaspy for centralized monitoring and alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that provides live location and telemetry for fleet management and anti-theft protection.
- Integrated 4.3" or 7" TFT touchscreen and embedded digital camera for driver navigation, incident capture and dispatch communication.
- Real-time tracking via GPRS/Internet and SMS with LBS fallback to maintain location updates in variable coverage.
- Remote immobilizer support \(engine cut-off\) and listen-in/voice monitoring for enhanced vehicle security and incident response.
- Advanced alerts: overspeed, geo-fence, SOS emergency alarm and power cut-off with internal backup battery.
- Fuel monitoring and support for up to two external fuel sensors plus optional anti-theft fuel protection and temperature alarms.
- Compact telemetry packets \(34-byte\) and internal flash logging \(optional up to 5 days\) to reduce data usage and retain key events.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the NR028 sends compact, frequent telemetry that Plaspy ingests for live dashboards, historical playback and alerts. Plaspy receives GPS coordinates, alarm states and configured sensor data \(fuel, temperature, SOS\) and translates them into actionable events, scheduled reports and automated notifications for operations teams. The device's support for SMS and GPRS/IP ensures location and status reach Plaspy even when networks vary, while LBS fallback maintains basic positioning.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via GPRS/IP or SMS.
- Alarm and status events: overspeed, geo-fence breaches, SOS emergency, power cut-off and listen-in/voice monitoring indications.
- Fuel monitoring and up to two external fuel sensors reported to Plaspy for consumption and anti-theft alerts.
- Remote immobilizer/engine cut-off commands can be issued from Plaspy to the NR028 for anti-theft response.
- Driver communication and dispatch via two-way voice and text messaging surfaced in Plaspy as telematics events; Plaspy can also accept navigation and camera event metadata for incident records.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: live tracking, route dispatch, driver communication and telemetry reporting to improve utilization and service levels.
- Vehicle security & anti-theft: remote engine cut-off, SOS alarm, geo-fence and listen-in monitoring to respond quickly to theft or unauthorized movement.
- Fuel monitoring and diagnostics: external fuel sensors and telemetry to detect theft, measure consumption and optimize refueling schedules.
- Dispatch & driver navigation: in-cab touchscreen navigation and two-way voice/text for coordinated field operations and safer routing.
- Incident documentation: embedded camera and event logging for accident reconstruction and driver behavior coaching.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The NR028 is built for operators who need a consolidated in-vehicle solution that combines navigation, multimedia and robust telemetry. When paired with Plaspy, the NR028 becomes a powerful asset for real-time tracking, telemetry-driven decision-making and anti-theft response. Plaspy handles the incoming GPS tracker data—position, alarms, fuel levels and voice/text events—and presents them in centralized dashboards, automated alerts and historical reports that scale from single vehicles to large fleets.

Choose the NR028 with Plaspy integration when you require dependable real-time tracking, flexible remote immobilizer control and detailed telemetry for fuel monitoring and driver behavior. For environments where Bluetooth sensors are used, Plaspy can also integrate those sensor feeds at the platform level to supplement vehicle telemetry. Together, the NR028 and Plaspy provide a secure, low-data-cost approach to fleet optimization, anti-theft protection and operational visibility.
