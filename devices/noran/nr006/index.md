# Noran - NR006

<div className="device-logo">
![NR006](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The NR006 Mini GPS Tracker is an ultra compact tracking device designed for motorcycles and small vehicles. It provides discreet installation and consistent location reporting using built in GPS and GSM antennas, with LBS fallback for poor signal areas. The unit is described as lightweight at approximately 100 g and optimized for environments where space and concealment matter.

As supplied, the NR006 is Plaspy compatible out of the box and can deliver real time tracking data to a Plaspy account via SMS or Internet. Its low data usage design and concise position packets make it a practical choice for Plaspy users who need continuous visibility without high bandwidth consumption, and it supports common fleet and security workflows such as route history, alarms, and remote immobilization when integrated with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra compact, motorcycle friendly form factor for discreet mounting and theft deterrence
- Plaspy compatible for real time location, historical routes, and alerting via SMS or Internet
- Low data operation using concise 34 byte position packets to reduce GPRS usage and operating costs
- Built in GPS with LBS fallback to improve reporting in urban or low signal conditions
- Security and safety alarms including overspeed and geo fence notifications plus a power saving sleep mode
- Remote engine cut off capability using the supplied relay for immobilizer control
- Optional backup battery and internal flash memory for power cut alerts and offline logging

## How It Works with Plaspy

The NR006 sends compact position packets and event messages over SMS or Internet that Plaspy receives and processes. Once data arrives in Plaspy, it becomes available for live tracking, route playback, alerting, and reporting, enabling operational oversight for small vehicle fleets and security teams.

- Real time location updates and telemetry delivered to Plaspy via Internet or SMS for continuous visibility
- GPS positioning with LBS fallback helps maintain location reporting in challenging coverage areas
- Alarm and event notifications such as overspeed and geo fence breaches appear in Plaspy as configurable alerts
- Remote immobilizer support: Plaspy can send commands to trigger the supplied relay for vehicle cut off when configured
- Offline logging and optional backup battery permit short term data retention and power cut alerts until Plaspy receives stored records

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for motorcycle couriers and small vehicle fleets where space and weight are limited
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows with remote immobilization and immediate alerts through Plaspy
- Driver behavior oversight using overspeed alerts and route playback for coaching and safety improvements
- Maintenance planning and utilization reporting where compact telemetry supports preventative scheduling
- Low bandwidth deployments that benefit from small packet sizes and reduced data costs

The NR006 is a sensible choice when you need a compact, low profile tracker that integrates into an established fleet management platform. Its small form factor and low data reporting make it well suited to motorcycle installations and other tight mounting situations, while the built in fallback and optional offline logging help keep location data flowing into Plaspy even when connectivity is intermittent.
