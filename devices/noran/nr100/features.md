# Noran - NR100 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Noran NR100 and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for centralized fleet tracking and monitoring. It focuses on user facing features, operational benefits, and how the NR100 reports location and event data into Plaspy for visibility and alerts.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. Where appropriate this page notes practical caveats, and users should consult the official Noran documentation for device specific and current technical details.

## Feature Overview

The NR100 is a compact OBD II plug in GPS tracker designed for quick deployment and continuous tracking. It combines GPS positioning with LBS fallback, supports Internet and SMS reporting, and exposes event alerts and OBD telemetry that Plaspy can ingest for mapping, reporting, and alerting.

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for fast installation without vehicle disassembly
- Dual positioning using GPS plus LBS to improve coverage in urban or weak signal areas
- Configurable reporting intervals to balance update frequency and data usage
- Event alerts for overspeed, power cut, and geo fence events to support incident response
- Remote audio monitor and remote self failure analysis for security and device health insight

## Core Features of Noran - NR100

- OBD II plug in installation for quick connection to vehicle diagnostics and ignition signals
- Dual GPS and LBS positioning with a SiRF GPS receiver for consistent location updates
- Configurable reporting intervals by time or distance to suit fleet requirements
- Immediate alerts for overspeed, power cut, and geo fence events
- Support for SMS and Internet reporting channels to transmit telemetry and events
- Remote audio monitoring and remote self failure analysis for security and diagnostics
- Optional accessory support such as an external relay or other vendor accessories where supplied

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the NR100 is connected and configured to report to Plaspy, the device streams location and event data into the Plaspy platform for live tracking, historic playback, and automated workflows. Plaspy ingests telemetry and alerts, visualizes trips on maps, and exposes events for notifications and reporting.

- Real time location updates and fallback positioning are shown on Plaspy maps and live tracking widgets
- Overspeed, power cut, and geo fence notifications create immediate alerts and trigger configured workflows
- OBD II telemetry such as ignition state and supported vehicle PIDs can be reflected in Plaspy reports when available from the vehicle
- Remote audio monitor can be surfaced through Plaspy workflows where audio capture is permitted by local law
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and centralizes incoming reports for unified device management

## Typical Use Cases

- Rapid deployment of tracking on service vehicles and light commercial fleets via OBD II plug in installation
- Anti theft workflows using power cut and geo fence alerts combined with Plaspy notifications
- Delivery and route monitoring with configurable reporting intervals for ETA and route optimization
- Driver behavior oversight using overspeed alerts and trip playback for coaching and compliance
- Security monitoring and post incident investigation using remote audio monitor and event logs
- Vehicle telemetry integration where OBD II PIDs are available to support ignition and basic diagnostics

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature availability depends on device firmware version and the specific hardware revision shipped by the manufacturer
- Some OBD II telemetry functions require the vehicle to expose the relevant PIDs and may vary by vehicle make and model
- Optional accessories and certain remote control functions are sold separately and may require additional installation
- Regional cellular variants and regulatory requirements can affect available reporting channels and behavior
- Always confirm current capabilities and supported features in the official Noran documentation for your device revision

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the Noran NR100 with Plaspy gives fleet and security teams a centralized view of vehicle location, event alerts, and basic vehicle telemetry. Plaspy helps translate the NR100's position fixes, event messages, and OBD II signals into actionable dashboards, automated notifications, and historical reports that support operational oversight and faster incident response.
