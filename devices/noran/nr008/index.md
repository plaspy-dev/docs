# Noran - NR008

![NR008](./tracker.jpg)

The NR008 is a robust automobile GPS tracker designed for deployments that require reliable real time location updates and vehicle control. Built around a SiRFstar GNSS chipset with SIMCOM GSM and GPRS communications, the NR008 provides continuous position reporting, SOS alerts, geo fencing, over speed alarms, movement detection and remote immobilization capabilities suited to vehicle security and fleet oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the NR008 can transmit position and event data to the Plaspy platform using SMS or GPRS so fleet managers have centralized visibility and alerting. Its combination of telemetry and remote control functions makes it practical for Plaspy users who need straightforward integration for tracking, alert routing and basic vehicle command workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking and centralized fleet visibility.
- Remote immobilizer and engine control capability for anti theft response.
- SOS emergency alerts and voice monitoring for on board incident response.
- Geo fencing, over speed alarms and movement detection for proactive alerts.
- Designed for in vehicle use and supplied with practical accessories for deployment.
- SiRFstar GNSS and SIMCOM cellular components provide dependable positioning and connectivity.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the NR008 transmits location fixes and event messages over GSM GPRS or SMS to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy ingests this data to provide map based tracking, alert management and historical reporting so operators can monitor assets and act on events from a single interface.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy for map tracking and live oversight.
- Geo fence and movement alerts forwarded to Plaspy to trigger notifications and workflows.
- SOS alerts and voice monitoring events escalated to authorized contacts via Plaspy alert channels.
- Remote immobilizer and engine control commands issued from Plaspy via SMS or GPRS where supported by the vehicle wiring.
- Historical position and event data stored for reporting, route review and operational analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft protection with anti dismantle alerts, remote immobilization and recovery support.

- Driver safety and incident response using SOS alerts and in vehicle voice monitoring.
- General telematics for maintenance planning, utilization tracking and operational optimization.
