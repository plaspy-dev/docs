# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - AAP PERSONAL

![AAP PERSONAL](./logo.jpg)

The AAP Personal monitoring system, developed by OKB Tehnoavtomatika, is a comprehensive solution for tracking and monitoring objects. This device is powered by an internal battery, making it ideal for monitoring objects that are located far away from a power supply or do not have access to one, such as transported cargo or remote employees. With the AAP Personal, you can easily keep track of the location of these objects and ensure their safety and security.

One of the standout features of the AAP Personal is its versatile notification system. The device can notify the owner through various means, including internet-based notifications via GPRS, SMS alerts to a specified number, or monitoring through a service control center. This ensures that you are always informed about the status and location of your tracked objects, no matter where you are.

The AAP Personal is a standalone search solution that utilizes a 50-channel high sensitivity GPS receiver for accurate and reliable positioning. It operates on the DC-GSM 900/1800 frequency and supports data transmission and reception through GPRS and SMS. The device is designed for personal use and can be used for monitoring goods, personnel, and more.

For corporate clients, OKB Tehnoavtomatika offers a software and hardware complex for monitoring vehicles on favorable terms. The client part of the program is provided free of charge, and the connection of devices to the server side is performed for a small fee. This allows businesses to efficiently track and manage their fleet of vehicles, ensuring optimal performance and security.

### Key Features:

- Power supply from internal battery for remote monitoring
- Notification options include internet-based notifications, SMS alerts, and monitoring through a service control center
- Standalone search solution with a 50-channel high sensitivity GPS receiver
- Supports data transmission and reception through GPRS and SMS
- Designed for personal use, ideal for monitoring goods and personnel
- Software and hardware complex available for corporate clients for vehicle monitoring
