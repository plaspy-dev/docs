# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - AAP PERSONAL

![AAP PERSONAL](./logo.jpg)

The AAP PERSONAL monitoring system from OKB Tehnoavtomatika is a standalone tracking device designed for personal and asset monitoring. It is powered by an internal battery and uses a high sensitivity GPS receiver to provide reliable positioning for objects that are remote or lack a fixed power source. The device supports data transmission and notification via GPRS and SMS and can report status to a service control center, making it suitable for tracking goods, personnel, and other mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AAP PERSONAL can feed location and notification data into the Plaspy platform to provide centralized visibility and management. Integration with Plaspy enables organizations to view device locations on a shared map, monitor movements over time, and receive alerts and reports that help maintain operational oversight for remote or transported assets.

## Key Highlights

- Battery powered design suited for objects without continuous power
- High sensitivity GPS receiver for improved location reliability
- Multiple notification options including internet based messages and SMS alerts
- Standalone operation ideal for personal monitoring and portable assets
- Corporate software and server side options available for fleet level monitoring
- Practical choice for transport monitoring where continuous connectivity is intermittent

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the AAP PERSONAL supplies location and status updates that Plaspy displays and processes for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy aggregates device messages and presents them alongside other fleet data to support operational decision making.

- Real time and recent location visibility on Plaspy maps for tracked devices
- Consolidated alerts and notifications routed into Plaspy workflows for incident awareness
- Historical position logs and movement summaries to support reporting and review
- Fleet level oversight to group and monitor multiple AAP PERSONAL units together
- Centralized monitoring through Plaspy to combine device reports with other system data

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring transported cargo during temporary or long distance shipments
- Tracking remote employees or field personnel for safety and coordination
- Temporary asset tracking where permanent installation is not practical
- Small scale vehicle or equipment monitoring using the corporate server option
- Situations where periodic reporting and concise notifications are sufficient

The AAP PERSONAL is a practical option for organizations that need a battery powered, standalone tracker integrated into a broader monitoring platform. Its notification flexibility and GPS sensitivity make it well suited to tracking items and people in environments where continuous power and connectivity are not guaranteed.
