# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - AAP PERSONAL Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive feature overview for the OKB Tehnoavtomatika AAP PERSONAL tracker and how it can be used with Plaspy for location monitoring and notification. It summarizes the practical capabilities that are relevant for integrating the device into fleet or asset visibility workflows on the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a general guide and consult the device manufacturer documentation for definitive, model-specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The AAP PERSONAL is described as a standalone personal monitoring device designed for remote tracking where external power is not available. Its primary value is delivering location awareness and timed or event-driven notifications using mobile data and messaging channels, suitable for tracking goods, personnel, and other mobile assets.

- Battery powered operation for monitoring where external power is not available
- High sensitivity GPS reception for reliable position fixes in typical outdoor conditions
- Data transmission and alerts via GPRS and SMS for flexible notification delivery
- Support for monitoring through a service control center or internet based systems
- Designed for personal and small asset monitoring scenarios such as transported cargo and personnel

## Core Features of OKB Tehnoavtomatika - AAP PERSONAL

- Internal battery power to enable tracking of assets without a continuous external power source
- 50 channel high sensitivity GPS receiver for accurate position determination
- Support for GPRS data transmission for internet based notifications and remote monitoring
- SMS alert capability to deliver concise notifications to specified phone numbers
- Standalone search and monitoring functionality intended for personal and cargo tracking
- Manufacturer offered software and hardware options for corporate vehicle monitoring deployments
- Configurable notification pathways including service control center integration

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AAP PERSONAL's core reporting capabilities can be used to provide continuous visibility and alerting inside the Plaspy platform. Plaspy is designed to accept location and notification data from compatible trackers and present that information for operational use.

- Plaspy displays device location updates received from the tracker so teams can monitor asset movement
- SMS or GPRS based reports from the device can be reflected as events or status updates in Plaspy
- Notification configurations on the device or via the manufacturer service can complement Plaspy alerts and dashboards
- Historical position data reported to Plaspy can be used for simple playback and location history review
- Plaspy detects many tracker protocols automatically, simplifying device onboarding and initial visibility

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring transported cargo on routes where vehicle power is not available
- Tracking remote personnel or field staff during missions away from fixed infrastructure
- Short term asset tracking for loaned equipment or temporary deployments
- Small scale fleet or vehicle monitoring through manufacturer-provided corporate solutions
- Use in service center workflows where devices are supervised by a monitoring operator
- Situations where multiple notification channels (GPRS and SMS) are required for redundancy

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific capabilities and command sets depend on the device firmware and may change across firmware updates
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can alter supported frequency bands or notification behaviors
- Certain functions may require configuration at installation or may be enabled only through manufacturer systems
- SMS and GPRS availability depends on mobile network coverage and local operator support

Using Plaspy with an AAP PERSONAL tracker brings the device's location and notification capabilities into a single monitoring environment that teams can use for day to day oversight. Plaspy helps translate periodic position reports and alert messages into a unified view for operations, reducing the need to check multiple notification channels separately.
