# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - MPU-01GLONASS

![MPU-01GLONASS](./logo.jpg)

The MPU-01 GLONASS Tracking System from OKB Tehnoavtomatika is a compact and portable tracking device designed for vehicle and asset location monitoring. It features a 24 channel GLONASS GPS receiver NV08C-CSM for precise positioning, a small footprint of 90 × 50 × 16 mm, and a weight under 200 grams. The unit supports GSM 900 and 1800 frequency bands and can receive and hand over SMS messages, making it suitable for deployments where text based communication or basic remote messaging is useful.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MPU-01GLONASS can be used to provide location visibility and operational oversight within the Plaspy fleet management platform. Its physical size and flexible input output configuration options allow it to be deployed across a range of vehicles and mobile assets, while its positioning and messaging capabilities make it a practical choice for organizations looking to integrate tracked units into Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and basic remote communication.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design suited to vehicles and portable assets
- 24 channel GLONASS GPS receiver NV08C-CSM for reliable position data
- Supports GSM 900 and 1800 frequency bands and can receive SMS messages
- SMS handover capability for basic message relay between tracker and user
- Flexible configuration options including combinations of digital and analog inputs and outputs
- Practical form factor for fleet and asset tracking use cases

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected with Plaspy, the MPU-01GLONASS supplies position and status information that Plaspy presents on maps and in operational dashboards. Plaspy can consolidate the device's location data and messaging signals into a single view for monitoring and decision making.

- Display live device location and movement on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Store and review historical routes and location history in Plaspy reports
- Configure alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on position changes or input status
- Use device input signals to reflect simple asset or vehicle status in Plaspy views
- Leverage the device messaging capabilities where supported to complement Plaspy notifications
