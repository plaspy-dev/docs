# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - MPU-01GLONASS

![MPU-01GLONASS](./logo.jpg)

The MPU-01 GLONASS Tracking System, developed by OKB Tehnoavtomatika, is a reliable and efficient GPS tracker designed to meet the tracking needs of various applications. With its compact size and lightweight design, measuring 90 × 50 × 16 mm and weighing less than 200 grams, this tracker is highly portable and can be easily installed in a variety of vehicles or assets.

Equipped with a 24-channel GLONASS/GPS receiver NV08C-CSM, the MPU-01 GLONASS Tracking System ensures accurate and precise location tracking. It supports GSM 900/1800 frequency bands, allowing for seamless communication and receiving SMS messages from subscribers. Additionally, it features the capability to handover SMS messages, providing convenient and efficient communication between the tracker and the user.

The MPU-01 GLONASS Tracking System offers flexible configuration options to suit different tracking requirements. Depending on the task at hand, it can be configured with 1 digital input and 1 output, 1 analog input, or 2 digital inputs and 1 analog input. This versatility allows for customization and integration with various sensors or devices, expanding the functionality and potential applications of the tracker.

### Key Features:

- Compact and lightweight design for easy installation and portability
- 24-channel GLONASS/GPS receiver for accurate and precise location tracking
- GSM 900/1800 frequency bands for seamless communication and SMS message reception
- Flexible configuration options to suit different tracking requirements
