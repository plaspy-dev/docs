# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - MTA-03

<div className="device-logo">
![MTA-03](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Transport Control System MTA-03 from OKB Tehnoavtomatika is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for a range of monitoring and control tasks. Its specification emphasizes flexible configuration for specific tasks, support for monitoring both vehicles and stationary objects, and multiple notification channels including internet GPRS, SMS alerts, and monitoring via a service control center. The device uses a high sensitivity 50 channel GPS receiver and offers a broad DC input range plus a built in rechargeable battery, making it suitable for varied deployment scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MTA-03 can feed position and alarm information into a centralized fleet management platform for improved visibility and operational oversight. When integrated with Plaspy, incoming location updates and notifications from the MTA-03 can be presented alongside other fleet data to support monitoring, alerting, and reporting workflows without requiring you to change how you manage assets.

## Key Highlights

- Flexible configuration options to tailor tracking and monitoring behavior for different tasks
- Supports monitoring of both moving vehicles and stationary objects for security or asset oversight
- Multiple notification channels including internet GPRS, SMS alerts, and service control center reporting
- High sensitivity 50 channel GPS receiver for reliable position acquisition
- Wide DC power input range with a built in rechargeable battery for resilience
- Compact and lightweight form factor for discreet installation and easy deployment

## How It Works with Plaspy

The MTA-03 delivers position and alarm messages that Plaspy can use to provide centralized tracking and operational insight. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it in a unified interface so teams can monitor assets, respond to alarms, and review historical activity.

- Centralized live location display for tracked vehicles and stationary assets
- Consolidated alerts and notifications routed into Plaspy for timely operator awareness
- Historical route and event logging to support reporting and operational review
- Unified visibility across mixed fleets and asset types for easier oversight
- Configurable notifications and event handling within Plaspy based on incoming device messages

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for trucks and commercial vehicles
- Monitoring of agricultural machinery such as harvesters
- Security and oversight of stationary assets and equipment
- Remote equipment monitoring for operations and maintenance planning
- Logistics and transport operational management where consolidated tracking is required
