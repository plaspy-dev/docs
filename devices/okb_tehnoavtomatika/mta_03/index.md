# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - MTA-03

![MTA-03](./logo.jpg)

The Transport Control System MTA-03 by OKB Tehnoavtomatika is a versatile and comprehensive GPS tracker designed to meet specific monitoring and control needs. With flexible settings and configuration options, this device can be used to track and monitor various vehicles and equipment, including trucks, harvesters, and even stationary objects for security purposes.

One of the standout features of the MTA-03 is its ability to provide notifications to the owner through various means. Whether it's through internet connectivity \(GPRS\), SMS alerts to a specified number, or monitoring by a service control center, you can stay informed about the status and location of your assets at all times.

**Key Features:**

- Flexible settings for specific tasks
- Can monitor both vehicles and stationary objects
- Internet \(GPRS\) and SMS notifications
- Satellite monitoring and GSM alarm
- GPS tracking with high-sensitivity 50-channel receiver
- Power supply options: DC \(9V to 33V\) and built-in rechargeable battery
- Compact and lightweight design \(110 × 80 × 32 mm, 300g\)

The MTA-03 is a reliable and efficient GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features to help you monitor and control your assets effectively. Whether you need to track your fleet of vehicles or ensure the security of your stationary objects, this device has you covered.
