# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - MTA-02 GLONASS

<div className="device-logo">
![MTA-02 GLONASS](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The MTA-02 GLONASS from OKB Tehnoavtomatika is a compact satellite tracker designed for reliable position monitoring of vehicles, assets, and personnel. It integrates a 50 channel high sensitivity GLONASS GPS receiver MNP-M7 to deliver consistent location data, and supports common mobile communications including GSM 900 1800, GPRS, and SMS for transmitting and receiving information. The unit accepts a wide supply voltage range from 9V to 50V and includes a built in rechargeable battery to support operation during brief power interruptions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MTA-02 GLONASS can feed position and status information into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its multiple communication options and standby readiness make it suitable for use with Plaspy when building a tracking solution that needs reliable satellite positioning, periodic reporting, and basic text message interaction. Plaspy can use the device data to provide visibility, alerts, and historical routes without requiring specialized hardware changes.

## Key Highlights

- 50 channel high sensitivity GLONASS GPS receiver for improved satellite visibility
- Wide operating voltage range from 9V to 50V for flexible power options
- Built in rechargeable battery with a standby time of at least 6 hours
- Supports GSM 900 1800, GPRS, and SMS for data transmission and reception
- Fast technical readiness time under 3 minutes to minimize downtime
- Compact and lightweight design with an IP30 enclosure suitable for many installations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the MTA-02 GLONASS provides positional and basic status data that Plaspy ingests for tracking, monitoring, and reporting. Plaspy can present live locations, maintain histories, and trigger operational workflows based on the information received from the device.

- Send location updates to Plaspy over available mobile data channels such as GPRS
- Use SMS as an alternative channel for location or status messages when needed
- View real time location and recent movement history inside the Plaspy platform
- Configure alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on location, connectivity, or timed reports
- Include MTA-02 GLONASS devices in fleet dashboards for operational oversight and dispatching
