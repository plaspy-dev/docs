# OKB Tehnoavtomatika - MTA-12

![MTA-12](./logo.jpg)

The Tracking System MTA-12-CAN \(FMS\) by OKB Tehnoavtomatika is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker designed to meet the specific needs of modern transport. With its flexible system configuration, this device can be tailored to solve various tracking problems. It is particularly suitable for monitoring different vehicles, including specialized equipment.

One of the standout features of the MTA-12-CAN is its ability to provide additional information on the functioning of the machine through the Controller Area Network \(CAN\). This allows for fuel control and ignition control of the state, giving owners valuable insights into the performance of their vehicles.

The MTA-12-CAN offers multiple ways to receive notifications and monitor the tracker. Owners can choose to receive notifications via the internet using GPRS, through SMS messages to a specified number, or by monitoring through a service control center. This flexibility ensures that owners can stay connected and informed about their vehicles at all times.

### Key Features:

- Flexible system configuration for specific tracking needs
- Controller Area Network \(CAN\) for additional machine information
- Multiple notification options: internet \(GPRS\) and SMS
- Fuel control and ignition control of the state

### Technical Specifications:

- Channel tie with DC - GSM 900/1800
- 50-channel high-sensitivity GPS receiver for accurate coordinates
- Power supply: DC from 9V to 68V
- Built-in rechargeable battery with a capacity of up to 600 mA/h
- Enclosure IP30
- Overall dimensions: 90 × 65 × 30 mm
- Weight: 200 g
