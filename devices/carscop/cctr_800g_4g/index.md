# Carscop - CCTR-800G-4G

![CCTR-800G-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The CCTR-800G-4G is a rugged, portable GPS tracker designed for long duration outdoor tracking and flexible cloud integration. Built for demanding environments, the device combines global cellular connectivity with high sensitivity GNSS positioning, a large rechargeable battery, IP65 weather protection, and a strong built in magnet for quick mounting on metallic surfaces. Those features make it suitable for real time location, fleet oversight, and anti theft monitoring in a range of operational settings.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CCTR-800G-4G is designed to be pointed at third party platforms through a configurable upload address and an open protocol approach. That flexibility lets fleet managers and integrators route telemetry and alarm events into Plaspy for live tracking, alerts, and reporting while retaining offline recording and SMS fallback options to maintain continuity when cellular coverage is limited.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via configurable upload address and open protocol for straightforward integration with fleet software.
- Large 6000 mAh rechargeable battery and multiple power modes to extend operational life between charges.
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning with BeiDou support and assisted fixes for reliable location acquisition.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and strong built in magnet for covert mounting and outdoor resilience.
- Offline track recording to internal memory and SMS locating as a backup when platform connectivity is not available.
- Configurable alerts including shock alarm, SOS notifications, and low battery alerts to support anti theft response.
- OTA firmware upgrade capability and OEM options for branding and deployment flexibility.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the CCTR-800G-4G sends location fixes and event data to the platform address you configure. Plaspy ingests those telemetry streams to present live positions, event timelines, and fleet level summaries, while the device provides fallback reporting and stored tracks that sync after connectivity resumes.

- Real time location updates and configurable upload intervals to support live fleet monitoring.
- Alarm delivery to Plaspy for events such as shock alarms, SOS notifications, and low battery conditions.
- Offline track storage on the device with automatic upload to Plaspy once the connection is restored.
- OTA firmware updates to maintain compatibility and add enhancements relevant to Plaspy integrations.
- SMS locating and automatic network configuration as fallback methods if platform connectivity is temporarily unavailable.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert asset tracking for trailers, machinery, or cargo where discreet mounting and rugged housing are required.
- Vehicle tracking for cars and light trucks to support anti theft alerts and location based oversight.
- Temporary deployments or field personnel monitoring where portability and long standby times matter.
- Fleet management for route playback, live fleet views, and event based reporting to improve utilization and response.
- Remote telemetry where offline recording and SMS fallback ensure continuity in low coverage areas.
