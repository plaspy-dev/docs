# CCTR-804 Vehicle GPS Tracker

The CCTR-804 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for reliable vehicle tracking across global 2G/3G/4G networks. Purpose-built for fleet management, rental cars, taxis, buses and logistics vehicles, the CCTR-804 blends hidden installation, robust telemetry and remote immobilizer control to meet commercial anti-theft and operational monitoring needs.

With built-in GPS and cellular modules plus A-GPS for fast position fixes, the CCTR-804 delivers real-time tracking and essential telemetry to Plaspy and other platforms. Its rechargeable backup Li battery, shock-sensor controlled uploading and configurable upload intervals make it an economical choice for continuous tracking even when external power is lost.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates with Plaspy for real-time tracking, alerts and reporting using the device’s open GPRS protocol.
- Multi-network connectivity: global 2G/3G/4G support reduces coverage gaps for fleet management and long-haul operations.
- Hidden, low-profile installation: built-in antenna and compact size \(87 × 47 × 20 mm, ~70 g\) require only 2–4 wires to install.
- Remote immobilizer for anti-theft: stop vehicles remotely via platform control or SMS to reduce theft and unauthorized use.
- Smart telemetry and power savings: shock-triggered uploading, configurable upload intervals \(default 30 s\) and stop-upload-after-idle conserve data and battery.
- Offline recording and last-known position reporting for robust tracking when GSM/GPS is unavailable.
- OTA firmware upgrades and auto APN/GPRS configuration for simplified deployment and maintenance.
- Dealer/distributor and white-label support: scalable options for resellers, custom apps and personal domain/login pages.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Connecting the CCTR-804 to Plaspy is straightforward: the tracker uploads positional and telemetry packets over the cellular network to a server endpoint. Because the unit uses an open GPRS protocol and supports automatic APN configuration, integrators can point device data to Plaspy for live mapping, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy-compatible integration enables fleet operators to manage vehicles centrally and trigger remote actions from the platform.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and route playback.
- Remote immobilizer control: initiate vehicle stop commands from Plaspy or via SMS as a safety/anti-theft measure.
- Shock and movement-triggered alerts to Plaspy for collision or tamper detection.
- Configurable upload intervals and stop-upload-after-idle ensure efficient data usage for fleet monitoring.
- Offline storage and last-known-position reporting keep asset histories complete during coverage gaps; data is uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity resumes.
