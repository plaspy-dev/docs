# Carscop - CCTR-622G-4G Features

This page provides public feature context for the Carscop CCTR-622G-4G when used with Plaspy. It describes the device capabilities that are relevant to monitoring, alarm handling, and location reporting in Plaspy, and explains how those capabilities are typically reflected in Plaspy dashboards and workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes consult the manufacturer documentation linked at the end of this page.

## Feature Overview

The CCTR-622G-4G is a compact, no-install tracker designed for personal and light‑asset tracking with straightforward Plaspy compatibility. It emphasizes flexible reporting modes and essential alarms to balance responsiveness and battery life for portable deployments.

- Real‑time location reporting using GPS plus BeiDou dual GNSS with A GPS assistance for faster fixes.
- Global cellular connectivity across 2G, 3G, and 4G networks to maintain coverage in diverse regions.
- Portable no‑install form factor with a replaceable Li ion battery for temporary or mobile tracking.
- Emergency SOS button with configurable contacts and shock triggered reporting for security events.
- Two way voice calling and silent listen in to provide situational awareness when needed.
- Over the air firmware upgrades (FOTA) and an open protocol that facilitates integration with platforms like Plaspy.

## Core Features of Carscop - CCTR-622G-4G

- Dual GNSS positioning with GPS and BeiDou plus A GPS assistance for improved time to first fix.
- Multi generation cellular connectivity supporting 2G, 3G, and 4G networks for wide area coverage.
- Portable design with a replaceable 1000 mAh battery suitable for temporary or mobile use.
- SOS alarm with support for alerting preset phone numbers and forwarding alarm events to platforms.
- Shock triggered reporting to capture possible impact or tamper events.
- Configurable reporting modes including adjustable upload intervals and timed on/off operation to manage battery life.
- Two way voice calling and silent listen in for live communication and monitoring.
- Built in memory for offline track recording when network coverage is not available.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the CCTR-622G-4G sends location updates, alarms, and status messages that Plaspy displays on its web and mobile interfaces. Plaspy recognizes common tracker messages and surfaces them as map positions, event notifications, and historical tracks to support operational visibility.

- Real time position updates and movement reports appear on Plaspy maps with timestamps and location history.
- SOS, shock, and low battery alerts are forwarded into Plaspy event streams where you can configure notifications and escalation.
- Offline stored points are uploaded after connectivity is restored and become part of the historical track in Plaspy.
- Configurable upload intervals and timed on/off modes allow tuning of reporting behavior visible in device settings and activity logs.
- Two way voice and silent listen in are represented as call or audio events managed through conventional voice channels while location and alarm data continue to flow into Plaspy.
- Plaspy can ingest the device protocol and display device status, simplifying initial setup and ongoing monitoring.
