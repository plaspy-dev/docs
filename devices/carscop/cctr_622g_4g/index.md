# Carscop - CCTR-622G-4G

![CCTR-622G-4G](./tracker.JPG)

The CCTR-622G-4G is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for discreet personal and light-asset tracking and is fully Plaspy compatible for seamless platform integration. From outdoor workers and pet tracking to transported goods, this no-install tracker delivers reliable real-time tracking across global 2G/3G/4G networks, SOS alarm capability, and flexible reporting modes so you can monitor location and key telemetry on Plaspy’s web and mobile interfaces.

Built around a GPS + BeiDou dual-mode GNSS module with A-GPS assistance, the CCTR-622G-4G balances responsiveness and battery life with configurable upload intervals, shock-triggered reporting and timed on/off operation. The device supports two-way voice, silent listen-in and online firmware upgrades, and its open protocol makes it straightforward to connect directly to Plaspy for alerts, historical tracks and ongoing fleet management visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and alarm delivery on an established telematics platform.
- Global connectivity: supports 2G GSM, 3G WCDMA and 4G LTE \(FDD/TDD\) networks for wide area coverage.
- Portable, no-install form factor with replaceable 1000mAh Li‑ion \(BL-5C\) battery for flexible deployment.
- Dual GNSS: built-in GPS + BeiDou with A-GPS assistance for faster position fixes and reliable location telemetry.
- Emergency and security functions: SOS button \(alerts up to three numbers\), shock-triggered reporting and low-battery alarms.
- Two-way voice calling and silent listen-in provide live communication and situational awareness when needed.
- Open protocol and platform customization \(private domain, logo, app\) enable integration into dealer or fleet management systems.
- Internet firmware upgrade \(FOTA\) keeps devices up to date without manual intervention.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is straightforward thanks to the CCTR-622G-4G’s open protocol and standard telemetry outputs. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker sends regular location updates, alarm events and status messages that Plaspy displays in real time on web and mobile dashboards. Plaspy can ingest these messages to provide live maps, geofencing alerts, historical tracks and configurable notifications for fleet management and personal security workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: position, timestamp and movement-based reports are uploaded at configured intervals.
- SOS and alarm reporting: SOS button, shock alarm and low-battery alerts are forwarded to Plaspy and/or preset contacts via SMS or call.
- Offline data handling: built-in memory records tracks while GSM is unavailable and uploads stored points when coverage returns.
- Configurable reporting modes: shock-triggered uploads, always-online or timed on/off modes to tune responsiveness and battery life.
- Two-way voice and silent listen-in provide additional operational telemetry via call events \(managed through conventional voice channels\).
