# Carscop - CCTR-622G-4G

![CCTR-622G-4G](./tracker.JPG)

The CCTR-622G-4G is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for discreet personal and light asset tracking. It offers real time tracking and alarm capabilities in a no install form factor, making it suitable for applications from outdoor workers and transported goods to pet monitoring. The device combines a dual GNSS approach with assisted positioning to deliver responsive location updates and supports configurable reporting modes for balance between responsiveness and battery life.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CCTR-622G-4G integrates with Plaspy’s dashboards and notification systems to provide live maps, alerts and historical track data. Its open protocol and standard telemetry outputs mean that location updates, SOS events and status messages can be forwarded into Plaspy for fleet visibility, personal security workflows and operational reporting without extensive customization.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking and alarm delivery on an established telematics platform
- Global connectivity across common cellular networks for wide area coverage
- Portable no install design with a replaceable battery for flexible deployments
- Dual GNSS positioning with assisted fixes for improved time to first fix and reliable location telemetry
- Emergency and security features including SOS alert and shock triggered reporting
- Two way voice calling and silent listen in for situational awareness when needed
- Open protocol and over the air firmware updates to simplify device management and integration

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is straightforward thanks to the tracker’s open protocol and standard telemetry outputs. When connected, the CCTR-622G-4G sends location updates, alarm events and status messages that Plaspy displays on web and mobile dashboards for monitoring and response.

- Real time location updates and movement based reports uploaded at configurable intervals
- SOS and alarm forwarding so emergency signals and shock alerts appear in Plaspy notifications
- Offline data buffering that uploads stored track points when connectivity is restored
- Configurable reporting modes to balance responsiveness and battery life for different operational needs
- Two way voice and silent listen in events available alongside location data for richer situational context
