# Carscop - CCTR-830G-4G

![CCTR-830G-4G](./tracker.JPG)

The CCTR-830G-4G is a compact OBD II GPS tracker designed for plug and play vehicle tracking and basic diagnostics. It combines dual GNSS positioning with A GPS assistance to deliver dependable location fixes and is built to access vehicle diagnostic information and telemetry through the vehicle interface without extra wiring. The unit is intended for rapid deployment across single vehicles or fleet installations where straightforward installation and continual monitoring are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CCTR-830G-4G integrates vehicle location, diagnostic trouble code data and basic telemetry into Plaspy for real time visibility, configurable alerts and historical reporting. Its backup battery, remote management capabilities and protocol options make it a practical choice when you need centralized monitoring and scalable device management on the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for quick installation and centralized fleet monitoring with Plaspy.
- Dual GNSS operation including GPS and BeiDou with A GPS assistance for faster positioning.
- On device DTC reading and CAN bus data access to surface vehicle diagnostics and telemetry.
- Built in backup battery for tamper and power down alerts and to protect against short power losses.
- Configurable upload intervals and offline track history to preserve location data when cellular is unavailable.
- Remote firmware and protocol management to simplify deployment and platform integration.
- Customizable cellular band options to match regional mobile network requirements.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the CCTR-830G-4G streams location and vehicle telemetry over a cellular connection so fleet managers and operators can monitor assets in real time, review diagnostic codes and configure alerts from a single platform. Plaspy ingests device data and makes it available for mapping, reporting and operational workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry updates on configurable intervals for live fleet monitoring.
- DTC and vehicle status reporting available within Plaspy for diagnostic review and trend analysis.
- Power down and tamper alerts triggered by the backup battery state to support anti theft response.
- Offline storage of tracks when cellular service is interrupted and automatic resume of uploads when connectivity returns.
- Centralized alert rules, geofencing and historical reporting to support operations and compliance tracking.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, taxis, rental vehicles and light commercial fleets requiring location and diagnostic visibility.
- Anti theft monitoring where power down alerts and offline track retention aid vehicle recovery.
- Vehicle health monitoring and basic diagnostics reporting for preventive maintenance planning.
- Operational telemetry and driver oversight to improve routing, utilization and response times.
- B2B deployments and distribution programs where remote management and protocol customization are required.
