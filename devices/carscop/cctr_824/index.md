# Carscop - CCTR-824

![CCTR-824](./tracker.JPG)

The CCTR-824 is a compact, battery powered long life GPS tracker designed for covert vehicle, equipment and rental asset tracking. It is built for quick DIY deployment without permanent wiring and mounts securely using strong magnetic pins or an optional magnet cover. The unit is optimized for long unattended operation and includes a low detect deep sleep mode, light based removal alarm, and configurable locate intervals to support extended field use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CCTR-824 uploads dependable location and telemetry to Plaspy via GPRS so teams can monitor assets in real time, review historical tracks, and receive tamper or removal alerts without complex installation. Its low power profile and remote configuration options make it a practical match for Plaspy workflows that need long duration visibility and simple, scalable deployment.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for GPRS upload of location and telemetry to a server address for viewing on web and mobile dashboards
- Ultra long battery life with a high capacity battery pack designed for months or years of monitoring at low report rates
- Compact covert form factor with magnet mount for non permanent installation and easy concealment on vehicles and equipment
- Very low power deep sleep mode to minimize detection and extend unattended operation
- Light sensor that supports removal and tamper alerts useful for anti theft workflows
- Fast GNSS fixes and reasonable positioning accuracy for routine tracking and history playback
- Remote configuration via SMS and automated APN or GPRS settings to simplify field setup

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the CCTR-824 with Plaspy is straightforward: the tracker transmits GPRS packets to the server address you configure so Plaspy can ingest location and device telemetry for monitoring and reporting. Once connected, Plaspy provides real time visibility, alert routing, and historical playback to support fleet and asset management tasks.

- Real time location and telemetry updates forwarded to Plaspy for dashboard monitoring
- Light sensor removal or fall alarms sent as alerts to notify operations of possible tampering
- Configurable reporting intervals let you balance battery life and tracking granularity within Plaspy workflows
- Remote device setup and auto configuration reduce hands on time during deployments
- History tracking and route playback available in Plaspy for audit, recovery, and analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle tracking for recovery and theft deterrence on rental or company vehicles
- Long term monitoring of equipment and construction assets left on site without mains power
- Rental asset management for trailers, bikes, and seasonal equipment where quick install and removal are important
- Surveillance of containers, parked machinery, and other stationary or intermittently moved assets
- Temporary deployments for audits or inspections where recoverable, non permanent trackers are preferred

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CCTR-824 is a practical option for organizations using Plaspy who need a low maintenance, concealed tracker capable of long unattended operation. Its strengths in battery life, magnet mounted mounting, tamper alerting, and simple remote configuration reduce operating overhead and make it easy to add location visibility to assets that cannot be hard wired.

While this model focuses on location and tamper aware tracking and does not include ignition, immobilizer, or Bluetooth sensors, it complements Plaspy s broader telemetry ecosystem. Use the CCTR-824 to provide reliable position and alert data into Plaspy and combine it with other Plaspy compatible devices when additional vehicle or fuel telemetry is required.
