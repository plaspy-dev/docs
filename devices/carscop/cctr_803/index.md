# Carscop - CCTR-803

![CCTR-803](./logo.jpg)

The Carscop CCTR-803 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for vehicles, trucks, and cars. It combines satellite positioning with GSM base station locating to provide continuous location reporting, and includes features intended to support discreet installation and in-vehicle monitoring. The device offers SMS and call based location options, alarm reporting, an external microphone for voice monitoring, and a rechargeable backup battery to preserve tracking and alerting during power interruptions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CCTR-803 can deliver location and event data into fleet management workflows. The tracker supports configurable reporting to client platforms and can be set up to forward alarms and position updates to a third party system. That flexibility makes it a practical option for operators who want to use Plaspy for real-time visibility, alerting, and historical reporting across mixed vehicle fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for vehicle use with a built-in antenna for discreet placement and concealed installation.
- External microphone for in-cabin voice monitoring where permitted.
- Dual positioning through GPS and GSM base station locating to improve availability in mixed signal conditions.
- Supports remote software upgrades and automatic APN configuration to reduce onsite maintenance.
- Multiple reporting options including SMS location with map links and platform uploads for server based monitoring.
- Alarm set and SOS messaging to preset numbers and to a tracking platform for timely alerts.
- Built-in backup battery and local track memory to preserve data and alarms during power loss or temporary network outages.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy can consume the CCTR-803 output when the device is configured to send data to a client platform. The tracker’s ability to report positions, send alarm messages, and accept configurable upload settings allows Plaspy to present real-time maps, alerts, and historical tracks for operational oversight.

- Receive live location updates and show vehicle position on Plaspy maps for continuous fleet visibility.
- Forward SOS and alarm events from the tracker to Plaspy so teams can respond to emergencies and threshold alerts.
- Store and review historical tracking on Plaspy, benefiting from the device’s local recording and server upload options.
- Use role based access and reporting in Plaspy to share appropriate visibility with drivers, fleet supervisors, and dealers.
- Configure reporting behavior from the tracker so Plaspy displays frequent updates while also supporting reduced uploads when vehicles are stationary.
- Issue platform commands where supported, such as remote immobilization options, when device configuration and local law permit.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, vans, and light trucks that require centralized visibility and alerts.
- Rental car and vehicle hire operations needing discreet tracking and remote event notifications.
- Taxi and transportation services that benefit from live location, trip history, and SOS reporting.
- School bus and passenger transport monitoring where event alerts and historical playback are important.
- Tracking of high value or mobile equipment that needs backup tracking and local record storage.
