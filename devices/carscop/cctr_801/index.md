# Carscop - CCTR-801

![CCTR-801](./logo.jpg)

The Carscop CCTR-801 GPS tracker is a compact and easy-to-install device that provides real-time tracking of vehicles. With its small size and simple installation process \(requiring only 2-4 wires\), this tracker can be easily hidden in a car, bus, taxi, or rental vehicle. It can even be attached to the metal body of a vehicle using the included magic tape. 

One of the standout features of the CCTR-801 is its ability to turn off the car engine remotely, either through the tracking platform or by sending an SMS. This anti-theft feature provides an added layer of security for your vehicle. In case of emergencies, the tracker also comes with an external SOS button that can be used to send an alert to the tracking platform and preset phone numbers.

The CCTR-801 offers a range of other useful features, including the ability to listen to the sound around the tracker in silence mode, set the upload interval to local time, and record track data in the built-in memory when there is no GSM network available. It also has a shock sensor that can be used as a car shock and movement alarm. The tracker uploads its location to the tracking platform every 10-30 seconds, and after the vehicle has been stationary for 2 minutes, it goes into sleep mode to conserve battery power. The CCTR-801 is powered by a rechargeable Li battery with a battery life of 1-2 days.

The CCTR-801 GPS tracker is designed to be user-friendly, with no need to download any software. Users can simply visit the Carscop website, log in, and track their vehicles or view the history of tracking. The platform uses free Google Maps, so there are no additional map charges. The tracker also allows for the presetting of 3 phone numbers to receive alerts via SMS. It can send alerts to the platform when the vehicle goes out of a restricted area or exceeds a restricted speed. With its comprehensive features and ease of use, the CCTR-801 is an ideal choice for individuals and businesses looking to track and secure their vehicles.

### Outstanding Features:

- Real-time tracking of vehicles
- Easy installation with 2-4 wires
- Can be hidden or attached to the metal body of a vehicle
- Remote engine shutdown via SMS or platform
- No platform service charge \(free tracking platform\)
- Ability to listen to sound around the tracker in silence mode
- Upload interval can be set to local time
- Built-in memory to record track data without GSM network
- Shock sensor for car shock and movement alarm
- Rechargeable Li battery with a battery life of 1-2 days
