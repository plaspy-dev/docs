# Carscop - CCTR-808

![CCTR-808](./logo.jpg)

The Carscop CCTR-808 is a compact, versatile GPS tracker designed for long term asset monitoring. Key hardware features include a solar panel with a large battery for extended standby, an IP56 rated water proof enclosure for outdoor use, and a strong magnetic pin for easy attachment to vehicles or equipment. The device also supports cell ID based locating to improve position reporting in challenging indoor or underground environments, and offers configurable upload intervals with SMS control for flexible tracking behavior.

As a model compatible with Plaspy, the CCTR-808 can be integrated into fleet and asset management workflows to provide consistent location visibility and operational oversight. Its combination of extended standby, weather resistance, and magnetic mounting makes it a practical option for low maintenance deployments, while cell ID fallback and configurable reporting help maintain useful location data when GPS signals are limited. Plaspy users can add devices like the CCTR-808 to their monitoring setup to centralize tracking, reporting, and alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Solar assisted power and a large onboard battery for extended standby and reduced maintenance.
- IP56 water proof design suitable for outdoor and exposed environments.
- Extra strong magnetic pin for simple and secure mounting on vehicles and equipment.
- Cell ID locate function provides fallback location information in indoor or underground areas.
- Configurable upload interval and SMS controls allow flexible reporting strategies.
- No platform service charge included with the device, reducing ongoing tracking costs.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the CCTR-808 provides location and availability information that can be viewed alongside other devices in a single fleet management platform. Plaspy can consume the tracker data to improve operational awareness and simplify management of distributed assets.

- Display real time and recent location history of CCTR-808 units on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility.
- Use configurable upload intervals from the device to balance reporting frequency with battery life for different operational needs.
- Rely on the cell ID locate feature as a fallback in areas with limited GPS reception and visualize fallback locations within Plaspy.
- Group and monitor multiple CCTR-808 trackers for centralized oversight of vehicles, containers, or remote equipment.
- Generate location reports and summary exports in Plaspy to support operational reviews and decision making.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers, containers, or parked vehicles where low maintenance is a priority.

- Assets that may enter indoor or underground areas where cell ID fallback improves traceability.
- Temporary or reusable deployment where magnetic mounting speeds installation and removal.
- Fleet visibility for organizations that prefer configurable reporting intervals to extend battery life.
