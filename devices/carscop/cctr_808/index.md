# Carscop - CCTR-808

![CCTR-808](./logo.jpg)

The Carscop CCTR-808 GPS Tracker is a versatile and reliable tracking device that offers a range of features to meet your tracking needs. With its solar panel and big battery, this tracker can provide extended standby time, making it ideal for long-term tracking applications. The IP56 water-proof design ensures that the tracker can withstand harsh weather conditions, making it suitable for outdoor use. Additionally, the extra strong magnetic pin allows for easy and secure attachment to vehicles or other objects.

One of the standout features of the CCTR-808 is its cell ID locate function, which allows for accurate location tracking even in indoor or underground environments. This feature provides a link to a cell ID location map, ensuring that you can always keep track of your assets. The tracker also offers the convenience of no platform service charge, allowing you to track your assets without any additional fees. With the ability to set the upload interval and control the upload via SMS, the CCTR-808 provides flexibility and control over your tracking operations.

Whether you need to track vehicles, containers, or other moving objects, the Carscop CCTR-808 GPS Tracker is a reliable and versatile solution. With its solar panel, big battery, and waterproof design, this tracker can provide extended standby time and withstand harsh weather conditions. The cell ID locate function ensures accurate tracking even in indoor or underground environments, while the extra strong magnetic pin allows for easy attachment. With no platform service charge and the ability to set the upload interval, the CCTR-808 offers flexibility and control over your tracking operations.
