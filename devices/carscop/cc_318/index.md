# Carscop - CC-318

<div className="device-logo">
![CC-318](./tracker.JPG)
</div>

The CC-318 is a compact vehicle tracker designed for car rental, car sharing and fleet management environments. Built to provide continuous position reporting and remote vehicle control, the CC-318 combines GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity and vehicle control outputs to support real-time tracking, remote immobilization and basic anti-theft functions. The device also includes an internal backup battery and a power down alarm to help maintain visibility when main power is interrupted.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CC-318 can feed location, alarm and control events into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational workflows. Its combination of position reporting, security inputs and remote outputs makes it a practical option for operators who want to manage vehicle fleets, rental pools or shared mobility services from Plaspy while retaining the ability to act remotely on individual vehicles.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for fleet, rental and car sharing use with continuous location reporting and history.
- Remote control features such as lock/unlock, light or horn activation and on demand engine cut for rental and security workflows.
- GNSS positioning with assistance and cell based fallback to maintain location visibility in varied signal conditions.
- Rechargeable internal backup battery and power down alarm to detect tampering or main power loss.
- Alarm inputs for events such as door, shock and ignition to generate security and driver behavior alerts.
- Onboard logging for offline track retention and configurable upload intervals to preserve continuity.
- Optional expansions to extend telemetry and vehicle data where additional modules are fitted.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the CC-318 reports location updates, security input changes and device events so fleet managers and operators can monitor assets in real time, receive alerts, and trigger remote workflows. Plaspy ingests the device data for mapping, notifications and historical reporting, enabling operational oversight across a vehicle fleet or rental pool.

- Real time location and historical tracking available in the Plaspy platform for fleet visibility.
- Security and alarm events such as door, shock and power down are forwarded to Plaspy as alerts.
- Remote immobilizer and control outputs can be used from Plaspy to support rental returns and theft response.
- Onboard logging and configurable upload intervals help maintain tracks during intermittent connectivity.
- Optional upgrades provide expanded telemetry that Plaspy can include in reports and dashboards.
- Configurable alerts, geofencing and reporting in Plaspy turn device events into operational workflows.

## Typical Use Cases
