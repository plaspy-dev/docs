# Carscop - CCTR-800 Features

This page provides a public overview of the Carscop CCTR-800 features and how the device can be used with Plaspy for tracking, monitoring, and operational visibility. It focuses on practical, user facing capabilities and explains how those capabilities are reflected when the device reports into the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For detailed device settings, firmware notes, and the most current specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and the device firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The CCTR-800 is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for discreet installation and extended field use. It combines multiple location methods with long battery life and simple remote configuration to support mobile asset tracking where ease of placement and reliable uptime are important.

- Compact form factor for discreet placement and hidden installation
- Strong magnetic attachment for secure mounting to metal surfaces
- Large Li battery with extended standby time to reduce recharging frequency
- Dual locate modes including GPS and LBS base station locate for improved coverage
- SMS and call based locate options plus SMS location messages with Google map links
- IP56 rating to tolerate outdoor exposure to water spray and dust

## Core Features of Carscop - CCTR-800

- Portable and compact design suitable for hidden placement on vehicles and equipment
- Powerful magnetic mounting for fast and stable attachment to metal surfaces
- Large 5200 mAh Li battery offering long standby time as described by the manufacturer
- IP56 water proof rating for outdoor use where splashes and dust are a concern
- GPS position reporting complemented by LBS GSM base station locate when GPS signal is weak
- Locate by SMS or by calling the device without requiring server connectivity
- SMS location messages that can include a Google map link or text description
- Remote configuration and GPRS air upgrade support via online platform or SMS

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and interprets the location reports and events sent by the CCTR-800 so fleet managers and users can view device status and historical movement in one interface. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and surfaces the device reports in maps, lists, and event logs.

- Location points and LBS fallbacks are shown on the Plaspy map to maintain visibility in mixed coverage areas
- SMS and call based locate methods are complementary; where the device sends server reports, Plaspy records and archives the incoming positions
- Device configuration settings that support remote APN and GPRS provisioning can be managed from the device side and reflected in Plaspy once reporting is established
- Geofence events configured on the device (for example enter or break alarms) can be presented in Plaspy as events and notifications
- Long battery standby behavior affects reporting intervals and uptime shown in Plaspy device status indicators
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol for easier setup; the CCTR-800 may be configured to report to a Plaspy server endpoint using standard TCP or UDP transport as supported by the device

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet tracking of rental cars and shared vehicles where hidden mounting and long battery life are valuable
- Portable monitoring of moving equipment that needs secure temporary attachment to metal surfaces
- Personal or asset tracking where compact size and SMS locate options provide flexible retrieval methods
- Transportation and logistics use where LBS fallback helps maintain position awareness in urban canyons or indoor edges
- Field deployments requiring extended standby time between charges to reduce maintenance frequency

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version differences can change available settings, supported SMS commands, and battery management behavior
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may alter certifications, network behavior, or accessory support
- Some features depend on how the device is installed and powered; battery performance will vary with reporting frequency and environmental conditions
- Manufacturer platform features such as free tracking service, mobile app locate, WeChat locate, and historical data retention are provided by Carscop and may differ from third party platform policies
- Always review the device configuration and test it in your intended deployment scenario before relying on a particular feature
