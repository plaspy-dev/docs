# Pointer - Cello Track 3Y Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Pointer Cello Track 3Y family with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect when registering and monitoring these devices in Plaspy, and how the device family is typically applied for mobile and fixed asset tracking.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation for each family member. Where appropriate, this page notes family differences such as the power monitoring capability in the Power variant and the compact design of the Lighter variant.

## Feature Overview

The CelloTrack 3Y family is intended for long term asset tracking in a range of environments. Below are the core practical capabilities that make these devices useful for operational tracking and asset oversight.

- Continuous location tracking and monitoring for mobile and fixed assets to improve visibility and asset management.
- Long life battery designed to support multi year operation typically around three years under expected usage patterns.
- IP67 rated weatherproof casing for protection against dust and water in outdoor or harsh environments.
- CelloTrack Power 3Y provides power consumption monitoring for assets where power usage data helps operations or diagnostics.
- CelloTrack Lighter 3Y offers a compact lightweight form factor suited to small or discreet asset installations.

## Core Features of Pointer - Cello Track 3Y

- Precise location reporting intended for continuous asset tracking and recovery workflows.
- Long life battery architecture targeted at multi year deployments without frequent maintenance.
- Durable IP67 weatherproof enclosure for outdoor resilience and reliable field operation.
- Distinct family variants to match use cases, including a power monitoring model and a compact lighter model.
- Designed for both mobile and fixed asset use cases to support a variety of operational needs.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and device status data from compatible trackers and presents it via maps, timelines, and device lists to help teams maintain operational awareness. The CelloTrack 3Y family’s long life battery and environmental protection reduce maintenance and support cleaner long term reporting in Plaspy.

- Live and recent location visibility on the Plaspy map to track asset position and movement.
- Historical location playback and event history for operational review and audit.
- Device presence and basic health indicators in Plaspy to help schedule maintenance for long life battery devices.
- Power consumption telemetry from the CelloTrack Power 3Y can be surfaced in Plaspy to support energy monitoring workflows when the device is configured to report that data.
- Compact devices such as the Lighter model are easy to assign to small assets in Plaspy asset inventories.

Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and supports standard tracker configuration flows, making initial device onboarding straightforward for most Pointer CelloTrack units.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers, containers, and non powered equipment where infrequent maintenance is preferred.
- Monitoring outdoor assets exposed to weather and rough conditions thanks to IP67 protection.
- Energy and power usage monitoring for assets instrumented with the CelloTrack Power 3Y.
- Discreet tracking of small items and portable equipment using the compact Lighter model.
- Asset inventory and location reconciliation for logistics and field service operations.
