# Pointer - Cello Track 3Y

![Cello Track 3Y](./logo.jpg)

The Cello Track 3Y is part of the CelloTrack 3Y Family from Pointer, a line of adaptable tracking devices intended for mobile and fixed asset management. The family includes the standard CelloTrack 3Y, the CelloTrack Power 3Y for monitoring power consumption, and the compact CelloTrack Lighter 3Y for smaller items. These devices provide continuous tracking and monitoring of asset location and are designed to support extended deployments with a durable battery life and weather resistant casing.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the Cello Track 3Y family is relevant for customers who need reliable location visibility in a fleet or asset management platform. Its long life battery and IP67 casing make it suitable for long term and outdoor deployments, while the family variants give organizations options for monitoring power or tracking smaller assets within the Plaspy environment.

## Key Highlights

- Part of the CelloTrack 3Y Family offering standard, power monitoring, and compact variants
- Approximately three year battery life for extended deployments
- IP67 weatherproof casing for reliable outdoor and harsh condition performance
- Continuous tracking and monitoring of precise asset location
- Compact and discreet Lighter 3Y option suited to smaller assets and covert placement
- Power monitoring variant available to observe power consumption trends for monitored equipment

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Cello Track 3Y provides asset location data and device status that feed into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools, enabling operational visibility and oversight across fleets and distributed equipment. Plaspy can incorporate the tracker data to support monitoring, alerts, and historical analysis without changing the device hardware.

- Location tracking and live visibility of assets within Plaspy monitoring views
- Fleet level monitoring and grouping for organized operational oversight
- Configurable alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on movement or geofence activity
- Historical reporting and audit trails for asset movements and deployments
- Use of the Power 3Y variant data to correlate power usage patterns with operational events

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers, containers, and parked equipment where battery life is essential
- Outdoor asset tracking that requires weather resistant devices for rugged environments
- Monitoring high value or frequently moved fixed assets in warehouses or yards
- Tracking small or portable items with the Lighter 3Y for discreet asset management
- Combining location and power consumption monitoring for equipment health and utilization

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Cello Track 3Y family offers a practical balance of durability and longevity that fits common Plaspy implementations for asset and fleet tracking. Organizations that prioritize extended battery life and environmental resistance will find the standard unit useful, while the Power and Lighter variants add flexibility for power aware monitoring or compact placements. Using these devices with Plaspy helps centralize location data and simplifies operational oversight across different asset types.

Compatibility with Plaspy makes the Cello Track 3Y family a straightforward choice for customers who want dependable location visibility integrated into a fleet management platform.
