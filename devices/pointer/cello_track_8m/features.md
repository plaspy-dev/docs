# Pointer - Cello Track 8M Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Pointer Cello Track 8M family and how these devices operate when used with Plaspy. It is written to help fleet managers, asset owners, and integrators understand the practical capabilities of the CelloTrack 8M models and how those capabilities map to monitoring and reporting in Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, firmware details, or hardware differences consult official Pointer documentation and the device release notes.

## Feature Overview

The CelloTrack 8M family is positioned as a cost effective entry level tracking solution for assets that require long interval reporting and extended battery runtime. It combines basic GPS location reporting and GPRS communication with a rechargeable battery design to deliver long deployment life between charges for low duty cycle use.

- Up to eight months of single daily GPS reading with GPRS transmission based on the included 2AH rechargeable battery specification.
- Up to two months of four daily GPS readings and transmissions using the same 2AH battery depending on configuration.
- Family variants include the standard CelloTrack 8M, CelloTrack Power 8M, and CelloTrack Lighter 8M to suit different tracking needs.
- Core functions include GPS location reporting, routine communication over mobile networks, and maintenance oriented features comparable to Pointer CelloTrack 3Y.
- Visual distinction with a white label that differentiates the 8M family from other CelloTrack models.

## Core Features of Pointer - Cello Track 8M

- Cost effective design for basic asset tracking and monitoring.
- 2AH rechargeable battery that supports long interval operation claims supplied by the manufacturer.
- Multiple product variants within the family to match deployment needs.
- GPS based location reporting for tracking vehicle and equipment positions.
- GPRS communication for sending periodic location updates to a server.
- Maintenance and operational features aligned with Pointer product practices similar to the CelloTrack 3Y family.
- White label appearance that identifies this entry level offering.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the regular location and communication updates sent by the CelloTrack 8M family and presents them in a centralized interface for monitoring and historical review. Plaspy is designed to detect supported tracker protocols and show incoming data without requiring extra manual protocol selection.

- Plaspy displays the tracker location history and recent position reports sent via GPRS.
- Device identity and family variant information can be recorded in Plaspy for inventory and management.
- Long interval reporting patterns from the tracker are visible in Plaspy as periodic updates and can be used for operational planning.
- Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol and integrates incoming messages into the platform workflow.
- Maintenance related events and status sent by the device appear in Plaspy logs if the device transmits them.

## Typical Use Cases

- Periodic location checks for trailers, equipment, or non powered assets where daily or multi daily reporting is sufficient.
- Long term deployments where minimizing charge cycles is important and occasional GPS updates are acceptable.
- Cost sensitive fleet and asset tracking projects that require reliable basic tracking without advanced telemetry.
- Rental and seasonal equipment tracking where extended battery life reduces maintenance visits.
- Asset inventory and location reconciliation across dispersed sites.
