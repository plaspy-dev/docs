# Pointer - Cello Track XT Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive feature overview of the Pointer Cello Track XT family and how these trackers are used with Plaspy for location visibility and operational monitoring. It is intended to explain practical capabilities, deployment value, and what Plaspy users can expect when tracking compatible devices.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use the summary below as a practical guide and consult the manufacturer documentation for device specific technical details and the latest firmware behavior.

## Feature Overview

The Cello Track XT family is engineered for asset tracking in extreme environmental conditions and is compatible with Plaspy for centralized tracking and event visibility. These units focus on durability, long deployment intervals, and predictable behavior by sharing firmware and platform characteristics with Pointer's CelloTrack 3Y family.

- Designed for operation in extreme climates including arctic and desert environments
- Wide operating temperature range from negative 30 degrees to positive 70 degrees Celsius for dependable field use
- Charging supported across a broad temperature range to enable reliable recharging in challenging conditions
- Extended operational lifetime between charges with multi month reporting profiles using a 4.25 Ah rechargeable Li Poly battery
- Family includes both Cello Track XT and Cello Track Power XT with similar firmware and platform behavior for easier fleet standardization

## Core Features of Pointer - Cello Track XT

- Purpose built rugged design for extreme weather asset tracking
- Operational temperature rating down to -30°C and up to +70°C
- Charging temperature window from -10°C to +60°C
- Up to thirteen months of operation using a single daily GPS reading and GPRS transmission profile, depending on use pattern
- Rechargeable 4.25 Ah Li Poly battery for extended deployments
- Part of the CelloTrack XT family which shares firmware and platform level behavior with the CelloTrack 3Y family
- Visual distinction from the standard family via a yellow label for easier identification in mixed fleets

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and displays location and event reports from compatible trackers, letting users monitor assets in challenging environments and long duration deployments. The Cello Track XT family’s long life and rugged design help maintain visibility in remote or harsh conditions when paired with Plaspy.

- Regular location and GPRS transmission events are surfaced in Plaspy for map visualization and historical playback
- Devices in Plaspy are automatically detected for protocol handling which simplifies onboarding of Cello Track XT units
- Trackers can be configured to report to Plaspy endpoints using standard TCP or UDP transport settings where supported
- Long battery life profiles reduce the need for frequent maintenance while still allowing Plaspy to provide timely status and location updates
- Shared firmware characteristics with the CelloTrack 3Y family mean similar behavior and fewer surprises when managing mixed fleets in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Remote asset monitoring in arctic, desert, and otherwise extreme environments
- Long term equipment tracking where infrequent reporting and long battery life are required
- Fleet and asset standardization where multiple Pointer models share firmware and operational practices
- Seasonal equipment staging and monitoring across harsh temperature ranges
- Industrial logistics and heavy equipment tracking in remote worksites

## Feature Availability Notes

- Exact operating and charging temperature behavior depends on the specific hardware revision and firmware installed
- Report intervals, battery life, and payload counts vary with configuration and real world usage patterns
- Some features may differ between the Cello Track XT and the Cello Track Power XT model; review model labeling and documentation
- Regional product variants or installation method can affect behavior and approved operating ranges
- Always verify firmware version and release notes for feature additions or changes before wide deployment

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with Pointer Cello Track XT devices gives organizations centralized visibility and simplified device management for assets operating in demanding climates. Plaspy’s platform is designed to ingest and display location and transmission events so teams can monitor remote assets, track long duration deployments, and maintain operational oversight without frequent site visits.
