# Pointer - Cello Track XT

![Cello Track XT](./logo.jpg)

The CelloTrack XT Family by Pointer is specifically designed for asset tracking in extreme weather conditions. Whether you're operating in arctic or desert environments, these trackers are built to withstand the harshest conditions. The family includes two products: the CelloTrack XT and the CelloTrack Power XT.

One of the standout features of the CelloTrack XT Family is its wide operational temperature range. These trackers can handle temperatures as low as -30°C and as high as +70°C, making them suitable for use in even the most extreme climates. Additionally, they have a wide charging temperature range of -10°C to +60°C, ensuring that they can be charged and ready to go no matter the conditions.

With up to thirteen months of single GPS reading and GPRS transmissions per day, the CelloTrack XT Family offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. These trackers are powered by a 4.25AH rechargeable Li-Poly battery, providing long-lasting performance. Despite their rugged design and extreme weather capabilities, the CelloTrack XT Family maintains the same set of capabilities as the standard CelloTrack 3Y family, including the same firmware and almost identical PL. The only visual difference is the use of a yellow label, distinguishing them from the standard CelloTrack 3Y family.
