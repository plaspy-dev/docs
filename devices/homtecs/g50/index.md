# Homtecs - G50

![G50](./logo.jpg)

The Homtecs G50 High Speed 4G LTE Rugged Router is a versatile and reliable internet of things wireless communication router. It supports 5 modes and 13 bands or 7 modes and 15 bands of mobile broadband networks, providing convenient and high-speed network transmission. This router is equipped with an industrial-grade ARM dual-core communication processor and an embedded real-time operating system, ensuring safe, stable, and high-speed 3G/4G wireless network connectivity. It also offers various interfaces including 4 gigabit Ethernet ports, RS-485/232, and 2.4G/5G WIFI, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.

The G50 router features an industrial design with a plate metal CRS shell for excellent heat dissipation and a high degree of protection \(IP34, extendable to IP51\). It supports low power consumption modes such as sleep mode, timing online and offline mode, and timing switch on and off mode. With its network characteristics, the router provides port flow detection, real-time monitoring, multiple VPN protocols, serial port DTU function, and support for dialing as required \(audio, SMS, data trigger\) and UPnP.

In terms of stability, the G50 router is equipped with soft and hardware watchdogs, multilevel link detection, and fault automatic diagnosis and recovery capabilities, ensuring stable and safe operation. It also has multiple mechanisms for equipment self-checking and ESD protection for each port to prevent static shock. The router features a wireless network signal indicator light \(NET\) for easy monitoring. Additionally, the G50 router offers remote management capabilities, including remote parameter configuration, backup, reboot, log query, upgrading, and online monitoring, providing convenience and flexibility for remote operations.
