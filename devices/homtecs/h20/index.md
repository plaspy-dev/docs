# Homtecs - H20

![H20](./logo.jpg)

The HomtecsM2M H20 3G Router is a versatile and reliable internet of things wireless communication router. It utilizes HSPA+ mobile broadband network technology to provide fast and convenient transmission capabilities. With support for existing 3G networks, this router offers high-speed connectivity with HSPA+ speeds of up to 21.6Mbps, as well as standard HSPA, EDGE, and GPRS connectivity options.

Designed with industrial applications in mind, the HomtecsM2M H20 3G Router is compact, rugged, and packed with features. It is an ideal solution for various industries such as M2M, CCTV, security, media, energy, utilities, retail, and transportation, where reliable IP connectivity between devices and hosts is crucial. This router can be used with fixed IP SIMs or regular data SIMs and supports Dynamic DNS for seamless connectivity across different operator networks.

The HomtecsM2M H20 3G Router is built to stay connected and online, making it perfect for remote site communications, particularly in applications like CCTV and telemetry. Its robust design ensures resistance to electromagnetic interference, heat endurance, and radiation ability. Additionally, it offers multiple VPN and network protocol support, as well as various functions like DHCP, DNS, firewall, NAT, and more. With its high data speed, strong connectivity, and advanced features, the HomtecsM2M H20 3G Router provides a reliable and secure communications solution for a wide range of industrial applications.

### Outstanding Features:

- Industrial robust design and compact metal housing
- High data speed via 3G network, backward compatible with 2.5G
- Strong electromagnetic interference resistance, heat endurance, and radiation ability
- Support for multiple VPN and network protocols
- Built-in watch dog and multi-link detection for stable and secure operation
- 802.11b/g/n Wi-Fi support with up to 21Mbps data rate
- 1 GSM antenna and 1 Wi-Fi antenna included
- 1 LAN port and 1 WAN port for flexible connectivity options
- Always online with automatic reboot feature
- Support for APN and VPDN wireless private network access
- Easy configuration through web, CLI, SSH, and management platform
- Shock and vibration resistant for rugged environments
