# Supermate - K20

![K20](./logo.jpg)

The Supermate K20 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for a wide range of tracking needs. It provides continuous location monitoring and is positioned for discreet placement on vehicles, equipment, or personal items. The K20 emphasizes portability and stealth while offering features such as live tracking, geo fencing, and an SOS alert function to support safety and recovery use cases.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the K20 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet and asset management environment to provide real time visibility and event notifications. This compatibility makes the K20 a practical option for organizations and individuals who want to combine a small form factor tracker with Plaspy's monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight tools.

## Key Highlights

- Lightweight and compact design suited for discreet asset placement
- Real time location tracking for continuous visibility
- Geo fencing capability with boundary alerts to monitor movement
- Built in SOS alert for instant emergency notifications
- Designed to operate across varied environmental conditions
- Optimized for broad GSM band coverage and high GPS sensitivity

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Supermate K20 feeds location and event information into Plaspy's monitoring platform so teams can view, analyze, and act on tracking data. Plaspy adds a central layer for organizing devices, configuring alerts, and generating operational reports.

- Live location display on Plaspy dashboards for active monitoring
- Geo fence events create notifications and can be used to trigger operational rules
- SOS alerts forwarded to Plaspy so responders or supervisors can be notified quickly
- Historical location data available for route review and incident investigation
- Fleet level visibility and grouping to monitor multiple K20 devices together

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and route oversight for small to medium fleets
- Portable asset protection for rental equipment or tools
- Personal safety monitoring for lone workers or family members
- Theft recovery and location verification for high value items
- Logistics and short term asset assignment tracking

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Supermate K20 pairs a discreet physical profile with the real time and alert features that matter to operations teams. For organizations using Plaspy, the K20 offers a straightforward way to extend visibility to mobile assets without adding bulk or complexity. Its environmental resilience and sensitivity help maintain consistent tracking in varied conditions, which complements Plaspy's focus on reliable monitoring and reporting.

Because the K20 is compatible with Plaspy, it can be incorporated into established workflows for alerts, historical review, and fleet oversight while keeping device management relatively simple.
