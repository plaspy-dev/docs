# Supermate - D11-G

<div className="device-logo">
![D11-G](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Supermate D11-G is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for general asset management and security applications. Built for discreet placement and portability, the D11-G is suitable for tracking vehicles, personal belongings, equipment, or individuals. Its core features include real time location reporting, geo fencing to define virtual boundaries, and an emergency SOS alert function for critical situations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D11-G can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet and asset monitoring workflows to provide continuous visibility and operational oversight. Compatibility makes it straightforward to bring the D11-G into a centralized tracking environment where location history, alerting, and reporting are managed alongside other devices on the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking for continuous position visibility and monitoring
- Geo fencing capability to receive alerts when assets enter or exit defined areas
- Emergency SOS button for immediate alerting in urgent situations
- Lightweight and compact design for discreet and portable placement
- Designed for global use with broad network band support and wide temperature tolerance
- Durable construction for everyday use and compliance with common international standards

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Supermate D11-G provides live location updates and event alerts that feed into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools. This integration helps operators monitor assets at scale, set automated notifications, and analyze movement patterns without needing separate vendor software.

- Centralized visibility of D11-G device locations from within Plaspy
- Receive geo fence alerts and SOS notifications routed through Plaspy alerting systems
- Use Plaspy reporting to review historical location data and movement trends
- Group and manage multiple D11-G units for fleet level oversight and operational coordination
- Configure notification preferences in Plaspy to match operational workflows and stakeholders

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location monitoring and oversight
- Tracking high value portable assets during transit or storage
- Personal safety monitoring for vulnerable individuals with SOS support
- Rental equipment management and location reconciliation
- General asset inventory visibility across multiple sites or regions
