# Supermate - D26-T

![D26-T](./logo.jpg)

The Supermate D26-T is a compact, lightweight GPS tracker designed for a wide range of tracking needs. Suited for personal, commercial, and industrial use, the D26-T emphasizes portability and discreet placement while providing continuous location monitoring. Its feature set described by the manufacturer highlights real time tracking, geo fencing, an emergency SOS button, and construction meant to handle everyday wear and a broad range of climates.

As a model compatible with Plaspy, the D26-T can be integrated into a centralized tracking platform to combine its on device features with Plaspy's visibility and reporting tools. That compatibility makes the D26-T a practical option for teams that want straightforward device deployment together with fleet-level monitoring, alerts, and historical location data provided by Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight form factor for discreet placement on vehicles or assets  
- Real time tracking capability for continuous location visibility  
- Geo fencing support to notify when assets enter or exit defined areas  
- Emergency SOS button for urgent alerting and rapid awareness  
- User friendly installation that suits non technical deployment needs  
- Durable design and support for wide operating conditions to handle daily use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Supermate D26-T sends location updates and event notifications to the Plaspy platform where administrators and operators can view, manage, and report on device activity. Integration with Plaspy brings device data into a unified interface for operational oversight and archived records.

- Live location display and map tracking within Plaspy for active monitoring  
- Geo fence configuration and alert routing to notify teams when boundaries are crossed  
- SOS event forwarding so emergency alerts are visible to designated responders  
- Fleet grouping and status views for multi vehicle or multi asset oversight  
- Historical playback and basic reporting to review past movements and incidents

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and route oversight for small to medium fleets  
- Asset monitoring for portable or high value equipment in transit or storage  
- Personal safety tracking for lone workers or family members using the SOS feature  
- Temporary deployments for event logistics or site monitoring where discreet placement matters  
- Mixed operational environments that require a rugged yet compact tracker
