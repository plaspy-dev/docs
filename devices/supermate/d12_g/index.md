# Supermate - D12-G

![D12-G](./logo.jpg)

The Supermate D12 GPS Tracker is the ultimate solution for asset management and security. Whether you need to protect personal valuables, manage a fleet of vehicles, or ensure the safety of loved ones, this tracker is designed to meet all your tracking needs. Its compact and lightweight design allows for discreet placement on any asset, seamlessly integrating into your daily operations. Installation is quick and easy, making it accessible to users of all technical levels.

One of the standout features of the Supermate D12 GPS Tracker is its real-time tracking capability. You can receive live location updates, allowing you to maintain constant surveillance of your assets. Additionally, the tracker offers geo-fencing functionality, enabling you to set up geographic boundaries and receive alerts when your assets enter or leave these areas. This feature is particularly useful for fleet management or keeping an eye on personal belongings.

In case of emergencies, the Supermate D12 GPS Tracker is equipped with an SOS button. With just a press of a button, you can instantly send an alert, providing peace of mind in urgent situations. The tracker is also versatile in its use, making it equally effective for tracking vehicles, personal belongings, or even loved ones.

The Supermate D12 GPS Tracker is built to withstand diverse environmental conditions, with high GPS sensitivity for precise location tracking even in densely built-up areas. It is designed to be durable and meets international standards for safety and efficiency. With its adaptable power input and support for various frequency bands, this tracker offers reliable global usage and comprehensive GSM network coverage.

Whether you need to protect your assets for personal or professional reasons, the Supermate D12 GPS Tracker is an essential tool. With its advanced security features and dependable location monitoring, you can keep what matters most under vigilant supervision, no matter where you are in the world.
