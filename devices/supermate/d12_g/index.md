# Supermate - D12-G

<div className="device-logo">
![D12-G](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Supermate D12-G is a compact GPS tracker designed for asset management and personal security. It is intended for discreet placement on vehicles, equipment, or valuables and aims to provide continuous location visibility. The device emphasizes ease of use with quick installation and a lightweight form factor, making it suitable for a wide range of operational environments and simple day to day deployment.

As a tracker compatible with Plaspy, the D12-G can feed location updates and event signals into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. That compatibility makes it a practical option for organizations that want to combine the D12-G hardware with Plaspy software features such as real time map views, alerting and operational oversight without requiring complex configuration from end users.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design for discreet placement on assets and vehicles
- Real time location updates to support continuous tracking and visibility
- Geo fencing support to trigger alerts when an asset enters or leaves defined areas
- Integrated SOS button to notify responders or supervisors in urgent situations
- High GPS sensitivity and rugged construction for reliable positioning in varied environments
- Adaptable power input and broad operational coverage for use across different regions
- Simple installation suitable for users with varying technical experience

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Supermate D12-G transmits location and event information that Plaspy can present through its dashboards and monitoring tools. Once the device is added to a Plaspy account, teams can use platform features to turn raw device signals into operational insights for fleet and asset management.

- View live location on Plaspy maps to monitor assets in near real time
- Configure geo fences in Plaspy and receive automated alerts when boundaries are crossed
- Use SOS alerts from the D12-G to trigger incident workflows and notify designated contacts
- Group and monitor assets in Plaspy for fleet level visibility and assignment tracking
- Access historical position data and generate reports for audits and operational review

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and oversight for routing and utilization monitoring
- Tracking of high value or portable equipment used across job sites
- Protection of personal valuables and luggage for individual users
- Safety monitoring for family members or caretaking scenarios
- Rental asset tracking to monitor location and usage during lease periods

The Supermate D12-G offers a balance of compact design and essential tracking features that fit many operational needs. When paired with Plaspy, its real time updates, geo fencing and SOS capability can be turned into actionable alerts, reports and dashboards that support day to day fleet and asset management tasks.
