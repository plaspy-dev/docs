# Supermate - D11

<div className="device-logo">
![D11](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Supermate D11 is a compact GPS tracker designed for flexible asset management and personal security. Built for discreet placement, the D11 is suitable for use on vehicles, portable equipment, and personal belongings. The device emphasizes portability and durability while offering real time location tracking, geo fencing, and an emergency SOS alert feature to support ongoing monitoring and rapid response when needed.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D11 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet and asset management workflows to provide continuous visibility and actionable alerts. Its straightforward installation and broad operational design make it a practical choice for organizations and individuals who want to add reliable position reporting and boundary alerts to their monitoring setup using Plaspy's platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight form factor that supports discreet placement on a wide range of assets
- Real time location tracking to maintain continuous visibility of mobile items and personnel
- Geo fencing capability for defining zones and receiving entry and exit alerts
- Built in SOS alert function to notify stakeholders during emergencies
- Designed for global use with broad operational compatibility and robust environmental tolerance
- High GPS sensitivity for improved location performance in challenging urban environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Supermate D11 supplies the location and event information Plaspy uses to display asset position, trigger alerts, and feed reporting tools. Plaspy ingests the D11's updates and presents them within dashboards, maps, and notification channels so teams can monitor and manage assets from a single platform.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for real time tracking and operational oversight
- Geo fence alerts delivered through Plaspy notifications when assets cross defined boundaries
- SOS events routed into Plaspy workflows to escalate incidents and log responses
- Historical position data available for review and reporting inside Plaspy for audits and analysis
- Centralized visibility for mixed fleets and asset types when the D11 is deployed alongside other compatible trackers

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet visibility for small vehicle fleets where discreet trackers are needed
- Asset protection for portable equipment, tools, and high value items in transit
- Personal safety monitoring for lone workers or family members with emergency alert needs
- Logistics oversight for tracking movements and ensuring compliance with route plans
- Temporary deployments for short term projects where lightweight, easy to place tracking is required

The Supermate D11 pairs practical tracking features with a form factor that fits many real world monitoring scenarios.
