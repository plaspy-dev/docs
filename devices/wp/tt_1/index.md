# WP - TT-1

<div className="device-logo">
![TT-1](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The TT-1 by WP is a high performance GPS GSM GPRS trailer tracking device built for asset and fleet management. It combines a compact, concealment friendly enclosure with a high sensitivity GPS receiver and a rugged IP65 class casing to support outdoor operating environments. The device continues the lineage of WP models such as the VT10 and VT200 and is described as offering diversified location based services and long operation time suitable for trailers and other transported assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TT-1 brings those tracking capabilities into a fleet management platform that emphasizes visibility and operational oversight. Its multiple tracking modes, geo fencing control, roaming control, and reporting for mileage, battery level, idling, and emergency alarms make it a practical match for Plaspy users who need configurable tracking, alerts, and historical reporting for trailers and mobile assets.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity GPS receiver designed to improve location acquisition in varied environments
- Quad band GSM support for broad cellular reach in many regions
- Multiple tracking modes including time interval, distance interval, smart combination mode, and idle tracking
- Geo fencing control and roaming control for perimeter and movement monitoring
- Reports for mileage, battery level, idling, and emergency alarms to support operational insight
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and operating temperature range for outdoor use
- Compact form factor at 190 mm x 115 mm x 50 mm for easier concealment on trailers and assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TT-1 supplies location and status updates that Plaspy can present as live positions, alerts, and reports. Plaspy can use the device's configurable modes and reports to reduce data noise, trigger notifications, and generate operational summaries for fleet managers.

- Display live position and recent track history for each TT-1 equipped asset within Plaspy
- Map device tracking modes to Plaspy reporting intervals to balance visibility and data volume
- Trigger geo fence alerts and roaming notifications based on TT-1 perimeter settings
- Receive mileage, idling, battery level, and emergency alarm reports inside Plaspy dashboards
- Use historical replay and exportable reports to analyze trips and utilization over time

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer visibility for logistics and freight operators requiring concealed, rugged trackers
- Asset management for high value mobile equipment operating in outdoor environments
- Long haul and regional fleets that benefit from configurable tracking intervals and roaming control
- Rental fleets and equipment hire where mileage and idling reports support billing and maintenance
- Emergency alerting and oversight for dispersed assets in mixed operating conditions
