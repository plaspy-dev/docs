# WP - TT-1 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the WP TT-1 tracker with Plaspy. It highlights the TT-1’s practical capabilities for trailer and asset tracking and explains how those capabilities are reflected in Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a general features guide and consult the device manufacturer for model specific and up to date technical specifications.

## Feature Overview

The TT-1 is a compact GPS/GSM/GPRS trailer tracking device designed for outdoor use and long operation times. It emphasizes flexible tracking modes, location reporting, and remote status reports that are useful for asset and fleet management.

- Multiple tracking modes including time interval, distance interval, smart mode that can combine time distance and ignition conditions, and idle tracking modes for adaptive reporting.
- Geo-fencing and roaming control to help track entry and exit events and maintain operation across networks.
- Status and event reports such as mileage, battery level, idling, and emergency alarm reports to support operational visibility.
- Rugged hardware profile with IP65 enclosure, wide operating temperature range, and compact dimensions for concealed installation on trailers and assets.
- High sensitivity GPS reception and multi band GSM support for reliable positioning and cellular connectivity.

## Core Features of WP - TT-1

- High sensitivity GPS engine with 66 channels and industry level sensitivity for improved signal reception in challenging conditions.
- GSM support for 850 900 1800 1900 MHz bands for broad cellular compatibility.
- Multiple configurable tracking modes: time interval, distance interval, smart mode combining time distance and ignition conditions, and idle tracking mode.
- Geo-fencing control to define virtual boundaries and receive location based events.
- Roaming control to manage behavior across different cellular networks.
- Mileage reporting and periodic battery level reports for operational insight.
- Emergency alarm reporting and idling reports to support event driven monitoring.
- Rugged design including IP65 rated enclosure and a wide operating temperature range for outdoor deployment.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes the TT-1’s location and event data so operators can see position history, status, and alerts from a single platform. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and supports standard TCP and UDP reporting, simplifying device onboarding.

- Location updates and tracking mode behavior appear in Plaspy as position history and route playback so fleets can review movement and stops.
- Geo-fence events and roaming indicators are presented as configurable alerts and timeline events inside Plaspy for operational notifications.
- Mileage and battery level reports are shown in device status summaries and historical reports to assist maintenance planning and fleet reporting.
- Idling and emergency alarm reports are surfaced as alerts and can be reviewed in Plaspy’s event logs for quick situational awareness.
- Plaspy supports tracker reporting to its server domain and common reporting ports and will auto detect the tracker protocol to aid setup.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer fleet tracking where concealed installation and rugged mounting are required.
- Long duration asset monitoring that benefits from configurable idle and low frequency tracking modes.
- Geo-fence based location control for gate and yard entry monitoring.
- Remote battery and mileage visibility to plan maintenance and schedule servicing.
- Cross network roaming management for assets that travel through different cellular regions.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware differences between production batches can change available tracking modes and report behavior.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may affect supported GSM bands and enclosure options.
- Some features depend on correct installation and wiring, for example ignition condition based reporting used in smart mode.
- Battery performance and backup duration depend on usage patterns and are best validated under real world conditions.
- Always consult the manufacturer documentation for the most current technical limits and supported configurations.
