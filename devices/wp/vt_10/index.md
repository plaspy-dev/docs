# WP - VT-10

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-10](./logo..png)
</div>

The VT-10 from WP is a compact vehicle GPS tracking device designed for real-time tracking and monitoring. It pairs a high sensitivity GPS receiver with GSM communications to provide continuous location information, journey logging, and event reporting. The VT-10 supports multiple tracking modes such as timed intervals, distance intervals, and a smart mode for adaptive reporting, and can store a large history of waypoints for later review.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-10 can feed location and event data into Plaspy’s fleet management platform to deliver visibility and operational insights. Its built in reporting features — including geo fencing, mileage, and a variety of alerts — map directly to common Plaspy workflows for vehicle monitoring, incident notification, and historical reporting, making it a practical option for organizations already using or evaluating Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity GPS receiver designed to maintain a reliable fix even in challenging urban environments
- Flexible tracking modes including time interval, distance interval, and smart adaptive mode
- Large onboard journey logging with capacity for over 100,000 waypoints for detailed historical playback
- Built in reporting for geo fencing, mileage, custom events, and emergency alerts
- Event alerts for speeding, towing, and power low or power lost conditions
- Compact and lightweight form factor that is easy to place in vehicle installations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT-10 supplies position fixes and event reports that Plaspy ingests to provide live maps, history playback, and alerts to operations teams. Plaspy can unify VT-10 data with other devices in a fleet for centralized monitoring and reporting.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for live fleet visibility
- Historical journey playback using the device’s stored waypoints and Plaspy reporting tools
- Forwarding of device alerts such as geo fence triggers, speeding, towing, and power state events into Plaspy notification channels
- Aggregated mileage and route reporting to support fleet cost analysis and compliance tracking
- Configurable notifications and reports so operations teams receive only the events that matter
- Integrated asset oversight allowing Plaspy users to correlate VT-10 data with vehicle assignments and schedules

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch where continuous location and history improve routing and utilization
- Vehicle security and theft response using real time tracking and towing alerts
- Asset and vehicle monitoring for businesses that need detailed trip logs and mileage reporting
- Contractor or rental vehicle oversight where journey logs and geofence alerts support accountability
- Logistics and delivery operations that benefit from route history and operational reporting
