# WP - VT-10 Features

This page provides a public overview of the WP VT-10 tracker and how its user-facing features are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities, reporting behavior, and the kinds of monitoring and fleet workflows the VT-10 supports when paired with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. The descriptions here are based on the VT-10 product characteristics published by the manufacturer and are intended as a general feature reference for use with Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The VT-10 is positioned as a high sensitivity GPS/GSM/GPRS vehicle tracker intended for reliable real-time location and journey logging. It emphasizes robust satellite reception in challenging environments and flexible reporting options that suit fleet management and vehicle security workflows.

- High sensitivity GPS receiver designed to obtain a fix in urban canyon conditions for improved location reliability.
- Multiple tracking modes including time based, distance based, and a smart mode to balance update frequency with network usage.
- Journey logging with large onboard storage capacity for more than 100,000 waypoints to preserve movement history.
- Built in reports and alerts covering geo-fencing, mileage, user defined events, emergency alerts, speeding and towing detection.
- Support for GSM communication and common transfer methods including SMS and GPRS plus UDP and TCP connectivity.
- Power management features including DC input operation and an onboard backup battery to support continued reporting when primary power is interrupted.

## Core Features of WP - VT-10

- High sensitivity GPS module with a multi channel receiver for improved satellite tracking in obstructed areas.
- Configurable tracking modes: time interval, distance interval, and smart mode for adaptive reporting.
- Journey storage capable of holding over 100,000 waypoints for later retrieval and route analysis.
- Built in reporting functions such as geo-fencing events, mileage logs, and self defined event reports.
- Alerts for speeding, towing, emergency, and power low or power lost conditions.
- GSM quad band support and compatibility with SMS and GPRS data channels plus UDP and TCP transport.
- Power input designed for standard vehicle DC ranges with an integrated backup battery to maintain continuity.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, VT-10 data becomes part of a unified monitoring and reporting view. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding and to present tracker data consistently across the platform.

- Real time position updates appear on the Plaspy map when the VT-10 is configured to transmit location data.
- Journey logging stored on the device can be uploaded and represented in Plaspy as historical tracks and playback routes.
- Geo-fence, mileage, and self defined event reports from the VT-10 are reflected as events and notifications inside Plaspy dashboards.
- Alerts such as speeding, towing, and power loss are shown as actionable events so operators can review and respond.
- Configurable tracking intervals set on the VT-10 (time, distance, smart) determine update behavior that Plaspy will display and use for reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for tracking vehicle locations, routes, and mileage across an organization.
- Vehicle security and recovery workflows using real time monitoring and towing alerts.
- Long term journey logging for route analysis, compliance, or operational review.
- Rental and shared vehicle oversight with event and mileage reporting.
- Operations in urban or signal-challenged environments where high sensitivity GPS improves fix reliability.
- Situations requiring low power continuity where onboard backup battery and power alerts are useful.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Certain features and reporting behaviors depend on the VT-10 firmware version and manufacturer configuration.
- Hardware revisions and optional wiring during installation can affect availability of some alerts and power management signals.
- Regional GSM band support and cellular service provisioning can influence connectivity and data reporting via GPRS or SMS.
- Some capabilities require specific device configuration or activation of GPRS data on the installed SIM card.
- Always check the device settings and confirm behavior after installation to ensure alerts and reports appear as expected in Plaspy.
