# WP - M7 D2 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the WP M7 D2 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities that are relevant to location monitoring, event visibility, and operational oversight when the device reports into Plaspy.

Exact feature availability, reported telemetry, and on-device behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details or the latest firmware notes, consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The WP M7 D2 is a portable, magnet-mount GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset tracking. It combines high-sensitivity GPS positioning, quad-band GSM connectivity, and a large rechargeable battery in a compact, sealed enclosure to support long-term deployments and temporary installations.

- Real-time location reporting using SMS, GPRS, UDP, or TCP for flexible data transmission.
- Magnetic, compact housing and IP67 rated enclosure for quick mounting and outdoor use.
- Tamper or detached alert and built-in tampering switch to notify when the unit is removed.
- Motion reporting and a built-in acceleration sensor to detect movement and support route analysis.
- Long standby capability enabled by a high-capacity internal battery and power management settings.
- Voice call tracking option for location retrieval by phone in environments where data is limited.

## Core Features of WP - M7 D2

- High sensitivity GPS receiver with multi channel support for reliable position fixes.
- Quad band GSM GPRS communication for global cellular connectivity.
- Multiple data transmission options including SMS, GPRS, UDP, and TCP.
- Built-in tamper switch and detached alert for physical security notifications.
- Motion reporting enabled by a 3-axis acceleration sensor for movement detection.
- Large built-in rechargeable battery with extended standby capability and configurable power management.
- IP67 rated ABS enclosure with magnetic mounting for durable outdoor use and easy installation.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and interprets the WP M7 D2 reporting so that location, movement, and basic device events can be presented in a unified tracking view. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and surfaces the most relevant information for monitoring and reporting.

- Real-time position updates shown on Plaspy maps when the device transmits location data.
- Motion events and movement summaries available for route reconstruction and activity timelines.
- Tamper or detached alerts appear as events or notifications to support security monitoring.
- Battery status and power related alerts reflected in Plaspy device status to aid maintenance scheduling.
- Historical location playback and event logs that help analyze trips and device behaviour over time.

## Typical Use Cases

- Temporary or covert vehicle tracking using magnetic mounting for short term assignments.
- Fleet oversight where portable units need quick installation and reliable location reporting.
- Rental asset monitoring that benefits from detachable alerts and movement reporting.
- Remote asset tracking that requires long standby life and rugged weather protection.
- Situations where a mix of reporting methods (SMS and data) improves coverage flexibility.
