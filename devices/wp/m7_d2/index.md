# WP - M7 D2

![M7 D2](./logo..jpg)

The WP M7 D2 is a portable GPS tracking device that offers superior GPS and GSM/GPRS modules, making it an ideal solution for vehicle tracking applications. With its compact size and magnetic installation, it can be easily mounted or hidden on the metal part of a vehicle. This tracker is designed to be reliable, convenient, and flexible, providing accurate location information and advanced features for tracking purposes.

One of the standout features of the M7 D2 is its data transmission capability via SMS, GPRS, UDP, and TCP. This allows for real-time tracking and monitoring of the device. Additionally, it has a detached alert feature that notifies users when the device is removed from its mounting location, ensuring the security of the tracked vehicle. The motion report feature provides detailed information about the movement of the vehicle, allowing users to analyze and track its routes and activities.

Other notable features of the M7 D2 include roaming preference setting, low battery level alert, power management setting, tracking by voice call, and easy installation in seconds. It also boasts an impressive standby time of up to 3 years+, thanks to its built-in 11400 mAh Li-ion rechargeable battery. The device is housed in a durable ABS plastic enclosure with an IP67 rating, making it waterproof and suitable for outdoor use.

### Hardware Specifications:

- Dimensions \(L x W x H\): 102mm x 47mm x 68mm
- Weight \(net\): 420g
- Communication module: Quad-band, GSM 850/900/1800/1900 MHz
- GPS receiver: 66 channels, -165dBm high sensitivity tracking
- Memory: 4MB
- Back-up battery: Built-in 11400 mAh Li-ion rechargeable battery
- Unit charging: 5V DC, 900 mA
- Tampering switch: Built-in
- GPS antenna: Built-in
- GSM antenna: Built-in
- 3-axis acceleration sensor: Built-in
- Enclosure: ABS plastic, IP67 rated
- LED indicator: One LED with 3 colors for status indicating
- Mounting: Magnetic
