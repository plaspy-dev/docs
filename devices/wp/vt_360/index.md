# WP - VT-360

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-360](./logo..png)
</div>

The WP VT-360 is a compact vehicle tracking device that combines GPS and GLONASS positioning with 2G and 3G cellular connectivity to provide continuous real time tracking and monitoring. Designed for in-vehicle use, the VT-360 offers journey logging with the ability to store a large number of waypoints, ignition detection, and a range of alerting features aimed at improving operational visibility.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-360 can deliver location and status data into Plaspy for fleet management, reporting, and alerting workflows. Its reporting flexibility and support for over the air configuration make it a practical choice for organizations that need configurable tracking behavior together with the centralized monitoring and analytics Plaspy provides.

## Key Highlights

- Dual mode GPS and GLONASS positioning for reliable location data.
- 2G and 3G cellular connectivity for continuous real time reporting.
- Large journey logging capacity with support for over 100,000 waypoints.
- Ignition detection plus configurable alerts for speeding, towing, and input changes.
- Geo fencing and GSM jamming alerts to support operational security.
- Support for remote voice monitoring and two way voice communication and OTA device configuration.
- Compact and lightweight form factor for flexible installation in a range of vehicle types.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT-360 streams location and event information that Plaspy can ingest to provide live maps, historical routes, and event driven alerts. Once connected to Plaspy, the device feeds vehicle status and trip data into a central dashboard where operators can monitor assets, generate reports, and configure alerts.

- Live location and route playback displayed on Plaspy maps for real time visibility.
- Journey history and waypoint data used in Plaspy reports for mileage and trip analysis.
- Event alerts such as ignition on off, speeding, towing, and geo fence breaches routed to Plaspy notification channels.
- Device configuration and reporting cadence adjusted in the VT-360 and reflected in Plaspy reporting for consistent data flow.
- Alerts and summaries aggregated in Plaspy for operational oversight and compliance reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for logistics and delivery operations requiring continuous location visibility.
- Rental and shared vehicle monitoring where journey logging and ignition events are important.
- Field service vehicle oversight to optimize routes and verify visit histories.
- Asset security and recovery use cases leveraging geo fencing and anti jamming alerts.
- Business mileage and activity reporting using stored waypoint and trip data.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT-360 combines practical tracking features with flexible reporting and alert options that align well with Plaspy use cases. Its support for large journey logs and real time updates helps teams maintain a clear operational picture, while configurable alerts provide timely notifications for common fleet events. Because the device supports over the air configuration and voice features, it can adapt to evolving operational requirements without frequent hands on intervention.

If you need a compact tracker that can feed reliable location and event data into a fleet management platform, the VT-360 is a suitable option to consider alongside Plaspy.
