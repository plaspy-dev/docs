# WP - VT-360

![VT-360](./logo..png)

The WP VT-360 is a high-performance GPS+GLONASS/2G/3G vehicle tracking device designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities. With its compact dimensions of 115mm x 75mm x 32mm and lightweight design weighing only 130g, it is easy to install and conceal in any vehicle. 

One of the standout features of the VT-360 is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the location of your vehicle at any time. It also offers journey logging, which can store over 100,000 waypoints, providing a detailed record of the vehicle's movements. 

The ignition detection feature allows you to receive notifications when the vehicle's ignition is turned on or off, providing valuable information about its usage. Additionally, the VT-360 offers self-defined reporting, giving you the flexibility to customize the type and frequency of reports you receive.

In terms of hardware specifications, the VT-360 is equipped with a GPS+GLONASS dual-mode system, ensuring accurate and reliable positioning. It supports GSM communication bands \(2G\) and five bands UMTS/HSDPA \(3G\) for seamless connectivity. The device is powered by a DC 8-35V source and has a power consumption of 50mA in normal mode and less than 4mA in sleep mode.

The VT-360 also offers a range of additional features, including geo-fencing reports, GSM jamming alerts, mileage reports, towing alerts, speeding alerts, and input status changing alerts. It also supports voice wiretapping and two-way voice communication, allowing for easy communication with the vehicle. The device can be configured and upgraded over the air, providing convenience and flexibility in managing its settings.

Accessories and peripherals such as external GSM/GPS antennas, power/input/output cables, USB cables, microphones, loudspeakers, RFID readers and tags, LED displays, temperature sensors, and remote controllers are available to enhance the functionality of the VT-360.
