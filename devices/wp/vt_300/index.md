# WP - VT-300

![VT-300](./logo.jpg)

The WP VT-300 is a versatile GPS tracker designed specifically for vehicle tracking. It offers a wide range of communication options, including voice, CS data, SMS, GPRS UDP/TCP for 2G networks, and voice, SMS, Edge, UMTS, and HSDPA for 3G networks. This ensures reliable and efficient communication between the tracker and the user.

One of the standout features of the VT-300 is its expandable design for customization. It comes with a user-defined interface \(UDI\) that can be easily expanded to meet specific tracking needs. This allows users to tailor the tracker to their unique requirements, making it a highly flexible and adaptable solution.

In terms of tracking and logging, the VT-300 offers configurable modes based on time interval, distance interval, speeding changes, heading changes, and IG status. This allows for precise and accurate tracking, ensuring that users have access to real-time and detailed information about their vehicles.

Other notable features of the VT-300 include GPS/GSM antenna tampering alert, GSM jamming alert \(optional\), geo-fencing control, mileage reporting, power low/lost alert, towing away alert, speeding alert, power management, roaming preference settings, and internet surfing via USB connection. It also supports configuration and firmware upgrading over the air, making it easy to update and maintain the tracker remotely.

With its advanced features and customizable design, the WP VT-300 is an excellent choice for vehicle tracking applications. Whether you need to track a single vehicle or manage a fleet, this tracker offers the reliability, flexibility, and functionality you need to stay connected and in control.
