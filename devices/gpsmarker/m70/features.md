# GPSMarker - M70 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the GPSMarker M70 and explains how those capabilities relate to using the device with Plaspy. It is written to help fleet and asset managers, integrators, and technical evaluators understand the practical functions the M70 offers when connected to Plaspy for tracking and monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and the manufacturer's current implementation. For device specific configuration, commands, and the most current technical details consult the official GPSMarker documentation and firmware notes.

## Feature Overview

The GPSMarker M70 is presented as a versatile, low operating cost tracker with multiGNSS positioning and a set of sensors and user features aimed at vehicle and asset tracking. The device emphasizes long battery life, over the air firmware updates, and a no subscription fee model where ongoing costs are limited to messaging and data usage.

- No subscription fee model where ongoing cost is limited to outgoing SMS and GPRS traffic
- 99 channel GPS and GLONASS receiver for improved position reliability and accuracy
- Firmware update over GPRS so updates can be applied remotely without a local computer
- Long battery life with multi year operation and configurable wake intervals to extend service life
- Multiple built in sensors including movement detection, crash and impact sensors, and a revolution sensor
- Panic button SOS and simple natural command interface with Russian and English language support

## Core Features of GPSMarker - M70

- 99 channel GPS GLONASS receiver for enhanced satellite visibility and position fixes
- No subscription fee model; costs are tied to outgoing SMS and GPRS data consumption
- Over the air firmware update capability through GPRS for remote maintenance
- Long battery endurance designed for multi year operation with configurable wake schedule
- Automatic balance control and low battery notifications to help maintain continuity of service
- Movement detection, crash sensor, impact sensor, and revolution sensor for event awareness
- Panic button SOS for immediate alerting
- User friendly natural command system with Russian and English language support

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives position and event data from compatible trackers like the M70 and presents that information in dashboards, maps, and reports. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols, so M70 devices can be registered and start reporting without manual protocol selection in most deployments.

- Real time and periodic position updates from the M70 appear on Plaspy maps for location awareness
- Sensor events such as movement, impact or crash notifications can be surfaced as events or alerts inside Plaspy
- Low battery and balance notifications reported by the device can be displayed as device health indicators
- SOS panic button activations can be highlighted in Plaspy to aid rapid response and event tracking
- Over the air firmware updates remain a manufacturer operation; Plaspy can log firmware related events reported by the device

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle tracking for visibility of routes, stops, and position history
- Asset monitoring where long battery life and periodic wake schedules reduce maintenance frequency
- Remote equipment protection with movement and impact detection to detect tampering or incidents
- Workforce safety or lone worker scenarios using the SOS panic button for rapid alerting
- Fleet maintenance planning using low battery and balance notifications to schedule servicing
- Situations where low recurring operating cost is important due to the no subscription fee model
