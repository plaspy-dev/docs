# GPSMarker - M70

![M70](./logo.jpg)

The GPSMarker M70 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features and functionalities to meet your tracking needs. One of the standout features of this tracker is that it does not require a subscription fee. Instead, you only pay for outgoing SMS messages and GPRS traffic, making it a cost-effective option for long-term use. 

The M70 is equipped with a 99-channel GPS/GLONASS receiver, which significantly increases the reliability and accuracy of the coordinates. This ensures that you can track your assets or vehicles with precision. Additionally, the tracker comes with a complete thermo SIM card with a special tariff plan optimized for online monitoring systems, ensuring seamless connectivity and efficient data transmission.

With the M70, you can easily update the firmware via GPRS without the need to connect the device to a computer. This makes it convenient to keep your tracker up to date with the latest features and improvements. The tracker also features automatic balance control, which reminds you of the remaining balance in your account, ensuring that you never run out of credit unexpectedly.

In terms of battery life, the M70 offers impressive longevity. With a battery life of up to 3 years or more, you can set the tracker to "awaken" once a week, extending the battery life to 8 years. The tracker also includes automatic notification of low battery, so you'll always be aware of when it's time to replace the batteries.

The M70 is equipped with a range of sensors, including a sensor for detecting movement, a crash sensor, an impact sensor, and a revolution sensor. These sensors provide added security and allow you to monitor the status of your assets or vehicles. The tracker also features a panic button SOS, which enables you to quickly inform others about any impending danger.

With its user-friendly interface and natural command system, the M70 is easy to operate and does not require memorization of codes or digital sequences. It supports both Russian and English languages, allowing you to choose the language that is most convenient for you. The tracker also offers multiple display modes, including Google Maps, Yandex Maps, latitude and longitude, and distance and direction from the nearest town, giving you flexibility in how you view and interpret the tracking data.

Overall, the GPSMarker M70 is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that offers excellent value for money. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or personal belongings, this tracker provides the functionality and convenience you need to stay connected and in control.
