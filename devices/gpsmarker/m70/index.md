# GPSMarker - M70

![M70](./logo.jpg)

The GPSMarker M70 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for reliable long term tracking of vehicles and assets. It ships with a full set of features intended to reduce ongoing subscription costs by operating without a mandatory service fee, instead using outgoing SMS and GPRS traffic on a pay as you go basis. The device includes a high sensitivity 99 channel GPS GLONASS receiver, a prepared SIM tariff for online monitoring, and a range of built in sensors plus a panic SOS button for added security.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the M70 fits into monitoring and fleet management workflows where low operating cost and long battery life matter. Its ability to receive firmware updates over GPRS and to report sensor and status events makes it a practical choice for teams using Plaspy for real time visibility, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy users can incorporate the M70 into dashboards and operational workflows to improve oversight without changing familiar platform tools.

## Key Highlights

- No mandatory subscription fee; billing based on outgoing SMS and GPRS usage
- 99 channel GPS GLONASS receiver for reliable coordinate acquisition
- Supplied with an optimized SIM tariff for online monitoring systems
- Firmware updates available over GPRS for remote maintenance
- Long battery life with multi year operation depending on reporting interval
- Built in sensors including movement, crash, impact, revolution and SOS alerts
- User friendly interface with support for Russian and English and multiple display modes

## How It Works with Plaspy

The M70 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous positional awareness and event reporting for vehicles and tracked assets. Data transmitted by the tracker can appear in Plaspy for live monitoring, alarm configuration, and historical review, while the device features help reduce hands on maintenance.

- Real time location visibility on Plaspy maps for fleet and asset tracking
- Event alerts in Plaspy for SOS signals, impact or crash detection, and movement changes
- Battery and balance notifications can be monitored from Plaspy to manage uptime and credit
- Historical routes and location playback available for reporting and incident review
- Low data cost operation supports long term deployments under Plaspy monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for routine oversight and route review
- Remote asset monitoring where long battery life reduces maintenance visits
- Safety and panic alerting for lone workers or priority assets
- Trailer and container tracking during long distance transport
- Seasonal or intermittent tracking where pay as you go connectivity is preferred
