# GPSMarker - M60

![M60](./logo.jpg)

The GPSMarker M60 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for a range of tracking tasks. Notable features include an extended battery life of up to two years, an integrated temperature sensor for monitoring environmental conditions, and language options in Russian or English. The M60 aims to be user friendly by avoiding complex command memorization, offering simple configuration, a built in clock with accurate timekeeping, and automatic tuning for reliable satellite reception. It also provides flexible coordinate displays including Google Maps and Yandex Maps, latitude and longitude, and relative distance and direction from nearby towns.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the M60 can serve as a cost effective option for organizations that want consolidated location and condition data inside a single fleet management platform. Its ability to operate without a monthly fee and to work over data channels commonly used by fleet servers makes it a practical candidate for integration with Plaspy for live tracking, temperature monitoring, and historical reporting. The M60’s preset modes and straightforward configuration help reduce setup time while providing useful operational visibility when paired with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Long battery life up to two years for low maintenance deployments
- No monthly fee option for the tracker hardware itself, helping reduce ongoing costs
- Integrated temperature sensor for real time temperature monitoring
- Simple user interface with Russian and English language options
- Multiple display choices including Google Maps, Yandex Maps, and latitude longitude
- Three preset modes for different tasks: AUTO, SEARCH, and WAIT
- Ability to configure without SMS and work over GPRS data channels with self software upgrades

## How It Works with Plaspy

The M60 can feed location and condition data into Plaspy so teams get centralized visibility of assets and vehicles. Plaspy can display position updates, record historical tracks, and surface temperature readings alongside other fleet information so operators can monitor status and trends.

- Live position tracking and map display for individual units and fleets
- Temperature telemetry visible in Plaspy dashboards and alerts for temperature excursions
- Historical track playback and basic reporting to review past movements and events
- Event and alarm visibility for mode changes such as SEARCH or WAIT
- Low maintenance operation and long battery life that supports long term asset monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for light duty vehicles and equipment
- Asset tracking for trailers, containers, and remote equipment
- Monitoring temperature sensitive goods in transit or storage
- Search and recovery workflows for stolen or lost property using SEARCH mode
- Invisible monitoring or discreet tracking scenarios using WAIT mode

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The M60 is a practical option for organizations looking for a simple, durable tracker that pairs with Plaspy for unified visibility. Its long battery life and no monthly fee positioning can lower total operating cost for long term tracking projects. The built in temperature sensor and multiple display options add operational flexibility for teams that need environmental awareness alongside location data.
