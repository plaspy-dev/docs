# GPSMarker - M60

![M60](./logo.jpg)

The GPSMarker M60 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features to meet your tracking needs. One of the standout features of this tracker is that it does not require a monthly fee, making it a cost-effective option for long-term tracking. With a battery life of up to 2 years, you can rely on the M60 to provide continuous tracking without the need for frequent recharging.

Another notable feature of the M60 is its integrated temperature sensor, which allows you to monitor temperature changes in real-time. This can be particularly useful for tracking temperature-sensitive goods or monitoring the temperature in vehicles or storage facilities. The M60 also offers a choice of Russian or English language interface, making it accessible to a wide range of users.

The M60 is designed with a user-friendly system that does not require memorization of complex commands or codes. Setting alarms and configuring the tracker is as simple as entering a command, making it easy for anyone to use. The built-in clock with a quartz resonator ensures accurate timekeeping, and the auto-tuning feature ensures a strong GPS signal by automatically adjusting to GPS satellites.

With the M60, you have multiple options for displaying coordinates, including Google Maps, Yandex Maps, latitude and longitude, and distance and direction from the nearest town. This flexibility allows you to choose the display mode that works best for your tracking needs. The M60 also offers complimentary self software upgrades, ensuring that you always have access to the latest features and improvements.

Additional features of the M60 include the ability to configure the tracker without sending SMS, work on GPRS data transfer channel, and compatibility with popular free fleet management servers. The M60 also offers three preset modes: AUTO mode for motorists, SEARCH mode for searching for stolen property, and WAIT mode for invisible tracking with user commands. With its range of features and user-friendly design, the GPSMarker M60 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker for a variety of tracking applications.
