# ArkNav - RX-10 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the ArkNav RX-10 and how its capabilities align with Plaspy for fleet and asset tracking. It summarizes the practical, non-sensitive features you can expect when using the RX-10 with the Plaspy platform and highlights the types of visibility and workflows the device can enable in Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installed accessories, and manufacturer implementation choices. Where appropriate this page calls out optional modules and configuration-dependent items so you can match RX-10 capabilities to your deployment and check the manufacturer documentation for definitive details.

## Feature Overview

The RX-10 is a rugged vehicle tracker engineered for long term fleet use, trailer and heavy equipment monitoring, and anti-theft workflows. It combines built-in GNSS and cellular antennas, broad cellular fallback, and modular accessory support to deliver location, event, and temperature telemetry that is useful across a wide range of fleet operations.

- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and built in antennas for concealed installation in harsh environments.
- 4G LTE cellular connectivity with 2G and 3G fallback and UMTS antenna for broad coverage and continuous reporting.
- Real time location reporting plus motion and ignition events for accurate trip and usage visibility.
- Support for driver ID and immobilizer accessories to assist anti theft and recovery processes.
- Temperature monitoring via support for up to eight 1-Wire thermometers for cold chain tracking.
- Over the air firmware and configuration updates to manage large fleets remotely.

## Core Features of ArkNav - RX-10

- Rugged design with IP67 protection and built in GPS and UMTS antennas for concealed vehicle installation.
- Cellular connectivity across 4G LTE with 2G/3G fallback for continuous tracking where networks vary.
- Dual processor, multi thread architecture and multiple hardware watchdogs for stable long term operation.
- Motion and ignition event reporting, collision detection using a 3 axis accelerometer, and geofence support.
- Driver ID and immobilizer relay support via optional accessories for anti theft and PAYD workflows.
- Temperature telemetry support for up to eight 1‑Wire digital thermometers and analog input for additional sensors.
- Vehicle power resilience with protection designed to tolerate high voltage surges in heavy machinery systems.
- Remote management including over the air firmware updates and configuration provisioning for large deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the RX-10 is connected to Plaspy, the device becomes a continuous source of location and event data that feeds dashboards, alerts, and reports. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and ingests the RX-10 data to provide centralized monitoring, alerting, and remote device management.

- Real time location updates and motion events appear in Plaspy maps and activity timelines for live fleet visibility.
- Ignition, door, and motion inputs can be used to segment trips, calculate usage, and support driver behavior analysis in reports.
- Driver ID and immobilizer events are available to trigger anti theft alerts and to correlate identity with vehicle activity.
- Temperature telemetry from 1‑Wire sensors is aggregated in Plaspy for cold chain monitoring and threshold alerts.
- Geofence breach notifications, speed limit events, and collision alerts can be routed to Plaspy rules and notifications.
- Over the air firmware and configuration updates are coordinated through Plaspy to reduce on site maintenance for large fleets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Urban and long haul fleet management with real time tracking, trip segmentation, and driver analytics.
- Trailer and heavy equipment monitoring where IP67 ruggedization and surge tolerance are required.
- Cold chain logistics using multiple 1‑Wire thermometers to monitor temperature sensitive cargo.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using driver ID, immobilizer support, and real time alerts.
- PAYD insurance and proof of usage scenarios that rely on ignition and trip telemetry.
- Remote provisioning and maintenance for large scale deployments using FOTA and centralized configuration.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features require optional accessories or specific hardware revisions, for example immobilizer relays or driver ID probes.
- Firmware version can affect available functions and telemetry formats, and new features may be added or adjusted by the manufacturer.
- Regional cellular bands and model variants influence network compatibility and should be checked before purchase.
- Installation location and wiring affect sensor availability and resilience, particularly for ignition and analog inputs.
- Always confirm accessory compatibility and installation guidance with ArkNav documentation for safe and reliable operation.
