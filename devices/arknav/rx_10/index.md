# ArkNav - RX-10

![RX-10](https://app.plaspy.com/r/Trackers/arknav/rx10/tracker.png)

The RX-10 GPS tracker is a rugged, IP67-rated vehicle tracker designed for fleets, trailers and heavy machinery. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the RX-10 combines built-in GPS and UMTS antennas with 4G LTE connectivity \(with 2G/3G fallback\) to deliver reliable real-time tracking, robust telemetry and fast, concealed installation for demanding field deployments.

The RX-10 is engineered for long-term fleet management and anti-theft applications: dual processors, a multi-thread architecture and multiple hardware watchdogs ensure stable performance while modular accessories \(driver ID, immobilizer relay, 1-Wire thermometers and more\) expand capability for telemetry, driver behavior analytics and temperature-sensitive cargo monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for harsh environments with IP67 waterproof rating and heatproof housing.
- 4G LTE cellular connectivity with 2G/3G fallback and built-in UMTS antenna for broad coverage and reliable real-time tracking.
- Robust vehicle power resilience: designed with up to +65V surge protection to tolerate heavy machinery electrical systems.
- Comprehensive fleet features — motion/ignition activation, driver behavior and collision detection, geofencing \(up to 100 areas with speed limits\), and real-time alerts.
- Immobilizer and driver ID support for anti-theft, vehicle recovery and PAYD insurance workflows.
- Scalable telemetry: 12-bit analog input, active high/low inputs and support for up to eight 1-Wire digital thermometers for temperature monitoring.
- Modular architecture, optional accessories and over-the-air updates reduce installation time and after-sales maintenance for large fleets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the RX-10 becomes a high-availability data source for fleet management dashboards, alerts and reports. The device streams location and event data to Plaspy for real-time tracking, telemetry aggregation and automated workflows. Plaspy-compatible integration supports alerts and actions such as geofence breach notifications, ignition/door events, driver ID matching and immobilizer control.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates transmitted over 4G LTE with fallback for continuous tracking.
- Ignition and motion events for accurate trip start/stop and driver behavior analysis.
- Temperature telemetry via up to eight 1-Wire digital thermometers \(useful for cold-chain logistics and temperature-controlled cargo\).
- Driver ID and immobilizer support for anti-theft workflows and remote immobilization when required.
- Collision/acceleration events from the ±16G 3-axis accelerometer and optional surveillance image upload \(where supported\) for proof of incidents.
- Over-the-air firmware and configuration updates \(FOTA\) to manage large deployments remotely through Plaspy.
