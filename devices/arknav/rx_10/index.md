# ArkNav - RX-10

![RX-10](./)

The RX-10 is a rugged IP67 rated vehicle tracker from ArkNav designed for fleets, trailers and heavy machinery. It combines built-in GNSS and UMTS antennas with 4G LTE connectivity and fallback to older cellular generations to provide continuous real-time tracking, robust telemetry and a compact profile for concealed installation in demanding field conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the RX-10 can feed location, event and telemetry data directly into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its modular accessory options such as driver ID, immobilizer relay and multiple 1-Wire thermometers make it suitable for a range of fleet workflows that Plaspy supports, from anti-theft and driver management to temperature sensitive cargo oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for harsh environments with IP67 waterproof rating and heatproof housing for concealed vehicle installation.
- 4G LTE cellular connectivity with 2G/3G fallback and built-in UMTS antenna for broad coverage and reliable real-time tracking.
- Built for vehicle electrical systems with documented surge resilience to tolerate heavy machinery power conditions.
- Modular accessories including driver ID, immobilizer relay and support for multiple 1-Wire thermometers extend telemetry and security capability.
- Engineered for reliability with dual processors, multi threaded architecture and hardware watchdogs to reduce service interruptions.
- Over-the-air firmware and configuration updates simplify large scale deployments and ongoing maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the RX-10 acts as a continuous data source for fleet dashboards, alerts and operational reports. Plaspy ingests the device's location and events to deliver visibility, automated notifications and historical records that support fleet operations and security processes.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy for map based tracking and fleet visibility.
- Ignition and motion events to support trip segmentation, utilization reporting and driver behavior analysis.
- Temperature telemetry from 1-Wire sensors for cold chain monitoring and temperature sensitive cargo alerts.
- Driver ID and immobilizer events to enable anti theft workflows and administrative control via Plaspy.
- Collision and acceleration events to trigger incident alerts and support post event review in Plaspy.
- Remote firmware and configuration management to keep deployed devices up to date from Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch for urban and long haul operations requiring continuous tracking and driver oversight.
- Trailer and heavy equipment tracking where ruggedization and surge resilience are important.
- Temperature controlled logistics using multiple 1-Wire thermometers for cold chain visibility.
- Anti theft and vehicle recovery workflows using driver ID, immobilizer support and real-time alerts.
- Usage based workflows such as PAYD insurance support and delivery verification through event and trip data.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RX-10 is a practical choice for organizations that need a rugged, flexible tracker that integrates with a fleet platform like Plaspy. Its hardware design focuses on durability and stable operation in vehicle and heavy equipment environments, while modular inputs and accessories let fleets tailor telemetry and security features to specific operational needs.

Paired with Plaspy, the RX-10 becomes part of a centralized management workflow for alerts, reporting and remote device maintenance. This combination is especially useful for operators who require both reliable field hardware and a scalable platform for oversight across large or diverse vehicle fleets.
