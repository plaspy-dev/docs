# ArkNav - RX-9 3G

![RX-9 3G](./logo.jpg)

The ArkNav RX-9 3G is a waterproof vehicle tracker built for a broad range of mobile assets, including cars, vans, trucks, trailers, and heavy machinery. Designed with a rugged, IP67 rated enclosure and built in antennas, the RX-9 3G emphasizes durability and discreet placement for applications where exposure to harsh weather and vibration is likely. The device includes location capability, driver identification support, temperature sensing, and tamper resistant placement that help fleets maintain continuous visibility of their vehicles and equipment.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the RX-9 3G can feed location and key event data into Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and alerting. Its combination of robust hardware, temperature sensing, driver ID and immobilizer features makes it well suited to fleet management workflows that Plaspy supports, such as route monitoring, asset recovery, and temperature aware logistics. The RX-9 3G characteristics align with common Plaspy capabilities for operational oversight and historical reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged waterproof IP67 enclosure suited to harsh environments and outdoor installations
- Built in GPS and GSM modules for fast and reliable position reporting
- Integrated temperature sensor for monitoring temperature sensitive loads
- Driver ID support and immobilizer capability for added security and control
- Power surge protection up to 65 volts for improved reliability in vehicle electrical systems
- Stores and resends a large number of location records to prevent data loss
- OTA capable for remote configuration and updates to reduce maintenance visits

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the RX-9 3G provides continuous location and event information that Plaspy displays, aggregates, and uses to trigger notifications. Plaspy can consume the tracker data to enhance operational visibility and produce actionable insights for fleet managers.

- Real time location visibility and map display of vehicles and equipment inside Plaspy
- Historical route replay and stored location review for trip analysis and audits
- Alerts and notifications for events such as speeding, low external power, and other configurable conditions
- Temperature readings surfaced in Plaspy for monitoring temperature controlled transports
- Driver ID and immobilizer events available for driver assignment, security tracking, and recovery workflows
- Reporting and exportable logs for operational review and compliance needs

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for mixed vehicle fleets including light vehicles and heavy machinery
- Construction and agricultural equipment tracking where exposure to harsh conditions is common
- Temperature sensitive deliveries that require monitoring during transit
- Vehicle recovery and anti theft monitoring using discreet placement and immobilizer functions
- Trailer and asset tracking where autonomous battery backup and data buffering are valuable

The RX-9 3G pairs a rugged, weatherproof hardware design with features that support common fleet and asset tracking needs. Its built in antennas and waterproof housing reduce installation complexity and exposure to tampering, while temperature sensing and driver ID support add operational value for fleets that need more than basic location telemetry. Combined with Plaspy, the RX-9 3G can become part of a broader monitoring and reporting solution that helps teams maintain visibility, respond to incidents, and manage assets proactively.
