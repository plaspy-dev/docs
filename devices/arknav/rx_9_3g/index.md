# ArkNav - RX-9 3G

![RX-9 3G](./logo.jpg)

The ArkNav RX-9 3G/GPS Waterproof Vehicle Tracker is a versatile and reliable tracking solution designed for a wide range of vehicles, including cars, vans, trucks, trailers, and heavy machinery. It offers a range of features and functions that make it ideal for fleet management, vehicle recovery, construction and agricultural machinery, and more.

One of the standout features of the RX-9 is its robust and waterproof enclosure, which allows it to withstand harsh environmental conditions and dramatic changes in temperature. This makes it perfect for use in traveling vehicles where it may be exposed to extreme weather conditions. Additionally, the tracker's built-in antenna design not only reduces installation costs but also allows for discreet placement, reducing the risk of tampering and increasing the chances of recovery in the event of theft.

The RX-9 is equipped with the latest uBlox GPS and GSM modules, providing fast and accurate location reports. It also features a temperature sensor, allowing for the monitoring of temperature-controlled fleets. The tracker supports driver ID information and immobilization, adding an extra layer of security and control. With its power surge protection of up to 65 volts, the RX-9 offers reliable performance and reduces the risk of tracker burnout.

With its low installation and after-sales service costs, along with its high-quality components and low defect rate, the ArkNav RX-9 3G/GPS Waterproof Vehicle Tracker is an excellent choice for fleet owners and managers looking for a reliable and cost-effective tracking solution.

### Key Features:

- Latest uBlox GPS and GSM modules for accurate location tracking
- IP-67 waterproof and heatproof up to 85 °C
- Power surge protection up to 65 volts
- Stores and resends up to 50,000 locations
- Driver ID I-Button and immobilizer for added security
- Digital temperature sensor for temperature-controlled fleets
- Firmware/configuration update over the air \(OTA\)
- Built-in antennas for easy installation and discreet placement
- Activated by motion/ignition for efficient power usage
- Up to 3450 mAh back-up battery for uninterrupted tracking
- 3 axis G-force sensor with configurable threshold
- Intelligent tracking with independent settings for time, distance, and angle
- Heartbeat for maintaining GPRS connection
- Configurable real-time/idle tracking interval
- Speeding alert, low battery power alert, and external power input lost alert
- Optional solar charger available for extended battery life
- Sends Google hyperlinks for missed phone calls
