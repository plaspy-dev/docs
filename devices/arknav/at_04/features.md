# ArkNav - AT-04 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the ArkNav AT-04 Compact GPS Tracker and the practical feature context for using the device with Plaspy. It explains the capabilities Plaspy can ingest and display, and how common reporting modes and device behaviors map to platform visibility and alerts.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, regional cellular support, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current and device-specific technical details refer to ArkNav documentation while using this page as a practical guide for Plaspy integration and operational planning.

## Feature Overview

The AT-04 is an ultra-compact, Plaspy-compatible tracker designed for discreet tracking of children, high-value deliveries, and portable assets. It balances small size and simple operation with location assistance and configurable reporting profiles to extend battery life while maintaining useful situational awareness.

- Ultra-small form factor for covert placement on portable assets, deliveries, or personal carry items.
- Built-in GPS with AGPS assistance and cell-based location to improve position fixes where GPS alone is limited.
- Long battery life from a built-in rechargeable battery with timer and movement-activated reporting profiles to optimize standby time.
- Simple single-button user interaction plus call and SMS location request support for quick locations on demand.
- On-device backup storage and motion detection to preserve recent fixes and report movement events when connectivity is intermittent.

## Core Features of ArkNav - AT-04

- Plaspy compatible device that reports location and motion data for platform ingestion and display.
- Compact, easy-to-hide enclosure intended for discreet asset and personal tracking.
- Built-in GNSS with AGPS assistance and cell-location support to improve acquisition and indoor performance.
- Configurable timer-based reporting to balance update frequency and battery endurance.
- Movement-activated reporting that triggers transmissions when the device detects motion.
- Call and SMS location request capability that returns quick-view links for mobile verification.
- On-device backup storage to hold recent location data when network connectivity is unavailable.
- Integrated motion sensing via a 3-axis accelerometer to detect movement and generate motion events.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and motion reports from compatible devices such as the AT-04 and presents them as live positions, event logs, and historical tracks. Reporting behavior on the platform reflects the device's configured mode, whether frequent updates for real-time visibility or less frequent timer or movement-triggered updates for long-term asset monitoring.

- Real-time or near real-time position feeds in Plaspy when the device is configured for frequent reporting subject to battery and network conditions.
- Motion and movement events shown in Plaspy as alerts or timeline entries when the onboard accelerometer reports activity.
- Timer and movement reporting modes help Plaspy manage update density and historical records while conserving device battery.
- Call and SMS location requests provide an alternative way to obtain a quick location when standard uploads are unavailable; results can be reconciled with Plaspy records.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and accepts device reports to simplify device onboarding and reduce manual configuration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet tracking of high-value package and cash deliveries with periodic location confirmation.
- Portable asset protection for tools, equipment, or rental items that require covert monitoring.
- Child protection and personal safety where a compact device and simple request-for-location workflows are desirable.
- Short-route courier and small delivery operations that need centralized visibility without vehicle I/O.
- Temporary field operations or short-term surveillance where size, battery life, and indoor assistance are priorities.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and hardware revision differences can change available settings, reporting behavior, and feature sets; always confirm with the device firmware release notes.
- Regional cellular band variants and SIM provisioning affect connectivity and SMS/call-based location requests; verify the model variant for your region.
- Some features such as call or SMS location returns rely on cellular service and carrier support in the deployment area.
- On-device storage and accelerometer sensitivity behaviors may be configurable and subject to firmware defaults.
- Manufacturer accessories and provisioning tools such as USB programming cables may be required for some configuration tasks; consult ArkNav documentation.
