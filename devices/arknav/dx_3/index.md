# ArkNav - DX-3

![DX-3](./tracker.png)

The DX-3 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for safety and dependable incident response. It is built for scenarios such as elderly care, lone workers, patrols and vulnerable-person protection. The device emphasizes immediate emergency signalling, two-way voice communication, accessibility aids like vibration and audio cues, and a splash resistant enclosure for everyday wearability and practical deployment.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the DX-3 is suited to privacy aware, on demand tracking and rapid alerting workflows. Location sharing is deliberately limited to Start Tracking sessions or emergency activations, which lets caregivers and operators receive actionable alerts through Plaspy without continuous surveillance. This behavior makes the DX-3 a useful option for organizations that need fast incident response while respecting user independence.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for personal safety and vulnerable person monitoring with Plaspy compatibility.
- Dedicated emergency panic button and lanyard activation for immediate alerts.
- Two-way hands free voice via built in speaker and microphone to support communication during incidents.
- Accessibility features including vibration, audio cues and clear LED icons to assist diverse users.
- Privacy first operation that shares continuous location only when tracking is started or an emergency occurs.
- Rugged splash resistant housing and stackable charging dock for straightforward daily use.
- Long standby availability to support extended readiness between charges.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the DX-3 delivers discrete alarm events and on demand location updates so caregivers, dispatchers and monitoring teams can prioritize responses without continuous telemetry. Plaspy groups alarm information and status context to help teams act quickly while preserving the device behavior that protects user privacy.

- Location updates appear on Plaspy maps when Start Tracking is engaged or during an emergency session.
- Panic and man down alerts are routed into Plaspy for prompt notification and escalation workflows.
- Two way voice sessions can be initiated during alerts so operators or responders can communicate directly with the user.
- Battery and charger status reported to Plaspy help maintain device readiness and signal when charging is needed.
- Standardized alarm messaging such as Contact ID support enables integration with monitoring systems and Plaspy workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elderly safety and assisted living where discreet wearable protection and immediate alerts are required.
- Lone worker programs that need on demand check ins and fast voice contact during incidents.
- Patrol and security rounds for situational alerting without continuous tracking of staff.
- Community care and vulnerable person monitoring where privacy and independence are priorities.
- Short term deployments or events that benefit from stackable chargers and long standby life.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The DX-3 is a focused option for organizations that need a compact, accessible personal tracker integrated into a broader monitoring platform. Its emergency signalling, two way voice and accessibility features support faster, clearer response in urgent situations, while the device behavior reduces unnecessary continuous tracking. Those attributes make the DX-3 a practical match for Plaspy workflows centered on privacy aware, on demand monitoring.
