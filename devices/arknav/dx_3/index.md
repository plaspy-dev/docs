# ArkNav - DX-3

![DX-3](./tracker.png)

The DX-3 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for personal safety and dependable incident response. Built for elderly care, lone workers, patrols and vulnerable-person protection, the DX-3 combines clear emergency signalling, two-way voice, accessibility aids and long standby life in a splash-resistant package—making it an ideal Plaspy-compatible device for privacy-first, on-demand tracking and rapid alerting.

The device prioritizes user independence and practical deployment: continuous location is only shared when the Start Tracking button is pressed or an emergency activates, and panic or man-down alerts are sent immediately. When integrated with the Plaspy platform the DX-3 provides caregivers and operators fast, actionable notifications without invasive 24/7 monitoring, while still delivering reliable GPS tracker capabilities and hands-free voice communication in urgent situations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for personal safety and vulnerable-person monitoring.
- Dedicated rubber emergency \(panic\) button and lanyard activation for immediate alerts.
- Two-way hands-free voice via built-in speaker and microphone for direct communication during incidents.
- Accessibility features: vibration, audio cues and iconic LED indicators to assist hearing- or sight-impaired users.
- Privacy-first tracking: no continuous tracking unless Start Tracking is pressed or an emergency occurs—reduces unnecessary telemetry and liability.
- Rugged, splash-resistant IP54 housing and stackable charging dock for practical, "fit-and-forget" daily use.
- Long standby battery \(1100 mAh\) with approximately 72+ hours between charges for reliable availability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the DX-3 delivers clear incident and location data to the platform so caregivers, dispatchers or monitoring teams can act quickly. Plaspy receives discrete alarm events, location updates when tracking is enabled, and status information so teams can prioritize responses without maintaining continuous surveillance.

- Real-time location updates when Start Tracking is engaged or during emergency sessions—Plaspy displays the user’s position on the live map.
- Panic alerts and man-down events transmitted to Plaspy for immediate notification routing and escalation.
- Two-way voice sessions initiated during alerts allow Plaspy operators or responders to speak directly with the user if network voice capability is available.
- Battery and charger status reported so Plaspy can notify caretakers about low power and maintain uptime for lone worker safety.
- Contact ID \(CID\) protocol support enables standardized alarm messaging into alarm receiving systems and Plaspy integrations where Contact ID is used.

## Use Cases

- Elderly safety: discreet wearable protection with immediate panic and man-down alerts for caregivers and monitoring centers.
- Lone worker protection: on-demand Start Tracking for check-ins and fast two-way voice during incidents.
- Patrol guard and security rounds: simple emergency signalling and hands-free communication without constant tracking.
- Vulnerable-person monitoring in assisted living or community care settings where privacy and independence are priorities.
- Temporary deployments and events: stackable charging docks and long standby life enable easy maintenance and rotation of devices.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

DX-3 is a focused, trustworthy choice when you need a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that emphasizes safety, accessibility and privacy. Its panic and man-down alerts, clear user interface and two-way voice link reduce response times and improve outcomes in emergencies. The device’s “start tracking on demand” behavior gives organizations a scalable way to manage vulnerable-person protection without the overhead of continuous telemetry.

While the DX-3 is optimized for personal protection rather than fleet management or telemetry-rich vehicle tracking, it integrates smoothly into Plaspy alongside other devices: operators can manage DX-3 alarm and location events within the same Plaspy dashboard used for broader deployments that include assets with fuel monitoring, ignition, immobilizer or Bluetooth sensors. For customers focused on anti-theft, detailed vehicle telemetry or fleet-centric features, Plaspy enables combined visibility across specialized trackers and the DX-3 personal safety profile.

Choose the DX-3 with Plaspy when you need a compact, accessible GPS tracker that provides reliable emergency alerts, practical everyday use, and privacy-aware tracking for elderly care, lone workers and other vulnerable individuals.
