# ArkNav - R-12 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the ArkNav R-12 and how its capabilities are presented and used within Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive details that help fleet managers, integrators, and technicians understand what the device can provide when paired with Plaspy for real-time tracking and operational monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional modules fitted at purchase, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Refer to the official ArkNav documentation for the most current device specifications and to your Plaspy settings for platform-specific configuration and visibility.

## Feature Overview

The ArkNav R-12 is a compact IP67 waterproof GPS vehicle tracker designed for long-term vehicle and asset tracking where concealment, reliability, and low maintenance are priorities. It combines cellular connectivity with a GNSS receiver and event-driven reporting to deliver continuous location and status updates into Plaspy for mapping, alerts, and reporting.

- LTE‑M primary connectivity with EGPRS 2G fallback for broad cellular coverage and resilient tracking.
- IP67 sealed enclosure and concealed antennas for discreet installations and resistance to dust and water.
- Built‑in GNSS receiver with AGPS and integrated antennas to provide robust positioning for vehicles and assets.
- Ignition‑aware and event driven reporting for motion, speed, and trip segmentation to support fleet workflows.
- OTA firmware and configuration updates to reduce field visits and keep large deployments consistent.
- Vehicle power protection and configurable outputs to support anti-theft and operational control scenarios.

## Core Features of ArkNav - R-12

- LTE‑M (Cat‑M1) cellular connectivity with EGPRS quad‑band fallback for continuity in mixed coverage areas.
- IP67 rated waterproof and dust tight enclosure with concealed antennas for secure vehicle installation.
- u‑Blox GNSS receiver with AGPS support and built‑in GNSS antenna for faster position fixes.
- Ignition input and ignition‑aware reporting to identify trip start and stop events.
- Two configurable outputs that can be used for remote control workflows such as immobilizer relays when supported by installation.
- ±16G accelerometer for motion and impact detection and event triggering.
- Voltage surge protection up to 60 V and optional ISO‑7637 protection for harsh electrical environments.
- OTA firmware and remote configuration management to streamline updates across a fleet.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the R-12 streams GNSS positions, status updates, and event messages so operators can monitor vehicles in real time and respond to operational events. Plaspy ingests location and event data to display on maps, trigger alerts, and feed reporting tools used for fleet oversight.

- Real-time location updates and telemetry appear in Plaspy maps and timelines for continuous visibility.
- Ignition, motion, and speed events are used by Plaspy to mark trips, generate alerts, and improve route and duty reporting.
- Configurable outputs on the device can be controlled via Plaspy commands where remote immobilization or control workflows are implemented.
- OTA firmware and configuration delivery supports consistent device management through Plaspy device management tools.
- Local flash backup helps preserve recent position and event data during temporary connectivity loss and is reconciled to Plaspy when service resumes.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding and reduce setup complexity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft workflows and remote immobilization using configurable outputs and Plaspy control.
- Tracking trailers and heavy machinery in harsh environments where IP67 protection and rugged power handling are required.
- Delivery, dispatch, and route monitoring with ignition and motion events for accurate trip reporting and ETAs.
- Usage based insurance and driver behavior analysis using event-driven telemetry for PAYD scenarios.
- Stolen vehicle recovery and discreet asset protection through concealed installation and continuous tracking.
- Large scale deployments managed with OTA updates to minimize maintenance overhead.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Functionality depends on firmware version and software features provisioned by ArkNav; feature sets can change with updates.
- Hardware revisions and optional modules such as ISO‑7637 protection or external interfaces may be offered as variants and affect available inputs or outputs.
- Installation choices, antenna placement, and vehicle wiring affect signal quality and the availability of ignition or power‑loss reporting.
- Regional cellular band support varies by product variant; confirm LTE‑M and 2G band compatibility for your region with the manufacturer.
- Always consult ArkNav documentation and release notes for the most current technical and installation guidance.
