# ArkNav - R-12

![R-12](https://app.plaspy.com/r/Trackers/arknav/r12/tracker.png)

The R-12 LTE M Waterproof GPS Vehicle Tracker is a compact, IP67-rated tracker purpose-built for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking across cars, vans, trucks, trailers and heavy machinery. With LTE‑M \(Cat‑M1\) main connectivity and EGPRS quad‑band fallback, a u‑Blox GNSS receiver and built‑in antennas, the R‑12 delivers robust positioning and telemetry where fleet management and anti-theft readiness are essential.

Designed for fit-and-forget installations, the R‑12 pairs neatly with Plaspy for live location, ignition-aware reporting and configurable alerts. Its rugged, concealed-antenna enclosure, surge protection and OTA firmware/configuration updates reduce field service and simplify large-scale deployments for system integrators and fleet operators seeking dependable, Plaspy compatible tracking solutions.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking over LTE‑M with EGPRS \(2G\) fallback for wide-area coverage and reliability.
- IP67 waterproof and dust-tight enclosure with concealed antennas for discreet installations and reduced tampering risk.
- Intelligent event reporting \(speed, motion, ignition\) to optimize fleet management and trigger timely alerts.
- Robust vehicle power protection — voltage surge protection to 60 V and optional ISO‑7637 module for harsh electrical environments.
- Two configurable outputs \(optionally usable as immobilizer control\) to support anti-theft and remote immobilization workflows with Plaspy.
- Built‑in u‑Blox GNSS with AGPS for faster fixes, plus a ±16G accelerometer for motion detection and impact events.
- OTA firmware and configuration updates to streamline maintenance and scale deployments without physical access.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The R‑12 streams GNSS positions, status and telemetry to Plaspy via LTE‑M \(with EGPRS fallback\) so fleet managers get continuous, Plaspy compatible real‑time tracking and insight. Plaspy ingests location packets and event-triggered messages \(speed, motion, ignition\) to display live maps, generate alerts, and feed reports and APIs for integration with back-office systems.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates transmitted over LTE‑M / EGPRS to Plaspy for mapping and reporting.
- Ignition, motion and speed event reporting for accurate trip start/stop, driver behavior and PAYD scenarios.
- External power loss notification and low battery/voltage alerts to prevent silent failures and enable proactive service.
- Remote immobilizer control possible via the device’s configurable outputs \(when used with an immobilizer relay\) for anti-theft response through Plaspy commands.
- OTA firmware and configuration updates delivered through Plaspy-supported device management to keep devices current and consistent.
