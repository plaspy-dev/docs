# ArkNav - R-12

![R-12](./)

The R-12 is a compact, IP67 rated LTE-M waterproof GPS vehicle tracker from ArkNav designed for reliable real time tracking across cars, vans, trucks, trailers and heavy machinery. It combines LTE-M connectivity with EGPRS fallback, a u‑Blox GNSS receiver and built in antennas to provide robust positioning and telemetry where fleet management and asset protection are required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the R-12 integrates into Plaspy's fleet management environment to deliver live location, ignition aware reporting and configurable alerts. Its rugged enclosure, surge protection and support for OTA firmware and configuration updates make the R-12 a practical option for large deployments managed through Plaspy's device and fleet workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real time tracking over LTE-M with EGPRS fallback for broad coverage and resilience
- IP67 waterproof and dust tight enclosure with concealed antennas for discreet and durable installations
- Intelligent event reporting for speed, motion and ignition to support accurate trip and behavior tracking
- Vehicle power protection to 60 V and optional ISO 7637 support for harsh electrical environments
- Two configurable outputs that can be used with immobilizer relays for anti theft workflows
- Built in u Blox GNSS with AGPS support and a ±16G accelerometer for motion and impact detection
- Over the air firmware and configuration updates to reduce field service and simplify scaling

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the R-12 streams GNSS positions and event telemetry to the platform so fleet managers and system integrators can monitor assets on live maps, receive alerts, and include device data in reports and integrations. Plaspy ingests location packets and event notifications to provide operational visibility and support automated responses.

- Real time location and telemetry displayed on Plaspy maps for live fleet oversight
- Ignition, motion and speed events used to mark trip start and stop and to inform reports
- Power loss and voltage alerts to notify operators of potential electrical issues before they escalate
- Remote immobilizer control via configurable outputs to support anti theft responses through Plaspy
- OTA firmware and configuration management to keep devices updated and consistent across fleets

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft programs and remote immobilization using configurable outputs and Plaspy commands
- Trailer and heavy equipment tracking where IP67 protection and rugged power handling are important
- Delivery, dispatch and route monitoring with ignition aware trip reporting and ETA support
- Pay as you drive insurance and driver behavior analytics based on event driven telemetry
- Caravan or asset recovery scenarios where discreet installation and fast location are needed

The R-12 offers a balanced combination of rugged hardware and connectivity features that align well with Plaspy fleet management capabilities. Its resilient communications, concealed antenna design and vehicle grade power protections reduce downtime and tampering risk, while OTA support and event driven reporting help lower maintenance overhead for large deployments.

Paired with Plaspy, the R-12 becomes a dependable building block for ignition aware tracking, anti theft workflows and scalable fleet operations.
