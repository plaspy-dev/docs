# ArkNav - RX-12

![RX-12](./)

The RX-12 is a compact, rugged GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable deployment across cars, vans, trucks, trailers and heavy machinery. It combines an integrated LTE‑M and 2G cellular module with a high sensitivity u‑blox GNSS receiver and built in antennas inside an IP67 rated, heat tolerant enclosure to deliver consistent real time tracking and telemetry while minimizing exposure to tampering and environmental hazards.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RX-12 functions as a straightforward endpoint for fleet monitoring and security workflows. Event driven reporting for speed, motion and ignition, plus OTA firmware and configuration updates and hardened power protection, make the RX-12 a practical choice for operators who want to feed reliable location and status data into Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reports with minimal ongoing maintenance.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible with native support for event driven real time tracking and telemetry integration.
- Rugged IP67 waterproof and dust tight enclosure with heat tolerance for demanding field use.
- Integrated LTE M with 2G fallback for broad cellular reach and consistent connectivity.
- Event triggers for speed motion and ignition to reduce unnecessary reporting and data use.
- OTA firmware and configuration updates to simplify remote maintenance and updates.
- Built in antennas and low profile form factor to reduce tampering and simplify mounting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the RX-12 transmits GNSS positions and event driven telemetry over cellular networks to Plaspy’s cloud platform. Plaspy ingests position fixes and IO or accelerometer driven events to provide visibility, alerts and historical reporting that support operational oversight and security workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and monitoring.
- Event driven reporting on speed motion and ignition to enable intelligent trip start stop and driver event logging.
- Alerts and reporting in Plaspy based on input states and accelerometer events for security and operational triggers.
- Remote control possibilities using output channels such as immobilizer relay where fitted to support anti theft workflows.
- OTA update status and configuration visibility to help manage device fleet health from Plaspy.
- No built in Bluetooth on the RX-12 but Plaspy can incorporate Bluetooth sensor data from other compatible devices in mixed deployments.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light and heavy vehicles with route replay and driver event logging.
- Trailer and heavy machinery tracking where IP67 protection and heat tolerance matter.
- Vehicle recovery and anti theft deployments using discreet mounting and optional immobilizer control.
- Delivery dispatch and workforce coordination using event triggers for timely location updates.
- PAYD and usage based insurance workflows that rely on accurate trip and motion data.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RX-12 pairs rugged, low maintenance hardware with event driven reporting that aligns well with Plaspy’s fleet monitoring and alerting capabilities. Its protective enclosure and built in antennas reduce installation complexity and the risk of damage, while LTE‑M with 2G fallback helps maintain connectivity across varied operating areas. Together with Plaspy, the RX-12 can serve as a dependable endpoint for real time tracking, anti theft workflows and usage based reporting.
