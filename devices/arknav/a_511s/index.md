# ArkNav - A-511S

![A-511S](./logo.jpg)

The ArkNav A-511S is a high-performance GPS receiver designed for a wide range of applications such as car navigation, mapping, surveying, and security. It is built with a SiRFIII chipset, which ensures reliable and accurate positioning, velocity, and time information. With its 20 channels 'All-In-View' tracking capability, the A-511S can quickly acquire and maintain satellite signals even in challenging environments.

One of the standout features of the A-511S is its superior sensitivity, with a tracking sensitivity of -159 dBm. This allows for precise and consistent tracking, even in areas with weak GPS signals. The receiver also boasts impressive start-up times, with cold, warm, and hot start times averaging at 42, 38, and 1 second respectively. This means that you can get up and running with the A-511S in no time.

In terms of connectivity, the A-511S offers multiple interface options including USB, PS2, and DB9, providing flexibility for different setups and devices. Its low power design ensures efficient operation, with a tracking power consumption of just 80mA. The receiver is also designed for easy installation, thanks to its super-cohesive magnetic base.

With its compact size of 65.0" \(L\) x 53.0 mm \(Diameter\) x 19.2 mm \(H\) and wide operating temperature range of -40°C to +85°C, the ArkNav A-511S is a reliable and versatile GPS receiver that can meet the demands of various applications. Whether you need accurate navigation for your vehicle or precise positioning for surveying purposes, the A-511S is a smart choice.

### Key Features:

- Built-in SiRFIII chipset
- 20 channels 'All-In-View' tracking
- Cold/Warm/Hot start time: 42/38/1 sec. \(average\)
- Superior sensitivity: -159 dBm tracking
- Reacquisition time: 0.1 sec
- Low power design \(80mA, tracking\)
- Super-cohesive magnetic for installation
- USB/PS2/DB9 interface connection port
- Size: 65.0" \(L\) x 53.0 mm \(Diameter\) x 19.2 mm \(H\)
- Operating temperature: -40°C to +85°C
