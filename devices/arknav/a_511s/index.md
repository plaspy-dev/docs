# ArkNav - A-511S

<div className="device-logo">
![A-511S](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The ArkNav A-511S is a compact, high performance GPS receiver designed for navigation, mapping, surveying, and security applications. Built around a SiRFIII chipset, the A-511S offers 20 channel All‑In‑View tracking and high sensitivity reception, enabling fast satellite acquisition and stable position, velocity, and time information even in challenging signal conditions. Its small footprint, magnetic mounting option, and wide operating temperature range make it suitable for a variety of field and vehicle deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the A-511S can be used to bring precise location data into Plaspy for live monitoring and historical analysis. The unit's quick start times, strong sensitivity, and multiple interface options provide practical benefits when adding hardware to a Plaspy-powered tracking or fleet management workflow. Organizations can leverage the A-511S to improve visibility, mapping accuracy, and operational oversight within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- SiRFIII chipset and 20 channel All‑In‑View tracking for reliable satellite acquisition
- High tracking sensitivity at -159 dBm for improved performance in weak signal areas
- Fast average start times for cold, warm, and hot starts to reduce downtime
- Multiple interface options including USB PS2 and DB9 for flexible connectivity
- Low tracking power consumption for efficient continuous operation
- Super‑cohesive magnetic base for simple mounting in mobile or temporary setups
- Rugged operating temperature range suitable for broad environmental conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ArkNav A-511S supplies accurate position and timing data that Plaspy can ingest for real time display, logging, and fleet management. When integrated, the A-511S becomes a source of location updates that Plaspy uses to provide situational awareness, reporting, and operational alerts.

- Real time location visibility on Plaspy maps for single units or fleets
- Historical tracking and replay to analyze movements and routes over time
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on location events or movement patterns
- Reporting tools in Plaspy that use position data for operational summaries and audits
- Flexible connectivity options to match different installation approaches and data collection setups

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle navigation and fleet tracking for small to medium fleets
- Mapping and surveying projects requiring consistent position fixes
- Security and asset tracking where reliable reception in weak signal areas is needed
- Temporary deployments that benefit from magnetic mounting and compact size
- Operations in wide temperature ranges and outdoor environments
