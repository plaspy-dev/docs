# ArkNav - A-511S Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the ArkNav A-511S and how its capabilities relate to use with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on non-sensitive, user facing details such as positioning performance, interfaces, and practical benefits you can expect when integrating the A-511S into Plaspy for monitoring and tracking.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. The information below reflects the A-511S product description and common operational characteristics, but you should verify current device capabilities and firmware behavior with ArkNav documentation for the most accurate details.

## Feature Overview

The ArkNav A-511S is a compact, high performance GPS receiver designed to deliver reliable position, velocity, and timing information in a range of environments. Its sensitivity and multi channel tracking make it well suited to applications where consistent satellite reception and quick signal reacquisition improve operational visibility.

- High sensitivity and 20 channel all in view tracking for stable position fixes in weak signal conditions
- Fast cold warm and hot start times to reduce downtime after power cycles
- Multiple physical interface options for flexible integration with vehicle or fixed installations
- Low power tracking consumption suitable for continuous use scenarios
- Super cohesive magnetic mounting for easy installation and repositioning
- Wide operating temperature range for demanding environments

## Core Features of ArkNav - A-511S

- Built in SiRFIII chipset providing the receiver core used for positioning
- 20 channels with all in view tracking for quick satellite acquisition and improved lock retention
- Superior tracking sensitivity rated at approximately 159 dBm for consistent fixes in weak signal areas
- Cold warm and hot start times averaging about 42, 38, and 1 second respectively
- Reacquisition time around 0.1 second for fast recovery after brief signal loss
- Low power design with typical tracking current around 80 mA
- Multiple interface ports including USB PS2 and DB9 for integration flexibility
- Rugged operating range with wide temperature tolerance and compact physical dimensions

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the location and timing data that the A-511S produces and uses it to provide mapping visibility location history and operational oversight. Plaspy can automatically detect common tracker protocols and accept connections from devices configured to connect to the platform using the standard Plaspy device port and transport options.

- Real time location display and historical track visualization within Plaspy using the A-511S position reports
- Improved visibility in areas with weak reception as the device sensitivity yields more consistent location samples
- Faster availability of location after power events thanks to the A-511S warm and hot start characteristics
- Flexible hardware integration to existing vehicle or equipment interfaces simplifies deployment that feeds Plaspy
- Low power operation helps sustain continuous reporting scenarios and reduces the need for high capacity power systems
- Plaspy auto detection of tracker protocol reduces manual configuration steps when adding A-511S devices
