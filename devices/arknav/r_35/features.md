# ArkNav - R-35 Features

This page provides a public, features-focused overview of the ArkNav R-35 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for tracking and monitoring. It describes the practical functions you can expect to use inside Plaspy and summarizes the device attributes that matter for deployment, without delving into private configuration specifics.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where possible this page sticks to the manufacturer-provided public details about the R-35 and explains how those features typically translate to visibility and operational use inside Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The ArkNav R-35 is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for vehicle, personal, and asset tracking. Its design focuses on concealment and flexible placement while offering cellular connectivity and a backup power option to support continued reporting when primary power is lost.

- Small portable form factor suited for pocket carry or discreet placement in goods and equipment
- Full quadband GSM support for broad cellular coverage across many regions
- Supports GSM, GPRS, and TCP communication methods for sending location data to a tracking platform
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas to simplify installation and concealment without external antenna routing
- Built-in backup battery to preserve tracking capability during power loss
- Durable plastic housing for everyday handling and general field use

## Core Features of ArkNav - R-35

- Versatile use cases for vehicle personal and asset tracking thanks to small portable size
- Full quadband GSM band support providing broad cellular network compatibility
- Multiple communication modes including GSM GPRS and TCP for reporting location and status
- Internal GPS antenna to obtain satellite-based location fixes without external antennas
- Internal GSM antenna to maintain mobile network connectivity in a compact enclosure
- Backup battery to continue sending updates when external power is interrupted
- Robust plastic enclosure designed for routine handling and concealed installation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The R-35's basic reporting capabilities align with Plaspy's platform features for device visibility, location history, and operational monitoring. Plaspy automatically detects a connected tracker protocol and surfaces device updates so teams can maintain situational awareness without manual protocol selection.

- Location updates from the R-35 are reflected on Plaspy maps and in position history for review and playback
- Continuous reporting supported by the device's backup battery helps Plaspy maintain tracking during temporary power loss
- Cellular reporting modes like GPRS and TCP enable the device to send position and connectivity updates into Plaspy for live monitoring
- Internal antennas reduce the need for external antenna wiring, simplifying deployments that are then administered and monitored in Plaspy
- Device connectivity and status changes are visible in Plaspy so operators can respond to offline or low connectivity conditions

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet tracking of high value goods during transit or storage
- Temporary asset tracking for equipment moved between sites
- Personal location tracking when portability and concealment are required
- Vehicle tracking where a compact or hidden installation is preferable
- Short term rentals or loaned equipment tracking to maintain custody records

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature availability may depend on the device firmware version and manufacturer firmware options
- Hardware revisions or regional model variants can alter supported cellular bands or communication behavior
- Installation method and antenna placement can affect GPS reception and cellular performance
- Manufacturer documentation should be consulted for detailed electrical installation, battery specs, and region specific carrier guidance
- Plaspy presents whatever device data is reported by the tracker; bespoke firmware or custom configurations may change the set of visible telemetry
