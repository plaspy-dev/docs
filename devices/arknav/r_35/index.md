# ArkNav - R-35

![R-35](./logo.jpg)

The ArkNav R-35 GPS Tracker is a versatile and compact device designed for vehicle, personal, and asset tracking. Its portable size allows it to be easily carried in a pocket, hidden in luxury goods, or attached to equipment or a person. With its advanced features and reliable performance, the R-35 is an excellent choice for individuals and businesses looking to track and trace their valuable assets.

The R-35 GPS Tracker from ArkNav offers a range of outstanding features that make it a reliable and efficient tracking solution. It operates on a full quadband GSM band, ensuring reliable communication and coverage. The device supports GSM, GPRS, and TCP communication methods, allowing for seamless data transmission. It also features a backup battery, ensuring continuous tracking even in the event of a power outage. The R-35 is equipped with internal GPS and GSM antennas, eliminating the need for external antennas and making it easy to conceal. The device is housed in a durable plastic casing, ensuring its longevity and resistance to wear and tear. Made in China, the R-35 GPS Tracker combines quality craftsmanship with advanced technology to deliver accurate and reliable tracking results.

Key Features:

- Full quadband GSM band for reliable communication and coverage
- GSM, GPRS, and TCP communication methods for seamless data transmission
- Backup battery for continuous tracking even during power outages
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas for easy concealment
- Durable plastic casing for longevity and resistance to wear and tear
