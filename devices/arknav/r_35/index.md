# ArkNav - R-35

<div className="device-logo">
![R-35](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The ArkNav R-35 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker intended for vehicle, personal, and asset tracking. Designed for portability and discreet placement, the R-35 can be carried in a pocket, hidden inside luxury items, or attached to equipment or a person. Its internal GPS and GSM antennas and durable plastic casing make it straightforward to conceal while maintaining reliable location reporting.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the R-35 can feed location data into Plaspy for monitoring and management. Its support for quadband GSM communications and common cellular data methods allows it to transmit position information to fleet and asset management platforms like Plaspy, making it a practical option for organizations and individuals seeking continuous visibility over mobile assets.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and portable form factor suitable for vehicle personal and asset tracking
- Full quadband GSM support for broad cellular coverage
- Uses common cellular data methods including GSM GPRS and TCP for data transmission
- Built in GPS and GSM antennas simplify concealment and reduce external hardware
- Backup battery provides continued tracking during power interruptions
- Durable plastic housing for everyday wear and concealment
- Versatile placement options for discreet monitoring of goods people and equipment

## How It Works with Plaspy

The R-35 transmits location and status information over cellular data channels so Plaspy can collect and present that data in dashboards reports and monitoring tools. Plaspy ingests the device's position updates to provide ongoing visibility and operational oversight across vehicles people and portable assets.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for live tracking
- Historical position data stored for trip reports route analysis and audit trails
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on device movement or absence of expected updates
- Asset grouping and labeling in Plaspy to organize trackers by vehicle user or site
- Centralized fleet visibility to monitor multiple R-35 units alongside other compatible devices

## Typical Use Cases

- Compact vehicle tracking for small cars motorcycles or light commercial vehicles
- Personal safety tracking for field staff couriers or individuals requiring discreet monitoring
- Protection and recovery of high value or luxury goods during transport or storage
- Equipment and portable asset tracking for rental fleets construction tools and leased items
- Temporary deployments where a portable concealed tracker is required for short term monitoring

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The R-35 is a good fit for organizations and individuals who need a small discreet tracker that can be added to Plaspy for unified monitoring. Its quadband GSM capability and internal antennas simplify deployment across different regions and placement scenarios, while a backup battery helps maintain continuity of tracking during power interruptions.

For teams managing mixed fleets and portable assets, pairing the R-35 with Plaspy provides a straightforward way to consolidate location data into one platform for reporting alerts and operational oversight.
