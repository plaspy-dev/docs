# ArkNav - AT-5000

![AT-5000](./logo.jpg)

The ArkNav AT-5000 is a powerful GPS tracker designed for long-term operation in challenging environments. With its advanced GPS and GSM technology, this tracker is ideal for container and equipment management, as well as security and law enforcement applications. Whether you need to track large equipment, monitor containers in remote areas, or conduct private investigations, the AT-5000 offers the visibility and reliability you need.

One of the standout features of the AT-5000 is its long battery life. Equipped with a large capacity rechargeable battery, this tracker can operate for up to 1 year under motion activation mode, making it a truly fit-and-forget product. Additionally, its waterproof and heatproof plastic casing ensures durability in harsh environments, with a rating of IP67 and the ability to withstand temperatures up to 85 °C.

With pin-point accurate cell locations and AGPS capabilities, the AT-5000 provides precise tracking information. It can be activated by timer and movement, and even allows users to request the current location simply by making a phone call. The tracker also offers superb power management, allowing for tracking once an hour under timer activation mode for up to 50 days. Furthermore, it supports SMS Google map hyperlinks to cell phones, providing convenient access to location information.

Overall, the ArkNav AT-5000 is a reliable and efficient GPS tracker that offers peace of mind for business owners. Its rugged design, long battery life, and user-friendly interface make it an ideal choice for managing containers, trailers, large equipment, and point of service machines. Whether you need to improve asset visibility, increase dispatch efficiency, or enhance security, the AT-5000 is a versatile solution that delivers exceptional performance.

### Key Features:

- Large battery capacity
- Waterproof rating IP67
- Heatproof casing, up to 85 °C
- No-power-button design
- Activated by timer and movement
- Pin-point accurate cell locations
- AGPS capabilities
- Request current location by phone call
- Stand-by time up to 1 year under motion activation mode
- Tracking once an hour under timer activation mode, operating times of up to 50 days
- Superb power management
- SMS Google map hyperlinks to cell phones
