# ArkNav - AT-5000

<div className="device-logo">
![AT-5000](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The ArkNav AT-5000 is a rugged GPS tracker designed for long term deployments in challenging environments. It combines GPS positioning and GSM communication with a large capacity rechargeable battery, an IP67 rated waterproof housing, and heat resistant casing that tolerates high ambient temperatures. Activation options include timer and movement triggers, and the device supports remote location requests and SMS map links for convenient access to position information.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-5000 is well suited for asset and fleet visibility scenarios where low maintenance and reliable long duration tracking are priorities. Its extended standby operation and durable construction make it a practical choice to pair with Plaspy for consolidated monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight across containers, heavy equipment, trailers, and similar assets.

## Key Highlights

- Long standby operation with motion activated mode that can reach extended run times for low maintenance deployments
- Rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure and heat resistant design for reliable field use
- Multiple activation modes including timer and movement to balance tracking frequency and battery life
- Remote location request and SMS map link support for on demand position checks
- Designed for container, equipment, and asset management where long term visibility is needed

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the AT-5000 provides timely location updates and on demand position reports that Plaspy can display, archive, and present as part of fleet and asset management workflows. Plaspy ingests the device location data and makes it actionable for dispatch, security, and operational teams.

- Live and historical map visualization of AT-5000 reported positions within Plaspy
- Motion and timer based updates appear as track points for route playback and dwell analysis
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy can be tied to movement or schedule changes reported by the device
- Reporting and exportable activity logs for audits and operational review
- Use SMS location links and remote request responses as quick adjuncts to Plaspy tracking when needed

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term container monitoring in remote or port environments
- Tracking of heavy equipment and trailers between jobs or sites
- Asset security and recovery support for high value items
- Field service and point of service machine location oversight
- Private investigations and discrete on demand location checks

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT-5000 is aimed at organizations that need dependable, low maintenance positioning for assets operating away from frequent service intervals. Its combination of long battery life, durable housing, and flexible activation modes suits deployments where infrequent but accurate updates are sufficient, and where environmental resilience is essential.

Paired with Plaspy, the AT-5000 becomes part of a centralized visibility platform that helps teams monitor locations, analyze historical movement, and manage alerts and reports. That combination is helpful for operations focused on container logistics, equipment fleets, and remote asset oversight where both device endurance and platform capabilities matter.
