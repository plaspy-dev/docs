# ArkNav - AT-5000 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for using the ArkNav AT-5000 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It describes the device capabilities that are relevant to asset visibility, remote monitoring, and fleet or equipment management when the tracker is paired with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant we describe practical functions and how they are typically reflected in Plaspy, but please check manufacturer documentation for the latest device-specific details.

## Feature Overview

The ArkNav AT-5000 is a rugged, long-life GPS tracker intended for long-term deployments where durability and battery longevity are priorities. It combines GNSS and cellular capabilities with multiple activation modes and power management options to support extended unattended operation.

- Long capacity rechargeable battery designed for extended standby and periodic reporting
- Waterproof IP67 rated casing and heatproof plastic housing rated up to 85 °C for harsh environments
- Motion and timer activation modes to balance update frequency and battery life
- AGPS support and cell location reporting to improve position availability in challenging areas
- Remote location request via phone call and SMS Google map hyperlinks for convenient access to current location

## Core Features of ArkNav - AT-5000

- Large rechargeable battery supporting very long standby times under motion activation
- Motion activation mode for event driven wakeups and reporting
- Timer activation mode for scheduled position reporting with configurable intervals
- AGPS assistance to reduce time to first fix and improve location performance
- Pin-point accurate cell location reporting as a fallback where GNSS visibility is limited
- SMS Google map hyperlink support to share location directly to mobile phones
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and heatproof design for outdoor and industrial usage
- No power button design for tamper-resistant, always-ready deployments

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AT-5000 is used with Plaspy, its reporting behavior and rugged design map directly to the visibility and monitoring features Plaspy provides. The device's long battery life and activation modes help you tune reporting to match operational needs while Plaspy stores and presents received locations and events.

- Plaspy displays position reports received from the tracker on maps and device timelines
- Long standby and sparse reporting modes reduce required update frequency while retaining historical location history in Plaspy
- AGPS and cell location results feed into Plaspy as position updates so you can track assets even when GNSS signals are intermittent
- SMS Google map links can be used alongside Plaspy reporting to provide ad hoc location sharing to phones
- Motion and timer activation translate into the types of position events and intervals visible in Plaspy reporting and history

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking for long-term, unattended inventory visibility
- Monitoring large or portable equipment deployed in remote or harsh environments
- Security operations and evidence location for private investigations or asset recovery
- Point of service machine monitoring where infrequent but reliable reporting is preferred
- Logistics and dispatch oversight for assets that move infrequently but require periodic checks

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific behavior can vary between firmware versions; firmware updates may add or change features and power profiles
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect cellular compatibility and exact performance characteristics
- Installation method and placement of the tracker influence GNSS and cell location performance
- Some convenience functions such as phone-call location requests or SMS links depend on network and carrier support
- Always verify the feature set and limits for your purchased unit against the manufacturer documentation
