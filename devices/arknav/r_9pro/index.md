# ArkNav - R-9PRO

The R-9PRO is a real time vehicle GPS tracker from ArkNav designed for fleet management, anti theft protection, and continuous telemetry in demanding environments. Built with a rugged aluminum housing and a compact footprint, the R-9PRO combines satellite positioning with mobile data transmission and over-the-air programming to support reliable location reporting and remote configuration. The device includes a comprehensive I/O set, an internal backup battery, and an optional waterproof variant for extended operational flexibility.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the R-9PRO can be integrated into Plaspy’s fleet platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s location updates and configurable I/O events feed into dashboards, alerts, and reports that help fleet operators monitor vehicles, respond to incidents, and manage remote device settings without frequent field visits.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for seamless real time tracking and centralized fleet management.
- Rugged aluminum case with a compact form factor suitable for vehicle installations.
- Satellite positioning paired with mobile data transmission for continuous location updates.
- Extensive configurable I/O lines and analog inputs to capture vehicle events and sensor data.
- Internal backup battery to maintain tracking after main power loss.
- Over the air programming and firmware updates for remote maintenance and configuration.
- Optional waterproof variant (R-9W V2) and Bluetooth support for integration options.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The R-9PRO transmits GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry to Plaspy where data is visualized, recorded, and acted upon. Plaspy ingests the device’s location stream and input events to deliver real time visibility, configurable alerts, and historical reporting that support operational decision making.

- Real time location updates and live vehicle positions displayed on Plaspy maps.
- Event driven alerts based on configurable I/O such as ignition, door, and alarm signals.
- Analog sensor telemetry such as fuel level and temperature available for reporting and trend analysis.
- Panic and immobilizer inputs coordinated with Plaspy alerts to support anti theft responses.
- Remote configuration and over the air updates reduce the need for physical device access.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, vans, and light trucks with live tracking and route monitoring.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using immobilizer control and panic reporting.
- Fuel monitoring and basic telemetry to support cost control and preventive maintenance.
- Driver and security event logging including driver ID checks and door/alarm monitoring.
- Deployments in harsh environments where rugged hardware and backup power are important.
