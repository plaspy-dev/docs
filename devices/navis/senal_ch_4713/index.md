# Navis - Señal CH - 4713

![Señal CH - 4713](./logo.jpg)

The Navis Señal CH-4713 is a GPS tracker designed specifically for vehicles. It is equipped with GLONASS, GPS, and SBAS-enabled mode A-GNSS, allowing for real-time monitoring of the vehicle's location, condition, and route. This on-board navigation and communication terminal is perfect for fleet management and vehicle tracking applications.

The CH-4713 offers a range of outstanding features that make it a reliable and efficient GPS tracker. It supports modes like "Assisted GNSS" for enhanced positioning accuracy. It also has protection against unauthorized disconnection of connectors and antennas, ensuring the tracker remains operational at all times. The CH-4713 can be configured over the air, eliminating the need for physical access to the device.

With its built-in navigation device and display in the cab, the CH-4713 provides easy access to navigation information for drivers. It also offers advanced configuration algorithms for optimal performance. The tracker has the ability to block certain vehicle units and even allows for covert listening booths. Additionally, it features a system optimization of the length of the transmitted message, ensuring efficient data transmission.

The Navis Señal CH-4713 is a powerful GPS tracker that offers accurate and stable determination of coordinates, even in weak and multipath environments. It provides a wide range of functionalities, including receiving navigation signals, transmitting vehicle information, enabling communication between the monitoring center and connected devices, and facilitating voice communication between operators and drivers. With its robust specifications and versatile features, the CH-4713 is an excellent choice for vehicle tracking and fleet management needs.

### Key Features:

- GLONASS, GPS, and SBAS-enabled mode A-GNSS
- Assisted GNSS mode for enhanced positioning accuracy
- Protection against unauthorized disconnection of connectors and antennas
- Configuration over the air
- Built-in navigation device with a display in the cab
- Advanced configuration algorithms
- Blocking action on vehicle units
- Possibility of covert listening booths
- System optimization of the length of the transmitted message
- Built-in driver identification \(iButton\)
- Programmable security functions
