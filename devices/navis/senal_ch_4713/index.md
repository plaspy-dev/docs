# Navis - Señal CH - 4713

<div className="device-logo">
![Señal CH - 4713](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Navis Señal CH-4713 is a vehicle GPS tracker and on board navigation terminal designed for fleet and vehicle tracking. It supports GLONASS, GPS and SBAS enabled A GNSS modes and offers assisted GNSS functionality for improved positioning. The unit includes an in cab display and navigation functionality, onboard event recording, and protections to reduce the risk of unauthorized disconnection of connectors and antennas.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CH-4713 can feed vehicle location, routing and status information into Plaspy to support fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Its support for over the air configuration, driver identification features, and messaging optimization make it a practical option for organizations that want remote device management and continuous visibility through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Multi constellation positioning with GLONASS GPS and SBAS enabled GNSS modes for stable location determination
- Assisted GNSS support to enhance positioning performance in challenging conditions
- Built in navigation and cab display to provide drivers with route information and status at the vehicle
- Over the air configuration capability for remote parameter updates and device management
- Protective measures against unauthorized disconnection of connectors and antennas
- Driver identification and programmable security functions to support operational control

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and status data from compatible trackers like the CH-4713 to present live vehicle position, historical routes, and event information in a single fleet view. Integration focuses on operational visibility, alerting, and reporting rather than low level device configuration details.

- Real time location and route playback in Plaspy for tracking vehicle movements and history
- Event and status reporting to trigger alerts and workflows for fleet managers
- Use of driver identification events to link trips and driver behavior to individual operators
- Remote configuration and management of device parameters via Plaspy where supported by the device
- Aggregated reporting and exportable data for operational analysis and compliance needs

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and route monitoring for logistics and transport operators
- Driver identification and trip attribution for payroll and compliance processes
- Remote device management and configuration for large vehicle fleets
- Asset protection and tamper detection using anti disconnection features
- In cab navigation and communication support that helps drivers follow assigned routes

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Señal CH-4713 pairs GNSS positioning capability with practical fleet features such as an in cab display, driver identification, and over the air configuration. Those capabilities make it a suitable option for organizations looking to centralize vehicle visibility and device management within Plaspy without relying on frequent physical access to hardware.

Because the CH-4713 is designed as an on board navigation and communication terminal, it can serve fleets that need both driver facing navigation and centralized monitoring. When connected to Plaspy, the device contributes to a fuller operational picture that helps dispatchers and fleet managers track routes, respond to events, and generate reports for performance review.
