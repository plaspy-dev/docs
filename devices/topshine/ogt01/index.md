# TopShine - OGT01

![OGT01](./tracker.jpg)

The OGT01 OBD II Car Tracking Device is a compact, plug-and-play GPS tracker designed for vehicles and fleet assets that require reliable, continuous location and diagnostics data. Built to fit directly into any standard OBD-II port, the OGT01 provides Plaspy compatible real-time tracking, onboard telemetry and OBD diagnostics without the need for complicated wiring or a separate power source.

With built-in GPS and GSM antennas, LBS/GPS dual locating, an internal 2 MB data logger for GSM/GPRS blind spots and a backup battery that can keep the unit reporting for up to 15 hours in power-saving mode, the OGT01 is targeted at fleet management, anti-theft workflows and operators who want vehicle-level fuel monitoring and engine telemetry accessible through a free web platform and mobile app.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD II installation for fast deployment across cars and light vehicles — no professional installation required.
- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking via SMS and GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) with a complimentary web platform and Android app \(demo: www.track-car.com; mobile: http://www.track-car.com/M; demo/officetest: officetest / 123\).
- Onboard OBD diagnostics and telemetry including throttle position, engine speed, average fuel consumption, temperature, live load and fault/error codes for actionable vehicle insights.
- Continuous power from the vehicle with a built-in backup battery that supports up to 15 hours of tracking in power-saving mode if the car battery is drained.
- 2 MB internal data logger that records position and events while in GSM/GPRS blind areas, automatically uploading stored data when connectivity is restored.
- Operational alarms and safety features: geo-fence alerts, over-speed alarms, movement alerts, odometer reporting and engine on/off detection for anti-theft and fleet control.
- Commercial-ready: free lifetime tracking platform provided by the manufacturer, CE/FCC/RoHS certifications, 2-year warranty and OEM/ODM customization options.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed in the vehicle OBD port, the OGT01 streams location and diagnostic data to the Plaspy-compatible platform using GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) or SMS. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and OBD reports, consolidating location, engine parameters and event alerts into real-time dashboards, geofencing rules, and exportable reports for fleet managers and vehicle owners.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GPRS/TCP/UDP and SMS for immediate visibility on Plaspy dashboards.
- Engine/ignition status reporting \(engine on/off\) for stop/start analysis and anti-theft monitoring.
- Fuel monitoring and OBD-based telemetry: average fuel consumption, engine speed and throttle position available for fuel-efficiency reporting.
- Event-driven alerts pushed to Plaspy: geo-fence violations, over-speed, movement and stored error codes.
- Offline logging: the 2 MB internal data logger caches location and event data during connectivity gaps and syncs when the device reconnects to the network.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: continuous location, OBD telemetry and fuel consumption reporting for route optimization and cost control.
- Anti-theft and recovery: engine on/off detection, movement alerts and geo-fence alarms to detect unauthorized use and trigger timely responses.
- Remote diagnostics: real-time error codes and live engine parameters to reduce downtime and prioritize maintenance.
- Vehicle utilization & driver behavior: odometer reporting, over-speed alarms and throttle/engine speed telemetry to monitor usage and encourage safer driving.
- Backup tracking for intermittent coverage: internal data logger caches trips and events while in GSM blind spots, syncing later to the platform.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The OGT01 offers a balanced combination of plug-and-play simplicity, robust OBD telemetry and Plaspy compatible real-time tracking that suits small fleets, rental operators and individual vehicle owners. Its direct OBD connection eliminates hardwiring and provides access to standard OBD parameters \(fuel consumption, engine speed, throttle position and error codes\) so Plaspy can deliver richer telemetry and actionable reports without expensive retrofitting.

Commercial advantages include a free lifetime tracking platform, Android app access, OEM/ODM options, CE/FCC/RoHS compliance and a 2-year warranty. Operational features such as an internal 2 MB data logger and a backup battery extend tracking continuity in low-coverage areas or during battery drain events, making the OGT01 a practical choice for businesses focused on reliability, cost control and easy Plaspy integration.
