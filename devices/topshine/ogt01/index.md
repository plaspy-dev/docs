# TopShine - OGT01

<div className="device-logo">
![OGT01](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TopShine OGT01 is a compact plug and play OBD II car tracking device designed for vehicles and light fleet assets. It fits into a standard OBD II port to provide continuous vehicle location and on board diagnostics without additional wiring. The unit includes built in GPS and GSM antennas, LBS and GPS dual locating, a 2 MB internal data logger for periods with limited cellular coverage, and a backup battery that can keep reporting for extended periods in power saving mode.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the OGT01 can stream location and telemetry to a Plaspy enabled platform, allowing fleet managers and vehicle owners to combine GPS tracking with OBD level diagnostics. That combination makes the device relevant to organizations that need both live visibility and vehicle level insights accessible through Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II installation for fast deployment across cars and light vehicles.
- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking and telemetry reporting via GPRS and SMS.
- Onboard OBD diagnostics including fuel consumption estimates, engine speed, throttle position and fault code reporting.
- Continuous vehicle power with an internal backup battery that supports extended reporting in power saving mode.
- 2 MB internal data logger caches position and events during cellular blind spots and uploads when connectivity returns.
- Operational alerts such as geo fence, over speed, movement alerts, odometer reporting and engine on off detection for fleet control and anti theft workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed in a vehicle OBD port the OGT01 streams location and OBD telemetry to a Plaspy compatible backend, where that information is normalized and presented in dashboards, maps and reports for operational use. Plaspy consolidates the device data with rule based alerts and historical reporting to support fleet oversight and maintenance planning.

- Real time location updates and telemetry appear on Plaspy maps and live views for immediate visibility.
- Engine and ignition status reporting supports stop start analysis and helps detect unauthorized use.
- Fuel monitoring and OBD based telemetry are available inside Plaspy for efficiency and cost reporting.
- Event driven alerts such as geo fence violations, over speed, movement and logged fault codes can be delivered to operators.
- Offline caching from the internal data logger synchronizes historical position and events to Plaspy when the unit regains connectivity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for route tracking, utilization monitoring and fuel efficiency analysis.
- Anti theft and recovery with movement alerts, engine on off detection and geo fence notifications.
- Remote diagnostics to prioritize maintenance using live error codes and engine parameters.
- Driver behavior and vehicle utilization monitoring through odometer reporting and over speed alerts.
- Backup tracking in areas with intermittent cellular coverage using the internal data logger.

The OGT01 combines straightforward deployment with vehicle level diagnostics, making it a practical option for operators who want richer telemetry without complex installation. Paired with Plaspy, the device enables integrated fleet monitoring where location, engine parameters and event alerts are visible in one platform for operational decision making.
