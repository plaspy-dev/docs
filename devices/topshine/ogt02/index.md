# TopShine - OGT02

![OGT02](./tracker.jpg)

The TopShine OGT02 is a 4G OBD2 GPS tracker designed for fast plug and play deployment in vehicles. It installs in the vehicle OBD II port so no hard wiring or professional installation is required. The unit combines cellular communication, a high precision GPS receiver, and a built in accelerometer to provide continuous location, event alerts, and vehicle telemetry to compatible platforms.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the OGT02 supplies the core telematics that Plaspy uses for fleet visibility and operational oversight. Position updates, movement detection, odometer reporting and historical track playback are available to Plaspy dashboards and alerting workflows, making the OGT02 a practical choice for fleet managers, rental operators, and individual vehicle owners who want centralized tracking and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II installation for rapid rollout and minimal vehicle downtime
- Plaspy compatibility for centralized real time tracking, dashboards and alerts
- 4G connectivity with support for 2G and 3G fallback and GPRS or SMS working modes
- High precision GPS positioning together with a built in accelerometer for movement and impact detection
- Vehicle telemetry via OBD including odometer reporting and diagnostic PIDs where supported by the vehicle
- Compact CE certified form factor suited to vehicle environments and scalable deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

The OGT02 transmits location and available OBD telemetry to Plaspy over cellular links, enabling live tracking, automated alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests the device data to display vehicle positions, raise notifications from accelerometer events, and populate trip and vehicle health reports.

- Real time location updates on Plaspy maps for live fleet monitoring and route oversight
- Movement and sudden event alerts from the built in accelerometer for theft detection and safety monitoring
- OBD based telemetry such as odometer and supported diagnostic PIDs surfaced in Plaspy reports
- Geofence and displacement alarms processed by Plaspy to notify managers of unauthorized movements
- Historical track playback accessible within Plaspy for trip reconstruction and compliance review

## Typical Use Cases

- Distributed fleet management with centralized visibility for dispatch and operations teams
- Rental and car sharing fleets where fast plug and play installation reduces vehicle downtime
- Anti theft monitoring and rapid response using movement alerts and geofence notifications
- Remote diagnostics and preventive maintenance workflows using OBD telemetry where available
- Compliance, route verification and incident reconstruction using historical track playback

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The OGT02 offers a pragmatic mix of simplicity and telematics capability for organizations using Plaspy. Its OBD II form factor makes deployment fast and cost efficient, while integrated GPS and accelerometer inputs give Plaspy the signals needed for live tracking, event alerts, and trip reporting. Where a vehicle exposes additional OBD PIDs, Plaspy can surface those fields to help correlate driving behavior with vehicle diagnostics.
