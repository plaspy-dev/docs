# TopShine - OGT02

![OGT02](./tracker.jpg)

The OGT02 4G OBD2 GPS tracker is a plug-and-play device designed for fast deployment and reliable Plaspy compatible real-time tracking. Made for fleet managers, rental companies, and individual vehicle owners, the OGT02 installs directly in the vehicle’s OBD‑II port so there is no wiring or professional installation required. It combines GSM/GPRS communication, a high‑precision GPS receiver, and a built‑in accelerometer to deliver continuous location, event alerts, and OBD telemetry to Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the OGT02 delivers essential telematics — position, movement detection, odometer reporting and historical track playback — while supporting GPRS and SMS working modes for global connectivity. Its compact OBD form factor and CE certification make it an efficient solution for vehicle health monitoring, anti‑theft alerts, and fleet management workflows with fast, scalable rollout across multiple vehicles.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD‑II installation — install in seconds with no wiring or garage time, ideal for rapid fleet deployment.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and centralized fleet management using Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- 4G \(with 2G/3G support\) GSM/GPRS communication for worldwide connectivity and reliable data uplink.
- High‑precision GPS positioning \(GPS frequency 1575.42 MHz\) plus a built‑in accelerometer for displacement and impact detection.
- Vehicle telemetry via OBD: odometer reporting, diagnostic PIDs and other OBD data available to Plaspy where supported by the vehicle.
- Security features including geofence \(electronic fence\), displacement alarm, and historical track playback up to 180 days.
- Compact, durable form factor with wide operating temperature and humidity range for real-world vehicle environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

OGT02 sends location and OBD telemetry to Plaspy using GPRS or SMS, enabling real‑time tracking, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests the device’s GPS coordinates, accelerometer events, and available OBD data to present live positions on Google Maps, trigger geofence notifications, and populate trip and vehicle health reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live vehicle tracking and route monitoring.
- Movement and sudden-event alerts from the built-in accelerometer for anti‑theft and safety monitoring.
- OBD‑based telemetry \(engine PIDs, odometer and fuel‑related data where available from the vehicle\) surfaced in Plaspy reports.
- Geofence enforcement and displacement alarms processed by Plaspy to notify managers of unauthorized movements.
- Historical track playback \(up to 180 days\) accessible within Plaspy for trip reconstruction and compliance checks.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

OGT02 offers a practical balance of ease‑of‑use and telematics capability for organizations that need Plaspy compatible GPS tracking without complex installation. Its OBD‑II form factor reduces deployment time and labor costs, while the integrated GPS and accelerometer provide the core inputs Plaspy needs for real‑time tracking, anti‑theft alerts, and telemetry dashboards. Where vehicle OBD supports additional PIDs, Plaspy surfaces fuel monitoring, ignition status and engine data so fleet managers can correlate driving behavior with diagnostics and fuel use.

Choosing the OGT02 with Plaspy delivers a reliable, scalable telemetry foundation — fast installation, robust connectivity \(2G/3G/4G\), and long historical playback — making it a solid option for fleets, rental fleets, and owners who want immediate insights and centralized management. If you require external immobilizer control, Bluetooth sensors, or other integrations, Plaspy can integrate those capabilities when the vehicle or additional hardware supports them, allowing you to expand functionality over time without replacing your core tracking units.
