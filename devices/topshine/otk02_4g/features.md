# TopShine - OTK02-4G Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the TopShine OTK02-4G and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for vehicle monitoring. It covers the core, user facing functions you can expect when using the OTK02-4G as a Plaspy compatible tracker and highlights operational value for fleet managers and vehicle owners.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation type. For device specific limits, alarm behavior, or the latest technical specifications consult the official manufacturer documentation in addition to your Plaspy configuration settings.

## Feature Overview

The OTK02-4G is a plug and play OBD II tracker intended for fast deployment and continuous reporting to cloud platforms such as Plaspy. It combines cellular location reporting, OBD sourced telemetry, and offline resilience to provide live visibility and historical playback for vehicle monitoring workflows.

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for quick installation without hard wiring.
- 4G LTE CAT1 connectivity with GSM GPRS fallback to maintain telemetry across networks.
- OBD diagnostics exposure including mileage and engine on off status for maintenance and utilization tracking.
- AGPS assisted positioning for faster fixes and improved route playback.
- Built in offline logger and backup battery to preserve data through temporary network loss.
- Alarm support for geo fence, towing or movement, overspeed, and low battery conditions to drive alerts.

## Core Features of TopShine - OTK02-4G

- OBD II plug and play installation for immediate use in compatible vehicles.
- Cellular connectivity using 4G LTE CAT1 with fallback to GSM GPRS for broader coverage.
- OBD level telemetry including mileage reporting and engine on off detection.
- AGPS assistance for faster time to first fix and reliable positioning.
- Onboard flash data logger for offline storage during network outages.
- Integrated backup battery to keep minimal logging active in GSM blind spots.
- Support for common alarms such as geo fence, towing or movement alerts, overspeed, and battery low.
- Lifetime access to the manufacturer supplied web and Android app for tracking and reports.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the OTK02-4G is used as a Plaspy compatible device, its location and OBD sourced messages are ingested into Plaspy so they appear in dashboards, event logs, and historical reports. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and normalizes incoming data to present vehicle status and events alongside other fleet assets.

- Real time location updates appear in Plaspy maps for live monitoring and tracking.
- OBD derived engine on off and ignition status are surfaced in Plaspy event logs for trip segmentation.
- Mileage and trip history reported from the OBD interface become available for maintenance planning and utilization reports.
- Alarm events such as geo fence, towing or movement, overspeed, and low battery are forwarded as notifications and logged for auditing.
- Offline logger entries and buffered telemetry sync to Plaspy when connectivity is restored, enabling continuous historical playback.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch where fast installation and OBD mileage help optimize vehicle utilization.
- Anti theft and security monitoring using geo fence, movement alerts, and low battery notifications.
- Preventive maintenance planning based on OBD reported mileage and trip logs.
- Rental and shared vehicle programs that need quick installation and returnable trackers.
- Temporary or seasonal deployment for distributed assets where wiring is not practical.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware revisions can change available reports, alarm thresholds, or message formats. Verify the installed firmware for exact behavior.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect supported cellular bands and network fallback behavior.
- Some features depend on vehicle OBD implementation and may vary between makes and models.
- Offline logger capacity and backup battery runtime are finite and depend on device configuration and reporting frequency.
- Manufacturer supplied apps or services may provide additional tooling but Plaspy will present the telemetry received from the device.
