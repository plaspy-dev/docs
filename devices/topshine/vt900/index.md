# TopShine - VT900

![VT900](./logo.jpg)

The TopShine VT900 is an advanced GPS/GSM/GPRS tracking device designed for real-time tracking and security of vehicles. Equipped with superior GPS and GSM modules, the VT900 offers excellent sensitivity and stable performance. It is specifically designed to work seamlessly with our web-based tracking system, making it ideal for companies looking to establish a tracking server or manage their fleet. The VT900 is a fully functional tracker that supports camera and voice prompt/broadcasting functions, and it offers strong expandability and customization options.

With the VT900, you can track your vehicles in real-time using SMS or GPRS \(TCP/UDP\). It also supports tracking based on time or distance intervals. The device features an OTA \(Over-The-Air\) function, allowing for easy firmware updates. You can track your vehicles in real-time using our web-platform, and you can even get the position location via call or SMS. The VT900 also offers engine on/off detection, built-in backup battery, remote engine cut off for added security, and wiretapping/remote listening capabilities.

Other notable features of the VT900 include mileage calculation, door open/close status detection and control, built-in motion sensor for power and GPRS flow saving mode, 4MB memory with position logging capacity, and various alerts such as over speed, geo-fence, power failure/low power, SOS panic button, harsh braking and acceleration, and sudden acceleration/brake alerts. With its comprehensive features and reliable performance, the TopShine VT900 is an excellent choice for vehicle real-time tracking, security, and fleet management.
