# TopShine - MT06 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the TopShine MT06 GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities map into Plaspy for fast deployment into fleet and security workflows. It highlights practical, user-facing functions and the operational value the MT06 brings when paired with Plaspy for motorcycle and small vehicle tracking.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and installation method. For the latest device-specific details, firmware notes and installation guidance consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The MT06 is a compact, IMEI-registered GPS tracker built for motorcycles and small vehicles that emphasizes discreet installation, low power consumption and reliable position reporting. It is Plaspy compatible and designed to provide continuous location, speed and status reporting together with alarms and remote control features useful for fleet and anti-theft scenarios.

- Real-time GPS position and movement reporting for live tracking and historical playback.
- Remote relay control to support engine cut-off as part of anti-theft response workflows.
- Built-in geofence and over-speed alarm functions for perimeter and compliance monitoring.
- Low-power design to reduce mobile data and operating costs while maintaining regular telemetry.
- Waterproof mini housing with integrated GPS and GSM antennas for concealed installations.

## Core Features of TopShine - MT06

- IMEI registered tracker suitable for lawful deployment and device identification.
- Integrated GPS and GSM antennas in a compact, waterproof enclosure for discreet mounting.
- Low-power firmware and design to minimize GPRS data usage during normal operation.
- Continuous reporting of position, speed and device status for operational visibility.
- Relay control to enable remote engine cut-off and resume actions via supported workflows.
- Geofence support for perimeter monitoring and automated event generation.
- Over-speed alarms for speed compliance and incident detection.
- SMS location reply capability including Google Map links for quick manual lookups.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the MT06 is connected to Plaspy, its position fixes, status messages and alarm events are ingested into the platform so teams can monitor assets on maps, review playback and respond to incidents from a single interface. Plaspy detects compatible tracker messages and surfaces device events alongside other fleet telemetry.

- Live map views and location updates show the MT06 position and movement for real-time monitoring.
- Geo-fence and over-speed events appear as alerts in Plaspy for immediate notification and record keeping.
- Relay-controlled engine cut-off can be coordinated through Plaspy workflows to support incident response.
- Power loss and mileage or odometer reports are available for maintenance tracking and tamper awareness.
- SMS location replies provide a fallback when data connectivity is limited and can be used alongside Plaspy monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle anti-theft and recovery where discreet mounting and rapid intervention matter.
- Small fleet tracking for scooters, light vehicles and compact delivery fleets.
- Last-mile logistics and delivery operations that need compact hardware and event reporting.
- Rental or shared vehicle monitoring combining location visibility with remote immobilization.
- Preventive maintenance programs using power cut and mileage telemetry to schedule service.
- Compliance monitoring for routes and speed rules with geofence and over-speed alerts.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature support depends on the device firmware and the specific hardware revision installed in each unit.
- Regional variants may have different cellular band support and regulatory markings; check the vendor band list for your region.
