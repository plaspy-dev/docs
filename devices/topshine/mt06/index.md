# TopShine - MT06

![MT06](./tracker.jpg)

The MT06 is a compact, IMEI-registered GPS tracker engineered for motorcycles and small vehicles and is fully Plaspy compatible for fast deployment into your tracking stack. With integrated GPS and GSM antennas, a waterproof mini housing and a low-power design that minimizes GPRS data consumption, the MT06 delivers reliable real-time tracking and security without adding bulk. Certified to CE, FCC and RoHS standards, the unit is ready for commercial fleet management and anti-theft applications where concealment and durability matter.

Designed to work with the free web and mobile platform provided by the manufacturer \(https://www.track-car.com – demo: Officetest / 123\), the MT06 supplies continuous location, speed and status reporting to Plaspy. It also supports remote engine cut-off via relay control, geo-fencing, over-speed alarms and SMS location replies with Google Map links, making it a practical GPS tracker choice for operators who need actionable telemetry, rapid incident response and easy Plaspy integration.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking: seamless integration of GPS position and status into Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- Anti-theft immobilizer support: remote engine cut-off and resume through relay control for rapid intervention.
- Compact, waterproof form factor with concealed GSM/GPS antennas — ideal for motorcycle and small vehicle installation.

- Built-in geofence, over-speed alarm and odometer reporting for fleet management and compliance monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the MT06 transmits GPS position fixes, movement telemetry and alarm events over its GSM data connection or SMS channel. Plaspy ingests these messages to provide live map views, historical playback, event notifications and fleet reports. The device’s relay control and alarm signals can trigger Plaspy workflows \(alerts, immobilize commands, or follow-up actions\) so operations teams get immediate, actionable data.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GPS position, speed and status flow into Plaspy for live tracking and reporting.
- Geo-fence and over-speed events: perimeter breaches and speed violations are sent as alarms to Plaspy for instant notification.
- Remote immobilizer \(engine cut-off\) via relay: authorized stop and resume commands can be coordinated through Plaspy workflows.
- External power cut alarm and odometer reporting: power loss and mileage telemetry are available for maintenance and anti-tamper detection.
- SMS location replies with Google Map links: quick lookup when data connectivity is limited; links are usable alongside Plaspy tools.
