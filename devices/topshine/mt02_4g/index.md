# MT02-4G GPS Tracker — Plaspy Compatible

The MT02-4G is a compact 4G vehicle GPS tracker purpose-built for fleet management, anti-theft protection and advanced vehicle telemetry. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the MT02-4G pairs with Plaspy’s web and mobile interfaces to deliver real-time tracking, historical route playback and configurable alerts for commercial fleets, cars and motorcycles.

Designed for reliability in commercial vehicle environments, the MT02-4G combines a high-performance ARM9 microcontroller with robust power handling, a 500mAh backup battery and onsite data logging. Its OTA firmware upgrade capability and free tracking apps make deployment and maintenance simple for operations teams who need accurate GPS tracker data integrated into Plaspy for reporting and operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: seamless integration with Plaspy web and mobile apps for live location and management.
- 4G connectivity for future-proof cellular coverage and reliable real-time tracking.
- Built-in 2MB data logger \(expandable to 64MB\) storing ~26,000 offline points for uninterrupted telemetry.
- Wide input voltage \(DC 9V–90V\) and EV-level power processing for stable operation on cars, trucks and motorcycles.
- 500mAh backup battery for offline positioning and emergency reporting when main power is lost.
- OTA \(over-the-air\) firmware upgrades via SMS or platform — reduce service visits and keep devices current.
- Support for fuel monitoring, driver identification and remote immobilizer control via optional accessories.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the MT02-4G is connected to Plaspy, location and vehicle telemetry flow into Plaspy’s dashboard and mobile apps in real time. The device sends GPS coordinates, motion and I/O status over 4G; Plaspy displays live position, route history and event-driven alerts. OTA firmware updates can be initiated from the platform or by SMS command, so your fleet stays up to date without physical intervention.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via 4G.
- Event alerts supported: overspeed, geo-fence, SOS panic, harsh braking/acceleration and power failure/low battery.
- Fuel monitoring and leak/refuel alarms when ultrasonic or cuttable fuel sensors are installed and integrated.
- Remote immobilizer capability when used with relay accessory \(engine cut-off control\) for anti-theft response.
- Offline logging and automatic upload: up to ~26,000 stored points in the built-in 2MB logger for post-event analysis.
