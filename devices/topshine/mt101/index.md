# TopShine - MT101

<div className="device-logo">
![MT101](./logo..png)
</div>

The TopShine MT101 is a compact online GPS motorcycle tracker designed for discreet mounting and continuous location monitoring. Built for high sensitivity and waterproof performance, the MT101 includes a built in antenna and a small form factor that makes concealment straightforward. Key on device capabilities from the manufacturer include dual SIM support with automatic or manual network switching, the ability to switch to a foreign network without roaming charges, GSM signal jamming detection, two way positioning even when GPS signal is limited, and optional features such as a relay for engine cutoff, microphone for voice listening, and an SOS panic button.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the MT101 can feed real time location and status updates into the Plaspy platform for map visualization, alerting, and operational oversight. Plaspy can leverage the MT101’s reporting options to present geofence events, movement history, power and engine status, and other notifications alongside your wider fleet or asset data. This makes the MT101 a practical option for organizations that want a compact motorcycle tracker integrated into a broader monitoring and fleet management workflow on Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact waterproof construction and built in antenna for easy concealment on motorcycles
- Dual SIM support with automatic or manual switching and intelligent foreign network selection
- GSM signal jamming detection to help detect interference with tracking
- Two way positioning that can provide location information even when GPS signals are weak
- Optional relay for remote engine cutoff and optional SOS and voice listening features
- Real time tracking via SMS or GPRS for web platform integration and continuous monitoring
- Wide voltage compatibility and an internal rechargeable backup battery for power resilience

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the MT101 sends location and status updates that Plaspy displays on maps and in reports, enabling centralized visibility and alerting for motorcycles and other small vehicles. Integration focuses on delivering actionable location data and device state information to support everyday fleet and security operations.

- Live location and historical routes shown on Plaspy maps for visibility and auditing
- Alerting for geofence events, over speed conditions, SOS triggers, power cut, and jamming detection
- Device status and engine on/off state presented in Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight
- Use of the MT101’s remote relay feature where enabled to support immobilization workflows within Plaspy
- Consolidated reporting and exports for fleet management, compliance, and maintenance planning
