# TopShine - MT101

![MT101](./logo..png)

The TopShine MT101 \(also known as the MT210\) is an online tracking mini GPS motorcycle tracker designed for high sensitivity and waterproof performance. With its compact size and built-in antenna, it is easy to install and conceal on your motorcycle. This tracker supports the installation of two SIM cards, allowing for automatic or manual switching to the best network. It also has the ability to intelligently switch to a foreign network without roaming charges. Additionally, it features GSM signal jamming detection, ensuring the security and reliability of your tracking system.

One of the standout features of the TopShine MT101 is its two-way positioning capability. Even when there is no GPS signal, it can still show the position on the map, providing you with accurate and real-time tracking information. The tracker also has a relay for remotely cutting off the engine \(optional feature\), giving you added control and security. Real-time tracking can be done through SMS or GPRS using the web platform, allowing you to monitor your motorcycle's location and movement at any time. The wide voltage support of 9V - 36V DC ensures compatibility with a variety of motorcycles.

The TopShine MT101 is equipped with a 650mAh inbuilt rechargeable battery, providing backup power in case of a power failure. It also has a microphone for voice listening \(optional feature\) and an anti-robbery SOS button for emergency situations \(optional feature\). The tracker offers ultra power-saving capabilities, extending the battery life and reducing the need for frequent recharging. Other notable features include car alarm functions, real address name/Google Map link retrieval via SMS, geo-fence alerts, over-speed alerts, and engine ON/OFF detection.

With its versatile functionality and durable design, the TopShine MT101 is an ideal choice for vehicle tracking, fleet management, equipment security, asset tracking, and even RFID alarm applications \(optional feature\). Its compact size, waterproof construction, and easy installation make it a reliable and discreet tracking solution for motorcycles. Whether you're a motorcycle enthusiast or a business owner looking to protect your assets, the TopShine MT101 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that will meet your tracking needs.

### Technical Specifications:

- Dimension: 80\*58\*22mm
- Weight: 95g
- Input voltage: DC +9V~+36V/1.5A
- Back-up Battery: 650mAh
- Operating Temperature: -20°C~75°C
- Humidity: 5%~95% Non-condensing
- Altitude Limit: 18,000 meters \(60,000 feet\) max
- LED: 2 LED lights to show GPS, GSM status
- Cold start: 42sec., average
- Hot Start: 1 sec., average
- GSM Frequency: 850/900/1800/1900Mhz
- GPS Sensitivity: -159dB
- I/O: 2 Input, 1 Output, 1 USB Port
