# TopShine - MT101 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TopShine MT101 (also marketed as MT210) and how its capabilities are typically used with the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the model's practical functions such as compact waterproof design, dual SIM support, GSM jamming detection, two way positioning even when GPS is unavailable, and common alert types that can be reported into Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional parts installed, and the way the device is integrated and installed on a vehicle. Optional items mentioned in manufacturer materials such as the relay, microphone, SOS button, or RFID alarm may not be present on every unit or may be enabled only on specific firmware builds, so check the manufacturer documentation for the precise details on your unit.

## Feature Overview

The TopShine MT101 is a compact, waterproof motorcycle tracker designed for concealed installation and continuous online tracking. It emphasizes reliable network connectivity through dual SIM support and intelligent network switching, and includes features aimed at theft detection and remote monitoring. Below are the most relevant practical capabilities that fleet operators and individual users commonly rely on.

- Compact waterproof housing with a built in antenna for discreet installation on motorcycles and small vehicles.
- Dual SIM support with automatic or manual switching and intelligent foreign network switching to maintain connectivity.
- GSM signal jamming detection to notify when cellular signals are being interfered with.
- Two way positioning that can provide a location on the map even when GPS signal is unavailable.
- Real time tracking via SMS or GPRS to a web platform, plus backup power from an internal rechargeable battery.
- Optional remote relay for engine cut off and optional emergency functions such as microphone listening and SOS button.

## Core Features of TopShine - MT101

- Dual SIM card slots with automatic/manual network switching and foreign network switching behavior.
- GSM signal jamming detection for improved security visibility.
- Two way positioning able to report location when GPS signals are weak or absent.
- Real time tracking support using SMS and GPRS to a web platform.
- Optional relay output for remote engine cut off where fitted and supported by firmware.
- Built in rechargeable backup battery and ultra power saving modes to extend operation during power loss.
- Wide input voltage compatibility to suit a variety of motorcycle power systems.
- Miniature, waterproof design with built in antenna for easy concealment and durable operation.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the MT101 can report location updates, alarm events, and status messages to the platform so operators can monitor vehicles and respond to incidents. Plaspy is designed to automatically detect tracker protocols, and devices can be configured to report to the Plaspy server domain. Integration behavior depends on the device firmware and the enabled features on the unit.

- Live location and movement shown on Plaspy maps when the MT101 sends position updates, including positions reported when GPS is not available.
- Alarm and event visibility in Plaspy for triggers such as geo fence breaches, overspeed alerts, external power cut off, and GSM jamming detection if the device reports those events.
- Battery and power loss notifications in the platform when the device transmits backup battery or external power status.
- Remote command capabilities such as relay control may be available in Plaspy when the tracker firmware supports remote commands and the integration exposes those commands.
- Plaspy auto detects the tracker protocol and supports configuring devices using common transport options so onboarding is typically straightforward.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle theft detection and recovery monitoring, with alerts for tampering and GSM jamming.
- Small fleet visibility for delivery scooters, couriers, and light vehicle fleets.
- Rental and sharing services that need discreet installed trackers and remote monitoring.
- Asset protection for mounted equipment and trailers where compact, waterproof units are required.
- Long term parking supervision with backup battery support to report power loss conditions.
- Remote monitoring for vehicles that operate in areas with variable cellular service through dual SIM switching.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Optional items such as the relay for engine cut off, microphone listening, SOS button, and RFID alarm are not guaranteed on every MT101 unit and depend on the specific product variant or installation.
- Firmware and hardware revisions can change available commands, reported fields, and alert behavior. Manufacturer firmware updates can enable or disable features.
- Installation choices such as placement, wiring, and antenna orientation can affect radio reception and GPS performance.
- Regional cellular banding and SIM provisioning influence network selection and foreign network switching behavior.
- Always check the manufacturer documentation and release notes for the exact feature list for your device serial number and firmware version.
