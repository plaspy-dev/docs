# TopShine - 2FD-100

<div className="device-logo">
![2FD-100](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The 2FD-100 is a two-way OBD2 GPS tracker combined with a smart 4G car alarm, designed for professional vehicle security and connectivity. As an integrated unit, it merges positioning, alarm inputs and outputs, a mobile WiFi hotspot and two-way voice communication to offer real-time tracking and anti-theft protection for demanding vehicle installations. Built with industrial grade components and a legal IMEI GSM/GPS module, the device is intended for fleet deployments and environments where reliable telemetry and alarm handling are important.

This model is compatible with Plaspy and can be configured to report positions, alarms and vehicle status to Plaspy for centralized monitoring and management. Its included connectivity and control features make it relevant for organizations that want to integrate camera monitoring, passenger WiFi and remote immobilization into their Plaspy workflows while maintaining the option to use TopShine's own platform out of the box.

## Key Highlights

- Two way OBD2 tracker combined with a smart 4G car alarm for integrated security and tracking.
- Built in WiFi hotspot to support passenger internet access and on board camera monitoring.
- Two way voice communication and remote control functions for lock unlock trunk release and immobilization when enabled.
- Alarm suite covering crash overspeed geofence movement detection and SOS panic alerts.
- Industrial grade construction with an independent GSM GPS module and legal IMEI for reliable telemetry.
- Expandable with optional accessories such as siren sensors and fuel monitoring options for tailored installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured for Plaspy, the 2FD-100 sends location updates, alarm events and vehicle status to Plaspy so fleet operators can view live positions, review history and respond to incidents from a single platform. Plaspy receives event data and exposes common monitoring and control functions that are typically used in vehicle management.

- Live location and historical route playback for fleet visibility and dispatch support.
- Event and alarm reporting including crash alerts geofence breaches overspeed and SOS notifications.
- Remote command delivery for lock unlock trunk release and optional immobilizer control through the Plaspy interface.
- Camera and WiFi device monitoring support to view connected cameras and two way audio where camera hardware provides it.
- Fleet level reporting and alerts to help with operational oversight maintenance planning and incident response.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for daily route oversight dispatching and asset tracking.
- Anti theft protection and rapid response using alarm events and remote immobilization.
- Passenger vehicles and VIP transport offering in vehicle WiFi and camera monitoring for safety and comfort.
- Rental and leasing operations needing geofence alerts crash and SOS monitoring and remote control capabilities.
- Fuel and consumption monitoring when optional fuel sensors are integrated for cost control and theft detection.
