# M35 GSM GPS Vehicle Tracker \(Model VT200\)

The VT200 \(M35 GSM\) is a professional GPS tracker built for businesses that require reliable Plaspy compatible tracking for fleet management, asset security and fuel monitoring. Designed for vehicle installations, the VT200 offers 2G GSM connectivity, an internal backup battery and a 2MB data logger to ensure continuous telemetry in areas with intermittent coverage. Its combination of remote engine control, multiple inputs/outputs and optional ultrasonic fuel sensing makes the unit a strong choice for operators who need anti-theft protection and accurate fuel monitoring integrated with Plaspy.

The VT200 integrates easily into monitoring platforms that accept standard GPRS/TCP/UDP or SMS position reports; when connected to Plaspy, it enables real-time tracking, alerts and historical reporting for fleets, rental vehicles and high-value assets. Supplied with GPS and GSM antennas, an SOS button and wiring harness, the VT200 is ready for professional installations that require immediate Plaspy compatible deployment.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking via GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) or SMS for reliable location feeds.
- Built-in 2MB data logger and 650mAh internal backup battery to maintain tracking during power loss or GSM blind spots.
- Optional ultrasonic no-drill fuel sensor support for accurate fuel monitoring without modifying the fuel tank.
- Multiple I/O for vehicle telemetry and anti-theft: 2 digital inputs, 1 analog input and 2 output ports for remote engine cut-off \(immobilizer\) or relay control.
- Standard vehicle features: geo-fence, over-speed alert, external power cut alarm, SOS panic button and motion sensing for power saving.
- Includes GPS and GSM antennas, SOS button, wiring harness and relay; free lifetime access to the manufacturer’s web and Android platforms.
- CE, FCC and RoHS certified with 2-year warranty and OEM/ODM support for fleet-scale deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT200 transmits position and telemetry to Plaspy using standard 2G GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) packets or SMS messages. Plaspy ingests GPS coordinates, input/output states and sensor telemetry to provide live maps, alerts and historical reports. The VT200’s data logger stores records when there is no GSM coverage and uploads them automatically when connectivity is restored, ensuring no gaps in fleet management data.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) or SMS to Plaspy.
- Ignition and digital input status reporting for event-driven alerts \(e.g., unauthorized engine start\).
- Fuel monitoring support with optional ultrasonic sensor for integration into Plaspy fuel reports and alerts.
- Remote immobilizer/engine cut-off via the device’s relay outputs, controllable from Plaspy where supported.
- Optional Bluetooth smartphone reader and accessories \(RFID, crash sensor, temperature\) are recognized through the device for extended telemetry.
