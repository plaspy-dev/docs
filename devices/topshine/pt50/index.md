# TopShine - PT50

![PT50](./tracker.jpg)

The TopShine PT50 is a 4G solar GPS tracker designed for long term unattended monitoring of containers, trailers, boats, vehicles and remote assets. Built for durability, the PT50 combines solar charging, a sealed IP67 enclosure and magnet adsorption mounting to extend deployments where mains power is unavailable and servicing is infrequent.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT50 can stream location and event data into Plaspy for real time visibility, alerts and historical playback. Its multi mode positioning and customizable MQTT integration allow organizations to incorporate the PT50 into Plaspy workflows for fleet oversight, asset protection and long duration telemetry without complex custom development.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible integration via Web, mobile app, SMS and customizable MQTT for platform and IoT workflows
- Solar powered autonomy with internal battery for extended unattended deployments
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi for improved outdoor and indoor location reliability
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure for water and dust resistance in harsh outdoor and maritime environments
- Magnet adsorption mounting for fast attachment to metal containers, trailers and vessels
- Built in alerts including SOS, geofence, low battery and movement detection for security monitoring
- Compact and lightweight form factor suitable for discreet installation on a variety of assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the PT50 delivers location updates and event notifications into the platform so teams can track assets, respond to incidents and review historical routes. Plaspy ingests device messages, normalizes telemetry and exposes the information through dashboards, reports and integrations that support operational decision making.

- Real time location updates and event streaming into Plaspy dashboards for immediate visibility
- Geofence and SOS alerts forwarded to Plaspy for perimeter monitoring and priority notifications
- Movement and low battery notifications surfaced in Plaspy to support anti theft response and maintenance planning
- Historical route playback and reporting to analyze asset movement and utilization over time
- MQTT support to route device telemetry into Plaspy or other centralized systems for custom workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term container and trailer tracking in yards and maritime environments where mains power is not available
- Remote or auxiliary asset management for fleets that require intermittent monitoring and long battery life
- Boat and marine asset tracking that benefits from waterproofing and wide operating tolerances
- Anti theft and SOS monitoring for distributed equipment and high value assets
- Distributed telemetry for logistics and centralized operations using MQTT and platform integrations

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT50 is a practical choice for organizations that need durable, low maintenance trackers that can be integrated into a central management platform. Its solar charging and sealed enclosure reduce the need for frequent service visits, while magnet mounting simplifies deployment on metal assets. Paired with Plaspy, the PT50 becomes part of a broader tracking and operational toolset that supports alerts, reporting and system level automation.

Because the PT50 supports customizable MQTT messaging and standard cellular reporting, it is straightforward to map device events into Plaspy workflows and combine location telemetry with other operational data sources.
