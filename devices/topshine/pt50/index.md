# TopShine - PT50

![PT50](./tracker.jpg)

The 4G PT50 solar GPS tracker is Plaspy compatible and built for long-term, unattended monitoring of containers, trailers, boats, vehicles, and remote assets. With multi-mode positioning \(GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi\) and 4G/GSM communications, the PT50 delivers reliable real-time tracking and event alerts through Web, mobile app and SMS interfaces — and integrates with Plaspy via customizable MQTT for seamless telemetry and system-level workflows.

The rugged IP67-rated enclosure, magnet-adsorption mounting and integrated solar charging extend deployments without mains power or frequent servicing. When combined with Plaspy, the PT50 becomes a dependable building block for fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and long-duration asset telemetry that requires minimal maintenance and robust data delivery.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates location and event data via Web, APP, SMS and a customizable MQTT protocol for platform and IoT workflows.
- Solar-powered autonomy: built-in solar charging plus a 2500mAh battery reduces maintenance and supports long unattended deployments.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS + AGPS + LBS + WiFi for outdoor accuracy around 5 m and improved indoor positioning \(approx. 30 m via WiFi\).
- Rugged design: IP67 waterproof rating and a wide operating temperature range for harsh outdoor and maritime environments.
- Simple installation: magnet adsorption mounting enables quick attachment to metal containers, trailers and vessels without drilling.
- Security and alerts: SOS alarm, geofence \(GPS and WiFi fence\), low-battery alarm and movement/anti-theft detection for real-time protection.
- Lightweight, compact form factor: small dimensions and modest weight make it suitable for many asset types and discreet installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the PT50 streams location and event data in real time and enables historical route playback, alerts and system integrations. Plaspy ingests data from the device over cellular \(GPRS/SMS/4G\) or MQTT, normalizes telemetry, and exposes it through dashboards, reports and API endpoints for fleet management or asset-monitoring workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via cellular \(GPRS/4G\) and MQTT for low-latency display in Plaspy dashboards.
- Geofence events \(GPS and WiFi fence\) delivered as immediate alerts to Plaspy for perimeter breach tracking.
- SOS and movement/anti-theft detection forwarded to Plaspy for priority notifications and response workflows.
- Low-battery alarms and device health indicators visible in Plaspy for proactive maintenance scheduling.
- Customizable MQTT protocol support to integrate device messages into Plaspy, third-party systems or centralized IoT platforms.

## Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking — long-term solar autonomy makes the PT50 ideal for maritime shipping and yard storage where mains power is unavailable.
- Fleet management for remote or auxiliary assets — track location history, movement events and uptime for irregularly powered equipment.
- Boat and marine asset monitoring — IP67 waterproofing and wide temperature tolerance support coastal and inland watercraft deployments.
- Remote asset anti-theft and SOS monitoring — geofence, movement detection and SOS alarms provide security alerts via Plaspy.
- Distributed telemetry for logistics — MQTT and Web/App/SMS support enable integration into centralized logistics platforms and custom workflows.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The 4G PT50 is designed to minimize maintenance while maximizing uptime — a strong combination for Plaspy users who need scalable, reliable real-time tracking and telemetry. Solar charging plus a 2500mAh battery reduces visits for battery replacement; magnet mounting simplifies installation on metal assets; and an IP67 rating with wide operating temperature and humidity ranges ensures durability in tough environments. Plaspy leverages the PT50’s real-time updates, geofence and alarm events to provide centralized fleet management, anti-theft alerts and historical route playback.

Architecturally, the PT50’s support for a customizable MQTT protocol makes it straightforward to incorporate device data into Plaspy workflows or broader IoT stacks. While the device focuses on position, power autonomy and event reporting, Plaspy can combine PT50 location telemetry with additional systems — for example, fuel monitoring, ignition/immobilizer controllers or Bluetooth sensor data — at the platform level to deliver fuller fleet insights. Choose the PT50 with Plaspy when you need low-maintenance, durable GPS tracking for long-term asset protection and operational visibility.
