# TopShine - PT50 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TopShine PT50 and explains the practical capabilities you can expect when using this tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing features, integration points, and operational value rather than device programming or private configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical and regulatory details confirm the most current information with the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The TopShine PT50 is a solar assisted 4G GPS tracker designed for long term unattended monitoring of containers, trailers, boats, vehicles, and remote assets. When paired with Plaspy the PT50 streams location and event data to enable continuous visibility, alerts, and historical playback for fleet and asset workflows.

- Multi mode positioning that combines GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi for improved location coverage and practical accuracy.
- Solar charging with an internal battery to support long unattended deployments and reduce maintenance visits.
- 4G and GSM communications with support for MQTT for integration into Plaspy and IoT workflows.
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and magnet adsorption mounting for simple, durable attachment to metal assets.
- Built in alarms and event detection including SOS, geofence, low battery and movement or anti theft notifications.
- Web, mobile app and SMS reporting channels supported by the device and integrated into Plaspy dashboards.

## Core Features of TopShine - PT50

- Multi mode positioning with GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi to improve outdoor and challenging environment coverage.
- 4G cellular communications with GSM fallback for persistent telemetry and event reporting.
- Customizable MQTT support to forward device messages into Plaspy or broader IoT systems.
- Solar charging plus internal battery to enable long term, low maintenance deployments.
- IP67 rated enclosure for waterproofing and protection in harsh outdoor or maritime settings.
- Magnet adsorption mounting for quick installation on metal containers, trailers and vessels.
- Built in alerts including SOS alarm, geofence support for GPS and WiFi fences, low battery alarm, and movement detection.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event messages from the PT50, normalizes telemetry, and exposes data through dashboards, alerts and historical route playback. This integration helps teams monitor asset health and respond to priority events from a single platform.

- Real time location updates and historical track playback visible within Plaspy maps and reports.
- Geofence events for GPS and WiFi fences delivered as alerts to Plaspy for perimeter breach monitoring.
- SOS and movement or anti theft alarms forwarded to Plaspy for prioritized notifications and response workflows.
- Low battery and device health indicators surfaced in Plaspy for proactive maintenance planning.
- MQTT message support enables flexible routing of PT50 telemetry into Plaspy and downstream systems.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term container and trailer tracking where solar autonomy reduces the need for mains power.
- Fleet management of auxiliary or irregularly powered equipment requiring periodic location and event reporting.
- Boat and marine asset monitoring where waterproofing and wide temperature tolerance are important.
- Remote asset anti theft protection using geofences and movement detection forwarded to Plaspy alerts.
- Distributed logistics telemetry where MQTT and web app reporting integrate with centralized workflows.
- Unattended equipment monitoring at worksites, storage yards, and ports.
