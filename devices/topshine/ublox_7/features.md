# TopShine - Ublox 7 Features

This page provides a public, features-oriented overview of the TopShine Ublox 7 (MT06) tracker and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for real time tracking, monitoring and basic remote control workflows. It summarizes the practical functions you can expect when using this compact, industrial grade tracker with Plaspy while keeping the technical detail focused on user facing value.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional accessories and installation method. Optional items shown in manufacturer materials such as relays, auxiliary batteries, or microphone inputs may not be present on every unit and may require configuration or additional hardware to work with Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The Ublox 7 Mini is a small waterproof GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, small vehicles and light equipment that is Plaspy compatible out of the box. It combines a high sensitivity GNSS receiver with a certified GSM/GPRS modem and supports standard transports and an open protocol so location, alarms and basic telemetry can be delivered to Plaspy for mapping, alerts and reporting.

- Real time GNSS based position reporting to Plaspy via GPRS (TCP/UDP) or SMS for simple setups.
- Engine ON/OFF detection and status reporting for ignition based events and trip visibility.
- Configurable alarms including overspeed and auto tracking by time or distance for automated reporting.
- Compact waterproof housing and rugged design suitable for motorcycles and small vehicles.
- Optional relay interface for remote engine cut off to support anti theft workflows.
- Open protocol and certified GSM modem for straightforward integration with Plaspy and other platforms.

## Core Features of TopShine - Ublox 7

- High sensitivity uBlox 7 GNSS receiver for fast satellite acquisition and reliable fixes.
- Certified M35 GSM/GPRS module providing SMS and GPRS connectivity on standard cellular bands.
- Support for SMS uplink and GPRS over TCP or UDP plus an open protocol for third party integration.
- Engine ON/OFF detection and digital input for basic vehicle status monitoring.
- Optional relay output to enable remote engine cut off functionality where configured.
- Configurable auto tracking by time or distance and configurable overspeed alarms.
- Rugged waterproof housing and compact form factor for discreet installations.
- Status LEDs for GPS and GSM indication and a small set of I O options for accessory connections.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position and telemetry reports sent by the Ublox 7 and surfaces that data as live locations, history, alerts and reports. Because the tracker uses an open protocol and common network transports, Plaspy can receive and display device states and configured alarms to support monitoring and operational decisions.

- Real time location updates and trip history appear on Plaspy maps for operational visibility.
- Engine ON/OFF reports are usable in Plaspy as ignition events for start stop logs and workflows.
- Overspeed alarms and auto tracking messages are parsed into Plaspy alerts and event history.
- SMS can act as a fallback transport for basic reporting where GPRS is not available.
- Optional relay control and accessory inputs can be included in Plaspy workflows where the device and installation support remote control features.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small vehicles and motorcycles requiring continuous position and trip history.
- Anti theft protection using engine status detection and optional remote engine cut off.
- Motorcycle and scooter tracking where small size and waterproofing are important.
- Light equipment and portable asset monitoring when rugged mounting and compact form factor matter.
- Simple installations that use SMS reporting as a low complexity tracking solution.
- Operational monitoring where overspeed alerts and auto tracking reduce manual checks.
