# 4G MT01 GPS Vehicle Tracker

The 4G MT01 GPS Vehicle Tracker is a compact, weatherproof tracker designed for fleets and individual vehicles that need reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking. Built for heavy-duty use across motorcycles, cars, trucks and e-bikes, the MT01 combines wide-voltage support, multi-band cellular connectivity and a small mini form factor \(80 × 58 × 22 mm\) to simplify installation and concealment in demanding environments.

Certified to CE, FCC and RoHS standards and backed by a two-year warranty, the MT01 is engineered for long-term telemetry and fleet management tasks. It includes a 650 mAh backup battery, integrated antenna, offline data logging and a set of smart inputs and optional sensors for fuel monitoring, driver safety alerts and anti-theft immobilizer control — all of which can be integrated into the Plaspy platform for unified monitoring, alerts and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and telemetry across vehicles and assets.
- Wide input voltage \(9–90V DC\) supports a range of vehicle electrical systems, including heavy-duty 90V fleets.
- Multi-band 2G/3G/4G cellular connectivity with integrated antenna for robust international coverage.
- Built-in 4MB data logger preserves location and event data in GSM/GPRS blind spots.
- Advanced safety and anti-theft features: SOS panic button, remote engine cut-off \(immobilizer\), external power cut-off alarm.
- Optional sensors and integrations: fuel sensor compatibility, alcohol and crash sensors, RFID/iButton identification, driver fatigue camera option.
- Compact, weatherproof housing with operating range -20°C to 65°C for harsh environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed and connected, the MT01 delivers location, vehicle telemetry and event data to the Plaspy platform using SMS/GPRS or cellular data. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s reports and presents live vehicle positions, geofence alerts, driver events and historical traces in web and mobile apps. Remote commands and configuration changes can be issued through Plaspy to adjust reporting intervals, alarms and power-saving modes.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for mapping and route playback.
- Driver and vehicle events: SOS panic, over-speed, geo-fence breach, harsh acceleration/braking, external power cut-off.
- Fuel monitoring when a compatible fuel sensor is connected, enabling fuel monitoring and consumption reports in Plaspy.
- Remote immobilizer / engine cut-off control and confirmations via two-way communication.
- Support for optional driver-safety integrations \(alcohol sensor, crash sensor, fatigue camera\) with event reporting into the platform.
- Offline data handling: 4MB flash logger stores GPS and event data during temporary connectivity loss for later upload to Plaspy.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management — centralized tracking, route playback, driver behavior monitoring and telemetry reporting for mixed fleets.
- Anti-theft and immobilization — remote engine cut-off, external power cut-off alarm and SOS panic support for rapid response.
- Fuel monitoring and fuel theft prevention — connect a compatible fuel sensor to gather consumption metrics and alerts.
- Driver safety and compliance — alcohol sensor, crash sensor and optional fatigue camera integration for drowsiness and behavior alarms.
- Cold-chain or temperature-sensitive logistics — pair with an optional temperature sensor to track environmental conditions alongside GPS position.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT01 offers a practical balance of rugged hardware and fleet-grade features that make it a strong option for businesses deploying Plaspy compatible GPS tracking across diverse vehicle types. Its wide voltage tolerance, compact weatherproof design and multi-band cellular support reduce installation friction and improve uptime for telemetry and real-time tracking. Built-in data logging preserves traces through temporary coverage gaps, while smart inputs and optional sensors enable fleet managers to add fuel monitoring, driver-safety alarms and anti-theft immobilizer control without complex retrofits.

Paired with Plaspy, the MT01 delivers reliable real-time tracking, event-driven alerts and historical reporting to support efficient fleet management, anti-theft workflows and operational telemetry. Plaspy also supports Bluetooth sensors and other peripheral integrations on the platform level; if Bluetooth sensor connectivity is required for a particular installation, consult your Plaspy integrator or the MT01 supplier for accessory compatibility and configuration options.
