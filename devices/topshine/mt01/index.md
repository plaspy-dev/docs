# TopShine - MT01

<div className="device-logo">
![MT01](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TopShine MT01 is a compact 4G GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable tracking across motorcycles, cars, trucks and e-bikes. It combines a small mini form factor (80 × 58 × 22 mm) with a weatherproof housing, integrated antenna and wide voltage support to simplify deployment in demanding environments while remaining discreet. The unit includes built in data logging, a backup battery and a set of smart inputs plus optional sensor support for tasks such as fuel monitoring and driver safety alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT01 can deliver real time location, telemetry and event data into the Plaspy platform for unified monitoring and reporting. Plaspy ingests the tracker reports and makes live positions, geofence alerts, driver events and historical traces available to fleet managers and operators, enabling centralized oversight and actionable alerts from MT01 equipped vehicles.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking and fleet telemetry.
- Wide input voltage support for diverse vehicle electrical systems.
- Multi band 2G 3G 4G cellular connectivity with integrated antenna for broad coverage.
- Built in 4 MB data logger preserves location and event data during temporary connectivity loss.
- Advanced safety and anti theft features including SOS panic and remote immobilizer control.
- Optional sensor integrations for fuel monitoring, alcohol and crash detection, and driver ID.
- Compact weatherproof housing with broad operating temperature range for harsh conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the MT01 reports location and event data over cellular channels to Plaspy, where the information is consolidated into maps, alerts and reports. Plaspy presents this data in web and mobile interfaces and supports remote commands and configuration changes to adjust tracking behavior and alarm thresholds.

- Real time location and telemetry updates available in Plaspy for mapping and route playback.
- Event reporting for driver and vehicle incidents such as SOS panic, geofence breaches and external power cut alerts.
- Fuel monitoring support when a compatible fuel sensor is attached, enabling fuel reports and consumption visibility in Plaspy.
- Remote immobilizer control and confirmation messages to support anti theft workflows.
- Optional driver safety inputs like alcohol and crash sensors can generate platform alerts and logs.
- Offline data upload from the MT01 logger preserves historical traces for later import into Plaspy after connectivity is restored.

## Typical Use Cases

- Mixed fleet tracking for cars, trucks, motorcycles and light electric vehicles with unified monitoring.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using remote engine cut off and external power cut alarms.
- Fuel theft prevention and consumption reporting when a compatible fuel sensor is connected.
- Driver safety and compliance monitoring with optional alcohol, crash and fatigue related sensors.
- Logistics and route verification with historical trace playback for operational analysis.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT01 offers a balanced combination of rugged hardware and fleet oriented features that suit organizations seeking dependable Plaspy compatible tracking. Its wide voltage tolerance and compact weatherproof design reduce installation friction across vehicle types, while integrated data logging helps maintain continuity of records through temporary coverage gaps. Optional sensor and input flexibility allows fleets to expand monitoring capabilities over time without full device replacement.
