# TopShine - MT01W-4G

<div className="device-logo">
![MT01W-4G](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The MT01W-4G is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for fleet management, passenger vehicles and security focused operators. It combines vehicle location tracking, integrated video monitoring via a built in WiFi hotspot, and a suite of event alerts in a discreet waterproof enclosure intended for straightforward deployment across mixed vehicle fleets.

As a device that supports live video streaming, onboard recording and a range of vehicle security events, the MT01W-4G is relevant to organizations using Plaspy because it consolidates location, telemetry and video into a single feed that Plaspy can ingest. That compatibility enables centralized visibility, alerting and operational workflows within the Plaspy platform while reducing the number of separate devices required per vehicle.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with 4G CAT1 connectivity for reliable near real time tracking and low latency updates.
- Built in WiFi hotspot for multi camera support and in vehicle video monitoring to capture incidents and driver behavior.
- Integrated video recorder with SD storage and live streaming for evidence capture and incident review.
- Wide voltage input and an internal backup battery to support continuous operation across mixed fleets.
- Vehicle security features including SOS panic, crash alert, geo fence, over speed and theft or refill alerts to support anti theft workflows.
- Remote engine cut off via relay for immobilization when coordinated from the tracking platform.
- Driver identification support and optional two way voice for driver authentication and in vehicle communication.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the MT01W-4G sends position data, event alarms and video related indicators to the Plaspy platform so operators can view location, receive alerts and review recorded footage in a single interface. Plaspy maps the device activity alongside available video streams and translates device events into notifications and reports for operational use.

- Real time location updates and historical playback available in Plaspy for route review and fleet oversight.
- Video integration links WiFi camera streams and SD recordings with GPS traces to support incident verification and coaching.
- Alarm and event translation into Plaspy notifications for SOS, crash, geo fence and over speed events to speed response.
- Driver identification events logged in the platform to associate trips with personnel and support accountability.
- Remote immobilizer commands sent from Plaspy to trigger relay based engine cut off where business rules require it.
- Fuel related alerts reported to Plaspy when optional sensors are connected, enabling fuel analytics and theft detection workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route oversight for mixed vehicle operations with integrated video for driver performance review.
- Anti theft and security workflows using SOS, crash alerts and remote immobilization coordinated through Plaspy.
- In vehicle video monitoring for incident capture, passenger safety, and claims reduction.
- Passenger vehicle and shuttle monitoring where both connectivity and onboard recording are needed.
- Fuel monitoring and theft detection when used with optional fuel sensing equipment.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT01W-4G combines video capability, wide input power tolerance and an internal backup battery into a single, compact unit that reduces hardware complexity per vehicle. For operators who want synchronized video and location evidence, plus driver identification and remote immobilization, the device aligns with Plaspy workflows to provide centralized monitoring, alerts and operational reporting.
