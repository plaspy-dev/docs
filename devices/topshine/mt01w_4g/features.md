# TopShine - MT01W-4G Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive feature overview of the TopShine MT01W-4G and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet and vehicle monitoring. It focuses on the practical functions you can expect when pairing this model with Plaspy and clarifies the types of data and operational workflows the tracker can support within the platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a feature guide for Plaspy integration and consult the device manufacturer for the most current, device specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The MT01W-4G is a compact, video enabled GPS tracker intended for fleet management and vehicle security where integrated camera support and telemetry are required. It combines cellular connectivity, a built in WiFi hotspot for cameras, onboard recording and a set of configurable safety and security alerts to reduce the need for multiple devices.

- Real time GPS tracking over 4G CAT1 for timely location updates and low latency telemetry.
- Built in WiFi hotspot to connect multiple WiFi cameras for live streaming and multi camera monitoring.
- Integrated SD card video recording and remote access to camera streams for incident review.
- Vehicle security alerts including SOS panic, crash alert, geo fence, over speed and theft or refill notifications.
- Remote immobilizer support via relay for secure engine cut off when paired with platform commands.

## Core Features of TopShine - MT01W-4G

- 4G CAT1 cellular connectivity for continuous position and telemetry reporting.
- Built in WiFi hotspot supporting many WiFi cameras for live video and synchronized monitoring.
- Onboard video recorder with SD card storage for local evidence capture and playback.
- Wide vehicle power input range and an internal backup battery to maintain operation during power loss.
- Vehicle security and safety alerts including SOS, crash detection, geo fence and over speed notifications.
- Remote engine cut off via relay to support immobilization workflows when combined with remote commands.
- Driver identification support using iButton or passive RFID to associate trips with personnel.
- Optional two way voice support with speaker and microphone for in vehicle communication.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the MT01W-4G is integrated into Plaspy, the tracker’s location, alerts and video related events feed into the Plaspy dashboard to give centralized visibility across a mixed fleet. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and ingests the device’s reported positions and alarms so you can act on events and review historical data.

- Live location and telemetry appear on maps and can be played back in historical traces in Plaspy.
- Alerts such as SOS, crash, geo fence and over speed are forwarded into Plaspy as notifications and event records.
- Driver identification events are logged to associate trips and timestamps with specific personnel in the platform.
- Video streams from connected WiFi cameras and SD recordings can be accessed alongside GPS traces for synchronized incident verification.
- Remote immobilizer commands and related workflows are available to administrators where business rules require vehicle disablement.
- Plaspy centralizes the device data so fleets can combine location, video evidence and alerting into operational reports.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operations that need live tracking plus synchronized in vehicle video for safety and coaching.
- Anti theft workflows that combine geo fence, theft alerts and remote immobilization for rapid response.
- Incident reconstruction and claims support using SD card recordings and live camera streams aligned with GPS data.
- Driver accountability programs using RFID or iButton identification tied to trip logs.
- Mixed fleet deployments that require a compact, waterproof unit with wide voltage tolerance and backup power.
- Fuel monitoring and theft detection when the device is paired with supported external sensors and configured to report related events.
