# TopShine - MT210 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TopShine MT210 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for vehicle tracking and telemetry. Content here focuses on user facing functions and practical workflows that are relevant when the MT210 reports location and status into the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and optional accessories supplied by the manufacturer. Where specifics matter for deployment or safety critical functions, consult the manufacturer documentation and current product specifications before relying on a particular capability.

## Feature Overview

The MT210 is a compact vehicle grade tracker designed for continuous connectivity and discreet installation. It combines a GNSS receiver, certified quad band GSM connectivity and vehicle telemetry interfaces to provide position reporting, event alarms and optional immobilizer and SOS accessory support when paired with Plaspy.

- Real time GPS position reporting for live location in Plaspy dashboards
- Dual SIM failover and SMS or GPRS reporting for resilient connectivity
- Vehicle telemetry such as ACC ignition detection, power cut alerts, geofence and overspeed alarms
- Optional relay based immobilizer and SOS panic button support for anti theft and emergency workflows
- Built in backup battery and wide DC input to maintain reporting during power interruptions
- Compact waterproof enclosure for concealed installations in cars and motorcycles

## Core Features of TopShine - MT210

- Plaspy compatible reporting using SMS or GPRS so location and alarms appear in the Plaspy platform
- Dual SIM support with automatic or manual switching to reduce cellular downtime
- Sensitive GNSS receiver for accurate position fixes and reliable tracking
- Vehicle telemetry inputs including ACC ignition detection and external power cut alarm support
- Geofence and overspeed alarm generation for automated event notifications
- Optional relay output support for remote immobilizer control when installed
- SOS panic button accessory support to send emergency alerts and notifications
- Built in rechargeable backup battery and wide DC 9–36V input range for robust vehicle usage

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the MT210 is used with Plaspy, its position fixes and event messages are ingested by the platform so operators can monitor and act on vehicle status in real time. Plaspy translates incoming reports into map positions, event logs, alerts and historical playback to support fleet oversight.

- Live location updates and historical playback shown on Plaspy maps and timelines
- ACC ignition and external power loss events logged and available for alerting and reports
- Geofence and overspeed events forwarded to Plaspy for automated notifications and audit trails
- Dual SIM and SMS/GPRS reporting help maintain continuity of data visible inside Plaspy
- Remote commands such as immobilize/unlock can be supported through Plaspy where the device and installation allow relay control

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, motorcycles and light commercial vehicles with real time tracking and event reporting
- Anti theft monitoring using power cut alerts, immobilizer control and SOS accessory support
- Concealed personal vehicle or motorcycle tracking where compact waterproof installation is required
- Equipment and asset security for trailers or portable machinery that benefit from location and power loss alerts
- Emergency response workflows that leverage an SOS button and immediate Plaspy notifications
- Operational oversight where uptime and resilient cellular reporting are important

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features such as optional relay immobilizer or SOS accessories may require additional parts or specific wiring during installation
- Firmware revisions or hardware variants can enable or change behavior for alarms, reporting intervals and accessory support
- Installation method and vehicle wiring determine availability of ACC ignition detection and power cut alarms
- Regional hardware or carrier differences can affect SMS and GPRS behavior and should be validated for your deployment area
- Always review the manufacturer documentation for accessory compatibility and exact electrical specifications

Pairing the MT210 with Plaspy gives organizations a practical path to consistent location visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy aggregates the tracker’s position fixes and event messages into maps, alerts and reports so fleet managers can monitor movement, respond to alarms and analyze historical activity without maintaining a separate ingestion pipeline.
