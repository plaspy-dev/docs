# TopShine - PT30

![PT30](./logo.jpg)

The TopShine PT30 is a power-saving GPS personal tracker that provides an easy way to track your targets and offers peace of mind by showing you the location of your targets via SMS or GPRS. This compact tracker is perfect for people tracking and security, such as keeping an eye on kids, the elderly, or tourists. It can also be used for assets tracking and security, as well as pets tracking and security.

The PT30 is designed for convenience with its mini size and easy portability. It does not require any installation, making it hassle-free to use. You can track your targets by SMS or GPRS, and it supports both TCP and UDP protocols. Real-time tracking is available via the web-platform, allowing you to monitor the location of your targets in real-time. You can also track on demand or set a time interval for tracking.

The PT30 comes with a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your targets. It has a two-way conversation capability, allowing you to communicate with the tracker remotely. In case of emergencies, there is an SOS panic button that can be activated to send an alert. The built-in backup battery ensures that the tracker keeps working even when the power source is disconnected. It has a sleep mode for extended battery life and a geo-fence alarm to notify you when the tracker goes beyond a predefined area. Additionally, it has a speeding alarm to alert you when the target exceeds a certain speed limit.

The PT30 also offers convenient features such as the ability to get the physical address name via SMS and a built-in memory for position logging. Even without a GPRS signal, the tracker can store up to 10,000+ waypoints. It also provides a Google Maps URL for location via SMS, allowing you to view the location on a map via your mobile phone. With three buttons for making phone calls and sending messages, the PT30 offers a comprehensive solution for tracking and security needs.
