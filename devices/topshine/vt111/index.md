# TopShine - VT111

![VT111](./logo.jpg)

The TopShine VT111 is a mini GPS vehicle tracker that offers a highly cost-effective solution for tracking all kinds of vehicles. It serves as both a GPS tracker and a car alarm, providing features such as tracking, Arm/Disarm by SMS or Call, and ACC on/Door open/Movement alarm to protect your vehicles from theft. With the optional RFID part, the device can also automatically Arm/Disarm, making it a convenient and comfortable choice for vehicle security. Its small size allows for easy concealment and installation, making it an ideal tracker for fleet management and car security.

The VT111 offers a range of features to enhance vehicle tracking and security. It supports auto tracking by SMS/GPRS, allowing you to track your vehicle by time interval or distance. You can check the car's location, speed, and direction via SMS or through a tracking platform. The device also provides the ability to check the physical address via SMS, with support from a center or tracking platform. Additionally, you can cut off the car's power or fuel supply by SMS or through the tracking platform to immobilize the vehicle. The VT111 also offers features such as movement alerts, geo-fence alerts, over-speed alerts, and engine status detection \(optional\). It even has a built-in rechargeable backup battery and can send an alert in case of external power failure. With its flexible design, the VT111 can work independently or with the support of a center, allowing for monitoring and control of multiple vehicles. Overall, the TopShine VT111 is a reliable and easy-to-install GPS tracker that provides comprehensive tracking and security features for your vehicles.

### Key Features:

- Auto tracking by SMS/GPRS
- Check car's location, speed, and direction via SMS or tracking platform
- Check physical address via SMS
- Cut off car power/fuel supply by SMS or tracking platform
- Movement alerts
- Geo-fence alerts
- Over-speed alerts
- Built-in rechargeable backup battery
- External power failure alert
- Two kinds of location information: GPS and CPS \(cellular positioning system\)
- Voice monitoring via telephone
- Anti-robbery switch for high-jack situations \(optional\)
- Flexible design for standalone or center-supported operation
- Anti-tamper design in small size
- Google map link for location via SMS
