# TopShine - VT111

![VT111](./logo.jpg)

The TopShine VT111 is a compact mini GPS vehicle tracker designed to deliver cost effective tracking and vehicle security. It combines standard tracking capabilities with car alarm functions and supports features such as Arm/Disarm by SMS or call, movement and door open alerts, and optional RFID based automatic arming. Its small footprint makes it easy to conceal and suitable for a wide range of vehicle types where discreet monitoring is desirable.

As a device listed as compatible with Plaspy, the VT111 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet and asset monitoring workflows to provide location visibility, alerts, and control options from a central platform. Plaspy can receive location updates and alerts from the VT111 and present them alongside other devices for consolidated monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Miniature form factor for discreet concealment in vehicles
- Combined GPS tracking and car alarm functions for security and monitoring
- Remote Arm and Disarm via SMS or call, with optional RFID automatic arming
- Supports periodic auto tracking by SMS or GPRS for regular position updates
- Alerts for movement, geo fence breaches, over speed, and external power loss
- Ability to immobilize vehicle by cutting off power or fuel supply through platform commands
- Built in rechargeable backup battery to report on external power failure

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT111 can feed its location and alert events into Plaspy so operators and managers can monitor vehicles on a single platform. Plaspy collects position updates and alarm notifications from compatible devices, letting teams respond, generate reports, and configure operational rules.

- Consolidate VT111 location fixes with other devices in Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Receive movement, geo fence, over speed, and external power alerts as notifications in Plaspy
- Use Plaspy reporting to analyze route history, stop behavior, and alert trends for VT111 equipped vehicles
- Issue immobilization or control commands from Plaspy where the VT111 is configured to accept platform control
- Group and monitor multiple VT111 units for fleet level visibility and operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light duty vehicles where a compact tracker is preferred
- Vehicle theft prevention and recovery with alarm and remote immobilization features
- Rental car monitoring to track location, movement, and tamper events
- Delivery and service vehicles requiring discreet installation and consistent tracking updates
- Small business or corporate vehicle pools that need a low cost tracking and alarm solution

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT111 is a practical option for organizations seeking a small, cost effective tracker that pairs basic alarm capabilities with standard tracking. Its combination of location reporting, movement and tamper alerts, and remote immobilization can support both security and operational monitoring tasks within Plaspy. The optional RFID and flexible standalone or center supported operation make it adaptable to different workflows and installation preferences.

When used with Plaspy, the VT111 provides a straightforward path to centralized monitoring without requiring complex device management. For teams that prioritize discreet installation, simple alarm handling, and platform based control and reporting, the VT111 represents a sensible fit that complements Plaspy's fleet visibility and alerting features.
