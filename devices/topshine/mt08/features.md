# TopShine - MT08 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the TopShine MT08 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities you can expect when the MT08 is connected to Plaspy for real time tracking, alerts, and basic telemetry. The intent is to help fleet managers, installers, and riders understand how the MT08 can be used within Plaspy without exposing sensitive configuration or private protocol details.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, chosen accessories, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where the MT08 offers optional functions such as fuel sensing, SOS, or remote listening, those require the matching accessory and correct wiring or configuration to appear in Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The MT08 is a compact waterproof tracker designed for motorcycles and small vehicles that integrates with Plaspy for live location and event reporting. It combines cellular connectivity, Bluetooth support, a broad input voltage range, and a set of configurable alerts to provide discreet tracking and security for two wheel vehicles and small fleets.

- Compact waterproof design suitable for concealed under seat installation on motorcycles and scooters
- 4G with 2G fallback plus SMS and GPRS reporting for consistent data delivery in variable coverage areas
- Built in Bluetooth for smart alarm functions and optional driver identification workflows
- Motion aware sleep and movement alerts to reduce power use and protect against unauthorized movement
- Alerts for over speed, geo fence events, and power loss to support anti theft monitoring
- Optional accessories support including fuel sensors, SOS panic button, and remote listening for expanded telemetry

## Core Features of TopShine - MT08

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward integration into real time dashboards and reports
- 4G and 2G cellular connectivity with SMS/GPRS reporting and call or SMS based location retrieval
- Built in Bluetooth to enable smart alarm functions and optional driver ID workflows
- Compact waterproof enclosure engineered for under seat or concealed motorcycle installations
- Wide operating input voltage range to support a variety of two wheel and small vehicle electrical systems
- Motion sensor modes for sleep and wake behavior to conserve power and detect movement
- Standard and optional alert types including geo fence, movement, over speed, and power failure
- Support for optional accessories such as fuel sensors, SOS panic button, and remote listening microphone

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the MT08 is installed and configured, it sends position, status, and alarm messages into Plaspy where those inputs become visible on maps, in event logs, and in historical reports. Plaspy ingests the device messages and surfaces them as actionable events and telemetry for operators and administrators.

- Real time location updates and status reporting appear in Plaspy maps and asset lists
- Movement, geo fence, over speed, and power loss alerts generate notifications and are recorded in event history
- Bluetooth based events and optional driver ID indicators can be logged in Plaspy when the accessory is present
- Optional fuel sensor and SOS events are shown as telemetry or alarm entries when configured and supported
- SMS and GPRS reporting modes provide fallback delivery that Plaspy accepts for continuity in low coverage areas

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle courier and delivery fleet tracking for route visibility, mileage oversight, and speed alerts
- Concealed anti theft tracking for personal motorcycles with movement and power failure alerting
- Rental scooter and short term rental operations using driver ID and runtime reporting for accountability
- Small vehicle fleet installations that need compact, waterproof hardware and flexible power tolerance
- Fuel sensitive operations using optional fuel sensors to monitor consumption when installed
- Remote alarm verification and emergency alerts using optional SOS or remote listening accessories

## Feature Availability Notes

- Optional features such as fuel sensing, SOS, and remote listening require compatible accessories and proper installation
- Alerts and behavior depend on firmware and hardware revision; different production runs or region variants may expose different settings
- Installation method and wiring determine which inputs and outputs are available and reported to Plaspy
- Some reporting modes and accessory behaviors are configurable by SMS or GPRS commands and may differ across firmware
- Always confirm accessory compatibility and configuration steps in the manufacturer documentation before deployment
