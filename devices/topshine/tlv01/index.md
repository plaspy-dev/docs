# TopShine - TLV01

![TLV01](./tracker.jpg)

The TLV01 Mini Hide GPS Tracker is a compact, covert GPS tracker designed for discreet asset protection and vehicle security. Built for installers and fleet managers who need a small form factor with flexible power options, the TLV01 supports GSM/GPRS and SMS reporting plus hybrid GPS + LBS positioning \(AGPS\), making it a practical Plaspy compatible tracker for real-time tracking and anti-theft response.

The TLV01 is ideal for covert installation in cars, motorcycles, portable electronics and other assets where concealment, continuous telemetry and remote immobilization matter. When paired with Plaspy, the TLV01 delivers continuous position updates, alarms and remote control of engine/oil cut-off functions for effective fleet management and security monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking via GSM/GPRS and SMS — continuous location updates to your platform.
- Compact mini-hide form factor \(45 x 25 x 8 mm installation size\) for covert placement in vehicles and portable assets.
- Wide input voltage \(+5V to 110V\) for flexible installations across cars, motorcycles and non-automotive equipment.
- Backup 250mAh battery and smart power-saving modes maintain telemetry when external power is lost.
- Hybrid GPS & LBS positioning with AGPS support to improve fixes in weak-signal or indoor environments.
- Configurable reporting by time, distance or course plus alerts for geo-fence, tow and overspeed events.
- Remote engine/oil cut-off \(immobilizer\) and optional external power-cut alarm for anti-theft and vehicle control.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the TLV01 with Plaspy enables real-time tracking, alarm delivery and remote control from a single dashboard. The device sends location and event data over GSM/GPRS or SMS; Plaspy ingests that data to present maps, telemetry and alerts. Installers can configure reporting intervals and alarm thresholds on the device, and Plaspy will present actionable notifications and history for fleet management and security teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GSM/GPRS \(internet tracking\) and SMS to Plaspy.
- Geo-fence, tow and overspeed alarms — events are forwarded to Plaspy in real time for instant notification.
- Ignition and immobilizer control — Plaspy can leverage the TLV01's remote engine/oil cut-off to stop a vehicle when authorized.
- External power-cut alarm — power loss events are reported so Plaspy can flag potential tampering.
- AGPS/LBS fallback — improves positioning accuracy indoors and in weak GPS conditions; Plaspy uses the best available fix for mapping and reports.
- Telemetry & integrations — Plaspy consolidates positional and event telemetry from the TLV01; fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensors are supported through Plaspy when used with compatible external interfaces or accessories \(not built-in to the TLV01 specification\).
