# TopShine - MT08B

<div className="device-logo">
![MT08B](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The MT08B Portable Motorcycle GPS Tracker from TopShine is a compact, waterproof device designed for reliable real time tracking and vehicle security. Sized for discreet installation on motorcycles and small vehicles, the MT08B uses a SIRF-Star III GNSS chipset for positioning and reports location and status over global GSM networks. Its small matchbox form factor and integrated anti theft features make it well suited to protection and monitoring tasks where space is limited.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT08B can forward location and telemetry to Plaspy using SMS or GPRS, enabling live map views, alerts and historical reporting within the Plaspy platform. This compatibility makes the MT08B a practical option for fleet managers, motorcycle owners, and security teams who want compact hardware combined with Plaspy’s visibility and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for live tracking and reporting via SMS or GPRS to Plaspy
- High sensitivity GNSS using SIRF-Star III with strong positioning performance and practical accuracy for vehicle tracking
- Compact waterproof design in a matchbox sized enclosure about 62 x 50 x 22 mm and lightweight for discreet mounting
- Built in anti theft and security features including motion detection, geo fence, over speed alerts, power cut notifications, SOS and remote engine immobilizer support
- Configurable inputs and output to monitor engine or door status and support external controls
- Wide operating voltage range with internal backup battery to continue reporting after main power loss
- Vendor certifications and warranty that support long term fleet deployment

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the MT08B transmits position and status messages to Plaspy’s servers where the data is processed for map display, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy organizes incoming messages into live location updates, event alerts and timeline histories so operators can monitor assets, investigate incidents and review past routes.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for mapping and tracking including position, speed and timestamp information
- Motion and security alerts such as geo fence breaches, over speed events and motion detected notifications routed into Plaspy for immediate attention
- Power cut and backup battery events reported to Plaspy to support anti theft workflows and continuity monitoring
- Ignition and I O events conveyed to Plaspy to reflect engine or door status and to enable remote immobilizer actions when required
- SOS signals and two way listen in events that can be logged and managed within Plaspy for emergency response and verification
