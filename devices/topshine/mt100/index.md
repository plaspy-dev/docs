# TopShine - MT100

![MT100](./tracker.jpg)

The TopShine MT100 is a professional vehicle GPS tracker designed for fleet management and driver accountability. Built for logistics, taxi and bus operations, and rental services, the MT100 provides reliable position reporting, driver identification using RFID iButton or fingerprint readers, and event logging that associates driving events with specific drivers. Its design emphasizes vehicle use cases where consolidating location and driver data improves operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT100 can feed real-time location, driver ID events, alarms and telemetry into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. That compatibility makes the MT100 a practical option for fleets that want to combine in-vehicle driver association with Plaspy visibility, alerts and historical analysis without adding custom integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for centralized real-time location and fleet management.
- Integrated driver identification via RFID iButton or fingerprint to tie trips and events to individual drivers.
- 4G primary communication with GSM fallback for broad coverage and timely updates.
- Remote immobilizer relay and SOS input for anti-theft response and emergency signaling.
- Extensive event alarms including power cut door status ignition and geo-fence triggers.
- Fleet telemetry support such as mileage running time and idle alerts for operational analysis.
- Optional accessories available for fuel monitoring driver monitoring and voice communication.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the MT100 transmits position updates driver identification events and configured alarms so fleet managers can monitor activity and produce driver focused reports. Plaspy consolidates those inputs to provide a single view of vehicle location history alerts and operational metrics.

- Real-time location and historical playback available in Plaspy for route review and oversight.
- Driver ID events logged and associated with trips stops and alarm events for accountability reporting.
- Ignition and running time data used in Plaspy to analyze idle time and driver behavior trends.
- Alarms such as power cut door open and geo-fence breaches forwarded as platform alerts.
- Optional fuel and accessory data sent to Plaspy when corresponding sensors are installed.
- Remote immobilizer and SOS events can be surfaced in Plaspy for rapid response and record keeping.

## Typical Use Cases

- Mixed commercial fleets requiring driver identification to attribute trips and incidents.
- Taxi and ride services needing driver linked logs and rapid incident handling.
- Rental and car share operations that benefit from driver verification and remote immobilizer control.
- Logistics and delivery fleets focused on mileage running time and idle reduction.
- Public transport and bus operations that use driver logs to improve scheduling and safety.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT100 pairs practical vehicle grade tracking and multiple driver identification methods with Plaspy to deliver a consolidated solution for fleets that need both location visibility and driver accountability. Its emphasis on event logging and anti-theft features makes it suitable for organizations that want to reduce unauthorized use and improve operational efficiency through platform level reporting.
