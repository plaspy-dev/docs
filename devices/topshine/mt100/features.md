# TopShine - MT100 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TopShine MT100 and how its capabilities are presented when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, non sensitive aspects of the tracker that matter for fleet management, driver accountability and platform integration, and explains how key functions appear inside Plaspy for operational monitoring and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on device firmware, hardware revision, installed accessories, wiring and the manufacturer's implementation. Some functions described here are optional or accessory dependent and may vary by region or by the specific MT100 model variant, so review manufacturer documentation for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The TopShine MT100 is a vehicle grade GPS tracker designed for fleet usage where driver identification, remote immobilization and reliable connectivity are required. Below are the most relevant practical capabilities that operators and fleet managers can expect when pairing MT100 devices with Plaspy.

- Real time location tracking over cellular networks for live monitoring and historical playback in Plaspy.
- Driver identification using RFID, iButton or fingerprint to associate trips and events with individuals.
- Remote immobilizer control via relay plus SOS input for emergency handling and theft response.
- Event alarms for power cut, door status changes, engine on/off and idle conditions to support operational alerts.
- Mileage and running time reporting together with ignition detection to measure usage and reduce idle time.

## Core Features of TopShine - MT100

- 4G LTE primary connectivity with GSM fallback for broad cellular coverage and consistent telemetry.
- Integrated driver identification support including RFID reader, iButton and fingerprint options to link driving events to drivers.
- Relay output for remote engine immobilizer control and an SOS input for emergency alerts.
- GNSS based real time position reporting for live tracking and route history.
- Extensive event logging and alarms such as external power loss, door open, ignition on/off, idle alerts and geo fence triggers.
- Mileage and running time telemetry with ignition ACC detection for utilization and driver behavior analysis.
- Support for optional accessories including fuel sensors, driver monitoring devices and two way voice when deployed.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an MT100 is configured to report to Plaspy, the device sends position, driver ID events and alarm messages to the platform where they are presented for monitoring, alerts and reporting. Plaspy simplifies device onboarding and visibility so fleet teams can act on driver linked events and telemetry.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks appear on the Plaspy map for live oversight and playback.
- Driver ID events (RFID, iButton, fingerprint) are recorded and associated with trips, stops and alarms in Plaspy reports.
- Alarms and event notifications such as power cut, door open or SOS can be forwarded to operators or sent as platform alerts.
- Mileage, running time and ignition on/off records are available in Plaspy for utilization reporting and idle time analysis.
- Remote commands such as immobilizer control and SOS handling can be issued or coordinated through Plaspy workflows when supported by the device configuration.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and accepts device connections via UDP or TCP on the platform port used for all devices to simplify setup.

## Typical Use Cases

- Preventing and responding to vehicle theft with remote immobilizer and power cut alerts.
- Enforcing driver accountability in taxi and logistics operations using driver ID linked trip logs.
- Monitoring fleet utilization and reducing idle time with mileage and running time reports.
- Rental and car share operations that need driver identification, SOS support and optional two way voice.
- Long haul and delivery fleets adding optional fuel monitoring and driver cameras for operational insight.
- Public transport and bus operations that benefit from driver specific logs, idle alerts and mileage tracking.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features are optional and require accessory modules or specific MT100 variants such as fuel sensors or driver cameras.
- Firmware version and hardware revision can change how certain events are reported or which settings are available.
- Installation wiring and vehicle interfaces affect detection of ignition, door status and impulse sensors.
- Regional cellular band support and network behavior can influence connectivity and telemetry frequency.
- Always consult the manufacturer documentation for up to date accessory compatibility and firmware release notes.

Using the TopShine MT100 with Plaspy provides a consolidated view of vehicle location, driver identification and event telemetry in a single platform. Plaspy brings those device signals into dashboards, alerts and reports so fleet managers can improve operational oversight, reduce idle time, and tie driving events to individual drivers for training and accountability.
