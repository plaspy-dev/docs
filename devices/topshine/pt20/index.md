# TopShine - PT20

<div className="device-logo">
![PT20](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TopShine PT20 is a compact magnetic 4G portable GPS tracker designed for reliable location reporting of vehicles and mobile assets. It combines hybrid GPS and LBS positioning with rechargeable battery power and a small magnetic housing for quick, discreet mounting. The PT20 is described as capable of configurable tracking intervals, overspeed alerts, historical track queries, and buffered data resend after temporary cellular outages.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT20 can send position and alarm packets to third party tracking platforms such as Plaspy for live map display, alerting, and reporting. Its buffered data behavior and configurable telemetry make it suitable for Plaspy users who need continuous visibility, historical playback, and basic alarm workflows without permanent wiring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time position and alarm reporting to external tracking platforms.
- Compact magnetic housing suited for discreet temporary or semi permanent mounting.
- Rechargeable 2500mAh battery designed for extended standby operation under low power settings.
- 4G 3G 2G cellular connectivity with local buffering to preserve track history through signal gaps.
- Configurable tracking intervals and overspeed alerts to support monitoring and notifications.
- Historical track queries for retrospective analysis and audit trails.
- Wide operating temperature range for use in varied field conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the PT20 uploads its GPS and cellular assisted location packets over a standard SIM card data connection. Plaspy receives those packets and surfaces position, movement history and alarm events in dashboards, maps and reports. The device buffer ensures data continuity by forwarding stored points after temporary cellular interruptions so Plaspy maintains an accurate movement history.

- Real time location updates sent over cellular networks to Plaspy for map visualization.
- Overspeed and alarm reports delivered to Plaspy so operators can be notified and respond.
- Historical track queries accessible in Plaspy for playback and analysis of past movements.
- Buffered data resend preserves continuity in Plaspy when the tracker passes through areas of poor coverage.
- Requires a SIM card and data plan for active tracking and telemetry uploads to Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for rental vehicles and temporary fleet expansion where rapid redeployment is beneficial.
- Anti theft monitoring by discreetly mounting the unit on vehicles, trailers, or containers.
- Asset tracking for equipment such as generators or construction tools that move between sites.
- Short term field deployments and investigative tracking where easy removal and relocation are needed.
- Maintaining data continuity in low coverage areas to support later analysis and reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT20 is a practical choice for organizations that need a portable, discreet tracker that streams location and basic alarms to a third party platform. Its magnetic form factor and rechargeable battery reduce installation time and increase flexibility compared with hard wired options, while its buffering and configurable reporting help preserve useful telemetry in real world conditions.
