# TopShine - PT20 Features

This page provides a public feature summary of the TopShine PT20 portable GPS tracker and how its capabilities map to the Plaspy platform. It covers the practical, non sensitive feature context users will encounter when deploying the PT20 with Plaspy for real time location, alerts, and historical track visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, regional variants, and manufacturer implementation. Where necessary this page notes limitations called out by the manufacturer and encourages verification against the latest supplier documentation.

## Feature Overview

The PT20 is a compact magnetic tracker designed for discreet mounting, battery powered operation, and continuous location reporting to third party tracking platforms such as Plaspy. It combines cellular connectivity and hybrid positioning to provide live updates and preserved movement history when coverage is intermittent.

- Portable magnetic housing for quick covert mounting on vehicles and assets
- 4G/3G/2G cellular connectivity with hybrid GPS and LBS positioning for broad coverage
- Rechargeable 2500mAh battery with low power modes for extended standby operation
- Configurable tracking intervals and overspeed alert reporting for operational monitoring
- Buffered data storage with resend after cellular blind spots to preserve historical tracks

## Core Features of TopShine - PT20

- Plaspy compatible real time position upload to external tracking platforms
- Hybrid GNSS and cellular base station assisted positioning for location fixes
- Configurable telemetry reporting intervals to balance detail and battery life
- Overspeed alerts and alarm reporting for event driven notifications
- Local buffer that stores points while out of cellular range and forwards them when connectivity returns
- Historical track queries available through platform reporting for retrospective analysis
- Wide input voltage support for vehicle power integration when continuous power is required
- Rugged operating range suitable for varied environmental conditions

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the PT20 reports location and alarm data to the platform where it becomes visible on maps, dashboards, and reports. Plaspy ingests device packets and surfaces real time and historical information for operational oversight and incident response.

- Real time position updates displayed on Plaspy maps and live tracking views
- Overspeed and alarm notifications surfaced as alerts for operator attention
- Historical track playback and export using recorded points preserved by the device buffer
- Buffered data resend ensures movement history remains accurate in Plaspy after short GSM outages
- Configurable reporting intervals reflected in Plaspy as the frequency of updates and track density

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for portable or temporary vehicles and auxiliary equipment
- Anti theft monitoring of cars, trailers, containers and other high value assets
- Asset tracking for equipment that needs rapid redeployment or covert mounting
- Short term investigative or field deployments requiring discreet placement and easy removal
- Maintaining continuity of movement history in areas with intermittent cellular coverage
