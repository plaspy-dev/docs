# TopShine - OTK01 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TopShine OTK01 GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device features that are relevant to fleet managers, rental operators, and individuals when the OTK01 is integrated into Plaspy for real time tracking, telemetry ingestion, and historical route playback.

Exact feature availability can vary with device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where behavior or limits depend on firmware or regional variants, Plaspy users should confirm current details from the manufacturer and verify device behavior on their own units before relying on any particular function for critical operations.

## Feature Overview

The OTK01 is a compact plug and play OBDII tracker focused on fast AGPS positioning, continuous vehicle telemetry, and straightforward integration with tracking platforms such as Plaspy. It is designed to minimize installation work while providing useful reporting for location, motion, and basic vehicle diagnostics.

- Tool free OBDII plug and play installation for quick deployment and discreet placement in vehicles
- AGPS accelerated positioning for faster first fixes and improved responsiveness in urban or obstructed environments
- Continuous reporting of latitude and longitude plus speed, direction, and odometer readings for route and usage visibility
- Engine ON/OFF (ACC) detection and power cut alarm to support tamper and theft alerting workflows
- Onboard data logging with thousands of waypoints for historical playback when connectivity is intermittent
- GPRS reporting with SMS command support for primary reporting and fallback communication modes

## Core Features of TopShine - OTK01

- Plug and play OBDII connection for fast vehicle onboarding without additional wiring
- AGPS assisted GPS for quicker location fixes in challenging reception areas
- GPRS upload of position and telemetry with SMS command and fallback reporting options
- Vehicle telemetry reporting including latitude and longitude, speed, direction, odometer, and basic OBD diagnostic data
- Engine ignition ACC status reporting and power failure or cut off alarm for tamper detection
- Onboard data logger storing up to 5,520 waypoints for delayed upload and route reconstruction
- Small compact form factor with an internal backup battery for short duration power loss operation
- OEM and ODM friendly design for branding and flexible fleet deployment options

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When added to Plaspy, OTK01 devices feed location and vehicle telemetry into the platform so operators can monitor live activity and review historical movement. Plaspy presents incoming reports, event alerts, and logged waypoints in dashboards and playback tools to support operational decisions and incident response.

- Real time location and telemetry updates are visible in Plaspy when the OTK01 uploads position via GPRS
- SMS command support and fallback reporting can be used where network coverage or GPRS availability is limited
- Engine ON/OFF (ACC) status maps to state based alerts and rules inside Plaspy for ignition dependent workflows
- Power cut and tamper alarms trigger immediate event notifications to help with theft detection and rapid response
- Stored waypoints are uploaded when connectivity resumes and appear in Plaspy for complete route playback and audit trails
- Reports can include address resolution and map links to streamline operator navigation and response

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch for real time vehicle location, route replay, and operational oversight
- Rental and shared vehicle tracking to simplify onboarding, billing, and recovery of missing vehicles
- Logistics and delivery operations that benefit from quick installation and ongoing vehicle telemetry
- Anti theft and tamper detection using ACC and power cut alerts for immediate notifications
- Historical route reconstruction and compliance reporting using the onboard data logger
- Basic vehicle diagnostics monitoring by reading OBD derived telemetry where supported

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior can vary by firmware version and hardware revision; shipping units may differ from published samples
- Some telemetry and diagnostic fields depend on vehicle OBD support and how the device reads OBD data on a given vehicle
- Regional cellular capability and SMS behavior depend on local network providers and the specific device variant
- OEM or reseller models may include custom branding or configuration changes that affect feature sets
- Always verify the exact feature set, limits, and command syntax with the manufacturer documentation for your unit
