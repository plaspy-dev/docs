# Meitrack - MD500S Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Meitrack MD500S with Plaspy. It explains the main capabilities exposed to a fleet management platform and how those capabilities are commonly used in centralized tracking, telematics, and video evidence workflows. The content is focused on practical, non sensitive descriptions and on how Plaspy can consume and present the device data.

Exact feature availability depends on the unit's firmware, regional hardware revision, installation choices, and manufacturer implementation. Where the MD500S supports video telematics, AI-driven driver assistance, extensive I O and network fallbacks, those functions may be optional or behave differently across firmware versions and regional variants.

## Feature Overview

The MD500S combines multi network cellular connectivity, integrated GNSS, and four channel H.264 video capture to deliver location tracking and synchronized video for vehicles. It is designed to provide a single device solution for live tracking, onboard video, driver assistance alerts, and peripheral telemetry that can be ingested by Plaspy for operational visibility.

- Real time GPS location reporting combined with GNSS support for reliable positional tracking.
- Four channel video capture and H.264 streaming for synchronized incident review and driver monitoring.
- AI based driver assistance features such as ADAS, DMS and BSD to flag driving events alongside location data.
- Multi network connectivity and dual band Wi Fi for continuous reporting and on site data offload.
- Extensive I O and vehicle interfaces including CAN and RS232 to surface ignition, sensor and peripheral telemetry.
- Remote management tools including FOTA OTA for scalable deployment and firmware updates.

## Core Features of Meitrack - MD500S

- Integrated GNSS support including GPS, BeiDou and GLONASS for accurate positioning.
- Multi network cellular connectivity with 4G LTE plus 3G and 2G fallback for broad coverage.
- Four channel H.264 video capture with dedicated ADAS and DMS channels and two additional 720p channels.
- AI video telematics offering ADAS DMS and BSD event detection to augment location data.
- Dual band Wi Fi for local connectivity, configuration, and data offload.
- Extensive I O set including digital inputs and outputs, analog inputs, CAN bus and RS232 for vehicle telemetry.
- microSD expandable storage up to 256 GB for onboard recording and event clips.
- Remote configuration and firmware management via FOTA OTA and accompanying device management tools.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can ingest location fixes, status events, and telemetry from the MD500S and surface those items in dashboards, maps, and reports. Video clips and event flags generated by the device can be associated with tracked positions in Plaspy to support incident review and operational analysis.

- Real time location and status updates appear on Plaspy maps for live fleet monitoring and route replay.
- AI event flags such as ADAS or driver monitoring alerts can be forwarded as telemetry or alarm records for review.
- Video clips and synchronized audio recorded by the MD500S can be linked to timestamped events in Plaspy for context during investigations.
- I O and CAN derived signals such as ignition or door events are visible as status changes and can trigger rules or notifications.
- Fuel and sensor telemetry transmitted from analog and external sensors can be displayed in Plaspy reports and used in analytics.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols so device reporting is normally straightforward to integrate.

## Typical Use Cases

- Bus and passenger fleet monitoring with synchronized video and telemetry for safety and compliance.
- Delivery and logistics operations using driver assistance alerts and route replay to improve performance.
- Security and anti theft workflows that combine ignition and door event logs with relay controls and location history.
- Cold chain or temperature sensitive transport when combined with digital sensor telemetry and location reporting.
- Incident capture and evidence collection using ADAS DMS triggered clips and synchronized GPS positions.
- Driver coaching programs leveraging AI event flags and video to provide actionable feedback.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature availability varies by firmware release and may differ between regional hardware variants.
- Some video, sensor, or peripheral functions require specific camera models, cabling, or optional accessories during installation.
- Network dependent functions such as live streaming and remote offload rely on cellular or Wi Fi bandwidth and coverage.
- Manufacturer supplied tools and FOTA OTA capability determine how firmware updates and configurations are applied.
- Confirm region specific radio band support and variant differences with the manufacturer documentation.
