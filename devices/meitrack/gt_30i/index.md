# Meitrack - GT-30i

![GT-30i](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack GT-30i is a compact, user friendly personal GPS tracking device designed to deliver reliable position data to a specified mobile phone or server. It combines satellite positioning with cellular communication to report location at preset intervals or on demand, and includes features aimed at personal safety and continuity of data such as an SOS panic button and internal memory for storing locations when a connection is unavailable.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT-30i can feed position and event data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and historical review. Its support for two way voice communications, configurable reporting, and offline storage make it a practical choice for users who need real time monitoring, alerting, and continuity of tracking data within Plaspy without requiring specialized modifications.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and user friendly design suitable for personal carry or attachment to pets
- Sends location updates to phone or server using its built in GPS and cellular communications
- Two way voice calling and SOS panic button for direct guardian or emergency contact communication
- Configurable reporting intervals and track on demand for flexible monitoring schedules
- Internal memory that stores coordinates when network connectivity is unavailable
- Built in alert features such as movement alarm, geo fence control, low battery and speeding alarms
- Support for SMS communication and GPRS based data transmission as provided by the device

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GT-30i becomes part of a centralized monitoring setup that displays live locations, preserves historical tracks, and triggers operational alerts. Plaspy ingests the location and event messages from the device so teams can monitor status and review past activity from a single interface.

- Live position display and map based visibility for individual devices and groups
- Alert routing for SOS events, geo fence breaches, movement alarms, and low battery notices
- Historical playback and reports using stored points uploaded when the device reconnects
- Geofencing management and event timelines to support operational oversight
- Consolidated activity logs and exportable reports for audits and fleet reviews

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for children, elderly users, or lone workers
- Pet tracking and recovery where compact device size is important
- Short term monitoring for events or temporary assignments
- Caregiver or guardian monitoring with two way call capability and SOS alerts
- Situations requiring continuity of location data in areas with intermittent connectivity
