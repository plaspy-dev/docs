# Meitrack - T399L/T399E Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Meitrack T399L/T399E family and explains how those features are used with Plaspy for fleet, logistics, and commercial vehicle monitoring. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information relevant to operators, integrators, and fleet managers planning deployments with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and the details of an individual installation. For device-specific configuration, firmware behavior, and fully detailed technical specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from Meitrack.

## Feature Overview

The T399L/T399E series is a rugged vehicle tracker designed for real-world fleet and industrial deployments. It combines high-accuracy GNSS positioning, multi-generation cellular connectivity, and broad peripheral support to deliver location, telemetry, and event data into Plaspy for operational visibility.

- High-accuracy GNSS positioning for precise tracking in vehicle fleets
- Multi-generation cellular connectivity options for broad regional coverage
- Rugged IP67-rated enclosure and extended voltage range for harsh installations
- Flexible peripheral support including CAN RS232 RS485 and Bluetooth sensors
- Fuel telemetry and environmental sensor support for operational analytics

## Core Features of Meitrack - T399L/T399E

- High-accuracy GNSS positioning with a focus on reliable fixes for fleet tracking
- Multi-generation cellular support with regional band variants to suit global deployments
- Rugged hardware with IP67 ingress protection and a temperature-tolerant backup battery
- Flexible wired interfaces including CAN, RS232 and RS485 for vehicle telemetry integration
- Bluetooth support for external temperature and humidity sensors and beacons
- Fuel monitoring compatibility with ultrasonic and wired fuel sensors
- Driver behavior analysis and event detection for safety insights and incident review
- OTA FOTA remote firmware update support on the T399L model

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location fixes, telemetry and event messages from the T399L/T399E to provide live tracking, reporting and operational alerts. The platform maps device inputs to dashboards, incident workflows and historical reports so teams can act on vehicle and asset data.

- Live location and movement visibility in Plaspy based on GNSS fixes reported by the device
- Vehicle telemetry inputs such as CAN and serial data surface as parameters for monitoring and reporting
- Safety and driver behavior events are captured as incident records for review and coaching
- Fuel level telemetry and trends are available in Plaspy for consumption and loss analysis
- Environmental sensor data from Bluetooth devices can be displayed for temperature sensitive loads
- Peripheral events and relay inputs can trigger notifications and workflow automation inside Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and logistics with real-time vehicle tracking and route performance monitoring
- Fuel management for ongoing tank level monitoring and consumption trend analysis
- Rental car and car sharing services using driver ID and event logging for secure access and billing
- Driver safety programs that review harsh driving events and fatigue indicators
- Heavy equipment and off road assets where rugged IP67 hardware is required
- Environmental monitoring for temperature sensitive cargo using Bluetooth sensors

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature support can differ between T399L and T399E variants; OTA FOTA is available on T399L as specified by the manufacturer
- Firmware version can enable or change behavior for telemetry parsing, event detection and peripheral handling
- Regional cellular band variants and model selection affect network compatibility in different countries
- Physical installation and wiring affect which interfaces and sensors are available to the device
- For precise electrical specifications, environmental limits and peripheral wiring guidance consult Meitrack documentation

Using the Meitrack T399L or T399E with Plaspy gives organizations a practical path to turn vehicle location and telemetry into operational insight. The combination of rugged hardware, multi generation cellular connectivity and broad peripheral support lets fleet teams collect consistent data from diverse vehicles and integrate that data into Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reports for day to day operations.
