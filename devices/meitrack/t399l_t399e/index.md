# Meitrack - T399L/T399E

<div className="device-logo">
![T399L/T399E](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Meitrack T399L/T399E is a rugged, IP67 rated vehicle tracker built for fleet, logistics and commercial vehicle monitoring. Designed for harsh conditions and global deployments, the T399 series delivers high accuracy GNSS positioning, multi generation cellular connectivity and flexible peripheral support that help fleet operators collect position and telemetry data in real time.

This model is fully compatible with Plaspy, allowing fleet managers to feed location fixes, sensor telemetry and event data into the Plaspy platform. Compatibility makes the T399L/T399E a practical choice when you need integrated tracking, driver behavior insights and fuel or environmental monitoring presented inside a single fleet management system.

## Key Highlights

- Fully Plaspy compatible for real time tracking and fleet telemetry.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure suitable for demanding vehicle and equipment deployments.
- High accuracy GNSS positioning with typical 2.5 meter accuracy.
- Multi generation cellular options and regional variants for broad coverage.
- Driver behavior analysis and event detection for safety and coaching.
- Support for Bluetooth sensors and a range of peripheral inputs for fuel and environmental monitoring.
- OTA firmware updates available on the T399L for remote maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the T399L/T399E streams GNSS fixes, event reports and peripheral telemetry into the platform so operators gain live visibility and historical insights. Plaspy ingests position updates and sensor inputs to power dashboards, alerts and reports that support operational decisions and fleet oversight.

- Live location tracking and map visualization of vehicles and assets.
- Event and incident reporting for harsh driving, fatigue alerts and collisions visible in Plaspy.
- Fuel level and consumption trends from ultrasonic or wired fuel sensors shown in reports.
- Environmental telemetry from Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors for sensitive cargo.
- Peripheral inputs and relay state changes captured to support access control, immobilizer workflows and automation rules.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long haul trucking and logistics operations requiring reliable global tracking and telemetry.
- Delivery and courier fleets that need route visibility, driver scoring and incident alerts.
- Rental and car sharing services using driver ID and access logging for secure usage billing.
- Heavy equipment and off road assets needing rugged hardware and remote monitoring.
- Fuel sensitive operations that benefit from continuous tank level monitoring and analytics.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The T399L/T399E combines rugged hardware and flexible I O to meet the operational needs of commercial fleets while integrating smoothly with Plaspy. Its multi generation cellular options and regional band variants make it suitable for broad deployments, and built in environmental and fuel sensor support helps extend Plaspy insights beyond simple location tracking.

Choosing the T399 series with Plaspy gives fleet teams a consolidated view of position, driver behavior and peripheral telemetry, plus options for remote maintenance via OTA on the T399L. This pairing helps reduce on site upkeep and centralizes telemetry so fleet managers can act on data faster and at scale.
