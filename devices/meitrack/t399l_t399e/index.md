# Meitrack - T399L/T399E

![T399L/T399E](./tracker.png)

The T399L/T399E is a rugged, IP67-rated vehicle tracker designed for fleet, logistics and commercial vehicle monitoring and is fully Plaspy compatible. Built for harsh conditions and global deployments, the T399 series combines high-accuracy GNSS positioning, multi-generation cellular connectivity and flexible peripheral support so fleet managers can deploy real-time tracking, telemetry and anti-theft measures with minimal integration effort.

Whether you run long-haul trucks, delivery vans, rental fleets or heavy equipment, the T399L/T399E delivers precise location, driver behavior analysis and fuel telemetry that feed directly into Plaspy for actionable fleet management insights. The series supports Bluetooth sensors, CAN/RS232/RS485 vehicle integrations and remote firmware updates \(OTA/FOTA on T399L\) to keep devices secure and up to date across large deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and telemetry for fleets, logistics and rental services.
- High-accuracy GNSS with typical 2.5 m positioning accuracy and built-in GNSS antenna.
- Multi-generation cellular support \(4G LTE Cat 1/4/M1/NB2, plus 3G/2G variants\) for broad coverage.
- Advanced driving behavior analysis—detects fatigue, idling, harsh acceleration/braking/cornering and collisions.
- Bluetooth-enabled for temperature & humidity sensors and beacons, enabling environmental monitoring.
- Flexible I/O and peripheral support \(RFID, iButton, relays, ultrasonic/wired fuel sensors\) for anti-theft and fuel monitoring.
- Rugged design: IP67 water resistance, wide voltage range and temperature-tolerant backup battery for demanding installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The T399L/T399E streams location, vehicle telemetry and event data into Plaspy using standard telematics protocols. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes and sensor telemetry to provide dashboard views, live tracking, geofencing, alerts and historical reports. Integration is straightforward: Plaspy reads periodic position updates, CAN bus parameters and peripheral inputs to power fleet management workflows, safety alerts and fuel analytics.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS fixes, speed, heading\) delivered to Plaspy for live tracking.
- Driver behavior and safety events \(fatigue alerts, harsh braking, collisions\) appear as Plaspy incident events for review.
- Fuel monitoring via ultrasonic and wired fuel sensors reports tank levels and consumption trends to Plaspy.
- Bluetooth sensors \(temperature, humidity\) and beacons supply environmental telemetry for asset-sensitive loads.
- Relay outputs and peripheral inputs enable remote control and event-driven automation; relay control can be used to implement cut-off or immobilizer workflows when configured.
