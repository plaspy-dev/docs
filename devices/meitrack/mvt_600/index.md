# Meitrack - MVT-600

![MVT-600](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack MVT-600 is a truly unique GPS vehicle tracker that offers a wide range of functions and features. One of its standout features is the ability to monitor the interior of the vehicle by attaching an optional camera. This camera can take snapshots when certain events occur, such as starting the car or opening and closing doors. This feature provides an extra layer of security and allows for better monitoring of the vehicle's surroundings.

In addition to the camera, the MVT-600 can also be equipped with other accessories such as fuel sensors and an RFID reader. The fuel sensor is particularly useful for vehicles with refrigeration units that need to be monitored. It can send alerts if the temperature suddenly fluctuates, ensuring that perishable goods are kept at the right temperature at all times. The RFID reader, on the other hand, allows for the vehicle to be operated only by selected individuals who carry the RFID card. This adds an extra level of safety and ensures that the vehicle is only operated by authorized personnel.

The Meitrack MVT-600 offers a wide range of functions that make it suitable for various applications. It provides real-time tracking, allowing you to monitor the location of your vehicle at any time. It also offers geo-fencing, which allows you to set up virtual boundaries and receive alerts if the vehicle enters or exits these boundaries. The GPS logging feature allows you to keep track of the vehicle's movements over time, while the speed alert feature notifies you if the vehicle exceeds a certain speed limit. The listen-in feature allows you to listen to the conversations happening inside the vehicle, providing valuable insights into driver behavior and passenger safety. The MVT-600 also includes an S.O.S. button for emergency situations, AGPS for faster and more accurate positioning, and the ability to remotely stop the engine in case of theft or unauthorized use.

The Meitrack MVT-600 is suitable for a wide range of applications, including private vehicles, rental fleets, and company vehicles. Its log reports provide detailed information about the vehicle's usage, allowing for better fleet management and maintenance planning. With its advanced features and reliable performance, the MVT-600 is a top choice for anyone looking for a high-quality GPS vehicle tracker.
