# Meitrack - P99L Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Meitrack P99L and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for real time tracking and telemetry. It focuses on practical, user facing functions and clarifies how the device integrates into Plaspy deployments without presenting detailed configuration or internal implementation specifics.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional radio variant, and installation method. Where details are vendor dependent, we reference the device family characteristics and advise verifying device specific capabilities with Meitrack and the installed firmware before relying on a particular behavior.

## Feature Overview

The P99L is a compact, rugged portable tracker designed for long duration outdoor use and field operations. Built with hybrid positioning and cellular uplink, it targets scenarios where sustained battery life and resilient positioning matter.

- Robust IP67 rated enclosure and shock resistant design for outdoor activity and field work.
- Hybrid positioning using GNSS, Wi Fi and LBS to improve location availability in partial signal conditions.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity for timely location uplink and assisted positioning.
- Long runtime with a 2000 mAh internal battery, intelligent sleep management and extended standby.
- Magnetic and wireless charging options to simplify field recharging without exposed ports.
- Over the air firmware update support and regional radio variants to match market requirements.

## Core Features of Meitrack - P99L

- Rugged portable form factor with IP67 water resistance and shock resistant enclosure.
- Hybrid positioning modes including GNSS, Wi‑Fi and LBS for better coverage in urban and remote areas.
- Cellular uplink via 4G LTE Cat 1 with regional variants to support different markets.
- Internal 2000 mAh battery plus intelligent sleep management delivering extended standby and multi hour run times in normal use.
- Magnetic and wireless charging support for quick recharging in the field.
- Over the air OTA firmware update support for remote device maintenance.
- Built in 2.4 GHz Wi‑Fi for assisted positioning and local connectivity where available.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a P99L is deployed in a Plaspy environment, its location fixes and device telemetry are received by the platform and presented as position updates, history, and device status. Plaspy normalizes the incoming telemetry so teams can monitor assets and personnel from the same dashboards used for other compatible trackers.

- Real time position updates and hybrid fix types shown on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- Battery level and device health telemetry visible for proactive maintenance and alerts.
- Location history and breadcrumb trails available for route review and incident analysis.
- OTA firmware update status and device firmware metadata presented for fleet maintenance workflows.
- Regional variant identification to help manage devices across markets within Plaspy.
- Device events and reporting timelines made available for notifications and operational rules.
