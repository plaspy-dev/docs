# Meitrack - MD600 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Meitrack MD600 mobile DVR and explains how its capabilities are commonly used with Plaspy for fleet video telematics and telemetry. It is written for fleet administrators, integrators, and technical reviewers who want a practical summary of what the MD600 can deliver when paired with Plaspy's tracking and management tools.

Exact feature availability, behavior, and supported peripherals can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional configuration, and installation. For precise, device level details and the latest technical specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes before design or deployment.

## Feature Overview

The MD600 is a vehicle grade mobile DVR built to combine multi-channel video evidence with rich telemetry and peripheral support. Its design targets fleets that need synchronized video, robust connectivity for real-time uploads, and flexible I/O for event-driven workflows.

- Multi-channel AHD video capture with configurable H.264 and H.265 compression for evidence quality and efficient storage.
- Multiple connectivity options including cellular with fallback, dual-band Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, and Ethernet for reliable telemetry and media streaming.
- Broad I/O and peripheral support for digital and analog inputs, relays, RFID, BLE beacons and fuel sensor integration to capture vehicle and driver events.
- Scalable local storage with M.2 NVMe SSD plus dual SD card support for long retention of video and telemetry records.
- Built-in GNSS positioning to feed Plaspy mapping, live tracking, and historical route playback with positioning suitable for fleet use.
- Rugged vehicle mounting design and environmental ratings suitable for commercial vehicle deployments.

## Core Features of Meitrack - MD600

- Six channel AHD video recording supporting mixed resolutions and configurable frame rates with H.264 and H.265 codec options.
- Multi-channel audio inputs and outputs to pair with each camera channel and in-cab intercom or microphone systems.
- Cellular connectivity with fallback modes plus dual-band Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth and Ethernet for flexible networking.
- Extensive I/O and serial interfaces including multiple digital inputs and outputs, analog inputs, RS232/RS485 ports, CAN interface, and ACC input for event logging.
- Scalable storage architecture with M.2 NVMe SSD support and two removable SD card slots for high capacity video retention.
- Built-in GNSS support across major satellite constellations for continuous position and speed reporting.
- Support for common telematics and media streaming protocols to enable telemetry and video transport into fleet platforms.
- Rugged, vehicle-oriented construction and environmental tolerance intended for commercial fleet use.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the MD600 contributes synchronized video and telemetry that enhance situational awareness and incident review without requiring separate systems for media and location data. Plaspy maps, dashboard tools, and playback functions can combine the MD600's data streams for comprehensive operational visibility.

- Video and audio streaming enable incident evidence and synchronized playback alongside GPS traces in Plaspy dashboards.
- GNSS position and speed updates feed Plaspy live tracking and historical route analysis for fleet location awareness.
- Digital inputs and ACC signals become event markers in Plaspy for ignition, door, and alarm monitoring and alerting.
- Fuel sensor and analog input readings can be presented in Plaspy for fuel level monitoring and anomaly detection when sensors are connected.
- Relay and digital outputs support remote control and immobilization workflows coordinated through Plaspy when configured by the operator.
- Bluetooth and peripheral data such as BLE beacons or driver ID tokens can enrich telemetry records in Plaspy for driver identification and sensor correlation.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and remote immobilization workflows combining ignition detection and relay control.
- Driver safety monitoring and video-backed incident review for collision analysis and coaching.
- Continuous fuel level monitoring and fraud detection when paired with supported fuel sensors.
- Public transport and enforcement evidence capture using synchronized multi-channel video and timestamped telemetry.
- Cold-chain, environmental, and delivery monitoring by pairing BLE sensors with video and GPS for audit trails.
- Long-haul and logistics operations that require high retention of video and telemetry for compliance and dispute resolution.
