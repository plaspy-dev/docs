# Meitrack - MD600

![MD600](./tracker.png)

The MD600 mobile DVR from a leading telematics manufacturer delivers a powerful Plaspy compatible video telematics solution for fleets that require high-resolution evidence, driver safety features and integrated vehicle telemetry. Built for vehicle environments, the MD600 combines six-channel AHD video capture, configurable H.264/H.265 compression, multi-channel audio and a broad set of I/O and sensor interfaces so you can deploy real-time tracking, anti-theft measures and advanced driver assistance alongside Plaspy's fleet management tools.

Designed for fleet management, public transport and commercial vehicle applications, the MD600 pairs robust GNSS positioning and cellular connectivity \(4G LTE Cat 4, 3G, 2G\) with dual-band Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth and Ethernet to stream video, telemetry and events into Plaspy. Its flexible storage, environmental ratings and peripheral support \(RFID, BLE beacons, fuel sensors, relays and more\) make it a practical platform for telemetry, fuel monitoring, ignition event logging and automated immobilizer workflows when integrated with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Six-channel AHD video recording up to 6×1080P@15fps with configurable H.264/H.265 compression for efficient storage and high-quality evidence.
- Plaspy compatible connectivity: 4G LTE Cat 4, 3G/2G fallback, dual-band Wi‑Fi and Ethernet for reliable real-time tracking and telemetry upload.
- Rich I/O and sensor support—up to 8 DI/8 DO, analog inputs, CAN, RS232/RS485 and ACC input—enabling ignition/event logging and remote control integration.
- Scalable storage with M.2 NVMe SSD \(2280, PCIe x2/x4\) plus two SD cards \(up to 2 TB + 2×512 GB\) for long retention of video and telemetry records.
- Built-in GNSS \(GPS/BEIDOU/GLONASS\) with 2.5 m accuracy and -162 dBm sensitivity to feed Plaspy's mapping and geofencing features with precise location data.
- Extensive peripheral compatibility \(RFID, iButton, relays, microphones, BLE beacons, fuel sensors\) for fleet anti-theft, driver identification and fuel monitoring workflows.
- Rugged vehicle-grade design: CE certified, operates from -20°C to 70°C \(without battery\) and adheres to ISO/IEC environmental profiles for in-vehicle use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The MD600 streams and reports telemetry and event-rich video into Plaspy using standard telematics and media transport protocols. When integrated, Plaspy uses the MD600's GNSS, digital inputs and CAN data to deliver real-time tracking, alerts and historical playback on a single dashboard that combines video and telemetry for accurate incident review and operational insights.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GNSS position, speed and heading feed Plaspy maps and live tracking functions.
- Ignition and digital event logging — ACC input and digital inputs capture ignition, door and alarm events for immediate alerts in Plaspy.
- Fuel monitoring — connect ultrasonic or capacitive fuel sensors to report fuel level and anomalies into Plaspy's fuel monitoring modules.
- Remote immobilizer/control — use digital outputs and relays to implement immobilization or remote vehicle control workflows through Plaspy \(when configured by the fleet operator\).
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons — BLE peripherals such as temperature sensors or driver ID beacons can be paired and their data relayed into Plaspy for telemetry enrichment.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization — ACC inputs combined with relay control allow fleet managers to detect unauthorized ignition events and implement remote immobilizer workflows through Plaspy.
- Driver safety and ADAS/DMS — integrated AI algorithms for ADAS, DMS and BSD provide video-based alerts and recorded evidence for collision avoidance and driver monitoring when streamed to Plaspy.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry — support for ultrasonic and capacitive fuel sensors enables continuous fuel level reporting and fraud detection inside Plaspy dashboards.
- Public transport and law enforcement evidence — multi-channel synchronized video with timestamped telemetry helps recreate incidents and supports compliance reporting.
- Cold-chain and environmental monitoring — pair BLE temperature/humidity sensors with video and GPS telemetry for monitored deliveries and incident audits.
