# Meitrack - MD600

<div className="device-logo">
![MD600](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Meitrack MD600 is a vehicle mobile DVR designed for high resolution video telematics in fleet and commercial vehicle environments. It combines multi channel AHD video capture, configurable video compression, multi channel audio and a wide range of I O and sensor interfaces to collect synchronized video and vehicle telemetry for evidence, safety monitoring and operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MD600 can stream video and report telemetry into Plaspy to unify visual evidence with location and event data. That compatibility makes the MD600 a practical choice for fleets that need integrated video backed incident review, real time tracking and telemetry driven alerts inside Plaspy's fleet management environment.

## Key Highlights

- Multi channel AHD video capture for synchronized evidence across multiple viewpoints
- Configurable video compression options to balance storage and image quality
- Multiple connectivity options including cellular, Wi Fi, Bluetooth and Ethernet for reliable data upload
- Rich I O and peripheral support to capture ignition, door events and external sensor data
- Scalable local storage to retain extended video and telemetry records on vehicle
- Rugged vehicle design suited for commercial fleet and public transport deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the MD600 feeds synchronized video, GNSS position and event data into a single monitoring and reporting environment. Plaspy uses that combined stream to present live location, recorded evidence and event timelines together for faster incident analysis and ongoing fleet oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry visible on Plaspy maps and live tracking dashboards
- Event and ignition logging for immediate alerts and historical incident timelines
- Fuel and sensor data forwarded into Plaspy modules for monitoring and anomaly detection
- Remote control and relay status usable in Plaspy workflows for immobilization or operational control when configured by the operator
- BLE peripherals and external sensors integrated to enrich telemetry and link environmental data to video evidence

## Typical Use Cases

- Multi camera video evidence capture for incident reconstruction and compliance reporting
- Driver monitoring and safety programs that combine video with telemetry for coaching and review
- Anti theft and immobilizer workflows tied to ignition and relay control for fleet security
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry reporting for fuel management and loss detection
- Public transport and commercial vehicle surveillance with synchronized location stamped video

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MD600 pairs high quality multi channel video capture with broad telemetry and peripheral support, making it a suitable telematics node for fleets that require video backed evidence alongside real time tracking. Its flexible connectivity and storage capacity help reduce dependence on constant bandwidth while ensuring events and footage remain available for later review in Plaspy.

For organizations looking to combine video, location and event data in one platform, the MD600 offers a practical and rugged option that integrates with Plaspy for unified monitoring, alerting and reporting.
