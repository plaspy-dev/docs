# Meitrack - MT-80

![MT-80](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack MT-80 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for a range of small scale tracking needs. Its small footprint and lightweight design make it suitable for carrying on a person or attaching to belongings, and it includes built in location logging and common alarm features that help monitor movement and safety. The device description highlights a low power profile for extended standby, multiple tracking modes, and onboard utilities such as an accelerometer and internal storage.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT-80 can be integrated into fleet and asset monitoring workflows to provide location visibility and event alerts inside the Plaspy platform. Plaspy can ingest the tracker data to present live and historical location, trigger notifications for SOS or geofence events, and include MT-80 data in routine reporting and oversight. This compatibility makes the MT-80 a practical option for organizations and individuals who want a small form factor tracker that works with a full featured tracking platform.

## Key Highlights

- Very compact and lightweight form factor for easy carrying or attachment, dimensions 61x42x15mm and about 50g.
- Low power consumption enabling extended standby time for less frequent charging.
- Multiple tracking modes including track by SMS or GPRS using MEITRACK protocol, on demand, time interval, and distance interval.
- Built in accelerometer and internal 4Mb memory for local logging of movement and locations.
- Alerts and reports including SOS alarm, geo fence alarm, GPS blind area alarm, low battery alarm, speeding alarm, and mileage report.
- Quad band operation and SiRF III positioning for consistent tracking performance across regions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the MT-80 provides positional updates and event notifications that Plaspy surfaces for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can combine those inputs with maps, alert rules, and historical logs to give operational visibility.

- Live location display and periodic position updates based on the tracker tracking mode.
- Event alerts in Plaspy for SOS, geofence breaches, low battery, speeding, and GPS blind area notifications.
- Historical route and mileage reporting using the device logs and recorded position history.
- Configurable tracking intervals in Plaspy to match the MT-80 tracking modes for battery and data use optimization.
- Centralized monitoring for small fleets, lone workers, or asset pools where each MT-80 is assigned to an individual or item.

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking children and ensuring quick awareness of SOS events or geofence exits.
- Monitoring elderly individuals who may need timely notifications and location visibility.
- Lone worker safety and location oversight for staff operating alone or in remote situations.
- Asset protection for portable equipment or items that require discreet, compact tracking.
- Pet tracking for animals that need a lightweight and small form factor tracker.
