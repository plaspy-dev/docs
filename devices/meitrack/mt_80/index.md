# Meitrack - MT-80

![MT-80](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack MT-80 is a mini personal tracker that is suitable for a variety of applications, including tracking children, elderly individuals, lone workers, assets, and even pets. With its compact size \(61x42x15mm\) and lightweight design \(only 50g\), it is easy to carry and can be attached to a belt clip for added convenience.

One of the standout features of the MT-80 is its low power consumption, which allows for a long standby time. This means that you can rely on the tracker to provide accurate and up-to-date location information without having to constantly recharge or replace batteries. The tracker operates on the SiRF III and Quad Band 850/900/1800/1900Mhz, ensuring reliable and consistent tracking performance.

The MT-80 offers multiple tracking options, including track by SMS/GPRS using the MEITRACK protocol, track on demand, track by time interval, and track by distance interval. This flexibility allows you to customize the tracking settings based on your specific needs. Additionally, the tracker features an inbuilt accelerometer, internal 4Mb memory for logging, and an inbuilt battery for added convenience.

Other notable features of the Meitrack MT-80 include SOS alarm, geo-fence alarm, GPS blind area alarm, low battery alarm, speeding alarm, and mileage report. These features provide added security and peace of mind, allowing you to monitor and track your loved ones or valuable assets with ease.
