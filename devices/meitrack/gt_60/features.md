# Meitrack - GT-60 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Meitrack GT-60 with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing capabilities described by the manufacturer and how those capabilities are relevant when the device reports location and status into Plaspy. The goal is to provide an accurate, non-sensitive feature overview to help you decide whether the GT-60 fits your tracking needs when paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where details are important for deployment or integration, review the official Meitrack documentation and verify the device firmware and settings before relying on any specific function.

## Feature Overview

The Meitrack GT-60 is a compact, user oriented GPS tracker designed primarily for pet tracking while also doubling as a portable emergency cellular phone. It combines position acquisition and cellular reporting to deliver location visibility and basic voice communication for emergency use.

- Accurate position tracking using the device GPS receiver for pet location and movement awareness
- GSM reporting to send position data to mobile phones or a server for remote monitoring
- Portable form factor designed for attachment to pets or carrying as a personal device
- Two-way voice communication capability allowing direct calls through the device in emergencies
- Speed dialing support for up to three preset numbers to streamline emergency contact
- Multi day battery runtime on a full charge for extended monitoring between charges

## Core Features of Meitrack - GT-60

- Built in GPS module for obtaining position coordinates suitable for pet tracking
- GSM based reporting that transmits location information to phones or centralized servers
- Two-way voice call capability that enables the device to act as a basic emergency phone
- Speed dial for three preset contacts to simplify rapid voice contact
- Compact and lightweight enclosure intended for pet attachment or personal carry
- Designed for easy use with straightforward controls and preset dialing
- Battery life designed to provide more than two days of operation under typical use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a GT-60 is configured to report to Plaspy, the platform receives the device position updates and presents them alongside other tracked assets. Plaspy provides a unified view for monitoring and historical context so teams can manage location data from many devices in one place.

- Plaspy displays received GPS positions and timestamps for location awareness and tracking history
- Location updates from the GT-60 appear on maps and in device timelines within Plaspy
- Two-way voice calls are handled by the device itself, while Plaspy records the device last known position and reporting activity
- Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols to simplify onboarding of compatible devices
- Use Plaspy to centralize monitoring, visualize movements, and export position history for operational review

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet location monitoring to track a companion animal during daily activity or outings
- Personal safety device for short trips where basic voice contact and location reporting are needed
- Emergency communication device for field workers who need a compact fallback phone
- Temporary tracking for items or people where a small, portable tracker is appropriate
- Situations that require a balance of location visibility and the ability to place voice calls
