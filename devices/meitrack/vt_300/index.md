# Meitrack - VT-300

![VT-300](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack VT-300 is a high-performance GPS/GSM/GPRS tracking device designed specifically for vehicle real-time tracking and fleet management. Equipped with advanced GPS and GPRS modules, the VT-300 offers excellent sensitivity and stable performance, ensuring accurate GPS fix even in remote areas. This makes it an ideal choice for businesses and individuals looking to effectively monitor and manage their vehicles.

One of the standout features of the VT-300 is its SMS and GPRS TCP/UDP communication capability, using the Meiligao Protocol. This allows for seamless and reliable communication between the device and the tracking platform, ensuring real-time tracking and monitoring of the vehicle's location. Additionally, the VT-300 supports AGPS \(Assisted GPS\) with GSM Base Station ID, further enhancing its positioning accuracy.

The VT-300 offers a range of tracking options to suit different needs. Users can track the vehicle on demand, view its location directly on a mobile phone, or set up tracking by time interval or distance. This flexibility allows for customized tracking based on specific requirements. The device also includes a SOS panic button, enabling users to send an emergency alert in case of any unforeseen situations.

Other notable features of the VT-300 include movement alarm, geo-fencing control, low battery alarm, speeding alarm, GPS blind area alarm \(in/out\), power-cut alarm, and engine cut \(stop engine\) functionality. These features provide comprehensive security and control over the vehicle, ensuring peace of mind for users.

In summary, the Meitrack VT-300 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers accurate real-time tracking and fleet management capabilities. With its superior performance and extensive range of functions, it is an excellent choice for businesses and individuals looking to enhance the security and efficiency of their vehicles.
