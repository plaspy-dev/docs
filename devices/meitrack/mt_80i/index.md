# Meitrack - MT-80i

<div className="device-logo">
![MT-80i](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Meitrack MT-80i is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for a range of tracking scenarios including personal protection, pet monitoring, asset security, and vehicle tracking. Its small footprint and light weight make it easy to carry or attach to collars and belts. The device uses SiRF III GPS positioning and supports AGPS with GSM base station identification to improve positioning, and it operates on Quad Band GSM for broad cellular coverage.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT-80i can be integrated into fleet and asset management workflows to provide location visibility and event alerts. The unit supports multiple reporting modes including SMS and GPRS using the MEITRACK protocol, on demand reporting, interval based reports by time or distance, and has onboard logging, motion detection, listen in, and an SOS alarm which Plaspy can surface in its monitoring and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Small and lightweight design suitable for personal and pet use and discreet asset attachment
- Low power consumption with extended standby time for longer deployment between charges
- SiRF III GPS with AGPS support for reliable position acquisition
- Quad Band GSM compatibility for global cellular connectivity
- Multiple tracking modes including SMS and GPRS reporting using the MEITRACK protocol
- Built in motion sensor, internal logging memory, listen in function, and SOS alarm for situational awareness

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and process location and event data from the MT-80i so teams can monitor assets, people, and vehicles from a central platform. The device transmits position updates and status events which Plaspy converts into live map positions, alerts, and historical records to support operational oversight.

- Live location visualization on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy for SOS events, motion activity, and custom triggers
- Historical track and trip review using device logs combined with platform reporting
- Geofence and perimeter monitoring to notify teams when a device enters or leaves defined areas
- Consolidated reporting to track device uptime, movement patterns, and deployment status

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring where compact form factor is important
- Pet tracking with collar attachment and periodic position updates
- Small asset protection and recovery for equipment that needs discreet tracking
- Fleet supplementation for light vehicles or trailers that require basic location reporting
- Temporary rentals and equipment loans where easy redeployment and monitoring are needed
