# Meitrack - TA255

<div className="device-logo">
![TA255](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Meitrack TA255 is a rugged solar asset GPS tracker designed for long term, low maintenance monitoring of trailers, containers and other high value assets. It combines integrated solar charging and stackable battery modules with a weather resistant enclosure to provide extended runtime for remote and long haul deployments. Onboard GNSS positioning, multi mode cellular connectivity, Wi Fi and Bluetooth support allow the device to deliver continuous location updates and sensor telemetry for operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TA255 can feed live location and sensor data into the Plaspy platform for visualization, alerts and reporting. Its unattended operation model, sensor support and remote update capability make it a practical choice for fleet and asset managers who want centralized monitoring through Plaspy with reduced field service needs. The device is suitable for deployments where long battery life, environmental durability and scalable device management are priorities.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated solar charging with stackable battery modules supporting high capacity configurations for extended unattended operation.
- IP67 ingress protection and wide operating temperature range for outdoor and harsh environment use.
- Multi network cellular connectivity with fallback to ensure broad coverage and resilient communications.
- GNSS positioning with dependable accuracy suitable for real time tracking of mobile assets.
- Built in support for environmental and fuel sensors including temperature, humidity and fuel level monitoring.
- Remote firmware and configuration updates to simplify large scale deployments and ongoing maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TA255 streams location and sensor telemetry to a centralized monitoring environment where fleet teams can track assets, set alerts and analyze historical movement. Plaspy ingests the device data to present maps, event timelines and reports that support operational decision making and security workflows.

- Real time location and movement reporting for live situational awareness on Plaspy dashboards.
- Event and input reporting such as motion, drop detection and digital input states for door or container monitoring.
- Fuel level telemetry integration to enable consumption trending and discrepancy alerts within Plaspy.
- Relay based control workflows for remote immobilization or event driven outputs where local regulations and installation permit.
- Bluetooth sensor readings forwarded through the tracker to Plaspy for environment monitoring and cold chain visibility.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container monitoring for long haul logistics with minimal battery maintenance.
- Long distance freight tracking to maintain route visibility and reduce asset loss.
- Equipment and asset fleet management including movement alerts and utilization tracking.
- Fuel monitoring to detect theft, leakage or abnormal consumption patterns.
- Cold chain and environmental monitoring using external temperature and humidity sensors.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TA255 is suited to organizations that need durable, low maintenance tracking for remote or mobile assets and want to centralize telemetry in Plaspy. Its solar charging and modular battery approach reduce the need for frequent visits to devices, while multi modal connectivity and sensor support enable a range of monitoring and anti theft workflows. Remote update support helps keep fleets consistent and manageable at scale.
