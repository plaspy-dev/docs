# Meitrack - T1

![T1](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack T1 GPS vehicle tracker is a versatile and unique model that can be used for both fleet and personal vehicle tracking. It offers a wide range of features that make it stand out from other trackers on the market. With real-time tracking, you can monitor the location of your vehicle in real-time, ensuring that you always know where it is. The T1 also offers GPS logging, allowing you to track the historical movement of your vehicle.

One of the standout features of the T1 is its two-way audio capability. This means that you can not only track your vehicle, but also communicate with the driver or passengers. This can be particularly useful for fleet management, allowing you to provide instructions or gather information from the driver. The T1 also includes an S.O.S. button, which can be used in case of emergencies. In addition, it offers a speed alert feature, allowing you to set a speed limit and receive notifications if the vehicle exceeds it.

The T1 is compatible with a range of accessories, such as a handset phone or an RFID reader. With the handset phone, you can make and receive calls directly from the tracker, adding an extra level of convenience and functionality. The RFID reader allows you to restrict access to the vehicle, ensuring that only authorized individuals can use it. Overall, the T1 is a high-quality GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features and functionality, making it a popular choice for both fleet and personal vehicle tracking.

### Outstanding Features:

- Real-time tracking
- GPS logging
- Two-way audio
- S.O.S. button
- Speed alert
- Compatible with accessories like a handset phone and RFID reader
