# Meitrack - T622L-F9

![T622L-F9](./logo..jpg)

The Meitrack T622L-F9 is a 4G Iridium GPS tracker designed for vehicle tracking applications where precise positioning and reliable communications are important. Positioned as a high end device for fleet managers and individual vehicle owners, the T622L-F9 combines market proven tracking performance with support for safety related inputs such as Mobileye Fleet Safety and alerts for harsh acceleration and braking. Its compact form factor and environmental tolerances make it suitable for a range of vehicle types and operating conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T622L-F9 can feed location, safety events, and peripheral inputs into the Plaspy platform to support centralized fleet oversight. The tracker s Iridium communication option is particularly relevant for organizations that need continuous visibility in areas with poor cellular coverage. Paired with Plaspy, the unit can support consolidated monitoring, alerting, and reporting workflows that fleet teams rely on for operational control.

## Key Highlights

- Precise vehicle positioning for consistent location awareness and history
- Iridium communication mode to maintain tracking in areas with poor GSM coverage
- Support for Mobileye Fleet Safety to surface driver assistance related events
- Alerts for harsh acceleration and harsh braking to help monitor driving behavior
- Compact dimensions and light weight for discreet vehicle deployment with dimensions 105 mm x 65 mm x 26 mm and weight 190 g
- Wide operating temperature range and high humidity tolerance for rugged environments
- Compatibility with various peripherals to enable tailored monitoring setups

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the T622L-F9 s location and event data so fleet managers can view, filter, and act on vehicle activity from a single platform. The combination lets teams connect remote coverage and safety events to Plaspy workflows for improved operational awareness.

- Real time location and historical tracks displayed within Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Iridium enabled tracking data extends visibility into remote and low GSM coverage areas
- Safety events such as Mobileye alerts and harsh driving notifications are surfaced as platform alerts for timely review
- Peripheral inputs from the tracker can be mapped to Plaspy custom inputs for tailored monitoring and rule based alerts
- Trip summaries, geofence events, and fleet status use tracker data to drive regular reporting and operational insights
- Alerting and notification rules in Plaspy help teams respond to unusual behavior or key events detected by the device
