# Meitrack - GT-30X

![GT-30X](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack GT-30X is a compact, user friendly personal GPS tracker that uses GPS and GPRS to report position to a configured mobile phone or server. It is designed primarily for monitoring people and pets, combining location reporting, SOS support, and two way voice communications in a small package. The device also stores position data locally when connectivity is unavailable, which helps preserve tracking history in areas with poor network coverage.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT-30X can feed position and event data into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its configurable reporting intervals, SOS alarm and movement notifications make it useful for Plaspy users who need reliable visibility of tracked people or small assets. Internal logging and remote reporting options help maintain continuity of data inside Plaspy even when temporary connectivity issues occur.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and user friendly personal GPS tracker suitable for people and pets.
- Accurate position reporting with configurable periodic updates and track on demand.
- Two way voice communication and SOS panic button for direct guardian contact.
- Internal memory for logging coordinates when GPRS coverage is unavailable.
- Multiple alert features including movement alarm, geo fencing, low battery and speeding alarms.
- Power saving motion sensor to extend on device availability between reports.
- Supports SMS and GPRS communication and common reporting modes for remote monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GT-30X delivers location and event data to the platform so operators can view live positions, receive alerts, and analyze historical tracks. Plaspy collects incoming reports and exposes them through maps, notifications, and reporting tools used for operational oversight and incident response.

- Display live device location and recent breadcrumb history on Plaspy maps.
- Trigger and forward SOS, movement, and geo fence alarms as notifications to operators or designated contacts.
- Store and replay historical routes in Plaspy using device reports and stored logs once connectivity resumes.
- Configure reporting frequency and event thresholds to match operational needs and reduce noise in Plaspy dashboards.
- Include battery and status indicators in device lists to support proactive maintenance and monitoring.
- Generate standard reports for compliance or operational review based on GT-30X position and event data.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for children, elderly relatives, or vulnerable individuals.
- Pet tracking for owners who need location visibility and SOS alerts.
- Lone worker monitoring where compact, voice enabled tracking is preferred.
- Short term or ad hoc asset monitoring where a small, discrete tracker provides sufficient visibility.
- Situations requiring offline logging so location history is preserved until a connection is restored.
