# Meitrack - P88L Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the Meitrack P88L features as they relate to usage with Plaspy. It covers the practical capabilities you can expect to use through Plaspy for real time tracking, basic telemetry and alerting, and clarifies how the P88L can contribute to person centric monitoring and safety workflows on the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on the device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant and how the unit is installed or configured. Manufacturer settings, optional feature packs, and firmware updates can enable or change functions, so use this page as a general feature guide and consult the manufacturer documentation for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The P88L is a compact GNSS tracker designed for personal safety and family tracking with multiple positioning modes and a simple user interface. Its blend of multi mode positioning, dedicated alert buttons and extended standby performance makes it useful for continuous monitoring scenarios where reliable location and timely alerts are important.

- Multi mode positioning using GNSS for outdoor accuracy and cellular location plus optional Wi Fi and Bluetooth to improve coverage indoors and in dense areas.
- Dedicated SOS button and man down alert to report incidents and request help.
- Low power design with long standby in smart working modes and fast magnetic charging for quick turnaround.
- Offline buffer memory and OTA update capability to preserve data during coverage gaps and enable remote maintenance.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and compact form factor for daily carry and light outdoor use.

## Core Features of Meitrack - P88L

- Multi-technology positioning including GNSS with typical high accuracy when satellites are available, plus cellular location and optional Wi Fi and Bluetooth assistance.
- Dedicated SOS button and configurable man down alert for rapid incident notification.
- Optional Bluetooth for anti lost scenarios and short range proximity monitoring.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 compatibility with backward support for earlier cellular generations and multiple regional hardware variants.
- Fast magnetic charging and long standby times in smart working mode for extended deployments.
- Offline data buffer (8 MB) to store location and event records during temporary connectivity loss.
- Over the air firmware update support to apply remote fixes and improvements.
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and wide operating temperature range for dependable daily use.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives position reports and event messages from the P88L so operators and caregivers can view live location, receive alerts and review recent history. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and presents location and alert data in dashboards and logs for operational oversight and response.

- Real time location updates and historical position playback derived from GNSS and supplemental positioning modes.
- SOS and man down alerts forwarded to Plaspy with timestamped locations to support incident response workflows.
- Offline buffer replay to Plaspy when connectivity is restored so location history remains continuous after coverage gaps.
- OTA update status and remote maintenance workflows reflected in device management views when supported by the device firmware.
- Basic activity and step counting data available for wellness monitoring and contextual reporting when the tracker reports such telemetry.
- Optional BLE proximity events can generate Plaspy alerts for anti lost or proximity based notifications.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for seniors with SOS and man down reporting to caregivers or monitoring teams.
- Child tracking and family monitoring where compact form and real time location help guardians maintain situational awareness.
- Lone worker protection with remote location visibility and quick alerting through Plaspy.
- Event staff or temporary personnel tracking where compact, wearable trackers and long battery life simplify deployments.
- Asset pairing with people using BLE to detect separation between a person and a small item or equipment.
- Short term field operations that need easy recharge, ruggedness and continuous tracking with buffered data during spotty coverage.
