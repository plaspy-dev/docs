# Meitrack - TC68L/E

![TC68L/E](./tracker.png)

The TC68L/E is a plug and play OBD II GPS tracker designed for rapid deployment in vehicles using the standard OBD II port. It provides real time GNSS positioning with reported accuracy around 2.5 meters, combined with vehicle telemetry gathered from the OBD interface such as fuel consumption, mileage, speed and engine temperature. The unit also includes Bluetooth support for peripherals and an onboard 2.4 GHz Wi Fi hotspot for passenger connectivity, plus remote firmware update capabilities for ongoing device management.

This model is Plaspy compatible out of the box, making it suitable for operators who want to bring OBD sourced diagnostics and live location data into a unified fleet platform without complex installation. When paired with Plaspy the TC68L/E can stream location and vehicle telemetry for live monitoring, alerts and historical reporting, supporting use cases like fleet management rental and car share operations and vehicle anti theft programs.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for fast vehicle deployment and minimal installation downtime.
- GNSS positioning with approximately 2.5 meter accuracy for precise location reporting.
- OBD sourced telemetry including fuel monitoring mileage speed and engine temperature for operational insight.
- Built in 2.4 GHz Wi Fi hotspot that supports up to eight passenger devices for added value services.
- Bluetooth 4.2 and 5.0 support to extend telemetry through compatible peripheral sensors.
- Multi mode cellular coverage across regional SKUs to support broad geographic deployments.
- Remote firmware updates via FOTA for ongoing maintenance and compatibility improvements.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the TC68L/E delivers a combined feed of GNSS location and OBD telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools, enabling operators to view vehicle movement and performance alongside alerts and historical records. Plaspy can correlate position and diagnostic data to support monitoring workflows, preventive maintenance, and security alerts.

- Real time location updates and route replay available in Plaspy for operational visibility.
- OBD parameter reporting for fuel consumption mileage speed and engine temperature to inform maintenance and analytics.
- Instant removal or disconnect alerts to surface potential tampering or theft events to administrators.
- Bluetooth sensor integration to include supplemental sensor data such as temperature or cargo condition in Plaspy.
- Wi Fi hotspot status and usage metadata available for passenger connectivity reporting and service monitoring.
- FOTA support enables remote firmware updates to keep devices aligned with Plaspy features and security practices.
