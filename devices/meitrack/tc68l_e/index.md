# TC68L/E OBD II GPS Tracker

The TC68L/E is a plug-and-play GPS tracker designed for quick installation into any vehicle OBD II port. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this tracker brings real-time tracking and rich vehicle telemetry to fleet management, rental and car-share operations, and vehicle anti-theft programs without complex wiring or installation downtime.

Compact and feature-rich, the TC68L/E combines GNSS positioning \(~2.5 m accuracy\), multi-mode cellular connectivity \(4G/3G/2G\), OBD data reading for fuel monitoring and diagnostics, Bluetooth 4.2/5.0 for peripheral sensors, and an onboard 2.4 GHz Wi‑Fi hotspot that supports up to eight passenger devices. With FOTA updates and remote management, it is built to scale with Plaspy-powered tracking and telemetry workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD II installation for fast deployment and instant Plaspy compatible tracking.
- Real-time tracking and GNSS positioning with approximately 2.5 m accuracy for precise location data.
- Comprehensive telemetry from OBD: fuel consumption, mileage, speed, engine temperature and driving behavior to support preventive maintenance and analytics.
- 2.4 GHz Wi‑Fi hotspot for passenger connectivity—supports up to 8 devices while on the move.
- Bluetooth 4.2 and 5.0 support for Bluetooth sensors and peripherals to enrich sensor telemetry.
- Global cellular coverage options \(4G LTE Cat 1/4/M1/NB2, 3G, 2G\) available across regional SKUs for worldwide deployments.
- Remote firmware updates \(FOTA/OTA\) and a compact, vehicle-oriented form factor for low-profile fleet integration.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TC68L/E streams GNSS location and OBD-sourced telemetry into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting. Because the unit plugs into the vehicle OBD port, it can continuously report diagnostic and performance metrics alongside position data, enabling a unified view of vehicle health and movement inside Plaspy dashboards and APIs.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and route replay.
- OBD parameter reporting for fuel monitoring, mileage, speed and engine temperature—data that Plaspy uses for diagnostics and preventive maintenance alerts.
- Instant removal/disconnect alerts to notify administrators of possible tampering or theft.
- Bluetooth sensors: integrate supplemental sensor data \(BLE\) for temperature, cargo conditions or other BLE peripherals supported by Plaspy.
- Wi‑Fi hotspot status and usage metadata available for passenger connectivity reporting in Plaspy.
- FOTA support ensures the TC68L/E firmware can be updated remotely to maintain compatibility with Plaspy features and security updates.
