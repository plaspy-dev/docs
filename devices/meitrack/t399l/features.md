# Meitrack - T399L Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Meitrack T399L GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities are typically used with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing functions such as Bluetooth sensor support, configurable inputs, environmental monitoring, and driving behavior analysis to help you understand what the device can offer when paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The content below is based on the vendor description of the T399L; for device specific configuration and the latest technical details consult Meitrack documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Meitrack T399L combines GPS tracking with Bluetooth communication and configurable I/O to support a range of monitoring needs. It is designed for outdoor and vehicle use, with rugged protection and a wide power input range that make it adaptable to different installations.

- Bluetooth 4.2 and Bluetooth 5.0 support for connecting temperature and humidity sensors and managing multiple beacons
- Environmental monitoring via Bluetooth sensors for temperature and humidity reporting
- Configurable I/O ports to integrate external devices such as fuel sensors, temperature sensors, or panic buttons
- Driving behavior analysis with detection of driver fatigue, idling, harsh acceleration and braking, rough cornering, and collisions
- IP67 water resistant enclosure for reliable operation in wet and dusty conditions
- Compact form factor and wide DC input range for flexible vehicle integration

## Core Features of Meitrack - T399L

- Bluetooth 4.2 and Bluetooth 5.0 compatibility for external sensors and beacons
- Support for Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors
- Ability to detect and work with multiple Bluetooth beacons simultaneously
- Configurable I/O ports that can be assigned to external sensors and alarm inputs
- Driving behavior analysis including fatigue detection, idling, harsh acceleration and braking, rough cornering, and collision detection
- IP67 rating for protection against dust and water exposure
- Compact dimensions and a wide vehicle power input range for flexible installation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides a centralized platform to view and act on location and event data sent by compatible trackers like the T399L. When the T399L reports sensor readings and driving events, Plaspy can surface those items alongside GPS location to give operational context.

- Location and movement history displayed on Plaspy maps for route and position awareness
- Environmental sensor readings from Bluetooth temperature and humidity devices visible in vehicle or asset details
- Driving behavior events such as harsh braking or collisions flagged in Plaspy for review and reporting
- Inputs from configurable I/O ports mapped to events or sensor channels inside Plaspy
- Plaspy’s device onboarding is designed to detect supported tracker protocols automatically to simplify setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet driver safety programs that monitor driver behavior patterns and events
- Environmental monitoring for temperature sensitive cargo using Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors
- Asset tracking and proximity awareness using multiple Bluetooth beacons
- Vehicle installations in outdoor or industrial settings where IP67 protection is required
- Integration with external sensors or panic inputs via configurable I/O for expanded monitoring
- Mixed fleet deployments that require a compact tracker with a wide vehicle power range
