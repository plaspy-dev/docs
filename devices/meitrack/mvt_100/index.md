# Meitrack - MVT-100

<div className="device-logo">
![MVT-100](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Meitrack MVT-100 is a GPS tracker designed for fleet management and vehicle monitoring. It combines an integrated battery with extended runtime, a 3D acceleration sensor for motion and tampering detection, and platform-level features such as live tracking, reporting, and alerts. The device also supports driver identification via iButton technology and can integrate with Garmin supported devices for navigation assisted workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MVT-100 can feed location updates, movement alerts, mileage information, and driver ID events into Plaspy for centralized fleet oversight. Plaspy can use these feeds to provide real time visibility, historical reports, and operational alerts so fleets can monitor vehicles, review activity, and respond to events from a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated battery with extended runtimes offering long standby and normal operation periods for intermittent tracking needs
- Built in 3D acceleration sensor for towing detection and vibration based tampering alerts
- Compatibility with Garmin supported devices to combine navigation with tracking workflows
- MS03 tracking platform for relaying commands and issuing information reports to drivers
- Support for live tracking, GPS logging, geofencing, and alert reporting in typical deployments
- Driver identification support via iButton and built in mileage reporting for operational records

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the MVT-100 supplies vehicle location and event data to Plaspy’s fleet management tools so operators can view status, set alerts, and generate reports from one interface. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and converts it into operational insights and notifications for teams responsible for vehicle fleets.

- Real time vehicle location and movement visibility on Plaspy maps
- Event and alert processing for vibration, towing, geofence entry and exit, and other triggers
- Historical route and mileage reporting to support billing, maintenance planning, and compliance reviews
- Driver identification events mapped to driver profiles for accurate assignment of trips
- Centralized notifications and dashboards so dispatch and operations teams can act on tracker events

## Typical Use Cases

- Corporate fleet monitoring for deliveries and service vehicles
- Rental car tracking to record mileage and detect unauthorized movement
- Long haul truck and big rig oversight with live tracking and reporting
- Vehicle security and anti theft monitoring using motion and vibration alerts
- Driver assignment and trip logging using iButton based identification

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MVT-100 is a practical choice for organizations seeking a compact fleet tracker with extended battery capability and motion based security features. Its combination of a reliable tracking platform and driver ID options makes it suited to mixed fleets where both operational visibility and simple driver identification are important.

Paired with Plaspy, the unit’s core features translate into actionable fleet intelligence without requiring extensive customization. For teams that need continuous oversight, alerting, and routine reporting, this tracker provides the device level data Plaspy needs to deliver consolidated monitoring and management.
