# Meitrack - MVT-100

![MVT-100](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack MVT-100 is a top-of-the-line GPS tracker designed specifically for the fleet management industry. With its reliable specifications and innovative features, this tracker is sure to meet all your tracking needs. One standout feature of the MVT-100 is its integrated battery, which can provide up to 80 hours of power saving mode and 9 hours of normal mode. This ensures that you can track your vehicles for extended periods without worrying about battery life.

Another impressive feature of the MVT-100 is its 3D acceleration sensor, which can be used as a towing alarm or a vibration detection power switch. This allows you to monitor any unauthorized movement or tampering with your vehicles, providing an extra layer of security. Additionally, the MVT-100 is compatible with Garmin Supported Devices, allowing you to integrate advanced GPS navigation with powerful tracking capabilities. This combination provides a more complete solution for fleet management.

The MVT-100 also boasts an upgraded MS03 GPS tracking platform, which serves as the central hub for relaying commands and information directly to the driver. With live vehicle tracking, information reports, and dedicated alerts, you can stay connected and informed about your fleet at all times. Whether you need real-time tracking, geo-fencing, GPS logging, or SMS/GPRS communication, the MVT-100 has you covered.

Ideal for fleet management, vehicle tracking, rental cars, big rigs, and corporate delivery vehicles, the MVT-100 offers a wide range of functions to suit your specific needs. It is compatible with iButton technology, allowing for easy driver identification. The tracker also provides mileage reports and has a built-in vibration and acceleration sensor for added security. With its exceptional features and reliable performance, the Meitrack MVT-100 is the perfect choice for any fleet management operation.
