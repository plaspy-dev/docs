# Meitrack - MT-90 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Meitrack MT-90 with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities you can expect to see when the MT-90 is used with Plaspy for location visibility, event monitoring, and device oversight. The MT-90 V4 is a compact personal tracker noted for a long standby battery, a built in man down alarm, two way audio via a microphone and speaker, and an IP65 water resistant rating.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where the MT-90 documentation or firmware indicates optional modes or configurable reports, those details determine what Plaspy receives and shows; users should verify device firmware and configuration when planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The MT-90 combines user oriented alarms and reporting with a compact and rugged design suited to personal safety and monitoring scenarios. In Plaspy, the device can provide regular position reports, event flags, and history data that support routine tracking and incident response.

- Long standby battery life designed to extend monitoring intervals and reduce recharge frequency
- Built in man down alarm to notify caretakers or operators of potential emergencies
- Two way audio hardware with microphone and speaker enabling voice communication and listen in
- Water resistant IP65 rating that supports use in a range of outdoor conditions
- Configurable reporting such as time and distance interval reports and history logging for route and activity review

## Core Features of Meitrack - MT-90

- Long lasting standby battery enabling extended intervals between charges
- Man down alarm for automated emergency event signaling
- Built in microphone and speaker for two way audio and listen in capability
- Compact user interface with only two buttons for simplified operation
- IP65 water resistant rating for protection against dust and low pressure water jets
- Time and distance interval reporting to control how often location updates are sent
- Event and history reporting including mileage, alarm events, speed and parking history
- Detection events such as GPS antenna cut and external power cut reported by device when configured

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the MT-90 is connected to Plaspy, the platform provides centralized visibility and history for the device reports and events the tracker sends. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and ingests location and event data so teams can monitor device activity and alerts.

- Receive and display periodic location updates based on the device time or distance reporting settings
- Log and search historical routes and events such as alarms, parking events, and speed reports
- Surface man down alerts and other alarm events in timelines and device event feeds for fast review
- Display device online status and recent report timestamps so operators can track device activity
- Include notes about device configuration or installation that affect data frequency and visibility

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for lone workers or field staff
- Tracking and welfare monitoring for children and elderly individuals
- Support for people with autism or special needs where discreet monitoring is required
- Monitoring outdoor activities and trips where extended battery life is advantageous
- Patient or care recipient location awareness in community care scenarios
- Tracking larger pets or animals where a compact personal tracker is suitable

## Feature Availability Notes

- Certain features and report types depend on the device firmware version and configuration options enabled by the manufacturer
- Hardware revisions or regional variants of the MT-90 may include or omit specific detection events or accessories
- Installation choices and how the device is configured (report intervals, alarm settings) directly affect what Plaspy receives and displays
- For features such as man down alerts and listen in, operator permissions and local regulations may influence configuration and usage
- Always confirm which functions are enabled on a device before relying on them for operational processes
