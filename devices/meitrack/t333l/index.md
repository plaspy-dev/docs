# Meitrack - T333L

![T333L](./tracker.png)

The T333L by its manufacturer is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for commercial fleet management, vehicle security and advanced telematics. As an LTE Cat 1 GNSS tracker, the T333L delivers reliable real-time tracking and rich telemetry — from tire pressure monitoring and driving-behavior events to two-way voice — so fleet operators and security teams can monitor vehicles with confidence.

The device is designed to integrate smoothly with the Plaspy platform so you can centralize position, TPMS, sensor and event data for route optimization, anti-theft workflows and compliance reporting. With RS232 peripheral support, OTA/FOTA updates, and variant LTE/GSM banding for regional deployments, the T333L is practical for mixed fleets and third-party sensor ecosystems.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering consistent real-time tracking and 2.5 m positioning accuracy for precise fleet visibility.
- Built-in tire pressure monitoring \(TPMS\) for proactive tire health and safety alerts to reduce downtime and improve fuel economy.
- Driving behavior analysis \(fatigue, idling, harsh acceleration/braking, harsh cornering, collision\) to support driver coaching and risk reduction.
- Two-way voice and remote listen-in via external speaker and microphone for driver communication and on-the-spot security checks.
- Flexible RS232 interface to integrate cameras, fuel sensors, temperature probes, RFID/iButton readers and LED displays for expanded telemetry and fuel monitoring.
- Robust connectivity with LTE Cat 1, multiple regional band variants, external antennas and OTA/FOTA firmware update support.
- Long standby operation and backup battery for power-loss resilience and buffered logging \(8 MB\) when connectivity is intermittent.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the T333L streams GNSS positions and event telemetry to the platform for mapping, alerts and reporting. Plaspy ingests the device’s location, TPMS and driving-behavior events and correlates them with route history, fuel telemetry and third-party sensor inputs connected through RS232. This creates a unified, actionable view for fleet managers focused on safety, efficiency and anti-theft response.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS positions, speed, heading\) for live asset tracking and geofencing.
- Tire Pressure Monitoring System \(TPMS\) data and alerts to Plaspy for preventive maintenance workflows.
- Driving behavior events \(fatigue, idling, harsh events, collisions\) sent as actionable alerts and aggregated reports.
- Fuel monitoring and temperature telemetry via RS232-connected sensors, enabling consumption analytics and cold-chain checks.
- Remote listen-in and two-way calling events routed through Plaspy logs to support security and driver assistance use cases.
- OTA/FOTA firmware management coordinated with Plaspy for streamlined device maintenance at scale.
