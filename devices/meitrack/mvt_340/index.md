# Meitrack - MVT-340

![MVT-340](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack MVT-340 is a reliable and essential GPS vehicle tracker that provides both tracking and security features. This tracker is perfect for users who prioritize peace of mind and want a high-quality GPS tracker without logging capabilities. Despite being a base model, the MVT-340 offers a range of useful features that make it anything but basic.

One of the standout features of the MVT-340 is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the location of your vehicle at any time. Additionally, the tracker offers a geo-fence feature, which allows you to set virtual boundaries for your vehicle. If the vehicle crosses these boundaries, you will receive an alert, ensuring that you are always aware of its whereabouts.

Another notable feature of the MVT-340 is its S.O.S. button, which provides an added layer of security. In case of an emergency, the button can be pressed to send an immediate alert to a designated contact. The tracker also offers sleep mode, AGPS functionality, and speeding alerts, further enhancing its versatility and usefulness.

The MVT-340 is an ideal choice for a range of vehicles, including limousines, taxi cabs, private vehicles, motorcycles, rentals, and company vehicles. Its compact design and easy installation make it suitable for various vehicle types. Whether you are a business owner looking to track your fleet or an individual wanting to ensure the safety of your personal vehicle, the MVT-340 is a reliable and efficient choice.

### Key Features:

- Real-time tracking for accurate location monitoring
- Geo-fence feature to set virtual boundaries for your vehicle
- S.O.S. button for emergency situations
- Sleep mode to conserve battery life
- AGPS functionality for improved location accuracy
- Speeding alerts to promote safe driving habits
