# Meitrack - MVT-340

<div className="device-logo">
![MVT-340](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Meitrack MVT-340 is a compact and reliable GPS vehicle tracker designed to provide core tracking and security features for a wide range of vehicles. Positioned as a base model with practical capabilities, the MVT-340 delivers real time location monitoring, configurable geo fence alerts, an emergency S.O.S. button, sleep mode for power management, AGPS assistance, and speeding notifications. Its feature set focuses on vehicle visibility and occupant safety without including logging functions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MVT-340 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet management environment to provide live location data and event alerts alongside other assets. Compatibility makes the tracker a straightforward option for organizations or individuals who need dependable location awareness and simple security features within a broader tracking platform. Plaspy can surface the MVT-340 data to support monitoring, alerting, and operational reporting across mixed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking for continuous visibility of vehicle location
- Geo fence capability to generate alerts when virtual boundaries are crossed
- Dedicated S.O.S. button for immediate emergency notification
- Sleep mode to support longer device idle periods and power conservation
- AGPS assisted positioning to improve location responsiveness
- Speeding alerts to help enforce safer driving practices
- Compact design suitable for a variety of vehicle types

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Meitrack MVT-340 provides live position updates and event notifications that Plaspy can display, log, and act upon for operations and safety workflows. Integration lets users combine MVT-340 signals with Plaspy features to improve situational awareness and fleet oversight.

- Display real time vehicle locations on Plaspy maps for centralized visibility
- Configure geo fence alerts within Plaspy to receive notifications when boundaries are breached
- Surface S.O.S. events and speeding alerts in Plaspy dashboards and notifications
- Use Plaspy reporting to analyze movement patterns and alert histories for operational review
- Incorporate MVT-340 devices into mixed fleet views to manage different vehicle types together

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operators tracking company cars, taxis, or rental vehicles for operational control
- Limousine and chauffeur services maintaining location awareness and safety monitoring
- Individual vehicle owners seeking simple real time tracking and emergency alerting
- Motorcycle or light vehicle tracking where compact hardware is preferred
- Businesses needing a reliable base model tracker for general security and oversight
