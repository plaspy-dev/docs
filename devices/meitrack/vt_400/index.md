# Meitrack - VT-400

![VT-400](./logo.jpg)

The Meitrack VT-400 is a powerful GPS/GPRS tracking device specifically designed for heavy machinery equipment, construction machines, and vehicles. With its built-in GPS module, the VT-400 can accurately obtain position data and utilize its GSM capability to send this data to a specified mobile phone or server base for tracking and management purposes. This makes it an ideal solution for businesses looking to monitor and manage their fleet of vehicles or machinery.

One of the standout features of the VT-400 is its rugged and durable design. With an IP66 waterproof rating, it can withstand harsh weather conditions and is built to last in demanding environments. This ensures that the device will continue to function reliably even in the toughest conditions.

In addition to its robust design, the VT-400 offers a wide range of functions and features to enhance tracking and monitoring capabilities. These include SMS and GPRS TCP/UDP communication, AGPS with GSM Base Station ID, track on demand, track by time interval or distance, mileage reporting, and inbuilt motion sensor for power saving. The device also includes an internal memory for logging, an inbuilt backup battery, and an SOS panic button for emergency situations.

Furthermore, the VT-400 provides various alarm features such as movement alarm, geo-fencing control, low battery alarm, speeding alarm, GPS blind area alarm, GPS antenna disconnection alarm, power-cut alarm, and voltage detection for external power. It also offers engine cut functionality, allowing users to remotely stop the engine of the tracked vehicle or machinery if necessary.

With 2 digital inputs, 7 analog inputs, 1 open circuit output, and 1 relay output, the VT-400 provides flexibility for different reports and applications. It even has an optional RS232 output for additional connectivity options. Overall, the Meitrack VT-400 is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that is well-suited for heavy machinery equipment, construction machines, and vehicles.
