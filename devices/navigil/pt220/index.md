# Navigil - PT220

![PT220](./logo.jpg)

The Navigil PT220 is a reliable GPS tracker designed to track the location and ensure the safety of people. With its easy deployment and intelligent software, this device is perfect for personal use or for keeping track of loved ones.

One of the standout features of the PT220 is its ease of deployment. Simply insert a SIM card and configure access rights, and the device is ready to be used. It is powered by an internal battery that can be recharged with a wall charger or even from any computer using the USB cable. This makes it incredibly convenient to use and allows you to track your loved ones without any hassle. Just drop it in your pocket, briefcase, or backpack, and you are ready to go.

The intelligence software of the PT220 is another impressive feature. It can be configured to operate in automatic GPRS mode or manual SMS mode. In GPRS mode, the device will automatically send location reports periodically or at specified events such as geofence breaches. In SMS mode, you can query the location with a phone call or SMS message. This flexibility allows you to choose the mode that best suits your needs and preferences.

Additionally, the Navigil PT220 boasts a long battery life. Thanks to the low-power sleep modes of the hardware and intelligent power management of the firmware, the device can survive long periods without charging while still maintaining periodic reporting. It can be woken up from a power-saving mode by a scheduled event, motion, or an external input. This ensures that you can rely on the PT220 to provide accurate and up-to-date location information when you need it most.
