# Gator - M528

<div className="device-logo">
![M528](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Gator M528 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for fleet management and anti theft applications. Intended for use on construction trucks, rental cars, logistics vehicles, and public transportation, the M528 pairs location tracking with a suite of vehicle security and status features. The device uses modern positioning hardware and cellular connectivity to deliver frequent location updates and event reports to a central monitoring platform.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the M528 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized visibility, alerting, and reporting. Its support for both on demand and interval reporting, SOS alarm handling, low power and power disconnect notifications, and event resend from signal dead zones makes it a practical option for operators who need reliable tracking and oversight through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking capability with on demand or periodic location updates suitable for fleet monitoring
- SOS alarm and multiple configurable alarms for speeding, geo fence, parking, fatigue driving, and external power disconnect
- Built in battery backup to maintain tracking when vehicle power is interrupted
- Ability to resend stored data after signal outages to reduce gaps in history
- Inputs and output ports for basic integration with vehicle signals and remote actions
- Remote firmware update support for keeping the device current with vendor improvements

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Gator M528 provides continuous position and event information so operators can monitor vehicles from a single platform. Plaspy ingests the tracker feeds to present location history, generate alerts, and support operational decisions across a fleet.

- Live location and map visibility for individual vehicles and aggregated fleet views
- Alarm routing and notifications for SOS, geo fence breaches, speeding, low battery, and power disconnect events
- Historical reporting including mileage and event timelines to support operations and compliance
- Event logging for engine and accessory status changes, door and ACC events where the device reports them
- Use of outputs for remote actions subject to configuration and local policies
- Data continuity handling through resend behavior to reduce missing segments after coverage gaps

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring construction and heavy vehicles operating across variable sites
- Rental car tracking and loss prevention workflows
- Managing logistics and delivery fleets with real time location updates
- Oversight of public transport vehicles for route adherence and passenger safety
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery support for high value assets

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Gator M528 combines a practical set of tracking and security features with connectivity options that suit mixed fleet environments. Its battery backup, alarm suite, and the ability to buffer and resend data make it a sensible choice where continuous visibility and event integrity matter. For organizations using Plaspy, the M528 offers data points that can be mapped into operational dashboards, alerts, and reports without unnecessary complexity.

While the M528 presents a broad set of capabilities, integration details and supported event mappings depend on the specific device configuration and firmware.
