# Gator - M588T

<div className="device-logo">
![M588T](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Gator M588T is a vehicle oriented GPS tracker that combines a GPS receiver and a GSM communication module to deliver continuous and reliable location data. It is designed to transmit position information to a server so users can view device location and history from a PC or mobile phone. The M588T is commonly deployed on motorcycles, taxis, cars, buses, and trucks where steady tracking and straightforward data delivery are required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the M588T can feed location updates and alarm events into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its core capabilities such as wide GSM frequency support, broad vehicle voltage tolerance, continuous positioning, and a set of security features make it a practical choice for organizations that want to consolidate vehicle visibility and operational alerts inside Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Dual module design combining GPS positioning and GSM data transfer for continuous location reporting.
- Quad band GSM support for wide coverage and compatibility across regions.
- Broad 9 to 36 V DC input range suitable for a variety of vehicle types.
- Continuous GPS positioning with configurable reporting intervals for up to date tracking.
- Built in theft prevention and safety features including vibration alarm, SOS alarm, and power disconnect alert.
- Functional fleet features such as ACC ignition detection, tele cutoff capability, geo fence setup, and mileage statistics.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Gator M588T supplies location records and event notifications that Plaspy displays on maps and in fleet dashboards. Plaspy ingests the device data to provide situational awareness, allow historical review, and support operational workflows.

- Real time location display and live tracking for individual vehicles and entire fleets.
- Event and alarm handling so SOS, vibration, power disconnect, and other alerts are surfaced to operators.
- Historical track and mileage reports for route review and operational analysis.
- Geofence alerts and boundary monitoring integrated into Plaspy notifications and logs.
- Fleet level dashboards to monitor vehicle status, recent activity, and summary metrics.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operations that need continuous vehicle location and mileage reporting.
- Taxi and ride services requiring real time tracking and passenger safety alerts.
- Delivery and logistics fleets monitoring routes and geofence compliance.
- Public transport and bus operators tracking vehicle location and schedule adherence.
- Theft prevention and recovery workflows using vibration alarm, SOS, and power disconnect alerts.
