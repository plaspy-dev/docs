# Coban - GPS303 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Coban GPS303 with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker’s practical capabilities, how those capabilities appear in a fleet or asset tracking workflow, and what you can expect when integrating the device into Plaspy for monitoring and oversight.

Exact feature availability for any GPS303 unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. For device-specific setup, advanced configuration, or firmware details consult the official manufacturer documentation and your installation provider.

## Feature Overview

The GPS303 is a compact GPS tracker designed to report location and basic event information over the cellular network. It supports both on-demand and continuous reporting modes and includes several common alarm and alert functions that are useful for vehicle, asset, and personal tracking scenarios.

- Real time location reporting via internet or SMS for immediate position visibility
- Automatic continuous tracking mode for live movement monitoring
- Geo-fencing capability to generate enter and exit notifications for defined areas
- Dedicated SOS button to send an emergency alert to predefined contacts
- Over-speed and movement alarms for event-driven alerts
- Low battery alarm to help ensure long term availability and maintenance

## Core Features of Coban - GPS303

- Location tracking using GPS satellite positioning combined with cellular data reporting
- Real time updates available either by internet reporting or SMS commands
- Automatic tracking mode for periodic or continuous position updates
- Geo-fencing support for virtual boundary alerts
- SOS emergency alert function via the device's SOS button
- Over-speed alarm to notify when configured speed thresholds are exceeded
- Movement alarm to detect and alert on unauthorized or unexpected motion
- Low battery alarm to warn of decreasing device power

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present the GPS303’s location and event information to provide operational visibility and historical context. When the device reports positions and alarms, Plaspy surfaces those records in maps, timelines, and notification feeds to support monitoring and response.

- Live map view showing current GPS position updates reported by the device
- Event and alarm notifications for SOS, over-speed, movement, and low battery conditions
- Historical position history and route playback based on the device’s reported points
- Geo-fence events shown as enter and exit alerts tied to defined areas in Plaspy
- Aggregated monitoring across multiple devices for fleet visibility and oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and live tracking of vehicles in transit
- Asset movement monitoring for trailers, equipment, or high-value goods
- Personal tracking for lone workers or monitored individuals requiring SOS support
- Route verification and playback for operational audits or delivery confirmation
- Continuous location reporting for temporary rentals or short term assignments
- Event-driven monitoring where alerts trigger follow up or dispatch actions

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some functions depend on the tracker firmware and manufacturer configuration options
- SMS-based commands and alerts require an active SIM with voice/SMS or data service as applicable
- Behavior and thresholds for alarms such as over-speed or movement can vary by firmware or settings
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may alter available features or supported bands
- Always verify the installed device firmware and configuration with the official manufacturer documentation

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the Coban GPS303 together with Plaspy gives operators a straightforward way to centralize location reporting and event notifications. Plaspy presents live locations, alarm events, and historical tracks in a single view, helping teams maintain situational awareness, respond to emergencies, and analyze movement patterns over time.

To learn more about Plaspy and how it can integrate with devices like the Coban GPS303 visit https://www.plaspy.com. For the most current, device specific feature descriptions, firmware notes, and manufacturer guidance consult the official Coban resources at https://www.coban.net/.
