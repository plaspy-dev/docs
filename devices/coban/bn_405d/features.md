# Coban - BN-405D Features

This page provides a public features overview for the Coban BN-405D and its use with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities that are relevant when the tracker is integrated into Plaspy for real time location, alarms, and telemetry visibility across mixed network environments.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, region, and manufacturer implementation. Always verify device specific functions and limits against the official Coban documentation and your installed firmware before planning production deployments.

## Feature Overview

The BN-405D is a compact vehicle mounted GPS tracker built for professional fleet and asset applications. It combines multi band cellular connectivity and a high sensitivity GNSS receiver to provide continuous position reporting, event alarms, and remote control features that integrate into Plaspy for monitoring and response.

- Reliable position reporting and historical track playback for vehicle visibility and route review
- Comprehensive alarm reporting including geo fence, overspeed, door and shock events for security monitoring
- Ignition and ACC notifications to correlate vehicle state with location and usage
- Remote control options such as fuel or power cut and immobilization support for theft response
- Optional sensor and camera support for extended telemetry and evidence capture when installed

## Core Features of Coban - BN-405D

- Multi band cellular connectivity for stable coverage across 4G 3G and 2G networks
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver for accurate location fixes and dependable tracking
- Ignition and ACC detection for trip and engine state reporting
- Full alarm suite including geo fence, overspeed, shock, door open, movement, and low battery events
- Remote immobilizer and fuel or power cut capabilities via relay control
- SOS emergency and remote listening functionality for safety and incident response
- Support for optional external sensors such as fuel level and environmental sensors
- Ability to connect optional Wi Fi cameras for photo and video evidence capture

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the BN-405D reports position, status and alarm events so operators can monitor fleets, respond to incidents, and automate workflows. Plaspy ingests device reports and presents them alongside map views, history, and alerting tools to support operational oversight.

- Live location and historical tracks appear in Plaspy for situational awareness and route analysis
- Alarm events such as geo fence breaches, overspeed and door open are shown in Plaspy and can trigger notifications or workflows
- Ignition and ACC states help Plaspy correlate vehicle activity with driver events and dispatch decisions
- Remote immobilizer and fuel or power cut actions are available for coordinated security response when supported by the installed device and configuration
- Optional sensor and camera data reported by the device can be surfaced in Plaspy where available for evidence and telemetry review

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch optimization with live tracking and route playback
- Anti theft and recovery workflows leveraging instant alarms and remote immobilizer control
- Public transport and passenger safety monitoring with door and ACC event visibility
- Fuel monitoring and loss detection when external fuel sensors are fitted
- Construction equipment and off road vehicle tracking for location history and security
- Incident evidence collection using optional cameras and remote listening features

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some functions depend on the installed firmware and may be added, modified, or disabled by manufacturer updates
- Hardware revisions and optional modules determine support for external sensors and camera attachments
- Installation method and wiring influence availability of ignition, ACC, door and relay controlled functions
- Regional cellular band variants affect network coverage and roaming performance
- For definitive compatibility and configuration steps consult the manufacturer documentation for your BN 405D unit

Using the BN-405D with Plaspy gives organizations centralized visibility into vehicle location, state, and security events. Plaspy translates the tracker reports into map views, alerts, and historical records that help operators reduce response times, enforce policies, and analyze fleet performance.
