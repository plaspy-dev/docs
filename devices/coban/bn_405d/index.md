# Coban - BN-405D

![BN-405D](./tracker.png)

The BN-405D is a compact vehicle mounted GPS tracker designed for professional fleet management, logistics, construction and public vehicle applications. Built to provide reliable real time tracking, the BN-405D combines multi band cellular connectivity with a high sensitivity GNSS receiver to deliver position, telemetry and alarm reporting across mixed network environments. Its form factor and vehicle focused feature set make it suitable for embedded installation in 12–24V vehicles used in commercial operations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the BN-405D can deliver continuous location and event data to Plaspy dashboards for visibility and operational oversight. When paired with Plaspy, the unit’s telemetry and alarm reporting support fleet optimization, security workflows and automated alerts, enabling organizations to monitor vehicles, respond to incidents and centralize tracking in a single telematics platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible with integration options including TCP UDP and SMS for direct reporting to Plaspy servers and fast onboarding.
- Multi band cellular connectivity for stable coverage and roaming across mixed network environments.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver with typical position accuracy around 5 m for dependable location reporting.
- Comprehensive alarm suite including overspeed geo fence shock door open movement low battery and external power disconnect for strong anti theft protection.
- Remote control and security features such as fuel or power cut via relay arming disarming and SOS emergency alarms for rapid response.
- Flexible telemetry support with optional fuel level and environmental sensors plus support for external camera modules and remote listening.
- Vehicle grade design intended for embedded installation with a compact form factor and backup capability for tamper alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The BN-405D reports position, status and alarm events to Plaspy so fleet managers can view live location, playback historical tracks and trigger automated alerts and workflows. Integration leverages the device reporting methods to feed Plaspy with continuous, actionable data for monitoring and incident response.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy using TCP UDP or SMS transport as supported by the device.
- Event and alarm reporting such as ignition ACC door open shock and movement allow Plaspy to correlate driving behavior and security incidents.
- Optional fuel level and sensor telemetry can be integrated into Plaspy for fuel management and theft detection when external sensors are present.
- Remote immobilizer and power or fuel cut capabilities enable Plaspy initiated actions for theft prevention and risk control.
- SOS emergency alarms remote listening and camera evidence support safety workflows and incident investigations inside Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch optimization with live tracking and route playback for commercial vehicle fleets.
- Anti theft protection and immobilization for high value vehicles using alarms and remote relay control.
- Public transport and passenger vehicle safety monitoring with ACC and door event visibility and SOS response.
- Fuel and asset control using optional fuel sensors and telemetry to detect consumption anomalies and theft.
- Construction and off road fleet tracking for vehicles operating in mixed coverage areas with robust alarm reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The BN-405D offers a practical combination of connectivity, positional accuracy and vehicle oriented features that align well with Plaspy’s fleet monitoring and security capabilities. Its multi band cellular support and high sensitivity GNSS help maintain reliable tracking across different coverage scenarios, while the built in alarm logic and remote control functions give operators tools for anti theft response and operational control.

When used with Plaspy, the BN-405D can be quickly integrated into centralized dashboards to provide real time visibility, event driven alerts and historical reporting. This makes it a suitable choice for organizations looking to standardize telematics across mixed fleets and add remote security and telemetry driven workflows.
