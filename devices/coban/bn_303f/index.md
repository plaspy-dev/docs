# Coban - BN-303F

<div className="device-logo">
![BN-303F](./tracker.png)
</div>

The BN-303F, also listed as model 303FG, is a compact vehicle mounted GPS tracker designed for reliable deployment in anti theft, fleet management and real time tracking scenarios. Built for concealed installation in cars, commercial trucks and rental vehicles, the BN-303F delivers GNSS positioning, continuous telematics and configurable alarms to keep fleet managers and vehicle owners informed and in control.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the BN-303F integrates with cloud telemetry and fleet monitoring workflows to provide live visibility and operational oversight. Its 2G GSM GPRS communication, event driven reporting modes and remote control capabilities make it a practical option for pairing with Plaspy to handle location updates, alarm handling and immobilization workflows in managed fleets and vehicle security setups.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking and historical track playback.
- Configurable alarm set including SOS, movement shock, geofence, door open and ignition alerts.
- Remote control options for fuel or power cutoff to support immobilization workflows.
- Compact concealed form factor suitable for passenger cars and commercial vehicles.
- Continuous GNSS positioning for reliable location reporting in fleet environments.
- Flexible reporting modes and communication fallbacks for consistent telemetry delivery.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the BN-303F sends location and status telemetry so the platform can present live positions, play back tracks and raise alerts. Plaspy ingests event driven messages and device reports to enable monitoring, reporting and operational responses across a fleet.

- Live location updates and track playback for operational visibility and route review.
- Event based alerts for SOS, geofence breaches, ignition and door status to notify operators.
- Remote immobilization and control actions triggered from Plaspy when the device and wiring support those functions.
- Consolidated telemetry in Plaspy for fleet level dashboards, alert routing and incident tracking.
- Historical reporting and exportable data to support compliance, maintenance planning and operational analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium commercial vehicle fleets requiring continuous tracking and route history.
- Vehicle anti theft protection with concealed installation, SOS alerts and remote immobilization options.
- Rental and lease vehicle monitoring using geofences, ignition and door alarms to protect assets.
- Logistics and route oversight for delivery confirmation and trip validation across distribution operations.
- Company vehicle oversight to support maintenance scheduling and driver behavior review.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The BN-303F is a practical telematics endpoint that pairs well with Plaspy’s cloud features. Its compact size, GNSS positioning and event driven reporting deliver the core capabilities fleet operators need for tracking, alarms and remote response. When combined with Plaspy’s dashboards, alerting and reporting, the device supports everyday fleet supervision and anti theft measures without unnecessary complexity.
