# Coban - GPS-109 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Coban GPS-109 and explains how those features relate to use with Plaspy. It describes the GPS-109's practical capabilities for locating and monitoring targets, and how Plaspy can present position and event information for operational visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary with device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. For device specific configuration, limits, and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Coban GPS-109 is a compact GPS tracker that uses GSM GPRS networks and GPS satellites to deliver location information and event reports. Its design focuses on portability, durable operation in a range of environments, and a set of user-facing functions for position requests and basic alarms.

- Location reporting over GSM GPRS with GPS satellite positioning for regular or on demand tracking
- Multiple control methods supported including SMS and connection from a computer or PDA for remote queries
- Compact and lightweight form factor suited to concealed or portable installations
- High GPS sensitivity and accuracy to provide precise location information in typical conditions
- Built in power source and waterproof enclosure for extended field use in a variety of conditions
- Built in alarm and control functions including geo-fence, overspeed, movement, and low battery alerts

## Core Features of Coban - GPS-109

- Operation on GSM GPRS networks combined with GPS satellite positioning for location reporting
- Multiple control methods supported such as SMS queries and PC or PDA interfaces
- Compact physical dimensions and light weight for discreet placement and portability
- GPS sensitivity of -165 dBm and stated GPS accuracy around 5 m for street level location
- Fast GPS start times with distinct cold warm and hot start characteristics
- Rechargeable onboard battery designed for extended use and field operation
- IP67 rated enclosure providing water resistance suitable for many outdoor uses
- User functions including adding and deleting authorized phone numbers, single locate requests, continuous auto track, geo-fence setup, low battery alarms, and overspeed and movement alerts

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can collect and present the GPS-109 location and event data so fleets and teams can monitor assets and respond to incidents. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and can accept device reports configured to send to Plaspy servers.

- Live and historical location display on the Plaspy map to support tracking and route review
- Event and alarm entries for geo-fence, overspeed, movement, and low battery notifications when the device sends corresponding messages
- Continuous tracking streams from the device shown as updates on Plaspy for operational awareness
- Authorized number and SMS control functions remain device side while Plaspy surfaces events generated by those settings
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and accepts reporting over common transport methods so devices can be pointed to Plaspy servers such as d.plaspy.com

## Typical Use Cases

- Temporary equipment tracking during projects or maintenance windows
- Remote site monitoring to detect movement or low battery conditions
- On demand single locate queries for verification of device position
- Continuous tracking for tasks that require real time location updates
