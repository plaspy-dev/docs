# Coban - GPS-109

![GPS-109](./logo..png)

The Coban GPS-109 is a versatile GPS tracker that operates on the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites, allowing you to locate and monitor remote targets with ease. Whether you prefer to use SMS, a computer, or a PDA, this tracker provides reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. With its compact dimensions of 85×65×27mm and lightweight design weighing only 188g, the GPS-109 is highly portable and can be easily carried or concealed as needed.

One of the standout features of the GPS-109 is its impressive GPS sensitivity of -165dBm, ensuring accurate and precise location tracking. The tracker boasts a GPS accuracy of 5m, providing you with the exact street address of your target. Additionally, the GPS start time is quick, with a cold status start time of 45 seconds, a warm status start time of 35 seconds, and a hot status start time of just 1 second.

The GPS-109 is equipped with a rechargeable 3.7V 5000mAh Li-ion battery, ensuring long-lasting performance. It has a wide operating temperature range of -20°C to +55°C, allowing it to withstand various weather conditions. The tracker is also waterproof with an IP67 rating, providing protection against water immersion up to 1 meter for 30 minutes.

With a range of useful functions, the GPS-109 offers great flexibility and convenience. You can easily add and delete authorized phone numbers, enabling you to control who has access to the tracker. The single locating feature allows you to quickly obtain the current location of your target. The auto track continuously function provides real-time tracking, ensuring you stay updated on the movements of your target. You can also set up a geo-fence, receive low battery alarms, and configure over speed and movement alarms for added security.

Overall, the Coban GPS-109 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers accurate tracking capabilities and a range of useful functions. Whether you need to monitor vehicles, assets, or loved ones, this tracker provides the tools you need to stay informed and in control.
