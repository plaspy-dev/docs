# Coban - TK103B

![TK103B](./logo.jpg)

The Coban TK103B is a vehicle oriented GPS tracker designed for positioning, security monitoring, and real time tracking. It combines satellite positioning with cellular data transmission and SMS reporting to provide location visibility, emergency alarms, and surveillance features. The device is commonly used to locate vehicles, monitor movement, and trigger alerts for events such as SOS, low battery, geofence breaches, and overspeed situations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK103B can feed location and event information into Plaspy for centralized fleet oversight. Its support for multiple transmission methods and typical vehicle focused features make it a practical choice for organizations that want to consolidate tracking data within Plaspy for map visualization, alerting, and operational reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for vehicle tracking with positioning and security focused features.
- Multiple reporting options including SMS and cellular data for flexible connectivity.
- Built in features such as SOS alarm, geo fencing, overspeed alerts, and low battery warning.
- Optional capabilities available for fuel sensing and remote door control depending on configuration.
- Dual SIM support for improved coverage and continuity in service.
- Sleep mode and other power management features to conserve energy when idle.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TK103B delivers location and event data that Plaspy presents on maps and reporting dashboards for fleet managers and operators. Plaspy can aggregate the tracker data alongside other devices to provide consistent operational visibility and historical playback.

- Live vehicle location and movement displayed on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring.
- Event and alarm handling in Plaspy for SOS, geofence breaches, overspeed alerts, and low battery notices.
- Track history and route playback to review trips and support operational analysis.
- Centralized reporting and exportable logs to support fleet performance reviews and record keeping.
- Scaling from single vehicle monitoring to larger fleets with consolidated visibility and oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to mid size vehicle operations.
- Vehicle security and recovery with SOS and geofence alerts.
- Delivery and logistics tracking to monitor routes and stop patterns.
- Remote monitoring of high value vehicles for operational oversight.
- Driver safety programs focusing on speed and location based alerts.
