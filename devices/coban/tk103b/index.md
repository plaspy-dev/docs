# Coban - TK103B

![TK103B](./logo.jpg)

The Coban TK103B GPS Tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a wide range of functions for accurately positioning, security, monitoring surveillance, emergency alarms, and tracking. Designed specifically for vehicles, this GPS tracker is equipped with multiple features that make it ideal for various applications in the field of management, traffic safety, and location-based services.

With support for SMS, GPRS, and internet data transmission, the Coban TK103B GPS Tracker can be used for large-scale monitoring, emergency scheduling, and real-time tracking. It operates on GSM/GPRS wireless telecommunication internet and GPS satellite global positioning system, allowing you to track your vehicle through multiple methods. You can use the monitor software on your PC, track through your PDA cell phone, or even use Google Earth for real-time tracking.

The Coban TK103B GPS Tracker comes with a range of outstanding features that enhance its functionality. These features include dual SIM card support, fuel sensor \(optional\), lock and unlock door \(optional\), GPS and location-based service \(LBS\), real-time tracking via SMS or GPRS, track on demand by calling or SMS command, voice surveillance, low battery alarm, SOS button for emergencies, geo-fencing control, overspeed alarm, and much more. Additionally, the tracker is equipped with a sleep mode function, allowing it to conserve power when not in use.

Overall, the Coban TK103B GPS Tracker is a reliable and feature-packed device that offers accurate positioning, security, and monitoring capabilities for vehicles. Whether you need to track your fleet of vehicles for management purposes or ensure the safety and security of your personal vehicle, this GPS tracker is an excellent choice.
