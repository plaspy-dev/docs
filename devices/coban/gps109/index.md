# Coban - GPS109

<div className="device-logo">
![GPS109](./Logo.png)
</div>

The Coban GPS109 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed to locate and monitor remote targets using the GSM GPRS mobile network and GPS satellites. With small dimensions and a lightweight build, the GPS109 provides accurate position reporting and a set of practical tracking features including single locate and continuous auto tracking, geo fence, movement and over speed alerts, low battery warnings, and an IP67 waterproof rating for use in varied environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GPS109 can feed location and status information into the Plaspy platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can display the device on maps, aggregate location history, and surface alerts and reports so teams can manage vehicles and assets from a single interface while taking advantage of the tracker’s on device features.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight form factor suitable for portable deployment and discreet placement
- Accurate location reporting with high sensitivity GPS and typical horizontal accuracy around 5 meters
- Long run capability enabled by a high capacity rechargeable battery for extended operation
- IP67 rated enclosure for reliable performance in wet or dusty conditions
- Built in alarms and configurable functions including geo fence, movement, over speed, shock, and low battery alerts
- Works over existing GSM GPRS mobile networks for broad geographic coverage

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GPS109 can be integrated with Plaspy so that position updates and device alerts are delivered to the Plaspy platform for monitoring, reporting, and operational use. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s location and event data and provides a single place to visualize device activity and respond to incidents.

- Live map view for real time vehicle and asset location tracking inside Plaspy
- Geofence alerting and history to notify teams when units enter or leave defined areas
- Movement and over speed notifications routed to Plaspy for operational alerts and follow up
- Battery and device status alerts displayed in Plaspy for proactive device management
- Historical route and position reporting to support trip reconstruction and performance review

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small and medium sized fleets requiring portable, durable devices
- Asset monitoring where compact form factor and waterproofing help protect the tracker in harsh environments
- Temporary rentals and equipment hire where easy deployment and reliable location updates are needed
- Delivery and logistics visibility to track routes, stops, and time on site
- Remote personnel or lone worker location monitoring when discreet portable trackers are preferred

The GPS109 is a practical choice when you need a balance of compact size, accurate positioning, and a useful feature set such as geofencing, movement and overspeed alarms, and waterproof protection. When paired with Plaspy, the device’s location stream and event signals become part of a unified monitoring and reporting workflow that supports fleet oversight, alerts, and historical analysis.
