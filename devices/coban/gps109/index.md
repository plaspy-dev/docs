# Coban - GPS109

![GPS109](./Logo.png)

The Coban GPS109 is a versatile GPS tracker that allows you to locate and monitor remote targets using the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites. With its compact dimensions of 85 x 65 x 27mm and lightweight design weighing only 188g, this tracker is highly portable and easy to use. It operates on the GSM/GPRS network with a frequency band of 850/900/1800/1900MHz, ensuring reliable and accurate tracking capabilities.

Equipped with a high-sensitivity GPS module, the GPS109 offers precise location tracking with an accuracy of 5 meters. It has a fast start time, with cold status taking 45 seconds, warm status taking 35 seconds, and hot status taking just 1 second. The tracker is powered by a rechargeable 3.7V 5000mAh Li-ion battery, providing long-lasting performance. It has a wide operating temperature range of -20°C to +55°C and a storage temperature range of -40°C to +85°C, making it suitable for use in various environments.

The Coban GPS109 offers a range of useful features to enhance your tracking experience. You can add and delete authorized phone numbers, allowing you to control who can access the tracker's information. The tracker supports single locating, auto track continuously, and provides the exact street address of the target. It also offers features such as SIM card balance inquiry, low battery alarm, geo-fence, movement alarm, over speed alarm, shock sensor alarm, sleep mode, and GPRS configuration. With its IP67 waterproof level, the GPS109 is resistant to water and can withstand various weather conditions.

Overall, the Coban GPS109 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that is suitable for a wide range of applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or even people, this tracker provides accurate and real-time location information to help you stay connected and in control.
