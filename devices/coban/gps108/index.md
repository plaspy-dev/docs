# Coban - GPS108

![GPS108](./Logo.jpg)

The Coban GPS108 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers accurate location tracking and a wide range of applications. It can be used to guard vehicles against theft, protect children, elderly, disabled individuals, and pets, manage personnel, and even track criminals discreetly. This tracker operates based on the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites, allowing you to locate and monitor remote targets through SMS, computer, or PDA.

One of the standout features of the Coban GPS108 is its ability to provide exact street addresses, ensuring precise location information. It also offers a range of functions such as adding and deleting authorized phone numbers, single tracking, continuous auto tracking, low battery alarm, geo-fencing, movement alarm, over-speed alarm, shock sensor alarm, sleep mode, and GPRS configuration. With its comprehensive set of features, this GPS tracker is suitable for a variety of tracking and monitoring needs.

The Coban GPS108 has impressive technical specifications as well. It operates on a 4-band GSM frequency \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\) and has a GPS sensitivity of -165dBm, ensuring reliable and accurate tracking. The GPS accuracy is 5 meters, providing precise location information. The tracker also has a quick start time, with cold status taking 45 seconds, warm status taking 30 seconds, and hot status taking just 1 second. It supports multi-track ways, including GPS, LBS, and a three-axis sensor. With a 3.7V 10000mAh Li-ion battery, the GPS108 offers a long standby time of up to 6 months in sleep mode or up to 6.9 years in schedule mode. Additionally, it has an IP67 waterproof rating, making it suitable for use in various environments.
