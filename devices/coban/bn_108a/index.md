# Coban - BN-108A

![BN-108A](./tracker.png)

The BN-108A is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for mobile asset management, covert vehicle tracking and anti-theft protection. Its combination of strong magnetic adsorption for tool-free mounting, Bluetooth automatic induction for arming/disarming, and a very large 10,000 mAh rechargeable battery delivers long standby times and dependable real-time tracking when integrated with the Plaspy platform.

Designed to plug into Plaspy for fleet management and telemetry workflows, the BN-108A provides continuous position updates, multi-type alarms, and remote voice monitoring via a mobile app or TCP/UDP/SMS links. These features make the BN-108A an effective choice for businesses that need reliable asset oversight, anti-theft response, and straightforward platform integration.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and playback via app or server.
- Massive 10,000 mAh rechargeable Li‑ion battery for extended standby and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Strong magnetic adsorption for quick, tool‑free mounting to vehicles, trailers, or high‑value assets.
- Bluetooth automatic induction for simplified arming and disarming and support for Bluetooth sensors.
- Multiple alarms including low battery, movement, shock, overspeed and geofence for anti‑theft alerting.
- Flexible positioning strategies \(real-time, smart mode, power‑saving\) to balance power and reporting cadence.
- Remote voice monitoring and SMS control for on-demand situational awareness and configuration.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the BN-108A streams location and event data via GSM/GPRS \(TCP, UDP or SMS\) to provide continuous situational awareness. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions, alarms and telemetry to display live location, trigger alerts, and populate historical tracks for playback and reporting. The unit’s Bluetooth induction and on-device alarms feed status updates to Plaspy to support anti-theft workflows and fleet management dashboards.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(position, timestamp, movement status\) via TCP/UDP/SMS.
- Alarm reporting: low battery, movement, shock, overspeed and geofence events forwarded to Plaspy for instant alerts.
- Remote voice monitoring: pick up ambient sound on demand and record events for incident review.
- Bluetooth sensors and automatic induction: use BLE accessories or smartphone proximity to arm/disarm tracking modes.
- Flexible reporting modes \(online, smart, power‑saving\) tailored through Plaspy to optimize battery life and update frequency.

The BN-108A combines long battery endurance, flexible reporting modes and simple mounting to deliver a low‑maintenance GPS tracker that integrates cleanly with Plaspy for real-time tracking and fleet management. Its broad GSM band support and TCP/UDP/SMS protocols make server connectivity straightforward, while Bluetooth automatic induction and multi-type alarms add practical anti-theft and telemetry capabilities. For fleets and asset owners who need reliable position data, geofence and alarm handling, and remote voice monitoring, the BN-108A offers a practical, Plaspy compatible solution that scales from single-asset deployments to larger telemetry programs.
