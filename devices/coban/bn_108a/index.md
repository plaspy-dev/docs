# Coban - BN-108A

![BN-108A](./tracker.png)

The BN-108A is a compact GPS tracker from Coban designed for mobile asset management, covert vehicle tracking, and anti theft protection. It combines strong magnetic adsorption for tool free mounting, Bluetooth automatic induction for simplified arming and disarming, and a very large 10,000 mAh rechargeable battery to deliver extended standby times and dependable real time location updates.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the BN-108A can be integrated into fleet monitoring and telemetry workflows to provide continuous position updates, multi type alarms, and remote voice monitoring. When paired with Plaspy the unit helps centralize tracking data, alerting, and historical playback for operational oversight and anti theft response.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker for real time tracking and historical playback through platform dashboards.
- Large 10,000 mAh rechargeable battery that reduces maintenance intervals and supports long standby operation.
- Strong magnetic adsorption enables quick tool free mounting on vehicles, trailers, or other assets.
- Bluetooth automatic induction for easy arming and disarming and support for proximity based workflows.
- Multiple alarm types including low battery, movement, shock, overspeed and geofence for anti theft alerting.
- Remote voice monitoring and SMS control for on demand situational awareness and lightweight remote management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the BN-108A sends position and event information to the platform where it can be visualized, filtered, and used to trigger operational alerts. Plaspy ingests the device data to present live location, alarm notifications, and historical tracks that support fleet management and incident review.

- Live location and telemetry visible on Plaspy dashboards for continuous situational awareness.
- Alarm forwarding to Plaspy for instant notifications on low battery, movement, shock, overspeed and geofence breaches.
- Historical track playback and reporting to analyze routes, events, and asset movement over time.
- Remote voice monitoring events captured and logged for incident review within Plaspy records.
- Bluetooth based arming and disarming status reflected in Plaspy to support anti theft and proximity workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle tracking and anti theft protection for high value cars, rental vehicles, and service fleets.
- Trailer and container oversight where magnetic mounting and very long battery life are priorities.
- Fleet security and operational monitoring including route playback and event based alerting.
- Protection of portable equipment and assets in transit or storage with on movement and shock alerts.
