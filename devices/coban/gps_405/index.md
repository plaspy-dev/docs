# Coban - GPS-405

![GPS-405](./logo..png)

The Coban GPS-405 is a versatile vehicle tracker designed to provide real time management and monitoring for a range of vehicle types. Built to operate with the BAANOOL IOT platform and APP platform, the GPS-405 delivers movement tracking, geo-fencing, overspeed alerts, door and ACC detection, shock detection, external power cut notifications, and an SOS button. Certain models expand functionality further with options such as a Wi Fi hotspot, temperature and humidity sensing, and an integrated Wi Fi camera, making the model adaptable to different operational needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GPS-405 brings those tracking and alert features into Plaspy for centralized fleet visibility and operational oversight. Its support for multiple cellular bands and a mix of location methods combined with an IP66 waterproof rating and industry certifications make the GPS-405 a practical option for organizations that want to integrate a proven vehicle tracker into Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Real time GPS plus auxiliary location methods for continuous vehicle visibility
- Broad event and alert set including geo fencing, overspeed, shock, ACC, and door monitoring
- Built in emergency SOS and options for external engine or power cutoff control
- Models available with additional sensors and accessories such as Wi Fi camera and environment monitoring
- Multi network band support for consistent connectivity across regions
- IP66 rating and industry certifications for reliable field use

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GPS-405 can feed its location and event data into Plaspy so fleet managers see vehicle positions, histories, and safety events in a unified interface. Plaspy collects the tracker reports and translates them into live maps, alerts, and reports that support operational decision making.

- Live location display and historical route playback for each vehicle
- Geofence and overspeed events surfaced as alerts within Plaspy for immediate attention
- Door, ACC, shock, external power and SOS events integrated into notification workflows
- Consolidated reporting and exportable trip and event summaries for fleet analysis
- Centralized monitoring across mixed fleets so GPS-405 equipped vehicles appear alongside other compatible devices

## Typical Use Cases

- Logistics and freight fleet tracking for route oversight and delivery visibility
- Cash in transit and bank vehicle monitoring where security and rapid alerts are critical
- Anti theft and recovery monitoring for passenger cars and commercial vehicles
- Official vehicle management and oversight for public or corporate fleets
- Environmental or specialized cargo monitoring when units include temperature and humidity options

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPS-405 combines a comprehensive feature set with practical ruggedness and multiple connectivity options, making it well suited to fleets that need both visibility and security features. When used with Plaspy, the tracker’s events and positional data can be consolidated into dashboards and alert rules that improve day to day fleet operations and incident response.
