# Coban - GPS-405

![GPS-405](./logo..png)

The Coban GPS-405 is a versatile vehicle tracker that utilizes the BAANOOL IOT platform and APP platform to provide real-time management of vehicles. It is commonly used in the logistics and freight industry, as well as for car anti-theft, bank cash transport vehicles, and official vehicle management. With a wide range of functions and accessories, the GPS-405 is a reliable and efficient solution for tracking and monitoring vehicles.

One of the standout features of the GPS-405 is its comprehensive set of functions. It offers movement tracking, geo-fencing, overspeed alerts, door monitoring, ACC detection, shock detection, and external power cut-off. In case of emergencies, the tracker also includes an SOS button for immediate assistance. Additionally, the GPS-405 supports remote oil and power cut-off, allowing for remote control of the vehicle's engine. For added convenience, certain models of the GPS-405 also feature a Wi-Fi hotspot, oil detection, temperature and humidity detection, and a Wi-Fi camera.

The GPS-405 operates on various network frequency bands, including 4G, 3G, and 2G, ensuring reliable and uninterrupted tracking capabilities. With a tracking accuracy of -162dBm and a precision of 5 meters, you can trust that the GPS-405 will provide accurate and up-to-date location information. Furthermore, the tracker is IP66 waterproof rated, making it suitable for use in various weather conditions. It is also certified by CE and FCC, guaranteeing its quality and compliance with industry standards.
