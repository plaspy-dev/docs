# Coban - BN-407

![BN-407](./tracker.png)

The BN-407 is a compact 4G bicycle management terminal designed for concealed installation inside a bike frame. Purpose built for discreet anti theft protection and fleet use, it provides live location updates, motion telemetry, configurable geo fence alerts and track playback without altering a bicycle’s appearance. Its small cylindrical form factor and low weight make it suitable for mountain bikes, shared fleets and personal bicycles where tamper resistance and unobtrusive mounting matter.

As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the BN-407 delivers the core location and event data Plaspy needs for real time monitoring and historical review. When used with Plaspy, the device’s tracking reports, motion alarms and geo fence notifications become part of a unified fleet or asset management workflow, helping operators and individual users keep visibility on rides, triggers and battery status while maintaining a discreet installation.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for bicycles with a concealed frame embedded form factor that minimizes visual impact and tampering risk.
- Real time tracking and track playback for ride histories and route review.
- Motion and safety alarms including shock movement overspeed low battery and geo fence notifications to support anti theft and safety workflows.
- Designed for low power operation with smart positioning strategies to extend runtime between charges.
- Rechargeable battery and USB charging for practical field use and easy maintenance.
- Multi mode transport support and network compatibility to work across regional deployments and integrate with tracking platforms like Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The BN-407 sends position and event data to Plaspy where it is ingested for live monitoring rule based alerts and historical playback. Plaspy translates the device telemetry into maps reports and notifications so fleet managers and individual users can monitor activity remotely and respond to incidents quickly.

- Real time location updates and telemetry appear in Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Alarm events such as shock movement overspeed geo fence and low battery map to Plaspy alerts and automated workflows.
- Track playback in Plaspy uses the device history for incident review route analysis and utilization reporting.
- Smart offline behavior conserves battery while stationary and ensures timely reporting on alarms or movement for dependable oversight.
- Device state changes such as inductive key arming and disarming are reported and can be reflected in Plaspy status indicators.

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet anti theft protection and recovery for privately owned bicycles and high value bikes.
- Shared bike and rental fleet management with utilization tracking route history and alerting.
- Supervision of children's rides providing parents or guardians with location visibility and motion alerts.
- Ride history and incident review for performance analysis or event reconciliation.
- Small asset tracking where concealed installation and long runtime are priorities.
