# BN-408A GPS Tracker \(Model 408\)

The BN-408A is a portable GPS tracker designed for reliable asset management and anti-theft protection, fully Plaspy compatible. Its strong magnetic adsorption and high-capacity 3.7V 10,000 mAh Li-ion battery make it ideal for long-term, maintenance-light monitoring of vehicles, trailers, containers, and other movable assets without permanent installation.

Built for real-time tracking and remote oversight, the BN-408A supports 2G/3G/4G networks and communicates over TCP, UDP, or SMS so Plaspy can receive live location, alarms, and telemetry. A built-in infrared tamper sensor, multiple alarm types and configurable power strategies combine to deliver dependable anti-theft capability and flexible fleet management integration.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with real-time tracking and platform-ready transport protocols \(TCP/UDP/SMS\).
- Strong magnetic adsorption for tool-free mounting on vehicle chassis and portable assets — ideal when no fixed installation is desired.
- Long-life 3.7V 10,000 mAh rechargeable Li-ion battery provides extended standby for long-term monitoring.
- Infrared tamper sensor plus movement, overspeed, shock and low-battery alarms for robust anti-theft protection.
- Multiple positioning strategies — Real-time Online, Smart Mode \(sleep while stationary\), and Power Saving Mode — to balance reporting frequency and battery life.
- Real-time location, track playback and geo-fence alerts for fleet management, route audit and recovery workflows.
- Remote voice monitoring via the mobile app or platform for on-site verification and situational awareness.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The BN-408A sends GPS location and status data to Plaspy over TCP, UDP or SMS, enabling immediate integration into Plaspy dashboards and alerts. Plaspy-compatible reporting allows operators to view live position, replay historical tracks, and act on alarms such as tamper events or low battery notifications. Remote configuration via SMS setup commands enables quick field updates when server-side configuration is not available.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(position, signal quality, battery level\).
- Infrared tamper status and shock/movement alarms for anti-theft detection.
- Geo-fence alerts and track playback for route validation and fleet management audits.
- Power-saving and Smart Mode reporting for extended battery life during long deployments.
- Remote voice monitoring for situational checks and verification of onboard events.
- TCP/UDP/SMS transport so Plaspy can ingest reports across common telematics channels.
