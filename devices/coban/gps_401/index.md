# Coban - GPS-401

![GPS-401](./logo..png)

The Coban GPS-401 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for motorcycles and small cars. It operates on both 2G \(850/900/1800/1900Mhz\) and 4G \(B1/B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8/B28/B40\) GSM/GPRS networks, allowing for reliable and accurate tracking and monitoring of remote targets. Whether you want to keep an eye on your motorcycle or small car, this tracker has you covered.

One of the standout features of the GPS-401 is its waterproof design with an IP67 rating. This means that it can withstand exposure to water and dust, making it ideal for outdoor use. Additionally, it has a wide voltage range of DC \(12V/24V\), ensuring compatibility with various vehicles.

With GPS and LBS real-time tracking, you can easily locate and monitor your vehicle's whereabouts. The tracker supports various functions such as sleep tracking strategy, forwarding incoming SMS messages, SIM card balance enquiry, ACC ON/OFF alert, geo fence, cut off engine & resume engine, low battery alarm, external power disconnection alarm, blind area alarm, movement alarm, overspeed alarm, arming/disarm by SMS, ACC alarm, shock alarm, check status, check IMEI, local time zone setting, reset hardware, TCP/UDP switch, configure SMS monitoring center number, configure GPRS heartbeat package interval, and initialization.

Overall, the Coban GPS-401 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers comprehensive tracking and monitoring capabilities for motorcycles and small cars. Its waterproof design, wide voltage range, and extensive range of functions make it a top choice for vehicle owners looking for peace of mind and enhanced security.
