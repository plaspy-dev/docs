# Coban - GPS-401

<div className="device-logo">
![GPS-401](./logo..png)
</div>

The Coban GPS-401 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed primarily for motorcycles and small cars. It supports both 2G and 4G GSM networks for position reporting and offers GPS and LBS real time tracking. The device is built with an IP67 waterproof rating and a wide DC voltage range, making it suitable for outdoor use and a variety of vehicle power systems.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GPS-401 can feed location updates, status reports, and alarm events into the Plaspy fleet management platform. That integration enables centralized visibility and monitoring of tracked motorcycles and small cars, so operators can use Plaspy to view live location, configure alerts, and generate reports based on the tracker’s incoming data.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for motorcycles and small cars with a compact form factor suitable for constrained installations
- Operates on 2G and 4G GSM networks to provide broad coverage for location reporting
- IP67 waterproof rating for reliable outdoor and exposed use
- Wide DC voltage support for compatibility with common vehicle electrical systems
- Real time GPS and LBS tracking for continuous location visibility
- Built in alert features such as geofence, overspeed, movement, low battery, and external power disconnect
- Supports remote control and status functions including engine cut off and resume engine as reported by the device

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GPS-401 sends position and event data that Plaspy ingests, normalizes, and displays through its monitoring and reporting tools. Plaspy uses the device reports to provide operational visibility and centralized management for fleets or individual vehicles.

- Live location tracking and historical route playback in Plaspy maps and timelines
- Alert and event handling for geofence breaches, overspeed, movement, low battery, and power disconnects
- Fleet dashboards and group views to monitor multiple GPS-401 devices at once
- Event-driven notifications and escalations configurable within Plaspy for operational oversight
- Aggregated reporting for utilization, trips, and alarm history to support fleet decision making

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle fleet tracking for security and route oversight
- Small car monitoring for rental, delivery, or corporate pool vehicles
- Personal vehicle protection for outdoor or exposed use thanks to waterproofing
- Last mile or light commercial fleet visibility for small vehicle operators
- Remote monitoring of vehicles where a compact, weatherproof tracker is required

Its network support and real time tracking capabilities allow Plaspy to present timely location and alarm information to operators, while the device’s alert set covers common operational risks for small vehicles.
