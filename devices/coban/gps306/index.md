# Coban - GPS306

![GPS306](./logo.jpg)

The Coban GPS306 is a compact GPS tracker designed to locate and monitor people, vehicles, and other assets using GPS satellites together with the GSM GPRS mobile network. It supports multiple frequency bands for broad regional coverage and provides real time location updates through SMS or internet reporting. Common features highlighted for this model include geo fencing, over speed alerts, movement detection, and notifications suited to both personal and commercial tracking needs.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GPS306 can feed location and alert data into a centralized fleet management platform for live oversight and historical reporting. Plaspy can receive the GPS306 location updates and translate them into map views, configurable alerts, and operational reports, making the tracker a practical choice for organizations that want to combine Coban hardware with Plaspy monitoring and analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location updates available via SMS or internet reporting for immediate visibility.
- Multi band support for broad geographic coverage across common GSM frequencies.
- Built in geo fencing and over speed alert features to support zone and behavior monitoring.
- Movement and low battery alarms help maintain awareness of asset status.
- Compact design that is suited for discreet tracking of vehicles and portable assets.
- Practical for both personal monitoring and small to medium fleet applications.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GPS306 delivers periodic location and event messages that Plaspy can ingest to provide consolidated tracking, alerting, and reporting across fleets and assets. Once configured to send updates, the device's position and alarm events appear in Plaspy for live monitoring and operational oversight.

- Live map display of GPS306 devices within Plaspy for real time location awareness.
- Configurable alerts in Plaspy based on geo fence entry or exit, speeding events, or movement triggers.
- Historical location trails and basic reporting to analyze routes and usage over time.
- Centralized device management for grouping trackers and assigning them to vehicles or users.
- Integration with Plaspy notification channels to deliver alerts to managers and dispatch.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for small delivery or service vehicle operations.
- Personal tracking for lone workers or family safety monitoring.
- Asset protection for portable equipment or high value items in transit.
- Route monitoring and basic compliance oversight for operational scheduling.
- Temporary tracking deployments where compact, easy to deploy devices are preferred.
