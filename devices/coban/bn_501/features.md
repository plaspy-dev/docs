# Coban - BN-501 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Coban BN-501 wearable GPS tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users including positioning methods, alert types, configuration options, and the practical value these features provide when integrated into the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. For device specific limits, regional band support, or the latest firmware details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from Coban.

## Feature Overview

The BN-501 is a compact jewelry style wearable designed for discreet personal safety and concealed asset monitoring. When used with Plaspy, the tracker provides continuous or periodic location reporting, alarm signaling, and remote configuration options that help teams and caregivers monitor assets and people without visible tracking hardware.

- Multi network cellular connectivity including GSM GPRS, NB‑IoT and LTE Cat M for broad coverage and low power operation.
- GPS positioning complemented by WiFi assisted fixes and BLE 5.0 to improve indoor aid and proximity scenarios.
- On device alarms including SOS help alarm plus movement, shock, low battery and geo fence alerting for immediate incident reporting.
- Configurable power profiles such as Real time, Smart and Power saving to balance update frequency and battery life.
- Compact, strap or jewelry form factor for comfortable wear and high concealment suited to personal and pet tracking.
- Local configuration via Bluetooth and SMS with TCP/UDP reporting options for integration into Plaspy.

## Core Features of Coban - BN-501

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward integration with real time tracking and alert dashboards.
- GPS positioning with WiFi assisted improvements for better fixes in challenging environments.
- BLE 5.0 for device configuration and proximity based interactions during setup and deployment.
- Multi network cellular support including GSM GPRS, NB‑IoT and LTE Cat M for flexible connectivity and power options.
- SOS help alarm for immediate alerting of emergency situations to monitoring systems.
- Movement and shock alerts to detect unusual motion or impacts and trigger events in Plaspy.
- Configurable power modes to prioritize real time reporting or extended battery life depending on the use case.
- SMS and Bluetooth configuration options to manage settings in field without requiring physical access to a computer.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the BN-501 is connected to Plaspy, its location fixes, alarms and telemetry are ingested and displayed through Plaspy dashboards and alerting workflows. Plaspy translates device reports into map updates, event records, and notifications so operators can act on incidents quickly while preserving privacy and discretion where required.

- Real time and periodic location updates appear on Plaspy maps to support live monitoring and historical playback.
- SOS help alarm events are forwarded to Plaspy so operators can escalate or dispatch based on established procedures.
- Movement, shock and geo fence alerts are recorded as events in Plaspy for anti theft and personnel safety workflows.
- Battery level and low battery alerts feed into Plaspy telemetry for proactive device maintenance and replacement planning.
- BLE configuration support enables initial setup and proximity assisted features that complement Plaspy managed deployments.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and caregiver monitoring for elderly or vulnerable people using a discreet wearable alarm and location device.
- Child supervision with a jewelry style tracker that provides guardians with location updates and geo fence alerts.
- Pet tracking for concealed monitoring of small animals where comfort and form factor are critical.
- Unobtrusive personnel safety for staff who need discreet protection while working in public or remote locations.
- Concealed asset monitoring in luggage or personal items where small form factor and motion alerts help detect tampering.
- Short term hire or rental item tracking where compact size and simple configuration reduce deployment time.
