# Coban - BN-303G Features

This page provides a public overview of the Coban BN-303G feature set and explains how those capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet tracking, anti theft monitoring, and general vehicle telemetry. The content focuses on practical, non sensitive feature information relevant to operators and integrators who plan to use the BN-303G with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and specific manufacturer implementation. For deployment planning, verify device capabilities for your unit and installation, since alarms, optional accessories, and remote command behavior may differ between revisions and regional variants.

## Feature Overview

The BN-303G is a compact, vehicle mounted GPS tracker designed to deliver reliable location reporting and a broad alarm set for anti theft and fleet workflows. It is built for concealed installation on cars and commercial vehicles and supports multiple transport options for integration with tracking platforms such as Plaspy.

- High sensitivity GNSS positioning with typical position accuracy around 5 meters for dependable location fixes.
- Quad band 2G GSM GPRS connectivity with TCP, UDP and SMS transport options for flexible backend reporting.
- Rechargeable internal backup battery and intelligent power saving smart mode to conserve vehicle battery while parked.
- Wide alarm suite including SOS, overspeed, geo fence, door open, low battery and external power disconnection for fast event detection.
- Remote telecommand support such as remote voice listening, one way call and remote fuel or power cutoff for anti theft response and remote control.

## Core Features of Coban - BN-303G

- GNSS receiver with high sensitivity and typical ~5 m positioning accuracy for accurate mapping and tracking.
- Quad band 2G GSM GPRS communications supporting TCP, UDP and SMS transport for message delivery to backend platforms.
- Intelligent power saving smart mode that reduces power draw while parked and wakes on movement or configured alarms.
- Internal rechargeable backup battery to preserve settings and provide short term operation when external power is lost.
- Comprehensive alarm and alert suite: SOS, overspeed, geo fence breach, door open, low battery and external power disconnect.
- Ignition state and basic vehicle event monitoring to support event driven reporting and workflows.
- Remote telecommands including remote voice listening, one way call and remote fuel or power cutoff for immobilization style control.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the BN-303G reports location and event data so fleet managers and operators can view real time positions, receive alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy automatically detects the device protocol and ingests telemetry and alarm messages to present consistent dashboards and reports.

- Real time location updates and periodic telemetry delivered to Plaspy for mapping and live monitoring.
- Alarm and event notifications such as SOS, geo fence, overspeed and power loss surfaced in Plaspy as alerts for immediate action.
- Ignition and door event visibility used to trigger workflows, group alerts, and contextual reporting inside Plaspy.
- Remote immobilizer style commands and telecommands reflected as events in Plaspy when supported by the device and configured by the installer.
- Historical route and event logs retained in Plaspy for analytics, audits and route review.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft monitoring with real time alerts and remote immobilization options to respond to unauthorized movement.
- Delivery and logistics tracking to monitor vehicle location, route history and overspeed events for operational oversight.
- Rental vehicle oversight including geofence monitoring, ignition event logging and SOS alerts for customer safety and policy compliance.
- Municipal or public fleet telemetry for centralized monitoring of buses, taxis and service vehicles.
- Optional fuel monitoring when an external fuel sensor is installed and supported by the hardware variant.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets such as alarms, remote telecommands, and optional fuel monitoring can vary by firmware version and hardware revision.
- Optional accessories like relays, external microphones, sirens and fuel sensors may be supported on some hardware variants but are not guaranteed on all units.
- Installation wiring and vehicle wiring differences affect which events are available (for example ignition and door inputs depend on how the unit is connected).
- Regional variants and cellular banding may change device behavior and reporting intervals; confirm the unit shipped for your region.
- Always consult the manufacturer documentation for the specific device serial number and firmware to confirm exact capabilities.
