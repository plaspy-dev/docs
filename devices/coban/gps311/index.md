# Coban - GPS311

![GPS311](./Logo.png)

The Coban GPS311 is a versatile GPS tracker designed specifically for vehicles. It combines multiple functions such as accurate positioning, security, monitoring surveillance, emergency alarms, and tracking. With support for SMS, GPRS, and internet data transmission, this tracker can be widely used in various areas of management, including large-scale monitoring, emergency scheduling, location-based services, and traffic safety.

Based on GSM/GPRS wireless telecommunication internet and GPS satellite global positioning system, the GPS311 offers multiple tracking options. You can track in real-time through the monitor software on your PC, track using your PDA cell phone, or even track using Google Earth. The compact design of the GPS311, with dimensions of 60x36x18mm, makes it easy to install and conceal in your vehicle.

The GPS311 comes with a range of features to enhance its functionality. It accepts a wide voltage range from 8V to 40V, allowing it to work with various vehicles such as cars, E-bikes, and motorcycles. It also supports functions like change password, authorization, single locating, auto track continuously, GPS drift suppression, location-based service \(LBS\), and more. Additionally, it has alarm functions such as low battery alarm, external power off alarm, GPS blind spot alert, geo-fence, movement alarm, over speed alarm, ACC working alarm, and shock sensor alarm.

With its comprehensive features and reliable performance, the Coban GPS311 is an excellent choice for vehicle tracking and management. Whether you need to monitor a fleet of vehicles or ensure the security of your personal vehicle, the GPS311 provides the necessary tools and functionalities to meet your needs.
