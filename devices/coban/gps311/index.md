# Coban - GPS311

<div className="device-logo">
![GPS311](./Logo.png)
</div>

The Coban GPS311 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for a range of transportation assets. It combines positioning and monitoring features with a suite of security and alert functions, including emergency alarms, geo fencing, movement and over speed notifications, and other vehicle related alerts. The device supports location transmission via SMS, GPRS, and internet data and can be monitored through PC software, mobile devices, or mapping tools such as Google Earth. Its small footprint and broad vehicle voltage range make it suitable for cars, e bikes, and motorcycles.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GPS311 can feed location and alert data into Plaspy's fleet management environment to provide centralized visibility and reporting. Plaspy can receive the GPS311 location streams and present them on maps, incorporate alarms into operational workflows, and include the unit in historical playback and fleet reports. This compatibility makes the GPS311 a practical option for organizations seeking straightforward vehicle tracking paired with Plaspy monitoring and management tools.

## Key Highlights

- Vehicle focused tracker combining positioning, monitoring, and security features
- Supports SMS, GPRS, and internet data transmission for flexible connectivity

- Compact design that is easy to place and conceal in vehicle installations
- Wide input voltage range that supports a variety of vehicle types
- Suitable for both single vehicle use and large scale monitoring environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GPS311 provides continuous location and alarm data that Plaspy uses to deliver real time visibility and operational oversight. Data from the tracker can be shown on maps, included in alerting rules, and retained for reporting and playback.

- Display live GPS positioning on Plaspy maps for individual units and fleets
- Trigger configurable alerts in Plaspy for events such as geo fence breaches, over speed, and movement
- Store and replay historical tracks for route analysis and incident review
- Aggregate vehicle status across a fleet for operational dashboards and monitoring
- Include GPS311 events and summaries in Plaspy reporting for compliance and performance review
