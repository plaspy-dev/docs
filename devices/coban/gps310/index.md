# Coban - GPS310

![GPS310](./Logo.jpg)

The Coban GPS310 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers accurate location tracking and a range of useful features. It is designed to be used in various applications such as guarding vehicles against theft, protecting children, elderly or disabled individuals, managing personnel, and tracking criminals discreetly. This compact device works based on the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites, allowing you to locate and monitor remote targets through SMS, computer, or PDA.

The GPS310 has a sleek and portable design, with dimensions of 68.3 x 48.3 x 16mm, making it easy to carry and conceal. It operates on a 4-band GSM frequency \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\) and has a GPS sensitivity of -159dBm, ensuring reliable and accurate tracking. The device is powered by a 3.7V 1000mAh battery, providing a long standby time of up to 22 days in sleep mode and up to 9.9 months in schedule mode.

One of the standout features of the Coban GPS310 is its long standby time, which ensures that you can rely on it for extended periods without needing frequent recharging. Additionally, its compact and portable design makes it easy to carry and conceal, allowing for discreet tracking. The GPS310 is also suitable for tracking valuable items such as luggage cases, as it can record the rolling over of the case for compensation in case of any damages. With functions like time tracking, low battery alerts, geo-fencing, SOS alerts, and records rolling over, the Coban GPS310 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker for various tracking and monitoring needs.
