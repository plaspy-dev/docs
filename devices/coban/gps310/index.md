# Coban - GPS310

![GPS310](./Logo.jpg)

The Coban GPS310 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed to provide accurate location tracking for a variety of situations. Built to work with existing GSM GPRS networks and GPS satellites, the GPS310 can report position and status via SMS or to a computer or PDA. The device is small enough to carry or conceal, and the manufacturer lists several practical features such as time tracking, geo fencing, SOS alerts, low battery warnings, and a record rollover function that make it suitable for both personal and asset monitoring.

As a model compatible with Plaspy, the GPS310 can be used to feed location data and alerts into the Plaspy fleet management platform for consolidated monitoring and reporting. Its long standby characteristics and portable form factor make it a sensible option when you need extended operation between charges and discreet placement. Plaspy can take the device data and present it alongside other fleet or asset information to support operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Accurate location tracking using GPS satellites combined with GSM based reporting for remote monitoring.
- Compact portable design at 68.3 x 48.3 x 16 mm for easy carrying and discreet placement.
- Long standby performance that supports extended monitoring cycles without frequent recharging.
- Useful alert functions including geo fencing, SOS alerts, and low battery notifications.
- Time tracking and record rollover features useful for logging and incident verification.
- Suitable for a range of scenarios from personal safety to small asset protection and fleet oversight.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Coban GPS310 can be integrated as a device source into Plaspy to provide location updates and alert events that appear in the same dashboard used for other trackers. Once connected, Plaspy makes GPS310 data visible and actionable for operators and managers.

- Live position display for individual devices on the Plaspy map for real time visibility.
- Historical tracks and event logs stored and accessible in Plaspy for review and reporting.
- Geofence setup and alerting in Plaspy using the device signals for boundary monitoring.
- Alert routing for SOS and low battery events so teams can respond promptly.
- Consolidated reporting and export capabilities to include GPS310 data in operational reports.

## Typical Use Cases

- Guarding vehicles and deterring theft with continuous or periodic location reporting.
- Protecting children, elderly, or disabled individuals with SOS and location alerts.
- Managing personnel or temporary field staff who require portable tracking.
- Discreet tracking for security operations or evidence gathering when a compact device is needed.
- Tracking valuables such as luggage or small equipment where compact size and standby life matter.
