# Coban - BN-403C

<div className="device-logo">
![BN-403C](./tracker.png)
</div>

The BN-403C is a compact vehicle-mounted GPS tracker designed for concealed installation and continuous vehicle management. It provides real-time tracking with LTE 4G and 2G fallback, a wired vehicle power feed plus a small rechargeable backup battery for position reporting during power interruptions, and a set of alarms and remote control features commonly used in fleet and anti-theft applications.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the BN-403C can stream location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Integration with Plaspy enables live maps, notifications, history playback and remote commands, making the model suitable for operators who need reliable telemetry, ignition monitoring, and remote immobilizer capabilities within a single fleet management platform.

## Key Highlights

- Out of the box compatibility with Plaspy for straightforward integration into fleet dashboards and workflows.  
- LTE 4G connectivity with 2G fallback for improved cellular coverage and continuous reporting.  
- Compact form factor intended for concealed vehicle mounting and discreet deployment.  
- Built in backup battery to maintain position reporting during vehicle power loss.  
- Suite of alarms including ignition, door open, shock, overspeed, geo-fence and SOS for event driven monitoring.  
- Remote immobilizer and relay based power cut options to support anti theft responses from a central platform.  
- Bluetooth configuration and automatic induction arming for simplified local setup and proximity based state changes.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the BN-403C delivers GNSS positions and alarm events to Plaspy using supported transport methods. Plaspy ingests those updates to present live location, trigger alerts, and retain history for playback and reporting. Device parameters can be managed locally via Bluetooth or controlled remotely through Plaspy’s device management features where supported.

- Real time location updates feed Plaspy live maps and fleet overview dashboards.  
- Alarm events such as ACC ignition, door open and shock are reported to Plaspy to trigger alerts and automated workflows.  
- History playback and report queries in Plaspy let operators analyze trips, stops and event sequences.  
- Remote commands such as immobilizer or relay control can be issued from Plaspy to respond to theft or unauthorized movement.  
- Bluetooth based arming and support for Bluetooth sensors help automate state changes that are reflected in Plaspy monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars and light commercial vehicles requiring continuous tracking and event monitoring.  
- Private car anti theft protection with door and ignition alerts plus remote immobilizer control.  
- Rental and finance monitoring to track vehicle location, movement and geo fence compliance.  
- Logistics and transportation operations that need consistent position visibility and event driven notifications.  
- Insurance and risk control scenarios where history playback and tamper alerts support investigations.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The BN-403C pairs practical hardware features with Plaspy’s centralized visibility to deliver a balanced solution for vehicle monitoring and anti theft control. Its cellular resilience, backup reporting capability, and broad alarm set make it a useful option for operators who want dependable tracking combined with remote command capabilities through a single platform.
