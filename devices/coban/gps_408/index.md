# Coban - GPS-408

![GPS-408](./logo..png)

The Coban GPS-408 is a versatile vehicle tracker that is designed to provide real-time tracking and management of vehicles. It is compatible with the BAANOOL IOT platform and APP platform, making it ideal for use in various industries such as logistics and freight, car anti-theft, bank cash transport vehicles, and official vehicle management. With its advanced features and reliable performance, the GPS-408 is a reliable solution for businesses looking to enhance their vehicle tracking capabilities.

One of the standout features of the GPS-408 is its compatibility with 2G, 3G, and 4G communications networks. This ensures that the tracker can be used in a wide range of locations, providing reliable and accurate tracking data. Additionally, the GPS-408 is equipped with a large-capacity battery with 10,000 mAh, offering a long standby time for extended use. The installation method is also convenient, as it features strong magnetic adsorption, eliminating the need for complex installation processes.

The GPS-408 is designed with user convenience in mind. It features a built-in GSM and GPS antenna design, ensuring strong concealment and reliable signal reception. The tracker is also equipped with an infrared sensor anti-demolition feature, providing added security and protection against tampering. With its IP67 waterproof rating, the GPS-408 is capable of withstanding various weather conditions, making it suitable for use in outdoor environments.

With a wide range of functions and features, the GPS-408 offers comprehensive tracking capabilities. It supports GPS real-time positioning, allowing users to track the location of their vehicles in real-time. The tracker also features a remote voice monitoring function, enabling users to listen in on the surroundings of the vehicle. Other functions include remote engine cut off and resume \(Model B\), movement alarm, geo-fence, overspeed alarm, low battery alarm, tamper alarm, and SOS \(Model B\) functionality. With its advanced features and reliable performance, the GPS-408 is an excellent choice for businesses in need of a reliable and efficient vehicle tracking solution.

### Key Features:

- Compatible with 2G, 3G, and 4G communications networks
- Large-capacity battery with 10,000 mAh for long standby time
- Strong magnetic adsorption for easy installation
- Built-in GSM and GPS antenna design for strong concealment
- Infrared sensor anti-demolition for added security
- IP67 waterproof rating for durability in outdoor environments
