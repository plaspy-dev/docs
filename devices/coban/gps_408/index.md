# Coban - GPS-408

![GPS-408](./logo..png)

The Coban GPS-408 is a versatile vehicle tracker designed to provide real time tracking and management for vehicles across a range of industries. Built for applications such as logistics and freight, vehicle anti theft, bank cash transport vehicles, and official vehicle management, the GPS-408 combines location reporting with security oriented features to help organizations maintain visibility and control over mobile assets.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GPS-408 can feed position and event information into Plaspy’s fleet management environment. Its long standby capability, robust enclosure, and anti tamper features make it a practical choice for operators who want continuous tracking and operational alerts surfaced inside Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Real time GPS positioning suitable for continuous vehicle monitoring
- Compatibility with major cellular network generations for wide area coverage
- Large capacity internal battery for extended standby and reduced maintenance
- Strong magnetic mounting option for convenient placement and concealment
- Infrared anti demolition feature to detect tampering attempts
- IP67 rated enclosure for reliable outdoor use and weather resistance
- Remote functions such as voice monitoring and remote engine cut off and resume (Model B)

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GPS-408 supplies location and status updates that Plaspy presents on maps, timelines, and reports so fleet managers can track assets and review events. Plaspy can aggregate the device’s events and alarms to create actionable alerts and operational insights.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for real time fleet visibility
- Event and alarm forwarding such as movement alerts, geo fence violations, overspeed, tamper, and low battery
- Historical route playback and reporting for trip review and compliance
- Notification and alerting workflows to notify operators by email or SMS when configured
- Use of remote monitor and control related events in Plaspy dashboards where supported

## Typical Use Cases

- Long haul and local logistics monitoring to improve route visibility and delivery tracking
- Vehicle anti theft and recovery operations using tamper and movement alerts
- Secure transport of valuables such as bank cash in armored vehicles
- Government and corporate fleet oversight for official vehicle management
- Temporary tracking of assets requiring discreet mounting and extended standby

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPS-408 is a practical option for organizations that need a balance of long standby performance, discreet installation, and durable construction. Its combination of location reporting, anti tamper detection, and outdoor rating helps maintain reliable tracking in demanding environments. When paired with Plaspy, these device capabilities become accessible through a centralized fleet management platform that supports visibility, alerts, and reporting.
