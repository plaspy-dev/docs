# Coban - GPS-201

![GPS-201](./logo..png)

The Coban GPS-201 is a pet GPS tracker designed to provide comprehensive solutions for pet positioning, tracking, training, and socialization. With its hardware and integrated APP pet management system, this tracker aims to address various challenges faced by pet owners, such as neighbor disturbance, human-pet interaction, and pet socialization. 

One of the standout features of the GPS-201 is its tracking and positioning capabilities. It utilizes a 2G network \(850/900/1800/1900MHZ\) and NB \(B1/B3/B5/B8/B20\) to ensure accurate and real-time tracking of your pet's location. With a positioning accuracy of -162dBm and a range of 5 meters, you can easily keep tabs on your furry friend's whereabouts.

In addition to tracking, the GPS-201 also offers pet training and punishment functionalities. This allows you to remotely train and discipline your pet, ensuring their safety and well-being. Moreover, the tracker includes a calling home feature, enabling you to communicate with your pet even when you're not physically present.

The GPS-201 is equipped with a 450mAh lithium battery, providing an impressive 350 hours of real-time online standby time. This means you can effortlessly monitor your pet's location and status, as well as interact with them, without worrying about battery life. Furthermore, the tracker boasts an IP67 waterproof rating, ensuring its durability and reliability in various weather conditions.

With certifications including CCC, CE, and FCC, you can trust in the quality and performance of the Coban GPS-201. Whether you're concerned about your pet's safety, want to train them effectively, or simply wish to enhance their socialization, this pet GPS tracker is an excellent choice.

### Key Features:

- Tracking and positioning functionality for accurate and real-time location monitoring
- Pet training and punishment capabilities for effective discipline
- Calling home feature to communicate with your pet remotely
- Pet socialization support for enhanced interaction with other pets
- Pet identity information query for easy access to important details
- Low power consumption with a 450mAh lithium battery and 350 hours of standby time
- IP67 waterproof rating for durability and reliability in various weather conditions

### Technical Specifications:

- Network: 2G \(850/900/1800/1900MHZ\) + NB \(B1/B3/B5/B8/B20\)
- GPS positioning accuracy: -162dBm, 5 meters
- Waterproof grade: IP67
- Certifications: CCC, CE, FCC
