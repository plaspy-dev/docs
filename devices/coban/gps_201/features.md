# Coban - GPS-201 Features

This page provides a public features overview for the Coban GPS-201 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device functions that are suitable for pet positioning, tracking, training, calling, and socialization and explains how those functions map to operational visibility when the device is connected to Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on the device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, regional variants, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device specific details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GPS-201 is a pet focused GPS tracker intended to support real time location monitoring, remote interaction, and basic pet management functions. Its design emphasizes outdoor durability, extended standby operation, and integration into a pet management workflow.

- Real time position reporting suitable for tracking a pet on the move
- Dual network support including 2G and NB bands for broad connectivity in varied regions
- Positioning accuracy reported as approximately five meters for typical fixes
- Remote pet training and punishment functions to assist behavior management
- Calling home feature for remote voice interaction with the pet
- IP67 waterproofing and extended standby battery life for outdoor use

## Core Features of Coban - GPS-201

- Real time tracking and positioning for pet location awareness
- Support for 2G and NB network bands to maintain connectivity in areas with cellular coverage
- Positioning accuracy suitable for close proximity location detection
- Remote training and punishment controls for behavioral management
- Calling home capability to enable remote voice contact with the pet
- Pet socialization support and pet identity query features for managing pet information
- IP67 water resistant design for reliable outdoor operation
- Long standby operation enabled by the built in battery

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present the GPS-201 device data to provide centralized visibility and historical context. When a GPS-201 is connected and configured for use with Plaspy, its positioning and status information become available for mapping and operational monitoring.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness
- Location history and timeline playback to review recent movements and routes
- Device connectivity and online status visible in Plaspy so you can confirm the tracker is reporting
- Event markers or activity notes to indicate interactions such as calling or training events when those are reported
- Centralized device listing and identification to manage multiple pet trackers from one interface

## Typical Use Cases

- Locating a lost or wandering pet with real time position updates
- Conducting remote training sessions and reviewing interaction history
- Monitoring outdoor pets for safety during walks or free time
- Using pet identity information to manage multiple animals and profiles
- Supporting owner pet socialization activities with location context
- Keeping the tracker in service in wet or inclement weather thanks to its water resistant rating

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets can vary by firmware version and manufacturer firmware updates may add or change behavior
- Network support and performance depend on regional cellular coverage and the specific 2G or NB bands deployed in your area
- Hardware revisions and regional variants of the GPS-201 may include different feature mixes or certifications
- Installation method and collar or mounting choices affect real world positioning performance and range
- Verify specific capabilities such as exact battery runtime, positioning accuracy, and training controls with the manufacturer documentation

Using the Coban GPS-201 with Plaspy provides a straightforward way to centralize pet tracking and management tasks. Plaspy presents device locations on maps, preserves recent movement history, and shows device connectivity so owners and caretakers gain clearer situational awareness without needing to switch between vendor applications.
