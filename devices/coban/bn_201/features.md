# Coban - BN-201 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Coban BN-201 pet tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the BN-201’s practical functions such as real time location reporting, remote voice monitoring, and geofence alerts as they relate to monitoring pets and small companion animals through the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional network support, and manufacturer implementation. For deployment planning and the most current device details consult the manufacturer documentation and the device shipping label for firmware and hardware specifics.

## Feature Overview

The BN-201 is a compact pet terminal built for everyday wear that combines position reporting, low power network options, and remote voice functions to give owners and service providers situational awareness. In Plaspy the tracker can provide live location, event alerts, and a history of movements framed by device status and user configured boundaries.

- Real time GNSS location reporting for live tracking and history playback in Plaspy
- Remote voice broadcast and listening for on the ground monitoring or emergency audio cues
- Geofence support for configurable safe areas with departure alerts
- Dual network operation using 2G and NB IoT to balance coverage and low power telemetry
- Reporting via TCP UDP and SMS to integrate with Plaspy and fallback reporting paths

## Core Features of Coban - BN-201

- High sensitivity GNSS positioning with typical accuracy around 5 meters as reported by the manufacturer
- Dual network connectivity supporting 2G GSM GPRS and NB IoT for extended standby and low data uplinks
- Remote voice broadcast and listening capability for situational awareness and owner communication
- Geo fence electronic fence support with device side alerts suitable for pet containment monitoring
- Standard reporting channels using TCP UDP and SMS to transmit position and status to backend servers
- Lightweight pet friendly form factor at approximately 52 g designed for collar attachment and daily wear
- SMS based configuration and documented setup procedures for quick field provisioning

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the BN-201 is connected to Plaspy, the device’s position and event messages are decoded and presented on maps timelines and alert channels so owners and operators can monitor pets in real time and review historical movement. Plaspy is designed to detect common tracker protocols and present device events in a consistent dashboard view.

- Live location updates and map tracking based on GNSS telemetry reported by the device
- History playback and trip review using stored position records gathered from the tracker
- Geofence event notifications sent to users when the device crosses configured boundaries
- Integration of remote voice functions through supported device commands surfaced in Plaspy workflows
- Flexible reporting visibility whether the device uses IP based reporting or SMS fallback for status and configuration

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time pet location for owners during walks outings or while pets are unsupervised
- Geofence based safe area alerts to notify caretakers when a pet leaves a designated zone
- Remote voice messages for behavioral cues training or to help locate a lost animal
- Emergency listening to assess on site situations when safety concerns arise
- Lightweight tracking of small assets where compact size and long standby are beneficial

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware versions may enable or change specific behaviors such as reporting intervals voice command sets and power management
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect supported cellular bands and NB IoT availability in some markets
- Installation method and antenna placement on a collar or harness can influence GNSS performance and voice monitoring quality
- SMS configuration and carrier SMS handling may vary by operator and affect remote setup or fallback reporting
- Always consult the device user manual and release notes for the exact command set and supported features for your unit
