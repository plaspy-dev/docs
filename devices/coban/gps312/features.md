# Coban - GPS312 Features

This page provides a public overview of the Coban GPS312 features and how those capabilities are used with Plaspy for vehicle tracking and monitoring. It describes the main, non sensitive functions you can expect when integrating the GPS312 with Plaspy and how those functions support operational visibility and security.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. The information on this page reflects the device description and general usage patterns; verify device-specific details and the latest firmware capabilities through the manufacturer documentation when needed.

## Feature Overview

The GPS312 is a compact vehicle tracker designed to provide location, monitoring, and alarm functions over cellular networks and GPS satellites. It supports multiple communication methods for reporting and can be used for real-time tracking, location based services, and emergency notifications.

- Accurate GPS positioning with published sensitivity and nominal accuracy suitable for vehicle tracking.
- Multi mode communication using cellular data and SMS for flexible reporting in different network conditions.
- Real time tracking and visibility through PC or mobile devices and compatibility with third party map viewers.
- Built in alarm and alert features such as SOS alarm and tamper alert for security monitoring.
- Two way audio and voice reporting capabilities to support remote monitoring and in vehicle communication.
- Compact form factor and internal battery for discreet installation and temporary operation without external power.

## Core Features of Coban - GPS312

- GPS positioning with stated accuracy around 5 meters and sensitivity suitable for vehicle use.
- Support for cellular connectivity across GSM GPRS 3G and 4G networks for SMS and internet data transmission.
- Real time tracking and location reporting for continuous visibility of vehicle position.
- Geo fencing capability to define virtual boundaries and receive alerts when they are crossed.
- SOS alarm and emergency notification functions for rapid incident signaling.
- Two way audio communication and voice dialing or voice reporting for remote listening or alerts.
- Tamper detection using a light sensor and fall alert to help detect unusual conditions.
- Integrated rechargeable Li ion battery with standby capability for short term power without external supply.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present the information the GPS312 reports, making it possible to monitor location, events, and alerts from a central platform. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and can be configured to accept data from devices reporting to Plaspy servers.

- Location updates from the device appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and historical playback.
- Alarm events such as SOS, tamper, or fall alerts can be captured and surfaced as notifications for operators.
- Geo fence entries and exits reported by the device can be translated into events and triggers inside Plaspy.
- Two way audio and voice reporting status can be shown as device capabilities or event logs when supported by the device and platform workflow.
- Devices compatible with Plaspy can be pointed to the Plaspy server domain to begin reporting; Plaspy also auto detects tracker protocol to simplify setup.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet location monitoring and route oversight for vehicle fleets of various sizes.
- Emergency response and rapid incident notification using SOS and alarm functions.
- Security monitoring including tamper detection and location verification after suspected theft.
- Location based services and schedule verification for delivery and field operations.
- Driver assistance and remote communication using two way audio and voice reporting.
- Traffic safety management and incident awareness through fall alert and alarm visibility.
