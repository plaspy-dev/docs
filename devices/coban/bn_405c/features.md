# Coban - BN-405C Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Coban BN-405C tracker with Plaspy. It describes practical capabilities, common operational uses, and how the device integrates with Plaspy for live location, alerts, and historical playback. The intent is to provide clear feature-level guidance for fleet managers, installers, and technical evaluators considering this model for vehicle tracking projects.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installed option set, and how the device is fitted in the vehicle. Manufacturer defaults, optional accessories, and regional cellular variants may affect which functions are present. For device specific configuration and the latest technical details consult the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The BN-405C is a compact vehicle tracker designed for fleet, logistics, and anti-theft use. It combines multi network cellular connectivity with a high sensitivity GNSS receiver, a backup battery, and a broad alarm suite to keep vehicles visible and provide event driven alerts for operational oversight.

- Real time location reporting for live map tracking and route visibility.
- Multi network cellular connectivity with support for standard platform protocols for straightforward integration with Plaspy.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver for accurate position and trajectory playback.
- Comprehensive alarm functions including geo fence, overspeed, movement and external power cut alerts to support theft detection and safety monitoring.
- Vehicle inputs and control features such as ACC ignition reporting and remote fuel or power cut control for intervention workflows.
- Optional sensor and camera support to extend telemetry and incident verification when equipped.

## Core Features of Coban - BN-405C

- Real time GPS tracking with high sensitivity GNSS for accurate position updates and trip history.
- Multi network cellular support and standard protocols TCP UDP and SMS for platform integration.
- Backup internal battery to maintain reporting when external power is removed.
- Comprehensive alarm suite covering geo fence overspeed movement shock door open low battery and external power cut alerts.
- ACC ignition input for engine on off reporting and event correlation.
- Remote fuel or power cut control to support anti theft interventions when configured.
- Optional support for external sensors such as fuel level and temperature humidity to expand telemetry.
- Integration capability for external microphones and Wi Fi cameras to assist incident verification where supported.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a BN-405C is connected to Plaspy the device reports location, status updates, and configured alarms so fleet operators can monitor vehicles in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy receives and normalizes incoming messages to present them in maps dashboards and alert feeds for operational decision making.

- Live location and position updates are displayed on Plaspy maps for tracking and dispatch.
- Alarm events such as geo fence breaches overspeed and power loss are presented as notifications and searchable events.
- Ignition and input events from ACC are shown in timelines to correlate trips and runtime with incidents.
- Optional sensor readings and camera or audio evidence are attached to events when those accessories are configured and reporting into Plaspy.
- Trip history and playback use the device location stream and stored messages to reconstruct routes for reporting and compliance.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch where live location and ignition events optimize routing and utilization.
- Logistics and transport monitoring to verify route adherence and provide cargo protection with geofencing.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using movement alarms external power cut detection and remote cut control.
- Public service and utility vehicle oversight that benefits from concealed installation and robust telemetry.
- Insurance and risk control scenarios that require event evidence and historical playback.
- Situational verification when optional cameras or audio features are installed and integrated.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence depends on firmware version and hardware revision; some functions may require newer firmware or specific regional models.
- Optional sensors and accessories such as fuel level probes cameras or external microphones require separate purchase and correct installation to report into Plaspy.
- Installation type and wiring determine whether inputs like ACC and remote cut control are available and safe to use.
- Manufacturer configuration via SMS or remote commands can enable or disable specific alarms and reporting behaviors.
- Always check the manufacturer documentation for supported options and installation guidance for your region and vehicle type.
