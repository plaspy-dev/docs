# Coban - BN-401B

![BN-401B](./tracker.png)

The BN-401B is a compact 4G GPS tracker and motorcycle management terminal engineered for straightforward anti-theft protection and real-time tracking. Designed for small vehicles and private cars, the BN-401B delivers reliable GPS positioning, configurable alarm logic, and essential telemetry so fleet managers and owners can monitor assets from Plaspy with minimal installation effort. Its 4G connectivity and lightweight form factor make it an ideal Plaspy compatible device for applications that prioritize fast position updates, simple immobilization and on-demand status checks.

Built to work over TCP/UDP/SMS and with Bluetooth configuration for local setup, the BN-401B supports standard Plaspy workflows such as live location, track playback, Geo-fence alerts and emergency SOS. The unit’s backup battery, smart sleep strategy and wide operating voltage \(12–24V\) provide dependable anti-theft performance and persistent telemetry in real-world vehicle environments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real-time tracking and position updates over TCP/UDP/SMS.
- Compact motorcycle/vehicle form factor \(6.78 × 4.1 × 1.84 cm; ~58 g\) for discreet installation.
- Comprehensive alarm suite: SOS, movement, overspeed, shock, low battery, external power cut and Geo-fence.
- Remote arming/disarming and remote fuel/power cut immobilizer to support anti-theft response.
- Bluetooth configuration for local parameter setup and easy commissioning without a wired interface.
- Smart sleep and configurable reporting intervals to balance battery/back-up power and real-time telemetry needs.
- Backup 3.7V 90 mAh lithium-ion battery for continued reporting during external power loss.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is straightforward: the BN-401B sends location and alarm data to Plaspy’s servers via TCP, UDP or SMS transport. Plaspy consumes those messages to provide real-time tracking, alerts and historical reporting. You can rely on its reported telemetry—GPS position, ACC ignition status and alarm events—to trigger Plaspy workflows such as immobilization commands, Geo-fence notifications and track playback.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy over 4G networks.
- ACC ignition alarm and ACC working notifications to authorized numbers for ignition-based events.
- Low-battery and external power disconnect alarms to notify Plaspy and users of power issues.
- Remote immobilizer via remote fuel/power cut capability for coordinated anti-theft action through Plaspy.
- Bluetooth configuration for local setup; app-based monitoring and track playback through Plaspy-enabled workflows.
