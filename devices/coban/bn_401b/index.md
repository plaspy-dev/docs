# Coban - BN-401B

![BN-401B](./tracker.png)

The BN-401B is a compact 4G GPS tracker and motorcycle management terminal designed for straightforward anti theft protection and real time tracking. Geared toward small vehicles and private cars, it provides reliable GPS positioning, configurable alarm logic and essential telemetry so owners and fleet managers can monitor assets with minimal installation effort. Its lightweight form factor and backup power capability support continuous monitoring in everyday vehicle environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the BN-401B integrates into common fleet workflows to deliver live location, track playback, Geo fence alerts and emergency SOS notifications. The unit supports TCP UDP and SMS transport for server reporting and offers Bluetooth configuration for local setup, making it well suited for operators who want fast position updates, simple immobilization options and on demand status checks through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- 4G connectivity with TCP UDP and SMS reporting for timely position updates and alerts.
- Compact motorcycle and small vehicle form factor for discreet deployment.
- Comprehensive alarm suite including SOS movement overspeed shock low battery external power cut and Geo fence alerts.
- Remote immobilization support via fuel or power cut to assist anti theft response.
- Bluetooth configuration for local parameter setup and easy commissioning.
- Backup battery and smart sleep strategy for continued reporting during external power loss.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is straightforward: the BN-401B reports location and alarm events to Plaspy servers using its supported transport methods, and Plaspy processes those messages to provide real time tracking, alerting and historical reporting. Local Bluetooth configuration can be used for initial setup while Plaspy provides remote monitoring and operational oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry updates displayed in Plaspy for live fleet visibility.
- Geo fence and SOS alerts forwarded to Plaspy so teams receive immediate notifications.
- Track playback and historical position data available in Plaspy for incident review and reporting.
- Power loss and low battery alarms routed to Plaspy to highlight potential device issues.
- Remote immobilization commands coordinated through Plaspy workflows to support anti theft actions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and rapid immobilization for motorcycle fleets and light vehicles.
- Personal motorcycle or private car security with SOS and movement or shock alerts.
- Urban delivery and courier operations requiring frequent position updates and overspeed alerts.
- Rental vehicle monitoring to track ignition status and reduce loss or misuse.
- Telemetry and event logging for maintenance planning and incident investigation using Plaspy reporting.
