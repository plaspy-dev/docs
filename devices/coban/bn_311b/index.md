# Coban - BN-311B

<div className="device-logo">
![BN-311B](./tracker.png)
</div>

The BN-311B is a compact GSM GPS motorcycle management terminal designed for reliable real time tracking and anti theft protection of small vehicles. Built for concealed installation on motorcycles and small cars, it combines GNSS positioning, 2G connectivity, and a rechargeable backup battery in a small form factor to support vehicle security and monitoring needs.

This model is compatible with Plaspy and is intended to stream location and telemetry to the platform for live monitoring and historical review. Its alarm inputs and reporting capabilities make it suitable for integrating into Plaspy workflows for fleet oversight, perimeter alerts, and remote immobilization actions when required.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet form factor suitable for motorcycles and small vehicles.
- Real time GNSS positioning with practical accuracy for tracking and route playback.
- 2G GSM connectivity with TCP UDP and SMS reporting options for flexible delivery.
- Rechargeable backup battery to maintain operation during main power loss.
- Multiple alarm inputs including ignition movement shock low battery and external power disconnect.
- Remote fuel or power cut off support for immobilization and theft response.
- Intelligent reporting modes to reduce data usage when the vehicle is stationary.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the BN-311B delivers location and event data to the platform where it is processed for live mapping alerts and reporting. Plaspy ingests telemetry and alarm signals to provide situational awareness, historical playback, and configurable notifications tailored to fleet or individual vehicle needs.

- Live location updates and mapping for real time visibility of each unit.
- Alarm and status events such as ignition movement shock and low battery to trigger Plaspy notifications and workflows.
- Geo fence and overspeed alerts configured and enforced through Plaspy for perimeter and speed management.
- Remote immobilizer commands available through Plaspy to assist with anti theft measures.
- Historical route playback and reporting in Plaspy for incident review and operational analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle fleet management with centralized tracking route history and status monitoring.
- Individual vehicle anti theft protection using concealed installation alarms and remote immobilization.
- Remote monitoring of ignition and external power status for operational oversight.
- Rental and shared vehicle security where size and concealment are important.
- Incident investigation and driver behavior review using historical playback and event logs.
