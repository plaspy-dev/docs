# Coban - BN-311B

![BN-311B](./tracker.png)

The BN-311B is a compact GSM/GPS motorcycle management terminal purpose-built for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and anti-theft protection of small vehicles. Designed for concealed installation on motorcycles and private cars, the BN-311B brings accurate GNSS positioning, robust 2G connectivity, and a rechargeable backup battery together in a small form factor to support fleet management and individual vehicle security workflows on the Plaspy platform.

Built to integrate with Plaspy, the BN-311B streams location and telemetry data using TCP/UDP/SMS and supports historical route playback, geo-fence alerts, and a range of alarm types. Operators get an easy-to-install GPS tracker that reports ignition \(ACC\) state, movement and shock events, overspeed conditions, and supports remote fuel/power cut-off for immobilization—making it a practical choice for anti-theft, telemetry and compact fleet tracking scenarios.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering real-time tracking and historical route playback via app or platform integration.
- Compact, concealed installation ideal for motorcycles and small vehicles—measures 6.0 × 3.5 × 1.8 cm and weighs 50 g.
- Reliable 2G GSM/GPRS connectivity on 850/900/1800/1900 MHz bands with TCP/UDP/SMS communication for flexible server delivery.
- High-precision GNSS receiver with ~5 m GPS accuracy and strong sensitivity \(–165 dBm\) for dependable positioning.
- Rechargeable 3.7V 180mAh Li-ion backup battery keeps the tracker operational during external power loss.
- Multiple alarm types \(ACC ignition, shock, low battery, external power disconnect, movement, overspeed, geo-fence\) for anti-theft and fleet safety.
- Supports remote fuel/power cut-off control for vehicle immobilizer workflows and rapid response to unauthorized use.
- Intelligent positioning modes to conserve data and battery when the vehicle is stationary while maintaining real-time alerting when active.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed, the BN-311B transmits GNSS-derived location and vehicle telemetry to Plaspy using TCP/UDP or SMS. Plaspy ingests that data to deliver live location updates, configurable alerts, and detailed reports for fleet management or individual vehicle protection. The unit’s intelligent reporting modes let Plaspy balance real-time tracking needs with data efficiency, while onboard alarms and status inputs provide contextual event triggers for automations and notifications.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy for live mapping and tracking dashboards.
- ACC ignition status and alarm events \(ACC alarm, shock alarm, movement alarm\) available to trigger Plaspy alerts and workflows.
- Geo-fence reporting and overspeed alarm notifications configured through Plaspy for perimeter and speed policy enforcement.
- Remote immobilizer capability \(remote fuel/power cut-off\) to disable vehicle operation from Plaspy when required for anti-theft actions.
- Historical route playback and reports delivered via Plaspy’s platform and app for fleet analysis and incident review.
