# Riti - 690 (IDU-401)

![690 (IDU-401)](./tracker.jpg)

Locator 690 (IDU-401) is a vehicle mounted Mobile IoT device designed for modern fleet management. The unit combines GPS telemetry with integrated image capture and on device AI detection to identify abnormal driving events. It is engineered to work alongside existing DVR and MDVR systems, automatically capturing and buffering pre event, during event, and post event images to support faster incident review.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the Locator 690 brings image evidence and real time tracking into the Plaspy platform without replacing installed video recorders. Its event driven workflows, configurable IO triggers and local buffering allow fleet managers using Plaspy to retrieve live images on demand, index event photos with location data, and accelerate evidence driven decisions across operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible vehicle tracker with on device AI for event detection and automated image capture
- Event driven image evidence for rollover, collision, harsh maneuvers, overspeed, SOS and other abnormal events
- Live image retrieval through the Plaspy platform and mobile apps for immediate situational awareness
- Configurable inputs and outputs with support for up to five digital inputs and multiple analog and temperature ports
- Cellular connectivity across 2G 3G and 4G plus MT3333 GNSS for reliable real time tracking
- Local buffering of approximately 30,000 GPS records and circular image storage to preserve data during outages
- Compact vehicle mounted form factor suited to commercial fleet environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Locator 690 synchronizes GPS telemetry and indexed image evidence to the cloud platform so fleet teams can investigate events with photos tied directly to tracks and timestamps. Plaspy surfaces alerts, live images and event histories so managers can correlate driver behavior with photographic proof and location context.

- Synchronized telemetry and image uploads to Plaspy for unified event timelines and searches
- Event driven alerts and automated image capture for faster incident detection and response
- On demand live image retrieval via Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps to verify situations in real time
- Configurable IO triggers to extend event detection using vehicle inputs and connected peripherals
- Local buffering and forward on connect to ensure GPS records and event images are retained during temporary connectivity loss

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet incident investigation where pre during and post event images shorten evidence collection
- Driver safety and behavior programs that link harsh events to photographic proof for coaching
- Public transport and municipal fleets requiring indexed event reporting and on demand live images
- Logistics and delivery operations combining GPS telemetry with image evidence and peripheral sensors for proof of service
- Operations that retain DVR or MDVR systems but need searchable cloud indexed images without replacing existing recorders
