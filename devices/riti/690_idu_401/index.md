# Riti - 690 (IDU-401)

![690 (IDU-401)](./tracker.jpg)

Locator 690 \(IDU-401\) is a Plaspy compatible, vehicle-mounted Mobile IoT device engineered for modern fleet management that combines intelligent image capture with robust GPS telemetry. Designed to integrate with existing DVR/MDVR systems, the Locator 690 performs on-device AI detection to identify abnormal driving events and automatically capture and upload event images to your cloud fleet platform. For fleet managers using Plaspy, this device speeds incident investigation by indexing pre-, during- and post-event photos alongside location and trajectory data, enabling faster, evidence-driven decisions.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, Locator 690 adds real-time tracking and image evidence without replacing installed video recorders. The unit supports on-demand live image retrieval via the platform and mobile apps and can be configured to trigger captures from up to five IO inputs. Its combination of event-driven workflows, local buffering, and cellular/GNSS connectivity makes it a practical fit for logistics, public transport, municipal fleets and other commercial vehicle operations that require reliable telemetry, event evidence and improved driver behavior management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with integrated, on-device AI for event detection and automated image capture.
- Event-driven image evidence \(rollover, collision, harsh maneuvers, overspeed, SOS, idling, engine off and more\) for faster incident resolution.
- Real-time remote retrieval of live images through the Plaspy platform and mobile apps for immediate situational awareness.
- Flexible IO: up to 5 configurable digital inputs, analog inputs and multiple outputs to connect sensors and trigger workflows.
- Robust cellular connectivity \(2G/3G/4G\) and MT3333 GNSS chipset for reliable real-time tracking and telemetry.
- Local buffering of ~30,000 GPS records and circular event image storage to ensure data continuity during outages.
- Compact, vehicle-mountable form factor with industrial operating range for commercial fleet use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, Locator 690 delivers synchronized telemetry and image evidence to the cloud platform. The device’s AI detects abnormal driving events and captures images that are uploaded and indexed in Plaspy for conditional search, reporting and visualization. Fleet managers can use Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps to receive alerts, view live images on demand, and correlate photos with GPS tracks and event metadata.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via 2G/3G/4G cellular connectivity and MT3333 GNSS.
- Event-driven image capture and automated upload for rollover, collision, hard turn, harsh braking, rapid acceleration, overspeed, SOS and more.
- Configurable IO-triggered captures: extend event detection with up to five digital inputs and additional analog/temperature inputs.
- Local buffering and forward-on-connect: stores GPS and event data \(~30,000 records\) and images \(up to 200 events\) when offline.
- Live image retrieval from the vehicle through Plaspy for on-demand situational monitoring and rapid response.

## Use Cases

- Fleet incident investigation — capture pre/during/post-event images to shorten evidence collection and accelerate claims handling.
- Driver behavior and safety programs — detect harsh braking, rapid acceleration, harsh turns and overspeed events and link them to photographic proof.
- Public transport and municipal fleets — real-time live image retrieval and indexed event reporting for passenger safety and operational oversight.
- Logistics and delivery operations — combine GPS telemetry with event images and peripheral sensors \(temperature, barcode scanners, RFID\) for proof of service and compliance.
- Vehicle monitoring where DVR/MDVR is retained — augment existing video recorders with intelligent image capture and cloud indexing without replacing installed systems.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Locator 690 is built for organizations that need more than position data — it provides actionable image evidence tied to GPS and event telemetry, and is Plaspy compatible out of the box. By combining on-device AI, configurable IO triggers and reliable cellular/GNSS connectivity, the device reduces time spent searching long video recordings and improves incident reconstruction. For fleet managers focused on safety, compliance and operational efficiency, this unit adds a searchable, image-backed layer to Plaspy’s real-time tracking and reporting capabilities. Use the device’s digital outputs and inputs to integrate with vehicle systems and platform workflows, and leverage Plaspy to correlate telemetry with fuel monitoring, ignition state, immobilizer actions or external Bluetooth sensors managed through the platform for a complete fleet visibility solution.
