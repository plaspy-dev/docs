# Riti - SLS-00886

![SLS-00886](./logo.jpg)

The Riti SLS-00886 GPS vehicle tracker is a reliable and efficient device designed for tracking and managing vehicles. With its SMS/GPRS functions and built-in high sensitive SiRF Star III GPS and Quad-Band GSM module, this tracker ensures accurate and real-time location tracking, remote monitoring, and management of vehicles. Whether you need to track a fleet of vehicles or monitor the whereabouts of your personal vehicle, the SLS-00886 is a perfect solution.

One of the standout features of the SLS-00886 is its ability to deliver GPS coordinates to a back-end server via the GPRS network or send SMS messages directly to user-defined cell phone numbers via the GSM network. This allows for seamless and convenient tracking and monitoring of vehicles, ensuring that you are always aware of their location and status.

Additionally, the SLS-00886 comes bundled with a FREE professional version platform, making it suitable for personal customers and small to medium-sized businesses. It is also highly versatile and can be easily installed on motorcycles and electric bicycles. With its designated functional features for security purposes, such as vehicle battery voltage detection, location tracking, driving route monitoring, and history data recording, the SLS-00886 offers a cost-effective solution with maximum performance.

Key Features:

- SMS/GPRS functions for seamless tracking and monitoring
- Built-in high sensitive SiRF Star III GPS and Quad-Band GSM module
- Delivers GPS coordinates to a back-end server via GPRS network
- Sends SMS messages to user-defined cell phone numbers via GSM network
- Comes bundled with a FREE professional version platform
- Suitable for motorcycles and electric bicycles
- Vehicle battery voltage detection
- Location tracking and route monitoring
- History data recording
