# Riti - SLS-012SF

![SLS-012SF](./logo.jpg)

The Riti SLS-012SF is a GPS vehicle tracker designed for fleet management, location services, remote monitoring, and industrial control scenarios. It combines a high sensitivity GPS module with a mobile communication module to provide continuous location reporting and backend data transmission. The device is built to integrate with a range of external equipment such as PND units, i-Button, barcode scanners, RFID readers, tire pressure sensors, and temperature sensors, making it useful for both vehicle tracking and broader telemetry tasks.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the SLS-012SF can feed real time location and sensor data into the Plaspy platform for visualization, reporting, and operational oversight. Its proven use on large fleet management systems demonstrates reliability and makes it a practical option for organizations that want to centralize vehicle and sensor data in Plaspy for analysis, alerts, and fleet level decision making.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity GPS module for consistent and accurate location tracking
- Mobile communication support for real time data transmission to backend servers
- Flexible integration with external devices such as PND, i-Button, barcode scanners, RFID, tire pressure and temperature sensors
- Real time data delivery to backend systems for instant storage and processing
- Easy access to collected data via internet or direct download to client computers
- Proven reliability and operational use on large fleet management platforms

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SLS-012SF sends location and auxiliary device data to the platform so fleet managers can monitor assets and people in near real time. Plaspy ingests the incoming streams and makes them available through dashboards, reports, and alerting tools.

- Consolidate GPS position and external sensor inputs into Plaspy for unified visibility
- Monitor vehicle locations and movement history through Plaspy maps and timelines
- Configure alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on incoming data patterns
- Use Plaspy reporting to analyze fleet performance and sensor trends over time
- Enable two way transmission scenarios where backend commands and acknowledgements are managed through Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and route oversight for logistics and transport operations
- Remote monitoring of vehicle conditions and attached sensor data for maintenance planning
- Industrial control applications where location and sensor inputs need central aggregation
- Asset and equipment tracking when combined with RFID or barcode scanners
- Monitoring temperature sensitive cargo or tire pressure information alongside position
