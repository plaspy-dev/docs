# Riti - SLS-012SF

![SLS-012SF](./logo.jpg)

The Riti SLS-012SF GPS vehicle tracker is a versatile and reliable device designed for fleet management, location services, remote monitoring, and industrial control applications. With its high sensitivity GPS module and mobile communication module \(GSM/SMS/GPRS\), the SLS-012SF can seamlessly integrate with various external devices such as PND, i-Button, barcode scanners, RFID equipment, tire pressure sensors, and temperature sensors. This makes it an ideal choice for back-end platforms that require data collection, two-way transmission, analysis, and management for both vehicles and human beings.

The SLS-012SF GPS vehicle tracker sends real-time data to the backend server for instant storage and processing. Users can easily access this data through the internet or download it directly to client-end computers for efficient fleet monitoring and management. This device has been successfully operating on the largest fleet management platform in Taiwan for several years, proving its reliability, system-end software tools, and outstanding functional features. With its proven track record, the Riti SLS-012SF GPS vehicle tracker offers significant advantages to customers in terms of reliability, performance, and functionality.

Key Features:

- High sensitivity GPS module for accurate and reliable tracking
- Mobile communication module \(GSM/SMS/GPRS\) for seamless connectivity
- Flexible integration with various external devices such as PND, i-Button, barcode scanners, RFID equipment, tire pressure sensors, and temperature sensors
- Real-time data transmission to the backend server for instant storage and processing
- Easy access to data through the internet or direct download to client-end computers
- Proven reliability and performance on a large fleet management platform
