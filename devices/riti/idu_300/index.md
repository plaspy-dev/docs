# Riti - IDU-300

![IDU-300](./logo.jpg)

The Riti IDU-300 is a 3G Intelligent Data Unit designed for modern fleet management. It provides real time tracking, dispatching support, and reporting of vehicle abnormalities while integrating with fleet management platforms and mobile apps. The IDU-300 also supports optional accessories such as a dispatch panel and temperature sensor, enabling broader monitoring beyond basic location data.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the IDU-300 can feed location and accessory data into Plaspy to improve visibility and operational control. Plaspy users can leverage the tracker to monitor vehicles, receive alerts, and use reporting tools that help inform fleet decisions while maintaining the flexibility to work alongside the device's native platform integrations.

## Key Highlights

- 3G connectivity for enhanced tracking capabilities compared to older 2G devices
- Real time tracking and dispatching features that support operational oversight
- Compatibility with accessories such as dispatch panels and temperature sensors for expanded monitoring
- Designed to assist fleet managers with decision support and abnormality reporting
- Integration friendly with fleet platforms including the device native eLocation system and Plaspy
- Suitable for both small fleets and larger scale operations seeking centralized visibility

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected with Plaspy, the IDU-300 transmits vehicle location and accessory information so that fleet managers can view and act on that data within the Plaspy platform. This enables consolidated monitoring across a mixed fleet and supports both live operations and post event analysis.

- Live vehicle location and movement visualization in Plaspy dashboards
- Monitoring of accessory inputs such as temperature and dispatch panel status when available
- Alerts and notifications for reported abnormalities and notable events
- Support for dispatch workflows and operational oversight through Plaspy tools
- Historical tracking and reporting to aid analysis and performance reviews

## Typical Use Cases

- Centralized dispatch and route supervision for delivery and service fleets
- Temperature sensitive transport monitoring when a temperature sensor is attached
- Fleet wide abnormality reporting and incident review
- Scaling from small fleet pilots to broader deployments across mixed vehicle types
- Coordinating mobile resources and improving decision making with timely location data

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The IDU-300 is a practical option for organizations moving beyond legacy 2G equipment and looking for a device that supports accessory expansion and real time operations. Its design focus on fleet decision support aligns with Plaspy use cases around visibility, alerts, and reporting, making it a sensible device to pair with the platform for many operational scenarios.
