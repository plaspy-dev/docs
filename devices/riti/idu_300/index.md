# Riti - IDU-300

![IDU-300](./logo.jpg)

The Riti IDU-300 is a cutting-edge 3G IDU \(Intelligent Data Unit\) that revolutionizes fleet management. Unlike traditional 2G trackers, the IDU-300 offers a superior solution for monitoring and dispatching vehicles, as well as reporting abnormalities. With the eLocation fleet managing platform system and mobile app, this GPS tracker is designed to provide seamless integration and real-time tracking capabilities for fleet managers.

One of the standout features of the IDU-300 is its compatibility with additional accessories, such as a dispatch panel and temperature sensor. By connecting these accessories to the IDU-300, customers can monitor a wide range of data through the eLocation tracking platform. This versatility makes the IDU-300 suitable for various applications, allowing fleet managers to make informed decisions based on accurate and up-to-date information.

With years of experience in the MRM \(Mobile Resource Management\) domain, Riti has leveraged its expertise to develop the IDU-300. This GPS tracker is designed to provide the best managing decision support for customers, ensuring that fleet management becomes more efficient and effective. Whether you need to monitor a small fleet or a large-scale operation, the IDU-300 offers the features and capabilities to meet your needs.

### Key Features:

- 3G connectivity for superior tracking capabilities
- Seamless integration with eLocation fleet managing platform system and mobile app
- Compatible with additional accessories for monitoring various data
- Provides real-time tracking, monitoring, and dispatching capabilities
- Designed to offer the best managing decision support for fleet managers
