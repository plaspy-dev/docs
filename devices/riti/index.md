# Riti

Riti is a manufacturer known for compact GPS tracking devices used in vehicle and asset monitoring. Many users choose Riti hardware when they need practical tracking endpoints that can be integrated into third party platforms. This page explains how Riti devices work with Plaspy, what you can expect from their typical capabilities, and how to find the right device for your needs.

Plaspy works with compatible Riti devices to provide live location, historical playback, geofencing, and fleet monitoring features. Below you will find guidance on integration and usage with Plaspy along with common use cases and frequently asked questions.

## How Riti devices work with Plaspy

Riti trackers transmit location and telemetry data over cellular networks using standard GNSS positioning. When configured to send data to Plaspy, the device will report location, movement, and status events to Plaspy servers where that data is processed and displayed in the Plaspy web app and dashboards.

Typical steps to connect a Riti device to Plaspy include:
- Ensuring the device has a compatible APN and active SIM with data service.
- Setting the device reporting server and protocol details as required by Plaspy.
- Verifying data reception in the Plaspy device list and map.

Specific device setup details and connection parameters are available in the device configuration guides hosted in Plaspy documentation and within the device pages listed in the catalog below.

## Common features found in Riti devices

Riti devices commonly provide the following capabilities that are relevant for use with Plaspy:
- GNSS based location reporting for real time tracking and route history.
- Motion and ignition detection for activity based reporting.
- OTA or configuration interfaces for setting reporting intervals and server endpoints.
- Battery powered and vehicle wired form factors suitable for asset or vehicle use.

## Typical use cases for Riti GPS trackers

Riti hardware is often employed in scenarios such as:
- Small fleet vehicle tracking where simple installation and reliable reporting are priorities.
- Portable asset monitoring where compact battery powered devices are needed.
- Light equipment tracking for construction or rental fleets.
- Route verification and driver activity logging when combined with Plaspy reporting tools.

Plaspy adds value by aggregating device telemetry into maps, alerts, and reports so operators can monitor fleets and assets at scale.
