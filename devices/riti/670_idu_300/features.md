# Riti - 670 (IDU-300) Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Riti Locator 670 (IDU-300) with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities this in vehicle GPS tracker brings to Plaspy deployments, how those capabilities become visible inside Plaspy, and what operational value fleet teams can expect from integrating this device with the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. The Locator 670 is a discontinued 3G IDU model, so deployment characteristics for legacy fleets or mixed environments should be verified against current device firmware and Riti documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Locator 670 is a Plaspy compatible in vehicle GPS tracker designed for frequent position reporting and multi sensor telemetry. It is positioned for fleet managers who need reliable location visibility, per second mileage reporting, battery voltage monitoring, and extended peripheral support for workflows such as cold chain and asset protection.

- Real time position reporting with frequent updates for responsive fleet visibility
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver for improved satellite lock in challenging environments
- Per second mileage and odometer reporting for accurate trip and utilization analytics
- Battery voltage monitoring to detect low voltage conditions and alert operators
- Support for temperature sensors, fuel level inputs, DVR and peripheral integration for extended telemetry
- Local data buffering to preserve records during temporary connectivity loss and upload when cellular recovers

## Core Features of Riti - 670 (IDU-300)

- Plaspy compatible real time GNSS tracking for live mapping and monitoring
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver providing stronger satellite lock and reliable fixes
- Frequent position reporting capable of updates as often as every 15 seconds
- Per second mileage calculation for more accurate odometer and route analytics
- Battery voltage monitoring to surface low voltage conditions in Plaspy
- Broad peripheral and I O support including temperature sensor inputs and fuel level inputs
- Local data buffering with automatic uplink when connectivity is restored
- Remote diagnostics and firmware update support to aid maintenance and keep units aligned with Plaspy

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a Locator 670 is integrated with Plaspy, its GNSS positions and connected sensor telemetry are forwarded to the platform so teams can monitor vehicles on live maps, review historical trips, and act on exception conditions. Plaspy ingests the device feed and exposes location and telemetry in reports, alerts, and event logs.

- Live location updates and map visibility based on frequent position reports
- Trip and utilization reports that use per second mileage for cleaner route analytics
- Battery voltage readings surfaced as telemetry fields and used to trigger low voltage alerts
- Temperature and fuel level inputs forwarded into Plaspy for cold chain and fuel monitoring workflows
- Peripheral events such as DVR alerts, SOS triggers, and external input events recorded in event logs
- Local buffering ensures records generated offline are uploaded and reconciled by Plaspy when connectivity returns

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch for trucks, delivery vehicles, and service fleets requiring responsive location tracking
- Cold chain and temperature controlled deliveries using temperature sensor inputs and Plaspy alerts
- Vehicle safety and emergency fleet monitoring where SOS events and synchronized DVR events are important
- Fuel and asset monitoring through fuel level inputs and external reader integrations
- MDVR and telematics integrations where RS 232 and multiple I O allow synchronized event data
- Legacy fleet support where the discontinued 3G IDU model remains in service within a mixed deployment
