# Aplicom - A11 BLE

![A11 BLE](./logo..jpg)

The Aplicom A11 BLE is a versatile and field-proven telematics device that offers a comprehensive solution for fleet and asset management. With global mobile connectivity capabilities, a wide range of digital and analog connections, customizable configuration options, Over-The-Air \(OTA\) updates, and parallel interface handling, this device is designed to provide hassle-free control over fleet assets.

One of the standout features of the A11 BLE is its easy device connectivity with Bluetooth. It can be paired with Bluetooth LE 5.0 beacons and sensors, allowing for additional data collection or device identification. This opens up a range of possibilities, such as using Bluetooth tags and beacons to identify users, assets, or act as wireless sensors that provide measurement data and on/off-information. The interaction between beacons and A11 units enables a variety of adaptable use-cases.

Another notable feature of the A11 BLE is its global roaming telematics capabilities. With 4G LTE communications, this device ensures data availability even in remote locations. Additionally, device configuration and updates are easy to manage thanks to the OTA functionality. This means that assets can continue their operations while the telematics unit takes care of any necessary updates automatically, allowing you to focus on running your business.

Overall, the Aplicom A11 BLE is a reliable and feature-rich telematics device that offers seamless connectivity, customizable configuration options, and global roaming capabilities. Whether you need to track and manage a fleet of vehicles or monitor and protect valuable assets, the A11 BLE is an ideal choice.
