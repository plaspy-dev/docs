# Aplicom - A9 Quick

![A9 Quick](./logo.jpg)

The Aplicom A9 Quick is a versatile and easily movable vehicle telematics unit designed for tracking and tracing purposes. With its simple installation process and ability to be moved from one vehicle to another, this device offers convenience and flexibility. To start using the A9 Quick, all the driver needs to do is plug the unit into the cigarette lighter socket, and they can immediately take advantage of its preconfigured and versatile Aplicom A9 NEX functionalities.

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the A9 Quick is compatible with the Aplicom 3PAD, a small 3-button keypad that can be used for various purposes, such as an electronic driver logbook or any other interface where user reporting is required. This feature expands the usage possibilities of the A9 Quick and provides added convenience for users.

The Aplicom A9 Quick comes with a range of outstanding features that make it a reliable and efficient tracking solution. It offers fast first fix with A-GPS, ensuring quick and accurate positioning. The device also includes a back-up battery, ensuring continuous operation even in the event of a power outage. With its extensive memory capacity, the A9 Quick can smoothly handle large amounts of data and can be expanded to include additional features. The unit features a highly reliable two-processor architecture, providing optimal performance and stability.

Other notable features of the A9 Quick include GPS/GLONASS positioning with Cell ID positioning back-up, internal GSM and GPS/GLONASS antennas for reliable connectivity, and a 3D accelerometer for acceleration measurement, movement detection, and wake-up functionality. The device also supports geofencing, allowing users to define geofences in different shapes \(circle, box, polygon\) and receive geofence in/out reporting.
