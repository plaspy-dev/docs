# Aplicom - A9 Quick

<div className="device-logo">
![A9 Quick](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Aplicom A9 Quick is a portable vehicle telematics unit designed for tracking and tracing. Intended for quick deployment, the device is plugged into the vehicle cigarette lighter socket so it can be moved easily between vehicles. It provides preconfigured A9 NEX functionality and supports an external 3 button keypad called the Aplicom 3PAD for simple driver input and reporting.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the A9 Quick can be integrated into Plaspy for centralized fleet visibility and operational oversight. Its portability and preconfigured setup make it suitable for fleets that need rapid onboarding of temporary or rotating vehicles while maintaining location history, driver reporting, and geofence monitoring within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Portable plug in design for fast transfer between vehicles and immediate operation
- Preconfigured A9 NEX functionality for quicker setup and deployment
- Fast first fix with assisted GPS to reduce initial positioning time
- Internal antennas and GPS GLONASS positioning with cell based backup for reliable coverage
- Built in backup battery to maintain operation during power interruptions
- 3D accelerometer for movement detection and wake up features
- Support for geofencing with multiple shape options and geofence in out reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the A9 Quick provides continuous position updates and event reporting that feed directly into Plaspy dashboards and reports. Its preconfigured nature reduces integration effort, and the device can supply driver initiated events when used with the Aplicom 3PAD.

- Real time location visibility for vehicles managed in Plaspy
- Geofence alerts and historical geofence reporting inside Plaspy
- Movement and activity notifications derived from the device accelerometer
- Driver reporting inputs from the Aplicom 3PAD reflected in Plaspy logs
- Historical route and event data available for Plaspy reporting and analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Short term rentals and shared vehicle pools where units are frequently moved
- Temporary or seasonal fleets requiring rapid installation and removal
- Last mile delivery and service fleets that need simple driver reporting
- Field teams or contractors that require portable tracking with minimal setup
- Use cases needing geofence monitoring and basic motion detection

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The A9 Quick is a practical choice for organizations that prioritize portability and simple deployment. Its plug in approach and preconfigured settings reduce time to operation, while features like assisted positioning, backup power, and geofencing help maintain reliable location data for fleet oversight.

Paired with Plaspy, the A9 Quick can deliver the core visibility and reporting most fleet managers need without lengthy setup. For businesses that rotate units between vehicles or need straightforward driver input options, this device offers a balance of convenience and dependable tracking functionality.
