# Aplicom - T10

<div className="device-logo">
![T10](./logo.png)
</div>

The Aplicom T10 is a compact, rugged GPS tracker and telemetry device designed for fleet telematics and industrial IoT. Housed in a weather resistant IP67 enclosure, the T10 is built for long term field operation across vehicles, trailers, fixed assets, and dispersed equipment. It supports Aplicom Silver Cloud for remote configuration, monitoring, and firmware updates to reduce on site maintenance.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T10 integrates into Plaspy workflows to provide real time location and telemetry visibility alongside fleet management features. Variants in the T Series, including the T10G with CAN bus reading and 2G connectivity, make the model versatile for operations that need resilient hardware plus centralized device management and tracking inside Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for centralized real time tracking and fleet management.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure for reliable operation in harsh weather and industrial settings.
- Remote device management through Aplicom Silver Cloud for configuration and firmware management.
- Compact form factor suitable for vehicles, trailers, vending machines, and fixed assets.
- T Series flexibility with variants and configurable functionality to match deployment needs.
- T10G variant adds CAN bus data reading and 2G network support for vehicle telemetry.
- Starter kits and a downloadable datasheet available to speed deployment and planning.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the Aplicom T10 forwards position and device telemetry to the platform so operators can monitor assets, set alerts, and generate reports from a single operational view. Plaspy ingests the data the T10 collects and presents it alongside other fleet and asset information for operational oversight and decision making.

- Real time location updates and continuous situational awareness for fleet and asset monitoring.
- Telemetry and vehicle data from T10G variants can be combined with position data in Plaspy dashboards.
- Alerts and rules in Plaspy can be used to flag events such as geofence breaches, movement, or status changes.
- Remote device status and configuration visibility through Aplicom Silver Cloud helps reduce onsite visits while Plaspy centralizes operational data.
- Reporting and historical playback in Plaspy let teams analyze routes, uptime, and event trends for operational improvement.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and routing for light and heavy vehicle fleets needing durable trackers.
- Trailer and cargo tracking where rugged mounting and weather resistance are required.
- Vending machine and remote kiosk telemetry for dispersed commercial assets.
- Industrial IoT deployments that require long term field reliability and remote management.
- Anti theft and immobilization workflows where device I O and vehicle interfaces are provided and configured.

The Aplicom T10 offers a balance of rugged hardware and remote manageability that pairs well with Plaspy for organizations that need dependable tracking in challenging environments. Its compact IP67 design and T Series options make it suitable for a range of asset types, while Aplicom Silver Cloud support helps keep devices configured and updated without frequent site visits.
