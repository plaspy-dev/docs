# Aplicom - T10 G

<div className="device-logo">
![T10 G](./logo.png)
</div>

The Aplicom T10 G is a rugged GPS tracker purpose built for vehicle telemetry and fleet management. Manufactured by Aplicom, the T10 G combines a robust IP67 enclosure and plug and play installation with direct vehicle bus access to capture location and vehicle level signals for real time tracking and diagnostics. It is designed for demanding environments and roaming fleets that require continuous location reporting and telemetry.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T10 G can feed location and vehicle telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and workflows. With cellular connectivity, remote device management through Aplicom Silver Cloud, and vehicle bus data capture, the T10 G provides the kinds of signals fleet operators typically bring into Plaspy for monitoring, alerts and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device that integrates vehicle telemetry and location reporting into fleet workflows.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure for dependable operation in harsh vehicle and industrial environments.
- Built in CAN bus access enables collection of vehicle bus signals such as ignition state and diagnostic information when available.
- Cellular connectivity designed for roaming operations with fall back options to maintain coverage across regions.
- Plug and play installation to reduce deployment time across mixed fleets.
- Remote provisioning and lifecycle management via Aplicom Silver Cloud for scaled device administration.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Aplicom T10 G streams location and vehicle telemetry to Aplicom’s device management layer and can be connected to Plaspy so fleet teams receive real time position, status and diagnostic information in their chosen workflows. Plaspy consumes those telemetry feeds to provide live monitoring, alerts and operational reporting for fleets and vehicle assets.

- Real time location updates and continuous position reporting for fleet visibility.
- Vehicle bus data such as ignition state, diagnostic messages and fuel related telemetry when those signals are present on the vehicle bus.
- Remote provisioning and configuration through Aplicom Silver Cloud to accelerate onboarding into Plaspy.
- Network resilience through cellular roaming and fallback options to keep telemetry flowing in variable coverage areas.
- Starter kit support for pilot deployments and phased rollouts before large scale integration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Centralized fleet management with live tracking and route monitoring in Plaspy.
- Anti theft and security workflows using location and ignition status to detect unauthorized movement.
- Remote telemetry for industrial vehicles to support preventive maintenance and operational oversight.
- Trailer and asset tracking across regions where a rugged enclosure and roaming connectivity are required.
- Pilot testing and phased deployment using starter kits or region specific variants.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Aplicom T10 G offers a practical balance of rugged hardware and vehicle level visibility that aligns with common Plaspy fleet workflows. Its vehicle bus access and continuous location reporting allow operators to bring richer telemetry into Plaspy for reporting, alerts and operational decision making. Remote device management via Aplicom Silver Cloud and plug and play installation help reduce deployment overhead for mixed or roaming fleets.

If your operation needs a durable telematics device that can stream vehicle telemetry into Plaspy for tracking, diagnostics and fleet reporting, the T10 G is a good candidate to evaluate.
