# Aplicom - A9 IPEX

![A9 IPEX](./logo.jpg)

The Aplicom A9 IPEX is a rugged GPS tracker designed to collect and communicate vehicle and asset information to a back-end service. Built for demanding environments, the unit emphasizes reliable location tracking and operational data capture for fleets, construction machines, and other mobile assets. Its IP67 enclosure provides dust and water protection, making it suitable for outdoor and industrial use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the A9 IPEX can feed positional data and event information into Plaspy's fleet management platform to support monitoring, reporting, and operational workflows. The tracker's support for GPS/GLONASS positioning, assisted positioning methods, accelerometer-based movement detection, geofence reporting, and integration interfaces for vehicle and reefer systems means Plaspy users can incorporate this device into visibility, alerting, and analytics use cases without replacing existing back-end workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged IP67 enclosure for reliable operation in dusty and wet conditions.
- GPS and GLONASS positioning with assisted location methods for consistent tracking.
- 3D accelerometer enables movement detection and basic activity reporting.
- Geofence support with multiple shapes including circles, boxes, and polygons.
- Interfaces for vehicle system integration and serial reefer reading for cold chain units.
- Designed for cost-efficient fleet and asset monitoring in industrial environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Aplicom A9 IPEX delivers location and event data to the platform where it can be visualized, analyzed, and used to trigger operational rules. Plaspy ingests the tracker's standard position updates and event messages to provide a unified view of vehicles and assets alongside reporting and alerting features.

- Real-time location visibility and historical route playback in Plaspy dashboards.
- Geofence in/out alerts and zone-based reporting for compliance and operational control.
- Movement and activity events from the accelerometer used for motion alerts and idle detection.
- Integration of vehicle and reefer data read via the tracker's interfaces into Plaspy reporting where applicable.
- Aggregated reports and alerts to support fleet utilization, security, and productivity monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet location tracking and route history for light and heavy vehicle fleets.
- Construction site vehicle and equipment monitoring in harsh outdoor conditions.
- Cold chain trailer or reefer monitoring using serial data integration for temperature unit readouts.
- Asset security and geofence based access control for worksites and storage yards.
- Operational oversight and reporting to improve utilization and reduce downtime.
