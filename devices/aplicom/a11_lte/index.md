# Aplicom - A11 LTE

![A11 LTE](./logo..jpg)

The Aplicom A11 LTE is a powerful telematics device designed for fleet and asset management. With its global mobile connectivity capabilities, this device ensures that you can track and manage your assets no matter where they are located. Whether you have a small fleet or a large number of assets, the A11 LTE is a versatile and field-proven solution that can meet your needs.

One of the standout features of the A11 LTE is its easy device connectivity with Bluetooth. This allows you to pair the device with Bluetooth LE 5.0 beacons and sensors, enabling additional data collection and device identification. You can use Bluetooth tags and beacons to identify users, assets, or even act as wireless sensors that provide measurement data and on/off-information. This opens up a range of adaptable use-cases and gives you greater control over your fleet assets.

Another key advantage of the A11 LTE is its global roaming capabilities. With 4G LTE communications, this device ensures that data availability is not limited to specific regions. Even in remote sites, you can rely on the A11 LTE to provide real-time data and updates. Additionally, the device is equipped with Over-The-Air \(OTA\) functionality, making device configuration and updates easy to manage. This means that your assets can continue their operations while the telematics unit takes care of any needed updates automatically, allowing you to focus on running your business.

### Key Features:

- Global mobile connectivity capabilities
- Easy device connectivity with Bluetooth
- Pairing with Bluetooth LE 5.0 beacons and sensors
- Global roaming with 4G LTE communications
- Over-The-Air \(OTA\) functionality for easy device configuration and updates
