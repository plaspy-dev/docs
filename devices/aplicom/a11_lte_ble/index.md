# Aplicom - A11 LTE BLE

<div className="device-logo">
![A11 LTE BLE](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The Aplicom A11 LTE BLE is a versatile, field proven telematics device designed for fleet and asset management. It combines global mobile connectivity with local wireless pairing to extend identification and data collection through Bluetooth beacons and tags. The device is positioned for organisations that need continuous location visibility and adaptable identification for assets or users across mixed operations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the A11 LTE BLE can be integrated into Plaspy for consolidated monitoring and operational oversight. Its combination of wide area connectivity and Bluetooth based tagging makes it a flexible option for tracking vehicles, equipment, and tagged assets while minimising manual maintenance through built in over the air update support.

## Key Highlights

- Field proven telematics platform suitable for fleet and asset tracking
- Global mobile connectivity for remote and wide area coverage
- Bluetooth pairing with beacons and tags for asset or user identification
- Over the air update capability to simplify device maintenance
- Scalable for small fleets or larger asset deployments
- Flexible data collection options to support varied operational needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the A11 LTE BLE provides location and device status data that Plaspy surfaces for fleet managers and operators. Plaspy can consolidate inputs from the device and related beacons to improve visibility and decision making across vehicles and assets.

- Live and historical location visualization on Plaspy maps for routing and analysis
- Fleet monitoring and grouping to manage vehicles and tagged assets together
- Alerts and notifications for movement, status changes, and custom conditions
- Reporting and exportable logs for operational review and compliance
- Asset identification and proximity awareness using paired Bluetooth beacons
- Device connectivity and health indicators to help plan maintenance and updates

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle fleet tracking for routing, dispatch, and utilization monitoring
- Equipment and asset location tracking across yards and remote sites
- Identification of specific assets or personnel with Bluetooth tags and beacons
- Remote asset monitoring where global roaming ensures continuous data
- Rental and logistics operations that require flexible tracking and reporting

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The A11 LTE BLE is a practical choice for organisations that need a balance of wide area connectivity and local asset identification. Its Bluetooth pairing options let teams tag assets or users for finer grain identification while global mobile connectivity helps maintain consistent tracking across regions. For operations that value remote management, the device's over the air update capability reduces the need for manual interventions.

Choosing the A11 LTE BLE with Plaspy gives you a combined solution for visibility, operational oversight, and simplified device lifecycle management. Plaspy brings consolidated maps, alerts, and reporting while the A11 unit supplies the location and tag based inputs that many fleets and asset managers rely on.
