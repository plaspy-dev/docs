# Aplicom - A11 LTE BLE

![A11 LTE BLE](./logo..jpg)

The Aplicom A11 LTE BLE is a versatile and field-proven telematics device that offers a comprehensive solution for fleet and asset management. With global mobile connectivity capabilities, this device ensures that you can track and monitor your assets no matter where they are located. Whether you have a small fleet or a large number of assets, the A11 LTE BLE provides hassle-free control and reliable data collection.

One of the standout features of the A11 LTE BLE is its easy device connectivity with Bluetooth. This device can be paired with Bluetooth LE 5.0 beacons and sensors, allowing for additional data collection and device identification. Bluetooth tags and beacons can be used to identify users, assets, or act as wireless sensors, providing measurement data and on/off-information. This interaction between beacons and the A11 unit opens up a range of adaptable use-cases, making it a highly flexible and customizable solution.

Another key advantage of the A11 LTE BLE is its global roaming telematics capabilities. With 4G LTE communications, this device ensures data availability even in remote locations. Additionally, the A11 LTE BLE offers Over-The-Air \(OTA\) functionality, making device configuration and updates easy to manage. This means that your assets can continue their operations while the telematics unit takes care of any necessary updates automatically. With the A11 LTE BLE, you can focus on running your business without worrying about connectivity or device maintenance.

Overall, the Aplicom A11 LTE BLE is an excellent choice for fleet and asset management. Its global mobile connectivity, Bluetooth compatibility, and OTA functionality make it a reliable and user-friendly solution. Whether you need to track vehicles, monitor equipment, or manage a large fleet, the A11 LTE BLE provides the features and capabilities you need to streamline your operations and improve efficiency.
