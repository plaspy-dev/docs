# Aplicom - A11

![A11](./logo..jpg)

The Aplicom A11 Series telematics devices are a reliable and versatile solution for fleet and asset management. With global mobile connectivity capabilities, these devices offer a wide range of digital and analogue connections, customizable configuration options, Over-The-Air \(OTA\) updates, and parallel interface handling. Whether you need 3G or 4G LTE connectivity, the A11 series has you covered, and there are even Bluetooth variants available for hassle-free control over your fleet assets.

One standout feature of the Aplicom A11 devices is their easy device connectivity with Bluetooth. You can pair these devices with Bluetooth LE 5.0 beacons and sensors to enhance data collection or device identification. Bluetooth tags and beacons can be used to identify users, assets, or act as wireless sensors that provide measurement data and on/off-information. This opens up a range of adaptable use-cases and allows for seamless integration with other Bluetooth-enabled devices.

Another notable feature of the A11 series is its global roaming telematics capabilities. With LTE and BLE devices, you can enjoy global roaming and ensure data availability even in remote locations. The OTA functionality makes device configuration and updates a breeze, allowing your assets to continue their operations while the telematics unit takes care of any necessary updates automatically. This means you can focus on running your business without worrying about the technicalities of device management.
