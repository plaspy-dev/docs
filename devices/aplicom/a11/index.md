# Aplicom - A11

![A11](./logo..jpg)

The Aplicom A11 Series is a family of telematics devices designed for reliable fleet and asset management. Models in the A11 series offer global mobile connectivity options, a mix of digital and analogue connections, Bluetooth capabilities, and support for Over The Air updates. These characteristics make the A11 suitable for organizations that need consistent location visibility and adaptable device configuration across different vehicle and equipment types.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the A11 integrates with fleet management workflows to provide location and status visibility within the Plaspy platform. Its support for global roaming connectivity and Bluetooth pairing expands the kinds of data and asset identification that Plaspy can surface for operators, while OTA functionality helps keep device settings aligned with deployment policies without manual on site intervention.

## Key Highlights

- Global roaming capable telematics series suitable for broad geographic coverage
- Available with 3G or 4G LTE options and versions that include Bluetooth connectivity
- Support for Bluetooth LE 5.0 pairing with beacons and tags for enhanced identification
- Over The Air update functionality for remote device configuration and maintenance
- Flexible digital and analogue connections to support a variety of asset interfaces
- Designed for fleet and asset management use with durable telematics features

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Aplicom A11 can be connected to Plaspy to provide continuous fleet and asset visibility, allowing operators to monitor location, status, and asset associations from a central dashboard. Plaspy ingests the telematics and identification data the A11 provides so teams can act on movement, presence, and event information.

- Surface device location and movement on Plaspy maps for operational oversight
- Use Bluetooth tag and beacon data to identify drivers, assets, or cargo in Plaspy records
- Configure alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on events or presence signals from A11 devices
- Include A11 device data in Plaspy reports and exports for fleet performance analysis
- Manage device health and configuration workflows in Plaspy while leveraging A11 OTA capability

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle fleet tracking for daily routing and location visibility across regions
- Asset monitoring for trailers, equipment, and high value goods that move between sites
- Worker or asset identification using Bluetooth tags and beacons to link people to assets
- Remote sites and mobile assets where global roaming connectivity is required
- Large deployments that benefit from OTA updates to keep device configurations consistent

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Aplicom A11 series is a practical choice for organizations that need a flexible telematics unit with global connectivity and Bluetooth based identification options. When paired with Plaspy, the A11 helps create a centralized view of moving assets and connected tags, enabling better operational decisions without requiring frequent manual device visits.
