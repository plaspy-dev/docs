# Enfora - Mini MT

![Mini MT](./logo.gif)

The Enfora Mini MT is a certified quad-band GPS tracker that is specifically designed for today's demanding mobile workforce and private citizens who prioritize location-based safety, security, and connectivity. This compact device is optimized to provide complete GSM/GPRS communications, allowing you to track and monitor your workforce, small fleets, and even loved ones such as Alzheimer's patients, children, and teenagers on the go.

Equipped with next-generation technology, the Enfora Mini MT offers excellent GPS performance and sensitivity in a small and ruggedized voice-enabled platform. It is fully programmable and comes with a panic/emergency response button, two-way voice communications, messaging capabilities, and much more. The device is designed to be easily deployed and provisioned, making it ideal for the most demanding workforce tracking applications. Additionally, it features a motion sensor and vibrating alerts, making it suitable for personal and remote worker programs of all sizes.

Key Features:

- Quad-band GSM/GPRS \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\)
- Motion sensor and vibrating alerts
- Designed for personal/remote worker programs
- FOTA \(Firmware-Over-The-Air\) enabled for remote upgrades
- Rugged and voice enabled
- Programmable with panic/emergency response button

Recommended Use Cases:

- Personal & Remote Worker Tracking
- Emergency Response
- Child & Teen Tracking
- Elderly Locator
- Mobile/Home Healthcare
