## How Tytan SAT devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy acts as the telemetry platform that receives and displays location and sensor data from compatible tracking devices. Tytan SAT devices that are compatible with Plaspy send position updates and telemetry over their supported networks. When a device is configured to report to Plaspy using its device identifier and reporting settings, Plaspy can ingest the data and present it in maps, reports, and alerts.
