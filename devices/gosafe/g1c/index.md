# Gosafe - G1C

![G1C](./logo..jpg)

The Gosafe G1C is a small GPS tracker that offers an economical and full-featured solution for various automotive applications. Whether you need it for UBI, stolen vehicle recovery, BHPH, auto rental, motorbike, power sports, or any other related use, the G1C is designed to meet your needs. 

One of the standout features of the G1C is its connectivity options. It supports LTE CAT 1 and GPRS, providing worldwide connectivity for reliable tracking. Additionally, it features extremely sensitive GPS capabilities, ensuring accurate and precise location tracking. With internal antennas for both cellular and GPS, there's no need for wired antennas, making installation quick and hassle-free. 

In terms of functionality, the G1C offers a range of features to enhance your tracking experience. It supports crash data recording with a 100Hz recording rate, allowing you to gather detailed information in the event of an accident. It also supports driving behavior monitoring, giving you insights into how the vehicle is being driven. The G1C features an ignition detection feature, as well as a digital input for a panic button, adding an extra layer of security. 

With its compact and water-resistant design \(IP65 compliant\), the G1C can be mounted virtually anywhere in the vehicle, ensuring discreet and reliable tracking. It is the smallest fleet management GPS tracker available, making it ideal for applications where space is limited. Despite its small size, the G1C boasts low power consumption, ensuring long-lasting performance. 

Overall, the Gosafe G1C is a versatile and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers reliable tracking and monitoring capabilities for a wide range of automotive applications. Its compact size, easy installation, and comprehensive feature set make it an excellent choice for anyone in need of a small GPS tracker.
