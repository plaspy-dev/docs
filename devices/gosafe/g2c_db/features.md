# Gosafe - G2C-DB Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Gosafe G2C-DB tracker and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for fleet management, asset monitoring and anti-theft workflows. The content focuses on practical, non-sensitive feature context that helps planners and operators understand what the device contributes when paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer implementation for a given regional model. For device specific limits and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The G2C-DB is a rugged, long-life GPS tracker built for low-maintenance asset monitoring and Plaspy compatible deployments. It combines multi-constellation positioning, robust environmental protection, and flexible reporting modes so it can operate for long periods on a replaceable battery while still providing useful telemetry when needed.

- Replaceable high capacity battery pack designed for extended standby operation to reduce maintenance visits.
- Multi-constellation GNSS positioning for reliable location fixes in a variety of outdoor environments.
- Rugged IPX7 waterproofing and shock resilient enclosure for deployment on trailers, containers and remote equipment.
- Flexible reporting modes including periodic, movement-based and configurable active tracking to balance update frequency and battery life.
- Store-and-forward local memory to preserve recorded events when cellular coverage is intermittent.
- Cellular connectivity with SMS and TCP reporting plus over-the-air firmware updates for remote maintenance.

## Core Features of Gosafe - G2C-DB

- Long life replaceable battery pack designed for years of standby operation under conservative reporting schedules.
- Multi-constellation GNSS support including GPS and Beidou for consistent position fixes.
- Rugged, weatherproof enclosure rated to IPX7 with shock and vibration resilience for harsh environments.
- Flexible reporting modes: periodic intervals, movement-based triggers and configurable active tracking.
- On-board motion sensing via a 3D accelerometer to detect movement and related events.
- Local flash memory for store-and-forward buffering of roughly 1,000 records to protect against temporary connectivity loss.
- Cellular telemetry with support for GSM GPRS and LTE Cat-1 variants, plus SMS and TCP reporting channels.
- FOTA support for remote firmware updates and protocol maintenance when managed through an integration platform.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G2C-DB delivers location and device telemetry that Plaspy ingests for visibility, reporting and operational workflows. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and surfaces the device data alongside other fleet assets for consolidated monitoring.

- Location fixes and timestamps reported into Plaspy for mapping, history and geospatial visibility.
- Motion and shock events from the on-board accelerometer appear as event indicators for anti-theft monitoring and tamper detection.
- Store-and-forward memory ensures historical records are delivered to Plaspy once connectivity resumes, preserving event continuity.
- Battery state and device health telemetry help operators schedule maintenance and battery replacements in Plaspy dashboards.
- Flexible reporting modes allow Plaspy administrators to tune update frequency for each device to balance real-time needs and battery life.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long-term trailer and container tracking where infrequent reporting and long battery life reduce maintenance overhead.
- Remote equipment oversight for construction, rental and agriculture assets where movement-based alerts indicate use or tampering.
- Anti-theft workflows that rely on motion events, persistent logging and delayed upload to capture incidents in intermittent coverage areas.
- Periodic location audits for dispersed inventory in yards and depots where daily or weekly checks are sufficient.
- Low-maintenance asset programs that need reliable environmental protection and years of standby operation.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Exact reporting behavior and available modes depend on firmware version and manufacturer configuration; features may vary by device batch.
- Cellular banding and LTE Cat-1 availability vary by regional model and should be confirmed for the intended deployment location.
- Store-and-forward capacity and the number of buffered records are approximate and can be affected by firmware and record size.
- Installation method and antenna placement can influence GNSS reception and cellular performance.
- For the latest specifications, configuration options and firmware releases consult the official manufacturer documentation.
