# Gosafe - G6C

<div className="device-logo">
![G6C](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The G6C is a rugged, vehicle grade GPS tracker designed for fleet tracking service providers, systems integrators and enterprise customers that require reliable real time tracking and telematics. It combines a high sensitivity GNSS engine with quad band cellular connectivity and optional newer cellular variants, delivering accurate location, driving behavior data and on vehicle telemetry suitable for large scale deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G6C integrates its position and telemetry streams into Plaspy for unified fleet monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its buffering behavior, wide input set and over the air device management make the model a practical choice when you need continuous visibility and scalable device administration within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged vehicle grade design aimed at fleet and enterprise deployments for reliable in vehicle use
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver for accurate positioning and consistent location updates
- Quad band cellular connectivity with optional UMTS HSPA and LTE variants for broad network reach
- Built for large scale rollouts with compact hardware, low power characteristics and over the air device management
- Comprehensive I O and expandability including ignition sense, analog inputs and digital outputs for accessory control
- On board motion sensing and GSM jamming detection to support anti theft workflows and driving behavior alerts
- Buffered message storage to preserve telematics during temporary connectivity interruptions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the G6C streams location and telematics data over the cellular network so fleet operators can monitor vehicles in real time and review historical records. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates, motion events and input states from the G6C, enabling dashboards, rule based alerts and operational reporting for dispatch and security teams.

- Real time location and telemetry updates feed Plaspy dashboards for live fleet visibility
- Buffered messages are delivered to Plaspy after network recovery to preserve continuity of records
- Ignition and digital input events report vehicle status into Plaspy for routing and automation rules
- Analog input readings can be used by Plaspy to track fuel or other sensor levels and generate alerts
- Accelerometer events and geo fence breaches are correlated in Plaspy to support safety and compliance reports

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch with real time location, route oversight and driver behavior monitoring
- Anti theft protection using jamming detection, remote immobilization workflows and recovery procedures
- Usage based insurance and driver scoring by capturing motion events and ignition history for analytics
- Fuel and sensor telemetry integration using analog inputs for consumption tracking and alerts
- Driver identification and safety workflows using accessory inputs such as identification tokens and panic signals

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G6C is engineered for organizations that need durable, scalable vehicle tracking hardware that pairs naturally with Plaspy. Its focus on reliable GNSS performance, cellular versatility and remote device management helps reduce operational complexity when deploying at fleet scale. For teams using Plaspy, the G6C supplies the core telemetry—location, input state, motion events and buffered messaging—that enables timely alerts, historical reporting and operational oversight.
