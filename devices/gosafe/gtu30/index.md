# Gosafe - GTU30

![GTU30](./tracker.jpg)

The GTU30 Easy Install is a compact, low-cost GPS tracker purpose-built for fast installation and dependable vehicle telemetry. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the GTU30 delivers precise GNSS positioning and cellular connectivity \(LTE CAT1 + 2G\) so fleets and service operators can deploy real-time tracking and reporting with minimal downtime and wiring.

Ideal for light vehicle fleets, car rental and leasing, and insurance telematics, the GTU30 combines a small IP65-rated enclosure, simple two-pin battery mounting and a 32-channel GNSS receiver to balance installation speed and tracking accuracy. When integrated with Plaspy, this GPS tracker supports real-time tracking, crash and driving behavior telemetry, and straightforward integration into anti-theft and fleet management workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for quick, low-cost installation using a two-pin battery mount and multiple mounting options \(tie wrap, velcro, adhesive\).
- High-precision GNSS: 32-channel GPS + GLONASS + BeiDou with Assisted GPS and SBAS accuracy around 10 m CEP for reliable location in varied environments.
- Dual-mode cellular: LTE \(CAT1\) and 2G GSM support with SMS and TCP data modes for robust real-time tracking and fallbacks.
- Compact, rugged form factor \(78 x 33 x 15 mm, 30 g with battery\) and IP65 water resistance suited to automotive environments.
- On-board 3D accelerometer, trip detection and 50 Hz crash sampling enable driving behavior telemetry and event-driven reporting to Plaspy.
- Wide operating voltage \(9–90 V DC\) with a small 220 mAh Li-ion backup battery to preserve data and limited operation on power loss.
- Low-power operation with typical sleep current ~4 mA at 12 V and 29 mA during active tracking to limit battery drain when parked.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the GTU30 with Plaspy provides a turnkey path to real-time tracking and fleet telemetry. The device transmits GNSS fixes, accelerometer events and status using LTE CAT1 or 2G over TCP or SMS. Plaspy ingests that data to provide live maps, alerts, historical playback and reports that support fleet management, anti-theft response and insurance telematics programs.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates from GPS+GLONASS+BeiDou with Assisted GPS for fast fixes and reliable position history.
- Virtual ignition sensing and trip detection—Plaspy uses these signals for mileage, run-time, and driver scoring reports.
- Crash and harsh-event reporting \(3D accelerometer with 50 Hz sampling\) to trigger immediate alerts and post-incident analysis.
- Power-loss and backup operation—onboard Li-ion battery allows the device to report critical events when vehicle power is interrupted, aiding anti-theft workflows and recovery.
- Platform-level integration—Plaspy can combine GTU30 telemetry with external inputs \(CAN adapters, fuel sensors, immobilizer controls, or Bluetooth sensors managed at the platform\) to enable fuel monitoring and immobilizer workflows without changing the tracker hardware.
