# Gosafe - GTU30

<div className="device-logo">
![GTU30](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GTU30 Easy Install is a compact, low cost GPS tracker designed for fast installation and dependable vehicle telemetry. Built around a multi GNSS receiver and cellular connectivity, the GTU30 emphasizes quick deployment for light vehicles while providing continuous position reporting, event detection and a rugged IP65 enclosure suitable for automotive use.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the GTU30 can feed live location and event data into Plaspy for fleet monitoring, reporting and alerting. That compatibility makes the GTU30 a practical choice for operators who need to roll out tracking across rental, leasing or service fleets with minimal downtime and simple hardware handling.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for quick low cost installation with a compact IP65 rated enclosure and simple mounting options.
- Multi GNSS positioning using a 32 channel receiver for reliable location tracking and consistent position history.
- Dual cellular connectivity supporting LTE CAT1 and 2G for real time reporting and fallback coverage.
- Onboard accelerometer and trip detection to capture crash events and driving behavior for post event analysis.
- Internal backup battery and wide operating voltage to allow limited reporting during power interruptions.
- Small form factor and low power operation suitable for short term installs or densely packed fleet rollouts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the GTU30 transmits GNSS fixes, event data and status updates so Plaspy can present live maps, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to provide operational visibility across fleets, enabling managers to respond to incidents, audit trips and analyze vehicle activity.

- Real time location updates and position history displayed on Plaspy maps for monitoring and replay.
- Trip detection and virtual ignition signals used by Plaspy to calculate mileage, runtime and basic driver activity summaries.
- Crash and harsh event alerts surfaced in Plaspy from accelerometer data to support rapid incident response and investigation.
- Power loss notifications and battery backed reports help Plaspy alert teams to potential theft or tampering events.
- Platform level reporting and scheduled exports enable fleet managers to include GTU30 data in operational dashboards and compliance records.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rapid roll out of real time tracking for light vehicle fleets in rental, leasing and service operations.
- Insurance telematics for event logging, trip summaries and crash detection used in usage based programs.
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows that leverage battery backed reporting and platform alerts.
- Driver safety and coaching programs using trip detection and accelerometer driven harsh event analysis.
- Short term or seasonal asset installs where small size and quick mounting reduce installation overhead.
