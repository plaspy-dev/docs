# Gosafe - GTU300 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Gosafe GTU300 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet tracking, stolen vehicle recovery, and telematics workflows. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about what the device reports and how those signals translate into operational visibility inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and regional or manufacturer configurations. For device specific installation, firmware behavior, and technical parameters consult the manufacturer documentation for the most current details.

## Feature Overview

The GTU300 is a compact, vehicle grade tracker designed to deliver continuous location and telemetry to a fleet management platform. It combines multi GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity and multiple I O and serial interfaces to provide a flexible data source for Plaspy's tracking and reporting features.

- Global LTE Cat 1 connectivity with GPRS fallback for resilient data transport
- Multi GNSS positioning with Wi Fi fallback to improve location availability in urban areas
- BLE 4.2 support for nearby accessory and sensor pairing
- Vehicle oriented I O and serial interfaces including ignition sense, digital inputs and outputs, RS232 and RS485
- Onboard backup battery and wide vehicle voltage support for reliable operation during power events

## Core Features of Gosafe - GTU300

- Multi GNSS receiver with SBAS assisted positioning for improved accuracy in open sky
- LTE Cat 1 cellular modem with GPRS fallback and support for TCP UDP and SMS transports
- BLE 4.2 radio for wireless sensor and accessory integration
- Multiple physical interfaces including ignition sense, digital inputs, open collector outputs, RS232, RS485 and 1 Wire
- Internal backup battery and vehicle grade power input range for 12 V and 24 V systems
- OTA firmware update support and compatibility with device management platforms
- Compact vehicle mount form factor with standard connector options and accessory support

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The GTU300 streams location and event telemetry to Plaspy so fleet teams can monitor vehicles, review trip histories, and act on alerts. Plaspy ingests the tracker telemetry and exposes it through dashboards, maps, and event reports to support operational decisions.

- Real time location updates in Plaspy using GNSS fixes with Wi Fi fallback when available
- Ignition sense and digital input events appear as trip start stop and input state changes in Plaspy
- BLE paired sensors and accessory data can be forwarded into Plaspy for condition monitoring
- Serial and 1 Wire telemetry can be captured and surfaced as sensor values or custom events in Plaspy
- OTA updates and device management workflows help keep fleet firmware aligned with Plaspy expectations

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet tracking and route oversight for commercial vehicle fleets
- Stolen vehicle recovery and location history for security teams
- Usage based insurance and mileage tracking for insurance telematics deployments
- Rental and car sharing fleets using BLE accessories for check in check out and condition checks
- Remote telemetry and sensor monitoring for auxiliary vehicle systems
- Asset monitoring where compact form factor and wide voltage support are required

## Feature Availability Notes

- Certain features such as BLE sensor profiles, serial integrations, and fallback behaviors depend on firmware and may differ between hardware revisions
- Regional cellular band support and LTE Cat 1 availability vary by product variant and market
- I O pin functions and default behavior can change depending on installer configuration and wiring harness used
- Manufacturer device management platforms and OTA mechanisms may be required to enable some advanced features
- Always verify the exact feature set for your device serial number and firmware through the official manufacturer documentation
