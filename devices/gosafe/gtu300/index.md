# Gosafe - GTU300

![GTU300](./tracker.png)

The GTU300 from a proven telematics hardware family is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for professional fleet management, stolen vehicle recovery, and usage-based insurance deployments. With global LTE Cat‑1 connectivity, GPRS fallback, multi‑GNSS positioning, Wi‑Fi fallback and BLE 4.2, the GTU300 delivers reliable real-time tracking and rich telemetry to Plaspy for actionable fleet insights. Its compact form factor, wide vehicle voltage support and on-board backup battery make it ideal for rapid installation across cars, trucks and rental fleets.

The GTU300 integrates multiple I/O and serial interfaces—ignition sense, configurable digital inputs and outputs, RS232/RS485, 1‑Wire and an onboard accelerometer—enabling Plaspy to ingest vehicle telemetry, event data and sensor readings. Support for dual SIM/eSIM, TCP/UDP and SMS transports plus OTA firmware updates ensures robust connectivity and remote device management through Plaspy and supported device management platforms.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Global LTE Cat‑1 connectivity with GPRS fallback and TCP/UDP/SMS for resilient communications.
- High-sensitivity multi‑GNSS engine \(56 channels\) with SBAS accuracy down to 2.0 m and Wi‑Fi fallback for reliable position fixes.
- BLE 4.2 support for wireless sensors \(temperature, door, humidity\) and accessory pairing.
- Comprehensive I/O: ignition sense, 2 digital inputs, 3 open-collector outputs, RS232/RS485 and 1‑Wire for telemetry and peripheral control.
- Vehicle-grade power design \(9–40 VDC\), low-power sleep modes and internal 220 mAh Li‑ion backup battery to maintain reporting during power events.
- OTA firmware updates and support for device management platforms \(GEM event manager, GICUS\) for scalable maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GTU300 streams location and vehicle telemetry over LTE Cat‑1 or GPRS to deliver live tracking, geofence alerts, trip histories and event-driven reports. Plaspy consumes the GTU300’s telemetry—GPS/Wi‑Fi location, accelerometer events, ignition status and serial/1‑Wire sensor data—to power dashboards, alerts and integrations for fleet operations teams. Dual SIM/eSIM and multiple transport options \(TCP/UDP/SMS\) help maintain connectivity in variable network conditions, while OTA updates keep device firmware aligned with Plaspy features.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS + Wi‑Fi fallback\)
- Ignition sense and digital input events for trip start/stop and alarm monitoring
- Fuel monitoring and other sensor data via supported fuel sensors and external accessories
- Remote immobilizer capability via external relay integration using the device outputs
- BLE sensors and beacons for temperature, door, and proximity monitoring

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the GTU300 for Plaspy deployments delivers a balanced combination of robust connectivity, precise positioning and flexible I/O that telematics teams need. Its LTE Cat‑1 global connectivity with GPRS fallback minimizes data interruptions while dual SIM/eSIM support improves uptime. Multi‑GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi fallback and strong sensitivity ensures accurate real-time tracking even in urban canyons. The unit’s serial interfaces, 1‑Wire support and BLE 4.2 expand telemetry beyond location — enabling fuel monitoring, temperature sensing and driver/event data to flow into Plaspy for richer analytics.

Operationally, OTA firmware updates and compatibility with established device management platforms reduce maintenance overhead and scale deployments. The GTU300’s wide voltage range, compact form factor and accessory ecosystem \(RFID, iButton, relays, fuel sensors, external GPS antennas, SOS buttons\) make it straightforward to tailor installations for fleet management, anti‑theft, insurance telematics and rental operations. In short, GTU300 plus Plaspy provides reliable real-time tracking, telemetry and control to help fleets improve utilization, reduce risk and strengthen security.
