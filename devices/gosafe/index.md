Gosafe is a GPS tracking brand recognized for producing tracker hardware used in vehicle and asset monitoring. ## How Gosafe devices work with Plaspy

Gosafe tracking units collect location, movement, and status information and send that telemetry over cellular networks to a tracking platform. Plaspy can ingest data from compatible Gosafe devices so you can view location, configure alerts, and generate reports inside Plaspy.

Integration typically involves:
- Identifying the device in the Plaspy interface using its unique identifier.
- Configuring the device to forward data to Plaspy using the device specific settings documented on each model page.
- Verifying connectivity and telemetry appearance in Plaspy maps and reports.

For device specific connection parameters and step by step instructions, open the device page from the catalog. Those pages provide the technical details needed to pair a given Gosafe unit with Plaspy.

## Typical use cases for Gosafe GPS trackers

Gosafe devices are used in a variety of scenarios. Common applications include:
- Fleet tracking for day to day vehicle monitoring and route oversight
- Asset tracking for trailers, equipment, and non powered assets when paired with appropriate hardware
- Security and recovery for vehicles and high value items
- Mobile workforce visibility to monitor location and status of field teams

Plaspy provides the platform tools to operationalize these use cases, including live maps, geofencing, alerts, and historical playback.
