# Gosafe - G2P

![G2P](./logo.jpg)

The G2P Personal Tracker from Gosafe is a reliable and versatile device designed to provide peace of mind for families, caregivers, and employers. With its compact and lightweight design, the G2P is easy to carry and can be discreetly attached to personal belongings or worn as a pendant. This tracker is perfect for keeping an eye on loved ones, elderly individuals, and mobile workforce, allowing users to know their precise location at all times.

One of the standout features of the G2P is its user-friendly interface, which allows users to easily send a simple SMS command or log in to the tracking platform from any computer or smartphone. This makes it incredibly convenient to track the device and receive real-time updates on the location of the person or asset being monitored. Whether you're a concerned family member, a caregiver, or an employer, the G2P provides you with the peace of mind of knowing where your loved ones or employees are at all times.

Key Features:

- Compact and lightweight design
- Easy to attach or wear
- User-friendly interface
- Real-time location tracking
- Simple SMS commands
- Compatible with tracking platform
