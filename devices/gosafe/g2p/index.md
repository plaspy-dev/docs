# Gosafe - G2P

![G2P](./logo.jpg)

The Gosafe G2P is a compact personal tracker designed for simple, everyday location monitoring. It is lightweight and easy to carry or wear, making it suitable for tracking loved ones, elderly individuals, and mobile workers. The device emphasizes ease of use with a straightforward interface and the ability to send simple SMS commands or access tracking information from a web or mobile platform.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G2P can feed location data into Plaspy's fleet and asset management environment, providing centralized visibility and basic monitoring capabilities. Its simplicity and portable form factor make it a practical option for users who need consistent position updates without complex hardware requirements.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design suitable for carrying or wearing
- Easy to attach to personal belongings or wear as a pendant
- User friendly interface for straightforward operation
- Real time location tracking for continuous visibility
- Simple SMS commands for quick status checks
- Designed for families caregivers and employers monitoring mobile staff

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Gosafe G2P supplies location updates that Plaspy displays and organizes for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy serves as the central platform to view device positions, review recent history, and manage notifications, making it easier to maintain oversight of people or assets tracked by the G2P.

- Live map view in Plaspy to see current device location and movement
- Notifications and alerts configured in Plaspy for events such as movement or entry and exit of defined areas
- Location history and reporting to review past movements and generate simple reports
- Device grouping and user access controls in Plaspy for team or family management
- Use G2P SMS commands alongside Plaspy monitoring for quick checks or remote queries

## Typical Use Cases

- Keeping track of elderly family members for caregiver peace of mind
- Monitoring mobile workers or lone staff in small organizations
- Securing personal belongings that are frequently moved or transported
- Short range asset oversight where portability and simplicity are priorities
- Basic fleet or group tracking for small teams using compact personal devices

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G2P is a sensible choice when portability, ease of use, and straightforward location updates are the main priorities. Its lightweight design and SMS command capability make it accessible to users who prefer minimal setup and a device that can be carried or worn without inconvenience. Pairing the G2P with Plaspy adds centralized management, historical views, and notification options that improve operational oversight for families, caregivers, and small teams.
