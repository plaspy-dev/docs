# Gosafe - G1S

![G1S](./logo.jpg)

The Gosafe G1S is a portable GPS tracker built for flexible deployments. It supports either replaceable CR123 batteries or rechargeable batteries charged via an external connector, and its compact, durable design makes it suitable for both indoor and outdoor tracking. With a total replaceable battery capacity reported as 6000mAh, the G1S can achieve extremely long standby intervals when configured for low frequency reporting, which is useful for long term asset monitoring.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the G1S can feed location data into a central fleet and asset management platform for visualization and operational oversight. Plaspy users can use the G1S to add portable assets and low maintenance trackers into their monitoring workflows, taking advantage of the device design and battery options to balance reporting frequency and battery life.

## Key Highlights

- Portable and compact form factor suitable for a range of tracking scenarios
- Dual power options with replaceable CR123 batteries or rechargeable batteries
- Replaceable battery capacity of 6000mAh supports very long standby life with low frequency reporting
- Rechargeable option and external charging connector for flexible power management
- Durable construction suitable for indoor and outdoor use
- Well suited for long term or intermittent reporting deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the G1S provides location updates that integrate into fleet and asset views for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can display the device on maps, include its history in reports, and use its status updates to support operational decisions.

- Map visibility for live location and historical playback of G1S position reports
- Configurable alerts and notifications in Plaspy for movement or region entry and exit
- Scheduled reporting and low frequency updates to align with the G1S long battery life profile
- Aggregated reporting and exportable history for audits and operational analysis
- Grouping and tagging within Plaspy to organize G1S devices by fleet, asset type, or location

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset monitoring where infrequent reporting maximizes battery life
- Portable asset tracking for items that move between locations or vehicles
- Fleet tracking for small vehicles or trailers requiring occasional updates
- Personal item tracking such as luggage or equipment during transport
- Remote or low maintenance deployments where charging access is limited

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G1S is a practical option for organizations that need a simple, portable tracker with flexible power choices. Its replaceable battery option supports deployments where changing batteries periodically is preferred, while the rechargeable option allows for reuse and convenient charging. Paired with Plaspy, the G1S can be incorporated into fleet and asset workflows to provide map based visibility, alerting, and reporting without requiring continuous power.
