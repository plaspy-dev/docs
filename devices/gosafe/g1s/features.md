# Gosafe - G1S Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Gosafe G1S and how the device can be used with Plaspy. It is intended to describe practical, non sensitive capabilities you can expect when pairing this portable tracker with the Plaspy fleet and asset management platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on the device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For final details on firmware behavior, battery handling, charging methods, and other device specific information consult the official Gosafe documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Gosafe G1S is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for flexible power options and long deployment life. Its combination of replaceable battery support and rechargeable capability makes it useful for applications where ease of maintenance and long unattended operation are priorities.

- Portable design suitable for short term deployments and long term placement
- Dual power options with support for replaceable CR123 batteries or rechargeable cells
- High replaceable battery capacity option providing up to three years at a one message per day profile
- External power connector for convenient recharging when using rechargeable cells
- Practical for indoor and outdoor tracking scenarios where compact size and durability matter

## Core Features of Gosafe - G1S

- Portable form factor for discreet and flexible placement
- Support for replaceable CR123 batteries allowing long unattended deployments
- Option to use rechargeable batteries with an external charge connector
- Notable quoted battery capacity with replaceable cells enabling very low duty cycle operation
- Durable compact housing suitable for a variety of tracking contexts
- Designed to provide reliable GPS location reporting for asset and personnel monitoring
- Compatibility with third party tracking platforms including Plaspy

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides a central view and historical record of position messages sent by the G1S so teams can track assets, review movement history, and reduce maintenance needs with long life battery options.

- Plaspy receives periodic position messages and displays device locations on maps
- Long battery life options reduce frequency of device maintenance reflected in Plaspy device uptime
- Portable design and compact size make it simple to register and manage G1S units in Plaspy
- Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols automatically to simplify onboarding
- Historical location data and reporting frequency recorded in Plaspy help with planning battery replacement or recharge cycles

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term unattended asset tracking where infrequent reporting preserves battery life
- Temporary or ad hoc monitoring of valuables during transport or storage
- Field deployments and studies that require compact, portable location devices
- Monitoring equipment or property that is difficult to access regularly
- Short term monitoring of people or assets where portability and discreet placement are needed

## Feature Availability Notes

- Reported battery life assumes low reporting frequency and can vary with reporting interval, GPS fix time, and environmental conditions
- Firmware revisions and hardware variants can change feature behavior and available settings
- Deployment method and installation can affect reception and reporting reliability
- Regional variants or manufacturer updates may alter supported batteries or charging accessories
- Always verify specific feature lists and compatibility with the manufacturer for your device serial and firmware

Using the Gosafe G1S with Plaspy gives organizations a straightforward way to collect, visualize, and analyze position messages from a portable tracker designed for flexible power options. Plaspy helps translate the device value into operational oversight by centralizing location history, simplifying device management, and reducing maintenance burden for long term deployments.
