# Gosafe - G1RUS

![G1RUS](./tracker.png)

The G1RUS is a purpose-built GPS tracker designed for long unattended deployments on trailers, containers and high-value assets. Plaspy compatible out of the box, G1RUS combines an extremely low-power hardware platform with a rugged enclosure and flexible power options so fleet managers and asset owners can deploy reliable real-time tracking and telemetry solutions that run for months or years without maintenance.

With quad-band GSM communications and optional 3G/LTE variants, a 56-channel u‑blox GNSS engine, configurable digital I/O and an optional Bluetooth 5.0 module, G1RUS delivers the core data Plaspy needs for geofencing, route management, anti-theft alerts and telemetry-based workflows. Its replaceable CR123 battery option, rechargeable Li‑Po support and external power capability make it simple to match power strategy to each asset and to integrate fuel monitoring, ignition detection or immobilizer workflows in Plaspy when paired with the appropriate sensors or vehicle systems.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for extremely low power consumption—multi-year standby on replaceable CR123 batteries with daily reporting.
- Flexible power: use four CR123 replaceable batteries \(6,000 mAh total\), an optional 5,000 mAh Li‑Po pack, or external power \(8–60 VDC on LB variant\).
- Robust location performance from a 56‑channel u‑blox GNSS receiver with SBAS, GLONASS and assisted GPS for fast fixes.
- Multiple communications modes \(SMS, TCP, UDP\) and variants including optional 3G hexa‑band and LTE Cat‑1 to suit network availability and Plaspy integration needs.
- Rugged mechanical design with screw, bolt or magnetic shell mounting for covert or rapid installations on trailers and containers.
- Onboard telemetry: 3D accelerometer, configurable digital input/output and status LEDs for GPS, cellular and power diagnostics.
- Advanced features such as FOTA, conditional profile management, time & speed geofences and up to 128 waypoints for delivery management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

G1RUS transmits position and telemetry data in formats Plaspy accepts \(SMS, TCP, UDP\), enabling seamless real-time tracking and historical reporting. Plaspy reads GNSS fixes, accelerometer events and digital I/O statuses to generate live maps, alerts and fleet management reports. Conditional reporting and low-power profiles minimize cellular usage while preserving the device's long battery life.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS position, accelerometer-based movement events\)
- Low-power conditional reporting: time, speed and event-based profiles to optimize battery life
- Geofences and waypoints: time & speed based geofencing and up to 128 delivery waypoints for route and delivery management
- Device status and diagnostics: battery state \(replaceable or rechargeable\), power input detection \(LB variant\) and status LEDs for easier field checks
- Bluetooth sensors: optional Bluetooth 5.0 module allows integration of BLE sensors and beacons for temperature, door or proximity monitoring
- FOTA and remote provisioning: firmware updates over the air and conditional profile management for mass deployments

## Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking: long unattended operation and rugged mounting options for intermodal assets.
- Delivery and route management: use up to 128 waypoints and time/speed geofences to validate stops and optimize routes in Plaspy.
- Asset anti-theft and recovery: accelerometer events, movement alerts and Plaspy alerts enable fast response; pair with immobilizer workflows via external vehicle systems when required.
- Environmental or condition monitoring: optional Bluetooth sensors can report temperature, door/open status or cargo presence into Plaspy alongside GPS and accelerometer telemetry.
- Long-term telemetry for seasonal fleets or parked equipment: multi-year battery life options reduce maintenance and improve total cost of ownership.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

G1RUS is built for reliability, flexibility and long life — qualities that directly benefit Plaspy deployments that require unattended assets to remain connected and reportable for months or years. Its low-power electronics and multiple power options let you pick the right balance of maintenance cost and reporting cadence. With proven GNSS performance, rugged environmental ratings and enterprise features like FOTA and conditional profiles, G1RUS provides accurate GPS tracking, telemetry and status data that feed Plaspy’s real-time tracking, fleet management and anti-theft workflows.

While G1RUS does not include dedicated fuel probes or immobilizer hardware out of the box, its telemetry, configurable digital I/O and optional Bluetooth sensors make it straightforward to implement fuel monitoring, ignition detection and remote immobilization in Plaspy when paired with the appropriate external sensors or vehicle interfaces. For organizations that need durable, low-maintenance GPS trackers that integrate cleanly with Plaspy for real-time tracking, telemetry and asset protection, G1RUS is a practical and scalable choice.
