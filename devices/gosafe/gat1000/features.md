# Gosafe - GAT1000 Features

This page provides a public, feature-oriented overview of the Gosafe GAT1000 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for tracking and asset management. It focuses on user-facing functionality and practical value when the device is integrated into Plaspy deployments.

Exact feature availability for any given GAT1000 unit can vary by firmware revision, hardware batch, installation choices and the manufacturer's implementation. For device specific configuration and the latest technical details consult the official Gosafe documentation or product support channels.

## Feature Overview

The GAT1000 is a rugged asset tracker designed for heavy equipment, trailers and high value outdoor assets. It combines wideband LTE Cat‑1 connectivity with a sensitive multi GNSS receiver, industrial I/O and BLE sensor support to deliver persistent position reporting and telemetry for remote monitoring and security workflows.

- Real time location reporting suitable for fleet and asset visibility across wide areas
- Multi GNSS positioning with SBAS level accuracy for improved location precision in open sky
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and shock vibration ratings for outdoor and heavy equipment installations
- Internal rechargeable backup battery and wide input voltage support for continued operation during power interruptions
- Flexible I O and sensor support including ignition sense, analog inputs and open collector outputs for telemetry and control
- BLE support for local wireless sensors to extend environmental or door monitoring into Plaspy

## Core Features of Gosafe - GAT1000

- LTE Cat‑1 cellular connectivity with legacy GPRS fallback for persistent real time reporting
- 56‑channel multi GNSS receiver (GPS GLONASS Galileo BeiDou) with SBAS level accuracy to support precise location fixes
- Rugged, water resistant IP67 enclosure and industrial shock vibration ratings for outdoor asset deployments
- Internal 5000 mAh rechargeable backup battery plus wide 8–40 V DC input for vehicle and asset power environments
- Comprehensive I O including ignition sense, configurable digital inputs, analog ADC inputs and open collector outputs for telemetry and remote control
- BLE 4.2 support for pairing with wireless sensors and beacons to surface environmental data in Plaspy
- Low power modes designed to extend operation between power availability events

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GAT1000 reports position fixes and I O events into Plaspy’s tracking environment so teams can monitor assets, respond to events and analyze historical movement. Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols and supports TCP or UDP device reporting to the platform.

- Real time location and telemetry appear in Plaspy maps and dashboards for live situational awareness
- Ignition sense and digital input events map to vehicle on off status, door events or alarm signals in Plaspy
- Analog inputs enable fuel level and other sensor readings to be imported and graphed for anomaly detection and reporting
- BLE sensor readings can be surfaced in Plaspy to support temperature, humidity or door status monitoring alongside location
- Open collector outputs allow controlled relay workflows that Plaspy can trigger or display as part of anti theft or immobilization procedures
- Plaspy records historical routes and event timelines to support audits, reporting and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for construction equipment and heavy vehicles where rugged hardware and accurate positioning are required
- Trailer and container tracking with battery backup to maintain visibility when main power is disconnected
- Asset anti theft and recovery workflows using real time tracking plus remote relay control
- Fuel monitoring and telematics using analog inputs feeding Plaspy dashboards and alerts
- Cold chain or environmental monitoring via BLE temperature and humidity sensors paired with the tracker
- Remote equipment monitoring in harsh outdoor environments where IP67 protection and shock resistance matter
