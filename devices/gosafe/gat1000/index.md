# Gosafe - GAT1000

![GAT1000](./tracker.png)

The GAT1000 Asset Tracker from Gosafe is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for heavy equipment, trailers and high-value outdoor assets. Built around LTE Cat-1 wideband connectivity and a highly sensitive 56-channel multi‑GNSS engine, the GAT1000 delivers reliable real-time tracking and rich telemetry for fleet management, anti-theft protection and remote asset monitoring.

Designed for low power consumption and harsh environments, the GAT1000 combines an IP67 water-resistant enclosure, an internal 5000mAh rechargeable backup battery and flexible I/O for fuel monitoring, ignition sensing and Bluetooth sensors. When integrated with Plaspy, this tracker provides scalable location intelligence, actionable alerts and fleet-level reporting to drive uptime and security.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat‑1 connectivity for reliable global real-time tracking and telemetry.
- Multi‑GNSS engine \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BeiDou\) with SBAS accuracy to 2.0 m \(open sky\) and -162 dBm tracking sensitivity for strong location performance.
- Rugged IP67 water‑resistant enclosure and industrial shock/vibration ratings \(SAE J1455, MIL‑STD‑202G\) for heavy equipment and trailer use.
- Large internal 5000mAh Li‑ion backup battery plus 8–40V DC input for dependable operation on 12V/24V vehicles and assets.
- Comprehensive I/O: ignition sense, digital inputs, analog ADCs, open‑collector outputs and serial interfaces to support fuel monitoring, telemetry and immobilizer control via external relays.
- BLE 4.2 support for Bluetooth sensors and beacons to add door, temperature, humidity or other wireless sensing to Plaspy dashboards.
- Low sleep current \(400 µA\) and efficient active tracking \(120 mA @ 12V\) to extend service life between power interruptions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GAT1000 transmits location and telemetry to Plaspy using LTE Cat‑1 or fallback GPRS/GSM channels over TCP/UDP or SMS. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes, I/O events and sensor readings to provide real‑time tracking, configurable alerts and historical reports. Integration delivers actionable insights for fleet management, anti‑theft responses and preventive maintenance programs.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via LTE/GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) and SMS for persistent connectivity.
- Ignition sense and digital inputs report vehicle on/off status and door or alarm events to Plaspy in real time.
- Fuel monitoring and analog sensor inputs enable Plaspy to display fuel levels and detect anomalies for fuel monitoring workflows.
- Support for remote immobilizer functionality by controlling external relays through the device’s open-collector outputs when configured with Plaspy rules.
- Bluetooth sensors \(BLE 4.2\) extend Plaspy visibility to temperature, humidity and door sensors for cold-chain or security monitoring.
