# Gosafe - G6S

![G6S](./logo.jpg)

The Gosafe G6S is a powerful GPS tracker designed specifically for service providers, integrators, and enterprise customers. It is built to enhance mobile resources and improve dispatch systems, making it an ideal choice for fleet management, insurance telematics, vehicle location and recovery, and more. With its quad-band GSM/GPRS tracking capabilities, the G6S ensures reliable and accurate tracking of your vehicles.

One of the standout features of the G6S is its over-the-air device management and maintenance system. This system allows for easy programming, updates, and logistics management, saving you time and effort. Additionally, the G6S supports FOTA \(Firmware update over the air\), ensuring that your device is always up to date with the latest features and improvements.

When it comes to safety and security, the G6S has you covered. It features GSM jamming detection, which alerts you if any attempts are made to interfere with the tracker's signal. The device also supports up to 28 hardware-based geo-fences, allowing you to set up virtual boundaries and receive notifications when your vehicles enter or exit these areas. With the Gosafe G6S, you can have peace of mind knowing that your vehicles are protected.

Technical Specifications:

- Quad-band GSM/GPRS \(3G version also available\)
- Over-the-air device management and maintenance system
- FOTA \(Firmware update over the air\)
- GSM jamming detection
- Supports up to 28 hardware-based geo-fences
