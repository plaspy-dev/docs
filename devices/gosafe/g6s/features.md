# Gosafe - G6S Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Gosafe G6S and how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It is intended for service providers, integrators, and enterprise users who want to understand the practical functions of the G6S when integrated into Plaspy for fleet tracking, dispatch support, and operational monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary depending on device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information below is based on the device description and focuses on high level, non sensitive functional context; consult manufacturer documentation for device specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The Gosafe G6S is designed as a robust vehicle tracking unit for enterprise deployments, combining location tracking with remote management and basic anti interference detection. Its capabilities are focused on reliable position reporting, remote maintenance, and perimeter monitoring to support fleet operations and insurance telematics.

- Quad band GSM/GPRS tracking for wide network coverage with a 3G variant available
- Over the air device management and maintenance to simplify large deployments
- FOTA support for firmware updates delivered over the air
- GSM jamming detection to raise awareness of signal interference attempts
- Support for up to 28 hardware based geo fences for perimeter and route control

## Core Features of Gosafe - G6S

- Quad band GSM/GPRS communications with available 3G model options
- Over the air device management and maintenance for configuration and logistics
- FOTA firmware update capability to keep deployed units up to date
- GSM jamming detection that can trigger alerts when interference is detected
- Up to 28 hardware based geo fences for virtual boundary monitoring
- Designed for service providers, integrators, and enterprise fleet use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides a centralized platform to collect and present device reports from compatible trackers like the Gosafe G6S. When the G6S reports location, status, or events, Plaspy can surface that information for operational teams while automatically handling protocol detection for most tracker models.

- Real time and historical location visibility for assets reported by the G6S
- Geofence events created by the device can be reflected as entries in Plaspy event logs
- Alerts such as GSM jamming detection reported by the device are visible in Plaspy dashboards and notifications
- Device connection and reporting are handled by Plaspy with automatic protocol detection to simplify integration
- Device status and reported firmware or maintenance indicators can be recorded in Plaspy for fleet oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and dispatch support for service vehicle management
- Vehicle recovery and location monitoring for stolen or misplaced assets
- Insurance telematics scenarios where remote tracking and device updates are required
- Remote device maintenance and firmware rollouts across a large installed base
- Perimeter control and access monitoring using hardware geo fences
- Enterprise deployments where centralized monitoring and event history are required

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and behavior depend on device firmware version and manufacturer configuration
- Hardware revisions or regional model variants (for example GSM only versus 3G) can change available functions
- Installation type and wiring can affect which hardware features are active in a given deployment
- Manufacturer over the air systems are used for device maintenance and FOTA; Plaspy records device reports but does not replace manufacturer update procedures
- Always confirm details for your specific serial numbers and firmware with official manufacturer documentation
