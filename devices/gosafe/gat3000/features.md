# Gosafe - GAT3000 Features

This page provides a public-facing feature overview for the Gosafe GAT3000 and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for fleet and asset monitoring. It summarizes practical, non-sensitive details about the device, focusing on features relevant to Plaspy users and deployments.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and regional variants from the manufacturer. Use this page as a general guide and consult the manufacturer documentation for device-specific technical details and the most current specifications.

## Feature Overview

The GAT3000 is a solar-assisted, Plaspy compatible tracker designed for long-term outdoor assets such as trailers, containers, and heavy equipment. Its combination of extended battery capacity, rugged enclosure, multi-constellation GNSS, cellular connectivity, and Bluetooth sensor support is optimized for deployments where low maintenance and reliable location reporting matter.

- Solar-assisted power with a large rechargeable battery for extended standby and reduced maintenance intervals.
- Multi-constellation GNSS for accurate location fixes in open-sky conditions.
- LTE Cat-1 cellular connectivity with fallback and roaming options to maximize coverage.
- BLE 4.2 support to integrate environmental and door sensors for additional telemetry.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure for outdoor and anti-theft deployments.
- Over-the-air configuration and remote management to simplify fleet-wide updates.

## Core Features of Gosafe - GAT3000

- Solar-assisted power system with a high-capacity rechargeable battery designed for long standby and frequent reporting.
- Multi-constellation GNSS (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou) with SBAS support for improved positional accuracy.
- LTE Cat-1 cellular connectivity with fallback to GSM/GPRS and dual-SIM options where available to enhance coverage and roaming.
- IP67-rated enclosure suitable for outdoor and harsh environment installations.
- BLE 4.2 interface for integrating Bluetooth temperature, humidity, and door/open sensors.
- Onboard tamper or proximity detection and accelerometer-based motion events for anti-theft and tamper alerts.
- Configurable inputs and outputs to connect auxiliary sensors or digital event sources.
- Over-the-air configuration and remote firmware management to support scalable deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the GAT3000’s location and telemetry so teams can monitor assets in real time, review history, and receive alerts tied to device events. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and accepts device reports over common transport modes to present device state and events within the platform.

- Live and historical location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps for route review and position history.
- Tamper and movement alerts forwarded to Plaspy so operators can receive immediate notifications and investigate potential theft or intervention.
- BLE sensor readings (for supported sensors) surfaced in Plaspy dashboards and logs to monitor environmental conditions and door/open events.
- Device status and telemetry, including battery and connectivity indicators, available in Plaspy for operational oversight and maintenance planning.
- Remote configuration and firmware workflows managed through Plaspy to tune reporting intervals and behavior across a fleet.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking where solar recharge and long standby reduce the need for field maintenance.
- Heavy equipment monitoring combining GPS location with motion events for utilization and anti-theft workflows.
- Remote asset anti-theft protection using tamper detection, motion alerts, and centralized alarming in Plaspy.
- Environmental telemetry for sensitive cargo by pairing BLE temperature and humidity sensors to the tracker and ingesting readings into Plaspy reports.
- Fleet telemetry and remote diagnostics for distributed assets operating in areas with intermittent access to service.
