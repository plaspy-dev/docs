# Gosafe - GAT3000

![GAT3000](./tracker.png)

The GAT3000 Solar Powered Tracker is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for long-term outdoor asset monitoring. Built for trailers, containers, heavy equipment and other high-value assets, the GAT3000 combines a large 10000mAh rechargeable Li‑ion battery with an efficient 6 VDC solar panel to deliver extended standby time and frequent, reliable location updates for real-time tracking and fleet management.

With an IP67-rated enclosure, multi-constellation GNSS, LTE Cat-1 cellular connectivity and BLE 4.2 for Bluetooth sensors, the GAT3000 offers robust telemetry, anti-theft monitoring and simple integration with Plaspy for alerts, reports and remote device management. Its low power consumption and over-the-air configuration make it ideal for scalable deployments where uptime and data quality matter.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for long-term asset deployments and fleet management workflows.
- Solar-assisted operation with a 10000mAh rechargeable Li‑ion battery for extended standby and frequent reporting \(more than 15 messages/day in normal solar conditions\).
- High-accuracy, multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BeiDou\) with SBAS \(~2.0 m in open sky\) and excellent sensitivity \(-162 dBm\) for reliable location fixes.
- Global LTE Cat-1 cellular connectivity with GSM/GPRS fallback and dual-SIM options to maximize coverage and roaming reliability.
- Rugged IP67-rated enclosure and wide operating temperature range for outdoor anti-theft and asset-tracking use cases.
- BLE 4.2 support for Bluetooth sensors \(temperature, humidity, door/open sensors\) and an onboard proximity/tamper sensor to trigger anti-theft alerts.
- Low power consumption profiles \(deep sleep to active tracking\) and configurable I/Os for integration with external telemetry or immobilizer circuits where required.
- Over-the-air configuration and platform integration ready — supports remote firmware updates and device management workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GAT3000 transmits GNSS positions and telemetry to Plaspy using LTE Cat-1 \(with GSM/GPRS fallback\) over TCP/UDP or SMS. Plaspy ingests location, accelerometer events, tamper alerts and Bluetooth sensor readings so you can run real-time tracking, set geofence alarms, and generate fleet reports. Over-the-air configuration allows Plaspy administrators to tune reporting intervals, thresholds and I/O logic remotely.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: multi-constellation GNSS data is sent to Plaspy for live map tracking and history playback.
- Tamper and movement events: onboard proximity/tamper sensor and 3D accelerometer report dismantle or shock events as alerts to Plaspy.
- Bluetooth sensor integration: BLE 4.2 allows temperature, humidity and door/open sensors to relay environmental telemetry into Plaspy dashboards.
- I/O and external sensor telemetry: configurable inputs/outputs can transmit digital events or connect to auxiliary sensors — useful for condition monitoring and anti-theft workflows.
- Remote commands and updates: Plaspy can push configuration changes and leverage over-the-air firmware management linked to the device’s management interfaces.

## Use Cases

- Trailer, container and cargo tracking — long standby with solar recharge minimizes maintenance while feeding Plaspy real-time asset locations.
- Heavy equipment and machinery monitoring — combine GPS, accelerometer events and BLE sensors for utilization and anti-theft workflows.
- Remote asset anti-theft and tamper detection — tamper sensor and configurable I/Os enable immediate alerts and intervention actions through Plaspy.
- Environmental condition monitoring — pair BLE temperature and humidity sensors to track sensitive cargo and log telemetry into Plaspy reports.
- Fleet telemetry and remote diagnostics — send location and device status to Plaspy for centralized fleet management and preventive maintenance planning.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the GAT3000 with Plaspy delivers a practical path to reliable, solar-assisted real-time tracking and telemetry. The device’s rugged IP67 design, multi-constellation GNSS accuracy and LTE Cat-1 connectivity provide the foundation for accurate location services and fleet management at scale. Low-power modes and a large battery with solar support lower operating costs and reduce field service visits, while BLE 4.2 and configurable I/Os expand telemetry options — from temperature monitoring to door sensors and external integrations like immobilizers where wiring is available.

For operators focused on anti-theft protection, utilization insight and environmental telemetry, the GAT3000’s tamper detection, accelerometer events and BLE sensor support, combined with Plaspy’s alerting and reporting capabilities, produce actionable intelligence without complex on-site maintenance. Remote configuration and over-the-air updates keep a dispersed fleet current and manageable, making this GPS tracker a reliable, Plaspy compatible option for demanding asset-tracking programs.
