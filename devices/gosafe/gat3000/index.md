# Gosafe - GAT3000

![GAT3000](./tracker.png)

The GAT3000 Solar Powered Tracker is a rugged GPS tracking device designed for long term outdoor asset monitoring. It is built for trailers, containers, heavy equipment and other high value assets and pairs a large rechargeable battery with a solar panel to extend standby time and reduce maintenance frequency. The device is packaged in an IP67 rated enclosure and includes multi constellation GNSS and cellular connectivity to provide reliable location updates for real time tracking and fleet oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GAT3000 can feed location, event and environmental telemetry into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its low power profile and over the air configuration capabilities make it suitable for scalable deployments managed through Plaspy, allowing operators to keep dispersed assets visible and to tune reporting behavior remotely for operational needs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for long term outdoor asset deployments and fleet workflows.
- Solar assisted operation with a 10000mAh rechargeable battery and integrated solar panel for extended standby and regular reporting.
- Multi constellation GNSS for dependable location fixes and improved position accuracy in open sky conditions.
- Cellular connectivity designed for wide area coverage with options to maximize roaming and redundancy.
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure suitable for outdoor anti theft and asset tracking applications.
- Bluetooth support for wireless sensor integration and onboard tamper detection for anti theft alerts.
- Over the air configuration and remote device management to simplify large scale fleet operations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GAT3000 transmits periodic location and event data so operators can monitor assets in real time, receive alerts, and analyze historical movement. Plaspy ingests telemetry and presents it on maps and in reports, while administrators can adjust reporting behavior and thresholds to balance battery life and update frequency.

- Live location and history playback on Plaspy maps for situational awareness and route review.
- Tamper and movement alerts delivered to Plaspy for immediate notification and response.
- Environmental telemetry from wireless sensors relayed into Plaspy dashboards for condition monitoring.
- Configurable geofence and threshold alarms to trigger notifications and workflow actions in Plaspy.
- Remote configuration and firmware management workflows to keep deployed devices aligned with operational policies.
