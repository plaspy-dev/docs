# Gosafe - G602

![G602](./tracker.jpg)

The G602 Easy Install is a compact, two-wire GPS tracker purpose-built for rapid deployment and robust fleet telematics. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the G602 delivers high-sensitivity real-time tracking, crash telemetry and driver-behavior data in a device that installers can fit in under 10 minutes. Its LTE Cat-1 cellular core with GSM/GPRS fallback and TCP/UDP/SMS reporting make the G602 a reliable data source for fleet management, insurance telematics, dispatch and recovery workflows.

Designed for vehicle fleets, insurers and service providers who need rich telemetry without installation complexity, the G602 combines a 56-channel multi-GNSS engine, 3D accelerometer with up to 100 Hz crash recording, BLE 4.2 for external sensors, and flexible over-the-air firmware updates \(FOTA\). As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, it integrates seamlessly with Plaspy’s platform to provide location, event and sensor data for monitoring, alerts and analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Fast two-wire, plug-and-play installation — fit most vehicles in under 10 minutes to scale large deployments quickly.
- Plaspy compatible for reliable real-time tracking and telemetry delivery via TCP/UDP/SMS.
- High-sensitivity multi-GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BeiDou\) with SBAS, internal patch antenna and ~2 m open-sky accuracy.
- Crash-ready telemetry: 3D accelerometer, 100 Hz crash data recording and driving-behavior analytics.
- BLE 4.2 support for external Bluetooth sensors \(temperature, door, humidity\) to extend telemetry beyond GPS.
- Wide vehicle voltage support \(8–40 V DC\) with configurable low-power modes for reliable operation in 12 V and 24 V systems.
- Flexible connectivity: worldwide LTE Cat-1 with GPRS Class 10 fallback, internal SIM access and multiple communication options \(TCP/UDP/SMS\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

The G602 streams position, event and sensor data directly to Plaspy using standard TCP/UDP or SMS transports. Plaspy ingests high-frequency telemetry and crash events to provide near real-time tracking, alerts and historical reporting. Installation and configuration are straightforward: the two-wire connection provides power and a virtual ignition input, while the Plaspy platform maps incoming data to vehicle records, geofences and alert rules.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via TCP/UDP or SMS.
- High-rate crash and impact reporting \(up to 100 Hz\) for instant incident detection and insurer workflows.
- Virtual ignition detection for engine on/off state without dedicated ignition wiring.
- Hardware-based geo-fences \(up to 200\) with time/day/speed rules that trigger Plaspy alerts and reports.
- BLE 4.2 sensors connect to the device and forward sensor readings to Plaspy for temperature, door or humidity monitoring.
- Wi‑Fi-assisted location helps Plaspy refine position in urban or indoor-edge scenarios.
- FOTA support enables remote firmware updates coordinated through your Plaspy-managed fleet.
