# Gosafe - G797

<div className="device-logo">
![G797](./tracker.png)
</div>

The G797 is a compact plug and play OBD2 telematics device from Gosafe designed for fleet management, insurance telematics, and rental or leasing programs. It connects to a vehicle J1962 OBDII port to provide GNSS location together with vehicle diagnostic data and inertial event sensing. The G797 is described as a unit that combines a Ublox GNSS engine with on board accelerometry and optional inertial sensors to support event reconstruction, driver monitoring and multi source odometer accumulation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G797 can stream GNSS positions, OBD derived telemetry and accelerometer events into Plaspy for real time monitoring, reporting and alerts. Its plug and play form factor, remote configuration options and cellular variants make it a practical choice for organizations that want to combine location visibility and vehicle diagnostics inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD2 installation providing vehicle VIN extraction and diagnostic telemetry for fleet and insurance workflows.
- GNSS positioning based on a Ublox engine to support accurate location tracking and historical replay.
- Built in self calibrating accelerometer with optional expanded inertial sensor suite for event detection and FNOL workflows.
- OBD derived odometer and diagnostic values that complement GNSS for mileage, maintenance and usage reporting.
- Flexible cellular connectivity with GPRS EDGE and optional newer cellular variants to suit regional deployments.
- Remote provisioning and firmware update capability to reduce vehicle downtime during large fleet rollouts.
- Rugged design intended for vehicle environments to support fleet operations and rental programs.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the G797 supplies location, diagnostic and event streams that feed live tracking, telematics dashboards and automated alerts. Plaspy ingests the device data to provide operational oversight, reporting and incident review across fleets or insurance portfolios.

- Real time GNSS location and telemetry updates for live fleet visibility and historical route replay.
- VIN and OBD diagnostic values delivered to Plaspy to support vehicle identification, maintenance planning and usage analytics.
- Combined odometer inputs from OBD and GNSS to improve mileage accuracy for billing and lease reconciliation.
- Accelerometer and optional inertial event data for FNOL alerts and incident reconstruction inside Plaspy.
- Remote configuration and firmware management to streamline deployments and reduce time in the field.
- Optional accessory support such as Bluetooth where enabled, allowing additional sensor data to be associated with Plaspy records.

## Typical Use Cases

- Usage based insurance and telematics where driving behavior, crash events and GPS traces inform underwriting and FNOL.
- Large fleet management requiring real time location, driver monitoring and consolidated vehicle diagnostics.
- Rental and leasing operations needing reliable mileage reporting, VIN based asset identification and remote visibility.
- Safety and incident reconstruction workflows that leverage accelerometer and inertial data for event review.
- Deployments that favor plug and play OBD2 provisioning for rapid rollout and centralized management.

The G797 pairs vehicle level diagnostics and GNSS positioning in a single, easy to deploy device, making it well suited for organizations that need both location visibility and in vehicle telemetry. Its direct OBD connection provides VIN and diagnostic sources that enhance asset identification and mileage accuracy when combined with GNSS data in Plaspy.
