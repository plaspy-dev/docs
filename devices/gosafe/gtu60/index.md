# Gosafe - GTU60

![GTU60](./tracker.webp)

The GTU60 from a proven OEM is an economical, full-featured GPS tracker designed for fast installation and reliable Plaspy compatible integration. Built for light commercial fleet tracking, rental and BHPH operations, and stolen vehicle recovery, the GTU60 combines LTE CAT1 and 2G connectivity with a high-sensitivity 32-channel GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou receiver and AGPS to deliver consistent real-time tracking and telemetry to Plaspy’s platform.

Compact and tamper-resistant, the GTU60 features an IP65 water-resistant enclosure, internal antennas and a wide input voltage range for both 12 V and 24 V vehicle systems. Its low-power design, onboard crash-capable accelerometer and simple I/O make it a practical choice for fleet management, anti-theft workflows and remote immobilizer control when integrated through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking: LTE/GPRS data and TCP/SMS reporting for continuous location and telemetry updates.
- High-sensitivity GNSS: 32-channel GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou receiver with SBAS accuracy \(~10 m CEP\) and tracking sensitivity down to −162 dBm.
- Vehicle-grade power and backup: Wide 9–90 V DC input, suitable for 12 V and 24 V systems, plus a 220 mAh rechargeable Li‑ion backup battery.
- Low power consumption: Sleep current ≈4 mA at 12 V and ~29 mA during active tracking for extended vehicle uptime and reduced parasitic drain.
- Anti-theft and immobilizer support: 1-wire ignition sense and an open-drain digital output for relay-controlled starter cut or immobilization workflows.
- Crash and motion telemetry: 3D accelerometer records crash events at 50 Hz for accident logging and automated alerts through Plaspy.
- Tamper-resistant, compact form factor: Internal SIM and antennas, IP65 water-resistant design, and flexible mounting options for discreet installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed in a vehicle, the GTU60 transmits location and telemetry to Plaspy using its LTE \(CAT1\) or 2G connection via TCP or SMS. Plaspy ingests the device’s GNSS position, accelerometer events and I/O status to provide real-time tracking, alarm routing and historical reports. Integration uses standard telematics feeds, so fleet managers can enable live maps, geofence alerts, trip reports and automated anti-theft responses without complicated configuration.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over LTE/GPRS \(TCP\) or SMS for redundancy.
- Ignition sense \(1-wire\) reported to Plaspy for runtime, driver scoring and unauthorized start alerts.
- Crash detection and impact logging via the 3D accelerometer, enabling automated incident notifications.
- Remote immobilizer capability using the open-drain digital output with relay modules \(starter cut integration supported through accessories\).
- Fuel monitoring and temperature sensor data available through supported aftermarket sensors and serial/1-wire integrations; Plaspy can map these inputs into fuel monitoring and telemetry dashboards.
