# Gosafe - G737

<div className="device-logo">
![G737](./tracker.png)
</div>

The G737 Ankle Bracelet by Gosafe is a purpose built GPS tracker designed for continuous electronic monitoring of parolees and offenders. It pairs a high precision 56 channel uBlox GNSS engine with SBAS support, LTE CAT 1 cellular connectivity, a tamper evident fiber optic strap and BLE home beacon capability to provide real time location, tamper alerts and event telemetry suitable for corrections and community supervision programs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G737 delivers the location fixes, tamper and zone events, and recorded history that Plaspy needs to power live maps, alerts and compliance reporting. Its edge recording, geofence enforcement and FOTA support make it a practical option for agencies and supervisors who require centralized visibility, automated notifications and reliable audit trails in a managed monitoring platform.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built ankle bracelet for continuous offender and parolee monitoring with strong event logging
- High accuracy positioning using a 56 channel uBlox GNSS engine with SBAS support for improved fixes
- Tamper evident fiber optic strap that reports cuts and interference as tamper events
- LTE CAT 1 cellular communications with fallback options and onboard edge recording for coverage gaps
- BLE home beacon support to improve home zone verification and enable local Bluetooth workflows
- Rugged, waterproof design and extensive onboard storage to support long term field use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G737 supplies continuous location and event streams that feed monitoring dashboards, alert engines and reporting modules. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry to provide supervisors with live situational awareness, history playback and compliance records while preserving device records during temporary connectivity interruptions.

- Real time location updates and historical playback visible within Plaspy using cellular delivered fixes
- Immediate tamper and strap interference alerts routed to Plaspy for follow up and case management
- BLE home beacon events and geofence transitions used by Plaspy to validate home zone and curfew compliance
- SOS, motion and other event signals presented as actionable alerts and incident entries in Plaspy
- Onboard edge recording uploaded to Plaspy after coverage gaps to maintain a continuous audit trail
- FOTA capability allowing device firmware updates to be managed without returning units to the field

## Typical Use Cases

- Parole and probation monitoring with tamper alerts and location history for case managers
- House arrest and home confinement verification using BLE home beacon and geofence checks
- Curfew enforcement and pre trial release supervision with automated zone rules and timestamps
- Rapid welfare checks and emergency escalation using SOS and motion event reporting
- Scaled monitoring programs that require centralized reporting, audit trails and remote management
