# Gosafe - G3S

![G3S](./logo.jpg)

The Gosafe G3S is a low cost GPS tracker designed for service providers, integrators, and enterprise customers. Built to enhance mobile resources and improve dispatch systems, the G3S provides real time tracking and monitoring with accurate location information. Its compact and durable design makes it suitable for a wide range of vehicle applications where continuous position visibility is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G3S can feed location data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Compatibility with Plaspy allows businesses to consolidate tracking data, streamline dispatch workflows, and use Plaspy reporting and visibility features alongside the device's real time tracking capabilities.

## Key Highlights

- Affordable GPS tracking solution targeted at service providers and enterprise customers
- Real time tracking and monitoring to support active fleet oversight
- Accurate location information for vehicle position and recovery tasks
- Compact and durable form factor suitable for vehicle installation
- Quad band GSM GPRS connectivity for broad cellular coverage
- Applicable for fleet management, dispatch, insurance telematics, and recovery

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the G3S supplies location updates that can be displayed and managed inside the platform, enabling centralized monitoring and reporting. Plaspy ingests the device's position data to provide operational context and tools that support day to day fleet activities.

- Live map visibility for tracking vehicle positions in real time
- Fleet monitoring and status overview to improve dispatch decisions
- Alerts and notifications configured in Plaspy based on movement or location changes
- Historical reporting to analyze routes, utilization, and operational trends
- Integration into Plaspy workflows for centralized management of assets

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium sized service fleets
- Dispatch optimization for field service and delivery operations
- Vehicle location and recovery support for asset protection
- Insurance telematics and usage monitoring scenarios
- General mobile resource tracking for enterprise operations
