# Gosafe - G616

![G616](./tracker.jpg)

The G616 rugged GPS tracker is a professional-grade telematics device engineered for enterprise fleet management and Plaspy compatible integrations. Designed for reliable real-time tracking, accident reconstruction, and insurance telematics, the G616 combines multi‑GNSS precision, LTE Cat‑1 and 2G worldwide connectivity, high-rate crash data capture and a rich set of vehicle I/O to meet demanding dispatch, recovery and fleet analytics requirements.

The G616 is ideal for service providers and integrators who need a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that delivers telemetry, ignition monitoring and extensible sensor support \(including Bluetooth sensors and fuel sensors\). Its rugged electrical and mechanical design, FOTA capability and internal antenna options make installation and remote management straightforward across large vehicle fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat‑1 connectivity and GSM/GPRS fallback for reliable global coverage and real-time tracking.
- Multi‑GNSS engine \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BeiDou\) with SBAS accuracy down to 2.0 m in open sky for precise vehicle location and route verification.
- High-resolution crash and driving data capture: up to 100 Hz crash sensing combined with GPS updates at 4 Hz for accurate accident reconstruction and driver behavior analysis.
- Extensive vehicle I/O — ignition sense, analog and digital inputs, open‑drain outputs and a latched output — enabling telemetry, immobilizer implementations and custom integrations.
- BLE 4.2 support for wireless sensors and accessories \(temperature, asset tags\), plus RS232 and 1‑Wire® for driver ID and peripheral connectivity.
- Low power design for 12V/24V vehicle systems with optional internal LI‑PO backup battery and very low sleep currents to preserve vehicle battery.
- FOTA \(firmware over‑the‑air\) updates and onboard storage for scalable remote management and firmware maintenance across a fleet.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G616 streams location, telemetry and event data using TCP/UDP or SMS data modes, enabling real-time tracking, historical playback and configurable alerts. Plaspy reads high-frequency telemetry and crash logs to build driver profiles, trigger geofence alerts and produce compliance or insurance reports. The device’s flexible I/O and BLE support allow Plaspy to ingest ignition status, sensor data and remote control events for actionable fleet insights.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via LTE Cat‑1 \(with GSM/GPRS fallback\) to Plaspy for live map tracking and route replay.
- Ignition status and input monitoring \(ignition‑sense line and configurable digital/analog inputs\) for accurate engine run-time and route segmentation.
- Fuel monitoring and sensor telemetry when paired with compatible fuel sensors; Plaspy displays fuel trends and alerts for consumption anomalies.
- Remote immobilizer capability using configurable outputs and a latched output, enabling Plaspy to issue immobilize/unlock commands through vehicle wiring.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons \(BLE 4.2\) for temperature, door/movement detection or driver presence; Plaspy integrates BLE telemetry into fleet dashboards.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch: real‑time tracking, route optimization and telemetry feeds for large vehicle fleets using Plaspy dashboards.
- Anti‑theft and vehicle recovery: live location, configurable geofence alerts \(up to 200 hardware geofences\) and remote immobilizer control via outputs.
- Insurance telematics and accident reconstruction: high‑rate crash data \(100 Hz\) with GPS 4 Hz updates for precise event timelines and driving behavior scoring.
- Fuel monitoring and preventive maintenance: integrate fuel sensors and analog inputs to Plaspy for consumption monitoring and engine‑run diagnostics.
- Driver ID and auxiliary sensors: 1‑Wire® driver ID, BLE sensors and RS232 peripherals for seat‑level or cargo monitoring and validated driver logs.
