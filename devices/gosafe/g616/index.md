# Gosafe - G616

<div className="device-logo">
![G616](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Gosafe G616 is a rugged, professional grade GPS tracker built for enterprise telematics and fleet management. It is designed to provide reliable real time tracking, high rate crash data capture for accident reconstruction, and comprehensive vehicle telemetry. With multi GNSS positioning and cellular connectivity, the G616 targets demanding dispatch, recovery and analytics workflows where precise location and event data are required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G616 can stream location, telemetry and event information into Plaspy to support live map tracking, historical playback and fleet reporting. Its ignition monitoring, extensible sensor support and flexible I O options make the device suitable for integrators and service providers who need to feed vehicle data into Plaspy for operational oversight, safety analysis and fleet optimization.

## Key Highlights

- Professional grade rugged tracker aimed at enterprise fleet management and insurance telematics.
- Multi GNSS positioning for improved location precision and reliable route verification.
- High rate crash sensing and frequent GPS updates to support accident reconstruction and driver behavior analysis.
- Wide vehicle I O support for ignition monitoring, analog and digital inputs, and outputs for remote control functions.
- Wireless sensor compatibility and peripheral connectivity for fuel sensors and driver identification.
- Built for fleet scale with remote firmware updates and onboard storage to handle intermittent connectivity.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G616 provides continuous visibility into vehicle position, events and sensor telemetry so operations teams can monitor fleets and respond to incidents. Plaspy ingests the device data to produce alerts, reports and historical timelines that support dispatch, compliance and safety programs.

- Real time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy for live map tracking and route replay.
- Ignition and input monitoring to segment trips, calculate engine run time, and improve duty reporting.
- Sensor and fuel telemetry integration so Plaspy can display consumption trends and trigger alerts for anomalies.
- Remote control support via outputs to enable immobilizer workflows or other vehicle interventions through Plaspy.
- High rate event logs used by Plaspy for crash reconstruction, driver scoring and incident reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch for service and delivery fleets requiring live tracking and historical playback.
- Anti theft and vehicle recovery with geofence alerts and remote output control.
- Insurance telematics and accident reconstruction using high frequency crash data and GPS timelines.
- Fuel monitoring and preventive maintenance programs that combine analog sensor data with vehicle usage.
- Driver identification and auxiliary sensor monitoring for validated driver logs and cargo or temperature tracking.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G616 is a fit for organizations that need a rugged, extensible tracker that can feed high fidelity telemetry into a fleet platform like Plaspy. Its combination of precise positioning, crash grade sensing and flexible I O makes it useful where safety, recovery and analytics matter. For integrators and operators managing large vehicle fleets, FOTA support and onboard storage help minimize manual intervention while maintaining data continuity.
