# Xexun - U02 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Xexun U02 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about the U02 as a Plaspy compatible wearable for precise indoor and short range positioning, tamper detection, and personnel telemetry.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, anchor and installation design, and manufacturer implementation. For the latest device specific details consult the official Xexun documentation and your deployment integrator before finalizing operational plans.

## Feature Overview

The U02 is a professional UWB positioning watch designed to provide high precision indoor location and personnel telemetry where GPS is limited. It is intended for secure institutional environments and integrates with Plaspy to deliver unified situational awareness alongside outdoor GPS trackers.

- Centimetre level UWB positioning for precise indoor and short range outdoor locating where GPS is unreliable.
- Built in 2.4 GHz RFID two way messaging to communicate with anchors and support anchor assisted workflows.
- Conductive strap anti tamper loop that triggers immediate tamper alerts if the strap is cut or removed.
- Integrated motion and heart rate sensors plus vibration alerts and a touch button emergency alarm for personnel monitoring.
- Rugged wearable design with IP68 durability, long standby battery behavior, and remote firmware upgrade support.
- Designed to complement Plaspy managed GPS trackers for unified indoor and outdoor location visibility.

## Core Features of Xexun - U02

- Ultra Wideband UWB ranging and anchor assisted positioning for sub meter to centimetre level fixes where anchors are deployed.
- 2.4 GHz RFID module enabling two way messaging between the watch and anchor network.
- Conductive strap loop for active anti tamper detection and immediate alerting.
- Integrated motion sensor and heart rate sensor for live personnel telemetry and event detection.
- Touch button emergency alarm and vibration alerts for on device signaling and rapid incident signaling.
- IP68 rated enclosure and wearable wristwatch form factor intended for continuous operations.
- Remote firmware upgrade capability to keep device behavior current with deployment needs.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the U02 augments platform visibility by providing high resolution indoor locations and personnel events in a way that complements outdoor GPS devices. Plaspy consolidates U02 location fixes and sensor events with other telemetry to create a single operational view.

- Real time UWB derived positions and sensor data appear in Plaspy maps for live monitoring and historical playback.
- Tamper events from the conductive strap and touch button emergency alarms are surfaced as immediate alerts for operators.
- Heart rate and motion telemetry are uploaded and logged so Plaspy can present personnel health and activity trends.
- Two way messaging via the RFID system supports simple system messages from the platform to the watch where the anchor network and device support it.
- U02 location events complement GPS tracker telemetry in Plaspy, giving precise indoor fixes next to wide area vehicle or asset data.

## Typical Use Cases

- Correctional facility monitoring and anti tamper security where strap removal must trigger rapid response.
- Staff safety and well being tracking in hospitals and industrial sites using location plus heart rate and motion telemetry.
- Patrol, inspection, and checkpoint verification workflows that need centimetre level indoor positioning.
- School and institutional deployments for managed wearable tracking and fast location during incidents.
- Short range asset or personnel locating in buildings and compounds where GPS coverage is poor.

## Feature Availability Notes

- UWB accuracy and range depend on anchor density, placement, and environmental conditions; anchor system design affects performance.
- Specific sensor behavior, alarm thresholds, and messaging functions can differ by firmware and hardware revision.
- Some features such as optional interfaces or charging methods may vary by model variant or regional configuration.
- Integration behavior with Plaspy requires appropriate backend and anchor system forwarding so location and events reach the platform.
- Always confirm device firmware capabilities and regional regulatory compliance through the manufacturer documentation.
