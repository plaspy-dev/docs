# Xexun - TK-103

<div className="device-logo">
![TK-103](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Xexun TK-103 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for a range of vehicle and equipment tracking scenarios. Commonly deployed for private cars, vehicle leasing fleets, outdoor mechanical equipment anti theft tracking, and corporate vehicle management, the TK-103 provides continuous location reporting and a suite of monitoring features. Notable device capabilities include immediate inquiry location, scheduled time tracking, address SMS inquiries, historical track playback, electronic fence functionality, and multiple alarm types to notify of abnormal events.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the TK-103 can feed location updates and event notifications into Plaspy for centralized fleet visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can present the tracker data on maps, incorporate alarms into alerting workflows, and retain historical tracks for reporting and review. This makes the TK-103 a practical option for organizations that want to combine a feature rich GPS unit with Plaspy's fleet monitoring and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Supports real time location queries and scheduled time tracking for ongoing visibility
- Multiple alarm types including SOS, speed, vibration, and power related alerts for asset protection
- Historical track playback and electronic fence features for incident review and perimeter monitoring
- Address SMS inquiry and GSM base station location support for flexible positioning and location lookup
- Remote monitoring and remote upgrade capability to help manage devices over time
- Dual SIM card support for improved network availability and optional SD card for local storage
- Optional remote power off of fuel supply for additional control in theft response scenarios

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK-103 supplies location and event data that Plaspy uses to create actionable operational views. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s position updates and alarms so fleet managers can monitor units in real time and analyze historical behavior.

- Display live vehicle and equipment positions on Plaspy maps for centralized visibility
- Forward device alarms to Plaspy so users receive alerts for SOS, geofence breaches, speed events, and power issues
- Replay historical tracks within Plaspy for post event analysis and route verification
- Use geofence events to trigger notifications and reports for compliance or security monitoring
- Consolidate multiple TK-103 devices in Plaspy for fleet level reporting, status overviews, and operational insights

## Typical Use Cases

- Private vehicle tracking for owner location queries and theft deterrence
- Vehicle leasing and rental fleets requiring occupancy and movement oversight
- Corporate fleet management to monitor routes, generate reports, and respond to incidents
- Outdoor mechanical equipment protection on worksites or remote locations
- Logistics and delivery monitoring where position history and alerts support operations
