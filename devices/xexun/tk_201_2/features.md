# Xexun - TK-201-2 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Xexun TK-201-2 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing capabilities and practical behavior you can expect when the device is integrated with the Plaspy platform, and how those capabilities can support everyday monitoring and location awareness.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Users should consider those factors when planning deployments and confirm device-specific details with manufacturer documentation and on-device configuration where applicable.

## Feature Overview

The TK-201-2 is a compact, cost-effective tracker designed primarily for pet tracking but applicable to broader personal and light commercial tracking scenarios. It provides configurable reporting behavior, basic alerts, and historical position review designed to help owners and operators keep track of a person or animal over time.

- Periodic location updates using configurable time interval reporting for regular tracking
- Distance-based reporting that sends updates when a movement threshold is reached
- Device identification via GSM ID tracking to manage multiple units
- Movement alert to notify when motion is detected after inactivity
- Overspeed alert and low battery alert to support safety and maintenance awareness

## Core Features of Xexun - TK-201-2

- Track by time interval for scheduled location reporting
- Track by distance interval to reduce reports when stationary and increase when moving
- GSM ID tracking for straightforward identification of individual devices
- Movement alert to indicate when the device starts moving
- Overspeed alert to flag when reported speed exceeds a configured limit
- Low battery alert to warn of declining power and need for recharge or replacement
- History trace checking for reviewing past positions and movement paths
- Geo-fence alarm to trigger alerts when the device enters or leaves a defined area

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK-201-2's reporting and alerts become visible within the platform to support monitoring and response workflows. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and presents incoming data as positions, events, and history that can be reviewed by operators.

- Time and distance based reports appear as location points on the map with timestamps
- Movement, overspeed, low battery, and geo-fence events are surfaced as alerts or events in Plaspy for operator attention
- History-trace checking is available through Plaspy map playback and timeline review to analyze past movement
- GSM ID and device identifiers help keep multiple TK-201-2 units organized in device lists and reports
- Alerts and location updates enable basic operational oversight for personnel, pets, or assets being tracked

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet tracking and recovery for lost or wandering animals
- Monitoring children, elderly family members, or individuals who need location oversight
- Managing outworkers and lone workers for location visibility during shifts
- Security and discreet recovery operations where a small tracker is required
- Business travel oversight for staff safety when operating in the field

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and exact behavior depend on the device firmware version provided by Xexun
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may change supported functions or default settings
- Installation method and how the tracker is mounted can affect movement detection and reporting
- Manufacturer configuration options and parameter names may vary; check official device documentation for command and setup details
- Plaspy presents incoming data based on what the device reports; any change in device reporting affects visibility in the platform

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the TK-201-2 together with Plaspy gives you a straightforward way to centralize location data, alerts, and history for small personal trackers. Plaspy turns periodic and event-driven reports from the tracker into map positions, timeline history, and alert items that are useful for everyday monitoring, incident response, and record keeping.
