# Xexun - TK-201-2

<div className="device-logo">
![TK-201-2](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Xexun TK-201-2 is a cost effective GPS tracking device designed primarily for pet tracking. Its compact, lightweight form factor makes it suitable to attach to a pet collar or harness and provides location updates that help owners monitor movements and quickly locate a lost animal. The device also includes a set of built in tracking features such as interval based updates, movement alerts, and geo fence alarms that make it useful beyond pet use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK-201-2 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and management. That compatibility allows owners and operators to view live and historical positions, receive alerts, and integrate the device into broader monitoring workflows on the Plaspy platform. This makes the TK-201-2 a practical option for individuals and small organizations looking for affordable tracking with straightforward platform integration.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design suitable for attaching to a pet collar or harness, supporting discreet everyday use.
- Primary focus on pet tracking with features that support location updates and basic safety monitoring.
- Configurable tracking modes including time interval and distance interval updates to balance update frequency and resource use.
- Built in alert functions such as movement alert, overspeed alert, low battery alert, and geo fence alarm for timely notifications.
- History trace checking and GSM ID tracking to review past locations and identify devices in multi unit deployments.
- Practical for a range of personal and small scale operational uses due to simple feature set and portable form.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK-201-2's location and event messages are presented in a consolidated interface that supports monitoring, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy ingests device updates and exposes them through map views, timelines, and configurable notifications so users can keep track of pets or people from a single dashboard.

- Live location monitoring on Plaspy maps for quick awareness of current position.
- Geo fence rules integrated with Plaspy to trigger alerts when a device enters or leaves defined areas.
- Historical location playback and trace checking inside Plaspy for incident review and movement analysis.
- Alerts from the device such as movement, overspeed, and low battery forwarded through Plaspy notification channels.
- Device identification and grouping using the tracker ID to manage multiple units and assign them to pets or personnel.
- Operational oversight for small field teams by consolidating individual device data into Plaspy reports and views.
