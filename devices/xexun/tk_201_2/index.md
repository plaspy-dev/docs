# Xexun - TK-201-2

![TK-201-2](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun TK-201-2 is a cost-effective GPS tracking device designed specifically for pet tracking. With its compact size and lightweight design, it can easily be attached to a pet's collar or harness, providing real-time location updates and peace of mind for pet owners. But its applications go beyond just pet tracking. This versatile tracker can also be used to protect children, the elderly, or individuals with disabilities, ensuring their safety and well-being at all times. Additionally, it can be used to manage outworkers and protect business people, allowing employers to keep track of their employees' whereabouts and ensure their safety while on the job. And for those looking for a discreet tracking solution, the TK-201-2 can even be used for tracking criminals secretly.

With a range of advanced features, the Xexun TK-201-2 offers a comprehensive tracking solution. Some of its standout features include track by time interval, allowing users to set specific time intervals for location updates. It also offers track by distance interval, which enables users to set distance thresholds for location updates. The GSM ID tracking feature allows for easy identification of the tracker, making it simple to keep track of multiple devices. Other features include movement alert, overspeed alert, low battery alert, initialization, history-trace checking, and geo-fence alarm. These features ensure that users are always aware of their pet or loved one's location and can take appropriate action if needed.

### Key Features:

- Track by Time Interval
- Track by Distance Interval
- GSM ID tracking
- Movement alert
- Overspeed alert
- Low battery alert
- Initialization
- History-trace checking
- Geo-fence alarm
