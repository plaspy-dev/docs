# Xexun - X04

![X04](./tracker.jpg)

The GPS/Beidou Mini Tracker X04 is a compact, professional-grade GPS tracker designed for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking of people, vehicles, and portable assets. With hybrid positioning that combines GPS, Beidou, WiFi and LBS, the X04 delivers fast position fixes and consistent coverage where pure GNSS signals can be weak. It transmits location and telemetry over domestic 2G/3G/4G cellular networks to a cloud server so you can monitor devices via Plaspy's web-based management platform and mobile app.

Built for straightforward deployment and everyday use, the X04 supports scheduled reporting, instant SOS alarms, configurable electronic fences, blind‑zone data buffering and remote firmware upgrades. The device is an excellent fit for organizations and individuals who need a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that balances small form factor, meaningful battery life and the safety features required for fleet management, anti-theft protection and personal security.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible compact GPS tracker for real-time tracking and historical route playback.
- Hybrid positioning: GPS, Beidou, WiFi and LBS for improved accuracy and faster fixes.
- Continuous cellular data transmission over domestic 2G/3G/4G networks for cloud-based monitoring.
- Configurable geofence alerts, scheduled reporting and instant SOS emergency alarm.
- Blind-zone data buffering with automatic retransmission when the network is restored.
- Remote listening capability for safety and situational awareness.
- Built-in rechargeable battery \(600 mAh\) with low-battery warning and up to 5 days standby.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The X04 integrates with Plaspy by sending location and telemetry to a cloud endpoint over cellular networks, enabling Plaspy's web and mobile clients to display live positions, play back history, and manage alerts. Plaspy-compatible workflows can ingest the device's scheduled reports and event-driven updates to provide centralized fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and personal safety dashboards.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates transmitted over 2G/3G/4G to Plaspy for live map tracking.
- Geofence in/out alerts and time‑period fencing trigger immediate notifications in Plaspy.
- SOS emergency alarm that appears in Plaspy with timestamp and last known location.
- Blind‑zone \(offline\) data storage on the device with automatic upload when connectivity resumes.
- Remote listening \(voice monitoring\) available to authorized Plaspy users for safety verification.
- Scheduled \(periodic\) reporting and history route playback accessible from Plaspy's platform.
- Low battery alerts and remote firmware \(OTA\) upgrade status provided to Plaspy for device lifecycle management.
