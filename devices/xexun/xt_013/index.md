# Xexun - XT-013

<div className="device-logo">
![XT-013](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Xexun XT-013 is a compact and lightweight GPS tracker designed to provide real time location updates and continuous auto tracking. Its small form factor makes it easy to conceal while the waterproof enclosure supports reliable use in a range of outdoor conditions. The device includes a built in antenna and an internal battery and reports movement, geofence events, overspeed occurrences, and low battery status.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT-013 can feed location and event data into the Plaspy fleet management platform to give operators centralized visibility and alert management. When paired with Plaspy, the XT-013’s movement, geofence, overspeed, and battery alerts can be surfaced alongside vehicle and asset data so teams can monitor operations, respond to events, and keep accurate tracking records.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design suited for discreet asset and vehicle tracking
- Waterproof housing for dependable outdoor use in varied weather
- Real time location updates with built in antenna and internal battery
- Move alert and geofence notifications for location change monitoring
- Overspeed alert to mark and notify when a configured speed threshold is exceeded
- Low battery alert to support timely maintenance and recharge planning
- Manufacturer backed 3 year warranty for long term confidence

## How It Works with Plaspy

The XT-013 transmits location and event data that Plaspy can ingest to present live positions, events, and history on its platform. Plaspy consolidates those inputs so teams can monitor assets, receive timely alerts, and generate operational reports.

- Live map view and tracking visibility for individual devices and groups
- Geofence and move alerts routed into Plaspy notifications for rapid attention
- Overspeed events highlighted in monitoring dashboards and alert feeds
- Low battery notifications available in Plaspy to aid preventive maintenance
- Historical position data retained for route review and reporting
- Fleet level dashboards and summaries to support operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle fleet tracking for route oversight and asset security
- Securing high value portable equipment and mobile assets
- Monitoring rental or leased vehicles and equipment
- Logistics and delivery visibility for operations and customer service
- Personal or companion tracking where a compact waterproof device is preferred
