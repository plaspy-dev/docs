# Xexun - XT-013

![XT-013](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun XT-013 is a compact and lightweight GPS tracker that offers real-time location updates and auto-tracking capabilities. Its small size makes it easy to conceal and its waterproof design ensures durability in various weather conditions. 

With a built-in antenna and battery, the XT-013 provides reliable and accurate tracking. It also features a low battery alert, so you'll never have to worry about losing track of your assets. 

One of the standout features of the XT-013 is its move and geofence alert. This means that you'll receive notifications whenever the tracker is moved from its designated location or enters/exits a predefined area. Additionally, the overspeed limit alert will notify you if the tracker exceeds a specified speed limit. 

As an added bonus, Xexun offers a 3-year warranty on the XT-013, giving you peace of mind and assurance in the quality of the product. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or loved ones, the Xexun XT-013 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that won't disappoint.
