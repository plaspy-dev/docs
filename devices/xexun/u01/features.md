# Xexun - U01 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Xexun U01 and how the device integrates with Plaspy for tracking and telemetry. It describes the practical capabilities you can expect when the U01 is deployed as part of a Plaspy-managed indoor positioning system and highlights how positional and physiological data are presented for operational use.

Exact availability and behavior for any specific U01 device can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation choices, and manufacturer implementation. Review manufacturer documentation and your deployment settings to confirm which features are active in your installation.

## Feature Overview

The U01 is a wristwatch form factor positioning tag designed for high-precision indoor location and continuous physiological telemetry. When used with a UWB anchor network and a positioning server, the U01 provides centimeter-level position updates and streams vital signs to a central platform such as Plaspy.

- Centimeter-level UWB positioning for indoor location awareness when used with anchors and a positioning engine.
- Continuous physiological telemetry including heart rate, blood pressure, and SpO2 for safety and health monitoring.
- Built for enterprise use with an IP68-rated enclosure and environmental operating range for demanding sites.
- Safety and event reporting features such as an SOS button, vibration alerts, and low-battery notifications.
- Remote management capabilities including OTA firmware upgrades and configurable broadcast intervals for lifecycle control.

## Core Features of Xexun - U01

- UWB high-precision positioning compatible with anchor-based positioning servers to support up to roughly 10 cm accuracy.
- Continuous telemetry streams for heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, and activity and sleep sensing for situational awareness.
- SOS emergency alarm via a touch-button press and vibration notifications for immediate local alerting.
- IP68-rated rugged enclosure designed for industrial and institutional environments.
- Low-power design with rechargeable battery and magnetic charging connector plus low-battery alerting.
- Remote OTA firmware upgrades for device lifecycle management and updates.
- Optional NFC and built-in RFID capability for proximity interactions and identity workflows.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated into Plaspy, the U01 contributes both high-precision location points and ongoing physiological and device state telemetry. Plaspy ingests positional and event data to display real-time maps, timelines, and alerts that support operational oversight.

- High-precision coordinates appear on Plaspy maps and dashboards for real-time location tracking and historical trajectories.
- Vital-sign metrics and activity data are available in Plaspy for monitoring personnel health and incident review.
- SOS and other event notifications are routed into Plaspy alarm workflows to alert operators and trigger responses.
- Device state indicators such as battery level and OTA update status help Plaspy users plan maintenance and replacements.
- RFID and NFC proximity events can be reflected in Plaspy as ID or access events for audit trails and proximity-based workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personnel tracking and attendance monitoring in factories, hospitals, schools, and other institutional sites.
- Safety monitoring and emergency response where SOS alerts and location plus vital-sign telemetry improve response times.
- Access control and proximity workflows using RFID or NFC for secure ID checks and event logging.
- Patrol and compliance monitoring where precise indoor positioning and event history support audits.
- Workflow and space optimization using historical trajectories and activity patterns to reduce bottlenecks.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence can depend on the device firmware version and the specific hardware revision shipped for your order.
- Some telemetry or optional features such as NFC or RFID may be region or configuration dependent.
- Installation choices, UWB anchor density, and positioning engine settings directly affect achievable accuracy and update frequency.
- Manufacturer configuration, local policies, and privacy settings influence which physiological metrics are transmitted to Plaspy.
- Always confirm OTA update availability and recommended firmware before large scale rollouts.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the U01 with Plaspy gives organizations a way to combine high-precision indoor location with continuous physiological telemetry and event reporting in a single operational view. Plaspy can unify U01 positional data, SOS events, and vital-sign streams with other site devices to improve situational awareness, accelerate responses, and simplify reporting.
