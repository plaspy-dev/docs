# Xexun - TK-101

![TK-101](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun TK-101 is a versatile GPS tracker that utilizes GSM/GPRS and GPS technologies to provide accurate and real-time location tracking and monitoring. With this device, you can easily locate and monitor any remote target using SMS or GPRS. The tracker allows you to obtain latitude and longitude coordinates, which can be checked on Google Earth for precise positioning.

One of the standout features of the TK-101 is its auto-reporting capability, which ensures that you receive regular updates on the target's position. Additionally, if the tracker enters a blind area where GPS signals are unavailable, it will automatically send the last known location to keep you informed.

The TK-101 offers a range of useful features, including the ability to add or change usernames and passwords for enhanced security. It also includes monitoring and SOS functionality, allowing you to keep a close eye on the target and send distress signals if needed. The tracker is equipped with geofencing capabilities, which means it can send alerts if the target breaches a restricted area. It can also notify you when the target starts moving or exceeds a predefined speed limit. Furthermore, the TK-101 will send low battery alerts and automatically alert you in case of theft or unauthorized startup.

With its wide range of applications, the Xexun TK-101 is suitable for various scenarios. It can be used for managing rental vehicles, monitoring outside equipment, or keeping track of personal belongings such as cars, cargo, kids, pets, and even watches. Its compact size and reliable performance make it an ideal choice for anyone in need of a reliable GPS tracker.
