# Xexun - XT008

<div className="device-logo">
![XT008](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Xexun XT008 is a versatile GPS tracking device designed for vehicle and asset monitoring. It combines real time tracking with interval tracking options and provides street level address replies to help pinpoint locations. The XT008 also supports hybrid location tracking to maintain position updates when satellite reception is limited, and it includes a range of alerts such as geofence, movement, and overspeed to assist in operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT008 can feed its location and alert data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its configurable update frequency, event alerts, and optional extensions like external sensors and media enable organizations to tailor tracking to their needs while using Plaspy as the platform for visibility, historical playback, and fleet management workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking with configurable interval updates for flexible monitoring
- Street address reply to receive readable location information
- Hybrid location tracking to maintain tracking when GPS coverage is limited
- Geofence, movement, overspeed, SOS, and low power alerts for operational awareness
- Optional add ons such as SD card, RFID, remote engine control, fuel and temperature monitoring, and camera support for expanded use cases
- Dual SIM capability for improved network availability and continued reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the XT008 supplies location points and event notifications that Plaspy uses to present live location, history playback, and alerting in a unified dashboard. Plaspy can ingest the XT008 data to create reports, trigger notifications, and support fleet level oversight without changing how the tracker generates its events.

- Display live vehicle or asset location and recent movement on Plaspy maps
- Playback historical tracks for investigations and route verification
- Receive geofence, overspeed, movement, and SOS alerts inside Plaspy for timely response
- Use interval tracking settings from the device to balance update frequency and visibility needs in Plaspy
- Surface optional telemetry such as fuel or temperature readings as custom data points in Plaspy reports and dashboards
- Centralize alerts and status reports from multiple XT008 units for fleet level monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location and route monitoring for logistics and distribution
- Asset tracking for trailers, equipment, and high value portable items
- Rental and shared vehicle oversight with geofence and movement alerts
- Field service team tracking and job assignment visibility
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring when paired with external temperature sensing options
- Remote site security and recovery enabled by SOS and tamper alerts
