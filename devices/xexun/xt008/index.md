# Xexun - XT008

![XT008](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun XT008 GPS tracker is a versatile and feature-rich device that offers a wide range of functions to meet your tracking needs. With real-time tracking, you can monitor the location of your vehicle or asset in real-time, ensuring that you always know where it is. The interval tracking feature allows you to set the frequency at which the tracker sends updates, giving you control over how often you receive location information.

In addition to real-time tracking, the XT008 also offers other useful features such as real street address replying, which provides you with the actual street address of the tracker's location. This can be especially helpful when trying to locate a specific address or landmark. The tracker is also capable of tracking even when there is no GPS signal available, thanks to its GPS+LBS tracking feature.

Some other notable features of the XT008 include geofence alert, which allows you to set up virtual boundaries and receive alerts when the tracker enters or exits those boundaries. The movement alert feature notifies you when the tracker starts moving, making it ideal for tracking vehicles or assets that should remain stationary. The overspeed alert feature alerts you when the tracker exceeds a certain speed limit, helping you monitor and control the speed of your vehicles.

With its wide range of features and optional add-ons such as an SD card, RFID, remote engine control, fuel monitoring, temperature monitoring, and even a camera, the Xexun XT008 GPS tracker offers a comprehensive solution for all your tracking needs. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or even people, the XT008 provides the functionality and reliability you need.
