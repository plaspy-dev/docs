# Xexun - X02

![X02](./tracker.jpg)

The X02 is a compact rechargeable GPS Beidou tracker from Xexun designed for industrial vehicle location management and asset tracking. Rugged and portable, the device combines hybrid GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS assistance plus multi network cellular uplink to provide real time location, historical playback and basic telemetry for vehicles, motorcycles and portable assets. A strong built in magnet and a high capacity rechargeable battery make the X02 suitable where hardwiring is impractical.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the X02 feeds location and alarm data directly into the Plaspy cloud platform and mobile app. That compatibility enables operators to view live positions, run history reports, receive geofence and tamper alerts, and incorporate the X02 into broader fleet workflows managed within Plaspy for improved operational visibility and incident response.

## Key Highlights

- Native compatibility with Plaspy for real time tracking, history playback and cloud reporting.
- Hybrid GNSS positioning using GPS and Beidou with Wi‑Fi and LBS assistance for faster fixes.
- High capacity rechargeable battery and strong internal magnet for flexible, non wired installation.
- Geofence alerting and tamper detection to support security and anti theft workflows.
- Blind zone buffering with automatic retransmission to protect data continuity in intermittent coverage.
- Remote management features including over the air firmware updates to simplify device maintenance.
- Designed for multi network cellular uplink to provide wide area coverage for fleet operations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The X02 transmits multi source location and device telemetry to Plaspy over its cellular link. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions and assisted fixes, stores historical tracks, and surfaces those data on maps, dashboards and mobile views so operators can monitor assets in real time and review incidents after the fact.

- Live location and telemetry in Plaspy for fleet visibility and operational oversight.
- Historical playback and track queries for route verification and incident review.
- Geofence breach alerts and tamper or low battery alarms forwarded to Plaspy for notification and workflow triggers.
- Offline buffering on the device with automatic retransmit to Plaspy when connectivity returns.
- Remote device management and firmware updates coordinated through Plaspy to reduce field maintenance.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, vans and light commercial vehicles requiring real time position and history.
- Asset security and anti theft monitoring with tamper detection and alerting.
- Motorcycles and electric bikes where compact rechargeable mounting is preferred over wired installs.
- Temporary or rental assets such as trailers and seasonal equipment that need easy redeployment.
- Personal vehicle protection and remote location monitoring without constant hardwired power.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The X02 pairs practical hardware features with Plaspy platform capabilities to deliver dependable location tracking where wired power is not available. Its combination of hybrid GNSS positioning, assisted fixes, robust battery life and magnetic mounting make it a flexible choice for operators who need straightforward real time visibility and security alerts through Plaspy.

Plaspy can consume the X02’s core location, alarm and status data to enable reporting, geofence management and automated notifications across fleets. For organizations that later need expanded telemetry or integrations, Plaspy supports extending workflows with additional peripherals and platform features while continuing to use the X02 for location and security monitoring.
