# Xexun - X02

![X02](./tracker.jpg)

The X02 GPS/Beidou Rechargeable Tracker is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for industrial vehicle location management. Compact and rugged, the X02 combines hybrid GNSS positioning \(GPS + Beidou\), Wi‑Fi and LBS assist, and multi‑network cellular uplink to deliver reliable real‑time tracking and historical playback through the Plaspy cloud tracking platform and mobile app. With a strong built‑in magnet and a high‑capacity rechargeable battery, the X02 is ideal where wired power is impractical.

Built for fleet management, asset security and personal vehicle protection, the X02 supports continuous telemetry, geofence alerts and tamper detection while conserving power for extended deployments. As a Plaspy compatible device, it integrates into Plaspy workflows to enable real‑time tracking, alerting and reporting for vehicles, motorcycles and electric bikes.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: native data feed to Plaspy for real‑time tracking, history playback and cloud reporting.
- Hybrid positioning: GPS + Beidou with Wi‑Fi and LBS assistance for faster fixes and typical accuracy under 10 m \(environment dependent\).
- Long run time: large 10000 mAh rechargeable polymer battery for extended autonomous operation and up to ~2 months standby at 1‑hour reporting interval.
- Easy installation: powerful internal magnet and compact form factor for quick mounting on vehicles and assets.
- Anti‑theft and tamper protection: built‑in light/dismantle sensor and low‑battery alerts to support security workflows.
- Robust telemetry: blind‑zone buffering with automatic retransmission, scheduled tracking intervals and remote history queries.
- Remote management: supports remote firmware upgrades \(FOTA\) and operates on domestic 2G/3G/4G cellular networks.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The X02 transmits multi‑source location and telemetry data to Plaspy over the device’s cellular connection. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates, network‑assisted fixes \(Wi‑Fi/LBS\), and device telemetry, then presents that information in real‑time on maps, dashboards and mobile apps. Alerts and reports generated by the X02 \(geofence breaches, tamper/dismantle, low battery\) are forwarded to Plaspy and can trigger automated notifications, workflows or operator actions.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and fleet visibility.
- Geofence alerts and historical track queries available within Plaspy for incident review and compliance.
- Low‑battery and tamper/dismantle alarms routed to Plaspy to support anti‑theft response.
- Blind‑zone buffering ensures no data loss: the X02 stores events offline and retransmits when connectivity is restored.
- Remote firmware upgrades through Plaspy/OTA workflows keep devices updated without field visits.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real‑time position and history playback for cars, service vans and small commercial vehicles, enabling route verification and asset utilization tracking.
- Anti‑theft and vehicle security: tamper/dismantle alarms and remote audio monitoring support quick response to unauthorized access or theft attempts.
- Electric bikes and motorcycles: compact, rechargeable installation with magnetic mounting simplifies deployment on two‑wheel vehicles without hardwiring.
- Remote or temporary assets: ideal for trailers, seasonal equipment or rented vehicles where long battery life and easy installation are essential.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The X02 offers a strong combination of long battery life, flexible installation and Plaspy compatibility—making it a practical choice for operators who need dependable real‑time tracking without constant hardwired power. Its hybrid GPS/Beidou positioning and multi‑source location assistance improve fix times and accuracy, while blind‑zone buffering and automatic retransmission maintain data integrity in areas with intermittent coverage. For fleet management and telemetry use cases, the X02 feeds Plaspy with the core location, alarm and status data required for reporting, geofence management and operational workflows.

While specialized telemetry like fuel monitoring, ignition control or immobilizer functions require additional sensors or vehicle integrations, the Plaspy platform supports those workflows alongside the X02’s core data. That means you can deploy the X02 for robust location and security tracking today, and extend capabilities through Plaspy and compatible peripherals as your fleet management needs evolve.
