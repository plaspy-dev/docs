# Xexun - X05 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Xexun X05 pet GPS tracker and explains how those capabilities appear when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive feature context that helps owners and fleet managers understand what the tracker can do and how Plaspy surfaces location, alerts, and device management functions.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where necessary, consult the device documentation from the manufacturer and your Plaspy administrator to confirm how a particular X05 is configured and managed.

## Feature Overview

The X05 is a compact, collar friendly tracker designed for pet and small asset tracking that combines satellite and alternative positioning with cellular connectivity to report location and basic telemetry to a cloud platform. Its design emphasizes battery life and practical monitoring features that are useful for everyday pet safety and oversight.

- Hybrid positioning using GPS and BeiDou with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to improve coverage in mixed environments.
- Cellular upload over domestic 2G/3G/4G networks to send location fixes and telemetry to a cloud service for live maps and history.
- Compact collar form factor with a rechargeable 600 mAh battery and motion sensing to reduce power use while stationary.
- User-facing alerts including geofence triggers and low battery warnings to keep owners informed.
- Blind‑zone buffering that stores data offline and automatically retransmits when network access is restored.
- Remote management capabilities such as firmware upgrades and listen‑in voice monitoring where enabled by the manufacturer.

## Core Features of Xexun - X05

- Hybrid satellite and network positioning: GPS and BeiDou plus Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to extend tracking in urban or indoor scenarios.
- Cellular connectivity across domestic 2G/3G/4G bands for transmitting location and device telemetry to a cloud platform.
- Rechargeable 600 mAh battery with motion sensor driven sleep modes to help extend standby time during inactivity.
- Geofence support and low battery alerts for event based notifications.
- Timed tracking with configurable reporting intervals to balance location granularity and battery life.
- Blind‑zone data buffering with automatic retransmit when the device regains network access.
- Remote voice monitoring (listen‑in) and over the air firmware upgrade functionality where provided by the device firmware.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an X05 is set up to report to Plaspy, location fixes and available telemetry are collected by Plaspy and presented through Plaspy’s real time tracking interface. Plaspy consolidates events, maps, and historical data so owners and managers can monitor status and respond to alerts from a single platform.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for real time tracking and situational awareness.
- Geofence configuration and instant alerts routed through Plaspy when a device leaves or enters a defined area.
- Configurable reporting intervals managed from Plaspy to tune frequency versus battery life.
- Historical route playback and reporting in Plaspy for reviewing walks, trips, or movement history.
- Event and alert notifications for low battery and other supported conditions routed through Plaspy channels.
- Remote device management tasks such as initiating a firmware update are visible in Plaspy when the device firmware supports cloud upgrade workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet recovery and anti‑theft tracking with real time location and geofence alerts.
- Indoor and urban location enhancement using Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback alongside satellite fixes.
- Monitoring walks and activity with historical route playback to review movement patterns.
- Remote oversight during travel, boarding, or external care with low battery and movement notifications.
- Shelter and rescue operations using compact trackers for individual animal oversight.
- Lightweight asset tracking where small size and discrete mounting are important.

## Feature Availability Notes

- For the latest detailed specifications and region specific variants consult the manufacturer documentation.
