# Xexun - X06

![X06](./tracker.jpg)

The X06 GPS/Beidou Positioning Badge is a Plaspy compatible wearable GPS tracker designed for reliable people-location management. Built for daily use in education, eldercare, corporate attendance and security scenarios, the X06 fuses GPS and BeiDou with WiFi and LBS to deliver faster, more accurate positioning and smooth integration with Plaspy for real-time tracking and alerting. Its compact badge form factor, integrated voice module and long standby make it a practical choice where unobtrusive continuous monitoring is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the X06 transmits position and status over domestic 2G/3G/4G cellular networks to cloud platforms and mobile apps. That combination gives managers instant access to location, historical tracks, geofence alerts and SOS events through Plaspy’s interface, while retaining local buffering and retransmission when coverage is interrupted. The result is dependable situational awareness and faster incident response for personnel and targeted groups.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible wearable GPS tracker for personnel monitoring, enabling real-time tracking on Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps.
- Multi-source positioning \(GPS + BeiDou + WiFi + LBS\) for improved fix speed and typical accuracy of &lt;10 m in open-sky conditions.
- Built-in 1000 mAh rechargeable battery with about 2-hour charge time and up to ~5 days standby \(one report per hour\), suitable for continuous daily use.
- SOS emergency alarm \(long-press SOS\) and two-way voice calling for rapid incident response and caregiver communication.
- Geofence \(electronic fence\) alerts, blind-area buffering and retransmission to ensure no events are lost during temporary signal loss.
- Incoming-call whitelist and remote OTA firmware upgrade for secure communications and simplified device maintenance.
- Badge-style, eco-friendly plastic housing \(88 × 49 × 15 mm, ~60 g\) for unobtrusive wear and easy deployment at scale.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the X06 is provisioned as a Plaspy compatible tracker, it forwards position updates, alarms and status messages through its cellular connection to the vendor cloud and into Plaspy. Plaspy displays live location, plays back historical tracks and raises geofence or low-battery alerts—enabling centralized monitoring and reporting. The X06’s buffering logic ensures continuity: if cellular service is lost, location records are held locally and retransmitted automatically when connectivity returns.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and historical playback.
- SOS emergency alarm and two-way voice for fast response; incoming-call whitelist reduces nuisance calls.
- Geofence alerts and timed interval tracking \(automatic position reporting\) available in Plaspy rule sets.
- Blind-area data buffering and retransmission to prevent gaps in reporting during temporary outages.
- OTA firmware upgrade support for remote management and feature updates through the cloud platform used with Plaspy.
