# Xexun - X06

<div className="device-logo">
![X06](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Xexun X06 is a wearable GPS and Beidou positioning badge designed for people location management. Its compact badge form factor houses multi source positioning with WiFi and LBS assistance, an integrated voice module and an SOS alarm, delivering a practical solution for unobtrusive continuous monitoring in education, eldercare, corporate attendance and security contexts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the X06 forwards position updates, alarms and status to cloud services and into Plaspy so teams can visualize live locations, review historical tracks and receive geofence or SOS alerts. The unit’s local buffering and retransmission behavior helps maintain continuity of tracking data during temporary coverage gaps, making it suitable for centralized personnel oversight on the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable badge style tracker combining GPS and BeiDou with WiFi and LBS assistance for faster fixes in mixed environments.
- Designed for people location use cases with integrated SOS alarm and two way voice capability for direct communication.
- Long standby performance and rechargeable battery targeted at daily continuous use.
- Geofence alerting and local buffering to preserve events during temporary signal loss.
- Compact, lightweight badge form factor for unobtrusive wear and straightforward deployment at scale.
- OTA firmware update support and incoming call whitelist for simplified remote management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When provisioned as a compatible device, the X06 transmits its position, alarms and status updates over cellular networks into the cloud and into Plaspy. Plaspy surfaces those updates through dashboards and reporting tools so managers can maintain situational awareness, trigger rules and coordinate response actions. The device retains records locally if connectivity drops and forwards them when service is restored to prevent data gaps.

- Real time position updates and historical track playback available inside Plaspy for monitoring and review.
- SOS alarm and two way voice events shown as alerts to accelerate incident response and caregiver communication.
- Geofence notifications and timed reporting configurable within Plaspy rule sets for zone based monitoring.
- Local buffering and retransmission preserve events during temporary outages, improving data continuity.
- Remote device management and firmware updates managed via the cloud platform paired with Plaspy.
