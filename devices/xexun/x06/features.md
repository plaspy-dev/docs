# Xexun - X06 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the Xexun X06 when used with Plaspy. It focuses on the capabilities that matter for people-location management and how those capabilities map to monitoring, alerting, and operational workflows in Plaspy. Use this material to understand the X06's practical value for real-time tracking, geofence monitoring, SOS handling, and basic remote management when integrated into a Plaspy deployment.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. Where specific details matter for deployment or compliance, consult the official Xexun documentation and release notes to confirm device behavior and supported options for your unit.

## Feature Overview

The X06 is a compact wearable positioning badge that combines multi-source location technologies with on-device communications and power management designed for daily personnel monitoring. Paired with Plaspy, the device provides centralized visibility, event notification, and historical playback to support safety and operational oversight.

- Multi-source positioning using GPS and BeiDou with WiFi and LBS assistance for faster fixes and improved location reliability.
- Wearable badge form factor with compact dimensions and a rechargeable battery intended for continuous daily use.
- SOS emergency alarm and two-way voice to enable direct on-device communication during incidents while Plaspy surfaces alarms.
- Geofence alerts and blind-area buffering with retransmission to reduce gaps in location history when coverage is interrupted.
- Remote firmware upgrade capability and incoming-call whitelist to simplify maintenance and reduce nuisance calls.

## Core Features of Xexun - X06

- GPS and BeiDou positioning supplemented by WiFi and LBS assistance for faster initial fixes and improved performance in mixed environments.
- Wearable badge design suitable for personnel monitoring in education, eldercare, corporate attendance, and security use cases.
- Built-in rechargeable battery sized for daily operation and reasonable standby between charges.
- SOS emergency alarm activated by a long-press for rapid escalation of incidents.
- Two-way voice module to support direct conversations between the wearer and a caregiver or operator.
- Geofence or electronic fence alerts to notify managers when predefined areas are entered or exited.
- Local buffering of location data during signal loss and automatic retransmission when connectivity returns.
- OTA firmware upgrade support to apply updates and improvements remotely via the vendor cloud platform.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the X06 is provisioned as a Plaspy compatible device, its position updates, alarms, and status messages are forwarded to the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. Plaspy displays live locations, retains historical tracks for playback, and surfaces configured alerts so teams can act quickly based on device events.

- Live position updates and telemetry available in Plaspy for real-time situational awareness.
- Historical track playback and reports to support audits, attendance checks, and incident reviews.
- SOS alarm events and status messages raised in Plaspy to trigger notifications and response workflows.
- Geofence alerts and time-based position reporting integrated into Plaspy rule sets for automated monitoring.
- Buffering and retransmission behavior help ensure Plaspy receives continuous location records even when cellular coverage is intermittent.
- OTA firmware update status and device health indicators visible through the management channel used with Plaspy.
