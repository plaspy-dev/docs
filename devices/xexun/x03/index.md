# Xexun - X03

![X03](./tracker.jpg)

The X03 is a compact industrial grade GPS and BeiDou tracker built for long term vehicle and asset monitoring. Designed for cars, motorcycles and electric bikes, the device combines multi source positioning with domestic cellular reporting to deliver continuous location, event alerts and basic telemetry. Its long standby design and store and forward behavior make it suitable for assets that need unattended operation and reliable history upload after intermittent connectivity.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the X03 can feed its location and event data into Plaspy’s cloud platform for map based visibility, alerting and historical playback. That compatibility makes the X03 relevant to operators who want a low maintenance tracker they can integrate into Plaspy fleet workflows for tracking, geofence notifications and routine monitoring without extensive on site management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for direct location and telemetry integration into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools.
- Hybrid positioning using GPS, BeiDou, Wi Fi and LBS fusion for faster fixes and improved performance in mixed signal environments.
- Very long standby capability under conservative reporting profiles, reducing maintenance and battery change frequency.
- Geofence alerts and tamper detection via a built in light and tilt sensor for anti theft readiness.
- Store and forward caching to preserve location history during network outages and upload when connectivity returns.
- Remote listening and remote firmware upgrade support to assist troubleshooting and device maintenance.
- Compact form factor with magnetic mounting for quick, low profile installation on vehicles and assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to work with Plaspy, the X03 transmits its fused positioning and event telemetry to Plaspy’s cloud where that data is presented on maps, timelines and reports. Plaspy organizes the device feeds so operations teams can monitor location, respond to alerts, and review historical movement across managed fleets and assets.

- Real time location and telemetry shown on Plaspy map views for live tracking and operational oversight.
- Geofence events and tamper alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and escalation.
- Periodic reporting and low battery notifications available to trigger maintenance workflows in Plaspy.
- Blind area store and forward ensures historical positions are imported into Plaspy after connectivity resumes.
- Remote listening and firmware update events coordinated through Plaspy for device management and diagnostics.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small and medium vehicle fleets needing continuous location and route playback.
- Anti theft monitoring for private cars, motorcycles and e bikes using covert magnetic mounting and tamper alerts.
- Rental vehicle tracking to monitor location history and battery status with minimal maintenance.
- Logistics and asset tracking for trailers or containers where long standby and data caching are important.
- Remote diagnostics and maintenance planning by using low battery and event reports to reduce onsite visits.

The X03 offers a practical combination of long autonomy, multi source positioning and straightforward deployment that fits common fleet and asset tracking needs. Its design emphasizes unattended operation and reliable event reporting, which pairs well with Plaspy’s tools for map visualization, alerts and historical analytics.
