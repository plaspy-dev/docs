# Xexun - TK-201

![TK-201](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun TK201 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for personal tracking purposes. With its small size, it can easily be carried or worn, making it ideal for tracking children, the elderly, or even pets. This mini tracker offers a range of features that ensure the safety and security of your loved ones.

One of the standout features of the Xexun TK201 is its real-time tracking capability. You can easily track and locate the device at any given interval, either through SMS or by making a call. This allows you to keep a close eye on the whereabouts of your loved ones, providing peace of mind.

In addition to real-time tracking, the TK201 also offers voice surveillance, allowing you to listen in on the surrounding environment. This feature can be particularly useful in emergency situations or when you need to check on the well-being of the tracker's user.

Another notable feature of the Xexun TK201 is its ability to report the last known location even without GPS coverage. This means that even if the device is in an area with poor GPS signal, you will still receive information about its last known location. This can be crucial in situations where GPS coverage is limited.

The TK201 also supports tracks playback, allowing you to review the tracker's movement history. This can be useful for analyzing patterns, identifying routes, or simply keeping a record of the tracker's movements.

Furthermore, the Xexun TK201 offers a 48-hour battery life, ensuring that the device remains operational for an extended period of time. This is particularly important for long trips or situations where charging options may be limited.

Finally, the TK201 provides a range of alerts to keep you informed of any potential issues. These alerts include geofence notifications, movement alerts, over speed alerts, low power alerts, and SOS alerts. These features allow you to set up customized notifications and respond quickly to any emergencies or unexpected events.

Overall, the Xexun TK201 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers peace of mind and enhanced safety for personal tracking purposes. Its compact size, real-time tracking capabilities, and range of alerts make it an excellent choice for tracking loved ones or pets.
