# Xexun - TK-201 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Xexun TK-201 when used with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker’s user-facing capabilities and explains how those capabilities map to visibility and monitoring within Plaspy without exposing private or sensitive implementation details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation choices, regional product variants, and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, limits, and the latest technical details, consult the official Xexun documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Xexun TK-201 is a compact personal GPS tracker positioned for discrete carry or wear, designed to report location and events for people or small assets. Its core strengths are straightforward real time location reports, simple voice monitoring, historical track playback, and a set of alert types useful for safety monitoring.

- Compact, wearable form factor suited for personal tracking of children, elderly, or pets.
- Real time location querying and periodic reporting using standard cellular methods such as SMS or voice call control.
- Voice surveillance capability to listen to the device surroundings when activated.
- Ability to provide last known location information when live GPS fixes are not available.
- Track playback for reviewing movement history and route patterns.
- Built in alerts including geofence notifications, movement alerts, overspeed alerts, low battery alerts, and SOS notifications.

## Core Features of Xexun - TK-201

- Compact personal tracker size suitable for carry or attachment.
- Real time tracking and on demand location queries via SMS or phone call.
- Voice surveillance for remote listening when supported and permitted.
- Last known location reporting in scenarios without active GPS fix.
- Tracks playback to review historical movements and routes.
- Multiple alert types including geofence, movement, overspeed, low power, and SOS.
- Extended operational battery life reported around 48 hours under typical use patterns.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides a platform view of device location reports, historical tracks, and alert events reported by compatible trackers such as the TK-201. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and presents location and event data on maps and timelines for operational oversight.

- Location updates and last known positions appear on Plaspy maps for live situational awareness.
- Historical track data reported by the device is available for playback inside Plaspy to review routes and timings.
- Alerts from the TK-201 such as geofence, movement, overspeed, low battery, and SOS are captured as events and can be monitored in Plaspy dashboards and notifications.
- Device status indicators, including recent contact and basic battery reports, help operators know whether the tracker is active and reporting.
- Plaspy simplifies setup by automatically recognizing compatible tracker protocols so devices can be pointed at the Plaspy server domain for reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children, seniors, or other vulnerable individuals.

- Temporary asset tracking for items that need discreet location reporting.
- Route and trip review where playback of recent movements is useful for scheduling or analysis.
- Rapid alerting for emergencies using SOS and movement alerts to notify carers or responders.
- Situations where last known location reporting is critical due to intermittent GPS availability.
