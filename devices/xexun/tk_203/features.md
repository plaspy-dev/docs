# Xexun - TK-203 Features

This page provides a public, features oriented summary of the Xexun TK-203 GPS tracker and how its reported capabilities can be used with Plaspy for location monitoring and alerts. It focuses on user facing functions and practical behavior you can expect when the TK-203 reports into Plaspy, without reproducing proprietary configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a general compatibility and capability overview and consult the device maker for the most current technical specifics.

## Feature Overview

The Xexun TK-203 is a compact GPS tracker built around GPS location and GSM GPRS communications. It is designed for regular position reporting, emergency alerting, and basic event notifications, and its waterproof casing makes it appropriate for use cases where exposure to moisture is possible.

- Periodic automatic position reporting so you receive regular location updates without manual polling
- GPS based location combined with GSM GPRS reporting for live tracking via data or SMS
- Waterproof design suitable for everyday use cases involving children, elders, or outdoor conditions
- SOS emergency alert feature for sending a distress notification when activated
- Event alerts including geofence, movement, overspeed, and low battery notifications
- Ability to provide a last known location when GPS coverage is lost

## Core Features of Xexun - TK-203

- GPS location tracking with periodic automatic reporting
- GSM and GPRS connectivity for remote transmission of location and status
- SMS and GPRS reporting options for flexible notification and tracking
- Waterproof enclosure designed for normal life exposure to water
- SOS emergency alert for sending immediate distress notifications
- Geofence alerts for entry and exit of predefined areas
- Movement alerts, overspeed alerts, and low battery alerts for event driven reporting

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event reports from compatible trackers and presents them through its device management and mapping tools. When a TK-203 reports, Plaspy surfaces location updates and event notifications so operators can monitor devices in near real time.

- Regular position reports appear on Plaspy maps and in the device activity timeline
- SOS alerts are translated into high priority events for immediate attention within Plaspy
- Geofence, movement, overspeed, and low battery signals generate notifications and event records inside the Plaspy interface
- Last known location information is preserved in Plaspy so you can review the most recent reported position when GPS coverage was unavailable
- Remote monitoring capabilities allow status checks and historical playback using Plaspy tools

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal protection and monitoring for children or elderly family members who may need waterproof and wearable tracking
- Employee safety and oversight for mobile staff working in environments where location awareness is required
- Field worker tracking and basic fleet oversight where regular position reporting is needed
- Covert or discreet tracking scenarios where small form factor and remote reporting are required
- Asset location and recovery when periodic updates and last known positions are important
