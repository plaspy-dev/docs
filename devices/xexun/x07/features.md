# Xexun - X07 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Xexun X07 wearable with Plaspy. It explains the main capabilities the device reports into Plaspy, how those capabilities are useful for monitoring people, and what to expect when integrating the watch into Plaspy for centralized tracking and alerting.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation choices, and how the manufacturer implements specific options. For device specific details and the latest firmware behavior consult the manufacturer documentation before finalizing deployments.

## Feature Overview

The X07 is a Plaspy compatible wearable designed for personal safety and elder care. It combines multi mode positioning with onboard physiological sensors so caregivers and operations teams can view location and vital sign telemetry together, and configure alerts and historical reports in Plaspy.

- Multi mode positioning using GPS and BeiDou with WiFi and LBS fusion for faster and more reliable fixes in mixed environments.
- Continuous health telemetry including heart rate, blood pressure, and motion state alongside location data.
- Two way voice calling and in watch alerts to keep caregivers connected to the wearer.
- Scheduled tracking and configurable reporting intervals to balance update frequency and battery life.
- Remote device management and firmware upgrade support to simplify maintaining many devices.
- Compact wearable design suited to everyday personal monitoring use cases.

## Core Features of Xexun - X07

- GPS and BeiDou positioning with WiFi and LBS fusion for improved fix times and coverage.
- Onboard physiological sensors for heart rate and blood pressure telemetry.
- Motion state reporting to indicate activity or movement changes.
- Two way voice capability for direct communication with the wearer.
- Scheduled reporting and timed tracking modes to adjust telemetry frequency.
- Support for domestic 2G 3G and 4G cellular networks for wide area connectivity.
- Remote firmware upgrade and cloud integrated device management.
- Wearable form factor designed for daily use in personal safety and elder care scenarios.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the X07 location and telemetry and presents those signals alongside other assets so teams can monitor people and devices from the same platform. Integration focuses on visibility, timely alerts, and centralized device status.

- Real time location display and historical route playback for each wearable on Plaspy maps.
- Vital sign telemetry such as heart rate and blood pressure available as tracked metrics and for rule based alerts.
- Geofence and out of bound alarms configurable in Plaspy to notify caregivers or operations staff when a wearer enters or leaves defined zones.
- Device status visibility including connection and battery condition shown in the Plaspy device list.
- Scheduled tracking settings and reporting cadence reflected in Plaspy so teams can manage resolution and data usage for monitoring profiles.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elder care and assisted living monitoring with combined location and vital sign visibility for caregivers.
- Lone worker or vulnerable person safety monitoring using location updates, motion state, and two way voice.
- Personal location services for routine tracking, check ins, and geofence notifications.
- Remote health aware activity monitoring where movement and basic vitals inform follow up actions.
- Managed device fleets for home care providers who need centralized device management and firmware updates.
- Short term monitoring for clinics or community health programs requiring location aware telemetry.

## Feature Availability Notes
