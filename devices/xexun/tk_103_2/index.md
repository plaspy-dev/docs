# Xexun - TK-103-2

![TK-103-2](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun TK-103-2 GPS tracker is a versatile device that can be used in various applications, including private car and vehicle leasing industry, outdoor mechanical equipment anti-theft tracking, and corporate vehicle management monitoring. With its wide range of features, this tracker provides real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities to ensure the safety and security of your assets.

One of the standout features of the TK-103-2 is its ability to provide immediate location inquiries. You can easily track the location of your vehicle or equipment at any time, giving you peace of mind and the ability to respond quickly in case of any emergencies. Additionally, the tracker supports regularly scheduled time tracking, allowing you to set specific intervals for location updates.

The TK-103-2 also offers a range of alarm features, including shift alarm, speed alarm, low battery alarm, vibration alarm, SOS emergency alarm, GPS antenna cut off alarm, external power supply cut off alarm, and illegal fire alarm. These alarms help to ensure that you are promptly notified of any suspicious activities or potential issues with your assets.

Other notable features of the TK-103-2 include historical track playback, electronic fence capabilities, remote upgrade, remote monitoring, dual SIM card support for automatic network switching, and optional features such as an SD card for additional storage and remote power off the oil. With its comprehensive set of features, the Xexun TK-103-2 GPS tracker is an excellent choice for anyone looking to enhance the security and management of their vehicles or equipment.
