# Xexun - DDX04

![DDX04](./tracker.jpg)

The DDX04 is an anti tamper wearable tracking watch designed for supervision, safety, and remote monitoring. It combines multi source positioning with tamper detection and health telemetry in a compact wrist worn form factor. The device is built to support continuous real time tracking, on demand location queries and historical playback while providing alerts and sensor data that are useful for compliance and welfare monitoring programs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the DDX04 can feed position data, tamper events and health telemetry into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor subjects, enforce geofence rules and review movement history from a single dashboard. Its anti tamper wristband, rugged IP68 construction and remote management features make it well suited to supervised programs and other workflows that Plaspy helps manage.

## Key Highlights

- Multi source positioning including GPS and BeiDou plus auxiliary location methods for improved fix reliability.
- Integrated wristband tamper detection that triggers immediate alarms for removal or interference.
- Continuous real time tracking with timed interval reports, on demand queries and history playback for auditing.
- Built in health telemetry including heart rate and blood pressure monitoring to support vulnerable user oversight.
- IP68 rated wearable construction and a compact display for daily use in real world conditions.
- Remote management support for over the air updates and platform driven instructions to reduce manual maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the DDX04 transmits its location, sensor telemetry and alert events to the platform so operators can maintain situational awareness and manage compliance workflows. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it through tools for monitoring, alerting and reporting.

- Live position updates displayed on the Plaspy dashboard for immediate tracking of subjects.
- Tamper and removal alerts routed to operators to enable rapid response and case management.
- Health telemetry such as heart rate and blood pressure available alongside movement data to inform welfare checks.
- Low battery and connectivity notifications to support proactive device maintenance and ensure continuity.
- History playback and reporting to recreate movements and produce records for audits and oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Electronic monitoring and compliance programs for community corrections and court supervised residents.
- Remote health and activity monitoring for elderly care programs and patients requiring location plus vital signs oversight.
- Institutional staff safety and lone worker monitoring where rapid alerts and location visibility are required.
- Facility or program based supervision where history playback, geofence enforcement and tamper detection aid operational control.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The DDX04 pairs tamper resistant hardware and multi source positioning with health telemetry and remote management features, making it a practical option for organizations that need reliable, supervised tracking. Its wearable design and environmental protection reduce day to day maintenance while enabling continuous monitoring within Plaspy.

Used with Plaspy, the DDX04 provides a unified stream of location and event data that supports configurable alerts, geofence rules and audit ready history playback. This combination helps programs maintain compliance, respond to incidents faster and manage larger deployments without excessive manual intervention.
