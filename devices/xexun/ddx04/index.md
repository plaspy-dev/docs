# DDX04 Anti-tamper GPS/BeiDou Tracking Watch — Plaspy compatible

The DDX04 Anti-tamper GPS/BeiDou Tracking Watch is a professional-grade wearable GPS tracker purpose-built for supervision, safety and remote monitoring. Designed for subjects under community correction, court monitoring, home quarantine, elderly care and psychiatric supervision, the DDX04 pairs multi-source positioning \(GPS + BeiDou + WiFi + LBS\) with robust tamper protection and 4G cellular telemetry for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and reporting.

Simple to deploy and rugged in daily use, the DDX04 delivers continuous real-time tracking, on-demand location queries and history playback while sending sensor and health telemetry to backend platforms. Its integrated anti-tamper wristband, IP68 rating, heart-rate and blood-pressure sensors, and support for remote firmware upgrades make the DDX04 a practical option for compliance-driven programs and monitoring workflows managed through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with multi-source positioning \(GPS + BeiDou + WiFi + LBS\) for fast, accurate fixes.
- Built-in tamper detection circuit in the wristband that triggers immediate alarms on removal — ideal for anti-theft and compliance monitoring.
- Continuous real-time tracking plus timed interval reporting, on-demand queries and history playback for complete event auditing.
- Integrated health telemetry: heart-rate and blood-pressure sensors, motion and sleep monitoring for vulnerable-user oversight.
- IP68 water- and dust-resistant construction, compact wearable form factor, and long standby life for dependable daily operation.
- Rechargeable 500 mAh battery with clip-on wireless mobile power pack \(no cable required\) and remote firmware upgrade support.
- Clear 1.3-inch color display and physical buttons for local control and status visibility.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The DDX04 transmits multi-source location fixes and telemetry to a backend server over domestic 2G/3G/4G cellular networks. When used with Plaspy, the device’s position, sensor data and alerts are ingested into Plaspy’s dashboard so operators can see live locations, receive boundary and tamper alerts, review health telemetry and play back historical movements. Integration focuses on secure, low-latency delivery of tracking and event data to support compliance and safety workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via cellular networks for immediate situational awareness.
- Tamper/removal alerts from the wristband alarm circuit to enforce anti-tamper policies and anti-theft measures.
- Health telemetry \(heart-rate, blood-pressure\) plus motion and sleep data to support remote wellness monitoring.
- Low-battery and connectivity alerts for proactive device maintenance and uninterrupted monitoring.
- Remote instructions and over-the-air firmware upgrades to keep devices current without physical retrieval.
