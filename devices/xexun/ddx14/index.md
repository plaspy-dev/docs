# Xexun - DDX14

<div className="device-logo">
![DDX14](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The DDX14 is a purpose built ankle bracelet style GPS tracker by Xexun designed for supervised monitoring environments. It combines GPS and Beidou satellite positioning with WiFi and LBS assisted fixes, and is aimed at community corrections, healthcare supervision, and other special industry monitoring where continuous location reporting and tamper detection are required. The device emphasizes anti tamper measures and proximity detection to meet the needs of cases that demand persistent oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the DDX14 can be enrolled as a tracked endpoint within the Plaspy platform to provide centralized visibility and case management. Its anti tamper strap, optional high precision proximity module and offline data buffering allow Plaspy to receive timely alarms and location history for real time supervision, configurable alerts, and audit grade playback without assuming specific implementation details beyond the device description.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker for centralized real time tracking and case workflows
- Hybrid positioning using GPS and Beidou fused with WiFi and LBS for faster fixes and improved accuracy
- Tamper proof conductive strap loop that triggers immediate tamper alerts
- Optional UWB proximity module for high precision ranging when used with monitoring base stations
- Blind zone local storage with automatic re upload after network restoration for data continuity
- Clip style wireless mobile power pack that supports charging while the wearer moves
- Built in speaker for custom voice prompts and audible alerts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the DDX14 appears as a managed device that transmits location and status telemetry to the platform for immediate display, alerting, and archival. Plaspy ingests the device's alarms and tracks to support operational oversight, notifications, and historical review without requiring users to manage low level device behavior.

- Real time location and telemetry updates available on Plaspy dashboards
- Immediate reporting of tamper and strap conductive loop alarms, with configurable notifications
- Support for proximity and over distance alerts when UWB base stations are deployed
- Automatic retransmission of stored tracks and alarms after network recovery to preserve continuity
- Geo fence alerts, scheduled tracking intervals, route deviation monitoring and historical track queries in Plaspy
- Remote device management functions accessible from the platform for routine maintenance

## Typical Use Cases

- Probation and parole monitoring where tamper detection and geo fence enforcement are needed
- House arrest or controlled release supervision with continuous location reporting
- Elderly or vulnerable person care that benefits from real time location and caregiver alerts
- Institutional supervision and psychiatric monitoring requiring tamper proof hardware and audit trails
- Quarantine or isolation compliance monitoring with configurable boundaries and voice prompts
