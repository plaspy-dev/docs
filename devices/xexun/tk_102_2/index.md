# Xexun - TK-102-2

![TK-102-2](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun TK-102-2 is an upgraded iteration of the widely used TK102 series. This model adds an SD card slot and a larger battery to the baseline feature set, and it is built around an ARM7 processor for responsive operation. It supports non server based location reporting and includes practical functions such as real time polling, SMS based auto tracking, voice surveillance, geo fence alerts, movement and overspeed warnings, SOS button support, and configurable phone authorization for controlled access.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK-102-2 can be used to feed location and alert information into the Plaspy platform for consolidated monitoring and reporting. Its onboard logging and standalone tracking capabilities make it a flexible option for deployments that need both local data recording and cloud based visibility. Plaspy can present the tracker data alongside other fleet devices to provide unified situational awareness.

## Key Highlights

- Onboard SD card slot for local recording of GPS standpoints and data retention.
- Larger battery designed to extend operational time between charges.
- ARM7 processor for efficient and responsive tracking behavior.
- Support for non server based tracking enabling standalone operation without a dedicated server.
- Remote polling and SMS based auto track options for querying position and status.
- Built in alert features including geo fence movement overspeed low battery and SOS functions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the TK-102-2 provides position updates and event alerts that Plaspy can display and manage as part of your fleet or asset view. Plaspy organizes incoming device reports into maps timelines and reports so operations teams get clear visibility and can act on exceptions quickly.

- Display live location and recent history for each TK-102-2 device on the Plaspy map.
- Configure alert rules and notifications in Plaspy for geofence events overspeed movement and low battery reports.
- Use Plaspy reporting to analyze location history and event trends for operational insight.
- Combine TK-102-2 data with other devices in Plaspy for unified fleet monitoring and oversight.
- Retain local SD logged standpoints on the device as a fallback for post analysis or audit when needed.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal vehicle tracking for safety and location awareness.
- Small to medium sized fleet monitoring where local logging and flexible reporting are valuable.
- Asset protection and recovery for mobile equipment or valuable items.
- Remote deployments that benefit from standalone tracking capabilities and SMS based polling.
- Situations where concise alerting such as SOS geo fence or low battery improves operational response.

The TK-102-2 is a practical choice for organizations that value both device level data retention and cloud based monitoring.
