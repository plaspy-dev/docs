The Xexun TK-202 is presented as a compact, wearable GPS tracker intended for a wide range of tracking roles. The description supplied emphasizes versatility and practical use across vehicles, personal wear, and asset attachment, and highlights support for connecting to external devices to extend functionality.

- Compact and wearable form factor suitable for use as a watch or attached to keys or collars
- Real time location tracking for vehicles people and portable equipment
- Multi use design that can serve vehicle tracking, asset monitoring, and personal protection
- Ability to connect with external devices to expand practical applications
- Practical for business use including field staff oversight and fleet monitoring
