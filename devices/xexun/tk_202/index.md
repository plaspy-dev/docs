# Xexun - TK-202

![TK-202](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun TK-202 is a compact, wearable GPS tracker designed for flexible tracking of vehicles, assets, and people. Marketed as a multifunction device, the TK-202 can be used as a watch or attached to a keychain or pet collar, and the manufacturer highlights uses that range from private car tracking and rental fleet oversight to protection of children, elderly or disabled individuals. The model is described as reliable and user friendly, intended to provide accurate and real time location information for a variety of personal and business applications.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TK-202 can be integrated into a centralized tracking platform to give organizations and individuals consistent location visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy compatibility allows data from the tracker to appear within a single fleet or asset management view, helping businesses monitor field staff, manage company vehicles, and keep tabs on valuable equipment while also supporting personal safety use cases.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the TK-202 provides location updates and device visibility within the Plaspy platform so users can monitor assets in one place. Plaspy receives the device data and presents it on maps and dashboards for ongoing operational awareness and historical review.

- View live location and recent movement history for TK-202 devices from Plaspy map views
- Organize trackers by vehicle asset or person for streamlined fleet and personnel oversight
- Configure notifications and alerts in Plaspy for movement events and location changes
- Generate trip and location reports for operational analysis and record keeping
- Use centralized device lists and dashboards to assign and monitor field staff activity
