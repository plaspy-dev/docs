# Xexun - TK-102

![TK-102](./logo.jpg)

The Xexun TK-102 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a range of features to meet your tracking needs. With its SD card slot, you can easily save GPRS data directly onto an SD card, ensuring that you never miss any important information. This feature is especially useful for those who require extensive data storage or want to have a backup of their tracking data.

In addition to the SD card slot, the TK-102 also boasts a larger battery and an ARM7 processor, providing enhanced performance and longer battery life. This means that you can rely on the TK-102 to track your assets or loved ones for extended periods without worrying about the battery running out.

The TK-102 offers a wide range of support and features to make tracking convenient and efficient. It supports non-server based location, allowing you to track your device without the need for a dedicated server. The SD card memory enables you to record GPS standpoints, providing a comprehensive record of your device's movements.

With the TK-102, you can authorize or delete up to 5 preset phone numbers, ensuring that only authorized individuals can access the tracking information. Real-time polling, auto-track by SMS, and voice surveillance features allow you to monitor your device in real-time and gather accurate information about its location and surroundings.

The TK-102 also offers a range of alert features, including geo-fence alert, movement alert, overspeed alert, low battery alert, and SOS button. These alerts help you stay informed and take immediate action in case of any emergencies or unauthorized movements.

Other notable features of the TK-102 include hidden number tracking, SMS center, GSM ID, motion sensor, and mobile tracking. These features enhance the functionality and usability of the tracker, making it an ideal choice for various tracking applications.
