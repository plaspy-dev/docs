# EElink - K6

![K6](./logo.jpg)

The EElink K6 is a versatile GPS tracker designed to protect and monitor mobile assets and people. It offers global quad band cellular support for broad geographic coverage, two way communication for quick contact, and a dedicated SOS button for emergency alerts. The device combines GPS with cell based positioning to provide position updates and supports data upload for real time tracking and history playback, while also supporting local track recording and export for offline analysis.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the K6 can be integrated into Plaspy fleet and asset workflows to centralize location data and alerts. Its core features map naturally to common Plaspy capabilities such as live location visibility, route history, and alarm handling. For organizations evaluating trackers for use with Plaspy, the K6 offers a practical balance of safety features, global reach, and data export options that support both operational monitoring and post trip analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Global quad band cellular support for use across many regions
- Two way voice capability and one key family call for simple communication
- Dedicated SOS button to trigger immediate attention and remote monitoring
- Dual positioning with GPS and cell based location to improve tracking reliability
- Real time tracking and history playback with local track export in PLT format
- Removable battery with low battery alarm and support for OTA updates and multiple protocols

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the K6 uploads location and status information so Plaspy can present live positions, playback routes, and surface important alarms. Plaspy acts as the centralized platform to view device activity across a fleet and to configure alerting and reporting that fit operational needs.

- Live location visibility for monitoring vehicles or assets in real time
- Route history playback and archived tracking for investigations and compliance
- Alarm forwarding and handling for SOS, geo fence, and low battery events
- Fleet level dashboards and reporting to monitor utilization and movement
- Export and analysis of recorded tracks for offline review and evidence

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring company vehicles and drivers for operational oversight
- Personal safety and family tracking where two way call and SOS are important
- Tracking rental equipment and movable assets between locations
- Delivery and logistics tracking with history playback for route verification
- Field service and remote worker location visibility for coordination

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The K6 is a practical option when you need a device that emphasizes coverage, basic voice contact, and straightforward tracking features. Its combination of GPS plus cell based positioning, removable battery and local track export provides flexibility for organizations that want both live oversight and the ability to retain recorded traces for later analysis. Plaspy complements these features by providing consolidated visibility, alert management, and fleet reporting across K6 units and other devices.
