# EElink - K6

![K6](./logo.jpg)

The EElink K6 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed to provide reliable location monitoring and basic telematics for assets and personnel. It supports global quad band cellular operation and combines GPS with LBS positioning to improve location availability. The device offers two way communication with a one key dial, an SOS button for emergency alerts, removable battery operation, and the ability to record local tracks and export PLT files for further review.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the K6 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and management. Its real time tracking and history playback features map directly to common Plaspy workflows for fleet visibility, alerts, and reporting. Organizations using Plaspy can incorporate K6 units into broader asset tracking sets while leveraging Plaspy dashboards and operational tools to monitor status and respond to incidents.

## Key Highlights

- Global quad band support for wide geographic coverage and flexible deployment
- Two way communication and one key dial for simple voice contact with preset numbers
- Dedicated SOS button for fast emergency signaling and remote monitoring
- Combined GPS and LBS positioning for improved location availability in mixed environments
- Real time tracking and history playback via cellular data uploads for operational oversight
- Removable battery with low battery alarm and local track recording with PLT export

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the K6 supplies location updates and device events that Plaspy displays and archives for operational use. Plaspy ingests the tracker's status and event notifications to provide unified visibility across fleets and assets.

- Display live location and device status on Plaspy maps for real time situational awareness
- Playback historical routes and exported tracks to review past movements and activity
- Surface critical alerts in Plaspy such as SOS, geo fence triggers, speed alerts, and low battery notices
- Include K6 units in fleet dashboards and reporting for utilization and operational analysis
- Use device event history to support incident investigations and operational planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking vehicles and trailers for basic fleet oversight and location reporting
- Monitoring high value portable assets that need periodic reporting and recovery capability
- Personal safety devices for lone workers or vulnerable individuals requiring an SOS function
- Short term rentals and field equipment where removable batteries and local recording are useful
- Small fleet operations that need global coverage and straightforward voice contact options
