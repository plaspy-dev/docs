# EElink - K6

![K6](./logo.jpg)

The EElink K6 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a wide range of features to ensure the safety and security of your assets. With support for quad bands \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\), this tracker can be used anywhere in the world, making it a truly universal solution. 

One of the standout features of the K6 is its two-way communication capability, allowing you to easily dial a family number with just one key. In case of emergencies, the tracker also has a dedicated SOS button for quick help and remote monitoring. 

The K6 utilizes GPS precise positioning and uploads data via GPRS, enabling real-time tracking and history track playback. It also features double module technology, combining GPS and LBS positioning for enhanced accuracy. 

With the Keelin Tracking Service Platform and Keelin APP Client, you can easily access and manage your tracker's data. The platform and app offer a range of features including real-time tracking, route playback, listening, SOS alarm, speed alarm, geo-fence alarm, and low battery alarm. 

The K6 is equipped with a removable battery and features a low battery alarm to ensure that you never lose track of your assets. It also has the ability to locally record GPS tracks and generate PLT files for further analysis. The tracker supports multiple protocols and can be upgraded via OTA for added convenience.
