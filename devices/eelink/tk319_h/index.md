# EElink - TK319‑H

![TK319‑H](./tracker.jpg)

The TK319‑H is a compact 3G GPS tracker designed for reliable vehicle tracking and telemetry. It provides real time tracking with GPS and base station positioning, AGPS assistance, and a suite of motion and safety alarms. The device includes ACC ignition detection, serial and GPIO expansion, and optional relay and temperature sensor interfaces, all packaged in a small form factor intended for diverse vehicle installations.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the TK319‑H can integrate directly with Plaspy’s fleet management platform. Telemetry, alarms, and location reports from the TK319‑H are ingested by Plaspy to provide live map views, alerting, trip history, and operational oversight, making it a practical choice for fleets and asset managers who want fast onboarding and centralized monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible using MoveLink EELINK protocol for straightforward integration into Plaspy dashboards and workflows.
- Real time tracking with GPS and LBS positioning plus AGPS support for improved location responsiveness.
- Built in alarms for crash, overspeed, vibration, acceleration and deceleration to support safety and incident detection.
- ACC ignition detection and serial GPIO expansion for telemetry and state monitoring relevant to fleet operations.
- Optional relay and temperature sensor interfaces enable immobilization workflows and environmental monitoring when needed.
- Compact size and wide DC input range for flexible vehicle installations while maintaining continuity with backup battery alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is designed to be simple: the TK319‑H transmits location updates, status messages, and alarm events to Plaspy where they are visualized, logged, and used to trigger operational workflows. Plaspy receives the device messages and converts them into actionable information for fleet oversight, alerts, and reporting.

- Real time location and telemetry updates feed Plaspy map visualization and vehicle routing views.
- ACC or ignition detection helps Plaspy distinguish driving versus parked states for accurate reports and utilization metrics.
- Safety and motion alarms are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification, incident logging, and dispatcher workflows.
- Optional relay control can be used from Plaspy to enact immobilization or controlled power cut off in approved scenarios.
- Base station LBS fallback maintains approximate location updates when GPS signals are degraded, helping Plaspy preserve tracking continuity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for continuous vehicle tracking, utilization reporting, and route oversight.
- Rental vehicle monitoring to track usage, ignition status, and help enforce policies.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using alarms and optional remote cut off managed through Plaspy.
- Crash and safety incident detection to notify dispatchers and log events for review.
- Temperature aware installs for sensitive cargo where an optional temperature sensor is fitted and monitored via Plaspy.
