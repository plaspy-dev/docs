# EElink - GPT12

![GPT12](./logo.jpg)

The EElink GPT12 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your assets. With support for quad bands \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\), this tracker can be used anywhere in the world. It utilizes GPS/LBS double-positioning mode, with support for A-GPS, to provide accurate and reliable location information. 

One of the standout features of the GPT12 is its impressive battery life. With a 5000mAh battery, this tracker can standby for up to 3 years. It also has a long standby mode, where it wakes up for just 5 minutes a day before going back to sleep. In emergency situations, the tracker can switch to emergency mode for real-time tracking. 

The GPT12 also offers additional security features such as GEO-fence alarm and low battery alarm. These alerts can be configured to notify you when the tracker enters or exits a predefined area or when the battery is running low. 

Tracking and configuring the device is made easy with the PC, APP, and SMS query options. You can track the device in real-time, playback routes, and remotely configure settings. The GPT12 also supports EELINK protocol and can be integrated into custom platforms. Firmware updates can be done over-the-air \(OTA\) for added convenience. 

The platform and app provided with the GPT12 offer a range of features including emergency mode tracking, route playback, remote setting configuration, geo-fence alarm, and low battery alarm. These features ensure that you have full control and visibility over your assets at all times.
