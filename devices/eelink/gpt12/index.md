# EElink - GPT12

![GPT12](./logo.jpg)

The EElink GPT12 is a compact, versatile GPS tracker designed to protect and monitor assets over long intervals. It combines GPS and LBS double positioning with A GPS assistance and quad band cellular support to enable location reporting across many regions. The device emphasizes extended autonomy with a 5000mAh battery, long standby modes and an emergency tracking option for situations that require real time updates.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GPT12 can contribute reliable location and alert data to Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its geofence alarm, low battery notifications, remote query options and support for the EELINK protocol make it straightforward to integrate device signals into Plaspy dashboards, route playback and operational alerts, providing teams with actionable visibility over assets.

## Key Highlights

- Global quad band support for broad regional coverage  
- GPS and LBS double positioning with A GPS assistance for improved location reliability  
- Large capacity battery with very long standby life and sparse wake cycles for low maintenance  
- Emergency mode for real time tracking when immediate visibility is required  
- Configurable geo fence alarm and low battery alerting for proactive management  
- Remote tracking and configuration via PC, app, and SMS plus OTA firmware update capability

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location updates and alert events from compatible trackers like the GPT12 to present unified fleet and asset views. When integrated, GPT12 data appears in Plaspy tools for live tracking, historical playback and alert handling so operators can monitor activity and respond to exceptions.

- Real time location and emergency mode events shown in Plaspy dashboards  
- Geofence entry and exit alerts routed into Plaspy notifications and rules  
- Low battery alerts surfaced to help schedule maintenance and replacements  
- Route playback and history available in Plaspy for review and reporting needs  
- Centralized fleet monitoring combining GPT12 devices with other assets for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset monitoring where minimal maintenance and long battery life matter  
- Remote equipment or containers that report infrequently but require reliable location data  
- Fleet and vehicle tracking across regions that benefit from wide cellular band support  
- Portable or personal asset tracking with emergency tracking capability  
- Logistics and shipment oversight using geofence alerts and route playback for audits

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPT12 is a practical choice for organizations that need a balance of long battery life and reliable location reporting. Its global cellular compatibility and dual positioning approach help maintain visibility in diverse operating environments, while geofence and low battery alerts support routine operational workflows. For teams using Plaspy, the GPT12 provides event types and history that fit naturally into centralized monitoring, alerting, and reporting processes.
