# EElink - GPT12

![GPT12](./logo.jpg)

The EElink GPT12 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker designed to provide dependable location and security monitoring for assets. It combines multiple positioning methods, global cellular band support, and an integrated platform experience for real time tracking, route playback, and configurable alerts. The device emphasizes long standby operation and offers emergency mode for more frequent reporting when needed.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GPT12 can be used within Plaspy's fleet and asset management workflows to give teams persistent visibility and actionable alerts. Plaspy users can incorporate GPT12 units into monitoring groups, view location history, and use geo fence and low battery notifications to support operational oversight and asset protection.

## Key Highlights

- Dual positioning support with GPS and LBS positioning modes for flexible coverage.
- Global band compatibility for use in multiple regions.
- Very long standby life enabled by a high capacity battery and low duty cycle operation.
- Emergency mode for increased reporting during critical events.
- Configurable geo fence alarm and low battery alarm for proactive alerts.
- Multiple query and configuration options via PC app and SMS for on field adjustments.
- Over the air firmware update capability and standard protocol support for integration.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GPT12 provides continuous location visibility and integrates its alerting features into the platform to support fleet monitoring and operational reporting. Plaspy can ingest the device's position updates and alarms to present dashboards, notifications, and historical playback for operational review.

- Real time location display in Plaspy for active tracking and situational awareness.
- Route playback and history to review past movements and analyze asset usage.
- Geo fence and low battery alerts routed into Plaspy notifications for prompt response.
- Emergency mode events surfaced in Plaspy for immediate attention by operations teams.
- Remote configuration and status checks coordinated through Plaspy alongside other devices.
- Integration-friendly protocol support that allows GPT12 units to be grouped and managed within Plaspy fleets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset monitoring where extended standby life reduces maintenance visits.
- Fleet tracking for light vehicles, rental units, or portable equipment.
- Remote equipment supervision in areas with intermittent connectivity.
- Security and recovery applications where geo fence alerts and emergency tracking are needed.
- Route verification and historical playback for operational audits and reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPT12 is a practical choice for organizations that need a balance of long battery life, global cellular reach, and core tracking features. Its emergency mode and configurable alarms complement Plaspy's platform tools, making it suitable for teams that need dependable visibility without frequent device maintenance.

Because the device supports standard platform integration and over the air updates, it can be managed alongside other trackers in Plaspy, simplifying fleet administration and reporting. Plaspy's dashboards and notification features help turn GPT12 location and alarm data into operational insights for security, logistics, and asset management teams.
