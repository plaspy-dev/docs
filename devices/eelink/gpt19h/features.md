# EElink - GPT19‑H Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the EElink GPT19‑H magnetic asset tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive details about tracking, event telemetry, and the types of monitoring and workflows you can build in Plaspy when the GPT19‑H is part of your fleet or asset inventory.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer implementation. Where timings, alarms or operational modes are described, they reflect the manufacturer description and typical use, but installation, configuration and firmware updates can change how individual units operate in the field.

## Feature Overview

The GPT19‑H is a covert magnetic asset tracker built for long term deployments where durability and minimal maintenance matter. When integrated with Plaspy, its location fixes and event alerts feed dashboards and notification workflows that support anti theft, perimeter control and inventory visibility.

- Magnetic mounting and IP67 rated enclosure for discreet outdoor placement on trailers, containers and equipment
- GPS plus LBS hybrid positioning to improve location reporting in marginal signal environments
- Replaceable 4200 mAh battery with configurable wake intervals for extended standby life and reduced maintenance visits
- Emergency continuous tracking mode for live situational awareness during recovery events
- Vibration wake up, motion and fall alerts, and a light sensor tamper alarm to provide actionable telemetry to Plaspy

## Core Features of EElink - GPT19‑H

- Magnetic asset tracker form factor designed for covert mounting on heavy assets and containers
- GPS positioning combined with LBS cell based location for broader coverage in urban or obstructed areas
- Removable 4200 mAh battery with documented standby profiles based on wake interval settings
- Configurable wake intervals to balance reporting frequency and battery longevity
- Emergency mode supporting continuous real time tracking for high priority recovery scenarios
- Vibration wake up plus motion and fall alert reporting for tamper and movement detection
- Light sensor tamper alarm to detect cover removal or tampering attempts
- Remote configuration over the air to adjust operational parameters without physical access

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the GPT19‑H location and event data and maps those events to visual tracking, alerts and reporting within a single fleet or asset management view. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and can receive device reports so telemetry appears in live maps and audit logs alongside other fleet data.

- Real time location and telemetry updates shown on Plaspy maps including normal periodic reporting and emergency continuous tracking
- Motion, fall and light sensor tamper alerts translated into Plaspy events and routed to notification channels you configure
- Geofence enter and exit monitoring based on the device GPS and LBS fixes to support perimeter control and anti theft workflows
- Remote configuration actions sent from Plaspy to adjust wake intervals, reporting cadence and alarm thresholds without site visits
- Plaspy consolidates GPT19‑H telemetry with other fleet device data for unified operational visibility across vehicle and asset inventories

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert trailer and container tracking for long term visibility and theft recovery
- Construction and heavy equipment monitoring where discreet mounting and ruggedness are required
- Seasonal asset management and storage monitoring using low wake intervals to preserve battery
- Site perimeter enforcement and geofence monitoring for high value assets
- Emergency recovery scenarios that require switching to continuous real time tracking
- Inventory visibility for dispersed assets where maintenance access is limited
