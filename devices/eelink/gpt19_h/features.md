# EElink - GPT19‑H Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the EElink GPT19‑H magnetic asset tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It covers the practical, non sensitive aspects of the device that matter when integrating the unit into Plaspy for long term asset visibility, anti theft workflows and remote monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as guidance on what to expect in Plaspy and consult the official EElink documentation for the most current device specifics.

## Feature Overview

The GPT19‑H is a compact magnetic asset tracker built for long term covert deployments where battery life, durability and discreet mounting are priorities. In Plaspy the device contributes location updates and event telemetry that support perimeter monitoring, recovery workflows and inventory tracking.

- Magnetic mounting and IP67 rated enclosure for discreet, weather resistant attachment to trailers, containers and equipment.
- Hybrid location using GPS plus cell based LBS for better coverage in marginal signal conditions.
- Replaceable 4200 mAh battery with configurable wake intervals delivering multi year standby life under typical settings.
- Emergency continuous tracking mode for live, high priority recovery and situational awareness.
- Motion, fall and vibration wake alerts plus a light sensor based tamper alarm for actionable anti theft telemetry.
- Remote configuration support to adjust reporting cadence and alarm thresholds without site visits.

## Core Features of EElink - GPT19‑H

- Magnetic form factor with rugged IP67 protection for covert and outdoor mounting.
- GPS positioning combined with LBS cell based fixes for hybrid location reporting.
- Replaceable 4200 mAh battery and configurable wake intervals to maximize standby life.
- Emergency mode for continuous real time tracking during recovery events.
- Motion detection and vibration wake up to conserve power while remaining responsive to movement.
- Fall detection and a light sensor tamper alarm to surface potential tampering or unexpected events.
- Remote over the air configuration to modify reporting intervals and alarm settings.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GPT19‑H forwards its position fixes and event signals into a single fleet or asset management view. Plaspy maps the device telemetry to visual markers, alerts and historical records so teams can act on movement and tamper information without digging through device logs.

- Live position updates and support for emergency continuous tracking are visible in Plaspy maps and tracking panels.
- Motion, fall and tamper events become Plaspy alerts that can trigger notifications and escalation workflows.
- GPS and LBS fixes feed geofence enter and exit logic in Plaspy for perimeter control and audit trails.
- Remote configuration commands issued from Plaspy allow tuning of wake intervals and reporting cadence where the device and firmware support it.
- Historical telemetry and event logs stored in Plaspy support post event analysis and recovery documentation.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert trailer and container tracking for long term cargo visibility and recovery readiness.
- Construction site and heavy equipment monitoring to detect unauthorized movement and respond quickly.
- Seasonal asset management where long standby life reduces maintenance trips while retaining periodic telemetry.
- Perimeter enforcement and geofence monitoring for stored assets and yards.
- High priority recovery operations that switch the device into emergency continuous tracking for live pursuit.
