# EElink - GPT19‑H

![GPT19‑H](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT19‑H is a magnetic asset tracker designed for long term covert deployments and reliable remote monitoring. Built with a powerful magnet and IP67 protection, it is intended for discreet mounting on trailers, containers, equipment and other assets where durability and low maintenance are priorities. The device combines GPS and LBS positioning and supports motion, fall and tamper alerts to provide ongoing situational awareness.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the GPT19‑H integrates its location and event telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and alerting workflows. Its long standby life from a replaceable 4200 mAh battery and configurable wake intervals reduce maintenance visits, while emergency continuous tracking and remote configuration make it a practical choice for asset visibility, anti‑theft monitoring and operational oversight in Plaspy fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS and LBS asset tracker for continuous remote monitoring and fleet visibility.
- Replaceable 4200 mAh battery with long standby life up to about 2 to 3 years depending on wake interval settings.
- Emergency mode supports continuous real time tracking for high priority recovery events.
- Covert magnetic mounting and IP67 ruggedness suited to trailers, containers and outdoor equipment.
- Motion and fall alerts, vibration wake up and light sensor tamper alarm provide actionable anti theft telemetry.
- Hybrid GPS plus LBS positioning improves location reporting in marginal signal conditions.
- Remote configuration capability reduces the need for onsite adjustments and allows tuning from Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, GPT19‑H location fixes and event data are sent into a single fleet or asset management view so operators can monitor live positions, trigger alarms and produce reports. Plaspy maps the tracker’s motion, fall and tamper alerts into notifications and stores event history for operational follow up and recovery workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry updates including normal reporting and emergency continuous tracking modes.
- Motion, fall and tamper events routed as Plaspy notifications to support anti theft response.
- Geofence enter and exit events generated by Plaspy using the device GPS and LBS data for perimeter control.
- Remote over the air configuration to adjust wake intervals, reporting cadence and alarm thresholds from Plaspy.
- Complementary integration options: while the GPT19‑H does not include ignition or immobilizer inputs, Plaspy can consolidate its telemetry with data from other devices in the same fleet for unified reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking for long term visibility and recovery readiness.
- Construction and heavy equipment anti theft monitoring with vibration and tamper alerts.
- Seasonal or stored asset monitoring where extended battery life reduces maintenance.
- Site perimeter and geofence enforcement to detect unexpected movement or boundary breaches.
- High risk recovery scenarios that require activating continuous real time tracking for pursuit and handoff.

The GPT19‑H is well suited to organizations that need durable, discreet and low maintenance asset tracking integrated into a broader fleet management platform. Its replaceable battery and configurable wake intervals help lower operating costs for dispersed or infrequently accessed assets, while IP67 protection and a strong magnet support secure placement in tough environments.
