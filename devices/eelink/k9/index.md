# EElink - K9

![K9](./logo.jpg)

The EElink K9 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a wide range of features to ensure the safety and security of your loved ones or valuable assets. With support for quad bands \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\), this tracker can be used anywhere in the world, making it a truly universal solution.

One of the standout features of the EElink K9 is its two-way communication capability, allowing you to easily dial a family number with just one key. In case of an emergency, the tracker also has a dedicated SOS button for immediate help, as well as the ability to remotely monitor the surroundings.

Thanks to its GPS and LBS positioning, the EElink K9 provides precise and accurate location information, which is regularly uploaded via GPRS for real-time tracking. You can also access the history track playback feature to review past routes and movements. Additionally, the tracker supports GEO-Fence and speed alarms, ensuring that you are promptly notified if the device enters or exits a predefined area or exceeds a certain speed limit.

The EElink K9 is equipped with a removable battery, and it will alert you when the battery is running low. It also has the capability to locally record GPS tracks and generate PLT files for further analysis. The tracker supports multiple protocols and can be easily upgraded via OTA \(Over-The-Air\) to ensure you always have the latest features and improvements.

The EElink K9 GPS tracker comes with a user-friendly platform and mobile app, allowing you to access real-time tracking, route playback, and various alarm features. You can listen in to the tracker's surroundings, receive SOS alarms, speed alarms, geo-fence alarms, and low battery alarms, all from the convenience of your smartphone or computer.

#### Outstanding Features:

- Quad band support for global use
- Two-way communication with one-key dialing
- SOS button for emergency assistance
- GPS and LBS positioning for accurate tracking
- Real-time tracking and history track playback
- GEO-Fence and speed alarms for added security
- Removable battery with low battery alarm
- Local record of GPS tracks and PLT file generation
- Support for multiple protocols and OTA upgrades
- User-friendly platform and mobile app with various alarm features
