# EElink - GPT45

<div className="device-logo">
![GPT45](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GPT45 is a compact, rechargeable GPS tracker from EElink designed for vehicles and portable assets that need continuous tracking and extended runtime. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback, a high capacity rechargeable battery, and an IP65 rated enclosure to provide resilient location updates and basic environmental durability. Onboard motion and environmental sensors add telemetry beyond simple coordinates, making the device suitable for monitoring both location and asset condition.

As a Plaspy compatible device the GPT45 can feed its location fixes and sensor events into Plaspy for live monitoring, historical playback, alerting, and reporting. Optional Bluetooth gateway capability and USB‑C support simplify sensor integration and maintenance while EELINK protocol compatibility helps connect the device to Plaspy powered fleet and asset workflows. For organizations using Plaspy, the GPT45 supplies rich telemetry that can be used to automate notifications and improve operational visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker with multi constellation GNSS and fallback positioning for resilient location updates
- Large rechargeable battery for extended operation between charges suitable for vehicles and portable assets
- Built in motion and environmental sensors including collision, vibration, fall and speed alarms plus temperature humidity and barometric pressure sensing
- Optional Bluetooth gateway functionality to relay beacon and sensor data into monitoring workflows
- USB C support for convenient firmware management and device maintenance
- Compact IP65 enclosure for discreet mounting and outdoor use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the GPT45 streams position and sensor data so teams can monitor assets in real time and act on events. Plaspy ingests device messages and exposes them via dashboards, geofences, alerts, and reports for operational oversight and analysis.

- Real time location and fallback positioning feed Plaspy dashboards for live tracking and route visibility
- Event driven alarms such as collision vibration fall and speed alerts trigger Plaspy notifications and workflows
- Environmental telemetry is recorded in Plaspy for condition monitoring and trend reporting
- Bluetooth beacon and sensor data can be forwarded into Plaspy when the tracker is used as a gateway
- Remote configuration and firmware management workflows help keep devices up to date and aligned with Plaspy settings

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and driver safety monitoring using location plus speed and collision events
- Anti theft protection with vibration and geofence alerts routed into Plaspy notifications
- Condition monitoring for sensitive cargo using temperature humidity and pressure readings
- Beacon based proximity tracking and asset tagging when deployed as a Bluetooth gateway
- Portable equipment tracking where rechargeable power and a compact IP65 housing are priorities

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPT45 is a practical choice for teams that need a balance of compact design rechargeable operation and multi sensor telemetry. Its combination of resilient positioning fallback environmental sensing and optional Bluetooth gateway capability makes it useful across fleet management asset protection and environmental monitoring scenarios. Integration with Plaspy brings those data streams into a single platform for visualization alerting and reporting without adding unnecessary hardware complexity.
