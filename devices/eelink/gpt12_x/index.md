# EElink - GPT12‑X

<div className="device-logo">
![GPT12‑X](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GPT12‑X is a slim, long‑standby GPS tracker intended for multi‑year deployments and enterprise scale monitoring. It combines low‑power wide area cellular connectivity with multi‑GNSS positioning to provide dependable real‑time tracking, event alarms, and extended battery life for assets and fleets that require low maintenance and consistent uptime. The device is built for harsh conditions and includes event detection features to support security and recovery workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the GPT12‑X integrates via the EELINK protocol so telemetry, geofencing and alarms can be routed into Plaspy dashboards and workflows with minimal setup. Its long standby profile and remote management capabilities make it a practical option for large rollouts where reduced field visits and centralized lifecycle management are priorities for operations teams using Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via native EELINK protocol support for efficient integration and telemetry ingestion.
- Ultra‑long standby operation from a 5000 mAh battery, designed to reduce maintenance frequency.
- Dual LPWA cellular options for broad low‑power coverage and cost effective data transfer.
- Multi‑GNSS positioning for improved location reliability across challenging environments.
- Built‑in event intelligence including vibration wake‑up, collision and fall detection, and light‑sensor tamper alarms.
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware maintenance to simplify large scale device management.
- Rugged, slim and waterproof housing suitable for flexible mounting on vehicles or portable assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the GPT12‑X sends compact telemetry and alarm messages over LPWA cellular using the EELINK protocol. Plaspy ingests those messages to populate live maps, trigger alerts, and produce historical reports, while remote configuration and firmware updates can be managed centrally from the platform. This enables continuous operational visibility with minimal device maintenance.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates visible on Plaspy live maps.
- Collision, fall and vibration alarms routed to Plaspy for immediate incident notification.
- Tamper alerts from the light sensor to detect enclosure access and trigger workflows.
- Geofence entry and exit events with configurable zone alarms for automated monitoring.
- Battery level and standby/emergency mode notifications to help manage device health and maintenance planning.
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware updates coordinated through Plaspy for consistent fleet settings.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet monitoring for low‑usage or seasonal vehicles where multi‑year battery life reduces service visits.
- Anti‑theft and security monitoring for parked vehicles, trailers, or portable equipment.
- Remote asset tracking for containers, storage units, and offsite infrastructure where LPWA coverage is advantageous.
- Emergency recovery tracking where the device can switch from standby to frequent updates during retrieval operations.
- Geofence and telemetry driven operational workflows that require centralized alerting and reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPT12‑X is well suited to organizations that need low‑touch, high‑reliability tracking for large numbers of dispersed assets. Its combination of long standby life, multi‑GNSS positioning, and LPWA cellular connectivity complements Plaspy’s strengths in centralized monitoring, alerting, and reporting. EELINK protocol integration and remote management reduce the operational burden of firmware updates and configuration changes across a fleet.
