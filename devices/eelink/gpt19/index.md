# EElink - GPT19

![GPT19](./logo..jpg)

The EElink GPT19-H Long-Lasting GPS Tracker is a reliable and versatile solution for various applications such as vehicle rental, logistics, property conservation, and the Internet of Things \(IoT\). With its IP67 water resistance, this tracker is capable of withstanding adverse conditions and continue to function optimally. Additionally, its magnetic installation makes it easy to discreetly and securely place on any vehicle or asset.

One of the standout features of the GPT19-H is its long-lasting battery life, which can be easily replaced. This allows for continuous monitoring without the need for frequent recharging. It also features advanced functions such as dual-way GPS/LBS, providing fast and accurate location through AGPS and LBS assistance. It also has a long standby mode, allowing for daily activation for up to 2 years or activation every two days for up to 3 years.

The GPT19-H tracker also offers notifications and remote control for added security and convenience. With the GEO-Fence alarm, an alert can be set up when the device enters or exits a specific area. Additionally, remote configuration can be done through the server, application, or text messages \(SMS\).

In terms of technical specifications, the GPT19-H has dimensions of 95mm in length, 58mm in width, and 24.5mm in height, with a weight of 152g. It has a 4200mAh battery and a voltage of 3.6V. It also has certifications such as PTCRB, GCF, FCC, IC, Anatel, AT&T, and CCC, ensuring its quality and compatibility. The tracker is also compatible with the EELINK 2.0 integration protocol, making it easy to integrate into different platforms.

In summary, the EElink GPT19-H Long-Lasting GPS Tracker is a reliable and versatile choice for various applications. With its water resistance, magnetic installation, and long-lasting battery life, it offers continuous and accurate monitoring. Additionally, its advanced features and remote notifications provide added security and control. With certifications and compatibility with the EELINK 2.0 integration protocol, this tracker is a reliable and user-friendly solution.
