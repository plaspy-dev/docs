# EElink - GPT50

![GPT50](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT50 is a heavy duty GPS tracker built for long life deployments and robust field use. Designed for asset and vehicle tracking where power availability is limited, the device combines multi GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback and global cellular connectivity to deliver reliable location data and telemetry. Its rugged IP67 enclosure and extended standby capability make it suitable for trailers, containers, heavy equipment and other dispersed assets that require persistent monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GPT50 streams position and telemetry to Plaspy for real time tracking, fleet oversight and historical reporting. The tracker’s selectable working modes and remote management capabilities allow organizations to balance battery life and tracking fidelity while using Plaspy to centralize alerts, geofence rules, and device provisioning across large fleets or widely distributed assets.

## Key Highlights

- Long standby endurance with dual 12000 mAh lithium batteries providing extended deployments and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Multi GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback for improved location reliability in urban and indoor environments.
- Global cellular coverage on LTE and GSM networks to support consistent tracking across regions.
- Multiple working modes including long standby, emergency real time mode, and sensor triggered wake up for flexible reporting strategies.
- Integrated temperature sensing and geofencing support for basic environmental telemetry and location based alerts.
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and wide operating temperature range suited to outdoor and industrial asset tracking.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GPT50 delivers GNSS positions and device telemetry over cellular links so Plaspy can present live location, status, and historic tracks in a single platform. Plaspy ingests the device data and applies rules, notifications and reports appropriate to your fleet or asset management workflows.

- Live location and device state updates to support real time visibility and operational oversight in Plaspy dashboards.
- Temperature telemetry and geofence events that can trigger alerts, notifications, and automated workflows in Plaspy.
- Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback increases useful fixes when GNSS performance is degraded, improving Plaspy map accuracy for indoor or urban assets.
- Configurable wake up triggers and emergency mode allow Plaspy to receive high frequency updates when immediate tracking is required.
- Remote configuration and over the air updates enable centralized tuning of reporting intervals and thresholds through Plaspy management tools.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trailers, leased vehicles and off grid equipment with minimal maintenance access.
- Asset anti theft monitoring and recovery using geofencing and emergency real time tracking combined with Plaspy alerts.
- Remote equipment and container monitoring where temperature telemetry and periodic checks are needed.
- Heavy equipment tracking in construction, mining and staging yards that demand rugged hardware.
- Seasonal or infrequently accessed assets where long standby life and periodic location verification are priorities.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPT50 is a practical choice for organizations that need a durable, low maintenance tracker paired with a centralized tracking platform. Its long standby capability helps lower total cost of ownership for distributed assets, while multi GNSS positioning and fallback methods improve the consistency of location data used for routing, historical analysis, and incident investigations. Using Plaspy as the management and reporting layer enables scalable device provisioning, consolidated alerts, and operational reporting across mixed fleets and asset types.
