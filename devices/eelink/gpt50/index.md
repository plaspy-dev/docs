# EElink - GPT50

![GPT50](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT50 is a heavy duty GPS tracker built for long life deployments and reliable field operation. It combines multi GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback and global cellular connectivity to provide continuous location data and device telemetry. The unit is designed with endurance in mind, featuring dual high capacity batteries, an IP67 rated enclosure and a wide operating temperature range suitable for trailers, containers, heavy equipment and other assets that operate in challenging environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device the GPT50 can stream position updates and telemetry into Plaspy for live tracking and fleet management. Its selectable working modes and remote management capabilities let Plaspy apply business rules that balance battery life with tracking fidelity. For organizations that need long term unattended deployments, the GPT50 provides a practical hardware option to feed Plaspy with the information needed for alerts, reporting and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Extremely long standby life with dual high capacity batteries for minimal maintenance cycles
- Multi GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to improve fixes in indoor and urban areas
- Global cellular connectivity for consistent reporting across regions
- Multiple working modes including long standby and emergency real time mode to balance battery and responsiveness
- Built in temperature telemetry and geofencing support for environmental monitoring and alerts
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and wide operating temperature for outdoor and industrial use
- Remote configuration and over the air updates to simplify fleet provisioning and maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GPT50 sends position and telemetry data to Plaspy over cellular connections so that device state and location are available in real time. Plaspy ingests these updates to present live location views, trigger alerts, and generate historical reports. Because the tracker supports different reporting modes and event triggers, Plaspy can enforce fleet level policies to manage battery consumption while keeping critical assets visible when they need it.

- Real time location and telemetry delivered to Plaspy for live visibility and routing decisions
- Temperature readings and device state available for environmental monitoring and alarm generation in Plaspy dashboards
- Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback improve usable fixes when GNSS signals are weak or obstructed
- Event driven tracking via light or motion triggers and an emergency mode for immediate high frequency updates
- Remote configuration and OTA updates allow administrators to adjust reporting intervals thresholds and behavior centrally through Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term fleet and trailer tracking where battery life and unattended operation matter
- Asset anti theft monitoring with geofencing and emergency tracking support
- Remote equipment monitoring with periodic telemetry and alarming for environmental conditions
- Heavy equipment and container tracking in construction mining and staging yards
- Seasonal or infrequently accessed assets that require periodic location checks without frequent maintenance

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPT50 is a good match for organizations that need durable low maintenance hardware feeding a modern fleet management platform. Its emphasis on long standby life and rugged construction reduces physical service needs for widely distributed assets, while multi GNSS positioning and fallback mechanisms help improve location reliability for both live tracking and historical analysis. Plaspy compatible integration adds centralized device provisioning automated alerts and consolidated reporting so teams can operationalize the GPT50 across fleets and asset groups.
