# EElink - GPT50

![GPT50](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT50 is a heavy-duty GPS tracker designed for long-life deployments and seamless Plaspy compatible integration. Built for asset and vehicle tracking where power availability is limited, the GPT50 combines multi-GNSS positioning, Wi‑Fi/LBS fallback and global cellular connectivity to deliver reliable location data and telemetry to Plaspy for real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.

The device is engineered for endurance and field reliability: two high-capacity 12000 mAh lithium batteries provide up to ten years of standby time, while the rugged IP67 enclosure and an operating range from -20 °C to 65 °C make the GPT50 suitable for heavy equipment, trailers, containers and other assets that require anti-theft monitoring, persistent telemetry, and emergency real-time tracking through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Extreme standby endurance — up to ten years with dual 12000 mAh batteries for long-term asset deployments.
- Multi-GNSS positioning \(GPS, BDS, GLONASS\) with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to improve location accuracy indoors and in urban canyons.
- Global cellular coverage on LTE FDD/TDD and GSM networks for consistent real-time tracking across regions.
- Multiple working modes: long-standby daily wake-up, emergency real-time mode, and sensor-triggered wake-up \(light or motion\) to balance battery life and responsiveness.
- Integrated temperature sensor and geofencing support for environmental telemetry and location-based alerts.
- Rugged IP67-rated housing, compact form factor \(130 × 103 × 30 mm\), and wide operating temperature for outdoor and industrial use.
- Remote configuration and OTA updates simplify fleet rollout, maintenance, and firmware management through Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GPT50 streams precise GNSS positions and device telemetry to Plaspy using secure cellular connections. Plaspy ingests location updates, temperature readings and device state to provide real-time tracking, alerts, and historical reports. Because the GPT50 supports selectable working modes, Plaspy can apply business rules to balance power consumption and tracking fidelity across large fleets or dispersed assets.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live asset visibility and routing.
- Temperature telemetry available for environmental monitoring and alarm generation via Plaspy dashboards.
- Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback improves indoor/urban positioning and increases the frequency of useful fixes when GNSS is degraded.
- Configurable wake-up triggers \(light/motion\) enable event-driven tracking, with emergency real-time mode for immediate high-frequency updates.
- Remote configuration and OTA updates let administrators tune device behavior, thresholds, and reporting intervals centrally through Plaspy.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: long-term tracking of trailers, leased vehicles, and off-grid equipment with minimal maintenance cycles.
- Asset anti-theft and recovery: geofencing, emergency real-time tracking and Plaspy alerts help detect unauthorized movement and support recovery workflows.
- Remote equipment monitoring: temperature telemetry and motion-triggered wake-ups for battery-powered sensors on generators, pumps, or storage containers.
- Heavy equipment and container tracking: rugged IP67 enclosure and wide temperature range make the GPT50 suited for construction, mining and maritime staging yards.
- Infrequently accessed assets: long-standby mode for seasonal assets or dormant inventory where long battery life and periodic location checks are critical.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

When combined with Plaspy, the GPT50 becomes a proven solution for organisations that need durable, low-maintenance GPS tracker hardware with enterprise-grade telemetry. Its decade-scale standby capability reduces total cost of ownership for widely distributed assets, while multi-GNSS positioning and Wi‑Fi/LBS fallback increase fix reliability for real-time tracking and historical route analysis. Plaspy-compatible integration enables centralized fleet management, automated geofence alerts, and scalable device provisioning and OTA updates — supporting anti-theft measures, telemetry-driven maintenance, and operational reporting. For fleets and asset managers that also use fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer systems and external Bluetooth sensors, Plaspy can consolidate GPT50 location and sensor telemetry with those additional data sources to deliver a single pane of operational insight without implying the GPT50 itself directly provides those specific vehicle interfaces.
