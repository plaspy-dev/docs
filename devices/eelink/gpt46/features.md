# EElink - GPT46 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the EElink GPT46 and how its capabilities map to monitoring and fleet workflows in Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing functions and practical integration points, describing what the tracker is optimized for and how Plaspy can surface its location and telemetry in dashboards, alerts, and history views.

Exact feature availability and behavior for any specific unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation choices. For the most current and device specific information verify details with the manufacturer and review firmware release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The GPT46 is a compact LTE Cat M1/NB1 GPS tracker with BLE host capability designed for long-standby, low-power asset monitoring in logistics, transport, and movable asset programs. It is purpose-built to deliver consistent position reporting and BLE sensor integration while operating with centralized fleet management platforms such as Plaspy.

- Reliable real-time location reporting over cellular IoT networks to support operational visibility.
- Long-standby, low-power design that reduces maintenance visits and preserves battery life for long deployments.
- BLE host support to pair with Bluetooth sensors and beacons for added environmental or presence context.
- Compact, concealment-friendly industrial form factor suited to trailers, containers, equipment, and other movable assets.
- Designed for scalable deployment in fleet and anti-theft programs with centralized reporting into Plaspy.
- Optimized telemetry that supports history playback, movement detection, and status checks in monitoring workflows.

## Core Features of EElink - GPT46

- LTE Cat M1 and NB1 cellular connectivity for low-power wide area networking and consistent telemetry.
- GPS position fixes for location awareness and route history.
- BLE host functionality to pair with Bluetooth sensors and beacons for richer asset context.
- Long standby and infrequent reporting modes aimed at reducing field maintenance.
- Compact industrial design intended for discreet installation on transport and non-powered assets.
- Designed to integrate with cloud platforms for centralized reporting and monitoring.
- Optimized for logistics and movable asset management scenarios where battery life and reliable reporting matter.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location updates and telemetry from the GPT46 and maps those inputs into dashboards, event streams, and historical views so teams can monitor assets, review movement, and configure alerts. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and unifies device reporting under a single platform connection, enabling operations teams to focus on workflows rather than low level protocol details.

- Live location visualization and history playback based on GPS fixes transmitted by the device.
- Telemetry and check-in status surfaced as last communication time and device health indicators in Plaspy.
- BLE sensor data paired to the tracker can be shown as supplemental context for temperature, presence, or proximity when available.
- Configurable alerts and workflows for movement, offline devices, and scheduled check-ins to support anti-theft and lifecycle management.
- Scalable device registration and fleet grouping so GPT46 units can be managed alongside other asset classes in Plaspy.
