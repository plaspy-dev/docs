# EElink - GPT46

![GPT46](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT46 from Shenzhen Eelink Communication Technology is a compact, durable LTE Cat M1 IoT GPS tracker designed for long standby and industrial asset tracking. It is engineered to provide reliable position reporting and low-power telemetry for logistics, transport, and movable asset programs where reduced maintenance and continuous visibility are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GPT46 can deliver location fixes and telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and workflows for centralized fleet monitoring. Its BLE host capability and long standby behavior make it a practical choice for organizations that need periodic check ins, sensor augmentation, and consolidated operational reporting through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built and Plaspy compatible for long standby asset monitoring with reduced maintenance intervals.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB1 connectivity for consistent, low power real-time tracking and telemetry.
- BLE host functionality to pair with Bluetooth sensors and beacons for additional asset context.
- Compact, concealment friendly industrial form factor suitable for transport and equipment tracking.
- Designed for logistics and movable asset management with steady reporting and location visibility.
- Industrial grade durability to support demanding field deployments.
- Scalable deployment potential across fleets and anti theft monitoring programs.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GPT46 transmits location and telemetry over cellular IoT networks to Plaspy, where positional data and status updates are mapped into fleet dashboards and alerting systems. Plaspy ingests those updates to provide live visualization, history playback, and configurable notifications so operations teams can monitor assets and respond to events.

- Live location and telemetry appear in Plaspy for immediate situational awareness and map based monitoring.
- BLE host support lets sensors augment GPS data with proximity or environmental context that Plaspy can display alongside location.
- Long standby operation means Plaspy receives periodic check ins that reduce the need for frequent battery maintenance.
- Telemetry and status updates feed Plaspy reporting for movement history, last contact time, and operational oversight.
- Alerts and workflows in Plaspy can be configured to notify teams of unauthorized movement, missed check ins, or other conditions detected by the tracker.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trailers, non powered assets, and equipment that require steady location visibility.
- Logistics and transport tracking for high value shipments and movable inventory across routes.
- Anti theft monitoring with alerts and location recovery workflows managed through Plaspy.
- Sensor augmented monitoring using BLE paired devices for temperature or proximity checks in transit.
- Long term asset monitoring for rental equipment, construction tools, and deployed movable assets.

The GPT46 is a practical option for organizations that need a balance of long standby operation, BLE extensibility, and a compact industrial form factor. When integrated with Plaspy, the tracker turns positional fixes and sensor inputs into consolidated dashboards and actionable alerts that support theft response, utilization tracking, and lifecycle monitoring without excessive maintenance overhead.
