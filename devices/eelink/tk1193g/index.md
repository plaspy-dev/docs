# EElink - TK119‑3G

![TK119‑3G](./tracker.jpg)

The TK119‑3G is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable deployment across fleets and individual vehicles. It provides GPS and base station positioning with AGPS assistance and supports WCDMA and GSM networks to deliver real time tracking and telemetry. The unit includes vehicle ignition detection, an RS232 expansion port for peripherals, an optional relay for remote immobilization, and a set of safety alarms for crash, vibration and overspeed events.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK119‑3G can feed location, event and status information into Plaspy’s fleet management platform for monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its combination of position continuity features, ignition and alarm reporting, and peripheral expansion makes it a practical choice for operators who want to integrate vehicle telemetry, anti theft workflows and driver safety monitoring into Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for real time fleet tracking and operational visibility.
- Dual cellular support with GPS and LBS positioning plus AGPS assistance for faster fixes.
- Ignition ACC detection to capture engine on and engine off events for accurate logs.
- Optional relay for remote immobilizer or fuel and power cut interventions.
- Safety alarms for crash, fall, vibration and overspeed with automatic power cut options.
- RS232 expansion port to add external telemetry such as fuel monitoring or other peripherals.
- Compact and lightweight form factor with a backup battery for power loss and low power alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK119‑3G transmits periodic location updates and event notifications over cellular networks where Plaspy processes the device data for live tracking, alerting and historical reporting. Plaspy uses the device's positioning and alarm signals to provide operational oversight and to trigger workflows for dispatch, theft response and safety monitoring.

- Live location updates and position history available in Plaspy dashboards.
- Ignition and engine state reporting used for runtime analytics and driver logs.
- Immediate alerting for crash, vibration and overspeed events routed to operators and response teams.
- Remote immobilization commands using the optional relay coordinated through Plaspy for anti theft actions.
- Peripheral telemetry from RS232 devices relayed into Plaspy for fuel and diagnostic insight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization using remote relay control and overspeed power cut.
- Driver safety and incident response leveraging crash, fall and vibration alarms.
- Fuel monitoring and extended telemetry via RS232 peripheral integration.
- Real time dispatch, route oversight and driver assignment with continuous location feeds.
- Tamper and power loss detection to maintain visibility during unauthorized removal or shutdown.
