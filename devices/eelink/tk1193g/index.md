# EElink - TK119‑3G

![TK119‑3G](./tracker.jpg)

The TK119‑3G is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable Plaspy compatible deployment across fleets and individual vehicles. Supporting WCDMA and GSM networks with GPS/LBS positioning and AGPS assistance, the TK119‑3G delivers real-time tracking and telemetry essential to modern fleet management, anti-theft workflows and driver-safety programs.

Small and robust, the TK119‑3G combines ACC \(ignition\) detection, RS232 expansion for peripherals and an optional relay for remote fuel or power cut‑off. Its suite of safety alarms — including crash/fall, vibration and overspeed alerts with automatic power cut functionality — makes it a practical choice for operators who need dependable location, event and status data fed directly into Plaspy for monitoring, alerts and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management visibility.
- Dual network support \(WCDMA and GSM\) with GPS/LBS positioning and AGPS assistance for faster fixes.
- ACC \(ignition\) detection and telemetry reporting to capture vehicle on/off events and driver behavior.
- Optional relay for remote immobilizer or fuel/power cut‑off to support anti-theft and safety interventions.
- Crash/fall, vibration and overspeed alarms with automatic power cut on overspeed for enhanced protection.
- RS232 expansion port to integrate peripherals and telemetry devices, useful for fuel monitoring and external sensors.
- 70 mAh backup battery that provides power‑loss and low‑power alarms to maintain visibility during tampering or shutdowns.
- Compact form factor \(87 × 41.6 × 12 mm, 50 g\) for discreet installation on vehicles or assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK119‑3G pushes location and event data over cellular networks to Plaspy’s platform where it is processed for real-time tracking, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy leverages the unit’s AGPS and base‑station \(LBS\) fallback to maintain position continuity in challenging signal conditions and transforms the device’s telemetry into actionable insights for dispatch, compliance and theft response.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GPS/LBS + AGPS acceleration\).
- Ignition/ACC event reporting for accurate engine-on/engine-off logs.
- Crash/fall, vibration and overspeed alarms with immediate notification and logging.
- Remote immobilizer/fuel cut capability via optional relay for anti-theft intervention.
- RS232 peripheral data \(e.g., fuel sensors or third‑party telemetry devices\) relayed into Plaspy for fuel monitoring and diagnostics.

Choosing the TK119‑3G as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker gives operators a balanced mix of compact hardware, proven positioning and practical inputs/outputs for real-world fleet management and anti-theft workflows.
