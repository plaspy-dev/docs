# EElink - GPT48‑X

![GPT48‑X](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT48‑X is a long standby GPS tracker designed for durable asset tracking and low maintenance deployments. Built around low power cellular connectivity and multi GNSS positioning, the GPT48‑X provides persistent location awareness for remote or intermittently connected assets. Its rugged IP67 enclosure and strong magnetic mounting make it suitable for fleet units, containers, and other equipment where reliable location data and minimal field visits are priorities.

This model is relevant for Plaspy users because it is designed for seamless integration into the Plaspy platform. The GPT48‑X communicates using the EELINK protocol and supports remote configuration via platform, mobile app, or SMS. Plaspy can ingest device events, geofence alerts, tamper notifications, and emergency reporting from the GPT48‑X to deliver live tracking, historical reports, and operational alerts with reduced maintenance overhead.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra long standby with an 8000 mAh battery and design focused on multi year battery life for minimal maintenance cycles
- Low power cellular connectivity using LTE Cat M and NB IoT for wide area coverage and efficient data transmission
- Multi GNSS positioning including GPS, BDS, GLONASS, Galileo, and QZSS for improved location reliability
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and strong magnetic mounting for durable attachment to metal assets
- Tamper detection and smart wake features such as vibration wake up and light sensor alarms to detect movement or intrusion
- Remote management via OTA firmware upgrades and configuration through platform, mobile app, or SMS and EELINK protocol compatibility for Plaspy onboarding

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the GPT48‑X acts as a low maintenance tracking node that delivers location and device state into Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and reports. Plaspy decodes EELINK formatted messages and uses the multi GNSS positions and device status to support operational monitoring and automated responses.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for live monitoring of assets on Plaspy maps
- Tamper and movement alerts forwarded to Plaspy to trigger notifications and operator workflows
- Geofence management and exit alerts to support recovery and zone based reporting
- Remote configuration and OTA updates coordinated through Plaspy to reduce onsite maintenance
- Historical reporting and device health dashboards to track battery trends and deployment performance

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset monitoring for parked trailers, containers, and seasonal equipment
- Fleet management for low use or intermittently active vehicles where long battery life reduces maintenance
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows using tamper alarms and emergency reporting modes
- Remote equipment yard oversight to reduce site visits and support planned maintenance
- Container and cargo tracking where rugged mounting and reliable positioning are required
