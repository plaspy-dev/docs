# EElink - GPT12‑L

![GPT12‑L](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT12‑L is a compact IoT asset tracker from the GPT Series designed for discreet, long‑term monitoring of vehicles and high value assets. It combines multi‑constellation GNSS positioning with LTE Cat M1 and NB2 cellular connectivity and a 4500 mAh lithium manganese battery to provide versatile real‑time tracking and extended standby operation. The device's slim form factor and stated GNSS accuracy under 2 m make it suitable for concealed mounting and ongoing location monitoring where space and maintenance intervals are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GPT12‑L can feed location data, event messages and operational status into Plaspy dashboards and reporting. That compatibility makes the tracker relevant for operators who want to combine low power, event driven reporting with Plaspy's live maps, alerts and historical route analysis to support fleet oversight, anti‑theft workflows and asset telemetry without frequent battery interventions.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS asset tracker for straightforward platform integration and real time monitoring
- Extremely long standby using configurable sleeping profiles with reported operation up to 3 years on a single 4500 mAh battery
- Emergency real time mode that supports sustained live tracking windows for high intensity monitoring needs
- Multi GNSS support with stated GNSS accuracy under 2 m for dependable positioning
- Compact ultra slim form factor (96 × 50 × 11.2 mm, 70 g) for concealed mounting on vehicles and assets
- Global cellular reach via LTE Cat M1 and NB2 connectivity with GSM fallback and remote configuration options

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GPT12‑L reports GNSS positions, event messages and battery or operational telemetry to Plaspy ingestion points so operators can view live positions, receive alerts and review historical routes. Integration emphasizes event driven reporting and configurable wake cycles to balance timely information with prolonged battery life.

- Deliver real time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy when the device is awake or placed into emergency mode
- Forward geo‑fence alarm events to Plaspy for instant alerts and rule based notifications
- Manage scheduled wake up and sleep behavior through Plaspy compatible server commands, app configuration, or SMS for remote tuning
- Stream frequent updates during emergency mode to Plaspy for concentrated tracking periods up to the device's stated limit
- Incorporate device data into Plaspy reporting and dashboards alongside other fleet telemetry for consolidated operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with scheduled check ins and on demand tracking to monitor vehicle utilization while conserving battery life
- Anti theft protection using geo‑fences and emergency mode for rapid response and extended live tracking during recovery
- Remote asset monitoring for trailers, containers, equipment and other assets requiring long intervals between maintenance
- Telemetry augmentation where frequent GNSS updates during activity and minimal reporting in idle periods reduce data costs
- Temporary or event based tracking that requires intense monitoring windows without hardware changes
