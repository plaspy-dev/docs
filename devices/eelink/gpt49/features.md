# EElink - GPT49 Features

This page provides a clear, public overview of the EElink GPT49 tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is written for readers who want to understand practical, non-sensitive feature context for integrating GPT49 units into Plaspy for location tracking, event visibility, and remote device management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. For device specific parameters, signal band coverage, and the latest firmware behavior consult the official EElink documentation as noted below.

## Feature Overview

The GPT49 is a Plaspy compatible 4G LTE GPS tracker designed for long deployments and global positioning. It balances multi constellation GNSS positioning, wide cellular support, and selectable low power modes to provide long standby life while still offering emergency real‑time tracking and tamper detection useful for asset management.

- Multi constellation GNSS support for faster fixes and better global coverage
- 4G LTE connectivity with fallback cellular support for consistent reporting
- Large 6500 mAh battery optimized for ultra long standby in low power mode
- Selectable daily wake low power mode plus emergency real‑time tracking mode
- Vibration‑wake and light‑sensor tamper alarms and geofence reporting for anti‑theft monitoring

## Core Features of EElink - GPT49

- Multi constellation GNSS including GPS GLONASS BeiDou Galileo and QZSS for robust positioning
- Cellular connectivity across GSM WCDMA and LTE networks to report locations and events
- Large internal 6500 mAh battery supporting extended standby between charges
- Dual operation modes offering low power daily wake and emergency real‑time tracking
- Vibration wake and light sensor tamper alarms to detect movement or unauthorized access
- Geofencing support for perimeter monitoring and entry exit events
- Remote configuration and over‑the‑air firmware updates for fleet scale management
- Rugged waterproof and dustproof enclosure sized for outdoor and industrial deployments

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the GPT49 location fixes and event flags to provide live maps, history, and alerting that are useful for operations and security teams. When a GPT49 is deployed, Plaspy surfaces the device's reported positions and event notifications so administrators can monitor assets without physical retrieval.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks visible on Plaspy maps and reports
- Tamper and vibration events routed into Plaspy alerts and notification workflows
- Geofence entry and exit events appear in Plaspy for perimeter monitoring and auditing
- Selectable device reporting modes let Plaspy balance reporting frequency against battery life
- Remote configuration and FOTA integrate with Plaspy device management to adjust intervals thresholds and firmware remotely

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of heavy equipment and construction tools where infrequent maintenance is required
- Protection of seasonal or stored assets that need long standby tracking and occasional location checks
- Container pallet and cargo monitoring where geofence and tamper alerts provide early detection of movement
- Remote site asset cataloging at depots substations or temporary project sites without mains power
- Small fleet and equipment monitoring to consolidate location and event data into a single Plaspy dashboard
- Unattended asset installations that rely on long battery life and periodic status reporting
