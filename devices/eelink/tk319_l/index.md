# EElink - TK319‑L

<div className="device-logo">
![TK319‑L](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK319‑L is a compact GPS tracker from EElink designed for modern fleet management and vehicle security. Built to provide continuous location reporting and event detection, the device supports dual positioning methods and vehicle‑centric inputs so operators can monitor vehicle movement, ignition status, and alarm conditions. Its small footprint and broad operating voltage make it suitable for a range of commercial vehicles and mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK319‑L integrates its location, I/O states, and alarm events with Plaspy’s fleet management tools. Using the vendor protocol supported by the device, Plaspy can ingest the TK319‑L’s telemetry to present live tracking, generate alerts, and include sensor inputs in dashboards and reports. This compatibility makes the tracker a practical choice for organizations that want to centralize vehicle visibility and operational workflows on Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for vehicle and asset installations where space is limited
- Plaspy compatible communication for reliable ingestion of location and event data
- Dual positioning with GPS and assisted location to improve tracking continuity
- Vehicle inputs and alarms such as ignition detection, crash and vibration alerts, and power notifications
- Optional relay for remote engine cut‑off and SOS button support for emergency signaling
- Expansion ports for connecting telemetry peripherals to extend monitoring capabilities
- Broad operating voltage range for use across light vehicles and heavier equipment

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the TK319‑L delivers location updates and event telemetry to the platform where fleet managers can view live positions, review historical tracks, and configure alerting rules. Plaspy maps the tracker’s inputs and alarm states into its dashboard and reporting modules so teams can act on movement, safety, and asset status information from a single interface.

- Live location and route playback in Plaspy for operational oversight and post‑trip review
- Mapping of ignition and trip events to enable automated trip segmentation and utilization reports
- Alarm forwarding to Plaspy notification rules for overspeed, crash, low power, and power off events
- Remote immobilization support through optional relay control when configured and authorized
- Sensor and peripheral data from expansion ports available in Plaspy for telemetry reporting and alerts

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for daily vehicle tracking, trip logging, and utilization monitoring
- Anti‑theft workflows that combine real‑time location, alarms, and optional remote immobilizer
- Safety monitoring where crash, fall, and vibration alarms notify operations teams and responders
- Telemetry and fuel monitoring by connecting external sensors and capturing their feeds in Plaspy
- Mixed fleet asset tracking across cars, vans, trucks, and field equipment with varied power systems
