# EElink - GPT15

![GPT15](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT15 is a slim, travel-focused GPS tracker designed for luggage and personal assets and is fully Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and alerting. With triple positioning \(GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS\), Bluetooth 4.0 smartphone pairing and a compact 96 × 50 × 11.2 mm form factor, the GPT15 delivers accurate location updates, tamper detection and long run times in a lightweight 65 g package.

Built for consumers and small-scale asset deployments, the GPT15 pairs easily with the Plaspy platform to provide telemetry, geofence alerts and low‑battery warnings. Its rechargeable 1800 mAh battery and remote configuration options \(app or SMS\) make the GPT15 a practical Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for anti‑theft protection, travel monitoring and everyday asset visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering real-time tracking through GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS for layered location accuracy.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for smartphone pairing and proximity alerts to help prevent theft or loss of personal items.
- On‑device light sensor provides tamper alerts when luggage or assets are opened or disturbed.
- Geofence alerts and low‑battery warnings pushed to Plaspy for fast, actionable notifications.
- Long‑lasting 1800 mAh rechargeable battery for extended tracking without frequent charging.
- Compact, lightweight design \(96 × 50 × 11.2 mm; 65 g\) ideal for luggage, bags and small assets.
- Remote configuration via app or SMS simplifies setup and management while in the field.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GPT15 transmits layered location and status telemetry to Plaspy, enabling continuous visibility and configurable alerts. Plaspy ingests GPS, Wi‑Fi and base‑station \(LBS\) location data, interprets sensor events and consolidates them into real‑time maps, history reports and mobile notifications. Integration requires no custom hardware changes — the GPT15’s standard outputs are compatible with Plaspy’s device data model for immediate deployment.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates from GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS for continuous tracking.
- Geofence events delivered to Plaspy for enter/exit alerts and perimeter monitoring.
- Low‑battery warnings surfaced in Plaspy so managers and owners can act before devices go offline.
- Tamper/light sensor alerts forwarded to Plaspy for theft and handling detection.
- Bluetooth 4.0 proximity/pairing events reported for smartphone‑assisted anti‑theft workflows.

## Use Cases

- Luggage and travel: attach the GPT15 to suitcases and travel bags for real‑time tracking and tamper alerts during transit.
- Personal asset protection: keep tabs on backpacks, camera bags and other valuable personal items with proximity alerts via Bluetooth.
- Parcel and small courier tracking: monitor high‑value parcels in last‑mile delivery where compact size and real‑time location matter.
- Event and temporary rentals: track shared equipment or rented gear during short‑term deployments without permanent installation.

The GPT15 offers a focused, cost‑effective option for Plaspy users who need compact, reliable GPS tracking for luggage and personal assets. Its triple positioning approach \(GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS\) delivers robust location data, while the built‑in light sensor and Bluetooth 4.0 add practical anti‑theft layers. Remote configuration via app or SMS and Plaspy compatibility mean rapid onboarding and centralized visibility for managers and owners.
