# EElink - TK319

<div className="device-logo">
![TK319](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The EElink TK319 is a compact 3G GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset monitoring across a range of operational contexts. It combines GPS and LBS locating with AGPS assistance to provide reliable position data and supports regular data uploads over cellular networks. The device includes features commonly needed for fleet operations such as ignition status detection, remote relay control, geofence alerts, speed and collision alarms, and optional temperature monitoring for sensitive cargo.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK319 can feed location, status, and alert information into Plaspy for centralised visibility and management. Its support for remote configuration and advanced protocols makes it suitable for integration with cloud fleet platforms like Plaspy, enabling operators to monitor vehicles and assets in real time, receive alarms, and generate operational reports from a single dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- GPS and LBS dual way tracking with AGPS for faster location fixes and improved coverage
- Designed for real time uploads over cellular networks to support live monitoring
- ACC detection for monitoring ignition status and asset usage
- Relay option for remote engine cut off to support recovery or immobilization workflows
- Optional temperature sensor for monitoring temperature sensitive cargo
- Built in alarms including geofence, speed, and collision or falling alerts for security and risk management

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK319 becomes part of a unified fleet and asset management environment that presents location, alerts, and device status on maps and reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker's periodic updates and event messages so operators can act on live intelligence and build historical records for analysis.

- Live location and path history displayed on Plaspy maps for route review and dispatching
- Alarms such as geofence entry and exit, overspeed, and collision events routed into Plaspy alerting workflows
- Ignition and usage state from ACC detection surfaced in Plaspy for operational oversight and utilization reporting
- Relay control available to authorised users through Plaspy where remote immobilization is enabled and configured
- Temperature readings from an optional sensor available in Plaspy dashboards for monitoring sensitive loads
- Device management and remote configuration supported through the tracker protocols to simplify fleet administration

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle rental fleets requiring location monitoring and immobilization options for asset protection
- Logistics and cargo operations that need real time tracking and temperature monitoring for sensitive shipments
- Bus and public transport fleet oversight with geofence and speed alerting for safety and compliance
- Risk management and recovery workflows using alarms and remote relay control to reduce theft impact
- IoT asset tracking where compact form factor and periodic reporting meet monitoring needs
