# K30 Wearable GPS Tracker

The K30 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for reliable personal and staff safety monitoring. Lightweight and water-resistant, the K30 combines GPS/Wi‑Fi/LBS triple positioning with real‑time tracking and emergency features so caregivers, managers, and operations teams can maintain visibility and act quickly when events occur.

Built for children, elderly users and on‑the‑move staff, the K30 delivers essential telemetry — location updates, SOS emergency calling, two‑way voice and step counting — while minimizing battery drain with intelligent power‑saving modes. Its remote configuration via platform, app or SMS makes integration with Plaspy straightforward for alerts, geofencing and reporting workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivering dependable real‑time tracking for people and staff safety.
- Triple positioning \(GPS/Wi‑Fi/LBS\) for improved location accuracy indoors and outdoors.
- SOS emergency calling with long‑press button and two‑way voice for rapid incident response.
- Geofencing, speed and low‑battery alarms to automate alerts and reduce manual monitoring.
- Intelligent power‑saving modes that adapt reporting frequency based on motion to extend battery life.
- Compact, lightweight \(20 g\) IP65 design suitable for daily wear and local staff/fleet assignments.
- Remote configuration via platform/app/SMS allows fast setup and updates through Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the K30 streams location and event data that the platform uses for maps, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy ingests GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS position fixes, plus device telemetry such as battery state, SOS activations and motion status. Administrators can configure geofence zones, set alarm thresholds and receive push/SMS notifications directly from Plaspy based on K30 events.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates via GSM network, displayed on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- SOS emergency alerts and two‑way voice events forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and response workflows.
- Step counting and motion‑based reporting feed simple activity telemetry into Plaspy reports and dashboards.
- Geofence entry/exit notifications and speed alarms trigger Plaspy alerts for rapid action.
- Remote configuration using platform/app/SMS — change reporting intervals, alarms and thresholds from Plaspy.
- Note: the K30 focuses on personal/asset telemetry; it does not include vehicle‑grade fuel monitoring, ignition sensing or immobilizer control commonly found in vehicle trackers.

## Use Cases

- Child safety and parental monitoring — real‑time tracking, SOS and two‑way voice for immediate reassurance.
- Elderly care and lone‑worker protection — automatic alerts, low‑battery warnings and geofencing to support caregivers.
- Staff tracking in mixed fleets or campuses — lightweight wearable for staff location, activity monitoring and incident response.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The K30 offers a focused, reliable solution when Plaspy customers need Plaspy compatible wearables for people and light assets. Its blend of GPS/Wi‑Fi/LBS positioning, SOS emergency calling and two‑way voice supports real‑time tracking and quick incident handling without unnecessary vehicle‑grade features. The device’s intelligent power management and remote configuration via platform, app or SMS reduce maintenance and simplify large‑scale deployments for staff safety or child care programs.

By integrating the K30 into Plaspy, organizations gain immediate access to actionable telemetry — locations, step counts, SOS events and geofence alerts — presented on Plaspy dashboards and routed into existing notification workflows. For customers seeking a lightweight, IP65 wearable GPS tracker that emphasizes safety, accessibility and ease of integration, the K30 is a practical choice. Note that vehicle telematics features such as fuel monitoring, ignition sensing or immobilizer control are outside the K30’s scope; the device focuses on personal telemetry and emergency communications.
