# EElink - K30

![K30](./tracker.jpg)

The K30 is a compact wearable GPS tracker designed for personal and staff safety monitoring. Lightweight and water resistant, the device combines GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS positioning with real time tracking and emergency features such as an SOS button and two‑way voice to help caregivers and operations teams maintain visibility and respond quickly when events occur.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the K30 integrates its location and event data into the Plaspy platform for mapping, alerts and reporting. Remote configuration via platform, app or SMS simplifies onboarding and lets organizations use Plaspy to centralize geofencing, alarm thresholds and operational oversight for people tracking and light asset protection.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible wearable tracker built for personal safety and staff monitoring.
- Triple positioning with GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS to improve location accuracy across environments.
- SOS emergency alerting with two‑way voice to support rapid incident response.
- Geofencing and alarm functions for entry exit notifications and low battery alerts.
- Intelligent power saving to extend operating time between charges.
- Compact IP65 rated design suitable for daily wear and light duty assignments.
- Remote configuration through platform, app or SMS to streamline fleet scale updates.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the K30 sends location fixes and event telemetry that the platform uses to populate maps, trigger notifications and create historical reports. Plaspy ingests position updates and device events so administrators can monitor people in real time, automate alerts and review past activity from a single interface.

- Real time location and status updates shown on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- SOS activations and voice events forwarded into Plaspy alert workflows for immediate attention.
- Motion and step counting information available for activity monitoring and basic reporting.
- Geofence entry and exit notifications trigger Plaspy alerts for perimeter control and compliance.
- Remote configuration and reporting intervals adjusted through Plaspy or supported device channels.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and parental monitoring with fast SOS notification and location visibility.
- Elderly care and lone worker protection where quick alerts and location history support caregivers.
- Staff tracking across campuses or mixed site operations for activity oversight and incident handling.
- Personal item or backpack awareness where compact form factor and alarms help deter loss.
- Event and facility supervision to monitor personnel movement and enforce designated zones.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The K30 focuses on people and light asset telemetry, offering a balance of positioning, emergency communication and manageable battery life for daily use. For organizations that need Plaspy compatible wearables, the device provides the core features required for safety workflows without vehicle grade telematics complexity.

Integrating the K30 with Plaspy gives teams centralized visibility of locations, SOS events and geofence activity, while remote configuration options reduce maintenance overhead. Its compact, water resistant form and straightforward feature set make it a practical option for programs centered on staff safety, caregiving, and on the move personnel tracking.
