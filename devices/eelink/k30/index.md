# EElink - K30

<div className="device-logo">
![K30](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The K30 is a compact wearable GPS tracker designed for reliable personal and staff safety monitoring. Lightweight and water resistant, the device combines GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS positioning with real time tracking and emergency features so caregivers, managers, and operations teams can maintain visibility and act quickly when events occur. Built for everyday wear, the K30 emphasizes essential telemetry such as location updates, SOS emergency calling, two way voice and basic activity information while minimizing user burden.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the K30 integrates into the Plaspy platform to deliver location and event data where teams expect to see it. Plaspy ingests the K30’s position fixes and event triggers to provide map visualization, geofence alerts, reporting and notification workflows. Remote configuration via platform, app or SMS makes it straightforward to include the K30 in monitoring, alerting and operational routines managed from Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact wearable form factor suitable for children, elderly users and on the move staff.
- Triple positioning using GPS Wi‑Fi and LBS for broader coverage indoors and outdoors.
- SOS emergency calling with a long press button and two way voice for rapid response.
- Geofencing and alarm features to automate alerts and reduce manual monitoring.
- Intelligent power saving and motion aware reporting to extend operational time between charges.
- Remote configuration via platform app or SMS to simplify ongoing device management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the K30 streams location and event data that Plaspy uses for maps dashboards and alerts. Administrators and operations teams can view real time positions and receive notifications from K30 events while leveraging Plaspy reporting and oversight features.

- Real time location updates and device status visible on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- SOS activations and two way voice events forwarded into Plaspy for immediate notification workflows.
- Geofence entry and exit alerts plus configurable alarm thresholds routed through Plaspy notifications.
- Activity and motion indicators incorporated into Plaspy reports for operational oversight.
- Remote parameter changes and reporting interval adjustments managed through Plaspy or supported remote channels.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and parental monitoring with SOS and location updates for reassurance.
- Elderly care and remote supervision where geofences and low battery alerts support caregivers.
- Lone worker and staff location tracking across campuses or mixed site environments.
- Personal item awareness and quick alerting for backpacks or portable equipment.
- Event or facility supervision to monitor personnel movement and enforce perimeters.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The K30 is a focused wearable solution for organizations that need Plaspy compatible devices aimed at people and light assets rather than vehicle telematics. Its combination of multi mode positioning emergency call capability and remote management helps reduce administrative overhead while providing the core telemetry Plaspy customers rely on for safety monitoring and simple activity reporting.
