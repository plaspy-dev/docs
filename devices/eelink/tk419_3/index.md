# EElink - TK419‑3

<div className="device-logo">
![TK419‑3](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK419‑3 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker from EElink designed for real‑time tracking and telemetry. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with AGPS assistance, LTE Cat 1 and legacy GSM connectivity, and event driven alerts to provide continuous location updates and status reporting. The device ships in a rugged IP65 enclosure with a wide 9–72 V DC input range and a built in emergency battery, making it suitable for a variety of vehicle and asset mounting scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK419‑3 can integrate directly into Plaspy’s fleet management platform to deliver live positions, alarm events, and I/O states to dashboards and reports. Plaspy users can take advantage of the tracker’s ignition detection, optional relay control, accessory inputs, and configurable alarms for fleet monitoring, anti‑theft workflows, and remote diagnostics without having to manage low level device details inside the platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for direct integration into real time tracking dashboards and fleet workflows
- Multi constellation GNSS with AGPS assistance for improved fix responsiveness
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with GSM fallback for broad coverage and continuous telemetry
- Vehicle I/O support including ACC ignition detection and optional relay for remote cut off
- Expansion interfaces such as GPIO and RS232 to connect external sensors and accessories
- Rugged compact form factor with IP65 protection, 9–72 V DC input range, and built in emergency battery
- Event driven alarms for collision, vibration, speed, SOS, and geofence notifications

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the TK419‑3 streams location fixes, alarm events, and I/O telemetry into the platform where they are normalized for map visualization, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy exposes the device data in fleet views, event logs, and configurable notifications so operators can monitor vehicle status and respond to incidents.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy for map visualization and live tracking
- Ignition detection and status reporting to support usage analytics and duty logging
- Configurable event alerts for collision, vibration, geofence entry and exit, SOS, and speed
- Accessory telemetry from GPIO and RS232 available in Plaspy reports and asset detail screens
- Remote commands and parameter updates from Plaspy to adjust reporting and enable relay actions
- Historical route and event reporting for operational review and compliance workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for efficient dispatch and utilization tracking
- Anti theft workflows using geofencing, alarms, and optional relay based immobilization
- Driver safety and incident response with collision, fall, and SOS alerts routed through Plaspy
- Temperature sensitive cargo oversight by connecting external sensors to GPIO or RS232
- Rental and mixed fleet vehicle tracking where a compact footprint and wide voltage support are required
