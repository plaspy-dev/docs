# EElink - TK119

![TK119](./logo..jpg)

The EElink TK119 is a compact 3G network GPS tracker designed for a broad range of tracking needs. Marketed as a versatile device, the TK119-3G supports vehicle and asset monitoring use cases including rental fleets, logistics operations, motorcycle tracking, and ship management. Its durable construction and IP67 waterproof rating make it suitable for outdoor and mobile installations where exposure to weather and vibration is expected.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK119 can deliver location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and analysis. The tracker provides real-time position updates and a set of alerting capabilities that map naturally into fleet dashboards, historical playback, and operational reports on the Plaspy platform. This compatibility lets organizations combine the TK119 hardware with Plaspy software to manage assets and respond to events from a single pane of glass.

## Key Highlights

- 3G WCDMA connectivity with stated support for broader cellular networks for reliable coverage across many areas
- IP67 waterproof rating for durability in harsh or wet environments
- GPIO extension for flexible input and output options to support additional monitoring needs
- Real-time tracking and GPS/LBS dual mode location reporting for continuous visibility
- Built in battery to maintain basic reporting during power interruptions
- Remote configuration and support for EELINK Protocol 2.0 to simplify device management at scale

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK119 sends position updates and status events to the Plaspy platform where data is normalized, displayed, and archived. Plaspy ingests the device data to provide live visibility and historical context for fleet and asset managers.

- Live location tracking and map display inside Plaspy for monitoring moving assets
- Event and alert forwarding so collisions, vibration alarms, speed events, and geofence triggers surface in dashboards
- Historical route playback and location history for operational review and incident analysis
- Fleet monitoring views and reporting tools to track utilization and compliance over time
- Remote device management workflows to adjust settings and review device health through Plaspy interfaces

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental car and vehicle tracking to monitor usage and location
- Logistics and delivery fleet oversight for route visibility and performance reporting
- Motorcycle tracking where compact, weatherproof devices are needed
- Vessel and ship management for position reporting of small craft and auxiliary assets
- IoT device risk management where durable, networked tracking is required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK119 combines a small footprint with robust environmental protection and flexible IO, making it a practical option for operations that need dependable asset visibility. Coupled with Plaspy, organizations gain a centralized platform to collect, visualize, and act on the tracker data without managing separate systems for each device type.

While the TK119 offers a clear set of features for tracking and alerts, the best fit depends on your operational priorities such as coverage area, reporting cadence, and the specific inputs you need to monitor. Plaspy helps bring that data together so teams can focus on decision making and operational improvements rather than device integration.
