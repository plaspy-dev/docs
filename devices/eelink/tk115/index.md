# EElink - TK115

![TK115](./tracker.jpg)

The TK115 is a compact GPS tracker designed for electric motorcycles and scooters. It provides core location and event reporting features such as assisted GPS and LBS positioning, ACC ignition detection, an optional relay for remote immobilization, and a built in backup battery to report power loss and low battery conditions. The unit is built with a small form factor and operating tolerances suited to vehicle environments, making it appropriate for two wheel mobility applications and light commercial use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK115 can feed real time position, status, and alarm events into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational workflows. That compatibility makes the TK115 a practical choice for operators who want to combine vehicle focused telemetry and security events with Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and reporting without relying on bespoke integrations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for electric motorcycles and scooters for easy integration into real time tracking dashboards
- Wide DC input support 9–60 V to match common two wheel and light vehicle electrical systems
- GPS and LBS positioning with AGPS assistance for faster fixes and location accuracy in the 5–15 m range
- ACC ignition detection and an optional relay that can be used to support remote immobilizer or power cut workflows
- Multiple alarm types including displacement towed detection, vibration, speed alerts, and geofence violations
- Compact lightweight design with an onboard backup battery to maintain connectivity and send power loss or low battery alerts
- Rugged operating range suitable for outdoor and vehicle mounted use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK115 transmits location updates, device status, and alarm events to a central platform where operators can monitor fleets, receive notifications, and run reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and makes it accessible for real time visibility and automated responses.

- Real time location and telemetry updates using GPS and LBS with AGPS assistance for improved fix times
- ACC ignition status reporting for clearer trip start and stop detection and ignition based alerts
- Optional relay control to support remote immobilization and power or fuel cut commands coordinated through Plaspy
- Displacement, vibration, speed, and geofence alarms sent into Plaspy as instant events for notifications and escalation
- Power loss and low battery alerts from the onboard backup battery to support continuous monitoring and quick response
- Integration with fleet telemetry workflows and reporting in Plaspy to support maintenance scheduling and operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft workflows using remote immobilization and immediate alarms for unauthorized movement
- Scooter and electric motorcycle sharing operations requiring geofencing, speed control, and real time location
- Last mile delivery and courier services on two wheel vehicles using ACC detection and trip reporting for operations tracking
- Towed or displacement detection and tamper alerts to enable fast incident response
- Fleet telemetry and maintenance programs that combine location and status events with Plaspy analytics

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK115 pairs practical vehicle focused features with Plaspy to offer a streamlined solution for managing two wheel fleets and individual scooter or motorcycle assets. Its wide voltage acceptance and compact design make it adaptable across a range of vehicle types, while ACC detection and an optional immobilizer relay provide clear anti theft controls that feed directly into Plaspy workflows.

Choosing the TK115 for use with Plaspy helps operators gain consistent location visibility, timely alarm events, and actionable telemetry that can be used for incident response, operational reporting, and maintenance planning.
