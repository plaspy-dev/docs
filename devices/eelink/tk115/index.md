# EElink - TK115

![TK115](./tracker.jpg)

The EElink TK115 is a compact GPS tracker designed for electric motorcycles and scooters. It delivers core location and event monitoring features such as assisted GPS and LBS positioning, wide input voltage acceptance for common vehicle electrical systems, ACC ignition detection, an optional relay for remote immobilization or power cut, and an internal backup battery to report power loss or low battery conditions. The TK115 targets mobility operators and fleet owners who need continuous positional awareness and basic security controls on two wheel vehicles.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK115 can feed its location, status signals, and alarm events into Plaspy’s fleet management platform for centralized monitoring and operational workflows. This compatibility makes the unit useful for anti theft workflows, geofencing and speed control enforcement, displacement detection, and routine fleet oversight — all visible and actionable from Plaspy’s dashboards and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for electric motorcycles and scooters with a compact form factor suited to vehicle mounting.
- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into real time tracking dashboards and alerting workflows.
- Wide DC input that supports a range of vehicle electrical systems common in two wheel fleets.
- AGPS enabled GPS/LBS positioning for faster fixes and reliable location data with typical accuracy in the meter range.
- ACC ignition detection and an optional relay to enable remote immobilizer or power cut capabilities.
- Backup battery and power-loss notifications to maintain continuity of telemetry during main power interruptions.
- Security alarm set including displacement, vibration, speed, and geofence events to trigger immediate alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK115 uploads its positional and event data to a central platform where operators can monitor vehicles in real time, configure alerts, and generate reports for operational insights. Plaspy ingests location coordinates, ignition status, and alarm events from the tracker so teams can respond to incidents, enforce rules, and analyze fleet performance.

- Real time GPS/LBS location updates with AGPS assistance for live vehicle visibility in Plaspy.
- ACC ignition status reporting to improve trip classification and enable ignition based alerts and reporting.
- Remote relay control via Plaspy to support immobilization or controlled power actions when needed.
- Instant event forwarding for displacement, vibration, speed, and geofence violations into Plaspy notifications.
- Power-loss and low-battery alerts fed into Plaspy to keep operators informed about device health and connectivity.
- Integration into fleet workflows for monitoring, reporting, and scheduled maintenance when combined with Plaspy analytics.

- Last mile delivery and courier fleets on two wheel vehicles requiring accurate trip and ignition reporting.
- Towed or displacement detection for quick response to unauthorized vehicle movement.
