# EElink - GPT60

![GPT60](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT60 is a compact, rechargeable GPS tracker designed primarily for personal safety monitoring and continuous location telemetry. It combines multi constellations GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and cellular LBS fallback to maintain dependable real time tracking and event reporting for people and portable assets. Built in two way voice, an SOS button, and automatic fall and vibration alerts make the device well suited to lone workers, seniors, and outdoor users who need fast incident escalation and straightforward location sharing.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the GPT60 integrates into Plaspy workflows to deliver visibility and event management without complex custom development. Telemetry, alerts, and remote configuration flow into Plaspy so administrators can monitor location history, respond to SOS and fall events, and include portable trackers alongside vehicle assets in a single fleet view.

## Key Highlights

- Compact personal tracker focused on safety and continuous location telemetry
- Multi mode positioning using GNSS with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback for improved coverage
- Built in microphone and speaker for two way voice communication and check ins
- SOS button plus automatic fall and vibration alerts for rapid incident notification
- Rechargeable battery with intelligent power modes to balance runtime and accuracy
- Cellular connectivity and IP65 ingress protection for wide area and outdoor use
- Bluetooth support for pairing local sensors and activity telemetry

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GPT60 delivers location, status, and event data into a unified monitoring platform so teams can act on incidents and analyze behavior over time. Device events and telemetry are sent to Plaspy in near real time, enabling mapping, alerts, and remote management alongside other tracked assets.

- Real time location and telemetry updates displayed on Plaspy maps and in playback history
- SOS, fall, vibration, and low battery alerts visible in Plaspy alert dashboards and notification channels
- Geofence entry and exit events generated by the device and processed in Plaspy for automated workflows
- Two way voice capability available for incident coordination initiated from the device or through Plaspy procedures

- Remote configuration supported so administrators can adjust reporting behavior and thresholds via Plaspy compatible commands
