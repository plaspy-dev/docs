# EElink - GPT60

![GPT60](./tracker.jpg)

The GPT60 is a compact, rechargeable GPS tracker designed for personal safety monitoring and continuous location telemetry. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the GPT60 combines GNSS \(BDS/GPS/GLONASS\) positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to provide dependable real-time tracking and event reporting for people and portable assets. Its built-in microphone and speaker enable two-way voice communication for check-ins and emergency coordination, while an SOS button and automatic fall/vibration alerts make the device ideal for lone workers, seniors, and outdoor users.

Engineered for long use between charges, the GPT60 runs on a 600 mAh rechargeable battery managed by intelligent power modes to balance accuracy and battery life. With LTE‑FDD/TDD and GSM network support and a waterproof IP65 rating, the GPT60 is a practical GPS tracker that plugs into existing Plaspy workflows for telemetry, alerts, geofencing, and remote configuration via the EELINK 2.1 protocol.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that delivers reliable real-time tracking via GNSS, Wi‑Fi, and LBS.
- Two‑way voice communication with built‑in microphone and speaker for rapid incident response.
- SOS button, fall/vibration alerts, and low‑battery warnings for personal safety and quick escalation.
- 600 mAh rechargeable battery with intelligent power modes to extend runtime between charges.

- LTE‑FDD/TDD and GSM network support to ensure wide cellular coverage.
- IP65 waterproof rating for dependable outdoor and daily-use durability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the GPT60 with Plaspy gives administrators and users a single place to view location, status, and events. The device uses GNSS \(BDS/GPS/GLONASS\) as its primary location source and supplements position fixes with Wi‑Fi and LBS when satellite signals are weak. Telemetry and events flow to Plaspy using the EELINK 2.1 protocol, enabling near real-time tracking, alarm handling, and remote configuration.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates streamed to Plaspy for mapping and history playback.
- SOS alerts, fall/vibration events and low-battery warnings visible in Plaspy’s alert dashboard.
- Geofence entry/exit events generated on the device and delivered to Plaspy for automated notifications.
- Two-way voice support for live communication initiated from the device or via Plaspy workflows.
- Bluetooth sensors and step-count telemetry available to Plaspy for richer activity and local-sensor data.
