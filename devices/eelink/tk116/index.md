# TK116 GPS Tracker

The TK116 is a compact 2G GPS tracker designed for dependable fleet management and motorcycle tracking. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this device delivers real-time tracking, robust telemetry and configurable alerts so operators can monitor vehicles, respond to incidents, and reduce theft risk with a single integrated platform.

The TK116 combines GPS and LBS positioning with AGPS assistance to maintain continuous location updates even in challenging environments. With ACC detection, crash and vibration alarms, geofencing and optional relay and SOS features, the unit supports anti-theft measures, remote immobilizer-style control and streamlined fleet workflows when paired with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your real-time tracking and fleet management dashboard.
- Accurate positioning using GPS \(5–15 m\) and LBS \(base‑station ~200 m\) with AGPS assistance for faster fixes.
- ACC detection plus crash/fall, vibration and speed alarms for improved driver and asset safety.
- Optional relay supports remote fuel/power cut-off — enables immobilizer-style anti-theft actions.
- Optional microphone and SOS button provide remote listening and emergency alert capabilities when fitted.
- Compact, lightweight form factor \(79.5 × 38.5 × 13.5 mm, 45 g\) suitable for vehicles and motorcycles.
- 200 mAh backup battery with external power cut/low-power alerts to maintain continuity and avoid blind spots.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed and registered in Plaspy, the TK116 streams position and status data to the platform for immediate visualization, alerting and reporting. Plaspy reads the device’s telemetry and event messages to provide real-time tracking, historical traces, and configurable notifications to fleet managers and dispatchers.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates are transmitted over 2G to Plaspy for continuous monitoring.
- ACC/ignition status \(ACC detection\) is reported for vehicle run/stop analytics and ignition-based rules.
- Crash/fall, vibration and speed alarms generate instant alerts in Plaspy to speed incident response.
- Remote immobilizer functionality via optional relay lets Plaspy trigger fuel/power cut-off as part of anti-theft workflows.
- SOS button and optional microphone events are captured and routed to Plaspy for emergency handling and remote listening where authorized.
