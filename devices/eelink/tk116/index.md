# EElink - TK116

![TK116](./tracker.jpg)

The EElink TK116 is a compact 2G GPS tracker designed for vehicle and motorcycle tracking. It combines satellite positioning with cell site assistance to provide continuous location updates, and includes vehicle inputs and event alarms such as ACC detection, crash and vibration alerts. Optional features such as a relay, SOS button and microphone expand its suitability for anti theft and emergency response scenarios while a small backup battery helps maintain brief continuity during external power loss.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK116 can feed position and event data directly into the Plaspy platform for visualization, alerting and reporting. Its combination of location updates, ignition status and alarm events makes it a practical fit for fleet operators and motorcycle users who want consolidated monitoring and automated notifications managed from Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Native compatibility with Plaspy for streamlined integration into real time tracking and fleet dashboards
- GPS plus LBS positioning with AGPS assistance for faster fixes in mixed coverage environments
- ACC detection and alarms for crash, vibration and speed to support safety and incident workflows
- Optional relay and SOS features to enable remote immobilizer style actions and emergency alerts
- Compact form factor and small backup battery suitable for motorcycle and vehicle installations
- Configurable alerts and remote configuration options to reduce blind spots and support fleet operations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the TK116 is registered in Plaspy, its location and event messages are presented in the platform for live monitoring, historical playback and configurable notifications. Plaspy interprets incoming telemetry and alarms from the tracker so dispatchers and managers can respond, analyze trends and enforce operational rules.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps and moveable asset lists for continuous visibility
- Ignition and run stop events are reported for use in run time analytics and rule based alerts
- Crash, vibration and speed alarms generate immediate notifications to operators for incident management
- Relay triggered actions and immobilizer style workflows can be coordinated through Plaspy for theft response
- SOS events and optional audio alerts are captured and routed into Plaspy for prioritized handling

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet route monitoring and dispatch for cars, vans and light trucks
- Motorcycle tracking and recovery where compact hardware is required
- Anti theft workflows using optional relay controlled immobilization and alerting
- Safety and emergency response using crash, fall and SOS notifications
- Operational oversight and reporting using ignition events and historical traces
