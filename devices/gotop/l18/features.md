# GOTOP - L18 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the GOTOP L18 smartwatch GPS tracker together with Plaspy. It focuses on the L18’s practical capabilities, how those capabilities are reflected inside Plaspy, and typical operational uses for teams and care providers. The goal is to provide clear, non-sensitive information about what the device can do when integrated with Plaspy for monitoring and incident response.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and the way the device is installed and provisioned. For precise technical and configuration details, consult the official manufacturer documentation from GOTOP and the device release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP L18 is a wrist-worn 4G LTE GPS tracker designed for real-time location, emergency response, and personal health monitoring. Its compact smartwatch form factor combines multi-constellation GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity and a suite of biometric sensors, providing continuous situational awareness for caregivers, field teams, and organizations.

- Real-time position updates from GPS, BDS, and GLONASS to support location awareness in urban and mixed environments.
- Wearable SOS emergency alerts and two-way voice calling for rapid incident confirmation and response.
- Integrated health telemetry including heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, and temperature for wellbeing monitoring.
- Multi-band cellular connectivity with nano SIM or optional eSIM for broad network coverage and reliable data delivery.
- BLE and Wi‑Fi scanning plus OTA firmware upgrades to support device management and contextual location enhancements.

## Core Features of GOTOP - L18

- Smartwatch form factor for continuous personal wear and immediate access to SOS and voice functions.
- Multi-constellation GNSS support with configurable GPS, BDS, and GLONASS combinations for improved positioning.
- 4G LTE and GSM cellular connectivity with multi-band support and optional eSIM for flexible provisioning.
- SOS emergency key and two-way voice calling to enable direct communication during incidents.
- Health sensors reporting heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, and body temperature as onboard telemetry.
- IP67 water resistance and compact design for daily wear in varied environments.
- OTA firmware upgrade capability and remote software management features supported by the device.
- BLE and Wi‑Fi hotspot scanning to provide auxiliary context for location and nearby devices.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the L18 sends location fixes, alarms, and periodic sensor telemetry to the platform so teams can monitor status and respond to events from web or mobile dashboards. Plaspy receives and interprets the device’s reporting to present actionable information for dispatch, monitoring, and historical review.

- Live location points appear on Plaspy maps so operators can track individuals in near real time.
- SOS alerts are surfaced as actionable alarms in Plaspy so responders can be notified and dispatched quickly.
- Health telemetry such as heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, and temperature can be displayed as periodic readings and used to trigger event-driven notifications.
- Geofence, alarm, and historical position data feed into Plaspy reporting and timeline views for audits and incident reconstruction.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding and visibility.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elder care and personal emergency response where continuous location and SOS handling support caregivers and family members.
- Workforce attendance, remote check-ins, and basic status verification for field staff wearing a compact tracker.
- Visitor safety and guided group monitoring in public venues where two-way voice and emergency alerts increase confidence.
- Personnel tracking alongside vehicle telematics to provide a people-focused view of operations.
- On-site monitoring in assisted living or healthcare settings that benefit from aggregated location and health telemetry.
