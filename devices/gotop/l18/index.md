# GOTOP - L18

![L18](./tracker.jpg)

The L18 is a Plaspy compatible 4G LTE GPS tracker in the form of a smartwatch, engineered for personal safety, health monitoring, and reliable two-way communication. Designed for caregivers, field staff, and organizations that need compact real-time tracking combined with physiological telemetry, the L18 brings GPS/BDS/GLONASS positioning, multi-band cellular connectivity \(including optional eSIM\), SOS emergency calling, and a suite of health sensors into a single wearable device.

Ready for integration with Plaspy and other telematics platforms, the L18 delivers continuous location updates, SOS alerts, and heart rate, blood pressure and SpO2 data to web and mobile apps. With IP67 protection, OTA firmware upgrades, and support for BLE and Wi‑Fi scanning, the L18 is a versatile GPS tracker for personnel monitoring, attendance, urban management and personal emergency response.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker in a wearable form factor—real-time tracking and SOS alerts directly from the wrist.
- Multi-constellation GNSS: configurable GPS / BDS / GPS+BDS / GPS+GLONASS for accurate location in urban and mixed environments.
- Integrated health telemetry: heart rate, blood pressure, SPO2 and temperature sensors for actionable personal monitoring.
- Robust cellular connectivity: 4G FDD/TDD multi-band support and GSM fallback with nano SIM or optional eSIM for broad network coverage.
- Two-way voice calling plus an SOS key for immediate assistance and voice-based verification.
- Practical field durability: IP67 waterproof rating, magnetic 2‑pin charging, and a compact 40 g design for daily wear.
- Remote device management via OTA firmware upgrades, web/mobile apps, and 30-day server data retention for compliance and reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the L18 streams location and sensor telemetry over cellular or Wi‑Fi pathways into the Plaspy platform so dispatchers and managers can view live positions, receive SOS alarms, and consume health data in near real-time. Data is delivered securely from the watch’s chipset to cloud services, where Plaspy interprets GPS fixes, alerts and sensor readings for mapping, geofencing and historical reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via 4G/GSM \(or eSIM where provisioned\), with optional Wi‑Fi scanning and BLE-assisted context.
- SOS emergency alerts and two-way voice calling enable immediate incident response and confirmation from Plaspy dashboards or mobile notifications.
- Health telemetry \(heart rate, blood pressure, SPO2, temperature\) transmitted as periodic telemetry for wellbeing monitoring and event-driven alarms.
- Geofence, low-battery and SOS alarm events appear as actionable alerts in Plaspy for dispatching and incident workflows.
- Bluetooth sensors/beacons supported \(BLE 4.0/5.0 optional\) to extend situational awareness where required, such as proximity detection or device pairing.

## Use Cases

- Elder care and personal emergency response — continuous location with SOS and health telemetry for caregivers and family.
- Workforce attendance & task assignment — simple wearable for attendance, location verification and remote check-ins in field operations and urban management.
- Tourism and visitor safety — two-way voice and emergency alerts for guided groups or vulnerable visitors in public areas.
- Field staff tracking for fleet management — complements vehicle telematics by tracking personnel movement and status alongside vehicle data.
- On-site monitoring for healthcare or assisted living facilities — aggregated health metrics and location for event-driven workflows.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The L18 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that balances compact wearable design with a robust sensor suite and global cellular flexibility. For organizations that need reliable real-time tracking and personal telemetry, the L18 simplifies integration: multi-band 4G and optional eSIM support reduce connectivity gaps, OTA updates keep devices current, and the watch’s SOS and two-way voice features accelerate incident response. While the L18 is not a vehicle module for fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer control, it complements fleet management solutions by providing people-centric telemetry—positioning, health metrics and events—that can be used alongside vehicle data in Plaspy dashboards.

Choose the L18 when you need a trustworthy wearable GPS tracker that is easy to deploy, compatible with Plaspy, and purpose-built for personal safety and operational visibility. Its combination of GNSS flexibility, health sensors, IP67 durability and app/cloud management delivers scalable, actionable insights for care teams, security operators and field supervisors.
