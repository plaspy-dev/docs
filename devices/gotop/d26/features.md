# GOTOP - D26 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP D26 and how its capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about what the device can do, how Plaspy displays and leverages device data, and what operators should consider when deploying the D26 for outdoor asset tracking.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation type. Where applicable, this page highlights the D26 features described by the manufacturer while recommending that users confirm specific capabilities and firmware behavior with official GOTOP documentation.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP D26 is a solar powered, rugged GNSS tracking terminal intended for long-term outdoor asset monitoring. It pairs built-in solar charging, high-sensitivity GNSS positioning, cellular connectivity, and on-device intelligence to deliver persistent location updates and event reporting for remote containers, trailers, machinery, and similar assets.

- Continuous outdoor operation enabled by a built-in solar charging system for extended deployments without frequent servicing
- Rugged IP67-rated enclosure with a full built-in antenna for low-maintenance outdoor mounting and reliable satellite reception
- Cellular connectivity using 4G and GPRS for real-time location reporting and telemetry over mobile networks
- High-sensitivity GPS and BDS positioning with AGPS to reduce time to first fix and improve accuracy in challenging reception environments
- On-device motion awareness via a 3-axis acceleration sensor for intelligent wake-up, motion detection, and power saving
- Built-in memory for offline data storage that buffers locations and events during coverage gaps for later upload

## Core Features of GOTOP - D26

- Solar charging system designed for cyclical charging and discharging to support long-term outdoor operation
- IP67-rated rugged housing and industrial full built-in antenna for harsh environments and low maintenance
- 4G LTE and GPRS cellular connectivity with TCP/IP support for reliable server communications
- GPS and BDS GNSS positioning with AGPS support and anti-jamming antenna characteristics for stable fixes
- Built-in 3-axis acceleration sensor used for motion detection, intelligent wake-up, and power management
- Multiple alarm types supported including vibration alarm, over-speed alarm, geofence events, and SOS alerts delivered via SMS or call and uploaded to the platform
- Onboard memory for offline data buffering and supplementary transmission when connectivity resumes
- Remote configuration and online firmware upgrade capability for fleet-wide management

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the D26 streams location and event data into a centralized telematics environment where operators can monitor assets, receive alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and surfaces GNSS fixes, telemetry, and alarms for visualization and operational workflows.

- Real-time GNSS updates and telemetry appear on Plaspy live maps for continuous tracking of remote assets
- Event uploads such as vibration alerts, geofence breaches, over-speed events, and SOS notifications are surfaced in Plaspy to trigger alerts and notifications
- Offline buffer data stored on the device is forwarded to Plaspy after connectivity is restored to preserve event history and maintain accurate playback
- Motion-driven wake-up and power-saving behavior on the D26 reduces unnecessary reporting while preserving critical events visible in Plaspy
- Remote configuration and firmware updates streamline large-scale maintenance when devices are managed through Plaspy compatible workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Long-term container and trailer tracking where solar charging and geofence alerts reduce the need for frequent inspections
- Remote equipment monitoring for machinery left in the field with vibration and motion detection to indicate movement or tampering
- Fleet oversight of semi-permanent or off-road assets that require intermittent connectivity buffering and reliable outdoor performance
- Construction and agricultural asset protection where rugged IP67 construction and event alerts improve site security
- General outdoor asset telemetry to centralize GNSS location, motion events, and alarms for reporting and historical analysis

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and behavior depend on device firmware version and manufacturer configuration; not every unit may expose all features out of the box
- Hardware revisions or regional model variants can change cellular band support, available I/O, and the exact set of supported alarms
- Installation type and mounting orientation can affect GNSS reception and solar charging performance in the field
- Offline buffering and supplementary transmission behavior depends on memory configuration and firmware settings
- For integrations that require additional signals or I/O, confirm the specific D26 hardware variant and available interfaces before deployment
