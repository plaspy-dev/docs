# GOTOP - G60 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the GOTOP G60 as used with Plaspy. It explains the tracker’s main capabilities, how the device reports into Plaspy, and the practical value this model offers for logistics, fleet and asset tracking without diving into private or manufacturer-only configuration steps.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional cellular configuration, and manufacturer implementation. For device-specific commands, up-to-date firmware notes, and regulatory details refer to the manufacturer documentation while using this page to understand how the G60 maps to Plaspy workflows.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G60 is a compact magnetic 4G tracker built for discreet, long-duration asset and vehicle tracking. It is designed to provide reliable location reporting and event telemetry for containers, rental fleets and other assets where hidden mounting and extended standby are priorities.

- 4G LTE-FDD plus quad-band GSM connectivity for wide cellular coverage and flexible reporting.
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas with EPO and active time calibration to help speed satellite fixes.
- Magnetic no-wiring attachment and IP65-rated housing for discreet and durable installations.
- Reporting via GPRS or SMS into Plaspy to support real-time tracking and fallback reporting.
- Vibration and movement alarms plus low-battery notifications to support anti-theft and operational monitoring.

## Core Features of GOTOP - G60

- 4G LTE-FDD and quad-band GSM cellular support for broad network availability.
- Internal GPS with assisted time calibration (EPO and active calibration) to improve fix times.
- Robust physical design with IP65 water resistance and strong magnetic mount for hidden placement.
- Long standby battery capability and configurable reporting intervals for extended deployments.
- Alarm telemetry including vibration or movement notifications and low battery alerts.
- Reporting methods via GPRS (primary) and SMS (fallback), plus remote parameter queries via SMS.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position reports and event messages from the G60 to provide centralized visibility, alerting and historical playback. The platform maps incoming GPRS or SMS reports to device location and event timelines so teams can monitor assets remotely and respond to notifications.

- Real-time and periodic location updates appear on Plaspy maps and history views for route playback.
- Alarm events such as vibration, movement, and low-battery generate Plaspy alerts and can trigger notification workflows.
- Configurable reporting intervals let you balance update frequency with battery life inside Plaspy device settings and operational plans.
- Remote parameter queries and version checks via SMS allow administrators to confirm device status without physical access and reflect results in operational checks.
- When GPS fixes are slow, cellular base-station positioning acts as a fallback source that Plaspy can display for continued location awareness.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental vehicle and light fleet tracking where discreet mounting and long standby are required.
- Container and cargo monitoring for long-duration transport and hidden installation on chassis or units.
- Anti-theft monitoring using vibration and movement alarms forwarded into Plaspy alerting.
- Long-term asset surveillance where configurable wake and reporting intervals conserve battery.
- Logistics and route visibility for operational transparency and historical route review.
- Temporary relocatable asset tracking where magnet mounting enables fast redeployment.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware revisions may enable, alter, or extend specific telemetry messages, alarm thresholds, and command names.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants can affect supported cellular bands and network behavior.
- Installation details such as mounting location, surrounding metal, and antenna orientation influence GPS reception and reporting reliability.
- SMS versus GPRS behavior depends on the device configuration and the cellular plan in use for the installed SIM.
- Always check the latest manufacturer documentation for device-specific SMS commands, firmware notes, and regulatory compliance.
