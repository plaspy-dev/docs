# GOTOP - G25 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the GOTOP G25 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the user-facing capabilities and practical functions that matter when the G25 is used for asset tracking and monitoring in the Plaspy platform. The content is intended to help fleet managers, asset owners, and integrators understand how the device capabilities translate into visibility and operational value inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and the way the device is installed or configured. Where appropriate this page notes those variations and encourages verification with the manufacturer and current product documentation before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G25 is a 4G asset tracker built for long-term tracking and protection of a wide range of assets. It combines cellular connectivity, built-in GPS and antenna design, and high-capacity batteries to deliver persistent location reporting and basic alarm functions that are useful for remote monitoring.

- Multi generation cellular support for reliable network connectivity across regions
- Built-in GPS and 4G antenna enabling wireless and concealed installation
- High waterproof rating (PIX-6) and a super magnet for secure attachment to assets
- Large battery options for extended standby and low maintenance operation
- Real-time tracking with Google map link locating for quick position checks
- Alarms and power-save modes including drop-off, movement, and low battery alerts

## Core Features of GOTOP - G25

- 4G LTE with fallback to 3G and 2G networks for broad cellular coverage
- Integrated GPS and 4G antenna allowing wireless installation and simpler mounting
- High waterproof rating (PIX-6) combined with a strong magnet for rugged outdoor use
- Large battery capacity options designed for long standby time and infrequent servicing
- Real-time tracking and Google map link locating for fast access to position data
- Support for web platform and mobile app tracking for remote access on multiple devices
- Alarm functions including drop-off alarm, movement alarm, and lower battery alarm
- Sleeping power-save mode to extend battery life during long-term deployments

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the GOTOP G25 reports location and events, Plaspy captures that information and presents it through maps, timelines, and event feeds to make asset status and movements visible to operators.

- Real-time position updates from the G25 appear on the Plaspy map and device list for live monitoring
- Drop-off, movement, and low battery events are recorded as alerts or events in Plaspy for operational awareness
- Long battery life supports lower-frequency reporting scenarios and extended unattended tracking visible in Plaspy history
- Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols so device reporting can appear without deep protocol tuning
- The G25 supports SMS parameter settings and query functions at the device level; Plaspy complements those device controls by showing reported states rather than replacing device-side configuration

## Typical Use Cases

- Long-term asset tracking for containers, trailers, and equipment left in the field
- Fleet and rental vehicle oversight where concealed wireless installation is preferred
- Tracking of lightweight or hard-to-wire assets such as bicycles, e-bikes, and motorcycles
- Monitoring of goods in transit or stored items where drop-off or movement alerts are useful
- Security protection of high-value portable assets and toolbox or carton monitoring

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior and names may vary by firmware version and hardware revision; check the exact model and firmware release for details
- The G25 is offered with different battery capacity options; available runtime depends on reporting intervals and power-save settings
- Installation method (magnet mount versus different mounting practices) can affect signal reception and event reliability
- Regional cellular support depends on the device variant and the local operator bands supported by that variant
- Manufacturer documentation should be consulted for the most current configuration procedures and legal compliance information

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy together with the GOTOP G25 gives organizations a simple way to convert raw device reporting into operational insight. Plaspy’s mapping, event handling, and historical playback make it easier to see where assets are, how they have moved over time, and when alerts like low battery or unexpected movement occur. For teams managing dispersed or long-term assets, the combination of the G25’s battery life and rugged mounting options with Plaspy visibility reduces the need for frequent physical checks.
