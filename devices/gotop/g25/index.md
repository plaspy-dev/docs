# GOTOP - G25

![G25](./logo..png)

The GOTOP G25 is a 4G asset tracker built for reliable and efficient monitoring of a wide range of assets. Designed with a large battery capacity, it is intended for long term tracking and protection of items such as private cars, rental vehicles, fleet trucks, equipment, containers, bicycles, e-bikes, motorcycles, and parcel loads. The device includes an integrated GPS and 4G antenna, a high waterproof rating, and a strong magnet for secure placement, and it provides real time location updates plus common alarms like drop off, movement, and low battery alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G25 can be used with Plaspy's fleet and asset management features to provide live visibility and operational oversight. Its long standby capability, waterproof construction, and web and mobile app support make the tracker a practical choice for users who want persistent location reporting and simple remote access through a platform like Plaspy. Integrating the G25 with Plaspy lets organizations consolidate tracking, alerts, and historical location data in one place.

## Key Highlights

- 4G connectivity with fallback to 3G and 2G for broad network reach
- Built in GPS and 4G antenna for wireless placement and simpler mounting
- High waterproof rating PIX 6 and strong magnet for durable, secure attachment
- Large battery capacity options for extended standby and reduced maintenance
- Support for web platform and mobile app tracking for remote monitoring
- Built in alarms including drop off alarm, movement alarm, and lower battery alarm
- Real time tracking with Google map link locating and a power save sleeping mode

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GOTOP G25 streams its location and alarm events into a centralized fleet management environment so teams can monitor assets, respond to alerts, and generate reports. Plaspy displays the device position on maps, stores historical tracks, and uses alarm events to trigger notifications or workflows.

- Real time location display and map links within the Plaspy interface
- Alarm and status notifications routed into Plaspy for immediate attention
- Historical route and location data available for reporting and audits
- Centralized visibility across mixed fleets and dispersed assets
- Battery and uptime visibility to plan maintenance and reduce downtime
- Use of mobile and web access in Plaspy to check asset status from anywhere

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of rental vehicles and private cars for asset recovery and oversight
- Monitoring of fleet vehicles and delivery trucks for route visibility and operational planning
- Protection and location tracking of portable equipment and containers on job sites
- Tracking bicycles, e bikes, and motorcycles for theft deterrence and recovery
- Securing parcels, trunk loads, and cartons during transport or storage
- General security protection for valuable or mobile assets that need remote monitoring
