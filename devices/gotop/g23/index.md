# GOTOP - G23

<div className="device-logo">
![G23](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP G23 is a mini wire GPS tracker designed for discreet vehicle installation and continuous location monitoring. Its compact ABS housing, internal antennas and slim profile make it well suited to cars, motorcycles, e bikes and light commercial vehicles where a low profile is required. The G23 uses hybrid positioning with GPS, BeiDou and LBS for typical location accuracy around five meters and includes vehicle inputs and alarms commonly used for theft prevention and fleet oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G23 can forward its location and event telemetry into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting. That compatibility means fleet operators and security teams can include the G23 in Plaspy dashboards and workflows to track vehicles in real time, receive power off or tamper alarms, and act on ignition and immobilizer events without replacing their tracking platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact mini wire form factor that supports discreet and concealed installations for theft prevention.
- Hybrid GNSS positioning with GPS and BeiDou plus LBS for reliable location fixes and route playback.
- Vehicle telemetry support including ignition detection, overspeed alerts and vibration movement alarms.
- Remote power or fuel cut capability useful for immobilizer style interventions where permitted.
- Wide DC input range for use across cars, motorcycles, e bikes and light commercial vehicles.
- Low sleep current and an 80 mAh backup battery to report offline events and power loss alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once installed and connected, the G23 transmits location and event data to Plaspy where those messages are parsed into live maps, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy receives the tracker reports and maps them to notifications, rules and fleet views so operators can monitor vehicles, respond to incidents, and generate operational reports.

- Real time GNSS and LBS location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and route playback.
- Ignition ACC detection and illegal engine start alerts are surfaced in Plaspy to help detect unauthorized use.
- Remote power or fuel cut commands can be managed through Plaspy interfaces when allowed by installation and local rules.
- Power off and backup battery notifications are used by Plaspy to flag possible tampering or power loss events.
- Overspeed and vibration alarms feed directly into Plaspy rules for alerts, safety workflows and driver coaching.
- Historical data from the G23 is available in Plaspy for reporting, analysis and operational review.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small delivery vans, rental vehicles and mixed light commercial fleets.
- Anti theft monitoring and immobilization for passenger cars and motorcycles.
- Tracking for motorcycles and e bikes where compact size and wide voltage tolerance are important.
- Driver behavior and safety monitoring using overspeed and vibration alerts for coaching and compliance.
- Mixed fleet telematics where a single tracker type supports diverse vehicle classes.

The GOTOP G23 pairs a discreet hardware profile with the centralized visibility and rules engine of Plaspy. Its vehicle focused inputs and alarm set provide the practical events many fleet and security operators rely on, while Plaspy turns those events into actionable notifications, control actions and historical reports. For organizations that need a small form factor tracker that still provides key anti theft and fleet telemetry, the G23 is a practical option to include in a Plaspy deployment.
