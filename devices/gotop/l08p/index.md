# GOTOP - L08P

![L08P](./tracker.jpg)

The Watch GPS Tracker L08P is a rugged, health-focused 4G GPS smartwatch built for dependable personal tracking and emergency support. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the L08P brings accurate real-time tracking, robust connectivity \(4G/3G/2G, eSIM and nano‑SIM support\), and a full suite of health and safety telemetry to caregiver platforms and monitoring services. Its bright 1.43-inch 466x466 AMOLED touchscreen and IP66-rated enclosure make it practical for daily wear by seniors, people with dementia, lone workers, and anyone who needs continuous location and health visibility.

The L08P integrates multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, BDS, GLONASS\), BLE 5.0 sensor support, 2.4 GHz WiFi for improved indoor positioning, and OTA firmware updates, enabling smooth integration with Plaspy for alarm delivery, location history, and remote device management. With a 650 mAh battery and power-optimized dual-CPU architecture \(Unisoc W307 + GR5515 sensor hub\), the watch balances performance and battery life so caregivers receive reliable telemetry — heart rate, SpO2, optional ECG, and emergency SOS — while maintaining continuous real-time tracking and alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker watch for seamless platform integration and alert delivery.
- Real-time tracking with multi‑GNSS \(GPS, BDS, GLONASS\) for consistent location updates indoors and outdoors.
- Comprehensive health telemetry: heart rate \(PAH8009\), SpO2, blood pressure, step counting, and optional ECG \(TI 1291\).
- 4G/3G/2G cellular plus eSIM and nano‑SIM support, 2.4G WiFi, and BLE 5.0 for flexible connectivity and remote management.
- SOS button, two‑way calling, geo‑fencing, and low‑battery alerts for dependable anti‑theft and personal safety workflows.
- Rugged, IP66 water resistance and durable materials \(PC+ABS+ceramics casing, 316L stainless steel buttons\) for everyday use.
- OTA firmware updates and WiFi connectivity simplify maintenance and scale device management across deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the L08P operates as a real-time GPS tracker and telemetry node: it transmits GNSS positions, health metrics, and safety events over cellular or WiFi to Plaspy servers or your monitoring instance. Plaspy compatible integration supports alarm routing, location playback, and configurable alerts so caregivers and operations teams can act immediately on SOS, geo-fence breaches, or medical telemetry thresholds.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via 4G/3G/2G or WiFi to Plaspy for continuous tracking and history.
- SOS alarms, two-way calling, geo-fencing notifications, and low-battery alerts delivered to Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps.
- Health monitoring data \(heart rate, SpO2, blood pressure, optional ECG\) forwarded as telemetry for caregiver review and event-triggered alerts.
- Remote management features via OTA firmware updates and WiFi for fleet‑style device maintenance and configuration.
- BLE 5.0 support enables local sensor or beacon integrations for expanded telemetry or proximity-based events.
