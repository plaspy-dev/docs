# GOTOP - TL-206

<div className="device-logo">
![TL-206](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP TL-206 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for real time tracking and monitoring of people, vehicles, and pets. It uses GPS satellites for position fixes and can transmit coordinates via SMS or by sending data to a designated server using the device data connection. With a very small enclosure and an advertised standby time up to 200 hours, the TL-206 is intended for situations where discreet, continuous tracking is required.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TL-206 can forward its location data to a tracking platform for map display, alerts, and historical review. Plaspy can incorporate the TL-206 into centralized monitoring workflows, enabling fleet managers, caretakers, and security teams to view positions, receive geofence and SOS notifications, and manage devices from a single dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- Small form factor at approximately 65 mm x 40 mm x 18 mm for discreet carry or mounting
- Real time locating and tracking with position reporting via SMS or server data upload
- Long standby time of up to 200 hours for extended use between charges
- Two way voice capability to enable direct communication with the device
- Built in positioning chipset noted for solid accuracy even in weak signal conditions
- Safety features such as SOS button and configurable geo fence monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TL-206 can be configured to send its location and status to a tracking server, allowing Plaspy to receive and present that data for live monitoring and historical analysis. In Plaspy, the TL-206 functions as a standard tracked asset that contributes location updates and event notifications for operational oversight.

- Live position display on Plaspy maps for real time visibility of tracked assets
- Geofence setup and alerting so Plaspy can notify users when the device enters or leaves defined zones
- SOS and alert handling to surface emergency events within Plaspy workflows
- Historical route and event logs for reporting and review inside Plaspy
- Centralized device list and basic device status for easier fleet or personal asset management

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for children, elderly family members, and vulnerable individuals
- Pet tracking for outdoor monitoring of dogs or other animals
- Anti theft tracking for small vehicles, equipment, and portable assets
- Remote monitoring for field personnel or travelling staff
- Business use where a compact, long standby tracker is needed for occasional location reporting

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TL-206 pairs a very small footprint with straightforward position reporting options, making it a practical choice when discretion and battery life are important. Its two way voice and SOS features add an extra layer of direct communication and emergency notification that complements Plaspy's centralized monitoring and alerting capabilities.

For organizations and individuals who need compact personal trackers integrated into a broader tracking platform, the TL-206 offers a balance of portability, continuous tracking, and safety features. When used with Plaspy, the device can become part of an operational view that supports location awareness, geofence alerts, and basic reporting without adding unnecessary complexity.
