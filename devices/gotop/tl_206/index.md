# GOTOP - TL-206

![TL-206](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TL-206 is a personal GPS tracker designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring for personal, vehicle, and pet use. It works based on existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites, allowing you to transmit the longitude and latitude coordinates to your cell phone via SMS, as well as to a designated server via GPRS for location display on tracking software. With its compact size of 65mm x 40mm x 18mm, it is one of the smallest GPS trackers available in the market. Despite its small size, it offers a long standby time of up to 200 hours, ensuring reliable and continuous tracking.

One of the standout features of the TL-206 is its two-way voice communication capability. It has a ringing sound when there is an incoming call in 2-way voice mode, allowing you to communicate with the tracking target just like a cell phone. Additionally, it supports SMS communication or GPRS TCP connection, giving you multiple options for obtaining position information. The TL-206 utilizes a built-in SIRF Star III Chipset, which ensures excellent positioning accuracy even in weak signal conditions. It also has built-in GSM/GPS modules and antennas, making it compatible with networks worldwide.

The TL-206 is suitable for a variety of applications. It can be used to monitor and protect children, the elderly, young women, and disabled individuals, providing peace of mind for their safety. It is also an effective tool for guarding against theft of strongboxes, vehicles, outdoor equipment, and cargo. For businessmen, it offers personalized services based on GSM network and the internet, allowing them to track and monitor their assets remotely. The TL-206 supports up to five authorized telephone numbers and has a listen-in function, providing added security and convenience. With its portable and compact design, as well as power-saving smart-mode, the TL-206 is a reliable and user-friendly GPS tracker for personal use.

### Key Features:

- Real-time locating and tracking
- Geo-fence for setting up restricted areas
- Two-way talking communication
- SOS button for emergency situations
- Built-in SIRF Star III Chipset for accurate positioning
- Built-in GSM/GPS modules and antennas
- Support for SMS communication or GPRS TCP connection
- Long standby time of up to 200 hours
- Compact size and power-saving design
