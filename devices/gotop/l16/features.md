# GOTOP - L16 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the GOTOP L16 GPS Smart Watch with Plaspy. It focuses on the main capabilities that are relevant to monitoring, alerts, and operational use through Plaspy rather than device configuration minutiae. Where useful, the content highlights how the L16 reports location and status to the Plaspy platform for real time visibility and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, installation, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific details such as firmware change history, hardware variants, or the full technical specification, consult the official GOTOP documentation.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP L16 is a compact wearable tracker designed for everyday safety and remote monitoring. It combines multi mode positioning, built in health telemetry, emergency features, and smartphone class connectivity to deliver continuous status and location updates to Plaspy for dashboards, alerts, and historical playback.

- Wearable GPS Smart Watch form factor for continuous personal monitoring
- Multi mode positioning including GPS and Beidou plus AGPS LBS Wi Fi and BLE assisted location
- Health telemetry including heart rate SpO2 blood pressure and temperature reported to monitoring platforms
- One key SOS alarm and two way voice calling for immediate emergency communications
- IP67 rated durability and multi day battery life for daily use and reduced maintenance

## Core Features of GOTOP - L16

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for real time tracking and status updates
- Multi mode positioning list includes GPS Beidou AGPS LBS Wi Fi and BLE assisted positioning
- Integrated health sensors reporting heart rate SpO2 blood pressure and temperature
- Emergency communications with a one key SOS alarm and two way voice calling from the watch
- IP67 water and dust resistance for everyday wear
- Multi day battery operation supported by a 680 mAh battery for normal usage patterns
- BLE support for local positioning assistance and pairing with Bluetooth peripherals
- Cellular connectivity with LTE Cat 1 and GSM fallback plus Nano SIM and eSIM options

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the L16 streams position updates and telemetry so caregivers and teams can monitor status in real time and review historical data. Plaspy ingests location points SOS events call notifications and sensor telemetry and surface those items in dashboards and alert rules to support timely response.

- Real time location updates and historical playback visible in Plaspy dashboards
- Health telemetry such as heart rate SpO2 blood pressure and temperature available for monitoring and logging
- SOS alarm events and two way call notifications forwarded to Plaspy so teams receive immediate alerts
- Geofence and low battery alerts can be generated inside Plaspy based on incoming device events
- The L16 can be pointed to Plaspy servers and supports UDP or TCP device reporting and Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for elders children and vulnerable users with SOS and two way calling
- Caregiver and family tracking where health telemetry and location history matter
- Lone worker situational awareness including emergency alarm and location reporting
- Short term wearable asset or parcel tracking where a watch form factor and BLE sensors are useful
- Remote health snapshot monitoring for non clinical oversight and trend awareness

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the GOTOP L16 with Plaspy brings wearable location and health telemetry into a single monitoring workflow. Plaspy captures position updates SOS events and sensor data so administrators and caregivers can centralize alerts review history and act on incidents without switching between multiple systems. The combination is designed to simplify response for personal safety programs and small scale monitoring deployments.
