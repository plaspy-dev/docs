# GOTOP - TK-120

![TK-120](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TK-120 is a mini GPS tracker designed for reliable and discreet tracking. Its compact form factor makes it suitable for monitoring people, assets, vehicles, and pets. The device uses GPS satellites together with the GSM network to determine position and offers both SMS location replies and GPRS reporting for flexible tracking methods.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the TK-120 can be used to feed location updates into Plaspy for map visualization, reporting, and operational oversight. Its ability to transmit coordinates by SMS and to send periodic position data via GPRS makes the tracker adaptable for users who need simple SMS access or continuous tracking through a platform like Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Miniature and discreet form factor suited for covert placement and compact use
- Dual tracking options: SMS location responses with Google Maps links and GPRS reporting
- User configurable reporting intervals for scheduled position updates
- Useful for monitoring people, assets, vehicles, and pets in a variety of environments
- Straightforward location information that can be viewed quickly via Google Maps link
- Practical choice where a small footprint and flexible reporting are primary requirements

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK-120 can deliver location data to the platform for centralized monitoring and management. Plaspy receives position updates sent by the device and presents those updates on live maps and in historical logs, enabling fleet managers and administrators to maintain situational awareness.

- Receive periodic position reports from the tracker and display them on Plaspy maps
- Use location updates for real time visibility and simple routing oversight
- Configure alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on position changes or movement patterns
- Generate location history and activity reports for operational review and compliance
- Combine TK-120 data with other devices in Plaspy for consolidated asset and fleet monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet monitoring of personal items or high value movable assets
- Vehicle location tracking for small fleets or individual cars
- Tracking and protection of pets during walks or travel
- Temporary deployments where a small, easy to conceal tracker is needed
