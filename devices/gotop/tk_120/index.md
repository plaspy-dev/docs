# GOTOP - TK-120

![TK-120](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TK-120 is a mini GPS tracker that offers reliable and discreet tracking capabilities. Designed to be compact and easy to hide, this tracker is perfect for monitoring and protecting individuals, assets, vehicles, and even pets. It utilizes the existing GSM network and GPS satellites to provide accurate location information.

One of the standout features of the TK-120 is its ability to transmit longitude and latitude coordinates to your cell phone via SMS. Additionally, it can reply to SMS messages with a Google Maps link. By clicking on the link, you can view the detailed position information directly on Google Maps. This feature provides a convenient and user-friendly way to track and monitor the device.

In addition to SMS tracking, the TK-120 also supports tracking via GPRS. This means that it can transmit position data to tracking software at regular intervals, as set by the user. This allows for real-time tracking and monitoring of the device's location. Whether you prefer SMS or GPRS tracking, the TK-120 offers flexibility and convenience.

With its small size and versatile tracking capabilities, the GOTOP TK-120 is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a reliable and discreet GPS tracker. Whether you want to keep an eye on your loved ones, protect your valuable assets, or ensure the safety of your pets, the TK-120 has you covered.
