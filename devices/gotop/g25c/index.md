# GOTOP - G25C

![G25C](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP G25C is a compact 4G GPS tracker designed for Internet of Things applications and general asset tracking. It combines LTE wireless communication with GPS and BDS satellite positioning to deliver real time location information. Built with an industrial grade full built in antenna and a rugged enclosure, the G25C is positioned for use on private cars, rental vehicles, fleet trucks, bicycles, and other mobile assets.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the G25C can feed location and alert data into a centralized tracking platform for operational visibility. Its long life battery and configurable power saving modes make it a practical choice for deployments where periodic updates, event alerts, and remote monitoring are needed. Plaspy users can integrate the tracker to support monitoring, notifications, and basic reporting across mixed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- 4G LTE wireless connectivity combined with GPS and BDS satellite positioning for real time tracking
- High capacity 20000 mAh battery offering very long standby times and extended field operation
- Three configurable power saving modes to balance reporting frequency and battery life
- Multiple alarm functions including drop off alarm, movement alarm, low battery alarm, shake alarm, and geo fence alarm
- Voice monitoring and recording capability for remote audio checks where permitted
- IP65 waterproof rating and strong magnet mounting for flexible field deployment
- Usable with web platforms and mobile apps and supports SMS parameter settings and SMS map links

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy can accept location updates and event notifications from compatible trackers like the G25C so fleet managers and operators gain a single view of asset location, status, and alerts. Once connected, the device data can be used for monitoring, geofence enforcement, and historical review within Plaspy.

- Real time location display for individual units and group views in Plaspy dashboards
- Alarm forwarding so drop off, movement, low battery, shake, and geo fence events appear as notifications
- Battery status visibility to track remaining capacity and plan maintenance or recharge cycles
- Historical tracking and replay to support route analysis and incident review
- Reporting and exportable location logs for operational oversight and record keeping

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small to medium sized trucking or delivery operations
- Rental car and shared vehicle monitoring to track usage and location
- Asset protection for high value portable equipment and bicycles
- Long term location monitoring for assets that require infrequent reporting
- Site tools and temporary equipment tracking where waterproofing and magnet mounting are useful

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G25C is a practical option for organizations that need a balance of long battery endurance and dependable satellite positioning. Its alarm features and flexible power modes make it suitable for deployments where intermittent updates and event driven alerts are more important than continuous high frequency reporting. When paired with Plaspy, the device becomes part of a larger visibility solution that centralizes tracking data for operational decisions.

For teams evaluating hardware for use with Plaspy, the G25C stands out for its battery life, rugged mounting options, and a feature set aimed at common tracking scenarios. Its compatibility with web and mobile platforms means data from the device can be surfaced to users in the field and in the office through Plaspy tools.
