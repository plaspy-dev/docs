# GOTOP - G20 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive summary of the GOTOP G20 GPS tracker and how its features map to Plaspy for device monitoring and fleet or asset tracking. It covers practical capabilities you can expect when using the G20 with Plaspy while staying focused on general operational behavior rather than private configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For final device setup, advanced configuration, or the most current specifications, consult the official GOTOP documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G20 is a compact GPS tracker built for person and asset tracking with multiple positioning sources and a selection of user-facing alarms and audio features. It balances long battery options with a small form factor and an IP65 rated housing for everyday use in vehicles and portable asset scenarios.

- Multi source positioning using GPS BD and LBS for improved location reliability
- Precise reported location with a stated accuracy of approximately 5 meters under good signal conditions
- Multiple battery capacity options for extended tracking intervals and deployment flexibility
- Audio monitoring and recording features for remote listening and event capture
- Motion and vibration detection plus overspeed and low voltage alerts for operational awareness
- Small, magnet-friendly enclosure and IP65 rated housing for secure, discreet placement

## Core Features of GOTOP - G20

- GPS+BD+LBS positioning system for combined satellite and network based location
- Positioning accuracy around 5 meters in favorable conditions
- Multiple battery capacities available: 1500 mAh, 3000 mAh, or 5000 mAh polymer options
- Audio features including remote audio monitoring and voice recording and sound controlled recording
- Motion related alerts such as move alarm and vibration alarm
- Vehicle or asset alerts including overspeed alarm and low voltage alarm
- Compact ABS plastic housing with IP65 rating and optional powerful magnet for attachment

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can ingest position and event reports from the G20 and present them in the platform for monitoring, history, and alerts. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols, and devices configured to report into Plaspy will appear with location and event visibility consistent with the data the device sends.

- Live location and breadcrumb history from GPS BD LBS positioning shown on Plaspy maps
- Alert events such as overspeed move vibration and low battery surfaced in Plaspy event logs
- Remote audio or recorded events indicated as device-reported activities where available and allowed by the device
- Battery status and alerting available when the device reports voltage or low voltage events
- Plaspy supports standard device connectivity and can accept device reports over common transport methods; Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol for compatible units

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental vehicle tracking for location history and low battery or movement alerts
- Person tracking where compact size and audio monitoring can assist in safety or check in scenarios
- Portable asset tracking where small form factor and magnetic mounting are helpful
- Taxi and passenger vehicle monitoring for location and overspeed event awareness
- Light truck and cargo monitoring for movement detection and battery status visibility

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence depends on the specific G20 model variant and selected battery capacity
- Firmware version and manufacturer configuration can enable or disable audio, recording, and certain alarm behaviors
- Installation method and antenna exposure can affect reported positioning accuracy and cold or hot start times
- Regional cellular band support affects connectivity in different countries; confirm 2G support and cellular availability for your region
- For the most current technical details consult GOTOP manufacturer documentation and official product pages

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the GOTOP G20 centralizes location, alert, and event data so operations teams can monitor people and assets from a single platform. Plaspy presents location history, event timelines, and alerting based on the reports the G20 sends, helping teams maintain situational awareness and respond to operational events.
