# GOTOP - G08-4G

![G08-4G](./tracker.png)

The G08-4G is a compact, plug and play OBD vehicle tracker designed for real time tracking and fleet management. Built as an OBD plug in with integrated GPS and GSM antennas and 4G connectivity, the device provides continuous position reporting, geo fencing, alarm notifications and route history logging without separate wiring installation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G08-4G connects to Plaspy to deliver live location, telemetry and alerting workflows that support anti theft monitoring and operational visibility. Its plug and play form factor and communications design make it a practical option for vehicle owners and fleet operators who want fast deployment and centralized tracking within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD installation for immediate vehicle tracking and fleet management integration with minimal setup.
- 4G LTE communications with multi network fallback and SMS reporting to maintain connectivity in varied coverage areas.
- Integrated GPS and GSM antennas with typical position accuracy suitable for precise location reporting and route history.
- Alarm capabilities including geo fencing, vibration or movement alerts, low battery and main power cut notifications to support theft detection workflows.
- Internal backup battery for short term reporting when external power is interrupted.
- Compact dimensions and wide operating voltage for compatibility across cars, light trucks and other vehicles.
- Supports both data and SMS reporting to Plaspy for flexible telemetry and alert delivery.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the G08-4G transmits location, status and alarm messages over cellular data or SMS so Plaspy can display real time positions, trigger alerts and retain historical trip data. Plaspy receives those updates and presents them through dashboards, map views and notification channels to support operational oversight and incident response.

- Real time location and status updates delivered to Plaspy via cellular data with SMS fallback when needed.
- Geo fence entry and exit alerts plus movement or vibration alarms pushed to Plaspy dashboards and notification workflows.
- Low battery and main power cut notifications flagged in Plaspy to help detect tampering or disconnected devices.
- Short term location reporting from the internal backup battery when external power is lost.
- Route history and trip logs available in Plaspy for post trip analysis and operational reporting.
- Where vehicle OBD level telemetry is available, Plaspy can consume that data to enrich dashboards and event detection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft monitoring with instant alerts for unexpected movement, vibration or power loss.
- Fleet management and driver oversight using real time tracking and route history for optimization.
- Rental and car sharing programs that benefit from plug and play deployment and accurate location logs.
- Last mile delivery and service fleets that rely on geo fencing and notification workflows to enforce service zones.
- Vehicle owners who want continuous monitoring and simple integration into a centralized tracking platform.

The G08-4G pairs practical simplicity with features fleet operators commonly need: quick installation, continuous reporting, alarms for tampering or power loss, and route history. Combined with Plaspy, the device becomes part of a centralized monitoring and reporting workflow that helps teams maintain visibility across vehicles and respond to events from a single platform.
