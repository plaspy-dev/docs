# GOTOP - G08-4G

![G08-4G](./tracker.png)

The G08-4G OBD Tracker is a compact, plug-and-play GPS tracker designed for vehicle real-time tracking and fleet management. Manufactured as an OBD-II plug-in with integrated GPS/GSM antennas and 4G LTE connectivity, the G08-4G delivers continuous position reporting, geo-fencing, and alarm notifications without a separate wiring installation. As a Plaspy compatible tracker, it connects to Plaspy for live location, telemetry, and alerting workflows that help fleets and vehicle owners reduce theft risk and improve operational visibility.

The G08-4G combines reliable 4G communications with multi-network fallback \(4G/3G/2G\), an internal backup battery for short-term reporting after power loss, and a compact form factor for easy installation at the vehicle OBD port. It supports both data and SMS reporting, vibration and movement alarms, low battery and main power cut notifications, and route history logging — making it an effective option for commercial fleets, rental vehicles, and personal cars requiring continuous monitoring through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible plug-and-play GPS tracker — install at the vehicle OBD-II port for immediate real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Reliable 4G LTE communication with multi-network fallback \(4G/3G/2G\) and SMS reporting for areas with limited data coverage.
- Built-in GPS/GSM antennas and GPS accuracy typically within 5 meters for precise location reporting and route history.
- Alarm set: geo-fencing, vibration/movement alarms, low battery alert and main power cut notification to support anti-theft workflows.
- Integrated backup battery \(3.7V 180 mAh\) for short-term tracking if external power is interrupted.
- Wide operating voltage \(9–35 V\) and compact dimensions \(59 x 45 x 22 mm, 42 g\) for compatibility with cars, trucks and other vehicles.
- Supports SMS and 4G data reporting to Plaspy for flexible telemetry and alert delivery.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the G08-4G transmits location, status and alarm messages over 4G data or SMS. Plaspy receives those updates to display real-time tracking on maps, trigger geofence alerts, log route history, and forward notifications to operations teams. The device’s OBD-II connection provides vehicle power and can supply OBD-level telemetry where available; Plaspy can use that telemetry for fuel monitoring and ignition event reporting when the vehicle exposes those parameters.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via 4G data or SMS fallback.
- Geo-fence entries/exits and movement/vibration alarms push instant alerts to Plaspy dashboards and mobile notifications.
- Low battery and main power cut notifications to detect tampering or disconnected devices.
- Short-term location reporting from the backup battery when external power is lost.
- Route history and trip logging available in Plaspy for fleet analytics and route replay.
- OBD-derived telemetry \(where available from the vehicle\) can feed Plaspy dashboards for fuel monitoring, ignition event detection and other metrics.
