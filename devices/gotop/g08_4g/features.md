# GOTOP - G08-4G Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP G08-4G OBD tracker and explains how its capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on user facing features such as plug and play installation, continuous location reporting, geo fencing, alarm notifications, and route history that Plaspy can use for fleet visibility and anti theft workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, advanced telemetry details, or the latest firmware behaviors please consult official GOTOP documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The G08-4G is a compact OBD II plug in tracker designed for quick installation and continuous vehicle tracking. It combines cellular data and SMS reporting with built in GPS to provide location and event updates that Plaspy can surface to operations teams and dashboards.

- Plug and play OBD II installation for immediate tracking without separate wiring
- 4G LTE connectivity with multi network fallback and SMS reporting for resilient communications
- GPS position reporting with route history logging and typical location accuracy within about 5 meters
- Alarm support including geo fencing, movement or vibration alarms, low battery, and main power cut notifications
- Internal backup battery for short term reporting after external power loss
- Reports location, status, and alerts to Plaspy for live map display and notifications

## Core Features of GOTOP - G08-4G

- OBD II plug in form factor for fast, non intrusive installation
- 4G LTE communications with automatic fallback to 3G and 2G as needed
- GPS location reporting and trip route history
- Geo fence support for zone entry and exit detection
- Movement and vibration alarm reporting for tamper and theft awareness
- Low battery and main power cut notifications to detect power loss or disconnection
- Internal backup battery to provide short term tracking when external power is interrupted
- Both data reporting and SMS reporting modes for flexible delivery

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the G08-4G transmits location, status, or alarm messages, Plaspy receives those updates and incorporates them into live tracking, alerting, and historical reporting. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and maps incoming events into platform workflows so teams can monitor vehicles without manual protocol selection.

- Real time map updates show device location and movement as reported by the tracker
- Geo fence events and movement alarms generate alerts and can be routed to dashboards and notifications
- Low battery and main power cut notifications appear in Plaspy to support tamper detection processes
- Route history and trip logs are stored and viewable in Plaspy for replay and analytics
- Short term reporting from the device backup battery lets Plaspy retain location continuity after power loss
- OBD derived telemetry that the vehicle exposes can be forwarded into Plaspy dashboards when available

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft monitoring using movement, vibration, and power cut alerts
- Centralized fleet tracking and route history for operations and dispatch
- Rental and shared vehicle monitoring where rapid deployment and removal are required
- Last mile delivery and service vehicle oversight with geo fences for service zones
- Personal vehicle tracking that benefits from plug and play OBD installation

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific features and menu options can differ by firmware version and manufacturer firmware updates
- Hardware revisions and regional cellular variants may affect supported cellular bands and fallback behavior
- Some vehicle level telemetry depends on what the vehicle exposes via the OBD port and may vary by make and model
- SMS reporting and data reporting behavior can be influenced by SIM service and regional network conditions
- Always verify device behavior and capabilities against the official GOTOP documentation for your model and firmware

Pairing the GOTOP G08-4G with Plaspy gives organizations quick deployment of vehicle tracking combined with a platform for monitoring, alerting, and historical analysis. The plug and play OBD form factor reduces installation complexity while Plaspy provides a centralized view of location, alarms, and trip history that operations teams can act on.
