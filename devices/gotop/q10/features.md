# GOTOP - Q10 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the GOTOP Q10 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the Q10's practical capabilities, how those capabilities are reflected in Plaspy, and the common scenarios where the device can add value for personal safety and small asset tracking. Content here is meant for general informational and SEO purposes and to help you understand how the Q10 functions when reporting into Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional product variant, and how the manufacturer implements specific functions. For device configuration details, firmware notes, or the most current technical specifications consult the official GOTOP documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP Q10 is an ultra-compact personal GPS tracker designed around emergency response and simple continuous reporting. It combines an easy to use SOS function, waterproof construction, and multiple reporting modes so the device can provide immediate alerts and regular telemetry to a monitoring platform such as Plaspy.

- Dedicated SOS button for rapid emergency alerts and coordinate delivery to authorized contacts.
- GPRS real time reporting to a server or platform for live maps, historical playback, and telemetry.
- SMS coordinate replies with clickable Google Maps links and direct location query support from authorized phone numbers.
- Voice surveillance and two way calling for remote listening and direct communication when needed.
- Motion sensing power saving and long standby capability for extended on body or carried use.
- Compact waterproof enclosure with an included holder for easy carry or attachment.

## Core Features of GOTOP - Q10

- SOS emergency button that triggers immediate alerts and location sharing.
- GPRS based reporting for continuous location streaming into platforms like Plaspy.
- SMS coordinate replies and direct location query support for out of band checks.
- Voice surveillance and two way calling to verify situations or check in remotely.
- Motion sensing power saving to extend standby life during periods of inactivity.
- Bluetooth connectivity to pair local accessories or enable nearby device interactions.
- Geo fence alarms to notify on entry or exit of predefined areas.
- Ultra compact waterproof design with a small form factor and accessory holder.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Q10's reporting modes provide visibility and incident context on live maps and in historical reports. Plaspy can combine the device's periodic location updates and event signals into dashboards, alerts, and playback for operational oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry appear on Plaspy live maps for monitoring and dispatch.
- SOS events and emergency coordinates can be surfaced in Plaspy alarm workflows for rapid response.
- Geo fence notifications generated by the device are tracked and presented in Plaspy for perimeter monitoring.
- SMS and direct call location replies serve as out of band checks that complement GPRS reporting when data connectivity is limited.
- Voice monitoring and two way call events can be managed alongside positional data to provide situational awareness.
- Historical playback of Q10 reports in Plaspy helps reconstruct movement and event timelines.
