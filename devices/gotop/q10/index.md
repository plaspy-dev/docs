# GOTOP - Q10

![Q10](./tracker.jpg)

The GOTOP Q10 is an ultra compact SOS GPS tracker designed for dependable personal safety and simple real time location tracking. Built around a prominent SOS button and a waterproof enclosure, the Q10 is supplied with a holder and is sized for unobtrusive everyday carry. It is commonly used to protect children, the elderly, lone workers, pets and other users who need an easy to carry, easy to use safety device.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the Q10 can feed position and event data into Plaspy for live mapping, alerts and historical playback. The device supports SMS coordinate replies with clickable map links, direct location queries from authorized numbers, and continuous GPRS reporting to a server or platform such as Plaspy, making it a practical addition to deployments focused on personal security and small asset tracking.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking and server reporting via GPRS.
- Dedicated SOS button for immediate emergency alerts to authorized contacts.
- Multi mode reporting including SMS coordinate replies with Google Maps links and continuous data reporting.
- Voice surveillance and two way calling for remote check ins and situation verification.
- Waterproof enclosure and ultra compact form factor for discreet carry and wearability.
- Motion sensing power saving and up to 96 hours standby for extended coverage.
- Bluetooth connectivity for local accessory or sensor pairing where supported.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Q10 integrates with Plaspy by sending location and status updates to your Plaspy instance using its data reporting mode. Reporting intervals can be adjusted so the device provides periodic updates for live monitoring and historical records. When cellular data is not available, SMS and direct call features provide out of band location checks and SOS notifications to authorized numbers while key events and alerts remain available to Plaspy when connectivity is restored.

- Real time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy for live map visualization and playback.
- SOS alerts and emergency coordinates forwarded to authorized contacts and registered in Plaspy alert workflows.
- Geo fence notifications available when the device enters or exits predefined areas, visible in Plaspy.
- Low battery and status alerts forwarded to Plaspy and designated contacts to reduce downtime.
- Voice monitoring and two way calling accessible as event driven communications alongside Plaspy tracking.
- Local Bluetooth data from paired accessories can be associated with device records where pairing is used.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child and elderly protection using SOS, voice monitoring and location queries.
- Lone worker safety with check in calls, geo fence alerts and emergency reporting.
- Pet tracking for lightweight waterproof monitoring and periodic location updates.
- Personal security and personnel management with on demand queries and continuous tracking via Plaspy.
- Small asset protection where compact size and reliable position reporting are required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Q10 is aimed at personal safety and small asset monitoring rather than vehicle level telemetry. Its compact, waterproof design, SOS capability and flexible reporting modes make it well suited to caregivers, security teams and organizations that need clear position visibility and rapid alerting. When paired with Plaspy, Q10 events appear on live maps and in reports, supporting quicker incident response and consistent historical tracking without introducing unnecessary complexity.
