# GOTOP - GV3

![GV3](./tracker.png)

The GV3 is a compact 4G car GPS tracker designed for reliable vehicle and fleet monitoring. Intended for automotive use, the device provides real time tracking over 4G with 2G fallback, geo fencing, movement alerts, SOS emergency notifications, and remote immobilizer support. Built for harsh conditions, the GV3 carries an IP67 rating and supports a wide operating voltage range suitable for cars, trucks, motorcycles, and mixed fleets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GV3 integrates its location and status telemetry into Plaspy for centralized fleet management and anti theft response. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker supplies the platform with positioning data, alarm events, and input states that can be used for mapping, alerting, reporting, and operational oversight across a fleet.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for straightforward integration into real time tracking dashboards and workflows
- Dual connectivity with 4G and 2G fallback to maintain location updates in varied coverage areas
- Rugged IP67 design and wide 9–95V operating range for use across cars, trucks, and motorcycles
- High precision GNSS positioning with typical accuracy within about 5 meters for route replay and recovery
- Vehicle oriented inputs including ACC ignition detection, SOS emergency button, vibration and movement alarms
- Remote engine cut immobilizer support to aid anti theft workflows when managed through Plaspy
- Internal backup battery to preserve last known location and alerts briefly after main power loss

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GV3 sends its GPS position and device status to Plaspy so fleet managers can view live locations, receive alarms, and analyze historical movement. Plaspy ingests the telemetry and makes it available in maps, alerting rules, and reports to support operational decision making and security responses.

- Live location updates and telemetry visible in Plaspy dashboards for continuous fleet visibility
- ACC and ignition state reported to Plaspy to help distinguish engine on and engine off events
- Geo fencing, movement, and vibration alarms pushed into Plaspy for instant notification and escalation
- Low battery and main power cut alerts trigger maintenance or recovery workflows in Plaspy
- SOS button events forwarded to Plaspy for rapid incident handling and dispatcher notification
- Remote immobilizer actions coordinated from Plaspy when authorized to support vehicle recovery

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking for route monitoring, dispatching, and utilization reporting
- Anti theft protection with vibration alarms, location reporting, and remote immobilizer control
- Driver safety and emergency response using SOS alerts and immediate notification to dispatch
- Mixed fleet deployments where a wide voltage tolerance allows one tracker for multiple vehicle classes
- Long term position history and geo fence enforcement for asset oversight and compliance

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GV3 pairs practical automotive tracking features with durability and flexible power support, making it a solid choice for organizations that need dependable position data and timely alerts. When used with Plaspy, the tracker’s telemetry becomes part of a centralized fleet management workflow that supports mapping, automated notifications, historical reports, and intervention options such as immobilization.
