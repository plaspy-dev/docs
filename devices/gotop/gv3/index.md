# GOTOP - GV3

![GV3](./tracker.png)

The GV3 4G Car GPS Tracker is a compact, Plaspy compatible tracking device engineered for reliable vehicle and fleet monitoring. Designed for automotive use, the GV3 delivers real-time tracking over 4G and 2G networks, geo-fencing, movement alerts, and emergency notifications — all integrated seamlessly with the Plaspy platform for centralized fleet management and anti-theft response.

With a rugged IP67 rating and a wide 9–95V operating range, the GV3 is suitable for cars, trucks, motorcycles and mixed fleets. It combines high-precision positioning \(typical GPS accuracy within 5 meters\) with practical inputs such as ACC/ignition detection, SOS, and remote engine cut \(immobilizer\), making it a strong choice for operators who need dependable telemetry, driver safety monitoring, and quick immobilization options through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for easy integration into real-time tracking dashboards and fleet management workflows.
- Dual-mode connectivity: 4G LTE for high-frequency updates and fallback to 2G where required.
- Compact, IP67-rated design that withstands harsh vehicle environments and offers wide voltage support \(9–95V\).
- Built-in GPS/GSM antennas and accurate GNSS positioning \(≈5 m\) for precise route replay and recovery.
- Critical alarms and telemetry: ACC/ignition detection, vibration and movement alarms, SOS emergency button, low battery and main power cut alerts.
- Remote engine cut \(immobilizer\) support for anti-theft response and secure vehicle recovery when combined with Plaspy controls.
- Internal backup battery \(3.7V 60 mAh\) for short-term operation after power loss to preserve last-known location and alerting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GV3 transmits GPS location and status messages over 4G \(with 2G fallback\) to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry in real time for mapping, alerts, historical reports, and fleet analytics. Integration is straightforward: once the GV3 is registered in Plaspy, location points, alarms, and input states flow into Plaspy dashboards, enabling automated alerting, geofence enforcement, and operational reporting.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via 4G LTE \(with 2G fallback\) for continuous tracking.
- ACC/ignition detection reported to Plaspy for engine-on/engine-off events and driver behavior analysis.
- Geo-fencing, movement and vibration alarms pushed to Plaspy for instant incident notifications.
- Low battery and main power cut alerts sent to Plaspy to trigger recovery or maintenance workflows.
- Remote immobilizer \(engine cut\) supported for anti-theft interventions when authorized through Plaspy controls.
- SOS emergency button events forwarded to Plaspy for rapid response and dispatching.
