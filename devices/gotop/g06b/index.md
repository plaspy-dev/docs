# GOTOP - G06B

![G06B](./logo..png)

The GOTOP G06B is a 4G car GPS tracker designed for vehicle locating and tracking. With its compact size and waterproof IP67 rating, it is suitable for use in various weather conditions. This GPS tracker offers a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle.

One of the standout features of the G06B is its support for 4G LTE connectivity, allowing for real-time tracking and monitoring. You can track the location of your vehicle via SMS or through the 4G network. The tracker also features ACC detection, which enables you to monitor the status of your vehicle's ignition. In case of an emergency, the SOS button can be pressed to send an alert for immediate assistance. Additionally, the G06B allows for remote engine cut-off, providing an extra layer of security.

The G06B also offers a range of alarm features to keep you informed of any unusual activities. It includes geo-fencing alarm, low battery alarm, vibration alarm, movement alarm, and main power cut alarm. These alarms help you stay updated on the status of your vehicle and take necessary actions when needed.

With its reliable GPS chip and 4G connectivity, the GOTOP G06B provides accurate and real-time tracking for your vehicle. Its compact size and waterproof design make it suitable for various applications. Whether you want to track your personal car or monitor a fleet of vehicles, the G06B offers the features and functionality you need for efficient and effective tracking.

### Key Features:

- 4G LTE connectivity for real-time tracking
- ACC detection to monitor vehicle ignition
- SOS button for emergency assistance
- Remote engine cut-off for added security
- Geo-fencing alarm to define virtual boundaries
- Low battery, vibration, movement, and main power cut alarms
- Compact size and waterproof IP67 rating

### Technical Specifications:

- Frequency bands: LTE-FDD \(B1/B3/B5/B7/B8/B20\), LTE-TDD \(B38/B40/B41\), WCDMA \(B1/B5/B8\), GSM \(B3/B8\)
- Size: 80 x 38 x 17 mm
- Weight: 80g
- Inbuilt GPS/GSM antenna
- Inbuilt backup battery: 3.7V 80mAh
- GPS chip: AT6558D
- 4G Chip: EC21AUFA-512-STD
- Network: 4G/3G/2G
- GPS accuracy: within 5m
