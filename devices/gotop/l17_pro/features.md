# GOTOP - L17 PRO Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP L17 PRO GPS smartwatch and explains how its capabilities can be used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, user-facing functions you can expect when the L17 PRO is registered and managed in Plaspy, without diving into private configuration details.

Exact feature availability on any individual L17 PRO can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and installation choices. For the most up to date device specifics, consult the manufacturer's documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The L17 PRO is a compact GPS smartwatch designed for personal tracking, health telemetry and emergency assistance. When paired with Plaspy the watch streams location and selected telemetry so administrators and caregivers can monitor status and respond to incidents through Plaspy dashboards and notifications.

- Real-time GPS tracking combined with BLE and Wi‑Fi assisted positioning to improve indoor accuracy and continuity.
- Health telemetry including heart rate, SpO2 and temperature available as monitored data streams.
- Dedicated SOS button and two-way calling for immediate alerting and voice communication.
- Global cellular roaming on 2G 3G and 4G with nano SIM and eSIM compatibility for international use.
- Durable daily-wear design with water resistance and multi-day battery life for continuous use.

## Core Features of GOTOP - L17 PRO

- Plaspy compatible live location streaming and telemetry reporting for centralized monitoring.
- Health sensors: PPG heart rate, SpO2 and onboard temperature measurement for basic vital-signs visibility.
- Dedicated SOS button plus two-way call support to escalate incidents and permit direct voice contact.
- BLE 5.0 and Wi‑Fi support to enhance indoor positioning and enable proximity based detection.
- Multi-network cellular roaming across 2G 3G and 4G for broad coverage in many regions.
- Compact, wearable design with IP66 water resistance and multi-day battery performance.
- On-device accelerometer and activity tracking to provide movement and step related telemetry.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an L17 PRO is registered in Plaspy the device reports its location and supported telemetry so that teams can monitor status and respond from the Plaspy platform. Plaspy consolidates feeds into maps, timelines and configurable alerts for operational visibility.

- Live location and periodic position updates appear in Plaspy maps and playback timelines.
- SOS button and alarm events are routed to Plaspy for immediate notification rules and response workflows.
- Health metrics such as heart rate SpO2 and temperature are presented as telemetry streams for monitoring and incident review.
- BLE and Wi‑Fi assisted positioning help improve indoor location visibility inside Plaspy dashboards.
- Geofence breach, low battery and other alarm notifications use the watch's onboard alerts and are surfaced by Plaspy.
- Short term device history storage is available for recent playback inside Plaspy dashboards for troubleshooting and auditing.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elder care monitoring with location awareness, vital-signs visibility and rapid SOS escalation.
- Child safety and parental tracking using geofence alerts two-way calling and real-time location.
- Lone worker protection where position updates health telemetry and an SOS control are required.
- International personal tracking for travelers who need roaming connectivity and remote monitoring.
- Everyday wellness monitoring to consolidate activity logs and basic health data into Plaspy views.
- Anti-theft and recovery scenarios where remote location and alarm events support response.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features require specific firmware builds or hardware revisions; firmware level can affect available telemetry and behavior.
- Cellular band support and roaming depend on regional variants and local network operator availability.
- BLE and Wi‑Fi based indoor positioning performance depends on local infrastructure such as beacons and access point density.
- Two-way calling and emergency voice functions require active cellular service and appropriate local network support.
- Plaspy visibility of certain telemetry streams can depend on device settings and the permissions configured during device registration.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the GOTOP L17 PRO with Plaspy gives organizations and caregivers a single place to view location, health telemetry and alarm events from wearable personal trackers. Plaspy centralizes device feeds into map views timelines and alerting workflows so teams can scale monitoring across many users and respond consistently to incidents.
