# GOTOP - L17 PRO

<div className="device-logo">
![L17 PRO](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The L17 PRO is a compact GPS tracker packaged as a smartwatch designed for personal tracking, health telemetry and emergency assistance. Built for daily wear, it combines global cellular roaming, Wi‑Fi and BLE connectivity with integrated sensors to provide continuous location updates, activity logging and on demand vital sign readings. The device includes a dedicated SOS button and two way calling to support rapid communication in urgent situations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the L17 PRO streams location and telemetry to Plaspy for visualization, alerts and reporting. That compatibility makes the watch useful for families, care providers and organizations that need centralized monitoring of location, activity and basic health signals via Plaspy mobile and web dashboards. Plaspy consolidates the device data into configurable views and notifications to support faster response and better operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking and remote monitoring through Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- Global cellular roaming plus Wi‑Fi and BLE for broad coverage and improved indoor positioning.
- Integrated health telemetry including heart rate, SpO2 and temperature alongside activity tracking.
- Dedicated SOS button and two way calling for immediate communication and emergency escalation.
- Lightweight and durable design with IP66 resistance for everyday wear and field use.
- Multi day battery life suitable for daily wear cycles and extended standby periods.
- BLE 5.0 and Wi‑Fi support to enhance indoor accuracy and nearby device detection.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the L17 PRO is registered to a Plaspy account, the watch transmits location fixes and telemetry to Plaspy’s cloud so administrators and caregivers can view live location, health metrics and event history. Plaspy receives the device streams and exposes them through dashboards, configurable alerts and historical playback to support monitoring and incident response.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered via cellular and Wi‑Fi connectivity.
- SOS alarm and two way call events routed into Plaspy notification workflows for quick response.
- Health metrics such as heart rate, SpO2 and temperature available as telemetry streams for monitoring and incidents.
- BLE assisted indoor positioning and beacon detection to improve location accuracy inside buildings.
- Geofence alerts, low battery notifications and other onboard alarms supported through Plaspy rules.
- Short term data retention for device history and playback to review recent activity and events.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elder care monitoring with location, activity and vital sign awareness and SOS escalation.
- Child safety and parental tracking with geofence alerts and two way communication.
- Lone worker protection where remote position updates and emergency alerts are required.
- International personal tracking for travelers benefiting from global roaming and eSIM compatibility.
- Everyday wellness and activity monitoring with consolidated telemetry in Plaspy dashboards.

The L17 PRO is a practical choice for organizations and families that need a wearable device which combines location tracking, basic health telemetry and direct emergency communication.
