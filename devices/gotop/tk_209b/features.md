# GOTOP - TK-209B Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GOTOP TK-209B and explains how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It is intended to help fleet managers, rental operators, logistics teams, and asset managers understand the practical functions of the device when reporting location and events into the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information here is based on the device description supplied by the manufacturer and focuses on user facing capabilities rather than device internals.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP TK-209B is designed for long term tracking in outdoor and industrial environments where long standby time and simple installation are priorities. Its combination of long battery life, waterproof housing, and magnet mounting make it suitable for discreet and durable deployments across a range of assets.

- Extremely long standby time suitable for long term deployments and infrequent maintenance
- IP67 waterproof rating for outdoor and wet environment use
- Super magnet mounting for quick installation without wiring
- Multiple alarm types for tamper and movement detection
- Quad band GSM support enabling broad regional cellular coverage
- Flexible reporting via SMS or GPRS for different connectivity workflows

## Core Features of GOTOP - TK-209B

- Long standby battery life reported up to 400 days for low maintenance tracking
- Large capacity battery included to support extended operation periods
- IP67 waterproof enclosure suitable for outdoor exposure and rain
- Super magnet design enabling non wired installation on vehicles or assets
- Alarms including dropped off, low battery, vibration, and movement alerts
- Quad band GSM support for use in multiple countries and regions
- Supports SMS or GPRS based tracking and reporting modes
- Inbuilt GPS and GSM antennas for compact installation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location and event data from compatible trackers and presents those updates in dashboards and reports. The TK-209B features translate into operational visibility and event notifications inside Plaspy, making it easier to monitor assets that need long term, robust tracking.

- Location updates appear on Plaspy maps so assets can be tracked in near real time depending on reporting configuration
- Battery status and low battery alerts from the TK-209B provide visibility into device health within Plaspy
- Alarm events such as dropped off or movement are shown as events that can trigger notifications or logging in Plaspy
- Waterproof and magnet mounting features support outdoor deployments and easy relocation while Plaspy maintains continuous tracking history
- The device’s SMS or GPRS reporting modes allow Plaspy to receive position updates in environments with differing data connectivity options

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle rental tracking where long battery life reduces maintenance touchpoints
- Fleet management for periodic or long term asset monitoring
- Container and cargo tracking for outdoor logistics operations
- Discreet asset tracking where magnet mounting is beneficial
- Long duration deployments in remote or seasonal operations
- Situations requiring simple install and remove without vehicle wiring

- Cellular coverage and network availability determine SMS or GPRS reporting reliability in different regions

Using the GOTOP TK-209B with Plaspy gives organizations an easy path to collect long term location history and event signals from assets deployed outdoors or in transit. Plaspy consolidates incoming position updates and alarms into maps, reports, and notification streams so teams can maintain operational oversight without frequent device maintenance.
