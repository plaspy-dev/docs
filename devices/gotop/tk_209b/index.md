# GOTOP - TK-209B

<div className="device-logo">
![TK-209B](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP TK-209B is a compact GPS tracker designed for long term tracking of vehicles and assets. Its standout features include an extended standby time reported up to 400 days, a high capacity 20000mAh battery, an IP67 waterproof rating, and a strong built in magnet for easy attachment without wired connections. The device supports location updates via SMS or GPRS and offers a set of alarm functions such as dropped off alarm, low battery alarm, vibration alarm, and movement alarm, making it suitable for a range of outdoor and mobile applications.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TK-209B can be integrated into a fleet or asset monitoring workflow to provide persistent location visibility and event notifications. Plaspy can ingest the tracker data to display positions on maps, manage alerts for tampering or low battery, and include the device in group reporting and operational dashboards. This combination makes the TK-209B a practical choice when long standby and ruggedness are priorities for Plaspy users.

## Key Highlights

- Extremely long standby time up to 400 days for reduced maintenance and fewer charging cycles
- IP67 waterproof rating suitable for outdoor and harsh environments
- Strong super magnet for non wired mounting and flexible placement
- Multiple alarm options including dropped off, low battery, vibration, and movement alerts
- Quad band support for use across multiple regions and basic network reporting via SMS or GPRS
- Large 20000mAh battery and built in GPS GSM antenna for extended autonomous operation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to report location updates via SMS or GPRS, the TK-209B can feed position and alarm data into Plaspy where it is processed and presented alongside other devices. Plaspy provides the tools to turn those raw updates into operational insight and ongoing oversight for fleets and assets.

- Real time and near real time location display on Plaspy maps for operational visibility
- Historical route playback and position history for incident review and audit
- Centralized alert handling in Plaspy for dropped off tamper alerts and low battery warnings
- Scheduled reports and exportable data for fleet performance and asset utilization
- Grouping and status dashboards to manage multiple TK-209B units across a fleet or asset pool

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle rental fleets that need discreet long term tracking between rentals
- Fleet management for light commercial vehicles and shared assets
- Container and cargo tracking where long battery life and waterproofing matter
- Fixed asset monitoring for remote equipment and temporary installations
- Covert or discreet asset tracking for valuables during transport or storage

The GOTOP TK-209B pairs long battery endurance and rugged protection with straightforward reporting methods, making it a strong option for operations that need low touch, long duration tracking.
