# GOTOP - TK-209B

![TK-209B](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TK-209B is a GPS tracker with an impressive battery life, making it an ideal choice for various tracking applications such as vehicle rental, fleet management, container tracking, cargo tracking, and asset tracking. With its long standby time of up to 400 days, you can rely on this tracker to provide continuous tracking without the need for frequent recharging.

One of the standout features of the TK-209B is its IP67 waterproof rating, ensuring that it can withstand harsh weather conditions and environments. This makes it suitable for outdoor use, even in rainy or wet conditions. Additionally, the tracker is equipped with a super magnet, eliminating the need for wire connections. This makes it easy to install and remove, providing flexibility and convenience.

The TK-209B also offers various alarm features to enhance security and monitoring. It includes a dropped off alarm, alerting you if the tracker is removed or tampered with. It also has low battery, vibration, and movement alarms, providing comprehensive protection for your assets. The tracker can be easily hidden and taken, allowing for discreet tracking.

With its quad-band compatibility \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\), the TK-209B can be used in multiple countries and regions. It supports tracking via SMS or GPRS, giving you the flexibility to choose the most convenient method for your tracking needs. The tracker also comes with a multi-language tracking platform, ensuring ease of use for users from different regions.

Technical Specifications:

- Inbuilt GPS/GSM antenna
- Standby time: 400 days
- IP67 waterproof
- Super magnet, no need for wire connection
- Charging voltage: 5V
- 20000mAh battery
