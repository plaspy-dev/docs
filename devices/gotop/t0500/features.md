# GOTOP - T0500 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the GOTOP T0500 when used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities, monitoring behaviors, and how telemetry and alerts from the T0500 appear in Plaspy without covering private configuration details or step by step setup. Use this as a guide to understand what the device can provide when paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability depends on the installed firmware, hardware revision, regional variant and the chosen installation method. Behavior such as alert thresholds, reporting intervals, network compatibility and accessory support can vary by manufacturer implementation and configuration, so always check device firmware notes and manufacturer resources for the version you plan to deploy.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP T0500 is a solar assisted animal GPS tracker built for long term collar deployment. It is designed to deliver reliable location updates and basic telemetry suitable for livestock and large animal monitoring while minimizing maintenance through solar recharge and a large internal battery.

- Solar assisted power with a built in rechargeable battery to extend time between maintenance visits.
- Rugged IPX7 rated enclosure suitable for outdoor and immersion conditions common in pasture deployments.
- Secure collar mounting with tamper and collar cut alerts to support theft detection workflows.
- GNSS positioning with GPS and BDS support plus LBS fallback for location continuity in poor satellite conditions.
- Motion and vibration sensing that supports activity or stationary alerts and power saving modes.
- Compatibility with Plaspy for real time tracking, history route playback and alert delivery.

## Core Features of GOTOP - T0500

- Solar assisted power system with a large internal rechargeable battery to reduce maintenance frequency.
- IPX7 waterproof rating for reliable operation in harsh outdoor environments and temporary immersion.
- Collar mounting design with locking mechanism and collar cut or tamper alert capability.
- Integrated GNSS positioning using GPS and BDS with position accuracy reported at or better than the manufacturer stated level.
- GPRS class communications for reporting location and telemetry to cloud platforms such as Plaspy, with an optional 3G variant for wider network coverage in some regions.
- Built in vibration and motion sensor to enable motion alerts and power saving strategies.
- LBS fallback positioning to provide coarse location when GNSS reception is limited.
- Support for history route playback and remote viewing via standard web and mobile interfaces when connected to a platform like Plaspy.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the T0500 is connected to Plaspy, position updates, motion and tamper alerts, and basic status messages are received by the platform so operators can monitor animals in near real time and review historical movements.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps for real time visibility of individual animals and herds.
- Collar cut and tamper alerts are delivered into Plaspy alert streams so teams can be notified and respond quickly.
- Motion and vibration events are visible in Plaspy as activity or stationary alerts to support welfare and behavior monitoring.
- Low battery and power status notifications are processed to help schedule maintenance or battery replacement visits.
- Historical route playback and exported traces enable analysis of movement patterns directly within Plaspy.
- LBS fallback and coarse location reports provide continuity of coverage in areas with limited GNSS reception.

## Typical Use Cases

- Distributed livestock monitoring across large ranches to maintain location awareness and manage grazing rotations.
- Anti theft workflows where collar cut or tamper alerts trigger rapid investigation or response.
- Long term remote deployments where solar charging reduces the need for frequent battery swaps or manual recharging.
- Pasture and route analysis using historical tracking data to improve grazing plans and resource allocation.
- Monitoring high value breeding stock or performance animals where continuous location transparency is important.
- Integration into broader operational dashboards to combine animal location with other farm telemetry and management tasks.
