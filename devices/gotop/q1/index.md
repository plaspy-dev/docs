# GOTOP - Q1

![Q1](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP Q1 Mini GPS Tracker is a compact and versatile device that offers reliable tracking capabilities. With its small size and lightweight design, it is highly portable and can be easily attached to various assets for tracking purposes. The Q1 is also equipped with a waterproof IPX7 rating, ensuring its durability and functionality even in harsh weather conditions or when exposed to water. This makes it an ideal choice for asset tracking, such as motorcycles, cars, E-bikes, and more.

One of the standout features of the Q1 is its motion alert capability. This means that it can detect any movement or motion, and send an alert notification to the user. This is particularly useful for security purposes, as it allows users to be notified immediately if there is any unauthorized movement or tampering with the tracked asset. Additionally, the Q1 supports real-time tracking and SMS location, providing accurate and up-to-date information about the location of the device.

The Q1 Mini GPS Tracker offers a range of other features to enhance its functionality and usability. It supports positioning using both GPS and LBS \(Location-Based Service\) technology, ensuring accurate and reliable tracking. Users can track the device using various methods, including SMS, web platform, and dedicated mobile apps. The Q1 also incorporates power-saving management and low battery alerts, ensuring that users are notified when the battery is running low and allowing for efficient power usage.

Technical Specifications:

- Size: 48x27x17\(mm\)
- Weight: 33g
- GSM band: 850/900/1800/1900
- GPRS: MTK
- Quad band: GSM 850/900/1800/1900 MHz
- GPS: MT2503D, 50 channel
- Rechargeable 700mAh Lithium Battery
- Built-in Vibration/motion sensor
- Antenna: Internal, GPRS Class 12
- Position Accuracy: >=5m
- Cold start: \<27s, Warm Start: \<5s, Hot Start: 1s
- A-GPS: AssistNow Online and AssistNow Offline service
- Sensitivity: Tracking: \(R\)C161 dB, Cold starts: \(R\)C148 dB, Hot starts: \(R\)C156 dB
