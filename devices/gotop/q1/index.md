# GOTOP - Q1

<div className="device-logo">
![Q1](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP Q1 Mini GPS Tracker is a compact and versatile tracking device designed for portable asset monitoring. Its small form factor and lightweight construction make it easy to attach to a variety of assets such as motorcycles, cars, and e bikes. The Q1 is rated IPX7 for waterproofing, supports GPS and LBS positioning, and includes motion alert functionality plus rechargeable power to support continuous tracking and occasional SMS reporting.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Q1 can be used to feed location and alert information into the Plaspy platform, giving operators a unified view of tracked assets. Plaspy users can leverage the Q1 for real time visibility, basic alerting, and historical position review while benefiting from centralized monitoring and reporting across mixed fleets and asset types.

## Key Highlights

- Very small and lightweight design suitable for portable asset attachment
- IPX7 waterproof rating for use in wet environments
- Motion alert and vibration detection for security related notifications
- Real time tracking with SMS location as an alternative reporting method
- Dual positioning using GPS and LBS to improve location availability
- Power management with low battery notifications to help maintain uptime

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GOTOP Q1 delivers position updates and motion alerts to the Plaspy platform where they appear on maps and in activity logs. Plaspy consolidates incoming data so teams can monitor location, respond to alerts, and analyze historical movement from the same interface.

- Live location displayed on Plaspy maps for operational visibility
- Motion alert notifications routed into Plaspy for security monitoring
- Low battery alerts surfaced in Plaspy to prompt maintenance or recharge
- Historical position records available in Plaspy for route review and incident analysis
- SMS location events can be used as a fallback input method alongside platform reporting
- Grouping and tagging in Plaspy to organize Q1 units by vehicle or asset type

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and recovery tracking for motorcycles, scooters, and bikes
- Location monitoring of rental vehicles and light transport assets
- Asset visibility for mobile equipment and tools that move between sites
- Compact tracking for personal items where discreet size is important
- Small fleet oversight where low cost and portability are priorities

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GOTOP Q1 is a practical choice for organizations that need a discreet, weather resistant tracker for individual assets or small vehicle fleets. Its motion alert capability and waterproof housing make it well suited to outdoor equipment and vehicles that may face exposure to the elements. Combined with Plaspy, the Q1 provides a simple path to centralized tracking and alert management without introducing unnecessary complexity.

For teams that prefer a compact, battery powered tracker that can also report via SMS, the Q1 offers a balanced feature set. Plaspy extends the value of the device by providing mapping, alert handling, and historical reporting so operators can keep assets visible and actionable.
