# GOTOP - Q1 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GOTOP Q1 Mini GPS Tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for asset tracking and operational visibility. It summarizes the device features that are useful for tracking and monitoring, explains how those features map into Plaspy, and helps you understand what to expect when using the Q1 with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device-specific details consult the official GOTOP documentation and your device firmware notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP Q1 is a compact, waterproof tracker designed for portable asset tracking with motion alerts, real time location reporting, and basic power management features. Its size and rugged rating make it suitable for small vehicles and portable assets where discreet, weather resistant tracking is needed.

- Compact mini form factor that is easy to mount on small assets
- Waterproof IPX7 rating for protection in wet conditions
- Motion alert reporting via a built in vibration and motion sensor
- Real time tracking plus SMS location reporting for fallback visibility
- Dual positioning using GPS and LBS to balance accuracy and coverage

## Core Features of GOTOP - Q1

- Miniature and lightweight design suitable for motorcycles, e bikes, cars, and portable assets
- Waterproof rating IPX7 to maintain operation in rain and brief submersion
- Motion and vibration alerts to notify on movement or tampering
- Real time location reporting for ongoing position updates
- SMS location capability for direct location queries via text
- GPS plus LBS positioning to provide location where GPS may be limited
- Power saving management and low battery alerts to preserve runtime
- Internal antenna and integrated cellular connectivity across quad band GSM for wide regional coverage

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives device reports from compatible trackers and presents location and event data in a unified interface. When the Q1 is configured to report into Plaspy, you can view locations, motion events, and battery alerts alongside other assets on the same platform.

- Real time positions appear on Plaspy maps for live visibility
- Motion alerts from the Q1 are recorded as events and can trigger notifications or workflows in Plaspy
- Low battery alerts are surfaced so operators can schedule maintenance or recharge cycles
- GPS versus LBS positioning is reflected in reported accuracy and helps explain differences in location precision
- SMS location can be used as a fallback when network reporting is not available, while Plaspy consolidates incoming reports when the device reconnects
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocol and accepts device reports to simplify onboarding

## Typical Use Cases

- Theft deterrence and recovery monitoring for motorcycles and scooters
- Tracking personal or commercial e bikes and small vehicles
- Monitoring portable equipment and rental assets that require discreet trackers
- Security monitoring of trailers or stored goods with motion alert notifications
- Temporary tracking assignments where a small waterproof device is preferable
- Situations where SMS location or combined GPS LBS positioning provides useful redundancy

Using the GOTOP Q1 with Plaspy gives teams a centralized way to monitor small assets and vehicles while taking advantage of the device's compact, waterproof design and motion alert capabilities. Plaspy consolidates location updates, event alerts, and battery notifications so operators can maintain situational awareness across mixed fleets and asset types.
