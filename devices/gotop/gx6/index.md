# GOTOP - GX6

![GX6](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP GX6-4G is a 4G vehicle tracking GPS tracker designed for fleet management and vehicle tracking solutions. With its precise positioning accuracy of 5 meters, this tracker ensures that you can track your vehicles with utmost precision. It utilizes a GPS+BD+LBS positioning system, providing multiple options for accurate location tracking. 

One of the standout features of the GX6-4G is its wide range of functions. It includes ACC detection, remote power/fuel off, DC detection, power off alarm, overspeed alarm, remote audio monitoring, remote door open/close, Geo-fence, and more. These features allow you to have complete control over your vehicles and ensure their safety and security. 

The GX6-4G is designed with an industrial-grade build, with a voltage input range of 9V-45V. This makes it suitable for a variety of vehicles, including rental vehicles, credit vehicles, passenger vehicles, taxis, and trucks. It also comes with a backup battery for uninterrupted tracking even in case of power failure. 

In terms of technical specifications, the GX6-4G is equipped with a ZKMicro AT6558D GPS/BD positioning module for accurate and fast positioning. It has a cold start time of an average of 32 seconds and a hot start time of an average of 1 second. The tracker uses a QUECTEL EC20 communication module, supporting various bands including FDD-LTE, TDD-LTE, WCDMA, TD-SCDMA, EVDO/CDMA2000 1x, and GSM/EDGE. It also has external ports for speaker output, mic input, I/O output, I/O input, UART-RS232, CAN, and K Cable. 

With its compact dimensions of 117mm \* 90mm \* 32mm and a weight of 255g, the GX6-4G is easy to install and doesn't take up much space. Its metal housing ensures durability and protection against harsh environments. Whether you need to track a single vehicle or manage a fleet, the GOTOP GX6-4G is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that will meet your needs.
