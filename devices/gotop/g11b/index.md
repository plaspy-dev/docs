# GOTOP - G11B

![G11B](./tracker.jpg)

The GOTOP G11B Waterproof Pet Animal Tracker is a compact, collar-mounted GPS tracker designed for reliable outdoor location and protection of pets and farm animals. Built with 4G LTE connectivity, built-in GPS and 4G antennas, and deep waterproofing \(IP65\), the G11B delivers consistent real-time tracking and alerts where it matters most. Now Plaspy compatible, the G11B integrates with Plaspy for centralized live monitoring, telemetry capture, and alert routing across small fleets of animals or mixed asset deployments.

The G11B is optimized for wireless installation on collars and outdoor harnesses, requiring no complex wiring to begin operation. With support for GOTOP web and mobile apps plus SMS location reporting \(Google Maps link\), the tracker provides flexible configuration and immediate location queries. Paired with Plaspy, you get enhanced telemetry, geofence management and history playback in a single dashboard tailored to pet tracking and animal protection workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GOTOP G11B forwards its GPS fixes, status updates and alarm events to the Plaspy platform so you can manage tracking, alerts and historical routes in one place. Plaspy collects the device telemetry and presents location, movement, and battery data in dashboards, maps and reports. SMS-based location sharing remains available as a standalone fallback for quick, on-the-spot location links.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and mapping.
- Geo-fence alarm and movement detection alarm reported to Plaspy for instant notifications.
- Low battery alerts forwarded to Plaspy so you can schedule recharges before field downtime.
- SMS location with Google Maps link for immediate sharing when mobile connectivity or app access is limited.
- History playback and route review from device logs accessible through Plaspy and GOTOP apps.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the GOTOP G11B as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker gives you a practical, purpose-built device for animal tracking combined with a powerful integration layer. The G11B’s rugged IP65 enclosure, long-life 30000 mAh battery and 4G real-time tracking reduce downtime and false negatives in outdoor environments. When routed into Plaspy, device telemetry becomes part of a broader asset and fleet management workflow — enabling centralized dashboards, alarm routing and history playback across mixed deployments.

For organizations and pet owners that need focused animal monitoring rather than full vehicle telematics, the G11B offers a simple wireless installation and easy configuration via SMS or GOTOP apps. At the same time, Plaspy’s platform supports fleet management concepts like telemetry collection, geofencing and centralized alerts. While advanced telematics features such as fuel monitoring, ignition status or immobilizer control and Bluetooth sensors are platform-level capabilities best applied to compatible devices, pairing G11B with Plaspy ensures animal location and status are visible within the same management environment when needed.
