# GOTOP - G11B

<div className="device-logo">
![G11B](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP G11B is a compact collar mounted GPS tracker designed to provide reliable outdoor location and protection for pets and farm animals. Built for rugged use, it includes 4G LTE connectivity, built in GPS and 4G antennas, and IP65 waterproofing to support consistent real time tracking and alerting in wet conditions. The device is optimized for wireless mounting on collars and harnesses and offers SMS location reporting alongside GOTOP web and mobile platform features.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G11B can forward location fixes, status updates and alarm events into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and management. This compatibility makes it straightforward to include animal trackers in broader asset oversight, enabling live maps, telemetry capture, geofence management and history playback for small fleets of animals or mixed deployments managed from the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that brings animal location into a unified monitoring platform
- 4G LTE connectivity with SMS location links for immediate sharing and fallback reporting
- Rugged collar mounted form factor with IP65 waterproof protection for outdoor use
- Long life 30000 mAh battery capacity and power saving modes to extend field operation
- Built in GPS and 4G antennas for stable positioning and communication without external antennas
- Geo fence and movement detection alarms plus low battery alerts to speed response times

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GOTOP G11B sends its GPS fixes and alarm events to the Plaspy platform so operators can manage tracking, alerts and historical routes from a single dashboard. Plaspy collects and visualizes device telemetry and presents location, movement and battery information in maps, dashboards and reports to support operational oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear in Plaspy for live tracking and mapping
- Geo fence and movement detection alarms reported to Plaspy enable instant notifications
- Low battery alerts forwarded to Plaspy help schedule recharges and reduce downtime
- SMS location links remain available as a quick fallback for immediate location sharing
- History playback and route review available from device logs within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet tracking and recovery during walks, outings or free range time
- Farm animal monitoring to observe livestock movement across paddocks
- Outdoor animal protection for working animals and wet environment deployments
- Remote location reporting when app access is limited using SMS Google Maps links

The GOTOP G11B is a purpose built animal tracker that balances ruggedness and long run time with simple wireless installation.
