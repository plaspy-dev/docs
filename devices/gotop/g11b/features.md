# GOTOP - G11B Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the GOTOP G11B Waterproof Pet Animal Tracker and explains how its capabilities map into the Plaspy platform. It focuses on non sensitive, user facing information about location reporting, alerts, and practical deployment for pets and farm animals when used with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Always consider those variables when planning deployments and refer to manufacturer resources for device specific details and the latest firmware notes.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G11B is a compact, collar mounted GPS tracker designed for outdoor animal monitoring and recovery, now Plaspy compatible for centralized monitoring and alerting. It emphasizes simple wireless installation, robust weather protection, and multiple reporting paths including real time 4G updates and SMS location links.

- Real time 4G LTE location reporting for on demand tracking and continuous monitoring.
- Collar mounted, waterproof IP65 design intended for pets and outdoor animals.
- Built in GPS and 4G antennas for stable positioning and communications without external antennas.
- Long life 30000 mAh battery with power saving sleep mode to extend field operation.
- Geo fence and movement detection alarms plus low battery alerts to support timely responses.
- Wireless installation and SMS location with Google Maps link for quick sharing or fallback.

## Core Features of GOTOP - G11B

- 4G LTE connectivity for live position updates and remote location queries.
- Built in GPS antenna for GNSS position fixes suitable for outdoor tracking.
- IP65 waterproof rating for protection against rain and wet conditions.
- 30000 mAh battery capacity and configurable power saving sleep mode for extended uptime.
- SMS location reporting that can include a Google Maps link for easy sharing.
- Geo fence alarm and movement detection alarm capabilities for event based alerts.
- Low battery notifications to help avoid unexpected downtime in the field.
- Wireless, collar mounted form factor for tool free installation on animals and harnesses.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When linked to Plaspy, the G11B forwards its location fixes, status updates, and alarm events so you can manage animal tracking and alerting alongside other assets. Plaspy aggregates device telemetry and presents it in maps, dashboards, and history views tailored to operational needs.

- Live location and telemetry shown on Plaspy maps for situational awareness and tracking.
- Geo fence and movement detection events routed into Plaspy for notification and response workflows.
- Low battery alerts reflected in Plaspy so teams can schedule charging or replacement proactively.
- History playback and route review available in Plaspy using stored device logs for investigations and auditing.
- SMS location capability remains a useful fallback for quick sharing outside the platform when needed.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet tracking and recovery during walks, off leash time, or neighborhood roaming.
- Livestock location monitoring across paddocks with movement and fence alerts.
- Protection of working animals and outdoor companions in wet conditions.
- Mixed deployments where animal trackers and other asset types are managed from a single dashboard.
- Remote checks using SMS location links when immediate app access is not available.
