# GOTOP - G07E

![G07E](./tracker.jpg)

The G07E 4G Solar Magnet GPS tracker is a Plaspy compatible solution designed for low‑maintenance, long‑term asset monitoring. Built around an integrated solar panel and a high‑capacity rechargeable, removable battery, the G07E sustains continuous operation in outdoor environments and pairs with Plaspy for real‑time tracking, alerting and fleet management workflows. Rugged IP67/IPX7 waterproofing and a powerful integrated magnet make it simple to attach to containers, trailers, heavy equipment and other metal chassis where sustained, autonomous tracking is essential.

The G07E supports real‑time GPRS tracking, SMS location reporting \(including a Google Maps link\), and Wi‑Fi assisted positioning for improved indoor accuracy — all of which Plaspy ingests to provide live maps, history, telemetry and alerting. Its hybrid positioning \(A‑GPS, Wi‑Fi, GSM\) plus offline logging to microSD \(up to 32 GB\) ensures position continuity in remote or intermittent coverage areas, making this GPS tracker a dependable component of anti‑theft, telemetry and fleet optimization programs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G solar magnet GPS tracker for long‑term, low‑maintenance asset tracking.
- Integrated solar panel and removable high‑capacity rechargeable battery for continuous operation.
- Rugged IP67/IPX7 waterproof enclosure and strong integrated magnet for secure, outdoor mounting.
- Hybrid positioning: A‑GPS \(AssistNow\), Wi‑Fi assisted positioning and GSM/GPRS for improved indoor/outdoor accuracy.
- Real‑time tracking via GPRS, SMS location links \(Google Maps\), and web/mobile platform access.
- Offline logger with up to 32 GB microSD storage, drop‑trigger alarm, SOS alarm and motion detection.
- Precision "Radar Find Me" function for close‑range indoor location and quick recovery.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated into Plaspy, the G07E supplies continuous location and event data that Plaspy uses for mapping, reporting and alert automation. The tracker transmits position updates over GPRS and can send SMS location links; Wi‑Fi assisted positioning enhances indoor accuracy and offline logging preserves location history when coverage drops. Plaspy processes these inputs to deliver live tracking, geofence alerts, historical playback and fleet‑level telemetry dashboards.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates via GPRS \(and SMS location links when required\).
- Motion detection, drop‑trigger alarms and SOS alarm events forwarded to Plaspy for instant alerts.
- Geofence violation notifications and historical route playback using onboard logging and microSD exports.
- Battery and power status including low‑battery alerts; solar charging status can be monitored for maintenance planning.
- Radar "Find Me" and Wi‑Fi assisted positioning for precision indoor/close‑range recovery when GPS is weak.
