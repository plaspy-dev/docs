# GOTOP - G07E Features

This page provides a public, features‑oriented overview of the GOTOP G07E and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non‑sensitive information about the tracker’s functions, rugged design, and the types of location and event data Plaspy ingests for mapping, alerts, history, and fleet oversight.

Exact feature availability depends on the device firmware, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, this page highlights common behaviors and practical uses, but you should verify device specifics and the latest firmware features with the manufacturer for deployment planning.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G07E is a solar powered magnetic tracker built for long‑term outdoor asset monitoring. It combines hybrid positioning, robust mounting, and offline logging to keep position continuity for metal chassis assets such as containers, trailers and heavy equipment, while reporting events and locations to Plaspy for live tracking and alerting.

- Integrated solar panel plus a removable rechargeable battery for extended, low maintenance operation.
- Strong integrated magnet and waterproof IP67/IPX7 enclosure for secure outdoor mounting on metal assets.
- Hybrid positioning using A‑GPS, Wi‑Fi assisted positioning and GSM/GPRS to improve indoor and outdoor accuracy.
- Real‑time reporting via GPRS plus SMS location links that include map links for quick recovery.
- Offline logging to microSD up to 32 GB to preserve history when coverage is intermittent.
- Onboard event detection like motion, drop‑trigger and SOS alarms to support alert workflows.

## Core Features of GOTOP - G07E

- Solar charging with a removable high‑capacity battery designed for long deployments.
- Rugged magnetic housing rated to IP67/IPX7 for water and dust resistance.
- Hybrid GNSS positioning with A‑GPS assist and Wi‑Fi assisted positioning for improved indoor performance.
- Real‑time GPRS tracking and SMS location reporting that Plaspy can ingest for live updates.
- Offline logger supporting microSD cards up to 32 GB to retain location history during signal gaps.
- Event reporting including motion detection, drop‑trigger alarm and SOS alarm.
- Precision short‑range recovery feature marketed as Radar Find Me for close‑range locating tasks.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a G07E is connected to Plaspy, location fixes and event messages are processed into map views, timelines and alert rules that teams can use for operational decisions. Plaspy receives hybrid positioning inputs and event telemetry to maintain visibility even when a device temporarily loses satellite lock.

- Live map tracking and historical route playback using GPRS position updates and offline log imports.
- Alerting for motion events, drop triggers and SOS alarms so operations teams receive timely notifications.
- Geofence notification and event history for recovery and compliance workflows.
- Battery and power status reporting to plan maintenance cycles and monitor solar charging effectiveness.
- Use of Wi‑Fi assisted positioning and Radar Find Me information in Plaspy to improve short‑range and indoor recovery.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking where solar charging reduces the need for manual battery replacement.
- Heavy equipment and plant machinery monitoring for assets parked outdoors or left in the field.
- Tracking of non‑powered assets across long durations with intermittent cellular coverage.
- Anti‑theft and recovery workflows that rely on motion, SOS and short‑range recovery features.
- Fleet and asset utilization reporting where historical logs and live updates inform scheduling and logistics.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware differences can change available functions and event formats; verify firmware version behavior before large deployments.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect supported cellular bands and charging performance.
- Installation matters: magnetic mounting is effective on metal chassis but is not suitable for non‑metal surfaces without additional hardware.
- microSD logging depends on whether a card is installed and on any configured logging policies.
- Manufacturer documentation should be consulted for precise alarm thresholds, timing behavior and configuration details.
