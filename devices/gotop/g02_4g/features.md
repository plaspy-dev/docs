# GOTOP - G02-4G Features

This page provides public feature context for using the GOTOP G02-4G tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users and explains how the tracker’s core functions appear in Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device specific details verify manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G02-4G is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, e-bikes, and cars. It is Plaspy compatible out of the box and provides multi network connectivity, positioning accuracy suitable for vehicle tracking, and a set of event alarms that support anti theft and fleet workflows.

- Real time tracking over 4G LTE with fallback to 3G and 2G plus optional SMS reporting for redundancy.
- IP67 rated enclosure and compact form factor suited to outdoor use and small vehicles.
- Wide operating voltage range of 9–95V to simplify installation across diverse vehicle types.
- Accurate GNSS positioning with reported accuracy within approximately 5 meters for reliable location data.
- Event alerts including ACC ignition detection, geo fencing, vibration and movement alarms, low battery and main power cut.
- Small dimensions and light weight for discreet installations on motorcycles and e bikes.

## Core Features of GOTOP - G02-4G

- 4G LTE primary connectivity with 3G and 2G fallback and SMS reporting as a secondary channel.
- AT6558D GNSS chipset providing positioning accuracy within about 5 meters.
- Vehicle grade wide voltage support for 9–95V installations.
- IP67 rated waterproof and dustproof enclosure for outdoor and on vehicle use.
- Built in alarms for ACC ignition detection, geo fence triggers, vibration and movement alerts.
- Low battery and main power cut alarms for tamper and power loss detection.
- Compact size and light weight for easy mounting on motorcycles, e bikes and cars.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the G02-4G is connected to a mobile network and configured to report to Plaspy, location and event information is ingested into Plaspy for live monitoring and historical analysis. Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol and presents telemetry in dashboards, maps, and alerting workflows without requiring manual parsing of device messages.

- Real time position updates and route history visible on Plaspy maps when the device is online.
- Event alerts such as ignition state changes, geo fence entries or exits, vibration and movement alarms appear as configurable notifications in Plaspy.
- Low battery and main power cut events can be surfaced to operators to indicate possible tampering or power issues.
- SMS reporting provides a fallback channel that Plaspy can incorporate when network based reporting is unavailable.
- Device status and recent telemetry are available for replay and reporting to support investigations and fleet oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Centralized fleet tracking for mixed vehicle fleets including cars, motorcycles and e bikes.
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows using geo fence, vibration and movement alarms combined with live location.
- Micro mobility and e bike deployments where compact size and IP67 protection ease installation.
- Theft recovery and tamper detection for light vehicles using main power cut and low battery alerts.
- General asset tracking for small outdoor equipment and trailers needing rugged hardware and mobile connectivity.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Available features and exact alarm behavior depend on device firmware version and hardware revision.
- Regional cellular band support and network fallback behavior vary by model variant and market region.
- SMS reporting requires an active SIM and carrier support in the deployment region.
- Some event signals such as ACC ignition detection require a wired installation to the vehicle wiring harness.
- Manufacturer documentation should be consulted for details on firmware options, configuration commands, and installation best practices.
