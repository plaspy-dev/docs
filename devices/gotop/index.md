## How GOTOP devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy connects to GOTOP devices using standard GPS tracking protocols supported by the devices. Once a GOTOP device is configured to send location and status data to Plaspy, the platform consolidates that data for real time tracking, historical playback, geofencing, alerts, and reporting.

Typical steps for using a GOTOP device with Plaspy:
- Verify the device can send data to a third party tracking platform.
- Configure device reporting parameters such as APN or server settings according to the device guide.
- Claim and manage the device in Plaspy to view live locations and telemetry.
