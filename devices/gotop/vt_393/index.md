# GOTOP - VT-393

![VT-393](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-393 is a GPS tracker designed for fleet management and vehicle security. It combines a high performance ARM9 processor with features aimed at reliable location reporting and activity logging. Notable capabilities in the VT-393 include camera support for capturing photos with location and driving information, ample storage with an SD card slot, and a range of inputs and outputs for connecting vehicle signals and accessories.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the VT-393 can be integrated into a fleet tracking workflow to provide location visibility, event alerts, and media capture where supported. Plaspy users can leverage the VT-393 to bring vehicle location, alerting, and logged images into a unified platform for monitoring and reporting without relying on speculative technical details.

## Key Highlights

- Camera support that ties images to location and driving information for visual documentation
- ARM9 based design for consistent performance and reliable operation in fleet use
- Generous local storage with support for large SD cards to retain logs and pictures
- Multiple analog and digital inputs for monitoring fuel related signals and other vehicle data
- Built in support for bidirectional communication to enable two way interaction between manager and driver
- Over the air update capability via GPRS to keep device software current where available

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT-393 can feed location, event and media information into Plaspy to enhance fleet visibility and operational oversight. Once the tracker is configured to communicate with Plaspy, its location updates, alerts, and recorded images can be presented alongside other fleet assets for consolidated monitoring.

- Vehicle location and movement visible on Plaspy maps for real time and historical review
- Alerts such as SOS, geo fence, over speed, harsh braking and accident notifications routed into Plaspy for immediate attention
- Image capture associated with events or time ranges available within Plaspy as supporting media for incidents
- Mileage reporting and movement logs available in Plaspy for operational analysis and record keeping
- Remote control capabilities and digital input states surfaced in Plaspy to support workflows and corrective actions

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operators documenting driver behavior and incidents with location tagged photos
- Delivery and logistics companies tracking mileage and movement for operational reporting
- Vehicle security and theft deterrence using RFID and movement alerts integrated into a monitoring platform
- Service fleets monitoring fuel related inputs and vehicle status for cost control
- Managers using two way communication and alerts to coordinate drivers and respond to events

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT-393 is a practical choice for organizations that need a balance of onboard media capture, flexible I O, and event oriented alerts. Its combination of camera support, multiple inputs, and bidirectional communication can add useful context to location data when combined with Plaspy reporting and monitoring features. For teams that rely on visual evidence or need to keep extensive local logs, the VT-393 offers storage and port options that align with those operational needs.

Because Plaspy consolidates tracking, alerts, and media into a single view, pairing the VT-393 with Plaspy helps turn raw device data into actionable insights for fleet managers. If your requirements include image documentation, configurable alerts, and a capable set of inputs and outputs, the VT-393 is a model to consider for integration with a Plaspy based tracking solution.
