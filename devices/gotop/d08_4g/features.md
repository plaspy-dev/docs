# GOTOP - D08-4G Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the GOTOP D08-4G tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the capabilities most relevant to vehicle monitoring, event reporting, and fleet oversight when the device streams position and alarm events into the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional product variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes consult the official GOTOP documentation.

## Feature Overview

The D08-4G is an OBD-mounted 4G GPS tracker built for straightforward vehicle deployment and continuous tracking into Plaspy. It combines cellular uplink, multi-mode positioning, and a set of vehicle alarms so you can monitor locations, detect common tamper or power events, and review historical routes within Plaspy.

- OBD-mounted form factor for fast installation on compatible vehicle OBD ports.
- 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain uplink in wider coverage areas.
- Multi-mode positioning that uses GPS, Beidou, Wi‑Fi and LBS to improve fixes in urban or indoor settings.
- Standard vehicle alarm set including geofence, power-failure, low battery, vibration tamper, and offline alerts.
- Built-in backup battery and wide operating voltage to maintain short-term reporting during vehicle power loss.

## Core Features of GOTOP - D08-4G

- OBD-mounted design that connects through the vehicle OBD port for rapid installation.
- Cellular connectivity using 4G LTE with 2G fallback and SIMCom 7670-series module support as provided by the manufacturer.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS and Beidou GNSS plus Wi‑Fi and LBS assisted fixes.
- Vehicle alarm events: geofence, power-failure, low battery, vibration tamper, and offline alert reporting.
- Wide input voltage support suitable for a range of vehicle electrical systems and an internal backup battery for short term operation.
- Compact ABS enclosure sized for OBD installation with manufacturer published dimensions and weight.
- Typical power consumption characteristics useful for planning fleet deployments and power budgeting.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the D08-4G is installed and active, it transmits location updates and alarm events to Plaspy for visualization, alerting, and historical analysis. Plaspy ingests the device feeds so operations teams can monitor vehicles in real time and review event timelines.

- Real-time position updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and dispatch visibility.
- Geofence events and electronic fence notifications are logged and displayed for alerting and incident review.
- Power-failure, low battery, and vibration tamper alerts are presented in Plaspy to support anti-theft monitoring and response workflows.
- Historical route playback and stored position history enable compliance reporting and route analysis inside Plaspy.
- Wi‑Fi and LBS assisted positioning can improve location visibility in urban canyons where satellite signals are degraded.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light commercial vehicles where quick OBD installation reduces deployment time.
- Anti-theft monitoring and recovery workflows using geofence, tamper, and power-failure alerts.
- Urban delivery and last mile operations that benefit from Wi‑Fi and LBS assisted fixes for better stop detection.
- Operational oversight with historical route playback for utilization and routing analysis.
- Mixed fleet rollouts where an OBD-mounted tracker simplifies installation across many vehicle types.
