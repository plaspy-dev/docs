# GOTOP - D08-4G

<div className="device-logo">
![D08-4G](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The D08-4G is an OBD mounted 4G GPS tracker designed for vehicle monitoring in both fleet and individual car scenarios. It combines cellular connectivity with multi mode positioning options including GPS, Beidou BD, Wi‑Fi and LBS to provide continuous location data in a compact form factor. Standard vehicle alarm functions such as geofence, power failure, low battery, vibration tamper and offline alerts make it suitable for security and operational monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D08-4G streams position and event data into the Plaspy platform so operations teams can use the tracker for real time visibility, alerting and historical playback. Its OBD mounted design and broad power support make it straightforward to deploy across mixed fleets, and the built in alarms feed the event workflows that Plaspy uses for anti theft response and fleet oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time position and alarm event streaming into fleet dashboards
- 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback for broad cellular coverage and stable uplink
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, Beidou BD, Wi‑Fi and LBS to improve fixes in challenging areas
- Vehicle alarms including geofence, power failure, low battery, vibration tamper and offline alerts
- Compact OBD mounted design for fast deployment across vehicles with an OBD port
- Wide input voltage support and internal backup battery to maintain short term operation during power loss

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the D08-4G is active and connected, it transmits location updates and event notifications to Plaspy so teams can monitor vehicles in real time and react to incidents. Plaspy receives position packets and alarm events, logs history for playback, and surfaces alerts to users through configurable rules.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy live maps for tracking and dispatch
- Geofence events and configurable electronic fence notifications recorded and delivered by Plaspy
- Power failure, low battery and vibration tamper alerts pushed into Plaspy for security workflows
- Historical route playback and position history available for compliance and analysis
- Wi‑Fi and LBS assisted position fixes help improve visibility in urban and indoor environments

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light commercial vehicles requiring centralized tracking and route history
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows driven by geofence and tamper alerts
- Urban last mile and delivery where Wi‑Fi and LBS assist position accuracy in dense areas
- Usage based monitoring and utilization reporting using stored route and event logs
- Rental or shared vehicle tracking to monitor location and generate activity history

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The D08-4G offers a practical combination of connectivity, multi mode positioning and vehicle friendly form factor for organizations that want a Plaspy compatible tracker without complex installation. Built in alarm functions provide the event signals Plaspy needs to trigger alerts and automate responses, while the device form factor and power support help keep deployments consistent across mixed fleets.
