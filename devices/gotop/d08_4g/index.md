# GOTOP - D08-4G

![D08-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The D08-4G is an OBD-mounted 4G GPS tracker designed for reliable, Plaspy compatible vehicle monitoring. Built for fleet management and individual car tracking, the D08-4G combines 4G LTE with 2G fallback, multi-mode positioning \(GPS, Beidou/BD, Wi‑Fi and LBS\), and a compact OBD form factor so you can deploy real-time tracking and security monitoring quickly across a vehicle fleet.

With standard vehicle alarm functions—geofence, power-failure alarm, low battery alarm, vibration \(tamper\) alarm and offline alert—the D08-4G feeds Plaspy with the location and event data needed for anti-theft workflows, historical route playback, and operational telemetry dashboards. Its wide input voltage range and internal backup battery make it suitable for diverse vehicle types while keeping installation simple via the OBD port.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: streams position and alarm events for real-time tracking and fleet management dashboards.
- 4G LTE with 2G fallback: SIMCom 7670-series module for broad cellular coverage and stable data transmission.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS, Beidou \(BD\), Wi‑Fi and LBS improve fixes in urban and indoor environments.
- Vehicle alarm set: geofence, power-failure, low battery, vibration \(tamper\) and offline alerts for anti-theft use cases.
- OBD-mounted compact design: quick installation on OBD port; compact ABS enclosure \(51 × 47 × 23 mm, 54 g\).
- Robust power support: wide DC 9V–35V operating range with 3.7V/100 mAh internal backup battery for short-term power-loss operation.
- Low typical power draw: typical current consumption values help estimate fleet power budgets \(73.6 mA @ 12V operating, 38.2 mA sleep\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

The D08-4G integrates with Plaspy to deliver continuous position updates, alarm events and historical data for both single vehicles and large fleets. Once the tracker is installed on the vehicle OBD port and the SIM is activated, the device transmits location and status packets over LTE/4G \(with 2G fallback\) to Plaspy’s platform for real-time visualization, reporting and alerting.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live map tracking.
- Geofence alarm events and configurable electronic fence notifications displayed and logged in Plaspy.
- Power-failure, low battery and vibration \(tamper\) alerts pushed to Plaspy for anti-theft monitoring and response.
- Historical route playback: position history stored and accessible in Plaspy for compliance and analysis.
- Wi‑Fi and LBS-assisted fixes improve urban and indoor tracking accuracy where satellite signals are weak.

- Fleet management: deploy across light commercial vehicles for real-time tracking, route playback and operational oversight in Plaspy.
- Anti-theft and recovery: geofence, vibration and power-failure alerts help detect tampering and support recovery workflows.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The D08-4G offers a practical balance of connectivity, multi-mode positioning and vehicle-grade form factor for teams that need a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker without complex installation. Its 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback and SIMCom 7670-series radio support reliable uplink in wide coverage areas, while built-in alarms provide the basic anti-theft and operational signals needed by fleet operations. Because it mounts to the OBD port and supports a broad input voltage range, it is fast to install across mixed fleets and keeps downtime low.

Paired with Plaspy, the D08-4G becomes part of a complete solution: real-time tracking, event-driven alerts, and historical playback are centralized in the Plaspy platform so operations teams can act on telemetry and location data quickly. For deployments that also require fuel monitoring, ignition status, immobilizer control or Bluetooth sensor integration, Plaspy can combine position feeds from the D08-4G with vehicle or sensor data where those inputs are available from the vehicle or from other compatible devices in your fleet ecosystem.
