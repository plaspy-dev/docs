# GOTOP - A5

![A5](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP A5 Car Charger GPS Tracker is a versatile and cost-effective solution for both security and real-time tracking. This device not only functions as a GPS tracker but also doubles as a car charger, making it a convenient and practical accessory for your vehicle. With its compact size and plug-and-play design, the A5 is easy to install and use without the need for any batteries.

Equipped with GPS, LBS, WIFI, AGPS, and BDS locating technologies, the A5 provides accurate and reliable tracking information. You can track the device in real-time using GRPS, SMS, web platform, or the dedicated mobile app. The A5 also offers history route tracking, allowing you to review past movements. Additionally, you can enable voice monitoring to listen in on the surroundings of the tracker.

Some outstanding features of the GOTOP A5 Car Charger GPS Tracker include:

- GPS/LBS/WIFI/AGPS/BDS locating
- Car Charger + GPS Tracker 2in1
- No battery, Plug and play
- Supports up to 32GB TF Card
- GRPS Real-time tracking
- History route tracking
- Voice Monitoring
- Tracking by SMS/Web platform/APP
- Power saving management
- Find the device
- Low battery alert/Pedometer
- Automatic Time Update
- Built-in GPS/GSM antenna
- Vibration alarm
- Voice Recorder
- Remote Power-off/Restart
- Color: Black

The GOTOP A5 Car Charger GPS Tracker is designed with a compact size of 40x25x75 mm and weighs only 30g, making it easy to install and conceal. It features a powerful chip combination of MTK2503, MTK3333, and MTK5931, ensuring reliable performance. The A5 supports quad-band GSM frequencies \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\) for wide coverage.

With its internal GPS and GSM antennas, the A5 provides accurate positioning and communication capabilities. The GPS positioning accuracy is 10-15m in open sky conditions, while the WiFi positioning accuracy is 5-50m within WiFi areas. The A5 also supports LBS positioning with an accuracy of 100-1000m. It operates in a wide temperature range from -20℃ to +70℃ and has a working humidity range of 20% to 80% RH.

Overall, the GOTOP A5 Car Charger GPS Tracker is a reliable and versatile device that combines the functionality of a car charger and a GPS tracker. Whether you need to track your vehicle's location or charge your devices on the go, the A5 offers a convenient and efficient solution.
