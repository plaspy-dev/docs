# GOTOP - VT-391

![VT-391](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-391 GPS tracker is a versatile and feature-rich device designed for fleet management and vehicle security purposes. One of its standout features is the built-in WIFI module, which allows the tracker to function as a wireless device and transmit data via WIFI. This feature enables seamless and efficient communication between the tracker and the user. Additionally, the VT-391 supports a camera, allowing for location and driving information to be logged on pictures.

Another notable feature of the VT-391 is its RFID driver identification capability. This feature ensures automatic anti-theft and driver identification, providing an extra layer of security for your vehicle. The tracker also supports a maximum 64GB SD card for storing data and pictures, allowing for extensive data logging and storage.

In terms of functionality, the VT-391 offers two-way communication, allowing for both listening and speaking. It also features four A/D connectors for multi-fuel sensor monitoring, making it ideal for fleet management applications. The tracker is equipped with harsh braking and acceleration alarms, as well as an accident alarm, providing real-time alerts for any sudden or unexpected events.

Other notable features of the VT-391 include tracking via SMS, GPRS, and WIFI, ARM9 high-speed microcontroller, OTA \(over-the-air\) function for firmware updates, remote control engine, mileage reporting, built-in motion sensor for power saving, and the ability to check the car's real physical address. The tracker also supports various alerts such as SOS alert, movement alert, geo-fence alert, and over-speed alert, ensuring comprehensive vehicle security.

The VT-391 offers extensive connectivity options with its I/O ports, including five digital inputs, five outputs, four analog inputs, one camera port, one RS232 port, and one SD card slot. This allows for seamless integration with other devices and sensors, expanding the functionality and versatility of the tracker.
