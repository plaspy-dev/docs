# GOTOP - VT-391

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-391](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP VT-391 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for fleet management and vehicle security. It combines position reporting with a built in WiFi module and optional camera support so location and driving information can be logged alongside pictures. The VT-391 also provides RFID driver identification, a large SD card slot for local storage, two way audio capability, multiple analog inputs for fuel monitoring, and a range of configured alarms for events such as harsh braking, accidents, and over speed.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the VT-391 is suitable for organizations that want integrated tracking, alerts, and recorded evidence managed from a single platform. Plaspy can receive and present the VT-391's location updates, event alerts, mileage reports, and driver ID information to provide visibility and operational oversight across a vehicle fleet while leveraging the unit's storage and camera features for additional context.

## Key Highlights

- Built in WiFi connectivity for local data transmission and flexible communications
- Support for an onboard camera to attach pictures to location and driving events
- RFID driver identification for automatic driver recognition and anti theft workflows
- Expandable local storage with support for up to 64 GB SD card for logs and images
- Multiple analog and digital inputs for fuel and external sensor monitoring
- Two way audio and a set of safety alarms including SOS, movement, geofence, and over speed
- OTA updates and mileage reporting for easier maintenance and operational records

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT-391 supplies vehicle position, events, and supporting media to the Plaspy platform where teams can monitor activity and generate reports. Plaspy consolidates the device data into maps, alerts, and historical records so fleet managers gain a unified view of vehicle status and incidents.

- Live and historical location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps for route oversight
- Event and alarm mapping such as SOS, geo fence breaches, harsh braking, and over speed alerts
- Driver identification records using RFID logs to associate trips with personnel
- Photo and local media association to provide visual context for incidents and inspections
- Mileage and trip reporting to support operational analytics and maintenance planning
- Sensor and input status reporting to monitor fuel levels and external device conditions

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet route monitoring and mileage tracking for transport and delivery operations
- Vehicle security and anti theft workflows using RFID identification and movement alerts
- Driver behavior oversight with alarms for harsh braking, acceleration, and accidents
- Visual evidence capture for incidents, inspections, or proof of delivery using the camera
- Fuel monitoring and operational telemetry using the analog inputs for consumption tracking

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT-391 combines a broad feature set with flexible connectivity and local storage, making it a practical choice for fleets that need more than basic location reporting. Its camera support and RFID driver identification offer additional layers of operational insight and security that integrate naturally into Plaspy's monitoring and reporting tools.
