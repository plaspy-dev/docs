# GOTOP - G59

<div className="device-logo">
![G59](./tracker.png)
</div>

The G59 is a compact IP67 waterproof GPS tracker designed as a collar tag for pet monitoring. Built for continuous outdoor use, it offers 4G LTE real-time tracking, SMS location messages with Google Maps links, and two-way voice interaction so owners can locate and communicate with pets during walks, outdoor adventures, or everyday activities. The device also includes pet-focused features such as sound and light locating, a large speaker for remote voice calling, pedometer and feeding reminders, geo-fence alerts, and low battery notifications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G59 can feed location fixes, status messages, and activity data into Plaspy for consolidated monitoring, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy ingests the device’s positional and status updates so owners and managers can view live location, history, and notifications in one platform while retaining the G59’s pet-specific capabilities for interaction and safety management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible pet GPS collar tag with 4G LTE real-time tracking and SMS location links to Google Maps.
- IP67 waterproof rating designed to withstand rain and temporary immersion for active pets.
- Multi-mode positioning using satellite and network-based methods to improve location reliability in diverse environments.
- Two-way voice calling plus remote monitoring and sound/light locating to help find and reassure pets.
- Pet-centric features including pedometer, feeding reminders, geo-fence alerts, and low battery notifications.
- Intelligent power management with a motion sensor to extend standby time and support frequent outdoor use.
- Compact collar-friendly design intended for continuous wear and outdoor activity.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Connecting a G59 device to Plaspy allows owners to centralize tracking, alerts, and simple activity metrics in a single dashboard. The tracker transmits location and status updates over cellular networks, and Plaspy presents that data as live location, history, and configurable alerts so users can monitor pets and respond quickly when needed.

- Real-time position updates and location history displayed in Plaspy for situational awareness.
- Geo-fence alerts and low battery warnings delivered through Plaspy notifications and optional SMS forwarding.
- Voice call and monitoring events logged in Plaspy so administrators can review device interactions and status.
- Activity metrics such as step counts and power state available in Plaspy for basic health and battery oversight.
- SMS location messages with Google Maps links offer an alternate quick-share method when app access is not available.

## Typical Use Cases

- Daily walks and urban tracking where Wi‑Fi and network fallback help locate a pet in dense areas.
- Outdoor recreation and water play where IP67 protection and multi-mode positioning maintain tracking reliability.
- Rapid recovery and anti-theft scenarios using geo-fence alerts, live location links, and two-way voice.
- Basic activity and care monitoring with pedometer counts and feeding reminders to support routine management.
- Shared access and emergency notification when SMS location links are needed for quick position sharing.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G59 is well suited for pet owners and small organizations that need consistent, easy-to-manage tracking integrated into a unified platform. Its combination of waterproof construction, multi-mode positioning, and two-way voice makes it practical for both urban and outdoor use, while Plaspy provides the tools to view live locations, maintain history, and receive alerts in one place. For users focused on pet safety and routine monitoring rather than vehicle telemetry, the G59 delivers a compact, pet-centric solution that works naturally with Plaspy.
