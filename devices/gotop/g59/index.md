# GOTOP - G59

![G59](./tracker.png)

G59 Waterproof Pet Tracker

The G59 is a compact, IP67 waterproof GPS tracker designed as a collar tag for reliable pet tracking in both urban and outdoor environments. Optimized for continuous outdoor use, the G59 provides Plaspy compatible, 4G LTE real-time tracking, SMS location messages with Google Maps links, and two-way voice interaction so owners stay connected with their pets wherever they roam.

The G59 blends robust durability with pet-focused features: sound and light locating, a large‑volume speaker for remote voice calling and monitoring, pedometer and feeding reminders, geo‑fence alerts, and low battery notifications. Built with multi-mode positioning \(GPS, BeiDou, Wi‑Fi and LBS\) and intelligent power-saving via a 3D sensor, the G59 is tailored to keep pets safe while delivering clean, compatible data to Plaspy for tracking, alerts, and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with 4G LTE real-time tracking and SMS location links for instant access to position on Google Maps.
- IP67 waterproof collar tag — resistant to rain and temporary immersion so pets can play and swim without losing tracking capability.
- Multi-mode positioning \(GPS, BeiDou, Wi‑Fi, LBS\) for improved accuracy across urban canyons and open areas.
- Two-way voice calling, remote monitoring and recording with a large speaker for direct interaction and reassurance.
- Pet-focused safety features: geo-fence alerts, sound & light locating, pedometer and feeding reminder, plus low battery notifications.
- Intelligent power management using a 3D sensor for extended standby and quick charging support for fast turnaround.
- Compact, collar-friendly form factor in black for continuous outdoor use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the G59 with Plaspy gives pet owners a seamless, Plaspy compatible experience for real-time tracking, alerting, and activity insights. The tracker sends positional fixes and status messages over 4G LTE and GSM networks; Plaspy ingests that data to display live location, history, and alerts in one dashboard. Owners can also receive SMS location messages with a Google Maps link — useful for quick sharing or when the app is not available.

- Real-time location updates and position history via GPS/BeiDou/Wi‑Fi/LBS reported to Plaspy.
- Geo-fence alerts and low battery notifications delivered as Plaspy alerts and SMS when configured.
- Remote voice calling, monitoring and recording available for live audio interaction through the device; call activity and status are visible in Plaspy logs.
- Activity telemetry such as pedometer counts and power-saving state reported to Plaspy for health and battery management.
- Remote power-off via the app or SMS to conserve battery or manage device behavior; Plaspy can reflect device online/offline status.

## Use Cases

- Daily pet walking and urban tracking — real-time tracking and Wi‑Fi/LBS support help locate pets in dense environments.
- Outdoor and adventure pets — IP67 waterproofing and BeiDou/GPS positioning keep location services working during hiking or water play.
- Anti-theft and rapid recovery — geo-fence alerts, live location links, and remote voice calling aid in quick recovery if a pet is lost or taken.
- Health and care routines — pedometer and feeding reminders provide basic telemetry for activity monitoring and routine reminders.
- Shared access and emergency notification — SMS location links allow quick sharing of a pet’s position when app access is limited.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G59 pairs pet-centric hardware with Plaspy compatibility to deliver reliable, easy-to-manage tracking for owners who want consistent real-time visibility. Its mix of multi-mode positioning, 4G LTE connectivity and waterproof construction ensures accurate location data whether in the city or outdoors. Plaspy users gain a simple integration path for alerts, history and live status, while features like two-way voice, geo-fences and low-battery warnings add practical safety layers. Although the G59 is designed specifically for pets rather than vehicle telemetry, its real-time tracking and anti-theft alerts make it a strong solution for pet protection and everyday monitoring. For customers who need specialized vehicle features such as ignition-based telemetry, immobilizer control or fuel monitoring, those systems are typically implemented on vehicle-focused devices; the G59 is optimized for pet use and companion monitoring.
