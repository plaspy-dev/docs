# GOTOP - G23D

![G23D](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP G23D is a compact and reliable CDMA GPS tracker designed for various tracking solutions, including cars, e-bikes, motorcycles, and more. With its precise positioning accuracy of 5 meters, you can track your vehicles with confidence. The tracker utilizes a GPS+BDS+LBS positioning system, ensuring accurate and reliable location information.

One of the standout features of the G23D is its wide voltage input range of 9V-95V, making it compatible with a wide range of vehicles. Whether you have a rental vehicle, credit vehicle, passenger vehicle, taxi, or truck, this tracker is suitable for your needs. It also offers a range of useful functions, including ACC detection, remote power/fuel off, DC detection, power off alarm, and overspeed alarm, providing you with comprehensive vehicle monitoring capabilities.

In terms of technical specifications, the G23D is equipped with a ZTE MC8618 module for CDMA2000 1X 800MHZ support. It features a Micro SIM card slot and an internal aerial for communication. The GPS/BD positioning is handled by a ZKMicro AT6558D module, offering quick cold starts in an average of 32 seconds and hot starts in just 1 second. The tracker also includes various external ports, including I/O channels for power/fuel off and ACC detection, as well as a UART-TTL channel for easy connectivity.

With its compact dimensions of 77mm \* 28mm \* 15mm and lightweight design of 32g, the G23D is easy to install and conceal. The housing is made of durable ABS plastic, ensuring long-lasting performance even in challenging environments. Whether you're a fleet manager, a vehicle owner, or simply want to keep track of your personal vehicles, the GOTOP G23D CDMA GPS tracker is a reliable and feature-packed solution.
