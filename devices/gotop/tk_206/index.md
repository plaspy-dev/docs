# TK-206 GPS Tracker

The TK-206 is a compact GSM/GPRS/GPS car tracker built for dependable, discreet vehicle tracking and recovery. Designed as one of the smallest high-performance car trackers, the TK-206 installs easily and hides unobtrusively in the vehicle. Plaspy compatible out of the box, it delivers real-time tracking, geofence alerts, and remote immobilization so fleet managers and vehicle owners can monitor location and respond quickly to theft or safety events.

The TK-206 supports SMS and GPRS reporting plus dedicated Android and iOS apps and web-based tracking platforms, making it a flexible choice for telemetry, fleet management, and anti-theft workflows. With relay-driven engine cut \(immobilizer\), SOS emergency button, voice monitoring, and a suite of alarms \(vibration, low battery, power cut\), the TK-206 gives practical, proven capabilities that integrate smoothly with Plaspy for live location, history playback, and actionable alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Small, discreet form factor \(95 × 55 × 15 mm\) for hidden mounting and theft resilience.
- Plaspy compatible: real-time tracking over GPRS and SMS with mobile app and web platform support.
- Comprehensive anti-theft features — relay-driven remote engine cut \(immobilizer\), SOS button, and geofence alarms.
- Voice monitoring via built-in microphone for remote audio checks and incident verification.
- Robust GPS performance using a UBLOX chip: high sensitivity \(-159 dBm\) and ~5 m positioning accuracy.
- Low power mode, low battery alarm, and main power-cut alarm to protect vehicle battery and alert on tampering.
- Wide operating voltage range \(9–75 V\) — suitable for cars, trucks, and many commercial vehicles.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK-206 becomes a core telemetry node for vehicle fleets and individual cars. Location, alarms, and status messages are forwarded to Plaspy via GPRS \(preferred for continuous updates\) or SMS for fallback reporting. Plaspy receives positional data and alarms, displays real-time tracking on maps, and triggers notifications and reports for fleet management and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates transmitted to Plaspy over GPRS \(with SMS fallback\).
- Remote engine cut / immobilizer control via the included relay — can be activated from Plaspy for theft response.
- Geofence alerts, SOS emergency button events, low battery, vibration/movement, and main power-cut alarms forwarded to Plaspy for instant notification.
- Voice monitoring and microphone audio events available for incident validation through the platform's monitoring tools.
- Plaspy can combine TK-206 location and event data with additional telemetry \(fuel or third‑party sensors\) where external sensors are part of the installation.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization: remotely cut engine power via relay when unauthorized movement is detected and recover vehicles faster with Plaspy alerts and coordinates.
- Personal car security and emergency response: SOS button and voice monitoring provide immediate alerts to Plaspy for rapid assistance and location sharing.
- Delivery and logistics tracking: real-time location, speed data, and history playback help validate routes, deliveries, and driver behavior.
- Rental and shared vehicle management: geofence alerts, low battery and power-cut alarms, and remote immobilization help protect assets and enforce operational rules.
- Tamper detection and parked vehicle protection: vibration and movement alarms plus sleeping mode conserve battery while maintaining security monitoring.
