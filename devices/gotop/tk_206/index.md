# GOTOP - TK-206

<div className="device-logo">
![TK-206](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK-206 is a compact GSM/GPRS/GPS car tracker designed for dependable and discreet vehicle tracking and recovery. As one of GOTOP's smaller high performance car trackers, it is intended for hidden mounting in cars and light commercial vehicles while providing continuous location updates, geofence alerts, SOS signaling, and remote immobilization functions that support theft response and safety monitoring.

This model is Plaspy compatible out of the box, making it a practical choice for organizations and vehicle owners who want to integrate a proven tracker into centralized tracking and fleet management workflows. TK-206 reporting over GPRS and SMS, combined with its alarm features and remote relay control, allows Plaspy to present live location, history playback, and timely notifications to operators and managers.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet form factor suitable for hidden installation in cars and light commercial vehicles
- Real time tracking and reporting via GPRS with SMS as fallback for flexible connectivity
- Built in anti theft features including relay driven remote engine cut and dedicated SOS button
- Voice monitoring via onboard microphone to assist incident verification
- UBLOX GNSS based positioning with high sensitivity and practical accuracy for vehicle tracking
- Power monitoring and tamper alerts such as low battery and main power cut for improved asset protection
- Wide operating voltage range to support a variety of vehicle electrical systems

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK-206 functions as a telemetry source that streams location and event data into the platform so teams can monitor vehicles, receive alerts, and review historical tracks. Plaspy displays the unit on maps, triggers notifications based on device alarms, and archives telemetry for reporting and operational analysis.

- Real time location and status updates forwarded to Plaspy over GPRS with SMS as a fallback reporting method
- Remote engine cut and immobilizer events can be managed from the platform to support theft response procedures
- Geofence alerts, SOS button activations, low battery and power cut alarms are relayed to Plaspy for immediate notification
- Voice monitoring events from the device microphone can be used in Plaspy to assist incident assessment
- Plaspy can combine TK-206 location and event data with other fleet information to support reporting and operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and recovery workflows where remote immobilization and rapid location sharing are required
- Personal vehicle security with SOS alerts and remote monitoring for emergency response
- Delivery and logistics operations that need real time location, route verification, and history playback
- Rental and shared vehicle programs seeking geofence notifications, tamper alerts, and remote control options
- Parked vehicle protection and tamper detection using vibration and power cut alarms
