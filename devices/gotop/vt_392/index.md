# GOTOP - VT-392

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-392](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP VT-392 is a versatile vehicle GPS tracker that combines location monitoring with alarm and driver identification features. It supports integration with car alarm systems and includes an RFID phone reader function that lets a smartphone act as an authorized tag. The unit can recognize configured phones, arm the tracker automatically when the authorized phone leaves the vehicle, and send alerts and calls to the configured cell phone or platform if the vehicle is opened, started, or moved.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-392 can report status and events into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and management. Its Driver ID Identify Solution sends driver identity information to a server platform, enabling administrators to view who is operating a vehicle. That same event and alarm information can be routed into Plaspy to support fleet visibility, alerts, and driver accountability without replacing existing fleet management workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Combines GPS tracking with vehicle alarm integration for enhanced security
- RFID phone reader lets a smartphone act as a recognized tag for seamless arming and disarming
- Driver ID Identify Solution provides per driver identity reporting to a platform
- Sends notifications and can place a call to the configured phone when alarm events occur
- Vehicle status and driver identity are reported to a server platform for monitoring
- Useful for both single vehicle protection and multi vehicle fleet oversight

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT-392 can feed its location, alarm events, and driver identification data into Plaspy so fleet managers and vehicle owners see those items alongside other fleet telemetry. Plaspy can present the VT-392 data in dashboards, alerts, and reports to support everyday operational decisions and security monitoring.

- Vehicle location and movement updates appear in Plaspy for route and asset visibility
- Alarm events and notifications from the tracker are relayed into Plaspy alerts for timely response
- Driver ID records sent from the tracker are available in Plaspy for accountability and reporting
- Automatic arming and disarming status based on recognized phones can be tracked in platform logs
- Event history from the VT-392 can be included in Plaspy reports for audits and operational review

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental fleets or shared vehicles that require driver identification and usage logs
- Organizations that need alarm notifications consolidated into a single monitoring platform
- Managers who want consolidated reporting of vehicle events and driver identity
