# GOTOP - G07

![G07](./tracker.jpg)

The G07 is a heavy-duty, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for long-term, low-maintenance tracking of vehicles, containers and other high-value mobile assets. With very large internal lithium battery options \(10,000 mAh and 20,000 mAh\), robust IP65 protection and six powerful NdFeB magnets for covert, non-invasive mounting, the G07 delivers reliable location and event telemetry for rental fleets, cargo recovery and remote asset oversight. Its GPS + BeiDou + LBS hybrid positioning, combined with on-device alarms and remote audio monitoring, makes it straightforward to use the G07 with Plaspy for persistent, real-time tracking and anti-theft workflows.

The G07 is designed where long standby times and rugged outdoor performance matter. Built around an MT6261D 2G GSM modem and a ZKMicro AT6558D GNSS module, it reports positions with roughly 5-meter accuracy under good conditions and supplies a broad suite of security alerts \(tamper/demolition, overspeed, movement/vibration, low battery\) plus remote voice and sound-controlled recording. As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the G07 integrates its location and telemetry into Plaspy's real-time tracking, reporting and alerting platform to support fleet management, anti-theft response and long-term asset monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Long-duration tracking: large internal lithium battery options \(10,000 mAh or 20,000 mAh\) for extended standby between maintenance cycles.
- Plaspy compatible: transmits GNSS, LBS and device alarms over GSM for real-time tracking, alerts and historical playback on Plaspy.
- Hybrid positioning: GPS + BeiDou + LBS for reliable location fixes with reported accuracy around 5 meters in favorable conditions.
- Rugged & covert mounting: IP65-rated ABS housing and six NdFeB magnets enable weatherproof, discreet attachment to vehicles and containers.
- Comprehensive security telemetry: tamper/demolition, overspeed, move/vibration and low-voltage alarms support anti-theft and recovery scenarios.
- Remote audio capabilities: internal microphone supports remote audio monitoring, remote voice recording and sound-triggered recording for situational awareness.
- Low-power standby: optimized sleep current characteristics suitable for assets that report infrequently yet must remain locatable for months.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The G07 uses its integrated MT6261D GSM modem and Micro SIM to send GNSS \(GPS + BeiDou\) positions, LBS fallback data and event telemetry to Plaspy's backend. Once configured with Plaspy-compatible reporting intervals and alert thresholds, the device's position updates, alarms and battery/voltage status appear in Plaspy's dashboard for real-time tracking, geofencing and incident response.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered over 2G GSM to Plaspy for live mapping and historical playback.
- Tamper/demolition, overspeed, move and vibration alarms forwarded to Plaspy as instantaneous alerts for anti-theft and recovery actions.
- Low-voltage and battery-level telemetry to help schedule maintenance and preserve asset uptime.
- Remote audio monitoring, remote voice recording and sound-controlled recording accessible via Plaspy when audio features are enabled.
- For fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer control and Bluetooth sensors: these functions are not native to the G07; integrate external telematics gateways or sensor hubs with Plaspy if those capabilities are required.

## Use Cases

- Rental car and fleet monitoring — long battery life and discreet mounting reduce maintenance and improve recovery rates for short- or long-term rentals.
- Container and cargo tracking — IP65 protection and strong magnetic attachment let you monitor shipments exposed to outdoor conditions.
- Asset recovery and anti-theft — tamper/demolition alarms, move/vibration detection and overspeed alerts support rapid response through Plaspy notifications.
- Remote or low-service locations — extended standby and hybrid GPS/BD/LBS positioning enable tracking where power and connectivity windows are limited.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the G07 with Plaspy gives organizations a practical, low-maintenance solution for real-time tracking and anti-theft workflows. The combination of very large internal batteries, rugged IP65 construction and secure magnetic mounting minimizes downtime and installation effort, while the device’s alarm set and remote audio features enhance situational awareness during recovery operations. Because the G07 transmits GNSS, LBS and device status over 2G, it integrates cleanly into Plaspy’s mapping, alerting and reporting tools to deliver fleet management, telemetry and anti-theft capabilities without continuous onsite maintenance. For deployments that require additional telemetry \(fuel monitoring\), ignition sensing or immobilizer control, consider supplementing the G07 with Plaspy-supported external sensors or telematics gateways to extend functionality while keeping the G07 as the long-life location and security node.
