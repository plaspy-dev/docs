# GOTOP - G07

<div className="device-logo">
![G07](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The G07 is a heavy duty GPS tracker designed for long term, low maintenance tracking of vehicles, containers and other high value mobile assets. It pairs very large internal battery options with an IP65 rated housing and six strong magnets for discreet, weather resistant attachment, and includes GNSS positioning, on device alarms and remote audio monitoring to support persistent location and event telemetry.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G07 integrates its location updates and alarm events into Plaspy's tracking platform so teams can monitor assets in real time, review historical playback and receive incident alerts. Its long standby characteristics and rugged design make the G07 a practical choice where prolonged deployment and minimal onsite maintenance are priorities for Plaspy users.

## Key Highlights

- Long duration operation with large internal lithium battery options for extended standby between maintenance.
- Plaspy compatibility for live mapping, alerts and historical playback of location and device telemetry.
- Hybrid GNSS positioning with BeiDou and GPS plus LBS fallback for reliable fixes under varied conditions.
- Rugged IP65 ABS housing and six NdFeB magnets for discreet, weather resistant mounting to metal assets.
- Built in alarms for tamper, demolition, movement and overspeed to support anti theft workflows.
- Remote audio monitoring and sound activated recording for additional situational awareness during recovery.
- Designed for low power standby behavior to keep assets locatable over long periods with infrequent reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the G07 delivers position reports and event telemetry into Plaspy's dashboard so operators can maintain visibility, set alerts and produce reports across their fleet or asset inventory. The device's alarms and audio features can be surfaced in Plaspy to support timely responses and investigations.

- Real time position updates and historical playback available in Plaspy for tracking and route review.
- Instant alerting of tamper, movement, overspeed and low battery events routed to Plaspy for response workflows.
- Battery and voltage telemetry presented in Plaspy to help schedule maintenance and avoid unexpected downtime.
- Remote audio and sound triggered recordings accessible through Plaspy when audio features are enabled on the device.
- Geofencing, incident reporting and fleet oversight facilitated by combining G07 telemetry with Plaspy reporting tools.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental fleet monitoring where discreet mounting and long battery life reduce maintenance and improve asset recovery.
- Container and cargo tracking in outdoor conditions that require rugged, weather resistant attachment.
- Asset recovery and anti theft operations that benefit from tamper alerts, movement detection and remote audio.
- Remote installations with infrequent connectivity windows that need long standby and dependable location reporting.
- Long term oversight of high value equipment that must remain locatable without continuous power or servicing.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GOTOP G07 is suited to organizations that prioritize low maintenance, rugged deployment and reliable location reporting. Its combination of large internal batteries, weather resistant enclosure and strong magnetic mounting reduces the need for frequent onsite intervention while still delivering the alarms and audio capabilities that support recovery and security workflows in Plaspy.
