# GOTOP - G07 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP G07 and how its telemetry, alarms, and positioning can be used with Plaspy for real time tracking, alerting, and historical playback. It summarizes practical capabilities relevant to fleet operators, rental companies, cargo handlers, and asset recovery teams using Plaspy as their tracking platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional product variant, and installation method. Manufacturer configuration choices and firmware upgrades may enable or disable particular functions, so consult official GOTOP documentation for device specific details when planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G07 is a heavy duty, long life GPS tracker designed for low maintenance tracking of vehicles, containers and other high value mobile assets. It combines rugged mounting, hybrid positioning and a broad alarm set so that location and incident telemetry feed directly into Plaspy for monitoring and response.

- Very large internal lithium battery options for extended standby between maintenance cycles
- Rugged IP65 ABS housing with six powerful NdFeB magnets for covert, non invasive mounting
- Hybrid positioning using GPS plus BeiDou with LBS fallback for improved fixes in mixed coverage
- On device security alarms including tamper and demolition, overspeed, movement and low battery
- Remote audio monitoring and voice or sound triggered recording for situational awareness
- Optimized low power behavior to support long term asset tracking where infrequent reports are required

## Core Features of GOTOP - G07

- Internal lithium battery options available in 10,000 mAh and 20,000 mAh configurations for long standby life
- IP65 rated ABS enclosure and six NdFeB permanent magnets for secure outdoor mounting without invasive installation
- Hybrid GNSS positioning with GPS and BeiDou plus LBS fallback, with reported position accuracy around 5 meters under favorable conditions
- 2G GSM modem based reporting for sending GNSS, LBS and event telemetry to remote servers and platforms
- On device alarms and security telemetry including tamper/demolition, overspeed, movement/vibration and low voltage alerts
- Remote audio capability with internal microphone supporting live monitoring, remote voice recording and sound triggered recording
- Low current standby modes and power optimizations suitable for assets that report infrequently

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the G07 reports location fixes, fallback LBS data and alarm events so operators can see live position and incident context on Plaspy maps and dashboards. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and ingests the device telemetry for monitoring, alerting and historical analysis.

- Real time location updates and historical playback shown on Plaspy mapping for operational visibility
- Tamper, demolition, overspeed and movement alarms forwarded as actionable alerts in Plaspy to support recovery workflows
- Battery and low voltage telemetry available in Plaspy to schedule maintenance and reduce downtime
- Remote audio and recorded clips accessible through Plaspy when audio features are enabled on the device
- LBS fallback reports improve location visibility in areas with weak satellite reception and are reflected in Plaspy event records

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental fleet tracking where long battery life and discreet mounting reduce maintenance and improve recovery chances
- Container and cargo monitoring for outdoor shipments exposed to weather and rough handling
- Asset recovery and anti theft workflows using movement and tamper alarms routed to Plaspy alerts and incident tools
- Remote asset oversight in low service areas where extended standby and hybrid positioning matter
- Long term placement of high value equipment that requires infrequent check ins but reliable locatability

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and hardware revisions can change exact alarm behavior, audio functions, and power profiles; confirm capabilities for your unit before deployment
- Regional network availability of 2G GSM can affect reporting and should be verified for the intended operating area
- Some features such as remote audio or specific alarm triggers may require configuration on the device and consent where applicable
- Installation method and mounting location affect GNSS performance and magnetic mounting suitability
- For additional telemetry types such as fuel sensing or immobilizer control, integrate external sensors or telematics gateways that Plaspy supports rather than relying on native G07 functions
