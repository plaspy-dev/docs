# GOTOP - D22-4G Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP D22-4G Waterproof GPS Tracker and how its capabilities are exposed when used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about the device's tracking, telemetry, and alarm functions and what fleet managers can expect to see in the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where behavior depends on firmware or optional wiring, the manufacturer documentation should be consulted for device-specific setup and the most current feature list.

## Feature Overview

The D22-4G is a rugged vehicle terminal designed to deliver continuous location and event data to fleet platforms such as Plaspy. It combines high-sensitivity GNSS positioning with LTE communications and on-device buffering so that location, movement, and status messages reach Plaspy reliably for monitoring, alerts, and reporting.

- High-sensitivity GPS and BDS dual-satellite positioning for consistent location fixes in vehicle deployments.
- 4G LTE communications with integrated antenna hardware for continuous data transmission to fleet servers.
- Onboard storage and blind-area retransmission to preserve telemetry when coverage is interrupted.
- Vehicle status inputs and outputs including ACC/ignition detection, door sensor input, and external relay control for immobilizer use.
- Movement and safety alarms such as overspeed and vibration detection to support theft prevention and driver safety workflows.

## Core Features of GOTOP - D22-4G

- Dual GNSS support with GPS and BDS positioning for improved satellite availability.
- 4G LTE terminal with integrated antenna design for cellular connectivity.
- ACC/ignition detection and door sensor inputs to report vehicle start/stop and access events.
- Overspeed and vibration alarms plus a built-in 3-axis acceleration sensor for movement and event detection.
- External relay control for remote fuel cut-off as an immobilizer function.
- Waterproof industrial form factor suitable for vehicle and fleet installations.
- Large-capacity offline storage and blind-area retransmission to maintain a continuous historical record.
- Two-way voice support with built-in speaker and microphone and an SOS alarm facility for urgent alerts.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the D22-4G is connected to Plaspy, device location and status messages are ingested into the platform so operators can monitor vehicles, receive alerts, and access historical reports. Plaspy presents the telemetry and event data in dashboards and logs to support operational decisions and incident response.

- Real-time location updates and movement history displayed on Plaspy maps and timelines.
- Ignition and door status events visible in Plaspy for ignition-aware workflows and access monitoring.
- Alarm events such as overspeed, vibration, SOS, and power-failure alerts pushed to Plaspy for notification and escalation.
- Offline buffering and blind-area retransmission ensure Plaspy retains a continuous data trail after coverage gaps.
- Remote immobilizer and relay events logged in Plaspy to support recovery and vehicle control processes.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and utilization reporting for cars, vans, and commercial vehicles.
- Anti-theft monitoring and rapid recovery workflows using movement alarms, SOS, and remote fuel cut-off.
- Driver safety and compliance through overspeed and vibration event detection.
- Remote status monitoring including ignition and door activity for operations and security.
- Telematics continuity in areas with intermittent cellular coverage via onboard storage and retransmission.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version can affect which alarms, reporting intervals, and commands are supported; confirm firmware capabilities before deployment.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may change supported cellular bands and included accessories; check the device label and supplier details.
- Some functions require correct wiring or optional external components, for example the external relay used for fuel cut-off.
- Two-way voice and SOS behavior may depend on the configured monitoring numbers and service setup.
- Plaspy can ingest the device data, but exact field names and event labels displayed in the platform depend on the device firmware and Plaspy parsing rules.
