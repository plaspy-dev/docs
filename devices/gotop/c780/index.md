# GOTOP - C780

![C780](./tracker.jpg)

The GOTOP C780 is a compact GPS tracker designed for cars, motorcycles, e-bikes and other light vehicles that need reliable location monitoring and security. It combines satellite positioning with cellular connectivity to deliver continuous position updates, history playback and alerting, while a small form factor makes it suitable for discreet installation on a variety of vehicles.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the C780 can stream location and status information into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, reporting and incident response. That compatibility makes the C780 a practical option for operators who want to consolidate vehicle telemetry, alarms and route history inside Plaspy for daily operations and fleet oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real time tracking with GPRS uploads and SMS position reporting for fallback alerts.
- Compact, lightweight enclosure suitable for discreet mounting on cars, motorcycles and e-bikes.
- Built in backup battery and wide input range to maintain reporting after external power loss.
- U‑BLOX GNSS positioning for reliable fixes and typical location accuracy suitable for fleet and recovery workflows.
- Vehicle security features including ignition detection, wire cut and motion alerts to support anti theft monitoring.
- Durable design with water resistance for dependable 24/7 vehicle tracking in varied conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the C780 sends position and status data to the platform so operators can view live location, review historical routes and receive configurable alerts. Plaspy ingests telemetry from the device and presents it in dashboards, maps and reports to support monitoring, incident response and operational reporting.

- Continuous location updates and status data forwarded to Plaspy for live map visualization and fleet oversight.
- Tamper and motion alerts pushed to Plaspy to trigger notifications and rapid response workflows.
- History playback and event logs available in Plaspy for route verification, compliance and incident investigation.
- Ignition or ACC status shown in Plaspy dashboards to support trip analytics and runtime reporting.
- SMS position reporting with map links available as a fallback channel when required by network conditions or workflow preferences.
- If additional local sensors are needed, their data can be coordinated alongside C780 telemetry within Plaspy using compatible gateway approaches.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light commercial vehicles where centralized visibility, trip history and basic telemetry are required.
- Anti theft and recovery programs using tamper alerts, motion detection and location reporting to support fast response.
- Personal vehicle protection for motorcycles and e-bikes with discreet tracking and emergency location reporting.
- Remote monitoring of service and delivery vehicles to verify routes, support proof of delivery and analyze utilization.
- Maintenance and operations alerts driven by power loss and ignition events to improve vehicle uptime management.
