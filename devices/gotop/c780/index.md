# GOTOP - C780

![C780](./tracker.jpg)

The C780 is a compact GPS tracker designed for cars, motorcycles, e-bikes and light vehicles that require reliable location monitoring and security. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the C780 delivers real-time tracking, historical route playback and alerting via GPRS and SMS so fleet managers and vehicle owners can centralize telemetry in Plaspy for monitoring, reporting and incident response.

The device pairs satellite positioning from a U‑BLOX GNSS module with quad-band GSM/GPRS connectivity to provide continuous position updates, tamper alerts and power-fail resilience. With wide input voltage, a built-in backup battery and IPX7 water resistance, the C780 is engineered for discreet installation and dependable 24/7 vehicle tracking and anti-theft protection when integrated into Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking via GPRS uploads and SMS position reporting with Google Maps links for easy visualization.
- Compact, lightweight form factor \(73 × 26 × 13 mm; 50 g\) for concealed installation on cars, motorcycles and e-bikes.
- Wide 9V–95V input range with a built-in 370 mAh Li‑ion backup battery to maintain tracking after external power loss.
- Robust positioning: U‑BLOX G7020‑ST 50‑channel GNSS with typical position accuracy around 5 meters and A‑GPS AssistNow support.
- Vehicle security features including ACC/ignition detection, wire‑cut alerts, fuel‑cut capability and a motion/vibration sensor for tamper alarms.
- Quad‑band GSM \(850/900/1900/2100\) and GPRS Class 12 support for wide telecom compatibility and efficient telemetry uploads.
- Durable design with IPX7 waterproof rating and an internal watchdog CPU to reduce crashes and maintain uptime.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the C780 streams position and status data to the Plaspy platform over GPRS or sends position links via SMS. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry for live map display, historical route playback, configurable alerts and fleet reporting. The combination provides turnkey real-time tracking, anti-theft notifications and basic telemetry without complex setup.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy via GPRS \(Class 12\) for continuous visibility.
- ACC/ignition detection for runtime and trip analytics, visible in Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Fuel-cut capability usable as a remote immobilizer action \(fuel cutoff\) when coordinated through Plaspy workflows and authorized commands.
- Motion/vibration sensor and wire‑cut alerts generate tamper and movement alarms pushed to Plaspy immediately.
- History route playback and event logs uploaded to Plaspy for compliance, proof of delivery and incident investigation.
- SMS position reporting with Google Maps links as a fallback for networks or workflows that prefer SMS-based alerts.
- Note on Bluetooth sensors: the C780 focuses on GPS/GPRS telemetry. If you require BLE sensor data in Plaspy, external BLE gateways or companion devices can be used to forward sensor data into Plaspy alongside C780 telemetry.
