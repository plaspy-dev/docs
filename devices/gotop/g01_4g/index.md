# GOTOP - G01-4G

![G01-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The G01-4G GPS tracker is a Plaspy compatible vehicle tracking solution built for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management. Designed for cars, taxis, rental vehicles and light trucks, the G01-4G uses 4G LTE with SMS fallback to deliver fast position updates, configurable geo-fence alarms, SOS emergency reporting, and vehicle-specific telemetry such as ACC \(ignition\) detection and remote engine cut control. Its IP67-rated, ABS enclosure and wide operating voltage range make it suitable for professional fleet deployments and harsh automotive environments.

When integrated with Plaspy, the G01-4G becomes part of a centralized monitoring and anti-theft toolkit: Plaspy ingests the tracker’s location, alarm and telemetry streams to provide live maps, event alerts, and actionable reports for fleet managers. The device’s listen-in microphone, external speaker support, vibration/movement alarms and main power cut detection all feed into Plaspy’s alerting and historical reporting so teams can respond faster to theft, breakdowns and safety incidents.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for precise, low-latency real-time tracking and mapping.
- Robust IP67-rated housing and wide operating voltage \(DC 9V–100V\) for vehicle and fleet use.
- Vehicle telemetry included: ACC \(ignition\) detection, SOS button, vibration/movement and main power cut alarms.
- Remote immobilizer capability \(remote engine cut\) to support anti-theft response workflows in Plaspy.
- Built-in microphone and external speaker support for listen‑in and two-way audio scenarios.
- 4G/LTE communication with SMS fallback keeps tracking reliable across cellular conditions.
- Compact form factor with multiple I/O and interfaces \(UART/RS232/CAN/K-Line\) for external sensors and telematics integration.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the G01-4G with Plaspy turns the device’s raw GNSS fixes and vehicle events into operational intelligence. Plaspy consumes the tracker’s 4G or SMS updates and translates them into live location, geofence notifications, and driver/vehicle telemetry. Alerts from the tracker—SOS presses, vibration alarms, power loss, and ignition changes—are surfaced in Plaspy dashboards and can trigger workflows, notifications or automated reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered over 4G/LTE with SMS fallback for continuity.
- Ignition and door/alarm status: ACC detection and digital inputs report vehicle state to Plaspy.
- Fuel monitoring and advanced telemetry: integrates with external fuel or sensor systems via the device’s I/O and serial interfaces \(requires compatible peripherals\).
- Remote immobilizer: remote engine cut control is available and can be activated through Plaspy-managed commands for anti-theft response.
- Audio monitoring and emergency handling: SOS button and listen-in microphone events feed into Plaspy incident workflows. \(Bluetooth sensors are managed separately by Plaspy; the G01-4G does not list built-in BLE.\)

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real-time tracking, ignition events, and centralized reporting for taxis, delivery vehicles and service fleets.
- Rental and financed vehicles: geo-fencing, remote immobilization and SOS support to reduce theft and speed recovery.
- Passenger vehicles and taxis: live position, driver safety alerts, and listen-in capability for emergencies or incident validation.
- Asset protection and anti-theft: vibration/movement alarms, main power cut detection, and engine cut control integrated into Plaspy workflows.
- Telematics integrations: connect external fuel sensors, RFID readers or cameras via UART/RS232/CAN/K-Line for extended telemetry and fuel monitoring capabilities.
