# GOTOP - G01-4G

<div className="device-logo">
![G01-4G](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP G01-4G is a vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management. Built for cars, taxis, rental vehicles and light trucks, the device uses 4G LTE with SMS fallback to provide fast position updates, configurable geo-fence alarms, SOS emergency reporting, ACC ignition detection and remote engine cut control. Its IP67 rated ABS enclosure and wide operating voltage range make it suitable for professional fleet deployments and challenging automotive environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G01-4G integrates its location, alarm and telemetry streams into Plaspy to create a centralized monitoring and anti theft toolkit. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s updates and events to present live maps, event alerts and historical reports so fleet managers can track vehicles, respond to incidents and run operational reports from a single platform. Audio features and power or vibration alarms further enhance incident visibility within Plaspy workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for low latency real time location updates with SMS fallback for continuity
- Designed for vehicles including cars, taxis, rental fleets and light trucks
- Vehicle telemetry support such as ACC ignition detection, SOS button, vibration and main power cut alarms
- Remote immobilizer capability to support anti theft response managed through Plaspy
- Rugged IP67 ABS enclosure and broad operating voltage range for professional fleet use
- Built in microphone and external speaker support for listen in and two way audio scenarios
- Multiple I O and serial interfaces to connect external peripherals and extend telemetry where required

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the G01-4G transforms device position fixes and vehicle events into operational intelligence accessible from Plaspy dashboards. The platform consumes 4G or SMS updates from the tracker and surfaces location, geofence and alarm events alongside historical data for analysis and response.

- Real time location and telemetry updates visible on Plaspy live maps and vehicle lists
- Geofence alerts, vibration and power loss alarms delivered to Plaspy event streams for immediate notification
- Ignition status and remote immobilizer control shown in vehicle state and available for managed response workflows
- SOS and listen in events routed into Plaspy incident handling to support emergency response and verification
- Historical reporting and exportable event logs to support fleet performance analysis and investigations
- Integration with external peripherals and sensors captured by the device and included in Plaspy reporting where applicable

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for taxis, delivery and service vehicles requiring live tracking and event reporting
- Rental and financed vehicle oversight using geo fencing and remote immobilization to reduce theft risk
- Passenger vehicle safety monitoring with SOS handling and audio incident validation
- Asset protection and anti theft workflows combining vibration, power cut and engine cut controls with Plaspy alerts
- Telematics extensions such as external fuel or sensor integrations for expanded operational visibility

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G01-4G is a practical choice for organizations that need dependable vehicle tracking, essential anti theft controls and straightforward integration into a centralized platform. Its combination of fast cellular updates, vehicle inputs and rugged automotive design aligns with common fleet requirements, while Plaspy converts the tracker’s streams into actionable dashboards, alerts and reports that support day to day operations and incident response.
