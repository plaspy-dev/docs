# GOTOP - TE-207S Features

This page provides a public overview of the GOTOP TE-207S Waterproof GPS Tracker and how its features are used with Plaspy for centralized tracking, alerts, and historical telemetry. It describes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, focusing on practical functions such as location reporting, emergency signaling, voice monitoring, and event alerts.

Exact feature behavior and availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration details, advanced settings, and the latest firmware notes consult the official GOTOP documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The TE-207S is a compact, rugged personal tracker designed for reliable real-time tracking and safety monitoring in wet or demanding environments. In Plaspy the device provides position updates and event notifications that support rapid response, historical playback, and centralized monitoring workflows.

- Real time location updates via GPRS or SMS for map visualization and history in Plaspy
- IPX6 waterproofing and compact form factor for use in outdoor and damp conditions
- Dedicated SOS button and emergency alerting for immediate notification of incidents
- Two way calling and voice monitoring for discreet communication and situation assessment
- Event driven alarms including fall detection, movement or shock alerts, geo fence and speeding
- Internal waypoint memory for offline logging when connectivity is intermittent

## Core Features of GOTOP - TE-207S

- U‑blox 7 GNSS with AGPS for rapid fixes and reliable position reporting
- Quad band GSM GPRS connectivity supporting global GPRS or SMS reporting
- IPX6 waterproof enclosure suitable for exposure to splashes and rain
- Dedicated SOS button that triggers emergency reporting and notifications
- Two way calling and voice monitoring capabilities for remote listening and contact
- Fall down detection and 3D G sensor movement or shock alarms for safety monitoring
- Geo fence and speeding alarm functions for perimeter and behaviour alerts
- Internal memory for waypoint logging to preserve location history during offline periods

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TE-207S reports position or event data, Plaspy ingests those messages and exposes them in maps, alerts, and history so operators can monitor assets and people from a single dashboard. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and surfaces incoming telemetry without requiring per device protocol selection.

- Live position and periodic updates appear on Plaspy maps for real time tracking and situational awareness
- SOS and emergency alarms are forwarded to Plaspy so configured alert workflows and notifications can be triggered
- Geo fence and speeding events are processed by Plaspy to generate rule based alerts and escalation actions
- Fall down and movement or shock events are recorded in Plaspy for incident logging and later review
- Two way calling and voice events are device native and can be logged in Plaspy as call records or incident notes

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety for children, elderly family members, and lone workers needing SOS and location reporting
- Field staff and mobile healthcare teams requiring real time tracking plus fall detection in wet conditions
- Water resistant asset protection where devices may be exposed to rain or splashes while maintaining telemetry
- Traveling staff and escorts who need discreet two way communication alongside continuous location history
- Event logging and incident investigation supported by long standby and onboard waypoint storage

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature support can vary by firmware version and hardware revision; not all units may include every function out of the box
- Some behaviors depend on device configuration and the type of reporting selected such as GPRS versus SMS
- Regional variants or carrier provisioning can affect network connectivity and GPRS performance
- Installation method and mounting can influence sensor performance for fall detection or movement alerts
- Always confirm the exact capabilities and firmware history for a specific serial or batch with manufacturer documentation

Using the GOTOP TE-207S with Plaspy gives organizations centralized visibility over position and safety events while preserving device native features like SOS and two way voice.
