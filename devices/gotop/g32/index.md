# GOTOP - G32

<div className="device-logo">
![G32](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP G32 is a compact, purpose built mini GPS tracker designed primarily for cats and dogs. It provides always on location visibility using cellular transmissions, AGPS assisted positioning and a direct Google Maps link for instant location viewing. The device is optimized for small form factor use and includes features such as configurable alarms and remote monitoring intended to help pet owners locate and watch over animals more reliably.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the G32 can forward its location and event data into Plaspy so owners and caretakers see live positions, receive notifications and review history in one place. Built in mechanisms for preserving data after brief signal loss and support for common alert types make the G32 a practical option to integrate into Plaspy dashboards for centralized tracking and timely notifications.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible mini GPS tracker optimized for pets and small form factor deployments
- Real time tracking via SMS and 4G for prompt location updates
- AGPS support with MTK GPS chipset for faster position fixes in typical outdoor conditions
- Rechargeable 800mAh battery offering extended operation between charges
- GPRS blind area data re upload to preserve location history after temporary connectivity loss
- Voice monitoring for remote situational awareness
- Configurable alarms including geofence movement and low battery notifications

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the G32 sends its location and events via cellular messages to the configured endpoint so Plaspy can display live positions on the map and retain historical tracks. Plaspy ingests the device updates and surfaces alerts and events alongside other assets, giving a consolidated view of activity and notifying users when configured thresholds are met.

- Real time location and telemetry updates become visible on Plaspy maps and lists
- Geofence and movement alarms are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and response
- Low battery and other device alerts appear as events in Plaspy so you can act on them quickly
- GPRS blind area data re upload helps Plaspy reconstruct tracks when connectivity is intermittent
- Google Maps links included in device messages provide quick map access from Plaspy alerts and history

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet recovery and anti theft monitoring with geofence alerts and instant location updates
- Active outdoor monitoring for hikes, walks and roaming behavior with movement notifications
- Multi animal care where small trackers are managed and monitored centrally
- Sharing location access with pet sitters or caretakers through map links and Plaspy accounts
- Discreet tracking of small assets or personal items where a compact tracker is required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G32 pairs a purpose built pet tracking hardware profile with a platform designed for centralized monitoring. Its compact size and AGPS assisted positioning help deliver timely location updates, while battery capacity and data re upload behavior give practical resilience for everyday use. Plaspy complements the device by aggregating live data, managing alert delivery and providing historical playback so owners and operators avoid juggling multiple tools.
