# GOTOP - G32 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP G32 mini GPS tracker and how its capabilities integrate with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical, non-sensitive functions you can expect when using the device with Plaspy for centralized location visibility, alerting, and history playback.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, manufacturing batch, installation method, and regional device configuration. For the most current and device specific details, consult the official GOTOP documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G32 is a compact tracker built for cats and dogs that offers always-on location visibility and configurable alerts. Its design emphasizes fast fixes, continuous track continuity, and small form factor operation useful for pet owners and other small asset scenarios.

- Real-time tracking via SMS and 4G for immediate location updates
- AGPS support with MTK GPS technology for faster time to first fix
- Rechargeable 800mAh battery for extended operation between charges
- GPRS blind-area data re-upload to preserve tracking history after signal loss
- Voice monitoring for remote listen-in situational awareness
- Configurable alarms such as geofence, movement, low-battery, and speeding alerts

## Core Features of GOTOP - G32

- Out of the box compatibility with Plaspy for centralized tracking and alerts
- Real-time location updates transmitted over cellular networks
- AGPS assisted positioning powered by MTK GPS technology
- Rechargeable 800mAh lithium battery for compact endurance
- GPRS blind-area re-upload to recover points recorded during connectivity gaps
- Remote voice monitoring capability for situational context
- Configurable event alarms including geofence and movement notifications
- Low-battery and speeding alerts to support timely action

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the G32’s location and event messages are consolidated into a single dashboard where live position, alerts, and history are visible. Plaspy presents device events and stored tracks so owners can monitor movement, respond to notifications, and review past activity.

- Live positions appear on Plaspy’s real-time map for immediate situational awareness
- Geofence, movement, low-battery, and speeding alarms are recorded as events in Plaspy
- Historical tracks are reconstructed in Plaspy, including points re-uploaded after blind-area connectivity
- Voice monitoring indicators and related events are available as device-level records
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols so pairing is streamlined

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet recovery and anti-theft where quick location updates and geofence alerts help locate a missing animal
- Monitoring off-leash walks, hikes, or roaming behavior with movement alerts and location history
- Managing multiple animals at small kennels or shelters with centralized tracking and low-battery notifications
- Sharing location with pet sitters or caregivers through Plaspy or via Google Maps links included in device messages
- Discreet tracking of small personal items where a compact form factor is required
