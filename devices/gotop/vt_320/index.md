# GOTOP - VT-320

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-320](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP VT-320 is a compact GPS/GSM/GPRS tracking device designed primarily for motorcycle real-time tracking and vehicle security. Built to provide stable position fixes even in challenging locations, the VT-320 supports multiple tracking modes including time interval, distance interval, and SMS or GPRS reporting. It also includes practical security features such as an SOS panic button, geo-fence alarm, over-speed alarm, a built in motion sensor for power saving, and remote engine cut capability.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-320 can feed location and alert data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and fleet oversight. Plaspy can use the VT-320 data to display real time positions, manage geo-fence and over-speed events, and collect mileage information for reporting. The tracker’s wide power range and small form factor make it a versatile choice for motorcycle and 12V or 24V vehicle deployments that need straightforward integration with Plaspy monitoring and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for motorcycle real time tracking and vehicle security
- Multiple tracking options including SMS and GPRS reporting
- Safety features such as SOS panic button, geo fence alarm, and over speed alarm
- Built in motion sensor for power saving and efficient operation
- Remote engine cut support and mileage reporting for fleet oversight
- Wide power supply range 6V to 24V and compact design for easy installation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the VT-320 supplies position updates and alarm events that Plaspy presents in a single dashboard for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can leverage the tracker’s built in features to enhance operational visibility and to streamline response to security events.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps for live monitoring of single vehicles or full fleets
- Geo fence and over speed alerts forwarded into Plaspy for immediate notification and event history
- Mileage data and tracking intervals imported for reporting and route analysis
- SOS alarm events routed to Plaspy alerting workflows for rapid awareness
- Use of tracker outputs such as remote engine cut can be coordinated through Plaspy when device configuration permits

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time motorcycle tracking and anti theft monitoring for individual riders
- Fleet tracking for taxis, buses, delivery vehicles, and light trucks
- Remote asset tracking and recovery for mobile equipment
- Location based services that need frequent position updates and SMS map links
- Security and emergency response workflows using SOS and geo fence alerts

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT-320 is a practical choice for organizations that need a small, cost conscious tracker with core security and reporting features. Its compatibility with both SMS and GPRS reporting, combined with a wide vehicle power range, makes it suitable for mixed fleets that include motorcycles and 12V or 24V vehicles. These attributes help keep deployments simple while providing the basic telemetry and alarm signals Plaspy uses for monitoring and reporting.

Combining the VT-320 with Plaspy gives teams a centralized platform to view locations, manage alerts, and produce mileage and event reports without complex configuration.
