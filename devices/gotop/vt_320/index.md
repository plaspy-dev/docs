# GOTOP - VT-320

![VT-320](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-320 is a GPS/GSM/GPRS tracking device specifically designed for motorcycle real-time tracking and security. Equipped with superior GPS and GPRS modules, this tracker offers excellent sensitivity and stable performance, ensuring accurate GPS fix even in remote areas. It is an ideal solution for motorcycle/motorbike tracking, vehicle tracking, vehicle security/recovery/anti-hijacking, mobile asset tracking, location-based services, and fleet management for cars, taxis, buses, trucks, and more.

One of the standout features of the VT-320 is its wide power supply range of 6V-24V, making it compatible with a variety of vehicles. It offers multiple tracking options, including tracking by SMS/GPRS, time interval, and distance interval. In case of emergencies, the tracker is equipped with an SOS panic button for immediate assistance. It also features a geo-fence alarm and over-speed alarm to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle.

The VT-320 comes with a built-in motion sensor for power saving, allowing for efficient battery usage. It provides a Google map URL for location via SMS, enabling you to view your vehicle's location on a map directly from your mobile phone. Additionally, you can obtain the physical address name via SMS for added convenience. The tracker also offers remote engine cut functionality and provides mileage reports for effective fleet management.

With its mini size, simple installation, and low cost, the GOTOP VT-320 is an excellent choice for motorcycles and 12V/24V vehicles. It combines advanced tracking features with ease of use, making it an essential tool for ensuring the safety and security of your vehicles.

### Key Features:

- 6V-24V power supply compatibility
- Track by SMS/GPRS
- Track by time interval
- Track by distance interval
- SOS panic button
- Geo-fence alarm
- Over-speed alarm
- Built-in motion sensor for power saving
- Google map URL for location via SMS
- Physical address name retrieval via SMS
- Remote engine cut
- Mileage report
- 1 positive input, 1 negative input, and 2 outputs
- Mini size and simple installation
- Low cost tracker for motorcycles and 12V/24V vehicles
