# GOTOP - D50 Features

This page provides a public overview of the GOTOP D50 pet GPS tracker and the features relevant to using it with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities visible to users and administrators in Plaspy, including location reporting, event notifications, and history playback, while avoiding sensitive implementation details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, cellular region, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. For the most current device specifics consult the official GOTOP documentation and the manufacturer website.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP D50 is a compact collar tracker built for pets, combining GNSS positioning with cellular uplink and cell tower fallback to provide continuous location visibility. In Plaspy the device delivers live position updates, event notifications, and historical routes that help owners and operators monitor pets in real time.

- Compact waterproof collar design for comfortable continuous wear during outdoor activity.
- GNSS positioning with A GPS assistance to shorten satellite acquisition and improve fix times.
- Cellular uplink with GSM/GPRS reporting plus LBS cell tower fallback for best effort locations in poor GPS conditions.
- Built in motion detection and SOS emergency alert for incident signaling and faster response.
- Power saving deep sleep modes and a rechargeable battery to extend standby life under low reporting configurations.
- Route history playback and geofence alerts available in Plaspy for review and automated notifications.

## Core Features of GOTOP - D50

- Real time GNSS location reporting suitable for live tracking in Plaspy.
- Assisted A GPS support to reduce time to first fix after cold or warm starts.
- GSM/GPRS data reporting and LBS fallback to maintain location reporting where satellite reception is limited.
- Motion and vibration sensor to detect activity and provide event-driven updates.
- SOS emergency alert capability for immediate incident notification.
- IPX7 rated housing for day to day outdoor exposure and pet activities.
- Rechargeable Li Polymer battery with configurable sleep modes to balance tracking frequency and standby life.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests standard position and event reports from supported trackers and presents those data points on maps, alert lists, and historical reports. For the D50 this means location fixes, motion events, and SOS notifications appear alongside route playback and geofence tools to provide organized visibility and operational oversight.

- Live GPS and fallback LBS positions appear on Plaspy maps for real time location awareness.
- Timestamped position reports enable route history playback and trip review inside Plaspy.
- Geofence creation in Plaspy generates entry and exit alerts when the D50 reports crossing events.
- Motion and SOS events forwarded by the device are surfaced as alerts for rapid attention and incident tracking.
- Device online and sleep status are reflected in Plaspy so users can see when the tracker is actively reporting or conserving power.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time pet location tracking during walks, outdoor play, and travel.
- Emergency alerts when an SOS button is activated or an unexpected motion event occurs.
- Geofence monitoring to notify owners if a pet exits a yard or predefined safe area.
- Best effort indoor or urban locating using cell tower fallback when GPS reception is limited.
- Lightweight monitoring for boarding, pet sitters, or light commercial pet care services.
- Reviewing past movement and routes to understand roaming patterns or recurring behaviors.
