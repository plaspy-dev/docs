# GOTOP - T1600 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GOTOP T1600 and how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It describes the tracker’s practical functions such as real time tracking, motion alerts, long battery life, waterproofing, and notification channels as they appear in Plaspy’s tracking and fleet management workflows.

Exact behavior and available functions depend on device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific operational details, firmware change notes, and the most current specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The T1600 is a bicycle focused GPS tracker engineered for long deployments and anti theft protection, designed to feed position and event data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and alerting. It emphasizes low maintenance operation, outdoor durability, and multiple notification paths so owners and fleet managers can monitor bikes and assets in real time.

- Real time GPS tracking and history route playback for continuous location awareness and incident review
- Configurable motion and tamper alerts with immediate notifications via SMS, web platform, and mobile app
- Long life internal rechargeable battery with very low standby consumption and support for dynamo charging during rides
- IPX7 waterproof enclosure and rugged design suited for outdoor bicycle mounting and long term deployments
- Visible high intensity LEDs for deterrent signaling and improved rider visibility when alerts occur

## Core Features of GOTOP - T1600

- U‑BLOX 50 channel GNSS module for consistent position fixes under typical outdoor conditions
- MTK GPRS chipset with support for GPRS reporting and SMS as a redundant reporting channel
- Built in vibration and motion sensor with configurable tamper and motion alerting
- Internal rechargeable 5200mAh battery and extremely low power consumption yielding long standby periods
- IPX7 rated waterproof enclosure for reliable outdoor use in wet conditions
- Support for Flower Drum Dynamo charging to sustain power during long rides
- Rear and side visible high intensity LEDs to act as deterrents and status indicators
- Notification support across SMS, the web platform, and mobile app with Google Maps links for location sharing

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the T1600’s position reports and events feed into centralized maps and dashboards so operators can monitor assets, respond to incidents, and review history. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and accepts reporting over common transport methods to place device data into fleet views and alerting rules.

- Live location and periodic position updates appear on Plaspy maps and device lists for immediate tracking
- Motion and tamper alerts generate notifications in Plaspy dashboards and can be routed via the platform notification channels
- Battery and device status reports allow operators to set low battery alerts and plan maintenance for long deployments
- History route playback from the device is available in Plaspy for incident investigation and route analysis
- GPRS reporting combined with SMS redundancy helps maintain continuity of telemetry into Plaspy in varied coverage conditions

## Typical Use Cases

- Bicycle anti theft protection with motion alerts, visible deterrent LEDs, and rapid notifications to owners or fleet managers
- Rental bike fleet monitoring to track usage, location history, and battery status across distributed assets
- Long distance touring and endurance rides where dynamo charging and long standby extend operational time between mains charges
- Outdoor asset monitoring for equipment that requires waterproofing and extended battery life
- Shared location and incident response workflows for ride support and recovery operations
- Usage analytics and route review to inform maintenance planning and operational decisions

## Feature Availability Notes

- Exact feature set and alert behavior can vary by firmware version and hardware revision; manufacturer updates may add or change functionality
- Installation choices and mounting location can affect GPS reception, motion sensor sensitivity, and overall device performance
- Regional cellular band support and GPRS behavior depend on the device variant shipped for specific markets
- Some notification features rely on configuration both on the device and within Plaspy to ensure alerts and links display as intended
- Always review the latest GOTOP documentation and firmware notes for details about supported behaviors and configuration options

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the T1600 with Plaspy centralizes location and event data into a single fleet management environment, making it easier to monitor distributed bikes and outdoor assets, respond to tamper or low battery alerts, and analyze historical routes. Plaspy’s dashboards and alerting capabilities turn the T1600’s telemetry into operational insight for theft prevention, fleet oversight, and maintenance planning.
