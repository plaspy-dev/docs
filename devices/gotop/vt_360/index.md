# GOTOP - VT-360

![VT-360](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-360 is a GPS/GSM/GPRS tracking device designed for vehicle real-time tracking and fleet management. With its superior GPS and GPRS modules, the VT-360 offers excellent sensitivity and stable performance, ensuring accurate GPS fix even in remote areas. This device is perfect for businesses or individuals looking to track their vehicles and manage their fleet effectively.

One of the standout features of the VT-360 is its ability to support SMS link with Google Maps. This means that you can easily track your vehicle's location by simply sending an SMS and receiving a link to Google Maps with the exact location. Additionally, you can track your vehicle in real-time using SMS or GPRS, allowing you to monitor its movements and ensure its safety.

The VT-360 also offers various alarm features to enhance security and provide peace of mind. It has an SOS alarm, geo-fence alarm, GPS blind area alarm, low battery alarm, overspeed alarm, and even an engine cut feature that allows you to remotely stop the engine. This device also supports wiretapping and remote listening, giving you the ability to monitor the surroundings of your vehicle.

In terms of connectivity, the VT-360 has 2 inputs and 2 outputs, providing flexibility for additional accessories or functionalities. It also has an analog input for temperature or fuel sensor monitoring and detection, allowing you to keep track of important parameters. With its internal backup battery, the VT-360 ensures continuous operation even in the event of a power failure.
