# GOTOP - VT-360

![VT-360](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-360 is a vehicle GPS/GSM/GPRS tracking device designed for real time tracking and fleet management. It combines high sensitivity GPS and stable GPRS communication to provide accurate location fixes even in more remote areas. The VT-360 supports location queries via SMS with a direct Google Maps link and continuous tracking by SMS or GPRS, and includes a set of alarm functions such as SOS, geofence alerts, GPS blind area alert, low battery warning, overspeed alarm, and an engine cut capability. Additional features include remote listening, two inputs and two outputs, an analog input for monitoring parameters like temperature or fuel, and an internal backup battery for continued operation during power interruptions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-360 can feed its position and alarm information into the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility and fleet oversight. Plaspy can receive the VT-360 location updates and alerts to display them on maps, consolidate events across a fleet, and apply platform level reporting and notification workflows. Compatibility makes the VT-360 a practical option for organizations that want the device capabilities combined with Plaspy’s monitoring, alerting, and reporting environment.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity GPS and stable GPRS connectivity for reliable location reporting in varied environments
- Real time tracking options via SMS or GPRS plus SMS link to Google Maps for quick location access
- Comprehensive alarm set including SOS, geofence, GPS blind area, overspeed, and low battery alerts
- Remote engine cut feature and remote listening capability for enhanced security and control
- Two digital inputs and two outputs plus an analog input for temperature or fuel monitoring
- Internal backup battery to maintain reporting during vehicle power interruptions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the VT-360 becomes part of a centralized tracking workflow where device reports are visualized, processed, and archived for operational use. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it actionable through the platform’s monitoring and management tools.

- View live and recent VT-360 positions on Plaspy maps for single vehicles or entire fleets
- Receive and manage VT-360 alarm events in Plaspy such as SOS, geofence breaches, overspeed, and low battery
- Use historical tracks and event logs for route review, incident investigation, and compliance reporting
- Monitor input and analog values reported by the VT-360 to track accessories or important vehicle parameters
- Send remote commands supported by the device through Plaspy when configured, for example to control outputs or trigger engine cut where permitted

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small and medium transport or delivery operations
- Rental car and vehicle monitoring to track location and receive alerts
- Security and recovery monitoring for company vehicles and high value assets
- Service and field operations where location history and geofence alerts aid dispatch and oversight
- Situations requiring remote alerts and limited remote control such as engine disable or accessory management
