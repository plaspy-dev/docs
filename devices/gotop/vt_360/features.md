# GOTOP - VT-360 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the GOTOP VT-360 as used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant for vehicle tracking and fleet management and explains how those capabilities map to visibility and monitoring inside the Plaspy platform.

The VT-360 is described as a GPS/GSM/GPRS tracker with strong GPS and GPRS modules, support for SMS link to Google Maps, multiple alarm functions, I/O and analog inputs, and an internal backup battery. Exact availability and behavior of individual features can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation, so this page aims to give practical context without replacing the device manual.

## Feature Overview

The VT-360 is designed for real-time vehicle tracking and fleet oversight with a focus on reliable positioning, flexible reporting, and security-related alarms. Its combination of GPS positioning, cellular communications, and configurable inputs makes it suitable for continuous monitoring and event-driven alerts.

- Reliable GPS positioning intended to provide accurate location even in challenging or remote areas.
- Real-time tracking via GPRS and SMS reporting, including an SMS link format for quick access to Google Maps.
- Security and safety alarms such as SOS, geo-fence, GPS blind area, low battery, and overspeed alerts.
- Remote engine cut capability and support for remote listening functions for situational awareness.
- Hardware I/O including two digital inputs, two outputs, and an analog input for temperature or fuel sensor monitoring.
- Internal backup battery to maintain operation during main power interruptions.

## Core Features of GOTOP - VT-360

- GPS positioning combined with GSM GPRS communication for live location reporting and history.
- Dual reporting modes: SMS based location links (including Google Maps link) and GPRS real-time updates.
- Multiple alarm types: SOS alarm, geo-fence alarm, GPS blind area alarm, low battery alarm, and overspeed alarm.
- Remote engine stop functionality to allow authorized immobilization when supported by installation.
- Wiretapping and remote listening capability for remote environmental monitoring where permitted.
- Two digital inputs and two outputs for connection to vehicle signals or accessories.
- One analog input suitable for temperature or fuel sensor monitoring and detection.
- Internal backup battery to preserve tracking and alarm reporting during power loss.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event reports from compatible trackers such as the VT-360 and makes them visible in a centralized interface for monitoring, alerting, and historical review. Where the device reports alarms or I/O state changes, Plaspy surfaces those events so operators can act.

- Real-time positions and location history appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and playback.
- Alarms reported by the device (for example SOS, geo-fence breaches, overspeed, low battery, and engine shutoff) are recorded and available as events and notifications.
- I/O and analog input status reported by the VT-360 can be shown in Plaspy as inputs, allowing monitoring of connected sensors such as temperature or fuel.
- Remote listening or wiretapping events are represented as device-reported events; use of such features must follow applicable laws and policies.
- Plaspy automatically detects the device protocol and processes incoming reports so devices can start reporting without manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet location and route monitoring for light and commercial vehicles in urban and remote environments.
- Rapid location lookup using SMS Google Maps links for managers or drivers without constant data connections.
- Security monitoring with SOS alerts and geo-fence breach notifications for high-value vehicles or assets.
- Remote immobilization workflows for theft response when engine cut is supported and installed.
- Monitoring auxiliary parameters such as temperature or fuel level via the analog input and digital I/O.
- Ensuring continuity of tracking during power interruptions using the device internal backup battery.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Exact alarm behavior and menu options depend on the VT-360 firmware version and the configuration applied by the installer.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect available cellular bands, connectors, or accessory compatibility.
- Some features require proper electrical installation and optional external accessories or sensors to function.
- Remote engine cut and remote listening features may be disabled or restricted by local regulation or by manufacturer configuration.
- Always verify feature sets and configuration procedures against the manufacturer documentation for the specific unit and firmware installed.
