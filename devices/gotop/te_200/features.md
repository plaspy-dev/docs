# GOTOP - TE-200 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP TE-200 mini pet tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device features relevant to Plaspy users, explains practical functions you can expect to see in Plaspy dashboards, and clarifies what aspects depend on firmware and manufacturer choices.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a practical guide to the TE-200 capabilities that are commonly supported and compatible with Plaspy, and consult the manufacturer documentation for device specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The TE-200 is a compact, collar friendly GPS tracker designed for pet monitoring and small asset tracking. It combines GNSS positioning with quad band GSM connectivity to provide continuous location awareness, configurable alerts, and data continuity features that map directly to Plaspy monitoring and alerting workflows.

- Real time location reporting over GPRS and SMS for live tracking and history in Plaspy.
- Compact form factor and lightweight design intended for collars, backpacks, or discreet assets.
- High precision GNSS positioning from a U blox 7 engine for accurate location display.
- Two way voice and voice monitoring for situational awareness around tracked pets.
- Configurable alerts including geo fence, movement, low battery, and speeding notifications.
- Internal log buffer with blind area re upload to preserve history when cellular coverage is lost.

## Core Features of GOTOP - TE-200

- GNSS positioning with U blox 7 for sub 2.5 meter accuracy under typical conditions.
- Quad band GSM GPRS and SMS reporting for both real time data and fallback messaging.
- Two way voice capability and voice monitoring to check surroundings remotely.
- Configurable alarm types such as geo fence, movement detection, low battery and speeding alerts.
- Internal memory buffer for logged points and automatic re upload after coverage gaps.
- IP67 rated enclosure for water and dust protection suitable for outdoor use.
- Rechargeable 3.7 V 900 mAh Li ion battery with low standby current for extended uptime.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TE-200 reports position, event and battery data so operators can monitor status, respond to alerts, and review history. Plaspy surfaces those device updates on maps, timelines and alert feeds to support everyday tracking workflows.

- Real time positions and telemetry appear on Plaspy maps and device timelines for location awareness.
- Geo fence, movement, low battery and speeding events are forwarded to Plaspy for alerts and historical logging.
- Voice monitoring and two way voice events are usable alongside Plaspy for verification and situational checks.
- Logged points stored during coverage gaps are re uploaded and reflected in Plaspy history to avoid tracking gaps.
- Standard coordinate formats and map links simplify locating devices and sharing positions from Plaspy.
