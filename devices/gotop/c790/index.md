# GOTOP - C790

![C790](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP C790 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for a range of car models. It combines GPS and Beidou satellite positioning with GPRS communication to deliver real time location tracking. The device includes practical vehicle security and monitoring features such as remote fuel cut control, ACC detection, anti detection capability, multiple alarm types, an SOS button, and up to 180 days of driving history review.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the C790 can feed its location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. Plaspy can use the tracker data to display position history, manage alerts, and support fleet level visibility, making the C790 a suitable choice for users who want to integrate vehicle level controls and security events into a broader tracking platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for many vehicle models and discreet installation
- Dual satellite positioning with GPRS communication for real time location updates
- Remote fuel cut capability to control vehicle oil and power via remote command
- Built in ACC detection plus anti detection functionality for enhanced monitoring
- Multiple alarms including vibration, over speed, power failure, and SOS emergency
- Geofence electric fence alerts and up to 180 days of driving history storage

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GOTOP C790 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous location and event reporting to a centralized fleet platform. Once configured, Plaspy can visualize positions on a map, record histories, and use device events to trigger notifications or operational workflows.

- Real time location visibility and map tracking inside the Plaspy platform
- Event and alarm forwarding for vibration, overspeed, power loss, and SOS alerts
- Geofence alerts sent to Plaspy when a vehicle enters or exits predefined areas
- Historical route playback and driving history review stored for analysis
- Centralized fleet monitoring to view multiple C790 units and their statuses

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal vehicle security and theft deterrence with remote fuel cut and SOS
- Fleet vehicle monitoring for small to medium sized commercial operators
- Rental car oversight to track usage patterns and respond to alarms
- Delivery or service vehicle tracking to improve route visibility and accountability
- Vehicle recovery support through real time position reporting after an incident
