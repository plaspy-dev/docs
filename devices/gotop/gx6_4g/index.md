# GOTOP - GX6-4G

<div className="device-logo">
![GX6-4G](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GX6-4G is a vehicle focused 4G GPS tracker designed for fleet management and reliable real time tracking. Built for rental cars, taxis, trucks and passenger vehicles, the unit offers multi mode positioning including GPS, BeiDou and LBS, plus vehicle grade inputs and outputs that make it suitable for continuous fleet monitoring and operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GX6-4G streams location and vehicle telemetry into the Plaspy platform so operators can leverage live maps, event alerts and historical reports. Its fleet oriented features such as ignition detection, remote immobilization options, geo fencing and remote audio or door control can be surfaced in Plaspy to support anti theft workflows and day to day fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for reliable real time tracking across fleets and individual vehicles.
- Multi mode positioning with GPS and BeiDou plus LBS fallback for precise location and route playback.
- Vehicle grade inputs and outputs including ignition detection, digital I O and remote power or fuel cut off for operational control.
- Designed for vehicle environments with wide DC input range and an optional internal backup battery for tolerant installations.
- Industrial metal housing with support for external antennas and environmental tolerance for demanding fleet use.
- CAN bus and serial telemetry channels to aggregate vehicle signals and telemetry into a unified feed.
- Compact form factor and low sleep current suited to long term discrete vehicle installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GX6-4G delivers continuous location and telemetry to the Plaspy cloud so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in real time and act on event driven alerts. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates, vehicle I O and telemetry streams to provide visibility, reporting and remote control where supported by the unit and local configuration.

- Real time location updates and map tracking with LBS fallback to maintain visibility in varied signal conditions.
- Ignition and digital input reporting to detect start stop events, door status and other vehicle conditions within Plaspy.
- Telemetry and fuel monitoring via vehicle bus channels and voltage detection forwarded to Plaspy for consumption analysis and alerts.
- Remote immobilization and control options available through Plaspy commanded outputs for anti theft and recovery workflows where permitted.
- Audio monitoring and door control events can be activated and logged through Plaspy for security review and incident investigation.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trucks taxis and passenger vehicle operations requiring live tracking and route history.
- Anti theft protection and immobilization for high value vehicles and rental fleets.
- Rental vehicle oversight with ignition and door status monitoring to enforce rental terms.
- Fuel and engine telemetry aggregation for consumption analysis and preventative maintenance planning.
- Security incident review using logged events and remote audio or door control for verification.
