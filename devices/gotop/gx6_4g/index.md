# GOTOP - GX6-4G

![GX6-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The GX6-4G is a robust 4G GPS tracker purpose-built for vehicle fleet management and reliable Plaspy compatible real-time tracking. Designed for rental cars, taxis, trucks and passenger vehicles, the GX6-4G delivers precise multi-mode positioning \(GPS + BeiDou + LBS\), industrial-grade connectivity and vehicle-grade interfaces so you can monitor locations, telemetry and safety events inside the Plaspy platform with confidence.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GX6-4G brings fleet-level anti-theft and operational control to your dashboard: ignition detection, remote power/fuel cut-off, overspeed alerts, geo-fencing and remote audio or door control are all supported by the unit and can be surfaced in Plaspy for actionable alerts, reports and remote interventions.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for reliable real-time tracking across fleets and individual vehicles.
- Multi-mode positioning \(GPS + BeiDou + LBS\) with approximately 5 m accuracy for precise location and route playback.
- Vehicle-grade inputs and outputs: ACC detection, digital inputs for door/ignition, speaker/mic for remote audio monitoring, and power/fuel cut-off output for anti-theft/immobilizer control.
- Wide DC input range \(9–45 V\) and optional internal backup battery \(180 mAh\) for tolerant installations across 12 V and 24 V vehicles.
- Industrial design with metal housing, external GNSS and cellular antenna support, and temperature and humidity tolerance for harsh vehicle environments.
- Multiple CAN bus channels and UART-RS232 for telematics integration and telemetry data retrieval such as fuel monitoring and engine signals.
- Compact form factor and low sleep current for discrete, long-term vehicle installations and continuous fleet monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GX6-4G streams real-time location and vehicle telemetry to the Plaspy cloud using its 4G cellular link. Plaspy ingests GNSS \(GPS/BeiDou\) coordinates plus vehicle inputs, CAN bus data and alarm events to present live tracking, event-driven alerts and historical reports for fleet management and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GNSS coordinates and LBS fallback provide continuous tracking on Plaspy maps.
- Ignition and digital I/O status: ACC detection and dedicated digital inputs report start/stop, door and light events to Plaspy.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry: CAN bus channels and DC detection enable fuel level, voltage and engine data to be forwarded to Plaspy for analysis.
- Remote immobilizer and control: Remote power/fuel cut-off output supports immobilization workflows via Plaspy command control \(subject to local regulations and installer configuration\).
- Audio and door control: Remote audio monitoring \(speaker/mic\) and remote door open/close control can be activated and logged through Plaspy for security and incident review.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real-time tracking, route playback and telematics aggregation for trucks, taxis and passenger vehicles.
- Anti-theft and immobilization: remote power/fuel cut-off combined with geo-fencing and power-off alarms to protect high-value or rental vehicles.
- Rental and credit-controlled vehicles: ACC detection, door status and remote control features to enforce rental terms and recover assets.
- Fuel monitoring and diagnostics: CAN bus integration and DC detection feed fuel and voltage telemetry into Plaspy for consumption analysis and maintenance alerts.
- Incident review and security: remote audio monitoring and door control help verify events and support security workflows when integrated with Plaspy logs and alerts.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GX6-4G combines vehicle-grade hardware with full compatibility for Plaspy’s platform to deliver dependable, actionable fleet intelligence. Its multi-mode GNSS positioning, broad cellular band support and robust I/O make it suitable for mixed fleets and challenging installation environments. By pairing GX6-4G hardware with Plaspy you get real-time tracking, telemetry-rich reports and remote control capabilities—enabling improved utilization, theft prevention and faster incident response without compromising reliability.

Note on sensors: the GX6-4G provides extensive CAN, UART and digital I/O for telemetry and external sensors. While the device itself does not include Bluetooth sensors, Plaspy can combine tracker data with Bluetooth sensor feeds at the platform level when a compatible Bluetooth gateway or integrated device is present.
