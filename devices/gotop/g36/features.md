# GOTOP - G36 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP G36 car charger GPS tracker and how it integrates with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on the practical capabilities and operational value the G36 brings when used together with Plaspy for mapping, alerts, route history, and fleet oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and how the manufacturer configures the unit. Review manufacturer documentation and local device settings for the precise feature set available on a specific G36 unit before deploying it at scale.

## Feature Overview

The G36 is a compact in-vehicle unit that combines continuous location reporting with dual USB fast charging. It is intended as a plug and play option for vehicles that need location visibility without complex installation, while also keeping driver devices powered.

- Real time GPS and BeiDou positioning with supplemental WiFi and LBS for improved urban performance
- Dual 2.1A USB ports that supply device charging while the tracker remains powered and reporting
- Alarm capabilities including geofence events, power failure, low battery, vibration detection, and offline notifications
- Cellular connectivity via Micro SIM with LTE FDD and GSM band support for wide area coverage
- Small backup battery and built in antenna to allow short power interruption reporting and simpler installation

## Core Features of GOTOP - G36

- GNSS positioning using GPS and BeiDou for primary location fixes
- Supplemental WiFi and LBS positioning to improve fixes in urban canyon areas
- Dual USB outputs rated at 2.1A each for simultaneous device charging
- Alarm suite including electronic fence, power failure, low battery, vibration, and offline alerts
- Micro SIM based cellular modem with LTE FDD and GSM compatibility for network reporting
- Built in backup battery (50 mAh, 3.7V) for brief reporting during power loss
- Compact plug in form factor sized for vehicle power outlets with a small ABS enclosure
- Built in antenna and reported chipset used by the manufacturer for device operation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the G36 location and event streams to provide live tracking, historical playback, and alerting. The platform presents device status and events in map and reporting interfaces to support operational decision making and incident response.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps with historical route playback for review
- Geofence events delivered to Plaspy to trigger alerts and workflow actions for operators
- Power failure and low battery notifications surfaced in Plaspy to indicate possible tampering or power issues
- Vibration and offline alarms shown in Plaspy to help detect unauthorized movement or connectivity loss
- Plaspy can combine G36 location streams with other telematics inputs where available to build richer fleet telemetry and reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Small to medium fleet vehicle tracking where rapid deployment and simple installation are priorities
- Anti theft monitoring for cars and motorcycles using geofence and vibration alerts
- Service, delivery, and courier vehicles needing a charger for driver devices and continuous position reporting
- Rental, dealer loaner, and shared vehicle scenarios where easy plug in tracking reduces setup time
- 12–24V vehicle coverage for cars, motorcycles, and light commercial vehicles that benefit from a compact tracker

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version and manufacturer configuration can change how alarms and positioning modes behave
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may affect supported cellular bands and accessory features
- Installation type and vehicle electrical environment can influence backup battery performance and power related alarms
- Supplemental WiFi and LBS positioning are environment dependent and provide best results in populated urban areas
- Confirm capability set, supported bands, and alarm behavior against the manufacturer documentation for the exact unit you have
