# GOTOP - G36

<div className="device-logo">
![G36](./tracker.png)
</div>

The G36 is a compact car charger GPS tracker designed for in vehicle use. It combines continuous location reporting with a dual USB fast charger, allowing the device to plug into a vehicle power outlet and provide both charging and tracking functions. Positioning is provided by GPS and BeiDou and is supplemented by WiFi and LBS in urban environments, while built in cellular connectivity relays location and event data to external platforms.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G36 feeds real time position, alarm events and device status into the Plaspy platform for mapping, alerts, route history and reporting. That combination of convenient on board charging and persistent tracking makes the G36 a practical option for fleets, dealers and individual vehicle owners who want basic anti theft monitoring and operational oversight integrated with Plaspy services.

## Key Highlights

- Dual USB fast charging ports for driver device power alongside continuous vehicle tracking.
- Multi mode positioning using GPS and BeiDou with WiFi and LBS to improve urban fixes.
- Configurable alarm set including geofence, power failure, low battery, vibration and offline alerts.
- Cellular connectivity via Micro SIM for wide area reporting and integration with Plaspy.
- Small backup battery and built in antenna for short interruptions in vehicle power.
- Compact plug in form factor that combines charging convenience with telemetry capability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected in a vehicle, the G36 collects GNSS position data and augments it with WiFi and LBS where GNSS signals are less effective. The device sends location updates and event notifications over the cellular network to Plaspy, which presents live location, alerts, journey playback and reports through its monitoring interface.

- Live location updates and historical route playback presented in Plaspy for visibility and analysis.
- Geofence events delivered to Plaspy to trigger alerts and workflow actions for operators.
- Power loss and low battery notifications routed to Plaspy to highlight potential tampering or maintenance needs.
- Vibration and offline alarms reported to Plaspy to help detect unauthorized movement or connectivity loss.
- Device status and event streams used by Plaspy to support fleet monitoring, notifications and basic reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Small to medium fleet tracking where simple deployment and driver device charging are priorities.
- Anti theft monitoring for passenger cars and light commercial vehicles with geofence and vibration alerts.
- Service and delivery vehicles that benefit from onboard charging plus continuous location visibility.
- Owners and dealers seeking a low profile tracking accessory that combines power and telemetry.
- Vehicle coverage across common 12 to 24 volt platforms where a compact plug in tracker is preferred.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G36 is a practical choice for organizations that need dependable position reporting and alarm events without complex installation. Its car charger form factor reduces installation time while keeping driver devices powered, and the device supplies the core location and event data Plaspy uses for mapping, alerts and route history.

Plaspy complements the G36 by collecting the device streams and integrating them into fleet dashboards, alerting rules and reporting. For operations that start with location first and later add richer telemetry, pairing G36 units with Plaspy provides a straightforward path to scale monitoring and operational oversight.
