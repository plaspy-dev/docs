# G36 Car Charger GPS Tracker

The G36 is a compact car charger GPS tracker that combines dependable Plaspy compatible real-time tracking with the convenience of a dual USB fast charger. Designed for in-vehicle use, the G36 plugs into the vehicle power supply and delivers continuous location reporting via GPS and BeiDou, supplemented by WiFi and LBS positioning in urban environments. Its two 2.1A USB ports keep driver devices powered while the unit records movements and triggers configurable alarms for security and fleet oversight.

The G36 is ideal where space and simplicity matter: a single accessory provides both charging and tracking for cars, motorcycles and other 12–24V vehicles. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the G36 feeds real-time position and event data to the Plaspy platform for mapping, alerts, route history and reporting — helping fleets, dealers and vehicle owners improve anti-theft protection and telemetry-driven operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering real-time tracking and map visualization for fleet management and individual vehicles.
- Dual USB fast-charging ports \(2.1A each\) integrate device charging with continuous in-vehicle tracking — one compact unit for power and security.
- Multi-mode positioning with GPS, BeiDou \(BD\), WiFi and LBS for reliable fixes in both open-sky and urban canyon environments.
- Comprehensive alarm suite: electronic fence \(geofence\), power failure, low battery, vibration and offline alarms to support anti-theft workflows.
- Wide cellular compatibility: LTE-FDD and GSM support across multiple global bands for broad network coverage using a Micro SIM.
- Small built-in backup battery \(50 mAh, 3.7V\) and built-in antenna allow continued reporting during short power interruptions.
- Compact, lightweight ABS enclosure \(85 × 49.2 × 26 mm, 41 g\) that plugs directly into the vehicle power outlet for easy installation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed in a vehicle, the G36 continuously acquires GNSS position data and supplements it with WiFi and LBS where GNSS quality may be reduced. Location, alarm events and device status are transmitted over the cellular network to the Plaspy platform in near real-time. Plaspy ingests the G36 data to present live location, journey playback, geofence alerts and configurable notifications via web and mobile interfaces.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and historical route playback.
- Geofence \(electronic fence\) events delivered to Plaspy for immediate alerts and actionable workflows.
- Power failure and low battery alarms reported to Plaspy to flag potential tampering or power issues.
- Vibration and offline alarms help detect unauthorized movement and loss of connectivity.
- Micro SIM-based cellular communications enable wide-area coverage and integration with Plaspy’s alerting and reporting system.

Note on extended telemetry: Plaspy supports additional vehicle telemetry \(fuel monitoring, ignition state, immobilizer control, and Bluetooth sensors\) when those data sources are available. The G36 supplies robust location and alarm data directly; where richer CAN-bus or sensor feeds are required, Plaspy can combine G36 location streams with other integrated telematics inputs to deliver full fleet telemetry and vehicle control features.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the G36 as your Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivers practical value: you get dependable GPS/BD positioning and multi-mode location \(WiFi/LBS\) wrapped in a compact car charger form factor that reduces installation time and keeps driver devices powered. For fleet managers focused on real-time tracking, anti-theft protection and straightforward deployment, the G36 provides the core location and alarm data Plaspy needs to drive notifications, route analytics and compliance reporting.

While the G36 concentrates on robust position reporting, alarms and charging convenience, Plaspy can extend its capabilities through integrations — combining G36 location data with additional telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition state or immobilizer control and Bluetooth sensors when those data sources are present in a vehicle. This flexible approach lets operations scale: use the G36 for location-first deployments, and layer in richer vehicle telemetry across your fleet as required.
