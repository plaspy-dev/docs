# GOTOP - D08

<div className="device-logo">
![D08](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP D08 is a compact 4G OBD GPS tracker designed for vehicle location and monitoring. It supports both 4G LTE and 2G frequency bands and combines multiple positioning methods including GPS, BeiDou, WiFi, and LBS to provide location awareness. The device also includes a range of alarm modes such as electronic fence, power failure, low battery, vibration, and offline alerts, and it supports real-time tracking as well as historical route playback.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the D08 can be integrated into fleet and asset monitoring workflows to provide vehicle visibility within the Plaspy platform. Its small footprint and monitoring features make it a practical option for organizations and individuals who need continuous location data, alerts, and playback capabilities accessible through Plaspy for operational oversight and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- 4G and 2G connectivity for broad network reach and consistent transmission
- Multiple positioning methods (GPS, BD, WiFi, LBS) for improved location accuracy
- Variety of alarm modes including geofence, power loss, low battery, vibration, and offline alerts
- Supports real-time tracking and historical route playback for operational review
- Compact, lightweight design with durable ABS shell for discreet vehicle fitting
- Built-in battery for temporary backup and continuous tracking during power events

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GOTOP D08 feeds location and event information into the platform so teams can monitor vehicles from a central interface. Plaspy can process the tracker's location updates and alert signals to support routine oversight and incident handling.

- Display live vehicle positions on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility
- Receive and manage alarm notifications triggered by the tracker through Plaspy
- Review historical routes and playback trips for auditing and analysis
- Use Plaspy reporting tools to extract movement summaries and activity logs
- Configure geofence monitoring and see fence events recorded in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch oversight for small to medium vehicle fleets
- Rental car monitoring and return tracking with alerting capabilities
- Personal vehicle tracking for security and location history
- Logistics and route verification to confirm deliveries and driver routes
- Asset monitoring where discreet, vehicle-mounted tracking is required
