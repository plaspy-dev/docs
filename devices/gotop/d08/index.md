# GOTOP - D08

![D08](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP D08-4G is a 4G OBD GPS tracker that offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. With support for both 4G LTE and 2G frequency bands, this tracker ensures seamless connectivity and compatibility with various networks. Whether you need real-time tracking or historical route playback, the D08-4G has got you covered.

One of the standout features of the D08-4G is its support for multiple positioning methods, including GPS, BD, WIFI, and LBS. This allows for precise and accurate location tracking, ensuring that you always know the whereabouts of your vehicle. Additionally, the tracker offers various alarm modes such as electronic fence, power failure alarm, low battery alarm, vibration alarm, and offline alarm, providing comprehensive security and peace of mind.

The D08-4G is designed with convenience in mind. It features a compact and lightweight design, measuring just 51mm x 47mm x 23mm and weighing only 54 grams. This makes it easy to install and conceal, ensuring that it doesn't interfere with the vehicle's aesthetics. The shell is made of durable ABS plastic, providing protection against wear and tear.

Technical Specifications:

- Power supply mode: Battery power supply
- Operating voltage range: DC 9V - 35V
- Working current: 12V/73.6MA
- Sleep current: 12V/38.2MA
- Built-in battery capacity: 100 mAh \(3.7V polymer battery\)
- Communication module brand/chip model: SIMcom7670E, SIMcom7670SA
- Communication frequency band: LTE/4G: B1/B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8/B28/B66, GSM/EDGE/2G: 850/900/1800/1900MHz
- SIM card: Micro SIM card
- Communication Antenna: Built-in Antenna

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the GOTOP D08-4G is an excellent choice for individuals and businesses looking to enhance the security and tracking capabilities of their vehicles. Whether you need to monitor a fleet of vehicles or keep an eye on your personal car, this GPS tracker delivers the functionality and peace of mind you need.
