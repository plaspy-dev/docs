# GOTOP - D05 Features

This page documents the public feature context for using the GOTOP D05 tracker with Plaspy. It explains the device capabilities that Plaspy can display, monitor, and record, focusing on user facing features like location reporting, event alarms, and long standby operation that matter for fleet and asset management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, the chosen installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. Where relevant, this page highlights practical capabilities while encouraging verification of current device and firmware details from the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP D05 is a compact, magnet-mountable 4G GPS tracker designed for long-term unattended tracking and discreet vehicle or asset placement. It pairs extended battery life with high-accuracy multi constellation positioning and secure telemetry, making it suitable for fleet tracking, asset protection, and remote monitoring when used with Plaspy.

- Ultra long standby operation enabled by a high capacity internal battery for reduced maintenance cycles
- Global 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain telemetry in mixed coverage areas
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning including GPS and Beidou for faster fixes and sub 5 meter accuracy in many conditions
- Compact magnet mount design for discreet placement on vehicles and assets
- On device movement and light sensor alarms to detect tampering or unexpected motion
- Optional Wi Fi assistance available to improve first fix times in supported environments

## Core Features of GOTOP - D05

- Long duration internal battery ideal for months of unattended monitoring
- 4G LTE primary connectivity with 2G fallback for resilient data reporting
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning for accurate location fixes
- Magnet mount form factor for hidden or rapid installs on metal surfaces
- Moving alarm for event driven notifications when movement is detected
- Light sensor alarm for tamper or cover detection events
- Optional Wi Fi assistance to speed time to first fix in urban settings
- Encrypted data transmission to protect position and event data in transit

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the D05 is integrated with Plaspy, its location updates and alarm events are visible in the platform for monitoring, alerts, and historical review. Plaspy ingests the tracker telemetry so operations and security teams can use mapping, notifications, and reports to maintain situational awareness.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps and live views for fleet and asset monitoring
- Movement and light sensor alarms are forwarded to Plaspy for configurable notifications and alert workflows
- Historical routes and playback let teams review past movements and reconstruct events
- Optional Wi Fi assistance and multi constellation fixes can improve position reliability shown inside Plaspy
- Secure encrypted transmission aligns device telemetry with Plaspy dashboards and history retention

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet anti theft tracking for parked vehicles and seasonal equipment
- Long term asset tracking for trailers, containers, and stored machinery
- Security monitoring of parked or stored assets using light and movement alarms
- Remote field asset tracking where long battery life reduces maintenance visits
- Fleet oversight and route history review for operational planning and incident analysis

Combining the GOTOP D05 with Plaspy provides a straightforward path to continuous visibility, event driven alerts, and historical tracking for assets and vehicles.
