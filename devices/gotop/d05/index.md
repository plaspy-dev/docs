# GOTOP - D05

<div className="device-logo">
![D05](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP D05 is a smart 4G GPS tracker designed for long term asset, vehicle, and personal tracking. It pairs a compact magnet mount enclosure with a high capacity 10000mAh battery to deliver extended standby operation and reliable real time location updates. The device combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with optional Wi Fi assistance to speed fixes and improve urban accuracy, and supports encrypted data transmission for secure telemetry.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D05 is ready to integrate with Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and history playback through Plaspy web and mobile interfaces. Its continuous LTE connectivity with 2G fallback and built in alarms make it a practical option for fleet managers, security teams, and asset owners who want durable, low maintenance tracking hardware that feeds location and event data into Plaspy dashboards and reports.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker built for long standby and continuous real time tracking
- Large 10000mAh battery for extended unattended operation and reduced maintenance cycles
- Multi constellation GNSS with optional Wi Fi assistance for fast fixes and high positional accuracy
- Global 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback for consistent telemetry across broad coverage areas
- Compact magnetic mounting for discreet placement on vehicles and assets
- On device movement and light sensor alarms for immediate event detection
- Encrypted data transmission for secure integration with Plaspy dashboards and alerts

## How It Works with Plaspy

The D05 sends encrypted position fixes and event packets to Plaspy where that data appears in live maps, dashboards, and historical reports. Once onboarded into Plaspy, the device becomes a source of location and alarm data that can be used for operational oversight, notifications, and record keeping.

- Real time location updates and live map visibility within Plaspy
- Movement and light sensor alarms forwarded to Plaspy for instant notifications
- Historical route recording and playback for incident review and analysis
- Geofence monitoring and event driven alerts configured in Plaspy
- Consolidation of D05 telemetry with other fleet data inside Plaspy reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet anti theft tracking for parked vehicles and seasonal equipment with long standby needs
- Long term asset tracking for trailers, containers, and high value equipment
- Security monitoring of stored or idle assets using movement and light alarms
- Remote field operations where robust cellular coverage and fallback are required
- Fleet oversight for mixed vehicle inventories needing continuous position and history data
