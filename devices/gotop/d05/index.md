# GOTOP - D05

![D05](./tracker.jpg)

The Smart GPS Tracker D05 is a Plaspy compatible, 4G global location device engineered for long-term asset, vehicle, and personal tracking. With a high-capacity 10000mAh battery and a compact, magnet-mountable design, the D05 delivers ultra-long standby and reliable real-time tracking for fleet management and anti-theft applications. Built on an ASR1603 cellular platform and a ZKMicro GNSS module, the D05 combines multi-constellation positioning and optional Wi‑Fi assistance to give fast fixes and sub-5 meter accuracy when you need it.

The D05 is ready to integrate with Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and history playback via a web portal or mobile app. Its encrypted data transmission and support for 4G LTE with 2G fallback provide consistent telemetry suitable for enterprise deployments. Whether you are protecting high-value equipment, monitoring trailers, or adding discreet vehicle anti-theft capability to your fleet, the D05 is a durable, low-maintenance GPS tracker that works smoothly with Plaspy’s mapping and reporting features.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker built for long standby and continuous real-time tracking.
- Ultra-long 10000mAh battery for extended unattended operation and fewer maintenance cycles.
- High-accuracy GNSS: GPS + Beidou positioning with fast cold/hot start performance.
- Global 4G LTE with GSM/EDGE 2G fallback for reliable data transmission across wide areas.
- Compact, strong magnetic mounting for discreet installation on vehicles and assets.
- On-device alarms: moving alarm and light sensor alarm provide immediate event detection.
- Secure, encrypted data transfer for safe integration with Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The D05 transmits encrypted GNSS position fixes and status events over LTE \(with 2G fallback\) to Plaspy, where the data appears in real-time on maps, dashboards, and reports. Plaspy ingests location, movement, and alarm packets from the D05 so fleet managers and security teams can set geofences, receive push or SMS alerts, and review historical tracks. Setup is straightforward using the device’s SIM-enabled connectivity and Plaspy’s device onboarding tools.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates visible in Plaspy maps and live views
- Movement and light sensor alarms forwarded to Plaspy for instant notifications
- Historical routes and playback for post-incident review and route analysis
- Optional Wi‑Fi assistance improves first fix times and urban accuracy
- Encrypted data transmission for secure integration with Plaspy’s platform

- Fleet anti-theft and recovery — discreet vehicle placement with long standby makes the D05 ideal for parked vehicles and seasonal equipment.
- Asset tracking for trailers, containers, and high-value equipment that require multi-month unattended operation and reliable GNSS accuracy.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The D05 balances long endurance, accurate multi-constellation GNSS, and contemporary 4G connectivity to provide a dependable Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for enterprises and security-conscious users. Its 10000mAh battery reduces maintenance cycles and downtime, while the compact magnetic housing simplifies discreet installs for anti-theft scenarios. When paired with Plaspy, the D05 becomes part of a scalable fleet management and monitoring solution, delivering real-time tracking, history playback, and event-driven alerts.

Beyond core location data, integrating the D05 with Plaspy lets you consolidate telemetry across your operation. While the D05 supplies continuous position and alarm data, Plaspy can combine those inputs with other vehicle telemetry — such as ignition, fuel monitoring, immobilizer status, and Bluetooth sensors — when those signals exist in your fleet architecture. That flexibility makes the D05 a practical building block for modern fleet management, asset protection, and remote security systems.
