# GOTOP - TK-208

![TK-208](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TK-208 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for reliable location reporting. It uses GPS satellite positioning together with cellular reporting methods to send longitude and latitude coordinates to a phone via SMS or to a remote server for online display. The device is suitable for personal use, vehicles, and pets, and includes practical features such as voice monitoring, geo fencing, low battery alerts, movement and vibration alarms, and a sleeping mode for power efficiency.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK-208 can feed position updates and alarms into the Plaspy platform for consolidated monitoring and reporting. Its support for server reporting and SMS makes it straightforward to integrate into Plaspy workflows, and the long standby life and optional magnetic attachment make it useful for deployments that need low maintenance and flexible placement. Plaspy can use the tracker data to provide visibility, alerts, and historical location reporting for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Personal GPS tracker suitable for people, vehicles, and pets
- Sends location via SMS or to a server using cellular data reporting
- Long standby battery life for reduced maintenance intervals
- Geo fencing and multiple alarm types for location based alerts
- Voice monitoring feature to listen to the tracker surroundings
- Optional strong magnet for secure attachment to metal surfaces

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TK-208 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide live location updates and event alerts alongside other devices in your fleet or asset inventory. Plaspy ingests the position and alarm messages sent by the tracker and maps them into dashboards and reports that support operational decisions.

- Receive location updates in Plaspy from the device when it transmits via server reporting
- Convert geo fence triggers and alarms into Plaspy notifications and alert workflows
- View historical tracks and recent position points in Plaspy mapping and reporting tools
- Use low battery, movement, and vibration alarms to trigger maintenance or operational responses
- Combine TK-208 data with other device data in Plaspy for consolidated monitoring and oversight
