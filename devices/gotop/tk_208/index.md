# GOTOP - TK-208

![TK-208](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TK-208 is a personal GPS tracker that offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. It works based on the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites, making it suitable for personal, vehicle, and pet tracking. With this tracker, you can easily transmit the longitude and latitude coordinates to your cell phone via SMS, or to a designated server via GPRS. The location can then be displayed using tracking software.

One of the standout features of the TK-208 is its long battery life. With up to 60 days of standby battery life, you can rely on this tracker for extended periods without needing to recharge. Additionally, it offers a strong magnet \(optional\) for easy attachment to metal surfaces, making it ideal for covert tracking applications.

The TK-208 also comes equipped with a range of useful features. It supports tracking via SMS or GPRS, allowing you to choose the method that suits your needs. It also offers voice monitoring, allowing you to listen in on the tracker's surroundings. The geo-fencing alarm feature allows you to set up virtual boundaries and receive alerts when the tracker enters or exits these boundaries. Other features include low battery alarm, vibration alarm, movement alarm, and sleeping mode.

With the TK-208, you also get access to online tracking software and an Android app, making it easy to monitor and track the device from your computer or smartphone. Whether you need to keep an eye on your loved ones, track your vehicle, or monitor your pets, the GOTOP TK-208 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that can meet your needs.
