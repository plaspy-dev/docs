# GOTOP - L16 PRO Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GOTOP L16 PRO and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the model's practical functions for real-time location, safety alerts, and health telemetry when the device is integrated into the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability depends on firmware version, hardware revision, local installation choices, and the manufacturer's implementation. Always verify device behavior and supported functions against current manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The L16 PRO is a wearable 4G GPS tracker in a smartwatch form factor designed to deliver continuous location tracking together with health and safety telemetry. When paired with Plaspy, it provides caregivers and operators a single view of location, alerts, and recent device telemetry for monitoring and response.

- Real-time outdoor GPS tracking combined with BLE assisted indoor positioning to improve location continuity.
- Continuous health telemetry streams including PPG heart rate, SpO2, and temperature for trend monitoring.
- Safety functions such as SOS alarm, two-way calling, fall detection, geofence alerts, and low-battery notifications routed into Plaspy.
- Multi-band cellular support for wide regional coverage and roaming where available.
- Compact, water resistant wearable design for daily use and multi-day battery life.
- Server-side storage and optional analytics for short-term historical review and health trend analysis.

## Core Features of GOTOP - L16 PRO

- Integrated GPS for reliable outdoor positioning and regular location updates.
- BLE 5.0 support to augment indoor positioning and connect to nearby beacons or compatible accessories.
- Continuous health sensors: PPG heart rate, SpO2, and an onboard temperature sensor for telemetry collection.
- 3D accelerometer enabling activity awareness and fall detection logic.
- Safety and communication: SOS alarm button and two-way calling for voice communication with caregivers.
- Multi-band cellular connectivity with nano SIM and eSIM compatibility for broad network reach.
- Rugged wearable design with IP66 water and dust resistance and battery designed for multi-day use.
- Dual-CPU architecture and RTOS design for stable operation and efficient power management.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the L16 PRO is integrated into Plaspy, device streams are ingested into the platform to provide visibility, alerting, and historical data review. Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols and maps incoming telemetry into dashboards and rules without requiring device-specific reengineering.

- Live location display and movement history for situational awareness and playback.
- BLE-assisted indoor/outdoor switching reflected in location reports to improve positional accuracy in covered spaces.
- Health telemetry panels and historical charts for heart rate, SpO2, and temperature where the device sends those metrics.
- Immediate routing of SOS alarms, fall events, geofence breaches, and low-battery alerts to Plaspy users and notification channels.
- Short-term server storage and analytics that allow administrators to review recent device behavior and trends.
- Plaspy visibility for voice-capable events such as two-way calls alongside other safety alerts.

## Typical Use Cases

- Assisted living and elderly care monitoring with continuous location and physiological trend visibility.
- Personal safety for vulnerable users where SOS and two-way calling provide rapid response options.
- Remote health telemetry for short-term monitoring and alerting of abnormal readings.
- Indoor/outdoor tracking in campuses, care homes, and buildings using BLE to supplement GPS.
- Lone worker or field personnel safety monitoring where wearables are preferred over vehicle trackers.
- Centralized monitoring of mixed fleets of people and assets within a single Plaspy account.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware determines which telemetry fields and alerts are available and how frequently they are reported.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect supported cellular bands, roaming behavior, and accessory compatibility.
- Installation choices and account configuration on Plaspy can change reporting cadence, alert routing, and storage retention.
- Some features such as advanced analytics or extended storage may depend on backend service options or platform subscriptions.
- Always consult the device user manual and the manufacturer for the most current information about hardware capabilities.
