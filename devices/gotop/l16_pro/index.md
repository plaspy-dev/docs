# GOTOP - L16 PRO

![L16 PRO](./tracker.jpg)

The L16 PRO is a Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker in a smartwatch form factor designed for dependable real time tracking, continuous health telemetry, and everyday safety for seniors and other vulnerable users. It combines outdoor GPS positioning with BLE assisted indoor awareness, onboard sensors for heart rate, SpO2 and temperature, and safety features such as SOS and fall alerts to support personal monitoring and rapid response.

As a device that pushes live location and sensor readings to Plaspy, the L16 PRO becomes part of an operational monitoring setup for caregivers and teams. Plaspy ingests the watch streams to provide dashboards, alerts, geofence rules, and historical reports so administrators can track location, respond to incidents, and review health trends without needing separate systems.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable 4G GPS tracker in a smartwatch format for continuous real time location monitoring.
- Integrated health telemetry including heart rate, SpO2, and temperature for ongoing status visibility.
- Safety oriented features such as SOS alarm, fall detection, two way calling, geofence alerts, and low battery notifications.
- BLE 5.0 assisted indoor positioning to improve accuracy when GPS reception is limited.
- Compact, water resistant design optimized for all day wear and multiple days of battery life.
- Global cellular reach and server side storage with optional analytics to support reporting and trend review.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the L16 PRO streams location and sensor data to the platform where it is turned into actionable information. Plaspy collects the device data and provides configurable visibility, alerting, and reporting tools that suit caregiving, safety, and operational workflows.

- Real time location updates and indoor assisted positioning visible on Plaspy maps and device lists.
- Health telemetry ingestion for trend charts, threshold alerts, and historical review in the Plaspy dashboard.
- SOS, fall, geofence, and low battery events routed to Plaspy alerts so teams or caregivers can be notified immediately.
- Voice and two way call events correlated with device alerts to support response workflows when required.
- Archived device data and analytics available for reporting, audit, and operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Assisted living and elderly care monitoring where continuous location and physiological alerts help caregivers.
- Personal safety and lone worker protection using SOS and geofence rules for remote personnel.
- Short term healthcare telemetry and trend capture to inform caregivers or clinicians.
- Indoor and outdoor personnel tracking in campuses, care facilities, or buildings with mixed reception.
- Unified personnel and asset oversight when wearable trackers complement vehicle or fixed trackers in one Plaspy account.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The L16 PRO pairs wearable convenience with focused health and safety capabilities, making it a practical choice for organizations that need real time location and telemetry for people rather than vehicles. Its combination of GPS, BLE assisted indoor positioning, and multiple physiological sensors supplies the kinds of streams that Plaspy is built to collect, alert on, and present to caregivers and managers.

For teams seeking a compact, user friendly device that feeds into a central monitoring platform, the L16 PRO provides a clear link between wearable telemetry and Plaspy workflows. Plaspy turns those device streams into geofence rules, incident alerts, and historical reports so you can centralize monitoring, response, and analysis across people and assets.
