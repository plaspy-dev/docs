# GOTOP - L16 PRO

![L16 PRO](./tracker.jpg)

The L16 PRO is a Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker in a smartwatch form factor designed for dependable real-time tracking, continuous health telemetry, and everyday safety for seniors and vulnerable users. With multi-band cellular connectivity, integrated GPS, and BLE 5.0 for enhanced indoor positioning, the L16 PRO pairs seamlessly with Plaspy to provide live location, SOS alerts, and sensor-driven health data to your Plaspy dashboard or mobile app.

Engineered around a dual-CPU architecture \(Unisoc W307 Cortex‑A53 plus GR5515 BLE sensor hub\) and an RTOS for stable performance and power efficiency, the L16 PRO is compact, water resistant \(IP66\), and optimized for multi-day battery life. Its suite of PPG heart rate, SpO2, temperature, and 3D accelerometer sensors deliver actionable telemetry to Plaspy for alerting, reporting, and historical review.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker in a smartwatch: reliable outdoor GPS with BLE-assisted indoor positioning for consistent real-time tracking.
- Comprehensive health telemetry: PPG heart rate, SpO2, and temperature sensors for continuous monitoring and trend analysis.
- Safety-first features: two-way calling, SOS alarm, geofence alerts, fall detection and low-battery notifications routed to Plaspy.
- Global cellular reach: multi-band LTE/WCDMA/GSM support with roaming across 130+ countries and 200+ carriers.
- Long battery life and compact design: 680 mAh battery with 3–7 days of use depending on profile; lightweight, water resistant, and wearable all day.
- BLE 5.0 for indoor accuracy and accessory support: enables Bluetooth sensors and beacons to enhance positioning and situational awareness.
- Server-side storage and analytics: device data stored for up to 30 days and optional AI backend for health data analysis.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the L16 PRO becomes a compact GPS tracker and health telemetry node that feeds live and historical data into the Plaspy platform. Location and sensor readings are transmitted over the device’s cellular link; Plaspy ingests that data to provide dashboards, geofence rules, alerting, and reports for administrators and caregivers.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — continuous GPS outdoors with BLE-assisted indoor positioning for accurate indoors/outdoors switching.
- Health and activity telemetry — heart rate, SpO2, and temperature readings, plus activity tracking and fall detection from the 3D accelerometer.
- SOS and safety alerts — immediate SOS alarm transmission, geofence breach alerts, and low-battery notifications to Plaspy users.
- Two-way calling and voice alerts — RTC-capable connectivity for voice communication when required by caregivers or response teams.
- Server storage & analytics — device data is stored up to 30 days and can be enriched by the device’s AI backend for health trend detection.

## Use Cases

- Elderly care and assisted living — continuous location awareness, fall detection and physiological alerts routed to caregivers via Plaspy.
- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring — SOS alarm, two-way calling, and geofence monitoring for remote personnel.
- Healthcare telemetry — remote capture of heart rate, SpO2, and temperature for short-term monitoring and trend detection.
- Indoor/outdoor personnel tracking — BLE 5.0 ensures better indoor positioning in buildings, care homes or campuses when GPS is limited.
- Asset-aware personnel management — track and protect high-risk staff or vulnerable users where a wearable GPS tracker is preferable to vehicle units.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The L16 PRO delivers a focused combination of wearable convenience, medical-grade telemetry, and robust 4G GPS connectivity that makes it an excellent Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for personal safety and monitoring programs. Its dual-CPU design and RTOS stability ensure consistent reporting and power efficiency, while BLE 5.0 provides reliable indoor positioning and support for Bluetooth sensors. Plaspy integration turns device streams into actionable alerts, geofence rules, and historical reports — giving caregivers and managers real-time tracking, telemetry, and the situational awareness they need.

Although the L16 PRO is optimized for people-centric applications \(health monitoring, SOS and fall detection\), integrating it into a Plaspy deployment is straightforward and scalable. For organizations that also need vehicle-specific telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition state or immobilizer controls, the L16 PRO can complement vehicle trackers in the same Plaspy account so you can centralize fleet management, personnel tracking, and anti-theft workflows from one platform.
