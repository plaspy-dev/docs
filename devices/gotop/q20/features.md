# GOTOP - Q20 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP Q20 GPS tracker and how the device works with Plaspy for real time tracking and safety monitoring. It summarizes practical capabilities you can expect when integrating the Q20 with Plaspy, including location reporting, SOS and alarm handling, two way voice, hybrid positioning, and buffered data handling for intermittent connectivity.

Exact feature presence and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. Use this page as a product feature guide for Plaspy integration and refer to the device manufacturer for the definitive, up to date technical specifications and implementation details.

## Feature Overview

The Q20 is a compact, rugged personal tracker designed for people and small assets that need reliable location and safety telemetry. It combines multi network cellular connectivity with hybrid positioning and a set of user safety features to provide continuous situational awareness where it matters.

- Real time location reporting and history playback for monitoring movement and routes
- Large SOS button plus two way voice for immediate contact and remote audio monitoring
- GPS plus WiFi and LBS assisted positioning to improve fixes in urban and indoor settings
- Multi network cellular support for wider coverage and continuous reporting
- Buffered data storage to retain position records when connectivity is unavailable
- IP67 rated enclosure and compact form factor for durable outdoor and personal use

## Core Features of GOTOP - Q20

- Plaspy compatibility out of the box for straightforward integration with real time dashboards and alerts
- SOS alarm with rapid notification delivery and support for callback or voice monitoring
- Two way voice via built in microphone and speaker for direct communication with the tracked device
- Hybrid positioning combining GNSS, WiFi scanning, and LBS to improve fix speed and accuracy
- Multi band cellular connectivity across 4G, 3G, and 2G networks for broad regional operation
- 6 axis motion sensor supporting fall and man down style detection for safety monitoring
- Buffered GPRS and SMS storage that preserves records while offline and uploads when connected
- IP67 rated enclosure with a compact portable form factor and magnetic charging for easy deployment

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the Q20 reports telemetry, Plaspy receives and normalizes the device messages so location and alarm data appear on dashboards and in notification workflows. Integration is designed to surface safety events and position history quickly while preserving record continuity during brief connectivity loss.

- Real time location markers and route history appear in Plaspy for live tracking and retrospective review
- SOS alerts and alarm events are delivered to Plaspy and can trigger notifications, escalation, or operator callbacks
- Two way voice events are reflected in Plaspy as alarm context and can be used by operators for remote voice monitoring where supported
- Sensor triggered events such as fall detection are passed into Plaspy and can be used to drive automation or alert flows
- Buffered position uploads ensure Plaspy receives missed records when the device regains coverage, preserving continuity of tracking
- Plaspy detects the tracker protocol and maps incoming data to the platform so devices appear and report without manual protocol selection

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety for children and older adults where SOS and fall detection provide rapid alerts to guardians
- Lone worker protection with man down alerts and two way voice for check ins and emergency contact
- Pet and small asset tracking where compact waterproof design and hybrid positioning maintain visibility
- Event or temporary workforce tracking that benefits from fast deployment and magnetic charging
- Anti theft or recovery scenarios using buffered storage to retain position history even when coverage is intermittent
- Remote or urban monitoring where hybrid fixes reduce blind spots and speed location acquisition
