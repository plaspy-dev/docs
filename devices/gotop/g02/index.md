# GOTOP - G02

![G02](./logo..jpg)

The GOTOP G02-4G is a 4G LTE waterproof GPS tracker designed for motorcycle, E-bike, and car tracking. With its compact size of 90 x 57 x 20 mm and weight of 68g, it can easily be installed and hidden in various vehicles. The tracker operates on a wide voltage range of 9-95V, making it compatible with a variety of vehicles.

One of the standout features of the G02-4G is its waterproof rating of IP67, ensuring that it can withstand harsh weather conditions and provide reliable tracking even in wet environments. The tracker supports tracking via SMS or 4G LTE, allowing users to conveniently monitor their vehicles in real-time. It also features ACC detection, which can detect the ignition status of the vehicle and provide accurate tracking information.

The G02-4G offers several alarm features to enhance security. It has a geo-fencing alarm that alerts users when the vehicle enters or exits a predefined area. The low battery alarm notifies users when the tracker's battery is running low, ensuring that it is always operational. Additionally, the vibration alarm and movement alarm provide alerts in case of unauthorized movement or tampering of the vehicle. The main power cut alarm is another useful feature that notifies users when the tracker's main power source is disconnected.

The G02-4G is equipped with an inbuilt GPS/GSM antenna and a backup battery with a capacity of 3.7V 140 mAh. It utilizes the AT6558D GPS chip and the EC21AUFA-512-STD 4G chip, providing accurate GPS positioning within 5 meters. The tracker supports multiple frequency bands, including LTE-FDD, LTE-TDD, WCDMA, and GSM, ensuring compatibility in different countries. Whether you need to track your motorcycle, E-bike, or car, the GOTOP G02-4G is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers peace of mind and enhanced security.
