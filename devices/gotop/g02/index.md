# GOTOP - G02

<div className="device-logo">
![G02](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP G02 is a compact 4G LTE waterproof GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, E-bikes, and cars. With a small footprint and light weight it is intended to be placed discreetly in a variety of vehicles. The device supports tracking via SMS and 4G LTE, offers ACC detection for ignition status, and operates across a wide voltage range, making it suitable for many vehicle types. An IP67 rating provides protection against wet conditions and rough outdoor use.

As a Plaspy compatible device the G02 can feed live location and status data into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. Plaspy users can take advantage of the G02s basic alarms and detection features such as geo fencing, vibration and movement alerts, low battery notices, and main power cut warnings to build operational workflows, notifications, and history for fleet or asset management.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for hidden installation in motorcycles E bikes and cars
- IP67 rated for reliable operation in wet and exposed conditions
- Works over 4G LTE and supports SMS based tracking for flexible connectivity
- Wide input voltage range to support different vehicle electrical systems
- ACC detection for ignition state awareness and more accurate tracking context
- Multiple security alarms including geo fencing low battery vibration movement and main power cut
- Built in GPS and backup battery for continuity of location reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the GOTOP G02 supplies regular position updates and alarm events that Plaspy uses to present location, status, and history for each device. Plaspy ingests the device data so fleets and operators can monitor vehicles from a single dashboard and configure alerts and reports to match operational needs.

- Real time location visibility in Plaspy for vehicles equipped with the G02
- Geo fencing alerts and movement alarms forwarded into Plaspy notifications
- Event history and trip reporting to analyze routes and activity over time
- Device status monitoring such as low battery and main power cut shown in Plaspy
- Centralized fleet oversight allowing mixed device types to be managed alongside the G02

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking and security monitoring for motorcycles and E bikes
- Small fleet or delivery vehicle location visibility and route history
- Anti tamper and movement alerting for parked vehicles
- Rental vehicle oversight and basic operational monitoring
- Asset location and status checks where a compact waterproof tracker is required
