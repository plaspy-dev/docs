# GOTOP - G02 Features

This page provides a public, feature oriented summary for the GOTOP G02 GPS tracker and how its capabilities are presented when used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive details about the device and the kinds of monitoring and alerts you can expect while using Plaspy as your tracking platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current and device specific information consult the official GOTOP documentation and release notes as needed.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP G02 is a compact 4G LTE GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, e-bikes, and cars, with weatherproofing and a range of security alarms. Below are the most relevant practical capabilities that users typically rely on when pairing the device with a fleet or asset monitoring platform such as Plaspy.

- 4G LTE connectivity with fallback to 3G and 2G networks for location reporting and remote monitoring
- IP67 waterproof rating for reliable outdoor or exposed installations on motorcycles and e bikes
- Wide operating voltage range for compatibility with many vehicle electrical systems
- Multiple motion and power alarms to detect movement, vibration, low battery, or main power cut
- Geo fencing support to trigger alerts when a vehicle enters or exits a predefined area

## Core Features of GOTOP - G02

- 4G LTE tracker with multi generation cellular support including 3G and 2G
- IP67 rated waterproof enclosure suitable for exposed vehicle installations
- Wide working voltage range of 9 to 95 volts for use across motorcycle, e bike, and car installations
- ACC detection to report vehicle ignition status for more accurate tracking context
- Geo fencing alarm to notify on area entry and exit events
- Movement alarm and vibration alarm for tampering and unauthorized movement detection
- Main power cut alarm and low battery alarm to report powerrelated issues
- Built in GPS and GSM antenna with onboard backup battery for short term reporting when main power is lost

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and displays location and event data from compatible trackers like the GOTOP G02, giving teams centralized visibility into vehicle status and alerts. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols to streamline integration and device onboarding.

- Real time location updates and historical route playback for vehicles using Plaspy maps and logs
- Alarm and event visibility for geo fencing, movement, vibration, low battery, and main power cut within Plaspy notifications and device timelines
- Ignition status (ACC detection) shown as part of device state to help correlate movement with engine on or off conditions
- Backup battery and power loss events recorded so operations teams can track device availability and charge status
- Multi network reporting over cellular allows Plaspy to continue receiving position and alarm reports when conditions change

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle tracking and recovery with discreet installation and IP67 protection
- E bike fleet oversight where compact size and waterproofing are important
- Car tracking for location visibility and power related alerts
- Anti tampering and theft detection using movement and vibration alarms
- Monitoring ignition state for usage reporting and operational analysis
- Short term offline reporting when main power is cut using the device backup battery
