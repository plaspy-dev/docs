# GOTOP - TK-100 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the GOTOP TK-100 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing capabilities such as location reporting, alarms, and the ways those capabilities are useful when the device is connected to Plaspy for visibility and monitoring.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and the way the device is installed or configured. This page uses the manufacturer description as its basis and aims to explain practical behavior rather than device internals.

## Feature Overview

The TK-100 is presented as a compact pet GPS tracker designed for real time location and alerting. It supports both SMS and GPRS reporting methods and is built to withstand outdoor conditions, with multiple alarms intended to notify owners about movement, low battery, or leaving a predefined area.

- Real time location reporting via SMS or GPRS for ongoing pet tracking
- Geo fencing alarm to notify when a pet exits a defined area
- Low battery alarm to help ensure continuous operation
- Vibration and movement alarms to detect activity and potential escapes
- Waterproof and dustproof housing for reliable use outdoors

## Core Features of GOTOP - TK-100

- Real time tracking using SMS or GPRS reporting as described by the manufacturer
- Geo fencing alarm to support boundary monitoring and alerts
- Low battery alarm that provides notification when device power is low
- Vibration alarm that signals detected shaking or similar motion events
- Movement alarm to detect and report motion events associated with the pet
- Sleep mode support to conserve power during longer idle periods
- Compatibility with online tracking software and an Android app for monitoring

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TK-100 is connected to Plaspy, its location reports and alarms become visible within Plaspy for monitoring and historical review. Plaspy automatically recognizes compatible tracker data and presents device events alongside location history, so teams and pet owners can maintain awareness without managing raw device details.

- Location updates appear on the Plaspy map for ongoing situational awareness
- Geo fence events are shown as alerts when the device leaves or enters configured zones
- Low battery flags and movement or vibration alerts are surfaced in Plaspy event lists
- Historical tracks and recent position history can be reviewed in Plaspy for past movements
- Users can combine device alerts with Plaspy notifications and reporting tools for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time pet location and recovery assistance when a pet wanders off
- Boundary monitoring for yards or local areas using geo fencing alerts
- Activity monitoring to know when a pet is moving or stirring after rest
- Remote notification of low battery to prompt recharging or replacement
- Outdoor adventures where waterproof and dustproof design reduces weather risk

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature names and behavior such as alarms or sleep mode may vary by firmware version or regional device model
- Hardware revisions or accessory mounts may affect mounting options and environmental resistance
- Manufacturer supplied mobile apps and online portals may provide additional settings not mirrored exactly in Plaspy
- Installation and configuration choices can influence how quickly events are reported and which alerts are active
- For the most accurate and current technical details consult the manufacturer documentation

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the GOTOP TK-100 centralizes location and alarm information so owners and teams can monitor pet status from a single platform. Plaspy presents location markers, event history, and alerts in a consistent interface, helping users act on geo fence breaches, movement events, and low battery conditions without switching between systems.
