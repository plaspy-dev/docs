# GOTOP - VT-330 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the GOTOP VT-330 and how the device is used with Plaspy for real time tracking and fleet management. It focuses on practical, non sensitive descriptions of capabilities that are relevant when integrating the VT-330 into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation wiring, regional variants, and how the manufacturer implements specific functions. Review the manufacturer documentation and your device firmware notes when planning deployments or relying on a particular feature set.

## Feature Overview

The VT-330 is a compact dual SIM GSM/GPRS GPS tracker designed for vehicles including motorcycles, golf cars and standard cars. It provides dependable positioning, basic vehicle I O, an SOS input, and a built in backup battery that can help the device remain operational when main power is interrupted.

- Dual SIM GSM/GPRS connectivity to improve network availability and reduce data interruptions for Plaspy reporting.
- SiRF Star III GNSS positioning with approximately 10 m 2D RMS accuracy for reliable location updates.
- Built in 850 mAh backup battery and wide operating voltage range to support continuous operation across vehicle types.
- Integrated SOS button plus digital inputs and outputs with a supplied relay for basic anti theft and remote immobilizer workflows.
- Compact form factor and environmental tolerance for discreet installations on motorcycles, golf cars, and light vehicles.

## Core Features of GOTOP - VT-330

- Dual SIM GSM GPRS support for carrier redundancy and improved uptime.
- SiRF Star III GNSS engine providing fast reacquisition and meter level positioning accuracy.
- Built in 850 mAh backup battery to maintain reporting during short power interruptions.
- Wide operating voltage range to support installation on a variety of vehicle electrical systems.
- Physical SOS button for emergency event signalling to Plaspy.
- Two digital inputs suitable for door ignition or alarm signal monitoring when wired.
- Two outputs and an included relay that can be used for remote immobilizer or other switched control.
- Rugged environmental range and small housing for flexible mounting in demanding conditions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a VT-330 is deployed with Plaspy, its position and event messages are reflected in the platform to support live tracking, event alerts, and historical analysis. Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols and ingests the device messages for operator visibility and reporting.

- Real time position updates appear on live maps in Plaspy and are available for route playback and history reports.
- SOS button events are delivered as alert items so operators can escalate or dispatch as needed.
- Digital input changes can be shown as events or status fields in Plaspy to indicate door open, ignition on, or alarm triggers depending on wiring.
- Output control and the supplied relay can support immobilization or other remote actions when configured through vehicle wiring and platform permissions.
- Dual SIM operation reduces gaps in reporting so Plaspy receives more consistent telemetry for fleet oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars and light commercial vehicles requiring continuous position and route history.
- Anti theft deployments using SOS alerts and remote immobilizer workflows to respond to unauthorized use.
- Motorcycle and electric golf car tracking where compact size and wide voltage tolerance are important.
- Route monitoring and verification for service and delivery operations.
- Discreet asset tracking for parked equipment where backup battery resilience is desirable.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Function behavior depends on firmware and hardware revision; features listed here reflect the device specification but may vary by manufacturing batch.
- Some regional variants may differ in GSM band support or minor hardware options; check the device label and documentation for your unit.
- Digital inputs, outputs, and immobilizer functions require correct wiring and installer configuration to operate safely and legally.
- Bluetooth is not specified in the supplied VT-330 documentation so do not assume Bluetooth sensor support for this model.
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols, but device configuration and reporting intervals should be validated after installation.
