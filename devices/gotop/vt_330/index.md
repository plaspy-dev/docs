# GOTOP - VT-330

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-330](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP VT-330 is a compact dual SIM car GPS tracker designed for reliable real time location tracking and basic vehicle telemetry. Built for use on motorcycles, electric golf cars and standard vehicles, the VT-330 combines a SiRF‑Star III GNSS engine with GSM GPRS communication and practical vehicle I O to deliver position updates, SOS alerts and event notifications suitable for everyday fleet and asset monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-330 can forward location and event data into Plaspy for live mapping, alerting and historical reporting. Its dual SIM arrangement and onboard backup battery help maintain continuity of updates, while the device I O and supplied relay enable workflows such as remote immobilization and alarm notifications that integrate with Plaspy monitoring and operational processes.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker for dependable real time tracking and fleet management.
- Dual SIM GSM GPRS connectivity to improve network availability and reduce data gaps.
- SiRF‑Star III GNSS engine providing practical position accuracy for vehicle tracking.
- Built in backup battery and wide +9V to +36V operating range for continued operation.
- Compact and discreet form factor well suited to motorcycles golf cars and standard vehicles.
- Integrated SOS button plus digital inputs and output with supplied relay for immobilizer use.
- Rugged environmental tolerance for use in challenging conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the VT-330 transmits GNSS position and event messages to the platform so operators can view live locations, respond to alerts and analyze historical trips. Plaspy receives the device data and presents it alongside other fleet telemetry to support monitoring, dispatch and incident response.

- Live location updates and route playback in Plaspy for operational visibility.
- SOS button events forwarded to Plaspy to raise immediate alerts for operators.
- Digital input events such as door or ignition status report to Plaspy for event driven workflows.
- Remote immobilizer control using the onboard output and supplied relay can be coordinated through Plaspy when configured.
- Improved update continuity from dual SIM handling supports more consistent reporting to Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light vehicles and service fleets requiring continuous tracking.
- Anti theft deployments using SOS alerts and remote immobilizer functionality.
- Motorcycle and electric golf car tracking where compact size and voltage flexibility matter.
- Route monitoring and verification with historical position playback.
- Discreet asset tracking for parked or stored vehicles supported by the backup battery.

The VT-330 pairs practical GNSS positioning and dual SIM resilience with simple vehicle interfaces that map directly to Plaspy features for monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its small footprint, backup power and broad operating voltage make it adaptable across a range of vehicle types, while the included relay and I O provide straightforward options for anti theft and event driven control when coordinated through the platform.
