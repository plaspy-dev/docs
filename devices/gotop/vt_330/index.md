# GOTOP - VT-330

![VT-330](./tracker.jpg)

The VT-330 Dual SIM Car GPS Tracker is a compact, reliable GPS tracker that is Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and fleet management. Designed for motorcycles, electric golf cars and standard vehicles, the VT-330 combines SiRF-Star III satellite positioning with GSM/GPRS communication and dual SIM capability to maximize network availability and keep vehicle location, SOS alerts and basic telemetry flowing to Plaspy for monitoring and response.

With an 850 mAh backup battery, wide +9V to +36V operating voltage and a rugged environmental range, the VT-330 supports continuous operation in demanding conditions. Its discreet 80 × 58 × 22 mm form factor and relay-enabled immobilizer make it well suited to anti-theft deployments, route monitoring and general asset tracking where dependable position accuracy and simple integration with Plaspy are required.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for dependable real-time tracking and fleet management.
- Dual SIM GSM/GPRS connectivity for improved network resilience and roaming.
- SiRF‑Star III GNSS engine delivering ~10 m position accuracy \(2D RMS\) and fast reacquisition.
- Built-in 850 mAh backup battery and wide +9V–+36V power range for continuous operation.
- Compact, lightweight housing \(80×58×22 mm, 90 g\) for discreet vehicle or asset mounting.
- Integrated SOS button, 2 digital inputs and 2 outputs plus supplied relay for remote immobilizer use.
- High environmental tolerance \(‑30°C to 75°C, 5%–95% non‑condensing humidity\) for harsh field conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT-330 sends GNSS position data and vehicle telemetry over GSM/GPRS to Plaspy, enabling live maps, alerts and historical reports. Dual SIM support reduces data drops by allowing the device to switch between carriers; Plaspy receives continuous location and event updates for efficient fleet oversight and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GSM/GPRS to Plaspy for live tracking and route playback.
- SOS button events are delivered to Plaspy so operators can trigger immediate responses.
- Digital inputs can report door, ignition or alarm status to Plaspy when wired to vehicle signals.
- Remote immobilizer control via the included relay/output — Plaspy can initiate immobilization when configured with vehicle wiring and platform permissions.
- Note on Bluetooth sensors: the VT-330 specification does not list Bluetooth; Plaspy can also accept Bluetooth sensor data from compatible trackers where available.
