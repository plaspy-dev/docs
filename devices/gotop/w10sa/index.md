# GOTOP - W10SA

![W10SA](./tracker.jpg)

The W10SA Personal GPS Tracker is a compact, battery-powered GPS tracker engineered for reliable everyday tracking and Plaspy compatible deployments. Lightweight and ready to use out of the box, the W10SA delivers real-time positioning, tracking playback and configurable alarm telemetry for people, luggage and small vehicles. Its built-in GPS/GSM antenna and universal protocol support make integration into the Plaspy platform straightforward for operators who need immediate location insight without complex installation.

Designed for personal safety, asset protection and fleet management of small vehicles, the W10SA combines long standby performance with flexible communication settings. With 4G and 2G cellular support, multiple alarm types \(power-failure, overspeed, vibration/shock and electronic geofence\) and configurable server IP/port, the W10SA provides the core telemetry and alerting a Plaspy-compatible solution requires to scale secure tracking and anti-theft workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracking device: supports configurable server IP/port and common tracking protocols for easy platform integration.
- Real-time tracking and playback: GPS positioning with GSM fallback for continuous location updates and historical route playback.
- Long-life internal battery: 800 mAh rechargeable battery \(3.7V\) with Type-C charging for extended standby and tracking duration.
- Comprehensive alarm set: power-failure, overspeed, vibration/shock and electronic geofence alerts to support anti-theft and safety scenarios.
- Multi-network cellular support: LTE-FDD \(4G\) and 2G compatibility to maintain connectivity across regions and legacy networks.
- Universal protocol support: GT06, 808 and Tianqin protocols for broad server compatibility and simplified telemetry ingestion.
- No installation required: compact, lightweight housing for quick deployment on people, luggage or portable assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the W10SA with Plaspy is centered on straightforward telemetry delivery and alert forwarding. The device sends periodic GPS coordinates and alarm signals over the cellular network using industry-standard protocols. Plaspy ingests that data to provide real-time tracking, historic routes, and configurable alerts to users and operations teams. Because the W10SA allows modification of the platform IP and port and supports GT06/808/Tianqin protocols, it can be pointed directly to a Plaspy endpoint or routed via middleware for centralized fleet management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates for immediate tracking and situational awareness.
- Alarm forwarding: power-failure, overspeed, vibration/shock and electronic fence alerts are transmitted to Plaspy for instant notifications.
- Configurable server IP/port: point the W10SA to Plaspy servers or a private gateway for seamless ingestion.
- Protocol compatibility: GT06, 808 and Tianqin support ensures reliable parsing and mapping of telemetry fields into Plaspy dashboards.
- GSM fallback: when GPS signal or higher-band networks are limited, GSM/GPRS/EDGE maintains connectivity for continued reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the W10SA for a Plaspy deployment gives operators a compact, no-install GPS tracker that emphasizes reliable position reporting and actionable alarms. Its multi-band cellular support and universal protocol compatibility reduce integration time, while the rechargeable 800 mAh battery and Type‑C charging ensure the device stays operational across long tracking sessions. For organizations building telemetry solutions—whether focused on fleet management, anti-theft workflows or personal safety—the W10SA provides the core location and alert data Plaspy needs to deliver real-time insights, notifications and historical reports.

While the W10SA focuses on GPS-based telemetry and alarm reporting, Plaspy can combine its location stream with broader fleet telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition-event tracking, immobilizer actions or Bluetooth sensor data when those signals are available from other onboard systems or external sensors. This makes the W10SA a practical Plaspy compatible option for teams that want a lightweight, reliable GPS tracker to serve as the location and alerting backbone of a larger telematics solution.
