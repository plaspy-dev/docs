# GOTOP - W10SA

<div className="device-logo">
![W10SA](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The W10SA Personal GPS Tracker is a compact, battery powered device designed for everyday tracking of people, luggage and small vehicles. It provides real time positioning, route playback and a set of configurable alarms for power loss, overspeed, vibration and geofence events. With an integrated GPS and cellular antenna the W10SA is intended to be lightweight and ready to use without complex installation.

As a Plaspy compatible device the W10SA can deliver location updates and alarm telemetry directly into fleet and asset management workflows. Its support for configurable server settings and common tracking protocols makes integration into Plaspy straightforward, enabling operators to receive live location insight, historical routes and alert notifications within Plaspy dashboards and monitoring tools.

## Key Highlights

- Compact battery powered personal tracker suitable for people luggage and portable assets
- Real time positioning and historical route playback for clear movement visibility
- Configurable alarm set including power failure overspeed vibration shock and electronic geofence
- Built in GPS and GSM antenna for out of the box use and simplified deployment
- Configurable server IP and port plus broad protocol support to ease platform integration
- Multi network cellular compatibility to maintain connectivity across different regions
- Rechargeable internal battery with Type C charging for extended standby and field use

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the W10SA with Plaspy relies on the device sending periodic GPS coordinates and alarm signals over the cellular network using common tracking protocols. Plaspy ingests that telemetry to provide real time mapping, historical playback and alerting so operations teams can monitor assets and respond to events quickly.

- Real time location updates streamed into Plaspy for immediate situational awareness
- Alarm forwarding so power loss overspeed vibration and geofence events generate notifications
- Configurable server settings allow pointing the device to Plaspy endpoints or a private gateway
- Protocol compatibility supports reliable parsing of telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and reports
- Historical route playback and reporting inside Plaspy for post event analysis and audits

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for vulnerable individuals or lone workers with live alerts
- Luggage and travel protection with lightweight tracking and route playback
- Small vehicle and scooter fleet tracking with overspeed alerts and location history
- Asset recovery and anti theft monitoring using vibration and power loss alarms
- Temporary or rented equipment tracking where fast deployment and portability matter

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The W10SA is a practical choice for organizations that need a small reliable location and alerting device to feed into a larger telematics or monitoring solution. Its focus on core GPS telemetry and configurable server options reduces integration effort, while rechargeable battery operation and a compact form factor make it convenient for personal and portable asset deployments.
