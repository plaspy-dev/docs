# Car GPS Tracker G01

The Car GPS Tracker G01 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for reliable vehicle tracking, fleet management, and anti-theft protection. Designed for commercial fleets, rental cars, taxis and private vehicles, the G01 combines GPS and BDS positioning with a broad vehicle voltage range and a set of proven inputs/outputs to deliver real-time tracking and actionable telemetry to Plaspy's platform.

With an emphasis on secure connectivity and practical vehicle controls, the G01 supports remote audio monitoring, SOS alarm, ACC detection, and a remote power/fuel cut-off to enable immobilizer-style interventions. The device's robust electrical and environmental specifications make it suitable for diverse deployments where Plaspy’s dashboards, alerts and reporting are used to optimize operations and respond quickly to incidents.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Dual GNSS positioning \(GPS + BDS\) with up to 5 m accuracy for precise location reporting.
- Wide vehicle voltage support \(DC 9V–75V\) — fits cars, taxis, trucks and other commercial vehicles.
- Vehicle control and anti-theft features: remote power/fuel cut-off \(immobilizer-style\), ACC detection and SOS alarm.
- Backup 180 mAh battery ensures continued reporting during power loss and helps detect tampering or power-off events.
- Practical telematics I/O: external mic input, ACC/H-IN, SOS/L-IN and a low output channel for remote cut-off.
- Lightweight, compact ABS housing \(98 × 53 × 13 mm; ~59 g\) for discreet installation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G01 streams positional fixes and vehicle telemetry over GSM to provide real-time tracking, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the device's location, status inputs and alarm events so fleet managers and vehicle owners receive instant insight and can take action from the Plaspy web or mobile interface.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via GSM 2G to Plaspy for live monitoring and mapping.
- Ignition \(ACC\) detection for trip start/stop events, odometer/drive-time calculations and driver behavior monitoring.
- Remote power/fuel cut-off \(immobilizer capability\) controllable through Plaspy workflows to support anti-theft responses.
- SOS alarm, power-off alarm and vibration/move alarms feed into Plaspy alerts to trigger notifications and incident workflows.
- Remote audio monitoring and external mic input can assist recovery and verification during theft or security events.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: live vehicle tracking, trip logs and driver behavior monitoring to improve utilization and reduce costs.
- Anti-theft protection and remote immobilization for rental cars, taxis and high-value vehicles using the power/fuel cut-off.
- Safety and emergency response: SOS alarm, power-off alarm and remote audio monitoring to accelerate incident handling.
- Commercial truck deployment: wide working voltage and rugged environmental ratings suit heavy-vehicle telematics needs.
- Rental and shared mobility: ACC detection and tamper-aware backup battery ensure accurate billing, usage tracking and theft response.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G01 is a practical, value-oriented GPS tracker for organizations that need Plaspy compatible devices with proven vehicle interfaces. Its dual GNSS positioning and reliable GSM communications provide the core real-time tracking and telemetry required for modern fleet management. Built-in vehicle-level controls — ACC detection, SOS, vibration alarms and remote power/fuel cut-off — enable anti-theft workflows and immobilizer actions directly from Plaspy.

For fleets and vehicle owners who want straightforward integration, the G01 offers the essential inputs and outputs to tie vehicle events into Plaspy alerts and reports without overcomplication. The backup battery, wide input voltage range and rugged environmental tolerance make deployment simple across mixed fleets. If you require Bluetooth sensors or advanced FOTA management, Plaspy can integrate devices that provide those capabilities alongside G01 units to build a comprehensive telemetry solution.
