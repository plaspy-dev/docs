# GOTOP - TL-216

<div className="device-logo">
![TL-216](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP TL-216 is a compact portable GPS tracker that combines satellite positioning with GSM GPRS network reporting to provide on the move location monitoring. Designed for flexible use, it is commonly applied to pet tracking, asset protection, and vehicle tracking where a small form factor and basic remote reporting are valuable. The device supports real time location updates, SMS or data transmission of coordinates, and the ability to view positions via Google map links on a smartphone.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TL-216 can feed location data and basic alerts into the Plaspy fleet and asset management platform to provide centralized visibility and historical records. Plaspy can use the device updates for map visualization, alerting, and reporting, making the TL-216 a practical option for users who need a portable tracker that integrates with a broader monitoring workflow.

## Key Highlights

- Portable design suitable for on the go tracking of people or movable assets
- Combines GPS satellite positioning with GSM GPRS reporting for real time location updates
- Long standby time supported by an internal rechargeable battery for extended field use
- Sends location by SMS or data with Google map link support for easy smartphone viewing
- Built in memory storage captures data when the GSM network is unavailable
- Geo fence alarm feature notifies authorized numbers when a virtual boundary is breached
- Low battery alert informs users when recharging is needed

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TL-216 transmits position information and status messages that Plaspy can ingest to display live locations, generate alerts, and store history for later analysis. When combined with Plaspy, the tracker becomes part of a centralized monitoring setup that supports operational oversight and routine reporting.

- Map visualization of live location points within the Plaspy interface
- Historical playback and reporting for trips and location history retained by the platform
- Geo fence alerts from the device routed into Plaspy for perimeter breach notifications
- Low battery warnings surfaced in Plaspy so managers can schedule maintenance or recharge
- Offline data stored by the tracker is synchronized to Plaspy when network service resumes
- Configurable update intervals from the device reflected in the platform to balance detail and battery life

## Typical Use Cases

- Portable asset tracking for equipment that moves between sites or vehicles
- Vehicle tracking for occasional or secondary vehicles where a compact tracker is preferred
- Pet tracking when a small, wearable device is needed for location recovery
- Short term rental or loaner equipment monitoring to ensure return and location history
- Mobile worker location oversight for staff operating away from fixed assets
