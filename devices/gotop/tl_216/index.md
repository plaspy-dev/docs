# GOTOP - TL-216

![TL-216](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TL-216 is a portable GPS tracker that combines GPS satellite and GSM/GPRS network technology, making it an ideal choice for business professionals who need to track their assets on the go. With its compact and portable design, this tracker can be easily carried and used for various security purposes, such as pet tracking, asset tracking, and car tracking. 

One of the standout features of the TL-216 is its long battery life. Equipped with a built-in rechargeable 3.7V 1800mAh battery, this tracker can last up to 10 days under standby time, ensuring that you can track your assets without worrying about frequent recharging. 

The TL-216 offers real-time locating and tracking capabilities. It can transmit the longitude and latitude coordinates via SMS or GPRS. If the device has a GPS fix, it will send you real-time location information. In cases where GPS fix is not available, it will reply with the last known location. You can also set the interval time for location updates, allowing you to receive location information based on your preferred schedule. 

Another convenient feature of the TL-216 is its ability to show location information directly on your mobile phone. It supports Google map links, so when you receive an SMS with a link, you can simply click on it to view the detailed position information on Google maps. This feature works best with smartphones. 

In addition, the TL-216 has memory storage capabilities, allowing it to store data when the GSM network is lost. This ensures that you won't miss any important location information even in areas with poor network coverage. 

The TL-216 also offers geo-fence alarm functionality. You can set up a virtual boundary, and if the device breaches that boundary, it will send a message with the geo information to the authorized number. This is particularly useful for keeping track of assets and ensuring their security. 

Lastly, the TL-216 has a low battery alert feature. It will send an alert to the authorized phone number when the battery is running low, ensuring that you can take timely action to recharge the device. 

With its range of features and portable design, the GOTOP TL-216 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that can meet the tracking needs of business professionals and individuals alike.
