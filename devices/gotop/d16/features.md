# GOTOP - D16 Features

This page provides a public, high level description of the GOTOP D16 features and how the device is used with Plaspy for tracking, fleet oversight, and rider visibility. It focuses on practical capabilities visible to operators and riders when the D16 is paired with Plaspy, rather than low level protocol or private implementation details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and how the unit is installed. Where relevant this page notes common differences and encourages checking manufacturer documentation for the most current device specifics.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP D16 is a compact, bicycle-focused GPS tracker designed for real-time location, theft protection, and improved rider visibility. When connected to Plaspy, the D16 provides position updates, event reporting, and remote control of its LED signaling to support fleet operations and personal bike security.

- Real-time tracking over cellular connectivity for live location monitoring and map display.
- Integrated LED signaling with brake and turn indicators to improve rider visibility and safety.
- Geofence alerts and event logging to notify operators and riders of unauthorized movement.
- Route history playback for trip review, analytics, and rental billing reconciliation.
- Internal rechargeable battery with long standby behavior suitable for bike deployments.
- Compact, weather resistant enclosure intended for bicycle mounting and daily use.

## Core Features of GOTOP - D16

- 4G LTE connectivity as the primary communications channel with an optional 2G variant available in some regions.
- GPS based positioning for live tracking and historical route playback.
- Integrated LED signaling: a red LED strip for braking and two yellow LEDs for left and right turn indication.
- Remote control support to toggle LED indicators and send simple commands to the tracker.
- Geofence alarm to detect and report boundary breaches to operators and riders.
- Low battery alarm to alert when the internal battery needs recharge.
- IPX4 rated housing to resist splashes and light rain for outdoor cycling use.
- Compact form factor tailored for installation on city bikes, e-bikes, and rental fleets.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the D16 is connected to Plaspy, the device’s location and event messages are presented in the platform to support monitoring, alerts, and operational workflows. Plaspy ingests the device updates and makes them available in web and mobile interfaces for operators and riders.

- Live location markers and movement status visible on Plaspy maps for real time oversight.
- Event logs and notifications for geofence breaches and low battery conditions routed to configured users.
- Route history playback inside Plaspy for trip review, operational analytics, and billing reconciliation.
- Remote LED control accessible via Plaspy so operators or riders can toggle brake and turn indicators remotely.
- Consolidated device status (battery, last known position, recent events) available in the Plaspy device dashboard.
- Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols automatically so D16 units integrate without manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental bike fleet operations that need live tracking, geofence enforcement, and usage history for billing.
- Personal and shared bicycle anti-theft monitoring with geofence alerts and real-time location reporting for recovery.
- Rider visibility enhancement using remotely controllable brake and turn LEDs to improve safety in traffic.
- Route analysis and operational planning using historical trip playback to optimize routes and fleet deployment.
- Urban micromobility programs that require compact, weather resistant trackers for distributed bicycles.
- On-demand remote signaling for safety or operational communications between operator and rider.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features, signaling behavior, and available bands may vary by firmware revision and regional device variant.
- The optional 2G variant may be offered in certain markets; check the specific model label or manufacturer product listing.
- Battery life and standby duration depend on reporting intervals, signal conditions, and how frequently LEDs or remote commands are used.
- Installation location and mounting method on the bicycle can affect GPS reception and LED visibility.
- Always consult the official manufacturer documentation for the exact hardware revision and supported features for a given unit.
