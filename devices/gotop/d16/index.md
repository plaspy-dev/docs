# GOTOP - D16

![D16](./tracker.jpg)

The GOTOP D16 is a compact 4G bike GPS tracker designed for bicycle safety, theft protection, and fleet management. Purpose built for city bikes, e-bikes, and rental fleets, the D16 provides continuous location updates, route history playback, geofence alerts, and a distinctive LED signaling system to increase rider visibility. Its compact, weather resistant enclosure and focused feature set make it suitable for outdoor cycling environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the D16 integrates with Plaspy's platform and mobile interfaces to deliver live location feeds, alarm notifications, and remote control of LED signals. That compatibility means operators and riders can use Plaspy to monitor devices, review trips, receive geofence and low battery alerts, and manage a bike fleet with the D16 as a dedicated bicycle telemetry source.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real time tracking and bicycle fleet management
- 4G LTE connectivity for low latency position updates and cloud integration
- Integrated LED signaling for brake and turn indication to improve rider visibility
- Remote control support to toggle LED indicators and send basic remote commands
- IPX4 rated housing for protection against splashes and light rain
- Geofence and low battery alarms to help protect assets and prompt recovery actions
- Compact bicycle focused form factor with extended standby time on internal battery

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the D16 reports position and status updates to the platform so operators can view live locations, track movement history, and receive timely alarms. Plaspy converts those updates into map markers, event logs, and notifications that support operational oversight and rider safety.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Geofence alerting and event logging to notify operators of unauthorized movement
- Route history playback for analytics, billing reconciliation, and operational review
- Remote LED control through Plaspy to activate brake and turn signals for safety
- Low battery notifications routed to operators and riders to maintain device uptime

## Typical Use Cases

- Management of rental bike fleets with live tracking, trip history, and geofence enforcement
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery for personal bicycles and shared fleets
- Visibility and rider safety enhancement using remote controlled LED brake and turn signals
- Operational reporting and route analysis for service planning and asset utilization
- City bike and e bike deployments where compact, weather resistant tracking is required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GOTOP D16 is a focused option for organizations and individuals that need a bike centric tracker with practical safety features. Its combination of compact design, LED signaling, geofence alerts, and Plaspy compatibility makes it well suited to rental operators, city bike programs, and riders who want straightforward anti theft and visibility tools integrated into a cloud platform.
