# GOTOP - VT-106

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-106](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP VT-106 is a compact motorcycle GPS tracker designed to provide reliable and accurate location monitoring for two wheeled vehicles. It uses GPS satellites for positioning and the existing GSM GPRS network to transmit location and status information. The device supports sending coordinates via SMS and forwarding data to a designated server, and it includes practical features for motorcycles such as a waterproof enclosure, an inbuilt magnet for secure attachment, and an internal backup battery.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-106 can feed location, alarm, and status data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and management. Because the tracker can transmit coordinates and alarm messages to a server, Plaspy can receive that data and present it alongside other assets for live tracking, history playback, and operational reporting. This makes the VT-106 a sensible option for organizations that want motorcycle specific hardware working with Plaspy for monitoring and security workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for motorcycle tracking with a compact waterproof housing and inbuilt magnet for secure mounting
- Uses GPS for positioning and GSM GPRS for transmitting coordinates and status to a server
- Supports both SMS reporting and GPRS data upload for flexible tracking options
- Includes alarms such as geo fence low battery speeding and main power lost to support active monitoring
- Remote vehicle stop capability by cutting off circuit and fuel for enhanced security control
- Built in GPS and GSM antennas and a 1000mAh internal backup battery for continued operation during power loss

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, data sent from the VT-106 can be processed into location views, alerts, and reports that support fleet management and security monitoring. Plaspy receives coordinate and alarm messages from the tracker and translates them into actionable information in a single platform.

- Real time location display of motorcycles on Plaspy maps for operational oversight
- Historical route playback and location history for audits and trip analysis
- Alarm handling and notifications in Plaspy for geo fence triggers low battery and speeding alerts
- Status reporting and device health indicators to monitor main power and backup battery events
- Use of remote immobilization signals through Plaspy workflows where supported by device setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle fleet tracking for couriers and delivery services
- Asset protection and theft response for personal and company motorcycles
- Rental scooter and bike monitoring to track usage and location history
- Field service or last mile operations that require compact trackers on two wheeled vehicles
- Security monitoring where geo fence and remote stop capabilities are desirable
