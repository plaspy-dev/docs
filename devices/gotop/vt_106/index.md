# GOTOP - VT-106

![VT-106](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-106 is a high-quality motorcycle GPS tracker that offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. This GPS tracker works based on the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites, ensuring that you can track your motorcycle's location with ease. It is a professional system that consists of a high-sensitive and reliable GPS tracker and server tracking software.

With the GOTOP VT-106, you can not only receive the longitude and latitude coordinates of your motorcycle via SMS, but you can also transmit the coordinates, vehicle status, and alarm information to a designated server via GPRS. This allows you to have a comprehensive view of your motorcycle's whereabouts, its past locations, and its current status. Additionally, you have the ability to remotely stop the vehicle by cutting off the circuit and fuel, providing an extra layer of security.

The GOTOP VT-106 is equipped with a range of features that make it an excellent choice for motorcycle tracking. It is waterproof, ensuring that it can withstand various weather conditions. It also has an inbuilt super magnet, allowing for easy installation and secure attachment to your motorcycle. The tracker supports tracking via SMS and GPRS, providing multiple options for monitoring your motorcycle's location. The Google link function allows you to view the location on a map, making it convenient and user-friendly. Other notable features include geo-fence alarm, low battery alarm, speeding alarm, one input and one output, main power lost alarm, and a 1000mAh internal backup battery. With its built-in GPS and GSM antenna, you can expect reliable and accurate tracking performance.
