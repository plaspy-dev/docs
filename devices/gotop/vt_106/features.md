# GOTOP - VT-106 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP VT-106 motorcycle GPS tracker and how those capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the VT-106’s practical tracking functions, alarm types, and installation-friendly design in the context of Plaspy compatibility so fleet managers and riders can understand what to expect when the device reports into the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, and how the tracker is installed. Use this page for general guidance on capabilities and integration with Plaspy, and consult the device manufacturer for the final, device specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP VT-106 is designed as a motorcycle tracker that uses GPS satellites for positioning and the GSM GPRS network for reporting. When used with Plaspy, the tracker can provide ongoing location visibility, alarm reporting, and remote control actions where the device firmware and installation support them.

- Real time location reporting to a server via GPRS and coordinate delivery via SMS for redundant reporting.
- Transmission of coordinates, vehicle status, and alarm information to a designated server for visibility in Plaspy.
- Remote vehicle stop capability by cutting a circuit or fuel line where the unit and installation provide a safe, authorized output.
- Onboard safety and convenience features including waterproof housing and an inbuilt magnet for secure mounting.
- Alarm support including geo fence alarm, low battery alarm, speeding alarm, and main power lost alarm.
- Built in backup battery for short term operation when main power is lost and integrated GPS and GSM antennas for reliable reception.

## Core Features of GOTOP - VT-106

- GPS satellite based positioning for locating motorcycles and recording coordinates.
- GSM GPRS connectivity for reporting location, status, and alarms to a tracking server.
- SMS based coordinate reporting as an alternative or fallback reporting method.
- Support for geo fence alarm, low battery alarm, speeding alarm, and main power lost alarm.
- One digital input and one output available for monitoring and control functions.
- Remote stop function capability by cutting circuit or fuel via the device output where installed and authorized.
- Waterproof enclosure and inbuilt super magnet for flexible and durable motorcycle mounting.
- 1000 mAh internal backup battery to maintain tracking and alarm reporting during main power loss.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a VT-106 is connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s reporting and alarms can be reflected in the platform to provide location awareness and operational oversight. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents device data for monitoring and event handling.

- Real time location points and historical tracks are viewable on Plaspy maps when the device reports to the platform.
- Alarms reported by the VT-106 such as geo fence, low battery, speeding, and power loss are surfaced in Plaspy for notifications and review.
- Device status and basic telemetry reported by the tracker are logged to support troubleshooting and auditing of events.
- Where the device and installation allow, Plaspy can facilitate remote commands such as authorized vehicle stop signals through the tracker’s output, subject to firmware and wiring.
- Plaspy groups devices, filters events, and provides a consolidated view for fleets that include VT-106 units.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle theft deterrence and recovery using continuous location reporting and remote stop where supported.
- Fleet tracking for delivery or courier motorcycles with alarm visibility and historical route playback.
- Rental motorcycle monitoring to ensure compliance with boundaries and to receive alerts for battery or power loss.
- Urban mobility and dispatch operations that need small, discreet tracking devices with secure mounting.
- Long term location logging for maintenance records, route analysis, or incident investigations.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware level and device configuration determine which alarms and remote commands are available and how they behave.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants of the VT-106 may change antenna arrangement, battery capacity, or supported network bands.
- Installation wiring and the presence of authorized control circuits determine whether the remote stop output can be used safely and legally.
- SMS reporting and GPRS reporting availability depend on local mobile network coverage and SIM service configuration.
- Always check the manufacturer documentation and the specific unit configuration to confirm which features are enabled.
