# GOTOP - G35 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP G35 animal GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is focused on practical, non sensitive information about device functions, telemetry types, and the ways Plaspy can display and act on the tracker data.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. The content below is grounded in the G35 product description and highlights common capabilities and operational uses without replacing official device documentation.

## Feature Overview

The G35 is a collar mounted GPS tracker built for long duration deployments in outdoor and harsh environments. It combines multiple positioning methods, long battery life, and on device recovery aids to support pet, livestock, and wildlife tracking while allowing telemetry and alerts to be integrated into Plaspy for centralized monitoring.

- Multi mode positioning including GPS, BeiDou BD, AGPS, LBS and WiFi to improve fix reliability in varied environments.
- Very large rechargeable battery designed for extended standby between charges, supporting long deployments.
- Rugged IP67 waterproof collar housing suitable for pets, livestock and outdoor use.
- On device recovery tools such as audible locate tone, LED flash and voice monitoring to help find animals in low light or dense cover.
- Offline waypoint memory for historical route logging and later synchronization or import into Plaspy.

## Core Features of GOTOP - G35

- Multi GNSS and supplementary positioning modes: GPS and BeiDou BD with AGPS, plus LBS and WiFi positioning.
- Long endurance power system: a large rechargeable battery with extended standby characteristics for long term field use.
- Rugged, IP67 rated collar form factor suitable for outdoor and wet conditions.
- Onboard 3 axis G sensor for motion detection and movement alarms.
- Voice monitoring capability and an onboard speaker to support remote listening.
- Audible locate tone and LED flash for physical recovery and night time locating.
- Offline waypoint memory with substantial storage for route logging and historical playback.
- Cellular connectivity via micro SIM for SMS and LTE data reporting to enable remote telemetry.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can ingest position updates and event notifications from the G35, enabling central visibility and alerting across deployments. The platform is designed to present location, status, and alarm information so teams can monitor animals alongside other assets.

- Real time position updates visible on Plaspy maps when the device reports location over mobile data or when SMS based location is relayed through supported workflows.
- Movement events and motion alarms triggered by the G sensor appear as alerts in Plaspy to notify teams of unusual activity.
- Geofence and low battery notifications can be forwarded into Plaspy for rule based notifications and escalation.
- Historical routes and waypoint logs can be synchronized or imported for playback and route analysis inside Plaspy.
- On demand actions reported by the device such as triggered locate tone, LED flash or voice monitoring are visible in event histories to aid recovery operations.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet recovery and lost animal searches using real time tracking combined with audible locate and LED flash.
- Livestock monitoring on farms and ranches with movement alerts and geofence notifications to detect stray or stolen animals.
- Wildlife research and field studies requiring long battery life and offline waypoint logging for later analysis.
- Transport monitoring for animals where centralized telemetry provides route oversight and alarm escalation.
- Long term deployments where rugged housing and magnetic charging simplify maintenance in the field.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features such as voice monitoring, locate tone, and LED activation may depend on the device firmware and manufacturer app backend.
- Battery endurance and standby estimates depend on reporting frequency, network conditions and enabled sensors.
- Hardware revisions or regional cellular variants can affect supported cellular bands and connectivity behavior.
- Offline waypoint memory requires periodic synchronization or upload through the manufacturer software to be available for Plaspy import.
- Confirm feature details, supported workflows and any regional differences with the official manufacturer documentation.
