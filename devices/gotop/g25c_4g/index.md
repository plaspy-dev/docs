# GOTOP - G25C-4G

![G25C-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The G25C-4G from GOTOP is an industrial grade IoT tracking terminal designed for long term vehicle and asset monitoring. It combines LTE wireless communication with multi mode positioning including GPS and BDS plus AGPS, WiFi and LBS to improve location reliability. Built for field use, the unit features an IP65 waterproof housing, a full built in antenna and strong magnetic mounting, and it offers SMS Google Map location links for quick situational checks.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G25C-4G is a practical option for organizations that need persistent tracking, remote alerts and historical playback within a centralized platform. Its long battery life and configurable power saving modes reduce maintenance needs for remote assets, while onboard memory and automatic resend behavior preserve telemetry for accurate reporting inside Plaspy. The tracker’s alarm capabilities and remote SMS controls make it suitable for anti theft workflows and fleet operations managed through Plaspy dashboards.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible industrial tracker for long term vehicle and asset monitoring and historical playback
- Multi mode locating with GPS, BDS, AGPS, WiFi and LBS to improve position availability in varied environments
- Large 20000 mAh battery with extended standby and three configurable power saving modes for low maintenance deployments
- Rugged design with IP65 waterproof rating, full built in antenna and magnet mounting for flexible placement
- Comprehensive alarm suite including drop off, movement, shake, geo fence, low battery and overspeed for proactive alerts
- Built in memory for offline data storage and automatic resend to maintain telemetry continuity during connectivity gaps
- Remote SMS controls and quick Google Map links for fast location sharing and field configuration

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G25C-4G delivers position updates, event alarms and stored telemetry to your Plaspy account so you can monitor assets in real time and review historical tracks. The device’s multi mode positioning and onboard storage help ensure continuous visibility, while remote controls and alert parameters can be coordinated through Plaspy workflows for operational response.

- Real time location and telemetry displayed on Plaspy dashboards for live monitoring and fleet oversight
- Configurable push alerts in Plaspy for drop off, movement, low battery, shake, geo fence breaches and overspeed events
- Offline storage with automatic resend preserves event history and enables accurate playback and reporting in Plaspy
- SMS Google Map links for quick location checks without immediate platform access
- Remote parameter updates and power controls via SMS to reduce site visits and simplify field management

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trucks and service vehicles needing continuous tracking and route history
- Container and long term asset protection where waterproofing and long standby are essential
- Rental vehicle monitoring and short term installs that benefit from magnetic mounting and remote controls
- Remote equipment and machinery tracking at sites with intermittent connectivity
- High value cargo or pallet protection using movement and drop off alarms for theft detection
