# GOTOP - A12

<div className="device-logo">
![A12](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP A12 is a purpose built GPS tracker designed for animal tracking and comes with a collar for convenient attachment. It combines GPS positioning with GSM connectivity to provide real time location updates and history route tracking. The device includes a built in motion sensor for movement alerts, supports voice monitoring, and is rated IPX7 for waterproof protection, making it suitable for outdoor use in a range of environments. At 84 x 47 x 45 mm and about 145 g, the A12 is compact and intended to be comfortable for animals to wear.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the A12 can feed location and event data into Plaspy's tracking platform for unified visibility and management. Using the tracker with Plaspy brings motion alerts, low battery notifications, history playback, and live location into a single monitoring workflow. This compatibility makes the A12 a practical option for teams and individuals who want to track animals while leveraging Plaspy for oversight, reporting, and operational monitoring without needing separate systems for location and alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Specially designed for animal tracking and supplied with a collar for easy mounting
- Real time tracking and history route playback to review past movements
- Built in motion sensor enables movement alerts for activity monitoring
- Waterproof IPX7 rating for reliable outdoor use in wet conditions
- Multiple tracking access methods including SMS, web platform, and mobile app
- Power management with low battery alert to help maintain continuous operation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GOTOP A12 streams location and event information into Plaspy's platform so users can monitor animals alongside other tracked assets. Integration enables centralised access to location data, alerts, and route history without changing how the device collects its basic signals.

- Live location visible on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness
- Motion and low battery alerts forwarded into Plaspy for timely notifications
- Route history available in Plaspy for playback and retrospective analysis
- Voice monitoring events accessible through the platform alongside other telemetry
- Role based visibility and reporting to support different operational users
