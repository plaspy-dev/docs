# GOTOP - G033

![G033](./tracker.jpg)

The GOTOP G033 is a name card style GPS tracker designed for discreet personal and small scale personnel tracking. Styled to resemble a business card, the G033 provides satellite positioning plus cellular reporting and supports multiple positioning modes, voice monitoring and local recording. Its compact dimensions and lightweight design make it suitable for carrying in a wallet, bag, or personal item while still providing routine position reports and alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G033 can feed live location and event data into the Plaspy platform for monitoring, playback and alerting. The tracker’s multiple reporting methods, multi mode positioning and onboard recording features align with Plaspy’s tools for visibility and operational oversight, making the G033 a practical option for teams that need discreet, person focused tracking within the Plaspy ecosystem.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible with real time tracking via cellular data and SMS location links for rapid access.
- Ultra compact card form factor 71.8 × 45.6 × 8.5 mm and 30 g for discreet personal and asset placement.
- Multi mode positioning including GPS, LBS, WiFi and assisted GPS to improve coverage in varied environments.
- Voice monitoring and local recording with TF card support up to 32 GB for on device audio logs.
- Long standby performance from a rechargeable battery to support extended unattended use.
- Multiple reporting methods including SMS links and GPRS style real time updates for flexibility in connectivity.
- Built in safety and alert features such as vibration alarm, low battery notification, remote commands and find device function.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the G033 with Plaspy enables live map visibility, historical route playback and the delivery of alerts generated by the device. Once the unit is configured to report to Plaspy, monitoring teams can consolidate location updates and event notifications in a single interface for situational awareness and review.

- Live location updates visible on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring and response.
- SMS reply with direct map link as a fallback method to obtain location when cellular data is not available.
- Historical route playback using onboard logs and platform stored positions for audit and review.
- Alerts such as vibration alarm and low battery surfaced in Plaspy so teams can act on events.
- Voice monitoring and recorded audio stored locally on a TF card can provide context for incidents when accessed according to device workflow.

## Typical Use Cases

- Student protection and family safety with discreet carry and SOS style monitoring.
- Lone worker oversight where remote monitoring and alerting improve responder awareness.
- Small scale personnel management and check in tracking for mobile staff or couriers.
- Asset tracking for items that require low visibility placement such as briefcases or equipment.
- Short term rental and shared equipment oversight using history playback and find device features.
