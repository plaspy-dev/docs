# GOTOP - S7

![S7](./tracker.png)

The GOTOP S7 is a waterproof, rugged GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset tracking across cars, motorcycles, boats and commercial fleets. Built for outdoor and fleet applications, the S7 combines integrated GPS and cellular antennas with a wide 8–92V DC input range and IP66 protection to deliver reliable position updates and basic telemetry in demanding conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S7 can stream live location, ignition and analog readings, and peripheral data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its built in fuel gauge support and configurable RS232 port make it a flexible option for organizations that want to add fuel monitoring, peripheral integrations or anti theft workflows within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking, alerting and cloud telemetry integration.
- IP66 waterproof enclosure with integrated antennas for dependable outdoor use.
- Wide 8–92V DC input range to suit a variety of vehicle power systems.
- High sensitivity GNSS and cellular modem for consistent positioning and connectivity.
- Built in fuel theft and fill detection using the vehicle factory fuel gauge with no external sensor needed.
- Flexible I O including ignition digital input, analog input, open drain output and RS232 for peripherals.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The S7 transmits GPS coordinates and telemetry to Plaspy where the data is visualized, analyzed and used to trigger alerts and reports. Plaspy ingests position, ignition status, analog fuel readings and RS232 sourced events to provide continuous operational visibility across fleets and mobile assets.

- Live location on Plaspy maps for real time vehicle tracking and dispatching.
- Ignition and trip logging based on ACC input for automated trip reports and hours tracking.
- Fuel monitoring and anomaly alerts in Plaspy using readings from the factory fuel gauge or analog inputs.
- Alerts for movement, tampering or fuel events that feed into Plaspy workflows and notifications.
- Historical reporting and exportable telemetry for compliance, audits and performance analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet management for vans, trucks and mixed vehicle fleets requiring robust power tolerance.
- Anti theft and immobilization workflows using movement detection, alerts and configurable outputs.
- Fuel management and loss prevention by monitoring factory fuel gauge behavior without heavy retrofits.
- Asset tracking for boats, motorcycles and equipment exposed to outdoor conditions.
- Specialty integrations where cameras, RFID or other RS232 peripherals supplement GPS telemetry.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The S7 is a practical choice for organizations that need a rugged tracker with reliable connectivity and built in fuel telemetry. Its IP66 rating and broad input voltage range reduce constraints in harsh environments and across diverse vehicle types, while the RS232 option allows peripheral data to be correlated with location and event streams in Plaspy.

Paired with Plaspy, the S7 helps centralize live tracking, alerting and historical analysis so operators can monitor fleet status, respond to incidents and review telemetry trends from a single platform. For teams that require fuel monitoring, anti theft controls and peripheral integration without extensive retrofitting, the S7 offers a balanced feature set that complements Plaspy's dashboards and reporting.
