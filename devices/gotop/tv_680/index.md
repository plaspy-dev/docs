# GOTOP - TV-680

![TV-680](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TV-680 is a compact personal GPS tracker that uses satellite positioning to determine its location and the cellular network to transmit that information. It is described as a device intended for protecting and keeping track of elderly individuals and children, and for general safety and remote positioning to help protect property. The tracker provides single location replies on demand and supports two way voice communication with preset call functions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TV-680 can feed location information and event signals into a centralized tracking platform for visibility and operational oversight. Its call based location reply and two way talk features make it useful for scenarios where both position awareness and direct voice contact are important. When used with Plaspy, the TV-680 can contribute to consolidated maps, monitoring views, alerts, and reporting across people and assets.

## Key Highlights

- Uses satellite based GPS positioning combined with GSM transmission via a SIM card for remote location reporting.
- Designed for personal safety and remote positioning, suitable for elder care and child monitoring scenarios.
- Call based single location reply that returns longitude and latitude after ringing the device a defined number of times.
- Two way voice capability with programmable call buttons for quick contact to preset numbers.
- Incoming call handling with SOS button answer, automatic answer after a timeout, and manual hang up option.
- Simple, event driven interaction model that complements centralized tracking platforms like Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TV-680 can be integrated into Plaspy by using its location and event signals as inputs for Plaspy's monitoring and reporting features. Plaspy collects location updates and event information to present them on maps, feeds, and dashboards so teams can act on them from a single interface.

- Display of current device position on Plaspy maps for fast situational awareness.
- Location history logging to review recent movements and past positions.
- Alerting and notifications in Plaspy when predefined conditions are met or when SOS events occur.
- Two way call events and incoming call indications available as contextual notes for operators.
- Consolidated fleet or user lists so personal trackers like the TV-680 appear alongside other assets for unified oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring of elderly family members or assisted living residents for routine location checks.
- Child safety and caregiver tracking for school runs or outings.
- Remote positioning for small property or portable asset protection.
- Lone person safety where a combination of location reply and voice contact is desirable.
- Situations that require quick two way communication in addition to positional awareness.
