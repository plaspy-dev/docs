# GOTOP - VT-380

![VT-380](./tracker.jpg)

The VT-380 is a professional GPS/GPRS vehicle tracker built for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and fleet management. Designed for fleet operators, rental companies and vehicle security integrators, the VT-380 pairs a high-sensitivity SiRF‑Star III GPS module with a quad-band GSM modem to deliver consistent position fixes, configurable reporting and resilient communications via SMS or GPRS \(TCP/UDP\). Its long-term logging, comprehensive alarms and engine-cut output make it a practical choice for anti-theft and telematics workflows on the Plaspy platform.

Plaspy compatibility means you can feed VT-380 telemetry — location, alerts and sensor data — directly into Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reports for centralized fleet visibility. When GPRS is unavailable the VT-380 stores up to 180,000 waypoints in internal flash and automatically uploads stored data once connectivity resumes, preserving route history and event details for compliance and analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for robust real-time tracking and fleet management.
- High-sensitivity SiRF‑Star III GNSS for reliable position fixes and typical 10 m \(2D RMS\) accuracy.
- Quad-band GSM with SMS and GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) communication plus internal flash logging for offline data retention.
- Comprehensive alarms: SOS panic, geo-fencing, movement, speeding, power-cut and GPS blind area entry/exit.
- Engine-cut output \(remote immobilizer\) and included relay for anti-theft and remote engine control.
- Two analog inputs for fuel monitoring or other sensor telemetry and five digital inputs for vehicle event signals.
- Compact, vehicle-grade design with 850 mAh backup battery and low-power operation via built-in motion sensor.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the VT-380 with Plaspy is straightforward: VT-380 transmits location and event packets over GPRS \(or SMS as fallback\) to Plaspy's server endpoints using TCP/UDP. Plaspy ingests telemetry and maps it to vehicle records to enable live tracking, historical playback and rule-based alerts. When the device loses GPRS connectivity, stored waypoints are uploaded automatically after reconnection so Plaspy has a complete route history.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy via GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) or SMS fallback.
- Alarm events \(SOS, geo-fence, speeding, movement, power-cut, GPS blind area entry/exit\) stream into Plaspy for immediate alerts and notifications.
- Fuel monitoring via the VT-380's analog inputs can be transmitted to Plaspy for fuel-level tracking and reports.
- Remote immobilizer / engine-cut is supported and can be controlled or tracked through Plaspy integrations when configured.
- When GPRS is unavailable, VT-380 logs up to 180,000 waypoints in flash memory and uploads them to Plaspy on reconnection.
- If Bluetooth sensors are required in your solution, Plaspy can also correlate external BLE gateway data with VT-380 telemetry to deliver combined sensor and location views.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management — live location, route replay and configurable tracking intervals for dispatch optimization and compliance reporting.
- Anti-theft & immobilization — SOS button, geo-fence alerts and engine-cut relay enable fast response and remote engine immobilization when needed.
- Fuel monitoring & telemetry — analog inputs capture fuel-level signals for consumption analysis and theft detection when paired with Plaspy reporting.
- Remote monitoring & security — power-cut alarm, movement alarm and optional in-vehicle microphone support surveillance and incident investigation workflows.
- Long-term logging — internal flash storage preserves long historical tracks for assets operating in low-connectivity areas until upload is possible.

Choosing the VT-380 for Plaspy-managed deployments delivers a reliable mix of accurate GNSS positioning, proven GSM connectivity and practical vehicle I/O for telemetry and control. Its large on-device log buffer and configurable reporting intervals reduce data gaps for vehicles that operate in intermittent coverage areas. Paired with Plaspy, the VT-380 becomes part of a scalable telematics solution: you get real-time tracking, alerting for anti-theft and safety events, fuel monitoring via analog inputs, and remote immobilization — all accessible through Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reports. The result is lower operational risk, better fleet visibility and straightforward integration into existing fleet management workflows.
