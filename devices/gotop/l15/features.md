# GOTOP - L15 Features

This page summarizes the public features and practical capabilities of the GOTOP L15 pendant GPS tracker as used with Plaspy. It focuses on how the device's tracking, emergency and sensor features present value inside Plaspy for caregiver monitoring and alerting, without reproducing manufacturer service manuals or private configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and the way the device is installed or provisioned. For device specific technical specifications, firmware capabilities and the latest implementation notes consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from GOTOP.

## Feature Overview

The L15 is a compact, wearable pendant tracker designed for eldercare and personal emergency response. It combines cellular 4G connectivity with hybrid positioning methods and emergency voice support to provide caregivers with location and event visibility through Plaspy.

- Real time location tracking using outdoor GPS supplemented by BLE Wi Fi and LBS for better indoor or hybrid positioning
- One touch SOS and two way voice calling with a large speaker for caregiver communication and verification
- Multi day battery life and visible battery and charging status to reduce unexpected downtime
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and compact pendant form factor suitable for daily wear
- Optional health and activity telemetry such as step counting and infrared temperature sensing to support wellbeing monitoring

## Core Features of GOTOP - L15

- 4G cellular connectivity paired with GPS for continuous real time position reporting
- Dual LDS GPS GSM antennas to improve satellite reception and signal reliability outdoors
- BLE 5.0 support for indoor positioning assistance and pairing with nearby beacons or sensors
- Hybrid positioning using GPS plus Wi Fi and LBS to improve coverage in urban and indoor scenarios
- One touch SOS button and two way calling capability with a large speaker for clear voice communication
- Optional sensors and telemetry including a 3D accelerometer for movement and step counting and an optional infrared temperature sensor
- Replaceable nano SIM slot and magnetic 2 pin charging for convenient field use and provisioning
- IP67 rated housing for water resistance and everyday wear durability

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the L15 device telemetry and events so caregivers can monitor location, device status and emergency alerts in a unified interface. Data from the pendant appears as location updates, event notifications and telemetry points that integrate with Plaspy workflows for monitoring and response.

- Real time GPS coordinates and location history displayed in Plaspy for tracking and route review
- SOS event alerts and associated device status forwarded to caregiver dashboards and notification channels
- Battery level and charging status visible in device summaries to plan maintenance and charging cycles
- Activity and movement telemetry such as step counts and movement events available for wellbeing monitoring and trend analysis
- BLE and Wi Fi assisted positioning information used to improve indoor location accuracy when available
- Replaceable SIM provisioning and basic device metadata available for inventory and carrier management inside Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Eldercare monitoring where caregivers need immediate alerts and location visibility for assisted living residents and home care clients
- Personal safety for vulnerable adults who require a simple SOS button and two way communication with a caregiver or response team
- Care program telemetry to collect periodic wellbeing signals like step counts and optional temperature readings for trend monitoring
- Daily wear tracking for active seniors who want a compact, water resistant pendant for outings and light exercise
- Centralized monitoring where multiple device types are managed together for coordinated response and oversight

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features listed as optional such as infrared temperature sensing or particular activity workflows may require specific hardware revisions or firmware levels
- Indoor positioning performance depends on local availability of BLE beacons Wi Fi access points and the environment; results vary by installation
- Regional cellular band support and carrier compatibility may differ by product variant; confirm the model SKU for your region
