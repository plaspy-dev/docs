# GOTOP - L15

![L15](./tracker.jpg)

The GOTOP L15 is a wearable 4G pendant GPS tracker designed for eldercare and personal emergency response. Compact and rugged, the device combines cellular connectivity with hybrid positioning support and built in voice communication to provide real time tracking, one touch SOS alerts and two way calling in a form factor intended for everyday wear.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the L15 brings location, event alerts and basic telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and workflows. That integration allows caregivers and administrators to monitor location history, receive immediate SOS notifications and track device status from within Plaspy for coordinated responses and ongoing wellbeing oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible pendant tracker tailored for eldercare and personal emergency response.
- 4G cellular connectivity with GPS plus BLE and Wi Fi LBS support for hybrid indoor and outdoor positioning.
- One touch SOS button and clear two way voice calling through a large built in speaker for emergency communication.
- Multi day battery life suitable for daily use and standby monitoring to reduce charging frequency.
- Rugged IP67 water resistant enclosure and compact pendant shape for comfortable daily wear.
- Optional health oriented features such as step counting and temperature sensing to support caregiver telemetry.
- Replaceable nano SIM and magnetic charging for straightforward device provisioning and field use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The L15 transmits location data, SOS events and basic telemetry to Plaspy where the platform aggregates those inputs into maps, alerts and device status panels. Plaspy ingests the device messages and presents them to caregivers and operational staff so they can act on location updates and emergency signals in real time.

- Real time location updates and history visible in Plaspy maps and tracking views.
- SOS event notifications routed into Plaspy alert workflows for rapid caregiver response.
- Battery level and device status monitoring to help manage device availability from the Plaspy console.
- Activity and motion summaries delivered to Plaspy for trend reporting and wellbeing checks.
- Two way voice event context available alongside alerts to verify incidents and coordinate responses.
- Hybrid indoor positioning support through BLE and Wi Fi LBS to improve location visibility in mixed environments.

## Typical Use Cases

- Eldercare and assisted living programs that need reliable emergency alerts and location visibility for residents.
- Personal safety monitoring for vulnerable adults who require quick access to caregiver support.
- Home care and remote wellbeing programs that track activity patterns and receive periodic sensor updates.
- Day to day use by active seniors who want a compact, water resistant pendant for outings and light exercise.
- Response center workflows where SOS events and voice confirmation are used to triage incidents.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The L15 is purpose built for personal safety and caregiver monitoring, and its compatibility with Plaspy makes it a practical choice for organizations that require integrated alerting, location visibility and simple device management in a single platform. Its wearable design, durable housing and multi day battery life reduce day to day friction while two way voice and SOS features give caregivers a direct way to confirm and respond to incidents.

Because the L15 focuses on personal emergency response rather than vehicle telemetry, it fits well into care programs, response centers and safety initiatives that prioritize rapid alerts and continuous monitoring. When paired with Plaspy, the device contributes location, event and basic sensor data into workflows that support coordinated care and operational oversight.
