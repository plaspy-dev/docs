# GOTOP - D22

<div className="device-logo">
![D22](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP D22 is a waterproof GPS tracker designed for vehicle tracking and fleet management. It combines 4G wireless communication with GPS and BDS satellite positioning to provide real time location and monitoring for vehicles. The unit includes an industrial high integration design with a full built in antenna and a built in 3 axis sensor to support reliable tracking in a range of operational conditions.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the D22 is relevant for organizations that need continuous visibility and event reporting from their vehicles. Its support for multiple alarm types, two way audio, electronic fence, external relay control and local data storage makes it a practical option to pair with Plaspy for fleet monitoring, alerts, historical playback and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Waterproof construction suitable for vehicle use and exposure to the elements
- Real time tracking via 4G communication with GPS and BDS satellite positioning
- Built in high sensitivity antenna and 3 axis sensor for stable location reporting
- Multiple alarm functions including power failure overspeed movement and low battery alerts
- Support for external oil cut relay and door sensor integration for added security
- Two way communication with built in microphone and speaker plus SOS alarm capability
- Local storage for offline data retention and subsequent supplementary transmission

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GOTOP D22 supplies location and event data that Plaspy uses to present live maps, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to give teams a consolidated view of vehicle status and alarm conditions.

- Live position updates displayed on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility
- Alarm forwarding and notifications for events such as power failure overspeed movement and SOS
- Geofence monitoring and electronic fence event logging within Plaspy
- Historical playback and reporting that include offline stored data uploaded by the device
- Status indicators for door sensor and relay related events to support security workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location and status monitoring for transportation companies
- Security and remote immobilization workflows using external relay support
- Delivery and logistics tracking with geofence and movement alerts
- Asset protection for commercial vehicles with SOS and door sensor integration
- Service and field fleets that require two way communication and event logging
