# GOTOP - VT-810

<div className="device-logo">
![VT-810](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP VT-810 is a compact vehicle tracker that combines a GPS module with a GSM GPRS communication module to deliver accurate and timely position information. Its small form factor and discreet design make it suitable for concealed installation in a wide range of vehicles, and the device is designed to provide continuous location updates for fleet oversight and anti theft applications. When satellite signals are limited, the VT-810 can fall back to GSM base station positioning to keep location data available.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the VT-810 can forward its location feed to the Plaspy platform for mapping, monitoring, and operational oversight. Integration with Plaspy enables businesses to consolidate vehicle location data into a single tracking dashboard, making it straightforward to use the VT-810 for fleet visibility, route oversight, and routine location reporting without changing how teams manage tracking data.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet design suitable for concealed mounting in vehicles
- Accurate GPS satellite positioning for regular location updates
- GSM base station positioning provides location fallback when GPS is limited
- Real time location reporting to system platforms or preset phone numbers
- Designed for straightforward integration with tracking platforms
- Practical choice for fleet monitoring and anti theft tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT-810 sends its positioning information to a system platform, which is how it works together with Plaspy to provide live vehicle visibility and historical location records. Once the VT-810 is configured to report to Plaspy, the platform can process the incoming location feed and present it alongside other fleet data.

- Plaspy displays VT-810 location updates on interactive maps for live monitoring
- The device feed supports fleet level visibility and vehicle by vehicle tracking
- Plaspy can use incoming location data to trigger alerts for movement or boundary events
- Location history from VT-810 can be included in reporting and operational summaries
- Plaspy makes it possible to centralize VT-810 data for dispatching and oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small and medium vehicle fleets requiring regular location updates
- Anti theft monitoring where discreet placement is desirable
- Rental vehicle tracking to maintain visibility of vehicle location and movements
- Delivery and logistics visibility for route monitoring and accountability
- Owner operated vehicles where remote position updates are needed
