# GOTOP - VT-810

![VT-810](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-810 is a highly reliable and efficient vehicle tracker designed to provide accurate and real-time positioning information. This compact device combines a GPS module and a GSM/GPRS module, ensuring precise location tracking and seamless communication. With its small size and discreet design, the VT-810 can be easily installed and concealed in your vehicle, making it an ideal choice for fleet management and anti-theft purposes.

Equipped with advanced GPS technology, the VT-810 can provide accurate satellite positioning information, allowing you to track the exact location of your vehicle at any given time. In situations where GPS satellite information is unavailable, the tracker can rely on GSM base station positioning to provide you with the latitude and longitude coordinates. This ensures that you always have access to reliable and up-to-date location data.

With the VT-810, you can conveniently send the positioning information to a system platform or a preset mobile phone number. This enables you to easily monitor and manage your vehicle, ensuring its safety and security. Whether you're a business owner looking to optimize your fleet operations or a concerned individual wanting to keep track of your personal vehicle, the GOTOP VT-810 is a reliable and user-friendly solution.

#### Key Features:

- Compact size and good concealment
- High precision GPS satellite positioning
- GSM base station positioning when GPS is unavailable
- Real-time location tracking
- Easy integration with system platforms
- Supports preset mobile phone number for location updates
