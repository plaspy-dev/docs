# GOTOP - G30B

<div className="device-logo">
![G30B](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The G30B is a compact, magnet-mounted GPS tracker designed for long-term, low-maintenance tracking of vehicles and movable assets. Marketed in equipment catalogs as a discreet unit, the device combines hybrid GNSS positioning (GPS and Beidou) with LBS fallback, a substantial internal battery, and strong NdFeB magnets for non permanent mounting. Its small form factor and focus on extended standby life make it suitable where infrequent servicing and reliable location updates are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G30B can feed location and status information into Plaspy for centralized fleet visibility and event handling. Its long battery life, tamper alerts, configurable reporting windows, and basic external integration options align with common Plaspy workflows for remote asset monitoring, anti theft alerts, and scheduled reporting that balance operational visibility with battery preservation.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for centralized tracking and alerting across fleets and assets.
- Hybrid GNSS plus Beidou positioning with LBS fallback to improve location availability.
- Large internal battery designed for extended standby intervals to minimize maintenance.
- Magnet mounted ABS housing for quick, non permanent attachment to vehicles or equipment.
- Anti theft features including tamper and dismantle alarms and anti detection operating modes.
- UART integration option for connecting external modules to support additional fleet workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the G30B transmits its location fixes and status events to the platform where those packets are processed for live maps, alerts, history playback, and routine reporting. Plaspy can be configured to use the device reporting to optimize between timeliness and battery life, and to surface security events and maintenance indicators to operators.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy for map display and route monitoring.
- Tamper and dismantle alarms forwarded as security events to trigger notifications and recovery workflows.
- Battery level and sleep wake telemetry available in Plaspy to plan maintenance and replacements.
- Configurable reporting schedules to limit transmissions during idle periods and extend device life.
- Integration of device events into Plaspy reporting and historical playback for audits and incident reconstruction.
- Ability to combine G30B feeds with other data sources in Plaspy for richer operational context.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental vehicle tracking where discreet mounting and long battery life reduce retrieval and maintenance cycles.
- Financed or collateralized vehicles that need tamper alerts and event history to support recovery efforts.
- Taxi and passenger vehicle monitoring where accurate position updates and configurable reporting support operations.
- Trailers and equipment that require long duration visibility when regular power is unavailable.
- General movable asset tracking for construction equipment, containers, and other high value items.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G30B is a practical choice for organizations that need a low maintenance, discreet tracker to provide regular position updates and security alerts. Its combination of hybrid positioning, magnet mounting, and long standby life complements Plaspy’s platform capabilities for fleet oversight, alarm management, and historical analysis without requiring frequent on site intervention.

Plaspy can ingest the G30B’s telemetry and surface it alongside other fleet data to create operational reports, trigger anti theft workflows, and schedule maintenance based on battery status.
