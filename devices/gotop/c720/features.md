# GOTOP - C720 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GOTOP C720 Relay Car GPS Tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing features, operational value, and the types of telemetry and alerts you can expect when integrating the device with Plaspy for vehicle tracking and anti theft workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information below is grounded in the C720 product description and highlights commonly reported capabilities; for the most current device specific details consult the official GOTOP documentation and your installer.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP C720 is a compact, hardwired relay style tracker designed for discreet vehicle installation. It combines assisted GNSS positioning, internal antennas, and GSM GPRS communications to provide real time location updates and alarm telemetry for integration with cloud tracking platforms such as Plaspy.

- Ultra small relay style form factor for hidden installation in cars and motorcycles
- AGPS assisted GNSS reception with U Blox module for fast and accurate position fixes
- GSM GPRS and SMS communication to send locations and alarms to online platforms
- Remote immobilizer and main power cut detection to support anti theft workflows
- Multi alarm telemetry including geo fencing triggers, vibration or movement alerts, and low battery notifications

## Core Features of GOTOP - C720

- AGPS assisted locating using a U Blox GNSS module for improved fix time and accuracy
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas with multi channel GNSS reception for reliable position reporting
- GSM GPRS communication plus SMS and call reporting for flexible telemetry delivery
- Compact relay style hardwired design sized for discreet installation inside vehicles
- Remote immobilizer or power cut off capability to support vehicle disablement
- Multi alarm support including geo fence alarms, movement or vibration alerts, low battery and main power cut detection
- Low power sleeping mode to reduce draw when the vehicle is off and extend monitoring intervals
- Support for common GSM frequency bands for broad cellular coverage where available

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When configured and connected, the C720 sends position and status messages that Plaspy ingests for visualization, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy displays real time locations, stores historical tracks, and surfaces alarms so fleet managers and operators can act on events reported by the device.

- Real time location updates and historical route playback appear on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- Geo fencing events reported by the device generate notifications and can be used to trigger workflows in Plaspy
- Movement and vibration alarms provide immediate alerts in Plaspy for potential theft or unauthorized use
- Main power cut detection and remote immobilizer events can be reflected in incident logs and monitoring dashboards
- SMS or GPRS delivered telemetry from the C720 is mapped into Plaspy reports and notification channels for centralized oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle tracking and recovery after theft due to compact hidden installation
- Fleet management for service vehicles where discrete installation is desirable
- Anti theft interventions combining movement alarms and remote immobilizer actions
- Route replay and compliance audits using stored historical tracks in Plaspy
- Motorcycle tracking and monitoring where small form factor is required
- Monitoring of high value or sensitive assets that need discreet telemetry
