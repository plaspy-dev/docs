# GOTOP - C750 Features

This page provides a public, feature-oriented overview of the GOTOP C750 OBD GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is intended to describe practical functions, event reporting behavior, and the typical value delivered when the device is connected to the Plaspy platform for real-time monitoring and alerting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a general guide to the C750 in Plaspy and consult the official GOTOP documentation for the latest, device specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The GOTOP C750 is a compact plug-and-play OBD-II tracker built for fast deployment in cars and light commercial vehicles. Designed to report location and events to Plaspy over cellular data, the C750 emphasizes quick installation, basic telemetry reporting, and anti-theft monitoring without the need for hardwiring.

- Plug-and-play OBD-II form factor for rapid installation and minimal setup time.
- GSM/GPRS cellular reporting combined with U‑Blox GNSS positioning for continuous location updates.
- Configurable alerts such as overspeed, geo-fence breach, movement and vibration alarms, and power loss reporting.
- Built-in rechargeable backup battery to continue sending critical reports during external power interruptions.
- Google Maps location links available via SMS or data reporting for quick visual verification of vehicle position.

## Core Features of GOTOP - C750

- OBD-II plug-in installation for noninvasive vehicle connection and quick deployment.
- GSM/GPRS connectivity for real-time tracking and event reporting over cellular networks.
- U‑Blox GNSS positioning with A‑GPS assistance for reliable location fixes.
- Configurable alerting including overspeed, geo-fence breach, movement, vibration, and power cut notifications.
- Built-in rechargeable backup battery to allow limited reporting during vehicle power loss.
- SMS and GPRS reporting options for flexible data delivery methods.
- Compact, lightweight design suitable for unobtrusive in-vehicle use.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected, the C750 sends position and event data that Plaspy ingests, correlates, and displays for operational visibility. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and maps incoming reports into its dashboard, alerting, and reporting systems.

- Real-time location updates from the C750 appear on Plaspy maps to support live monitoring and historical tracking.
- Overspeed and geo-fence events reported by the device are processed by Plaspy to trigger notifications and alerts.
- Movement, vibration, and power-cut reports are surfaced in Plaspy to assist anti-theft workflows and incident investigation.
- SMS-delivered Google Maps URLs or GPRS position reports enable quick verification of vehicle position within Plaspy or by direct message.
- Plaspy correlates device events with other fleet data to support reporting, filtering, and operational workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars and light commercial vehicles requiring fast, low-effort installation.

- Rental and shared vehicle fleets that need temporary or easily transferable tracking hardware.
- Driver behavior monitoring such as overspeed detection and route verification.
- Small fleet operational visibility where quick deployment and straightforward telemetry are priorities.
