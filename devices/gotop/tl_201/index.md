# GOTOP - TL-201

![TL-201](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP TL-201 is a compact and powerful GPS tracker designed for personal positioning, pet monitoring, and vehicle security. It utilizes the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites to provide real-time tracking and location information. With its small size and sleek design, the TL-201 can easily be attached to a keychain or carried in a pocket, making it convenient for personal use. 

One of the standout features of the TL-201 is its ability to transmit longitude and latitude coordinates via SMS or GPRS. This means that you can receive real-time location updates on your cell phone, allowing you to keep track of your loved ones, pets, or vehicles. Additionally, the TL-201 can reply to SMS messages with a Google map link, which, when clicked, displays the detailed position information on Google Maps. This feature is especially useful for smartphone users who can easily view the location directly on their mobile devices.

The TL-201 also offers voice surveillance capabilities, allowing you to dial the tracker and listen in to the surrounding environment. It also supports two-way communication, enabling you to have a conversation with the holder of the device. In case of emergencies, the TL-201 is equipped with an SOS button that, when pressed, sends a help alert with location information to authorized phone numbers. Furthermore, the device features a geo-fence alarm, which sends a message to authorized numbers if it breaches a predefined district. With its low battery alert, the TL-201 ensures that you are notified when the battery is running low. It also supports the establishment of up to five authorized telephone numbers, providing flexibility and convenience for multiple users.
