# GOTOP - L17

<div className="device-logo">
![L17](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP L17 is a compact 4G SOS GPS smart bracelet designed for personal safety and health monitoring. Built in a wristwatch form factor with IP67 protection, the L17 combines GNSS positioning, LTE cellular connectivity, and optional Bluetooth support to deliver continuous location and telemetry. It pairs sensor rich health tracking with an SOS button and two way calling, making it suitable for children, seniors, lone workers and general personal asset protection.

Because the L17 transmits location and sensor data over cellular networks, it can feed real time tracking and telemetry into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerting and reporting. Plaspy compatibility lets organizations and families view location history, receive SOS notifications, and incorporate health and activity signals into dashboards and alarms alongside other devices.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible wearable that delivers real time location and telemetry to Plaspy for alerts and reports
- 4G LTE connectivity with wide band coverage for reliable data transmission across regions
- Integrated health sensors including heart rate, SpO2, blood pressure and temperature for ongoing monitoring
- SOS button and two way calling for immediate alerting and voice response when needed
- IP67 wristwatch form factor that is comfortable for daily wear and personal use
- Support for nano SIM and eSIM with long battery life suitable for multi day use
- Optional Bluetooth support to extend short range telemetry with nearby sensors or beacons

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the L17 sends GNSS location fixes and health or activity telemetry over its cellular link to cloud services. Plaspy ingests these streams into a unified interface where location, SOS events and sensor data are available for live monitoring, historical review and automated alerts.

- Real time location and telemetry on Plaspy maps for live oversight and dispatch
- SOS and voice events forwarded to Plaspy alarm workflows so responders can be notified
- Health and activity data available to trigger alerts or enrich incident context in Plaspy
- Bluetooth paired sensor data relayed through the device and surfaced in Plaspy for short range monitoring
- Historical tracking and geo fencing stored and accessible in Plaspy for compliance and review
- Alerts such as low battery or geofence exit can be configured and routed through Plaspy notification channels

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety with wrist mounted tracking, SOS alerts and geofence notifications for caregivers
- Elderly monitoring where health telemetry and fall detection are important for caregiver oversight
- Lone worker protection with a wearable that supports emergency alerts and two way voice
- Personal anti theft and travel protection for individuals carrying valuables or moving through unfamiliar areas
- Short range sensor scenarios where BLE beacons or sensors augment location and status data

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The L17 is a useful fit for organizations and families that need a people centric wearable which combines location, emergency alerting and health telemetry. Integrating the L17 with Plaspy offers a single platform to monitor live position, respond to SOS events, and review historical data for audits or investigations. Its wristwatch form factor and sensor set make it especially appropriate where continuous personal use and discreet monitoring are required.
