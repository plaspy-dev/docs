# GOTOP - VT360 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the GOTOP VT360 GPS tracker and how its capabilities integrate with Plaspy for fleet management and vehicle monitoring. It summarizes the VT360's primary functions as described by the manufacturer and explains how those functions are typically reflected inside Plaspy for visibility, alerts, and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, this page highlights practical capabilities rather than exhaustive technical specifications; for device specific details please consult the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The VT360 is a professional vehicle tracker designed for reliable positioning and status reporting to third party platforms such as Plaspy. It pairs high sensitivity GNSS positioning with GSM GPRS communications and a set of security and telemetry features useful for fleet operators and vehicle security deployments.

- Real time location reporting via GPRS or SMS for immediate tracking and map sharing.
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning for consistent fixes and stable tracking performance.
- Internal backup battery to preserve telemetry and location reporting during main power loss.
- Comprehensive alarm set including SOS, geo fence, blind area alerts, low battery and overspeed warnings.
- Remote control functions including engine cut immobilizer and remote listening for recovery scenarios.

## Core Features of GOTOP - VT360

- SiRFstar III GNSS module for high sensitivity satellite tracking and reliable position fixes.
- GSM GPRS communications supporting SMS and GPRS based reporting to fleet platforms.
- Time and distance based reporting plus mileage logs for operational and compliance records.
- Comprehensive security alarms including SOS, geo fence alerts, GPS blind area alerts, overspeed warnings, and low battery notifications.
- Remote immobilizer or engine cut capability to assist with theft response and vehicle recovery.
- Analog input for external sensor telemetry such as fuel or temperature sensors where supported by installation.
- Internal backup battery to maintain tracking and event reporting during main power interruptions.
- SMS link support for immediate location viewing on mapping services for quick sharing and response.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event messages sent by the VT360 so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in real time, receive alerts, and review historical activity. Integration typically involves configuring the device to forward its SMS or GPRS reports to the account endpoint or phone number associated with your Plaspy setup.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks become visible on Plaspy maps and reports.
- VT360 alarm messages such as SOS, geo fence entries or exits, blind area alerts, overspeed and low battery are presented as Plaspy alerts for timely response.
- Time and distance reports and mileage transmissions feed Plaspy reporting tools for operational analysis and record keeping.
- Analog sensor data from the VT360 can be displayed in Plaspy as telemetry trends when the device is configured to send those inputs.
- Remote commands such as immobilizer or output control are executed via SMS or GPRS workflows supported by Plaspy and the tracker configuration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and recovery workflows using SOS, geo fence alerts, and remote engine cut.
- Route and mileage reporting for operational billing, compliance, and route optimization.
- Driver safety monitoring with overspeed alerts and historical track review for coaching.
- Fuel and temperature monitoring through the analog input to support efficiency programs.
- Incident investigation and situational awareness using remote listening and event history.
- Continuous tracking during power loss thanks to the internal backup battery for critical visibility.
