# GOTOP - TV-690

<div className="device-logo">
![TV-690](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GOTOP TV-690 is a compact personal remote positioning device that combines a GPS module with GSM GPRS connectivity to provide accurate location information. Designed for straightforward tracking, the TV-690 returns location coordinates that can be viewed on mapping services and is positioned for applications where portability and reliable position reporting matter.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TV-690 can be integrated into centralized tracking workflows to give organizations and caregivers consistent visibility over tracked assets and people. Its support for SMS location replies and Google Maps links makes it a practical choice for pairing with Plaspy’s monitoring, reporting, and alerting features without requiring complex setup.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for personal carrying or discreet placement
- Integrates GPS positioning with GSM GPRS connectivity for remote reporting
- High accuracy in dynamic conditions for reliable location updates
- SMS based location replies and Google Maps link support for easy access to positions
- Suitable for tracking people such as children or elderly individuals
- Can be used for property protection and basic animal tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GOTOP TV-690 becomes part of a centralized tracking platform where location updates and historical routes are collected and presented on a map for operational oversight. Plaspy can consolidate position reports from the device and surface them in dashboards and reports to support day to day monitoring.

- Receive and visualise location updates from TV-690 units inside Plaspy for real time visibility
- Configure notifications and alerts around location events to support safety and oversight
- Maintain historical location logs and generate reports for audits or operational review
- Group and manage multiple TV-690 devices as assets or profiles within Plaspy
- Use map links and coordinate data provided by the TV-690 to validate positions shown in Plaspy
