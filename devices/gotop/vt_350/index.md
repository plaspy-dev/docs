# GOTOP - VT-350

![VT-350](./logo.jpg)

The OBD GPS Tracker VT-350 from GOTOP is a reliable and easy-to-use device for vehicle tracking and fleet management. It is designed to be inserted directly into the car's OBD II interface, making installation a breeze. With its built-in GSM and GPS antennas, this tracker utilizes the GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellite positioning system to provide accurate and real-time tracking information.

One of the standout features of the VT-350 is its ability to track on command or by time interval via SMS/GPRS. This means that you can easily monitor your vehicle's location and movement by simply sending a text message or using the GPRS data connection. Additionally, you can track by distance or even by the location of the GSM base station, giving you multiple options for tracking accuracy.

With the VT-350, you can also check the car's real physical address, thanks to its advanced GPS technology. By sending a mobile SMS, you can retrieve information such as latitude, longitude, speed, direction, and even the odometer reading. The device also allows you to check the location directly on Google Maps, providing a visual representation of your vehicle's whereabouts.

Other notable features of the VT-350 include mileage reporting, over-speed and geo-fence alerts, and a movement alert that can be used as an alarm. In the event of a power cut, the device will also send an alarm notification. With its built-in GPS and GSM antennas, you can trust that the VT-350 will provide reliable and accurate tracking information for your vehicle or fleet.
