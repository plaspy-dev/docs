# GOTOP - VT-350

![VT-350](./logo.jpg)

The GOTOP VT-350 is an OBD GPS tracker designed for straightforward vehicle tracking and fleet management. The device plugs into a vehicle OBD II interface and uses built in GSM and GPS antennas to report position and movement. It supports tracking on command or at regular intervals over SMS or GPRS and can provide location coordinates, speed, direction, odometer readings, and mileage information that help keep fleets and vehicles visible in real time.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT-350 can feed its location and alert data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. When connected through standard SMS or GPRS reporting, the VT-350's position updates and event messages can be used inside Plaspy to improve operational oversight, enable geofence and over speed alerts, and keep a historical record of vehicle mileage and movement for fleet managers.

## Key Highlights

- Simple plug in connection to the vehicle OBD II interface for fast deployment.
- Built in GSM and GPS antennas for satellite positioning and cellular reporting.
- Supports on demand tracking via SMS and continuous reporting over GPRS.
- Provides location coordinates, speed, direction, and odometer information.
- Built in alerts for over speed, geofence exit or entry, movement, and power loss.
- Capability to view locations on mapping services for visual verification.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT-350 becomes part of a managed tracking workflow that adds visibility and context to raw device reports. Plaspy ingests the VT-350 position updates and event messages to present consolidated maps, alerts, and logs for operators and fleet administrators.

- Display real time location and recent track history of VT-350 equipped vehicles on Plaspy maps.
- Receive and manage alerts from the device such as over speed, geofence triggers, movement, and power loss within Plaspy notification channels.
- Aggregate odometer and mileage reports into Plaspy for periodic review and operational reporting.
- Use Plaspy reporting to analyze vehicle movement patterns and generate summaries for fleet oversight.
- Filter and search VT-350 device activity to support dispatching and route verification tasks.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small to medium sized commercial fleets requiring simple deployment.
- Rental car monitoring to verify vehicle location and mileage between rentals.
- Delivery and service vehicle oversight where regular position updates and alerts improve operational control.
- Theft recovery and movement alerts when an unexpected vehicle departure or power loss occurs.
- Basic driver behavior monitoring focused on speed events and route histories.
