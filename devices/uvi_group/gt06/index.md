# UVI Group - GT06

![GT06](./logo.jpg)

The UVI Group GT06 is a GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and real-time tracking of remote targets. It utilizes the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites to locate and monitor vehicles or motorcycles. With its compact design and easy installation, the GT06 is suitable for a variety of applications, including business, rental, or personal vehicles for monitoring or anti-theft purposes.

Using the GT06 is simple and convenient. The GSM module captures GPS data and sends it to the authorized mobile number via SMS. This allows users to view the current location on their phone or track the location on Google Earth or Google Maps. Additionally, GPS data can be transmitted via GPRS to an Internet server, enabling real-time tracking on a computer.

The GT06 offers a range of features to enhance its functionality. It supports continuous GPS positioning and real-time reporting through GPRS. Users can also inquire about the location through SMS. With its GSM quad-band frequency, the GT06 can be used worldwide. The GPS chip from UBLOX and the MTK60 GSM module ensure reliable and accurate tracking performance. The tracker operates on a wide voltage range of 9-24V DC and has low power consumption, making it suitable for long-term use.

Technical Specifications:

- GSM frequency ranges: 850/900/1800/1900
- Network: GSM/GPRS/GPS
- GPS chip: UBLOX
- GSM module: MTK60
- GPRS: Class10, TCP/IP
- Working voltage: 9~24V DC
- Working current: ≈22mA \(12V DC\), ≈12mA \(24V DC\)
- GPS positioning time: cold start≈38s \(Open sky\), warm start≈32s, hot start≈2s \(Open sky\)
- GPS positioning accuracy: 10m \(2D RM\)
- Working ambient temperature: -20°C~+70°C
- Working ambient humidity: 20%~80% RH
- Web-based platform: www.gps228.com
- Work voltage: 9V-36V or 24V-80V
- Storage Temp.: -40°C to +85°C
- Operation Temp.: -20°C to +55°C
- Humidity: 5%--95%
- Dimensions: 97 mm x 51 mm x 15 mm
- Weight: 80g
