# UVI Group - VT02N

<div className="device-logo">
![VT02N](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The UVI Group VT02N is a compact and stylish GPS vehicle tracker designed for discreet real time tracking and monitoring. Its mini form factor and plastic cover make it suitable for unobtrusive installation across a variety of vehicle types. The VT02N uses GSM quad band communication and pairs intelligent software logic with a smart hardware layout to provide consistent position reporting and operational reliability.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT02N can feed location and status information into Plaspy's fleet management environment for visibility and oversight. Its combination of compact size, static drift resistance, and power saving behavior makes it a practical option for organizations that need continuous tracking with reduced maintenance demands. Plaspy can display VT02N data alongside other fleet devices to give a unified operational picture.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet physical design suitable for a wide range of vehicles
- Real time tracking capability for improved location visibility
- GSM quad band connectivity for broad communication coverage
- Intelligent power saving technology to extend operational intervals between charges
- Static drift resistance to help maintain reliable position reporting
- Simple and reliable connector plus wide voltage input range for vehicle compatibility

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT02N streams location and basic device status to Plaspy where that information is presented on maps, timelines, and reports to support fleet oversight and decision making. Plaspy ingests compatible tracker data and makes it actionable for monitoring, alerting, and operational analysis.

- Live location visualization and historical route playback for each VT02N device
- Configurable alerts and notifications based on movement and location events
- Fleet level monitoring to track asset allocation and vehicle availability
- Reporting and exportable logs for utilization and operational review
- Centralized device list in Plaspy so VT02N units appear alongside other compatible trackers

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring company cars and light commercial vehicles for operational control
- Tracking rental or loaner vehicles to maintain inventory and usage records
- Personal vehicle tracking for peace of mind and theft deterrence
- Small fleet management where discreet tracking and long service intervals are preferred
- Asset movement visibility for businesses that need basic real time location data

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT02N is a good match for organizations that prioritize a small footprint device with reliable position reporting and energy efficient behavior. Its broad GSM band support and wide input voltage compatibility make it flexible for many vehicle environments, while static drift resistance helps maintain meaningful tracking accuracy in varied conditions. When combined with Plaspy, VT02N devices contribute to a consolidated view of fleet activity without adding unnecessary complexity.
