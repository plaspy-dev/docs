## How UVI Group devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy can ingest location and status data from UVI Group devices that support standard tracking protocols and telemetry formats. When a UVI Group tracker is configured to send data to Plaspy, you can monitor real time location, historical routes, and device status from the Plaspy platform. The integration focuses on receiving the device telemetry and presenting it in Plaspy dashboards and reports so teams can manage fleets and assets efficiently.

Key integration points typically include device identification, GPS coordinates, timestamps, and basic I O or sensor values. For setup details specific to a model refer to the device documentation that appears in the Plaspy device pages or contact Plaspy support for assistance.

## Help finding the right UVI Group device

Choosing the right tracker depends on your installation environment and monitoring goals. Consider these factors when selecting a UVI Group device:

- Power source and installation method whether vehicle wired or battery powered
- Required connectivity type such as cellular or satellite if available
- I O and sensor needs for inputs like ignition or door status

- Environmental durability and mounting constraints
