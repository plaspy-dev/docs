# UVI Group - GT02

![GT02](./logo.jpg)

The UVI Group GT02 is a compact, portable vehicle tracker designed for monitoring company vehicles and private cars. Described as a mini portable car vehicle tracker, it provides real time location reporting and monitoring through GPS together with GSM GPRS connectivity. The device emphasizes anti theft capability and practical vehicle oversight by sending position, speed, and direction information to a designated server or service for access by phones or computers.

This tracker is listed as compatible with Plaspy, making it a suitable choice for users who want to integrate GT02 data into a fleet management or tracking platform. Because the GT02 can send GPS data over GPRS to a monitoring site, Plaspy can ingest those updates to provide mapping, history, alerts, and operational visibility for vehicles tracked with this device.

## Key Highlights

- Compact mini portable design suitable for open or hidden installation
- Real time tracking and monitoring for vehicle position, speed, and direction
- GPS with high sensitivity and SIRF Star III based positioning for reliable fixes
- GSM GPRS connectivity with quad band support 850 900 1800 1900 MHz for wide network compatibility
- Automatic start when powered and built in monitoring CPU to help maintain reliable operation
- SMS command support, LED status indicators, and physical SOS and power buttons
- Small footprint at approximately 90 x 45 x 12 mm and lightweight at about 45 g

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, GT02 location messages and status reports are routed to the Plaspy platform where they are displayed, stored, and used for operational monitoring. Plaspy leverages the device feed to provide map visualization, alerting, and historical reporting tailored to fleet needs.

- Live map display of vehicle location updates received from the GT02
- Alerts and notifications based on movement, speed, or custom event rules
- Historical tracks and reporting for route review and operational analysis
- Status and connectivity monitoring to identify devices that are offline or need attention
- Centralized fleet view for managing multiple GT02 units across vehicles

## Typical Use Cases

- Company fleet tracking and basic telematics for small to medium fleets
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery support for private and commercial vehicles
- Rental car and shared vehicle oversight to track usage and location
- Field service vehicle supervision for route and dispatch visibility
- Personal vehicle monitoring for family cars and individual owners

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GT02 is a practical choice for organizations that need a compact, cost conscious tracker that provides the core location and status information required for fleet oversight. Its combination of high sensitivity GPS positioning and quad band GSM GPRS connectivity makes it well suited to capture and transmit position data to cloud platforms like Plaspy for mapping and operational workflows.

Because the GT02 is compatible with Plaspy, users can combine the device hardware with Plaspy features such as live location, history, and alerting to create a rounded tracking solution. The device's small size and straightforward feature set make it a fit for deployments where discreet placement and reliable positional updates are priorities.
