# Ulbotech - T363B

![T363B](./tracker.jpg)

The T363B is a plug-and-play OBD GPS vehicle tracker built for robust vehicle monitoring and native Plaspy compatibility. Designed to connect directly to a vehicle’s OBD-II port, the T363B delivers accurate GPS position, CAN/OBD telemetry and driver-behavior events over a quad-band GSM/GPRS link — making it an ideal GPS tracker for fleets, rental services, insurers and vehicle security programs that rely on Plaspy for visualization, alerts and reporting.

With an internal u‑blox 6M GNSS module, Bluetooth 4.0 \(BLE\) support and an integrated immobilizer \(engine cut\) function, the T363B combines anti-theft resilience with rich vehicle telemetry. Its low-power design, internal backup battery and 3‑axis accelerometer ensure continued tracking, motion detection and meaningful telematics for fleet management, safety monitoring and remote immobilization via Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible plug-and-play OBD installation — quick deployment via the vehicle’s OBD port for fast onboarding.
- Reliable real-time tracking over quad-band GSM/GPRS \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) for global coverage and frequent updates.
- High-sensitivity u‑blox 6M GPS with A‑GPS for fast position fixes and sub‑3 m autonomous accuracy.
- Rich vehicle telemetry from OBD and SAE J1939 CAN bus for fleet management, driver profiling and fuel monitoring analytics when integrated with Plaspy.
- Internal immobilizer \(engine cut\) and a digital output for remote ignition control to support anti-theft strategies.
- Integrated Bluetooth 4.0 \(BLE\) to connect sensors or beacons for enhanced monitoring \(e.g., driver ID or cargo sensors\).
- Motion detection and driver behavior analytics using the 3‑axis accelerometer \(harsh braking, rapid acceleration, cornering, high RPM\).
- FOTA firmware updates, auto APN/time zone recognition and configurable tracking by time, distance, course or status.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the T363B streams location and vehicle data via GPRS to the Plaspy backend where data is mapped, analyzed and presented through dashboards, alerts and reports. Plaspy ingests GPS coordinates, OBD/CAN telemetry and event flags so operators can view live positions, playback routes, monitor driver behavior and trigger automated workflows such as immobilization or geofence alerts.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over quad-band GPRS for continuous tracking and historical playback.
- Vehicle status and ignition-related data read from OBD/CAN protocols \(useful for ignition and engine status reporting\).
- Vehicle telemetry \(battery voltage, engine data, RPM and sensor inputs\) that Plaspy can use for fuel monitoring and maintenance analytics when configured.
- Remote immobilizer support via the device’s digital output for controlled engine cut from Plaspy dashboards or alert rules.
- Bluetooth sensors/beacons support \(BLE 4.0\) to pair driver ID tags, temperature sensors or other accessories and feed their data into Plaspy.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management — live vehicle tracking, route playback, driver behavior scoring and remote immobilization to reduce theft and unauthorized use.
- Insurance telematics — collect driver behavior events and trip profiles for usage‑based insurance programs and risk assessment.
- Rental and fleet monitoring — plug-and-play installation for fast turnover, disconnect alerts and remote engine cut for non‑compliance or theft prevention.
- Roadside assistance and safety — location sharing, voice monitoring and telemetry to speed incident response and verify vehicle status.
- Asset protection with BLE sensors — combine BLE tags or temperature sensors for cargo monitoring, teen driver supervision and secure asset tracking.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The T363B delivers a balanced mix of accurate GPS, deep OBD/CAN telemetry and built‑in anti‑theft capability that integrates cleanly with Plaspy. Its plug‑and‑play OBD form factor reduces installation time and minimizes vehicle modification, while BLE support and an internal immobilizer extend use cases beyond basic tracking into driver identification and theft mitigation. FOTA support and robust memory ensure devices remain up to date and retain critical records even when connectivity is intermittent.

For operations that require reliable real‑time tracking, fleet management analytics, telemetry-driven maintenance and secure immobilization, the T363B provides the data and control Plaspy needs to deliver actionable insights. Its combination of motion detection, driver behavior detection and OBD/CAN data makes it a versatile choice for businesses and programs focused on safety, efficiency and anti‑theft protection.
