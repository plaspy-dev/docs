# Ulbotech - T363B

![T363B](./tracker.jpg)

The T363B is a plug and play OBD GPS vehicle tracker designed for robust vehicle monitoring and telematics collection. It connects directly to a vehicle OBD port and provides GPS positioning, OBD and CAN telemetry, and driver behavior events over a cellular link. The device combines positioning with motion detection and an integrated immobilizer function, making it suitable for fleet, rental, insurance, and vehicle security applications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T363B delivers the types of location and vehicle data that Plaspy uses for visualization, alerts and reporting. Its plug and play form factor and telemetry capabilities make onboarding fast and allow operators to view live positions, analyze driver behavior, and trigger operational workflows from within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD form factor for fast deployment and minimal vehicle modification.
- Accurate GPS positioning using an internal u‑blox GNSS module for reliable location data.
- Rich vehicle telemetry from OBD and CAN for maintenance insight, driver scoring and fleet analytics.
- Integrated immobilizer and digital output to support remote engine cut and anti theft workflows.
- Bluetooth support to pair driver ID or external sensors for extended monitoring scenarios.
- Motion detection with a 3 axis accelerometer to capture driver behavior events and sudden maneuvers.
- Internal backup power design to maintain basic tracking and event logging when main power is lost.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the T363B is used with Plaspy, the device streams location, vehicle status and event data to the Plaspy backend where it is mapped, analyzed and surfaced in dashboards, alerts and reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to provide operational visibility and to trigger rules based on location, vehicle state and defined events.

- Live location and historical route playback for fleet oversight and dispatch coordination.
- Vehicle status and ignition related events for accurate trip segmentation and reporting.
- OBD and CAN telemetry fed into Plaspy for maintenance analytics and fuel or engine monitoring when configured.
- Remote immobilizer control available through Plaspy workflows to support theft response and fleet policies.
- BLE sensor and driver tag data integrated to link driver identity or cargo conditions to trips and events.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, driver behavior monitoring and route playback.
- Rental and shared vehicle programs requiring quick install, disconnect alerts and remote immobilization.
- Insurance telematics programs collecting trip and behavior profiles for risk assessment.
- Asset protection and cargo monitoring by combining BLE sensors with vehicle location data.
- Safety and roadside assistance scenarios where location and vehicle telemetry speed response.

For organizations using Plaspy, the device supplies the core datasets Plaspy needs to deliver visibility, automated alerts and operational reporting without complex installations.
