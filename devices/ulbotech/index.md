# Ulbotech

Ulbotech is a brand known for producing compact GPS tracking hardware used in fleet and asset tracking applications. This page explains how Ulbotech devices can be used with Plaspy, what to expect from the hardware family in general terms, and how to find the right device in the catalog. If you are evaluating Ulbotech trackers for vehicle tracking, asset monitoring, or remote telemetry, this guide will help you determine whether Plaspy is the right platform for your needs.

## How Ulbotech devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy accepts location and telemetry data from a wide range of GPS tracker brands, including Ulbotech models that are compatible with standard tracking protocols. In most deployments, an Ulbotech tracker collects GNSS position data and sensor inputs, then transmits that data over cellular networks to Plaspy for processing and visualization.

Typical integration steps are:
- Configure the device to send output to the Plaspy platform using the device web interface or configuration SMS.
- Ensure the device APN and mobile connectivity are active so it can reach Plaspy servers.
- Add the device to your Plaspy account so data is associated with the correct vehicle or asset profile.

For device specific connection parameters and configuration commands, consult the device reference in the Brand Catalog below or reach out to Plaspy support for guidance on adding an Ulbotech tracker to your account.
