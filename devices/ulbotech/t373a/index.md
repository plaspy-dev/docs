# Ulbotech - T373A

<div className="device-logo">
![T373A](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The T373A is a plug and play OBD II GPS tracker engineered for vehicle tracking and fleet management. Designed to connect to any standard OBD II port, it provides real time location and in vehicle telemetry without complex wiring. The device reads extensive OBD II data and combines GNSS positioning and cellular connectivity to deliver the visibility fleets, rental operators, insurers and roadside assistance services require for operational oversight and theft deterrence.

As a fully Plaspy compatible device, the T373A can stream position, OBD II parameters and event data directly into the Plaspy platform for visualization, alerts and reporting. Its plug and play form factor and built in telemetry features make it particularly well suited to deployments where fast insertion, centralized management and scalable remote updates are priorities for Plaspy users.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II connection for fast deployment and minimal installation time.
- Real time tracking and detailed vehicle telemetry including RPM, speed, coolant temperature, fuel level and fuel consumption.
- Onboard immobilizer engine cut digital output to support anti theft workflows when integrated with Plaspy controls.
- Integrated GNSS receiver for reliable position fixes alongside multi band cellular connectivity for data uplink.
- Bluetooth 2.0 support for pairing peripherals and extending telemetry where needed.
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer for driver behavior events such as harsh braking or rapid acceleration.
- FOTA firmware updates and auto configuration features to simplify large scale fleet maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the T373A delivers live location and vehicle telemetry to the platform where data is normalized, visualized and actioned for fleet operations. Plaspy ingests OBD II parameters, diagnostic events, accelerometer signals and GPS positions to provide live maps, configurable alerts and operational reporting suitable for maintenance, safety and anti theft use cases.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for live monitoring and route replay.
- OBD II live data and diagnostic trouble codes for maintenance planning and fault detection.
- Fuel monitoring and consumption reporting to support efficiency analysis.
- Remote immobilizer engine cut control via the digital output when enabled by Plaspy policies.
- Bluetooth peripheral support to extend sensor data and accessory integration.
- Driver behavior events and geofence breach alerts for safety and compliance workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, route history and fuel consumption oversight.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using immobilizer control and fast alerts.
- Insurance and rental telematics for driver scoring, usage reporting and DTC based insights.
- Roadside assistance and emergency response with location sharing and vehicle diagnostics.
- Driver safety programs using geofencing and harsh driving event analysis.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The T373A combines practical OBD II telemetry with GNSS positioning and cellular data to deliver the core inputs Plaspy needs for fleet visibility and operational analytics. Its plug and play design reduces installation time and cost, while remote firmware management and auto configuration features help administrators manage devices across large fleets from a central platform.
