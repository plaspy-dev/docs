# Ulbotech - T366

![T366](./tracker.jpg)

The T366 is an enhanced OBD GPS tracker designed for vehicle monitoring and Plaspy compatible deployments. Plugs into any standard OBD‑II port for tool‑free installation, the T366 combines a u‑blox 6M GNSS module with assisted‑GPS, internal GSM and Wi‑Fi radios, and a built‑in immobilizer to deliver reliable real‑time tracking, anti‑theft capability, and rich vehicle telemetry for fleet management and driver behavior analysis.

Optimized for integration with Plaspy, the T366 automatically switches between Wi‑Fi and GPRS to balance connectivity, reduce data costs and preserve continuous reporting. With full OBD-II and CAN support \(including SAE J1939 and J1708/J1587\), configurable driver behavior detection, voice monitor and FOTA updates, the T366 is a compact, professional solution for fleet managers, insurers and rental operators who need precise, Plaspy compatible tracking and actionable vehicle intelligence.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible OBD GPS tracker: quick OBD‑II plug‑in installation with full protocol support for fast integration and deployment.
- Accurate GNSS positioning: u‑blox 6M with A‑GPS, autonomous accuracy under 3 m and SBAS to about 2.0 m for reliable real‑time tracking.
- Dual‑mode communications: automatic Wi‑Fi / GPRS switching to optimize connectivity and data costs while maintaining real‑time updates.
- Built‑in immobilizer and digital engine cut output for anti‑theft control and remote immobilization through Plaspy.
- Comprehensive vehicle telemetry: OBD/CAN data, battery voltage monitoring and configurable driver behavior detection for fleet management and driver profiling.
- Firmware updates over the air \(FOTA\) via GPRS or Wi‑Fi for easier remote management and security maintenance.
- Compact, rugged form factor for vehicle use with wide power range \(8–32 VDC\) and onboard backup battery for continued reporting on power loss.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The T366 transmits GNSS coordinates, OBD and CAN telemetry, event alerts and driver behavior events to the Plaspy platform using either GPRS or Wi‑Fi. Plaspy ingests these data streams for real‑time tracking, alerting and historical reporting, enabling fleet managers to act on location, performance and security insights.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates: GNSS positions and OBD/CAN parameters sent to Plaspy for live maps and replay.
- Ignition, engine cut and immobilizer controls: digital output and built‑in immobilizer support remote immobilization workflows through Plaspy.
- Driver behavior and event alerts: eight configurable behavior detections \(e.g., rapid acceleration, harsh braking, fatigue driving\) appear as events in Plaspy for scoring and coaching.
- Battery and power loss reporting: vehicle battery voltage monitoring plus a 3.7 V, 180 mAh backup battery ensure continuity of critical alerts.
- FOTA and remote device management: update firmware and adjust settings over GPRS or Wi‑Fi to keep devices current when connected to Plaspy management tools.
