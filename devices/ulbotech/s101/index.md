# Ulbotech - S101

<div className="device-logo">
![S101](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Ulbotech S101 is a compact plug and play OBD II adapter designed to provide vehicle telemetry to devices running compatible apps. It is not a standalone GPS tracker; instead, the S101 connects to a vehicle OBD II port and streams standard engine and sensor data over a Bluetooth 4.0 link. The adapter uses an ELM327 based processor to deliver denser live parameter updates for diagnostics and fuel monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S101 enriches location tracking with onboard diagnostic data when paired with BLE enabled smartphones, tablets, or PCs running Plaspy compatible applications. That combination allows fleet operators and individual drivers to view vehicle health alongside position data in Plaspy, enabling better fuel analysis, fault diagnosis, and event driven alerts without adding cellular hardware to the vehicle.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible telemetry that forwards OBD II engine and sensor data via a Bluetooth link for enhanced tracking and reports
- ELM327 based design offering quicker live updates and denser parameter graphing compared with many generic adapters
- Broad OBD II protocol coverage to support a wide range of vehicles and diagnostic PIDs
- Low power draw suitable for leaving the adapter continuously plugged into the vehicle OBD II port
- Comprehensive telemetry exposing RPM, coolant temperature, fuel related metrics and other common diagnostic PIDs
- Ultra compact footprint that minimizes intrusion into driver space and stays durable for everyday use

## How It Works with Plaspy

The S101 functions as an OBD II telemetry source that complements Plaspy GPS tracking by streaming standard PIDs over Bluetooth to a Plaspy compatible app on a nearby device. Plaspy merges that telemetry with GPS position data from the paired device or a Plaspy GPS tracker to provide unified location and vehicle health information in real time.

- Real time location and telemetry updates combining GPS position with live engine parameters
- On demand diagnostic codes for viewing and, where supported by the app, clearing MIL or check engine codes
- Fuel monitoring inputs feeding Plaspy reports for efficiency analysis and trend tracking
- Event driven alerts using OBD data such as elevated coolant temperature or detected fault codes
- Integration with other Bluetooth sensors managed by the same device running Plaspy for consolidated fleet visibility

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet maintenance diagnostics to prioritize service and reduce vehicle downtime
- On the spot code reading and clearing for mechanics and DIY technicians
- Fuel monitoring and driver coaching using fuel trims and related telemetry in Plaspy reports
- Live data graphing for mechanics and tuners who need denser parameter traces during troubleshooting
- Anti theft and security workflows that correlate diagnostic triggers with GPS location for faster response

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Although the S101 is not a GPS tracker on its own, it is a practical companion for organizations using Plaspy that want stronger vehicle telemetry without installing additional cellular hardware. Its ELM327 based processing and stable Bluetooth 4.0 connection make it useful for operations that need timely engine parameters, fuel analytics, and diagnostic visibility alongside position tracking.

For fleet managers, service teams, and individual drivers who already rely on Plaspy for location based oversight, the S101 offers a straightforward way to add diagnostic depth to tracking workflows. Its compact form, low continuous power draw, and broad vehicle compatibility make it a useful option when the goal is to combine vehicle health data with Plaspy reporting, alerts, and operational monitoring.
