# Ulbotech - T373B

<div className="device-logo">
![T373B](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Ulbotech T373B is a plug and play OBD II GPS tracker designed for vehicle monitoring and fleet management. Built around a Telit xE910 cellular modem and a u blox MAX 7 GNSS module, the T373B provides real time location and vehicle telemetry through a compact unit that connects to the vehicle OBD II port. Features described for this model include on board diagnostics reporting, driver behaviour event detection, an immobilizer output and Bluetooth 4.0 LE for local extensions and configuration.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T373B can stream GPS positions, diagnostics and event data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet visibility, alerting and reporting. Plaspy can use the tracker s location, trip profiles, DTC alerts and immobilizer control to deliver operational oversight and remote management from a single dashboard, making the T373B a practical option for deployments that need fast time to first fix and rich vehicle telemetry.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for quick vehicle connection and rapid deployment.
- Real time tracking using a u blox MAX 7 GNSS module for reliable position updates.
- Integrated Telit xE910 cellular modem for wide area data connectivity.
- On board diagnostics and DTC reporting to surface engine and vehicle status.
- Built in driver behaviour detection and accelerometer based event reporting for coaching and safety analysis.
- Immobilizer digital output for remote engine cut and anti theft response.
- Bluetooth 4.0 LE for local configuration, mobile pairing and accessory extension.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the T373B forwards location, diagnostics and event driven alerts so operations teams receive actionable telemetry alongside map based tracking and reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker s streams and aligns them with fleet workflows to provide situational awareness, maintenance triggers and driver performance insights.

- Continuous location and motion updates to support live tracking and route playback.
- OBD and DTC data delivery to enable maintenance alerts and fuel related reporting.
- Immobilizer control and status reporting for anti theft workflows and remote actions.
- Driver behaviour events such as harsh braking and rapid acceleration for safety dashboards and scoring.
- Bluetooth based local connections for on site configuration and accessory data pairing.
- Support for remote firmware updates to keep devices current and reduce field maintenance effort.
