# Ulbotech - T360 Features

This page provides a public overview of the Ulbotech T360 GPS tracker features and how those capabilities are used with Plaspy for vehicle tracking and fleet telematics. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about the device as it integrates into Plaspy systems and dashboards.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation type and the manufacturer's implementation. Review the manufacturer documentation and your device configuration for the definitive list of functions available on a particular unit and firmware version.

## Feature Overview

The T360 is an OBD II plug-and-play tracker designed for quick installation in cars and light commercial vehicles. It combines GNSS positioning, GSM data connectivity and onboard sensors to deliver location, event reporting and driving behavior insight that can be reported into Plaspy for monitoring and reporting.

- Fast OBD II installation that avoids invasive wiring for rapid fleet rollout.
- Real-time position reporting and scheduled uploads for continuous location awareness.
- Driving behavior detection using an onboard 3-axis accelerometer for cornering, hard braking and speeding events.
- Internal engine cut digital output and voice monitor support to assist anti-theft and safety workflows.
- Built-in antennas, auto APN and automatic time zone detection to reduce configuration steps and simplify deployment.

## Core Features of Ulbotech - T360

- OBD II plug-and-play form factor for straightforward vehicle installation and consistent power.
- u-blox 6M GNSS engine with assisted GNSS support for reliable position fixes.
- Quad-band GPRS/GSM connectivity for global cellular data reporting.
- Onboard 3-axis accelerometer that detects cornering, hard braking and speeding events.
- Internal digital output for engine immobilizer support for remote engine cut workflows.
- Voice monitor capability for remote audio monitoring where permitted.
- Internal GSM and GPS antennas plus auto APN and time zone detection to simplify provisioning.
- Firmware update over GPRS for remote maintenance and firmware management.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the T360 telemetry and location stream to present live tracking, events and reports in dashboards and alerting systems. The platform translates device messages into actionable information for fleet managers and vehicle owners while simplifying backend integration.

- Periodic and immediate position updates appear as live location points and history in Plaspy.
- Accelerometer driven events are surfaced as driving behavior reports and event markers for safety scoring.
- Geo-fence triggers and alarm messages report into Plaspy to generate alerts and workflow actions.
- Immobilizer output and status messages can be represented in Plaspy for anti-theft workflows and event logging.
- Device management signals such as FOTA and APN detection ease provisioning and remote maintenance when visible to Plaspy.
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols so initial backend integration is straightforward.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, route replay and scheduled position reporting for operational oversight.
- Anti-theft and recovery workflows using engine immobilizer output and geo-fence alerts.
- Rental and insurance telematics with non-invasive OBD installation and driving behavior monitoring.
- Vehicle health and roadside assistance alerts using vehicle and backup battery telemetry and temperature monitoring.
- Safety programs for drivers that use speeding, hard braking and cornering reports to inform training and coaching.
- Rapid deployment for mixed fleets where plug-and-play OBD solutions reduce installation time.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature availability can depend on device firmware version and manufacturer firmware configuration.
- OBD-provided vehicle values vary by vehicle make and model and by how the vehicle exposes OBD data.
- Cellular connectivity and GPRS behavior depend on local network availability and SIM configuration.
- Voice monitor and immobilizer functions may be subject to regional rules and installation practices.
- Some functions such as FOTA require an active data connection to perform remote updates.
