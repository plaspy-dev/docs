# Ulbotech - T362

<div className="device-logo">
![T362](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Ulbotech T362 is a compact plug and play OBD II telematics unit designed for real time vehicle tracking, fleet management and anti theft protection. Built for quick deployment in cars and heavy vehicles, the T362 provides GNSS position data and OBD derived telemetry while connecting to backend platforms for continuous monitoring and reporting.

This model is listed as compatible with Plaspy and is a practical choice when you need immediate device activation and reliable data flow into a fleet management platform. The T362's combination of OBD connectivity, dual SIM network resilience, on device event detection and remote firmware update capability makes it well suited to the core visibility and operational workflows that Plaspy provides.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering real time tracking and continuous data delivery to backend platforms.
- Plug and play OBD II form factor for fast, no wiring deployment across vehicles and heavy units.
- Dual SIM connectivity for improved online resilience and consistent telemetry forwarding.
- Integrated immobilizer output to support remote engine disable workflows for theft response.
- Internal accelerometer based driver behaviour detection for events like hard braking and aggressive maneuvers.
- Firmware over the air updates to simplify maintenance and keep devices up to date.
- Configurable geo fences and alerting to support perimeter monitoring and operational triggers.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the T362 sends standardized position, OBD telemetry and event records to Plaspy so fleet managers can view live locations, receive alerts and run reports. Plaspy ingests the device feed and maps events and telemetry into dashboards, alerts and historical reports to support operational oversight and decision making.

- Real time location and telemetry updates including GPS position and OBD sourced parameters.
- Driver behaviour and event records available in Plaspy for scoring, review and incident investigation.
- Immobilizer control exposed as a digital output that can be used within Plaspy workflows where supported.
- Geo fence alerts and configurable notifications for perimeter breaches and defined operational events.
- OBD sourced vehicle data enabling additional reporting such as fuel related insights when the vehicle exposes those parameters.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for live tracking, route monitoring and historical trip analysis.
- Vehicle security and anti theft workflows using immobilizer control and perimeter alerts.
- Driver monitoring and safety programs that rely on event detection and behaviour scoring.
- Rental and temporary vehicle monitoring where plug and play deployment reduces installation time.
- Telematics driven maintenance planning using OBD derived engine and battery parameters.
