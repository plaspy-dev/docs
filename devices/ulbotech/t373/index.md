# Ulbotech - T373

![T373](./logo.jpg)

The Ulbotech T381 is an advanced OBDII GPS tracker that combines 4G network and WIFI technology to provide a range of powerful features. With its integrated 4G LTE and WIFI capabilities, the T381 can transfer the 4G signal of a mobile operator to a WIFI signal, effectively creating a WIFI hotspot. This allows up to 10 smartphones, tablets, and laptops to connect to the internet via WIFI, enabling high-speed sharing of resources and seamless communication between devices within the local area. Additionally, the T381 supports WIFI bridging, which means it can connect to surrounding WIFI hotspots, allowing the device and terminal data to access the internet through these hotspots, saving 4G data consumption.

The T381 is equipped with an internal u-blox MAX-7 GNSS module that supports GPS and GLONASS, ensuring accurate and reliable positioning. It features a high-gain active antenna and the Assist Now AGPS function, enabling fast and precise positioning even when the device is installed in hidden or hard-to-reach areas of the vehicle. Furthermore, the T381 includes On-board Diagnostics \(OBDII\)/SAE J1939 functionality, which allows it to obtain real-time on-board data such as RPM, speed, engine coolant temperature, fuel level, and fuel consumption. It also provides in-time monitoring of Diagnostic Trouble Codes \(DTC\), OBD II parameters, and alarms.

With its 3D acceleration capability, the T381 can detect motion and analyze eight different types of driving behavior, providing valuable insights into driver performance and fuel consumption. It offers an immobilizer \(engine cut off\) output for anti-theft purposes, making it suitable for a wide range of applications including fleet management, insurance and rental services, roadside assistance, driver behavior monitoring, and safety and security. The Ulbotech T381 is a versatile and feature-rich GPS tracker that combines advanced technology with practical functionality.

### Key Features:

- Integrated 4G LTE and WIFI technology for WIFI hotspot functionality
- Supports up to 10 devices to connect to the internet via WIFI
- WIFI bridging capability for data access through surrounding WIFI hotspots
- Internal u-blox MAX-7 GNSS module for GPS and GLONASS positioning
- High-gain active antenna and Assist Now AGPS function for fast and accurate positioning
- On-board Diagnostics \(OBDII\)/SAE J1939 function for real-time vehicle data
- 3D acceleration for motion detection and analysis of driving behavior
- Immobilizer \(engine cut off\) output for anti-theft purposes
- Suitable for fleet management, insurance and rental, roadside assistance, driver behavior monitoring, and safety and security applications
