# Ulbotech - T303

![T303](./tracker.jpg)

The T303 vehicle GPS tracker is a professional-grade telematics device designed for fleet management, anti-theft protection and driver behaviour monitoring. Plaspy-compatible out of the box, the T303 brings reliable real-time tracking, configurable geo-fencing and remote immobilization options to organisations that need accurate location, safety alerts and up-to-date telemetry for vehicles of any size.

The T303 builds on the proven T301 platform and adds a u-blox 6M GNSS module, quad-band GSM/GPRS, FOTA support and a comprehensive set of inputs and outputs for ignition detection, engine control and sensor telemetry. When paired with Plaspy, the T303 delivers actionable reports, live tracking and emergency alerts that simplify fleet operations, anti-theft workflows and driver profiling.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into real-time tracking and fleet management dashboards.
- Quad-band GSM with GPRS class 12 ensures wide cellular coverage for consistent telematics data.
- u-blox 6M GNSS chipset with A-GPS delivers high-sensitivity positioning and sub-3 m accuracy in typical conditions.
- Internal immobilizer and a digital engine-control output support remote immobilization and anti-theft workflows.
- Multiple tracking modes \(time, distance, course, status\) and configurable geo-fences \(circle, rectangle, polygon\).
- Built-in 3-axis accelerometer and seven types of driver behaviour detection for safety and driver profiling.
- Analog input for fuel or temperature sensors plus vehicle battery monitoring for telemetry and fuel monitoring use cases.
- FOTA \(firmware update over GPRS\), auto APN and time zone identification for reduced maintenance overhead.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Connecting the T303 to Plaspy is designed to be straightforward: the device transmits GNSS fixes and telemetry over GPRS while Plaspy ingests the data stream, applies rules, and surfaces real-time location, alarms and reports. Plaspy's platform interprets T303 events \(ACC, SOS, immobilizer, geo-fence breaches and driver behaviour alerts\) so fleet managers receive timely notifications and can act immediately.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry streamed over GPRS for live maps and history playback.
- Ignition \(ACC\) detection and engine status — ACC input enables trip segmentation and ignition-based reports.
- SOS button emergency alerts — immediate notification in Plaspy when SOS is triggered.
- Remote immobilizer / engine cut — use the device’s digital output under Plaspy control for anti-theft immobilization workflows.
- Fuel monitoring and analog sensors — analogue input supports fuel or temperature probes for telemetry and fuel monitoring dashboards.
- Driver behaviour and safety events — seven built-in behaviour detections plus accelerometer readings feed Plaspy’s driver scoring and alerts.

## Technical Overview

| Connectivity | GSM quad-band 850 / 900 / 1800 / 1900 MHz with GPRS multi-slot class 12 |
| --- | --- |
| Bands / Receiver Sensitivity | GSM receiver sensitivity Class II RBER 2% \(-107 dBm\) |
| GNSS | u-blox 6M chipset with A-GPS; autonomous sensitivity -147 dBm, hot start -160 dBm, tracking -162 dBm; position accuracy &lt;3 m \(autonomous\), ~2.0 m \(SBAS\) |
| TTFF | Cold start ~25 s \(open sky with A-GPS\), warm &lt;25 s, hot &lt;1 s |
| Power & Battery | Operating voltage 8 V–32 V DC; internal backup Li-Pol 3.7 V 250 mAh; power consumption ~70 mA \(active\), ~5 mA \(sleep\), max &lt;250 mA |
| Interfaces | Micro USB \(config/debug\), RS232 UART, 1 digital output \(engine control\), 2 digital inputs \(ACC and SOS\), 1 analog input for fuel/temperature sensors; external GPS/GSM antenna connectors supported |
| Sensors | Built-in 3-axis accelerometer \(±2g/±4g/±8g/±16g\), vehicle battery voltage monitoring, backup battery voltage, temperature sensor |
| Memory | Onboard memory ~8 Mb \(approx. 15,000 records\) |
| FOTA & Remote Management | Firmware update over GPRS \(FOTA\), auto APN configuration and time zone identification |
| Form Factor & Environmental | Dimensions 85 × 58 × 22 mm, weight ~90 g; operating temp -30 °C to +80 °C \(without battery\), storage -40 °C to +85 °C |
| Geo-fencing | Supports up to 5 geo-fences: circles, rectangles and polygons \(up to 32 points\) |
| Bluetooth | No built-in Bluetooth specified; note Plaspy can integrate wireless sensor inputs if required via supported gateways |

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and remote immobilization — immediate tracking plus engine cut via the digital output and internal immobilizer.
- Driver behaviour monitoring and safety — seven behaviour detections and accelerometer data for speeding, harsh braking and cornering analysis.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry — analog input for fuel sensors and vehicle battery monitoring to detect fuel loss or anomalies.
- Roadside assistance and emergency response — SOS button and real-time location feed to dispatch teams through Plaspy.
- Vehicle profiling and compliance — configurable tracking modes and geo-fences support route verification, compliance reporting and vehicle utilization studies.

The T303 is engineered for organisations that require dependable, professional telematics. Its combination of a high-sensitivity u-blox GNSS module, quad-band GSM/GPRS communications and robust I/O makes it an excellent match for Plaspy's platform. You get accurate real-time tracking, configurable geo-fencing, driver behaviour analytics and remote immobilization — all manageable within Plaspy for centralized fleet control.

Operational advantages include reduced maintenance through FOTA, flexible sensor inputs for telemetry and fuel monitoring, and built-in safety features such as SOS and ACC detection. Together with Plaspy’s dashboards, alerts and reporting, the T303 provides a complete solution for fleet management, anti-theft protection and telemetry-driven operational improvements.
