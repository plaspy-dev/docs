# Ulbotech - T371

![T371](./tracker.jpg)

The Ulbotech T371 is a Plaspy compatible OBD II GPS tracker designed for professional fleet and vehicle management. It combines plug-and-play OBD connectivity with a Telit xE910 cellular modem and a u-blox MAX-7 GNSS engine to provide reliable real-time positioning and rich vehicle telemetry. The unit is built to deliver fast time to first fix and continuous data streams for locations, diagnostics and event detection in moving vehicles.

As a device certified for integration with Plaspy, the T371 can stream location and OBD-derived vehicle parameters into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring, alerting and reporting. Its telematics features, internal immobilizer output and accelerometer event detection make it a practical choice for fleets, rental operations, insurance telematics and anti-theft applications that rely on Plaspy for visibility and operational control.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD II form factor for rapid deployment and direct vehicle telemetry access.
- Cellular connectivity via Telit xE910 for dependable real-time data upload and remote reporting.
- u-blox MAX-7 GNSS receiver for accurate positioning and fast time to first fix.
- Comprehensive vehicle telemetry including engine and diagnostic data available through OBD/CAN.
- Security and event features such as an internal immobilizer output and a 3-axis accelerometer for motion events.
- Flexible tracking modes and geo-fencing support to enable automated alerts and location rules in Plaspy.
- Remote device management capabilities including firmware over the air for simplified maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the T371 sends GPS positions, OBD telemetry and event flags over the cellular link so Plaspy can display live locations, generate alerts and store historical records for analysis. Plaspy ingests the device data to provide visibility across fleets and to support reporting, alert workflows and operational dashboards.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates pushed from the device to Plaspy for live maps and status views.
- Engine and vehicle status reporting from OBD II data for diagnostics, RPM and speed monitoring.
- Fuel-related parameters and consumption metrics available for analytics and efficiency reporting.
- Remote control coordination such as immobilizer actions that can be reflected in Plaspy workflows.
- Accelerometer-based events for harsh driving detection, movement alerts and driver behaviour insights.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with continuous tracking, diagnostic feeds and operational reporting.
- Rental car programs that need mileage logging, condition monitoring and theft deterrence.
- Insurance telematics solutions for usage based policies, claims support and driver scoring.
- Anti-theft installations using immobilizer control and motion events to protect assets.
- Roadside assistance and vehicle profiling where rapid location and basic diagnostic data speed service.
