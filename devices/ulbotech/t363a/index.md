# Ulbotech - T363A

<div className="device-logo">
![T363A](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The T363A is a plug and play OBD GPS tracker designed for fleet management, anti theft protection and driver behavior monitoring. Connecting directly to a vehicle OBD port, the device delivers real time location and vehicle telemetry to backend systems. Its compact form factor and integrated GNSS and cellular components make it suitable for rapid deployment across mixed fleets and for operators that need immediate visibility without hardwiring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T363A integrates with Plaspy to provide location, OBD derived diagnostics and configurable alerts for fleets, rental operators and vehicle owners. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker supplies location and event data that can be used for live monitoring, reporting and automated workflows, helping teams turn vehicle signals into operational insights.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD installation for fast deployment across vehicle fleets.
- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking, alerts and telematics reporting.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver with assisted positioning for faster fixes and accurate positions.
- Quad band cellular connectivity to support broad regional coverage for data delivery.
- Onboard Bluetooth for pairing optional accessories or external sensors.
- Internal immobilizer output and accelerometer based event detection to support anti theft and driver monitoring workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The T363A transmits GNSS positions, OBD derived telemetry and event data over cellular data to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy consumes these data streams to display live vehicle locations, generate alerts, and compile historical reports that support operational decision making. Device settings and behavior can be managed within Plaspy to align incoming signals with business rules and reporting needs.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for live map views and route playback.
- OBD based ignition and diagnostic signals used by Plaspy for maintenance triggers and operational workflows.
- Vehicle usage and fuel related parameters where available are surfaced in Plaspy reports to inform cost and consumption analysis.
- Remote immobilizer workflows enabled by the device digital output can be integrated into Plaspy alerting and response processes.
- Bluetooth connected accessories provide additional context to Plaspy for richer event correlation.
- Geofence and event alerts feed Plaspy notifications and historical logs for compliance and operational review.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, route analysis and driver behavior telemetry.
- Anti theft monitoring with tamper alerts, backup power and remote immobilizer control.
- Rental fleet operations and usage based tracking including geofencing and event logs.
- Roadside support and vehicle diagnostics using OBD derived status and fault information.
- Driver coaching and profiling using accelerometer driven event detection for harsh braking or rapid acceleration.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The T363A is a practical choice for organizations that want a plug and play telematics device that pairs with a full featured tracking platform. Its combination of GNSS positioning, OBD derived diagnostics and event detection means fleets can gain immediate operational visibility without complex installation. For operations already using Plaspy, the T363A supplies the core signals Plaspy expects for mapping, alerts and reporting workflows.
