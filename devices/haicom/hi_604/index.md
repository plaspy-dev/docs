# Haicom - HI-604

<div className="device-logo">
![HI-604](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Haicom HI-604 is a compact, multi-function GPS tracking device that provides real-time location monitoring using a combination of GPS, GSM, GPRS, SMS, and DTMF technologies. Designed for flexible deployment, the HI-604 builds in a large internal battery, a waterproof enclosure, and a moving sensor with a deep sleep mode to extend operational life when movement is minimal. The unit also includes strong magnetic mounting and is intended to monitor assets from any computer using a GPRS tracking website or Haicom tracking software.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the HI-604 can feed its location data into Plaspy for map-based visibility and operational oversight. Its configurable update behavior and global communication capability make it suitable for continuous monitoring or intermittent check-ins, and Plaspy can present that information alongside other devices to support fleet monitoring, asset management, and incident response workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Multi-mode tracking using GPS combined with GSM GPRS SMS and DTMF for wide area coverage
- Built-in large battery and deep sleep moving sensor for extended standalone operation
- Waterproof housing for use in harsh or outdoor environments
- Strong magnetic mounting for convenient attachment to many assets
- Live real-time tracking with configurable update intervals and on demand monitoring
- Designed to support security and recovery workflows for valuable assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Haicom HI-604 supplies location updates that Plaspy displays and manages through its mapping, monitoring, and reporting features. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s position reports and provides a unified view across a fleet or mixed device deployments, enabling operational teams to monitor assets continuously and react when attention is required.

- Live map visualization of device positions and movement over time
- Configurable reporting intervals and on demand location queries visible in Plaspy
- Alerts and notifications based on device activity and configured thresholds
- Historical tracking and simple route playback for incident review and records
- Grouping and status overview for fleet level monitoring and asset accountability

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet and asset tracking for logistics and transport operations
- Long term covert or overt surveillance for investigations and security teams
- Cargo protection and remote monitoring of valuable shipments
- Anti-theft and asset recovery where remote location visibility is critical
- Field equipment monitoring where waterproof and long battery life are required
