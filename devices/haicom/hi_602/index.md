# Haicom - HI-602

<div className="device-logo">
![HI-602](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Haicom HI-602 is an all in one tracking device that combines GPS positioning with multiple communication options including SMS, GSM, DTMF, and GPRS. Designed as a compact and rechargeable unit, the HI-602 supports real time location monitoring and is suitable for tracking a wide range of moving objects such as personal items, motorbikes, parcels, or small assets. It ships with PC based Haicom Tracking software and offers flexibility through customization and concealment options.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the HI-602 can feed location updates into Plaspy for map based visualization and operational oversight. Its multiple communication modes make it adaptable to different tracking workflows, and the device can be used to provide continuous visibility or on demand position reports within Plaspy. This compatibility helps organizations and individuals consolidate live tracking data in a single fleet management platform.

## Key Highlights

- All in one tracker supporting GPS location and multiple communication modes including SMS DTMF and GPRS
- Rechargeable and compact design suitable for personal tracking and small asset monitoring
- Real time location capability for live map visualization and movement monitoring
- DTMF mode enables tracking using a standard SIM card with minimal additional infrastructure
- Includes PC based Haicom Tracking software for local live mapping and monitoring
- Customizable form factor for special applications or discreet placement

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the HI-602 supplies position information that Plaspy displays and organizes for operational use. Plaspy can consolidate the incoming location updates and present them alongside other devices for unified fleet or asset oversight.

- Live map visualization of HI-602 reported positions for real time tracking
- Consolidated device list and status monitoring within Plaspy for fleet level visibility
- Historical playback and basic reporting to review movements over time
- Alerting and event handling in Plaspy based on device updates or user defined criteria
- Operational oversight features such as location filters and grouping to support workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal item tracking such as backpacks or luggage
- Monitoring small vehicles or motorbikes for routing and security
- Tracking parcels or cartons during transit for delivery visibility
- Temporary or ad hoc asset tracking where a compact rechargeable device is needed
- Situations where DTMF or SMS based fallback tracking is desirable

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The HI-602 is a practical choice when you need a compact, flexible tracker that can operate in a variety of scenarios. Its combination of GPS positioning and multiple communication options gives users latitude in how they collect and forward position data to Plaspy. For organizations that want to centralize visibility across diverse devices, adding HI-602 units can extend coverage to personal items and smaller assets that larger telematics devices are not suited for.

Because Plaspy focuses on consolidating location data and presenting it for operational decision making, the HI-602 fits well as a source of position updates and basic movement history. If your use case emphasizes discrete placement, rechargeable operation, and flexible reporting modes, the HI-602 paired with Plaspy is a sensible option.
