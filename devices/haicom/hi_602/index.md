# Haicom - HI-602

![HI-602](./logo.jpg)

The Haicom HI-602 is an all-in-one tracking solution that combines GPS, SMS, GSM, DTMF, and GPRS technologies to provide real-time tracking capabilities. This versatile tracker is perfect for a wide range of applications and offers flexible tracking options to suit your needs.

With the Haicom HI-602, you can track objects in real-time and see their movements on a map simultaneously. It comes with a rechargeable battery, making it ideal for personal tracking purposes. Whether you want to track a person, a backpack, a carton, a motorbike, or any other moving object, the HI-602 allows you to monitor its location globally.

One of the standout features of the Haicom HI-602 is its DTMF mode, which allows you to track objects using a normal SIM card from any operator. There are no additional charges other than the phone calls made from the tracker to your phones. Additionally, the HI-602 comes with free PC-based Haicom Tracking software that enables you to see the moving object live on a map with just a few simple steps.

The Haicom HI-602 is compact in size, making it easy to conceal or fit into a waterproof case. It also offers the option to customize and modify the tracker to fit your specific tracking needs. Whether you need real-time tracking for personal or professional use, the Haicom HI-602 is a reliable and versatile solution.

### Key Features:

- Real-time tracking using GPS, SMS, GSM, DTMF, and GPRS technologies
- Rechargeable battery for personal tracking purposes
- DTMF mode for tracking with a normal SIM card
- Free PC-based Haicom Tracking software
- Compact size for easy concealment or fitting into a waterproof case
- Customizable and modifiable for special applications
