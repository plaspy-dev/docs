# Haicom - HI-603

![HI-603](./logo.jpg)

The Haicom HI-603 is an all-in-one tracking solution that offers GPS, SMS, GSM, DTMF, and GPRS capabilities in real-time. It is a highly suitable device for tracking and fleet management, combining the latest technology with affordability. With the HI-603, you have a wide range of options at your disposal.

One of the standout features of the HI-603 is its DTMF free live tracking solution. With DTMF mode, Haicom provides a free PC-based Haicom Tracking software that can be easily installed on your computer. This software allows you to see the moving object live on the map, with or without an internet connection. DTMF live tracking is completely free, and the tracker only requires a normal SIM card from any operator to function.

In addition to DTMF tracking, the HI-603 also offers a GPRS tracking solution. Haicom provides a free web-based tracking platform that can be accessed from any web-enabled PC or smartphone. This platform allows you to see the real-time location of your car and offers the flexibility to program the GPRS data interval according to your preferences. If you have your own server, Haicom can even program the tracker with your server protocol for free, allowing you to integrate it seamlessly with your existing system.

The HI-603VT is a versatile device that offers GSM, GPRS, SMS, and DTMF capabilities in one solution. Users have the option to switch between DTMF mode and GPRS mode, depending on their tracking needs. The tracker supports worldwide coverage and works well even in areas with limited sky view, such as urban canyons. It is capable of tracking hundreds of vehicles at once and offers features like geofences, speed alerts, and an SOS button for immediate rescue/action.

With its high sensitivity, advanced GPS chipset, and built-in backup battery, the HI-603 is an ideal choice for fleet management and security applications. It comes with a two-year warranty and is certified with CE, FC, RoHS, and WEEE. Haicom also provides 24-hour customer support for all their products, ensuring that you have assistance whenever you need it.

Overall, the Haicom HI-603 is a reliable and affordable fleet management solution that combines multiple tracking technologies into one device. Whether you have one vehicle or a fleet of thousands, the HI-603 offers the features and capabilities you need to effectively track and manage your assets.
