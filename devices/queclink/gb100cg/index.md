# QuecLink - GB100CG

![GB100CG](./tracker.png)

The GB100CG is a compact, battery-mounted GPS tracker designed for demanding automotive telematics and insurance \(UBI\) applications. Built with LTE Cat 1 connectivity and 2G fallback, IP67 waterproofing, and a high-frequency 6-axis motion sensor, the GB100CG is Plaspy compatible and optimized to deliver reliable real-time tracking, high-fidelity driving behavior data, and robust event reporting for insurers, fleet managers and vehicle security systems.

Easy to install and engineered for accuracy, the GB100CG combines broad cellular band support, BLE accessory pairing, and configurable reporting to feed Plaspy with the telemetry and location intelligence required for fleet management, anti-theft workflows, crash reconstruction and policy-based usage analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback for wide-area coverage and reliable real-time tracking.
- High-frequency 6-axis motion sensor \(3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyroscope\) with up to 1600 Hz sampling for precise driving behavior, crash detection and mileage logging.
- Compact, battery-mounted form factor with IP67 waterproof enclosure and simple 2‑wire vehicle installation for covert or unobtrusive deployment.
- BLE 5.2 support for Bluetooth sensors and short-range accessory pairing to extend telemetry \(temperature, presence, proximity\) to Plaspy dashboards.
- Flexible event reporting \(time, distance, mileage\) plus geofencing \(up to 20 circular + 20 polygon regions\) and configurable speed and tow alarms.
- Robust anti-tamper and security features including jamming detection, tow alarm \(ignition-off towing detection\), and crash data collection to support anti-theft and incident workflows.
- Wide operating voltage \(8–32V DC\) with internal NiMH backup battery for continued reporting on power loss and LED status indicators for diagnostics.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GB100CG integrates smoothly with Plaspy to deliver ongoing telemetry, location and event reporting. It transmits GNSS position and high-rate motion data over LTE or GSM using TCP, UDP or SMS, enabling Plaspy to provide real-time tracking, alerts and analytics. Plaspy ingests the device's scheduled reports, geofence events and crash records to power dashboards, notifications and downstream workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates over LTE Cat 1 \(primary\) with GSM/GPRS/EDGE fallback to maintain connectivity.
- Virtual ignition detection \(voltage + accelerometer\) used by Plaspy for trip segmentation, ignition-based alerts and usage-based billing or UBI reporting.
- High-frequency motion data and crash event records feed Plaspy’s crash reconstruction and aggressive-driving analytics.
- Geofence entry/exit, speed alarms, tow and power-on reports deliver immediate alerts and historical event logs for fleet management and anti-theft responses.
- BLE 5.2 enables pairing with Bluetooth sensors and beacons; Plaspy can correlate short-range sensor telemetry \(e.g., temperature, accessory presence\) with GPS events.

## Use Cases

- Insurance telematics \(UBI\): high-frequency motion sampling and scheduled reporting for usage-based insurance, risk scoring and claims reconstruction.
- Fleet management: reliable real-time tracking, mileage logging and geofence/speed event alarms to improve routing, compliance and driver coaching.
- Stolen vehicle recovery and anti-theft: compact IP67 enclosure and tow/jamming detection to support quick response and recovery workflows.
- Crash detection and incident analysis: crash data collection with high-sample-rate sensors enables Plaspy to generate detailed reconstructions and insights for claims handling.
- Sensor-augmented telemetry: BLE sensor pairing for temperature, occupancy or proximity sensing that complements GPS-based tracking.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

For organizations deploying a Plaspy compatible solution, the GB100CG delivers a balanced mix of rugged design, broad network compatibility and precise motion sensing. Its LTE Cat 1 throughput with GSM fallback and TCP/UDP/SMS support ensures resilient connectivity for real-time tracking and telemetry. The device’s high-rate 6-axis sensor and GNSS diversity \(GPS/BDS/GLONASS/Galileo\) supply the driving behavior and crash data insurers and fleet managers need to reduce risk and accelerate incident response.

Because the GB100CG is compact, waterproof and supports simple 2‑wire installation with backup battery support, it installs quickly and continues to report during power loss — a practical advantage for anti-theft workflows and tow detection. BLE 5.2 makes it easy to add Bluetooth sensors, and Plaspy can combine on-board telemetry with external fuel monitoring or immobilizer workflows in your telematics stack to provide comprehensive vehicle insights. In short, the GB100CG is a dependable GPS tracker that extends Plaspy’s capabilities for real-time tracking, fleet management, anti-theft protection and high-fidelity telemetry.
