# QuecLink - GB100CG

![GB100CG](./tracker.png)

The GB100CG is a compact, battery mounted GPS tracker designed for automotive telematics and usage based insurance workflows. It combines a rugged IP67 enclosure with broad cellular connectivity and a high frequency 6 axis motion sensor to deliver continuous position updates, driving behavior data and event records suitable for insurers, fleet operators and vehicle security systems.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GB100CG can feed Plaspy with location, motion and event telemetry that powers real time monitoring, alerts and analytics. Its configurable reporting and accessory pairing options make it practical for fleet visibility, anti theft workflows and detailed incident analysis when used with Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Compact battery mounted form factor with IP67 rated enclosure for durable field use.
- High frequency 6 axis motion sensor for detailed driving behavior and crash event capture.
- LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain coverage across varied areas.
- Configurable reporting and event alarms for geofence, speed, tow and power events.
- BLE accessory pairing to extend short range telemetry with external sensors.
- Designed for automotive telematics, UBI and vehicle security use cases where reliable reporting and compact installation are priorities.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the GB100CG supplies location, motion and event data that Plaspy ingests to present live tracking, alerts and historical analysis. Plaspy uses the device reports and event records to populate dashboards, trigger notifications and support downstream workflows for operations and claims.

- Real time location and periodic position updates for fleet visibility and route monitoring.
- Trip segmentation and virtual ignition logic based on motion and power state for trip reporting and usage metrics.
- High rate motion records and crash event data that feed incident reconstruction and driving behavior analytics.
- Geofence entry and exit, speed and tow alerts that generate immediate notifications and historical event logs.
- Correlation of short range accessory telemetry from BLE paired sensors with GPS events for richer situational context.

## Typical Use Cases

- Usage based insurance programs that require high fidelity motion sampling and scheduled reporting for risk scoring and claims analysis.
- Fleet management for real time tracking, mileage logging and event based alerts to improve operations and driver oversight.
- Stolen vehicle recovery and anti theft workflows using tow detection and tamper reporting to support rapid response.
- Crash detection and incident reconstruction to assist claims teams and safety programs with detailed event data.
- Sensor augmented monitoring where BLE accessories provide supplementary telemetry such as temperature or occupancy.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GB100CG offers a balanced combination of rugged packaging, broad network reach and high fidelity motion sensing that aligns with common telematics and insurance requirements. For organizations using Plaspy, the device’s configurable reporting and accessory support make it straightforward to feed the platform with the location and event data needed for tracking, alerts and analytics without unnecessary complexity.

Because the GB100CG is designed for automotive deployment and compact placement, it fits use cases where unobtrusive installation, continued reporting during power loss and reliable event detection are important. Paired with Plaspy, the tracker can help teams improve fleet oversight, accelerate incident response and support UBI style analytics through consistent telemetry and structured event reporting.
