# QuecLink - GL200 Features

This page provides a public overview of the QuecLink GL200 features and how the device operates when used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, user-facing capabilities that fleet managers, logistics operators and security teams rely on when they integrate the GL200 with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. Use this page for general guidance and operational context, and consult the manufacturer documentation for the most current device-specific details.

## Feature Overview

The GL200 is presented as a compact, robust GPS tracker intended for discreet asset protection and continuous position reporting. When paired with Plaspy, the device is used to provide consistent location visibility, incident support and operational oversight across a range of environments.

- Compact and discreet form factor suitable for concealed placement in vehicles, cargo and personal assets
- Continuous position reporting to enable route reconstruction and recovery operations
- Water resistant construction for use in outdoor and refrigerated transport scenarios
- Wide operating temperature tolerance for deployments in cold chain and harsh environments
- Proven field reliability with documented recovery of stolen goods enabled by continuous reporting
- Plaspy compatibility out of the box for centralized monitoring and history review

## Core Features of QuecLink - GL200

- Compact enclosure designed for covert installations in vehicles, cargo compartments and on portable assets
- Continuous GPS position reporting suitable for real time tracking and historical playback
- Water resistant construction that supports outdoor installations and refrigerated transport
- Broad operating temperature range appropriate for cold chain logistics and extreme environments
- Proven practical performance with at least one documented recovery using continuous reporting
- Built to provide dependable telemetry to backend platforms such as Plaspy
- Support for expanded telemetry streams where available including ignition and immobilizer events, fuel monitoring and Bluetooth sensor inputs when those data paths are provided by the device or connected peripherals

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy captures the GL200’s reported positions and presents them through centralized dashboards and history views so teams can monitor movement, investigate incidents and coordinate responses. Plaspy is designed to automatically detect compatible tracker protocols and surface device-reported events alongside location data.

- Live location and position history displayed on Plaspy maps for operational visibility
- Continuous reporting enables route reconstruction and supports coordinated recovery workflows
- Device-reported events and status visible in Plaspy for faster alerting and investigation
- Environmental tolerance of the GL200 helps maintain consistent reporting from refrigerated and outdoor assets
- Additional telemetry streams such as ignition status, immobilizer events, fuel data and Bluetooth sensor inputs can appear in Plaspy when the device or installation provides those inputs

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft protection and remote recovery with discreet installations in cargo or vehicles
- Cold chain and refrigerated transport tracking across wide temperature ranges
- Lone worker location visibility and personal safety monitoring where a compact tracker is needed
- Pet and small asset tracking where a small, weather-resistant device is required
- Discreet monitoring of trailers, equipment and high value goods for operational oversight
- Evidence collection and incident reconstruction through continuous historical position data
