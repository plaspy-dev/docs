# QuecLink - GV620MG Features

This page provides public feature context for the QuecLink GV620MG when used with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to fleet and asset monitoring on the Plaspy platform, focusing on the practical functions you can expect to see in Plaspy rather than configuration specifics.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. For deployment planning and technical configuration, consult manufacturer documentation and the device firmware release notes to confirm the capabilities present on a particular unit.

## Feature Overview

The GV620MG is a rugged trailer tracker designed for heavy duty transport use and delivered ready for integration with Plaspy. It combines multi band cellular connectivity, long life internal battery backup, multi GNSS support and sensor I O to keep trailers and similar assets visible and report critical telemetry to Plaspy.

- Real time GNSS tracking with multi constellation positioning for reliable location reporting.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB2 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback for broad network coverage and uptime.
- IP67 sealed enclosure and integrated antenna design for simplified installation on trailers and tankers.
- High capacity internal battery to continue reporting while external power is removed.
- BLE 5.0 support for wireless temperature and accessory sensors plus wired inputs for telemetry and ignition status.
- Accelerometer driven events and configurable alerts for motion and impact detection.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GV620MG

- Global LTE Cat M1 and NB2 cellular modem with fallback to 2G for persistent connectivity across regions.
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure with internal antennas suited to trailer, tanker and refrigerated unit mounting.
- Internal battery pack 2 × 18650 Li ion with long standby capability to maintain reporting during power loss.
- Multi GNSS receiver supporting GPS GLONASS Galileo and Beidou for improved position accuracy.
- BLE 5.0 radios for integration with wireless temperature humidity and proximity sensors.
- Multiple wired interfaces including RS232 analog input positive ignition input digital inputs and open drain outputs plus 1 Wire temperature channels.
- Accelerometer based motion and impact event reporting and OTA remote control of digital outputs.
- Configurable reporting intervals geofence alarms and remote management options for operational control.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the tracker position updates telemetry and sensor states and surfaces that data for live tracking alerts and historical reporting. The GV620MG is compatible with Plaspy out of the box and its telemetry feeds map directly into the platform for operational monitoring and alerting.

- Live map tracking and history playback using GNSS position data reported by the device.
- Telemetry panels that show battery status cellular signal and device health reported from the unit.
- Input and output events such as ignition state digital inputs and remote output actions shown as events and usable in rule based alerts.
- BLE sensor readings and 1 Wire temperature channels available in Plaspy for refrigerated trailer monitoring and sensor driven alerts.
- Motion and accelerometer events available as triggers for alarms and geofence related workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer fleet tracking and route replay for logistics and asset visibility.
- Refrigerated load monitoring by pairing BLE temperature sensors with Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- Tanker and flatbed tracking where a rugged IP67 enclosure and robust connectivity are required.
- Anti theft monitoring and remote immobilization style workflows using accelerometer alerts geofences and output control.
- Remote telemetry integration with refrigeration controllers and third party sensors through analog and serial interfaces.
- Continued asset visibility during power loss using the internal battery for periodic reporting.
