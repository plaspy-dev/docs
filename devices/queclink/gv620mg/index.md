# QuecLink - GV620MG

![GV620MG](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GV620MG is a rugged trailer GPS tracker designed for heavy duty vehicles such as trailers, tankers, refrigerated units and flatbed trucks. Built to withstand harsh transportation conditions, the device provides GNSS position reporting, input and ignition status, accelerometer event detection and BLE sensor data over global LTE Cat M1 and NB2 networks with 2G fallback. Its sealed enclosure and integrated antenna approach are intended to keep assets visible even in demanding environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the GV620MG delivers location and telemetry directly into the Plaspy platform for live tracking, alerts and historical reporting. That compatibility makes the unit practical for fleet operators who need trailer visibility, sensor driven monitoring for refrigerated loads, and anti theft workflows managed from Plaspy dashboards and rule sets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker optimized for trailers and heavy vehicle fleet management.
- Global LTE Cat M1 and NB2 connectivity with 2G fallback for broad coverage and reliable reporting.
- IP67 rated enclosure and integrated antenna design for rugged installations on trailers and tankers.
- High capacity internal battery for extended standby tracking when external power is not available.
- Multi constellation GNSS position capability and accelerometer events for improved location and motion awareness.
- BLE support and wired I O options enable sensor integration for temperature monitoring and third party telemetry.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GV620MG transmits GNSS positions, telemetry and sensor states to Plaspy where that data is ingested for live maps, event processing and historical analysis. Plaspy users can centralize visibility across trailers and configure notifications and reporting to match operational needs.

- Live location updates and history playback in Plaspy for route review and asset visibility.
- Input and ignition status reported to Plaspy for run state monitoring and operational dashboards.
- BLE and wired sensor readings available in Plaspy for refrigerated cargo monitoring and environmental alerts.
- Geo fence alarms, accelerometer driven motion alerts and configurable thresholds managed from Plaspy.
- Remote commands and output control initiated from Plaspy to support immobilizer style or remote switching workflows.
