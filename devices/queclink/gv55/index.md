# QuecLink - GV55

![GV55](./tracker.png)

The GV55 mini vehicle GPS tracker from Concox \(model GV55\) is a compact, covert GPS tracker designed for reliable in-vehicle telematics and Plaspy compatible deployments. With an internal u-blox All-in-One GNSS receiver and built-in GSM/GPRS connectivity, the GV55 delivers precise positioning and robust telemetry for fleet management, stolen vehicle recovery and usage-based insurance applications when integrated with Plaspy’s real-time tracking platform.

Plaspy compatible out of the box, the GV55 provides the essential signals and event data required for modern fleet and mobility solutions: ignition detection, remote immobilizer control, crash and harsh-driving events, geo-fencing and multi-protocol transport \(TCP/UDP/SMS\). Its small, covert form factor, internal antennas and internal 250 mAh backup battery make it ideal where discreet, continuous tracking and anti-theft monitoring are necessary.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with internal u-blox GNSS for accurate, real-time tracking and telemetry.
- Built-in GSM/GPRS \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) connectivity with TCP, UDP and SMS transport options for flexible data delivery.
- Advanced event detection: ignition input, remote immobilization, panic button interface, crash detection and driving behavior monitoring \(harsh braking/rapid acceleration\).
- Covert mini form factor \(63 x 50 x 21.8 mm, 50 g\) with internal antennas for discreet installation in light vehicles.
- Internal 250 mAh Li-Polymer backup battery keeps tracking alive during power interruptions; buffers up to 10,000 messages.
- Comprehensive alarms and management: geo-fencing \(up to 20 regions\), tow and speed alarms, jamming detection, and OTA control of outputs.
- Regulatory certifications \(FCC, PTCRB, CE, E‑Mark, Anatel\) for global deployment and commercial fleet rollouts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GV55 streams position and event telemetry directly into the Plaspy platform for real-time tracking, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy consumes the device’s GNSS coordinates, digital input/output events and alarm states to deliver dashboards, live maps and automated notifications that fleet managers and service providers rely on.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GNSS fixes \(u-blox\), timestamps, and movement data feed into Plaspy for live geolocation and replay.
- Ignition and immobilizer status: digital I/O provides ignition detection and remote immobilization control for anti-theft workflows.
- Crash and driving behavior: harsh braking, rapid acceleration and crash event data are reported so Plaspy can trigger emergency workflows or driver scoring.
- Geo‑fencing and alarms: up to 20 internal geo-fence regions, tow/speed/special input alarms and jamming detection are pushed to Plaspy for immediate alerts.
- Telemetry aggregation: message buffering \(up to 10,000 messages\) and scheduled reporting by time, distance or mileage ensure reliable data delivery to Plaspy even during temporary connectivity loss.
- Integration with fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensors: while the GV55 does not include built-in BLE sensors, telemetry and I/O from GV55 can be combined in Plaspy with external systems \(fuel probes or Bluetooth sensor feeds\) to provide comprehensive fleet telemetry.
