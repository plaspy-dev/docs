# QuecLink - GL502MG Features

This page provides a public overview of the QuecLink GL502MG tracker and how its documented features relate to use with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive feature context for asset, trailer, and container tracking deployments, and explains what you can expect when pairing the GL502MG with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional model, and manufacturer implementation. Where applicable this page highlights device capabilities described by QuecLink but does not replace the manufacturer documentation for device-specific limits and current firmware behavior.

## Feature Overview

The GL502MG is positioned for long-term, low-maintenance asset tracking in demanding environments. It combines a rugged IP68 enclosure and multiple wireless positioning options to deliver reliable location reporting with minimal on-site servicing.

- Ultra high capacity battery pack rated at 57000mAh with up to 10 years standby life under certain reporting profiles
- Rugged IP68 waterproof design suitable for outdoor and marine exposed assets
- LTE Cat M1 and NB2 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback for broad network coverage
- Built-in Bluetooth Low Energy 5.2 and WiFi positioning to improve location accuracy in dense or indoor environments
- u‑blox All‑in‑One GNSS receiver for satellite positioning and USB Type‑C interface for firmware upgrade and debug

## Core Features of QuecLink - GL502MG

- 57000mAh ultra high capacity battery designed for extended standby deployments
- User replaceable battery for lower maintenance costs over device lifetime
- IP68 rated enclosure for water and dust resistance in outdoor asset tracking
- LTE Cat M1 and NB2 support with EGPRS 2G fallback for global cellular coverage
- Built in BLE 5.2 for local wireless accessory connectivity and proximity workflows
- WiFi based positioning to supplement GNSS in urban or indoor assisted scenarios
- u‑blox GNSS receiver with high sensitivity for reliable satellite fixes
- USB Type‑C port and LED indicators for firmware work and basic device status monitoring

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present the GL502MG’s reported positions and status on its fleet and asset dashboards, giving operators visibility into location, health, and long term deployment trends. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and accepts device reporting so that incoming location and status data are integrated without manual protocol selection.

- Map and timeline views in Plaspy show GNSS positions and network assisted locations reported by the device
- Long standby battery behavior translates into low-frequency reporting options in Plaspy for unattended assets
- Device status and connectivity indicators allow operators to spot offline assets and monitor reporting health
- BLE and WiFi positioning data reported by the tracker can appear as supplementary location sources in Plaspy when the device supplies those fields
- Plaspy detects tracker protocol automatically to simplify on boarding of GL502MG devices into a Plaspy account

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term unattended container tracking at intermodal yards and ports
- Trailer and equipment location monitoring during storage and transit
- Remote asset monitoring where extended battery life reduces maintenance visits
- Asset visibility in harsh or wet environments where IP68 protection is required
- Urban deployments where WiFi and BLE assist GNSS for improved location accuracy
