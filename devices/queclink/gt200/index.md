# QuecLink - GT200

![GT200](./logo.jpg)

The GT200 GPS Safety Phone by QuecLink is a powerful and reliable device designed specifically for children's safety. With its built-in GPS receiver, it offers superior sensitivity and fast time to first fix, ensuring accurate and timely location tracking. The quad band GPRS/GSM subsystem supports frequencies of 850/900/1800/1900 MHz, providing wide coverage and connectivity options.

One of the standout features of the GT200 is its embedded full-featured @Track protocol, which allows for seamless system integration. This protocol supports a wide range of reports, including emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, low battery notifications, and scheduled GPS positions with cell-ID reporting. This comprehensive reporting system ensures that parents or guardians are always informed about their child's whereabouts and well-being.

The GT200 also includes an internal u-blox chipset, known for its high performance and reliability in GPS tracking. Additionally, it features a built-in 3-axis accelerometer for man-down detection, providing an extra layer of safety and security. The device is equipped with internal GSM and GPS antennas, eliminating the need for external antennas and simplifying installation.

Other notable features of the GT200 include quick dial buttons with four preset numbers, allowing children to easily contact their parents or guardians in case of an emergency. The device also boasts low power consumption, ensuring a long standby time with its internal battery. Furthermore, the GT200 is CE certified, meeting the highest standards of safety and quality.

Overall, the GT200 GPS Safety Phone by QuecLink is a reliable and feature-rich device that offers peace of mind to parents and guardians. With its advanced tracking capabilities, comprehensive reporting system, and user-friendly design, it is an ideal choice for ensuring the safety and well-being of children.
