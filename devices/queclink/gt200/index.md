# QuecLink - GT200

![GT200](./logo.jpg)

The GT200 GPS Safety Phone by QuecLink is a compact tracking device designed principally for children's safety. It combines a sensitive built in GPS receiver with a quad band GPRS GSM subsystem to deliver timely location information across wide coverage areas. The device includes an embedded full featured @Track protocol for system integration, an internal u blox chipset, a built in 3 axis accelerometer for man down detection, internal GSM and GPS antennas, quick dial buttons, low power consumption, and CE certification.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT200 can feed its position and alert reports into Plaspy for centralized visibility and management. The tracker s embedded reporting capabilities cover emergency alerts, geo fence boundary crossings, low battery notices, and scheduled GPS positions with cell ID reporting, which makes it straightforward to surface location updates and safety events within Plaspy dashboards, notifications, and reporting views.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for child safety with simple quick dial buttons for emergency contact
- High sensitivity GPS receiver and fast time to first fix for timely location updates
- Quad band GPRS GSM support for broad connectivity in many regions
- Embedded @Track protocol enabling structured reports for integration
- Internal u blox chipset and internal antennas for reliable reception and simplified handling
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer for man down detection and additional safety monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GT200 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous location visibility and safety alerts from devices in the field. Once configured to send its reports, Plaspy can ingest position and event messages and present them through maps, alerts, and historical data views.

- Live and historical position display on Plaspy maps to track movements over time
- Alert routing for emergency signals and geo fence boundary events to notify operators or guardians
- Scheduled position reporting and cell ID fallback allow Plaspy to maintain awareness even when GPS is intermittent
- Low battery notifications surfaced in Plaspy to help manage device uptime and maintenance planning
- Man down detection events forwarded to Plaspy for prompt operational response and oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Parental monitoring and location awareness for children during daily routines
- Schools and childcare programs tracking group movements for safety and accountability
- Caregiver oversight for vulnerable individuals who need location and emergency alerting
- Small scale personnel monitoring where compact devices and quick contact options are preferred
- Situations that benefit from internal antennas and long standby time to reduce maintenance
