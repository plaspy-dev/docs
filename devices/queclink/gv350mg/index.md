# QuecLink - GV350MG

![GV350MG](./tracker.png)

The GV350MG is a compact LTE Advanced vehicle tracker designed for professional fleet management, cold chain logistics and transportation monitoring. It combines high sensitivity GNSS positioning with vehicle integration features and multiple I O channels to deliver continuous location and telemetry for commercial vehicles. The device is intended for operators that need accurate tracking and broad connectivity in a rugged form factor suitable for demanding vehicle environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the GV350MG can be added to Plaspy fleets to provide live location, event reporting and telematics data within the Plaspy platform. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker helps teams centralize monitoring, apply alerting rules and produce reports that support operational workflows like route oversight, temperature sensitive transport and anti theft procedures.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for direct integration that supports real time tracking and telematics reporting.
- Global LTE Cat M1 and NB1 primary connectivity with 2G fallback for broader coverage and resilience.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver with sub meter grade positioning performance for accurate location services.
- Full vehicle integration options including CAN bus support and multiple serial and digital I O interfaces for engine and sensor data.
- Built for commercial fleets with wide input voltage range, internal backup power and extended operating temperature tolerance.
- Advanced telematics features such as geo fence alarms, tow and crash detection, driving behaviour insights and local message buffering.
- Accessory friendly design with support for temperature sensors and external peripherals to extend functionality.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the GV350MG location and vehicle telemetry to provide a unified dashboard for monitoring, alerts and historical reporting. The device reports GNSS fixes, vehicle bus parameters and input/output events to Plaspy, where rules and visualizations turn raw data into operational insights for fleet and logistics teams.

- Real time location and telemetry updates streamed into Plaspy for live tracking and trip replay.
- Configurable alerts and geo fence enforcement to notify teams of route deviations, door openings or tow events.
- Vehicle and sensor events delivered to Plaspy for fuel monitoring, temperature oversight and maintenance workflows.
- Local buffering on the device ensures data continuity and delayed delivery handling when connectivity is intermittent.
- Remote control of outputs and event driven responses as managed through Plaspy rules and dashboards.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization workflows with geo fences and remote output control for recovery.
- Cold chain logistics monitoring using temperature sensor channels and precise GNSS location for refrigerated trailers.
- Fuel management and loss detection by combining fuel sensor inputs with vehicle telemetry.
- Driver behaviour and safety programs using crash detection and event based driving summaries for coaching.
- Vehicle telemetry and accessory integration for cameras and third party devices to support accountability and compliance.

The GV350MG is a practical choice for organizations that require reliable telematics, continuous connectivity and vehicle level integration. Its combination of high sensitivity GNSS, vehicle bus interfaces and accessory support makes it suitable for a range of commercial deployments from fleet oversight to temperature sensitive transport.
