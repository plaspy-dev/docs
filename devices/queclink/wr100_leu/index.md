# QuecLink - WR100 LEU

![WR100 LEU](./tracker.png)

The WR100 LEU is an industrial LTE Cat 4 router designed as a compact communications gateway for Plaspy compatible GPS tracker deployments. Built for data intensive IoT and telematics applications, it provides resilient cellular connectivity with dual SIM redundancy alongside wired and WiFi links so tracker telemetry and location data can be delivered reliably to cloud platforms.

As a Plaspy compatible gateway, the WR100 LEU complements GPS trackers by providing secure backhaul, automatic failover and remote management capabilities. Its OpenWrt based routing, VPN and firewall features help keep real time tracking, alerts and telemetry flowing into Plaspy dashboards with the availability and protections required for fleet management and remote asset monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Acts as a reliable cellular and WiFi backhaul for Plaspy compatible GPS tracker telemetry and real time tracking feeds.
- LTE Cat 4 performance with fallback support for broader coverage and consistent data throughput.
- Dual SIM redundancy and automatic failover help minimize service interruptions for fleet and remote deployments.
- Industrial I O and serial options allow integration of ignition, door alarms, fuel monitoring and other telemetry from attached devices.
- Advanced security features including VPN and firewall protections for secure transport of Plaspy data.
- Compact, rugged design with wide input voltage and extended temperature range suited to vehicle and outdoor environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The WR100 LEU functions as a high availability communications gateway that forwards GPS coordinates, event messages and telemetry from Plaspy compatible trackers to the Plaspy platform. Trackers connected via cellular, serial or local WiFi send packets to the router, which routes them over its available links and maintains connectivity through link monitoring and SIM failover.

- Forwarding of real time location and telemetry to enable Plaspy mapping and live fleet visibility.
- Transport of ignition, door and alarm signals so Plaspy can evaluate rules and issue notifications.
- Delivery of fuel monitoring and other serial or sensor telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Secure command and control paths for remote actions and configuration when routed through VPN protected tunnels.
- Support for tracker setups that relay local BLE sensor data via attached gateways or serial links into Plaspy systems.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for buses, trucks and service vehicles requiring continuous real time tracking and route telemetry.
- Anti theft and immobilization scenarios where low latency transport of alarms and control commands is essential.
- Remote telemetry collection for industrial sites and equipment monitoring feeding Plaspy reporting and alerts.
- Smart city and transport applications acting as a local gateway for in vehicle or roadside trackers.
- Retail and kiosk connectivity maintaining online status for Plaspy connected POS and asset trackers via LTE and wired fallback.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Although the WR100 LEU is an industrial router rather than a standalone GPS tracker, it is a strategic component for Plaspy deployments that require robust network resilience and security. By serving as the communications backbone, the device reduces downtime for real time tracking and ensures telemetry and event streams from Plaspy compatible trackers reach the platform even during primary network outages.

Organizations that need a managed, secure backhaul for their Plaspy enabled trackers will find the WR100 LEU useful for integrating serial telemetry, keeping remote gateways maintainable via remote management, and establishing redundant links for mission critical monitoring. Its industrial design and management features make it suitable for distributed fleet and infrastructure projects where connectivity is a priority.
