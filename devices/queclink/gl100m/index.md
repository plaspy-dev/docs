# QuecLink - GL100M

<div className="device-logo">
![GL100M](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The QuecLink GL100M is a compact GPS tracker designed for vehicle, pet, and asset tracking. It combines high sensitivity positioning with a fast time to first fix and delivers periodic position reports over cellular channels. The device is built to operate across quad band GSM frequencies and includes a clear white OLED display for on device information. A water resistant enclosure and low power consumption make the GL100M suitable for diverse deployment environments and extended standby use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GL100M can report location and event data to fleet and asset management backends. Its embedded @Track protocol and support for reporting via cellular data or SMS allow integration with Plaspy systems for live visibility, scheduled position uploads, and alerting workflows. This compatibility makes the GL100M a practical option for organizations seeking reliable tracking hardware that can feed operational dashboards and reporting in Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Quad band GSM connectivity for broad cellular coverage and reliable reporting
- Fast time to first fix and high receive sensitivity for consistent location updates
- Water resistant design suitable for outdoor and mixed environment use
- Built in white OLED display for straightforward local status and information
- Low power consumption for extended standby and long term deployments
- Built in 3D motion sensing to enable movement detection and trigger alerts
- Certified to common industry standards enhancing deployment confidence

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GL100M transmits position and event data to the Plaspy platform where it can be monitored, analyzed, and reported. Plaspy ingests the tracker reports and presents them in maps, timelines, and alert feeds to support operational oversight and decision making.

- Send periodic position reports and receive them in Plaspy for real time location visibility
- Trigger motion based alerts and have Plaspy notify operators or log events for review
- Use geofence and boundary crossing notifications within Plaspy to monitor asset movement
- Aggregate device data for fleet level reporting and historical playback in Plaspy
- Configure scheduled reporting to balance update frequency and data usage for operations

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet tracking where compact size and water resistance are important
- General asset tracking for equipment that requires periodic position reports

- Mobile assets that benefit from motion triggered alerts and scheduled positions
