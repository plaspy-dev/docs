# QuecLink - GL52S Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the QuecLink GL52S and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device attributes relevant to asset tracking, GNSS location reporting, and long term deployments, and explains how those capabilities map to visibility and monitoring inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The content here is based on the supplied device description and highlights general, non-sensitive feature context; for device specific technical details consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The GL52S is a Sigfox micro standby asset tracker built for long duration deployments where discreet installation and low operational cost are priorities. It uses GNSS positioning for location fixes and leverages Sigfox connectivity for low power, low data reporting suitable for battery powered asset monitoring.

- Sigfox network connectivity for low power wide area reporting
- GNSS positioning for point location tracking
- Micro form factor for covert or space constrained installations
- Standby time rated for multiple years for long term asset deployments
- Resistance to common jamming techniques to help maintain location reporting
- Optional IP67 waterproof case for outdoor or harsh environment use

## Core Features of QuecLink - GL52S

- Sigfox connectivity designed for low data usage and battery efficiency
- GNSS positioning to provide geographic location updates
- Micro size that supports discreet mounting and concealed installations
- Multi year standby life suitable for long-term asset monitoring
- Built with resistance to existing jamming techniques as described by the manufacturer
- Optional IP67 rated enclosure available to improve environmental protection
- Designed for asset monitoring, inventory control, and stolen vehicle recovery scenarios

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy is compatible with the GL52S and can ingest location reports and device events so teams can monitor assets centrally. Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols automatically and can receive reported data from the device for display, alerting, and historical playback.

- Location fixes reported by the GL52S are presented on Plaspy maps for real time and historical tracking
- Long standby and low data reporting characteristics make the GL52S suitable for low maintenance, long term tracking projects in Plaspy
- Device visibility in Plaspy helps surface installation or connectivity issues that may affect reporting frequency
- Environmental protection and jamming resistance improve the likelihood of continuous reporting into Plaspy in exposed or challenging conditions
- Plaspy supports common transport configurations for trackers so the GL52S can be connected to the platform with minimal protocol setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset monitoring where battery life and low maintenance are critical
- Stolen vehicle recovery support by providing covert location reporting
- Inventory tracking for high value or frequently moved equipment
- Discreet installations where a small form factor is required
- Outdoor asset deployments when paired with the optional IP67 enclosure

Using Plaspy with the QuecLink GL52S brings a centralized view of location reports and device status, helping operations teams track assets over long periods without frequent maintenance.
