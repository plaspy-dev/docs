# QuecLink - GL52S

![GL52S](./logo..png)

The QuecLink GL52S is a Sigfox micro standby asset tracker that offers reliable and efficient tracking capabilities. This device is designed to support GNSS positioning, ensuring accurate and precise location tracking. With a standby time of over 4 years, the GL52S is perfect for long-term asset monitoring and stolen vehicle recovery.

One of the standout features of the GL52S is its micro size, which allows for covert installation. This makes it ideal for applications where discreet tracking is necessary. Additionally, the device is resistant to existing jamming techniques, ensuring uninterrupted tracking even in challenging environments.

The GL52S comes with an optional IP67 waterproof case, providing added durability and protection against harsh weather conditions. This makes it suitable for outdoor use and ensures reliable performance in various environments.

With its integration of Sigfox technology, the GL52S offers low power consumption and cost-effective tracking solutions. Sigfox is known for its low data usage and lower network costs, making it an excellent choice for battery-powered applications.

Overall, the QuecLink GL52S is a reliable and efficient asset tracker that is perfect for asset monitoring, stolen vehicle recovery, and inventory control. Its small size, long battery life, and resistance to jamming techniques make it an excellent choice for various tracking management applications.
