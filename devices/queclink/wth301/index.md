# QuecLink - WTH301

![WTH301](./tracker.png)

The WTH301 is a compact BLE temperature and humidity sensor built for professional cold-chain logistics and transportation monitoring. Plaspy compatible when deployed with BLE-capable vehicle trackers or gateways, the WTH301 adds precise environmental telemetry to any Plaspy-based real-time tracking solution — enabling better compliance, proactive alerts, and more complete fleet management data.

Designed for trailers, refrigerated containers and asset-level environment monitoring, the WTH301 combines long battery life, IP66 waterproof protection, and BLE 5.1 connectivity to deliver continuous, long-term sensing with simple low-cost installation. When paired with a Plaspy-compatible GPS tracker it extends standard location and vehicle telemetry \(ignition, immobilizer-capable workflows, fuel monitoring from the tracker\) with high-resolution temperature and humidity data for end-to-end visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible BLE sensor: pairs with BLE-capable trackers and gateways to feed environmental telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- High accuracy temperature sensing: −40°C to +70°C range with ±0.2°C accuracy between 5°C–60°C and 0.01°C resolution for precise cold-chain monitoring.
- Reliable humidity monitoring: 0–100% RH, ±2% RH accuracy \(20–80% RH\) with 0.01% resolution to track moisture-sensitive cargo.
- Long operational life: 1200 mAh LiFe battery with more than 3 years runtime under typical reporting conditions, reducing maintenance visits and battery swaps.
- Rugged and simple to install: IP66 waterproof housing and 3M sticker mounting for quick deployment in trailers, containers and assets.
- BLE 5.1 connectivity with up to 150 m open-air range, supporting robust links to vehicle trackers or fixed gateways.
- Compact form factor \(75 × 38 × 19 mm, 48 g\) that integrates cleanly into fleet and asset installations without adding bulk.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The WTH301 communicates environmental telemetry over Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\). In a Plaspy-enabled deployment the typical flow is: the WTH301 broadcasts temperature and humidity values at configurable intervals; a Plaspy-compatible BLE gateway or BLE-enabled GPS tracker picks up those broadcasts and forwards the data to Plaspy’s platform along with GPS location and vehicle telemetry. Plaspy then correlates location, ignition/door status and other vehicle signals with the environmental data so operations teams get real-time tracking and actionable alerts.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — environmental readings arrive alongside GPS data for context-aware tracking and historical playback.
- Ignition, door and alarm status — when paired with a tracker that reports ignition/door/alarm events, Plaspy can combine those events with sensor readings for smarter alerts and investigations.
- Fuel monitoring integration — Plaspy-level fuel monitoring from the vehicle tracker remains available while environmental telemetry adds cold-chain context.
- Remote immobilizer workflows — Plaspy’s immobilizer or anti-theft actions \(where supported by the paired tracker\) can be informed by combined telemetry and location for faster incident response.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons — WTH301 functions as a BLE sensor node; Plaspy receives its sensor broadcasts via BLE-enabled gateways or trackers to enrich fleet telemetry.

## Use Cases

- Cold-chain logistics: continuous temperature and humidity monitoring for pharmaceuticals, food and other temperature-sensitive cargo during transit and storage.
- Refrigerated transport and trailers: discreetly mounted inside trailers or containers to supply per-load environmental telemetry to Plaspy and fleet management systems.
- Container and asset environment tracking: long-life BLE sensors for multi-month installations where maintenance visits are costly.
- Compliance reporting and alerts: automated threshold alerts and historical reporting for regulatory compliance and proof-of-condition in claims handling.
