# QuecLink - WTH301

![WTH301](./tracker.png)

The WTH301 is a compact temperature and humidity sensor from QuecLink designed for professional cold chain logistics and transport monitoring. Built for trailer and container environments, it provides continuous environmental sensing with rugged IP66 protection and a small form factor that fits discreetly inside refrigerated assets. The device is aimed at operations that require reliable condition data for sensitive cargo during transit and storage.

As a Plaspy compatible device when used with BLE capable vehicle trackers or gateways, the WTH301 adds environmental telemetry into Plaspy based tracking solutions. Paired sensors feed temperature and humidity readings into Plaspy alongside location and vehicle signals, enabling combined visibility for compliance, alerts, and historical reporting within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible BLE sensor that pairs with BLE enabled trackers and gateways to feed environmental telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- High accuracy temperature sensing covering a wide range suitable for cold chain operations.
- Reliable humidity monitoring to track moisture sensitive cargo and help support compliance workflows.
- Long operational life that reduces maintenance overhead and battery replacements in large deployments.
- Rugged IP66 housing and compact form factor for simple mounting inside trailers, containers and other assets.
- BLE 5.1 connectivity for extended range to vehicle trackers or fixed gateways and low power operation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with a Plaspy compatible BLE gateway or BLE enabled GPS tracker, the WTH301 broadcasts temperature and humidity readings that are collected and forwarded to Plaspy. The platform then correlates those environmental values with GPS location and vehicle telemetry so teams can monitor conditions in real time and review historical trips with environmental context.

- Real time location and telemetry integration so environmental readings appear alongside GPS data for context aware tracking and playback.
- Alerts and thresholds in Plaspy that can notify teams when temperature or humidity move outside configured limits.
- Combined event correlation where ignition, door or alarm status reported by the paired tracker is viewed together with sensor data for troubleshooting.
- Enriched reporting in Plaspy that includes environmental metrics for compliance and claims support.
- Operational oversight features such as route level condition monitoring and aggregated sensor summaries for fleet managers.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain logistics monitoring for pharmaceuticals food and other temperature sensitive cargo during transit.
- Refrigerated transport and trailer monitoring where per load environment data is required alongside vehicle location.
- Container and asset environment tracking for long life installations that reduce maintenance visits.
- Compliance reporting and historical proof of condition for regulatory or claims processes.
- Fleet level condition monitoring to support operational decisions and exception management.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the WTH301 with Plaspy compatible trackers provides a focused solution for organizations that need location aware environmental telemetry. The combination extends basic tracking with precise temperature and humidity data that helps operations teams detect issues faster support compliance reporting and maintain visibility across refrigerated assets.
