# QuecLink - GL500MG

![GL500MG](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GL500MG is a rugged asset GPS tracker designed for long life deployments and wide area monitoring. Built with an IP67 enclosure and low power hardware, the GL500MG combines GNSS positioning, cellular connectivity and on-device sensors for temperature, light and motion to support secure asset tracking, yard management and anti-theft workflows. The device is intended for deployments where low maintenance and long standby life are essential.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the GL500MG can deliver its location and sensor telemetry into Plaspy for mapping, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests device messages to provide fleet and asset visibility, leverage motion and environmental readings for event triggers, and present long-duration telemetry in dashboards and reports that help teams reduce visits to field assets.

## Key Highlights

- Proven long battery life for multi-year deployments, reducing maintenance visits and operating cost.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure suitable for outdoor assets and harsh environments.
- Built-in motion, temperature and light sensing to enable anti-theft and environmental monitoring.
- Wide area cellular connectivity with fallback for broad coverage across regions.
- High-sensitivity GNSS positioning for reliable location reporting in typical asset scenarios.
- Large on-device message buffering and configurable reporting modes to preserve data continuity.
- Manufacturer lifecycle note with a recommended successor for new purchases.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GL500MG forwards GNSS positions and internal sensor events to Plaspy where those inputs become part of live maps, alerts and historical datasets. Plaspy processes incoming device messages to generate operational insights, notify users of important events, and keep a persistent audit trail of asset movement and sensor states.

- Real-time and periodic location updates displayed on Plaspy maps and route history views.
- Motion based wakeup and tamper alerts routed into Plaspy workflows for anti-theft responses.
- Temperature and light readings available in Plaspy for environmental alerts and timeline reporting.
- Buffered messages and scheduled reports ensure Plaspy receives data after temporary coverage loss.
- Geofence and event triggers in Plaspy can be driven by GL500MG location and sensor events.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain and environmental monitoring where internal temperature telemetry supports integrity checks.
- Warehouse and yard management for inventory and lot tracking with minimal maintenance.
- Cargo and container tracking across wide area routes using cellular coverage with fallback.
- High value asset protection using accelerometer based movement detection and alerting.
- Remote infrastructure monitoring where rugged design and long standby life are priorities.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GL500MG is well suited to organizations that need a durable, low maintenance tracker feeding consistent telemetry into a fleet and asset platform. When paired with Plaspy, its sensor readings and motion events become actionable, supporting alerting, historical analysis and operational oversight without frequent battery changes or field visits. Plaspy’s device management and mapping features make it straightforward to include GL500MG units in mixed fleets and enterprise reporting.
