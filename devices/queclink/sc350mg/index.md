# QuecLink - SC350MG

![SC350MG](./logo..png)

The QuecLink SC350MG is a compact and water-resistant GNSS tracker specifically designed for E-bikes. With its LTE Cat M1/NB2 connectivity, it provides reliable and efficient communication, with 2G fallback for areas with limited LTE coverage. The device features a wide voltage range of DC 8-60V, making it compatible with a variety of E-bike models. Its compact size allows for easy and covert installation, ensuring that it doesn't interfere with the bike's aesthetics or functionality.

One of the standout features of the SC350MG is its ability to collect and read ECU/BMS data. It can communicate with vehicle controllers, instruments, and battery management systems via CAN or UART ports, enabling intelligent E-bike management. This functionality allows for remote monitoring and control of the E-bike, enhancing security and providing valuable insights into the bike's performance and usage.

In addition to its telematics capabilities, the SC350MG also includes other useful features such as BLE \(Bluetooth Low Energy\) for unlocking the E-bike, vibration detection for anti-theft purposes, and a 3-axis acceleration sensor for motion detection. With an IPX5 water resistance rating, the tracker is built to withstand various weather conditions, ensuring reliable performance even in challenging environments.

### Key Features:

- LTE Cat M1/NB2 with 2G fallback for reliable communication
- Water-resistant design \(IPX5\) for durability
- ECU/BMS data reading for intelligent E-bike management
- Wide voltage range of DC 8-60V for compatibility with different E-bike models
- Compact size for easy and covert installation
- BLE for unlocking the E-bike
- Vibration detection for anti-theft purposes
- 3-axis acceleration sensor for motion detection

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the QuecLink SC350MG is an excellent choice for E-bike owners and fleet managers who want to enhance the security, efficiency, and management of their vehicles.
