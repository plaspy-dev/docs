# QuecLink - SC350MG

![SC350MG](./logo..png)

The QuecLink SC350MG is a compact, water resistant GNSS tracker designed specifically for E bikes. It combines cellular connectivity with a wide input voltage range to suit a variety of electric bicycle installations, and its small form factor supports covert placement that maintains the bike's appearance. The device also supports reading ECU and BMS data, offering more than basic location information for intelligent vehicle monitoring.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the SC350MG can feed location and telematics into a centralized fleet management platform. That compatibility makes it a practical option for operators who want to monitor E bike fleets, track usage patterns, and use vehicle controller and battery data to inform maintenance and operational decisions through Plaspy dashboards.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for E bikes with a compact, water resistant design for everyday use in varied weather conditions
- LTE Cat M1 and NB2 connectivity with 2G fallback for broad cellular coverage and reliable reporting
- Wide voltage input range DC 8 60V to support a range of E bike electrical systems
- Ability to read ECU and BMS data via CAN and UART for richer telematics and remote monitoring
- Built in vibration detection and 3 axis acceleration sensor to support motion and anti theft monitoring
- BLE support for unlocking workflows and nearby device interactions combined with covert installation suitability

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the SC350MG streams location and available vehicle data into a single fleet platform where operators can visualize and act on insights. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to provide real time visibility, alerting, and historical reporting tailored to E bike operations.

- Real time location tracking and map visualization for single vehicles and fleets
- Fleet monitoring views to see status and distribution of E bikes across routes or regions
- Alerts for motion, vibration, or abnormal events to support security and rapid response
- Historical trip and usage reporting to analyze utilization and plan deployments
- Remote visibility of ECU and BMS parameters inside Plaspy dashboards to aid maintenance planning
- Unlock and BLE related events recorded as activity logs to track access patterns

## Typical Use Cases

- Shared mobility and E bike rental fleets needing covert tracking and usage analysis
- Commercial E bike fleets for last mile delivery requiring operational oversight
- Theft deterrence and recovery programs using motion and vibration alerts
- Maintenance planning and remote diagnostics driven by ECU and BMS data trends
- Performance and utilization reporting to optimize fleet size and deployment

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SC350MG is a practical choice for organizations focused on E bike operations because it blends compact hardware with telematics capabilities beyond simple GPS. Its ability to surface ECU and BMS information alongside location makes it useful for operators who need both security and operational intelligence. The device's broad voltage compatibility and water resistant design also help ensure adaptability across different bike models.

Pairing the SC350MG with Plaspy gives fleet managers a unified interface to monitor location, receive alerts, and review historical data. That combination can reduce unknowns in daily operations, support proactive servicing, and improve security workflows without changing the look or function of the bike.
