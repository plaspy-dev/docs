# QuecLink - GV200

![GV200](./logo.jpg)

The QuecLink GV200 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for a wide range of vehicle tracking applications. With its multiple I/O interfaces, it can be used to monitor and control external devices, making it suitable for various tracking needs. The built-in GPS receiver of the GV200 offers superior sensitivity and fast time to first fix, ensuring accurate and reliable location tracking. It supports quad band GPRS/GSM, allowing real-time or periodic tracking of the device's location through a backend server or mobile devices.

One of the standout features of the GV200 is its built-in 3-axis accelerometer, which enables towing detection. This feature is particularly useful for fleet management and security purposes. The GV200 also boasts a wide operating voltage range of 8V to 32V DC, making it compatible with a variety of vehicles. With its low power consumption and long standby time, thanks to the internal battery, the GV200 offers extended usage without frequent recharging.

The GV200 is equipped with multiple I/O interfaces, providing flexibility for monitoring and control. It also supports the full-featured @Track protocol, which offers a wide range of reporting options, including emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, low battery notifications, and scheduled GPS positions. The device is CE/E-Mark certified, ensuring compliance with European safety and performance standards.

### Key Features:

- Wide operating voltage range 8V to 32V DC
- Internal u-blox chipset for superior GPS performance
- Low power consumption, long standby time with internal battery
- Quad band GSM/GPRS 850/900/1800/1900 MHz for global coverage
- Embedded full-featured @Track protocol for comprehensive tracking options
- Multiple I/O interfaces for monitoring and control
- Internal 3-axis accelerometer for towing detection and power conservation
- Three analog inputs for connecting external sensors
- Internal and optional external GSM and GPS antennas for enhanced signal reception
- CE/E-Mark certified for compliance with European safety standards
