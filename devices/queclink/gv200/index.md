# QuecLink - GV200

<div className="device-logo">
![GV200](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The QuecLink GV200 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for a broad range of vehicle tracking applications. It combines a sensitive internal GPS receiver and an internal 3 axis accelerometer to provide reliable location tracking and towing detection. The unit supports quad band GPRS/GSM connectivity and offers multiple I O interfaces for monitoring and controlling external devices, making it adaptable to a variety of fleet and security scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GV200 can feed location, movement, and event data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its reporting options, built around a full featured @Track protocol and support for scheduled or real time position updates, align with common fleet monitoring workflows on Plaspy. The device's wide operating voltage range and low power standby characteristics make it a practical option for mixed vehicle fleets that need continuous tracking with long standby capability.

## Key Highlights

- Accurate GPS positioning with high sensitivity receiver for reliable tracking
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer enabling towing detection and motion based events
- Multiple I O interfaces for flexible monitoring and external device control
- Quad band GPRS GSM support for global cellular coverage and reporting
- Wide operating voltage range of 8V to 32V for compatibility across vehicle types
- Low power consumption with internal battery for extended standby time
- CE and E Mark certified for compliance with common regional safety standards

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GV200 sends position and event reports that Plaspy ingests for visualization, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy can use the device's data to create real time and historical views of vehicle movement and status, helping operators make operational decisions and maintain oversight.

- Centralized location visibility in Plaspy dashboards for live tracking and playback
- Alerting and notifications for events such as towing detection, geo fence crossings, and low battery
- Scheduled reporting and historical position logs for route analysis and compliance
- Use of device I O state data in Plaspy to monitor external inputs and simple control signals
- Consolidated reporting across a mixed fleet to support operations, maintenance, and security workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium commercial vehicle fleets requiring reliable location reporting
- Vehicle security and theft or towing detection using the built in accelerometer
- Delivery and service vehicles that need scheduled position reporting and centralized oversight
- Mixed fleet deployments where a wide input voltage range and long standby time are valuable
- Asset monitoring where external inputs and alerts are used to track status and exceptions

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GV200 is a practical choice for organizations that need a balance of accurate positioning, event detection, and flexible input options. Its built in accelerometer and multiple I O interfaces make it useful for both security and operational monitoring, while the wide voltage tolerance and low power profile suit diverse vehicle types.

Pairing the GV200 with Plaspy provides a straightforward path to turn device reports into actionable insights. Plaspy can consolidate GV200 data with other devices in your fleet to provide consistent monitoring, alerting, and reporting without needing complex custom integration.
