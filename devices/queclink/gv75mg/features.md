# QuecLink - GV75MG Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the QuecLink GV75MG and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for real time tracking and fleet management. It summarizes the tracker’s practical functions, telemetry and event behavior as they are typically consumed by Plaspy, and is intended for operators, installers and fleet administrators looking for compatibility and operational context.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and the specifics of installation or configuration. For device model details, firmware notes and the most current technical specifications consult official Queclink documentation and the device release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GV75MG is a rugged vehicle tracker purpose built for motorcycles, watercraft, quad bikes and other outdoor assets where waterproofing, long standby and reliable cellular coverage matter. It combines low power cellular connectivity, high sensitivity GNSS positioning and durable I O options to support continuous monitoring and event capture in harsh conditions.

- IP67 rated enclosure for waterproof and dustproof installations in exposed vehicles and outdoor assets.
- Low power LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT cellular connectivity with 2G EGPRS fallback for broad coverage and long standby.
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning with industry grade tracking performance for accurate location fixes in open sky.
- Buffered messaging and multiple transmission modes to preserve and forward telemetry after coverage gaps.
- Configurable digital inputs and outputs with remote OTA control for event driven actions and interventions.
- Durable local interfaces including serial port and status indicators for installation diagnostics.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GV75MG

- Rugged IP67 housing suited to motorcycles, marine craft and exposed outdoor equipment.
- Low power LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT cellular connectivity with 2G fallback for resilient telemetry.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver delivering precise position fixes and reliable tracking.
- Buffered storage for thousands of messages to reduce data loss during temporary signal outages.
- Configurable digital inputs, a standard digital output and a latched output for remote control use cases.
- RS232 serial interface and local status indicators to assist with installation and diagnostics.
- Onboard event detection and alarm support including geofence, parking, tow, crash and configurable input alarms.
- OTA control of outputs to support immobilizer style or other remote interventions when coordinated with platform workflows.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GV75MG reports location, inputs and alarm events into the platform so operators can monitor assets, receive alerts and review historical activity. Plaspy ingests the device’s telemetry and makes location and event data available for rules, dashboards and reporting.

- Real time and historical location visibility from GNSS fixes presented in Plaspy for tracking and playback.
- Digital input events and ignition detection mapped into Plaspy for trip reporting and engine on off status.
- Alarm messages such as geofence breaches, parking or tow alerts and crash events surfaced as platform notifications.
- Buffered message delivery preserves telemetry continuity during coverage gaps and is uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity resumes.
- Remote output control actions coordinated through Plaspy workflows allow controlled interventions and response steps.
- Device status and basic diagnostics available in Plaspy to support remote troubleshooting and installation checks.
