# QuecLink - WTS100

<div className="device-logo">
![WTS100](./tracker.png)
</div>

The QuecLink WTS100 is a compact, battery powered wireless temperature sensor designed for cold chain logistics, asset monitoring, and other temperature sensitive deployments. It provides periodic temperature telemetry from refrigerated trucks, containers, warehouses, and field assets where lightweight, long life sensing is needed. While the WTS100 does not include GPS, it is intended to deliver reliable environmental data that complements location and fleet information.

As a Plaspy compatible device when integrated via supported QuecLink receivers or gateways, the WTS100 brings continuous temperature measurements into the Plaspy platform. This makes it straightforward to view temperature alongside existing fleet and asset records, configure alerts for excursions, and include sensor health in operational reporting within Plaspy dashboards and workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via QuecLink receivers and gateways so temperature telemetry appears in the Plaspy dashboard and alerting flows.
- Long field life powered by common batteries with very low power consumption for reduced maintenance needs.
- Purpose built for cold chain and asset temperature monitoring with an operating range suitable for most refrigerated transport and storage.
- Compact lightweight form factor for discreet mounting on pallets, containers, or equipment.
- Reliable RF link to compatible receivers for robust telemetry delivery in typical deployments.
- Simple in field pairing and status feedback via a side button and LED to speed mapping and troubleshooting.
- End of life notice from the manufacturer with guidance to plan migrations to recommended replacements and future sensor options.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the WTS100 transmits temperature reports to a compatible QuecLink receiver or gateway, that gateway forwards sensor data to backend systems. Once the gateway is configured to deliver sensor telemetry into Plaspy, temperature readings, device health, and alarm events become available for teams to monitor and act on within the Plaspy environment.

- Real time telemetry updates enable near real time condition monitoring as readings arrive via the gateway.
- Battery and device health information is visible in Plaspy so maintenance can be scheduled before failures affect operations.
- Threshold based alerts and notifications in Plaspy can be triggered by temperature excursions reported by the sensor.
- Device identification and pairing workflows are supported through the sensor status indicators and gateway mapping to link a WTS100 to a Plaspy device record.
- Historical reporting in Plaspy lets teams analyze temperature trends and produce compliance or audit records alongside fleet data.
- Migration planning support in Plaspy helps manage transitions from EOL devices to recommended sensor replacements.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain monitoring for refrigerated trucks and containers to detect temperature excursions and trigger alerts.
- Warehouse asset and pallet temperature tracking where discreet, low maintenance sensors are preferred.
- Field asset environmental sensing for remote goods or equipment without constant mains power.
- Integrating temperature telemetry with fleet management to correlate environmental conditions with shipments or equipment use.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The WTS100 is a practical choice for organizations that need focused temperature telemetry without adding heavy power or GPS hardware. Its compact size and long battery life reduce installation and maintenance overhead, and the device is designed to work through supported QuecLink receivers and gateways that forward data into Plaspy. This combination helps operations teams maintain centralized visibility of environmental conditions alongside vehicle and asset information.

Because QuecLink has announced an End of Life for RF433 accessories including the WTS100, organizations should consider migration plans to manufacturer recommended replacements or alternative sensor options while continuing to use Plaspy for integrated monitoring and alerting.
