# QuecLink - GL520MG

![GL520MG](./tracker.png)

The Queclink GL520MG is a waterproof, long-life GPS tracker designed for low-maintenance asset monitoring and environmental sensing. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the GL520MG pairs multi-year battery life and rugged IP67 protection with LTE Cat M1 / NB2 connectivity and 2G fallback, delivering dependable real-time tracking and telemetry for static and mobile assets.

Engineered for large-scale deployments where maintenance windows are limited, the GL520MG provides accurate GNSS positioning, onboard temperature and light sensing for tamper detection, and a 3‑axis accelerometer for motion alerts. When integrated with Plaspy, this tracker becomes a turnkey solution for fleet management, cold-chain logistics, warehouse asset visibility, and secure high-value asset monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat M1 and NB2 connectivity plus EGPRS \(2G\) fallback for broad coverage.
- Extra-long battery life — multi-year standby operation \(up to seven years under conservative reporting profiles\).
- IP67-rated, waterproof enclosure with optional magnetic housing for flexible mounting on metal surfaces.
- Internal u‑blox GNSS module for high-accuracy positioning \(autonomous \< 2.5 m CEP\) and fast TTFF.
- Built-in sensors: temperature and light for environmental and tamper sensing, plus a 3‑axis accelerometer for motion detection.
- Large message buffer \(up to 10,000 messages\), visual GNSS/status LEDs, and vibration confirmation for simple field operations.
- Queclink protocol support \(@Track\), TCP/UDP/SMS transport, geofence support and intelligent reporting to optimize power usage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GL520MG streams position fixes, sensor telemetry and status events into the platform using standard TCP/UDP and SMS channels. Plaspy ingests the GL520MG’s Queclink-formatted messages, normalizes telemetry and exposes location, temperature, motion and device health in dashboards, alerts and reports for operational teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via TCP/UDP or SMS.
- Motion and tamper alerts from the 3‑axis accelerometer and light sensor trigger Plaspy notifications and workflows.
- Temperature telemetry for cold-chain and environmental monitoring is forwarded to Plaspy for threshold alerts and historic trending.
- Device health and power status \(low battery alarms, activation reporting\) appear in Plaspy device inventory and maintenance queues.
- Geofence events \(up to 20 regions supported on-device\) create automated Plaspy triggers for routing, anti-theft, and compliance workflows.

## Use Cases

- Cold‑chain logistics: temperature monitoring and position tracking for refrigerated shipments and pallets.
- Static high‑value asset protection: long standby life and tamper detection for outdoor equipment, containers and site assets.
- Pallet and container tracking: rugged enclosure and optional magnetic housing for easy attachment to metal assets.
- Warehouse and inventory visibility: scheduled reporting and motion detection to track stock movement without frequent battery changes.
- Fleet asset pools: track trailers, equipment and non-powered assets that require multi-year battery operation and reliable telemetry.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GL520MG is a highly efficient GPS tracker that scales for large deployments where battery life, ruggedness and reliable cellular connectivity matter. As a Plaspy compatible tracker, it integrates smoothly into Plaspy’s real‑time tracking and telemetry workflows, supplying accurate GNSS fixes, temperature and motion data while minimizing maintenance overhead. This combination supports fleet management and asset visibility goals while enabling anti-theft alerts and operational reporting without frequent field servicing.

While the GL520MG focuses on long-life telemetry such as temperature, motion and tamper detection, Plaspy can aggregate its data alongside other devices that provide additional capabilities — for example, ignition or immobilizer events, fuel monitoring, or Bluetooth sensors — giving operations teams a single pane of glass for comprehensive asset control. Choose the GL520MG with Plaspy when you need precise positioning, robust enclosure ratings, proven cellular certifications, and power-optimized reporting for multi-year deployments.
