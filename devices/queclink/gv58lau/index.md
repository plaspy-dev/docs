# QuecLink - GV58LAU

<div className="device-logo">
![GV58LAU](./tracker.png)
</div>

The QuecLink GV58LAU is a compact GPS tracker designed for vehicle security and fleet management applications. It combines a high sensitivity u blox GNSS receiver with LTE Cat 4 cellular connectivity to provide reliable real time location and resilient telemetry. The unit is intended for discreet installation in cars, rental fleets and light commercial vehicles where accurate positioning and dependable communication are important for operations and recovery workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GV58LAU can feed live position updates, I O events and accessory telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and reports. Its support for BLE accessories, ignition detection and configurable digital outputs makes it a practical choice for customers who want to extend Plaspy visibility to driver identification, environmental sensors and remote control actions without extensive rewiring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking and fleet management integration
- Multi constellation u blox GNSS with high position accuracy useful for recovery and precise tracking
- LTE Cat 4 connectivity with multi network fallback to maintain telemetry and low latency updates
- Compact covert form factor suitable for discreet mounting in cars rental fleets and light commercial vehicles
- BLE 5.2 support for accessory sensors and driver identification to extend telemetry without extra cabling
- Ignition detection and configurable digital outputs for trip segmentation and remote control workflows
- Advanced alarms and reporting modes for geo fence tow and driving related alerts to support proactive responses

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GV58LAU streams position fixes and event data into the platform so operators can monitor vehicles in real time and analyze historical trips. Plaspy ingests GNSS data, input events and accessory telemetry and converts those inputs into map views, alerts and reports that support operational oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for live tracking and route replay within Plaspy
- Ignition and digital input events used for trip segmentation and engine hour reporting
- BLE accessory data such as environmental sensors or driver ID integrated into asset condition and attribution views
- Remote output control via device outputs coordinated through Plaspy for immobilization or alarm actions
- Configurable alarms for geo fence breaches tow and crash related events that trigger notifications and automated workflows
