# QuecLink - GV53MG

![GV53MG](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GV53MG is a compact vehicle GPS tracker engineered for discreet in line installation in cars and light vehicles. It combines low power wide area cellular connectivity with a high sensitivity u blox GNSS receiver to provide reliable real time location and telemetry. Designed with an ultra thin form factor and internal antennas the unit is well suited to covert placements for asset protection and recovery workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box the GV53MG delivers the core capabilities fleets and service providers expect for tracking and anti theft programs. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and buffered messages to present live position, event alerts and historical reports while supporting operational features such as geofencing scheduled reporting and remote output control for immobilization or other recovery actions.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking fleet dashboards scheduled reporting and event driven alerts.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB2 connectivity with EGPRS 2G fallback for broad coverage and economical data use.
- High sensitivity u blox GNSS receiver with strong position performance for reliable location updates.
- AES 256 capable security to help protect telemetry and location data as it moves into Plaspy.
- Built in backup battery and wide operating voltage to maintain operation during power interruptions.
- Ultra thin form factor with internal antennas suited to covert installations for rental leasing and finance programs.
- Remote OTA control of digital outputs to support immobilizer and remote actuation workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the GV53MG streams secure location and telemetry to Plaspy's backend where device messages are interpreted for instant visualization, geofence enforcement, and historical reporting. Plaspy handles buffered records and event notifications so operators see consistent tracking and can act on alerts even after intermittent connectivity.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live fleet visibility.
- Ignition and input based detection enable ignition aware rules scheduled reporting and odometer or runtime workflows when inputs are present.
- Tow alarm crash and aggressive driving events produce immediate alerts inside Plaspy for safety and recovery monitoring.
- Remote immobilizer function via OTA control of a digital output supports anti theft and recovery procedures.
- Message buffering on the device helps Plaspy receive queued data once connectivity is restored.
