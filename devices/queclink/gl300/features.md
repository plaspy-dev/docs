# QuecLink - GL300 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the QuecLink GL300 and explains how its capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive details about the tracker, how it delivers location and telemetry into Plaspy, and the operational value it provides for monitoring and situational awareness.

Exact feature availability for any installed GL300 can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and specific manufacturer implementation. For device specific parameters, configuration options, and the most current technical details, always consult the official QuecLink documentation.

## Feature Overview

The QuecLink GL300 is a compact, water-resistant GNSS tracker designed for continuous location reporting in outdoor and exposed environments. When used with Plaspy, the GL300 feeds live position data and telemetry that support mapping, historical playback, and event monitoring across personnel and small asset tracking scenarios.

- Multi-constellation GNSS support for improved satellite availability and positioning consistency in varied terrain.
- Water-resistant rugged design to maintain tracking continuity in rainy and coastal conditions.
- Compact form factor suitable for mounting on people or small portable assets without adding bulk.
- Proven field performance in live event monitoring where uninterrupted location feeds are critical for safety.
- Real-time integration into Plaspy to provide live maps, route playback, and operational reporting.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GL300

- Multi-constellation GNSS positioning with GPS and GLONASS support to enhance fix availability.
- Water-resistant enclosure suited for outdoor and coastal deployments.
- Compact, lightweight form factor designed for personal and small asset use.
- Continuous real-time tracking capability for live location feeds into monitoring platforms.
- Field-proven reliability demonstrated in endurance event and outdoor monitoring scenarios.
- Telemetry output designed to integrate with third party tracking platforms such as Plaspy.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the GL300 location and telemetry streams and makes that information available for mapping, monitoring, and reporting. The platform displays live positions, preserves historical tracks for playback, and exposes the events and status needed for operational awareness.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps and dashboards for situational awareness.
- Historical route playback in Plaspy helps reconstruct movements for post event review.
- Geofencing and alerting workflows in Plaspy make use of continuous position feeds for notifications and operational triggers.
- Plaspy surfaces device status and location trends to support safety teams and event organizers.
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols to simplify adding the GL300 into an existing deployment.

## Typical Use Cases

- Event safety and participant tracking during endurance races and outdoor challenges.
- Personal safety for hikers, guides, and field personnel operating in remote or exposed areas.
- Portable asset visibility for equipment that needs protection in wet or coastal environments.
- Anti-theft monitoring when combined with operational alerting in Plaspy dashboards.
- Operational monitoring and post event analysis using historical route data and reports.
