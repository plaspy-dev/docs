# QuecLink - GL100

![GL100](./logo.jpg)

The QuecLink GL100 is a powerful GPS tracker designed for vehicle, pet, and asset tracking. With its superior receive sensitivity and fast TTFF \(Time To First Fix\), you can easily monitor its location or receive periodic reports on a backend server or other device. The GL100 operates on quad-band GSM frequencies \(850/900/1800/1900\), ensuring reliable communication wherever you are.

One of the standout features of the GL100 is its built-in SIRF Star III chipset, which provides exceptional tracking sensitivity and autonomous sensitivity. With a tracking sensitivity of -158 dBm and an autonomous sensitivity of -142 dBm, you can rely on accurate and precise location data. The GL100 also boasts a fast TTFF and high accuracy, ensuring that you can quickly and accurately locate your vehicle, pet, or asset.

In addition to its impressive technical capabilities, the GL100 also offers a range of convenient features. It is water-resistant, making it suitable for use in various environments. The built-in 3D motion sensor allows for advanced tracking and monitoring capabilities. The GL100 also features a white OLED display \(128\*96\), providing clear and easy-to-read information. With low power consumption and long standby time, you can rely on the GL100 for extended periods without needing to recharge.

The GL100 is certified with PTCRB, FCC, and CE, ensuring compliance with industry standards. It also comes with the embedded full-featured @Track protocol, allowing system integrators to easily set up custom tracking platforms to communicate with the GL100. Whether you need to track vehicles, pets, or assets, the QuecLink GL100 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that will meet your needs.
