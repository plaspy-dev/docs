# QuecLink - GL100

<div className="device-logo">
![GL100](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The QuecLink GL100 is a compact GPS tracker designed for vehicle, pet, and general asset tracking. It combines a sensitive SIRF Star III receiver with fast time to first fix to deliver reliable position reports. The unit supports quad band GSM frequencies for wide area communication, includes a white OLED display for basic local readout, and offers water resistance and a built in motion sensor for practical use across different environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GL100 can deliver location and periodic reports to a backend platform such as Plaspy for visibility and operational oversight. Its embedded @Track protocol and industry certifications make it straightforward to integrate with fleet and asset management workflows, allowing organizations to view real time positions, historical routes, and device status within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity SIRF Star III receiver for improved satellite acquisition and positioning.
- Fast time to first fix for quicker initial location fixes when tracking begins.
- Quad band GSM support for broad regional communication coverage.
- Water resistant design and a 3D motion sensor for reliable use on vehicles pets and assets.
- Clear white OLED display for local information and status feedback.
- Low power consumption and extended standby performance suitable for longer deployments.
- Certified with PTCRB FCC and CE for regulatory compliance and easier deployment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GL100 can be connected to Plaspy to provide centralized tracking and reporting for vehicles and assets. Plaspy ingests the device's location updates and status messages so teams can monitor movements, respond to alerts, and generate operational reports.

- View live location and breadcrumb history from GL100 devices on Plaspy maps.
- Configure geofences and receive alerts in Plaspy when a device enters or leaves defined areas.
- Collect periodic position reports for route analysis and historical playback.
- Organize devices into groups for fleet level monitoring and role based visibility.
- Use Plaspy reporting to summarize trips activity and device uptime based on GL100 data.
- Monitor device connectivity and basic status indicators to support operational decision making.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time vehicle tracking for small fleets and personal vehicles.
- Pet tracking for monitoring location and movement patterns.
- Asset protection for portable equipment and valuable items in transit.
- Rental equipment tracking to verify locations and usage history.
- Mixed asset fleets where a compact and water resistant tracker is beneficial.
