# QuecLink - GL501MG

![GL501MG](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GL501MG is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker intended for long term unattended asset monitoring. Based on the GL500MG platform, this model adds modern low power cellular connectivity, wireless charging and Bluetooth sensor support while keeping a sealed IP67 enclosure and internal antennas for straightforward deployment on trailers, containers, pallets and other remote assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the GL501MG can deliver location and onboard telemetry into Plaspy systems for continuous visibility. Its combination of long standby life, internal environmental sensors and BLE accessory connectivity makes it a relevant choice when you need low maintenance tracking with cloud based monitoring and alerting through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker designed for long term unattended asset monitoring with modern low power cellular connectivity and 2G fallback.
- High sensitivity u‑blox GNSS receiver with sub few meter autonomous accuracy for dependable location reporting.
- Long battery standby life for infrequent reporting scenarios, with Qi wireless charging for easy replenishment.
- IP67 sealed enclosure with internal temperature and light sensors plus motion detection for environmental and tamper awareness.
- Built in BLE support to pair accessories and extend telemetry without additional wiring.
- Optional magnetic case and internal antennas simplify mounting and reduce installation footprint.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GL501MG transmits position and sensor data in a Plaspy compatible format so devices can be onboarded quickly into Plaspy fleets. Once connected, the tracker supplies regular location updates and sensor signals that feed Plaspy dashboards, rules and notifications for operational oversight.

- Continuous location visibility for moving and stationary assets, supporting scheduled reporting frequency for battery conservation.
- Telemetry and status alerts such as motion, tamper indications, and low battery to trigger Plaspy notifications and workflows.
- Environmental monitoring via internal temperature and light readings to support cold chain and warehouse condition tracking inside Plaspy reports.
- BLE accessory integration to bring additional sensor data into Plaspy without physical rewiring.
- Geofence and event driven reporting to generate automated alerts and historical movement reports in Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer, container and lot management where long unattended battery life reduces maintenance visits.
- Anti theft and tamper monitoring with motion and light based alerts feeding Plaspy incident workflows.
- Cold chain and warehouse environmental monitoring using onboard sensors and BLE probes for condition logging.
- Remote asset standby tracking for equipment and stored goods that require periodic location checks.
- Fleet inventory and yard management where compact sealed devices simplify deployment and tracking.
