# QuecLink - GV58CG

![GV58CG](./tracker.png)

The GV58CG is a compact GNSS telematics tracker from QuecLink designed for fleet management and vehicle security. It combines precise positioning, practical I O and accessory support, and a small form factor to deliver real time tracking, telemetry and vehicle control capabilities suitable for fleet operators, car rental services and stolen vehicle recovery workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GV58CG can stream location and vehicle status into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts and historical reporting. Its support for cellular connectivity with fallback, high sensitivity GNSS, driver identification options and BLE sensor integration makes it a flexible choice for organizations that want to extend Plaspy visibility to vehicle level telemetry and anti theft workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking and fleet management integration
- Cellular connectivity with fallback to help maintain continuous telemetry delivery
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning for accurate vehicle location reporting
- Ignition detection and fuel cut off control for anti theft and immobilizer workflows
- BLE 5.2 and 1 wire support for driver identification and sensor based telemetry
- Compact form factor with internal backup battery and advanced alarm reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GV58CG streams position updates, status events and sensor telemetry to the Plaspy platform where fleet managers can view live location, receive alerts and generate historical reports. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it available across dashboards, maps and reporting tools for operational oversight.

- Live location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for continuous fleet visibility
- Ignition detection reported to Plaspy to support engine on off events and shift logs
- Fuel monitoring via analog input and optional BLE fuel sensors with trends and alerts in Plaspy
- Remote immobilizer and fuel cut off actions executed from Plaspy via configurable commands
- BLE sensors and iButton driver ID integrated into Plaspy workflows for environment monitoring and driver attribution

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization management using ignition and fuel cut off controls
- Car rental and leasing operations for driver identification and trip accountability
- Stolen vehicle recovery with continuous tracking and tow or alarm notifications
- Logistics and route compliance monitoring with scheduled reporting and geo fence alerts
- Asset environment monitoring using BLE sensors for temperature humidity or tilt data

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GV58CG pairs practical, purpose built telematics hardware with the centralized visibility and reporting that Plaspy provides. Its compact design and supported inputs make it suited to installations where space is limited but dependable tracking and simple vehicle control are required. For fleets and integrators, that combination enables streamlined monitoring, theft mitigation and basic telemetry without large or complex hardware.
