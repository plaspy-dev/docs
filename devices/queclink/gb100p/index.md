# QuecLink - GB100P

<div className="device-logo">
![GB100P](./tracker.png)
</div>

The QuecLink GB100P is a compact, battery mounted GPS tracker built for cost effective, reliable telematics in vehicle programs. Designed for harsh vehicle environments, it combines a high performance GNSS receiver, quad band GSM GPRS connectivity and BLE 4.2 support with a high rate inertial sensor suite to capture crash events and detailed driving motion. Its small form factor and IP65 enclosure make it suitable for discreet installations where robust telemetry and message retention are required.

As a device listed as compatible with Plaspy, the GB100P can forward location, high resolution inertial data and accessory telemetry into Plaspy cloud services for real time monitoring and historical analysis. That compatibility makes the GB100P relevant for usage based insurance programs, automobile finance tracking and fleet operations that rely on continuous data capture, buffered delivery and crash event reconstruction through Plaspy dashboards and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible compact GPS tracker suitable for vehicle telematics and usage based insurance deployments
- High rate inertial sensing with a 6 axis sensor suite for crash event capture and driving behavior monitoring
- BLE 4.2 support to extend telemetry with accessory sensors and beacon workflows
- Robust data retention and message buffering to preserve telemetry during intermittent cellular coverage
- Rugged design with IP65 protection and an internal backup battery for engine compartment style installations
- Proven option for existing programs with recommended newer models offered for new projects

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GB100P sends location, compressed GPS packets and high resolution inertial events to Plaspy cloud services where data is processed for live tracking, alerts and reporting. Plaspy ingests buffered messages and accessory telemetry so operators can maintain continuity of vehicle history and run analytics for safety, risk and operational oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry delivery into Plaspy for live fleet visibility
- High rate crash and driving behavior packets used for event reconstruction and scoring
- Geo fence, parking and speed alerts to trigger notifications and workflows
- Buffered message delivery so stored data uploads to Plaspy after connectivity is restored
- BLE accessory data passed to Plaspy for driver ID, sensor telemetry and proximity use cases

## Typical Use Cases

- Usage based insurance programs requiring crash capture and driving behavior analytics
- Automobile finance and asset tracking for locating financed vehicles and recovery workflows
- Fleet operations that need reliable telemetry in areas with intermittent cellular coverage
- Driver safety and coaching programs using high frequency inertial data for behavior monitoring
- BLE enabled workflows for driver identification and accessory state tracking

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GB100P pairs precise GNSS positioning with high frequency inertial sensing and BLE extensibility, making it a capable option for organizations using Plaspy to power UBI, safety and finance related telematics. Its buffering capabilities and rugged packaging support deployments where connectivity is variable and reliable post event data is important for analysis and compliance.

QuecLink has issued an end of life notice for the GB100P with orders ceased November 15, 2024, so while the device remains a proven choice for existing installations, teams planning new projects should consider the recommended replacement models noted by the manufacturer.
