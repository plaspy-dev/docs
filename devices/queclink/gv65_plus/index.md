# QuecLink - GV65 Plus

<div className="device-logo">
![GV65 Plus](./tracker.png)
</div>

The GV65 Plus is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for professional fleet management and vehicle security. Purpose built for logistics, courier services, stolen vehicle recovery and long distance trucking, the device delivers dependable location, telemetry and event data while maintaining a small footprint suitable for covert installations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GV65 Plus integrates into real time tracking platforms to provide continuous visibility, alerts and reporting. Its internal backup battery, flexible I/O and sensor support make it a practical choice for fleet managers who need reliable anti theft, telemetry and operational oversight within the Plaspy environment.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking, alerts and fleet management dashboards.
- Internal 250 mAh Li Polymer backup battery for continued operation during external power loss or tampering.
- Compact, covert form factor with internal GNSS and GSM antennas and option for external GNSS antenna.
- Broad cellular support with multi mode reporting using TCP UDP and SMS for wide coverage.
- Flexible I O and expandability including 1 wire support, ignition and digital inputs, analog input and output control via accessory modules.
- On board sensors and alarms such as a 3 axis accelerometer, geo fencing regions, tow and crash detection for event reporting.
- Large message buffer to preserve data when connectivity is intermittent.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GV65 Plus streams location and vehicle telemetry so dispatchers and managers can monitor assets, respond to alerts and generate operational reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s reports and presents position, event and status information within dashboards and reporting tools to support day to day fleet operations.

- Real time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for continuous fleet visibility.
- Ignition status and digital input events reported to Plaspy to support trip start stop and alarm logic.
- Fuel level and analog input data can be captured and graphed in Plaspy with configurable alerts for low fuel or abnormal trends.
- Remote output control allows Plaspy to initiate immobilization or other remote actions where permitted.
- 1 wire temperature sensors and iButton driver ID entries can be reported into Plaspy for identity and environmental monitoring.
- Buffered messaging preserves events during connectivity gaps so Plaspy receives recorded data when a connection is restored.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and stolen vehicle recovery with continuous tracking and backup battery support.
- Driver identification and behavior monitoring using driver ID readers and accelerometer based event detection.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry to track consumption patterns and detect shrinkage.
- Long distance trucking and logistics where compact install and message buffering support intermittent connectivity.
- Vehicle bus data capture and diagnostics when using optional expansion accessories for richer telematics.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GV65 Plus combines a discreet form factor with practical features that support common fleet management needs. Its internal backup battery and robust positioning performance help ensure continuous tracking and event reporting, while flexible inputs and expansion options let organizations capture ignition, fuel and driver ID without wholesale vehicle modifications.

Paired with Plaspy, the GV65 Plus becomes part of a complete fleet management workflow for visibility, alerts and reporting. For teams that need a small, dependable tracker with scalable sensor integration, this model is a sensible Plaspy compatible option that supports anti theft, telemetry and operational oversight.
