# QuecLink - GV75W

![GV75W](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GV75W is a rugged, waterproof GPS tracker engineered for motorcycles, quad bikes, watercraft and light heavy machinery. Built for compact vehicle installations and harsh outdoor environments, the GV75W combines high sensitivity GNSS positioning with worldwide cellular connectivity and extreme low power behaviour to deliver reliable real time tracking, theft protection and fleet telemetry where space and durability matter.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the GV75W integrates with Plaspy to provide live location, geo fence alerts, driving behaviour reporting and remote control of digital outputs. Its combination of durable enclosure, power saving features and event driven reporting makes it a practical option for organizations using Plaspy for fleet monitoring, asset protection and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking and telemetry integration via TCP UDP or SMS
- IP67 waterproof housing suited to motorcycles watercraft and outdoor equipment
- Ultra low power behaviour with zero power consumption when ignition is off to preserve vehicle battery
- High sensitivity u‑blox GNSS with position accuracy under 2.5 m CEP and strong tracking sensitivity
- Wide cellular coverage with UMTS HSPA and quad band GSM for global data transmission
- Built in backup Li Polymer battery and broad operating voltage range for vehicle use
- Multiple I O and serial interface options for ignition detection external device integration and event reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the GV75W streams GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry to the cloud using TCP UDP or SMS. Plaspy ingests scheduled position reports alarm signals and event notifications from the tracker to power live maps historical playback alerts and operational analytics. Event driven reporting and remote control features allow operators to respond quickly to theft or unauthorized movement.

- Real time location and telemetry updates transmitted over cellular networks into Plaspy for live map visibility
- Ignition status and runtime signals used by Plaspy dashboards for engine on analytics and usage reporting
- Alarm and geo fence events such as parking alarm tow alarm and low battery forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notifications
- Remote control of digital outputs through Plaspy using OTA commands to activate relays or accessories when wired accordingly
- Driving behaviour and crash detection events sent to Plaspy to support driver scoring incident reconstruction and safety programs

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle anti theft and recovery where waterproof ruggedness and battery preservation are critical
- Fleet management for small vehicles and utility fleets operating in exposed environments
- Asset monitoring for quad bikes light construction equipment and watercraft needing reliable location and tow alarms
- Driver behaviour and incident reporting for safety and compliance programs
- Remote control and immobilization scenarios where Plaspy triggers digital outputs to manage vehicle access

The GV75W offers a focused blend of durability precise GNSS positioning and power saving design that complements Plaspy’s real time tracking and fleet analytics.
