# QuecLink - GV75W Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the QuecLink GV75W and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for live tracking and fleet telemetry. It focuses on the practical, non sensitive aspects of the GV75W that matter when integrating the device with Plaspy, including connectivity options, environmental ratings, and common event reporting that Plaspy consumes.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by factory firmware, hardware revision, regional model, or installation method. For the most current and device specific details consult the official QuecLink documentation while using this page to understand how those features are typically used in Plaspy deployments.

## Feature Overview

The GV75W is a compact rugged tracker designed for motorcycles, watercraft, ATVs and light machinery where waterproofing and battery preservation are primary concerns. It combines high sensitivity GNSS reception with global cellular data links and low power modes to support long parked intervals and reliable position reporting into platforms such as Plaspy.

- IP67 rated waterproof enclosure for exposed vehicle and equipment installations.
- High sensitivity GNSS performance with reported position accuracy under 2.5 m CEP for precise location on Plaspy maps.
- Ultra low power behavior including zero power consumption when ignition is off to protect vehicle batteries.
- Multi network cellular connectivity with UMTS HSPA and quad band GSM to transmit data to Plaspy by TCP UDP or SMS.
- Built in backup Li Polymer battery and wide operating voltage range for resilient operation across vehicle types.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GV75W

- Waterproof IP67 enclosure and compact form factor suitable for motorcycles and small outdoor equipment.
- High sensitivity u blox GNSS receiver with sub 2.5 m CEP accuracy and deep signal tracking sensitivity.
- Cellular data connectivity supporting UMTS HSPA and quad band GSM for worldwide coverage where networks are available.
- Ultra low power operation with zero power draw in ignition off state to prevent battery drain during extended parking.
- Multiple I O inputs and outputs plus RS232 serial interface for ignition detection and external device integration.
- Built in backup Li Polymer battery to maintain reporting and alarms if main power is removed.
- OTA control for digital outputs and firmware updates via cable for remote management and configuration.
- LED status indicators and internal antennas for straightforward installation feedback.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the GV75W reports positions and event messages that Plaspy uses to display live location, history, and alerts. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and accepts reports over TCP UDP or SMS, letting operators monitor units on maps, configure notifications, and issue OTA commands when supported by the device firmware.

- Live GNSS positions appear on Plaspy maps for real time tracking and playback of historical routes.
- Event messages such as ignition status, tamper or tow alarms, and low battery reports are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and logging.
- Geo fence and parking related events generated by the tracker can be displayed as alerts and history items in Plaspy.
- Remote activation of digital outputs through Plaspy when OTA control is enabled by the device firmware supports remote accessory control workflows.
- Backup battery and power state data shown in Plaspy help with battery health monitoring and maintenance planning.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle anti theft and recovery tracking where waterproofing and low power consumption are essential.
- Fleet management for small vehicles and utility fleets that need rugged, compact trackers with continuous location reporting.
- Asset monitoring for quad bikes, watercraft and light machinery used in outdoor or harsh environments.
- Long term parked asset protection where zero power draw conserves vehicle battery while still providing tow and movement alerts.
- Remote telemetry and event driven alerting for operators who require timely notifications and historical route data.
