# QuecLink - GV355CEU

![GV355CEU](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GV355CEU is a high precision LTE vehicle GPS tracker designed for demanding fleet management and regulatory compliance. Built for mixed fleets and intensive use, the device provides real time tracking, comprehensive vehicle telemetry and remote tachograph retrieval to help fleets monitor vehicle location, driver hours and critical diagnostic information from a single system.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GV355CEU integrates with Plaspy to centralize location, tachograph and vehicle data for operational oversight. When paired with Plaspy, fleets can use the tracker to maintain high accuracy positioning, automate DDD file retrieval for compliance workflows and surface alerts and reports that support safety, maintenance and anti theft procedures.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker offering reliable real time tracking and visibility across mixed fleets
- Remote tachograph support for automated DDD file downloads and driver hours management
- Broad vehicle interface support for rich telemetry and diagnostic data extraction
- High precision GNSS positioning with autonomous accuracy under 2 meters for accurate routing and geofencing
- Support for external sensors and driver identification for cargo and driver management
- Compact rugged form factor with internal antennas and backup reporting during power events

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the GV355CEU streams location and vehicle telemetry into the platform so managers get a unified view of assets, drivers and compliance status. Plaspy ingests position updates, vehicle parameters and tachograph files and uses that data to generate alerts, reports and operational workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for live tracking and historical playback in Plaspy
- Remote tachograph DDD file downloading for regulatory reporting and driver hours reconciliation
- Input and ignition status used by Plaspy to trigger rules, alerts and automation workflows
- Vehicle telemetry from supported interfaces available for diagnostics, fuel monitoring and maintenance reporting where exposed by the vehicle
- Remote output control to support immobilizer or engine cutoff workflows when configured through Plaspy
- Sensor and driver ID data fed into Plaspy for temperature monitoring, cargo oversight and driver assignment

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet compliance and tachograph management for regulatory audits and driver hours reporting
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using geo fences, ignition monitoring and remote outputs
- Mixed fleet telemetry for maintenance planning across heavy and light vehicles
- Cold chain and cargo monitoring by combining temperature sensors with GPS position in Plaspy
- Driver identification and behaviour monitoring to support safety coaching and payroll reconciliation

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GV355CEU is suited to organizations that require dependable tracking plus deeper vehicle data accessible from a centralized telematics platform. Its focus on precision positioning, tachograph support and wide vehicle interface compatibility makes it a practical choice for fleets that need compliance, safety and operational insights in one view.
