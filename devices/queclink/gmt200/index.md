# QuecLink - GMT200

![GMT200](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GMT200 is a dedicated motorcycle GPS tracker built for covert installation, stable connectivity and precise GNSS positioning. It is designed specifically for motorcycle security and small vehicle asset protection, providing continuous location reporting and event driven alerts such as movement detection and ignition on and off notifications. The GMT200 has been used in real world deployments for theft recovery and ongoing fleet visibility.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GMT200 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational workflows. When integrated with Plaspy, the tracker’s real time telemetry and alarms become part of a broader fleet or security service, enabling live maps, alarm handling and historical playback within the Plaspy platform.

- Plaspy compatible for centralized tracking and alarm management
- Real time tracking with event driven alerts for movement and ignition on off events

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GMT200 supplies continuous location updates and event notifications that Plaspy ingests for live monitoring, alerting and reporting. Plaspy transforms device telemetry into actionable information for operators, dispatchers and vehicle owners to support rapid response and longer term analysis.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Movement detection and ignition on off events appear as instant alarms in Plaspy
- Geofence monitoring and perimeter alerts tied to automated rules and notifications
- Historical route playback and event logs for investigations and compliance review
- Centralized alarm workflows and reporting to coordinate recovery and operational actions
