# QuecLink - GMT200

![GMT200](./tracker.png)

The Queclink GMT200 is a dedicated motorcycle GPS tracker engineered for covert installation, stable connectivity and precise GNSS positioning. Designed for motorcycle security and small-vehicle asset protection, the GMT200 is Plaspy compatible and built to deliver real-time tracking and event-driven alerts that integrate with monitoring platforms and mobile apps.

The GMT200 has proven its value in real-world deployments \(for example, the TRAKBIKE anti-theft program in Hong Kong\), where movement detection and ignition on/off alarms led to rapid recovery of stolen motorcycles. Its discreet form factor and continuous location reporting make it a practical choice for anti-theft protection, fleet management of motorcycle fleets, and aftermarket security services that require reliable telemetry and fast alarm handling.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GMT200 provides continuous telemetry and event notifications so administrators and riders get fast, actionable information. Plaspy ingests the GMT200’s location and event data for live maps, alerting, and reporting — enabling anti-theft workflows, rapid response, and long-term fleet visibility.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Movement detection and ignition on/off alerts available as instant alarms in Plaspy’s console and mobile app.
- Geofence support and alarm management for perimeter breach notifications and automated rules.
- Historical route playback for recovery investigations and compliance reporting.
- Platform integrations can extend telemetry to fuel monitoring or remote immobilizer workflows where supported by installation and backend configuration.
