# QuecLink - GV310LAU

![GV310LAU](./tracker.png)

The GV310LAU is a rugged LTE Cat 4 GPS tracker designed for demanding vehicle applications including heavy trucks and cars. It pairs multi constellation GNSS positioning with high speed cellular connectivity to provide reliable, real time location and telemetry. The unit is compact and built for continuous vehicle use, with configurable inputs, CAN capture and onboard features intended for fleet management and anti theft workflows.

This model is Plaspy compatible out of the box, so data from the GV310LAU can be ingested into Plaspy for dashboarding, alerts and reporting. Its advanced alarm capabilities and remote control pathways map naturally to Plaspy workflows, enabling operators to monitor vehicle state, respond to events and integrate sensor data into fleet operations without needing extensive additional configuration.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat 4 connectivity and fallback for resilient real time tracking
- Multi constellation u blox GNSS for high precision position fixes and fast time to first fix
- Rich vehicle I O support including CAN capture and configurable digital and analog inputs for broad telemetry
- Internal BLE 5.2 support for Bluetooth sensors and beacons to extend monitoring to temperature and proximity
- Advanced alarms including crash, jamming, tow and geofence events that can trigger Plaspy actions
- Wide operating voltage tolerance and backup battery to maintain data flow during power interruptions
- Compact rugged form factor suited to heavy vehicles and mixed fleet deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GV310LAU transmits location, vehicle signals and sensor telemetry so Plaspy can display live positions, replay routes and trigger rule based notifications. Configurable inputs and alarm states from the tracker appear as events in Plaspy, allowing fleet managers to create automated responses, scheduled reports and operational alerts.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered over cellular connectivity for continuous visibility in Plaspy
- Vehicle status reporting from ignition and other inputs for accurate work cycle and mileage tracking
- CAN and analog input telemetry for fuel monitoring, engine data and diagnostics displayed in Plaspy dashboards
- Remote outputs and alarm mapping enable Plaspy initiated interventions such as immobilizer control where workflows and regulations permit
- BLE sensor and beacon data integrated into Plaspy for temperature sensitive or presence monitoring of cargo or assets

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet management for heavy trucks and mixed fleets requiring continuous tracking and reporting
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using alarms and remote intervention capabilities coordinated through Plaspy
- Insurance and risk monitoring programs leveraging crash and aggressive driving events for telematics
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring using BLE sensors to feed Plaspy alerts and logs
- Vehicle diagnostics and fuel level monitoring via CAN and analog inputs for operational efficiency
