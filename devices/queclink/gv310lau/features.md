# QuecLink - GV310LAU Features

This page provides a public, feature oriented overview of the QuecLink GV310LAU and how its capabilities relate to using the device with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical functions and operational value the tracker delivers when paired with Plaspy for fleet monitoring, alerts, and telemetry ingestion.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. For device specific configurations, firmware details, and the latest technical specifications consult the official QuecLink documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GV310LAU is a rugged LTE Cat 4 tracker designed for demanding vehicle applications. It combines high speed cellular connectivity with multi constellation GNSS positioning, flexible vehicle I O, CAN capture, and BLE support to provide continuous tracking and extended telemetry for fleet and anti theft workflows.

- High speed LTE connectivity with fallback to 3G and 2G for broad coverage and reliable real time updates.
- Multi constellation GNSS for accurate location reporting suitable for route replay and precise positioning in Plaspy.
- Vehicle oriented I O and CAN capture for engine and sensor telemetry plus support for digital and analog inputs.
- Internal BLE 5.2 radio to extend telemetry via Bluetooth sensors and beacons for cargo or accessory monitoring.
- Advanced alarms and safety features such as crash detection, jamming detection, tow and power alarms, and geofence monitoring.
- Rugged compact form factor with wide operating voltage range and internal backup battery to maintain continuity during power events.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GV310LAU

- LTE Cat 4 cellular modem with fallback to legacy networks for continuous connectivity.
- Multi constellation GNSS receiver supporting GPS GLONASS Galileo and Beidou for improved positioning accuracy.
- CAN data capture to surface vehicle diagnostics and sensor telemetry when available.
- Configurable inputs and outputs including RS232 serial port digital and analog inputs and a latched digital output.
- Internal BLE 5.2 for connecting Bluetooth sensors and beacons to add temperature or proximity telemetry.
- Advanced event and alarm capabilities including crash detection towing and power loss alerts, plus geofence support.
- Built in backup battery and wide operating voltage range to support installations on heavy trucks and mixed fleets.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the GV310LAU telemetry and events to provide live visibility, historical playback, and configurable alerts. The tracker maps its inputs and alarms to Plaspy events so operators receive actionable information in dashboards and reporting tools.

- Real time location updates and route history appear in Plaspy to support tracking and fleet oversight.
- Digital and analog input changes such as ignition and auxiliary sensors are available as events for status tracking and duty cycle reporting.
- CAN and sensor telemetry can be visualized in Plaspy dashboards for diagnostics, fuel level monitoring, and performance trends where vehicle data is exposed.
- Alarms and geofence triggers from the device generate Plaspy notifications and can be used to drive automated workflows or operator alerts.
- BLE sensor data and beacon presence reported by the device can be shown in Plaspy for cargo monitoring and asset proximity checks.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet tracking and route replay for heavy trucks and mixed vehicle fleets.
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows with alarm reporting and remote interventions where supported.
- Usage based insurance and safety programs using crash and aggressive driving event reporting.
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring using BLE sensors and beacon data for refrigerated loads.
- Vehicle diagnostics and fuel monitoring by combining CAN capture with analog sensor inputs.
- Continuous monitoring in installations that require rugged hardware and wide vehicle voltage tolerance.
