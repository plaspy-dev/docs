# QuecLink - GV51MG

![GV51MG](./logo..png)

The QuecLink GV51MG is a compact, ultra thin LTE vehicle tracker designed for covert installation. It offers global LTE Cat M1 and NB1 connectivity with 2G fallback and a unique antenna solution, making it suitable for a wide range of vehicle tracking scenarios. Its small form factor and discreet profile allow the device to be easily hidden within a vehicle while delivering essential tracking and status information.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GV51MG can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet management workflows to provide location visibility, basic vehicle state monitoring, and secure data transmission. The unit's simple wiring via an in line connector and its configurable digital I O options make it a practical choice for organizations that need reliable, low profile tracking tied into Plaspy for operational oversight and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra thin, compact form factor for discreet or covert installation in vehicles
- Global LTE Cat M1 and NB1 support with 2G fallback for broad coverage
- Basic digital I O for ignition detection and vehicle control along with an extra configurable I O
- AES 256 encryption to protect data integrity and help secure business critical telemetry
- In line connector for straightforward installation and vehicle integration
- Well suited for applications where size, security, and reliable connectivity are important

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the QuecLink GV51MG delivers location and device status to the platform so teams can monitor vehicles and respond to events. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to provide a consolidated view of fleets, enable alerts, and produce operational reports that help manage assets.

- Real time location visibility in Plaspy maps for tracking vehicle movements
- Alerts and notifications for geofence events, motion changes, and ignition state updates
- Fleet level dashboards and historical reports for utilization and activity review
- Support for recovery workflows by surfacing last known location and recent activity
- Centralized device management and visibility to keep trackers organized within Plaspy
