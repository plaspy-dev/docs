# QuecLink - GL33CG

![GL33CG](./logo..png)

The QuecLink GL33CG is a versatile GPS tracker designed for cargo recovery. It features LTE CAT1 fallback 2G connectivity, allowing for reliable and fast data transmission. The tracker is disguised as a box, making it ideal for tracking expensive merchandise packed in carton boxes that may be at risk of theft during transport. With its compact dimensions of 97 x 43.5 x 21mm and lightweight design weighing only 91g, the GL33CG can easily be concealed within cargo without drawing attention.

One of the standout features of the GL33CG is its RF433 transmission capability. This allows the tracker to transmit GPS/LBS location information to a server and can be detected by a search device operating at 433/434MHz through RF signals. Additionally, the tracker supports LBS location, providing an alternative means of tracking in areas where GPS signals may be weak or unavailable.

The GL33CG offers a range of advanced functionalities to enhance tracking and security. It has a flight mode that can be activated to turn off cellular functions when airborne, complying with aviation regulations. The tracker also features a built-in temperature sensor for temperature monitoring, allowing users to receive alerts when the detected temperature is outside or inside a predefined range. With its long-lasting Li-Polymer backup battery with a capacity of 4000mAh, the GL33CG can operate for extended periods without requiring frequent recharging.

Overall, the QuecLink GL33CG is a reliable and discreet GPS tracker that is well-suited for cargo recovery applications. Its LTE CAT1/2G connectivity, RF433 transmission, box-disguised appearance, and advanced features make it an excellent choice for tracking valuable merchandise during transport.

#### Key Features:

- LTE CAT1 fallback 2G connectivity
- RF433 transmission for tracking
- Box-disguised appearance for covert tracking
- Flight mode for compliance with aviation regulations
- LBS location for areas with weak GPS signals
