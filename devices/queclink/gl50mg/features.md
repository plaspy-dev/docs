# QuecLink - GL50MG Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the QuecLink GL50MG and explains how the tracker can be used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive capabilities that are relevant when deploying the GL50MG for asset protection, fleet monitoring, and stolen vehicle recovery using Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and manufacturer implementation. For device-specific technical details and the latest compatibility information, consult QuecLink documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GL50MG is a compact, waterproof asset tracker designed for long-term deployments where concealment, durability, and low maintenance are priorities. It combines low-power cellular connectivity, an extended battery profile, and optional wireless accessory support to reduce service visits while keeping assets visible in a monitoring platform such as Plaspy.

- Low-power wide-area cellular support with LTE Cat M1 and NB-IoT and fallback to 2G for broad regional coverage.
- Up to three years of battery life under typical reporting profiles to minimize maintenance cycles.
- IP67-rated waterproof and dustproof enclosure for harsh outdoor and concealed installations.
- Built-in Bluetooth Low Energy to connect compatible wireless accessories and sensors without hardwired interfaces.
- Programmable button for quick alerts or status checks that can be forwarded to Plaspy as events.
- Micro, installation-free form factor designed for discreet mounting on vehicles and other high-value assets.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GL50MG

- LTE Cat M1 and NB-IoT connectivity with 2G backward compatibility.
- Extremely long standby battery life designed to support multi-year deployments.
- IP67 enclosure for water and dust resistance suitable for outdoor and concealed placements.
- Integrated Bluetooth Low Energy for accessory and sensor pairing.
- Programmable user button that generates event notifications.
- Compact, installation-free micro form factor for discreet mounting and tamper resistance.
- Multi-band cellular support to enable broader geographic coverage when deployed in regional variants.
- Plaspy compatibility for sending location and event data to a centralized monitoring platform.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GL50MG streams location and event data that Plaspy processes for real-time visibility, historical playback, and alerting workflows. Plaspy ingests the device messages and makes them available for mapping, reporting, and integration with operational systems.

- Real-time location updates and historical routes available in Plaspy for monitoring and analysis.
- Programmable button events forwarded to Plaspy as alert triggers to support emergency notifications or theft reporting.
- BLE accessory data that the device forwards can be surfaced in Plaspy when accessories and firmware support the specific telemetry.
- Long battery life reduces the frequency of device maintenance events shown in Plaspy asset records.
- Discreet mounting and IP67 sealing complement Plaspy anti-theft workflows by enabling concealed installation combined with continuous monitoring.
