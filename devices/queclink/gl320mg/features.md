# QuecLink - GL320MG Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the QuecLink GL320MG and describes how its capabilities map to use with Plaspy. It is intended to help fleet managers, asset owners, and technical evaluators understand the practical functions of the GL320MG when used with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, accessory configuration, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device configuration, deployment, and the most current technical details consult the official QuecLink documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The GL320MG is an LTE Advanced asset tracker designed for long battery life and global cellular coverage with fallback for legacy networks. It is positioned for temporary and longer term tracking of assets, vehicles, and personal items where periodic real time location reporting and reliable connectivity are priorities.

- Global LTE Cat M1 and NB2 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback for broad network reach
- Internal battery offering extended standby time, quoted up to 300 hours when reporting every 10 minutes
- Support for accessory options including an external battery kit and an IP67 waterproof magnetic case for rugged mounting
- Integrated GNSS receiver for consistent location reporting compatible with Plaspy
- Compact form factor and magnetic mounting option suited to temporary or semi-permanent installations

## Core Features of QuecLink - GL320MG

- Global LTE Cat M1 and NB2 support with 2G fallback to maintain connectivity across regions
- Internal rechargeable battery optimized for extended standby and periodic reporting
- Accessory compatibility including a high capacity external battery kit and waterproof magnetic enclosure
- Integrated GNSS positioning using the device's onboard receiver
- Designed from the GL300 series with proven deployments in North America and other regions
- Compact and lightweight housing for discreet placement and easy installation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and processes location and status reports from supported trackers including the GL320MG. The platform interprets incoming device data so teams can monitor assets and vehicles in near real time and manage reporting behavior according to operational needs.

- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and accepts device reports for visibility on the platform
- Location points from the GL320MG are shown on Plaspy maps and timelines for route and history review
- Battery and accessory-based deployment choices help Plaspy users balance reporting frequency and operational runtime
- Waterproof magnetic enclosure and external battery options aid in physical deployment that Plaspy can track as asset attributes
- The GL300 series heritage means many operational behaviors are familiar to Plaspy users who have deployed similar trackers
