# QuecLink - GL500

![GL500](./logo.jpg)

The QuecLink GL500 is a compact GPS tracker built for fixed asset tracking. It ships with two user replaceable CR123A lithium batteries and is optimized for extremely low power consumption, enabling standby operation measured in years rather than weeks. The device includes an internal u-blox GPS chipset for reliable position fixes, a built in 3 axis accelerometer to detect movement, internal GSM and GPS antennas, and an optional internal microphone for two way communication when required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GL500 can feed long term location and movement data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and reporting. Plaspy can use the GL500 signal and alert capabilities to provide ongoing asset oversight, bring movement alerts into operational workflows, and preserve historical location records for audits and analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for fixed asset tracking with a focus on very long standby life
- Two user replaceable CR123A lithium batteries for simplified field maintenance
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer that triggers movement alerts
- Internal u blox GPS chipset and internal antennas for self contained positioning
- Communication via quad band GSM GPRS and SMS for remote reporting
- Optional internal microphone for two way audio where needed

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GL500 provides periodic position reports and event alerts that Plaspy can ingest and display alongside other fleet and asset data. Plaspy consolidates location history, notifies users of movement events reported by the tracker, and offers reporting and operational oversight tailored to long term asset deployments.

- Live and historical location visibility for each GL500 tracked asset
- Movement and tamper alerts from the accelerometer routed into Plaspy notifications
- Long term monitoring and low maintenance asset status tracking thanks to extended battery life
- Scheduled and on demand reporting for audits and operational analysis
- Grouping and status dashboards to manage fleets of fixed assets in Plaspy
- Optional audio capability surfaced to operators when devices are equipped with the internal microphone

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers, containers, and unattended assets in yards
- Monitoring construction or agricultural equipment during extended storage
- Asset inventory oversight where infrequent reporting and long battery life are priorities
- Remote location verification and movement alerts for high value items
- Deployments that require minimal maintenance cycles and clear historical records

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GL500 is a practical choice when organizations need reliable, low maintenance tracking for fixed assets. Its extended autonomous operation reduces the frequency of field visits for battery replacement, and the built in movement detection helps teams respond quickly to unauthorized activity. Using Plaspy to aggregate GL500 data adds centralized visibility, configurable alerts, and reporting tools that help turn device signals into operational intelligence.

If your primary goals are long term asset oversight, clear movement alerts, and straightforward integration into a monitoring platform, the GL500 paired with Plaspy offers a focused solution that balances endurance and visibility.
