# QuecLink - GL500

![GL500](./logo.jpg)

The QuecLink GL500 is a powerful GPS tracker specifically designed for fixed asset tracking applications. It is equipped with two user replaceable CR123A lithium batteries, ensuring long-lasting power. With its optimized configuration, the GL500 can operate autonomously for an impressive 1000 days. This makes it an ideal choice for long-term asset tracking without the need for frequent battery replacements.

One of the standout features of the GL500 is its built-in 3-axis accelerometer, which allows it to detect asset movement and transmit an alert message. This ensures that you are immediately notified if there is any unauthorized movement or tampering with your assets. Additionally, the GL500 is equipped with a quad-band GSM/GPRS module, enabling it to communicate with a customer's mobile phone or a backend server via GPRS/SMS. This allows for real-time tracking and the transfer of reports on GPS position and other relevant information.

The GL500 also boasts an internal u-blox chipset, ensuring accurate and reliable GPS positioning. It is equipped with internal GSM and GPS antennas, eliminating the need for external antennas and simplifying installation. For added convenience, the GL500 can be optionally equipped with an internal microphone, allowing for two-way communication when necessary.

With its low power consumption and long standby time, the QuecLink GL500 is a reliable and efficient GPS tracker that is perfect for fixed asset tracking applications. Its robust features, including the 3-axis accelerometer and integrated @Track protocol, make it easy to incorporate into existing tracking systems. Whether you need to track vehicles, equipment, or other valuable assets, the GL500 is a reliable solution that will provide you with peace of mind.
