# QuecLink - GL521MG

![GL521MG](./tracker.png)

The GL521MG is a rechargeable, long‑standby GPS tracker designed for robust asset monitoring and batch management. It combines cellular connectivity with internal GNSS positioning, Qi wireless charging, BLE accessory support and an IP67 rugged enclosure to deliver reliable location and environmental telemetry suited to cold‑chain, warehouse and static asset deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GL521MG can feed position data, environmental readings and alarm events into Plaspy for centralized visibility. Its low‑maintenance design, configurable power modes and common telemetry elements make it straightforward to integrate into Plaspy workflows for tracking, alerts and scheduled reporting across mixed asset fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Rechargeable long‑standby asset tracker built for low maintenance and extended field deployments.
- Global cellular connectivity with fallback for broader coverage and energy efficient telemetry.
- IP67 rugged enclosure with magnetic mounting option for vehicle, container and static asset use.
- Integrated environmental sensing including internal temperature and light sensing for condition monitoring.
- Qi wireless charging support for convenient field recharging and reduced handling time.
- BLE accessory support to extend telemetry through beacons or external sensors.
- Motion detection and geofence alarm capabilities to support anti‑theft and activation reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests standard telemetry and alarm events from the GL521MG so teams can monitor assets in real time and review historical activity. The device’s protocol compatibility and built‑in sensors allow Plaspy to unify location, environmental and status data into dashboards, alerts and reports.

- Real‑time and scheduled position updates to Plaspy for live tracking and historical playback.
- Temperature and light sensor readings delivered to Plaspy for environmental alerts and trend reporting.
- Motion and tamper events routed to Plaspy to trigger security workflows and notifications.
- Geofence alarms and activation reports used to automate site entry, exit and chain of custody alerts.
- BLE accessory data aggregated into Plaspy to enhance telemetry from external beacons or probes.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold‑chain logistics where combined location and temperature monitoring support compliance and alerts.
- Road freight and batch asset management for pooled containers and multi‑stop routes requiring long standby.
- Warehouse and indoor asset tracking using BLE accessories and compact, mountable housings.
- High‑value static asset surveillance benefiting from IP67 protection, magnetic mounting and movement alarms.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GL521MG pairs practical deployment features with the telemetry types Plaspy uses most: GNSS positions, environmental sensors, motion and geofence events. Its rechargeable design, wireless charging convenience and configurable power modes help operations balance responsiveness and runtime, while the rugged housing reduces service visits for widely distributed assets.

If you want a device that feeds a range of asset and environmental data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, reporting and alerting, the GL521MG represents a capable, easy to deploy option.
