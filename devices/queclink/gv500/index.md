# QuecLink - GV500

![GV500](./logo.jpg)

The QuecLink GV500 is a versatile vehicle tracking device that offers seamless connectivity through its OBDII port. With its compact design, installation is a breeze, making it an ideal choice for fleet management or personal vehicle tracking. The GV500 features an internal OBD reader that can extract valuable information from the vehicle's on-board computer and transmit it over GPRS networks. This allows for real-time monitoring of the vehicle's location or periodic tracking through a backend server and mobile devices.

Equipped with a high-sensitivity GPS receiver, the GV500 ensures fast and accurate positioning. Its quad-band GPRS/GSM subsystem supports frequencies of 850/900/1800/1900 MHz, enabling reliable communication across different regions. The device operates within a wide voltage range of 8V to 32V DC, ensuring compatibility with various vehicle types.

One of the standout features of the GV500 is its built-in 3-axis accelerometer, which serves multiple purposes. It enables motion detection, allowing for enhanced security and anti-theft measures. Additionally, the accelerometer contributes to power conservation through sophisticated algorithms, extending the device's standby time. The GV500 also boasts an internal battery, further enhancing its longevity.

System integration is made easy with the GV500, thanks to its embedded full-featured @Track protocol. This protocol supports a wide range of reports, including emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, low battery notifications, and scheduled GPS positions. The device is FCC, CE, and E-Mark certified, ensuring compliance with industry standards.

### Key Features:

- OBDII connectivity for easy installation
- Quad-band GSM/GPRS frequencies \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\)
- Wide operating voltage range \(8V to 32V DC\)
- Internal u-blox chipset for superior GPS performance
- Low power consumption and long standby time with internal battery
- Embedded full-featured @Track protocol for seamless integration
- Internal 3-axis accelerometer for power conservation and motion detection
- Internal GSM and GPS antennas for streamlined design
- FCC/CE/E-Mark certified for compliance and reliability
