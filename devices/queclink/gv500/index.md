# QuecLink - GV500

![GV500](./logo.jpg)

The QuecLink GV500 is a compact vehicle tracking device that connects through the vehicle OBDII port. It combines a high sensitivity GPS receiver with a quad band cellular subsystem to provide reliable positioning and remote data transmission. The device includes an internal OBD reader to extract vehicle on-board data, a 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection, and an internal battery to support standby and backup operation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GV500 can feed location and status information into the Plaspy platform for fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Its embedded @Track protocol and reporting features make it suitable for use with backend servers and mobile interfaces, allowing Plaspy users to consolidate vehicle visibility, alerts, and scheduled position reports within a single fleet management environment.

## Key Highlights

- OBDII connectivity for straightforward vehicle integration and data access
- High sensitivity GPS receiver for fast and accurate positioning
- Quad band GPRS/GSM support for broad regional communication coverage
- Internal 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection and power conservation
- Internal battery for extended standby and backup reporting
- Embedded full featured @Track protocol for server reporting and alerts
- Compliance with FCC CE and E-Mark certifications for industry standards

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GV500 supplies vehicle location and status data that Plaspy can ingest for live tracking, history, alerts, and reporting. Once the device is configured to send reports to a Plaspy compatible server, the platform can present the device data alongside other fleet assets to improve operational awareness.

- Real time and periodic GPS positions shown in Plaspy maps and timelines
- Motion based events and accelerometer alerts routed to Plaspy notification rules
- Scheduled position reports and emergency alerts available for Plaspy reporting
- OBDII derived vehicle information visible in Plaspy dashboards where supported
- Fleet level overviews and historical playback to support operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for dispatch and route oversight
- Theft detection and recovery support using motion and location alerts
- Rental or shared vehicle monitoring with scheduled position reporting
- Operational visibility for small to medium sized commercial fleets
- Backup location reporting when main vehicle power is interrupted

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GV500 is a practical option for organizations that want a compact, OBDII based tracker that can deliver consistent location updates and vehicle data to a central platform. Its combination of GPS performance, internal accelerometer, and embedded reporting protocol makes it a reasonable fit where easy installation and reliable reporting are priorities.

Paired with Plaspy, the GV500 can become part of a broader fleet management solution that emphasizes visibility, alerts, and consolidated reporting. If you need a tracker that works with a hosted fleet platform and supports vehicle level reporting, the GV500 is worth evaluating as part of your Plaspy deployment.
