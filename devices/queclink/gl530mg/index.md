# QuecLink - GL530MG

![GL530MG](./tracker.png)

The QuecLink GL530MG is a compact, waterproof asset tracker designed for long term, low maintenance monitoring of remote and high value equipment. It combines global low power cellular connectivity with fallback options, robust tamper and environmental sensing, and an enclosure rated for outdoor use. The unit is built for scenarios where long standby life and reliable position reporting matter, such as trailers, containers, static assets and refrigerated pallets.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the GL530MG can be integrated into Plaspy for centralized visibility and management. Plaspy ingests the device position, motion and environmental events to deliver live mapping, alerts and historical reports, making the GL530MG a practical choice for operations that need durable field hardware alongside cloud based fleet and asset oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible asset tracker with global low power cellular connectivity and EGPRS fallback for broad network reach
- Long standby life that minimizes battery changes and reduces ongoing maintenance
- Rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure with tamper and light sensors suitable for outdoor deployments
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver with autonomous position accuracy under typical conditions for dependable location fixes
- Built in motion detection and configurable reporting modes to balance battery life and update frequency
- Multiple mounting options including clip on and magnetic attachments for flexible deployment on trailers and containers
- Support for standard transport and device command methods to simplify integration with Plaspy

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GL530MG streams location and telemetry in formats that Plaspy accepts, allowing the platform to interpret position fixes, motion and tamper events, battery state and environmental readings. Plaspy uses that data to power live maps, alerts and reporting for operational oversight and incident response.

- Real time location updates and history playback for fleet and asset visibility
- Motion and tamper alerts routed to Plaspy for anti theft workflows and incident handling
- Environmental and temperature readings reported to Plaspy for cold chain monitoring and condition alerts
- Scheduled wake up and low battery reports to support predictable maintenance cycles
- Geo fence events forwarded to Plaspy for perimeter alerts and zone based reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain logistics for tracking location and internal environmental conditions of refrigerated pallets and containers
- Trailer and cargo monitoring with long term battery operation for parked or in transit assets
- Static high value asset oversight such as generators, construction equipment and site infrastructure with tamper detection
- Warehouse and yard management for parked trailers and inventory using scheduled reports and motion alerts
- Stolen asset recovery using higher frequency tracking modes and Plaspy alerting to assist location and retrieval
