# QuecLink - GL530MG Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the QuecLink GL530MG with Plaspy. It focuses on the tracker capabilities relevant to Plaspy users — location reporting, environmental sensing, tamper detection, connectivity options and long‑term battery performance — and explains how those capabilities map into practical visibility and workflows inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation method. If you plan a deployment that depends on a specific function, verify the current device documentation from the manufacturer and review firmware release notes to confirm the features you intend to use.

## Feature Overview

The GL530MG is a compact, waterproof asset tracker designed for long standby life and low‑maintenance deployments. It combines low‑power cellular connectivity with GNSS location, internal environmental and tamper sensing, and flexible reporting modes to support remote asset tracking and anti‑theft workflows.

- Long standby operation and configurable reporting modes to minimize maintenance and battery swaps.
- Global LTE Cat M1 / NB2 connectivity with EGPRS fallback for broader network reach.
- IP67 rugged enclosure and mounting options for outdoor or vehicle attachment.
- Internal GNSS for accurate position fixes together with motion, light and tamper sensors for event detection.
- Support for TCP, UDP and SMS transport and native @Track protocol commands for easy integration.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GL530MG

- Plaspy compatibility out of the box, enabling direct integration for real‑time tracking and history playback.
- Low‑power design and extended battery life with multiple reporting strategies to suit long‑term asset monitoring.
- GNSS positioning via an integrated receiver offering high sensitivity and accurate fixes for reliable location visibility.
- Internal motion detection (3‑axis accelerometer) for wake‑up and movement reporting.
- Built‑in light and tamper sensors to detect removal, interference or enclosure breaches.
- IP67 rated enclosure for waterproof and dust resistant outdoor operation.
- Flexible data transport using TCP, UDP and SMS, and support for @Track protocol commands for scheduled and event reports.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a GL530MG is configured to report to Plaspy, device messages are received and parsed to present location and event data in dashboards and reports. Plaspy interprets position updates, motion and tamper events, battery state and environmental readings to create actionable alerts and historical traces.

- Real‑time location updates and historical tracks displayed on Plaspy maps for asset visibility.
- Motion and tamper events generate alerts and timeline entries for anti‑theft and incident workflows.
- Battery and low‑power reporting help schedule maintenance and predict replacement windows.
- Scheduled and wake‑up reports provide predictable telemetry for inventory and long‑standby assets.
- Temperature and internal environmental readings reported to Plaspy can be used for condition monitoring and alerting where available.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold‑chain pallet and container monitoring where long battery life and environmental telemetry are required.
- Remote trailer and cargo tracking using magnetic or clip‑on mounting for reusable asset visibility.
- Static high‑value asset monitoring such as generators or equipment with tamper detection and geo‑fence awareness.
- Yard and lot management for parked trailers, containers and stored inventory with scheduled reporting.
- Stolen asset recovery workflows that use motion detection and continuous tracking to locate items.
