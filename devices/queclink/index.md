# QuecLink

QuecLink produces a range of GPS tracking devices commonly used across fleet management, asset tracking, and vehicle telematics. Many QuecLink models are compatible with Plaspy, allowing organizations to centralize location data, telemetry, and alerts in a single management platform. This page explains how QuecLink devices work with Plaspy, what to expect from device capabilities in general terms, and how to find the right tracker for your needs.

## How QuecLink devices work with Plaspy

QuecLink devices that are compatible with Plaspy transmit location and telemetry data over cellular networks to Plaspy servers. Plaspy ingests this incoming data and makes it available through live maps, historical tracks, alerts, and reporting tools. Device configuration and telemetry are accessed from Plaspy so you can manage device settings, view status, and receive event notifications without switching platforms.

Integration highlights to expect when using QuecLink devices with Plaspy
- Real time and periodic location updates displayed on Plaspy maps
- Telemetry and event forwarding into Plaspy for rule based alerts
- Centralized device health and connectivity status in the Plaspy dashboard
- Device configuration workflows available from the Plaspy interface where applicable

Note that device specific setup steps and advanced configuration are documented on the individual device pages and in Plaspy support materials.

## Common features often found in QuecLink trackers

While specific features vary by model, GPS tracking devices from QuecLink commonly include capabilities you will want to consider for Plaspy integration:
- GNSS based location reporting for real time and historical tracking
- Cellular data connectivity for remote reporting and remote management
- Configurable reporting intervals and power management options
- Digital and analog inputs for sensors and external triggers
- Motion detection and basic event reporting such as ignition or tamper events

For model specific feature sets and exact supported interfaces, consult the device page linked in the catalog.
