# QuecLink - GT300

![GT300](./logo.jpg)

The QuecLink GT300 is a powerful GSM/GPS safety phone designed with the needs of seniors and lone workers in mind. It features a built-in GPS receiver with superior sensitivity and fast time to first fix, ensuring accurate and reliable location tracking. With quad band GPRS/GSM support for 850/900/1800/1900 MHz, the GT300 offers seamless connectivity in various regions.

One of the standout features of the GT300 is its integration with the @Track protocol, which provides a wide range of reporting options. Users can receive emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, low battery notifications, scheduled GPS positions with cell-ID reporting, and even a man down alert. The device also supports inbound SMS display and has a configurable 20 number address book, making it easy to stay connected with loved ones or emergency contacts.

The GT300 is equipped with an internal u-blox chipset, ensuring reliable and accurate GPS tracking. It also boasts a white OLED display with a resolution of 128\*96, providing clear and easy-to-read information. The device is designed with power conservation in mind, featuring a low power consumption and long standby time with its internal battery. Additionally, the GT300 has an internal 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection and power conservation, including a man down alert feature for added safety.

With its internal GSM/GPS antennas and CE certification, the QuecLink GT300 offers a reliable and user-friendly solution for seniors and lone workers who require a reliable safety phone with advanced GPS tracking capabilities.
