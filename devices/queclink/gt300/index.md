# QuecLink - GT300

<div className="device-logo">
![GT300](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The QuecLink GT300 is a compact GSM/GPS safety phone designed for seniors and lone workers who need dependable location monitoring and simple emergency communications. It combines a sensitive internal GPS receiver based on a u‑blox chipset, quad band GPRS/GSM connectivity, an easy to read white OLED display, and built in motion detection to provide accessible and continuous tracking in a small form factor.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GT300 can feed location and alert information into a centralized tracking platform for visibility and operational oversight. Its support for reporting options such as emergency alerts, geo fence events, scheduled position reports, and man down notifications makes it a practical choice where remote monitoring, incident awareness, and timely response are important.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built safety phone for seniors and lone workers with GPS tracking capabilities
- u‑blox based GPS receiver for reliable position fixes and consistent location updates
- Quad band GPRS/GSM support for broad regional connectivity
- Integrated reporting options including emergency alert, geo fence, scheduled position, and man down notifications
- Internal 3 axis accelerometer for motion detection and power conservation
- Clear white OLED display with a compact address book for easy contact access
- CE certified with internal antennas for a streamlined user experience

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GT300 delivers position data and event reports into the Plaspy platform where they can be visualized, archived, and acted upon. Plaspy ingests the device reports and makes them available alongside other fleet or device data so operators can monitor status and trends.

- Centralized location display on Plaspy maps for individual units and groups
- Event and alert handling for emergency calls, geo fence crossings, and man down signals
- Scheduled position reporting consolidated into Plaspy for historical review and route analysis
- Configurable notifications and operational alerts routed through Plaspy for rapid awareness
- Reporting and export options in Plaspy for operational oversight and audit trails

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for seniors living independently
- Lone worker protection for staff working in remote or isolated environments
- Vulnerable user tracking where quick emergency notification is required
- Mobile asset or small device tracking where compact form and simple alerts are needed
- Situations requiring a straightforward safety phone that doubles as a GPS tracker
