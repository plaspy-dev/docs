# QuecLink - GV55W Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the QuecLink GV55W and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for cloud reporting, alerts, and fleet analytics. It is focused on practical, non sensitive information about what the tracker can do when used with Plaspy and which user workflows the device commonly supports.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and regional device variants. For device specific configuration, firmware behavior and the latest technical details, consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from QuecLink.

## Feature Overview

The GV55W is a compact WCDMA/3G vehicle tracker optimized for discreet installation in light vehicles. It combines an integrated u‑blox GNSS receiver, internal antennas, multi band cellular connectivity and a small standby battery to deliver continuous positioning and common telematics events for fleet workflows.

- Compact covert form factor with internal antennas for discreet fitment in light vehicles.
- Integrated u‑blox GNSS for high sensitivity and stated sub 2.5 m CEP position accuracy.
- Multi band 3G and GSM cellular connectivity with TCP, UDP and SMS transport for reporting into cloud platforms.
- Digital inputs and outputs including ignition detection, panic input and a latched output suitable for remote immobilizer control.
- Crash and driving behavior event detection, scheduled reporting, internal geo fences and jamming detection for anti theft and analytics use cases.

## Core Features of QuecLink - GV55W

- Integrated u‑blox All in One GNSS receiver providing high sensitivity positioning.
- WCDMA/3G and GSM/GPRS multi band cellular support with TCP, UDP and SMS data transport.
- Digital inputs for ignition and panic reporting plus digital outputs including a latched output for immobilizer or remote control.
- Crash detection and driving behavior monitoring such as harsh braking and acceleration events.
- Scheduled reporting options for time, distance and mileage along with internal geo fence support.
- Jamming detection and alarms to assist anti theft workflows.
- Compact dimensions and internal Li‑Polymer backup battery for standby operation during power loss.
- Mini USB interface for firmware updates and device diagnostics; LED indicators for status feedback.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, GV55W telematics and event messages are received by the platform, normalized and presented for live monitoring, playback and rule based alerts. Plaspy automatically detects many supported tracker protocols and ingests position and event data for operational use.

- Real time location updates and historical playback available in Plaspy dashboards for continuous tracking.
- Ignition status and digital input events such as panic or door signals appear as event markers for trip segmentation and driver behavior review.
- Crash and driving behavior events feed into incident timelines to support reconstruction and fleet safety analytics.
- Latched output control and remote immobilizer actions can be triggered from Plaspy when secure control channels are configured.
- Geo fence and scheduled reporting events are mapped to Plaspy rules to generate alerts and automated workflows.
- Data can arrive via TCP, UDP or SMS to Plaspy endpoints for flexibility in connectivity and regional network conditions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle monitoring for car rental, leasing and repossession workflows.
- Usage based insurance programs using driving behavior and crash telemetry for scoring and analysis.
- Stolen vehicle recovery and anti theft response leveraging jamming detection, geo fences and remote immobilizer outputs.
- Buy here pay here and finance fleet management where discreet tracking and event alerts assist enforcement and recovery.
- Light fleet operations for small delivery, service and sales vehicles requiring live tracking, scheduled reports and event alarms.
