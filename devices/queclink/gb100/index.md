# QuecLink - GB100

![GB100](./tracker.png)

The GB100 is a vehicle mounted GPS tracker from an established telematics device family. Designed for large scale deployments, it delivers real time location and high resolution behavior data while minimizing installation complexity. The unit houses internal GNSS and GSM antennas inside a waterproof IP65 enclosure and a compact form factor so it can reliably report position and event data even when installed under a metal hood.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GB100 streams position, speed and accelerometer events into Plaspy for live dashboards, alerts and analytics. Its flexible communications and on device buffering help ensure dependable data delivery to Plaspy for fleet monitoring, usage based insurance programs and automotive financing workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker optimized for telematics rollouts, usage based insurance and automotive financing.
- Waterproof IP65 enclosure and compact dimensions for discreet under hood or hidden vehicle installation.
- High sensitivity GNSS performance and accurate position reporting suitable for real time monitoring.
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer with shock data and pre post event traces for driver behavior and incident analysis.
- Flexible communications options and robust on device message buffering to protect data during network outages.
- Internal backup power and wide operating voltage range for broad vehicle compatibility.
- Designed to reduce installation costs and ongoing support overhead for large scale fleet projects.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GB100 supplies continuous position and event telemetry so operators can monitor vehicles in near real time and run analytics on historical behavior. Plaspy ingests the device data to populate live maps, trigger alerts, and feed reporting and scoring systems for fleets and insurance programs.

- Real time location and telemetry updates with configurable reporting intervals while driving.
- Driver behavior telemetry including harsh braking, rapid acceleration and shock packages for analytics and scoring.
- Power and battery status reporting with alerts that help detect tampering or power loss events.
- Geofence and speed alerts configurable on the device and actionable within Plaspy workflows.
- Offline message buffering so queued telemetry is delivered to Plaspy when connectivity is restored.

## Typical Use Cases

- Large scale UBI deployments where high frequency GNSS and accelerometer data feeds scoring algorithms.
- Fleet management and route oversight with live tracking, speed monitoring and geofence alerts.
- Automotive financing and asset monitoring to detect power loss and help manage repossession risk.
- Incident reconstruction and driver coaching using pre and post event traces from shock data.
- Anti theft detection and recovery workflows using timely location updates and power status alerts.

The GB100 is a practical choice for organizations that need dependable vehicle telemetry at scale while keeping installation and support costs low. Its compact waterproof design, internal antennas and event buffering combine to provide the consistent position and behavioral data that Plaspy uses for live monitoring, alerts and historical analysis. For fleets and programs where reliable data delivery and driver behavior insight are central, the GB100 offers a balanced blend of features and deployment efficiency.
