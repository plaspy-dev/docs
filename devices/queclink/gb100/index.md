# QuecLink - GB100

![GB100](./tracker.png)

The GB100 vehicle-mounted GPS tracker from an established telematics device family is Plaspy compatible and engineered to lower installation costs for large-scale telematics projects such as Usage‑Based Insurance \(UBI\), automotive financing, and fleet management. With an internal GNSS/GSM antenna set inside a waterproof IP65 enclosure and a compact form factor, the GB100 reliably delivers real-time tracking and high‑resolution behavior data even when installed under a metal hood.

The GB100 combines robust telemetry \(position, speed, accelerometer events\) with long message buffering and flexible communications \(TCP/UDP/SMS\). That makes it an ideal GPS tracker for fleets and insurance programs that require accurate location, driver behavior monitoring, and dependable data delivery to Plaspy for live dashboards, alerts, and analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for telematics rollouts, UBI and automotive financing.
- Waterproof IP65 enclosure and compact size \(91.5 × 51.5 × 11 mm\) for hidden under‑hood installations.
- High-sensitivity u‑blox All‑in‑One GNSS receiver with position accuracy &lt;2.5 m CEP and fast TTFF \(~1 s hot start\).
- Quad‑band GSM \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) with GPRS multi‑slot class 10 and TCP/UDP/SMS communication options for real‑time tracking to Plaspy.
- Built‑in 3‑axis accelerometer \(100 Hz\) with shock‑data packages and pre/post‑event traces for driver behavior and accident reconstruction.
- Internal rechargeable NiMH backup battery \(200 mAh\) plus wide operating voltage \(8–32 V DC\) for broad vehicle compatibility.
- On‑device buffering of up to 10,000 messages to protect data integrity during temporary network outages.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GB100 streams position and telemetry in near real time and supplies the event data needed for dashboards, alerts, and long‑term analytics. Communications can be routed over TCP or UDP for continuous telemetry or via SMS for fall‑back messages, ensuring Plaspy receives critical updates whether the device is on‑network or in a coverage gap.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates \(configurable reporting intervals down to 1 second while driving\).
- Driver behavior telemetry: harsh braking, rapid acceleration, and shock‑data packages from the 3‑axis accelerometer for Plaspy analytics and UBI scoring.
- External power and internal battery status reporting and alerts to detect tampering or power loss.
- Geofence and speed alerts \(up to 20 circular geofences configurable on the device\) for fleet management and anti‑theft monitoring via Plaspy workflows.
- Robust offline buffering \(up to 10,000 messages\) so Plaspy receives queued telemetry when the connection is restored.

## Use Cases

- Large‑scale UBI deployments: high‑frequency GNSS and accelerometer data feed Plaspy scoring algorithms for usage‑based insurance programs.
- Fleet management and route oversight: real‑time tracking, speed alerts and geofence monitoring for logistics and service fleets.
- Asset financing and repossession risk reduction: continuous telemetry plus power‑loss alerts help monitor financed vehicles.
- Accident reconstruction and driver coaching: shock‑data packages and pre/post‑event traces supply evidence for incident analysis and driver training.
- Anti‑theft detection and alerting: location tracking and external power/battery alerts provide the telemetry Plaspy needs to initiate recovery workflows.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GB100 was purpose‑built for cost‑sensitive telematics rollouts that require dependable, high‑quality data. Its waterproof, compact enclosure and internal antennas simplify hidden installations that reduce fitment time and material costs. With u‑blox GNSS performance, a high‑frequency accelerometer and staunch message buffering, the GB100 provides the consistent location and behavioral telemetry Plaspy needs for real‑time tracking, fleet management analytics, UBI scoring and anti‑theft workflows.

Choosing a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker like the GB100 means faster integration, reliable data flow and lower ongoing support overhead for large programs. Its flexible communications \(TCP/UDP/SMS\), power‑loss monitoring and built‑in event capabilities reduce the need for extensive hardware customization while delivering the telemetry and alerts that drive actionable insights in Plaspy — from live maps to historical reports and behavior‑based policies.
