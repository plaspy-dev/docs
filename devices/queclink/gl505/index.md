# QuecLink - GL505

<div className="device-logo">
![GL505](./tracker.png)
</div>

The GL505 is a compact QuecLink GPS tracker designed for long duration, low maintenance monitoring of livestock and remote outdoor assets. Built for pastoral and remote environments, the GL505 combines high precision GNSS location with configurable geo fences and motion based inactivity alerts in a weatherproof, tamper friendly enclosure sized to fit inside animal collars.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GL505 delivers reliable location and event data into your Plaspy platform so you can maintain situational awareness across herds and assets. Its emphasis on extended standby life and rugged construction makes it a natural fit for Plaspy users who need continuous visibility with minimal field maintenance.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy to deliver location and alert data into a centralized monitoring platform
- Exceptional standby life with up to five years when configured for very low reporting frequency
- Rugged, weatherproof IPX7 enclosure suitable for dust, mud and prolonged outdoor exposure
- High precision GNSS positioning with movement history to support location tracking over time
- Configurable geo fences to trigger alerts when animals enter or leave predefined zones
- Motion sensor with inactivity detection to notify caretakers of prolonged immobility
- Tamper friendly design that can be installed and concealed within animal collars

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GL505 feeds location and event telemetry into the platform so you can view live positions, receive alerts, and retain historical movement records for analysis and recovery workflows. Plaspy uses those inputs to provide centralized visibility and configurable notifications that fit agricultural and remote asset needs.

- Real time location updates and history playback inside the Plaspy dashboard for live monitoring
- Geo fence events and alerts to inform managers of perimeter breaches or entry to restricted areas
- Motion based inactivity alarms to help detect potential welfare issues or lost animals
- Tamper and placement notifications to reduce theft risk and device loss through discreet mounting
- Data formatted for integration into Plaspy reporting and operational workflows for audit and recovery

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and rapid recovery for cattle, sheep and goats using geo fence alerts and location history
- Welfare monitoring where motion based alerts identify animals that have stopped moving
- Remote herd management to reduce on the ground checks and automate perimeter monitoring
- Pasture rotation and movement analysis to inform grazing plans and resource allocation
- Security and monitoring of remote equipment or static rural assets where long battery life matters

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GL505 is purpose built for environments where devices must survive harsh conditions and operate for long periods without frequent battery replacement. Paired with Plaspy, the GL505 turns precise GNSS and motion events into actionable insights that support theft response, welfare monitoring, and routine herd management.

If you want a compact, low maintenance tracker that feeds location and alerts into a centralized management platform, the GL505 is a practical option to consider for agricultural and remote asset deployments.
