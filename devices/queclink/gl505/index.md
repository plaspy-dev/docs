# QuecLink - GL505

![GL505](./tracker.png)

The GL505 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for long-duration, low-maintenance livestock monitoring and outdoor asset security. Made for pastoral and remote environments, the battery-powered GL505 delivers high-precision GNSS location, configurable geo-fences and motion-based inactivity alerts — all packaged in a weatherproof, tamper-friendly enclosure that fits inside animal collars.

Optimized for integration with Plaspy, the GL505 brings reliable real-time tracking and automated telemetry into herd and asset workflows. Its extreme standby life and rugged IPX7 construction make it ideal for deployments where frequent battery changes are impractical and where owners need continuous situational awareness to prevent theft and speed recovery.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: integrates location and alerts directly into your Plaspy dashboard for centralized monitoring.
- Exceptional battery life — up to 5 years standby when configured to report once per day, minimizing field maintenance.
- Rugged, weatherproof design \(IPX7\) built to withstand dust, mud, humidity and prolonged sunlight in pastoral settings.
- High-precision GNSS positioning with movement history for accurate animal location and tracking over time.
- Custom geo-fence support to trigger alerts when animals enter or leave predefined areas.
- Motion sensor with inactivity detection to notify owners if an animal stops moving for a set period.
- Discrete, tamper-resistant installation — can be screwed into and concealed inside an animal collar.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GL505 streams location and event data into the Plaspy platform so you can view real-time positions, receive configurable alerts, and produce movement reports. Plaspy-compatible integration enables easy setup of geo-fence rules, inactivity thresholds, and alert destinations \(SMS, email, or app notifications\) while keeping historical telemetry for audit and recovery purposes.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and history playback.
- Custom geo-fence events to notify managers when animals leave grazing areas or enter restricted zones.
- Motion-based inactivity alarms for early detection of injury, illness, or stuck animals.
- Tamper-resistant mounting notifications and discreet placement inside collars to reduce theft risk and device loss.
- Integration-ready data format for Plaspy to combine with other telemetry sources and operational workflows.

## Use Cases

- Anti-theft recovery for cattle, sheep and goats — geo-fence breach alerts and location history help locate animals quickly.
- Inactivity and welfare monitoring — motion sensor alerts identify animals that have stopped moving or may be injured.
- Remote herd management — reduce manpower by automating perimeter monitoring and movement reporting across large grazing areas.
- Pasture rotation and grazing analytics — track movement patterns and density to inform grazing decisions and resource allocation.
- Asset security for remote equipment or static rural assets where long battery life and weatherproofing are essential.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GL505 is built for the realities of pastoral operations: long unattended deployments, harsh conditions, and the need for discreet, reliable location data. When used with Plaspy, it becomes a practical GPS tracker for agricultural operations that require both real-time tracking and low total cost of ownership. The combination of up to five years of standby, IPX7-rated durability and high-precision GNSS ensures dependable telemetry without frequent maintenance.

Plaspy compatibility adds centralized visibility and configurable alerting so you can turn raw GNSS and motion data into operational decisions — from theft response to herd health monitoring. While Plaspy also supports broader telemetry types used in fleet management \(for example, fuel monitoring, ignition status, immobilizer and Bluetooth sensors in other devices\), the GL505 focuses on accurate location, geo-fencing and inactivity detection optimized for livestock and rural assets. This focus makes the GL505 an efficient, purpose-built choice for agricultural tracking integrated into your Plaspy workflow.
