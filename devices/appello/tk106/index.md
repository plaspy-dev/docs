# Appello - TK106

<div className="device-logo">
![TK106](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Appello TK106 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for reliable location monitoring across a range of applications. It supports GSM and GPRS connectivity across multiple frequency bands and is built around an ARM CPU with the New Star NS-1315 GPS module, offering position accuracy reported to be within approximately 5 meters. With small dimensions and a lightweight form factor, the TK106 is suitable where discreet placement and portability are important.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TK106 can provide continuous location data and basic device status to Plaspy's tracking platform. Its multi band cellular compatibility and integrated GPS make it well suited for integration into fleet and asset monitoring workflows inside Plaspy, where visibility, historical tracks, and operational oversight are central to daily use.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor at roughly 64 x 46 x 17 mm for flexible placement  
- GSM GPRS network compatibility across multiple frequency bands for broad coverage  
- New Star NS-1315 GPS chip delivering location accuracy reported to about 5 meters  
- ARM CPU for responsive device operation and tracking performance  
- Built in 3.7V 850 mAh battery providing extended standby time for periodic reporting  
- Durable environmental range with wide operating and storage temperature specifications and humidity resistant casing

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK106 supplies location and connectivity information that Plaspy uses to present live positions, historical routes, and basic device state for fleet and asset oversight. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and makes it available through mapping, alerts, and reporting tools to support operational decisions.

- Real time location visibility on Plaspy maps for individual devices and grouped fleets  
- Historical track playback and exportable position history for route analysis  
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on changes in device reporting or location events  
- Device status monitoring within Plaspy to help detect offline devices or low reporting frequency  
- Reporting and summaries that combine TK106 position data with Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle location and route monitoring for small fleets or individual vehicles  
- Tracking portable assets that benefit from a compact, easy to conceal device  
- Periodic check ins for field personnel or mobile workers where discreet tracking is needed  
- Rental equipment location visibility and basic usage oversight  
- Remote monitoring in environments that require a device with a wide temperature range

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK106's compact size and multi band cellular compatibility make it a practical option for organizations that need a small but capable tracker that works with a centralized platform like Plaspy. Its GPS accuracy and long standby battery behavior align with common fleet and asset tracking needs where periodic updates and reliable positioning matter.

Because the TK106 is compatible with Plaspy, it can be added to mixed device fleets and managed alongside other compatible trackers inside the Plaspy platform. This allows operators to leverage Plaspy's mapping, alerting, and reporting features while taking advantage of the TK106's small footprint and environmental tolerance.
