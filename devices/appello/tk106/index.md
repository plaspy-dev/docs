# Appello - TK106

![TK106](./logo.jpg)

The Appello TK106 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker that offers reliable tracking capabilities for a variety of applications. With its GSM/GPRS network compatibility and support for multiple frequency bands \(900/1800/1900Mhz- 850/900/1800/1900Mhz\), this tracker ensures seamless connectivity and accurate location tracking. Powered by an ARM CPU and equipped with the New Star NS-1315 GPS chip, the TK106 delivers exceptional performance and precise GPS accuracy of up to 5 meters.

Measuring at just 64 x 46 x 17mm, the TK106 is incredibly compact and lightweight, making it easy to conceal and install in various settings. It comes with a 3.7V 850mAh Li-ion battery that provides a standby time of up to 100 hours, ensuring long-lasting tracking capabilities. The tracker also features a wall charger with 110-220V input and 5V output, allowing for convenient and efficient charging.

With a wide operating temperature range of -20°C to +55°C and a storage temperature range of -40°C to +85°C, the TK106 is designed to withstand harsh environmental conditions. It is also equipped with a humidity-resistant casing, with a non-condensing range of 5% to 95%, ensuring reliable performance in various weather conditions.

#### Key Features:

- GSM/GPRS network compatibility
- Support for multiple frequency bands
- ARM CPU for enhanced performance
- New Star NS-1315 GPS chip for accurate tracking
- Compact and lightweight design
- 3.7V 850mAh Li-ion battery for long standby time
- Wide operating temperature range
- Humidity-resistant casing

Overall, the Appello TK106 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers accurate tracking capabilities in a compact and durable design. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or loved ones, the TK106 is an excellent choice for ensuring peace of mind and real-time location information.
