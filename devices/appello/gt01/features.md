# Appello - GT01 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the Appello GT01 feature set and how those features are used when the device reports into Plaspy. It summarizes the practical capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy and clarifies how the tracker’s design supports common tracking scenarios.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device level details such as firmware change logs, configuration commands, or the latest spec sheet, consult the manufacturer documentation linked at the end of this page.

## Feature Overview

The Appello GT01 is a compact, lightweight GPS tracker designed for flexible tracking of vehicles, assets, and people. Its combination of GPS sensitivity, multi band GSM support, and a sealed IP67 enclosure make it suited to many outdoor and mobile deployments where reliable position fixes and network coverage matter.

- Compact form factor and low weight for discreet installation and easy mounting
- Quad band GSM module for broad regional network compatibility
- High sensitivity GPS receiver for improved location acquisition in challenging environments
- Built in rechargeable battery for portable operation and short term off vehicle use
- IP67 rated enclosure for protection against dust and temporary water immersion
- Compatible with Plaspy for centralized reporting and visibility

## Core Features of Appello - GT01

- Compact dimensions and light weight for flexible placement and minimal intrusion
- Quad band GSM support based on MTK series frequencies for broad cellular coverage
- GPS module using a high sensitivity receiver to improve fix reliability
- Built in rechargeable battery specified at 3.7 V 350 mA for portable operation
- IP67 waterproof rating to support outdoor and rugged use
- Designed for tracking vehicles, portable assets, and people where permitted
- Compatibility with Plaspy for server based tracking and monitoring

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the GT01 is connected to Plaspy, its core capabilities enable reliable location visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and accepts device reports so teams can monitor position and status from a single platform.

- Device reports are received and shown in Plaspy as position updates and status entries
- Broad GSM band support helps maintain connectivity across regions, improving continuity of reports in Plaspy
- High sensitivity GPS contributes to more frequent and accurate position fixes visible on Plaspy maps
- Portable battery operation allows tracking during temporary deployments and shows device activity in Plaspy
- Waterproof enclosure helps ensure continuous operation in outdoor conditions and reduces downtime shown in platform histories
- Plaspy accepts tracker connections to the platform endpoint and auto detects protocol for quick onboarding

Note: Plaspy accepts common tracker connections and will detect the active protocol to integrate the GT01 into the platform. Configuration and activation steps can vary by installer and firmware.

## Typical Use Cases

- Asset tracking for equipment that is moved between sites or vehicles
- Temporary portable tracking when items or people need short term monitoring
- Outdoor equipment and field tools that require rugged, water resistant tracking
- Short term rentals and loaned asset visibility during transit and use
