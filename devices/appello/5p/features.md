# Appello - 5P Features

This page provides a public overview of the Appello 5P tracker and how its documented capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, user facing feature set and explains how the tracker can report location and status into Plaspy for monitoring and operational use.

Exact feature availability may vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional product variants, and how the device is installed or configured. For definitive device specifications and the most current details please consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Appello 5P is a compact, lightweight GPS tracker intended for personal and asset tracking where discreet mounting and long battery life are priorities. It combines GNSS positioning with 2G mobile connectivity and device-level features intended to support long-term location visibility.

- Small form factor and low weight for discreet attachment and easy portability
- GNSS positioning using a U‑Blox MAX 7 module for GPS and GLONASS location fixes
- 2G GPRS connectivity via a SIMCOM SIM800C module for network reporting
- Internal rechargeable battery with manufacturer rated battery life up to 140 hours
- Cloud upgrade capability to receive firmware updates and improvements
- Buzzer for local audible notifications and a design compatible with followit Wi Fi networks though the device itself does not include built in Wi Fi

## Core Features of Appello - 5P

- Compact housing sized for easy mounting or discreet carry
- Durable APS+PC enclosure designed for everyday wear and tear
- U‑Blox MAX 7 GNSS receiver for position fixes using GPS and GLONASS
- SIMCOM SIM800C 2G GPRS modem for mobile network reporting
- Built in rechargeable battery with advertised endurance up to 140 hours under typical conditions
- Cloud upgrade support to facilitate firmware updates from the manufacturer or service provider
- Integrated buzzer for audible local alerts
- High reported GPS sensitivity supporting reliable reception in typical outdoor conditions

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Appello 5P can contribute real time and historical location visibility and device status to help teams monitor assets and people. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and accepts device reports, enabling integration without manual protocol selection in most cases.

- Location updates from the tracker are shown on the Plaspy map for live monitoring and historical playback when the device reports positions
- Device online and offline status is visible in Plaspy based on incoming connections and reported telemetry
- Battery condition and runtime are surfaced in Plaspy when the tracker reports battery information, supporting operational planning
- Plaspy supports devices communicating over UDP or TCP to the platform and will automatically detect the tracker protocol for integration
- Cloud upgrade capability is a device feature; Plaspy provides visibility into device connectivity that can help coordinate firmware updates and maintenance with the manufacturer or service provider

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal tracking for individuals and equipment that require discreet, portable devices
- Asset tracking for tools, luggage, or compact property in transit or storage
- Short and long term monitoring where small size and extended battery life are important
- Fleet support for non powered assets that need occasional location reporting
- Field staff equipment tracking to confirm presence and movement of work tools

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware revisions and manufacturer updates can change behavior, available reports, and power consumption characteristics
- Hardware revisions and regional variants of the 5P may differ in supported mobile bands or regulatory approvals
- The device uses 2G GPRS network connectivity; local cellular network availability affects reporting and service
- The followit Wi Fi compatibility noted by the manufacturer does not imply built in Wi Fi hardware in the tracker
- Battery life depends on reporting frequency, GNSS fix behavior, and environmental conditions; field results will vary

Pairing the Appello 5P with Plaspy helps organizations gain straightforward visibility into location and device status without extensive device configuration. Plaspy’s automatic protocol detection and device management features reduce setup friction so teams can focus on monitoring, alerts, and operational workflows.
