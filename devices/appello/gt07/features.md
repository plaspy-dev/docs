# Appello - GT07 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Appello GT07 GPS tracker and how its capabilities map to monitoring and fleet workflows in Plaspy. It summarizes the GT07's practical, user facing attributes for motorcycle tracking and explains what you can expect when using the device with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. The descriptions below use the GT07 manufacturer information as the primary source and focus on user oriented capabilities that are relevant when the device is connected to Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The Appello GT07 is designed as a compact, durable tracker for motorcycles with real time location tracking and broad vehicle voltage compatibility. It is intended to provide continuous position updates suitable for live monitoring and operational oversight when paired with a compatible platform such as Plaspy.

- Real time tracking for continuous visibility of motorcycle location and movement.
- Compact form factor and durable construction suitable for motorcycle installation.
- Wide working voltage range making it compatible with a variety of motorcycle electrical systems.
- GPS accuracy within approximately 5 meters for practical location precision.
- Manufacturer backed 12 month warranty for standard product support.

## Core Features of Appello - GT07

- Real time position reporting designed for live location monitoring.
- Compact and durable design intended for motorcycle mounting and daily use.
- Wide working voltage range of 6 to 78 V for broad vehicle compatibility.
- GPS position accuracy specified to within about 5 meters under normal conditions.
- 12 month warranty period provided by the manufacturer.
- Compatibility with Plaspy for integration into centralized tracking and monitoring.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the GT07 is connected and configured to report into Plaspy, its location and operational status become visible through the platform to support monitoring and oversight.

- Real time position updates are displayed on Plaspy maps for live tracking of motorcycles.
- Reported GPS accuracy is visible so operators can assess location precision in context.
- Location history and recent movement can be reviewed in Plaspy to reconstruct trips.
- Device connectivity and recent update timestamps help indicate reporting health inside Plaspy.
- Grouping and organization in Plaspy allow multiple GT07 units to be managed as a fleet or assets list.

## Typical Use Cases

- Theft deterrence and recovery support through live location visibility for motorcycles.
- Fleet oversight for businesses operating multiple motorcycles, such as couriers and delivery services.
- Rental and sharing services that need to track and locate individual motorcycles in real time.
- Personal motorcycle owners who want continuous location monitoring for peace of mind.
- Operational monitoring of motorcycle movements for route management and dispatching.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions can change device behavior and available reporting options; check the installed firmware on each unit.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may alter supported features or electrical characteristics.
- Installation method and wiring can affect which electrical ranges and signals are available to the tracker.
- Manufacturer warranty terms and support channels can vary by vendor and region; confirm specifics at the manufacturer site.

Combining the Appello GT07 with Plaspy gives organizations and individual users a centralized way to view real time location data, review recent movement history, and maintain operational awareness across one or many motorcycles. Plaspy presents device position, timing, and basic status information so teams can use that data for monitoring, dispatch, or asset management without needing to parse raw device reports.
