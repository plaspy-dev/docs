# Appello - OBD

<div className="device-logo">
![OBD](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Appello OBD is a compact GPS tracker designed for vehicle monitoring and fleet management. It combines a high sensitivity U Blox 7020 GPS chipset with a GSM communication module and support for four band GSM and TCP communication, delivering fast position fixes and steady connectivity. The tracker reports quick hot start times and brief warm starts, while operating across a wide temperature and humidity range, making it suitable for a variety of vehicle environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Appello OBD can be integrated into Plaspy to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its small form factor and OBD interface make it a practical option for automotive deployments, and its low standby current and standard 12V requirement help minimize impact on vehicle power. When used with Plaspy, the device can contribute to real time tracking, fleet monitoring, and reporting workflows that support business operations.

## Key Highlights

- Precise location tracking enabled by the U Blox 7020 GPS chipset with high sensitivity
- Reliable GSM connectivity with four band support for broad network reach
- Very fast position acquisition with average hot start around one second
- Durable operation across wide temperature and humidity ranges for varied climates
- Low standby current and standard 12V supply for energy conscious vehicle use
- Compact design and OBD interface for straightforward vehicle integration

When paired with Plaspy, the Appello OBD provides continuous position updates and status information that feed into Plaspy's tracking and fleet management tools. Plaspy ingests device data for visualization, alerting, and reporting so operators can supervise vehicles from a single platform.
