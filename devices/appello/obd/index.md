# Appello - OBD

![OBD](./logo.jpg)

The Appello OBD GPS tracker is a compact and powerful device designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring for vehicles. With its GSM module MTK program and support for GSM 4 Band and TCP protocol, this tracker ensures reliable and efficient communication. The GPS chipset U-BLOX 7020 offers high sensitivity, with a rating of -162dB, allowing for accurate and precise location tracking.

This OBD tracker is incredibly fast, with a hot start time of just 1 second on average, ensuring that you can quickly get up and running with your tracking activities. Even in warm start situations, the tracker takes only around 30 seconds to initialize, making it ideal for situations where time is of the essence.

The Appello OBD tracker is built to withstand various environmental conditions, with an operating temperature range of -20°C to 65°C. It is also designed to handle high humidity levels, with a non-condensing humidity range of 5% to 95%. This ensures that the tracker can perform reliably in a wide range of climates and weather conditions.

With a voltage requirement of 12V and an average current of less than 84mA when in standby mode, this tracker is energy-efficient and won't drain your vehicle's battery. Its compact size and OBD interface make it easy to install and use, making it an excellent choice for fleet management, vehicle tracking, and other applications where real-time monitoring is essential.

Outstanding features of the Appello OBD GPS tracker include:
- GSM module MTK program for reliable communication
- Support for GSM 4 Band and TCP protocol
- GPS chipset U-BLOX 7020 for accurate and precise location tracking
- Fast hot start time of 1 second on average
- Warm start time of around 30 seconds
- Wide operating temperature range of -20°C to 65°C
- High humidity tolerance of 5% to 95% non-condensing
- Energy-efficient with a voltage requirement of 12V and average current of less than 84mA in standby mode

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the Appello OBD GPS tracker is an excellent choice for individuals and businesses looking to track and monitor their vehicles in real-time.
