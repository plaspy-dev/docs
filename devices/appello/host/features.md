# Appello - Host Features

This page provides a public feature overview for using the Appello Host tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the manufacturer supplied description and highlights the practical capabilities you can expect to see when the device is integrated with Plaspy for tracking, alerts, and operational visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation choices, and the manufacturer's implementation. Where the device reports specific status or events, Plaspy reflects that information; for final, device specific details always consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Appello Host is presented by the manufacturer as a compact pet GPS tracker designed for outdoor use with a separate power base that acts as a control center. Relevant capabilities focus on reliable positioning for small and medium pets, extended standby life, and simple collar attachment paired with a rechargeable power base.

- Compact collar mount unit for easy attachment and daily wear
- Extended standby time reported by the manufacturer up to 12 months under optimal conditions
- Waterproof design intended for outdoor use and exposure to the elements
- Wireless charging through a dedicated power base with a high capacity battery
- Power base functions as a local control center and supports remote management
- Proximity alert when the pet moves out of the power base range, reported as an event

## Core Features of Appello - Host

- Collar mount GPS unit sized for small and medium pets
- Manufacturer stated battery standby time up to 12 months under ideal use
- Waterproof construction for outdoor positioning
- Wireless charging capability using a dedicated power base
- Power base includes an internal high capacity battery and acts as a control hub
- Proximity based alert when device leaves the approximate power base range
- Remote management features via the power base as described by the manufacturer

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Host's public feature set is reflected in platform visibility and event handling to support monitoring and operational oversight.

- Location updates from the device appear on Plaspy maps and in the device timeline when the tracker reports position
- Long standby characteristics reduce the frequency of maintenance events shown in Plaspy for battery checks when the device reports battery status
- Out of range or proximity alerts generated by the power base can be represented as events or notifications inside Plaspy
- Power base status and remote management signals are shown in Plaspy when the device transmits that information
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and will present available status and events that the device transmits

## Typical Use Cases

- Outdoor pet monitoring for small and medium sized dogs and cats
- Home base monitoring where the power base defines a safe radius and alerts on separation
- Low maintenance tracking scenarios that benefit from long standby between charges
- Caretaker oversight for pets that require remote visibility of location and alerting
- Situations where a wireless charging base simplifies daily charging and device management

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specified standby times are manufacturer stated maximums and depend heavily on usage patterns and configuration
- Some features are affected by firmware level or hardware revision and may differ between units
- The behavior and range of the power base, including the approximate 20 meter alert range described by the manufacturer, can vary with installation and environment
- Regional or model variants may alter available functions or wireless charging behavior
- Always confirm device capabilities, firmware updates, and the latest specifications from the manufacturer documentation
