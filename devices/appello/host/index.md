# Appello - Host

![Host](./logo.jpg)

Introducing the Appello 4P Pet GPS Tracker by Appello. Designed with the safety and well-being of your pets in mind, this tracker offers a range of features that will give you peace of mind and save you from unnecessary trouble. With its compact size, long battery life, and waterproof design, the Appello 4P is perfect for outdoor positioning for medium and small-sized pets.

One of the standout features of the Appello 4P is its impressive standby time. Unlike other pet trackers on the market that require frequent charging, the Appello 4P is equipped with a large-capacity battery that can last up to 12 months without needing to be charged. This means you can rest assured that your pet is always being tracked, without the hassle of constantly monitoring battery levels.

The Appello 4P consists of a small GPS unit that can be easily attached to any pet collar, and a power base with a built-in 6000mAh lithium battery. The power base not only charges the GPS unit wirelessly but also acts as a control center, allowing you to manage the device remotely. When your pet goes out of the range of the power base, which is approximately 20 meters, you will receive an alert on your phone, ensuring that you are always aware of your pet's whereabouts.

With the Appello 4P Pet GPS Tracker, you can say goodbye to the worries and fears that come with traditional pet trackers. Enjoy the security and happiness that comes with knowing your pet is safe and sound, all while saving time and effort. Invest in the Appello 4P and make your pet's safety a top priority.

### Outstanding Features:

- Compact size for easy attachment to any pet collar
- Long battery life of up to 12 months
- Waterproof design for outdoor use
- Wireless charging and remote management
- Automatic alert when pet goes out of range
