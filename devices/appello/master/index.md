# Appello - Master

![Master](./logo.jpg)

The Appello Master GPS tracker is a reliable and versatile device designed to provide accurate location tracking and monitoring. With its compact size of 85.00X78X20mm and lightweight design weighing only 93g, it is easy to install and conceal, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.

Equipped with a powerful ARM 7 CPU and the advanced New Star NS-1315 GPS chip, the Appello Master offers exceptional GPS sensitivity of -159dBm and an accuracy of 5m. This ensures precise and reliable tracking, even in challenging environments. The device operates on the GSM/GPRS network, with support for bands 850/900/1800/1900Mhz, providing wide coverage and reliable communication.

The Appello Master GPS tracker features a QUECTEL M35 M35-02-NCH-STD M35AR01A10 GSM/GPRS module, which enables seamless connectivity and efficient data transmission. It has a 12-24V input and 5V output, allowing for easy integration with various vehicles and equipment. The built-in 3.7V 240mAh Li-ion battery provides up to 48 hours of standby time, ensuring continuous tracking and monitoring.

With a wide operating temperature range of -20°C to +55°C and a storage temperature range of -40°C to +85°C, the Appello Master is built to withstand harsh environmental conditions. It is also designed to be humidity-resistant, with a non-condensing range of 5% to 95%. These features make it suitable for use in a variety of industries, including fleet management, asset tracking, personal tracking, and more.
