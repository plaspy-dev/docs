# Appello - TK103 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive feature summary of the Appello TK103 and how its capabilities map to monitoring and tracking on the Plaspy platform. It is intended to help users and technical evaluators understand the practical functions of the TK103 when used for vehicle and asset tracking with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary between firmware releases, hardware revisions, installation choices, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a general guide and consult the device documentation for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The Appello TK103 is a compact GPS tracker designed for real time location monitoring across GSM networks. The device combines a capable GPS receiver and onboard processing with a small form factor and battery options suitable for temporary or concealed installations.

- Compact size and light weight for discreet installation in vehicles and assets
- Fast and accurate positioning with New Star NS-1315 GPS chip and quoted 5 m accuracy
- GSM GPRS connectivity across common bands for real time reporting
- Built in 3.7 V 800 mAh battery offering extended standby up to roughly 48 hours
- Wide operating temperature range for use in varied climates and conditions

## Core Features of Appello - TK103

- New Star NS-1315 GPS chip for reliable position fixes
- ARM 7 class CPU for on device processing and responsive tracking
- Quoted GPS accuracy of approximately 5 meters under typical reception
- GSM GPRS support with multi band compatibility for broad network reach
- GPS sensitivity specification to support signal reception in challenging locations
- Internal 3.7 V 800 mAh Li ion battery with extended standby capability
- Compact physical dimensions and low weight for flexible installation
- Support for 12 V to 24 V input and a 5 V output for integration with vehicle power

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and status updates from compatible trackers and presents them in dashboards, maps, and reports for operational visibility. The TK103’s positioning and network features translate directly into location awareness, historical tracking, and monitoring inside Plaspy.

- Real time position updates appear on Plaspy maps and live tracking views
- Battery powered operation allows temporary or covert installations while Plaspy shows activity windows and offline status
- Multi band GSM compatibility helps maintain connectivity so Plaspy receives regular location reports
- On device positioning accuracy contributes to better map precision and route reconstruction in Plaspy
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and accepts reporting from supported devices for easy onboarding

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time vehicle location monitoring for cars and small trucks
- Motorcycle or personal vehicle tracking where compact size and concealment matter
- Short term asset tracking where internal battery operation is required
- Route visibility and historic position review for operational oversight
- Remote monitoring of assets that transit through mixed coverage areas
