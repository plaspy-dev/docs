# Appello - TK103

![TK103](./logo.jpg)

The Appello TK103 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for accurate real time location monitoring of vehicles and portable assets. It combines a small form factor and light weight with positioning hardware that supports consistent location fixes. The device is reported to use a New Star NS 1315 GPS chip and an ARM 7 class processor, with positioning accuracy around 5 meters and a high sensitivity that helps maintain reception in difficult environments.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the TK103 can feed live location and device status into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its support for GSM GPRS networks, standby battery capability, and broad operating temperature range make it suitable for a range of field deployments where Plaspy is used to track units, generate alerts, and produce operational reports.

## Key Highlights

- Compact dimensions and low weight for discreet placement in vehicles or portable assets
- Precise positioning with the New Star NS 1315 GPS chip and typical accuracy around 5 meters
- ARM 7 based processing for reliable position updates and device responsiveness
- GSM GPRS connectivity for real time tracking integration with platforms like Plaspy
- Up to 48 hours of standby operation from the onboard battery for intermittent use
- Wide operating temperature range to suit diverse environmental conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK103 provides location and basic device status that Plaspy can present alongside other fleet and asset data. Plaspy receives periodic updates from the tracker and applies platform features to improve operational visibility and management.

- Live location display for individual units and grouped fleets
- Historical route playback and event timeline for post trip review
- Alerts and notifications based on movement, status change, or predefined rules
- Consolidated reporting for vehicle utilization, trip summaries, and operational trends
- Status indicators for device connectivity and power state to help manage device health

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal car and motorcycle tracking for theft recovery and location monitoring
- Small fleet tracking for light commercial vehicles and service fleets
- Asset protection for portable equipment and high value items in transit
- Rental vehicle monitoring to record usage and location history
- Operational oversight where occasional battery powered tracking is required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK103 is a practical option for organizations and individuals who need a discrete tracker that supplies reliable position updates. Its compact size, reported positioning accuracy, and compatibility with GSM based networks make it a sensible match for Plaspy users who want straightforward location visibility without complex hardware requirements.

While the TK103 provides core tracking functionality that integrates well with Plaspy's monitoring, reporting, and alerting features, organizations should match the tracker capabilities to their operational needs. For many light vehicle and asset tracking scenarios the TK103 offers a balanced combination of portability, accuracy, and battery backed operation.
