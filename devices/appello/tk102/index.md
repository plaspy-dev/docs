# Appello - TK102

![TK102](./logo.jpg)

The Appello TK102 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for reliable location tracking across a range of applications. Built around an STC CPU and the New Star NS-1315 GPS chip, the TK102 delivers high GPS sensitivity and positioning accuracy, while a small form factor and lightweight design make it easy to deploy where discreet tracking is needed. The device supports GSM GPRS communications on common frequency bands and includes a rechargeable battery that provides extended standby time for longer operations.

As a device that is compatible with Plaspy, the TK102 can feed positional data into Plaspy's tracking platform to support monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight. Its combination of accurate positioning, compact size, and extended standby life makes it a practical option for organisations that want to integrate a straightforward tracker into Plaspy for vehicle, asset, or personnel tracking tasks without complex hardware demands.

## Key Highlights

- High GPS sensitivity and typical accuracy around 5 meters for reliable position fixes
- Compatible with GSM GPRS bands common to many regions for cellular connectivity
- Compact dimensions and light weight for discreet placement and easy portability
- Rechargeable 3.7V 850mAh battery offering up to 48 hours standby for prolonged use
- Robust environmental tolerances including wide operating and storage temperature ranges
- Supplied wall charger for convenient recharging across common mains voltages

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TK102 can provide continuous location updates that Plaspy ingests to present live and historical tracking data in a unified platform. Plaspy uses these positional feeds to give teams visibility into asset and vehicle movements and to support routine reporting and alerting workflows.

- Live position display and breadcrumb trails for movement history
- Geofence and location based alerts configured in Plaspy to notify operators
- Aggregated reporting for route analysis and operational review
- Centralised device management views to monitor tracker status and battery level
- Integration of TK102 location data into fleet dashboards for oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Basic vehicle tracking for small fleets or single asset monitoring
- Portable asset tracking where compact size and concealment are beneficial
- Short to medium term deployments that require simple GPS reporting
- Personnel location monitoring when discreet, rechargeable trackers are needed
- Situations where tolerance to a wide range of temperatures is required

The Appello TK102 offers a practical balance of compact design, solid GPS performance, and extended standby time that fits many routine tracking needs. For organisations using Plaspy, the TK102 provides a straightforward data source that can be incorporated into live maps, alerts, and reports without complex integration work.
