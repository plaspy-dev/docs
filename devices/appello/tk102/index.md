# Appello - TK102

![TK102](./logo.jpg)

The Appello TK102 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker that offers reliable tracking capabilities for a variety of applications. With its GSM/GPRS network compatibility and support for the 900/1800/1900Mhz bands, this tracker ensures seamless communication and accurate positioning. Powered by an STC CPU and equipped with the New Star NS-1315 GPS chip, the TK102 delivers exceptional GPS sensitivity and accuracy, with a sensitivity of -159dBm and an accuracy of 5 meters.

Featuring a 3.7V 850mAh Li-ion battery, the TK102 offers a standby time of up to 48 hours, making it ideal for long-term tracking operations. Its compact size of 64 x 46 x 17mm and lightweight design of 50g make it easy to conceal and carry, ensuring discreet tracking. The tracker also comes with a wall charger that supports 110-220V input and provides a 5V output, allowing for convenient and hassle-free charging.

With a wide operating temperature range of -20°C to +55°C and a storage temperature range of -40°C to +85°C, the TK102 is built to withstand various environmental conditions. It is also designed to handle high humidity levels, with a non-condensing humidity range of 5% to 95%. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or even individuals, the Appello TK102 is a reliable and efficient GPS tracker that delivers accurate positioning and long-lasting performance.

### Outstanding Features:

- GSM/GPRS network compatibility
- Support for 900/1800/1900Mhz bands
- STC CPU for reliable performance
- New Star NS-1315 GPS chip for exceptional sensitivity and accuracy
- 3.7V 850mAh Li-ion battery for up to 48 hours of standby time
- Compact size and lightweight design for easy concealment and portability
- Wide operating temperature range of -20°C to +55°C
- Storage temperature range of -40°C to +85°C
- Non-condensing humidity range of 5% to 95%
