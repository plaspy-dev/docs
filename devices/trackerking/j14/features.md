# TrackerKing - J14 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the TrackerKing J14 feature set as it is used with Plaspy. It describes the practical capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy dashboards and reports when the J14 is deployed for continuous vehicle tracking and event monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. Where details are unspecified by the device description, this page stays deliberately high level and recommends checking manufacturer documentation for device specific items.

## Feature Overview

The TrackerKing J14 is a compact hardwired vehicle tracker built for persistent on vehicle installation. It combines dual GNSS positioning with a rugged enclosure and a wide operating voltage to deliver continuous position fixes and event reporting useful for fleet management, recovery and anti theft workflows.

- Dual GNSS positioning with GPS plus BDS for faster and more reliable fixes in many environments
- Rugged IP65 enclosure for dust and water resistance suitable for exposed mounting locations
- Wide 9–90 V DC input range to support cars, light trucks and heavy vehicles without extra converters
- More than ten alarm mechanisms including geofence, overspeed, movement and power cut to drive timely alerts
- Small discreet form factor for concealed, hardwired installation and persistent connectivity
- Event driven telemetry and historical route data to support playback and investigations

## Core Features of TrackerKing - J14

- Dual GNSS positioning GPS plus BDS for improved fix speed and redundancy
- Rugged IP65 rated housing for durable on vehicle use
- Wide operating voltage range 9 to 90 V DC for broad vehicle compatibility
- Comprehensive alarm suite with 10 plus mechanisms including geofence breach, overspeed, movement and power cut alerts
- Compact, low profile hardwired form factor for discreet mounting and continuous monitoring
- Event reporting that supports historical route playback and time stamped logs
- Designed for continuous, on vehicle installation for persistent telemetry

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the J14 supplies position fixes and event messages that Plaspy ingests to provide live visibility and historical records. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and maps incoming location and alarm data into dashboards and reporting functions for operational use.

- Live map updates and position tracking in Plaspy based on J14 GNSS fixes
- Alarm and event forwarding so geofence, overspeed, movement and power cut conditions trigger Plaspy alerts and workflows
- Time stamped event logs and route playback for incident review and compliance reporting
- Centralized fleet view allowing operators to manage many J14 units and monitor states from a single Plaspy account
- Correlation of J14 events with other signals or vehicle metadata available in Plaspy to enrich telematics analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for logistics and delivery operations requiring continuous vehicle location and route history
- Vehicle finance recovery where discreet, hardwired installation and event alerts support recovery efforts
- Anti theft protection for commercial vehicles using movement and power cut alarms to detect tampering
- Long term asset tracking on heavy equipment and trailers where wide voltage support reduces installation complexity
- Security and operations teams using historical playback to investigate incidents or verify routes
