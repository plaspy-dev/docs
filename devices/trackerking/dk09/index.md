# TrackerKing - DK09

<div className="device-logo">
![DK09](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The DK09 is a compact 4G GPS tracker built for discreet vehicle anti theft and continuous location monitoring. Packaged to resemble a conventional relay, the unit is intended for covert installation in cars, motorcycles, and a range of equipment. It provides continuous positioning, event alerts, and historical route playback to support recovery and operational visibility.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the DK09 feeds position and event data into the Plaspy platform so fleet managers and vehicle owners can access live tracking, alarm notifications, and route history from a central interface. Its support for wide input voltage, an internal backup battery with power loss alarm, and remote engine or oil cut off control make it a practical choice for mixed fleets and heavy equipment when paired with Plaspy monitoring and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real time tracking and history playback
- Relay style form factor designed for discreet installation in cars and motorcycles
- Remote engine and oil cut off capability to support rapid anti theft response
- Comprehensive alarm suite including movement, vibration, overspeed, geo fence, and ACC ignition detection
- Wide input voltage range 9–90V with internal backup battery and power failure alarm
- Mileage statistics and route logging to support fleet reporting and operational review

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the DK09 transmits location and event data so operators can monitor assets in real time, respond to alarms, and review historical movements from Plaspy dashboards. Plaspy aggregates the tracker telemetry and presents it alongside alerts and reports, enabling centralized oversight across vehicle types.

- Real time location updates and position history available on Plaspy for continuous visibility
- Ignition ACC status and relay control accessible through Plaspy to support immobilizer actions
- Geo fence, overspeed, movement, and vibration alerts routed to Plaspy for fast notification and response
- Historical route playback and mileage statistics surfaced for incident review and fleet reporting
- Centralized alarm routing and status monitoring to help coordinate recovery and operational decisions

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, vans, and mixed vehicle fleets that need location and mileage tracking
- Vehicle anti theft deployments where covert installation and remote immobilizer control assist recovery
- Motorcycle tracking where a small relay style footprint enables discreet mounting while maintaining real time monitoring
- Heavy equipment and machinery monitoring that benefit from wide voltage tolerance and power failure alerts
- Rental and shared vehicle programs seeking route history and alarm driven oversight
