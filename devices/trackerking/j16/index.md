# TrackerKing - J16

![J16](./tracker.jpg)

The J16 is a wired vehicle GPS tracker engineered for dependable real time tracking and professional anti theft protection. Built around a Quectel cellular module and supporting 4G with automatic fallback to 2G, the J16 provides broad network coverage for live telemetry, route history, mileage reporting and remote immobilization. It is purpose built for secure installation within a vehicle wiring harness and designed to integrate with third party telematics platforms.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the J16 can report location, status and alarm events directly to the Plaspy platform using standard telematics protocols. That compatibility means fleet operators and vehicle owners can use Plaspy to monitor live positions, replay routes, track mileage and receive theft or tamper alerts while retaining the J16's professional anti theft capabilities and wired relay control for immobilization.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible out of the box with support for GT06, JT808 and Tianqin protocols for straightforward server integration.
- Reliable cellular connectivity with 4G Cat 1 and automatic fallback to 2G for persistent tracking in mixed coverage areas.
- Professional anti theft features including remote engine and fuel cut off, vibration alarm, geofence alarm and overspeed alerts.
- Wide input voltage range and internal backup battery with power failure alarm to maintain reporting across vehicle types.
- Blind area retransmission that caches GPS and odometer data when offline and uploads history when coverage is restored.
- Built for fleet management with odometer and mileage statistics plus history route playback for operational reporting.
- Wired plug and play form factor intended for installation inside vehicle harness to reduce tampering and support relay control.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the J16 with Plaspy is straightforward because the device implements widely used telematics protocols and a reliable cellular modem. When the device is configured to report to Plaspy, it streams location and status updates at configurable intervals so teams can monitor vehicles in real time, replay routes and receive alerts for incidents.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and fleet dashboards.
- Ignition detection and virtual ignition reporting to support utilization metrics and trip segmentation.
- Remote immobilizer support allowing Plaspy to issue engine or fuel cut off commands when authorized.
- Blind area retransmission where cached GPS and odometer data uploads to Plaspy after coverage is restored.
- Alarm events such as vibration, geofence, overspeed and power failure pushed to Plaspy for fast response and audit logging.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch where ongoing location, mileage and route history improve utilization and maintenance planning.
- Anti theft and recovery operations using vibration and geofence alarms combined with remote immobilizer control.
- Rental car monitoring to validate usage, mileage and to immobilize vehicles in cases of contract violation.
- Logistics and delivery operations that need preserved route history and power failure alerts for tamper detection.
- Mixed coverage or regional operations that benefit from fallback cellular connectivity and cached data retransmission.
