# TrackerKing - DK05

![DK05](./tracker.jpg)

The DK05 is a compact smart pet GPS tracker designed for reliable, real-time collar tracking and now fully Plaspy compatible. Tailored for dogs and cats, the DK05 combines wide cellular coverage \(4G + 2G Cat‑1, quad‑band GSM\) with an IP67 waterproof housing and a 1000 mAh rechargeable battery, so owners get continuous location updates, movement alerts, and remote voice monitoring via the Plaspy platform.

Easy to mount on a collar or harness, the DK05 delivers practical telemetry for pet safety without extra bulk. When integrated with Plaspy, you get centralized real‑time tracking, configurable geo‑fence alarms, low battery warnings, and historical location data — all managed from Plaspy’s dashboard and mobile apps.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: integrates with Plaspy for real‑time tracking, alerts, and history.
- Compact, lightweight design \(≈7.5 × 2.9 × 1.7 cm; ≈39.5 g\) made specifically for collars and harnesses.
- Wide cellular coverage with 4G + 2G Cat‑1 and quad‑band GSM for broad network availability.
- IP67 waterproof rating to withstand wet outdoor conditions and active pets.
- 1000 mAh rechargeable battery with an app‑adjustable power‑saving mode for extended operation.
- Comprehensive safety alerts: geo‑fence breaches, movement and vibration alarms, and low battery warnings.
- Remote voice monitoring for real‑time audio check‑ins through the Plaspy app.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the DK05 streams GPS position and telemetry to the Plaspy platform over cellular networks. Plaspy receives location updates and event data, then converts that information into live map tracking, push/SMS/email alerts, and historical reports. Integration requires adding the DK05 device profile to your Plaspy account and configuring update intervals and alert preferences in the Plaspy dashboard.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and history playback.
- Movement detection and vibration alarms trigger immediate Plaspy notifications for abnormal activity.
- Geo‑fence creation and breach alerts managed through Plaspy for safe‑zone monitoring.
- Low battery warnings sent to Plaspy so owners receive proactive recharge alerts.
- Voice monitoring \(remote audio listening\) accessible from the Plaspy app for a quick audio check of a pet’s environment.
- Note: DK05 is optimized for pet telemetry and does not include features typically used in vehicle fleet management such as ignition sensing, immobilizer control, or fuel monitoring.
