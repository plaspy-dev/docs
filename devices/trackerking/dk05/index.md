# TrackerKing - DK05

<div className="device-logo">
![DK05](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The DK05 is a compact smart pet GPS tracker designed specifically for collar and harness mounting. Built to provide reliable real time location updates, the device is targeted at dog and cat tracking and prioritizes continuous visibility of a pet's position. Its feature set includes broad cellular coverage, a waterproof housing rated for outdoor use, and a rechargeable battery to support ongoing tracking sessions and timely alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the DK05 integrates its telemetry into the Plaspy platform so owners and managers can monitor pets from a centralized dashboard and mobile apps. Plaspy receives location updates, movement events, and status notifications from the DK05 and turns that data into live map views, configurable geofence alerts, low battery warnings, and historical playback for recovery and analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible pet GPS tracker for real time location updates and alerting.
- Compact lightweight form factor designed for collar and harness use.
- Broad cellular connectivity options to support wide network availability.
- IP67 rated waterproof housing suitable for outdoor and wet conditions.
- 1000 mAh rechargeable battery with app controlled power saving for extended operation.
- Safety alerts including geofence breaches, movement and vibration alarms, and low battery warnings.
- Remote voice monitoring for quick audio check ins through the Plaspy platform.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the DK05 is added to a Plaspy account, the device transmits position and event data to Plaspy where that information is presented as live tracking, notifications, and historical records. Plaspy consolidates updates from multiple DK05 units to give owners or teams a single view for monitoring, alerts, and basic reporting.

- Live location updates and history playback available through the Plaspy map and timeline.
- Movement and vibration events generate immediate Plaspy notifications for unusual activity.
- Geofences configured in Plaspy produce entry and exit alerts for safe zone monitoring.
- Low battery notifications are forwarded to Plaspy so users can receive proactive recharge reminders.
- Remote voice monitoring is accessible from Plaspy for on demand audio checks of a pet environment.
- Note that DK05 is optimized for pet telemetry and does not include vehicle specific features such as ignition sensing or immobilizer control.

## Typical Use Cases

- Daily location monitoring of dogs and cats to ensure pet safety and owner peace of mind.
- Water resistant tracking for pets that swim or are active in rainy conditions.
- Geofence protection to notify owners when a pet leaves a defined safe area.
- Lost pet recovery using live tracking combined with historical route playback in Plaspy.
- Remote wellbeing checks using movement alerts and voice monitoring for quick situational awareness.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The DK05 pairs pet focused design with centralized monitoring through Plaspy, making it a practical choice for owners and small teams who need straightforward, reliable tracking. Its compact size and waterproof construction reduce interference with normal pet activity while providing core telemetry and safety alerts that Plaspy can present and manage.
