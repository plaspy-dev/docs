# TrackerKing - DK19 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TrackerKing DK19 and how it can be used with Plaspy for vehicle tracking, fleet oversight, and anti theft workflows. It summarizes the DK19's practical capabilities that matter to Plaspy users and explains how common telemetry and event data are reflected inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary depending on device firmware, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer's implementation. The notes on this page are intended as general public guidance; consult manufacturer documentation and your installer for device specific behavior and the latest firmware capabilities.

## Feature Overview

The DK19 is a compact 4G vehicle tracker designed to provide continuous location and status reporting for cars, motorcycles and other powered vehicles, with emphasis on anti theft operations and fleet monitoring. It supports standard tracking protocols for quick integration and includes vehicle-focused inputs and alarms that translate to useful events inside Plaspy.

- Real time GPS location reporting for live map tracking and route playback.
- Vehicle status telemetry including ACC ignition detection and mileage statistics.
- Remote engine and fuel cut off capability for anti theft immobilization workflows.
- Multi mode cellular connectivity with 4G Cat 1 core, optional Cat M and NB IoT, and fallback to 2G for wider coverage.
- Blind area retransmission to fill gaps and improve continuity in low signal environments.
- Compact covert form factor and wide operating voltage for versatile vehicle installations.

## Core Features of TrackerKing - DK19

- Plaspy compatible out of the box with support for common tracking protocols such as GT06, CRX3, JT808 and Tianqin.
- Real time GPS tracking and historical route playback for operational review.
- ACC ignition detection to report engine on off state and support driver behavior analysis.
- Remote immobilizer support including engine and fuel cut off control for anti theft response.
- Blind area retransmission to reduce lost points in tunnels, garages and dense urban areas.
- Built in alarms including vibration alarm, geo fence breach alerts and overspeed alarm.
- Mileage statistics and odometer reporting for usage tracking and basic fleet metrics.
- Wide operating voltage range to support installation across cars, motorcycles and similar vehicles.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the DK19's standard telemetry and event packets to provide centralized visibility, alerting and reporting. Once a DK19 device is onboarded, users can monitor location, status and alarms from a single Plaspy dashboard and build event driven rules that use DK19 inputs.

- Live map tracking and dashboard widgets show current position and movement in real time.
- Historical route playback presents past trips and mileage summaries for auditing and reporting.
- Ignition status and mileage data appear as telemetry fields that can be used in reports and alerts.
- Anti theft commands such as remote immobilizer or fuel cut off can be executed from Plaspy where supported and allowed by local installation.
- Alarms and retransmitted points from blind area retransmission are shown alongside live data to improve situational awareness.
- Protocol compatibility ensures Plaspy can parse standard packets and display device alarms and statistics without custom format work.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft response and rapid immobilization after unauthorized vehicle movement.
- Service vehicle tracking and route verification for maintenance, delivery or field service teams.
- Driver behavior monitoring using ignition events and overspeed alarms to improve safety.
- Covert asset protection where compact installation and blind area retransmission are important.
- Mileage based reporting and simple usage analytics for billing or maintenance planning.
- Motorcycle tracking and monitoring where wide input voltage and small form factor matter.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware and hardware revisions may enable or change specific functions listed here.
- Some features depend on the chosen protocol and local network availability, with optional Cat M and NB IoT features varying by regional cellular support.
- Installation choices and vehicle wiring determine whether ACC detection and remote cut off functions are available.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and presents standard telemetry fields in the platform for compatible devices.
- Check the tracker firmware version and manufacturer release notes to confirm availability of alarms, retransmission behavior and immobilizer commands.
