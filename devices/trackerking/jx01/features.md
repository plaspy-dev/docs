# TrackerKing - JX01 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TrackerKing JX01 and how it integrates with Plaspy for real time location reporting, alarms, and remote configuration. It is written to help fleet managers, caregivers, and administrators understand the practical capabilities they can expect when using the JX01 with Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. The descriptions below are grounded in the JX01 public product profile and emphasize how the device typically operates when paired with Plaspy, while recommending verification against manufacturer documentation for model specific details.

## Feature Overview

The JX01 is a compact, rechargeable personal tracker designed for anti loss and personal protection scenarios. When used with Plaspy it delivers location updates and a set of real time alarms that appear inside Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and historical review.

- Compact, portable form factor intended for handheld use or temporary attachment to luggage, pets, or portable assets
- Rechargeable battery designed for extended standby and portable operation without hardwiring
- Real time location reporting for mapping and movement visibility inside Plaspy
- Multiple alarm types including movement alarm, vibration alarm, and geofence alerts to support theft prevention and rapid recovery
- Voice monitoring capability for remote listening events to provide situational context during incidents

## Core Features of TrackerKing - JX01

- Portable personal GPS tracking with built in GNSS location reporting
- Rechargeable battery for non wired deployments and longer standby operation
- Movement alarm and vibration alarm for unauthorized motion detection
- Geofence alert support to notify entry and exit events
- Voice monitoring remote listening to assist situational awareness in emergencies
- Remote configuration via companion app to change reporting frequency and power modes
- Power saving and low power modes configurable to balance update frequency and battery life
- Designed for plug and play use without vehicle hardwiring

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a JX01 is registered to Plaspy, its location reports and alarm events are ingested and presented in the platform so operators can view live position, receive alerts, and maintain historical telemetry for incident review.

- Location pings from the JX01 appear on Plaspy maps to show current position and movement history
- Movement and vibration alarms generate alert events in Plaspy so administrators can respond quickly
- Geofence entry and exit events are recorded and delivered as Plaspy alerts and included in reports
- Low battery telemetry and power state changes are visible in Plaspy to prompt maintenance or recharge actions
- Voice monitoring sessions are shown as events in Plaspy to provide context for incident investigation
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and reflects configuration changes made through the device companion app

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for children, caregivers, or lone workers who need discreet location monitoring
- Anti theft protection for luggage, backpacks, and other portable belongings during travel
- Temporary monitoring of portable equipment and rental assets without vehicle wiring
- Event deployments where handheld or transient tracking is required for duration of an activity
- Caregiver oversight and rapid response workflows using alerts and voice monitoring events
- Short term use for field teams or transient personnel needing portable positional awareness

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware and hardware revisions can change available functions or alarm behavior over time
- Some features depend on companion app settings and user configuration rather than being fixed device defaults
- Regional cellular variants or carrier coverage can affect connectivity and reporting frequency
- Installation method and physical placement can influence movement and vibration alarm sensitivity
