# TrackerKing - JX05

![JX05](./tracker.jpg)

The JX05 is a portable 2G GPS tracker engineered for long standby and anti-theft asset protection. Designed as a no-wiring solution with a strong magnetic housing and a 6000 mAh internal battery, the JX05 delivers dependable, long-duration real-time tracking for trailers, containers, construction equipment and other high-value unattended assets. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, it forwards location and alarm data to your Plaspy account or a custom backend using GT06 protocol \(with JT808 or Tianqin mapping options available\), enabling fast deployment and centralized monitoring.

Simple to deploy and easy to manage, the JX05 focuses on autonomy and covert mounting so sites and fleet managers can secure assets without permanent wiring. When paired with Plaspy, the device’s movement, vibration, geofence and low-battery alerts become part of an integrated telemetry stack that supports fleet management dashboards, anti-theft workflows and event-driven notifications to mobile apps or operations teams.

## Key Highlights

- Long autonomous operation: 6000 mAh internal battery for extended standby and reduced maintenance intervals.
- No-wiring installation: strong magnetic mounting for covert attachment and fast redeployment across assets.
- Real-time tracking and alarms: continuous location reporting plus vibration, movement, geofence, overspeed and low-battery alerts.
- Protocol flexibility: native GT06 support with JT808 and Tianqin options to match many server backends and integrators.
- Power-saving modes: configurable via app or platform to extend battery life for long-term asset monitoring.
- Ideal for remote or unattended equipment: designed for trailers, containers, parked vehicles and construction machinery.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The JX05 integrates with Plaspy by sending standard tracker messages over 2G using the GT06 protocol \(or JT808/Tianqin where selected\). Plaspy ingests those messages to provide real-time tracking, alarm routing and historical telemetry. Integrators can select the protocol mapping that best matches their backend to ensure smooth, reliable delivery of position and event data into Plaspy dashboards and APIs.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: periodic GPS positions streamed to Plaspy for live tracking views.
- Movement and vibration alarms: immediate events for unauthorized movement or tampering forwarded to Plaspy alerting rules.
- Geofence and overspeed events: zone-entry/exit and speed threshold notifications available in Plaspy reports.
- Low-battery notifications: battery status messages enable proactive maintenance workflows.
- Protocol options for backend compatibility: GT06 by default with JT808/Tianqin available to match server requirements.

## Use Cases

- Fleet asset tracking: monitor trailers and non-powered equipment that require long battery autonomy and occasional position reports.
- Anti-theft protection: covert magnet mount and vibration/movement alarms provide quick detection of unauthorized relocation.
- Construction site equipment: keep track of high-value machinery and tools left at remote sites without permanent wiring.
- Container and storage monitoring: geofence alerts and low-battery warnings help protect stationary assets in yards or depots.
- Short-term rentals and equipment sharing: rapid deployment and removal without wiring simplifies turnover and auditing.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the JX05 with Plaspy provides a practical balance of autonomy, simplicity and backend compatibility. The large 6000 mAh battery and configurable power-saving modes make it cost-effective for long-term asset monitoring, while the magnet mount reduces installation time and labor. Because the device supports GT06 and offers JT808/Tianqin options, integrators can reliably map messages into Plaspy or other platforms without custom firmware changes. In Plaspy, JX05 data becomes actionable: real-time tracking, alarm routing, and historical telemetry are available alongside other fleet management telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition and immobilizer events or Bluetooth sensors when those systems are present in vehicles or assets.

For operators seeking a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker focused on long standby, easy deployment and anti-theft alerts, the JX05 delivers a straightforward solution. It minimizes hands-on maintenance, accelerates instalment across a dispersed fleet of assets, and feeds the telemetry needed for effective decision-making and security workflows.
