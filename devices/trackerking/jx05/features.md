# TrackerKing - JX05 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the TrackerKing JX05 with Plaspy. It describes the practical capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy when the JX05 is deployed, and how its core functions map into centralized tracking and alerting workflows. The goal is to help fleet and site managers understand operational value without presenting device configuration steps.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and manufacturer implementation choices. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes, always consult the official TrackerKing documentation or contact the manufacturer.

## Feature Overview

The JX05 is a portable 2G GPS asset tracker designed for long standby use and covert mounting. It focuses on unattended asset protection by combining a large internal battery, strong magnetic housing for no-wiring installation, and event reporting that supports anti-theft and asset monitoring workflows.

- Long standby operation supported by a 6000 mAh internal battery for reduced maintenance visits.
- No-wiring magnetic housing for quick, covert mounting and fast redeployment across assets.
- Real-time location reporting plus movement and vibration alarms for theft and tamper detection.
- Geofence and overspeed event reporting together with low battery alerts for proactive maintenance.
- Protocol flexibility with GT06 as the default and JT808 or Tianqin mapping options for backend compatibility.

## Core Features of TrackerKing - JX05

- Portable magnet-mounted form factor for attachment to trailers, containers, machinery and parked vehicles.
- Large capacity internal battery optimized for long-term standby monitoring.
- GPS location reporting for real-time and historical position tracking.
- Movement and vibration alarms that trigger event messages when tampering or unauthorized movement is detected.
- Geofence event reporting for zone entry and exit notifications.
- Overspeed and low battery alarms to support safety monitoring and maintenance planning.
- Protocol options including GT06 with JT808 and Tianqin mapping choices to match common server backends.
- Configurable power saving modes to extend battery life during long deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, JX05 position and alarm messages are ingested into a centralized monitoring environment where they become actionable telemetry. Plaspy consolidates location history and events from the device so operations teams can respond, audit, and analyze asset behavior.

- Real-time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and situational awareness.
- Movement and vibration alarms are surfaced as alerts that can be routed to operators or notification channels.
- Geofence entry and exit events are recorded in Plaspy and can trigger rules or reports for site access monitoring.
- Low battery and other device status reports help schedule maintenance and battery replacement before service disruption.
- Protocol mapping ensures messages from GT06 or selected JT808/Tianqin modes are translated into Plaspy telemetry for consistent dashboards and histories.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking where devices must remain unattended for long periods.
- Anti theft protection for parked vehicles and non powered equipment using covert magnetic mounting.
- Construction site equipment monitoring to detect unauthorized movement or relocation.
- Yard and depot asset management with geofence and low battery alerting for inventory oversight.
- Short term rentals and shared equipment tracking where quick installation and removal reduce turnaround time.

- Firmware versions can enable or change alarm behavior, reporting intervals, and power management settings.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may affect supported network bands and physical mounting options.
- Installation approach and mounting location impact GPS reception and battery life in real deployments.
