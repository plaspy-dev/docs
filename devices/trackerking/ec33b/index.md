# TrackerKing - EC33B

<div className="device-logo">
![EC33B](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The EC33B is a compact, multi function 4G GPS tracker designed for reliable vehicle and asset monitoring. Built around a Quectel 4G Cat 1 module with automatic fallback to 2G, the EC33B provides continuous real time tracking and history route playback so fleets and individual owners can monitor location, mileage and status. Its wired form factor and broad input range make it suitable for a range of vehicle types without extra conversion hardware.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the EC33B integrates its telemetry and alarm streams into the Plaspy platform for unified visibility and control. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker’s offline data caching, ignition and mileage reporting, and remote immobilizer capabilities become components of a broader fleet monitoring and alerting workflow, helping operations teams maintain continuity of tracking and respond to incidents from the same platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for consolidated tracking, alerts and reporting.
- 4G Cat 1 connectivity with automatic 2G fallback to help preserve tracking in variable coverage areas.
- Robust offline data handling with a large cache to prevent loss of history during signal gaps.
- Wide 9–90V working range suited to cars, vans and commercial vehicles.
- ACC ignition detection and mileage reporting for operational and driver behavior insight.
- Remote engine and fuel cut off for anti theft and fleet control interventions.
- Multiple alarm inputs including vibration, geofence, overspeed and SOS with support for external microphone monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the EC33B with Plaspy allows the device to deliver location, status and alarm messages into Plaspy dashboards, maps and reports so teams can monitor assets in real time and review historical routes. The tracker communicates using common tracking protocols and can be configured using standard methods; Plaspy ingests those messages to provide alerts, playback and remote control actions.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear in Plaspy for live monitoring and route playback.
- ACC ignition and virtual ignition status feed Plaspy reports for mileage and engine on time analytics.
- Remote immobilizer control via engine and fuel cut off commands issued from Plaspy when needed for recovery or operational control.
- Event driven alerts such as vibration, geofence, overspeed and SOS are pushed into Plaspy for immediate notification and escalation.
- External battery voltage detection and power failure alerts are reported to Plaspy to support maintenance and power monitoring workflows.
- Offline cached points are retransmitted to Plaspy after coverage restoration to preserve historical accuracy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with continuous tracking, mileage reporting and ignition based summaries for cars, vans and trucks.
- Anti theft workflows using remote immobilization and event driven alerts to secure vehicles.
- Operational telemetry for compliance and safety monitoring with overspeed and geofence notifications.
- Emergency response and driver safety using SOS input and remote voice monitoring.
- Asset tracking in low coverage areas where offline data caching preserves movement history.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The EC33B is a practical option for organizations that need a compact, reliable tracker that pairs with Plaspy to provide unified fleet visibility and control. Its combination of 4G connectivity with fallback, wide voltage support and a focus on data integrity makes it suitable for mixed vehicle fleets and asset protection tasks. For operations teams, features like ACC detection, mileage reporting and remote immobilizer functions translate directly into actionable items inside Plaspy reports and alerts.
