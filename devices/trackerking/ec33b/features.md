# TrackerKing - EC33B Features

This page provides a public feature-oriented overview of the TrackerKing EC33B and how its capabilities integrate with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on the practical functions and monitoring features you can expect when using the EC33B as part of a Plaspy deployment, and how those features translate into visibility and control inside the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation wiring and accessories, or regional manufacturer variants. For device specific details such as configuration options, wiring diagrams and the latest firmware behavior consult the official TrackerKing documentation and confirm features during installation and testing.

## Feature Overview

The EC33B is a compact vehicle tracker designed for continuous location monitoring, resilience in areas with variable cellular coverage, and integration with fleet management workflows. When paired with Plaspy, the EC33B supplies live location, history playback and event data that support operational monitoring and anti theft workflows.

- Real time GPS location and history route playback for operational visibility and post trip review
- 4G connectivity with automatic fallback to 2G to maintain tracking across mixed coverage areas
- Robust offline storage that caches thousands of points to preserve history during signal gaps
- Wide 9 to 90 volt input suitable for diverse vehicle types without additional power converters
- ACC ignition detection and mileage reporting to support engine on off analytics
- Remote engine and fuel cut off for immobilization and anti theft interventions

## Core Features of TrackerKing - EC33B

- 4G Cat 1 cellular module with automatic fallback to 2G to maintain connectivity in lower coverage zones
- Support for common tracking protocols such as GT06 for straightforward platform integration
- Offline cache capable of storing up to 8,000 data points for blind area retransmission after coverage is restored
- Wide operating voltage range of 9 to 90 volts to support cars, vans and commercial vehicles
- ACC ignition detection and virtual ignition reporting for engine state and mileage calculations
- Remote immobilizer capability via remote engine and fuel cut off for anti theft and fleet control
- Multiple alarm inputs including vibration, geofence, overspeed and SOS for event driven alerts
- External microphone and SOS cable input to support remote voice monitoring and emergency response workflows

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the EC33B telemetry stream and presents it within dashboards, maps and reports so operations teams can monitor fleets, receive alerts and execute remote control actions. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding and reporting.

- Real time location and continuous telemetry appear in Plaspy for live monitoring and route playback
- Event driven alerts such as vibration, geofence entry exit, overspeed and SOS are pushed into Plaspy notifications and event logs
- ACC and virtual ignition status report into Plaspy to support engine on time, driver behavior and mileage reporting
- Offline cache retransmissions from the device are incorporated into historical tracks and reports once coverage is restored
- Remote engine and fuel cut off commands issued from Plaspy can be used for immobilization actions where supported by the installation
- Telemetry such as external battery voltage and power failure alarms surface as alerts for operational follow up

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars vans and trucks with continuous tracking and mileage reporting
- Anti theft workflows that combine real time tracking with remote immobilization
- Compliance and safety monitoring using alarm types for overspeed and geofence breaches
- Emergency response and driver safety using SOS input and remote voice monitoring
- Asset tracking in areas with intermittent coverage using the large offline cache
- Commercial vehicle installations where a wide input voltage range simplifies power wiring

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets and menu options can vary by firmware version and the specific hardware revision supplied by the manufacturer
- Installation choices and wiring determine availability of functions such as immobilizer control, ACC detection and external microphone use
- Regional device variants or carrier limitations can affect cellular bands and fallback behavior
- Protocol support and configuration may require setting the device to the correct mode for Plaspy to auto detect and ingest messages

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the EC33B with Plaspy centralizes location, event and telemetry data so teams gain operational oversight without juggling multiple systems. Plaspy’s mapping, alerts and reporting make it straightforward to monitor real time movement, review historical routes and act on events such as SOS or geofence violations, while remote control actions enable rapid responses to theft or misuse.
