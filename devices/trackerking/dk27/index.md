# TrackerKing - DK27

![DK27](./tracker.jpg)

The DK27 is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for anti-loss protection and personal asset monitoring, now fully Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your tracking workflows. With 4G + 2G Cat-1 connectivity, quad-band GSM support and a 1200 mAh rechargeable battery, the DK27 delivers dependable real-time tracking and alerting without wiring — ideal for users who need quick deployment, covert placement and simple management via a companion app or platform.

The DK27 is built for everyday convenience and safety: one-button SOS emergency alerts, voice monitoring for remote listen-in and motion/vibration alarms all work together to protect vehicles, motorcycles, pets and people. When paired with Plaspy, the DK27 brings location, motion events and geofence alerts into a single fleet management and telemetry dashboard for both personal and light commercial use.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: easy integration for real-time tracking, alerts and historical playback.
- 4G + 2G \(Cat-1\) connectivity with quad-band GSM for broad cellular coverage.
- Built-in 1200 mAh rechargeable battery for portable, no-wiring deployment.
- One-button SOS and voice monitoring for fast emergency response and remote listening.
- Vibration, movement and geofence alarms for practical anti-theft and anti-loss protection.
- Adjustable power-saving mode via app to extend standby and tailor telemetry intervals.
- Compact form factor suited to vehicles, motorcycles, pets, children, elderly and general assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the DK27 sends location updates and event data to the Plaspy platform over cellular networks so you can monitor assets in real time, configure alerts and generate reports. Plaspy ingests the device’s telemetry and exposes it through maps, dashboards and alerting rules, enabling immediate situational awareness and historical analysis.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and route playback.
- SOS button events forwarded to Plaspy for priority notifications and escalation workflows.
- Motion and vibration alarms register as events in Plaspy to trigger alerts and automated actions.
- Geofence boundary alerts appear in Plaspy so you can enforce zones and monitor compliance.
- Low-battery and power-saving status reported to Plaspy to help manage device uptime and maintenance.
- Voice monitoring events can be logged in Plaspy \(where supported by platform workflow\) to augment incident response.

## Use Cases

- Personal safety and elder/child tracking — SOS button and voice monitoring provide quick response tools for caregivers.
- Anti-theft and anti-loss for motorcycles and cars — motion and vibration alarms plus geofencing help detect unauthorized movement.
- Pet tracking — lightweight and portable attachment for collars to monitor location and movement while roaming.
- Portable asset tracking — protect tools, luggage or high-value portable equipment when wiring is not practical.
- Light commercial monitoring — simple telematics and event alerts for occasional vehicles or equipment that need minimal installation.

The DK27 is an excellent Plaspy compatible option when you need a no-wiring, low-friction GPS tracker that prioritizes safety alerts and portability over complex vehicle integrations. Its reliable 4G + 2G connectivity and battery-powered form make it fast to deploy across numerous small assets and personal use cases.
