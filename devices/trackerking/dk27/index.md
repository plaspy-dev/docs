# TrackerKing - DK27

![DK27](./tracker.jpg)

The DK27 is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for anti loss protection and personal asset monitoring. It combines cellular connectivity, a rechargeable battery, and a small form factor to enable no wiring deployment and covert placement. Core features highlighted by the manufacturer include one-button SOS alerts, voice monitoring for remote listen in, motion and vibration alarms, and geofence event support — all aimed at protecting vehicles, motorcycles, pets and people.

Because the DK27 is fully Plaspy compatible, its location updates and event signals can be integrated into Plaspy dashboards and workflows. That compatibility brings DK27 telemetry, SOS events and geofence alerts into a single fleet management view so operators and caregivers can monitor devices in real time, configure notifications, and review historical routes and events through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into tracking workflows and dashboards
- 4G plus 2G Cat 1 cellular connectivity with quad band GSM for wide coverage
- Built in 1200 mAh rechargeable battery for portable, no wiring deployment
- One button SOS and voice monitoring to support emergency response and remote listen in
- Motion and vibration alarms plus geofence events for anti theft and anti loss protection
- Compact form factor suitable for vehicles, motorcycles, pets, children, elderly and portable assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the DK27 transmits location and event data to the Plaspy platform where that telemetry is presented on maps, in reports and as configurable alerts. Plaspy ingests the device events so teams can maintain situational awareness, respond to incidents, and analyze past movements for operational insights.

- Real time location updates and route playback available in Plaspy for live monitoring
- SOS button events forwarded to Plaspy so notifications and escalation rules can be triggered
- Motion and vibration alarms recorded as events in Plaspy to initiate alerts or automated actions
- Geofence boundary alerts appear in Plaspy to help enforce zones and monitor compliance
- Low battery and power saving status visible in Plaspy to assist with device maintenance and scheduling
- Voice monitoring events can be logged in Plaspy where supported by platform workflows to augment incident records

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and caregiver monitoring for children or elderly users using SOS and voice features
- Anti theft protection for motorcycles and light vehicles with motion alarms and geofencing
- Pet tracking for collars and portable attachments to monitor roaming and location
- Portable asset protection for luggage, tools or equipment where wiring is not practical
- Light commercial tracking for occasional vehicles or equipment needing quick deployment

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The DK27 is a practical choice for organizations and individuals who need a low friction, battery powered tracker that focuses on safety alerts and portability rather than deep vehicle integration. Its compact design and rechargeable battery make it easy to deploy across many small assets or personal carries, and Plaspy brings those distributed feeds into a unified operational view for monitoring and response.

For fleet managers, caregivers and asset custodians looking for a simple GPS tracker that pairs with a robust management platform, the DK27 with Plaspy offers a balanced solution for real time location, event alerts and historical review.
