# TrackerKing - DK27 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TrackerKing DK27 and how the tracker integrates with Plaspy for real time tracking, alerting and historical playback. It summarizes practical capabilities you can expect when using the DK27 with the Plaspy platform while keeping the focus on user facing functions and operational value.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer implementation. Before deployment, confirm device capabilities and any model specific differences with the official TrackerKing documentation and release notes to ensure the DK27 meets your requirements.

## Feature Overview

The DK27 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker designed for quick deployment and portable asset or personal protection. When paired with Plaspy, the DK27 sends location and event data to a unified monitoring platform so you can view position, alarms and historical movement from a single dashboard.

- Portable no wiring form factor for covert placement and easy transfer between assets
- 4G plus 2G Cat 1 cellular connectivity and quad band GSM for broad network reach
- Built in rechargeable 1200 mAh battery with adjustable power saving for longer standby
- One button SOS emergency alerts and voice monitoring for remote listen in
- Motion and vibration alarms plus geofence event reporting for anti loss and theft detection

## Core Features of TrackerKing - DK27

- Real time GPS location reporting for live tracking and route playback
- One button SOS emergency alert to notify monitoring systems and caregivers
- Voice monitoring capability for remote audio check in where permitted
- Motion and vibration alarms to detect movement and potential tampering
- Geofence support to define zones and receive entry or exit events
- Low battery notification and power saving modes to manage device uptime
- Compact portable design for use with vehicles motorcycles pets or personal items

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the DK27 is configured to report to Plaspy, the device’s location and event messages are ingested into the platform so they appear on maps, timelines and alert feeds. Plaspy unifies telemetry from many devices so DK27 events can be correlated with other fleet or asset data.

- Live position updates appear on Plaspy maps for situational awareness and route tracking
- SOS button presses are surfaced as priority events that can trigger notifications and escalation workflows
- Motion vibration and geofence events are logged in Plaspy to drive alerts and rule based actions
- Battery and power saving status can be monitored to schedule maintenance or recharge cycles
- Historical location playback and event timelines support incident review and reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children elders or lone workers using the SOS and voice monitoring features
- Portable anti theft protection for motorcycles scooters or small vehicles with motion and geofence alerts
- Pet tracking with lightweight attachment and position reporting while roaming
- Luggage and portable asset protection where no hard wiring is feasible
- Light commercial oversight for occasional equipment or assets that require simple deployment and monitoring
- Caregiver or emergency response scenarios where fast notification and location are essential
