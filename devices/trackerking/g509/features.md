# TrackerKing - G509 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TrackerKing G509 and how the device functions when used with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing capabilities and operational value, describing the features that operators can expect to see in Plaspy dashboards and mobile views. The content is intended for planning, comparison, and operational awareness rather than step-by-step configuration.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where necessary, confirm device-specific details with TrackerKing documentation and with the installer to ensure the G509 is commissioned and configured correctly for your Plaspy deployment.

## Feature Overview

The G509 is a compact relay-style GPS tracker designed for concealed, hardwired installation in vehicles and mobile equipment. It provides continuous real-time tracking and historical route playback while supporting a set of operational and anti-theft alarms that feed into centralized monitoring platforms such as Plaspy.

- Discreet relay form factor suited for concealed mounting in commercial vehicles, passenger cars, and movable equipment.
- Continuous real-time GPS location updates and historical route playback for operational review and audits.
- Anti-theft and alarm capabilities including movement detection, vibration alarm, overspeed alerts, geofence notifications, and power-failure alarm with internal backup battery.
- Relay outputs to support remote engine or oil cutoff for immobilization when authorized and properly installed.
- Mileage statistics and trip logging to support basic reporting and operational analysis.
- Hardwired 9–36V design intended for vehicle installations requiring professional wiring.

## Core Features of TrackerKing - G509

- Relay-style housing for discreet installation and integration with vehicle relay circuits.
- Real-time GPS tracking for live location visibility in monitoring platforms.
- Historical route playback to review trips and verify routes.
- Movement detection and vibration alarm for early tampering or theft indicators.
- Overspeed alerts and geofence event reporting for operational control and compliance monitoring.
- Power-failure alarm with internal backup battery to detect tampering or electrical disruptions.
- Relay outputs that enable remote engine or oil cutoff when configured and authorized by administrators.
- Mileage and trip logging to support basic fleet reporting and analytics.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the G509 is connected to Plaspy, its location updates and alarm events are delivered into Plaspy so operators can monitor vehicles, receive alerts, and analyze historical data from a centralized interface. Plaspy displays device telemetry and events to support situational awareness and routine fleet operations.

- Live positions appear on Plaspy maps to support dispatch, tracking, and recovery workflows.
- Alarm events such as movement, vibration, overspeed, geofence breaches, and power-failure are surfaced as notifications and logged for review.
- Historical route playback and trip logs are accessible in Plaspy for auditing routes and mileage reporting.
- Mileage statistics and trip summaries help with basic consumption analysis and operational reporting inside Plaspy.
- Remote immobilizer actions supported by the device’s relay can be initiated through Plaspy where the workflow and permissions are configured and authorized.

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet anti-theft protection for high-value vehicles using concealed relay installation combined with movement and vibration alarms.
- Remote immobilization to assist in vehicle recovery or to prevent unauthorized use when authorized by company policy.
- Fleet route verification and driver monitoring via historical route playback and trip logging.
- Tamper and power monitoring to detect attempted disabling or electrical issues using the power-failure alarm and backup battery status.
- Asset tracking for movable equipment and trailers that operate on vehicle power supplies in the supported voltage range.
- Small to medium fleet operations that need economical, real-time tracking and basic telemetry reporting.

- Firmware and hardware revisions may change how specific alarms, logging, and relay behaviors operate; verify the device firmware level when planning deployments.

- Regional variants and regulatory constraints can influence available features and recommended wiring practices.
- Manufacturer documentation and wiring diagrams should be consulted for safe installation and to confirm supported behaviors.
