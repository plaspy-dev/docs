# TrackerKing - G509

![G509](./tracker.jpg)

The G509 relay GPS tracker is a purpose-built device for discreet vehicle security and fleet operations. Housed to resemble a conventional relay, the G509 delivers continuous real-time tracking and reliable historical route playback while remaining compact enough for concealed installation in commercial vehicles, passenger cars, and movable equipment. As a Plaspy compatible option, the G509 integrates with Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps to provide instant location visibility, configurable alerts, and centralized fleet telemetry for operators who need rapid situational awareness and straightforward control.

Designed with anti-theft and fleet management workflows in mind, the G509 pairs continuous GPS reporting with a set of safety and operational alarms: movement detection, overspeed, geofence notifications, vibration alarm, and a power-failure alarm backed by an internal battery. The unit’s relay capability supports remote engine and oil cutoff, enabling immobilizer-style interventions through your tracking platform when authorized. Whether you are managing a small commercial fleet or protecting high-value vehicles, the G509 offers an economical, Plaspy compatible solution for real-time tracking and operational control.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the G509 is configured to report to Plaspy, it feeds location and event data directly into the Plaspy platform so fleet managers and vehicle owners can view live positions, receive alerts, and review historical journeys. Plaspy’s mapping and reporting tools interpret the device’s GPS and alarm signals to create actionable telemetry and event logs for compliance, security, and operational analysis.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and dispatch.
- Alarm events \(movement, vibration, overspeed, geofence breaches\) pushed as instant notifications and logged for audit trails.
- Mileage statistics and trip logs available in Plaspy reports to help with basic fuel consumption analysis and route cost accounting.
- Remote immobilizer actions: Plaspy can initiate relay commands \(engine/oil cutoff\) to immobilize vehicles when configured and authorized.
- Power-failure and backup battery status reported so operators can detect tampering or electrical issues promptly.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft protection: discreet relay installation combined with vibration and movement alarms for early theft detection.
- Remote immobilization: authorized cut-off of engine or fuel \(oil\) via relay to recover vehicles or prevent unauthorized use.
- Fleet management and route auditing: real-time tracking and historical route playback to verify routes, driver behavior, and mileage.
- Vehicle tamper and power monitoring: power-failure alarm and internal battery backup to alert managers to tampering or electrical issues.
