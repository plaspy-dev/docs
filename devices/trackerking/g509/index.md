# TrackerKing - G509

![G509](./tracker.jpg)

The TrackerKing G509 is a relay style GPS tracker designed for discreet vehicle security and fleet operations. Housed to resemble a conventional relay, the G509 provides continuous real-time tracking and reliable historical route playback while remaining compact enough for concealed installation in commercial vehicles, passenger cars, and movable equipment. Its feature set focuses on anti-theft workflows and basic fleet telemetry to support situational awareness and recovery actions.

As a Plaspy compatible option, the G509 can integrate with Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps to provide instant location visibility, configurable alerts, and centralized fleet telemetry. When configured to report to Plaspy, the device’s tracking and alarm signals appear alongside other fleet data so operators can monitor live positions, review history, and take authorized immobilization actions through the platform where appropriate.

## Key Highlights

- Discreet relay form factor suited for concealed installation in vehicles and mobile equipment
- Real-time GPS tracking with historical route playback for audit and review
- Anti-theft and safety alarms including movement detection, vibration alarm, overspeed, and geofence notifications
- Relay output supports remote engine or oil cutoff for immobilizer style interventions when authorized
- Power-failure alarm with internal backup battery for tamper and power monitoring
- Plaspy compatible for centralized dashboards, alerts, and basic mileage and trip reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the G509 is configured to report to Plaspy, its location and event data flow into the Plaspy platform so fleet managers and vehicle owners can view live positions, receive alerts, and review historical journeys. Plaspy’s mapping and reporting tools interpret device events to create actionable telemetry and event logs for security, compliance, and operational analysis.

- Real-time location updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and dispatch
- Alarm events such as movement, vibration, overspeed, and geofence breaches pushed as notifications and logged for audits
- Historical route playback and trip logs available in Plaspy for route verification and driver review
- Mileage statistics and basic trip reporting surfaced in Plaspy reports for operational insight
- Remote immobilizer commands can be issued from Plaspy to the device relay when configured and authorized
- Power-failure and backup battery status reported so operators can detect tampering or electrical faults

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft protection with discreet installation and vibration or movement alerts
- Remote immobilization for vehicle recovery or to prevent unauthorized use when authorized by policy
- Route auditing and driver behavior monitoring using historical playback and overspeed alerts
- Tamper and power monitoring to detect attempts to disable tracking or electrical issues
- Asset tracking for movable equipment and trailers operating on standard vehicle voltage ranges

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G509 pairs a compact, covert form factor with a focused set of security and operational features that make it a practical choice for fleets and vehicle owners requiring discreet tracking and immobilizer functionality. Its relay capability adds a tangible theft-mitigation option while the core tracking and alarm events feed directly into Plaspy for centralized visibility.

For organizations using Plaspy, the G509 is a fit when you need dependable location reporting, basic mileage and trip logs, and integrated alarm handling in a device that can be concealed. Because the unit is intended for hardwired installation and interacts with vehicle circuits for immobilization, professional installation is recommended to ensure safe and correct configuration.
