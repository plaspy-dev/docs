# TrackerKing - G109

![G109](./tracker.jpg)

The G109 is a 4G GPS tracker built for reliable vehicle and motorcycle tracking and marketed for anti-theft and anti-loss protection. Designed for continuous real-time tracking and history route playback, the G109 delivers timely location data and critical telemetry for cars, motorcycles, and trucks. As a Plaspy compatible tracker, it integrates smoothly into Plaspy’s platform to provide live position updates, alarms, and trip history for fleet management and personal vehicle security.

The G109 combines a wide operating voltage range and an internal long-life backup battery so it continues reporting during power interruptions and supports blind-area retransmission to bridge coverage gaps. With ACC ignition detection, remote engine and fuel cut-off, vibration alarm, geo-fence and overspeed alerts, and mileage/odometer reporting, the G109 provides the core telemetry and anti-theft features modern fleet and security operators expect from a Plaspy compatible device.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real-time tracking across vehicles and motorcycles.
- Wide working voltage \(9–90V\) supports most vehicle power systems including cars, trucks, and motorcycles.
- Internal long-life backup battery with power-failure alarm keeps reporting during tampering or power loss.
- Remote engine and fuel cut-off \(immobilizer capability\) for rapid anti-theft response.
- ACC ignition detection, mileage/odometer statistics, and external battery voltage detection for complete telemetry.
- Blind-area retransmission and history route playback to recover tracks from low-coverage periods.
- Vibration, geo-fence and overspeed alarms to protect assets and enforce safety policies.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G109 feeds continuous 4G location and vehicle telemetry into the Plaspy platform so operators receive real-time tracking, event alerts, and historical reports via web and mobile dashboards. Plaspy ingests the device’s position, ignition state, alarm conditions and mileage data to enable immediate incident response and ongoing performance analysis.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates streamed over 4G for accurate tracking and mapping.
- ACC ignition status reporting to indicate vehicle on/off state and support driver behavior monitoring.
- Remote engine/fuel cut-off \(immobilizer\) control available from Plaspy when configured for anti-theft response.
- Blind-area retransmission and history route playback to reconstruct trips and offline segments.
- Alarm events \(vibration, geo-fence breach, overspeed, power-failure\) pushed to Plaspy for immediate notifications and workflows.
