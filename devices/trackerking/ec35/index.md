# TrackerKing - EC35

![EC35](./tracker.jpg)

The TrackerKing EC35 is a rugged GPS tracker designed for demanding vehicle environments. Built around a Quectel 4G Cat1 module with automatic fallback to 2G, the EC35 delivers real time location tracking, route history playback and reliable telemetry for cars, trucks and motorcycles. It offers IP68 waterproofing, a wide 9–90V input range and an internal backup battery to maintain uptime in challenging conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the EC35 can act as a full telematics endpoint for fleet and asset management. Its support for widely used tracking protocols and its range of sensor interfaces make it practical to pair with Plaspy for live maps, alerts, historical playback and remote immobilizer workflows without extensive custom integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible through support for standard tracking protocols for straightforward server integration.
- 4G Cat1 connectivity with automatic 2G fallback to help maintain tracking across varied coverage areas.
- IP68 rated enclosure and wide 9–90V input range for reliable use in cars, trucks, motorcycles and heavy equipment.
- Internal backup battery to preserve reporting during primary power loss and provide power failure alerts.
- Integrated inputs for fuel level, temperature sensors and driver ID with optional Bluetooth relay modules.
- Remote engine and fuel cut off capability for anti theft and immobilizer style control from management platforms.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the EC35 sends location, sensor telemetry and status events to Plaspy servers using industry standard tracking formats. Plaspy ingests these reports to present live locations, event driven alerts, route playback and fleet level dashboards while enabling remote actions where supported by the device.

- Real time location and telemetry streaming into Plaspy for continuous visibility across the fleet.
- Ignition and virtual ignition events reported to Plaspy for mileage tracking and usage analytics.
- Fuel level and external temperature sensor data delivered to Plaspy for consumption monitoring and cold chain visibility.
- Remote engine and fuel cut off actionable from Plaspy dashboards for theft response and immobilizer workflows.
- Driver attribution via IButton and optional Bluetooth relay presented inside Plaspy for driver level reporting and events.
- Route history playback and retrievable reports for incident review and operational analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route optimization with real time position, ignition events and historical playback.
- Anti theft protection and recovery using immobilizer control combined with geofence and alerting.
- Fuel monitoring to detect theft, inefficiency and to analyze consumption trends across vehicles.
- Cold chain and temperature sensitive cargo monitoring with external temperature sensor telemetry.
- Motorcycle and heavy equipment tracking where waterproofing and wide input voltage are required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TrackerKing EC35 is a practical option for organizations that need a durable vehicle tracker that integrates into an established telematics platform. Its combination of rugged hardware, broad input voltage tolerance and backup power helps reduce data gaps and downtime for day to day fleet operations. Sensor inputs and driver identification options extend the device beyond basic position tracking, enabling richer operational reporting inside Plaspy.
