# Castel - IDD-213N

<div className="device-logo">
![IDD-213N](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Castel IDD-213N is an intelligent on board diagnostic and all in one device built for both passenger and commercial vehicles. Its plug and play design reads diagnostic information from the vehicle ECU and captures location data, forwarding that information to a backend server for remote real time diagnostics and tracking. The unit is positioned for fleet management and vehicle monitoring scenarios where consolidated diagnostic and location visibility is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the IDD-213N can supply the platform with vehicle health and position data for monitoring, reporting, and alerting. Its support for common vehicle diagnostic standards and cellular connectivity makes it suitable for integration into Plaspy workflows that need combined operational oversight and remote diagnostic insight.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated on board diagnostics and location tracking in a single device
- Plug and play form factor to simplify deployment across passenger and commercial vehicles
- Support for 3G networks in 800 850 1700 1900 MHz bands for reliable data transmission
- Compliance with OBD II EOBD J1939 and J1708 for broad vehicle compatibility
- Ability to read diagnostic trouble codes and freeze frame data for troubleshooting
- Driving behavior and usage metrics including speed RPM mileage fuel consumption and idle detection
- SMS alarm notifications and ability to connect to a backend server via domain or IP address

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the IDD-213N can feed both location and diagnostic streams into the platform so operations teams can monitor vehicles in real time and act on alerts or maintenance signals. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it alongside fleet reports and geolocation tools for operational use.

- Live location visibility on Plaspy maps for asset tracking and route oversight
- Remote diagnostic readings and trouble code visibility to support maintenance decision making
- Configurable alerts for driving behavior incidents such as speeding hard acceleration deceleration and prolonged idling
- Aggregated reports for mileage fuel consumption and vehicle usage to support fleet reporting
- Integration via the device backend connection to Plaspy for centralized data collection and historical analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for delivery vans trucks and mixed vehicle fleets requiring combined tracking and vehicle health
- Car rental monitoring to track location usage and capture diagnostic events during rentals
- Insurance telematics programs that use driving behavior and diagnostic indicators for risk assessment
- Driving schools and training programs monitoring student performance and vehicle condition
- Service shops and maintenance providers performing remote diagnostics prior to service visits

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The IDD-213N combines diagnostic capability and location tracking in a single unit, which can reduce hardware complexity for organizations that need both vehicle health and positional data. Its support for established vehicle diagnostic standards and industrial class components makes it a practical option for fleets and commercial operations that prioritize durability and broad vehicle compatibility.

Paired with Plaspy, the device's data contributes to a unified view of fleet operations, enabling real time monitoring, alerts, and reporting that support maintenance workflows and operational decisions. While device capabilities are well suited to telematics use cases, teams should confirm that the specific diagnostic parameters and alerts they require are available from the IDD-213N within their Plaspy deployment.
