# Castel - IDD-213GL

![IDD-213GL](./logo.jpg)

The Castel IDD-213GL is an intelligent on-board diagnostic device designed for both passenger and commercial vehicles. With its plug-and-play technology, this GPS tracker can easily read diagnostic information from the vehicle's ECU and capture location data, which is then sent to a backend server for real-time remote diagnostic and tracking purposes. This device is compatible with OBD II/EOBD, J1939, and J1708 protocols, making it suitable for a wide range of vehicles.

One of the standout features of the IDD-213GL is its comprehensive data collection and analysis capabilities. It can not only read diagnostic data such as vehicle speed, RPM, and ECT, but also retrieve diagnostic trouble codes and freeze frame data. Additionally, it offers mileage and fuel consumption statistics, allowing for efficient fleet management and monitoring of vehicle performance. The device also tracks driving behavior, including speeding, hard acceleration/deceleration, and idle engine, providing valuable insights for improving driver safety and efficiency.

With its industrial-class components and high-quality construction, the IDD-213GL is built to withstand the demands of everyday use. It has a compact design, measuring 63mm x 48mm x 28mm and weighing just 50g, making it easy to install and conceal. The device operates on a wide voltage range of 9-36VDC and has a low power consumption, ensuring reliable performance without draining the vehicle's battery. It also features an internal backup battery for unplug notification, ensuring continuous operation even when the device is disconnected from the vehicle's power source.

Overall, the Castel IDD-213GL is an advanced GPS tracker that combines diagnostic capabilities with real-time tracking functionality. It is an ideal solution for fleet management, service shops, car dealers, and insurance companies, providing valuable data for optimizing vehicle performance, improving driver safety, and enhancing operational efficiency.
