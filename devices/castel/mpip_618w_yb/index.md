# Castel - MPIP-618W-YB

![MPIP-618W-YB](./logo.jpg)

The Castel MPIP-618W-YB is a versatile vehicle travelling data recorder that offers effective real-time tracking and monitoring of your vehicle. With its advanced features, it provides accurate information about the vehicle's location, speed, and various states. This GPS tracker uploads vehicle location, running speed, and alert information \(such as speeding, fatigue driving, and emergency alerts\) to a real-time monitoring center via a wireless telecommunication network.

One of the standout features of the MPIP-618W-YB is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor your vehicle's movements with precision. It supports communication methods such as GPRS and SMS, ensuring reliable and efficient data transmission. The tracker also includes a traveling record function, which provides valuable insights into the vehicle's past routes and destinations.

In terms of safety, the MPIP-618W-YB offers various alerts to keep you informed about potential issues. It can notify you of low voltage of the terminal main power supply, abnormal fuel consumption, illegal vehicle ignition, and illegal door opening. Additionally, it supports remote power or oil cut-off/supply and remote recording, giving you control over your vehicle's functions even from a distance. The SOS alert and SMS alert features provide an extra layer of security in case of emergencies.

The Castel MPIP-618W-YB is an ideal choice for a range of applications, including logistics vehicles, engineering vehicles, dangerous goods vehicles, taxi rentals, and public transportation vehicles. Its compact dimensions of 188mm\*100mm\*60mm and lightweight design of 620g make it easy to install and conceal within the vehicle. With its durable aluminum alloy protective leverage, it can withstand harsh conditions and ensure long-lasting performance.
