# Castel - MPIP-618W-YB

![MPIP-618W-YB](./logo.jpg)

The Castel MPIP-618W-YB is a vehicle travelling data recorder designed for real time tracking and monitoring. It provides continuous position and speed information along with a traveling record that archives past routes and destinations. The device uploads location, running speed, and alert information to a monitoring center using wireless telecommunications and supports communication by GPRS and SMS for data transmission.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MPIP-618W-YB can be integrated into fleet monitoring workflows to surface live location, alert events, and trip histories inside the Plaspy platform. Its alerting capabilities for events such as speeding, fatigue driving, emergency alarms, low main power, abnormal fuel consumption, illegal ignition, and door opening make it relevant for operators who need timely notifications and operational visibility through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Real time GPS tracking and traveling record to review past routes and trip details.
- Uploads location speed and alerts to a monitoring center via wireless telecom channels using GPRS and SMS.
- Built in alert suite including speeding fatigue driving SOS low main power abnormal fuel consumption illegal ignition and illegal door opening.
- Remote control features such as remote power or oil cut off and remote recording where device configuration allows.
- Compact and lightweight form factor with durable aluminum alloy protection for concealed installation and rugged use.
- Broad operating range supporting typical vehicle voltage and wide temperature tolerances for varied environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The MPIP-618W-YB sends its location updates and alert messages to a real time monitoring backend, and Plaspy can consume that information to provide mapping visualization and operational oversight. When connected to Plaspy the device data becomes part of fleet dashboards enabling centralized tracking and event handling.

- Live location and speed display on Plaspy maps for real time operational awareness.
- Alert forwarding to Plaspy so events like SOS speeding or unauthorized ignition appear in notification streams.
- Trip and traveling records accessible through Plaspy reports for route analysis and historical review.
- Centralized fleet monitoring that consolidates device status and alerts for supervisory teams.
- Use of SMS and GPRS as delivery methods means Plaspy can receive updates where supported by the tracker configuration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Dangerous goods transport where alerting and status monitoring improve oversight.
- Taxi and rental fleets needing location based monitoring and incident alerts.
- Public transportation operations that benefit from trip records and fleet supervision.
