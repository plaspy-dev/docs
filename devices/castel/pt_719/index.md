# Castel - PT-719

![PT-719](./logo.jpg)

The Castel PT-719 is a compact GPS tracker designed for real time location monitoring and portable use. Operating on 2G networks with quad band GSM compatibility, the device combines GPS positioning with an accompanying mobile app so users can check location and device status on a cell phone. Its small, lightweight form factor and neck hanging option make it practical for personnel tracking and transportable monitoring tasks.

This model is compatible with Plaspy, making it suitable for organizations that want to consolidate location and attendance data in a single platform. PT-719 location updates and RFID based attendance events can be used inside Plaspy to provide visibility, reporting, and operational oversight for fleets and field staff without requiring complex hardware changes.

## Key Highlights

- Real time GPS location reporting for continuous visibility
- Built in RFID function for attendance logging and event capture
- Quad band 2G GSM connectivity for broad regional coverage
- Compact and lightweight design suitable for personnel use and portability
- Mobile app access for on the go checking of location and device status
- Multi day standby performance on a single charge for extended use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the PT-719 feeds location and status updates into the Plaspy platform so teams can monitor assets and personnel alongside other tracked devices. Plaspy can consolidate the device data into dashboards, reports, and event timelines to support day to day operations.

- Live location display in Plaspy dashboards for tracking movement and position
- Historical route and event reporting to review past movements and attendance logs
- Attendance records from RFID events available for reporting and export
- Alerts and notifications based on position or status changes as configured in Plaspy
- Device health and basic status monitoring within Plaspy when such updates are provided by the tracker

## Typical Use Cases

- Outdoor staff management and location oversight for field teams
- School attendance tracking using RFID check in and logging
- Logistics monitoring for movable assets and personnel during transport
- Workforce check in and out for companies with dispersed field employees
- Short term or portable tracking where a lightweight wearable unit is preferred

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT-719 is a practical choice for organizations that need a straightforward, wearable tracker with attendance capabilities. Its combination of GPS positioning and RFID provides a simple way to capture both location and personnel presence, and its compact form supports easy deployment across staff or small assets.

Paired with Plaspy, the device becomes part of a broader visibility and reporting system. Plaspy can collect the PT-719 updates and present them alongside other fleet data, helping teams make operational decisions based on consolidated location and attendance information.
