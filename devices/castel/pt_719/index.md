# Castel - PT-719

![PT-719](./logo.jpg)

The Castel PT-719 is a GPS tracker that operates on the 2G network and is equipped with a GPS system, allowing it to send real-time location data. With the convenience of an accompanying mobile app, users can easily check the location and other status of the tracker on their cell phones. This feature makes it ideal for a variety of applications, including outdoor staff management, school attendance tracking, and logistics monitoring.

One standout feature of the PT-719 is its RFID function, which enables attendance statistics to be gathered. This makes it particularly useful for organizations that require accurate attendance tracking, such as schools or companies with field staff. The tracker also boasts a quad-band GSM network compatibility, ensuring reliable connectivity across various regions. Its compact and lightweight design allows for easy portability, and it can be conveniently hung around the neck. Additionally, the PT-719 features a patented design, adding a touch of uniqueness to its overall appeal.

With its reliable GPS system, real-time location tracking, and RFID functionality, the Castel PT-719 is a versatile GPS tracker that can meet the needs of outdoor staff, schools, logistics companies, and more. Its compact size, lightweight design, and user-friendly mobile app make it a convenient and efficient solution for tracking and monitoring purposes.

### Technical Specifications:

- Dimension: 100mm x 60mm x 7.5mm
- Weight: 50g
- Shell Material: ABS+PC
- Battery Volume: 750mA
- Battery Voltage: 3.4V - 4.2V
- Charging time \(Full\): 2.5h
- Charging current: 650mA \(Max\)
- Standby time: 103 hours, 10 minutes per charge
- Standby current: average ≤ 5mA
- Max current: &lt; 200mA \(Peak\)
