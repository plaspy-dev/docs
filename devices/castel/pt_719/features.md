# Castel - PT-719 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Castel PT-719 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, including real time location reporting, RFID attendance functionality, and how those capabilities can be reflected in Plaspy for monitoring and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The summary below is grounded in the PT-719 public description and specifications; for device specific procedures and the latest details consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Castel PT-719 provides basic GPS location reporting over 2G networks combined with an RFID attendance option and a companion mobile app for on the go visibility. Its compact, lightweight form factor and quad band GSM compatibility make it suitable for portable and field use across multiple regions.

- Real time GPS location reporting suitable for live monitoring and tracking
- 2G quad band GSM connectivity for broad regional network compatibility
- Built in RFID function to support attendance collection and check ins
- Companion mobile app for quick access to location and status on a cell phone
- Compact and lightweight design intended for portable wear or hanging around the neck

## Core Features of Castel - PT-719

- GPS based position reporting for location awareness and tracking
- 2G quad band GSM network support for wide area connectivity
- RFID capability to gather attendance statistics and tag readings
- Companion mobile app integration for direct user access to location and status
- Compact form factor and low weight for easy portability
- Patented enclosure design as described by the manufacturer
- Battery characteristics including 750 mA capacity with quoted standby time and charging specifications in manufacturer documentation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can ingest location and status data from PT-719 devices and present that information alongside other fleet and asset data. The platform is designed to make tracked positions, event records, and basic device status easy to view and act on within maps, timelines, and reports.

- Live position updates and recent location history displayed on Plaspy maps
- RFID events reported by the device can be recorded as attendance or check in events in Plaspy when the device sends tag data
- Device identity and connectivity status visible in Plaspy for operational oversight
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and accepts device reports for display
- PT-719 devices may be configured to report to the Plaspy server domain and connect using UDP or TCP on the standard port used by Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Outdoor staff management where portable location and check ins are required
- School attendance tracking using RFID readings to generate presence records
- Logistics monitoring for small assets and personnel in transit
- Field workforce check ins and simple proof of presence workflows
- Short term rental or event staffing where wearable trackers provide presence and location visibility
- Situations that require a compact, wearable tracker for easy distribution to people or lightweight assets

## Feature Availability Notes

- RFID functionality and how attendance data is reported can depend on firmware and regional device variants
- 2G network suitability varies by country and mobile operator; verify regional 2G availability before deployment
- Battery life and standby figures are manufacturer supplied and depend on usage patterns and firmware configuration
- Some capabilities may require specific setup steps in the device firmware or mobile app before reporting to Plaspy
- Confirm hardware revision and feature list with the manufacturer to ensure the device matches deployment needs

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the Castel PT-719 with Plaspy gives organizations a straightforward way to combine compact GPS tracking and RFID based attendance into a single operational view. Plaspy provides mapping, event logging, and simple oversight tools that make it easier to monitor personnel movements, collect attendance records, and produce basic historical reports from incoming PT-719 data.
