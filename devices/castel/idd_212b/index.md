# Castel - IDD-212B

![IDD-212B](./logo.jpg)

The Castel IDD-212B is a Bluetooth 4.0 Car OBD scanner designed to read vehicle engine parameters directly from the ECU. It supports common diagnostics such as speed, RPM, temperature, and diagnostic trouble codes, and it provides readings for fuel consumption and mileage. The unit is MFi certified and compatible with iPhone and Android devices, and it includes features such as a G-sensor for impact detection and on-board flash memory capable of storing up to two months of data.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the IDD-212B can be used to bring detailed OBD-level information into a centralized fleet or vehicle monitoring environment. Its onboard storage and Bluetooth connectivity make it suitable for operations where intermittent connectivity occurs, and Plaspy can use the data provided by the scanner to enhance visibility, reporting, and operational oversight for vehicles that use this device.

## Key Highlights

- Bluetooth 4.0 connectivity for mobile device pairing with iPhone and Android
- MFi certification indicating compatibility with Apple devices
- Reads key engine parameters including speed, RPM, temperature, and error codes
- Fuel consumption and mileage monitoring to support efficiency analysis
- Built-in flash memory for up to two months of stored vehicle data
- Integrated G-sensor for recording sudden movements or impacts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, data from the IDD-212B can be ingested and presented alongside other fleet telemetry to provide a more complete picture of vehicle health and usage. Plaspy can use the scanner's recorded and real-time data to support monitoring workflows and reporting needs.

- Collect diagnostic parameters and present them in Plaspy dashboards for quick inspection
- Use fuel and mileage readings to support fuel efficiency reports and trend analysis
- Surface G-sensor events as incident notes to aid incident review and security checks
- Access up to two months of stored data for historical playback when devices reconnect
- Combine OBD data with Plaspy location and fleet management tools for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet managers monitoring vehicle performance and identifying inefficient vehicles
- Small fleets or owner operators tracking fuel consumption and mileage trends
- Maintenance teams using diagnostic readings to inform service scheduling
- Security and operations teams reviewing G-sensor events after impacts or harsh maneuvers
- Situations where intermittent connectivity benefits from local device storage

The IDD-212B is a practical choice for organizations that need OBD-level diagnostics in conjunction with a broader fleet management platform.
