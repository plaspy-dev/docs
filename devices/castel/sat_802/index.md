# Castel - SAT-802

<div className="device-logo">
![SAT-802](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Castel SAT-802 is a compact GPS tracker designed for global coverage and reliable asset monitoring. It combines iridium SBD satellite capability with GPRS communications to reduce blind spots and provide wide area tracking for high value assets. The device uses GPS positioning and a dual communication module to maintain two-way data exchange and prioritize GSM where available.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SAT-802 can feed location and status information into Plaspy's fleet monitoring environment to provide continuous visibility across regions where conventional networks are limited. Its global reach and power management features make it relevant for organizations that need consolidated tracking inside Plaspy alongside other fleet assets.

## Key Highlights

- Dual communication modules combining iridium SBD satellite and GPRS for wide area coverage
- Two-way data transmission with automatic selection favoring GSM when available
- GPS positioning suitable for continuous location updates
- Broad operating voltage range from 9 V to 36 V for compatibility with many vehicle and asset power systems
- Built for a wide temperature range and basic dust and moisture resistance with IP30 protection
- Multiple power saving modes to reduce average current draw during standby

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SAT-802 supplies location and connectivity status that Plaspy uses to present unified maps, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy can ingest the tracker data to help teams maintain operational oversight of assets across different regions and communications conditions.

- Live and historical location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on movement, geofence, or connectivity changes
- Consolidated reporting in Plaspy for route history and asset uptime across satellite and cellular coverage
- Centralized monitoring that lets operators see assets that transition between GSM and satellite links
- Use of Plaspy dashboards to group SAT-802 devices with other trackers for mixed fleet management

## Typical Use Cases

- High value asset tracking where uninterrupted coverage is required
- Long range or remote fleet vehicles that operate beyond standard cellular networks
- Container and cargo monitoring during transit across regions with variable coverage
- Centralized fleet operations that require a mix of satellite and cellular telemetry
- Assets needing robust temperature and voltage tolerance for varied environments

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SAT-802 is a practical option for operators who need broad geographic coverage combined with the convenience of cellular priority where available. Its dual-module design helps reduce blind spots, while the GPS positioning and power management options support continuous monitoring without excessive power draw. In Plaspy, those capabilities translate into more complete asset visibility and more consistent reporting across mixed coverage areas.

Because the SAT-802 provides basic environmental ratings and a wide voltage range, it can fit many vehicle and asset fleets, but note the device has IP30 protection which offers limited moisture resistance; consider environmental mounting and enclosures as appropriate. For organizations using Plaspy, the SAT-802 can be a useful component in a layered tracking strategy that covers both urban cellular zones and remote locations.
