# Castel - MPIP-620

![MPIP-620](./logo.jpg)

The Castel MPIP-620 GPS tracker is specifically designed for motorcycles, offering high precision GPS positioning and utilizing GPRS and SMS transmission for communication. With its sleek appearance and user-friendly IOS and Android app "Lite Guardian," this tracker provides motorcycle owners with positioning monitoring, security features, and real-time information on the running status of their vehicles. It also includes fortification and towing alarms to ensure the safety of the motorcycle in case of danger.

Some outstanding features of the Castel MPIP-620 GPS tracker include the ability to track the motorcycle's location through GPS and GPRS, timing positioning, a built-in motion sensor, and the option to check the history of routes on the platform. The tracker also offers power-saving mode, Google Maps integration on mobile devices, geo-fencing, movement alerts, over-speed alerts, low battery alarms, and mileage reporting. Additionally, it comes with an Android and iOS app for easy access and control.

With its compact dimensions of 75mm×48mm×20mm and lightweight design weighing only 80g, the Castel MPIP-620 is suitable for motorcycles. It features a plastic shell with a protective leverage rating of IP54, ensuring durability and resistance to dust and water. The tracker operates on a voltage range of 9-72VDC and includes a standby battery of 3.7V/500mAH Li-ion battery for backup power. It has a wide operating temperature range of -30℃ to +70℃ and can withstand storage temperatures from -40℃ to +85℃. The positional accuracy of the tracker is less than 15m, and it supports quad-band GSM frequencies \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\) and GPS frequency of 1575.42MHz.

The Castel MPIP-620 GPS tracker is an ideal choice for various applications, including logistics vehicles, driving schools, motorcycles, public transportation vehicles, and the tourist industry. Its advanced features and reliable performance make it a valuable tool for monitoring and securing motorcycles.
