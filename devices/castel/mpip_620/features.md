# Castel - MPIP-620 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Castel MPIP-620 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to motorcycle monitoring and how those capabilities are typically represented within Plaspy. The content focuses on user facing functions and operational value rather than device internals.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and how the manufacturer implements particular functions. For device specific commands, setup details, or firmware notes consult the manufacturer documentation and the device manual.

## Feature Overview

The Castel MPIP-620 is a compact motorcycle tracker designed to provide positioning, movement monitoring, and basic security alerts while reporting data over cellular networks. Its combination of GPS positioning, GPRS and SMS transmission, and an accompanying mobile app makes it suitable for owners and fleet operators who need ongoing location and status visibility.

- High precision GPS positioning with reported positional accuracy under 15 meters for location awareness.
- GPRS and SMS communication to transmit location and status updates to remote platforms and mobile apps.
- Built in motion sensor plus fortification and towing alarms to detect and report unauthorized movement.
- Route history and timing positioning functions to review past trips and operating patterns.
- Power saving mode, low battery alarm, and a built in standby battery for backup power on the motorcycle.
- Google Maps integration on mobile devices and support for geo fencing, movement alerts, over-speed alerts, and mileage reporting for operational oversight.

## Core Features of Castel - MPIP-620

- GPS location reporting with stated positional accuracy of less than 15 meters.
- Dual communication methods through GPRS and SMS for flexibility in reporting.
- Built in motion sensor to detect movement and trigger towing or fortification alarms.
- Route history recording for later review of past tracks and timing positions.
- Geo fencing and movement alert support to notify on boundary crossings and unexpected movement.
- Over speed alert and mileage reporting for basic operational monitoring.
- Compact IP54 rated housing and lightweight design intended for motorcycle installation.
- Built in standby 3.7V 500 mAh Li ion battery and wide input voltage range for vehicle power compatibility.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the MPIP-620's location and event reports are collected and presented to provide continuous visibility and historical context. Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols and maps incoming reports into location points, alerts, and trip histories for operational use.

- Location updates from the device appear as live position points on the Plaspy map and can be viewed in real time.
- Route history recorded by the tracker is available in Plaspy so operators can review past trips and timing positioning.
- Movement, towing, fortification, over speed, and low battery alerts are surfaced as events in Plaspy for monitoring and notification.
- Geo fencing configured on Plaspy can be used with device movement alerts to generate boundary notifications.
- Plaspy accepts tracker reports via the standard server endpoint and automatically detects the tracker protocol to simplify integration.
