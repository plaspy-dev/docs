# Castel - MPIP-619

![MPIP-619](./logo.jpg)

The Castel MPIP-619 is a hard-wired GPS tracker designed specifically for motorcycle and vehicle management. With its compact size and industrial IP54 dustproof and waterproof feature, this tracker is built to withstand tough conditions. It supports quad-band frequency and can connect with different accessories to provide features such as remote engine cut-off, real-time positioning, and remote parameter setting via SMS and GPRS.

One of the standout features of the MPIP-619 is its real-time location tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the exact location of your vehicle at any given time. It also offers vehicle diagnostic analysis, mileage statistics, remote power interrupt, SMS alert and configuration, SOS and speeding alarms, geo-fencing, and data storage in zero signal zones. The device is easy to install and can be plug-n-play, making it convenient for users.

The Castel MPIP-619 has a dimension of 75mm\*48mm\*20mm and weighs only 70g, making it compact and lightweight. It has a protective leverage of IP54, ensuring its durability in harsh environments. The tracker operates on a voltage range of 9-36 VDC and has a standby battery of 3.7V/850mAH Li-ion battery. With an operational temperature range of -30℃ to 70℃ and a positional accuracy of less than 15m, this tracker is reliable and accurate.

Overall, the Castel MPIP-619 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that is ideal for motorcycle and vehicle management. Its compact size, real-time tracking, and various additional features make it a valuable tool for monitoring and managing your vehicles.
