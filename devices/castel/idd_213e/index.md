# Castel - IDD-213E

![IDD-213E](./logo.jpg)

The Castel IDD-213E is an intelligent on-board diagnostic and all-in-one device designed for both passenger and commercial vehicles. With its plug-and-play technology, this GPS tracker is easy to install and use. It can read diagnostic information from the vehicle's ECU and capture location data, which is then sent to a backend server for real-time remote diagnostic and tracking purposes.

One of the standout features of the IDD-213E is its support for 3G network connectivity, ensuring reliable and fast data transmission. It is also built with industrial-class components of high quality, ensuring durability and longevity. The device is compliant with OBD II/EOBD, J1939, and J1708 standards, making it compatible with a wide range of vehicles.

In terms of data collection and analysis, the IDD-213E offers comprehensive capabilities. It can read diagnostic data such as vehicle speed, RPM, and ECT, as well as diagnostic trouble codes and freeze frame data. The device also provides mileage and fuel consumption statistics, allowing for efficient fleet management and vehicle tracking. Additionally, it monitors driving behavior, including speeding, hard acceleration/deceleration, and idle engine, providing valuable insights for improving driver safety and efficiency.

The IDD-213E also offers SMS alarm functionality, sending alerts to the user's phone in case of any issues or emergencies. It can connect to a backend server via domain or IP address, enabling seamless integration with existing fleet management systems.

With its wide range of features and compatibility, the Castel IDD-213E is an ideal choice for fleet management and vehicle tracking applications. It is also suitable for use in the car rental industry, vehicle insurance, driving schools, and car service shops. Its combination of diagnostic capabilities and real-time tracking makes it a valuable tool for optimizing vehicle performance and ensuring driver safety.
