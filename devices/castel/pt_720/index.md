# Castel - PT-720

<div className="device-logo">
![PT-720](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Castel PT-720 is a compact kids GPS tracker watch designed to help caregivers monitor a child’s location and wellbeing. As a wearable, it combines two-way calling, multi-mode positioning (GPS, LBS, and WiFi), SOS alerting to preset contacts, and basic activity tracking. The device also supports geo-fencing, a month of historical route viewing, and a pedometer, all packaged in a lightweight form factor with a 1.3 inch display and standby time suitable for daily use.

This model is compatible with Plaspy and can be used alongside the platform to centralize visibility and alerts. Plaspy can receive location and event information from the PT-720, making it practical for families and small organisations that want consolidated tracking, geofence notifications, and history playback in a single monitoring platform.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built wearable for children with comfortable, lightweight dimensions and a compact display
- Two-way talk functionality for direct calls between caregiver and child
- Multi-mode positioning that combines GPS, LBS, and WiFi for improved location availability
- SOS alert feature with calls and messages to multiple emergency contacts
- Geo-fence support and automatic notifications when boundaries are crossed
- Up to one month of historical route data available for review
- Built-in pedometer for simple activity tracking and step history

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the PT-720 becomes part of a broader monitoring and notification workflow. Plaspy can display live location data and surface events from the tracker so caregivers get timely situational awareness.

- Real-time location visible on the Plaspy map for easy monitoring of current position
- Geo-fence creation and alerting managed through Plaspy so breaches trigger notifications
- SOS events forwarded into Plaspy as alerts, enabling escalation and record keeping
- Historical route replay in Plaspy to review past movement for up to the device supported period
- Activity and movement summaries available alongside location history for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Parental monitoring of children during school commute or daily activities
- Supervising groups of children during outings or field trips
- After-school pickup coordination and confirming arrival at safe locations
- Daycare or small group care oversight where individual wearable tracking is preferred
- Short term location monitoring during travel or events
