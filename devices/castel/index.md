## How Castel devices work with Plaspy

Castel trackers transmit location and status information from assets and vehicles. Plaspy accepts telemetry from compatible trackers and processes that data into live locations, historical trips, alerts, and reports. If a Castel device is listed in the catalog above, it can be connected to Plaspy by configuring the device to send data to the platform and then mapping its telemetry within Plaspy. The BrandCatalog lists the models that have known compatibility so you can identify the correct device and follow the platform specific setup steps on the device detail pages.

## Common features found in Castel devices

While individual capabilities vary by model, many Castel devices share common telematics features that make them suitable for fleet and asset tracking:
- GPS position reporting for live location and breadcrumb trails
- Movement and ignition detection useful for vehicle tracking
- Basic digital and analog input support for sensors and state monitoring
- Battery and power reporting for portable and vehicle installed trackers

For model specific feature sets and configuration parameters, select a device in the catalog above and follow the device page instructions.

## Typical use cases for Castel GPS trackers

Castel devices are commonly used in scenarios such as:
- Fleet vehicle tracking for routing and dispatch visibility
- Asset monitoring for trailers, equipment, and rented assets
- Status monitoring where simple digital inputs report on equipment state
- Battery powered asset tracking for non powered equipment where periodic location is sufficient

These use cases map well to the capabilities offered by Plaspy, which aggregates telemetry, manages alerts, and provides reporting for operational insights.
