# MiniFinder - Watch Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the MiniFinder Watch with Plaspy. It covers the practical capabilities a caregiver, administrator, or technical reviewer will see when the wearable is connected to Plaspy, including real time tracking, health telemetry, fall detection, SOS alerts, and indoor positioning support as described by the manufacturer.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where appropriate this page highlights which capabilities are platform visible in Plaspy and notes common limitations to verify against current MiniFinder documentation.

## Feature Overview

The MiniFinder Watch is a Plaspy compatible wearable designed for personal safety and supervised care. It provides continuous location awareness plus health and event telemetry that can be routed into a Plaspy account for monitoring, alerting, and historical review.

- Real time GNSS tracking combined with indoor beacon and Wi Fi assisted positioning for better in building awareness.
- Health telemetry including heart rate and SpO2 readings alongside automatic fall detection and one touch SOS alerts.
- LTE Cat M1 and GSM cellular connectivity with voice call capability for two way communication when needed.
- Bluetooth BLE and Wi Fi support for sensor pairing and proximity based location enhancement in care environments.
- Battery powered wearable design with IPX7 water resistance and remote firmware updates to simplify maintenance.

## Core Features of MiniFinder - Watch

- Real time multi constellation positioning for outdoor tracking and movement history.
- Indoor beacon support and Wi Fi assisted positioning to improve room level accuracy in care facilities.
- Heart rate monitoring and SpO2 telemetry for basic health oversight integrated into event logs.
- Automatic fall detection that generates alerts for rapid caregiver notification.
- One touch SOS button to trigger immediate incident reporting and location sharing.
- LTE Cat M1 and GSM cellular connectivity with a preinstalled SIM and voice call support for direct contact.
- Bluetooth BLE5.0 and Wi Fi 2.4 GHz for sensor pairing and reduced reliance on GNSS when indoors.
- Firmware over the air support to manage updates and configuration remotely.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When registered to a Plaspy environment the MiniFinder Watch streams location, event, and telemetry data so caregivers and operators can maintain situational awareness. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it alongside other monitored assets for centralized oversight.

- Real time location points and movement history appear in Plaspy maps and timeline views for live tracking and review.
- SOS alarms and fall detection events are routed into Plaspy alert queues and can notify predefined contacts or monitoring teams.
- Heart rate and SpO2 telemetry are available in Plaspy for trend visualization and to support caregiver decision making.
- Indoor beacon and Wi Fi assisted positioning improve room level accuracy in Plaspy for deployments in care homes and institutions.
- Remote device management via FOTA is supported through the Plaspy compatible backend to help keep firmware current.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and integrates the Watch without complex custom configuration.
