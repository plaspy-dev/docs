# MiniFinder - ATTO Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the MiniFinder ATTO and how those capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on the tracker features relevant to real time pet and animal monitoring, what you can expect in day to day use, and how the ATTO feeds data into Plaspy for visibility on desktop and mobile apps.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information below is based on the current public description of the ATTO; for device specific limits and the most current technical details consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The MiniFinder ATTO is a compact pet tracker designed to provide ongoing positioning and activity awareness with a strong emphasis on battery efficiency. It is intended for users who need a small, lightweight device that can be monitored in real time and that offers extended standby and sleep modes to preserve battery between movements.

- Real time location tracking viewable on computer and iOS or Android apps
- Smart sleep mode that extends tracking life by optimizing reporting while idle
- Tracking on demand via SMS for immediate location checks
- Geo fence alarm to notify when a device enters or leaves a defined area
- Bark detector and passive tracking modes for animal specific monitoring
- Waterproof construction rated to IP67 for outdoor use

## Core Features of MiniFinder - ATTO

- Compact physical design and low weight suitable for pets and animals
- Smart in house sleep algorithm that can provide up to 15 days of tracking in deep sleep mode
- Standby time capability up to 20 days when no movement is detected
- Real time tracking combined with tracking on demand via SMS
- Geo fence alarm support for perimeter monitoring
- Bark detector and passive tracking features tailored to animal use cases
- Waterproof rating IP67 for protection against weather exposure
- Built in 3D G sensor and status LEDs for motion detection and device feedback

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy presents ATTO tracking data in a centralized monitoring platform so owners and teams can follow positions, events, and status updates in a single place. Plaspy is designed to recognize and process common tracker protocols and will display the ATTO location and events alongside other devices you manage.

- Real time location points displayed on Plaspy mapping for continuous visibility
- Geofence events and alerts appear in Plaspy event lists and can be used for notifications
- Battery and standby behavior from the ATTO can be monitored to plan recharging or replacement
- Preconfigured devices can be added to Plaspy quickly and Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocol
- Passive tracking and on demand location checks are reflected as map history and ad hoc position updates
- Status indicators such as movement detected or stationary states are available for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Keeping track of pets during outdoor activities and walks
- Monitoring working animals or service animals in field operations
- Temporary monitoring during transport or boarding situations
- Recovering a lost or wandering animal with real time location checks
- Research or observational projects where small form factor tracking is required

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware revisions may enable or change certain features such as sleep timing and event reporting cadence
- Hardware variants and production batches can differ in sensors, battery capacity, or accessory fitment
- Regional cellular network differences can affect on demand SMS functions and real time reporting reliability
- Preconfiguration options and available features may vary by vendor or reseller
- Always confirm the exact feature set for a given device serial or batch with the manufacturer documentation

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the MiniFinder ATTO with Plaspy gives owners and teams a practical way to centralize location and event data for pets and animals. Plaspy surfaces live positions, historical tracks, and geofence events so you can monitor movement patterns, respond to alerts, and manage multiple devices from the same platform.
