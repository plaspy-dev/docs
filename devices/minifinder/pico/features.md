# MiniFinder - Pico Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the MiniFinder Pico tracker with Plaspy. It describes the practical capabilities you can expect to see when Pico devices are registered into a Plaspy account and how those capabilities are typically reflected in Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

Exact feature availability and reported behavior can vary by device firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details, firmware notes and the latest capabilities consult the official MiniFinder documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

MiniFinder Pico is a compact keyring style GPS tracker intended for personal safety and small asset protection. When paired with Plaspy, Pico delivers position updates, incident alerts and basic motion-based sensing into centralized monitoring workflows to support live tracking, incident response and historical review.

- Real time GNSS location updates suitable for live tracking of people and small assets.
- SOS alerting and two way call events to notify responders and support verbal contacts.
- Motion and fall detection powered by an onboard accelerometer for incident awareness.
- Geofence entry and exit alerts and configurable motion or speed alarms.
- Waterproof keyring form factor and compact design for discreet placement and wearability.

## Core Features of MiniFinder - Pico

- Precision GNSS positioning for location telemetry and map display.
- Integrated SOS button with event reporting for rapid alerting.
- Two way calling support to reach preset contacts in response to an SOS.
- Accelerometer based motion and fall detection to generate event reports.
- Geofence support for zone based notifications and monitoring.
- Low battery and GPS signal loss alerts to help with device maintenance.
- Compact IPX7 rated housing suitable for wearable or discreet asset placement.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a MiniFinder Pico device is connected to Plaspy, its location and event messages become actionable items inside Plaspy for monitoring, alerting and historical analysis. Plaspy receives position updates and event notifications so teams can respond, log incidents and replay past movements.

- Live location points displayed on Plaspy maps for real time situational awareness.
- SOS and alarm events forwarded into Plaspy alert streams and notification channels.
- Geofence triggers and motion or fall events generate configurable notifications inside Plaspy.
- Battery and connectivity warnings show as maintenance alerts so devices can be serviced.
- Historical position data can be reviewed in Plaspy to support incident reconstruction and route analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for elderly or vulnerable people using SOS and fall alerts.
- Child or family tracking where compact form factor and geofence notifications are needed.
- Lone worker oversight using event alerts and two way calling for rapid assistance.
- Small asset protection for items that benefit from discreet live location and history.
- Short term rentals and shared equipment tracking where portability and quick deployment matter.
