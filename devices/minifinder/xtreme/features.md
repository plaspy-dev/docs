# MiniFinder - Xtreme Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the MiniFinder Xtreme tracker with Plaspy. It describes the device capabilities that are relevant to real time tracking, alerting, and historical playback when the Xtreme is connected to the Plaspy platform. The information here focuses on practical, user facing features and how they are reflected inside Plaspy for operational use.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional product variant, and manufacturer implementation. For definitive, model specific specifications and instructions refer to the manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes from MiniFinder.

## Feature Overview

The MiniFinder Xtreme is a rugged, magnetic GPS tracker intended for long term, covert tracking of vehicles and portable assets. When used with Plaspy, the Xtreme provides location updates and event telemetry that support anti theft workflows, fleet oversight, and historical route analysis.

- Real time GPS positioning for continuous location visibility and live map tracking in Plaspy.
- Long life rechargeable battery and energy efficient GNSS for extended field deployment between charges.
- Rugged IP68 rated housing and strong magnetic mount for installation free placement on metal assets.
- Motion and tamper event reporting to trigger alerts and reduce unnecessary reporting when assets are stationary.
- Local logging for historical playback combined with platform storage to support investigations and route review.

## Core Features of MiniFinder - Xtreme

- IP68 waterproof enclosure and ruggedized form factor for harsh environments.
- Strong magnetic mounting for covert, installation free attachment to metal surfaces.
- Rechargeable large capacity battery designed for extended field operation.
- High performance GNSS receiver for fast, accurate positioning.
- Global cellular connectivity with support for multiple LTE and GSM bands.
- Motion detection and tamper/sabotage sensing to support event driven reporting.
- Onboard status LEDs and internal logging memory for local data retention and deployment checks.
- Compact dimensions and weight suitable for trailers, engines, and portable assets.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the Xtreme's position fixes and event notifications and converts those inputs into actionable visibility for users and operations teams. The platform presents location and event data on maps, routes, and in alert channels so teams can monitor assets remotely.

- Live map visualization of incoming position updates from the Xtreme for situational awareness.
- Geofence events and configurable alerts in Plaspy triggered by entries and exits of defined areas.
- Tamper, motion, and low battery events presented in Plaspy alarm lists and notification workflows.
- Historical position playback using the tracker logs and platform storage for incident review.
- Event driven reporting reduces data transmissions when the asset is stationary while enabling timely updates when movement or alarms occur.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert tracking of trailers, cargo units, and unpowered assets on behalf of fleet operations.
- Theft prevention and recovery for boats, outboard engines, and marine equipment thanks to waterproof design.
- Seasonal vehicle and powersport monitoring for ATVs and snowmobiles where long battery endurance is important.
- Remote equipment monitoring on worksites to detect unauthorized movement and improve utilization.
- Logistics and container tracking with history playback to support delivery investigations and chain of custody.
