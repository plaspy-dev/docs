# MiniFinder - Xtreme

![Xtreme](./tracker.png)

The MiniFinder Xtreme is a rugged, magnetic GPS tracker designed for long-term, covert tracking of high-value vehicles and equipment. Built by MiniFinder and now supported as a Plaspy compatible device, the Xtreme delivers reliable real-time tracking and robust telemetry for fleet management, anti-theft protection, and remote asset monitoring. Its IP68 waterproof enclosure, strong magnetic mounting, and large rechargeable battery make it ideal for harsh environments where endurance and concealment matter.

Whether you are protecting trailers, boat outboards, ATVs, snowmobiles, or worksite equipment, the Xtreme pairs with Plaspy to deliver continuous position updates, configurable alerts, and historical playback. The tracker’s long-life 7800 mAh battery, Qualcomm Gen 8C GNSS receiver, and motion/tamper sensors provide the data Plaspy needs for geofencing, alarm handling, and operational reporting — helping teams reduce theft risk and improve fleet utilization.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged, waterproof \(IP68\) housing with strong magnetic mount for fast, installation-free attachment to metal surfaces.
- Long-life 7800 mAh rechargeable battery \(3.7V\) optimized for multi-year field operation depending on reporting intervals and configuration.
- Fast and accurate GNSS positioning \(Qualcomm Gen 8C receiver\) with typical outdoor accuracy of 0–5 m.
- Global cellular coverage: LTE bands and quad-band GSM to operate in 190+ countries.
- Built-in motion detection and tamper/sabotage alarms for reliable anti-theft alerts and event-driven reporting.
- Covert form factor \(88 × 62 × 34 mm, 290 g\) suitable for boats, trailers, engines, and portable assets.
- Onboard LEDs and internal memory \(4 Mb\) for local logging and status indication during deployment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the MiniFinder Xtreme feeds location and event telemetry into a centralized platform for real-time tracking, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy receives position fixes and sensor events from the Xtreme and converts those inputs into live maps, geofence actions, and automated notifications to users and dispatch teams. The tracker’s event-driven design reduces data use by sending updates on motion, tamper, geofence crossing, and low battery — while configurable reporting intervals enable balance between battery life and update frequency.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Geofence alerts forwarded to Plaspy for instant notifications on entry/exit of defined areas.
- Tamper/sabotage and low-battery notifications routed into Plaspy alarm workflows for fast response.
- GPS signal loss and regain warnings reported to Plaspy for troubleshooting and asset status awareness.
- Historical position logging and playback \(internal memory + platform storage\) for route replay and investigations.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the MiniFinder Xtreme as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivers a balance of durability, covert installation, and long-term operation that many asset owners need. Its IP68-rated housing and magnetic mount make it straightforward to deploy on metal assets without wiring, while the large rechargeable battery and energy-efficient GNSS minimize maintenance intervals. When paired with Plaspy, the Xtreme provides actionable telemetry for fleet management, anti-theft alerts, and operational reporting. Organizations that also require integration with fuel monitoring, ignition/immobilizer controls, or Bluetooth sensors can combine the Xtreme’s positional and event data in Plaspy with other telemetry sources to create a complete fleet intelligence solution.
