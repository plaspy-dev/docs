# MiniFinder - Rex Features

This page provides a public summary of the MiniFinder Rex feature set and how the device integrates with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on user-facing capabilities and explains the practical value Rex delivers when used for real-time location, behavior alerts, and team coordination inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability for Rex can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, how the unit is installed on a collar or harness, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. For the most current technical details and supported functionality refer to the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

MiniFinder Rex is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for hunting dogs and working animals, and is compatible with Plaspy for mapping, alerts, and historical reporting. The tracker combines high-sensitivity positioning with local wireless connectivity and hunting-focused sensors to support situational awareness in the field.

- Real-time GNSS positioning suitable for live tracking and mapping in Plaspy.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and compact form factor for collar or harness mounting.
- Hunting-focused sensors and outputs such as a guiding LED, barking detection, vibration and voice command modes, and speaker/microphone communication.
- Cellular connectivity for broad coverage and BLE/Wi‑Fi for short-range interactions and configuration.
- Long-life rechargeable battery and local event storage to preserve data between uploads.

## Core Features of MiniFinder - Rex

- High-sensitivity GPS and GLONASS positioning for precise location updates.
- Cellular connectivity including LTE‑M and GSM bands for wide area coverage.
- Rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure sized for collar mounting.
- Local wireless: Bluetooth 5.0 for short-range pairing and 2.4 GHz Wi‑Fi for nearby sync where supported.
- Hunting-specific features: configurable guiding LED, barking detection, vibration and voice command modes, and built-in speaker and microphone.
- G-sensor and local event logging with onboard flash memory for temporary storage.
- Rechargeable battery with USB‑C charging and extended low-activity operation modes.
- Compact dimensions and lightweight design intended for working dogs and active use.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Rex streams location and status information that Plaspy ingests to provide live maps, alerts, and historical playback. Plaspy surfaces the device data in ways that help handlers and teams maintain situational awareness and coordinate activity across a hunt or operation.

- Live position updates shown on Plaspy maps for real-time tracking of each animal.
- Alerts and events such as barking detection, stop or movement events, and place history available for review and notifications.
- Remote actions initiated from the platform or connected app, allowing handlers to trigger the guiding LED, vibration, or audible signals when supported by the device.
- Historical routes and event logs accessible in Plaspy for after-action review and analysis.
- BLE and Wi‑Fi interactions permit quick configuration or firmware sync where the device and workflow allow.

## Typical Use Cases

- Live tracking of hunting dogs to monitor location and keep teams coordinated in the field.
- Use of the guiding LED and audible signals to locate a dog in low light or dense cover.
- Remote behavior alerts such as barking detection to inform handlers of activity or potential issues.
- Team-based operations where multiple handlers share live positions and geofence alerts for coordinated responses.
- Post-operation analysis and route review using place history and telemetry logs stored and displayed in Plaspy.
- Search and rescue scenarios where compact, rugged tracking and real-time mapping support team movement.
