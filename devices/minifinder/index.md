MiniFinder produces a range of GPS tracking products designed for location awareness, personal safety, and fleet operations. ## How MiniFinder devices work with Plaspy

MiniFinder devices compatible with Plaspy transmit position and status information over cellular networks to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy receives, processes, and presents that data in dashboards, maps, and alerts so you can monitor assets, people, or vehicles in real time. The integration enables standard tracking functionality such as live location, history playback, and event notifications while allowing Plaspy to bring centralized device management and reporting to your deployment.

Plaspy supports common device workflows including initial provisioning, online status monitoring, and alert routing. For device specific setup steps or configuration parameters please open the relevant model page in the catalog where model level documentation and setup instructions are available.

While capabilities vary across models, many MiniFinder devices offer features commonly used in GPS tracking deployments. Typical capabilities you may find include:
