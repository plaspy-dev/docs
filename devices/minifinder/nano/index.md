# MiniFinder - Nano

![Nano](./tracker.jpg)

The MiniFinder Nano is a compact wearable GPS tracker designed for personal safety and care environments. Offered as a wristwatch or lanyard wearable, Nano combines outdoor positioning with assisted indoor location methods and an integrated cellular connection to provide continuous location and alarm capabilities. The device is purpose built for scenarios where lightweight wearability, SOS functionality and straightforward alarm handling are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Nano feeds location, status and alarm data into the Plaspy platform so organizations can monitor users and incidents from a single dashboard. Plaspy compatibility brings MiniFinder Nano alerts, fall detection and location history into centralized workflows for notifications, escalation and reporting, making the device useful in care, lone worker and portable asset monitoring programs.

## Key Highlights

- Compact wearable form factor ideal for wrist or lanyard use and daily wear in care environments.
- Compatibility with Plaspy for centralized real time tracking, alarm routing and telemetry management.
- Hybrid positioning combining outdoor GNSS and assisted indoor location to improve situational awareness.
- Dedicated SOS panic button and two way voice support to speed human response to incidents.
- Fall detection and motion sensing to automate safety alerts and reduce manual reporting.
- Embedded cellular connectivity for broad geographic reach and remote monitoring without local SIM swaps.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, MiniFinder Nano transmits positional data and alarm events so teams can see live locations, respond to SOS triggers, and review incident history. Plaspy ingests telemetry from the device and applies rules, notifications and reporting to make alerts operational and auditable.

- Live location updates feed Plaspy dashboards for immediate visibility of wearer position.
- SOS and fall alarms are routed through Plaspy notification channels to designated responders.
- Event history and timestamps are aggregated for reporting, compliance checks and post incident review.
- Two way voice events and alarm context can be associated with incidents inside Plaspy for richer response information.
- Bluetooth assisted location and sensor data improve indoor tracking and can be included in Plaspy event logs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Eldercare monitoring where continuous location, fall detection and rapid SOS handling are required.
- Lone worker safety for staff who operate remotely and need a compact personal alarm solution.
- Healthcare settings for patient and visitor protection with integrated voice and alarm workflows.
- Child and family safety applications where wearability and simple SOS functions are important.
- Portable asset protection when a compact tracker and BLE sensors help monitor small valuable items.
