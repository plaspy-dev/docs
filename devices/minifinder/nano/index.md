# MiniFinder Nano

The MiniFinder Nano is a compact, wearable GPS tracker and personal alarm aimed at healthcare, eldercare and lone‑worker safety. Designed as a wristwatch or lanyard wearable, Nano pairs reliable outdoor GPS positioning with Wi‑Fi, BLE and an embedded eSIM to deliver real‑time tracking and alarm management. As a Plaspy compatible device, Nano brings personal safety telemetry, SOS and fall alerts into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, rapid notification and audit‑ready event history.

Plaspy compatibility enables organizations to combine MiniFinder Nano’s rich location and status data with Plaspy’s dashboards, alerting rules and reporting tools. The result is a compact GPS tracker that slots into telematics, personal safety and care workflows, providing caregivers and operations teams with immediate situational awareness, historical telemetry and integrated alarm handling.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable GPS tracker in a compact watch form factor — lightweight \(60 g\) and easy to wear as a wristband or carry on a lanyard.
- Plaspy compatible for real‑time tracking, alarm routing and centralized telemetry management across users and care teams.
- Reliable hybrid positioning: GNSS \(AG3352QN\) for 0–5 m outdoor accuracy plus Wi‑Fi and BLE 5.0 for improved indoor location.
- Dedicated SOS panic button and two‑way voice calling for fast, human‑centred response to alarms.
- Advanced safety sensors including 3‑axis accelerometer, fall detection and a proximity sensor to detect wrist wear and trigger automatic alerts.
- Global cellular reach via embedded eSIM and LTE/GSM bands for operation in more than 190 countries.
- Low‑power design with MLPC™ algorithm and flexible battery runtimes to match active and standby use cases.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, MiniFinder Nano transmits positional fixes, sensor events and alarm signals to Plaspy in near real time. Plaspy receives telemetry and uses its rules engine to trigger notifications, route alarms to responsible caregivers, and record events for reporting. Integration focuses on clean delivery of location, status and alarm metadata so teams can respond quickly and review incidents later.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates: GNSS positions, Wi‑Fi/BLE assisted indoor fixes and status timestamps feed Plaspy for live tracking.
- SOS panic and fall alarms: button presses and automatic fall detection generate immediate alerts routed through Plaspy to preconfigured contacts or alarm centers.
- Two‑way voice context: alarm events can be paired with voice call metadata so responders know when a voice session occurred.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons: BLE 5.0 support and iBeacon compatibility enable enhanced indoor positioning and sensor attachments for temperature or proximity monitoring.
- Telemetry and event history: MiniFinder Nano stores event history \(up to one year via MiniFinder Live\) and Plaspy can aggregate this for reports and compliance logs.
- Note on vehicle telematics: Nano is optimized for personal safety and wearable monitoring. For vehicle‑specific telemetry such as ignition control, immobilizer commands or fuel monitoring, vehicle trackers remain the recommended choice; however, Nano’s telemetry can complement broader fleet management or anti‑theft strategies when used for personnel or portable assets.

## Use Cases

- Eldercare monitoring — continuous location, fall detection and SOS calling for seniors living independently or in assisted care.
- Healthcare and vulnerable person protection — fast alerts and two‑way voice links for patients, visitors or staff who require immediate assistance.
- Lone worker safety — compact personal alarm for field staff, social workers or home care providers where real‑time tracking and panic capabilities are essential.
- Child and family safety — easy‑to‑wear GPS tracker with SOS and voice to enhance personal security during travel or daily activities.
- Portable asset protection — attachable to valuable, portable equipment where compact form, BLE sensors and GPS location support anti‑theft workflows when combined with Plaspy monitoring.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the MiniFinder Nano with Plaspy gives organizations a compact GPS tracker that is purpose‑built for personal safety but also versatile enough to feed telematics and telemetry workflows. Nano’s hybrid positioning \(GNSS plus Wi‑Fi and BLE\), SOS and automated fall detection deliver immediate, actionable alerts; Plaspy adds centralized rules, escalation paths and reporting to make those alerts operational. The embedded eSIM and multiple cellular bands provide wide geographic coverage, while low‑power design and clear status indicators simplify day‑to‑day use.

For teams looking to extend Plaspy’s capabilities into personal safety and small asset tracking, MiniFinder Nano offers reliable real‑time tracking, event history and Bluetooth sensor support without sacrificing wearability. If your deployment requires vehicle‑specific telemetry such as ignition, immobilizer control or fuel monitoring, combine Nano with vehicle trackers in Plaspy for a comprehensive fleet and personal protection strategy.
