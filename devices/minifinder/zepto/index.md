# MiniFinder - Zepto

![Zepto](./tracker.png)

The MiniFinder Zepto is a compact OBD2 plug and play GPS tracker designed for discreet vehicle tracking and continuous real time monitoring. Built for internal mounting and regular vehicle operation, Zepto provides live location and telemetry suited to private cars and fleet vehicles. Its small form factor and constant vehicle power supply make it practical for long term deployment where unobtrusive tracking and ongoing connectivity are priorities.

Zepto is Plaspy compatible out of the box, which means it can stream location and OBD sourced telemetry directly into the Plaspy platform for live visibility, alerts, and historical reporting. That compatibility makes Zepto a straightforward option for organizations that want a low maintenance tracker feeding data into Plaspy for fleet management, anti theft workflows, and trip journaling.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD2 connection for continuous vehicle powered tracking with no user charging required.
- Multi constellation GNSS for precise outdoor positioning and reliable location updates.
- Small internal form factor for discreet mounting and reduced risk of discovery.
- Internal backup battery for short emergency reporting if vehicle power is interrupted.
- Historical trip logging and configurable geofencing for replay and compliance reporting.
- Event alarms such as overspeed, motion, and tamper notifications to support fleet and anti theft workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the MiniFinder Zepto streams live position and OBD derived event data into your Plaspy account so teams can monitor vehicles in real time, receive alerts, and run reports. Plaspy presents this incoming data in dashboards and playback views to support operational decision making and incident review.

- Deliver real time location updates to Plaspy for live tracking dashboards and map views.
- Trigger configurable geofence and event alerts inside Plaspy for immediate notification of important incidents.
- Replay historical trips and access stored journey logs for post incident analysis and compliance reporting.
- Surface OBD sourced events such as ignition or alarm signals in Plaspy when those signals are available from the vehicle feed.
- Include trip and engine hour data in Plaspy reports to help with mileage journals and operational auditing.
- Support multi user access and reporting workflows so managers and stakeholders can work from the same tracked data.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for continuous tracking, route optimization, and driver performance review.
- Anti theft protection through discreet installation, motion and sabotage alarms, and fast alerts.
- Driver trip journals and tax compliant mileage recording using stored trip logs and replay.
- Mixed fleet oversight across cars, trucks, buses, and other vehicles to centralize telemetry in Plaspy.
- Operational reporting for maintenance planning using trip history and engine hour summaries.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MiniFinder Zepto is a practical choice for organizations and private owners looking for a discreet, vehicle powered tracker that connects directly to Plaspy for live monitoring and historical reporting. Its plug and play OBD2 design reduces the need for maintenance while the multi constellation positioning and broad cellular support help maintain consistent coverage in many regions. Feeding Zepto data into Plaspy enables unified dashboards, configurable alerts, and playback tools that simplify fleet oversight and incident analysis.

If your deployment needs more advanced integrations such as external sensors or immobilizer control, verify those specific capabilities for your vehicle and accessory choices. Zepto focuses on OBD sourced tracking and event reporting and integrates into Plaspy flows that rely on those data streams.
