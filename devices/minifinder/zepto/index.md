# MiniFinder - Zepto

![Zepto](./tracker.png)

The MiniFinder Zepto is a compact OBD2 plug-and-play GPS tracker engineered for discreet vehicle tracking and continuous real-time tracking. Plaspy-compatible out of the box, Zepto delivers precise multi-constellation positioning and constant connectivity for private cars and fleet operations. Its small form factor and internal mounting reduce the risk of discovery compared with external magnetic trackers while providing the live location and telemetry modern fleet management and anti-theft workflows require.

Designed to run from the vehicle OBD port \(10–30V DC\), the Zepto requires no user charging and preserves a continuous data stream to Plaspy or other compatible platforms. Robust GNSS performance, stored historical trips, configurable geofencing, and event alarms make Zepto a practical choice for companies and drivers who need reliable tracking, tax-compliant trip journals, and playback for post-incident analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for seamless integration into your real-time tracking dashboard and fleet management workflows.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, AGPS\) with typical outdoor accuracy of 0–5 m for precise location and telemetry.
- Plug-and-play OBD2 connection \(10–30V DC\) — continuous power from the vehicle battery and no user charging required.
- Small, internal form factor \(67 × 49 × 25 mm\) that is easy to hide for improved anti-theft protection.
- Global cellular coverage across LTE: B1/B3/B7/B8/B20/B28 and GSM: B2/B3/B5/B8 — suitable for over 190 countries.
- Event reporting and short emergency operation supported by a 170 mAh internal backup battery if vehicle power is lost.
- Feature set for fleet use: historical trip logging, geofencing, overspeed alerts, motion and sabotage alarms, and multi-user access.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the MiniFinder Zepto streams location and OBD-derived telemetry to your Plaspy account for real-time visibility, alerts, and historical reports. Plaspy-compatible integration lets fleet managers and private users see live position, replay trips, and receive configurable alarms. The Zepto’s OBD interface supplies vehicle-powered operation and the ability to capture driving events; Plaspy consumes those events and presents them as actionable insights on mobile and web.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for immediate visibility and live tracking dashboards.
- Ignition and alarm status — Plaspy can display ignition or alarm events when these signals are available through the vehicle OBD interface.
- Fuel monitoring — where fuel data is exposed by the vehicle’s OBD system or via compatible external sensors, Plaspy can incorporate fuel metrics into reports; Zepto reports engine hours and trip logs as provided by the OBD feed.
- Remote immobilizer — Plaspy supports immobilizer workflows where the tracker and vehicle support such control; Zepto is focused on OBD-sourced data and event reporting, so confirm immobilizer capability for specific use cases.
- Bluetooth sensors — Plaspy supports Bluetooth sensors when paired to compatible trackers; Zepto’s published specs do not list on-board Bluetooth, so Bluetooth sensor support depends on the broader system architecture or accessory options.

- Fleet management: continuous real-time tracking and historical trip logs for route optimization, compliance, and driver performance analysis.
- Anti-theft protection: discreet OBD installation and motion/sabotage alarms provide quick alerts to suspected theft or unwanted movement.
- Driver and tax-compliant trip journals: automatic electronic driving journal generation for mileage reporting and tax records when used with compatible platforms.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the MiniFinder Zepto as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker gives organizations and private owners a reliable, low-profile OBD solution for accurate position, continuous telemetry, and long-term reporting. The Zepto’s multi-constellation GNSS and broad cellular band support help ensure consistent coverage and high-precision location across regions, while its OBD-powered design eliminates the need for user charging and reduces maintenance overhead. For fleet management and anti-theft use cases, Zepto’s motion and sabotage alarms, geofencing, and overspeed alerts feed directly into Plaspy for fast alerts and unified dashboards.

Operational teams benefit from stored history \(up to one year\), multi-user access, and the ability to generate driving journals and engine-hour reports without extra hardware. If your deployment requires fuel monitoring, immobilizer control, or Bluetooth sensor integration, verify those specific capabilities within your broader Plaspy setup or accessory ecosystem — Zepto focuses on OBD-sourced tracking and telemetry and integrates smoothly into platforms that support these advanced workflows.
