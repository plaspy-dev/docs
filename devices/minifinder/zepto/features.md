# MiniFinder - Zepto Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the MiniFinder Zepto features and how the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect when the Zepto is integrated into Plaspy for live location, historical trips, and fleet monitoring work flows.

Exact availability of specific features can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, and how the tracker is installed. Always verify critical, deployment-specific details with the manufacturer and your device supplier before assuming a particular capability in production.

## Feature Overview

The MiniFinder Zepto is a compact OBD2 plug-and-play tracker designed for continuous real-time tracking and discreet vehicle installation. When connected to a compatible platform such as Plaspy, Zepto provides live position updates, historical trip storage, and event reporting that support operational visibility and post-event review.

- Continuous OBD2 powered operation with no user charging required for standard use
- Multi-constellation GNSS positioning for improved location accuracy and reliability
- Historical trip logging with stored trips available for replay and reporting
- Configurable geofencing and event alarms such as overspeed and motion or sabotage alerts
- Small internal form factor suited to discreet installation and reduced risk of discovery

## Core Features of MiniFinder - Zepto

- Plug-and-play OBD2 connection designed to run from vehicle 10–30V DC power
- Multi-constellation GNSS support including GPS GLONASS Galileo BeiDou and AGPS for precise positioning
- Continuous real-time tracking with stored historical trips retained for reporting and playback
- Configurable geofencing and event alarms for perimeter and behavior monitoring
- Internal backup battery for short emergency operation and event reporting if vehicle power is lost
- Global cellular coverage across LTE and GSM bands for wide regional availability
- Compact internal design to support discreet installation inside vehicles

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When Zepto devices report into Plaspy, location and event data become available in real time and as historical records for operational review and compliance reporting. Plaspy consumes the tracker data and presents it through maps, alerts, and reports that are useful for fleet managers and private users alike.

- Live location updates and map tracking for immediate visibility of vehicle position
- Trip history and replay tools for post-incident review and tax or compliance journal generation
- Geofence events and overspeed or motion alarms surfaced as configurable notifications
- Engine hours and trip log entries presented in reports when supplied by the OBD feed
- Multi-user access and role control to allow teams to view and manage tracker data

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route optimization with live tracking and historical analysis
- Anti-theft protection using discreet OBD installation combined with motion and sabotage alerts
- Tax compliant trip journals and mileage records generated from automatic trip logging
- Mixed-vehicle and equipment tracking to centralize location and utilization data
- Maintenance planning and utilization reports informed by trip history and engine hour entries

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware differences and software updates can add or change behavior; check device firmware level for exact capabilities
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect supported cellular bands and available features
- Installation method matters for signal reception and access to OBD-sourced data; professional installation can reduce variability
- Certain vehicle-derived data fields depend on the vehicle OBD implementation and may not be present on all makes or models
- Optional accessories such as OBD extension cables or adapters can affect installation flexibility and placement

Using MiniFinder Zepto with Plaspy provides a practical combination of a low-profile OBD tracker and a real-time fleet monitoring platform. The Zepto delivers continuous location, trip history, and event alarms while Plaspy aggregates that information into maps, alerts, and reports that support operational decision making, compliance, and post-incident analysis.
