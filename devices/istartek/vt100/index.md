# iStartek - VT100

![VT100](./tracker.jpg)

The VT100 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker from iStartek designed for professional fleet and anti theft applications. It combines GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity in a rugged IP66 enclosure and supports a broad input voltage range to suit many vehicle types and operating environments. The VT100 is intended to provide continuous location reporting, motion detection, offline logging and remote control features valuable for transportation and security workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT100 can feed real time location, events and status data into Plaspy dashboards and reporting. Integration with Plaspy enables operations and dispatch teams to view live maps, receive alerts for security events and generate fleet reports while taking advantage of the VT100 hardware features such as offline storage, immobilizer control and remote configuration.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker for live vehicle location and fleet dashboards.
- Dual GNSS support with GPS and BEIDOU for reliable positioning in diverse conditions.
- Rugged IP66 enclosure and wide DC input to support harsh environments and varied vehicle power systems.
- Built in motion detection and accelerometer based event reporting for collision and driver behavior awareness.
- Offline flash logging to preserve trip history when connectivity is lost and automatic upload when restored.
- Remote management features including firmware updates and configurable command options to reduce field maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the VT100 serves as a telematics endpoint that transmits location and event data to the platform for normalization, visualization and alerting. Plaspy ingests the device feed so teams can track assets in real time, review historical movement and configure event driven workflows that match operational needs.

- Real time location and telemetry appear on Plaspy maps and live monitoring screens.
- Event alerts for SOS, collision and immobilizer actions can trigger notifications and response procedures in Plaspy.
- Route history and trip replay enable post incident review and operational analysis.
- Fuel and auxiliary telemetry provided via optional sensors can be processed by Plaspy for consumption and alarm reporting.
- Offline logged positions are forwarded to Plaspy after network restoration to maintain complete trip records.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for delivery services and company vehicle oversight with live tracking and reporting.
- Public transport and school bus supervision to monitor routes and handle safety alerts.
- Taxi and ride services requiring trip telemetry, live location sharing and theft deterrence.
- Vehicle leasing and insurance programs that need mileage reports, incident telemetry and remote immobilization.
- Private car anti theft protection with SOS functionality, listen in support and remote engine cut off.

The VT100 pairs practical, field hardened hardware with Plaspy’s tracking and reporting capabilities to support a range of fleet and security workflows. Its combination of GNSS positioning, motion detection, offline logging and remote management reduces monitoring gaps and helps maintain operational continuity across vehicle fleets and dispersed assets.
