# iStartek - VT800-L

<div className="device-logo">
![VT800-L](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The VT800-L from iStartek is a professional 4G CAT4 GPS tracker engineered for demanding fleet management and vehicle anti theft deployments. It provides real time tracking and rich telemetry intended for trucks, buses, taxis, rental fleets and high value private vehicles. The device combines a dual mode GNSS receiver and a 4G communication module with local flash buffering to help preserve tracking records during connectivity gaps.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the VT800-L can feed position, status and alarm data directly into Plaspy for live dashboards, alerts and historic playback. Its design goal is to deliver continuous data flow and operational visibility, making it a practical choice when integrating hardware into Plaspy powered fleet monitoring and anti theft workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into existing fleet monitoring and tracking workflows.
- 4G CAT4 connectivity with a Quectel communication module for wide area coverage and timely data uploads.
- High precision dual mode GNSS receiver with local 128Mbit flash buffering to preserve history during signal loss.
- Rich I O and expansion options including serial ports, analog and digital inputs, and high current outputs for peripherals.
- Support for multiple temperature sensors and optional fuel monitoring to extend telemetry capabilities.
- Compact rugged form factor with wide vehicle power range and internal backup battery for uninterrupted operation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT800-L sends location, telemetry and alarm records to Plaspy over its cellular link and uses local buffering to retain history while disconnected. Plaspy ingests these records to present live tracking, alerts and reports that help operators manage fleets and respond to incidents.

- Real time location and status reporting visible on Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight.
- Alarms and geofence events forwarded to Plaspy so dispatch and security teams receive timely notifications.
- Buffered data upload after reconnection preserves trip history and supports compliance and reporting in Plaspy.
- Sensor telemetry such as temperature and optional fuel data mapped into Plaspy reports and alerts.
- Peripheral inputs like driver ID or accessory signals can be surfaced in Plaspy for driver logs and workflow integration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trucks, buses and taxis requiring continuous position tracking and route monitoring.
- Anti theft protection where tamper alerts and location updates support rapid response and recovery.
- Rental fleet monitoring with real time visibility into vehicle status and historical trip playback.
- Temperature sensitive transport monitoring using multiple sensor inputs for condition reporting.
- Fuel monitoring and consumption oversight when paired with optional sensing hardware.
