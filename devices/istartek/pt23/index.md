# iStartek - PT23

![PT23](./tracker.jpg)

The PT23 Kids Watch GPS Tracker by iStartek is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for child safety and everyday wear. Combining multi-mode positioning \(GPS/Beidou, Wi‑Fi and LBS\) with two-way voice, SOS calling and smart geo-fence alerts, the PT23 makes reliable real-time tracking and situational awareness simple for parents and administrators through SMS, mobile app and web platform access.

Built for active use, the PT23 is IP67 waterproof and lightweight \(45 x 39 x 15 mm, 40 g\) with a 1.30" 240x240 display and a 450mAh backup battery that charges via magnetic suction. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the PT23 integrates into monitoring dashboards and reporting workflows so you can get real-time location, historical routes \(up to three months\) and safety alerts without complicated setup.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for child safety: real-time tracking through app, web and SMS delivery.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS/Beidou for outdoor accuracy, Wi‑Fi for indoor improvement and LBS for fallback coverage.
- Two-way voice and voice monitoring with a dedicated SOS button for fast, direct communication in emergencies.
- IP67 waterproof construction and lightweight ergonomic design for daily wear and active use.
- Flexible reporting and alerting: time/distance/mileage triggers, smart geo-fence alerts and low-battery alarms.
- 450mAh backup battery with magnetic charging and configurable power profiles for extended standby.
- No server subscription required from iStartek—customers provide local 2G SIM calling/data and access the server at no monthly fee.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the PT23 with Plaspy gives parents and operators a streamlined monitoring experience. The watch sends location and status messages over TCP, UDP or SMS to iStartek’s server or directly to Plaspy-compatible endpoints. Plaspy ingests those messages to provide live maps, alerts and historical playback so you can respond quickly to SOS events, geo-fence breaches or routine check-ins.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via TCP/UDP or SMS for live tracking on Plaspy dashboards.
- Two-way audio and remote voice monitoring available for direct communication and situational assessment.
- SOS events: dedicated button triggers immediate alerting and call capabilities integrated into Plaspy workflows.
- Smart geo-fence alerts and historical route playback \(up to three months\) for route review and attendance verification.
- Device status telemetry \(battery level, reporting mode\) and configurable triggers \(time, distance, mileage, low-battery\) for automated notifications.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT23 paired with Plaspy offers a focused solution for child-centered tracking that balances accuracy, durability and cost-effectiveness. Its multi-mode positioning and MTK GNSS chipset deliver reliable outdoor accuracy, while Wi‑Fi and LBS provide indoor fallback so Plaspy can present comprehensive location data. Two-way voice, SOS and configurable alerts give you actionable events instead of raw coordinates, and iStartek’s server access model eliminates ongoing platform fees — you only cover local 2G SIM costs.

While the PT23 is purpose-built as a wearable GPS tracker for kids and does not include features such as fuel monitoring, ignition control or immobilizer functions, Plaspy supports a broad range of telemetry and control features for other compatible devices. That makes the PT23 a straightforward Plaspy compatible choice for organizations and families who need dependable real-time tracking, geo-fence protection, and simple integration into existing monitoring workflows.
