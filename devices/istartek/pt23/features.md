# iStartek - PT23 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the iStartek PT23 kids watch GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities can be used with Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and historical review. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about what the device does, how it communicates, and what operators and parents can expect when integrating the PT23 into Plaspy monitoring workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary with firmware level, hardware revision, installation type, regional cellular support, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific parameters such as firmware notes, supported bands in your area, or configuration steps, consult the official iStartek documentation and your device vendor.

## Feature Overview

The PT23 is a compact wearable tracker designed for child safety and daily use. It combines multi mode positioning with voice capability and emergency alerts so parents and administrators can receive timely location and status updates through Plaspy and other monitoring channels.

- Multi mode positioning using GPS and Beidou for outdoor accuracy, with Wi Fi and LBS for indoor or fallback coverage.
- Two way voice and voice monitoring with a dedicated SOS button for direct communication and rapid alerts.
- Real time tracking and alerts delivered through app, web and SMS workflows and visible inside Plaspy dashboards.
- Rugged wearable design with IP67 water resistance and a lightweight form factor suitable for everyday use.
- Flexible reporting and alerts including time distance and mileage triggers plus smart geo fence notifications and low battery alarms.
- 450 mAh backup battery with magnetic charging and configurable power profiles to balance runtime and update frequency.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT23

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for integration into monitoring dashboards and reporting workflows.
- Multi mode positioning: GPS and Beidou complemented by Wi Fi and LBS for improved coverage in mixed environments.
- Two way voice and voice monitoring with a dedicated SOS button for emergency communication.
- History playback of routes up to three months for review and attendance verification.
- Configurable reporting by time distance and mileage along with smart geo fence and low battery alarms.
- IP67 waterproof construction and compact wearable dimensions with a color display oriented for everyday wear.
- Data transmission options including TCP UDP and SMS for flexible server delivery and remote status reporting.
- Remote management model that allows server access provided by the manufacturer without mandatory ongoing platform fees

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the PT23's location and event messages feed into Plaspy monitoring so families and operators can see live positions, receive alerts, and review historical movement. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and represents incoming messages as map positions, events, and device status items.

- Live map tracking and location updates displayed in Plaspy when the device reports position data.
- SOS and alarm events surfaced as high priority notifications for quick awareness and response.
- Historical route playback up to three months for route review and verification inside Plaspy.
- Device status such as battery level and reporting mode available as telemetry so triggers and notifications can be configured.
- Geo fence events and configured triggers reflected in Plaspy alerting workflows to support attendance checks and perimeter monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and parental monitoring for daily travel to school playground and activities.
- School routing and attendance verification using geo fence alerts and route history.
- Emergency response coordination using the SOS button and two way voice for rapid situational checks.
- Short term wearable tracking for supervised outings and field trips where compact form factor is required.
- Routine check ins and remote status monitoring for caregivers and administrative staff.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets can differ between firmware versions and hardware revisions. Always check the firmware level on each device for exact behavior.
- Cellular voice and SMS functionality depend on local mobile network support and an active SIM that permits calling and data.
- Battery runtime depends on reporting interval and usage patterns. Power saving profiles and reporting intervals materially affect standby and runtime.
- Some positioning performance differences occur between outdoor GNSS, Wi Fi assisted positioning, and LBS fallback depending on local coverage.
- Manufacturer provided server access and configuration options may vary by vendor or distributor. Confirm provisioning details with iStartek or your reseller.
