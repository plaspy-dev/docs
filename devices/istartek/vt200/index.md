# iStartek - VT200

<div className="device-logo">
![VT200](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The VT200 is a rugged 2G waterproof GPS tracker from iStartek designed for reliable real time tracking and telemetry in demanding vehicle and asset applications. Built with an IP66 enclosure and multi constellation GNSS positioning, the VT200 is intended to provide persistent location data, on device buffering to handle coverage gaps, and remote device management for fleets and mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT200 can stream position fixes, buffered records, and event notifications into the Plaspy platform to enable live maps, alerts, and historical reports. Its peripheral support for cameras and in cab audio, configurable inputs and remote outputs make the VT200 a practical choice for users who want consolidated location, media, and event context within Plaspy for operational oversight and loss prevention.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking, geofencing and fleet reporting
- IP66 waterproof design and wide operating voltage for diverse vehicle installations
- Multi constellation GNSS with high positioning accuracy for reliable location data
- Quad band 2G cellular connectivity for broad coverage where 2G networks are available
- On device flash buffer and dual server upload to preserve and deliver data through blind spots
- Rich I O and peripheral support including camera and audio for event media and diagnostics
- FOTA support for remote firmware management and simplified device maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the VT200 sends location and event data to the Plaspy cloud where operators can view live positions, receive alerts, and run reports. Plaspy ingests the device stream and buffered records so fleets maintain continuity of history and can act on triggers generated by the tracker.

- Live location and telemetry visible on Plaspy maps for real time fleet oversight
- Buffered data upload ensures missing records are reconciled in Plaspy after coverage gaps
- Event triggered photo upload and two way audio provide richer incident context in Plaspy
- Configurable digital inputs map to Plaspy alerts for ignition, door or auxiliary events
- Remote output control and commands issued from Plaspy to support immobilization or other workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, route replay and operational reporting
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using continuous location streams and geofence alerts
- Asset telemetry for trailers, special equipment and high value movable assets
- Camera enabled incident documentation for claims, compliance and safety reviews
- Fuel level monitoring and oversight when paired with optional external sensors

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT200 pairs a rugged hardware design with a versatile set of telematics features that fit mixed fleet and asset deployments. Its waterproof enclosure, wide voltage tolerance and on device buffering are practical strengths for fielded vehicles and remote assets, while peripheral support expands what operators can monitor from a single device. In Plaspy, those device capabilities translate into consolidated visibility, event context, and tools for managing alerts and remote actions.
