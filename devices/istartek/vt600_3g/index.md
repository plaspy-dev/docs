# iStartek - VT600-3G

![VT600-3G](./logo.jpg)

The iStartek VT600-3G is a versatile GPS tracker designed for a wide range of applications. With its compact size and lightweight design, measuring 65mm x 61mm x 26mm and weighing only 90g, it can be easily installed in various vehicles or assets. The tracker is powered by a 10.6V - 36V power supply and features a backup battery with a capacity of 500mAh, ensuring continuous operation even in case of power loss. 

Equipped with the latest GPS SIRF-Star IV chipset, the VT600-3G offers reliable and accurate positioning. It supports various frequency bands, including UMTS/HSDPA and GSM/GPRS, allowing for seamless communication in different regions. The tracker features 20 channel all-in-view tracking, providing precise location information with a position accuracy of 10 meters. It also offers velocity accuracy of 0.1 m/s and time accuracy of 1 us synchronized to GPS time.

The VT600-3G comes with a range of useful features, including two LED lights to indicate GPS/GSM status, an SOS button for emergency situations, and multiple interfaces for digital and analog inputs. It also has a flash memory of 8MB for data storage. The tracker is compatible with various optional accessories such as a USB data cable, fuel sensor, microphone, pin switch, relay, iButton, buzzer, and temperature sensor, allowing for customization based on specific needs.

With its reliable performance, compact design, and versatile features, the iStartek VT600-3G is an excellent choice for fleet management, asset tracking, personal tracking, and other applications where real-time location monitoring is essential. Whether you need to track vehicles, monitor valuable assets, or ensure the safety of individuals, this GPS tracker provides the necessary tools and capabilities to meet your requirements.
