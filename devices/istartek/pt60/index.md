# iStartek - PT60

![PT60](./logo..jpg)

The iStartek PT60-L 4G Wireless GPS Tracker is a versatile and reliable device designed for pan-IoT applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or monitor unattended locations, this GPS tracker has you covered. With its built-in high-sensitivity GPS and 4G communication antenna, you can expect fast and accurate positioning. The PT60-L is also installation-free, making it incredibly convenient to use. It features a rechargeable 7500mAh battery, providing an ultra-long standby time of up to 3650 days. This means you can rely on the tracker for extended periods without worrying about frequent recharging.

One of the standout features of the PT60-L is its tamper alarm, which is triggered by a light sensor. This ensures that any attempts to disassemble or tamper with the device are immediately detected. Additionally, the tracker comes with a strong magnetic cover, allowing for flexible and easy installation. Whether you need to attach it to a truck, trailer, container, luggage, or even a person, the PT60-L offers a hassle-free setup. It also offers multiple working modes, including normal working mode, sports normal working mode, sports power-saving mode, timer wake-up mode, and alarm clock mode, giving you the flexibility to adapt to different tracking scenarios.

With its 4G LTE CAT1/CATM compatibility, the PT60-L ensures full network compatibility, providing reliable and seamless connectivity. It supports multiple positioning systems, including GPS, BDS, and QZSS \(Glonass optional\), ensuring accurate real-time location tracking. The device also features a 16Mb flash memory to store location data, allowing you to keep track of historical information. Additionally, the PT60-L supports FOTA \(Firmware Over-The-Air\) updates, making it quick and easy to upgrade the firmware remotely. You can also set dual servers for simultaneous data sending, providing redundancy and ensuring data reliability.

Overall, the iStartek PT60-L 4G Wireless GPS Tracker is a powerful and versatile device that offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. Whether you need to monitor vehicles, assets, or unattended locations, this tracker provides the features and functionality you need. With its long battery life, tamper alarm, and easy installation, it offers convenience and peace of mind. Choose the PT60-L for your tracking needs and experience the reliability and performance it has to offer.
