# iStartek - PT60

![PT60](./logo..jpg)

The iStartek PT60 is a compact 4G wireless GPS tracker designed for pan IoT applications. It is marketed as an installation free device for tracking vehicles, assets, and unattended locations, offering high sensitivity positioning and 4G connectivity. The PT60 includes a rechargeable 7500mAh battery claiming extended standby life, a light sensor tamper alarm, a strong magnetic cover for flexible placement, and multiple working modes to suit different reporting needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT60 can feed position and device status into fleet and asset management workflows for operational oversight. Its long standby profile, multi GNSS positioning support, and options for remote updates and dual server reporting make it a practical choice for customers who need low maintenance trackers that integrate into a centralized monitoring platform. Plaspy can take advantage of the PT60 to provide visibility, alerts, and historical playback for mixed fleets and dispersed assets.

## Key Highlights

- 4G LTE connectivity for wide area coverage and timely location reporting
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning with support for multiple satellite systems
- Rechargeable 7500mAh battery with an advertised ultra long standby capability
- Tamper detection via light sensor for added security on unattended assets
- Strong magnetic cover for easy, tool free placement on a variety of assets
- Multiple configurable working modes to balance reporting frequency and power use
- 16Mb local flash memory plus remote firmware update support for maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the PT60 delivers location updates and device status into Plaspy dashboards so teams can monitor assets and fleets from a single platform. Plaspy can use the device data to surface alerts and historical location information while leveraging the PT60s long battery life to reduce maintenance events.

- Real time and near real time location visibility in Plaspy for vehicles and assets
- Forwarded tamper alerts and device events to Plaspy notification channels
- Historical route and location playback using on device storage and server reports
- Mode aware monitoring so Plaspy can adapt reporting expectations when power saving modes are active
- Redundancy via dual server support to improve data reliability for Plaspy ingestion

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset tracking for containers, trailers, and equipment in remote locations
- Fleet visibility for trucks and service vehicles where installation free devices are preferred
- Temporary or portable asset monitoring such as rented equipment or cargo shipments
- Security monitoring of unattended sites with tamper alerting requirements
- Personal or group tracking scenarios that require an easy to place magnetic device

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT60 is suitable for organizations that prioritize low maintenance tracking and flexible placement. Its long standby claims and multiple operating modes help reduce the frequency of physical interventions, while tamper detection and magnetic mounting support common operational needs across logistics, rental, and asset management use cases. For teams using Plaspy, the PT60 provides a straightforward source of location and device state data that can be incorporated into routines for alerts, reporting, and operational oversight.
