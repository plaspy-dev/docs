# iStartek

iStartek produces compact GPS tracking hardware used in fleet, asset, and vehicle monitoring. This brand page explains how iStartek devices work with Plaspy and guides you to the available device models and documentation. Use this page to determine fit for your deployment and to navigate to specific models in the visual catalog below.

## How iStartek devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy accepts location and telematics data from a wide range of trackers using standard GNSS and cellular communication. iStartek devices that are compatible with Plaspy typically send GPS positions, status messages, and basic telemetry over mobile networks. When paired with Plaspy, iStartek hardware can report location to the Plaspy platform where you can view real time position, route history, and alerts.

Plaspy handles device ingestion, telemetry parsing, and mapping so you can focus on fleet operations rather than low level protocol issues. For device specific setup steps such as APN configuration, heartbeat intervals, or server settings, consult the device documentation available through the model pages in the catalog below.

## Why choose Plaspy for iStartek device monitoring

Plaspy provides a unified platform to manage trackers from many brands including iStartek. Key advantages include:

- Centralized device management and visual mapping
- Configurable alerts and notifications for geofence, movement, and status events
- Historical route playback and reporting for compliance and analysis
- Flexible device data handling to accommodate varied reporting intervals and message formats

Using Plaspy lets teams standardize monitoring across mixed fleets and reduces the operational overhead of dealing with each device’s native software.
