# iStartek - VT300

![VT300](./logo..jpg)

The iStartek VT300 (VT300-L variant) is an entry level 4G GPS tracker intended for vehicles such as trucks, trailers, and bicycles. It combines multi‑constellation positioning (GPS, BDS, QZSS) with a wide operating voltage range, an internal backup battery, and IP66 protection, making it a practical choice for basic vehicle and asset tracking needs. The device also supports fuel monitoring, temperature sensors, tamper alerts, and local buffering for continuity when connectivity is lost.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT300 can feed location and sensor data into Plaspy’s fleet management environment to provide live visibility, alerts, and historical reporting. Its cost-effective design and sensor options make it suitable for organizations that need straightforward tracking plus fuel and temperature insights, while Plaspy handles aggregation, visualization, and operational workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Affordable 4G tracker designed for vehicle and asset tracking across a range of vehicle types
- Multi GNSS positioning with GPS BDS and QZSS for improved location accuracy
- Supports fuel monitoring and up to eight temperature sensors for fleet and cold chain oversight
- Internal backup battery for short term operation during power loss and 16MB flash for buffer storage
- Dual server upload for redundancy and automatic tamper alarm for security events
- Rugged IP66 enclosure and wide 9–100V operating range suitable for varied vehicle environments
- Multiple I/O options to support practical integrations and monitoring needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the VT300 supplies location and sensor readings to the platform where data is normalized, stored, and presented through Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting tools. Plaspy can use the incoming data to provide operational visibility and automated notifications for events defined by your organization.

- Real-time location tracking and map visualization inside Plaspy for fleet monitoring
- Alerts and notifications for tamper events, fuel anomalies, and temperature thresholds
- Historical route reconstruction using buffered data when connectivity is restored
- Driving behavior and event summaries available for reporting and operational review
- Redundancy from dual server uploads helps maintain continuity of data displayed in Plaspy
- Aggregated fleet-level dashboards and exportable reports for analysis and compliance

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for light and heavy vehicles including trucks and trailers
- Fuel monitoring programs to detect consumption patterns or potential loss
- Cold chain transportation where temperature monitoring across multiple sensors is required
- Trailer and bicycle asset tracking where a compact low-cost tracker is preferred
- Rental fleets and logistics operations needing location visibility and basic driving behavior alerts
