# iStartek - VT202

<div className="device-logo">
![VT202](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The iStartek VT202 is a compact mini GPS tracking device designed for motorcycles, electric bicycles, and cars. It combines a built in GPS module with a flexible power input range so it can be installed in a variety of vehicles including trucks, trailers, and light two wheeled transport. The VT202 reports position data to a designated mobile phone or server, offering real time tracking and basic remote management functions suitable for personal and small fleet use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT202 can feed location and alarm data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its anti theft alarms, remote cut off capability, and multiple reporting methods make it a practical option for organizations and individuals who want to add compact hardware into their Plaspy monitoring setup without relying on device specific complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Miniature form factor suited for motorcycles, electric bicycles, and cars
- Built in GPS positioning with support for supplementary network based positioning
- Wide voltage power input for use across a variety of vehicle types
- Multiple anti theft alarms including vibration alarm, wire cut off alarm, and ACC alarm
- Remote cut off function to interrupt fuel supply for theft response
- Position reporting via SMS, mobile app, and web interfaces for flexible monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the VT202 streams location updates and alarm events into Plaspy dashboards and notification channels so teams can monitor assets in real time. Plaspy consolidates data from the device and provides tools for oversight, alerts, and historical review.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps for individual VT202 units
- Alarm forwarding so vibration, wire cut off, and ACC alerts appear in Plaspy notifications
- Fleet level visibility for monitoring multiple VT202 devices from a single interface
- Historical tracks and basic reporting in Plaspy for route review and operational analysis
- Remote command support through Plaspy to trigger device capabilities such as remote cut off where the device and deployment permit

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle and electric bicycle anti theft and recovery monitoring
- Car tracking and management for small fleets or individual vehicles
- Trailer and light asset tracking in mixed vehicle operations
- Rental vehicle oversight and remote immobilization as part of loss prevention
- Delivery or courier services needing compact trackers for bikes and light vehicles

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT202 is a practical option when you need a small, adaptable tracker that can be deployed across different vehicle types. Its broad voltage range and compact size lower the barrier to deployment across motorcycles, bicycles, cars, and light trailers, while alarm features and remote cut off provide tangible operational controls for theft mitigation.

Paired with Plaspy, the VT202 becomes part of a larger monitoring and reporting environment that centralizes location, alerts, and historical information. This combination is useful for operators who want straightforward device behavior and the platform level tools to manage visibility and response without excessive device level complexity.
