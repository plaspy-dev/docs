# iStartek - VT202

![VT202](./logo..jpg)

The iStartek VT202 is a mini GPS tracking device that offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. This compact device is designed for motorcycle and electric bicycle tracking, as well as car management. With its built-in GPS module, the VT202 can obtain precise position data and send it to a specified mobile phone or server for real-time tracking and fleet management.

One of the standout features of the VT202 is its wide range of voltage power supply, which allows it to be used in a variety of vehicles, including trucks, trailers, electric bicycles, and bicycles. This makes it a versatile choice for different tracking needs. Additionally, the VT202 offers various anti-theft alarms, such as vibration alarm, wire cut-off alarm, and ACC alarm, providing instant alerts in case of any suspicious activity.

The VT202 also comes with a remote cut-off function, which allows you to remotely stop the vehicle by breaking off the fuel connection. This can be useful in case of theft or unauthorized use of the vehicle. Furthermore, the device supports GPS and LBS positioning, ensuring accurate and reliable location tracking. It can be tracked through SMS, app, and web interfaces, providing flexibility and convenience for users.

In summary, the iStartek VT202 is a high-quality mini GPS tracking device that offers a range of features for efficient tracking and management of motorcycles, electric bicycles, and cars. Its compact size, wide voltage range, and multiple alarm functions make it a reliable choice for various tracking needs.
