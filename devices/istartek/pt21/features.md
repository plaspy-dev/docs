# iStartek - PT21 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the iStartek PT21 and how the device can be used with Plaspy. It summarizes the PT21’s practical capabilities for discreet personal tracking, pet and small asset protection, and how those capabilities are reflected in Plaspy’s real‑time monitoring and alerting workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer’s implementation. Use this page as a product feature overview and consult the manufacturer for device‑specific technical details where needed.

## Feature Overview

The PT21 is a compact, waterproof GPS tracker that combines hybrid positioning and 2G/GPRS communications to provide reliable real‑time location and event reporting for small items and personal use. Its small form factor and voice features make it suitable for situations where discreet monitoring and rapid location awareness are priorities.

- Hybrid positioning using GPS, LBS and Wi‑Fi to improve fix times and indoor/outdoor coverage.
- Real‑time tracking and telemetry reporting over 2G/GPRS with TCP, UDP or SMS transport options.
- Two‑way audio and voice monitoring plus an SOS emergency button for safety and remote checks.
- Long standby battery behavior and intelligent power management for extended deployments.
- IP67 rated rugged housing suitable for pets, luggage and exposed outdoor use.
- Geofence alerts, scheduled reporting (time, distance, mileage) and historical route playback up to three months.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT21

- Standard TCP/UDP/SMS reporting for straightforward integration with platforms like Plaspy.
- GPS plus LBS and Wi‑Fi hybrid positioning to help obtain faster and more reliable fixes.
- Built‑in two‑way audio capability for voice monitoring and communication.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button to trigger priority alerts.
- Long standby battery performance with intelligent power management for reduced maintenance.
- IP67 waterproof enclosure for use in wet or outdoor conditions.
- Geofence event generation, low battery notifications and scheduled reporting options.
- Historical route playback storage for up to three months of location history.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the PT21 is configured to report to Plaspy, its position and event messages are ingested into the Plaspy platform so you can monitor live locations, receive alerts and review historical movement. Plaspy detects the tracker protocol automatically and presents device data alongside other fleet and asset telemetry.

- Live position updates and telemetry appear on Plaspy’s map for immediate situational awareness.
- SOS events and two‑way audio occurrences can be surfaced as priority alerts tied to the device’s last known location.
- Geofence entries and exits, low battery warnings and scheduled reports are presented as actionable notifications in Plaspy.
- Historical route playback is available in Plaspy for review and audit using the device’s stored reports.
- Standard reporting formats simplify configuration so PT21 units can be added to Plaspy without custom middleware.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet tracking for dogs, cats and other small animals where discreet size and waterproofing are important.
- Personal safety and lone worker protection using the SOS button and two‑way audio for remote checks.
- Luggage and small asset protection during travel with route history and theft alert capabilities.
- Outdoor and sports tracking where a small, rugged tracker provides occasional telemetry updates.
- Lightweight equipment and tool tracking in small site or fleet contexts where low overhead is preferred.
- Short term deployments for event security or temporary asset monitoring.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware updates may add, change or refine features; check firmware release notes for specifics.
- Hardware revisions and regional model variants can affect supported bands, battery performance and accessory options.
- Placement and installation (for example antenna exposure or enclosure mounting) influence positioning accuracy and reporting reliability.
- 2G/GPRS network availability varies by region and can affect real‑time telemetry delivery and SMS fallbacks.
- Some features such as historical retention, reporting intervals and power management behaviors are configurable and can differ by configuration.
