# iStartek - VT150

![VT150](./tracker.png)

The VT150 is a compact, rugged 2G GPS tracker from iStartek designed for reliable vehicle monitoring. As a member of a proven device family, it provides continuous real time tracking with high precision GNSS position reporting, on device logging and a broad set of alarms and remote control features. Its small form factor and IP66 protection make it suitable for commercial vehicles and security focused installations.

This model is listed as compatible with Plaspy and integrates with the platform to deliver live location, telemetry and alarm events to fleet managers and dispatchers. When connected to Plaspy, the VT150's reporting and logging capabilities support operational workflows such as fuel monitoring, ignition state reporting and remote immobilization while enabling real time oversight and historical track review.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and rugged 2G GNSS tracker built for continuous vehicle monitoring.
- Dual positioning approach using GNSS and GSM base station information for improved location reliability.
- Comprehensive alarm suite including geo fence, speeding, GPS loss, low battery, door and engine status, impact and towing vibration.
- Remote control features for immobilization style security and configurable ignition reporting.
- On device logging and remote firmware update support for offline data retention and management.
- IP66 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water in vehicle environments.
- Support for optional wired accessories to extend telemetry such as fuel sensors and load sensing.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Connecting the VT150 to Plaspy enables centralized visibility of position fixes, alarm events and telemetry so teams can monitor assets in real time and act on important alerts. Plaspy visualizes incoming data from the VT150, retains historical tracks, and uses events and telemetry to power alerts and reporting for fleet operations.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for continuous tracking and route playback in Plaspy.
- Ignition status reporting and configurable upload intervals to monitor engine and vehicle use.
- Delivery of rich alarm events to Plaspy for geo fence breaches, speeding, GPS loss, impacts and other security alerts.
- Remote immobilizer style control available through the device and reflected in Plaspy for rapid response to theft or unauthorized movement.
- On device logging allows Plaspy to retrieve historical data when connectivity is intermittent, supporting accurate reports and audits.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet management and dispatch with continuous tracking and historical route analysis.
- Anti theft installations and rental vehicle protection using movement and alarm events plus remote cut off features.
- Taxi and ride hail operations for ignition and door state monitoring and driver behavior review.
- School bus and public transport safety monitoring using geo fences and incident detection.
- Lease and insurance tracking scenarios where mileage, idle reporting and fuel related alarms are needed.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT150 pairs practical durability and a well rounded telemetry and alarm set with Plaspy's visualization and reporting capabilities, making it a sensible option for operators who need dependable vehicle tracking and security features. Its compact IP66 design and on device logging help maintain continuity of data for fleets that require resilient real time monitoring and historical playback.

For organizations focused on actionable vehicle telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition state, harsh driving detection and remote immobilization, the VT150 provides the hardware features that integrate naturally with Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reporting.
