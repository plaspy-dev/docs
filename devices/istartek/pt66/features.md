# iStartek - PT66 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the iStartek PT66 4G Watch Elder GPS Tracker and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and caregiver workflows. The content focuses on non-sensitive, user-facing features that are relevant when the PT66 is used alongside Plaspy for real-time location awareness and personal safety.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, regional variant, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. For device specific details and the latest specifications consult the official iStartek documentation.

## Feature Overview

The PT66 is a wrist-worn GPS tracker designed for elder care scenarios. It combines multi-mode positioning with caregiver-focused health telemetry and safety alerts so families and care teams can maintain situational awareness and respond quickly when needed.

- Multi-mode positioning using GPS, Beidou, Wi‑Fi and LBS for frequent location updates and practical accuracy for caregiver tracking.
- Wearable health telemetry including continuous heart rate, blood pressure, sleep monitoring and step counting to support routine wellness checks.
- Immediate safety alerts such as an SOS button, fall-down alarm, geofence notifications and low-battery alerts for quick caregiver notification.
- Two-way voice and video call support plus voice monitoring and remote photo capture to verify a user’s status without a site visit.
- Long standby and magnetic fast charging for reliable daily operation in typical elder care use.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT66

- Wearable form factor with a 1.30 inch IPS color touchscreen for straightforward on-device interaction.
- Multi-mode positioning combining GNSS plus Wi‑Fi and LBS assistance for improved location reliability.
- Continuous health telemetry including heart rate and blood pressure monitoring, sleep tracking and step counting.
- Safety and alerting functions including an SOS button, fall detection alarm, geofence notifications and low-battery alerts.
- Real-time two-way communication with voice and video call support and remote photo capture for situational verification.
- Companion app support for remote monitoring and device management alongside Plaspy integration.
- IP67 rated design for everyday use in wet conditions as part of routine wear.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the PT66 is registered and reporting into Plaspy, location updates and safety or health events appear alongside other devices so caregivers and administrators can act from a single platform. Plaspy aggregates device data into maps, notifications and historical reports that improve visibility and response coordination.

- Live location updates and map tracking for multi-device oversight and caregiver routing.
- Safety alert forwarding to Plaspy for SOS, fall-down alarms, geofence breaches and low-battery events.
- Health telemetry visibility so caregivers can review heart rate, blood pressure trends, sleep and step data within Plaspy dashboards or linked workflows.
- Remote verification options such as two-way calls and remote photos referenced in Plaspy event history to reduce false alarms and confirm status.
- Consolidated reporting and event timelines to support routine check-ins and incident review across personal and organizational deployments.
