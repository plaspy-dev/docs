# iStartek - PT66

![PT66](./tracker.jpg)

The iStartek PT66 is a wearable 4G watch designed for elder care and personal safety. Packaged as a comfortable wristwatch with a 1.30 inch IPS color touchscreen and IP67 waterproofing, the PT66 offers multi mode positioning including GPS and Beidou alongside Wi Fi and network based assistance. It pairs health focused telemetry and safety alarms with everyday wearable comfort so families and care teams can keep situational awareness without intrusive hardware.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT66 is suitable for real time tracking, SOS response and remote monitoring workflows. When integrated with Plaspy the watch’s location updates, safety events and caregiver oriented telemetry appear alongside other tracked devices, enabling consolidated dashboards, geofence alerts and notifications that support coordinated responses and routine check ins.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable form factor with 1.30 inch IPS color touchscreen and IP67 waterproof rating for daily use.
- Multi mode positioning combining satellite and network based assistance for reliable location updates.
- Caregiver focused telemetry including heart rate, blood pressure, sleep monitoring and step counts.
- Immediate safety features such as an SOS button, fall detection alarm and configurable geofence alerts.
- Two way communication plus remote photo capture and voice monitoring to verify situations remotely.
- Extended standby life and convenient magnetic charging for reduced maintenance and reliable availability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once registered in Plaspy, the PT66 streams location and safety events into the platform so caregivers and managers can monitor status on live maps, receive alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy consolidates the watch data with other compatible devices to provide a unified operational view and supports notification routing for caregiver workflows.

- Live location updates and position history visible on Plaspy maps for situational awareness.
- Real time safety notifications including SOS, fall alarms, geofence breaches and low battery alerts forwarded to configured contacts.
- Health telemetry such as heart rate and sleep summaries accessible for caregiver review and routine checks.
- Remote verification features like two way calls and photo capture help validate incidents without immediate travel.
- Consolidated reporting and device management allow PT66 wearables to be tracked alongside other assets and units in Plaspy.

- Daily routine support with medication reminders, step counting and sleep tracking to help maintain wellbeing.
- Remote wellbeing verification through voice calls and photo capture for rapid assessment.
- Mixed deployments combining personal wearables with other Plaspy compatible devices for broader operational oversight.
