# iStartek - PT24 Features

This page provides a public overview of the PT24 Solar GPS Tracker and the practical feature context for using the device with Plaspy. It focuses on the capabilities that Plaspy can surface for operators, including location reporting, sensor-driven alerts, and long-duration deployment benefits for animal and remote asset monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and how the manufacturer configures each unit. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes refer to the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The PT24 is designed for extended field deployments where durable packaging, solar charging and multi-mode positioning are important. When used with Plaspy, the tracker supplies continuous location and event data that supports real-time maps, historical playback and configurable alerts for operational oversight.

- Solar charging with long life battery designed to reduce maintenance and extend time in the field.
- Multi-mode positioning using GPS, Beidou, Wi‑Fi assistance, LBS and AGPS to improve fix availability and speed.
- Rugged IP67-rated enclosure suited to outdoor and pastoral environments.
- Built-in motion and light sensing plus tamper and SIM removal detection for event-driven alerts.
- SOS, listen-in and ring to find features to support rapid response and welfare checks.
- Cellular connectivity across common 4G/3G/2G bands for wide area uplink to cloud services.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT24

- Solar charged battery designed for long-duration deployments and low standby current.
- Multi-mode positioning stack combining GNSS and network assisted methods for faster fixes.
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure for water and dust resistance in outdoor conditions.
- Motion detection via built-in G-sensor and ambient light sensing for behavioral and tamper events.
- Tamper detection and SIM removal alerts to support anti-theft monitoring.
- SOS call, listen-in and ring to find capabilities for rapid location assistance and welfare checks.
- Cellular communications on common cellular generations to report location and events.
- Designed collar or asset mount form factor intended for livestock and remote asset attachment.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location and event data from compatible PT24 units and translates those inputs into dashboards, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols so incoming telemetry can appear in the platform without manual protocol selection, and Plaspy tools convert sensor events into operational notifications and map markers.

- Real-time location updates appear on Plaspy live maps and are available for historical playback.
- Tamper and SIM removal events are routed into Plaspy alert streams for operator notification.
- Geo-fence events and configurable rules in Plaspy enable automated containment and breach workflows.
- SOS, listen-in and ring to find events can be surfaced in Plaspy to support rapid response.
- Motion and light sensor events are shown as telemetry events to help identify unusual behavior or handling.

## Typical Use Cases

- Livestock tracking and pasture management to monitor movement patterns and containment.
- Working animals and search dogs where SOS and listen-in support field safety.
- Remote asset supervision such as trailers, equipment or equipment cabinets with tamper alerts.
- Behavioral monitoring and anomaly detection using motion and light sensor events.
- Long-term field studies and research projects relying on solar charging and low-power reporting.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version and regional hardware variants can affect which telemetry and events are available on a given unit.
- Installer configuration and physical mounting can influence sensor behavior and event sensitivity.
- Cellular coverage and network band support in your region will affect reporting frequency and reliability.
- Manufacturer supplied documentation should be consulted for any safety, mounting, or firmware upgrade instructions.
- Plaspy compatibility covers cloud reception and display of supported telemetry but does not modify built in device firmware behavior.

Using the PT24 with Plaspy gives organizations centralized visibility into location and sensor events for assets and animals spread across large areas. Plaspy turns raw tracker telemetry into operational insight with live maps, configurable alerts and historical reporting tools that help teams respond faster and manage routines with less manual effort.
