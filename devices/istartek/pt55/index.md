# iStartek - PT55

![PT55](./tracker.jpg)

The PT55 is a compact GPS tracker from iStartek designed for long-term asset and personal tracking where discreet installation and low maintenance are priorities. It pairs a strong magnetic housing with reliable real-time positioning and LBS fallback, and supports multiple telemetry and alert channels. The device emphasizes multi-year standby and configurable reporting, making it suitable for applications where infrequent physical access is expected.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT55 integrates into Plaspy dashboards and workflows to provide continuous location visibility, event alerts and history playback. Its message-based reporting options and remote configuration capabilities allow Plaspy users to include the PT55 in fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and operational reporting without frequent on-site intervention.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking via UDP or SMS and presentation on web and mobile platforms.
- Designed for long standby life with marketing claims of multi-year standby at low reporting intervals.
- Compact form factor with strong magnetic mounting for discreet vehicle or asset attachment.
- GPS positioning with LBS fallback and anti-fake-LBS protection to improve location reliability in difficult environments.
- Flexible alerting including tamper and light triggers plus low battery notifications for event-driven responses.
- Remote message-based configuration and management to support larger deployments without physical recall.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the PT55 supplies position updates, telemetry and alarms that are decoded and displayed in real time for monitoring and operational response. Plaspy can ingest the tracker’s UDP or SMS messages, present location and history, and trigger notifications and automated workflows based on the device’s event signals.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates transmitted to Plaspy for dashboard display and mobile notifications.
- LBS fallback maintains location reporting where GNSS signals are limited, with protections to reduce false cell-based locations.
- Tamper and light triggered alarms along with low-battery alerts are forwarded to Plaspy to support timely response and recovery workflows.
- Configurable reporting modes let Plaspy users balance update frequency and battery life for different asset types.
- On-device historical track storage supports playback and incident reconstruction within Plaspy for up to the device supported retention.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long-term covert tracking of trailers and shipping containers where infrequent maintenance is required.
- Asset protection for high-value goods and luggage using discreet mounting and tamper alerts.
- Fleet management for intermittently used equipment that benefits from configurable reporting and long battery life.
- Historical route reconstruction and incident investigation using stored track history.
- Remote deployments in areas with variable connectivity where SMS and UDP reporting help maintain visibility.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT55 is a practical choice for organizations and individuals seeking a low-maintenance tracker that integrates into Plaspy for monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its combination of compact magnetic mounting, extended standby capability and message-based configuration supports deployments where discreet installation and minimal intervention are important. By working with Plaspy, the PT55 delivers location, event and history feeds that fit into broader fleet, asset protection and operational workflows.
