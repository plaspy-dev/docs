# iStartek - VT110-L

<div className="device-logo">
![VT110-L](./tracker.png)
</div>

The VT110-L is a compact 4G car tracking device from iStartek designed for reliable real time vehicle location and event reporting. It pairs high precision GNSS positioning with cellular base station fallback to keep telemetry flowing in varied environments. With a small form factor and vehicle grade inputs and outputs, the VT110-L provides a practical feature set for both fleet management and private vehicle monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT110-L integrates into Plaspy workflows for live visualization, alerts and remote control. Its event reporting for ignition and door status, impact and fatigue detection, and support for remote immobilizer commands align with common fleet and anti theft requirements. This compatibility makes the VT110-L a suitable option when adding tracked vehicles into Plaspy for dispatching, reporting and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for accurate real time tracking and continuous telemetry
- Multi constellations GNSS support for improved positioning in urban and rural areas
- Rich event and alarm suite including geo fence, speeding, idling, GPS loss, impact and fatigue detection
- Remote immobilizer support for fuel or electricity cut off to assist anti theft workflows
- Wide input voltage range and a built in backup battery for limited runtime on power loss
- IP66 rated compact enclosure for rugged vehicle operation and discreet installations
- Remote firmware update support and onboard flash memory for local data retention

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the VT110-L delivers positional fixes, status events and sensor driven alarms to the platform, enabling live monitoring, historical playback and automated notifications. Plaspy correlates the device data with fleet profiles and business rules so operations teams can act on location based events and telemetry.

- Real time location and telemetry updates enable dispatch and live tracking views inside Plaspy
- Digital input events such as ignition and door status support trip reporting and safety workflows
- Impact, speeding, idling and fatigue events trigger alerts and feed driver behavior analytics
- Remote immobilizer commands can be issued from Plaspy as part of anti theft response procedures
- Firmware update notifications and dual server configurations support device maintenance and delivery reliability

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live dispatch, route replay and mileage reporting across commercial vehicles
- Anti theft and theft recovery workflows using remote immobilizer and event driven alerts
- Operational safety monitoring with fatigue detection, impact alerts and door or ignition status
- Usage based insurance and lease monitoring through mileage and incident reporting
- Public transport and school bus oversight with route adherence and passenger safety notifications
