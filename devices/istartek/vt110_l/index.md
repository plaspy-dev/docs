# iStartek - VT110-L

![VT110-L](./tracker.png)

The VT110-L by VT \(model VT110-L\) is a compact 4G car tracking GPS device engineered for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and robust anti-theft protection. Designed for fleet management and private vehicle monitoring, the VT110-L combines high-precision GNSS positioning with cellular base-station \(GPS+GSM\) fallback to maintain continuous telemetry and event reporting across varied environments.

With a small form factor and vehicle-grade inputs/outputs, the VT110-L delivers rich event reporting — from ignition and door alarms to impact/vibration and fatigue driving detection — and integrates seamlessly with Plaspy for dispatch, reporting and remote control workflows such as immobilizer/fuel cut-off. Its practical feature set and remote firmware update support make it a strong choice for operational fleets, taxis, school buses and anti-theft installations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for accurate real-time tracking and continuous telemetry.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(BDS/GPS/GLONASS/QZSS\) for improved positioning in urban and rural areas.
- Comprehensive event and alarm suite — geo-fence, speeding, idling, GPS loss, impact/vibration, low-battery and more.
- Remote fuel/electricity cut-off \(immobilizer\) support for anti-theft and security workflows.
- Wide input voltage range \(DC 9–90 V\) and a built-in 120 mAh backup battery for ~30 minutes of operation on power loss.
- IP66 waterproof rating and compact form factor \(90 x 33.8 x 16.6 mm, ~54 g\) for rugged vehicle installation.
- FOTA \(firmware over-the-air\) and 16M-bit flash memory for secure data storage and remote maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VT110-L communicates vehicle position, sensor readings and status events to Plaspy over 4G networks, enabling live visualization, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes and cellular-based location data \(GPS+GSM\), correlates telemetry like mileage and heading-change, and triggers business rules or notifications based on the device’s alarms. Dual-server configuration options increase delivery reliability for mission-critical fleet management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered over LTE to Plaspy for dispatch and monitoring.
- Ignition and door status events \(configurable digital input, default ACC detection\) for trip and safety workflows.
- Impact/vibration \(towing\), speeding, idling and fatigue driving detection for driver behavior and safety analytics.
- Remote immobilizer: support for fuel/electricity cut-off commands routed through Plaspy for anti-theft response.
- Plaspy can enrich fleet data with external telemetry \(for example fuel monitoring workflows\); note that external sensors and integrations are handled by the platform when paired with compatible peripherals.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: live dispatch, mileage reporting, route replay and driver behavior analytics across buses, vans and taxis.
- Anti-theft and immobilization: remote fuel/electricity cut-off via Plaspy for stolen vehicle recovery and theft prevention.
- Commercial vehicle compliance: fatigue driving detection, idling alerts and geo-fence reporting for operational safety.
- Vehicle insurance and lease monitoring: mileage, heading-change reports and incident alarms to support usage-based insurance and lease audits.
- Public transport and school bus safety: door/engine status alerts, route adherence and impact/towing detection for passenger safety.
