# iStartek - PT28

<div className="device-logo">
![PT28](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The iStartek PT28 is a compact 4G GPS watch designed for personal safety and dependable real time tracking. It combines multi mode positioning with AGPS support to provide frequent location updates and route history, plus two way voice communication and an SOS one key emergency function. The PT28 is built as a wearable solution for individual monitoring, with a waterproof enclosure and a lightweight touchscreen form factor suitable for everyday use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT28 can forward location, event, and telemetry data directly into the Plaspy platform for monitoring, alerts, and reporting. That compatibility makes it a practical option for caregivers and managers who want transparent tracking without complex integrations, while manufacturer server arrangements can reduce recurring platform fees so users mainly cover SIM calling and data costs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for integrated real time tracking, alerts, and route history reporting.
- Multi mode positioning GPS Beidou Wi Fi and LBS with AGPS for faster fixes and improved accuracy.
- SOS one key emergency call and two way voice for direct communication and rapid response.
- IP67 waterproof design with a 1.30 inch IPS touchscreen and lightweight wearable form factor.
- Heart rate and step telemetry plus up to three months of stored route history for review.
- Built in camera and remote voice monitoring for additional situational awareness.
- Manufacturer provided server option that can reduce ongoing platform fees while SIM costs still apply.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PT28 transmits location and event data to Plaspy so the platform can display live positions, trigger alerts, and store historical tracks for analysis. Plaspy ingests the watch's location stream, SOS events, and available telemetry to support monitoring, incident response, and reporting. Registration and authorization steps connect the device feed into Plaspy and enable platform features for caregivers and operators.

- Real time location updates and telemetry visible in Plaspy dashboards and maps.
- SOS and emergency call events surfaced as alerts for immediate notification and response.
- Historical route storage and export for up to three months available for post event review.
- Remote voice monitoring and two way calls represented as event flags to aid situational assessment.
- Geo fence alerts and movement notifications to support perimeter monitoring and operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child monitoring and parental safety with SOS alerts and two way check in capability.
- Elderly care and wellbeing checks using location sharing and health telemetry.
- Outdoor activity tracking where waterproof wearable tracking and route history are valuable.
- Travel and international use with cellular connectivity for operators who need remote visibility.
- Short term personnel tracking for small teams and lone worker oversight in field work.
