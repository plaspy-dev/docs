# iStartek - PT28 Features

This page provides a public, feature‑focused summary of the iStartek PT28 and how its capabilities map into the Plaspy platform. It covers the practical, non-sensitive aspects of the PT28 that matter for monitoring, alerts, and historical route review when used with Plaspy, and explains which device functions are visible to caregivers and operators through the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and how the manufacturer enables server features. For the most current, device‑specific details refer to the official iStartek documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The PT28 is a wearable 4G GPS watch designed for personal safety and situational awareness. It combines multi‑mode positioning, communication, and basic health telemetry in a compact, waterproof package that can report locations and events into Plaspy for real‑time monitoring and historical review.

- Multi‑mode positioning using GPS, Beidou, Wi‑Fi, and LBS with AGPS assistance for faster fixes and improved tracking continuity.
- SOS one‑key emergency alerts plus two‑way voice calls and remote voice monitoring for direct incident response and situational assessment.
- Health and activity telemetry including heart rate and step counts to help caregivers track wellbeing indicators.
- Up to three months of stored route history and smart geo‑fence alerts for post‑event analysis and operational oversight.
- Durable IP67 housing and a touchscreen interface suitable for child safety, elderly care, and outdoor activities.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT28

- Real‑time location reporting via multi‑GNSS positioning (GPS + Beidou) with AGPS assistance.
- Indoor fallback positioning through Wi‑Fi and LBS to improve visibility where GNSS is weak.
- SOS one‑key emergency alerting that generates immediate notifications for caregivers and monitoring platforms.
- Two‑way voice calls and remote voice monitoring to enable live assessment and direct contact from Plaspy users.
- Health telemetry streams including heart rate and step counts for wellbeing monitoring.
- On‑device storage of route history for review, with up to about three months of retained tracks as reported by the manufacturer.
- IP67 rated enclosure and a compact touchscreen form factor for everyday wearability.
- Built‑in camera for situational snapshots where supported by the firmware and companion app.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position, event, and supported telemetry from compatible trackers and surfaces them in dashboards, alerts, and reports. The PT28 can be registered to the platform so location updates, SOS events, and available health data are visible to authorized users for monitoring and incident response.

- Real‑time location and position history are displayed in Plaspy for tracking movement and reviewing routes.
- SOS alerts and emergency call records are forwarded to Plaspy so operators receive timely notifications and context.
- Health and activity telemetry such as heart rate and step counts appear as supplementary indicators in device detail views.
- Remote voice monitoring and two‑way call events are flagged in Plaspy to support incident verification and response.
- Devices compatible with Plaspy can be pointed to the Plaspy server domain (d.plaspy.com) and configured to communicate over UDP or TCP on port 8888; Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol to simplify integration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and parental monitoring with immediate SOS alerts and two‑way communication for check‑ins.
- Elderly care and remote wellbeing checks using heart rate, steps, and remote voice monitoring to verify status.
- Outdoor activities and adventure tracking where waterproof protection and multi‑mode positioning improve reliability.
- Temporary personnel tracking or lone‑worker oversight when a wearable, easy‑to‑deploy device is preferred.
- Travel and international use where LTE/WCDMA support helps maintain connectivity roaming with a local SIM.
