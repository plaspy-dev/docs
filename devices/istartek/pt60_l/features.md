# iStartek - PT60-L Features

This page provides a public, features-focused summary of the iStartek PT60-L and how the device is used with Plaspy for asset and vehicle tracking. It is intended to describe the practical capabilities you can expect when pairing a PT60-L with the Plaspy platform and how those capabilities are typically presented to users.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical limits and the latest firmware notes, always consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The PT60-L is an installation free 4G GPS tracker built for long standby life and unattended deployments. It combines a high capacity rechargeable battery, a high sensitivity GNSS module, rugged magnetic mounting and configurable reporting modes to balance real-time visibility with extended battery life.

- Long life rechargeable battery and ultra low deep sleep current for multi month or multi year standby in timer wake up modes.
- High sensitivity L76K GNSS for GPS BeiDou and QZSS positioning, with optional GLONASS for improved coverage.
- 4G LTE cellular connectivity with regional module variants and support for periodic reporting or more frequent updates.
- Rugged, installation free design with strong magnets and internal antennas for covert attachment to trailers, containers and equipment.
- Onboard telemetry including tamper detection, driving behavior events and low battery alerts integrated into Plaspy.
- Local flash logging and dual server reporting plus FOTA support for device management and redundancy.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT60-L

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker suitable for long term unattended asset tracking.
- High sensitivity GNSS module L76K supporting GPS BeiDou QZSS and optional GLONASS.
- Large rechargeable battery with ultra low deep sleep current and multiple working modes to extend life.
- Magnetic mount and internal antennas for rugged, installation free attachment.
- Tamper detection via onboard light sensor with alarm reporting to Plaspy.
- Driving behavior event reporting such as harsh acceleration braking turning and impact noted in device telemetry.
- Local flash memory for logging and buffering when connectivity is interrupted.
- Remote firmware update capability and dual server reporting for redundancy.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a PT60-L is paired with Plaspy, the device transmits location and onboard telemetry to the platform where it is presented as location points, device status, alarms and historical events. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and presents data in dashboards and reports so teams can act on alerts and monitor assets over time.

- Configurable reporting intervals appear in Plaspy as adjustable update frequency to balance immediacy and battery consumption.
- Tamper alarms from the onboard light sensor are shown as events and can trigger notifications or workflows inside Plaspy.
- Driving behavior telemetry is recorded as events for fleet safety review and maintenance planning.
- Low battery alerts and device health indicators are available in Plaspy to help manage charging cycles and replacements.
- Local flash logging and dual server reporting increase the likelihood that Plaspy receives data after temporary connectivity loss.
- FOTA readiness enables managed updates that Plaspy can coordinate with device firmware processes where supported.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term fleet asset tracking for trailers or unpowered equipment where infrequent reporting preserves battery life.
- Anti theft monitoring using magnetic mount placement and tamper alarm notifications through Plaspy.
- Remote asset telemetry for distributed vehicles providing location and event data for operations teams.
- Seasonal or parked equipment tracking requiring months or years of standby before movement.
- Pan IoT deployments that need redundancy and remote firmware updates for widely dispersed devices.
- Situations where a low maintenance, installation free tracker is preferable to hardwired solutions.
