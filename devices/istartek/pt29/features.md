# iStartek - PT29 Features

This page provides the public feature context for using the iStartek PT29 with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical capabilities you can expect when the PT29 is integrated into the Plaspy platform, focusing on location reporting, alerts, history playback, and device management workflows that are useful for pet owners, small asset custodians, and managers.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, cellular region, and manufacturer implementation. For device-specific technical details, limitations, or the latest firmware behavior, consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The PT29 is a compact IP67 rated tracker designed for pets, small livestock and portable assets. When used with Plaspy, it delivers continuous location visibility, alerting, and historical trails while supporting practical remote management capabilities.

- Compact, lightweight and waterproof design suitable for collars and small assets for outdoor use.
- Multi mode positioning including GPS and BeiDou plus Wi Fi and LBS to improve fix reliability in mixed environments.
- 4G cellular connectivity with backward compatibility to older network generations for broad coverage.
- SOS emergency alerts combined with two way voice and remote voice monitoring for rapid response.
- Historical route playback via the platform, with device-reported history retention of up to three months.
- Remote configuration and over the air firmware updates to keep fleet behavior aligned with operational needs.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT29

- Multi mode positioning: GPS and BeiDou assisted by Wi Fi and LBS to help achieve faster and more consistent fixes.
- Cellular connectivity: 4G LTE CAT1 support with compatibility modes for broader regional networks.
- Rugged form factor: IP67 waterproof rating and compact dimensions for attachment to collars and small portable items.
- Safety communications: SOS emergency reporting and two way voice to enable direct voice checks with the device.
- Remote voice monitoring: passive listening capability for situational checks as supported by the device firmware.
- Historical tracking: stored route history accessible through the platform for recovery and review up to the period reported by the manufacturer.
- Remote management: support for OTA firmware upgrades and remote configuration to adjust reporting and behavior.
- Movement sensing: built in motion sensing reported as status or activity events to indicate movement or rest.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes the PT29 telemetry so location, status, and event data appear on live maps, in alerts, and in historical reports. The platform turns device messages into operational visibility and configurable notifications.

- Real time location updates plotted on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness and tracking.
- SOS and emergency alerts routed to Plaspy notification channels and user configured escalation paths.
- Geofence in and out alerts and rules enforced inside Plaspy for containment and automated responses.
- Historical route playback presented by Plaspy for search, recovery, and activity analysis.
- Remote device configuration and OTA firmware actions initiated through the Plaspy management interfaces where supported by the device.
- Movement and status events from the PT29 represented as activity or motion indicators in Plaspy dashboards and reports.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet containment and recovery with geofence alerts and SOS-assisted reunification.
- Monitoring of small livestock or free roaming animals where lightweight, waterproof design is required.
- Protection of portable assets and equipment with continuous position tracking and history trails.
- Emergency response coordination using SOS alerts and two way voice to verify situations and locations.
- Behavioral and activity monitoring using movement events and history playback for welfare checks.
- Short term deployments where simple activation with a Nano SIM and cellular coverage is preferred.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features such as two way voice, SOS call routing, and remote voice monitoring depend on carrier support and local cellular network capabilities.
- Historical retention and exact tracking intervals depend on device firmware settings and the configured reporting policy in Plaspy.
- Regional modem variants and network band differences may affect cellular compatibility; choose the variant intended for your deployment region.
- Integration requires an active Nano SIM and adequate network coverage for real time reporting and voice features.
- Manufacturer firmware changes, hardware revisions, or custom installations can change supported functions or default behaviors; always verify on the manufacturer site.
