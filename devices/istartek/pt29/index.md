# iStartek - PT29

<div className="device-logo">
![PT29](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The PT29 is a compact 4G Mini Pet GPS Tracker from iStartek designed for pets, small livestock and portable assets. It combines a rugged IP67 rated enclosure and a lightweight form factor with multi-mode positioning to provide continuous location visibility, SOS emergency alerts and route playback. The device is built for day to day outdoor use where reliable real-time tracking and quick response are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the PT29 is engineered to send location, telemetry and status messages into the Plaspy platform for monitoring and management. That compatibility makes the PT29 a practical choice for pet owners, asset custodians and small fleet managers who want Plaspy maps, alerts and history playback without complex integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Compact, lightweight design with IP67 waterproofing for outdoor use on collars and portable assets.
- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking, geofence alerts, history playback and remote management.
- Multi-mode positioning for improved outdoor and indoor fix performance using satellite and network aids.
- 4G cellular connectivity with backward network compatibility to support broad regional coverage.
- Safety and control features including SOS emergency alerting and two-way voice for rapid response.
- Up to three months of historical route playback available through the Plaspy platform.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the PT29 becomes a continuously reporting tracker whose location and status messages are normalized and displayed in the Plaspy interface. Plaspy plots positions on live maps, applies alert rules, stores history for reporting and enables remote device management. Activation requires an active cellular SIM and network coverage so that telemetry flows into Plaspy for immediate operational visibility.

- Real time location and telemetry updates sent into Plaspy for live map visualization and monitoring.
- SOS emergency signals routed into Plaspy notifications and configurable escalation paths.
- Two way voice and remote voice monitoring available to support on site checks and coordination.
- Geofence in and out alerts with configurable actions for containment and location-based rules.
- Historical route playback for recovery, audit and behavioral analysis retained for up to three months.
- Remote configuration and over the air updates managed through Plaspy supported channels.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet containment and recovery using geofences and instant alerts when an animal leaves a safe area.
- Activity and welfare monitoring to review movement patterns and detect changes in behavior.
- Emergency response coordination using SOS alerts and two way voice for fast recovery.
- Outdoor and small farm animal tracking where lightweight waterproof hardware is preferred.
- Small asset protection for portable equipment that benefits from continuous location visibility and trails.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT29 pairs a compact, durable tracker design with multi-mode positioning and built-in safety features that suit pet owners and managers of small assets. Its Plaspy compatibility simplifies turning device reports into actionable telemetry — maps, alerts, history playback and remote device updates — so organizations can scale from single units to larger deployments without heavy integration work.
