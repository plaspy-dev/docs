# iStartek - PT29

![PT29](./tracker.jpg)

The PT29 4G Mini Pet GPS Tracker is a compact, IP67-rated GPS tracker designed for pets, small livestock and portable assets. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the PT29 pairs multi-mode positioning \(GPS+BD, Wi‑Fi, LBS\) with 4G LTE CAT1 cellular connectivity to deliver reliable real-time tracking, SOS emergency alerts and historical route playback via SMS, a mobile app and a web platform. Its lightweight, rugged form factor and long standby battery make it an ideal choice for owners who need continuous location visibility and quick response when a pet wanders or goes missing.

Engineered for simple Plaspy integration, the PT29 sends location, telemetry and status messages over cellular networks so fleet managers, pet owners and asset custodians can monitor movement, receive geofence alerts and review up to three months of history. With two-way voice, remote voice monitoring and OTA updates, this Plaspy compatible tracker brings pet containment, anti-theft protection and on‑demand checks into a single, easy-to-deploy device.

## Key Highlights

- Compact, lightweight GPS tracker \(≈49 g\) with IP67 waterproofing — built for outdoor use on collars and small assets.
- Plaspy compatible: real-time tracking, geofence alerts, history playback and remote configuration through Plaspy-connected apps and web portals.
- Multi-mode positioning \(GPS + BeiDou + Wi‑Fi + LBS\) for fast fixes and improved indoor/outdoor accuracy.
- 4G LTE CAT1 cellular with backward compatibility to 3G/2G and multi-band variants for regional deployments.
- Safety and control features: SOS/emergency call, two-way voice, remote voice monitoring and low-battery alerts.
- Up to three months of historical route playback via the platform and remote OTA firmware upgrade support.
- High-sensitivity GNSS and 4G antennas plus internal G-sensor for movement detection and activity monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the PT29 becomes a continuously reporting GPS tracker for pets and small assets. The device publishes standardized location and status messages over cellular networks to Plaspy endpoints, where the platform normalizes the data, plots positions on live maps, triggers alerts, and stores history for reporting and playback. Integration requires a Nano SIM and network coverage; once activated, the PT29’s telemetry flows directly into Plaspy for instant visibility and automated workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for immediate map visualization and monitoring.
- SOS/emergency call and automatic alerting routed into Plaspy notifications and user-defined escalation paths.
- Two-way voice and remote voice monitoring for on‑the‑ground checks and rapid response.
- Geofence in/out alerts and configurable rules for containment or activity zones.
- Historical route playback \(up to three months\) for recovery, audit and behavioral analysis.
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware updates via Plaspy-supported management channels.

## Use Cases

- Pet containment and recovery — set geofences in Plaspy and receive instant alerts if a pet leaves the safe zone.
- Activity and welfare monitoring — use movement telemetry and history playback to assess daily activity and detect unusual behavior.
- Emergency response — SOS buttons and two-way voice help owners and responders coordinate quick recovery.
- Outdoor and farm animals — lightweight, waterproof design works for livestock and free‑roaming animals that need reliable positional reporting.
- Small asset tracking — protect equipment or portable assets with continuous GPS, geofence alerts and historical trails.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT29 delivers a focused blend of portability, multi-mode positioning and Plaspy compatibility that suits pet owners and small-asset managers who require dependable real-time tracking without bulky hardware. Its 4G connectivity and backward network support help maintain uptime across regions, while SOS, two-way voice and geofence functionality provide practical anti-theft and safety capabilities. Plaspy integration turns raw location and status messages into actionable telemetry — maps, alerts, reports and OTA device management — enabling scalable deployment for single pets or larger groups.

Note on broader telemetry: the PT29 is optimized for pet and asset location. It does not include vehicle-specific inputs such as ignition sensing or immobilizer control; however, Plaspy enables combined workflows where PT29 location data can be correlated with other telemetry sources \(fuel monitoring, ignition state, immobilizer-capable devices, or Bluetooth sensors\) in your overall fleet management or asset protection system.
