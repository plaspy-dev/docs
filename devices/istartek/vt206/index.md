# iStartek - VT206

<div className="device-logo">
![VT206](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The VT206 from iStartek is a compact GPRS hidden motorcycle GPS tracker designed primarily for motorcycles and electric bicycles while also supporting a wide vehicle voltage range that accommodates trucks, trailers, and bicycles. It combines an internal GPS antenna and an internal GSM antenna to provide position data suitable for real time tracking and basic fleet oversight. The device is light and small for discreet installation and includes features commonly used for vehicle security such as remote cut off, ACC detection, SOS emergency call, and GPS plus LBS positioning.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT206 can feed location and status data into the Plaspy platform so operators can consolidate motorcycle and small vehicle tracking alongside other fleet assets. Plaspy users can view live position, monitor ignition status and SOS events, and use platform tools for alerts and reporting while taking advantage of the VT206 hardware features such as remote cut off and input output monitoring where available.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for motorcycles and electric bicycles while supporting a broad 9 to 36 V power range suitable for various light vehicles.
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas for compact hidden installation and reliable position reporting.
- Remote cut off function for immobilization in theft or unauthorized use scenarios.
- ACC detection for monitoring ignition status and basic operational insight.
- SOS emergency button for urgent calls and rapid alerting.
- GPS plus LBS positioning and multiple interfaces for flexible monitoring via SMS app and web.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the VT206 streams location and status updates that enable oversight of motorcycle and small vehicle fleets. The device features used together with Plaspy enhance situational awareness, simplify incident response, and support routine operational reporting.

- Live location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps for real time visibility.
- Alerts and notifications for SOS events, ignition changes, and defined operational triggers.
- Remote immobilization control via the tracker remote cut off feature managed through Plaspy where supported by the device configuration.
- Historical route and event logging for post trip review and basic reporting.
- Input output signals and status markers available in the platform for operational monitoring and external control points.

## Typical Use Cases

- Theft prevention and recovery for motorcycles and electric bicycles through remote cut off and live tracking.
- Fleet management of motorcycle based delivery or courier services requiring compact, concealed tracking.
- Rental and shared scooter or bike operations that need ignition monitoring and SOS capability.
- Oversight of mixed small vehicle fleets where a small form factor tracker is preferred.
- Monitoring of trailers and light commercial vehicles when low voltage compatibility is required.
