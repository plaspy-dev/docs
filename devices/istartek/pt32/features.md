# iStartek - PT32 Features

This page provides a public, feature oriented overview of the iStartek PT32 as it is used with the Plaspy tracking platform. It focuses on user facing capabilities, practical benefits for pet tracking and recovery, and how the PT32 integrates into Plaspy for location, alerts, and history playback. The content here is intended for general guidance and SEO visibility rather than detailed configuration instructions.

Exact availability of specific features depends on the unit firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation chosen by iStartek. Differences in regional variants, firmware versions, or accessory installations can affect which functions are present or how they behave in the field.

## Feature Overview

The PT32 is a compact 4G GPS tracker designed for collar mounting and pet use. It combines multi GNSS positioning, multiple working modes for extended battery life, on device pet search alarms, and Plaspy compatibility for centralized monitoring and historical route review. The device is tailored to provide practical location awareness and activity signals useful for pet owners, shelters, and pet services.

- Compact, lightweight design and IP65 splash resistance for collar mounting and everyday pet activity.
- Multi GNSS positioning including GPS and BDS for improved satellite coverage and faster fixes.
- 4G LTE connectivity with GSM fallback for wide regional network reach and real time reporting.
- Built in pet focused alarms including vibration, sound, and light for remote pet locating.
- Intelligent power modes and a 700 mAh battery to balance operational runtime with device size.
- Historical tracking retention for replay and incident review on the Plaspy platform.

## Core Features of iStartek - PT32

- 4G LTE communication with GSM fallback for broad cellular compatibility.
- Dual or multi GNSS positioning support including GPS and BDS plus assisted positioning modes where available.
- High sensitivity GNSS module referenced by the manufacturer to improve satellite acquisition.
- Compact form factor with IP65 splash resistance and collar friendly weight and dimensions.
- 700 mAh battery with multiple working modes to extend operating time according to usage.
- Built in 3D accelerometer for movement and activity derived alerts.
- Pet focused alarms such as vibration alert, sound and light for remote pet finding.
- Magnetic fast charging and visible LED indicators for status feedback.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the PT32 location and status data to provide consolidated visibility across devices and users. When a PT32 reports to Plaspy, position updates, movement events and device state are presented on maps, timelines, and in alert channels so teams and pet owners can act quickly.

- Real time location updates and map display in Plaspy using the device GNSS reports.
- Movement and activity events surfaced as alerts or timeline entries based on accelerometer data.
- Geo fence entry and exit notifications and low battery warnings routed through Plaspy alert channels.
- Historical route playback and stored position history available for review on Plaspy for the retention period.
- Remote pet search actions such as triggering sound and light or device finder functions via Plaspy commands when supported by the device.
- Remote management and firmware update support reflected in Plaspy when FOTA is available on the PT32.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rapid pet recovery by combining real time location reporting with pet seek alarms.
- Activity monitoring to observe movement patterns and general exercise levels for pets.
- Safe zone management where shelters or owners set geo fences and receive immediate notifications.
- Multi pet or fleet style workflows for shelters and pet services that require centralized device oversight.
- Historical review for adoption records or incident investigation using stored tracking history.
- Remote locating and assisted searches using on device sound and light triggered from Plaspy.

Using the PT32 with Plaspy provides a centralized way to monitor location, movement, and device health across multiple trackers.
