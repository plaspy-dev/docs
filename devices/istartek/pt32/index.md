# iStartek - PT32

![PT32](./tracker.jpg)

The PT32 is a compact 4G GPS tracker engineered for reliable pet tracking and fast recovery, now Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your tracking workflow. Combining 4G LTE communication with GPS/BDS dual-satellite positioning and multiple positioning modes, the PT32 delivers accurate real-time tracking and activity data tailored for collars and small animals.

Designed for everyday use, the PT32 balances long battery life, ruggedness, and intelligent power modes. Its small form factor, IP65 splash resistance, magnetic fast charging, and on-device alarms \(vibration, sound and light\) make it an excellent choice for pet owners, shelters, and pet services that want dependable location updates, historical playback, and easy remote management through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for quick integration and centralized real-time tracking across devices.
- High-sensitivity GPS/BDS module \(AT6558R\) for fast satellite acquisition and stable positioning.
- 4G/LTE variants with fallback to GSM for broad regional network support.
- Compact, lightweight design \(42.2 g\) and IP65 water resistance for collar mounting and active pets.
- 700 mAh battery with intelligent low-power management and multiple working modes to extend operation.
- Pet-focused alarms: vibration alert, geo-fence notifications, low battery warning, and remote pet search via sound/light.
- Historical tracking storage up to 180 days for replay and incident review on Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PT32 streams location and device status to Plaspy over 4G \(or GSM where applicable\). Plaspy ingests GPS/BDS/GLONASS/AGPS and LBS positioning data to present real-time location, movement telemetry and alerting in dashboards, maps and reports. Integration enables geofencing, push or SMS alerts, historic route playback, and remote firmware updates when FOTA is supported by the device.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates using GPS/BDS/GLONASS/AGPS and LBS.
- Vibration and movement alerts from the built-in 3D accelerometer for activity monitoring.
- Geo-fence alerts and low battery notifications routed through Plaspy alert channels.
- Pet search features: remote ring \(device finder\), sound and light triggered via Plaspy commands or SMS.
- Historic tracking retention \(up to 180 days\) for route analysis and recovery investigations.

## Use Cases

- Real-time pet location and recovery—quickly locate lost or roaming pets through Plaspy maps and alerts.
- Activity monitoring—use accelerometer-derived movement data to monitor exercise and behavior patterns.
- Safe-zone management—create geo-fences and receive instant notifications when a pet leaves or enters defined areas.
- Shelter and service workflows—track multiple animals, retain history for adoption or medical review, and manage devices remotely.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the PT32 becomes a focused pet-tracking solution that combines precise satellite positioning, compact hardware, and platform-grade data management. Plaspy provides the central dashboard, alerting and reporting tools used by pet owners and organizations, while the PT32 supplies accurate location, activity telemetry and pet-specific alarms. Together they deliver reliable real-time tracking, long history retention, and convenient remote management via FOTA—without burdening collars with bulky hardware.

Although Plaspy also supports broader GPS tracker use cases—such as fleet management, anti-theft systems, telemetry, fuel monitoring, and devices with ignition or immobilizer inputs—the PT32 is optimized specifically for pet safety and recovery. If you need a small, rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with long-term location history, on-device pet alarms and regional 4G variants, the PT32 offers a practical, easy-to-deploy option.
