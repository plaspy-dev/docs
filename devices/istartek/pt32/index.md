# iStartek - PT32

<div className="device-logo">
![PT32](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The PT32 is a compact 4G GPS tracker engineered for reliable pet tracking and fast recovery. It combines 4G LTE communication with GPS and BDS dual-satellite positioning and multiple positioning modes to deliver accurate real-time location and activity data tailored for collars and small animals. Designed for everyday use, the device balances ruggedness and power management in a small form factor suitable for pets and mobile animal care workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT32 can integrate into centralized tracking workflows to provide live visibility, alerting and historical playback. Plaspy users can view location updates, receive pet-specific alarms, and manage device data from the platform, making the PT32 a practical choice for owners, shelters, and pet services who need dependable tracking and straightforward remote oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for quick integration and centralized real time tracking across devices
- GPS and BDS dual-satellite positioning for stable and accurate location updates
- 4G LTE communication with broader regional network support
- Compact, lightweight design and IP65 splash resistance optimized for collar mounting
- Long runtime with intelligent power modes and an on-device magnetic fast charge port
- Pet-focused alarms including vibration alert, sound and light for remote pet search
- Historical tracking retention for route playback and incident review

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PT32 streams location and device status to Plaspy so you can monitor pets on maps, configure alerts, and review historical routes from a single platform. Plaspy ingests positioning and device state information to present real-time views, automated notifications, and retained location history for analysis and recovery support.

- Real-time location updates and device status displayed on Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Movement and vibration alerts routed through Plaspy channels for immediate notification
- Geo-fence alerts and low battery notifications to help protect pets and manage devices
- Remote pet search controls to trigger sound and light on the device from Plaspy or via carrier messaging where supported
- Historic route playback and data retention for review and reporting
- Remote configuration and firmware updates when FOTA is supported by the device

## Typical Use Cases

- Rapid location and recovery of lost or roaming pets using live maps and alerts
- Activity monitoring to observe exercise and movement patterns for animal welfare
- Shelter and rescue operations tracking multiple animals and retaining adoption history
- Pet services and daycares managing device status and safe zone notifications
- Remote pet search and on-demand location checks during outings or travel

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT32 is purpose built for pet safety and recovery: its small size, splash resistance, and pet-oriented alarms make it well suited for collars and active animals. When paired with Plaspy, the device benefits from platform-grade visibility, alerting, and historical reporting so organizations and individual owners can manage location data consistently and efficiently.

Plaspy provides the central dashboard and notification tools while the PT32 supplies accurate positioning and pet-focused features. For users seeking a compact, Plaspy compatible tracker with durable hardware and long history retention, the PT32 offers a balanced option for everyday pet tracking and recovery workflows.
