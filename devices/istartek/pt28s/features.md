# iStartek - PT28S Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the iStartek PT28S GPS Tracker Watch and how its capabilities map into Plaspy for monitoring, alerting, and historical review. It is intended to describe practical functions that operators and caregivers can expect when the PT28S is used with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant and installation choices. For device specific technical details, firmware behavior, and supported functions consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The PT28S is a wearable GPS tracker watch designed for personal safety and continuous location awareness. When paired with Plaspy it streams position and safety telemetry so operators can monitor users in near real time and review incident history.

- Real time location updates using multi mode positioning for improved fix availability in varied environments
- One key SOS emergency signaling and two way voice for rapid incident escalation and verification
- Health and activity telemetry including heart rate, fall alerts and step counts for caregiver monitoring
- Durable IP67 rated wearable design suitable for everyday use and outdoor activity
- Remote firmware and parameter management plus up to three months of route playback for investigative review

## Core Features of iStartek - PT28S

- Multi mode positioning with GPS and Beidou plus Wi Fi and LBS assistance for improved coverage and fix reliability
- Global cellular support including LTE FDD and WCDMA for broad network availability
- Dedicated emergency functions including SOS one key dialing and two way voice communication
- Health and safety telemetry such as heart rate monitoring, fall detection and step counting
- Rugged wearable design with IP67 water protection and compact form factor for daily wear
- On device display and controls including a 1.30 inch IPS screen and a multifunction SOS button
- Remote management capabilities for firmware upgrades and remote parameter settings
- Historical route playback up to three months for post incident analysis

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the PT28S location and telemetry streams to present live maps, alerts and historical traces in the central dashboard. This enables operational oversight and timely responses to safety events.

- Live location markers and continuous tracking on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- SOS and emergency events surfaced as priority alerts for rapid notification and response
- Health telemetry items such as heart rate and fall alerts visible on dashboards and in event timelines
- Historical route playback and timeline review for incident reconstruction and audits
- Two way voice and remote monitoring events represented in the platform to support confirmation workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and parental monitoring with instant SOS reporting and two way voice contact
- Elderly care and remote monitoring for caregivers using heart rate and fall detection alerts
- Outdoor recreation and activity tracking where durable wearable design and GNSS reliability are needed
- Daily personal safety for commuters and lone workers who need emergency signaling and location visibility
- Integration of wearable location into broader operations dashboards to correlate personal telemetry with other assets

Pairing the PT28S with Plaspy provides a practical way to turn wearable location and safety telemetry into operational insight. Plaspy centralizes live tracking, alerting and historical playback so families, caregivers and operations teams can respond to SOS events, monitor health signals and review movement history from a single platform.
