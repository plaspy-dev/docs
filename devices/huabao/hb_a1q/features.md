# Huabao - HB-A1Q Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Huabao HB-A1Q electronic seal and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, user facing functions you can expect when the HB-A1Q is deployed and reporting into Plaspy for monitoring and incident response.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional module configuration, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical limits, installation guidance, and firmware details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The HB-A1Q is a compact, purpose built electronic seal designed for container, freight, and logistics asset protection. It pairs rugged construction with GNSS position reporting and chain integrity detection to provide continuous visibility during transit and prompt alarms on tamper events.

- Real time GNSS location reporting for continuous route and position visibility.
- Tamper and chain status monitoring with immediate alarm reporting when chain integrity is compromised.
- Ruggedized IP65 housing and stainless chain designed for maritime and long haul logistics.
- Flexible cellular connectivity options including optional 2G 3G 4G modules and eSIM for regional deployments.
- Internal battery designed for extended standby periods to suit long shipments and intermittent reporting.

## Core Features of Huabao - HB-A1Q

- Electronic seal form factor focused on cargo and container security rather than vehicle telematics.
- GNSS support including GPS and BDS for reliable positional fixes suitable for logistics tracking.
- Tamper and chain cut detection that generates immediate alerts and position reports.
- IP65 rated enclosure and stainless chain with defined traction capacity for outdoor and maritime use.
- Internal 1500 mAh battery with multi day standby performance when configured for periodic reporting.
- Optional cellular modules and eSIM support to simplify connectivity and regional roaming choices.
- Compact and lightweight design for simple installation as a reusable or single use seal.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the HB-A1Q becomes a focused telematics endpoint for tamper detection and position awareness. Device events and periodic reports are received by Plaspy and turned into live monitoring elements, alerts, and historical records that operations teams can act on.

- Real time location updates and GNSS fixes appear on Plaspy live maps for continuous tracking.
- Tamper and chain cut events generate immediate alerts in Plaspy to trigger notifications and incident workflows.
- Low battery and device status messages surface in Plaspy to help maintain operational readiness across shipments.
- Historical position and event history are available for post incident review and audit trails inside Plaspy.
- HB-A1Q seal events can be correlated with other devices in Plaspy to provide richer operational context for fleet and asset workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container monitoring and anti theft during sea and land transport using chain status and position reporting.
- Cross border and long haul logistics where eSIM and optional module choices ease regional connectivity.
- High security shipments and regulated cargo requiring tamper evidence and position history.
- Asset management for pallets, trailers, and unsupervised goods needing lightweight, reusable seals.
- Maritime shipments and port operations where IP rated protection and extended standby matter.
