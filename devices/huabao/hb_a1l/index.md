# Huabao - HB-A1L

<div className="device-logo">
![HB-A1L](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The HB-A1L Slave Electronic Lock is a rugged, Bluetooth connected slave locking device designed to provide door security and centralized management for logistics fleets. As a slave unit intended to pair with a GPS enabled master lock, the HB-A1L extends anti theft protection and access control across multi door vehicles such as oil tankers and box trucks while reporting lock status and alarm events for consolidated monitoring.

Because the HB-A1L forwards RFID reads, tamper and chain cut alarms, and battery status to a paired GPS master, it is a practical choice for operators who want lock events and access logs visible in a single fleet platform. When integrated with Plaspy through the master device, the HB-A1L contributes real time lock telemetry to Plaspy dashboards and alerts, making it relevant for fleets seeking unified tracking and security oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible integration that forwards lock status and alarms into a centralized fleet platform for real time tracking and management.
- Bluetooth 4.0PLUS slave pairing with a GPS master, allowing one master to manage multiple HB-A1L units for multi door vehicles.
- Rugged protection with IP67 waterproofing and ATEX explosion proof design suitable for hazardous cargo environments.
- Integrated RFID access supporting ISO IEC 14443 Type A and B for operator card reads and access logging.
- Tamper and chain cut detection with illegal unlock alarms to improve anti theft response times.
- Long main battery capacity with low battery alerts to support extended operation between maintenance intervals.
- Heavy duty mechanical design with flame retardant housing and strong chain options for secure cargo protection.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The HB-A1L operates as a Bluetooth slave that sends door events and security alarms to a paired HB-A1Lm GPS master. The master aggregates lock events, RFID logs and device health, couples those records with vehicle location and telemetry, and then pushes the combined data to Plaspy so operators see a unified view of security and fleet status.

- Real time location correlation — lock events are presented alongside GPS position from the master so actions can be taken with location context.
- Immediate alarm visibility — illegal unlock and chain cut alarms are forwarded to Plaspy for timely notifications and incident tracking.
- Access logging — RFID reads and operator access events appear in Plaspy for audit trails and operational oversight.
- Battery and health reporting — main battery level and low battery alerts are visible in the platform to help schedule maintenance.
- Remote unlock management — authorized remote unlock commands can be issued through the paired master and managed from the platform or app.
- Consolidated fleet telemetry — lock data is combined with vehicle telemetry available from the master for richer operational reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft protection for oil tanker trucks and other hazardous cargo vehicles requiring ATEX rated hardware.
- Multi door box trucks and refrigerated trailers where a single GPS master manages several slave locks.
- Access controlled deliveries where RFID cards grant authorized personnel door access and events are recorded.
- High value cargo protection using chain cut detection and tamper alarms to reduce loss and diversion risk.
- Maintenance planning and device health monitoring using battery level and alert data forwarded to a central platform.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the HB-A1L with Plaspy gives fleet operators a scalable way to add door level security to GPS based vehicle tracking. The slave lock keeps the device compact and cost effective while the GPS master supplies location and network connectivity, allowing Plaspy to present integrated security events, access logs, and vehicle telemetry in a single interface.

For teams focused on operational efficiency and theft prevention, the HB-A1L offers rugged physical protection, access control, and actionable alerts. Integrated through Plaspy, this combination helps reduce response times, simplify audit trails, and centralize management of multi door fleets without adding cellular hardware to every lock.
