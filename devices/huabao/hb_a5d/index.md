# Huabao - HB-A5D

![HB-A5D](./tracker.png)

The HB-A5D is a compact 4G waterproof GPS vehicle tracker from Huabao designed for private cars, motorcycles and demanding commercial fleets. It combines built-in positioning antennas, an IP65 rated enclosure and dual 2G plus 4G cellular connectivity to provide continuous location and telemetry reporting. The device aims to offer dependable real-time tracking and mobility reporting for logistics, passenger transport, rental and taxi operations.

As a Plaspy compatible unit, the HB-A5D can feed location, status events and basic sensor telemetry into the Plaspy platform for visibility and operational oversight. Its design for stable connectivity, low power consumption and remote configuration makes it a practical option for fleet managers who want to integrate devices into Plaspy for monitoring, alerts and historical reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker providing real-time tracking and telemetry for fleet management and asset protection.
- IP65 rated waterproof housing suitable for vehicle and outdoor use.
- Dual cellular connectivity with 2G and 4G support for broad coverage and steady reporting.
- GPS and BDS positioning with reported high sensitivity and typical cold start performance for reliable location fixes.
- Vehicle inputs and relay output for ignition detection and remote immobilizer style control when configured.
- Built-in backup battery to preserve last known location on main power loss and support short standby operation.
- Remote maintenance including OTA updates and configuration via GPRS or SMS to simplify large scale deployment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the HB-A5D transmits position data, status events and sensor readings that Plaspy processes into map views, alert notifications and historical reports. Plaspy translates the incoming telemetry into operational insights and configurable alerts so teams can monitor vehicles, respond to exceptions and analyze activity over time.

- Real-time location and movement updates appear on Plaspy live maps and dashboards for immediate situational awareness.
- Ignition state and power loss events are shown as discrete events in Plaspy to support anti-theft workflows and rapid response.
- Alarms such as overspeed, geofence entry and exit, and low power are delivered to Plaspy for notification and escalation.
- External sensor telemetry for fuel or temperature can be received and trended in Plaspy for operational monitoring.
- Relay control supported by the device can be used within Plaspy command workflows for authorized remote actions.
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware updates reduce the need for physical intervention when managing distributed fleets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and vehicle recovery where real-time alerts and remote immobilizer control improve security.
- Passenger transport and long distance bus fleets requiring continuous tracking, mileage reporting and overspeed monitoring.
- Logistics and hazardous goods transport that need rugged tracking hardware with waterproof protection and reliable connectivity.
- Rental and taxi services that rely on ignition detection, geofencing and historical routes for billing and dispute resolution.
- Fuel monitoring and temperature sensitive cargo oversight using external sensor inputs for telemetry and reporting.
