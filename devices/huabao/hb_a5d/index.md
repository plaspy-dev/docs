# Huabao - HB-A5D

![HB-A5D](./tracker.png)

The HB-A5D 4G Waterproof GPS Vehicle Tracker by HB is a Plaspy compatible, easy-to-install telematics unit designed for private cars, motorcycles and demanding commercial fleets. With dual 2G+4G network support, built-in GPS/GSM antennas and IP65 waterproof protection, the HB-A5D delivers reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for logistics, passenger transport, hazardous goods, rental and taxi operations.

Engineered for low power consumption and stable connectivity, the HB-A5D pairs with Plaspy to provide fleet management, anti-theft measures and fuel monitoring in a compact package. Remote configuration via GPRS or SMS and OTA updates simplify deployment and maintenance for large or distributed vehicle fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering real-time tracking and telemetry for fleet management and asset protection.
- IP65 waterproof enclosure and compact form \(85 × 50 × 16 mm, 65 g\) for vehicle and outdoor use.
- Dual network connectivity: 2G \(900/1800MHz\) plus 4G LTE bands for broad cellular coverage and stable reporting.
- Built-in GPS/BDS positioning with high sensitivity \(capture -148 dBm, track -162 dBm\) and typical cold start ~32 s.
- Vehicle inputs and controls: ACC detection input, relay output for remote fuel cut-off/immobilizer-style control, and IO/AD for temperature or fuel sensors.
- On-board backup battery \(80 mAh\) with approximately 30 minutes standby to preserve last-known location on power loss.
- Key alarms and reports: overspeed, geo-fencing, mileage calculation, power-off and low-power alarms for anti-theft and operational safety.
- Remote maintenance via OTA updates plus micro USB and SMS/GPRS configuration for flexible provisioning.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the HB-A5D streams location and vehicle telemetry in real time to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy ingests GPS/BDS position data, status events and sensor inputs from the tracker and translates them into live maps, alerts and historical reports suitable for fleet managers and operations teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: Plaspy receives periodic GPS/BDS positions and movement metrics for live tracking dashboards.
- Ignition and power events: ACC detection plus power-off and low-power alarms appear as immediate events in Plaspy for anti-theft monitoring.
- Alarms and geofence events: Overspeed, geo-fence entry/exit and power-off alarms are forwarded to Plaspy for push/email/SMS alerts.
- Fuel and temperature telemetry: IO/AD connections accept external fuel and temperature sensors; sensor readings can be displayed and trended in Plaspy.
- Remote control: Relay output enables remote fuel cut-off \(immobilizer-style action\) through Plaspy command workflows when configured and authorized.
- Remote configuration and maintenance: OTA updates plus GPRS/SMS configuration keep device firmware and settings synchronized without physical access.

- Long-distance passenger and bus fleets: continuous real-time tracking, mileage calculation and overspeed monitoring for safety and compliance.
