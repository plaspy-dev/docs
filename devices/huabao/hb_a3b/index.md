# Huabao - HB-A3B

![HB-A3B](./tracker.jpg)

The HB-A3B is a vehicle grade GPS tracker and control unit designed for deployments that require enforced speed control, compliance logging, and reliable real time tracking. The unit combines GNSS positioning with cellular communications and dedicated local control outputs to detect overspeed events, signal alarms, record trips, and drive active interventions such as relay or drive-by-wire speed limiting. It is built to support commercial fleets, passenger transport and mandated government installations where auditability and operational control are primary concerns.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the HB-A3B supplies the platform with continuous location updates, event alerts, and buffered records from communication blind zones. When integrated with Plaspy, the device’s telemetry and control capabilities can be surfaced in centralized dashboards, automated alerting workflows, and compliance reports, enabling fleet managers and regulators to monitor behavior and coordinate enforcement without altering existing vehicle architectures.

## Key Highlights

- Vehicle grade design with support for enforced speed limiting and governor functions for compliance focused deployments.
- GNSS positioning combined with cellular communications for real time location and event reporting.
- Active control outputs for relay or mechanical valve actions to implement fuel cut or speed limiting measures.
- Comprehensive event detection including ignition state, overspeed, SOS input, antenna cut and power tamper alarms.
- Onboard trip logging and offline buffering to retain driving records through coverage gaps for later upload.
- Optional fuel monitoring and printer support for fleets that require consumption tracking and hard copy trip logs.
- Rugged hardware tolerances suitable for commercial vehicle environments and extended operating temperatures.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the HB-A3B delivers location fixes, state changes, and alarm events to the platform so teams can monitor vehicles in real time, replay histories, and produce compliance evidence. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry to trigger notifications, populate dashboards, and keep audit trails consistent even when vehicles move through areas without coverage.

- Real time location and telemetry updates are displayed on Plaspy maps and used for historical playback and route analysis.
- Ignition and driving state monitoring help correlate vehicle usage, duty cycles and driver behavior inside Plaspy reports.
- Overspeed, SOS, antenna cut, and power tamper alarms generate immediate alerts for operational response and incident logging.
- Buffered storage on the device ensures Plaspy receives complete trip data after vehicles return to coverage, preserving audit continuity.
- Fuel monitoring data from an optional sensor can be forwarded to Plaspy for consumption analysis and theft detection workflows.
- Speed limiting actions implemented by the device can be coordinated with Plaspy workflows to support remote enforcement and recovery processes.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and speed governance for buses, coaches and commercial trucks that require auditable speed control.
- Government regulated vehicle installations where mandated speed limiter requirements must be enforced and recorded.
- Rental, logistics and long haul operations that need overspeed mitigation, trip records for billing, and data retention through remote areas.
- Anti-theft and recovery workflows that rely on SOS inputs and tamper alarms to trigger coordinated immobilizer actions.
- Fuel monitoring and operational cost control programs using optional sensors to feed consumption telemetry for analysis.
