# Huabao - HB-A1Lm Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Huabao HB-A1Lm and how it functions when used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive descriptions of the device capabilities that are relevant for fleet managers, logistics operators, and security teams evaluating Plaspy compatibility and operational fit.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Details such as optional modules, region specific cellular variants, power management settings, and multi‑door configurations may differ between units and projects, so this page describes the common, publicly documented capabilities of the model as a guide for Plaspy users.

## Feature Overview

The HB-A1Lm is a heavy duty electronic lock and GPS tracker designed for containers, trailers, flatbeds and box trucks. When paired with Plaspy, the device provides location awareness, security event reporting, and remote unlock auditing to support anti-theft and logistics workflows.

- Real time GPS and BDS positioning for continuous location awareness in Plaspy
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and flame retardant housing for outdoor and transport use
- Multiple unlock methods including RFID and Bluetooth app with audit logging
- Comprehensive anti theft alarms such as chain cut alarm and tamper detection
- Large 15,200 mAh battery with multi week standby behavior and low battery management
- Expandable multi door support with slave lock pairing for synchronized access control

## Core Features of Huabao - HB-A1Lm

- GPS and BDS positioning for location tracking and route visibility
- Multi network cellular connectivity with 2G 3G and 4G variants depending on region
- Rugged IP67 protection and flame retardant PC plus fiberglass housing for harsh environments
- Large 15,200 mAh battery with reported standby durations up to 28 days under example reporting intervals
- Anti theft alarms including chain cut detection, tamper or dismantle alerts, and low battery notifications
- Multiple unlock methods supported: RFID, Bluetooth app, SMS, and platform GPRS commands
- Expandable architecture that pairs with up to seven slave locks for multi door or compartment control
- Bluetooth sensor support for temperature and humidity telemetry and optional LoRa for special deployments

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the HB-A1Lm is integrated into Plaspy, the device becomes a tracked security node in your fleet ecosystem. Plaspy receives location, status, and event data so operations and security teams can monitor assets and respond to alerts from a central platform.

- Real time location and telemetry updates flow into Plaspy for continuous asset visibility and route monitoring
- Security events such as illegal unlock, tamper/dismantle and chain cut alarms are forwarded to Plaspy as platform alerts
- Battery and power state reporting including low battery notifications and documented sleep behavior are visible in device status
- Unlock events from RFID, Bluetooth, platform GPRS, and SMS are recorded centrally for audit and access control workflows
- Bluetooth sensor readings for temperature and humidity are available in Plaspy dashboards when those sensors are paired
- Slave lock status and multi door event logs allow synchronized monitoring and consolidated event history in the platform

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer anti theft where chain cut and tamper alarms trigger platform alerts and location tracking
- Multi door box truck or tanker operations that require a main lock paired with multiple slave locks for coordinated access
- Refrigerated shipments using Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors to feed telemetry into Plaspy dashboards
- Customs inspections and high value cargo where RFID and platform unlock records support audit and chain of custody
- Remote fleet operations needing consolidated location, event history and remote unlock control from a single console
- Long haul logistics requiring rugged enclosure and long battery life for intermittent reporting intervals

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets depend on firmware version and the specific hardware revision shipped for a project
- Regional variants may include different cellular modules and optional features such as LoRa or explosion proof versions
- Certain behaviors such as sleep thresholds and which unlock methods remain available at critically low battery depend on device firmware and configuration
- Installation type and wiring can affect which inputs and external sensors are usable for a given deployment
- Plaspy can automatically detect many common tracker protocols and will surface available telemetry in the platform once the device is connected
