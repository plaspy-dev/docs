# Autoseeker - AT-14 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Autoseeker AT-14 and explains how those features relate to use with Plaspy. It is intended to help fleet managers, logistics teams, and technical evaluators understand the practical capabilities of the device when paired with Plaspy for location monitoring and asset oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary with firmware version, hardware revision, method of installation, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific parameters and configuration details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The Autoseeker AT-14 is a compact wireless mini GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset tracking where long standby life and secure mounting are important. It is promoted for use on vehicles, trucks, containers, and industrial equipment, with an emphasis on simple placement, continuous location reporting, and long term deployment.

- Compact portable form factor suited for covert or temporary mounting on vehicles and assets
- Powerful magnetic attachment with strong pull for reliable physical mounting to metal surfaces
- Long standby performance for extended reporting intervals and reduced servicing
- 2G based location reporting for wide compatibility with legacy cellular networks
- Intended use cases include vehicle fleets, cargo containers, construction equipment, and general asset monitoring

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-14

- Wireless mini GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset applications
- Long time standby operation to minimize the need for frequent recharging
- High strength magnetic mount rated by the manufacturer for strong attachment to metal surfaces
- 2G cellular connectivity for location reporting and movement updates
- Compact and portable design for flexible installation and repositioning
- Suitable for mounting on vehicles, trucks, containers, and similar assets

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides location visualization and operational oversight for compatible GPS trackers like the AT-14. When the device reports position data, Plaspy integrates that information into maps, history, and status dashboards to support monitoring and fleet workflows.

- Live and last known location displayed on Plaspy maps for asset tracking
- Historical position history accessible in Plaspy to review movement over time
- Online status and last report times in Plaspy help identify devices in long standby or awaiting a next transmission
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocol and presents incoming positions in the platform
- Practical support for trackers that use legacy cellular networks so devices like the AT-14 can report into Plaspy servers

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring trucks and service vehicles for location awareness and routing
- Tracking cargo containers during storage and transit
- Managing equipment and machinery location on construction or industrial sites
- Temporary or covert asset tracking where a small magnetic unit is appropriate
- Long term deployments where minimized maintenance and long standby are required
