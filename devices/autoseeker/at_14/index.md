# Autoseeker - AT-14

<div className="device-logo">
![AT-14](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The Autoseeker AT-14 is a compact wireless mini GPS tracker designed for vehicles, trucks, and containers. Its specification emphasizes a long standby time for extended deployments and a high strength magnetic mount rated at 300 pounds of pull, making it suitable for a wide range of mobile and fixed asset mounting scenarios. The device includes GPS location reporting and uses 2G connectivity for location updates, and its portable form factor is intended to minimize installation complexity for temporary or semi-permanent use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-14 can be integrated into Plaspy fleet and asset management workflows to provide location visibility and operational oversight. When paired with Plaspy, this tracker helps organizations monitor movements, maintain location history, and get timely alerts related to asset position and status. Its long standby capability and strong magnetic mounting make it a practical choice for assets that require low maintenance and secure attachment while being tracked through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact wireless mini GPS tracker suitable for vehicles trucks and containers
- Strong magnetic mounting with approximately 300 pounds pull for secure attachment
- Long standby time designed for extended deployments and reduced maintenance
- GPS based location reporting with 2G connectivity for real time updates
- Portable and easy to place on a variety of assets including industrial equipment and cargo
- Practical choice for long term asset monitoring where continuous tracking is needed

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT-14 can be used with Plaspy to bring location data into a centralized tracking platform for visibility and operational control. Plaspy ingests location updates from compatible trackers and presents them alongside other fleet and asset information so teams can monitor and manage resources efficiently.

- Live location visualization and historical position playback for individual assets
- Grouping and fleet overview to monitor multiple AT-14 units from a single dashboard
- Geofence and movement alerts to notify teams when assets enter or leave defined areas
- Reporting and exportable activity logs for operational analysis and audit trails
- Online and battery status indicators in Plaspy to help prioritize device maintenance
