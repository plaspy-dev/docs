# Autoseeker - AT-24

![AT-24](./tracker.jpg)

The AT-24 is a compact 4G GPS tracker designed for reliable real time tracking of vehicles and mobile assets. Built for permanent, discreet installation in protected vehicle locations such as under dashboards or beneath seats, the AT-24 combines cellular connectivity with a wide operating voltage range to support cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats and other fleet assets. Its small form factor and modular wiring help make installations quick and unobtrusive while keeping the unit suited for routine fleet use and security monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-24 supplies the positional and event data that Plaspy needs to deliver fleet visibility, alerts and reports. With features like ignition detection, geofencing, remote immobilization and a backup battery for continued reporting during power loss or tampering, the AT-24 is a practical option for fleets and owners who want continuous tracking and the ability to act on security events through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real time tracking and fleet management across a range of vehicle types.
- Wide operating voltage range suited to cars, commercial trucks, motorcycles and small marine vessels.
- Internal backup battery for continued reporting during power loss or tampering, improving anti theft response.
- Ignition detection and remote fuel cut immobilizer capability to support ignition based reporting and secure immobilization.
- Geofencing and event alert support to drive operational alerts and compliance workflows in management software.
- Compact and discreet form factor with modular wiring for reliable permanent installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AT-24 provides continuous position and event telemetry that Plaspy ingests and displays via dashboards, alerts and reports tailored for fleet operations. Plaspy translates tracker events into actionable notifications and historical records so teams can monitor vehicle status, respond to incidents and analyze fleet performance.

- Real time location updates and event feeds available inside Plaspy for visibility across assets.
- Ignition on and off events feed trip logs and driver activity reports within the Plaspy platform.
- Remote immobilizer events and tamper reports enable coordinated anti theft response and command actions where permitted.
- Geofence breaches and overspeed events trigger notifications and create audit trails for incident review.
- Low battery and power loss alerts improve continuity of data and help prioritize asset checks and maintenance.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking for continuous visibility, route auditing and driver event reporting.
- Anti theft protection for personal vehicles and commercial assets using covert installation and remote immobilization.
- Motorcycle and small boat tracking where compact size and a wide voltage range are important.
- Logistics and delivery monitoring using geofencing and historical telemetry to improve accountability.
