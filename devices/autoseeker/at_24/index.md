# Autoseeker - AT-24

![AT-24](./tracker.jpg)

The AT-24 is a compact 4G GPS tracker engineered for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking of vehicles and mobile assets. Designed for permanent installation in protected vehicle areas — under dashboards or beneath seats — the AT-24 combines robust cellular connectivity with a wide operating voltage range to serve cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats and other fleet and logistics assets. Its small form factor and modular wiring make installation quick and discreet while enabling full fleet management and anti-theft workflows when paired with Plaspy.

With built-in telemetry reporting, ACC \(ignition\) detection, geo-fencing and remote fuel cut \(immobilizer\) capability, the AT-24 supports the operational controls fleets need and the security features owners expect. A 300 mAh backup battery keeps the tracker reporting through power loss or tampering, improving anti-theft response and uninterrupted position updates for event-driven alerts and historical reporting on the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management across vehicles and mobile assets.
- Wide 9–95V operating voltage supports cars, commercial trucks, motorcycles and marine installations.
- Built-in 300 mAh backup battery provides continued reporting during power loss or tampering for improved anti-theft protection.
- Remote fuel cut \(immobilizer\) and ACC on/off detection enable secure remote immobilization and ignition-based reporting.
- Supports SMS/GPS/GSM/GPRS \(TCP\) reporting plus geofence, overspeed and low-battery alerts for operational safety and compliance.
- Compact, covert form factor \(79 × 33 × 16 mm\) with modular wiring for fast, reliable installations.
- Optional features include remote voice monitoring and SOS emergency button for enhanced safety workflows when required.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT-24 streams position and event data to the Plaspy platform using its cellular data connectivity and standard reporting protocols. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s telemetry and presents real-time location, geofence events, overspeed alarms and status changes through dashboards, alerts and reports tailored for fleet operations and theft response.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via 4G/LTE and fallback GSM reporting.
- ACC \(ignition\) on/off detection and alarms feed ignition-based reporting and driver/event logging in Plaspy.
- Remote immobilizer capability \(fuel cut\) provides immediate anti-theft action and can be controlled through Plaspy where policy and local law permit.
- Geofence and overspeed alerts trigger Plaspy notifications and audit trails for compliance and operational oversight.
- Low-battery and power-loss reports \(backed by the 300 mAh internal battery\) improve continuity of data during tampering or accidental disconnection.
