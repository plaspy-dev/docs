# Autoseeker - AT-3

<div className="device-logo">
![AT-3](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Autoseeker AT-3 is a compact, weatherproof GPS pet tracker built for reliable outdoor tracking of dogs and other animals. Designed with multi-band cellular connectivity and low-power operation, the AT-3 provides continuous position updates and basic retrieval aids such as a buzzer and LED to help locate animals in the field. Its rugged ABS housing and collar or harness form factor make it suitable for routine walks, active outings, and shelter use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-3 sends location and status telemetry to the Plaspy platform for live maps, configurable alerts, and historical route playback. Integration with Plaspy lets owners and animal-care teams monitor animals in real time, receive geofence and low-battery notifications, and review movement trails for post-event analysis, all within a centralized tracking workflow.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking over 4G LTE Cat-1 for continuous location updates and timely telemetry.
- Long standby performance with a 700mAh rechargeable cell that supports extended use between charges.
- IP67 rated ABS enclosure for water and dust resistance in outdoor environments.
- Built-in buzzer and LED retrieval aids to assist in locating a pet from the Plaspy interface.
- Configurable geo-fence alarms and low-battery notifications visible in Plaspy dashboards.
- Compact, lightweight design intended for collar or harness mounting and everyday wearability.
- History playback available in Plaspy for reviewing routes and events after an outing.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy, the AT-3 transmits GNSS position fixes and device status over cellular networks to Plaspy’s servers. Plaspy displays location points on maps and surfaces relevant events and alerts so caretakers can act quickly. This combination of device-side tracking and Plaspy platform services supports continuous visibility and operational oversight.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring.
- Geo-fence entry and exit events are forwarded to Plaspy for instant notifications to caretakers.
- Remote activation of the buzzer and LED can be triggered from Plaspy to aid in retrieval.
- History playback and route review in Plaspy let users analyze past movements and incidents.
- Low-battery alerts are sent to Plaspy so teams can schedule recharges and reduce downtime.
- Device status and event flags integrate into Plaspy dashboards and reporting for centralized management.

## Typical Use Cases

- Daily walks and outdoor recreation where live location and retrieval aids increase pet safety.

- Veterinary field services and rescue operations requiring a compact, weatherproof tracker.
- Off-leash and free roaming scenarios where geo-fence alerts provide automated boundary notifications.
