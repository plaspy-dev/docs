# Autoseeker - AT-3 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Autoseeker AT-3 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for pet tracking and management. It summarizes practical functions you can expect when the AT-3 is registered and monitored through the Plaspy platform, focusing on real world value such as live maps, alerts, and history playback.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes before making operational decisions.

## Feature Overview

The AT-3 is a compact, weatherproof pet tracker optimized for collar or harness mounting and intended for continuous location awareness and retrieval assistance. Designed for low power consumption and broad cellular coverage, it provides position updates and status telemetry that integrate into Plaspy for central monitoring and alerting.

- Real time GPS tracking compatible with Plaspy for live location on maps and dashboards
- Geo fence alarms and low battery notifications delivered to Plaspy for proactive alerts
- Built in buzzer and LED for retrieval cues that can be remotely activated via Plaspy
- Rugged IP67 ABS enclosure and compact form factor for collar or harness mounting during outdoor use
- Rechargeable battery with extended standby life to support multi day use between charges
- History playback of routes and events inside Plaspy for review and training purposes

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-3

- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with multi band support for broad coverage
- Real time position reporting for low latency updates in Plaspy
- Geo fence detection and alarm generation for boundary monitoring
- Low battery alerts to signal when recharging is needed
- Built in buzzer and LED light for audible and visual retrieval assistance
- IP67 rated ABS enclosure for water and dust resistance during outdoor activity
- Compact, lightweight design intended for collar or harness mounting with magnetic charging

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an AT-3 is registered with Plaspy, location fixes and device status are visible within Plaspy maps and monitoring tools. Plaspy receives the device telemetry and presents it as live points, event flags, and historical trails to help caretakers maintain situational awareness without needing to parse raw device messages.

- Live map tracking and status indicators for immediate visibility of pet location
- Geo fence events appear as notifications so caretakers are alerted when a pet crosses set boundaries
- Low battery notifications are forwarded to Plaspy for scheduling recharges and avoiding downtime
- Remote activation of the buzzer and LED from Plaspy to assist in locating a hidden or lost animal
- Historical route playback and event timelines for post event review and training documentation

## Typical Use Cases

- Daily walks and outdoor recreation where live location and retrieval aids reduce search time
- Training and performance review for working or sporting dogs through route playback
- Shelters and pet care facilities managing multiple animals and needing consolidated monitoring
- Field operations and rescues where a compact weatherproof tracker supports transport and search tasks
- Off leash or free roaming monitoring with geo fence alerts for boundary breaches
