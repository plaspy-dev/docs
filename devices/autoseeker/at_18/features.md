# Autoseeker - AT-18 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Autoseeker AT-18 tracker with the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, explains how the device typically reports location and events, and highlights the kinds of operational value you can expect when the AT-18 is paired with Plaspy for centralized monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. Wherever possible this page uses the AT-18 description as the factual basis, but you should verify model specifics with the manufacturer for critical deployments.

## Feature Overview

The Autoseeker AT-18 is a compact, waterproof GPS tracker intended for discreet permanent installations in vehicles and mobile assets. It provides real time location reporting and a set of configurable alarms that are useful for fleet management and asset protection, and it is designed to integrate with Plaspy out of the box.

- Real time GPRS reporting with SMS fallback to preserve alerts when packet data is unavailable.
- Compact waterproof enclosure for covert or protected permanent installation on vehicles, motorcycles, and marine craft.
- Configurable alarms for ignition events, overspeed, low battery, and geofence breaches.
- ACC on/off detection for trip and ignition reporting to support usage and duty cycle visibility.
- Wide input voltage support and onboard backup battery for sustained operation across vehicle types.

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-18

- Native compatibility with Plaspy for centralized tracking and dashboard visibility.
- GPRS TCP based real time position reporting with SMS as a secondary reporting channel.
- Configurable geofence alerts to detect perimeter entry and exit events.
- Overspeed alarm configuration for speed based notifications and logging.
- ACC ignition status detection to mark trip start and stop events.
- Low battery alerting to support proactive maintenance and battery health monitoring.
- Compact, waterproof housing suitable for concealed permanent installations.
- High sensitivity GNSS reception for consistent fixes in typical vehicle placements.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an AT-18 is connected to Plaspy, the device's position updates and configured alarms are presented through Plaspy's monitoring and reporting features. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and surfaces important events to operators without requiring deep device level configuration.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and route visibility.
- Geofence, overspeed, and low battery alarms are forwarded to Plaspy for notification, logging, and historical review.
- ACC on/off reports are used by Plaspy to identify trips, display ignition status, and support utilization reports.
- SMS fallback events can be reflected as alerts in Plaspy when packet reporting is interrupted and the tracker falls back to SMS.
- Device status and recent telemetry are available in Plaspy to support troubleshooting and operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, vans, and trucks requiring position visibility and ignition based trip detection.
- Covert anti theft monitoring with discreet permanent installation and timely geofence or overspeed alerts.
- Motorcycle and small vehicle protection using compact form factor placements under seats or panels.
- Marine asset tracking where a waterproof enclosure enables reliable placement on boats and small craft.
- Permanent asset monitoring for trailers, equipment, and fixed mobile infrastructure.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Functionality depends on the device firmware and hardware revision; not all features may be present on every unit.
- Installation choices affect performance and detectability; placement under metal or inside sealed enclosures can influence GNSS reception.
- SMS fallback relies on GSM network availability and operator support in the region where the device is installed.
- Regional module variants may differ in cellular band support and should be confirmed for the intended deployment area.
- For exact parameter names, alarm configuration options, and wiring details consult the Autoseeker documentation.

Pairing the AT-18 with Plaspy provides a practical way to centralize location feeds, alarms, and historical records across a mixed fleet of vehicles and assets. Plaspy aggregates the tracker reports into map views, event notifications, and reporting tools that help operations teams respond to incidents, analyze utilization, and maintain asset security.
