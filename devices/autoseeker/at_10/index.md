# Autoseeker - AT-10

![AT-10](./Logo.png)

The Autoseeker AT-10 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed to provide real time tracking and monitoring for cars and fleets. It supports 4G LTE connectivity for timely location reporting and includes features for trip history and mileage logging. The AT-10 also offers a broad set of alarm reporting capabilities such as SOS alerts, geo fence notifications, external power cut alerts, and low battery warnings to help maintain vehicle security and operational awareness.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-10 can feed its location and alarm data into the Plaspy platform to consolidate tracking, reporting, and alerts in a single dashboard. That compatibility makes the AT-10 a practical choice for organizations that want to combine reliable device features with Plaspy visibility and fleet management tools. Optional features such as remote cut off and towing alarms can extend control and alerting when used alongside Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Real time vehicle tracking with 4G LTE connectivity for prompt location updates
- Detailed trip history and mileage data to support usage and reporting
- Multiple alarm reporting functions including SOS, geo fence, and external power cut alerts
- Optional features for remote cut off and towing or movement alerts to enhance control
- Battery monitoring with low power alerts for both backup and vehicle battery
- Designed to serve single vehicle monitoring and fleet tracking scenarios

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AT-10 relays its tracking and alarm information into Plaspy so fleet managers and vehicle owners can view status, history, and alerts from the same system. Plaspy uses that feed to provide map based visibility, historical records, and configurable notifications to support daily operations and security workflows.

- Live location visibility on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness
- Historical trip and mileage reports accessible in Plaspy for usage review
- Configurable alerts in Plaspy for SOS, geo fence events, power loss, and low battery
- Fleet level dashboards and reports to monitor utilization and operational patterns
- Status and alarm logs that help correlate device events with operational activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time location and history tracking for rental and shared vehicles
- Security and theft recovery support with SOS and power cut alerts
- Monitoring of high value or mission critical vehicles with movement alarms
- Usage and mileage tracking for maintenance planning and cost control
