# Autoseeker - AT-10 Features

This page provides a public, features-oriented overview of the Autoseeker AT-10 as a GPS tracker and describes how its capabilities map to Plaspy for monitoring and fleet management. It is written to help prospective users and administrators understand the practical functions of the device when used with Plaspy rather than to replace manufacturer documentation.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional configuration, and how the device is installed and provisioned by the manufacturer or installer. Optional alarms and control functions mentioned here may require specific firmware, accessories, or configuration set by the installer.

## Feature Overview

The Autoseeker AT-10 is designed for real time vehicle tracking and reporting over modern cellular networks. Its capabilities focus on location visibility, trip history, and alarm reporting to support security and fleet oversight. When integrated with Plaspy, the AT-10 can provide continuous operational visibility and historical records that teams use for routing, security checks, and usage analysis.

- Real time GPS tracking for continuous location awareness
- Detailed vehicle trip history and mileage reporting for route review
- Multiple alarm reports such as geo fence, power cut, and SOS for security alerts
- Optional control and protective functions like remote cut off fuel or ignition as implemented by the installer
- 4G LTE network support with fallback behavior for reliable connectivity across coverage areas

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-10

- Real time tracking over cellular networks for location updates
- Trip history and mileage data for route and usage analysis
- SOS alarm reporting for rapid emergency notification when enabled
- Geo fence alarm and reporting to detect entry and exit of defined areas
- External power cut off alarm and low battery alerts for power monitoring
- ACC switch status reporting to indicate ignition or accessory state
- Optional remote cut off for fuel or ignition and towing or movement alarm where supported
- Over speed alarm and reporting when configured by the device or installer

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes status and location reports from the AT-10 so operators can view live positions and historical records in a unified interface. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents alarms and trip data alongside other fleet telemetry for oversight and reporting.

- Live location updates shown on Plaspy maps for operational tracking
- Trip history and mileage visible in Plaspy for review and export
- Alarm events such as geo fence, SOS, and power alerts logged and time stamped in Plaspy
- Device status indicators and recent activity available for troubleshooting and verification
- Optional control-related events, when enabled by the device, displayed as actionable alerts in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet route monitoring and historical route analysis
- Vehicle security and theft alerting with SOS and power cut alarms
- Rental vehicle monitoring for usage and mileage verification
- Towed or unauthorized movement detection for parked assets
- Driver oversight where ignition or ACC reporting is needed
- Over speed detection and operational safety alerts when configured

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features are optional and depend on the specific firmware and configuration chosen by the manufacturer or installer.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may change supported cellular bands or certified modules, affecting connectivity behavior.
- Installation method and wiring affect which inputs and alarms are available and how they report status.
- Optional functions such as remote fuel or ignition cut off may require additional wiring, permissions, or accessories and are controlled by local regulations.
- Always confirm feature availability and configuration steps with the official Autoseeker documentation for your device serial and firmware level.
