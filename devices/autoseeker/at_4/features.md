# Autoseeker - AT-4 Features

This page provides a public overview of the Autoseeker AT-4 features that are relevant when using the device with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, user facing capabilities and explains how the AT-4’s tracking, alerts, and owner interaction features are reflected in Plaspy for monitoring and operational use.

Exact availability and behavior of individual features can vary by device firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific setup, firmware changelogs, and the latest specifications consult the official manufacturer resources.

## Feature Overview

The Autoseeker AT-4 is a purpose built 4G CAT 1 pet GPS tracker designed for longer deployments in outdoor and open range environments. It combines rugged housing, a high capacity battery, and owner facing controls like two way calling and remote voice monitoring to provide reliable position and status updates that integrate with platforms such as Plaspy.

- Real time GPS positioning and periodic status updates for monitoring animal location and movement.
- Long endurance operation on a high capacity rechargeable battery for extended field use.
- Two way calling and remote voice monitoring to enable direct owner contact or passive audio monitoring.
- Geofence alerts and history playback for boundary monitoring and route review.
- Night search support with a built in searching light and audible buzzer to aid recovery in low light.

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-4

- 4G LTE connectivity with GSM fallback for broad cellular coverage in supported regions.
- Long standby and operational life from a 3000mAh rechargeable battery for longer deployments.
- Two way voice calling and remote voice monitor for direct audio interaction with the tracked animal environment.
- Geofence alarm support to notify when a device crosses user defined virtual boundaries.
- Low battery alerts to help maintain device uptime and reduce unexpected downtime.
- History playback to review routes and past positions inside Plaspy or other compatible platforms.
- Rugged IP67 rated housing with ABS shell suited for outdoor conditions.
- Built in searching light and buzzer to assist in locating animals at night or in poor visibility.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the AT-4’s position and event messages so operators can view location, receive alarms, and replay history using Plaspy dashboards and notifications. Plaspy is designed to detect commonly used tracker protocols and map incoming data into device timeline events and live maps.

- Real time location updates and periodic position reports become live markers and tracks on Plaspy maps.
- Geofence events are converted into platform alerts so operators receive immediate notifications when boundaries are crossed.
- History playback data is stored and presented for route review and behavior analysis over selectable time ranges.
- Low battery and other status messages are surfaced as device alerts to help maintain operational readiness.
- Voice related features are represented as device functions in Plaspy allowing operators to see when two way calling or remote monitoring events occur.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long range pet tracking for hunting dogs and large working animals operating in open terrain.
- Breeder and handler oversight across large properties with geofence monitoring and route history.
- Night time recovery and search scenarios using the searching light and buzzer.
- Remote welfare checks using two way calling or passive voice monitor when an animal is out of sight.
- Training and movement analysis through history playback to refine routes and behavior patterns.
- Managing multiple tracked animals in a centralized Plaspy view for coordinated field operations.
