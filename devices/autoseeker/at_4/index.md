# Autoseeker - AT-4

![AT-4](./tracker.jpg)

The AT-4 is a purpose-built 4G CAT 1 pet GPS tracker engineered for reliable, long-duration tracking of larger animals. As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-4 delivers accurate positioning, long standby life, and direct owner interaction via two-way calling and remote voice monitoring — making it a strong choice for owners, breeders, and handlers who need dependable real-time tracking in outdoor and open-range environments.

The AT-4 combines robust outdoor durability \(IP67-rated ABS shell\), a high-capacity 3000mAh rechargeable battery, and broad cellular coverage on 4G LTE-FDD and fallback GSM bands to keep location and status updates flowing to smartphone apps and platforms that accept standard GPS/GSM trackers. Its feature set — geofence alarms, low-battery alerts, history playback, searching light, and audible buzzer — integrates smoothly with Plaspy’s platform for instant notifications, route review, and remote monitoring of pets in the field.

## Key Highlights

- Long battery life: 3000mAh rechargeable polymer battery delivers up to 30–31 days standby for extended deployments without frequent recharges.
- Plaspy compatible: Sends real-time tracking and status updates through 4G LTE-FDD to platforms and smartphone apps that accept standard GPS/GSM trackers.
- Two-way calling and remote voice monitoring: Direct vocal contact and passive monitoring help owners comfort or check on their animal remotely.
- Durable outdoor build: IP67-rated enclosure and ABS shell resist dust and water exposure during outdoor activities.
- Night search and alerts: Built-in searching light and buzzer aid in locating animals at night or in low-visibility conditions.
- Geofence and history playback: Define virtual boundaries and review detailed route history to support safety and training workflows.
- Broad cellular coverage: Multi-band 4G LTE-FDD and GSM support ensures connectivity across diverse regions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AT-4 streams position and status data over its cellular link to the platform so owners and operators can monitor animals in real time, receive alarms, and review telemetry history. Plaspy ingests the device’s location and event messages to power live maps, geofence notifications, and history playback, making it straightforward to incorporate the AT-4 into workflows for pet safety and field management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered via 4G LTE-FDD to Plaspy-compatible apps and dashboards.
- Two-way calling and remote voice monitor enable live audio contact and passive listening through the device.
- Geofence alarm support with immediate notifications when an animal leaves a defined area.
- Low-battery alerts and remote monitor functions to maintain oversight and reduce downtime.
- History playback for route review and behavior analysis over time.

## Technical Overview

| Connectivity | 4G LTE-FDD \(CAT 1\); fallback to GSM |
| --- | --- |
| Bands | 4G LTE-FDD: B1/B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8/B28/B66; GSM: B2/B3/B5/B8 \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) |
| Battery & Power | 3000mAh rechargeable polymer battery \(3.7V\); operating voltage DC 3.4V–4.5V |

| GNSS | ZKW AT6558D GPS chipset \(GPS positioning; chipset specified in product docs\) |
| Interfaces & Alerts | Built-in buzzer, searching light, magnetic charge interface; supports two-way calling and remote voice monitoring |

| Protection & Housing | IP67 water/dust resistance; ABS plastic shell for outdoor durability |
| Physical | Dimensions 49 × 82 × 38.7 mm; weight approximately 400 g |

Its 3000mAh battery and efficient 4G LTE-FDD connectivity reduce charging overhead and keep the device online for days or weeks, while IP67 protection and an ABS shell withstand real-world use. Two-way calling, remote voice monitor, geofence alarms, and searching light are practical features that enhance safety and make on-the-ground recovery and comfort easier.

For teams or individuals who also use Plaspy for broader asset management, the platform supports a range of telemetry use cases. While the AT-4 focuses on pet tracking, Plaspy-compatible solutions can support fleet management scenarios that include telemetry, anti-theft measures, and vehicle-specific features such as fuel monitoring, ignition control, and immobilizer functions when paired with devices designed for those purposes. That flexibility makes Plaspy an effective hub for combining pet-focused trackers like the AT-4 with other telemetry-enabled devices and Bluetooth sensors in a single operational view.
