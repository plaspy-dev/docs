# Autoseeker - AT-15

![AT-15](./Logo.jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-15 is a versatile GPS mini tracker that is designed to meet the tracking needs of various applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, trucks, containers, or even monitor the safety of senior citizens or individuals with medical challenges such as Parkinson's or Alzheimer's disease, the AT-15 has got you covered.

With its compact size and advanced GPS technology, the AT-15 offers real-time tracking and accurate location information. You can easily monitor the whereabouts of your vehicles or loved ones through a user-friendly mobile app or web platform. The tracker also supports geofencing, allowing you to set up virtual boundaries and receive instant notifications when the tracker enters or exits a designated area.

Key Features:

- Real-time tracking for vehicles, trucks, containers, and individuals
- Supports tracking of senior citizens and persons with medical challenges
- Compact and portable design
- Advanced GPS technology for accurate location information
- User-friendly mobile app and web platform for easy monitoring
- Geofencing capabilities for setting up virtual boundaries
- Instant notifications for entering or exiting designated areas
