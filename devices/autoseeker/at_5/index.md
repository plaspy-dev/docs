# Autoseeker - AT-5

![AT-5](./logo..jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-5 is a wireless Magnetic GPS Asset Tracker that has gained popularity for its reliability and effectiveness, earning it the reputation of being "police-proven." This versatile tracker is suitable for use in vehicles, trucks, and even for personal tracking. With its impressive range of features and precise positioning capabilities, the AT-5 is a top choice for those seeking a reliable GPS tracking solution.

One of the standout features of the Autoseeker AT-5 is its 4G full network compatibility, making it compatible with China Mobile, China Unicom, and Telecom. This ensures that you have a reliable and stable connection for accurate tracking. The AT-5 utilizes GPS, Beidou, wifi, and LBS four-mode positioning, providing you with multiple options for tracking and locating your assets or loved ones.

The Autoseeker AT-5 is equipped with a range of functions to enhance its usability and security. These include a demolition alarm, two-way call capability, an SOS button for urgent help, vibration for incoming calls, overspeed alarm, displacement alarm, vibration alarm, and low battery alarm. These features ensure that you are always aware of the status and location of your tracked assets.

With a built-in 5000mAh large-capacity rechargeable battery, the Autoseeker AT-5 offers long-lasting performance. Its strong magnetic force allows it to be easily adsorbed to iron plates, providing a secure and discreet tracking solution. The device is also designed to withstand various environmental conditions, with an IP68 rating for water and dust resistance.

#### Key Features:

- 4G full network compatibility
- GPS, Beidou, wifi, and LBS four-mode positioning
- Demolition alarm
- Two-way call capability
- SOS button for urgent help
- Overspeed alarm
- Displacement alarm
- Vibration alarm
- Low battery alarm
- Built-in 5000mAh rechargeable battery
- Strong magnetic force for easy installation
- IP68 water and dust resistance

The Autoseeker AT-5 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers precise positioning, versatile compatibility, and a range of useful functions. Whether you need to track vehicles, trucks, or personal assets, the AT-5 is a top choice for its performance and ease of use.
