# Autoseeker - AT-5

<div className="device-logo">
![AT-5](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The Autoseeker AT-5 is a wireless magnetic GPS asset tracker designed for reliable position tracking of vehicles, trucks, and personal assets. Known for its "police proven" reputation, the AT-5 combines 4G full network connectivity with multi mode positioning including GPS, Beidou, wifi, and LBS to deliver flexible and resilient location data. The unit includes a range of practical security features such as a demolition alarm, SOS button, two way call capability, vibration notifications, overspeed and displacement alerts, low battery warning, and an IP68 rating for resistance to dust and water.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-5 can feed location and status updates into Plaspy for real time visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy users can leverage the AT-5 to consolidate tracking, alerts, and reporting across fleets and assets while keeping track of battery status and alarm events. The magnetic mount and long life battery make the AT-5 a convenient option for deployments where discreet attachment and extended runtime are important.

## Key Highlights

- Magnetic mount for discreet and secure placement on iron surfaces  
- 4G full network compatibility for broad connectivity across carriers  
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, Beidou, wifi, and LBS for more resilient location fixes  
- Built in 5000mAh rechargeable battery for extended operational periods  
- Multiple alarm functions including demolition, SOS, vibration, overspeed, displacement, and low battery alerts  
- IP68 rated housing for durability in wet and dusty conditions  
- Two way call capability for basic voice communication with the device

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Autoseeker AT-5 supplies location coordinates and device state information that Plaspy presents through its tracking and fleet management tools. This allows operators to monitor assets in a single interface and respond to alerts coming from the device.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for operational visibility  
- Alarm forwarding to notify teams of SOS events, demolition alerts, displacement, and low battery conditions  
- Historical route and movement reporting to review asset activity over time  
- Fleet level monitoring to see multiple AT-5 devices together for dispatch and oversight  
- Configurable notifications and geofence style monitoring to support operational rules and response workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle and truck tracking for fleet oversight and asset recovery  
- Hidden or temporary asset monitoring where magnetic attachment is preferred  
- Personal safety tracking for lone workers or vulnerable passengers using SOS alerts  
- Long duration deployments that benefit from a larger battery and robust enclosure  
- Situations requiring basic two way voice contact plus remote alerts
