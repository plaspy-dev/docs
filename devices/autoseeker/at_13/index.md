# Autoseeker - AT-13

<div className="device-logo">
![AT-13](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The Autoseeker AT-13 is a compact magnetic GPS tracker designed for real time tracking and locating of vehicles. Its small form factor and magnetic attachment allow for quick placement on any metal surface of a car, making it a practical option for brief deployments or when discreet placement is needed. The device is described as providing remote monitoring capability so users can check location and movement from a smartphone or computer.

This model is compatible with Plaspy, which means you can bring AT-13 location updates into Plaspy's fleet tracking platform to monitor vehicles alongside other assets. Using Plaspy with the AT-13 lets individuals and fleet managers centralize visibility, see current positions on a map, and include the tracker in broader operational workflows managed through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact magnetic design for easy placement on metal vehicle surfaces
- Real time location tracking suitable for cars and light fleet assets
- Long standby capability supported by a 1500mAh backup battery
- Remote monitoring access from smartphone or computer
- Discreet and portable for temporary or covert deployments
- Practical for both single vehicle use and multipiece fleet oversight

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Autoseeker AT-13 provides location updates that Plaspy presents alongside your other tracked devices to give a consolidated view of operations. Plaspy processes incoming location data to enable monitoring, historical analysis, and operational alerts.

- View live vehicle positions on Plaspy maps for real time situational awareness
- Monitor multiple AT-13 units together to manage fleet movement and assignments
- Access historical tracks and reports to analyze trips and uptime trends
- Configure alerts and notifications in Plaspy for movement, scheduled check ins, or location changes
- Include AT-13 data in Plaspy dashboards and exports for operational reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Short term vehicle monitoring during long distance trips or deliveries
- Discreet tracking for recovery and location verification
- Fleet oversight for small vehicle fleets or mixed asset groups
- Rental or shared vehicle tracking for periodic location checks
- Surveillance and remote monitoring where compact placement is required
