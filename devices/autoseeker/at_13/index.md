# Autoseeker - AT-13

![AT-13](./Logo.jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-13 is a compact and powerful magnetic GPS tracker designed for real-time tracking and locating of vehicles. With its small size and magnetic attachment, the AT-13 can be easily placed on any metal surface of a car, making installation a breeze. This tracker is perfect for individuals who want to keep an eye on their vehicles or fleet managers who need to monitor the whereabouts of their vehicles at all times.

One of the standout features of the AT-13 is its long standby time. Equipped with a 1500mAh backup battery, this tracker can operate for extended periods without needing to be recharged. This is especially useful for situations where continuous tracking is required, such as long-distance trips or surveillance operations.

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the AT-13 also supports remote monitoring. This means that users can access real-time information about their vehicles from anywhere, using a smartphone or computer. Whether you want to check the location of your car or monitor its movements, the AT-13 provides you with the tools to do so.

Overall, the Autoseeker AT-13 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that offers real-time tracking, long standby time, and remote monitoring capabilities. Its small size and magnetic attachment make it easy to install, while its powerful battery ensures continuous operation. Whether you're an individual looking to keep tabs on your vehicle or a fleet manager in need of efficient tracking solutions, the AT-13 is an excellent choice.
