# Autoseeker - AT-12 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Autoseeker AT-12 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities you can rely on for vehicle tracking and monitoring and how those capabilities map to monitoring and reporting inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and how the manufacturer configured the unit. For precise, model specific details consult the Autoseeker documentation and firmware release notes in addition to this Plaspy-focused overview.

## Feature Overview

The Autoseeker AT-12 is a compact vehicle tracker designed for global GSM/GPRS coverage and continuous location reporting. Its integrated antennas and onboard battery make it suitable for discreet or temporary installations while providing routine reporting and alerts.

- Quad band GSM GPRS support for broad cellular coverage across common global bands
- Reporting via SMS and GPRS with TCP capability for real time tracking
- Automatic time zone adjustment to keep timestamps aligned with local time
- Daily reports and historical tracking logs for reviewing past movements
- Integrated GSM and GPS antennas for a compact installation and simplified mounting

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-12

- Quad band GSM GPRS support enabling operation on multiple frequency bands
- SMS reporting and GPRS TCP data paths for position and status messages
- Automatic time zone adjustment so recorded times reflect the vehicle location
- Daily reporting and stored tracking history for retrospective review
- Internal GSM and GPS antennas to support a compact device footprint
- Internal battery with low power consumption and a low battery alarm
- Motion detection and power management features for efficient standby use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position and status messages from compatible trackers and presents them in dashboards, reports, and history views. The platform automatically detects supported tracker protocols and makes device data available to users for monitoring and analysis.

- Real time position updates and location history appear in Plaspy maps and timeline views
- Daily reports and historical tracks from the AT-12 are available for review inside Plaspy reporting tools
- Time zone adjustments from the device help keep timestamps consistent in Plaspy reports
- Low battery alerts and status messages surface as events so operators can schedule maintenance
- Motion detection events are visible in Plaspy to indicate activity or start of movement

Note: Plaspy accepts device reports over TCP or UDP and automatically detects the tracker protocol. Devices compatible with Plaspy commonly point to the Plaspy server domain for reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet location tracking with periodic position updates and daily summary reports
- Short term or covert installations where a compact, self contained tracker is required
- Monitoring vehicle movement with motion detection to minimize power draw during inactivity
- Routine maintenance planning triggered by low battery alerts and status messages
- Historical route analysis using daily reports and stored tracking history
- Temporary deployments where integrated antennas and internal battery improve ease of install

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence can depend on the device firmware version and manufacturer firmware configuration
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may change antenna configuration or supported network bands
- Installation method and vehicle wiring can affect power management and standby behavior
- Certain reporting behaviors may require specific configuration on the device or within Plaspy
- Always confirm the exact feature set for your serial number and firmware with the manufacturer

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the Autoseeker AT-12 with Plaspy provides a straightforward way to bring compact, GSM based vehicle tracking into a fleet or monitoring workflow. Plaspy organizes position updates, timestamps, alerts, and daily reports so operators can maintain visibility across assets without needing to manage multiple vendor toolchains.
