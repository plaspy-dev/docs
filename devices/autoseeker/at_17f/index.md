# Autoseeker - AT- 17F

<div className="device-logo">
![AT- 17F](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The Autoseeker AT-17F is a rugged, long life GPS tracker designed for versatile asset and vehicle monitoring. It combines multi GNSS positioning, broad cellular connectivity options, and a sealed IP68 enclosure to deliver reliable location reporting in demanding environments. The unit supports scheduled reports, real time tracking, and common alert types such as geo-fence, low power, removal, and motion based notifications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-17F can be brought into Plaspy to provide consolidated visibility across fleets and assets. Its long standby capabilities and flexible power options make it a practical choice for organizations that need low maintenance remote tracking. Plaspy can ingest the device data to enable location monitoring, alerting, and reporting alongside other tracked devices.

## Key Highlights

- Multi GNSS positioning with support for multiple satellite systems for better location availability
- Long standby performance suitable for long term deployments and periodic reporting
- IP68 rated housing for dust and water resistance in outdoor environments
- Built in magnet mount option for quick attachment to metal assets
- Common alert features including geo-fence, removal, low power, and motion alerts
- Flexible configuration via web, USB, or SMS and support for rechargeable or disposable batteries

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT-17F can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous location visibility and event reporting across your fleet. Once the device is connected and configured, Plaspy can display position history and surface alerts to operations staff through its monitoring tools.

- Consolidate AT-17F location updates into Plaspy for live position maps and historical tracks
- Configure and receive geo-fence and removal alerts in Plaspy to protect high value assets
- Use Plaspy reporting to schedule and review periodic position summaries and activity logs
- Monitor battery and low power notifications in Plaspy to plan maintenance and replacements
- Combine AT-17F data with other devices in Plaspy for unified fleet oversight and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset tracking where infrequent reporting and long standby are priorities
- Mobile equipment monitoring on sites that expose devices to dust and water
- Vehicle fleet visibility for light vehicles and trailers needing discreet magnet mounting
- Rental asset tracking where scheduled reports and removal alerts help prevent loss
- Remote equipment monitoring where low maintenance and reliable alerts are important
