# Autoseeker - AT-17F Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Autoseeker AT-17F tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical capabilities you can expect when integrating the AT-17F into Plaspy for asset and fleet monitoring, including its rugged design, low power profile, positioning features, and event reporting that feed into Plaspy workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and how the manufacturer implements particular functions. For device specific configuration, firmware tools, and the most current technical specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The AT-17F is a rugged, long life GPS tracker that provides unattended, low maintenance tracking for remote assets. It is designed to deliver periodic and event driven position updates and status alerts for assets such as trailers, containers, construction equipment and watercraft, and is compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking and alerting.

- Rugged IP68 sealed enclosure and strong magnetic mounting for secure attachment in harsh environments.
- Multimode cellular connectivity with LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT plus 2G fallback to maximize coverage and battery life.
- Ultra low power consumption and selectable battery configurations to enable multi year deployments with scheduled reporting.
- Rapid GNSS positioning and assisted positioning modes for fast time to first fix to support timely location updates.
- Event reporting such as geo fence entry and exit, tamper or removal alerts, low battery warnings, and scheduled reports.
- Open protocol and remote configuration options to ease integration into Plaspy and other telematics platforms.

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-17F

- Rugged design with IP68 ingress protection for reliable operation in demanding outdoor conditions.
- Powerful magnetic mounting option engineered for secure attachment to trailers, containers and heavy equipment.
- Multi radio cellular connectivity with LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT plus fallback 2G for regional coverage and power efficiency.
- Extremely low sleep current to support long battery life on non rechargeable primary cells or extended life on rechargeable packs.
- Qualcomm Gen8C GNSS based positioning with assisted positioning modes for fast TTFF and regular fixes.
- Geo fence alerts and tamper or removal notifications to support anti theft and perimeter monitoring workflows.
- Scheduled reporting and smart reporting modes to balance update frequency with battery conservation.
- Open protocol support and compatibility with common telematics platforms which simplifies Plaspy integration.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AT-17F is connected to Plaspy the device streams location fixes and event telemetry to the platform, making asset location, history and alerts available to operators for monitoring and incident response. Plaspy can ingest the device reports and present them in dashboards, maps and alert rules to match operational needs.

- Real time position updates and historical track playback for asset visibility in Plaspy.
- Geo fence entry and exit events, removal or tamper notifications, and low battery alerts surfaced as platform notifications or rules.
- Scheduled reporting and smart motion driven updates appear in Plaspy as periodic telemetry to reduce maintenance while preserving situational awareness.
- Remote configuration options enable adjustment of reporting intervals and thresholds to suit Plaspy dashboards and alerting logic.
- Open protocol compatibility and multi platform support make it straightforward to aggregate AT-17F feeds alongside other telemetry in Plaspy.
