# Autoseeker - AT-17G

![AT-17G](./tracker.jpg)

The AT-17G 4G GPS Asset Tracker is a heavy-duty, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for long-term asset protection and efficient fleet management. With built-in 4G LTE connectivity, a high-capacity 20000mAh rechargeable battery and six internal high-strength magnets, the AT-17G delivers reliable real-time tracking and covert mounting on trucks, trailers, containers, construction machinery and other high-value mobile assets.

Designed for demanding transport and logistics deployments, the AT-17G combines durable IP65-rated construction with extended standby times, multiple alert types and remote audio monitoring. When paired with Plaspy, this device feeds precise location and telemetry into your platform of choice to support anti-theft workflows, route oversight, and scalable fleet management across international cellular bands.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with 4G LTE for accurate, low-latency real-time tracking.
- Large 20000mAh rechargeable battery for extended standby \(several months depending on reporting frequency and work mode\).
- Six internal high-strength magnets for secure, covert mounting on metal assets; quick, tool-free installation.
- Movement and overspeed alerts, geofence notifications, low-battery warnings and history trace checking for robust anti-theft and monitoring.
- Remote audio monitoring to assist in theft investigations and asset recovery.
- Multiple sleep and low-power work modes to maximize battery life and reduce maintenance intervals.
- Broad cellular band support for international deployments \(4G LTE FDD + GSM fallback\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the AT-17G with Plaspy enables straightforward ingestion of GPS fixes and telemetry into your tracking dashboards, reports and alert rules. The device transmits position updates, event flags and battery status over 4G \(with GSM fallback\) so Plaspy users get continuous situational awareness for anti-theft response and fleet management decisions.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for mapping, alerts and history playback.
- Movement, overspeed and geofence alerts routed to Plaspy for automated notifications and escalation.
- Low-battery notifications and history trace checking to simplify maintenance planning and asset audits.
- Remote audio monitoring data available through Plaspy-compatible platforms to assist incident verification.
- Ignition/door/alarm status and immobilizer actions: where vehicle telematics or external interfaces are present, the AT-17G can be used within Plaspy workflows to support ignition/immobilizer scenarios \(requires vehicle-side integration or external control hardware\).
- Telemetry and fuel monitoring: Plaspy users can correlate AT-17G location and motion telemetry with fuel monitoring streams when external fuel sensors or telematics sources are integrated into the platform.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and recovery: covert magnetic mounting, movement alerts and remote audio help locate and recover stolen trucks, trailers and equipment.
- Container and cargo tracking: long battery life and IP65 protection make the AT-17G ideal for sea/land container monitoring and chain-of-custody reporting.
- Construction and heavy machinery: secure attachment to excavators, cranes and other equipment for usage tracking and loss prevention.
- Maritime and ground support equipment: track boats and airport ground equipment where rugged mounting and extended runtime are required.
- General high-value asset monitoring: portable, covert deployment for temporary or permanent protection of valuable mobile assets.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT-17G is a purpose-built Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for organizations that need dependable long-term tracking without frequent servicing. Its 20000mAh battery and low-power work modes minimize downtime, while broad LTE and GSM band support enables international fleet management. The strong magnetic mount and IP65 housing provide secure, covert placement and weather resistance—important for anti-theft and logistics workflows.

Paired with Plaspy, the AT-17G streams real-time tracking and telemetry into a unified platform where dispatchers, fleet managers and security teams can act on alerts, analyze history traces and orchestrate recovery actions. Where external telematics or sensors exist, operators can combine AT-17G location data with fuel monitoring, ignition status and immobilizer controls inside Plaspy workflows, delivering a flexible solution for demanding transport and asset protection scenarios.
