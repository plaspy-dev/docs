# Autoseeker - AT-17G Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Autoseeker AT-17G GPS asset tracker and explains how its capabilities map to use with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive details about the device, how it operates in the field, and what Plaspy users can expect when they pair the AT-17G with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where the description indicates conditional or optional behavior, this page highlights those conditions so you can confirm precise behavior against manufacturer documentation and your installed firmware version.

## Feature Overview

The AT-17G is a heavy duty 4G GPS asset tracker built for long-term deployments on trucks, trailers, containers, construction equipment and other high value mobile assets. It combines extended battery life, rugged IP65 construction, and covert magnetic mounting with event reporting and remote audio monitoring to support theft prevention and fleet oversight.

- 4G LTE connectivity with GSM fallback for wide area coverage and responsive location updates.
- Large rechargeable 20000mAh battery and multiple low power work modes to reduce maintenance frequency.
- Six built in high strength magnets for quick and covert mounting to metal assets.
- Movement, overspeed, geofence and low battery alerts to support anti theft workflows.
- Remote audio monitoring and history trace playback to aid investigations and recovery.
- IP65 rated housing for dust and splash resistance in demanding outdoor environments.

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-17G

- 4G LTE with GSM fallback for reliable cellular connectivity across supported bands.
- High capacity 20000mAh rechargeable battery to support extended standby and long deployments.
- Six internal high strength magnets for secure, tool free mounting on metal surfaces.
- IP65 rated enclosure for dust and splash resistance in field conditions.
- Movement alerts, overspeed notifications, and geofence reporting for event driven monitoring.
- Remote audio monitoring capability to assist in incident verification and recovery.
- Multiple sleep and low power work modes to maximize runtime between charges.
- Clear telemetry elements such as battery status and history trace checking for asset audits.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AT-17G is used with Plaspy, the device sends location updates and event flags into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor assets, respond to alerts, and review historical traces. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and ingests the device telemetry into maps, reports, and alert rules.

- Real time location updates appear in Plaspy mapping and history playback for operational visibility.
- Movement, geofence and low battery alerts can trigger Plaspy notifications and escalation workflows.
- Battery status and history traces in Plaspy help schedule maintenance and verify asset activity.
- Remote audio monitoring metadata can be surfaced in compatible Plaspy workflows to assist incident review.
- Devices may be configured to send data via UDP or TCP to Plaspy at d.plaspy.com on the platform port; Plaspy will detect the tracker protocol automatically.
