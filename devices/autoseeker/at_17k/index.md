# Autoseeker - AT-17K

![AT-17K](./tracker.jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-17K is a 4G solar wireless magnetic GPS tracker built for long-term, low-maintenance asset and vehicle monitoring. It combines a rugged IP68 waterproof enclosure with a strong magnetic mount and a high-capacity internal battery plus solar recharge to provide continuous location reporting and status updates for fleets, containers, boats, and heavy equipment.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-17K streams location and status data over cellular networks so Plaspy users can access live tracking, history playback, and event alerts in one dashboard. Its extended runtime and tamper/impact alarms make the AT-17K a practical choice where reliable remote monitoring and reduced service visits are important.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and history playback for fleet and asset visibility.
- IP68 rated waterproof housing suitable for marine and outdoor deployments.
- Large internal battery with integrated solar recharge for extended standby and reduced maintenance.
- Strong magnetic mount with optional screw-mounting for secure attachment to vehicles and equipment.
- Built-in alarms for vibration, tamper detection, and impact events to support anti-theft workflows.
- Global cellular connectivity for continuous uplink of position and status to Plaspy.
- Low average power consumption to prolong deployments between maintenance cycles.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed on an asset, the AT-17K transmits GPS position and onboard status to Plaspy so your team can monitor movement, receive alerts, and analyze historical activity from a single platform. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it available through maps, geofences, reports, and notification rules to support day to day operations and incident response.

- Live location updates and telemetry appear in Plaspy for dispatch and monitoring.
- History playback and route review help verify journeys and investigate incidents.
- Geofence events and anti-theft alarms trigger notifications and automated workflows.
- Battery and solar recharge status are available in Plaspy to plan maintenance and replacements.
- Device events can be included in fleet reports and operational dashboards for oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for trucks and service vehicles that require long runtime and reliable location data.
- Anti-theft protection for trailers, containers, and high value assets using magnetic mounting and tamper alerts.
- Marine tracking for small boats and remote vessels that benefit from waterproofing and solar recharge.
- Construction and heavy equipment monitoring on worksites where rugged mounting and durability are needed.
- Rental fleets and insurance telematics programs that require long term deployments with low maintenance.

The AT-17K pairs endurance and ruggedness with Plaspy compatibility to deliver a dependable option for organizations tracking assets in harsh or remote environments. Its combination of waterproof sealing, strong magnetic mounting, and solar-assisted battery life reduces the need for frequent service visits while feeding timely location and alarm data into Plaspy for operational use.
