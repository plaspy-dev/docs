# Autoseeker - AT-17K

![AT-17K](./tracker.jpg)

The AT-17K 4G Solar Wireless Magnetic GPS Tracker is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for long-term, low-maintenance asset and vehicle monitoring. Designed for harsh maritime and terrestrial conditions, the AT-17K pairs rugged IP68 waterproof construction and a 300 lb magnetic mount with a high-capacity 10000mAh battery and solar recharging to deliver reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for fleets, containers, boats, and heavy equipment.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-17K streams location and status data over 4G LTE and GSM networks so you get history playback, geo-fencing, anti-theft alerts, and fleet management insights in your Plaspy dashboard. Its combination of extended standby runtime, low power consumption, and impact/tamper alarms makes the AT-17K ideal for insurance telematics, rental fleets, construction machinery, and marine applications that require continuous, secure tracking.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker providing real-time tracking and history playback for fleet management and asset protection.
- Rugged IP68 waterproof housing and wide operating temperature range \(-20°C to 65°C\) for marine and outdoor deployments.
- 10000mAh internal battery with solar recharging \(5V / 2.5W\) for extended standby and reduced maintenance intervals.
- Strong magnetic mounting \(~300 lb pull\) plus screw-mount option for fast, secure installation on vehicles, containers, and equipment.
- Comprehensive alarms and telemetry: vibration alarm, light-sensing anti-tamper, fall/impact alarm, and low-battery alerts.
- Global-ready cellular connectivity on multiple 4G LTE FDD and GSM bands to maintain reliable uplink for Plaspy integration.
- Low average power consumption \(approx. 68mA at 5V\) and flexible recharging via Micro-USB \(DC 5V/1000mA\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT-17K transmits GPS position and onboard telemetry to Plaspy over cellular networks so you can view live location, historical routes, and event alerts in one place. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s telemetry and exposes it through maps, reports, geofence rules, and notification workflows for immediate operational use and long-term analysis.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live dispatch and monitoring.
- Geo-fencing and history playback for route verification, compliance, and incident review.
- Low-battery, vibration, light-sensing tamper, and fall/impact alarms that trigger Plaspy notifications and workflows.
- Flexible power telemetry: internal battery status and solar recharge metrics available to Plaspy for maintenance planning.
- Integrates with Plaspy data models and can be combined with vehicle-sourced signals—such as ignition, fuel monitoring, or immobilizer inputs—when those data streams are available in your fleet environment.
