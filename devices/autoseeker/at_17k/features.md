# Autoseeker - AT-17K Features

This page provides a public overview of the Autoseeker AT-17K features as they relate to use with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy, explains practical uses, and highlights how the device streams location and status data into Plaspy for monitoring, playback, and alerting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific and up to date technical details, consult the manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes before planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The AT-17K is a Plaspy compatible, solar assisted 4G GPS tracker designed for long term, low maintenance monitoring of vehicles and assets in demanding environments. It combines rugged weatherproof construction, a high capacity battery with solar recharging, and a strong magnetic mount to support deployments that require endurance and tamper awareness.

- Real time location streaming and history playback into Plaspy for operational visibility.
- IP68 rated waterproof housing for marine and outdoor exposure.
- Large internal battery with solar recharge to extend standby intervals and reduce maintenance.
- Heavy duty magnetic mount plus screw mount option for quick and secure installation.
- Onboard alarms and telemetry such as vibration, light sensing tamper, fall or impact alerts, and low battery notifications.

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-17K

- 4G LTE and GSM cellular connectivity for continuous uplink of GPS and status information to Plaspy.
- Rugged IP68 waterproof housing suitable for marine and exposed outdoor use.
- Integrated 10000mAh internal battery with solar recharging support to extend deployment time.
- Strong magnetic mount rated for heavy pull plus a screw mount option for permanent installation.
- Alarms and telemetry including vibration alarm, light sensing anti tamper, fall or impact alarm, and low battery alerts.
- Micro USB recharge support for flexible on site charging.
- GNSS position capability with fast acquisition behavior as provided by the device chipset and firmware.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected over cellular networks, the AT-17K sends GPS positions and telemetry to Plaspy so you can monitor location, alarms, and power status from a single dashboard. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and ingests the device data for mapping, alerting, and reporting.

- Live location and periodic position updates displayed on Plaspy maps and route playback.
- Event alerts such as vibration, tamper, fall or low battery that can trigger Plaspy notifications and workflows.
- Battery and solar recharge status reported into Plaspy for maintenance planning and service scheduling.
- Geo fence and history tools in Plaspy used for incident review and route verification based on AT-17K reports.
- Plaspy accepts device connections to the platform and will auto detect compatible protocols so telemetry is available without manual protocol selection.
- Device data can be combined with other fleet signals in Plaspy when those additional data streams are present in the deployment.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for trucks and service vehicles that need reliable location history and incident review.
- Long term asset protection for containers, trailers, and equipment using magnetic mount and tamper alerts.
- Marine and small vessel monitoring where waterproofing and solar recharge reduce maintenance visits.
- Construction and heavy equipment oversight on job sites with durable mounting and robust housing.
- Rental fleet monitoring and insurance telematics that require extended deployments and event logging.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Features and alarm behavior depend on the device firmware version and may change with manufacturer updates.
- Hardware revisions and regional cellular variants can affect supported bands and connectivity characteristics.
- Installation method and mounting location influence signal reception, solar charging effectiveness, and alarm sensitivity.
- Some integrations and combined vehicle data streams are available only when those inputs are present in the wider vehicle or fleet setup.
- Always verify the precise feature set and configuration options with the manufacturer documentation for your serial number and firmware.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the AT-17K with Plaspy provides centralized visibility into location, battery and alarm events so operations teams can monitor assets continuously and respond to incidents quickly. Plaspy brings mapping, history playback, geofence rules, and notification workflows that make the AT-17K telemetry actionable for dispatch, security, and maintenance planning.
