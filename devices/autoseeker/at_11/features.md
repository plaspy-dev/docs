# Autoseeker - AT-11 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Autoseeker AT-11 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for vehicle tracking and fleet management. It focuses on the practical, non-sensitive functions of the AT-11 when reporting location and event telemetry into Plaspy compatible tracking servers and dashboards.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation type. The information here is grounded in the device description provided by the manufacturer and is intended for general planning and integration context rather than as a substitute for the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The AT-11 is a compact plug-and-play vehicle tracker designed to sit in a cigarette lighter socket and provide continuous position reporting and security alerts while also serving as a mobile phone charger. Its design emphasizes fast deployment, continuous 4G connectivity with 2G fallback, and basic anti-theft and operational alarms that integrate into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerts, and history playback.

- Plug-and-play cigarette lighter installation for rapid deployment without hardwiring.
- Integrated mobile phone charging function combined with real-time GPS and Beidou positioning.
- Global 4G connectivity with 2G fallback and Nano SIM support for broad cellular coverage.
- Security and operational alarms including geo-fence, overspeed, movement/towing, vibration, illegal ignition, and power-failure or unplug alerts.
- Internal backup battery and power saving modes to allow safe shutdown and short-term operation after power loss.
- Durable compact ABS enclosure with internal antennas for unobtrusive vehicle installation.

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-11

- Plug-and-play cigarette lighter form factor eliminating the need for hardwiring in many deployments.
- Dual function as a high precision GNSS tracker and an in-car USB charger for driver convenience.
- GPS and Beidou satellite positioning with published sub 10 meter accuracy under typical conditions.
- 4G LTE wideband connectivity with GSM GPRS EDGE 2G fallback for extended network reach.
- Multiple security alerts reported by the device including geo-fence, overspeed, movement or towing, vibration, and illegal ignition detection.
- Power-failure and unplug detection with visibility to device status and internal backup battery level.
- Compact physical design and internal antennas for a low profile in-vehicle installation.
- Remote configuration support through compatible tracking servers and vendor platforms; firmware update behavior not specified.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AT-11 is connected to a Plaspy compatible tracking server, its position reports and event telemetry appear on Plaspy dashboards and can be used to trigger notifications and reporting workflows. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents location and event information alongside other fleet devices for unified oversight.

- Live mapping of GNSS coordinates and timestamped position updates for real-time location awareness.
- Alarm events such as geo-fence breaches, overspeed, movement, and power-failure routed into Plaspy for immediate notification and audit trails.
- Historical playback and trip reports based on the device telemetry stored by Plaspy for operational analysis.
- Device health and status indicators visible in Plaspy including connection state and reported backup battery status.
- Configurable geo-fences, speed thresholds, and alerting rules in Plaspy to match operational policies for the AT-11 equipped vehicles.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars and light vehicles where fast deployment and minimal installation time are priorities.
- Rental and dealership tracking where devices must be easily installed, moved, or swapped between vehicles.
- Insurance telematics programs that require event history, odometer or mileage workflows, and tamper/power-failure awareness.
- Anti-theft monitoring using unplug and movement or illegal ignition alerts to support rapid response.
- Individual vehicle owners wanting discreet tracking with the added convenience of an in-car charger.
