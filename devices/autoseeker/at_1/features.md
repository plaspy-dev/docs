# Autoseeker - AT-1 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Autoseeker AT-1 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing functions, typical operational value, and how the device integrates into Plaspy for tracking and monitoring. The information here is intended for general guidance and to help users understand what they can expect when pairing an AT-1 with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability for any individual AT-1 unit can vary depending on firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and the manufacturer implementation. For the most current and device specific technical details please consult the official Autoseeker documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Autoseeker AT-1 is a compact waterproof 4G GPS tracker designed for low power operation and flexible tracking modes. It combines a professional GNSS receiver with cellular connectivity options to support personal and light asset tracking scenarios while providing weather resistant protection.

- Compact IP68 waterproof enclosure for outdoor and everyday carry use
- 4G Cat M1 and NB IoT plus GSM network support for broad cellular connectivity
- Professional GNSS positioning with fast hot start performance for timely location fixes
- Multiple work modes including real time tracking and history tracking for adaptable reporting
- Geofence support to send alerts on entry and exit of predefined areas
- Rechargeable battery with widely varying runtime depending on reporting interval

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-1

- 4G Cat M1 optimized cellular connectivity with NB IoT and GSM fallback as listed by the manufacturer
- IP68 waterproof rating for protection against dust and immersion
- Rechargeable battery design using a built in 650 mAh polymer cell
- Multiple operation modes, including real time tracking and history tracking among eight available modes
- Geofence capability for perimeter alerts when the unit crosses set boundaries
- Qualcomm Gen 8C GNSS receiver offering high sensitivity and positioning performance
- Fast hot start and reasonable cold start times for reliable fixes under many conditions
- Small form factor and light weight for unobtrusive use on people or small belongings

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides a platform to receive, display, and manage location and event data from compatible devices such as the AT-1. When an AT-1 is reporting, Plaspy shows live and historical positions, and surfaces supported events for operational use.

- Location updates and history playback for routes reported by the device
- Geofence events appear in Plaspy when the tracker sends entry or exit notifications
- Plaspy accepts device connections targeting d.plaspy.com and port 8888 using TCP or UDP and automatically detects the tracker protocol
- Compact device data is presented alongside maps and timeline views for situational awareness
- Platform level alerts and notifications can be configured to act on the events the device provides

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal tracking for children, elderly family members, or individuals who benefit from location awareness
- Pet tracking when a waterproof and compact tracker is needed
- Temporary tracking of small personal items or equipment that require periodic monitoring
- Remote monitoring where low power cellular operation reduces operating cost
- Situations that require geofence notifications for entry and exit awareness

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific work modes, event types, and reporting behavior depend on the device firmware and manufacturer settings
- Cellular band support and network availability vary by region and by the hardware variant shipped for that region
- Battery life is highly dependent on reporting interval and the chosen work mode; examples from the manufacturer show large differences between daily reports and frequent interval reporting
- Waterproof performance depends on intact seals and correct installation in the field
- Manufacturer documentation should be consulted for detailed electrical, environmental, and regulatory specifications

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the Autoseeker AT-1 helps organizations and individuals centralize location visibility, manage geofence events, and review movement history from a single platform. Plaspy is designed to accept compatible tracker reports and present them in a way that supports monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight without requiring deep device specific knowledge from end users.

To learn more about how the AT-1 can work within Plaspy visit https://www.plaspy.com. Please verify the latest device specific features, firmware behavior, and manufacturer details with Autoseeker at https://autoseekergps.com/ since hardware revisions and firmware updates can change available functions over time.
