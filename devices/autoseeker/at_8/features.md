# Autoseeker - AT-8 Features

This page provides public feature context for using the Autoseeker AT-8 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the AT-8 capabilities that are relevant for centralized monitoring within your Plaspy account and explains how those capabilities are typically reflected in Plaspy for real world livestock and asset management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation method. For definitive, device level details consult the manufacturer documentation and firmware notes as implementation can change over time.

## Feature Overview

The AT-8 is a purpose built 4G animal tracker designed for long duration deployments on large livestock. In Plaspy the device is used to provide location history, event visibility, and practical retrieval aids that reduce field time and improve response when animals move beyond expected areas.

- Real time location reporting and history playback for tracking herd movements over time
- Large rechargeable battery designed to extend maintenance intervals for remote deployments
- Rugged IP67 ABS housing for durable performance in outdoor and agricultural environments
- Configurable working modes to balance reporting frequency and battery life for different deployment needs
- Built in buzzer and illumination to assist rapid device retrieval in pasture or transit situations

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-8

- Plaspy compatible for centralized tracking and event logging across multiple devices
- 4G CAT 1 connectivity with GSM fallback for broad regional cellular coverage
- High capacity rechargeable 20,000 mAh battery designed for extended standby and reduced servicing
- IP67 rated ABS enclosure for dust and water resistance in field conditions
- Three configurable reporting modes to tune update cadence and conserve power
- Built in buzzer and illumination to help locate devices on animals in large areas
- Compact mountable form factor sized for large livestock applications
- GNSS position fixes and location history suitable for mapping and playback in Plaspy

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the AT-8 streams location and status information into a single platform so teams can monitor animals, review history, and receive alerts without switching systems. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and displays incoming data alongside other devices in your account.

- Live location markers and movement trails displayed on the Plaspy map for situational awareness
- History playback to review past positions and analyze movement patterns
- Geofence alerts and breach notifications using configured boundary areas in Plaspy
- Device status and battery indicators to plan recharging and maintenance rounds
- Use retrieval aids reported by the device to locate animals quickly after alerts or returns
- Reporting mode changes on the tracker help manage how often Plaspy receives updates to balance visibility and battery life

## Typical Use Cases

- Ranch scale livestock tracking to monitor herd distribution and movement across pastures
- Geofence based alerts for rapid response when animals leave designated grazing areas
- Recovery workflows that use buzzer and light aids to locate animals quickly in the field
- Transport monitoring for animals during loading and transit where location visibility is required
- Operational planning and grazing rotation analysis using history playback in Plaspy
- Remote deployments where long battery life reduces visit frequency and lowers operational cost
