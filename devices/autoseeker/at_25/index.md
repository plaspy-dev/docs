# Autoseeker - AT-25

![AT-25](./Logo.jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-25 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable location monitoring and basic vehicle security functions. It supports position updates via SMS and GPRS communication and also works over 4G internet, offering multiple channels for reporting location. The device includes features such as time interval tracking for scheduled position reports, history logging, geofence alerts, overspeed warnings, shake alarm, alert on power off, ACC illegal engine on detection, and a built in backup battery to maintain operation if vehicle power is removed.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-25 can feed its location and alert information into Plaspy for centralized visibility across vehicles and assets. Plaspy can present updates from this tracker alongside other fleet devices, making it easier to monitor movements, review history, and respond to alerts from a single platform. Compatibility enables organizations to use the AT-25 within Plaspy workflows for monitoring and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location reporting using SMS and GPRS with 4G internet support for more stable connectivity
- Time interval tracking to schedule regular position updates and manage reporting frequency
- Built in backup 100 mAh battery ensures continued operation when main power is interrupted
- Security alerts including ACC illegal engine on detection, shake alarm, overspeed alert, and power off alert
- Geofence alerting and history tracking for movement review and per vehicle audit trails
- Remote engine cut off capability to stop the vehicle when supported by the tracker and platform
- Time zone exchange to keep timestamps consistent across regions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the AT-25 delivers location and alert data into a single fleet management environment so you can monitor assets in real time, review historical tracks, and act on critical events. Plaspy ingests the tracker reports and makes them available for mapping, alerting, and reporting across your fleet.

- Receive periodic location updates from the AT-25 on Plaspy to display current vehicle positions on the platform map
- Use Plaspy to collect and present historical tracks sent by the tracker for playback and trip review
- Configure alert rules in Plaspy to notify operators about geofence breaches, overspeed events, shake alarms, power off, and ACC illegal engine on detections
- Employ Plaspy reporting to summarize uptime, movement history, and alert incidents for operational analysis
- Where supported by device setup and permissions, manage remote control actions such as engine cut off through Plaspy as part of incident response

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle monitoring for delivery vans and service vehicles that need scheduled location updates
- Theft prevention and recovery by combining alerts and live tracking with Plaspy visibility
- Rental and asset management where history tracking and geofence alerts help enforce usage policies
- Overspeed and driver behavior oversight for organizations focused on safety and compliance
- Remote shutdown capability for high value vehicles when integrated into a centralized response process

The AT-25 is a practical choice for organizations that want a straightforward tracker with multiple reporting channels and a set of common security alerts. Its time interval reporting, history tracking, and backup battery make it suitable for daily fleet operations that rely on consistent position reporting and the ability to review past movements. When paired with Plaspy, these capabilities become part of a unified monitoring and reporting workflow that simplifies oversight across mixed fleets.
