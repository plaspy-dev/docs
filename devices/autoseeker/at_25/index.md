# Autoseeker - AT-25

![AT-25](./Logo.jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-25 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a wide range of features to help you keep track of your vehicles. With its tracking capabilities, you can easily monitor the location of your vehicle in real-time using SMS or GPRS \(TCP\) communication. The tracker also supports 4G internet, ensuring a fast and stable connection for seamless tracking.

One of the standout features of the Autoseeker AT-25 is its ability to track by time interval. This means that you can set specific time intervals for the tracker to send location updates, allowing you to have a clear picture of your vehicle's movements throughout the day. Additionally, you can also get position location updates via SMS, providing you with an alternative method of tracking.

The Autoseeker AT-25 is equipped with several advanced features to enhance the security and safety of your vehicle. It has ACC illegal engine on detection, which alerts you if the engine is turned on without authorization. The tracker also has a built-in backup 100 mAh battery, ensuring that it continues to function even if the vehicle's power is disconnected. In case of emergencies, you can remotely cut off the engine to stop the car in a safe condition.

Other notable features of the Autoseeker AT-25 include time zone exchange, shake alarm, over speed alert, geo-fence alert, alert when power off, and history tracking. These features provide you with comprehensive monitoring and control over your vehicle, allowing you to ensure its safety and efficiency.
