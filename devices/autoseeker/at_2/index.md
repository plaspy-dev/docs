# Autoseeker - AT-2

![AT-2](./logo..jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-2 is a compact, waterproof personal GPS tracker designed to provide discreet location monitoring and emergency alerting for seniors and people with medical challenges such as Parkinsons and Alzheimers. As the smallest model in Autoseeker's lineup, the AT-2 emphasizes portability and concealment while offering positioning with GPS plus Beidou with reported accuracy around five meters. The device supports full band 2G and 4G connectivity, includes an SOS emergency call function, and offers a suite of alarms and recording features targeted at personal safety use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-2 can feed location and event information into the Plaspy platform to give caregivers, managers, and organizations centralized visibility. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker’s emergency alerts, movement notifications, and battery status become part of a unified monitoring workflow, helping teams respond faster and maintain oversight without needing multiple systems.

## Key Highlights

- Small and lightweight personal tracker designed for discreet carry and concealment
- GPS plus Beidou positioning with reported 5 meter location accuracy
- Supports 2G and 4G full band networks for wide cellular coverage
- SOS emergency call and multiple alarm functions for personal safety
- Long standby capability from a built in 1500 mAh rechargeable battery
- IP65 rated ABS housing and strong magnetic attachment for flexible placement

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT-2 transmits location and event data that Plaspy can display and manage, turning raw device signals into actionable information for monitoring and reporting. In Plaspy, the tracker is represented as a device that produces location updates and alerts which can be viewed alongside other fleet or personal assets.

- Real time location visualization on Plaspy maps for continuous awareness
- Alert handling for SOS events, movement or displacement alarms, vibration and low power notifications
- Historical tracks and activity reports to review movements and timelines
- Device status and battery reporting to help manage uptime and charging cycles
- Grouping and oversight tools for caregivers or small fleet managers to keep multiple units under a single view

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring seniors and individuals with medical needs to improve response times in emergencies
- Caregiver oversight in residential or community care settings
- Discreet personal safety tracking for individuals prone to disorientation or wandering
- Short term asset or equipment tracking where a small waterproof device is beneficial
- Situations that require long standby times between charges for intermittent monitoring
