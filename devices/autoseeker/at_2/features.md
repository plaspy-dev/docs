# Autoseeker - AT-2 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the Autoseeker AT-2 when used with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, including how the tracker reports location, alarms, and status into the Plaspy platform for monitoring, alerting, and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and regional carrier support. For device specific configuration and the most current technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and firmware notes from Autoseeker.

## Feature Overview

The AT-2 is a compact 4G mini GPS tracker intended for personal safety and portable asset monitoring. Its combination of cellular connectivity, dual constellation positioning, audible two-way voice features, and a broad alarm suite make it suitable for discreet tracking and responsive monitoring use cases.

- Compact, lightweight form factor with magnet attachment options for discreet mounting on luggage or equipment
- Full band 4G LTE FDD with GSM fallback for broad cellular coverage and continuous location reporting
- GPS plus Beidou positioning for reliable fixes and practical location accuracy for tracking
- Two-way voice, SOS emergency calling, and remote listening for caregiver and responder contact
- Long standby battery life with a rechargeable battery and power save modes for extended deployments
- IP65 water resistance and an alarm suite for tamper, movement, displacement and low battery alerts

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-2

- Plaspy compatible endpoint that reports position, telemetry, and alarm events into the platform
- Cellular connectivity with 4G LTE FDD and GSM fallback to maintain reporting where networks are available
- Dual GNSS positioning using GPS and Beidou for practical location awareness
- Two-way voice communications plus SOS emergency call and remote listening capabilities
- Rechargeable 3.7V 1500mAh battery with extended standby performance in power save modes
- Intelligent alarms including tamper, vibration or movement, displacement, overspeed and low battery notifications
- Compact IP65 rated housing with magnet attachment for portable asset and personal safety use
- Remote configuration support through compatible tracking platform integrations

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated into Plaspy, the AT-2 becomes an active data source for location and event monitoring. The device uploads positions, status updates, and alarm events over cellular networks into Plaspy where teams can view live locations, receive alerts, and act on incidents.

- Real time location updates and GNSS fixes are displayed on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- SOS emergency alerts and alarm events are surfaced to operators for rapid response
- Battery state and power save notifications help Plaspy users plan maintenance and avoid unexpected offline periods
- Alarm types such as tamper, displacement and vibration are forwarded to Plaspy for incident tracking and escalation
- Remote configuration and device management options are available through platform supported methods where supported by the device firmware

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children, seniors, or vulnerable individuals where SOS and two way voice are useful
- Lightweight asset tracking for luggage, backpacks, portable equipment, or temporary deployments
- Short term vehicle or boat tracking when a compact, discreet tracker is desired
- Anti theft monitoring using tamper, displacement and vibration alarms routed into Plaspy for alerts
- Field personnel check ins and basic location oversight for small scale fleet or contractor monitoring
