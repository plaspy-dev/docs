# Autoseeker - AT-9 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Autoseeker AT-9 and explains how its capabilities map to monitoring and management in Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive descriptions of the tracker's functionality and what users can expect when deploying the device with Plaspy for real-time tracking and asset protection.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional model, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device-specific details consult the Autoseeker documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Autoseeker AT-9 is a rugged magnetic asset tracker designed for covert and heavy duty installations where long battery life and environmental protection are required. It combines multi-mode positioning with cellular connectivity, onboard alarms, and user-facing features such as two-way voice and an SOS button to support anti-theft and fleet workflows.

- Rugged IP68 enclosure and a strong integrated magnet for tool free mounting to metal assets.
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, BeiDou, Wi‑Fi and LBS to improve location availability in varied environments.
- 4G LTE and GSM cellular connectivity for real time reporting and wide area coverage.
- Rechargeable long life battery with magnetic charging and optional solar or backup charging support noted by the manufacturer.
- Motion, vibration and tamper alarms plus overspeed and displacement alerts for asset security.
- SOS button and two way voice for emergency signaling and direct communication with the asset.

## Core Features of Autoseeker - AT-9

- IP68 rated waterproof housing suitable for outdoor and marine adjacent use.
- Built in strong magnet for covert, tool free mounting on metal surfaces.
- Multi mode GNSS and positioning fallbacks including GPS and BeiDou alongside Wi‑Fi and LBS.
- Cellular data support across 4G LTE and GSM networks for telemetry and location reporting.
- Long capacity rechargeable battery intended for extended standby and periodic check ins.
- Motion, vibration, tamper/demolition, overspeed and displacement alarms for theft and misuse detection.
- SOS button and two way voice capability to support emergency and on asset communication.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location updates, alarms and telemetry from compatible trackers to provide unified visibility and alerting across fleets and dispersed assets. When an AT-9 is connected to cellular service and configured to report, Plaspy presents location and event data alongside history and alerting tools to help with monitoring and response.

- Real time location and fallback position sources appear on Plaspy maps and historical route playback.
- Motion, vibration and tamper alerts are delivered into Plaspy event streams to trigger notifications and incident workflows.
- Battery level and low battery warnings are visible in device status to help plan charging or replacement.
- SOS button presses and two way voice session indicators are surfaced as incident events for rapid response.
- Displacement and overspeed events can be used with Plaspy geofencing and reporting to support operational rules and audits.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert anti theft tracking for trailers, service vehicles and high value mobile equipment.
- Container and cargo monitoring where waterproofing and fast magnetic installation are required.
- Construction and heavy equipment oversight with tamper and displacement detection.
- Long term remote deployments that depend on extended battery life and optional solar charging.
- Marine auxiliary asset tracking for rigid equipment and support gear where waterproofing is beneficial.
