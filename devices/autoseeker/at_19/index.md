# Autoseeker - AT-19

![AT-19](./tracker.jpg)

The AT-19 2G Mini GPS Tracking Device is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for discreet vehicle installation. Built for cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks and vans, the AT-19 delivers real-time tracking and essential telemetry over GSM/GPRS so fleet managers and vehicle owners can monitor position, speed and driving routes through the Plaspy platform.

With support for TCP/GPRS and SMS reporting, inline installation options and enterprise customization, the AT-19 is positioned as a practical choice for fleet management, anti-theft protection and logistics operations. Optional features—including remote voice monitoring and SOS emergency alerts—make it suitable for safety-conscious deployments when matched with the correct firmware or accessories.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking — GPS position and route history available on Plaspy dashboards.
- Compact, covert form factor \(89 × 51 × 15 mm\) ideal for short-term or hidden vehicle installation.
- 2G GSM/GPRS connectivity with TCP and SMS reporting for reliable telemetry and alerts.
- Vehicle telemetry features: ACC on/off \(ignition\) monitoring, overspeed alerts, door open/close alarms and geofence notifications.
- Remote fuel cut \(immobilizer\) capability to support anti-theft interventions when integrated via Plaspy.
- Optional remote voice monitoring and SOS emergency alerts for enhanced security and incident response.
- Customizable for enterprise deployments with advertised lifetime free platform access \(verify terms with supplier\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT-19 transmits GNSS position and vehicle telemetry to Plaspy via GSM/GPRS \(TCP\) or SMS. Once connected, Plaspy displays live location, speed, route history and alarm events, enabling dispatchers and fleet managers to react to incidents, optimize routes and enforce policies in real time. Remote commands sent from Plaspy can actuate supported outputs such as fuel cut where hardware and permissions allow.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: continuous position, speed and route reporting to Plaspy.
- Ignition and alarm status: ACC on/off detection, door open/close alarms and overspeed alerts are reported for immediate notification.
- Fuel monitoring and immobilizer actions: remote fuel cut support provides an anti-theft option through Plaspy command workflows \(requires correct wiring and authorization\).
- Geofence and event-based alerts: entry/exit notifications and configurable alarm thresholds feed directly into Plaspy alerting and reporting.
- Optional remote voice monitoring and SOS: can be integrated to trigger live audio monitoring or emergency escalation through Plaspy where supported by firmware/accessory.
- Bluetooth sensors: the AT-19 product page does not list built-in Bluetooth sensors; Plaspy deployments can often integrate external BLE accessories where platform integrations exist.

- Delivery and logistics — real-time location updates and geofence notifications improve ETA accuracy and proof-of-route.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT-19 couples a compact, covert design with essential vehicle telemetry and Plaspy compatibility to deliver cost-effective real-time tracking and anti-theft features. For fleet management teams the device supplies ignition \(ACC\) status, door and overspeed alarms, geofence events and remote fuel-cut capability — all of which are actionable through Plaspy dashboards and alerts. Its TCP/GPRS and SMS transport options make it flexible for deployments in areas with 2G coverage.

Before purchase, verify the battery capacity, supported voltage range and 2G network availability for your region, since some specification items differ between marketing text and the technical table and 2G is being phased out in certain countries. When matched with Plaspy, the AT-19 provides a scalable solution for telemetry, theft prevention and route oversight — and can be customized for enterprise workflows where additional firmware or accessories are required.
