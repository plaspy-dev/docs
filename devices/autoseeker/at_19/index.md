# Autoseeker - AT-19

![AT-19](./tracker.jpg)

The Autoseeker AT-19 is a compact 2G Mini GPS tracking device designed for discreet vehicle installation. Intended for cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks and vans, the AT-19 offers real-time position reporting and essential vehicle telemetry over GSM and GPRS channels. Its small footprint and vehicle-focused feature set make it suitable for fleet monitoring, theft deterrence and basic route oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT-19 can send location, speed and alarm events into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring and historical review. Support for GPRS data reporting and SMS alerts, together with common vehicle inputs and outputs, enables the AT-19 to feed Plaspy dashboards with the core information fleet managers rely on for visibility, alerting and operational control.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real time tracking with position and route history visible on Plaspy dashboards
- Compact, covert form factor suitable for short term or hidden vehicle installation
- 2G GSM GPRS connectivity with TCP and SMS reporting for telemetry and alerts
- Vehicle telemetry features including ignition status, overspeed alerts, door alarms and geofence notifications
- Remote fuel cut immobilizer support to assist anti theft workflows when integrated with Plaspy
- Optional remote voice monitoring and SOS emergency alerts for enhanced safety response
- Enterprise customization options and advertised platform access subject to supplier terms

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the AT-19 transmits GNSS position and vehicle status to Plaspy using GPRS data or SMS. Plaspy ingests those updates to present live location, speed and event information, and to trigger alerts or reporting as configured by fleet administrators. Remote commands initiated from Plaspy can operate supported outputs where the device hardware and permissions allow.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live mapping and monitoring
- Ignition and alarm status reporting including ACC on off detection and door open alerts
- Support for remote immobilizer actions to aid theft response workflows when authorized and wired
- Geofence entry and exit notifications feeding Plaspy alert channels and event logs
- Optional SOS and remote voice monitoring available to escalate emergencies through Plaspy
- Route history and event reporting for operational review and compliance reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route optimization for light and medium vehicle fleets
- Anti theft monitoring and immobilization as part of vehicle recovery processes
- Delivery and logistics tracking to improve ETA accuracy and route proofing
- Personal vehicle security with optional emergency alert capability
- Short term covert monitoring for investigative or temporary tracking requirements

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT-19 pairs a discreet physical design with the core vehicle telemetry features fleet teams expect. Its support for GPRS and SMS reporting, along with ignition and alarm inputs, enables straightforward integration into Plaspy for continuous visibility, alerting and basic remote control. For organizations that need a practical, low profile tracker to feed location and event data into Plaspy, the AT-19 presents a balanced choice.
