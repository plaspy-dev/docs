# SinoTrack - ST-908

![ST-908](./Logo.jpg)

The SinoTrack ST-908 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features and capabilities. With its compact design and durable construction, it is suitable for use in various vehicles, including cars, trucks, motorcycles, and more. The tracker operates in a temperature range of -15°C to 80°C, making it suitable for use in different weather conditions. It also has a protection degree of IP53, providing resistance against dust and water splashes.

One of the standout features of the ST-908 is its advanced GPS module, which utilizes Sirf IV technology and offers a high level of accuracy. With a position accuracy of 10m CEP without S/A, you can rely on precise location tracking. The tracker also supports 24-hour satellite positioning, allowing you to track your vehicle's location in real-time via SMS or GPRS connectivity. Additionally, it offers features such as over-speed alarm, SOS button, and main power on/off alarm, ensuring the safety and security of your vehicle.

The ST-908 is equipped with various input and output options, including ACC, door sensor, shock sensor, and fuel sensor. It also allows for remote control of fuel and electricity, providing added convenience and control. The tracker can be easily configured and customized through SMS or platform commands, allowing you to set parameters such as SOS and wiretapping TEL number, over-speed value, position report time interval, and more. It also supports break point storage, ensuring that location data is stored and reported when GSM signal is available.

With its comprehensive features and reliable performance, the SinoTrack ST-908 is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a high-quality GPS tracker for their vehicle. Whether you need to track your personal car or manage a fleet of vehicles, this tracker offers the functionality and flexibility you need.

### Key Features:

- 24-hour satellite positioning
- SMS location tracking
- Real-time location tracking via GPRS
- Authorized wiretapping with inside microphone
- Over-speed alarm
- SOS button
- Main power on/off alarm
- ACC, door sensor, shock sensor, and fuel sensor inputs
- Remote control of fuel and electricity
- Configurable parameters via SMS or platform commands
- Break point storage for GSM blind areas
- Camera connectivity for taking photos
