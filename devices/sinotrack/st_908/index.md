# SinoTrack - ST-908

<div className="device-logo">
![ST-908](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The SinoTrack ST-908 is a compact, vehicle-mounted GPS tracker designed for use in cars, trucks, motorcycles, and similar mobile assets. It combines a durable enclosure with a wide operating temperature range and an IP53 level of protection, making it suitable for a variety of environmental conditions. The device uses a Sirf IV GPS module to provide position accuracy typically around 10 m CEP and supports continuous satellite positioning with options for SMS and GPRS reporting.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST-908 can deliver location and event data into the Plaspy platform for monitoring and management. Its support for real time reporting, configurable alarms such as over-speed and SOS, and inputs for ACC, door, shock, and fuel sensors make it a practical choice for organizations that want to consolidate vehicle visibility and alerts inside Plaspy. Break point storage and platform command support further help ensure that historical positions and remote controls can be managed centrally through a fleet platform like Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and durable design suitable for cars, trucks, motorcycles, and similar vehicles
- Wide operating temperature range from -15°C to 80°C for varied climates
- IP53 protection rating for dust resistance and water splash protection
- Sirf IV GPS module with position accuracy around 10 m CEP without S A
- Continuous satellite positioning with SMS and GPRS reporting options
- Built in alarms including over speed, SOS, and main power on off alerts
- Multiple inputs for ACC door shock and fuel sensors plus remote control of fuel and electricity

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the ST-908 can stream position updates and alarm events into a centralized monitoring environment so operators can view vehicle status, respond to incidents, and generate reports. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it alongside other assets for unified fleet oversight.

- Live location tracking and map visualization of vehicle positions within Plaspy
- Alarm forwarding for events such as over speed SOS and main power changes
- Visibility into input states like ACC door shock and fuel sensor triggers
- Remote command support through platform controls where the device supports platform commands
- Synchronization of stored positions after GSM gaps using break point storage for complete trip reconstruction

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle monitoring for daily operations and route oversight
- Personal vehicle tracking for security and recovery purposes
- Motorcycle and light vehicle asset tracking in mixed fleets
- Theft deterrence and emergency response using SOS and alarm notifications
- Monitoring of engine or power status and sensor events for operational control

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ST-908 combines a practical feature set with environmental resilience, making it a sensible option for organizations that need reliable location reporting and event alerts. Its built in alarms and multiple input options allow teams to monitor both location and key vehicle signals, while break point storage helps preserve location history when connectivity is intermittent.

Paired with Plaspy, the ST-908 can be managed as part of a broader fleet or asset tracking strategy, enabling consolidated monitoring, alerting, and historical reporting. Because the device supports platform commands and configurable parameters, Plaspy can be used to tailor reporting and responses to operational needs without losing central oversight.
