# SinoTrack - ST-904L Features

This page provides public feature context for the SinoTrack ST-904L and how the device can be used with Plaspy for centralized tracking, alerting, and basic telemetry. It summarizes practical capabilities and the user value you can expect when integrating this model into Plaspy's platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior on any individual unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. Verify device specifics with the manufacturer documentation when planning deployments or critical workflows.

## Feature Overview

The ST-904L is a compact, discreet GPS tracker intended for vehicle and personal tracking scenarios. It combines cellular connectivity, on device location sensing, and basic alert and voice features to provide live tracking and situational awareness that can be routed into Plaspy.

- 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback for broad network coverage and SMS or GPRS reporting.
- Built-in high sensitivity GPS and GSM antennas in a small form factor suitable for motorcycles, bicycles, cars, pets, and valuables.
- SOS alarm and alarm reporting for immediate notification when triggered.
- Two-way voice and SMS control to allow direct communication and remote queries to the unit.
- Multiple power modes and an internal battery to balance tracking responsiveness and standby runtime.
- SMS configurability for server IP and APN to point the tracker to third party platforms like Plaspy.

## Core Features of SinoTrack - ST-904L

- Real time GPS position reporting via GPRS and SMS for live maps and breadcrumb trails.
- SOS alarm support to send an emergency alert to the monitoring platform or contacts.
- Two-way voice capability to call the device and speak through its built in voice channel.
- SMS based configuration and management including IMEI query and change commands where supported.
- Multiple power modes including Keep Working, Move, and Standby to optimize battery life.
- Motion or vibration wake and motion triggered tracking behavior for movement aware reporting.
- Geofence and overspeed alarm support as part of event reporting options.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When added to Plaspy, the ST-904L can report location and alarm events to provide visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy ingests position reports and alerts from compatible trackers and presents them in centralized maps, reports, and notification channels.

- Live location and history: GPRS or SMS position reports appear as live location updates and map history in Plaspy.
- Event forwarding: SOS, geofence, and overspeed alarms appear as events in Plaspy for notifications and incident tracking.
- Remote control and queries: SMS configuration workflows and two-way voice allow operators to query or contact the device while managing it from Plaspy.
- Simple platform integration: SMS commands that set server and APN can point the device to your Plaspy account so data is received by the platform.
- IMEI and activation workflow: IMEI management helps ensure a device is registrable and visible before adding to Plaspy inventory.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for family members or caregivers using SOS and two-way voice.
- Motorcycle and bicycle anti theft monitoring where discreet installation is required.
- Basic car tracking for location history, geofence alerts, and overspeed notifications.
- Portable asset protection for luggage or valuables that need occasional or motion triggered monitoring.
- Light fleet or single vehicle tracking where compact installation and basic telemetry are sufficient.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and regional variants can change which commands or alarms are available on a particular unit.
- Hardware revisions or bundled accessories may affect antenna performance, battery capacity, or mounting options.
- Installation and placement affect GPS reception and alarm reliability; professional installation can help meet expectations.
- IMEI change and local registration capabilities may be restricted or behave differently by country or firmware.
- Confirm available features on your specific unit against the manufacturer documentation before relying on them for critical workflows.
