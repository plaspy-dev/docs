# SinoTrack - ST-915L

![ST-915L](./tracker.jpg)

The SinoTrack ST-915L is a heavy-duty GPS tracker built for long-term, maintenance-free tracking and is Plaspy compatible when configured to report to a Plaspy server. Housed in a waterproof magnetic enclosure, the ST-915L is designed for covert or external mounting on cars, trucks, motorcycles and other mobile assets. With a 3.7V 10000 mAh rechargeable battery and LTE Cat-1 + 2G connectivity, it delivers reliable real-time tracking and long standby life for fleet management and anti-theft protection.

The ST-915L pairs with cloud tracking platforms and can be pointed at Plaspy by updating the device IP and APN settings via SMS. Its UBLOX UBX-G7020 GNSS chip provides ~5 meter positional accuracy and fast TTFF performance, making this GPS tracker a practical choice for telemetry-driven workflows, long-haul logistics, seasonal asset monitoring, and mixed-vehicle fleets that require robust, maintenance‑free devices.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: configurable to send location and telemetry to a Plaspy server via the device IP/APN SMS commands.
- Long standby life: large 3.7V 10000 mAh Li‑ion battery offers up to ~120 days standby under typical conditions.
- Rugged, covert mounting: waterproof magnetic enclosure for discreet external placement on vehicles and assets.
- Reliable GNSS performance: UBLOX UBX‑G7020 chip with ~5 m accuracy and fast TTFF \(Cold/Warm/Hot: 45s/35s/1s\).
- Dual cellular connectivity: LTE Cat‑1 for modern networks with legacy 2G GSM fallback \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\).
- Compact & lightweight: approximately 10 × 8 × 2.5 cm and ~45 g for flexible installation.
- Free online platform: SinoTrack Pro app and web platform available without subscription fees for immediate live tracking and history playback.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is straightforward because the ST-915L supports setting server IP and port via SMS configuration commands. Point the device to your Plaspy server address and APN, and the tracker will deliver position and telemetry packets over LTE/2G to your Plaspy instance for real-time tracking, alerts, and reporting.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over LTE Cat‑1 / 2G to the configured server.
- Fast and accurate position fixes: typical GNSS accuracy of ~5 m, with cold/warm/hot TTFF of ~45s/35s/1s.
- Battery and status reporting to Plaspy so managers can monitor device health and standby time remotely.
- History playback and geofencing workflows on Plaspy by importing device reports into the platform.
- Configurable via SMS: set IP, port and APN commands to redirect reporting to Plaspy \(examples supplied by the manufacturer\).
