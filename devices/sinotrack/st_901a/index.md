# SinoTrack - ST-901A

![ST-901A](./Logo.jpg)

The SinoTrack ST-901A is a compact GPS tracker designed for vehicle tracking and fleet oversight. It combines a small form factor with core tracking capabilities suited to both personal vehicles and commercial fleets. The device uses a Ubox 7020 GPS module and advertises position accuracy up to around 10 meters, along with tracking and acquisition sensitivity figures intended to support reliable satellite reception.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST-901A can deliver location and basic alarm data into a centralized fleet platform for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can ingest position updates sent from the tracker and present them alongside other assets for operational visibility, making the ST-901A a practical option when looking to add straightforward GPS tracking and alerting to your Plaspy deployment.

## Key Highlights

- Compact design suitable for use in both personal vehicles and fleet assets
- Positioning based on Ubox 7020 GPS module with advertised accuracy up to 10 meters
- Multiple tracking options including SMS requests and GPRS based real time reporting
- Alarm features for main power cut, shock events, and over speed notifications
- Remote control capabilities for fuel and power to support vehicle security
- Sensitivity ratings intended to support reliable satellite acquisition in varied conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the ST-901A supplies location updates and alarm notifications that Plaspy displays in a unified fleet management interface. Plaspy receives the tracker data and makes it actionable for monitoring, alerting, and basic reporting across vehicles.

- Real time position updates sent via GPRS appear in Plaspy for live tracking and route review
- Location queries via SMS can be used alongside Plaspy logging to validate current position on demand
- Alarm events such as power cut, shock, or speeding are forwarded into Plaspy to trigger notifications and incident records
- Remote fuel and power control actions reported by the device can be represented in Plaspy status dashboards
- Aggregated location history from the tracker supports basic trip reconstruction and operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location tracking for delivery and service operations
- Monitoring personal or company cars for location and security alerts
- Rental and shared vehicle oversight with basic immobilization controls
- Business reporting on vehicle movements and incident notifications
- Asset security where compact trackers and alarm alerts are required
