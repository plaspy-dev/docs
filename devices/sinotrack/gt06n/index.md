# SinoTrack - GT06N

![GT06N](./Logo.png)

The SinoTrack GT06N is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features to help you keep track of your vehicles. With its compact and slim design, it is easy to install and can be powered by either a cigarette lighter or the vehicle's battery power. This makes it a convenient choice for a variety of vehicles, including cars, trucks, motorcycles, and more.

One of the standout features of the GT06N is its accurate positioning capabilities. It utilizes a USA original SIRF III/LP ultra-low power consumption GPS chip, which ensures precise and reliable location tracking. With a position accuracy of 10 meters and a tracking sensitivity of -159dBm, you can trust that you will always know the exact whereabouts of your vehicle.

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the GT06N also offers a range of other useful features. It has an alarm function that can alert you in the event of a main power cut off, shock, or SOS situation. It also has the ability to remotely control fuel and power, allowing you to disable or enable them as needed. Furthermore, the GT06N supports wiretapping, allowing you to listen to the vehicle's inside voice when authorized.

Overall, the SinoTrack GT06N is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that is suitable for a wide range of applications. Whether you need to keep track of your personal vehicle or manage a fleet of vehicles for your business, the GT06N has the capabilities to meet your needs.

### Key Features:

- Accurate positioning with a USA original SIRF III/LP ultra-low power consumption GPS chip
- Position accuracy of 10 meters and tracking sensitivity of -159dBm
- Alarm function for main power cut off, shock, and SOS situations
- Remote control of fuel and power
- Supports wiretapping for authorized phone numbers
- Compact and slim design for easy installation
- Built-in switching power with a wide voltage input range
- Can be powered by cigarette lighter or vehicle battery power
