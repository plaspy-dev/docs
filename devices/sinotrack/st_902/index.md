# SinoTrack - ST-902

![ST-902](./tracker.jpg)

The SinoTrack ST-902 is a compact plug and play OBD2 GPS tracker designed for rapid deployment in cars, taxis and commercial fleets. It connects to a vehicle OBD2 port to provide continuous position reporting using cellular data and SMS fallback, and includes core alarm features such as shock, overspeed, geo fence and low battery notifications. A small internal rechargeable backup battery supports brief operation when external power is removed.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST-902 can be configured to forward location updates and alarm events to Plaspy for centralized visualization and reporting. Its SMS based configuration and support for standard server assignment make it straightforward to point the device at a Plaspy collection endpoint, enabling real time tracking, historical route analysis and alerting within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD2 form factor for fast vehicle onboarding and minimal installation effort
- Cellular data connectivity with SMS reporting as a fallback for reliable position updates
- Built in alarm suite including shock impact, overspeed, geo fence and low battery notifications
- Small internal rechargeable backup battery to maintain brief operation during power loss
- UBLOX GNSS receiver with manufacturer stated accuracy suitable for route and fleet monitoring
- Simple SMS configuration and server assignment so the device can be pointed to Plaspy
- Compatibility with SinoTrack PRO platform while allowing redirection of telemetry to Plaspy

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ST-902 reports location and alarm events using standard cellular reporting and SMS commands, and it can be configured to send that data to Plaspy. Once the device is set to forward telemetry to a Plaspy endpoint, its position updates and events appear in Plaspy dashboards and can be used for alerts and historical reporting.

- Real time location updates sent to Plaspy with SMS fallback for continued reporting when needed
- Immediate reporting of alarm events such as shock impact, overspeed, geo fence and low battery so Plaspy can trigger notifications
- Vehicle OBD2 data exposure can be consumed by Plaspy when the vehicle exposes relevant parameters and those values are enabled
- Remote device configuration via SMS for server assignment, APN settings and basic queries where direct access is impractical
- Ships ready for SinoTrack PRO but can be reconfigured to send unified telemetry into Plaspy for fleet level oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Rapid fleet onboarding where vehicles need fast activation without specialist installation
- Anti theft monitoring and tamper detection using shock and geo fence alerts for timely response
- Taxi and rideshare vehicle tracking for dispatch and utilization visibility
- Short term rental and car share programs where quick turnover and position logging are priorities
- Commercial vehicle tracking to monitor routes, alarm events and basic vehicle telemetry

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ST-902 is a practical choice for organizations that prioritize quick installation and straightforward configuration. Its OBD2 plug and play design reduces rollout time, and its cellular reporting with SMS fallback makes it suitable for day to day fleet tracking and anti theft monitoring. When paired with Plaspy, the ST-902 delivers centralized visibility of location and alarm events, enabling operational oversight and reporting without complex device management.
