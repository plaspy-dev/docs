# SinoTrack - ST-905/915

![ST-905/915](./Logo.jpg)

The SinoTrack ST-905/915 is a high-quality GPS tracker designed for various tracking applications. With its compact and durable design, this tracker is built to withstand harsh environments and provide accurate and reliable tracking information. It is equipped with advanced features and specifications that make it an ideal choice for personal and asset tracking.

One of the standout features of the ST-905/915 is its long-lasting battery life. With a built-in 10000mAh battery, this tracker can provide up to 120 days of standby time, ensuring continuous tracking without the need for frequent recharging. This makes it perfect for long-term tracking applications where extended battery life is essential.

In terms of positioning accuracy, the ST-905/915 utilizes a Ubox 7020 GPS module, which provides a position accuracy of up to 10 meters with 2D RMS. This level of accuracy ensures precise tracking and location information, allowing you to monitor the movement of your assets with confidence.

The ST-905/915 also offers a range of alarm features to enhance security and safety. It includes low battery alarm, shock alarm, and over-speed alarm, providing real-time notifications in case of any unexpected events or violations. Additionally, the tracker features a voice monitor function, allowing you to remotely listen to the surrounding environment for added peace of mind.

With its magnet and waterproof design, the ST-905/915 is easy to install and can withstand various weather conditions. Whether you need to track vehicles, motorcycles, or other valuable assets, this tracker offers a reliable and efficient solution. Its compatibility with SMS and GPRS connectivity allows for convenient tracking via SMS commands or real-time tracking through GPRS.

In summary, the SinoTrack ST-905/915 is a feature-packed GPS tracker that combines durability, accuracy, and advanced tracking capabilities. Whether you need to track personal belongings, vehicles, or other assets, this tracker provides the reliability and functionality you need.
