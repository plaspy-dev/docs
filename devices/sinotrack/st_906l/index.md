# SinoTrack - ST-906L

<div className="device-logo">
![ST-906L](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The SinoTrack ST-906L is a compact 4G GPS tracker designed for cars and motorcycles. It combines LTE and GSM cellular connectivity with a high sensitivity built in GPS and GSM antenna to provide reliable real time location reporting. The unit supports SMS and GPRS reporting and ships with a free SinoTrack Pro web and mobile app for route history playback and device management, while offering core anti theft features such as ACC detection, main power off alarm, SOS panic alert, overspeed and shock notifications, and a relay controlled immobilizer.

As a fully Plaspy compatible device, the ST-906L can feed its location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring across single vehicles and fleets. Plaspy can ingest the tracker’s live updates and alerts to provide unified mapping, historical playback, configurable notifications, and operational oversight, making the ST-906L a practical choice for organizations that want to combine proven tracker hardware with Plaspy platform capabilities.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for both motorcycles and cars for discreet and practical installation.
- 4G LTE and GSM cellular connectivity with a high sensitivity built in GPS and GSM antenna for consistent positioning.
- Core anti theft and safety features including SOS panic alert, main power off alarm, ACC ignition detection, overspeed and shock alerts.
- Relay controlled immobilizer for remote cut off combined with backup battery reporting to help monitor power loss and theft scenarios.
- Supports SMS and GPRS reporting and works with the free SinoTrack Pro platform alongside Plaspy for route history and account management.
- Designed for fleet and single vehicle use to balance reliable tracking with straightforward setup and ongoing monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the ST-906L streams its location updates and event notifications into a single Plaspy dashboard so operators can monitor vehicles in real time and review historical activity. Typical interactions between the tracker and Plaspy include device configuration via carrier settings and simple SMS commands, then ongoing data delivery for mapping and alerts.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via GPRS and LTE with SMS as a complementary channel where available.
- Ignition and ACC status visible in Plaspy for operational awareness and simple vehicle state reporting.
- Power loss and backup battery alerts forwarded into Plaspy for continuity monitoring and rapid response.
- Geo fence, overspeed, shock and SOS events sent as alerts to Plaspy for immediate notification and historical auditing.
- Remote immobilizer actions can be coordinated through Plaspy where relay control is enabled on the device and permitted by local setup.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small and medium vehicle fleets requiring live tracking and historical route playback.
- Anti theft protection for cars and motorcycles using SOS, main power off alerts, and remote immobilizer features.
- Rental and shared vehicle monitoring with ignition reporting, geo fencing and instant alerts for unauthorized use.
- Driver safety and compliance monitoring using overspeed and shock event reporting for incident review.
- Personal emergency response for riders or drivers using the SOS panic function tied into centralized alerting.
