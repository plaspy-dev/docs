# SinoTrack - ST-907L Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the SinoTrack ST-907L as used with Plaspy. It summarizes the model's practical capabilities, the kinds of telemetry you can expect to see in a Plaspy deployment, and the operational benefits for vehicle security and fleet monitoring. The goal is to help fleet managers, installers, and vehicle owners understand how this Plaspy compatible tracker fits into real time tracking workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and the specifics of installation. Where possible this page uses the manufacturer supplied description as the factual basis, but customers should confirm device specific details and firmware behavior with the official manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The ST-907L is a compact wired tracker designed for motorcycles and cars with a focus on reliable positioning, anti theft protection, and remote vehicle control. When integrated with Plaspy, the unit supplies location fixes, event alarms, and control functions that feed into live maps, history reports, and alerting workflows for operations and security teams.

- Dual GNSS positioning with GPS and GLONASS for stable location fixes and consistent tracking performance.
- Cellular connectivity across 4G LTE and 2G GSM for wide area coverage and fallback connectivity.
- Built in backup battery to preserve alerts and basic tracking during main power loss for improved anti theft recovery.
- Remote immobilizer support and event alarms such as vibration, power off, low battery, and overspeed to support proactive security actions.
- Small form factor and wide operating voltage for easy installation across a variety of vehicle types.

## Core Features of SinoTrack - ST-907L

- Dual GNSS support using a SIMCOM 7670SA module for GPS and GLONASS positioning with AGPS assisted fixes.
- 4G LTE and 2G GSM network support to maintain connectivity and allow fallback when needed.
- Built in backup battery to maintain tracking and send alerts if vehicle power is cut.
- Vibration or displacement alarm to detect movement or tampering when parked.
- Power off and low battery alarms to notify operators of potential tampering or power issues.
- Remote engine cut off and resume functions to support immobilization workflows.
- Mileage reporting for basic fleet analytics and vehicle usage tracking.
- Anti pseudo base station detection to help protect telemetry integrity against fake cell tower attacks.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the ST-907L is connected to Plaspy, its location fixes and event messages become visible in a centralized tracking account. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions, alarms, and mileage reports so teams can monitor assets, configure alerting, and review historical activity from a single interface.

- Live map tracking and periodic position reports appear in Plaspy dashboards for real time visibility and route replay.
- Geofence events and perimeter alerts are usable in Plaspy to trigger notifications and operational workflows.
- Vibration, power off, and low battery alarms are forwarded to Plaspy for instant notification to administrators or designated contacts.
- Remote immobilizer controls reported by the device can be reflected as actionable controls in Plaspy for anti theft response.
- Mileage data is available for basic reporting and fleet utilization analysis inside Plaspy.
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol to simplify integration and reduce manual configuration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium vehicle fleets that require location tracking and mileage reporting.
- Anti theft protection with remote immobilization and backup battery tracking to recover stolen vehicles.
- Motorcycle and scooter tracking where compact size and wide voltage tolerance simplify discreet installation.
- Delivery and service vehicle monitoring to validate stops, review routes, and resolve customer disputes.
- Mixed fleet asset security for vehicles operating on 12V or 24V systems using a single device model.
