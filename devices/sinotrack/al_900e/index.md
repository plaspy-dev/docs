# SinoTrack - AL-900E

![AL-900E](./Logo.png)

The SinoTrack AL-900E is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for a wide range of vehicles including cars, trucks, and motorcycles. It pairs durable construction with environmental resilience, operating across a broad temperature range and offering IP53 protection against dust and splashes. The unit uses a Sirf IV GPS module with multiple channels to provide position fixes and reports location with stated accuracy suitable for routine tracking and fleet oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AL-900E can forward its location and event data to the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and management. Its support for SMS and platform based reporting, configurable alarms, input and output monitoring, and remote parameter adjustments mean the tracker can be integrated into Plaspy workflows for real time visibility, alerting, and historical route analysis while retaining flexibility for different operational needs.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and robust design suitable for cars trucks and motorcycles
- Wide operating temperature range from -15°C to 80°C for varied environments
- IP53 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water splashes
- Sirf IV GPS module with multichannel reception and a typical position accuracy of around 10 m CEP
- Multiple alarm types including overspeed SOS and main power on off for security monitoring
- Inputs and outputs for external sensors and remote control of fuel and power related functions
- Supports SMS location tracking real time reporting and platform based data upload with breakpoint storage for intermittent coverage

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the AL-900E sends position and event messages that Plaspy ingests to provide maps alerts and historical tracking. Plaspy presents that data in dashboards and reporting tools so fleet managers and operators can monitor assets from a single interface.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps using the tracker position reports
- Automatic event and alarm notifications such as overspeed and SOS forwarded to Plaspy alerting systems
- Historical route playback and trip reporting leveraging stored location points and breakpoint storage
- Remote configuration and parameter updates via platform based messaging where supported by the device
- Visibility into input output events such as door or fuel sensor changes presented within Plaspy dashboards
- Consolidated reporting and exportable logs for operational review and compliance

## Typical Use Cases

- Small and medium fleet tracking for cars delivery vans and light trucks
- Vehicle security and recovery monitoring with alarm and SOS reporting
- Mixed vehicle operations including motorcycles and utility vehicles
- Delivery and logistics visibility with historical route analysis
- Remote or regional operations that require rugged temperature tolerance and breakpoint data capture
