# Megastek - MT80 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Megastek MT80 Series GPS watch with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities, monitoring workflows, and the ways MT80 telemetry and events can be used inside Plaspy for real time visibility and historical review.

Exact feature availability depends on firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation choices. Firmware behavior, optional modules, and manufacturer settings can change what telemetry and alarms are present on a given unit or deployment.

## Feature Overview

The MT80 Series is a wearable GPS tracker built for health and personal safety use cases. It combines location reporting with on‑body health telemetry and safety alarms to support caregivers, monitoring services, and supervised deployments.

- Wearable GPS tracking for continuous position updates and historical route playback
- On‑body health telemetry including skin temperature, SpO2 and heart rate forwarding
- SOS and two‑way voice for urgent assistance and responder coordination
- Event alarms such as geo‑fence, low battery, belt on/off and cut detection
- Dual hardware variants offering Bluetooth forwarding or Wi‑Fi assisted positioning

## Core Features of Megastek - MT80

- Real time location reporting via standard tracking channels such as SMS and GPRS
- Health sensors for skin temperature, blood oxygen saturation SpO2 and heart rate monitoring
- SOS alarm and two‑way voice for direct communication and emergency signaling
- Event alarms covering geo‑fence, low battery, belt off or cut detection, and similar status events
- Bluetooth 5.0 forwarding on the MT80BLE variant to pair sensors or a phone gateway
- Wi‑Fi assisted positioning and upload on the MT804G variant to improve urban fixes and data delivery
- Proven GNSS positioning using a Ublox MAX‑8Q module for consistent fixes and track queries
- Wearable design features including water resistance and wireless charging for daily use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location, event and health telemetry reported by the MT80 to provide unified visibility across devices and users. The platform can surface alarms, show live positions on a map, and retain historical telemetry for review and reporting.

- Live location and event reports appear on Plaspy dashboards for situational awareness and dispatch
- Health telemetry forwarded by the watch or via a paired phone can be routed into Plaspy as supplemental device data
- SOS and alarm events trigger notifications and can be linked to monitoring workflows inside Plaspy
- Wi‑Fi assisted fixes and Bluetooth forwarded data improve the reliability of positions and health updates shown in Plaspy
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and supports mapping device fields into platform alarms and reports for easy integration

## Typical Use Cases

- Outdoor elderly care and patient monitoring where location and vitals help caregivers respond rapidly
- Personal protection for supervised individuals using SOS and two‑way voice to summon help
- Parole or supervised release monitoring where geo‑fence and status alarms support oversight
- Lone worker safety programs that require wearable alarms and location visibility
- Medical transport or asset supervision where wearable biometric context complements location tracking

## Feature Availability Notes

- The MT80 Series includes multiple hardware variants; specific features like Bluetooth forwarding or Wi‑Fi assistance vary by model
- Firmware versions and regional device configurations can change telemetry frequency, alarm types, and data fields
- Installation and pairing choices (for example using a phone gateway) affect how health telemetry reaches Plaspy
- Manufacturer documentation is the authoritative source for supported sensors and regional cellular bands
- Integrators may use protocol customization on the device to map fields into Plaspy workflows but should validate mappings with test data

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the Megastek MT80 Series with Plaspy gives organizations a unified view of location and wearable health telemetry. Plaspy consolidates position updates, alarms, and sensor reports so monitoring teams and responders can make faster, better informed decisions without switching between systems.
