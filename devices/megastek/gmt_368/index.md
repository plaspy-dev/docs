# Megastek - GMT-368

![GMT-368](./logo.jpg)

The Megastek GMT-368 is a powerful GPS tracker specifically designed for motorcycles. With its compact size and robust features, it is the perfect solution for tracking and monitoring your motorcycle's location and activities. Whether you are a motorcycle enthusiast or a fleet manager, the GMT-368 offers a wide range of features to ensure the safety and security of your motorcycle.

One of the standout features of the GMT-368 is its ability to track your motorcycle in real-time. Using SMS or GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) communication, you can easily monitor the location of your motorcycle at any time. Additionally, the tracker supports regular tracking intervals, allowing you to set the frequency at which the device sends location updates. This feature is particularly useful for fleet management, as it provides accurate and up-to-date information on the whereabouts of your motorcycles.

Another notable feature of the GMT-368 is its geo-fencing capability. You can set up virtual boundaries, or geo-fences, for your motorcycle, and receive instant alerts whenever the motorcycle enters or exits these predefined areas. This feature is ideal for monitoring unauthorized use or ensuring that your motorcycle stays within a designated area.

The GMT-368 also offers over-speed and no GPS signal alarms, providing additional security and peace of mind. In the event that your motorcycle exceeds a pre-set speed limit, you will receive an alert, allowing you to take immediate action. Similarly, if the GPS signal is lost, you will be notified, ensuring that you are aware of any potential issues with the tracker.

With its ACC detection feature, the GMT-368 can detect the ignition status of your motorcycle. This allows you to monitor the on/off status of your motorcycle, providing valuable information for theft prevention and recovery.

In summary, the Megastek GMT-368 is a feature-rich GPS tracker that offers real-time tracking, geo-fencing, over-speed and no GPS signal alarms, and ACC detection. Whether you are a motorcycle enthusiast or a fleet manager, this tracker provides the necessary tools to ensure the safety and security of your motorcycle.
