# Megastek - MT-100

![MT-100](./logo.jpg)

The Megastek MT-100 is a handheld GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable tracking for various applications. With its waterproof design \(waterproofing grade: IP66\), this tracker can withstand harsh weather conditions and is suitable for outdoor use. Whether you need to track your pet, monitor a vehicle, or ensure the safety of a loved one, the MT-100 offers a range of features to meet your needs.

One of the standout features of the MT-100 is its AGPS position assisted technology, which helps to improve the speed and accuracy of location tracking. In case of an emergency, the tracker also features an SOS alarm with LBS position assistance, allowing for quick and efficient response. Additionally, the MT-100 supports two-way communication, enabling you to communicate with the tracker remotely.

Other notable features of the Megastek MT-100 include real-time tracking, over speed alarm, geo-fence alarm, vibration alarm, low battery alarm, and data logging capabilities using a Micro SD card. The tracker also supports SMS and GPRS communication, allowing for seamless communication and tracking. With its long standby time mode and support for up to 5 authorized cell phone numbers, the MT-100 offers flexibility and convenience.

Overall, the Megastek MT-100 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that combines advanced features with a rugged design. Whether you need to track your assets, monitor your loved ones, or ensure the safety of your pets, the MT-100 is a reliable choice.

### Key Features:

- Waterproof design \(waterproofing grade: IP66\)
- AGPS position assisted technology
- SOS alarm with LBS position assistance
- Two-way communication
- Real-time tracking
- Over speed alarm
- Geo-fence alarm

- Low battery alarm
- Data logging capabilities using a Micro SD card
- Support for up to 5 authorized cell phone numbers
