# Megastek - MT-100

<div className="device-logo">
![MT-100](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Megastek MT-100 is a handheld GPS tracker designed for reliable location tracking in a variety of environments. Built with a waterproof IP66 rating, the MT-100 is intended for outdoor use and everyday field work. It combines assisted GPS positioning to improve location speed and accuracy with practical features such as real time tracking, SOS alarm with LBS position assistance, two way communication, and on device data logging using a Micro SD card.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT-100 can feed location updates and alert events into a centralized monitoring platform. This makes it useful for organizations that need consolidated visibility across assets, personnel, or vehicles. Plaspy can use the MT-100's position reports, alarms, and logged history to provide map based tracking, alerts, and operational reporting without requiring changes to the tracker hardware.

## Key Highlights

- Handheld design with IP66 waterproofing for outdoor durability
- AGPS assisted positioning for improved location speed and consistency
- Built in SOS alarm with LBS assistance for emergency notifications
- Real time tracking and a range of configurable alarms including geo fence and over speed
- Two way communication plus support for SMS and GPRS reporting
- Micro SD data logging and support for multiple authorized phone numbers

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the MT-100 becomes a source of live and historical location information that can be displayed and analyzed from a single interface. Plaspy ingests position updates and alarm events, then translates them into map views, alerts, and reports to support operational decisions.

- Live location display for single units or groups on Plaspy maps
- Alert forwarding for SOS, geo fence, overspeed, vibration, and low battery events
- Historical tracking and data playback using logged position data
- Fleet and asset monitoring with configurable notifications and reporting
- Centralized visibility for teams using multiple MT-100 units
- Use of two way communication where supported to confirm status or send basic commands

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring with SOS alarm and live tracking
- Vehicle or light equipment monitoring in outdoor conditions
- Pet or vulnerable person tracking where waterproofing and portability matter
- Short term rental or field deployments requiring location logs on removable media
- Small fleet oversight where handheld trackers are preferable to fixed installations

The MT-100 combines a rugged handheld form factor with a practical set of alarms and logging features that suit many field and safety oriented applications. Its AGPS assisted positioning and SOS capability are particularly relevant for users who need faster fixes and reliable emergency notification in outdoor settings.
