# Megastek - GVT-390

![GVT-390](./logo.jpg)

The Megastek GVT-390 is a high-quality GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable tracking for various applications. With its compact size and advanced features, it is suitable for both personal and commercial use. The GVT-390 utilizes a SiRF Star III GPS chipset, ensuring precise location tracking. It also features a SIM900 GSM chipset, providing quad-band connectivity \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) for seamless communication.

One of the standout features of the GVT-390 is its ability to support multiple instructions simultaneously. This means you can set various commands and configurations at the same time, allowing for greater customization and flexibility. Additionally, the tracker supports setting a SMS center and up to 5 authorized cell phone numbers, ensuring secure and controlled access to the device.

The GVT-390 offers a range of useful functions, including track by demand, power saving mode, SOS button for immediate rescue and alarm, geo-fencing alarm, low power warning, over speeding alarm, motion sensor, and background voice surveillance. These features make it an ideal choice for fleet management, asset tracking, personal safety, and more.

With its compact dimensions of 104X97X37mm and a net weight of 730g, the GVT-390 is easy to install and conceal. It comes in a sleek black color and is packed securely in a box measuring 18.5\*13\*9CM. The tracker is also equipped with inputs and outputs, including 4 digital inputs \(including ACC\) and 2 analog inputs, as well as 2 outputs for added functionality.

Overall, the Megastek GVT-390 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers precise tracking, advanced functionality, and ease of use. Whether you need to monitor your fleet, track valuable assets, or ensure personal safety, the GVT-390 is a versatile solution that delivers exceptional performance.

### Key Features:

- SiRF Star III GPS chipset for accurate tracking
- SIM900 GSM chipset for quad-band connectivity
- Supports multiple instructions simultaneously
- Supports setting a SMS center and authorized cell phone numbers
- Track by demand, power saving mode, and SOS button
- Geo-fencing alarm, low power warning, and over speeding alarm
- Motion sensor and background voice surveillance
- Supports Data Logger using Micro SD card
- Inputs/Outputs: 4 digital inputs \(including ACC\), 2 analog inputs, 2 outputs
