# Megastek - MT100

<div className="device-logo">
![MT100](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The MT100 is a compact 4G personal GPS tracker from Megastek designed for personal protection, security personnel, and public safety applications. It combines multi mode positioning, rugged IP67 housing, two way voice communication and SOS emergency alerts to provide dependable real time location and incident signalling in both outdoor and mixed indoor environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT100 can feed location fixes, event alarms and device status into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. Open protocol documentation and support for remote firmware updates make the MT100 straightforward to integrate with Plaspy dashboards, alerting rules and reporting workflows for teams that require reliable tracking and safety features.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible with open protocol documentation to simplify integration for real time tracking and alerts.
- Multi mode positioning that leverages GNSS and assisted methods to improve location fixes in challenging environments.
- Two way voice and SOS emergency alarm for direct communication and rapid incident reporting.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure with wireless charging and long standby capability for continuous field use.
- Built in movement detection and vibration alarm to support anti theft and tamper workflows.
- Configurable alerts including SOS coordinates and geofence notifications to surface critical events in Plaspy.
- Remote management features for firmware updates and configuration to reduce maintenance overhead.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the MT100 supplies continuous location and event streams that Plaspy presents on maps, timelines and reports. Plaspy can use those inputs to drive geofence rules, responder notifications and historical playback so teams maintain situational awareness and operational control.

- Real time location and telemetry displayed on Plaspy maps for live monitoring and route review.
- SOS emergency alerts forwarded to Plaspy to notify designated responders and log incident coordinates.
- Geofence and movement or vibration events reported to Plaspy to support anti theft and tamper detection workflows.
- Voice call and monitoring events available to be logged and correlated in Plaspy when voice metadata is captured.
- Remote firmware updates and configuration changes streamlined to keep devices aligned with Plaspy reporting needs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal security and lone worker protection using SOS alerts and live tracking for rapid response.
- Security teams and public safety staff tracking for patrol management and resource coordination.
- Asset protection and anti theft monitoring for portable equipment and valuables.
- Event safety and operations where compact waterproof trackers are needed for staff in mixed environments.
- Managed deployments and remote device maintenance using over the air updates and centralized control.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT100 pairs practical hardware features with integration friendly protocol support, making it a suitable choice for organizations that need reliable personal tracking and incident signalling. Its combination of multi mode positioning, SOS and two way voice helps maintain visibility of personnel and assets, while remote management options reduce ongoing maintenance work for administrators.
