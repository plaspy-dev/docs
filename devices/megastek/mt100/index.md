# MT100 4G Personal GPS Tracker

The MT100 4G Personal GPS Tracker is a compact, rugged, and Plaspy compatible device designed for personal protection, security personnel, and public safety applications. With multi‑mode positioning \(GPS + Wi‑Fi + A‑GPS + LBS\), IP67 waterproofing and a U‑blox GNSS module, the MT100 delivers reliable real‑time tracking and dependable telemetry where it matters most.

The device combines two‑way voice communication, SOS emergency alarm with coordinate transmission, wireless charging and long standby modes to provide a practical anti‑theft and lone‑worker safety solution. Its open protocol support and firmware‑over‑the‑air \(FOTA\) updates make the MT100 straightforward to integrate into Plaspy for fleet management, emergency response workflows and centralized monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: open protocol documentation and third‑party platform support simplify integration with Plaspy for real‑time tracking and alerts.
- Multi‑mode positioning \(GPS + Wi‑Fi + A‑GPS + LBS\) for fast, accurate location fixes even in urban canyons and indoor‑edge scenarios.
- Two‑way voice and voice monitoring for direct communication with personnel or remote listening during an incident.
- Compact, waterproof IP67 housing with wireless charging and long battery standby for continuous field use.
- Built‑in G‑sensor and vibration alarm to detect movement or tampering and trigger telemetry or geo‑fence alerts.
- Comprehensive alerts: SOS coordinates, low battery, geo‑fence breach and configurable interval or real‑time tracking.
- Remote management with FOTA ensures devices stay updated and compatible with evolving Plaspy features and telemetry standards.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the MT100 with Plaspy lets organizations combine precise location data and device events with Plaspy’s real‑time dashboard, geofencing rules and reporting tools. The MT100 transmits GNSS fixes, Wi‑Fi assisted positions and LBS location over cellular \(GPRS/TCP/UDP\), so Plaspy receives continuous telemetry for live monitoring, historical playback and automated alerts.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates sent over 2G/3G/4G networks for live tracking on Plaspy maps.
- SOS emergency alarm that sends coordinates to Plaspy and triggers notifications to designated responders.
- Geo‑fence alarms and G‑sensor/vibration events reported to Plaspy for anti‑theft and tamper detection workflows.
- Voice call and monitoring events available to be logged and correlated in Plaspy when voice metadata is enabled.
- FOTA and remote configuration allow Plaspy administrators to keep MT100 firmware current and adjust reporting intervals for telemetry, power‑saving, or real‑time tracking.
