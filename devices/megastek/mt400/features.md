# Megastek - MT400 Features

This page provides a public overview of the Megastek MT400 feature set as it relates to use with Plaspy. It summarizes the device's practical capabilities for location tracking and continuous health telemetry, and explains how those capabilities can be reflected on the Plaspy platform for monitoring, alerts, and history. Use this content to understand what the MT400 is designed to do when integrated with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and the specifics of an installation. The details here are based on the device description and public manufacturer information; always check the manufacturer documentation for the most current device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The MT400 combines wearable medical telemetry and positioning in a compact watch form factor intended for patient and senior monitoring scenarios. It pairs real-time ECG and continuous heart rate telemetry with hybrid positioning and platform-friendly communications so monitoring systems like Plaspy can present location and health events together.

- Medical grade ECG waveform capture and continuous heart rate streaming for ongoing health monitoring.
- Hybrid positioning that uses GPS with network and Wi‑Fi assisted methods to improve indoor and outdoor coverage.
- Emergency and safety features including SOS alerts, fall detection, and two-way voice for caregiver communication.
- Multi network cellular plus Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth 4.0 connectivity to maintain data delivery and sensor integration.
- Platform friendly protocols with GPRS data logging, SMS alerts and protocol customization to integrate into Plaspy and other monitoring systems.

## Core Features of Megastek - MT400

- Real-time ECG waveform capture for event awareness and retrospective review.
- Continuous heart rate monitoring streamed to a monitoring platform.
- Hybrid positioning support combining GPS with network and Wi‑Fi assistance for more reliable tracking.
- SOS alarm and fall detection to trigger timely notifications and escalation.
- Two-way voice and voice monitoring to enable direct communication between wearer and caregiver.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for auxiliary sensor support and proximity based functions.
- Multi generation cellular support plus Wi‑Fi connectivity to maximize coverage and data delivery.
- Protocol customization and platform interoperability to simplify integration with Plaspy.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the MT400 can feed location and health telemetry into a single monitoring view so caregivers and operations teams can correlate movement and medical events. Plaspy ingests standard tracking messages from the device and presents live maps, event notifications, and historical records relevant to patient safety and remote care.

- Real time location updates and position history displayed on Plaspy maps for operational tracking.
- ECG waveform and continuous heart rate data presented as telemetry or alerts within the monitoring interface.
- SOS and fall detection events routed to Plaspy as platform alerts for immediate attention.
- Two-way voice and voice monitoring supported through platform connections for caregiver communication.
- Hybrid positioning indicators and Wi‑Fi assisted fixes shown as improved position accuracy in monitoring views.

## Typical Use Cases

- Remote patient monitoring where continuous ECG and heart rate feed into a centralized monitoring service.
- Assisted living and elderly safety programs using fall detection and SOS alerts for faster response.
- Home care and mobile health visits with location aware monitoring and caregiver communication.
- Clinical research and trials that require time stamped physiological telemetry with location context.
- Personal safety monitoring for vulnerable individuals who need combined health and location visibility.
