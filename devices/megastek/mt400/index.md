# Megastek - MT400

![MT400](./tracker.jpg)

The Megastek MT400 ECG Medical Watch is a wearable GPS tracker designed to combine continuous health monitoring with dependable location services. Intended for patients, seniors, and remote care workflows, the MT400 captures ECG waveform and continuous heart rate while offering position reporting and two-way voice in a compact wrist device. Its design emphasizes simultaneous visibility of personal health signals and movement for caregivers and operations teams.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT400 can stream location and medical telemetry into Plaspy for unified monitoring and response. The watch’s platform-friendly behavior and protocol customization make it suitable for integration with Plaspy, enabling caregivers and dispatch teams to view live location, receive medical alerts, and maintain historical records within a single monitoring environment.

## Key Highlights

- Medical grade telemetry on the move with ECG waveform capture and continuous heart rate monitoring.
- Plaspy compatible through protocol customization and standard tracking interfaces for straightforward integration.
- Hybrid positioning for improved indoor and outdoor coverage to support reliable location tracking.
- Emergency features including SOS, fall detection, and two-way voice for rapid caregiver contact.
- Multi connectivity support including cellular, Wi Fi and Bluetooth 4.0 to maintain data delivery in varied conditions.
- Wearable form factor designed for patient and senior use with a color touch display and accessible controls.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the MT400 provides a combined view of location and health telemetry so monitoring teams can act on both movement and medical events. Plaspy ingests the watch’s reporting messages and presents them alongside other assets to support operational oversight and caregiver workflows.

- Live location updates and position history for tracking movement and route review.
- Continuous heart rate and ECG waveform streaming used to trigger medical alerts and event logging.
- SOS and fall detection routed to Plaspy for immediate notification and escalation workflows.
- Two-way voice and voice monitoring available through platform integration to facilitate direct communication.
- Geofence alerts and configurable notifications for perimeter breaches and location based rules.

## Typical Use Cases

- Remote patient monitoring with continuous ECG and heart rate data available to care teams.
- Elderly safety and assisted living scenarios using fall detection and SOS alerts for rapid response.
- Mobile home care and community nursing where location aware visits and condition monitoring are needed.
- Clinical studies requiring time stamped ECG capture combined with location context for event analysis.
- Personal safety for vulnerable users and lone workers with real time position sharing and alerts.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT400 is a practical option for organizations that need to converge health telemetry and location tracking on a single platform. Its wearable form and medical telemetry capabilities let Plaspy users monitor physiological events alongside movement, helping caregivers and operations teams prioritize responses and maintain patient histories without juggling separate systems.

Because the device is designed for platform interoperability and offers customization options, integrating the MT400 into Plaspy deployments typically supports common tracking workflows such as live maps, alarm routing, and historical reporting. For teams that require both personal safety monitoring and broader operational visibility, the MT400 can complement other asset trackers within Plaspy to create a unified monitoring solution.
