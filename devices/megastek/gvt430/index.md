# Megastek - GVT430

![GVT430](./tracker.jpg)

The GVT-430 is a vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable real time tracking and telemetry in cars, trucks and commercial fleets. It pairs a high sensitivity GNSS receiver with cellular communications and supports position reporting by both data and SMS channels. The device is built with a wide DC input range and an internal backup battery, making it suitable for demanding fleet management and anti theft deployments where continuity of tracking is important.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the GVT-430 can deliver location updates, alarm events and logged routes into Plaspy for operational visibility and reporting. Its configurable communication options and available protocol documentation make it a practical choice for integrators and fleet operators who want to map device telemetry into Plaspy dashboards, alerts and historical playback without extensive custom development.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into existing fleet monitoring workflows and dashboards
- Real time location reporting via data and SMS channels to provide redundancy during connectivity issues
- High sensitivity GPS receiver for consistent position fixes and reliable historical route playback
- Multiple alarm types including geofence breach, overspeed, low battery, distance alarm and external power cut detection
- Wide DC input range combined with an internal backup battery to reduce downtime across vehicle types
- Onboard flash memory for local data logging when network connectivity is interrupted
- Multi band cellular SKUs and optional Wi Fi variant to better match regional network environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

Connecting the GVT-430 to Plaspy uses the tracker’s configurable reporting modes so position and telemetry data are delivered into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerts and analytics. SMS reporting can act as a fallback channel when packet data is unavailable, and provided protocol documentation helps map device fields into Plaspy event and telemetry structures.

- Real time location and telemetry updates feed Plaspy dashboards for live fleet visibility
- Alarm events such as geofence breaches and overspeed are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and workflow triggers
- Local data logging enables route playback and recovery of missed telemetry after temporary network loss
- SMS position reports provide an alternate notification path for urgent alerts or position confirmation
- Configurable communication protocols and documentation simplify mapping device telemetry into Plaspy reports and alerts
- OEM customization options support bespoke integrations and additional telemetry mapping when required

## Typical Use Cases

- Daily fleet management for cars, vans and trucks with live tracking and historical route playback
- Anti theft monitoring using geofence alerts, power cut detection and SMS failover
- Remote asset tracking for trailers, rental vehicles and offsite equipment with intermittent connectivity
- Operational monitoring and compliance reporting with overspeed and distance alerts
- Integration and OEM projects that require protocol mapping and configurable device behavior

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GVT-430 is a solid option for organizations seeking a balance of resilient connectivity, local logging and configurable integration options. Its redundancy in reporting channels and onboard storage help maintain continuity of tracking in variable network conditions, while provided documentation and protocol settings make it easier to integrate device telemetry into Plaspy for alerts, dashboards and reports.
