# MT90N NB‑IoT Personal GPS Tracker — Plaspy compatible

The MT90N is a compact, NB‑IoT personal GPS tracker designed for reliable personal safety and light asset monitoring. Engineered for children, the elderly, pets and lone workers, the MT90N combines u‑blox GNSS, Wi‑Fi and LBS positioning to deliver accurate location data in challenging environments. As a Plaspy compatible device, it integrates smoothly into Plaspy for real‑time tracking, alerts and historical playback.

With IP66–67 water protection, a dedicated SOS button and up to 10 days standby in personal mode, the MT90N balances endurance and portability. The device reports location over TCP/UDP and supports geo‑fence alerts, low battery warnings and configurable reporting intervals — making it a practical GPS tracker for personal safety, short‑term vehicle monitoring and broader telemetry workflows when used with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: Integrates location and telemetry into Plaspy for unified monitoring and reporting.
- Multi‑mode positioning: GPS + Wi‑Fi + LBS improves accuracy indoors and in urban canyons.
- NB‑IoT connectivity \(B1/B3/B5/B8\) for low‑power, wide‑area cellular reporting.
- Compact and rugged: 50 × 40 × 20 mm, ~50 g and IP66–67 rated for outdoor use.
- Dedicated SOS button and LED status indicators for immediate alerts and easy diagnostics.
- Long standby: Up to 10 days in personal mode \(dependent on reporting interval and settings\).
- Simple charging and operation: Mini USB charging and two‑button physical controls \(SOS + power\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

The MT90N sends position fixes and telemetry to Plaspy over TCP/UDP using NB‑IoT connectivity. Plaspy ingests those messages and displays real‑time tracking, triggers geo‑fence events, logs historical tracks and issues low battery or SOS alerts. Wi‑Fi and LBS assistance reduce time‑to‑first‑fix and improve accuracy in built environments, while the u‑blox chipset provides high sensitivity for weak signal reception.

- Real‑time tracking: Regular location updates and configurable reporting intervals for continuous monitoring.
- SOS and emergency alerts: One‑press SOS sends an immediate alarm through Plaspy for prompt response.
- Geo‑fence and event alerts: Create zones and receive entry/exit notifications via Plaspy.
- Historical track playback: Store and review movement history in Plaspy for audit and analysis.
- Wi‑Fi/LBS assisted positioning: Improves location performance indoors and in dense urban settings.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT90N is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that brings reliable, low‑power NB‑IoT connectivity together with multi‑mode positioning and a compact, rugged design. It’s tailored for personal safety scenarios where battery life, accurate positioning and straightforward emergency signaling matter most. When integrated with Plaspy, the MT90N delivers real‑time tracking and alerting while allowing operators to correlate location data with broader telemetry workflows. Plaspy can combine MT90N location streams with other vehicle or fleet telematics \(for example, fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer events produced by vehicle‑grade devices\) to create a complete operational view without requiring those vehicle‑specific sensors on the MT90N itself.

Choose the MT90N with Plaspy when you need a trustworthy, easy‑to‑deploy GPS tracker for personal safety, pet recovery or short‑term asset/vehicle monitoring. Its NB‑IoT efficiency, u‑blox sensitivity and Wi‑Fi/LBS assistance make it a practical component in scalable fleet management and telemetry strategies that rely on Plaspy’s real‑time visualization, alerting and historical analysis tools.
