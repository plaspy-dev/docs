# Megastek - MT90N

![MT90N](./tracker.jpg)

The MT90N is a compact NB IoT personal GPS tracker built for personal safety and light asset monitoring. It combines GNSS positioning with Wi Fi and LBS assistance to improve location performance in challenging environments. The device includes a dedicated SOS button, rugged water protection, and configurable reporting behavior suitable for children, the elderly, pets and lone workers.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT90N can feed location and event data into Plaspy for real time visibility, alerting and historical playback. Its low power NB IoT connectivity and multi mode positioning make it a practical choice when you need persistent location updates, emergency notifications and straightforward integration into Plaspy based monitoring or fleet workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for unified location and event monitoring across your deployment.
- Multi mode positioning using GNSS with Wi Fi and LBS assistance to improve indoors and urban performance.
- NB IoT connectivity designed for low power wide area reporting and extended standby.
- Rugged design with water and dust protection suitable for outdoor personal and asset use.
- Dedicated SOS button and status indicators for immediate alerts and simple diagnostics.
- Configurable reporting intervals plus geo fence and low battery alert support for operational control.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the MT90N sends position fixes and event messages, Plaspy ingests those streams and presents them in real time for operators. Plaspy can then trigger alerts, log movement history and include MT90N data alongside other fleet telemetry to support monitoring and reporting tasks.

- Real time tracking and configurable reporting intervals for continuous visibility in Plaspy.
- SOS and emergency notifications routed to Plaspy so teams can respond promptly.
- Geo fence events and low battery alerts delivered into Plaspy for automated notifications.
- Historical track playback and logs available in Plaspy for review and analysis.
- Combine MT90N location streams with other assets in Plaspy dashboards for consolidated oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and guardian monitoring with SOS alerts and location history.
- Elder care and lone worker safety where simple emergency signaling and periodic reporting are needed.
- Pet tracking for location recovery and virtual boundary alerts using geo fences.
- Short term vehicle or rental monitoring when temporary tracking is required.
- Light asset tracking for portable equipment that benefits from waterproof protection and compact form factor.

The MT90N is a practical option for organizations using Plaspy that need a compact, low power tracker with reliable positioning and emergency signaling. Its combination of GNSS, Wi Fi and LBS assistance helps reduce time to first fix in difficult environments, while NB IoT connectivity supports extended standby between reports. Integrated SOS, geo fence and battery alerts make the device suitable for personal safety and light asset monitoring scenarios.
