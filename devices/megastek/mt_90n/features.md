# Megastek - MT-90N Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Megastek MT-90N and describes how its capabilities map into Plaspy for day to day tracking, alerting, and historical review. The content is focused on practical, non-sensitive feature context relevant to operators and administrators who plan to use the MT-90N with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional cellular support, and the specifics of how the unit is installed. For the most current device details consult the manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The MT-90N is a compact NB-IoT personal tracker built for reliable location reporting and basic personal safety functions. Its hybrid positioning strategy and low profile make it useful where small size, weather resistance, and long standby are priorities.

- Compact, lightweight form factor for discreet personal use or temporary equipment tracking
- NB-IoT cellular connectivity with fallback GPRS reporting where supported for wide area coverage
- Hybrid positioning using GPS plus Wi‑Fi and LBS to improve location availability in urban or indoor settings
- Built-in SOS emergency alarm and geo-fence capability for immediate situational awareness
- Historical track logging and playback for event review and route analysis
- Automatic APN and time zone configuration to simplify integration with third party platforms such as Plaspy

## Core Features of Megastek - MT-90N

- NB-IoT connectivity with support for common NB-IoT bands as provided by the manufacturer
- Hybrid positioning combining GNSS with Wi‑Fi and LBS to enhance accuracy and fix times
- u-blox GNSS chipset reported in device specifications for reliable satellite reception
- SOS button for emergency alarm generation and notification
- Geo-fence support for boundary monitoring and alarm generation
- Historical track recording and playback for reviewing past movements
- Automatic APN query and automatic time zone configuration to reduce setup tasks
- Rugged, compact enclosure with IP66 to IP67 outdoor protection rating

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the MT-90N is connected to Plaspy, the device reports location and event messages that Plaspy displays and uses to generate alerts and historical views. Plaspy is designed to ingest compatible tracker messages and present them on a unified dashboard for monitoring and reporting.

- Real-time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and situational awareness
- SOS events are forwarded into Plaspy alerting workflows so operators can respond quickly
- Geo-fence triggers and alarms are reflected in Plaspy to support perimeter monitoring and recovery workflows
- Historical tracks logged by the device are available for playback and analysis in Plaspy
- Device status indicators such as battery alerts and connection state are shown in Plaspy to help prevent downtime
- Automatic APN and time zone handling on the device reduces manual configuration when adding units to Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Child and pet safety tracking where a small, waterproof device is required
- Caregiver monitoring for elderly or vulnerable individuals with SOS alerting
- Short term vehicle monitoring and situational awareness for rentals or temporary assignments
- Light commercial telemetry for couriers, delivery teams, or portable equipment
- Event and group tracking where many compact devices are deployed and centrally monitored

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version and hardware revision can change available functions and reported telemetry fields
- Regional carrier support for NB-IoT and specific cellular bands varies by market and may affect connectivity
- Some features require correct installation and placement to achieve optimal GNSS and hybrid positioning performance
- Bluetooth and some optional interfaces are not specified in the basic MT-90N public documentation; verify options with the manufacturer
- Plaspy integration behavior depends on the device sending supported message types and on current firmware behavior
