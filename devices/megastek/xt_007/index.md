# Megastek - XT-007

![XT-007](./logo.jpg)

The Megastek XT-007 is a highly advanced vehicle tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable tracking capabilities. With its water resistance rating close to IP67, this tracker is built to withstand various weather conditions, ensuring optimal performance even in challenging environments. Whether you need to track a fleet of vehicles or monitor the whereabouts of your personal vehicle, the XT-007 is a reliable choice.

One of the standout features of the XT-007 is its compatibility with both built-in and external antennas. This allows for flexible installation options, ensuring that you can achieve the best possible signal reception. Additionally, the tracker offers a range of alarm features, including a remove alarm, geo-fence alarm, over speed alarm, vibration alarm, low battery alarm, and more. These alarms help to enhance the security of your vehicle and provide real-time notifications in case of any suspicious activity.

With its power-saving mode, the XT-007 is designed to conserve battery life, making it ideal for long-term tracking applications. The tracker also supports two-way communication, allowing for remote monitoring and even emergency calling in case of an SOS situation. Furthermore, the XT-007 features a data logger with built-in 8M flash memory, ensuring that you can access historical tracking data even when there is no GPS signal available.

Overall, the Megastek XT-007 is a feature-packed vehicle tracker that offers reliable tracking capabilities and a range of advanced features. Whether you need to track a single vehicle or manage a fleet, this tracker provides the necessary tools to ensure the safety and security of your assets.

### Key Features:

- Water resistance \(close to IP67\)
- Compatible with built-in or external antennas
- Remove alarm
- Tracking via SMS/GPRS \(TCP/UDP\)
- Real-time tracking
- Power-saving mode
- Two-way communication \(optional\)
- SOS emergency calling
- Geo-fence alarm
- Over speed alarm
- Vibration alarm
- Low battery alarm
- No GPS signal alarm
- Data logger \(built-in 8M flash memory\)
- Monitoring remote or two-way communication \(optional\)

- Oil detection
- Remote fuel/engine cut off
- Cut external power off alarm
