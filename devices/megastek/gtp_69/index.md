# Megastek - GTP-69

![GTP-69](./logo.jpg)

The Megastek GTP-69 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features to help you keep track of your valuable assets. With its compact design and lightweight construction, it is easy to install and conceal, making it ideal for a variety of applications. Whether you need to track your vehicle, monitor the movements of a loved one, or keep an eye on your fleet of vehicles, the GTP-69 is up to the task.

One of the standout features of the GTP-69 is its GPS chipset, which utilizes SiRF Star III technology. This ensures accurate and reliable positioning, allowing you to track your assets with confidence. The tracker also features a GSM chipset \(SIM 900\) that supports quad-band frequencies \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\), ensuring wide coverage and compatibility with most cellular networks.

The GTP-69 offers a range of tracking options, including track on demand and time interval tracking. You can set up to three authorized cell phone numbers to receive real-time tracking updates, ensuring that you are always in the loop. The tracker also features a power-saving mode, which helps to extend battery life, and a motion sensor that can detect movement and trigger an alert.

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the GTP-69 offers a range of safety features. It includes an SOS button that can be used to send an immediate rescue and alarm signal in case of an emergency. The tracker also supports geo-fencing, allowing you to set up virtual boundaries and receive an alert if the tracker enters or exits the designated area. Furthermore, it has an over-speed limit warning to help you monitor and control the speed of your assets.

The GTP-69 also includes useful features such as a "No GPS" warning, which alerts you if the tracker is unable to establish a GPS connection, and a low battery warning to ensure that you never lose track of your assets due to a dead battery. Additionally, the tracker has built-in memory for data logging, allowing you to store and retrieve location data even when there is no cellular coverage.

The Megastek GTP-69 has a compact dimension of 80\*54\*21mm and a net weight of 80g, making it easy to install and conceal. It is available in black and silver-gray colors, giving you options to match your preferences. The tracker comes packaged in a box with dimensions of 18.5\*13\*9cm and a gross weight of 550g. It is also available in a bulk package of 50 units per carton, with a carton size of 67\*28\*47cm.
