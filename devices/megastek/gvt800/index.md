# Megastek - GVT800

![GVT800](./tracker.jpg)

The GVT800 \(GVT-800\) by Megastek is a Plaspy compatible 4G LTE GPS tracker built for heavy-duty vehicles, intercity and school buses, and commercial fleet management. Combining a high-sensitivity Ublox GNSS module with multi-network cellular communications, the GVT800 delivers reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for fleet operators who need robust position reporting, alarm handling, and remote control capabilities across long-haul and urban routes.

Designed for industrial environments, the GVT800 supports server uploads over UDP/TCP and can also provide quick SMS link location sharing for mobile viewing. With a wide input voltage range, a rechargeable 750 mAh backup battery, multiple I/O interfaces and a compact enclosure, this GPS tracker integrates into Plaspy to provide anti-theft workflows, fuel monitoring extensions, ignition/power event reporting and centralized fleet management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and centralized fleet management via UDP/TCP server integration.
- Multi-network cellular: 4G LTE/WCDMA/GSM quad-band connectivity for reliable coverage and telemetry uploads.
- High-sensitivity Ublox GNSS with \<10 m position accuracy and fast hot/warm/cold start performance.
- Built-in 750 mAh rechargeable battery and wide DC 9–33V input for dependable backup and vehicle compatibility.
- Comprehensive alarm set including impact/knock, overspeed, GPS loss, external power disconnect and SOS emergency.
- Rich I/O: digital and analog inputs, multiple RS232/RS485 ports for CAN/device integration and remote engine/power cut control.
- Optional fuel level, temperature and camera integrations for advanced telemetry and fuel monitoring workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GVT800 integrates with Plaspy by transmitting GPS position and telemetry to the Plaspy server using standard UDP or TCP transport. Plaspy ingests location updates, alarms and I/O events so fleet managers can view live vehicle locations, replay trips, and configure automated alerts for anti-theft and safety workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over 4G LTE/WCDMA/GSM using UDP/TCP to Plaspy servers.
- Alarms and status: impact/knock, overspeed, GPS loss, external power disconnect and internal battery low are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate alerting.
- Ignition/power events and digital input signals can be reported to Plaspy to trigger engine or operational logic.
- Remote immobilizer-style control: the unit supports remote engine \(oil\) and power cut commands which Plaspy can initiate when configured for anti-theft or emergency response.
- Fuel monitoring and sensors: optional capacitive/ultrasonic oil level and temperature sensors can be paired with the GVT800 and their telemetry displayed in Plaspy dashboards and reports.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management for long-haul trucking and intercity buses — real-time tracking, trip replay and telemetry reporting via Plaspy.
- School and passenger buses — SOS emergency alerts, driver/vehicle alarms, and remote engine cut for safety and anti-theft response.
- Logistics and distribution fleets — overspeed and impact alarms, route monitoring and centralized fuel monitoring when connected with optional sensors.
- High-value cargo protection — external power disconnect and GPS antenna cut detection combined with Plaspy alarming for immediate incident response.
- Mixed-vehicle installations requiring multiple serial/analog interfaces — use RS232/RS485 ports for cameras, RFID readers, or third-party telemetry devices.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the GVT800 with Plaspy gives fleet operators a dependable, scalable solution for real-time tracking, anti-theft workflows and operational telemetry. The GVT800’s multi-network cellular modem and Ublox GNSS deliver accurate location and fast fixes, while flexible I/O and optional sensor integrations support fuel monitoring, temperature tracking and remote immobilization. Plaspy leverages the unit’s UDP/TCP or SMS reporting to provide live dashboards, automated alerts and historical replay for better decision-making in fleet management.

For operations that require industrial reliability — school fleets, intercity coaches or heavy trucks — the GVT800’s ruggedized power range, backup battery and wide temperature rating make it a practical choice. With remote firmware upgrade support and manufacturer protocol documentation, integration with Plaspy is straightforward, enabling fleet teams to deploy real-time tracking, telemetry and anti-theft controls without replacing their existing monitoring platform.
