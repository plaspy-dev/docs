# Megastek - GVT800

![GVT800](./tracker.jpg)

The Megastek GVT800 is a rugged 4G LTE GPS tracker designed for heavy duty vehicles, intercity and school buses, and commercial fleet management. It combines a high sensitivity Ublox GNSS receiver with multi network cellular communications to provide continuous real time position reporting, alarm handling and remote control capabilities suited to long haul and urban routes. The design emphasizes dependable tracking and telemetry for operators who need reliable location visibility and event reporting across a range of vehicle types.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GVT800 can send position updates, alarms and I O events to the Plaspy platform for centralized fleet monitoring. That compatibility makes the unit a practical option for teams who want to leverage Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reporting without changing their existing hardware choice. Plaspy can ingest the tracker uploads and present location, status and historical trip data alongside other fleet assets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for centralized real time tracking and alarm management through UDP TCP server uploads and SMS link reporting
- Multi network cellular connectivity for broad coverage across long haul and urban routes
- High sensitivity Ublox GNSS receiver for reliable position fixes and consistent tracking
- Built in rechargeable backup battery and wide vehicle input voltage support for uninterrupted operation
- Comprehensive alarm set including impact overspeed GPS loss external power disconnect and SOS emergency
- Flexible I O and serial interfaces to support optional sensors and third party devices for extended telemetry
- Compact industrial enclosure suitable for heavy duty and passenger vehicle deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GVT800 transmits location and telemetry to the platform so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in real time, receive alarm notifications and review historical movement. Plaspy organizes incoming updates into live maps, alerts and reports that help teams maintain operational oversight and respond quickly to incidents.

- Real time location updates and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for live fleet visibility
- Alarm events such as impact overspeed GPS loss and external power disconnect are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification
- Ignition and power events along with digital input signals can trigger rules and workflows inside Plaspy
- Remote control commands supported by the device can be issued from Plaspy when configured for anti theft or emergency response
- Optional sensor telemetry such as fuel level and temperature can be displayed in Plaspy for consolidated reporting and analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for long haul trucking and intercity bus services requiring continuous tracking and trip replay
- School and passenger bus operations that need SOS alerts driver and vehicle alarms and remote immobilization options
- Logistics and distribution fleets monitoring routes overspeed events and optional fuel telemetry
- Protection of high value cargo with alarm forwarding for antenna cut external power disconnect and immediate response
- Mixed vehicle installations that require multiple serial and analog interfaces for cameras RFID readers and sensor devices
