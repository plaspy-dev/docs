# Megastek - MT80T Features

This page covers the public feature context for using the Megastek MT80T tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the MT80T's wearable design, medical grade sensor set, GNSS positioning, multi mode cellular uplink, safety features such as SOS and fall detection, and how those capabilities are reflected when the device is used on the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and how the unit is installed. For device specific settings, firmware behavior and the latest feature set consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes in addition to the guidance on Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The MT80T is a wrist worn medical grade tracker that combines continuous physiological monitoring with GNSS location and safety alarms. It is designed for continuous use, outdoor conditions, and integration into centralized monitoring platforms such as Plaspy for alerts, historical reporting and operational oversight.

- Continuous heart rate, SpO2 and body temperature telemetry for ongoing health visibility.
- Real time GNSS tracking plus historical route logging for location history and review.
- Multi mode cellular connectivity supporting eMTC, NB IoT or GSM for wide network coverage.
- Dedicated SOS button, fall detection and wristband disconnection alarm for rapid incident alerts.
- Two way voice capability to connect monitored users with caregivers or dispatch.
- Durable waterproof wearable with wireless charging and a 1.28 inch display for daily use.

## Core Features of Megastek - MT80T

- Continuous physiological monitoring including heart rate, SpO2 and body temperature.
- High sensitivity Ublox GNSS receiver for reliable position fixes and track logging.
- Multi mode cellular uplink with support for eMTC, NB IoT or GSM and GPRS data logging.
- Safety features: SOS button, fall detection and wristband removal tamper alarm.
- Two way voice communication for direct contact between wearer and responder.
- Wearable design with wireless charging, IP66 to IP67 rated water resistance and a 1.28 inch e ink display.
- Simple physical controls with a main button and a dedicated SOS button.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the MT80T's position, event triggers and sensor telemetry so administrators and caregivers can monitor status, receive alerts and review historical records from a single platform. Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols automatically and presents device events and location updates in dashboards and reports.

- Live location and periodic position history visible in Plaspy maps and track logs.
- Health telemetry and sensor updates appear as events or timeline entries for monitoring and review.
- SOS presses, fall detection and wristband removal generate immediate alerts and notification workflows.
- Geo fence events and historical track traces are available for route review and incident correlation.
- Two way voice availability and alert events are shown alongside location and status to support coordinated response.

## Typical Use Cases

- Eldercare monitoring where continuous vitals and location help caregivers prioritize response.
- Personal safety programs for vulnerable individuals requiring SOS and fall alerts.
- Lone worker safety with real time tracking and emergency voice contact.
- Healthcare programs and studies requiring synchronized location and physiological telemetry.
- Parole or compliance monitoring where tamper detection and geo fence alerts are useful.
- Outdoor and travel safety for users who need waterproof wearable tracking and long term logging.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware updates can add, change or refine sensor behavior, event thresholds and reporting intervals.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect supported cellular modes or environmental ratings.
- Installation and wrist placement can affect physiological sensor accuracy and fall detection performance.
- Network coverage and the chosen cellular mode determine uplink frequency and data availability.
- Consult manufacturer configuration guides for APN settings and recommended deployment steps.
