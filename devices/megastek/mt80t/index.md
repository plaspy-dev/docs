# MT80T — TWIN MASK Medical-Grade GPS Tracker & Smart Bracelet

The MT80T from TWIN MASK is a compact medical-grade GPS tracker and smart bracelet designed for continuous health monitoring and reliable personal location tracking. Built around an Ublox GNSS module and multi-mode cellular connectivity \(eMTC, NB‑IoT or GSM\), the MT80T delivers real-time tracking and physiological telemetry in a wearable form factor that supports everyday use and outdoor safety. As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT80T integrates smoothly into Plaspy’s platform for centralized monitoring, alerts and historical reporting.

Purpose-built for eldercare, workforce safety and personal security, the MT80T combines 24-hour heart rate, SpO2 and body temperature monitoring with GPS positioning, two-way voice and an SOS button for rapid incident response. With waterproof construction \(IP66–67\), wireless charging and features such as fall detection, wristband disconnection alarm, geo-fence alerts and historical track logging, this GPS tracker is ideal where continuous health telemetry and real-time tracking are required.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the MT80T streams location and health telemetry to a single monitoring platform so administrators and caregivers receive timely insight and automated alerts. Plaspy ingests the device’s GNSS position, event triggers and sensor data, allowing real-time tracking, rule-based notifications \(geo-fence, SOS, fall\) and consolidated historical reports for compliance and operational review.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates from the Ublox GNSS and onboard health sensors.
