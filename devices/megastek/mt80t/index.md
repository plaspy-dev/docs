# Megastek - MT80T

<div className="device-logo">
![MT80T](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The MT80T is a compact medical grade GPS tracker presented in a smart bracelet form factor, designed for continuous health monitoring and dependable personal location tracking. According to the device description, it uses a high sensitivity Ublox GNSS receiver and multi mode cellular connectivity to deliver real time positioning alongside continuous physiological telemetry. The wearable form, waterproof construction and SOS features make it suitable for everyday use where ongoing visibility and quick incident response are required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT80T integrates into centralized monitoring workflows to bring location data, event triggers and health telemetry into a single platform. That compatibility makes the MT80T relevant for organizations and caregivers who rely on Plaspy for consolidated dashboards, automated alerts and historical reporting, providing both situational awareness and records for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Medical grade physiological monitoring with continuous heart rate, SpO2 and body temperature telemetry for proactive care.
- Plaspy compatible for centralized dashboards, rule based alerts and consolidated historical reports.
- High sensitivity Ublox GNSS receiver for reliable position fixes and track logging.
- Multi mode cellular connectivity supporting deployment across diverse network environments.
- Safety focused features including an SOS button, fall detection and wristband disconnection alarm.
- Durable wearable design with waterproof rating and wireless charging for daily use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the MT80T streams position updates, event notifications and health sensor readings into the Plaspy platform so administrators and responders can monitor activity from a single interface. Plaspy can apply rules and workflows to those inputs to generate alerts, store history and present operational views for teams.

- Real time location updates and historical track logging visible within Plaspy maps and timelines.
- Automated alerts for SOS presses, fall detection and wristband removal routed to operators and caregivers.
- Telemetry visibility to support situational awareness and trend reporting for monitored users.
- Geo fence triggers and location based notifications for perimeter or route monitoring.
- Two way voice and alarm events surfaced in Plaspy to enable rapid response and direct contact.

## Typical Use Cases

- Eldercare monitoring to combine continuous vital signs and location tracking for remote caregivers.
- Workforce safety for lone workers who need location visibility and emergency alerting.
- Personal security and parolee monitoring where tamper detection and geo fence alerts are required.
- Healthcare programs and clinical coordination where tagged location and time stamped telemetry matter.
- Outdoor safety and travel situations that require a wearable, waterproof tracker with persistent logging.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT80T pairs wearable medical sensors with a compact, rugged design, making it a practical choice for programs that need both health telemetry and real time location visibility. When used with Plaspy, device events and sensor data are consolidated into a single operational view, allowing teams to set up alerts, review incident histories and manage response workflows without separate systems.
