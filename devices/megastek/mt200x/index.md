# Megastek - MT200X

<div className="device-logo">
![MT200X](./tracker.png)
</div>

The MT200X Prisoner Tracking Ankle is a rugged wrist style GPS tracker engineered for continuous electronic monitoring and supervision. Purpose built for parolee and prisoner monitoring, the MT200X combines high sensitivity GNSS positioning with a cellular communication module and hardened hardware to deliver reliable real time tracking and telemetry in demanding environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT200X can integrate into managed monitoring workflows to provide location visibility, alerts and historical reporting. Its multi mode positioning and security features make it a practical choice for agencies and service providers that need continuous oversight, configurable notifications and durable hardware paired with Plaspy monitoring tools.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built ankle tracker for electronic monitoring and parole supervision
- Multi mode positioning for improved outdoor and indoor assisted localization
- Broad cellular coverage for reliable real time data transmission
- Durable compact form with stainless steel wristband and tested environmental resistance
- Long duration battery designed for extended operation between charges
- Security features including SOS, tamper and belt cut alerts plus two way voice support

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the MT200X acts as a managed endpoint that delivers location fixes and device telemetry to the Plaspy platform for continuous monitoring. Administrators can view current and historical movement, receive device and security alerts, and apply geofence rules and notification workflows to support supervision and incident response.

- Real time location and telemetry delivered to Plaspy for continuous situational awareness
- Two way voice and monitoring features available within Plaspy workflows for supervised communications
- Geofencing and tamper alerts push immediate notifications into Plaspy for rapid response
- Battery and health status updates feed maintenance and escalation processes in Plaspy
- Wi Fi base station support enhances indoor localization and integrates with Plaspy location fusion

## Typical Use Cases

- Continuous electronic monitoring of parolees and detainees with real time tracking and alerting
- Supervised transport and custody movements where location history and voice support are needed
- Home confinement and curfew enforcement using Wi Fi assisted localization for indoor accuracy
- Incident detection and response for tamper events, SOS activations and security breaches
- Audit and compliance reporting through logged telemetry and historical movement records

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT200X pairs hardened hardware and multi mode positioning with features designed for supervised monitoring programs. Its combination of extended run time, assisted localization and security alerts supports operational needs common to correctional and community supervision environments. For organizations using Plaspy, the device offers practical telemetry and alarm signals that map into common monitoring workflows.
