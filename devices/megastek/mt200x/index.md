# Megastek - MT200X

![MT200X](./tracker.png)

The MT200X Prisoner Tracking Ankle is a rugged, wrist‑style GPS tracker engineered for continuous electronic monitoring and supervision. Purpose-built for parolee and prisoner monitoring, the MT200X pairs high-sensitivity Ublox GNSS positioning and a SIMCOM7600 cellular module with hardened hardware, delivering reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and telemetry in demanding environments.

The device supports multi-mode positioning \(GPS + LBS + A-GPS + Wi‑Fi\), extensive 2G/3G/4G LTE-FDD cellular bands, and a long-duration 1900 mAh battery that sustains more than 35 hours of operation at a 1-minute reporting interval. With two-way voice, tamper detection, geofencing and configurable alerts, the MT200X integrates smoothly with Plaspy and other tracking platforms to provide secure, actionable location intelligence for correctional programs and secure transport scenarios.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed specifically for electronic monitoring and parole supervision.
- Multi-mode positioning \(GPS + LBS + A‑GPS + Wi‑Fi\) for reliable outdoor and improved indoor localization.
- Broad cellular coverage via SIMCOM7600: 2G, 3G and extensive 4G LTE‑FDD bands for global connectivity and real-time tracking.
- Durable, compact design \(70 × 64 × 20 mm, 242 g\) with stainless steel wristband and IP68-grade testing reports.
- Long-life 1900 mAh polymer battery \(3.8 V\) with typical working time >35 hours at 1‑minute reporting intervals.
- Security features including SOS button, tamper/belt cut alarms, loud siren and two‑way voice and voice monitoring.
- Flexible tracking modes \(power saving, vehicle, smart\), geofencing, low-battery alerts and GPRS data logging for audit trails.
- Optional wireless charger and Wi‑Fi base station for enhanced indoor/home localization and simple field charging.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the MT200X becomes a fully managed node in a secure monitoring ecosystem. The tracker transmits GNSS fixes and telemetry over cellular data to Plaspy’s platform where administrators can view real-time location, receive status alerts and generate historical movement reports. Plaspy-compatible protocol mapping and OEM protocol integration make setup straightforward so agencies can apply rules, geofences and notification workflows quickly.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered over 2G/3G/4G to Plaspy for continuous monitoring.
- Two-way voice and voice monitoring enable supervised communications and evidence capture when required.
- Geofencing, hit/impact and tamper/belt cut alarms push immediate alerts into Plaspy for incident response.
- Battery, low-charge notifications and device health telemetry feed automated escalation and maintenance workflows.
- Wi‑Fi base station support improves indoor/home localization and integrates with Plaspy location fusion for higher accuracy.
