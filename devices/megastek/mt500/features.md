# Megastek - MT500 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Megastek MT500 ankle tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities relevant to monitoring, geofence enforcement, tamper detection, and remote management when the MT500 is integrated into Plaspy-powered operations and reporting workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior for any MT500 unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, cellular network coverage, and manufacturer implementation. For device-specific technical details and the most current specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The MT500 is a purpose-built ankle worn GPS tracker designed for continuous correctional supervision and integration with monitoring platforms such as Plaspy. It focuses on reliable location reporting, physical tamper resistance, and operational features that support compliance programs.

- Direct compatibility with Plaspy for centralized monitoring and alerts
- Tamper resistant metal ankle enclosure designed to discourage unauthorized removal
- Continuous or periodic GNSS location reporting used for real-time tracking and geofence enforcement
- Cellular communications for data delivery to a Plaspy control center
- Remote configuration and firmware management options to reduce manual maintenance

## Core Features of Megastek - MT500

- GNSS positioning for continuous location awareness as reported into Plaspy
- Cellular data communications to transmit location and event data to monitoring systems
- Tamper and removal detection with event reporting suitable for compliance logging
- Rugged metal ankle form factor engineered for correctional and supervision use
- Geofence and boundary breach reporting to support inclusion and exclusion zones
- Long battery endurance designed to minimize downtime between maintenance actions
- Remote configuration and firmware management over the network to streamline updates

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, MT500 telemetry and security events are normalized and presented in unified dashboards and case workflows so supervision teams can respond quickly to violations or tamper incidents.

- Real-time location and historical tracks displayed on Plaspy maps and timelines
- Tamper and removal alerts surfaced in Plaspy alerting workflows with event logging for audit trails
- Geofence enforcement events appear as boundary breach notifications to support curfew and exclusion zone policies
- Remote device settings and firmware updates can be coordinated through Plaspy managed workflows and device configuration tools
- Consolidated reporting and case management that combines MT500 events with other Plaspy data sources for situational awareness

## Typical Use Cases

- Continuous monitoring of individuals on parole or probation to support supervision requirements
- House arrest and curfew enforcement using geofence notifications and scheduled reporting
- Community corrections and case management where centralized reporting and audit trails are required
- Transport and temporary custody oversight to maintain visibility during transfers
- Programs requiring tamper detection and documented compliance reporting

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific capabilities may differ between firmware versions and hardware revisions; verify the features available on a given unit before deployment
- Regional variants and cellular band support affect connectivity and may impact real-time reporting performance
- Installation method and attachment quality influence tamper detection and reliable operation
- Manufacturer settings and provisioning workflows determine how remote configuration and firmware updates are performed
- For full technical and operational details consult the official Megastek documentation and firmware release notes

Pairing the MT500 with Plaspy centralizes location telemetry, tamper events, and geofence enforcement into a single operational view. Plaspy's event handling and reporting capabilities help agencies and programs convert device data into actionable alerts, case records, and compliance reports while reducing manual steps for device management.
