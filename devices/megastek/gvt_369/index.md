# Megastek - GVT-369

<div className="device-logo">
![GVT-369](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Megastek GVT-369 is a compact, durable GPS tracker designed for accurate location tracking across personal and commercial applications. It uses a SiRF Star III GPS chipset for position accuracy and a SIM 900 based quad band GSM radio for broad cellular connectivity. The unit includes features commonly expected in professional trackers such as power saving mode, an internal backup battery, SOS button, motion sensor, geo-fence alarm, over speed and low battery warnings, no GPS signal notification, background voice surveillance, and onboard data logging for situations where cellular coverage is intermittent.

This tracker is compatible with Plaspy, making it a practical option for customers who want to combine the GVT-369 hardware with a modern fleet and asset monitoring platform. When paired with Plaspy, the device's event reporting and stored location data can be surfaced in dashboards, notifications, and reports to support real time visibility and historical analysis for vehicles and mobile assets.

## Key Highlights

- Precise location reporting powered by a SiRF Star III GPS chipset for reliable positioning.
- Quad band GSM connectivity via SIM 900 for wide cellular coverage across regions.
- SOS button and emergency alerting to notify designated numbers or systems.
- Power saving mode plus internal backup battery for extended operation and resilience.
- Built in data logging to store location history when network connectivity is unavailable.
- Multiple inputs and outputs for integration with vehicle signals and external devices.
- Motion sensor, geo fence, over speed, and low battery warnings for operational alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GVT-369 can feed its location updates and event notifications into Plaspy so teams can monitor assets on a map, receive alerts, and analyze historical trips. Plaspy ingests the tracker events and makes them usable for dispatch, reporting, and operational oversight without requiring changes to the device itself.

- Real time location on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility and dispatch decisions.
- Geofence events and alerts routed into Plaspy for perimeter monitoring and instant notification.
- SOS and emergency signals visible in Plaspy so responders and managers can act quickly.
- Historical route and trip retrieval using the device's data logging combined with Plaspy reporting.
- Event based alerts for over speed, low battery, and no GPS signal to maintain operational safety.
- Digital input and output states available in Plaspy for monitoring vehicle status and remote control scenarios.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for deliveries, service vehicles, and corporate car pools.
- Asset monitoring where compact form factor and discreet installation are important.
- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring using SOS and motion detection features.
- Rental or leasing operations that require tamper awareness and usage reporting.
- Logistics and transit where historical route playback and geofence monitoring are needed.
