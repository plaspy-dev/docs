# Megastek - MT60PRO

![MT60PRO](./tracker.jpg)

The Megastek MT60PRO 4G Parolee Tracking Ankle is a rugged, ankle‑worn GPS tracker built for corrections and community supervision. Designed for secure parolee and prisoner monitoring, the MT60PRO is Plaspy compatible and integrates multi‑mode positioning, tamper detection, two‑way voice, and remote lock/unlock controls to deliver reliable real‑time tracking and supervision across correctional programs.

With IP68 ingress protection and a reinforced, patent‑adjustable bracelet, the MT60PRO focuses on wearer safety and anti‑tamper resilience while providing telemetry and event reporting that integrate directly with Plaspy for case management, alerts, and reporting. Agencies can use the MT60PRO with Plaspy to centralize location data, alarms, and supervised‑person status in a single interface.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible — integrates location, alarms, and voice telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and workflows for streamlined supervision.
- Rugged, tamper‑resistant design — IP68 waterproof rating and reinforced bracelet resist cutting and environmental exposure.
- Multi‑mode positioning — GPS \(U‑blox\), AGPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS hybrid positioning for robust real‑time tracking even in mixed signal environments.
- Secure physical and electronic locking — dedicated mechanical key plus remote electronic lock/unlock commands for controlled device management.
- Two‑way voice and remote monitoring — remote voice monitoring and two‑way communication for welfare checks and compliance verification.
- Comprehensive alarm set — geo‑fence, SOS, low battery, belt cut/on‑off tamper, and siren alarms with GPRS logging to Plaspy.
- Practical power options — built‑in 1500mAh battery, wireless charging support, and an optional mobile battery cradle that extends operation by 3–4 days.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the MT60PRO streams positioning and status data to the platform for real‑time tracking, automated alerts, and historical telemetry. Plaspy ingests the device’s GPS+LBS+AGPS+Wi‑Fi positioning and GPRS data logs to display location traces, trigger compliance rules, and notify supervisors of events. Commands from Plaspy \(such as electronic lock/unlock or configuration updates\) can be relayed to the MT60PRO for remote management.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates \(GPS U‑blox + AGPS + LBS + Wi‑Fi hybrid positioning\).
- Tamper and belt‑cut alarms are reported immediately to Plaspy for anti‑theft and compliance responses.
- Geo‑fence events and SOS emergency alarms generate instant Plaspy notifications and location snapshots.
- Remote lock/unlock commands and remote voice monitoring enable supervised‑person control and welfare checks.
- GPRS data logging and periodic uploads allow Plaspy to build historical movement reports and audit trails.

- Parolee and probation supervision — continuous real‑time tracking, geo‑fence enforcement, and remote check‑ins managed through Plaspy.
- Corrections and electronic detention programs — tamper detection, secure locking, and voice monitoring for secure community supervision.
- Home detention and electronic home monitoring — use with a home Wi‑Fi base station and Plaspy rules to enforce curfew and location restrictions.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT60PRO is purpose‑built for secure, scalable electronic supervision. Its tamper‑resistant hardware, waterproof housing, and hybrid positioning provide dependable real‑time tracking and forensic location history essential to corrections programs. Paired with Plaspy, agencies gain centralized telemetry, automated alerts, and command capability — reducing manual checks and improving response times. The device’s remote lock/unlock, two‑way voice, and accessory options \(wireless charger, mobile battery cradle, smart lock, home base\) make it a flexible choice for programs that require high reliability and clear audit trails.

Note on vehicle‑oriented features: the MT60PRO is an ankle‑worn personal GPS tracker and does not list vehicle ignition, immobilizer, or fuel monitoring interfaces in the manufacturer description. Agencies needing telemetry for vehicles and fleet management can still route MT60PRO location data into Plaspy alongside vehicle trackers to create unified fleet management and supervision views.
