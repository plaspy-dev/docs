# Megastek - MT60PRO

![MT60PRO](./tracker.jpg)

The Megastek MT60PRO is an ankle worn GPS tracker designed for corrections and community supervision programs. Built for secure parolee and prisoner monitoring, the MT60PRO combines rugged physical protection with monitoring features such as tamper detection, two way voice, remote lock and unlock controls, and multi mode positioning to deliver continuous location and status information for supervised individuals.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT60PRO integrates its positioning and alarm telemetry with Plaspy to centralize case data, alerts, and reporting. Agencies using Plaspy can ingest MT60PRO location traces and event reports to simplify supervision workflows, automate notifications, and maintain auditable histories for compliance and case management.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built ankle worn design with reinforced adjustable bracelet for secure wear and tamper resilience.
- IP68 rated housing for dependable operation in challenging environmental conditions.
- Multi mode positioning and hybrid location reporting to improve tracking in mixed signal environments.
- Two way voice and remote lock and unlock capabilities to support welfare checks and remote device management.
- Comprehensive alarm set including geo fence, SOS, tamper and belt cut detection routed into Plaspy workflows.
- Designed for integration with supervision platforms such as Plaspy to provide centralized telemetry and reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the MT60PRO is used with Plaspy, the device streams location, telemetry, and alarm events into the Plaspy platform so supervisors can monitor status in real time and review historical movement. Plaspy receives the device events and makes them available for automated rules, notifications, and case level reporting.

- Real time location updates and traces that appear on Plaspy maps for live supervision and review.
- Immediate reporting of tamper, belt cut, SOS, and geo fence events to trigger Plaspy alerts and supervisor actions.
- Remote commands such as electronic lock and unlock and voice monitoring can be coordinated through Plaspy for centralized control.
- Historical data uploads allow Plaspy to build audit trails and movement reports for case reviews and compliance checks.
- Integration with Plaspy workflows helps consolidate supervised person status alongside other program data for efficient oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parolee and probation supervision with continuous monitoring and automated compliance alerts.
- Corrections and community detention programs requiring tamper detection and remote welfare checks.
- Home detention monitoring combined with Plaspy rules to enforce curfew and permitted area constraints.
- Incident response and emergency handling using SOS alerts and instant location snapshots routed to supervisors.
- Program management and auditing where logged histories support reporting and reviews in Plaspy.
