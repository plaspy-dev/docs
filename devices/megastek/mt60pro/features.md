# Megastek - MT60PRO Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Megastek MT60PRO ankle GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for supervision, monitoring, and reporting. The content focuses on practical, non sensitive information about what the device can do and how Plaspy surfaces those capabilities for operational use.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, accessory configuration, installation method, and regional model variants. For device specific technical details, firmware behavior, and the latest manufacturer guidance consult Megastek documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The MT60PRO is an ankle worn electronic monitoring unit designed for corrections and community supervision programs. It combines a rugged physical design with hybrid positioning and event reporting to support real time supervision and audit trails when integrated with a platform such as Plaspy.

- Ankle worn form factor with a reinforced, patent adjustable bracelet for secure attachment and tamper resilience.
- Hybrid positioning using GPS with AGPS plus Wi Fi and LBS for improved location availability in mixed signal environments.
- Tamper detection and alarms including belt cut and on off tamper reporting to notify supervisors of device removal attempts.
- Two way voice and remote voice monitoring to perform welfare checks and communicate with the wearer.
- Remote electronic lock and unlock in combination with a secure mechanical key for controlled device management.
- Power and endurance options including an internal rechargeable battery, wireless charging support, and an optional external battery cradle for extended operation.

## Core Features of Megastek - MT60PRO

- Rugged, tamper resistant housing with IP68 ingress protection for water and dust resistance.
- Multi mode positioning that combines GPS (U blox) with AGPS, Wi Fi, and LBS hybrid positioning.
- Tamper and belt cut detection with immediate event reporting for compliance workflows.
- Two way voice communication and remote voice monitoring for supervised person contact and welfare checks.
- Mechanical key lock plus remote electronic lock and unlock commands for secure control.
- Comprehensive alarm set including geo fence, SOS, low battery, siren, and tamper alarms.
- GPRS data logging and periodic uploads to support historical movement records and audits.
- Built in battery with wireless charging support and an optional mobile battery cradle to extend operational time.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, MT60PRO telemetry and event data are captured to provide real time visibility, rule based alerts, and historical reports to support case management. Plaspy ingests the device reporting and maps status updates into dashboards, alerts, and audit logs used by supervisors and program managers.

- Real time location traces from the device hybrid positioning are shown on Plaspy maps and playback tools.
- Tamper events, belt cut alerts, SOS activations, and geo fence breaches generate platform notifications and assigned workflows.
- Two way voice and remote monitoring events are surfaced as part of the device activity history to document welfare checks and communications.
- Remote lock and unlock commands issued from Plaspy can be relayed to the unit for remote device state control when supported by the deployed configuration.
- GPRS logged telemetry and periodic uploads feed Plaspy reports and exportable audit records for compliance and program analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parolee and probation supervision with continuous tracking, geo fence enforcement, and event driven alerts.
