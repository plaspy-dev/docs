# Megastek - MT-350 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Megastek MT-350 wristband with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker’s practical capabilities for indoor real-time location, alarm signaling, and device health reporting as they relate to Plaspy-compatible deployments without reproducing private technical protocols or internal configuration specifics.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current and device-specific details consult the official Megastek documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The MT-350 is a compact, wristwatch-sized UWB positioning device designed to extend high-accuracy indoor tracking and alarm-driven monitoring into Plaspy deployments. It pairs with UWB ceiling base stations to provide continuous wearability, event reporting, and centralized visibility where GPS is unavailable.

- Ultra-wideband indoor positioning with 10–30 cm accuracy for precise real-time location.
- Wristband form factor with ergonomic design and wireless charging plus a snap-on portable charging pouch.
- Mechanical SOS button and tamper / anti-disassembly protections for immediate alarm signaling.
- Motion sensing via a configurable G-sensor and vibration reminders for activity awareness.
- Device health and status uplink plus support for remote firmware upgrades to simplify maintenance.

## Core Features of Megastek - MT-350

- High-precision UWB positioning using 802.15.4a for reliable indoor location.
- Wristband form factor designed for continuous wear and personnel tracking.
- Mechanical SOS button to raise alarms and trigger operator workflows.
- Anti-disassembly and tamper detection to support custody and safety use cases.
- Motion sensing (G-sensor) with configurable sensitivity for activity and movement monitoring.
- Wireless charging support and portable charging pouch for extended deployments.
- Device health reporting and remote firmware update support for centralized maintenance.
- Integration capability with UWB ceiling base stations to form a complete indoor RTLS.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, MT-350 location and status messages are presented alongside other tracked assets to provide a unified operational picture. Plaspy-compatible setups receive location updates, alarms, and device telemetry from the RTLS infrastructure so teams can monitor position and respond to critical events.

- Real-time position updates displayed on Plaspy maps and location views for indoor awareness.
- SOS and tamper events forwarded into Plaspy alerting and incident workflows for rapid response.
- Battery and device health telemetry visible in Plaspy dashboards to assist maintenance planning.
- Motion and activity indications available for monitoring dwell, movement, and abnormal inactivity.
- Configurable reporting intervals reflected in Plaspy as adjustable update frequency to balance visibility and battery life.

## Typical Use Cases

- Correctional facilities requiring precise indoor location for staff and monitored individuals with tamper and SOS protections.
- Hospitals and care environments where patient and staff location and rapid alarm notification improve response times.
- Industrial sites and commercial facilities needing accurate indoor personnel tracking and restricted-area awareness.
- Parole and supervised release monitoring that combines continuous location reporting with tamper and low-battery alerts.
- Warehouses and logistics centers that require close-range indoor localization to augment outdoor tracking systems.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior and exact telemetry fields depend on the device firmware and the supplier firmware build active on each unit.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can change available sensors, battery life, or accessory support.
- Installation choices, such as base station placement and network integration, affect overall accuracy and reporting cadence.
- Plaspy presents data reported by the RTLS infrastructure; some alarm handling and display options may be configurable in Plaspy or on the RTLS side.
- Always verify the specific capabilities and configuration options for your units with the manufacturer documentation.
