# Megastek - MT-350

<div className="device-logo">
![MT-350](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The MT-350 is a precision indoor positioning wristband designed for continuous wear and high accuracy personnel and asset tracking. As a compact wristwatch sized device, it uses Ultra Wideband positioning to provide reliable real time location with typical accuracy in the 10 to 30 centimeter range. The ergonomic bracelet form factor, wireless charging support and mission oriented safety features make it suitable for sustained use in environments where precise indoor location matters.

Because the MT-350 is built to integrate with Plaspy compatible location platforms, it extends Plaspy deployment coverage into indoor spaces where GPS cannot reach. The wristband transmits location and status updates via UWB infrastructure, and its SOS, tamper detection, motion sensing and device health reporting allow Plaspy to present unified location, alarm and maintenance data for personnel and high value assets.

## Key Highlights

- High precision indoor positioning with UWB based location and 10–30 cm accuracy for close range tracking.
- Wristband form factor for continuous wear and occupant comfort in personnel tracking scenarios.
- Wireless charging support and long battery life to reduce service interruptions and keep devices operational.
- Built in safety features including SOS button, tamper detection, vibration and audible alerts to support rapid response.
- Device health and status reporting to simplify centralized maintenance and uptime monitoring.
- Configurable reporting intervals and motion sensitivity to balance location frequency with battery consumption.
- Designed for integration with ceiling mounted UWB base stations to form a complete RTLS that is Plaspy compatible.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with UWB base stations, the MT-350 forms part of an indoor RTLS that forwards location and status messages to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy can ingest that data to display positions on maps, correlate alarms and provide operational reporting alongside other telemetry in a centralized view.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered from UWB infrastructure into Plaspy for mapping and monitoring.
- SOS button events and tamper alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate operator notification and incident workflows.
- Battery and device health telemetry included in Plaspy dashboards to support predictive maintenance and asset uptime.
- Motion and activity data used to infer movement states and trigger configurable alerts inside Plaspy.
- Adjustable reporting intervals allow Plaspy users to tune frequency for accuracy or extended battery life based on operational needs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Correctional facilities needing precise indoor tracking with SOS and anti disassembly protections for safety and security.
- Hospitals and care facilities for patient and staff location, fall awareness and rapid response integration.
- Industrial sites and manufacturing floors where indoor personnel location and restricted area alerts improve safety.
- Parolee or supervised release programs requiring continuous indoor reporting with tamper and low battery alerts.
- Warehouses and logistics centers that need close range localization to complement outdoor vehicle tracking.

The MT-350 offers focused indoor positioning performance that complements Plaspy’s broader tracking capabilities. Its precise UWB location, wearable design and safety oriented features make it a practical choice when indoor situational awareness and alarm driven workflows are required alongside outdoor fleet or asset tracking.
