# Megastek - GMT-368SG

<div className="device-logo">
![GMT-368SG](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Megastek GMT-368SG is a vehicle GPS tracker designed to deliver accurate and dependable tracking for a variety of vehicle types. It features a waterproof IP66 enclosure to withstand rain and harsh weather, and supports tracking over SMS and GPRS (TCP/UDP). The device provides regular interval reporting, real time tracking, and a suite of alarms and monitoring features including geo fencing, over speed alarm, vibration alarm, low battery alarm, no GPS signal alarm, and an external power connected or disconnected alarm. It also includes a data logger to store location data when cellular coverage is unavailable.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GMT-368SG can be integrated into fleet management workflows to provide visibility and operational oversight. When connected to Plaspy, its location updates, configured alerts, and stored log data can be surfaced in the Plaspy platform for monitoring, alerting, and reporting. The unit's multiple inputs and outputs also allow organizations to extend tracking with additional signals that Plaspy can present alongside location information.

## Key Highlights

- Waterproof IP66 design for reliable outdoor and all weather operation
- Supports SMS and GPRS reporting for real time and scheduled location updates
- Built in alerts including geo fencing, over speed, vibration, low battery, and no GPS signal
- Data logger stores location when there is no cellular coverage for later synchronization
- Multiple I O options with 2 digital outputs 3 digital inputs and 2 analog inputs for added integrations
- External power connect disconnect alarm plus power saving mode and authorized phone number support

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the GMT-368SG provides vehicle location and event data that Plaspy can display and act on through the platform. Plaspy ingests the tracker reports and presents them in dashboards and maps so fleet managers can monitor movement and respond to events.

- Live location tracking and map views for active vehicle monitoring
- Historical route and position reporting using regular tracking intervals and logged data
- Geofence alerts and over speed notifications surfaced inside Plaspy for operational response
- Vibration low battery and external power alarms available as events in the Plaspy alert system
- Data logger synchronization after coverage gaps so Plaspy can reconstruct missing segments
- Use of digital and analog inputs to reflect external device states or external triggers in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small to medium commercial fleets
- Delivery and logistics operations requiring location visibility and route history
- Outdoor vehicles and equipment operating in harsh weather environments
- Rental vehicle monitoring including geo fence and unauthorized movement alerts
- Remote asset tracking where intermittent cellular coverage is expected
