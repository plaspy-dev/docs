# Megastek - GMT-368SG

![GMT-368SG](./logo.jpg)

The Megastek GMT-368SG is a high-quality GPS vehicle tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable tracking for your vehicles. With its waterproof design \(IP66\), this tracker can withstand harsh weather conditions, ensuring that your tracking is not affected by rain or other environmental factors.

One of the standout features of the GMT-368SG is its ability to track your vehicles using SMS/GPRS \(TCP/UDP\). This means that you can easily monitor the location of your vehicles in real-time, allowing you to keep track of their movements and ensure their safety. Additionally, the tracker supports regular tracking, allowing you to set intervals at which the tracker will update its location.

The GMT-368SG also offers a range of other useful features, including the ability to set 3 authorized cell phone numbers, power saving mode to conserve battery life, real-time tracking, geo-fencing to set virtual boundaries for your vehicles, over-speed alarm to alert you when your vehicles exceed a certain speed, vibration alarm to detect any unauthorized movement, low battery alarm, and no GPS signal alarm. It also has a data logger to store location data when there is no cellular coverage.

Furthermore, the GMT-368SG comes with 2 digital outputs, 3 digital inputs, and 2 analog inputs, allowing you to connect additional devices and sensors to enhance the functionality of your tracking system. It also provides an external power connected/disconnected alarm, notifying you when the tracker is connected or disconnected from an external power source.
