# Megastek - GT-99 Features

This page provides a public summary of the Megastek GT-99 features and explains how those capabilities are commonly used with Plaspy for tracking, alerts, and operational visibility. The focus here is on user-facing functions and how those functions appear in a fleet or asset monitoring workflow with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation choices, regional variants, and how the manufacturer implements specific functions. For device-specific configuration, firmware release notes, and the most current technical details please consult the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The GT-99 is a compact tracker designed to deliver reliable position reporting, basic alerting, and data logging for mobile and fixed assets. It combines a SiRF Star III GPS chipset for position accuracy with a SIM900 quad-band GSM module for broad cellular coverage, and includes a set of built-in alerts and power management options to suit longer deployments.

- Precise GPS positioning using the SiRF Star III chipset for consistent location updates
- Quad-band GSM connectivity with wide regional coverage for reporting and alerts
- Configurable tracking modes such as track on demand and scheduled interval reporting
- Safety and alerting features including an SOS button, geo-fencing alarm, and over-speed warning
- Onboard data logger that retains position records when cellular connection is unavailable
- Power saving mode and low battery warning to support extended deployments

## Core Features of Megastek - GT-99

- SiRF Star III GPS chipset for location accuracy
- SIM900 quad-band GSM support for broad cellular compatibility
- Support for up to three authorized cell phone numbers for controlled access
- Track on demand and periodic time interval reporting modes
- SOS emergency button that generates an alarm notification
- Geo-fencing alarm to notify entry or exit from predefined areas
- Over-speed limit warning for speed monitoring alerts
- Built-in data logger and low battery and No GPS warnings for operational visibility

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present the events and position updates that the GT-99 generates, turning device signals into actionable operational data and historical records. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and maps incoming messages to location, event, and status views so teams can monitor devices without deep protocol work.

- Live and periodic location updates from the GT-99 appear on Plaspy maps as current position and track history
- SOS, geo-fence, and over-speed events are recorded as alerts and can be reviewed in the event timeline
- Data logger uploads enable Plaspy to reconstruct historical movement when real-time coverage was interrupted
- Battery level and No GPS warnings are shown as device status indicators for maintenance and troubleshooting
- Tracking mode changes such as track on demand or interval reporting affect how Plaspy receives updates and displays recent activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle tracking for small fleets needing position, over-speed alerts, and geofence events
- Personal safety monitoring where the SOS function provides an immediate alert when pressed
- Asset monitoring for equipment that benefits from data logging when cellular coverage is intermittent
- Rental or shared vehicle oversight combining position records, speed alerts, and status warnings
- Long-term deployments where power saving and low battery notifications help extend operational life

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and hardware revisions can change how specific alarms, thresholds, or message formats behave
- Installation method and wiring (power source, antenna placement) affect GPS reception and some warnings such as No GPS
- Regional cellular coverage and operator compatibility determine the effectiveness of GSM reporting
- Some features such as authorized number configuration, geo-fence setup, and SOS recipient behavior are managed on the device and may require manufacturer instructions
- Always confirm which functions are enabled on your specific unit and firmware level before deploying at scale
