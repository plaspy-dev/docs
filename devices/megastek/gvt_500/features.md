# Megastek - GVT-500 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Megastek GVT-500 tracker with Plaspy. It is intended to help fleet managers, integrators, and technical decision makers understand what the device can report and how those capabilities are commonly surfaced in Plaspy for monitoring and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and runtime behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer configuration. The statements on this page are based on the device description provided by the manufacturer and describe user-facing functions that are relevant when the GVT-500 is used together with Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The GVT-500 is a full featured vehicle tracker combining multi constellation positioning, onboard inputs and outputs, and several alarm and control capabilities. When connected to Plaspy the device provides location awareness, event notifications, and remote control options that support security and operational workflows.

- Accurate position reporting using GPS and Glonass satellite positioning for improved location precision
- Onboard inputs and outputs including digital and analog channels for status monitoring and remote control actions
- Configurable alarms and notifications such as geo fence alerts, over speed warning, low power alerts, and SOS activation
- Remote fuel and electricity cut off capability for theft response and vehicle immobilization scenarios
- Local data logging via Micro SD card to retain position history when required
- Power saving modes and motion sensing to extend device operational time and reduce unnecessary reporting

## Core Features of Megastek - GVT-500

- Dual constellation positioning with GPS and Glonass support for reliable tracking
- RS-232 interface for wired connectivity and integration with vehicle systems
- Remote fuel and electricity cut off function for controlled immobilization
- SOS button for immediate alarm and rescue notification
- Geo fencing alarm and over speed limit warning for automated alerts
- Motion sensor and low power warning for activity and battery monitoring
- Background voice surveillance capability for situational awareness where supported
- Data Logger support using Micro SD card plus 4 digital inputs (including ACC), 2 analog inputs, and 2 outputs for flexible integrations

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives tracking and event messages from compatible devices and presents location, alarms, and status in a single operational view. The GVT-500 can be configured to report to Plaspy so that its alerts and controls are visible to dispatchers and administrators.

- Real time location and historical positions displayed on Plaspy maps and playback tools
- Geo fence breaches, overspeed alerts, SOS triggers, and low power warnings shown as events and notifications
- Device inputs and outputs represented as status indicators and event triggers within the Plaspy interface
- Remote cut off capability surfaced as a commandable control when the device and installation support remote actuation
- Plaspy can accept device reporting to d.plaspy.com and will automatically detect the tracker protocol to simplify setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and route monitoring for delivery and service vehicles
- Theft response and vehicle immobilization using remote fuel or power cut off
- Safety monitoring with SOS alerts and background voice surveillance where permitted
- Asset tracking with motion sensing and data logger retention for off line history
- Remote telemetry and status monitoring through digital and analog inputs
- Over speed and geo fence enforcement for compliance and operational control

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature availability depends on device firmware and factory configuration; some functions may be optional or require specific firmware versions
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may change the presence or behavior of inputs, outputs, and alarms
- Installation and wiring determine whether remote cut off and certain inputs are functional in a vehicle
- Background voice surveillance and SOS behavior may be regulated by local law and require appropriate consent and configuration
- Check manufacturer documentation for exact command syntax, wiring diagrams, and supported firmware features

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the Megastek GVT-500 with Plaspy brings device-level capabilities into a centralized monitoring and operational platform. Plaspy consolidates location, alarms, and device status so teams can respond to incidents, manage vehicle behavior, and analyze historical movement from a single dashboard. The combination of dual constellation positioning, digital inputs, and remote control options makes the GVT-500 useful for security sensitive and operational applications when paired with an accessible tracking platform.
