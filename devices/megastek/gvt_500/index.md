# Megastek - GVT-500

![GVT-500](./logo.jpg)

The Megastek GVT-500 is a highly reliable GPS tracker that combines Glonass and GPS chipset technology for accurate and precise tracking. With its RS-232 interface, it offers seamless connectivity and easy setup. This tracker is designed to be user-friendly, allowing you to set multiple instructions simultaneously and configure a SMS center for convenient communication.

One of the standout features of the GVT-500 is its remote Fuel & Electricity Cut-off Function, which provides an added layer of security and control. You can also set up to 5 authorized cell phone numbers, ensuring that only trusted individuals have access to the tracker's information.

With the GVT-500, you can track your assets on demand and take advantage of power-saving mode to extend battery life. In case of emergencies, the SOS button allows for immediate rescue and alarm activation. The tracker also offers geo-fencing alarm, over speed limit warning, low power warning, motion sensor, and background voice surveillance for comprehensive monitoring and security.

The GVT-500 supports Data Logger using a Micro SD card, allowing you to store and retrieve important tracking data. It features 4 digital inputs \(including ACC\) and 2 analog inputs, as well as 2 outputs for versatile connectivity options. With its compact dimensions of 104X97X37mm and a net weight of 730g, the GVT-500 is easy to install and transport.

Overall, the Megastek GVT-500 is a powerful and feature-rich GPS tracker that offers advanced tracking capabilities and a range of useful functions. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or loved ones, the GVT-500 is a reliable choice that provides peace of mind and enhanced security.
