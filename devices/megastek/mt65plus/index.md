# Megastek - MT65PLUS

![MT65PLUS](./tracker.jpg)

The MT65PLUS is a professional grade ankle bracelet GPS tracker designed for continuous personal monitoring and tamper resistant deployments. Built in a reinforced form factor, the device combines physical anti tamper measures with physiological telemetry and two way voice to support reliable real time tracking, incident alerting, and supervised interventions in demanding use environments.

As an out of the box Plaspy compatible device, the MT65PLUS streams location, status and event data into Plaspy for live monitoring and historical review. That compatibility makes the bracelet suitable for organizations that need centralized visibility, configurable alerts, and operational workflows for supervised release, care management, and other monitored programs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking and event reporting on monitoring dashboards.
- Robust anti tamper design with reinforced strap, physical lock, removal detection and audible alarms.
- Built in heart rate sensor and physiological telemetry for continuous health aware monitoring.
- Multi mode positioning and Wi Fi assisted fixes to improve accuracy in outdoor and indoor settings.
- Two way voice and SOS alerting to enable direct communication and rapid incident verification.
- Rugged, certified hardware with waterproof protection and reinforced construction for professional deployments.
- Optional mobile battery cradle to extend operating time and simplify field maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the MT65PLUS delivers location updates and telemetry to Plaspy maps and event streams so supervisors can maintain situational awareness, configure alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy consumes the device status and alarm events and makes them available to monitoring teams as part of standard workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry displayed on Plaspy map views with historic playback for audits.
- Tamper and removal events plus low battery or power loss warnings trigger configurable alerts to supervisors.
- Continuous heart rate telemetry appears as part of the user profile for care and incident dashboards.
- SOS alerts and two way voice events are logged to Plaspy and can be routed into response procedures.
- Remote lock and unlock events are recorded and can be managed as part of supervised intervention workflows.
- Wi Fi assisted positioning and other status channels help improve indoor or home monitoring accuracy within Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Correctional and parole monitoring where tamper alerts and supervised controls are required.
- Parolee and offender supervision with SOS and two way voice for incident verification.
- Elderly and special care monitoring combining location and heart rate telemetry for caregiver oversight.
- Home and indoor monitoring using Wi Fi assisted positioning to complement outdoor fixes.
- Professional deployments that require durable certified hardware integrated into central monitoring systems.

MT65PLUS is purpose built for personal monitoring programs that demand tamper resistance, dependable tracking, and basic physiological telemetry.
