# Megastek - GT-89

<div className="device-logo">
![GT-89](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Megastek GT-89 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker built to deliver reliable real time location information. It uses a SiRF Star III GPS chipset together with a SIM 900 GSM chipset and supports quad band frequencies for broad cellular coverage. With a small footprint of 86 x 44 x 22 mm and a net weight of 65 g, the GT-89 is designed for easy deployment in a range of mobile and portable tracking scenarios while offering options such as multiple tracking modes, power saving, and on device data logging.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT-89 can feed its tracking data and event notifications into the Plaspy fleet management platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. The device reports position updates and supports alert conditions such as SOS, geo fencing, overspeed warnings, low battery, and motion events, which Plaspy can surface for monitoring, alerting, and reporting across a fleet or for individual assets.

## Key Highlights

- Built with a SiRF Star III GPS chipset for consistent position reporting
- SIM 900 GSM quad band support for wide cellular coverage
- Compact dimensions and low weight for easy placement and portability
- Supports up to three authorized phone numbers for direct device access
- Multiple tracking modes including track on demand and timed intervals
- Embedded safety and alert features such as SOS, geo fencing, and overspeed warning
- Onboard data logging and power saving mode for extended operation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GT-89's position updates and event signals become part of a centralized tracking workflow for monitoring and historical analysis. Plaspy can visualize location history, manage alerts, and consolidate device status alongside other fleet assets to support operational decisions.

- Map visualization of live position updates and historical tracks
- Alerting and notifications for SOS, geo fence breaches, overspeed, low battery, and no GPS conditions
- Consolidated device status and activity logs for fleet oversight
- Reporting and exportable histories using the GT-89 data logs as a source
- Operational dashboards and filtering to monitor groups of devices or individual units

## Typical Use Cases

- Small vehicle or asset fleet tracking for route visibility and oversight
- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring with SOS and quick dial features
- Portable equipment tracking where a compact and lightweight device is preferred
- Rental or leased asset monitoring with geofence and motion alerts
- Situations that need simple two way communication and direct authorized call access

The GT-89 is a practical option for organizations seeking a compact tracker that balances essential tracking features with straightforward operation. Its combination of reliable GNSS performance, broad cellular compatibility, and a set of safety and alert functions make it a good match for businesses that want persistent location visibility without added complexity.

Paired with Plaspy, the GT-89 can contribute useful real time insight and historical data to fleet and asset management workflows.
