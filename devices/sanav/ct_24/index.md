# Sanav - CT-24

![CT-24](./logo.jpg)

The SANAV CT-24 is a GPS tracker that is specifically designed for long battery operation time. With its easy installation process, this tracker is a convenient choice for various tracking applications. The high GPS and GSM sensitivities of the CT-24 allow it to function effectively even when placed under the dashboard of a vehicle, eliminating the need for an external antenna. This makes it a discreet and versatile option for vehicle tracking, asset/container tracking, personal tracking, pet tracking, or covert tracking.

In addition to its impressive functionality, the SANAV CT-24 offers a range of features that enhance its performance. It provides two sets of open I/O, allowing for flexible customization and integration with other devices. The tracker also includes a vibration sensor and reed switches, further expanding its capabilities. With its full quadband GSM band, voice capabilities, backup battery, and internal memory, the CT-24 is a reliable and efficient GPS tracker.

Overall, the SANAV CT-24 is a high-quality GPS tracker that combines ease of use, advanced features, and long battery life. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, individuals, or pets, this tracker offers the reliability and functionality you need.

### Key Features:

- Long battery operation time
- Easy installation
- High GPS and GSM sensitivities
- Can function without external antenna
- Multiple tracking applications
- 2 sets of open I/O
- Vibration sensor and reed switches
- Full quadband GSM band
- Voice capabilities
- Backup battery
- Internal memory
