# Sanav - CT-58

![CT-58](./logo.jpg)

The Sanav CT-58 is a mini GPS tracker that is perfect for various tracking purposes, including vehicle, container, or asset tracking. With its high sensitive vibration sensor, the CT-58 can accurately detect movement and provide real-time location updates. This makes it an ideal tool for monitoring and protecting your valuable properties.

One of the standout features of the CT-58 is its built-in vibration sensor. This sensor is designed to detect any movement of the tracker, and when activated, it will send out reports to keep you informed. Additionally, the CT-58 can be equipped with an external panic button \(optional\), which, when pressed, will trigger an SOS report, ensuring that help is just a button press away.

The CT-58 has also been improved in terms of GPS and GSM receptions, ensuring reliable and accurate tracking data. Its compact and robust enclosure makes it durable and suitable for various environments. With its small size of 8.4\(L\) x 6.4\(W\) x 2.3\(H\) cm and lightweight design of 98g \(with battery\), the CT-58 is easy to install and conceal.

Overall, the Sanav CT-58 is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker that offers advanced features such as a vibration sensor and optional panic button. Whether you need to track vehicles, containers, or assets, the CT-58 is the perfect companion to help you monitor and protect your valuable properties.
