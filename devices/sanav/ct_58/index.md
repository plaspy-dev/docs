# Sanav - CT-58

![CT-58](./logo.jpg)

The Sanav CT-58 is a compact mini GPS tracker designed for flexible tracking needs such as vehicles, containers, and movable assets. It includes a high sensitivity vibration sensor to detect movement and send reports when activity is detected. Its improved GPS and GSM reception, together with a robust small enclosure, make it suitable for discreet monitoring in a range of operational environments.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the CT-58 can feed location and movement reports into Plaspy's fleet management and tracking platform. That compatibility allows organizations to view real time locations, monitor motion events triggered by the vibration sensor, and include the CT-58 in broader visibility, reporting, and alerting workflows that Plaspy provides.

## Key Highlights

- Miniature form factor that is easy to place and conceal for asset and vehicle monitoring
- Built in high sensitivity vibration sensor for movement detection and event reporting
- Optional external panic button for SOS reporting when immediate attention is required
- Improved GPS and GSM reception to help maintain consistent location updates
- Compact and robust enclosure for use in varied environments
- Lightweight design suitable for asset and container tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the CT-58 is used with Plaspy, its location and movement reports can be integrated into Plaspy's monitoring interface so operators gain situational awareness and historical context. Plaspy ingests the tracker reports and presents them alongside other fleet or asset data to support operational decisions.

- Real time location display of CT-58 equipped assets on Plaspy maps
- Movement alerts based on the built in vibration sensor to notify operators of activity
- SOS or panic button events routed into Plaspy alert streams for quick response
- Historical position logs and basic route visibility for incident review and reporting
- Inclusion of CT-58 devices in Plaspy groups and monitoring dashboards for centralized oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Concealed or compact vehicle tracking where minimal size is important
- Container monitoring to detect movement and track location during transport
- Portable asset protection for high value or frequently relocated items
- Rental fleet oversight where movement alerts and location visibility are useful
- Situations that require discreet installation and periodic tracking updates

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CT-58 is a practical choice for organizations seeking a small, movement aware tracker to pair with a comprehensive tracking platform like Plaspy. Its vibration detection and optional panic reporting provide simple event triggers that can be turned into operational alerts within Plaspy, helping teams react to unexpected movement or emergency signals.

While the CT-58 focuses on compact physical design and reliable location reporting, pairing it with Plaspy brings the benefits of centralized visibility, alerting, and reporting. This combination is well suited to fleets, logistics operators, and asset managers who need straightforward tracking and event awareness without bulky hardware.
