# Sanav - MU201S1

![MU201S1](./logo.jpg)

The SANAV MU201S1 is a compact GPS tracker designed for pet and asset tracking, as well as other applications that require long battery life. This tracker can transmit its location in real-time or retrospectively via GPRS. It offers remote configuration options via both SMS and GPRS, allowing for maximum flexibility in the field. The firmware can also be upgraded over GPRS, making maintenance easy. The MU201S1 supports various event reports, including emergency SOS alerts, geofence violations, and low power alerts. With its reliable performance, user-friendly interface, and excellent GPS/GSM capabilities, this tracker makes tracking your assets or pets much easier.

One of the standout features of the SANAV MU201S1 is its built-in motion sensor, which allows for accurate tracking and reporting. It also supports reporting based on time and distance intervals, as well as reporting when the tracker is moved. In case of emergencies, the SOS alert feature can be activated, and the tracker also provides over-speed and low power alerts. With a power on/off alert, you can easily monitor the status of the tracker. Additionally, the MU201S1 supports up to 10 circular geofences, allowing you to set boundaries and receive alerts when the tracker enters or exits these areas. You can even acquire the position of the tracker by sending a simple SMS command.

The SANAV MU201S1 operates on quad-band GSM frequencies \(850/900/1800/1900MHz\) and supports GPRS multi-slot class 10 and GPRS mobile station class B. It has a maximum RF power of 33.0dBm±2dBm and a receiving sensitivity of Class II RBER≤2.4％\(-109dBm\). The GPS chipset used is u-blox7, and it has a receiver type of GPS L1 frequency, C/A Code. With 56 channels for all-in-view tracking, the MU201S1 provides accurate and reliable GPS positioning. The tracker is powered by a 1200mAh Li-Polymer battery, which can be charged via the mini USB charging interface. It also has a power button for easy on/off control.

In terms of dimensions, the SANAV MU201S1 measures 60mm x 35mm x 25.5mm and weighs 60g with the battery. It supports various air interface protocols, including TCP, UDP, SMS, and HTTP for data transmission. The tracker also offers 10 geo-fence regions that can be defined, allowing you to set specific boundaries for tracking. With a low power alarm, the MU201S1 will alert you when the battery is running low. It also provides a power on report, which sends a report when the tracker is powered up. The operating temperature range is -10°C to +55°C, and the tracker is designed to withstand relative humidity levels of 5% to 95%, non-condensing.
