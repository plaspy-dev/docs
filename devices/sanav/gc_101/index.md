# Sanav - GC-101

![GC-101](./logo.jpg)

The SANAV GC-101 is a compact GPS tracker designed for intensive tracking applications such as fleet management and life security. It uses GPRS to upload RMC sentences that include latitude, longitude, speed, and time to an assigned web server. The device offers full quadband GSM coverage, onboard memory and a backup battery to preserve data during power interruptions, and a durable plastic enclosure with internal GSM antennas for a low profile installation.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GC-101 is well suited to provide continuous location feeds and basic telemetry that Plaspy can ingest for monitoring and reporting. Its support for TCP and UDP communications, configurable position reporting by time or distance, sleep mode to reduce transmissions, and panic input make it a useful option for organizations that want consistent visibility of vehicles and assets within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- GPRS upload of RMC sentences containing latitude, longitude, speed, and time for straightforward position reporting  
- Full quadband GSM support for wide area connectivity in varied locations  
- Backup battery and internal memory to preserve data during power loss or network outages  
- Support for TCP and UDP server communication for flexible data delivery  
- Position reporting based on time and distance to tailor update frequency to operational needs  
- Sleep mode to reduce transmissions and preserve power between reports  
- Predefined inputs such as panic button for safety alerts, compact plastic housing and internal antennas

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GC-101 can feed positional RMC data to Plaspy servers where it is displayed, logged, and used to trigger alerts and reports. Plaspy interprets the time, position, and speed data to provide live visibility and historical playback while allowing operators to configure notification rules and reporting intervals to match operational needs.

- Real time location display and map tracking inside Plaspy based on uploaded RMC sentences  
- Historical route playback and reporting using the tracker timestamps and stored positions  
- Alerting in Plaspy from predefined inputs such as panic button events for rapid response  
- Use of time or distance based reporting to balance update frequency against data usage and battery life  
- Data continuity handling in Plaspy when the device uses internal memory and backup power to store and later upload missed positions

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and operational oversight for transport and service fleets  
- Asset security monitoring for valuable equipment and trailers  
- Life security and personal safety monitoring where panic inputs are required  
- Delivery and logistics visibility with configurable reporting intervals  
- Remote or cross region tracking where quadband GSM ensures broader connectivity
