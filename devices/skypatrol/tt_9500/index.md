# SkyPatrol - TT 9500

![TT 9500](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol TT 9500 is a compact, all in one GPS tracker built for volume tracking needs across a wide range of environments. Its rugged splash proof housing and durable construction make it suitable for outdoor and industrial use, while design elements such as low power consumption and a built in relay support long operational life and remote control of connected equipment. The TT 9500 also includes advanced geofencing options and a high sensitivity GPS receiver to support reliable location monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TT 9500 can be integrated into fleet and asset management workflows to provide continuous visibility and event driven notifications. Plaspy can consume the tracker data and present location, geofence events, counters, and relay control actions alongside other devices in the platform, making the TT 9500 a practical option for organizations that need scalable tracking with centralized operations oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and versatile all in one tracker suited to volume deployment and general fleet use  
- Rugged splash proof housing for reliable operation in outdoor and industrial settings  
- Device based geofencing with support for up to 250 circular geofences for flexible zone monitoring  
- Internal relay for remote activation or deactivation of connected equipment or systems  
- Low power consumption for extended deployment life and reduced maintenance needs  
- Global GSM compatibility via quad band modem and an ultra sensitive GPS receiver for reliable positioning

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TT 9500 feeds location and event information into a central fleet management environment so teams can monitor assets, respond to alerts, and run reports. Plaspy surfaces the device events alongside other fleet data to support operational decisions and historical analysis.

- Real time location updates and position history available through Plaspy dashboards and maps  
- Geofence event handling using the device based geofences for entry and exit notifications and logging  
- Remote control commands routed through the tracker to operate the internal relay from Plaspy when allowed by deployment policies  
- Counters and event tallies integrated into Plaspy reporting for mileage or usage tracking and operational metrics  
- Alerts and notifications configured in Plaspy to trigger on movement, geofence breaches, or counter thresholds

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial vehicle fleet tracking and route oversight for logistics operations  
- Asset tracking for equipment that needs periodic remote monitoring and control  
- Security and perimeter monitoring where geofencing and remote relay control are useful  
- Rental or shared vehicle programs tracking usage and location for operational reporting  
- Industrial site monitoring where rugged devices and long runtime are required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TT 9500 is a practical choice for organizations that require a durable, easy to deploy tracker with strong geofencing capabilities and an internal relay for remote control. Its design emphasizes volume deployment and ongoing operational efficiency, which aligns with Plaspy environments that aggregate many devices and require consolidated visibility.

Because the tracker supports a range of geofence types and counters, it can be used to implement detailed monitoring rules and reporting in Plaspy without relying on additional hardware. For operations that need global coverage and dependable positioning, the TT 9500 paired with Plaspy provides a straightforward option to centralize tracking, alerts, and basic remote control functions.
