# SkyPatrol - SP7600

<div className="device-logo">
![SP7600](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The SkyPatrol SP7600 is a compact OBD GPS tracking device series designed for fast plug and play deployment and flexible use across vehicles. The SP7600 family emphasizes portability and intelligent power management, making it well suited for applications that require frequent device reassignment or minimal on vehicle intrusion. Common positioning and security features described for the series support fleet oversight and vehicle recovery needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP7600 can deliver the location visibility and operational data fleet teams expect into Plaspy's tracking and management platform. Its compact OBD form factor and portable design make it straightforward to add devices to Plaspy for vehicle monitoring, reporting, and alerts without extensive hardware changes. That compatibility helps organizations centralize tracking, security, and UBI style monitoring within the Plaspy environment.

## Key Highlights

- Compact OBD design that fits directly with a wide range of vehicles
- Intelligent power management to minimize vehicle impact during use
- Plug and play installation for quick deployment and device reassignment
- Portable and easy to move between cars for flexible asset use
- Provides instant location tracking to support security and recovery
- Suited for fleet visibility, dealership lot management, and UBI programs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SP7600 provides vehicle location and status information that Plaspy ingests and presents for operational use. This compatibility allows fleet managers and administrators to use Plaspy tools to monitor vehicles, set alerts, and review historical movement data from SP7600 devices.

- Live map visibility of SP7600 equipped vehicles inside the Plaspy interface
- Configurable alerts and notifications for movement, location entries and exits, and security events
- Historical trip and time reports to support fleet reporting and usage based insurance analysis
- Easy reassignment and device management to reflect the SP7600 series portability
- Centralized oversight combining SP7600 data with other fleet information in Plaspy
