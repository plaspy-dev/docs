# SkyPatrol - SP4603

<div className="device-logo">
![SP4603](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The SkyPatrol SP4603 Series is a feature rich GPS tracking device designed for demanding applications such as fleet management and field dispatch. It is also suitable for mainstream uses like insurance telematics and vehicle location and recovery. Offered in 2G and 3G variants and built on a quad band GSM GPRS platform, the SP4603 provides positioning with an integrated GPS module and a compact durable form factor aimed at mobile vehicle deployments.

This model is compatible with Plaspy, making it a practical option for organizations that want to combine SkyPatrol hardware with Plaspy fleet and asset monitoring. Plaspy can receive location updates and event notifications from the SP4603, surface geofence activity and device status in the Plaspy platform, and help operators monitor fleets and respond to incidents with a unified dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for professional fleet management and field dispatch environments
- Available in 2G and 3G versions to suit regional deployment needs
- Over the air device management and maintenance capabilities for remote updates
- Supports FOTA to keep device firmware current without manual intervention
- Built in GSM jamming detection to increase reliability and security awareness
- Up to 28 hardware based geofences for flexible virtual boundary monitoring
- Various input and output options and a compact durable physical design

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SP4603 provides continuous location visibility and event reporting that feeds into Plaspy workflows for monitoring and operations. Plaspy can collect position and event data from the device and present it alongside routing, alerting, and reporting tools so teams can manage vehicles and assets efficiently.

- Live and historical location tracking visible in Plaspy dashboards
- Geofence event notifications and boundary reports for operational alerts
- Fleet level monitoring for dispatch and operational oversight
- Alerts for security events such as GSM jamming detection surfaced to operators
- Device status and management information integrated into Plaspy reporting
- Consolidated reporting and exportable history for compliance and analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet management and route dispatch for field teams
- Insurance telematics for usage based monitoring and recovery support
- Vehicle location and recovery services where continuous tracking is required
- Field service operations that need reliable position reporting and geofence alerts
- Organizations seeking remote device management to reduce onsite maintenance
