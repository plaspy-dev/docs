# SkyPatrol - SP3600

<div className="device-logo">
![SP3600](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The SkyPatrol SP3600 Series is a vehicle focused GPS tracking device designed to deliver reliable location monitoring and operational visibility. Built for transportation and fleet environments, the SP3600 offers quad band GSM GPRS connectivity, real time tracking, geofencing, and a set of onboard sensors including an accelerometer and a temperature sensor. Its robust design aims to address common needs such as fleet management, insurance telematics, dispatch coordination, and vehicle recovery.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP3600 can be brought into a centralized tracking platform to enhance visibility and reporting across a mixed fleet. Plaspy can receive location updates, geofence events, and sensor related alerts from SP3600 devices to provide consolidated dashboards, alerts, and historical reports that support day to day operations and longer term analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Vehicle oriented design with features aimed at fleet and recovery use cases
- Quad band GSM GPRS connectivity for wide area communications
- Real time tracking to monitor vehicle location continuously
- Geofencing capability for virtual boundaries and entry exit alerts
- Onboard accelerometer and temperature sensor for basic motion and environmental data
- Durable construction suitable for demanding vehicle environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SP3600 becomes part of a unified fleet monitoring solution that consolidates tracking and operational data. Plaspy ingests device updates and presents them in an accessible interface so teams can monitor vehicles, respond to events, and generate reports.

- Live location visualization for immediate operational awareness
- Geofence event handling to trigger alerts and notifications within Plaspy
- Aggregated historical tracking for trip review and route analysis
- Sensor driven alerts to highlight motion changes or temperature conditions
- Fleet level reporting to track utilization and support decision making

## Typical Use Cases

- Day to day fleet management and route monitoring for transportation providers
- Dispatch coordination to assign assets and monitor progress
- Vehicle location and recovery following loss or theft
- Insurance telematics use cases that require location and basic motion data
- Monitoring temperature sensitive cargo in general transport applications
