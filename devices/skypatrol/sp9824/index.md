# SkyPatrol - SP9824

![SP9824](./tracker.png)

The SP9824 is a Plaspy compatible portable GPS tracker designed for long-term deployments where maintenance windows are rare and environmental data matters. Built around innovative power management, the SP9824 delivers up to seven years of battery life in field conditions, combined with an IP67 waterproof enclosure and onboard light and temperature sensors. These attributes make it a strong choice for cold chain logistics, cargo protection, and static asset monitoring when integrated into the Plaspy platform.

As a Plaspy compatible asset tracker, the SP9824 feeds location and telemetry into Plaspy’s fleet management and asset monitoring dashboards for reliable real-time tracking and historical reporting. Its tamper detection and environmental sensing enable anti-theft alerts and condition-based notifications, helping operations reduce loss, protect sensitive loads, and optimize asset utilization without frequent battery swaps.

## Key Highlights

- Exceptional battery life — up to seven years on optimized power management for low-maintenance deployments.
- Rugged, waterproof IP67 enclosure for outdoor, maritime, and refrigerated environments.
- Integrated light and temperature sensors for cold chain telemetry and environmental condition monitoring.
- Tamper detection to trigger immediate alerts in Plaspy for rapid anti-theft response.

- Designed to feed telemetry and location data into Plaspy for real-time tracking and reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SP9824 provides location and sensor telemetry that Plaspy ingests to deliver live maps, event alerts, and historical reports. Plaspy’s integration captures periodic location updates \(subject to device configuration and network connectivity\), plus the SP9824’s environmental and tamper events, turning raw device messages into actionable fleet management and asset-protection workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — Plaspy displays position fixes and sensor values on live maps and dashboards.
- Tamper detection and alarm events — instant push notifications and event logs inside Plaspy for anti-theft response.
- Temperature and light telemetry — ideal for cold chain monitoring with threshold alerts and trend reports.
- Static asset and battery health reporting — long-duration deployments reduce maintenance while Plaspy alerts on low battery.
- Ignition/immobilizer and fuel monitoring: not applicable to this portable asset tracker; intended for asset/cargo use rather than vehicle control.
