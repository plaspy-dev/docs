# SkyPatrol - SP9824 Features

This page provides the public feature context for using the SkyPatrol SP9824 tracker with Plaspy. It describes the practical capabilities you can expect to surface in Plaspy dashboards and reports, based on the SP9824 description and how the device typically integrates with Plaspy for location and telemetry ingestion.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For deployment planning, verify device specifications and current firmware behavior with the manufacturer and consider how configuration choices affect reporting cadence and sensor events in Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The SP9824 is a Plaspy compatible portable asset tracker focused on long life, environmental sensing, and tamper awareness for low maintenance deployments. It is designed to provide periodic location updates and condition telemetry into Plaspy so teams can monitor assets over long intervals without frequent servicing.

- Long life power management designed to deliver up to seven years of battery life in field conditions for low maintenance deployments.
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure to tolerate outdoor and harsh environmental use cases including refrigerated and maritime settings.
- Onboard temperature and light sensors for environmental monitoring and condition-based alerts.
- Tamper detection to signal possible interference or unauthorized access, with events reportable into Plaspy.
- Portable form factor suitable for pallets, containers, trailers, and static assets where vehicle interfaces are not required.

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP9824

- Long duration battery performance with optimized power management for extended field deployments.
- IP67 waterproof and dust resistant enclosure for outdoor and exposed installations.
- Integrated temperature sensing for cold chain and condition monitoring.
- Integrated light sensing to help detect exposure or opening events.
- Tamper detection to report potential interference or removal attempts.
- Designed to provide location and telemetry to Plaspy for real time visibility and historical reporting.
- Portable and compact design suitable for asset and cargo attachment rather than vehicle control.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the SP9824 is configured to report to Plaspy, location fixes and onboard sensor telemetry are ingested by the Plaspy platform and turned into map positions, alerts, and historical records. Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol in many cases and can present the SP9824 data alongside other fleet and asset information for unified monitoring.

- Live and historical location visualization on Plaspy maps and timeline reports.
- Environmental telemetry such as temperature and light values visible in dashboards and trend charts.
- Tamper events ingested as alerts and recorded in event logs for incident review and response.
- Battery and static asset reporting to help schedule maintenance and detect low battery conditions over long deployments.
- Configurable alerting in Plaspy for threshold breaches and tamper conditions to support operational response workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain logistics monitoring for refrigerated shipments where temperature tracking and long battery life matter.
- Container and pallet tracking to detect tampering and maintain location visibility across transit.
- Static asset monitoring at remote or infrequently serviced sites where long battery life reduces maintenance visits.
- Temporary or seasonal site monitoring where rugged, waterproof design and environmental sensing are useful.
- Cargo protection and anti-theft workflows that combine tamper alerts with location data in Plaspy.
