# SkyPatrol - SP3801

![SP3801](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP3801 is an ultra-compact GPS tracker that offers exceptional reliability and performance. Designed to withstand the harshest vehicle environments, this device is perfect for fleet management and personal vehicle tracking. With its small size, it can be easily installed and hidden in any vehicle.

One of the standout features of the SP3801 is its optional OBDII quick connect cable, which allows for quick and hassle-free installation. Within minutes, you can have the device up and running, providing real-time tracking and accurate vehicle positioning. Whether you're monitoring a fleet of vehicles or keeping an eye on your personal car, the SP3801 ensures fast and reliable tracking.

With the SkyPatrol SP3801, you can have peace of mind knowing that your vehicles are always within reach. Its compact size, easy installation, and reliable performance make it an excellent choice for anyone in need of a high-quality GPS tracker.

### Outstanding Features:

- Ultra-compact design
- Proven reliability in harsh vehicle environments
- Optional OBDII quick connect cable for easy installation
- Fast and reliable vehicle positioning
