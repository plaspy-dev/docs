# SkyPatrol - SP3801

![SP3801](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP3801 is an ultra compact GPS tracker engineered for reliable vehicle tracking in demanding conditions. Designed to withstand harsh vehicle environments, the SP3801 is suitable for both fleet management and personal vehicle monitoring. Its small form factor makes it easy to position discreetly inside a vehicle while providing consistent location updates for operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP3801 can be integrated into Plaspy's tracking platform to deliver location visibility and fleet monitoring alongside other devices. The combination of the SP3801's compact design and optional OBDII quick connect makes it a practical choice for organizations that need dependable vehicle positioning with minimal disruption. Plaspy users can add the SP3801 to their device roster to centralize tracking, alerts, and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra compact design that is easy to position and conceal in vehicles
- Built to perform reliably in harsh vehicle environments
- Suited for both fleet management and personal vehicle tracking
- Optional OBDII quick connect cable for faster installation when available
- Designed to provide fast and reliable vehicle positioning
- Practical choice for organizations needing discreet, dependable tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the SP3801 sends location data to the Plaspy platform where vehicles can be monitored alongside other assets. Plaspy aggregates position updates, provides map visualization, and enables reporting to support daily operations and oversight.

- Centralized map view for live location visibility of SP3801 equipped vehicles
- Fleet monitoring dashboards to track vehicle movement and status
- Alerts and notifications configurable in Plaspy for events like entry and exit of defined areas
- Historical reports and playback for route review and operational analysis
- Operational oversight tools to help manage dispatch, utilization, and accountability

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal vehicle tracking for theft deterrence and recovery support
- Rental and shared vehicle oversight for location and usage visibility
- Field service or delivery vehicle monitoring to verify routes and tasks
- Discreet asset tracking where a compact footprint is required
