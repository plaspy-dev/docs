# SkyPatrol - ST7200 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the SkyPatrol ST7200 and how the device can be used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on the practical, non sensitive capabilities of the tracker and explains what owners and operators can expect when monitoring powersports vehicles through Plaspy.

Exact feature availability, behavior, and wording can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. For the most current device specific details, consult the official SkyPatrol documentation.

## Feature Overview

The SkyPatrol ST7200 is built for powersports vehicles and emphasizes long term reliability, low power draw, and resilience in outdoor conditions. Below are the primary user facing capabilities to consider when pairing the device with Plaspy.

- Purpose designed for motorcycles and other powersports vehicles for discreet and durable installation
- Low power consumption to help preserve the vehicle battery during long periods of inactivity
- Internal backup battery that maintains tracking capability if the vehicle battery is disconnected or drained
- Water resistant and vibration resistant construction to withstand off road and marine environments
- Operates on a widely adopted 2nd generation digital network for established cellular coverage
- Strong recovery history used successfully to recover many powersport vehicles

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - ST7200

- Designed specifically for powersports vehicle installations such as motorcycles, ATVs, UTVs, snowmobiles, and personal watercraft
- Low power draw to reduce impact on vehicle battery life when installed long term
- Internal backup battery for continued device operation independent of vehicle power
- Water resistant housing to tolerate wet environments common in powersports use
- Vibration resistant design to handle rough terrain and vehicle vibration
- Operation on a 2nd generation digital cellular network to provide reliable location reporting in supported regions
- Proven recovery performance demonstrated in real world recoveries for powersports assets

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the ST7200's capabilities translate into visibility and operational oversight through a centralized fleet platform. Plaspy is compatible with the ST7200 and will ingest device reports so teams can monitor location and status across assets.

- Real time and near real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps and device lists according to the device reporting behavior
- Battery and connectivity status reported by the tracker are visible in Plaspy for monitoring device health and backup battery activity
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify onboarding and reduce configuration steps
- The device's resilience to water and vibration supports continuous reporting from rugged environments, which improves tracking continuity in Plaspy
- Historical position data from the ST7200 can be viewed in Plaspy for review and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle owners who want ongoing location visibility and a recovery aid for theft scenarios
- Powersports fleet managers tracking ATVs, UTVs, or personal watercraft used in rental or service operations
- Owners of seasonal vehicles who need low power consumption and battery backup during storage periods
- Service providers monitoring vehicle movement and location during transport or delivery
- Operators who require a durable tracker that tolerates wet and vibration heavy conditions
