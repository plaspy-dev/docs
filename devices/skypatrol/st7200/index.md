# SkyPatrol - ST7200

![ST7200](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol ST7200 is a GPS tracking device purpose built for motorcycles and other powersports vehicles. It has a strong field record in vehicle recovery across a range of platforms including motorcycles, ATVs, personal watercraft, snowmobiles, and UTVs. The device is designed for the unique needs of powersports owners with low power consumption, an internal backup battery, and resistance to water and vibration to withstand rugged conditions.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the ST7200 can be integrated into fleet and asset monitoring workflows to provide ongoing location visibility and operational oversight. Its efficiency and backup power make it well suited to Plaspy users who need persistent tracking for vehicles that see intermittent use, while its rugged design aligns with the kinds of assets commonly managed through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for motorcycles and a wide range of powersports vehicles
- Proven track record in vehicle recovery across powersports categories
- Low power consumption to help preserve vehicle battery life
- Internal backup battery to maintain tracking if vehicle power is lost
- Water and vibration resistant for harsh or off road environments
- Operates on a widely adopted 2nd generation digital network for reliable location reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the ST7200 provides location and status data that Plaspy displays and stores to support monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can take the device data and present it in fleet views, activity timelines, and alerting workflows to improve operational awareness.

- Real time and historical location visibility within Plaspy dashboards
- Configurable alerts for movement or unexpected activity routed through Plaspy
- Fleet monitoring and grouping to manage multiple powersports assets together
- Reporting features to analyze trips, recoveries, and asset usage over time
- Operational oversight for managers who need consolidated tracking and status information

## Typical Use Cases

- Theft recovery and location tracking for motorcycles and ATVs
- Management of rental or shared powersports fleets and inventory
- Monitoring stored or seasonally used vehicles for unexpected movement
- Oversight of service and logistics for dealers and service providers
- Tracking UTVs and work vehicles used in remote or rugged environments

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ST7200 aligns with common requirements for powersports tracking: efficient power use for infrequently used vehicles, an internal backup battery for continued operation, and a rugged build to handle vibration and exposure. For organizations using Plaspy, those device traits help ensure the platform receives consistent data for monitoring, alerting, and reporting even when vehicles are stored or used in demanding conditions.

Combining the ST7200 hardware characteristics with Plaspy software capabilities provides a practical solution for owners and operators who need dependable location visibility and recovery support. Plaspy adds centralized dashboards, alerting, and reporting that let teams act on the ST7200 data without needing to manage device level details.
