# SkyPatrol - TT 9200

![TT 9200](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol TT 9200 is a small yet powerful GPS tracker that combines modern GPS chipsets with Skypatrol's EDDIE+ Custom Protocol. This innovative combination allows the device to support up to 250 device-based geofences or 24 point device-based corridors simultaneously. With its compact size and advanced features, the TT 9200 is an ideal solution for a wide range of tracking applications.

One of the standout features of the TT 9200 is its low power consumption, ensuring long-lasting battery life and reducing the need for frequent recharging. Additionally, the tracker is equipped with a quad-band modem, making it compatible with GSM networks in any country. This global compatibility makes it an excellent choice for international tracking needs.

The TT 9200 is built to withstand the rigors of daily use, thanks to its durable and high-quality splash-proof housing. This ensures that the tracker remains protected from water damage, extending its lifespan and reliability. Moreover, the device boasts ultra-sensitive GPS capabilities, with a sensitivity of -162dBI. This high level of sensitivity allows for accurate and precise tracking, even in challenging environments.

With circular geofencing, polygonal geofences, and route geofencing capabilities, the TT 9200 offers versatile tracking options. It also features seven different types of counters, providing users with valuable data and insights. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or people, the TT 9200 offers reliable GSM/GPS functionality to meet your tracking needs.
