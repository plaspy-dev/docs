# SkyPatrol - SP8502

![SP8502](./logo.jpg)

The Skypatrol SP8502 is a compact and portable GPS tracker that offers a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your loved ones or employees. With its small size, it can easily be carried or concealed, making it ideal for tracking field personnel, seniors, and children. Whether you need to monitor employee movement or keep an eye on your family members, the SP8502 is a reliable solution.

One of the standout features of the SP8502 is its user-activated alert button, which provides an added level of security. In case of an emergency or any other situation requiring immediate attention, the user can simply press the alert button to send an alert to a designated contact. This feature is particularly useful for police/security officers, sales reps, couriers, and anyone who may find themselves in potentially risky situations.

Additionally, the SP8502 allows you to track the device's location in real-time using any smartphone or PC. You can easily locate the tracker and receive updates on its whereabouts, giving you peace of mind knowing that your loved ones or employees are safe. Furthermore, you can set up alerts to notify you if the tracker goes beyond predefined boundaries, ensuring that you are immediately informed if someone wanders off.

With its durability and reliability, the Skypatrol SP8502 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of applications for both personal and business use. Whether you need to track field personnel, monitor employee movement, or keep an eye on your family members, the SP8502 provides a convenient and effective solution.

#### Key Features:

- Compact and portable design
- User-activated alert button for added security
- Real-time tracking using smartphones or PCs
- Boundary alerts to notify you if the tracker goes beyond predefined boundaries
- Durable and reliable construction
