# SkyPatrol - SP5824 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the SkyPatrol SP5824 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users and fleet operators, focusing on practical functions such as location reporting, crash alerts, and telemetry integration as they appear in Plaspy dashboards and workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where appropriate, this page flags areas that commonly differ so administrators and installers can confirm current behavior with manufacturer documentation and their device firmware notes.

## Feature Overview

The SP5824 is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for motorcycles and powersport vehicles. It pairs weatherproof construction and a low installation footprint with cellular connectivity and crash detection so owners and operators can maintain visibility and incident awareness for small vehicles and watercraft.

- Compact form factor tailored for motorcycles, ATVs, snowmobiles, jet-skis and small boats for discreet installation.
- IP67 water resistance for operation in wet and marine environments common to powersport use.
- 4G LTE Cat M1 connectivity for consistent real-time location reporting and lower power cellular operation.
- Built-in crash detection to generate immediate incident alerts that can be routed to Plaspy.
- RS232 serial port for adding third party telemetry modules and accessories to extend data sent into Plaspy.

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP5824

- Plaspy compatibility for centralized monitoring, alerts, and reporting.
- 4G LTE Cat M1 cellular radio for real-time tracking and telemetry reporting.
- Crash detection that produces event notifications for safety and incident workflows.
- IP67 rated enclosure to tolerate wet conditions and occasional immersion.
- Zero power consumption design to minimize drain on the host vehicle battery when powered off.
- RS232 serial interface for external telemetry modules and accessory integration.
- Small, rugged form factor optimized for constrained mounting locations on powersport vehicles.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the SP5824 supplies location updates, crash events, and connected telemetry so fleet managers and vehicle owners can monitor status and respond from a single system. Plaspy automatically interprets standard device messages and surfaces them in maps, alerts, and reports.

- Real-time GPS location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and historical route review.
- Crash detection events are forwarded to Plaspy to trigger incident notifications and escalation workflows.
- RS232 sourced telemetry from third party modules can flow into Plaspy as auxiliary inputs or status fields for ignition, fuel state, immobilizer signals, or compatible sensors.
- Low power zero consumption design reduces false low battery notices and keeps devices Plaspy ready during long storage periods.
- IP67 protection helps ensure continuous data flow to Plaspy in wet or marine operating conditions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for powersport rental operations to track location, usage, and incident history.
- Anti-theft monitoring for motorcycles and ATVs with rapid location updates and alerting.
- Marine and watercraft tracking for jet-skis and small boats where water resistance is required.
- Safety and incident response workflows that rely on crash detection routed into Plaspy notifications.
- Telemetry expansion via RS232 for ignition state, fuel monitoring, or integration with immobilizer controllers through third party modules.
- Long term storage scenarios where zero power consumption preserves vehicle battery health while leaving the device ready to report.
