# SkyPatrol - TT 8750+

![TT 8750+](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol TT 8750+ is a compact and rugged GPS tracker designed for a wide range of tracking applications. It combines a quad band modem for broad cellular coverage with an ultra sensitive GPS engine and an embedded antenna array for reliable position fixes. The unit also includes an SMA connector for an optional external GPS antenna, multiple digital and analog inputs and outputs, a motion sensor, a backup battery, and a splash proof housing suitable for demanding environments.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TT 8750+ can feed accurate location and event data into a centralized fleet management platform. Its flexible inputs and outputs support event-driven tracking and control scenarios, while the motion sensor and backup battery help preserve tracking continuity. These characteristics make the TT 8750+ a practical choice for organizations that want Plaspy to provide real time visibility, alerts, and historical reporting across mobile assets.

## Key Highlights

- Quad band modem for wide cellular coverage in most regions
- Ultra sensitive GPS engine with embedded antenna array for consistent positioning
- SMA connector available for optional external GPS antenna in complex setups
- Multiple inputs and outputs including digital inputs, analog input, and digital outputs for flexible event handling
- Wide operating voltage range for compatibility with a variety of vehicle types
- Motion sensor and backup battery to support persistent tracking and wake on movement
- Small, splash proof housing with low power consumption for durable, long term deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TT 8750+ provides location and status updates that the platform uses to deliver monitoring, alerts, and reports. Plaspy ingests data from the tracker and presents it in fleet dashboards, maps, and historical logs so operators can manage assets more efficiently.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps for live fleet visibility
- Configurable alerts and notifications based on movement, inputs, or geofence events
- Event and status monitoring of digital and analog inputs through Plaspy reporting tools
- Historical route playback and trip reporting to support operational analysis
- Low power and motion based reporting to reduce noise and extend operational life between events

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and operational monitoring for buses, vans, and service vehicles
- Rental and leasing asset oversight to track usage and location
- Heavy equipment and trailer monitoring where durable housing and broad voltage range matter
- Security and theft recovery workflows supported by motion detection and backup power
- Field service and delivery operations needing reliable location updates and event alerts
