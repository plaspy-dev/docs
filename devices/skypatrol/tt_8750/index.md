# SkyPatrol - TT 8750+

![TT 8750+](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol TT 8750+ is a powerful and dynamic GPS tracker that is suitable for a wide range of tracking applications. It is equipped with a quad-band modem, allowing it to work anywhere there is cellular service. The tracker features an ultra-sensitive GPS engine with a powerful embedded antenna array, ensuring accurate and reliable tracking. Additionally, it has an SMA connector for an optional external GPS antenna, which can be useful for complex installations.

The TT 8750+ offers 2 digital inputs \(plus ignition\), 1 analog input, and 3 digital outputs, providing flexibility for various tracking needs. It operates on a voltage range of 9 to 32 V, making it compatible with multiple vehicle types. The tracker also includes a motion sensor and backup battery, allowing for different configuration options. Its small, high-quality, and splash-proof housing is designed to withstand harsh environments and ensure durability. Furthermore, the TT 8750+ boasts low power consumption, making it an energy-efficient choice for long-term tracking.

Key Features:

- Quad Band Modem
- Ultra Sensitive GPS Engine
- Embedded Antenna Array
- 2 Digital Inputs \(plus ignition\)
- 1 Analog Input
- 3 Digital Outputs
- 9 - 32 V Operating Voltage
- Motion Sensor & Backup Battery
- Small, High-Quality & Splash Proof Housing
- Low Power Consumption
