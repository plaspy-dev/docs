# SkyPatrol - SP9600

<div className="device-logo">
![SP9600](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The SkyPatrol SP9600 Series is a portable GPS asset tracker designed for long duration monitoring of mobile and valuable items. It is intended for tracking assets such as shipping containers, critical medical supplies, heavy equipment, military gear, and vehicles during transport or storage. The SP9600 Series emphasizes reliable location reporting over extended periods with a rugged build suitable for demanding operational environments.

This model is compatible with Plaspy, making it a practical choice for organizations that want to monitor assets within a unified fleet tracking platform. Plaspy can integrate location reports and status updates from SP9600 devices to provide visibility, historical tracking, and operational oversight across distributed assets. The SP9600's flexible power options and extended reporting life align well with Plaspy workflows that prioritize low-maintenance, long-term asset tracking.

## Key Highlights

- Portable GPS asset tracker built for extended tracking of mobile assets
- Designed for a range of assets including containers, medical shipments, heavy equipment, and vehicles
- Option for replaceable CR123 batteries with a combined capacity of 6000mAh allowing one message per week for up to three years
- Supports rechargeable battery use and charging via an external power connector for flexible power management
- Durable construction suitable for prolonged field use and transport conditions
- Well suited to long interval reporting workflows where maintenance access is limited

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the SP9600 provides location and status updates that feed into Plaspy's fleet and asset management views. Plaspy can capture the device's scheduled reports and present them alongside other fleet telemetry to support operational decisions and reporting.

- Aggregate SP9600 location reports in Plaspy dashboards for asset visibility
- View historical movement and transit progress for tracked shipments and equipment
- Configure alerts in Plaspy based on reporting cadence or location changes to surface exceptions
- Use Plaspy reporting tools to generate operational summaries and audit trails for tracked assets
- Monitor distributed assets with long battery life without frequent on site maintenance

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking shipping containers and freight in transit across long routes
- Monitoring critical medical supplies during distribution and delivery
- Managing the location of heavy construction and industrial equipment
- Accountability and movement logging for military gear and logistics
- Periodic location checks for stored or infrequently moved vehicles and assets

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SP9600 is a practical fit for organizations that need reliable, low-maintenance asset tracking integrated into a fleet management platform. Its emphasis on long-duration battery life and flexible power options reduces the frequency of field interventions, which complements Plaspy environments designed for centralized monitoring and operational oversight.

Because the SP9600 focuses on durable, portable tracking for a wide set of asset types, it pairs well with Plaspy when the primary goals are visibility, simple reporting schedules, and reduced maintenance overhead. Where frequent updates are not required, the SP9600 and Plaspy combination delivers clear tracking records and consolidated management across distributed assets.
