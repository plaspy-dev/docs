# SkyPatrol - SP9600

![SP9600](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP9600 Series is a portable GPS asset tracker that is perfect for tracking mobile assets over extended periods of time. Whether you need to know the current location or track the progress of valuable items during transportation, this tracker is the ideal solution. It is commonly used for tracking shipping containers, critical medical supplies, heavy equipment, military gear, vehicles, and more.

One of the standout features of the SP9600 Series is its unique design that allows users to choose between replaceable or rechargeable batteries. With replaceable CR123 batteries, which have a total capacity of 6000mAh, this tracker can send one message per week for up to three years. This makes it incredibly reliable and ensures that you can track your assets without worrying about battery life. Additionally, the SP9600 devices can also accommodate rechargeable batteries and can be charged via an external power connector, providing even more flexibility and convenience.

With its durable construction and advanced tracking capabilities, the SkyPatrol SP9600 Series is a top choice for anyone in need of a reliable and long-lasting GPS asset tracker. Whether you're tracking shipping containers, medical supplies, or vehicles, this tracker will provide you with accurate and up-to-date information on the location and progress of your assets.
