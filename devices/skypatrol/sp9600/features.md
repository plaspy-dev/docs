# SkyPatrol - SP9600 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the SkyPatrol SP9600 Series and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It covers practical, non-sensitive information about the tracker, typical operational value, and how Plaspy surfaces location and status for asset monitoring workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and the manufacturer's configuration choices. For device specific technical details, firmware release notes, or installation instructions consult the official SkyPatrol documentation.

## Feature Overview

The SP9600 Series is a portable GPS asset tracker designed for long duration deployments where battery life and reliable location reporting are priorities. It supports flexible power options and is commonly deployed on assets that require extended unattended tracking.

- Portable GPS asset tracking suitable for long shipments and mobile equipment
- Choice of replaceable CR123 batteries or rechargeable batteries with an external power connector
- Very long battery lifetime with replaceable batteries capable of extremely low reporting cadence
- Durable construction intended for transport and field use across asset types
- Designed to provide current location and progress visibility for assets in transit

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP9600

- Portable form factor for easy attachment to containers, equipment, or crates
- Support for replaceable CR123 batteries with a combined capacity described by the manufacturer as 6000mAh
- Option to use rechargeable batteries and charge via an external power connector for repeated deployments
- Low duty cycle reporting designed to conserve battery for multi‑year shipments when using replaceable batteries
- Suited to track a wide range of mobile assets including shipping containers, medical supplies, heavy equipment, military gear, and vehicles
- Durable build and design focused on extended field use and transport conditions
- Provides regular location updates to support location awareness and shipment progress monitoring

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy accepts reports from compatible devices like the SP9600 and makes location and status information available in the Plaspy platform. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and presents device data alongside other fleet and asset telemetry.

- Location updates from the SP9600 are shown on Plaspy maps for current position awareness
- Historical position data and movement history are available in Plaspy to review a shipment or asset route
- Battery and power mode information reported by the device is visible in Plaspy to help plan maintenance or battery replacement cycles
- Device identity and asset association can be maintained in Plaspy to keep track of specific containers or equipment
- Plaspy surfaces device status so operations teams can monitor long duration deployments without frequent manual checks

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking shipping containers and pallets during international or domestic transit
- Monitoring transport of critical medical supplies or temperature sensitive consignment where location visibility is essential
- Asset visibility for heavy equipment moved between sites or rental fleets
- Long duration tracking of military gear or deployed equipment requiring minimal maintenance
- Vehicle or trailer location tracking when a portable tracker is preferred over permanent installation
- Extended unattended shipments where battery life and infrequent reporting are priorities

## Feature Availability Notes

- Battery type and configuration (replaceable CR123 versus rechargeable) affect reporting cadence and deployment duration
- Firmware version can change behavior for reporting intervals, power management, and message formats
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may alter supported features or connectors
- Installation and mounting method can affect GPS reception and reporting reliability
- Consult SkyPatrol documentation for the most current device specifications and firmware guidance

Using the SP9600 with Plaspy gives teams a single place to view location and status for long duration asset tracking. Plaspy's platform aggregates reported positions and device status to help operations teams maintain oversight across many dispersed assets, reduce manual checking, and support logistic workflows.
