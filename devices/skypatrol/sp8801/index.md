# SkyPatrol - SP8801

![SP8801](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP8801 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed to provide quick and accurate location tracking for individuals. It is intended for users who need to monitor children, elderly family members, or disabled loved ones, and it is also suitable for businesses that need to keep tabs on field personnel such as police officers, security guards, sales representatives, and couriers. The device emphasizes discreet placement and straightforward use, allowing it to be carried in a purse, backpack, or pocket while offering real time location visibility and boundary alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP8801 can feed location and alert information into Plaspy to centralize monitoring and oversight. Plaspy can aggregate the tracker data alongside other devices and provide a single interface for live tracking, alert management, and reporting. Compatibility with Plaspy makes the SP8801 a practical option for organizations and caregivers that want to combine a small portable tracker with a full fleet and personnel management platform.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location tracking for continuous visibility of the tracked person
- Customizable emergency alert button that notifies designated contacts
- Boundary or geofence alerts to notify when a device leaves predefined areas
- Discreet and portable form factor suitable for personal carry in pockets or bags
- Accessible location viewing via smartphone or PC interfaces
- Applicable for both personal safety monitoring and business field personnel tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the SP8801 supplies live location updates and alert events to the Plaspy platform so teams and caregivers can monitor activity from a centralized dashboard. Plaspy uses that incoming data to present location on maps, trigger notifications, and build historical views for analysis and reporting.

- View live location and recent movement of SP8801 devices on Plaspy maps
- Configure geofence zones in Plaspy and receive alerts if the tracker crosses a boundary
- Receive and manage emergency alerts from the device within Plaspy for rapid response
- Consolidate multiple SP8801 devices with other assets to monitor people and small fleets together
- Use Plaspy reporting and history views to review past routes and activity for operational insight
