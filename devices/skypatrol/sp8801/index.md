# SkyPatrol - SP8801

![SP8801](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP8801 is a personal GPS tracker designed to provide quick and accurate tracking and location services for individuals. Whether you need to keep an eye on your children, elderly family members, or disabled loved ones, this tracker offers peace of mind by allowing you to easily locate and monitor their movements. It is also a valuable tool for businesses, enabling the tracking of field personnel such as police officers, security guards, sales representatives, and couriers.

One of the standout features of the SP8801 is its real-time tracking capability, which can be accessed using a smartphone or PC. By simply downloading the SkyPatrol App and logging in, you can instantly see the exact location of the tracker and track its movements in real-time. Additionally, you have the option to set up alerts that notify you if the tracker goes beyond predefined boundaries, ensuring that you are immediately informed if there is any deviation from the expected route or location.

The SP8801 is designed to be discreet and easy to use. It can be conveniently placed in a purse, backpack, or pocket, making it ideal for personal use. In case of an emergency or if assistance is needed, the tracker features a customizable alert system that can be activated with the push of a button. This will trigger the sending of a text message or email to your designated contacts, notifying them of the situation and providing them with the necessary information to provide assistance.

With its user-friendly interface, reliable tracking capabilities, and versatile applications, the SkyPatrol SP8801 is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a personal GPS tracker. Whether you are a concerned parent, a caregiver, or a business owner, this device offers the peace of mind and security you need to keep track of your loved ones or employees.

#### Key Features:

- Real-time tracking using a smartphone or PC
- Customizable alert system for emergencies or assistance
- Boundary alerts to notify you if the tracker goes beyond predefined boundaries
- Discreet and portable design for easy placement in a purse, backpack, or pocket
- Perfect for personal use \(children, seniors, disabled\) or business use \(field personnel tracking\)
