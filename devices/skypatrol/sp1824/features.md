# SkyPatrol - SP1824 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the SkyPatrol SP1824 and how it can be used with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, including connectivity, core sensors, and the practical telemetry the tracker can deliver into Plaspy for real time monitoring and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the way the manufacturer implements optional interfaces. For precise device specifications, supported firmware features, and installation guidance consult the manufacturer documentation and your chosen hardware vendor.

## Feature Overview

The SP1824 is an economical vehicle tracker designed to deliver dependable location updates and basic vehicle telemetry into Plaspy while keeping installation simple. It pairs wide area cellular connectivity with internal antennas and offers optional vehicle interface options to enrich fleet visibility.

- Dual network cellular connectivity with 4G LTE Cat 1 and fallback to 2G for broader coverage
- Internal GPS and cellular antennas for streamlined installation with fewer exposed components
- Embedded accelerometer for motion detection and driver behavior event reporting
- Optional OBD II installation to surface vehicle diagnostics and fuel relevant parameters when exposed by the vehicle
- Small form factor and integrated 140 mAh backup battery to continue reporting during brief power loss
- Designed for cost sensitive deployments such as high volume fleet installs and rental vehicles

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP1824

- Plaspy compatible real time tracking support for integration into monitoring and reporting workflows
- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain service in mixed coverage areas
- Internal GPS receiver paired with internal antennas for location fixes and route tracking
- Embedded accelerometer for motion detection and event generation such as harsh braking and movement alerts
- Optional OBD II interface to transmit vehicle diagnostic and engine related telemetry where the vehicle exposes those parameters
- Integrated backup battery for continued reporting during temporary power loss or tampering events
- Compact vehicle focused form factor for hardwired or OBD II installations
- Economical design targeted at fleets and high volume deployments

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the SP1824 is integrated with Plaspy, the device's location, motion events, and optional vehicle telemetry are captured in Plaspy for visualization, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify onboarding and ingest the device feed into the platform.

- Live location updates and historical route replay visible on Plaspy maps and timeline views
- Accelerometer driven events such as movement detection and harsh braking reflected as events for operator review
- Optional OBD II telemetry forwarded to Plaspy to enable engine and fuel relevant reporting where available
- Backup battery and power loss notifications surfaced in Plaspy to maintain visibility during tamper or disconnect incidents
- Device visibility and event feeds available for alerts, geofencing, and scheduled reporting within Plaspy dashboards

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small and medium sized fleets seeking cost effective real time location and utilization data
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows that rely on live location and power loss alerts
- Driver behavior programs using accelerometer events to flag harsh braking and sudden maneuvers
- Fuel monitoring and basic diagnostics when the optional OBD II interface is used and the vehicle exposes relevant parameters
- Rental vehicle tracking and recovery where compact economical hardware is important
- High volume deployments where lower cost per unit and simplified installation reduce total cost of ownership

## Feature Availability Notes

- Optional OBD II telemetry requires the OBD II installation and depends on which parameters the vehicle exposes
- Cellular band support and regional variants may affect network compatibility in different markets
- Firmware and configuration options can change the set of events and telemetry a device reports to Plaspy
- Installation type such as hardwired versus OBD II can influence available telemetry and wiring behavior
- For the most current hardware revision details and supported features consult the manufacturer documentation
