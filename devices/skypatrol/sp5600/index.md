# SkyPatrol - SP5600

![SP5600](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP5600 series is a family of compact GPS tracking devices designed for motorcycles and other powersport vehicles, including ATVs, jet skis, snowmobiles, and small boats. Built with a water resistant form factor, low power consumption, and an internal backup battery, the SP5600 is intended to deliver persistent location tracking and basic tamper awareness in demanding outdoor environments where space and power are limited.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP5600 can feed location and status updates into the Plaspy fleet and asset monitoring platform, enabling centralized visibility across powersport assets. Combining the tracker hardware characteristics with Plaspy's mapping, alerting, and reporting tools gives owners and operators a practical way to monitor vehicle location, respond to potential tampering, and maintain oversight of dispersed equipment.

## Key Highlights

- Compact, discreet form factor suited for installation on motorcycles and small powersport vehicles
- Water resistant design to withstand exposure common in outdoor and marine use
- Low power consumption to reduce impact on vehicle battery when the vehicle is idle
- Internal backup battery to continue reporting if the main power source is interrupted
- Built-in GSM jamming detection to help identify attempts to interfere with connectivity
- User friendly installation and operation aimed at quick deployment on multiple assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SP5600 provides live and historical location information that Plaspy presents alongside other fleet data for operational management. Plaspy ingests the device updates and makes them available through mapping, alerting, and reporting features so teams can monitor assets from a single interface.

- Real time map visibility of each SP5600 tagged vehicle within the Plaspy platform
- Alerts and notifications for events reported by the device such as power loss or tampering indications
- Location history and trip reports to review past movement and support loss or recovery investigations
- Grouping and filtering to manage multiple powersport assets across locations or facilities
- Operational dashboards to track asset status and prioritize follow up actions

## Typical Use Cases

- Theft prevention and recovery for motorcycles and scooters used by individuals or fleets
- Rental fleet monitoring for powersport operators and marinas
- Tracking marine toys and small boats kept in marinas or seasonal storage
- Oversight of off road vehicles used by maintenance teams, resorts, or tour operators
- Asset visibility for organizations that store or transport compact powersport equipment

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SP5600 is well suited to organizations that need a small, robust tracker for powersport assets where space and battery impact matter. Its water resistant build and backup battery make it a practical choice for outdoor and marine environments, while features like jamming detection add a layer of tamper awareness useful for security workflows.

Paired with Plaspy, the SP5600 becomes part of a broader monitoring and management solution. Plaspy helps surface location data, alerts, and activity history from compatible devices so teams can keep an eye on dispersed vehicles, respond to incidents, and generate operational reports without juggling multiple tools.
