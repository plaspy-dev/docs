# SkyPatrol - SP5600 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused summary of the SkyPatrol SP5600 series and how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It is intended to describe practical, non sensitive aspects of the device and show the kinds of visibility and monitoring you can expect when the SP5600 is connected to the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical limits and the latest firmware behavior, consult the manufacturer documentation as the authoritative source.

## Feature Overview

The SkyPatrol SP5600 is a compact, water resistant GPS tracker family designed for motorcycles and other powersport vehicles. Its design focuses on low power consumption and persistent tracking even when vehicle power is removed, with tamper detection features intended to alert owners to interference attempts.

- Compact form factor suited for motorcycles, ATVs, snowmobiles, jet skis, and small boats
- Water resistant construction for use in wet or marine environments
- Low power consumption to minimize impact on vehicle battery when installed
- Internal backup battery to provide continued tracking if main power is disconnected
- GSM jamming detection to warn of potential signal interference or tampering

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP5600

- Designed for powersport vehicle tracking including motorcycles and ATVs
- Water resistant housing for exposure to rain and splashes
- Low current draw to preserve vehicle battery life during long periods of inactivity
- Onboard backup battery for continued operation after mains power loss
- GSM jamming detection that flags interference attempts to the device
- Compact size for discreet installation in tight spaces
- User oriented installation approach for straightforward mounting and wiring

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides a centralized platform for receiving and displaying location and event information from compatible trackers such as the SP5600. When the device reports into Plaspy, its on-device capabilities translate into clearer operational visibility and alerting inside the platform.

- Plaspy displays location updates reported by the tracker on maps for monitoring vehicle positions
- Battery and backup power events from the device can be represented as status changes within Plaspy
- Tamper and GSM jamming detection events reported by the SP5600 appear as alerts or events for operator review
- Low power operation helps maintain regular reporting intervals without excessive battery drain as seen in Plaspy history logs
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols to simplify connecting devices to the platform

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle owners monitoring vehicle location and history for theft prevention and recovery
- Rental and powersport operators tracking ATVs, jet skis, and similar vehicles for asset management
- Marine use on small watercraft where water resistance and compact size are important
- Long term parked vehicle monitoring where low power consumption preserves battery life
- Situations where backup power and tamper alerts increase confidence in continuous tracking
- Small fleets of powersport vehicles requiring simple installation and central monitoring
