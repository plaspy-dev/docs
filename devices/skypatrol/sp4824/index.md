# SkyPatrol - SP4824

![SP4824](./tracker.png)

The SP4824 is a latest-generation GPS tracker engineered for broad carrier and country certifications and designed to integrate seamlessly with Plaspy. Built for vehicle location applications from subprime automotive to commercial fleet deployments and consumer telematics, the SP4824 delivers reliable real-time tracking, robust telemetry, and flexible I/O to support a wide range of monitoring and anti-theft workflows.

The device’s combination of LTE 4G Cat1/2G connectivity, high-sensitivity GNSS, two-way audio and an internal backup battery makes the SP4824 a practical Plaspy compatible choice for fleet management, driver identification, remote diagnostics and secure vehicle recovery. Installers and fleet operators will appreciate the serial port and 1-wire interface for temperature sensors or driver ID keys, while Plaspy handles data ingestion, reporting and alerts for immediate operational value.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for carrier and country certifications, simplifying global rollouts and deployment.
- Real-time tracking over LTE 4G Cat1 with 2G fallback for broader network support and continuous location updates.
- High-sensitivity GNSS receiver for accurate positioning in urban and rural environments.
- Multiple digital and analog I/O plus a serial port enable telemetry integration, ignition status monitoring and custom sensor inputs.
- Built-in two-way audio for driver communication and event verification during anti-theft or recovery operations.
- Internal backup battery ensures data delivery and basic tracking after vehicle power loss, improving anti-theft resilience.
- 1-wire interface supports temperature sensors or driver ID keys, enabling simple driver authentication and cold-chain monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SP4824 streams location and peripheral data into a unified telematics platform. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates, telemetry from analog/digital I/Os and serial-port messages to provide real-time tracking, alerts and historical reports. The device’s backup battery and robust connectivity help ensure that the platform receives critical events even during power interruptions or connectivity transitions.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live map tracking and geofence alerts.
- Digital/analog I/O events reported as ignition, door or alarm status depending on installation wiring.
- Serial port data available for external sensor integration or third‑party device communication.
- 1-wire interface data for temperature sensor or driver ID, enabling driver identification and temperature logging in Plaspy.
- Two-way audio channel available for voice verification during anti-theft events or driver support calls.
