# SkyPatrol - SP7401

<div className="device-logo">
![SP7401](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The SkyPatrol SP7401 is a compact CDMA GPS tracking device built specifically for automobiles. It combines vehicle tracking with access to the vehicle diagnostics interface, making it suitable for standard automotive monitoring needs. The SP7401 is described as easy to install, cost effective, and designed for applications that require consistent location updates and basic diagnostic visibility.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP7401 can feed location and vehicle status information into the Plaspy fleet management platform. That compatibility makes the unit a practical option for organizations that want to centralize vehicle tracking, monitor basic diagnostic data from the OBD II interface, and maintain continuity during temporary power loss thanks to the device backup battery.

## Key Highlights

- CDMA based connectivity for reliable signal reception in supported regions
- Designed for straightforward installation in automobiles
- OBD II compatibility provides access to vehicle diagnostic data
- Real time location tracking for active monitoring and oversight
- Integrated backup battery to maintain operation during power interruptions
- Positioned as an affordable option for automotive tracking needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SP7401 sends position and vehicle status updates to the Plaspy platform so fleet managers and administrators can view and act on that data through Plaspy tools. Integration centers on providing visibility and operational context rather than device level configuration.

- Real time location updates appear in Plaspy for live monitoring and map views
- Vehicle diagnostic information from the OBD II interface can be surfaced in reports and dashboards
- Historical trip data and route playback are available for review and analysis
- Configurable alerts and notifications can be used to flag events relevant to operations and safety
- Fleet level dashboards in Plaspy consolidate SP7401 units alongside other compatible devices for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Automotive insurance telematics where location and basic diagnostics inform risk assessment
- Driver behavior monitoring and fleet safety programs
- Auto rental and shared vehicle fleets requiring tracking and status reporting
- General fleet asset tracking and routine operational oversight
- Vehicle diagnostics and maintenance planning when OBD II data is needed

The SP7401 is a sensible match for organizations that need a vehicle focused tracker that balances functionality and cost.
