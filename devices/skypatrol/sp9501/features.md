# SkyPatrol - SP9501 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the SkyPatrol SP9501 and how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It covers the tracker’s primary, user-facing functions and practical value when the device is deployed and monitored through the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. Refer to manufacturer documentation for device specific details and confirm the capabilities present on any unit before relying on a particular function in production.

## Feature Overview

The SP9501 is a CDMA asset tracker designed for long-term deployments where durable construction, extended battery life, and reliable GPS performance are priorities. Its feature set focuses on continuous location reporting and low-maintenance operation for assets that need long-term visibility.

- CDMA connectivity for wide area asset tracking
- Wireless charging capability to simplify maintenance and improve GPS performance
- Superior GPS performance for accurate location updates
- Multi year battery life for extended deployments and reduced service visits
- Real time tracking and monitoring suitable for operational oversight
- Durable design aimed at long term asset installations

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP9501

- CDMA based asset tracking capability suitable for coverage in supported regions
- Wireless charging support to enable maintenance friendly deployments
- High precision GPS performance for consistent location reporting
- Designed for multi year battery operation to minimize upkeep
- Real time position reporting to support live monitoring and operational decisions
- Rugged construction targeted at long term outdoor or mobile use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes location reports and status updates from compatible trackers such as the SP9501, making them visible on maps, timelines, and reports. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and presents device positions and history alongside other fleet or asset data for a unified view.

- Live position updates appear on Plaspy maps for immediate visibility
- Historical tracks and location history are stored for playback and review
- Asset grouping and labeling in Plaspy helps organize SP9501 devices by site, customer, or deployment
- Long battery life and wireless charging reduce field maintenance events visible in operational logs
- Plaspy’s device integration simplifies onboarding by detecting compatible protocols and linking devices to the platform
