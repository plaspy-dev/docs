# SkyPatrol - SP9501

![SP9501](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP9501 is a CDMA asset tracker built for reliable, long term deployments. It combines a durable enclosure with wireless charging and a focus on consistent GPS performance to provide real time location tracking for vehicles, equipment, and other valuable assets. The unit is described as suitable for scenarios where extended battery life and dependable tracking are important, making it a candidate for deployments that span months or years.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP9501 can feed location and activity information into the Plaspy platform to support centralized monitoring and reporting. Its design for long term use and industries such as title loan services and freight shipping makes it a practical option for organizations that need continuous visibility with minimal maintenance, while Plaspy provides the tools to view, alert, and analyze that data within a single fleet management environment.

## Key Highlights

- Durable design intended for long term asset deployments in demanding environments
- Wireless charging capability to support reliable GPS performance
- Multi year battery life suitable for extended monitoring without frequent service
- Real time tracking and monitoring for ongoing asset visibility
- Cost effective solution for asset management in sectors like title loan services and freight shipping
- Well suited to applications where long unattended operation is a priority

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the SP9501 supplies location and status updates that integrate into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools to improve operational visibility. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and presents it in ways that support day to day fleet oversight, historical review, and exception handling.

- Display live location on Plaspy maps for fleet and asset monitoring
- Configure alerts and notifications for geofence events and movement detection
- Access historical location playback and reporting for route review and audit
- Group and label assets for organized monitoring and operational workflows
- Use aggregated reporting in Plaspy to analyze utilization and asset movement trends

## Typical Use Cases

- Title loan and repossession services requiring persistent asset location visibility
- Freight shipping and cargo monitoring across long haul and storage intervals
- Long term equipment tracking for rental and construction fleets
- Monitoring of trailers, containers, and other assets that need low maintenance tracking
- Any deployment that benefits from extended battery life and reliable GPS reporting
