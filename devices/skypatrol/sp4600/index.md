# SkyPatrol - SP4600

![SP4600](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP4600 Series is a feature-rich GPS tracking device designed to meet the demands of highly demanding applications such as fleet tracking and field dispatch. With its advanced features and functionality, this device is also well-suited for mainstream uses like insurance telematics and vehicle location/recovery. Available in both 2G and 3G versions, the SP4600 Series offers flexibility to match the needs of any enterprise.

One of the standout features of the SP4600 Series is its quad-band GSM/GPRS capability, ensuring reliable and efficient communication. This device is powered by an over-the-air device management and maintenance system, allowing for easy programming, updates, and logistics. With FOTA \(Firmware update over the air\) and GSM jamming detection, the SP4600 Series offers maximum flexibility and capability.

Additionally, the SP4600 Series is equipped with 28 hardware-based geofences, allowing for precise and customizable location tracking. Whether you need to monitor a fleet of vehicles or track the location of valuable assets, this device provides the necessary tools to ensure accurate and reliable tracking.

With its robust features and reliable performance, the SkyPatrol SP4600 Series is the perfect choice for businesses and individuals in need of a high-quality GPS tracking device. Whether you're managing a fleet or looking to enhance the security of your assets, the SP4600 Series delivers the functionality and flexibility you need.
