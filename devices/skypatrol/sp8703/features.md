# SkyPatrol - SP8703 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the SkyPatrol SP8703 and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, user facing functions that matter when deploying the SP8703 in Plaspy compatible monitoring and safety workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a practical summary and consult the device maker for the most current technical specifics.

## Feature Overview

The SP8703 is a compact personal tracker built for low power continuous tracking and safety reporting. It combines cellular and Wi Fi connectivity with an SOS button and an accelerometer to deliver location updates and event alerts suitable for people and small portable assets.

- Dual cellular connectivity and Wi Fi to improve location reliability in urban and indoor settings
- One button SOS panic alert for fast emergency signaling and operator notification
- 3 axis accelerometer for motion and fall related event detection and basic tamper awareness
- Internal rechargeable battery and power saving modes to extend operational life during carry and standby
- Compact, lightweight design for unobtrusive body carry or attachment to small assets

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP8703

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward integration into centralized monitoring
- Built in Wi Fi to assist positioning where GNSS may be limited
- Dual cellular support for 3G and 2G to maximize network reach in varying regions
- SOS panic button for immediate alert generation and rapid response workflows
- 3 axis accelerometer for motion events, fall detection, and movement based reporting
- Internal rechargeable battery combined with low power consumption strategies
- Compact form factor optimized for personal carry and portable asset use
- Designed specifically for personal safety and portable monitoring rather than vehicle telemetry

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the SP8703 is deployed with Plaspy, its status and position updates flow into Plaspy dashboards so administrators and caretakers can maintain visibility, receive alerts, and review history. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify onboarding and monitoring.

- Real time location and historical playback available inside Plaspy for operational visibility
- SOS events forwarded to Plaspy alarm channels with the device last known location for rapid follow up
- Motion and fall events from the accelerometer appear as actionable events and can trigger alert rules
- Wi Fi assisted positioning is consolidated into Plaspy map views to improve indoor or urban accuracy
- Battery level and low power alerts are reported to Plaspy so device uptime can be monitored

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and lone worker protection where a compact SOS enabled tracker is required
- Caregiver monitoring for elderly or vulnerable persons with fall detection and location history
- Portable asset monitoring for briefcases, equipment, or small valuables in transit
- Event staff and temporary workforce tracking for short term deployments with low power needs
- On foot delivery or courier personnel monitoring where vehicle features are not needed
