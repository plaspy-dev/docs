# SkyPatrol - SP8824

![SP8824](./tracker.png)

The SP8824 is a compact, next‑generation personal GPS tracker designed for everyday asset and mobility monitoring. Built with LTE Cat M1 cellular connectivity and an integrated GPS antenna, the SP8824 delivers reliable location updates with exceptionally low power consumption. Its discreet form factor makes it ideal for children, seniors, and professionals who require unobtrusive, continuous monitoring.

Fully Plaspy compatible, the SP8824 integrates into Plaspy's platform to provide easy setup, real‑time tracking, and event alerts. Whether you need simple personal protection, location-based check‑ins, or basic telemetry for mobile assets, the SP8824 gives you the core capabilities you expect from a modern GPS tracker while offering long standby times from its internal battery.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible personal GPS tracker optimized for discreet, everyday use.
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity for efficient cellular uplink and extended battery life.
- Integrated GPS antenna for dependable position fixes and mobility tracking.
- 3‑axis accelerometer provides motion detection and activity-based reporting.
- Long standby time from an internal rechargeable battery—ideal for prolonged deployments.
- Low power consumption designed to maximize intervals between charges.
- Compact, discreet design suitable for children, seniors, and professionals on the move.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SP8824 sends location and motion information over LTE Cat M1 to Plaspy’s platform for real‑time tracking, geofence alerts, and simple telemetry. Plaspy normalizes incoming data so organizations and families can view positions, receive motion alerts, and review recent activity in a single dashboard.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates transmitted via LTE Cat M1.
- Motion detection triggers from the 3‑axis accelerometer to detect movement or inactivity.
- Battery and device status reporting supports proactive notification of low power or disconnection.
- Geofence and alerting through Plaspy for instant notifications on entry/exit events.
- Simple onboarding into Plaspy for monitoring personal assets, caregivers, and on‑the‑go professionals.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SP8824 combines essential GPS tracker functions with the Plaspy platform to deliver dependable, low‑maintenance personal tracking. Its LTE Cat M1 radio and GPS antenna balance power efficiency and connectivity for extended standby between charges, while motion detection enables activity‑based alerts without constant polling. For families, care providers, and organizations that need straightforward real‑time tracking and telemetry, the SP8824 paired with Plaspy offers a compact, reliable solution.

Note on broader telematics features: the SP8824 is tailored for personal and small‑asset tracking. For vehicle fleet management scenarios that require fuel monitoring, ignition sensing, immobilizer control, or integrated Bluetooth sensors, consider dedicated vehicle telematics devices designed for those interfaces. The SP8824 excels where discreet form factor, long battery life, and simple Plaspy‑compatible GPS tracking are the priority.
