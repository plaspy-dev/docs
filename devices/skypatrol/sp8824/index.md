# SkyPatrol - SP8824

![SP8824](./tracker.png)
</div>

The SP8824 is a compact, next generation personal GPS tracker designed for everyday asset and mobility monitoring. With LTE Cat M1 cellular connectivity and an integrated GPS antenna, the SP8824 provides dependable location updates while emphasizing low power consumption and long standby times from its internal rechargeable battery. Its discreet form factor makes it suitable for children, seniors, and professionals who need unobtrusive, continuous monitoring.

As a fully Plaspy compatible device, the SP8824 can be integrated into Plaspy's platform to enable real time tracking, event alerts, and simple onboarding for monitoring personal assets. The combination of efficient cellular uplink, motion detection, and prolonged battery life aligns with common visibility and alerting workflows on Plaspy, making the SP8824 a practical choice for straightforward personal and small asset tracking scenarios.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible personal GPS tracker optimized for discreet everyday use
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity for efficient cellular uplink and extended battery life
- Integrated GPS antenna for reliable position fixes and mobility tracking
- 3 axis accelerometer provides motion detection and activity based reporting
- Internal rechargeable battery with long standby time for prolonged deployments
- Compact, unobtrusive design suitable for children, seniors, and mobile professionals

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SP8824 transmits location and motion information to the platform so users can view positions, receive alerts, and monitor recent activity in a single dashboard. Plaspy normalizes incoming data and presents it alongside other devices to support fleet and personal monitoring workflows.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- Motion detection events forwarded to Plaspy for activity and inactivity alerts
- Battery and device status visible in Plaspy to help manage charge cycles and connectivity
- Geofence creation and entry or exit notifications through Plaspy alerting
- Simple onboarding and grouping of devices for family, care provider, or operational views

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and caregiver location sharing with motion based alerts
- Senior care monitoring for passive mobility and activity oversight
- Field and mobile worker tracking for basic check ins and location visibility
- Small asset protection for luggage, bags, or portable equipment
- Short term or temporary deployments where long standby battery is beneficial

The SP8824 is a focused personal tracker that pairs well with Plaspy when the priority is discreet, low maintenance location monitoring.
