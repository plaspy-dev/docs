# SkyPatrol - TT8850

<div className="device-logo">
![TT8850](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The SkyPatrol TT8850 is a compact live GPS tracking device designed for discreet and covert applications. Its sleek, low profile makes it suited to situations where visibility must be minimized while maintaining continuous location monitoring. The TT8850 combines long run time on a single charge, an IPX5 water resistance rating, an integrated 3D motion sensor, and an emergency SOS button, making it a practical choice for extended, unobtrusive tracking needs.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TT8850 can be integrated into Plaspy's tracking platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its portability, magnetic mounting option, and over the air update capability allow organizations to deploy and maintain units without frequent physical access. For teams using Plaspy for personnel or asset tracking, the TT8850 offers a balance of discretion, endurance, and basic safety alerting that aligns with common monitoring workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for covert and compact deployments where low visibility is important
- Long continuous tracking time of roughly 10 to 12 days from a single charge
- Standby duration up to about 25 days for extended periods of inactivity
- IPX5 water resistance for use in a variety of weather conditions
- Integrated 3D motion sensor to detect movement and support activity awareness
- Built in SOS button to send an immediate alert to designated contacts
- Supports quad band GSM GPRS connectivity and over the air updates for remote maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TT8850 feeds location and activity information into a single monitoring environment, where teams can view live position data and manage alerts. Plaspy can ingest the device signals to support centralized tracking, reporting, and operational decision making while keeping device visibility discreet.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- SOS alerts forwarded through Plaspy to notify designated responders
- Motion based status changes available in Plaspy for activity monitoring
- Historical location traces retained for review and reporting in Plaspy
- Remote device management and updates supported through the TT8850 OTA capabilities and Plaspy operational processes

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert personnel tracking where discreet monitoring is required
- Short term surveillance or investigative assignments with limited access
- Portable asset monitoring where easy attachment and removal is needed
- Safety and lone worker scenarios using the SOS alert feature
- Temporary deployments that benefit from long battery life and OTA updates
