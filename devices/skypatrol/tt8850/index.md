# SkyPatrol - TT8850

![TT8850](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol TT8850 is a Live GPS tracking device designed for compact and covert applications. With its sleek and discreet design, it is perfect for situations that require covert tracking without drawing attention. This tracker is ideal for personnel tracking or any situation where you need to keep an eye on someone without their knowledge.

One of the standout features of the TT8850 is its impressive battery life. With a single charge, this tracker can provide continuous tracking for 10-12 days, making it perfect for long-term surveillance operations. Additionally, it has a standby time of up to 25 days, ensuring that it will last even during extended periods of inactivity.

The TT8850 is also water-resistant with an IPX5 rating, making it suitable for use in various weather conditions. It is equipped with a built-in 3D motion sensor, allowing it to detect movement and provide accurate tracking data. The tracker operates on quad-band GSM/GPRS networks, ensuring reliable and global coverage.

For added convenience and safety, the TT8850 features an integrated SOS button. In case of an emergency, the user can press the button to send an alert to a designated contact. This feature can be crucial in situations where immediate assistance is needed.

The TT8850 is 100% portable and can be easily attached using the optional snap-on magnetic mounting accessory. This allows for quick and discreet installation on any metallic surface. The tracker also supports FOTA \(Firmware Over-The-Air\) and OTA \(Over-The-Air\) capabilities, making it easy to update and maintain the device remotely.

Overall, the SkyPatrol TT8850 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracking device that excels in covert applications. With its long battery life, water resistance, and integrated SOS button, it is the perfect choice for discreet tracking needs.
