# SkyPatrol - SP1603 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the SkyPatrol SP1603 and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for centralized monitoring and electronic supervision workflows. It is intended to help administrators, integrators, and procurement teams understand the tracker’s practical functions when used with Plaspy and what to expect from everyday operations.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation chosen for a given program. For final technical details, configuration steps, and firmware notes, please refer to official SkyPatrol documentation and your device supplier.

## Feature Overview

The SkyPatrol SP1603 is a purpose-built ankle tracker aimed at offender monitoring and home detention programs. It focuses on persistent location reporting, tamper detection, and on-body reliability to support supervision scenarios where continuous situational awareness and auditability are required.

- Continuous real-time GPS tracking for location visibility and time stamped logs.
- Dual SIM cellular connectivity to improve on-air resilience and reduce single carrier outages.
- Dedicated tamper detection and a fiber-optic belt that signals strap compromise.
- Internal rechargeable battery to maintain operation between charges.
- Optional in-home beacon integration to assist with residence presence verification.

## Core Features of SkyPatrol - SP1603

- Real-time GPS location reporting suitable for supervision and incident records.
- Dual SIM cellular support for improved connectivity and uptime.
- Tamper detection mechanisms engineered for ankle-wearable custody monitoring.
- Fiber-optic belt that provides a measurable signal when strap integrity is affected.
- Internal rechargeable battery to support continuous monitoring during use.
- Optional in-home beacon support for validating presence inside an approved residence.
- Form factor and ergonomics designed specifically for ankle use in electronic monitoring programs.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the SP1603 becomes part of a telemetry and alerting workflow that centralizes location and event data for case management and response. Plaspy ingests device telemetry, normalizes common event types, and makes them available for visualization, rules, and reporting.

- Real-time location updates appear on Plaspy maps and are recorded in time stamped history for audit and review.
- Tamper and fiber-optic belt alerts are surfaced as actionable events so supervisors can prioritize response.
- Battery status and power indicators are available in Plaspy to help schedule recharges and maintenance.
- Beacon-assisted presence reports are shown as presence verification events to support home detention workflows.
- Device connectivity and status information help administrators understand cellular resilience and device health across the monitored population.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parole and probation monitoring where continuous location and tamper events are required for compliance.
- Home detention or house arrest programs using beacon integration to confirm residence presence.
- Rapid incident response when strap compromise or an unexpected departure from an approved area is detected.
- Caseworker and supervisor workflows that depend on battery and connectivity telemetry for scheduling check-ins.
- Short term or temporary supervision assignments that require a discreet ankle-wearable tracker.
