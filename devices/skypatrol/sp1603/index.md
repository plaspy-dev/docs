# SkyPatrol - SP1603

![SP1603](./tracker.png)

The SP1603 is a purpose-built GPS tracker for offender monitoring and home detention programs, designed to be Plaspy compatible for fast, secure integration into electronic monitoring workflows. As a multifunctional parolee ankle tracker, the SP1603 delivers accurate real-time tracking, persistent cellular connectivity via dual SIM support, and dedicated tamper detection technologies — all engineered to help probation officers and program administrators maintain safety, compliance, and situational awareness.

The SP1603 pairs on-body reliability with flexible site supervision: an internal rechargeable battery for uninterrupted operation, a fiber-optic belt that clearly signals tampering, and optional in-home beacon integration to verify presence within approved premises. When combined with Plaspy, this GPS tracker becomes part of a complete telemetry and alerting solution for case management and incident response.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: straightforward integration for real-time location and reporting.
- Accurate real-time tracking to support compliance monitoring and rapid response.
- Dual SIM cellular connectivity to reduce service outages and maximize on-air time.
- Tamper detection mechanisms and a fiber-optic belt that provide clear evidence of strap compromise.
- Internal rechargeable battery for continuous operation between charges.
- Optional in-home beacon integration to validate residence presence and supervised activities.
- Designed specifically for offender monitoring and home detention use cases, emphasizing wearability and reliability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SP1603 streams compliant location and status data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, automated alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy receives and normalizes telemetry from the device, then applies configurable rules to trigger notifications to caseworkers, supervisors, or response teams. Integration focuses on actionable events — location breaches, tamper alerts, low battery, and beacon-assisted home presence verification — so teams can prioritize incidents and maintain audit-ready records.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for map visualization and time-stamped logs.
- Tamper and fiber-optic belt alerts: immediate notification when strap integrity is compromised.
- Battery and power-status reporting to ensure monitoring continuity and trigger recharge workflows.
- Optional in-home beacon presence detection to validate that an individual remains inside an approved residence.
- Dual SIM connectivity status visible in Plaspy, improving uptime tracking and carrier-failure diagnostics.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the SP1603 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivers a focused solution for electronic monitoring programs that need reliable location data, tamper detection, and simple field deployment. Dual SIM support increases resilience against cellular outages, while the fiber-optic belt provides clear, machine-detectable evidence of strap compromise — an important anti-tamper feature for custody-grade monitoring. The internal rechargeable battery and ankle-wearable form factor reduce logistics friction for both subjects and supervising officers.

Plaspy’s platform complements the SP1603 by ingesting GPS and telemetry streams and turning them into compliance dashboards, incident alerts, and audit-ready logs. While the SP1603 is tailored to offender monitoring and home detention, Plaspy also supports a broader ecosystem of devices for fleet management, telemetry, fuel monitoring, ignition and immobilizer control, and Bluetooth sensors when those hardware features are required. Together, SP1603 and Plaspy provide a scalable, secure foundation for modern supervision programs, enabling faster response, clearer evidence trails, and lower administrative overhead.
