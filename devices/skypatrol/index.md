# SkyPatrol

SkyPatrol is a recognizable name in GPS tracking hardware, offering a range of devices used for vehicle tracking, asset monitoring, and field operations. Plaspy is compatible with SkyPatrol devices, enabling you to manage location data, alerts, and reporting in a single unified platform. Use the catalog below to view all SkyPatrol models that can be connected to Plaspy.

## How SkyPatrol devices work with Plaspy

SkyPatrol devices collect location, movement, and status data from GPS and onboard sensors, then transmit that data over cellular networks. Plaspy ingests that incoming telemetry, maps devices to your account, and makes location and event data available through dashboards, alerts, and integrations.

Typical setup steps include registering the device in Plaspy, configuring the device to send telemetry to Plaspy with the identifiers and credentials shown in your Plaspy device settings, and tuning reporting parameters to match your operational needs. For step by step device configuration consult the device details displayed in the catalog and the manufacturer documentation.

- Vehicle fleet monitoring to track routes, stop times, and utilization
- Asset tracking for trailers, equipment, and generators

- Recovery and security monitoring for high value assets

Plaspy provides tools to visualize, alert, and report on these use cases, helping teams act on the data SkyPatrol devices provide.
