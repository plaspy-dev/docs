# SkyPatrol - SP8600

![SP8600](./logo.jpg)

The SkyPatrol SP8600 is a portable plug and play GPS tracker series designed for quick deployment and flexible use across vehicles. Units are intended to be powered from a vehicle accessory or charger outlet so they can be moved between vehicles in seconds. The SP8600 series emphasizes ease of use and practical portability while offering global connectivity through quad band GMS modem technology and motion detection via a 3-axis accelerometer and G-Force sensor.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SP8600 is well suited to organizations and individuals who need fast, low-friction tracking that integrates with fleet management workflows. When paired with Plaspy the SP8600 provides location visibility, trip data, and motion event reporting that can be incorporated into monitoring, alerts, and simple reporting without requiring permanent installation.

## Key Highlights

- Portable plug and play design for rapid transfer between vehicles
- Powered from a vehicle accessory or charger outlet for minimal installation time
- Quad band GMS modem technology for wide area coverage and international use
- 3-axis accelerometer and G-Force sensor enabling movement and impact detection
- Cost effective option for short term or temporary tracking needs
- Useful for capturing trip data and validating vehicle usage across multiple drivers

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SP8600 feeds location and motion data into Plaspy so teams can monitor vehicle positions, review trips, and receive event notifications. Plaspy collects the device data and presents it on maps, timelines, and reports for operational oversight.

- Real time and historical location visibility on Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Motion and impact events from the accelerometer and G-Force sensor shown as alerts or timeline markers
- Trip summaries and mileage records available for reimbursement or usage verification
- Fleet monitoring and device status helps managers track temporary assignments and rentals
- Configurable notifications in Plaspy for movement, arrivals, or unusual activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle rental companies tracking temporary assignments and ensuring responsible use
- Parents monitoring teenage drivers to review trip behavior and encourage safe driving
- Businesses recording mileage for reimbursement and validating fuel card usage
- Field teams and contractors using portable units for occasional or seasonal vehicles
- Organizations needing rapid redeployment of trackers between vehicles

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SP8600 is a practical choice when you need an affordable, easy to move tracker that integrates with a fleet platform like Plaspy. Its plug and play nature reduces deployment friction, making it suitable for rental fleets, temporary vehicle monitoring, and situations where devices must be frequently relocated. The built in motion sensors provide event context that Plaspy can use for alerts and trip analysis without requiring complex hardware changes.

Paired with Plaspy, the SP8600 can deliver fast operational visibility and straightforward reporting, helping teams manage vehicle usage and respond to incidents more effectively. For organizations that prioritize portability and quick setup, this combination offers a low overhead path to fleet tracking and trip data collection.
