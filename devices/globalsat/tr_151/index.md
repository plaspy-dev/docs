# GlobalSat - TR-151

![TR-151](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-151 is a reliable and robust GPS/GSM/GPRS tracker designed for tracking vehicles and assets. With its durable and water-resistant construction, this tracker can withstand harsh environments and ensure accurate tracking in any condition. Whether you need to monitor your fleet of vehicles or keep an eye on valuable assets, the TR-151 is the perfect solution.

One of the standout features of the TR-151 is its ability to send SMS messages with its precise location \(longitude and latitude\) to your cell phone via the GSM network or to your personal computer through the GPRS network. This allows you to easily track the location of your vehicles or assets in real-time. Additionally, the TR-151 is equipped with a high-capacity Li-ion battery, ensuring long operation times and minimizing the need for frequent recharging.

Installation and concealment of the TR-151 is a breeze, thanks to its compact size and user-friendly design. You can easily install it in your vehicle or hide it discreetly to perform covert tracking. The tracker also features an SOS button for emergency situations, providing an added layer of security and peace of mind.

Overall, the GlobalSat TR-151 is an ideal solution for vehicle tracking and equipment/assets monitoring. Its durability, water resistance, and reliable tracking capabilities make it a top choice for individuals and businesses alike.

#### Key Features:

- Durable and water-resistant construction
- GPS/GSM/GPRS technology for accurate tracking
- SMS notifications with precise location
- Long operation time with high-capacity Li-ion battery
- Easy installation and concealment
- SOS button for emergency situations
