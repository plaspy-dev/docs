# GlobalSat - TR-151

![TR-151](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-151 is a compact, rugged GPS/GSM/GPRS tracker built for vehicle and asset tracking. Designed with durable and water resistant construction, the TR-151 is intended to maintain reliable location reporting in challenging conditions. It provides location messages via GSM and data connectivity via GPRS, and is equipped with a high capacity Li ion battery plus an SOS button for emergency signaling.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TR-151 can feed location updates and alerts into Plaspy's fleet management platform to improve visibility and operational oversight. Its compact size and support for SMS and GPRS reporting make it a practical option for organizations that need straightforward location reporting and the ability to monitor assets or vehicles within Plaspy alongside other fleet devices.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged, water resistant housing suitable for outdoor and vehicle use
- GPS location reporting combined with GSM and GPRS communication options
- Sends SMS location messages for simple phone based tracking
- High capacity Li ion battery for extended operation between charges
- Compact form factor that supports discreet placement and concealment
- SOS button for emergency notifications

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TR-151's location and alert messages become part of a unified monitoring environment. Plaspy ingests incoming positional information and makes it available through dashboards, reporting, and alerting workflows so teams can act on location data in near real time.

- Display live and historical positions on Plaspy maps for vehicle and asset visibility
- Create alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on received location or SOS events
- Use location feeds for fleet monitoring, route oversight, and operational reporting
- Combine TR-151 device data with other fleet telematics for consolidated views
- Export or schedule reports in Plaspy that include TR-151 location history and activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for delivery, service, or field teams
- Monitoring valuable movable assets across sites or job locations
- Covert or discreet tracking when compact size and concealment are required
- Emergency alerting and response using the built in SOS function
- Temporary tracking deployments where SMS or GPRS reporting is preferred
