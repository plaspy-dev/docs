# GlobalSat - LT-10

![LT-10](./logo..jpg)

The GlobalSat LT-10 is a solar-powered ear tag GPS tracker specifically designed for cattle. This innovative device utilizes LoRaWAN technology to provide accurate and reliable tracking capabilities for range animals. With its high-performance solar cell, the LT-10 can operate for many years of regular use, making it a cost-effective solution for livestock management. Please note that the optimized report interval may vary depending on the sunlight conditions of the region and season.

The LT-10 is built to withstand the rugged conditions of the field. Its compact and durable design ensures that it can endure the demands of tracking and managing animals over long distances. The tracker features a high sensitivity GNSS for precise location tracking, as well as a built-in 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection. Additionally, it is equipped with a red LED indicator and a Hall switch for added functionality.

With the LT-10, you can easily configure the device using the iOS and Android app, allowing for seamless customization and control. The tracker offers periodic reporting and low battery alerts, ensuring that you stay informed about the status of your animals. Furthermore, the LT-10 is IPX7 water-resistant, providing protection against water immersion up to 1 meter for 30 minutes.

Dimensions: 60\*50\*14mm  

Weight: 35g

### Key Features:

- Supported LoRa® frequencies: US 915 MHz / AS 923 MHz / EU 868 MHz
- High performance solar cell
- High sensitivity GNSS
- Configurable by iOS and Android APP
- Built-in 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection
- 1 Red LED indicator
- 1 Hall switch
- Periodic report
- Low battery alert
- IPX7 water-resistant
