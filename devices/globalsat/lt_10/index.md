# GlobalSat - LT-10

![LT-10](./logo..jpg)

The GlobalSat LT-10 is a solar powered ear tag GPS tracker designed specifically for cattle and range livestock. It pairs a high performance solar cell with a high sensitivity GNSS receiver and LoRaWAN connectivity to deliver long life location tracking in remote pasture environments. Built for field conditions, the LT-10 is compact and rugged with IPX7 water resistance, motion detection, visual indication, and easy configuration via iOS and Android apps.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LT-10 can feed livestock location and motion data into Plaspy for operational visibility and reporting. Its solar endurance and periodic reporting model make it a practical choice for long term animal monitoring, while features such as low battery alerts and motion events help Plaspy users maintain oversight without frequent manual checks. Report intervals may vary with sunlight conditions, so Plaspy dashboards and alerts can be tuned accordingly.

## Key Highlights

- Solar powered ear tag form factor optimized for cattle and range animals
- LoRaWAN based connectivity for long range coverage in rural areas
- High sensitivity GNSS for improved location accuracy in the field
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer for motion detection and activity reporting
- IPX7 rated water resistance for protection against immersion during field use
- Configurable by iOS and Android apps with periodic reporting and low battery alerts
- Compact and lightweight design suitable for attachment to animals

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy can ingest the LT-10 data stream and present it alongside other fleet and asset information, enabling consolidated monitoring of livestock movements and device status. Integration focuses on location visibility, activity reporting, and alerting so you can manage animals and devices from the Plaspy platform.

- Map based tracking of animal locations and movement history in Plaspy
- Motion event and activity indicators derived from the LT-10 accelerometer
- Periodic report and low battery notifications routed into Plaspy alerting workflows
- Aggregated device status and uptime views to support device maintenance planning
- Historical reports and exportable location logs to aid operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring grazing patterns and general location of cattle in large pastures
- Long term asset tracking where solar power reduces maintenance visits
- Detecting movement or prolonged inactivity using motion events for operational awareness
- Managing device inventory and battery health for large herds through central dashboards
- Remote monitoring in seasonal or off grid grazing areas where long range connectivity is needed

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LT-10 pairs endurance focused hardware with a compact ear tag design that suits livestock operations where minimizing maintenance is a priority. Its solar charging capability and rugged construction reduce the need for frequent battery changes and help ensure continuous data collection over extended deployments. When combined with Plaspy, the LT-10 becomes part of a unified monitoring and reporting workflow that supports operational decision making for pasture and herd management.

Choosing the LT-10 for use with Plaspy makes sense for organizations that need durable, low maintenance trackers for distributed livestock, and who want to consolidate animal location and activity data into a single management platform. The device characteristics and Plaspy features together support visibility, alerting, and historical analysis without excessive operational overhead.
