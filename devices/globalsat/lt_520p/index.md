# GlobalSat - LT-520P

![LT-520P](./tracker.jpg)

The LT-520P is an ultra rugged, battery powered GPS tracker designed for long term livestock monitoring. Built for wide pasture deployments, it combines GNSS positioning with low power LoRaWAN uplinks and integrated Bluetooth Low Energy for local configuration and diagnostics. The device emphasizes extended field life and continuous activity analysis so operators can monitor location and herd behavior with minimal site visits.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LT-520P delivers periodic compact telemetry and behavior events to Plaspy for mapping, alerts, and herd analytics. Its design aligns with farm management workflows that prioritize long battery life and low maintenance, while BLE support enables local configuration and updates that complement Plaspy backend management. This makes the LT-520P a practical option for livestock operations that want telemetry integrated into Plaspy dashboards and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra rugged enclosure and battery powered design for multi year pasture deployments.
- GNSS positioning that uses GPS and GLONASS for outdoor location tracking.
- Low power LoRaWAN uplinks to transmit periodic GPS and behavior telemetry to Plaspy.
- Integrated BLE for field configuration, diagnostics, and on site device updates.
- On device activity analysis focused on heat detection and herd level alerts.
- Long deployment life with multi year battery expectations under typical reporting profiles.
- Configurable reporting cadence to balance update frequency and battery longevity.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LT-520P works with Plaspy by sending compact LoRaWAN uplinks containing GNSS positions and activity summaries while using BLE for local device management. Plaspy ingests the periodic messages to present locations, event alerts, and historical trends on maps and dashboards, letting you choose reporting profiles that match operational priorities.

- Location visibility on Plaspy maps from GNSS position uplinks.
- Activity and heat detection alerts delivered to Plaspy for monitoring and workflows.
- Configurable reporting frequency to trade off near real time updates and extended battery life.
- Historical activity trends and herd level analytics displayed in Plaspy dashboards.
- Local BLE access for technicians to perform configuration and diagnostics that complement Plaspy management.

## Typical Use Cases

- Herd level heat detection and automated alerts to farm managers.
- Long term GNSS tracking of animals grazing across large pasture areas.
- Continuous activity monitoring to identify changes in behavior or potential health issues.
- Field configuration and maintenance using BLE for technicians on site.
- Low maintenance deployments where extended battery life reduces servicing frequency.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LT-520P is suited to operations that need resilient, low maintenance telemetry and livestock specific analytics integrated into Plaspy. Its combination of GNSS positioning, efficient LoRaWAN uplinks, and BLE based local management provides a practical balance between visibility and battery life, helping reduce field visits while delivering the mapping and alerting needed for herd management.

If your priorities are pasture based telemetry, heat detection, and long term deployments, the LT-520P is a strong match for Plaspy driven workflows. For automotive or fleet telematics needs such as ignition or fuel monitoring, consider Plaspy integrations with devices tailored for those applications instead.
