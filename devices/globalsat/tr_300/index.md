# GlobalSat - TR-300

![TR-300](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-300 is a reliable and versatile 3G network personal tracker designed with the safety of children and elders in mind. This compact and lightweight device is incredibly easy to use, making it ideal for individuals of all ages. With its built-in microphone and speaker, the TR-300 offers convenient hands-free two-way communication, allowing the owner to stay connected at all times. 

One of the standout features of the TR-300 is its high-capacity battery, which eliminates the need for frequent charging. This ensures that the device remains powered for extended periods, providing peace of mind to both the user and their loved ones. In case of emergencies, the TR-300 is equipped with an SOS button that allows the owner to quickly call pre-set phone numbers and send an SOS alarm with just a single push.

The TR-300 also offers advanced tracking capabilities, allowing a monitoring center to remotely configure and track the device owner in real-time or at specified intervals. This can be done by sending GPRS/SMS commands directly to the device. Additionally, the TR-300 supports geo-fence configuration, enabling the creation of permissible or restricted areas. This feature provides more flexibility in monitoring the device owner and ensures their safety within designated boundaries.

With its user-friendly design, reliable performance, and a range of advanced features, the GlobalSat TR-300 is an excellent choice for anyone looking to enhance the safety and security of their loved ones. Whether it's keeping an eye on children or monitoring the well-being of elderly family members, this personal tracker offers peace of mind and reliable communication in any situation.

### Key Features:

- 3G network compatibility for reliable and fast communication
- Compact and lightweight design for easy portability
- Built-in microphone and speaker for hands-free two-way communication
- High-capacity battery for extended usage without frequent charging
- SOS button for quick access to emergency contacts and sending SOS alarms
- Remote configuration and real-time tracking via GPRS/SMS commands
- Geo-fence configuration for creating permissible or restricted areas
