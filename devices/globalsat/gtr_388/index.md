# GlobalSat - GTR-388

![GTR-388](./logo..jpg)

The GlobalSat GTR-388C1 is a compact GPS tracker designed for eBikes, motorcycles, scooters, and a wide range of vehicles. It combines a rugged waterproof enclosure with cellular connectivity and a high sensitivity GPS receiver to provide continuous location monitoring and motion detection in demanding environments. The unit's small footprint and durable design make it suitable for outdoor use and for vehicles that require a discreet tracking solution.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GTR-388 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized fleet monitoring and reporting. Its support for common communication methods and I O options means it can be integrated into Plaspy workflows to improve visibility, enable alerts based on motion or position, and support operational oversight for individual vehicles or mixed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and robust IPX7 rated construction suited for outdoor vehicle use
- 4G LTE connectivity with fallback to earlier cellular generations for broader coverage
- High sensitivity GPS receiver for improved location accuracy
- Built in motion sensor to detect movement and support security alerts
- Multiple communication methods and I O options for flexible integration
- Over the air firmware update capability to streamline device maintenance
- Designed for small vehicles such as eBikes motorcycles and scooters as well as larger vehicles

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GTR-388 provides continuous position and event data that Plaspy ingests and displays through its fleet monitoring tools. Plaspy can use the tracker's messages and inputs to increase situational awareness, create alerts, and produce historical movement reports for operational analysis.

- Real time location visibility for each tracked vehicle or asset within Plaspy
- Motion based alerts and status updates to flag unexpected movement or stops
- Geofencing and boundary notifications using position updates from the tracker
- Historical route playback and reporting for trip analysis and operational review
- Use of the device I O events to trigger custom alerts and integration workflows
- Leverage the device communication methods to ensure reliable delivery of tracking data to Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- eBike and electric scooter fleet monitoring for sharing or rental operations
- Motorcycle security and recovery tracking for individual owners or fleets
- Mixed vehicle fleet tracking where compact unobtrusive devices are preferred
- Last mile and urban delivery fleets needing continuous position updates
- Equipment or asset tracking in outdoor or weather exposed environments

The GTR-388 is a practical choice for organizations that need a small rugged tracker capable of reliable location reporting across different vehicle types. Its combination of durable waterproof design, strong GPS sensitivity, and flexible communication and I O options makes it useful for both security oriented deployments and routine fleet operations.

Paired with Plaspy, the GTR-388 helps consolidate tracking data into a single platform for visibility and operational control.
