# GlobalSat - TR-203

![TR-203](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-203 is a personal tracking device that combines fast acquisition time with a compact and stylish design. Equipped with GPS/GSM/GPRS wireless network capabilities, this tracker allows for seamless and reliable tracking wherever you go. 

One of the standout features of the TR-203 is its built-in data logger function, which enables you to record up to 150,000 positions data. This means that you can track your movements and review your route history at any time. Whether you're on a hiking adventure or simply want to keep track of your daily commute, the TR-203 has got you covered.

Configuring the TR-203 is a breeze, thanks to its user-friendly interface. You can easily set it up via USB, SMS, or GPRS, and then display the location using specific map software. This makes it incredibly convenient to use, even for those who are not tech-savvy.

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the TR-203 also offers voice monitoring and Geo-fence functions. With voice monitoring, you can remotely listen in on the tracker's surroundings, providing an extra layer of security and peace of mind. The Geo-fence function allows you to set up virtual boundaries, and you will receive an alert if the tracker goes outside of these boundaries. This is particularly useful for keeping track of loved ones or valuable assets.

Overall, the GlobalSat TR-203 is a reliable and versatile personal tracking device that offers a range of features to suit your needs. Whether you're using it for personal or professional purposes, this tracker is sure to exceed your expectations.
