# GlobalSat - TR-203

<div className="device-logo">
![TR-203](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GlobalSat TR-203 is a compact personal tracking device designed for reliable location monitoring and route history capture. It pairs quick position acquisition with a small form factor and supports GPS location reporting together with GSM and GPRS wireless connectivity. A notable capability is the built in data logger that can store up to 150,000 position records, allowing users to review past routes and movements without continuous live connectivity.

As a device listed as compatible with Plaspy, the TR-203 can feed position updates and event alerts into the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility. Its options for configuration via USB, SMS, or GPRS and features such as voice monitoring and Geo fence alerts make it a practical choice for organizations that want a portable tracker that integrates with Plaspy for fleet or personnel oversight, historical playback, and alerting workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and stylish design suited for personal or portable use
- Fast position acquisition for timely location updates
- Built in data logger with capacity for up to 150,000 position records
- Configurable via USB SMS or GPRS for flexible deployment and setup
- Voice monitoring capability to support remote audio awareness where permitted
- Geo fence function that triggers alerts when boundaries are crossed
- Works over standard cellular networks for broad coverage

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy can ingest location reports and event messages from the TR-203 so you can manage tracking, alerts, and historical data in one platform. When connected to Plaspy, the TR-203 becomes a source of continuous location information and recorded route history that can be used for operational monitoring and reporting.

- Live and periodic location updates appear on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Stored data logger records can be uploaded or synchronized for route playback and analysis
- Geo fence events from the device generate notifications within Plaspy for boundary alerts
- Alerts and status messages can be routed into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools
- Voice monitoring events provide an extra layer of situational awareness alongside location tracking

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and lone worker location monitoring
- Tracking of field staff movements and route verification
- Outdoor activities and recreational route logging
- Security monitoring for valuables or portable equipment
- Route history review for compliance or operational analysis

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TR-203 is a practical option when you need a small, easy to carry tracker that still offers robust position logging and basic monitoring features. Its large onboard data store is particularly useful in scenarios with intermittent connectivity, since recorded positions can be reviewed later and integrated into Plaspy for analysis. The multiple configuration paths make initial setup and adjustments straightforward for teams that manage mixed deployments.

When paired with Plaspy, the TR-203 contributes to a clearer operational picture by supplying location, Geo fence alerts, and historical traces to a unified platform. This combination benefits organizations seeking simple, reliable personal tracking with centralized reporting and alerting.
