# GlobalSat - LW-360HR Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GlobalSat LW-360HR wearable GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing functions such as long range LoRaWAN connectivity, GPS location, health and activity telemetry, and how those data points appear in Plaspy for monitoring and alerts.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation or provisioning choices. For device specific configuration, firmware details, and the latest technical specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from GlobalSat.

## Feature Overview

The LW-360HR is a compact GPS tracker watch designed for low power wide area deployments where continuous cellular coverage is not required. It combines long range LoRaWAN communication, GPS positioning, and local BLE connectivity with health and activity telemetry to support remote monitoring and group safety scenarios.

- Long range LoRaWAN connectivity for wide area reporting without continuous cellular service
- GPS positioning for outdoor location reporting and geofence triggered notifications
- Continuous health and activity telemetry including optical heart rate, skin temperature, steps and calories
- BLE 4.0 for local mobile pairing, provisioning, and firmware OTA updates
- Dedicated SOS and geofence alerting for rapid notification and escalation
- Practical wearable design with multi day battery life and 5 ATM water resistance

## Core Features of GlobalSat - LW-360HR

- LoRaWAN Class A connectivity with Helium network friendly operation for wide area coverage
- Integrated GPS for outdoor position fixes and route history
- BLE 4.0 for smartphone pairing, local provisioning and over the air firmware upgrades
- Optical heart rate monitoring with stated accuracy ranges for rest and motion
- Skin temperature sensor and pedometer derived metrics such as steps and calories
- SOS button and geofence alert support for rapid incident notification
- Compact wearable design with 0.96 inch round display and water resistant enclosure
- Rechargeable battery designed for multi day typical use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and telemetry data from compatible trackers to provide map based visibility, event notifications, and historical reporting. When an LW-360HR is deployed as a Plaspy compatible device, its GPS and sensor data are routed into Plaspy for operational monitoring and alerting.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks displayed on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- Health and activity telemetry such as heart rate and skin temperature surfaced in monitoring dashboards
- SOS button presses and geofence breaches generate alert notifications inside Plaspy for escalation
- BLE assisted provisioning and OTA simplify device setup and firmware maintenance ahead of cloud reporting
- Long range LoRaWAN reporting enables coverage in areas with limited cellular reception while Plaspy consolidates data streams for unified oversight
