# GlobalSat - LT-501H

<div className="device-logo">
![LT-501H](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The LT-501H is a compact LoRa GPS tracker from GlobalSat designed for long life and wide area personal and small asset tracking. The device family combines GPS reception with onboard Bluetooth beacon capabilities, motion detection and configurable reporting modes to deliver location and basic telemetry for both indoor and outdoor scenarios. Its small form factor, rechargeable internal power and built in notification features make it suitable for wearable or attached use where low power and long range are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LT-501H can forward periodic positions, beacon detections and status uplinks into Plaspy via LoRaWAN. That compatibility makes it a practical choice for organizations that want to centralize location visibility, alerting and historical reports in Plaspy while relying on low power wide area connectivity for extended field use.

## Key Highlights

- Out of the box LoRaWAN compatibility for easy ingestion into cloud platforms such as Plaspy
- Combined GPS and BLE beacon support to enable indoor/outdoor hybrid location workflows
- Designed for long life operation with configurable reporting modes to reduce power consumption
- Compact and rugged form factor suited to wearable and small asset deployments
- Built in audible alerts and motion detection to support help reporting and activity monitoring
- Variants and accessory options to accommodate regional bands and external antenna needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the LT-501H delivers location and status uplinks over LoRaWAN which Plaspy ingests, decodes and presents in a unified workspace for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can use those feeds to build live maps, notify teams on defined events and retain historical tracks for analysis. Device reporting behaviour can be managed through configuration that selects periodic updates or more frequent messages when network and device settings allow.

- Real time and historical location display on Plaspy maps for fleet and asset visibility
- BLE beacon detections consumed by Plaspy to improve indoor proximity and hybrid tracking
- Motion and help events routed into Plaspy alert workflows for incident response
- Geofence evaluation in Plaspy to trigger entry and exit notifications based on location
- Device health and power status updates available in Plaspy for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Lone worker safety monitoring with help button and audible notification integration
- Personal security and anti theft tracking for luggage, equipment or portable assets
- Wide area asset monitoring where LoRaWAN coverage and long battery life are priorities
- Indoor and outdoor hybrid tracking using BLE beacons indoors and GNSS outdoors
- Fleet and operational telemetry dashboards that combine location and basic status feeds

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LT-501H provides a practical combination of long range LoRaWAN connectivity, beacon assisted indoor positioning and compact rugged hardware that integrates cleanly into Plaspy workflows. For teams that need reliable, low power trackers for people and small assets, the LT-501H supplies the basic location and event feeds Plaspy needs to deliver mapping, alerts and historical reports without adding unnecessary complexity.
