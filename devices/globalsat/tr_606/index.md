# GlobalSat - TR-606

![TR-606](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-606 is a versatile and cost-effective GPS tracker designed for a wide range of AVL \(Automatic Vehicle Location\) applications. It combines a high-sensitivity GPS chipset, a dual-band UMTS/HSDPA, and a quad-band GSM communication module, all integrated into a compact and lightweight device.

The TR-606 is housed in a sturdy enclosure, making installation quick and hassle-free. Once installed, it provides real-time GPS location and accurate vehicle status information to the server, allowing users to track their vehicles with ease. Additionally, the TR-606 offers remote vehicle control capabilities, enabling users to command their vehicles with a simple click on their computer.

With its advanced features, the TR-606 is an ideal solution for fleet management, enhancing vehicle safety, emergency response, and merchandise/package transportation. Its extensive applications make it a valuable tool for businesses looking to optimize their operations and improve efficiency.

Key Features of the GlobalSat TR-606:

- High-sensitivity GPS chipset for accurate and reliable tracking
- Dual-band UMTS/HSDPA and quad-band GSM communication module for seamless communication
- Compact and lightweight design for easy installation
- Real-time GPS location and vehicle status updates
- Remote vehicle control capabilities
- Ideal for fleet management, vehicle safety, emergency response, and merchandise/package transportation
