# GlobalSat - TR-350

![TR-350](./tracker.jpg)

The TR-350 is a compact, Plaspy compatible 4G LTE personal GPS tracker engineered for reliable safety monitoring of seniors, children and lone workers. With high-sensitivity GPS/GNSS, Wi‑Fi positioning and Bluetooth LE beacon support for improved indoor accuracy, the TR-350 delivers real-time tracking and immediate emergency communications in a small, wearable form factor.

The device is built specifically for mobile personal emergency response systems \(MPERS\) and pairs directly with Plaspy to provide actionable telemetry: SOS button events, motion and fall advisories, battery and connectivity status, and location updates. Two-way voice, hands-free operation, vibration/ringtone alarms and IPX6 water resistance make the TR-350 a practical Plaspy-compatible option for personal safety programs and monitoring services.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for streamlined integration into real-time tracking dashboards and alerting workflows.
- High-sensitivity GPS/GNSS plus Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth LE beacon scanning for improved outdoor and indoor positioning.
- One-button SOS that calls pre-set emergency numbers — fast, simple help when it’s needed most.
- Two-way voice with built-in microphone and speaker for direct caregiver or response-center communication.
- Automatic motion reporting and fall advisory functions to detect incidents and trigger alerts.
- Long battery life with increased capacity and advanced power management for extended operation between charges.
- IPX6 water resistance and alarm notifications via vibration or ringtones for durable, wearable use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TR-350 uploads location and status data over 4G LTE to enable continuous monitoring and rapid incident response. Plaspy ingests the device’s GNSS fixes alongside Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth beacon data to improve indoor positioning, and converts SOS, motion and fall events into notifications and rules-based alerts. Integration is focused on delivering real-time tracking, dependable telemetry and clear event context to operators and caregivers.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS coordinates, Wi‑Fi/BLE-assisted indoor position\).
- SOS / one-button emergency call events routed into Plaspy alerting and incident workflows.
- Motion reporting and fall advisory notifications for automated incident detection.
- Battery level and connectivity status reporting to manage device health and uptime.
- Bluetooth LE beacon scanning to supplement indoor positioning and proximity-based triggers.

- Senior care: continuous monitoring, SOS calls and fall advisories for assisted living and independent seniors.
- Child safety: location tracking, two-way voice and one-button emergency calls for quick parental response.
- Lone-worker protection: motion reporting and emergency alerting for remote or at-risk workers.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the TR-350 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker gives service providers and caregivers a focused combination of reliable real-time tracking, indoor positioning enhancements and direct emergency communications. The device’s high-sensitivity GNSS, Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth LE support means Plaspy receives richer location context for faster, more accurate responses. Meanwhile, built-in two-way voice and one-button SOS simplify emergency workflows and improve outcomes for vulnerable users.

For organizations that already use Plaspy for telemetry, the TR-350 fits into monitoring programs without adding unnecessary complexity. It is designed for personal safety rather than vehicle telemetry — features like ignition, immobilizer or fuel monitoring are not applicable to this device — but Plaspy’s platform can still consolidate TR-350 telemetry with broader datasets for unified reporting and alerting. The result is a compact, durable and easy-to-deploy Plaspy compatible GPS tracker well suited to personal care, lone-worker protection and on-demand safety services.
