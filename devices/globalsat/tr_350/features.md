# GlobalSat - TR-350 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GlobalSat TR-350 as used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant to monitoring, alerting, and location visibility inside Plaspy, drawing on manufacturer descriptions and practical functional context for service providers and caregivers.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer's implementation choices. Before deployment, confirm current firmware behavior, supported functions and provisioning steps with the manufacturer and your device supplier.

## Feature Overview

The TR-350 is a compact 4G LTE personal tracker designed for mobile personal emergency response systems and wearable safety use. Its mix of high-sensitivity GNSS, Wi‑Fi positioning and Bluetooth LE beacon support is intended to provide improved outdoor and indoor location context, while integrated SOS and two-way voice enable direct emergency communications.

- Real-time GNSS location reporting with Wi‑Fi assisted and Bluetooth LE beacon support to improve positioning in mixed environments.
- One-button SOS for rapid emergency signalling and preconfigured call routing to caregivers or response centers.
- Two-way voice with built-in microphone and speaker for direct conversations between wearer and responder.
- Motion reporting and fall advisory alerts to help detect incidents and trigger timely notifications.
- Battery and connectivity status telemetry to monitor device health and uptime for long term deployments.

## Core Features of GlobalSat - TR-350

- Compact wearable form factor built for personal safety and MPERS deployments.
- High-sensitivity GPS GNSS for reliable outdoor positioning.
- Wi‑Fi based positioning to supplement GNSS where satellite signals are weaker.
- Bluetooth Low Energy beacon scanning to enhance indoor location accuracy and proximity awareness.
- One-button SOS with emergency call capability and event transmission.
- Two-way voice with integrated microphone and speaker for hands-free communications.
- Motion reporting and fall advisory functions to support automated incident detection.
- IPX6 water resistance and vibration or ringtone alarms for durable daily use.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location and status telemetry from compatible devices and presents that information in dashboards, alerts and reporting workflows. When a TR-350 is paired with Plaspy, telemetry such as location fixes, SOS events and device health are mapped into Plaspy event streams so operators and caregivers can act on timely context.

- Location fixes and assisted positioning data appear in Plaspy maps and history views to support real-time tracking and playback.
- SOS button events are converted to alert messages and can be routed into notification and incident workflows.
- Motion and fall advisories become monitorable events, enabling rule based responses and operator review.
- Battery level and connectivity indicators show device health to help maintain uptime and schedule maintenance.
- Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth LE assisted data improve indoor positioning context shown alongside GNSS coordinates.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and brings TR-350 telemetry into the platform for unified visibility; devices report into Plaspy endpoints such as d.plaspy.com as part of normal provisioning.
