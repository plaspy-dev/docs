# GlobalSat - TR-151SP

<div className="device-logo">
![TR-151SP](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GlobalSat TR-151SP is a GPS/GSM/GPRS tracker designed for extended operation and reliable position reporting. With a high capacity 15A battery and a standby time of up to one month, the device is aimed at scenarios where long unattended runtime is important. The tracker sends location information via SMS and data channels and includes a simple reset control and easy mounting for attachment to equipment, assets, or cargo.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TR-151SP can be used to feed location updates into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its long battery life and straightforward reporting behavior make it a practical choice for teams that need intermittent or continuous tracking without frequent maintenance. Plaspy can incorporate those reports into live maps, alerts, and reports so organizations can monitor assets tracked by the TR-151SP.

## Key Highlights

- High capacity 15A battery that supports extended standby and operation
- Standby time advertised up to one month for reduced charging frequency
- GPS location reporting paired with GSM GPRS connectivity for remote updates
- Sends SMS location messages that can be used by monitoring platforms
- Quick and simple installation for attachment to equipment and assets
- Reset button for straightforward device operation and recovery
- Well suited for tracking cargo and movable assets during transport

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TR-151SP provides position reports that Plaspy ingests to present location and status on maps and in dashboards. Plaspy takes the tracker data and makes it actionable for fleet managers and operations teams.

- Centralized live location on Plaspy maps for assets using the TR-151SP
- Historical route and movement views to support reporting and audits
- Configurable alerts in Plaspy triggered by location updates or lack of reporting
- Consolidated device list and status overview for operational monitoring
- Reporting and export options to combine TR-151SP records with other fleet data

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking cargo and shipments during transport for visibility and custody checks
- Monitoring equipment and movable assets on sites where power is limited
- Temporary or seasonal deployments that benefit from long battery standby
- Asset visibility for businesses that require straightforward SMS or data reporting
- Situations where quick installation and low maintenance are priorities
