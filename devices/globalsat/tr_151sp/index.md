# GlobalSat - TR-151SP

![TR-151SP](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-151SP is a powerful GPS/GSM/GPRS tracker designed for long operation time and reliable tracking. With its high capacity 15A battery, this tracker offers an impressive standby time of up to one month, ensuring continuous monitoring without the need for frequent recharging. Whether you need to track equipment, assets, or cargo during transportation, the TR-151SP is an ideal solution.

Installation of the TR-151SP is quick and easy, allowing you to attach it to your equipment or assets for seamless tracking. This tracker utilizes both GSM and GPRS networks to send out SMS messages with its GPS location. You can receive these messages on your cell phone or personal computer, providing you with real-time updates on the location of your valuable items. The TR-151SP also features a convenient reset button for easy operation.

With its long battery life, reliable tracking capabilities, and versatile applications, the GlobalSat TR-151SP is a top choice for anyone in need of efficient and effective equipment or asset monitoring. Whether you're a business owner looking to track your cargo during transportation or an individual wanting to keep tabs on your valuable assets, the TR-151SP is a reliable and user-friendly solution.

### Outstanding Features:

- High capacity 15A battery for long operation time
- Standby time of up to one month
- GPS/GSM/GPRS technology for accurate and reliable tracking
- SMS notifications with GPS location sent to cell phone or personal computer
- Quick and easy installation on equipment or assets
- Reset button for convenient operation
- Ideal for cargo tracking during transportation and equipment/assets monitoring
