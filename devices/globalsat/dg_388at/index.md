# GlobalSat - DG-388AT

![DG-388AT](./tracker.jpg)

The DG-388AT is a compact GPS tracker style data logger from GlobalSat designed for personal and light asset tracking where detailed route history is the primary requirement. As a standalone logger, it records time, date, speed, altitude and GPS coordinates to create dense waypoint histories. Recorded tracks can be exported in common formats for further analysis and mapping.

This model is relevant for Plaspy users because it provides reliable historical telemetry that can be imported into Plaspy for visualization, reporting and route analysis. With Bluetooth pairing and GPX export via the ez-Connect workflow, DG-388AT logs are straightforward to retrieve and consume in Plaspy, making it a practical choice for workflows that emphasize post‑event review over continuous cellular telemetry.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via GPX export and ez-Connect integration for straightforward import of recorded routes and history.
- Bluetooth Low Energy pairing for simple transfer to iOS and Android devices without a cellular subscription.
- Large onboard storage capable of holding up to 500,000 waypoints for extended logging between offloads.
- Long runtime from a built in rechargeable battery delivering more than 50 hours of typical logging operation.
- Rugged, compact design with IPX7 water resistance when used without external cables for outdoor use.
- Flexible logging modes and on device POI recording to match different activity and telemetry needs.
- G sensor assisted power saving to extend uptime and practical feedback via vibrator and beeper.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the DG-388AT's exported route files so historical tracks recorded on the logger become useful telemetry inside the Plaspy platform. Users retrieve recorded tracks via Bluetooth using the vendor app or PC utility, export to GPX, and then upload those files to Plaspy where the data is visualized and included in reporting and operational review.

- Import GPX route histories exported from ez-Connect into Plaspy to visualize journeys on maps.
- Reconstruct timelines using recorded time and coordinate data for post event analysis and auditing.
- Combine DG-388AT history with other data sources in Plaspy to produce unified fleet movement reports.
- Include POI and waypoint markers from the logger to add contextual information to Plaspy maps and timelines.
- Use Plaspy reporting tools to generate activity summaries and historical telemetry reports from imported tracks.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal outdoor activities such as hiking, cycling and running where route history is needed for later review.
- Long distance travel logging where thousands of waypoints are collected and imported to Plaspy for itinerary analysis.
- Historical movement records for vulnerable persons such as children or elderly where continuous cellular tracking is not required.
- Asset movement history for equipment or shipments that need audited route records rather than live tracking.
- General standalone GPS logging scenarios that require reliable waypoint capture and easy export to mapping tools.
