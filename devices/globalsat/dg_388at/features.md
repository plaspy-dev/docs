# GlobalSat - DG-388AT Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the GlobalSat DG-388AT with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are most relevant for importing recorded tracks, building route history, and performing post‑event analysis in Plaspy. The aim is to help you understand what the DG-388AT can contribute to Plaspy workflows without delving into private configuration details.

Exact feature availability for any individual DG-388AT unit can vary depending on firmware revision, hardware variant, installation choices, and manufacturer implementation. Review manufacturer documentation and release notes for the most current device behavior and confirm which features are present on your specific unit before planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The DG-388AT is a compact standalone GPS data logger that focuses on reliable route capture and dense waypoint storage rather than continuous cellular telemetry. It uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for data retrieval and produces GPX compatible exports that can be imported into Plaspy for mapping, reporting, and historical analysis.

- Records timestamped GPS coordinates along with speed and altitude for route reconstruction and post‑event review.
- Bluetooth Low Energy pairing enables on‑demand file download to iOS and Android devices without a cellular subscription.
- Large onboard storage supports long excursions and batch uploads to Plaspy via GPX export.
- Rugged, water resistant design supports outdoor use and extended logging sessions.
- Flexible logging modes help tailor waypoint density to activity and storage needs.

## Core Features of GlobalSat - DG-388AT

- GPX export and ez‑Connect app integration for straightforward import into Plaspy.
- Bluetooth Low Energy pairing for data transfer to mobile devices and BLE‑enabled laptops.
- Records time, date, speed, altitude and GPS coordinates to create detailed route histories.
- High waypoint capacity for long term logging and reduced need for frequent offloads.
- Built‑in power management and long runtime to support extended recording sessions.
- IPX7 water resistance when used without external cables for outdoor reliability.
- On‑device POI recording to capture contextual markers along a route.
- G‑sensor assisted power saving plus user feedback via vibrator and beeper confirmations.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The DG-388AT integrates with Plaspy primarily through exported GPX files and mobile app workflows. After pairing and downloading logs with the manufacturer app or PC utility, recorded tracks and POI data can be uploaded into Plaspy where they become part of historical telemetry and mapping datasets.

- Import GPX files exported from the DG-388AT into Plaspy to visualize route history on maps.
- Use recorded timestamps, speed, and coordinates to reconstruct journeys and analyze past movements.
- Include POI and waypoint metadata from the export to preserve contextual markers inside Plaspy.
- Combine DG-388AT historical data with other Plaspy device sources for consolidated reporting.
- Leverage Plaspy mapping and reporting tools to turn logged tracks into analytics and history reports.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal outdoor route logging for hiking, cycling, and running with later analysis in Plaspy.
- Travel and trip recording where long continuous waypoint capture is needed for itinerary review.
- Historical movement records for elderly or child location history without continuous cellular tracking.
- Asset movement history for later compliance checks and transport analysis.
- General standalone GPS logging when batch export and post processing are preferred over real time telemetry.
