# GlobalSat - TR-900

![TR-900](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-900 is a versatile and user-friendly GPS tracker designed for easy installation and seamless integration with your vehicle. This 3G network OBDII dongle type plug and play tracker not only provides accurate GPS positioning but also captures OBDII diagnostic data directly from your vehicle. Simply plug the dongle tracker into the OBDII port, which is available on all passenger vehicles made after 1998, and it will draw power and receive OBDII diagnostic data effortlessly.

Equipped with 3G network modules, the TR-900 can connect to both UMTS and GPRS networks, ensuring reliable and fast data transmission. The device also features a high-sensitive G sensor, which allows it to measure G force values for evaluating harsh driving behaviors and generating impact alert reports. Additionally, the TR-900 offers a range of other useful functions, including mileage accumulation reports, over speeding alerts, RPM over revving alerts, coolant temperature alerts, MIL status alerts, and diagnostic trouble code \(DTC\) capturing. With its highly customizable event-based protocol, you can easily configure the tracker to perform various custom alert reports by combining OBDII data and GPS location data.
