# GlobalSat - NW-360HR

![NW-360HR](./tracker.jpg)

The NW-360HR is a wearable GPS tracker watch engineered for personal safety, lone-worker monitoring, and continuous physiological telemetry. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this compact, rugged device combines LTE‑M1 and NB‑IoT connectivity with GPS and BLE beacon support so caregivers and operators get reliable real-time tracking and indoor/outdoor positioning alongside health metrics.

Designed for elder care, lone-worker protection, and multi-purpose IoT deployments, the NW-360HR pairs emergency reporting \(Help/SOS\) and fall advisory with optical heart rate, skin temperature, and a 3‑axis G‑sensor. It delivers timely alerts and periodic or motion-triggered reports to backend platforms—making integration with Plaspy straightforward for real-time tracking, telemetry, and event-driven notifications.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible wearable GPS tracker: combine location and vitals in one device for centralized monitoring.
- Low-power wide-area connectivity via LTE‑M1 and NB‑IoT for reliable, cost-effective data uplink.
- Real-time tracking and indoor localization using GPS plus BLE beacon support for more accurate positioning.
- Integrated SOS \(Help\) button and fall advisory for rapid emergency notifications and automated incident detection.
- Continuous physiological telemetry: optical heart rate and skin temperature sensors for health and activity monitoring.
- Motion detection and activity tracking via a 3‑axis accelerometer \(G‑sensor\) with motion-triggered reporting.
- Practical battery life \(~48 hours on a 1‑hour reporting interval\) and supplied charging cable for routine daily use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The NW-360HR transmits GPS location, BLE beacon data, sensor telemetry, and event notifications over LTE‑M1 or NB‑IoT to an IoT backend. Plaspy ingests that data stream for real-time tracking, alerting, and reporting. Integration uses standard IoT backend interfaces so emergency SOS messages, fall advisories, and physiological metrics appear in Plaspy dashboards and workflows without custom hardware protocols.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via LTE‑M1 / NB‑IoT
- SOS \(Help\) button triggers immediate alerts and location sharing for rapid response
- Fall advisory and motion-triggered reports generate automated incident events
- Physiological telemetry \(heart rate, skin temperature\) shown alongside location for contextual monitoring
- Bluetooth sensors / BLE beacon support for improved indoor positioning and proximity events
