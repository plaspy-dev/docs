# GlobalSat - TR-313

![TR-313](./logo.jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-313 is a highly advanced 3G network personal tracker that offers a compact, lightweight, and user-friendly design. With its fast acquisition GPS module, this device ensures accurate location tracking, making it an ideal choice for personal tracking applications. But what sets the TR-313 apart is its additional features that provide enhanced safety and convenience.

Equipped with a built-in microphone and speaker, this tracker allows for hands-free two-way communication. This means that not only can you track the location of the device, but you can also communicate with the person carrying it. This feature is particularly useful for parents who want to keep an eye on their children or for caregivers who need to monitor the safety of elderly family members.

One standout feature of the TR-313 is its emergency button. With just one push, the owner can quickly call pre-set phone numbers and send emergency alerts. This provides an added layer of security and peace of mind, especially in urgent situations. Whether it's for personal safety or for monitoring the well-being of loved ones, the TR-313 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that delivers on both functionality and convenience.

#### Key Features:

- Compact and lightweight design
- Fast acquisition GPS module for accurate location tracking
- Built-in microphone and speaker for hands-free two-way communication
- Emergency button for quick access to pre-set phone numbers and emergency alerts
- Ideal for personal tracking applications, including child and elder safety monitoring
