# GlobalSat - TR-616

![TR-616](./logo..jpg)

The GlobalSat TR-616C1 is a 4G LTE GPS tracker designed specifically for vehicle tracking applications. This multi-functional and economical communication platform integrates a high sensitivity GPS module and a multi-band support LTE-FDD/LTE-TDD/HSPA+/EDGE/GPRS/GSM communication module into a compact board. With its powerful microcontroller and solid housing, the TR-616C1 is easy to install and provides real-time GPS positions anytime and anywhere in the open sky.

One of the standout features of the TR-616C1 is its support for LTE CAT1/HSPA+/EDGE/GPRS data transfer, ensuring fast and reliable communication. It also comes with a built-in high sensitivity GPS system with an active antenna, allowing for accurate positioning. The tracker supports AT-Command for easy and fast application development, making it highly customizable to suit specific tracking needs.

In terms of communication, the TR-616C1 can be communicated via SMS, TCP, or UDP, and offers remote control capabilities via LTE, SMS, GSM, or GPRS. This allows for seamless monitoring and control of the tracker from a central control computer. The tracker also supports real-time GPS tracking, providing precise position and vehicle status information for enhanced fleet management and improved vehicle safety.

Other notable features of the TR-616C1 include support for geo-fencing function, OTA firmware upgrading, data buffer storage of up to 3,000 points, power low/lost detection alarm, and 3 LED indicators for cellular, GPS, and power status. It also comes with a G-sensor for detecting and reporting sudden movements or impacts.
