# GlobalSat - LT-520

<div className="device-logo">
![LT-520](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GlobalSat LT-520 is a rugged GPS tracking device designed for long term asset monitoring in mixed indoor and outdoor environments. It combines GNSS positioning with low power wide area connectivity and hybrid location techniques, using BLE, NFC and Wi‑Fi scans to supplement outdoor fixes. The LT-520 is built for extended service intervals and configurable reporting, making it suitable for deployments where low maintenance and reliable location data are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the LT-520 can be integrated into real-time tracking and fleet management workflows to provide location updates, motion events and device health telemetry. Plaspy can ingest the LT-520’s uplinks and combine GNSS fixes with BLE and Wi‑Fi context to improve indoor handover and event-driven alerts, enabling operational visibility across dispersed assets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker with GNSS positioning and low power wide area uplinks for long range reporting.
- Hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning that uses BLE, NFC and Wi‑Fi scans to supplement GNSS in buildings and dense areas.
- High capacity 19 Ah non rechargeable battery designed for multi year deployments under common reporting schedules.
- Built in accelerometer for motion detection and tamper alerts to support anti theft monitoring and movement analytics.
- Rugged design and wide operating temperature range for industrial and outdoor asset tracking.
- OTA firmware management via BLE for field updates and reduced on site maintenance.
- Optional ATEX IECEx variant available for hazardous environments; consult the manufacturer for certification details.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LT-520 delivers position fixes, motion events and health data into the platform so teams can monitor assets in real time and configure automated workflows. Plaspy uses the device uplinks and hybrid location signals to present consolidated location history, generate alerts, and support reporting that aligns with fleet and asset management needs.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks visible in Plaspy dashboards for operational awareness.
- Motion and tamper alerts from the accelerometer to trigger notifications and anti theft workflows.
- Hybrid indoor context from BLE and Wi‑Fi scans combined with GNSS to improve location accuracy during indoor outdoor transitions.
- Battery and device health telemetry reported to Plaspy to support maintenance planning and service scheduling.
- Configurable reporting intervals and event driven uplinks allow Plaspy to balance visibility with battery conservation.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term fleet and asset tracking where extended battery life reduces field maintenance.
- Industrial asset monitoring and anti theft detection using motion alerts and remote notifications.
- Warehouse and campus deployments that require combined indoor and outdoor location context.
- Remote equipment monitoring in harsh environments that demand a rugged device with wide temperature tolerance.
- Personnel or lone worker tracking where hybrid positioning improves indoor location context.
