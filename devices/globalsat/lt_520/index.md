# GlobalSat - LT-520

![LT-520](./tracker.jpg)

The LT-520 from GlobalSat is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for long-term asset monitoring in mixed indoor/outdoor environments. Built around LoRaWAN low-power wide-area connectivity and an integrated GNSS receiver \(GPS + GLONASS\), the LT-520 delivers dependable location data, motion detection and hybrid positioning using BLE, NFC and Wi‑Fi. Its high-capacity 19 Ah non-rechargeable battery and configurable reporting make it an excellent choice for deployments that prioritize battery life and low maintenance.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LT-520 integrates smoothly into real-time tracking and fleet management workflows—supporting telemetry-driven alerts, anti-theft monitoring and indoor/outdoor handover when BLE or Wi‑Fi signals are available. An ATEX/IECEx variant \(LT-520 ATEX\) is available for hazardous environments; contact the manufacturer for certification status and recommended installation guidance for explosive atmospheres.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker combining LoRaWAN and GNSS \(GPS + GLONASS\) for reliable position reporting and long battery life.
- Hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning using BLE, NFC and Wi‑Fi to supplement GNSS in buildings and dense environments.
- High-capacity 19 Ah non-rechargeable battery optimized for long deployments—typical life up to 5 years at a once-daily GPS reporting profile.
- Built-in 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection, tamper alerts and configurable periodic reporting to support anti-theft and movement analytics.
- Rugged operating range \(-20°C to +60°C\) for industrial and outdoor asset tracking in challenging conditions.
- OTA firmware updates via BLE for field updates and lifecycle management without removing the unit from service.
- Optional ATEX/IECEx variant for hazardous areas; consult GlobalSat for current certification and deployment guidance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LT-520 integrates with Plaspy to provide real-time tracking, telemetry and event reporting suitable for fleet management and asset monitoring. Position fixes acquired by the GNSS engine are transmitted over LoRaWAN uplinks to Plaspy, while BLE and Wi‑Fi scans provide indoor location context. The device’s accelerometer reports motion events and tamper conditions to trigger alerts and automated workflows in Plaspy.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via LoRaWAN to Plaspy \(subject to regional LoRaWAN network configuration and duty-cycle limits\).
- Motion and tamper detection from the 3-axis accelerometer, enabling anti-theft alerts and movement analytics in Plaspy dashboards.
- Hybrid indoor positioning using BLE and Wi‑Fi scans—Plaspy can combine these signals with GNSS for improved accuracy when outdoors-to-indoors transitions occur.
- Battery and health telemetry \(battery state, device temperature\) reported to Plaspy for predictive maintenance and remote monitoring.
- Support for telemetry integrations—LT-520 data can feed fuel monitoring, ignition state tracking or immobilizer workflows through Plaspy when paired with appropriate backend integrations or external sensors.

The LT-520 is positioned for deployments that require durable, low-maintenance GPS tracking with hybrid connectivity. When used as a Plaspy compatible tracker, it delivers the telemetry and location data needed for effective fleet management, anti-theft response and long-term asset monitoring without frequent battery replacement. Its LoRaWAN backbone ensures low-power uplinks across wide areas, while BLE and Wi‑Fi support enhance indoor positioning accuracy—allowing Plaspy to reconcile GNSS fixes with local beacon data.

Operational teams benefit from extended service intervals \(19 Ah battery with multi-year life in common reporting profiles\), over-the-air firmware management via BLE for reduced field visits, and a robust environmental spec for industrial use. For deployments that require work in potentially explosive atmospheres, the LT-520 ATEX variant is available; consult GlobalSat and Plaspy integration guidance to ensure certification, installation and reporting profiles match your regulatory and safety requirements. Overall, the LT-520 offers a reliable, Plaspy compatible solution for telemetry-driven tracking, fuel monitoring integrations, ignition and immobilizer workflows via system integrations, and scalable fleet management across indoor and outdoor environments.
