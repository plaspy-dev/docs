# GlobalSat - LT-501

<div className="device-logo">
![LT-501](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The GlobalSat LT-501 is a compact LoRa GPS asset tracker designed for both indoor and outdoor monitoring of objects. As part of the LT-501R series, it combines location reporting, motion detection, and short range indoor positioning to provide flexible visibility for portable assets. The device emphasizes a lightweight form factor and long battery life, making it suitable for passive and periodic reporting in varied environments.

This tracker is relevant to Plaspy users because it is compatible with Plaspy and built to deliver the types of location and motion updates that fleet and asset management platforms consume. With LoRa connectivity and support for Helium network environments, the LT-501 can feed location, motion, and geofence events into Plaspy to support monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting for assets tracked across sites.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight form factor suitable for attaching to portable assets
- Designed for both indoor and outdoor location monitoring
- Compatible with wide area LoRa networks and Helium network connectivity
- Long battery life supported by the included 19A battery for extended deployments
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer enables motion detection alerts and activity monitoring
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for indoor positioning with beacons and proximity use
- Geo fencing capability allows virtual boundary alerts for asset movement

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the LT-501 provides location and motion data that the platform visualizes and acts upon for fleet and asset oversight. Plaspy can ingest the tracker updates and present them within its monitoring, alerting, and reporting tools so teams can maintain operational visibility.

- Location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live and historical position tracking
- Motion detection events can trigger activity alerts and help identify asset movement
- Geo fence entries and exits generate notifications within Plaspy for perimeter monitoring
- Periodic and real time reporting modes support different operational reporting cadences
- BLE based indoor positioning can complement GPS reports for closer range asset location

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking pallets, containers, and other movable inventory across warehouses and yards
- Monitoring equipment and tools on construction sites or distributed worksites
- Long duration asset tracking where battery life and low maintenance are priorities
- Indoor asset location via beacon assisted positioning combined with outdoor GPS
- Geo fence monitoring for high value assets that require perimeter alerts

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LT-501 is a practical option for organizations that need a durable, low maintenance tracker that covers both indoor and outdoor scenarios. Its support for long range LoRa connectivity and Helium network compatibility helps deliver location updates even in wide area deployments, while motion sensing and BLE add useful context for asset activity and indoor positioning. These attributes pair well with Plaspy, which is designed to consolidate location feeds, deliver alerts, and provide reporting for operational decision making.

Because product lines and feature sets can vary, the LT-501 is best selected when its form factor and reported data types match your operational needs. Plaspy can help turn the tracker updates into actionable insights, but confirming the device capabilities against your specific monitoring requirements is a good practice.
