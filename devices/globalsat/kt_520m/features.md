# GlobalSat - KT-520M Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the GlobalSat KT-520M and how its capabilities map into Plaspy for tracking and fleet workflows. It focuses on the practical, non-sensitive features you can expect to use when the KT-520M is integrated with Plaspy for location history, motion events, battery status, and lifecycle management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and how the manufacturer configures the unit. For the most current device specific details and the latest firmware notes consult GlobalSat documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The KT-520M is designed for long term, low maintenance deployments where consistent location visibility across mixed coverage areas is required. Its combination of cellular and satellite connectivity, long life battery, GNSS positioning, and local configuration options make it suitable for remote assets, trailers, and equipment that need years of unattended operation.

- Dual mode connectivity with LTE‑M as the primary channel and Kineis satellite fallback to extend tracking into low coverage areas.
- Long life, maintenance free SAFT 17Ah non rechargeable battery sized for multi year deployments under moderate reporting intervals.
- Native GNSS positioning paired with a 3‑axis accelerometer to detect motion and tamper events.
- BLE for local configuration and over the air firmware updates, plus remote firmware updates via LTE‑M for fleet scale management.
- Rugged IP69K rated enclosure and a wide operating temperature range for demanding vehicle and equipment environments.
- Compact, lightweight form factor enabling discreet or permanent mounting on mobile assets.

## Core Features of GlobalSat - KT-520M

- LTE‑M primary connectivity for efficient cellular reporting in covered areas.
- Kineis satellite fallback to preserve location traces and alerts when cellular is unavailable.
- Built in GNSS receiver for accurate location telemetry.
- 3‑axis accelerometer for motion detection, tamper identification, and movement events.
- SAFT 17Ah maintenance free non rechargeable battery optimized for long deployments.
- BLE interface for local setup, configuration of reporting intervals, and OTA updates.
- Remote OTA firmware updates via LTE‑M for large scale device maintenance.
- Rugged mechanical design with IP69K ingress protection and wide temperature tolerance.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a KT-520M is connected to Plaspy, the device supplies position, motion events, and battery information so asset visibility and event monitoring are available through the Plaspy platform. Plaspy ingests the tracker telemetry to produce location history, trigger notifications, and combine the KT-520M data with other operational signals where relevant.

- GNSS coordinates and location history are viewable in Plaspy when the device reports over LTE‑M or via satellite fallback.
- Motion and tamper events generated by the 3‑axis accelerometer appear as discrete events and can be used to trigger alerts or workflows.
- Battery state and power reporting allow Plaspy to surface low energy warnings and help schedule maintenance for long life deployments.
- BLE based local configuration enables field technicians to adjust reporting intervals or apply firmware updates before returning devices to service.
- Remote firmware management via cellular simplifies fleet wide updates and lifecycle management when devices have coverage.
- Plaspy automatically processes incoming telemetry and makes it available for reporting, mapping, and correlation with other integrated data sources.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking across mixed coverage routes where cellular is intermittent and satellite fallback preserves continuity.
- Anti theft and tamper detection using accelerometer events combined with Plaspy alerts.
- Long term equipment and trailer monitoring where battery longevity and low maintenance are priorities.
- Remote infrastructure and off grid asset tracking using satellite fallback for periodic location reports.
- Aggregating GNSS and motion telemetry in Plaspy alongside other vehicle signals for operational reporting.
- Permanent or covert installations that require a compact, rugged enclosure and multi year battery life.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Battery life estimates depend on message frequency and reporting configuration; the stated multi year estimate assumes moderate reporting intervals.
- Satellite fallback behavior and message cadence on Kineis may differ from LTE reporting and can be subject to regional service conditions.
- BLE features for local configuration and OTA require physical proximity to the device and appropriate field tools.
- Some features may be enabled or limited by firmware version or hardware revision; manufacturer firmware updates can change behavior.
- Always confirm regional variants and certification differences with the manufacturer before large scale deployments.
