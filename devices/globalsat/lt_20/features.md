# GlobalSat - LT-20 Features

This page describes the public feature set and practical capabilities of the GlobalSat LT-20 series tracker as it relates to use with Plaspy. It focuses on the non sensitive, user facing aspects of the device that matter for asset tracking workflows on the Plaspy platform and summarizes how the LT-20 family reports location and event information for operational oversight.

The exact feature availability and behavior for a given unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and how the device is installed. The description on this page is grounded in the manufacturer's public information for the LT-20P solar LoRa variant and is intended as general guidance for use with Plaspy rather than a substitute for the official product manual.

## Feature Overview

The GlobalSat LT-20P is a compact, solar powered LoRa GPS tracker intended for outdoor asset tracking such as livestock and farming equipment. Its design emphasizes long field life, basic motion awareness, and reliable GNSS location reporting across several regional LoRa bands. When connected to Plaspy, these capabilities provide persistent location visibility and event alerts suitable for remote asset monitoring.

- Solar powered LoRa tracking for extended field deployments
- Support for regional LoRa frequencies including US 915 MHz, AS 923 MHz, and EU 868 MHz
- High sensitivity GPS GNSS for location reporting in open outdoor environments
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer for motion detection and tamper awareness
- Low power alert to notify operators when battery levels require attention
- IPX7 rated enclosure for resistance to water exposure in outdoor use

## Core Features of GlobalSat - LT-20

- Solar power system designed to extend operational life without frequent battery replacement
- LoRa radio support across multiple regional frequency plans for flexible deployment
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver to obtain reliable location fixes outdoors
- 3 axis accelerometer to detect motion events and potential tampering
- Low power alert function to report depleted or low battery status
- IPX7 water resistance suitable for many outdoor weather conditions
- Compact and lightweight enclosure for easy mounting on animals or equipment
- Design targeted at asset tracking scenarios such as livestock and field machinery

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event reports from compatible trackers and makes them available for mapping, alerts, history, and operational workflows. The LT-20P feature set maps to Plaspy capabilities that help teams keep track of remote assets and receive timely notifications about movement and battery status.

- GNSS location reports appear on Plaspy maps and position history for each device
- Motion events from the accelerometer can be shown as device events or triggers for notifications
- Low battery alerts are surfaced in Plaspy as device warnings so operators can schedule maintenance
- Long life operation from solar charging reduces the need for frequent physical checks and shows as steady reporting on Plaspy
- Regional radio compatibility helps ensure the device can be deployed in supported LoRa regions and report into Plaspy without special platform changes
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols so supported devices begin reporting with minimal configuration

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking grazing livestock such as cattle and sheep across large pastures
- Monitoring tractors, trailers, and other field equipment for location and movement
- Long duration asset deployments where solar charging reduces maintenance visits
- Tamper or unexpected movement detection for remote assets
- Seasonal or rotating deployments where lightweight, portable trackers are needed
- Operations in wet or variable weather environments where water resistance matters

## Feature Availability Notes

- The LT-20P variant described here is a solar LoRa GNSS tracker from GlobalSat; other LT-20 series revisions may differ in hardware or features
- Supported LoRa frequency bands vary by regional model; confirm you have the correct frequency version for your deployment region
- Firmware level can change behavior for reporting intervals, event detection, and power management; device behavior may differ between firmware releases
- Installation and mounting affect solar charging performance and GNSS reception; placement is important for reliable operation
- Always consult the manufacturer documentation for the exact specification and compatibility details for your device unit

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with a compact solar LoRa GNSS tracker like the LT-20P gives organizations a practical way to maintain visibility over dispersed outdoor assets. Plaspy aggregates location history, presents devices on maps, and surfaces motion and battery events so teams can prioritize field visits, detect unusual activity, and maintain operational continuity without constant manual checks.
