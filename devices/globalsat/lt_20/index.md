# GlobalSat - LT-20

![LT-20](./logo..jpg)

The GlobalSat LT-20P is a solar-powered LoRa GPS tracker specifically designed for tracking assets such as cattle, cows, livestock, and farming equipment. With its high-performance solar panel, this tracker can provide continuous LoRa transmission, making it ideal for outdoor long-lasting tracking applications. Its lightweight and compact design allow for easy installation on the surface of the asset.

The LT-20P supports LoRa frequencies of US 915 MHz, AS 923 MHz, and EU 868 MHz, ensuring compatibility with different regions. It features a high-sensitivity GPS/GNSS system, providing accurate and reliable location information. The built-in 3-axis accelerometer allows for motion detection, ensuring that you are alerted in case of any movement or tampering with the asset. Additionally, the tracker has a low power alert feature, notifying you when the battery is running low.

The LT-20P has an IPX7 waterproof rating, making it suitable for use in various weather conditions. Its dimensions are 80 x 19.17 x 79.10 mm, and it weighs 80 ± 2 g, ensuring it is compact and lightweight for easy installation and portability. Whether you need to track livestock or farming equipment, the GlobalSat LT-20P is a reliable and efficient solution.

- Supports LoRa frequencies: US 915 MHz / AS 923 MHz / EU 868 MHz

- Low power alert
