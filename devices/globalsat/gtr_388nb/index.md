# GlobalSat - GTR-388NB

<div className="device-logo">
![GTR-388NB](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GlobalSat GTR-388NB is a compact, rugged NB‑IoT GPS tracker designed for eBikes, motorcycles, scooters and other light vehicles. It combines a waterproof enclosure, built in GNSS and GSM antennas, AGPS support and an internal motion sensor to provide reliable position fixes and basic vehicle telemetry in outdoor and shared mobility environments. The device includes an internal rechargeable backup battery and a low power design suited to long standby deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GTR-388NB can forward its location and telemetry into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents real time map markers, geofence events and trip history so fleets and vehicle owners can use the tracker data alongside other operational information managed in Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight form factor for unobtrusive mounting on eBikes scooters and motorcycles.
- Rugged IPX7 waterproof enclosure for reliable outdoor operation in harsh conditions.
- NB‑IoT connectivity with UDP transport for low bandwidth wide area deployments.
- Built in high sensitivity GNSS antenna with AGPS support for consistent position fixes.
- Internal motion sensor and LED status indicators for movement detection and visual diagnostics.
- Internal rechargeable 820 mAh backup battery with optimized power management for extended standby.
- Optional accessories such as relay and emergency button to support anti theft and immobilizer workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GTR-388NB sends NB‑IoT UDP packets that Plaspy converts into actionable fleet data. Plaspy normalizes incoming telemetry and exposes it through live maps, configurable alerts and historical reports so operators can monitor vehicles and respond to events efficiently.

- Real time location updates and history playback so vehicles can be tracked live and reviewed later.
- Motion and ignition related events forwarded for trip segmentation and operational insights.
- Analog voltage readings and other telemetry available in Plaspy for simple sensor monitoring and reporting.
- Relay and digital output control represented in Plaspy workflows for remote immobilization or accessory control.
- Emergency input and tamper related notifications routed to Plaspy for prompt alerting and response.
- Data consolidation within Plaspy to combine tracker feeds with other fleet information for unified oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light vehicle and motorcycle fleets requiring real time location and trip logs.
- Anti theft protection and remote immobilization workflows for scooters and motorcycles.
- Shared micromobility deployments where compact waterproof trackers minimize visual impact.
- Simple telemetry monitoring such as battery or voltage reporting integrated into fleet reports.
- Outdoor asset tracking in demanding environments where IPX7 protection is valuable.

The GTR-388NB is a practical fit for organizations using Plaspy that need a small rugged tracker with NB‑IoT connectivity and essential vehicle I O points. Its combination of waterproof construction, internal backup battery and motion detection helps maintain continuity of tracking in outdoor situations, while optional accessories expand its role in anti theft and immobilization solutions.
