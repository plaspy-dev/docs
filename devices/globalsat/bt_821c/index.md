# GlobalSat - BT-821C

![BT-821C](./tracker.jpg)

The BT-821C Bluetooth GNSS receiver is a compact, high-performance positioning device designed to extend accurate satellite location to smartphones, tablets, laptops, and other Bluetooth-enabled hosts. As a Plaspy compatible positioning source, the BT-821C delivers reliable GNSS data streams that improve real-time tracking and location quality for mapping, fleet management dashboards, and field telemetry applications.

The BT-821C combines a MediaTek \(MTK\) high-performance chipset, a built-in high-gain antenna, and support for standard NMEA-0183 and RTCM protocols to provide fast Time-To-First-Fix \(TTFF\) and robust low-signal performance. With up to 24 hours of continuous operation on its rechargeable lithium-ion battery and clear LED status indicators for satellite lock, battery and Bluetooth connection, this receiver is suited for extended mobile use in navigation, GIS collection, and Plaspy-enabled tracking workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible external GNSS source — pairs via Bluetooth to provide accurate location for apps and gateways using Plaspy for real-time tracking.
- High-sensitivity MTK chipset — supports up to 210 PRN channels with 66 search channels and 22 simultaneous tracking channels for fast TTFF and improved low-signal reception.
- Standard protocol support — outputs NMEA-0183 sentences \(GGA, GSA, GSV, RMC\) and accepts RTCM corrections for improved positioning accuracy where available.
- Long battery life — rechargeable lithium-ion battery provides up to 24 hours of continuous operation for full-day fieldwork without recharge.
- Built-in antenna and compact form factor — simple, portable solution for augmenting device-integrated GNSS on tablets and phones.
- Visual status LEDs — satellite \(green\), battery \(red/amber\), and Bluetooth \(blue\) indicators simplify setup and monitoring in the field.
- SBAS and regional augmentation — supports WAAS, EGNOS, GAGAN, MSAS and QZSS to enhance accuracy in supported regions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The BT-821C integrates with Plaspy-enabled hosts by streaming standard GNSS sentences and correction data over Bluetooth. When paired to a Plaspy-capable device or gateway, the receiver becomes the primary source of positioning, enabling real-time tracking, improved telemetry, and higher-quality location data for Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via NMEA sentences \(GGA, GSA, GSV, RMC\) delivered over Bluetooth.
- RTCM support enables use of differential corrections to reduce position error where network corrections are available.
- SBAS and QZSS compatibility for regional accuracy improvements used by Plaspy mapping and geofencing.
- Bluetooth pairing simplifies integration without additional wiring — ideal for mobile devices and temporary installations.
- Provides reliable position feed that complements vehicle telemetry, fuel monitoring and immobilizer workflows when combined with Plaspy-connected telematics hardware.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the BT-821C with Plaspy brings a practical path to better location data without rewiring or replacing existing devices. Its high-sensitivity MTK chipset, SBAS/QZSS support, and RTCM compatibility make it a robust external GNSS option for real-time tracking and fleet management scenarios that need reliable positioning. The long battery life and compact design keep operations mobile and simple, while Bluetooth connectivity enables fast setup with Plaspy-compatible hosts.

For organizations using Plaspy for telemetry, real-time tracking, anti-theft workflows, fuel monitoring dashboards, or immobilizer control tied to accurate geofencing, the BT-821C supplies the positioning layer that enhances detection accuracy and reduces false alerts. Because it outputs standard NMEA and RTCM data, the BT-821C integrates smoothly into Plaspy environments and complements existing telematics and Bluetooth sensors to deliver dependable location insights in the field.
