# GlobalSat - BT-821C

![BT-821C](./tracker.jpg)

The GlobalSat BT-821C is a compact Bluetooth GNSS receiver designed to bring high performance satellite positioning to smartphones, tablets, laptops, and other Bluetooth enabled hosts. It combines a MediaTek high performance chipset with a built in high gain antenna and outputs standard GNSS data formats to extend accurate location to host devices. The unit is built for mobile field use with clear LED status indicators and a rechargeable battery for extended operation.

As a Plaspy compatible positioning source, the BT-821C can feed reliable GNSS data streams into Plaspy enabled devices and gateways to improve real time tracking, mapping, and fleet management dashboards. Its support for standard NMEA sentences and RTCM corrections makes it a practical external positioning option for organizations that use Plaspy for monitoring, geofencing, and field telemetry workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact Bluetooth GNSS receiver that augments device integrated positioning for mobile hosts.
- High sensitivity MediaTek chipset for fast time to first fix and improved low signal reception.
- Outputs standard NMEA sentences and accepts RTCM corrections for enhanced location quality.
- Rechargeable battery designed for long continuous operation to support full day fieldwork.
- Built in antenna and portable form factor simplify use with tablets, phones, and gateways.
- Visual LED indicators for satellite lock, battery status, and Bluetooth connection to aid field monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with a Plaspy capable host, the BT-821C becomes a primary positioning source by streaming standard GNSS sentences and correction data over Bluetooth. Plaspy can consume this feed to provide more accurate location information across dashboards, alerts, and reporting tools used for fleet and field operations.

- Real time position updates via NMEA sentences delivered to Plaspy enabled devices and gateways.
- RTCM correction support allows Plaspy to reduce positioning error where network corrections are available.
- SBAS and regional augmentation compatibility contribute to improved accuracy on Plaspy maps and geofences.
- Bluetooth pairing enables easy, cable free integration for mobile deployments and temporary setups.
- Provides a reliable location feed that enhances fleet visibility, route verification, and telemetry reporting inside Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Improving smartphone and tablet navigation accuracy in areas where onboard GNSS is limited.
- GIS and mapping fieldwork that requires a dependable external position source for data collection.
- Fleet tracking augmentation to provide higher quality location data for Plaspy dashboards.
- Portable telemetry and temporary instrumentation where a standalone GNSS source is needed.
- Mobile app development and testing where consistent satellite data is required across devices.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The BT-821C is a useful fit for organizations that need to raise the quality of position data feeding into Plaspy without modifying existing vehicles or host devices. Its use of standard GNSS outputs and correction formats makes integration straightforward for Plaspy enabled hosts and gateways, while the portable design supports field operations and temporary installations.

For teams focused on fleet monitoring, mapping, or field telemetry, pairing the BT-821C with Plaspy provides a practical route to improved location fidelity and more reliable operational insights.
