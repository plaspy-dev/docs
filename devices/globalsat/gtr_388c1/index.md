# GlobalSat - GTR-388C1

![GTR-388C1](./tracker.jpg)

The GTR-388C1 from a rugged device family is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for eBikes, motorcycles and small vehicles. Built around 4G LTE \(Cat 1\) connectivity with automatic fallback to 3G and 2G, the GTR-388C1 delivers reliable real-time tracking and telemetry to the Plaspy platform, packaged in an IPX7 waterproof enclosure designed for exposed outdoor installations.

With a high-sensitivity GNSS receiver \(A-GPS\), integrated antennas, an 820 mAh rechargeable backup battery, and firmware updates over-the-air, the GTR-388C1 is optimized for fleet management, anti-theft protection and compact vehicle deployments where concealed mounting, robust sealing and efficient power management matter.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and fleet management via TCP/UDP/HTTP or SMS.
- 4G LTE \(Cat 1\) with automatic fallback to dual-band UMTS/HSDPA \(3G\) and dual-band GSM \(2G\) for wide area coverage.
- High-sensitivity GNSS with A-GPS for fast position fixes and accurate route replay.
- IPX7 waterproof enclosure with waterproof cable connector and thermal solution for exposed vehicle installations.
- Built-in 820 mAh rechargeable backup battery to maintain operation on power loss and support anti-theft scenarios.
- Rich I/O: ACC detection \(ignition\), multiple digital inputs \(including emergency button\), one analog input, and a digital output for relay control.
- Lightweight, compact form factor \(~66.5 g\) with accessory options for flexible mounting and integration.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed and configured, the GTR-388C1 streams GNSS location and vehicle telemetry to Plaspy using standard protocols. Plaspy ingests position updates and I/O events for live dashboards, geofencing alerts, route replay and scheduled reports—turning device-level signals into actionable fleet intelligence.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates routed to Plaspy via TCP, UDP, HTTP or SMS.
- Ignition \(ACC\) detection reports allow Plaspy to correlate driving time, idling and ignition events for driver and vehicle reporting.
- Motion detection by the built-in G-sensor supports movement alerts and tamper/theft detection workflows.
- Analog input can be integrated for telemetry such as fuel-level sensor readings when connected and calibrated through Plaspy.
- Digital output can drive an external relay \(optional accessory\) to implement remote relay control or immobilizer workflows under Plaspy control.

The GTR-388C1 pairs rugged hardware and focused connectivity with the Plaspy platform to deliver reliable real-time tracking and actionable telemetry for small vehicles and two-wheel fleets. Its IPX7-rated enclosure and thermal design make it suitable for exposed installations, while the built-in backup battery and efficient power management ensure continuity during power interruptions—critical for effective anti-theft and fleet monitoring. With versatile I/O \(ACC detection, emergency input, analog telemetry and relay output\) and support for FOTA, the device simplifies installation, maintenance and scalable fleet rollouts on Plaspy.
