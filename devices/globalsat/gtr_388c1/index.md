# GlobalSat - GTR-388C1

<div className="device-logo">
![GTR-388C1](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GlobalSat GTR-388C1 is a compact member of a rugged device family designed for eBikes, motorcycles and small vehicles. It combines high sensitivity GNSS with integrated antennas, 4G LTE connectivity with automatic fallback to 3G and 2G, an IPX7 waterproof enclosure, and a rechargeable backup battery to maintain operation during power loss. The unit is engineered for exposed outdoor installations where concealed mounting, sealing, and reliable position updates matter.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GTR-388C1 streams location and vehicle telemetry into Plaspy for live visibility, alerts and historical analysis. Its versatile I O and telemetry capabilities—such as ignition detection, motion sensing, analog input and a digital output for relay control—make it a practical option for fleet managers and mobility operators who want to combine compact hardware with Plaspy dashboards, geofencing and reporting workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for eBikes motorcycles and small vehicles in exposed environments
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with automatic fallback to 3G and 2G for broad area coverage
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver with A GPS and integrated antennas for faster fixes
- IPX7 waterproof enclosure and waterproof cable connector for outdoor installations
- Built in rechargeable backup battery to sustain operation during power interruptions
- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking via standard reporting methods and supports over the air firmware updates
- Versatile I O including ignition detection multiple digital inputs analog input and a digital output for relay control

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once provisioned, the GTR-388C1 delivers GNSS location and device events to Plaspy where those signals are translated into live maps, alerts, and reports that support operational decisions. Plaspy ingests position updates and I O events to provide a coherent view of vehicle status and history across a fleet.

- Real time position updates and telemetry routed to Plaspy for live tracking and map views
- Ignition ACC detection enables Plaspy to report driving time idling and ignition events for operational reporting
- Motion detection and tamper related events support movement alerts and anti theft workflows within Plaspy
- Analog input can be used to report external telemetry such as calibrated fuel level readings when configured
- Digital output can be used to trigger remote relay control or immobilization workflows managed through Plaspy
- Over the air firmware updates simplify device maintenance and rollouts via platform guided procedures

## Typical Use Cases

- Compact fleet tracking for last mile delivery and small vehicle operations that need real time location and route replay

- Telemetry extensions such as fuel monitoring or relay based control when paired with appropriate accessories
