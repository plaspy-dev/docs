# AOVX - AL300 Features

This page covers the public feature context for using the AOVX AL300 with Plaspy. The AL300 is a rugged asset tracker designed for remote and industrial monitoring, with a focus on long-duration deployments, tamper awareness, and reliable location visibility for containers, trailers, generators, heavy machinery, and maritime assets.

Because device behavior can vary by firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation, the exact feature set available in Plaspy may differ from one deployment to another. The information below reflects the public capabilities described for the AL300 and should be used as practical guidance rather than a substitute for current manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The AOVX AL300 is built for asset monitoring scenarios where durability, service life, and event awareness matter more than frequent manual maintenance. In Plaspy, its strengths are most useful when you need dependable reporting for long-term assets that may be exposed to harsh outdoor conditions or require intermittent visibility over extended periods.

- Rugged asset tracking for harsh environments and long-term deployments
- IP68 sealing for dust protection and water resistance in demanding conditions
- Tamper and motion awareness for relocation or unauthorized access monitoring
- Optional Bluetooth sensor expansion for additional environmental or state data
- Long-life battery design for reduced maintenance cycles
- OTA and USB firmware update support for easier remote lifecycle management

## Core Features of AOVX - AL300

- Rugged enclosure designed for industrial and outdoor asset tracking
- IP68-rated protection for challenging environmental conditions
- ATEX minimum safety compliance for use in potentially explosive atmospheres
- Built-in motion sensor for movement awareness
- Configurable light detector and tamper switch for interference detection
- Optional Bluetooth sensor expansion for added telemetry
- Long-life battery design suited to extended deployments
- Firmware maintenance support through USB and over-the-air updates

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AL300 reports into Plaspy, its public features can be translated into practical monitoring value such as location visibility, event history, and operational oversight. This makes the device suitable for organizations that need to track assets without adding unnecessary service complexity.

- Location updates can be viewed in Plaspy for asset visibility and route awareness
- Motion and tamper-related events can support alerts and incident review
- Long-life tracking behavior helps reduce the need for frequent field intervention
- Optional Bluetooth sensor data can be combined with position information in Plaspy
- Firmware update support helps maintain devices over time with less disruption
- Reporting intervals can be used to balance tracking detail with battery conservation

## Typical Use Cases
