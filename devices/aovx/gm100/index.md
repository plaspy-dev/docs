# AOVX - GM100

<div className="device-logo">
![GM100](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AOVX GM100 is an ultra-thin GPS tracker designed for discreet goods and shipment monitoring. It is built to attach easily to packages and pallets without requiring installation, making it suitable for temporary or reusable tracking scenarios where visibility matters and equipment should stay unobtrusive.

The GM100 is relevant as a Plaspy compatible device because it is designed to provide location reporting and shipment telemetry that can be centralized in Plaspy. For teams looking for AOVX GM100 tracking software, Plaspy helps turn device data into usable tracking views, alerts, and history for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for shipment visibility and centralized tracking management
- Ultra-thin, installation-free design for discreet attachment to goods and pallets
- Multi-mode positioning using GPS, Beidou, cellular base station, and Wi-Fi
- Built for low-power operation across Cat.M1 and NB-IoT networks
- Temperature and humidity sensing for condition-sensitive transport
- Tamper and unpacking alerts that support cargo protection
- BLE 5.0 support for broader device interaction in compatible workflows

## How It Works with Plaspy

With Plaspy, the GM100 can be used as a shipment and asset visibility device that sends location and condition data into a single monitoring platform. This makes it easier to follow goods in transit, review movement history, and react to alerts when conditions change during transport.

- Track package and pallet movement in near real time within Plaspy
- Review historical location records to understand route progress and delivery timing
- Monitor temperature and humidity data for sensitive shipments
- Receive tamper, unpacking, and low battery alerts as operational events

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain logistics where temperature and humidity visibility are important
- Shipment protection for high value goods that require discreet monitoring
- Pallet and package tracking during regional or long distance transport
- Temporary asset tracking for reusable containers and other movable cargo
- Operational oversight for logistics teams that need cargo level visibility in Plaspy
