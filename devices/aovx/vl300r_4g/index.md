The AOVX VL300R - (4G) is a robust wired vehicle GPS tracker built for fleet operators and integrators that need dependable location visibility and broader telemetry support. It is designed for demanding vehicle installations and combines multi-constellation GNSS, internal antennas, LTE FDD and GSM connectivity, and onboard memory for large numbers of position records. That makes it a practical option for organizations looking for a durable tracking device for fleet management, anti-theft workflows, and vehicle telematics.

This model is also relevant for users searching for an AOVX VL300R - (4G) GPS tracker that works with Plaspy. Because Plaspy can use tracking data, alerts, and event-driven telemetry from compatible devices, the VL300R - (4G) fits well in environments where real-time oversight, driver identification, and operational control matter. For teams standardizing on Plaspy tracking software, it offers a useful balance of connectivity, memory, and interface flexibility.
