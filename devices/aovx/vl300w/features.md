The VL300W is presented as a professional-grade GPS tracker for demanding fleet management and asset monitoring applications. In Plaspy, that translates into practical visibility for vehicles, cargo, and connected telemetry sources, with a focus on real-time tracking, historical review, and event-based oversight.

The AOVX VL300W features are centered on dependable tracking, flexible device integration, and operational monitoring. For organizations evaluating AOVX VL300W GPS tracker features, the most relevant value is its combination of location visibility and support for external sensors and control functions in a compact wired form factor.

- Real-time tracking for vehicles and monitored assets
- Support for fleet and cargo visibility in operational environments
- Flexible I/O for connecting external devices and status signals
- Support for Bluetooth sensors and wired telemetry sources
