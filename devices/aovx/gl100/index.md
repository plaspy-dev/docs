# AOVX - GL100

<div className="device-logo">
![GL100](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AOVX GL100 is a compact GPS asset tracker designed for long-term monitoring of merchandise, packages, and vehicle cargo. It is built for deployments where installation-free tracking is important and where location data may need to be combined with environmental information such as temperature and humidity. With GNSS positioning, Wi-Fi based location assistance, and onboard sensing, it is aimed at practical tracking scenarios rather than heavy vehicle telematics.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GL100 can feed location and condition data into Plaspy for monitoring, alerting, and reporting. That makes it relevant for organizations looking for AOVX GL100 tracking software that supports fleet visibility, anti-theft workflows, and condition-aware delivery oversight in one platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for location tracking, alerts, and reporting in one workspace
- Designed for long-term asset monitoring with an installation-free form factor
- Supports GNSS positioning with Wi-Fi assistance for broader tracking use
- Includes onboard temperature and humidity sensing for condition monitoring
- Suitable for merchandise, packages, and vehicle cargo
- Built for unattended use with power-aware reporting behavior
- Offers deferred data upload through onboard storage for offline periods

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GL100 can contribute both position information and environmental telemetry to a centralized tracking view. This helps teams follow assets over time, review movement history, and respond to alerts based on reporting patterns or sensed conditions. In practice, Plaspy turns the tracker data into operational visibility that supports logistics, asset protection, and fleet management tasks.

- Display tracked assets on Plaspy maps for live or periodic visibility
- Review historical movement and reporting activity in Plaspy reports
- Use alerting workflows for location changes or condition events
- Monitor temperature and humidity readings for sensitive shipments
- Support anti-theft and loss-prevention processes with timely updates
- Use stored records for offline periods when connectivity is temporarily unavailable

## Typical Use Cases

- Parcel and package monitoring for logistics operations
