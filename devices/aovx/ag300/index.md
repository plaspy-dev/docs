# AOVX - AG300

<div className="device-logo">
![AG300](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AOVX AG300 is an industrial asset tracker designed for reliable location visibility in demanding environments. It combines multi-mode positioning with a rugged build, making it suitable for organizations that need to track valuable equipment, containers, or other assets across both indoor and outdoor settings.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AG300 fits into workflows that depend on continuous location monitoring, alerting, and operational oversight. It is relevant for teams looking for an AOVX AG300 GPS tracker that can support fleet tracking, asset recovery, and long-term visibility through Plaspy tracking software.

## Key Highlights

- Multi-mode positioning helps provide location visibility across varied environments, including areas where satellite positioning alone may be limited.
- Industrial-grade design supports demanding asset tracking scenarios and long-term use.
- Rugged IP68 protection adds confidence for outdoor and harsh-environment deployments.
- Motion, vibration, and tamper alerts support event-driven monitoring for valuable assets.
- BLE 5.0 support allows the tracker to work with external Bluetooth sensors in compatible use cases.
- Ultra-low power design supports low-maintenance tracking over extended periods.
- Optional strong magnetic mounting can be useful for metal assets and portable equipment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the AG300 contributes location and event data to a platform built for monitoring, visibility, and reporting. This makes it useful for organizations that want to follow assets in near real time, review movement history, and respond quickly to abnormal activity.

- Plaspy can display the AG300 in live tracking views to support ongoing location awareness.
- Location history in Plaspy helps teams review routes, movement patterns, and past activity.
- Motion, vibration, and tamper events can support alerts for security-focused monitoring.
- Plaspy reporting tools can help turn tracker data into operational insights for asset control and oversight.
- Indoor and outdoor positioning support can improve visibility in mixed environments where assets move between open areas and covered locations.
- BLE sensor support can extend the tracker’s usefulness in deployments that rely on external sensor data alongside location tracking.

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking high-value equipment that moves between job sites, storage areas, and transport routes
- Monitoring containers and other durable assets in logistics or yard operations
- Supporting asset recovery workflows when movement or tamper activity needs attention
- Improving visibility for mixed indoor and outdoor asset deployments
- Helping organizations manage low-maintenance tracking for long-duration use cases
- Adding location context to sensor-based asset monitoring programs
