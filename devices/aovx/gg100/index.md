# AOVX - GG100

<div className="device-logo">
![GG100](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AOVX GG100 is an ultra-thin goods GPS tracker built for deployment in logistics and supply-chain environments where installation-free asset tracking is important. It is designed for shipments, pallets, cartons, and containers, with a focus on practical visibility rather than complex vehicle telematics.

For organizations using Plaspy, the GG100 is relevant because it is explicitly compatible with the platform and is suited to large-scale tracking scenarios. That makes it a useful option for teams looking for AOVX GG100 tracking software that can centralize location data, environmental monitoring, and operational oversight in one place.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for centralized monitoring and reporting
- Ultra-thin, installation-free design for easy placement on goods and assets
- Multi-mode positioning with GPS, Beidou, base station, and Wi-Fi support
- Built-in temperature and humidity sensing for sensitive cargo visibility
- Long standby life for extended transit and storage monitoring
- Tamper and unpacking alarm for added chain-of-custody awareness
- Recyclable construction suited to high-volume logistics use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GG100 can contribute position and condition data to a single tracking environment, helping teams follow goods through transit, storage, and handoff points. Its mix of location methods supports broader visibility in environments where a single positioning source may not always be enough.

- View shipment and asset locations in Plaspy for day-to-day monitoring
- Review historical movement records to better understand transit behavior
- Receive alerts related to tamper events or unpacking conditions
- Track temperature and humidity trends for goods that need environmental oversight
- Support logistics workflows that require scalable visibility across many assets
- Combine tracker data with Plaspy reports to improve operational control

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain logistics where temperature and humidity visibility matter
- Pallet tracking across warehouses, depots, and transit routes
- Container monitoring for high-value or sensitive goods
- Shipment oversight for large logistics and supply-chain operations
- Asset visibility for temporary or reusable transport packaging
- Chain-of-custody monitoring where tamper awareness is useful
