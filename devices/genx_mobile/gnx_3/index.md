# GenX Mobile - GNX-3

<div className="device-logo">
![GNX-3](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The GenX Mobile GNX-3 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for a wide range of tracking needs, from vehicle fleets to mobile resource management. Built with configurable settings and high quality components, the GNX-3 delivers reliable and accurate real time location tracking. It includes an auto calibrating 3 axis accelerometer to monitor events such as rapid acceleration and harsh driving, offering deeper visibility into movement and behavior.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GNX-3 can be used to feed location and event data into Plaspy's fleet management environment. Its configurable nature makes it a practical choice for organizations that need to tailor reporting and alerts, while the device's emphasis on reliability and accurate positioning aligns with Plaspy features for monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Highly configurable to suit diverse tracking and reporting requirements
- Real time and accurate location tracking for mobile resources and vehicles
- Auto calibrating 3 axis accelerometer for detecting driving events and behavior
- Designed specifically for mobile resource management and vehicle tracking
- Built with high performance internal cellular and GPS antennas for stable connectivity
- Suitable for businesses that require both tracking precision and operational flexibility

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GNX-3 supplies location and event information that Plaspy presents through dashboards, alerts, and reports. Plaspy ingests the device data to provide fleet managers and operators practical visibility into asset movement and key events.

- Live location tracking and breadcrumb history for route review and playback
- Event reporting based on accelerometer detected events to support driver behavior analysis
- Configurable alerts and notifications for activity thresholds and operational exceptions
- Consolidated reporting and analytics to support performance reviews and compliance
- Operational oversight tools such as asset assignment and status visualization within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for daily operations and route monitoring
- Mobile resource management for service and field teams
- Driver behavior insight and safety monitoring using accelerometer events
- Asset tracking where configurable reporting and reliable positioning are needed
- Operational reporting and historical playback for performance analysis
