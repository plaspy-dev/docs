# GenX Mobile - GNX-5P

![GNX-5P](./logo.jpg)

The GenX Mobile GNX-5P is a highly versatile and configurable mobile asset tracking device designed for a broad range of location aware applications. It is positioned for Mobile Resource Management and vehicle tracking use cases, and includes features such as HSPA wireless connectivity, internal cellular and GPS antennas, and an auto calibrating 3 axis accelerometer to capture rapid acceleration and other movement events. The device emphasizes reliability and adaptability, making it suitable for organizations that need consistent location visibility across mixed fleets and mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GNX-5P can feed location and event data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight. Its configurable reporting and geofencing capabilities align with Plaspy workflows for real time visibility, while the built in accelerometer provides additional event signals that Plaspy can use to surface driver behavior and safety related alerts. Together, the GNX-5P and Plaspy create a flexible tracking solution that can be adapted to many operational needs.

## Key Highlights

- HSPA wireless connectivity for modern mobile network compatibility
- Internal cellular and GPS antennas for consistent signal reception
- Auto calibrating 3 axis accelerometer for monitoring rapid acceleration and related events
- High configurability to support real time tracking, geofencing, and custom reporting
- Designed for mobile asset and vehicle tracking across diverse use cases
- User oriented interface and configurable options for easier management

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GNX-5P supplies location and event information that Plaspy normalizes and presents for fleet and asset management workflows. Plaspy uses those inputs to provide visibility, alerts, and historical reporting tailored to operational needs.

- Live location visibility and map based tracking for vehicles and mobile assets
- Event driven alerts including movement and behavior signals derived from accelerometer data
- Geofence monitoring and notifications for entry and exit events
- Historical route and activity reporting to support analysis and compliance
- Customizable dashboards and reports that incorporate GNX-5P telemetry for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and dispatch coordination
- Mobile resource management for field teams and equipment
- Driver behavior monitoring for safety and training programs
- Route history and delivery oversight for logistics operations
- General location aware services for asset visibility and recovery
