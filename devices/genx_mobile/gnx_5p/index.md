# GenX Mobile - GNX-5P

![GNX-5P](./logo.jpg)

The GenX Mobile GNX-5P is a highly versatile and customizable mobile asset tracking device that caters to a wide range of market and industry needs. Whether you require Mobile Resource Management, vehicle tracking, or any other location-aware applications and services, the GNX-5P is the ideal solution. It boasts state-of-the-art HSPA wireless technology, ensuring compatibility with the latest wireless network architecture.

One of the standout features of the GNX-5P is its superior reliability. It comes equipped with internal cellular and GPS antennas, ensuring optimal signal strength and accuracy. Additionally, it features an auto-calibrating 3-axis accelerometer, which allows for monitoring and reporting of rapid acceleration, deceleration, harsh cornering, and other events. This feature is particularly useful for fleet management, as it provides valuable insights into driver behavior and vehicle performance.

The GNX-5P is designed to be highly configurable, allowing you to tailor its functionality to your specific requirements. Whether you need real-time tracking, geofencing capabilities, or customized reporting, the GNX-5P can be easily customized to meet your needs. Its user-friendly interface and intuitive software make it easy to set up and manage, even for those with limited technical expertise.
