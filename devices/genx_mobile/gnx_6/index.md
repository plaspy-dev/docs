# GenX Mobile - GNX-6

![GNX-6](./logo.jpg)

The GenX Mobile GNX-6 is a versatile GPS tracker designed to serve a range of tracking needs from vehicle monitoring to mobile resource management. Built with configurable options and a focus on reliable performance, the GNX-6 combines robust hardware and calibrated motion detection to provide accurate location and event awareness for many commercial and industrial applications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GNX-6 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet and asset management environment to provide unified tracking, alerts, and reporting. Its configurable nature and on board motion sensing make it a practical choice for organizations that want to extend Plaspy visibility to diverse vehicles and mobile assets while keeping deployment flexible.

## Key Highlights

- Versatile design suitable for vehicle tracking and broader mobile resource management
- Highly configurable to adapt to different operational and reporting requirements
- Robust construction with high performance internal cellular and GPS antennas for reliable reception
- Auto calibrating 3 axis accelerometer to detect rapid acceleration deceleration and harsh events
- Practical for fleets and mixed asset environments where consistency and uptime matter
- Supports event driven monitoring that complements location based visibility

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GNX-6 provides location and event data to Plaspy's monitoring and reporting tools, enabling centralized oversight of mobile resources. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to present location history, alerts, and operational insights that help teams manage vehicles and assets more effectively.

- Real time and near real time location visibility within Plaspy dashboards
- Fleet monitoring and status overview to track asset movement and availability
- Configurable alerts for movement and accelerometer detected events such as hard braking or sudden impacts
- Historical reporting and route playback to support analysis and operational review
- Operational oversight features such as geofence alerts and activity summaries for dispatch and management

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle fleet tracking for logistics and delivery operations
- Mobile asset management for equipment and off site resources
- Monitoring driver behavior and events for safety programs
- Field service fleet visibility for scheduling and dispatch efficiency
- Asset security and recovery readiness for high value items

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GNX-6 pairs practical configurability with robust reception and motion sensing, making it a sensible option for organizations that need adaptable tracking hardware. Its ability to report motion events in addition to location supports safety and operational programs without requiring specialized additional devices.

Choosing the GNX-6 with Plaspy can simplify fleet and asset oversight by combining dependable hardware with a platform designed for monitoring, alerts, and reporting. For teams that require flexible configuration and clear visibility across vehicles and mobile resources, this combination offers a straightforward path to better operational control.
