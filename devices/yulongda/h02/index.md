# YulongDa - H02

![H02](./logo.jpg)

The YulongDa H02 GPS tracking unit is a reliable and efficient device that utilizes the Global Positioning System \(GPS\) to accurately determine the location of vehicles, individuals, or other assets to which it is attached. With its advanced technology, this tracker records the position of the asset at regular intervals, providing valuable data for tracking and monitoring purposes.

Equipped with a cellular modem \(GPRS or SMS\) or a satellite modem, the YulongDa H02 can transmit the recorded location data to a central database or an internet-connected computer. This enables users to view the asset's location in real-time or analyze the track later using a GPS tracking platform. Whether you need to keep an eye on your fleet of vehicles, track the movements of a loved one, or monitor valuable assets, the YulongDa H02 GPS tracking unit is an ideal solution.

Key Features:

- Precise GPS location tracking
- Regular interval recording of asset's position
- Cellular or satellite modem for data transmission
- Real-time location display on GPS tracking platform
- Efficient and reliable tracking performance
