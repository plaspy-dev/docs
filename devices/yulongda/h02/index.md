# YulongDa - H02

<div className="device-logo">
![H02](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The YulongDa H02 is a GPS tracking unit designed to determine and record the location of vehicles, people, or other assets. It captures position data at regular intervals and stores those records for real time monitoring or later analysis, making it suitable for a variety of tracking and oversight needs.

This model can transmit recorded locations using either a cellular modem such as GPRS or SMS or via a satellite modem, enabling the H02 to report position data to a central platform. Because the H02 is compatible with Plaspy, organizations can view live locations and historical tracks inside Plaspy for fleet oversight and asset monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Precise GPS location tracking for vehicles people and assets
- Regular interval recording to support historical route analysis
- Options for cellular or satellite data transmission to reach remote areas
- Real time location visibility through a compatible tracking platform
- Reliable and efficient performance for continuous monitoring
- Suitable for mixed fleets and dispersed asset deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the YulongDa H02 sends location updates to the Plaspy platform where those updates appear as live positions and historical tracks. Plaspy ingests the transmitted data to provide visibility and operational tools that help teams monitor movement and performance.

- Live map view of current device locations and status
- Playback of recorded tracks to review routes and timing
- Alerting and notification support based on device reports and configured conditions
- Reporting and export of location history for analysis and record keeping
- Operational oversight for dispatching and route verification

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for route monitoring and utilization review
- Asset protection and recovery for high value portable equipment
- Field staff tracking to improve dispatch and worker coordination
- Remote or off grid asset monitoring using satellite transmission
- Route auditing and historical movement analysis for compliance or optimization

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The H02 offers a straightforward profile focused on accurate position reporting and flexible data transmission. For organizations already using Plaspy the H02 can serve as a practical device choice when the goal is consistent location visibility and reliable record keeping across vehicles or mobile assets.

Because the model supports both cellular and satellite transmission methods it can fit scenarios that require coverage in urban areas and in more remote locations. Pairing the H02 with Plaspy gives teams a unified view of location data and the tools needed for monitoring, alerts, and reporting without requiring complex device specific configuration.
