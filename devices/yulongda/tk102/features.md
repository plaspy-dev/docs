# YulongDa - TK102 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the YulongDa TK102 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to monitoring, mapping, and operational oversight in Plaspy while keeping the discussion at a product and integration level suitable for public documentation.

Exact feature availability for any individual TK102 unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and the way the device is installed. Always crosscheck specific behavior against the manufacturer documentation and the device configuration in your deployment.

## Feature Overview

The YulongDa TK102 is a compact GSM/GPRS GPS tracker designed for straightforward location tracking across a range of applications. Its small size and battery powered operation make it useful where portability or discreet installation is required, while its GPS performance supports reliable position reporting for Plaspy.

- Compact and lightweight form factor for flexible placement and easy installation
- Multi band GSM/GPRS support to work with common mobile networks in different regions
- SIRF3 GPS receiver with high sensitivity and reported position accuracy around 5 meters
- Rechargeable 3.7V 800mAh battery with stated standby and continuous operation figures
- Included charging accessories for vehicle and mains charging to support varied use scenarios

## Core Features of YulongDa - TK102

- Compact dimensions (64 mm x 46 mm x 17 mm) and low weight for portable and discreet use
- GSM/GPRS network connectivity across commonly deployed frequency bands for wide coverage
- SIRF3 GPS chipset with high sensitivity for stable satellite reception
- Reported GPS accuracy on the order of 5 meters under typical conditions
- Rechargeable 3.7V 800mAh Li ion battery with extended standby capability and multiple charging options
- Operating temperature range and storage range suitable for a variety of environments
- Designed humidity tolerance suitable for noncondensing environments
- Compatibility with Plaspy for reporting and visualization of location data

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives compatible device reports and presents location and device presence information on a centralized platform. For TK102 units, the practical result is that the device's positioning and connectivity characteristics translate into visible location updates and tracking history inside Plaspy.

- Real time and periodic location updates from the TK102 appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring
- Historical position playback lets teams review movement and time stamped location records
- Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols and accepts device connections using the platform's standard port configuration
- Reporting frequency and actual runtime depend on the device firmware and the chosen configuration, which affects how often Plaspy receives position updates
- Plaspy aggregates device presence and movement into fleet views and device lists for operational oversight
