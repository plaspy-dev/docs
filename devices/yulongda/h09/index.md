# YulongDa - H09

<div className="device-logo">
![H09](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The YulongDa H09 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for real time tracking and monitoring across a wide range of vehicles. It uses GSM quad band connectivity for broad geographic coverage and accepts a wide input voltage range, making it suitable for different vehicle electrical systems. The device also includes practical security and operational features such as a built in vibration sensor for anti theft alerts, ACC ignition detection, an external SOS button, a backup battery, and the option to control vehicle power via an external relay.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the H09 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide unified visibility and management of tracked vehicles. When used with Plaspy, the tracker’s position updates and alarm signals can feed into dashboards, alerts, and reporting workflows so fleet managers and operators can monitor status, respond to incidents, and keep operational records in one platform.

## Key Highlights

- GSM quad band support for broad regional coverage
- Wide input voltage range DC 9 to 24V for use in many vehicle types
- Built in vibration sensor and movement alarm for theft detection
- ACC ignition detection to report vehicle on off status
- External SOS button and backup battery for emergency alerts and continued reporting
- External relay plus microphone and loudspeaker for remote immobilization options and audio monitoring uses

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected through Plaspy, the H09 delivers location updates and alarm events into the Plaspy platform so teams can track asset movement, receive alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy consolidates the device data alongside other fleet information to simplify monitoring and operational oversight.

- Real time location tracking visible on Plaspy maps and vehicle lists
- Alarm forwarding for vibration movement alerts, SOS presses, and power loss notifications
- Vehicle status updates such as ignition on off events for activity monitoring
- Historical route and event reporting for audits and operational review
- Remote control actions where supported by hardware such as relay commands managed from Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small and medium commercial fleets
- Anti theft monitoring for passenger cars and light commercial vehicles
- Emergency alerting for personnel or drivers using the SOS button
- Rental car or shared vehicle oversight with ignition and movement monitoring
- Logistics and delivery visibility for route tracking and exception handling

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The H09 is a practical option for organizations that need a balance of global GSM coverage and core vehicle security features. Its combination of vibration detection, ignition reporting, SOS capability, and a backup power source makes it useful where continuous monitoring and timely alerts are important. Because the device accepts a wide voltage range and is relatively compact, it fits a variety of vehicle types and deployment scenarios.

Pairing the H09 with Plaspy helps centralize vehicle visibility and alerting into a single platform. Plaspy can aggregate the H09 signals with other fleet data to support monitoring, reporting, and operational response, enabling teams to act on movement alerts, emergency signals, and status changes more efficiently.
