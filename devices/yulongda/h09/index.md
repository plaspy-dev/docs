# YulongDa - H09

![H09](./logo.jpg)

The YulongDa H09 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring for a wide range of vehicles. With its GSM quad-band frequency, it can be used globally, ensuring that you can track your vehicle no matter where it is. The tracker has a wide input voltage range of DC 9-24V, making it compatible with various types of vehicles.

One of the standout features of the YulongDa H09 is its built-in vibration sensor, which acts as a smart anti-theft system. It detects any unauthorized movement or tampering with the vehicle and sends an alert to the owner. Additionally, the tracker has ACC ignition detection, allowing you to receive notifications about the vehicle's status, such as when the engine is turned on or off.

In case of emergencies, the YulongDa H09 is equipped with an external SOS button, which can be used to send distress signals. The tracker also has a built-in backup battery, ensuring that it can continue to function and send alarms even if the main power is cut off illegally. With the external relay, you can remotely cut off the engine power or oil, providing an extra layer of security.

Other notable features of the YulongDa H09 include an external microphone for voice monitoring, an external loudspeaker for phone calls or alarms, and a movement alarm that alerts you when the vehicle is in motion without authorization. With its compact dimensions of 90.0\(L\)\*45.0\(W\)\*6.0\(H\)mm and lightweight design of 45g, the tracker can be easily installed and concealed in your vehicle.

### Key Features:

- GSM quad-band frequency for global use
- Wide input voltage range of DC 9-24V
- Built-in vibration sensor for smart anti-theft
- ACC ignition detection for vehicle status notification
- External SOS button for emergency situations
- Built-in backup battery for illegal power cut-off alarms
- External relay for remote engine power or oil cut-off
- External microphone for voice monitoring
- External loudspeaker for phone calls or alarms
- Movement alarm for unauthorized vehicle motion

### Technical Specifications:

- GSM frequency: 900/1800MHz or 850/1900MHz
- GPRS: Class12, TCP/IP
- Voltage range: DC 9-24V
- Standby Current: ≈0.2mA
- Power Consumption: 0.3w/h
- GPS initialization: Cold boot 38s \(Open sky\), Warm boot 2s \(Open sky\)
- GPS Accuracy: 10m \(2D RM\)
- Working Temperature: -2℃ - +70℃
- Working Humidity: 20%-80%RH
- Dimension: 90.0\(L\)\*45.0\(W\)\*6.0\(H\)mm
- Net weight: 45g
