# YulongDa - H08

![H08](./logo.jpg)

The YulongDa H08 is a compact GPS tracker built for real time vehicle location and basic security monitoring. It supports quad band GSM for global cellular coverage and operates across a wide input voltage range of DC 9 to 24V, making it suitable for a variety of cars and commercial vehicles. The device includes features commonly required for vehicle tracking such as a vibration sensor for anti theft alerts, ACC ignition detection to report vehicle status, a small internal backup battery to alert on main power cut off, and an external relay option for remote engine or fuel cut control.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the H08 can feed location updates and event alerts into the Plaspy fleet and asset management platform. When paired with Plaspy, the H08 becomes part of a monitored fleet environment where its position, movement events, power status, and tamper alerts contribute to real time visibility, operational oversight, and reporting. This compatibility makes the H08 a practical choice for organizations that want a discreet tracker with essential security features integrated into Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Quad band GSM support for broad cellular coverage in many regions
- Wide input voltage range DC 9 to 24V for flexible vehicle fit
- Optional built in vibration sensor for smart anti theft alerts
- ACC ignition detection to report engine or ignition status
- Internal backup battery and main power cut off alarm for continued monitoring
- External relay capability to allow remote engine or fuel cut control where configured
- Compact form factor and lightweight design suitable for discreet installation

## How It Works with Plaspy

The H08 delivers periodic GPS fixes and event notifications to Plaspy over the device cellular link. Plaspy ingests those location and status messages and presents them on maps, timelines, and in configurable alert rules so fleet managers can monitor vehicles and respond to incidents.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for live tracking
- Vibration and tamper alerts routed into Plaspy for theft detection and response
- Ignition state events visible in Plaspy to support operational insights and usage reporting
- Power loss and backup battery alerts forwarded to Plaspy for continuity monitoring
- Relay control integrated where supported to enable remote immobilization commands through Plaspy subject to local setup and permissions
- Historical tracking and basic reporting available in Plaspy for route review and operational analysis

- Anti theft monitoring and alerting for private cars or service vehicles

- Field service and utility vehicles needing basic telematics and event reporting
