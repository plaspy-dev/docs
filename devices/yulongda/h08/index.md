# YulongDa - H08

![H08](./logo.jpg)

The YulongDa H08 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device designed to provide accurate and real-time tracking of vehicles. With its GSM quad-band frequency, it can be used globally, ensuring that you can track your vehicle no matter where it is. The wide input voltage range of DC 9-24V allows for easy installation in a variety of vehicles, making it suitable for both personal and commercial use.

One of the standout features of the YulongDa H08 is its built-in vibration sensor, which provides smart anti-theft functionality. This optional feature detects any unauthorized movement or tampering with the vehicle and sends an alert to the owner. Additionally, the ACC ignition detection feature notifies the owner of the vehicle's status, ensuring that you are always aware of its whereabouts.

The YulongDa H08 also comes with a built-in backup battery, which supports illegal main power cut-off alarms. This means that even if the main power is cut off, the tracker will continue to function and send alerts, providing an added layer of security. Furthermore, the external relay can be used to remotely cut off engine power or oil, allowing for additional control over the vehicle.

With its compact dimensions of 50.0\(L\)\*35.0\(W\)\*10.0\(H\)mm and lightweight design of 26g, the YulongDa H08 is discreet and easy to install. It operates in a wide temperature range of -2℃ to +70℃ and can withstand humidity levels of 20%-80%RH, ensuring reliable performance in various conditions.

Technical Specifications:

- GSM frequency: 900/1800MHz or 850/1900MHz
- GPRS: Class12, TCP/IP
- Voltage range: DC 9-24V
- Standby Current: ≈0.2mA
- Power Consumption: 0.3w/h
- GPS initialization: Cold boot 38s \(Open sky\), Warm boot 2s \(Open sky\)
- GPS Accuracy: 10m\(2D RM\)
- Working Temperature: -2℃ - +70℃
- Working Humidity: 20%-80%RH
- Dimension: 50.0\(L\)\*35.0\(W\)\*10.0\(H\)mm
- Net weight: 26g
