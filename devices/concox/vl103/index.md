# Concox - VL103

![VL103](./logo..png)

The Concox VL103M is a compact yet powerful GPS tracker designed for industrial and commercial fleet applications. Whether you're managing a rental agency, public transportation, or freight transport, this device is packed with features to enhance the security and efficiency of your fleet. One standout feature is the external buzzer/horn alarm, which makes it easy to locate your vehicle in case you can't find it. This added security measure ensures that you can quickly and easily locate your fleet, even in crowded or unfamiliar areas.

In addition to the external buzzer/horn alarm, the VL103M offers a range of other features to support your fleet management strategy. The device supports GPS, BDS, and LBS positioning systems, ensuring accurate location tracking that is displayed on the cloud platform. It also features a speeding buzzer and supports audible/light warnings to alert drivers of speeding or fatigue driving. This helps promote safe driving practices and can contribute to reducing accidents and improving overall fleet performance.

Another valuable feature of the VL103M is the remote listen-in capability. With an inconspicuous microphone, you can remotely monitor the audio environment around the device. This can be useful for ensuring driver safety, detecting potential issues, or even for security purposes. Additionally, the device is IP66 dust and water-resistant, making it rugged and durable enough to withstand tough conditions.

#### Key Features:

- GPS, BDS, and LBS positioning for accurate location tracking
- External buzzer/horn alarm for easy vehicle location
- Speeding buzzer and audible/light warnings for driver safety
- Low battery alert to monitor motorcycle battery voltage
- Multiple alerts for atypical events such as abnormal vibration and geo-fence entry/exit
- Remote listen-in capability for audio monitoring
- IP66 dust and water resistance for durability
