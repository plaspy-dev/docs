# Concox - VL103

<div className="device-logo">
![VL103](./logo..png)
</div>

The Concox VL103M is a compact GPS tracker built for industrial and commercial fleet use. It combines multi constellation positioning with a set of practical features aimed at vehicle security and driver safety, including an external buzzer or horn alarm to help locate vehicles, audible and light warnings for speeding or fatigue events, and remote listen in functionality. The unit is designed to be rugged in the field with IP66 dust and water resistance, making it suitable for demanding fleet environments.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the VL103 can feed location data and event alerts into Plaspy's fleet management platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy users can use the VL103 to monitor vehicle location, receive configurable alerts, and include device data in reporting and dispatch workflows without complex additional hardware changes.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and purpose built for industrial and commercial fleet applications
- Multi constellation positioning for consistent location tracking
- External buzzer or horn alarm for rapid vehicle locating in busy areas
- Speeding buzzer and audible or light warnings to encourage safer driving
- Remote listen in capability for monitoring on demand where permitted
- Multiple alerts including low battery, abnormal vibration, and geo fence events
- IP66 rated for dust and water resistance in harsh conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the VL103 streams location and event information to the platform so fleet operators can view and act on device data from a single interface. Plaspy aggregates the tracker's position and alerts alongside other fleet devices, enabling unified monitoring and reporting.

- Real time location display and map history for route review and playback
- Configurable alerts delivered through Plaspy for speeding vibration geo fence and low battery events
- Use audible alarm events and remote listen in indicators to support vehicle recovery and driver oversight
- Fleet level dashboards and reports that include VL103 data for operational review
- Event based workflows and notifications to help dispatch and maintenance teams respond quickly

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental vehicle fleets where fast vehicle locating reduces downtime and improves customer service
- Public transportation monitoring to track vehicle movement and manage schedules
- Freight and logistics fleets needing consistent location visibility across routes
- Motorcycle or small vehicle fleets benefiting from low battery and vibration alerts
- Security focused deployments where audible locating and remote monitoring support rapid response

The VL103 pairs practical in field features with rugged design, making it a sensible option for organizations that need reliable position tracking and straightforward alarm functions. Its combination of locating aids and event alerts aligns well with the operational needs of rental agencies public transit and freight operators who use Plaspy for centralized fleet oversight.
