# Concox - VL103 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the Concox VL103 tracker and how its features relate to use with Plaspy. It describes the practical capabilities you can expect to see in the Plaspy platform and highlights how the device’s primary functions support fleet monitoring, safety alerts, and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where a capability depends on firmware or configuration (for example remote listen in or audible warnings), this page notes the behavior in general terms and encourages checking official manufacturer documentation for the most current device specifics.

## Feature Overview

The Concox VL103 is a compact industrial grade tracker designed for commercial fleets and motorcycles with a focus on locating assets, driver safety alerts, and rugged field use. In Plaspy, the device can be used to display location, surface alerts, and support remote controls where the device firmware and configuration allow.

- Multi system positioning support including GPS, BDS, and LBS for improved location visibility
- External buzzer or horn alarm to help locate vehicles in crowded areas
- Speeding buzzer and audible or light warnings to alert drivers and promote safer driving behavior
- Remote listen in capability via the device microphone for situational awareness when enabled
- IP66 dust and water resistance for reliable operation in demanding environments
- Alerts for low battery, abnormal vibration, and geo-fence entry and exit events

## Core Features of Concox - VL103

- GPS BDS and LBS positioning to provide practical location reporting for mapping and tracking
- External buzzer or horn alarm that can be used to audibly locate a vehicle
- Speeding buzzer and audible or light warnings designed to prompt driver attention
- Remote listen in via an integrated microphone for audio monitoring when supported
- IP66 rating for dust and water resistance to suit outdoor and vehicle installations
- Low battery alert to signal declining vehicle battery voltage
- Alerts for abnormal vibration and geo-fence entry and exit to identify unusual events

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests device reports and presents location and event information in a cloud dashboard so operations teams can monitor fleets and respond to alerts. The VL103 can be configured and used with Plaspy to surface location, events, and supported remote functions depending on device firmware and setup.

- Real time location and positioning shown on the Plaspy map using the tracker’s GPS BDS and LBS reports
- Alerts such as speeding buzzer events, low battery, vibration, and geo-fence transitions appear as platform events for easy filtering and notification
- Where firmware and configuration allow, Plaspy can show device capabilities related to audible warnings and remote buzzer activation for operational use
- Remote listen in is represented as a device capability; Plaspy can indicate availability and trigger actions per device configuration and permissions
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and accepts reports from compatible devices when the tracker is pointed to the Plaspy server address and configured transport

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental and shared vehicle fleets that need quick vehicle location using the external buzzer feature
- Public transport and shuttle services monitoring vehicle location and driver warnings
- Freight and delivery operations tracking assets across routes and responding to geo-fence events
- Driver safety programs using audible warnings and speeding alerts to encourage safer behavior
- Security monitoring where remote listen in and vibration alerts support incident assessment
- Maintenance and operations teams alerted by low battery notifications to prevent service disruptions
