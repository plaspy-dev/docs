# Concox - JM-VG01U

![JM-VG01U](./tracker.png)

The JM-VG01U is a vehicle GNSS tracker from Concox designed for professional fleet management, usage based insurance, and auto finance applications. It pairs inertial navigation support with multi constellation GNSS and LBS assistance to provide continuous positioning and enriched telemetry suitable for real time monitoring and analytics.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JM-VG01U integrates its location, motion and input event telemetry into Plaspy for centralized visibility. Fleets and program operators can use the device feed in Plaspy to receive alerts, view accurate mileage, monitor driving behavior, and initiate immobilizer actions when needed.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for fleet, insurance, and finance programs.
- INS aided positioning combined with GNSS and LBS assistance for more reliable fixes in difficult environments.
- Six axis motion sensing to detect a range of driving behaviors for safety analytics.
- Accurate mileage reporting useful for billing and usage based insurance programs.
- Digital inputs and relay output for ignition detection, SOS events, and remote immobilizer control.
- Vehicle grade durability with a compact enclosure suitable for field deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the JM-VG01U streams GNSS positions, INS derived motion data, and input events over the cellular link to Plaspy back end systems. Plaspy ingests and normalizes that telemetry to present live location on maps, trigger configurable alerts, and generate historical reports for fleet oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for live tracking and geofence monitoring.
- Ignition and SOS events map to trip and driver records for rapid notification and incident handling.
- Mileage telemetry feeds utilization and billing reports to support usage based workflows.
- Relay based immobilizer output can be controlled from Plaspy for anti theft response when supported by device setup.
- Driving behavior events are surfaced as safety alerts and analytics to assist risk management.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and recovery workflows using SOS alerts and remote immobilizer capability.
