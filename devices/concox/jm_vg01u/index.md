# Concox - JM-VG01U

![JM-VG01U](./tracker.png)

The JM-VG01U is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for professional fleet management, usage-based insurance, and auto finance applications. Built as an INS-aided vehicle GNSS tracker, the JM-VG01U combines inertial navigation, multi-constellation GNSS, and LBS assistance to deliver continuous, reliable positioning and rich telemetry for real-time tracking and analytics.

Designed to integrate smoothly with Plaspy, this compact vehicle tracker delivers high-accuracy location, driving-behavior detection, and anti-theft control via a relay immobilizer. Fleets and fleet managers using Plaspy benefit from immediate alerts, precise mileage reporting, and actionable driver insights to reduce risk and optimize operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and centralized fleet management.
- INS-aided positioning with LBS assistance for reliable location in GPS-challenged environments.
- 6-axis motion sensing \(3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyroscope\) to detect eight unsafe driving behaviors.
- Accurate mileage calculation \(error &lt; 2%\)—useful for telemetry, billing, and usage-based insurance.
- Integrated ignition \(ACC\) detection, in-cabin SOS button, and relay-based remote cut-off \(immobilizer\) for anti-theft control.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and wide operating temperature range for vehicle and field deployments.
- Compact form factor with Micro-SIM GSM quad-band connectivity for broad cellular coverage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the JM-VG01U streams GNSS positions, INS-derived motion data, and digital input events through the cellular network to Plaspy’s backend. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and translates it into real-time location on maps, safety events, and fleet reports. This integration supports live monitoring, configurable alerts, and historical analytics for fleet managers.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered via GSM to Plaspy for live tracking and geofencing.
- Ignition \(ACC\) detection and in-cabin SOS events mapped to driver and trip records for immediate alerts.
- Accurate mileage telemetry \(error &lt; 2%\) feeds fuel and utilization reports—helping with fuel monitoring strategies when combined with vehicle sensors and Plaspy reporting.
- Remote immobilizer support via relay output for anti-theft actions directly from Plaspy dashboards.
- Driving behavior events \(harsh acceleration, braking, cornering, sudden lane change, collision, skidding, rollover, roll & pitch\) are forwarded to Plaspy as actionable safety alerts and analytics.
