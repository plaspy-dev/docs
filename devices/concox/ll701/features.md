# Concox - LL701 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive feature overview of the Concox LL701 and how its capabilities are typically used with Plaspy for tracking and asset management. It focuses on user facing functions and operational value without disclosing low level protocol details. The LL701 is compatible with Plaspy and can be used to monitor long standby asset trackers within the platform.

Exact behavior and feature availability for the LL701 can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a practical reference for what the device is designed to do and how it can integrate into Plaspy, and consult manufacturer documentation for device specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The LL701 is a long standby asset tracker designed for heavy duty vehicles and valuable assets that require occasional position reporting and event driven alerts rather than continuous real time tracking. It emphasizes long battery life, rugged environmental protection, and a small set of event notifications that support asset security and logistics workflows.

- Long standby operation enabled by replaceable CR123A batteries and optional external power support
- IP67 rated enclosure for protection in harsh or outdoor environments
- Non real time tracking suited to heavy duty vehicles, goods, and remote assets
- Tamper and cover dismantlement alerts triggered by the device light sensor
- Unpacking alert that reports when a package or enclosure has been opened
- Bluetooth based configuration via an iOS app for quick local setup

## Core Features of Concox - LL701

- Replaceable CR123A battery design for lower maintenance costs and straightforward field service
- Long battery life potential of up to five years depending on reporting frequency and configuration
- Support for external power input to extend operational life when installed with a vehicle power source
- IP67 waterproof rating for reliable operation in wet or dusty conditions
- Event triggered alerts including tamper detection and cover dismantlement based on a light sensor
- Unpacking alert to detect and report when a package has been opened
- Bluetooth configuration capability through an iOS application to assist with initial setup and parameter changes
- Rugged enclosure and mechanical design intended for heavy duty vehicle and asset tracking contexts

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The LL701 is designed to send periodic location reports and event notifications that Plaspy can display and act on. Plaspy provides centralized visibility and historical context for device check ins and alerts, so operations teams can monitor asset presence and security events over time.

- Device check ins and periodic location updates are shown on the Plaspy map and timeline for each asset
- Event alerts such as tamper, cover dismantlement, and unpacking are surfaced as discrete notifications and events in Plaspy
- Long standby behavior and replaceable battery design support low maintenance workflows that Plaspy can help schedule and track
- Bluetooth configuration is used locally for setup while Plaspy receives the device reports once the tracker is active
- Plaspy typically auto detects compatible tracker protocols so devices like the LL701 can be configured without deep protocol knowledge

## Typical Use Cases

- Periodic tracking of heavy duty vehicles where continuous real time telemetry is not required
- Monitoring of trailers, containers, and equipment stored outdoors or in harsh environments
- Asset security for shipped goods using unpacking and tamper alerts to detect unauthorized access
- Long duration deployments where battery replacement intervals and low maintenance are a priority
- Temporary or portable installations that benefit from replaceable batteries and Bluetooth setup
- Remote asset tracking where IP67 protection reduces failures due to water or dust exposure

## Feature Availability Notes

- Reported battery life depends strongly on the chosen reporting frequency and configured event behavior
- Firmware version and hardware revision can change how alerts are triggered or labeled in platform reports
- Placement and mounting can affect the effectiveness of light sensor based tamper and unpacking alerts

- Regional variants or custom configurations from the manufacturer may alter available features or default settings
- Always refer to the device firmware release notes and manufacturer guidance for the most current behavior
