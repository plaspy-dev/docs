# Concox - LL701

![LL701](./logo..png)

The Concox LL701 is a Long-Standby Asset Tracker specifically designed for the management of heavy-duty vehicles. With its CR123A battery, this tracker offers enhanced availability and portability. The replaceable battery design also helps fleet managers effectively reduce fleet maintenance costs. Depending on the reporting frequency, the LL701's low power consumption can provide up to 5 years of working time on just 3 batteries. Additionally, it supports an external power supply for even longer working life.

One of the standout features of the LL701 is its IP67 waterproof rating, making it suitable for use in harsh environments. This tracker is perfect for non-real-time tracking of heavy-duty vehicles, goods, and other valuable assets. It also comes equipped with a suite of event-triggered alerts, including tamper alerts and cover dismantlement alarms. The tamper alert feature sends an alert when the light sensor detects device removal, ensuring the security of your assets. The LL701 also offers Bluetooth configuration, allowing for fast and simple device setup via an iOS app. Additionally, it has an unpacking alert feature that triggers the light sensor and sends an alert when the package has been unpacked.

The LL701's rugged design and IP67 rating ensure continued optimal performance, even in the toughest conditions. Whether you need to track heavy-duty vehicles or valuable assets, the Concox LL701 is a reliable and versatile solution that offers long standby times, tamper alerts, and a range of other useful features.
