# Concox - LL701

![LL701](./logo..png)

The Concox LL701 is a long standby asset tracker designed for the management of heavy duty vehicles and valuable assets. It uses replaceable CR123A batteries to provide extended availability and portability, and can deliver up to five years of working time on three batteries depending on reporting frequency. The device supports an external power supply for extended life and carries an IP67 waterproof rating for reliable operation in harsh environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LL701 is suitable for non real time fleet and asset monitoring within Plaspy. Its long standby design, rugged enclosure, and event triggered alerts such as tamper and unpacking alarms make it a practical choice for organizations that need durable, low maintenance tracking. Plaspy can integrate LL701 data to provide visibility, alerting, and reporting for fleets and assets in varied operating conditions.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for long standby operation with replaceable CR123A batteries for reduced maintenance
- Up to five years of working time on three batteries depending on reporting settings
- IP67 waterproof rating for reliable use in harsh or outdoor environments
- Event triggered alerts including tamper detection and unpacking notifications
- Supports external power supply to extend operational lifetime
- Bluetooth configuration for quick setup via an iOS app

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LL701 can be used with Plaspy to provide periodic location updates, event alerts, and device availability information for fleet and asset management. Plaspy receives the tracker data and makes it available through dashboards, maps, and reports geared toward operational oversight.

- Display periodic location points and historical movement for assets on Plaspy maps
- Receive and manage event alerts from the device such as tamper and unpacking notifications
- Track device last seen and availability to support maintenance planning and operational checks
- Build reports and summaries that reflect non real time tracking patterns for long term assets
- Group and monitor assets by fleet, location, or asset type for simplified oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of heavy duty vehicles that are not monitored in real time
- Monitoring trailers, containers, and stationary equipment in harsh outdoor conditions
- Asset security use cases that benefit from tamper and unpacking alerts
- Portable asset tracking where replaceable batteries and low maintenance are priorities
- Situations requiring rugged, weatherproof devices for intermittent reporting schedules
