# Concox - JM-BL11

![JM-BL11](./tracker.png)

The JM-BL11 is a rugged GNSS bike sharing lock engineered for large scale micro mobility and shared bicycle fleets. Designed for demanding outdoor use, it combines GNSS positioning with A GPS and LBS fallback, Bluetooth 4.0 LE local control, tamper resistant mechanics, and long life power with optional solar charging to support distributed deployments and reduce routine maintenance.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JM-BL11 delivers the location, telemetry, and event data that fleet operators rely on for real time visibility and operational control. When integrated with Plaspy, JM-BL11 units provide the core inputs for live maps, geo fence enforcement, tamper and motion alerts, and fleet health reporting, making it a practical option for operators running large shared mobility services.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for micro mobility and shared bicycle fleets with a rugged, tamper resistant enclosure
- GNSS positioning with A GPS and LBS fallback for consistent location reporting across environments
- Long life battery with optional solar charging to extend field uptime and reduce service visits
- Bluetooth 4.0 LE local control and QR code unlocking for user interactions and operational workflows
- Motion and tamper alerts plus secure TLS communications to support anti theft protection and event reporting
- Designed for large scale deployments with on device buffering and compact form factor suitable for locks

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the JM-BL11 streams location fixes, event signals, and device telemetry so operators can monitor fleet status and respond to incidents. Plaspy ingests the device data to populate maps, alerts, and historical records that support efficient operations and security workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry surfaced on Plaspy live maps for visibility and dispatch
- Motion and tamper alerts trigger notifications and event records for fast incident handling
- Battery and solar charging status used by Plaspy to schedule maintenance and optimize uptime
- Bluetooth unlocking and QR code events recorded for usage logs and access audits
- Geo fence events and zone alerts enforced and reported through Plaspy for theft prevention

## Typical Use Cases

- Shared bicycle and stationless rental fleets requiring scalable tracking and availability monitoring
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using geo fences, tamper alerts, and location reporting
- Distributed urban deployments that benefit from solar assisted power to lengthen service intervals
- App driven unlocking and customer workflows using Bluetooth and QR code interactions
- Maintenance planning based on battery telemetry and motion history to prioritize servicing
