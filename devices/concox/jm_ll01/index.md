# Concox - JM-LL01

![JM-LL01](./tracker.png)

The JM-LL01 is a compact, rugged asset GNSS tracker designed for long-term, low-power deployments and is fully Plaspy compatible. Built for reliable, efficient real-time tracking across wide geographic areas, the JM-LL01 combines LTE Cat M1 / NB-IoT connectivity with GSM 2G fallback, multi-source positioning \(GPS + BDS with LBS fallback\), and a large 10,000 mAh rechargeable battery to deliver telemetry and location information where prolonged unattended operation is required.

Optimized for fleet management, rental assets, logistics and general asset monitoring, the JM-LL01 provides industry-grade durability \(IP65\), tamper detection, BLE 4.2 for local configuration, and on-device memory for offline logging. When paired with Plaspy, this tracker brings reliable real-time tracking, telemetry, and event alerts into a single platform for actionable insight and improved anti-theft and asset control workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into real-time tracking and fleet management dashboards.
- Long-life 10,000 mAh Li‑Polymer battery with configurable Standby, Intelligent, and Power‑saving modes for 6 days up to 3 years of operation depending on reporting strategy.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT \(NB2\) with GSM 2G fallback for wide-area coverage and low-power telemetry.
- High-accuracy GNSS positioning \(GPS + BDS\) with LBS fallback and reported accuracy under 2.5 m CEP.
- Built-in tamper detection \(light sensor\), 3‑axis accelerometer for movement detection, and vibration/over-speed alerts for anti-theft monitoring.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure with magnetic base or strap mounts for fast, tamper-resistant installation on vehicles, containers, and equipment.
- BLE 4.2 for local configuration and the ability to pair with Bluetooth sensors where supported.
- On-device memory for up to 1,000 GPS data entries to ensure continuity when out of network coverage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the JM-LL01 with Plaspy enables real-time tracking, event-driven alerts, and historical telemetry reporting for fleet management and asset protection. The device transmits GNSS positions and sensor telemetry over LTE Cat M1, NB-IoT or 2G networks. Plaspy ingests that data to display live location, initiate geofence rules, and generate reports that support operational and security workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates over LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT with 2G fallback for continuity.
- Tamper and cover removal alerts \(light sensor\) forwarded to Plaspy for immediate anti-theft notifications.
- Low battery, vibration and over-speed events reported to Plaspy to trigger maintenance or security actions.
- On-device logging \(1,000 GPS entries\) uploads to Plaspy when connectivity is restored to preserve historical tracks.
- BLE 4.2 support for local configuration and optional pairing with Bluetooth sensors via Plaspy workflows where required.
