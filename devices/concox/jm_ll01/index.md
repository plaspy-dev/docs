# Concox - JM-LL01

![JM-LL01](./tracker.png)

The JM-LL01 is a compact, rugged GNSS asset tracker from Concox designed for long term, low power deployments. It combines multi source positioning with wide area cellular connectivity and a large rechargeable battery to deliver reliable location and telemetry for unattended assets across broad geographic areas. Built with durability and extended runtime in mind, the JM-LL01 suits situations where prolonged operation without frequent maintenance is a priority.

As a fully Plaspy compatible device, the JM-LL01 can feed real time positions, event alerts, and logged history directly into the Plaspy platform. That compatibility makes it practical to consolidate live tracking, geofence rules, and telemetry driven workflows within Plaspy dashboards and reports, helping operations teams monitor assets, respond to tamper events, and preserve historical tracks when connectivity is intermittent.

## Key Highlights

- Fully Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into real time tracking and fleet management dashboards.
- Large 10,000 mAh rechargeable battery designed for extended unattended operation with configurable power profiles.
- Multi source GNSS positioning plus fallback location methods to maintain coverage across wide areas.
- Cellular connectivity optimized for low power wide area networks with fallback to ensure continuity.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and tamper detection to support outdoor and industrial asset deployments.
- On device logging to preserve location history when network connectivity is not available.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the JM-LL01 transmits its GNSS positions and event telemetry so Plaspy can present live location, alert on defined events, and retain historical tracks for reporting and analysis. Plaspy ingests device data to power operational visibility, security alerts, and scheduled or on demand reports that support fleet and asset management.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered into Plaspy for live monitoring and dispatch support.
- Tamper and movement alerts forwarded to Plaspy so teams can receive immediate notifications and act on potential theft or interference.
- Battery status, vibration, and other device events surfaced in Plaspy to trigger maintenance or security workflows.
- Logged location history uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity returns to preserve continuous tracking records.
- Local configuration options via Bluetooth can simplify on site setup prior to Plaspy activation.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset monitoring for containers, trailers, and rental equipment where battery life and unattended operation matter.
- Fleet tracking for logistics and distribution with live positions and event alerts to improve route compliance and oversight.
- Anti theft monitoring for high value assets using tamper detection and movement alerts routed into Plaspy.
- Remote site and infrastructure telemetry where intermittent coverage occurs and on device logging preserves history.
- Rapid redeployment and temporary installs using rugged mounting options to attach the device to vehicles or equipment.

The JM-LL01 offers a balanced mix of durability, battery endurance, and multi source positioning that aligns well with Plaspy use cases for fleet and asset management. Its design for long term, low maintenance operation reduces touch points for field teams while enabling Plaspy to deliver continuous visibility, alerting, and historical reporting that support operational decision making.
