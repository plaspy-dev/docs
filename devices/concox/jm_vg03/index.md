# Concox - JM-VG03

![JM-VG03](./logo..png)

The Concox JM-VG03 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that is compatible with a wide range of vehicles. With its support for a voltage range of 9-90V, this tracker can be easily installed in cars, trucks, motorcycles, and more. Its compact size and inbuilt large antenna ensure faster and more accurate positioning, while also allowing for hidden installation if desired. This makes it an ideal choice for both personal and commercial use.

One of the standout features of the JM-VG03 is its anti-theft capabilities. It comes with alerts for power supply disconnection and tampering, providing an added layer of security for your vehicle. In the event of any suspicious activity, you will be immediately notified, allowing you to take appropriate action.

In addition to its anti-theft features, the JM-VG03 offers a range of other useful functionalities. It includes driving behavior analysis, which can detect and alert you to dangerous driving behaviors such as harsh braking, sharp turns, and speeding. This can be particularly valuable for fleet management or monitoring the driving habits of family members.

The JM-VG03 also boasts IP65 dust and water resistance, ensuring that it can withstand even the toughest conditions. Whether you're driving through dusty terrain or encountering heavy rain, this tracker will continue to perform optimally.

With its remote cut-off feature, you have the ability to immobilize a vehicle by cutting off its power source or fuel supply. This can be done remotely via an installed relay, providing an effective measure to prevent unauthorized use or theft.

Other notable features of the JM-VG03 include ignition detection, which constantly monitors the ACC/ignition status of the vehicle, and geo-fencing, which allows you to set virtual boundaries on the platform. If the vehicle crosses these boundaries, you will receive an instant notification. Additionally, the tracker provides multiple alerts for atypical events such as low battery, speeding, removal, and drifting.
