# Concox - JM-VG03

<div className="device-logo">
![JM-VG03](./logo..png)
</div>

The Concox JM-VG03 is a compact GPS tracker designed for a wide range of vehicles. It supports a broad operating voltage range, making it suitable for cars, trucks, motorcycles, and other vehicle types. The unit emphasizes reliable positioning through a built in large antenna and is sized for discreet mounting. Physical protection against dust and water is rated to IP65, helping it tolerate challenging environments.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the JM-VG03 delivers the basic capabilities fleet managers and vehicle owners expect for monitoring and security. Key features such as anti theft alerts for power disconnection and tampering, driving behavior notifications, remote cut off capability via a relay, ignition detection, and geo fencing are all relevant to Plaspy users who need centralized visibility, alerting, and reporting across mixed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Wide voltage tolerance suitable for cars, trucks, and motorcycles for versatile fleet use
- Compact form factor with a large built in antenna for improved positioning and hidden installation
- Anti theft alerts including power disconnection and tampering notifications
- Driving behavior analysis that can flag events such as harsh braking, sharp turns, and speeding
- IP65 rated for dust and water resistance to support outdoor and adverse conditions
- Remote cut off capability using an installed relay to immobilize a vehicle when required
- Geo fencing and multiple event alerts for low battery, removal, and drifting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the JM-VG03 provides location and event data that Plaspy can display, alert on, and include in reports. The device's event notifications and status signals translate into actionable items within Plaspy for monitoring and operational oversight.

- Live and historical location visibility inside Plaspy for fleet tracking and route review
- Event alerts such as power disconnect, tampering, ignition changes, and geo fence crossings forwarded to Plaspy for real time notification
- Driving behavior events presented in Plaspy dashboards and reports to support safety and coaching programs
- Remote immobilization actions represented in Plaspy for centralized control where the device and install support relay commands
- Summary and exportable reports for fleet performance, incident review, and compliance monitoring
