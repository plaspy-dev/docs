# Concox - WeTrack140

![WeTrack140](./tracker.png)

The WeTrack140 is an AIS140-approved, multifunctional vehicle GPS tracker engineered for the Indian market and optimized for Plaspy-compatible fleet management. Built to meet the needs of government fleets, courier delivery services, and car rental companies, the WeTrack140 delivers precise real-time tracking of vehicle location, speed and distance traveled while supplying rich telematics and status inputs that integrate seamlessly with Plaspy's platform.

Designed for resilience and operational control, the WeTrack140 combines GNSS positioning \(GPS + IRNSS + GLONASS\), quad-band cellular communications, a 450 mAh backup battery and robust IP66 protection. Its safety and control features — including remote cut-off \(fuel/power\), panic button, voice monitoring and driving behavior analysis — make it a practical choice for anti-theft protection, telemetry, fuel monitoring workflows and intelligent fleet management when paired with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- AIS140-approved for Indian regulatory compliance and trusted fleet deployment.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking, reporting and centralized fleet management.
- GNSS: GPS + IRNSS + GLONASS with high sensitivity and sub-2.5 m CEP positioning accuracy.
- Operational control via remote cut-off \(fuel/power\) and panic button for emergency alerts.
- Driving behavior analysis \(harsh acceleration, harsh braking, harsh cornering, collision detection\) to support safer driving programs and telemetry insights.
- Voice monitoring and remote listen-in capability for on-demand situational awareness \(range up to ~5 m\).
- Rugged build and IP66 rating for reliable performance in harsh automotive environments.
- Large onboard data capacity and long standby performance to preserve historical records during outages.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The WeTrack140 transmits GNSS and telematics data over GSM/GPRS to Plaspy in real time, enabling live location views, route playback and actionable alerts. Plaspy ingests device telemetry and status inputs to create dashboards, automated reports and event-driven notifications for fleet operators. Integration is straightforward for fleets and service providers who need a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that supports regulatory requirements and practical vehicle controls.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS position, speed, distance traveled\).
- Status inputs such as door status and other telematics signals \(including ignition-type inputs where wired into the vehicle harness\).
- Remote cut-off \(fuel/power\) for anti-theft and asset control—can be implemented as an immobilizer-style intervention.
- Emergency notifications via panic button and remote listen-in / voice monitoring for incident response.
- Driving behavior and collision detection events sent to Plaspy for analytics and compliance reporting.
