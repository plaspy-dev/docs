# Concox - EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker

<div className="device-logo">
![EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Concox EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker purpose built for electric scooters, e bikes and other light electric vehicles. In a cigarette lighter form factor with plug and play installation, the EG02 is designed to deliver reliable real time tracking and event telemetry while keeping installation time and complexity low for micro mobility operators and fleet managers.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the EG02 integrates vehicle location and event data into a centralized fleet platform for monitoring, alerts and historical reporting. Its anti theft features and remote control options make it a practical choice for organizations that want unified visibility and control of small EV fleets through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for micro mobility that brings GPS and LBS positions into centralized monitoring and reporting.
- Compact plug and play form factor suitable for fast retrofit or factory installation without cutting vehicle wiring.
- Built in anti theft and remote immobilizer features with remote lock and unlock capability via mobile app or SMS.
- Immediate audible alarm and external buzzer support to deter theft and help locate vehicles quickly.
- Broad 9 to 90 V operating range and low standby current to suit a variety of scooters, e bikes and light EV platforms.
- Accurate GNSS positioning for dependable real time location updates and history playback.
- Event alerts for speeding, geo fence entry and exit, power off and vibration support telemetry and safety workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the EG02 sends location, alarm and status telemetry so operators can monitor fleets in real time and act on events. Plaspy surfaces these inputs for dashboards, alerting rules and historical reports, and can be used to control remote immobilization and audible alerts when required.

- Real time location and telemetry delivery to Plaspy for live tracking and route history.
- Status events such as lock/unlock and ignition state are represented in Plaspy for operational oversight.
- Alerts for power off, vibration or unauthorized movement, speeding and geo fence triggers are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification.
- Remote immobilizer and audible siren control accessible from Plaspy dashboards to support theft response.
- Historical reporting and playback in Plaspy help analyze usage patterns and investigate incidents.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for micro mobility operators monitoring scooter and e bike locations and utilization.
- Anti theft protection for shared and rental scooters using automatic arming, audible alarms and remote immobilization.
- Aftermarket retrofits and factory fitments where a compact plug and play tracker reduces installation time.
- Safety monitoring and incident response with vibration and speed alerts to support maintenance and operator safety checks.
- Tracking light industrial and utility vehicles such as golf carts and three wheel EVs used on campuses or facilities.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The EG02 pairs a compact form factor with focused anti theft controls and reliable positioning, making it a sensible option for organizations that need fast deployment and centralized monitoring. Its wide voltage tolerance and low standby draw reduce compatibility concerns across diverse small EVs, while plug and play wiring helps keep rollout and servicing efficient for larger fleets.

Integrating EG02 with Plaspy gives operators a practical combination of location visibility, event alerts and remote control capabilities that support theft deterrence and operational oversight. For micro mobility operators, fleet managers and service providers who require a compact, purpose built tracker for scooters and light EVs, EG02 offers a balanced solution when used with Plaspy.
