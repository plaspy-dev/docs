# Concox - EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker

![EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker](./tracker.png)

The EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker purpose-built for electric scooters, e-bikes and other light electric vehicles. With a cigarette-lighter form factor and plug-and-play installation, EG02 delivers reliable real-time tracking and event telemetry while minimizing installation time for micro-mobility operators and fleet managers.

Designed for anti-theft protection and efficient fleet management, the EG02 combines GPS + LBS positioning, remote lock/unlock \(via mobile app or SMS\), and immediate audible alarms on unauthorized movement. Its broad 9–90 V operating range, low standby current and robust positioning accuracy make it an ideal GPS tracker for last-mile fleets, rental scooters, three-wheel EVs and light industrial vehicles when integrated with Plaspy for centralized monitoring and control.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for micro-mobility: integrates GPS/LBS location and alert data into Plaspy for real-time tracking and reporting.
- Compact, plug-and-play form factor \(77 × 29 × 13 mm\) for fast retrofit or factory installation without cutting vehicle wiring.
- Intelligent anti-theft and remote immobilizer: automatic power cut to enter arming state plus remote lock/unlock by app or SMS.
- Immediate audible alarm and external buzzer support to locate scooters and deter thefts on the spot.
- Low power draw and wide voltage tolerance \(9–90 V\) suitable for e-scooters, e-bikes, golf carts and small EVs.
- Accurate GNSS tracking \(under 2.5 m CEP\) with fast TTFF for dependable real-time location updates.
- Event alerts for speeding, geo-fence entry/exit, power-off and abnormal vibration—good for telemetry and safety monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the EG02 streams location and event data over GPRS \(TCP/IP\) to your Plaspy account while also supporting SMS-based commands where network conditions require. Plaspy receives GNSS/LBS positions, alarm and status telemetry and exposes those data points for dashboard monitoring, automated alerts, and historical reporting. Remote immobilization, audible alarms and geo-fence rules configured in Plaspy let operators act immediately on theft or safety events.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GPS + LBS delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and history playback.
- Keyless start / ignition state and remote lock/unlock control surfaced in Plaspy as status events.
- Alerts forwarded to Plaspy for power-off, vibration/unauthorized movement, speeding and geo-fence entry/exit.
- Remote immobilizer support: immobilize vehicles from Plaspy \(or via SMS/app\) to stop theft in progress.
- External buzzer/siren control and locate-beep commands accessible through Plaspy dashboards or mobile alerts.
