# Concox - EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker Features

This page provides public feature context for the Concox EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker and explains how those capabilities are used when the device reports into Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive descriptions of what the tracker does and how Plaspy surfaces location and event data for micro mobility and light EV fleets.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. Users should treat this page as a feature summary based on the device description and confirm any device specific or firmware dependent details with the manufacturer and in-device documentation.

## Feature Overview

The EG02 is a compact, plug and play GPS tracker built for electric scooters, e-bikes and other light electric vehicles. It pairs GNSS and LBS positioning with theft deterrence controls to provide real time location awareness and event notifications suitable for rental fleets and last mile operations.

- Compact cigarette lighter form factor for fast retrofit or factory installation without cutting vehicle wiring.
- Combined GPS and LBS positioning for reliable location reporting in urban and mixed coverage areas.
- Remote lock and unlock control via mobile app or SMS and a remote immobilizer mode for theft response.
- Audible alarm and external buzzer support to locate vehicles and deter unauthorized movement.
- Wide operating voltage range and low standby current to suit a variety of light EV power systems.
- Event alerts including geo fence entry and exit, power off, vibration or unauthorized movement, and speeding.

## Core Features of Concox - EG02 Intelligent Motorbike Tracker

- Plug and play installation designed for scooters, e-bikes and light electric vehicles.
- Dual positioning with GNSS and LBS to provide both outdoor accuracy and fallback positioning.
- Remote lock/unlock and immobilizer controls accessible through supported interfaces.
- Audible alarm and external buzzer control for on the spot vehicle locating and deterrence.
- Wide input voltage tolerance and low standby power for flexible vehicle integration.
- Event reporting for power off, abnormal vibration or movement, geo fence transitions and speed alerts.
- Built in data storage and backup battery to maintain basic telemetry between connections.
- Ruggedized form factor with water resistance suitable for street use.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the EG02 is integrated with Plaspy, its location and event telemetry are forwarded into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor status, receive alerts and act through centralized tools. Plaspy presents device positions, alarms and controls in dashboards and reporting workflows to support fleet operations and theft response.

- Live location updates and history playback in Plaspy using GNSS and LBS positions reported by the tracker.
- Alarm and status events such as power loss, vibration alerts, geo fence entry and exit, and speeding shown as actionable events.
- Remote immobilize and lock/unlock commands coordinated from Plaspy dashboards or operator workflows where supported.
- External buzzer and siren commands available through Plaspy to help locate vehicles on site.
- Event-triggered notifications and reporting for operational oversight and incident follow up.
