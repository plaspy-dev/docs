## How Concox devices work with Plaspy

Concox trackers send location and status data over cellular networks to a server. Plaspy accepts device reports from a wide range of GPS tracker manufacturers, and Concox units can be configured to forward their data to your Plaspy account. On the Plaspy platform you can view live positions, review trip history, and create alerts based on the incoming telemetry from Concox devices.
