# Concox - LL301 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive feature overview of the Concox LL301 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for tracking, monitoring, and alerting. It focuses on the practical functions you can expect when integrating the LL301 with Plaspy and highlights what operators can monitor without delving into private configuration details.

Exact feature availability can vary between hardware revisions, firmware versions, regional variants, and installation methods. Where manufacturer behavior or specific firmware-dependent functions are relevant, we note those dependencies and recommend verifying current device specifications with the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LL301 is a 4G LTE Cat 1 asset GNSS tracker designed for long-term, low-maintenance deployments and is compatible with Plaspy out of the box. Its combination of high-capacity battery, multi-constellation positioning, rugged housing, and built-in alerts makes it suitable for remote asset monitoring and fleet oversight.

- Ultra-long battery life suitable for long-term asset deployments and low maintenance cycles.
- Multi-constellation GNSS (GPS + BDS + LBS) for accurate positioning and reliable fixes in varied environments.
- IP65-rated enclosure and industrial temperature tolerance for outdoor and worksites.
- Built-in event alerts including tamper, cover removal, vibration, and low battery to support security workflows.
- Bluetooth 5.0 provisioning and a strong magnetic base for fast, low-effort installations.
- Regional LTE and GSM variants that provide cellular connectivity and fallback where required.

## Core Features of Concox - LL301

- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with regional GSM fallback for broad coverage across deployments.
- High-capacity 10,000 mAh industrial Li‑Polymer battery with multiple power modes for extended standby life.
- Multi-constellation GNSS positioning combining GPS, BDS, and LBS for precise location reporting.
- IP65 weatherproof housing and rugged operating temperature range for outdoor asset use.
- Motion and vibration detection via a built-in 3-axis accelerometer and pressure sensitive tamper detection.
- Bluetooth 5.0 support for quick device configuration and optional BLE accessories.
- Strong magnetic mounting base to simplify installation on vehicles, trailers, and metal assets.
- Event and telemetry reporting including tamper, cover removal, vibration, low-battery, and battery status.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, LL301 status, position fixes, and event alerts become visible through the platform for real-time monitoring and historical review. Plaspy receives location and telemetry updates and presents them alongside configurable notifications and reporting tools.

- Real-time location updates and periodic position reports visible on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- Tamper, cover removal, and vibration alerts posted to Plaspy notification channels to support anti-theft workflows.
- Battery level and low-battery notifications surfaced in Plaspy to help prioritize maintenance and replacements.
- Historical track playback and event timelines in Plaspy for incident review and movement analysis.
- Device provisioning and initial setup using Bluetooth 5.0, followed by ongoing reporting into Plaspy for operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Logistics and shipment monitoring where long standby life and accurate GNSS provide extended transit visibility.
- Fleet and rental vehicle oversight with tamper and vibration alerts to detect potential misuse or theft.
- Construction and heavy equipment tracking that requires rugged hardware and reliable outdoors operation.
- Remote asset monitoring for trailers, containers, and static assets that need low-maintenance tracking.
- Incident review and operational analytics using accelerometer events and historical tracks in Plaspy.

- Regional cellular variants affect supported LTE and GSM bands; confirm the correct model for your deployment region.
- Some features such as event thresholds, timing/intelligent/battery-saving modes, and remote capabilities depend on device firmware and vendor configuration.
- Physical installation and mounting method can influence motion and tamper sensing performance.
- Manufacturer cloud and mobile app features are separate from Plaspy; integration and available telemetry may vary by firmware and configuration.
