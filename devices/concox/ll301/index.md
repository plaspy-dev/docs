# Concox - LL301

![LL301](./tracker.png)

The LL301 is a 4G LTE Cat 1 asset GNSS tracker built for long-term, low‑maintenance deployments and is Plaspy compatible out of the box. With a high-capacity 10,000 mAh industrial Li‑Polymer battery, IP65-rated housing, multi‑constellation positioning \(GPS + BDS + LBS\) and regional LTE/GSM variants, the LL301 delivers reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for fleet management, logistics and construction asset monitoring.

Designed for straightforward integration with Plaspy, the LL301 provides instant alerts \(tamper, cover removal, vibration, low battery\) and remote monitoring via the vendor cloud platform and mobile app. Its Bluetooth 5.0 configuration, strong magnetic base and long standby life make it an ideal GPS tracker for anti-theft protection and remotely managed asset tracking projects that require minimal onsite maintenance.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra-long battery life: 10,000 mAh Li‑Polymer battery with up to 2 years standby in battery-saving mode—ideal for long-term asset deployments.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking: LTE/GSM connectivity and multi-constellation GNSS deliver accurate position updates into Plaspy for live monitoring and historical playback.
- Robust, weatherproof design: IP65 housing and industrial operating range \(‑20°C to +70°C\) for reliable operation in harsh environments.
- High positioning accuracy: GPS + BDS + LBS with \<2.5 m CEP and industry-grade sensitivity for precise location reporting.
- Comprehensive alerts and telemetry: built-in 3‑axis accelerometer, tamper/cover removal detection, vibration alerts and low-battery notifications.
- Easy deployment and configuration: magnetic base for near-zero installation and Bluetooth 5.0 for quick mobile app setup.
- Regional cellular variants: LL301‑E and LL301‑L cover a wide range of LTE‑FDD and GSM bands with automatic fallback to GSM where needed.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LL301 forwards GNSS position fixes, sensor telemetry and event alerts to Plaspy over LTE/GSM. Plaspy displays real-time tracking on maps, triggers configurable notifications and logs historical tracks for reporting. The device’s modes \(timing positioning, intelligent positioning and battery-saving\) let Plaspy operators balance update frequency versus battery life.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via LTE/GSM.
- Tamper, cover removal and abnormal vibration alerts for anti-theft and asset security workflows.
- Battery status and low-battery notifications to prioritize maintenance and recharging cycles.
- Bluetooth 5.0 support for quick configuration and optional BLE accessories managed through the mobile app and linked to Plaspy.
- Historical track playback and event timelines accessible from Plaspy for fleet management and incident review.

## Use Cases

- Logistics and shipment tracking: long battery life and accurate GNSS make the LL301 suited for container and pallet monitoring across transit legs.
- Vehicle rental and fleet management: Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and tamper/vibration alerts support operational oversight and anti-theft measures.
- Construction and heavy equipment: IP65 housing and rugged operating range enable tracking of off‑road assets and machinery on worksites.
- Remote asset monitoring: magnetic mounting and BLE provisioning simplify deployment on trailers, containers and static assets requiring infrequent maintenance.
- Mobile telemetry and incident review: accelerometer events and historical tracks help reconstruct movement patterns and trigger exceptions in Plaspy workflows.
