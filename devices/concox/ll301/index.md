# Concox - LL301

<div className="device-logo">
![LL301](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Concox LL301 is a rugged asset GNSS tracker designed for long term, low maintenance deployments. It combines multi constellation positioning with regional LTE and GSM cellular connectivity and a high capacity 10,000 mAh industrial battery in an IP65 rated enclosure. The device is intended for real time location tracking and telemetry across asset types where long standby life and durable construction are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the LL301 sends position fixes, event alerts and battery status into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring and historical review. Its built in alerting for tamper, vibration and low battery, along with Bluetooth based configuration and magnetic mounting, make the LL301 a practical option to integrate into Plaspy workflows for centralized fleet and asset oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Long life battery designed for low maintenance deployments with a 10,000 mAh industrial cell.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning and regional LTE GSM connectivity for reliable location reporting.
- IP65 rated housing and industrial operating range suited to outdoor and site based assets.
- Built in alerting for tamper, cover removal, vibration and low battery to support security workflows.
- Bluetooth 5.0 configuration and magnetic mounting for straightforward provisioning and placement.
- Regional device variants to support different cellular band needs across deployments.
- Designed for real time tracking and historical playback when used with a compatible platform like Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LL301 forwards position updates, sensor events and status messages so operators can monitor assets in real time and review historical movement. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it actionable through maps, alerts and reporting, enabling centralized operational oversight for fleets and field equipment.

- Real time location updates and telemetry displayed on Plaspy maps for live visibility.
- Event alerts such as tamper, cover removal and abnormal vibration routed to Plaspy for immediate notification.
- Battery status and low battery warnings integrated into Plaspy to help prioritize maintenance visits.
- Historical track playback and event timelines available in Plaspy for incident review and reports.
- Configurable notification rules in Plaspy let teams tailor alerts and escalation for different asset types.

## Typical Use Cases

- Logistics and shipment tracking for containers, pallets and high value consignments during transit.
- Fleet and rental vehicle oversight where tamper detection and long battery life reduce service visits.
- Construction and heavy equipment monitoring on worksites with rugged environmental needs.
- Remote asset tracking for trailers, containers and static assets that require minimal onsite maintenance.
- Incident reconstruction and operational reporting using accelerometer events and historical tracks.
