# Concox - JM-VG01

<div className="device-logo">
![JM-VG01](./logo..png)
</div>

The JM-VG01U from Concox is an advanced vehicle tracker designed for robust fleet and vehicle monitoring. Its feature set includes multi axis motion sensing for driving behavior analysis, INS aided GPS tracking to maintain positioning where GPS is poor, ignition monitoring, a panic button for emergencies, remote cut off capability, and a rugged IP65 enclosure. The device emphasizes analytics and dependable tracking to support operational oversight and safety programs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JM-VG01 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide location visibility, driving behavior alerts, mileage reporting, and event notifications within the same fleet management environment. Plaspy users can leverage the tracker’s analytical outputs alongside other fleet data to improve safety, monitor vehicle use, and support operational decision making.

## Key Highlights

- Multi axis motion sensing that supports detection of multiple improper driving behaviors
- INS aided GPS tracking for improved continuity in areas with poor GPS reception
- Driving behavior analysis with alerts for eight types of dangerous driving patterns
- High precision mileage calculation with accuracy claims of at least 98 percent
- Ignition monitoring and a panic button for emergency notification
- Remote cut off capability to assist in vehicle immobilization when required
- IP65 rated housing for protection against dust and water exposure

## How It Works with Plaspy

The JM-VG01 connects to Plaspy as a compatible vehicle tracker to feed location and event data into Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting tools. Once integrated, the device’s sensor outputs and status events can be surfaced inside Plaspy to enhance visibility and operational oversight.

- Live and historical location tracking displayed alongside other fleet assets
- Driving behavior alerts routed into Plaspy for immediate notification and review
- Mileage and distance reporting used in Plaspy reports for billing and maintenance planning
- Ignition status and panic events exposed as actionable events for dispatch or supervisors
- Remote immobilization status tracked in Plaspy to confirm execution and response
- Improved continuity of tracking in low GPS conditions due to INS aided positioning

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet safety programs that monitor harsh driving events and coach drivers
- Long haul and urban operations where GPS reception can be intermittent
- Mileage reporting for billing reconciliation and maintenance scheduling
- Emergency response workflows using panic alerts and ignition status
- Vehicle theft response using location visibility and remote immobilization
