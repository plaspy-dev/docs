# Concox - JM-VG01

![JM-VG01](./logo..png)

The JM-VG01U is an advanced vehicle tracker device from Concox that offers exceptional analytical capacity. Equipped with a 3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis gyroscope, it can detect eight types of improper driving behaviors, ensuring increased safety and security on the road. One of its standout features is the INS-aided GPS tracking, which ensures constant tracking even in areas with poor GPS signal or no signal at all. This means that you can rely on the JM-VG01U to accurately track your vehicle's location regardless of the circumstances.

Another impressive feature of the JM-VG01U is its driving behavior analysis. With this advanced feature, you can receive alerts when any of the eight dangerous driving behaviors are detected. This allows you to take immediate action and address any potential risks or issues. Additionally, the JM-VG01U offers precise mileage calculation with an accuracy of at least 98%. This is made possible through a unique algorithm that minimizes mileage errors to less than 2%, ensuring accurate tracking of your vehicle's distance traveled.

The JM-VG01U also boasts a 6-axis accelerometer and gyroscope, which enables sensitive detection of linear acceleration and angular velocity. This allows for more accurate monitoring of your vehicle's movements and behavior. Furthermore, the device features ignition detection, constantly monitoring the ACC/ignition status of the vehicle. In case of emergencies, the JM-VG01U is equipped with a panic button that allows you to discreetly contact dispatch or emergency support. Additionally, the device offers remote cut-off capabilities, allowing you to immobilize a vehicle by cutting off its power source or fuel supply via an installed relay.

Designed to withstand tough conditions, the JM-VG01U is IP65 dust and water-resistant. This rugged design ensures optimal performance even in the harshest environments. Whether you're tracking a single vehicle or managing a fleet, the JM-VG01U is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers advanced analytical capabilities and enhanced safety features.
