# Concox - VG502

![VG502](./tracker.png)

The VG502 is a plug and play OBDII GNSS tracker designed for quick deployment in private cars, company vehicles, and usage based insurance programs. Its OBDII form factor allows it to connect to vehicle diagnostics and deliver continuous location and vehicle telemetry without hardwiring, which makes it suitable for fleets that need fast installation and straightforward device management.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VG502 streams GNSS fixes and OBD derived telemetry into the Plaspy platform so fleets and insurers can use a single dashboard for tracking, alerts, and reporting. The combination of location data, driving behavior analysis, and diagnostic information makes the VG502 a practical option for organizations that want richer operational insight with minimal installation overhead.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBDII GNSS tracker for rapid deployment in private and fleet vehicles.
- Dual GNSS positioning and quoted accuracy better than 2.5 m CEP for precise location reporting.
- Direct vehicle telemetry including mileage, fault codes, ACC and ignition status, fuel use, battery voltage, and engine speed.
- Driving behavior analysis that identifies harsh acceleration braking cornering and collision events for safety programs and UBI.
- BLE 4.0 support for local configuration firmware updates and optional Bluetooth sensor connectivity.
- Instant alerts for fault codes collisions geo fence entry exit device removal and power loss to speed response.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the VG502 forwards location fixes and OBDII telemetry so your Plaspy workspace can present live maps alerts and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the device events and diagnostics and applies geofencing rules reporting and analytics to support operational decisions and insurer workflows.

- Real time location and trip playback on Plaspy maps using GNSS fixes from the device.
- Vehicle status and trip segmentation using ACC and ignition state in Plaspy event logs.
- Fuel and mileage reporting in Plaspy derived from OBDII telemetry for cost control and efficiency analysis.
- Driving behavior alerts such as harsh acceleration braking and collisions surfaced as notifications and scorecards.
- Geofence and device removal alerts routed through Plaspy to support security and recovery workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rapid fleet rollout where plug and play installation reduces downtime and simplifies device swap between vehicles.
- Usage based insurance programs that need driving behavior scoring and mileage aggregation.
- Company car monitoring for vehicle health checks fuel consumption and compliance reporting.
- Asset protection and anti theft monitoring using device removal power loss and geo fence alerts.
- Operational reporting for mixed vehicle fleets to centralize location and diagnostics in a single system.
