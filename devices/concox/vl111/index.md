# Concox - VL111

![VL111](./tracker.png)

The VL111 by VL \(model VL111 / VL111S\_LA / VL111S\_EM\) is a compact 4G LTE vehicle GPS tracker engineered for reliable, discreet Plaspy compatible real-time tracking. Built for motorcycles and light vehicles, the VL111 combines multi-constellation GNSS, LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback, BLE 5.0, and a built-in relay for remote immobilization—delivering the core telemetry and control features fleet managers and vehicle dealers rely on for anti-theft protection and driver monitoring.

Designed to integrate with platforms like Plaspy, the VL111 provides continuous location, trip playback and vehicle telemetry while protecting battery health with automatic power-disconnect and an internal backup. Its rugged IP66 housing, light weight and small form factor make it easy to install on a wide range of vehicles while maintaining the telemetry and control channels that modern fleet management, fuel monitoring programs and anti-theft workflows require.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and secure telemetry reporting over LTE Cat 1 with 2G GSM fallback.
- High-accuracy multi-constellation GNSS: GPS, BeiDou \(BDS\), GLONASS with AGPS and LBS fallback \(accuracy &lt; 2.5 m CEP\).
- Built-in PCB relay for remote immobilizer / cut-off of fuel or power, supporting theft prevention and return-to-service workflows.
- BLE 5.0 and internal Bluetooth antenna for sensor connectivity and proximity use cases with low power consumption.
- Vehicle voltage monitoring 0–90V, internal battery fallback and automatic power disconnection to protect the vehicle battery.
- Driving behavior telemetry via onboard accelerometer: harsh acceleration, harsh braking, harsh cornering and collision detection.
- Rugged IP66 housing, wide temperature range \(−20°C to +70°C\) and compact form factor for easy, discreet installation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VL111 forwards GNSS positions, vehicle telemetry and event data to Plaspy over LTE Cat 1 \(with 2G fallback\) so dispatchers and managers can use Plaspy’s dashboard for live monitoring, alerts and historical analysis. Typical integration takes the device’s periodic location/telemetry packets and maps them into Plaspy’s real-time view, enabling command-and-control actions from the platform.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GNSS positions, speed, heading and timestamped location history for live tracking and trip playback.
- Immobilizer / relay control: remote cut-off \(fuel or power\) supported by the onboard relay—Plaspy can issue immobilization commands where the device-platform connection permits.
- Vehicle health & battery telemetry: voltage monitoring and low internal battery alerts to protect vehicle power and enable scheduled maintenance.
- Driving behavior & safety events: accelerometer-based harsh driving and collision alerts feed into Plaspy for driver coaching and safety reporting.
- Bluetooth sensors and proximity beacons: BLE 5.0 support for future sensor integrations or local device pairing to enhance telemetry and asset context.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management for light vehicles and motorcycles: real-time tracking, trip playback and driver behavior monitoring to improve operational efficiency.
- Anti-theft protection for motorcycles and dealer inventory: remote immobilizer plus geo-fence and vibration/tamper alerts for rapid response.
- Battery and power protection programs: automatic power disconnection and internal battery fallback to prevent vehicle battery drain during long idle periods.
- Safety and compliance: accelerometer-driven events \(harsh braking/acceleration\) fed into Plaspy for coaching, incident review and insurance workflows.
- Discrete asset installs: compact, rugged form suitable for dealers and rental fleets that need unobtrusive GPS asset monitoring.

For organizations seeking a compact, proven tracker with robust telemetry and immobilizer capability, the VL111 is a dependable Plaspy compatible choice.
