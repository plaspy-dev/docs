# Concox - GV20 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Concox GV20 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the GV20's primary capabilities as reported by the manufacturer, with a focus on how those capabilities appear and provide value within Plaspy's fleet and device management platform. The content is intended for users evaluating device capabilities and how the tracker integrates into Plaspy workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and regional product variants. Where a feature depends on configuration or wiring (for example remote cutoff), confirm the specifics with your installer and the manufacturer's documentation before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The Concox GV20 is positioned as a 3G GPS tracker for vehicles, motors, and buses that emphasizes real-time location, route visibility, and operational controls. Its feature set aims to support monitoring, incident review, and control workflows for fleet operators and vehicle owners.

- 3G GPS tracking designed for vehicle, motor, and bus use for continuous location reporting.
- Real-time location and route monitoring to support live tracking and dispatch visibility.
- Flashback route playback for reviewing historical routes and trip details.
- Remote fuel and power cutoff capability to stop fuel or power supply when needed.
- Ignition status reporting to identify engine on/off states and support usage monitoring.
- Instant alert notifications for route deviations, abnormal activity, and other configured events.

## Core Features of Concox - GV20

- 3G GPS positioning suited for on-road vehicle tracking and route monitoring.
- Real-time location updates to support live tracking and operational oversight.
- Route flashback (historical route playback) for trip review and incident analysis.
- Remote fuel/power cutoff enabling authorized remote interruption of fuel or power.
- Ignition status reporting to indicate vehicle engine state.
- Instant alerts and notifications for deviations and abnormal events reported by the device.
- Built for vehicle, motor, and bus installations where continuous tracking and control are needed.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy presents GV20-reported data and events within its device management and mapping tools so teams can monitor fleets, review trips, and respond to alerts. Plaspy's platform is designed to detect compatible tracker protocols and display the device's reporting in a unified interface for operational use.

- Live map view showing current GV20 location and movement for dispatch and monitoring.
- Route history and flashback playback within Plaspy for incident review and route analysis.
- Alert surfacing and notification delivery so route deviations and abnormal activity are visible to operators.
- Visibility of ignition status and related events to support usage reporting and driver oversight.
- When the GV20 and its installation support remote cutoff, Plaspy can reflect that status and provide controls or workflow steps where enabled and configured by the operator.
- Plaspy handles device detection and displays the supported features for each connected GV20 unit during setup.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real-time fleet tracking for small to mid-size vehicle fleets to improve dispatch and routing.
- Bus route monitoring and historical playback for schedule adherence and trip reconstruction.
- Remote fuel or power cutoff as a response tool for unauthorized use or emergency situations.
- Ignition status monitoring to support shift handovers, usage accounting, and operational checks.
- Alert-driven workflows to detect and respond to route deviations or abnormal vehicle activity.
- Historical route review to investigate incidents or verify driver reports.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence may depend on the GV20 firmware version and the specific hardware revision shipped for a given region.
- Remote fuel or power cutoff typically requires correct installation and wiring; confirm with your installer that the device is set up for that function.
- Network coverage and regional 3G availability can affect real-time reporting and telemetry frequency.
- Some alerts and behaviors may require particular configuration on the device and on the Plaspy platform to appear as expected.
- Always check the manufacturer documentation and release notes for the GV20 to confirm feature sets and firmware changes.
