# Concox - GT02

![GT02](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT02 is a compact GPS tracker that combines GPS, GSM, and GPRS technologies to deliver real time position monitoring. Designed for global use with quad band GSM support, the GT02 pairs a high sensitivity GPS chipset with built in GSM and GPS antennas to provide consistent location reporting. The device supports continuous monitoring through auto start when power is connected and gives visual status via three LEDs for GPS, GSM, and power.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the GT02 can feed location and basic device status into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and fleet management. Its resilience features, including an internal watchdog and an acceleration sensor for intelligent power handling, make it a practical option for businesses and individuals who need persistent tracking and remote oversight across regions.

## Key Highlights

- Global ready quad band GSM support for international deployments
- High sensitivity GPS chipset with built in antennas for reliable positioning
- Real time tracking capability for live location visibility
- Auto start on power connection to ensure continuous operation
- Built in watchdog for automatic reboot in case of device issues
- Acceleration sensor for improved power management and movement detection
- Flexible installation options including covert or open mounting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GT02 transmits location updates and status information to the Plaspy platform where teams can view, analyze, and act on that data. Plaspy aggregates the incoming device data and presents it through maps, reports, and alert workflows to support operational decision making.

- Live map tracking and position updates for vehicles and assets
- Historical route playback and basic reporting for movement analysis
- Configurable alerts and notifications for events such as movement or power status
- Visual status indicators in the platform reflecting GPS GSM and power conditions
- Fleet grouping and assignment to organize assets for operations and oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small and medium sized operations
- Cross border and international transport where global GSM coverage is needed
- Asset security and recovery for high value mobile equipment
- Covert or visible installation depending on operational requirements
- Monitoring of rental or shared vehicles for operational control
