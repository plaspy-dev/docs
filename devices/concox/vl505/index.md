# Concox - VL505

<div className="device-logo">
![VL505](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Concox VL505 is an ultra compact plug and play 4G OBD II GNSS GPS tracker designed for both private cars and commercial fleets. Installed via the vehicle OBD II port, the VL505 provides continuous real time location and telemetry over LTE Cat 1 and combines multi source positioning with onboard motion sensing and audio monitoring to support tracking, movement events and configurable alerts.

As a device that is compatible with Plaspy, the VL505 can stream position updates and device events into Plaspy for live monitoring, historical replay and fleet reporting. Its small footprint and straightforward installation make it well suited for broad deployments where operators want reliable positioning, event notifications and actionable data feeding into Plaspy dashboards and workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II installation for fast deployment and reliable vehicle power
- LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity for stable, low latency data transmission
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning and assisted location methods for dependable real time tracking
- Onboard accelerometer and microphone to capture movement events and audio for incident investigation
- Compact form factor with a small internal backup battery to provide temporary logging during short power interruptions
- Regional variants and industry certifications to support rollouts across different markets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the VL505 streams location and telemetry over cellular to supply live positions, movement events and device alerts. Plaspy ingests those updates so fleet managers can view vehicles on a map, receive notifications, and run reports that support operational oversight and safety programs.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and replay
- Event alerts such as geofence entry and exit, vibration or power disconnection surfaced in Plaspy
- Driving behavior events and movement detection provided to Plaspy for safety monitoring and coaching
- Temporary on device logging that preserves recent points during short connectivity gaps before Plaspy synchronizes historical data
- Device level configuration and provisioning information can be managed alongside Plaspy workflows to align reporting behavior with fleet policies

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and real time dispatching for cars and light commercial vehicles
- Vehicle anti theft monitoring and rapid recovery using geofences and power loss alerts
- Usage based insurance programs that require driving behavior and trip records
- Driver safety and compliance monitoring including collision and harsh driving events
- Private vehicle location sharing and family fleet oversight with discreet installation
