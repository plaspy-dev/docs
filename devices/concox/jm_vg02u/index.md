# JM-VG02U

The JM-VG02U is an INS-aided OBDII GNSS vehicle GPS tracker engineered for fast plug-and-play installation through the vehicle OBD II port. Designed for fleet management and vehicle monitoring, the JM-VG02U delivers continuous, reliable positioning by fusing multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/BDS\) with an inertial navigation system \(3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyroscope\). As a Plaspy compatible tracker, it integrates seamlessly into Plaspy’s real-time tracking and telemetry environment to provide accurate location, driving analytics, and instant alerts without complicated wiring.

Compact and tolerant of a wide input voltage range \(9–36 VDC\), the JM-VG02U is ideal for corporate car fleets, usage-based insurance \(UBI\), eco-driving programs, and general vehicle monitoring where fast deployment and dependable data are essential. Plaspy users can leverage the device’s high positioning accuracy, INS continuity in poor GPS coverage, and advanced behavior detection to improve safety, reduce risk, and streamline operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBDII installation for rapid deployment across vehicle fleets — no hardwiring required.
- INS-aided multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/BDS\) with sensor fusion for continuous position tracking even in challenging environments.
- High positioning performance: tracking sensitivity −165 dBm and high accuracy \(＜2.5 m CEP\) with fast hot start \(≤1 s\).
- Comprehensive driving behavior detection — detects eight improper behaviors \(harsh accel/brake, harsh cornering, sudden lane change, collision, skidding, rollover, roll & pitch\).
- Instant alerts for over-speeding, vehicle movement, low battery, power disconnection, and geo-fence breaches to support anti-theft and safety workflows.
- Precise mileage calculation \(≥98% accuracy\) and ignition detection via external power voltage for accurate odometer and trip logging.
- Compact, rugged form factor with onboard storage and a small Li-Polymer backup battery for brief offline operation and data persistence.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the JM-VG02U supplies continuous GNSS/INS position fixes, motion and attitude telemetry, and event-driven alerts to Plaspy’s cloud. Plaspy ingests these data streams in real time for visualization, geofencing, reporting, and automated workflows. The tracker’s OBDII connection simplifies access to vehicle power and ignition state, and its onboard sensors enable platform-level driving behavior analysis and safety alerts.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS + INS fused fixes\) for reliable tracking.
- INS-aided positioning and LBS assistance to maintain tracking where GPS is degraded or unavailable.
- Ignition status detected via external power voltage for accurate trip segmentation.
- Driving behavior events and safety alerts \(harsh acceleration/braking, collisions, roll events\) fed into Plaspy analytics.
- Mileage and odometer reporting \(≥98% accuracy\) for usage-based insurance, maintenance scheduling, and fleet optimization.
- Instant notifications for over-speed, geo-fence breaches, power disconnect, and low battery for theft detection and operational alerts.
- Onboard logging \(32+32 Mb\) to buffer data during temporary connectivity loss, synchronized to Plaspy when service resumes.
