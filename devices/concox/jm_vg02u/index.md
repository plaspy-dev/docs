# Concox - JM-VG02U

![JM-VG02U](./tracker.png)

The JM-VG02U is an INS aided OBDII GNSS vehicle GPS tracker designed for fast plug and play installation via the vehicle OBD II port. It combines multi constellation GNSS reception with inertial navigation sensors to provide continuous positioning and motion data for fleet management and vehicle monitoring without complicated wiring.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the JM-VG02U can feed real time location, driving analytics, and event alerts into Plaspy’s platform. Its plug and play form factor and sensor fusion capabilities make it a practical option for organizations that need quick deployment, reliable tracking in mixed coverage environments, and vehicle behavior data integrated into Plaspy for monitoring and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBDII form factor for rapid deployment across vehicle fleets with no hardwiring required
- INS aided multi constellation GNSS combining GPS GLONASS and BDS with sensor fusion for continuous tracking
- High positioning performance and fast hot start behavior for accurate location reporting in normal conditions
- Comprehensive driving behavior detection covering multiple unsafe event types to support safety programs
- Instant alerts for over speed movement detection power disconnect and geo fence breaches for operational response
- Precise mileage reporting and ignition detection to support trip logging maintenance planning and UBI use cases
- Compact rugged design with onboard storage and a small backup battery for short term data persistence

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the JM-VG02U streams fused GNSS and inertial position fixes along with motion and event telemetry into Plaspy. Plaspy ingests these feeds for visualization, alerting, and reporting so fleet managers and operators can monitor location and vehicle behavior in real time and through historical analysis.

- Real time location and telemetry updates using fused GNSS and INS fixes for reliable tracking
- INS aided positioning and network assistance to help maintain continuity when satellite signals are degraded
- Ignition and trip segmentation inferred from vehicle power status for accurate trip and mileage records
- Driving behavior events and safety alerts integrated into Plaspy analytics for coaching and incident review
- Mileage and odometer reporting to support usage based insurance maintenance planning and operational metrics
- Buffered onboard logging to preserve events during temporary connectivity loss and synchronize when connection returns

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for real time tracking route monitoring and mileage based reporting
- Usage based insurance deployments requiring accurate trip segmentation and mileage reconciliation
- Eco driving and driver safety programs that rely on event detection and post trip analytics
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using movement detection power disconnect alerts and geo fence triggers
- Corporate car pools and temporary vehicle assignments where fast plug and play installation is preferred
