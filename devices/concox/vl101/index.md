# Concox - VL101

![VL101](./logo..png)

The Concox VL101G is a powerful 4G vehicle tracker that combines advanced GNSS positioning technology with dual-frequency GPS for accurate and real-time location tracking. Whether you need to monitor a single vehicle or manage a fleet, this tracker offers the features and reliability you need. With LTE communication and GSM fallback, you can count on a solid connection in almost any situation. Plus, the inertial navigation system \(INS\) ensures accurate tracking even in areas with weak GPS signals.

One of the standout features of the VL101G is its ability to utilize multiple positioning systems, including GPS, BDS, GLONASS, and Galileo. This synergy ensures that locations are accurately displayed on the cloud platform, giving you peace of mind knowing the exact whereabouts of your vehicles. Additionally, the dual-frequency GPS provides a more stable connection, more accurate positioning, and higher resistance to interference.

In situations where GPS signals are weak or even non-existent, the INS-aided GPS tracking feature comes into play. This technology ensures constant tracking by using inertial sensors to estimate the vehicle's position and velocity. This means you can still monitor your vehicles even in areas with poor GPS signal coverage.

The VL101G is built to withstand the elements with its IP66 dust and water resistance rating. This waterproof case allows for easy deployment on roof panels, providing better signal acquisition and continued optimal performance regardless of the weather conditions. Whether it's rain, snow, or dust, this tracker will keep working reliably.

Other notable features of the VL101G include driving behavior analysis, ignition detection, and remote engine cut-off. With driving behavior analysis, you can receive alerts when dangerous driving behaviors are detected, allowing you to take appropriate action. The ignition detection feature provides information about when the vehicle is turned on or off, giving you valuable insights into usage patterns. And with remote engine cut-off, you have the ability to immobilize a vehicle by cutting off its power source or fuel supply via an installed relay.

The VL101G also offers versatility with its RS232 and TTL interfaces, allowing you to connect with different accessories for feature expansions. Whether you need to integrate additional sensors or devices, this tracker provides the flexibility to meet your specific requirements.
