# Concox - VL101

![VL101](./logo..png)

The Concox VL101G is a vehicle tracker designed for reliable real time location tracking and fleet oversight. Built around multi constellation GNSS positioning and dual frequency GPS, the device combines standard satellite positioning with an inertial navigation system to maintain location awareness even in areas of weak satellite coverage. It supports cellular communication with modern network fallback and is housed in an IP66 rated enclosure for durable outdoor use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VL101G can feed vehicle location and status into Plaspy's fleet management platform to provide centralized visibility and operational control. Its core features — multi GNSS positioning, INS aided continuity, ignition detection, driving behavior analysis, and remote engine cut off capability — align with common monitoring and control workflows in Plaspy, making it a practical choice for fleets that require accurate positioning and actionable telematics.

## Key Highlights

- Multi GNSS support including GPS, BDS, GLONASS, and Galileo for broader satellite coverage
- Dual frequency positioning for improved stability and accuracy in demanding environments
- INS aided tracking to maintain position estimates when satellite signals are weak
- 4G communication with fallback for consistent connectivity in varied coverage areas
- IP66 rated casing suitable for roof mounting and outdoor exposure
- Driving behavior analysis and ignition detection to support operational insights
- Remote engine cut off capability and expandable interfaces for accessory integration

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VL101G sends location and status updates to the platform so operators can monitor vehicles from a single dashboard. Plaspy consolidates the tracker's inputs into maps, alerts, and reports that support daily operations and long term analysis.

- Real time location display and breadcrumb trails for live monitoring and historical review
- Alerts and notifications for driving behavior events and ignition on off transitions
- Historical reporting for trips, routes, and asset utilization to support fleet planning
- Remote immobilization commands for vehicle recovery or administrative control where configured
- Device connectivity and status monitoring to surface communication or power issues
- Integration with Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for small to medium commercial vehicle operations
- Vehicle security and recovery with remote immobilization options
- Rental and shared vehicle monitoring to capture ignition and usage data
- Last mile delivery and logistics with emphasis on accurate positioning
- Equipment and vehicle deployment in outdoor or harsh weather conditions

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VL101G is a practical option for organizations that need a resilient tracker with strong positioning reliability and continued operation in poor signal areas. Its combination of multi GNSS support, dual frequency positioning, and INS aided tracking enhances location accuracy and continuity, which is valuable for fleets operating in urban canyons or varied terrain.

Paired with Plaspy, the tracker becomes part of a centralized telematics solution that emphasizes visibility, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy can consolidate the VL101G data into maps, event streams, and operational reports so teams can make informed decisions about routing, safety, and vehicle utilization. Where device wiring and permissions are configured, Plaspy can also surface remote immobilization controls to support security workflows.
