# Concox - JM-LG01

![JM-LG01](./tracker.png)

The JM-LG01 is a Plaspy compatible, portable GNSS GPS tracker built for long-term monitoring of unpowered assets. Engineered for low-maintenance fleet management and asset protection, the LG01 blends high-sensitivity positioning, configurable power profiles, and tamper-aware telemetry to deliver reliable, long-life tracking for car rental fleets, vehicle mortgage protection, and general asset tracking.

Designed for near-zero installation with a strong magnetic mount and IPX5 water-resistant housing, the JM-LG01 is ideal where long battery life and minimal servicing are priorities. When connected to Plaspy, the device provides real-time tracking, historical route playback, and configurable alerts — enabling proactive anti-theft workflows and data-driven asset operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: seamless integration for real-time tracking and cloud visualization.
- Ultra-long standby: intelligent power management offers up to 3 years standby \(1 fix/day, Wi‑Fi position only\).
- High-precision GNSS: outdoor accuracy under 2.5 m CEP for dependable location data.
- Durable, low-maintenance design: IPX5 water resistance and strong magnetic mount for quick attachment.
- Robust telemetry: battery level, movement detection, tamper \(light sensor\) and geo-fence alerts.
- Local data storage: on-board memory for logging when connectivity is intermittent.
- Compact and lightweight: easy to deploy across distributed assets without impacting handling.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the JM-LG01 streams validated GNSS and cellular-assisted location data into Plaspy’s dashboards and reporting tools. Plaspy ingests the device’s position fixes, telemetry, and alarm events to enable live monitoring, historical route playback, and configurable notifications to operations or security teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS + LBS/Wi‑Fi positioning modes\).
- Tamper and light-sensor alerts for anti-theft monitoring and event-driven notifications.
- Low-battery alerts and status reporting to schedule maintenance or battery replacement.
- Geo-fence breach events and schedule-based working modes processed as Plaspy alerts.
- Movement detection via onboard accelerometer to trigger wake-up, tracking, or alarms.

## Use Cases

- Car rental fleet management — long-term, low-touch GPS tracker for off-service vehicles and recovery support.
- Auto finance and vehicle mortgage protection — tamper alerts and persistent location history to protect collateral.
- General asset tracking — portable tracking for trailers, equipment, and other unpowered assets where battery longevity is essential.
- Remote installations and seasonal assets — extended standby and configurable reporting schedules to minimize maintenance.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The JM-LG01 brings a practical combination of long battery life, precise GNSS positioning, and tamper-aware telemetry that complements Plaspy’s fleet management and security features. Its strong magnetic mount and IPX5 design make deployment fast and reliable, reducing installation time and maintenance cost. Integrated local storage and intelligent power modes ensure continuity of tracking in areas with intermittent cellular coverage, while Plaspy’s cloud tools convert that data into actionable insights — from real-time tracking and anti-theft alerts to historical playback for audits and reporting.

For operations that require broad telemetry and device control such as fuel monitoring, ignition control, immobilizer functions, or Bluetooth sensors, the JM-LG01 is best used as a long-life location and event sensor within a broader Plaspy-enabled solution. Plaspy can correlate JM-LG01 location and tamper data with other telemetry sources in your deployment to deliver a complete picture of asset health and status without compromising the LG01’s core strength: low-maintenance, long-term GNSS tracking.
