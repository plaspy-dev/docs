# Concox - JM-LG01

<div className="device-logo">
![JM-LG01](./tracker.png)
</div>

The JM-LG01 is a portable GNSS GPS tracker from Concox designed for long term monitoring of unpowered assets. Built for low maintenance fleet management and asset protection, the device emphasizes long battery life, high sensitivity positioning, tamper-aware telemetry, and a rugged, water resistant enclosure with a strong magnetic mount for near zero installation effort.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the JM-LG01 can feed location fixes, event alerts, and telemetry into Plaspy’s fleet and asset management dashboards. This compatibility makes the LM01 relevant for teams that need reliable long duration tracking, historical route playback, and configurable notifications within Plaspy’s cloud platform without high ongoing servicing demands.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking, historical playback, and cloud visualization.
- Ultra long standby capability supporting extended deployments with minimal maintenance.
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning for dependable outdoor location data.
- Durable, water resistant housing and strong magnetic mount for quick deployment.
- Tamper aware telemetry including movement detection and light sensor alerts.
- Local logging to preserve position history when connectivity is intermittent.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the JM-LG01 streams validated location fixes and event telemetry into Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting tools so teams can maintain visibility across distributed assets. Plaspy ingests device positions and alarms to enable live oversight and automated workflows.

- Live location updates and position visualization in Plaspy dashboards.
- Historical route playback to review movements and support audits.
- Tamper and light sensor alerts routed to operations or security teams.
- Low battery and status notifications to plan maintenance and battery replacement.
- Geo fence breach alerts and schedule based reporting processed as Plaspy events.

## Typical Use Cases

- Car rental fleet management for off service vehicles and recovery support.
- Vehicle mortgage and collateral protection with persistent location history.
- Tracking trailers, equipment, and other unpowered assets where battery life is critical.
- Remote or seasonal installations that require long standby and low maintenance.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The JM-LG01 pairs long life tracking and tamper aware telemetry with a compact, lightweight form factor that reduces installation time and operational overhead. Its design is aimed at organizations that need a low touch, durable location sensor to feed Plaspy’s monitoring, alerting, and reporting features.
