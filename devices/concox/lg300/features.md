# Concox - LG300 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Concox LG300 and how the device is used with Plaspy for fleet management and asset tracking. It summarizes the LG300 capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users and describes how those capabilities map into monitoring, alerting, and operational workflows on the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation method. Manufacturer firmware settings, cellular network coverage, and device configuration will affect reporting options and runtime characteristics, so always confirm device details against the manufacturer documentation and current firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The LG300 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible asset tracker designed for long standby deployments where environmental resilience and persistent location telemetry are required. It combines multi constellation positioning, a high capacity battery, tamper detection, and flexible reporting options to support extended monitoring of vehicles, containers, and portable assets.

- High sensitivity multi constellation positioning for reliable location fixes in diverse environments.
- Large capacity battery designed for extended standby deployments and redeployable use.
- Rugged IP66 housing to protect the device from dust and water exposure in demanding field conditions.
- Tamper and motion detection plus sound triggered alerts to support anti theft monitoring and incident context capture.
- Remote reporting via cellular cloud and SMS with configurable tracking modes to balance update frequency and battery life.

## Core Features of Concox - LG300

- Multi constellation GNSS positioning for improved location reliability.
- Long life rechargeable battery sized for extended standby operation.
- IP66 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water ingress in field deployments.
- Tamper detection using a light sensor and a 3 axis accelerometer for movement and tamper events.
- Sound triggered alerting with automatic and on demand remote audio recording for incident context.
- Remote monitoring via SMS or cloud reporting and configurable tracking modes to tune reporting cadence.
- Remote firmware updates over the air to keep deployed devices up to date.
- Compact rugged form factor intended for vehicles, trailers, containers, and portable assets.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event data from compatible LG300 devices to present consolidated tracking, alerting, and historical playback for operations teams. The platform uses the device event stream and reporting cadence to display real time status while allowing administrators to tune notifications and retention to match operational needs.

- Real time location updates and historical playback based on the device reporting cadence.
- Motion and tamper events surfaced as alerts for anti theft and unauthorized movement monitoring.
- Battery state and low battery notifications provided to help plan maintenance and redeployment.
- Sound triggered alerts and recorded audio events made available for incident review when enabled.
- Configurable tracking modes reflected in Plaspy as differing update frequencies and event densities to balance visibility and battery life.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet and vehicle tracking where long standby performance reduces maintenance visits.
- Container and trailer monitoring in logistics operations that require rugged enclosures.
- High value cargo protection using motion detection and audio context for incident investigation.
- Rental asset tracking where redeployable battery and remote status reporting simplify turnover.
- Remote asset monitoring of off grid equipment that needs periodic location and event telemetry.
