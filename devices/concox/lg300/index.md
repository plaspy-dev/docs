# Concox - LG300

<div className="device-logo">
![LG300](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Concox LG300 is a rugged asset tracker built for persistent industrial grade tracking of vehicles, containers and high value cargo. Designed for long standby deployments, the device combines multi constellation positioning with a high capacity battery and an IP66 enclosure to deliver reliable location and tamper telemetry in demanding field conditions. Built to record movement events and capture audio context when needed, the LG300 focuses on durable, low maintenance tracking for remote or mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LG300 integrates with Plaspy to provide consolidated fleet management and real time tracking. Location updates and event telemetry from the LG300 feed into Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reporting so teams can monitor asset status, respond to possible theft or incidents, and balance reporting cadence against battery life. This compatibility makes the LG300 a practical option for organizations that want rugged hardware tied into a scalable tracking platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS asset tracker designed for persistent, long standby tracking.
- Multi constellation positioning for improved location resilience in the field.
- High capacity battery designed for extended deployments and redeployment cycles.
- IP66 rated enclosure for protection against dust and heavy spray in harsh environments.
- Tamper detection and motion alerts to support anti theft monitoring and event notification.
- Sound triggered alerts and remote audio capture to add incident context when required.
- Remote reporting via cloud or SMS and configurable tracking modes to optimize battery life.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the LG300 provides continuous location and event data that Plaspy ingests and presents through platform tools for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy uses the device stream to generate real time position views, alert workflows and historical playback while allowing teams to configure reporting behavior to extend battery life for long deployments.

- Real time location and telemetry delivered to Plaspy for live map views and operational monitoring.
- Tamper and motion events forwarded as alerts to support anti theft workflows and immediate investigation.
- Audio triggered events and recorded snippets associated with incidents for contextual reporting in Plaspy.
- Low battery and status notifications routed to Plaspy so operators can plan maintenance or redeployment.
- Configurable reporting modes enable trade offs between position update frequency and battery longevity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and vehicle rental where long idle periods require persistent visibility and tamper alerts.
- Logistics and freight tracking for containers and trailers that need rugged protection and long standby operation.
- High value cargo protection using motion detection and audio capture to support investigations.
- Rental asset lifecycle and redeployment scenarios where rechargeable long life battery simplifies turnover.
- Remote or off grid assets that require reliable position fixes and event telemetry over extended intervals.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LG300 is suitable for organizations that require a rugged, long life tracker that can be consolidated into an enterprise tracking platform. Its combination of resilient enclosure, extended battery performance and event capture features makes it a practical choice for asset owners who need reliable visibility without frequent servicing. When paired with Plaspy, LG300 telemetry becomes part of broader fleet operations, enabling monitoring, alerts and reporting alongside other vehicle or asset data managed by the platform.
