# Concox - LG300

![LG300](./tracker.png)

The LG300 rugged asset tracker is Plaspy compatible and built for persistent industrial-grade tracking of vehicles, containers and high-value cargo. As a GPS tracker engineered for long standby deployments, the LG300 combines multi-constellation positioning with an industrial 10,000 mAh battery and an IP66 enclosure to deliver reliable location and tamper telemetry where long life and environmental resilience are required.

Designed to integrate with Plaspy for fleet management and real-time tracking, the LG300 sends location and event data to the cloud or via SMS, enabling anti-theft monitoring, remote incident audio capture, and configurable reporting to balance telemetry detail and battery longevity. When paired with Plaspy, this Plaspy compatible device becomes part of a scalable tracking solution that complements additional fleet telemetry such as fuel monitoring or immobilizer controls managed by the platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Multi-constellation positioning \(GPS + BDS + LBS\) with reported accuracy under 2.5 m CEP.
- Long standby operation powered by a 10,000 mAh rechargeable Li‑Polymer battery for extended deployments.
- Rugged IP66 enclosure and operating range to withstand field conditions and harsh environments.
- Tamper detection via light sensor and 3-axis accelerometer for anti-theft alerts and movement monitoring.
- Sound-triggered alert and automatic/remote audio recording to capture incident context.
- Remote monitoring via SMS or cloud platform and configurable tracking modes to optimize battery life vs. reporting frequency.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LG300 transmits GNSS position fixes and event telemetry over GSM to Plaspy’s ingestion endpoints or can be polled/commanded via SMS when network connectivity requires it. Plaspy ingests the LG300’s location stream, motion and tamper events, battery state, and audio-trigger alerts to provide consolidated dashboards, alerts and historical reports for fleet management teams. Configurable reporting modes let you trade reporting frequency for battery life; Plaspy uses the device’s reporting cadence to generate accurate real-time tracking and historical playback.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via GSM/cloud or SMS.
- Tamper and motion detection \(light sensor + 3-axis accelerometer\) for anti-theft alerts.
- Low battery alerts \(user-configurable threshold; device alerts at \<20% by default\) reported to Plaspy.
- Sound-triggered alerts with automatic and on-demand remote audio recording for incident context.
- Configurable working modes \(regular GPS mode and dedicated tracking mode\) to balance reporting frequency and battery life.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LG300 is a focused Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for organizations that need robust, field-proven telemetry without frequent maintenance. Its multi-constellation GNSS performance, high-sensitivity tracking, and long-life 10,000 mAh battery make it well suited to extended standby deployments common in fleet management and logistics. Integrated tamper detection, sound-triggered events and remote audio recording add practical anti-theft and incident-capture capabilities that feed directly into Plaspy’s alerting and reporting workflows.

By integrating the LG300 into your Plaspy environment you gain reliable real-time tracking and telemetry while preserving battery life through configurable reporting modes. Plaspy can consolidate LG300 data alongside other telemetry — for example fuel monitoring or immobilizer/ignition controls managed by the platform — to provide a complete operational view. The combination of rugged hardware, OTA firmware management, and cloud-ready reporting makes the LG300 a dependable asset for demanding fleet and asset tracking programs.
