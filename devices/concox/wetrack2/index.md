# Concox - WeTrack2

![WeTrack2](./tracker.png)

The WeTrack2 is a compact vehicle GNSS tracker engineered for e-scooters, motorcycles, light vehicles and industrial equipment. Built to industrial electronics standards with an IP65-rated enclosure and a wide 9–90 VDC operating range, WeTrack2 delivers reliable real-time tracking and fleet management capabilities while minimizing battery drain and downtime. This Plaspy compatible GPS tracker brings essential telemetry and anti-theft controls—such as ignition \(ACC\) detection and remote fuel/power cut-off—into a small, rugged package for dependable field use.

Designed for operators who need accurate location, driving-event alerts and simple integration, the WeTrack2 provides sub-2.5 m positional accuracy and long-range GNSS sensitivity. Its compact form, onboard backup battery and local data buffering make it a solid choice where continuous visibility and remote immobilizer control are required. Integrate WeTrack2 with Plaspy to unlock live dashboards, alerts and reports for better fleet oversight and faster stolen-vehicle recovery.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivering dependable real-time tracking for scooters, motorcycles and light vehicles.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and industrial-grade design for outdoor fleet management and anti-theft applications.
- Wide 9–90 VDC operating range fits a broad set of vehicles including scooters, golf carts and industrial equipment.
- Remote immobilizer capability \(external relay\) for fuel/power cut-off to support theft response and operational control.
- High-sensitivity GNSS \(GPS + BDS + LBS\) with \<2.5 m CEP accuracy for precise location and route monitoring.
- Low-power design and optimized battery protection with a 270 mAh Li-Polymer backup battery to prevent vehicle battery drain.
- Onboard event detection \(accelerometer\) for driving-event alerts, over-speed warnings and movement notifications.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the WeTrack2 streams location and vehicle telemetry into the Plaspy platform for real-time tracking, alerting and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions, event triggers and status updates from the tracker, converting them into geofences, speed alerts, ignition reports and dashboard metrics you can act on immediately.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent from GPS/BDS/LBS to Plaspy for live tracking and replay.
- Ignition \(ACC\) detection reported to Plaspy to indicate vehicle on/off status and to trigger workflow events.
- Movement, over-speed and geo-fence entry/exit alerts for proactive fleet management and driver coaching.
- Remote immobilizer control \(external relay\) enabling Plaspy users to execute fuel/power cut-off for anti-theft response.
- Onboard buffering \(32+32 Mb\) and a backup battery to preserve data and maintain reporting during short network outages.
