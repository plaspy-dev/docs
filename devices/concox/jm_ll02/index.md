# Concox - JM-LL02

<div className="device-logo">
![JM-LL02](./tracker.png)
</div>

The JM-LL02 from Concox is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for long term asset deployments where near zero installation effort and extended standby are essential. It combines multi source positioning with cellular connectivity and a high capacity industrial battery to provide continuous location reporting and event telemetry for movable assets such as trailers, containers and construction equipment. The device is built with an IP67 enclosure, integrated magnetic mount and on board Bluetooth configuration to support tool free redeployment and straightforward field setup.

As a Plaspy compatible device the JM-LL02 can stream its location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its emphasis on battery longevity, tamper detection and reliable positioning makes it well suited to Plaspy workflows that focus on asset security, fleet oversight and logistics visibility. Organizations that use Plaspy can leverage the JM-LL02 to reduce maintenance cycles, improve anti theft response and maintain continuous tracking across long hauls.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat M1 and NB2 connectivity and GSM fallback for resilient cellular coverage
- Long life 6,000 mAh industrial battery designed for extended standby and reduced maintenance intervals
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and magnetic mount for non permanent attachment and redeployment
- Multi source positioning with high sensitivity and sub 2.5 m CEP accuracy for precise location reporting
- On board Bluetooth 5.0 for cable free configuration and fast provisioning in the field
- Scenario alerts for low battery tamper vibration over speed and geo fence events to support anti theft workflows
- Local buffering to protect against temporary connectivity loss and preserve recent telemetry

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the JM-LL02 turns GNSS and on board events into actionable dashboards and automated alerts. The tracker reports location and scenario notifications to Plaspy so teams can monitor assets in real time, review historical routes and respond to events. Bluetooth simplifies initial setup so devices can be provisioned to report into Plaspy with minimal field effort.

- Real time location updates and historical route playback in Plaspy mapping views
- Geofence entry and exit notifications routed to Plaspy for automated alerting and workflows
- Scenario alerts such as low battery tamper vibration and over speed delivered into Plaspy event streams
- Buffered local storage to ensure recent data is forwarded to Plaspy after temporary connectivity interruptions
- Bluetooth based provisioning and redeployment to reduce setup time when assigning devices to assets

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for rental vehicles and light trailers where magnetic mounting and long battery life lower operating costs
- Asset monitoring for construction equipment and movable plant with tamper and vibration alerts for security
- Container and logistics monitoring during extended transit requiring long standby and buffered reporting
- Anti theft and recovery workflows that rely on geo fence and tamper notifications delivered to Plaspy
- Rapid redeployment programs where Bluetooth configuration and non invasive mounting speed up asset turnover
