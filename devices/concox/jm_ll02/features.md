# Concox - JM-LL02 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Concox JM-LL02 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet management, asset tracking and security monitoring. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about what the device reports and how those reports are reflected inside Plaspy rather than step by step provisioning or private implementation details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a functional summary for planning and operational purposes, and consult the device manufacturer for the most current technical details when you need exact specifications.

## Feature Overview

The JM-LL02 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible tracker designed for long standby deployments and easy redeployment. It combines cellular connectivity, robust battery life and tool free mounting to deliver practical location and event telemetry for movable assets.

- Long life battery designed for extended standby and reduced maintenance cycles
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and compact magnetic mount for non permanent installations
- Multi source positioning with high sensitivity and sub 2.5 meter CEP accuracy for reliable location reports
- Bluetooth 5.0 for on site, cable free configuration and quick provisioning
- Scenario alerts such as tamper, low battery, vibration, over speed and geo fence for anti theft and operations monitoring

## Core Features of Concox - JM-LL02

- Cellular connectivity using LTE Cat M1 and NB2 with GSM fallback for resilient reporting to Plaspy
- High capacity 6,000 mAh industrial Li Polymer battery and power saving modes for long deployments
- IP67 rated enclosure with wide operating temperature range for harsh environments
- Integrated strong magnetic mount and magnetic charging port for fast, non invasive installation and maintenance
- GPS plus LBS positioning with high GNSS sensitivity and sub 2.5 m CEP positioning accuracy
- Bluetooth 5.0 radio for direct device configuration during deployment or re provisioning
- Tamper detection using an integrated light sensor and support for vibration and over speed alerts
- Local buffered storage to retain data when cellular coverage is temporarily unavailable

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The JM-LL02 reports location and event telemetry to Plaspy where that information becomes part of dashboards, alert streams and historical records. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and presents device events in a unified operational view so teams can act on location and safety signals quickly.

- Real time location updates and historical route playback in Plaspy mapping views
- Geofence entry and exit events converted into rules and notifications inside Plaspy
- Tamper, low battery, vibration and over speed alerts sent to Plaspy for automated workflows
- Buffered reporting from local storage ensures Plaspy receives missed events after temporary connectivity loss
- Bluetooth enabled provisioning simplifies initial setup so devices arrive configured to report into Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking where magnetic mounting and long battery life reduce installation time and maintenance
- Construction equipment and movable plant monitoring with tamper and vibration alerts for security workflows
- Rental vehicle tracking for fleets that benefit from non permanent attachment and quick redeployment
- Logistics monitoring for long hauls where standby time and buffered storage preserve reporting between charges
- Anti theft processes that rely on geo fence, tamper and movement alerts routed into centralized operations

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware differences may enable or change specific alert thresholds, reporting intervals and power modes
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect supported cellular bands and precise behavior in the field
- Installation method influences available signals for tamper detection and battery maintenance procedures
- Bluetooth provisioning capabilities depend on the device firmware and paired configuration tools
