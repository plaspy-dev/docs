# Concox - VL101G

<div className="device-logo">
![VL101G](./tracker.png)
</div>

The VL101G is a rooftop vehicle terminal designed for professional fleet management and vehicle telematics. Built for exposed installations, it pairs high precision positioning with robust cellular connectivity and vehicle oriented inputs and outputs to provide real time tracking, tamper resilience, and vehicle status data suitable for fleets, rental operations, and usage based insurance programs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VL101G feeds GNSS positions, telemetry and discrete I O events into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor assets, respond to security events, and analyze driving behavior. Its combination of dual frequency GNSS, inertial navigation support, cellular uplink and onboard data buffering makes it a practical choice where continuous visibility and reliable history are important for fleet oversight within Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for real time tracking and fleet management workflows
- Dual frequency GNSS L1 and L5 with GPS BDS GLONASS and Galileo support for improved positioning in difficult environments
- LTE Cat 1 cellular uplink with GSM fallback for broad coverage and dependable data transmission
- Vehicle focused inputs and outputs plus relay control readiness for ignition detection and immobilizer applications
- Rugged IP66 enclosure and wide operating temperature range for rooftop and exposed vehicle use
- Onboard storage and an industrial backup battery to preserve logs and position history during power or connectivity loss
- Built in accelerometer and gyroscope to support driving behavior analytics and event detection

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the VL101G streams location fixes, vehicle telemetry and input/output events to the platform so fleets gain real time visibility, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy normalizes and presents the device feed within dashboards and workflows to help operators monitor movement, evaluate risk and act on security or operational events.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy over the cellular uplink
- Ignition and movement events mapped to Plaspy dashboards and alerts for start stop detection and unauthorized movement
- Driving behavior events such as harsh acceleration or harsh braking reported as telematics events for coaching and risk scoring
- Remote immobilizer and relay control workflows supported to assist with anti theft and recovery procedures
- Offline data buffering and backup battery operation maintain continuity of logs and position history during connectivity interruptions

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for continuous tracking routing driver scoring and maintenance planning
- Vehicle rental and leasing for usage monitoring geo fence enforcement and asset protection
- Usage based insurance programs that require high fidelity driving events and accurate position records
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using ignition detection remote immobilizer and movement alerts
- Telematics and diagnostics where consolidated vehicle telemetry supports operational reporting

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VL101G is suited to operators who need precise positioning and resilient data flow from rooftop mounted trackers. Its dual frequency GNSS and inertial navigation elements reduce position drift in marginal signal conditions, while cellular resilience, onboard storage and a backup battery help preserve historical data for later analysis in Plaspy. Vehicle oriented I O and relay readiness allow integration into security and immobilizer workflows that Plaspy can manage and report on.

If you want a tracker that combines strong positioning, vehicle level control and data continuity for use with Plaspy, the VL101G is a practical option to consider.
