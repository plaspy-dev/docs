# Concox - GT06N

<div className="device-logo">
![GT06N](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Concox GT06N is a multifunctional vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable fleet management and vehicle monitoring. Built for continuous position reporting and event detection, the GT06N supports ignition detection, SOS panic alerts, audio monitoring and remote immobilizer control, and includes a compact form factor and backup battery for continuity in the field.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the GT06N feeds location and event data into Plaspy for visualization, alerts and reporting. Its combination of precise GNSS positioning, practical I O and multiple event triggers makes it well suited to organizations that want to add vehicle tracking and anti theft controls to their Plaspy deployment.

## Key Highlights

- Proven vehicle tracker designed for fleet management and anti theft monitoring.
- High precision GNSS positioning with accuracy suitable for vehicle tracking.
- Remote immobilizer support for authorized fuel or power cut off as part of anti theft workflows.
- ACC ignition input and digital I O for vehicle state detection and event reporting.
- Built in audio monitoring with external microphone support for in cab listening where permitted.
- Rugged electrical design with wide input voltage range and industrial grade backup battery to maintain tracking during power interruptions.
- Multiple event alerts including overspeed, geo fence, vibration, low battery and power off to drive operational notifications.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the GT06N transmits location, input states and alarm events to the platform so operators can monitor fleets in real time and review historical trips. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and makes it available for mapping, alerting rules and reporting to support daily operations and incident response.

- Real time location updates and historical playback for route review and audit.
- Ignition status and digital input events mapped into Plaspy to differentiate running and parked states.
- SOS panic and power off reports routed to Plaspy alerts for rapid incident notification.
- Remote immobilizer control available to authorized operators through Plaspy workflows for theft response.
- Event driven alerts such as overspeed, geo fence and vibration tied into Plaspy rules and notification channels.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization workflows for corporate vehicle fleets and rentals.
- Real time fleet monitoring for dispatch, route oversight and incident response.
- Rental and lease operations requiring SOS alerts and event logs for driver safety and dispute resolution.
- Logistics and delivery monitoring where vibration and position alerts assist in tamper detection.
- General telemetry expansion when accessory inputs are used to capture additional vehicle status.

The GT06N offers a balanced set of features that align with common fleet management needs: reliable GNSS positioning, practical I O for vehicle events, and anti theft controls that integrate into platform workflows. Paired with Plaspy, these capabilities become actionable through mapping, alerting and reporting tools that help operations teams maintain visibility and respond to incidents.
