# GT06N Vehicle Tracker \(Plaspy Compatible\)

The GT06N is a proven, multifunctional GPS tracker designed for reliable fleet management and vehicle monitoring. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the GT06N delivers precise location data, robust telemetry, and anti-theft controls so fleet operators can manage vehicles with confidence. Its compact design, industrial-grade backup battery and wide input voltage range make the GT06N a practical choice for corporate fleets, rentals and logistics operations.

Built for real-time tracking and secure remote control, the GT06N supports ignition \(ACC\) detection, SOS panic alerts, audio monitoring and remote immobilizer \(fuel/power cut-off\) via relay control. With support for multiple alerts — overspeed, geo-fence, abnormal vibration, low battery and power-off — the unit complements Plaspy’s alerting and reporting tools to keep your operations running smoothly and securely.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for seamless integration into real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.
- High-precision GNSS positioning using GPS + BDS + LBS with accuracy better than 2.5 m CEP.
- Remote immobilizer capability \(fuel/power cut-off\) via relay control for effective anti-theft response.
- ACC/ignition input and digital I/O for vehicle telemetry and event detection.
- Built-in voice monitoring with external microphone support for in-cab audio if required by policy.
- Rugged electrical design with 9–36 V DC wide input and 450 mAh backup battery for uninterrupted tracking.
- Multiple event alerts \(overspeed, geo-fence, vibration, low battery, power-off\) to trigger Plaspy notifications and workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GT06N integrates with Plaspy to provide continuous, actionable vehicle data. Once installed and configured, the unit transmits GNSS coordinates, status inputs and alarm events to Plaspy where data is visualized on maps, included in reports and routed to alerting rules for operations teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and historical playback.
- ACC/ignition status reporting to distinguish running versus parked states and to support ignition-based alerts.
- Digital input events such as SOS panic button and power-off reports mapped into Plaspy alerts and incident logs.
- Remote immobilizer \(relay\) control enabling authorized operators to cut fuel/power through Plaspy-managed commands.
- Peripheral expansion support allows integration of fuel level probes or external Bluetooth gateways where fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensors are required.
