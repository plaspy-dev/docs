# Concox - LL302 Features

This page provides a public overview of the Concox LL302 tracker and the practical feature context for using the device with Plaspy. It summarizes the LL302’s user-facing capabilities, typical operational value, and how telemetry from the device appears in Plaspy for monitoring and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant (LL302‑E / LL302‑L), optional peripherals, and how the device is installed. For complete device-specific details consult the manufacturer’s official documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The LL302 is a Plaspy compatible 4G LTE Cat 1 asset tracker designed for long deployments and secure mounting. It combines multi‑source positioning, durable battery capacity, and a small, stealthy form factor to deliver reliable location and sensor data for fleet and asset monitoring.

- Multi‑source positioning using GNSS plus network and Wi‑Fi assistance to improve fix reliability and accuracy.
- Long endurance from an industrial 6,000 mAh battery with configurable reporting modes for tracking or power saving.
- Built‑in sensors for movement and tamper detection plus optional environmental sensors for condition monitoring.
- Rugged magnetic mount and IPX5 water resistance suitable for long term external mounting on metal assets.
- Regional hardware variants and cellular fallback to broaden coverage for global deployments.

## Core Features of Concox - LL302

- 4G LTE Cat 1 primary cellular connectivity with 2G GSM fallback for resilient reporting.
- Multi‑source positioning: GPS and BDS with LBS and Wi‑Fi assisted location to enhance accuracy.
- Large 6,000 mAh industrial Li‑Polymer battery with configurable modes for tracking and extended standby life.
- Accelerometer for vibration and movement detection to support tamper and motion alerts.
- Light sensor for tamper exposure detection and hall effect sensor support for door or status monitoring.
- Optional temperature and humidity sensors for environmental telemetry when fitted.
- Stealthy magnetic mounting design and IPX5 water jet protection for rugged installations.
- Fast GNSS performance characteristics enabling rapid fixes in many environments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy the LL302 streams location and sensor telemetry so operators can monitor assets and vehicles in near real time. Plaspy ingests position updates and events, allowing configurable alerting and reporting for operational oversight.

- Real‑time location updates and historical route playback based on GNSS fixes sent by the device.
- Movement and tamper events from the accelerometer and light sensor appear as alerts in Plaspy dashboards.
- Door and status changes driven by the hall effect sensor are available as discrete events for monitoring.
- Optional temperature and humidity readings are recorded and can be used for condition monitoring and threshold alerts.
- Configurable geo‑fence, low battery, and movement alerting workflows enable automated notifications and operator response.
- Plaspy automatically recognizes compatible trackers and surfaces available telemetry and status without requiring manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental vehicle fleets that need stealthy installation, long battery life, and tamper alerts to reduce loss.
- Logistics and transportation monitoring where real‑time location and optional environmental sensing improve operations.
- High‑value equipment and trailer tracking using magnetic mounting and precise GNSS fixes.
- Cold‑chain and container monitoring when optional temperature and humidity sensors are fitted.
- Long‑term asset management for distributed equipment where maintenance intervals must be minimized.
- Site or yard monitoring for vibration and exposure events on parked assets.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Functionality can depend on firmware version; features such as reporting modes and sensor reporting cadence may differ by release.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants (LL302‑E vs LL302‑L) affect cellular band support and should be confirmed for your region.
- Optional peripherals such as temperature and humidity sensors may not be present on every unit and could require specific configuration.
- Installation method and antenna placement influence GNSS performance and reported accuracy.
- Refer to Concox documentation for regulatory and regional differences that may change available features.
