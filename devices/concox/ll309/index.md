# Concox - LL309

![LL309](./tracker.png)

The LL309 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for refrigerated cargo, parcel delivery and sensitive asset protection where continuous environmental monitoring matters. Combining multi-source positioning \(GPS + BDS + LBS\) with a built-in temperature and humidity sensor, the LL309 delivers precise location and environmental telemetry over LTE Cat.1 for reliable real-time tracking and alerting across cold chain and high-value logistics.

The device is optimized for harsh logistics environments: IP67 protection, internal GNSS and Bluetooth antennas, multi-mode power management and robust data retention for offline periods. Plaspy integration lets operations teams ingest location, temperature/humidity, tamper and battery status into fleet management dashboards and anti-theft workflows, while local BLE configuration and Type‑C export simplify deployment and post-trip analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that delivers real-time tracking and environmental telemetry for cold chain and parcel logistics.
- Built-in temperature and humidity sensor provides continuous condition monitoring and exception alerts to protect perishable goods.
- LTE Cat.1 cellular uplink for timely data transmission; caches up to 20,000 records when coverage is unavailable and exports via Type‑C USB.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and wide operating temperature range \(–20°C to +70°C\) suitable for refrigerated cargo and outdoor assets.
- Low-power 2,500 mAh Li‑Po battery with up to 30 days life at 1 update/hour and multiple power modes \(Timing, Intelligent, Power‑saving\).
- Local BLE 5.1 configuration and internal antennas reduce installation complexity and speed integration with Plaspy.
- Multi-alarm capability: temperature/humidity exceptions, tamper \(light sensor\), vibration alert and low battery notifications for immediate action.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed, the LL309 streams location and environmental telemetry to Plaspy over LTE Cat.1 for real-time tracking and automated alerts. Plaspy receives GNSS-derived coordinates and sensor readings and applies configurable rule sets and notifications for fleet management, cold chain monitoring and anti-theft responses. If cellular coverage is interrupted, the LL309 caches readings locally and either uploads them automatically when connectivity returns or allows bulk export via the Type‑C interface for forensic analysis.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy via LTE Cat.1 for continuous visibility.
- Temperature and humidity exception alerts forwarded instantly to Plaspy rules and notification channels.
- Tamper \(light sensor\), vibration and low battery status sent as events for anti-theft and maintenance workflows.
- Offline caching of up to 20,000 records plus 5,000 GPS buffer entries; bulk export via Type‑C for post-trip data recovery and compliance reporting.
- BLE 5.1 local configuration using Jimi IoT Lab app, Tracksolid Pro, SMS or PC tools for quick commissioning with Plaspy integration settings.

The LL309 is a purpose-built Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for logistics teams that need both location and environmental telemetry in one compact unit. Its LTE Cat.1 uplink supports real-time tracking while robust offline caching and Type‑C export maintain data continuity for audits and analysis. For operations that require continuous condition monitoring alongside precise GPS tracking and dependable data retention, the LL309 offers a focused, reliable option to integrate into your Plaspy fleet management and anti‑theft ecosystem.
