# Concox - LL309

<div className="device-logo">
![LL309](./tracker.png)
</div>

The LL309 is a compact GPS tracker designed for refrigerated cargo, parcel delivery, and sensitive asset protection where continuous environmental monitoring is required. It combines multi-source positioning with an integrated temperature and humidity sensor to provide location and condition data suited to cold chain and high-value logistics. The device is built for demanding transport scenarios, offering rugged environmental protection and persistent data retention during offline periods.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LL309 feeds location and environmental telemetry into Plaspy fleet management dashboards and alerting workflows. That compatibility makes it straightforward for operations teams to include condition monitoring alongside vehicle and asset tracking, enabling rule based alerts, visibility across shipments, and consolidated reporting within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Real-time location and environmental telemetry tailored for cold chain and parcel logistics.
- Built-in temperature and humidity sensor for continuous condition monitoring and exception alerts.
- Cellular uplink for timely data transmission plus local caching and Type‑C export for post-trip recovery.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and wide operating temperature range for refrigerated cargo and outdoor use.
- Internal antennas and local configuration options reduce installation complexity and speed commissioning.
- Multi-alarm capability including temperature and humidity excursions, tamper and low battery notifications.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once integrated, the LL309 sends location fixes and environmental sensor readings into Plaspy so teams can monitor shipments, trigger alerts, and run operational reporting. Plaspy ingests the device events and telemetry, applies configurable rules, and surfaces notifications and historical data to support cold chain compliance and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates provide continuous visibility in Plaspy dashboards.
- Temperature and humidity exceptions are forwarded to Plaspy rules and notification channels for rapid response.
- Tamper, vibration, and low battery events are captured as actionable events for maintenance and security workflows.
- Offline caching and bulk export preserve data for post-trip analysis and regulatory traceability when coverage is intermittent.
- Local configuration and on-device export simplify commissioning and forensic data retrieval when integrated with Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain freight protection with continuous condition monitoring and automated alerts.
- Parcel level monitoring for perishable goods such as food, pharmaceuticals, and biologics.
- High-value asset tracking where tamper and vibration alerts support anti-theft response.
- Logistics operations with intermittent connectivity that require reliable data retention and post-trip export.
- Last-mile and multi-modal shipments needing a compact tracker that fits inside packages and refrigerated compartments.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LL309 is a focused solution for organizations that need combined location and environmental telemetry within their fleet management stack. Its compact form, environmental sensors, rugged housing, and offline data handling make it a practical choice for cold chain and parcel logistics teams that want to bring condition monitoring into Plaspy without adding separate systems.
