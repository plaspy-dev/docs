# Concox - LL309 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Concox LL309 GPS tracker and how its capabilities are presented when used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, non-sensitive aspects of the device such as location tracking, built-in temperature and humidity telemetry, rugged packaging, and how those data points feed into Plaspy dashboards and alerting workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Refer to official Concox documentation and release notes for the most current device-specific technical details and configuration options.

## Feature Overview

The LL309 is a compact tracker designed for refrigerated cargo, parcel delivery and sensitive asset protection where environmental monitoring is required alongside location. It combines multi-source positioning with an internal temperature and humidity sensor and a cellular uplink optimized for logistics workflows.

- Continuous location updates using multi-source GNSS positioning to support route visibility and recovery.
- Built-in temperature and humidity telemetry for ongoing condition monitoring of perishable goods.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and internal antennas for reliable use in harsh logistics environments.
- Local BLE configuration and Type‑C export to simplify commissioning and post-trip data retrieval.
- Offline data caching to preserve records during connectivity interruptions and ensure continuity for compliance.

## Core Features of Concox - LL309

- Multi-source positioning (GPS + BDS + LBS) for consistent location awareness in mixed reception environments.
- Integrated temperature sensor and humidity sensor for environmental condition monitoring.
- LTE Cat.1 cellular uplink for timely telemetry transmission to fleet platforms.
- Offline data storage with the ability to export cached records via Type‑C USB when needed.
- BLE 5.1 local configuration for quick commissioning and adjustments without disassembly.
- IP67 rated housing for dust and water resistance in demanding logistics and refrigerated conditions.
- Multi-alarm reporting including temperature/humidity exceptions, tamper (light sensor), vibration alerts, and low battery notifications.
- Multiple power management modes and a rechargeable battery to balance operating life and reporting frequency.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the LL309 streams location and environmental telemetry so operations teams can monitor conditions and respond to exceptions. Plaspy receives positional fixes and sensor readings and makes them available for visualization, rules, and notifications.

- Real-time location points and tracking history visible in Plaspy maps and playback tools.
- Temperature and humidity readings forwarded as telemetry and used to trigger configurable alerts and workflows.
- Tamper, vibration, and battery status surfaced as event records for maintenance and anti-theft processes.
- Cached data uploaded or recovered for continuity so Plaspy can present a complete trace after connectivity gaps.
- Local BLE configuration and device export options simplify initial setup and allow Plaspy-compatible settings to be applied during commissioning.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain freight protection with continuous temperature and humidity monitoring and Plaspy alerts for excursions.
- Parcel-level environmental tracking for perishable deliveries and pharmaceutical logistics.
- High-value asset monitoring where tamper and vibration events feed anti-theft response workflows.
- Intermittent-coverage shipments that rely on robust caching and later bulk upload for compliance reporting.
- Last-mile placements inside containers or refrigerated compartments where compact size and internal antennas reduce installation work.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions and feature sets may differ between units; some alarms or telemetry rates can be firmware dependent.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants (for example cellular band support) may affect connectivity and performance.
- Installation details such as antenna orientation, placement inside insulated compartments, or mounting can influence GNSS accuracy and sensor readings.
- Offline cache sizes, export behavior, and exact battery life depend on configured reporting intervals and power mode selection.
- Always consult the device manual and the manufacturer for the most current specifications and configuration guidance.
