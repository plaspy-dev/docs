# Concox - LL702

![LL702](./tracker.png)

The LL702 by an industrial tracker manufacturer is a compact 4G Cat.1 GPS tracker designed for long-term asset monitoring and covert installation. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the LL702 pairs multi-source positioning \(GPS, BDS, LBS and Wi‑Fi hotspots\) with LTE Cat 1 communication and 2G GSM fallback so fleets and asset managers get reliable location data across wide coverage areas.

Engineered for minimal maintenance, the LL702 features a 4,200 mAh industrial Li‑Manganese battery and a strong magnetic base for near-zero installation on metal surfaces. When integrated with Plaspy, this GPS tracker delivers persistent telemetry and alerting—supporting anti-theft workflows, fleet management dashboards, and long‑term asset programs that require covert mounting and multi-year standby.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat.1 and 2G GSM fallback for broad cellular coverage.
- Exceptional standby life: 4,200 mAh Li‑Mn battery with up to 3 years in default power‑saving mode \(one position/day\).
- Multi‑source positioning \(GPS, BDS, LBS, Wi‑Fi hotspots\) for reliable fixes in challenging environments.
- Covert, low‑profile design with a strong magnetic base for secure hidden mounting on metal assets.
- Configurable working modes \(tracking, power‑saving, week‑based, alarm‑based\) to tailor reporting cadence and power use.
- Instant multi‑event alerts: tamper \(light sensor\), low battery, geofence entry/exit, abnormal vibration, and device fall/removal.
- Onboard storage for position buffering when the device is in a cellular blind spot; queued fixes upload when connection resumes.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once connected to Plaspy, the LL702 feeds location and event data into Plaspy’s platform for real‑time tracking, alerting, and historical reporting. Plaspy reads the LL702’s multi‑source GNSS and auxiliary event messages to build a continuous activity trail for vehicles and assets. Configure the LL702’s working mode through Plaspy or the device configuration workflow to balance tracking frequency and battery life.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates when configured in tracking mode and connected to cellular networks.
- Tamper and light‑sensor alerts forwarded to Plaspy for anti‑theft and tamper monitoring workflows.
- Geofence entry/exit notifications and abnormal vibration/fall alerts for immediate event handling.
- Offline position buffering in cellular blind spots with automatic upload once connectivity is restored.
- Configurable reporting cadence \(power‑saving, week‑based, alarm‑driven\) to extend field life and reduce data usage.
