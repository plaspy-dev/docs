# Concox - GT03A

![GT03A](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT03A is a powerful GPS vehicle tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable tracking for your vehicles. With its quad-band GSM compatibility \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\), it ensures seamless communication and tracking capabilities across different regions. Whether you need to track a fleet of vehicles or monitor the whereabouts of your personal car, the GT03A is an excellent choice.

One of the standout features of the GT03A is its dual locating capabilities. It combines GPS and LBS \(Location-Based Service\) technology to provide precise and real-time tracking information. This ensures that you always have accurate location data for your vehicles, no matter where they are.

In case of emergencies, the GT03A is equipped with an SOS emergency alarm. With just a press of a button, the device will send an alert to your designated contacts, allowing for quick response and assistance. This feature is particularly useful in situations where immediate help is needed.

The GT03A also boasts a large capacity polymer battery, providing a super long standby time. This means that you can rely on the device to keep tracking your vehicles for extended periods without the need for frequent recharging.

Installation of the GT03A is a breeze, thanks to its built-in powerful magnets. Simply draw steel and attach the device to any metal surface, and you're good to go. This makes it ideal for covert tracking or for situations where you need to quickly install or remove the tracker.

Other notable features of the GT03A include geo-fencing, voice monitoring, built-in moving sensor for alarm functions, three color LED indicators for status updates, and compatibility with a global position service platform. With these features, you can have full control and visibility over your vehicles, ensuring their safety and security at all times.
