# Concox - LL303 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Concox LL303 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It covers the model's practical capabilities, how those capabilities are reflected in a Plaspy deployment, and the typical scenarios where the LL303 is a good fit. The purpose is to help fleet managers, integrators, and technical evaluators understand what the LL303 can do when paired with Plaspy without replacing the device manual.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, accessory configuration, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. For device specific parameters and the latest technical details, consult the official Concox documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The LL303 is a rugged, solar assisted 4G tracker designed for long standby deployments on construction vehicles and vessels. It combines multiple positioning methods, environmental durability, and peripheral compatibility to support remote asset monitoring where power and weather resistance matter.

- Solar charging and magnetic charging options for extended field operation
- 4G LTE connectivity with 2G GSM fallback to maintain reporting where networks vary
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, WiFi, LBS, and BDS for improved location reliability
- IP67 dust and water resistance for harsh outdoor and maritime environments
- Bluetooth accessory compatibility and optional RFID reader for extended monitoring
- Built in alerts for device removal, abnormal vibration, and abnormal temperature and humidity

## Core Features of Concox - LL303

- 4G LTE primary communication with GSM 2G fallback for broader network coverage
- GPS plus WiFi, LBS, and BDS positioning to enhance location fixes in varied environments
- Solar panel charging and magnetic charger support to extend operational uptime
- IP67 rated enclosure to tolerate dust ingress and temporary water immersion
- Multiple working modes to adapt reporting frequency and behavior to operational needs
- Bluetooth accessory support for external sensors and peripherals such as fuel or door monitors
- Optional RFID reader for reading compatible tags and device related identification
- Alerts for device removal and abnormal environmental events like vibration and temperature changes

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides visibility and management tools that reflect the LL303 feature set, allowing operators to incorporate the device into fleet and asset workflows. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and accepts reports from devices configured to report to the Plaspy server domain, simplifying setup for supported models.

- Live and historical location display using the LL303 location data reported into Plaspy
- Event visibility for alerts such as device removal, abnormal vibration, and temperature or humidity warnings
- Accessory and peripheral state reporting in Plaspy when Bluetooth accessories and RFID are available and reporting
- Configuration and reporting options supported over common transport modes; Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol to assist with integration
- Long standby and solar charging behavior visible through long term telemetry and online/offline history in Plaspy
- Positioning fallback awareness where Plaspy can display fixes derived from GPS, WiFi, LBS, or BDS based on the device reports

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking construction vehicles and heavy equipment working at remote or outdoor sites
- Monitoring small vessels and marine assets where water resistance and battery autonomy are important
- Long term deployments requiring solar extendable power for low maintenance operation
- Remote asset installations that benefit from magnetic charger convenience during service
- Integrations using Bluetooth accessories to monitor door status or fuel related telemetry
- Fleet operations that need multi source positioning for improved location reliability

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features such as Bluetooth accessory reports and RFID depend on optional accessories and local configuration
- Alerts and working modes may vary by firmware version and hardware revision from the manufacturer
- Installation choices and mounting location can affect reported GPS, WiFi, and LBS quality and reliability
- Regional cellular band support and network fallback behavior depend on the specific unit variant and local carriers
- Always confirm accessory compatibility and feature support for your exact LL303 unit with Concox documentation
