# Concox - LL303

<div className="device-logo">
![LL303](./logo..png)
</div>

The Concox LL303 is a solar powered 4G GPS tracker engineered for monitoring construction vehicles and vessels. Designed with a rugged IP67 rated enclosure, an integrated solar panel and magnetic charging option, the LL303 targets deployments that need extended standby time and reliable operation in harsh outdoor environments. Its multiple working modes and support for peripheral accessories such as Bluetooth devices and an optional RFID reader make the device adaptable to a range of asset tracking scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LL303 can feed location and event data into Plaspy to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its connectivity options and built in alerting for events like device removal, abnormal vibration, and temperature or humidity anomalies align with the monitoring, notification, and reporting features available in Plaspy, making the LL303 a practical choice for fleets and remote assets managed on the platform.

## Key Highlights

- Solar assisted power with magnetic charging option for reduced maintenance and longer deployments
- 4G LTE communication with 2G GSM fallback to maintain connectivity across varied coverage conditions
- IP67 dust and water resistance for dependable use in harsh outdoor and marine environments
- Multiple working modes to adapt reporting and behavior to different operational needs
- Supports Bluetooth accessories and optional RFID reader to extend monitoring capabilities
- Built in alerts for device removal, abnormal vibration, and environmental anomalies

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LL303 supplies location updates and event notifications that Plaspy can display and act on, helping teams monitor assets, respond to incidents, and generate operational reports. Integration with Plaspy enables unified tracking and historical review across mixed fleets that include construction machinery and vessels.

- Real time and historical location display within Plaspy for route playback and situational awareness
- Event and alert forwarding so Plaspy can notify teams about device removal, vibration events, or environmental warnings
- Peripheral data from Bluetooth accessories and RFID can be surfaced in Plaspy for additional operational context
- Fleet level dashboards and reports to track utilization and monitor asset availability
- Centralized operational oversight for mixed deployments combining land and marine assets

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring construction vehicles and site equipment across large or remote worksites
- Tracking vessels and small marine assets where rugged water resistance is required
- Long standby or off grid asset tracking using solar charging to minimize servicing
- Anti tampering and removal alerts for high value mobile equipment
- Accessory based monitoring such as door or fuel related telemetry via Bluetooth peripherals and RFID based identification

Its solar charging capability and IP67 protection reduce downtime and service visits, while multiple working modes and accessory support give operational flexibility.
