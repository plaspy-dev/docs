# Concox - LL303 Pro

![LL303 Pro](./tracker.png)

The LL303 Pro is a Plaspy compatible, solar-powered 4G LTE GNSS asset GPS tracker designed for long-term deployment on construction machinery, heavy equipment, and vessels. Built for harsh outdoor environments, the LL303 Pro combines integrated solar charging, a large 10,000mAh Li‑Polymer battery and an IP67 rugged enclosure to deliver reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for off‑grid assets. Its multi-mode reporting and event-triggered alerts make it an ideal choice for customers who need Plaspy-compatible devices that reduce site visits and improve visibility of high-value equipment.

The LL303 Pro integrates GPS, BDS and LBS positioning with high GNSS sensitivity and fast TTFF for accurate location updates \(typical \<2.5 m CEP\). Cellular connectivity across LTE \(with Cat 1, Cat M1 and NB2 variants\) plus GSM \(2G\) fallback maximizes coverage, while BLE 5.0 enables Bluetooth sensors for fuel monitoring, door sensors and other accessory telemetry. When paired with Plaspy, the LL303 Pro becomes a powerful building block for fleet management, anti-theft workflows and remote asset telemetry.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for long-term outdoor deployment and off-grid assets.
- Solar charging plus 10,000mAh Li‑Polymer battery delivers extended standby \(up to one year in power-saving mode\) and fewer maintenance visits.
- Robust outdoor protection: IP67-rated rugged enclosure for dust- and water-resistant operation in harsh environments.
- Multi-mode reporting \(timing, intelligent, power-saving\) balances real-time tracking with battery life for fleet management and asset monitoring.
- High-accuracy positioning: GPS + BDS + LBS with typical \<2.5 m CEP and GNSS sensitivity of –165 dBm.
- Flexible cellular connectivity: LTE \(Cat 1 / Cat M1 / NB2 variants\) with GSM \(2G\) fallback and nano‑SIM support for wide coverage.
- Onboard sensors and BLE 5.0 support for telemetry expansion — tamper \(Hall-effect\), accelerometer, vibration alerts and external Bluetooth sensors \(fuel, door\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy-compatible integration makes the LL303 Pro simple to deploy into existing Plaspy fleets or asset-tracking projects. The tracker streams GNSS position and device telemetry to Plaspy’s platform using the cellular link; Plaspy then handles visualization, geofencing, event rules and reporting. Real-time alerts and historical reports in Plaspy let operations teams act quickly on anti-theft events, maintenance triggers and unusual movement.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live map tracking and historical playback.
- Event-triggered alerts \(tamper, vibration, low battery\) forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notifications and automated workflows.
- Fuel monitoring and accessory telemetry via Bluetooth sensors \(BLE 5.0\) integrated into Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Configurable reporting modes \(timing, intelligent, power-saving\) mapped to Plaspy rules to balance frequency and power consumption.
- Plaspy can coordinate immobilizer or ignition workflows where vehicle peripherals or third‑party integrations provide that capability, enabling anti-theft responses when supported by the installation.
