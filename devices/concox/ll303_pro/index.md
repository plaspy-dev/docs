# Concox - LL303 Pro

<div className="device-logo">
![LL303 Pro](./tracker.png)
</div>

The LL303 Pro is a solar powered 4G LTE GNSS asset GPS tracker designed for long term deployment on construction machinery, heavy equipment, and vessels. Built for harsh outdoor environments, it combines integrated solar charging, a large 10,000 mAh Li-Polymer battery and an IP67 rugged enclosure to provide extended standby and reliable real-time tracking for off grid assets. Multi-mode reporting and event triggered alerts help reduce maintenance visits and improve visibility of high value equipment.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LL303 Pro can be added to Plaspy fleets and asset projects to provide continuous location and telemetry data. When paired with Plaspy, the device streams GNSS positions and device events into the platform so teams can monitor assets on live maps, configure geofences and rules, and generate reports for operational oversight and anti-theft workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Solar assisted power with a large onboard battery for extended standby and reduced site visits.  
- Rugged IP67 enclosure suited to dust and water exposure in outdoor deployments.  
- Multi-mode reporting to balance tracking frequency with battery conservation for long term asset monitoring.  
- High accuracy positioning and enhanced GNSS sensitivity for dependable location updates.  
- Cellular connectivity options and fallback for broad coverage in remote areas.  
- BLE support and onboard event sensors to extend telemetry for accessories and alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location and telemetry from the LL303 Pro over its cellular connection and presents that data in dashboards, maps and automated workflows. Plaspy can use the device's reporting modes and event triggers to optimize visibility while limiting power use on long deployments.

- Live location updates and historical playback on Plaspy maps for asset tracking and route review.  
- Event triggered alerts such as tamper, vibration or low battery routed to Plaspy for notifications and actions.  
- Accessory and sensor telemetry integrated into Plaspy dashboards to track fuel, doors, and other BLE connected data.  
- Configurable reporting mapped to Plaspy rules to balance real time visibility with power saving strategies.  
- Centralized reporting and exportable logs in Plaspy to support maintenance scheduling and operational audits.

## Typical Use Cases

- Remote fleet management of construction machinery and heavy equipment to reduce on site checks.  
- Anti-theft monitoring and rapid response workflows for high value assets.  
- Fuel and auxiliary telemetry for consumption monitoring and anomaly detection.  
- Vessel and maritime asset tracking where solar charging and IP67 protection extend unattended operation.  
- Long term logistics and equipment staging where off grid power and extended standby are essential.
