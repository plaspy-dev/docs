# Concox - LL303 Pro Features

This page provides a public feature overview for using the Concox LL303 Pro with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive capabilities and explains how the tracker’s functions map to monitoring and operational workflows inside the Plaspy platform. Use this content to understand what the device can deliver when integrated with Plaspy and the type of visibility you can expect for off‑grid assets.

Exact feature availability may vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and the specifics of each installation. Differences in solar exposure, accessory sensors, cellular network variants and manufacturer firmware updates can affect behavior; always validate critical details against the device firmware release notes and official Concox documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LL303 Pro is a solar‑assisted 4G LTE GNSS asset tracker intended for long‑term outdoor deployment on construction machinery, heavy equipment and vessels. It is designed to keep assets visible over extended periods with reduced maintenance and to extend telemetry using Bluetooth accessories where available.

- Solar charging combined with a large 10,000 mAh Li‑Polymer battery for extended standby and fewer site visits
- Rugged IP67 enclosure suited for dust and water exposure in harsh outdoor environments
- High accuracy GNSS positioning using GPS plus BDS and LBS to provide reliable location updates for remote assets
- Multi‑mode reporting options to balance location update frequency and battery life for long deployments
- BLE 5.0 support for accessory telemetry such as fuel and door sensors, and onboard sensors for tamper and vibration events

## Core Features of Concox - LL303 Pro

- Solar charging system plus integrated large capacity battery designed for long standby operation
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure for durable outdoor use on equipment and vessels
- GNSS positioning with GPS, BDS and LBS for improved fix reliability and location accuracy
- Multi‑mode reporting including timing, intelligent and power saving modes to manage update intervals and energy use
- Cellular connectivity across LTE variants with GSM fallback to maximize coverage in diverse regions
- BLE 5.0 accessory support enabling integration of external sensors for extended telemetry
- Onboard tamper detection and motion/vibration alerting for anti‑theft and event monitoring
- Configurable event triggers for forwarding alerts and telemetry to a fleet platform like Plaspy

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LL303 Pro streams location and device telemetry so teams can monitor asset health and movement from a centralized interface. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents position and event information for operational decision making.

- Real time location updates and historical playback appear in Plaspy for live monitoring and post event analysis
- Event alerts such as tamper, vibration and low battery are forwarded to Plaspy for notifications and rule based workflows
- BLE sensor telemetry integrated into Plaspy dashboards lets teams track auxiliary data like fuel or door status where those sensors are paired to the device
- Configurable reporting modes on the tracker map to Plaspy rules so reporting frequency and power usage can be balanced for each asset
- Plaspy supports scalable fleet visualization and reporting that helps reduce site visits and speed response to incidents

## Typical Use Cases

- Remote construction equipment tracking to monitor location and reduce on site inspection frequency
- Anti‑theft monitoring for heavy machinery using tamper and vibration alerts routed to operations teams
- Fuel and accessory telemetry via BLE sensors for remote consumption monitoring and basic integrity checks
- Vessel and maritime asset monitoring where solar charging and IP67 protection extend unattended operation
- Long term logistics and asset stewardship for equipment in remote or off‑grid locations
