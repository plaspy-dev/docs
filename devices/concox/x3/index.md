# Concox - X3

![X3](./tracker.png)

The X3 is a compact, multifunctional vehicle GPS tracker designed for Plaspy-compatible deployments that demand reliable real-time tracking, fleet management and security monitoring. Built for vehicle installations, the X3 provides accurate location reporting \(GPS + BDS + LBS\), two-way communication and driving behavior analytics to help fleets reduce risk, improve driver safety and accelerate incident response.

As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the X3 feeds live telemetry and event data into Plaspy dashboards and workflows so operators can monitor vehicles, trigger alerts and remotely act on incidents such as theft or unauthorized movement. Its flexible peripheral interfaces \(RS232/TTL, relay outputs, external microphone\) and wide 9–36 VDC input range make it an adaptable choice for fleet operators, logistics providers and security installations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- High-accuracy GNSS \(GPS + BDS + LBS\) with positioning accuracy &lt;2.5 m CEP for precise vehicle locations.
- GSM quad-band connectivity \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) for broad cellular coverage across regions.
- Remote immobilizer / cut-off control via relay for anti-theft response and power isolation.
- Driving behavior analytics: harsh acceleration, harsh braking, harsh cornering and collision detection to support safety programs.
- Flexible I/O: RS232 and TTL accessory interfaces, digital ACC and SOS inputs, plus multiple outputs for relays and buzzers.
- Built-in accelerometer \(G-sensor\) and in-cab voice monitoring \(external microphone\) for incident verification and two-way communication.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The X3 transmits GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry over GSM to Plaspy, where real-time tracking, alerts and historical reports are available in a unified interface. Plaspy ingests status signals \(ACC/ignition, SOS, inputs/outputs\) and driving-behavior events from the X3 to enable automated notifications, geofence enforcement and operational reporting. Remote relay control from Plaspy enables immobilizer-type actions \(fuel/power cut-off\) when rapid intervention is required.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for map visualization and tracking.
- Ignition/ACC and digital input reporting for trip detection and ignition-based rules.
- Remote immobilizer/cut-off control via relay output for anti-theft intervention.
- Driving behavior alerts \(harsh acceleration/braking/cornering and collision\) pushed as events to Plaspy.
- Geofence, movement and power-disconnect alerts to reduce response time and support fleet security.
- Voice monitoring and two-way communication capability to verify incidents and support driver communications.
- When fuel monitoring is required, Plaspy can correlate X3 I/O signals and external sensor inputs \(where installed\) to populate fuel-monitoring workflows.
- Although the X3 does not include internal Bluetooth sensors, Plaspy can aggregate BLE/third-party sensor data alongside X3 telemetry to provide a full telematics picture where supported in the deployment.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and rapid immobilization — use relay-based cut-off combined with Plaspy alerts to secure vehicles after an incident.
- Driver safety and compliance — monitor harsh acceleration, braking and cornering to coach drivers and reduce risk.
- Truck logistics and delivery tracking — real-time tracking and geofencing help manage routes, ETA and custody events.
- In-cab incident verification — external microphone and two-way communication provide voice context for accidents or disputes.
- Mixed-asset telematics — integrate X3 vehicle telemetry into Plaspy to consolidate location, ignition, and sensor-driven alerts across a fleet.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The X3 delivers a balanced combination of precise GNSS positioning, robust GSM connectivity and flexible hardware interfaces that make it a practical choice for Plaspy-compatible deployments. Its compact form factor, wide input voltage range and RS232/TTL support simplify installation across diverse vehicle types. Feeding X3 telemetry into Plaspy enables centralized real-time tracking, telemetry reporting and automated anti-theft workflows \(including remote immobilizer actions\), helping operations minimize downtime and improve asset security. For fleets that need voice verification, driving behavior analysis and customizable I/O integration, the X3 provides dependable vehicle-level data that Plaspy can convert into actionable insights for safer, more efficient fleet management.
