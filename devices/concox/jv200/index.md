# Concox - JV200

<div className="device-logo">
![JV200](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Concox JV200 is a vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable real time online tracking. Built for vehicle applications, the JV200 uses GSM GPRS GPS wireless communication and includes AGPS for fast positioning, delivering a cold start time under 10 seconds. Its compact form and robust construction make it suitable for continuous use in a variety of vehicle environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JV200 can forward location updates and basic tracking information to the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility. That compatibility makes the JV200 a practical option for organizations that want to incorporate real time location data into Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight without switching hardware ecosystems.

## Key Highlights

- Real time online tracking for continuous vehicle visibility
- AGPS capability for fast positioning with cold start under 10 seconds
- Uses GSM GPRS GPS wireless communication technology for location updates
- Compact design that supports discrete vehicle use
- Durable construction intended for regular vehicle operation
- Compatible with Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to work with Plaspy, the Concox JV200 sends position data to the Plaspy platform where fleet managers and operators can view live locations and review recent activity. Integration enables consolidation of vehicle tracking into Plaspy dashboards and reports to support daily operations and decision making.

- Live location visibility on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring
- Fleet level monitoring to track multiple JV200 devices together
- Alerts and notifications routed through Plaspy for movement or status changes
- Historical route playback and reporting for trip review and analysis
- Operational oversight features such as vehicle assignment and status checks
