# Concox - JV200

![JV200](./logo.jpg)

The Concox JV200 is a reliable and efficient vehicle GPS tracker that offers real-time online tracking capabilities. Designed specifically for vehicle tracking, this GPS tracker utilizes GSM+GPRS+GPS wireless communication technology to provide accurate and up-to-date location information. With its AGPS function, the JV200 can achieve extremely fast positioning, with a cold start time of less than 10 seconds.

With the Concox JV200, you can easily keep track of your vehicles and monitor their movements in real-time. Whether you're managing a fleet of vehicles for a business or simply want to keep tabs on your personal vehicle, this GPS tracker offers a practical and user-friendly solution. Its compact design allows for easy installation, and its robust construction ensures durability and reliability.

Key Features:

- Real-time online tracking
- GSM+GPRS+GPS wireless communication technology
- AGPS function for fast positioning \(Cold start &lt; 10s\)
- Compact and easy-to-install design
- Durable and reliable construction
