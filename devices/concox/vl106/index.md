# Concox - VL106

![VL106](./tracker.png)

The Concox VL106 is a compact, vehicle focused GPS tracker engineered for demanding deployments that need continuous and accurate location and motion data. It combines an INS aided navigation approach with multi constellation GNSS to maintain reliable positioning in tunnels, underground parking and other GNSS challenged areas. Designed with a rugged form factor and an automotive grade power input, the VL106 is intended for professional fleet, security and finance environments where sustained telemetry and event capture are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VL106 streams location, motion and event telemetry into the Plaspy platform so operators can visualize, analyze and act on vehicle data in real time. Its integrated motion sensors, ignition detection and relay output complement Plaspy workflows for fleet monitoring, anti theft response and usage based analytics, making it a practical choice when you need continuity of data and clear operational visibility within the Plaspy ecosystem.

## Key Highlights

- INS aided positioning and multi constellation GNSS for continuous tracking in GNSS degraded environments
- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking, alerts and fleet management dashboards
- High positioning accuracy and dependable location fixes for vehicle deployments
- Integrated IMU with onboard capture to support driving behavior analysis and collision event logging
- Automotive I O including ACC ignition detection, SOS panic input and relay output for remote immobilizer or fuel cut off
- Rugged compact design with wide input voltage range and onboard storage to preserve events during connectivity loss

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VL106 delivers vehicle GNSS and motion telemetry to Plaspy's real time platform where data is visualized, trended and converted into operational alerts and reports. Plaspy ingests position and event streams from the device to support live maps, historical replay and automated responses.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for live fleet monitoring
- ACC and ignition status plus SOS panic events reported to Plaspy for immediate alerting
- Relay output usable within Plaspy workflows for remote immobilizer or fuel cut off actions
- High rate IMU upload for impact analysis and post incident reconstruction
- Onboard storage retention so critical events are available to Plaspy after temporary signal loss

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for continuous tracking, utilization monitoring and operational reporting
- Usage based insurance to support driving behavior analytics and risk profiling
- Auto finance and repossession workflows using remote immobilizer capability
- Collision investigation and safety analysis using pre and post event motion logs
- Anti theft and security monitoring with instant alerts and recovery workflows
