# Concox - VG102

![VG102](./tracker.png)

The VG102 is a compact 2G GNSS GPS tracker purpose-built for motorcycles and two-wheeler fleets. Designed to integrate seamlessly with Plaspy, the VG102 delivers dependable positioning and anti-theft capabilities that lending institutions, leasing companies, and motorcycle fleet managers require. Its small form factor, IP66-rated enclosure, and internal backup battery provide resilient operation in harsh environments, making the VG102 an excellent Plaspy compatible choice for real-time tracking and asset protection.

With high-sensitivity GNSS \(GPS/BDS/GLONASS\), a built-in relay for remote immobilization, tip-over and tamper alerts, and an optional plug-and-track adapter cable for damage-free installation on popular motorcycle models, VG102 streamlines fleet management and motorcycle recovery workflows. When paired with Plaspy, the VG102 feeds telemetry and status alerts into centralized dashboards for actionable intelligence, faster recovery, and improved operational control.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for two-wheel vehicles — compact design for discreet installation and easy integration into fleet management workflows.
- High-sensitivity GNSS \(GPS/BDS/GLONASS\) with L1/B1 frequency for accurate, reliable real-time tracking with \<2.5 m CEP stated positioning performance.
- Built-in relay for remote immobilization \(fuel/power cut-off\) to support anti-theft and recovery operations.
- Comprehensive event alerts: tip-over, vibration, speed, SOS, tamper, geo-fence entry/exit and low battery — ideal for telemetry-driven security and compliance.
- IP66-rated housing for dust and water resistance, supporting dependable operation in varied weather and road conditions.
- Optional plug-and-track adapter cable for damage-free installs on common motorcycle models, reducing installation time and service calls.
- Low standby current and intelligent power management: automatic switch to internal battery when vehicle power is critically low to preserve tracking and alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

VG102 transmits GNSS position, event alerts, and status telemetry over 2G cellular links to Plaspy. Once the data arrives, Plaspy normalizes and displays real-time location, history and event-driven notifications on dashboards and mobile apps. The device’s onboard alerts and relay control complement Plaspy’s rule-based workflows to automate immobilization, send immediate SMS/push notifications, and generate recovery tasks for field teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS position, speed, movement status\).
- Tip-over, vibration, speed and tamper alerts mapped to Plaspy workflows for instant operator notification.
- Geo-fence entry/exit events and SOS notifications routed to dispatch or automated escalation flows.
- Remote immobilizer / relay control \(fuel/power cut-off\) available for anti-theft and recovery actions through Plaspy command channels.
- Low internal battery alerts and device health indicators for proactive maintenance and uptime assurance.
