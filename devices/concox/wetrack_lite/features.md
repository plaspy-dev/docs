# Concox - Wetrack Lite Features

This page presents the public feature context for using the Concox Wetrack Lite with Plaspy. It focuses on the model's practical capabilities, event behavior, and the types of telemetry Plaspy receives and displays. The intent is to help fleet managers, rental operators, and tracking integrators understand what the tracker delivers in Plaspy without replacing manufacturer documentation.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. Where applicable this page highlights general capabilities described by the device specifications, but you should confirm device firmware and local variant details with the manufacturer and installation provider.

## Feature Overview

The Wetrack Lite is a compact, low profile GPS tracker built for discreet vehicle monitoring and continuous operational visibility. It combines multi constellation positioning with vehicle-focused event reporting and local buffering to preserve trip history during temporary connectivity loss.

- Multi constellation GNSS positioning using GPS plus BDS and LBS for improved location accuracy in mixed satellite visibility.
- Vehicle telemetry including ACC ignition detection and accelerometer based movement and vibration alerts for event driven reporting.
- Wide operating voltage and battery protection to support a variety of vehicle types while reducing installation constraints.
- Onboard storage buffering to retain location and event records during short GSM outages so data is available to Plaspy once connectivity returns.
- Compact mini form factor and hidden LED design for concealed installations in rental, fleet, and light logistics applications.

## Core Features of Concox - Wetrack Lite

- GPS plus BDS plus LBS positioning with high reported accuracy and suitability for routing and geofence functions.
- ACC ignition detection for reporting vehicle on and off states to support utilization and idle monitoring.
- Accelerometer based movement and abnormal vibration alerts to flag possible tampering or unauthorized motion.
- Event driven alerts including overspeed, geo fence entry and exit, movement, and power disconnection.
- Wide input voltage support and vehicle battery protection to accommodate cars, scooters, and small industrial equipment.
- Onboard storage buffering to queue location and event data during temporary cellular outages.
- Compact and lightweight enclosure with hidden LED indicators for discreet mounting and a lower visual profile.
- IPX5 water resistance and extended operating temperature range for reliable field deployment.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the Wetrack Lite sends positions and event packets that Plaspy normalizes into live maps, alerts, and historical reports. Plaspy customers can use the tracker telemetry to build operational rules, notifications, and reporting workflows suited to fleet oversight and anti theft monitoring.

- Real time location updates and corrected positions visible on Plaspy maps for route monitoring and asset routing.
- Ignition ACC status reflected in Plaspy to indicate vehicle on off state and to support usage analytics.
- Event notifications for movement, vibration, overspeed, and geo fence entry and exit delivered as alerts inside Plaspy.
- Power disconnection and battery detection events surfaced as status alerts to help detect tampering or battery issues.
- Buffered data from the device is uploaded when connectivity resumes so Plaspy receives a complete trip history and fewer gaps in reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small and medium vehicle fleets needing location awareness and ignition based usage reporting.
- Car rental and shared mobility services requiring discreet trackers to monitor returns and unauthorized movement.
- Anti theft workflows using movement, vibration, and power disconnection alerts to accelerate recovery and response.
- Two wheel and light vehicle tracking where wide voltage range and compact size reduce installation constraints.
- Short haul logistics and shuttle services that benefit from on device buffering to preserve trip data during coverage gaps.
