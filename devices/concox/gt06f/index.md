# Concox - GT06F

![GT06F](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT06F is a compact GPS vehicle tracker designed to provide reliable location tracking for vehicles and valuable possessions. It combines a small, discreet form factor with core tracking features such as real time location updates, geofencing, remote monitoring, multiple alarm types, and history playback. The device is described as user friendly and suitable for discreet placement in vehicles or on other tracked items.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT06F can integrate into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can ingest location and event data from the tracker so organizations and individuals can monitor assets, receive alerts, and review historical routes within the same platform. Compatibility makes the GT06F a practical option for users who want straightforward tracking combined with Plaspy tools for fleet and asset management.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet form factor suitable for vehicles and portable assets
- Real time tracking for up to date location visibility
- Geo fencing support to trigger alerts when boundaries are crossed
- Remote monitoring capability to capture environmental audio around the device
- Multiple alarm types including overspeed and power notifications as described
- History playback for reviewing past routes and locations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GT06F feeds its tracking data into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools to provide continuous visibility and event awareness. This pairing allows users to centralize tracking operations and leverage Plaspy features for monitoring and analysis.

- Display live location of GT06F units on Plaspy maps for real time oversight
- Configure geofence alerts in Plaspy to receive notifications when a tracker enters or exits defined areas
- Use Plaspy reporting to review history playback and analyze past routes for operational review
- Receive and manage alarm notifications in Plaspy from the tracker's built in alert types
- Include GT06F units in Plaspy fleet views for consolidated monitoring of multiple devices
