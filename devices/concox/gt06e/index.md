# Concox - GT06E

![GT06E](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT06E is a versatile and advanced 3G GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features for efficient fleet tracking and management. With its cutting-edge GPS technology, this device provides accurate and real-time tracking information, making it an ideal choice for businesses looking to optimize their operations.

One of the standout features of the GT06E is its multifunctionality. In addition to the traditional tracking functions such as tele-cutoff petrol, geo-fence, SOS alarm, and overspeed alert, this tracker also offers optional digital outputs for door detection, vehicle pinpoint by audio-visual alert, and external power voltage monitoring. These additional features provide enhanced security and control over your fleet, allowing you to monitor and manage your vehicles with ease.

With its 3G capabilities, the GT06E ensures faster and more reliable communication, enabling you to receive real-time updates and track your vehicles with precision. Whether you need to monitor a single vehicle or an entire fleet, this GPS tracker offers the functionality and performance you need to streamline your operations and improve efficiency.

#### Key Features:

- 3G capabilities for faster and more reliable communication
- Traditional tracking functions such as tele-cutoff petrol, geo-fence, SOS alarm, and overspeed alert
- Optional digital outputs for door detection, vehicle pinpoint by audio-visual alert, and external power voltage monitoring
- Accurate and real-time tracking information
- Ideal for fleet tracking and management
