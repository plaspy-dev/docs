# Concox - JM-VL03 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Concox JM-VL03 and explains how its capabilities are presented and used within Plaspy. It focuses on the practical, non-sensitive aspects of the tracker that matter to fleet managers, insurers, and asset protection teams when integrating the device with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where functions depend on wiring, configuration, or regional model variants, confirm the specifics with the official manufacturer documentation before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The JM-VL03 is a compact 4G LTE Cat 1 tracker with GSM fallback designed for continuous real-time tracking and event-driven telemetry across a wide range of vehicle types. Its combination of connectivity, vehicle inputs, and event reporting enables practical fleet and security workflows when paired with Plaspy.

- Reliable real-time tracking and position updates using GNSS with GPS and BDS positioning.
- LTE connectivity with GSM fallback to maintain reporting across cellular network conditions.
- Wide 9–90 VDC input and a small internal backup battery allow installation on cars, motorcycles, scooters, and light equipment.
- Ignition ACC detection and a relay output provide the foundation for ignition-aware workflows and remote immobilization actions.
- Driver behavior events and event-triggered alerts such as over-speed, vibration, geo-fence and power events support safety and anti-theft monitoring.
- Compact IP65 enclosure for discreet vehicle mounting and outdoor durability.

## Core Features of Concox - JM-VL03

- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with GSM fallback for continuous cellular reporting.
- Wide operating voltage range of 9–90 VDC plus an internal backup battery for short offline reporting.
- Ignition ACC detection to track vehicle on and off status.
- Relay output for remote immobilizer control to support controlled shutdown workflows.
- Driver behavior analysis with harsh acceleration, harsh braking, harsh cornering and collision event detection.
- GNSS positioning using GPS and BDS with published positioning accuracy figures and fast time to first fix in typical conditions.
- Event-triggered alerts including geo-fence, over-speed, vibration, low battery and power-off notifications.
- Compact IP65 rated enclosure suitable for vehicle and outdoor installations.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event telemetry from the JM-VL03 and surfaces that information in dashboards, maps, reports and rules engines. The device's inputs and event streams provide sources for alarms, automation, and operational reporting inside Plaspy.

- Real-time location and telemetry appear on Plaspy maps and fleet dashboards for live tracking and historical routes.
- Ignition ACC status is available for utilization reporting and ignition-aware rules.
- Relay-based immobilizer capability can be triggered from Plaspy workflows where remote cut-off is supported by installation and local regulations.
- Driver behavior events are recorded as telemetry events for safety analytics, driver scoring, and incident review.
- Geo-fence entry and exit, over-speed, vibration, removal and power alerts feed Plaspy notification channels and rule-based automation.
- Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding and configuration.
