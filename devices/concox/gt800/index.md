# Concox - GT800

<div className="device-logo">
![GT800](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Concox GT800 is a multifunctional vehicle GPS tracker designed to deliver reliable and accurate location tracking for cars and light commercial vehicles. It combines GPS and AGPS tracking with event detection features such as door status monitoring, two way communication, and an SOS call function to support both routine monitoring and urgent situations. The GT800 targets users who need practical tracking capabilities with a good balance of performance and cost.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT800 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet management platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Compatibility means you can use Plaspy to view location data, receive status alerts such as door open events and SOS signals, and include GT800 devices in fleet reports and monitoring workflows managed from Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- GPS and AGPS based location tracking for timely position updates and route visibility
- Door status detection to notify when vehicle access changes
- Two way communication capability for remote interaction with the device when supported
- SOS call feature for emergency alerts and rapid attention to critical events
- Practical balance of features and price suitable for both individual vehicles and fleet deployments
- Reliable option for basic fleet monitoring and vehicle security needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GT800 streams location and event information to a single platform where fleet managers and operators can monitor assets, configure alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy brings together the data from GT800 devices to improve operational awareness and help teams respond to incidents.

- Live location display and breadcrumb history for each GT800 equipped vehicle
- Event alerts delivered through Plaspy for door status changes and SOS activations
- Fleet level monitoring to track vehicle status, movement, and recent events in one view
- Reporting and activity logs for performance review and operational analysis
- Use of two way communication features through Plaspy enabled workflows where supported by device setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Small to medium fleet management requiring location visibility and basic event alerts
- Vehicle security and monitoring for company cars and service vehicles
- Rental vehicle oversight where door status and location history matter
- Transport and delivery operations that need position tracking and incident notifications
- Personal vehicle tracking for added safety and emergency alerting

The GT800 is a sensible choice for organizations and individuals who need dependable vehicle tracking without unnecessary complexity.
