# Concox - GT800

![GT800](./logo.jpg)

The GT800 Vehicle GPS Tracker from Concox is a high-end multifunctional device designed to provide reliable and accurate tracking for vehicles. With its solid performance and reasonable price, this GPS tracker is an excellent choice for individuals and businesses alike. 

One of the standout features of the GT800 is its GPS and AGPS tracking capabilities. This allows for real-time tracking of the vehicle's location, ensuring that you always know where your vehicle is at any given time. Additionally, the device supports door status detection, providing an extra layer of security by alerting you if the doors of your vehicle are opened or closed. 

Another impressive feature of the GT800 is its two-way communication functionality. This allows you to communicate with the tracker remotely, making it easy to send commands or receive updates on the status of your vehicle. Additionally, the device includes an SOS call feature, allowing for quick and easy emergency communication in case of an accident or other emergency situation. 

Overall, the GT800 Vehicle GPS Tracker is a reliable and feature-packed device that offers exceptional value for its price. Whether you're looking to track a single vehicle or manage a fleet, this GPS tracker is sure to meet your needs.
