# Concox - EG02

![EG02](./logo..png)

The Concox EG02 is a compact GPS tracker designed primarily as an anti theft solution for electric scooters. It offers plug and play installation and supports real time location tracking using GPS and LBS. The EG02 provides remote lock and unlock capability through a companion mobile app, triggers an audio alarm on unauthorized movement, and includes multiple alerts for events such as abnormal vibration, speeding, power off, and geofence entry or exit.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the EG02 can be integrated into Plaspy's tracking platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its wide working voltage range and optimized battery protection make it a flexible choice for a variety of scooters and light vehicles, and when paired with Plaspy the device's alerts and remote functions can be used within a fleet monitoring workflow to improve security and manage vehicle use.

## Key Highlights

- Compact anti theft tracker tailored for electric scooters and similar light vehicles
- Plug and play design for straightforward installation and quick deployment
- Real time location tracking using GPS and LBS for continuous visibility
- Remote lock and unlock plus keyless start options via companion controls
- Wide working voltage range 9 to 90 V for compatibility with many vehicle types
- Multiple event alerts including vibration, speeding, power off, and geofence entry or exit
- Optimized battery protection and support for external buzzer to aid theft deterrence

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the EG02 supplies location and alert information to a centralized platform for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy aggregates device data so operators can track movement, receive event notifications, and review historical activity for operational decisions.

- Live location and breadcrumb tracking visible within Plaspy dashboards
- Event and alert notifications for unauthorized movement, power off, vibration, and geofence changes
- Remote immobilize or lock and unlock actions available through Plaspy where supported by the device configuration
- Historical playback and trip reporting to analyze routes and usage patterns
- Fleet grouping and device management to monitor multiple scooters or small vehicle fleets
- Power and status alerts to highlight battery protection events and device power changes

- Asset protection and rapid recovery operations for small vehicle pools
