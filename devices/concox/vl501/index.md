# Concox - VL501

![VL501](./tracker.png)

The VL501 is a plug and play LTE GPS tracker designed for quick installation and reliable fleet monitoring. Its removable cigarette lighter power design and compact form factor make it easy to deploy across vehicles without permanent wiring. The device combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with modern cellular connectivity and Bluetooth to provide accurate location and on device telemetry for fleet operations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VL501 streams location and event data into Plaspy for live tracking, alerts and reporting. Built in motion detection, anti theft alerts, SOS capability and local buffering for intermittent connectivity help ensure continuous telemetry and actionable notifications inside the Plaspy platform, making the VL501 suitable for fast rollouts and ongoing fleet oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play installation using a removable cigarette lighter power connector for fast deployment across vehicles
- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking and fleet management over cellular and Bluetooth connectivity
- High accuracy multi constellation GNSS positioning with reported sub 2.5 m CEP in open sky conditions
- Onboard motion detection, collision and unplug alerts, SOS button and in cabin microphone in supported regions for safety and anti theft workflows
- 64 Mb local buffer to store events during connectivity gaps and OTA firmware updates for simpler field maintenance
- Compact form factor with USB charging ports and LED indicators for easy use and monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the VL501 sends GNSS positions and event telemetry to Plaspy over the cellular link while Bluetooth attached devices can enrich the telemetry stream. Plaspy receives buffered data after connectivity resumes and presents location, alerts and history on dashboards for fleet managers to monitor and act upon.

- Real time location updates and route replay for live fleet visibility
- Event and alert forwarding to Plaspy including collision, vibration, unplug and SOS incidents
- Geo fence notifications and incident events integrated into Plaspy alerting and reporting workflows
- Bluetooth sensor and beacon data relayed to Plaspy where supported to enhance telemetry
- Local buffering and OTA firmware support for resilient operation and remote device management

## Typical Use Cases

- Last mile delivery and courier fleets requiring quick deployment and continuous tracking
- Car rental and leasing operations that prefer removable non wired trackers for temporary monitoring and recovery
- Usage based pilots and driver behavior programs where accurate position and event data inform scoring
- Temporary or seasonal fleets and contractors that need plug and play installation and buffered storage
- General fleet management and asset protection with geo fencing and anti theft alerts

The VL501 is a practical option for organizations that value rapid deployment and straightforward device management alongside dependable positioning. Its combination of plug and play power, onboard event detection and Bluetooth expansion makes it a flexible choice for fleets managed through Plaspy, allowing teams to bring vehicles online quickly and keep telemetry flowing during intermittent coverage.
