# Concox - VL501 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the Concox VL501 tracker features as they relate to use with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect when deploying the VL501 for real time fleet monitoring, alerts, and telematics inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where behavior depends on regional variants, firmware releases, or optional configurations, consult the manufacturer documentation and your Plaspy deployment settings for the current details.

## Feature Overview

The VL501 is a plug and play LTE GPS tracker designed for fast deployment and continuous tracking with support for Bluetooth sensors. It emphasizes quick installation via a removable cigarette lighter connector together with multi constellation GNSS for improved position reliability and local buffering for intermittent connectivity.

- Plug and play cigarette lighter installation for rapid deployment without hardwiring
- Multi constellation GNSS for higher position reliability and sub 2.5 m CEP performance in open sky
- LTE connectivity and Bluetooth to stream location and attached sensor telemetry into Plaspy
- Motion based alerts and anti theft notifications including unplug and low external power events
- Local buffering to preserve location and events during temporary connectivity gaps
- OTA firmware updates and USB charging ports to simplify maintenance and device handling

## Core Features of Concox - VL501

- Plug and play power via cigarette lighter connector with wide input range for quick installation
- Multi constellation GNSS support including GPS GLONASS BDS AGPS and QZSS for robust positioning
- LTE cellular connectivity with regional variants to deliver real time telemetry
- Bluetooth LE support for external sensors and beacon integration
- Onboard motion sensors including accelerometer and gyroscope to detect vibration and movement events
- Built in SOS panic button and in cabin microphone available in North America for safety workflows
- Local 64 Mb buffering to store locations and events during connectivity interruptions plus a small backup battery for short term power loss protection
- OTA firmware update support and LED indicators for field maintenance and status visibility

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests GNSS positions, sensor telemetry, and event alerts from the VL501 so fleet managers get live maps, incident notifications, and historical reports in one platform. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and surfaces device events alongside location history to support operational workflows.

- Real time location updates and route replay inside Plaspy dashboards for live monitoring
- Collision vibration and driving behavior alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and reporting
- Unplug installation and low external power warnings visible in Plaspy to support anti theft workflows and device health checks
- Geo fence and SOS panic events integrated into Plaspy incident and dispatch rules where configured
- Bluetooth sensor telemetry relayed into Plaspy when sensors are attached and supported by the device firmware
- Buffered data upload after connectivity restoration to preserve event continuity in Plaspy reports

## Typical Use Cases

- Last mile and courier fleets that need rapid tracker deployment and continuous real time tracking
- Car rental and leasing operations that prefer removable plug in trackers for temporary monitoring and recovery
- Usage based insurance pilots that rely on position accuracy and driving behavior telemetry for risk analysis
- Seasonal or temporary fleets and contractors that require non wired installation and buffered storage for intermittent coverage
- Field service and logistics fleets that need incident alerts and quick redeployment between vehicles
- Asset protection and anti theft workflows using unplug and motion alerts to trigger investigation
