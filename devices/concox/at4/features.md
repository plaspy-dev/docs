# Concox - AT4 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Concox AT4 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the AT4's practical capabilities as a portable magnet mounted GPS tracker that is compatible with Plaspy out of the box and designed for long standby deployments, tamper detection, and discreet audio monitoring for enhanced asset security and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where specific capabilities are referenced in this page they reflect the model description and common operating modes, but you should verify device configuration and current feature details with the manufacturer for mission critical deployments.

## Feature Overview

The AT4 delivers long endurance tracking and rugged, near invisible installation for vehicles and movable assets. Its strengths are extended battery life, a strong magnetic mount, and support for location, tamper and event reporting that feed directly into a fleet management platform such as Plaspy.

- Long standby capability for low maintenance deployments thanks to a high capacity internal battery.
- Magnet mounted, compact housing and IPX5 enclosure for easy attachment to vehicles, trailers, and equipment.
- GPS plus LBS positioning for reliable location reporting in mixed signal environments.
- Tamper detection and vibration or motion based alerts to support anti theft workflows.
- On device storage and cellular reporting that allow Plaspy to receive cached data after temporary connectivity loss.

## Core Features of Concox - AT4

- Portable, magnet mounted form factor for discreet installation on vehicles and assets.
- High capacity 10,000 mAh internal battery with extended standby operation in power saving modes.
- GPS plus LBS positioning with manufacturer stated accuracy performance under optimal conditions.
- Rugged IPX5 rated enclosure to tolerate field use and light exposure to water spray.
- Tamper aware design including a pressure sensitive tamper switch and cover removal alerts.
- Built in microphone for remote audio monitoring where permitted and enabled.
- Accelerometer based vibration detection and over speed type event reporting for movement monitoring.
- On device data storage and quad band cellular connectivity for broad network coverage and buffered reporting.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location, status, and event data from compatible trackers like the AT4 to provide a unified view of asset position and activity. When integrated, AT4 reports become visible in Plaspy for live tracking, event notification, and historical playback, helping teams respond to security events and monitor fleet operations from a single platform.

- Live position updates and historical route playback in Plaspy based on GNSS and cellular derived locations.
- Tamper, power and vibration events routed to Plaspy to trigger notifications, escalation rules, and workflow actions.
- Over speed and geo related events reported into Plaspy for automated alerting and compliance reporting.
- Remote audio monitoring capability surfaced in Plaspy where the feature is enabled and legally permitted.
- On device storage buffers tracking data during outages so Plaspy receives cached reports once connectivity is restored.

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle rental fleets that need discreet, removable tracking for recovery and usage oversight.
- Trailer and container tracking where long battery life reduces the need for frequent maintenance.
- Anti theft protection for equipment and movable assets using tamper alerts and vibration detection.
- Discreet asset monitoring for security investigations where audio recording is permitted.
- Short term deployments on leased or shared equipment where magnet mount installation is preferred.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature set and default behavior may differ by firmware version and hardware revision. Always confirm the active firmware on your unit.
- Some functions such as remote audio monitoring may be restricted by local law or require explicit device configuration and permissions.
- Battery life and standby estimates depend on reporting frequency, event rates, and environmental conditions.
- Regional cellular band support and network availability affect connectivity and reporting performance.
- Manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes are the authoritative source for device specific limits and configuration options.
