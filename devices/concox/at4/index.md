# Concox - AT4

![AT4](./tracker.png)

The AT4 is a portable, magnet-mounted GPS tracker designed for long standby deployments and rugged field use. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the AT4 gives fleet managers and asset owners reliable location intelligence, tamper alerts, and discreet audio monitoring for improved anti-theft protection and operational oversight.

The AT4 combines GPS+LBS positioning with a high-capacity 10,000mAh battery and an IPX5-rated enclosure to deliver extended deployments without maintenance. Compact and easy to install with a strong magnetic base, the AT4 is an ideal choice for fleet management, vehicle rental, trailer tracking and other asset tracking scenarios where long battery life, tamper detection and remote telemetry are essential.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that reports accurate location and movement to your Plaspy dashboard in real time.
- Ultra-long standby: 10,000mAh industrial Li‑Polymer battery with up to 2.5 years standby in power‑saving mode for low-maintenance deployments.
- Rugged, IPX5-rated enclosure and strong magnetic mount for near-zero installation on vehicles, trailers, and other assets.
- GPS+LBS positioning with \<2.5 m CEP accuracy under optimal conditions and fast TTFF \(hot start ≤1 s, cold start ≤32 s\).
- Integrated microphone for remote audio monitoring \(effective to ~3 meters\), plus accelerometer-based vibration detection and over-speed alerts.
- Tamper-aware design with a pressure-sensitive tamper button and immediate cover-removal alerts to enhance anti-theft workflows.
- On-device data storage and quad-band GSM connectivity for broad network coverage using a standard SIM.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the AT4 streams location, status, and event data into your real-time tracking and fleet management workflows. Plaspy ingests GNSS and GSM-derived positions, telemetry and event notifications from the device so you can view live positions, replay routes, set geo-fences, and respond to security events from a single pane of glass.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and historical plays.
- Tamper and power-off notifications routed to Plaspy to trigger alerts and escalation policies.
- Over-speed, vibration and geo-fence events reported directly to Plaspy for automated alerting and reporting.
- Remote audio monitoring capability \(microphone\) accessible through Plaspy where audio monitoring is enabled and permitted.
- On-device storage buffers location data if connectivity drops, allowing Plaspy to receive cached reports when the connection resumes.

## Use Cases

- Vehicle rental fleets: discreet, magnet-mounted tracking for short-term rentals and recovery if a vehicle is lost or misused.
- Fleet management and logistics: long battery life enables trailer and asset tracking between depots and during long hauls without frequent recharging.
- Anti-theft for trailers and equipment: tamper alerts, vibration detection and geo-fence events help detect and respond to unauthorized movement.
- Discreet asset monitoring: remote audio monitoring and position history for investigation and verification in security-sensitive deployments.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT4 is built to deliver dependable, low-maintenance tracking for assets that require long standby life and rugged protection. Combined with Plaspy, the AT4 becomes part of a comprehensive telematics solution that brings together real-time tracking, actionable alerts, and historical route playback. Its strong magnet mount and IPX5 rating simplify installation while its tamper detection and remote audio monitoring add layers of security for high-value assets.

For organizations that need reliable GPS tracker performance without frequent servicing, the AT4’s industrial battery and power-saving modes reduce operational overhead. Plaspy users can also combine AT4 location and event data with other telemetry sources—such as fuel monitoring, ignition status or immobilizer systems—from compatible fleet hardware to create a full picture of vehicle health and behavior. The result is a scalable, secure asset tracking solution that supports fleet management, anti-theft strategies, and efficient operational workflows.
