# Concox - AT4

![AT4](./tracker.png)

The Concox AT4 is a portable, magnet mounted GPS tracker built for long standby deployments and rugged field use. It combines GPS and network based positioning with a high capacity battery and an IPX5 rated enclosure to provide reliable location intelligence, tamper alerts, and discreet audio monitoring. Its compact form factor and strong magnetic base make it suitable for temporary or semi permanent attachment to vehicles, trailers, and other mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the AT4 can stream location, status, and event data directly into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. Fleet managers and asset owners can use Plaspy dashboards to view live positions, receive tamper and power notifications, and include AT4 event data in reporting and alerting workflows without additional gateway hardware.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that reports location, movement, and events to your Plaspy dashboard.
- Ultra long standby capability with a high capacity 10,000 mAh battery for low maintenance deployments.
- Rugged IPX5 rated enclosure and strong magnetic mount for quick, discreet placement on assets.
- Combined GPS and LBS positioning for reliable location estimates under varied conditions.
- Tamper aware design with cover removal and power alerts to support anti theft workflows.
- Integrated microphone for discreet audio monitoring and accelerometer based vibration detection.
- On device data storage and broad cellular coverage to buffer reports when connectivity is intermittent.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AT4 delivers position reports, status updates, and event notifications into a single fleet management view so teams can monitor assets in real time and investigate historical activity. Plaspy ingests the device's location and event streams and presents them alongside other fleet data for operational decision making.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear in Plaspy for live tracking and route playback.
- Tamper and power off notifications are routed to Plaspy to trigger alerts and escalation rules.
- Vibration, over speed and geo fence events are reported to Plaspy for automated notifications and reporting.
- Remote audio monitoring capability is available through Plaspy where monitoring is permitted and enabled.
- On device storage lets the AT4 buffer location data during outages so Plaspy receives cached reports when connectivity resumes.

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle rental fleets that need discreet, magnet mounted tracking for recovery and misuse monitoring.
- Trailer and asset tracking where long battery life reduces the need for frequent servicing.
- Anti theft protection for equipment with tamper alerts, vibration detection, and geofence monitoring.
- Discreet security monitoring where audio verification and position history assist investigations.

The AT4 is well suited for organizations that need dependable, low maintenance tracking for moveable assets. Its long standby life and rugged housing reduce operational overhead for fleets and asset owners, while tamper detection and remote monitoring add practical security layers. When combined with Plaspy, AT4 data becomes part of a broader telematics solution that supports live visibility, automated alerts, and historical analysis.
