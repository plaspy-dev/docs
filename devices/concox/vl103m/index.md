# Concox - VL103M

![VL103M](./tracker.png)

The VL103M from a leading telematics manufacturer is a compact, rugged 4G GPS tracker purpose-built for motorcycles and light commercial vehicles. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the VL103M delivers reliable real-time tracking, robust anti-theft alerts and practical telemetry features designed for fleet management, vehicle rental operations and stolen vehicle recovery.

Small but feature-rich, the VL103M combines multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS + BDS + LBS\) with LTE Cat 1 cellular communications and GSM fallback to provide continuous location updates and event-driven reports. With remote listen-in, buzzer/horn output, configurable vibration and tamper alarms and low-voltage detection, this tracker is a strong fit when you need accurate positioning, motion detection and dependable alerts integrated into the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for easy integration into your real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.
- High-accuracy positioning: GPS+BDS+LBS with better than 2.5 m CEP for precise location reporting.
- Rugged, weather-resistant design \(IP66\) and compact form factor ideal for motorcycles and small vehicles.
- Remote audio monitoring \(listen-in\) plus relay/IO for buzzer or horn to assist audible locating and warnings.
- Multiple event triggers—vibration, speeding, geo-fence entry/exit, tamper and cover removal—enable proactive anti-theft alerts.
- Wide power input \(9–90 VDC\) and internal 270 mAh backup battery to maintain telemetry during power interruptions.
- Fast GNSS acquisition \(Avg. hot start ≤ 1 s\) and strong sensitivity \(down to −165 dBm\) for challenging environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The VL103M communicates secure location and telemetry to Plaspy over LTE Cat 1 with GSM fallback, enabling real-time tracking, historical playback and configurable alerts inside the Plaspy dashboard. Integration is straightforward: Plaspy ingests periodic position updates and event reports from the tracker, and you can configure alert rules, map views and fleet reports to match operational needs.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Event-driven alerts: vibration, geo-fence entry/exit, tamper and cover removal feed directly into Plaspy notifications.
- Low-voltage detection and ACC/SOS digital input status available to support battery protection and emergency workflows.
- Remote listen-in audio capability and relay/IO control for buzzer/horn events usable as part of Plaspy alarm responses.
- Onboard storage \(16+16 Mb\) buffers data during temporary loss of connectivity for later upload to Plaspy.
