# Concox - VL103M Features

This page provides public feature context for using the Concox VL103M with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users and explains how those capabilities appear and behave at a high level inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and regional variant from the manufacturer. Where precise configuration or limits matter, review the official Concox documentation and firmware notes for the device you have installed.

## Feature Overview

The VL103M is a compact, rugged 4G tracker purpose built for motorcycles and light commercial vehicles. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with cellular communications and a set of practical inputs and outputs to deliver location, motion and anti theft reporting that integrates into Plaspy for real time visibility.

- Precise GNSS positioning using GPS plus BDS and LBS for accurate location reporting in Plaspy.
- Cellular connectivity with LTE Cat 1 and GSM fallback to maintain continuous updates and event reporting.
- Motion and vibration detection plus tamper and cover removal alerts for anti theft monitoring.
- Remote listen in and buzzer/horn output to assist audible locating and recovery workflows.
- Low voltage detection and digital inputs such as ACC and SOS to support battery and emergency monitoring.

## Core Features of Concox - VL103M

- Multi constellation GNSS positioning GPS BDS LBS for improved fix reliability and accuracy.
- LTE Cat 1 cellular data with GSM fallback for resilient connectivity across networks.
- Motion and vibration detection via onboard accelerometer to trigger event reports.
- Tamper and cover removal alerts to support anti theft workflows.
- Remote audio listen in capability and relay or IO output for buzzer or horn activation.
- Low voltage detection and internal backup battery to preserve telemetry during power loss.
- Digital inputs including ACC and SOS for basic status and emergency signaling.
- Local data buffering to store records when cellular connectivity is temporarily unavailable.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives position updates and event reports from the VL103M so fleets and operators can monitor location and alarms in real time. Within Plaspy, the tracker’s telemetry and events are presented as map markers, timeline events and configurable alerts that support operational responses.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps and support live tracking and historical playback.
- Event driven alerts such as vibration tamper and geo fence entry and exit are available as notifications in Plaspy.
- Digital input statuses like ACC and SOS can be reflected in device status panels and used in alert rules.
- Remote listen in and buzzer activation can be used as part of Plaspy alarm response procedures where supported by device configuration.
- Buffered data uploaded after connectivity restoration is ingested by Plaspy for complete historical records.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle fleet tracking where compact rugged hardware and accurate positioning are required.
- Light commercial vehicle operations that need real time location and motion based alerts.
- Vehicle rental management including geofence enforcement and low voltage protection.
- Stolen vehicle recovery using tamper vibration alerts combined with remote audio and buzzer actions.
- Emergency response and dispatch workflows leveraging SOS and ACC status notifications.

- Installation choices such as wiring of digital inputs and placement of the tracker can affect motion detection, tamper sensing and low voltage reporting.

- Regional cellular band variants may affect connectivity performance in certain areas; verify the model variant for your region.
- For comprehensive technical limits and parameter lists consult Concox firmware release notes and the official product manual.
