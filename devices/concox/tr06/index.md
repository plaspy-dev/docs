# Concox - TR06

![TR06](./logo.jpg)

The Concox TR06 is a vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable position reporting and basic vehicle security. It combines GPS satellite positioning with GSM GPRS connectivity to deliver continuous location updates, real time tracking, monitoring surveillance, and emergency alarm capabilities. The device is marketed for use in vehicle tracking and fleet management, and includes functionality such as remote engine off for added theft deterrence and control.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TR06 can feed its location and alarm data into Plaspy's fleet management platform to give operators centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can ingest the TR06's tracking and alert messages to present live location, historical routes, and event notifications alongside other assets, helping teams manage vehicles and respond to security events from a single interface.

## Key Highlights

- Combines GPS satellite positioning with GSM GPRS connectivity for continuous tracking
- Real time vehicle location updates suitable for active fleet monitoring
- Security focused features including emergency alarms and monitoring surveillance
- Remote engine off capability to help respond to theft or unauthorized use
- Integrated GPS software support for live tracking and location display
- Suitable for both single vehicle tracking and broader fleet deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Concox TR06 provides location and event information that Plaspy displays and uses for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy consolidates feeds from TR06 devices with other fleet data so teams can manage operations from a unified dashboard.

- Continuous location visibility for each TR06 equipped vehicle in the Plaspy interface
- Event and alarm forwarding so emergency alerts appear alongside other platform notifications
- Historical route playback and timeline review for trip analysis and incident investigation
- Fleet level dashboards and reports that include TR06 data for utilization and operational metrics
- Configurable alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on incoming TR06 messages and events

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet tracking for delivery and service vehicle operations
- Theft response and recovery workflows using emergency alarms and remote engine off
- Monitoring of high value or high risk vehicles with live alerts and location history
- Centralized oversight for mixed fleets combining TR06 devices with other trackers
- Route review and operational reporting to improve dispatch and utilization
