# Concox - TR06

![TR06](./logo.jpg)

The TR06 Vehicle GPS Tracker from Concox is a reliable and versatile device that combines the power of the GSM/GPRS network with the accuracy of GPS satellite positioning. This tracker offers a wide range of features, including security, positioning, monitoring surveillance, and emergency alarms, making it an ideal choice for vehicle tracking and fleet management.

One of the standout features of the TR06 is its remote engine off capability. With this feature, you can remotely turn off the engine of your vehicle in case of theft or unauthorized use, providing an extra layer of security and control. Additionally, the tracker comes with a GPS software that allows you to easily track and monitor your vehicle's location in real-time, ensuring that you always know where your assets are.

With the TR06 Vehicle GPS Tracker, you can enjoy peace of mind knowing that your vehicles are secure and well-monitored. Whether you are a business owner looking to manage your fleet or an individual wanting to keep track of your personal vehicle, the TR06 offers the reliability and functionality you need.

#### Key Features:

- Remote engine off capability
- GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellite positioning
- Security, positioning, monitoring surveillance, and emergency alarms
- Real-time tracking and monitoring with GPS software
