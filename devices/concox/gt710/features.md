# Concox - GT710 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Concox GT710 and explains how its core capabilities relate to use with the Plaspy platform. It is intended to help fleet managers, asset owners, and technical evaluators understand what the GT710 offers and how Plaspy can surface the device's tracking information for operational visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The content here is based on the device description and focuses on public, non-sensitive capabilities; for full technical specs and the latest details consult the official Concox documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Concox GT710 is described as a compact, waterproof asset GPS tracker designed for long standby operation and reliable location reporting. It is positioned for fixed asset tracking across many industries where discreet, durable, and long-lasting tracking is required.

- Long standby design for extended tracking intervals without frequent recharging
- Compact form factor for discreet installation on equipment and goods
- Waterproof construction for protection in outdoor or harsh environments
- Targeted at fixed asset tracking use cases such as rentals, storage, and equipment monitoring
- Practical for tracking vehicles, heavy machinery, and specific high value goods

## Core Features of Concox - GT710

- Long standby time suited for assets that require extended unattended operation
- Compact and low profile housing for discreet placement on assets
- Waterproof enclosure to withstand exposure to weather and challenging conditions
- Optimized for fixed asset tracking scenarios rather than continuous vehicle telemetry
- Broad applicability across industries including leasing, equipment rental, and cargo storage
- Designed for straightforward installation to minimize setup complexity
- Reliable basic tracking capability to support location awareness and asset presence

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present location updates and status information from the GT710 so organizations can monitor assets centrally. Plaspy’s device detection and mapping features make it easy to bring GT710 devices online and view asset movement and presence.

- Device location appears on Plaspy maps for real time position awareness
- Historical location trails and timestamps help show asset movement over time
- Online and offline visibility in Plaspy reflects device reporting and standby behavior
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols so GT710 units can be added with minimal configuration
- Long standby operation translates to longer reporting intervals while preserving visibility in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking leased vehicles and assets to support recovery and billing workflows

- Managing stored or in transit valuable goods and cargo
