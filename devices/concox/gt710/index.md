# Concox - GT710

![GT710](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT710 is a versatile asset GPS tracker that offers long standby time and reliable tracking capabilities. This compact and waterproof device is specifically designed for fixed asset tracking applications, making it ideal for a wide range of industries and use cases.

One of the standout features of the GT710 is its long standby time, which ensures that you can track your assets for extended periods without the need for frequent recharging. This is particularly useful for applications such as leasing, credit vehicles, valuable storage and transportation, heavy machinery and equipment rentals, cattle grazing management, automobile, motorcycle electric vehicles, and specific goods monitoring.

With its durable and waterproof design, the GT710 can withstand harsh environmental conditions, ensuring that your assets are protected even in challenging situations. The device is also compact and easy to install, allowing for discreet tracking without drawing attention to the asset being monitored.

Overall, the Concox GT710 is a reliable and efficient asset GPS tracker that offers long standby time and a range of features to meet the needs of various industries. Whether you need to track vehicles, equipment, or valuable goods, the GT710 provides the peace of mind and real-time tracking capabilities you require.
