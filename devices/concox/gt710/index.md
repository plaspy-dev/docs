# Concox - GT710

![GT710](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT710 is a compact asset GPS tracker designed for fixed asset tracking applications. It emphasizes long standby time and durable, waterproof construction to provide reliable location tracking for assets that may be stored, transported, or deployed in challenging environments. Its small form factor and straightforward design make it suitable for discreet monitoring of a wide range of items.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT710 can be used to feed asset location data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. The tracker’s long standby capability and rugged enclosure make it a practical choice for organizations that need persistent tracking without frequent maintenance, and Plaspy can surface that data in maps, reports, and alerting workflows to support fleet and asset management needs.

## Key Highlights

- Long standby time for extended tracking between maintenance intervals  
- Compact and discreet form factor suitable for fixed asset deployment  
- Waterproof and durable housing for use in harsh environments  
- Designed specifically for fixed asset tracking scenarios  
- Reliable location tracking for storage, transport, and rental applications  
- Simple, industry oriented design that fits a variety of use cases

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GT710 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous asset location data and status awareness. Once connected, Plaspy can use the device data to present maps, history, and notifications so teams can manage assets more effectively.

- Real time and historical location visibility on Plaspy maps and dashboards  
- Configurable alerts and notifications for movement, inactivity, or custom events  
- Grouping and tagging of assets for easier fleet and inventory management  
- Reporting and exportable history to support audits and operational analysis  
- Operational monitoring to help reduce loss, improve utilization, and plan maintenance

## Typical Use Cases

- Leasing and credit vehicle tracking to maintain oversight of collateral assets  
- Heavy machinery and equipment rental management for site visibility  
- Valuable storage and transportation monitoring to protect high value shipments  
- Cattle grazing and agricultural asset tracking in remote areas  
- Automobile, motorcycle, and electric vehicle tracking for fleet oversight  
- Monitoring specific goods and high value items during storage or transit
