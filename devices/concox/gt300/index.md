# Concox - GT300

![GT300](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT300 is a versatile GSM/GPS safety phone that is specifically designed for the safety and security of children, elderly individuals, luggage, and pets. With its built-in GPS receiver, this tracker offers superior sensitivity and fast time to first fix, ensuring accurate and reliable location tracking. 

This GPS tracking equipment features a quad-band GPRS/GSM subsystem that supports frequencies of 850/900/1800/1900 MHz, allowing for seamless connectivity in various regions. The system integration of the GT300 is straightforward, thanks to the comprehensive documentation provided for its full-featured functions. 

One of the standout features of the Concox GT300 is its support for a wide variety of reports, including emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, low battery notifications, and SOS alarms. These features provide peace of mind to users, allowing them to monitor the safety and well-being of their loved ones or valuable belongings. 

With its compact and portable design, the GT300 can be easily carried or attached to different objects, making it an ideal choice for tracking children, elderly individuals, luggage, or pets. Whether you need to keep an eye on your child's whereabouts, ensure the safety of an elderly family member, or track your luggage during travel, the Concox GT300 is a reliable and efficient solution.
