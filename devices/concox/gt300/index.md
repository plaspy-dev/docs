# Concox - GT300

![GT300](./logo.jpg)

The Concox GT300 is a compact GSM GPS safety phone designed for personal and small item tracking. It provides built-in GPS reception with strong sensitivity and a fast time to first fix, making it suitable for keeping track of children, elderly family members, luggage, or pets. The device includes a quad-band GPRS GSM subsystem and supports a range of report types such as emergency alerts, geo fence boundary crossings, low battery notifications, and SOS alarms.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT300 can feed location and alert information into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. Compatibility allows organizations and individual users to include the GT300 in Plaspy workflows for visibility, alerting, and historical location review without changing the device form factor or primary use cases.

## Key Highlights

- Built-in GPS receiver with strong sensitivity and fast time to first fix for reliable position updates.
- Quad band GPRS GSM support for wide regional connectivity.
- Designed as a portable safety phone suitable for children elderly luggage and pets.
- Supports multiple report types including emergency alerts geo fence events low battery and SOS.
- Compact and easy to carry or attach to personal items for discreet tracking.
- Documentation available to support system integration and setup.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GT300 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous location visibility and event reporting from personal tracking devices. Once device data is delivered to Plaspy, the platform can consolidate location history and alerts alongside other tracked assets for unified monitoring.

- Real time and historical location visibility within Plaspy dashboards and maps.
- Event and alert ingestion for emergency notifications geo fence crossings and low battery warnings.
- Centralized monitoring for mixed fleets and personal trackers including children and elderly monitoring.
- Reporting and exportable location history to support operational review and incident follow up.
- Notifications and operational alerts routed to Plaspy users for faster response and oversight.
