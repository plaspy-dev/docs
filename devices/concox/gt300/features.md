# Concox - GT300 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Concox GT300 and how its capabilities appear when used with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing functions, operational value, and high level integration notes so you can understand what the GT300 can do inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware behavior consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Concox GT300 is a compact GSM/GPS safety phone designed to provide reliable location tracking and event reporting for people and small items. It combines a sensitive GPS receiver with a quad band GSM GPRS subsystem and supports a range of alert reports that are relevant to monitoring and safety workflows.

- Built in GPS receiver with high sensitivity and fast time to first fix for accurate location updates
- Quad band GPRS GSM connectivity for broad regional coverage
- Support for emergency and SOS alerts to flag urgent situations
- Geo fence boundary crossing reports for location based notifications
- Low battery notifications to help maintain device availability
- Compact portable form factor suitable for children, elderly people, luggage, and pets

## Core Features of Concox - GT300

- Built in GPS receiver optimized for reliable position fixes
- Quad band GPRS GSM radio supporting 850 900 1800 1900 MHz bands
- Fast time to first fix and enhanced GPS sensitivity for quicker location acquisition
- SOS alarm and emergency alert reporting for urgent notifications
- Geo fence boundary crossing alerts to notify on entry and exit of defined areas
- Low battery status reporting to signal when the unit needs charging or service
- Small portable design intended for wear or attachment to people and personal items

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the GT300 is used with Plaspy, the device's location and event reports can be surfaced in the platform to provide operational visibility and timely alerts. Plaspy detects supported tracker protocols and presents standard events and location data alongside other fleet and device telemetry.

- Real time or periodic location updates displayed on Plaspy maps and device timelines
- SOS and emergency alerts delivered as high priority events inside Plaspy for rapid attention
- Geo fence events logged and available for alerting, reporting, and historical review
- Low battery notifications shown in the device status to help maintain uptime
- Device identity and connectivity state available in Plaspy for monitoring online status and recent activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring the location and safety of children or elderly family members
- Attaching to luggage and personal items for travel tracking and recovery
- Tracking pets during outings or transit
- Short term monitoring of valuable or portable assets
- Situational awareness and emergency notification for caretakers or guardians
