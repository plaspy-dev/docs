# VL110C Vehicle GNSS Terminal

The VL110C is a compact 4G LTE GPS tracker engineered for reliable vehicle management and Plaspy compatible deployment across motorcycles, passenger cars, light commercial vehicles and industrial equipment. With LTE Cat 1 connectivity and GSM \(2G\) fallback, the VL110C delivers real-time tracking and telemetry even in marginal coverage areas, making it an ideal choice for fleet management, stolen vehicle recovery and dealer-level tracking for small vehicles.

Built for harsh environments, the VL110C combines IP65 dust/water resistance, wide 9–90V DC input, and a 270 mAh industrial Li‑Polymer backup battery that allows continued operation when vehicle power is removed. Plaspy compatibility ensures you can ingest location, alarms and driving behavior data directly into your monitoring and fleet workflows for immediate alerting, reporting and remote actions such as immobilizer control.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management across diverse vehicle types.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 communication with GSM \(2G\) fallback to preserve connectivity in low-LTE areas.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and wide 9–90V DC input for motorcycles, cars, light commercial vehicles and industrial equipment.
- Internal GNSS antenna with GPS/BDS/GLONASS positioning under 2.5 m CEP for precise location data.
- Vehicle battery protection and 270 mAh backup battery to maintain telemetry during power loss or theft events.
- Remote cut-off via relay for anti-theft action and support for ignition \(ACC\) telemetry for driver/event monitoring.
- GNSS/LTE jamming detection and configurable alarms to enhance stolen vehicle recovery workflows.
- Compact, lightweight form factor and simple integration path with Tracksolid Pro and Plaspy-enabled platforms.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the VL110C with Plaspy brings its real-time GPS tracker data into a unified fleet management and monitoring environment. Position, telemetry and event data stream over LTE \(with 2G fallback\) and can be consumed by Plaspy for live maps, geofencing, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy-compatible integration enables remote commands from the cloud, automated workflows and consolidated telemetry across mixed-device fleets.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates for continuous situational awareness and route monitoring.
- Ignition \(ACC\) input reporting for basic driving state and event tagging in Plaspy dashboards.
- Remote immobilizer / relay control through Plaspy to support anti-theft and stolen vehicle recovery use cases.
- GNSS/LTE jamming detection alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate investigation and response.
- Driving behavior events \(harsh acceleration, braking, cornering, collision\) available for fleet safety reporting and coaching.
