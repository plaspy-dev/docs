# Concox - VL110C

![VL110C](./tracker.png)

The Concox VL110C is a compact vehicle GNSS terminal designed for reliable vehicle management across motorcycles, passenger cars, light commercial vehicles and industrial equipment. It combines rugged environmental protection, a wide input voltage range and an internal backup battery to maintain telemetry when vehicle power is removed, making it suitable for continuous tracking and basic vehicle security workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VL110C can deliver location, alarms and driving event data into Plaspy for monitoring, alerting and reporting. Its design and feature set align with common fleet and security needs, so organizations using Plaspy can ingest VL110C data to improve situational awareness, support stolen vehicle recovery and incorporate tracked assets into centralized operational workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact GNSS terminal built for small vehicles and equipment with IP65 dust and water resistance.
- 4G LTE connectivity with GSM 2G fallback to maintain reporting in varied coverage conditions.
- Wide 9–90V DC input and internal backup battery to preserve telemetry during power interruption.
- Supports ignition input reporting and a relay output for remote cut off to assist anti theft actions.
- GNSS positioning with multimode satellite support and sub 2.5 m CEP location accuracy.
- Built in jamming detection and configurable alarms to enhance recovery and security processes.
- Lightweight form factor suitable for dealer, rental and motorcycle fleet deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, VL110C devices stream position and event data into a single fleet monitoring environment so teams can view assets on live maps, create alerts and generate historical reports. Plaspy consumes incoming telemetry and events from the tracker to power dashboards, notifications and operational workflows that help reduce response times and improve fleet oversight.

- Real time location updates for live tracking and route monitoring on Plaspy maps.
- Event and ignition reporting for tagging driving state and correlating trips in reports.
- Remote immobilizer or relay control triggered from Plaspy to support recovery actions.
- Jamming and alarm notifications forwarded to Plaspy for immediate investigation.
- Historical trip replay and basic driving event records to support safety reviews.
- Consolidation of VL110C telemetry alongside mixed device fleets for unified reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Small vehicle and motorcycle fleet management with continuous location visibility and route history.
- Anti theft and stolen vehicle recovery combining jamming alerts and remote relay control.
- Dealer and rental fleet tracking for handover status, diagnostics and location audits.
- Lightweight equipment monitoring where compact form factor and backup operation are needed.
- Operational oversight for mixed fleets that require ignition state and event logging.
