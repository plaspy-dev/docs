# Concox - AT1 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Concox AT1 tracker with Plaspy. It covers the AT1's practical capabilities, how those capabilities map to Plaspy workflows, and the typical operational value you can expect when integrating this model into cloud telematics and asset tracking deployments.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and installation type. Manufacturer configuration, firmware updates and local regulations can affect certain functions, so treat the items below as a functional overview rather than a definitive device specification.

## Feature Overview

The Concox AT1 is a compact, redeployable GNSS tracker built for rugged outdoor asset tracking and fleet monitoring. It combines long battery autonomy, durable enclosure and onboard sensing to provide reliable position updates, event notifications and buffered data delivery where intermittent connectivity is expected.

- Precise GNSS positioning with LBS fallback to maintain location awareness in marginal reception.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure with strong magnetic mount for easy attachment to containers, trailers and outdoor assets.
- Long autonomy from a 6,000 mAh rechargeable battery suitable for long deployments and minimal maintenance.
- Onboard event detection including light and motion sensing for tamper and movement alerts.
- Waterproof microphone with remote voice monitoring and onboard audio recording for added situational context.
- Local data buffering to store positions and audio when connectivity is interrupted and forward them to the cloud when restored.

## Core Features of Concox - AT1

- GNSS positioning combined with LBS fallback for resilient location reporting in mixed coverage areas.
- IP67 rated enclosure and magnetic mounting for outdoor and container use without complex installation.
- 6,000 mAh rechargeable battery providing extended operation and configurable power saving modes.
- Onboard sensors including light sensor and accelerometer for tamper detection and movement events.
- Waterproof microphone with remote monitoring and onboard voice recording capability.
- Local storage buffering (32+32 Mb) to hold telemetry and audio until a connection is available.
- Quad band cellular support for wide 2G coverage where available.
- Configurable reporting modes to balance tracking frequency and battery life.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location, event and buffered telemetry from the AT1 so operators can view live positions, receive alerts and analyze historical tracks from a single platform. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and surfaces device events and telemetry in dashboards, reports and alerting tools.

- Live GNSS and LBS positions appear in Plaspy maps to provide continuous asset visibility.
- Tamper, movement and other sensor-triggered events are forwarded as alerts to Plaspy for immediate operational response.
- Local buffering ensures that positions and recorded audio are uploaded to Plaspy once connectivity is restored, preserving continuity of records.
- Audio features such as remote voice monitoring and stored recordings can be associated with event timelines in Plaspy for situational context.
- Reporting mode configuration on the device influences update frequency and battery consumption, which Plaspy can reflect in monitoring and reporting workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking trailers, pallets and non-powered assets during transit with low-installation effort.
- Monitoring freight and container exteriors where IP67 protection and magnetic mounting are required.
- Anti-theft and tamper detection workflows using light and motion sensors to trigger alerts.
- Caregiving or vulnerable-person monitoring with audio awareness features deployed under appropriate consent.
- Temporary or seasonal deployments where redeployable, long-autonomy tracking is needed.
- Long-term asset inventory and location audits for logistics and equipment management.
