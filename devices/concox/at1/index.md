# Concox - AT1

![AT1](./tracker.png)

The AT1 is a compact GNSS GPS tracker designed for rugged asset tracking and fleet monitoring, now Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into cloud telematics workflows. With an IP67 enclosure, strong magnetic mount and a long-life 6,000 mAh rechargeable battery, the AT1 delivers reliable real-time tracking and situational awareness for freight, containers, high-value assets and vulnerable-person monitoring.

The unit combines precise GNSS positioning with LBS fallback, onboard event detection and local data buffering to keep your Plaspy dashboards current even in challenging coverage areas. Plaspy compatible by design, the AT1 helps fleets and asset managers implement anti-theft workflows, telemetry reporting and long-autonomy deployments with minimal installation effort.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for redeployable asset tracking and fleet management with minimal installation.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and magnetic mounting for reliable outdoor and container use.
- Long autonomy: industrial 6,000 mAh Li‑Polymer battery offering ~25 days typical operation and ultra-long standby \(up to 1.5 years in power‑saving modes\).
- High-accuracy positioning: GPS with LBS fallback and position accuracy better than 2.5 m CEP under good reception for dependable real-time tracking.
- Onboard sensors and anti-tamper features — light sensor, accelerometer, over‑speed and geo‑fencing alarms — for anti-theft and movement detection workflows.
- Waterproof microphone, remote voice monitoring and onboard voice recording for audio-based situational awareness near the device.
- Local storage buffering \(32+32 Mb\) and standard protocol support to ensure telemetry delivery to Plaspy and third‑party systems.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AT1 sends location updates, events and telemetry over cellular links so you can view live positions, generate alerts and analyze historical tracks from a single platform. Plaspy can ingest the AT1’s GPS/LBS positions and sensor-triggered events to provide real-time tracking, alarm handling and reporting for fleet management and asset protection.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via quad-band GSM \(2G\) with LBS fallback for persistent coverage.
- Tamper, movement and over‑speed alerts delivered to Plaspy for immediate anti-theft and safety responses.
- Local data storage that buffers locations and voice recordings when coverage drops and forwards them to Plaspy when connectivity returns.
- Audio features \(remote voice monitoring and onboard recording\) that can be surfaced in the Plaspy interface for situational context.
- Configurable reporting modes \(regular GPS, tracking, power‑saving\) to balance reporting frequency and battery life across Plaspy-driven workflows.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management for trailers and non-powered assets — track location and movement between hubs with long battery life.
- Freight and container monitoring — IP67 ruggedness and magnetic mount enable secure placement on container exteriors for cross-border logistics.
- Anti-theft and tamper detection — light sensor, accelerometer and on/off disabling function help detect unauthorized handling and shutdown attempts.
- Vulnerable-person or caregiver support — remote voice monitoring and recording provide extra situational awareness when deployed with appropriate consent and policies.
- Temporary or redeployable asset monitoring — easy installation and multi-mode operation make the AT1 ideal for seasonal fleets and loaner equipment.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT1 brings together rugged hardware, long autonomy and practical sensing into a compact GPS tracker that is Plaspy compatible out of the box. For businesses that need dependable real-time tracking and telemetry without complex installation, the AT1 reduces deployment time while maintaining rich event data and audio context. Its local buffering and LBS fallback help ensure continuity of data to Plaspy in areas with intermittent cellular coverage.

Choose the AT1 with Plaspy when you need a trustworthy, low-maintenance solution for fleet management and anti-theft monitoring, plus the ability to extend telematics workflows \(such as fuel monitoring, ignition logging or immobilizer control\) through Plaspy when vehicle interfaces or external sensors are present. The result is a scalable, easy-to-manage tracking solution that delivers situational awareness and long-term operational savings for asset-centric deployments.
