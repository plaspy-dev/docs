# Concox - JM-VG02 Features

This page provides a public features summary for the Concox JM-VG02 family (including the JM-VG02U variant) and explains how those capabilities are presented when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing features, practical benefits, and the kinds of information you can expect to see inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where possible this page uses the device description as the factual basis, but customers should verify current device behavior with official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The JM-VG02 is an OBD II style vehicle tracker designed for quick installation and focused on driving behavior analysis and reliable tracking in challenging signal environments. Its sensor suite and algorithms aim to surface actionable driving events and accurate mileage information for fleet and vehicle monitoring.

- INS aided GPS tracking to maintain position continuity in areas with weak GPS signals
- Advanced driving behavior analysis with detection of eight types of improper driving behaviors
- Fatigue driving alerts that notify when a driver has been continuously driving for extended periods
- High mileage accuracy with an algorithm reported to achieve over 98 percent accuracy
- Plug and play OBD II installation for fast deployment without professional installation
- Multiple alert types including low battery, speeding, and abnormal vibration reported by the device

## Core Features of Concox - JM-VG02

- INS aided GPS tracking for improved location continuity when GPS reception is degraded
- 6 axis motion sensing using a 3 axis accelerometer and a 3 axis gyroscope for motion and event detection
- Detection and reporting of eight categories of unsafe or improper driving behaviors
- Fatigue driving alerts to help identify long continuous driving sessions
- High precision mileage calculation with reported accuracy of 98 percent or better
- Wide operating voltage range suitable for a broad set of vehicle electrical systems
- Simple installation via the OBD II socket for quick device activation and removal
- Multiple device alerts covering events such as low battery, speeding, and abnormal vibration

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes device reports so that location, events, and mileage can be presented alongside other fleet data. The platform surfaces events and historical traces so operators can review driving behavior, mileage summaries, and alerts in context.

- Continuous position traces and trip paths that reflect the device's INS aided GPS smoothing when GPS signal is intermittent
- Driving behavior events and alerts become tracked events in Plaspy for review, filtering, and reporting
- Mileage values reported by the device can be used by Plaspy for distance reporting and route history reconciliation
- Fatigue and other time based alerts appear as events that can be routed to dashboards or notification rules in Plaspy
- Plug and play OBD installation simplifies device onboarding and allows rapid enrollment of vehicles into Plaspy fleet views
- Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol and integrates incoming reports from compatible Concox devices for immediate visibility

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet safety programs monitoring harsh maneuvers and coaching drivers based on detected behaviors
- Long route or rural operations where INS aided tracking helps maintain usable traces through GPS outages
- Accurate mileage reporting for billing, maintenance scheduling, or reimbursement tracking
- Rental and lease fleets that benefit from fast OBD II installation and removal
- Monitoring driver fatigue exposure for long haul or shift based operations
- Small and medium businesses seeking an easy to deploy device that reports key driving events
