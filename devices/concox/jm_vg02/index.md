# Concox - JM-VG02

![JM-VG02](./logo..png)

The Concox JM-VG02 is an OBD II vehicle tracker designed for effortless installation and continuous vehicle monitoring. Based on the JM-VG02U description, it includes motion sensing hardware and driving behavior analysis capabilities that detect multiple types of unsafe driving events. It also uses INS aided GPS tracking to maintain reliable location information in areas where satellite signals are weak, and it provides fatigue driving alerts and a high level of mileage accuracy for consistent operational reporting.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the JM-VG02 can feed its core telemetry and safety events into a centralized fleet platform for visibility and oversight. Plaspy can ingest location updates, driving behavior alerts, and mileage reports from this tracker so fleets and managers can view routes, monitor driver safety, and generate useful operational reports without losing the practical benefits of an OBD II plug and play installation.

## Key Highlights

- OBD II plug and play form factor for quick installation and deployment
- INS aided GPS tracking to improve location reliability in poor signal areas
- Driving behavior analysis that identifies eight types of improper driving events
- Fatigue driving alerts to highlight extended continuous driving periods
- High mileage calculation accuracy reported at 98 percent or greater
- Broad vehicle compatibility through a wide operating voltage range
- Multiple alert types for safety and operational monitoring
