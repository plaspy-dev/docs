# Concox - JM-VG02

![JM-VG02](./logo..png)

The JM-VG02U is an OBD II vehicle tracker from Concox that offers effortless installation and a range of advanced features. With its built-in 3-axis accelerometer, 3-axis gyroscope, and practical algorithm, this tracker can detect 8 types of improper driving behaviors, ensuring the safety of both the drivers and the vehicle. It even works in areas with poor GPS signals, thanks to its INS-aided GPS tracking feature.

One of the standout features of the JM-VG02U is its driving behavior analysis. It can analyze and detect dangerous driving behaviors, such as harsh braking or sudden acceleration, and send alerts to the user. This helps promote safer driving habits and reduces the risk of accidents. Additionally, the tracker offers fatigue driving alerts, notifying the user when a driver has been continuously driving for an extended period of time.

The JM-VG02U also boasts a high level of accuracy when it comes to mileage calculation. With a unique algorithm, it can achieve a mileage accuracy of over 98%, ensuring precise tracking of distance traveled. The tracker is compatible with a wide range of input voltages, making it suitable for use in various vehicles. And with its effortless installation, simply plugging it into the OBD II socket, there's no need to rely on professionals for setup.

Overall, the Concox JM-VG02U is a reliable and feature-packed OBD II vehicle tracker that offers advanced driving behavior analysis, accurate mileage calculation, and effortless installation. With its multiple alerts and compatibility with a wide range of vehicles, it is an excellent choice for enhancing the safety and security of your vehicle.

#### Key Features:

- INS-aided GPS Tracking for constant tracking in areas with poor GPS signals
- Advanced Driving Behavior Analysis with alerts for 8 types of dangerous driving behaviors
- Mileage Accuracy ≥98% for precise mileage calculation
- 6-axis Accelerometer & Gyroscope for sensitive detection of linear acceleration and angular velocity
- Wide Operating Voltage Range \(9-90V\) for compatibility with diverse vehicles
- Effortless Installation by simply plugging into the OBD II socket
- Fatigue Driving Alert for continuous driving over a long time
- Multiple Alerts for low battery, speeding, abnormal vibration, and more
