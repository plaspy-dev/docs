# Concox - Qbit M

![Qbit M](./tracker.png)

The Qbit M from Concox is a compact GPS tracker built for personal safety and discreet asset monitoring. It combines GPS and QZSS positioning with LTE‑M cellular connectivity to deliver low power, near real‑time location updates and emergency alerting. The device is designed for easy carrying and continuous network access, with features intended to support everyday tracking needs such as geofencing, sound alarm and scheduled operation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Qbit M can feed location and event data into Plaspy dashboards and notification workflows. Plaspy users benefit from centralized visibility, history playback and configurable alerts that make the Qbit M useful for safety monitoring, lightweight fleet oversight and anti‑theft scenarios. Integration with Plaspy helps turn the tracker’s position and event signals into operational insights and timely notifications.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker offering GNSS positioning combined with LTE‑M cellular reporting for continuous monitoring.
- GPS plus QZSS positioning for improved location accuracy in many operating environments.
- Compact and durable form factor suitable for personal carry and small asset protection with IP65 dust and water resistance.
- Dedicated panic and functional button controls for immediate location sharing and emergency alerts.
- App driven features such as geofencing, sound alarm and scheduled operation to support caregiver and safety workflows.
- Low power design with multi mode operation to balance standby life and regular reporting intervals.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Qbit M streams position and event information to provide persistent situational awareness and configurable alerting. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s updates to populate live maps, device status views and historical tracks so teams can monitor movement, respond to alerts and maintain device fleets or personal safety deployments.

- Live location updates and device status shown on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring.
- Panic and emergency button events forwarded to Plaspy to trigger notifications or response procedures.
- Geofence enter and exit alerts, including Wi‑Fi hotspot tied zones, used to automate notifications and rules.
- Low battery and buzzer alerts surfaced in Plaspy to help schedule maintenance and replacements.
- Movement detection events used to drive motion triggered tracking, history playback and incident review.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for elderly family members or children with panic alerts and geofence notifications.
- Companion and small asset protection where a lightweight tracker with long standby is preferred.
- Discrete package and parcel tracking for small shipments that need movement detection and remote alerts.
- Caregiver and short range monitoring tasks such as scheduled check ins, location checks and sound alarm location aid.
- Lightweight fleet or device fleets where centralized tracking and simple alerting are required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Qbit M is well suited for organizations and individuals who need accurate, low power real time tracking in a small portable form. Its combination of GNSS positioning, LTE‑M connectivity and user focused features like a panic button and sound alarm deliver practical capabilities for personal safety and small asset supervision. Using Plaspy to aggregate and act on Qbit M data adds operational value through unified dashboards, notification rules and historical reporting.
