# Concox - Qbit M

![Qbit M](./tracker.png)

The Qbit M from Qbit \(model Qbit M\) is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for personal safety and discreet asset monitoring. By combining GPS + QZSS positioning with LTE‑M \(Cat M1 / Cat NB\) cellular connectivity, Qbit M delivers reliable, low‑power real‑time tracking and emergency alerting for elderly care, children, companions and small asset protection.

Designed for easy carrying and continuous network access, the Qbit M supports intelligent indoor/outdoor positioning, multiple working modes, and app-driven features such as geofencing, sound alarm and scheduled operation. Plaspy users can integrate Qbit M data into dashboards and alerts to support real‑time tracking, telemetry and anti‑theft workflows for light‑weight fleet or personal-device use cases.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: integrates GNSS + LTE‑M location updates for real‑time tracking and alerts.
- High accuracy positioning: GPS + QZSS with assisted positioning for indoor/outdoor coverage and stated \<2.5 m CEP accuracy.
- Long standby and low power: 650 mAh Li‑Polymer battery with Smart and Interval working modes—up to ~7 days typical standby in smart mode.
- Dedicated emergency controls: one functional button and a dedicated panic button for immediate location sharing and alarms.
- Compact, durable form factor: 40.0 × 45.0 × 15.0 mm, 30.5 g, IP65 dust/water resistance and wide operating temperature range.
- App features: geofence \(safety fence\) tied to Wi‑Fi hotspots, sound alarm to locate the wearer, scheduled working and low battery buzzer alerts.
- Robust sensitivity and fast fixes: high tracking sensitivity and competitive TTFF for timely telemetry and position reports.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Qbit M streams GNSS positions and device status via LTE‑M to provide continuous situational awareness and configurable alerts. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s location, motion and event data to power real‑time tracking maps, history playback and notification rules for safety, telemetry and light fleet management.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered over LTE‑M \(Cat M1 / Cat NB\) for continuous tracking.
- Panic/emergency button status and instant location sharing for rapid response workflows.
- Geofence exit/enter alerts \(safety fence\) and Wi‑Fi hotspot‑tied zones to trigger Plaspy notifications.
- Buzzer and low battery warnings forwarded to Plaspy for proactive device maintenance alerts.
- Movement detection using the built‑in accelerometer to support motion‑triggered tracking and intelligent positioning.

## Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for elderly family members or children with panic button alerts and geofence notifications.
- Companion and pet monitoring where a small, lightweight GPS tracker and long standby are required.
- Discrete package or parcel tracking for small shipments that benefit from LTE‑M connectivity and movement detection.
- Short‑range caregiver workflows—scheduled location checks, sound alarm to locate the wearer, and exit alerts tied to safe zones.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Qbit M is an ideal Plaspy compatible GPS tracker when you need accurate, low‑power real‑time tracking in a very small package. Its LTE‑M connectivity and GNSS mix provide dependable position reporting and telemetry, while the panic button, accelerometer and app features deliver practical anti‑theft and emergency alerting capabilities. Plaspy integration extends Qbit M’s value by centralizing location history, configurable notifications and live monitoring—helping teams scale from individual personal safety deployments to lightweight fleet management and telemetry scenarios.

Note: Qbit M focuses on personal and small‑asset tracking. For vehicle‑grade telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition sensing or immobilizer control, verify vehicle interface support and variant options before deployment. For integration with Bluetooth sensors or additional I/O, consult the device documentation or accessory specifications to confirm compatibility.
