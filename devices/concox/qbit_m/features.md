# Concox - Qbit M Features

This page provides a public, features-oriented summary of the Concox Qbit M and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about the device's tracking, alerting, and low-power operation as they relate to Plaspy compatibility and common monitoring workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. For device-specific configuration, firmware details, and the latest technical specifications consult the official Concox documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Qbit M is a compact, Plaspy‑compatible tracker designed for personal safety and small asset monitoring. It combines GNSS positioning and LTE‑M cellular connectivity to deliver low‑power, frequent location updates and event alerts suitable for companion tracking, caregiver workflows, and lightweight asset protection.

- Real‑time GNSS location updates using GPS plus QZSS for improved regional positioning.
- Low‑power LTE‑M (Cat M1 / Cat NB) cellular connectivity for continuous tracking with extended standby.
- Dedicated panic and functional buttons for immediate alerting and location sharing.
- App-driven features such as geofencing, scheduled operation, and sound alarm to locate the wearer.
- Movement detection via built‑in accelerometer to trigger reporting and intelligent positioning.
- Compact, durable design with IP65 ingress protection for everyday carry and small asset use.

## Core Features of Concox - Qbit M

- GNSS positioning: GPS plus QZSS positioning for improved accuracy in supported regions, with stated position accuracy under 2.5 m CEP.
- Cellular connectivity: LTE‑M Cat M1 and Cat NB support for low-power wide area networking and frequent telemetry updates.
- Power and standby: 650 mAh Li‑Polymer battery with smart and interval working modes; typical standby times vary with usage and configuration.
- Emergency and controls: one functional button and one dedicated panic button for rapid location sharing and alerts.
- App features: geofence (safety fence), scheduled operation, sound alarm, and low battery buzzer alerts available through the device app.
- Movement sensing: built‑in accelerometer for movement detection, motion‑triggered reporting, and intelligent indoor/outdoor positioning.
- Robust tracking performance: competitive receiver sensitivity and TTFF characteristics as published by the manufacturer for timely fixes.
- Compact, durable form factor: small dimensions, light weight, and IP65 dust/water resistance for personal and small asset deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the Qbit M’s location and event data to present live position, history, and alerts in a centralized monitoring environment. Device events and telemetry reported by Qbit M appear in Plaspy dashboards and notification rules to support operational oversight and response.

- Real‑time location and position history visible on Plaspy maps and playback tools for situational awareness.
- Panic button events and immediate location sharing trigger notification workflows and can be included in alert rules.
- Geofence enter/exit events and Wi‑Fi‑tied safety zones generate Plaspy notifications for perimeter monitoring.
- Battery and low battery buzzer alerts are surfaced in Plaspy to support proactive maintenance and device management.
- Motion detection and movement events from the accelerometer can enable activity‑based reporting and conditional notifications within Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for elderly relatives, children, or companions with panic alerts and geofence notifications.
- Lightweight caregiver workflows using scheduled checks, movement detection, and location history.
- Small asset and parcel monitoring where compact size, LTE‑M connectivity, and motion detection are required.
- Short‑range care settings where sound alarm and buzzer functions help locate the wearer quickly.
- Companion or pet monitoring where long standby and discreet form factor are priorities.
- Temporary rental or loaner device scenarios benefiting from app scheduling and remote alerting.
