# Concox - VL502

![VL502](./tracker.png)

The VL502 is a compact OBDII GNSS vehicle tracker from Concox designed for corporate fleets, usage based insurance programs, dealer monitoring and individual vehicle oversight. As a plug and play OBDII unit, it reports vehicle telemetry such as VIN, engine speed, coolant temperature, accumulated mileage, fuel consumption stats, battery voltage and ignition status, and transmits that data over mobile networks for real time visibility.

This model is compatible with Plaspy and is suited to organizations that need straightforward fleet tracking and vehicle diagnostics without complex installation. When connected to Plaspy, VL502 telemetry and event data feed into live maps, alerts and reports to support anti theft workflows, driver behavior analysis, fuel monitoring and operational optimization.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBDII installation for rapid fleet deployment and minimal disruption.
- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking, centralized monitoring and reporting.
- Rich vehicle telemetry including VIN, engine speed, coolant temperature, mileage, fuel consumption and battery voltage.
- Mobile connectivity with 4G LTE and automatic 2G fallback plus regional band variants for broad coverage.
- Dual GNSS positioning using GPS and BDS for improved location accuracy and quicker fixes.
- Built in accelerometer for driving behavior detection such as overspeed events and harsh maneuvers.
- Rugged compact form factor suitable for daily vehicle operation and fleet use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed, the VL502 streams GNSS positions and OBDII sourced telemetry to Plaspy, where that information is processed into dashboards, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy uses the incoming data to give fleet managers and insurance teams timely operational insight and configurable notifications.

- Real time location and telemetry updates in Plaspy for live tracking and route playback.
- Diagnostic parameters and VIN data available for maintenance planning and remote checks.
- Fuel consumption and mileage reporting to support fuel management and usage based insurance analytics.
- Ignition and idling events, plus device removal indicators, for operational monitoring and anti theft alerts.
- Accelerometer driven events for driving behavior scoring and safety reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Centralized fleet management with live tracking, route history and maintenance alerts.
- Usage based insurance programs relying on mileage and driving behavior data.
- Dealer and service center monitoring for VIN based diagnostics and service preparation.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using ignition status and geofence alerts.
- Driver safety and coaching programs using accelerometer event logs.

The VL502 combines convenient OBDII connectivity with the vehicle telemetry depth many fleets and insurance programs require. Its plug and play nature reduces installation time, while the device’s ability to report VIN, mileage and diagnostic parameters makes it useful for maintenance, compliance and usage based billing. Integrated GNSS positioning and an accelerometer provide the location and event data that Plaspy uses to generate actionable alerts and operational reports.
