# Concox - VL502 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Concox VL502 OBDII GNSS vehicle tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for tracking, telemetry and fleet oversight. It is written to help fleet managers, insurers, dealers and individual users understand the practical functions the VL502 can provide when reporting into the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary across firmware versions, hardware revisions, regional variants and installation methods. Use this page as a product feature summary and consult the device manufacturer for the most current and detailed technical specifications.

## Feature Overview

The VL502 is a compact plug and play OBDII tracker that delivers vehicle telemetry and GNSS positions to Plaspy over cellular networks. It is intended to give real vehicle diagnostics and motion events without complex wiring and to support rapid deployment across fleets.

- Plug and play OBDII installation for quick deployment and minimal vehicle downtime.
- Vehicle telemetry including VIN, engine speed, coolant temperature, accumulated mileage, fuel consumption statistics and battery voltage.
- Cellular connectivity with 4G LTE and automatic 2G fallback for wide area coverage.
- Dual GNSS positioning using GPS and BDS for improved location accuracy.
- Built in accelerometer to report driving events such as harsh braking, acceleration and collisions.
- Support for common OBD protocols to access diagnostic parameters and trouble codes.

## Core Features of Concox - VL502

- OBDII plug in form factor for simple installation and immediate access to vehicle bus data.
- Vehicle identity and diagnostics such as VIN and diagnostic trouble codes for maintenance workflows.
- Key telemetry metrics including engine speed, coolant temperature, mileage and fuel consumption.
- Battery voltage and ACC or ignition status monitoring for operational alerts.
- Dual GNSS positioning with GPS and BDS for reliable location reporting.
- Built in accelerometer for event detection used in driving behavior and collision reporting.
- Support for standard onboard diagnostic protocols to read vehicle parameters and fault codes.
- Cellular communications with multi band support and fallback to ensure persistent connectivity.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the VL502 is registered and reporting to Plaspy, the device telemetry and event data are processed into maps, reports and alerts that fleet and insurance teams can use for operational decision making. Plaspy ingests position and diagnostic data to present location awareness, event history and vehicle state in a centralized view.

- Live and historical location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps for route review and playback.
- Vehicle telemetry such as VIN, engine speed and coolant temperature shown in device details and reports.
- Diagnostic trouble codes and OBD parameters available for maintenance scheduling and remote diagnostics.
- Fuel consumption and accumulated mileage presented in fuel and usage reports for cost management and UBI analysis.
- Ignition and ACC state, plus accelerometer events, surfaced as alerts and event markers for safety and anti theft monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet management with centralized tracking, maintenance alerts and route replay.
- Usage based insurance programs that need mileage, fuel usage and driving behavior data.
- Dealer and service center monitoring for VIN based checks and pre service diagnostics.
- Anti theft workflows using ignition status, removal events and location history to aid recovery.
- Driver safety and coaching programs using accelerometer driven event logs and scoring.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature availability depends on the device firmware version and the hardware revision installed in the vehicle.
- Some telemetry and diagnostic parameters require vehicle support via the OBDII port and the vehicle manufacturer implementation of OBD protocols.
- Regional cellular band variants and regulatory requirements may affect network performance and connectivity options.
- Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols to simplify onboarding, but device configuration and reporting intervals can be affected by firmware settings.
- Always consult the latest manufacturer documentation for precise lists of supported OBD parameters and protocol behavior.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the VL502 together with Plaspy provides a practical way to convert raw vehicle bus data and GNSS positions into operational insight. Plaspy aggregates location, diagnostic and event data from the tracker into dashboards, alerts and reports so teams can monitor fleet health, analyze driver behavior and respond to incidents more efficiently.
