# Concox - MT200 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Concox MT200 MOPLUS motorcycle GPS tracker and how those features are typically used when the device is connected to Plaspy. It focuses on user facing capabilities, practical value, and what you can expect to monitor in the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For full technical details and the latest firmware related information consult the official manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The MT200 is designed specifically for motorcycles and focuses on robust location tracking and simple installation. It combines weather resistance and basic power management with features that suit single owners as well as rental and fleet operations.

- IP65 rated housing for dust and water resistance suitable for motorcycle use
- Real time location tracking to monitor vehicle position while in service
- Remote fuel and power cut off capability without complex wiring for simplified installs
- Intelligent power management aimed at protecting the motorcycle battery
- Intended use cases include individual security, motorcycle rental, and fleet tracking

## Core Features of Concox - MT200

- Motorcycle optimized form factor and rugged casing with IP65 protection
- Continuous location reporting for real time tracking and visibility
- Remote fuel and power cut off functionality as a theft deterrent option
- Intelligent power management to reduce drain on the vehicle battery
- Designed for straightforward installation without complex wiring requirements
- Suitable for fleet monitoring and rental management scenarios
- Built to withstand outdoor motorcycle conditions and regular use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the MT200 is used with Plaspy, the platform provides consolidated visibility and operational context for the device capabilities. Plaspy is designed to detect tracker protocols and present the incoming data in maps and device lists so teams can act on location and status information.

- Plaspy displays real time position updates on the map and in device lists for operational tracking
- Status and connectivity indicators in Plaspy help confirm whether a tracker is online and reporting
- Events such as power or fuel cut off actions are visible as device status changes when reported by the tracker
- Historical position data and basic movement history are available for review within Plaspy
- Plaspy supports managing multiple devices for rental fleets and motorcycle fleet oversight
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding
