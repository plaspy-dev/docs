# Concox - MT200

<div className="device-logo">
![MT200](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Concox MT200 MOPLUS is a motorcycle GPS tracker built for durability and practical vehicle control. Designed with an IP65 dustproof and waterproof enclosure, the MT200 withstands harsh outdoor conditions and continuous use on two wheel vehicles. The device offers core tracking functions together with a convenient fuel and power cut feature that does not require complex wiring, making it well suited to motorcycle installations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT200 can feed live position data and device status into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and fleet oversight. When paired with Plaspy, the MT200 helps operators and owners keep closer watch over motorcycles through location visibility, alerts, and remote control options, while the device level power management supports longer term battery protection.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for motorcycles with an IP65 rated dustproof and waterproof case
- Remote fuel and power cut capability without complex wiring
- Intelligent power management to help protect motorcycle battery health
- Real time location tracking for increased asset visibility and security
- Suitable for individual owners as well as rental and fleet operations
- Rugged design intended for everyday road and off road use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Concox MT200 supplies live location and device status that Plaspy displays and uses to trigger operational workflows. Plaspy provides a unified control layer so fleet managers and owners can monitor movement, receive alerts, and act on the MT200 feature set from a single platform.

- Live location and map visualization for single vehicles or grouped fleets
- Geofence and movement alerts to notify operators of departures or entries
- Remote immobilization using the MT200 fuel and power cut feature when enabled
- Device status notifications related to power management and operational health
- Historical trip logs and simple reports for route review and operational analysis
- Fleet grouping and oversight tools to manage multiple motorcycles in one view

## Typical Use Cases

- Theft prevention and recovery monitoring for individual motorcycles
- Rental fleet tracking and remote immobilization to enforce returns
- Fleet management for delivery and courier motorcycle operations
- Asset monitoring for contractors and mobile workers using motorcycles
- Operational oversight where rugged, weather resistant tracking is required

The MT200 is a practical choice for organizations and owners who need a motorcycle focused tracker that balances ruggedness with functional control. Its IP65 enclosure and battery aware design make it appropriate for vehicles exposed to weather and variable usage patterns, while the fuel and power cut capability gives an extra layer of operational control.

Paired with Plaspy, the MT200 becomes part of a centralized monitoring and reporting solution. Plaspy brings device visibility, alerting, and reporting together so managers can act on location data and remote control features from a single platform, simplifying motorcycle fleet oversight and security.
