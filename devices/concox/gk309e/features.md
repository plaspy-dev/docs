# Concox - GK309E Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Concox GK309E and how its capabilities relate to using the device with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about location and safety functions and clarifies what users can expect when monitoring the device through the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional accessories, installation choices, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest updates, always consult official Concox documentation.

## Feature Overview

The GK309E Second Generation GPS Kids Phone by Concox is designed to combine a low cost mobile phone form factor with location tracking and child safety features. It targets parents and caregivers who need basic location awareness, emergency contact functions, and simple call control for young users.

- Real time GPS location reporting for tracking child whereabouts.
- SOS emergency function to notify predefined contacts.
- Family numbers and call whitelist to limit incoming calls to trusted contacts.
- Optional RFID support for proximity identification where supported.
- Small phone style device suitable for students and everyday use.

## Core Features of Concox - GK309E

- GPS location tracking for positional updates and monitoring.
- SOS alarm feature enabling the device user to signal for help.
- Family numbers and emergency contact configuration to prioritize trusted callers.
- Incoming call restriction to prevent calls from unknown numbers.
- Optional RFID functionality for tag based proximity or identification when provided by the vendor.
- Compact phone form factor combining basic voice connectivity and tracking.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides centralized visibility and event handling for compatible trackers including the GK309E. When the device is connected and reporting, Plaspy can surface location and event information to users for monitoring and oversight.

- GPS location updates appear on Plaspy maps and device history for route and position review.
- SOS activations are presented as events or alerts so responders and caregivers can act quickly.
- Device identity and online status are visible in Plaspy to confirm reporting and connectivity.
- Optional RFID events may be reflected in Plaspy logs if the device and configuration transmit those reports.
- Call filtering and family number settings are primarily device side and may not appear as call records in Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parents monitoring a child during school hours or commutes.
- Caregivers checking location and emergency status for young dependents.
- Managers of programs for student safety during field trips or activities.
