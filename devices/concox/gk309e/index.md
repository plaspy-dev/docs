# Concox - GK309E

![GK309E](./logo.jpg)

The GK309E Second Generation GPS Kids Phone by Concox is a compact device built to help parents keep track of their children. Combining basic cell phone functionality with GPS tracking and optional RFID features, the GK309E is positioned as a low cost and practical option for student safety and everyday child location awareness. Built with SOS and family number support, the device enables quick communication and an emergency contact path when needed.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GK309E can be added to a centralized tracking platform to improve visibility and oversight. When paired with Plaspy, the GK309E's location and alerting capabilities become part of a larger monitoring workflow, allowing caregivers or administrators to view positions, receive critical alerts, and consolidate records for reporting and operational use.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for child location awareness with GPS based positioning.
- Optional RFID support for additional proximity or access use cases.
- SOS button and family number support to enable quick contact in emergencies.
- Call restrictions to prevent calls from unknown numbers for enhanced safety.
- Low cost combination of a basic cell phone and wireless GPS tracker ideal for students.
- Practical choice for routine monitoring such as school commutes and short trips.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GK309E integrates with Plaspy by sending location updates and alert events that Plaspy collects, displays, and manages. Plaspy presents device activity in a centralized interface so caregivers and administrators can monitor movements, respond to SOS events, and keep a record of device history.

- Live location visibility in Plaspy maps for real time tracking.
- SOS and emergency alerts forwarded into Plaspy notification streams for prompt attention.
- Historical location reports and trip playback for retrospective review and reporting.
- Geofence and area based alerts to notify when a device enters or leaves designated zones.
- Device grouping and management to organize multiple GK309E units for schools or family fleets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring children during daily school commutes and after school activities.
- Supervising students on field trips and excursions.
- Managing a set of student devices for a school or childcare provider.
- Providing peace of mind for parents of young children who need simple communication and location visibility.
- Coordinating pickup and drop off logistics for small groups of students.
