# Concox - GK309E

![GK309E](./logo.jpg)

The GK309E Second Generation GPS Kids Phone by Concox is a versatile and reliable device designed to keep parents informed about their children's whereabouts. With its GPS and RFID functions \(optional\), this device ensures that parents can easily track their kids' locations. It is a perfect combination of a low-cost cell phone and a wireless GPS tracking device, making it an ideal choice for students.

One of the standout features of the GK309E is its support for SOS and family numbers. This means that parents can rest assured knowing that their children can easily reach out for help in case of an emergency. Additionally, the device prevents strangers from calling in, providing an extra layer of security for elementary students.

With the GK309E, parents can have peace of mind knowing that they can keep a close eye on their children's whereabouts. Whether it's for everyday use or during school trips, this GPS Kids Phone offers a reliable and efficient solution for parents who want to stay connected with their kids.
