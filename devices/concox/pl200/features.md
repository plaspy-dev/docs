# Concox - PL200 Features

This page covers the public feature context for using the Concox PL200 personal GPS tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the PL200 capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy deployments, including real time tracking, emergency alerts, assisted positioning, and two way voice for lone worker protection and mobile workforce management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation type, and how the manufacturer implements a given function. Use this page to understand practical capabilities in Plaspy and consult the device maker for current, device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The PL200 is a compact, IP65 rated personal tracker designed to keep field personnel visible and reachable. It emphasizes portability, multi constellation positioning, emergency alerts, and assisted fixes to improve reliability in urban and indoor fringe areas.

- Real time tracking with multi constellation GNSS support for consistent location fixes.
- SOS panic button and alerting for rapid incident notification and response.
- Two way voice and text to speech for direct communication with field personnel.
- BLE and Wi Fi assist to improve positioning reliability and allow sensor proximity workflows.
- Long run time modes and configurable power settings to extend operational endurance for multi day shifts.

## Core Features of Concox - PL200

- Multi constellation GNSS positioning (GPS BDS GLONASS) with assisted LBS and Wi Fi assist for improved fixes.
- SOS panic button for immediate alerting and location sharing.
- Two way voice communication with speaker and microphone and text to speech support.
- BLE 5.0 for sensor and beacon support and Wi Fi assist to aid location in challenging environments.
- Configurable power modes and multi day battery endurance for extended field use.
- IP65 rated compact enclosure for outdoor durability and portable deployment.
- Remote configuration via SMS and online commands and onboard data buffering for intermittent connectivity.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When registered to Plaspy, the PL200 reports position, status, and event alerts into the Plaspy platform so operations teams can monitor and respond from a single dashboard. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and ingests the device updates for live monitoring and historical review.

- Real time GNSS fixes and assisted positions appear in Plaspy for live location tracking and route playback.
- SOS and panic events are forwarded to Plaspy notification workflows for escalation and response.
- Two way voice and TTS events can be used by dispatchers and incident handlers to communicate with personnel in the field.
- BLE and Wi Fi assisted positions improve visibility in urban canyons and fringe indoor areas when reflected in Plaspy maps.
- Remote device parameters and reporting intervals can be tuned to balance battery life and update frequency for Plaspy monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases
