# Concox - PL200

![PL200](./tracker.png)

The PL200 is a compact, IP65-rated 4G personal GPS tracker designed for Plaspy-compatible deployments where reliable real-time tracking, lone-worker protection, and mobile workforce management matter. Lightweight and portable, the PL200 pairs long battery life and robust connectivity with two-way voice, SOS functionality, and multi-constellation positioning to keep field teams visible and safe through Plaspy’s dashboard and alerting ecosystem.

The PL200 connects seamlessly as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for organizations that need dependable location intelligence and fast emergency response. With BLE and Wi‑Fi assist, configurable power modes, and a suite of alerts \(SOS, low battery, geofence, SIM change\), the device is optimized for sanitation crews, street cleaners, security guards and other front-line personnel who require continuous situational awareness without sacrificing portability.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for continuous real-time tracking and rapid alerting across field teams.
- Compact, lightweight form factor \(105.0 x 62.0 x 13.0 mm; 81 g\) with IP65-rated enclosure for outdoor durability.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/BDS/GLONASS\) with assisted LBS, Wi‑Fi and BLE for robust position fixes \(better than 2.5 m CEP\).
- 4G LTE Cat 1 with GSM fallback for reliable cellular connectivity; regional LTE/GSM band support depending on model.
- Long operational endurance — up to five days on a single charge using step-count power-saving Mode 0 and configurable timing Mode 1.
- Two-way voice, speaker/microphone and text-to-speech \(TTS\) plus whitelist contacts for quick communication and emergency voice monitoring.
- Panic button \(SOS\), accelerometer-based motion awareness, and multiple event alerts for fast incident detection and response.
- BLE 5.0 and Wi‑Fi assist for proximity and sensor integration, enabling Bluetooth sensors to extend monitoring capabilities.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PL200 integrates directly with Plaspy to deliver real-time location, status updates and emergency alerts into a single operational view. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes, BLE/Wi‑Fi-assisted positions and device telemetry over the cellular link, enabling live tracking, historical playback and configurable alerts for geofence, SOS and low battery events.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via 4G \(LTE Cat 1\) with GSM fallback.
- Two-way voice and text-to-speech messages routed through Plaspy workflows for rapid communication with lone workers.
- SOS/panic alerts and geofence entry/exit events trigger Plaspy notifications and escalation procedures.
- BLE 5.0 and Wi‑Fi assist improve fix reliability in urban canyons or indoor fringe areas; BLE also allows use of Bluetooth sensors and beacons.
- Remote configuration via SMS and online commands enables device parameter tuning from Plaspy or integrated management tools.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the PL200 delivers a balanced combination of portability, durability and intelligent connectivity that suits organizations focused on safety and operational efficiency. Its proven GNSS performance and assisted positioning ensure consistent real-time tracking, while BLE and Wi‑Fi assist improve fixes in challenging environments. Remote configuration and stored-location buffering keep data flowing into Plaspy even during brief connectivity gaps, supporting accurate telemetry and historical reporting.

For teams that require anti-theft alerting, real-time telemetry and Bluetooth sensors, the PL200 offers direct compatibility with Plaspy’s alerting and reporting tools. Note that the PL200 is purpose-built as a personal tracker; for vehicle-specific telemetry like fuel monitoring, ignition monitoring or immobilizer control, Plaspy can correlate PL200 person/location data with vehicle-grade devices to provide a complete fleet management solution. Choosing the PL200 as your Plaspy compatible GPS tracker means simpler deployment, intuitive emergency handling, and reliable location intelligence tailored to field personnel and lone-worker safety programs.
