# TK-Star - TK209B Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the TK-Star TK209B GPS tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities and operational value the device brings when reporting into Plaspy for real-time tracking, alerts, and historical route storage.

Exact feature availability for a given device can vary with firmware version, hardware revision, regional module variant, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device-specific technical details, consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from TK-Star.

## Feature Overview

The TK209B is positioned as a long-duration, rugged tracker for fleet, rental, and logistics use. It combines multi‑source positioning with extended battery operation and tamper detection to deliver reliable location updates and operational alerts to Plaspy.

- Long life 10000 mAh rechargeable battery for days to weeks of standby depending on reporting settings.
- Multi‑technology positioning that includes GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, LBS and Wi‑Fi to improve locating in mixed environments.
- IP65 rated enclosure and outdoor operating range for vehicle and equipment deployments.
- Built-in vibration sensor for move and tamper detection, enabling immediate alerts.
- Regional 4G support via SIM7600 module variants to match cellular networks in different markets.
- Server-side route history retention to support audits and reporting in Plaspy.

## Core Features of TK-Star - TK209B

- Multi-source GNSS positioning including GPS, GLONASS and BeiDou for improved accuracy.
- Support for LBS and Wi‑Fi positioning to assist locating in challenging or semi-indoor environments.
- High capacity 10000 mAh rechargeable battery with reported standby operation measured in days to weeks depending on configuration.
- Rugged IP65 water resistant housing suitable for outdoor asset and vehicle use.
- Integrated vibration sensor for movement and tamper detection and related alerts.
- Regional SIM7600 4G cellular module variants to provide cellular compatibility across regions.
- UBLOX GNSS chipset performance with typical accuracy around 5 m and documented time-to-first-fix characteristics.
- Historical route storage on the server for operational reporting and audits.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the TK209B location and telemetry over the cellular connection and presents that data for live monitoring, alerts, and historical review. Plaspy reconciles multi‑source positioning and surfaces operational events so fleets and asset teams can act quickly.

- Live location on maps combining GNSS, LBS and Wi‑Fi inputs for improved situational awareness.
- Geofence, movement and overspeed alerting routed through Plaspy for immediate notifications to teams.
- Battery and movement telemetry visible in Plaspy to support maintenance and recovery workflows.
- Historical routes and event logs retained in Plaspy for post‑event analysis and compliance reporting.
- Support for additional I/O or integration inputs where deployments provide them, allowing Plaspy to combine TK209B telemetry with external signals.
- Alerts and notifications delivered through supported Plaspy channels such as SMS and mobile notifications as configured.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking and route monitoring for operational efficiency and compliance.
- Rental vehicle and equipment monitoring where long battery life and geofence alerts reduce loss and misuse.
- Logistics and cargo location in terminals and yards using combined GNSS, LBS and Wi‑Fi positioning.
- Anti‑theft and tamper detection for parked or stored assets using vibration sensor alerts.
- Remote asset monitoring with intermittent reporting schedules to extend battery life.
- Seasonal or temporary site deployments where IP65 protection and long standby are required.
