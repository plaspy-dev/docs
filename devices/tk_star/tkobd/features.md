# TK-Star - TKOBD Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TK-Star TKOBD GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing functions such as real time tracking, alerts, and server side route history so fleet operators and vehicle owners can understand practical value when the device is connected to the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. This document uses the TKOBD description as its factual basis and highlights how those documented capabilities typically appear inside Plaspy, while recommending verification against the manufacturer documentation for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The TKOBD is an OBD plug‑in GPS tracker designed for quick installation and continuous vehicle monitoring. It combines multi‑source positioning with vehicle power from the OBD port and provides server retained history and configurable alerts that integrate into Plaspy for operational visibility and incident review.

- Quick OBD plug‑in installation for rapid fleet rollout and minimal installation work.
- Multi‑source positioning including GPS, GLONASS, BDS, LBS and Wi‑Fi to improve location reporting both outdoors and in challenging reception areas.
- Real time tracking plus configurable alerts for vibration, movement, geo‑fence breach, and overspeed routed into Plaspy.
- Server side route history stored for up to six months to support audit, compliance, and incident reconstruction.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and automotive operating range for everyday vehicle use and durability.

## Core Features of TK-Star - TKOBD

- OBD plug‑in form factor that draws vehicle power and simplifies installation without hardwiring.
- Multi‑source GNSS positioning combined with LBS and Wi‑Fi assisted location for better coverage.
- Real time location reporting suitable for live tracking and dispatch workflows.
- Configurable safety and security alarms including vibration alarm, move alert, geo‑fence breach, and overspeed alarm.
- Server side route history retention up to six months for retrospective review.
- IP65 ruggedized enclosure and automotive operating voltage compatibility to withstand vehicle environments.
- Internal backup battery to preserve short term reporting when vehicle power is interrupted.
- UBLOX GNSS chipset based positioning as documented by the manufacturer.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a TKOBD is connected and reporting, Plaspy ingests the device data and presents location, alerts, and history in the platform to support monitoring and operational response. Plaspy is designed to detect common tracker protocols and display incoming telemetry without requiring complex per device configuration.

- Live positions displayed on Plaspy maps for real time vehicle visibility and dispatch.
- Alerts such as vibration, move, geo‑fence breach, and overspeed routed into Plaspy notifications and event timelines.
- Server retained route history available in Plaspy for up to six months for trip review and compliance reporting.
- OBD plug‑in power simplifies deployment and reduces installation time when adding devices into Plaspy managed fleets.
- Consolidated view of device status, recent trips, and event logs in Plaspy to support operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and routing oversight to monitor vehicle location and optimize dispatch.
- Anti‑theft and recovery workflows using vibration and move alerts to notify operators of unauthorized movement.
- Rental and shared vehicle programs that benefit from quick OBD installation and retained trip history for audits.
- Driver behavior monitoring and compliance tracking leveraging overspeed and geo‑fence events.
- Incident investigation and proof of route history for insurance or operational review.
- Scalable rollouts across cars and light commercial vehicles where minimal installation time is a priority.
