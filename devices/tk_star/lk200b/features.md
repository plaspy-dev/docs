# TK-Star - LK200B Features

This page provides a public overview of the TK-Star LK200B GPS vehicle tracker and how its user-facing features are used when the device is connected to Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy for monitoring, location awareness, and event visibility without delving into private configuration specifics.

Exact feature availability and behavior for the LK200B can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation method. Where appropriate, the content below describes the typical, manufacturer-described functions and how those translate into operational value inside Plaspy, while encouraging verification against official TK-Star resources.

## Feature Overview

The LK200B is presented as a real-time vehicle tracker that combines satellite positioning with cellular reporting to provide continuous location updates, historical route review, and event notifications. In Plaspy, these device capabilities help teams maintain situational awareness of single vehicles or fleets and surface notable events for operators.

- Real time tracking to view current vehicle position and heading on a map
- Auto tracking with configurable update intervals for continuous monitoring
- Blind area tracking to capture movement and reconcile gaps when GPS signals are intermittent
- History trace checking for reviewing past routes and trip completion
- Geo fence support for virtual boundary alerts when a vehicle enters or exits defined zones
- Movement, overspeed, shaking, and low battery alerts to notify operators of important events

## Core Features of TK-Star - LK200B

- Real time GPS location reporting to enable up to the minute position awareness
- Auto tracking intervals that let the device send periodic updates for route continuity
- Blind area tracking to capture and report movement through areas with weak satellite reception
- History trace or route playback for post trip review and auditing
- Geo fence detection to trigger enter and exit notifications for defined zones
- Movement alert that signals when the device detects motion
- Overspeed alert to indicate when a vehicle exceeds a configured speed threshold
- Low battery alert to warn of reduced device power levels

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the LK200B's location updates and event messages to present them in maps, timelines, and alert streams. The platform is designed to surface the most relevant information from supported trackers so operators can act or investigate efficiently.

- Location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring and location history
- Periodic auto tracking updates build continuous traces visible in trip and history views
- Geo fence events are reflected in Plaspy alerts so administrators can see entries or exits
- Movement, overspeed, shaking, and low battery alerts are shown in the event feed and can be used to trigger notifications or workflows
- History trace checking becomes searchable trip data within Plaspy for review and reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring private vehicles and reviewing trips for personal security or convenience
- Tracking rental cars or shared vehicles to verify itineraries and returns
- Fleet oversight for route verification and operational planning
- Using geo fence alerts to manage access to facilities or sensitive zones
- Detecting potential misuse or unusual motion patterns through movement and shaking alerts
- Managing maintenance windows by tracking vehicle usage and low battery notifications

- Hardware revisions and regional variants may include slightly different sensor sets or feature availability
- Installation choices and vehicle wiring can change which signals the tracker can detect or report

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Connecting the LK200B to Plaspy gives operators a consolidated view of location, route history, and event signals from the tracker. Plaspy organizes device reports into maps, timelines, and alerts so teams can monitor assets continuously and respond to events such as unauthorized movement, overspeeding, or geo fence breaches.
