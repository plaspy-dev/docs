# TK-Star - XE108

![XE108](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star XE108 is a GPS portable tracker that offers a wide range of applications and features to ensure the safety and security of your assets. Whether you need to track your private car, rental vehicle, equipment, or even protect your loved ones such as children, the elderly, or pets, the XE108 is designed to provide peace of mind. It is also an excellent tool for businessmen to manage their field staff or track criminals discreetly. Additionally, it can be used to manage individuals who are on bail.

One of the standout features of the TK-Star XE108 is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the location of your assets or loved ones in real-time. It also offers auto-tracking, blind area tracking, and GPS+GSM tracking, ensuring that you have accurate and reliable location information at all times. The XE108 also provides a history-trace checking feature, allowing you to review the movement history of the tracker.

Other notable features of the TK-Star XE108 include geo-fencing, which allows you to set up virtual boundaries and receive alerts when the tracker enters or exits those boundaries. It also offers movement alerts, overspeed alerts, low battery alerts, and shaking sensor alerts, ensuring that you are notified of any unusual activity or events. The XE108 also has an SOS button for emergency situations and a sleeping working mode to conserve battery life.
