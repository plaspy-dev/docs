# TK-Star - XE108

<div className="device-logo">
![XE108](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The TK-Star XE108 is a portable GPS tracker designed to provide location visibility and basic tracking features for a wide range of assets. Based on its description, the XE108 is positioned for tracking private and rental vehicles, equipment, and people including children, the elderly, and pets. It offers features intended to increase security and situational awareness such as real time tracking, history trace review, geo-fencing, movement and overspeed alerts, SOS notification, and a sleep mode to conserve power.

As a model that is compatible with Plaspy, the XE108 can integrate into a centralized tracking platform to extend its practical value. When connected to Plaspy, the XE108's location updates, history traces, and event alerts become part of a broader monitoring and reporting environment, making it easier for operators and organizations to manage assets, supervise field staff, and respond to incidents from a single dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- Portable form factor suitable for vehicles, equipment, and personal tracking.
- Real time tracking and history trace features for live monitoring and reviewed movement.
- Geo-fencing capability to create virtual boundaries and receive entry or exit alerts.
- Multiple alert types including movement, overspeed, low battery, shaking sensor, and SOS notifications.
- Sleep mode to reduce power use during periods of inactivity.
- Useful for discreet or sensitive tracking needs such as personnel management or security oversight.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the XE108 feeds location and event information into a managed fleet and asset monitoring environment. Plaspy consolidates the tracker data so teams can monitor devices, receive alerts, and analyze movements without switching between tools.

- Display live location of XE108 units on Plaspy maps for centralized situational awareness.
- Receive and manage alerts from the tracker such as geo-fence breaches, movement alerts, and SOS events through Plaspy notifications.
- Review historical routes and location history within Plaspy to support audits, investigations, or operational analysis.
- Group and monitor multiple XE108 devices for fleet oversight or asset tracking across sites.
- Use Plaspy reporting and export features to summarize device activity and support operational decisions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring private cars and rental vehicles for location and security oversight.
- Tracking portable equipment to reduce loss and improve asset utilization.
- Protecting vulnerable individuals such as children or elderly family members.
- Managing field staff movements and attendance for small teams or mobile workers.
- Discreet location monitoring in investigations or supervised release scenarios.
