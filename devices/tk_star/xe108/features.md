# TK-Star - XE108 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the TK-Star XE108 portable GPS tracker with the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the XE108 capabilities that matter to fleet operators, asset managers, and individual users when the device is connected to Plaspy for location monitoring, alerts, and history review.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, how the unit is installed, and the manufacturer implementation. For authoritative device details and the latest firmware notes consult the official TK-Star documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The XE108 is a compact portable GPS tracker designed to provide continuous location awareness and event alerts for assets and people. When integrated with Plaspy it can provide live position updates, historical route playback, and event notifications that help with oversight and incident response.

- Real time location reporting to support live tracking and location checks
- Automatic tracking modes and blind area tracking for persistent coverage
- GPS plus cellular positioning to maintain location when available
- History trace checking for route review and activity reconstruction
- Geo fencing to define virtual boundaries and receive boundary alerts
- Alerts for movement, overspeed, low battery, shaking events, and SOS

## Core Features of TK-Star - XE108

- Real time tracking that reports device position for live monitoring
- Auto tracking and blind area tracking to reduce missed position updates
- GPS and GSM based location reporting to improve availability of fixes
- History trace checking to review previous movements and routes
- Geo fencing support to receive notifications when a device crosses set boundaries
- Movement and shaking sensor alerts to flag unexpected activity
- Overspeed alerts and low battery notifications for operational awareness
- SOS button for emergency signaling and a sleeping mode to conserve power

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes the XE108 location and event data so you can view positions, alerts, and history in a single interface. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and presents received data on maps and in device timelines without manual parsing of messages.

- Live positions displayed on the Plaspy map for immediate situational awareness
- Historical route playback and timeline events for review and auditing
- Geo fence events and movement alerts surfaced in the Plaspy event stream
- Status indicators such as battery and motion reported as device attributes
- Configurable notifications in Plaspy to forward alerts to operators or systems

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal vehicle tracking for location monitoring and theft deterrence
- Rental fleet oversight to review usage, movement history, and events
- Portable equipment tracking where intermittent placement and retrieval occurs
- Keeping track of family members such as children, elderly relatives, or pets
- Field staff management for remote teams requiring location visibility
- Discreet tracking in investigative or security scenarios where permitted

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior can change depending on device firmware version and manufacturer updates
- Hardware revisions and regional variants of the XE108 may alter supported functions
- Installation and mounting location can affect GPS reception and blind area tracking

Using the TK-Star XE108 with Plaspy gives organizations a straightforward way to centralize location data and event visibility across many devices. Plaspy’s platform presents live positions, historical traces, and alert streams together so operations teams can monitor assets and respond to incidents with context and continuity.
