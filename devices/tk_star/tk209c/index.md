# TK-Star - TK209C

<div className="device-logo">
![TK209C](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK209C is a heavy duty, long life GPS tracker designed for demanding vehicle and asset tracking applications. Built around a UBLOX GNSS module, the device combines GPS, BeiDou and GLONASS with LBS and Wi Fi positioning to provide reliable outdoor accuracy and improved indoor or multi mode location reporting. Its IP65 rated enclosure and built in vibration sensor make it suitable for outdoor use on vehicles, containers and temporary equipment where movement and anti theft monitoring are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK209C can be integrated into Plaspy’s tracking platform to deliver map based visibility, alerts and route history. Its extended battery capacity and multiple power options support long standby intervals and reduce maintenance for deployments where continuous mains power is not guaranteed, making it a practical option for fleet operators, rental services and remote asset managers using Plaspy for centralized monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Multi mode positioning with UBLOX GNSS plus LBS and Wi Fi to improve indoors and mixed environment tracking
- Very long autonomy from a high capacity rechargeable battery to reduce frequent recharges and service visits
- Rugged IP65 enclosure for outdoor and vehicle mount deployments under typical operating conditions
- Built in vibration sensor and movement alerts to support anti theft detection and motion based notifications
- Flexible charging and power options for varied deployment scenarios on vehicles or temporary assets
- Server side route history retention for retrospective playback and compliance reporting over months

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK209C reports location and event data that Plaspy translates into live map views, configurable alerts and historical analysis. The platform ingests the tracker’s multi mode positioning and motion events to provide operational teams with situational awareness and tools for oversight.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for fleet monitoring and asset tracking
- Geofence alerts triggered in Plaspy when the tracker enters or leaves predefined zones
- Motion and vibration notifications delivered to Plaspy for rapid awareness of movement or tampering
- Route playback and historical traces stored and accessible through Plaspy for audits and analysis
- Correlation of TK209C position and event data with other vehicle signals in Plaspy when those inputs are available

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for vehicles that require long standby and reliable tracking during long deployments
- Anti theft monitoring of parked or stored vehicles and containers using vibration and movement alerts
- Cargo and container oversight where durability and extended battery life reduce onsite maintenance
- Rental vehicle tracking to document location and movement while vehicles are in and out of service
- Remote equipment monitoring where mains power is intermittent or unavailable

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK209C is a practical choice for organizations that need rugged, long endurance tracking combined with multi mode positioning. Its design balances durability, autonomy and movement sensing, which complements Plaspy’s capabilities for centralized tracking, geofencing and historical reporting. For teams that manage distributed assets or mixed fleets, pairing the TK209C with Plaspy reduces the operational burden of frequent maintenance while keeping location and event data available for analysis.
