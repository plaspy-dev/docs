# TK209C GPS Tracker

The TK209C is a heavy-duty, long-life GPS tracker engineered for demanding vehicle and asset tracking applications and is Plaspy compatible for straightforward platform integration. Built around a UBLOX GNSS module, the TK209C combines GPS, BeiDou \(BD\), GLONASS, LBS and Wi‑Fi positioning to deliver accurate outdoor tracking and improved indoor/multi-mode positioning for rental fleets, cargo containers and remote equipment.

The IP65-rated enclosure and a sensitive built-in vibration sensor make the TK209C ideal for anti-theft monitoring and movement alerts. With an industry-leading 3.7V 20000 mAh rechargeable battery and multiple power options, the TK209C is purpose-built for long standby intervals and reliable real-time tracking when paired with the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: integrates with Plaspy for real-time tracking, alerts and route playback.
- Long autonomy: 3.7V 20000 mAh Li‑ion battery with up to 150 days standby, reducing maintenance and charging cycles.
- Multi-mode positioning: UBLOX GNSS with GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS plus LBS and Wi‑Fi for better indoor/outdoor coverage and ~5 m GPS accuracy.
- Rugged design: IP65-rated enclosure tolerates outdoor use on vehicles, containers and equipment \(operating -20°C to +55°C\).
- Advanced motion detection: sensitive built-in vibration sensor, move and overspeed alerts to support fleet management and anti-theft workflows.
- Flexible power options: 12–24V car charger input \(5V output\) and 110–220V wall charging for versatile deployment.
- Historical data: server stores route history for up to six months, enabling compliance reporting and route playback on mobile app and web.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK209C sends frequent location and event data that the platform uses for real-time tracking, geofencing, alerting and historical analysis. Plaspy ingests multi-mode GNSS, LBS and Wi‑Fi signals reported by the device and translates them into map views, telemetry dashboards and configurable notifications for operations teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GSM/GPRS to Plaspy for map-based tracking and fleet monitoring.
- Geo-fencing events: enter/exit alerts when the tracker crosses predefined zones set in Plaspy.
- Motion and vibration alerts: immediate notifications for slight shocks, movement beyond set distance, and overspeed incidents.
- Route playback: up to six months of stored route history available on Plaspy’s mobile app and web portal for audits and driver behavior analysis.
- Complementary telemetry: Plaspy can correlate TK209C position and movement data with other vehicle telemetry \(ignition, fuel or immobilizer signals\) when those inputs are available from the vehicle or additional interfaces.
