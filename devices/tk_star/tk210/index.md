# TK-Star - TK210

<div className="device-logo">
![TK210](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK210 is a compact 4G Android motorcycle GPS tracker built for motorcycles, electric vehicles, scooters, private cars and rental fleets. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with cellular 4G connectivity and indoor positioning methods such as LBS and Wi‑Fi to provide dependable location information whether a vehicle is on the street or indoors. The device is designed with practical anti theft and fleet management features like a vibration sensor, geo fence alerts, movement and overspeed notifications, and a remote engine cut off for immobilization.

As a Plaspy compatible device by design, the TK210 can feed real time location and event data into Plaspy’s platform to support monitoring, alerts and historical route review. That compatibility makes the TK210 a straightforward option for fleet managers and vehicle owners who want centralized visibility, automated notifications and retained route history to improve recovery times, reduce losses and simplify day to day fleet oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G tracker for real time visibility of motorcycles, scooters, EVs and rental vehicles.
- Multi constellation GNSS plus LBS and Wi‑Fi for improved indoor and urban coverage.
- Built in vibration sensor with movement and overspeed alerts for proactive anti theft protection.
- Geo fence alerting and event reporting to enforce zones and rental boundaries.
- Remote engine cut off immobilizer capability to stop and resume vehicle operation when authorized.
- Historical route data retention for retrospective review and operational reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK210 transmits location fixes and onboard events over cellular networks and assisted location channels to the Plaspy platform. Plaspy aggregates that telemetry into live maps, alert streams and stored route histories so teams can monitor vehicles in real time and review past activity for audits or investigations.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered into Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- Movement, vibration and overspeed alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notifications and workflow triggers.
- Geo fence enter and exit events sent to Plaspy to automate perimeter alerts and rental zone enforcement.
- Remote engine cut off commands coordinated through Plaspy for authorized immobilization actions.
- Historical route data accessible in Plaspy for reporting, auditing and dispute resolution.
- Event driven workflows in Plaspy help translate tracker alerts into operational tasks and notifications.
