# TK-Star - TK920

![TK920](./tracker.jpg)

The TK920 is a heavy duty 4G GPS tracker designed for long term vehicle and asset monitoring. It combines a rugged IP65 enclosure with a large 10000 mAh rechargeable battery and multi constellation positioning including GPS, BeiDou and GLONASS plus LBS and Wi Fi to provide dependable location data in demanding field conditions. The device is described as suitable for private cars, rental fleets, containers, construction equipment and other remote assets where endurance and environmental protection matter.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK920 can be integrated into Plaspy for real time tracking, fleet monitoring and security workflows. Its extended standby life and comprehensive alert set make it a practical choice for operations that need long term visibility and actionable notifications. When connected to Plaspy, location data, movement events and alarms are consolidated into dashboards, reports and notifications for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Heavy duty design with IP65 rated enclosure for protection in wet and dusty environments
- Large 10000 mAh rechargeable battery providing extended standby for long term deployments
- Multi constellation positioning plus LBS and Wi Fi to improve location reliability in varied environments
- Comprehensive alert set including vibration detection, geo fencing, move start and over speed alarms
- Real time tracking capability suitable for vehicle and asset monitoring in mobile operations
- Historical route storage viewable through server access for audit and trip review
- Flexible charging options for vehicle charging and wall charging to support continuous operation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK920 sends location points, movement events and alarm notifications into the Plaspy platform where they are presented in maps, dashboards and reports for monitoring and analysis.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy for monitoring, routing and operational visibility
- Movement and vibration alerts forwarded to Plaspy to support anti theft workflows and tamper detection
- Geo fence and over speed alarms available in Plaspy as triggers for notifications and escalation
- Historical route data accessible in Plaspy for trip replay, audits and compliance reporting
- Battery and charging status visible in Plaspy to help schedule maintenance and reduce downtime

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for rental and commercial vehicle fleets requiring long battery life and dependable tracking
- Anti theft protection for parked vehicles and containers using vibration detection and move alarms
- Remote equipment and container tracking at worksites or during transit where rugged protection is needed
- Long term asset monitoring for seasonal or sporadically used equipment that benefits from extended standby
- Operations that need centralized visibility and historical route access for compliance or analysis

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK920 pairs endurance and ruggedness with multi constellation positioning to deliver practical location visibility for field operations. Its large battery and extended standby capability reduce maintenance frequency, while the built in alert features provide actionable events that feed directly into Plaspy for faster operational response. Plaspy consolidates the tracker feeds into a single platform for mapping, notifications and reporting so teams can manage assets and fleets from one place.

For organizations that prioritize long term deployments and clear anti theft visibility, the TK920 is a relevant Plaspy compatible option.
