# TK-Star - TK910 4G Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TK-Star TK910 4G GPS tracker and how its capabilities map into Plaspy for tracking, alerting, and historical playback. It focuses on the practical, non-sensitive aspects of the device that are relevant to Plaspy deployments and fleet management visibility.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device specific details and supported variants, consult the official TK-Star documentation referenced at the end of this page.

## Feature Overview

The TK910 4G is a compact vehicle tracker designed for motorcycles, private cars, rental fleets and light equipment. It combines multi constellation GNSS with network assisted positioning and broad cellular options to deliver reliable real time location and event telemetry that Plaspy can ingest and present.

- Continuous real time tracking with GNSS plus LBS and Wi Fi assisted positioning for improved indoor and urban coverage.
- Anti theft and security alerts including vibration detection, movement and overspeed notifications and remote engine cut off for immobilization.
- Broad cellular connectivity across 4G LTE with WCDMA and GSM fallback plus NB IoT and Cat M1 support for diverse regional coverage.
- Internal backup battery to maintain critical telemetry and last known location reporting after vehicle power loss.
- Server side historical route playback and event history retention suitable for audits and fleet reporting.

## Core Features of TK-Star - TK910 4G

- Multi constellation GNSS support including GPS GLONASS and BeiDou for robust satellite positioning.
- Assisted positioning via LBS and Wi Fi to improve indoor and dense urban location fixes.
- Real time location streaming and event telemetry suitable for live monitoring in Plaspy.
- Geofence alerts and overspeed or movement notifications for automated monitoring workflows.
- Vibration detection to signal potential tampering or theft attempts.
- Remote engine cut off control for immobilization via oil supply control as reported by the manufacturer.
- Broad connectivity options with 4G LTE and fallback to WCDMA and GSM plus NB IoT and Cat M1 where available.
- Internal backup battery to preserve critical reporting for a limited period after primary power loss.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TK910 4G is connected to Plaspy, location and event data flow into the platform for visualization, alerting and historical analysis. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and presents incoming telemetry for operational use and reporting.

- Live location and position updates in Plaspy using GNSS plus assisted LBS and Wi Fi where available for better continuity indoors.
- Geofence breach, overspeed and vibration alerts delivered to Plaspy for immediate notification and operator workflows.
- Remote immobilizer actions reported through Plaspy so operators can see immobilization events and resume commands when supported by deployment setup.
- Historical route playback and event timelines available in web and mobile interfaces for audits and incident review with server side retention.
- Plaspy can correlate tracker telemetry with other available data sources in a deployment to enrich operational context when those external inputs exist.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle and scooter anti theft monitoring using vibration detection and remote engine cut off to reduce theft risk.
- Rental vehicle tracking with geofence and overspeed alerts to enforce rental terms and protect assets.
- Company car and light truck fleet management for dispatching, route oversight and compliance reporting.
- Light equipment and small vehicle tracking where compact form factor and wide voltage range are beneficial.
- Incident investigation and billing reconciliation using server side historical routes and event logs.

- Regional device variants may differ in cellular band coverage and the availability of NB IoT or Cat M1 support.
- Installation method and wiring determine which functions are available on a given unit for immobilization and persistent power monitoring.
- Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols and accepts telemetry from devices configured to send data to Plaspy infrastructure such as d.plaspy.com using TCP or UDP on the platform port.
