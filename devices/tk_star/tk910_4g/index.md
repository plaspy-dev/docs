# TK-Star - TK910 4G

![TK910 4G](./tracker.jpg)

The TK910 4G is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, private cars, rental fleets and light equipment. It combines multi-constellation GNSS positioning with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance to improve outdoor coverage and indoor positioning. The device offers real-time tracking, vibration detection, geofence alerts and a remote engine cut-off function to support anti-theft workflows and faster incident response.

As a Plaspy compatible device by design, the TK910 4G is suitable for deployments that need dependable telemetry feeding into a centralized fleet management platform. Its connectivity options and standard telemetry flows make it practical for businesses that want live location visibility, alerting and historical route playback within Plaspy for operational oversight and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suited to motorcycles, scooters, private cars and light equipment.
- Multi constellation GNSS with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance for improved urban and indoor positioning.
- Real time tracking with geofence alerts, vibration detection and remote engine cut off for anti theft response.
- Broad cellular coverage including 4G support with fallback options and low power wide area technologies where available.
- Internal backup battery preserves critical location reporting for short periods after power loss.
- Server side historical route playback and web mobile access for audits and operational review.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK910 4G streams location and event telemetry to the platform so operators can monitor assets, respond to alerts and analyze historical activity. Plaspy ingests the device data to present live positions, trigger notifications and retain route history for reporting and compliance.

- Live location updates and route visualization for ongoing fleet awareness.
- Geofence, overspeed and movement alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and operator action.
- Remote immobilizer control enabling engine cut off and resume actions through Plaspy workflows for theft mitigation.
- Historical route playback on web and mobile for incident investigation and audits.
- Correlation of the TK910 feed with other platform data to enhance operational insight and reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle and scooter anti theft monitoring with vibration alarms and remote immobilization.
- Rental vehicle tracking to enforce policies and protect fleet assets.
- Company car and light truck fleet management for dispatching and route analysis.
- Light equipment and small EV monitoring where compact size and broad voltage support matter.
- Historical route audits and incident investigations for billing reconciliation and compliance.

The TK910 4G is a practical option for organizations and individuals seeking a compact, Plaspy compatible tracker that balances positioning accuracy, coverage and anti theft features.
