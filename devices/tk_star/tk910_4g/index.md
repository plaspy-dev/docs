# TK-Star - TK910 4G

![TK910 4G](./tracker.jpg)

The TK910 4G is a compact vehicle GPS tracker purpose-built for motorcycles, private cars, rental fleets and light equipment. Plaspy compatible by design, this unit pairs multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou\) with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance to provide reliable outdoor and improved indoor positioning. For fleet management and personal vehicle protection, the TK910 4G delivers real-time tracking, geofence alerts, vibration detection and remote engine cut-off to help prevent theft and speed incident response.

Designed to integrate easily with platform providers, the TK910 4G supports standard telemetry flows and web/mobile playback — making it a practical choice for businesses that need dependable tracking data in Plaspy-compatible deployments. With broad 4G coverage plus fallback to WCDMA/GSM and support for NB‑IoT/Cat‑M1, the unit balances low-latency real-time tracking and long-term asset visibility across diverse regions.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker providing continuous real-time tracking for vehicles, motorcycles and light equipment.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou\) with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance for improved indoor and urban positioning.
- Anti-theft controls including vibration alarm, move/overspeed alerts and remote engine cut-off \(oil supply\) for immobilization.
- Broad cellular connectivity: 4G LTE regional bandsets with WCDMA/GSM fallback, plus NB‑IoT and Cat‑M1 support where available.
- Compact, lightweight form factor \(90.6 × 57 × 20 mm, ~68 g\) suitable for motorcycles, scooters and vehicle installations.
- Backup battery \(140 mAh\) keeps critical telemetry and last-known location reporting for up to ~2 hours after power loss.
- Server-side historical route playback and mobile/web access with data retention up to six months for audits and fleet reports.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK910 4G streams location and event telemetry to the platform for immediate monitoring, alerts and reporting. Plaspy-compatible ingestion takes advantage of the device’s GNSS accuracy and assisted positioning to present live vehicle location, event history and notifications in dashboards or mobile apps. Alerts such as geofence breaches, overspeed and vibration-triggered movement are pushed to operators or drivers to support rapid intervention and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS + assisted LBS/Wi‑Fi\) for live tracking and route visualization.
- Geofence, overspeed and move alerts delivered to Plaspy for immediate notification and automated responses.
- Remote immobilizer capability: engine cut-off and resume \(oil supply control\) for anti-theft intervention configurable through Plaspy.
- Historical route playback on web and mobile with server data retention up to six months for audits and compliance reports.
- Integration-ready telemetry: while the TK910 4G provides core vehicle telemetry and immobilization, Plaspy can correlate additional data sources — such as external fuel monitoring, ignition signals or Bluetooth sensors — when those inputs are available in a deployment.
