# TK-Star - TK720

![TK720](./tracker.jpg)

The TK720 relay GPS tracker is a compact, lightweight tracking solution ideal for motorcycles, cars, rental vehicles and light equipment. Built around a UBLOX GNSS receiver and multi‑positioning methods \(GPS, Beidou, GLONASS, LBS and Wi‑Fi\), the TK720 delivers reliable outdoor and indoor location data and integrates seamlessly with Plaspy for Plaspy compatible, real‑time tracking and fleet management.

The TK720 provides a focused set of security and management features — sensitive vibration detection, geo‑fencing, move and over‑speed alerts, plus remote engine cut‑off/immobilizer control — while storing historical route data on the server for approximately six months. When paired with Plaspy, fleet managers and vehicle owners get immediate alerts, route playback and telemetry reporting that supports anti‑theft workflows and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real‑time tracking via SMS, mobile app and web platform for live fleet visibility.
- Multi‑GNSS positioning \(GPS/Beidou/GLONASS\) plus LBS and Wi‑Fi for dependable outdoor and indoor location.
- Sensitive built‑in vibration sensor produces instant alerts on even slight impacts for anti‑theft response.
- Geo‑fencing, move alerts and over‑speed notifications to enforce routes, zones and driver behavior policies.
- Remote engine cut‑off and resume \(relay control\) for vehicle immobilization and recovery operations.
- Compact, lightweight form factor designed for discreet installation on motorcycles, scooters and light vehicles.
- Server‑side historical route storage \(approx. six months\) for audits, telemetry review and trip analysis.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed in a vehicle or asset, the TK720 periodically reports position and key event data to Plaspy over GSM/GPRS. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s location and alert telemetry and presents it in dashboards, maps and reports suitable for real‑time tracking and fleet management workflows. Alerts and commands travel between the device and Plaspy so authorized users can monitor status, review history and take remote action when needed.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Vibration/impact alerts, over‑speed and move alerts are forwarded instantly to Plaspy for notifications and escalation.
- Geo‑fencing events trigger Plaspy notifications and automated workflows when a vehicle enters or leaves predefined zones.
- Remote immobilizer/engine cut‑off and resume control routed through Plaspy for secure recovery or vehicle disablement.
- Historical route playback on Plaspy using up to approximately six months of stored server data for audits and driver behavior analysis.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK720 is designed for operators who need dependable, Plaspy compatible real‑time tracking in a compact package. Its multi‑GNSS approach \(GPS, Beidou, GLONASS\) together with LBS and Wi‑Fi provides strong positioning performance both outdoors and in challenging urban or indoor environments. Security features — a sensitive vibration sensor, geo‑fencing, move and over‑speed alerts — work hand‑in‑hand with Plaspy to deliver timely notifications and evidence for incident response.

For fleet management and telemetry needs, the TK720 provides server‑side historical storage and immediate event forwarding to Plaspy, so teams can analyze trips, monitor driving behavior and implement anti‑theft procedures. Remote engine cut‑off \(immobilizer control\) gives authorized users precise control when recovery is required. While the device focuses on core tracking and security, it integrates into Plaspy’s broader ecosystem and can be combined with additional telemetry or fuel monitoring systems and external Bluetooth sensors supported by the platform to create a fuller picture of vehicle health and usage.
