# TK905 4G GPS Tracker \(TKSTAR\)

The TK905 is a rugged, long-endurance 4G GPS tracker designed for reliable vehicle tracking and asset monitoring. Built for cars, rental fleets, cargo containers and outdoor equipment, the TK905 combines multi-mode positioning \(GPS + LBS + WiFi + BeiDou + GLONASS\) with a 5000 mAh battery and IP65 water resistance to deliver continuous, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and dependable anti-theft alerts.

As a device that integrates easily into online platforms, the TK905 provides the core telemetry and location data Plaspy needs for fleet management, route history, geo-fencing and movement alerts. With long standby life, vibration-triggered notifications and multi-constellation GNSS performance, it is a practical choice for managers who require accurate location, robust reporting and secure remote monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with real-time tracking for vehicles, fleets and assets.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS + LBS + WiFi + BeiDou + GLONASS for improved coverage indoor and outdoor.
- Long 3.7 V 5000 mAh Li-ion battery offering up to 30 days standby for long-term deployments.
- IP65-rated enclosure and built-in sensitive vibration sensor for anti-theft movement alerts.
- Comprehensive alerts: geo-fencing, overspeed, move/vibration alerts and historical route storage \(server stores up to six months\).
- Wide cellular support: 4G/LTE, WCDMA and GSM bands for global connectivity and reliable telemetry uplink.
- Compact and lightweight form factor tailored for vehicle and container mounting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK905 streams location and event data to the platform so you can monitor assets in real time, receive instant alerts and generate operational reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s GNSS fixes, network-based positions \(LBS/WiFi\), movement and alert flags and presents them on maps, dashboards and automated reports used in fleet management and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and mapping.
- Geo-fence alerts and overspeed notifications forwarded to Plaspy for instant operator alerts.
- Historical route storage \(server retains route data up to six months\) accessible via Plaspy web/app.
- Move and vibration alerts appear as events on Plaspy so anti-theft responses can be triggered quickly.
- Plaspy supports extended telemetry such as ignition and immobilizer state, fuel monitoring and Bluetooth sensors where the installation provides those inputs or when paired hardware is used; the TK905 supplies the core location and movement data Plaspy requires for full fleet management workflows.

The TK905 pairs practical hardware strength with Plaspy’s platform capabilities to deliver an effective GPS tracker solution for businesses and operators. Its multi-constellation positioning and LBS/WiFi support increase location reliability in challenging environments; the sizable 5000 mAh battery and IP65 rating make it suitable for long-term field use.
