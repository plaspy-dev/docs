# TK-Star - TKSTAR

![TKSTAR](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star TKSTAR GPS Portable Tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities. Whether you need to track your private car, rental vehicle, or equipment, or you want to ensure the safety of your loved ones such as children, the elderly, or pets, this GPS tracker has got you covered. It can also provide peace of mind for businessmen who need to manage their field staff or track criminals discreetly. Additionally, it can be used to manage individuals who are on bail.

With a range of outstanding features, the TK-Star TKSTAR GPS Portable Tracker offers a comprehensive solution for all your tracking needs. It supports real-time tracking, auto-tracking, and blind area tracking, ensuring that you always have accurate and up-to-date information about the location of your assets. The device utilizes both GPS and GSM tracking technologies, providing reliable and accurate positioning data. You can easily check the history trace of the tracker, set up geo-fences to receive alerts when the tracker enters or exits a specific area, and receive movement, overspeed, and low battery alerts.

Other notable features of the TK-Star TKSTAR GPS Portable Tracker include remote monitoring, shaking sensor alert, SOS button for emergencies, sleeping working mode to conserve battery life, and a waterproof rating of IP66, making it suitable for use in various environments. With its compact and portable design, this GPS tracker is easy to carry and install, ensuring that you can track and monitor your assets with ease. Whether you need to track your vehicles, loved ones, or valuable equipment, the TK-Star TKSTAR GPS Portable Tracker is a reliable and efficient solution.
