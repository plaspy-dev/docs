# TK-Star - LK106

<div className="device-logo">
![LK106](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The TK-Star LK106 is a portable GPS tracker designed to provide location awareness and basic safety monitoring across a wide range of applications. It offers real-time tracking, auto-tracking, blind area tracking, and history trace checking, combined with GPS and GSM location reporting. The device also includes a suite of alert options such as geofence notifications, movement and overspeed alerts, low battery warnings, a shaking sensor alert, and an SOS button for emergency situations. With an IP66 rating and a sleep working mode, the LK106 is built for outdoor use and extended deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK106 can feed location and event information into Plaspy to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can display live positions, manage geofence rules, record movement history, and surface alerts from the tracker in a single interface. That compatibility makes the LK106 a practical choice for organizations that want a simple portable tracker paired with a fleet and asset management platform like Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Portable GPS tracker offering real time location updates and history trace checking
- GPS and GSM based positioning for broad coverage in common tracking scenarios
- Multiple alert types including geofence, movement, overspeed, low battery, shaking sensor, and SOS
- Auto tracking and blind area tracking support for continuous monitoring and intermittent coverage
- IP66 waterproof rating for outdoor and harsh environment use
- Sleep mode to conserve power during periods of inactivity

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the LK106 transmits location and event data that Plaspy ingests to provide map visualization, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy centralizes the tracker data so operators can monitor assets and people in real time and review historical movements.

- Live map view with periodic position updates from the LK106
- Geofence management and notifications based on LK106 enter and exit events
- Event alerts forwarded into Plaspy for movement, overspeed, SOS, and low battery conditions
- History playback and trace reports to review past routes and locations
- Fleet grouping and device labeling for operational monitoring and oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring private cars and rental vehicles for location and movement history
- Tracking equipment and other valuable assets in outdoor environments
- Personal safety tracking for children elderly and pets with SOS alert capability
- Field staff management and supervision of mobile teams
- Supervised monitoring where discreet location awareness and history are required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LK106 pairs practical portability with a broad set of alerting and tracking features that map cleanly to common Plaspy workflows. Its combination of real time updates history trace and geofence alerts makes it suitable for small vehicle fleets asset protection and personnel safety programs where straightforward location reporting is the primary need. The IP66 rating and sleep mode also help when devices are deployed outdoors or need longer operational life between charges.
