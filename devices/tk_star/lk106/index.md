# TK-Star - LK106

![LK106](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star LK106 is a GPS portable tracker that offers a wide range of applications and features to ensure the safety and security of your assets. Whether you need to track your private car, rental vehicle, equipment, or even protect your loved ones such as children, the elderly, or pets, the LK106 is the perfect solution. It provides peace-of-mind for businessmen who need to manage their field staff or track criminals discreetly. It can even be used to manage individuals who are on bail.

One of the standout features of the LK106 is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the location of your assets or loved ones at any given moment. It also offers auto-tracking, blind area tracking, and history-trace checking, giving you a comprehensive view of the movements and whereabouts of the tracker. With GPS+GSM tracking, you can rely on accurate and reliable location information.

The LK106 is equipped with various alerts and notifications to keep you informed. It has a geo-fence feature that alerts you when the tracker enters or exits a predefined area. You can set up movement alerts, overspeed alerts, and low battery alerts to ensure the safety and proper functioning of the tracker. Additionally, the LK106 has a shaking sensor alert and an SOS button for emergency situations.

With its waterproof IP66 rating, the LK106 can withstand harsh weather conditions and environments, making it suitable for outdoor use. It also has a sleeping working mode to conserve battery life when not in use. The LK106 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that offers peace-of-mind and security in various applications.
