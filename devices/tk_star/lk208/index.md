# TK-Star - LK208

![LK208](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star LK208 is a portable GPS tracker designed to provide continuous location visibility for vehicles, equipment, cargo, and other mobile assets. It combines real time tracking and auto tracking with a set of alert functions and modes intended to support long battery life and reliable monitoring in a variety of environments. The LK208 is described as suitable for private cars, rentals, outdoor equipment, containers, and cargo, making it a flexible choice for both personal and light commercial use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK208 can feed location updates and event notifications into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and management. That compatibility makes the LK208 a practical option for teams that want to combine a portable hardware tracker with Plaspy reporting, alerting, and operational oversight without changing their existing tracking workflow. Plaspy can present LK208 data alongside other devices to give a unified view of fleet and asset status.

## Key Highlights

- Portable form factor suitable for vehicles equipment and cargo tracking
- Real time tracking plus auto tracking for scheduled location updates
- Blind area tracking capability using GSM tracking when GPS is not available
- Configurable alerts including geofence movement overspeed and low battery
- Remote monitoring and SOS button for emergency notification
- Long runtime supported by a high capacity 5000 mA battery and power saving modes

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the LK208 provides location and alert data that Plaspy can display and act upon for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy ingests the device updates and makes them available through dashboards and notification channels to support daily operations.

- Centralize LK208 real time locations on Plaspy maps for live visibility
- Receive geofence movement overspeed and low battery alerts through Plaspy to relevant users
- Use historical location data from LK208 for basic trip and activity review in Plaspy
- Monitor multiple LK208 units together to manage mixed fleets and portable assets
- Configure Plaspy notifications to match operational rules and escalation paths

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking private cars and rental vehicles for location oversight
- Monitoring portable construction or landscaping equipment on job sites
- Keeping visibility on containers and cargo during storage and transit
- Emergency alerting and remote monitoring for vulnerable assets
- Short term deployments where a portable tracker is preferred over fixed installation

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LK208 pairs a broad set of practical tracking features with portability and long battery life, making it a sensible option when you need flexible asset monitoring. Used with Plaspy the tracker’s real time updates and alerts can be incorporated into a broader fleet or asset management workflow without extensive changes to existing processes.

While the LK208 provides many useful features for general tracking needs, organizations should consider their specific reporting and operational requirements when selecting hardware. When combined with Plaspy, the LK208 offers a straightforward way to add portable GPS tracked assets to a single management platform, helping teams maintain situational awareness and respond to alerts efficiently.
