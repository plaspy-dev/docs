# TK-Star - LK208

![LK208](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star LK208 is a GPS portable tracker that offers a wide range of applications and features to ensure the safety and security of your vehicles, equipment, and cargo. Whether you need to track private cars, vehicle rentals, or equipment outside, or even track containers and cargo, the LK208 is designed to meet your tracking needs.

One of the standout features of the LK208 is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the location of your assets in real-time. It also offers auto-tracking, which automatically updates the location at regular intervals, ensuring you have the most up-to-date information. Additionally, the LK208 has blind area tracking, which means it can continue to track even when there is no GPS signal, using GSM tracking technology.

The LK208 also offers a range of alerts and notifications to keep you informed. You can set up a geo-fence, which will send an alert if the tracker goes outside a predefined area. It also has movement and overspeed alerts, so you can be notified if your assets are on the move or exceeding a certain speed. The low battery alert ensures that you never lose track of your assets due to a dead battery, and the shaking sensor alert detects any unauthorized movement or tampering.

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the LK208 also offers remote monitoring, allowing you to listen in on the surrounding environment. It also has an SOS button for emergency situations, as well as a sleeping working mode to conserve battery life. Speaking of battery life, the LK208 is equipped with a powerful 5000 mA battery, providing long-lasting performance.

With its comprehensive features and reliable performance, the TK-Star LK208 is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a GPS portable tracker. Whether you need to track vehicles, equipment, or cargo, the LK208 has you covered.
