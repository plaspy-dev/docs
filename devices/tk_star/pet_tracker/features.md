# TK-Star - Pet Tracker Features

This page provides a public, feature oriented overview of the TK-Star Pet Tracker and how its capabilities map to monitoring and fleet style tracking inside Plaspy. It summarizes the practical functions you can expect to use with Plaspy and highlights which tracker behaviors are relevant for location visibility, event monitoring, and basic device oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on the tracker firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. Firmware updates, regional variants, and how the tracker is configured can change details such as reporting interval, alert behavior, and app features, so treat the items below as a capabilities summary rather than a definitive technical specification.

## Feature Overview

The TK-Star Pet Tracker is positioned as an ultra portable pet collar tracker that offers live location reporting through both SMS and a smartphone app. It focuses on giving pet owners timely location information and simple safe zone alerts while remaining small enough to attach to a collar.

- On demand location return via text message with a Google Maps style link and location accuracy stated by the manufacturer
- Live tracking through the vendor smartphone app with configurable location report intervals between 3 and 30 seconds
- GeoFence boundary support that can trigger a prompt text message when the tracker leaves the defined safe zone
- Lightweight design intended for attachment to a pet collar for walks and daily activity
- Live feed and route tracking without the need to connect the device to a computer
- Battery life optimized for over 24 hours of active live tracking under vendor conditions

## Core Features of TK-Star - Pet Tracker

- SIM based communications for direct text message queries and location replies
- Immediate location return with a map link for quick recovery assistance
- Live tracking via vendor smartphone app with short interval reporting options
- Configurable location report frequency supported in the 3 to 30 second range
- GeoFence setup in the app that issues a prompt when the device leaves a preset area
- Lightweight, collar friendly form factor suitable for cats, dogs, and larger animals
- Over 24 hours of continuous live tracking reported by the manufacturer in typical use
- Live feed capability without requiring a computer for playback or review

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location reports and events from compatible trackers and displays them in a centralized map and device list. Where the tracker supplies location updates or boundary events, Plaspy surfaces those items so operators and pet owners can monitor activity over time.

- Live location updates appear on the Plaspy map and can reflect frequent report intervals for near real time tracking
- Route history and recent movement are stored and available for playback and review inside Plaspy
- GeoFence entry and exit events reported by the tracker can be shown as alerts in Plaspy to support recovery or monitoring workflows
- SMS based immediate location replies can be used as a fallback in parallel with Plaspy monitoring where supported by the device
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols allowing the platform to receive and present reported locations without manual protocol selection

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time tracking of a pet during walks or outdoor excursions
- Immediate location lookup by text message when a pet is lost or missing
- Safe zone monitoring for yard boundaries with prompt alerts if a pet leaves the area
- Route history review to understand common roaming patterns and activity
- Tracking of larger animals such as horses during supervised movement
- Using the vendor app and Plaspy together for both direct device control and centralized oversight

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer stated accuracy and battery life are conditional and can vary by firmware, satellite visibility, and network conditions
- Some features may differ between firmware versions or hardware revisions and may be enabled or disabled by the manufacturer
- Regional cellular support and SIM provisioning may affect SMS and live reporting behavior in different countries
- Device setup method and collar installation can influence signal reception and the quality of location reports
- Refer to the device app and manufacturer documentation for the most current instructions on geofence setup and live tracking options
