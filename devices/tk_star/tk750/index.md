# TK-Star - TK750

![TK750](./tracker.png)

The TK-Star TK750 is a compact 4G GPS tracker from an established telematics manufacturer, designed for motorcycles, private cars, electric vehicles, rental fleets and light trucks. It combines multi constellation positioning with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance and broad cellular support to deliver reliable outdoor positioning, improved indoor coverage and practical options for covert or hardwired installation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK750 can be integrated quickly into Plaspy for live tracking, fleet dashboards and anti theft workflows. Its event reporting and immobilization features make it a relevant choice for operators who want to monitor vehicle movement, receive alerts and review historical routes within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking and fleet management dashboards
- Multi constellation positioning with GPS GLONASS and BeiDou plus LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance
- Broad cellular support including 4G, WCDMA and GSM with NB IoT and Cat M1 where available
- Anti theft features such as a sensitive vibration sensor, geofence alerts and movement alarms
- Remote engine cut off and resume controls for immobilization and recovery support
- Compact, covert form factor suitable for motorcycles rental vehicles and tight installations
- Server integration with route history storage useful for compliance and incident review

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the TK750 is connected to Plaspy, it streams location and event data to your Plaspy account so you can visualize live positions, receive alerts and analyze historical trips. Plaspy uses device telemetry and alarm events to provide operational visibility and control across a mixed fleet.

- Real time location updates and map visualization in Plaspy for live monitoring
- Vibration and movement alerts forwarded to Plaspy for anti theft notifications
- Geofence and overspeed alerts managed through Plaspy for automated alarm handling
- Remote immobilization commands available via Plaspy when authorised and configured
- Historical route playback and reporting in Plaspy for incident review and fleet analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization where rapid response and remote cut off are required
- Motorcycle protection and discreet tracking for recovery and owner peace of mind
- Rental vehicle monitoring with geofence alerts overspeed notifications and route history for returns
- Light truck and equipment tracking across regions with varied network coverage
- City and regional fleet management for small to medium sized operations using Plaspy dashboards

The TK750 pairs a compact hardware footprint with multi constellation positioning and multiple location assistance methods to improve reliability in urban and indoor adjacent conditions. For organizations using Plaspy, the device provides practical event reporting and immobilization options that integrate into standard fleet workflows.
