# TK-Star - TK750

![TK750](./tracker.png)

The TK750 4G by an established telematics manufacturer is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, private cars, electric vehicles, rental fleets and light trucks. Combining multi-constellation positioning \(GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou\) with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance and wide cellular support, the TK750 delivers reliable outdoor positioning, improved indoor coverage and rapid integration into Plaspy for fleet management and anti-theft workflows.

Built for live tracking, simple hardwired installation and covert mounting, the TK750 supports 4G FDD/TDD, WCDMA and GSM plus NB‑IoT and Cat‑M1 where available. Its compact size, vibration sensor, geofence and remote engine cut-off make it a practical choice for operators who need real-time tracking, telemetry and theft mitigation in a Plaspy-compatible device.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and fleet management dashboards.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou\) with LBS and Wi‑Fi for improved indoor and urban coverage.
- Broad cellular support: 4G FDD/TDD, WCDMA, GSM and NB‑IoT / Cat‑M1 for global deployments.
- Anti‑theft features: sensitive vibration sensor, geofence alerts, move and overspeed alarms.
- Remote engine cut-off/resume control for immobilization and theft recovery.
- Compact, covert form factor ideal for motorcycles and rental vehicles.
- Server and mobile app integration with route history storage \(up to six months\) for compliance and analysis.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed and connected to Plaspy, the TK750 streams location and event data to your Plaspy account for live visualization, alerts and reporting. Plaspy reads the device's telemetry and alarm events so you can monitor vehicle status, set geofences, trigger immobilization commands and review historical routes for up to six months.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Vibration and movement alerts for anti-theft notifications and immediate response.
- Geofence and overspeed alerts forwarded to Plaspy for automated alarm handling.
- Remote engine cut-off/resume control available through Plaspy when configured with vehicle wiring and permissions.
- Historical route playback \(stored on server up to six months\) for incident review and fleet analysis.
