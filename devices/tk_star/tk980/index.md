# TK-Star - TK980

![TK980](./tracker.jpg)

The TK980 4G OBD2 is a plug-and-play GPS tracker engineered for rapid installation and continuous vehicle monitoring. As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK980 delivers reliable real-time tracking and OBD2-based telemetry without the need for wiring — simply plug into the vehicle OBD2 port and connect the unit to the Plaspy platform for immediate fleet management, anti-theft alerts and analytics.

Compact, rugged and designed for mixed urban/outdoor use, the TK980 supports multi-mode positioning \(GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, LBS and Wi‑Fi\) and wide cellular coverage including 4G with NB‑IoT, CAT‑M1 and 2G fallback. Built-in vibration sensing, geo-fencing and server-side historical route storage make this tracker a practical choice for commercial fleets, rental vehicles and private cars that need dependable location intelligence and telemetry through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible plug-and-play OBD2 installation — fast deployment for fleets and rental vehicles.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS, BeiDou and GLONASS with LBS and Wi‑Fi hybrid positioning for improved indoor/outdoor coverage.
- 4G cellular with NB‑IoT, CAT‑M1 and 2G fallback for global connectivity and reliable real-time tracking.
- Built-in vibration sensor for immediate anti-theft and unauthorized movement alerts.
- On-platform fleet features: geo‑fencing, overspeed and move alerts plus historical route storage for up to six months.
- Compact, IP66-rated housing for robust performance in vehicle environments.
- Small rechargeable backup battery \(~60 mAh\) provides temporary tracking and alarm delivery if vehicle power is cut.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK980 streams location and OBD2 telemetry to the cloud so you can monitor assets in real time, configure alerts and review historical trips via mobile app or web platform. Plaspy ingests the device’s hybrid positioning data and vehicle diagnostics to produce live maps, geofence enforcement and time-stamped event logs suitable for fleet management and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GPS/BD/GLONASS + LBS/Wi‑Fi\) for accurate vehicle tracking.
- Vehicle parameters read from the OBD2 port — engine and diagnostic data are passed to Plaspy for telemetry and status reporting \(fuel-related telemetry where provided by the vehicle’s OBD2 system\).
- Vibration-triggered movement and unauthorized motion alerts feed directly into Plaspy’s alarm and notification system for anti-theft response.
- Geo-fencing, overspeed and move alerts configured within Plaspy to automate fleet policies and compliance monitoring.
- Historical route storage on the server \(up to six months\) accessible through Plaspy’s mobile and web interfaces for audits and trip review.
