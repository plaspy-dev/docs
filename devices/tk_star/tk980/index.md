# TK-Star - TK980

<div className="device-logo">
![TK980](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK980 is a compact plug-and-play OBD2 GPS tracker from TK-Star designed for rapid installation and continuous vehicle monitoring. Engineered for mixed urban and outdoor environments, the device combines multi-mode positioning, built-in vibration sensing, and server side historical route storage to deliver dependable location intelligence without complex wiring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK980 streams real time location and OBD2 telemetry into the Plaspy platform for immediate fleet management, anti-theft alerts, and analytics. Its plug-in form factor and hybrid positioning approach make it a practical option for organizations that need fast deployment and consistent visibility in Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD2 installation for fast deployment across fleets and rental vehicles
- Multi-mode positioning including GPS, BeiDou and GLONASS with LBS and Wi‑Fi hybrid support
- Broad cellular coverage with 4G and fallback options to maintain reliable connectivity
- Built-in vibration sensor to trigger anti-theft and unauthorized movement alerts
- Server side historical route storage and time stamped events for audits and reporting
- IP66 rated compact housing suited for vehicle environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK980 delivers continuous location updates and vehicle telemetry to the cloud so fleets can monitor assets in real time, configure automated alerts, and review historical trips. Plaspy ingests the device data to provide maps, event logs, and policy enforcement for operational oversight.

- Real time location updates using hybrid positioning for improved coverage in mixed environments
- Vehicle parameters from the OBD2 connection passed to Plaspy for telemetry and status reporting
- Vibration triggered movement alerts feed into Plaspy alarm and notification workflows
- Geo fencing, overspeed and movement alerts configurable within Plaspy for automated monitoring
- Historical route access on the server for trip review and compliance checks

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management to track vehicles, optimize routes, and monitor driver behavior
- Vehicle anti theft with vibration detection and instant notifications to operators
- Rental and car sharing for rapid device deployment and server side trip logs for accountability
- Cargo and equipment transport where hybrid positioning improves tracking in urban and indoor areas
- Vehicle health awareness by relaying OBD2 telemetry to Plaspy for high level diagnostics and planning

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK980 pairs practical plug-and-play installation with multi-mode positioning and direct OBD2 telemetry to provide useful location and vehicle data inside Plaspy. Its compact, rugged design and broad cellular support make it suitable for a range of commercial and private vehicle deployments where fast onboarding and continuous visibility matter.

For teams using Plaspy, the TK980 offers an accessible way to add real time tracking, anti-theft alerts, and historical route records without complex installation.
