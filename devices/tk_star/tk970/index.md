# TK-Star - TK970

![TK970](./tracker.jpg)

The TK970 4G is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, private cars, rental fleets and mixed vehicle assets. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the TK970 4G delivers reliable multi‑mode positioning and telematics so operators and owners can use Plaspy for real‑time tracking, event alerts and historical reporting across urban and remote environments.

The TK970 4G combines precise GNSS, cellular connectivity \(4G/LTE, NB‑IoT, Cat M1 and 2G variants\) and a set of security and remote‑control features — including SOS, remote engine/oil cut‑off via relay, built‑in microphone and a sensitive vibration sensor — making it a practical GPS tracker for anti‑theft, fleet management and remote monitoring applications when integrated with the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: seamless integration for real‑time tracking, alerts and fleet dashboards.
- Multi‑mode positioning: GPS, BeiDou \(BD\), GLONASS plus LBS and Wi‑Fi fallback for improved indoor/outdoor coverage.
- Security features: SOS emergency button, vibration alarm and relay‑based remote engine/oil cut‑off for anti‑theft response.
- Compact and robust: small form factor \(98 × 54 × 16.5 mm\) and wide operating voltages suitable for motorcycles, cars and commercial vehicles.
- Extended cellular support: 4G LTE regional band variants plus NB‑IoT, Cat M1 and 2G for broad global deployment.
- On‑device telemetry: remote voice monitoring \(built‑in microphone\), geo‑fence, over‑speed and move alerts with server route history retention.
- Low‑power backup: internal 180 mAh battery provides approximately two hours standby when external power is lost.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once the TK970 4G is installed in a vehicle or asset, it transmits positioning and event data over cellular networks to Plaspy. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates, network fallback positions \(LBS/Wi‑Fi\), and telematics events so fleet managers and owners receive precise location updates, automated alerts and consolidated reports via the Plaspy web and mobile interfaces.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and mapping.
- SOS emergency notifications and remote alerting routed through Plaspy for fast response.
- Relay state and remote immobilizer actions \(engine/oil cut‑off and resume\) recorded and controllable through Plaspy.
- Event alerts supported: geo‑fence breaches, movement alerts, vibration alarms and over‑speed notifications.
- Historical route storage on the server \(data retention up to about six months\) for playback and compliance reporting.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real‑time tracking of cars, vans and trucks with route history and over‑speed reporting in Plaspy.
- Anti‑theft protection: vibration alarms, SOS and remote engine/oil cut‑off to immobilize stolen vehicles or prevent unauthorized use.
- Rental vehicle and scooter monitoring: location, movement alerts and historical playback for billing and incident investigation.
- Remote asset protection: compact installation on motorcycles and outdoor equipment where indoor/outdoor positioning and LBS/Wi‑Fi fallback matter.
- Operational telemetry: integrate TK970 position and event data with Plaspy dashboards to supplement third‑party telemetry \(for example, fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensors connected via the platform\) for consolidated fleet oversight.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK970 4G offers a balanced mix of compact hardware, broad cellular coverage and practical security features that pair well with Plaspy’s real‑time tracking and reporting capabilities. For fleet managers and individual owners who need reliable location data, event‑driven alerts and remote control actions, the TK970 delivers consistent telemetry without unnecessary complexity. Paired with Plaspy, you get centralized dashboards, alerting rules, and historical playback that turn the TK970’s raw GPS and event data into actionable insights for fleet management, anti‑theft response and ongoing operational efficiency.
