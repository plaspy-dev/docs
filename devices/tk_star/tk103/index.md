# TK-Star - TK103

![TK103](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star TK103 is a versatile vehicle GPS tracker that allows you to locate and monitor any remote target using the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites. With its range of features and functionalities, this tracker is suitable for various applications such as vehicle rental, fleet management, protecting wealth, providing peace-of-mind for businessmen, and managing personnel.

One of the main functions of the TK103 is positioning and tracking. You can easily locate and track your vehicle through the online positioning platform or by using your cell phone. The tracker also has an emergency alarm feature that can be triggered in case of an emergency, sending out an alarm to authorized numbers. Additionally, you can remotely control the oil and circuit of your vehicle using the unit by sending an SMS from anywhere and anytime.

The TK103 also offers remote monitoring capabilities, allowing you to call the phone number in the unit and listen to the sound within a 5-meter radius. It has a movement alert function that sends an alert to authorized numbers when the vehicle is in motion, as well as an overspeed alert feature that notifies you when the vehicle exceeds a certain speed limit. You can also set up a geo-fence to restrict the movement of the unit within a specific district, and it will send a message if the unit breaches the designated area.

Other notable features of the TK103 include mileage statistics, ACC checking, power cutting off, and compatibility with the original anti-theft alarm. It also allows you to modify the IP and Port of Communication Protocol for different online positioning platforms. With its comprehensive range of features and applications, the TK-Star TK103 is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a reliable and efficient vehicle GPS tracker.
