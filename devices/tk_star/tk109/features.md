# TK-Star - TK109 Features

This page provides a public, feature oriented overview of the TK-Star TK109 and how its capabilities are reflected when used with the Plaspy tracking platform. It describes the device's practical functions, common uses, and the kinds of events and location data Plaspy can display, while avoiding device configuration specifics that belong in manufacturer documentation.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information below is based on the device description and focuses on publicly observable capabilities such as multi GNSS positioning, SOS signaling, vibration alerts, IP rating, and how those signals are used within Plaspy for monitoring and historical review.

## Feature Overview

The TK109 is a compact, water resistant GPS tracker designed for personal safety and small asset protection. It combines multiple positioning methods and alarm signals to deliver both outdoor and improved indoor location awareness, with a focus on discreet use and extended standby operation.

- Multi mode positioning including GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, LBS and Wi‑Fi for improved location availability indoors and outdoors.
- Built in SOS button for emergency signalling and a sensitive vibration sensor for movement or tamper alerts.
- Lightweight, compact form factor with IP65 water resistance suitable for wearable and portable placements.
- Long standby orientation and rechargeable battery supporting longer field use and simpler maintenance.
- Real time location streaming and historical route storage so routes and events can be reviewed later in Plaspy.

## Core Features of TK-Star - TK109

- Multi GNSS positioning stack: GPS, BeiDou and GLONASS plus network and Wi‑Fi assisted positioning for better indoor coverage.
- SOS emergency button to generate an immediate alert and location report when pressed.
- Vibration sensor that triggers movement or vibration alarms for motion detection and tamper indication.
- Compact, lightweight design with IP65 water resistance for everyday carry and exposure to light weather.
- Rechargeable battery designed for extended standby life to reduce frequency of recharging.
- Quad band GSM/GPRS cellular connectivity for data reporting to Plaspy and remote visibility.
- Historical route storage capability when paired with Plaspy for playback and review over time.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TK109 is used with Plaspy, the tracker’s location fixes and alarm events are reported into the platform where they are normalized, displayed, and archived. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and ingests location and event messages so they appear in dashboards, maps, and notifications without manual protocol mapping by the user.

- Real time location updates are visible in Plaspy for live monitoring and dispatching.
- SOS button events are delivered to Plaspy and can trigger notifications to configured contacts and teams.
- Vibration and movement alerts are forwarded into Plaspy as events for immediate attention or automated workflows.
- Historical route playback and stored traces in Plaspy allow post incident review and reporting.
- Device status and recent communications are shown in Plaspy so administrators can monitor connectivity and activity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child and elderly safety monitoring with SOS alerts and location history for caregivers.
- Pet and small animal tracking where a lightweight, discreet device is required.
- Portable asset protection for equipment and shipments with vibration alarms and route logs.
- Lone worker or traveler emergency response using SOS signaling and live location sharing.
- Supplementary monitoring for small vehicles or tools where a compact tracker is preferred.

- Hardware revisions or regional variants may affect supported cellular bands, battery capacity, or available sensors.
- Installation type and placement influence GNSS and Wi‑Fi assisted positioning performance, especially indoors.
- Manufacturer settings and provisioning can enable or disable certain alarms and reporting modes.

Using the TK109 with Plaspy centralizes real time location, alarms, and historical routes into a single platform for monitoring and operational oversight.
