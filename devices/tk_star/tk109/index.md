# TK-Star - TK109

<div className="device-logo">
![TK109](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK-Star TK109 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for personal safety and asset protection. Built as a lightweight, water resistant unit, the TK109 uses multiple positioning modes to provide dependable outdoor and indoor location data and supports immediate alerting for SOS and vibration events. Its small form factor and protective enclosure make it suitable for wearable use or discreet attachment to portable assets.

As a device that integrates with Plaspy, the TK109 delivers real-time tracking, event alerts, and historical route review through Plaspy’s mobile and web interfaces. That compatibility means organizations and caregivers can add the TK109 to Plaspy-managed fleets and asset inventories for centralized monitoring, notifications, and route analysis without replacing existing tracking workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and historical route playback via mobile and web platforms
- Multi mode positioning including GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, LBS and Wi Fi for improved indoor and outdoor coverage
- Built in SOS button and sensitive vibration alerting for emergency signaling and movement detection
- Compact, lightweight and water resistant design suited for personal and portable asset applications
- Long standby performance and rechargeable battery that supports extended field use
- UBLOX GNSS receiver and practical positioning accuracy for dependable location fixes
- Server storage of historical tracking data to support review and reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TK109 streams location and event data over cellular connectivity to the Plaspy platform where information is processed and displayed in real time. Plaspy consolidates live position updates, events, and stored routes so operators can monitor status, receive alerts, and investigate past movements from a single dashboard.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy for monitoring and dispatch
- Historical route playback and server storage for review and compliance purposes
- Geo fencing, movement and vibration alerts forwarded as Plaspy events for quick response
- SOS button events routed to Plaspy notifications and configured emergency contacts
- Centralization of device events and location data alongside other Plaspy compatible devices

## Typical Use Cases

- Child and elderly safety monitoring with SOS and geo fencing alerts for caregivers
- Pet tracking for small animals requiring a lightweight, discreet device
- Portable asset protection with vibration alarms and route history for recovery and audits
- Lone worker or traveler safety where instant SOS signaling and live location sharing are needed
- Complementary tracking for light vehicles or equipment in mixed device fleets

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK109 is a practical choice when you need a small, rugged tracker focused on dependable location and alarm reporting. Its multi mode positioning improves chances of accurate fixes across environments, while the built in SOS and vibration alerts provide clear event signals that Plaspy can surface to operators and emergency contacts. Paired with Plaspy, the device becomes part of a centralized monitoring solution that supports real time decision making and historical analysis.
