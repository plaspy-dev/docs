# TK-Star - TK109

![TK109](./tracker.jpg)

The TKSTAR TK109 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for personal safety and asset protection. Manufactured as a lightweight, water-resistant unit, the TK109 combines multi-mode positioning technologies — GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, LBS and Wi‑Fi — to provide dependable outdoor and indoor location data. Integrating the TK109 with Plaspy enables real-time tracking, event alerts and historical route review through both mobile app and web platforms.

Designed for parents, caregivers, pet owners and field operators who need a discreet device with long standby life and comprehensive alarms, the TK109 delivers vibration alerts, SOS emergency signaling and geo‑fencing. With an efficient UBLOX GNSS engine, quad‑band GSM/GPRS connectivity and IP65 protection, this GPS tracker is a practical choice where reliability and easy Plaspy integration matter.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and historical route playback via mobile app and web platform.
- Multi‑mode positioning \(GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, LBS, Wi‑Fi\) for improved indoor and outdoor accuracy.
- Built-in SOS button and sensitive vibration sensor for immediate emergency and movement alerts.
- 1000 mAh rechargeable Li‑ion battery with up to ~7 days standby for extended field use.
- IP65 water resistance and compact form factor \(≈50 × 15 × 50 mm, ~50 g\) for flexible mounting and wearable use.
- UBLOX GNSS chip with −159 dBm sensitivity and ~5 m positioning accuracy for reliable location fixes.
- Server storage of historical data \(up to six months\) for compliance and review of past routes.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TK109 streams location and event data over cellular GPRS to the Plaspy server, where the platform processes and displays information in real time. Plaspy then provides live location, alerts, and historical playback through secure web and mobile interfaces. Alerts generated by the TK109 — such as SOS, vibration/movement, over‑speed and geo‑fence events — are forwarded into Plaspy’s notification and reporting engine so you can act quickly.

- Real-time location updates delivered to Plaspy for monitoring and dispatch.
- Historical route playback and server storage \(up to six months\) for review and reporting.
- Geo‑fencing, move alerts, over‑speed alerts and vibration alarms forwarded as Plaspy events.
- SOS button events routed to Plaspy notifications and configured emergency contacts.
- Note on telemetries: TK109 focuses on multi‑GNSS positioning and alarm signals; it does not include built‑in ignition, immobilizer, dedicated fuel‑sensor inputs or Bluetooth sensors. Plaspy’s platform supports those telemetries for other compatible devices, allowing you to centralize data from mixed fleets and assets.
