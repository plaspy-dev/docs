# TK-Star - XE209

![XE209](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star XE209 is a GPS portable tracker designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring for various applications. Whether you need to track private cars, vehicle rentals, equipment, containers, or cargo, the XE209 has you covered. With its compact and portable design, you can easily place it in any desired location for tracking purposes.

One of the standout features of the XE209 is its real-time tracking capability. You can monitor the location of your assets or vehicles in real-time, ensuring that you have up-to-date information at all times. Additionally, the XE209 offers auto-tracking, blind area tracking, and GPS+GSM tracking, giving you multiple options for tracking your assets.

The XE209 also comes equipped with a range of useful features such as history-trace checking, geo-fencing, dropped alert, overspeed alert, low battery alert, and shaking sensor alert. These features allow you to set up customized alerts and notifications, ensuring that you are promptly informed of any unusual activity or events.

With a long battery life of 20000 mA, the XE209 can operate for extended periods without the need for frequent recharging. This makes it ideal for long-term tracking applications where continuous monitoring is required.

Overall, the TK-Star XE209 GPS portable tracker is a reliable and versatile solution for tracking and monitoring various assets. Its range of features and long battery life make it a valuable tool for individuals and businesses alike.

### Key Features:

- Real-time tracking
- Auto-tracking
