# TK-Star - XE209

![XE209](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star XE209 is a compact portable GPS tracker designed for real-time tracking and monitoring across a variety of mobile and stationary assets. It is intended for use with private cars, vehicle rentals, equipment, containers, cargo, and other items that benefit from continuous location visibility. The XE209 emphasizes portability and straightforward placement so it can be deployed where fixed installations are impractical.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XE209 can be integrated into Plaspy's tracking and fleet management environment to provide centralized visibility and alerting. Its feature set — including real-time tracking, history trace, geo-fencing, and a suite of movement and status alerts — maps well to Plaspy workflows for monitoring assets, responding to events, and generating operational reports.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location tracking for continuous visibility of assets and vehicles
- Auto tracking and blind area tracking options to handle varying coverage conditions
- GPS plus GSM based tracking that supports typical mobile asset monitoring needs
- Built in alert types such as overspeed, dropped alert, low battery, and shaking sensor
- History trace and geo fence features for playback and geolocation based notifications
- Very long battery capacity cited at 20000 mA for extended deployments between charges
- Compact and portable form factor suitable for temporary or movable installations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the XE209 supplies location updates and event notifications that Plaspy can display, organize, and act upon. Plaspy users can assign trackers to assets, review historical routes, and combine XE209 alerts with other operational data for day to day fleet oversight.

- Display live position on Plaspy maps for active monitoring of vehicles and assets
- Receive geo fence, overspeed, dropped, and low battery alerts in Plaspy to trigger workflows
- Access history trace data inside Plaspy for route replay and incident review
- Use Plaspy reporting to aggregate XE209 tracking events into operational summaries
- Attach XE209 to an asset record in Plaspy to maintain inventory and assignment history

## Typical Use Cases

- Temporary tracking of rental vehicles and short term fleet assignments
- Monitoring of equipment and tools that move between sites
- Tracking containers and cargo during transport and storage
- Private car tracking for owner monitoring and recovery support
- Long duration monitoring for assets requiring infrequent maintenance checks

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The XE209 is a practical choice for organizations that need a portable tracker with strong battery endurance and a focused set of alerts and tracking modes. Its portability makes it easy to redeploy across different asset types, while the core features provide the visibility fleets and asset managers rely on for routine oversight.
