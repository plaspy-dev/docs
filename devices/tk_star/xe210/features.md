# TK-Star - XE210 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the TK-Star XE210 features and explains how those capabilities integrate with Plaspy for location visibility and monitoring. It is intended to help fleet managers, asset owners, and technical users understand the practical functions the XE210 can report into Plaspy and how those functions are typically used in the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior may vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, network coverage, and the manufacturer implementation. The material on this page summarizes publicly available feature context; check the manufacturer documentation for device specific details and the current feature set.

## Feature Overview

The XE210 is a compact GPS tracker designed to provide real time and historical location information over cellular networks while offering a set of alarms and monitoring modes for common tracking scenarios. Its mix of positioning, reporting, and alert features makes it useful for tracking vehicles, assets, and people where cellular and GPS coverage exist.

- Dual positioning with GPS and AGPS for improved location reliability in mixed signal conditions
- Multi network cellular connectivity for continuous reporting over GSM GPRS WCDMA and LTE networks
- Multiple reporting methods including SMS APP and Internet to match different monitoring workflows
- Built in alarms such as low battery vibration geo fencing and over speed for event driven notifications
- Historical route tracking and street view map support for post event analysis and route review

## Core Features of TK-Star - XE210

- GPS and AGPS dual positioning to improve fix acquisition and location accuracy
- Support for GSM GPRS WCDMA and LTE networks for data and location reporting
- Multiple tracking modes including SMS, mobile APP, and Internet based reporting
- Rechargeable 3.7V 370mAh Li ion battery with manufacturer stated standby around one day under typical conditions
- Low battery alarm to notify when device charge is low
- Vibration alarm to detect movement or tampering events
- Historical route tracking for reviewing past locations and trip data
- Remote voice monitoring capability and geo fencing and over speed alarm support as described by the manufacturer

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives compatible tracker data and presents location and event information in a centralized interface so teams can monitor devices and respond to events. The platform automatically detects common tracker protocols for compatible models, allowing the XE210 to report location and alarm data with minimal manual configuration in many cases.

- Real time location display in Plaspy maps for ongoing visibility of XE210 devices
- Route history and playback based on the tracker historical route data to review trips and stops
- Event and alarm notifications in Plaspy for low battery vibration geo fence and over speed alerts
- Device status and simple diagnostics visibility so administrators can check reporting health
- Integration of APP and Internet reporting methods into the Plaspy device feed depending on how the device is configured

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle tracking for small fleets and individual cars needing location and route history
- Asset monitoring where discreet installation and long standby are valuable
- Personal safety and check in scenarios using location reporting and remote monitoring
- Short term rentals and shared equipment tracking with historical route review
- Operational oversight where vibration and low battery alarms support maintenance and recovery workflows
- Fleet management tasks such as locating vehicles, auditing routes, and receiving alerts
