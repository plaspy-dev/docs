# TK-Star - XE210

![XE210](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star XE210 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that allows you to locate and monitor remote targets with ease. It utilizes the existing GSM/GPRS/WCDMA/LTE network and GPS satellites to provide accurate and real-time tracking information. Whether you need to keep an eye on your vehicle, loved ones, or valuable assets, this tracker has got you covered.

One of the standout features of the TK-Star XE210 is its advanced GPS and AGPS dual positioning technology. This ensures that you get precise and reliable location data, even in areas with weak GPS signals. The tracker also supports various tracking methods, including SMS, APP, and Internet, giving you multiple options to monitor your targets.

With a compact and lightweight design, the TK-Star XE210 is easy to install and conceal. It features a rechargeable 3.7V 370mAh Li-ion battery that provides a standby time of up to 1 day. The tracker also offers a range of useful features, such as low battery alarm, vibration alarm, historical route tracking, remote voice monitoring, geo-fencing, over-speed alarm, and more. Whether you're managing a fleet of vehicles or simply want to keep tabs on your personal belongings, this GPS tracker is a reliable and efficient solution.
