# TK-Star - GF 07

![GF 07](./logo..png)

The TK-Star GF 07 is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for easy tracking and monitoring. With a mini form factor and lightweight ABS construction, the device can be placed inside belongings or carried in a pocket. The GF 07 uses GSM positioning and a compatible SIM card to provide location updates, making it suitable for monitoring people, valuable items, and light vehicle tracking where a small footprint is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GF 07 can be used to feed location information into the Plaspy platform for live visibility and operational oversight. Its simple, portable design and SIM based location reporting make it a practical choice for teams that need straightforward tracking without complex hardware integration. Plaspy can present the device location, history, and basic alerts alongside other trackers for unified fleet and asset management.

## Key Highlights

- Compact portable design that fits in pockets and small compartments
- GSM positioning with SIM based location reporting for real time updates
- Lightweight ABS construction for everyday use
- Miniature product size suitable for personal and asset tracking
- Approximate GPS positioning accuracy as reported by the manufacturer
- One year warranty and simple deployment for temporary or mobile use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GF 07 provides location updates that Plaspy can display and track alongside other devices. Plaspy ingests compatible device reports and makes location information available through its tracking interface to help teams monitor assets and people in one place.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps for live visibility
- Location history and playback to review past movements
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on location changes or geofence rules
- Grouping and assignment of devices for fleet or asset organization
- Reporting and operational summaries to support oversight and planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal tracking for caregivers monitoring vulnerable relatives
- Monitoring valuable portable items such as luggage or equipment
- Temporary vehicle or asset tracking for short term assignments
- Small fleet oversight where compact trackers are preferred
- Field equipment or tool tracking for mobile teams

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GF 07 is a practical fit for organizations and individuals who need a small, easy to deploy tracker that reports location via a SIM card. Its compact size makes it unobtrusive and convenient for a range of use cases, and when paired with Plaspy it becomes part of a single view for location management across assets and people.

Plaspy adds value by consolidating GF 07 location feeds with other device data, providing mapping, history, alerts, and basic reporting to improve operational awareness. For teams seeking simple, portable tracking combined with a cloud based monitoring platform, the GF 07 and Plaspy together offer a straightforward solution.
