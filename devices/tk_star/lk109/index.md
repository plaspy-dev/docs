# TK-Star - LK109

![LK109](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star LK109 is a portable GPS tracker designed to provide continuous location visibility for vehicles, equipment, and people. It combines real time tracking with features such as auto tracking, blind area tracking, history trace checking, geo fence alerts, movement and overspeed notifications, an SOS button, shaking sensor alerts, and a sleeping mode to conserve battery. The unit is built for everyday use and outdoor conditions, offering an IP65 level of protection for operations that require some resistance to dust and water.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the LK109 can be integrated into a centralized tracking platform to give teams consolidated visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can receive the LK109 location updates and alerts to present live positions, replay historical routes, and surface event notifications so fleet managers, security teams, and caretakers can monitor assets and people from a single interface.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location updates for continuous visibility of assets and people
- Portable form factor suitable for vehicles, equipment, personal use, and temporary deployments
- Geo fence functionality with alerts for boundary crossings
- Movement, overspeed, shaking sensor, and SOS alerts to support response workflows
- History trace and blind area tracking for post event review and route reconstruction
- Remote monitoring and a sleeping mode to extend operational runtime
- IP65 rating for protection against dust and water exposure in typical outdoor conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the LK109 feeds location and event information into a unified tracking environment so teams can monitor devices alongside other assets. Plaspy consolidates the LK109 data into maps, alerts, and reports for easier operational decision making.

- Live map view showing current LK109 positions across a fleet or asset roster
- Geofence creation and alert delivery based on LK109 boundary events
- Movement and overspeed notifications surfaced in Plaspy for timely response
- Historical route playback and location history for compliance and review
- Low battery and SOS alerts presented in the Plaspy interface to highlight devices needing attention
- Grouping and filtering of LK109 units for role based monitoring and dispatch
