# TK-Star - LK109

![LK109](./logo.jpg)

The TK-Star LK109 is a GPS portable tracker that offers a wide range of applications and features to ensure the safety and security of your assets. Whether you need to track your private car, rental vehicle, equipment, or even protect your loved ones such as children, the elderly, or pets, the LK109 is the perfect solution. It provides peace of mind for businessmen who need to manage their field staff or track criminals discreetly. It can even be used to manage individuals who are on bail.

One of the standout features of the LK109 is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the location of your assets or loved ones at any given moment. It also offers auto-tracking, blind area tracking, and history-trace checking, giving you a comprehensive view of the movements and whereabouts of the tracker. With GPS+GSM tracking, you can rely on accurate and reliable location information.

The LK109 also includes a range of alerts and notifications to keep you informed. It has a geo-fence feature that allows you to set up virtual boundaries, and you will receive an alert if the tracker goes outside of these boundaries. It also has movement and overspeed alerts, ensuring that you are notified if the tracker is in motion or exceeding a certain speed limit. Additionally, the LK109 has a low battery alert, so you never have to worry about losing track of your assets due to a dead battery.

Other notable features of the LK109 include remote monitoring, shaking sensor alert, SOS button for emergencies, and a sleeping working mode to conserve battery life. With its waterproof IP65 rating, you can trust that the LK109 will withstand various weather conditions and environments.
