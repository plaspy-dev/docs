# TK-Star - TK209A

![TK209A](./tracker.jpg)

The TK209A is a compact 4G GPS tracker from TK-Star designed for reliable, long term vehicle and asset tracking. It combines multi mode positioning with a high capacity 3.7V 5000 mAh rechargeable battery and a weather resistant IP65 enclosure to support extended deployments on private cars, rental fleets, containers, construction equipment and other hard to reach assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK209A can feed live location, movement and alarm data into Plaspy’s fleet management workflows. Its multi constellation positioning and server side route storage make the device a practical option for organisations that need consistent real time visibility, alerting and historical route review within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Large 3.7V 5000 mAh rechargeable battery for extended standby and reduced maintenance.
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS plus LBS and WiFi assisted fixes for consistent location results.
- IP65 rated enclosure for rain resistance and suitability for exposed outdoor installations.
- Built in vibration alarm, move alert, geo fence and over speed notifications for anti theft and safety monitoring.
- Server side route history retention up to six months for audit, replay and reporting needs.
- Regional 4G module options to support deployments across multiple territories.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed, the TK209A collects GNSS and network based location data and sends telemetry to Plaspy’s cloud. Plaspy processes that incoming data to present real time positions on maps, trigger alerts, and retain historical routes for review and reporting, enabling operators to monitor assets and respond to incidents efficiently.

- Live location updates and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for map based tracking and status monitoring.
- Immediate alerts for vibration, movement, geo fence breaches and over speed events feed Plaspy anti theft and safety workflows.
- Server side route history of up to six months is available for route replay, compliance checks and operational analysis.
- Battery and power status reported to Plaspy supports low battery notifications and maintenance scheduling.
- Collected telemetry can be used in Plaspy reports and operational dashboards to support fleet monitoring and decision making.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet management for rental cars and light commercial vehicles with route history for operations and billing.
- Anti theft protection for private vehicles and on site equipment using vibration and move alerts routed to monitoring teams.
- Container and cargo visibility for logistics operations requiring rugged devices and multi network connectivity.
- Outdoor asset tracking for construction and remote equipment where long battery life and weather resistance are important.
- Record keeping and incident investigation using six months of stored route history for audits and analysis.

The TK209A pairs long battery life and multi constellation positioning with Plaspy’s platform to deliver practical real time tracking, alerting and historical route review.
