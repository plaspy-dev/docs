# TK-Star - TK209A

![TK209A](./tracker.jpg)

The TK209A is a Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker engineered for reliable, long-term vehicle and asset tracking. Backed by a high-capacity 3.7V 5000 mAh Li-ion battery and multi-mode positioning \(GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, LBS and WiFi\), the TK209A delivers consistent real-time tracking and telemetry for private cars, rental fleets, containers, outdoor equipment and other hard-to-reach assets. Rugged and water-resistant \(IP65\), it is designed to tolerate exposed installations and provide low-maintenance operation for extended deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK209A feeds live location, movement and alarm data into Plaspy’s fleet management and anti-theft workflows. Its support for multiple 4G regional modules and server-side route storage \(up to six months\) makes it a practical choice for fleet managers and operations teams who need scalable telematics, geo-fencing, overspeed alerts and historical route review without constant battery swaps or complex installations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Large 5000 mAh rechargeable battery provides up to 15 days standby under typical usage — ideal for off-grid assets.
- Multi-constellation positioning \(UBLOX GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS\) with ~5 m accuracy and high sensitivity \(-159 dBm\) for consistent location fixes.
- IP65-rated enclosure for rain resistance and outdoor installations; compact form factor for discreet vehicle or asset mounting.
- Theft-protection features including vibration alarm, move alert, geo-fence and over-speed alerts for anti-theft and safety monitoring.
- Global cellular options via multiple SIM7600 4G module variants to support deployments across North America, Europe, China, South America and more.
- Server-side route history retention \(up to six months\) accessible through web and mobile apps for audit and analysis.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed, the TK209A continuously collects GNSS and network-based location data and relays telemetry to Plaspy’s cloud. Plaspy ingests that data for real-time tracking, alerts and historical reporting so fleet managers and owners can monitor assets, configure anti-theft responses and generate actionable reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered over 4G for live map tracking and status dashboards.
- Immediate alerts to Plaspy for vibration, move events, geo-fence breaches and over-speed conditions to support anti-theft workflows.
- Historical routes stored on the server \(up to six months\) and available in Plaspy’s web and mobile apps for route replay and compliance checks.
- Battery and power status reporting to Plaspy for low-battery notifications and maintenance planning.
- Plaspy can also integrate with additional peripherals or immobilizer workflows where compatible hardware is present, enabling remote anti-theft controls when those systems are available.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK209A pairs long battery life, multi-constellation GNSS and broad regional 4G support with Plaspy’s platform to deliver an effective solution for real-time tracking, telemetry and anti-theft monitoring. For fleet managers and asset owners, the primary value is reduced maintenance \(fewer battery changes\), consistent positioning both outdoors and indoors using hybrid location methods, and actionable alerts that feed directly into Plaspy’s fleet management workflows. Plaspy can also extend telemetry to additional services \(such as fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer controls and Bluetooth sensor integration\) when compatible peripherals or vehicle wiring are deployed, giving businesses a pathway to richer telematics without swapping base trackers.

In summary, the TK209A is a practical, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for organizations that need dependable real-time tracking, anti-theft alerts and long standby operation in a weather-resistant package. Its server-side route retention, UBLOX positioning performance and flexible regional 4G modules make it well suited to mixed fleets and cross-border deployments where uptime and traceability matter.
