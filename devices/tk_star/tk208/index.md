# TK-Star - TK208

<div className="device-logo">
![TK208](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TK208 is a compact portable GPS tracker from TK-Star designed for reliable personal and asset protection. It combines multi-mode positioning — GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, plus LBS and Wi‑Fi fallbacks — with a high-sensitivity UBLOX GNSS receiver to improve outdoor accuracy and assist positioning indoors. The device is built for extended use in the field with endurance-focused power capacity and rugged IP65 protection, and it includes practical safety features such as vibration detection, geo-fence and an SOS alarm.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK208 can stream location and event data into Plaspy for real-time monitoring, alerting and historical route analysis. Its combination of long standby capability, robust alerts and multi-mode positioning makes it a useful option for users who need continuous visibility and simple integration into Plaspy workflows for tracking, reporting and incident response.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into live tracking and reporting workflows
- Multi-constellation positioning with GNSS plus LBS and Wi‑Fi fallback for better coverage in mixed environments
- High-sensitivity UBLOX GNSS receiver and typical GPS-level accuracy suitable for general location tracking
- Long endurance design with extended standby times to reduce recharge frequency for portable deployments
- Rugged field readiness with IP65 protection and vibration detection for anti-theft alerts
- On-device SOS alarm and geo-fence features that generate high-priority events for fast response
- Server-side historical route storage to support playback and reporting for operational review

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TK208 transmits position and event messages to a Plaspy account where those inputs are converted into live map markers, timeline events and stored route history. Plaspy ingests movement alerts and device status to provide dashboards, notifications and audit trails that help teams act on location intelligence.

- Real-time location updates and fallbacks feed Plaspy maps and dashboards for continuous visibility
- Movement and anti-theft events such as vibration triggers appear as instant alerts in Plaspy
- Geo-fence entry and exit events are logged and enforced through Plaspy for perimeter monitoring
- SOS presses create high-priority notifications so teams can prioritize response through Plaspy workflows
- Historical route playback and archived tracks support reporting and operational audits within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children, elderly family members, or lone workers with SOS alerting
- Asset protection for valuables and portable equipment that benefit from vibration and geo-fence alarms
- Light fleet or rental unit location tracking where compact long-endurance devices are preferred
- Field staff accountability and remote worker oversight with location history and emergency notification
- Mixed indoor/outdoor tracking scenarios where multi-mode positioning improves coverage

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK208 is a practical choice for organizations and individuals who need a compact, endurance-oriented tracking device that feeds straightforward, event-driven data into Plaspy. Its combination of multi-mode positioning, vibration detection, geo-fence and SOS capabilities aligns well with Plaspy features such as real-time mapping, alerting and historical route analysis without requiring complex hardware integrations.

Because the TK208 focuses on portable tracking and long battery life rather than vehicle specific I/O or advanced sensor ecosystems, it fits use cases where simplicity, reliability and battery endurance are priorities. When paired with Plaspy, the device provides a clear path to operational visibility and timely notifications for safety, anti-theft and basic fleet monitoring.
