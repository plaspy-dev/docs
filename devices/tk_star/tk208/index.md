# TK208 Portable GPS Tracker

The TK208 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for reliable personal and asset protection. With multi-mode positioning \(GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS, LBS and Wi‑Fi\) and a high-sensitivity UBLOX GNSS receiver, the TK208 delivers accurate outdoor and improved indoor positioning while integrating seamlessly with Plaspy for real-time tracking and alerts.

The device combines long battery life, rugged IP65 protection and practical safety features — including vibration detection, geo-fence and SOS — making it an ideal choice for anti-theft monitoring, family safety, and basic fleet management telemetry where compact, long-endurance hardware is required.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for easy integration into real-time tracking and reporting workflows.
- Multi-constellation positioning: GPS + BeiDou \(BD\) + GLONASS, plus LBS and Wi‑Fi for better indoor/outdoor coverage.
- High-sensitivity UBLOX GNSS chip \(−159 dBm\) with typical GPS accuracy around 5 m for precise location data.
- Long battery life: 3.7V 5000 mAh Li‑ion battery with up to ~30 days standby depending on reporting interval.
- Robust field readiness: IP65 rating, wide operating temperature range and vibration sensor for anti-theft alerts.
- Immediate emergency response via SOS call button and app/SMS alerting for fast intervention.
- Server-based historical route storage \(up to six months\) accessible through companion app and web platform.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK208 streams location and event data to your Plaspy account using GSM/GPRS connectivity. Plaspy ingests the device’s GNSS positions, movement alerts and status messages to provide live maps, geofence enforcement, and historical route analysis. Integration is straightforward for users who need reliable real-time tracking, timestamped telemetry, and alert-driven workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GNSS positions and LBS/Wi‑Fi fallbacks feed Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- Movement and anti-theft alerts: vibration-triggered notifications and move alerts appear in Plaspy as instant events.
- Geo-fence enforcement: exit/entry events are generated by the TK208 and logged in Plaspy for audit and alerting.
- SOS and emergency calling: SOS presses create high-priority alerts visible in Plaspy and sent via SMS/app.
- Historical routes: up to six months of server-stored tracks available for playback and reporting in Plaspy.

## Use Cases

- Personal safety: discreet tracking and SOS for children, elderly or vulnerable individuals with instant alerts to caregivers via Plaspy.
- Asset and pet protection: attach to valuables or carriers to detect movement and receive geo-fence or vibration alarms for anti-theft response.
- Light fleet and rental monitoring: use TK208 for basic fleet management telemetry — location, route history and overspeed notifications — integrated into Plaspy dashboards.
- Field staff and lone-worker monitoring: long standby battery and SOS capabilities support remote personnel safety and accountability.
- Indoor/outdoor tracking where multi-mode positioning helps: Wi‑Fi and LBS assist GNSS for improved positioning in mixed environments.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK208 offers a practical balance of endurance, proven GNSS performance and robust alerts that integrate cleanly with Plaspy’s real-time tracking and reporting platform. For organizations and families seeking dependable location awareness without heavy hardware complexity, TK208 brings high-sensitivity satellite positioning, multi-mode fallback \(LBS/Wi‑Fi/BD/GLONASS\) and long battery life — all useful for telemetry, anti-theft workflows and basic fleet management.

While the TK208 focuses on portable, long-endurance tracking rather than vehicle-specific I/O \(ignition or immobilizer\) and advanced sensor interfaces, its strengths make it a reliable choice when Plaspy customers need accurate real-time tracking, event-driven alerts, and access to six months of route history for audits and operational insight.
