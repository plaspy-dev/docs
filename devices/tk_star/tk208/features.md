# TK-Star - TK208 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TK-Star TK208 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for real time tracking, alerts, and route history. It summarizes practical, non-sensitive functionality and explains what you can expect when integrating the TK208 with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and the way the device is installed or configured. For precise, model-specific technical details, always consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The TK208 is a compact portable tracker designed for long endurance monitoring and basic anti-theft, personal safety, and light fleet use. It combines multi-mode positioning, event alerts, and long battery life to deliver useful location awareness in Plaspy without heavy vehicle-specific interfaces.

- Multi-constellation positioning using GPS, BeiDou, and GLONASS with LBS and Wi‑Fi fallbacks for improved indoor and mixed environment coverage.
- High-sensitivity u-blox GNSS receiver for precise outdoor positions and reliable fixes in typical conditions.
- Long runtime from an integrated rechargeable battery suitable for extended standby and periodic reporting.
- Built-in vibration detection and geo-fence capability to generate movement and zone events for alerts and audit trails.
- SOS emergency alert support to surface high-priority events in monitoring workflows.
- Server-side historical route storage accessible for playback and reporting for multiple months.

## Core Features of TK-Star - TK208

- Multi-mode positioning: GPS plus BeiDou and GLONASS with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance for better location continuity.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver from u-blox for improved fix performance and practical accuracy in outdoor use.
- Rechargeable long-life battery designed for extended standby between reports.
- IP65 rated portable housing for basic dust and water resistance in field conditions.
- Vibration sensor to detect movement and trigger anti-theft notifications.
- Geo-fence support to define virtual zones and receive enter/exit events.
- SOS alert button for immediate emergency signaling.
- Server-based historical route storage for playback and review over time.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the TK208 position and event messages to provide live maps, notifications, and historical playback so teams can monitor assets and people in real time. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and accepts standard device reports for mapping and alerting.

- Live location updates from GNSS plus LBS and Wi‑Fi fallbacks appear on Plaspy maps and dashboards for continuous situational awareness.
- Movement and vibration events are logged as discrete alerts in Plaspy so you can act on potential tampering or unauthorized movement.
- Geo-fence enter and exit events generated by the device are recorded and can trigger notifications or workflow rules inside Plaspy.
- SOS presses surface as high-priority alerts in Plaspy and can be routed to on-call staff or notification channels.
- Historical route storage from the device and server is available for playback and auditing directly inside Plaspy.
- Plaspy can accept TK208 reports configured to report to the platform endpoint, and the platform will handle protocol detection and event parsing for display.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety for children, elderly, or vulnerable individuals with SOS alerts and location history.
- Asset protection for portable valuables and equipment where vibration and geo-fence alerts provide early warning.
- Light fleet or rental monitoring for tracking location and route history of portable units.
- Lone worker or field staff monitoring where long battery life and emergency alerts improve accountability.
- Mixed indoor/outdoor tracking scenarios where LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance complement GNSS for better coverage.

Combining the TK208 with Plaspy gives teams a centralized way to view live location, receive event-driven alerts, and review historical routes for operational insight.
