# Xirgo - XT-5000

![XT-5000](./logo.jpg)

The Xirgo XT-5000 is a highly efficient and durable GPS tracker designed for long-term deployments in challenging environments. With its ultra-low power consumption and integrated GPS engine, this rugged modem is perfect for applications that require continuous tracking without access to an external power source.

Equipped with a powerful 32-bit microprocessor and a unique power management algorithm, the XT-5000 consumes less than 120 uA in sleep mode while still being capable of periodic reporting of health, status, and location of remote assets. Additionally, it features integrated cellular and PCS antennas, a motion detector, and an optional accelerometer for enhanced functionality.

One of the standout features of the XT-5000 is its impressive battery life. With an optional 9,000 mAh internal battery and proper configuration, this tracker can operate for up to 3 years without the need for battery replacement. This makes it an excellent choice for long-term deployments where regular maintenance may not be feasible.

The XT-5000 is built to withstand extreme environmental conditions, meeting the IEC 68-2-27 environmental standard. Its rugged construction ensures durability and reliability, making it an ideal solution for outdoor applications such as trailer and container tracking. Furthermore, the XT-5000 utilizes the latest GPS technology, allowing for accurate tracking even in challenging conditions where other competing products may fail.

With its advanced features, long battery life, and robust construction, the Xirgo XT-5000 is a top choice for businesses and organizations that require reliable and efficient GPS tracking in demanding environments.
