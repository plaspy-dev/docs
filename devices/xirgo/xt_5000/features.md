# Xirgo - XT-5000 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Xirgo XT-5000 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing capabilities and explains how the device’s low power design, integrated GPS engine, and rugged construction make it suitable for long term asset tracking when paired with Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional accessory choices, and installation method. Where the XT-5000 description indicates optional components such as an internal battery or accelerometer those items may not be present on every unit and may affect runtime and reporting behavior.

## Feature Overview

The XT-5000 is built for long durations in the field with a focus on minimal power draw and resilient construction. Its design centers on delivering reliable location and status reporting over long deployment cycles while minimizing maintenance needs.

- Ultra low sleep current to preserve battery life and enable multi year deployments with optional internal battery
- Integrated GPS engine for consistent location reporting in outdoor and challenging conditions
- Built in power management and a 32 bit microprocessor to enable scheduled health and status reporting
- Motion detector and optional accelerometer for basic movement detection and wake on motion behavior
- Integrated cellular and PCS antennas for maintained connectivity in remote locations
- Rugged construction meeting IEC environmental test criteria for shock and vibration resilience

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT-5000

- Ultra low power consumption with reported sleep currents under 120 microamps for extended standby life
- Integrated GPS engine for location fixes and tracking updates
- 32 bit microprocessor with a power management algorithm for efficient periodic reporting
- Optional internal battery support that can enable multi year operation in low duty cycle configurations
- Motion detector plus an optional accelerometer to detect movement and reduce false wake events
- Integrated cellular and PCS antennas to simplify installation and maintain connectivity
- Rugged design built to withstand environmental stress consistent with IEC 68 2 27 test criteria

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the XT-5000’s low power design and periodic reporting model help provide long term visibility into remote assets without frequent maintenance. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols so devices can report into the platform with minimal manual protocol selection.

- Location updates appear in Plaspy as tracked position points for asset visibility
- Periodic health and status reports can be used to monitor device uptime and reporting frequency
- Motion and accelerometer based wake events map to movement indicators or activity logs inside Plaspy
- Long battery life and low power modes support extended monitoring schedules and historical location retention
- Rugged device suitability for outdoor assets reduces downtime entries and supports reliable Plaspy reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container location tracking for long haul and storage periods
- Remote asset monitoring where mains power is not available
- Long term deployments such as seasonal equipment or parked fleet units
- Logistics visibility for rarely accessed assets requiring multi year battery life
- Asset tracking in harsh environments where ruggedized construction is required

- Optional components such as the internal battery or accelerometer are not present on every unit and may be sold as variants
- Firmware revisions and manufacturer configuration can change reporting intervals, sleep behavior, and available telemetry
- Hardware revisions and regional cellular variants may affect certified cellular bands and connectivity performance
- Installation choices such as antenna placement and mounting can influence GPS accuracy and motion detection behavior
- Always consult the device documentation and release notes for the exact feature set of the specific hardware and firmware you are using
