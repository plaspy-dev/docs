# Xirgo - XT62

![XT62](./tracker.webp)

The XT62 is an asset module designed for refrigerated trailers and cargo containers, focused on cold chain visibility. It provides continuous condition monitoring and location reporting so operations teams can track temperature, door status and remote I/O signals while assets are in transit. The device is targeted at refrigerated cargo scenarios where combined location and condition awareness is essential.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT62 feeds location and sensor data into Plaspy for unified telemetry, event alerts and fleet dashboards. That compatibility makes it straightforward to include refrigerated assets in broader fleet monitoring, reporting and operational workflows managed through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for cold chain monitoring on refrigerated trailers and containers.
- Compatible with Plaspy for centralized telemetry, mapping and reporting.
- Door and roll up sensor inputs for access event detection and logging.
- Remote I O interfaces to trigger alarms or interact with external equipment.
- Condition based alerts for temperature excursions and access events.
- Asset level visibility that combines location and condition for compliance and operations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the XT62 with Plaspy connects refrigerated asset telemetry and location into a single platform where data is normalized, visualized on maps, and used to generate alerts and reports that support operational decisions.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear in Plaspy for mapping and route monitoring.
- Continuous temperature monitoring with configurable excursion alerts delivered through Plaspy.
- Door and access event logging to support anti theft workflows and chain of custody reporting.
- Remote I O control to actuate alarms or trigger external devices from Plaspy.
- Consolidation of XT62 inputs with other fleet telemetry for unified dashboards and reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Reefer trailer and container monitoring with continuous temperature and door status tracking.
- Cold chain logistics for pharmaceuticals and perishable food where regulatory reporting is required.
- Fleet management of refrigerated assets to support routing, utilization and condition based oversight.
- Anti theft and cargo security workflows using door event logging and location tracking.
- Rental and intermodal container tracking with remote I O control for locks or alarms.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The XT62 is a practical option for organizations that need consistent cold chain oversight and want to manage refrigerated assets alongside their broader fleet. Its sensor inputs and remote I O capabilities provide the core condition data that Plaspy can use for automated alerts, consolidated reporting and map based visibility.

Choosing the XT62 with Plaspy helps operations teams reduce manual monitoring, respond faster to excursions or access events, and keep condition and location data together for compliance and analysis. For teams managing reefers or temperature sensitive cargo, the XT62 offers focused asset level telemetry that integrates into Plaspy workflows.
