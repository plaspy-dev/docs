# Xirgo - XT-4970

![XT-4970](./logo.jpg)

The Xirgo XT-4970 is an energy harvesting LTE smart trailer solution designed for long-term, remote deployments without the need to replace the battery. This dual carrier LTE platform supports both AT&T and Verizon networks, ensuring reliable connectivity in various locations. It is an ideal choice for trailer and container tracking, long-term unpowered applications, and heavy equipment monitoring.

One of the standout features of the XT-4970 is its energy harvesting capability, which allows it to operate without the need for frequent battery replacements. This is especially beneficial for applications where power sources are limited or inaccessible. The device also features embedded cellular and GPS antennas, ensuring strong signal reception and accurate location tracking. The integrated high-precision GPS engine provides precise positioning information, while the 3-axis accelerometer and motion detector enable motion detection and impact monitoring.

In addition to its core features, the XT-4970 offers optional Bluetooth, ZigBee, 1-Wire, and IO capabilities, allowing for seamless integration with other devices and sensors. It also includes a 10.6 Ah backup battery, ensuring continuous operation even during power outages. With an IP67 rating, the device is resistant to dust and water, making it suitable for use in harsh environments.

Overall, the Xirgo XT-4970 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that excels in long-term, remote deployments. Its energy harvesting capabilities, dual carrier LTE support, and robust features make it an excellent choice for trailer and container tracking, long-term unpowered applications, and heavy equipment monitoring.
