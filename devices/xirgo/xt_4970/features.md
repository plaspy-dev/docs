# Xirgo - XT-4970 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Xirgo XT-4970 with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities that matter to fleet and asset operators, and how those capabilities appear and are useful within the Plaspy platform. The XT-4970 is an energy harvesting LTE smart trailer solution designed for long-term remote deployments, and this document highlights the aspects of the device relevant to tracking, monitoring, and operational oversight when paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability, behavior, and data reporting depend on the device firmware, hardware revision, optional modules installed, and the specifics of each installation. Where the XT-4970 offers optional interfaces or functionality, those options must be present and enabled in your device and firmware to appear in Plaspy. Always verify device configuration and firmware level against the manufacturer documentation for your unit.

## Feature Overview

The XT-4970 is built for long-life, low-maintenance deployments where regular battery service is impractical. In Plaspy, this tracker provides location visibility, motion awareness, and environmental durability suited to trailers, containers, and other unpowered assets. The device combines cellular connectivity, a precision GPS engine, motion sensing, and optional peripheral support to deliver practical operational telemetry.

- Energy harvesting capability for extended deployments with reduced battery replacement needs
- Dual carrier LTE support for broad cellular coverage across major US networks
- Embedded cellular and GPS antennas plus a high precision GPS engine for reliable location reporting
- Integrated 3-axis accelerometer and motion detector to report activity and impacts
- Optional interfaces such as Bluetooth, ZigBee, 1-Wire, and general IO for add‑on sensors and integrations
- IP67 enclosure rating and an integrated backup battery for resilience in harsh environments

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT-4970

- Energy harvesting technology to sustain long term remote operation
- Dual carrier LTE connectivity to support major cellular networks
- Embedded GPS antenna and a high precision GPS engine for accurate positioning
- 3-axis accelerometer and motion detector for detecting movement and impacts
- Optional Bluetooth support for local peripheral connectivity
- Optional ZigBee and 1-Wire interfaces for sensor integration where deployed
- General IO options for external signals and integrations, when present
- IP67 rated enclosure and a 10.6 Ah backup battery for dust and water resistance and extended uptime

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and presents the XT-4970 telemetry to provide fleet and asset visibility. Data shown in Plaspy depends on the device firmware and enabled options, and Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol to simplify integration. Operators can monitor location, motion events, and device health through Plaspy dashboards and reports.

- Real time and historic location plotted on maps in Plaspy for route reconstruction and asset location
- Motion and impact events translated into activity indicators and alerts where supported by device firmware
- Long term uptime and battery backup behavior visible in device status to reduce maintenance scheduling
- Optional sensor data (Bluetooth, ZigBee, 1-Wire, IO) available in Plaspy when those interfaces are installed and configured
- Plaspy supports common TCP and UDP transport modes and will detect the tracker protocol automatically for streamlined setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term trailer and container location tracking for logistics and storage yards
- Remote asset monitoring where mains power is unavailable and maintenance access is limited
- Heavy equipment monitoring at job sites with infrequent on site servicing
- Seasonal assets and equipment staged for long periods between uses
- Fleet visibility for rental or leased trailers deployed across wide geographic areas

## Feature Availability Notes

- Optional interfaces such as Bluetooth, ZigBee, 1-Wire, and IO are model options and may not be present on every hardware revision
- Energy harvesting performance and effective service life depend on installation environment, mounting, and local ambient energy conditions
- Firmware version determines which events and sensor data are reported to Plaspy; features may be added or altered in firmware updates
- Regional cellular carrier support and certification vary by device SKU and should be confirmed for your deployment area
- Verify installation details such as antenna placement and enclosure sealing to maintain IP67 performance and GPS reception
