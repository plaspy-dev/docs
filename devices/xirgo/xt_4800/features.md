# Xirgo - XT-4800 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Xirgo XT-4800 and how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It summarizes practical, non-sensitive details about the tracker, focusing on real world value for asset location, device health, and remote monitoring when paired with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation choices, and manufacturer settings. For device specific commands, full configuration options, and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The XT-4800 is designed for long term remote deployments where regular telemetry and durable operation are priorities. Its combination of self charging power, rugged sealing, and integrated radios makes it suitable for assets that are not regularly serviced or that operate in exposed environments.

- Energy harvesting with a self charging battery for extended deployments without frequent battery replacement
- IP67 sealed enclosure suitable for marine, desert, and other harsh environments
- Integrated cellular and GPS antennas for compact installations and reliable connectivity
- High precision GPS engine for accurate location reporting
- Support for TCP UDP and FTP protocols for flexible data upload
- Optional interfaces including 1 Wire Bus and Zigbee compatibility plus extensive inputs and outputs for integration with external sensors

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT-4800

- Energy harvesting system that maintains charge for long term remote operation
- Self charging battery to reduce maintenance visits for battery replacement
- IP67 rated enclosure for dust and water protection in demanding environments
- Embedded cellular and GPS antennas to simplify installation and reduce external antenna routing
- Integrated high precision GPS engine for consistent location readings
- Support for TCP UDP and FTP protocols for data transfer to tracking platforms
- Extensive inputs and outputs for connection to external sensors and asset interfaces
- Optional 1 Wire Bus and Zigbee compatibility for added integration flexibility

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and present the XT-4800 telemetry to provide location awareness, status monitoring, and historical playback for fleet and asset managers. Because the device supports common transport protocols, Plaspy can ingest messages and surface the device data in maps and reports.

- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and can accept data from the XT-4800 over standard transports
- Location fixes from the high precision GPS engine appear on Plaspy maps for real time tracking and history
- Long life operation from energy harvesting reduces gaps in data and keeps assets visible for longer periods
- IP67 durability helps ensure the device continues reporting under harsh conditions so Plaspy dashboards remain up to date
- Inputs and outputs reported by the device can be reflected in Plaspy for event visibility and simple monitoring workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of non powered assets such as trailers, containers, and equipment
- Remote asset monitoring in harsh environments including marine and desert settings
- Fleet managers needing frequent location updates without frequent battery maintenance
- Sites where compact installation is required and integrated antennas simplify deployment
- Deployments requiring integration with external sensors using available inputs and optional buses
