# Xirgo - XT-4800

![XT-4800](./logo.jpg)

The Xirgo XT-4800 is an energy harvesting device that offers long-term, remote deployments without the need to replace the self-charging battery. This makes it an ideal choice for customers who require frequent data for location, health, and monitoring of their remote assets. Compared to other asset tracking devices in the market, the XT-4800 provides more frequent data for longer periods, ensuring that you stay connected to your assets at all times.

One of the standout features of the XT-4800 is its ability to withstand various environmental factors. With an IP67 certification, this device is fully sealed and protected, making it suitable for deployment in marine and desert environments. Whether you need to track assets in dry containers, non-powered assets, or heavy equipment, the XT-4800 is up to the task.

Some of the key features of the Xirgo XT-4800 include energy harvesting capabilities, an IP67 rating for durability, embedded cellular and GPS antennas for seamless connectivity, and an integrated high-precision GPS engine for accurate location tracking. Additionally, the XT-4800 supports TCP, UDP, and FTP protocols, allowing for easy data transfer. It also offers extensive inputs and outputs, as well as optional 1-Wire Bus and Zigbee® compatibility for added flexibility.
