# Xirgo - XT47 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Xirgo XT47 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities the XT47 contributes to Plaspy-based fleet and asset management workflows, including real-time location, event reporting, and telemetry for rugged remote equipment.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For final device specifications, deployment instructions, and firmware details consult the official Xirgo documentation and your device supplier.

## Feature Overview

The XT47 is a purpose-built GPS tracker designed to bring reliable location and telemetry into Plaspy for long-term remote deployments and high-value assets. It emphasizes rugged construction, flexible I/O for telemetry and alarms, and connectivity tuned for intermittent-power or low-coverage environments.

- Real-time GPS positioning and tracking for remote assets and mobile equipment.
- Rugged enclosure and mounting options suited for outdoor and mobile installations.
- Multiple inputs and outputs for telemetry, alarm reporting, and digital signaling such as ignition monitoring.
- Connectivity and power design optimized for intermittent power and low-coverage remote sites.
- Supports anti-theft workflows and remote control actions via configured I/O when used with Plaspy.
- Scalable deployment across mixed fleets including generators, construction machinery, containers, and trailers.

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT47

- Plaspy compatible integration for centralized tracking and fleet visibility.
- Rugged housing engineered for outdoor and mobile asset protection.
- Multiple I/O channels for telemetry, alarms, and digital inputs including ignition monitoring where configured.
- Connectivity optimized for remote asset environments and intermittent power scenarios.
- GPS positioning for live tracking and historical location playback.
- Support for wired sensor telemetry and integration into Plaspy dashboards when sensors are connected.
- Optional external sensor integration such as Bluetooth sensors depending on configuration and model variant.
- Design focused on long-term field deployment and practical remote control options.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the XT47 streams location and event information into the platform so operations teams can maintain visibility and act on asset status. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and I/O events to power maps, alerts, and reports for operational oversight.

- Live location updates and historical playback available inside Plaspy maps for asset tracking and dispatch.
- I/O event reporting shows alarm conditions, digital input changes, and ignition status as actionable events.
- Telemetry from wired sensors appears in dashboards and reports when those sensors are integrated with the XT47.
- Anti-theft and control workflows such as remote immobilizer actions are supported in Plaspy when the XT47 is configured with the necessary control outputs.
- Plaspy consolidates XT47 data with other asset data to provide unified reporting and operational alerts.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols to simplify initial device onboarding and data ingestion.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long-term monitoring of mobile generator sets and equipment located at remote job sites.
- Fleet management for mixed equipment fleets including excavators, trailers, and service units.
- Anti-theft protection for high-value remote assets using geofences, tamper alerts, and remote control workflows.
- Container and trailer tracking in low-power or intermittent-connection environments.
- Telemetry-driven preventive maintenance planning when sensor data is wired into the tracker.
- Centralized visibility and dispatch coordination for dispersed assets across multiple locations.

Using the XT47 with Plaspy gives organizations centralized visibility into rugged remote assets and the ability to convert location and telemetry into operational decisions. The combination of rugged hardware, flexible I/O, and Plaspy dashboards and alerts helps reduce downtime, shorten response times for theft or tamper events, and improve maintenance planning for dispersed equipment.
