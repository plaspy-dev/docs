# Xirgo - XT25 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Xirgo XT25 and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for fleet tracking and operational monitoring. It is intended to describe the practical functions the XT25 offers when used with Plaspy and to help fleet managers, integrators, and installers understand what to expect from this Plaspy compatible GPS tracker.

Exact feature availability for any XT25 unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where the device offers optional hardware or configuration choices, behavior and telemetry exposed to Plaspy will depend on the installed options and the vehicle environment.

## Feature Overview

The XT25 is a compact plug and play vehicle tracking unit designed for light duty and passenger vehicles with adaptable options for heavy duty installations. It provides location and motion data, OBD derived signals where available, optional Bluetooth pairing, and expandability for additional I O integration. When paired with Plaspy, the XT25 can stream real time tracking and event data for mapping, alerts, and reporting across mixed fleets.

- Plug and play installation for quick deployment in passenger and light duty vehicles with hardwire options for permanent installs.
- Integrated GPS and cellular radios that stream location fixes and telemetry to Plaspy for real time tracking.
- Optional Bluetooth interface for ELD and accessory sensor pairing where supported.
- OBD protocol support to surface ignition and basic diagnostics from compatible vehicles.
- Integrated accelerometer for motion detection and harsh event reporting to support anti theft and driver behavior monitoring.

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT25

- Compact vehicle tracking unit designed for quick OBD port installation or hardwired fits.
- Integrated GPS receiver for consistent location reporting.
- Cellular connectivity optimized for North American networks including LTE Cat M1 and LTE Cat NB2 variants.
- Optional Bluetooth for ELD pairing and accessory sensor connectivity.
- OBD protocol support to expose ignition state and diagnostics when the vehicle ECU supports those channels.
- Integrated accelerometer to detect motion and harsh events for alerts and event logging.
- Optional internal battery and internal buzzer to assist with power loss scenarios and driver alerts.
- Expandable with an external I O box to add digital inputs and outputs for additional control and sensors.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The XT25 is designed to report GPS fixes, motion events, and OBD derived signals into Plaspy so fleets can monitor live locations, trigger rules, and analyze vehicle behavior. Plaspy presents incoming telemetry alongside map and event history so operators can act on real time and historical data.

- Live location updates and route playback visible in Plaspy maps and history views.
- Ignition status and basic diagnostic signals reported via OBD support to enable engine on off reporting and ignition based rules.
- Motion and harsh event alerts driven by the integrated accelerometer to support anti theft workflows and driver behavior review.
- Bluetooth paired ELD or sensor data available to Plaspy where the XT25 is configured with the optional Bluetooth interface.
- External I O expansions reflected as inputs or outputs in Plaspy when the installation includes the I O box for alarms or control functions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and route monitoring for passenger cars, vans, and light trucks with easy OBD deployments.
- Anti theft monitoring with motion alerts, geofence checks, and optional immobilizer or alarm control through external I O.
- Driver behavior and safety programs using accelerometer driven harsh event reporting and optional buzzer alerts.
- Mixed fleet rollouts where OBD and hardwire installations coexist and Plaspy aggregates telemetry across vehicles.
- ELD and accessory sensor workflows where optional Bluetooth is used to pair logging devices or external sensors.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence depends on the device firmware and hardware revision supplied by the manufacturer or integrator.
- Optional capabilities such as Bluetooth, internal battery, buzzer, and external I O require the corresponding hardware option and installation.
- OBD derived data is available only when the vehicle ECU exposes the relevant signals and the unit is installed via the OBD port or appropriate wiring.
- Regional cellular variants and carrier selection affect network compatibility; confirm the supplied model matches your region and carrier needs.
- Confirm installation choices such as plug and play OBD versus hardwire to ensure the expected telemetry is accessible.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the XT25 with Plaspy gives organizations centralized visibility into vehicle location, motion events, and OBD derived signals so they can operationalize tracking, alerts, and reporting across mixed fleets. Plaspy aggregates the XT25 telemetry into maps, event feeds, and historical reports that support dispatching, compliance, and fleet performance analysis.
