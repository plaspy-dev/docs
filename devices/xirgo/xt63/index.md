# Xirgo - XT63

![XT63](./tracker.webp)

The XT63 is a vehicle telematics GPS tracker from Xirgo designed for fleet managers and equipment owners who require dependable, real time tracking and flexible telemetry options. Intended for passenger cars, light and heavy trucks, and commercial equipment, the XT63 brings together embedded cellular and GNSS positioning with optional Bluetooth support and vehicle interface options to deliver continuous location and event data.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT63 is well suited for integration into Plaspy-powered monitoring and fleet management workflows. Its support for vehicle telemetry interfaces such as OBD and JBUS, configurable communication options, and practical I/O for events and actuator control make it a practical choice when you need geofence notifications, event-driven alerts, and structured vehicle data routed into Plaspy dashboards and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker providing real time location and fleet management readiness out of the box
- Optional OBD and JBUS support to capture engine and diagnostic telemetry where available
- Instant geofence notifications and motion based alerts for anti theft and activity monitoring
- Embedded cellular and GPS with optional Bluetooth to support sensor extensions and simplify deployments
- Practical I O and event inputs for ignition, door, and alarm status monitoring and actuator control
- Flexible telemetry transport options and configurable data packets for integration with backend platforms

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the XT63 streams location and vehicle telemetry to the Plaspy platform so fleet operators can monitor assets, respond to events, and generate operational reports. Plaspy ingests the XT63 data to provide real time visibility, alerting, and historical insight without requiring custom middleware.

- Real time location updates and vehicle telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for continuous visibility
- Event driven alerts such as ignition, door open, and alarm triggers enable timely notifications and response
- Geofence events and motion alerts support perimeter monitoring and anti theft workflows within Plaspy
- Engine and fuel related telemetry from OBD and JBUS connections feed into Plaspy reports and diagnostics views when available
- Device outputs can be used for remote actuator control workflows coordinated through Plaspy policies
- Optional Bluetooth sensor data can supplement vehicle telemetry for cargo, temperature, or proximity monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for routing, asset visibility, and operational oversight across mixed vehicle types
- Anti theft and recovery with motion detection, geofence violations, and event driven alerts
- Fuel monitoring and diagnostics when connected to vehicle telemetry interfaces to support cost control
- Remote control and security features such as starter cut or immobilizer interaction where permitted
- Cold chain and asset monitoring using optional Bluetooth sensors for temperature or door status reporting

The XT63 offers a balanced set of capabilities that align with common requirements for Plaspy deployments: reliable position reporting, vehicle interface options, configurable telemetry, and practical I O for event monitoring. Its ability to capture engine and diagnostic data alongside location makes it useful for operators who need both operational insight and security features delivered to a centralized platform like Plaspy.
