# Xirgo - XT-2400

![XT-2400](./logo.jpg)

The Xirgo XT-2400 is a versatile plug and play OBD II device designed for passenger and light-duty vehicles. This GPS tracker directly monitors and communicates vital vehicle information, making it an ideal solution for various applications.

One of the key features of the XT-2400 is its scriptable capability, allowing it to be customized to meet precise solution requirements. This flexibility makes it suitable for a wide range of applications, including mobile resource management, aftermarket automotive solutions, consumer solutions, and driver behavior monitoring and modification.

The XT-2400 is equipped with embedded cellular and GPS antennas, ensuring reliable and accurate communication and location tracking. It also features an integrated high-precision GPS engine, providing precise location information. Additionally, the tracker includes an accelerometer, enabling the detection of vehicle movement and acceleration.

With its integrated OBDII interface, the XT-2400 allows for the monitoring of various parameters, such as location, speed, VIN, and ignition status. It can also provide diagnostic fault codes \(DFCs / DTCs\), allowing for efficient vehicle maintenance and troubleshooting.

Overall, the Xirgo XT-2400 is a powerful and versatile GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features and applications. Whether you need to track and manage a fleet of vehicles or monitor driver behavior, the XT-2400 is a reliable and efficient solution.
