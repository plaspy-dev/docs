# Xirgo - LX40

<div className="device-logo">
![LX40](./tracker.webp)
</div>

The Xirgo LX40 is a next generation vehicle GPS tracker and telematics platform developed as the evolution of the XG37 series. Built for fleet and vehicle centric deployments, the LX40 offers a scalable and configurable solution for tracking, telemetry collection, and device lifecycle management. Its design positions it as a practical choice for mixed fleets and retrofit projects by offering flexible install options, programmable I O, and CANBUS capable variants where richer vehicle data is available.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LX40 integrates tightly with Plaspy to deliver real time tracking, advanced telemetry, and remote device management. With a professional scripting engine, extensive telemetry objects, and OTA update support, the LX40 can be configured to report the vehicle and driver events that matter to your operations while Plaspy centralizes those feeds into dashboards, alerts, and reports for fleet visibility and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible platform for real time tracking and centralized fleet management
- Professional scripting engine exposing 500 plus controllable parameters and objects for custom workflows
- CANBUS capable variants to capture vehicle telemetry where supported by the vehicle
- Programmable I O and multiple harness options to support retrofit and mixed fleet installations
- Bluetooth Low Energy 5.4 support for wireless sensors and driver ID accessories
- Remote device management and over the air update capability to keep devices current
- Advanced driver ID and geofence features for accountability and automated event handling

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, LX40 devices forward location, driver events, and telemetry to the Plaspy platform where data is normalized, visualized, and used to trigger alerts and reports. Plaspy provides centralized device management and operational tools so fleet teams can monitor performance, respond to exceptions, and maintain devices remotely.

- Real time location and telemetry streams routed to Plaspy dashboards and APIs for visibility
- Driver ID and geofence events forwarded to Plaspy for automated alerts and driver scoring
- CANBUS telemetry from capable variants fed into Plaspy reports and maintenance workflows
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware updates managed through Plaspy compatible device tools
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons integrated as supplemental inputs for temperature cargo or driver presence monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with real time tracking, driver ID, and geofence alerts to improve routing and compliance
- Retrofit and mixed fleet installations where configurable I O and multiple harness options are required
- Vehicle telemetry and diagnostics using CANBUS capable variants to inform maintenance and utilization reporting
- Sensor based monitoring combining BLE accessories with location data for cargo or environmental oversight
- Driver accountability and automated event workflows using scripting rules and Plaspy alerts

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LX40 is well suited to organizations that need a configurable telematics platform coupled with a centralized fleet management system. Its scripting engine and extensive parameter set let integrators tailor device behavior and event handling to specific operational requirements, while Plaspy centralizes and acts on those data streams through dashboards, alerts, and reporting. Remote device management and OTA updates help reduce operational overhead and support scalable deployments across diverse vehicle types.
