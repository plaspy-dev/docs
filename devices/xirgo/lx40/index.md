# Xirgo - LX40

![LX40](./tracker.webp)

The LX40 is a next-generation vehicle GPS tracker and telematics platform built as the evolution of the XG37 series. Designed for fleets and vehicle-centric deployments, the LX40 delivers a robust, scalable, and customizable solution that integrates tightly with Plaspy for reliable Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and advanced telemetry. Its flexible install options, programmable I/O and CANBUS-capable variants make LX40 a practical choice for mixed fleets and retrofit projects.

With a professional scripting engine, extensive telemetry objects, and remote device management, the LX40 gives integrators and fleet managers granular control over device behavior and communication. When paired with Plaspy, LX40 enables fleet management teams to collect rich vehicle data, act on geofence and driver-ID events, and maintain devices remotely via OTA updates — all while supporting Bluetooth sensors for supplemental monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible platform for real-time tracking and centralized fleet management.
- Powerful scripting language exposing 500+ controllable parameters/objects for custom workflows and behavior.
- Variants with robust CANBUS support to capture vehicle telemetry and engine data where available.
- Programmable I/O and multiple harness/install options to fit diverse vehicle types and retrofits.
- Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) 5.4 for wireless connectivity to sensors, beacons, and driver-ID accessories.
- Advanced driver ID and geofence functions for driver accountability and automated alerts.
- Remote device management and OTA update capabilities to keep fleets current without physical recalls.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LX40 integrates with Plaspy to deliver actionable telematics and maintain device lifecycle remotely. Data from the LX40’s sensors, programmable I/O and CANBUS interfaces is sent to Plaspy where it is normalized, displayed, and used to trigger rules, alerts and reports. Plaspy-compatible connectivity ensures that location, driver events and telemetry update in near real-time so fleet managers can respond quickly to incidents, exceptions, and operating inefficiencies.

- Real-time tracking and telemetry updates routed to Plaspy dashboards and APIs.
- Driver ID and geofence event forwarding for automated driver scoring and location-based alerts.
- CANBUS telemetry \(where supported by the variant\) feeds engine and sensor data into Plaspy reports.
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware updates managed through Plaspy-compatible device management tools.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons integrated for auxiliary monitoring \(temperature, loads, driver presence\).

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real-time tracking, driver ID, and geofence alerts to improve routing and compliance.
- Anti-theft and recovery workflows: detect unauthorized movement and integrate with immobilizer workflows via programmable I/O and Plaspy alerts.
- Vehicle telemetry and diagnostics: capture CANBUS engine data where supported to feed maintenance and utilization reports.
- Sensor-based monitoring: use BLE sensors for temperature, cargo status or driver presence data alongside location updates.
- Retrofit and mixed-fleet installations: multiple harness options and programmable inputs make LX40 suitable for a variety of vehicle types.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the LX40 with Plaspy gives organizations a flexible GPS tracker platform that balances deep configurability with enterprise manageability. The LX40’s scripting engine and extensive parameter set allow installers and integrators to tailor data collection and event handling to precise operational needs, while Plaspy centralizes those data streams into actionable dashboards, reports and alerts. Remote management and OTA updates reduce downtime and total cost of ownership, and BLE 5.4 support extends telemetry with wireless sensors. For fleets that require scalable telemetry, customized I/O, and dependable Plaspy compatible real-time tracking, the LX40 offers a practical, upgradeable foundation.
