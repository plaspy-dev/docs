# Xirgo - XT-6300

![XT-6300](./logo.jpg)

The Xirgo XT-6300 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for a wide range of applications. With its OBDII and JBUS communication capabilities, this device is perfect for tracking and telematics applications. It also offers equipment diagnostics, tracking, monitoring, control, and maintenance functionalities, making it a valuable tool for businesses in various industries.

One of the standout features of the XT-6300 is its script-enabled firmware, which allows for customization and flexibility in tracking and monitoring operations. The device is equipped with embedded cellular and GPS antennas, ensuring reliable and accurate communication and location tracking. The integrated high-precision GPS engine provides precise location data, while the 3-axis accelerometer and motion detector enable advanced motion sensing capabilities.

In addition to its core features, the XT-6300 offers optional OBDII and JBUS interfaces, allowing for seamless integration with vehicle diagnostics systems. It also has optional Bluetooth® functionality, enabling wireless connectivity with compatible devices. The device supports Garmin FMI, GPIO, and 1-Wire bus interface, further expanding its capabilities for various applications.

With the XDMI device management platform enabled, users can easily manage and monitor the XT-6300, ensuring efficient and effective tracking and telematics operations. Whether you need to track vehicles, monitor equipment, comply with federal Hours of Service regulations, or perform diagnostics and maintenance, the Xirgo XT-6300 is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that can meet your needs.
