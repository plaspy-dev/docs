# Xirgo - XT53 Features

This page provides public, non sensitive feature context for using the Xirgo XT53 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the XT53’s practical capabilities for long term asset protection and day to day visibility and explains how those capabilities map into Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and operational workflows.

Exact feature availability for a specific XT53 unit can vary based on firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. Where precise device behavior matters for deployment or integration, consult the manufacturer documentation and verify device configuration before wide scale rollout.

## Feature Overview

The XT53 is an ultra compact GPS tracker built for long service life and low maintenance deployments. It is intended to provide periodic location updates and basic status reporting over cellular networks so assets remain visible in Plaspy without frequent field service.

- Up to seven years of battery life for reduced maintenance cycles and long deployments
- Ultra compact form factor that fits on small equipment, pallets, and containers
- Cellular uplink to transmit position reports and status updates into Plaspy
- Rugged construction for handling common field use and deployed asset scenarios
- Low touch operation with scheduled check ins to preserve battery life
- Designed to support asset protection and recovery workflows when integrated with Plaspy

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT53

- Long battery life up to seven years as stated by the manufacturer for low maintenance deployments
- Compact and discreet physical form factor suitable for small equipment and pallet mounting
- GPS location reporting for asset position awareness
- Cellular connectivity for sending uplinks to a tracking platform
- Ruggedized build to withstand typical handling in storage and transit
- Power efficient scheduled reporting to balance visibility and battery longevity
- Compatibility with Plaspy to feed location and basic telemetry into monitoring and reporting tools

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an XT53 is used with Plaspy, its periodic location and status uplinks become visible inside the Plaspy platform for mapping, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy ingests device reports and presents them as map positions, events, and exportable history so teams can track deployed assets over time.

- Device position reports appear on Plaspy maps for real time or near real time location awareness depending on the configured check in interval
- Battery status and periodic health reports are available to help schedule maintenance and battery replacement
- Configurable reporting intervals let organizations trade off update frequency and multi year battery life
- Historical position logs and event records are available in Plaspy for audits and recovery workflows
- Plaspy detects and adapts to supported tracker protocols so XT53 uplinks integrate into dashboards and alerts

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term pallet and container tracking during storage and transit
- Inventory monitoring for small equipment that moves between sites
- Anti theft readiness and recovery workflows using discreet placement and persistent visibility
- Seasonal or infrequently accessed assets where maintenance windows are limited
- Deployed asset registries for organizations needing low maintenance telemetry

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version and firmware configuration can change which reports and intervals are available from a specific device
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may alter radio support or battery characteristics for particular units
- Installation and mounting practices affect signal reception and reporting reliability in the field
- Reporting interval and power settings directly influence observed battery life in real deployments
- Always confirm device capabilities and supported reports with the manufacturer documentation for the unit you plan to deploy

Pairing the XT53 with Plaspy gives organizations a straightforward way to turn long life, low maintenance trackers into operational visibility. Plaspy centralizes location uplinks and status reports, enabling map based monitoring, event driven alerts, and historical reporting that support asset protection, inventory control, and recovery workflows.
