# Xirgo - XT53

![XT53](./tracker.webp)

The XT53 is an ultra-compact GPS tracker designed for long-term asset protection and everyday visibility of dispersed equipment. Built as a low-maintenance cellular device, the XT53 emphasizes extended service life with up to seven years of battery life, making it well suited to small equipment, pallets, containers, and other assets that need to remain online for years with minimal intervention.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT53 integrates into Plaspy's tracking and reporting environment to provide streamlined location awareness and secure reporting. That compatibility makes the XT53 a practical option for organizations seeking simple asset-level position checks or scalable devices to support broader fleet and inventory monitoring programs within Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Up to seven years of battery life for minimal maintenance and fewer battery replacements.
- Ultra-compact form factor suitable for small equipment, pallets, and containers.
- Cellular connectivity to reliably send location and status information to Plaspy.
- Rugged design intended for real-world handling and field conditions.
- Low-touch operation with scheduled check-ins to extend service life.
- Built to feed location and status data into Plaspy for tracking, alerts, and reports.
- Suited to asset protection workflows where discreet mounting and long runtime are important.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The XT53 uses cellular uplinks to transmit location updates and periodic status reports to Plaspy. Once the device is registered in Plaspy, its position reports and check-in events are presented on maps, incorporated into alerts, and stored for historical analysis based on configured reporting intervals.

- Real-time or near real-time location updates routed to Plaspy for map visualization and live monitoring.
- Battery and periodic status reports surfaced in Plaspy to help plan maintenance and reduce downtime.
- Configurable check-in intervals to balance continuous visibility with multi year battery life.
- Event reporting and historical position logs available in Plaspy for audits and recovery workflows.
- Integration into Plaspy dashboards and alerting to support anti-theft and asset recovery processes.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long-term pallet and container tracking during storage and transit without frequent battery changes.
- Small equipment inventory management for tools and compact machinery that move between sites.
- Anti-theft monitoring and recovery readiness with discreet placement and long runtime.
- Seasonal or infrequently accessed assets that require long battery life and low maintenance.
- Fleet support for deployed assets needing occasional position checks rather than constant telemetry.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The XT53 is a practical choice when low maintenance, longevity, and a compact footprint are primary requirements. Combined with Plaspy, the device becomes part of a managed tracking ecosystem where location uplinks and status reports are converted into actionable maps, alerts, and reports to support asset protection and operational oversight.
