# Xirgo - XT53

![XT53](./tracker.webp)

The XT53 is an ultra-compact GPS tracker designed for long-term asset protection and day-to-day visibility. Built as a low-maintenance cellular tracker, the XT53 is Plaspy compatible and engineered to deliver extended service life—up to seven years of battery life—making it an ideal choice for tracking small equipment, pallets, containers, and other deployed assets that must remain online for years with minimal intervention.

Compact and rugged, the XT53 integrates into Plaspy for streamlined telemetry, location awareness, and secure reporting. Whether you need a simple asset-level GPS tracker for sporadic position checks or a scalable device to support fleet management programs, the XT53 emphasizes reliability, low total cost of ownership, and seamless compatibility with Plaspy’s real-time tracking and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Up to seven years of battery life — minimizes maintenance and battery replacement cycles for long-term asset deployments.
- Ultra-compact form factor — easy to mount on small equipment, pallets, and containers without adding bulk.
- Cellular connectivity for dependable uplink to Plaspy — supports location and telemetry reporting over the mobile network.
- Rugged construction for real-world use — designed to withstand common handling and field conditions.
- Designed for low-touch operation — scheduled check-ins and power-efficient reporting reduce operational overhead.
- Plaspy compatible — built to feed location and status data into Plaspy for real-time tracking, alerts, and historical reporting.
- Ideal for asset protection and anti-theft workflows — long battery life and discreet mounting reduce risk and maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The XT53 connects over the cellular network to send location updates and basic telemetry to Plaspy. Once paired in Plaspy, the device’s position reports, battery status, and check-in events are available in real time \(or near real time depending on configured reporting intervals\). Plaspy ingests the XT53’s uplinks and converts them into map positions, events, and exportable reports that support fleet management, anti-theft workflows, and long-term asset inventories.

- Real-time tracking and location updates routed to Plaspy for map visualization and live monitoring.
- Battery life and periodic status reporting to help schedule maintenance and reduce downtime.
- Configurable check-in intervals to balance real-time visibility and multi-year battery life.
- Event reporting and historical position logs in Plaspy for audits and recovery scenarios.
- Seamless integration into Plaspy dashboards and alerts to support anti-theft and asset recovery processes.
