# Xirgo - XT-3200

![XT-3200](./logo.jpg)

The Xirgo XT-3200 is a plug and play OBD II GPS device built for passenger and light duty vehicles. Designed as an aftermarket tracker, it provides location awareness and vehicle parameter monitoring by leveraging an integrated OBD II interface, an embedded cellular antenna, and an accelerometer. These elements make the XT-3200 suitable for basic vehicle monitoring and driver behavior observation without extensive installation work.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT-3200 can feed vehicle location and behavior data into the Plaspy fleet platform to support monitoring, reporting, and operational workflows. Its straightforward form factor and focus on ignition status and driving events make it a practical choice for organizations and individuals who want quick deployment and visibility through Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II connection for easy aftermarket installation in passenger and light duty vehicles
- Integrated accelerometer to detect driving events such as harsh braking and rapid acceleration
- Embedded cellular antenna for continuous communication with fleet platforms
- Monitors ignition status and vehicle run/idle behavior
- Suited for driver behavior monitoring and modification programs
- Practical choice for both fleet managers and consumer vehicle tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the XT-3200 supplies vehicle and driving event information that Plaspy uses to provide real time visibility, alerts, and historical reporting. Integration focuses on bringing concise, operational data into Plaspy so fleets and managers can act on location and driver behavior insights.

- Real time location visibility and basic trip history inside Plaspy
- Driver event reporting for harsh braking, rapid acceleration, and excessive idling
- Ignition and trip start stop status to support route and utilization reports
- Alerts and notifications configurable within Plaspy based on driving events or ignition changes
- Aggregated reporting for safety coaching, utilization analysis, and operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet safety programs and driver coaching for light duty vehicle fleets
- Aftermarket tracking for businesses that need quick deployment without vehicle modification
- Insurance telematics initiatives that rely on driving behavior insights
- Consumer vehicle monitoring for theft deterrence and personal vehicle oversight
- Rental or pooled vehicle monitoring where ignition and usage tracking are important

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The XT-3200 is a pragmatic option for organizations that need a low friction, OBD II based tracker for passenger and light duty vehicles. Its plug and play nature reduces installation time and makes it easier to scale deployments across an existing fleet. When paired with Plaspy, the data from the XT-3200 becomes part of a broader visibility and reporting workflow that supports safety, utilization, and operational decisions.
