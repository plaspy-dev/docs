# Xirgo - XT21

<div className="device-logo">
![XT21](./tracker.webp)
</div>

The XT21 is a cost effective GPS tracker designed for remote asset telematics. It combines an integrated GPS engine with LTE cellular connectivity to deliver dependable real time location updates and essential telemetry for trailers, portable equipment, and other high value remote assets where power may be intermittent or minimal. Its compact form and focus on essential signals make it suitable for deployments that prioritize low power draw and straightforward monitoring.

As a device that is fully compatible with Plaspy, the XT21 can feed position data and basic event telemetry directly into the Plaspy platform for unified fleet and asset oversight. The tracker’s two all purpose digital inputs and one internal analog input for battery voltage provide the core signals Plaspy needs to generate alerts, log asset status, and support reporting workflows without unnecessary complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Built for remote assets with a compact low power design that supports intermittent power environments.
- Integrated GPS positioning combined with LTE connectivity for consistent location updates.
- Two all purpose digital inputs for basic event detection and status reporting.
- Internal analog input for continuous battery voltage monitoring and low voltage alerting.
- Cost effective approach focused on essential telematics for trailers and portable equipment.
- Simple footprint and feature set that reduce installation effort and ongoing management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the XT21 transmits its location and telemetry so that asset data is visible alongside other fleet information in a single platform. Plaspy ingests the XT21’s position, digital input events, and battery voltage readings to enable live monitoring, alerting, and historical reporting for operational decision making.

- Live location tracking and historical playback on Plaspy maps and timelines.
- Mapping of digital input events to Plaspy notifications for door, tamper, or auxiliary event workflows.
- Battery voltage telemetry routed to Plaspy for automated low battery alerts and uptime analysis.
- Geofence and status based alerts driven by XT21 location and input signals.
- Inclusion of XT21 data in Plaspy reports and dashboards to support utilization and security reviews.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking to detect movement and maintain location history.
- Monitoring high value portable equipment such as pumps, tools, or leased machinery.
- Rental fleet oversight to confirm returns and detect tampering or unexpected use.
- Remote site asset monitoring where mains power is not guaranteed and minimal telemetry is required.

The XT21 provides the core telemetry most organizations need to monitor distributed assets without adding unnecessary complexity. Its combination of location, digital inputs, and battery voltage monitoring aligns well with Plaspy’s strengths in map based visibility, alerting, and fleet level reporting. For operations that require reliable position updates and straightforward event signals from a low power device, the XT21 is a practical option.
