# Xirgo - XT21

![XT21](./tracker.webp)

The XT21 is a cost-effective GPS tracker designed for remote asset telematics and is fully Plaspy compatible for seamless fleet management and asset monitoring. Built around an integrated GPS engine and LTE cellular connectivity, XT21 delivers dependable real-time tracking and basic telemetry for trailers, portable equipment, and other high-value remote assets where power may be intermittent or minimal.

With two all-purpose digital inputs and one internal analog input to monitor battery voltage, the XT21 provides the essential signals Plaspy needs to detect events, log asset status, and trigger alerts. Its compact, low-power design makes the XT21 an ideal GPS tracker for organizations that need reliable location data, anti-theft alerts, and remote asset health monitoring without complex installation.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible out of the box: plug telemetry into your existing Plaspy dashboard for instant visibility and reporting.
- Real-time tracking over LTE: consistent, low-latency position updates for remote assets where cellular coverage is available.
- Low-power design for intermittent power environments—suitable for trailers, portable equipment, and field assets.
- Two all-purpose digital inputs for event detection \(door open, motion, keyed ignition, tamper alerts when wired\).
- Internal analog input for direct battery voltage monitoring—track asset power state and receive low-voltage alerts.
- Cost-effective telematics solution focused on essential location and telemetry data without unnecessary complexity.
- Compact form factor tailored to asset installations where space and power are limited.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The XT21 sends GPS position and telemetry over LTE to Plaspy, where the device data is unified with other fleet and asset sources. Plaspy ingests the XT21’s location, digital input events, and battery voltage readings to provide live maps, historical playback, alerts, and telematics reports that support operational decisions and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: position fixes are transmitted via LTE to Plaspy for live monitoring and historical logs.
- Event detection via digital inputs: wire the two all-purpose inputs to sense door open/close, auxiliary alarms, keyed ignition, or simple motion sensors; Plaspy maps these inputs to events and notifications.
- Battery voltage monitoring: the internal analog input continuously reports battery voltage so Plaspy can generate low-battery alerts and uptime analytics.
- Alerting and rules: Plaspy can trigger geofence alerts, inactivity warnings, or tamper notices based on XT21 inputs and location data.
- Integrate with other telematics feeds: combine XT21 location and telemetry with fuel monitoring, BLE sensor streams, or other device data in Plaspy for richer insights.
