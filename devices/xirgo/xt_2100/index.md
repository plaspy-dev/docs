# Xirgo - XT-2100

<div className="device-logo">
![XT-2100](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Xirgo XT-2100 is a compact, fully integrated GPS tracker designed for mobile and remote asset monitoring and control. It combines an integrated GPS engine with a cellular GPRS modem to provide continuous position updates, direction, and speed information. Its small form factor and built in antennas make it suitable for discreet placement in vehicles and other mobile assets. The device also includes general purpose digital inputs, an internal analog input for monitoring battery voltage, and a digital output that can be used to drive external relays.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT-2100 can deliver location and status data into Plaspy for fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Its support for common communication methods and over the air configuration and firmware updates means the tracker can be integrated into a managed tracking workflow. Plaspy can use the incoming data from the XT-2100 to provide real time visibility, alerts, and reporting for fleet and asset management needs.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated GPS engine and GPRS cellular modem for continuous position reporting
- Compact design with integrated antennas for discreet installation in tight spaces
- Provides location, speed, and direction data for vehicle and asset visibility
- Two general purpose digital inputs and one internal analog input for basic monitoring
- Digital output available to drive ignition or other relay control
- Supports over the air configuration and firmware updates for remote management
- Quad band cellular compatibility for broad geographic deployment

## How It Works with Plaspy

The XT-2100 can feed Plaspy with frequent and consistent telemetry so teams gain clear situational awareness across vehicles and assets. Once the device is configured to communicate with Plaspy, location and status messages become part of Plaspy's tracking ecosystem, enabling visualization, alerts, and historical reporting.

- Real time location and movement data appear in Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Digital input changes and analog voltage readings can be used to trigger alerts and events
- Plaspy can generate reports and playback trips based on XT-2100 position history
- Alerts for speed, geofence entry or exit, and other conditions can be configured in Plaspy
- Remote configuration and firmware capabilities reduce the need for physical device handling
