# Xirgo - XT-4700 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Xirgo XT-4700 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities you can expect to use within Plaspy for location awareness, asset monitoring, and periodic status reporting. Where relevant the XT-4700G variant is referenced when features are specific to that model.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, model variant, and installation method. Some features listed here are optional or present on specific variants, so verify the configuration and firmware state of your device before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The XT-4700 family is a rugged, self-contained cellular modem with an integrated GPS engine designed for outdoor asset tracking. It emphasizes low power consumption for long-term monitoring of remote or infrequently serviced assets, and its weatherproof enclosure supports use in harsh environments.

- Integrated high precision GPS engine for reliable location reporting in challenging conditions
- Low power design with an advanced power management algorithm to enable periodic reporting while conserving battery
- Rugged weatherproof enclosure rated for outdoor use with integrated mounting options for easy installation
- Embedded cellular and GPS antennas to simplify deployment and maintain a compact footprint
- Optional accelerometer and motion detector on supported variants for movement awareness
- Internal rechargeable battery option available on some variants for prolonged off-grid operation

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT-4700

- Integrated high precision GPS engine for consistent position fixes across environments
- Embedded cellular and GPS antennas to reduce external wiring complexity
- Low power sleep mode with sub milliamp standby current enabling periodic health and location reporting
- 32 bit microprocessor with a dedicated power management algorithm for efficient operation
- Rugged IP66 rated enclosure with integrated mounting flange for outdoor and industrial deployments
- Rechargeable internal battery available on specific variants to support off grid monitoring
- Optional accelerometer and motion detector on supported models to detect activity and wake reporting
- Variant-specific hardware options and mounting choices to suit containers, trailers, and similar assets

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an XT-4700 device is connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s core capabilities translate into practical monitoring and operational visibility within the platform. Plaspy consumes periodic location and device health messages to present asset status and history.

- Location updates appear on Plaspy maps and historical tracks for asset movement analysis
- Periodic health and battery status reports help Plaspy signal offline or degraded devices
- Motion or accelerometer events from supported variants can be surfaced as activity indicators in Plaspy
- Rugged design and integrated antennas reduce installation complexity and improve uptime reported to Plaspy
- Long sleep and low power reporting extend intervals between maintenance visits while still providing visibility in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Container tracking where durable weatherproofing and long term monitoring are required
- Trailer fleet visibility to confirm asset location and movement between depots
- Motorcycle or small vehicle tracking where compact rugged hardware is beneficial
- Monitoring of high value remote assets where low power consumption reduces service intervals
- Intermittent asset checks where periodic reporting preserves battery life while keeping assets visible
