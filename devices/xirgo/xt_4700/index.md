# Xirgo - XT-4700

<div className="device-logo">
![XT-4700](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Xirgo XT-4700 is a rugged, self contained cellular wireless modem with an integrated GPS engine designed for outdoor asset tracking. Built for monitoring high value assets such as containers, trailers, and other remote equipment, the XT-4700 emphasizes durability and long term operation in challenging environments. Its enclosure meets environmental testing standards and an IP66 rating, and the device is offered with embedded antennas, a rechargeable battery, and optional motion detection features.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT-4700 can feed location and status information into Plaspy to support visibility and operational oversight. Its low power design and periodic reporting capabilities make it well suited to Plaspy deployments that need reliable remote updates and extended field life for assets where power availability is limited. Plaspy users can add XT-4700 units to their asset lists and leverage the platform for centralized monitoring and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged outdoor housing with IP66 level protection for harsh environments
- Integrated high precision GPS engine for reliable location data
- Self contained cellular modem with embedded antennas for simplified deployment
- Designed for low power operation with advanced power management for periodic reporting
- Rechargeable battery capacity suitable for extended remote use
- Optional accelerometer and motion detector for movement based reporting
- Integrated mounting flange and multiple mounting options for versatile installation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the XT-4700 provides periodic location and status reports that feed directly into the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility and analysis. Plaspy can use this incoming data to present real time asset positions, support alerts, and generate operational reports across fleets and remote assets.

- Display live and recent location updates from XT-4700 devices on Plaspy maps
- Receive movement and status alerts using the device motion detection or periodic health reports
- Archive location history for playback and route review in Plaspy reporting tools
- Include XT-4700 assets in dashboard views and operational reports for fleet oversight
- Configure notification rules in Plaspy to highlight asset exceptions or status changes
