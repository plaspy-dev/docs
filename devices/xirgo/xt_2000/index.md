# Xirgo - XT-2000

<div className="device-logo">
![XT-2000](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Xirgo XT-2000 is a plug and play OBD II modem with an integrated GPS engine designed for passenger and light duty vehicles. Its compact form factor and J1962 style connector make it a straightforward option for fleet managers and aftermarket deployments that need vehicle location, speed, and OBD II data without a complex installation process. The device is positioned for applications such as mobile resource management, consumer telematics, driver behavior monitoring, and aftermarket automotive services.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the XT-2000 can forward vehicle and location telemetry into Plaspy for visualization, alerts, and reporting. Because it exposes key vehicle parameters available through the OBD II interface and includes a high sensitivity GPS engine plus an accelerometer for motion detection, the XT-2000 is well suited to provide the type of data fleets and service providers commonly consume in Plaspy for operational oversight and analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II modem that powers through the vehicle port for quick deployment in passenger and light duty vehicles
- Integrated high precision GPS engine and embedded antennas for reliable location and speed reporting
- Built in accelerometer to support motion detection and basic driver behavior signals
- Access to OBD II parameters including VIN, ignition status, vehicle speed, and diagnostic fault codes for vehicle health insights
- Support for common data transport methods and over the air firmware updates to keep devices current
- Compact design that reduces installation time and lowers deployment cost across large fleets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the XT-2000 streams its vehicle and location data into the Plaspy platform where it can be combined with maps, alerts, and reports to support daily operations and longer term analysis. Plaspy surfaces the device telemetry so fleet managers can turn raw inputs into actionable information.

- Real time location and speed tracking displayed on Plaspy maps for visibility into vehicle movements
- Vehicle diagnostics and fault code reporting from the OBD II interface exposed for maintenance and triage
- Motion and driving event visibility using accelerometer and ignition state to support driver behavior monitoring
- Configurable alerts and notifications in Plaspy for events such as ignition on off, diagnostic alerts, and movement outside scheduled windows
- Historical reporting and exportable data for fleet performance analysis and operational reviews

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for light duty and passenger vehicle fleets requiring quick OBD based deployment
- Aftermarket telematics for rental, leasing, or third party fleet management services
- Driver behavior monitoring and coaching programs using motion detection and ignition events
- Vehicle health monitoring and early detection of diagnostic fault codes to support maintenance planning
- Consumer oriented tracking and security services that benefit from a compact plug and play device

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The XT-2000 is a practical choice for organizations that need a low friction way to collect vehicle location and OBD II data. Its plug and play design reduces installation effort and cost, which is helpful for large scale rollouts or deployments where vehicle downtime must be minimized. The combination of integrated GPS, accelerometer, and access to OBD II parameters provides a useful dataset for core fleet management tasks.
