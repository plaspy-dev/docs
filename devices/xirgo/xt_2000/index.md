# Xirgo - XT-2000

![XT-2000](./logo.jpg)

The Xirgo XT-2000 OBD II modem with integrated GPS is a versatile plug and play device designed for passenger and light-duty vehicles. It is an ideal solution for various applications such as mobile resource management, aftermarket automotive, consumer solutions, and driver behavior monitoring and modification.

The XT-2000 is built on a technology platform that allows Xirgo to develop customized telematics devices quickly. It communicates vital information from vehicles using the OBD II protocol and cellular networks. With its optional integrated GPS engine, cellular and GPS antennas, and integrated OBDII interface, the XT-2000 provides fleet managers with the ultimate solution for monitoring location, speed, and parameters available on the OBD port of vehicles.

Installation of the XT-2000 is quick and easy, thanks to its integrated J1962 connector and compact design powered through the OBD port. This eliminates the need for complex installation procedures, reducing costs significantly. The device supports TCP, UDP, FTP, and over-the-air firmware updates, ensuring that it stays up to date with the latest features and enhancements.

With its highly sensitive GPS engine, integrated GPS antenna, and support for multiple OBD II protocols, the XT-2000 is compatible with a wide range of vehicles available in North America. It also features an accelerometer for advanced motion detection capabilities. The XT-2000 exemplifies Xirgo Technologies' commitment to a flexible design-to-manufacture model, enabling the rapid development of customized products for various M2M and telematics applications.

### Key Features:

- Embedded cellular and GPS antennas
- Integrated high-precision GPS engine
- Accelerometer for motion detection
- Integrated OBDII interface for monitoring location, speed, VIN, ignition status, and more
- Support for Diagnostic Fault Codes \(DFCs / DTCs\)
