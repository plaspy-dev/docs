# Xirgo - XT49 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Xirgo XT49 and how the device operates when used with Plaspy. It describes the practical capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy dashboards and reports, focusing on non-sensitive, user-facing details of the tracker and its integration with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. Confirm device-specific details, regional variants, and the latest firmware behavior with the manufacturer documentation before planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The XT49 is designed for long-term, low-maintenance deployments where regular service access is limited. It combines solar energy harvesting and LTE connectivity in a sealed, rugged enclosure to provide continuous GPS-based location reporting and operational status updates to fleet and asset management platforms such as Plaspy.

- Solar energy harvesting to extend deployment intervals and reduce field visits.
- LTE connectivity for reliable, near real-time location and telemetry reporting.
- Fully sealed rugged enclosure suited for marine and desert environments.
- Intended for containers, long‑haul trailers, and other remote assets with limited access.
- Sends operational status including battery and charge information for maintenance planning.

## Core Features of Xirgo - XT49

- Integrated solar energy harvesting to support extended unattended operation.
- LTE data connectivity for timely transmission of location and telemetry to Plaspy.
- GPS based location tracking for asset visibility and historical route reporting.
- Rugged, fully sealed enclosure engineered for harsh environmental conditions.
- Low maintenance emphasis to reduce visits and overall operating cost for remote fleets.
- Designed to forward operational status such as battery and charging information to backend platforms.
- Optimized for use on containers, trailers, and remote equipment where access is intermittent.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy, the XT49 forwards location updates and operational telemetry so operators can monitor assets from a single platform. Plaspy ingests the device data to present live locations, historical tracks, and status information useful for maintenance and security workflows.

- Real-time location updates and historical tracks visible in Plaspy.
- Battery and solar charge status reported into Plaspy to support maintenance scheduling.
- Movement and geofence alerts available for anti-theft monitoring and rapid response.
- Event reporting and history to support compliance, audits, and operational review.
- Configurable reporting intervals to balance battery life and tracking resolution in Plaspy views.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long‑haul trailer tracking where reduced maintenance visits lower operating costs.
- Container monitoring in marine terminals and desert logistics corridors.
- Remote equipment and asset monitoring for deployments with limited on-site servicing.
- Anti-theft monitoring using movement and geofence alerts integrated into Plaspy workflows.
- Seasonal or long-duration field projects requiring extended unattended operation.
- Large scale fleet programs where low-maintenance devices reduce total cost of ownership.

Using the XT49 with Plaspy gives operators centralized visibility into remote, long-term assets without frequent site visits. The combination of solar harvesting, robust enclosure, and LTE reporting helps maintain continuous tracking and operational awareness for fleets and asset programs deployed in challenging environments.
