# Jointech - JT301A

<div className="device-logo">
![JT301A](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Jointech JT301A Asset Tracker is a compact device built for long term asset visibility across logistics and distribution networks. It is designed for container, pallet, and box truck deployments and provides scheduled LBS location updates together with door open and illumination monitoring. This combination makes the JT301A suited to deployments where periodic location reporting and tamper alerts are needed without the cost and power drain of continuous real time tracking.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JT301A delivers scheduled telemetry and sensor driven events directly into Plaspy for reporting, alerting, and historical playback. Integrating JT301A devices with Plaspy gives logistics teams an economical way to extend asset coverage, add anti theft notifications, and maintain long duration deployments while keeping operational overhead low.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for scheduled location and status reporting
- Low power rechargeable design for long term, reusable deployment
- Timed LBS location updates that conserve energy while providing position awareness
- Door open and close detection for tamper and loading event alerts
- Illumination monitoring to flag unexpected access or container opening
- Compact and lightweight form factor for easy redeployment across mixed fleets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When attached to assets, the JT301A sends scheduled location fixes and sensor events that Plaspy ingests for visualization, notifications, and historical analysis. Plaspy can combine JT301A feeds with other device types to give teams a fuller operational picture while using cost effective telemetry for less critical assets.

- Scheduled LBS location and timed telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for asset visibility
- Door open and close events generate alerts and allow rapid investigation workflows
- Illumination alerts signal unexpected light exposure or access during transit and storage
- Device health and battery status sent to Plaspy support maintenance planning and redeployment
- Data from JT301A devices can be correlated with GPS tracker feeds to provide hybrid fleet tracking

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and pallet tracking during cross border shipments where periodic checks and tamper alerts are sufficient
- Box truck and freight cargo monitoring to detect loading, unloading, and unexpected access
- Long duration logistics runs where ultra low power operation and rechargeability reduce cost of ownership
- Supplementing a GPS tracker fleet by providing economical coverage for non critical or reusable assets
- Distribution center and warehouse asset supervision for high value pallets and consignments

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The JT301A is a practical choice for organizations that need reliable, low maintenance asset visibility integrated into Plaspy. Its focus on timed reporting and basic sensor events helps control data and power costs while still delivering the alerts and location awareness required for supply chain security and operational oversight. Paired with Plaspy, the JT301A lets teams combine scheduled telemetry with richer real time feeds where necessary, creating a balanced and cost efficient monitoring strategy.
