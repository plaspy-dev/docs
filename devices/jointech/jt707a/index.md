# JT707A Intelligent GPS Seal Tracker

The JT707A is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed specifically for cargo sealing and supply-chain security. Combining GNSS-based real-time tracking with secure seal monitoring, the JT707A delivers continuous location telemetry and instant tamper/unseal alerts so logistics teams can detect unauthorized access across road, rail, and cross-border shipments.

The device supports both rechargeable and disposable battery options to suit short-term shipments or long-term asset monitoring, and integrates with fleet management and customs supervision workflows through remote management platforms. When used with Plaspy, JT707A enhances visibility, streamlines auditing with event logs and playback, and strengthens anti-theft and tamper-response procedures for containers, vans, trucks, and parcels.

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that combines location telemetry and secure seal monitoring for end-to-end cargo protection.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The JT707A sends GNSS location fixes and seal-status telemetry to tracking platforms. When integrated with Plaspy, those data streams are mapped into Plaspy’s dashboard and alerting engine so operations teams can monitor shipments in real time, receive automated notifications, and review an auditable trail of events.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for continuous situational awareness.
- Tamper/unseal detection with immediate alerts to trigger security protocols and mobile notifications.
- Configurable reporting intervals to balance battery life and tracking resolution in Plaspy dashboards.
- Geofence alerts and event logging available in Plaspy for compliance, customs reviews, and incident analysis.
- Historical playback of location and seal events to support audits and claims handling.

## Integration & Deployment Notes

Deployment is designed to be straightforward: attach the JT707A to the seal point, configure reporting intervals to match battery strategy, and connect the device to Plaspy for centralized dashboards, geofence rules, and automated alerts. For large fleets or frequent shipments, leverage Plaspy’s grouping and reporting features to scale monitoring and incident response across routes and customers.
