# Jointech - JT707A

![JT707A](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The JT707A is a GPS tracker designed for cargo sealing and supply chain security. Built to deliver continuous GNSS location telemetry alongside secure seal monitoring, the JT707A reports location and tamper or unseal events so logistics operators can detect unauthorized access during transit. The product description highlights features useful for road, rail, and cross border shipments, and it supports both rechargeable and disposable battery options to suit short term and longer term deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JT707A integrates its location fixes and seal status into Plaspy’s fleet monitoring and alerting environment. When connected to Plaspy, the device enhances visibility across shipments, provides an auditable history of events and playback, and supports automated alerting for tamper incidents and geofence breaches, making it practical for operations that need centralized oversight of seal integrity and movement.

## Key Highlights

- Combines GNSS location telemetry with secure seal monitoring to protect cargo in transit.
- Real time tracking and immediate tamper or unseal notifications to support rapid response.
- Flexible power options with rechargeable and disposable battery choices for varied deployments.
- Configurable reporting intervals and geofence alerts that help balance battery life and monitoring needs.
- Event logging and historical playback to support audits, customs supervision, and chain of custody.
- Compact form factor suitable for containers, trailers, vans, and parcels for fleetwide rollout.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The JT707A delivers location and seal status data to Plaspy, where those streams are displayed, logged, and used to trigger alerts and reports. Plaspy maps telemetry and event signals from the tracker into dashboards and workflows so operations teams can monitor shipments, respond to incidents, and produce compliance records.

- Real time location and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for continuous situational awareness.
- Tamper and unseal detections generate immediate alerts in Plaspy to support security procedures.
- Configurable reporting intervals are respected in Plaspy views to balance battery use and tracking resolution.
- Geofence events and rule based alerts are available in Plaspy for route monitoring and compliance checks.
- Historical playback of locations and seal events helps with audits, claims handling, and incident review.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parcel and high value package monitoring combining location telemetry and seal state.
- Logistics and freight forwarding operations that require centralized event logs for inspections.
- Short term charters and multi modal transport using disposable battery options for single leg journeys.
