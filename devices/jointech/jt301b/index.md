# Jointech - JT301B

![JT301B](./tracker.jpg)

The Jointech JT301B is a compact, rechargeable asset GPS tracker designed for logistics, distribution and global supply chain monitoring. It combines multi-mode positioning with integrated temperature and humidity sensors and tamper detection to provide event-driven telemetry for containers, pallets, box trucks and other in-transit assets. Its reusable battery and configurable alarms are intended to support repeatable deployments without complex installation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JT301B delivers location and contextual sensor data into Plaspy’s platform so operations teams can monitor assets in real time. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s environmental readings and tamper alerts become part of live dashboards, alerts and historical reports that support anti-theft measures, compliance workflows and improved operational decision making.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible asset GPS tracker for real-time tracking and visibility across supply chains
- Multi-mode positioning using GPS, BeiDou and cellular LBS for continued location updates in varied environments
- Integrated temperature and humidity sensors for monitoring refrigerated and sensitive cargo
- Tamper and intrusion alerts including motion detection, unpacking detection and door-opening detection
- Rechargeable reusable battery for repeated deployments and long term logistics use
- Configurable alarms and event-driven reporting to support exception handling and compliance
- Compact form factor suitable for containers, pallets, box trucks and similar assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the JT301B streams its location fixes and environmental and tamper telemetry into the platform so fleet managers and logistics operators can act on live events. Plaspy ingests these inputs and exposes them via dashboards, alert rules and reporting tools to improve visibility and response.

- Real-time location and telemetry visibility to track assets across routes and modes of transport
- Tamper alerts such as door opening and unpacking detection delivered into Plaspy for rapid response
- Environmental monitoring with temperature and humidity data available for compliance and quality control
- Event driven reporting and configurable alarms to surface exceptions and automate notifications
- Historical logs and exportable reports for audits, chain of custody documentation and trend analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and pallet tracking during long haul and multimodal transport
- Refrigerated transport monitoring to protect perishable goods and support compliance
- Chain of custody and customs supervision using event logs and tamper alerts
- Protection of high value cargo with motion and unpacking detection for anti-theft response
- Reusable or temporary deployments for leased containers, returnable packaging and short term jobs
