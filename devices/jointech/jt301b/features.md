# Jointech - JT301B Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Jointech JT301B and how its capabilities are presented when used with Plaspy. It summarizes practical, non sensitive information about the device's role in logistics, distribution, and global supply chain monitoring, and explains how Plaspy can surface the JT301B's telemetry for operational use.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a general guide and consult the device manufacturer for the most current, device specific technical details.

## Feature Overview

The JT301B is a compact, rechargeable asset tracker designed for repeatable deployments where location accuracy and environmental awareness matter. It combines multi mode positioning with integrated environmental sensing and tamper detection to provide event driven telemetry useful for in transit asset monitoring.

- Multi mode positioning using GPS, BeiDou, and cellular LBS to maintain location visibility across varied environments.
- Integrated temperature and humidity sensors for monitoring refrigerated or sensitive cargo conditions.
- Tamper alerts including motion detection, unpacking detection, and door opening detection to protect high value shipments.
- Rechargeable, reusable battery and compact form factor for easy placement on containers, pallets, and box trucks.
- Configurable alarms and event driven reporting to reduce manual checks and speed exception handling.
- Designed for logistics use cases such as distribution, supply chain monitoring, and temporary asset deployments.

## Core Features of Jointech - JT301B

- Plaspy compatible asset tracking for real time location and telemetry ingestion.
- Multi mode positioning with GPS and BeiDou plus cellular based LBS positioning.
- Integrated temperature sensor for environmental monitoring.
- Integrated humidity sensor for humidity sensitive shipments.
- Tamper and intrusion detection including motion, unpacking, and door opening alerts.
- Rechargeable and reusable battery for repeatable deployments.
- Configurable alarm logic and event driven reporting for exception workflows.
- Compact form factor suited to containers, pallets, and box trucks.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the JT301B is deployed with Plaspy, the tracker reports location, environmental readings, and tamper events into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor assets and respond to exceptions. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and makes the JT301B telemetry available for dashboards, alerts, and historical reporting.

- Real time location visibility in Plaspy using the device's GNSS and cellular LBS data.
- Environmental telemetry views for temperature and humidity to support condition monitoring and compliance checks.
- Tamper event alerts routed into Plaspy for immediate notification and workflow escalation.
- Event driven reporting visible in Plaspy timelines and reports to simplify audits and chain of custody tracking.
- Ability to correlate JT301B data with other platform telemetry for broader operational context when external inputs are available.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and pallet tracking across multimodal transport routes for visibility and routing decisions.
- Refrigerated transport and perishable goods monitoring using temperature and humidity telemetry.
- Chain of custody and customs supervision with event logs and tamper alerts for inspection records.
- High value cargo protection with instant notifications for motion, unpacking, and door opening events.
