# Jointech - GP 5000

![GP 5000](./logo.jpg)

The Jointech GP 5000 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for real-time tracking, fuel level monitoring, and fleet management. With its advanced features and reliable performance, it is an ideal solution for businesses looking to optimize their operations and enhance their vehicle security.

One of the standout features of the GP 5000 is its SMS and GPRS TCP/UDP communication capabilities, allowing for seamless and efficient data transmission. This enables real-time tracking of vehicles, ensuring that businesses have accurate and up-to-date information on their fleet's whereabouts. Additionally, the tracker supports fuel consumption monitoring, providing valuable insights into fuel usage and helping businesses identify potential areas for optimization.

The GP 5000 also offers a range of security features, including a 14-channel vehicle signal alarm and initiative anti-theft functionality. This ensures that businesses can proactively protect their vehicles from unauthorized access and potential theft. The tracker also supports remote configuration and control, allowing for easy management and customization of tracking settings.

In terms of hardware specifications, the GP 5000 is compact and lightweight, measuring 121 x 82 x 29mm and weighing just 0.85kg. It features a 20-channel or above GPS receiver for accurate positioning, and operates on a power source of 10 to 33V DC. With its robust construction and wide operating temperature range of -40 to +85 degrees Celsius, the GP 5000 is built to withstand harsh environments and ensure reliable performance.

Overall, the Jointech GP 5000 is a feature-rich GPS tracker that offers real-time tracking, fuel level monitoring, and fleet management capabilities. With its advanced features, reliable performance, and user-friendly interface, it is an excellent choice for businesses looking to optimize their operations and enhance their vehicle security.

### Key Features:

- SMS and GPRS TCP/UDP communication
- Fuel consumption monitor
- 14-channel vehicle signal alarm
- Blind area data re-report
- Remote control fuel and power break
- Initiative anti-theft
- Remote configuration
- Real-time tracking
- Intelligent power management
- Power low/lost alarm
- 16 geo-fencing reports
- Journey report wireless download
- GPS mileage report/electronic mileage report
- GPS antenna disconnect/cut/short circuit alarm
- Up to 120,000 data log storage

### Technical Specifications:

- Dimension: 121 x 82 x 29mm
- Weight: 0.85kg
- GPS receiver: 20-channel or above GPS receiver
- Datum: WGS-84
- Power source: 10 to 33V DC
- Power consumption: Less than 100mA at 12V DC in operating mode
- Operating temperature: -40 to +85 degrees Celsius
- Communication: GSM 850/900/1800/1900MHz
- Serial port: 3 RS-232 serial ports
- Programmable input port: 14-channel
- Buffer memory: 128KB
- Built-in memory: 2MB
- Default baud rate: 9,600BPS
- Backup battery: 350mAh
- Peripherals: Supports at most 2 additional peripherals at the same time
