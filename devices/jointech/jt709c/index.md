# Jointech - JT709C

![JT709C](./tracker.jpg)

The JT709C Smart Electronic Tracking Lock by Jointech is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker-style sealing solution designed for logistics and asset security. Combining real-time positioning with tamper-evident locking, BLE remote unlocking and a persistent audit trail, the JT709C delivers reliable location visibility and controlled access for containers, vans, cash bags and warehouse doors. Its compact, rechargeable design and IP67 industrial enclosure make it a practical choice for long-term deployment in harsh transportation and storage environments.

Built for integration with fleet management and telemetry platforms like Plaspy, the JT709C enables operators to track assets, review unlock histories and trigger alerts from a single dashboard. The device stores more than 2,000 unlock records for traceability, supports Bluetooth \(BLE\) unlocking and mobile app control for convenient access management, and is optimized for logistics, customs supervision and terminal monitoring workflows where anti-theft controls and auditable seals reduce operational risk.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the JT709C becomes part of a centralized telemetry and fleet management workflow. Plaspy ingests the JT709C’s real-time location and event logs, converts unlock records into auditable events, and triggers alerts or automated reports based on geofence, tamper or access policies. Integration enables operations teams to correlate seal status with vehicle routes, delivery timelines and security events for faster response and improved oversight.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — Plaspy displays current position and recent movement history in dashboards and maps.
- Unlock event synchronization — more than 2,000 stored unlock records are available for upload and audit via Plaspy.
- Bluetooth status and access control — BLE remote unlock events and mobile APP authorizations appear alongside other telemetry.
- Battery and tamper alerts — Plaspy can surface low battery or tamper conditions so teams can act before service interruptions.
- Expanded telemetry correlation — while JT709C focuses on positioning and access logs, Plaspy can combine these records with fuel monitoring, ignition and immobilizer data from vehicle telematics or Bluetooth sensors where those systems are present.
