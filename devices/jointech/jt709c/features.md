# Jointech - JT709C Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Jointech JT709C smart electronic tracking lock and describes how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive functionality such as real-time positioning, BLE unlocking, and audit trail integration so operations teams can understand what to expect when the device is paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. For the most current device-specific details consult Jointech documentation and release notes from the manufacturer.

## Feature Overview

The JT709C is positioned as a compact, rechargeable electronic seal that brings together positioning and controlled access for transport and storage assets. In Plaspy, the device’s location and unlock events appear alongside other fleet telemetry to support traceability and operational oversight.

- Real-time location visibility to monitor secured containers, vans, and storage units during transit and at rest.
- Bluetooth BLE remote unlocking and mobile app authorization for controlled access without physical key exchange.
- Persistent audit trail with storage of more than 2,000 unlock records for traceability and compliance.
- IP67 industrial enclosure for reliable operation in dusty or wet field environments.
- Rechargeable, compact form factor designed for repeated use on seals and doors.

## Core Features of Jointech - JT709C

- Plaspy compatibility for integration of location and event records into fleet dashboards.
- Real-time positioning to provide current location and movement history.
- BLE remote unlock capability and mobile app control for authorized access.
- Robust audit trail storing over 2,000 unlock records for review and evidence.
- Tamper-evident lock design to indicate potential unauthorized access.
- IP67 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water exposure.
- Rechargeable battery power support for sustained deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the JT709C’s position updates and access events are treated as telemetry and audit data that can be correlated with other fleet information. Plaspy ingests the JT709C location and unlock logs so operators can monitor sealed assets and review access history from a single platform.

- Device location and recent movement appear in Plaspy maps and device timelines for situational awareness.
- Stored unlock records are synchronized into Plaspy as auditable events for compliance and investigations.
- BLE unlock and mobile app authorizations are reflected as access events alongside other telemetry.
- Tamper and battery-related conditions can be surfaced as alerts so teams can investigate before operational impact.
- Unlock histories and location context can be correlated with routes and delivery schedules to speed resolution of exceptions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Secure sealing of shipping containers and trailer doors during intermodal transport.
- Logistics chain-of-custody and customs supervision where auditable unlock events are required.
- Cash-in-transit and secure pouch sealing to maintain an access record for high-value logistics.
- Warehouse and terminal door monitoring to capture entry events and support inventory protection.
- Long-term asset tracking in outdoor or harsh storage conditions thanks to IP67 protection.
