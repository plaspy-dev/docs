# Jointech - JT709Ex

<div className="device-logo">
![JT709Ex](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The JT709Ex Explosion-proof Smart Lock from Jointech is a purpose built security device designed for hazardous environments such as fuel transport and tanker operations. It combines a certified explosion proof enclosure with long range LoRa telemetry and on site Bluetooth unlocking to deliver lock state reporting, tamper detection, and controlled access in locations where safety standards are required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the JT709Ex complements vehicle GPS trackers and fleet monitoring workflows by supplying lock and access events that Plaspy can correlate with location and operational data. Its low power design and remote reporting capabilities make it relevant for fleet managers and asset controllers who want integrated anti theft, compliance logging, and operational visibility in regulated logistics contexts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device that integrates lock status and tamper alerts into fleet management workflows
- Explosion proof certifications IECEx ATEx and GB 3836 for use in hazardous atmospheres
- Long range LoRa connectivity for low power telemetry and remote status reporting
- Bluetooth on site unlocking plus master slave remote unlock capability when paired with the JT701D master lock
- Emergency mechanical key with active alerts when mechanical access is used
- Ultra low power design with up to three year standby life to reduce maintenance visits
- Tamper and dismount detection for real time situational awareness and alerting

## How It Works with Plaspy

The JT709Ex functions as a telemetry and access control endpoint within a Plaspy managed environment. While it does not provide GPS location itself, its lock state, tamper events, and remote unlock records are reported into Plaspy and can be matched with GPS tracker feeds and fleet rules for a fuller operational picture.

- Real time status and telemetry reports sent over LoRa to Plaspy for centralized monitoring
- Tamper, dismount, and mechanical key usage alerts feed directly into Plaspy for anti theft workflows
- Lock and unlock event logging creates an auditable trail inside Plaspy for compliance and incident review
- On site Bluetooth unlocking can be recorded and associated with user or device IDs in Plaspy
- Works alongside GPS tracker location feeds in Plaspy to enable coordinated anti theft and recovery actions

## Typical Use Cases

- Fuel tanker security where certified locking and remote alerts are required
- Oil and gas transport monitoring in regulated or hazardous environments
- High value cargo protection with centralized lock state logs and alerts
- Fleet anti theft workflows that combine lock telemetry and GPS location for response
- Remote asset protection for tank farms and mobile storage units with low maintenance needs
