# Jointech - GP 4000

<div className="device-logo">
![GP 4000](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Jointech GP 4000 is a vehicle tracking device designed for fleet management and mobile asset monitoring. It combines GPS positioning with GSM communication and offers support for multiple external sensors, including high precision fuel sensors. The device produces fleet management reports and includes features aimed at continuous tracking and operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GP 4000 can feed location and status data into the Plaspy platform for visibility, reporting, and alerting. Its sensor support and reporting capabilities make it a practical option for organizations that want to use Plaspy for fleet monitoring, fuel tracking, and basic alarm handling while keeping deployment and data workflows straightforward.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for vehicle and fleet tracking with support for external fuel sensors
- Generates multiple fleet management reports useful for operational insight
- Supports more than 10 kinds of sensors for adaptable monitoring setups
- Communication options include SMS and GPRS TCP UDP for data transmission
- Blind area data re reporting helps preserve records when coverage is intermittent
- Multiple alarm functions with alarm transmission options including GPRS SMS and email
- Compact form factor with a wide operating temperature range for vehicle use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GP 4000 supplies position and sensor data so Plaspy can present live and historical views of vehicles and assets. Plaspy ingests the device data to enable monitoring, alerts, and reporting suited to fleet operations.

- Live location tracking and map visualization of vehicles and mobile assets
- Alerting and alarm routing in Plaspy based on device alarm events and thresholds
- Fuel monitoring and reporting using data from the original vehicle fuel sensor or supported external sensors
- Historical route playback and summary reports for operational analysis
- Consolidated fleet reports in Plaspy built from the GP 4000 telemetry and event logs

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle tracking and monitoring for commercial fleets
- Fleet performance reporting and usage analysis
- Fuel consumption monitoring and optimization with fuel sensor data
- Mobile asset tracking for equipment and trailers
- Security and anti theft monitoring with remote alarm notifications

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GP 4000 is a practical choice for organizations that need a flexible tracking device with strong sensor support and reporting features. Its ability to work with multiple sensors, combined with features for data continuity in blind areas, aligns well with Plaspy workflows that emphasize visibility, alerts, and operational reporting.

Because the device supports a range of fleet management reports and offers customization potential, it can be integrated into Plaspy to support common fleet use cases such as fuel monitoring, route analysis, and alarm management while keeping deployments consistent across mixed vehicle fleets.
