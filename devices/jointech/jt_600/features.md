# Jointech - JT 600 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the Jointech JT 600 feature set and how the tracker can be used with Plaspy for asset tracking, remote monitoring, and operational oversight. It is intended to describe practical capabilities you can expect to see reported into Plaspy when the device is configured to communicate with the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific details, advanced configuration, and the latest firmware notes, consult official Jointech documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Jointech JT 600 is designed as a rugged, portable tracker for a wide range of applications, combining long standby life, environmental protection, and several reporting and alerting functions. Below are the most relevant practical capabilities users rely on when pairing the JT 600 with Plaspy.

- IP67 waterproof rating for outdoor and harsh environment deployments
- Solar assisted power supply and intelligent power management for extended standby
- Two way voice communication capability for direct contact when supported by device setup
- SOS alert reporting and historical data recording for event tracking and audits
- GEO fence setup and multi platform tracking for flexible monitoring on computers and mobile devices

## Core Features of Jointech - JT 600

- IP67 waterproof enclosure for weather resistant deployments
- Solar power supply to extend operational life in remote locations
- Two way voice communication capability for on device voice calls
- Long standby performance supported by intelligent power management
- SOS alert generation for emergency notification
- Historical location and event recording for retrospective analysis
- GEO fence configuration for location based alerts
- Multi platform tracking support to access device location from various devices

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes location updates and device events reported by compatible trackers, allowing teams to monitor movement, review histories, and be notified of important events. Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocols to simplify onboarding of supported models such as the JT 600.

- Location updates and historical positions are displayed in Plaspy for route review and reporting
- SOS alerts and geofence triggers reported by the device appear as events inside Plaspy for quick operator response
- Device battery and power state changes reported by the tracker are visible in Plaspy dashboards when provided by the device
- Multi platform access lets operators view JT 600 data from desktop and mobile interfaces supported by Plaspy
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol during configuration to reduce manual setup steps; confirm specific transport options with your installer and manufacturer

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking mobile assets and equipment in outdoor work sites and remote locations
- Logistics and courier fleet monitoring where long standby and durable casing are important
- Government, enterprise, and field operations that require historical records and geofence alerts
- Police, defense, and security applications that need reliable device operation in varied conditions
- Travel, school transport, and agency tracking for oversight and incident reporting
- Geological investigations and field work where solar charging improves deployment duration
