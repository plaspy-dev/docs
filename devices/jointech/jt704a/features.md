# Jointech - JT704A Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Jointech JT704A tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect to surface in Plaspy for container transportation and long duration asset monitoring, including multi mode positioning and built in environmental telemetry.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, the installation method used, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. For precise device capabilities and configuration steps consult the official Jointech documentation and vendor release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The JT704A is a compact container tracker designed to provide resilient location awareness and environmental telemetry for long haul and intermodal operations while minimizing installation impact and maintenance. Its feature set emphasizes multi mode positioning and low power operation to support periodic reporting and extended standby.

- Multi mode positioning using GPS, BeiDou, and LBS for improved coverage across rail, marine, and cross border routes.
- Built in environmental telemetry with light sensing plus temperature and humidity monitoring for cold chain visibility.
- Ultra low power design and intelligent power management that enables multi year standby suitable for long term container deployments.
- Concealed form factor and non invasive installation that supports discreet container tracking and anti theft awareness.
- Configurable between periodic and real time tracking modes to balance update frequency and battery life.

## Core Features of Jointech - JT704A

- Compatibility with Plaspy for reporting location and sensor telemetry into a unified fleet monitoring platform.
- Multi mode positioning: GPS and BeiDou supplemented by LBS positioning to maintain location fixes in diverse environments.
- Built in light sensor for simple tamper or door opening awareness when paired with external inputs or sensors.
- Temperature and humidity sensing for environmental monitoring important to cold chain and sensitive cargo.
- Ultra low power architecture with intelligent power management for extended standby times up to three years as reported by the manufacturer.
- Discrete, hidden installation design aimed at container and intermodal applications such as railway and marine transport.
- Support for periodic reporting and configurable real time update modes to manage visibility and battery consumption.
- Tamper and door/opening awareness can be implemented when combined with external inputs or related sensors.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a JT704A is connected to Plaspy, its location fixes and sensor readings become part of the platform’s monitoring and reporting workflows. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and makes device telemetry available for visualization and alerting.

- Real time and periodic location updates appear on Plaspy maps and in historical location trails for operational oversight.
- Temperature and humidity readings are available in Plaspy for timeline charts, reporting, and cold chain checks.
- Light sensor and external input events can be configured as tamper or door alerts and used to trigger notifications in Plaspy.
- Power management and periodic reporting modes allow Plaspy administrators to tune update frequency to extend device life while keeping essential visibility.
- Plaspy can correlate JT704A container telemetry with other fleet data sources so operators can combine container location and environmental status with broader operational information.
