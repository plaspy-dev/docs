# Jointech - GP 6000

<div className="device-logo">
![GP 6000](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Jointech GP 6000 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset monitoring. It provides multiple communication options including SMS and GPRS, and supports TCP and UDP transmission as well as voice communication. The device offers configurable real time tracking with time based intervals, distance based intervals, and an intelligent mode, together with a set of reporting features and storage for historical log data.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GP 6000 can feed location and event data into Plaspy to support fleet visibility, reporting, and operational oversight. Its combination of flexible communication, configurable tracking modes, remote configuration via authorized SMS, and security features make it a practical option for organizations that want to connect hardware capabilities to Plaspy for monitoring and analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Multiple communication channels supported including SMS and GPRS with TCP and UDP support
- Real time tracking with time interval, distance interval, and intelligent mode options
- Voice communication capability for added operational flexibility
- Intelligent power management with multiple modes to balance runtime and reporting frequency
- Remote configuration via authorized SMS and firmware updates through PC COM port
- Emergency immobilization feature for enhanced vehicle security
- Built in barcode scanner solution and a wide range of reporting options including mileage and journey reports

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GP 6000 can deliver position and event data to Plaspy so that locations, alerts, and historical records are available inside the Plaspy platform. Plaspy ingests the device data and translates it into map views, alerts, and exportable reports to support day to day fleet operations and long term analysis.

- Live and historical location visibility on Plaspy maps for fleet monitoring
- Geofence event tracking and reporting based on the device's geo fencing setups
- Alerts and notifications for overspeed, parking, idling, and other supported events
- Aggregated reporting for daily mileage, journeys, and GSM signal monitoring
- Use of voice call records and immobilization events as part of operational incident logs

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for daily operations and route oversight
- Asset and inventory tracking where barcode scanning can be integrated into workflows
- Security and recovery workflows using emergency immobilization and alerts
- Operational reporting for mileage, journey summaries, idling, and driver oversight
- Logistics and delivery monitoring with historical logs for reconciliation

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GP 6000 is a good match for organizations that need a flexible tracker with several communication options and a rich reporting feature set. Its configurable tracking modes and power management options allow teams to balance reporting frequency with device runtime, and its data logging and geofence capacity support common fleet and asset management requirements.

When connected to Plaspy, the GP 6000's events and location streams become actionable information in maps, dashboards, and reports. This combination helps operations teams maintain visibility, respond to incidents, and produce regular operational summaries without needing to build custom ingestion tools.
