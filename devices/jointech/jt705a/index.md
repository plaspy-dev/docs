# Jointech JT705A Intelligent Container Monitor

The Jointech JT705A is a purpose-built GPS tracker and intelligent container monitor engineered for enterprise logistics and multimodal cargo operations. Designed for shipping containers, refrigerated units, vans and trucks, the JT705A delivers continuous location awareness and security status reporting. As a Plaspy compatible device, it brings container-focused telemetry and tamper protection into Plaspy’s real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.

With an emphasis on security and long-duration deployments, the JT705A combines a high-capacity battery, a local OLED display and real-time voice prompts to give field teams immediate, actionable feedback. The unit’s remote and on-site unsealing capabilities plus anti-theft and anti-dismantling protections make it especially well suited to customs supervision, cross-border shipments, refrigerated cargo and other high-risk consignments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for continuous, enterprise-grade container tracking and telemetry integration.
- High-capacity battery designed for extended deployments during long transits and multimodal legs.
- Local OLED display and real-time voice operation for clear on-site device status and audible alerts.
- Remote and on-site unsealing capability to support secure chain-of-custody workflows and inspections.
- Robust security features: anti-theft, anti-impact, anti-rollover and anti-dismantling protections to protect the device and cargo.
- Optimized for refrigerated cargo, customs supervision and high-value or financial-risk sensitive shipments.
- Generates tamper evidence and timely alarm notifications to accelerate incident response and reduce cargo loss.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the JT705A improves situational awareness for logistics and security teams by feeding container location and operational status into a centralized platform. Plaspy can ingest the JT705A’s location updates, tamper/unseal events and alarm notifications to provide real-time tracking, historical playback and alerting across fleets and multimodal routes. This makes it straightforward to include container telemetry in broader fleet management and compliance reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — JT705A supplies continuous position and status information to Plaspy for live monitoring and playback.
- Tamper and unseal status — remote and on-site unsealing events and tamper alarms are reported for chain-of-custody and customs checks.
- Local status and audible alerts — OLED display and voice prompts support quick field inspections and immediate local action.
- Alarm notifications — timely alerts for impact, tampering or security incidents are routed through Plaspy for escalation and response.
- Mixed-fleet correlation — Plaspy users can correlate JT705A container data with vehicle telematics \(fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer data\) from other Plaspy-compatible devices to get end-to-end visibility across assets.
