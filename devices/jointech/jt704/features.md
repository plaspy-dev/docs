# Jointech - JT704 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive feature overview of the Jointech JT704 and how the device is used with Plaspy for container visibility and logistics monitoring. It focuses on practical capabilities and the kinds of operational value the JT704 brings when it reports position and status into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type and manufacturer implementation. The descriptions below reflect the device characteristics provided by Jointech and summarize how those capabilities typically map into Plaspy workflows while encouraging verification against manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The JT704 is a compact container tracker designed for long duration, multimodal transport. It balances periodic scheduled reporting and real time location updates with power management and a discreet mounting approach to reduce service needs and tampering risk.

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for centralized tracking and telemetry ingestion
- Real time tracking combined with scheduled fixed time reporting for predictable check ins
- Intelligent power management and long standby capability to extend deployment intervals
- Perforation free, non invasive installation for high invisibility mounting
- Compact form factor optimized for standard and special purpose containers
- Adapted for multimodal logistics including rail and marine long haul scenarios

## Core Features of Jointech - JT704

- Out of the box compatibility with Plaspy for location and status reporting
- Real time position reporting to support live map views and tracking
- Scheduled fixed time reporting to enable predictable low power check ins
- Intelligent power management and long standby capability to reduce maintenance
- Perforation free non invasive installation for discreet mounting
- Compact device design optimized for container applications
- Optimized for multimodal transport including railway and marine scenarios

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the JT704 reports position and status, Plaspy ingests that data to provide visibility across maps, timelines and reports. The combination of real time updates and scheduled reports makes the device useful for continuous oversight while managing battery consumption for long trips.

- Live map views and location traces in Plaspy based on JT704 position updates
- Scheduled reporting appears as predictable check ins for long duration shipments
- Battery and power state information available for maintenance planning and alerts
- Historical playback and event timelines to reconstruct container movement
- Data usable for Plaspy alerts, reporting and integration with operational workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Long haul container visibility for multi week transits by rail and sea
- Cross border railway freight monitoring and multimodal handoffs
- Customs supervision and route verification for regulatory audits
- Monitoring special purpose containers where discreet mounting is required
- Enterprise logistics workflows that need container level telemetry alongside fleet data

Using the JT704 together with Plaspy provides centralized visibility for container shipments across long duration and multimodal routes. Plaspy consolidates position and status data into live views, historical playback and alerting rules that help logistics teams, customs authorities and fleet operators monitor movement and maintain audit records.
