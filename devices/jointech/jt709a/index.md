# Jointech - JT709A

![JT709A](./tracker.jpg)

The JT709A Smart Electronic Lock is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and asset security device designed for secure, long-term cargo protection. Combining triple-positioning \(BeiDou, GPS and LBS\) with Bluetooth and RFID access methods, the JT709A delivers precise, tamper-resistant location and event data that integrates with Plaspy for real-time tracking and fleet management visibility.

The JT709A is built for industrial logistics: IP67-rated durability, an ultra-low power design for more than six months standby, and a mechanical life rated at over 20,000 unlock cycles. Plaspy users can leverage JT709A location, status, and event telemetry to strengthen chain-of-custody controls, cut unnecessary site visits, and improve anti-theft response across containers, trailers, and high-value shipments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for accurate real-time tracking and location history of sealed assets.
- Triple-positioning \(BeiDou, GPS and LBS\) to maintain reliable location updates in diverse transport environments.
- Multiple access methods — Bluetooth code scanning, RFID card access, and remote unlocking — for flexible on‑the‑ground workflows.
- IP67-rated rugged design and tamper-resistant construction to protect sensitive cargo and reduce theft risk.
- Ultra-low power architecture with standby time exceeding six months to minimize battery servicing and operational costs.
- Durable mechanical life \(over 20,000 unlocks\) for high-frequency use in multimodal logistics and customs supervision.
- Delivers event telemetry \(unlock, tamper, battery status\) that complements fleet management, telemetry dashboards, and security workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The JT709A feeds location and status events into Plaspy so operations teams see a continuous, auditable record of where seals were opened, when tamper events occurred, and the device battery state. Plaspy ingests the JT709A's GNSS and LBS positions, Bluetooth/RFID unlock events, and tamper alerts to power alerts, geofencing, and chain-of-custody reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(BeiDou, GPS, LBS positions and event timestamps\)
- Unlock and tamper event notifications captured from Bluetooth code scans and RFID access
- Battery and device health telemetry for predictive maintenance and reduced downtime
- Remote unlock command management via the Plaspy platform where remote operation is configured
- Bluetooth sensor pairing for local access control and proximity-based workflows
