# Jointech - JT709A

<div className="device-logo">
![JT709A](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The JT709A Smart Electronic Lock is a purpose built asset security device that combines positioning and access control to protect long term cargo shipments. It integrates triple positioning using BeiDou GPS and LBS with local access methods such as Bluetooth and RFID to provide tamper resistant location and event data for sealed containers, trailers, and other high value assets. The JT709A is designed for industrial logistics with rugged IP67 protection, extended standby life, and a mechanical life suited for frequent unlocking cycles.

Because the JT709A outputs continuous location and event telemetry, it is a natural fit for Plaspy users who need real time visibility and an auditable chain of custody. Plaspy can ingest the JT709A location updates, unlock and tamper events, and battery status to provide monitoring, alerts, and reporting across a fleet or set of secured assets. This makes the JT709A relevant for operations that require both physical access control and integrated tracking in a single device.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and electronic lock for real time tracking and secure location history of sealed assets
- Triple positioning with BeiDou GPS and LBS to maintain location updates in varied transport environments
- Multiple access methods including Bluetooth code scanning and RFID card access for flexible on site workflows
- IP67 rated rugged construction and tamper resistant design to protect cargo and reduce theft risk
- Ultra low power architecture with standby time exceeding six months to lower maintenance frequency
- Mechanical durability rated for over 20,000 unlock cycles for high frequency use in logistics operations
- Event telemetry including unlock, tamper, and battery status to support security and operational reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the JT709A location positions and event streams and makes that information available in dashboards, reports, and alerting tools so teams can monitor sealed assets through transit and storage. The integration enables operational oversight without replacing existing vehicle telematics, focusing on asset security and chain of custody.

- Real time location and historical position tracking to view movements and timelines
- Unlock and tamper event notifications captured from Bluetooth and RFID access to trigger alerts
- Battery and device health reporting to plan maintenance and reduce unexpected service visits
- Geofencing and rule based alerts to detect unauthorized openings or unexpected movements
- Audit trails and chain of custody reports for compliance and incident review
- Remote unlock command handling where remote operation is configured and permitted

## Typical Use Cases

- Customs supervision and inspection workflows that need precise location history and unlock records
- High value goods transit where rugged, long life seals reduce maintenance and improve response
