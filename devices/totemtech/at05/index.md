# Totemtech - AT05

![AT05](./logo.jpg)

The Totemtech AT05 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a wide range of features to meet your tracking needs. One of its standout features is the ability to send data to two servers simultaneously, ensuring that you always have access to accurate and up-to-date information. Additionally, the AT05 is equipped with a 3-Axis Digital Accelerometer for movement status monitoring, allowing you to track the movement and location of your assets with precision.

With firmware upgrade capabilities via OTA \(Over-The-Air\), you can easily keep your tracker up to date with the latest features and improvements. The AT05 supports a wide range of power inputs, from DC 9v to 50v, and features over-voltage protection to ensure the safety and longevity of the device. You can send commands to the GPS tracker via GPRS or SMS, giving you full control and flexibility in managing your tracking operations.

The AT05 also offers real-time tracking and history data traces tracking, allowing you to monitor the location and movement of your assets in real-time or review past tracking data. It supports various alarm types, including over-speed, low power, tremble, parking, SOS, exterior power off, and internal power low alarm, ensuring that you are promptly notified of any potential issues or emergencies.

With its user-defined settings on I/O ports, you can customize the digital input and output to suit your specific requirements. The AT05 is compatible with both cell phone tracking and web-based tracking software, giving you the flexibility to track your assets using the method that is most convenient for you. Whether you need to track vehicles, equipment, or other valuable assets, the Totemtech AT05 GPS tracker is a reliable and feature-packed solution.

### Outstanding Features:

- Simultaneous data transmission to two servers
- 3-Axis Digital Accelerometer for movement status monitoring
- Firmware upgrade via OTA
- Support for DC 9v to 50v power input with over-voltage protection
- GPRS and SMS command support
- Real-time tracking and history data traces tracking
- Various alarm types for notifications
- User-defined settings on I/O ports
- Compatible with cell phone and web-based tracking software
