# Totemtech - AT09-4G

![AT09-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The AT09-4G is a rugged 4G GPS tracker built for demanding vehicle environments — cars, trucks, trailers, mining machines and more. Designed to be Plaspy compatible, the AT09-4G provides reliable real-time tracking and rich telemetry over LTE networks, combining multi-constellation GNSS positioning \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BDS\) with advanced vehicle-condition sensing for fleet management, anti-theft and operational visibility.

With extensive I/O, LoRa and Bluetooth connectivity, TPMS and multi-channel fuel monitoring, the AT09-4G gives fleet and asset managers a single device that streamlines location, safety and vehicle data into platforms such as Plaspy. The unit supports voice, optional video, a 4G Wi‑Fi hotspot and flexible reporting triggers — ideal for operations that demand accurate location plus deep telemetry for maintenance, security and compliance.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering real-time tracking and multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BDS\) for reliable positioning.
- Advanced telemetry: online TPMS \(up to 42 sensors\), multi-channel fuel monitoring \(up to 4 analog sensors\), temperature and sensor voltage reporting.
- Comprehensive I/O and peripheral support — RS232 \(2\), RS485 \(1\), CAN \(1\), TTL \(1\), 1-wire \(2\), digital inputs/outputs and analog inputs for rich vehicle integrations.
- 4G LTE connectivity with SMS, TCP/UDP GPRS and ability to send data to two servers simultaneously; ideal for fleet management and redundancy.
- LoRa and Bluetooth support for IoT connectivity; can operate as LoRa gateway or node and connect Bluetooth sensors/beacons for local monitoring.
- Built-in microphone and speaker for two-way voice, USB for OTA firmware updates, and optional video and Wi‑Fi hotspot for passenger or driver connectivity.
- Robust safety and alarm suite — geofencing, overspeed, tamper, towing, SOS and harsh-driving notifications for anti-theft and driver-safety workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the AT09-4G delivers continuous location and telemetry streams, giving dispatchers and managers live situational awareness. Device data is transmitted over LTE or GPRS to Plaspy using standard TCP/UDP or SMS fallback; configurable triggers control reporting frequency so you balance data usage with precision. Plaspy receives GNSS coordinates, TPMS and fuel sensor readings, alarm events and digital I/O states, enabling real-time tracking, alerts and historical reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GNSS position, speed, heading and multi-constellation fixes.
- Ignition and engine status: engine on/off detection and digital inputs for ignition events to support fuel and driver-session analytics.
- Fuel monitoring: simultaneous support for up to four analog fuel sensors, including Omnicomm and ultrasonic sensors for accurate fuel monitoring.
- Remote immobilizer support via optional relay or OBD peripherals — enables immobilizer workflows when configured in Plaspy.
- Bluetooth sensors and LoRa IoT: connect BLE sensors or use LoRa gateway/node modes to ingest temperature, cargo sensors or beacons into Plaspy.
- TPMS telemetry: live tire pressure, temperature and sensor voltage for tire-health alerts and predictive maintenance.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and logistics: real-time tracking, route optimization and driver verification for delivery and transport fleets.
- Anti-theft and security: geofencing, tamper and towing alarms, SOS wireless controllers and optional remote immobilizer for vehicle recovery.
- Construction and mining equipment: asset security, usage monitoring and telemetry for heavy machinery in remote or harsh environments.
- Public transport and school buses: passenger and driver safety, in-vehicle Wi‑Fi hotspot and CCTV/video options for compliance and oversight.
- Cold chain and regulated cargo: LoRa/Bluetooth sensors and TPMS/fuel telemetry to monitor environmental and vehicle conditions during transit.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT09-4G is a practical choice for organizations that require a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with comprehensive telemetry and flexible integrations. Its wide voltage range, backup battery and rugged form factor make it reliable for commercial and industrial vehicles. Extensive I/O and support for peripherals \(relays, OBD modules, iButton/RFID, cameras\) allow you to implement immobilizer workflows, driver identification and custom alarms without replacing core hardware. The built-in TPMS and multi-channel fuel monitoring reduce maintenance risk and enable fuel monitoring insights, while LoRa and Bluetooth sensors let you extend telemetry to cargo and environmental sensors. Paired with Plaspy, the AT09-4G delivers actionable, real-time tracking and fleet management data that improves security, lowers operational costs and simplifies vehicle telemetry across mixed fleets.
