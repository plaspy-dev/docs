# Totemtech - AT09

![AT09](./logo.jpg)

The Totemtech AT09 GPS tracker is a versatile and feature-rich device that offers a wide range of functionalities for real-time tracking and monitoring. With its tire pressure monitoring feature, you can keep track of your vehicle's tire pressure in real-time, ensuring optimal performance and safety on the road. The AT09 also supports the monitoring of up to 4 fuel sensors and 2 temperature sensors simultaneously, allowing you to keep an eye on fuel levels and temperature conditions.

In addition to its monitoring capabilities, the AT09 offers advanced connectivity options. It can send data to two servers simultaneously, ensuring reliable and redundant data transmission. The device also features three serial ports, which can be used to connect various devices such as displays, cameras, RFID readers, and Garmin Navigators. With its real-time tracking and history data traces tracking, you can easily keep track of your vehicle's movements and access detailed historical data. The AT09 also comes with a 3-Axis Digital Accelerometer for monitoring movement status and a firmware update feature that allows for over-the-air updates.

The Totemtech AT09 GPS tracker is equipped with a range of other useful features, including support for DC 9v-50v power supply with over-voltage protection, the ability to send commands to GPS trackers via GPRS, and the option to send GPRS data to an IP or domain name. It also includes a 16Mb Flash Memory for storing data, fuel/oil level detection with high accuracy, and the ability to send Google map links via SMS. With its voice monitoring function and microphone, you can listen in on the surroundings of the tracker, and the integration of a Micro SD card allows for additional storage capacity.

The Totemtech AT09 GPS tracker is an excellent choice for those looking for a reliable and feature-packed device for tracking and monitoring vehicles. With its tire pressure monitoring, fuel and temperature sensor support, and advanced connectivity options, it offers a comprehensive solution for fleet management, vehicle tracking, and asset monitoring applications.

### Outstanding Features:

- Tire pressure monitoring in real-time
- Support for up to 4 fuel sensors and 2 temperature sensors simultaneously
- Simultaneous data transmission to two servers
- Three serial ports for connecting multiple devices
- Real-time tracking and history data traces tracking
- 3-Axis Digital Accelerometer for movement status monitoring
- Firmware update by OTA
- Support for DC 9v-50v power supply with over-voltage protection
- Send commands to GPS trackers via GPRS
- 16Mb Flash Memory for data storage
- Fuel/oil level detection with high accuracy
- Voice monitoring function with microphone
- Micro SD card integration
