# Totemtech - AT09

![AT09](./logo.jpg)

The Totemtech AT09 is a versatile vehicle GPS tracker designed for real-time tracking and multi-sensor monitoring. It combines location tracking with specialized inputs for tire pressure, multiple fuel sensors, and temperature probes, plus peripheral connectivity for devices such as displays, cameras, and RFID readers. Built-in motion detection and local storage extend the device's usefulness for continuous monitoring and historical trace review.

As a model compatible with Plaspy, the AT09 can provide its telemetry and event data into Plaspy's fleet management environment. That compatibility makes the AT09 suitable for organizations that want to consolidate vehicle location, sensor readings, and peripheral events into a single operational view for monitoring, alerts, and reporting within Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Real-time location tracking with history traces for route review and playback  
- Tire pressure monitoring capability for ongoing safety awareness  
- Support for up to four fuel sensors and two temperature sensors for multi-point telemetry  
- Dual server transmission for redundant data delivery and improved reliability  
- Three serial ports to connect peripherals such as displays, cameras, and RFID readers  
- Built-in 3 axis accelerometer to report movement status and events  
- Over-the-air firmware updates to simplify device maintenance and updates

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the AT09 streams location and sensor data to the platform where it can be visualized, logged, and acted upon. Plaspy gathers incoming data from the device and translates it into vehicle position, sensor values, and event records that are usable across fleet workflows.

- Consolidate live location and historical traces into Plaspy maps and timelines  
- Surface tire pressure, fuel level, and temperature readings in dashboards and reports  
- Configure alerts in Plaspy for thresholds such as low fuel or abnormal tire pressure  
- Use Plaspy reporting tools to analyze sensor trends and vehicle utilization over time  
- Leverage dual server transmission to reduce data gaps in Plaspy reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle monitoring where tire pressure and fuel oversight improve safety and cost control  
- Long haul and logistics operations that need redundant tracking and historical route review  
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring using temperature sensors integrated with the tracker  
- Service and utility vehicles that attach cameras or RFID readers to capture operational events  
- Asset monitoring for equipment where movement detection and local storage are useful

The AT09 is a practical choice for fleets that need more than basic GPS location. Its support for multiple fuel and temperature sensors, tire pressure monitoring, and peripheral connections lets operators gather a broad set of operational data from each vehicle. Those capabilities map well to Plaspy, where sensor streams and events can be turned into alerts, reports, and actionable insights.

Plaspy helps bring AT09 data into a single operational layer, making it easier to monitor vehicle health, trigger notifications, and generate performance reports. If your operations depend on combining location with sensor telemetry and attached devices, the AT09 paired with Plaspy offers a balanced option for improved visibility and oversight.
