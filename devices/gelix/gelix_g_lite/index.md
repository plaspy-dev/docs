# Gelix - Gelix G Lite

<div className="device-logo">
![Gelix G Lite](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Gelix G Lite is a compact receptionist telematics GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable location information for vehicles and mobile assets. It combines GLONASS and GPS navigation with GSM GPRS communication to capture position data quickly, with a reported start time of around 29 seconds after power on. The device supports multi channel reception modes to improve positioning reliability and is intended for general fleet and asset tracking applications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Gelix G Lite can feed location and status information into the Plaspy platform for monitoring and reporting. Its support for relay control and compatibility with fuel level sensors and vehicle CAN bus adapters makes it suitable for integration into a Plaspy-managed tracking workflow, giving operators consolidated visibility of moving assets and selected vehicle data through the Plaspy interface.

## Key Highlights

- Dual navigation support using GLONASS and GPS for improved positioning reliability
- Fast start up with location capture in approximately 29 seconds after power on
- Multi channel reception modes to enhance signal acquisition and tracking consistency
- Relay support for remote control of electronic locks and simple outputs
- Compatibility with fuel level sensors to monitor fuel related metrics
- Works with vehicle CAN bus adapters for integration with vehicle monitoring systems

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Gelix G Lite supplies regular location updates and device status that Plaspy processes for live tracking, history, and reporting. Plaspy can present the incoming data as part of fleet dashboards, alerts, and operational reports to help teams manage assets effectively.

- Live location visibility on Plaspy maps for individual vehicles and grouped fleets
- Geofencing and alerting to notify operators of arrivals, departures, or boundary events
- Historical route playback and trip reporting to support operational reviews
- Use of relay outputs through Plaspy to manage electronic locks or simple remote controls
- Consolidation of sensor inputs such as fuel level and CAN bus derived metrics into Plaspy reports

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring company vehicles for route compliance and location awareness
- Asset tracking for equipment that benefits from reliable position reporting
- Remote control of electronic locks on trailers or secured compartments
- Fuel monitoring and basic vehicle data aggregation when paired with compatible sensors
- Small to medium fleet management where fast position acquisition and dependable GNSS are valued

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Gelix G Lite offers a balance of fast GNSS acquisition and practical integration points that make it a useful option for organizations seeking straightforward tracking capabilities. Its combined GLONASS and GPS reception improves positioning reliability in varied environments, while relay and sensor compatibility extend its usefulness beyond simple location reporting.

Paired with Plaspy, the device becomes part of a centralized fleet management workflow where location, alerts, and basic vehicle data are available in one place. For teams that need dependable position updates and the option to integrate locks or sensors, the Gelix G Lite can be a sensible addition to a Plaspy-managed deployment.
