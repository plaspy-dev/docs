# Gelix - Gelix 3

<div className="device-logo">
![Gelix 3](./logo.gif)
</div>

The Gelix 3 is a versatile power consumption data logger and GPS tracker designed to collect and deliver telemetry and event data for a wide range of applications. It features automated I O capability and an embedded modem or Ethernet connectivity option, making it suitable for environments where continuous monitoring of power usage, temperature, alarms, and related events is required. The device is presented as a single platform for logging, monitoring, and remote data delivery.

Because the Gelix 3 can integrate with meters, thermometers, access control readers, and alarm systems, it is a natural fit for fleet and asset management platforms such as Plaspy. When paired with Plaspy, the Gelix 3 can feed location information alongside power and event telemetry into a central system for visualization, alerting, and reporting, helping organizations combine operational oversight with energy and security monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated power consumption monitoring and data logging in one device
- Automated I O and embedded modem or Ethernet connectivity for remote data delivery
- Broad integration potential with meters, thermometers, access control readers, and alarm systems
- Supports event monitoring and device telemetry for operational awareness
- Capable of voice listening and access control related functions where required
- Designed for deployments that need combined security, control, and energy insight

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the Gelix 3 telemetry and presents it alongside location data to provide unified visibility into assets, energy use, and events. The platform uses incoming data to populate dashboards, trigger alerts, and produce historical reports that help teams make operational decisions.

- Real time visibility of device status and location within Plaspy dashboards
- Historical energy and event logs available for reporting and analysis
- Alerts and notifications based on power thresholds, alarms, or custom events
- Consolidated telemetry from meters, sensors, and access devices for easier troubleshooting
- Integration into operational workflows for monitoring, maintenance scheduling, and compliance reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Facility energy monitoring and historical consumption reporting
- Remote environmental monitoring including temperature trends and alerts
- Security and access control monitoring for buildings and restricted areas
- Alarm and event monitoring with centralized logging for incident analysis
- Remote telemetry and device status monitoring for dispersed assets
- Combining location tracking with power monitoring for mobile assets or field equipment

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Gelix 3 offers a practical combination of power monitoring and event telemetry that complements Plaspy's fleet and asset management capabilities. Organizations that need consolidated insight into energy usage, environmental conditions, and security events can benefit from keeping Gelix 3 data in the same platform where they manage location and operational status.

Because the device integrates with a wide range of peripheral equipment, it is well suited to deployments that require adaptable monitoring and reporting. Pairing Gelix 3 with Plaspy lets teams centralize data streams, set meaningful alerts, and generate reports that support both operational efficiency and oversight without relying on multiple disconnected systems.
