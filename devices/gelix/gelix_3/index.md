# Gelix - Gelix 3

![Gelix 3](./logo.gif)

The Gelix 3 is a versatile power consumption data logger that comes equipped with an automated I/O and an embedded GSM/GPRS modem or Ethernet port. This GPS tracker is designed to provide a comprehensive solution for power consumption monitoring, data logging, and delivery. With its advanced features, the Gelix 3 offers a wide range of applications including security, device control, event monitoring, device telemetry, voice listening, and access control.

One of the standout features of the Gelix 3 is its seamless integration with various devices such as power meters, digital thermometers, access control card readers, burglar alarms, fire alarms, radio adaptors, and custom devices. This allows for a highly customizable and adaptable solution that can meet the specific needs of different industries and applications.

With its reliable and efficient performance, the Gelix 3 is an ideal choice for businesses and organizations that require accurate power consumption monitoring, data logging, and remote device control. Whether you need to monitor energy usage, track temperature fluctuations, control access to secure areas, or ensure the safety of your premises, the Gelix 3 offers a comprehensive solution that combines advanced technology with ease of use.

### Key Features:

- Power consumption monitoring
- Data logging and delivery
- Security and device control
- Event monitoring
- Device telemetry
- Voice listening
- Access control
- Integration with various devices
