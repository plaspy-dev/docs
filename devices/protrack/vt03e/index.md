# Protrack - VT03E+

![VT03E+](./tracker.png)

The VT03E+ is a compact GPS tracker built for long term, low maintenance vehicle and asset tracking. It pairs an extended internal battery with a powerful built in magnet and an IP65 rated enclosure to provide reliable real time tracking and anti theft monitoring for cars, trailers, containers and other high value assets. The device is designed to be small and unobtrusive while offering durable weather protection for outdoor use.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the VT03E+ can deliver location and status updates into Plaspy so operators can monitor assets, receive alerts and generate reports. Its configurable operating modes and long standby life make it a practical option for teams that need covert or detachable tracking without frequent recharges, and Plaspy can use the unit's data to support fleet workflows and anti theft monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Extended internal battery life with manufacturer stated runtimes up to 35 days in typical active tracking and up to 15 months in deep standby.
- Plaspy compatible out of the box for mapping, alerts and fleet management visibility.
- Strong built in magnet for fast tool free attachment to metal surfaces, suitable for covert or temporary placement.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure for dust protection and resistance to low pressure water jets in outdoor environments.
- Compact form factor that fits on vehicles, trailers, equipment and other metal assets with limited space.
- Low maintenance operation with configurable wake and reporting modes to reduce field visits.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy the VT03E+ sends location and device status so you can watch assets live, replay historical traces and include the device in automated workflows. Plaspy ingests battery and movement information, applies geofence and alert rules, and produces reports to support operational oversight and anti theft response. The tracker’s long standby options make it straightforward to balance update frequency against battery life depending on your monitoring needs.

- Real time location updates and historical route replay available in Plaspy for monitoring and investigation.
- Battery and power status reporting to help schedule maintenance and reduce unexpected downtime.
- Movement detection and anti theft alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and event logging.
- Deep standby and periodic wake modes compatible with Plaspy reporting intervals to conserve battery.
- Integration with Plaspy features such as geofencing, scheduled reports and route history; Plaspy can also ingest additional sensor inputs when those are available on a hardware variant.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle anti theft monitoring with movement alerts routed to operations teams.
- Asset protection for trailers, containers and portable equipment using magnetic mounting for quick placement.
- Seasonal or deployed equipment tracking where long standby life reduces site visits.
- Low maintenance fleet operations that need periodic location history without frequent battery changes.
- Temporary or detachable tracking assignments where tool free mounting and quick retrieval are required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT03E+ combines endurance, simple mounting and weather resistance in a compact package, making it a solid candidate for organizations that need low maintenance tracking integrated into a fleet platform. Used with Plaspy, the tracker provides clear visibility into asset locations, supports geofence based alerts and adds telemetry that teams can use for scheduling, recovery and reporting.
