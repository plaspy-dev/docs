# Protrack - VT03E+

![VT03E+](./tracker.png)

The VT03E+ is a compact GPS tracker purpose-built for long-term, low‑maintenance vehicle and asset tracking. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the VT03E+ combines extended internal battery life, a powerful built-in magnet for quick metal mounting, and an IP65-rated enclosure to deliver reliable real-time tracking and anti-theft monitoring for cars, trailers, containers and other high-value assets.

The VT03E+ is ideal for fleet management and asset protection programs that need covert or detachable tracking without frequent recharges or invasive installation. With operating modes tuned for both frequent position updates and months‑long standby, the unit reduces field maintenance while feeding Plaspy with location and status data that operators can use for telemetry, alerts, and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Long operational life — large internal battery supports up to 35 days in typical active tracking and up to 15 months in deep‑standby.
- Plaspy compatible — designed to integrate with Plaspy for mapping, alerts, and fleet management workflows.
- Powerful built‑in magnet for fast, tool‑free attachment to metal surfaces — ideal for covert or temporary installs.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure — dust tight and protected against low‑pressure water jets for reliable outdoor use.
- Compact form factor — small footprint that fits on vehicles, trailers, equipment and other metal assets.
- Low‑maintenance operation — configurable operating modes reduce the need for frequent recharging or retrieval.
- Well suited for anti‑theft monitoring, telemetry reporting and long‑term seasonal deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the VT03E+ is paired with Plaspy, location updates and device status are delivered to Plaspy’s dashboard so you can monitor assets in real time or review historical traces. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s telemetry and battery information, applies geofencing and alert rules, and produces reports for fleet management and anti‑theft workflows. The unit’s long‑standby and configurable wake patterns make it straightforward to balance update frequency and battery life depending on the use case.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring and replay.
- Battery and power status reporting to support maintenance scheduling and remote diagnostics.
- Movement detection and anti‑theft alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notifications and event logging.
- Deep‑standby and periodic wake modes compatible with Plaspy’s configurable reporting intervals to conserve battery life.
- Supports integration with Plaspy features such as geofencing, route history, and scheduled reports; when additional sensor inputs \(ignition, immobilizer, fuel sensors, Bluetooth sensors\) are available on a hardware variant, Plaspy can ingest and display that data as well.
