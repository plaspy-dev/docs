# Protrack - VT03E+ Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Protrack VT03E+ and how the device works with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities relevant to fleet managers, asset protection teams, and operators who plan to register and monitor VT03E+ devices in the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavioral details can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical specifications, firmware behavior, and variant options consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The VT03E+ is a compact, magnetically mounted GPS tracker designed for long term, low maintenance tracking of vehicles and assets. It emphasizes extended battery life and a rugged enclosure to support deployments where infrequent servicing is a priority.

- Long internal battery life for extended deployments and reduced field maintenance.
- Built in powerful magnet for quick tool free mounting to metal surfaces.
- IP65 rated enclosure for reliable outdoor operation in dusty and wet conditions.
- Compact form factor suited to covert or detachable installation on vehicles, trailers, and equipment.
- Configurable operating modes tuned for either frequent position updates or months long standby.
- Out of the box compatibility with Plaspy for mapping, alerts, and reporting workflows.

## Core Features of Protrack - VT03E+

- Extended internal battery life with manufacturer guidance of up to 35 days in typical active tracking and up to 15 months in deep standby modes.
- Designed to integrate with Plaspy so location and status reports appear in a fleet management dashboard.
- Strong built in magnet for tool free attachment to metal assets enabling quick placement and retrieval.
- Rugged IP65 rated enclosure providing dust protection and resistance to low pressure water jets.
- Compact enclosure and small footprint for discreet mounting on vehicles, trailers, containers, and portable equipment.
- Multiple operating modes that balance update frequency and battery conservation for different deployment needs.
- Low maintenance operation intended for long term seasonal deployments and anti theft monitoring.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy, VT03E+ devices deliver location and status updates that Plaspy uses to provide visibility, alerts, and reporting for operators. Plaspy receives the device telemetry and presents it through maps, history views, and configurable rules.

- Real time location updates and historical route replay available in Plaspy for operational oversight.
- Battery and power status reporting presented to support maintenance scheduling and proactive servicing.
- Movement and anti theft events forwarded to Plaspy so teams can trigger notifications and incident workflows.
- Configurable device wake and reporting intervals match Plaspy reporting settings to balance detail and battery life.
- Plaspy can ingest additional telemetry when a VT03E+ hardware variant exposes extra sensor inputs and display those signals in the dashboard.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle anti theft monitoring using magnetic mounting and long standby life.
- Trailer and container protection where tool free mounting and rugged enclosure are desirable.
- Seasonal equipment tracking that benefits from months long standby between servicing.
- Low maintenance fleet monitoring for vehicles and assets that require periodic location history.
- Temporary deployments and spot checks where detachable mounting simplifies logistics.
- Asset recovery and incident response supported by real time location updates and alerts.
