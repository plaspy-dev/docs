# Protrack - VT08

![VT08](./Logo.png)

The Protrack VT08 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for real time location monitoring and basic fleet oversight. Described by the manufacturer as the VT08S in product literature, this unit emphasizes continuous and interval reporting, geo fencing, and a set of alerts that make it suitable for tracking cars, trucks, and other commercial vehicles. Its small form factor and compatibility with common vehicle voltages make it a practical choice where discreet, persistent tracking is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT08 integrates into Plaspy's fleet monitoring environment to provide location visibility, alert forwarding, and historical reporting. When used with Plaspy, the tracker supplies the core location and event information the platform needs to deliver live monitoring, geofence notifications, and operational reports for vehicle management and security workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking for continuous vehicle location visibility
- Geo fencing support for virtual boundary alerts and location policies
- Multiple reporting modes including continuous and interval based updates
- Built in alarms for overspeed detection and low battery notifications
- Compact design for flexible mounting and discreet installation
- Compatible with 12V and 24V vehicle systems for broad vehicle support
- Designed for straightforward setup and use in fleet environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT08 streams position and event information into the Plaspy platform so fleet managers and operators can monitor assets from a single interface. Plaspy aggregates the tracker data to provide context, visualization, and operational controls.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for real time fleet visibility
- Geofence alerts routed into Plaspy so teams can act on entry and exit events
- Scheduled and ad hoc reports available through Plaspy using the tracker data
- Overspeed and low battery alerts surfaced in Plaspy dashboards and notifications
- Historical route playback and event timelines to review vehicle activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking commercial delivery vehicles to improve route oversight
- Fleet management for small to medium sized companies seeking location visibility
- Vehicle security and recovery monitoring for high value assets
- Rental and shared vehicle operations that need geofencing and usage alerts
- Personal vehicle monitoring for owners who want simple real time tracking
