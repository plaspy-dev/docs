# Protrack - VT05C Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Protrack VT05C and how its capabilities are presented when the device is used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about the tracker, what operators can expect to monitor, and how Plaspy receives and displays events and location data.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, firmware updates, and installation requirements consult the official Protrack documentation and follow manufacturer guidance.

## Feature Overview

The VT05C is a compact wired GPS tracker built for continuous vehicle monitoring with an IP65 rated enclosure for reliable operation in outdoor and vehicle environments. Its combination of core telemetry and security features makes it suitable for fleet and personal vehicle use where uninterrupted tracking and tamper awareness are priorities.

- Continuous real time location reporting for map based monitoring and historical playback
- Wired power design for uninterrupted operation while installed in a vehicle
- ACC detection to record ignition on and off events and measure trip timing
- Vibration detection and geo fence alerts to notify of tampering or unauthorized movement
- Over speed warnings and historical route playback for driver behavior review and compliance

## Core Features of Protrack - VT05C

- Real time GPS positioning and periodic location reporting suitable for cars and motorcycles
- IP65 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water jets
- Wired power connection for continuous operation during vehicle use
- ACC detection for ignition state and trip identification
- Vibration detection for tamper or movement alerts
- Geo fencing support to detect entry and exit of defined areas
- Over speed warning to help identify potential driver behavior issues
- Fuel cutoff immobilizer support for remote engine disable actions when configured

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a VT05C is integrated into Plaspy, its reported locations and events become part of a unified monitoring environment where live maps, alerts, and historical records help teams maintain visibility and respond to incidents. Plaspy receives the device events and presents them in dashboards and reports for operations and fleet management.

- Live map visualization of VT05C position updates and recent movement
- Ignition and ACC events are logged in Plaspy to segment trips and support time based reporting
- Immobilizer and fuel cutoff events can be recorded and acted on through Plaspy when the device and installation support remote control
- Geo fence breaches, vibration alerts, and over speed warnings trigger notifications in Plaspy for immediate attention
- Historical playback and route export enable post trip analysis and driver behavior reviews
- Centralized alerting and reporting make it easier to aggregate VT05C data across a fleet

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft monitoring with immobilizer logging and tamper alerts
- Continuous vehicle tracking for dispatch and route verification
- Driver behavior monitoring using over speed warnings and historical playback
- Motorcycle and car security where compact, discreet installation is required
- Long term route and compliance reporting for service and delivery fleets
- Mixed asset monitoring alongside other devices in Plaspy for consolidated oversight
