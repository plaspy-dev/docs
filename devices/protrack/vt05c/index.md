# Protrack - VT05C

<div className="device-logo">
![VT05C](./tracker.png)
</div>

The VT05C is a compact wired GPS tracker from Protrack designed for reliable vehicle monitoring and theft protection. It features a rugged IP65 rated enclosure for dust and water resistance, a discreet lightweight form factor suitable for cars and motorcycles, and a wired power connection intended to deliver continuous operation while installed. The VT05C bundles common vehicle telemetry and security features such as ACC detection, vibration alerts, geo-fencing, over-speed warnings, historical playback, and a fuel cutoff immobilizer to support anti-theft workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT05C can transmit live location and event data into Plaspy’s unified monitoring platform, making it practical for fleet managers and individual vehicle owners who need centralized visibility. When connected to Plaspy, the VT05C contributes position updates and security events that feed into map views, alerts, and historical reports, allowing teams to monitor assets, respond to incidents, and review trip history within a single interface.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for centralized real time tracking and fleet oversight.
- IP65 rated enclosure for reliable performance in challenging vehicle environments.
- ACC detection to report ignition status and support trip timing and usage tracking.
- Engine immobilizer support for remote fuel cutoff as part of anti theft measures.
- Vibration detection and geo fencing to surface tamper and unauthorized movement alerts.
- Historical playback and over speed warnings to review routes and driver behavior.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the VT05C supplies live position and event information to Plaspy’s platform where data is processed, visualized, and stored for monitoring and reporting. Operators can use Plaspy to receive notifications, inspect past routes, and maintain an auditable record of vehicle activity.

- Real time tracking and map visualization for live monitoring of vehicles and motorcycles.
- Ignition and ACC event reporting to segment trips and detect unexpected starts.
- Immobilizer and anti theft events logged for rapid response and security workflows.
- Alerts for geo fence breaches, vibration tampering, and over speed conditions routed to Plaspy for notification and action.
- Historical playback and reporting to analyze routes, verify service activity, and support compliance review.
