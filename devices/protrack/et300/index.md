# Protrack - ET300

<div className="device-logo">
![ET300](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Protrack ET300 is a rugged GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable vehicle monitoring and basic fleet control. Housed in an IP65 rated enclosure, the ET300 is built to tolerate exposed installations and provide essential telemetry and event logging for cars, motorcycles and light commercial vehicles. Core functions described for this model include geofence alerts, over speed warnings, fuel cutoff control and historical trip recording.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the ET300 brings those capabilities directly into Plaspy’s tracking and fleet management environment. When paired with Plaspy, the ET300 supplies continuous location fixes and event reports that can be turned into live map views, timely alerts and straightforward historical reports, making it a practical option for small fleets and single vehicle owners who need dependable tracking without unnecessary complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for direct integration into real time tracking and fleet workflows
- Rugged IP65 enclosure suitable for exposed vehicle installations
- Fuel cutoff and immobilizer capability to support theft prevention workflows
- Power outage detection and reporting to surface tampering or loss of external power
- Configurable geo fence alerts and over speed warnings to help enforce routes and policies
- Historical trip recording for retrospective route analysis and compliance review
- Focused on essential telemetry and dependable event reporting for small fleets and individual vehicles

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the ET300 delivers location and event telemetry that Plaspy ingests and presents as actionable fleet information. Plaspy converts device data into live tracking views, alert notifications and historical reports so operators can monitor vehicles, respond to incidents, and analyze past trips.

- Real time location updates and map visualization for live monitoring
- Geo fence breach and over speed alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification
- Fuel cutoff events and related immobilizer actions recorded and surfaced in the platform
- Power outage notifications reported to highlight potential tampering or battery removal
- Historical trips and event history accessible for audits, driver coaching and route review

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and remote immobilization for cars and light commercial vehicles using fuel cutoff controls
- Driver behavior monitoring and speed policy enforcement with over speed alerts and trip review
- Small fleet tracking and route optimization through centralized real time visibility and historical data
- Motorcycle and exposed vehicle tracking where a water resistant enclosure is beneficial
- Event based monitoring such as power loss and geofence triggers to support rapid response

The ET300 is well suited to organizations and individual owners who need a durable, no frills GPS tracker that feeds dependable telemetry into a fleet platform. Its IP65 rating and core feature set make it a practical choice for exposed installations and straightforward fleet oversight without introducing unnecessary complexity.
