# Protrack - ET300

![ET300](./tracker.png)

The ET300 GPS Vehicle Tracker is a rugged, IP65-rated device designed for reliable vehicle monitoring and basic fleet control. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the ET300 delivers the essential telemetry and alerting small fleets and individual vehicle owners need — including geofence alerts, over-speed warnings, fuel cutoff control and historical trip recording — packaged in a water-resistant enclosure that tolerates exposed installations.

The ET300 pairs with Plaspy to provide consistent real-time tracking and event reporting for cars, motorcycles and light commercial vehicles. Its focus on durability and dependable data logging makes it a practical GPS tracker for anti-theft protection, driver behavior monitoring, and straightforward fleet management without unnecessary complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure suitable for exposed installations on cars and motorcycles.
- Fuel cutoff / immobilizer capability to support theft prevention and remote vehicle immobilization.
- Power outage detection and reporting so you know when a vehicle loses external power.
- Configurable geo-fence alerts and over-speed warnings to enforce routes and driver policies.
- Historical trip data recording for retrospective route analysis and compliance reporting.
- Designed for reliable basic telemetry and event reporting across small fleets and individual vehicles.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed and connected to Plaspy, the ET300 provides continuous location and event telemetry to the Plaspy platform for real-time tracking, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests the ET300’s position fixes, event triggers and logged trips, turning device data into actionable fleet insights, map views and automated notifications.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Geo-fence breach alerts and over-speed warnings forwarded instantly for immediate action.
- Fuel cutoff \(immobilizer\) events and related controls recorded and available through Plaspy’s alerting tools.
- Power outage detection reported so remote operators can identify tampering or battery removal.
- Logged historical trips and event history accessible in Plaspy for audits, driver coaching and route analysis.
