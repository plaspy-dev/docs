# Reachfar - RF-V40 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the Reachfar RF-V40 when used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive capabilities you can expect from this compact pet tracker and how those capabilities appear and behave within the Plaspy tracking environment. The information below helps owners and integrators understand day-to-day functionality rather than device internals or private configuration steps.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional radio variant and manufacturer implementation. Where manufacturer behavior or specific firmware features are relevant, you should confirm current details with Reachfar documentation and keep device firmware and configuration consistent with your monitoring needs.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V40 is a small, IP66 rated 3G tracker designed primarily for pets and small assets. It combines satellite, assisted and network-based positioning methods to improve accuracy both outdoors and indoors, delivers multiple reporting modes, and provides status alerts that assist in quick recovery workflows.

- Hybrid positioning using GPS with AGPS plus LBS and WiFi assisted fixes for improved indoor and urban accuracy
- Multiple tracking modes including periodic time interval updates, on-demand requests, SMS interface, and reporting to mobile app or web platform
- Alerts for low battery and SIM card change to support anti-theft and rapid response
- Compact, water resistant housing suitable for collars and outdoor use
- Long standby characteristics for extended monitoring between charges based on manufacturer estimates

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V40

- GPS positioning with AGPS assistance and LBS complemented by WiFi assisted location to reduce blind spots
- Multiple reporting modes: configurable time interval tracking, on-demand position queries, SMS and platform reporting
- Low battery notifications and SIM change alerts for timely status awareness and anti-tamper detection
- IP66 rated enclosure designed for outdoor exposure and pet use
- Compact form factor and lightweight design for comfortable collar mounting
- Built-in rechargeable battery with manufacturer estimated standby life suitable for typical pet monitoring cycles
- USB magnetic charging support and basic remote management through SMS, mobile app and web platform

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy collects position and status updates from the RF-V40 and presents them in a unified tracking interface so owners can view location, history and alerts alongside other devices. Plaspy automatically recognizes common tracker protocols and surfaces device events without requiring manual parsing of telemetry.

- Live location updates on Plaspy maps and dashboards using the tracker’s reported GPS/AGPS/LBS/WiFi coordinates
- Trace playback of historical routes and exported location history for review of past movements
- Geo-fence configuration and alerting within Plaspy to receive notifications when a pet leaves or enters defined areas
- Low battery and SIM change alerts delivered through Plaspy notification channels for timely response
- On-demand location queries and immediate position checks initiated from Plaspy or via supported device request methods
- Unified device list and status overview so RF-V40 trackers appear alongside other Plaspy compatible devices in one account

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet safety and recovery monitoring with real-time location and tamper alerts
- Walks and outdoor activities where on-demand checks and route history provide peace of mind
- Indoor and urban tracking scenarios where WiFi assisted fixes and AGPS can improve fix reliability
- Caretaker and boarding oversight to monitor location and battery status while pets are away from home
- Tracking small, portable assets or carriers where compact size and water resistance are important
