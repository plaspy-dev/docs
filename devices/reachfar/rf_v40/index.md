# Reachfar - RF-V40

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V40](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Reachfar RF-V40 is a compact 3G GPS tracker designed primarily for pet owners who need dependable real time location monitoring. It combines GPS positioning with AGPS, LBS and WiFi assisted fixes to improve accuracy both outdoors and inside buildings. Packaged in an IP66 rated enclosure with a small form factor and multiple tracking modes, the RF-V40 is intended for continuous mobile monitoring and quick recovery when a pet goes missing.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V40 integrates into Plaspy dashboards and mobile interfaces to centralize telemetry, alerts and history alongside other managed devices. Live location updates, trace playback and geofence alerts flow into Plaspy for easy viewing and notification, while low battery and SIM change alerts help owners maintain oversight without switching systems.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible pet GPS tracker for real time tracking and location history
- Hybrid positioning using GPS plus AGPS LBS and WiFi assistance to improve indoor fixes
- IP66 water resistant housing for outdoor use in varied weather
- Built in 700 mAh battery offering extended standby performance under typical reporting intervals
- Multiple reporting and query interfaces including on demand checks via SMS and mobile or web platform
- Compact and lightweight design suitable for collars and small pets
- Alerts for SIM change and low battery to support anti theft response

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the RF-V40 becomes part of a unified tracking environment: the tracker reports position and status over cellular data and Plaspy presents that information on maps and timelines for straightforward monitoring. Owners can set rules and notifications, replay historical routes and query a device on demand from the Plaspy interface.

- Real time location updates and device status including battery and connectivity information
- Trace playback and historical location reporting visible in Plaspy dashboards
- Geo fence alerts and low battery notifications delivered through Plaspy notification channels
- SIM change alerts to support tamper detection and anti theft workflows
- On demand location queries from SMS or Plaspy controls for immediate position checks

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet safety and anti theft monitoring with instant alerts and location history
- Walks, exercise and travel tracking with route playback for peace of mind
- Improved indoor and urban positioning where WiFi assisted fixes and AGPS help reduce blind spots
- Remote oversight for boarding, caretaking or pet sitters with battery and location status reporting
- Lightweight tracking of small assets or carrier crates that benefit from compact size and weather resistance

The Reachfar RF-V40 is a focused solution for pet owners who want reliable position reporting in a small, rugged package. Its hybrid positioning approach and weather resistant housing make it a practical choice for everyday outdoor use and for situations where indoor positioning can be a challenge.
