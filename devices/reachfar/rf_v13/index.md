# Reachfar - RF-V13

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V13](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Reachfar RF-V13 is a compact wireless door and window alarm system designed for discreet perimeter security. It combines a magnet sensor for intrusion detection with GSM reporting and basic location awareness to provide remote notifications for homes, shops, and small offices. The small form factor and rechargeable power source make it suitable for unobtrusive mounting on doors, windows, or other access points where lightweight, low-visibility protection is required.

This model is compatible with Plaspy when configured to send its alarm and location reports over GPRS TCP IP or as formatted SMS messages. That compatibility lets the RF-V13 feed event and positioning data into Plaspy for centralized alerting, mapping, and historical logging, making it a relevant choice for customers who need perimeter detection integrated into a broader monitoring platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via GPRS TCP IP reporting or formatted SMS alarm messages for event and location forwarding
- Compact and discreet host suitable for low visibility mounting on doors or windows
- Quad band GSM reporting to deliver remote SMS notifications and TCP IP alarm packets
- Dedicated magnet sensor provides immediate open and close alarm reporting for perimeter points
- LBS plus GPS positioning for contextual location information when GPS is available
- Rechargeable battery and lightweight enclosure designed for simple, unobtrusive deployments
- Two way voice call capability for immediate event verification and response coordination

## How It Works with Plaspy

When set up to work with Plaspy, the RF-V13 forwards alarms and location context to the platform so Plaspy can timestamp events, map positions, and route notifications according to user rules. The device is focused on perimeter alarm reporting rather than continuous fleet telemetry, and Plaspy treats its inputs as discrete alarm events and occasional location updates to support anti theft and access monitoring workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry delivery to Plaspy through GPRS TCP IP packets or via formatted SMS messages
- Door and window alarm events (magnet sensor open and close) forwarded to Plaspy for immediate alerting and audit logs
- Battery and device connectivity status reported so Plaspy can surface device health and low power conditions
- Two way voice call alerts complement platform notifications for fast verification of critical events
- Combine RF V13 event data with other Plaspy devices in the account to create richer monitoring and response rules

## Typical Use Cases

- Home and apartment perimeter monitoring with instant alerts and voice verification
- Small retail or kiosk security where discreet door or window detection is required
- Remote cabin or small asset monitoring when occasional location context is sufficient
- Pop up or mobile point of sale security that benefits from coarse LBS positioning and SMS reporting
- Sensitive entrance monitoring where two way call verification reduces false alarm responses

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V13 is a practical choice for organizations and individuals who need an inexpensive, easy to deploy door or window alarm that can feed events into a central monitoring platform. Its GSM reporting and LBS/GPS positioning are well suited to anti theft and perimeter alert scenarios, and pairing the device with Plaspy adds centralized notification, mapping, and event history for easier operational oversight.
