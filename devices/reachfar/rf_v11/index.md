# Reachfar - RF-V11

![RF-V11](./tracker.jpg)

**ReachFar RF-V11**

ReachFar

RF-V11

The ReachFar RF-V11 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and wireless anti-theft alarm designed to protect small assets, doors, windows and safes without wiring. Combining quad-band GSM connectivity with integrated GPS positioning and multi-mode sensing \(magnetic, vibration and sound\), the RF-V11 delivers discreet, battery-powered protection that reports location and alarm events to owners and central monitoring systems like Plaspy.

Built for installers and end users who need a no-wiring solution, the RF-V11 sends immediate SMS alerts and automatic calls when an alarm is triggered, supports remote SMS commands for configuration and battery queries, and includes a remote listening feature for quiet monitoring of the device surroundings. As a Plaspy compatible unit, it is ideal where real-time tracking and centralized alarm management are required without complex installation.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for centralized monitoring and real-time tracking of small assets.
- Quad-band GSM \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) for wide network coverage and reliable SMS/call alerts.
- Multi-sensor anti-theft protection: magnetic contact, vibration/shock detection, and sound/voice sensor.
- Self-contained, rechargeable battery with USB charging and approximately 12 days standby \(usage-dependent\).
- Automatic alarm SMS and auto-dial to pre-configured numbers for immediate notification.
- Remote listening \(quiet monitoring\) and SMS command set for flexible remote management by installers and owners.
- Very small form factor \(45 x 34 x 14 mm, 29 g\) for discreet installation in safes, doors, shutters and portable assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy enables centralizing the RF-V11's position reports and alarm events into a single dashboard for real-time tracking and alerting. The RF-V11 uses GSM to transmit SMS alerts and can supply GPS coordinates when an alarm or location query is triggered. Plaspy can ingest these reports and present location, event history and basic telemetry to operators and administrators.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GPS position reports can be forwarded to Plaspy for mapping and history.
- Alarm event forwarding — magnetic \(door open/close\), vibration/shock and sound/voice sensor alarms become actionable alerts in Plaspy.
- Remote listening and alert confirmation — the device supports remote calling for quiet monitoring and can be used alongside Plaspy notifications.
- Battery monitoring and low-battery alerts — SMS battery queries and automatic low-battery alerts help maintain uptime and are reflected in central monitoring.
- SMS command-based remote management — standard SMS commands allow remote enabling/disabling of voice detection and magnetic sensor monitoring; Plaspy users can coordinate commands where supported.

## Use Cases

- Safes and strongboxes — discreet GPS tracker and vibration/magnetic alarms for anti-theft protection and recovery support if moved.
- Shopfronts, shutters and doors — perimeter protection with immediate SMS/call alerts on forced entry or cutting attempts.
- Portable asset protection — small, battery-powered form factor ideal for valuables that need temporary or covert monitoring.
- Remote property or storage security — unattended cabins, kiosks and outbuildings where wiring is impractical.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V11 pairs the simplicity of a no-wiring anti-theft GPS tracker with the centralized visibility of Plaspy. Its compact size, multi-sensor alarm set and self-contained battery make it low-maintenance and easy to deploy, while quad-band GSM ensures broad network reach for alarm SMS and calls. When connected to Plaspy, you gain a single pane for real-time tracking, alarm consolidation and basic telemetry so small-object security can be managed alongside larger fleet or asset programs.

Note on functionality: the RF-V11 is optimized for vibration, magnetic and sound-based anti-theft detection and GPS location reporting. Specifications do not list dedicated ignition or immobilizer interfaces, fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensor support; organizations requiring advanced vehicle telemetry, ignition control or BLE sensor integration for fleet management should pair Plaspy with devices that explicitly provide those interfaces. For users focused on discreet anti-theft, perimeter protection and location recovery, the RF-V11 is a reliable, Plaspy compatible choice.
