# Reachfar - RF-V11

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V11](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Reachfar RF-V11 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker combined with a wireless anti theft alarm designed for small assets, doors, windows and safes. It is a no wiring device that pairs GPS positioning with multi mode sensing including magnetic contact, vibration and sound detection to provide discreet protection and location reporting. The RF-V11 is intended for installers and end users needing an easy to deploy alarm and tracking solution for items where running power or data cabling is impractical.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V11 can forward position reports and alarm events into a centralized monitoring environment for consolidated visibility and response. Its SMS and call alert features, remote command capability and compact form factor make it a practical choice when you want centralized tracking and alarm management in Plaspy without complex installation work.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for centralized monitoring and real time tracking of small assets
- Quad band GSM connectivity for wide network reach and reliable SMS and call alerts
- Multi sensor anti theft protection with magnetic contact, vibration shock detection and sound based sensing
- Self contained rechargeable battery with USB charging and approximately 12 days standby depending on use
- Automatic alarm SMS and auto dial notifications to pre configured numbers for immediate alerting
- Remote listening feature and SMS command set for remote management and situational confirmation
- Very small form factor for discreet placement in safes, doors, shutters and portable items

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, RF-V11 events and location reports can be collected into a single dashboard so operators and administrators see alarms and position information alongside other tracked assets. Plaspy ingests the device reports and presents them as mapped positions, event history and centralized alerts for easier operational oversight.

- Real time location updates and history for mapping tracked items in Plaspy
- Alarm event forwarding so magnetic, vibration and sound triggers appear as actionable alerts
- Remote listening and alert confirmation can be coordinated with Plaspy notifications for situational awareness
- Battery monitoring and low battery alerts reflected in central monitoring to help maintain device uptime
- Coordination of SMS based remote management where operators need to query or configure the device

## Typical Use Cases

- Safes and strongboxes where discreet anti theft detection and location reporting are required
- Shopfronts, shutters and doors that need perimeter protection and immediate notification on forced entry
- Portable asset protection for valuables that require temporary or covert monitoring
- Remote property, kiosks and outbuildings where wiring is impractical and standalone protection is needed

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V11 is a sensible option for organisations that need a compact, no wiring alarm and location device that can feed events into a central platform like Plaspy. Its combination of multi mode sensing and GPS location reporting provides both immediate alarm notification and the ability to recover or track an asset when movement occurs. For small object security, perimeter protection and portable assets, the RF-V11 offers low maintenance deployment and straightforward operational integration.
