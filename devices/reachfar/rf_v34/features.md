# Reachfar - RF-V34 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive feature overview for the Reachfar RF-V34 and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical functions visible in Plaspy and the operational value the tracker brings to livestock, grazing, and remote asset scenarios.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. Use this page as general guidance and consult official Reachfar documentation for device specifics before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V34 is a solar-augmented, collar-mountable GPS tracker built for extended outdoor deployments. It is purpose-built for livestock and remote assets where mains charging is unavailable, and it is designed to deliver location and basic telemetry into Plaspy for monitoring and reporting.

- Long runtime with a built-in high capacity battery supplemented by solar charging to reduce maintenance visits.
- Multi-mode positioning using GPS, WiFi, and LBS to retain location awareness in varied environments.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and wide operating temperature range for reliable outdoor use.
- Collar-mount design with included holder and magnetic USB charging for simple field maintenance.
- Plaspy compatible out of the box so location and telemetry can be viewed in real time and in historical reports.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V34

- Solar assisted 9000mAh internal battery for extended standby and multi-day to multi-week deployments.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS plus WiFi and LBS positioning to improve coverage where satellite visibility varies.
- 2G GPRS TCP/IP reporting for sending location and telemetry to cloud platforms such as Plaspy.
- Rugged IP67-rated enclosure for dust and water resistance in pasture and outdoor conditions.
- Collar mountable form factor with the supplied holder designed for animal comfort and secure attachment.
- Magnetic USB charging for convenient maintenance without removing mounting hardware.
- Lightweight compact dimensions suitable for animal attachment (97 × 69 × 31 mm; 255 g).
- Multi-language configuration and display support to simplify regional deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and device telemetry from the RF-V34 so operators can monitor animals and remote assets on maps, receive events, and review historical movement. When configured for operation, the RF-V34's positioning and battery state are presented in Plaspy alongside standard tracking tools.

- Real-time location updates and historical position logs visible in Plaspy mapping and reports.
- Battery and solar charging status reported into Plaspy to help plan maintenance cycles.
- Multi-mode position fixes (GPS, WiFi, LBS) processed by Plaspy to improve situational awareness across coverage conditions.
- Movement and location events such as geofence entries and exits available for alerting and dashboard views in Plaspy.
- Simple deployment workflow: mount the unit, configure reporting intervals, and Plaspy will display the device data for monitoring and analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Livestock location tracking and anti-theft monitoring across large pastures.
- Grazing and pasture management to observe herd distribution and movement over days or weeks.
- Remote asset protection for equipment and temporary installations where power is unavailable.
- Long-term site telemetry collection for seasonal or rotational grazing operations.
- Low-maintenance deployments where solar charging reduces the need for frequent visits.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware differences and hardware revisions can change available settings, telemetry fields, and behavior.
- Cellular coverage and regional network support (for example 2G availability) affect reporting and connectivity.
- Solar charging performance depends on installation angle and local sunlight exposure.
- Reporting intervals and configuration choices directly affect battery life and data granularity.
- Always confirm region specific variants and language options with the manufacturer documentation.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the RF-V34 with Plaspy gives operators a straightforward way to convert long-duration, low-maintenance tracking into actionable monitoring and reporting. Plaspy aggregates location, battery, and event data so teams can monitor animals and remote assets from a single platform, set alerts, and review historical movement patterns to support operational decisions.
