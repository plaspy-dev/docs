# Reachfar - RF-V34

![RF-V34](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V34 is a rugged, solar-powered GPS tracker engineered for long-term outdoor tracking of cattle, sheep and other livestock. Designed for remote pasture monitoring, the RF-V34 pairs extended battery life from a built-in 9000mAh cell with solar charging and IP67 water/dust protection to deliver reliable, low-maintenance tracking in harsh environments. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the RF-V34 brings accurate location and essential telemetry into your Plaspy platform for streamlined herd management and anti-theft monitoring.

Optimized for multi-day deployments where charging access is limited, the RF-V34 supports GPS, WiFi and LBS positioning and transmits via 2G GPRS \(TCP/IP\). Mountable on collars with the included holder and featuring magnetic USB charging for convenient maintenance, the RF-V34 is a practical Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for livestock operations, remote asset protection and grazing management.

## Key Highlights

- Solar-augmented 9000mAh internal battery for extended standby and multi-week operation in remote pastures.
- Plaspy compatible: sends GPS and telemetry via 2G GPRS \(TCP/IP\) for real-time tracking and historical reports on the Plaspy platform.
- Multi-mode positioning \(GPS / WiFi / LBS\) to maintain location coverage in varied environments and limited-satellite areas.
- Rugged, IP67-rated enclosure and -20°C to +70°C operating range for reliable outdoor livestock tracking.
- Easy collar mounting with included holder and magnetic USB charging for quick maintenance without removing the unit.
- Lightweight, compact form \(97 × 69 × 31 mm, 255 g\) built for animal comfort and long-term attachment.
- Supports multiple languages for configuration and platform display to simplify deployment across regions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF-V34 transmits location and device telemetry over 2G GPRS using TCP/IP packets that the Plaspy platform ingests and displays as real-time tracking, geofence events and historical position logs. Plaspy-compatible integration delivers mapping, alerts and reporting without additional gateways: install the RF-V34 on a collar, configure device reporting intervals, and Plaspy will process GPS/WiFi/LBS data to give operators live situational awareness and long-term analytics.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via 2G GPRS \(TCP/IP\).
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS for meter-level fixes, WiFi for intermediate coverage, LBS when satellite visibility is limited.
- Solar charge and battery status reported to Plaspy so you can monitor remaining runtime and plan maintenance.
- Movement-based and location-based alerts \(geofence notifications\) visible in Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps.
- Simple collar-mounted design for livestock tracking and anti-theft workflows managed in Plaspy.

## Use Cases

- Livestock location and anti-theft: continuous tracking of cattle and sheep on large properties, with geofence alerts via Plaspy.
- Grazing and pasture management: monitor herd movements and grazing patterns over weeks or months using long battery life and solar assistance.
- Remote asset protection: track equipment and temporary assets in rural environments where mains power is unavailable.
- Long-term telemetry for remote sites: use Plaspy to analyze historical movement, battery/solar performance and site accessibility.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V34 is a purpose-built Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for agricultural and remote deployments. Its combination of a high-capacity 9000mAh battery, solar charging and IP67-rated ruggedness minimizes maintenance visits and maximizes uptime for distributed livestock operations. By sending location and telemetry over 2G GPRS \(TCP/IP\), the RF-V34 integrates seamlessly into Plaspy for real-time tracking, geofence alerts and long-term reporting — enabling smarter herd management and straightforward anti-theft workflows.

While the RF-V34 focuses on durable, low-power animal tracking rather than vehicle telemetry interfaces \(ignition/immobilizer\) or onboard fuel monitoring, it complements the Plaspy ecosystem. Deploy alongside other Plaspy-compatible sensors or vehicle-focused trackers if you need integrated telemetry like fuel monitoring or immobilizer control. For farms, ranches and rural asset managers who need reliable, long-duration GPS tracking, the RF-V34 offers a low-maintenance, Plaspy compatible solution built for the outdoors.
