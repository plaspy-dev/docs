# Reachfar - RF-V34

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V34](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The RF-V34 is a rugged, solar powered GPS tracker engineered for long term outdoor tracking of cattle, sheep and other livestock. Designed for remote pasture monitoring, it combines a high capacity internal battery with solar charging and an IP67 rated enclosure to reduce maintenance cycles and deliver reliable operation in harsh field conditions. The unit is collar mountable and includes magnetic USB charging for convenient service without complex tools.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the RF-V34 brings location and essential device telemetry into the Plaspy platform for streamlined herd management and anti theft monitoring. Its multi mode positioning and cellular data transmission make it suitable for multi day deployments where regular access to mains power is limited, enabling Plaspy users to monitor animals and remote assets from a single fleet tracking interface.

## Key Highlights

- Solar augmented 9000mAh internal battery for extended standby and multi week operation in remote pastures.
- Plaspy compatible device that transmits location and telemetry to Plaspy for mapping, alerts and reporting.
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, WiFi and LBS to maintain location coverage in varied environments.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and wide operating temperature range for reliable outdoor livestock tracking.
- Collar mounting with included holder and magnetic USB charging for straightforward field maintenance.
- Lightweight compact design to keep long term attachment comfortable for animals.
- Battery and solar status reported so operators can plan maintenance visits effectively.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF-V34 sends location and device telemetry over cellular data to the Plaspy platform, where that information is processed into live maps, event alerts and historical position logs. Once active on the network and linked to a Plaspy account, the device provides persistent visibility of assets and enables configurable notifications and reporting for operational oversight.

- Real time location updates and device telemetry delivered to Plaspy for live tracking.
- Geofence and movement alerts visible in Plaspy dashboards to support anti theft workflows.
- Historical position logs and playback for grazing pattern analysis and operational reviews.
- Battery and solar charging status reported to Plaspy to help schedule maintenance and replacements.
- Simple collar mounted deployment for quick rollout across herds and remote assets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Livestock location and anti theft monitoring on large properties with limited infrastructure.
- Grazing and pasture management to observe herd movement and optimize land use over time.
- Remote asset protection for trailers, equipment and temporary installations in rural areas.
- Long term telemetry collection for seasonal planning and operational reporting.
- Distributed deployments where low maintenance and long battery life are primary requirements.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V34 is tailored for agricultural and remote deployments where durability, long runtime and low maintenance are priorities. Its solar assisted power system and IP67 ruggedness reduce the frequency of site visits, while Plaspy integration turns raw location and telemetry into actionable insights for herd management and security.
