# Reachfar - RF-V03

![RF-V03](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V03 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management. Built for vehicle deployments, the RF-V03 combines multi-mode positioning \(GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS\), low-power design, and vehicle security features so you can monitor routes, playback history and receive timely alarms through the Plaspy platform.

The RF-V03 integrates seamlessly with Plaspy for centralized telemetry, alerts and reporting across international fleets. With a rugged IP66 enclosure, wide 12–72 V DC input range and optional remote oil/power cut-off support, the RF-V03 is a practical choice for anti-theft workflows, route optimization and long-term fleet telemetry when paired with Plaspy’s real-time dashboards.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking, route playback and centralized fleet management.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS to maintain location coverage in diverse environments.
- Vehicle security: geo-fence, over-speed alarm and shearing-line \(cut-wire\) detection.
- Optional remote cut-off for oil and power \(requires specific external hardware\).
- Built-in microphone and G‑sensor enable movement detection and event verification.
- Ultra-low power management with wide operating voltage \(12–72 V DC\) and built-in backup battery.
- Rugged design: IP66 rating and wide temperature range for harsh vehicle environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the RF-V03 sends frequent position and telemetry updates over 2G GPRS \(TCP/IP\). Plaspy ingests GPS/Wi‑Fi/LBS location data and device events, enabling configurable alerts, live map tracking, and historical route playback. Fleet managers use Plaspy rules to translate the RF-V03’s alarms and sensor readings into notifications, automated reports and actionable workflows across vehicles and sites.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via 2G GPRS \(TCP/IP\) for live tracking on Plaspy.
- Geo-fence, over-speed and shearing-line \(cut-wire\) alarms appear as Plaspy alerts for immediate action.
- Historical route playback up to 3 months accessible in Plaspy for audits and incident review.
- Optional remote immobilizer / cut-off \(oil & power\) can be executed through Plaspy when the required external hardware is installed.
- Built-in microphone and G-sensor events report movement and impact to Plaspy for enhanced anti-theft telemetry.

- Fleet management: live tracking, route playback and centralized telemetry for multi-vehicle operations.
- Anti-theft monitoring: geo-fence, cut-wire alarm and G‑sensor events feed Plaspy alerts for rapid response.
- Remote immobilization: support for oil/power cut-off \(with required external hardware\) to secure stolen or misused vehicles.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V03 pairs practicality with proven positioning to deliver dependable Plaspy-compatible tracking for small-to-medium vehicle fleets. Its multi-mode location capabilities \(GPS/Wi‑Fi/LBS\) reduce blind spots while built-in low-power strategies extend device availability on vehicles. Use Plaspy to centralize telemetry, convert RF-V03 alarms into automated workflows, and scale monitoring across locations and languages.

For operations that require additional telemetry like fuel monitoring, Plaspy supports integrating external sensors and workflows; the RF-V03 serves as a robust location and event source within those mixed deployments. Similarly, if immobilizer or ignition-based control is needed, the RF-V03 supports remote cut-off scenarios when the appropriate external hardware is installed and configured through Plaspy, providing a flexible and secure solution for anti-theft and fleet-control policies.
