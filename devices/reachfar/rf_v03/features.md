# Reachfar - RF-V03 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the Reachfar RF-V03 GPS tracker and how those capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is written for users who want a clear overview of what the device can do when integrated into Plaspy for tracking, event visibility, and basic asset monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and specific manufacturer implementation choices. Always check the installed device firmware and the official manufacturer documentation when you need precise device behavior for a given deployment.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V03 is presented as a compact, weather resistant GPS tracker with basic and practical vehicle and asset tracking functions. It is designed to provide both immediate location visibility and post hoc route review, plus a set of alerting features that support security and operational monitoring.

- Dual tracking modes for real time location and historical route playback
- Geo-fence support for virtual boundary entry and exit alerts
- Over-speed alarm to notify when configured speed thresholds are exceeded
- Wire-cut or tamper alert to detect physical disconnection attempts
- Multi platform access for viewing device data across different client interfaces
- Rugged IP66 casing to resist dust and water exposure in typical outdoor use

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V03

- Real time tracking and periodic location reporting for immediate visibility
- Historical route display for reviewing past movements and trip routes
- Geo-fence functionality to define virtual areas and trigger alerts on entry or exit
- Over-speed alarm for configurable speed threshold notifications
- Wire-cut alarm to signal possible tampering or disconnection
- Built in G-sensor for detecting sudden movement or impact events
- AGPS support and a 22 channel GPS module for improved time to first fix
- Support for standard GSM bands to provide cellular connectivity in supported regions

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive location and event reports from the RF-V03 and surface them in the platform for monitoring, alerts, and review. When connected, the device's tracking and alert signals become visible as map positions, events, and timeline entries so operators can act on them.

- Real time positions appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and location awareness
- Historical route data is available for playback and trip analysis inside Plaspy
- Geo-fence triggers generate events and notifications that can be monitored in Plaspy
- Over-speed events are recorded and shown as alerts for operational review
- Wire-cut and G-sensor alerts are presented as tamper or impact events for operator attention
- Device connectivity and basic status indicators are shown so teams can verify reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle location tracking for small fleets and individual vehicles
- Asset protection and tamper detection for valuable equipment
- Route review and trip history analysis for operational audits
- Covert installations where small size and hidden mounting are required
- Geographically enforced operations using geo-fence entry and exit notifications
- Over-speed monitoring to support safety or compliance programs

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and exact behavior depend on device firmware and manufacturer settings
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may change supported GSM bands or certifiable features
- Installation method can affect the effectiveness of wire-cut detection and G-sensor reporting
- Waterproof rating and environmental durability are subject to manufacturer testing and proper installation
- Always confirm which features are enabled on a given device before relying on them operationally

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the RF-V03 combines the device's location and event reporting with Plaspy's visibility and alerting tools. This pairing helps teams keep track of assets, respond to potential tampering or overspeed incidents, and maintain an accessible record of historical movements for analysis.
