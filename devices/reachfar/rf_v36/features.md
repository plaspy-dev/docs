# Reachfar - RF-V36 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Reachfar RF-V36 wearable GPS tracker with Plaspy. It explains the RF-V36 capabilities relevant to caregivers and monitoring teams, and how those capabilities map to Plaspy for real-time tracking, alerts, and ongoing oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and how the device is installed or configured. Always verify device specifics and the latest firmware behavior against the manufacturer documentation and the device in your care before relying on any particular function in production workflows.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V36 is a wristwatch style wearable designed for family safety and remote health awareness. As a Plaspy compatible device, it combines multi-mode positioning, health telemetry, emergency features, and configurable reminders into a single unit to help caregivers maintain situational awareness.

- Wearable form factor for continuous personal monitoring and convenient daily use.
- Multi-mode positioning with outdoor GPS, indoor Wi‑Fi and assisted LBS to improve location coverage across environments.
- Health telemetry including heart rate and blood pressure monitoring reported for remote review.
- SOS emergency button plus two-way audio to enable immediate caregiver contact.
- Configurable reminders and activity alerts such as medication and sedentary prompts.
- IP67 water and dust resistance to support regular wearable use while retaining core functions.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V36

- Wearable wristwatch design optimized for eldercare and continuous personal monitoring.
- Outdoor GPS positioning combined with indoor Wi‑Fi and assisted LBS for better indoor/outdoor coverage.
- G-sensor with inertial drift correction to reduce false location jumps and improve movement reliability.
- Heart rate and blood pressure telemetry available on the device and reportable to monitoring platforms.
- SOS emergency function that triggers alerts to guardians or monitoring teams.
- High quality two-way audio enabling direct voice contact between wearer and caregiver.
- Reminder and activity features such as medication reminders, sedentary alerts, and step counting.
- IP67 rating for water and dust resistance suitable for everyday wearable scenarios.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the RF-V36 is connected to Plaspy, the device sends location, telemetry, and event data into the platform so caregivers can view and act on it from the web and mobile interfaces. Plaspy ingests standard device reports and surfaces them as map locations, timelines, and alerts for easier oversight.

- Real-time and historical location on the Plaspy map using combined GPS Wi‑Fi and assisted LBS data for improved situational awareness.
- Health telemetry such as heart rate and blood pressure visible in Plaspy for trend review and historical analysis where supported.
- SOS events and two-way audio triggers surfaced as high priority alerts so caregivers can respond quickly.
- Double-fence configuration support reflected as boundary events in Plaspy for both outdoor geo-fence and Wi‑Fi based indoor fence configurations.
- Device status and activity reminders delivered through Plaspy notification channels and mobile apps to keep guardians informed.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents device data in standard tracking workflows for visibility and reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elderly safety monitoring with location awareness plus heart rate and blood pressure telemetry for remote caretakers.
- Immediate emergency response workflows where SOS alerts and two-way audio connect caregivers to the wearer.
- Assisted living and home monitoring where indoor Wi‑Fi positioning improves coverage inside facilities.
- Routine care coordination using medication and sedentary reminders to help maintain daily routines.
- Locate and recovery of misplaced wearables within homes or nearby outdoor areas using multi-mode positioning.
- Peace of mind for families who want continuous visibility and a direct communication channel with a loved one.
