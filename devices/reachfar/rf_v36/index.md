# Reachfar - RF-V36

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V36](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The RF-V36 is a wearable GPS tracker in a wristwatch form factor designed for eldercare. It combines outdoor GPS positioning with indoor Wi‑Fi and assisted LBS positioning, a motion aware G-sensor, heart rate and blood pressure monitoring, SOS emergency signaling, two-way audio, and configurable reminders. The watch is built for continuous personal monitoring and includes IP67 water and dust resistance for everyday wear.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V36 feeds location, health telemetry and event alerts into Plaspy’s platform so caregivers and monitoring teams can view real time status, receive guardian notifications, and maintain historical records. This compatibility makes the RF-V36 a practical option for family safety workflows where actionable location and personal health information are combined in a single wearable device.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable form factor tailored for eldercare with integrated GPS and indoor positioning options.
- Multi mode positioning with outdoor GPS, indoor Wi‑Fi, assisted LBS and motion corrected readings from a G sensor.
- Built in health telemetry for heart rate and blood pressure visible to guardians through paired platforms.
- Emergency features including an SOS button and two way audio for immediate caregiver contact.
- Care and activity reminders such as medication and sedentary alerts plus step awareness for daily routines.
- IP67 rating for protection against dust and water exposure during normal daily use.
- Flexible control and notification methods including web and mobile access, WeChat public account support and SMS controls.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the RF-V36 delivers continuous location and personal telemetry into a centralized dashboard so caregivers can monitor status, receive alerts, and produce reports. Plaspy collects the device updates and makes them available across web and mobile interfaces, enabling configurable notifications and historical review.

- Real time location updates for outdoor and indoor contexts, with motion corrected position readings.
- Health telemetry reporting for heart rate and blood pressure to support remote monitoring and trend analysis.
- SOS alerting and two way voice routed through Plaspy workflows to ensure timely guardian notification.
- Configurable double fence geofencing including outdoor geo boundaries and Wi‑Fi based indoor fencing for intelligent alerts.
- Centralized visibility across web and mobile apps to manage devices, alerts, and historical location and telemetry data.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elderly safety and remote health monitoring where guardians need location plus vital sign awareness.
- Daily care management with medication and sedentary reminders to support routines and reduce missed care tasks.
- Indoor and outdoor location assurance in homes, assisted living facilities and community outings.
- Locating misplaced wearables and detecting unexpected removal or unusual movement.
- Family peace of mind through immediate voice contact and rapid SOS notifications.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V36 is focused on personal safety and health telemetry rather than vehicle or heavy asset monitoring. For organizations and families using Plaspy, this watch offers a compact way to combine location awareness with basic vital sign monitoring and immediate alerting. Plaspy’s platform can centralize RF-V36 data alongside other compatible devices, enabling caregivers to see location and telemetry in one place and configure the notification rules that suit their workflows.

Because the RF-V36 centers on individual care scenarios, it is a good fit for guardians, small care teams and monitoring services that need straightforward wearable tracking and emergency response. For deployments requiring different telemetry types or vehicle oriented features, Plaspy supports other compatible trackers that extend beyond the personal care capabilities of the RF-V36.
