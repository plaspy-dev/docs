# Reachfar - RF-V36

![RF-V36](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V36 is a wearable GPS tracker in the form of a wristwatch designed for eldercare, combining reliable location services with health monitoring and emergency features. As a Plaspy compatible device, it brings real-time tracking and guardian alerts to family safety workflows: GPS outdoor positioning, indoor Wi‑Fi and assisted LBS positioning, and a G-sensor with inertial drift correction reduce false location jumps so caregivers can trust the watch’s reported position. The RF-V36 also integrates heart rate and blood pressure monitoring, SOS emergency, two-way audio and configurable fences for continuous, actionable oversight.

The RF-V36 targets family safety and remote health awareness by bundling telemetry \(heart rate and blood pressure\), medication and sedentary reminders, and voice communication into a single IP67-rated wearable. Note: this product page indicates the RF-V36 is marked as out of production; however, devices already in the field remain useful when paired with Plaspy’s platform for ongoing real-time tracking, alerts and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker in wearable form — easy integration for real-time tracking and guardian notifications.
- Multi-mode positioning: outdoor GPS, indoor Wi‑Fi, assisted LBS and G-sensor with inertial drift correction for more accurate location data.
- Built-in health telemetry: 2-in-1 heart rate and blood pressure detection available locally on the watch and remotely through the companion app.
- Emergency-ready features including an SOS button and high-quality two-way audio calls for immediate caregiver contact.
- Care reminders and daily activity telemetry — pill/medicine reminders, sedentary reminders and step counting visible to guardians.
- IP67 water and dust resistance — wearable during everyday use including showering while retaining SOS functionality.
- Flexible platform controls: web tracking, Android/iOS apps, WeChat public account integration and SMS control, plus configurable double-fence protection \(outdoor geo-fence and indoor Wi‑Fi fence\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the RF-V36 delivers continuous, actionable data to caregivers and monitoring teams. Location updates, health telemetry and event alerts from the watch are transmitted via the device’s supported positioning and communication channels into Plaspy’s dashboard and mobile apps for real-time tracking, reporting and automated notifications.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GPS position outdoors, Wi‑Fi and assisted LBS when indoors, and motion-corrected readings from the G-sensor.
- Health telemetry: heart rate and blood pressure values reported to Plaspy for remote monitoring and historical trend analysis.
- SOS button and two-way audio — instant alerting to guardians with voice contact capability from the watch through Plaspy’s notification flows.
- Double-fence geofencing — configurable outdoor geo-fence and Wi‑Fi-based indoor fence generate intelligent boundary alerts for movement in and out of safe zones.
- Platform and control interfaces — web platform tracking, Android/iOS apps, WeChat public account positioning and SMS control for flexible management and alerts.

## Use Cases

- Elderly safety and remote health monitoring — guardians receive heart rate, blood pressure and location updates and can respond to SOS alerts immediately.
- Daily-care automation — medication reminders and sedentary alerts help maintain routines and reduce missed doses or prolonged inactivity.
- Indoor/outdoor location assurance — double-fence protection and Wi‑Fi positioning provide better coverage in homes, assisted living facilities and outdoor outings.
- Lost-item or anti-theft assistance for personal wearables — the RF-V36 helps locate a misplaced watch or detect unexpected removal or movement.
- Family peace of mind — two-way audio and phonebook dialing let caregivers maintain direct contact and intervene quickly when necessary.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the RF-V36 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker combines device-level health telemetry with Plaspy’s platform strengths: centralized real-time tracking, configurable alerts, and accessible mobile and web tools for caregivers. The RF-V36 is optimized for personal safety rather than vehicle telemetry — it focuses on heart rate and blood pressure monitoring, SOS, two-way voice and indoor/outdoor positioning rather than vehicle-specific features such as fuel monitoring, ignition sensing or immobilizer controls. Plaspy, however, supports a wide ecosystem of compatible trackers and can correlate RF-V36 personal telemetry and location data with other asset or fleet data where needed.

In practice, the RF-V36 with Plaspy delivers reliable, easy-to-read telemetry, prompt SOS workflows, and granular location context for family members and care providers. Because the RF-V36 is shown as out of production on the product page, check device availability and firmware/support status before purchase. For installations that require vehicle-focused telemetry \(fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer control\) or Bluetooth sensor ecosystems, Plaspy also supports other compatible trackers that provide those specific features alongside the RF-V36’s personal-care capabilities.
