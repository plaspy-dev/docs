# Reachfar - RF-V03-OBD

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V03-OBD](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The RF-V03-OBD is a plug-and-play OBDII GPS tracker designed for rapid deployment and simple fleet integration. It connects directly to a vehicle J1962 OBDII port to provide real time location, ignition status and basic vehicle diagnostics without the need for hardwiring. The form factor and standardized OBD interface make it well suited for mixed fleets, rental vehicles and logistics vehicles where quick, non invasive installation is a priority.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V03-OBD delivers telemetry into the Plaspy platform so fleet managers can view live positions, receive alerts and review historical routes within their existing workflows. Its support for multiple cellular variants, backup data storage and event alerts aligns with common Plaspy use cases for monitoring, security and operational reporting, making it a practical choice when adding OBD based trackers to a Plaspy managed fleet.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBDII installation for fast deployment via the vehicle J1962 port.
- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking, alerts and historical playback via SMS, mobile app and web.
- Multiple cellular variants to match regional connectivity needs.
- Ignition ACC status reporting and basic vehicle diagnostics through the OBD interface.
- Built in backup battery and dead zone data storage to preserve records during connectivity loss.
- Vibration alarm and geo fence alerts for anti theft and unauthorized movement notification.
- Historical route playback and event logging for compliance and operational review.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed, the RF-V03-OBD reports GPS positions and vehicle events over its cellular link to be consumed by Plaspy. Plaspy ingests these feeds for mapping, alerting and reporting so fleet teams can monitor vehicles in real time and analyze past activity. Local storage on the tracker ensures data continuity when coverage is interrupted and synchronizes with Plaspy once a connection is restored.

- Real time location and route visualization inside Plaspy for live fleet oversight.
- Ignition and engine status updates for tracking engine on off events and driver patterns.
- Basic OBD based diagnostics delivered to Plaspy for maintenance planning and operational insight where supported by the vehicle.
- Geo fence and vibration alarm events integrated into Plaspy alerts and notification rules.
- Dead zone data sync and historical route playback to recover missed telemetry after outages.
- Voice monitoring options on selected variants can be considered for security workflows subject to plan support.
