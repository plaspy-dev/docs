# Reachfar - RF-V03-OBD

![RF-V03-OBD](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V03-OBD is a plug-and-play OBDII GPS tracker built for rapid deployment and easy fleet integration. Designed to connect directly to a vehicle’s J1962 OBDII port, this Plaspy compatible tracker delivers real-time tracking, ignition \(ACC\) status, basic vehicle diagnostics and alarm events without the need for hardwiring—ideal for fleet managers, rental agencies and logistics teams who need fast, non-invasive vehicle telematics.

With multiple cellular variants \(CAT1, CAT4 and CAT-M1/NB2\), a built-in backup battery, vibration and geo-fence alerts, and three-way access via SMS, mobile app and web platform, the RF-V03-OBD offers reliable telemetry and playback for theft recovery, driver behavior monitoring and routine fleet management. Its compact form factor and standardized OBD interface simplify installation across a wide range of car models, enabling Plaspy users to enable GPS tracker capabilities quickly and securely.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBDII installation: fast deployment via the J1962 OBDII port—no wiring needed.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and fleet management through SMS, App and Web.
- Multi-variant cellular support \(CAT1, CAT4, CAT-M1/NB2\) to match regional band requirements.
- Engine/ignition \(ACC\) detection and basic vehicle diagnostics via OBD for telemetry and driver behavior insights.
- Voice monitoring available on GSM and LTE CAT4 models for added security and situational awareness.
- Built-in backup battery and dead-zone storage \(over 2,000 records\) to preserve data during network outages.
- Vibration alarm, geo-fence alerts and historical route playback for anti-theft and compliance reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF-V03-OBD sends location and vehicle telemetry to Plaspy using its cellular connection and OBDII interface. Once plugged into the vehicle, the tracker reports GPS positions, ignition status \(ACC\), vibration and geo-fence events and stores records locally when connectivity is lost. Plaspy receives these feeds for live mapping, alerts and historical playback, making it straightforward to add this GPS tracker to an existing fleet management workflow.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and route visualization.
- Ignition/engine \(ACC\) status reporting for driver behavior and engine-on/off event logging.
- Basic vehicle diagnostics via the OBD interface—can include fuel-related diagnostics where supported by the vehicle and OBD PIDs.
- Voice monitoring \(GSM & LTE CAT4 variants\) available for security or safety use cases; check your Plaspy plan for voice support options.
- Geo-fence, vibration alarm and history route playback integrated into Plaspy alerts and reports; dead-zone data syncs when connection resumes.

## Technical Overview

| --- | --- |
