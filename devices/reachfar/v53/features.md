# Reachfar - V53 Features

This page provides a public, user focused overview of the Reachfar V53 and how its functions map to monitoring and reporting in Plaspy. It summarizes the practical capabilities you can expect when the V53 is used in a Plaspy deployment, including location updates, pet telemetry, and locating tools that help with everyday tracking and lost pet recovery.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and installation method. Some functions described here depend on the device firmware and the manufacturer implementation; always verify device specific behavior against the official Reachfar documentation and any release notes from the manufacturer.

## Feature Overview

The Reachfar V53 is a compact collar mounted pet tracker built for continuous location visibility and pet focused telemetry. When paired with Plaspy, its position and status updates are available for live monitoring, historical review, and alerting through Plaspy's platform.

- Real time GNSS positioning with multi GNSS support for improved outdoor fixes and faster acquisition.
- LTE 4G connectivity for continuous transmission of location and telemetry to a cloud platform.
- Pet focused telemetry including an activity pedometer, low battery alerts, and night locating tools.
- Ringtone and rolling LED lights for locating a pet in low light, plus a physical collar anti tamper lock for secure mounting.
- Dual GPS and Wi Fi assisted geofencing for better detection in urban and indoor scenarios.
- Two way voice call capability for remote audio monitoring and direct communication.

## Core Features of Reachfar - V53

- Plaspy compatible device for live tracking and historical route playback.
- Multi GNSS positioning including GPS BeiDou and GLONASS for stronger outdoor performance.
- LTE 4G connectivity for prompt delivery of position and status updates.
- IP67 rated enclosure for water and dust resistance suitable for daily pet use.
- Physical collar anti tamper lock to reduce accidental detachment.
- Activity pedometer and related telemetry for monitoring pet movement.
- Low battery alerts to notify owners of power status.
- Two way voice and audible locate functions plus rolling LED lights for night locating.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the V53 position and telemetry feeds to present live location, historical routes, and alert events in a consolidated interface. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents received data in the platform for monitoring and notifications.

- Live position updates and historical route playback become available in Plaspy for situational awareness and review.
- Dual GPS plus Wi Fi assisted geofence events can be configured as safe zones in Plaspy to trigger departure or entry alerts.
- Activity and low battery notifications can be delivered through Plaspy as alerts or logged events for later analysis.
- Ringtone and LED locate commands and two way voice calls are represented as device actions and status in Plaspy when supported by the device and deployment.
- Device telemetry and status are consolidated with other Plaspy managed devices for unified monitoring and reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Everyday pet tracking to know live location and review daily movement patterns.
- Lost pet recovery using geofence departure alerts combined with ringtone LED locate and two way voice.
- Monitoring pet activity using pedometer logs to understand exercise and rest trends.
- Night time locating in low light environments using LED lights and ringtone functions.
- Better indoor and urban coverage through combined GNSS and Wi Fi assisted geofencing.
- Centralized monitoring of multiple pet trackers in Plaspy for households or small operators.
