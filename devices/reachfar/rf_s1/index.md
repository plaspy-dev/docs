# RF-S1 GPS Tracking Health Smart Watch

The RF-S1 GPS Tracking Health Smart Watch is a wearable personal GPS tracker designed for continuous health supervision and dependable real-time tracking. Built for caregivers and families, the RF-S1 combines precise positioning, SOS panic button support, two-way voice calls and removal alerts to deliver fast, actionable notifications. As a Plaspy compatible device, it integrates directly with the Plaspy platform to bring location, health telemetry and emergency events into a single dashboard for easier monitoring and response.

The RF-S1 is focused on personal safety and remote care: it monitors heart rate, blood oxygen and body temperature, supports medicine reminders and announces alarm voice notices while remaining rugged for everyday use with an IP67 water resistance rating. When paired with Plaspy, the watch turns into a reliable node for real-time tracking, caregiver alerts and secure two-way voice contact — ideal for elder care, child safety and remote patient supervision.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and centralized telemetry on the Plaspy dashboard.
- SOS panic button and two-way voice calls for immediate contact with caregivers or emergency contacts.
- Continuous health telemetry: heart rate monitoring, pulse diagnosis and blood oxygen \(SpO2\) reporting.
- Removal alarm and real-time alerts to prevent loss and notify caregivers promptly.
- IP67 water resistance for reliable daily wear — safe for hand washing and light splashes.
- Medicine reminders, customizable alarms and voice reminders that support routine care and adherence.
- Designed for fast response: predefined number dialing and voice chat features simplify emergency communication.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF-S1 delivers location and health data that Plaspy ingests and displays in real time. When worn, the watch reports position and telemetry to Plaspy, enabling live monitoring, historical playback and configurable alerts. Emergency events such as SOS presses, removal alarms and critical health thresholds route immediately to Plaspy so caregivers can act quickly. Integration is optimized for personal safety workflows rather than vehicle telematics, but it complements Plaspy’s broader platform capabilities.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates visible on Plaspy maps and reports.
- SOS panic button events and predefined-number dialing logged as high-priority alerts.
- Health telemetry: heart rate, pulse diagnosis and blood oxygen data streamed as status updates.
- Removal alarm and voice reminder events forwarded to Plaspy for instant notification.
- Voice call records and message events available for caregiver follow-up when allowed by the device and platform configuration.
