# Reachfar - RF-V43

![RF-V43](./Logo.jpg)

The Reachfar RF-V43 is a compact GPS tracker built primarily for pet safety and location monitoring. It uses 4G network connectivity together with three location methods — GPS, LBS, and WiFi — to provide continuous position updates and fallback options when GPS alone may be limited. The device includes practical features for everyday pet use such as configurable GPS and WiFi geofences, a remotely controllable LED light to aid visibility, sport activity reporting, a calling feature for two-way audio when needed, IP67 weather protection, and fast charging with a low battery alert to help maintain uptime.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the RF-V43 can be integrated into Plaspy’s tracking environment to provide visibility and alerts alongside other devices. When paired with Plaspy, the RF-V43’s location data, geofence events, activity summaries, and battery warnings become accessible within a unified monitoring platform. This makes the RF-V43 a practical option for pet owners and small operations that want to view location history, receive boundary alerts, and include pet trackers in broader operational dashboards managed in Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Real-time tracking over 4G with GPS, LBS, and WiFi location modes for layered coverage
- Configurable GPS-fence and WiFi-fence alerts to notify when a pet leaves a designated area
- IP67 rating for reliable use in wet or dusty outdoor conditions
- Built-in LED light for improved visibility in low light and a calling function for direct communication
- Activity or sport data to monitor pet movement and general exercise levels
- Fast charging and low battery alarm to reduce downtime and help maintain continuous tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF-V43 sends location updates and device status that Plaspy can display on maps, timelines, and device lists. Plaspy can surface the tracker’s position, geofence events, and status notifications so you can monitor pets alongside other tracked assets. Where the tracker exposes remote functions, those controls can be represented in Plaspy according to the configured integration.

- Live location and last known position visible on Plaspy maps for easy monitoring
- Geofence and WiFi-fence alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notifications
- Activity summaries and movement history available in Plaspy reports and timelines
- Battery and device status alerts routed into Plaspy so you can manage device uptime
- Grouping and tagging in Plaspy to organize multiple pet trackers or devices in one view

- Geofence alerts for yards, dog parks, or temporary containment areas

- Managing multiple pet devices from a single dashboard for small kennels or caretakers
