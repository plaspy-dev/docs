# Reachfar - RF-V16 Features

This page provides a public, feature-oriented view of the Reachfar RF-V16 and how its core capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the RF-V16’s practical functions for real-time tracking, emergency alerts, and monitoring through the Plaspy platform so organizations and caregivers can evaluate fit for personal safety and on-foot tracking scenarios.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and manufacturer implementation. For critical deployments, review the RF-V16 user manual and the manufacturer’s specifications to confirm current behavior and supported functions.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V16 is a super-mini GPS SOS tracker designed for rapid location reporting and simple emergency response. It combines a compact on-person form factor with GPS positioning, quad-band GSM connectivity, one-touch SOS, and two-way voice to support quick situational awareness and alerts through Plaspy.

- Compact and lightweight design for discreet on-person use, suitable for children, seniors, and lone workers.
- SiRF Star IV GPS positioning with typical accuracy for pedestrian and personal tracking use.
- One-touch SOS alarm that sends location and initiates auto-calls to configured emergency contacts.
- Two-way voice and remote listening to support immediate assessment during an alarm.
- Multi-channel reporting options including internet reporting and SMS for redundancy in alerts.
- Alerts for SIM change and low battery to improve theft awareness and device uptime.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V16

- SiRF Star IV GNSS receiver for fast fixes and position reporting under open-sky conditions.
- Quad-band GSM with GPRS TCP/IP reporting for sending location and event data to cloud platforms.
- One-touch SOS alarm with automatic calling to multiple preset emergency numbers.
- Two-way voice calling and remote listening for live audio during emergencies.
- Multi-channel notification and access options including web portal, mobile apps, WeChat, and SMS.
- Geo-fence and movement (displacement) alarm support for location-based event reporting.
- SIM-change and low-battery alerts to notify guardians or administrators of potential issues.
- Historical trace logging and replay available when data is recorded by the tracking platform.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When configured to report to Plaspy, the RF-V16 sends position updates and alarm events so Plaspy can present location and status to administrators and designated contacts. Plaspy ingests the device events and location packets to enable real-time tracking, alerting, and historical review.

- Real-time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness.
- SOS alarms and related events create notifications and can be routed to emergency contacts configured in Plaspy.
- Device alerts such as low battery or SIM change are captured and surfaced in Plaspy for operational response.
- Historical traces and event logs are stored and available for replay and audit within Plaspy.
- Multi-channel report events (e.g., SMS fallback) are reflected as status or alarm entries so teams can verify delivery paths.
- Plaspy shows event timestamps and location context while the RF-V16 manages voice calls and remote audio through its telephony functions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety for children, elderly relatives, and vulnerable individuals needing a portable SOS device.
- Lone-worker protection for on-foot staff who require emergency call and remote listening capabilities.
- Short-range personnel tracking for security teams and field staff without vehicle telematics needs.
- Rapid response when a compact device must provide location and voice communication to caregivers or control centers.
- Asset protection for portable equipment where immediate location and SIM-change alerts are valuable.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions and manufacturer configuration can change available features and alarm behavior.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may affect RF bands, network compatibility, or reporting behavior.
- Installation and on-person placement influence GPS performance and reported accuracy in practice.
- Network operator restrictions and SIM provisioning affect whether GPRS or SMS fallback is available.
- Confirm feature lists such as auto-calling limits, geo-fence behavior, and low-battery thresholds with current manufacturer documentation.
