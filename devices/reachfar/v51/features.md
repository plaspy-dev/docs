# Reachfar - V51 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Reachfar V51 personal GPS tracker with Plaspy. It outlines the device capabilities that are relevant for centralized monitoring, caregiver alerts, and real time location visibility inside the Plaspy platform. The focus is practical and non sensitive so organizations and caregivers can understand how the V51 behaves in typical deployments and what to expect when integrating it with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional SKU, and how the device is installed or configured. The information below is grounded in the V51 product description and is intended to be a clear, practical guide; always verify current device specifics with the manufacturer and test devices before operational rollout.

## Feature Overview

The Reachfar V51 is a compact 4G personal tracker built for everyday wear and carry, optimized for personal safety monitoring and emergency response. It combines multi network cellular connectivity with satellite positioning and caregiver focused utilities to deliver continuous visibility and timely alerts for vulnerable users.

- Real time tracking for live position visibility through Plaspy
- Dual satellite positioning with GPS plus Beidou for improved outdoor accuracy
- SOS and automatic fall detection to alert caregivers quickly
- Historical route playback and geofencing for movement review and boundary alerts
- IP67 rated housing for protection against water and dust in daily use
- Built in user utilities such as pill reminders, pedometer, talking clock, and remote ring to support daily routines and locate the device

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the V51 telemetry and events so caregivers and administrators can see live location, receive safety alerts, and review historical movement from a centralized dashboard. Integration lets organizations manage personal trackers alongside other assets and monitor device events and status in one place.

- Live position updates and map visualization for situational awareness
- SOS and fall alerts forwarded to Plaspy notification channels so caregivers can respond promptly
- Geofence event notifications and route playback available in Plaspy for audits and trend review
- Device status alerts such as low battery or SIM change surfaced in the platform to support reliable operation
- Centralized management that keeps personal safety trackers in the same operational view as other deployed devices
