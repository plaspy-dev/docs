# Reachfar - RF-V32 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Reachfar RF-V32 and how the device can be used with Plaspy for location visibility, alarm reporting, and historical trace review. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive descriptions of capabilities and how those capabilities are reflected inside Plaspy rather than detailed configuration steps.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional model, and the manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, the content below is based on the RF-V32 description and should be confirmed against the latest Reachfar documentation or firmware notes before purchase or deployment.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V32 is a compact, rugged tracker aimed at pets and livestock with multiple positioning methods and online reporting to Plaspy. It is designed to provide reliable outdoors GPS tracking with fallback options for weak-signal or indoor scenarios, plus alarm reporting and historical route playback for post-event review.

- Multi-mode positioning using GPS with A-GPS plus LBS and WiFi fallback to improve coverage in mixed environments.
- Online reporting via GPRS TCP/IP and SMS reporting for real-time updates and alarm delivery into Plaspy.
- IP67 rated enclosure and a compact lightweight form factor suitable for collars and outdoor use.
- Built-in alarms including geofence breach, low battery, and SIM-change to notify users through Plaspy.
- Historical trace playback support so movement can be reviewed in Plaspy after events or for behavior analysis.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V32

- GPS with A-GPS assistance and GNSS positioning for outdoor location fixes.
- LBS and WiFi positioning as fallback methods to improve indoor or weak-signal location estimates.
- GPRS TCP/IP reporting with SMS support for status and alarm transmission.
- Geofence alarm reporting plus low battery and SIM-change alerts to aid rapid response.
- IP67 rugged enclosure for water resistance and outdoor durability.
- Compact dimensions and light weight suitable for pet or livestock mounting.
- Built-in GSM/GPS antenna and quad-band GSM connectivity for broad cellular coverage.
- Trace playback capability for historical route review within compatible platforms.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the RF-V32 reports location and status, Plaspy ingests those updates and presents them through its mapping and alerting interfaces. Plaspy provides a single view of active locations, recent movement, and alarm events so owners and managers can act on near real time information.

- Real-time location updates appear on the Plaspy map when the device uploads using GPRS TCP/IP or when SMS reports are relayed into the platform.
- Geofence, low battery, and SIM-change alerts are forwarded into Plaspy so users can receive notifications and see the related event on the map.
- Trace playback data collected by the RF-V32 is available in Plaspy for historical route visualization and incident review.
- A-GPS, LBS, and WiFi fallback help improve the usefulness of location updates inside Plaspy in mixed indoor/outdoor environments.
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker reporting formats to simplify integration and reduce manual configuration steps.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet recovery and roaming detection for companion animals with real-time location and alerts.
- Livestock monitoring for grazing pattern visibility and locating strays across pasture.
- Rural property protection by monitoring animals near fences, gates, or sensitive areas.
- Historical movement analysis for animal behavior studies or incident investigation.
- Lightweight collar tracking where device size, battery life, and water resistance are important.

Pairing the RF-V32 with Plaspy gives owners and small managers a centralized way to view live locations, receive alarms, and replay historical movement.
