# Reachfar - RF-V32

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V32](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The RF-V32 is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for pets and livestock. It combines GPS positioning with LBS, A-GPS and WiFi fallback to provide location visibility in both outdoor and challenging signal environments. Built to withstand field use, the RF-V32 offers IP67 protection, an integrated GSM and GPS antenna, quad band GSM connectivity and multi mode reporting methods suitable for collar mounting and regular animal tracking tasks.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V32 can provide the core location and alarm data Plaspy requires for live monitoring and historical review. Its ability to report location via GPRS and SMS and to generate geofence, low battery and SIM change alarms means Plaspy users can view real time positions, receive timely notifications and replay movement history for pet recovery or livestock management scenarios.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via GPRS reporting and SMS integration for location and alarms
- Multi mode positioning with GPS plus LBS A GPS and WiFi fallback for mixed environments
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure suited for pet and livestock use in outdoor conditions
- Long standby characteristics to reduce maintenance intervals for deployed units
- Compact lightweight design that is easy to mount on collars and light enough for animals
- Built in alarm reporting for geofence breaches low battery and SIM change events
- Historical trace playback available through Plaspy for post event review and analysis

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration is straightforward: the RF-V32 transmits location and status messages which Plaspy ingests to display live positions and manage alerts. Plaspy provides a unified dashboard where owners and managers can monitor animals, configure geofences, and access historical movement data collected by the device.

- Live location visualization in Plaspy using data sent from the RF-V32 for near real time tracking
- Alarms such as geofence breach low battery and SIM change forwarded into Plaspy for notifications
- Historical trace playback uploaded by the device and viewable in Plaspy for route review
- Positioning reliability enhanced by A GPS and WiFi fallback to improve indoor or weak signal coverage
- Device focus on animal tracking makes it well suited for Plaspy deployments centered on pets and livestock

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet recovery and monitoring for dogs and other companion animals with collar attachment
- Livestock tracking to observe grazing patterns locate strays and manage herd movement
- Rural property and perimeter protection using geofences and alarm notifications
- Post event movement analysis and behavioral review using trace playback in Plaspy
- Small scale animal management where compact rugged hardware and long standby are priorities

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V32 is a practical choice for organizations and individuals who need a compact rugged tracker that pairs with Plaspy for reliable location visibility and basic alarm reporting. Its combination of GPS assisted positioning with LBS and WiFi fallback, plus IP67 protection and built in reporting, aligns well with common animal tracking requirements where consistent updates and low maintenance are important.

If your primary needs are real time tracking geofence management and historical playback for pets or livestock, the RF-V32 and Plaspy together provide a focused solution. For deployments requiring vehicle specific telemetry advanced sensor integrations or Bluetooth sensor ecosystems you may want to consider alternate Plaspy compatible models that include those features.
