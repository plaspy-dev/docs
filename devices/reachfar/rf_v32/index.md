# RF-V32 Pet & Animal GPS Tracker — Plaspy Compatible

The RF-V32 is a compact, rugged GPS tracker engineered for pets and livestock and fully positioned here as a Plaspy compatible device for reliable location visibility. With GPS, LBS, A-GPS and WiFi positioning built in, the RF-V32 offers flexible real-time tracking for outdoor ranges and fallback positioning for indoor or weak-signal scenarios. Its IP67 waterproof rating, integrated GSM/GPS antenna and quad-band GSM connectivity make it a practical choice for owners and small-scale managers who need consistent location updates and alarm reporting through Plaspy's platform.

Designed for long standby operation and straightforward integration, the RF-V32 supports online tracking via GPRS \(TCP/IP\), SMS, mobile app and web platform access. When used with Plaspy, the RF-V32 delivers location, historical trace playback and alarms — enabling pet owners and livestock managers to monitor animals, configure geofences and receive low-battery or SIM-change alerts in near real time.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates with Plaspy via GPRS \(TCP/IP\) and supports SMS/app/web reporting for location and alarms.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS with LBS, A-GPS and WiFi fallback for reliable tracking indoors and outdoors.
- Rugged & waterproof: IP67-rated enclosure for livestock and outdoor pet use in varied environments.
- Long standby battery life: built-in 400 mAh battery with up to ~300 hours GSM standby \(GPS off\) for extended deployments.
- Compact, lightweight form factor: easy to mount on collars for dogs, sheep or cattle without burdening the animal.
- Comprehensive alerts: geofence breach, low battery and SIM-change alarms to help protect animals and enable rapid response.
- Trace playback: historical route playback for post-event review and movement analysis through Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is straightforward because the RF-V32 transmits location and status information over GPRS \(TCP/IP\) and supports standard reporting methods \(SMS, app and web\). Plaspy ingests the device’s location and alarm payloads and provides a unified dashboard for real-time tracking, alerts, and historical analysis. For animal tracking use cases, this means faster recovery from escapes, better grazing management and easier monitoring of roaming behavior.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GPRS \(TCP/IP\) for live map visualization in Plaspy.
- Alarms forwarded to Plaspy: geofence breach, low battery and SIM-change alerts for timely notifications.
- Trace playback: historical route data uploaded and viewable in Plaspy for route analysis and incident review.
- WiFi anti-lost alarm and A-GPS enhance indoor or weak-signal positioning to improve accuracy in mixed environments.
- Note on vehicle telemetry: the RF-V32 is focused on pet and livestock tracking. It does not list vehicle-specific telemetry features such as ignition, immobilizer or fuel monitoring — for those fleet management features, other Plaspy-compatible devices are available.

## Use Cases

- Pet recovery: attach to dog collars to enable Plaspy real-time tracking and quick location if a companion animal goes missing.
- Livestock monitoring: track grazing patterns and herd movement for sheep and cattle to optimize pasture use and locate strays.
- Rural property protection: geofence sensitive areas and receive alerts via Plaspy when animals cross boundaries or enter restricted zones.
- Historical route analysis: use trace playback in Plaspy to investigate movement over time for animal behavior studies or incident reviews.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

For pet owners and livestock managers who want a compact, rugged GPS tracker that integrates with Plaspy, the RF-V32 delivers dependable outdoor GPS accuracy plus LBS/A-GPS/WiFi fallback for mixed environments. Its long battery standby and IP67 protection reduce maintenance and replacement frequency, while built-in reporting via GPRS and SMS ensures Plaspy receives timely location and alarms. Though this device is tailored to animals rather than vehicles — and does not include vehicle-specific telemetry like ignition, immobilizer or fuel monitoring — it is a practical, cost-conscious choice when Plaspy real-time tracking, geo-fencing and historical playback are the primary needs.

Before purchasing, note the RF-V32 is currently listed as out of production, so stock may be limited. Contact the manufacturer or an authorized distributor for availability, warranty support or recommendations for newer Plaspy-compatible models that include additional telemetry \(ignition, fuel monitoring\) or Bluetooth sensor support if those features are required.
