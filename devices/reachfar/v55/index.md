# Reachfar - V55

![V55](./tracker.jpg)

V55 4G Long Standby GPS Tracker

The V55 4G Long Standby GPS Tracker is a compact, rugged GPS tracker engineered for reliable, long-term monitoring of vehicles and assets. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the V55 combines multi-network cellular coverage with satellite and alternative positioning to deliver accurate real-time tracking and robust telemetry for fleet management, asset protection, and outdoor deployments.

Designed for field use, the V55 supports GPS and Beidou satellite positioning plus Wi‑Fi and LBS \(cell tower\) positioning to improve indoor and urban performance. With an IP67-rated enclosure and integration into a web tracking platform and mobile app, the V55 gives operators a Plaspy-ready device for real-time tracking, historical playback, and configurable alerts without extensive setup complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: straightforward integration for real-time tracking, alerts, and reporting on the Plaspy platform.
- Multi-network cellular connectivity \(4G LTE / 3G WCDMA / 2G GSM\) for wide regional coverage and fallback.
- Hybrid positioning: GPS + Beidou satellite positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS support for better indoor and urban accuracy.
- Rugged, IP67 waterproof housing suitable for exposed vehicle and outdoor asset installations.
- Long-standby design tailored for scenarios that require extended monitoring between charges or low-power reporting.
- Platform features include real-time tracking, configurable alerts, and up to 90 days of location playback through the integrated web/mobile apps.
- Dual GPS+WiFi geo-fence capability enables precise zone monitoring and reliable geofence alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the V55 streams positional and positioning-aid data to the Plaspy server so fleet managers and administrators can view live locations, access telemetry, and receive alerts on desktop or mobile. Plaspy ingests the V55’s satellite GNSS fixes plus Wi‑Fi and LBS-derived fixes, improving tracking quality in difficult signal environments and enabling reliable geofence and historical playback workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for immediate situational awareness and routing.
- Dual GPS+WiFi geofence monitoring for more precise zone detection and fewer false alerts in urban environments.
- Wi‑Fi and LBS \(cell tower\) positioning to supplement GNSS indoors or in canyon-like urban areas.
- Historical playback: the integrated system supports up to 90 days of track playback for auditing and analysis.
- Configurable alerts and reports in Plaspy based on movement, geofence breaches, and platform-defined thresholds.
- Optional vehicle telemetry inputs: when installation includes vehicle I/O, the V55 can be used within telemetry workflows such as ignition or fuel monitoring and immobilizer actions — consult the product manual for supported inputs and wiring.
- Documentation and setup guides simplify commissioning the device on Plaspy, with demo materials available for faster deployment.
