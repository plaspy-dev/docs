# Reachfar - RF-V45 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Reachfar RF-V45 and how its capabilities map to monitoring and tracking workflows when used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, user-facing functionality you can expect to use through Plaspy for real time tracking, alerts, visual verification, and basic activity telemetry.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. The content here is grounded in the RF-V45 product description and highlights how those documented features are commonly used inside Plaspy, while advising you to consult the manufacturer for the most current device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V45 is a compact 4G LTE personal tracker in a smart pendant form factor designed for personal safety and remote monitoring. It combines multi mode positioning with emergency communications and visual confirmation features to support caregivers and coordinators through Plaspy.

- Real time tracking using multi mode positioning including GPS A GPS LBS and Wi Fi assistance for better indoor and outdoor coverage
- One key SOS with preset numbers and instant alerting for fast emergency response
- Two way HD audio with auto answer to enable immediate voice contact with the wearer
- Built in HD camera with auto capture and image upload for visual context after an event
- Geo fence alerts and historical route playback to review movement and incidents in Plaspy
- Lightweight IP67 pendant design with magnetic charging for comfortable daily use

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V45

- Multi mode positioning support GPS with A GPS assisted positioning LBS and Wi Fi localization to improve accuracy in mixed environments
- One key SOS button with preset numbers enabling immediate alerts and escalation
- Two way HD voice calling with auto answer to support rapid intervention and two way communication
- Integrated HD camera with automatic capture and image upload to the companion platform for visual verification
- Real time telemetry and location reporting over cellular networks including 4G 3G and 2G
- Geo fence entry and exit alerts plus historical route playback for incident review
- Activity and basic health oriented functions such as step counting pill reminders and audible alarms
- Durable smart pendant design IP67 water resistance and magnetic charging for ease of daily wear
