# Reachfar - RF-V44 Features

This page provides public feature context for using the Reachfar RF-V44 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant to centralized tracking, monitoring, and operational visibility in Plaspy without replacing official manufacturer documentation.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and manufacturer implementation. Where necessary, confirm details with the device manufacturer before planning deployments or critical workflows.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V44 is a rugged, solar charged 4G tracker designed for long field deployments such as livestock collars and remote outdoor assets. It combines a high capacity internal battery and solar charging with GNSS positioning and cellular uplink so assets remain online for extended periods with reduced maintenance visits.

- Solar charged 9000 mAh internal battery with integrated charging circuit and battery protection for extended autonomous runtime
- Multi network cellular connectivity for broad coverage and reliable data uplink
- GNSS positioning with AGPS plus LBS and Wi‑Fi fallback to maintain location visibility in challenging areas
- Rugged IP66 enclosure and wide operating temperature range for outdoor collar mounting and remote installations
- Animal management oriented features including removal sensing, take off detection, buzzer alerts, audio monitoring, and extended route playback

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V44

- Solar charging and a high capacity internal battery to reduce maintenance visit frequency
- Multi network cellular support including 4G, 3G and 2G for wide geographic coverage
- GNSS positioning with AGPS support and fallback using LBS and Wi‑Fi location sources
- Rugged IP66 rated housing and wide temperature tolerance for outdoor use
- Compact form factor suitable for collar mounting, magnetic or screw installation
- Low power design optimized for long term deployments with periodic upload intervals
- Animal focused alarms and sensors such as infrared removal detection, take off detection, buzzer alerts, and remote audio monitoring
- Historical route storage and playback that supports months of track review

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the RF-V44 is connected to Plaspy, the tracker sends location fixes, battery and solar telemetry, and alarm events into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor assets and animals in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy presents the best available position and device state to dashboards and alerts.

- Real time location and telemetry appear on Plaspy maps and device dashboards for operational visibility
- Take off and removal alerts, low battery and charging status, and other alarm events are delivered into Plaspy for notification and follow up
- Historical route playback and stored tracks are available in Plaspy for grazing analysis and incident review
- LBS and Wi‑Fi fallback positioning are used to maintain presence in Plaspy when GNSS is degraded
- The RF-V44 can be configured to report into Plaspy server endpoints and the platform automatically detects the tracker protocol for integration

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and removal detection for collars and remote equipment
- Remote asset tracking for equipment located in open range or off grid sites
- Animal welfare monitoring including remote audio checks and buzzer activation
- Centralized oversight of mixed assets where Plaspy consolidates telemetry from multiple tracker types
