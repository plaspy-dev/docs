# Reachfar - RF-V28

![RF-V28](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V28 GPS Tracking SOS Pendant is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for continuous location monitoring and fast emergency response. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the RF-V28 brings reliable real-time tracking, multi-method positioning \(GPS, A-GPS, Wi‑Fi and LBS\), and direct emergency calling into a lightweight pendant form factor that caregivers and administrators can manage from the Plaspy platform or via SMS commands.

The RF-V28 is ideal when safety, discreet wearability, and straightforward integration matter. It delivers two-way calling, sequential multi-number SOS calling, fall-detection alarms, and configurable Wi‑Fi safe zones so Plaspy users can receive immediate alerts, view historical routes, and monitor a wearer’s status on Google Maps or within Plaspy dashboards. This combination of personal-safety features and Plaspy compatibility makes the RF-V28 a practical choice for eldercare, children, lone workers, and other personal-tracking needs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and alerting across your monitoring platform.
- Four-positioning methods \(GPS, A‑GPS, Wi‑Fi, LBS\) for accurate indoor and outdoor location reporting.
- Two-way voice calling plus multi-number SOS — the SOS button will sequentially call up to five whitelist contacts until answered.
- Automatic fall-detection alarm that triggers SOS mode for faster emergency response.
- Wi‑Fi fence configuration: define any Wi‑Fi area as a safe zone and trigger immediate alerts when the pendant leaves that area.
- Historical route playback for up to 90 days, enabling backtracking and incident review within Plaspy or the device app.
- Convenience features like mute-answer \(auto-answer\), a talking clock, and adjustable reporting intervals to extend standby to approximately 7–10 days.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When registered as a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V28 feeds location and event data into Plaspy so you can use the platform’s mapping, alerting, and reporting tools. Plaspy displays incoming GPS/Wi‑Fi/LBS positions on a live map, raises immediate notifications for SOS and geofence breaches, and archives route history for later review. Because the RF-V28 supports SMS command and app control, Plaspy can be used alongside those channels to ensure caregivers get consistent, actionable telemetry.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: Plaspy shows current position from GPS/A‑GPS/Wi‑Fi/LBS on maps and dashboards.
- SOS and fall-detection alerts: SOS button events and automatic fall alarms are delivered to Plaspy for instant notification and escalation.
- Geofence and Wi‑Fi fence events: Plaspy receives exit/enter events for configured safe zones and can trigger push/SMS/email alerts.
- Historical route playback: the RF-V28 provides up to 90 days of route data that Plaspy can display and archive for incident review.
- Integration flexibility: RF-V28 data can be combined with other Plaspy-compatible devices \(for example, BLE sensor feeds or vehicle telemetry trackers\) to support broader workflows such as fleet management or anti-theft monitoring.
