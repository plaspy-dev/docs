# Reachfar - RF-V28

![RF-V28](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V28 GPS Tracking SOS Pendant from Reachfar is a compact personal tracker designed for continuous location monitoring and rapid emergency response. Built as a wearable pendant, it provides multi-method positioning and an SOS button for direct emergency calling, together with fall detection and configurable safe zones to support everyday safety needs for individuals who require discreet, reliable monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V28 can be integrated into Plaspy monitoring workflows without complex configuration. Data and events from the pendant feed into Plaspy so caregivers and administrators can receive alerts, view live locations and historical routes, and manage device status from the Plaspy platform alongside other tracked assets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking and alert delivery within your monitoring platform.
- Multi method positioning including GPS, A GPS, Wi Fi and LBS for both outdoor and assisted indoor location reporting.
- Two way voice calling and sequential multi number SOS calling to reach designated contacts quickly.
- Automatic fall detection that triggers SOS mode for faster response in emergencies.
- Configurable Wi Fi safe zones to define trusted areas and receive immediate alerts on exits or entries.
- Historical route playback for incident review and contextual analysis within Plaspy.
- Wearable pendant form factor that prioritizes discreet comfort and straightforward daily use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy, the RF-V28 supplies location points and event notifications so the platform can present mapping, alerts, and historical reports. Plaspy centralizes the pendant's telemetry and alarm events, helping teams maintain situational awareness and respond to incidents efficiently.

- Live location tracking on Plaspy maps using reported GPS A GPS Wi Fi and LBS positions.
- SOS and fall detection alerts delivered to Plaspy for immediate notification and escalation workflows.
- Geofence and Wi Fi fence event handling so Plaspy can notify stakeholders on enter or exit events.
- Historical route playback and archived location history for post event review and reporting.
- Combined visibility with other Plaspy compatible devices to support broader operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Eldercare monitoring where fall detection and one touch SOS calling are critical for caregiver response.
- Child safety and parental monitoring with discreet wearable tracking and safe zone alerts.
- Lone worker protection for personnel who need an accessible emergency button and live location sharing.
- Personal security and anti theft scenarios requiring rapid location reporting and escalation.
- Mixed deployments combining personal trackers with fleet or facility telemetry to provide unified monitoring.
