# Reachfar - RF-V20 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Reachfar RF-V20 GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities relate to Plaspy. It is focused on user-facing functionality such as location reporting, sensor alerts, and battery monitoring as they are commonly used through Plaspy for visibility and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where helpful, this page summarizes manufacturer-provided specifications, but you should verify device-specific details with Reachfar and check firmware release notes for the most current behavior.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V20 is a portable GPS tracker designed for broad global GSM coverage and a set of security and utility features intended to protect and locate assets. It combines location reporting with several theft detection sensors and long battery life to support extended deployments or occasional check-ins.

- Global GSM quad band connectivity for use across standard cellular bands
- Multiple monitoring options including web, iOS, Android, WeChat, and SMS reporting
- Multi-sensor anti-theft detection using magnetic, vibration, and sound sensors
- Built-in audible buzzer alarm and voice monitor for on-site alerting and remote listening
- High-capacity battery with extended standby characteristics and low battery alert
- Additional utility features such as an integrated LED flashlight and power bank functionality

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V20

- GSM quad band support for broad regional compatibility and mobile connectivity
- GPS positioning with manufacturer-stated accuracy and cold/warm/hot fix timing under open sky conditions
- Magnetic sensor, vibration sensor, and sound sensor for layered anti-theft detection
- High-frequency buzzer alarm and voice monitor to assist with local alerts and remote listening
- Built-in battery rated by the manufacturer at 4500mAh and extended standby capability
- Low battery alarm and SIM card change alarm for operational awareness
- Utility features including an LED flashlight and power bank capability for field use
- Compact form factor intended for portable installation and easy repositioning

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the RF-V20's location and alert data become part of a centralized monitoring workflow that supports tracking, event management, and historical review. Plaspy detects compatible trackers and surfaces their reporting without requiring users to manage per-device protocol selection manually.

- Live location and device position appear on Plaspy maps and device lists for situational awareness
- Anti-theft sensor triggers generate events or alerts in Plaspy so operators can review and respond
- Battery level and low battery notifications are visible in device status to plan recharging or replacement
- SIM change and other critical device alerts are captured as events for audit and follow up
- Historical tracks and timestamped location reports are available for route review and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Portable asset protection where compact GPS tracking and theft alerts are needed

- Situations that benefit from both remote alerts and local audible alarms
- Temporary deployments or rentals where device portability and quick setup are important
- Personal belongings or luggage monitoring that uses SMS or app notifications for simple check-ins
- Remote workers or contractors who need a small device with battery backup and flashlight utility
