# Reachfar - RF-V03-SOS

![RF-V03-SOS](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V03-SOS is a rugged, multi-function vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable real-time tracking and fleet telematics. Built to accept a wide input voltage \(8–100V DC\), the RF-V03-SOS fits a broad range of vehicles and delivers dual positioning \(GPS + LBS\) with open-sky accuracy up to 10 meters. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, it integrates with Plaspy for live location, alerts and history playback — helping fleets, rental operations and private owners improve security and operational visibility.

The device emphasizes safety and remote control: one-click SOS, remote engine/power cut \(engine shutdown\), vibration alarm and voice monitoring \(available on GSM and LTE CAT4 variants\). Its waterproof enclosure, built-in backup battery and dead-zone data storage \(over 2,000 points\) make it suitable for uninterrupted telemetry and anti-theft use. Note: the manufacturer lists this model as out of production — consult the manufacturer for availability or recommended alternatives.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking, alerts and fleet management dashboards.
- Wide input voltage \(8–100V DC\) supports cars, trucks and industrial vehicles without extra converters.
- One-click SOS emergency call and vibration alarm for fast incident response and anti-theft protection.
- Remote engine/power cut \(engine shutdown\) and engine status detection for immobilizer-style control and fuel monitoring workflows.
- Dual positioning: GPS plus LBS with typical positioning accuracy up to 10 meters \(open sky\).
- Dead-zone storage for continuity: stores more than 2,000 data points when GPRS is unavailable.
- Voice monitoring supported on GSM and LTE CAT4 variants to aid recovery and situational assessment.
- Compact, waterproof design with internal backup battery for resilient telemetry in harsh conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the RF-V03-SOS becomes a turnkey asset for real-time tracking and fleet telemetry. The tracker reports position, status and event data over cellular networks to Plaspy’s servers. Plaspy displays live maps, powers alerting rules and stores history for playback and compliance reporting. Integration is designed to be straightforward — use the device’s three-way query options \(SMS, mobile App, Web\) along with Plaspy’s dashboard for centralized management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for immediate visibility and route monitoring.
- Ignition/engine status visible in Plaspy to support start/stop reports and idle-time analytics.
- Fuel/power cut \(remote engine shutdown\) controlled via Plaspy workflows for anti-theft and recovery.
- Geo-fence alerts and vibration alarms delivered through Plaspy for tamper and movement detection.
- Dead-zone data stored on the device \(2000+ points\) is uploaded to Plaspy automatically when connectivity returns.

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization — remotely cut engine power on stolen or high-risk vehicles and track movement in real time.
- Driver safety and incident response — one-click SOS and voice monitoring to assess emergencies and dispatch help quickly.
