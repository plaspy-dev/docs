# Reachfar - RF-V49

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V49](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The Reachfar RF-V49 is a compact GPS tracking device designed for broad personal and commercial use. Based on the model description, the RF-V49 provides accurate positioning and real time location updates, plus a set of safety oriented features such as a one key help SOS function, geo fencing, historical route logging, and IP67 level protection for durability in varied conditions. The device also supports convenience and security features like remote picture capture, LED notifications, fall alarm, anti removal alerts, voice messaging, and safety broadcast capabilities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the RF-V49 can be added to Plaspy for centralized visibility and management. Plaspy users can leverage the RF-V49 to monitor location and movement from the same interface used for other assets, receive alerts triggered by SOS or geofence events, and include historical route data in operational reports. Compatibility makes the RF-V49 a practical option for organizations and individuals who want to combine Reachfar hardware with Plaspy tracking and reporting workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Accurate real time location updates suitable for vehicle and personal tracking
- One key SOS help function for rapid notification of designated contacts
- Geo fence alerts and historical route logging for activity monitoring and review
- Multiple safety features including fall alarm and cap off anti removal alerts
- Remote picture capture and voice messaging to support situational awareness
- IP67 rated design for protection against dust and water in routine use

When paired with Plaspy, the RF-V49 provides location and event data into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools, allowing operators to manage tracking alongside other devices in their fleet or account. - Centralized live location display for single units or large fleets
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy for SOS presses, geo fence breaches, and other events
- Historical route playback and exportable records for analysis and compliance
- Inclusion in Plaspy reporting and operational views to support scheduling and oversight
- Grouping and labeling within Plaspy for role based monitoring and asset organization
