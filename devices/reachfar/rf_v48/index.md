# Reachfar - RF-V48

<div className="device-logo">
![RF-V48](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The RF-V48 is a compact 4G GPS tracker in a clip on bracelet form designed for dependable personal safety and continuous location monitoring. As a wearable device it combines multi mode positioning with built in voice and SOS features to provide timely situational information for caregivers, family members, and operators who need responsive visibility of people or light assets.

This model is Plaspy compatible, which makes the RF-V48 suitable for integration into Plaspy dashboards and mobile monitoring workflows. Plaspy can ingest the RF-V48 location and status data for live mapping, alerts, and historical playback, enabling centralized oversight of wearable devices alongside other units managed on the platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for direct integration into dashboards and mobile monitoring for real time tracking and alerts
- Multi mode positioning that leverages GPS with assistive methods to improve location availability across environments
- 4G connectivity with broad fallback to earlier cellular networks for wider coverage in mixed signal areas
- Personal safety functionality including two way voice communication and an SOS alert for rapid response
- Compact clip on wearable design that is lightweight and intended for daily use on people or small movable assets
- Onboard storage and responsive hardware suitable for timely location updates and historical route playback
- Rechargeable battery and durable operating range for ongoing field use and straightforward maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the RF-V48 streams multi source positioning and status information into the platform where it becomes actionable for monitoring teams. Plaspy processes incoming fixes and events so organizations can visualize movement, respond to alerts, and analyze historical patterns in a unified interface.

- Real time location and telemetry updates flow into Plaspy for live mapping and continuous monitoring
- Voice and SOS events are surfaced to the platform so responders and caregivers can act quickly
- Historical route playback and blind spot supplement uploads enable incident review and activity reporting
- Battery and device health status are tracked to trigger alerts for charging or maintenance needs
- Plaspy can correlate wearable data from the RF-V48 with other tracked assets to provide consolidated operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and monitoring for children or elderly family members using SOS and remote voice features
- Pet tracking and light asset monitoring where a compact clip on form factor is preferred
- Visibility for field staff or lone workers to support check ins and incident response on Plaspy dashboards
- Short term deployments and event use where discreet, rechargeable wearable tracking is required

The RF-V48 pairs a small, safety oriented wearable with a monitoring platform that is built for operational visibility. Organizations and caregivers benefit from combining the RF-V48s wearable convenience and safety features with Plaspy’s centralized mapping, alerts, and playback capabilities.
