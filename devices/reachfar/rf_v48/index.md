# Reachfar - RF-V48

![RF-V48](./tracker.jpg)

The RF-V48 is a compact 4G GPS tracker bracelet engineered for dependable personal safety and real-time monitoring. Designed as a clip-on wearable, the RF-V48 pairs accurate multi-mode positioning \(GPS/A-GPS/LBS/WiFi\) and voice/SOS features with low-power operation — and it is Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into mobile app and web-based monitoring platforms.

With a modern 9820E chip platform and dual-core ARM Cortex-A7 processor, the RF-V48 delivers responsive location updates, historical route playback and blind-spot supplement upload for continuous tracking. Whether you need a GPS tracker for children, elders, pets or discreet asset monitoring, the RF-V48 brings wearable convenience and platform-ready data to Plaspy-powered fleet management and safety workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: straightforward integration into Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps for real-time tracking, alerts and reporting.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS L1 \(22 channels\) with A‑GPS, LBS fallback and WiFi assistance for reliable location across environments.
- 4G connectivity with broad fallback: LTE Cat4 for higher-speed data plus DC‑HSPA+ \(3G\) and EDGE \(2G\) support for broad coverage.
- Personal safety features: two-way phone calls, SOS button and remote voice monitoring for immediate assistance and check-ins.
- Compact wearable form factor: clip-type bracelet design, lightweight \(≈35 g\), and durable operating range for everyday use.
- Built-in storage and responsive hardware: NAND + LPDDR2 memory \(2Gb RAM + 4Gb ROM\) and a fast processor for smooth platform interaction.
- Operational resilience: internal rechargeable battery with charging circuit and protection, wide temperature and humidity tolerance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF-V48 sends multi-source location and status data to the Plaspy platform where it becomes actionable in real time. Plaspy-compatible integration accepts GPS and assistive positioning inputs, routes them into live maps, and triggers alerts, reports and historical playback. This gives organizations and caregivers a clear, centralized view of wearable assets alongside other trackers and telematics devices.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GPS/A-GPS positions plus LBS and WiFi-assisted fixes flow into Plaspy for live mapping and tracking.
- Voice & SOS interactions: two-way calls, SOS alerts and remote voice monitoring are delivered through the platform for immediate response.
- Historical route playback & blind-spot supplement: stores and uploads route history for later review and compliance reporting in Plaspy.
- Low-power and status reporting: battery and power-state data help Plaspy manage alerts for charging and device health.
- Complementary device features: while RF-V48 focuses on wearable tracking and voice/SOS, Plaspy can correlate this device’s data with other telematics units that provide fuel monitoring, ignition/immobilizer control or Bluetooth sensors when these are present in a mixed-device deployment.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the RF-V48 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker means pairing a lightweight, wearable hardware platform with a powerful tracking and management ecosystem. The RF-V48 supplies multi-mode positioning and safety-focused features \(SOS, two-way calls, remote voice monitoring\) while Plaspy ingests those signals into configurable alerts, historical playback, and centralized reporting. This combination supports reliable real-time tracking, improves situational awareness and simplifies monitoring across individuals and small mobile teams.

For organizations that require mixed fleet management or broader telemetry, the RF-V48 integrates into Plaspy together with vehicle telematics and accessory sensors. Plaspy can correlate wearable location and status with other devices that provide fuel monitoring, ignition/immobilizer control or Bluetooth sensor telemetry — enabling a single-pane view of assets and people without over-claiming features on the wearable itself.
