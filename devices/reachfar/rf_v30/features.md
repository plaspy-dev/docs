# Reachfar - RF-V30 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the Reachfar RF-V30 and how its capabilities map to the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the RF-V30's practical functions for pet tracking, including its positioning options, alerting, endurance features, and the ways owners can use Plaspy to monitor location and routes.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, local configuration, mounting and installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as general guidance for Plaspy compatibility and consult the manufacturer for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V30 is a compact pet tracker designed for everyday location monitoring and recovery workflows. Its combination of GPS and WiFi-assisted positioning plus cellular data transmission makes it suitable for live tracking on a hosted platform like Plaspy while balancing size and battery life for collar mounting.

- Accurate outdoor positioning via GPS with additional WiFi-assisted fixes to improve urban and near-indoor coverage.
- Cellular connectivity for live location updates and alerts that Plaspy can display on maps and timelines.
- Geo-fence alerting and stored route history for activity review and recovery investigations.
- Small, lightweight IP66 housing built for collar mounting and routine outdoor use.
- Rechargeable battery with configurable standby modes to trade frequent reporting for longer runtime.
- Automatic LED smart rolling light for easier nighttime locating in low light.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V30

- Plaspy compatible for direct integration with a hosted tracking platform for map based monitoring and alerts.
- Quad band GSM and GPRS data transport using TCP IP for sending live position and telemetry.
- GPS positioning with open sky accuracy guidance for reliable outdoor fixes.
- WiFi assisted positioning to supplement GPS where satellite signal is limited.
- Geo fence support for boundary based notifications.
- Historical route recording with up to 90 days of playback for activity and recovery review.
- IP66 water and dust resistance and compact collar friendly form factor.
- Built in rechargeable battery with multiple standby modes to manage reporting cadence and battery life.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the RF-V30 is used with Plaspy, its location and telemetry are reported to the platform so owners can view current position, past tracks, and alerts from a single interface. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and presents position and event data consistently for monitoring and review.

- Live positions and updates appear on Plaspy maps for situational awareness.
- Geo fence events are shown as alerts so owners can be notified when a tracked pet leaves or returns to a defined area.
- Route history up to recorded retention limits is available for playback and activity analysis in Plaspy.
- Battery and standby telemetry reported by the device are surfaced as status indicators and can trigger low battery notifications.
- WiFi assisted location fixes are included alongside GPS fixes to improve display continuity in urban or mixed environments.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time pet location and recovery when an animal wanders or becomes lost.
- Reviewing daily or weekly routes to understand exercise patterns and movement.
- Setting safe roaming boundaries and receiving notifications when those boundaries are crossed.
- Nighttime locating where the integrated LED improves visual retrieval.
- Small asset tracking for items where compact size and weather resistance are important.
- Monitoring intermittent movement with configurable reporting intervals to preserve battery life.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware and configuration can change how features behave and which telemetry fields are reported to Plaspy.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants can affect radio behavior and supported bands; confirm the specific unit details for your region.
- Battery runtime estimates depend heavily on reporting frequency, GPS use, and standby mode settings chosen during installation.
- WiFi assisted positioning relies on available WiFi reference data and may not provide precise fixes in all environments.
- Some device behaviors such as LED operation or automatic modes may be implemented differently across firmware versions.
