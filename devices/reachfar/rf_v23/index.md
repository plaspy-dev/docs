# RF-V23 / RF-V22 4G Multifunctional GPS Tracker

The RF-V23 \(and sibling RF-V22\) is a rugged, long-life GPS tracker engineered for outdoor asset protection and continuous monitoring. Designed with solar-charge options, IP66 waterproofing and a high-capacity 9000mAh battery, this Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivers reliable real-time tracking and extended field operation without frequent maintenance.

Built on an ASR3603S RTOS platform with a single-core ARM Cortex‑R5 CPU, the RF-V23 supports multi-network connectivity \(GSM, WCDMA and FDD‑LTE Cat‑1\), GNSS positioning, WiFi positioning and advanced power management. When integrated with Plaspy, the unit becomes a turnkey solution for fleet management, anti-theft protection and telemetry reporting for remote vehicles and outdoor equipment.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and fleet management via web and mobile apps.
- Long endurance: built-in 9000mAh battery and optional solar charge model for extended off-grid use.
- Robust outdoor design: IP66 waterproof rating and compact rugged form factor \(97×69×31 mm, 255 g\).
- Accurate positioning: 72‑channel GNSS receiver with AGPS \(typical 5–15 m accuracy, 28 s cold boot\).
- Multi-mode location: GPS, LBS \(100–1000 m\) and WiFi positioning \(5–50 m\) for reliable fixes in challenging environments.
- Practical safety features: SOS two-way voice, remote voice monitoring, audio buzzer alerts and infrared tamper sensing.
- Low-power operation: ultra-low standby consumption and configurable upload intervals to maximize battery life.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the RF-V23 streams location and device telemetry to the Plaspy platform in near real-time. Plaspy ingests GNSS, WiFi and LBS position data together with device status, enabling map views, geofences, alerts and historical playback. Integration is straightforward for fleets and asset managers who need continuous visibility and actionable notifications.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy for live monitoring and dashboards.
- Historical route playback \(up to 90 days\) for trip auditing and incident review.
- Geo-fencing support \(GPS and WiFi fence\) with instant alerts for entry/exit events.
- Remote alerts and tamper detection via infrared distance sensing, audio buzzer and SOS two-way voice.
- Blind spot supplemental uploads and position drift processing for improved accuracy in intermittent coverage areas.
- Device configuration and commands via app, platform and SMS \(main monitor numbers, take‑off alerts, timed power on/off, remote ringing\).

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for light vehicles, trailers and service fleets that need long battery life between charges.
- Anti-theft protection for outdoor assets and equipment with SOS, remote ringing and tamper alerts routed to Plaspy.
- Remote equipment monitoring at job sites where solar charging and IP66 protection reduce maintenance visits.
- Historical trip playback and compliance reporting for operations that require up to 90 days of route data.
- Hybrid location tracking where GNSS is supplemented by WiFi and LBS to maintain coverage in urban canyons or indoors.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The RF-V23 is a purpose-built Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for customers who need rugged, long-endurance tracking with flexible positioning modes. Its large battery, optional solar charging and low-power design lower operational costs by reducing recharge cycles and site visits. Paired with Plaspy, you get reliable real-time tracking, geo-fencing, alerting and historical analytics—ideal for fleet management, anti-theft programs and telemetry-driven operations.

For deployments requiring expanded vehicle telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition status or immobilizer control, Plaspy can combine RF-V23 location and device health data with additional vehicle data sources or external interfaces to create a complete fleet management solution. The RF-V23’s support for voice monitoring, tamper alerts and blind‑spot uploads makes it especially useful where security and continuous situational awareness are critical.
