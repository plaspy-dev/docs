# Reachfar - RF-V46 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Reachfar RF-V46 and explains how its capabilities are typically used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about the device as a Plaspy compatible wearable for real time location, voice contact, and basic biometric monitoring.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation choices, and the manufacturer's implementation. The content below uses the provided device description as the basis for public feature context and recommends consulting Reachfar for device specific technical details and current specifications.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V46 is a wearable 4G GPS positioning video telephone watch intended for caregiver and personal safety scenarios. When paired with Plaspy, it streams location and basic biometric telemetry so caregivers and organizations can maintain situational awareness and communicate directly with the wearer.

- Real time GPS location reporting for live tracking on Plaspy dashboards.
- 4G cellular connectivity to support voice calls and faster position updates than legacy networks.
- Two way voice telephone functions with optional video call capability where supported by the network and device configuration.
- Onboard heart rate and blood pressure sensors to provide basic biometric context alongside location.
- Waterproof wristwatch form factor for everyday wear and continuous monitoring.
- User resources such as product video and downloadable instructions to simplify deployment and training.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V46

- 4G cellular support for voice and data transmission.
- GPS positioning for real time location awareness.
- Two way voice telephone capabilities for direct communication between caregiver and wearer.
- Optional video calling where supported by network conditions and device configuration.
- Onboard heart rate sensing for simple biometric monitoring.
- Onboard blood pressure sensing for basic health telemetry.
- Waterproof wristwatch design suitable for daily wear.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy acts as the platform layer that ingests location pings and available telemetry from compatible trackers like the RF-V46. Data reported by the device is presented alongside maps and event feeds to help caregivers and operators make timely decisions.

- Real time location appears on Plaspy maps for monitoring and response.
- Biometric readings such as heart rate and blood pressure are shown alongside position data to provide contextual awareness.
- Two way voice events and video availability are reflected in activity logs and can be used to initiate direct communication when supported.
- Event logging and history in Plaspy let teams review past positions and telemetry for audits or follow up.
- Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols and surfaces incoming telemetry for unified monitoring without requiring users to interpret raw device output.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elder care monitoring for families and assisted living staff who need ongoing location and basic health telemetry.
- Child safety and lone child monitoring where parents want live GPS tracking and direct voice contact.
- Personal safety for independent users who want an always worn device that supports immediate communication.
- Care coordination in small care teams where staff monitor residents status and location from a single dashboard.
- Aftercare and remote health follow up that combines simple biometric readings with location information.
