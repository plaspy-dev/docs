# Reachfar - RF-V8 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Reachfar RF-V8 when used with Plaspy. It summarizes the RF-V8 capabilities relevant to centralized monitoring, alerting, and historical trace replay inside Plaspy without reproducing manufacturer configuration specifics or private implementation details.

Exact feature availability and on-device behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, or regional model. Review the manufacturer documentation and device settings for the precise list of features and their current behavior before deploying at scale.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V8 is a super-mini GPS tracker designed for discreet anti-theft protection and real-time location reporting. It pairs compact hardware and GNSS positioning with multiple anti-theft sensors to provide actionable alerts and location updates that can be routed into Plaspy for unified monitoring and incident response.

- Compact form factor and lightweight packaging for covert placement on vehicles or personal items.
- GNSS positioning for timely location updates useful in live map tracking and historical route playback.
- Built-in anti-theft sensors including adjustable vibration sensitivity and a noise triggered alarm to raise immediate alerts.
- SIM-change and low-battery alerts plus remote listening capabilities for layered anti-theft visibility.
- Multiple control and monitoring options on the device side such as web, mobile app, WeChat and SMS controls.
- GPRS based connectivity that enables routing position and event data into Plaspy for centralized oversight.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V8

- Plaspy compatible GPRS connectivity for real time tracking and trace reporting.
- Super-mini enclosure suitable for covert mounting and discreet asset protection.
- GNSS positioning hardware providing rapid fixes and practical open sky accuracy for monitoring.
- Adjustable vibration sensor to detect tampering or movement and trigger alerts.
- Noise sensor alarm for audio based anti-theft triggering.
- SIM-change alert to notify administrators of potential tampering with the device SIM.
- Low-battery notifications to help maintain device uptime and preempt service loss.
- Remote listening capability for situations where covert audio monitoring is supported by the device and local regulations.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location updates and sensor events from compatible trackers, consolidating them into dashboards, alerting channels, and historical traces. For the RF-V8, that means position and anti-theft events reported by the device appear alongside other fleet and asset telemetry in Plaspy for simplified operational management.

- Live map visualization of RF-V8 location reports for real time tracking and dispatch.
- Event alerts in Plaspy for vibration triggers, noise alarms, SIM-change notices, and low-battery warnings.
- Historical route playback showing timestamps and positions received from the device for forensic review and reporting.
- Centralized management of multiple RF-V8 units and other trackers in a single Plaspy account for consistent operational oversight.
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and receives device reports sent to Plaspy endpoints for simple integration.
