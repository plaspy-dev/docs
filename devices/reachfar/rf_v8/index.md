# RF-V8 Super-Mini GPS Tracker — Plaspy Compatible

The RF-V8 is a super-mini GPS tracker engineered for discreet anti-theft protection and reliable real-time tracking. Lightweight and compact, the RF-V8 is ideal where covert installation and prompt alerts matter most. Its quad-band GSM and GPRS Class 12 connectivity make the RF-V8 Plaspy compatible, allowing you to route location and telemetry to Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts, and historical trace replay.

The device combines fast GNSS positioning with multiple anti-theft sensors — vibration sensitivity, ambient-noise alarm, SIM-change and low-battery alerts — and supports web, mobile app, WeChat and SMS control. Whether you need personal property tracking, asset protection in a warehouse, or a discreet vehicle anti-theft solution that integrates with fleet management workflows via Plaspy, the RF-V8 brings compact form factor and actionable telemetry to your security stack.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via quad-band GPRS \(TCP/IP\) for seamless real-time tracking and trace replay.
- Super-mini form factor \(40 × 34 × 14 mm, 24 g\) for covert placement on vehicles or personal assets.
- SiRF Star IV GNSS chipset delivering fast fixes and 5–15 m open-sky positioning accuracy for dependable location data.
- Multi-mode anti-theft sensors: adjustable vibration sensitivity and noise-triggered alarms for immediate alerts.
- Remote listening and SIM-change alerts provide layered anti-theft capabilities beyond position telemetry.
- Long standby capability — up to ~12 days depending on settings — plus low-battery SMS alert and emergency call window.
- Flexible monitoring options: website tracking, mobile app, WeChat and SMS control to suit operational preferences.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF-V8 transmits location and sensor events over GPRS \(TCP/IP\), which Plaspy ingests to provide real-time tracking, geofencing, alerts, and historical route playback. Plaspy lets you centralize multiple RF-V8 units alongside other trackers, consolidating telemetry and alerting into a single fleet management or asset-monitoring dashboard.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via GPRS/TCP-IP to Plaspy for live map visualization.
- Instant anti-theft alerts: vibration-triggered notifications and noise alarms are forwarded to Plaspy as events.
- Low-battery and SIM-change alerts are conveyed immediately so administrators can act before loss of service.
- Remote listening and trace replay: Plaspy receives event timestamps and position history for forensic review and response.
- Integrates with Plaspy’s notification channels \(app push, SMS, email\) so teams receive critical anti-theft alerts as they happen.
