# Reachfar - RF-V24 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the Reachfar RF-V24 and how its documented capabilities map to Plaspy for tracking, monitoring, and alerting. It focuses on practical feature context and explains what operators can expect when using the RF-V24 with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and manufacturer settings. For device specific configuration steps, supported firmware features, and the latest details consult the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V24 is a solar powered 4G GPS tracker designed for long term outdoor deployment where external power is limited. It combines persistent location reporting with onboard sensors and emergency voice features to support asset visibility and event driven alerts in Plaspy.

- Solar charged internal battery that reduces maintenance and extends standby life for long deployments.
- Real time 4G location reporting for continuous situational awareness of vehicles, trailers, and remote assets.
- Built in microphone, audible buzzer, and dedicated SOS button to support voice alerting and immediate emergency notification.
- Vibration and sound sensors for tamper and abnormal event detection that can trigger alarms.
- Geofencing and historical route playback to support incident review and operational reporting.

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V24

- Solar powered internal battery for extended standby and reduced maintenance cycles.
- 4G cellular reporting for real time position updates and location history.
- Built in microphone to enable voice monitoring and emergency voice interactions.
- Audible buzzer to provide local audible alerts.
- Dedicated SOS button for immediate emergency notification.
- Vibration detection for tamper and movement based alarms.
- Sound detection to capture abnormal audio events as triggers.
- Support for geofencing and historical route playback for review and reporting.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the RF-V24 is connected to Plaspy, location and event information reported by the device become visible to operators for monitoring and response. Plaspy automatically detects many compatible tracker protocols to simplify initial connectivity and device onboarding.

- Live position updates and location history are displayed on Plaspy maps for tracking and playback.
- SOS button presses and voice related events are surfaced as alerts so operators can respond through Plaspy dashboards.
- Vibration and sound triggered alarms appear in Plaspy as event notifications for anti theft and tamper awareness.
- Geofence entry and exit events are reported to Plaspy and can be used to drive alerts and activity reports.
- Device events and location data can be combined with other platform telemetry when those inputs are available.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term trailer and container tracking where solar charging reduces the need for battery changes.
- Remote asset monitoring for equipment left in the field with limited or no continuous power.
- Anti theft and tamper detection using vibration and sound sensors to trigger alarms and notifications.
- Emergency response scenarios where the SOS button and voice features provide an immediate alert channel.
- Route verification and compliance checks using historical playback and geofence reporting.
- Temporary or seasonal deployments such as rentals where low maintenance operation is desirable.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior and availability depend on the device firmware version and hardware revision installed by the manufacturer.
- Some functions require specific configuration during installation or settings enabled by the operator to forward events to Plaspy.
- Regional cellular bands and carrier configurations can affect 4G reporting performance and should be confirmed for your deployment area.
- Manufacturer supplied documentation and the downloadable manual provide the authoritative setup and configuration instructions.
- Integration with additional vehicle telemetry is possible when those inputs are present and enabled, but those inputs are not necessarily available on every RF-V24 unit.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the RF-V24 with Plaspy gives organizations a straightforward way to centralize location visibility, event alerts, and historical route data for dispersed assets. The combination of solar charging and 4G reporting helps maintain persistent coverage for trailers, containers, and equipment deployed in locations without continuous external power. Onboard voice and alarm features provide a practical emergency and tamper detection channel that Plaspy can surface to operators for timely response.
