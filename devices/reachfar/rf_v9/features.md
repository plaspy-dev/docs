# Reachfar - RF-V9 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Reachfar RF-V9 GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities relate to usage with Plaspy. Content here focuses on practical, user-facing functions and operational value when the RF-V9 is used as Plaspy compatible hardware for location reporting, alerts, and situational monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, regional cellular support, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific implementation details, configuration tips, and the latest firmware behavior consult the official ReachFar documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The RF-V9 is a compact GPS tracker designed to deliver continuous position updates and basic telemetry to compatible platforms such as Plaspy. It emphasizes reliable location reporting, tamper detection, and a small form factor for discreet installations, with features aimed at theft mitigation, fleet oversight, and personal asset protection.

- Real time GPS location reporting for live tracking and map visualization
- Vibration and sound sensor alarms to detect tampering or unexpected movement
- Two way voice and remote listening for on demand audio verification
- Built in rechargeable battery with remote low battery alerts to maintain uptime
- Support for trace replay and geofence style alerts on compatible platforms for route review and perimeter monitoring

## Core Features of Reachfar - RF-V9

- Real time GPS tracking for position reporting and historical route playback
- Quad band GSM cellular connectivity to report location and alerts over mobile networks
- Vibration and sound sensor alarms that generate SMS, call, and platform notifications
- Two way voice and remote listening for situational verification by authorized users
- Built in rechargeable battery with remote low battery reporting and standby behavior
- Trace replay and geofence style event support for post event analysis and perimeter monitoring
- Remote arming and disarming controls supported by device firmware and platform workflows
- Compact, discreet form factor for straightforward covert or inconspicuous installations

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the RF-V9 is used with Plaspy, its location messages and alarm events are ingested by the platform and presented alongside other fleet and asset data. Plaspy surfaces those inputs on live maps, in notifications, and within historical reports to help operators maintain situational awareness.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps allowing dispatchers to track units in real time
- Sensor alarm events such as vibration or sound are logged and can trigger notifications or alerts
- Historical trace replay is available for route audits and incident review when the platform supports it
- Battery status and low battery events are visible to help schedule recharge or recovery actions
- Two way voice and remote listening events are recorded as call or event entries depending on platform capabilities

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle anti theft monitoring and recovery with tamper alarms and location visibility
- Basic fleet tracking for route oversight and centralized location reporting
- Discreet protection of personal or portable high value assets where compact installation is needed
- Intermittent monitoring and remote verification using two way voice for situational checks
- Route auditing and historical playback for incident reconstruction or delivery verification
