# RF-V9 GPS Tracker

The RF-V9 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for reliable real-time tracking of vehicles, personal items, and discreet assets. Its quad-band GSM radio and built-in rechargeable battery deliver continuous location reporting and telemetry to internet platforms, mobile apps \(iPhone/Android\), WeChat and SMS. For businesses and individuals who require straightforward fleet management and anti-theft monitoring, the RF-V9 offers essential alarms, trace replay and two-way voice for on-demand audio monitoring.

The RF-V9 integrates easily with tracking platforms such as ReachFar’s inquiry service \(for example, http://www.gps123.org\) and standard tracking apps, making it a practical choice when you need Plaspy compatible hardware that reports location, motion and battery status in real time. Its small form factor and sensor alarms support covert installation for vehicle theft prevention, fleet oversight and personal protection where constant situational awareness matters.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking — continuous position updates to platforms and mobile apps for reliable fleet management and asset visibility.
- Quad-band GSM \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) ensures wide cellular coverage for global deployments where GSM networks are available.
- Compact and discreet form factor \(45 × 35 × 13.5 mm, 28 g\) for easy installation in cars, trucks, containers or personal items.
- Vibration and sound sensor alarms trigger SMS, calls and platform alerts to detect tampering, movement or forced entry.
- Two-way communication and remote listening enable quiet monitoring and voice checks from an authorized user.
- Built-in rechargeable battery with up to 12 days standby and remote low-battery alerts for proactive power management.
- Platform features such as geo-fence alerts, trace replay and remote arming/disarming support operational workflows and theft recovery.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy-compatible platforms, the RF-V9 streams real-time GPS coordinates and telemetry so dispatchers and managers can monitor vehicles and assets on a single dashboard. Plaspy reads the device’s location and alert messages and incorporates them into live maps, reports and notifications. That makes the RF-V9 a practical building block for anti-theft workflows and basic fleet management setups.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: position fixes are sent via GSM to Plaspy-compatible servers and apps for live tracking.
- Motion and tamper alerts: vibration and sound sensor alarms generate immediate SMS/call alerts and platform notifications.
- Trace replay and geo-fence: historical route playback and geofence alerts are available on supported platforms for route audits and theft detection.
- Two-way voice and remote listening: authorized users can call the unit for live audio monitoring, useful for situational verification.
- Battery and status telemetry: remote low-battery alerts let operators schedule charging or recovery before device downtime occurs.
