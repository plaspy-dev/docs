# RF‑V22 \(V22\) Telecom Smart Door Alarm

The RF‑V22 Telecom Smart Door Alarm is a Plaspy compatible cellular door-status detector designed for supervised entry control in quarantine facilities, healthcare settings, assisted living, and other controlled environments. Built to report opening and closing events over standard GSM/GPRS networks, the V22 provides reliable, IP67‑rated protection for indoor and semi‑outdoor mounting and integrates with monitoring platforms through the device’s GSM communications.

The V22 is ideal where low‑cost, cellular-based door supervision is required. It connects to telecom networks using quad‑band GSM/GPRS and can deliver near‑real‑time telemetry of door state to Plaspy for alerting, logging, and operational workflows. The unit ships in white or black and is supported by a product manual and installation video to simplify installation and configuration.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates door state telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and alerting rules for centralized monitoring.
- GSM/GPRS quad‑band connectivity for broad cellular coverage without relying on local Wi‑Fi.
- IP67 rated enclosure for dependable operation in demanding indoor and semi‑outdoor installations.
- Designed for epidemic isolation, patient room monitoring, and controlled‑entry scenarios where door open/close events are critical.
- Near‑real‑time door event reporting to enable fast response and automated workflows within Plaspy.
- Installer resources and video guidance for quick setup and configuration, reducing deployment time.
- Discrete form factor available in white or black to match facility aesthetics.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The RF‑V22 sends door open and close events over GSM/GPRS to a configured monitoring endpoint. When integrated with Plaspy, those events appear as telemetry inputs that can trigger notifications, log entries, and conditional automation. Plaspy acts as the central management layer, correlating V22 door state data with other field devices such as GPS trackers or telemetry sensors to form a unified operational picture.

- Real‑time telemetry of door open/close events delivered via GSM/GPRS to Plaspy.
- Alarm and status notifications forwarded to Plaspy for immediate alerting and incident tracking.
- Logs and event history available in Plaspy for compliance and audit trails.
- Seamless integration option: pair the V22 with Plaspy‑managed GPS trackers for combined location and access monitoring — useful for fleet management and asset protection workflows.
- Can be used alongside devices that provide fuel monitoring, ignition status, immobilizer control, or Bluetooth sensors when consolidated in Plaspy — the V22 contributes door and perimeter telemetry to these broader solutions.
