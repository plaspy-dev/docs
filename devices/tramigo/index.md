# Tramigo

Tramigo is a manufacturer of GPS tracking hardware commonly used for vehicle and asset tracking. On this page you will find information about how Tramigo devices work with Plaspy, what to expect in terms of features, and how to choose the right device for your tracking needs. Use the catalog below to browse the Tramigo models that are compatible with the Plaspy platform.

## How Tramigo devices work with Plaspy

Tramigo trackers send location and status data over mobile networks which Plaspy ingests, normalizes, and displays in the platform. Plaspy receives GPS positions, basic sensor telemetry, and standard device events, then presents them through live maps, history playback, alerts, and reports. Device-specific configuration and provisioning steps are handled on the device pages and within Plaspy device settings so that your tracker communicates correctly with the platform.

Because the BrandCatalog lists the compatible models, you can select a device and follow the model page for setup guidance, device parameters, and recommended configuration for best results on Plaspy.

## Common features found in Tramigo devices

While specific capabilities vary by model, Tramigo hardware typically includes a combination of these common features:
- Real time GPS location reporting and position history
- Movement and ignition detection for vehicle monitoring
- Basic digital and analog inputs for sensors and external alarms
- Configurable reporting intervals and movement based reporting
- Battery backup options for asset trackers
- Built in or external antennas to suit different installation scenarios

Plaspy interprets these signals and exposes them as configurable inputs for tracking, alerts, geofencing, and reporting within the platform.

## Typical use cases for Tramigo GPS trackers

Tramigo devices are used in a variety of tracking scenarios. Common use cases include:
- Fleet vehicle tracking and driver route oversight
- Long term asset tracking for trailers and equipment
- Theft recovery and tamper alerts for high value items
- Remote monitoring where cellular coverage is available

Plaspy’s mapping, alerting, and reporting tools let organizations convert raw device data into operational insights for these use cases.

Choosing the right Tramigo unit depends on the installation environment and the features you need. Consider the following when browsing models:
