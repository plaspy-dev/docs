# Tramigo - T23

![T23](./logo.jpg)

The Tramigo T23 vehicle tracking device is a highly advanced and popular GPS tracker designed for personal use and fleet management. It is the best-selling GPS vehicle security product in the market. With the Tramigo T23, you can always know the exact location of your vehicle, ensuring peace of mind and enhanced security. Installing the T23 is a breeze, as it can be easily hidden and powered from the vehicle's battery.

The Tramigo T23 offers a wide range of features that make it a top choice for track and trace, vehicle recovery, and fleet management applications. It operates on a full quadband GSM band, ensuring reliable and global connectivity. The T23 also supports voice communication, allowing you to communicate with the tracker remotely. It comes with a backup battery and internal memory, ensuring uninterrupted tracking even in case of power loss. The T23 communicates via GSM and GPRS, providing real-time tracking and monitoring.

With the Tramigo T23, you have multiple options for positioning, including time, distance, and angle change. It also features sleep mode, which helps conserve battery life when the vehicle is not in use. The T23 offers more than four digital inputs, allowing you to connect various external devices. It has pre-defined inputs for ignition and panic, enabling you to receive instant alerts in case of unauthorized vehicle use or emergencies. The T23 is equipped with GPS antennas for accurate positioning and comes in a durable plastic casing. It also offers extra connectivity options such as fixed antennas, microphone/speaker, temperature sensors, and a USB port. The Tramigo T23 is proudly made in Europe, ensuring high-quality and reliable performance.
