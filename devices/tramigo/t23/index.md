# Tramigo - T23

<div className="device-logo">
![T23](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Tramigo T23 is a vehicle tracking device designed for both personal use and fleet management. Positioned as a best selling GPS vehicle security product, the T23 provides continuous location visibility and features aimed at track and trace, vehicle recovery, and day to day fleet oversight. Its design supports hidden installation and vehicle power connection, while integrated backup power and internal memory help maintain tracking continuity if the main power is lost.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Tramigo T23 can feed location and event data into the Plaspy platform to support real time monitoring, alerting and reporting workflows. Plaspy users can leverage the T23's common vehicle tracking features—such as timed or distance based position reporting, panic and ignition inputs, and voice capability—within the Plaspy environment to improve operational visibility and respond faster to incidents.

## Key Highlights

- Proven track record as a popular vehicle security and tracking device suitable for personal and commercial fleets
- Real time location reporting using GSM and GPRS connectivity for continuous position updates
- Backup battery and internal memory to preserve tracking data if main power is interrupted
- Multiple positioning options including time interval, distance moved, and angle change for adaptable reporting
- Built in voice capability and more than four digital inputs with predefined ignition and panic inputs for events and alerts
- Expandable connectivity options such as external antennas, microphone and speaker, and additional sensor connections

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Tramigo T23 transmits location and event information to the platform where it becomes part of centralized fleet visibility and management. Plaspy translates incoming device data into mapped positions, timeline histories, and configurable alerts so teams can monitor vehicles and respond to exceptions.

- Display live and historical locations on Plaspy maps to follow routes and verify stops
- Configure alerts for panic, ignition events, or movement patterns and receive notifications through Plaspy
- Use trip and activity reports in Plaspy to analyze utilization, idle time, and route performance
- Combine T23 inputs with Plaspy monitoring to detect unauthorized use and support vehicle recovery workflows
- Aggregate data from multiple T23 units for fleet level oversight and operational planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle security and rapid recovery for personal cars and high value assets
- Small to medium fleet tracking for delivery, service, and logistics operations
- Rental and leasing oversight to monitor vehicle location and usage events
- Remote monitoring for field staff and company vehicles to improve dispatch and safety
- Incident alerting and response coordination using panic and ignition inputs

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Tramigo T23 is a practical option for organizations that need a mature vehicle tracking device paired with a flexible management platform. Its combination of continuous location reporting, event inputs, and backup power provides a reliable data source for Plaspy to deliver situational awareness and reporting. Voice capability and expandable connectivity make the T23 adaptable to a range of operational needs without requiring specialized modifications.

Because the T23 is positioned for both personal and fleet use, teams evaluating hardware for Plaspy can expect a balance of field-proven features and straightforward device behavior. That said, specific fit depends on your operational requirements, so consider the T23 in the context of your expected reporting cadence, alerting needs, and vehicle environment.
