The Fortuna Impex Disha 9320 is an intelligent vehicle tracking system designed for companies in services, delivery, and transportation industries. It provides both real time and historical tracking via a web interface, and it collects and analyzes asset information to help optimize resource utilization. The unit includes enclosed GPS and GPRS antennas and offers real time alerts, events, and scheduled reports delivered by SMS and email, making it suitable for continuous operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Disha 9320 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized fleet monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can present the device's real time positions and historical movements alongside other assets, and incorporate alerts and scheduled reports into a consolidated operational view. This makes the Disha 9320 a practical choice for organizations looking to combine dependable tracking hardware with Plaspy's fleet management features.

- Convert device events and alerts into Plaspy notifications and workflow triggers
- Generate consolidated reports in Plaspy including location history and activity summaries
