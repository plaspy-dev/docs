# Fortuna Impex - Disha 9320

![Disha 9320](./logo.jpg)

The Fortuna Impex Disha 9320 is an intelligent vehicle tracking system designed to help companies in the services, delivery, and transportation industries monitor and manage their mobile assets effectively. With the ability to collect and analyze asset information, this tracking system allows for optimum utilization of resources.

One of the standout features of the Disha 9320 is its real-time and historical tracking capabilities, accessible through a web interface. This provides users with accurate and up-to-date information about their vehicles' locations. Additionally, the system offers real-time alerts, events, and scheduled reports via SMS and email, ensuring that users are always informed about their assets' status.

Installation of the Disha 9320 is simple and hassle-free. The unit can be easily mounted on the front dashboard of the vehicle, with GPS visibility to the sky being a requirement. The system is powered by connecting two wires to the vehicle's battery. Notably, the GPS and GPRS antennas are enclosed within the unit, eliminating the need for separate antenna installation and reducing the risk of mechanical damage.

Key Features:

- Real-time and historical tracking
- Real-time alerts, events, and scheduled reports via SMS and email
- Simple installation
- Enclosed GPS and GPRS antennas
