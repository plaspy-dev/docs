# SkyMobile - SM-8570

![SM-8570](./logo.jpg)

The SM-8570 from SkyMobile is a highly advanced and versatile GPS locator designed for a wide range of tracking applications. Whether you need to track motorcycles, containers, merchandise, people, or any other valuable asset, this portable GPS device is up to the task. One of the standout features of the SM-8570 is its exceptional GPS reception sensitivity, which is far superior to other devices in its category. This ensures a fast Time to First Fix \(TTFF\) and reliable tracking performance.

The SM-8570 operates on a quad-band GSM module \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\), allowing for seamless communication and tracking capabilities. It can transmit location data to a real-time server or any other device via SMS. The device is based on the @Track communications protocol, making it compatible with various tracking platforms. System integrators can easily configure their tracking systems to communicate with the SM-8570 using this protocol.

Some of the outstanding features of the SM-8570 include its water-resistant design, integrated UBLOX GPS chipset with exceptional tracking sensitivity, integrated GSM/GPS antenna, built-in 3D motion sensor for energy-saving and motion detection, and a function key with vibration response for easy operation. The device also offers long standby times with its low power consumption and power management options. With a standby time of up to 400 hours and over 200 days with an external battery, you can rely on the SM-8570 for extended tracking periods.
