# Oigo Telematics - AR-2CX

![AR-2CX](./logo.jpg)

The Oigo Telematics AR-2CX is a compact GPS tracker from the AR Series designed for fleet management and stolen vehicle recovery applications. It emphasizes discreet vehicle installations and supports a range of event reporting and alert capabilities, making it suitable for organizations that need reliable location monitoring and incident notifications across a vehicle fleet.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the AR-2CX can feed location and event information into the Plaspy platform for consolidated monitoring and operational oversight. Its customizable event reports and discreet form factor make it a practical option for businesses that want to combine device-level alerts with Plaspy's tracking, reporting, and alert management features.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for fleet management and stolen vehicle recovery contexts
- Supports a diverse set of event reports and alerts for customizable notifications
- Compact and discreet form factor suited to hidden vehicle installation
- Useful for organizations seeking reliable location visibility across fleets
- Enables monitoring of vehicle status and behavior through configurable events
- Integrates into broader tracking workflows without drawing attention to the device

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AR-2CX provides continuous location updates and event messages that Plaspy translates into actionable insights for fleet managers. Plaspy collects the tracker data to present vehicle location, history, and alerts in a single interface for easy oversight.

- Centralize AR-2CX location data in Plaspy for live tracking and historical traces
- Map AR-2CX event reports to Plaspy alerts for speeding, unauthorized use, and similar events
- Use Plaspy dashboards and reports to review activity patterns and operational metrics
- Configure notification rules in Plaspy to surface AR-2CX events to relevant users
- Include AR-2CX tracked vehicles in fleet-level monitoring and management views

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operations that require continuous vehicle location visibility
- Stolen vehicle recovery programs needing discreet tracking hardware
- Organizations that monitor driver behavior through event based alerts
- Rental and shared vehicle services that need activity oversight
- Logistics and delivery fleets requiring consolidated tracking and reporting

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AR-2CX is a practical fit for organizations using Plaspy when discreet installation and flexible event reporting are priorities. Its support for a range of configurable alerts complements Plaspy's platform features, helping teams translate device events into operational actions without adding complexity to fleet workflows.

Because the AR-2CX is compatible with Plaspy, fleet managers can combine device-level notifications and location feeds with Plaspy's monitoring, reporting, and alerting tools to maintain better situational awareness across vehicles. This combination is useful for businesses that need an unobtrusive tracker that feeds meaningful event data into a centralized fleet management platform.
