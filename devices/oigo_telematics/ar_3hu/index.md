# Oigo Telematics - AR-3HU

![AR-3HU](./logo.jpg)

The Oigo Telematics AR-3HU is a versatile GPS tracker that is designed to seamlessly integrate into fleet management and stolen vehicle recovery systems. With its discreet vehicle installation design, it can be easily hidden from view, providing added security and peace of mind. 

One of the standout features of the AR-3HU is its support for a diverse set of event reports and alerts. This means that you can receive real-time notifications for various events such as speeding, harsh braking, and unauthorized vehicle use. This allows you to stay informed and take immediate action when necessary. 

The AR-3HU is equipped with 3G/HSDPA technology, ensuring fast and reliable communication between the tracker and the tracking platform. This ensures that you always have up-to-date and accurate location information for your vehicles. 

In terms of pricing, the AR-3HU is available for purchase starting from as little as US$69.00. Additionally, there is a lease option available upon request, providing flexibility for businesses of all sizes. 

Overall, the Oigo Telematics AR-3HU is a powerful and reliable GPS tracker that is perfect for fleet management and stolen vehicle recovery systems. Its discreet design, diverse event reporting capabilities, and affordable pricing make it an excellent choice for businesses looking to enhance their vehicle tracking capabilities.
