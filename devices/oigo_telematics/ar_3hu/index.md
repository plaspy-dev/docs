# Oigo Telematics - AR-3HU

![AR-3HU](./logo.jpg)

The Oigo Telematics AR-3HU is a compact, versatile GPS tracker designed for fleet management and stolen vehicle recovery systems. It is built for discreet vehicle installation and supports a range of event reports and alerts, making it suitable for continuous vehicle oversight. The device communicates over mobile data networks to keep location and event information updated in near real time.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AR-3HU can be integrated into Plaspy fleet monitoring workflows to provide location visibility, event notifications, and operational insights. Its discreet form factor and event reporting capabilities make it a practical option for organizations that need reliable tracking and timely alerts tied into a central monitoring platform like Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Discreet vehicle installation design suited to concealed mounting for improved security and theft recovery.
- Support for diverse event reporting and alerts such as speeding, harsh braking, and unauthorized use.
- Communication over 3G HSDPA networks to deliver timely position and status updates.
- Practical for both fleet management and stolen vehicle recovery applications.
- Entry level pricing with purchase and lease options to accommodate different budget needs.
- Compact form factor that fits a variety of vehicle types and deployment scenarios.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected with Plaspy, the AR-3HU forwards location updates and configured event reports into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor vehicles, receive alerts, and generate operational reports. Integration is focused on visibility, alerting, and centralized management rather than device level configuration.

- Display live and recent locations of AR-3HU equipped vehicles on Plaspy maps for operational oversight.
- Route and trip visibility to support dispatching and status checks through Plaspy dashboards.
- Event alerts from the device appear in Plaspy as notifications for speeding, harsh events, or unauthorized movement.
- Historical location and event data available in Plaspy for reporting and performance review.
- Use Plaspy rules and notification channels to forward critical AR-3HU alerts to managers or operators.

## Typical Use Cases

- Managing mixed fleets where discreet tracking and theft recovery are priorities.
- Monitoring driver behavior patterns such as frequent harsh events for coaching and safety programs.
- Rental and leased vehicle fleets that require affordable and trackable hardware options.
- Stolen vehicle recovery workflows where timely location updates are essential.
- Small to medium business fleet oversight needing low cost entry devices linked to a centralized platform.

The AR-3HU is a practical choice for organizations that want a discreet, event-aware tracker coupled with a centralized fleet management platform. Its support for a variety of event reports and mobile data communication makes it suitable for teams that need timely notifications and ongoing location visibility without complex device footprint.
