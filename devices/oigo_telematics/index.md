# Oigo Telematics

Oigo Telematics designs compact telematics hardware used for location and asset monitoring. This page explains how Oigo Telematics devices can be used with Plaspy, how to evaluate the right device for your use case, and where to go next to add a device to Plaspy. Use the catalog below to view all Oigo compatible models and navigate to device specific documentation.

## How Oigo Telematics devices work with Plaspy

Oigo Telematics devices report location and telemetry over cellular networks. When paired with Plaspy, those data streams are ingested into the Plaspy platform where they appear on maps, in reports, and in alert feeds. Integration typically involves registering the device in Plaspy and confirming that the device is configured to send its data to the Plaspy service.

Plaspy translates incoming telemetry into usable objects like vehicle location, speed history, sensor status, and custom inputs. You can then use Plaspy features such as live tracking, historical playback, alerts, and reporting to manage your fleet or assets that use Oigo devices.

## Common features found in Oigo Telematics devices

While specific features vary by model, Oigo Telematics devices commonly support capabilities useful for fleet and asset tracking:
- GNSS based location reporting for real time and historical tracking
- Configurable reporting intervals to balance accuracy and battery or data usage
- Digital and analog inputs for connecting sensors or ignition detection
- Motion and tamper detection for basic security monitoring
- Support for cellular connectivity to transmit telemetry over public networks

For precise hardware capabilities consult the model pages in the device catalog or the manufacturer documentation.

## Typical use cases

Oigo Telematics devices are typically used for:
- Fleet vehicle tracking for route oversight and operational visibility
- Asset monitoring for trailers, containers, and equipment
- Field service vehicle dispatch and location awareness
- Theft deterrence and recovery workflows using movement alerts
- Remote status monitoring for sensors attached to vehicles or assets

Plaspy provides the software layer to turn Oigo device telemetry into actionable insights and operational workflows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What Oigo Telematics devices are compatible with Plaspy?

Q: Can I use Oigo Telematics GPS trackers with Plaspy?
A: Yes. Oigo Telematics trackers that transmit standard telemetry can be added to Plaspy. Once a device is registered in Plaspy and configured to send data to the Plaspy service, its location and sensor data will appear in the platform.

Q: Does Plaspy support fleet monitoring with Oigo Telematics devices?
A: Plaspy supports fleet monitoring features such as live location, historical route playback, alerts, and reporting for devices that send compatible telemetry. These capabilities allow you to manage vehicles and drivers that use Oigo hardware.

Q: Where can I find Oigo Telematics device documentation in Plaspy?
