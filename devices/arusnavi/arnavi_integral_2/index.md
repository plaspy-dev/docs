# Arusnavi - Arnavi Integral 2

![Arnavi Integral 2](./logo.jpg)

The Arnavi Integral 2 is a navigation controller designed for remote monitoring of mobile objects. It provides real time tracking and location information, making it suitable for tracking vehicles, equipment, and other movable assets. The device is described as versatile and reliable with features such as geofencing and a compact, durable form factor that supports continuous monitoring in everyday operational conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Arnavi Integral 2 can be integrated into fleet and asset management workflows to deliver visibility and oversight. When used with Plaspy, the tracker supplies location updates and event information that help operations teams monitor movements, receive alerts for boundary crossings, and review historical activity for reporting and analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location reporting for continuous visibility of mobile assets
- Built in geofencing support to trigger alerts when assets enter or leave defined areas
- Versatile compatibility allowing use with Plaspy and other management platforms
- Compact and durable design suitable for everyday use on vehicles and equipment
- Designed for extended monitoring with long battery life and reliable performance
- Well suited to mixed asset fleets where a single controller can cover multiple use cases

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Arnavi Integral 2 sends location and event information that Plaspy uses to display assets on maps, generate alerts, and produce activity reports. Integration enables operators to centralize monitoring and manage responses from the Plaspy platform without depending on device specific tooling.

- Map visibility for tracked assets with live position updates
- Geofence alerts delivered through Plaspy to notify teams of boundary events
- Historical tracks and activity logs for reporting and operational review
- Fleet level monitoring to view status across multiple devices and groups
- Configurable alerts and notifications to support operational workflows
- Use of Plaspy reporting features to analyze movement patterns and utilization

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for logistics, deliveries, and service vehicles
- Construction and heavy equipment monitoring to manage utilization
- Rental asset oversight to detect location and movement of leased items
- Field worker location visibility for dispatch and safety monitoring
- Route monitoring and operational oversight for improving efficiency

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Arnavi Integral 2 is a practical choice for organizations seeking straightforward location and event monitoring. Its combination of real time tracking, geofencing, and a robust design makes it suitable for a range of fleets and asset types. Pairing the device with Plaspy brings device data into a unified management platform, helping teams turn raw location updates into actionable oversight and routine reporting.
