# Arusnavi - Integral 4

![Integral 4](./tracker.png)

Integral 4 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and universal navigation controller designed for professional fleet management, telemetry and vehicle monitoring. Built around a powerful microcontroller with multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS\) and internal cellular and navigation antennas, Integral 4 delivers accurate real-time tracking and extensive on‑board data collection that integrates smoothly with Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting.

Designed for installation in constrained spaces, Integral 4 combines 2G GSM connectivity with dual SIM support, internal Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) and a wide set of wired interfaces \(USB, 1-WIRE, RS-485/CAN options, UART/RS-232 optional\). This flexibility makes the device ideal for fleet telemetry, fuel monitoring, passenger transport control and asset supervision where Plaspy compatible devices are required for seamless data ingestion, alerts and analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with multi‑GNSS support for reliable real-time tracking and route information.
- Dual SIM 2G GSM modem and internal antennas for easier installation and continuous connectivity.
- Extensive wired and wireless interfaces \(USB, 1-WIRE, RS-485/CAN, optional UART/RS-232, internal BLE\) to collect telemetry and sensor data.
- Pairs with up to 10 Bluetooth sensors and supports a wide list of compatible BLE devices for temperature, proximity and auxiliary telemetry.
- Enterprise-ready power range \(8–50 V\), internal backup battery \(300–1800 mAh\) and black box logging up to ~100,000 records for offline continuity.
- Configurable I/O for event detection and control: multiple discrete inputs/outputs and an analog ADC input for ignition, door, alarm and actuator integration.
- Compact form factor \(70 x 52 x 22 mm, ~50 g\) suited to tight installations in vehicles and mobile assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integral 4 integrates with Plaspy by transmitting GNSS position, CAN/UART telemetry, sensor readings and discrete input events over its cellular link to Plaspy monitoring servers. The device supports standard industry protocols and configurable server settings so location, diagnostic data and event logs appear in Plaspy dashboards as real-time tracking, alerts and historical reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates from GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BeiDou/QZSS\) and vehicle buses.
- Ignition and door/alarm status monitoring via the device’s negative/positive discrete inputs and accelerometer-based motion detection.
- Fuel monitoring support using RS-485 connected sensors \(up to 8 DУT\) and CAN-bus fuel data where available.
- Remote control and immobilizer functionality can be implemented via configurable discrete outputs and integration with Plaspy control workflows.
- Bluetooth sensors/beacons: pairs with up to 10 BLE devices \(supported devices list included\) for temperature, RFID, passenger counting and other auxiliary telemetry.
- Offline black box logging stores ~100,000 records locally and forwards data to Plaspy when connectivity is restored, ensuring no gaps in history.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real-time tracking, route history and CAN-based vehicle diagnostics for scheduled maintenance and driver behavior analysis.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry: connect up to eight RS-485 fuel sensors \(DУT\) or use CAN fuel data to reduce fuel loss and optimize consumption.
- Passenger transport control: integrate passenger counters, RFID readers and BLE sensors for occupancy, route compliance and fare control.
- Anti-theft and security monitoring: detect ignition/door events, motion via accelerometer and trigger alerts or immobilizer actions via discrete outputs when integrated with Plaspy workflows.
- Asset and equipment telemetry: compact size and wide voltage range make Integral 4 suitable for containers, trailers and mobile equipment requiring multi-sensor inputs and offline logging.

Choosing Integral 4 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivers a balanced combination of accurate real-time tracking, broad telemetry interfaces and enterprise-grade logging and power handling. With remote configuration, firmware update capabilities and supplied documentation/command maps, Integral 4 integrates efficiently into Plaspy-powered fleet and asset management deployments—helping you reduce downtime, improve fuel efficiency and strengthen anti-theft and monitoring processes without complexity.
