# Arusnavi - Integral 4

![Integral 4](./tracker.png)

Integral 4 is a compact GPS tracker and universal navigation controller from Arusnavi designed for professional fleet management, telemetry and vehicle monitoring. It combines multi constellations for GNSS positioning with integrated cellular and navigation antennas and on board data collection to deliver accurate real time location and diagnostic information suitable for vehicle and asset oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, Integral 4 can feed position, sensor and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its combination of cellular connectivity, internal Bluetooth and a wide set of wired interfaces makes it practical for fleets and telemetry deployments that need seamless ingestion into Plaspy dashboards and workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with multi GNSS support for reliable real time positioning and route data.
- Dual SIM 2G cellular modem and internal antennas to support continuous connectivity in mobile environments.
- Wide interface set including USB, 1 WIRE, RS 485 and configurable vehicle bus options plus optional UART RS 232 for telemetry integration.
- Internal Bluetooth Low Energy with support for pairing up to 10 external sensors for temperature, proximity and auxiliary telemetry.
- Internal black box logging capable of storing large record sets for offline continuity and later upload to Plaspy.
- Configurable discrete inputs and outputs and an analog ADC input for monitoring ignition, doors and event driven signals.
- Compact form factor suited to constrained installation spaces in vehicles and mobile assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integral 4 transmits GNSS positions, telemetry from vehicle buses and sensor readings to Plaspy via its cellular connection so fleet operators can view live locations, events and history in the Plaspy platform. Configurable server settings and supported data formats enable location, diagnostic and event logs to appear as real time tracking, alerts and historical reports.

- Real time location and telemetry updates derived from multi constellation GNSS and on board telemetry sources.
- Event monitoring for ignition, door and alarm status using discrete inputs and motion detection for movement events.
- Fuel and sensor telemetry via RS 485 or vehicle bus integration where available to support consumption and loss detection workflows.
- Remote control and immobilizer actions implemented through configurable outputs and Plaspy control workflows.
- Bluetooth sensor and beacon support for auxiliary telemetry such as temperature monitoring, passenger counting or proximity detection.
- Offline black box logging stores records locally and forwards them to Plaspy when connectivity is restored to preserve history.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, route history and vehicle diagnostics for operations and maintenance planning.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry integration to detect anomalies and support consumption analysis.
- Passenger transport control combining occupancy sensors, RFID and BLE devices for route compliance and operational oversight.
- Anti theft and security monitoring using door and ignition events, motion detection and remote control outputs.
- Asset and equipment telemetry for trailers, containers and mobile machinery that require compact devices and robust logging.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Integral 4 is a practical choice for organizations using Plaspy when they need a compact device that balances accurate positioning, diverse telemetry interfaces and enterprise class logging. Its multi GNSS positioning, dual SIM cellular connectivity and internal Bluetooth simplify sensor integration and installation in constrained spaces, while configurable bus and serial options let teams capture vehicle diagnostics and fuel data for centralized analysis in Plaspy.

For deployments that require reliable offline continuity and a broad set of inputs and outputs, Integral 4 offers straightforward data capture and remote management capabilities that integrate into Plaspy driven monitoring, alerting and reporting workflows. Its combination of features supports common fleet and asset tracking needs without forcing complex platform adaptations.
