# Arusnavi - Arnavi

![Arnavi](./logo.jpg)

The Arusnavi Arnavi is a versatile navigation controller designed for remote monitoring of mobile objects. It is positioned as a flexible device for tracking and supervising vehicles and the equipment installed on them. The Arnavi supports a broad set of sensor types and integrates with vehicle data channels, making it suitable for capturing status and operational information from a variety of mobile assets.

As a device that integrates with standard tracking software, the Arnavi is a natural fit for Plaspy. When used with Plaspy, the Arnavi can feed location, sensor readings, and vehicle bus information into a unified fleet management view. This compatibility lets organizations consolidate monitoring, alerts, and reporting in Plaspy while leveraging the Arnavi hardware for on-vehicle data collection.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for remote monitoring of mobile objects such as cars and mobile equipment
- Broad sensor support including digital, discrete, analog, and frequency pulse inputs
- Compatibility with vehicle CAN bus for access to onboard vehicle data
- Easy integration into existing tracking platforms and software packages
- Flexible for mixed fleets and varied equipment monitoring needs
- Useful for capturing operational data to inform maintenance and logistics decisions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Arnavi acts as a data source that feeds location and operational information into Plaspy's fleet management environment. Plaspy can display and archive the device data alongside other fleet telemetry to support visibility and decision making.

- Centralized visibility of vehicle location and movement inside the Plaspy interface
- Display of sensor inputs and status indicators for equipment monitoring and oversight
- Inclusion of CAN bus derived values in Plaspy dashboards where available
- Configurable alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on Arnavi sensor or status conditions
- Reporting and historical data export for operational analysis and compliance
- Operational oversight features such as geofencing and activity timelines using Arnavi data

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and oversight for commercial vehicle fleets
- Monitoring of installed equipment condition on vehicles and mobile machinery
- Logistics visibility for transport and delivery operations
- Remote status monitoring for construction or agricultural assets
- Data collection for preventive maintenance planning and operational review

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Arnavi is well suited for organizations that need a flexible hardware option to capture both positional and vehicular data and bring it into a managed fleet platform. Its support for multiple sensor types and CAN bus connectivity means it can serve a range of monitoring needs, from simple location tracking to more detailed equipment status reporting. Paired with Plaspy, the device helps deliver a coherent operational picture across vehicles and assets without forcing a change to established software workflows.

For teams that prioritize interoperability and consolidated fleet visibility, using Arnavi with Plaspy provides a practical path to integrate vehicle location, sensor readings, and vehicle bus information into one platform.
