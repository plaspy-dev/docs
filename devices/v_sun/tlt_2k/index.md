# V-SUN - TLT-2K

![TLT-2K](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-2K is a versatile vehicle positioning device that utilizes GPS and GSM/GPRS technology to provide accurate and real-time location information. With this device, you can easily track the longitude and latitude coordinates of your vehicle and receive them on your mobile phone via SMS. Additionally, you can view the detailed location of the unit on popular map software such as Google Maps.

One of the standout features of the V-SUN TLT-2K is its real-time SMS query capability, allowing you to instantly retrieve the coordinates of your vehicle. This feature comes in handy when you need to quickly locate your vehicle or keep track of its movements. The device also offers time position and phone position features, providing you with comprehensive information about the whereabouts of your vehicle.

Remote monitoring is another notable feature of the TLT-2K. You can remotely monitor your vehicle using this device, giving you peace of mind and added security. In case of emergencies, the active help feature allows you to send distress signals and seek assistance. The speed alarm function alerts you when your vehicle exceeds a certain speed limit, helping you maintain safe driving habits.

The TLT-2K also offers historical data uploading, allowing you to review past routes and locations. This can be useful for analyzing driving patterns or keeping a record of your vehicle's movements. The device includes a low power reminder to ensure that you never run out of battery unexpectedly. Additionally, the running track monitoring feature enables you to monitor the path taken by your vehicle in real-time.

The V-SUN TLT-2K is an ideal choice for various applications, including tracking, fixed-point checking of cars, and special saving electricity functions. It is suitable for use with motorcycles, electric cars, and other vehicles. With its reliable GPS and GSM/GPRS technology, this device provides accurate and efficient vehicle positioning capabilities.
