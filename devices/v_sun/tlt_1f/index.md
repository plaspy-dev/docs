# V-SUN - TLT-1F

<div className="device-logo">
![TLT-1F](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The V-SUN TLT-1F is a vehicle tracking device designed for remote positioning and continuous monitoring of cars and commercial vehicles. It combines a high-performance GPS chipset with GSM/GPRS communication to deliver location information and event reports even in urban canyons, valleys, and other areas with limited visibility. The TLT-1F includes features commonly used for fleet and vehicle security such as SOS, geo-fencing, overspeed warnings, historical data upload, and alerts for power or fuel interruptions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TLT-1F can forward position updates and event notifications to the Plaspy fleet management platform for mapping, alerting, and reporting. This compatibility makes it suitable for organizations that want to consolidate vehicle visibility and operational oversight within Plaspy, enabling monitoring of movement tracks, responding to SOS events, and reviewing historical journeys from a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- Accurate positioning with a high performance GPS chipset optimized for sight limited areas
- Global GSM GPRS communication bands for wide regional coverage
- Multiple reporting and alert features including SOS, geo-fencing, and overspeed warnings
- Historical data upload and timed tracking for reviewing past movements
- Alerts for power failure and external power tampering to support vehicle security
- Voice call functionality available when connected to an earphone for additional convenience

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TLT-1F transmits location and event information to the platform where data is presented on maps, timelines, and in alert feeds. Plaspy consolidates incoming updates from compatible devices so fleet managers can track vehicles, receive notifications, and generate reports without switching systems.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps for ongoing visibility
- Event and alert ingestion for SOS, overspeed, geofence breaches, and power or fuel interruptions
- Historical route and trip playback using uploaded track data
- Configurable alerts and notifications routed through Plaspy to relevant staff
- Reporting and exportable records to support operational review and compliance

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking and monitoring private cars for security and recovery
- School and scholastic transport oversight with route and stop visibility
- Long distance passenger vehicle tracking for trip monitoring and safety
- Freight and goods vehicle tracking to improve dispatch and delivery visibility
- Small and medium fleet management for consolidated monitoring and alerts
