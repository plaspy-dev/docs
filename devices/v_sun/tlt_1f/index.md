# V-SUN - TLT-1F

![TLT-1F](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-1F Vehicle Tracking Device is a reliable and efficient car remote positioning and tracking device that utilizes GPS technology and GSM/GPRS technology. With its built-in high-performance GPS chipset, this device ensures accurate positioning even in areas with weak signals or limited visibility. It is designed to work seamlessly in urban areas, valleys, and other sight-limited districts.

Equipped with a GSM/GPRS module that supports GSM900/1800MHz \(850/1900 MHz\), the TLT-1F can operate worldwide, making it suitable for use in various countries and regions. It offers multiple communication options, including SMS and GPRS TCP connection, allowing users to receive position information via cell phone messages or access movement tracks on the internet.

The TLT-1F comes with a range of useful features, including SOS functionality, fuel cutoff/power failure alerts, warning notifications for cutting off external power wires, geo-fencing capabilities, overspeed warnings, historical data upload, parking throttle upload, and timed tracking uploads for moving vehicles. Additionally, it offers voice call functionality when connected to an earphone, providing added convenience and flexibility.

Overall, the V-SUN TLT-1F Vehicle Tracking Device is an excellent choice for private cars, scholastic vehicles, long-distance passenger vehicles, and freight vehicles. Its advanced features, reliable performance, and global compatibility make it a valuable tool for tracking and monitoring vehicles in various applications.
