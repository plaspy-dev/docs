# V-SUN - TLT-8A

![TLT-8A](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-8A GPS/GSM car tracker is a reliable and efficient device that combines GPS technology with GSM/GPRS technology to provide accurate positioning and tracking capabilities. With its built-in high-performance GPS chipset, this tracker can provide accurate positioning even in areas with weak signal, such as urban areas or valleys. It supports GSM900/1800MHz \(850/1900 MHz\), making it compatible worldwide.

One of the standout features of the TLT-8A is its versatility. It can be used in motorcycles, electric golf carts, and private cars, making it suitable for a wide range of vehicles. The device is easy to install, thanks to its built-in antennae. It can receive position information through SMS communications or GPRS TCP connection, allowing users to track their vehicles in real-time or view historical movement tracks on the internet.

The TLT-8A offers a variety of useful functions, including SOS, fuel/electricity shut-off, warning by cutting off external power wire, geo-fence function, overspeed warning, historical data upload, parking throttle upload, and moving vehicle tracking timing upload. It also has a power-saving function, improving its battery life and standby time. With its practicality, performance, and elegant design, the V-SUN TLT-8A is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a reliable and feature-rich car tracker.

### Product Characteristics:

- Built-in high-performance GPS chipset for accurate positioning
- Supports GSM900/1800MHz \(850/1900 MHz\) for worldwide compatibility
- Supports SMS communications or GPRS TCP connection for position information
- Functions include SOS, fuel/electricity shut-off, warning by cutting off external power wire, geo-fence function, overspeed warning, historical data upload, parking throttle upload, and moving vehicle tracking timing upload
- Power-saving function for improved battery life
