# V-SUN - TLT-7B

![TLT-7B](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-7B is a 3G GPS tracker that offers a range of advanced features to ensure accurate and reliable tracking. With its built-in high-performance GPS chipset, this tracker can provide precise positioning even in areas with weak signal or limited visibility. Whether you're in an urban area or a valley, you can count on the TLT-7B to deliver accurate location information.

One of the standout features of the TLT-7B is its global compatibility. It supports both GSM and WCDMA networks, allowing it to work seamlessly worldwide. With support for GSM 850/900/1800/1900MHz and WCDMA 850/900/1900/2100MHz, this tracker ensures that you stay connected no matter where you are.

In terms of communication, the TLT-7B offers multiple options. You can choose to receive position information through SMS messages or access it online through GPRS TCP connection. This flexibility allows you to easily track and monitor your assets using your preferred method.

The TLT-7B is packed with a range of useful functions to enhance your tracking experience. It includes features such as SOS, fuel cutoff/power failure alerts, geo-fencing, overspeed warnings, historical data upload, and more. Additionally, it supports voice call functionality when connected to an earphone, providing added convenience and versatility.

With its reliable electric circuit and compliance with industry standards, the TLT-7B ensures long-lasting performance and durability. It also supports the OBD II interface standard for cars, allowing you to access important vehicle data such as mileage statistics, fuel consumption, engine temperature, and more. Please note that the specific data parameters may vary depending on the type of car.

Overall, the V-SUN TLT-7B is a feature-rich 3G GPS tracker that offers accurate positioning, global compatibility, and a range of useful functions. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or loved ones, this tracker is a reliable and versatile choice.
