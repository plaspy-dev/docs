# V-SUN - TLT-3A

![TLT-3A](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-3A GPS Vehicle Tracker is a popular and versatile device suitable for a wide range of vehicles, including automobiles, motorcycles, and electric cars. With its advanced features and reliable performance, this tracker is designed to provide real-time tracking and speed upload, ensuring you have accurate and up-to-date information about your vehicle's location and movement.

One of the key features of the TLT-3A is its geo-fence function, which allows you to set up virtual boundaries for your vehicle. If the vehicle crosses these boundaries, you will receive an alert, ensuring that you are always aware of its whereabouts. Additionally, the tracker offers parking automatic protection, which means that if your vehicle is moved without authorization, you will be immediately notified.

The TLT-3A supports GSM850/900/1800/1900 MHZ, making it compatible with networks worldwide. This means that no matter where you are, you can track your vehicle with ease. The device also features a high stability GPS chipset from Philips, ensuring accurate and reliable positioning. Furthermore, it is equipped with a high-sensitive G-SENSOR that can detect the state of your vehicle, allowing for comprehensive tracking and monitoring.

Key Features:

- Real-time tracking and speed upload
- Geo-fence function for setting virtual boundaries
- Parking automatic protection
- Fuel shutoff
- Auxiliary tracking of base station
- Supports GSM850/900/1800/1900 MHZ for global compatibility
- High stability GPS chipset from Philips
- High-sensitive G-SENSOR for comprehensive tracking
