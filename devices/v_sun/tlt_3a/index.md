# V-SUN - TLT-3A

<div className="device-logo">
![TLT-3A](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The V-SUN TLT-3A is a compact GPS vehicle tracker designed for a wide range of vehicles including cars, motorcycles, and electric vehicles. It provides real time tracking and speed upload, supports virtual geofencing and parking protection features, and includes a high sensitivity G sensor to detect vehicle movement. The device is positioned as a versatile option for owners and fleet operators who need continuous location awareness and basic event notifications.

As a device known to be compatible with Plaspy, the TLT-3A can feed location, movement, and geofence events into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. This compatibility makes the tracker a practical choice for organizations that want to consolidate vehicle visibility and alerts in Plaspy alongside other compatible devices, enabling easier oversight of mixed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location updates and speed upload for up to date tracking information
- Geo fence support to create virtual boundaries and receive crossing alerts
- Parking automatic protection that notifies when the vehicle is moved without authorization
- High sensitivity G sensor for movement detection and event triggering
- Worldwide network compatibility using common GSM bands for broad coverage
- Designed for a variety of vehicle types including automobiles, motorcycles, and electric cars

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TLT-3A transmits location and event data that Plaspy displays on its maps and dashboards, allowing operators to monitor assets from a single interface. Plaspy uses the incoming data to generate alerts, build reports, and provide operational context for fleet decisions.

- Centralized location visibility on Plaspy maps for individual vehicles and whole fleets
- Geofence alerts routed into Plaspy to notify managers when predefined boundaries are crossed
- Parking protection events and movement detections appear as alerts for timely security responses
- Speed and movement information can be included in Plaspy reports for compliance and analysis
- Historical location trails available in Plaspy for route review and operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for mixed groups of vehicles including cars, motorcycles, and electric vehicles
- Asset protection and recovery through geofence and parking movement alerts
- Delivery and courier operations needing location visibility and route history
- Rental and shared vehicle programs that require monitoring of vehicle status and location
- Small and medium business tracking for driver oversight and operational reporting

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TLT-3A pairs practical tracking features with broad vehicle applicability, making it a reasonable option for organizations that need consistent location updates and basic event notifications. Its geofence and parking protection functions integrate naturally into Plaspy workflows, helping teams maintain visibility and respond to incidents without introducing overly complex device management.

Because the TLT-3A supports common network bands and includes a high stability GPS chipset and a sensitive movement sensor, it fits well where reliable positional data and simple movement alerts are the priority. For organizations using Plaspy to centralize fleet data, the TLT-3A offers a straightforward way to extend monitoring coverage across diverse vehicle types.
