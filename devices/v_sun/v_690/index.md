# V-SUN - V-690

![V-690](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN V-690 GPS tracker is a versatile device designed for various applications. It offers positioning, monitoring, safety protection, and SOS functions, making it suitable for a wide range of users. Whether you need to keep track of your children, elderly or disabled persons, or manage the safety of your field staff or business personnel, the V-690 has got you covered.

One of the standout features of the V-690 is its alarm system. It comes equipped with four speed dial alarm buttons and one SOS button. In case of an emergency, simply press the SOS button, and an SOS signal will be sent to the control center, allowing them to pinpoint your location accurately. This feature provides peace of mind and ensures that help is just a button press away.

In addition to the SOS function, the V-690 also offers active and passive monitoring options. The guardian can easily search for the user's location directly through messages, eliminating the need for a monitoring system. The device also supports time setting and continuous/discontinuous tracking functions, allowing you to customize the tracking experience according to your needs.

Furthermore, the V-690 supports electronic fence functionality, which means you can set designated ranges and receive alarms if the device exceeds those boundaries. This feature is particularly useful for ensuring the safety of your loved ones or monitoring the movements of your field staff. Additionally, the V-690 offers track recording and historical track playback functions, allowing you to review past routes and movements.

With its comprehensive set of features and versatile applications, the V-SUN V-690 GPS tracker is an excellent choice for anyone in need of reliable positioning, monitoring, and safety protection. Whether you're a concerned parent, a caregiver, or a business owner, this device will provide you with the peace of mind you deserve.
