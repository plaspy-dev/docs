# V-SUN - V-690

<div className="device-logo">
![V-690](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The V-SUN V-690 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for positioning, monitoring, safety protection, and emergency response. It combines location tracking with user-focused safety features such as an SOS button and dedicated alarm keys, making it suitable for personal safety and light duty field monitoring. The device supports continuous and intermittent tracking modes, electronic fence boundaries, and historical route playback to help users review past movements.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the V-690 can feed location and alarm events into Plaspy's fleet and personnel monitoring environment. This compatibility makes it practical for organizations and caregivers who want to centralize visibility and alerts within Plaspy, while preserving the device's built in SOS and alarm functions. With Plaspy, V-690 data becomes part of a broader monitoring and reporting workflow for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Built for positioning and monitoring with support for continuous and discontinuous tracking modes
- Dedicated SOS button for quick emergency signaling to a control center
- Four speed dial alarm buttons to enable rapid contact or alerting
- Electronic fence support to receive alerts when set boundaries are crossed
- Track recording with historical playback for route review and audit
- Active and passive monitoring options, including direct location queries by message

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the V-SUN V-690 delivers location updates and alarm events that Plaspy can present in maps, reports, and alert streams. Plaspy consolidates the device information so fleet managers, caregivers, or operations teams can monitor status and respond to incidents from a single platform.

- Live location visibility on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring
- Geofence management and alarm routing to notify supervisors when boundaries are crossed
- SOS and alarm event handling to surface urgent signals within Plaspy dashboards
- Historical track playback and reporting to analyze past movements and behaviors
- Support for active and passive monitoring workflows to query location or rely on periodic updates
- Centralized alerting and notification configuration for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children, elderly, or vulnerable individuals
- Lone worker or field staff tracking where SOS alerts and location visibility are important
- Small fleet or mobile personnel oversight requiring geofence alerts and route review
- Caregiver or guardian use where direct location queries and simple alarm buttons provide reassurance
- Businesses that need straightforward historical playback for route verification
