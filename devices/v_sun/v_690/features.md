# V-SUN - V-690 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the V-SUN V-690 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for tracking, monitoring, and safety awareness. It focuses on practical, non sensitive descriptions of what the device can do and how those functions translate to visibility inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior for a particular unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. For device specific technical or regulatory details consult the official V-SUN documentation and firmware notes.

## Feature Overview

The V-SUN V-690 is positioned as a versatile personal and personnel tracker with a focus on location awareness, emergency signaling, and configurable monitoring. It combines continuous and on demand reporting options with alarms and historical route recording to support safety and oversight workflows.

- Real time positioning for location awareness and map visualization
- Dedicated SOS button for emergency alerts to a control center
- Multiple speed dial alarm buttons for quick contact or notifications
- Active and passive monitoring modes including message based location queries
- Configurable tracking modes including continuous and discontinuous reporting
- Electronic fence support with alarms when the device leaves or enters designated areas

## Core Features of V-SUN - V-690

- Positioning and location reporting for live tracking and map updates
- SOS emergency alert that transmits an emergency signal to a control center
- Four speed dial alarm buttons to trigger preconfigured alerts or notifications
- Active monitoring and passive monitoring options for flexible oversight
- Time setting and configurable tracking behavior for continuous or intermittent reports
- Electronic fence functionality with entry and exit alarms for designated zones
- Track recording and historical playback for route review and audit

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and interprets the V-690 device reports so fleet managers and guardians can view location, alarm, and history information in a single platform. Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol and makes received events available for mapping, alerts, and playback.

- Live location updates appear on the Plaspy map for operational visibility
- SOS and alarm events are surfaced as priority events so teams can respond quickly
- Geofence events generated by the device show as zone entry or exit alerts in Plaspy
- Recorded tracks and historical route data can be reviewed within the platform
- On demand or message triggered location queries from guardians are reflected as location requests and responses in Plaspy

Note: Plaspy accepts tracker connections on the public ingestion endpoint d.plaspy.com and automatically detects compatible protocols to simplify integration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children, elderly, or people with special needs
- Lone worker and field staff safety oversight with emergency alerting
- Route review and historical playback for operational audits
- Geofence based perimeter monitoring for supervised movement
- Simple message based location queries when live monitoring is not required

- Some functions may depend on regional variants or optional hardware revisions

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the V-SUN V-690 with Plaspy brings device level alerts and location updates into a centralized monitoring environment. Organizations gain consistent operational visibility across units, prioritized handling of SOS and alarm events, and historical route playback for review and reporting. Plaspy's automatic protocol detection helps reduce setup complexity so devices can begin reporting with minimal manual configuration.
