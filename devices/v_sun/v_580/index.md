# V-SUN - V-580

![V-580](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN V-580 is a child tracking device presented as a small GPS phone with a child friendly cartoon design. It combines location tracking with safety features aimed at primary school students, including arrival and departure notifications, an SOS emergency call function, electronic fences for boundary alerts, and class time controls to limit messaging during lessons. The device is positioned to give parents timely updates about a child’s whereabouts while remaining unobtrusive for school use.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the V-580 can provide its core location and event information into a centralized tracking platform. That compatibility makes the device useful for parents and for organizations that need consolidated visibility across multiple devices. When paired with Plaspy, the V-580’s alerts and location events can be surfaced alongside other tracked assets to support monitoring, notifications, and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for children with a cartoon style that encourages regular wear and use
- Location tracking and arrival departure notifications for school and home
- SOS emergency button for rapid alerting in urgent situations
- Electronic fence support to notify when a child leaves a predefined area
- Class time control to restrict message sending and reduce classroom distraction
- Remote monitoring features that allow caregivers to check status and activity

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the V-580 is used with Plaspy it feeds location updates and event notifications into a unified tracking system so caregivers can view and act on those events from a single dashboard. Plaspy organizes the device data to make alerts and location history easier to manage across multiple devices.

- Centralized visibility of live and recent locations for one or many V-580 devices
- Geofence event handling to trigger notifications when a child enters or leaves a set area
- SOS event routing so emergency alerts are presented prominently in Plaspy
- Scheduled or conditional alerts such as arrival at school or home surfaced in platform notifications
- Historical location reporting to review recent movements and timelines

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring a primary school student during the commute to and from school
- Notifying parents when a child arrives at school or returns home
- Keeping children within predefined safe zones using geofence alerts
- Providing a quick emergency alert channel through the SOS button
- Limiting distractions during class by enforcing class time restrictions

The V-SUN V-580 combines child focused hardware features with straightforward event notifications that map well to Plaspy’s monitoring and alerting capabilities. Its emphasis on arrival alerts, geofencing, and an SOS function makes it a practical option for parents and schools that need simple, reliable location awareness integrated into a broader tracking environment.
