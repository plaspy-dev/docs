# V-SUN - V-3338

![V-3338](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN V-3338 is a compact, portable GPS tracking device that combines GPS positioning with GSM GPRS communication to provide remote location tracking. It can send latitude and longitude information via SMS for quick location retrieval and can upload positioning data to a designated server for online tracking and historical route review. The device includes a high performance GPS chipset from JRC to improve positioning in weak signal conditions and offers voice and SOS call features for emergency notification.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the V-3338 can feed location data into the Plaspy fleet management platform to deliver live visibility and historical tracking. Plaspy can use the device's SMS responses or uploaded GPRS data to present positions on maps, generate trajectory reports, and surface alerts for operational oversight. For organizations looking to combine a small handheld tracker with a cloud based tracking platform, the V-3338 is a practical option to consider.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and portable form factor suitable for handheld use and easy transport
- Dual reporting options via SMS for quick queries and GPRS upload for continuous tracking
- High performance JRC GPS chipset designed to help with positioning in weak signal areas
- Voice and SOS call features for emergency notifications and quick contact
- Low power consumption and lightweight design for extended field use
- Suitable for broad GSM band compatibility to support worldwide deployment

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the V-3338 delivers position updates and event information that Plaspy can display and analyze in a unified interface. Plaspy ingests the device's uploaded location data or interpreted SMS location messages to provide visualization and operational tools for fleet managers.

- Real time location display on maps within the Plaspy platform using uploaded positioning data
- Historical trajectory playback and route reporting for review and analysis
- Configurable alerts and notifications based on location events and SOS triggers
- Centralized device list and status overview to monitor multiple V-3338 units at once
- Reporting and export options for operational metrics and compliance tracking
