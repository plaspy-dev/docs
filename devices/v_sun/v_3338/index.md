# V-SUN - V-3338

![V-3338](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN V-3338 is a compact and portable GPS tracking device that utilizes GPS technology and GSM/GPRS technology to provide remote location tracking. With this device, users can access latitude and longitude location information via SMS and then view their location on Google Maps or other mapping software. Alternatively, the tracking device can upload positioning data via GPRS to a designated server, allowing users to track their location in real-time or view historical trajectory through the internet. 

One of the standout features of the V-3338 is its built-in high-performance GPS chipset from JRC. This chipset allows for accurate positioning even in weak signal conditions or areas with limited sky view, such as urban canyons. Additionally, the device is equipped with a GSM/GPRS module that supports GSM 900/1800 \(or 850/1900\) MHz, making it compatible worldwide. 

The V-3338 also offers voice features, allowing for communication via SMS or GPRS TCP connection. It can be used to receive location information, change control commands, or switch GPS tracking. In case of emergencies, the device can be pre-set with three numbers, and pressing the SOS button will initiate a call to the stored telephone numbers and send the exact location. 

With its lightweight, compact design and low power consumption, the V-SUN V-3338 is suitable for a variety of applications. It can be used as a handheld portable device, making it convenient for users to carry around. It is also ideal for caring for the elderly, children, and people in need of care. Additionally, it can be used for logistics and inspection purposes in official occasions. The V-3338 provides personalized services to companies, organizations, and individuals through the GSM network and the internet.
