# V-SUN - TLT-6C

![TLT-6C](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-6C is a highly advanced vehicle positioning terminal that combines GPS global positioning technology with mobile positioning technology. With its built-in dual-mode antenna, this vehicle computer ensures accurate and real-time visibility of vehicles in both open areas and high-rise or underground parking areas. It integrates a high-performance, low-power processor, an industrial-grade GPS module, an industrial CDMA module, and various tracking, orientation, and communication features. The TLT-6C is compact, concealed, sturdy, and reliable, making it easy to install in a variety of vehicles.

This vehicle location and positioning terminal is particularly suitable for taxi services, special vehicles, buses, long-distance transport vehicles, and scheduling purposes. It can also be used for general vehicle theft prevention. The TLT-6C seamlessly combines with China Telecom for transparent management of vehicle transportation. With its comprehensive features and reliable performance, this GPS tracker is an excellent choice for businesses and individuals looking to enhance vehicle tracking and management.

Key Features:

- Built-in dual-mode antenna for GPS and mobile positioning
- Integrates GPS global positioning technology and Qualcomm gpsOne mobile positioning technology
- Compact, concealed, sturdy, and reliable design
- High-performance, low-power processors
- Industrial-grade GPS module and CDMA module
- Tracking, orientation, and communication capabilities
- Suitable for taxi services, special vehicles, buses, and long-distance transport vehicles
- Can be used for general vehicle theft prevention
- Seamless integration with China Telecom for transparent vehicle transportation management

- GPS satellite signal channels: 12 channels
- Positioning data format: NEMA 0183 format
- Cold start time: Average 48s

- Positioning accuracy: 2.5M CEP
