# V-SUN - TLT-6C

![TLT-6C](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-6C is a compact vehicle positioning terminal that combines global GPS positioning with mobile positioning technology. Its built in dual mode antenna and industrial grade components are designed to maintain accurate, real time visibility of vehicles in open areas as well as more challenging locations such as high rise or underground parking. The unit is described as sturdy, concealed, and suited for a range of vehicle types, making it a practical option for fleet operators and service providers.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TLT-6C can supply position and status information to Plaspy for centralized monitoring and management. Its design and feature set make it a natural fit for fleet visibility workflows in Plaspy, enabling operators to combine the device level reliability of the TLT-6C with Plaspy reporting, mapping, and operational oversight tools.

## Key Highlights

- Dual mode antenna combining GPS global positioning and mobile positioning for improved coverage
- Industrial grade GPS and CDMA modules for robust field performance
- Compact, concealed form factor suitable for a wide range of vehicle installations
- Designed to provide reliable real time vehicle visibility in open areas and dense or underground environments
- Practical choice for taxi services, buses, long distance transport, and special vehicle fleets
- Built for continuous operation with a focus on low power consumption and durable construction

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TLT-6C provides location updates and status information that Plaspy presents on maps, timelines, and reports. Plaspy ingests the device data to give fleet managers an operational view and historical context for vehicle movement.

- Live location and position history displayed on the Plaspy dashboard for monitoring and analysis
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on motion, location changes, or other event conditions supported by the device
- Fleet level reporting and exportable trip summaries using position data from the TLT-6C
- Geographical oversight for dispatch and scheduling through Plaspy map tools
- Centralized device management and visibility for mixed fleets from within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Taxi fleet tracking and dispatch visibility
- Bus route monitoring and scheduling oversight
- Long distance transport vehicle tracking for operational coordination
- Special vehicle fleets requiring discreet, reliable positioning hardware
- General vehicle theft prevention and recovery visibility

The TLT-6C is a pragmatic choice for organizations that need a compact, durable tracking terminal with dual mode positioning to improve reception in difficult environments.
