# V-SUN - TLT-6C Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the V-SUN TLT-6C vehicle positioning terminal with Plaspy. It outlines the device capabilities that are relevant to location visibility, tracking, and fleet oversight when the TLT-6C is used on the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific limits, firmware updates, and configuration options consult the official V-SUN documentation in addition to your Plaspy deployment documentation.

## Feature Overview

The TLT-6C is presented as a compact, concealed vehicle positioning terminal that combines conventional GPS global positioning with Qualcomm gpsOne mobile positioning and an industrial CDMA cellular module. Its design targets vehicle visibility in both open sky and more challenging environments such as high rise or underground parking areas.

- Dual mode antenna support for GPS and mobile positioning to extend location availability in varied environments
- Integration of GPS global positioning and gpsOne mobile positioning for improved location coverage
- Industrial grade GPS and CDMA modules intended for continuous vehicle tracking
- Compact and concealed form factor suitable for discreet installation in taxis, buses, and transport fleets
- Designed for fleet scheduling, route oversight, and general vehicle theft prevention use
- Described compatibility with China Telecom network environments for regional deployments

## Core Features of V-SUN - TLT-6C

- GPS global positioning using an industrial GPS module
- Qualcomm gpsOne mobile positioning support for assisted location
- Integrated industrial CDMA communication module for cellular connectivity
- Built in dual mode antenna to support both GPS and mobile positioning signals
- Compact and rugged enclosure meant for concealed vehicle installation
- Low power, high performance processor intended for continuous operation
- External antenna interfaces for GPS and CDMA to support installation flexibility
- UIM card support consistent with cellular module requirements

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TLT-6C sends its location and status information into the Plaspy platform so you can monitor vehicles in real time and review historical movement. Plaspy is designed to detect many tracker protocols automatically and present consistent device data in the platform.

- Plaspy receives and normalizes position reports so GPS and mobile positioning data are visible on maps
- Devices like the TLT-6C can be configured to report into Plaspy endpoints such as d.plaspy.com where supported
- Plaspy displays location history and status updates from the tracker for operational oversight
- Combined GPS and gpsOne reports increase visibility in challenging environments and that improved coverage is reflected in Plaspy maps and trails
- External antenna and cellular module behavior influence signal quality and the reporting cadence visible inside Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Taxi and ride service fleet tracking and dispatch visibility
- Bus fleet location monitoring and schedule adherence oversight
- Long distance transport vehicle tracking for route monitoring
- Vehicle scheduling and dispatch coordination for special vehicle fleets
- General vehicle theft prevention and recovery monitoring
- Fleet reporting and historical trip analysis for operations teams

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the V-SUN TLT-6C together with Plaspy provides a practical way to bring vehicle location and movement data into a single monitoring and reporting platform. The combination of GPS and gpsOne mobile positioning in the device helps maintain location visibility where one method alone could be limited, and Plaspy presents that data for map visualization, history review, and operational oversight.
