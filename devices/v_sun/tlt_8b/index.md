# V-SUN - TLT-8B

![TLT-8B](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-8B is a GPS/GSM vehicle tracker that offers a range of features to enhance vehicle security and performance. This tracker supports OBD II protocols, allowing for vehicle examination and diagnostics. With its plug and play installation, there is no need for any circuit diagram changes, making it easy to install and use. The TLT-8B is designed for private cars and other vehicles that have an OBD II port and protocols.

One of the standout features of the TLT-8B is its high-sensitivity GPS chipset, which ensures accurate positioning even in areas with weak signals or limited sky space. This means that you can rely on the tracker to provide accurate location information, no matter where your vehicle is. Additionally, the TLT-8B is equipped with a GSM/GPRS module, supporting GSM900/1800MHz \(850/1900 optional\), making it compatible with networks worldwide.

In terms of functionality, the TLT-8B offers a range of features to enhance vehicle security and safety. These include SOS functionality, an unplug alarm, electronic G-fence, over speed alarm, and historical data upload. The tracker also supports SMS communication and GPRS TCP connectivity, allowing you to easily capture position messages via mobile phone or check the trajectory internally. With its highly reliable electric circuit design and compliance with electronic industry standards, you can trust the TLT-8B to deliver consistent and accurate performance.

Overall, the V-SUN TLT-8B is a versatile and reliable GPS/GSM vehicle tracker that offers a range of features to enhance vehicle security, performance, and safety. With its easy installation and compatibility with OBD II protocols, it is a convenient and effective solution for private car owners and other vehicle users.
