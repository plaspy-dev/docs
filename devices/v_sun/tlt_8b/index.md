# V-SUN - TLT-8B

![TLT-8B](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-8B is a compact GPS/GSM vehicle tracker designed for use in private cars and other vehicles equipped with an OBD II port. It combines a high sensitivity GPS chipset for reliable positioning in challenging signal conditions with a GSM/GPRS communication module for sending location and event data. The device also includes vehicle security and safety features such as SOS, unplug alarm, electronic geo fence, over speed alarm, and historical data upload.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TLT-8B can provide the location, alarm and basic diagnostic information that fleet managers and private owners depend on. Its plug and play design and OBD II compatibility make it straightforward to integrate into vehicle fleets that use Plaspy for centralized tracking, alerting, and historical playback, enabling improved visibility and operational oversight without extensive setup changes.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity GPS chipset for improved positioning in limited sky view areas  
- OBD II support for vehicle examination and basic diagnostic access  
- Plug and play installation that requires no circuit diagram changes for typical OBD II equipped vehicles  
- Built in vehicle security features including SOS, unplug alarm, geo fence and over speed alarm  
- GSM GPRS connectivity for position messaging and data upload  
- Reliable electrical design with compliance to common electronic industry standards

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TLT-8B streams location updates, alarm events, and available OBD II data to the Plaspy platform where it becomes visible to operators and managers. Plaspy organizes incoming messages for real time monitoring and historical review, letting teams act on alerts and analyze vehicle behavior over time.

- Real time location visibility and map tracking for individual vehicles and groups  
- Alert ingestion and notification management for SOS, unplug, geo fence and speed events  
- Historical route playback and data retention to review past trips and events  
- Access to basic vehicle diagnostics and status information passed through the OBD II interface  
- Consolidated reporting and export options to support operational analysis and compliance

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring private cars and light vehicles that have an OBD II port  
- Fleet tracking for small to medium vehicle fleets requiring location and event visibility  
- Security monitoring with SOS and unplug alarm integration for faster response  
- Speed monitoring and geofencing for operational compliance and driver oversight  
- Historical trip review for incident analysis and service planning
