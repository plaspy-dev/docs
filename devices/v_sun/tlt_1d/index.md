# V-SUN - TLT-1D

![TLT-1D](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-1D Remote Control Vehicle GPS Tracker is a compact location device designed for vehicles, motorcycles, electric golf carts, and private cars. It combines GPS positioning with GSM and GPRS communication to provide location information by SMS or over a data connection. The device aims to deliver accurate coordinates and basic trip details even in challenging signal environments such as urban canyons or valleys.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TLT-1D can feed location updates and event information into the Plaspy platform for monitoring and reporting. Whether you prefer position messages via SMS or continuous tracking via GPRS, integrating the tracker with Plaspy helps centralize visibility, historical route playback, and alerting across vehicle fleets and individual assets.

## Key Highlights

- Dual reporting options with SMS position messages and GPRS data for internet based tracking
- Designed to maintain positioning in weaker signal conditions for reliable location reports
- Built in safety and control features including SOS and emergency alarm for quick response
- Event and alarm capabilities such as geo fence, overspeed warning, movement alarm, and power cut alarm
- Two way voice listening and call function for remote communication with the vehicle area
- Support for historical data upload and route playback to review past trips
- Optional remote power or fuel cut off functionality when relay hardware is present

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TLT-1D sends its position and event data to the platform where it can be visualized, analyzed, and acted upon. Plaspy converts incoming messages and data records into map markers, tracks, and alerts so fleet managers and operators can maintain operational oversight.

- Live map visualization of current position and last known location
- Historical track playback for trip reconstruction and mileage review
- Alerting and notifications for geo fence breaches, overspeed, movement, and power events
- Event logs and timestamped records for compliance and operational analysis
- Centralized reporting and filters to monitor multiple TLT-1D devices across a fleet

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small commercial operations and service fleets
- Motorcycle and private vehicle monitoring for security and recovery
- Tracking of electric golf carts and light utility vehicles on campuses or resorts
- Remote monitoring where SMS fallback or intermittent data coverage is required
- Organizations needing basic two way voice or SOS capabilities alongside location tracking

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The V-SUN TLT-1D is a practical choice for fleets and individual owners who need straightforward location tracking with flexible reporting modes. Its mix of SMS and GPRS reporting, combined with a broad set of alarms and remote control features, makes it adaptable to varied operational needs without requiring specialized hardware changes.

Paired with Plaspy, the TLT-1D can provide a complete visibility layer for day to day fleet operations, incident response, and historical analysis.
