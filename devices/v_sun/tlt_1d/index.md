# V-SUN - TLT-1D

![TLT-1D](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN TLT-1D Remote Control Vehicle GPS Tracker is a versatile and reliable location tracking device that utilizes GPS technology and GSM/GPRS technology. With this tracker, you can easily track the location of your vehicle, motorcycle, electric golf cart, or private car. It provides accurate positioning even in areas with weak signals or sight limitations, such as urban areas or valleys. You can access the latitude and longitude of the tracker through cell phone messages, which can be used to locate the tracker on Google Maps or other mapping software. Alternatively, you can receive real-time and historical track data through GPRS, which can be accessed on the internet.

The TLT-1D GPS Tracker offers a wide range of features to enhance your tracking experience. It supports SMS communication or GPRS TCP connection, allowing you to receive position information through cell phone messages or track the device on the internet. It also provides functions such as SOS, fuel cutoff/power failure \(optional\), warning by cutting off external power wire, geo-fence, overspeed warning, historical data upload, parking throttle upload, and moving car tracking timing upload. Additionally, it has a voice call function and is equipped with a highly reliable electric circuit that complies with electronic industry standards.

With the V-SUN TLT-1D Remote Control Vehicle GPS Tracker, you can have peace of mind knowing that you can easily track the location of your vehicle and access important information such as speed, date, time, and longitude/latitude. It also offers advanced features like two-way communication/listening-in function, GSM blind area memory, built-in motion sensor for power saving, emergency alarm, movement alarm, geo-fence function, low battery alarm, towing alarm, speeding alarm, GPS blind area alarm, ACC detection function, power-cut alarm, engine stop function, mileage statistics, power/fuel cut off \(relay required\), and the ability to open and close the door remotely. This tracker is a reliable and convenient solution for all your tracking needs.
