# V-SUN - V-520

![V-520](./logo.jpg)

The V-SUN V-520 is a portable GPS tracker that combines GPS technology with GSM/GPRS technology to provide accurate and real-time positioning. With this device, users can receive latitude and longitude coordinates through cell phone messages and locate the tracker on Google Maps or other digital map software. Alternatively, positioning data can be uploaded to a specified server through GPRS, allowing users to track the device's real-time position and historical movements on the internet.

This versatile tracker is suitable for a variety of applications, including caring for the elderly and children, safeguarding property, and tracking animals. Its built-in high-performance GPS chipset ensures accurate positioning even in areas with weak signals or limited visibility, such as urban areas or valleys. The built-in GSM/GPRS module supports GSM900/1800MHz \(850/1900 MHz\), making it compatible worldwide. Users can communicate with the tracker via SMS or GPRS TCP connection, receiving position information through cell phone messages or accessing movement tracks online. Additionally, the V-520 features an SOS function for emergency situations.

With its highly reliable electric circuit design and compliance with GSM cell phone international standards, the V-SUN V-520 offers a portable, compact, and low power consumption solution for tracking and monitoring needs. Whether you need to keep an eye on loved ones or valuable assets, this GPS tracker provides peace of mind and real-time information at your fingertips.

### Outstanding Features:

- Accurate positioning even in weak signal areas
- Global compatibility with GSM900/1800MHz \(850/1900 MHz\)
- SMS or GPRS TCP connection for communication
- SOS function for emergencies
- Highly reliable electric circuit design
- Portable, compact, and low power consumption
