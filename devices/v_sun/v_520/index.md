# V-SUN - V-520

<div className="device-logo">
![V-520](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The V-SUN V-520 is a portable GPS tracker that combines satellite positioning with GSM GPRS connectivity to provide real time latitude and longitude reporting. It can send position updates via SMS and upload location data to a specified server for online viewing. Designed for mobility, the V-520 is positioned as a compact, low power device suitable for monitoring people, property, and animals, and it includes an SOS function for urgent notifications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the V-520 can be integrated into a centralized tracking platform to present live locations and historical movement on digital maps. Plaspy can receive the tracker data uploaded to an endpoint that the platform supports, allowing organizations to consolidate V-520 feeds alongside other devices for unified visibility, alerting, and reporting. This makes the V-520 a practical option for users who want portable tracking combined with Plaspy monitoring capabilities.

## Key Highlights

- Portable and compact form factor suitable for personal and asset tracking  
- Accurate positioning designed to perform in areas with weaker satellite visibility  
- Dual communication paths through SMS messaging and GPRS server uploads  
- SOS emergency function for immediate attention when needed  
- Global GSM band compatibility for broad regional use  
- Low power consumption with a reliable electronic design intended for continuous use

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the V-SUN V-520 provides position reports that Plaspy can display, log, and use for operational workflows. Plaspy ingests the uploaded location data and presents it alongside other fleet or device information for monitoring and decision making.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps for live monitoring and dispatch awareness  
- Historical route playback and position history for analysis and auditing  
- Alerting and notification handling for SOS events and configurable conditions  
- Consolidated reporting to help review device activity and movement patterns  
- SMS based updates can serve as a backup communication channel when server uploads are not available

## Typical Use Cases

- Caring for elderly family members or children with portable, easy to carry tracking  
- Safeguarding portable property and personal equipment during transit or storage  
- Tracking animals or wildlife when compact trackers are required  
- Monitoring temporary or short term assets without permanent installation  
- Field personnel location awareness for small teams or mobile workers
