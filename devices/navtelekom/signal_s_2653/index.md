# Navtelekom - SIGNAL S-2653

![SIGNAL S-2653](./tracker.jpeg)

The SIGNAL S-2653 from Navtelekom is a professional GLONASS vehicle tracker designed for demanding fleet management and telemetry deployments. Targeted at heavy duty and commercial vehicle installations, the S-2653 combines external GNSS antenna support, resilient cellular connectivity with dual SIM redundancy, onboard logging to removable media, and local access via Bluetooth to maintain continuous visibility and recorded history in challenging conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the SIGNAL S-2653 can feed real time position and vehicle data into Plaspy’s tracking environment. Its combination of continuous reporting, local data backup and a range of vehicle interfaces makes it relevant for fleet operators who want centralized oversight, event driven alerts, and retained historical records for later analysis within Plaspy workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Professional GLONASS GPS vehicle tracker built for heavy duty and commercial applications.
- Plaspy compatible for integration into real time tracking dashboards and telematics reports.
- Dual SIM 2G cellular connectivity for operator redundancy and sustained data transmission.
- 800 mAh backup battery and SD card logging for continued reporting and offline data storage.
- Multiple vehicle interfaces including CAN channels, RS-485, 1 Wire and configurable I O for telemetry and control.
- Robust input protection rated to withstand high voltage transients common in commercial vehicles.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for local configuration and diagnostics plus manufacturer tools and documentation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the SIGNAL S-2653 acts as a robust telematics endpoint sending position, event and vehicle bus data into Plaspy for monitoring and analysis. Onboard logging provides a fallback source of recorded data that Plaspy can reconcile when connectivity is restored, and the device inputs and outputs map to alerting and control workflows within the platform.

- Real time location updates and telemetry visible in Plaspy monitoring views.
- Event driven alerts for inputs such as ignition, doors or alarms routed through Plaspy notifications.
- Historical movement and logged events synchronized from the SD card into Plaspy when the unit reconnects.
- Telemetry and diagnostic data from vehicle interfaces available for Plaspy reporting and dashboards.
- Remote control actions coordinated from Plaspy using the tracker’s configurable outputs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route oversight for commercial vehicle operations.
- Anti theft workflows and remote immobilization as part of security procedures.
- Remote diagnostics and maintenance planning based on vehicle bus data and logged events.
- Fuel monitoring and consumption analysis integrated into fleet cost reports.
- Event driven monitoring with secure onboard logging for forensic review.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SIGNAL S-2653 is a practical option for organizations using Plaspy when they need a rugged, integration ready tracker for heavy duty vehicles. Its dual SIM design and onboard logging help maintain continuity of data flow and preserve critical events, while the range of vehicle interfaces and configurable inputs and outputs extend what can be monitored and controlled through Plaspy workflows.

For fleets and service providers that require persistent visibility, durable electrical protection and a device that can both stream real time data and retain recorded telemetry, the SIGNAL S-2653 pairs naturally with Plaspy to deliver centralized tracking, alerts and reporting without sacrificing local data reliability.
