# Navtelekom - СИГНАЛ S-2654

![СИГНАЛ S-2654](./tracker.jpeg)

The СИГНАЛ S-2654 is a GLONASS vehicle tracker from Navtelekom's SIGNAL product line designed for fleet management and industrial telemetry. According to the product description, the device combines a built-in 3G modem, dual SIM redundancy, local data logging and extensive I/O to support vehicle tracking, telemetry collection and integration with vehicle systems and sensors. Its feature set is aimed at continuous position reporting, buffered logging during connectivity gaps and robust connections to third party equipment in demanding vehicle environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S-2654 can be integrated into Plaspy for real time tracking, reporting and operational oversight. Plaspy can ingest the tracker’s location and telemetry streams to present live maps, alerts and historical playback while reconciling locally logged data when connectivity is restored. Even though the model is listed as archived by the manufacturer, available documentation and configuration tools make the S-2654 a practical option to connect assets into Plaspy workflows for visibility, auditing and anti theft operations.

## Key Highlights

- GLONASS vehicle tracker built for fleet and industrial telemetry with a factory supplied 3G modem and dual SIM redundancy.
- Local microSD logging and internal backup battery support buffered operation during temporary network outages.
- Rich set of inputs and outputs plus serial and bus interfaces to collect telemetry from fuel sensors, controllers and other vehicle systems.
- MODBUS and common serial/CAN style integrations enable third party sensor and controller data to be forwarded to a central platform.
- Robust input power protection and design considerations for vehicle and industrial power environments.
- Manufacturer documentation, NTC Configurator and DRC remote management resources remain available for configuration and maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the SIGNAL S-2654 with Plaspy allows fleets to bring GNSS position updates and collected telemetry into a single monitoring and reporting environment. Plaspy consumes the device data stream to provide situational awareness, event handling and historical records while using locally stored logs to fill gaps after connectivity interruptions.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking, route history and dispatch visibility.
- Universal inputs report ignition, door or alarm states to trigger event based alerts and automated workflows in Plaspy.
- Telemetry from fuel sensors and controllers is forwarded via supported serial or bus interfaces so Plaspy can include consumption and sensor data in reports.
- Configurable outputs enable remote anti theft or immobilization actions and integration with Plaspy driven control sequences.
- Buffered microSD logging provides a local backup that Plaspy can reconcile into reports when network connectivity returns.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for service vehicles, delivery fleets and transport operators.
- Anti theft workflows and remote immobilization for higher value or at risk assets.
- Fuel consumption tracking and engine or equipment telemetry collection for operational cost control.
- Industrial vehicle integration where serial or bus interfaces are needed to gather sensor and controller data.
- Offline logging and reconciliation to maintain trip records during coverage gaps and restore them centrally.
