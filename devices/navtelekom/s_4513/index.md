# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4513

![СМАРТ S-4513](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-4513 is a compact GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker engineered for reliable fleet management and asset monitoring. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the S-4513 delivers real-time tracking, robust telemetry, and flexible I/O so fleet operators and integrators can capture location, fuel and sensor data and feed it directly into Plaspy for dashboarding, alerts, and reports.

The unit combines a 4G modem, dual SIM redundancy, built-in GNSS and GSM antennas, and a rechargeable 380 mAh backup battery to keep the device online through short power interruptions. With industrial power protection \(up to 200 V\) and a wide set of wired and wireless interfaces — including RS-485, 1-Wire, MODBUS, and Bluetooth 4.0 — the SMART S-4513 is suited for real-time tracking, anti-theft workflows, fuel monitoring, temperature-controlled cargo, and remote telemetry when paired with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for seamless integration with Plaspy real-time tracking and fleet management features.
- 4G modem with dual SIM support for redundant cellular connectivity and improved uptime in mixed-network areas.
- Internal rechargeable 380 mAh battery and 200 V power protection to maintain telemetry during short power losses and harsh automotive conditions.
- Rich I/O suite — 4 digital inputs, 1 analog input \(configurable\), 2 control outputs — for telemetry, ignition monitoring, and remote control scenarios.
- RS-485, 1-Wire and MODBUS support enable direct integration of wired peripherals, fuel probes, and industrial sensors.
- Bluetooth 4.0 \(compatible with Bluetooth 5.0 devices\) for connecting wireless sensors such as temperature and fuel level beacons.
- Advanced event logic via Navtelecom Complex Events and remote device management through DRC and NTC Configurator for scalable fleet operations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the SMART S-4513 with Plaspy provides continuous location and telemetry streaming, configurable alerts, and report-ready data. The tracker transmits GNSS positions and sensor telemetry over the 4G cellular link to Plaspy’s ingestion endpoint. Plaspy then applies geofencing, route replay, and event rules to deliver actionable insights for fleet managers and dispatchers.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — position, timestamp, and movement metrics forwarded to Plaspy for live maps and history playback.
- Configurable inputs report ignition, door, or alarm status to Plaspy when wired to the tracker’s digital inputs.
- Fuel monitoring via analog inputs, MODBUS-connected fuel probes, or Bluetooth fuel sensors, with telemetry mapped into Plaspy’s fuel monitoring modules.
- Control outputs can be used to trigger immobilizer or engine-cut actions when integrated and configured through Plaspy alert rules and operator workflows.
- Bluetooth sensors \(temperature, movement, or fuel beacons\) pair with the tracker and forward sensor values to Plaspy for temperature-controlled cargo or asset condition monitoring.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization — monitor location in real time and use control outputs to implement remote immobilizer actions when configured with Plaspy workflows.
- Door and alarm event monitoring — map discrete inputs to door/hood status or alarm lines and create Plaspy alerts for unauthorized access.
- Fuel management optimization — connect fuel probes via analog/MODBUS or Bluetooth fuel sensors to capture consumption telemetry and reduce fuel loss.
- Temperature-controlled cargo — pair Bluetooth temperature sensors to the tracker and stream readings to Plaspy for compliance and exception reporting.
- Remote telemetry and industrial integration — use RS-485, MODBUS, and 1-Wire for wired sensors and actuators in specialized telematics deployments.
