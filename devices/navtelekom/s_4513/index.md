# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4513

![СМАРТ S-4513](./tracker.jpeg)

The СМАРТ S-4513 is a compact GLONASS and GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable fleet management and asset monitoring. It combines cellular connectivity, built in GNSS and GSM antennas, and an internal rechargeable backup battery to maintain telemetry during short power interruptions. The device is positioned for vehicle and asset deployments where continuous location reporting and sensor integration are required.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the S-4513 can transmit real time location and sensor data into Plaspy for dashboarding, alerts, and reports. Its flexible inputs and support for wired and wireless sensors make it a practical choice for fleet operators and integrators who want to feed location, fuel, and condition data directly into Plaspy for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker for direct integration with Plaspy real time tracking and fleet management features
- 4G cellular modem with dual SIM redundancy to improve connectivity in mixed network areas
- Built in GNSS and cellular antennas with an internal rechargeable backup battery for short power outage resilience

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the СМАРТ S-4513 streams GNSS positions and sensor telemetry to Plaspy where that data is used for live maps, history playback, and automated alerts. Plaspy ingests the device data and applies geofencing and reporting logic to produce actionable operational views for dispatchers and fleet managers.

- Live location updates and historical tracks presented in Plaspy maps and timeline views
- Digital input events reported to Plaspy for ignition, door, or alarm monitoring and alerting
- Fuel and sensor telemetry from analog, wired probe interfaces, or Bluetooth beacons fed into Plaspy fuel monitoring and sensor dashboards
- Control outputs can be coordinated with Plaspy rules to support operational actions and workflows
- Remote device management and configuration data can be used together with Plaspy event rules for scalable fleet operations
