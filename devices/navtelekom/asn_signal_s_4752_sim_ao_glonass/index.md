# Navtelekom - ASN SIGNAL S-4752 + SIM AO "GLONASS"

![ASN SIGNAL S-4752 + SIM AO "GLONASS"](./tracker.jpg)

The ASN SIGNAL S-4752 with a preinstalled SIM from AO "GLONASS" is a professional GPS tracker and telematics gateway built for rapid deployment and regulatory compliance. It ships preconfigured for ERA-GLONASS and operator registration, combines GLONASS and GPS positioning with a cellular modem, and provides local logging and multiple vehicle interfaces that suit demanding fleet environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S-4752 is designed to reduce onboarding time for monitoring and reporting workflows. Preconfigured communications, a supplied SIM and documentation simplify connection to Plaspy so fleets and integrators can begin location tracking, telemetry reporting and compliance operations with fewer configuration steps.

## Key Highlights

- Preinstalled AO GLONASS SIM and factory configuration to speed operator registration and initial deployment
- Certified for ERA-GLONASS reporting and approved for M2 M3 and N vehicle categories including hazardous goods transport
- Combined GLONASS and GPS positioning with a cellular modem for continuous location updates
- Wide vehicle interface support including serial ports, CAN and multiple digital and analog I O for sensor and engine data
- Local resilience via microSD based logging and an internal backup battery for data continuity during outages
- Power line protection and rugged design intended for professional vehicle telematics installations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the ASN SIGNAL S-4752 transmits location and telemetry data to Plaspy servers using its cellular link while also retaining locally logged data when connectivity is interrupted. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and makes it available for monitoring, alerts and reporting within fleet dashboards.

- Continuous position updates and time stamped telemetry delivered to Plaspy for real time tracking
- Vehicle bus and serial telemetry forwarded to Plaspy for engine and sensor data visibility
- Local microSD buffering used to preserve data during temporary network loss and forward when connection resumes
- Digital and analog inputs plus controlled outputs available for alarms, ignition detection and external device control monitored through Plaspy
- Complex event logic and MODBUS capable data can be used to trigger Plaspy alerts and customized reports

## Typical Use Cases

- ERA GLONASS regulatory reporting and simplified registration for compliance workflows

- Integration with third party telemetry and monitoring systems for unified operational oversight
