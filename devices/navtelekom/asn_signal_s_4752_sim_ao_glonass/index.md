# Navtelekom - ASN SIGNAL S-4752 + SIM AO "GLONASS"

<div className="device-logo">
![ASN SIGNAL S-4752 + SIM AO "GLONASS"](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The ASN SIGNAL S-4752 with a pre installed SIM from AO "GLONASS" is a professional grade GPS tracker and telematics gateway designed for fast deployment and regulatory compliance. It is supplied preconfigured for ERA GLONASS and AO "GLONASS" operator workflows and combines GLONASS and GPS positioning with an integrated cellular modem, broad I O and serial/CAN connectivity, local data logging and protected power inputs to meet vehicle telematics needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S-4752 speeds onboarding and simplifies registration with operator portals so fleets and integrators can begin real time tracking and telemetry reporting shortly after installation. Its factory configuration and included SIM reduce setup overhead while the unit's vehicle certifications and interface options make it a practical option for compliance driven fleet monitoring inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Preconfigured for AO "GLONASS" with supplied SIM to simplify operator registration and reduce time to first use
- Certified for ERA GLONASS reporting and approved for M2 M3 and N vehicle categories for compliance sensitive deployments
- Combined GLONASS and GPS positioning with built in cellular modem for continuous location tracking
- Extensive I O and serial/CAN interfaces to integrate vehicle sensors and auxiliary systems
- Local microSD logging and internal backup battery for data resilience during connectivity interruptions
- Electrical protection on power inputs to improve device survivability in vehicle environments
- Advanced event logic and MODBUS support for custom alarms and telemetry processing

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is streamlined by the S-4752's factory configuration and open telemetry capabilities. The device gathers GNSS position and vehicle data, buffers logs locally when required, and transmits telemetry via the onboard cellular link to configured server endpoints so Plaspy can present location, status and events in real time.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered over the cellular link to Plaspy
- Vehicle bus and sensor telemetry via CAN and serial interfaces for richer monitoring in Plaspy dashboards
- Local microSD logging provides buffered data during outages and smooths reporting when connectivity is restored
- Digital and analog inputs plus controlled outputs enable alarms, immobilizer control and external sensor integration visible in Plaspy
- Complex Events and MODBUS support allow custom rules and event delivery for automated alerts and reporting within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for buses trucks and commercial vehicles
- ERA GLONASS regulatory reporting and operator registration for compliant fleets

- Cargo and asset continuity with local logging during temporary network loss
- Integration with third party telemetry systems and dashboards through serial CAN and MODBUS interfaces
