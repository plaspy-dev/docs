# Navtelekom - СИГНАЛ S-2117

![СИГНАЛ S-2117](./tracker.jpeg)

The SIGNAL S-2117 is a robust vehicle GNSS tracker designed for fleet operations that require reliable location and telemetry data. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this GSM-based tracker with a GLONASS receiver delivers continuous vehicle monitoring, alarm event reporting, and remote control capabilities — all essential for real-time tracking, fleet management, and anti-theft workflows.

The S-2117 emphasizes practical telematics: support for multiple fuel sensors via RS-485, 1-Wire temperature and identification inputs, alarm and tamper monitoring, and two-way loud communication for driver-dispatch voice links. Although the model is discontinued by the manufacturer, it remains fully usable with vendor firmware and configuration tools and integrates cleanly into Plaspy to provide actionable telemetry, fuel monitoring, ignition and alarm insights across your vehicle fleet.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GNSS tracker for dependable real-time tracking and fleet management.
- GSM communication with GLONASS receiver for accurate vehicle positioning and route history.
- RS-485 support for up to three digital fuel level sensors — tailored fuel monitoring and consumption analytics.
- 1-Wire interface for digital temperature probes and iButton driver identification — ideal for refrigerated cargo and personnel workflows.
- Two-way loud voice communication and panic button for driver-dispatch voice links and emergency response.
- Alarm inputs and tamper detection to support anti-theft alerts and security events.
- Remote control outputs for switching external loads and actuators, enabling peripheral control and integrator-driven immobilizer solutions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the SIGNAL S-2117 streams location, sensor telemetry, and event data into Plaspy’s dashboards and reporting tools. Plaspy ingests the device’s GSM/GLONASS messages and translates alarm inputs, fuel sensor readings, and temperature telemetry into live map markers, alerts, and historical reports. This enables centralized operational oversight across mixed fleets that rely on both position and rich vehicle telemetry.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: position, speed, and route history via GLONASS/GSM.
- Alarm and tamper event reporting: vendor-provided alarm inputs are forwarded to Plaspy for instant alerts.
- Fuel monitoring: RS-485-connected digital fuel level sensors supply continuous fuel level and consumption metrics.
- Temperature and identification telemetry: 1-Wire digital temperature sensors and iButton driver IDs map cargo conditions and driver events in Plaspy.
- Remote control and voice: external actuator switching and two-way loud communication are integrated as actionable controls and event triggers in Plaspy workflows.
