# Navtelekom - СИГНАЛ S-2117

![СИГНАЛ S-2117](./tracker.jpeg)

The СИГНАЛ S-2117 is a robust vehicle GNSS tracker from Navtelekom designed for fleet operations that need continuous location and telemetry data. It is a GSM based device with a GLONASS receiver that provides persistent vehicle monitoring, alarm event reporting, and remote control features commonly required for real time tracking, fleet management, and anti theft workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the S-2117 can stream position and telemetry into Plaspy for centralized visibility. Its practical telematics support for multiple fuel sensors, temperature probes, driver identification, alarm monitoring, and two way loud communication makes it a relevant option where combined position and operational telemetry are important for fleet oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GNSS tracker for dependable real time tracking and fleet management
- GSM communication with GLONASS receiver for continuous vehicle positioning and route history
- RS 485 support for up to three digital fuel level sensors to enable fuel monitoring workflows
- 1 Wire interface for digital temperature probes and driver identification inputs
- Two way loud voice communication and panic button for driver dispatch links and emergency alerts
- Alarm inputs and tamper detection to support anti theft notifications
- Remote control outputs for switching external loads and actuator control

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the СИГНАЛ S-2117 sends location updates and sensor telemetry that Plaspy ingests and presents in dashboards, maps, and reports. Plaspy maps the device events and sensor readings into alerts and historical logs so fleet managers can track operations and respond to incidents from a single platform.

- Real time location and route history visible on Plaspy maps for operational oversight
- Alarm and tamper events forwarded to Plaspy for instant notifications and event tracking
- Fuel telemetry from RS 485 connected digital sensors used in fuel level and consumption reports
- Temperature and driver identification telemetry from 1 Wire inputs included in cargo and driver logs
- Remote control actions and two way voice usage presented as actionable controls and event triggers in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Continuous fleet tracking for cars trucks and service vehicles with route history and event logs
- Fuel monitoring and consumption analytics using multiple digital fuel level sensors
- Refrigerated cargo monitoring with temperature probes feeding alerts and temperature histories
- Driver identification and access logging using iButton style IDs and 1 Wire inputs
- Emergency response and driver dispatch through panic button and two way voice links
