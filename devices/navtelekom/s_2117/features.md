## Feature Overview

The СИГНАЛ S-2117 is a vehicle GNSS tracker designed for fleet operations that require consistent position reporting and practical telemetry. It combines GLONASS positioning with GSM communication and a set of vehicle-oriented interfaces to deliver location, sensor readings, and event reporting suitable for fleet management and security workflows.

- Real time position reporting and route history for vehicle tracking and operational oversight.
- RS-485 support for multiple digital fuel level sensors to monitor fuel state and consumption trends.
- 1-Wire interface for digital temperature probes and iButton driver identification to support refrigerated cargo and driver logging.
- Alarm inputs and tamper detection for anti-theft alerts and security event reporting.
- Two-way loud voice communication and a panic button for direct driver dispatch and incident response.
