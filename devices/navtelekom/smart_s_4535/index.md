# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4535

![СМАРТ S-4535](./tracker.jpeg)

The СМАРТ S-4535 is a vehicle grade GPS tracker from Navtelekom designed for modern fleet management and vehicle telemetry. It combines sensitive GLONASS and GPS positioning with 4G connectivity and dual SIM support to provide continuous location reporting and resilient communications. The device includes an internal backup battery and a set of vehicle interfaces that enable direct telemetry collection and event reporting for commercial fleets, logistics, and security transport applications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the СМАРТ S-4535 can forward location, telemetry and event data to Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its on device event logic and remote management capabilities simplify configuration at scale, while multi interface support lets operators collect vehicle level signals that Plaspy can present in dashboards, alerts and reports for daily fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker with combined GLONASS and GPS positioning for reliable location data.
- 4G modem with dual SIM redundancy and built in antennas to reduce communication gaps for fleet use.
- Internal 800 mAh backup battery that preserves position and event reporting during ignition off or temporary power loss.
- Vehicle level interfaces including CAN bus plus RS 232 RS 485 and 1 Wire for rich telemetry and sensor integration.
- On device Complex Events logic and DRC remote control support for scalable configuration and local event handling.
- Multiple I O channels with three universal inputs and two configurable control outputs to support status monitoring and remote actions.
- Bluetooth support and robust power input protections suited for demanding vehicle environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the СМАРТ S-4535 streams location updates telemetry and event messages to the Plaspy platform where data is visualized and archived for operational use. Plaspy consumes the device payloads and maps vehicle signals into monitoring views, alert rules and historical reports so fleet teams can act on timely information.

- Real time location and route playback visible on Plaspy live maps for operational monitoring.
- Telemetry and CAN derived signals forwarded to Plaspy for analysis such as fuel monitoring and condition tracking.
- Digital input and ignition status delivered as events to trigger alerts and automate workflows in Plaspy.
- On device event rules reduce false positives by handling common conditions locally before sending critical alerts to Plaspy.
- Configurable outputs can be coordinated with Plaspy alerts and operator actions for rapid response.
- Sensor and accessory data collected locally can be included in Plaspy reports for compliance and operational review.
