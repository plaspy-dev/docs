# Navtelekom - SIGNAL S-2651

<div className="device-logo">
![SIGNAL S-2651](./tracker.jpeg)
</div>

The SIGNAL S-2651 from Navtelekom is a compact GPS tracker aimed at professional fleet management, telematics and asset monitoring. It combines GLONASS and GPS multi constellation positioning with a 2G cellular modem and dual SIM support to provide dependable real time location and telemetry for commercial vehicles, special equipment and stationary assets. The device is engineered for integration with multiple vehicle and industrial interfaces and includes local logging and short range wireless capabilities for on site configuration.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S-2651 can feed continuous position updates, event reports and diagnostic information into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and analysis. Compatibility makes it a practical option for fleet operators who need straightforward device management, resilient connectivity and offline logging that syncs with Plaspy workflows for tracking, alerts and historical reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with GLONASS and GPS positioning for reliable real time tracking and route history.
- Dual SIM 2G cellular connectivity to help maintain telemetry transmission in variable coverage environments.
- Multiple vehicle and industrial interfaces plus configurable inputs and switched outputs for flexible event monitoring and control.
- Local SD card logging up to 32 GB for offline data retention and post trip analysis when network coverage is intermittent.
- Built in rechargeable backup battery and power line protection to preserve logging and support graceful power fail behavior.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for short range device configuration and integration with compatible local sensors.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SIGNAL S-2651 streams location, input events and available sensor telemetry to the platform for consolidated visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy uses these inputs to present live locations, trigger alerts and compile historical reports that help with fleet performance and asset security.

- Real time location updates from the S-2651 enable immediate situational awareness on the Plaspy map.
- Configurable inputs report ignition, door and other events that Plaspy can use for geofence alerts and driver behavior monitoring.
- Sensor and telemetry data from vehicle interfaces and Bluetooth sensors feed Plaspy reports for fuel awareness and operational analysis.
- Local SD logging preserves data during coverage gaps and allows Plaspy to reconcile missing intervals once connectivity is restored.
- Switched outputs can be tied into Plaspy workflows to support remote control actions and event driven responses.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking and route history for trucks, vans and service vehicles.
- Anti theft workflows and remote immobilization integrated into Plaspy alerting and recovery procedures.
- Telematics and diagnostics for vehicles and equipment using serial and CAN style interfaces for sensor data collection.
- Stationary asset monitoring where SD logging and external antennas support periodic uploads and reliable record keeping.
- Short range sensor and environmental monitoring using Bluetooth enabled devices for cargo or condition checks.
