# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-2435

![СМАРТ S-2435](./tracker.jpeg)

The СМАРТ S-2435 is a high functionality GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker designed for demanding telematics deployments. It combines a high sensitivity GNSS receiver with built in GSM antennas, dual SIM 2G modem connectivity, an internal rechargeable backup battery and a broad set of inputs and outputs to collect location and telemetry from commercial vehicles. The device is engineered to keep reporting in challenging vehicle electrical environments and supports local wireless connections and serial interfaces for expanded sensor and control options.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the S-2435 feeds position and telemetry into Plaspy for real time visibility, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy can ingest the device data and expose it through dashboards, rule based alerts and reports so fleets and operators can turn raw device signals into operational insights. The combination of the S-2435 hardware and Plaspy platform makes the model suitable for fleet tracking, cargo monitoring and workflows that require reliable telemetry and event driven responses.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into existing tracking dashboards and alerting workflows
- High sensitivity GLONASS/GPS receiver with built in GSM antennas for dependable positioning in mixed environments
- Dual SIM 2G modem for carrier redundancy and continuous cellular connectivity
- Internal rechargeable backup battery to maintain reporting through power interruptions for anti theft scenarios
- Extensive I O including serial and bus interfaces plus configurable inputs and outputs for broad telemetry and control
- Bluetooth 4.0 support for local sensor connections and configuration
- Robust electrical protection and vehicle grade design to withstand tough electrical environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the СМАРТ S-2435 delivers GNSS position, timestamps and telemetry to Plaspy over its cellular uplink. Plaspy processes incoming data to provide live maps, historical playback, alerts and scheduled reports. Device inputs and outputs can trigger events in Plaspy and platform rules can translate those events into automated notifications or control actions.

- Real time location updates and telemetry visible on Plaspy maps and live views
- Event driven alerts in Plaspy based on device inputs, sensor triggers or configurable conditions
- Historical route playback and reporting for trip analysis and compliance review
- Sensor and telemetry values exposed in Plaspy reports and dashboards for operational oversight
- Configurable outputs and rules enable remote control workflows and incident response automation
- Bluetooth sensor data and local inputs can be bridged into Plaspy for monitoring and alerts

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for buses, vans and light trucks requiring continuous location and telemetry
- Cargo monitoring where backup battery operation preserves tracking during tamper or power cut events
- Anti theft workflows using event detection and continuous reporting to support rapid response
- Industrial mobile equipment monitoring via serial and bus interfaces for operational telemetry
- Sensor based monitoring such as temperature or door status using local wireless sensors bridged to Plaspy
