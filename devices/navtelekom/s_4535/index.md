# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4535

![СМАРТ S-4535](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-4535 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for modern fleet management and vehicle telemetry. Built around sensitive built-in GLONASS/GPS and GSM antennas and a 4G modem with dual SIM support, the SMART S-4535 delivers reliable real-time tracking and redundant communications for commercial fleets, logistics and security transport. An internal 800 mAh backup battery preserves position and event reporting during ignition-off or temporary power loss.

Engineered for integration, the SMART S-4535 supports vehicle CAN bus connectivity plus RS-232, RS-485 and 1-Wire interfaces, enabling rich telemetry and fuel monitoring without extra gateways. Onboard logic \(Navtelecom’s Complex Events technology\) and remote management through the DRC remote control system make the device easy to configure and operate at fleet scale while remaining Plaspy compatible for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with GLONASS/GPS positioning and 4G connectivity for dependable real-time tracking across wide coverage areas.
- Dual SIM 4G modem and built-in antennas provide redundant communication channels to reduce data outages for fleet management applications.
- Internal 800 mAh backup battery maintains telemetry and location updates during ignition-off or transient power interruptions.
- Direct CAN bus support plus RS-232, RS-485 and 1-Wire interfaces enable rich vehicle telemetry, fuel monitoring and sensor integration without additional hardware.
- Three universal inputs and two configurable control outputs allow status monitoring and remote actions; outputs are commonly used by installers to implement immobilizer or relay-based anti-theft functions.
- On-device Complex Events logic creates customizable alarms, geofence actions and event rules to reduce server-side load and generate timely alerts.
- Bluetooth 4.0 support for local configuration and connection to Bluetooth sensors for temperature, cargo monitoring or accessory pairing.
- Robust hardware protections including power input protection and input line protection up to 200 V for harsh vehicle environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SMART S-4535 integrates with Plaspy to deliver continuous, actionable vehicle data. Using its 4G modem and dual SIM redundancy, the device transmits location, CAN-derived telemetry and digital input events to Plaspy’s platform for visualization, live tracking and historical reports. Plaspy reads the device’s telemetry payloads and maps CAN and sensor channels into dashboards, alerts and exportable logs.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over 4G to Plaspy for live maps and route playback.
- Vehicle CAN data and fuel monitoring streamed to Plaspy for fuel consumption analysis and driver scoring.
- Ignition and digital input status reported via the universal inputs for engine-on/off events, door or alarm monitoring.
- Configurable control outputs available to trigger remote actions; installers commonly use these outputs in anti-theft workflows such as external immobilizer integration.
- Bluetooth sensors supported locally for temperature, cargo or proximity monitoring; sensor data can be forwarded to Plaspy when required.
