# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4535

![СМАРТ S-4535](./tracker.jpeg)

The СМАРТ S-4535 from Navtelekom is a vehicle GPS tracker designed for modern fleet management and vehicle telemetry. It combines GLONASS and GPS positioning with a 4G modem and dual SIM capability, built around integrated GNSS and GSM antennas. The device includes an internal backup battery for continued reporting during temporary power loss and supports a range of vehicle interfaces and on‑device logic to collect and prefilter telemetry at the source.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the СМАРТ S-4535 can feed location, event and telemetry data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its redundant communication design, onboard event logic and remote management features make it suitable for fleets that need reliable live tracking, historical playback and operational oversight through Plaspy’s platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with GLONASS and GPS positioning plus 4G connectivity for reliable real‑time tracking.
- Dual SIM 4G modem and built in antennas for redundant communications and improved uptime in variable coverage areas.
- Internal backup battery preserves position and event reporting during ignition off or temporary power interruptions.
- Direct vehicle telemetry support including CAN bus plus RS-232, RS-485 and 1‑Wire interfaces for richer data collection.
- Onboard Complex Events logic enables local rule processing and customizable event generation to reduce unnecessary server load.
- Remote management through Navtelekom tools for configuration and maintenance at fleet scale.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The СМАРТ S-4535 transmits location and telemetry to Plaspy where that data is visualized, analyzed and used to trigger alerts. Plaspy ingests the device payloads and maps reported channels into dashboards, reports and notification rules so operators have continuous visibility and historical context for each vehicle.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps with route playback for trip reconstruction.
- Vehicle telemetry and CAN derived signals are presented in Plaspy dashboards for fuel and performance review.
- Digital inputs and ignition status reported by the device generate events and alerts inside Plaspy for operational monitoring.
- Onboard event rules reduce noise by filtering and delivering only relevant alarms to Plaspy.
- Configurable outputs can be represented in workflows to support remote actions tied to Plaspy alerts.
- Sensor data collected locally, including Bluetooth sensors where used, can be forwarded to Plaspy for consolidated reporting.
