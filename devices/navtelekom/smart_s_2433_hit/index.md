# Navtelekom - SMART S-2433 HIT

<div className="device-logo">
![SMART S-2433 HIT](./tracker.jpeg)
</div>

The SMART S-2433 HIT from Navtelekom is a compact professional grade GPS tracker designed for 2G telematics in fleet and industrial vehicle deployments. It combines GLONASS and GPS positioning with internal GNSS and GSM antennas and a single SIM 2G modem to provide continuous position and status reporting. The device includes a built in backup battery and hardened electrical protection, making it suitable for demanding vehicle electrical environments.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the SMART S-2433 HIT can feed location, status and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and fleet management. Its flexible input and output options, serial interfaces, MODBUS support, Bluetooth 4.0 and accelerometer features make it a practical choice where remote telemetry, event logging and anti theft workflows are required alongside Plaspy reporting and alerting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 2G GPS tracker built for fleet and industrial vehicle use
- Integrated GLONASS and GPS positioning with internal GNSS and GSM antennas
- Built in 800 mAh backup battery to sustain reporting during power interruptions
- High overvoltage protection for device and inputs to tolerate harsh electrical conditions
- Multiple I O options including universal inputs, control outputs and serial ports for sensors and actuators
- Bluetooth 4.0 for local configuration and accessory pairing
- Accelerometer enabled features for event detection and basic driving analysis

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the SMART S-2433 HIT delivers GNSS coordinates, IO state and telemetry to the Plaspy platform where data is visualized, logged and turned into operational insight. Plaspy ingests the device messages and makes them available for live maps, alerts, and historical reports to support fleet oversight.

- Live position updates and historical playback on Plaspy maps for route review and monitoring
- Event and status reporting from universal inputs to trigger configurable alerts in Plaspy
- Telemetry and analog or pulse inputs used to capture metrics such as fuel or meter readings for reporting
- Control outputs used within Plaspy workflows for remote actions like immobilization or actuator control
- Bluetooth paired accessories and local sensor data integrated into Plaspy for richer context around events

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and management for passenger vehicles and light commercial fleets
- Anti theft workflows including event driven alerts and remote immobilizer control
- Crash detection and safety monitoring using accelerometer events reported to dispatch
- Fuel and consumption monitoring via pulse or analog sensor integration for cost control
- Industrial vehicle and asset deployments in environments where electrical protection is essential

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SMART S-2433 HIT is a good fit for organizations that need a durable, professional grade tracker that can be integrated into a centralized fleet platform like Plaspy. Its combination of GNSS positioning, backup power and robust electrical tolerance reduces the likelihood of data gaps in challenging vehicle environments, while diverse inputs and serial interfaces support a range of telemetry and control scenarios.

Paired with Plaspy, the device helps consolidate location, event and sensor data into one operational view so teams can act on alerts, generate reports and manage daily fleet operations more effectively.
