# Navtelekom - SMART S-2433 HIT

![SMART S-2433 HIT](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-2433 HIT from Navtelekom is a compact professional grade GPS tracker designed for reliable 2G telematics in fleet and industrial vehicle deployments. It combines GLONASS and GPS positioning with internal GNSS and GSM antennas and a single SIM 2G modem to provide continuous position and status reporting for real time tracking and telemetry. The device also includes robust electrical protection and a built in backup battery to help maintain operation in challenging vehicle environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SMART S-2433 HIT can feed Plaspy with live GNSS coordinates, input states and telemetry so operators gain centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its flexible inputs and outputs, serial interfaces and MODBUS support make the unit adaptable to common fleet workflows such as event driven alerts, anti theft measures and telemetry reporting, while Bluetooth and onboard sensors extend local data collection possibilities for integration into Plaspy dashboards.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for centralized fleet tracking and monitoring
- Integrated GLONASS and GPS with internal GNSS and GSM antennas for reliable positioning
- Built in 800 mAh backup battery to maintain operation during power interruptions
- Robust electrical protection with high overvoltage tolerance for harsh vehicle systems
- Versatile I O set including universal inputs, control outputs and serial interfaces
- Bluetooth 4.0 and MODBUS support for local accessories and industrial integration
- Built in accelerometer for Eco Driving analysis and crash detection features

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the SMART S-2433 HIT is used with Plaspy, the device transmits position, status and event data to the Plaspy platform where it is visualized and logged for real time monitoring and reporting. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates together with I O and sensor reports so you can create alerts, review trip history and produce operational reports.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear on Plaspy maps with historical playback for route review
- Configurable inputs report ignition, door and alarm status to Plaspy for event based alerts and notifications
- Pulse and analog sensor inputs can feed fuel and consumption data into Plaspy reporting and dashboards
- Control outputs enable remote actions such as immobilization when coordinated through Plaspy workflows
- Bluetooth paired accessories and local sensor data extend Plaspy visibility for temperature, proximity and movement events

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for passenger cars and light commercial vehicles with real time tracking and mileage logging
- Anti theft workflows using event alerts and remote control outputs for immobilization
- Crash detection and safety monitoring using accelerometer events and immediate telemetry reporting
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry via pulse or analog sensor integration for consumption analysis
- Industrial asset tracking in vehicles and equipment with demanding electrical conditions
