# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4513

![СМАРТ S-4513](./tracker.jpeg)

The СМАРТ S-4513 from Navtelekom is a compact GLONASS and GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable fleet management and asset monitoring. Built for continuous operation in vehicle environments, the S-4513 combines cellular connectivity, onboard GNSS, backup power, and a range of wired and wireless interfaces to capture location and telemetry for moving assets and fixed equipment.

This model is Plaspy compatible out of the box, enabling direct integration with Plaspy for live tracking, dashboarding, alerts, and reporting. Fleet operators and integrators can route position and sensor data from the S-4513 into Plaspy to support operational oversight, fuel monitoring, anti-theft workflows, and temperature sensitive cargo monitoring without additional gateway hardware.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device for straightforward integration into Plaspy fleet monitoring and reporting workflows.
- 4G modem with dual SIM redundancy to help maintain cellular connectivity across varying coverage areas.
- Built-in GNSS and cellular antennas plus an internal rechargeable backup battery for continued telemetry during short power interruptions.
- Industrial power protection designed for automotive environments to increase resilience against voltage spikes.
- Rich I/O options including digital and analog inputs, control outputs, RS-485, 1-Wire, and MODBUS for wired sensor integration.
- Bluetooth 4.0 support for pairing wireless sensors such as temperature and fuel beacons.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the СМАРТ S-4513 streams GNSS positions and sensor telemetry so fleet managers can view live locations, playback history, and receive event-driven alerts. Plaspy ingests the device data and applies mapping, rules, and reporting so teams can act on tracked events and operational exceptions.

- Real-time location updates and history playback for vehicle tracking and route review in Plaspy.
- Discrete inputs report status changes such as ignition or door events into Plaspy alerting and logs.
- Fuel and sensor telemetry from analog or MODBUS devices and Bluetooth probes mapped into Plaspy fuel and sensor modules.
- Control outputs available to be triggered from Plaspy rules and workflows for remote actions where supported.
- Event data and telemetry feed Plaspy reports and dashboards for operational KPIs and compliance monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization workflows combining live tracking with control outputs for response.
- Door, hood, and alarm monitoring with discrete input alerts routed to dispatch or operations teams.
- Fuel monitoring using analog, MODBUS, or Bluetooth fuel sensors to track consumption and detect losses.
- Temperature controlled cargo monitoring by pairing Bluetooth temperature sensors and streaming readings to Plaspy.
- Remote telemetry and industrial sensor integration for specialized vehicles and mobile equipment.
