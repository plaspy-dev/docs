# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4513 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Navtelekom СМАРТ S-4513 and describes how its capabilities are used when paired with Plaspy for fleet tracking and telemetry. It focuses on user facing functionality and practical integration points rather than device configuration specifics.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installed option set, and how the unit is wired or mounted. For the most current device specific details consult Navtelekom documentation and confirm firmware capabilities for your installed units.

## Feature Overview

The СМАРТ S-4513 is a compact GLONASS GPS vehicle tracker intended for real time fleet management and asset monitoring. In practice it combines cellular redundancy, GNSS positioning, power resilience, and a broad I O set to deliver actionable location and sensor data into Plaspy.

- Real time GNSS position streaming for live maps and historical playback in Plaspy
- Dual SIM 4G cellular modem for improved connectivity and uptime across networks
- Backup battery and industrial power protection to maintain telemetry during brief power interruptions
- Rich wired and wireless interfaces including RS 485, 1 Wire, MODBUS and Bluetooth for sensor integration
- Configurable digital and analog inputs plus control outputs for status monitoring and basic remote actions

## Core Features of Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-4513

- GLONASS and GPS positioning for reliable location reporting
- 4G modem with dual SIM redundancy for cellular failover
- Internal rechargeable backup battery rated at 380 mAh to preserve connectivity through short power losses
- Industrial power protection up to 200 V for automotive environments
- I O suite including 4 digital inputs, 1 configurable analog input, and 2 control outputs
- Wired peripheral support via RS 485, 1 Wire and MODBUS for fuel probes and industrial sensors
- Bluetooth 4.0 for pairing wireless beacons and sensors
- On device event logic and remote management options provided by Navtelekom tools

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the S-4513 streams locations and sensor telemetry into the Plaspy platform where operators can monitor, alert, and report on vehicle activity. Plaspy receives and normalizes incoming data so fleet teams can combine location, inputs and sensor readings into operational views.

- Real time location and movement updates display on Plaspy maps and are available for history playback
- Discrete inputs report ignition, door or alarm status into Plaspy for alerting and event timelines
- Analog, MODBUS or Bluetooth sensor readings such as fuel level and temperature are mapped into Plaspy telemetry modules
- Control outputs can be reflected in Plaspy workflows for defined remote actions when combined with suitable hardware and operator rules
- On device event logic reduces chatter by sending only relevant events to Plaspy based on configured thresholds and rules

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet tracking and route replay for dispatch and operational oversight
- Anti theft monitoring and remote immobilization workflows when combined with control outputs and Plaspy alerts
- Fuel monitoring using analog inputs, MODBUS fuel probes or Bluetooth fuel sensors integrated into Plaspy reports
- Temperature controlled cargo monitoring by pairing Bluetooth temperature sensors and streaming readings to Plaspy
- Door, alarm and ignition monitoring for security and compliance reporting
- Remote telemetry and industrial sensor integration for specialized vehicle or asset installations
