# Navtelekom - SMART S-2411

![SMART S-2411](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-2411 by Navtelecom is a compact GLONASS/GPS vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable Plaspy compatible deployments. With integrated GNSS and GSM antennas, a 2G modem and an internal backup battery, the S-2411 delivers consistent real-time tracking and essential telemetry for fleet management, mobile asset monitoring, and anti-theft workflows without requiring external antenna installations in many installations.

Built for straightforward integration with platforms like Plaspy, the SMART S-2411 provides location, sensor inputs, and event-driven reporting to help fleets and operators maintain visibility and respond quickly to incidents. Its Bluetooth 4.0 local configurator support and documented firmware update pathway through Navtelecom’s remote management make it a practical choice where dependable telemetry, ignition/door state monitoring, and short-term power resilience are required.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Integrated GLONASS/GPS and GSM antennas reduce installation complexity and improves aesthetics.
- 2G GSM modem with single-SIM support for basic cellular telemetry and location reporting.
- Internal rechargeable 380 mAh backup battery for emergency reporting during power loss.
- Robust power protection up to 200 V for vehicle electrical surge resilience.
- Multiple inputs/outputs: 4 discrete digital inputs, 1 analog \(configurable\) input, and 1 switching control output for sensors and actuators.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for local configuration and accessory pairing using the NTC Configurator.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SMART S-2411 streams position fixes and telemetry over the 2G cellular network to Plaspy servers where data is processed for live maps, historical playback and reporting. Plaspy reads and interprets the S-2411’s digital and analog inputs, enabling event rules, alerts and telemetry dashboards for fleet managers. Local configuration via Bluetooth speeds initial setup and field maintenance without requiring a wired connection.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over 2G to Plaspy for live tracking and geofencing.
- Ignition and door state monitoring via discrete digital inputs for driver behavior and security alerts.
- Analog sensor telemetry \(e.g., fuel or level sensors\) reported to Plaspy when the analog input is used.
- Remote control of actuators and immobilizer-style functions can be implemented through the switching control output where permitted.
- Bluetooth 4.0 enables local configuration with the NTC Configurator and pairing with Bluetooth sensors for on-site adjustments.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real-time tracking, route replay and basic telemetry reporting for small-to-medium vehicle fleets.
- Anti-theft and security: ignition/door state monitoring with event alerts and remote actuator control for immobilization workflows where applicable.
- Fuel monitoring and analog telemetry: support for analog sensor inputs to feed fuel-level or other analog telemetry into Plaspy reports.
- Remote control and alarm response: switching output used to operate relays or actuators for remote interventions and alarm handling.
- Mobile asset tracking: compact form factor and integrated antennas make the S-2411 suitable for vans, trucks and other mobile equipment.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SMART S-2411 is a practical, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that balances simplicity with essential vehicle telemetry. Its integrated GNSS/GSM antennas reduce installation time and cost, while the 380 mAh backup battery and 200 V power protection improve resilience against brief power interruptions and electrical surges. For operators focused on straightforward fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and analog sensor telemetry, the S-2411 offers proven data points \(location, discrete inputs, analog readings\) that feed directly into Plaspy’s dashboards, alerts and reporting tools.

Navtelecom’s continued availability of documentation, firmware and remote management through the DRC system, together with local Bluetooth configuration via the NTC Configurator, makes the SMART S-2411 a trustworthy option where long-term support and compatibility with Plaspy’s real-time tracking and telemetry features are required. Choose the S-2411 when you need compact, durable GPS tracking with the core inputs and outputs required for fleet management, fuel monitoring, ignition/door state detection, and basic remote control functions.
