# Navtelekom - СИГНАЛ S-4753

![СИГНАЛ S-4753](./tracker.jpeg)

The СИГНАЛ S-4753 from Navtelekom is a professional GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker designed for demanding fleet management and telematics deployments. It combines cellular connectivity with dual SIM redundancy, dual CAN interfaces, on‑board data logging and a range of vehicle focused I/O to support continuous position reporting and extended telemetry capture in transport environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S-4753 can feed location data and vehicle signals directly into Plaspy’s fleet management platform. That compatibility enables operators to consolidate live tracking, historical logs and event data from the device into Plaspy dashboards for monitoring, alerting and operational reporting without custom low level integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward integration into fleet dashboards and tracking workflows.
- Dual CAN interfaces to capture vehicle telemetry from multiple CAN networks for richer analytics.
- 4G modem with dual SIM and external antenna support to improve connectivity and GNSS reception in mobile conditions.
- On‑board logging via SD card plus an internal backup battery to preserve data during temporary outages.
- Rich I/O set including universal inputs, controlled outputs, RS‑485, 1‑Wire and Bluetooth for flexible sensor and event integration.
- Vehicle oriented protection and remote management features suitable for professional fleet installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the СИГНАЛ S-4753 supplies GNSS positions and time stamped telemetry alongside I/O events and logged records, enabling unified visibility of vehicles and their behaviour. Plaspy processes position streams and telemetry to produce maps, alerts and reports that help fleet teams monitor operations and react to incidents.

- Real time location updates and historical replay for route monitoring and trip analysis.
- CAN telemetry mapping into meaningful metrics for fleet analytics and maintenance planning.
- Event detection from universal inputs and controlled outputs to trigger alerts and workflow actions in Plaspy.
- Use of on‑board SD logging and internal backup power to ensure data continuity and later upload to Plaspy for audit or reconstruction.
- Bluetooth and peripheral interfaces feed short range sensor data and additional events into Plaspy for cargo and trailer monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet tracking and route oversight for transport and service vehicles.
- Anti‑theft and immobilization workflows using controlled outputs together with Plaspy alerts and location history.
- Detailed vehicle telemetry collection for fuel monitoring and preventive maintenance programs.
- On‑board data logging for compliance, incident investigation and long term record keeping.
- Cargo and trailer monitoring using Bluetooth peripherals and external sensor integration.
