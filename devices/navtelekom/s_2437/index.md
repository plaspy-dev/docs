# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-2437

![СМАРТ S-2437](./tracker.jpeg)

The Navtelekom СМАРТ S-2437 is a compact GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable deployment in fleet management and commercial telematics projects. It combines a high-sensitivity GNSS receiver and integrated GSM antennas with an internal rechargeable backup battery, dual SIM cellular connectivity and Bluetooth for local configuration. The device includes multiple vehicle interfaces and industrial communication options intended to support a wide range of telemetry and monitoring scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the СМАРТ S-2437 can stream location and vehicle data into Plaspy for mapping, alerts and reporting. Its combination of persistent connectivity, backup power and vehicle bus access makes it suitable for continuous tracking, basic diagnostics and event reporting inside the Plaspy platform, helping fleets and service providers maintain operational visibility and respond to incidents.

## Key Highlights

- Compact vehicle tracker with GLONASS and GPS positioning for reliable real-time location.
- Dual SIM 2G cellular modem and integrated GSM antennas to maintain connectivity in variable coverage.
- Internal 800 mAh rechargeable battery that provides backup operation during vehicle power interruptions.
- Two CAN interfaces and broad I/O for deeper vehicle telemetry and integration with vehicle systems.
- Multiple industrial communication options, including RS-485, 1-Wire and MODBUS for third party sensors.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for on-site configuration and parameter tuning with local tools.
- Remote device and firmware management support via DRC to assist fleet scale provisioning.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the СМАРТ S-2437 forwards GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry so operators can view live locations, review historical routes and generate operational reports. Plaspy ingests time-stamped data and event notifications from the tracker to build dashboards, trigger alerts and produce scheduled reports that support fleet oversight.

- Real-time GNSS location updates and history for live tracking and route replay in Plaspy.
- CAN bus telemetry feeds for aggregated vehicle data that can be used in fuel and performance reports.
- Event and alarm forwarding from universal inputs and programmable outputs to drive notifications and workflows.
- Remote control options surfaced through Plaspy where configured, enabling coordination of responses to alerts.
- Onsite Bluetooth configuration and DRC remote management for device provisioning and over the air maintenance.
