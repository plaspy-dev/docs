# Navtelekom - СМАРТ S-2437

![СМАРТ S-2437](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-2437 is a compact GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable, Plaspy compatible deployment in fleet management and commercial telematics projects. Built with high-sensitivity GLONASS/GPS and integrated GSM antennas, the S-2437 delivers continuous real-time tracking even during vehicle power interruptions thanks to its internal 800 mAh rechargeable battery. Dual SIM 2G connectivity and Bluetooth 4.0 simplify onsite configuration and help maintain persistent links for telemetry and location data.

Optimized for robust vehicle integration, the SMART S-2437 includes two CAN interfaces for primary and secondary bus access, comprehensive I/O for alarms and control, and industrial communication options such as RS-485, 1-Wire, and MODBUS. With power-line protections rated for harsh automotive electrical environments and remote firmware/device management via the DRC system, this GPS tracker is a practical Plaspy compatible choice for fleets, anti-theft setups, fuel monitoring and advanced telemetry projects.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with GLONASS/GPS positioning for dependable real-time tracking across regions.
- Dual SIM 2G modem and integrated GSM antennas for resilient cellular connectivity and uninterrupted telemetry.
- Two CAN interfaces enable deep vehicle diagnostics, engine and fuel monitoring, and integration with ECU data.
- Internal 800 mAh rechargeable battery provides backup operation through power interruptions and helps support anti-theft workflows.
- Rich I/O and industrial protocols — three universal inputs, two programmable outputs, RS-485, 1-Wire and MODBUS — for sensor integration and remote control.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for quick local configuration with mobile tools and on-site parameter tuning via NTC Configurator.
- Robust electrical protection \(power protection up to 500 V, input protection up to 350 V\) suitable for challenging vehicle environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SMART S-2437 streams location and vehicle telemetry into the Plaspy platform for mapping, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy ingests GNSS position fixes and time-stamped CAN telemetry, correlates sensor inputs and I/O events, and exposes that data in dashboards and automated reports for fleet management and anti-theft monitoring.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GLONASS/GPS fixes are forwarded to Plaspy for live tracking and historical route analysis.
- CAN bus diagnostics — primary and secondary CAN interfaces provide engine, speed, and fuel-related telemetry to Plaspy for fuel monitoring and performance reports.
- Events and alarms — universal inputs and programmable outputs relay door, alarm or sensor events to Plaspy to trigger notifications or automated workflows.
- Remote control — programmable outputs can be configured for immobilizer or ignition control where local regulations and vehicle setup permit, enabling Plaspy-driven anti-theft actions.
- Local setup and OTA management — Bluetooth 4.0 supports onsite configuration; DRC remote management handles firmware updates and fleet-scale device provisioning for Plaspy-managed deployments.
