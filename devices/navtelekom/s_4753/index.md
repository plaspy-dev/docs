# Navtelekom - СИГНАЛ S-4753

![СИГНАЛ S-4753](./tracker.jpeg)

The СИГНАЛ S-4753 is a professional GLONASS and GPS vehicle tracker from Navtelekom engineered for demanding fleet management and telematics projects. Built with resilient hardware and focused on continuous data capture, the S-4753 combines 4G cellular connectivity with dual SIM redundancy, dual CAN interfaces, on-board SD logging and an internal backup battery to support uninterrupted position and vehicle data collection in transport environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S-4753 integrates with Plaspy’s fleet management platform to deliver real time tracking, history logging and event generation. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker supplies GNSS positions, CAN telemetry and I/O events to support monitoring, alerts and reporting workflows used by fleet operators, service providers and integrators.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward integration into fleet dashboards and monitoring.
- Dual CAN interfaces for capturing vehicle telemetry from multiple CAN buses.
- 4G modem with dual SIM and external antenna support for improved connectivity and reception.
- On-board logging via SD card plus an internal backup battery for data continuity during interruptions.
- Broad I/O and communications including universal inputs, controlled outputs, RS-485, 1-Wire and Bluetooth 4.0 to extend telemetry.
- Remote management support to simplify configuration and fleet scale deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the СИГНАЛ S-4753 delivers position fixes and time stamped vehicle data to Plaspy’s platform, where that information is visualized, stored and processed for operational oversight. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions and telemetry streams, correlates CAN signals and I/O events, and makes that information available for live monitoring and historical analysis.

- Live location updates and route monitoring from GLONASS and GPS positions for fleet visibility.
- CAN telemetry mapped to meaningful vehicle metrics and fed into Plaspy reports and dashboards.
- On-board SD logging and internal battery keep local records that can be retrieved or reconciled with Plaspy history after connectivity gaps.
- Universal inputs and controlled outputs generate events in Plaspy for ignition, door and alarm status and can be used in remote workflows.
- Bluetooth and auxiliary interfaces let short range sensor events and external device data appear in Plaspy as additional telemetry.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization workflows with event driven outputs and monitoring.
- Detailed vehicle telemetry and operational analytics using dual CAN data for maintenance and fuel oversight.
- Compliance and incident reconstruction with on-board SD logging and historical exports.
- Trailer and cargo monitoring with Bluetooth sensors and short range peripherals feeding Plaspy alerts.
- Integration of third party sensors and industrial devices via RS-485, 1-Wire and MODBUS for expanded telemetry.
