# SIGNAL S-4753

The SIGNAL S-4753 is a professional GLONASS/GPS vehicle GPS tracker built for demanding fleet management and telematics projects. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the S-4753 brings reliable 4G connectivity, dual SIM redundancy, dual CAN-bus access and on-board data logging to enable continuous real-time tracking and deep vehicle telemetry when integrated with Plaspy’s platform.

Designed for fleet operators, service providers and integrators, the S-4753 combines robust hardware — including an 800 mAh Li‑Po backup battery, external antenna support and SD card logging — with flexible I/O \(universal inputs, controlled outputs, RS‑485, 1‑Wire and Bluetooth 4.0\). Use it with Plaspy to capture location, CAN telemetry, sensor events and long-term logs for reporting, anti-theft workflows and operational analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: straightforward integration for real-time tracking and fleet management dashboards.
- Dual CAN interfaces: simultaneous connection to engine and comfort CAN for comprehensive telemetry capture.
- 4G modem with dual SIM and external antenna support for improved cellular and GNSS reception.
- On-board logging: SD card support \(up to 32 GB\) plus an 800 mAh Li‑Po backup battery for data continuity.
- Rich I/O and communications: six universal inputs, four controlled outputs, RS‑485, 1‑Wire, MODBUS and Bluetooth 4.0.
- Protected for vehicle installations: input and power line protection up to 200 V for transport environments.
- Remote firmware and configuration via Navtelecom’s DRC remote management system and NTC Configurator.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SIGNAL S-4753 delivers position and vehicle data to Plaspy using standard telematics integration paths. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions and time-stamped telemetry, maps CAN-bus signals to meaningful metrics and raises events for I/O changes. Together they enable live monitoring, historical replay, alerts and reporting for fleet managers.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates from GLONASS/GPS for live tracking and route monitoring.
- Dual CAN interfaces provide engine and comfort CAN data such as speed, RPM and other vehicle telemetry for fleet analytics and fuel monitoring workflows.
- SD card logging \(up to 32 GB\) and internal backup battery ensure continuous data capture during network interruptions.
- Universal inputs and controlled outputs allow Plaspy to register ignition, door and alarm events and to trigger remote control actions \(for example, controlled outputs used in immobilizer workflows when configured\).
- Bluetooth 4.0 support enables integration of Bluetooth sensors and beacons for trailer/cargo monitoring and short-range telemetry.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization workflows — track vehicles in real time and use controlled outputs or remote procedures for secure shutdowns when configured with Plaspy.
- Detailed vehicle telemetry and fuel monitoring — leverage dual CAN interfaces to capture engine and comfort CAN data for fuel consumption analysis and maintenance planning.
- On-board data logging for compliance and diagnostics — SD card recording plus telemetry exports help with audits, incident reconstruction and driver behavior analysis.
- Bluetooth sensors for cargo and trailer monitoring — connect BLE sensors to monitor temperature, door status or proximity and feed events to Plaspy alerts.
- Integration with external sensors and industrial buses — RS‑485, 1‑Wire and MODBUS expand telemetry to specialized sensors and third-party devices.
