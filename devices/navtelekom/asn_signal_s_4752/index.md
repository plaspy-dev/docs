# ASN SIGNAL S-4752

The ASN SIGNAL S-4752 is a certified vehicle GPS tracker built for reliable telematics and regulatory compliance. Designed specifically for fleet management, hazardous goods transport and industrial vehicle monitoring, the S-4752 provides GLONASS/GPS positioning, LTE Cat.4 cellular connectivity and robust I/O for telemetry, making it fully Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking, reporting and control.

The S-4752 combines rugged automotive protections, onboard logging and dual-SIM cellular redundancy to ensure continuous data flow for Plaspy-enabled platforms. With ERA-GLONASS registration options and approvals for M2, M3 and N class vehicles carrying dangerous goods, this tracker is optimized for operators who need compliant anti-theft measures, remote diagnostics and integrated telemetry in a single device.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with GLONASS/GPS positioning for accurate real-time tracking of vehicles and assets.
- LTE Cat.4 modem \(backward compatible with 2G/3G\) and dual SIM support for resilient cellular connectivity and uninterrupted fleet management data.
- Comprehensive I/O: six universal digital/analog inputs and four programmable outputs for ignition monitoring, alarms and control tasks.
- Onboard SD logging \(up to 32 GB\) and a 1000 mAh Li‑Po backup battery to maintain graceful shutdown and preserve data during power loss.
- Multiple vehicle interfaces \(RS-232, RS-485, CAN, 1-Wire\) plus MODBUS and Complex Events logic for flexible telemetry and industrial integrations.
- Certified to TR CU 018/2011 and approved for use on vehicles transporting dangerous goods under Russian regulations — ideal for compliant transport management.
- Power-line protection with input protection up to 200 V to withstand automotive electrical transients and support long-term reliability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the ASN SIGNAL S-4752 delivers continuous location, telemetry and event data streams to power fleet dashboards, geofencing, compliance reports and anti-theft workflows. Plaspy ingests the unit’s GNSS fixes and vehicle telemetry via cellular links and presents that data in real time for dispatchers, compliance officers and service teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GLONASS/GPS fixes transmitted over LTE for responsive tracking and history playback in Plaspy.
- Ignition, door and alarm status via universal digital/analog inputs or CAN messages, enabling event-driven alerts and automated rules.
- Fuel monitoring and engine telemetry when connected to vehicle CAN or analog fuel sensors, allowing Plaspy to include fuel consumption and range analytics.
- Remote immobilizer and control functions can be implemented using the programmable outputs to support anti-theft responses and remote shutdown through Plaspy workflows.
- Onboard SD logging ensures no data loss during temporary connectivity outages; Plaspy can reconcile logs and live data for complete trail reconstruction.
- Works within Plaspy ecosystems that may include Bluetooth sensors or other gateways — the device’s serial/CAN interfaces let integrators bring external sensor data into Plaspy where supported.
