The СМАРТ S-2430 START from Navtelekom is a compact GLONASS and GPS vehicle tracker engineered for reliable fleet management and automotive telematics. - Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with integrated GLONASS and GPS positioning for reliable location updates.

- Bluetooth 4.0 support for auxiliary sensor and accessory connectivity.
- Advanced telematics features aimed at vehicle safety and operations such as EcoDriving metrics, crash and tow detection, and jamming alerts.
- Durable IP54 enclosure designed for mixed vehicle installations.
