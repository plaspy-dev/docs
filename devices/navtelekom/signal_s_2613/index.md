# Navtelekom - СИГНАЛ S-2613

![СИГНАЛ S-2613](./tracker.jpeg)

The СИГНАЛ S-2613 from Navtelekom is a compact vehicle and asset GPS tracker designed to provide core telematics functions for professional use. As an entry level member of the SIGNAL family, it offers stable GLONASS and GPS positioning, a 2G GSM modem, a range of digital and analog inputs and outputs, RS‑485 connectivity, Bluetooth, external antennas, and an integrated backup battery. These features make it suitable for tracking, basic telemetry and event capture in automotive and industrial environments.

This model is compatible with Plaspy and can be integrated to feed location and telemetry into fleet dashboards, alert systems and reporting workflows. When paired with Plaspy, the S-2613 provides the kind of continuous location visibility and input event reporting that fleet managers and asset owners rely on for operational oversight, anti theft monitoring and routine reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device for direct ingestion of tracking data and event alerts.
- Combined GLONASS and GPS positioning with external antenna support for reliable location fixes.
- 2G GSM modem with single SIM slot suitable where GSM coverage is available.
- Robust power handling with protection up to 200 V and an integrated backup battery for continuity during power interruptions.
- Multiple I O options and RS‑485 interface for integrating external sensors and controllers.
- Bluetooth for local sensor pairing and an integrated hands free speakerphone for voice interaction.
- Compact and rugged form factor designed for vehicle and asset installations in demanding conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the СИГНАЛ S-2613 streams position and device telemetry into the platform, enabling live tracking, event driven alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy captures location coordinates, timestamps and input events so operators can monitor movement and respond to incidents from a centralized interface.

- Live location and telemetry visualization on Plaspy dashboards for real time fleet oversight.
- Event reporting from digital inputs to trigger alarms and automated workflows.
- Historical route and activity reports for analysis and compliance purposes.
- Remote control of outputs through Plaspy enabled workflows to support vehicle interventions or signaling.
- Integration of external sensor data via analog inputs and RS‑485 to extend telemetry available in reports and alerts.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light and medium vehicles requiring continuous location and route history.
- Cargo and asset monitoring where backup battery and external antennas maintain visibility during power loss.
- Anti theft and alarm monitoring using discrete inputs and output control integrated into alert workflows.
- Integration with external sensors for fuel level, temperature or other operational telemetry via analog inputs or RS‑485.
- Local sensor pairing and driver communication using Bluetooth and the hands free speakerphone.
