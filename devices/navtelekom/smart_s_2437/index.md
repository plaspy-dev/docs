The СМАРТ S-2437 from Navtelekom is a compact GLONASS and GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable operation in fleet management and commercial telematics projects. It combines high sensitivity GNSS positioning with integrated GSM antennas, a built in rechargeable backup battery, dual SIM connectivity and Bluetooth for local configuration. The device also offers multiple vehicle integration interfaces and industrial communication options, making it suitable for a broad range of mobile asset monitoring needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the S-2437 can deliver continuous location and telemetry into the Plaspy platform for mapping, alerts and reporting. Its combination of persistent connectivity, backup power and a variety of I O and bus interfaces makes it a practical choice for organizations that need consolidated visibility, event driven alerts and remote device management within Plaspy managed deployments.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity GLONASS and GPS positioning for dependable location tracking across regions.
- Dual SIM 2G modem with integrated GSM antennas to support resilient cellular connectivity.
- Built in 800 mAh rechargeable battery that provides backup operation during vehicle power interruptions.
- Two CAN interfaces plus broad I O and industrial protocol support for extended vehicle telemetry and sensor integration.
- Bluetooth 4.0 for quick onsite configuration using mobile tools and local parameter tuning.
- Remote device and firmware management via the DRC system for fleet scale provisioning and updates.
- Robust power protections designed for challenging automotive electrical environments.

- Real time location updates and historical route replay for fleet visibility and auditing.
- CAN bus telemetry forwarded to Plaspy to support engine, speed and fuel related analysis where vehicle data is available.

- Remote control and output actions can be coordinated through Plaspy driven workflows where vehicle setup and local regulations allow.
- Onsite Bluetooth configuration and DRC remote management simplify device provisioning and over the air updates for Plaspy managed fleets.
