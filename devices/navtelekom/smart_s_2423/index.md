# Navtelekom - SMART S-2423

![SMART S-2423](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-2423 is a compact GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable telematics and real-time tracking. It combines an integrated GSM modem with internal high-sensitivity GLONASS/GPS and GSM antennas and includes a built-in rechargeable backup battery to maintain reporting during temporary power loss. The device offers multiple physical interfaces and configurable inputs and outputs to support common vehicle telemetry and control needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SMART S-2423 can stream position and telemetry into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring, alerts, and reporting. Its configuration and remote management capabilities via NTC Configurator and the DRC system make it straightforward to include in managed deployments and mixed fleets monitored through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Fully Plaspy compatible GLONASS/GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet integration.
- Integrated 2G GSM modem with internal antennas and single SIM support for cellular connectivity.
- Compact vehicle form factor with a built-in rechargeable backup battery for continued reporting during power interruptions.
- Multiple physical interfaces including RS-485, 1-Wire and Bluetooth 4.0 for flexible peripheral and sensor connections.
- Three universal inputs and two configurable outputs for monitoring ignition door and alarm states and for remote control actions.
- Power and input protection designed for vehicle electrical environments to improve durability.
- Configuration documentation and remote management support via NTC Configurator and DRC for firmware and diagnostics.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the SMART S-2423 sends location fixes and telemetry to the platform where data is presented on maps, dashboards and in reports. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions and pairs them with inputs, outputs and sensor data from the device so fleet managers can monitor status and respond to events.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates available in Plaspy for maps, geofences and route history.
- Universal inputs report ignition door and alarm status that can trigger Plaspy alerts and automation rules.
- Telemetry and diagnostics from RS-485 and 1-Wire interfaces can be used in Plaspy reports and maintenance workflows.
- Configurable outputs support remote control scenarios such as immobilizer or lock actions coordinated through Plaspy policies.
- Bluetooth 4.0 enables wireless sensor and beacon connections that Plaspy can reflect in monitoring and event logic.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live vehicle location, route playback and operational telemetry for daily operations.
- Anti-theft monitoring and remote immobilization or lock control tied to event rules in Plaspy.
- Remote diagnostics and preventative maintenance using telemetry from serial and 1-Wire interfaces.
- Cargo and environment monitoring by pairing Bluetooth sensors for temperature proximity or status reporting.
- General vehicle and mobile asset tracking where compact form and backup power improve uptime.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SMART S-2423 is a practical choice for organizations that need dependable position reporting and flexible telemetry in a compact vehicle tracker. Its combination of GLONASS/GPS positioning, integrated cellular modem and multiple I O options offers a straightforward path to gather location and status data without adding intermediary hardware. Built-in backup power and electrical protection help maintain uptime in vehicle environments, and remote configuration plus firmware management tools reduce maintenance overhead for large deployments.
