# Navtelekom - SMART S-2423

![SMART S-2423](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-2423 is a compact GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker designed for reliable telematics and real-time tracking. With an integrated GSM modem, internal high‑sensitivity GLONASS/GPS and GSM antennas, and a built‑in rechargeable backup battery, the S-2423 is optimized for fleet management and asset monitoring and is fully Plaspy compatible for easy integration into existing monitoring platforms.

Engineered for practical deployment, the SMART S-2423 supports a single SIM on 2G cellular networks and offers multiple physical interfaces — RS-485, 1‑Wire and Bluetooth 4.0 — plus universal inputs and configurable outputs for alarms, locks and control elements. Full documentation, NTC Configurator support and DRC remote management make configuration, firmware management and remote diagnostics straightforward when used with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GLONASS/GPS tracker for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Integrated 2G GSM modem with internal antennas for simple single‑SIM connectivity and robust communications.
- Compact vehicle form factor with an internal rechargeable 380 mAh backup battery to sustain tracking during power loss.
- Multiple interfaces — RS-485, 1‑Wire and Bluetooth 4.0 — for flexible telemetry, sensors and peripheral connections.
- 3 universal inputs and 2 configurable outputs enable ignition/door/alarm state monitoring and control of immobilizer or lock actuators when configured.
- Power and input protection up to 200 V for durable operation in harsh automotive electrical environments.
- Documentation and remote management via NTC Configurator and the DRC system for firmware updates and version control.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the SMART S-2423 streams position and telemetry data to the platform for live fleet monitoring, alerting and reporting. Plaspy ingests location fixes from the device’s GLONASS/GPS receiver and pairs those with telemetry and sensor readings supplied via RS-485, 1‑Wire or Bluetooth. Configuration through NTC Configurator and firmware tracking via DRC ensures the tracker maintains compatibility and receives updates in managed deployments.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for maps, geofences and historical routes.
- Ignition, door and alarm status can be reported through the 3 universal inputs and reflected in Plaspy alerts and dashboards.
- Sensor and diagnostics data available from RS-485 and 1‑Wire interfaces for advanced telemetry and remote diagnostics.
- Configurable outputs can be used for remote immobilizer or lock control when integrated with Plaspy controls and policies.
- Bluetooth 4.0 support enables wireless connection to Bluetooth sensors and beacons for temperature, proximity or movement monitoring.
