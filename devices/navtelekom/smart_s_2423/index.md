# Navtelekom - SMART S-2423

![SMART S-2423](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-2423 is a compact vehicle tracker from Navtelekom that combines GLONASS and GPS positioning with an integrated GSM modem and internal antennas. Designed for reliable telematics and real-time tracking, the device includes a built in rechargeable backup battery and a compact form factor aimed at fleet management and asset monitoring applications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SMART S-2423 can be integrated into existing monitoring platforms to provide live location, telemetry and event reporting. Configuration and remote management tools provided by the manufacturer help maintain device settings and software versions, making the tracker a practical option for organizations that want to add tracked assets into Plaspy for operational visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for real time fleet tracking and telemetry aggregation.  
- Integrated GLONASS and GPS positioning with internal antennas for dependable location fixes.  
- Built in rechargeable backup battery to sustain reporting during temporary power interruptions.  
- Single SIM 2G GSM modem for straightforward cellular connectivity and communications.  
- Multiple physical interfaces including RS-485, 1-Wire and Bluetooth 4.0 for flexible telemetry and peripherals.  
- Universal inputs and configurable outputs to report and act on ignition, door, alarm and control events.  
- Support tools such as NTC Configurator and DRC remote management simplify configuration and device maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the SMART S-2423 streams position and telemetry to the platform, where it is used for live monitoring, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests location fixes together with sensor and input states to give fleet managers a consolidated operational view.

- Live location updates and route history visible on Plaspy maps and dashboards.  
- Event and status reporting from universal inputs reflected as alerts and activity logs.  
- Telemetry from RS-485 and 1-Wire interfaces available for diagnostics and sensor monitoring.  
- Configurable outputs can be activated via rules or controls in Plaspy for immobilization or access control workflows.  
- Bluetooth 4.0 enables connection to compatible wireless sensors and beacons whose data can be associated with tracked assets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, route replay and operational oversight.  
- Anti theft and immobilization control tied to event driven rules in a monitoring platform.  
- Remote diagnostics and preventative maintenance using serial and sensor interfaces.  
- Temperature or proximity monitoring through paired Bluetooth sensors for cargo oversight.  
- General vehicle and mobile asset tracking where compact form and backup power are beneficial.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SMART S-2423 offers a practical balance of positioning, connectivity and I O flexibility that suits mixed fleet deployments and asset monitoring programs. Its integrated modem, internal antennas and backup battery reduce the need for additional hardware, while multiple interfaces let organizations collect a range of telemetry without adding intermediaries.

Pairing the S-2423 with Plaspy brings centralized visibility and rule based management for fleets and assets. NTC Configurator and DRC remote management support help administrators keep devices configured consistently and track firmware lifecycle in larger deployments, which complements Plaspy operational workflows and reporting.
