# Navtelekom - SMART S-2412

![SMART S-2412](./tracker.webp)

The SMART S-2412 is a compact vehicle tracker from Navtelekom designed for fixed installation in commercial and fleet vehicles. It integrates GLONASS and GPS positioning with built-in GSM antennas to deliver continuous location updates and telemetry over cellular networks. The device also supports both wired and wireless fuel level sensors and wireless temperature sensors, making it suitable for applications that require cargo condition monitoring and fuel oversight without external antenna hardware.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SMART S-2412 can feed position fixes, sensor telemetry, and I/O events into the Plaspy platform for centralized fleet monitoring and reporting. Its vehicle-grade interfaces and local wireless sensor connectivity allow Plaspy to present location, fuel and temperature data, and device state in dashboards, alerts, and historical reports—helping fleet operators gain operational visibility and respond to events efficiently.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker with integrated GLONASS/GPS and GSM antennas for straightforward fixed installation and reliable positioning.
- Supports both wired and wireless fuel level sensors for fuel monitoring and consumption analysis.
- Wireless temperature sensor support plus Bluetooth 4.0 for cargo condition monitoring in refrigerated transport.
- Vehicle interfaces including multiple I/O and RS-485 for integration with third party equipment and event detection.
- Power protection up to 200 V to tolerate typical vehicle electrical conditions and improve uptime.
- Compact form factor for concealed installation in commercial vehicles while maintaining full telemetry capability.
- Backed by Navtelekom configuration tools and remote management for simplified setup and firmware maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the SMART S-2412 sends GNSS positions, sensor readings, and I/O events to the platform so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in real time, investigate history, and configure operational alerts. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and exposes the most relevant information through maps, reports, and notification workflows.

- Real-time location and history visualization for route tracking and playback in Plaspy dashboards.
- Sensor telemetry reporting so fuel level and temperature readings appear in reports and can trigger alerts.
- Digital input and output event handling to detect ignition, door, or alarm states and automate responses in Plaspy.
- Remote control support to operate outputs for immobilization or other field actions as part of Plaspy workflows.
- Geofence and alerting capabilities that use tracker events to notify teams of deviations or critical conditions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route oversight for commercial vehicle operations requiring persistent location visibility.
- Fuel monitoring and anti-fraud control using wired or wireless fuel sensors integrated into telemetry reports.
- Temperature sensitive transport such as refrigerated loads where wireless temperature sensors report cargo condition.
- Anti-theft workflows that combine digital inputs and control outputs to support remote immobilization and alerts.
- Integrator deployments that leverage RS-485 and multiple I/O channels to connect on-board equipment and third party sensors.
