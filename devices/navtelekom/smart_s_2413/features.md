# Navtelekom - SMART S-2413 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Navtelekom SMART S-2413 with Plaspy. It highlights the device capabilities that are relevant to fleet managers, integrators, and installers who want to deploy the tracker with Plaspy for real time location, telemetry, and event monitoring. The content focuses on practical, non sensitive descriptions of what the device can provide when integrated with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. Where applicable, consult the official Navtelekom documentation and device passport for the most current and detailed device specifications before planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The SMART S-2413 is a compact vehicle tracker designed for professional fleet and asset monitoring. It combines GNSS positioning, a cellular modem, flexible I O, and both wired and wireless sensor options to deliver consistent position reporting and telemetry streams that Plaspy can ingest for dashboards, reports, and alerts.

- Built in GLONASS and GPS reception for reliable location reporting in vehicle environments
- Internal GSM modem with a single SIM slot for cellular data and real time streaming to Plaspy
- Flexible telemetry channels including digital inputs, a configurable analog input, a controllable output, and RS 485 serial for peripherals
- Bluetooth 4.0 support to pair wireless sensors for fuel or temperature monitoring without additional wiring
- Internal rechargeable backup battery to maintain temporary operation during power interruptions
- Vehicle grade power protection to handle automotive electrical transients during installations

## Core Features of Navtelekom - SMART S-2413

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for standard telematics streams and platform integration
- GLONASS and GPS satellite reception with internal antennas for compact installs
- Internal GSM modem with single SIM slot for cellular connectivity
- Two digital inputs for status events and one configurable analog input for sensor telemetry
- One controllable output for remote actuation from a management platform
- RS 485 serial interface to connect wired peripherals and custom sensors
- Bluetooth 4.0 for wireless sensor support and beacon style integrations
- Internal rechargeable backup battery and vehicle grade power protection for resilient operation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the SMART S-2413 is connected and reporting, Plaspy consumes its standard telematics streams so location and telemetry appear in the platform with minimal configuration. Plaspy maps the device inputs and sensor channels to platform functions such as live tracking, history, and event generation so operators can monitor vehicles and assets centrally.

- Live location updates and route history appear in Plaspy dashboards from the device position reports
- Digital inputs and the analog input translate into status events in Plaspy for ignition, door open, or alarm conditions
- Bluetooth and wired sensors reported by the device supply telemetry that Plaspy can use for fuel and temperature monitoring workflows
- The controllable output can be driven from Plaspy managed commands when the device and deployment support remote actuation
- RS 485 connected peripherals can forward custom telemetry into Plaspy compatible streams for consolidated visibility
