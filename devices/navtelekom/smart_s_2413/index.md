# Navtelekom - SMART S-2413

![SMART S-2413](./tracker.jpeg)

The SMART S-2413 is a compact GLONASS/GPS vehicle tracker designed for professional fleet management and asset monitoring. It combines sensitive GNSS reception with an internal cellular modem and vehicle grade power protection to provide consistent position reporting and resilient operation in automotive environments. The device includes a mix of digital and analog inputs, an RS-485 interface, Bluetooth 4.0 support, and an internal backup battery for temporary operation during power interruptions.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the SMART S-2413 can feed standard telematics streams into Plaspy for live tracking, alerts, and reporting. Its flexible telemetry channels and options for local and remote configuration make it a practical choice for fleets, integrators, and installers who want to add reliable position and sensor data to Plaspy without reworking existing monitoring workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration with dashboards and alerts
- Combined GLONASS and GPS reception for reliable position reporting
- Internal cellular modem with single SIM slot for mobile connectivity
- Internal rechargeable backup battery for short term operation during power loss
- Vehicle grade power protection to withstand electrical transients
- Flexible I O and serial connectivity including digital inputs, an analog channel, a controllable output, and RS-485
- Bluetooth 4.0 support for connecting wireless sensors and beacons

## How It Works with Plaspy

When a SMART S-2413 is deployed with Plaspy, the device streams location and telemetry over the cellular network so Plaspy can present near real time visibility, route history, and event driven alerts. Plaspy ingests the tracker output and maps inputs, outputs, and serial data to platform features for consolidated fleet oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry updates feed Plaspy live tracking dashboards
- Digital inputs and the configurable analog input generate status events for ignition, doors, or alarms visible in Plaspy
- Fuel and temperature values reported from wired sensors or Bluetooth sensors are available for consumption reports and anomaly detection
- Controllable output can be used for remote actuations such as immobilizer control through Plaspy managed commands
- RS-485 serial data from peripherals can be forwarded into Plaspy for custom telemetry and advanced monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Centralized fleet management and route monitoring for commercial vehicle fleets
- Anti theft and immobilizer workflows using inputs and remote control outputs
- Fuel monitoring and consumption tracking with wired or Bluetooth sensors
- Temperature controlled transport with wireless sensors and serial peripherals for telemetry
- Specialized vehicle telemetry for construction or service fleets using RS-485 sensors

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SMART S-2413 is suited to organizations that need a compact, integration friendly tracker with both wired and wireless sensor support. Its combination of internal GNSS reception, cellular connectivity, and robust power protection helps reduce installation complexity and ongoing maintenance for vehicle fleets. The device's flexible inputs and serial interface make it adaptable to common telemetry needs without requiring multiple specialized units.

For teams using Plaspy, the SMART S-2413 offers predictable telematics output that the platform can consume for live tracking, reporting, and alerting.
