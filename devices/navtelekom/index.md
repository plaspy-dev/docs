# Navtelekom

Navtelekom is a manufacturer of GPS tracking and telematics devices used in vehicle and asset tracking. This page explains how Navtelekom devices integrate with Plaspy, what to expect from these trackers in general terms, and how to find the right device model for your monitoring needs. Use the catalog below to jump directly to compatible models and learn more about integrating them with the Plaspy platform.

## How Navtelekom devices work with Plaspy

Navtelekom trackers typically collect GPS location and basic telemetry data and transmit it over mobile networks. Plaspy can receive and process this data when a device is configured to send messages to the platform. On Plaspy you can view live locations, playback history, and receive status updates from connected trackers.

If you plan to connect a Navtelekom device to Plaspy, the usual steps are:
- Confirm the device is listed in the Plaspy device catalog on this page.
- Configure the device connection settings (server address and data format) according to the device documentation.
- Add the device to your Plaspy account so it appears on maps and reports.

For detailed setup steps please consult the device documentation and follow the connection instructions on the specific device page in the catalog.

## Common features found in Navtelekom devices

While features vary by model, many Navtelekom trackers share several common capabilities useful for fleet and asset management:
- GPS location reporting for live tracking and history
- Mobile network connectivity for remote reporting
- Basic telemetry inputs like ignition and movement detection
- Configurable reporting intervals for location and status
- Support for geo alerts and movement alerts at the platform level

## Typical use cases for Navtelekom GPS trackers

Navtelekom devices are commonly deployed for:
- Vehicle fleet tracking and route monitoring
- Short or long term asset tracking where cellular coverage is available
- Basic driver or equipment utilization monitoring
- Location based alerts and simple telematics reporting

These devices are often chosen when straightforward, reliable location reporting is needed without complex hardware integrations.
