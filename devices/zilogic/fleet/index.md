# Zilogic - Fleet

![Fleet](./logo.jpg)

The Zilogic BlackKite-Fleet is a GPS tracker specifically designed for mobile assets and fleet management applications. It features an integrated highly accurate SiRF IV GPS, ensuring precise location tracking. This device goes beyond traditional "Track and Trace" functionality, offering additional features to address a wide range of solutions.

One of the standout features of the BlackKite-Fleet is its dedicated interfaces for monitoring vehicle ignition, battery, and panic button. This allows for real-time monitoring of important vehicle parameters and ensures the safety and security of both the vehicle and its occupants. Additionally, the device offers three digital inputs that can be configured to remotely monitor in-vehicle status, such as door lock, oil level indication, and fare meter. This provides valuable insights into the condition and usage of the vehicle.

Furthermore, the BlackKite-Fleet provides three digital output lines that can be used to control on-board electronic devices and even immobilize the vehicle during unauthorized usage. This feature adds an extra layer of security and control for fleet managers. The device also includes two analog inputs, allowing for the monitoring and measurement of analog parameters like fuel level, temperature, and load. This enables fleet managers to keep track of important vehicle metrics and make informed decisions based on real-time data.

With its RS232 serial interface, the BlackKite-Fleet can easily connect to external devices such as RFID readers for operator authentication or digital sensors for parameter monitoring. This flexibility makes it a versatile solution for a wide range of fleet management applications.

### Key Features:

- Integrated highly accurate SiRF IV GPS
- Dedicated interfaces for vehicle ignition, battery, and panic button monitoring
- 3x digital inputs for remote monitoring of in-vehicle status
- 3x digital output lines for controlling on-board electronic devices
- 2x analog inputs for monitoring analog parameters
- RS232 serial interface for connecting external devices
