# CanTrack - G06L-4G Features

This page provides a public overview of the CanTrack G06L-4G and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for fleet management and real-time tracking. It focuses on practical, user-facing features you can expect when deploying the G06L with Plaspy, and explains the kinds of visibility and event reporting the device enables inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and how the device is installed. Where relevant this page notes common capabilities, but customers should confirm device specifics for their unit and deployment method with the manufacturer and installation team.

## Feature Overview

The G06L-4G is a vehicle-focused 4G LTE tracker designed for reliable position reporting, event alarms, and local buffering during connectivity gaps. It combines cellular resilience, a high-sensitivity GNSS receiver, and vehicle I/O to provide continuous tracking and event-driven alerts into Plaspy-enabled systems.

- 4G LTE connectivity with GSM fallback for resilient real-time reporting across variable coverage.
- High-sensitivity GNSS receiver delivering sub-5 meter positioning for accurate route and location data.
- Vehicle inputs and alarm suite including ignition ACC detection, movement, SOS, speeding, power-cut, and angle-change alerts.
- Local storage for up to 2000 location records and an internal backup battery to preserve event reporting during power loss.
- OTA firmware support and USB configuration to simplify maintenance and integration.
- Compact vehicle-ready form factor and wide input voltage range for cars, trucks, and commercial vehicles.

## Core Features of CanTrack - G06L-4G

- 4G LTE Cat 1 modem with GSM fallback to maintain reporting in mixed network environments.
- High-sensitivity GNSS engine with multi-channel tracking for reliable position fixes and improved TTFF.
- Ignition / ACC detection for event-driven tracking and ignition-based reporting.
- Movement, SOS, speeding, power-cut, and angle-change alarm support for anti-theft and incident notification workflows.
- Onboard storage of up to 2000 records to preserve route history when cellular is unavailable.
- Internal backup battery to enable emergency reporting during main power loss.
- Over the air firmware updates and micro USB configuration for remote and local device management.
- Support for optional remote immobilizer and remote controller interfaces where provided by the manufacturer or installer.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the G06L-4G’s position updates and events so fleet managers can view live location, receive notifications, and review historical routes. Plaspy’s platform detects common tracker protocols automatically and presents device telemetry and alarms in a consolidated dashboard for operational oversight.

- Real-time location updates and route playback displayed on Plaspy maps for dispatch and tracking.
- Alarm and event visibility inside Plaspy including ignition changes, movement, SOS, and power-cut notifications.
- Offline buffering from the device uploads to Plaspy when connectivity is restored, preserving continuity of history.
- Configurable reporting intervals and power modes on the device translate to adjustable data rates and battery use visible in Plaspy.
- OTA and USB-based configuration simplify maintaining firmware and settings for large deployments connected to Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti-theft monitoring and recovery with SOS and power-cut alerts presented in Plaspy for rapid response.
- Commercial fleet tracking for route visibility, dispatch coordination, and basic event monitoring.
- Driver event and incident logging using ignition and movement alarms to support operations and investigations.
- Long-haul or intermittent coverage deployments where local storage preserves data until upload.
- OEM or fleet-specific installations that require compact, vehicle-ready hardware and flexible inputs.
- Maintenance and rollout scenarios where OTA updates and USB configuration streamline device management.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version and device firmware configuration can enable or alter alarm behavior, reporting intervals, and power modes.
- Hardware revisions or regional LTE/GSM variants may affect supported cellular bands and accessory interfaces.
- Optional features such as immobilizer or remote controller support may require additional wiring, permissions, or hardware options from the installer or manufacturer.
- Local storage capacity and internal battery behavior depend on device configuration, storage settings, and firmware.
