# G06L 4G LTE Vehicle GPS Tracker

The G06L is a professional-grade 4G LTE hard-wiring GPS tracker built for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and fleet management. Designed for cars, trucks and commercial vehicles, the G06L combines SIMCom LTE Cat 1 connectivity with GSM fallback, a high-sensitivity GNSS receiver and a compact vehicle-ready form factor to deliver precise location, timely alerts and continuous telemetry to Plaspy-enabled platforms and mobile apps.

Whether you need anti-theft protection, driver behavior insights or scalable telematics across a mixed fleet, the G06L offers practical features—ACC/ignition detection, movement and SOS alarms, power-cut backup and local data buffering—so Plaspy users get dependable position reporting and event-driven notifications even when networks are intermittent.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G LTE tracker with GSM fallback for resilient, real-time tracking and fleet management.
- High-sensitivity GNSS \(66 channels, −165 dBm\) delivering location accuracy better than 5 m CEP for precise telemetry.
- Comprehensive alarm suite: movement, ignition \(ACC\) on/off, SOS, speeding, power-cut and angle-change alerts for anti-theft protection.
- 2000-point local storage locks position data when network is lost; configurable sleep and reduced GPRS modes to save power and data costs.
- Internal 200 mAh Li‑Polymer backup battery ensures event reporting during power loss and supports emergency reporting.
- OTA firmware updates and USB configuration simplify remote management and integration into Plaspy workflows.
- Compact, vehicle-grade design \(approx. 85 × 45 × 17 mm\) with wide input voltage \(9–36 VDC\) for cars, trucks and commercial vehicles.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed in a vehicle, the G06L reports GNSS position and telemetry via its LTE Cat 1 connection \(with GSM fallback\) to Plaspy-compatible servers. Plaspy receives location updates, alarm events and basic telemetry so fleet managers and dispatchers can view live maps, receive push or SMS alerts, and drill into historical routes. The device’s local storage, configurable reporting intervals and power modes ensure continuous operation and efficient data usage for long-term fleet deployments.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered over LTE/GPRS into Plaspy dashboards and mobile apps.
- Ignition/ACC detection and movement alarms for event-based tracking and driver behavior telemetry.
- Power-cut alarm supported by internal backup battery; device stores up to 2000 records during offline periods for later upload.
- Optional remote immobilizer \(remote fuel/electricity disable\) and remote controller support for anti-theft response.
- OTA firmware updates and USB configuration simplify maintenance and ensure compatibility with Plaspy platform updates.

## Use Cases

- Vehicle anti-theft and recovery: receive SOS and power-cut alerts, remotely disable vehicle power using the optional immobilizer function, and track recovery in Plaspy maps.
- Commercial fleet tracking and telematics: continuous location, ignition and speeding alerts help optimize routes, reduce idle time and improve dispatch efficiency.
- Driver behavior and compliance monitoring: ACC/ignition events, movement and angle-change alerts feed driver scoring and incident investigation tools.
- Intermittent-network operation: stores up to 2000 points while offline and uploads when connectivity is restored, maintaining route and event history for billing or compliance.
- OEM/ODM installations: customizable shell and functional options to meet specific fleet installation or mounting requirements.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G06L delivers a pragmatic balance of connectivity, precision and vehicle-focused inputs that make it a practical choice for Plaspy compatible deployments. Its LTE Cat 1 modem with GSM fallback ensures consistent real-time tracking and telemetry across mixed-coverage areas, while the high-sensitivity GNSS receiver provides sub-5 m positioning for reliable route reconstruction. Built-in safeguards—battery-backed power-cut alerts, local storage and configurable low‑power modes—reduce data loss and downtime, and OTA updates plus USB configuration simplify large-scale rollouts and maintenance.

For fleet managers and integrators using Plaspy, the G06L supports core telematics needs—real-time GPS tracker data, ignition/ACC events, alarm handling and optional immobilizer control—while offering the extendability required for fuel monitoring workflows or Bluetooth sensors via compatible gateways and integrations. That combination of stable connectivity, practical vehicle interfaces and remote management makes the G06L a reliable component in a Plaspy-enabled telematics ecosystem.
