# CanTrack - G06L-4G

<div className="device-logo">
![G06L-4G](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The G06L-4G is a professional grade 4G LTE vehicle GPS tracker designed for cars, trucks and commercial fleets. It pairs a vehicle ready form factor with a high sensitivity GNSS receiver and cellular connectivity that provides precise location updates, timely alarms and continuous telemetry for fleet management systems. The unit is intended for hard wired vehicle use and includes features for event reporting, local buffering and remote maintenance.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G06L-4G delivers the core inputs fleet managers expect to see in Plaspy dashboards and reports. Its alarm suite, ignition detection, backup power and offline storage help ensure position reporting and event driven notifications reach Plaspy even when networks are inconsistent, making it a practical option for organizations that need dependable tracking and operational oversight across mixed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- 4G LTE tracker with GSM fallback for resilient real time tracking and fleet visibility
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver for precise position reporting and route reconstruction
- Comprehensive alarm set including movement, ignition on off, SOS, power cut and angle change
- Local storage capacity to buffer thousands of records during offline periods
- Internal backup battery to support event reporting when vehicle power is lost
- OTA firmware update support and USB configuration for simplified device management
- Compact vehicle grade design and wide input voltage range suitable for cars and trucks

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected and configured, the G06L-4G transmits GNSS positions and event data to Plaspy compatible servers so fleet operators can monitor assets in near real time. Plaspy ingests location updates, alarms and basic telemetry to present live maps, alerts and historical route data for operational decision making and reporting.

- Real time location and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards and allow live monitoring of fleet movement
- Ignition and movement alarms feed event based tracking and help identify driver behavior or vehicle state changes
- Power cut alerts combined with the internal backup battery and local storage preserve critical events for later upload to Plaspy
- Optional remote immobilizer and remote controller support provide anti theft response options when used with compatible vehicle systems
- OTA firmware updates and USB configuration streamline maintenance and help keep devices aligned with Plaspy platform expectations

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle anti theft and recovery with SOS and power cut alerts shown in Plaspy maps
- Commercial fleet tracking for dispatching, route optimization and asset oversight
- Driver behavior monitoring and incident investigation using ignition and movement events
- Operation in intermittent network areas using local storage to retain route history for later upload
- OEM or integrator installations where a compact, vehicle focused tracker is required
