# CanTrack - G900LS-4G

<div className="device-logo">
![G900LS-4G](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The G900LS-4G is a compact vehicle GPS tracker from CanTrack designed for reliable real time location monitoring and fleet management. It offers global 4G LTE coverage with GSM fallback, continuous position reporting, and standard telemetry including speed, ignition status, and alarm events. Available in multiple wiring versions and with an optional relay for remote cut off, the device is intended for mixed fleet use and individual vehicle protection.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the G900LS-4G integrates with Plaspy platforms via its GT06 series protocol and standard TCP IP reporting. This compatibility allows fleet operators to ingest location and telemetry data into Plaspy for map visualization, alerting, and reporting. The combination of proven reporting methods and compact form factor makes the G900LS-4G a practical candidate for operators who want to manage assets through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compatibility with Plaspy through GT06 series protocol and TCP IP reporting for straightforward integration.
- Global 4G LTE communications with GSM fallback for dependable real time tracking across regions.
- Core telemetry reporting including GPS positions, speed, ignition detection, and alarm events.
- Power cut alarm plus an onboard backup battery to help maintain tracking during power interruptions.
- Optional remote relay control for fuel or electricity cut off to support immobilization responses.
- Compact and durable form factor suitable for cars, motorcycles, e bikes and mixed fleet assets.
- Wide input voltage range to accommodate diverse vehicle electrical systems.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the G900LS-4G sends regular position and telemetry packets that Plaspy parses to update maps, triggers, and reports. Plaspy uses the incoming data to provide operators with visibility into vehicle status and to automate alerts for predefined events.

- Real time location and movement updates to display vehicle positions on Plaspy maps.
- Ignition and ACC status reporting for trip detection and driver activity logging.
- Power cut alarm and backup battery reporting to detect tampering or unexpected power loss.
- Remote relay state reporting and control where supported, enabling immobilizer actions managed through Plaspy.
- Movement, angle change, speeding and vibration alarms routed into Plaspy alert workflows for prompt response.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for commercial vehicles where real time tracking and trip logs improve routing and utilization.
- Anti theft protection using movement and power cut alarms with optional remote immobilizer control.
- Driver behavior monitoring through speed and ignition event reporting to support safety programs.
- Mixed vehicle deployments including cars, motorcycles and light electric vehicles that benefit from a compact tracker.
- Remote command and monitoring scenarios where administrators need to receive alerts and query status from assets.

The G900LS-4G pairs practical hardware features with standard reporting methods that make it a suitable choice for organizations using Plaspy. Its combination of real time GPS tracking, ignition detection, alarm reporting and optional relay control supports common fleet and security workflows without requiring custom protocol work. For fleets that need a low profile tracker with broad regional coverage and straightforward platform integration, the G900LS-4G offers a balanced option.
