# CanTrack - TK06A

<div className="device-logo">
![TK06A](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CanTrack TK06A is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for locating and monitoring vehicles and mobile assets. It leverages GPS satellites for position fixes and the GSM GPRS network to deliver location data to authorized recipients. The device supports sending location information via SMS to mobile numbers and can forward GPS data via GPRS to an internet server for real time tracking on a computer or mapping platforms such as Google Maps or Google Earth.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK06A can feed position and alarm information into Plaspy for centralized fleet visibility and operational monitoring. Its combination of SMS and GPRS reporting provides flexible connectivity options that Plaspy can use to surface live locations, alerts, and historical tracks in the platform, making it a practical option for organizations evaluating trackers for integration into Plaspy based workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact design with built in GPS and GSM modules for vehicle location and monitoring
- GSM quad band support for broad regional compatibility
- Real time location reporting by SMS and by GPRS to an internet server
- Built in features for alarms and oversight such as overspeed, power failure, and ACC anti theft alerts
- Real time voice monitoring capability and authorized number controls
- Optional external relay and backup battery support for additional control and power failure alerting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to transmit GPS data to an internet server, the TK06A can deliver position updates and alarm events that Plaspy ingests for display and analysis. Plaspy can then present device locations, alerts, and historical tracks through its dashboard so teams can monitor assets in one place.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for continuous vehicle visibility
- Forwarding of overspeed, power failure, and other alarm events into Plaspy notifications
- Historical route playback and reporting for operational review and mileage tracking
- Fleet level monitoring and grouping to manage multiple TK06A devices together
- Use of SMS reporting as a fallback channel for critical alerts or when data connectivity is limited

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental fleet tracking to monitor vehicle location and usage
- Motorcycle, scooter, and bike anti theft and real time tracking
- Fleet management for delivery and service vehicles
- Personnel or asset tracking where a compact vehicle mounted tracker is required
- Remote monitoring of vehicles that need basic remote control features via external relay

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK06A is a straightforward tracker option for organizations that need reliable location reporting and a set of practical alarms and controls. Its SMS plus GPRS reporting model offers redundancy for teams that want mobile alerts in addition to server based tracking, and optional relay control provides a simple way to integrate basic vehicle control features into operational procedures.

Paired with Plaspy, the TK06A delivers a clear path from device telemetry to platform level visibility. Plaspy can consolidate location updates, surface alarms, and produce historical reports from TK06A units so operators can maintain situational awareness across a mixed fleet without requiring highly specialized device management.
