# CanTrack - G08L

![G08L](./tracker.jpeg)

The G08L is a Plaspy compatible 4-inch smart tail light GPS tracker designed to replace an existing vehicle tail light while preserving full lighting functionality. Built around a Quectel BG95 4G LTE module \(Cat M1 / Cat NB2\) and multiple LTE-FDD bands, the G08L delivers covert, continuous real-time tracking and fleet telemetry without altering vehicle exterior appearance — an ideal choice for fleet managers, rental operators, and logistics providers who need durable, unobtrusive location monitoring that integrates with Plaspy for live dashboards, alerts, and reporting.

The G08L combines GPS+BeiDou positioning \(typical accuracy under 5 meters\), a built-in G-sensor for driving behavior and crash detection, an internal rechargeable battery, and IP67 water resistance in a compact tail-light form factor. Its swap-in installation simplifies deployment and reduces tampering risk. With configurable geo-fencing, harsh driving detection, crash alarm, smart power modes and OTA firmware updates, the G08L provides reliable telemetry and real-time tracking data that Plaspy can ingest to power fleet management, anti-theft monitoring, and operational analytics.

## Key Highlights

- Covert tail-light design: replaces an original tail light for discreet, tamper-resistant GPS tracking and continuous monitoring.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and fleet management via LTE Cat M1 / Cat NB2 with multiple LTE-FDD bands.
- High-precision GNSS: GPS L1 + BeiDou positioning with typical accuracy &lt;5 m and speed accuracy better than 0.1 m/s.
- Driving behavior & safety: built-in G-sensor supports harsh acceleration/deceleration detection and crash alarms.
- Long-life power options: internal 3.7 V / 5000 mAh rechargeable battery with smart power modes; optional external power bank supported.
- Durable, weatherproof construction: IP67 water resistance and operating range suitable for vehicle installations.
- Remote maintenance: OTA firmware updates and power-optimized working/standby modes for long-term deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The G08L sends GNSS, motion and status telemetry over its LTE Cat M1 / NB2 connection to backend services. When integrated with Plaspy, the device delivers continuous location updates, event-driven alarms and telemetry so you can monitor routes, detect incidents, and automate alerts. Plaspy processes the data into live maps, geo-fence notifications, driver behavior reports and fleet-level dashboards for operational decision-making.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS position, speed, timestamp\).
- Driving-event reporting: harsh acceleration/deceleration and crash alarm events derived from the G-sensor.
- Battery and power status: internal battery level and device power state for remote monitoring and maintenance planning.
- Geo-fence and route compliance events configurable in Plaspy for alerts and reporting.
- OTA firmware update support to coordinate remote device maintenance through Plaspy or vendor tools.
- Ignition/immobilizer, fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensor interfaces are not specified in the product brief — verify vehicle-specific integration needs with the vendor when required.
