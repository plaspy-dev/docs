# CanTrack - G08L

<div className="device-logo">
![G08L](./tracker.jpeg)
</div>

The G08L is a Plaspy compatible 4 inch smart tail light GPS tracker designed to replace an existing vehicle tail light while maintaining full lighting function. It combines GNSS positioning with a cellular modem and on board motion sensing to provide continuous, covert location monitoring and event detection without altering the vehicle exterior. The tail light form factor and IP67 rated enclosure make it suitable for outdoor vehicle use where discreet, tamper resistant hardware is required.

As a device that can feed location, motion and status telemetry into Plaspy, the G08L is relevant for fleet managers, rental operators, and logistics teams that need real time dashboards, alerts and aggregated reporting. Plaspy ingests the G08L data to provide live maps, geo fence notifications, driver behavior summaries and fleet level analytics, enabling operational oversight while preserving the vehicle appearance and reducing tampering risk.

## Key Highlights

- Covert tail light design that replaces an original tail light for discreet, tamper resistant tracking and continuous monitoring.
- Plaspy compatible via integrated LTE Cat M1 and Cat NB2 cellular modem for reliable data uplink across multiple LTE bands.
- Dual constellation GNSS positioning with GPS and BeiDou for typical accuracy under 5 meters and strong location reliability.
- Built in G sensor for detecting harsh driving events and crash alarms to support safety and incident workflows.
- Internal rechargeable battery and configurable smart power modes to support long deployments and temporary off power scenarios.
- IP67 water resistant enclosure and vehicle oriented form factor for durable outdoor operation.
- Remote maintenance capability including over the air firmware updates to help keep fleets up to date.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the G08L is connected to a cloud platform like Plaspy it delivers GNSS positions, motion events and device status so fleet administrators can monitor assets in real time and act on incidents. Plaspy converts the incoming telemetry into maps, alerts and reports that support day to day operations and longer term analysis.

- Continuous location updates and timestamps for live tracking and route visibility.
- Event driven alerts for harsh driving, crash alarms and other motion based events derived from the built in G sensor.
- Battery and device power state reporting to help schedule maintenance and replacements.
- Geo fence and route compliance notifications configurable in Plaspy for automated alerts and auditing.
- OTA firmware update visibility to coordinate remote device maintenance as part of fleet operations.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking for delivery vans, box trucks and service vehicles where discreet hardware is preferred.
- Rental vehicle monitoring to reduce tampering and support recovery workflows through timely location alerts.
- Driver safety programs that use motion events and incident alarms to trigger investigation and coaching.
- Long term deployments where smart power modes and remote maintenance reduce onsite servicing.
- Logistics and route compliance monitoring using geo fences and proof of presence records.
