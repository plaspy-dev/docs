# CanTrack - G08L Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the CanTrack G08L and explains how its capabilities relate to operation with Plaspy. It summarizes the device features relevant to fleet managers, rental operators, and logistics teams who want to understand what the G08L can deliver when integrated with Plaspy for live maps, alerts, and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. Use this page for general guidance about capabilities and Plaspy compatibility, and consult the manufacturer for the most current device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The G08L is a 4 inch smart tail light tracker designed to replace an existing tail light while preserving normal lighting function. Its form factor and integrated telemetry make it suited to discreet, tamper resistant tracking for vehicle fleets. Below are the most relevant practical capabilities that operators can expect when using this tracker with Plaspy.

- Covert tail light installation that replaces the original light assembly to reduce tampering and preserve vehicle appearance.
- Dual constellation GNSS positioning using GPS and BeiDou with typical position accuracy under 5 meters for reliable location reporting.
- Built in G sensor for driving behavior monitoring and crash detection that can trigger event notifications.
- Internal rechargeable battery with smart power modes to support continuous or intermittent reporting and reduce onsite maintenance.
- IP67 rated enclosure intended for vehicle exposure, enabling outdoor use and long term deployment.
- OTA firmware update capability to apply vendor updates and configuration changes remotely.

## Core Features of CanTrack - G08L

- Tail light form factor for discreet installation while maintaining original lighting function.
- Cellular connectivity built around an LTE Cat M1 / Cat NB2 module for wide area data reporting and Plaspy compatibility.
- GPS plus BeiDou GNSS positioning with typical accuracy better than 5 meters and high speed accuracy for routing and proof of presence.
- Built in G sensor for harsh driving event detection and crash alarm generation.
- Internal rechargeable battery and configurable power modes to support extended field deployments.
- IP67 water resistance and vehicle grade environmental tolerances for outdoor use.
- Over the air firmware updates to maintain device software without physical access.
- Swap in installation that simplifies deployment and reduces installation time.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the G08L is integrated with Plaspy, its location, motion events and device status can be processed into maps, alerts and reports for operational oversight. Plaspy ingests the telemetry the device provides and presents it in dashboards and notification workflows for fleet teams.

- Live location updates and breadcrumb trails on Plaspy maps for route visibility and asset tracking.
- Event alerts for harsh driving and crash alarms derived from the G sensor, available for notification and incident workflows.
- Battery and power state monitoring so maintenance teams can plan recharging or replacement cycles.
- Geo-fence and compliance events configurable in Plaspy to trigger alerts when vehicles enter or leave defined areas.
- OTA update status and device health visible in Plaspy for coordinated remote maintenance and version control.
