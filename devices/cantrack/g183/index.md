# CanTrack - G183

![G183](./tracker.jpg)

The G183 series JOBD GPS Tracker is a compact, Plaspy compatible OBD-level tracker engineered for vehicle diagnostics and real-time telematics. Designed for passenger cars and commercial vehicles, the G183 delivers precise GNSS positioning, comprehensive diagnostic protocol support and continuous TCP/SMS reporting so fleet operators and service providers can integrate location, DTCs and vehicle status into Plaspy with minimal engineering effort.

The G183 combines robust in-vehicle connectivity with rich telemetry: mileage, fuel consumption, trip time, diagnostic trouble codes and extensive alarm reporting \(speeding, harsh driving, collision, towing, low voltage, ignition on/off, dongle plug/unplug, SOS and high temperature\). Its small form factor and OBD A/B interface make it ideal for scalable fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and vehicle health tracking when used with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible OBD-level GPS tracker built for fleet management and vehicle diagnostics.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/QZSS/COMPASS/Galileo\) with a 56-channel receiver and ~2.5 m CEP GPS accuracy.
- Supports OBDII, JOBD, J1939, J1708 and manufacturer-specific protocols \(Toyota, Nissan, Mazda, Honda, Mitsubishi, Subaru, Suzuki, Daihatsu, Hino, Isuzu\) for broad vehicle coverage.
- Real-time tracking and telemetry over TCP and SMS: mileage, fuel monitoring, trip time and DTC upload to Plaspy.
- Comprehensive alarm set including speeding, harsh acceleration/deceleration, sudden turns, collision detection, towing and SOS alerts.
- Compact, durable design \(62 x 50 x 25 mm, ~100 g\) with low sleep current for reliable fleet deployment.
- Built-in accelerometer \(±16 g, 3-axis\) and 8 MB flash buffering for event reconstruction and offline data storage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The G183 uploads location, diagnostic and event data over TCP or SMS so Plaspy can ingest real-time tracking and telemetry feeds. Once integrated, Plaspy dashboards and APIs can display live vehicle position, alarm events and maintenance-relevant DTCs for proactive fleet oversight.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered via TCP/SMS for Plaspy ingestion and visualization.
- Diagnostic Trouble Code \(DTC\) upload and protocol-aware vehicle parameters using OBDII/JOBD/J1939/J1708 and dedicated manufacturer protocols.
- Fuel monitoring and real-time mileage reporting for fuel consumption analysis within Plaspy reports.
- Ignition on/off detection and a wide range of alarm reports \(SOS, collision, tow, plug/unplug\) to trigger Plaspy alerts and workflows.
- Event reconstruction using the ±16 g accelerometer and local flash buffering to ensure accurate telematics history during temporary connectivity loss.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: combine real-time tracking, mileage and fuel monitoring to optimize routes and reduce operating cost with Plaspy analytics.
- Vehicle diagnostics and preventive maintenance: upload DTCs and telematics to Plaspy to prioritize repairs and reduce downtime.
- Anti-theft and safety monitoring: SOS alerts, dongle plug/unplug and towing alarms integrated into Plaspy for rapid response.
- Driver behavior and risk management: harsh acceleration/braking, sudden turns and collision detection feed into driver scorecards and coaching programs.
- Commercial vehicle telemetry: support for J1939/J1708 and multiple manufacturer protocols for heavy-duty trucks and mixed fleets.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the G183 for Plaspy integration delivers a practical balance of diagnostics depth and real-time tracking capability. Its broad protocol support ensures compatibility across passenger and commercial vehicle makes, while GNSS multi-constellation positioning and 56-channel reception provide reliable real-time tracking and accurate telemetry. Built-in accelerometer data and event buffering improve incident reconstruction and reduce data loss during temporary network outages.

For fleet operators and integrators, the G183 reduces deployment friction: OBD A/B connectivity, compact form factor, and multiple cellular module options accommodate regional network requirements. Plaspy-compatible TCP/SMS reporting and remote debugging capabilities accelerate system integration, enabling telemetry, fuel monitoring and ignition-aware workflows to be operational quickly. Where immobilizer or Bluetooth sensors are part of a larger solution, the G183 can contribute ignition and diagnostic data to Plaspy while external modules or gateways manage those peripheral functions.
