# CanTrack - 10000mAh Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the CanTrack 10000mAh magnetic asset GPS tracker (GF60 series) and explains how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about the device, what it reports, and the types of monitoring and alerts you can expect when the tracker is integrated with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, or how the unit is installed. Where necessary, verify device-specific details in the manufacturer documentation and consider deployment variables such as mounting location, cellular coverage, and configuration choices.

## Feature Overview

The 10000mAh GF60 series is a rugged, zero‑installation magnetic tracker designed for long deployments and discreet mounting on vehicles, trailers, and containers. It balances extended standby capacity with configurable reporting modes so operators can choose between near real‑time tracking and long battery life depending on mission needs.

- Magnetic, zero‑installation mounting for quick attachment to vehicles and assets without wiring
- IP67 rated enclosure and ABS fireproof housing for outdoor, harsh environment deployments
- Multi‑mode reporting to trade off reporting frequency and battery life for standby periods ranging from days to many months
- 4G/2G TCP/IP uploads and SMS configuration options to send location and status data to a tracking platform
- Anti‑tamper light sensor and vibration alarm for removal and movement alerts
- Optional remote voice listen‑in available for deployments that require audio monitoring

## Core Features of CanTrack - 10000mAh

- Plaspy compatibility via configurable APN and server settings so the device can report to a Plaspy account
- Magnetic N54 strength mount and compact ABS enclosure for discreet, non‑intrusive installation
- IP67 waterproof rating and rugged design suitable for outdoor asset tracking
- Marketed 10000mAh battery capacity with documented internal cell spec of 6000mAh and multi‑mode power management
- 4G LTE and 2G GSM TCP/IP reporting for live location uploads and telemetry reporting
- SMS command interface for remote configuration of reporting mode, APN and server settings
- Tamper detection using a light sensor plus vibration alarm for theft and unauthorized movement alerts
- GNSS positioning with multi‑channel receiver and published accuracy suitable for vehicle and asset location tracking

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When configured to report to Plaspy, the GF60 series sends positioning and status messages that Plaspy can ingest and present for monitoring, alerting, and historical review. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and maps reported points and events into its monitoring tools.

- Live location and recent position updates appear on Plaspy maps for operational visibility
- Historical route playback in Plaspy uses the device’s stored and uploaded points to show past movements
- Tamper, vibration and low battery events reported by the device can generate alerts and notifications inside Plaspy
- Multi‑mode reporting (real‑time, interval, clock) affects how frequently points arrive in Plaspy and can be adjusted to manage battery life and data granularity
- SMS configuration provides an alternative way to change device behavior when over‑the‑air updates are impractical
- Optional voice listen‑in availability can be noted in Plaspy incident workflows where audio context is required and permitted

## Typical Use Cases

- Stolen vehicle recovery and covert asset monitoring with anti‑tamper alerts and discreet magnetic mounting
- Container and trailer tracking for logistics operations that require rugged, waterproof long‑duration monitoring
- Fleet monitoring of off‑road or heavy equipment where wiring is impractical and battery‑powered trackers are preferred
- Short‑term rental and repossession workflows using historical route playback and movement alerts
- Remote asset surveillance where extended standby and configurable reporting reduce field maintenance
- Situations requiring rapid redeployment of trackers between assets without permanent installation

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware and regional hardware variants can change available features and behavior; features listed here reflect common GF60 series capabilities
- Optional functions such as remote voice listen‑in or extended telemetry may depend on model variant, regional approvals, and firmware settings
- Battery life estimates depend heavily on reporting mode, cellular signal quality, and environmental conditions; marketed capacity may differ from internal cell specifications
- SMS configuration and server/APN setup are common for field adjustments, but access and behavior may vary by carrier and region
- Integrations with additional telemetry or external sensors require compatible hardware and may need extra configuration on both the device and Plaspy
