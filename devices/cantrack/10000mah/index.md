# CanTrack - 10000mAh

![10000mAh](./tracker.JPG)

The 10000mAh Magnetic Asset GPS Tracker \(GF60 series\) is a Plaspy compatible, heavy‑duty magnetic tracker engineered for vehicle finance management, stolen vehicle recovery and long‑duration container tracking. With a wireless magnetic design and IP67 waterproof rating, the GF60 series delivers zero‑installation mounting and rugged outdoor performance for fleet management and anti‑theft deployments that require discreet, reliable real‑time tracking.

Marketed with a 10000mAh capacity and a documented rechargeable cell of 6000mAh, the tracker balances multi‑mode reporting for extended standby life \(7–365 days depending on configuration\) with near real‑time tracking when needed. The GF60 series supports 4G/2G TCP/IP uploads, rich SMS configuration and live tracking via web platform and mobile app, making it straightforward to configure as Plaspy compatible by setting the device APN and server IP/port.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: configure APN and server IP/port to integrate live GPS tracker data into Plaspy for real‑time tracking and telemetry.
- Zero‑installation magnetic mount: N54 level strong magnet for quick attachment to vehicles, trailers and containers without wiring.
- Rugged, weatherproof design: IP67 enclosure and ABS fireproof housing built for harsh outdoor environments and long deployments.
- Long standby and flexible modes: marketed 10000mAh battery \(6000mAh cell documented\) with reporting modes to optimize between real‑time tracking and extended battery life.
- Anti‑tamper and vibration alarms: light‑sensor anti‑tamper alerts and vibration detection for theft and removal notifications.
- Comprehensive remote configuration: SMS command support for mode switching, APN, server IP/port, reporting interval and factory reset.
- Optional remote voice listen‑in: available for specific deployments where audio monitoring is required.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GF60 series sends positioning and status data over GPRS/4G TCP/IP to your chosen server, so it works smoothly with Plaspy when you point the device to Plaspy’s server IP/port and configure the APN. Once connected, Plaspy can display live location, historical routes and alarm events delivered by the tracker. SMS configuration enables field adjustments if network access is limited.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates via 4G LTE/2G GPRS TCP/IP to Plaspy.
- Geo‑fence alerts and historical route playback available in Plaspy using the device’s reported points.
- Low battery, vibration and anti‑tamper \(light sensor\) alarms sent to Plaspy for immediate action.
- Multi‑mode reporting \(real‑time, interval, clock\) lets you trade reporting frequency for battery life within Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- Optional remote voice listen‑in configurable for deployments that require audio monitoring alongside GPS data.
- Telemetry suitable for integration into fleet management workflows; when paired with compatible external sensors or systems, telemetry can support fuel monitoring and ignition/immobilizer workflows managed in Plaspy.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the GF60 series for Plaspy deployments gives you a compact, rugged GPS tracker built for long‑duration monitoring and straightforward platform integration. The device’s server IP/port and APN configuration options, combined with TCP/IP uploads over 4G/2G, make it simple to connect to Plaspy for real‑time tracking, alarms and historical playback. Its magnetic, waterproof form factor reduces installation time and risk, while multi‑mode operation and low standby current deliver predictable battery life for fleet management and anti‑theft programs.

For operations that require broader telemetry—such as fuel monitoring or ignition/immobilizer control—Plaspy workflows can combine GF60 location and tamper telemetry with other wired or BLE sensor inputs \(when available from paired devices\). The result is a scalable, Plaspy compatible solution that prioritizes reliability, discreet mounting and configurable reporting to meet both real‑time tracking and long‑term asset monitoring needs.
