# CanTrack - G900LS

<div className="device-logo">
![G900LS](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The G900LS is a professional hard‑wiring 4G GPS tracker from CanTrack designed for vehicle owners and fleet operators who need precise, reliable positioning and remote control. Built for continuous vehicle monitoring, the device delivers real‑time tracking, rich telemetry, and configurable alarms so operations teams and dispatchers can see location, ignition status, and critical events. Its wiring variants and compact form factor make it suitable for discreet installation in a wide range of vehicle types.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the G900LS integrates into Plaspy to provide live maps, event alerts, historical reports, and remote command capability where supported. That compatibility lets fleet managers consolidate vehicle visibility and operational oversight within Plaspy dashboards and mobile access, enabling automated notifications and actionable reporting for day to day fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Ready for Plaspy integration for straightforward onboarding into existing fleet workflows and dashboards.
- Global 4G LTE coverage with GSM fallback to maintain connectivity across regions.
- High accuracy GNSS positioning for reliable route and asset monitoring.
- Multiple vehicle alarms and telemetry including movement, ignition on/off, speed alerts, and power‑cut detection.
- Built‑in backup battery to support power‑cut alerts and short autonomous operation after mains loss.
- Optional relay control for remote immobilization and theft response when supported by the platform.
- Available 4‑pin and 8‑pin wiring variants to match common vehicle harnesses.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the G900LS transmits location and event data so Plaspy can display live positions, trigger rules, and generate reports. Configurable upload intervals and event driven reporting let Plaspy balance update frequency and data usage while delivering the visibility operations teams require.

- Live location and telemetry feed into Plaspy maps for real time tracking and dispatching.
- Ignition status, movement and speed events populate Plaspy alerts and rules for automated notifications.
- Power‑cut and backup battery events are reported to Plaspy so teams can detect tampering or power loss.
- Remote relay or immobilizer commands available through Plaspy where the device and deployment support them.
- Historical trip and event logs accessible in Plaspy for reporting, analysis, and compliance review.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for route oversight, driver monitoring, and operational efficiency.
- Anti‑theft protection using movement and power‑cut alerts combined with remote immobilization.
- Logistics and delivery monitoring to validate stops and improve on time performance.
- Rental and leasing oversight for ignition and usage monitoring and remote disable options.
- Vehicle recovery and incident response using speed, angle change, and movement alerts.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G900LS is a practical option for organizations that need professional grade vehicle monitoring paired with a fleet platform like Plaspy. Its combination of accurate GNSS positioning, broad cellular coverage, and configurable alarms provides the core data most fleet and security teams rely on. Plaspy leverages those inputs to deliver maps, alerts, scheduled reports, and remote command workflows that support everyday operations and incident response.

Because the G900LS is Plaspy compatible out of the box, it can be added to an existing Plaspy account with minimal configuration, allowing teams to scale monitoring across vehicles and benefit from consolidated visibility and reporting within the platform.
