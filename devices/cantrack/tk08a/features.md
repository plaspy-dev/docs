# CanTrack - TK08A Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive feature overview of the CanTrack TK08A and how its capabilities map into Plaspy for tracking, telemetry, and anti theft workflows. It summarizes the practical features you can expect when using the TK08A with Plaspy and how those features support monitoring and fleet operations.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. For deployment specific details such as wiring, relay configuration for immobilizer control, and the latest firmware behavior consult the official CanTrack documentation and installer guidance.

## Feature Overview

The TK08A is a compact, hard wired GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, e bikes, scooters and small vehicles. It offers live location reporting and a focused alarm set that supports theft protection and basic fleet monitoring, while integrating with Plaspy for visibility and reporting.

- Real time location reporting via GSM GPRS or SMS into Plaspy for live tracking and history.
- Remote immobilizer capability to support engine disable or electricity cut off as part of anti theft workflows.
- Comprehensive alarms including vibration and power cut detection for tamper awareness.
- Internal backup Li Polymer battery to send short reports after mains power loss.
- On board storage for position buffering during cellular outages and automatic upload when connectivity returns.

## Core Features of CanTrack - TK08A

- Real time position reporting over GPRS or SMS compatible with Plaspy.
- Ignition ACC status reporting to detect trip start and stop events.
- Vibration and angle change alerts for movement and tamper detection.
- Power cut detection plus short term reporting on the internal backup battery.
- Remote immobilizer support for engine or power cut control when properly installed.
- Wide working voltage options to suit motorcycles e bikes and small vehicles.
- On board memory for storing positions while GSM is unavailable.
- Built in GSM and GPS antennas in a compact hard wired form factor.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK08A feeds location and event data into dashboards, alerts and reports so operators can monitor fleet activity and respond to incidents. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents device telemetry alongside mapping and history.

- Real time location and telemetry updates from the tracker are visible in Plaspy dashboards and maps.
- Ignition ACC status is used in Plaspy for trip detection and to separate driving versus parked events.
- Remote immobilizer events can be initiated and tracked from Plaspy where the device installation supports relay control.
- Power cut and backup battery reports are available as alerts to indicate possible tampering or power loss.
- Buffered positions stored on the device are uploaded to Plaspy after connectivity is restored to preserve route continuity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small vehicle and mixed power fleets needing compact trackers.
- Motorcycle scooter and e bike anti theft solutions using vibration detection and remote immobilizer.
- Workshop and service yard monitoring to log vehicle arrival departure and ignition status.
- Insurance telematics projects that require compact low power devices for trip and event logging.
- Last mile delivery and shared mobility where compact form factor and broad voltage tolerance simplify installation.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some capabilities depend on firmware version and device hardware revision; features may vary across production runs.
- Remote immobilizer and relay based functions require correct wiring and installation by a qualified technician.
- Alarm and upload behavior can be affected by installation location and cellular coverage; on board buffering helps preserve positions during outages.
- Regional variants and cellular band support may differ; confirm the device variant intended for your market.
- Configuration options include USB, SMS and GPRS based methods as provided by the manufacturer and may require specific setup steps.
