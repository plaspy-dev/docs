# CanTrack - TK06 v2

![TK06 v2](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack TK06 v2 is a versatile GPS tracker that utilizes the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites to locate and monitor remote targets. Designed for easy and compact use, this tracker is perfect for vehicle tracking. It comes with a built-in GPS and GSM module that captures GPS data and sends it to an authorized mobile number via SMS. You can easily track the current location of your vehicle on your phone or on platforms like Google Earth or Google Maps. Additionally, the GPS data can be transmitted via GPRS to an internet server, allowing for real-time tracking on a computer.

The CanTrack TK06 v2 has a wide range of applications, making it suitable for various scenarios. It can be used for vehicle rental and fleet management, ensuring that you can keep track of your vehicles at all times. It is also ideal for motorcycle, scooter, or bike anti-theft, providing you with peace of mind knowing that your valuable assets are protected. The tracker can be used for real-time tracking of motorcycles, scooters, or bikes, allowing you to monitor their movements and location. Additionally, it can be used for managing personnel, enabling you to keep track of your employees' whereabouts.

Some of the outstanding features of the CanTrack TK06 v2 include GSM quad-band frequency for global use, real-time tracking location via SMS/GPRS, real-time voice monitoring function, overspeed alarm, power failure alarm, ACC anti-theft alarm, and the ability to connect an external relay to control the oil and circuit of the vehicle. It also has a built-in backup battery for power failure alarm \(optional\). With its advanced features and reliable performance, the CanTrack TK06 v2 is an excellent choice for all your tracking needs.
