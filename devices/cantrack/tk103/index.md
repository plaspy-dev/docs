# CanTrack - TK103

![TK103](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack TK103 is a versatile GPS tracker that combines the power of GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites to provide a comprehensive tracking solution. This all-in-one device offers features such as tracking, listening, tracing, and anti-theft capabilities, making it an ideal choice for various applications.

With the CanTrack TK103, you can easily locate and monitor any remote targets using SMS or GPRS. It offers a range of advanced features to enhance your tracking experience. One of the standout features is the remote switch on and off, allowing you to control the tracker remotely. This feature comes in handy when you need to conserve battery or ensure privacy.

In addition to remote control, the CanTrack TK103 offers single tracking, monitoring, and data storage capabilities. You can track the real-time location of your target, monitor its movements, and store the tracking data for future reference. The device also includes a geo-fence alarm, which alerts you when the target moves outside a predefined area.

Other notable features of the CanTrack TK103 include overspeed alarm, vibration alarm, and sound-triggered callback. These features provide added security and peace of mind by notifying you of any unusual activities or events. You can also check the vehicle state to get information about the target's status and condition.

Overall, the CanTrack TK103 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers a wide range of functionalities. Whether you need to track your vehicle, monitor your assets, or enhance the security of your belongings, this tracker has got you covered.

### Key Features:

- Remote switch on and off
- Single tracking
- Monitoring
- Data storage
- Geo-fence alarm
- Movement alarm
- Overspeed alarm
- Vibration alarm
- Set all alarm mode
- Sound-triggered callback
- Check the vehicle state
