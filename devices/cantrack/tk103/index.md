# CanTrack - TK103

![TK103](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack TK103 is a compact GPS tracker that combines satellite positioning with GSM GPRS connectivity to provide location reporting, remote monitoring, and basic anti theft functions. It supports real time tracking, SMS and GPRS communication, audible monitoring, geo fence alerts, overspeed and vibration alarms, and remote on off control, making it a general purpose device for vehicle and asset tracking needs.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the TK103 can feed location and alert data into Plaspy for unified monitoring and reporting. Organizations using Plaspy can add TK103 devices to their fleet or asset lists to centralize visibility, receive movement and geofence notifications, and keep historical tracking records within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Combines GPS positioning with GSM GPRS communication for remote location reporting.
- Supports SMS and GPRS modes for flexible data delivery and command options.
- Built in anti theft features such as geo fence alarm, overspeed and vibration alerts.
- Remote switch on and off capability to manage device operation from a distance.
- Sound triggered callback and movement alarm for enhanced situational awareness.
- Local data storage and single tracking options for record keeping and review.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TK103 transmits location updates and event alerts using GSM GPRS or SMS, which can be received and processed by the Plaspy platform for live monitoring and historical reporting. Once configured to report to Plaspy, the device becomes part of a consolidated fleet view for administrators and dispatchers.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for real time visibility of tracked targets.
- Event and alarm ingestion such as geo fence breaches, overspeed or vibration alerts.
- Stored tracking history available in Plaspy for playback and route analysis.
- Centralized notifications and alerting rules managed through Plaspy for operational oversight.
- Fleet level dashboards and reporting to summarize device activity and events.

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle tracking for small fleets and personal vehicles.
- Asset protection and anti theft monitoring for mobile equipment.
- Remote monitoring where SMS or GPRS connectivity is available.
- Route review and movement history for operational audits.
- Situations requiring simple remote control and status checking of tracked targets.

The TK103 is a pragmatic option for organizations that need a straightforward tracking device combined with a capable fleet management platform.
