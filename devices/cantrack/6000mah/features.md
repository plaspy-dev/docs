# CanTrack - 6000mAh Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the CanTrack 6000mAh magnetic asset GPS tracker (model GF50) and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is intended to help fleet operators, asset managers, and security teams understand the practical functions they can expect when the device is integrated into Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and historical playback.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and manufacturer settings. For device-specific configuration details and the latest technical specifications, consult the official CanTrack documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The CanTrack 6000mAh GF50 is designed for long-term covert asset protection and fleet asset monitoring. It pairs a high-capacity rechargeable battery with a rugged, waterproof enclosure and a strong magnetic mount to support installations on trailers, containers, heavy equipment, and similar metallic assets. Plaspy-compatible reporting and configurable working modes let operators balance location update frequency against battery life.

- Long battery endurance supporting extended standby and interval tracking modes to reduce maintenance visits.
- IP67 rated enclosure and N54-grade magnet for durable, tool-free mounting on metal surfaces.
- Multiple working modes including real-time, interval, and clock/wakeup to optimize telemetry and power use.
- Built-in security alerts such as anti-tamper, geo-fence notifications, vibration alarms, and low-battery warnings.
- Multi-band cellular variants (2G and 4G/LTE) for broad network coverage and reliable data delivery to Plaspy.

## Core Features of CanTrack - 6000mAh

- 6000mAh rechargeable battery designed for long standby and periodic reporting.
- Rugged IP67 ABS housing for dust and water resistance in outdoor environments.
- Strong N54 magnetic mounting for covert, tool-free attachment to metallic assets.
- Configurable reporting modes: real-time, interval, and clock/wakeup to manage power and tracking cadence.
- Security and operational alerts including anti-tamper, geo-fence breach notifications, vibration alarms, and low-battery warnings.
- Plaspy compatibility for real-time monitoring and historical playback on web and mobile platforms.
- Remote configuration options via SMS commands and platform settings where supported by the device firmware.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the GF50 is registered to Plaspy, telemetry and event messages from the device are presented in the platform to support monitoring, alerting, and historical analysis. Plaspy can centrally manage reporting modes and display alarms so teams can respond quickly to asset incidents or exceptions.

- Live location updates and map visualization for immediate situational awareness in Plaspy.
- Alarm and event forwarding so anti-tamper, geo-fence, vibration, and low-battery events appear with timestamps and location context.
- Historical route reconstruction and playback for audits, recovery, and route review.
- Centralized management of reporting modes to balance tracking resolution with battery life.
- Correlation of GF50 events with Plaspy dashboards and rule engines for scheduled reports and notifications.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert tracking of trailers and containers to assist with theft recovery and proof of location during transport.
- Long-term surveillance of heavy machinery and construction assets where durable mounting and water resistance are required.
- Finance and asset management cases where historical route logs and tamper alerts support repossession and audits.
- Remote asset monitoring for seasonal or intermittent operations that require extended battery life between maintenance cycles.
- Security operations that need discreet installation and fast incident notification through a central platform.

Using the CanTrack 6000mAh GF50 with Plaspy gives organizations a practical combination of long-duration asset tracking and a centralized monitoring platform.
