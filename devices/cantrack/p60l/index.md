# CanTrack - P60L

![P60L](./tracker.jpg)

The P60L Personal GPS Tracker is a Plaspy compatible, 4G LTE personal tracking device built for reliable real-time tracking, two-way voice communication and environmental telemetry. Designed for personal safety, elder/child monitoring and asset use cases where voice and temperature sensing matter, the P60L pairs accurate GNSS positioning \(GPS + BeiDou\) with LTE/GPRS connectivity and flexible remote management to deliver dependable location and alerting to Plaspy-powered dashboards and mobile apps.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the P60L uploads location and sensor data in real time and supports offline logging for intermittent coverage. Built-in VoLTE, an SOS alarm button and onboard temperature sensing make it suitable for anti-theft alerts, emergency response and continuous telemetry. Remote OTA firmware updates \(FOTA\) and a comprehensive SMS command set simplify fleet management, configuration and integration with Plaspy workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and alerts with minimal integration effort.
- 4G LTE + GPRS \(SIMCom Cat‑1 A7680C + AT6558R\) for broad cellular coverage and low-latency telemetry.
- High-accuracy GNSS: GPS + BeiDou with a 66‑channel receiver and location accuracy &lt;5 m CEP.
- Two‑way voice \(VoLTE\) with microphone and speaker plus an SOS button for immediate response.
- Onboard temperature monitoring for asset or personal environmental telemetry.
- Internal 1200 mAh Li‑Poly backup battery with long standby and configurable sleep/real‑time modes.
- Offline logging of up to 2,000 location records to preserve history when connectivity is limited.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the P60L with Plaspy is straightforward: the device transmits GNSS position fixes and sensor telemetry over LTE or GPRS to Plaspy’s platform using TCP/IP, where the data is visualized, trended and used to drive alerts. Plaspy receives the device’s real‑time location stream, SOS events, voice-triggered incidents and temperature readings to support fleet management, personal safety monitoring and telemetry-based workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via LTE/GPRS \(TCP‑IP\).
- SOS alarm and smart voice broadcast for immediate incident notification and response.
- Temperature monitoring reported continuously or on event thresholds for environmental alerts.
- Offline logging: up to 2,000 stored points that upload when connectivity is restored.
- Remote configuration and firmware updates \(FOTA\) through Plaspy-compatible endpoints or SMS command set for large-scale deployments.

## Use Cases

- Personal safety and elder care — continuous location monitoring, SOS button and two‑way voice for quick assistance.
- Child tracking and lone worker protection — Plaspy receives alerts and location updates to enable rapid intervention.
- Asset monitoring with environmental telemetry — temperature sensing for sensitive cargo and offline logging for intermittent cellular areas.
- Pet or portable equipment tracking — compact form factor, long standby and real‑time tracking on demand.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The P60L delivers a balanced mix of accurate GNSS positioning, LTE connectivity and voice-capable telemetry in a compact, Plaspy compatible package. For organizations that need real-time tracking, reliable anti‑theft alerts and environmental telemetry, the P60L offers dependable data flow into Plaspy for mapping, rules-based alerts and reporting. Its FOTA capability and rich SMS command set simplify large-scale fleet management and remote configuration, while offline logging and robust GNSS performance ensure continuity of history and location accuracy even in marginal coverage areas.

If your deployment also requires ignition monitoring, immobilizer control, fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensor integration, Plaspy can aggregate data from dedicated sensors or complementary devices alongside the P60L to deliver a complete telemetry and fleet management solution. The P60L is ideal where two‑way voice, SOS and temperature telemetry are priorities and where Plaspy’s real‑time dashboards and alerting workflows will be used for rapid response and ongoing operational insight.
