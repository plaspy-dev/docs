# CanTrack - YG119

![YG119](./tracker.jpg)

The YG119 is a compact, rechargeable GPS tracker engineered for hunting dogs and active pets and is fully Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and recovery workflows. With automatic position uploads based on movement or a user-set reporting interval, the YG119 delivers reliable location updates to the Plaspy platform so handlers and owners can monitor animals from a mobile app or web console.

The device combines long endurance, multi-band cellular connectivity and practical recovery features — remote voice listen, an audible buzzer, geo-fence alerts and low-battery warnings — in a small, rugged form factor built for forests, fields and other demanding outdoor environments. Plaspy compatibility ensures those inputs and alerts are turned into actionable telemetry, notifications and historical reports for quicker recoveries and better situational awareness.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: automatic uploads and live position streaming to the Plaspy platform for real-time tracking and alerting.
- Long endurance: 4500 mAh rechargeable battery with typical standby performance of about 14 days under light daily activity.
- Accurate GNSS positioning: AT6558R chipset with L1 operation and sub-5 m accuracy in good reception conditions for precision recovery.
- Remote voice listen and audible buzzer: call the device to hear surrounding audio or trigger a buzzer to locate a lost animal on site.
- Configurable reporting: movement-triggered or interval-based uploads conserve power while maintaining timely updates during activity.
- Multi-band cellular support: LTE Cat 1 \(SIMCom A7670 series\) and GPRS Class 12 TCP/IP for broad network availability in Latin‑US and Asia variants.
- Power-saving and deep-sleep modes: extends operating time while ensuring quick wake and fixes when the animal is active.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integration with Plaspy is straightforward: the YG119 uploads position and status data over cellular \(LTE/GPRS\) using TCP/IP, and Plaspy ingests that data to provide real-time tracking, geo-fence alerts and history. Reporting can be based on movement detection or a preset interval, so Plaspy receives updates only when needed or continuously during active tracking sessions.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy via TCP/IP over the device’s cellular link.
- Geo-fence alerts and movement-triggered reports are captured and routed by Plaspy for instant mobile or web notifications.
- Battery status and low-battery warnings are uploaded so Plaspy can trigger conserve-mode recommendations or notify owners.
- Remote voice listen: Plaspy can log and display listen events \(device answers when called\) to aid in recovery operations.
- Audible buzzer activation is available for on-site locating and can be tracked as an event in Plaspy history.

## Use Cases

- Hunting and field work: track dogs in dense forest or grasslands with accurate fixes and audible locating when retrieval is needed.
- Trainer and performance monitoring: review movement patterns and location history on Plaspy after training sessions.
- Lost-pet recovery: use remote voice listen and the buzzer together with Plaspy’s real-time map to locate a missing animal quickly.
- Remote property oversight: monitor working animals across large properties where intermittent updates and long battery life are essential.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The YG119 pairs device-level reliability with Plaspy’s telemetry and alerting to create a focused solution for outdoor pet tracking. Its multi-band cellular modem and TCP/IP support ensure data reaches Plaspy reliably across regions, while the AT6558R GNSS engine supplies precise location fixes that matter when every meter counts. Power-saving modes and a large 4500 mAh battery reduce downtime and deliver longer intervals between charges, and the built-in recovery aids — remote voice listen and audible buzzer — turn a location ping into a tangible recovery action.

For teams and owners who require Plaspy compatible trackers but also need specialized telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition control or immobilizer interfaces, or Bluetooth sensors for temperature or proximity, Plaspy supports a broad device ecosystem. The YG119 is purpose-built for hunting dogs and active pets where compact size, long battery life and reliable real-time tracking are the priorities.
