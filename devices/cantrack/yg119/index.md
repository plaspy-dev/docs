# CanTrack - YG119

![YG119](./tracker.jpg)

The YG119 is a compact, rechargeable GPS tracker designed for hunting dogs and active pets. It provides regular position uploads based on movement or a user configured reporting interval, and includes practical recovery aids such as a remote voice listen function and an audible buzzer. Built for demanding outdoor environments, the YG119 combines multi band cellular connectivity, a high capacity battery and a rugged small form factor to support extended field use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the YG119 delivers those location and status updates directly into the Plaspy platform so handlers and owners can monitor animals via Plaspy mobile or web interfaces. Alerts like geo fences and low battery warnings are turned into actionable notifications and historical reports by Plaspy, helping speed recoveries and improve situational awareness when animals are working or off leash.

## Key Highlights

- Fully compatible with Plaspy for automatic uploads and live position streaming to a centralized tracking platform.
- Compact and rugged design intended for hunting dogs and active outdoor pets.
- Long endurance with a 4500 mAh rechargeable battery and typical standby performance suitable for multi day use under light activity.
- Accurate GNSS positioning for reliable fixes in good reception conditions to aid precise recovery.
- Remote voice listen and an audible buzzer to assist on site locating of a lost animal.
- Configurable reporting modes that use movement triggered updates or set intervals to balance timeliness and battery life.
- Multi band cellular connectivity for broad regional network availability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the YG119 sends position and status updates to the platform where those inputs are presented and managed for operational use. Plaspy ingests the device uploads and converts them into live map views, alerting rules and historical logs so owners and teams can act quickly when a recovery or response is needed.

- Real time location updates and status indicators appear in Plaspy maps and dashboards for immediate visibility.
- Geo fence triggers and movement alerts are captured and routed by Plaspy to deliver notifications to handlers.
- Battery status and low battery warnings are reported to Plaspy so users can plan recharges or swap devices.
- Remote voice listen and buzzer events are logged in Plaspy history to document recovery actions and support on site searching.
- Historical tracks and event reports are available for review in Plaspy to analyze movement patterns and past sessions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking hunting dogs during field work to maintain location awareness and assist retrieval.
- Training and performance review by recording movement history and session data for later analysis.
- Lost pet recovery using real time location, remote listen and buzzer assistance together with Plaspy mapping.
- Monitoring working animals across large properties where intermittent updates and long battery life matter.
- Situations where compact rugged hardware and reliable event alerts are needed for outdoor operations.
