# CanTrack - TK102

![TK102](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack TK102 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for both personal and vehicle tracking. It uses GPS satellites for positioning and the GSM GPRS network to deliver location data and alerts. The device can send location updates via SMS to authorized mobile numbers and can also transmit position data by GPRS to an internet server for real time tracking and monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK102 can feed position and alarm data into Plaspy to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can receive the GPRS transmitted data from the TK102 and present it on maps, include events in alert workflows, and store tracking history for reporting and audit. That combination makes the TK102 a practical option for users who want a simple tracker with a clear path into fleet and asset monitoring software.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for personal and vehicle tracking with both SMS and GPRS reporting options
- Real time tracking capability when connected to an internet server
- Remote voice surveillance to monitor surroundings by calling the device
- Multiple alarm functions including geo fencing, movement, speeding, SOS, low voltage, and vibration alerts
- Support for TF card storage to record data when cellular connectivity is unavailable
- Practical hardware features for discreet placement such as magnetic cover and waterproof bag

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the TK102 transmits location and alarm information over the cellular network to a server, Plaspy can ingest those updates and present them through its tracking interface. This enables centralized monitoring and management across multiple devices and users.

- Live device locations displayed on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Alarm events such as geo fence breaches, movement, speeding, and SOS forwarded into Plaspy notification channels
- Historical route playback and reporting for review of past movements recorded on the server
- Consolidated fleet monitoring to view multiple TK102 units in a single Plaspy account
- Centralized alerting and operational oversight to support response workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and basic vehicle monitoring for rental or small commercial fleets
- Asset protection and location visibility for company vehicles and equipment
- Personal safety and location monitoring for children, elderly relatives, or caregivers
- Pet tracking and recovery when a discreet device placement is required
- Situational monitoring and security surveillance that benefits from remote voice monitoring
