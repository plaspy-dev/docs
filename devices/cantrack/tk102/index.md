# CanTrack - TK102

![TK102](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack TK102 is a versatile GPS tracker that is designed for both personal and vehicle tracking. It works by utilizing the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites to locate and monitor remote targets. With its built-in GPS and GSM module, it can capture GPS data and send it to an authorized mobile number via SMS. This allows you to track the current location of the device on your phone or on popular mapping platforms like Google Earth or Google Maps. Additionally, the GPS data can be transmitted by GPRS to an internet server, enabling real-time tracking on a computer.

One of the standout features of the CanTrack TK102 is its voice surveillance function. By dialing the device number from your mobile phone, you can monitor the voice around the device, providing an added layer of security and peace of mind. The TK102 is also equipped with a range of other useful functions, including geo-fencing, movement alarm, speeding alarm, SOS alarm, low voltage alarm, and vibration alarm. It even supports a TF card for data storage when there is no GSM signal available.

The CanTrack TK102 is an ideal solution for a variety of applications. It can be used for vehicle rental and fleet management, providing real-time tracking and monitoring of your vehicles. It can also be used to protect children, the elderly, disabled individuals, or pets, ensuring their safety and well-being. For businessmen, the TK102 offers peace of mind by allowing them to track their assets and personnel. It can even be used for covert tracking of criminals. With its magnetic cover and waterproof bag, the TK102 can be discreetly hidden for covert tracking purposes. Overall, the CanTrack TK102 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers a wide range of applications and functionalities.

#### Outstanding Features:

- Real-time tracking of your car's location
- Continuous tracking with regular updates
- Remote voice surveillance for added security
- Geo-fencing to receive alarms when the device exceeds a designated area
- Movement alarm to alert you when the device moves from a designated place
- Speeding alarm to notify you when the driving speed exceeds a set value
- SOS alarm for emergency situations
- Low voltage alarm to indicate when the device's battery is running low
- Support for TF card for data storage
- Vibration alarm for additional security measures
