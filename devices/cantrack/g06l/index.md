# CanTrack - G06L

![G06L](./tracker.jpg)

The G06L is a compact 4G GPS tracker that is Plaspy compatible and optimized for vehicle-based deployments. Designed for motorcycles, cars, scooters and light trucks, the G06L combines a small internal-antenna housing with proven GNSS/GSM hardware and a backup battery so fleet operators and service providers can get reliable, real-time tracking and anti-theft protection with easy integration into Plaspy’s platform.

The device supports comprehensive telemetry and alarm reporting — including ignition detection, SOS, movement, speeding and power-cut alarms — and is configurable via PC \(USB\), GPRS or SMS. For businesses looking for a low-profile tracker that supports immobilizer-style remote cut-off, offline storage and OTA firmware updates, the G06L offers a flexible solution that pairs smoothly with Plaspy for fleet management, insurance telematics and vehicle security workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: seamless integration for real-time tracking, alarms and reports on the Plaspy web and mobile apps.
- Compact internal-antenna design \(85 × 45 × 17 mm\) for discreet installation on motorcycles, cars and light trucks.
- Wide input voltage range \(9–36 V DC\) and built-in 200 mAh backup battery to support power-cut alarm and continued location reporting.
- Ignition detection and digital output for remote power-off relay enable telemetry-driven immobilizer workflows and remote accessory control.
- Robust GNSS performance: 66-channel receiver with -165 dBm sensitivity and \<5 m CEP location accuracy for dependable real-time tracking.
- Memory buffer \(~2000 data points\) and configurable sleep modes help preserve data and battery when GPRS is unavailable or the vehicle is parked.
- OTA firmware updates plus PC-USB/GPRS/SMS configuration make remote management and scaling straightforward for integrators.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed, the G06L continuously acquires GNSS position and vehicle telemetry and transmits data to Plaspy over LTE/GPRS using TCP/IP. Plaspy ingests GPS coordinates, ignition and movement events, alarms and offline-recorded points to provide live location, historical playback, alerts and telemetry dashboards. Administrators can configure reporting intervals, alarm thresholds and remote commands from the Plaspy platform to maximize fleet safety and operational visibility.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via GPRS/LTE to Plaspy for live tracking and breadcrumb history.
- Ignition on/off detection and movement alarms appear in Plaspy to support driving behavior monitoring and event-driven workflows.
- Power-cut alarm and backup battery reporting ensure Plaspy receives a last-known position when main power is removed.
- Memory storage for offline recording \(≈2000 points\) ensures no gaps — queued data is uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity is restored.
- Optional remote immobilization \(fuel/electricity cut-off\) and remote accessory control can be executed through Plaspy when enabled and configured.

- Fleet management: track routes, monitor ignition events and speeding, and produce trip reports through Plaspy’s dashboard.
- Anti-theft protection for motorcycles and e-bikes: discreet internal installation, SOS alarms and power-cut reporting with backup battery.
- Vehicle insurance telematics: driving behavior and event logging for usage-based insurance programs and claims verification.
- Service workshops and rental fleets: receive ignition and movement alerts, remote immobilization options, and historical location playback.
- Light truck and delivery operations: compact tracker for route optimization, real-time asset visibility and operational telemetry.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G06L is a practical choice for integrators and fleet operators who need a small, reliable Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with robust telemetry and remote-control capabilities. Its internal antennas and compact form factor make installation simple and discreet; built-in GNSS performance and a backup battery ensure accurate, continuous reporting even during power loss. With ignition detection, configurable alarms, offline storage and optional immobilizer control, the G06L delivers the core features required for anti-theft, fleet management and insurance telematics.

Combined with Plaspy’s platform — which also supports additional telemetry sources such as Bluetooth sensors and external fuel monitoring devices — the G06L becomes part of a scalable ecosystem for real-time tracking, remote immobilization and actionable reporting. OTA updates, flexible configuration channels \(USB/GPRS/SMS\) and OEM/ODM options make the G06L a dependable, integrator-friendly tracker for deployments where accuracy, low profile installation and operational control matter.
