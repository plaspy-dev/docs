# CanTrack - G06L

![G06L](./tracker.jpg)

The CanTrack G06L is a compact 4G GPS tracker optimized for vehicle based deployments. Designed for motorcycles, cars, scooters and light trucks, the G06L combines a small internal antenna housing with GNSS positioning and cellular connectivity, plus a backup battery to provide continued reporting during power loss. The device is built for discreet installation while delivering telemetry and alarm reporting that helps operators maintain visibility over mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G06L integrates into Plaspy’s fleet management platform to provide live location, historical playback and alarm handling. Its support for ignition detection, SOS, movement, speeding and power cut alarms, together with offline data buffering and remote control features, makes the G06L a practical choice for operators who want straightforward pairing with Plaspy for tracking, security and operational reporting workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking, alarm delivery and reporting on the Plaspy platform.
- Compact internal antenna housing for low profile installation on motorcycles, cars and light trucks.
- Built in backup battery and power cut alarm support to provide last known position and short term reporting during power loss.
- Ignition detection and digital output for remote immobilizer style control and accessory management.
- Robust GNSS performance and memory buffer to capture and preserve location points when connectivity is interrupted.
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware updates to simplify device management at scale.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once deployed, the G06L collects GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry and transmits events and location updates to Plaspy. Plaspy ingests those data points and presents live tracking, event alerts and historical routes while allowing administrators to configure reporting behavior and execute remote commands when supported by the device.

- Real time location and telemetry feed into Plaspy for live tracking and breadcrumb history.
- Ignition, movement and speeding alarms appear as events in Plaspy for monitoring and workflow triggers.
- Power cut notifications and backup battery status help Plaspy show last known position when main power is lost.
- Offline recorded points stored on the device are uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity is restored to prevent data gaps.
- Optional remote immobilization and accessory control can be managed through Plaspy when enabled and configured.
