# CanTrack - TK100N

![TK100N](./tracker.png)

The TK100N is a hard wired, multi functional 2G vehicle GPS tracker from a proven OEM designed for professional deployments. It offers real time tracking along with key vehicle status features such as ACC detection, an SOS panic input, and remote relay control. The unit is intended for cars, motorcycles and e bikes and is built to operate across a wide vehicle voltage range for dependable in service use.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the TK100N integrates with Plaspy using the GT06N message format to deliver live location, status alerts and remote control actions into the platform. This makes the tracker a practical choice for fleets and security teams who want straightforward onboarding to Plaspy and reliable telemetry for monitoring and response workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via GT06N protocol for quick platform integration and standardized telemetry ingestion
- Real time tracking with SMS fallback to maintain location reporting when data conditions vary
- Vehicle status features including ACC detection and an SOS panic input for incident reporting
- Remote relay control to support immobilization and other remote interventions where configured
- High sensitivity MTK GNSS performance with typical position accuracy around 10 meters for dependable positioning
- Wide operating voltage range suitable for cars motorcycles and e bikes, supporting varied vehicle electrical systems

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the TK100N sends location and event messages that Plaspy parses to show live position, alarm states and vehicle status on the platform. Plaspy centralizes incoming telemetry, applies configured alerts and enables remote command actions so operators can monitor fleets, respond to incidents and maintain operational oversight.

- Live location and telemetry streaming to Plaspy using GT06N formatted messages for consistent ingestion
- ACC detection reported to Plaspy to mark trip start and stop for reporting and operational analysis
- SOS panic, vibration and power off alarms forwarded to Plaspy for immediate alerting and incident tracking
- Remote relay commands issued from Plaspy to enable immobilizer or other authorized remote actions
- SMS command fallback available for configuration and status checks when data connectivity is limited

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars and light commercial vehicles requiring live location and trip segmentation
- Anti theft protection with SOS alerts vibration and power off notifications to aid recovery efforts
- Protection and tracking for motorcycles and e bikes where compact form factor and low current draw matter
- Driver and vehicle monitoring using ignition events and movement history to support safety programs
- Telemetry driven operations where position data is combined with platform reporting for route and asset oversight

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK100N is a pragmatic choice for organizations that need a compact, hard wired tracker with straightforward Plaspy compatibility. Its GT06N protocol support simplifies server onboarding and reduces configuration overhead, while SMS fallback and robust alarm inputs provide multiple paths to maintain visibility and control. For operators who require reliable position reporting, ignition based trip data and remote relay capability, the TK100N delivers the core feature set useful for fleet management and security workflows.
