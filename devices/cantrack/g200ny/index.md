# CanTrack - G200NY

<div className="device-logo">
![G200NY](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The G200NY is a rugged 4G GPS tracker designed for long term livestock tracking and remote herd management. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with assisted positioning and cellular fallback to provide dependable location reporting across open ranges, marginal coverage and seasonal grazing routes. The enclosure and battery design make it suited to extended outdoor deployments where autonomy and durability matter.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G200NY can stream location and event data into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. This compatibility lets ranch operators and researchers use Plaspy dashboards and alerting to track animals, receive SOS or movement notifications, and manage devices remotely for scalable telemetry across single herds or enterprise deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for livestock and outdoor use with a rugged IK10 rated fire retardant ABS enclosure and IP67 waterproofing.
- Long autonomy provided by a high capacity 30000 mAh battery with extended standby performance under typical reporting intervals.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning including GPS and Beidou, plus assisted positioning and LBS fallback for improved coverage.
- Cellular 4G connectivity with 2G fallback for broad network reach and dependable reporting in remote areas.
- Remote configuration and OTA firmware upgrade capability to simplify device management and reduce field visits.
- Built for a wide operating temperature range and a compact form factor suitable for collar or harness mounting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the G200NY delivers periodic location and status updates that Plaspy ingests and presents through dashboards, alerts and reporting tools. Plaspy centralizes the device feed to help teams maintain situational awareness across large areas and long deployments.

- Real time location visualization and historical route playback for individual animals or groups.
- Geo fence alerts and movement detection forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and archiveable event logs.
- SOS and panic event reporting routed into Plaspy alert workflows for rapid attention.
- Configurable reporting profiles to balance tracking frequency and battery life as managed through the Plaspy interface.
- OTA updates and remote configuration capability coordinated via Plaspy to keep devices current and reduce maintenance overhead.

## Typical Use Cases

- Enterprise livestock management tracking herd movement, grazing patterns and location history across large properties.
- Ranch monitoring for vulnerable or high value animals with movement alerts and SOS reporting.
- Anti theft and rapid recovery efforts using real time location feeds to locate animals or field equipment.
- Field research and migration studies that require long autonomy and continuous GNSS tracking.
- Remote pasture management to reduce manual checks by combining geo fencing and movement alerts.

Its combination of multi constellation positioning, cellular fallback and large battery capacity reduces the need for frequent maintenance while delivering consistent location reporting that Plaspy can centralize and act on.
