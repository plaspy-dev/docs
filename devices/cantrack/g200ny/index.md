# G200NY Livestock GPS Tracker

The G200NY is a Plaspy compatible, rugged 4G GPS tracker engineered for long-term livestock tracking and remote herd management. Built to operate in harsh outdoor conditions, the G200NY combines multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS + Beidou\), assisted positioning \(AGPS\) and cellular fallback \(LBS\) to deliver dependable location reporting across open ranges, marginal coverage and seasonal grazing routes.

The device pairs seamlessly with Plaspy for real-time tracking, geo-fence alerts, SOS events and movement detection, giving ranch operators and researchers a scalable telemetry solution. With an IK10-rated, fire-retardant ABS enclosure, IP67 waterproofing and a high-capacity 30000 mAh pure cobalt polymer battery, the G200NY is purpose-built for long autonomy, reliable telemetry and simplified remote management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates GNSS and cellular telemetry for real-time tracking and alerts.
- Rugged, outdoor-ready design: IK10 shell strength, fire-retardant ABS and IP67 waterproofing.
- High-capacity 30000 mAh battery delivering up to 180 days standby under typical reporting intervals.
- Multi-constellation positioning: GPS + Beidou with AGPS and LBS fallback for reliable location in remote areas.
- 4G connectivity with 2G fallback via SIMCom LTE A7670C module for broad network compatibility.
- OTA firmware upgrades and remote configuration to minimize field visits and simplify fleet management.
- Long operating temperature range and compact, lightweight form factor for collar or harness mounting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the G200NY streams periodic location and status updates to your Plaspy dashboard using its LTE cellular link. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates, assisted positioning and LBS data to present accurate real-time tracking, historical routes and event-driven alerts. Remote configuration and OTA updates keep the device firmware current and reporting profiles optimized for battery life and coverage.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via 4G LTE \(with 2G fallback\) to Plaspy servers.
- Geo-fence alerts and movement detection reported to Plaspy for immediate notification and historical analysis.
- SOS/panic event reporting forwarded to Plaspy for rapid alerting and response workflows.
- Configurable reporting modes \(periodic, adaptive\) to balance real-time tracking and battery longevity.
- OTA firmware and remote configuration to manage fleets of trackers from the Plaspy console.

## Use Cases

- Enterprise-scale livestock management: track herd movement, grazing patterns and location history across large properties.
- Ranch and herd monitoring: remote monitoring of sick or vulnerable animals with SOS alerts and movement detection.
- Anti-theft tracking: rapid locating of stolen animals or equipment using Plaspy real-time location feeds.
