# CanTrack - G200NY Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the CanTrack G200NY features as they relate to use with the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the device capabilities relevant to tracking, alerts, and remote management so fleet managers, ranch operators, and researchers can understand how this tracker integrates with Plaspy for operational visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details, firmware notes, and the latest specifications please consult the official CanTrack documentation and product resources.

## Feature Overview

The G200NY is a rugged 4G GPS tracker designed for long term livestock and remote asset tracking, with GNSS redundancy and cellular fallbacks that improve location reliability in marginal coverage areas. It focuses on long autonomy, durable packaging for outdoor use, and remote management features that reduce field visits.

- Multi constellation GNSS positioning using GPS and Beidou for improved coverage in open ranges.
- Assisted positioning support with AGPS plus cellular based fallback LBS for locations with weak GNSS reception.
- Long life battery and configurable reporting modes to support extended deployments without frequent recharging.
- Rugged enclosure with high ingress protection and impact resistance for outdoor and collar or harness mounting.
- OTA firmware updates and remote configuration to manage device fleets centrally through Plaspy.

## Core Features of CanTrack - G200NY

- GPS plus Beidou multi constellation positioning for robust location fixes across wide areas.
- Assisted GPS support and LBS fallback to improve location reporting when direct GNSS signals are degraded.
- 4G LTE connectivity with 2G fallback for broad cellular network compatibility.
- Long duration 30000 mAh battery designed to support extended standby and reporting intervals.
- IK10 rated and IP67 waterproof enclosure suitable for outdoor livestock and harsh conditions.
- SOS event reporting and movement detection for immediate alerts and follow up.
- OTA firmware upgrades and remote configuration capabilities for reduced onsite maintenance.
- Type C charging interface and remote management features for operational convenience.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the G200NY reports GNSS coordinates, assisted positioning and cellular fallback information into the Plaspy platform for visualization, alerting, and historical analysis. Plaspy can ingest the device telemetry and expose location and event data alongside other assets to support operational decisions.

- Real time and historical location feeds visible on Plaspy maps and reports to track animals or assets.
- Geo fence and movement events reflected as alerts in Plaspy to notify teams of departures or activity changes.
- SOS event forwarding to Plaspy for rapid notification and escalation workflows.
- Configurable reporting modes such as periodic or adaptive reporting are represented in Plaspy to help balance battery life with tracking needs.
- OTA firmware and remote device configuration coordinated through Plaspy to keep trackers updated and aligned with deployment policies.
- Device connectivity and reporting are accepted by Plaspy servers which automatically detect tracker protocol; devices may be configured to use UDP or TCP for reporting.
