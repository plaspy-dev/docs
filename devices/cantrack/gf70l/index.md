# CanTrack - GF70L

![GF70L](./logo..jpeg)

The CanTrack GF70L is a powerful and versatile GPS tracker designed specifically for asset tracking. With its rechargeable 12000mAh battery, this tracker offers long-lasting performance and reliability. What sets the GF70L apart is its highly efficient solar conversion panels, which allow the device to harness solar energy and extend its battery life even further. This means that you can rely on the GF70L to provide continuous tracking without the need for frequent recharging.

One of the standout features of the GF70L is its strong magnetic installation. With its powerful magnets, this tracker can be easily attached to any metallic surface, making it ideal for tracking assets such as vehicles, containers, or machinery. The magnetic installation eliminates the need for complicated wiring or installation processes, saving you time and effort.

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the GF70L also offers a range of advanced features. It supports geo-fencing, allowing you to set up virtual boundaries and receive alerts when the tracker enters or exits these areas. The device also features low battery and vibration alarms, ensuring that you are always aware of its status. Furthermore, the GF70L can be equipped with an optional microphone, enabling remote detection and one-way calling.

With its real-time tracking, timing tracking, and smart tracking modes, the GF70L provides you with multiple options for monitoring your assets. Whether you need to track your assets in real-time, set up scheduled tracking intervals, or utilize intelligent tracking algorithms, this tracker has you covered.

#### Key Features:

- 12000mAh rechargeable battery for long-lasting performance
- Anti-scratch solar panels for efficient solar energy conversion
- Strong magnetic installation for easy attachment to metallic surfaces
- Optional microphone for remote detection and one-way calling
- Geo-fence, low battery, and vibration alarms for enhanced security
- Real-time tracking, timing tracking, and smart tracking modes for flexible monitoring
