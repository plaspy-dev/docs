# CanTrack - GF70L

![GF70L](./logo..jpeg)

The CanTrack GF70L is a rugged asset GPS tracker designed for long duration deployments. It features a high capacity 12000mAh rechargeable battery and integrated solar conversion panels to extend operating life. Strong magnetic mounting simplifies attachment to metallic assets such as vehicles, containers, or equipment, while built in alerts and flexible tracking modes provide practical monitoring options for many asset tracking scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the GF70L can be used within Plaspy for consolidated visibility and operational oversight. Its long endurance profile and magnetic installation make it especially suitable for assets that require infrequent maintenance and reliable location reporting. Plaspy users can add the GF70L to their device inventory to take advantage of location updates, status alerts, and reporting alongside other devices in a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- High capacity 12000mAh rechargeable battery for extended tracking intervals and reduced maintenance
- Integrated anti scratch solar panels to harvest solar energy and prolong operational time
- Strong magnetic mounting for quick and secure attachment to metallic surfaces without complex wiring
- Multiple tracking modes including real time, scheduled timing, and smart tracking to match monitoring needs
- Built in geo fencing support and alerts for entry exit notifications and perimeter control
- Low battery and vibration alarms help maintain awareness of device status and activity
- Optional microphone for remote detection and one way calling where audio monitoring is required

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GF70L feeds location and status updates into the platform so teams can monitor assets from a single dashboard. Plaspy organizes data from this tracker alongside other devices, enabling consolidated reporting and configurable notifications.

- View live location and recent movement history for individual GF70L units in Plaspy
- Configure geo fence regions and receive platform alerts when boundaries are crossed
- Monitor low battery and vibration alarms via Plaspy notifications to plan maintenance
- Use scheduled and smart tracking modes to balance reporting frequency and battery life within Plaspy
- Include GF70L devices in fleet summaries and asset lists for operational reporting and audit trails

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset tracking for containers and trailers where recharging is infrequent
- Monitoring remote machinery or equipment left at worksites with limited access
- Fleet assets that benefit from quick magnetic mounting and periodic relocation
- Rental or leased equipment tracking for location control and activity monitoring
- Situations needing configurable alerts like geo fence breaches or low battery warnings
