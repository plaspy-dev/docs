# CanTrack - GF70L Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the CanTrack GF70L with Plaspy. It summarizes the GF70L's practical capabilities for asset tracking and explains how those capabilities are typically reflected in Plaspy for monitoring, alerting, and operational oversight.

The exact availability and behavior of individual features can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device specific details consult the manufacturer documentation while using this page as a Plaspy-focused feature overview.

## Feature Overview

The CanTrack GF70L is designed for durable asset tracking with extended runtime and flexible mounting. Its combination of a large rechargeable battery plus solar charging options and a strong magnetic installation make it practical for long deployments on vehicles, containers, or heavy equipment. The tracker supports several alarm and reporting modes that provide operational visibility without constant maintenance.

- Long runtime from a rechargeable high capacity battery for extended deployments
- Solar assist via anti scratch solar panels to extend time between charges
- Strong magnetic mount for quick attachment to metallic assets without permanent wiring
- Geo-fence support for virtual boundary monitoring and alerts
- Low battery and vibration alarms to signal maintenance needs or movement events
- Optional microphone for remote detection and one way calling where supported

## Core Features of CanTrack - GF70L

- Rechargeable high capacity battery for prolonged tracking intervals
- Anti scratch solar panels to harvest solar energy and prolong battery life
- Strong magnetic installation for easy attachment to metallic surfaces
- Geo fencing to define areas and trigger entry or exit notifications
- Low battery alarm to notify when charge is running low
- Vibration alarm to detect movement of a mounted asset
- Optional microphone that enables remote audio detection and one way calling
- Multiple tracking modes including real time, timed reporting, and smart tracking

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy provides centralized visibility for devices like the GF70L and surfaces the tracker events and telemetry that the device reports. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and displays incoming positions and events alongside other fleet data so teams can monitor assets from a single interface.

- Real time positions and timed reports appear in Plaspy as location updates on the map and in the activity timeline
- Geo fence events generate alerts and history entries that help verify compliance with virtual boundaries
- Low battery and vibration alarms are shown as device events so operators can react to maintenance or security issues
- Optional microphone capability is represented as a device feature when available and visible in the device profile
- Multiple tracking modes reported by the device influence how often Plaspy receives updates and how history is recorded

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset tracking for containers or equipment where solar charging reduces maintenance
- Temporary attachment to vehicles or trailers using the magnetic mount for quick redeployment
- Remote monitoring of assets with geofence boundaries to detect entry or exit from specific sites
- Event driven notifications such as low battery or vibration alerts for faster operational response
- Scheduled or intelligent reporting to balance battery life and tracking frequency
- Situations requiring optional remote audio detection or one way calling where permitted
