# CanTrack - GT02

![GT02](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack GT02 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for locating and monitoring vehicles and mobile assets. It uses GPS satellite positioning together with GSM network connectivity to determine location and report it to authorized recipients. The GT02 can deliver location information via SMS to preset mobile numbers and can also send data over GPRS to an internet server for real time monitoring from a computer or mapping platform.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GT02 can be integrated into fleet and asset tracking workflows to provide vehicle visibility and operational oversight. Its combination of SMS fallback and GPRS reporting makes it suitable for basic remote tracking configurations, and the GT02's alarm and control options can be surfaced in Plaspy for alerts and operational responses.

## Key Highlights

- Compact design intended for vehicle tracking and remote monitoring
- Quad band GSM support for use across many regions
- Real time location reporting available by SMS or GPRS
- Built in voice monitoring function for remote listening when required
- Multiple alarm options including overspeed and power failure alerts
- Optional external relay and backup battery features for vehicle control and power failure notifications

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GT02 can feed location and status information into the platform so fleets and operators gain a unified view of assets. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and presents locations on maps, stores historical routes, and enables alerting and reporting workflows.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for active vehicle tracking
- Alerting and notifications in Plaspy triggered by device alarms like overspeed or power loss
- Historical route and event reporting to support operations and compliance
- SMS based location as a fallback when data connectivity is limited
- Voice monitoring events can be logged or referenced in Plaspy alongside other telemetry
- Use of optional relay control as part of operational responses coordinated through Plaspy workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium vehicle fleets requiring location visibility
- Vehicle rental tracking and oversight to monitor asset movements
- Asset protection and recovery support through real time alerts
- Management oversight for personnel using company vehicles
- Remote monitoring where SMS fallback is useful for intermittent data coverage

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GT02 is a practical choice for organizations that need a straightforward vehicle tracker with dual reporting options and a set of common alarm features. Its ability to report by SMS or GPRS gives flexibility in environments with varied connectivity, and the optional relay and backup power features add operational utility for vehicle control and power loss detection.

Paired with Plaspy, the GT02 can contribute to a simple, reliable tracking setup that brings visibility to vehicle movements and supports routine fleet operations. If you need a compact tracker that aligns with general fleet monitoring needs, the GT02 is a reasonable candidate to evaluate within a Plaspy deployment.
