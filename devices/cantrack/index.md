# CanTrack

CanTrack is a GPS tracker brand used across vehicle, asset, and fleet monitoring applications. Plaspy recognizes CanTrack devices and can receive and display their telemetry so you can monitor location, status, and alerts from a single platform. Use the catalog below to browse compatible CanTrack devices and jump straight to the models that match your needs.

## How CanTrack devices work with Plaspy

CanTrack devices transmit location and telemetry data over mobile networks to a tracking service. Plaspy can ingest that data when a device is configured to send to the Plaspy server and when device protocol settings are matched in our platform. Typical setup steps include assigning the device identifier inside Plaspy and confirming that server and protocol parameters are correctly entered on the device or its configuration portal. For device specific configuration guidance, see the device model pages available in the catalog above.

## Common features found in CanTrack devices

While individual features vary by model, many CanTrack devices commonly provide capabilities useful for monitoring and fleet operations. These commonly include:
- Periodic GPS position reporting for location history and live tracking
- Movement and motion detection for activity alerts
- Power and battery reporting for maintenance planning
- Input and output options for vehicle sensors and simple remote control
- Geofence and event based alerting for operational alerts

The BrandCatalog component lists the exact models and their published capabilities so you can compare features without guessing.

## Typical use cases for CanTrack GPS trackers

CanTrack devices are used in a range of telematics scenarios, including:
- Fleet vehicle location and route monitoring
- Asset tracking for equipment and trailers
- Theft recovery and unauthorized movement alerts
- Rental vehicle management and usage auditing
- Remote status monitoring for mobile or offsite assets

Plaspy helps translate raw device data into actionable information for these scenarios through mapping, alerts, and reporting.

## Help finding the right CanTrack device

Choosing the right tracker depends on the installation environment and monitoring needs. Consider these questions when evaluating models in the catalog:
- Will the device be hardwired to a vehicle or used as a battery powered asset tracker?
- What reporting interval is needed to balance real time visibility and battery life?
- Are external inputs required for ignition or sensor monitoring?
- Is tamper detection or waterproofing necessary for the installation environment?

Use the BrandCatalog above to filter models by form factor and features, then review the model details for power requirements and supported features.
