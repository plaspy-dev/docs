# CanTrack - G500M

![G500M](./tracker.jpg)

The CanTrack G500M Mini GPS Tracker is a compact plug and play OBD II unit designed for straightforward vehicle monitoring. It provides live location updates, historical playback and core telemetry through cellular data, with onboard memory to cover brief connectivity gaps. The G500M is positioned for practical vehicle oversight across light fleets, rental cars and individual vehicles where a reliable, low friction tracking solution is required.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the G500M integrates with Plaspy to feed real time positions, ignition state and motion events into centralized dashboards and reporting. That compatibility makes the G500M a convenient choice for teams that want quick deployment and consistent visibility inside Plaspy for tracking, alerts and operational review.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for fast, non invasive deployment across multiple vehicles
- Real time tracking and centralized fleet management compatibility with Plaspy via cellular data
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver for consistent position accuracy and reliable location updates
- Ignition state detection for automated trip logging and simplified event correlation
- G sensor and motion detection to report collisions and unusual vehicle movement
- Local position storage that automatically reuploads when connectivity is restored
- Power on and unplug alarms plus geo fence support for anti theft monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the G500M is connected to a vehicle, it collects location, ignition and motion information and transmits that data to Plaspy for immediate use in live maps, alerts and historical playback. Plaspy ingests the incoming telemetry to provide fleet visibility, event notifications and reporting while the device memory helps preserve continuity when coverage is intermittent.

- Real time location and telemetry feed into Plaspy maps and operational dashboards
- Ignition detection supports accurate trip start and stop logging in Plaspy reports
- Geo fence events, motion alerts and unplug notifications can be delivered as Plaspy alerts
- Stored positions from blind areas are reuploaded to Plaspy for complete travel history
- G sensor and angle change events are available in Plaspy for incident review and analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light vehicle fleets requiring quick device deployment and centralized monitoring
- Rental vehicle monitoring where rapid installation and unplug alerts help enforce policies
- Anti theft workflows that combine motion detection, unplug alarms and geo fence notifications
- Safety and incident reporting using G sensor events to support post event analysis
- Coverage gap resilience where local storage preserves position history until upload

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G500M offers a compact, low effort hardware option that pairs well with Plaspy for organizations that need straightforward vehicle visibility. Its plug and play design reduces installation time while ignition detection, motion alerts and local storage provide the core telemetry most operations teams rely on for trip logging and incident awareness. Integrating the G500M with Plaspy gives teams a consistent view of fleet location, alerts and historical data without extensive setup.
