# CanTrack - G500M Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the CanTrack G500M with Plaspy. It covers the G500M's practical capabilities as a compact OBD plug-and-play GPS tracker and explains how those capabilities are typically reflected inside Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and centralized fleet oversight.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary depending on device firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Always consider that some functions may be enabled or limited by firmware version, regional variants, or the way the device is installed in a vehicle.

## Feature Overview

The G500M is designed for simple deployment via the vehicle OBD II port while providing continuous position reporting and essential vehicle telemetry over cellular data. Its integrated antennas and local storage are intended to reduce data gaps and simplify installation for fleet and rental deployments.

- Plug and play OBD II installation for fast deployment without hardwiring.
- Real time tracking and telemetry reporting over cellular data for live visibility.
- ACC ignition detection to support automatic trip start and stop logging.
- Local position storage to record positions during coverage gaps and re-upload later.
- G sensor and angle change detection for collision and harsh movement events.
- Power on and unplug alarms plus geo-fence reporting to support anti theft workflows.

## Core Features of CanTrack - G500M

- OBD II form factor for noninvasive power and ignition signal access.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver (MTK chipset, 66 channels) for precise location.
- GSM GPRS connectivity with TCP/IP reporting for continuous data upload.
- ACC ignition status detection for automated trip and engine state telemetry.
- G sensor with motion detection and angle change reporting for event detection.
- Local memory storage for approximately 1,400 position records during blind spots.
- Power on and unplug alarms and scheduled timing reports for operational alerts.
- Geo-fence support and configurable position reporting for location based workflows.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the telemetry the G500M sends and presents it in maps, dashboards, and reports so operators can track vehicles in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify onboarding and to surface device events without manual protocol selection in many cases.

- Live location updates and historical playback appear in Plaspy maps for route review.
- ACC ignition events become start and stop markers for trip logging and utilization reports.
- Stored positions recorded during coverage gaps are re-uploaded and reconciled in Plaspy historical data.
- G sensor and angle change events are available for incident review and context in Plaspy event timelines.
- Alarms such as power on, unplug, and geo-fence crossings are delivered into Plaspy alerting and notification channels.
- Device diagnostics and reporting cadence are visible in Plaspy to support operational troubleshooting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light vehicles where fast, consistent installation is required.
- Rental car monitoring where noninvasive installation and unplug alerts are important.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using motion, unplug, and geo-fence alerts.
- Incident review for collisions or harsh events using G sensor and angle change data.
- Coverage gap resilience where onboard memory records positions until connectivity returns.
- Temporary or seasonal deployments that benefit from plug and play installation.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets and behaviour may differ by firmware version and manufacturer firmware updates.
- Hardware revisions and regional cellular band variants can affect connectivity and performance.
- Some functions such as remote listening or certain alarm types may require explicit configuration and may be subject to local regulations.
- Installation method and the specific vehicle OBD port wiring can influence which signals are available to the device.
- Consult manufacturer documentation for device specific limits and recommended configuration practices.

Using the CanTrack G500M with Plaspy provides a straightforward path from physical installation to centralized tracking and operational oversight. The plug and play OBD II form factor reduces deployment effort, while ignition detection, motion events, and onboard memory help maintain accurate trip logs and minimize data gaps.
