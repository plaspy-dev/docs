# CanTrack - G900LM-4G

![G900LM-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The G900LM-4G Car Mini GPS Tracker from a proven telematics family delivers compact, hard-wired vehicle tracking that is fully Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and fleet management. Designed for cars, trucks and commercial vehicles, the G900LM-4G combines reliable LTE/GSM connectivity with precise GNSS performance, an internal backup battery and multiple alarm types to protect assets and improve operational telemetry.

Install the G900LM-4G in minutes using the 4-pin or 8-pin wiring variants and connect it to Plaspy’s mobile app or web platform for continuous location updates, configurable alerts and remote control. Fleet managers and service providers will appreciate its broad band support, GT06-series protocol compatibility and remote relay control for fuel/power cut—giving anti-theft and immobilizer-like capabilities when integrated with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for continuous real-time tracking via mobile app and web platform.
- Flexible hard-wiring: 4-pin and 8-pin wiring versions to suit a wide range of vehicles and installations.
- Comprehensive safety and anti-theft alarms: movement detection, ignition on/off alerts, speeding, angle-change updates and power cut alerts.
- Optional remote relay control to cut fuel or electrical power—useful for remote immobilizer-style enforcement and theft response.
- Global-ready LTE/GSM/GPRS connectivity with multiple FDD and TDD LTE bands for regional deployments.
- High-sensitivity GNSS: 66 channels, -165 dBm sensitivity and location accuracy under 5 meters CEP.
- Small, discreet form factor with wide operating voltage \(9–90 VDC\) for cars, trucks and commercial fleets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The G900LM-4G sends location and status data to Plaspy over GPRS/TCP-IP using the widely supported GT06-series protocol. Once connected, Plaspy receives frequent telemetry updates for real-time tracking, alarms and reports. Remote commands and configuration can be applied via Plaspy’s interface or by SMS/platform commands supported by the device, enabling fast deployment and centralized fleet management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live maps and historical playback.
- Ignition on/off alerts and movement detection for driver and asset monitoring.
- Speeding and angle-change alerts to detect unsafe driving or unusual vehicle orientation.
- Power cut and backup-battery reporting for anti-theft visibility during tamper events.
- Optional remote relay control to cut fuel or power \(remote immobilizer-like control\) when authorized from Plaspy.
- Remote configuration via SMS and platform commands for APN, server, reporting intervals and alarm toggles.
