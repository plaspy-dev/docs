# CanTrack - G900LM-4G

![G900LM-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The CanTrack G900LM-4G is a compact hard wired car mini GPS tracker from a proven telematics family. It is designed for cars, trucks and commercial vehicles and combines LTE GSM connectivity with GNSS positioning, an internal backup battery and multiple alarm types to protect assets and improve operational telemetry. The device is available in 4-pin and 8-pin wiring variants for flexible installation options in a wide range of vehicles.

This model is fully compatible with Plaspy, making it suitable for organizations that want device level tracking integrated into a centralized fleet management platform. When paired with Plaspy, the G900LM-4G provides continuous location updates, configurable alerts and remote control options that help fleet managers monitor vehicles in real time and respond to theft or operational incidents.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for continuous real time tracking and fleet visibility.
- Compact hard wired form factor with 4-pin and 8-pin wiring choices for flexible installations.
- Multiple alarm types including movement detection, ignition change, speeding and power cut alerts for asset protection.
- Optional remote relay control to cut fuel or electrical power for immobilizer style response when authorized.
- Multi band LTE GSM connectivity for broad regional deployments and reliable data links.
- High sensitivity GNSS with internal backup battery to maintain tracking during power loss.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once the G900LM-4G is connected and configured, it transmits location and status data to Plaspy so fleet operators can view live positions, receive alarms and analyze historical movement. Plaspy acts as the central platform for alerts, reporting and remote commands, allowing managers to monitor fleet performance and respond to incidents from the web or mobile interface.

- Real time location updates and historical playback available within Plaspy for route review and auditing.
- Configurable alerts for ignition on off events, movement, speeding and power cut to support operational oversight.
- Remote relay control can be used from Plaspy to disable fuel or power where operational policies allow.
- Centralized reporting and event logs for fleet utilization, incident analysis and compliance tracking.
- Remote configuration and command support via platform messages and device supported commands to adjust reporting intervals and alarm settings.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars vans and trucks requiring continuous GPS telemetry and dispatcher visibility.
- Anti theft and immobilization workflows using movement alerts power cut detection and remote relay control.
- Delivery and logistics operations needing precise location updates and speed alerts for last mile performance.
- Mixed fleet deployments where small form factor and wide operating voltage simplify device standardization.
- Service and maintenance vehicle monitoring to improve utilization and response times.

The G900LM-4G is a practical choice for organizations using Plaspy because it balances small physical size with fleet grade features that matter in day to day operations.
