# CanTrack - P901

![P901](./tracker.jpg)

The CanTrack P901 is an ultra thin ID card style GPS tracker designed for wearable use. It combines GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity and built in voice and alarm features in a compact form factor suited to security personnel, event staff and lone workers. The P901 provides location reporting, one key SOS alerts and group push to talk voice communications in a slim device that can be carried or clipped during patrols and mobile operations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the P901 integrates location and status data into Plaspy for live visibility, historical route playback and incident alerting. Its combination of position reporting, SOS and on device voice means Plaspy users can correlate communications with asset location, monitor teams in real time and manage devices remotely through standard platform workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Slim ID card form factor for wearable deployment and discreet carriage
- Real time location reporting using GPS and Beidou with cellular fallback for broad coverage
- Integrated two way calling and group push to talk for immediate team voice communication
- One key SOS alarm with configurable alert behavior to escalate incidents quickly
- On device logging capacity for offline route capture and later upload to Plaspy
- Remote firmware update support and remote configuration to simplify maintenance
- Long life rechargeable battery and configurable upload intervals to balance endurance and reporting frequency

## How It Works with Plaspy

The P901 streams location and event data to Plaspy, allowing the platform to display units on maps, record historical movement and trigger alerts when alarms or status changes occur. Voice traffic and PTT are handled on the device while Plaspy uses position and event metadata to place communications in operational context.

- Real time location and telemetry visible within Plaspy dashboards and map views
- SOS alarms pushed to Plaspy for immediate notification and incident workflows
- Position tagged voice events and group PTT context available in activity logs
- Historical route playback and reporting based on on device stored records uploaded to Plaspy
- Remote device management and configuration initiated from Plaspy compatible workflows or supported command methods

## Typical Use Cases

- Security and patrol teams requiring paired location and group voice communications
- Event staff monitoring where wearable trackers provide quick contact and incident alerts
- Lone worker protection with SOS button, two way communication and remote monitoring
- Mobile supervisors and field teams needing live location plus route history for oversight
- Small asset or personnel tracking when a slim wearable device is preferred
