# GF70L-Solar Magnetic Asset GPS Tracker

The GF70L-Solar is a solar-assisted, rechargeable magnetic asset GPS tracker optimized for long-term, low-maintenance tracking of heavy equipment, trailers, containers and other off‑grid assets. Designed to work with Plaspy, this Plaspy compatible tracker brings extended standby through a 12000mAh rechargeable battery and anti-scratch solar panels, strong magnetic mounting for tool-free installation, and a compact form factor suited to challenging outdoor environments.

Built for real-time tracking, timed reporting and movement-based \(smart\) tracking modes, the GF70L-Solar uploads positions automatically over cellular networks and supports a rich set of alarms including geo-fence, low battery, vibration and anti-removal. Plaspy users can integrate GF70L data for fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and asset telemetry to simplify operations and speed recovery when assets move unexpectedly.

## Key Highlights

- Solar-extended 12000mAh rechargeable battery for long standby and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Magnetic tool-free mounting ideal for trailers, containers and heavy equipment.
- Plaspy compatible — automatic position uploads and configurable alerts for fleet management and anti-theft workflows.
- Multiple tracking modes: real-time, timed interval, and smart movement-based reporting to optimize battery life and data use.
- Comprehensive alarms: geo-fence, low battery, vibration and fall-off \(anti-removal\) to protect unattended assets.
- Optional built-in microphone for one-way remote listening where permitted and required for recovery investigations.
- Multi-band cellular options \(2G / 4G regional variants\) and robust GPS performance with accuracy typically under 10 meters.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GF70L-Solar sends GPS positions and alarm events to Plaspy over cellular data or via configured IP/port settings. Once connected to Plaspy, the device’s location, movement events and status reports appear in real-time on dashboards, enabling live monitoring, automated alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy leverages the device’s reporting modes to balance update frequency and battery life for fleet management and long-term asset tracking.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates when in active tracking mode for immediate visibility.
- Movement-based smart tracking reduces transmissions while preserving accurate recovery traces.
- Geo-fence, vibration and anti-removal alarms forwarded to Plaspy for instant notification and incident workflows.
- Low-battery alerts trigger platform notifications so maintenance teams can plan recharges or inspections.
- Optional one-way remote listening \(built-in microphone\) available for asset recovery investigations where local laws and policies permit.
