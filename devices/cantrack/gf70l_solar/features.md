# CanTrack - GF70L-Solar Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the CanTrack GF70L-Solar with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities you can expect to observe in the Plaspy platform and explains how the tracker’s reporting and alarm features are typically used for asset monitoring, anti-theft workflows, and long-term unattended installations.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, cellular variant and the manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a practical overview and consult the manufacturer documentation for device specific details that may change over time.

## Feature Overview

The GF70L-Solar is a solar-assisted magnetic asset tracker aimed at low-maintenance, long standby monitoring of trailers, containers, heavy equipment and other off-grid assets. Its core value is extending unattended operation while providing configurable reporting modes and alarm events that feed into Plaspy for visibility and incident handling.

- Solar-extended 12000mAh rechargeable battery for reduced maintenance and longer standby between charges
- Strong magnetic tool-free mounting suitable for trailers, containers and heavy machinery surfaces
- Multiple tracking modes including real-time, timed interval, and movement based smart reporting to balance visibility and power use
- Configurable alarms such as geo-fence, low battery, vibration and anti-removal to support theft detection and recovery
- Plaspy compatibility for automatic position uploads, event forwarding and configurable alert workflows

## Core Features of CanTrack - GF70L-Solar

- Solar assisted rechargeable battery rated at 12000mAh for extended unattended operation and fewer maintenance visits
- Magnetic mounting for quick tool-free installation and flexible placement on metal assets
- Multiple reporting modes: real-time tracking, scheduled interval reports, and movement triggered smart tracking
- Alarms and event types including geo-fence triggers, low battery alerts, vibration detection and anti-removal notifications
- Optional one-way listening via a built-in microphone where local rules and policies permit
- Multi-band cellular support with regional 2G and 4G variants to match local networks
- GPS performance designed for reliable positioning with typical accuracy within the manufacturer stated range

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the GF70L-Solar reports position and event data, Plaspy ingests those updates and makes them available across dashboards, alerts and history views. Plaspy is designed to present device locations and notable events in ways that help operations and security teams act quickly.

- Real-time and active tracking updates appear in Plaspy for immediate location visibility
- Movement based smart tracking reduces transmissions while preserving traces needed for recovery and auditing
- Alarms such as geo-fence breaches, vibration and anti-removal are forwarded to Plaspy for notification and incident workflows
- Low battery reports surface in Plaspy so maintenance teams can schedule inspections or recharges
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and integrates reported positions and status without requiring manual protocol selection

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers and containers where solar recharge reduces service visits
- Monitoring heavy equipment and attachments across job sites to improve utilization and reduce loss
- Construction site security using vibration and anti-removal alarms to detect unauthorized movement
- Theft recovery workflows that rely on movement triggered and real-time reporting for rapid location identification
- Rental fleet oversight to monitor location and battery state without frequent manual checks
- Remote asset visibility for off-grid installations where low maintenance and reliable reporting matter
