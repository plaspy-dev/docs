# CanTrack - TK100B

![TK100B](./tracker.jpg)

The TK100B Accurate Pro from Cantrack is a Plaspy compatible, hard-wired GPS tracker engineered for reliable in-vehicle installation and continuous real-time tracking. As the Pro evolution of the GT06/TK100 family, the TK100B brings professional-grade telemetry, anti-theft controls and remote vehicle management features — delivered over GSM/GPRS with TCP/IP — to fleets, taxis and private vehicle owners who require dependable location data and secure remote control.

Designed for fleet management and vehicle security, the TK100B supports two-way calling, SOS, geo-fencing, car-finding and one-key engine cut/restore. The unit’s accessory support — wireless remote controller, external horn \(siren\), relay for ignition/fuel cut and an external microphone — expands anti-theft capability and alerting, while Plaspy integration provides centralized dashboards, alerts and remote command workflows for fast operational response and better asset protection.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivering continuous real-time tracking over GSM/GPRS \(TCP/IP\) for fleet management and personal vehicles.
- Advanced anti-theft tools: one-key engine cut \(fuel cut\) and remote engine restore via relay control, plus wireless remote controller and external siren support.
- Two-way voice monitoring with external microphone for hands-free listening and driver communications or security checks.
- Professional MTK2503AV chipset and quad-band GSM \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) for broad cellular coverage and stable telemetry.
- Compact hard-wired form factor \(97 × 51 × 15 mm; 80 g\) designed for discreet vehicle installation on 12–36 V systems.
- Fast and accurate GNSS: -159 dBm sensitivity, ~10 m accuracy and predictable TTFF \(cold/warm/hot\) for dependable location fixes.
- Core telemetry and alerting: SOS, geo-fence alerts, car-finding, anti-theft arm/disarm and ACC/ignition sensing for richer vehicle data.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TK100B transmits location and vehicle telemetry to Plaspy over GSM/GPRS using TCP/IP. Plaspy ingests that data to provide real-time tracking, historical playback, configurable alerts and remote control actions. Fleet operators and vehicle owners use Plaspy to monitor movement, receive SOS or geo-fence notifications, and execute secure remote commands such as engine cut/restore through the device’s relay interface.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via TCP/IP over GSM/GPRS.
- Ignition/ACC detection and relay control for remote immobilizer \(fuel/engine cut\) actions.
- SOS button and geo-fencing alerts routed to Plaspy for immediate notifications and incident handling.
- Two-way voice and external microphone capability for remote listening and driver communication through supported Plaspy workflows.
- Accessory status and external siren/remote controller actions are reported and can be triggered or logged in Plaspy.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Paired with Plaspy, the TK100B delivers a practical balance of reliable real-time tracking and proven anti-theft controls. The MTK2503AV chipset and quad-band GSM support ensure broad cellular connectivity while the unit’s relay-based engine cut, wireless remote controller and external siren options give operators decisive remote-control capability. For fleet management, the TK100B supplies essential telemetry — ignition state, SOS events, geo-fence breaches and accessory signals — into Plaspy’s dashboards so teams can act quickly on alerts, perform remote immobilization when needed, and maintain historical records for compliance and analysis.

Plaspy-compatible deployment of TK100B is ideal when you need a compact, professional hard-wired GPS tracker that emphasizes vehicle security and operational control. For broader telemetry needs such as dedicated fuel-level sensing or environmental BLE sensors, Plaspy can combine TK100B data with additional telemetry inputs to produce a complete monitoring solution. Installation should be performed by qualified personnel to ensure correct wiring for ignition sensing, ACC detection and relay control, unlocking the full potential of TK100B with Plaspy's real-time tracking and management features.
