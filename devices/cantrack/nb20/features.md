# CanTrack - NB20 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the CanTrack NB20 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on non sensitive, user facing information about positioning technologies, daily operational features, and the ways NB20 data appears in Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and history review.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. Where the NB20 supports multiple modes or connectivity options, those functions may depend on the device firmware and the way the unit is configured by the manufacturer or installer.

## Feature Overview

The NB20 is a compact 4G Bluetooth smart pet tracker designed to deliver hybrid indoor and outdoor positioning while remaining easy to attach to collars and harnesses. Its multi technology approach helps provide continuous awareness in typical pet and small animal scenarios, with practical functions aimed at owners and caregivers.

- Hybrid positioning using GPS GNSS outdoors plus Bluetooth 5.0 LE and Wi Fi assistance for indoor locating
- Lightweight water resistant design suitable for collars and harnesses with remote controlled LED night light
- Multiple working modes to balance power and location update frequency including a long Bluetooth standby mode
- Real time tracking, geo fence alerts, low battery notifications and history playback visible inside Plaspy
- Cellular connectivity via Nano SIM or eSIM with LTE CAT M1 and NB2 support for broad low power wide area coverage

## Core Features of CanTrack - NB20

- GPS GNSS positioning for outdoor location with fast time to first fix and sub 2 meter quoted accuracy
- Bluetooth 5.0 LE for low energy standby, nearby locating and accessory pairing
- Wi Fi assisted indoor positioning to improve location accuracy when GPS signals are weak or unavailable
- Cellular uplink using Nano SIM or eSIM with LTE CAT M1 and CAT NB2 for consistent cloud reporting
- Multiple operating modes including Bluetooth standby with extended battery life and higher frequency tracking modes
- Remote controlled LED night light for improved visibility during low light conditions
- History trace playback and geo fence event support for reviewing past movement and zone alerts
- Water resistant IPX7 form factor and a compact lightweight design optimized for pet use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the NB20 is connected to Plaspy, location and status reports feed into the Plaspy platform so owners and managers can monitor pets in real time, receive alerts, and review history. Plaspy presents hybrid positioning results and device status in maps, reports, and notifications to support everyday tracking and response.

- Real time location updates and device status appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring
- Indoor location hints from Bluetooth and Wi Fi are combined with GPS outdoors to provide continuous coverage in Plaspy
- Geo fence alerts and low battery notifications are pushed to Plaspy so you can receive timely messages and audit trails
- History trace playback in Plaspy lets you review routes, walking patterns, and past events
- Bluetooth OTA update capability and device status shown in Plaspy help with remote maintenance and firmware management
- Plaspy compatibility enables straightforward onboarding so NB20 telemetry and events are visible without custom platform work

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet anti loss and recovery with geo fence notifications when a pet leaves a defined safe zone
- Daily walk tracking and history review to inspect routes and behavior over time
- Nighttime safety with LED night light control and visibility information
- Remote monitoring for kennels, boarding facilities or caregivers with live location and low battery alerts
- Indoor tracking in homes or shelters using Bluetooth and Wi Fi assistance for better accuracy
- Temporary or travel use where eSIM or Nano SIM connectivity provides broad cellular coverage

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware matters: mode names, timing, and exact capabilities depend on the NB20 firmware version and manufacturer settings
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect supported LTE bands, GNSS performance, and accessory compatibility
- Installation and mounting method influence reception and battery life so placement on a collar or harness affects real world results
- Battery life figures vary with selected working mode, update frequency, cellular connectivity and environmental factors
- Verify regional cellular support and device configuration with the manufacturer for deployments that require specific network bands
