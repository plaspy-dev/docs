# CanTrack - G02M

![G02M](./tracker.jpg)

The G02M Pet Tracker is a compact, lightweight GPS tracker designed for reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking of pets and other small targets. With GPS/GSM/GPRS connectivity and support for SMS-based location replies, the G02M makes it simple to locate a pet or portable asset from your phone or through Plaspy’s server-based platform. Its small form factor and 300 mAh rechargeable battery are optimized for daily use on collars or carried items where size and comfort matter.

Built to report position by SMS \(Google Maps links\) or to transmit continuous location data to a tracking server via GPRS, the G02M delivers straightforward telemetry, geofence alerts, and low-battery alarms. Because the unit supports server upload over GPRS and standard coordinate formats, it integrates easily with Plaspy for live monitoring, configurable alerts, and historical route playback — making it a practical GPS tracker choice for personal and small-asset tracking needs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: Sends GPRS data to tracking servers for live maps and history on Plaspy platforms.
- Real-time tracking: GPS + WiFi-assisted positioning and LBS fallback provide continuous location updates.
- SMS quick-view: Call or SMS the tracker to receive an instant Google Maps link with latitude/longitude.
- Compact, lightweight design \(≈4.3 × 3.7 × 1.5 cm; 40 g\) ideal for pet collars and handheld use.
- Useful alerts: Geofence notifications and low-battery alarms help protect pets and small assets.
- Configurable reporting: Adjustable upload intervals balance tracking frequency with battery life.
- Convenient charging: Rechargeable 300 mAh battery with USB or docking-station charging for ease of daily use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The G02M delivers location and basic telemetry to Plaspy by using standard GPRS data uploads or via SMS links for immediate lookups. Once configured to send data to your Plaspy server endpoint, the device reports coordinates and status at the upload interval you set. Plaspy then plots positions, triggers configured alerts \(such as geofence breaches\), and retains historical tracks for review and reporting.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over GPRS to Plaspy-compatible servers.
- SMS location reply with a Google Maps link for instant lookup from any phone.
- Geofence alerts forwarded to Plaspy so you can receive notifications, emails, or mobile app push messages.
- Low battery alarms reported to Plaspy for proactive device maintenance.
- Configurable upload intervals allow you to tune data frequency for battery life versus tracking resolution.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G02M offers a focused, cost-effective solution when you need a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for pets and small assets. Its compact size and simple setup—call or SMS the device for instant coordinates or point it at your Plaspy server for continuous GPRS uploads—make it practical for everyday use. The combination of GPS, WiFi-assisted positioning, and LBS ensures better coverage indoors and out, while geofence and low-battery alerts keep you informed and in control.

Note on advanced fleet features: the G02M is optimized for personal and pet tracking rather than full fleet management. While Plaspy supports telemetry-driven fleet workflows including fuel monitoring, ignition status and immobilizer control on other compatible devices, the G02M’s strengths lie in compact form factor, reliable real-time location reporting, and straightforward integration for small-scale tracking needs. If your requirements extend to vehicle telemetry or fuel monitoring, Plaspy can pair you with compatible trackers that offer those interfaces.
