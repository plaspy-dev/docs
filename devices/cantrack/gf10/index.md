# CanTrack - GF10

![GF10](./Logo.png)

The CanTrack GF10 Finger Tracker is a compact and versatile GPS tracking device designed for discreet location monitoring. At roughly finger size, the GF10 provides real time location updates using a combination of GPS, LBS, and AGPS technologies. It supports access to position information through SMS, platform access, or a mobile application, and includes features such as geofence alerts, low battery notification, vibration tamper alert, power saving modes, and local memory storage for periods without GSM signal.

Because the GF10 stores location data when network connectivity is lost and supports multiple reporting paths, it is a practical candidate for use with Plaspy as a tracked device. Plaspy can ingest and display the GF10 location data on its mapping and reporting layers, deliver notifications based on geofence and movement events, and include the GF10 in device groups and operational monitoring workflows for fleets or individual assets.

## Key Highlights

- Very small, discreet form factor suited to concealed placement and portable tracking
- Real time location using multiple positioning methods for broader coverage
- Data available via SMS or platform and mobile app access
- Geofence alerts and vibration alarm for movement or tamper detection
- Low battery alarm plus power saving and sleep modes to extend standby time
- Local memory storage preserves location records when GSM signal is unavailable
- Typical standby time of around 5 to 6 days under normal conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy can present GF10 data as a regular tracked device so operators see live and historical locations, receive alerts, and include the unit in reporting and monitoring workflows. Integration focuses on visibility, alerting, and operational oversight rather than on low level device configuration.

- Live position display and breadcrumb history on Plaspy maps for route review
- Geofence event alerts routed into Plaspy notifications for boundary monitoring
- Low battery and vibration movement alerts surfaced to operators for timely action
- Historical data and stored locations made visible when the device reconnects after a signal outage
- Device grouping and tagging in Plaspy to support fleet segmentation and asset categorization

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet tracking of valuable personal items or portable equipment
- Vehicle or small asset location monitoring where a compact tracker is preferred
- Short term or ad hoc monitoring needs that require portable placement
- Monitoring of individuals or companions when consent and local rules permit
- Situations where intermittent connectivity requires local storage of location data

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GF10 combines a very small footprint with a set of practical tracking features that make it suitable for both personal and light commercial uses. Its multi method positioning and memory storage improve chances of useful location data in mixed connectivity environments, and power saving features help extend operational time between charges or maintenance cycles.

When used with Plaspy, the GF10 becomes part of a broader monitoring and reporting environment. Plaspy adds centralized visibility, event management, and historical reporting that help turn the GF10 location feed into actionable operational insight for teams managing assets, personnel, or small vehicle fleets.
