# CanTrack - GT06

<div className="device-logo">
![GT06](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CanTrack GT06, also sold as the TK100, is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed to locate and monitor mobile targets using GPS satellites and the GSM GPRS network. It captures positional data and can provide immediate location information via SMS to authorized numbers, display positions on Google Maps or Google Earth, and transmit data over GPRS to an internet server for real time visibility on a computer.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GT06 is well suited for organizations that want to centralize location data, alerts, and basic reporting. Its combination of SMS reporting and server transmission makes it a practical option for teams that need both a phone based fallback and continuous online tracking, while Plaspy can consolidate device feeds into a single dashboard for fleet and asset oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Compact vehicle focused design for discreet installation and everyday use
- Built in GPS and GSM modules to capture location and report via SMS or GPRS
- Real time tracking visibility on maps when data is routed to an internet server
- Built in alarms including overspeed, SOS, ACC anti theft, and power failure alert
- Optional functions available such as two way calling and remote cut vehicle or circuit
- Quad band GSM support for broad regional compatibility

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to send its GPRS data to a compatible server endpoint, the GT06 can feed live location updates into Plaspy so vehicles and assets appear on Plaspy maps and fleet views. SMS reporting remains available for immediate location notices to authorized phones, while Plaspy provides centralized monitoring, history, and operational context.

- Live map tracking in Plaspy for devices transmitting location data to the server
- Alarm and event ingestion so overspeed, SOS, and power loss events are visible and actionable in Plaspy
- Historical route playback and basic reporting to review movements over time
- Fleet level dashboards and status indicators for grouped monitoring of multiple GT06 units
- Use SMS as a fallback notification channel while Plaspy serves as the primary centralized tracking platform

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and vehicle rental monitoring for small and medium fleets
- Protecting high value mobile assets with alerting and location recovery support
- Executive or business owner peace of mind through SOS and location reporting
- Personnel or field staff tracking to improve operational oversight and coordination
- Delivery, service, and short term vehicle lease tracking to monitor assignments and routes

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GT06 is a practical choice for organizations that need a straightforward vehicle tracker with a mix of SMS fallback and server based reporting. Its alarm set and optional features offer flexibility for basic security and operational controls, while its compact form factor suits many vehicle installations. For teams using Plaspy, the GT06 provides a reliable stream of positional data and events that Plaspy can unify into maps, alerts, and reports.
