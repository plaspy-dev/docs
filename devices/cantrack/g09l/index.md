# CanTrack - G09L

![G09L](./tracker.jpg)

The G09L by an established telematics manufacturer is a DOT-certified oval-type smart tail light GPS tracker engineered for trucks and heavy vehicles. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the G09L combines conventional tail light functions \(turn, brake, night lamp\) with an internal GPS module, G-sensor and a 3.7 V/5000 mAh backup battery so you can replace the vehicle’s original tail light and gain discrete, always-on real-time tracking and driving telemetry.

Built for heavy-duty fleet management, theft recovery and insurance telematics, the G09L runs on 4G LTE Cat M1/Cat NB2 \(Quectel BG95\) and integrates with cloud portals such as Cantrack and SecumorePlus. Rugged IP67 housing, DOT certification and FOTA support make it a practical choice for fleets that require integrated lighting, reliable positioning and remote firmware management while leveraging Plaspy for centralized tracking, alerts and reports.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for centralized real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Discrete oval tail-light form factor replaces original lights for covert installation.
- Integrated GPS + BeiDou positioning with &lt;5 m accuracy and fast warm starts.
- 4G LTE \(Quectel BG95 Cat M1 / Cat NB2\) connectivity for wide-area coverage and low-power operation.
- Built-in G-sensor for driving behavior monitoring, crash alarm and harsh-event detection.
- Internal 3.7 V / 5000 mAh backup battery plus optional 16000 mAh power bank support.
- IP67 rugged housing, DOT and FCC certification for heavy vehicle deployments.
- FOTA updates and SMS configuration for remote management and rapid fleet rollouts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The G09L transmits position and telemetry to cloud platforms and is configured to forward data to Plaspy by setting the device server IP/port and APN via SMS or the portal. Once provisioned, Plaspy receives live GPS fixes, G-sensor events \(harsh acceleration/deceleration, crash alarms\), geo-fence alerts and device status updates so you can monitor vehicles in real time, playback history and generate reports for fleet optimization.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GPS + BeiDou\) for accurate tracking.
- Crash alarms and driving behavior events \(harsh acceleration/deceleration\) reported to Plaspy for incident handling.
- Geo-fencing alerts and history playback for route compliance and incident investigation.
- Remote vehicle-finding \(beep\) command supported for quick recovery and on-site locating.
- Remote firmware updates \(FOTA\) and SMS command configuration for large-scale fleet maintenance.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the G09L with Plaspy gives fleet operators a discrete, compliant and rugged solution that minimizes installation complexity while delivering actionable telemetry. The oval tail-light design replaces existing lamps for a low-visibility install, the Quectel BG95 cellular module provides wide LTE Cat M1 / NB2 coverage with low-power operation, and the internal G-sensor and backup battery maintain critical reporting even when primary power is interrupted. With FOTA support, SMS-based remote configuration and compatibility with Cantrack and SecumorePlus portals, fleets can scale deployments, push updates and consolidate vehicle location, crash alarms and driving behavior into Plaspy’s dashboards for real-time decision-making.

For operations that require DOT-certified hardware, IP67 durability and ready integration into Plaspy’s platform, the G09L delivers a purpose-built mix of lighting and tracking. Use it where you need unobtrusive installation, reliable positioning, crash detection and centralized fleet telemetry — and configure reporting intervals, APN and server endpoints to match your Plaspy deployment and operational policies.
