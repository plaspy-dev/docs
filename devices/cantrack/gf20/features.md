# CanTrack - GF20 Features

This page provides a public overview of the CanTrack GF20 feature set and how those features are used when the device reports into Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about the GF20 as a mini magnet GPS tracker and explains which capabilities are visible and actionable inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and the way the device is installed. Wherever possible this page uses the manufacturer supplied description as the grounding for device capabilities and advises verifying device specific details with official CanTrack documentation.

## Feature Overview

The GF20 is a compact magnet mounted tracker intended for discreet and short term monitoring. It combines basic location technologies with several convenience features that make it suitable for temporary asset monitoring and discreet vehicle tracking. When used with Plaspy the GF20 can report position, alarms, and stored data for operational visibility.

- Small magnetized form factor for discreet placement and temporary installs
- Real GPS with LBS and AGPS assist for location reporting across conditions
- Remote tracking access via SMS platform and mobile app methods as supported by the device
- Geo fence support to generate boundary alerts for monitored assets
- Low battery and vibration alarms to indicate power state changes or potential tampering
- Optional voice remote listen and voice recording capability for audio monitoring where enabled

## Core Features of CanTrack - GF20

- Compact mini magnet housing for easy and discreet placement
- Real GPS plus LBS and AGPS for combined location methods
- Multiple reporting and access options including SMS and platform or app tracking
- Geo fence support for virtual boundary monitoring and alerts
- Low battery alarm to notify of depleted power and extend oversight
- Vibration alarm to detect movement or possible tamper events
- Optional voice remote listen and voice recording for audio monitoring where available
- Internal memory storage to retain location data when GSM is not available

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and displays the events and position reports that the GF20 sends, giving teams centralized visibility and historical context for each device. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and surfaces received events in the platform for monitoring and investigation.

- Live and historical location points appear on Plaspy maps as the GF20 reports position updates
- Geo fence triggers received from the device are shown as platform alerts so teams can act on boundary events
- Low battery and vibration alarms are presented as device events to help manage device health and possible tampering
- Stored locations buffered while GSM was unavailable are uploaded and reconciled in Plaspy when the device reconnects
- Voice listen or audio related events reported by the device are surfaced as events when the audio reporting is forwarded to the platform
- Device status and recent telemetry allow ops teams to confirm the GF20 is online and reporting as expected

## Typical Use Cases

- Short term tracking of a vehicle for temporary monitoring or recovery
- Discreet placement on personal items or portable assets for short duration oversight
- Asset movement verification across a journey or rental period
- Boundary monitoring for defined locations with geo fence alerts
- Situations where temporary, removable tracking is preferred over permanent installation

## Feature Availability Notes

- Voice remote listen and recording may be optional depending on the specific GF20 unit and regional variant
- Memory storage behavior and how buffered data is uploaded depend on firmware and configuration
- Battery standby time of 5 to 8 days will vary with reporting frequency and feature usage
- Geo fence, SMS reporting, and app platform features can be affected by local network conditions and service configuration
- Confirm exact capabilities and any regional differences by consulting the manufacturer documentation for the device model

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the CanTrack GF20 centralizes device reporting and event management, turning individual tracker signals into operational insight. Plaspy helps teams monitor locations on a map, receive and act on alarm events, and reconcile historical position data that the device stored while offline.
