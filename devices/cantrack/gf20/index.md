# CanTrack - GF20

<div className="device-logo">
![GF20](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The CanTrack GF20 is a mini magnet GPS tracker designed for discreet and short term tracking. At roughly 50mm by 27mm by 25mm it is compact enough to fit into a small bag or be concealed on a vehicle, making it suitable for temporary monitoring of personal items or assets. The device supports real GPS, LBS, and AGPS positioning and offers features like geo fencing, low battery and vibration alarms, an optional voice remote listen function, and multiple working modes to match different tracking needs.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GF20 can be integrated into a centralized tracking workflow to provide continuous visibility and event alerts alongside other devices. Plaspy can receive location updates and status events from the GF20 so teams can monitor movement, review history, and manage geofence alerts from a single platform. The GF20's data buffering during signal loss and its standby characteristics make it a practical choice for users who need temporary, discreet monitoring combined with Plaspy's operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Compact magnet form factor for discreet placement and short term deployments
- Multiple positioning methods including real GPS plus LBS and AGPS for flexible location reporting
- Optional voice remote listen capability for added situational awareness
- Geo fence, vibration alarm, and low battery alerts for proactive notifications
- Data memory for storing location records when cellular signal is lost
- Standby time in the range of several days to support temporary monitoring assignments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the GF20 becomes part of a unified tracking environment where device location and status are visible alongside other fleet and asset data. Plaspy can consolidate GF20 updates into dashboards, notifications, and historical records to support operational decision making.

- Real time location visibility and map tracking of GF20 devices within Plaspy
- Geofence configuration and alerts triggered when the GF20 enters or exits defined areas
- Event notifications for low battery and vibration alerts routed through Plaspy notification channels
- Historical playback and reporting using stored device positions uploaded after signal restoration
- Grouping and device management to organize multiple GF20 units for asset fleets or temporary deployments

## Typical Use Cases

- Short term tracking of personal belongings during travel or storage
- Discreet vehicle monitoring for temporary surveillance or recovery purposes
- Tracking portable assets and rental equipment while in transit or on site
- Temporary monitoring for investigative or security tasks requiring a compact device
- Short duration tracking of small cargo or consignments that need periodic checks

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GF20 is a practical option when discreet size and short term deployment are primary concerns. Its magnetized enclosure and compact footprint make it easy to position where visibility is limited, while features such as geo fencing and alarms support basic security workflows. Combined with Plaspy, the GF20's location and event data can be managed centrally, enabling teams to monitor movement, receive timely alerts, and review historical activity from one platform.
