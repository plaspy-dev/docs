# CanTrack - GF20-Mini

<div className="device-logo">
![GF20-Mini](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The GF20 Mini Magnet GPS Tracker is a compact, discreet asset tracker designed for short term covert tracking of vehicles, containers, and personal belongings. It provides real time tracking, geo fence alerts, and event notifications via SMS or platform messages, and supports simple integration with fleet or asset monitoring systems. Its small magnetic housing and focused feature set make it suitable for situations where rapid deployment and unobtrusive placement are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GF20-Mini can feed location fixes and event notifications into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. When paired with Plaspy, the device’s alerts, stored position history, and status messages are presented alongside other fleet telemetry to help operations, security, and recovery workflows act on timely location information.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for consolidated real time tracking and reporting within a single platform.
- Ultra compact magnetic housing for discreet placement under vehicles or on metal assets.
- High sensitivity MTK GNSS positioning with reliable location accuracy for operational visibility.
- Quad band GSM reporting and SMS support for broad cellular coverage and messaging.
- Onboard memory stores positions during connectivity interruptions to preserve history.
- Multiple alert types including geo fence, low battery, and vibration alarm for security workflows.
- Optional remote voice listening and voice recording to assist investigation and recovery efforts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the GF20-Mini delivers location updates and event messages into the Plaspy platform where they become part of the live map view, alerting engine, and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s supported reporting channels to provide operators with a unified overview of assets and incidents.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy through the tracker’s supported reporting channels.
- Geo fence triggers and vibration alarms forwarded to Plaspy as event notifications for immediate action.
- Stored position history synchronized with Plaspy once connectivity is restored, preserving continuity of records.
- Low battery and status messages surfaced in Plaspy to help maintain device availability.
- Optional voice event logs can be associated with incidents in Plaspy for investigation workflows.
- Plaspy can correlate GF20 position data with other fleet inputs and integrated telemetry when those sources are available.

## Typical Use Cases

- Temporary vehicle tracking for rental fleets, short term monitoring, or rapid deployment needs.
- Anti theft and recovery operations where discreet placement and timely alerts aid recovery.
- Container and equipment security with magnetic mounting for transit and storage monitoring.
- Covert investigations and surveillance that require a small footprint and optional audio monitoring.
- Monitoring of valuable personal items or small assets during short term movements.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GF20-Mini is a practical choice when discretion, quick deployment, and reliable position history are priorities. Its magnetic compact design and onboard storage help maintain tracking continuity through short connectivity interruptions, while configurable alerts support security and operational response. Integrated with Plaspy, the device becomes part of a centralized monitoring workflow that simplifies oversight of multiple assets and incidents.
