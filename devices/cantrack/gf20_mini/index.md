# CanTrack - GF20-Mini

![GF20-Mini](./tracker.jpg)

The GF20 Mini Magnet GPS Tracker is a compact, discreet asset tracker designed for short-term covert tracking of vehicles, containers, and personal belongings. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the GF20 Mini delivers reliable real-time tracking, geo-fence alerts, and event notifications through SMS, platform integration, or mobile app updates—making it an ideal choice when you need quick deployment and simple integration with your Plaspy fleet management or asset monitoring setup.

With a strong magnetic housing and a very small form factor \(approx. 50 x 27 x 25 mm\), the GF20 Mini combines MTK high-sensitivity GNSS positioning with quad-band GSM communications to maintain accurate location history even when network conditions fluctuate. Onboard memory records positions during GSM outages, while configurable working modes, low battery warnings, and optional voice listening/recording add operational flexibility for anti-theft and security use cases.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates with Plaspy-enabled platforms for consolidated real-time tracking and reporting.
- Ultra compact magnetic housing for covert placement under vehicles or inside metal enclosures.
- High-sensitivity MTK GPS chipset with \<10 m CEP accuracy and -165 dBm tracking sensitivity for dependable location fixes.
- Quad-band GSM \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) for global cellular coverage and SMS or platform reporting.
- Onboard memory stores position data during GSM outages to preserve location history and telemetry continuity.
- Multiple alerts: geo-fence, low battery, and vibration alarm to support anti-theft and security workflows.
- Optional voice remote listening and voice recording for enhanced investigation and recovery capability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GF20 Mini sends location and event data via SMS or platform/app messages that can be routed into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting. When integrated, Plaspy processes the device's GPS fixes, geo-fence triggers, and status messages to provide a unified view for fleet management, asset tracking, and security operations.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy through the tracker’s supported reporting channels \(SMS or platform/app\).
- Geo-fence alerts and vibration alarms are forwarded to Plaspy as event notifications for immediate action.
- Onboard memory ensures position history is retained during GSM outages and synchronized with Plaspy when connectivity is restored.
- Optional remote voice listening and recording events can be logged in Plaspy as part of incident investigation workflows.
- Plaspy can correlate GF20 position data with other telemetry sources \(fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer status, Bluetooth sensors\) when those inputs are available from device fleets or integrated systems, enabling richer analytics and asset control.

## Use Cases

- Temporary vehicle tracking for rental fleets or short-term monitoring where discreet installation is required.
- Anti-theft and recovery: vibration alarms, geo-fence notifications, and position history help locate stolen vehicles or assets.
- Container and equipment security: magnetic mount allows quick attachment to metal surfaces and continuous logging while in transit.
- Covert investigations and surveillance where a small form factor and optional remote audio monitoring are needed for evidence gathering.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choose the GF20 Mini when you need a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that balances discretion, reliable positioning, and simple integration. Its compact magnetic housing makes deployment fast and unobtrusive, while the MTK GNSS chipset, strong tracking sensitivity, and onboard memory ensure accurate real-time tracking and continuous record keeping even through short GSM outages. When paired with Plaspy, the GF20 Mini becomes part of a scalable fleet management and monitoring solution—delivering actionable alerts for anti-theft response, centralized telemetry for operational oversight, and historical location data for compliance or incident review.

For organizations seeking to centralize GPS tracker data, Plaspy compatibility means the GF20 Mini can feed location, geo-fence, and event data into a single dashboard alongside other telemetry types \(fuel monitoring, ignition/immobilizer events, Bluetooth sensors\) when those inputs are available across your device fleet—helping you streamline operations, reduce loss, and improve situational awareness.
