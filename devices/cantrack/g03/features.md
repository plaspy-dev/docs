# CanTrack - G03 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the CanTrack G03 and how its capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on user-facing functions, operational value, and how the device reports location and events into Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and history playback.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, regional module variant, and manufacturer implementation. For mission critical details and the latest specifications consult CanTrack documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The CanTrack G03 is a compact, purpose-built GPS tracker optimized for personal safety and portable asset protection. It is designed for low-power operation and straightforward integration with Plaspy, delivering real-time tracking, event alerts, and route history suitable for caregivers, family members, and managers.

- Compact and durable form factor with ABS housing and IP65 water resistance for everyday portable use.
- Real-time tracking and history playback on Plaspy for continuous visibility and retrospective review.
- Dedicated SOS event to notify pre-set contacts and register an alert within Plaspy for escalation.
- Triple positioning support (GPS + LBS + AGPS) with GPS/BDS positioning and typical sub-5 meter accuracy in good conditions.
- Long standby operation with a 3000 mAh battery and intelligent sleep modes plus low battery alarms to extend runtime.

## Core Features of CanTrack - G03

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for quick onboarding and immediate visibility on maps and dashboards.
- Dedicated SOS button that generates emergency events both to configured phone contacts and as an event in Plaspy.
- Triple positioning using GPS, LBS, and AGPS plus support for GPS and BDS satellite systems for robust location fixes.
- Durable IP65 ABS enclosure suitable for attachment to wheelchairs, personal carry items, and portable assets.
- Long standby capability driven by a 3000 mAh battery and configurable low-power sleep modes.
- Low battery alerts and status reporting to help caregivers and managers plan recharging or replacement.
- Real-time telemetry and historical route playback to support incident review and caregiver reporting.
- Compact dimensions intended for discreet, everyday use and easy mounting.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the G03 is connected to Plaspy, location and event signals are processed into platform-level visibility, notifications, and historical records. Plaspy provides the interface and tools to turn device telemetry into actionable operational insight.

- Real-time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps and dashboards for immediate situational awareness.
- SOS events generated by the device are recorded in Plaspy and can trigger notifications or escalation workflows.
- Low battery and sleep mode events are surfaced in Plaspy so caregivers receive timely alerts before tracking is interrupted.
- Geofence entry and exit events can be configured in Plaspy to monitor safe areas and restricted zones.
- History trace playback in Plaspy enables route review, incident analysis, and reporting based on stored device positions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Wheelchair user monitoring where caregivers need live location, SOS alerts, and route history for safety.
- Personal safety for elderly family members or children with one-press SOS and long standby between charges.
- Portable asset protection for bags, small equipment, and foldable mobility aids requiring a compact, water resistant tracker.
- Short-term monitoring for outdoor day trips or supervised excursions when low maintenance tracking is needed.
- Caregiver and community health programs that require unobtrusive devices and timely event notification.
