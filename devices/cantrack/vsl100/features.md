# CanTrack - VSL100 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the CanTrack VSL100 and explains how its capabilities map to use within Plaspy. It focuses on non sensitive, user facing details about tracking, speed governance, tamper and anti-jammer features, onsite evidence printing, and the kinds of telemetry the device can send to Plaspy for fleet monitoring and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, country variant and installation method. Manufacturers may change firmware behavior or add options, so the summary below describes the typical, publicly documented capabilities of the VSL100 as they relate to Plaspy integration.

## Feature Overview

The CanTrack VSL100 combines enforceable speed limiting with high-accuracy GNSS tracking and tamper protections, designed for heavy vehicles and fleets that require documented compliance and real time oversight. In practical use the device provides both operational controls and auditable records that feed into Plaspy for centralized monitoring.

- Enforceable speed governance with configurable preset limits and pre-warning behavior to reduce overspeed incidents.
- Real time GNSS tracking with high sensitivity and reported position accuracy for continuous location awareness in Plaspy.
- Tamper resistance and anti-jammer detection that generate alarms when interference or signal loss is detected.
- Onsite portable mini-printer for printing recent violation reports to provide physical evidence during inspections.
- Multiple configuration and data retrieval methods including PC tool, GPRS, SMS and USB flash download of recent historical data.
- Short term backup power and wide operating voltage to maintain reporting during brief power interruptions.

## Core Features of CanTrack - VSL100

- Enforceable speed limiting and governance with configurable preset maximums and pre-warning thresholds aligned to fleet policy.
- High accuracy GNSS tracking with a multi channel receiver for reliable position updates and improved fix performance.
- Tamper resistant enclosure and tamper detection logic that triggers alarms when tampering is suspected.
- Anti-jammer detection with alarm reporting when signal interference or extended signal loss is observed.
- Portable mini-printer support enabling one hour violation reports to be produced at roadside for inspectors or drivers.
- Multiple configuration channels including PC configuration tools, remote GPRS configuration and SMS commands.
- Local data logging and USB flash download of recent historical data for offline audit and analysis.
- Backup battery and wide input voltage range to preserve short term operation during power interruptions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the VSL100 is integrated with Plaspy, the device streams location, status and event information so fleet operators can view real time telemetry, audit violations and configure alerts centrally. Plaspy ingests the device events and preserves historical records for reporting and operational workflows.

- Real time location and tracking updates appear in Plaspy dashboards, enabling continuous visibility of vehicle positions.
- Speed governance events and recorded violations are logged in Plaspy so operators can review incidents and produce compliance reports.
- Tamper and anti-jammer alarms are delivered to Plaspy as events, allowing administrators to investigate and respond to potential interference.
- Historical data retrieved from the device or uploaded into Plaspy supports post incident analysis and offline audits.
- Onsite printed violation reports provide immediate physical evidence while Plaspy maintains the canonical electronic event history.

## Typical Use Cases

- Enforcing corporate or regulatory speed limits across heavy truck and delivery fleets to reduce risk and document compliance.
- Monitoring construction vehicles and site equipment where controlled speeds and immediate alerts improve on site safety.
- Managing transport of hazardous or regulated goods where tamper detection and auditable violation records are required.
- Supporting passenger transport and coach fleets with documented infractions and centralized incident reporting for regulators.
- Integrating real time tracking and speed events into dispatch and logistics workflows to improve route decisions and response.
- Producing forensic reports for compliance audits by combining device logs and Plaspy historical records.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Exact functions and parameter ranges depend on the device firmware version and manufacturer configuration; behavior can change between firmware releases.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect available interfaces, supported bands or bundled accessories such as the mini-printer.
- Installation choices and vehicle wiring can influence which sensors and outputs are active; verify installed options during deployment.
- Some event thresholds and enforcement behaviors are configurable and may be set by the installer or fleet administrator.
