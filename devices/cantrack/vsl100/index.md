# CanTrack - VSL100

<div className="device-logo">
![VSL100](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The VSL100 by CanTrack is a next generation GPS speed limiter and tracker engineered for heavy vehicles and fleet management. It combines computerized speed governance with high accuracy real time location tracking so operators can enforce preset maximum speeds, capture violations as they occur, and collect telemetry for centralized oversight. The device is purpose built for logistics fleets, construction machinery, dangerous goods carriers and passenger transport where controlled speeds, tamper resistance and auditable proof of infractions are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the VSL100 can feed its location updates, governor events and alarm signals into Plaspy for unified monitoring and reporting. Plaspy users can incorporate VSL100 telemetry into dashboards, alerts and historical reports to improve operational visibility, manage compliance processes, and respond quickly to incidents across a mixed fleet.

## Key Highlights

- Combines enforceable speed limiting with real time GPS tracking for simultaneous safety control and location visibility.
- Built for heavy vehicle and fleet use with tamper resistant design and anti jammer detection to support operational integrity.
- On the spot evidence via a portable mini printer that produces recent violation reports for roadside inspections.
- High sensitivity GNSS performance for reliable position updates suitable for continuous fleet monitoring in Plaspy.
- Multiple configuration and data retrieval options to support both remote management and offline auditing.
- Designed to maintain short term operation during power interruptions with built in backup power support and wide input voltage range.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the VSL100 streams location, status and violation events to the platform so operators receive live situational awareness and an auditable event history. Plaspy ingests governor events, tamper alarms and geofence triggers to power rule based alerts, operational workflows and compliance reporting across a fleet.

- Real time location and status updates from the VSL100 appear in Plaspy dashboards for continuous tracking.
- Over speed and governor events are forwarded to Plaspy so managers can record infractions and trigger follow up actions.
- Tamper, anti jammer and signal loss alarms are routed to administrators for rapid investigation and response.
- Recent historical logs can be retrieved and correlated in Plaspy for offline analysis and compliance evidence.
- On site printed violation records complement Plaspy retained event logs for inspections and audit trails.

## Typical Use Cases

- Enforcing corporate speed policies across heavy trucks and delivery fleets while maintaining an auditable violation log.
- Monitoring construction and heavy equipment to reduce overspeed incidents and notify dispatch in real time.
- Supporting safe transport of dangerous goods with combined speed governance, tamper alerts and geofence monitoring.
- Providing documented proof of infractions for passenger transport and coach operators during roadside checks.
- Integrating GPS speed governance into logistics and dispatch workflows to improve route compliance and safety.
