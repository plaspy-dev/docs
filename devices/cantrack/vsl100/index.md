# CanTrack - VSL100

![VSL100](./tracker.jpg)

The VSL100 by Cantrack is a next-generation GPS speed limiter and GPS tracker engineered for heavy vehicles and fleet management. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the VSL100 combines microcomputer-based speed governance with high-accuracy real-time tracking so fleet operators can enforce preset maximum speeds, capture violations on the spot and feed telemetry into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting.

The device is purpose-built for logistics fleets, construction machinery, dangerous-goods carriers and passenger transport where controlled speeds, tamper resistance and auditable proof of infractions are essential. With tamper detection, anti-jammer alerts and a portable mini-printer for one-hour violation reports, the VSL100 delivers robust anti-theft and compliance capabilities while integrating vehicle telemetry into Plaspy’s platform for efficient dispatch and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and speed governor — real-time tracking combined with enforceable speed limits for fleet management.
- Integrated speed-limiting with pre-warnings and fallback enforcement \(configurable scope 40–120 km/h\) to improve road safety and regulatory compliance.
- Tamper-resistant design with anti-jammer detection and automated alarm reporting to administrators when signal loss exceeds five minutes.
- Portable mini-printer for on-the-spot evidence: print violations from the last hour for immediate proof during roadside inspections.
- High-accuracy GNSS \(66 channels, -165 dBm sensitivity, \<10 m CEP\) for reliable real-time tracking and telemetry in Plaspy dashboards.
- Multiple configuration options \(PC tool, GPRS, SMS\) and USB flash download of recent historical data for offline audit and analysis.
- Low standby/working current, wide operating voltage \(9–36 VDC\) and a 500 mAh backup battery to maintain short-term operation during power interruptions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the VSL100 streams location, status and violation events to the backend platform in real time using its GSM/GPRS link. Plaspy ingests speed-governor events, tamper and fatigue-driving alarms, and geofence triggers to provide operators with live situational awareness, rule-based alerts and historical reports. Integration enables fleet managers to act on telemetry faster—issuing dispatch changes, generating incident logs, or producing compliance documentation.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates from the VSL100’s high-sensitivity GNSS chip for continuous tracking in Plaspy.
- Over-speed, tamper, fatigue driving, geofence entry/exit and emergency braking events transmitted to Plaspy for alerting and audit trails.
- Anti-jammer detection and loss-of-signal alarms routed to administrators to protect against deliberate interference.
- USB flash download of recent historical data \(72 hours at 5-second intervals\) for offline analysis or upload into Plaspy.
- On-site violation printing for immediate proof; Plaspy maintains central event records for long-term telemetry and compliance reporting.

## Use Cases

- Fleet speed governance: enforce corporate speed policies across heavy trucks and delivery fleets while recording violations for compliance.
- Construction and heavy equipment: prevent overspeed incidents on site vehicles and send real-time alerts to dispatch via Plaspy.
- Dangerous goods and tanker monitoring: combine speed limiting with tamper detection and geofence alerts to support safe transport.
- Passenger transport and coach fleets: provide documented proof of infractions with portable printing and centralized Plaspy reporting.
- Logistics and dispatch systems: integrate real-time GPS tracker telemetry into vehicle commanding and route management workflows.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VSL100 is designed for operators who need more than basic GPS tracking — it pairs enforceable speed control with high-fidelity location telemetry and tamper protection. As a Plaspy compatible device, it feeds actionable data into a single fleet management platform so teams can monitor compliance, respond to incidents and maintain reliable audit trails. The on-site printing capability and anti-jammer detection add operational assurance for safety-critical fleets and regulatory environments.

For organizations using Plaspy alongside broader telemetry systems, the VSL100 complements ignition and fuel monitoring workflows and can be combined with immobilizer strategies or Bluetooth sensors where additional hardware is applied. Its multiple configuration methods \(PC tool, GPRS, SMS\), compact form factor and wide voltage range make deployment straightforward across commercial and heavy-duty vehicles.
