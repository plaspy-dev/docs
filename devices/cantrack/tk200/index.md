# CanTrack - TK200

![TK200](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack TK200 is a compact GPS tracker that combines GPS satellite positioning with cellular connectivity to deliver location and monitoring capabilities. Designed for both personal and commercial scenarios, the TK200 supports live tracking, remote listening, tracing, and a suite of anti theft features. The device can report position and status via SMS or data, and it includes alarms and data storage for later review.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK200 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s location updates, alarms, and stored history become available alongside other fleet assets, helping teams monitor movement, investigate events, and generate basic operational reports from a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- Combines GPS satellite positioning with cellular reporting for broad coverage and real time location updates.
- Remote listening and monitoring capability to check surroundings for added security.
- Multiple anti theft alarms including geo fence, movement, overspeed, and vibration alerts.
- Remote switch on and off plus sound triggered callback for flexible remote control and response.
- On device data storage that enables historical route review and basic trace analysis.
- Vehicle state monitoring including battery voltage and GPS signal strength for operational status checks.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TK200 can feed location and alarm information into Plaspy, where that data is visualized, logged, and used to generate alerts and reports. Plaspy collects incoming updates from compatible trackers and presents them in maps and dashboards for fleet managers and operators.

- Live position display on Plaspy maps for real time tracking of vehicles or assets.
- Alarm routing and notification handling for geo fence, movement, overspeed, vibration, and sound events.
- Historical playback and stored data access to review trips and trace device movement over time.
- Status monitoring for device and vehicle state such as battery and GPS signal indicators within Plaspy.
- Grouping and fleet overview tools to manage multiple TK200 units alongside other devices for operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small to medium commercial operations.
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery support for personal and company vehicles.
- Asset movement tracking for portable or high value items that need periodic location updates.
- Remote monitoring of vehicles used in rental or shared mobility services.
- Safety and location monitoring for vulnerable users when combined with appropriate operational policies.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CanTrack TK200 offers a practical mix of location reporting, local alarm options, and stored history that make it a versatile choice for organizations looking to add basic real time tracking and security features to their operations. When paired with Plaspy, those capabilities are extended into a centralized platform that helps teams visualize activity, respond to alerts, and keep a historical record of events.

Because the TK200 supports common reporting modes and a range of alarm types, it fits well in deployments where straightforward tracking, anti theft alarms, and occasional remote monitoring are required. Plaspy users benefit from integrating TK200 data into dashboards and reports alongside other devices, enabling simpler day to day fleet management and incident review.
