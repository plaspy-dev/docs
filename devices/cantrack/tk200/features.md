# CanTrack - TK200 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the CanTrack TK200 GPS tracker and explains how those features map to visibility and monitoring inside Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing capabilities such as location reporting, alarms, monitoring, and data history as they are commonly used with a fleet and asset management platform.

The CanTrack TK200 combines GPS positioning with GSM GPRS communication and supports control and reporting via SMS or GPRS. Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation, so this page presents general, non-sensitive feature context for use with Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The TK200 is designed to provide position reporting, audible monitoring, configurable alarms, and historical data capture for personal and commercial tracking needs. In practical terms it lets you locate targets, receive alerts for defined events, and keep a record of past movements for review.

- Real time location reporting via GPRS or SMS for tracking and monitoring
- Remote control functions including a remote switch on and off to conserve power
- Monitoring or listen-in capability for situational awareness where supported
- Historical data storage for reviewing past routes and events
- Configurable alarms such as geo‑fence, movement, overspeed, vibration, and sound triggered callbacks
- Vehicle state checks including battery voltage and GPS signal status reported by the device

## Core Features of CanTrack - TK200

- GPS based location reporting combined with GSM GPRS communication
- Remote switch on and off to control device power state remotely
- Single tracking for real time position updates
- Monitoring/listening function to capture surrounding audio where allowed and supported
- Data storage of historical tracking records for later retrieval
- Geo‑fence alarm to alert when predefined boundaries are crossed
- Movement, overspeed, and vibration alarms to detect motion and tampering
- Sound triggered callback to notify a predefined number when sound is detected

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy collects and displays device reports to provide location awareness, event visibility, and operational oversight. When a TK200 is connected and configured to report to Plaspy, common device features become actionable items inside the platform for monitoring and review.

- Real time GPS positions appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and situational awareness
- Historical tracking data reported by the TK200 is available for playback and route analysis in Plaspy
- Alarms such as geo‑fence, overspeed, movement, vibration, and sound triggers are shown as events and can be used to generate notifications or workflows
- Monitoring or listening events reported by the device are logged as device activity; availability depends on device setup and legal constraints
- Remote control events and status changes from the device are reflected in Plaspy so operators can see when remote power or mode changes occur
- Plaspy detects common tracker protocols automatically to simplify device onboarding and basic reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for location visibility and basic route review
- Asset protection and anti‑theft monitoring with motion and vibration alerts
- Temporary rental or shared vehicle oversight using geo‑fence and movement alarms
- Personal or caregiver tracking where remote listening and location updates are required
- Delivery or service route confirmation using historical tracking records
- Remote status checks on a vehicle or asset using reported battery and GPS signal information

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature support can differ by firmware version; some alarm behavior and menu options may change with updates
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect available communication bands and feature subsets
- Installation type and wiring may enable or disable specific reporting items such as vehicle state information
- Listening and audio features may be subject to local laws and carrier restrictions; confirm legal and operational use
- SMS vs GPRS reporting depends on network availability and SIM configuration in the device
- Always review the device's official documentation for exact commands, limits, and supported configurations
