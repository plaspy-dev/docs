# CanTrack - GF200LS

<div className="device-logo">
![GF200LS](./tracker.png)
</div>

The GF200LS is a disposable long-standby asset GPS tracker from CanTrack built for low maintenance, long-term deployments. Designed for heavy duty vehicles, cargo containers, and loaned or leased units, the GF200LS combines an efficient low power design with a strong magnet mount, waterproof housing, and a light sensor for tamper detection. When configured for low reporting rates, the device can deliver up to three years of standby operation, making it suitable for assets that are infrequently accessed.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GF200LS can stream position and event data into Plaspy's web and mobile platforms for both periodic and real-time visibility. This compatibility makes the tracker a practical choice for organisations that need reliable location reporting, tamper alerts, and long term telemetry while minimising onsite maintenance. Plaspy can present GF200LS data alongside other fleet information to support monitoring, reporting, and operational workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra long battery life up to three years in low reporting modes for low maintenance asset tracking
- Plaspy compatible for real time monitoring and historical reporting on web and mobile platforms
- Multiple working modes to balance reporting frequency and battery endurance for different deployment needs
- Rugged IP65 ABS housing with strong removable magnet for external mounting on metal assets
- Light sensor anti tamper alert to detect unauthorized removal and support recovery workflows
- Fast GNSS performance with typical sub 5 m accuracy in normal conditions
- Multi network cellular support for wide area connectivity to deliver position reports

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, GF200LS location reports and events are received by the platform and presented via dashboards and mobile views to provide operational visibility. Plaspy ingests periodic positions, real time updates, and tamper events so fleet managers and asset owners can monitor asset location, receive alerts, and review historical activity.

- Real time tracking for short term active monitoring when the device is set to more frequent reporting
- Periodic telemetry modes that preserve battery life while providing scheduled location updates for long deployments
- Tamper and removal alerts forwarded to Plaspy to trigger notifications and escalation workflows
- Remote configuration options supported by the device that Plaspy can help coordinate to ensure consistent reporting
- Consolidation of GF200LS location and event data with other Plaspy compatible inputs to enrich reports and operational context

## Typical Use Cases

- Low maintenance monitoring of financed assets to maintain visibility on collateral
- Container and cargo tracking during long transit or storage periods with periodic location updates
- Stolen asset recovery assistance using discreet location telemetry and removal alerts
- Management of loaned or leased vehicles and equipment that are dispersed and intermittently used
- Long term inventory tracking for seasonal or rarely moved fleet assets

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GF200LS is a useful option for organisations that prioritise long battery life and low touch deployments. Its design targets scenarios where frequent maintenance is impractical but reliable location and tamper reporting remain critical. Paired with Plaspy, GF200LS data becomes more actionable: you can switch between real time and interval reporting, receive immediate tamper notifications, and review historical routes and status reports within the Plaspy platform.
