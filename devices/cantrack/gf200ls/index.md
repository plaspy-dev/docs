# GF200LS — 3 Years Disposable Asset GPS Tracker

The GF200LS is a Plaspy compatible, long-standby magnet-mounted asset GPS tracker built for low-maintenance, long-term deployments. Designed for heavy-duty vehicles, cargo containers, and loaned or leased units, the GF200LS balances exceptional battery life with flexible reporting modes to support both periodic and real-time tracking scenarios on Plaspy's web and mobile platforms.

The device uses three CR123 batteries and an efficient low-power design to deliver up to three years of standby operation when configured for one position per day. With IP65 waterproofing, a strong removable magnetic mount, and an anti-tamper light sensor, the GF200LS is optimized for fleet management, anti-theft monitoring, stolen vehicle recovery assistance, and other long-term asset management needs where low-touch telemetry and reliable location reporting are essential.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra-long battery life — up to three years at one location per day for low-maintenance asset GPS tracking.
- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and historical reporting on web and mobile platforms.
- Multiple working modes \(real-time, interval, clock\) let you balance reporting frequency and battery life.
- IP65 waterproof ABS housing with strong N54 magnet and removable cover for simple installation on metal assets.
- Fast and sensitive GNSS \(SIMCom A767 + AT6558R\) for quick TTFF and sub-5 m accuracy in typical conditions.
- Multi-network cellular support \(LTE-FDD, LTE-TDD plus 2G GSM\) for wide area connectivity and GPRS/TCP/IP reporting.
- Light-sensor tamper alert to detect unauthorized removal and help with anti-theft workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the GF200LS sends position and event data to Plaspy using its cellular link \(GPRS/TCP over LTE/2G\) or via the configured server IP/port. Plaspy ingests the device's location reports and alerts, allowing fleet managers and asset owners to view live positions, review historical routes, and receive tamper or status notifications through Plaspy's dashboards and mobile apps.

- Real-time tracking: When set to real-time mode, GF200LS can deliver continuous location updates \(approx. 24 hours on battery in this mode\) for short-term active monitoring.
- Periodic telemetry: Interval and clock modes \(e.g., one position per day\) enable long-term telemetry with multi-year battery life, ideal for container and loaned vehicle tracking.
- Tamper and removal alerts: Light-sensor anti-tamper reports are sent as events to trigger immediate Plaspy notifications and escalation workflows.
- Configuration and control: The device supports APN and server IP/port configuration plus SMS commands for remote mode switching and status checks; Plaspy can guide setup to ensure consistent reporting.
- Compatibility with complementary telemetry: Plaspy can combine GF200LS location and event data with other Plaspy-compatible inputs \(fuel monitoring, ignition/immobilizer data, or external Bluetooth sensors\) provided by additional hardware or integrations.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the GF200LS with Plaspy delivers a practical, cost-effective solution for organizations that need reliable position data without frequent onsite maintenance. The GF200LS stands out for its three-year standby capability in low-reporting modes, rugged IP65 build, and simple magnetic install — making it well suited to fleet management and anti-theft deployments where low-touch telemetry is essential.

On Plaspy, GF200LS data becomes actionable: real-time tracking when needed, scheduled interval reports for long-term monitoring, and immediate tamper notifications to trigger alerts or recovery workflows. While the GF200LS focuses on location and event telemetry, Plaspy can enrich those records by combining them with other telemetry sources \(fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer status, or Bluetooth sensors\) through additional Plaspy-compatible devices or integrations. Together, they provide scalable, trustworthy tracking and reporting that reduces operational overhead and improves asset security.
