# CanTrack - GT06 v2

![GT06 v2](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack GT06 v2 is a GPS tracker that utilizes the existing GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellites to locate and monitor remote targets. It is designed for easy and compact use in vehicle tracking applications. With its built-in GPS and GSM module, it can capture GPS data and send it to authorized mobile numbers via SMS. Users can view the current location on their phones or track the location on Google Earth or Google Maps. Additionally, GPS data can be transmitted by GPRS to an internet server for real-time tracking on a computer.

The CanTrack GT06 v2 has a wide range of applications, including vehicle rental and fleet management, wealth protection, providing peace-of-mind for businessmen, and personnel management. It offers various features to enhance tracking and security, such as real-time tracking location by SMS/GPRS, overspeed alarm, ACC anti-theft alarm, SOS alarm, and real-time voice monitoring function. It also has a built-in backup battery to provide power failure alarm and an optional feature to cut vehicle oil or circuit. Additionally, the GT06 v2 offers the option for two-way calling.

With its global GSM quad-band frequency, the CanTrack GT06 v2 is suitable for use worldwide. Whether you need to track your vehicles, protect your assets, or manage your personnel, this GPS tracker provides reliable and efficient tracking capabilities.

### Outstanding Features:

- GSM quad-band frequency for global use
- Real-time tracking location by SMS/GPRS
- Recover original password
- Set authorized number
- Overspeed alarm
- ACC anti-theft alarm
- SOS alarm
- Real-time voice monitoring function
- Built-in backup battery for power failure alarm
- Optional feature to cut vehicle oil or circuit
- Optional two-way calling
