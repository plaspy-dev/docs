# CanTrack - GT06 v2

![GT06 v2](./logo.jpg)

The CanTrack GT06 v2 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker that uses GPS satellites and the GSM GPRS network to determine and report location. Designed for vehicle tracking applications, it can send position information via SMS to authorized numbers and transmit GPS data over GPRS for real time server tracking. The device includes alarms for overspeed, ACC anti theft, SOS, and power failure, plus optional features such as cutting vehicle oil or circuit and two way calling.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GT06 v2 can provide vehicle location and alarm information into Plaspy for map visualization, monitoring, and reporting. Its support for SMS and GPRS reporting makes it suitable for integration with Plaspy's tracking platform to give teams operational visibility, historical location records, and alerting that help with fleet oversight and asset protection.

## Key Highlights

- Compact vehicle tracker that reports location via SMS or GPRS
- Global GSM quad band support for wide geographic coverage
- Built in backup battery to report power failure events
- Alarm features including overspeed, ACC anti theft, and SOS alerts
- Optional two way calling and optional remotely cut oil or circuit feature
- Designed for straightforward vehicle tracking and monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GT06 v2 sends location and alarm messages that Plaspy ingests to provide dashboards, maps, and event handling. Plaspy centralizes the device data so fleets and operations teams can monitor units together with other compatible trackers.

- Live and historical location display on Plaspy maps for operational oversight
- Alarm ingestion for overspeed, SOS, power loss, and anti theft events mapped to Plaspy alerts
- Consolidated fleet view to track multiple GT06 v2 units alongside other devices
- Reporting and exportable traces for route review and operational analysis
- Configurable notifications so teams receive critical events via Plaspy channels

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and vehicle rental tracking for location visibility and accountability
- Asset protection and recovery monitoring for cars and light commercial vehicles
- Executive and personnel tracking to provide peace of mind and operational oversight
- Remote monitoring where SMS or GPRS reporting is preferred for location updates
- Situations requiring basic remote alerting for overspeed, SOS, or power loss
