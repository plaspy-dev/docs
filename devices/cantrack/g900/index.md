# CanTrack - G900

![G900](./Logo.png)

The CanTrack G900 is a versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features to help you keep track of your assets. With real-time tracking, you can monitor the location of your vehicle or valuable equipment at any time. This can be especially useful for fleet management or for ensuring the security of your assets. The G900 also includes an overspeed alarm, which alerts you when your vehicle exceeds a certain speed limit. This can be helpful for promoting safe driving habits and preventing accidents. Additionally, the power cut alarm feature notifies you if the tracker's power source is disconnected, providing an extra layer of security.

The CanTrack G900 is designed to withstand the elements with its IP65 waterproof shell. This means that it is protected against dust and water splashes, making it suitable for use in various environments. Whether you need to track a vehicle, construction equipment, or other valuable assets, the G900 is a reliable and durable choice.

Key Features:

- Real-time tracking for accurate location monitoring
- Overspeed alarm to promote safe driving habits
- Power cut alarm for added security
- IP65 waterproof shell for durability in various environments

Technical Specifications:

- Network: GSM / GPRS / GPS
- Band: 850/900/1800/1900 Mhz
- GPS Chip: Ublox 7020
- GSM module: MT6261
- Accuracy: 5m
- Working voltage: DC9V-36V
