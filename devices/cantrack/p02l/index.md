# CanTrack - P02L

![P02L](./tracker.png)

The CanTrack P02L Smart Walkie-Talkie Tracker is a compact 4G personal GPS device designed for outdoor safety and two-way voice communication. It combines multi-constellation positioning (GPS plus BeiDou and LBS) with LTE connectivity to provide near real time location updates, a global intercom function for long range voice communication, and a set of safety oriented features such as SOS/panic buttons and fall‑down detection. The device also includes activity sensing and Bluetooth accessory support to broaden its telemetry and monitoring options.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the P02L can stream position, status and alarm events into the Plaspy platform so caregivers, dispatchers, or operations teams can monitor individuals or small teams from a single dashboard. Remote management features such as OTA updates and SMS commands help simplify configuration and maintenance at scale, making the P02L a practical choice for organizations that want voice enabled personal safety combined with centralized monitoring via Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible personal tracker for reliable real time position and telemetry ingestion.
- Global walkie talkie intercom and voice call support for long distance two way communication.
- Multi constellations for positioning including GPS and BeiDou with reported accuracy under 5 meters CEP.
- Dedicated safety features including SOS panic button and fall detection for rapid alerting.
- Long battery life suitable for extended deployments and low power standby operation.
- Remote management via OTA updates and SMS commands for group configuration and maintenance.
- Built in motion sensing and step counting plus Bluetooth accessory support for expanded telemetry.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The P02L uses its cellular data channel to relay location fixes, status updates and alarm events into Plaspy, enabling near real time visibility and centralized alerting. Once devices are connected to Plaspy, operators can view live positions, configure monitoring rules and receive priority notifications for critical events without needing direct access to each unit.

- Real time location and historical track viewing in Plaspy mapping and reporting tools.
- SOS, panic button and fall down alarm events surfaced as priority alerts for fast response.
- Activity telemetry such as step counts and motion status available for context and reporting.
- Bluetooth accessory data and local sensor status forwarded to Plaspy when supported by the device.
- Remote configuration and group management through OTA updates or SMS driven settings to reduce on site maintenance.

- Lone worker protection and patrol oversight with panic alerts and remote configuration for supervisors.
- Guided tours or field teams requiring instant voice coordination alongside centralized location visibility.
