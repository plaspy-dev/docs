# CanTrack - TK200AM

<div className="device-logo">
![TK200AM](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The CanTrack TK200AM is an ultra compact GPS tracker designed for long term asset tracking and anti tamper deployments. It combines a high sensitivity satellite receiver with supplemental positioning using cellular and Wi Fi sources to deliver reliable location fixes for vehicles, containers, motorcycles and other valuable assets while minimizing maintenance through extended standby capability.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK200AM can feed location updates, tamper alerts and basic telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and reporting. Its support for both low duty periodic reporting and a switchable higher frequency mode makes it a practical choice for users who need to balance long battery life with on demand tracking detail inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into existing fleet monitoring and reporting workflows.
- Ultra compact, low profile enclosure suitable for discreet placement on vehicles, containers, bikes and equipment.
- Dual mode power strategy offering ultra long standby during low duty cycles and a switchable real time mode for urgent tracking.
- Multiple positioning methods including GPS plus supplemental cellular and Wi Fi assistance for faster fixes in challenging environments.
- Built in anti tamper alarm that wakes the device and sends a location update if the unit is disturbed or removed.
- Remote wake on demand and vendor platform control to request immediate updates without sustaining continuous power draw.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected with Plaspy, the TK200AM supplies periodic or real time location packets and status messages that Plaspy ingests to present live position, historical routes and configurable alerts. Plaspy users can choose reporting profiles that prioritize battery life or tracking granularity and receive tamper notifications and device status through the platform.

- Real time updates while in active reporting mode and scheduled low frequency updates during power saving mode.
- Tamper and anti theft alerts reported to Plaspy for fast operational response.
- Remote wake on demand to retrieve immediate location and status from the device without leaving continuous tracking enabled.
- GSM GPRS uplink of position packets compatible with Plaspy ingestion for mapping, geofencing and reporting.
- Plaspy can correlate TK200AM feeds with other fleet data sources to provide consolidated dashboards and historical analysis.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term monitoring of low activity assets where extended unattended operation reduces maintenance visits.
- Vehicle rental and fleet services that require concealed tracking and tamper alerts when vehicles are off site.
- Motorcycle and electric bike tracking where small form factor and discreet placement are important.
- Container and logistics asset monitoring using low power periodic updates to preserve battery life.
- Insurance or risk management scenarios combining location history and movement detection with alerts.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK200AM is a good fit for organizations that need a discreet, low maintenance tracker that also offers the ability to escalate to real time tracking when required. Its compact size, anti tamper alarm and selectable reporting profiles make it suitable for anti theft and long term asset monitoring programs where battery life and concealment matter.

Integrated with Plaspy, the TK200AM provides reliable position and status feeds that can be visualized, alerted on and reported against alongside other fleet data. That combination gives fleet managers and asset owners practical operational oversight without excessive maintenance overhead.
