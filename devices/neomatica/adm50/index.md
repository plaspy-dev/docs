# Neomatica - ADM50

![ADM50](./tracker.png)

The ADM50 is a compact battery powered GPS tracker designed for long term personal and asset monitoring. It combines GNSS positioning with LBS assisted fixes, a large 3350 mAh Li Po battery and multiple operating modes to balance accurate location reporting and extended standby life. The device supports event reporting features such as a panic alarm button and movement detection to help capture incidents and preserve battery when idle.

Listed as Plaspy compatible, the ADM50 can integrate into Plaspy for live mapping, Alerts, and historical route reconstruction. Its configurable reporting intervals, motion triggered reporting and local track history make it a practical fit for deployments where both real time visibility and long duration monitoring are required. Note that the manufacturer lists this model as retired or discontinued, so confirm current availability before purchase.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible personal GPS tracker suitable for long term monitoring and real time visibility.
- Dual GNSS positioning with LBS assisted fixes to improve fix performance in varied environments.
- Large 3350 mAh Li Po battery and multiple operating modes to extend standby and beacon life.
- Physical panic alarm button for immediate event notification and incident alerting.
- Three axis accelerometer for motion detection and movement based reporting to save power.
- Local track history capacity to store extensive route records for later upload and analysis.
- Compact and lightweight form factor for discreet personal or asset placement.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the ADM50 forwards location and event telemetry for live tracking, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy leverages the device's configurable reporting behavior and event signals so you can tune how frequently positions are sent and which incidents generate notifications.

- Real time location updates and telemetry displayed on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- LBS assisted positioning supplements GNSS to improve fixes when satellite reception is limited.
- Motion based notifications from the accelerometer allow movement triggered reports and reduced reporting during inactivity.
- Panic button events are forwarded to Plaspy to create immediate alerts for operators or responders.
- Configurable reporting intervals and beacon modes help balance tracking granularity and battery life.
- Local track history stored on the device enables retrospective route recovery in Plaspy after intermittent connectivity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet or animal tracking and sporting events benefiting from long beacon mode life.
- Remote asset surveillance for seasonal or infrequently checked items that need long standby.
- Basic fleet or equipment tracking where reliable GNSS fixes and movement reporting suffice.
