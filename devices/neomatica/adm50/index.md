# ADM50 Personal GPS Tracker

The ADM50 is a compact, battery-powered GPS tracker designed for long-term personal and asset monitoring and listed as Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your Plaspy real-time tracking and telemetry workflows. With GPS/GLONASS positioning, LBS-assisted fixes, and a large 3350 mAh Li-Po battery, the ADM50 balances accurate tracking with extended standby times across multiple operating modes.

Engineered for use cases from personal safety to remote asset protection, the ADM50 supports detailed tracker and low-power beacon modes, a panic \(alarm\) button, a three-axis accelerometer for motion detection, and configurable reporting intervals. It is well suited for real-time tracking on Plaspy, offering flexible update rates and a substantial track history capacity for fleet management, anti-theft monitoring, and general telemetry needs. Note: the ADM50 is listed as retired/discontinued by the manufacturer—check availability before ordering.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that supports real-time tracking and configurable reporting intervals for flexible telemetry.
- Dual GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS\) via MT3333 chipset for reliable position fixes in varied environments.
- Large 3350 mAh Li‑Po battery with multiple operating modes: from high-frequency tracker updates \(hours of operation\) to beacon mode \(up to ~100 days\) for long-term monitoring.
- Panic \(alarm\) button for immediate alerts and event-driven reporting to Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- Three-axis accelerometer for motion detection, sleep/wake behavior, and movement-based reporting to preserve battery life.
- Track history memory for up to 100,000 route records—store long route logs locally for later upload to Plaspy.
- Compact, lightweight form factor \(88 × 56 × 25 mm, 120 g\) for discreet personal, pet, or asset placement.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ADM50 transmits GNSS coordinates and event telemetry over GSM/GPRS to Plaspy for live mapping, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy-compatible integration leverages the device's configurable reporting intervals and alarm signals so you can tune update frequency and alerting to match use cases from high-resolution tracking to battery-saving beacon mode. Because ADM50 stores large track histories locally, Plaspy can reconstruct routes even if connectivity is intermittent.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via GPRS for live tracking on Plaspy.
- LBS-assisted positioning supplements GNSS for faster or indoor fixes when satellite reception is limited.
- Motion and sleep/wake notifications from the three-axis accelerometer for movement-triggered reports and power savings.
- Panic/alarm button events forwarded to Plaspy for immediate alerts and incident handling.
- Configurable reporting intervals and multiple operating modes \(detailed tracker mode and beacon mode\) to balance tracking granularity and battery life.
- Local track history \(up to 100,000 records\) supports retrospective analysis and route recovery in Plaspy.
- Low-battery SMS alerts can be used as a secondary notification channel; manufacturer documentation and firmware are available for configuration guidance.

- Vehicle and cargo security: discreet placement for anti-theft monitoring and route history when granular telemetry is required.
- Employee and access control on restricted sites: monitor personnel movement and receive alerts for unauthorized events.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the ADM50 for your Plaspy deployment gives you a compact, power-efficient GPS tracker focused on long-term monitoring and dependable location telemetry. Its MT3333 GPS/GLONASS chipset, LBS-assisted positioning, and configurable reporting modes let you optimize data volume versus battery life—ideal for fleet management scenarios requiring both real-time tracking and long-duration beaconing. The device’s built-in panic button and accelerometer-based motion detection provide useful event telemetry that Plaspy can surface as alerts and actionable reports.

Be aware that the ADM50 emphasizes GNSS and motion telemetry and does not include built-in Bluetooth sensors, fuel monitoring, or ignition/immobilizer interfaces. If your Plaspy use case needs telemetry such as fuel level, ignition state, or remote immobilizer control, pair the ADM50 with appropriate external systems or select a tracker variant that explicitly supports those interfaces. For standard personal, asset, and basic fleet monitoring where long standby and reliable position reporting are priorities, the ADM50 remains a practical Plaspy compatible option—provided you confirm current availability from the manufacturer.
