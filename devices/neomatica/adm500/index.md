# Neomatica - ADM500

<div className="device-logo">
![ADM500](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Neomatica ADM500 is a professional grade GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset telematics. It combines multi constellation GNSS reception (GPS, GLONASS, BDS and Galileo) with cellular data connectivity to provide continuous real time location and route recording for fleets, public transport, agricultural machinery and passenger cars. The ADM500 includes features such as dual nanoSIM capability, a built in battery for temporary autonomous operation and broad sensor support, making it suitable for persistent tracking even when external power is interrupted.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ADM500 can deliver location and telemetry into Plaspy platforms for live monitoring, historical reporting and operational alerts. Its flexible sensor ecosystem and event reporting are a good match for Plaspy workflows that require fuel monitoring, temperature sensing, movement detection and remote control signals. The device’s design focuses on reliable data flow and continuity, which helps Plaspy maintain accurate tracking and consistent business reporting across vehicle fleets and distributed assets.

## Key Highlights

- Multi constellation GNSS for stable positioning and improved location reliability in diverse environments.
- Cellular connectivity with dual nanoSIM support to maintain continuous telemetry and redundancy.
- Built in backup battery for several hours of autonomous operation and on device route storage for extended histories.
- Broad sensor compatibility including wired and wireless fuel sensors, multiple temperature probes and BLE peripherals.
- Security oriented features such as accelerometer based movement detection and signal jamming alerts for tamper awareness.
- Multiple I O channels for ignition, analog and pulse inputs and open collector outputs for integration with vehicle systems.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the ADM500 transmits standardized location and telemetry messages that Plaspy ingests to provide mapping, alerts and reports. Plaspy uses these data streams to present live locations, trigger event based notifications and populate historical trip and sensor reports for operational oversight.

- Real time GPS coordinates and vehicle position updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and map visualization.
- Sensor telemetry from fuel probes, temperature sensors and BLE devices available in Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Event based alerts such as ignition changes, motion detection and jamming alerts routed to Plaspy for immediate notifications.
- Historical route and trip data stored locally on the device and synchronized to Plaspy for retrospective analysis and compliance reporting.
- Remote outputs and control signals exposed by the device can be incorporated into Plaspy workflows to support immobilization or remote commands where configured.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operations: live vehicle tracking, route history and utilization reporting for mixed fleets.
- Anti theft and security: movement and tamper detection integrated into monitoring and alerting workflows.
- Temperature controlled transport: sensor reporting for refrigerated cargo and environmental monitoring.
- Fuel management: wired or wireless fuel sensor integration for consumption analysis and anomaly detection.
- Semi permanent asset monitoring: battery backed tracking and external sensors for stationary equipment and trailers.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ADM500 pairs practical telematics hardware with a sensor centric design that fits many fleet and asset management requirements. Its multi constellation GNSS, redundant cellular connectivity and on device storage help keep location and event data consistent, while the broad I O and sensor support make it flexible for diverse telemetry needs. For teams using Plaspy, the ADM500 provides a capable data source that can be mapped into dashboards, alerts and reports without extensive customization.
