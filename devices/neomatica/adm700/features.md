# Neomatica - ADM700 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Neomatica ADM700 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet tracking, telemetry and operational monitoring. The goal is to describe practical, non sensitive feature context that helps fleet managers, integrators and technical users understand what the ADM700 delivers when paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability depends on device firmware, hardware revision, installation choices and how the manufacturer implements specific interfaces. Some functions and optional accessories may vary by region or build. For device specific configuration and the most current technical details please consult the official Neomatica documentation.

## Feature Overview

The ADM700 is a vehicle mounted GPS GLONASS terminal designed for heavy duty fleets and demanding environments. It combines continuous GNSS positioning, GSM GPRS telemetry and comprehensive vehicle interfaces to provide persistent tracking and data logging for trucks, vans and construction equipment.

- High sensitivity GNSS for reliable position, speed and course logging even in challenging signal conditions.
- GSM GPRS telemetry for real time tracking and data forwarding to Plaspy over mobile networks.
- Rugged IP65 and IK07 enclosure built for harsh environments and on vehicle use.
- Multiple vehicle interfaces including CAN bus FMS J1939, RS 232 RS 485 and 1 Wire for diagnostics and telemetry capture.
- Flexible inputs and outputs plus pulse inputs to integrate analog sensors, ignition signals and external devices.
- Large internal logging capacity with optional microSD expansion and an internal backup battery to preserve records during power loss.

## Core Features of Neomatica - ADM700

- GNSS positioning using GPS and GLONASS for continuous location fixes and movement data.
- GSM GPRS multi band connectivity for transmitting position and telemetry to remote servers.
- Dual SIM functionality with optional SIM chip and voice capability to maintain connectivity and fallback options.
- Rugged mechanical design rated IP65 for dust and water protection and IK07 for impact resistance.
- Vehicle interfaces including CAN bus FMS J1939, RS 232, RS 485 and 1 Wire for engine and sensor integration.
- I O set including multiple analog and discrete inputs, pulse inputs and open collector outputs for status and control.
- Large internal memory capable of storing many route records with optional microSD card for expanded history retention.
- Internal backup Li ion battery to keep logging and basic operation available during power interruptions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the ADM700 telemetry and location updates to provide fleet visibility, playback and reporting. The device forwards stored logs and live data over mobile networks and Plaspy maps that data into dashboards, historical routes and event records for operational use.

- Position updates and movement information appear in Plaspy for live tracking and historical route replay.
- CAN bus messages and vehicle telemetry can be mapped into Plaspy dashboards and reports for diagnostics and fuel analytics.
- Analog, pulse and discrete inputs are available in Plaspy as events or telemetry values that drive alarms and status indicators.
- Onboard logging is forwarded to Plaspy when connectivity is restored so offline events and routes are preserved in the platform.
- Dual SIM and resilient connectivity reduce data gaps and improve continuity of monitoring within Plaspy.
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and ingests ADM700 telemetry for display and reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trucks and delivery fleets with live location, route replay and dispatch support.
- Anti theft and recovery monitoring using offline logging and backup battery to maintain records during tampering.
- Fuel monitoring and consumption analysis using CAN based messages or pulse and analog sensor inputs.
- Vehicle diagnostics and maintenance planning by capturing engine and operational telemetry from CAN.
- Heavy equipment and construction machinery tracking where rugged enclosure and wide power range matter.
- Route compliance and driver oversight through continuous location and event history.
