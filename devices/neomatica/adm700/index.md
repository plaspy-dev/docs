# Neomatica - ADM700

<div className="device-logo">
![ADM700](./tracker.png)
</div>

The ADM700 is a vehicle mounted GPS GLONASS tracking terminal designed for reliable fleet management and Plaspy compatible deployments. As an add on unit for trucks, vans and heavy equipment, the ADM700 provides continuous logging of location, speed and direction and transmits telemetry over GSM GPRS for real time tracking, remote monitoring and dispatch. Its rugged IP65 and IK07 enclosure, dual SIM capability and internal backup battery make it suitable for demanding transport and logistics environments.

Because the ADM700 supports a broad set of vehicle interfaces and local logging, it is a natural fit for integration with Plaspy. The device can forward position fixes, event logs and sensor inputs to Plaspy so fleet operators get live visibility, route playback and historic records. Offline storage and robust connectivity options mean Plaspy can ingest records as soon as GSM coverage is available, helping maintain continuity for anti theft, diagnostics and operational reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS GLONASS tracker for real time tracking and dispatch via GSM GPRS
- High sensitivity GNSS reception for reliable satellite positioning in varied environments
- Rugged field ready design with IP65 dust and water protection and IK07 impact resistance
- Multiple vehicle interfaces including CAN bus FMS J1939 RS 232 RS 485 and 1 Wire for telemetry integration
- Flexible I O with analogue and discrete inputs plus pulse inputs and open collector outputs for sensors and external control
- Dual SIM support and an internal backup battery for connectivity fallback and short term power continuity
- Large onboard storage with capacity for extensive route records and optional microSD expansion for offline logging

## How It Works with Plaspy

ADM700 transmits position fixes, telemetry and event records to Plaspy over standard GSM GPRS links, where the platform consolidates location data, alerts and historical playback. Plaspy maps the device inputs to dashboards and reports so operators can monitor vehicle status and respond to events in near real time.

- Live location and telemetry updates for fleet visibility, geofence alerts and dispatch support
- CAN bus integration to surface vehicle engine and fuel related messages into Plaspy dashboards
- Analog and discrete inputs that feed sensor readings and ignition or status events for alarming and reporting
- Pulse inputs that can be processed for fuel flow or mileage analytics and historical calculations
- Open collector outputs available for external device control and coordinated workflows when managed through Plaspy
- Local logging to internal memory and optional microSD with automatic upload to Plaspy when connectivity resumes

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and dispatch for trucks, delivery vans and service vehicles with live monitoring and route replay
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows leveraging backup battery and event logging
- Fuel monitoring and loss detection using CAN messages or pulse and analogue sensor inputs
- Vehicle diagnostics and maintenance planning using engine telemetry captured from vehicle interfaces
- Tracking of heavy equipment and construction machinery where ruggedness and wide input voltage tolerance are required
