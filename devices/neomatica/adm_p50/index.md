# Neomatica - ADM P50

<div className="device-logo">
![ADM P50](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Neomatica ADM P50 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker designed for reliable personal and asset tracking. Built around a GNSS chipset and an energy optimized firmware, the ADM P50 supports operation modes ranging from continuous real time tracking to a low frequency beacon mode so users can balance position accuracy with long term autonomy. The device includes motion awareness, an SOS panic button, support for an indoor Bluetooth tag, and USB Type C configuration for straightforward management.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ADM P50 can feed location fixes, event messages, and basic telemetry into the Plaspy platform for immediate visibility and reporting. Its combination of GNSS positioning, LBS fallback, accelerometer driven motion reporting and emergency alerting makes it a suitable option when you need portable tracking tied into Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and historical route playback.

## Key Highlights

- Compact battery powered design suitable for personal and portable asset tracking
- Configurable operation modes from continuous tracking to low frequency beacon mode for extended runtime
- Long autonomy with a substantial rechargeable battery for long field deployments
- Emergency SOS button and motion detection using a 3 axis accelerometer for safety and alerting
- Indoor positioning support by pairing with an ADM34 Bluetooth tag to improve indoor location
- Dual SIM flexibility and USB Type C configuration with manufacturer firmware updates and documentation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the ADM P50 delivers position updates and event data in formats Plaspy accepts so operators can monitor assets and people on the same platform used for fleet and asset oversight. Plaspy ingests fixes and events to provide map visualization, alert routing, and historical reporting while leveraging device telemetry for operational awareness.

- Real time location streaming and periodic route uploads for map display and tracking
- SOS panic alerts forwarded to Plaspy alarm channels for rapid notification workflows
- Motion state and sleep transitions from the accelerometer reported as telemetry for activity monitoring
- Battery level and low battery events presented in Plaspy for operational planning
- Indoor positioning signals from paired Bluetooth tags incorporated into location views and proximity alerts

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring with immediate SOS alerts and motion based reporting
- Portable asset tracking for equipment that needs long battery life and intermittent reporting
- Event staff and temporary workforce tracking where compact, rechargeable devices are preferred
- Cargo protection and anti theft monitoring with historical route playback for investigations
- Indoor navigation augmentation using ADM34 Bluetooth tags for building level positioning

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ADM P50 is a practical choice for organizations that need portable, battery efficient tracking integrated into Plaspy. Its focus on accurate positioning combined with motion awareness and emergency alerting provides the key telemetry used for safety, asset protection, and short term deployments. Support for indoor Bluetooth tags and a sizeable route memory make it useful where mixed indoor and outdoor visibility is required.

While optimized for personal and asset monitoring rather than vehicle specific telemetry, the ADM P50 complements Plaspy deployments that benefit from portable units and long run time. Its configuration options and manufacturer supplied updates help keep devices manageable in the field, and Plaspy brings consolidated visualization, alerts, and historical reporting to make the most of the device data.
