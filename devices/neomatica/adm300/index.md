# Neomatica - ADM300

![ADM300](./tracker.png)

The ADM300 is a compact, on-board GPS/GLONASS vehicle tracking terminal from Neomatica designed for reliable, continuous monitoring of cars, trucks and special equipment. Built for fleet management and telematics deployments, the ADM300 delivers real-time tracking of position, speed and heading, stores long route history on microSD, and transmits telemetry and event data over GPRS to a designated server. With EGTS and an open protocol, the ADM300 is Plaspy compatible and integrates smoothly into Plaspy-based tracking platforms for immediate operational use.

Engineered for practical field use, the ADM300 combines high-sensitivity GNSS reception, a built-in Li‑Ion backup battery and a three-axis accelerometer to support anti-theft monitoring and movement detection. The terminal records input states from connected sensors, supports voice communication, and offers remote firmware updates via GPRS—making it a dependable GPS tracker for mixed fleets that require secure, real-time visibility and actionable telemetry.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via EGTS and open protocol—straightforward integration into Plaspy-based fleet management systems.
- Real-time tracking with GPS/GLONASS and high-sensitivity GNSS receiver \(–165 dBm, 132 channels\) for reliable position fixes.
- Robust event logging: up to 16,000 records internally and expandable storage up to millions of records on microSD for long route history retention.
- Multiple telemetry inputs \(dual analog and dual pulse\) and RS‑485 interface to capture sensor states and external device data.
- Built-in 1000 mAh Li‑Ion battery for backup operation, plus tamper detection \(case opening\) and password protection to improve anti-theft security.
- GPRS packet transfer over GSM \(850/900/1800/1900\) with Multi-slot Class 12 and remote firmware update capability for simplified maintenance.
- Compact form factor \(106×63×32 mm, 105 g\) with integrated antennas and voice call capability for operator communication when needed.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the ADM300 with Plaspy leverages the device’s open protocol and EGTS support. The terminal transmits location, telemetry and event data over GPRS to your Plaspy server \(static IP or domain\), where Plaspy processes real-time tracking, alerts and historical reports. Remote firmware updates and secure device settings are handled over the same GPRS link, simplifying device lifecycle management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent via GPRS to Plaspy for live mapping and route playback.
- Event and input state reporting: analog and pulse inputs plus accelerometer-based movement events are forwarded to Plaspy for rule-based alerts.
- Long-term route history available from internal memory or microSD for batch upload and historical analysis in Plaspy.
- Tamper alerts and voice call capability support anti-theft workflows and operator communication through Plaspy alerting channels.
- Remote firmware updates over GPRS ensure ADM300 units in the field remain compatible and secure with Plaspy integrations.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: continuous real-time tracking, route playback and telemetry for trucks, delivery vehicles and service fleets.
- Anti-theft monitoring: tamper alerts, accelerometer movement detection and voice call support for rapid response.
- Sensor telemetry: connect fuel level senders, door switches or other analog/pulse sensors to monitor fuel usage and door/alarm events.
- Special equipment tracking: reliable GNSS tracking and large onboard logging capacity for construction, agricultural and municipal machinery.
- Long-route history analysis: microSD expansion lets operations retain comprehensive route records for compliance and post-incident analysis.
