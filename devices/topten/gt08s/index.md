# TopTen - GT08S

![GT08S](./Logo.jpg)

The TopTen GT08S GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a wide range of features to help you keep track of your vehicles. Whether you need to monitor a car, motorcycle, or even a big truck, this tracker is suitable for all types of vehicles with its wide voltage range of 10V-60VDC. 

One of the standout features of the GT08S is its ability to track your vehicle on command or by time interval via SMS or GPRS. This means that you can easily keep tabs on your vehicle's location and movement, giving you peace of mind and added security. Additionally, you can arm or disarm the tracker using SMS, the platform, or even a phone call, making it convenient and easy to use. 

The GT08S also offers advanced features such as 2.4G RFID tag support for wireless immobilization, real-time tracking of the car's physical address, and the ability to check the location directly on Google Maps. It also has a data logger that can store up to 5000 waypoints, allowing you to review past routes and movements. 

With its built-in rechargeable backup battery, the GT08S ensures that you stay connected even if the car battery is cut off or low. It also has various alarm functions including over-speed alarm, movement alarm, engine on alarm, vibration alarm, and door opening alarm \(optional\). The tracker also supports two-way talking and has a wide range of customization options available. 

Overall, the TopTen GT08S GPS tracker is a reliable and feature-packed device that offers advanced tracking capabilities and a wide range of customization options. Whether you need to monitor a single vehicle or an entire fleet, this tracker is a great choice for its versatility and reliability.
