# TopTen - MT05

![MT05](./Logo.jpg)

The TopTen MT05 mini GPS Tracking device is packed with features and functions that make it an excellent choice for tracking and securing your vehicle. With the ability to track via SMS, web, or app, you can easily monitor your vehicle's location and status from anywhere. The GPS tracker also doubles as a car alarm, allowing you to arm or disarm it using SMS, web, or phone call.

One of the standout features of the MT05 is its ability to provide the real physical address of your vehicle, including the city and street name. This can be incredibly useful in case of theft or if you need to locate your vehicle in a crowded area. The device also has a smart engine ON/OFF status detection feature, allowing you to know when your vehicle's engine is turned on or off.

In terms of safety, the MT05 has several features to ensure the well-being of your vehicle. It can safely stop the car when the speed is less than or equal to 30Km/h, preventing unauthorized use. The device also has an over-speed alarm, movement alarm, engine on alarm, vibration alarm, and power failure alarm, providing comprehensive protection for your vehicle.

The MT05 is designed to withstand the elements with its waterproof design and compact size. It also has an odometer function, allowing you to keep track of the distance traveled by your vehicle. With a voltage range of 10V-60VDC, the device is suitable for motorcycles, cars, and most big trucks. It also features a reliable framework design with a hardware watchdog for added durability and reliability.

Overall, the TopTen MT05 mini GPS Tracking device is a feature-packed and reliable tracker that offers comprehensive tracking and security for your vehicle. Whether you need to monitor your vehicle's location, prevent theft, or ensure its safety, the MT05 has you covered.
