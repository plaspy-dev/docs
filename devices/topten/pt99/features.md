# TopTen - PT99 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the TopTen PT99 personal asset GPS tracker with the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the model's practical capabilities, how those capabilities appear in Plaspy, and the typical operational value you can expect when the PT99 reports into a fleet or asset management system.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. This page focuses on non-sensitive, user-facing information; for the most current and device-specific technical details consult the manufacturer documentation linked below.

## Feature Overview

The PT99 is a compact personal asset tracker designed for flexible reporting and remote control. It supports multiple tracking modes, remote arming and disarming, audible and vibration alerts, and several power saving options that extend deployment life when frequent charging is not possible.

- Multiple tracking modes including on command, fixed time intervals, and scheduled clock reporting for predictable location updates
- Remote control options to arm or disarm the device using SMS, a platform command, or a phone call
- Location reporting with latitude and longitude, speed, direction, and odometer readings returned via SMS or platform reporting
- Alarms for vibration, SOS, and overspeed conditions alongside a built-in shock sensor for event triggering and power saving
- A GPS assist function and LBS coordinate checking to improve location availability in weak-signal environments
- Voice monitoring and two-way talking to listen or communicate with the device remotely

## Core Features of TopTen - PT99

- Track on demand, by time interval, or by scheduled clock reporting
- Arm and disarm remotely via SMS, Plaspy platform command, or phone call
- Location details delivered as coordinates and a Google Maps URL for quick viewing
- Vibration alarm and shock sensor to detect movement and conserve power when idle
- SOS alarm for immediate alerting of a preconfigured contact or platform event
- Over-speed alert to notify when the tracked asset exceeds a set speed threshold
- A-GPS assist and LBS coordinate check to improve fix performance in challenging areas
- Voice monitoring and two-way talk for remote listening or short voice communication

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a PT99 is connected to Plaspy, its reporting and alerts become visible through Plaspy's monitoring tools. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and presents location and event data in a consistent interface so operators can take action quickly.

- Location updates appear on the Plaspy map using coordinates supplied by the device and can be accessed through standard device views
- Alarms such as SOS, vibration, shock, and over-speed are logged as events in Plaspy for immediate operator attention
- Remote commands used to arm or disarm the tracker can be issued from Plaspy when the device is online and configured for platform control
- Odometer, speed, and directional data reported by the PT99 are available in event logs and recent telemetry for operational review
- Power saving modes and battery low alerts surface as device state indicators so users can plan maintenance or charge cycles

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal asset tracking for portable equipment or high-value items where periodic location checks are needed
- Temporary vehicle or trailer monitoring using scheduled or on-demand reporting
- Remote monitoring of lone workers or caretaking scenarios where SOS and two-way voice are useful
- Long-term deployments that rely on extreme power save modes to minimize maintenance
- Security patrols or mobile assets where vibration and shock detection provide early warning of tampering

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and behavior can differ across firmware versions; some functions may be added or altered by manufacturer updates
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may change which alarms or reporting modes are available
- Installation method and wiring can affect available functions; certain features may require specific wiring or accessory connections
- Two-way voice and SMS-based commands depend on local cellular network conditions and service availability
- Verify alarm configuration and reporting intervals after installation to ensure they meet your operational requirements
