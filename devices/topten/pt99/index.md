# TopTen - PT99

<div className="device-logo">
![PT99](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The TopTen PT99 is a personal asset GPS tracker designed for flexible location monitoring and basic security oversight. It targets scenarios where visibility and occasional remote interaction are important, such as vehicle or valuable item tracking and personal safety monitoring. Key capabilities described for the PT99 include configurable tracking modes like on command, interval reporting, and scheduled clock reports, remote arm and disarm via SMS platform or phone call, address and coordinate reporting with Google Maps links, odometer reporting, and a set of alarms for vibration, SOS, overspeed, and low battery.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the PT99 can supply the location updates, alarms, and status messages that Plaspy uses to provide centralized monitoring, alerts, and reporting. Its A GPS and LBS assisted positioning plus multiple power save modes make the PT99 a practical candidate for managed tracking workflows where intermittent reporting, long battery life, and remote alerting are important. Plaspy can consolidate PT99 events into live maps, historical playback, and operational dashboards for fleet or asset oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Multiple tracking modes including on demand, timed intervals, and scheduled clock reporting for flexible visibility
- Remote control options to arm or disarm the device using SMS commands the platform or phone calls
- Location reporting that includes coordinates, speed, direction, and odometer readings with Google Maps link capability
- Built in alarms for vibration, SOS, overspeed, and battery low level to support prompt incident notification
- A GPS and LBS assisted positioning for improved coverage in challenging signal areas
- Four power saving modes including an extreme power save option to extend deployment intervals
- Voice monitoring and two way talking for remote audio checks where needed

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the PT99 feeds location and event messages into a single platform for monitoring and operational control. Plaspy can use the device reports and alarms to create actionable views, alerts, and historical records that help teams manage assets and respond to incidents.

- Receive and display real time and scheduled location updates on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- Consolidate alarms such as SOS, vibration, overspeed, and low battery into platform alerts and notifications
- Use periodic and on demand reports for historical playback and route review in Plaspy reporting tools
- Apply platform features like status dashboards and alert routing to PT99 events for operational oversight
- Combine PT99 location streams with other fleet data in Plaspy for centralized asset management

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for lone workers or vulnerable individuals needing occasional location checks
- Vehicle tracking for small fleets or single vehicles requiring periodic reporting and overspeed alerts
- Valuable asset protection for equipment and cargo that benefit from remote alarms and location links
- Long term asset deployments where power saving modes reduce maintenance and charging frequency
- Remote audio checks or two way communication for security or caretaker scenarios

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT99 is a practical option for organizations that need straightforward asset visibility with a mix of remote control and alerting features. Its variety of tracking modes and power saving options make it adaptable to both frequent report environments and long deployment scenarios. For teams using Plaspy, the PT99 provides the kinds of location and alarm data that the platform leverages for maps, alerts, and reporting without requiring complex configuration.

Because the PT99 pairs flexible reporting with built in alarms and audio monitoring, it can be a useful fit for operations that balance occasional live checks with automated alerts. Plaspy can help centralize PT99 data alongside other devices so you get a unified operational view across assets and workflows.
