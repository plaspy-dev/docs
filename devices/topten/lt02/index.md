# TopTen - LT02

<div className="device-logo">
![LT02](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The TopTen LT02 is a mini GPS tracker designed for vehicle tracking and basic telematics. It combines location reporting with alarm and monitoring functions, allowing owners and managers to check a vehicle's physical address including city and street, receive movement and over speed alerts, and monitor engine on status. The LT02 also includes a data logger capable of storing thousands of waypoints and provides odometer and vehicle voltage awareness for simple operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LT02 can feed its location and event data into the Plaspy platform to provide centralized visibility and fleet level insights. Compatibility means the LT02's core functions such as position updates, movement and engine alerts, and stored route history can be surfaced in Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and routine operational use without losing the device level features like SMS or app control.

## Key Highlights

- Mini form factor suitable for discreet vehicle installation and general use
- Location reporting available via SMS web and app control for flexible access
- Alarm functions including movement alarm, over speed alarm, and engine on alarm
- Data logger with capacity for thousands of waypoints for route reconstruction
- Vehicle voltage detection and odometer tracking for basic vehicle health and usage insight
- Hardware watchdog and power saving modes to support reliability and extended operation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LT02 supplies location updates and event notifications that the platform uses to display real time status and historical tracks. Plaspy aggregates those inputs so operators can monitor multiple LT02 devices together and act on alerts from a central dashboard.

- Map based visibility of current position and recent route history recorded by the LT02
- Centralized alerts for movement, overspeed, and engine events routed into Plaspy notifications
- Use of stored waypoints to generate trip reports and reconstruct past routes for review
- Odometer and voltage readings available in Plaspy for operational reporting and basic diagnostics
- Consolidated views for mixed fleets allowing LT02 devices to be managed alongside other compatible trackers

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet monitoring for small commercial fleets that need basic location and event alerts
- Vehicle security and theft recovery using movement and location reporting
- Rental or shared vehicle tracking where odometer and address reporting are helpful
- Motorcycle and car monitoring thanks to a voltage range suitable for both vehicle types
- Historical route review and trip logging using the onboard data logger

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LT02 is a practical choice when you need a compact, multi function tracker that balances location reporting with simple alarms and data logging. For organizations that want to combine device level controls with a centralized monitoring platform, the LT02 provides the core inputs Plaspy needs to present locations, events, and route history in a unified view.

Paired with Plaspy, the LT02 helps reduce the overhead of tracking multiple vehicles by bringing position data and alerts into a single platform for monitoring and reporting. Its combination of address reporting, movement and engine alerts, plus stored waypoints makes it a sensible option for owners and fleet managers looking for straightforward visibility without unnecessary complexity.
