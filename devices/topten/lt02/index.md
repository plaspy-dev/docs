# TopTen - LT02

![LT02](./Logo.jpg)

The TopTen LT02 mini GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a range of features and functions to help you keep track of your vehicle. Whether you want to monitor your car's location, receive alerts for various events, or ensure the safety of your vehicle, the LT02 has got you covered.

One of the standout features of the LT02 is its ability to track your vehicle using SMS, web, or an app. This means you can easily keep an eye on your car's whereabouts no matter where you are. Additionally, the tracker also functions as a car alarm, allowing you to arm or disarm it using SMS, web, or a phone call.

With the LT02, you can also check the real physical address of your car, including the city and street name. This can be incredibly useful in case of theft or if you need to locate your vehicle in a crowded area. The tracker also has a smart engine ON/OFF status detection feature, which provides you with real-time information about the status of your car's engine.

Other notable features of the LT02 include a data logger that can store up to 5000 waypoints, detection of the car's vehicle voltage, an odometer function, over-speed alarm, movement alarm, engine on alarm, and a voltage range suitable for motorcycles and cars. The tracker is designed with reliability in mind, featuring a robust framework with a hardware watchdog. It also offers flexible work modes with an extreme power-saving design.

Overall, the TopTen LT02 mini GPS tracker is a feature-packed device that provides you with the tools you need to monitor and protect your vehicle. Whether you're a concerned car owner or a fleet manager, this tracker offers the functionality and reliability you're looking for.
