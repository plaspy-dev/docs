# TopTen - TK510

![TK510](./Logo.jpg)

The TopTen TK510 is a versatile and feature-rich vehicle GPS tracker that offers a wide range of functions and features to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle. With its GPS tracking capabilities, you can easily track your vehicle in real-time, either on command or at regular intervals. You can also track the location of your vehicle by sending an SMS and receive detailed information such as latitude, longitude, speed, direction, and even the odometer reading.

One of the standout features of the TK510 is its RFID car alarm with long-distance tag. This allows for active RFID driver ID identification, providing an extra layer of security and ensuring that only authorized individuals can access and operate the vehicle. Additionally, the TK510 supports automatic arm/disarm functions using a 2.4G RFID tag, making it convenient and hassle-free to secure your vehicle.

The TK510 also offers a range of alerts and alarms to keep you informed and protected. It has over-speed alert, geo-fence alert, and movement alarm features to notify you of any unauthorized or suspicious activities. In the event of an emergency, the TK510 can even snap a photo and send it via MMS or email, providing visual evidence of any incidents.

With its wide working voltage range and flexible power-saving modes, the TK510 is suitable for use in both cars and big trucks. It also comes with a built-in shock sensor for power saving and triggering alarms, as well as a rechargeable backup battery to ensure continuous operation even if the car battery is cut off or low.

Overall, the TopTen TK510 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers advanced tracking capabilities, enhanced security features, and convenient remote control options. Whether you need to track your personal vehicle or manage a fleet of vehicles, the TK510 is a reliable choice.
