# TopTen - TK208

![TK208](./Logo.jpg)

The TopTen TK208 is an OBD2 3G GPS tracker designed for vehicle tracking and security. It combines position reporting with optional RFID driver identification and a suite of alarm features, aimed at improving vehicle monitoring and driver accountability. The device description highlights onboard event logging, alarm detection for movement and engine status, and several optional identification methods that make it suitable for mixed fleet environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK208 can be integrated into a centralized tracking platform to provide location visibility, alarm forwarding, and historical route playback. Plaspy users can take advantage of the TK208's reporting modes and driver identification options to link vehicles with drivers, monitor security events, and include the device's recorded waypoints in fleet reports and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- OBD2 form factor designed for direct vehicle connection and continuous power
- GPS tracking with flexible reporting modes including command, timed interval, or distance based updates
- Optional active RFID driver ID or Mifare1 IC card reading for driver identification and access control
- Built in alarms and event detection including engine on off status, overspeed alert, geo fence alert, movement alarm, and engine on alarm
- Shock sensor, power failure alarm, and built in rechargeable backup battery for continued operation during power interruptions
- Offline data logger with capacity for multiple waypoints for later upload and analysis
- Wide working voltage range to support a broad range of vehicle types

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TK208 provides vehicle position and event information to the platform for visualization, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy can display live location updates and historical tracks from the device while mapping security events to platform alerts for operational response.

- Real time location and status updates visible in Plaspy maps and vehicle lists
- Alarm and event forwarding to Plaspy so over speed, geo fence, movement, and power loss events generate platform notifications
- Driver identification data from RFID can be associated with trips and driver logs in Plaspy for accountability and reporting
- Offline logger data can be uploaded to Plaspy for route playback and retroactive analysis after reconnection
- Engine on off state and related events become part of vehicle status history for operational monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light commercial vehicles seeking combined tracking and driver ID
- Vehicle security and anti theft monitoring for corporate cars and service vehicles
- Driver accountability and shift handover tracking using RFID identification
- Rental or leasing fleets where driver assignment and event logs are required
- Delivery and field service operations that need reliable tracking and event based alerts
