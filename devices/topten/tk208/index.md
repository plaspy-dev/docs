# TopTen - TK208

![TK208](./Logo.jpg)

The TopTen TK208 is a powerful OBD2 3G GPS tracker designed for vehicles. It combines GPS tracking with RFID car alarm functionality, making it an ideal choice for fleet management and vehicle security. With its high-performance ARM7 processor, the TK208 offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities.

One of the standout features of the TK208 is its active RFID driver ID identification or Mifare1 IC card reading capability \(optional\). This allows for easy driver identification and monitoring, ensuring that only authorized individuals are operating the vehicle. The TK208 can track the vehicle on command, by time interval, or by distance, providing real-time location updates.

In addition to tracking, the TK208 offers a range of alarm features to enhance vehicle security. It can detect the engine ON/OFF status without any extra connections and can be armed or disarmed via SMS or phone call. The optional 2.4G RFID tag enables automatic arm/disarm functionality. The TK208 also supports features such as engine on alarm, over-speed alert, geo-fence alert, and movement alarm, providing comprehensive security for your vehicle.

The TK208 is equipped with a built-in shock sensor for power saving and triggering alarms. It has a wide working voltage range from 10V to 60VDC, making it compatible with a variety of vehicles. The tracker also features a power failure alarm and a built-in rechargeable backup battery, ensuring continuous operation even in the event of a power outage. With its 8M-bit offline data logger, the TK208 can store up to 9,090 waypoints, allowing for detailed tracking and analysis.

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the TopTen TK208 is an excellent choice for fleet management, vehicle security, and personal tracking. Whether you need to monitor your vehicles, protect against theft, or ensure driver accountability, the TK208 has you covered.
