# TopTen - TK108

![TK108](./Logo.jpg)

The TK108 Vehicle GPS Tracker from TopTen is a versatile device that offers more than just GPS tracking. It also functions as a simple alarm system, allowing you to arm or disarm it via SMS or phone call. This makes it a convenient and user-friendly option for vehicle security. 

One of the standout features of the TK108 is its ability to send alerts for engine on/off events and power failures. This ensures that you are promptly notified of any unauthorized use of your vehicle or any potential issues with its power supply. Additionally, the tracker has an SOS alarm function, providing an extra layer of safety and peace of mind.

The TK108 also supports analog input for fuel monitoring, allowing you to keep track of your vehicle's fuel consumption and detect any abnormalities. This can be particularly useful for fleet management or monitoring the fuel efficiency of your vehicles.

With its 8Mb data logger, the TK108 can store GPS waypoints even when offline, ensuring that you have a comprehensive record of your vehicle's movements. This can be especially valuable in situations where there is no cellular coverage or when you need to review historical data.

The TK108 boasts an industrial design and is CE and FCC certified, guaranteeing its quality and reliability. It offers a wide working voltage range of 6V-63VDC, making it suitable for various types of vehicles, including cars, buses, and big trucks.

Overall, the TK108 Vehicle GPS Tracker from TopTen is a feature-rich and dependable device that combines GPS tracking with alarm functionality. Its user-friendly SMS operation commands and support for multiple languages make it accessible to a wide range of users. Whether you need to track your vehicle's location, monitor fuel consumption, or enhance its security, the TK108 is a reliable choice.
