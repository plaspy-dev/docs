# TopTen

TopTen produces a range of GPS tracking devices used in vehicle tracking, asset monitoring, and mobile asset protection. Plaspy is compatible with TopTen devices and can ingest location and telemetry from these trackers so you can monitor assets, review history, and respond to alerts from a single platform.

## How TopTen devices work with Plaspy

TopTen devices transmit location and telemetry over cellular networks using industry standard protocols. Plaspy receives that data, normalizes it, and displays it on maps and dashboards alongside devices from other manufacturers. The integration lets you:

- View real time location and movement history
- Receive alerts and notifications based on configurable triggers
- Combine TopTen device data with other fleet and asset information in Plaspy

If you need assistance connecting a TopTen device to Plaspy, consult the device details visible in the catalog above and follow the device specific instructions found in Plaspy documentation.

## Common features found in TopTen devices

While specific functionality varies by model, TopTen devices commonly include the capabilities fleet and asset managers expect from modern GPS trackers:

- Periodic and event based location updates
- Battery status and power monitoring
- Motion or ignition detection for vehicle tracking
- Configurable reporting intervals for battery life and data usage balancing
- Basic tamper and SOS reporting where applicable

For exact capabilities of each device, view the model catalog above where each device links to full technical details.

## Typical use cases for TopTen GPS trackers

TopTen devices are used in a variety of scenarios, including:

- Fleet tracking for light and heavy vehicles
- Asset monitoring for trailers, equipment, and rentals
- Long term location history for compliance and auditing
- Remote monitoring for non powered assets using battery optimized devices

Plaspy helps turn device telemetry into operational insights so teams can reduce downtime and improve asset utilization.

If you manage mixed fleets or are evaluating a tracking platform, Plaspy simplifies day to day monitoring and long term reporting for TopTen devices.
