# TopTen - MT35

![MT35](./)

The TopTen MT35 is a compact 3G GPS tracker designed for vehicle tracking across a wide range of regions. It supports both 3G WCDMA and 2G networks and is built to provide location reporting via SMS, web, or a mobile app. The MT35 includes practical features such as an onboard data logger capable of storing thousands of waypoints, an odometer function, and multiple alarm types for over-speed, movement, vibration, engine on, and power loss. Its compact and waterproof design makes it suitable for use in motorcycles, cars, and larger vehicles.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MT35 can feed location and event data into the Plaspy platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can display the MT35's reported physical address and support handling of alarms and historical routes, making the device a useful option for both individual vehicle tracking and fleet monitoring scenarios.

## Key Highlights

- Multi network compatibility with broad regional band support for flexible deployment
- Multiple reporting methods including SMS, web, and mobile app access for location queries
- Onboard data logger with capacity for thousands of waypoints for route history and playback
- Suite of alarms including over speed, movement, vibration, engine on, power failure, and optional SOS
- Compact, waterproof form factor suitable for motorcycles, cars, and larger vehicles
- Wide operating voltage range for compatibility with a variety of vehicle power systems
- Optional features such as SOS, voice monitoring, RFID, and wireless immobilizer for added security where available

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the MT35 transmits location updates and event notifications that Plaspy ingests to provide real time monitoring, alerting, and reporting across devices and fleets. Plaspy acts as the central interface for visualizing the MT35's position, viewing alarm history, and accessing stored route data.

- Real time location display in Plaspy including reported physical address details such as city and street when available
- Forwarding and presentation of alarms and events like over speed, movement, engine on, vibration, power failure, and optional SOS
- Historical route playback and waypoint analysis using the MT35 data logger records
- Fleet level visibility and aggregated reporting for odometer readings and trip summaries
- Remote control actions such as arming and disarming where supported by both the device configuration and Plaspy platform

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal car tracking for real time location and security alerts
- Motorcycle and light vehicle tracking where compact and waterproof hardware is important
- Fleet management for small to medium vehicle fleets needing route history and event reporting
- Vehicle security and recovery workflows leveraging alarms and optional SOS or voice monitoring
- Operational oversight where odometer and movement records support maintenance planning or trip reconciliation

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MT35 is a practical choice for organizations and individuals who need a versatile tracker that works across different regional networks and provides multiple reporting channels. Its combination of a robust set of alarms, substantial onboard waypoint storage, and a compact waterproof enclosure makes it adaptable for varied vehicle types and operational environments.

Paired with Plaspy, the MT35 becomes part of a centralized monitoring and reporting solution that helps teams maintain visibility over assets, respond to alerts, and analyze historical movement. Optional security features and remote control capabilities can be integrated where available and configured, offering further flexibility for fleet and vehicle security use cases.
