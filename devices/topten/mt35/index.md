# TopTen - MT35

The TopTen MT35 is a versatile 3G GPS tracker that is compatible with both 3G WCDMA networks and 2G networks. It supports various frequencies, including 3G bands 1, 2, 5, and 8, as well as 2G bands 850MHz, 900MHz, 1800MHz, and 1900MHz. This wide compatibility ensures that the tracker can be used in various regions and with different network providers.

One of the standout features of the MT35 is its ability to track the location of your vehicle through SMS, web, or a dedicated mobile app. You can easily monitor the real-time physical address of your car, including the city and street name. Additionally, the tracker offers car alarm functions, allowing you to arm or disarm the system through SMS, web, or phone call.

The MT35 is designed with user convenience in mind. It features a compact and waterproof design, making it suitable for use in motorcycles, cars, or even large trucks. The tracker also includes a data logger that can store up to 5520 waypoints, ensuring that you have a comprehensive record of your vehicle's movements. Other notable features include an odometer function, over-speed alarm, movement alarm, engine on alarm, vibration alarm, power failure alarm, and optional SOS alarm and voice monitoring capabilities.

With its reliable hardware and flexible work modes, the MT35 offers a reliable and efficient tracking solution for your vehicle. It supports a wide voltage range of 10V-60VDC, ensuring compatibility with various types of vehicles. The tracker also includes A-GPS function for fast GPS signal acquisition, as well as support for RFID and wireless immobilizer functions \(optional\). Whether you need to track your personal car or manage a fleet of vehicles, the TopTen MT35 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that will meet your needs.
