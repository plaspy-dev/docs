# Teltonika - FMP100

![FMP100](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMP100 is a compact, plug & play GPS tracker engineered for fast deployment and Plaspy compatible integration. Designed to run directly from a vehicle cigarette lighter socket, the FMP100 brings instant, reliable GPS tracking to fleet managers, car sharing operators and rental services without hardwiring. Its lightweight installation model makes it ideal for temporary deployments, short-term rentals, or any use case that requires rapid provisioning of real-time tracking and telemetry.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMP100 pairs native GPS positioning with Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) sensor support and event signaling \(built-in button, RGB LED and buzzer\) to deliver a practical telematics package. The device supports remote configuration and firmware workflows via Teltonika’s knowledge base and FOTA/Web tools, enabling centralized management and fast integration into Plaspy-powered dashboards for real-time tracking, alerts and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plug & play installation via vehicle cigarette lighter — no hardwiring required for quick fleet deployment.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking, alerts and centralized fleet management workflows.
- 2G GSM connectivity \(B2/B3/B5/B8\) plus Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) for external sensor telemetry.
- Built-in user button with audible \(buzzer\) and visual \(RGB LED\) feedback for driver interaction and panic events.
- Integrated 1A charger output to power or charge smartphones and small accessories while tracking.
- BLE support for temperature, humidity, magnet detection and movement when paired with EYE Beacons/EYE Sensors.
- Remote configuration and management supported via Teltonika’s FOTA/Web tools and product wiki.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Teltonika FMP100 sends standard telematics data that Plaspy can consume for live location, event alerts and sensor telemetry. After physical installation in the vehicle and pairing BLE accessories where needed, the tracker provides continuous GPS positions and sensor inputs to Plaspy for mapping, geofencing, and report generation. Remote management and firmware updates use the vendor’s documented workflows to keep devices synchronized and secure.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates for accurate vehicle tracking on Plaspy maps.
- Button-triggered alarm events \(panic or trip-mode switching\) relayed as alerts into Plaspy.
- BLE sensor telemetry — temperature, humidity, magnet detection and motion — delivered to Plaspy when paired with compatible BLE accessories.
- Device status and driver feedback via RGB LED and buzzer reported in logs and event streams.
- Remote configuration and firmware management through Teltonika FOTA/Web tools integrated into Plaspy workflows \(refer to Teltonika documentation for configuration details\).

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: rapid deployment of vehicles to Plaspy for route monitoring and operational telemetry without workshop hardwiring.
- Car sharing and rental services: temporary or short-term installations to track location, usage events and provide panic alerts.
- Temporary deployments and seasonal fleets: pop-in tracking for pilot programs, events or seasonal vehicle fleets.
- Cargo and temperature-sensitive loads: BLE temperature/humidity sensors paired with the FMP100 report environmental telemetry to Plaspy for cold-chain visibility.
- Asset security and event monitoring: button-triggered alarms and magnet/movement BLE sensors help detect unauthorized movement or tampering.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Teltonika FMP100 is an attractive choice for organizations that prioritize speed of deployment, simplicity and Plaspy compatible real-time tracking. Its plug & play form factor minimizes installation time and complexity compared with hardwired trackers, while BLE sensor support expands telemetry beyond basic location to include temperature, humidity and movement detection. Built-in driver feedback \(RGB LED and buzzer\) and a user button for alarms provide immediate, locally visible event handling that integrates into Plaspy’s alerting and reporting systems.

For operations that require ignition inputs, immobilizer control or advanced fuel monitoring, consider hardwired Plaspy-compatible models specifically designed for those integrations. The FMP100 focuses on portability, rapid provisioning and BLE-enabled telemetry; for firmware, certification details, downloads and ordering assistance consult Teltonika support or the product wiki. Plaspy integration is straightforward: combine the FMP100’s live GPS and BLE telemetry with Plaspy dashboards to gain real-time visibility, improve fleet utilization and respond faster to anti-theft and safety events.
