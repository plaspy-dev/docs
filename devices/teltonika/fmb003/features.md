# Teltonika - FMB003 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the Teltonika FMB003 and how it is used with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker’s practical capabilities for fleet managers and technical readers and explains the kinds of telemetry and operational value the device can deliver when reporting into Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a compatibility and capability guide and consult official manufacturer documentation for the most current device-specific details.

## Feature Overview

The Teltonika FMB003 is an ultra-compact OBD-II plug-and-play tracker designed to provide OEM-level vehicle telemetry into fleet platforms such as Plaspy. Its small form factor and direct OBD connection make it suitable for rapid deployment across passenger car fleets where accurate mileage and OEM fuel or battery parameters matter.

- Plug-and-play OBD-II installation for fast deployment across passenger vehicles
- Reads OEM OBD parameters including odometer and fuel/battery level metrics for more accurate telemetry
- Compact 29.1 mm wide casing for discreet placement and broad passenger car compatibility
- Quad-band 2G GSM connectivity for wide cellular coverage in supported regions
- Works with remote device management tools for simplified provisioning and firmware updates

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMB003

- OBD-II interface for reading OEM vehicle parameters such as odometer and fuel/battery level where exposed by the vehicle
- Plug-and-play design suitable for passenger car OBD ports to minimize installation time and field work
- Compact physical form factor (29.1 mm width) designed for discreet placement
- Quad-band 2G GSM support covering B2, B3, B5, and B8 bands for network reach where 2G is available
- Compatibility with Teltonika remote management tools such as FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator for configuration and firmware management
- Packaging and ordering options including standard and custom bundles to support operational rollouts

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMB003 forwards its OEM telemetry so Plaspy can present location and vehicle parameter data in dashboards, reports, and alerts. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol, and devices are typically provisioned to send data to the Plaspy server endpoint for ingestion.

- OEM odometer and fuel or battery level values are visible in Plaspy for accurate mileage logging and fuel monitoring
- Real-time vehicle position and status appear on Plaspy maps and in live tracking views for operational oversight
- Device events and telemetry feed into Plaspy reporting and alerting so teams can build workflows around mileage, fuel usage, and vehicle status
- Remote provisioning and firmware updates performed via Teltonika tools complement Plaspy device lifecycle processes during large rollouts
- Devices configured to report into Plaspy can use standard transport options; Plaspy detects the tracker protocol automatically for straightforward onboarding

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental and car-sharing fleets needing discreet installation and verified odometer records for billing and disputes
- Delivery and service fleets requiring rapid, low-effort deployment across many passenger vehicles
- Fleet management and logistics teams that rely on accurate mileage and OEM fuel/battery data for reporting and optimization
- Electric vehicle programs where OEM battery metrics from OBD help monitor range and usage when available
- Service and maintenance scheduling based on reliable mileage records for planned servicing and warranty tracking
