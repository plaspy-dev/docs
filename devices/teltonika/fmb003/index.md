# Teltonika FMB003

The Teltonika FMB003 is an ultra-compact OBD-II GPS tracker designed for plug-and-play installation in passenger cars. As a Plaspy compatible tracker, it delivers OEM-grade telemetry and discreet installation that make it a practical choice for fleet managers who need accurate odometer and fuel/battery metrics in real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.

The FMB003 emphasizes simplicity and reliability: it plugs directly into the vehicle’s OBD port, reads OEM OBD parameters, and forwards that data to fleet platforms such as Plaspy for streamlined telemetry, fuel monitoring, mileage logging, and service optimization. Its small 29.1 mm wide casing and broad vehicle coverage help rental, car-sharing, delivery, and electric vehicle programs get reliable telemetry without complex installation.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible plug-and-play GPS tracker that installs quickly via OBD-II for fast deployment across fleets.
- OEM data access for accurate odometer readings and real fuel level/battery metrics rather than estimated CAN values.
- Ultra-compact 29.1 mm wide form factor for discreet placement and passenger car compatibility.
- Quad-band 2G \(GSM\) connectivity covering B2, B3, B5 and B8 for widespread cellular availability.
- Designed for rental, car-sharing, logistics, delivery services, and EV monitoring where precise mileage and fuel/battery telemetry are critical.
- Straightforward integration with fleet management platforms and remote device management tools for scalable deployments.
- Available in standard and custom packaging options to suit operational rollout needs.

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMB003 forwards OEM-level telemetry and vehicle status to deliver actionable insights. Plaspy ingests the device data for real-time tracking, alerts, reporting, and automated workflows—helping fleets reduce fuel waste, verify mileage, and monitor vehicle health with trusted OEM parameters.

- Real-time tracking and telemetry: vehicle position and OEM data feed into Plaspy dashboards and maps.

- Easy remote provisioning and device management via supported Teltonika tools, simplifying large-scale rollouts that connect into Plaspy.
