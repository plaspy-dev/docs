# Teltonika - FMC00A

<div className="device-logo">
![FMC00A](./logo..png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMC00A is a compact Plug & Play OBD II tracker built for vehicles in North America. It provides cellular connectivity with 4G LTE Cat 1 and fallback to 3G, and is designed to deliver continuous positioning using multi GNSS systems. The device can read OEM vehicle parameters such as odometer and fuel level, and includes event detection features like excessive idling, towing alerts, crash indication, and unplug detection.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMC00A can forward vehicle location and telematics to Plaspy for centralized fleet oversight. That compatibility makes it a practical option for organizations that want quick installation, richer vehicle data from OBD II, and visibility into trips and alerts through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plug & Play OBD II form factor for fast deployment across compatible vehicles
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with fallback to 3G to maintain coverage in North America
- Ability to read OBD OEM parameters including odometer and fuel level for better operational insight
- Multi GNSS support for improved position accuracy across different regions
- Built in event detection such as excessive idling, towing, unplug, crash, and jamming alerts
- Support for remote configuration and firmware updates using manufacturer tools
- Durable compact design with IP41 rating and wide operating temperature range

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMC00A sends location and supported vehicle data to the platform where it can be visualized, analyzed, and acted on. Plaspy ingests the telematics feed to provide live tracking, historical reporting, and configurable alerts that help operations teams manage fleets more efficiently.

- Real time vehicle location and trip visibility in the Plaspy dashboard
- Monitor odometer and fuel related data to support maintenance planning and usage reporting
- Configure geofence alerts and event notifications for towing, unplugging, and excessive idling
- Use historical trip playback and reports to analyze routes, idle time, and operational patterns
- Centralize alerts and fleet status to support dispatching and operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operators who need quick plug in rollout and centralized monitoring for North American vehicle fleets
- Rental and leasing companies tracking mileage and fuel related data for billing and maintenance
- Small and medium sized fleets seeking driver behavior insight for green driving initiatives
- Service and delivery businesses that require reliable live location and trip history for dispatch
- Asset protection scenarios using unplug detection and towing alerts to identify potential theft or tampering
