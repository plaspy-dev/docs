# Teltonika - FMC00A

![FMC00A](./logo..png)

The Teltonika FMC00A is an advanced Plug & Play OBD-II tracker with 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity, specifically designed for North America. It offers reliable network coverage with fallback to 3G, ensuring future-proof support for your fleet. With the FMC00A, you can easily track and monitor your vehicles in real-time, thanks to its advanced features and capabilities.

One of the standout features of the FMC00A is its ability to read OBD OEM parameters, providing you with real-time data on odometer readings and fuel levels. This allows you to accurately monitor the performance and efficiency of your vehicles. Additionally, the FMC00A supports a wide range of vehicles and data, with a comprehensive list of supported manufacturer models and parameters.

Installation of the FMC00A is effortless, thanks to its Plug & Play design. Simply plug the device into the OBD-II port of your vehicle and start tracking. The FMC00A is equipped with a Quectel EG91-NA module, which provides LTE \(CAT1\)/3G/ GNSS/BLUETOOTH technology for seamless connectivity. It supports GPS, GLONASS, GALILEO, BEIDOU, SBAS, QZSS, DGPS, and AGPS for accurate positioning.

The FMC00A also offers a range of additional features, including accelerometer sensors for green driving and over speeding detection, jamming detection, GNSS fuel counter, excessive idling detection, unplug detection, towing detection, crash detection, auto geofence, manual geofence, and trip scenarios. It supports various sleep modes to optimize power consumption, and can be easily configured and updated using FOTA WEB, FOTA, Teltonika Configurator, or the FMBT mobile application.

With its compact and durable design, the Teltonika FMC00A is built to withstand harsh operating environments. It has an IP41 ingress protection rating and operates in temperatures ranging from -40 °C to +85 °C. The FMC00A is also certified and approved by regulatory bodies such as IC and FCC, ensuring its compliance with industry standards.
