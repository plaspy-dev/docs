# Teltonika - FMB910

![FMB910](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMB910 is a compact 2G GPS tracker designed for cost conscious fleet management and basic vehicle protection. It delivers reliable real time tracking and historical location data, with built in accelerometer features for crash detection and support for external sensors and beacons via Bluetooth Low Energy. The unit is intended for straightforward deployment where simple, economical tracking and anti theft workflows are the primary requirements.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMB910 can forward location, event and sensor streams into the Plaspy platform to power live maps, alerts, reports and remote control workflows. This compatibility makes the FMB910 a practical option for small and medium fleets that want to combine affordable tracker hardware with Plaspy monitoring, reporting and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and economical 2G GPS tracker suitable for low cost fleet deployments where 2G is available
- Real time position reporting and location history for route visibility and recovery workflows
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer with configurable crash detection to support incident alerts and investigation
- I O driven remote control options for ignition and engine block monitoring to assist anti theft workflows
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for pairing external sensors and beacons to extend monitoring capabilities
- Supports eco driving telemetry to help fleets monitor driving behaviour and improve efficiency

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMB910 sends position updates, accelerometer events, I O state changes and Bluetooth sensor data that Plaspy ingests into dashboards, alerts and reports. Plaspy translates those incoming streams into actionable operational views and automated rules so teams can monitor fleets, respond to incidents, and manage anti theft procedures from a single platform.

- Live location updates and historical tracks appear on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility and route analysis
- Crash detection and accelerometer events flow into Plaspy alerts to notify operations teams and support incident review
- Ignition and engine block status reported via device inputs enable immobilization and anti theft workflows when configured
- Bluetooth sensor and beacon data are forwarded for cargo and environment monitoring within Plaspy dashboards
- Telemetry streams can be included in Plaspy reports and rules for eco driving monitoring and operational insights

## Typical Use Cases

- Cost effective fleet management for small and medium vehicle fleets requiring real time tracking
- Anti theft and stolen vehicle recovery where I O based remote control and tracking improve recovery chances
- Incident detection and crash reporting using accelerometer events to support safety programs
- Cargo and environment monitoring using Bluetooth sensors for temperature sensitive or tamper aware shipments
- Simple telematics and eco driving initiatives to track driving behaviour and identify efficiency improvements

The Teltonika FMB910 provides an economical entry point for organizations that need dependable location reporting, basic telemetry and simple remote control options in areas where 2G remains available.
