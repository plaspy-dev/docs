# Teltonika - FMB120

<div className="device-logo">
![FMB120](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMB120 is a compact, cost sensitive GPS tracker designed for basic vehicle telematics and remote fleet monitoring. Intended primarily for deployment where economical cellular connectivity is suitable, the device provides continuous location tracking, essential status monitoring and remote immobilization capability. Its small form factor and multiple packaging choices make it practical for rental cars, taxis, logistics vehicles, public transport and private vehicles that need dependable, low cost tracking and simple telemetry.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMB120 serves as a straightforward building block for real time tracking, anti theft workflows and sensor driven monitoring within the Plaspy platform. When integrated, the tracker streams position and event data to Plaspy dashboards and maps, and can extend monitoring with Bluetooth LE peripherals, 1-Wire temperature probes and RFID or iButton identification. This combination enables live location visibility, alerts and basic remote control from Plaspy for day to day fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Compact design aimed at cost conscious vehicle telematics deployments and fleet monitoring.
- Continuous GPS location tracking for live maps and route history.
- 2G cellular connectivity for economical telemetry in regions where 2G is available.
- Remote immobilizer support to support anti theft workflows and remote vehicle control.
- Bluetooth LE support for external beacons and sensors plus 1-Wire port for temperature probes and RFID iButton reader connectivity.
- Standard digital I O for ignition and vehicle event monitoring and flexible packaging options for different procurement needs.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The FMB120 connects over its cellular link to transmit location, event and sensor data to Plaspy. Once the device is onboarded, Plaspy ingests the incoming telemetry and exposes it through live maps, alerting rules and reports so operations teams can monitor vehicles and respond to incidents.

- Real time position updates and historical playback available in Plaspy maps and timelines.
- Digital input events such as ignition or door signals appear in Plaspy for trip logging and operational rules.
- Remote immobilizer commands can be issued from Plaspy to support anti theft responses and fleet control workflows.
- Bluetooth LE sensor and beacon data and 1 Wire temperature probe readings can trigger alerts and monitoring rules inside Plaspy.
- Geofence alerts, movement notifications and automated reports help maintain oversight and compliance.

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental, taxi and car share fleets that need economical tracking, driver identification and trip logging.
- Small and mid sized logistics fleets seeking basic route visibility and theft protection.
- Temperature monitored cargo using 1 Wire or external BLE sensors for condition alerts.
- Public transport and service vehicles where compact, low cost trackers provide fleet awareness.
- Asset and security monitoring where door, movement or tamper events are required for alerts.
