# Teltonika - FMM001

![FMM001](./Logo.png)

The Teltonika FMM001 is a cutting-edge GPS tracker that combines advanced communication technology with a range of features to provide a comprehensive solution for various applications. This device is the first Plug and Track LTE CAT M1 OBD device with GNSS and Bluetooth connectivity, making it a versatile and powerful tool for fleet management, car rental and leasing, driver log-books, insurance telematics, and more.

One of the standout features of the FMM001 is its ability to read OBD II parameters, allowing for detailed monitoring and analysis of vehicle performance. Additionally, the device offers effortless installation and provides detailed accelerometer data, making it an ideal choice for applications such as green driving, over speeding detection, GNSS fuel counter, excessive idling detection, unplug detection, towing detection, crash detection, auto geofence, manual geofence, and trip tracking.

The FMM001 also supports various Bluetooth Low Energy sensors and beacons, allowing for seamless integration with other devices and systems. Configuration and firmware updates can be easily performed using the FOTA Web, FOTA, Teltonika Configurator \(USB, Bluetooth\), or the FMBT mobile application. The device also offers SMS and GPRS command capabilities for configuration, events, and debugging purposes.

In terms of power management, the FMM001 offers multiple sleep modes, including GPS sleep, online deep sleep, deep sleep, and ultra deep sleep, ensuring efficient use of battery power. Time synchronization can be achieved through GPS, NITZ, or NTP, providing accurate and reliable time data. Fuel monitoring is made possible through OBD II compatibility, while ignition detection can be achieved using the accelerometer, external power voltage, or engine RPM.

The Teltonika FMM001 is a feature-rich GPS tracker that offers unparalleled functionality and versatility. Whether you need to track a fleet of vehicles, monitor driver behavior, or optimize fuel consumption, this device is a reliable and powerful solution.
