# Teltonika - FMB002

<div className="device-logo">
![FMB002](./Logo.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMB002 is an ultra small OBDII plug and play GPS tracker designed for fast, low friction vehicle deployment. As an OBDII device it reads standard vehicle data and combines that capability with location tracking, GNSS positioning, cellular connectivity, Bluetooth Low Energy support, and an integrated accelerometer to support a wide range of telematics features. Its compact form factor and plug and play approach make it well suited to light commercial vehicles and small fleet rollouts.

Because the FMB002 exposes vehicle data through the OBDII interface and supports common telematics features such as trip tracking, geofencing, crash and towing detection, and fuel related monitoring, it is a natural fit for Plaspy. Plaspy can ingest the device data for live location, fleet visibility, alerts, and reporting, helping operators turn the FMB002 telemetry into operational insight without complex integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBDII form factor for fast installation and low deployment cost
- Compact and discreet design suited to light commercial vehicles and cars
- Integrated GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity for continuous tracking
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for auxiliary sensors and configuration options
- Accelerometer enabled events such as crash, towing, and harsh driving detection
- Support for over the air and Bluetooth based configuration and firmware updates
- Multiple sleep modes to help manage power behavior and vehicle electrical considerations

## How It Works with Plaspy

The FMB002 can be paired with Plaspy to provide live tracking, historical trips, and event driven alerts. Plaspy consumes the standard telemetry and event data the device produces and presents it via dashboards, reports, and alert systems so operations teams can monitor vehicles and drivers efficiently.

- Live position and movement visibility for each vehicle on the Plaspy map
- Event alerts for geofence breaches, crash or towing detection, and unplug notifications
- Trip history and driver log style records for compliance and operational review
- Fleet level reporting and analytics combining location, trip, and OBDII derived data
- Remote configuration and status checks from Plaspy using the device data Plaspy receives

## Typical Use Cases

- Driver log book and trip recording for compliance and accountability
