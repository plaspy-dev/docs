# Teltonika - FMM150

![FMM150](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMM150 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for modern fleet management and advanced vehicle telemetry. Built around LTE Cat M1 with NB‑IoT support and 2G fallback, the FMM150 brings reliable connectivity and deep CAN bus visibility to Plaspy users who need real‑time tracking, accurate vehicle diagnostics, and actionable fleet data across light vehicles, EVs, trucks, buses and special machinery.

The integrated CAN data processor reads more than 100 CAN parameters, including odometer, fuel level and consumption, and EV battery status, making the FMM150 a compelling choice for operators who want Plaspy-compatible telemetry, fuel monitoring, preventive maintenance insights and driver behavior analytics without complex integrations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT connectivity for wide-area, low-power cellular telemetry.
- Integrated CAN bus processing — extracts 100+ vehicle parameters for deep fleet telematics and diagnostics.
- Global deployment ready: multiple cellular bands and product variants \(ordering codes\) cover worldwide frequency sets with 2G fallback where applicable.
- Designed for fleet management, fuel monitoring, EV telemetry and preventive maintenance workflows.
- Accessory-ready: works with Teltonika CAN adapters, RFID readers and BLE beacons to extend identification and sensor capabilities.
- Supported by Teltonika remote management tools \(FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator\) for simplified configuration and updates.
- Commercial packaging and kit variants include power supply cables and optional micro USB or extended CAN parameter packages depending on the order code.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMM150 streams location and vehicle telemetry so dispatchers, fleet managers and maintenance teams get real‑time tracking and contextual diagnostics. Plaspy ingests the FMM150’s CAN‑derived signals alongside GPS location to enable geofencing, alerts and detailed reports for operational optimisation and anti‑theft workflows.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered over LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT \(with 2G fallback\), enabling continuous tracking and history playback in Plaspy.
- Ignition and engine status available via CAN parameters where vehicle ECUs expose those signals—useful for driver behavior and runtime reporting.
- Fuel monitoring and odometer readings sourced directly from CAN for accurate fuel consumption analysis and fleet fuel optimization in Plaspy.
- EV battery and power metrics for electric vehicles, allowing Plaspy to monitor state‑of‑charge trends and maintenance needs.
- Bluetooth sensors and BLE beacons supported via accessory integration, enabling asset tagging, proximity-based workflows and enhanced identification inside Plaspy.
