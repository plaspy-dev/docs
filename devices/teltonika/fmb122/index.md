# Teltonika - FMB122

<div className="device-logo">
![FMB122](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMB122 is a compact 2G GPS tracker built for fleet management and anti theft vehicle security. It provides concise real time position reporting and supports accessory inputs for identification and environmental sensing. The device is designed for vehicle deployments where straightforward GSM connectivity, compact installation, and external antenna options are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMB122 can forward its location and supported sensor data into Plaspy for monitoring, alerting, and reporting. Dual SIM operation, Bluetooth Low Energy sensor support, and 1 Wire identification expand the types of telemetry and events that Plaspy can process, making the FMB122 a practical choice for operators who need basic but reliable tracking integrated into Plaspy workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact 2G GPS tracker suited to fleet telematics and anti theft use cases.
- Dual SIM support to help maintain cellular connectivity and reduce roaming exposure.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for integration with wireless sensors and beacons.
- 1 Wire interface compatible with RFID iButton identification and temperature sensors.
- Optional external GNSS antenna for improved satellite reception in difficult mounts.
- Designed for vehicle installations with a range of available accessories and adapters.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMB122 transmits position data and supported sensor inputs so Plaspy can present live locations, events, and historical logs. Plaspy ingests the device signals and makes them available for geofencing, alerting, and operational reporting, helping teams translate device telemetry into actionable insights.

- Real time location updates from the FMB122 displayed on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- Driver or asset identification using 1 Wire RFID iButton inputs logged in Plaspy for audit and shift records.
- Environmental condition monitoring from 1 Wire temperature sensors and BLE sensors fed into Plaspy for alerts and logs.
- BLE sensor and beacon events integrated into Plaspy rules for door, movement, or proximity notifications.
- Dual SIM resilience helping maintain a telemetry stream to Plaspy in variable coverage areas.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for route oversight, dispatch visibility, and location histories.
- Anti theft monitoring with continuous location reporting and accessory based tamper alerts.
- Service vehicle management where compact, vehicle friendly hardware is required.
- Asset condition monitoring for temperature sensitive loads using external sensors.
- Driver identification and accountability using RFID iButton workflows.
