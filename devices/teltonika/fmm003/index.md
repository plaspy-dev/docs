# Teltonika - FMM003

<div className="device-logo">
![FMM003](./logo..png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMM003 is an OBD plug and play vehicle tracker built for reliable fleet management. Designed around LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT with fallback to 2G, the FMM003 delivers broad cellular coverage and position reporting while offering direct access to OEM vehicle parameters through OBD data reading. It supports multi GNSS systems for positioning and includes on-board storage and cellular connectivity options suitable for continuous tracking across a range of vehicle types.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMM003 can feed location, odometer and fuel level information into the Plaspy platform to support live monitoring, history, and operational reporting. Its OBD OEM parameter capability and supported vehicle list make it straightforward to determine which manufacturer parameters are available, and Plaspy can surface that telemetry alongside standard tracking data to improve fleet visibility and decision making.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD connection for quick vehicle installation and access to OEM parameters.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT cellular connectivity with fallback to 2G for wide area coverage.
- Ability to read odometer and fuel level from the vehicle and a supported vehicle list for parameter availability.
- Multi GNSS support for accurate positioning under diverse conditions.
- Built in memory and micro SIM support to retain data and enable continuous operation.
- Rugged design with IP41 rating and wide operating temperature range for demanding environments.
- Built in event features such as overspeed, geofence scenarios, towing and unplug detection for operational alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the FMM003 provides vehicle location and OEM-derived telemetry to the Plaspy platform so fleet managers can monitor assets and receive event-driven insights. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to display live status, historical routes, and vehicle condition indicators in dashboards and reports.

- Live location tracking and route history visible in Plaspy for operational oversight.
- Odometer and fuel level telemetry shown alongside location data to support fuel monitoring and maintenance scheduling.
- Event and alert handling for overspeed, geofence entry and exit, unplug and towing scenarios, and similar triggers.
- Reporting and analytics support for utilization, trips, and summary metrics relevant to fleet performance.
- Visibility into device connectivity and stored data so operators can investigate gaps or confirm recent activity.
- Device configuration and firmware updates remain managed with Teltonika tools while Plaspy consumes and displays resulting telemetry and status.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet managers tracking vehicle location, mileage and fuel use for cost control and routing.
- Rental and leasing operators monitoring odometer data and detecting unplug or towing events.
- Service and delivery fleets using geofences and trip scenarios to manage schedules and compliance.
- Operations that need consistent coverage across regions thanks to Cat M1 and NB IoT connectivity.
- Businesses that require compact OBD devices for quick deployment and minimal vehicle modification.

The FMM003 is a practical choice for organizations that want straightforward access to OEM vehicle data without complex installation. Its plug and play OBD form factor coupled with multi mode cellular coverage makes it particularly useful where rapid deployment and broad connectivity are priorities.
