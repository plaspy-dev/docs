# FMB900

Teltonika

The FMB900 is a low-cost, compact 2G vehicle tracker that delivers straightforward, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking for fleets, assets and field teams. Designed for basic tracking, vehicle security and eco-driving applications, the FMB900 combines a slim form factor with Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) connectivity to support external sensors and beacons — making it an economical choice when space, cost-efficiency and Plaspy integration matter.

Optimized for fleet management and simple telemetry, the FMB900 supports remote device management via Teltonika’s FOTA WEB and configuration through Teltonika Configurator. Its small height \(approximately 12 mm\) and hidden-install suitability make it ideal for anti-theft installations and discreet asset tracking while providing the core features needed for ignition control, immobilizer use and BLE sensor telemetry when connected to Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker solution for real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Compact, low-profile design \(~12 mm height\) for easy installation in tight or hidden spaces.
- Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) for wireless attachments: supports EYE Beacon and EYE Sensor for temperature, humidity, magnet and movement data.
- 2G \(GSM\) cellular connectivity on bands B2, B3, B5 and B8 — a cost-efficient option for basic telemetry.
- Fleet control features including remote engine blocking \(immobilizer\) and remote monitoring for anti-theft and safety measures.
- Remote device management via Teltonika FOTA WEB and configuration with Teltonika Configurator for scalable deployment.
- Simple package options with power cable included for straightforward installation and commissioning.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMB900 streams essential tracking and telemetry data to your Plaspy dashboard so you can monitor vehicles and assets in real time. The device’s BLE support expands Plaspy capabilities by feeding external sensor inputs into fleet reports and alerts, while remote management features simplify updates and configuration across a fleet.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy for live tracking and route replay.
- Engine status and remote engine blocking \(immobilizer\) support for anti-theft and remote immobilization workflows.
- BLE sensor data \(temperature, humidity, magnet status, movement\) available to Plaspy for expanded monitoring of cargo and tamper events.
- Telematics data that supports eco-driving analysis and fuel-efficiency improvements through driving behavior reports in Plaspy.
- Remote device management \(FOTA WEB\) and Teltonika Configurator compatibility for fast provisioning and firmware maintenance across your Plaspy-connected devices.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft: discreet installation and remote engine blocking \(immobilizer\) to reduce theft risk and recover vehicles faster.
- Eco-driving and fleet management: telemetry and driving behavior reporting to help managers reduce fuel consumption and improve driving habits.
- Temperature-sensitive loads: BLE-connected EYE Sensor provides temperature and humidity telemetry for cold chain or sensitive cargo when paired with Plaspy alerts.
- Asset and workforce tracking: small form factor for assets, trailers or field equipment where a compact 2G GPS tracker is required.
- Tamper and movement monitoring: magnet and movement detection via BLE beacons and sensors for door/tamper alerts routed to Plaspy.
