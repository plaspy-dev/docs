# Teltonika - FMB150

![FMB150](./logo..png)

The Teltonika FMB150 is an advanced 2G GPS tracker with an integrated CAN data processor. It is designed for reading CAN data from light and electric vehicles, trucks, buses, and special machinery. With its built-in CAN bus data reading chip, it can provide valuable insights into vehicle performance and diagnostics. This makes it an ideal choice for fleet management and vehicle tracking applications.

One of the standout features of the FMB150 is its extended product usage scenarios. It supports Bluetooth LE-based wireless connections, allowing you to connect external devices such as Low Energy beacons and sensors. This opens up a wide range of possibilities for monitoring temperature, humidity, magnet detection, and movement. Additionally, the FMB150 supports CAN data reading from electric vehicles, making it compatible with over 40 supported vehicles in the EV market. This expands its usage scenarios and brings new value to electric vehicle tracking.

In terms of specifications, the FMB150 features a Teltonika TM2500 module that supports GSM/GPRS/GNSS/Bluetooth technology. It has a GNSS receiver with support for GPS, GLONASS, GALILEO, BEIDOU, SBAS, QZSS, DGPS, and AGPS. The tracker has a tracking sensitivity of -165 dBM and a position accuracy of less than 2.5 meters CEP. It operates on quad-band 2G GSM networks and supports GPRS data transfer.

The FMB150 is designed to withstand harsh operating environments, with an operating temperature range of -40 °C to +85 °C and an IP41 ingress protection rating. It has multiple interface options, including digital inputs, negative inputs, impulse inputs, digital outputs, analog inputs, CAN interfaces, and a 1-Wire interface. The tracker also supports various features such as CAN data reading, accelerometer sensors, sleep modes, configuration and firmware updates, SMS and GPRS commands, time synchronization, and ignition detection.

With its advanced features and robust design, the Teltonika FMB150 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that can meet the needs of various tracking and monitoring applications. Whether you need to track a fleet of vehicles or monitor the performance of electric vehicles, the FMB150 offers the functionality and flexibility to get the job done.
