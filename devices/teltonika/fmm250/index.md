# Teltonika - FMM250

![FMM250](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMM250 is a rugged, IP67-rated vehicle GPS tracker engineered for high-fidelity CAN data acquisition and reliable cellular connectivity. Designed for fleet management and demanding mobile environments, the FMM250 is Plaspy compatible — delivering rich telemetry and vehicle bus parameters to Plaspy for real-time tracking, predictive maintenance, and advanced diagnostics.

The FMM250 combines LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT \(NB2\) communications with fallback to 2G where applicable, a built-in CAN data processing chip capable of reading and reporting over 100 CAN bus parameters, and durable packaging for on-vehicle mounting in dusty or wet conditions. It is an ideal choice for organizations that want Plaspy-compatible GPS trackers that bring detailed vehicle telemetry into fleet dashboards, anti-theft workflows, and maintenance systems.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: outputs CAN and cellular telemetry for ingestion into Plaspy for real-time tracking and reporting.
- Advanced CAN data capture: reads and reports 100+ CAN bus parameters from light vehicles, EVs, trucks, buses and special machinery.
- Low-power wide-area cellular: LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT \(NB2\) connectivity with fallback to 2G \(variant-dependent\) for broad coverage and efficiency.
- Rugged IP67 form factor: water- and dust-resistant design for reliable operation in harsh, on-vehicle environments.
- Fleet-ready telemetry: supports predictive maintenance, driver behaviour analytics, and remote diagnostics via detailed CAN data sets.
- Accessory ecosystem: compatible with Teltonika CAN adapters, RFID readers and Bluetooth sensors to extend functionality.
- Multiple SKU options: standard and extended CAN-parameter packages and regional RF configurations \(select SKU per market\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

The FMM250 sends CAN-derived telemetry and cellular location updates over LTE Cat M1, NB‑IoT or 2G to backend endpoints. When integrated as a Plaspy compatible tracker, those telemetry streams — including vehicle location, CAN parameters and diagnostic values — are mapped into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy leverages the FMM250’s detailed data to deliver actionable insights for fleet management, reducing downtime and improving operational efficiency.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered over LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT with 2G fallback \(variant-dependent\).
- CAN bus parameter streaming: more than 100 available parameters for engine, battery, EV states, sensors and more.
- Ignition, door and alarm status: supported when exposed on the vehicle CAN or accessory inputs and mapped into Plaspy.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry: available when the vehicle exposes fuel data on the CAN bus; Plaspy ingests those parameters for fuel analytics.
- Anti-theft and immobilizer workflows: can be implemented using supported accessories or vehicle outputs and coordinated through Plaspy \(subject to vehicle wiring and SKU features\).
- Bluetooth sensors/beacons: extend telemetry with BLE sensors via Teltonika accessories for temperature, cargo monitoring or proximity events.
