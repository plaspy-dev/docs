# Teltonika - FMM250

![FMM250](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMM250 is a rugged, IP67 rated vehicle tracker designed for demanding mobile environments and fleet applications. It is built to capture high fidelity CAN bus data and provide reliable cellular connectivity, making it suitable for deployments that require detailed vehicle telemetry alongside position tracking. The device is intended for on-vehicle use where dust and moisture resistance are important.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the FMM250 delivers rich telemetry and vehicle bus parameters into Plaspy for real time tracking, predictive maintenance workflows, and diagnostics. Its ability to surface extensive CAN-sourced parameters and cellular location updates makes it a practical option for organizations that want to integrate deep vehicle data into Plaspy dashboards, reporting, and operational alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device that streams CAN and cellular telemetry for ingestion into Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Advanced CAN data capture with the ability to read and report well over 100 CAN bus parameters.
- Wide area cellular connectivity including LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT with fallback to 2G where applicable to support broad coverage.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure designed for reliable operation in dusty and wet vehicle environments.
- Fleet oriented telemetry useful for predictive maintenance, remote diagnostics, and driver behaviour insights.
- Supports an accessory ecosystem including CAN adapters, RFID readers and Bluetooth sensors to extend capability.
- Multiple SKU and regional RF options to match specific market and fleet requirements.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated as a Plaspy compatible tracker, the FMM250 sends CAN derived telemetry and cellular location updates to Plaspy where those streams are mapped for live monitoring, alerts and historical analysis. Plaspy uses the device data to provide visibility into vehicle condition, location, and performance so teams can act on maintenance needs and operational exceptions.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered over LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT with 2G fallback where supported by the variant.
- CAN bus parameter streaming into Plaspy for engine, battery, EV states and other vehicle subsystems.
- Status signals such as ignition, door and alarm states when exposed on the vehicle bus or accessory inputs and mapped into Plaspy.
- Fuel related parameters captured from CAN and ingested into Plaspy for consumption tracking and fuel analytics when available.
- Anti theft and immobilizer workflows that can be coordinated through Plaspy using supported accessories or vehicle outputs.
- Extended telemetry with Bluetooth sensors and beacons via compatible accessories for temperature, cargo monitoring or proximity events.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization workflows coordinating vehicle signals and accessory controls through Plaspy.
- Predictive maintenance and remote diagnostics using 100 plus CAN parameters to monitor engine health, EV systems and battery state.
- Driver behaviour monitoring and coaching by analysing CAN derived speed, braking and throttle related events.
- Fuel management programs that ingest CAN fuel parameters for tracking and cost control where the vehicle exposes that data.
- Harsh environment asset tracking for construction, agriculture, mining and special machinery where IP67 protection is required.
