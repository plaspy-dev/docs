# Teltonika - MSP500

![MSP500](./Logo.png)

The Teltonika MSP500 is a specialized tracking terminal designed to provide reliable vehicle tracking and control. It combines GNSS positioning, cellular connectivity and Bluetooth with internal antennas and an RS232 interface to support a range of monitoring needs. The unit includes an internal Ni-Mh backup battery to keep tracking active during power loss, and it exposes a set of event and sensor features such as an accelerometer, jamming detection, towing and crash detection, geofence support, trip tracking, excessive idling detection and a GNSS fuel counter.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the MSP500 can feed its location and event data into the Plaspy fleet platform to support real time monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can surface the MSP500's alarms and status events so fleet managers can use speed limiting controls, geofence alerts, trip histories and other device reported events within a unified operational view. Compatibility makes the MSP500 a practical option for organizations that want hardware with advanced vehicle control features paired with Plaspy for oversight and analysis.

## Key Highlights

- GNSS, GSM and Bluetooth connectivity with internal antennas for reliable positioning and communication
- Internal Ni-Mh backup battery to maintain tracking when external power is interrupted
- RS232 interface to support peripheral integrations and multiple serial modes
- Integrated speed limiting control with relay and buzzer for enforcing speed policies
- Onboard accelerometer enabling crash detection, towing detection and driving behaviour events
- Support for remote firmware updates and configuration via FOTA and Teltonika Configurator

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location, event and status messages from the MSP500 and translates those inputs into map views, alerts and reports that fleet operators use to manage vehicles and drivers. The device events and telemetry map naturally to Plaspy features for visibility and operational oversight.

- Real time location and route visibility on Plaspy maps for active tracking
- Alerts and notifications for events like overspeed, crash, towing and unplug detection
- Geofence events for entry and exit monitoring using auto and manual geofence settings
- Historical trip and idle time reports for performance and cost analysis
- Visibility of relay and speed limiting events to support policy enforcement
- Aggregated device status reporting including firmware and configuration changes as reported by the unit

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet speed enforcement and driver behaviour monitoring for commercial vehicle operations
- School and passenger transport monitoring where safety events and geofences are important
- Rental and leasing fleets that require trip tracking, unplug detection and tamper awareness
- Logistics and distribution with focus on idle time, route compliance and fuel monitoring
- Heavy equipment or vocational vehicles where towing, limp mode and crash alerts are relevant
