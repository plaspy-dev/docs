# Teltonika - FM 4200

![FM 4200](./logo.jpg)

The Teltonika FM 4200 is a versatile GPS tracker designed to acquire remote object location and transmit it over a cellular connection. It combines a high sensitivity GPS receiver with GSM GPRS and SMS capabilities, and offers multiple inputs and outputs for monitoring and control. Built‑in interfaces such as 1-Wire, CAN, and RS232 expand its applicability for temperature sensors, iButton readers, vehicle data acquisition, and peripheral communication.

As a compatible device with Plaspy, the FM 4200 can feed position and event data into fleet management workflows for real time visibility and historical analysis. Its configurable data sending and storage features make it a practical choice for operators who need reliable tracking, configurable event reporting, and remote monitoring inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- GPS positioning powered by a uBlox NEO-5M 50 channel receiver for accurate coordinate acquisition.
- GSM GPRS and SMS connectivity for data transmission and basic messaging functionality.
- Multiple physical interfaces including 1-Wire, CAN, and RS232 for sensor and vehicle data integration.
- Configurable digital and analog inputs plus open collector outputs for remote monitoring and control.
- Onboard accelerometer and internal backup battery for motion detection and brief power autonomy.
- Local storage capable of holding several thousand records to ensure data retention between uploads.
- Flexible firmware and configuration update options delivered over the network or via wired connection.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FM 4200 supplies location, event, and input state data that Plaspy uses to present maps, alerts, and reports for fleet operations. The tracker’s configurable reporting and geofence features make it straightforward to align device behavior with the monitoring rules and workflows configured in Plaspy.

- Real time location and status visibility on Plaspy maps for operational oversight.
- Event and geofence reporting to trigger alerts and workflow actions within Plaspy.
- Historical trip and position data retention for reporting and route analysis.
- Monitoring of digital and analog inputs to surface sensor states and external device activity.
- Aggregation of device events to support fleet level reporting and performance metrics.
- Remote configuration and firmware options help keep devices aligned with Plaspy data requirements.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location and route monitoring for logistics and delivery operations.
- Truck data acquisition and fleet oversight where CAN data from vehicle systems is useful.
- Remote asset monitoring including temperature or access logging via 1-Wire sensors and iButton.
- Driver behavior and movement monitoring using accelerometer based events.
- Equipment control and status monitoring through digital inputs and outputs.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FM 4200 is a practical option for organizations that need a balance of positioning accuracy, multiple interface options, and configurable reporting. Its combination of GPS performance, diverse input and output capabilities, and onboard storage makes it suitable for mixed fleets and asset monitoring projects that feed into a centralized platform like Plaspy.
