# Teltonika - FMU125

![FMU125](./Logo.png)

The Teltonika FMU125 is a small and professional real-time tracking terminal that offers reliable and accurate tracking capabilities. With its GNSS and 3G/GSM connectivity, this device ensures seamless communication and location acquisition of remote objects. It is equipped with internal GNSS and 3G antennas, as well as an accelerometer for enhanced sensor capabilities.

This GPS tracker is perfect for a wide range of applications, including fleet management, car rental companies, taxi companies, public transport, logistics companies, and personal cars. It offers various features and functionalities to meet the specific needs of different industries.

One of the standout features of the FMU125 is its ability to connect to various third-party external devices through its RS485/RS232 data interface. This allows for the integration of additional sensors or accessories to enhance the tracking capabilities and functionality of the device.

The FMU125 also offers a range of advanced features, including green driving monitoring, over speeding detection, GNSS fuel counter, excessive idling detection, immobilizer, iButton read notification, unplug detection, towing detection, crash detection, auto geofence, and manual geofence. These features provide comprehensive tracking and monitoring capabilities to ensure the safety and efficiency of your assets.

In terms of configuration and firmware updates, the FMU125 supports FOTA web, FOTA, Teltonika Configurator \(USB, Bluetooth\), and the FMBT mobile application for easy and convenient setup and management.

With its compact size, reliable performance, and extensive features, the Teltonika FMU125 is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a high-quality GPS tracker for their tracking and monitoring needs.
