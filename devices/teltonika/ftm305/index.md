# Teltonika - FTM305

<div className="device-logo">
![FTM305](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FTM305 is a compact 4G LTE Cat M1 e mobility GPS tracker built for demanding vehicle and equipment monitoring. Designed for electrified transport and industrial environments, the FTM305 combines a rugged IP67 enclosure with vehicle grade interfaces to deliver reliable positioning and telemetry for e bikes, forklifts, shuttle cars, utility machinery and other electric equipment.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FTM305 can stream GNSS coordinates and vehicle telemetry into Plaspy powered fleet dashboards for real time visibility and historical reporting. Its support for multi constellation GNSS and CAN bus data acquisition makes it a suitable option when teams want location plus actionable vehicle data in a single integrated platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device for real time GNSS and telemetry ingestion.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure for outdoor and industrial environments.
- Wide input voltage support suitable for low voltage micro mobility and higher voltage industrial platforms.
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity with NB2 fallback and 2G support where applicable for resilient communications.
- Enhanced GNSS receiver with multi constellation tracking for improved positioning.
- CAN bus data reading to surface vehicle telemetry and battery related signals where available.
- Compact, lightweight form factor for flexible mounting on constrained assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FTM305 delivers location updates and CAN derived telemetry that Plaspy can visualize, alert on, and include in reports. Plaspy maps incoming GNSS and vehicle signals into dashboards and operational workflows so teams can monitor fleets and equipment status from a central interface.

- Live map tracking and route analysis using GNSS updates provided by the tracker.
- CAN bus telemetry mapped into Plaspy dashboards for monitoring battery state, voltage trends and custom signals.
- Vehicle status events such as ignition or alarm signals available to trigger rules and notifications if exposed by the device.
- Automated workflows and alerts based on telemetry thresholds and geofence events.
- Regional cellular variants help maintain consistent connectivity for telemetry and positioning across different geographies.

## Typical Use Cases

- Urban micro mobility fleet tracking for e bikes and scooters with real time location and service scheduling.
- Forklift and warehouse vehicle monitoring to track usage, movements and operational hours.
- Battery management and BMS monitoring by reading CAN bus parameters for state of charge and health trends.
- Battery swapping logistics and asset location management to improve turnaround and availability.
- Outdoor equipment and utility machinery tracking in dusty or wet environments thanks to IP67 protection.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FTM305 offers a balanced combination of ruggedness, connectivity efficiency and vehicle level telemetry that fits many e mobility and industrial tracking scenarios. For organizations using Plaspy, this tracker can reduce the need for separate sensors by exposing CAN bus signals alongside GNSS data, allowing Plaspy to deliver richer fleet insights, geofencing, rules and historical reports from a single data source.

Plaspy integration helps convert the FTM305 data stream into operational intelligence for maintenance planning, asset security and day to day fleet oversight.
