# Teltonika - FMC880 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Teltonika FMC880 and explains how its capabilities relate to use with the Plaspy fleet management platform. It focuses on user-facing functions and practical value when the FMC880 is used for vehicle and asset tracking with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, optional configuration, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. Review the device configuration and Teltonika documentation for model specific details before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The FMC880 is a rugged, battery-mounted GPS tracker designed for demanding fleet and asset tracking tasks. It delivers cellular connectivity, improved GNSS positioning, and Bluetooth Low Energy support for external sensors to provide continuous location and environmental telemetry that Plaspy can consume.

- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with fallback to 2G for broader regional coverage and resilient telemetry.
- Dual-band GNSS receiver using L1 and L5 signals for improved positioning accuracy and faster fixes compared with single-band modules.
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) support to pair external beacons and sensors for environmental and proximity monitoring.
- IP65 rated enclosure and a U-type cable battery mounting system for fast, weather-resistant installation on vehicles and assets.
- Regional variants and optional accessory kits, including gyro and extended cable options, to match deployment and monitoring requirements.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMC880

- Plaspy compatibility out of the box for straightforward integration into fleet and asset monitoring workflows.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 data connection with automatic 2G fallback for operation across mixed network environments.
- Dual-band GNSS (L1 and L5) for higher positioning precision and improved time to first fix.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for connecting external temperature, humidity, magnet, and movement sensors or beacons.
- Rugged, IP65-rated housing for protection against dust and water spray in typical vehicle installations.
- Battery-mounted form factor with a U-type cable connector for quick fitment and redeployment.
- Multiple regional variants and optional configurations such as a gyro-equipped variant and extended cable kits.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an FMC880 reports into Plaspy, the device provides location fixes and connected sensor telemetry that Plaspy can display, log, and use for rules and alerts. Plaspy detects supported tracker protocols automatically and surfaces location and sensor events for operational use.

- Real-time and periodic location updates appear in Plaspy for live tracking and history playback.
- Dual-band GNSS position data is used by Plaspy to improve map positioning, stop detection, and route analysis.
- BLE sensor readings such as temperature, humidity, magnet detection, and movement are forwarded to Plaspy for monitoring and alerting.
- Optional gyro-based motion events can be used in Plaspy workflows for tamper or anti-theft notifications where the gyro variant is installed.
- Regional device variants ensure telemetry is reliably transmitted from areas with appropriate cellular band coverage and Plaspy can ingest that data consistently.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet telematics for continuous vehicle tracking, route replay, and operational oversight.
- Logistics and last mile delivery with BLE enabled cargo condition monitoring and precise positioning.

- Remote asset monitoring for equipment that benefits from rugged mounting and long-term deployment.

- Environmental monitoring of transported goods when paired with BLE temperature or humidity sensors.
