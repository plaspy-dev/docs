# Teltonika - FMC880

![FMC880](./tracker.png)

The FMC880 is a rugged, battery-mounted GPS tracker built for demanding fleet management and asset-tracking applications and is Plaspy compatible out of the box. With 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity and 2G fallback, dual-band GNSS \(L1/L5\) for higher positioning precision, and Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) support for external sensors and beacons, the FMC880 delivers robust real-time tracking and telemetry suited to logistics, car sharing, rental fleets, and remote asset monitoring.

The device’s IP65-rated casing and quick U‑type cable battery mounting make installation fast and weather-resistant, reducing downtime for vehicle fitment or redeployment. Multiple regional variants and optional configurations \(including gyro and extended cable kits\) allow integrators and fleet operators to choose the FMC880 configuration that best matches coverage, sensor needs, and environmental monitoring requirements while leveraging Plaspy’s platform for alerts, reports, and fleet-wide visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for fleet management and real-time tracking across mixed network environments.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with automatic fallback to 2G for broad regional coverage and reliable telemetry delivery.
- Standalone dual-band GNSS receiver \(L1 + L5\) for improved accuracy and faster time-to-first-fix compared with single-band modules.
- IP65-rated, water-resistant enclosure and quick battery-mounted installation via U-type cable connector—install in five minutes or less.
- Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) for connection to external beacons and sensors \(temperature, humidity, magnet, movement\) to extend asset and environmental monitoring.
- Regional variants and order codes for EU/MEA/APAC and LATAM with appropriate 2G/4G bands; customizable packaging and accessory options.
- Optional gyro variant available for enhanced motion and tilt/impact detection to support anti-theft and tamper alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the FMC880 delivers continuous position and sensor telemetry to the platform over its cellular connection. GNSS L1/L5 fixes and onboard motion data are transmitted to Plaspy for live location visualization, history playback, geofence alarms, and telemetry-based rules. BLE sensor readings \(temperature, humidity, magnet, movement\) are forwarded to Plaspy so you can monitor cargo conditions and trigger alerts when thresholds are exceeded.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates over 4G \(Cat 1\) with 2G fallback for resilient connectivity.
- Dual-band GNSS \(L1/L5\) data used by Plaspy for higher-accuracy routing, stop detection, and route replay.
- Bluetooth sensors/beacons: temperature, humidity, magnet detection, and movement data forwarded to Plaspy for environmental monitoring and proximity events.
- Optional gyro-based motion events for Plaspy anti-theft workflows and tamper detection \(available in gyro variants\).
- Regional variants ensure the FMC880 reports reliably in EU/MEA/APAC and LATAM markets with the correct cellular bands.
