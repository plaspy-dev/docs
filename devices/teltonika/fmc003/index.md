# Teltonika - FMC003

![FMC003](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMC003 is a plug and play OBD II GPS tracker designed for fleet management, logistics and e mobility programs. Its OBD II form factor and OEM parameter reading deliver vehicle sourced telemetry such as odometer, fuel level and EV battery metrics, while cellular connectivity supports continuous position and status updates for mixed vehicle fleets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMC003 supplies high fidelity telemetry into the Plaspy platform so operators can combine accurate vehicle data with location and event feeds. This makes the FMC003 well suited for rapid deployments where fast installation, consistent reporting and integrated EV monitoring are important to fleet and operations teams.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II installation for fast deployment across passenger and light commercial vehicles
- LTE Cat 1 4G connectivity with 2G fallback for broad cellular coverage and continuous telemetry
- OEM level telemetry reads true odometer and fuel level from the vehicle ECU for reliable reporting
- Explicit support for electric vehicles with battery and consumption metrics for e mobility fleets
- Compatible with Teltonika accessories such as extension cables and OBD II to FMS adapter for flexible mounting
- Remote device management supported via Teltonika tools to simplify updates and large scale configuration
- Regional SKUs and frequency variants available to match local network requirements

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to a vehicle, the FMC003 provides continuous location and OBD sourced telemetry that Plaspy ingests to populate dashboards, reports and alerts. Plaspy surfaces the device data alongside mapping and event streams so fleet managers gain consolidated visibility for tracking, maintenance and operational decision making.

- Real time tracking and telemetry displayed in Plaspy dashboards for live fleet monitoring
- Odometer and fuel data integrated into Plaspy reports to support maintenance planning and fuel analysis
- EV battery and consumption metrics available in Plaspy for range monitoring and charging insights
- Remote diagnostics and configuration updates coordinated with Teltonika management tools and reflected in Plaspy workflows
- Alerts and event handling for overspeed, geofence, and other operational triggers to support rapid response

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route oversight for mixed vehicle fleets that include ICE and electric vehicles
- Logistics and delivery operations needing fast rollouts and consistent vehicle telemetry
- EV fleet operations tracking battery consumption, range and charging events
- Fuel monitoring programs that rely on OEM fuel level and consumption data for cost control
- Preventive maintenance scheduling based on accurate odometer and diagnostic inputs
