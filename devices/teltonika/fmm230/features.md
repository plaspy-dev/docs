# Teltonika - FMM230 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Teltonika FMM230 and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for tracking, telemetry, and condition monitoring. It is intended to help fleet managers, integrators, and technical evaluators understand what the device can deliver in Plaspy-based deployments without exposing sensitive configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. For device-specific limits, supported accessory lists, and the latest firmware behavior consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from Teltonika.

## Feature Overview

The FMM230 is a compact, rugged tracker built for reliable field use and sensor extensibility. It combines wide-area cellular connectivity with BLE sensor support and vehicle I/O to provide location, condition data, and operational events that feed into Plaspy for monitoring and reporting.

- LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT (M1/NB2) cellular connectivity with 2G fallback for extended coverage in diverse deployments
- IP67-rated enclosure for dust and water resistance in outdoor and industrial installations
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for pairing with Teltonika EYE beacons and sensors to capture temperature, humidity, movement and magnet events
- Built-in backup battery to maintain reporting during vehicle power interruptions
- Flexible input/output options and compatibility with CAN adapters for integration with vehicle sensors and external systems

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMM230

- Multi-network cellular modem delivering LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT with 2G fallback for robust uplinks
- IP67 rugged enclosure and compact form factor for harsh environment installations
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for Teltonika EYE beacons and sensor nodes (temperature, humidity, movement, magnet detection)
- Backup battery to preserve operation and location reporting during power loss
- Flexible digital inputs and outputs for event reporting and integration with vehicle systems; compatible with Teltonika CAN adapter options
- Remote device management support including Teltonika FOTA WEB and standard configuration tools
- Tool-free, click-type mounting and multiple cable options to simplify installation

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMM230 streams location and accessory data into Plaspy dashboards and reporting tools so teams can monitor assets, respond to events, and analyze historical telemetry. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and surfaces device-reported events for operational oversight.

- Real-time location updates and telemetry visible in Plaspy for live tracking and route review
- BLE sensor readings from Teltonika EYE devices made available in Plaspy dashboards and condition reports
- Vehicle input/output and CAN-derived statuses can be reflected as events or attributes inside Plaspy where configured
- Power-loss and backup battery events reported to Plaspy to support anti-theft monitoring and maintenance workflows
- Remote update and configuration workflows supported alongside Plaspy device lifecycle practices to reduce on-site maintenance

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and continuous vehicle location tracking for route optimization and oversight
- Refrigerated transport and cargo condition monitoring using BLE temperature and humidity sensors
- Logistics and last‑mile delivery fleets requiring compact, quick-install devices with broad coverage
- Construction and equipment tracking in rough outdoor environments using IP67 protection and backup power
- Utilities and off-road vehicle telemetry where reliable cellular connectivity and flexible I/O are needed

Using the Teltonika FMM230 with Plaspy gives organizations a practical way to combine rugged field hardware with a centralized monitoring and reporting platform.
