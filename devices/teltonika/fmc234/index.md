# Teltonika FMC234

The Teltonika FMC234 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for demanding fleet management and asset protection in harsh environments. Built around 4G LTE Cat 1 communications with 2G fallback and housed in an IP67-rated enclosure, FMC234 delivers reliable real-time tracking, long standby autonomy, and easy integration with telematics platforms like Plaspy for fleet management, anti-theft and telemetry use cases.

Engineered for vehicles and high-value assets, the FMC234 pairs rugged hardware—an innovative click-type two-phase closing mechanism and water-resistant casing—with a high-capacity 1,000 mAh Li‑Po backup battery that can sustain autonomous operation for up to 8 days if external power is lost. That combination makes the FMC234 a practical choice for stolen vehicle recovery, remote equipment tracking, and temperature-sensitive logistics when used alongside compatible sensors.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Rugged IP67-rated housing with a tool-free click-type two-phase closing mechanism for harsh and wet environments.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with region-specific radio configurations and 2G fallback to maximize coverage.
- High-capacity 1,000 mAh internal Li‑Po backup battery — up to 8 days autonomous operation for anti-theft and recovery scenarios.
- Supports vehicle telemetry via CAN adapters and expands functionality with Teltonika accessories \(EYE Sensor, EYE Beacon, RFID, iButton\).
- Remote management supported through FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator for scalable fleet deployments.
- Available in regional variants and packaging options \(single or bulk\) for flexible procurement.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMC234 streams location and telemetry to the platform for live monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy receives standard telematics data from the tracker and translates it into actionable insights for fleet operators — improving response to theft events, optimizing routing and enabling condition-based maintenance when vehicle bus data or sensors are attached.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy for precise tracking and route playback.
- Vehicle bus/CAN telemetry available when the FMC234 is used with compatible CAN adapters \(ALL-CAN300, ECAN02, LV-CAN200\) — enables ignition and engine data streaming.
- Environmental and temperature data when paired with Teltonika EYE Sensor and EYE Beacon accessories — useful for cold chain and temperature-sensitive logistics.
- Alerts and rules in Plaspy \(geofence, movement, power loss\) triggered by FMC234 telemetry to support anti-theft and compliance workflows.
- Remote configuration and firmware updates via FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator for centralized device management within Plaspy deployments.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and anti-theft protection — real-time tracking and long backup autonomy help recover stolen vehicles.
- Cold chain and pharmaceutical logistics — pair with EYE Sensor for EN12830-compliant temperature monitoring and Plaspy alerts.
- Construction, mining and agricultural equipment tracking — rugged IP67 housing stands up to dust, water and vibration.

- Remote asset protection and long-term deployments — bulk packaging and remote management make large rollouts practical and cost-effective.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FMC234 offers a reliable, Plaspy compatible solution where durability, long battery autonomy and flexible connectivity matter. Its water-resistant IP67 design and click-style sealing reduce installation time and protect electronics in harsh environments, while regional LTE Cat 1 configurations with 2G fallback ensure broad coverage. Combining the FMC234 with Plaspy gives fleet operators and asset managers real-time tracking, telemetry and automated alerts, plus the option to add CAN-based fuel and ignition data or Bluetooth sensors for environmental monitoring. Remote management through FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator keeps devices updated and easy to manage at scale, making the FMC234 a practical choice for fleet management, anti-theft recovery and telemetry-driven operations.
