# Teltonika - FMC234

![FMC234](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMC234 is a rugged GPS tracker designed for demanding fleet management and asset protection in harsh environments. Built with cellular connectivity and an IP67 rated enclosure, the FMC234 delivers continuous location reporting, extended standby autonomy, and a durable form factor suited to vehicles and high value equipment. Its design emphasizes reliable tracking, simple enclosure sealing, and the ability to remain operational when external power is lost.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMC234 can stream location and telemetry into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerting, and historical reporting. This compatibility makes the FMC234 a practical option for operators who want a robust hardware platform paired with Plaspy visibility and management capabilities for anti theft recovery, remote asset tracking, and telemetry driven operations.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for real time tracking and fleet management integration
- Rugged IP67 rated housing with a click type two phase closing mechanism for wet and dusty conditions
- Cellular connectivity with regional configurations and fallback to maximize coverage
- Built in high capacity backup battery offering multiday autonomous operation if external power is lost
- Supports vehicle telemetry via compatible adapters and expands capability with Teltonika accessories
- Remote device management options for scalable fleet deployments
- Available in regional variants and flexible packaging to suit procurement needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMC234 provides continuous position and telemetry that Plaspy translates into maps, alerts, and reports for fleet operators. Plaspy uses the device data to power operational oversight, incident response, and historical analysis without requiring users to manage low level device details.

- Real time location updates and route playback in Plaspy for live monitoring and post trip review
- Telemetry from vehicle bus or compatible adapters available to enrich Plaspy dashboards with ignition and engine related events
- Environmental and proximity data from compatible Teltonika accessories can feed Plaspy alerts for temperature or security conditions
- Configurable alerts and rules in Plaspy such as geofence, movement, and power loss to support anti theft and compliance workflows
- Remote configuration and firmware management as part of centralized device lifecycle oversight when used alongside Plaspy deployments
