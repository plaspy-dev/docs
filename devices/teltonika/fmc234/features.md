# Teltonika - FMC234 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Teltonika FMC234 tracker with Plaspy. It describes the practical capabilities and operational value you can expect when integrating FMC234 devices into Plaspy for fleet management, asset protection, and telemetry. The content focuses on non-sensitive, user-facing features and how those features are reflected in the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and the specifics of each installation. Plaspy documentation and platform interfaces will expose the data the tracker reports, but device-level options such as supported accessories, battery endurance, radio bands, and telemetry detail depend on manufacturer configuration and installed hardware.

## Feature Overview

The FMC234 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for vehicles and high value assets that operate in demanding environments. It combines cellular connectivity with a durable IP67 enclosure and an internal backup battery to provide continuous tracking and telemetry in situations where power or coverage may be intermittent.

- Real time GPS location streaming for live monitoring and historical route playback in Plaspy.
- Rugged IP67 rated housing with a click type two phase closing mechanism for tool free installation in wet and dusty conditions.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback to broaden coverage across regions.
- Internal 1,000 mAh Li Po backup battery offering extended autonomous operation for anti theft and recovery scenarios.
- Support for vehicle bus telemetry via compatible CAN adapters and expansion with Teltonika accessories such as EYE Sensor and EYE Beacon.
- Remote device setup and firmware updates using Teltonika management tools for scalable deployments.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMC234

- Plaspy compatible device designed to report standard telematics and location information to fleet platforms.
- IP67 rated enclosure with a click type two phase closing mechanism for enhanced environmental protection.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 communications with region specific radio configurations and 2G fallback where applicable.
- Internal 1,000 mAh Li Po backup battery that can provide multiple days of autonomy depending on reporting interval and configuration.
- GNSS based positioning for vehicle and asset tracking.
- Support for vehicle bus integration through compatible CAN adapters to surface ignition and engine related telemetry.
- Compatibility with Teltonika accessories including EYE Sensor and EYE Beacon for environmental and proximity sensing, plus RFID and iButton options.
- Remote management via FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator for firmware updates and parameter management.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the telemetry and location streams that the FMC234 reports and turns those streams into actionable views, alerts, and historical records. The platform maps reported data to visualization, rule engines, and reporting tools that operators use to monitor fleets and assets.

- Real time location and route playback in Plaspy for operational visibility and post event review.
- CAN based telemetry reported by the FMC234 and compatible adapters can appear in Plaspy to indicate ignition state and other vehicle bus metrics when provided by the device.
- Environmental and proximity sensor data from supported Teltonika accessories can feed into Plaspy for condition monitoring and alerts.
- Alerts and automation in Plaspy such as geofence entry and exit, movement detection, and power loss can be triggered by FMC234 telemetry.
- Device status and firmware reporting available in Plaspy dashboards when the tracker reports health and management data.

- Fleet management with continuous vehicle location tracking and historical route analysis.
