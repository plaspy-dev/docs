# Teltonika - FMB930 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Teltonika FMB930 and how its capabilities map to Plaspy. It is intended to describe the practical, non sensitive functions the device offers and how those functions are commonly used when the tracker is connected to Plaspy for fleet visibility, anti theft monitoring, and BLE sensor telemetry.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional product variant, and how the manufacturer configures specific builds. Use the information here as a high level guide and refer to Teltonika documentation for the most current device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The FMB930 is a compact, low power GPS tracker designed for electric two wheelers and other light electric vehicles. Its combination of GNSS positioning, 2G cellular, internal rechargeable battery, and Bluetooth Low Energy support makes it practical for fleets where vehicles spend long periods parked and power budget is limited.

- Compact form factor built for e mopeds and light electric vehicles, suitable for discreet installations.
- Ultra low power Power Off sleep behavior for long autonomous standby and reduced drain on vehicle batteries.
- Internal rechargeable 170 mAh battery to provide off grid operation and anti theft monitoring when the vehicle is unpowered.
- 2G GSM connectivity on bands B2 B3 B5 B8 for broad GSM coverage in supported regions.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for pairing external sensors and beacons and forwarding sensor telemetry.
- Remote device management compatibility such as FOTA WEB for scaled firmware and configuration updates.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMB930

- GNSS based location reporting for real time tracking and historical route playback.
- Ultra low power sleep mode designed to minimize current draw during long idle periods.
- Internal rechargeable battery for off grid monitoring and anti theft use cases.
- 2G GSM connectivity on supported cellular bands for telemetry uplink.
- Bluetooth Low Energy interface to receive data from compatible external sensors and beacons.
- Remote management support enabling firmware and configuration updates using supported tools.
- Compact size and simplified wiring with a standard power supply cable included for typical installations.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the FMB930 reports position and BLE sensor telemetry into the Plaspy platform where managers can monitor devices on live maps, review event history, and receive alerts. Plaspy ingests the tracker telemetry and makes device status and sensor readings available alongside other fleet data for operational oversight.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and route review.
- BLE sensor readings forwarded by the tracker are available in Plaspy as telemetry points for monitoring temperature humidity magnet or motion sensors.
- Battery and power state indicators from the device help Plaspy show sleep state and low battery conditions for remote monitoring.
- Remote firmware and configuration workflows using FOTA WEB compatibility can be coordinated alongside Plaspy device management procedures.
- Device visibility in Plaspy can be correlated with other data sources or integrations to create a fuller fleet picture without assuming additional inputs are provided by the FMB930 itself.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft monitoring for e mopeds and e motorcycles with off grid alerts and movement detection.
