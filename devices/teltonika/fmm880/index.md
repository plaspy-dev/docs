# FMM880

The FMM880 is a Plaspy compatible, water-resistant GNSS tracker designed for fast, battery-mounted deployments where external power is not available. Built for fleet and asset tracking, the FMM880 combines low-power LTE Cat M1 \(with NB‑IoT options\) connectivity, a dual-band L1 + L5 GNSS receiver, and Bluetooth LE sensor support to deliver reliable real-time tracking and extended field life for seasonal, temporary or hard-to-wire installations.

Engineered for quick installation and rugged use, the FMM880 mounts directly to a vehicle battery with a U-type cable and special clamp connector so non-specialist personnel can fit units in under five minutes. Its IP65-rated enclosure and LTE Cat M1/NB2 cellular support make it ideal for fleet management, telemetry capture and Plaspy-driven anti-theft and asset monitoring workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT options for low-power, wide-area communications and long-range data delivery.
- Dual-band GNSS \(L1 + L5\) receiver for improved positioning accuracy and faster time-to-first-fix in challenging environments.
- Battery-mounted design with U-type cable and special clamp connector for installation under five minutes—no vehicle wiring required.
- IP65-rated, water-resistant enclosure built for outdoor fleet and asset tracking in harsh weather conditions.
- Bluetooth® LE support for external beacons and sensors to monitor temperature, humidity, magnet status and movement.
- 2G \(GSM\) fallback and global frequency band variants for broad regional compatibility and resilient connectivity.
- Packaged options and order codes available for standard and bulk deployments to simplify procurement and rollouts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMM880 streams GNSS positions and IoT telemetry to the Plaspy platform using efficient LPWA cellular links. Plaspy ingests location updates, Bluetooth sensor readings and device status to deliver real-time tracking, configurable alerts and historical reports for fleet management and asset control.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT \(with 2G fallback where applicable\) for responsive Plaspy dashboards and geofencing.
- GNSS position data \(dual-band L1 + L5\) forwarded to Plaspy for higher-quality tracks and faster fixes in urban and obstructed environments.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons: temperature, humidity, magnet/movement data available to Plaspy for environment and tamper monitoring.
- Event-driven reports and alerts in Plaspy for movement, sensor thresholds and connectivity status to support anti-theft and operational workflows.
- Designed to complement Plaspy workflows for telemetry, fleet management and temporary deployments; external ignition, immobilizer or fuel-sensor integrations can be supported when paired with compatible interfaces or third-party adapters.
