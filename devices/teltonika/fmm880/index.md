# Teltonika - FMM880

<div className="device-logo">
![FMM880](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMM880 is a compact, water resistant GNSS tracker designed for fast battery mounted deployments where external power is not available. Built for fleet and asset tracking, the FMM880 combines low power LPWA cellular connectivity with a dual band GNSS receiver and Bluetooth LE sensor support to deliver reliable position updates and extended field life for seasonal, temporary, or hard to wire installations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMM880 can stream location and sensor data into the Plaspy platform to support real time tracking, alerts and historical reporting. Its rapid clamp style installation and rugged enclosure make it a relevant option for operations that need quick rollouts, temporary installs, or battery powered tracking tied into Plaspy workflows for fleet oversight and asset monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for battery mounted deployments and rapid installation.
- Low power cellular connectivity with options for LPWA networks to extend field life.
- Dual band GNSS receiver for improved positioning quality and faster fixes in difficult environments.
- IP65 rated water resistant enclosure suitable for outdoor fleet and asset tracking.
- Bluetooth LE support for external beacons and sensors to extend telemetry options.
- Packaging and order variants to simplify procurement for bulk or seasonal rollouts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMM880 delivers GNSS positions, sensor readings and device status into the Plaspy platform where that data is used for visualization, alerts and reporting. Plaspy ingests the device streams to provide operational visibility and to support event driven workflows for fleets and monitored assets.

- Real time location updates forwarded to Plaspy for live tracking and historical playback.
- Sensor telemetry from Bluetooth beacons available in Plaspy for environment and tamper awareness.
- Configurable alerts and event reporting in Plaspy for movement, sensor thresholds and connectivity changes.
- Geofencing and zone based monitoring in Plaspy to trigger notifications and run operational rules.
- Aggregated reports and device status overviews to support fleet management and audit trails.

## Typical Use Cases

- Seasonal fleet tracking and short term vehicle deployments where permanent wiring is impractical.
- Anti theft and tamper detection for shared vehicles and portable assets.
- Tracking of trailers, rental equipment and portable machinery with quick clamp installation.
- Environmental monitoring in transit when paired with external temperature or humidity beacons.
- Rapid field rollouts for contractors and seasonal operations that need simple procurement and installation.
