# Teltonika - FMM880 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Teltonika FMM880 and explains how its capabilities are used when the device is connected to Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing functions, deployment considerations, and the practical value the tracker brings to fleet and asset monitoring workflows in Plaspy.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional order code and installation method. Where necessary we recommend checking the device firmware release notes and the manufacturer's documentation to confirm the precise behavior and supported options for your unit.

## Feature Overview

The FMM880 is a compact, battery-mounted GNSS tracker designed for rapid installation and long field life. It targets fleet and asset tracking scenarios where permanent wiring is impractical, combining low-power cellular connectivity, dual-band positioning, and Bluetooth LE sensor support to deliver timely location and environmental telemetry into Plaspy.

- Dual-band GNSS receiver (L1 and L5) for improved positioning accuracy and faster fixes in obstructed environments.
- LTE Cat M1 with NB‑IoT options and 2G fallback for low-power wide area communications and resilient connectivity.
- Battery-mounted form factor with U-type cable and clamp connector for installation in minutes without vehicle wiring.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for external beacons and sensors such as temperature, humidity, magnet and movement monitoring.
- IP65 rated enclosure to withstand outdoor and fleet use in variable weather conditions.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMM880

- Plaspy compatible tracker intended for use with fleet and asset management platforms.
- Dual-band GNSS receiver supporting L1 and L5 positioning for improved track quality.

- Battery-mounted design with a U-type cable and special clamp connector to enable rapid, non‑specialist installation.

- IP65 water resistant enclosure suitable for outdoor deployments and mobile assets.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the FMM880 is registered to Plaspy, the device sends GNSS positions and sensor telemetry into the platform so operators can monitor location, status and environmental data from a single interface. Plaspy displays current and historical feeds and can raise events based on movement, sensor thresholds and connectivity state.

- Location data from the dual-band GNSS is available in Plaspy as live positions and track history to support routing and recovery workflows.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT connectivity ensures periodic telemetry and status updates reach Plaspy while minimizing power draw for battery operation.
- Bluetooth LE sensor readings such as temperature, humidity and magnet/movement are forwarded into Plaspy for environmental monitoring and tamper detection.
- Event-driven alerts and reports in Plaspy can be configured around movement, sensor thresholds and device connectivity for operational notifications.
- Rapid installation and battery mounting make the device well suited to temporary assets and seasonal fleets managed through Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Seasonal or temporary fleet tracking where fast, non‑intrusive installation is required.
- Anti-theft and tamper monitoring using movement and magnet indications combined with Plaspy alerts.
- Tracking trailers, portable equipment and rented assets that lack permanent wiring.
- Environmental monitoring in transit by pairing BLE temperature and humidity sensors to the tracker and reporting into Plaspy.
- Rapid large scale rollouts for contractors and field operations using packaged order codes and simple installation.
- Remote asset telemetry in areas where low-power wide area networks are preferred for battery life reasons.
