# Teltonika - FMB225

![FMB225](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMB225 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for demanding outdoor and industrial operations. Housed in an IP67-rated enclosure, the FMB225 delivers reliable position tracking and advanced telemetry in agriculture, construction, mining and fleet environments where dust and temporary water immersion are common. Plaspy integration brings those location and sensor streams into a unified platform for real-time tracking, alerts and reporting.

Built with dual SIM support and quad-band 2G connectivity, the FMB225 reduces roaming costs and improves connection resilience across wide coverage areas. Its versatile interfaces — including RS232, RS485 and an impulse input for fuel flow meters — let operations capture fuel monitoring, external sensor data and other telemetry beyond basic GPS, making the unit a strong option for fleet management, anti-theft workflows and industrial asset monitoring when paired with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for harsh environments \(IP67-rated\) — ideal for agriculture, construction and mining.
- Dual SIM on quad-band 2G \(B2/B3/B5/B8\) to maximize connectivity and reduce roaming risk across coverage zones.
- Serial interfaces RS232 and RS485 for direct connection to fuel flow meters, PLCs and other field devices for advanced telemetry.
- Impulse input capability for precise fuel monitoring and pulse-based sensors, enabling fuel consumption and refill detection.
- Robust fleet management support when integrated with Plaspy for real-time tracking, alerts and operational reporting.
- Supported by Teltonika’s device configuration and remote management tools \(FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator\) for streamlined maintenance.
- Available global variants and packaging options to suit standard fleet rollouts or single-unit custom orders.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMB225 delivers continuous GPS positions and telemetry to the platform so dispatchers and managers can monitor assets in real time. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s location, serial telemetry and impulse input events to generate live maps, historical routes and custom alerts. Data from the FMB225’s interfaces can be fed into Plaspy dashboards and reports to support fleet management, telemetry analysis and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates routed into Plaspy for live tracking and geofenced alerts.
- Fuel monitoring via impulse input and external flow meters, with event reporting for refills and consumption trends.
- Serial telemetry over RS232/RS485 for external sensors, PLC data and specialized field instrumentation.
- Dual SIM connectivity provides fallback and continuity of data streams into Plaspy, reducing blind spots.
- Remote device configuration and firmware updates through Teltonika FOTA WEB, simplifying fleet-scale management integrated with Plaspy operations.
