# Teltonika - FMC250

![FMC250](./tracker.png)

The FMC250 is a robust, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for demanding vehicle telematics. With an IP67 water‑resistant enclosure and LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity \(with 2G fallback\), the FMC250 delivers reliable real‑time tracking and high‑fidelity telemetry for fleet management, diagnostics and preventative maintenance across cars, trucks, buses and special machinery.

The unit integrates a dedicated CAN bus data processor capable of reading over 100 CAN parameters, giving Plaspy direct access to engine, drivetrain and vehicle sensor data. Designed for on‑vehicle installation and extended data collection, the FMC250 supports advanced fuel monitoring, scheduled maintenance workflows and integration with Bluetooth sensors and identification accessories to extend vehicle and cargo visibility on Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for vehicle telematics and real‑time tracking in commercial fleets.
- Integrated CAN data processor able to read 100+ vehicle parameters for deeper telemetry and diagnostics.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G \(GSM\) fallback for improved coverage and stable data transmission.
- IP67 water‑resistant casing for reliable operation in harsh environments and outdoor vehicle installations.
- Available in Standard and Extended CAN parameter packages to match installation and reporting needs.
- Compatible with Bluetooth accessories \(EYE Beacon, EYE Sensor\) and iButton/1‑Wire RFID for driver/asset ID.
- Bundled options and multiple SKUs simplify procurement and deployment for fleets of different sizes.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMC250 streams GNSS location and CAN‑derived telemetry to provide continuous situational awareness and actionable reports. Plaspy ingests raw CAN parameters and normalizes them into dashboards, alerts and maintenance triggers — enabling operators to combine position data with engine and sensor metrics for smarter fleet management.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates sent over LTE Cat 1 \(with 2G fallback\) for consistent tracking.
- CAN engine, drivetrain and vehicle sensor data available to Plaspy for diagnostics and fuel monitoring.
- Fuel monitoring and performance metrics derived from CAN parameters to support preventative maintenance.
- Driver and asset identification via iButton/1‑Wire RFID readers, plus proximity or presence detection with Bluetooth sensors and beacons.
- Anti‑theft and immobilizer workflows can be implemented through CAN or vehicle wiring integrations; confirm per vehicle and integration scope.

Its integrated CAN reading capability provides telematics teams with engine‑level data not available from location alone, enabling richer telemetry, fuel monitoring and maintenance intelligence. LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback helps maintain real‑time tracking and reporting in mixed‑coverage environments, while IP67 protection reduces downtime due to environmental exposure.

For fleets that require scalable, Plaspy compatible tracking with diagnostics-grade data, the FMC250 supports modular deployment via Standard or Extended CAN parameter packages and a range of SKUs and accessory options. Combined with Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reporting, the FMC250 helps operators improve utilization, reduce theft risk with anti‑theft workflows, and implement predictive maintenance strategies — all while preserving flexibility for Bluetooth sensors, RFID identification and contactless CAN adapters.
