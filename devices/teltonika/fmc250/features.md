# Teltonika - FMC250 Features

This page summarizes public feature information for the Teltonika FMC250 and how those capabilities are reflected when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive details about the FMC250 hardware, telemetry channels, accessory support, and the types of data Plaspy can consume for fleet visibility and operational workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, SKU, installation type and manufacturer implementation. Before deployment, confirm the specific FMC250 variant and firmware feature set for your installation and review the official Teltonika documentation for the latest product details.

## Feature Overview

The FMC250 is a vehicle telematics tracker designed to combine reliable GNSS tracking, cellular connectivity and vehicle-level telemetry into a single unit. It is positioned for commercial fleet use where environmental durability and deep vehicle insight are required.

- Real time GNSS location tracking combined with continuous telemetry for fleet visibility.  
- LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain reporting across mixed coverage areas.  
- Integrated CAN bus data processor with support for over 100 vehicle parameters for diagnostics and analytics.  
- IP67 water resistant enclosure suitable for on vehicle installations in harsh environments.  
- Compatibility with Bluetooth accessories and iButton or 1 Wire RFID for driver and asset identification.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMC250

- GNSS location reporting suitable for vehicle telematics and route visibility.  
- LTE Cat 1 cellular modem with fallback to 2G networks for resilient data transmission.  
- Built in CAN data processing able to read and forward 100 plus CAN parameters for engine and vehicle sensor telemetry.  
- IP67 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water ingress in outdoor and vehicle applications.  
- Compatibility with Bluetooth accessories such as beacons and sensors for expanded sensing and presence detection.  
- Support for iButton and 1 Wire RFID based driver or asset identification workflows.  
- Multiple SKUs and packaged options including Standard and Extended CAN parameter packages to match reporting requirements.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the FMC250 location and CAN derived telemetry to provide combined position and vehicle metric visibility. Data from the FMC250 is normalized into dashboards, reports and alerts so operators can correlate location with vehicle health and usage patterns.

- Plaspy receives GNSS location updates and displays real time position on maps and route histories.  
- CAN parameters are processed into readable telemetry fields for diagnostics, fuel related analytics and maintenance planning.  
- Bluetooth accessory and iButton events are presented in Plaspy for driver identification and asset presence tracking.  
- Telemetry and fault signals can be used to drive maintenance triggers, alerts and reporting in Plaspy.  
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and normalizes incoming data so devices can be added without manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking where combined location and engine data support route compliance and utilization analysis.  
- Preventative maintenance programs using CAN derived metrics to schedule service before failures occur.  
- Heavy duty and utility vehicle monitoring where IP67 protection and robust cellular connectivity are required.  
- Driver and asset identification workflows using iButton or RFID plus Bluetooth proximity sensors.  
- Fuel monitoring and performance analytics derived from CAN parameters for cost control and reporting.  
- Theft prevention and immobilization or anti theft workflows implemented via CAN or vehicle wiring integrations depending on vehicle scope.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets may differ between FMC250 SKUs and between Standard and Extended CAN parameter packages.  
- Firmware version and manufacturer configuration affect which CAN parameters and accessory integrations are available.  
- Physical installation and vehicle wiring can change which telemetry channels and immobilizer workflows are possible.  
- Bluetooth accessory and RFID support depend on SKU options and accessory configuration at deployment.  
- Regional cellular bands and regulatory variants may influence connectivity behavior and should be verified for your market.
