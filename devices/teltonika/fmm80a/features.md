# Teltonika - FMM80A Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Teltonika FMM80A and explains how those capabilities are commonly used with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing features and operational value so teams can understand what the tracker delivers in typical fleet, rental, and delivery scenarios when paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current, device specific details consult Teltonika documentation and release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The FMM80A is a plug and play OBD II tracker optimized for fast installation and continuous telematics. It is designed to deliver position, basic vehicle telemetry and sensor data with minimal setup, and is compatible with Plaspy for real time monitoring and historical analysis.

- Plug and play OBD II connection for fast time to deployment and powered operation directly from the vehicle port.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT cellular connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain telemetry coverage across North America.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support to pair external beacons and sensors such as temperature, humidity, magnet, and movement devices.
- Configurable 3 axis accelerometer for crash detection and crash trace capture for post event review.
- GPS based position reporting for live tracking and route history.
- Integration with Teltonika remote management tools for firmware updates and centralized configuration.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMM80A

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for rapid installation and power from the vehicle port.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT connectivity with fallback to 2G to maximize coverage and power efficiency.
- Built in Bluetooth Low Energy for pairing external beacons and environmental sensors.
- Configurable 3 axis accelerometer for impact detection and detailed trace capture.
- GPS based location reporting for live tracking, geolocation, and history.
- Support for OBD II telematics parameters when available from the vehicle CAN OBD system including ignition status and other vehicle supplied data.
- Compatibility with Teltonika device management services for FOTA and remote configuration.
- North America optimized variants and packaging options for fleet scale and single unit deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the FMM80A is deployed with Plaspy, location and sensor data are received into the platform for visualization, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy presents the device telemetry in dashboards and history views to support everyday fleet operations and incident review.

- Real time GPS location and historical route playback available inside Plaspy for tracking and route analysis.
- Crash detection events and accelerometer traces transmitted to Plaspy for incident logging and review workflows.
- BLE sensor readings such as temperature and humidity can be passed to Plaspy to support condition monitoring and alerting.
- OBD II vehicle parameters like ignition status and other available vehicle telemetry are surfaced in Plaspy where the vehicle supplies those values.
- Teltonika remote management simplifies fleet administration while Plaspy provides centralized operational visibility across devices.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for urban and regional vehicle fleets using live tracking and historical reports.
- Rental and car sharing where quick OBD II installation reduces downtime and enables usage monitoring and incident logging.
- Delivery and logistics operations that combine real time location with BLE based condition monitoring for sensitive cargo.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows leveraging immediate location updates and movement alerts.
