# Teltonika - FMM80A

![FMM80A](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMM80A is a plug-and-play OBD II GPS tracker designed for quick deployment and dependable telematics. Intended for fleet, rental, and delivery applications, the device provides real-time position monitoring and vehicle telemetry with minimal installation effort by connecting directly to the vehicle OBD II port. The model is optimized for the North American market and includes cellular connectivity, Bluetooth Low Energy support for external sensors and beacons, and a configurable accelerometer for incident detection and trace capture.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the FMM80A can stream location and telemetry into the Plaspy platform to enable live maps, alerts, and historical reporting without custom development. Its plug-and-play form factor and remote device management integration make it a practical choice for operators who want rapid rollouts and centralized oversight within Plaspy-managed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD II connection for fast installation and immediate telemetry on compatible vehicles
- Real-time GPS location reporting to support live tracking and route monitoring
- LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT cellular connectivity with 2G fallback for broad North American coverage and efficient telemetry
- Bluetooth Low Energy support to extend telemetry with external sensors and beacons for condition monitoring
- Configurable 3 axis accelerometer for crash detection and post event trace capture
- Integration with Teltonika remote management tools for centralized configuration and updates
- Form factor and variants optimized for North American deployments and scalable distribution

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the FMM80A transmits location and vehicle telemetry into Plaspy so fleet managers can monitor assets in real time, receive critical alerts, and analyze historical trips. BLE sensor inputs and accelerometer events enrich vehicle data streams that Plaspy can use for dashboards, notifications, and reporting.

- Real-time GPS location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and route oversight
- Crash detection events and accelerometer traces available in the platform for incident review
- BLE sensor data from paired beacons integrated into Plaspy for condition monitoring and alerts
- Vehicle parameters provided via the OBD II connection incorporated into Plaspy where those parameters are available
- Remote device administration enabled by Teltonika management tools to support scaled Plaspy deployments

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for urban and regional vehicles requiring live tracking and operational reporting
- Rental and car sharing where rapid OBD II installation and usage monitoring speed vehicle turnarounds
- Delivery and logistics operations needing live location plus optional sensor data to protect shipments
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using immediate location updates and movement alerts
- Condition sensitive transport when BLE sensors are paired for temperature or humidity monitoring
