# Teltonika - TAT140

![TAT140](./tracker.png)

The TAT140 from Teltonika is a compact, battery-powered GPS tracker designed for long-term, unattended asset tracking and Plaspy compatible deployments. With 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity and 2G fallback, TAT140 delivers reliable worldwide coverage and configurable periodic reporting to meet real-time tracking and telemetry needs across freight, containers, rental equipment, and construction assets.

Built for durability and low maintenance, the TAT140 combines an IP68-rated housing and optional magnetic holder with power-saving modes that can extend battery life up to three years depending on reporting frequency and usage profile. For fleet management and anti-theft workflows, the device integrates with remote device management tools \(FOTA Web\) and can be deployed as a discreet backup tracker alongside Teltonika vehicle devices — making it an effective Plaspy compatible option for scalable asset visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible asset tracking with periodic and near real-time location reporting for reliable fleet management and asset visibility.
- Global 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with automatic 2G GSM fallback for extended coverage in mixed-network areas.
- Long-life, battery-powered design with configurable reporting intervals and power-saving modes — up to three years of operation in low-frequency scenarios.
- Rugged IP68 enclosure and optional magnetic holder for secure, weatherproof installation on containers, equipment, and heavy machinery.
- Flexible data sending scenarios and FOTA Web remote management for centralized firmware and configuration updates across device fleets.
- Compact, discreet form factor suitable for backup vehicle tracking, anti-theft deployments, and unattended asset monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TAT140 provides consistent position and status feeds that feed Plaspy’s real-time tracking, telemetry dashboards, and alerting. The tracker’s configurable periodic reporting lets you balance update frequency and battery life, while Plaspy handles map visualization, geofencing alerts, and historical route reports. FOTA Web and Teltonika support resources simplify remote maintenance and fleet-wide configuration changes from the Plaspy platform.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy based on configurable reporting intervals.
- Battery and power state reporting to enable automated alerts for low power or tamper events.
- Automatic LTE/2G network status reporting to maintain connectivity awareness in Plaspy dashboards.
- FOTA Web integration for remote firmware and configuration updates across devices managed in Plaspy.
- Operates as a discreet backup tracker alongside Teltonika vehicle devices to support anti-theft and immobilizer workflows when correlated in Plaspy.
