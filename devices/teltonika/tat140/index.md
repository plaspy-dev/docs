# Teltonika - TAT140

<div className="device-logo">
![TAT140](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Teltonika TAT140 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker designed for long term unattended asset tracking. Built for durability, it offers 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback and configurable periodic reporting to provide reliable position updates across freight, containers, rental equipment, and construction assets. An IP68 rated enclosure and optional magnetic holder support weatherproof attachment while power saving modes extend operational life depending on reporting frequency.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TAT140 can feed positional and status data into Plaspy dashboards and workflows to provide scalable asset visibility. Its configurable reporting and long battery life make it suitable for deployments where maintenance access is limited, and remote device management options simplify fleet level upkeep when used alongside Plaspy monitoring and alerting capabilities.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible asset tracker with configurable periodic and near real time location reporting for fleet and asset visibility
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with automatic 2G fallback for extended coverage in mixed network areas
- Battery powered design with power saving modes that can extend operation up to three years in low frequency scenarios
- Rugged IP68 enclosure and optional magnetic holder for weatherproof and discreet mounting
- Flexible reporting scenarios and remote management support for firmware and configuration updates
- Compact, low profile form factor suitable as a backup tracker or for hard to reach assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TAT140 supplies consistent location and status feeds that populate maps, telemetry dashboards, and alerting systems. Plaspy uses the device reporting to balance visibility and battery life while providing historical analysis and operational oversight for distributed asset fleets.

- Real time and periodic location updates delivered to Plaspy based on configurable reporting intervals
- Visibility into battery and power state to trigger automated low power and maintenance alerts
- Geofencing and alert rules in Plaspy to notify teams about unauthorized movement or boundary events
- Centralized device management workflows supported by remote update and configuration tools
- Correlation of TAT140 data with other vehicle trackers in Plaspy for layered anti theft and recovery workflows
