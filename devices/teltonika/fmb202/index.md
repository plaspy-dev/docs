# Teltonika - FMB202

<div className="device-logo">
![FMB202](./Logo.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMB202 is a compact, waterproof IP67 GPS tracker designed for asset and vehicle tracking where durability and extended autonomy are important. It includes internal high gain GNSS and GSM antennas, Bluetooth connectivity, a high capacity internal backup battery capable of operating up to two days in power saving mode, and support for a wide range of monitoring and reporting features. The device accepts a flexible input voltage range, making it suitable for diverse vehicle types including motorbikes and watercraft.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMB202 can deliver the location, status, and event information needed for fleet and asset management workflows on the Plaspy platform. Plaspy can ingest the tracker data to provide live location, historical routes, alerts and operational oversight, allowing organizations to use the FMB202 within an established fleet monitoring environment without changing their management workflows.

## Key Highlights

- IP67 rated waterproof housing for reliable outdoor and harsh environment use.
- Internal high gain GNSS and GSM antennas plus Bluetooth connectivity for flexible deployment and local configuration.
- High capacity internal backup battery with up to two days of operation in power saving mode for periods without external power.
- Wide input voltage compatibility suitable for a variety of vehicles including motorbikes and water transport.
- Onboard accelerometer and multiple inputs for movement detection and driving behavior monitoring.
- Multiple power save and sleep modes to extend operational autonomy when external power is unavailable.
- Support for remote configuration options and firmware updates using Teltonika tools and services mentioned in the device documentation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMB202 provides location and event data that Plaspy maps, aggregates, and presents through dashboards, alerts, and reports. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry to give fleet managers visibility and operational control without requiring deep device level configuration inside the platform.

- Live GPS position and route history for vehicles and assets tracked by the FMB202.
- Event driven alerts such as movement, overspeeding, or other device reported events routed to Plaspy for notifications.
- Status and battery backup monitoring so operators can see when a device is running on internal power.
- Reporting and analytics support for route efficiency, utilization, and driving behavior summaries based on accelerometer events.
- Geofence and operational monitoring tools in Plaspy that use FMB202 position updates for site entry and exit tracking.
- Centralized device list and health overview so administrators can monitor connectivity and recent activity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Agricultural machinery tracking where waterproofing and battery autonomy matter between uses.
- Delivery and logistics fleets monitoring to maintain route history and on time performance.
- Refrigerated transport oversight combined with general asset location tracking for trailers.
- Trailer and towed equipment tracking where independent power and durable housing are required.
- Security and emergency service asset tracking when reliable outdoor operation is needed.
