# Teltonika - FTM880 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Teltonika FTM880 and explains how its capabilities relate to deployments using Plaspy. It focuses on non-sensitive, user-facing information about what the tracker delivers in practical terms and how Plaspy surfaces location and device status for fleet and asset management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware revision, hardware SKU, regional variant, installation method and manufacturer implementation. For device specific details such as supported inputs, outputs, radio bands and current availability, consult the official Teltonika product documentation.

## Feature Overview

The FTM880 is a battery-mounted, rugged GPS tracker designed for long-term asset deployments where wiring is impractical. It combines robust environmental protection, multi-constellation GNSS performance and efficient cellular telemetry to support remote tracking, event reporting and remote device management in Plaspy.

- Battery-mounted form factor suited to trailers, containers and heavy equipment where wiring is limited.
- Rugged IP69K rated enclosure for dust and high pressure wash environments.
- LTE Cat M1 with NB‑IoT support on relevant variants for efficient cellular connectivity.
- Enhanced multi constellation GNSS performance with improved satellite visibility for better positioning.
- Optimized sleep modes to extend deployment life and reduce maintenance needs.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FTM880

- Plaspy compatibility for real-time tracking and integration with fleet dashboards.
- Battery mounted design for maintenance free and temporary installations.
- IP69K rugged housing suitable for harsh outdoor and industrial environments.
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity with NB‑IoT support on M1 variants for wide area telemetry.
- Enhanced multi constellation GNSS support for improved position accuracy.
- Optimized low power sleep modes to extend operational lifetime between maintenance.
- Remote device management and firmware update support via Teltonika FOTA WEB and the FT platform.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The FTM880 reports location, status and available telemetry to Plaspy where devices are represented on maps, in reports and in alerting rules. Plaspy processes incoming device data so operations teams can monitor assets, review history and act on events from a single platform.

- Live position updates and historical route playback visible in Plaspy maps and reports.
- Geofence events and perimeter monitoring available as configurable triggers inside Plaspy.
- Event and input recording such as ignition or alarm signals when exposed by the installed tracker wiring.
- Remote firmware updates and provisioning workflows coordinated through Teltonika remote management and reflected in Plaspy device inventories.
- Alerts and notification rules in Plaspy based on reported device events to support anti theft and operational monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking where a battery mounted unit avoids the need for complex wiring.
- Heavy equipment and construction asset monitoring in harsh environments requiring robust enclosures.
- Long term logistics and site asset tracking where low maintenance operation is important.
- Anti theft monitoring and location recovery workflows combined with Plaspy alerting.
- Remote site telemetry for generators, storage units and infrastructure with periodic reporting needs.
