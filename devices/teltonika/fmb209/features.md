# Teltonika - FMB209 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Teltonika FMB209 and how it is used with Plaspy for fleet and asset tracking. It summarizes the practical capabilities you can expect when integrating the device with Plaspy, focusing on GPS location reporting, RS232 telemetry, and rugged deployment characteristics that matter for real world monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary with firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation chosen for a given region. Where appropriate, this page points to operational uses in Plaspy but does not replace the device manual or Teltonika documentation for the most current technical details.

## Feature Overview

The FMB209 is a compact, water resistant GPS tracker designed for outdoor and trailer use. It is Plaspy compatible and intended to provide reliable position updates and external sensor telemetry in environments where dust and water resistance, battery backup, and serial sensor integration are important.

- Robust IP67 rated enclosure for outdoor and harsh environment deployments.
- RS232 serial interface to connect external telemetry modules such as fuel level equipment.
- Internal 800 mAh Li ion backup battery for continued reporting during temporary power loss.
- 2G GSM connectivity and regional firmware options to meet different certification requirements.
- Compact form factor suitable for trailer and asset mounting where space and protection are priorities.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMB209

- IP67 water and dust resistant casing for reliable outdoor operation.
- RS232 serial port for external sensor and telemetry integration.
- Internal 800 mAh Li ion backup battery to support autonomous reporting.
- 2G GSM connectivity with common regional bands as provided by the manufacturer.
- Regional firmware variants and certification options such as CE RED, E Mark, and AIS140 in specific SKUs.
- Compact, rugged enclosure designed for vehicle and asset installations.
- Compatibility with Teltonika remote management and configuration tools.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FMB209 sends position and telemetry data that Plaspy ingests for real time monitoring, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy reflects device status and external sensor streams so fleet administrators can act on location and telemetry information efficiently.

- Real time GPS positions appear on Plaspy maps and live tracking dashboards.
- RS232 sourced telemetry such as fuel level streams can be incorporated into Plaspy reports and alerts when the sensor data is forwarded by the device.
- Backup battery and online offline status allow Plaspy to surface resilient monitoring events during power interruptions.
- Regional firmware and SKU information can be used during provisioning to ensure correct handling and compliance within Plaspy.
- Devices compatible with Teltonika remote tools can be pre configured before onboarding to Plaspy for faster deployments.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and outdoor asset tracking where a water resistant enclosure and compact form factor are required.
- Fuel monitoring projects that use RS232 connected fuel modules to provide telemetry into Plaspy.
- Short term autonomous deployments where internal battery backup is relied upon during power loss.
- Telematics installations needing a serial interface to integrate custom sensors and telemetry systems.
- Rugged installations in agricultural, construction, or other outdoor environments.

## Feature Availability Notes
