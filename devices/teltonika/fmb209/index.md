# Teltonika FMB209 GPS Tracker — Plaspy Compatible

The Teltonika FMB209 is a compact, water‑resistant GPS tracker designed for reliable asset and vehicle monitoring in harsh outdoor conditions. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the FMB209 provides accurate GPS location and rich telemetry via an RS232 interface, making it an excellent choice for fleet management, trailer tracking, and fuel monitoring projects that require robust hardware and simple integration.

The IP67‑rated enclosure and internal 800 mAh Li‑ion backup battery ensure the FMB209 continues reporting during temporary power loss or when installed on unpowered assets. Available in regional variants with CE RED / E‑Mark and AIS140 firmware options, the tracker is ready for deployment with Plaspy for real‑time tracking, alerts, and telematics reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real‑time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure protects against dust and water for outdoor and harsh environment use.
- RS232 serial interface enables accurate fuel monitoring and integration with external sensors and telemetry systems.
- Internal 800 mAh Li‑ion backup battery supports prolonged autonomous operation when external power is unavailable.
- Regional variants and certifications: CE RED & E‑Mark \(Europe\) and AIS140 compliant firmware \(India\).
- Compact, battery‑backed design suitable for trailer, asset tracking, and short‑term autonomous deployments.
- Easy ordering through Teltonika channels with standard and custom package options.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the FMB209 with Plaspy provides reliable real‑time tracking and telemetry collection. Plaspy ingests GPS positions, device status, and external sensor data forwarded from the tracker so fleet managers and asset owners get immediate visibility, customizable alerts, and historical reports. The RS232 interface allows the FMB209 to deliver fuel level and other sensor readings directly into Plaspy’s telematics pipeline.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking dashboards.
- RS232 external sensor and fuel monitoring data mapped into Plaspy reports and alerts.
- Backup battery status and device online/offline events for resilient monitoring during power loss.
- Regional firmware handling \(CE RED/E‑Mark or AIS140\) simplifies regulatory compliance and Plaspy provisioning.
- Integration with Teltonika remote tools for device configuration prior to Plaspy onboarding.
