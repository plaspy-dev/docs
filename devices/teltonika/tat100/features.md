# Teltonika - TAT100 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the Teltonika TAT100 and how its features are used with Plaspy for asset tracking, monitoring, and reporting. It focuses on practical capabilities and the typical visibility you can expect inside Plaspy without detailing private configuration methods.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, SKU and installation method. Where applicable the content below notes optional capabilities and limitations; for the most current technical details consult the device manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The TAT100 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker built for long deployments on non powered assets. It pairs rugged, weather resistant packaging with configurable reporting logic so operators can trade off reporting frequency and battery life according to operational needs.

- Battery powered design with 2-cell and 3-cell options to extend deployment intervals between maintenance visits.  
- IP68 rated enclosure for reliable outdoor and industrial use.  
- Quad band 2G cellular connectivity for broad GSM network coverage where 2G is available.  
- Configurable reporting scenarios to tune update frequency for periodic telemetry or more frequent position updates.  
- Wire free installation with multiple mounting options for containers, equipment and discreet backup tracking.  
- Optional micro USB on selected SKUs for charging or local configuration on certain models.

## Core Features of Teltonika - TAT100

- Plaspy compatibility for straightforward ingestion of location and telemetry reports.  
- Rugged IP68 housing suitable for outdoor and industrial asset deployments.  
- Quad band 2G GSM connectivity for legacy cellular networks.  
- Multiple battery configurations, including 2-cell and 3-cell SKUs to balance runtime and size.  
- Configurable reporting logic to manage update frequency and battery consumption.  
- Wire free mounting and compact form factor for discreet placement on assets.  
- Optional micro USB support on some SKUs for charging or on site configuration.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy consumes the TAT100’s periodic location and device status reports to provide mapping, alerts, and history playback. Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and presents the incoming data in dashboards, reports and event feeds so operators can monitor distributed assets without manual protocol tuning.

- Live and near real time location plotting on Plaspy maps based on the tracker reporting interval.  
- Battery and device status telemetry shown in Plaspy to help plan maintenance and battery replacements.  
- Configurable reporting reflected as adjustable update frequency in Plaspy reporting and history.  
- Alerts and geofence events generated from received position reports and device status updates.  
- Historical route playback and exportable reports based on stored location records.  

## Typical Use Cases

- Backup tracking for trailers or leased vehicles where a wire free, discreet tracker adds redundancy.  
- Container and cargo monitoring that needs water resistant hardware and long battery life.  
- Construction and rental equipment tracking to reduce loss and improve utilization of non powered assets.  
- Logistics and storage asset monitoring with periodic telemetry for low maintenance operations.  
- Discreet anti theft awareness on high value or rented equipment integrated into fleet monitoring workflows.
