# Teltonika - FTC921 Features

This page provides a public, features oriented summary of the Teltonika FTC921 in the context of using the device with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities, how the tracker integrates into Plaspy workflows, and what fleet operators and security teams can expect from this model without presenting device internals or private configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional product variant, and the specifics of an installation. For the most current and detailed device specifications, firmware notes, and installation guidance consult the official Teltonika documentation.

## Feature Overview

The FTC921 is a high voltage, 4G LTE Cat 1 GPS tracker built for e mobility and anti theft scenarios and designed to work with remote management tools and Plaspy out of the box. Its combination of cellular fallback, GNSS reception improvements, and power management make it suitable for urban and low speed vehicle fleets that require consistent tracking and low maintenance operation.

- Plaspy compatible device that streams location and telemetry for real time fleet visibility.
- LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain coverage in varied cellular environments.
- High voltage input support for direct connection to electric motorcycles, mopeds, and similar vehicles.
- Enhanced GNSS reception capable of improving low speed and urban positioning with support for many visible satellites.
- Optimized sleep modes and power management to reduce vehicle battery drain and extend device uptime.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FTC921

- Compatibility with Plaspy for immediate integration into fleet tracking and reporting workflows.
- LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with fallback to 2G for broader coverage and stable telemetry.
- High voltage vehicle power input designed for e mobility platforms.
- Enhanced GNSS positioning performance aimed at urban and low speed accuracy.
- Optimized sleep and power management modes to minimize impact on vehicle batteries.
- Remote device management compatibility via Teltonika FOTA WEB and TCT tools.
- Packaging options for standard bulk kits and single unit testing to fit deployment scale.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the FTC921 location and telemetry streams to provide mapping, monitoring, and historical reporting. The tracker reports GNSS coordinates, power state information, and device status that Plaspy can display and use for operational alerts and analytics.

- Real time location updates ingested into Plaspy for live tracking and route playback.
- Power status and device health indicators available in Plaspy to support anti theft workflows and power loss notifications.
- Improved GNSS data feeding Plaspy maps for better positioning in urban and low speed contexts.
- Remote management and firmware updates coordinated through the device management tools while device telemetry remains visible in Plaspy.
- Integration into Plaspy dashboards and reports to support fleet oversight and operational decision making.

## Typical Use Cases

- Urban delivery and micromobility fleet tracking where low speed accuracy and power management are important.
- E mobility fleet management for electric motorcycles, mopeds, and similar vehicles with high voltage power systems.
- Anti theft monitoring including power loss detection and continuous location reporting.
- Small scale pilots or single unit testing using custom packaged options before fleet wide deployment.
- Basic track and trace operations for asset recovery and route history analysis.
