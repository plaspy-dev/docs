# Teltonika - FMB204 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Teltonika FMB204 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect when the FMB204 is integrated into Plaspy for real time tracking, fleet workflows, and asset monitoring. Information below reflects what is publicly documented about the FMB204 and how those capabilities surface in Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer choices. For device specific configuration, firmware updates, and the most current technical details consult the official Teltonika documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The FMB204 is a compact, water resistant 2G GPS tracker designed for long autonomous operation and outdoor asset use. It emphasizes resilient telemetry, Bluetooth Low Energy sensor support, and an internal backup battery to maintain reporting during power interruptions.

- IP67 rated housing for outdoor and trailer deployments where moisture resistance is important
- Built in 1,800 mAh internal backup battery to support prolonged autonomous operation and power loss notifications
- Bluetooth Low Energy support to pair environmental sensors and presence beacons for richer telemetry
- 2G cellular connectivity on commonly used bands suitable for many regional deployments
- Wide operating voltage range for flexible installation across trailers, two wheelers, and auxiliary equipment
- Compatibility with Teltonika remote management tools for field configuration and firmware management

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMB204

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for real time location reporting and fleet management integration
- IP67 water resistant enclosure for reliable outdoor use
- Large internal Li ion backup battery for continued reporting during primary power loss
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for connecting external sensors and beacons
- 2G cellular connectivity on specified bands for telemetry uplink
- Wide operating voltage range to suit low voltage vehicles and trailers
- Support for Teltonika remote management workflows such as FOTA WEB and Teltonika Configurator

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes the telemetry the FMB204 transmits so teams can monitor assets and trigger operational alerts. The tracker’s position updates and sensor inputs appear in Plaspy dashboards and reports, helping maintain continuous situational awareness across assets.

- Real time GPS position updates show asset location on Plaspy maps for live tracking and history playback
- BLE sensor data such as temperature, humidity, magnet and movement status can be forwarded into Plaspy alongside location for contextual alerts
- Internal backup battery state and power loss reporting allow Plaspy to surface power related alerts and continuity information
- Remote management compatibility helps keep firmware and configuration consistent so Plaspy receives predictable telemetry
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols so device onboarding is streamlined for standard Teltonika setups

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container security where water resistance and battery backup keep tracking active during power loss
- Two wheeler and scooter installations that require a compact tracker with a wide voltage range
- Remote asset monitoring for non powered equipment where BLE sensors add environmental context
- Cold chain or outdoor asset monitoring using BLE temperature and humidity sensors alongside location
- Presence and tamper detection using BLE beacons for magnet or movement alerts
- Fleet auxiliary device tracking for trailers, generators, and other attachable assets
