# Teltonika - FMB204

![FMB204](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMB204 is a compact, water resistant 2G GPS tracker built for long autonomous operation in harsh outdoor environments. Its IP67 rated casing, large internal backup battery, and BLE connectivity make it suitable for trailer tracking, two‑wheelers and remote asset monitoring where durable, continuous telemetry is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the FMB204 delivers position and sensor data into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerts and reporting. Its combination of rugged construction, wide operating voltage and onboard backup power aligns with common Plaspy workflows for anti‑theft, presence detection and environmental sensing, enabling straightforward integration into fleet and asset management setups.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real time tracking and fleet management integration
- IP67 water resistant housing for reliable outdoor asset and trailer deployments
- Large 1,800 mAh internal backup battery to support prolonged autonomous operation and power loss notifications
- Bluetooth Low Energy support to connect external sensors and beacons for additional telemetry
- Wide operating voltage of 6–30 V for compatibility with trailers, two wheelers and auxiliary devices
- 2G cellular connectivity on bands B2 B3 B5 B8 and rugged form factor for remote asset use
- Compatibility with Teltonika remote management tools for field configuration and updates

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the FMB204 forwards GPS positions, BLE sensor inputs and power backup state to the platform so teams can monitor assets in real time, receive alerts and analyze historical journeys. Plaspy ingests and visualizes the device telemetry so operators can act on location events and sensor conditions without needing custom integrations.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy for mapping and live tracking
- BLE sensor data such as temperature humidity magnet and movement sent alongside GPS for enriched situational awareness
- Internal backup battery enables continued reporting during primary power loss and supports power loss alerts in Plaspy
- Device variants and packaging options simplify deployments and match installation needs
- Teltonika remote management compatibility helps keep device configuration and software consistent so Plaspy receives predictable telemetry

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container security where water resistant design and backup battery maintain continuous tracking
- Two wheeler and scooter monitoring using the compact form factor and low voltage compatibility
- Fleet auxiliary device tracking for trailers generators and other non powered assets
- Environmental sensing for cold chain or outdoor equipment using BLE temperature and humidity sensors
- Remote asset presence detection with magnet and movement beacons to alert on tampering or movement

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FMB204 is a practical choice for organizations that need a resilient tracker for outdoor assets and trailers while also wanting to extend telemetry with wireless sensors. Its rugged enclosure and long backup runtime reduce the need for frequent maintenance and help maintain visibility during power interruptions, which is important for anti‑theft and continuous monitoring scenarios.

Plaspy can take the FMB204 telemetry and surface it in dashboards alerts and reports that support operational oversight and incident response.
