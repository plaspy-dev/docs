# Teltonika - FMB204

![FMB204](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMB204 is a compact, water‑resistant 2G GPS tracker built for long autonomous operation in harsh outdoor environments. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the FMB204 combines IP67-class protection, a large internal backup battery and Bluetooth Low Energy support so you can add environmental sensing and presence detection to your Plaspy real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.

Designed for trailer tracking, two‑wheelers and remote asset monitoring, the FMB204 delivers reliable telemetry and position data over 2G networks \(B2/B3/B5/B8\). Its rugged construction and wide operating voltage \(6–30 V\) make integration easy across vehicles and equipment while maintaining continuous operation for anti‑theft and asset protection use cases.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real‑time tracking and fleet management integration.
- IP67 water‑resistant housing for dependable outdoor asset and trailer deployments.
- Large 1,800 mAh Li‑ion internal backup battery for prolonged autonomous operation and power‑loss notifications.
- Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) support to connect external sensors and beacons for temperature, humidity, magnet and movement telemetry.
- Wide operating voltage \(6–30 V\) suitable for two‑wheelers, trailers and auxiliary fleet devices.
- 2G GSM connectivity on bands B2/B3/B5/B8 with standard and configurable product packages.
- Integration ready with Teltonika remote management tools \(FOTA WEB, Teltonika Configurator\) for field configuration and firmware updates.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the FMB204 supplies continuous location and sensor telemetry so you can monitor assets in real time, trigger anti‑theft alerts and build fleet management reports. The device captures GPS position and BLE sensor data, holds power‑backup state, and forwards telemetry via cellular networks to Plaspy. Plaspy then normalizes, displays and stores that data for live dashboards, alerts and historical analysis.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for mapping and live tracking.
- BLE sensor inputs for temperature, humidity, magnet status and movement are sent alongside GPS data for richer situational awareness.
- Internal backup battery enables continued reporting during primary power loss and supports power‑loss alerts in Plaspy.
- Device variants and packaging options simplify deployments: standard packages and custom kits \(for example a Micro USB cable variant\) are available to match installation needs.
- Teltonika remote management compatibility streamlines configuration and firmware updates so Plaspy receives consistent, well‑formatted telemetry.

## Use Cases

- Trailer and container security — water‑resistant design and backup battery maintain continuous GPS tracking for anti‑theft monitoring.
- Two‑wheeler installations — low‑voltage compatibility and compact form factor enable discreet mounting on scooters and motorcycles.
- Fleet auxiliary device monitoring — add telemetry to trailers, generators and non‑powered assets to extend fleet management visibility.
- Environmental sensing for cold chain or outdoor assets — BLE sensors report temperature and humidity alongside location in Plaspy dashboards.
- Remote asset presence detection — magnet and movement beacons paired via BLE alert Plaspy when an asset is opened, moved or tampered with.
