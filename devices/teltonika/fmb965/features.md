# Teltonika - FMB965 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the Teltonika FMB965 and how the device works with Plaspy for fleet management and asset monitoring. It summarizes the practical capabilities you can expect when using the FMB965 with Plaspy, and highlights the kinds of telemetry and operational value those capabilities deliver in Plaspy dashboards and workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional network conditions, and manufacturer implementation. The content below reflects the device description provided for the FMB965 and general Plaspy integration patterns; for the most current and detailed device specifications consult the official Teltonika documentation.

## Feature Overview

The FMB965 is a compact 2G GPS tracker designed for outdoor use and long autonomous operation. It combines a rugged IP67 enclosure and internal battery backup with Bluetooth Low Energy support to enable basic sensor pairing, making it suitable for small vehicles, trailers, marine leisure craft, and exposed assets.

- Real-time 2G GNSS location reporting for live tracking and historical routes in Plaspy
- IP67 rated enclosure for water and dust resistance in outdoor and marine environments
- Internal 1,200 mAh Li ion backup battery and ultra low power consumption for extended offline autonomy
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for pairing external beacons and sensors such as temperature, humidity, magnet and motion devices
- Standard telematics functions like geofencing and event reporting visible inside Plaspy
- Compatibility with Teltonika remote management tools for firmware updates and mass configuration prior to Plaspy deployment

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMB965

- 2G cellular connectivity for position and telemetry uplink
- IP67 rated casing for water and dust protection suitable for exposed installations
- Internal 1,200 mAh Li ion backup battery to maintain reporting when external power is absent
- Ultra low power consumption with a dedicated low power sleep mode to extend battery life
- Bluetooth Low Energy for connecting external beacons and environmental sensors
- Location tracking and geofencing capable of producing event reports for platform alerts
- Support for Teltonika remote management workflows including firmware updates via FOTA WEB
- Compact form factor designed for discreet installation on motorcycles, trailers, and small assets

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMB965 sends position updates, sensor readings, and event reports that Plaspy ingests to provide visibility, history, and alerts. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol during onboarding, and the device can be configured to communicate using standard Teltonika tools and remote management prior to connecting to Plaspy.

- Live position updates appear on Plaspy maps and are available for historical playback and route analysis
- Geofence breaches and configured event reports surface as alerts in Plaspy for operational follow up
- BLE sensor data such as temperature, humidity, magnet and motion readings can be forwarded to Plaspy as supplementary telemetry
- Remote management and FOTA workflows from Teltonika help prepare and maintain devices before and during Plaspy deployment
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and supports standard device reporting to the Plaspy server for streamlined onboarding
