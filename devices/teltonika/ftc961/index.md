# Teltonika - FTC961

![FTC961](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FTC961 is a rugged 4G LTE Cat 1 GPS tracker engineered for demanding mobile and off road applications. Designed with high precision GNSS, robust cellular connectivity and a sealed IP69K enclosure, the FTC961 targets fleets and heavy machinery that require reliable real time location and telemetry in harsh conditions. Remote device management options such as FOTA WEB and the Teltonika FT platform support simplified lifecycle handling for large deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FTC961 can integrate into Plaspy for fleet monitoring, alerting and reporting workflows. When paired with Plaspy, the device provides continuous location updates, event notifications and status information that fleet managers use to maintain operational visibility and improve decision making across vehicles, equipment and site assets.

## Key Highlights

- Built for harsh environments with IP69K rated enclosure for protection against dust and high pressure wash
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback for broad regional cellular coverage
- Enhanced GNSS precision supporting up to 41 visible satellites for improved positioning and low speed accuracy
- High voltage input and optimized power management to suit heavy machinery and industrial vehicles
- Digital inputs and outputs for ignition monitoring, event signaling and external control integration
- Remote management via FOTA WEB and Teltonika FT platform for firmware and configuration updates at scale

## How It Works with Plaspy

The FTC961 sends position and telematics data to Plaspy using standard reporting methods supported by Teltonika devices. Once configured, Plaspy receives live tracking, events and device status so teams can monitor assets in real time, review history and set up automated alerts and reports.

- Real time location updates and historical playback visible in Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight
- Event and ignition monitoring via digital inputs to detect engine state, door events and other inputs
- Telemetry delivery to Plaspy for fleet reporting and integration with management dashboards
- Remote control options through digital outputs when configured with Plaspy for workflows such as immobilizer actions
- Scalable device maintenance using FOTA WEB and FT platform support to keep fleet firmware and configurations consistent

## Typical Use Cases

- Heavy equipment monitoring in mining and construction where IP69K protection and high voltage support are needed
- Agricultural vehicle tracking for field machinery operating in dusty and wet conditions
- E mobility and industrial vehicle fleets that benefit from grouped remote updates and centralized management
