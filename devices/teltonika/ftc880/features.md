# Teltonika - FTC880 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Teltonika FTC880 and how the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on non sensitive, user facing capabilities and practical operational details that help fleet managers, integrators, and asset owners understand what the FTC880 delivers when paired with Plaspy for tracking and monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional module variant, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Before finalizing deployment plans, verify device specifics and the latest firmware behavior with Teltonika documentation and the device configuration tools.

## Feature Overview

The FTC880 is a battery mounted GPS tracker designed for long term operation in harsh environments. It combines rugged mechanical protection, multi constellation GNSS reception, and managed remote update capabilities to provide reliable position reporting and event visibility for a range of vehicle and asset scenarios.

- Battery optimized design with low power and sleep modes for extended off grid operation.
- Multi constellation GNSS reception with improved positioning and visibility of up to 41 satellites.
- Cellular connectivity with 4G LTE Cat 1 and fallback to 2G for broad coverage and telemetry.
- IP69K rated enclosure built for dust, high pressure, and steam washdowns in heavy equipment settings.
- Remote device management through Teltonika FOTA WEB for firmware updates and configuration.
- Regional module variants and packaging options to support different markets and deployment scales.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FTC880

- Battery mounted form factor engineered for prolonged operation away from vehicle power sources.
- Low power and sleep modes to balance reporting frequency and battery life.
- Multi constellation GNSS support with visibility improvements up to 41 satellites for better positioning.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity complemented by 2G fallback for resilient telemetry.
- IP69K certified housing for protection against dust and high pressure washdowns.
- Remote firmware update and configuration management via Teltonika Fleet Telematics tools and FOTA WEB.
- Basic I/O power supply cable included to support power and event reporting integration with vehicle wiring or harnesses.
- Regional hardware module options to match local cellular bands and certification requirements.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FTC880 provides position and device telemetry that Plaspy ingests for live monitoring, historical analysis, and operational alerts. Plaspy detects compatible device protocols automatically and reflects device state, location, and events in dashboards and reports to support day to day fleet operations.

- Live GNSS positions and device location updates appear on Plaspy maps and tracking timelines for operational visibility.
- Device events such as movement and power or connectivity status are visible in Plaspy event logs and can drive alerts or workflows.
- Remote firmware and configuration workflows are simplified when devices are managed with Teltonika tools alongside Plaspy device lifecycle processes.
- Configurable reporting intervals from the tracker help balance near real time visibility and battery consumption as seen in Plaspy reports.
- Integration with Plaspy telemetry and peripherals enables combining FTC880 data with vehicle inputs or external sensors for richer operational insights.

## Typical Use Cases

- Mixed fleet telematics where battery mounted trackers are required for trailers or non powered assets.

- Heavy equipment monitoring in construction, agriculture, and mining where rugged enclosure and washdown resistance matter.
- Remote or seasonal deployments where off grid operation and configurable reporting intervals extend service between maintenance visits.
- Site or equipment mobility tracking for assets that undergo frequent relocations across worksites.
