# Teltonika - FTC880

![FTC880](./tracker.png)

The FTC880 from Teltonika is a next-generation, battery-mounted GPS tracker designed for demanding vehicle and asset-tracking deployments. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the FTC880 pairs rugged hardware and advanced GNSS with Teltonika’s remote device management to deliver reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for fleet management, logistics, and heavy equipment in harsh environments.

Built for long-term battery operation and extreme conditions, the FTC880 provides dependable position updates, event reporting and remote maintenance via FOTA WEB and Teltonika tools. Its IP69K-rated casing, multi-constellation GNSS capability \(up to 41 visible satellites\) and LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback make it a practical choice when you need Plaspy compatible tracking that holds up in construction, agriculture, mining and across mixed fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management integrations.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with fallback to 2G \(GSM\) for broad coverage and reliable telemetry.
- Rugged IP69K waterproof housing engineered for dust, high-pressure and steam washdowns.
- Improved GNSS performance with multi-constellation reception and visibility of up to 41 satellites for better positioning.
- Advanced low-power and sleep modes optimized for prolonged battery-mounted operation and minimal vehicle battery drain.
- Remote device management via Teltonika FOTA WEB and support tools for firmware updates and configuration.
- Available regional module variants and bundle options to suit EU/MEA/APAC and LATAM deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated into Plaspy, the FTC880 feeds high-quality GNSS positions and device telemetry into Plaspy’s tracking dashboards and reporting workflows. The tracker’s cellular link transmits location, connectivity status and event reports in near real time while Teltonika’s remote management features simplify firmware updates and configuration management from the cloud.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent from FTC880 to Plaspy for live tracking and geofencing.
- Device event reporting \(movement, power/connectivity events\) visible in Plaspy for alerts and historical logs.
- Remote firmware and configuration management through Teltonika FOTA WEB integrated into device lifecycle workflows.
- Battery-operated tracking with configurable reporting intervals to balance real-time visibility and power consumption.
- When combined with vehicle telemetry inputs or Plaspy peripherals, operators can build workflows for fuel monitoring, ignition detection, immobilizer control or Bluetooth sensors — leveraging Plaspy’s telemetry and alerting capabilities.
