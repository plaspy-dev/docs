# Teltonika - FM 2100

![FM 2100](./logo.jpg)

The Teltonika FM2100 is a versatile GPS tracker with GSM connectivity, designed to accurately determine the coordinates of remote objects and transmit them via the GSM network. This device is ideal for applications where real-time location tracking of vehicles or other assets is required. The FM2100 is equipped with additional inputs and outputs, allowing you to control and monitor other devices on the remote objects.

One of the standout features of the FM2100 is its ability to track remote objects quickly and easily. With support for GPRS class 10 \(up to 44.8 kbps\) and SMS \(text/data\) communication, you can receive real-time updates on the location of your assets. The device operates on quad-band GSM frequencies, making it compatible with both European/Asian \(900 MHz/1800 MHz\) and American \(850 MHz/1900 MHz\) networks.

The FM2100 is built to withstand harsh environments, making it suitable for installation in vehicles, trucks, or other moving objects. Its robust casing ensures durability and reliability in challenging conditions. The device also features two digital inputs and two digital outputs, providing flexibility for various tasks on remote objects. Additionally, the FM2100 offers NMEA output via RS232, which can be used for navigation purposes.

With three LED indicators \("Modem," "Status," and "Navigate"\), the FM2100 provides clear visual feedback on its operation. Despite its advanced features, this GPS tracker is available at a very reasonable price, making it an excellent choice for businesses and individuals looking for a cost-effective solution for remote object tracking.

### Main Features:

- Track your remote objects \(trucks, cars\) quickly and easily
- Supports GPRS class 10 \(up to 44.8 kbps\) and SMS communication
- Quad-band GSM compatibility for global coverage
- Robust casing for installation in harsh environments
- 2 digital inputs and 2 digital outputs for versatile functionality
- NMEA output via RS232 for navigation purposes
- 3 LED indicators for clear visual feedback
- Advanced features at a reasonable price
