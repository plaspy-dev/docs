# Teltonika - TAT141 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Teltonika TAT141 with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical capabilities of the battery powered TAT141 for asset monitoring and explains how those capabilities map to Plaspy visibility, reporting, and device management workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The TAT141 is a compact, battery operated asset tracker designed for long term deployments where low maintenance and reliable location reporting are priorities. It combines low power wide area cellular connectivity, rugged environmental protection, and easy installation options to support long duration monitoring of goods, containers, and equipment.

- Battery powered operation with configurable power modes to extend service intervals and reduce maintenance
- LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT connectivity for broad regional coverage and low power data transfer
- IP68 rated enclosure to withstand harsh outdoor and industrial environments
- Magnetic mounting option to enable fast, non invasive installation on assets
- Configurable periodic location reports and flexible data sending scenarios to balance visibility and battery life

## Core Features of Teltonika - TAT141

- Battery powered design optimized for long term, low maintenance asset telemetry
- Dual low power wide area connectivity options using LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT
- Rugged IP68 environmental protection for outdoor and industrial use
- Magnetic mounting for quick attachment and removal without wiring
- Configurable reporting intervals and power modes to tune battery life versus visibility
- GNSS based location reporting supported for periodic position updates
- Integration with Teltonika device management workflows for remote configuration and firmware updates

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy the TAT141 provides periodic location and telemetry that Plaspy ingests for centralized tracking, historical playback, and alerting. Plaspy dashboards and workflows surface device state, reporting cadence, and alert history so operators can manage long term asset fleets from a single platform.

- Location updates and periodic reports appear in Plaspy location history and maps according to the configured reporting schedule
- Configurable reporting intervals from the device translate to controllable update frequency in Plaspy to balance battery life and visibility
- Device state and telemetry are available in Plaspy for monitoring long term asset health and connectivity
- Centralized alerting in Plaspy can use movement, geofence, or custom rule triggers based on the tracker location and reported events
- Remote provisioning and over the air firmware workflows supported through Teltonika management tools complement Plaspy device lifecycle operations

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term shipment and pallet tracking where minimal maintenance and long battery life are required
- Container monitoring for assets in transit or storage with durable environmental protection
- Backup tracking for vehicles or trailers to complement primary vehicle trackers and enhance transport security
- Equipment and heavy machinery location tracking on sites where wiring is impractical
- Distributed remote asset fleets that need centralized visibility and scheduled reporting

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets may differ by firmware version and Teltonika firmware updates can add or change behavior
- Hardware revisions and regional product variants can affect supported cellular bands and certifications
- Installation method and antenna placement can influence connectivity performance and reporting reliability
- Some management capabilities rely on Teltonika remote tools in combination with Plaspy workflows
- Confirm packaging and part codes for your region with the manufacturer or authorized distributor
