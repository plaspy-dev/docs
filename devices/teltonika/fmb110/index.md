# Teltonika - FMB110

![FMB110](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMB110 is a compact 2G GPS tracker designed for reliable vehicle tracking and basic telematics. With internal cellular and GNSS antennas, support for 2G GSM bands \(B2, B3, B5, B8\), and integrated interfaces for temperature sensors and RFID/iButton identification, the FMB110 is Plaspy compatible and ready to deliver real-time tracking, telemetry, and anti-theft features for fleet management, logistics, and car-sharing applications.

Built for straightforward integration with platforms like Plaspy, the FMB110 supports remote engine blocking \(immobilizer\), BLE connectivity to external beacons and sensors, and 1-Wire input for DS18B20 temperature probes and iButton/RFID tags. Its compact form factor and standard packaging options make it simple to deploy at scale for mixed vehicle fleets and cold-chain asset monitoring while taking advantage of Plaspy’s dashboards, alerts, and reporting tools.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering dependable real-time tracking over 2G GSM networks.
- Compact design with internal cellular and GNSS antennas for clean vehicle installations.
- 1-Wire interface for DS18B20 temperature sensors and iButton/RFID tag reading—ideal for cold-chain telemetry and driver identification.
- Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) support to connect wireless beacons and sensors for temperature, humidity, movement, and magnet status monitoring.
- Remote engine blocking \(immobilizer\) capability for fleet anti-theft and access control.
- Standard SKU and packaging options \(cables and box quantities\) facilitate procurement and deployment.
- Manufacturer support via Teltonika Knowledge Base, firmware downloads, FOTA Web and Teltonika Configurator for remote updates and device management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the FMB110 becomes a compact yet capable endpoint for vehicle telemetry and location intelligence. The device transmits GNSS positions and status information over GSM to Plaspy’s servers, where data appears in real-time on maps, in alerts, and in historical reports. Plaspy can consume the tracker’s telemetry and sensor streams to enable fleet management, anti-theft actions, and specialized monitoring like temperature logging.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates—track vehicle movement and status on Plaspy dashboards.
- Ignition and remote engine blocking—monitor ignition status and execute immobilizer commands for anti-theft or fleet control.
- Temperature monitoring via 1-Wire—log and alert on DS18B20 readings for cold-chain and asset protection.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons—bring external temperature, humidity, movement, or magnet state into Plaspy through BLE.
- Telemetry for eco-driving—feed Plaspy with driver behavior data to support fuel monitoring initiatives and safer driving programs.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization—use Plaspy to monitor location and trigger remote engine blocking if unauthorized use is detected.
- Cold-chain temperature logging—deploy DS18B20 probes via the 1-Wire interface and collect temperature telemetry in Plaspy for regulatory compliance and spoilage prevention.
- Car-sharing and driver identification—read driver iButton/RFID tags to manage access, billing, and usage logs within Plaspy.
- Trailer and cargo monitoring with BLE sensors—pair external BLE beacons to detect humidity, vibration, or magnetic tampering and surface alerts in Plaspy.
- Eco-driving and basic fuel monitoring programs—use telemetry and driver behavior data to support fuel efficiency initiatives and vehicle maintenance planning.
