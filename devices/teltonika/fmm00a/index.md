# Teltonika - FMM00A

![FMM00A](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMM00A is a plug-and-play OBD-II GPS tracker engineered for North American fleets, rental and sharing services, and mixed vehicle deployments. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the FMM00A combines LTE Cat M1 connectivity \(with NB‑IoT capability where available\), GNSS positioning and Bluetooth to deliver reliable real-time tracking, vehicle telemetry and OEM-level diagnostic data without complex wiring.

Designed for fast installation and mass provisioning, the FMM00A plugs into a vehicle’s OBD-II port and begins streaming location, odometer, fuel level and diagnostic parameters immediately. For fleet managers using Plaspy, this means rapid rollout, richer telematics \(real fuel monitoring, true odometer readings\), and seamless integration with Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reports.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD-II form factor for fast installation and minimal downtime.
- Plaspy compatible: streams GNSS and OBD telemetry into Plaspy for real-time tracking and analytics.
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity with NB‑IoT support where available for wide coverage and efficient data usage.
- Reads standard OBD-II PIDs and manufacturer OEM parameters for accurate odometer and fuel monitoring.
- Built-in accelerometer for motion, driving behaviour and crash detection insights.
- Bluetooth® connectivity for local configuration and integration with Bluetooth sensors and beacons.
- Supported by Teltonika remote management \(FOTA WEB\) for firmware updates and mass provisioning.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to a vehicle, the FMM00A collects GNSS position data and OBD-II diagnostic parameters, then transmits those telemetry packets over LTE Cat M1/NB‑IoT to your Plaspy instance. Plaspy receives the device payload, normalizes parameters and supplies real-time location, events and historical reports to fleet managers and operators.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates for live vehicle tracking and geofencing.
- Vehicle diagnostic and OEM parameter streaming \(odometer, fuel level and related PIDs\) for accurate fuel monitoring and service scheduling.
- Ignition and operational status via OBD-II data to drive trip detection and engine-on/engine-off events.
- Accelerometer-based motion events and driving behaviour data for safety and route analysis.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons support for temperature, movement or auxiliary asset monitoring through Plaspy dashboards.
