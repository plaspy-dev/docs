# Teltonika - FMM00A Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Teltonika FMM00A and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about what the device reports, how it integrates with Plaspy, and the operational value fleet and asset managers can expect from an OBD II plug and play solution.

Exact feature availability can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, vehicle support for specific OBD parameters, and installation choices. For the most current, device specific technical details rely on the manufacturer documentation and firmware notes from Teltonika.

## Feature Overview

The FMM00A is a plug and play OBD II tracker designed for fast installation in North American vehicle fleets, rental and sharing services, and mixed vehicle deployments. It combines cellular connectivity, GNSS positioning, OBD telemetry and local Bluetooth to provide location, diagnostic and vehicle state information suitable for integration with Plaspy.

- Plug and play OBD II form factor for rapid installation and quick fleet rollout.
- GNSS position streaming for real time location and historical route data.
- OBD telemetry including odometer and fuel level reporting where the vehicle exposes those PIDs.
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity with NB‑IoT capability where available for efficient wide area coverage.
- Bluetooth for local configuration and extension with compatible sensors and beacons.
- Built in accelerometer for motion events and driving behaviour insights when enabled.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMM00A

- OBD II interface for reading standard OBD PIDs and supported OEM parameters.
- GNSS receiver for continuous vehicle positioning and movement tracking.
- Cellular modem with LTE Cat M1 and optional NB‑IoT support for low bandwidth telematics.
- Bluetooth connectivity for local setup and integration with compatible Bluetooth sensors and beacons.
- Built in accelerometer to detect motion, impacts, and driving behaviour events.
- Plug and play design drawing power from the OBD II port for straightforward deployments.
- Support for remote device management and firmware updates via Teltonika remote management workflows.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives the telemetry the FMM00A transmits and normalizes it for dashboards, reports and alerts. Data coming from GNSS and OBD telemetry becomes actionable location and vehicle state information in Plaspy, helping operators monitor fleets without invasive installations.

- Real time location updates shown on Plaspy maps and in live tracking interfaces.
- OBD derived parameters such as odometer and fuel level appear in Plaspy reports and can feed service scheduling and fuel analytics.
- Motion and accelerometer based events become driving behaviour or incident indicators within Plaspy event streams.
- Bluetooth sensor signals and beacons paired to the tracker can be surfaced in Plaspy for auxiliary asset monitoring.
- Device status and remote management indicators help administrators track provisioning and firmware lifecycle alongside operational telemetry.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route optimisation using accurate odometer and fuel telemetry.
- Car rental and sharing services for rapid deployment and vehicle condition monitoring.
- Logistics and last mile delivery tracking with live position and driving behaviour visibility.
- EV and advanced vehicle monitoring where OEM OBD parameters expose battery and energy data.
- Supplemental asset tracking using Bluetooth beacons for trailers or auxiliary equipment.
- Mass installations where plug and play OBD II devices reduce installation time and cost.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature exposure depends on vehicle support for specific OBD II PIDs and OEM parameters; not all vehicles expose the same telemetry.
- Firmware versions and hardware revisions from the manufacturer can change available features and behaviour.
- Regional variants and cellular band support may differ for units intended for specific markets.
- Bluetooth accessory support and accelerometer event settings may require configuration during provisioning.
- Refer to Teltonika documentation and firmware release notes for the most current device capabilities and provisioning options.

Pairing the Teltonika FMM00A with Plaspy gives teams a fast way to add location awareness and richer vehicle telemetry to fleet operations without complex wiring. The combination of OBD II data, GNSS position, cellular connectivity and Bluetooth extensibility makes it practical to scale tracking, fuel monitoring and basic vehicle diagnostics across mixed vehicle fleets.
