# Teltonika - FMC150

![FMC150](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMC150 is a professional vehicle GPS tracker designed for fleet applications that require more than basic positioning. It includes a built in CAN bus data processor and is compatible with contactless CAN adapters, enabling extended vehicle insight such as engine and battery status, fuel consumption indicators, and EV specific metrics. The model is intended for mixed fleets and electric vehicles and can be paired with Bluetooth accessories and Teltonika peripherals to expand telemetry and asset identification.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMC150 feeds GPS position and CAN derived telemetry into Plaspy for centralized fleet oversight. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s location and vehicle data support real time monitoring, reporting, maintenance planning, and anti theft workflows while allowing fleet managers to combine CAN parameters with Plaspy dashboards and alerts for operational visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Streams GPS position and CAN derived telemetry for integrated fleet tracking with Plaspy.
- Built in CAN processor supports extensive vehicle parameters for engine, battery and EV insights.
- Cellular connectivity options designed for reliable remote data delivery across regions.
- EV ready parameter packages to capture electric vehicle specific metrics alongside traditional data.
- Works with Bluetooth sensors and Teltonika accessories to extend asset and environmental telemetry.
- Scalable ordering and regional module variants to suit mixed fleet deployments and logistics operations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the FMC150 supplies continuous position updates and CAN telemetry that Plaspy ingests, normalizes, and presents through maps, reports and alerts. Plaspy uses that data to populate dashboards, maintain historical routes, trigger maintenance reminders, and generate event notifications based on vehicle state.

- Real time location feeds into Plaspy maps and live monitoring dashboards.
- CAN derived parameters are available in Plaspy for diagnostics driven alerts and maintenance scheduling.
- EV telemetry can be surfaced for battery and charging event visibility when configured with EV packages.
- Bluetooth beacons and sensors report proximity and environmental data to Plaspy for asset level monitoring.
- Historical data and reports support route analysis, fuel monitoring, and operational performance reviews.

- Preventive maintenance programs using CAN indicators to schedule service and reduce downtime.
