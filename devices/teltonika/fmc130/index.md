# Teltonika - FMC130

![FMC130](./tracker.png)

The FMC130 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for modern fleet management. Designed for reliable, field-ready operation, the FMC130 combines 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity \(with automatic fallback to 2G networks\) and an internal backup battery to keep vehicles and assets visible even during temporary power loss. Its telemetry-focused design and accessory ecosystem make it a strong choice for operators who need accurate location, fuel monitoring, and remote control capabilities through the Plaspy platform.

The FMC130 integrates flexible wired I/O, a dedicated impulse input for precise fuel flow meter reading, CAN adapter compatibility for vehicle bus data, and Bluetooth® Low Energy support for external sensors and beacons. Paired with Plaspy, this tracker enables real-time tracking, fuel and temperature telemetry, anti-theft immobilizer actions, and centralized firmware and configuration management for fleets of any size.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity and fallback to 2G for broad regional coverage.
- Accurate fuel monitoring via an impulse input for direct fuel flow meter reading — ideal for fuel-conscious fleets.
- CAN adapter integration to read vehicle parameters such as fuel level, odometer, RPM, and engine temperature.
- Bluetooth LE support for external sensors and beacons to monitor temperature, humidity, magnet status, and movement.
- Remote engine blocking and immobilisation features for rapid anti-theft response and secure fleet control.
- Internal backup battery to maintain tracking and alerts during power interruptions.
- Remote firmware and configuration management using Teltonika FOTA WEB for streamlined fleet updates.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed in a vehicle or asset, the FMC130 streams position and telemetry data to Plaspy through its cellular link. Plaspy collects and normalizes the incoming data so fleet managers can monitor real-time tracking, generate reports, and configure automated alerts. The combination of the FMC130 hardware and Plaspy software delivers a complete telematics flow: on-device sensing → secure cellular transmission → Plaspy processing → dashboard, alerting and analytics.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and historical playback.
- Vehicle signals and digital I/O \(including ignition and other wired inputs\) reported to Plaspy for event-driven alerts and filtering.
- Fuel monitoring via the impulse input—Plaspy receives pulse-based fuel flow data to support fuel consumption and theft analysis.
- Remote immobilizer/engine blocking control available from Plaspy to support anti-theft responses and off-hours vehicle lockdowns.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons report temperature, humidity, and movement data to Plaspy for cold-chain and cargo-condition monitoring.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch — real-time tracking and CAN-sourced telemetry help optimize routing and maintenance scheduling.
- Fuel monitoring and loss prevention — impulse input enables precise fuel flow meter readings for fuel usage analysis and fuel-theft detection.
- Anti-theft and immobilisation — remote engine blocking and immobiliser control through Plaspy provide a fast response to unauthorized movement.
- Cold-chain and refrigerated cargo monitoring — Bluetooth sensors for temperature and humidity ensure compliance and product integrity during transit.
- Special machinery and heavy vehicles — CAN adapter integration allows telemetry collection from trucks, buses, and specialty equipment.

The FMC130 delivers the reliability and telemetry detail fleets require, while remaining Plaspy compatible for seamless integration. Its 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular link with 2G fallback keeps units connected across mixed coverage areas, and the internal backup battery helps maintain visibility during power interruptions. Finally, Teltonika FOTA WEB support simplifies firmware and configuration rollouts across a fleet, reducing downtime and administrative overhead. Together with Plaspy, this tracker is a practical, scalable option for fleets seeking dependable GPS tracking, comprehensive telemetry, and centralized control.
