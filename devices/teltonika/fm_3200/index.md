# Teltonika - FM 3200

![FM 3200](./logo.jpg)

The Teltonika FM3200 is a versatile GPS tracker with GSM connectivity, designed to accurately determine the coordinates of remote objects and transmit them via the GSM network. This device is ideal for applications that require real-time location tracking of assets or vehicles. The FM3200 is equipped with additional inputs and outputs, allowing you to control and monitor other devices remotely. It also features a USB port for NMEA output and configuration, providing flexibility and ease of use.

With its Teltonika TM1 dual band module and GPRS class 10 capabilities, the FM3200 ensures reliable and efficient communication. It supports SMS for text and data transmission, making it easy to receive important updates and notifications. The GPS features of the FM3200 include a 50-channel receiver and compatibility with various NMEA protocols, such as GGA, GGL, GSA, GSV, RMC, and WGS-84. The device offers exceptional sensitivity, with earlier versions boasting -160 dBm sensitivity and newer versions featuring a Skytraq \(Venus634LPx chipset\) with -161 dBm sensitivity.

The FM3200 is equipped with a powerful ARM7 TDMI processor and 1 MB internal Flash memory, ensuring smooth operation and efficient data storage. It also includes a built-in movement sensor and an internal backup battery for added reliability. The interface features of the FM3200 include a wide power supply range of 10 to 30V, a USB port for easy connectivity, 2 digital inputs, 2 open collector outputs, and 3 status LEDs for quick visual feedback.

One of the standout features of the FM3200 is its highly configurable data acquisition and sending capabilities. It allows you to set up triggers based on various elements such as external sensors, inputs, speed, temperature, and more. The device supports multiple Geofence areas, enabling you to define virtual boundaries and receive alerts when the tracked object enters or exits these areas. The FM3200 also offers sleep mode functionality, real-time process monitoring, an authorized number list for remote access, and firmware and configuration updates over GPRS, SMS, or USB port.

With its TCP/IP or UDP/IP protocol support, the FM3200 ensures seamless integration with existing systems and platforms. It can store up to 15,000 records, providing extensive historical data for analysis and reporting. The FM3200 is CE and E-mark certified, meeting the highest standards of quality and reliability. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or any other remote objects, the Teltonika FM3200 is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that delivers accurate and real-time location information.
