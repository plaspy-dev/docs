# Teltonika - FMC225

<div className="device-logo">
![FMC225](./tracker.png)
</div>

The FMC225 is a rugged GPS tracker from Teltonika designed for professional fleet management in demanding environments. Built for external mounting and heavy duty use, it offers cellular connectivity with fallback options and Dual SIM capability to keep vehicles connected for real time position reporting and telemetry. The device is marketed for applications across agriculture, construction, mining and other industries that require durable hardware and continuous asset visibility.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMC225 integrates with Plaspy to bring location, telemetry and event data into fleet dashboards and reporting workflows. Its IP67 rated enclosure, serial telemetry capability and impulse fuel inputs make it a practical choice for fleets that need reliable tracking, fuel monitoring and anti theft controls while maintaining remote device management during large scale deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivering real time tracking and fleet telemetry for professional operations.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure suitable for external mounting and protection in dusty or wet environments.
- Cellular connectivity with fallback and Dual SIM support to maximise uptime and maintain connectivity across regions.
- Serial interfaces and impulse fuel input for extended telemetry and accurate fuel monitoring integration.
- Digital and analog inputs and outputs to support peripheral control and event reporting for anti theft workflows.
- Regional variants and kit options to meet local deployment needs and frequency requirements.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the FMC225 sends GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry over cellular networks so operators can view live location, events and sensor data within Plaspy dashboards. Plaspy ingests serial telemetry, impulse fuel readings and digital I O events to power alerts, reports and automated workflows for operational oversight.

- Real time location updates and route playback for live monitoring and historical review.
- Fuel monitoring via impulse input and serial connected meters to feed consumption and cost reports in Plaspy.
- Serial telemetry integration for external sensors and third party adapters to extend diagnostics visible in Plaspy.
- Digital and analog inputs and outputs enable reporting of door, alarm and ignition events and support remote peripheral control.
- Coordination of remote device management and firmware updates alongside manufacturer tools to simplify rollouts.
