# Teltonika - FMC225

![FMC225](./tracker.png)

The FMC225 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for professional fleet management in the toughest conditions. Built for external mounting and heavy-duty use, the FMC225 combines 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback and Dual SIM support to keep vehicles online for reliable, real-time tracking and telemetry across agriculture, construction, mining and other demanding industries.

With an IP67-rated enclosure, serial interfaces for extended telemetry and impulse fuel inputs for accurate fuel monitoring, the FMC225 integrates smoothly with Plaspy to deliver continuous location, diagnostics and event data. It is a practical choice for fleets that need robust anti-theft measures, remote device management and deeper sensor integration without sacrificing field-proven durability.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering real-time tracking and telemetry for professional fleet management.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure for dust protection and water resistance—suitable for external mounting in harsh environments.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback and Dual SIM support to maximise uptime and minimise roaming costs.
- Advanced serial interfaces \(RS485, RS232\) plus impulse input for precise fuel monitoring and third‑party sensor integration.
- Digital and analog inputs/outputs for vehicle peripherals, remote control and anti‑theft workflows such as immobilizer activation where configured.
- Regional variants and kit options \(EU/MEA, APAC, LATAM\) to meet local frequency requirements and deployment needs.
- Manufacturer support resources and remote firmware/management tools to simplify large-scale rollouts and maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the FMC225 transmits GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry over LTE/2G to provide timely location, usage and sensor data in Plaspy’s dashboards and APIs. Plaspy ingests serial telemetry, impulse fuel data and digital I/O events to generate alerts, reports and automated workflows for fleet operators.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates for live monitoring and route playback.
- Fuel monitoring via impulse input and serial-connected fuel flow meters to feed fuel usage and consumption reports to Plaspy.
- RS232/RS485 serial telemetry integration for external sensors, CAN adapters and third‑party meters that supply extended diagnostics to Plaspy.
- Digital/analog inputs and outputs enable door, alarm and ignition event reporting and remote peripheral control—supporting anti‑theft scenarios and immobilizer workflows when configured.
- Remote device management and firmware handling through manufacturer tools—Plaspy can coordinate deployments and updates alongside those resources.
- Plaspy also supports additional integrations such as Bluetooth sensors in its ecosystem, enabling mixed-sensor deployments even when vehicle trackers do not include BLE natively.
