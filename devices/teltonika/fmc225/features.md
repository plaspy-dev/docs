# Teltonika - FMC225 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Teltonika FMC225 with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities, typical operational uses, and how the FMC225 reports location and telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and workflows while remaining non-sensitive and suitable for public documentation.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. Where applicable this page makes clear the general capabilities of the FMC225, but users should verify device specific details against manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The FMC225 is a rugged external mounting GPS tracker designed for professional fleet and asset tracking where durability and continuous connectivity are priorities. It combines cellular redundancy, field-grade ingress protection, and physical I/O and serial interfaces to collect location and telemetry that Plaspy can present for operational visibility.

- Durable IP67 rated enclosure for dust resistance and temporary water immersion, suitable for external mounting in harsh work sites.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback and Dual SIM support to maximise uptime and reduce roaming interruptions.
- Serial interfaces (RS232 and RS485) and impulse fuel input for integrating third party sensors and fuel meters.
- Digital and analog inputs and outputs to monitor vehicle peripherals and support anti theft and immobilizer workflows when configured.
- Designed for telemetry rich deployments in agriculture, construction, mining and other demanding industries.
- Regional variants and kit options to match local frequency requirements and installation preferences.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FMC225

- 4G LTE Cat 1 mobile connectivity with fallback to 2G for broader network reach.
- Dual SIM support for redundancy and roaming optimisation across regions.
- IP67 rugged enclosure suited to external mounting and harsh environmental conditions.
- RS232 and RS485 serial ports for third party sensor and telemetry integration.
- Impulse fuel input for pulse-based fuel flow meters and precise fuel event collection.
- Digital and analog inputs and outputs for door, ignition and peripheral monitoring and control.
- Manufacturer remote management and firmware update support for fleet scale maintenance.
- Regional hardware variants available to meet local cellular band requirements.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the FMC225 is deployed with Plaspy, its connectivity and telemetry streams enable continuous visibility and event reporting. Plaspy ingests GNSS positions, serial telemetry, impulse fuel events and digital I/O signals to power maps, alerts and analytics for fleet operators.

- Real-time location updates and route history visible in Plaspy dashboards for live monitoring and playback.
- Fuel pulse and serial fuel meter data fed into Plaspy to support fuel usage reports and consumption analysis.
- Serial telemetry from RS232 and RS485 interfaces presented in Plaspy as extended diagnostics or third party sensor readings.
- Digital input events and output status reflected in Plaspy to support door, alarm and immobilizer workflows when configured on the vehicle.
- Device connectivity and status information available in Plaspy to help operators spot network or power issues and manage device health.

## Typical Use Cases

- Asset protection and anti theft operations using digital outputs and event reporting to halt or immobilize vehicles where workflows are implemented.
- Tracking heavy equipment and machinery in agriculture, construction and mining with a device built for harsh external mounting.
- Accurate fuel monitoring for cost control and billing using impulse inputs and serial-connected fuel meters.
- Telematics rich fleet management where serial interfaces and CAN adapter integrations provide extended diagnostics for maintenance planning.
- Regional fleet deployments that leverage Dual SIM and variant options to maintain consistent coverage across borders.
