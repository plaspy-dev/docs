# Teltonika - FMC125

<div className="device-logo">
![FMC125](./tracker.png)
</div>

The FMC125 is a professional grade GPS tracker built for fleet management and vehicle telemetry. Designed for logistics, trucking and service fleets, the device provides cellular connectivity with fallback, dual SIM resilience, and rugged serial interfaces to collect GNSS position and external telemetry. The FMC125 supports impulse inputs for fuel flow meters and identification inputs such as RFID and 1‑wire sensors, enabling precise tracking and fuel monitoring in demanding operations.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the FMC125 can stream position and peripheral data into Plaspy to power live maps, alerts and reports. Its serial data capabilities and support for paired camera units make it useful for operators who need richer telemetry, fuel analytics and correlated video evidence alongside real time tracking in Plaspy dashboards.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible professional tracker for reliable fleet location and telemetry.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback and Dual SIM for improved uptime.
- RS232 and RS485 serial interfaces for integrating external telemetry and peripherals.
- Impulse input and support for RFID and 1‑wire sensors enable accurate fuel monitoring and driver identification.
- Support for Teltonika DualCam and Dashcam integration to correlate video with events.
- Regional module options and bulk packaging for scalable professional deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the FMC125 transmits GNSS position and serial telemetry to Plaspy where data is ingested, parsed and presented in maps, timelines and reports. Plaspy converts pulse counts, sensor events and serial streams into actionable information for fleet operators, enabling monitoring, alerting and historical analysis.

- Real time location updates and historical traces displayed on Plaspy maps and timelines.
- Fuel monitoring based on impulse input pulse counts, reported to Plaspy for consumption analytics and anomaly detection.
- Driver and asset identification events from RFID and 1‑wire sensors used for driver based reporting and security workflows.
- Serial integration for external telemetry and camera event correlation to enrich incident records.
- Connectivity resilience from Dual SIM operation helps maintain continuous data flow and supports Plaspy alerting when links are interrupted.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet telematics and dispatch with live tracking, route oversight and performance reporting.
- Fuel consumption monitoring and theft detection using impulse inputs and analytics in Plaspy.
- Driver identification, logging and compliance using RFID and 1‑wire sensor events.
- Video event capture and evidence collection by pairing with supported camera units and correlating footage in Plaspy.
- Asset protection and recovery workflows leveraging continuous connectivity and Plaspy alarms.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FMC125 is a practical option for organizations that require a rugged, integration ready tracker capable of handling serial telemetry and sensor inputs. Its resilience features and multiple regional variants make it suitable for mixed fleets operating across different areas, while the available interfaces support workflows for fuel analytics, driver identification and camera integration. Paired with Plaspy, the FMC125 helps convert vehicle level data into dashboards, alerts and reports that improve operational oversight.
