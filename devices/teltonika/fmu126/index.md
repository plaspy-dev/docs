# Teltonika - FMU126

![FMU126](./Logo.png)

The Teltonika FMU126 is a compact vehicle tracker built to deliver real time positioning and extended telematics functionality. It combines GNSS positioning and Bluetooth capabilities with cellular connectivity that supports 3G coverage and fallback to 2G. The device includes internal GNSS and cellular antennas, support for external peripherals via an RS232 interface, and features designed to address transport authority requirements such as those from the Department of Land Transport.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMU126 can feed location, event, and sensor data into Plaspy for fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Its support for external readers and BLE sensors, onboard buzzer, and expanded reporting options make it a versatile option for telematics deployments that require integration of third party peripherals and continuous record collection.

## Key Highlights

- Real time GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity for broad coverage and fallback options
- Built in GNSS and cellular antennas plus Bluetooth for local sensor and beacon support
- RS232 interface for integration with magnetic card readers and other external devices
- Long operational runtime allowing frequent data collection and continuous record capture
- Support for CAN adapters and fuel monitoring to broaden vehicle data capture options
- Onboard buzzer and +5V output to simplify certain integration scenarios and reduce installation overhead

## How It Works with Plaspy

The FMU126 transmits positioning and event information that Plaspy ingests to provide visibility across vehicles and assets. Plaspy can display location history, generate alerts, and aggregate sensor inputs from the tracker to support operational decision making.

- Real time location tracking and route history displayed in Plaspy for live monitoring and playback
- Event and status alerts such as ignition changes and configured scenarios routed into Plaspy notifications
- Integration of BLE sensors and beacons for proximity or asset presence monitoring inside the Plaspy platform
- Peripheral data from RS232 connected devices available for use in Plaspy dashboards and reports
- Aggregated reporting on fuel and vehicle events to support operational analysis and compliance
- Remote configuration and command workflows supported where the device capabilities allow

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and vehicle tracking for small to large commercial fleets
- Public transport and regulated vehicle fleets meeting Department of Land Transport requirements
- Driver or passenger card systems and fare collection integrations using magnetic reader support
- Asset and cargo monitoring with BLE beacons and extended sensor support
- Fuel monitoring and vehicle diagnostics aggregation for operational efficiency

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FMU126 offers a balance of positioning, peripheral integration, and sensor flexibility that suits many fleet and transport use cases. Its combination of GNSS, Bluetooth, RS232 connectivity, and extended data capture options makes it a practical choice when you need both location visibility and the ability to incorporate third party devices into your telematics workflow.

When paired with Plaspy, the FMU126 becomes part of a platform that turns raw device data into actionable fleet insights. Plaspy provides the visualization, alerting, and reporting layers that help teams turn FMU126 data into operational improvements without requiring extensive custom development.
