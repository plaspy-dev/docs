# Teltonika - FMB020

The Teltonika FMB020 is an ultra-small, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for ultra-fast, plug-and-play installation in passenger cars and light commercial fleets. Plug directly into the vehicle OBD-II port to enable immediate GPS tracking and vehicle parameter reading, then manage units and updates through Teltonika’s remote management tools and Plaspy integrations. Its compact 29.1 mm width and simple installation make the FMB020 ideal for large deployments where minimal downtime and quick rollout are priorities.

Designed for fleet management, logistics, car sharing and rental operations, the FMB020 adds Bluetooth Low Energy \(BLE\) expansion for sensors and beacons so teams can combine real-time tracking with contextual telemetry such as temperature, humidity, magnet detection and movement monitoring. With built-in eco driving scenarios, the device supports fuel monitoring and driver behaviour analysis to reduce operating costs and improve safety when used with Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: plug-and-play OBD-II integration for immediate real-time tracking and telemetry in Plaspy portals.
- Ultra-compact OBD-II form factor \(29.1 mm width\) for discreet installation and quick fleet rollout.
- Global 2G connectivity on B2/B3/B5/B8 for broad network coverage in supported regions.
- BLE support for external sensors and beacons \(temperature, humidity, magnet, movement\) to enable anti-theft and environmental monitoring.
- Eco driving scenario to support fuel monitoring and driver behaviour insights that help optimize fuel consumption and vehicle life.
- Backed by Teltonika remote management \(FOTA WEB, Teltonika Configurator\) and product documentation for efficient updates and provisioning.
- Available in standard and custom packages with accessory options for flexible deployment needs.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to a vehicle’s OBD-II port, the FMB020 streams GPS position and OBD-derived telemetry to Plaspy in real time. Plaspy receives location, OBD parameter readings and BLE sensor data to power live maps, geofences, alerts and reports. The combination of OBD telemetry and BLE sensor inputs lets operations teams create richer rules and automated workflows inside Plaspy for fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and environmental tracking.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking, route replay and history.
- OBD-II vehicle parameter reading, enabling ignition state and engine data to be reported to Plaspy for telemetry and event rules.
- Fuel monitoring and eco driving inputs: data used for driver scoring, fuel efficiency reports and behaviour alerts when analyzed in Plaspy.
- BLE sensors and beacons \(paired via BLE\) supply temperature, humidity, magnet and movement events to Plaspy for anti-theft and asset-awareness scenarios.
- Remote configuration and firmware updates \(FOTA WEB / Teltonika Configurator\) ensure devices remain compliant and up-to-date within Plaspy-managed fleets.
