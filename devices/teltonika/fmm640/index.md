# Teltonika - FMM640

![FMM640](./Logo.png)

The Teltonika FMM640 is a professional GPS tracker designed for wide ranging fleet and asset monitoring. It offers global cellular connectivity using modern low power wide area technologies and supports a broad set of tracking, detection, and peripheral options. The device is positioned for use across logistics, refrigerated transport, agriculture, construction, mining, and security operations, with built in movement detection, accelerometer driven event detection, geofencing, trip and odometer functions, fuel monitoring options, and support for external peripherals suited to specialized applications.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the FMM640 can feed location, movement, and event data into Plaspy for consolidated fleet visibility and operational oversight. Its feature set—such as geofencing, trip detection, offline tracking, and temperature or other peripheral monitoring—maps well to common Plaspy workflows for alerts, reporting, and route analysis. That alignment makes the FMM640 a practical choice for organizations looking to combine a capable Teltonika tracker with Plaspy fleet management tools.

## Key Highlights

- Global connectivity using LTE CAT M1 and NB IoT for wide area coverage and efficient data use.  
- Broad applicability for industries including logistics, refrigerated transport, agriculture, construction, mining, and emergency services.  
- Movement detection and accelerometer based event monitoring for behaviors like towing, crash alerts, and excessive idling.  
- Geofencing options, trip detection, and odometer tracking to support route management and location based alerts.  
- Fuel monitoring and support for multiple fuel level sensor types for fuel oversight and reporting.  
- Support for a wide range of peripherals and sensors including temperature monitoring, RFID, and other external devices for specialized use cases.  
- Power saving modes and remote configuration capabilities via over the air updates and remote commands.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the FMM640 supplies location and event data that Plaspy can process for live monitoring, historical reporting, and operational alerts. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to present a unified view of vehicle status, movement history, and configured events so fleets can act on timely information.

- Live location tracking and map based visibility within Plaspy dashboards.  
- Event and alarm forwarding for geofence breaches, speeding or other configured behaviors.  
- Trip and odometer data aggregated into reports for utilization and route review.  
- Sensor and peripheral data surfaced alongside location for temperature, immobilizer status, and other monitored inputs.  
- Offline tracking support and data continuity handling so historical position information is preserved and uploaded when connectivity resumes.  

## Typical Use Cases

- International and regional logistics fleets requiring reliable position updates and event monitoring.  
- Refrigerated transport where temperature and peripheral sensor readings are monitored alongside location.  
- Agriculture and construction equipment tracking for asset utilization and movement oversight.  
- Mining and offroad operations where robust detection and offline tracking are valuable.  
- Security, emergency, and service fleets that need geofence alerts, rapid location visibility, and event notifications.
