# Teltonika - FMC001

<div className="device-logo">
![FMC001](./Logo.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMC001 is a vehicle GPS tracker that combines OBD II connectivity with GNSS positioning and Bluetooth support to deliver a compact telematics solution. Designed for vehicle applications, the FMC001 reads OBD II parameters, provides detailed accelerometer information for driving behavior insights, and can work with Bluetooth Low Energy sensors and beacons to extend monitoring capabilities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMC001 is relevant for fleets and vehicle programs that need integrated location, diagnostics, and driver event visibility. Plaspy can ingest the FMC001 data to provide centralized tracking, alerts, and reporting, making it easier to turn the device telemetry into operational insight without needing specialized custom integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Combines OBD II data access with GNSS positioning for vehicle centric tracking.
- Detailed accelerometer output supports detection of driving events and behavior patterns.
- Bluetooth Low Energy sensor support enables expanded monitoring options.
- Multiple sleep modes allow configurable power management for varied deployment needs.
- Remote configuration and firmware update methods simplify device lifecycle management.
- Useful for fuel monitoring and ignition detection when paired with supported data sources.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and processes the FMC001 telemetry to provide real time and historical visibility into vehicle location, driving events, and selected vehicle parameters. The platform turns raw device data into maps, alerts, and reports that operational teams can use to monitor fleets and improve efficiency.

- Real time location and trip history for centralized fleet oversight
- Event detection and driver behavior events based on accelerometer readings
- Vehicle parameter reporting such as OBD II derived metrics and fuel related indicators
- Integration of Bluetooth sensor and beacon inputs into Plaspy dashboards and alerts
- Use of device sleep mode behavior to balance reporting frequency and power use
- Device lifecycle support through remote configuration and update workflows compatible with the FMC001

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light commercial vehicles and small truck fleets
- Driver log books and behavior monitoring for safety programs
- Car rental and leasing fleets requiring vehicle usage insight
- Usage based insurance and telematics programs needing driving event data
- Individual vehicle tracking for businesses with mixed vehicle types
