# Teltonika - FMC001

![FMC001](./Logo.png)

The Teltonika FMC001 is a cutting-edge GPS tracker that combines LTE OBD technology with GNSS and Bluetooth connectivity. This device is the first of its kind, offering effortless installation and the ability to read OBD II parameters, making it a perfect solution for a wide range of applications. Whether you need fleet management for light commercial vehicles, driver log-books, car rental and leasing, insurance telematics \(UBI\), or any other tracking needs, the FMC001 has you covered.

One of the standout features of the FMC001 is its detailed accelerometer data, which provides valuable insights into driving behavior and vehicle performance. This allows for the detection of scenarios such as green driving, over speeding, excessive idling, unplugging, towing, and even crash detection. Additionally, the device supports various Bluetooth Low Energy sensors and beacons, allowing for even more customization and functionality.

In terms of sleep modes, the FMC001 offers GPS Sleep, Online Deep Sleep, Deep Sleep, and Ultra Deep Sleep options, ensuring optimal power management and extended battery life. The device also supports configuration and firmware updates via FOTA Web, FOTA, Teltonika Configurator \(USB, Bluetooth\), and the FMBT mobile application.

With its OBD II fuel monitoring capabilities and ignition detection through the accelerometer, external power voltage, and engine RPM, the FMC001 provides comprehensive tracking and monitoring of your vehicles. It also offers time synchronization options through GPS, NITZ, and NTP, ensuring accurate and reliable data.

Overall, the Teltonika FMC001 is a versatile and feature-rich GPS tracker that delivers exceptional performance and functionality. Whether you're managing a fleet of vehicles or need to track individual cars, this device is a reliable and efficient solution.
