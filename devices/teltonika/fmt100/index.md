# Teltonika - FMT100

![FMT100](./tracker.png)

The FMT100 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for fast, reliable battery-mounted installation in vehicles and mobile assets. Built with an IP65-rated, water-resistant casing and a rugged design, the FMT100 is optimized for fleet rollouts, seasonal vehicle use, and rapid deployments where simple installation and dependable location and event reporting are priorities.

The device pairs Bluetooth® LE sensor support and a built-in 3‑axis accelerometer with quad-band 2G \(GSM\) connectivity, delivering crash trace details, event-driven alerts, and BLE-based environmental monitoring. Teltonika offers multiple bundle and order-code options for different installation kits; note that the product page includes End of Life \(EOL\) information, so check Teltonika’s support pages for current availability or recommended replacements.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates into Plaspy for centralized real-time tracking, alerts and reporting across mixed fleets.
- Rapid battery-mounted installation: designed to mount directly to a vehicle battery with optional clamp cable bundles for five-minute installs.
- Rugged and water-resistant: IP65-rated enclosure protects electronics for reliable field operation in varied weather.
- Crash-aware telemetry: built-in 3-axis accelerometer with configurable crash detection parameters for event capture and analysis.
- Bluetooth LE for sensors: pairs with low-energy beacons and external sensors \(temperature, humidity, movement, magnet\) to extend telemetry and asset-awareness.
- Low-cost 2G connectivity: quad-band GSM \(B2/B3/B5/B8\) provides broad legacy network support for basic tracking and event reporting.
- Bundle flexibility: available in multiple Teltonika order-code bundles with accessory options such as power cables, clamps, and micro-USB where applicable.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FMT100 feeds location updates, accelerometer events, and Bluetooth sensor data into Plaspy’s real-time tracking dashboard and reporting tools. Plaspy can consume the device’s event-driven messages to trigger alerts, generate crash traces, and feed telemetry into fleet-management workflows. Installers can deploy the FMT100 quickly on battery terminals and register the device in Plaspy to begin live monitoring and historical reporting.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: position and movement data are relayed to Plaspy for live tracking and historical route playback.
- Crash detection and trace reporting: accelerometer-driven events are transmitted to Plaspy for incident review and automated alerting.
- Bluetooth LE sensor integration: external temperature, humidity, movement, or magnet sensors can be paired and their readings forwarded to Plaspy for asset- or environment-aware monitoring.
- Event-driven workflows: Plaspy can use the device’s event messages to trigger notifications, geofence alerts, or operator tasks.
- Remote control and I/O-dependent features: features such as remote immobilizer or ignition monitoring depend on the vehicle harness and any I/O provided in specific bundle configurations—verify support before deployment.

## Use Cases

- Fleet telematics: fast, low-cost deployment across rental, logistics, and seasonal fleets that need reliable location and event reporting.
- Anti-theft and recovery: battery-mounted installation and location reporting support basic anti-theft workflows and recovery efforts; immobilizer control depends on vehicle wiring and bundle.
- Crash detection and incident analysis: accelerometer-based crash traces and configurable thresholds assist in post-incident review and automated alerting in Plaspy.
- Asset and environment monitoring: pair BLE temperature, humidity, movement, or magnet sensors to track conditions for sensitive cargo or auxiliary assets.
- Rapid deployment programs: compact footprint and simple mounting make the FMT100 suited to large rollouts or short-term seasonal use where speed and repeatability matter.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FMT100 offers a pragmatic balance of simplicity, ruggedness, and sensor flexibility that fits many fleet and asset-tracking projects. Its battery-mounted design and fast installation lower deployment labor and accelerate time-to-value for Plaspy-integrated tracking solutions. Bluetooth LE sensor support and accelerometer-driven crash detection provide richer telemetry than a basic tracker while keeping hardware costs modest. For fleets that need dependable location reporting, configurable event detection, and BLE-enabled environmental sensing, the FMT100 is a practical option—however, confirm current availability and EOL status with Teltonika, and validate any required I/O \(ignition, immobilizer control\) against your integration needs before purchase.
