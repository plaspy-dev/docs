# Teltonika - FMC640

![FMC640](./Logo.png)

The Teltonika FMC640 is a professional-grade GPS tracker designed to maximize fleet efficiency and provide comprehensive tracking capabilities. With 4G \(LTE Cat 1\) network coverage and fallback compatibility to 3G \(UMTS\) and 2G \(GSM\), this device ensures reliable and continuous connectivity. Equipped with GNSS/LTE and Bluetooth modules, the FMC640 offers accurate positioning and versatile communication options.

This GPS tracker is suitable for a wide range of applications, including international logistics, refrigerated transport, agriculture, construction & mining, security & emergency services, and more. It is packed with features that enhance fleet management, such as FMS CAN data \(J1939\), fuel CAN data \(J1708\), tachograph live data \(K-Line\), remote tachograph file download, and support for various third-party RS232 or RS485 devices. The FMC640 also supports Dual-SIM functionality, providing redundancy and ensuring uninterrupted connectivity.

Some outstanding features of the Teltonika FMC640 include:

- Movement detection and accelerometer for accurate tracking and monitoring
- Scenarios like Green/Eco Driving, Over Speeding detection, Jamming detection, and more
- Functionalities such as Auto Geofencing, Manual Geofencing, Trip detection, and Odometer
- Support for various peripherals including Garmin, RFID, iButton, temperature sensors, and more
- Sleep modes like GPS Sleep, Online Deep Sleep, and Deep Sleep for power optimization
- Configuration and firmware update options via FOTA Web and Teltonika Configurator
- SMS and GPRS commands for configuration, event notifications, and control
- Time synchronization options using GPS, NITZ, and NTP
- Fuel monitoring capabilities with support for analog and digital fuel level sensors
- Ignition detection using digital input, accelerometer, and external power voltage

The Teltonika FMC640 is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that offers advanced tracking and monitoring capabilities for fleet management. With its robust design and extensive functionality, it is an ideal choice for businesses in various industries.
