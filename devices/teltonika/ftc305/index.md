# Teltonika - FTC305

<div className="device-logo">
![FTC305](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FTC305 is a compact, vehicle grade GPS tracker built for e mobility and fleet telematics. It combines GNSS position reporting with vehicle telemetry capability, durable IP67 protection, and a wide input power range to suit electric transport platforms such as e bikes, forklifts, shuttle cars and utility machinery. The device is offered in sealed and no casing variants and supports external antenna and backup battery options for more resilient deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the FTC305 can feed location and vehicle telemetry into Plaspy for live mapping, alerts and reporting. This makes it a practical choice when you need steady positional visibility and richer vehicle data in a fleet management platform, helping operations teams use Plaspy to monitor assets, trigger responses and analyze usage patterns.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for e mobility and fleet telematics with compact vehicle grade design.
- Plaspy compatibility for direct ingestion of GNSS position and vehicle telemetry.
- Rugged IP67 casing with an available no casing factory variant for embedded installations.
- Wide input power support to suit a range of electric transport platforms.
- CAN bus telemetry support to surface vehicle derived metrics alongside location.
- External antenna and optional external backup battery choices to improve resilience.
- Variant radio profiles including 4G LTE Cat 1 with 2G fallback for broader coverage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FTC305 streams positional fixes and vehicle telemetry so fleet managers gain visibility across maps, alerts and historical data. Plaspy ingests the device data to power monitoring, geofencing and analytical workflows that support operational decisions and anti theft response.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps and support route monitoring and geofence alerts.
- CAN bus telemetry items can be displayed on Plaspy dashboards to support vehicle state monitoring and operational KPIs.
- Backup battery and external antenna options help maintain connectivity and allow Plaspy to continue receiving position traces during short power interruptions.
- Historical tracking and reporting in Plaspy enable review of routes, usage patterns and event timelines for analytics.
- Packaging and variant options simplify integration into both standard fleet vehicles and embedded e mobility platforms.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for electric vehicles and light industrial fleets seeking combined location and telemetry.
- Anti theft monitoring and rapid response workflows using real time position reporting and backup power traces.
- Material handling and warehouse vehicle tracking such as forklifts and shuttle carts to measure utilization and routes.
- Discreet embedded installations inside vehicle frames using no casing variants and external antenna options.
- Telemetry driven analytics combining position and vehicle signals to inform maintenance and operational planning.

The FTC305 is well suited where compact form factor, durable protection and vehicle level telemetry are priorities. Its support for CAN bus data and flexible power and antenna options make it a practical choice for electric transport fleets that need more than basic location tracking. When paired with Plaspy, the FTC305 can contribute both live operational oversight and richer datasets for dashboarding and historical analysis.
