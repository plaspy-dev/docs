# Teltonika - FTC305 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Teltonika FTC305 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on practical device capabilities, how those capabilities appear in Plaspy workflows, and the typical operational value the FTC305 can provide for e mobility and fleet telematics deployments.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, regional radio variant, and manufacturer implementation. Always confirm critical device details for your chosen SKU and firmware level with the manufacturer documentation before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The FTC305 is a compact 4G LTE Cat 1 tracker designed for electric transport and fleet telematics. It is built to provide reliable GNSS positioning, cellular connectivity and vehicle-grade telemetry for use cases where e mobility and industrial vehicles require durable hardware and integrated CAN-bus data.

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for integration of GNSS location and CAN-bus telemetry into fleet workflows.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity with 2G fallback for broad regional coverage and legacy network support.
- Wide power input range to support high voltage e mobility platforms and industrial vehicles.
- IP67 rugged casing or a factory no-casing variant for embedded installations and flexible mounting.
- Support for external GNSS and cellular antennas to improve signal reception in challenging installations.

## Core Features of Teltonika - FTC305

- Real-time GNSS position reporting suitable for live tracking and historical route analysis.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 modem with 2G fallback to maintain connectivity across networks where required.
- Wide input voltage support for high voltage e mobility platforms and utility vehicles.
- CAN-bus reading capability to surface ECU-derived telemetry when available on the vehicle.
- IP67-rated enclosure for sealed outdoor use and a no-casing variant for embedded installations.
- External antenna support for both GNSS and cellular antennas to improve fix reliability.
- Optional external backup battery available through specific factory packages for short term resilience.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FTC305 submits positional fixes and vehicle telemetry that Plaspy ingests for mapping, monitoring and reporting. Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols and presents location and CAN-derived data for operational use.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps for route monitoring and dispatch decisions.
- CAN-bus telemetry can be reflected in Plaspy dashboards to support vehicle state monitoring and derived metrics when those signals are exposed.
- External antenna support helps reduce loss of fixes and improves continuity of data presented in Plaspy.
- Backup battery operation enables Plaspy to continue receiving position traces or raise alerts during mains power loss.
- Device variants and packaging choices simplify integration into either visible installations or embedded use cases, which Plaspy can represent in device inventories.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for electric vehicles where location and vehicle telemetry support operations and maintenance planning.
- Anti-theft detection and response workflows using real-time tracking combined with backup-battery resilience.
- Material handling and industrial e mobility tracking for forklifts, utility carts and shuttle vehicles.
- Embedded installations where no-casing variants and external antennas enable discreet or frame-mounted deployments.
- Telemetry-driven analytics combining CAN-bus signals and positional history to inform KPIs for electric fleets.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets may differ between product SKUs and factory package options such as casing or backup battery inclusion.
