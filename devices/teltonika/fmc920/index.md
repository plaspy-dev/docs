# Teltonika - FMC920

![FMC920](./tracker.png)

The Teltonika FMC920 is a compact 4G LTE Cat 1 GPS tracker engineered for reliable vehicle tracking and fleet control. Built for tight installation spaces, the FMC920 delivers real-time tracking and essential telemetry in a slim 12 mm housing, and it is Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your Plaspy real-time tracking and fleet management workflows. Bluetooth® LE support extends the tracker’s usefulness beyond location to environmental and asset-condition sensing with wireless beacons and sensors.

Designed as a best-selling small tracker with a backup battery and remote engine blocking capability, the FMC920 is suited to mixed fleets and discreet anti-theft installations. Note: the product page indicates an End of Life \(EOL\) status for some variants — confirm current availability, support, firmware and certificates with Teltonika sales or product support before procurement.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrate FMC920 into Plaspy for consolidated real-time tracking, alerts and reports.
- Compact, concealed installation: slim 12 mm height for hidden mounting in vehicles and assets.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity \(with related Cat M1/NB‑IoT and 2G variants available\) to suit regional network requirements.
- Bluetooth LE for wireless pairing with temperature, humidity, magnet, movement and liquid-level beacons and sensors.
- Backup battery for continued reporting during momentary power loss and basic anti-theft scenarios.
- Remote engine blocking \(immobilizer\) and broad telemetry options for active fleet control and anti-theft response.
- Regional radio and hardware variants, including optional jamming detection for enhanced security.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The FMC920 sends GNSS position data and telemetry to Plaspy over cellular networks, enabling real-time tracking, geofencing alerts, and historical route reports. Plaspy ingests the device’s telemetry and presents it via dashboards, maps and automated notifications so fleet managers can act on critical events quickly.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — position reports transmitted over 4G \(Cat 1\) and routed to Plaspy for live tracking.
- Remote engine blocking \(immobilizer\) for rapid anti-theft response and vehicle immobilization through Plaspy controls.
- Fuel monitoring and other vehicle telemetry can be supported when integrated with vehicle CAN or external sensors \(when available and configured\).
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons for temperature, humidity, magnet \(door/lock\) status, movement and liquid-level monitoring.
- Alerts and reports — configure Plaspy to trigger notifications for geofence breaches, sensor thresholds, tamper or jamming indications where supported.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization — remotely block engines and receive real-time alerts via Plaspy if unauthorized movement is detected.
- Asset and vehicle tracking in constrained spaces — concealed installation for rental fleets, service vehicles, and high-value assets.
- Temperature-sensitive cargo monitoring — Bluetooth sensors report temperature and humidity to Plaspy alongside location for compliance and cold-chain oversight.
- Door, magnet and tamper monitoring — BLE magnet sensors and telemetry help detect unauthorized access or container door openings.
- General telematics and fleet management — location, movement and sensor data feed Plaspy dashboards for route optimization and operational visibility.
