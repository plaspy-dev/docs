# Teltonika - FMC920

<div className="device-logo">
![FMC920](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Teltonika FMC920 is a compact 4G LTE Cat 1 GPS tracker designed for reliable vehicle tracking and fleet control where space is limited. Its slim 12 mm housing makes it suitable for concealed installation in vehicles and assets, while built-in features such as Bluetooth LE support, a backup battery and remote engine blocking make it a practical option for fleet operators and anti-theft deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FMC920 can integrate into Plaspy’s real-time tracking and fleet management workflows to provide consolidated location visibility, alerts and reporting. The tracker’s ability to pair with wireless sensors adds environmental and condition monitoring to standard location telemetry, which broadens the types of operational data that Plaspy can present to fleet managers.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for consolidated real-time tracking, alerts and report generation.
- Slim 12 mm form factor for concealed mounting in vehicles and high value assets.
- 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity with related regional variants available for diverse deployments.
- Bluetooth LE support for pairing with wireless beacons and environmental sensors.
- Backup battery for continued reporting during momentary power loss and basic anti-theft scenarios.
- Remote engine blocking capability to support immobilization workflows and theft response.
- Multiple hardware and regional variants with noted EOL status for some models; verify availability.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the FMC920 transmits GNSS position and available telemetry over cellular networks to Plaspy, where data is visualized on maps, dashboards and reports. Plaspy ingests the device data to enable geofencing, historical route analysis and automated notifications so operators can monitor fleets and respond to events efficiently.

- Real-time location updates and live tracking on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- Remote engine blocking control integrated into Plaspy workflows for rapid anti-theft response.
- Bluetooth sensor data (temperature, humidity, magnet, movement, liquid level) presented alongside location for combined condition and location monitoring.
- Configurable alerts and reports for geofence breaches, sensor thresholds and tamper indications where supported.
- Continued reporting for short power interruptions supported by the device backup battery.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization with remote blocking and real-time alerts via Plaspy.
- Concealed vehicle and asset tracking for rental, service and high value asset fleets.
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring using Bluetooth sensors with location context.
- Door, magnet and tamper monitoring for containers and access control.
- General telematics for route visibility, historical playback and operational oversight.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the Teltonika FMC920 with Plaspy provides a compact, discreet tracking option that extends basic location monitoring with environmental sensor support and anti-theft features. Its slim profile and Bluetooth sensor ecosystem make it well suited to mixed fleets and installations where space is constrained, while the backup battery and immobilizer functions support critical theft mitigation workflows when combined with Plaspy alerts and controls.
