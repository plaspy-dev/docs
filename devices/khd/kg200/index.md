# KHD - KG200

<div className="device-logo">
![KG200](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The KHD KG200 is a high performance GNSS tracker designed for vehicle and ship tracking. It combines GPS or GLONASS positioning with GSM wireless communication to provide accurate location reporting and reliable connectivity for moving assets. The device is described as having superior receive sensitivity, making it suitable for demanding tracking environments where consistent position updates are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the KG200 can forward its location and event messages to Plaspy's fleet management platform using standard GSM based channels. That compatibility makes the KG200 a practical option for organizations that want to integrate GNSS tracking, geo fence events, emergency alerts, and scheduling information into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- GNSS positioning with support for GPS or GLONASS for accurate location data
- GSM based communication with GPRS or SMS reporting to backend servers
- Designed specifically for vehicle and ship tracking applications
- Higher receive sensitivity for improved performance in challenging environments
- Real time tracking and monitoring capability to support operational needs
- Built in features for emergency alerts, geo fence boundary events, and scheduling

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the KG200 transmits location and event data to the platform where it is visualized, logged, and made available for alerts and reporting. Plaspy ingests the device messages and presents them alongside other fleet telemetry so teams can monitor assets in one place.

- Live map visualization of vehicle and ship positions within Plaspy
- Event alerts such as geo fence crossings and emergency notifications routed to Plaspy dashboards
- Route history and playback for trips reported by the KG200
- Scheduling and status information available for operational planning and oversight
- Consolidated reports and activity logs for fleet managers to review performance

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time tracking of commercial or corporate vehicle fleets
- Monitoring and oversight of small to medium sized ships and marine assets
- Asset tracking where reliable GNSS reception is required
- Geo fence based perimeter monitoring and automated alerts
- Scheduling and route compliance for logistics and transport operations

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The KG200 pairs practical GNSS positioning with GSM reporting methods that are commonly used in fleet and asset tracking deployments. For organizations using Plaspy, the tracker offers a straightforward way to feed reliable location and event data into a centralized management platform without unnecessary complexity. Its focus on vehicle and ship applications makes it a clear candidate for mixed fleets where both road and marine assets are tracked.

While the KG200's published features emphasize positioning accuracy and communication flexibility, Plaspy adds value by consolidating those data streams into maps, alerts, and reports that help teams make operational decisions. Together they provide a simple path from field data to actionable oversight.
