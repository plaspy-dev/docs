# KHD - KG200

![KG200](./logo.jpg)

The KHD KG200 is a high-performance GNSS tracker specifically designed for vehicle and ship tracking. It seamlessly combines GSM wireless communication technology with the GPS \(or GLONASS\) system, providing superior receive sensitivity for accurate and reliable tracking. With its advanced features and capabilities, the KG200 is an ideal choice for fleet management and asset tracking applications.

One of the key features of the KG200 is its ability to communicate with the backend server through the GPRS/GSM network or SMS. This allows for real-time tracking and monitoring of vehicles or ships, as well as the ability to receive emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, and scheduling information. Users can easily set up their custom tracking platforms to communicate with the KG200 using a PC or a smartphone with Android or iOS operating systems.

The KHD KG200 offers a range of outstanding features that make it a reliable and efficient tracking solution. Some of these features include:

- Powerful GNSS technology for accurate and precise tracking
- GSM wireless communication for seamless connectivity
- Superior receive sensitivity for reliable tracking even in challenging environments
- Real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities
- Emergency alert reporting for immediate response
- Geo-fence boundary crossings for enhanced security
- Scheduling functionality for efficient fleet management
- Compatibility with PC and smartphone platforms for easy setup and customization

With its robust features and reliable performance, the KHD KG200 is an excellent choice for vehicle and ship tracking applications. Whether you need to monitor your fleet or track valuable assets, the KG200 provides the functionality and flexibility you need to effectively manage your operations.
