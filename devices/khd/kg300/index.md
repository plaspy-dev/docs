# KHD - KG300

![KG300](./logo.jpg)

The KHD KG300 is a GNSS tracker designed for vehicle tracking, monitoring, and advertising applications. It combines cellular wireless communication with GPS or GLONASS positioning to provide reliable location information and event reporting. The KG300 supports sending data to backend servers via GPRS/GSM and can also operate with SMS for alerting and lightweight commands, making it suitable for a range of vehicle-based tracking needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the KG300 can feed location and event data into the Plaspy fleet management platform for visualization, alerts, and operational reporting. Its ability to report emergency events, geo-fence boundary crossings, and scheduled information makes it a practical option for organizations that want to use Plaspy to centralize fleet oversight, monitor advertising runs, or automate event-driven notifications.

## Key Highlights

- GNSS based vehicle tracking supporting GPS or GLONASS positioning
- Cellular communication via GPRS/GSM and SMS for backend reporting
- Real time reporting of emergency alerts and geo fence events
- Scheduling and timed reporting features suitable for recurring route monitoring
- Designed for vehicle monitoring and on vehicle advertising use cases
- Flexible platform integration using PC or smartphone based custom tracking setups

## How It Works with Plaspy

The KG300 can send its location and event messages to Plaspy where those inputs are ingested into the platform for monitoring and analysis. Once connected, Plaspy provides dashboard views, alerts, and reporting to turn raw device messages into operational insight for fleet operators and administrators.

- Live location and history playback for individual vehicles and groups
- Geo fence alerts and boundary crossing notifications routed through Plaspy
- Emergency and event notifications visible in Plaspy alert streams and dashboards
- Scheduled reporting and route monitoring to support recurring operations
- Fleet level visibility for route adherence, idle times, and asset allocation
- Use case support for vehicle advertising by tracking routes and schedule compliance

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for commercial vehicle pools and service fleets
- Monitoring vehicles used for on vehicle advertising and route verification
- Security and rapid response where emergency alerts are required
- Scheduled route tracking for deliveries, transport services, and shuttles
- Rental and shared vehicle oversight with basic reporting and alerts

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The KG300 pairs practical GNSS positioning with flexible cellular reporting options, which makes it a fit for organizations that need straightforward vehicle tracking and event reporting. When connected to Plaspy, the device's data becomes part of a managed platform that supports alerting, reporting, and operational oversight without requiring deep changes to existing workflows.

Because the KG300 can communicate using standard backend channels and supports custom platform setups via common devices, it is a sensible option for teams that want to consolidate tracking into Plaspy while keeping deployment and management relatively simple. Plaspy can map incoming KG300 messages into fleet dashboards, geo fence rules, and scheduled reports to help turn device signals into useful operational information.
