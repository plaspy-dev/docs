# KHD - KG300

![KG300](./logo.jpg)

The KHD KG300 is a highly capable GNSS tracker that is specifically designed for vehicle tracking, monitoring, and advertising purposes. It seamlessly combines GSM wireless communication technology with the GPS \(or GLONASS\) system, ensuring superior receive sensitivity and accurate tracking capabilities. With the KG300, users can easily communicate with the backend server through the GPRS/GSM network or SMS, allowing for real-time reporting of emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, and scheduling information.

One of the standout features of the KG300 is its versatility and ease of use. Users can effortlessly set up their own custom tracking platforms to communicate with the KG300 using a PC or a smartphone with Android or iOS operating systems. This flexibility allows for a seamless integration of the KG300 into existing tracking systems or the creation of new ones tailored to specific needs.

With its powerful features and reliable performance, the KHD KG300 is an excellent choice for businesses and individuals looking for a high-quality GNSS tracker for vehicle tracking, monitoring, and advertising purposes. Its advanced technology and user-friendly interface make it a valuable tool for optimizing fleet management, enhancing security, and maximizing advertising effectiveness.

### Outstanding Features:

- Powerful GNSS tracker for vehicle tracking, monitoring, and advertising
- Combines GSM wireless communication technology with GPS \(or GLONASS\) system
- Superior receive sensitivity for accurate tracking
- Communicates with backend server through GPRS/GSM network or SMS
- Real-time reporting of emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, and scheduling
- Custom tracking platform setup using PC or smartphone \(Android or iOS\)
- Versatile and user-friendly
