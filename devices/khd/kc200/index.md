# KHD - KC200

![KC200](./logo.jpg)

The KHD KC200 is a GNSS tracker designed for vehicle and ship tracking. It combines GPS or GLONASS positioning with GSM wireless communication to provide location reporting and event notifications. The KC200 is described as having high receive sensitivity for reliable position fixes and supports reporting methods such as GPRS/GSM and SMS to send emergency alerts, geo-fence boundary crossings, and scheduled updates to a backend server. It is intended to work with custom tracking platforms on PC and smartphone devices.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the KC200 can feed position and event data into the Plaspy fleet management platform to give operators improved visibility and operational control. Plaspy can receive the KC200's reported locations and alerts and present them in dashboards, maps, and reports that help with monitoring vehicles and vessels, handling geo-fence events, and reviewing historical movements for operational decisions.

## Key Highlights

- GNSS tracker built for both vehicle and ship tracking use cases
- Supports GPS or GLONASS positioning for broader satellite coverage
- GSM based communication for reporting via GPRS/GSM and SMS
- High receive sensitivity aimed at improving location accuracy in challenging conditions
- Sends emergency alerts and geo-fence boundary notifications to a backend
- Compatible with customizable tracking platforms on PC and mobile devices

## How It Works with Plaspy

The KC200 can be connected to Plaspy to provide continuous location updates and event reporting, allowing fleet managers to monitor assets in real time and act on alerts. Plaspy ingests the device messages and transforms them into map views, alert streams, and operational reports that teams can use for day to day fleet oversight.

- Real-time location visualization of vehicles and vessels on Plaspy maps
- Alert handling for emergency notifications and geo-fence events within Plaspy
- Scheduled reporting and historical tracks for route review and compliance checks
- Fleet status and operational dashboards to monitor multiple units at once
- Reporting exports and logs to support administration and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for logistics and delivery operations
- Ship and small vessel tracking for coastal, port, or nearshore monitoring
- Security and recovery monitoring for mobile assets
- Route compliance and schedule verification for field operations
- Emergency alerting and location reporting for remote units

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The KC200 is a practical option for organizations that need a general purpose GNSS tracker capable of serving both vehicle and maritime roles. Its focus on reliable position reception and support for standard wireless reporting methods makes it a sensible match for Plaspy environments that prioritize consistent location feeds and event notifications. Using the KC200 with Plaspy can help consolidate tracking across mixed fleets and provide a single operational view for managers.
