# EV - EV-04

<div className="device-logo">
![EV-04](./tracker.png)
</div>

The EV-04 is a compact personal 4G GPS tracker pendant designed for dependable personal protection and remote patient monitoring. As a lightweight wearable, it combines one button SOS, sensitive fall detection, two way calling, and multiple positioning methods to provide caregivers and monitoring services with timely alerts and location information. The device is IP67 rated and built for daily wear with a soft touch housing and a dedicated charging base.

This model is confirmed compatible with Plaspy and integrates into Plaspy workflows for real time tracking, audible alerting, and centralized telemetry. When paired with Plaspy, the EV-04 can report SOS events, fall incidents, and location updates into a single monitoring dashboard so caregivers, monitoring centers, and operational teams can maintain situational awareness and respond efficiently.

## Key Highlights

- Compact pendant form factor ideal for personal safety and continuous wear
- One button SOS with integrated speaker and microphone for two way voice communication
- Highly sensitive fall detection designed to trigger automatic SOS alerts
- Multi technology positioning using GPS Wi Fi BLE beacon and cell based location for improved coverage
- Water resistant IP67 housing and lightweight design around 53 g for comfortable daily use
- Bluetooth support for beacons and optional pairing with medical devices to assist remote patient monitoring
- Remote firmware upgrades and platform level device management to simplify maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrated with Plaspy, the EV-04 becomes a managed personal tracking asset that streams position and event telemetry into Plaspy dashboards and alerting systems. Plaspy receives device events and location updates, presents them to operators, and can forward critical alarms to predefined contacts or monitoring teams for fast response.

- Real time location and telemetry visible in Plaspy including accelerated updates during SOS events
- One button SOS and automatic fall detection forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and logging
- Two way voice call events recorded and available for audit and follow up through Plaspy
- BLE beacon and Bluetooth sensor data used to improve indoor accuracy and feed optional RPM telemetry into Plaspy
- Centralized device health and FOTA status shown in Plaspy to support remote maintenance and fleet oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Remote patient monitoring where BLE paired devices and medication reminders support caregiver workflows

- Assisted living and home care programs seeking a lightweight wearable that reports location and events to a central system
