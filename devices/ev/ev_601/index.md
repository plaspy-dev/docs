# EV - EV-601

![EV-601](./logo.jpg)

The EV EV-601 is a versatile GPS tracker designed specifically for vehicles and motorcycles. With its advanced features and reliable performance, it provides real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities to ensure the safety and security of your vehicles.

One of the standout features of the EV-601 is its free real-time web-based online tracking service, which allows you to track your vehicles without any hidden costs. You can easily access the tracking information through the web or via SMS, giving you full control over your vehicles at all times.

The EV-601 also offers GSM/GPRS simultaneous communication, ensuring a stable and reliable connection. It is equipped with a built-in 8MB memory chip, allowing for efficient data storage and retrieval. Additionally, the tracker can reply with maps links and the car's address via SMS, providing you with accurate location information.

With the EV-601, you can set up geo-fencing boundaries to receive alerts whenever your vehicles enter or exit specific areas. It also offers auto-tracking by time interval, car power lost alarm, back-up battery low alarm, SOS panic button for help, over-speed alarm, GPS signal lost and recovery alert, movement alarm, and listen-in function.

Furthermore, the EV-601 allows for remote engine/oil cut-off by SMS and website, giving you the ability to immobilize your vehicles in case of theft or unauthorized use. It also features inputs detections for door open/close, ACC on/off, and outputs for door lock/unlock or siren sounding.

The EV-601 utilizes U-Blox GPS chipset technology, ensuring accurate and reliable positioning. It also supports firmware upgrades over the air, allowing for easy and convenient updates. With its customizable options, the EV-601 can be tailored to meet your specific tracking needs.
