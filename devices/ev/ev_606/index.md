# EV - EV-606

<div className="device-logo">
![EV-606](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The EV-606 is a versatile personal and asset tracking device designed for continuous location monitoring. It combines GPS satellite positioning with CellLocate technology to improve tracking in shielded or indoor environments such as parking structures and enclosed spaces. The unit includes a rechargeable and replaceable battery with extended run time, a built in 3D G sensor for motion and shock detection, voice communication capabilities, and onboard storage for tens of thousands of logged points.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the EV-606 is suitable for organizations that need reliable visibility and alerting across both outdoor and more challenging indoor scenarios. Plaspy can receive and display the EV-606 data streams for real time monitoring, event alerts, and historical review, making it practical for fleet administrators, asset managers, and personal safety workflows that rely on persistent tracking and concise incident information.

## Key Highlights

- Hybrid positioning with GPS and CellLocate to improve coverage in sheltered environments
- Long running, rechargeable and replaceable battery for extended tracking periods
- Built in 3D G sensor providing motion and shock alarm functionality
- Two way voice communication and voice monitoring features for direct contact
- Onboard flash memory with data logging capacity up to 60,000 locations
- Emergency SOS button plus geo fencing and over speed alarm support

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the EV-606 supplies location and event data that the platform uses to present actionable visibility for vehicles, equipment, and people. Plaspy presents live positions, historical routes, and device alerts alongside other tracked assets for unified oversight.

- Live location and history display so dispatch and managers can view current position and recent movements
- Alert handling for SOS triggers, motion or shock events, geo fence breaches, and signal loss or recovery
- Use logged location data and blind area re upload features to reconstruct movement when real time coverage is intermittent
- Event driven notifications and reporting for operational oversight and incident review
- Device status and usage metrics surfaced in Plaspy dashboards to help manage deployed units

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for lone workers or family members who require discreet tracking
- Asset recovery and protection for equipment stored in enclosed or mixed coverage areas
- Short term rental or shared equipment tracking where battery life and replaceable cells are valuable
- Incident and movement detection for high value items that benefit from shock and motion alerts
- Site and facility monitoring where indoor locating improves visibility in garages and warehouses

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The EV-606 combines a focused set of features that align well with Plaspy s monitoring and reporting capabilities. Its hybrid positioning approach and substantial onboard logging help fill gaps when continuous satellite signals are not available, while the motion sensor and SOS functions provide clear event signals that Plaspy can surface to operators.

For organizations that need straightforward personal or asset tracking with the ability to review detailed location history, the EV-606 is a practical option to pair with Plaspy. The device s replaceable battery and data logging capacity make it useful for deployments that require long term uptime and reliable incident records.
