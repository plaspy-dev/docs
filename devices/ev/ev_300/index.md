# EV - EV-300

![EV-300](./logo.jpg)

The EV-300 Vehicle/Motorcycle GPS Tracker by EV is a reliable and versatile tracking device designed to provide real-time location information for vehicles and motorcycles. With its super stability and wide operating voltage range of 6 to 36V DC, this tracker ensures consistent and accurate tracking performance.

One of the standout features of the EV-300 is its free real-time online tracking service, which allows users to monitor their vehicles or motorcycles remotely. The tracker also offers multiple inputs/outputs interfaces for monitoring and control, as well as an RS232 communication port for integrating peripheral accessories. Additionally, the EV-300 comes with internal antennas for easy installation and serviceability, making it a convenient choice for users.

Other notable features of the EV-300 include an internal 3-axis accelerometer for power saving and motion detection, smart power saving working mode, geo-fence management, over speed alarm, listen-in function, car power lost alarm, low power alarm, and remote engine/oil cut-off by SMS and website. The tracker also has a built-in 8Mb flash memory, capable of storing up to 60,000 waypoints, and supports firmware upgrade over the air. With a backup battery of 200mAh, the EV-300 ensures uninterrupted tracking even in case of power loss.
