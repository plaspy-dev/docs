# EV - EV-300

![EV-300](./logo.jpg)

The EV-300 Vehicle/Motorcycle GPS Tracker by EV is a compact tracking device built to deliver stable, real time location information for both vehicles and motorcycles. Designed with a wide operating voltage range and internal antennas, the EV-300 emphasizes consistent tracking performance and serviceability. It includes multiple inputs and outputs, an RS232 communication port for accessory integration, onboard storage for waypoints, a backup battery, and an internal accelerometer to support motion detection and power saving modes.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the EV-300 can feed live position and alarm data into a fleet management platform to provide visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can display location history captured by the device, receive geo fence and over speed alerts, and surface I/O events and power loss notifications to help teams monitor vehicles and motorcycles in mixed fleets. The combination makes the EV-300 a practical option for organizations that need dependable location reporting tied into Plaspy workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Real time online tracking service for continuous location visibility
- Wide operating voltage range suitable for vehicles and motorcycles
- Multiple inputs and outputs plus an RS232 port for accessory integration
- Internal 3 axis accelerometer for motion detection and power saving
- Built in flash memory storing up to 60,000 waypoints for offline logging
- Geo fence management, over speed alarm, and low power or power lost alerts
- Backup battery and support for firmware upgrade over the air for maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

The EV-300 transmits position, alarm and event data that Plaspy can ingest to present maps, alerts, and historical tracks. Within Plaspy, device events and stored waypoints help maintain continuity of location history and support operational reporting.

- Display live vehicle and motorcycle locations on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility
- Receive and act on geo fence and over speed alarms inside Plaspy alerting workflows
- Use stored waypoints from the device to backfill location history when connectivity is intermittent
- Surface power lost and low battery notifications in Plaspy for proactive maintenance
- Combine I/O events and remote control signals with Plaspy reporting for operational insight

## Typical Use Cases

- Day to day fleet monitoring for mixed vehicle and motorcycle fleets
- Delivery and courier services requiring real time location and route history
- Asset security and recovery workflows using listen in and remote cut off features
- Rental and shared vehicle programs that need usage tracking and geofence controls
- Remote vehicle oversight where backup battery tracking preserves continuity during power interruptions
