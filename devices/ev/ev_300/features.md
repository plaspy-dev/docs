# EV - EV-300 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the EV EV-300 GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities are relevant when the device is used with Plaspy. The content focuses on practical, user facing functions and how Plaspy surfaces location, events, and operational status from the EV-300 without delving into private configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware or hardware notes, consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The EV-300 is a vehicle and motorcycle tracker designed for stable, continuous tracking and remote monitoring. It combines standard tracking capabilities with power management and local storage to support reliable reporting and offline data capture.

- Real time online tracking for remote location visibility and monitoring.
- Wide operating voltage range for flexible vehicle and motorcycle installations.
- Multiple inputs and outputs plus an RS232 port for peripheral integration and external control.
- Built in flash memory to store waypoints locally when connectivity is interrupted.
- Motion detection and smart power saving enabled by an internal 3 axis accelerometer.
- Alarms and management features such as geo fence, over speed, low power, and power lost notifications.

## Core Features of EV - EV-300

- Real time tracking for vehicles and motorcycles, intended for continuous location reporting.
- Wide input voltage tolerance for installation across a variety of vehicle electrical systems.
- Multiple I O interfaces and an RS232 communication port for accessory integration and monitoring.
- Internal 3 axis accelerometer used for motion detection and power saving modes.
- Built in flash memory with capacity for significant waypoint storage to support offline logging.
- Geo fence management and over speed alarm for event based alerts.
- Listen in function and alarm types including car power lost alarm and low power alarm.
- Remote engine or oil cut off support via SMS and web controls as provided by the device and manufacturer services.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy acts as the platform to receive, display, and archive the EV-300's reporting and alerts so fleet managers and vehicle owners can monitor activity and respond to events. Plaspy automatically detects the device protocol and presents device data in the platform interface.

- Real time location and movement data reported by the EV-300 are visible on Plaspy maps for live tracking.
- Device generated alarms such as geo fence events, over speed, low battery, and power lost are captured and shown as events in Plaspy.
- Stored waypoints held in the tracker’s internal memory can be uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity resumes, allowing route reconstruction and historical review.
- Motion detection and power saving status reported by the accelerometer appear as state changes, helping operators see when a vehicle is active or stationary.
- Input and output events or RS232 accessory signals reported by the device are logged in Plaspy as available telemetry or event entries where the device sends those reports.

## Typical Use Cases

- Continuous vehicle and motorcycle location monitoring for fleets and individual owners.
- Route history and playback using stored waypoints after offline periods.
- Alerting for geo fence entry and exit or over speed conditions to support compliance and safety.
- Power loss and low battery detection to surface potential tampering or maintenance needs.
- Remote accessory monitoring or external device integration using the RS232 port and I O interfaces.
- Motion based reporting to reduce data usage during long idle periods while preserving important movement events.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and software updates can change feature behavior; some functions may require specific firmware revisions.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may alter available interfaces or alarms.
- Certain features such as remote engine cut off, listen in, or SMS controls depend on local regulations, SIM service capabilities, and manufacturer provisioning.
- Installation method and wiring affect which I O signals and alarms are available to the platform.
- Confirm supported features for your serial accessory or peripheral via the manufacturer documentation before planning integrations.
