# EV

EV is a hardware brand that produces GPS tracking and telematics devices used for vehicle and asset monitoring. Plaspy recognizes EV devices as compatible with its platform, allowing fleet managers and operators to connect EV hardware to Plaspy for real time location, alerts, and reporting. Use this page to understand how EV devices integrate with Plaspy and to find the right device for your deployment.

<BrandCatalog brand={brand} />

## How EV devices work with Plaspy

EV devices typically transmit location and sensor data over cellular networks to a remote server. Plaspy can ingest data from EV trackers when the device is configured to send telemetry to the Plaspy platform or to a gateway compatible with Plaspy. After the device is added to your Plaspy account and the tracker is sending data, you can view live location, historical routes, and configured alerts in the Plaspy web console.

Key integration ideas
- Configure the device to report to the correct server address and include the device identifier.
- Register the device in your Plaspy account so incoming telemetry maps to the right asset.
- Use Plaspy settings to enable alerts, geofences, and reporting for the device.

If you need step by step device setup, consult the device manual and the Plaspy device setup guides. For platform level questions, visit https://www.plaspy.com.

## Common features found in EV devices

While specific features vary by model, EV trackers commonly provide the following capabilities that work with Plaspy:
- GPS location and basic position reporting
- Movement and motion detection alerts
- Geofence triggers and related notifications
- Basic telemetry such as speed and direction
- Support for over the air configuration and remote updates depending on the model

## Typical use cases for EV GPS trackers

EV devices are used across several common scenarios:
- Fleet tracking for small and mid sized commercial fleets
- Asset tracking for trailers and equipment
- Vehicle recovery and theft deterrence
- Rental and subscription vehicle monitoring
- Field service and delivery route oversight

Plaspy provides dashboards, alerts, and reports useful for each of these use cases once your EV devices are connected.

## Help finding the right EV device

When evaluating EV hardware to use with Plaspy, consider:
- Connectivity requirements and supported cellular bands for your region
- Power options and installation style for the vehicle type
- Required inputs and outputs or external sensor compatibility
- Battery life and sleep modes for battery powered installations
- Ruggedness and environmental ratings if the tracker will be exposed to harsh conditions

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What EV devices are compatible with Plaspy?
A: The BrandCatalog above displays the EV models that are known to work with Plaspy. Instead of listing models here, use the catalog to view available units and then open the model page for device specific details and documentation.

Q: Can I use EV GPS trackers with Plaspy?
A: Yes. EV trackers can be configured to send telemetry to Plaspy. After you configure the device and register it in Plaspy, you will be able to monitor location and events from the Plaspy dashboard.

Q: Does Plaspy support fleet monitoring with EV devices?
A: Plaspy supports fleet monitoring for compatible EV devices by offering live tracking, alerts, geofencing, and reporting. You can manage multiple EV trackers alongside other hardware brands within the same Plaspy account.

Q: Where can I find EV device documentation in Plaspy?
A: Device level documentation is available through the model pages in the BrandCatalog and in the Plaspy support center. For device specific setup steps consult the tracker manual and the Plaspy device setup guides.

Q: How do I add an EV device to my Plaspy account?
A: General steps include configuring the EV device to report to Plaspy, noting the device identifier, and creating a device entry in your Plaspy account that matches the identifier. For detailed instructions consult your device manual and Plaspy setup help.

Q: What if my EV device is not sending data to Plaspy?
A: First verify power and cellular connectivity on the device. Check that the device is configured with the correct server address and identifier. If issues persist, review the device logs and Plaspy incoming data status, and contact your device vendor or Plaspy support for further troubleshooting.
