# EV - EV-07B Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Eview EV-07B wearable GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities integrate with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on user facing features relevant to monitoring, alerts, and location reporting when the device is used with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Users should review device firmware notes and vendor documentation to confirm capabilities for a particular device and deployment.

## Feature Overview

The EV-07B is a compact SOS pendant designed for discreet personal safety and monitoring. It combines multiple location sources, a one-button emergency workflow, and Bluetooth sensor support to provide responders and caregivers timely situational awareness through Plaspy.

- One-button SOS pendant that sends alarm events and location data into Plaspy for rapid response and logging.
- Continuous and high frequency location reporting during active alarms to support near real time tracking.
- Two-way voice calling and voice prompts to allow direct communication between the wearer and responders.
- BLE 5.0 pairing for external sensors and Eview beacons to improve indoor location and forward telemetry.
- Built for everyday wear with a lightweight form factor and waterproof rating for use in common personal safety scenarios.

## Core Features of EV - EV-07B

- One-button SOS alarm with automatic notification and escalation workflows.
- High frequency location updates available during active SOS events for improved responder visibility.
- Two-way voice calling and voice prompts for direct wearer to responder communication.
- Multi source location support including GPS plus Wi‑Fi, BLE Beacon and LBS augmentation.
- BLE 5.0 support to pair external sensors and Eview Home Beacons for enhanced telemetry and indoor positioning.
- Fall detection and no-motion alert types to indicate potential incidents to monitoring teams.
- Low-battery warnings and battery monitoring including charging base support with backup battery option.
- Available in 2G and 4G LTE variants with an AT&T 4G LTE certified option for expanded U.S. coverage where applicable.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the EV-07B forwards location, alarm and supported event data into Plaspy’s mapping, notification, and reporting layers so teams can monitor devices and respond through established workflows.

- SOS activations appear as events in Plaspy and can trigger high frequency location updates on the map.
- Fall detection and no-motion alerts are reported into Plaspy as event types for rapid triage and escalation.
- BLE paired sensor telemetry and beacon assisted location data are forwarded to Plaspy to enrich situational context.
- Battery status and low-battery alerts are exposed in Plaspy to support proactive maintenance and replacement planning.
- Plaspy logs voice call events and alarm history to provide an audit trail for monitoring and response activities.

## Typical Use Cases

- Assisted living and elderly care monitoring where a wearable SOS pendant provides immediate alerts to caregivers.
- Lone-worker safety programs that require a discreet alarm device with near real time location tracking.
- Remote patient monitoring workflows that combine BLE medical peripherals with location and alarm events.
- Travelers or children needing a lightweight wearable for immediate location and voice contact during incidents.
- Home companion systems using the charging base for at-home check ins and rapid escalation through monitoring teams.
- Small item anti-theft or recovery scenarios where a compact wearable offers immediate tracking and alarm support.

- Firmware versions can change how alerts, fall detection sensitivity, and reporting intervals behave.
