# EV - EV-206M Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the EV-206M pet GPS tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities you can expect to see reflected in Plaspy dashboards and alerts, and explains how the device’s location and telemetry feed into Plaspy-powered monitoring workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional cellular support, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device-specific details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The EV-206M is an ultra-light pet tracker designed for cats and small pets that combines outdoor GPS positioning with Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth beacon assistance for improved indoor awareness. It pairs a small IP67-rated enclosure and an energy efficient IoT architecture with a matched charging base that includes a Bluetooth beacon to support home-aware detection and straightforward charging.

- Ultra-light 19 g collar-friendly form factor for comfortable wear by cats and small dogs.
- Multi-mode positioning using GPS plus Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth beacon assistance for better indoor and near-home coverage.
- Cellular uplink via 4G LTE‑M and NB‑IoT for dependable data reporting and near real-time location updates.
- Live tracking capability with outdoor location updates as frequent as every 10 seconds when enabled.
- Activity monitoring and no-motion notifications to surface unusual movement patterns.
- Included charging base with a built-in Bluetooth beacon to simplify charging and improve home detection.

## Core Features of EV - EV-206M

- Ultra-light mechanical design optimized for small pets and extended wear.
- GPS positioning for outdoor location fixes and route history.
- Wi‑Fi assisted positioning to improve indoor location awareness where GPS is weak.
- Bluetooth Low Energy beacon support for home-aware arrival and departure detection via the included charging base and nearby beacons.
- Cellular connectivity over LTE‑M and NB‑IoT for reliable uplink to cloud platforms.
- Live tracking with configurable update frequency, including outdoors updates up to every 10 seconds when live tracking is active.
- Activity monitoring and no-motion alerts to support welfare monitoring and unusual behavior detection.
- IP67 water resistance for everyday wear and weather protection.

When configured with Plaspy, the EV-206M’s location fixes, activity telemetry, and home-aware events are ingested and presented in ways that help owners and operators maintain visibility and respond to events.

- Live map visualization of current position and near real-time movement when live tracking is enabled.

- Timeline history and route playback showing past movement and daily roaming patterns.

- Centralized event logs and alerts for quick review and incident follow up in Plaspy dashboards.
