# EV-206M Pet GPS Tracker — Plaspy compatible

The EV-206M is an ultra-light 4G pet GPS tracker designed for cats and small pets, built to deliver near real-time tracking and activity telemetry without burdening your animal. At roughly 19 grams with an IP67 water-resistant rating, the EV-206M pairs a cat-friendly form factor with multi-technology positioning \(GPS, Wi‑Fi, Beacon and Cellular 4G LTE‑M/NB\) so owners get dependable location awareness both outdoors and indoors.

The device is Plaspy compatible and integrates seamlessly into Plaspy-powered monitoring workflows to provide live tracking, geofence alerts, timeline history and activity notifications. Its energy-efficient IoT architecture, included charging base \(with a built-in Bluetooth beacon\) and support for home-aware detection mean the EV-206M is optimized for everyday pet safety and sleep-easy monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra-light 19 g design engineered for cats and small pets — minimal collar load for comfortable wear.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS for outdoor accuracy plus Wi‑Fi and Beacon-assisted indoor coverage.
- Cellular connectivity via 4G LTE‑M / NB-IoT for reliable uplink and real-time tracking to Plaspy.
- Live tracking with outdoors location updates as frequent as every 10 seconds when enabled.
- IP67 water resistance for everyday wear and weather protection.
- Activity monitoring, location history and no-motion alerts to help detect unusual behavior.
- Includes matched charging base with built-in Bluetooth beacon, USB cable and accessories for simple setup.
- Home-aware features using Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth beacon detection to recognize arrivals and departures.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The EV-206M transmits location fixes, activity telemetry and home-aware events to Plaspy so you can monitor pets in near real-time, receive geofence notifications and review route history. Plaspy consumes the device data to display live location on the map, push alerts to mobile or web dashboards, and archive timeline history for later review.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — outdoor live updates as often as every 10 seconds when live tracking is enabled.
- Activity and no-motion alerts — Plaspy surfaces movement trends and inactivity notifications from the EV-206M’s sensors.
- Geofence and home-aware events — Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth beacon detections trigger arrival/departure alerts in Plaspy.
- Location history and timeline — full-day routes and roaming territory are available for playback in Plaspy dashboards.
- Bluetooth sensors/beacons — the included charging base beacon and BLE scanning help improve indoor positioning and home detection.
