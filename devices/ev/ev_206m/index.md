# EV - EV-206M

![EV-206M](./tracker.png)

The EV-206M is an ultra light pet GPS tracker designed for cats and small pets. At roughly 19 grams and with an IP67 water resistant rating, it combines a compact collar friendly form factor with multi mode positioning and cellular uplink to provide dependable location awareness both outdoors and indoors. The manufacturer positions the device for everyday pet safety, with activity monitoring, home aware detection and a matched charging base included.

This model is compatible with Plaspy, making it a practical choice for users who want centralized monitoring of pet location and activity. Plaspy can ingest the EV-206M data to display live location on maps, generate geofence alerts, record timeline history and surface activity notifications, so owners and operators can use familiar Plaspy workflows to manage pet tracking and notifications.

## Key Highlights

- Ultra light 19 g design suited for cats and small pets to minimize collar load and maximize comfort
- Multi mode positioning that combines GPS, Wi Fi and beacon assistance to improve coverage indoors and outdoors
- Cellular connectivity for reliable uplink and near real time location reporting to monitoring platforms like Plaspy
- IP67 water resistant rating for day to day wear and exposure to the elements
- Activity monitoring and no motion alerts to help detect unusual behavior or extended inactivity
- Includes a matched charging base with a built in Bluetooth beacon to support home aware detection
- Energy efficient design intended to balance frequent updates with extended use between charges

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the EV-206M sends location fixes, activity telemetry and home aware events so operators can monitor pets in near real time and automate alerts. Plaspy displays the device position on a map, archives movement history for playback, and issues notifications based on geofences and movement rules.

- Live location visibility on Plaspy maps with regular outdoor updates and indoor assists from Wi Fi and beacon detections
- Configurable geofence alerts in Plaspy for arrivals and departures or when a pet moves outside a defined area
- Activity and no motion notifications surfaced by Plaspy to highlight changes in movement patterns
- Timeline history and route playback in Plaspy dashboards for review of daily range and roaming behavior
- Home aware events from the included charging base beacon and Wi Fi detection used to trigger arrival or departure notifications

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time pet location and safety monitoring for cats and small dogs that require lightweight trackers
- Roaming territory analysis to review route history and understand daily movement patterns
- Home aware alerts that notify owners when a pet leaves or returns via beacon or Wi Fi detection
- Welfare monitoring using no motion alerts to indicate potential inactivity or unusual behavior
- Improved indoor coverage in built environments where GPS alone may be limited
