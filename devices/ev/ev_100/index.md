# EV - EV-100

![EV-100](./logo.jpg)

The EV EV-100 is a versatile GPS tracker designed specifically for vehicles and motorcycles. With its weatherproof and dust-resistant construction, this tracker is built to withstand the rigors of the road. Its super stability ensures reliable performance, making it an ideal choice for fleet management and personal vehicle tracking.

One of the standout features of the EV-100 is its wide operating voltage range of 6 to 36V DC, allowing it to be compatible with a wide range of vehicles. The tracker also features internal antennas, making installation and serviceability more economical. Additionally, it is equipped with a 3-axis accelerometer for power saving and motion detection, ensuring efficient operation and accurate tracking.

With the EV-100, you can enjoy free real-time online tracking service, allowing you to monitor your vehicle's location and movement from anywhere. The tracker also offers various alarm features, including over speed alarm, car power lost alarm, and low power alarm. It even has the capability for remote engine and oil cut-off via SMS and website, providing an added layer of security.

Other notable features of the EV-100 include geo-fence management, reply maps link and real address information via SMS, built-in flash memory for storing waypoints, GPRS blind area data re-upload function, and firmware upgrade over the air. The tracker is also equipped with a backup battery with a capacity of 200mAh, ensuring continuous operation even in the event of a power loss.
