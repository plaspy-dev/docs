# EV - EV-100 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive feature overview of the EV EV-100 GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for vehicle and motorcycle visibility and management. It explains the practical functions you can expect when using the EV-100 with Plaspy and how those features support monitoring and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The descriptions below are based on the device model information provided by the manufacturer and are intended as general guidance rather than a definitive specification sheet.

## Feature Overview

The EV-100 is designed as a compact, durable vehicle and motorcycle tracker aimed at reliable location reporting and basic vehicle security functions. It emphasizes robust operation across a wide voltage range and includes features intended to keep tracking active even during temporary signal loss or power interruption.

- Weatherproof and dust resistant construction for road use and exposed installations
- Wide operating voltage range of 6 to 36 V DC for compatibility with many vehicle systems
- Internal antennas to simplify installation and lower service complexity
- Integrated 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection and power saving
- Built-in flash memory for waypoint storage and GPRS blind area data re-upload
- Support for firmware upgrade over the air and remote control features such as engine or oil cut-off

## Core Features of EV - EV-100

- Weatherproof and dust resistant enclosure for reliable outdoor operation
- Wide 6 to 36 V DC operating range for broad vehicle compatibility
- Internal antenna design to reduce installation complexity
- 3-axis accelerometer used for motion detection and power saving behavior
- Real-time online tracking support for live location visibility
- Alarm functions including over speed alarm, car power lost alarm, and low power alarm
- Geo-fence management and SMS reporting of map links and real address information
- Built-in flash memory for storing waypoints and re-uploading data after GPRS blind areas

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives device-reported location, status, and alarm messages from EV-100 units and surfaces that information for monitoring, alerting, and historical review. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and can be configured to accept reports from the device on the standard Plaspy endpoint.

- Location updates and movement events appear in Plaspy for live tracking and playback
- Device alarms such as overspeed, power loss, and low battery can be mapped to Plaspy alert rules and notifications
- Geo-fence entries and exits reported by the device can be visualized and logged within Plaspy
- Waypoint and stored data re-uploads from the device provide continuity for routes captured in GPRS blind areas
- Remote commands supported by the device, such as SMS or web based engine cutoff where available, can be coordinated through Plaspy workflows or operator processes
- Plaspy detects tracker protocol automatically and accepts connections to the standard Plaspy server configuration, simplifying setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location monitoring and route playback for operational oversight
- Motorcycle tracking where compact, weatherproof hardware is required
- Power interruption detection and alerting for stolen or tampered vehicles
- Geo-fence monitoring for site entry and exit reporting
- Remote immobilization workflows where the device and local regulations permit engine or oil cut-off
- Maintaining location continuity in areas with intermittent GPRS using onboard flash memory

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific alarms and remote cut-off functions depend on the device firmware and the configuration chosen at installation
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may change connector pinouts, available features, or voltage tolerances
- Installation type and wiring can affect signals used for alarms and remote control; professional installation is recommended for critical functions
- Firmware over the air and data re-upload behavior depend on network conditions and manufacturer firmware support
- Always confirm exact feature lists and limitations from the manufacturer documentation for the unit you receive
