# EV - EV-201M

![EV-201M](./tracker.png)

The EV-201M is a compact 4G LTE-M/NB GPS tracker designed for dependable pet tracking and activity monitoring. Built for outdoor use, it combines a rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure with an energy efficient 800 mAh battery and IoT power saving modes to provide continuous location history and regular position updates for dogs and other domestic animals.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the EV-201M can forward position fixes, telemetry, and event alerts into Plaspy for centralized visibility. Its pet-focused features — including frequent live updates, a manual location request button, Bluetooth assisted home detection, and an activity timeline — make it a practical option for owners who want location and activity information surfaced alongside other assets in Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for centralized tracking and alerting.
- IP67 waterproof rating suitable for outdoor pet use.
- Energy efficient 800 mAh battery with power saving modes for extended between-charge life.
- Fast outdoor live tracking with updates as frequent as every 10 seconds when Live Tracking is enabled.
- Dedicated manual location button for on demand single position requests.
- Activity monitoring with a timeline to review daily movement and consecutive locations.
- Bluetooth assisted home beacon detection to reduce false alerts when a pet returns home.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the EV-201M sends GNSS positions and device telemetry over cellular connectivity to Plaspy’s cloud platform where locations, alerts, and history are presented on a unified map and dashboard. Plaspy displays live feeds and generates notifications so pet owners can monitor location and activity in near real time alongside other tracked assets.

- Real time location and telemetry streamed to Plaspy for continuous visibility.
- Live Tracking and manual position requests appear on Plaspy maps and can be used for immediate location checks.
- Geo fence and alerting support for safe zone entry and exit notifications through Plaspy.
- Activity timelines and location history available in Plaspy for route review and behavioral context.
- Battery and device status alerts surfaced in Plaspy for operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Locating a lost or roaming pet with rapid live tracking updates.
- Monitoring daily routes and range to understand a pet’s typical territory.
- Configuring safe zones to receive notifications when a pet leaves or returns.
- Reviewing activity timelines to track exercise and movement patterns.
- Using Bluetooth home beacon detection to automatically mark home arrivals and reduce unnecessary alerts.
