# VisionTek - 87VTU Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the VisionTek 87VTU and explains how its capabilities relate to use with the Plaspy fleet tracking platform. It focuses on practical, non sensitive descriptions of what the device is designed to do and how those functions typically appear when used with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration steps and the most current technical details consult official VisionTek documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The VisionTek 87VTU is a compact vehicle tracking unit that combines GPS positioning with quad band cellular connectivity and onboard I O to support a range of tracking and monitoring workflows. It is intended for applications where reliable position reporting, local logging, and remote management are required.

- Real time GPS position reporting for vehicle location and direction
- Cellular connectivity for live tracking plus SMS fallback for key events
- Local storage and store and forward to preserve history during network gaps
- 3D motion sensor to minimize unnecessary updates and conserve power
- Remote commands and over the air firmware updates for field management

## Core Features of VisionTek - 87VTU

- Built in GPS receiver engine for continuous position and time data
- Quad band GSM modem for broad cellular coverage and SMS capability
- Onboard flash memory for local data logging and store and forward history
- USB port for firmware upload, device configuration, and log capture
- 3D motion sensor to detect sleep and motion states and reduce transmissions
- Tamper switch and status LEDs for basic device health and tamper indication
- Digital and analog inputs and digital outputs plus serial port for external integrations
- OTA commands and remote firmware update support where implemented by firmware

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can accept and display the positional and event data that the 87VTU transmits, and supports common device management workflows so tracking units like the 87VTU can be monitored and managed centrally.

- Location and heading reported by the 87VTU appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and playback
- Store and forward history logged by the device can be uploaded and shown in Plaspy timeline and trip history
- Motion state information helps Plaspy reduce noise from stationary assets and focus on meaningful movement events
- Tamper indications and LED status interpreted as device health signals in the Plaspy device status view
- Remote commands and OTA workflows supported by the device can be coordinated from Plaspy where remote management is enabled

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for delivery vehicles, service fleets, and light commercial vehicles
- Taxi and cab tracking to monitor routes, dispatch, and trip history
- Ambulance and emergency vehicle location visibility for operational coordination
- Logistics and cargo tracking where reliable position and history are required
- School transport and institutional vehicle monitoring
- Marine applications for small boats and near shore craft where compact trackers are preferred

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features such as geofence behavior, OTA update methods, and store and forward parameters depend on device firmware and may change between firmware releases
- Hardware revisions and installation choices, such as using the internal versus external antennas, can affect signal performance and GPS fix quality
- Regional cellular band support and SIM configuration can affect connectivity in different markets
- Certain functions require both appropriate firmware on the device and compatible support in the back end; confirm Plaspy feature support for specific device firmware versions
- For the most accurate and current technical details consult VisionTek documentation and firmware release notes

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the VisionTek 87VTU with Plaspy provides a centralized way to view location, history, and device health across an entire fleet. Plaspy presents positional data on maps, preserves historical trips logged by the device, and surfaces device status so operations teams can monitor vehicles and respond to incidents more quickly.

Plaspy also streamlines device onboarding and monitoring by automatically detecting many tracker protocols and exposing common device functions in a consistent interface. To learn more about Plaspy and how it supports GPS trackers like the VisionTek 87VTU visit https://www.plaspy.com. For the latest device specifications, firmware behavior, and manufacturer guidance verify details on the official VisionTek website at http://www.visiontek.co.in/.
