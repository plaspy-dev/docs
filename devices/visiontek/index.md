# VisionTek

VisionTek produces hardware used in GPS tracking and telematics for vehicles and mobile assets. This brand page explains how VisionTek devices can be used with Plaspy, what to expect from the hardware in general terms, and how to choose the right device for your tracking needs. Use the visual catalog below to review compatible models and navigate to specific device documentation.

## How VisionTek devices work with Plaspy

VisionTek GPS devices typically report location and sensor data over cellular networks. Plaspy receives and interprets those reports so you can view live location, history, and events in a single fleet management platform. Integration usually involves configuring the device to send telemetry to Plaspy using standard tracking protocols and the device's built in reporting options. Once connected, devices appear in your Plaspy account and can be managed alongside other compatible hardware.

Key integration points:
- Device reporting is processed by Plaspy to show positions, trips, and status on maps.
- Inputs and sensor readings forwarded by VisionTek devices are mapped to Plaspy telemetry fields for alerts and reports.
- Plaspy centralizes device configuration, alerting, and history for easier fleet operations.

## Common features found in VisionTek devices

While specific features vary by model, VisionTek devices compatible with Plaspy commonly provide:
- GPS positioning and time stamped location data
- Cellular connectivity for real time tracking and telemetry
- Digital and analog inputs for external sensors and vehicle signals
- Configurable reporting intervals and power management options
- Support for event reporting used by Plaspy to trigger alerts and rules

## Typical use cases for VisionTek GPS trackers

VisionTek devices are suited for many common tracking scenarios, including:
- Vehicle fleet tracking for route monitoring and driver accountability
- Asset tracking for high value equipment that requires periodic location updates
- Mobile workforce coordination where location and status updates improve dispatch
- Basic telematics where input signals (for example ignition or door status) inform operational decisions

Plaspy organizes incoming data from VisionTek devices into visual maps, reports, and alerts to support these use cases without forcing you to manage multiple tools.

## Why choose Plaspy for VisionTek device monitoring

Plaspy is designed to work with a wide range of GPS hardware, and it adds value when monitoring VisionTek equipment by:
- Consolidating devices and data into a single dashboard for easier fleet oversight
- Providing configurable alerts and geofencing to respond to events promptly
- Offering reporting and history tools that help analyze usage and optimize operations
- Supporting APIs and integrations to feed location data into other business systems

Choosing Plaspy lets your operations team focus on insights from tracking data rather than device management overhead.

## Help finding the right VisionTek device

Selecting the right VisionTek device depends on your installation and operational needs. Consider these factors before choosing:
- Power and mounting options: hardwired vehicle units vs battery powered asset trackers
- Reporting frequency and battery life trade offs for mobile assets
- Required inputs and sensor compatibility for vehicle telematics
- Coverage and cellular bands supported in your deployment region
