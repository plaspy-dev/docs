# Ruptela - Pro5 Lite

![Pro5 Lite](./tracker.png)

**Ruptela Pro5 Lite**

Pro5 Lite is a compact, future‑proof GPS tracker designed for commercial fleets and asset operators who require reliable, Plaspy compatible real‑time tracking. Built around a U‑blox GNSS module with an internal antenna and LTE connectivity \(with 2G fallback\), the Pro5 Lite delivers high‑precision location and rich telemetry for fleet management, cargo protection and driver identification. Its small footprint and ergonomic screwless housing make it simple to install in concealed or OBD locations while retaining a full set of vehicle interfaces.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, Pro5 Lite integrates seamlessly with Plaspy’s platform to provide live position updates, flexible telemetry streams and secure data delivery. Robust security features — including tamper and jamming detection and TLS v1.2 — protect data in transit, while BLE 5.0, CAN bus support and multiple I/O let you tailor telemetry, anti‑theft workflows and driver communication to your operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: native support for real‑time tracking and telematics integration with Plaspy.
- High‑precision GNSS: U‑blox module with internal antenna for consistent positioning in urban and highway environments.
- LTE connectivity with 2G fallback and region‑specific variants \(e.g., Pro5‑Lite‑LTE‑LA\) for global fleet coverage.
- Extensive vehicle integration: dual CAN \(FMS, J1939, HCV/LCV CAN, OBD\) plus CustomCAN for OEM parameters.
- Comprehensive I/O and interfaces \(3×DIN, 2×AIN, 4×DOUT, 1‑wire, RS232, RS485\) to support ignition detection, sensors and immobilizer workflows.
- Built‑in security and resilience: tamper/jamming detection, TLS v1.2, and 1050 mAh internal backup battery for continued operation.
- BLE 5.0 for Bluetooth sensors and accessory pairing, plus a 3.5 mm audio jack for driver communication.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Installing Pro5 Lite and connecting it to Plaspy provides a turnkey telematics data stream that powers dashboards, alerts and automated reports. Plaspy ingests GNSS and CAN telemetry from the device, presenting vehicle location, status and events in real time. Integration supports fleet operators who need granular vehicle diagnostics, behavioral analytics and anti‑theft monitoring.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates: GNSS fixes and LTE uplink feed position data into Plaspy for live monitoring and historical replay.
- CAN bus telemetry: FMS, J1939 and CustomCAN provide engine parameters, odometer, RPM, fuel level and fault codes for fleet management and fuel monitoring.
- Accelerometer events: the internal 3‑axis accelerometer enables motion detection, crash alerts and harsh driving reports within Plaspy.
- Digital inputs/outputs: DIN and DOUT interfaces allow ignition status monitoring, door/alarm event detection and integration with immobilizer workflows.
- Bluetooth sensors: BLE 5.0 supports Bluetooth sensors and beacons for temperature, cargo status or driver identification accessories.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and optimization: combine GNSS and CAN data in Plaspy to reduce idle time, optimize routes and track vehicle health.
- Anti‑theft and stolen vehicle recovery: tamper/jamming alerts, live tracking and remote cut‑off workflows using DOUT outputs for immobilizer integration.
- Fuel monitoring and telemetry: FMS/J1939 and CustomCAN data enable accurate fuel monitoring, consumption analysis and cost control.
- Driver behaviour & safety: accelerometer‑based harsh braking and crash detection feed Plaspy alerts and driver scoring modules.
- Cargo & accessory monitoring: BLE sensors and internal memory support temperature, door and cargo status logging for compliance and insurance needs.
