# Ruptela - Pro5 Lite

![Pro5 Lite](./tracker.png)

Pro5 Lite is a compact GPS tracker from Ruptela designed for commercial fleets and asset operators who require reliable real time tracking. Built around a U‑blox GNSS module with an internal antenna and LTE connectivity with 2G fallback, the device delivers precise location and telemetry in a small, screwless housing suitable for concealed or OBD locations. The Pro5 Lite is intended to provide consistent positioning and flexible vehicle integration for fleet, cargo and driver management.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, Pro5 Lite integrates with Plaspy to deliver live position updates, telemetry streams and secure data delivery for monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can ingest the Pro5 Lite data to present vehicle locations, status and events on dashboards and in alerts, making the device a practical choice for operators who want a compact tracker that works with a modern fleet management platform.

## Key Highlights

- Native Plaspy compatibility for live tracking and telematics integration.
- High precision GNSS positioning with an internal antenna for consistent coverage.
- LTE connectivity with 2G fallback and region specific variants for broad coverage.
- Broad vehicle integration capabilities and multiple I O options to support ignition, sensors and immobilizer workflows.
- Built in security features including tamper and jamming detection and secure data transmission.
- Compact screwless housing and internal backup battery to help maintain operation and enable discreet installation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, Pro5 Lite provides a continuous stream of location and telemetry that powers monitoring, alerts and reporting across the fleet. Plaspy presents device data in real time and retains historical records for replay and analysis, enabling operational oversight and decision making.

- Live position reporting for real time vehicle tracking and map views in Plaspy.
- Telemetry and vehicle data feeding dashboards and reports for fleet health and utilization.
- Event and alert delivery for tamper, motion and other configured conditions to support anti theft and safety workflows.
- I O driven status updates for ignition, door or alarm conditions to reflect vehicle state in Plaspy.
- Support for Bluetooth accessories and onboard telemetry to extend monitoring for cargo or driver related use cases.

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet tracking and route monitoring to improve utilization and dispatch.
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows using live tracking and tamper alerts.
- Fuel and engine parameter monitoring as part of fleet diagnostics and cost control.
- Driver behaviour and safety monitoring using motion and event based alerts.
- Cargo and accessory monitoring with Bluetooth sensor integration for status logging.
