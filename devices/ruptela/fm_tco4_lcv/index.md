# Ruptela - FM-Tco4 LCV

![FM-Tco4 LCV](./logo.jpg)

The Ruptela FM-Tco4 LCV is a GPS tracker designed specifically for tracking and monitoring light commercial vehicles such as vans and passenger cars. With its ability to read LCV on-board computer \(CANbus\) data, this device provides valuable information about the vehicle's location, route, speed, mileage, and more. One of the standout features of the FM-Tco4 LCV is its ability to gather fuel level and consumption information directly from the vehicle's on-board computer, eliminating the need for additional fuel level sensors. This makes it easier to monitor fuel levels and control fuel consumption, allowing for better fuel management and cost control.

In addition to its tracking and monitoring capabilities, the FM-Tco4 LCV offers a range of features that make it an ideal solution for fleet management and control. It can read OBD II engine error codes, providing early detection of possible engine failures and allowing for preventive maintenance to avoid breakdowns. The device also supports driver registration and identification, enabling you to track and monitor who is responsible for the vehicle at any given time. For added security, the FM-Tco4 LCV offers remote ignition blocking, helping to prevent theft and unauthorized use of the vehicle.

With its comprehensive set of features and capabilities, the Ruptela FM-Tco4 LCV is a powerful tool for optimizing fleet operations, improving fuel management, implementing eco-driving practices, and enhancing overall vehicle security. Whether you need to monitor your fleet, optimize routes, control fuel consumption, or ensure driver accountability, the FM-Tco4 LCV provides the functionality and reliability you need to effectively manage your light commercial vehicles.

### Key Features:

- Driver behaviour monitoring \(Eco-Drive\)
- On-board computer data reading \(CANbus LCV\)
- OBD II engine error codes reading
- Driver registration and identification
- Remote ignition blocking
- Fuel monitoring
- Internal Geozones
- Antijamming
- Various features via SMS
