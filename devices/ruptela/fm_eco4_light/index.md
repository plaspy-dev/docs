# Ruptela - FM-ECO4 Light

![FM-ECO4 Light](./logo.jpg)

The Ruptela FM-ECO4 Light is a compact and reliable GPS tracking device designed for monitoring and tracking vehicles. It offers a wide range of features and functionalities that make it an ideal choice for fleet monitoring and control, route optimization, fuel management, eco-driving, and temperature control.

One of the standout features of the FM-ECO4 Light is its small size and easy installation. Equipped with an internal GPS and GSM antenna, this device can be easily fitted anywhere in the vehicle, making the installer's job much easier. Despite its compact size, it offers robust tracking capabilities, allowing you to monitor the vehicle's location, speed, route, fuel level, mileage, and other activity parameters.

With the FM-ECO4 Light, you can also monitor driver behavior and promote eco-driving practices. The device allows for driver registration and identification, making it easier to track individual drivers and their performance. It also offers temperature monitoring, which is particularly useful for industries that require strict temperature control, such as food transportation.

Other notable features of the FM-ECO4 Light include remote ignition blocking, internal geozones for setting up virtual boundaries, and anti-jamming capabilities to ensure reliable and uninterrupted tracking. Overall, the Ruptela FM-ECO4 Light is a powerful and versatile GPS tracking device that offers a range of features to help you effectively monitor and manage your fleet.
