# Ruptela - FM-Eco4 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the Ruptela FM-Eco4 and how its capabilities are reflected when used with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical functions you can expect from the device for fleet monitoring, route oversight, fuel and mileage reporting, and driver-related features while connected to the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, vehicle installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, this page notes distinctions such as the FM-Eco4+ variant that includes an integrated battery and encourages verification against manufacturer documentation for device-specific behavior.

## Feature Overview

The FM-Eco4 is designed as a robust tracker for standard vehicle monitoring and fleet control. It focuses on reliable position and trip reporting combined with operational features that support fuel management, driver oversight, and installation in demanding environments.

- Real-time vehicle location, speed, route, and trip history for operational visibility.
- Fuel level and mileage tracking to support consumption analysis and reporting.
- Eco-Drive driver behavior monitoring for coachable driving practices and efficiency.
- Rugged IP67 dust and waterproof housing suitable for harsh vehicle environments.
- Additional functionality such as driver identification, internal geozones, antijamming, remote ignition blocking, and SMS-accessible features.

## Core Features of Ruptela - FM-Eco4

- Vehicle location and trip data including position, route, speed, and mileage reporting.
- Fuel level reporting and basic fuel management information.
- Eco-Drive driver behavior monitoring to support greener and safer driving.
- Driver registration and identification for driver assignment and accountability.
- Temperature monitoring functionality reported by the device.
- Remote ignition blocking capability for authorized immobilization actions.
- Internal geozones for simple location-based eventing.
- Rugged IP67 housing and optional integrated battery in the FM-Eco4+ model for continued reporting after power loss.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the FM-Eco4 is connected to Plaspy, device events and telemetry are presented through Plaspy’s tracking and reporting interfaces to help operations teams monitor fleets and act on important signals.

- Real-time position and speed are displayed on Plaspy maps and live monitoring views for route oversight.
- Trip history and mileage feed into Plaspy reports for activity summaries and operational analysis.
- Fuel level and consumption information appear in fuel reports and dashboards to support cost controls.
- Eco-Drive and driver identification events are available in Plaspy for reviewing driver performance and assigning responsibility.
- Temperature, antijamming, geozone, and remote ignition blocking events are surfaced as events or alerts so operators can respond to condition changes.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible device protocols so reporting can be consolidated without manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet route and order optimization using real-time location and trip history.
- Monitoring and managing fuel usage and mileage across a vehicle fleet.
- Encouraging eco friendly driving habits through Eco-Drive monitoring and reporting.
- Assigning and auditing drivers for trips using driver identification features.
- Protecting cargo or vehicles operating in harsh outdoor conditions thanks to IP67 housing.
- Responding to security events with remote ignition blocking and antijamming alerts.
- Monitoring temperature-sensitive loads when temperature reporting is enabled.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature support can depend on the device firmware version and hardware variant; the FM-Eco4+ includes an integrated battery not present on the base model.
- Some functions require specific installation wiring or vehicle interfaces and may vary by installer or vehicle type.
- SMS accessible features depend on cellular and SMS availability in the operating region.
- Antijamming and remote blocking behaviors are influenced by firmware and local regulatory constraints.
- Always confirm specific capabilities and configuration steps in the manufacturer documentation for your device revision.
