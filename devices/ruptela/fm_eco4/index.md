# Ruptela - FM-Eco4

![FM-Eco4](./logo.jpg)

The Ruptela FM-Eco4 is a reliable GPS tracker built for standard vehicle tracking and monitoring tasks. It captures essential vehicle information such as location, speed, route, fuel level, and mileage, and includes features for driver identification, temperature monitoring, driver behavior analysis, and security actions like remote ignition blocking. The unit is designed with a rugged dust and waterproof housing and is offered in a variant with an integrated battery to maintain data transmission after power loss.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FM-Eco4 can feed its telemetry and events into Plaspy for centralized fleet visibility and operations. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker’s core capabilities support route and order optimization, fuel control, driver behavior monitoring for eco driving, and operational oversight across a mixed fleet. This combination helps organizations monitor vehicles in demanding environments while benefiting from Plaspy reporting and management tools.

## Key Highlights

- Provides core vehicle telemetry including location speed route fuel level and mileage
- Driver behavior monitoring and Eco Drive features to support safer more efficient driving
- Rugged IP67 dust and waterproof housing for use in harsh environments
- FM-Eco4 plus version includes an integrated battery for continued reporting after power loss
- Built in features for driver identification registration and internal geozones
- Security oriented functions such as remote ignition blocking antijamming and SMS based controls

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the FM-Eco4 telemetry and events so fleet managers can view live status and historical performance in a unified interface. Integration enables operational monitoring and reporting without exposing device level configuration details.

- Live and historical location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps and timelines
- Fleet monitoring dashboards showing mileage fuel consumption trends and route efficiency
- Alerts and notifications for events such as geofence crossings temperature thresholds and ignition changes
- Driver activity and behavior reports to support coaching and compliance efforts
- Event logging for security incidents and device reported antijamming or tamper signals
- Inclusion of FM-Eco4 data in operational reports and exportable logs for analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring mixed vehicle fleets for location routing and mileage control
- Fuel consumption tracking and route optimization to reduce operating costs
- Driver behavior and Eco Drive programs to improve efficiency and safety
- Operations in harsh environments where IP67 ruggedization is needed
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring and alerting when required
