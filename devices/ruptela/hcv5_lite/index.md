# Ruptela - HCV5 Lite

![HCV5 Lite](./logo..png)

The Ruptela HCV5 Lite is a premium GPS tracker built for heavy and light commercial vehicle tracking. Designed with commercial fleets in mind, the HCV5 Lite offers versatile connectivity, multiple inputs and outputs, and broad vehicle coverage that supports common vehicle data sources. Built-in GNSS positioning and durability features make it suitable for continuous fleet operations where reliable location and operational oversight are required.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the HCV5 Lite can feed vehicle location and operational data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet monitoring, alerts, and reporting. Its support for accessory interfaces, vehicle parameter coverage, and security features like jamming detection make it a practical choice for organizations that want to consolidate tracking hardware with Plaspy based fleet management workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for heavy and light commercial vehicle tracking, suitable for mixed fleets
- Multiple inputs and outputs plus accessory interfaces for flexible vehicle integrations
- BLE 5.0 support for local connectivity with compatible accessories and tools
- Premium GNSS positioning technology by U-blox for reliable location data
- Security features such as jamming detection and a backup battery for continued operation
- Compact, ergonomic design with screwless housing and installation aids for faster deployment
- Broad vehicle coverage including support for common OBD and CANbus parameters

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the HCV5 Lite delivers vehicle location and status information to the platform so fleet managers can monitor operations in one place. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it available in dashboards, alerts, and reports that support everyday fleet management tasks.

- Real time and near real time location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps
- Configurable alerts and notifications based on movement, security events, or accessory inputs
- Historical reporting and trip playback for route analysis and operational review
- Vehicle metrics from supported OBD and CANbus parameters available for trend monitoring
- Mapping of accessory inputs and outputs into Plaspy events to reflect vehicle behaviors
- Fleet level visibility that combines HCV5 Lite data with other devices in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring mixed fleets of heavy trucks and light commercial vans for location and utilization
- Detecting and reporting jamming or tampering attempts as part of vehicle security processes
- Using vehicle parameter data to support maintenance planning and operational analysis
- Concealed or streamlined installations for rental, lease, or high value vehicle tracking
- Integrating accessory inputs to track door status, PTO usage, or other operational events

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The HCV5 Lite is a practical choice for organizations seeking a compact but capable tracker that covers a wide range of commercial vehicles. Its combination of reliable GNSS positioning, security features, and flexible connectivity options makes it well suited to feed the kind of location and vehicle data Plaspy uses for monitoring, alerting, and reporting.

Plaspy benefits from the HCV5 Lite's broad vehicle support and accessory interfaces, enabling fleet managers to consolidate telematics and operational oversight in a single platform. If you need a device that can adapt across vehicle types while delivering the core data Plaspy requires for fleet management, the HCV5 Lite is worth considering.
