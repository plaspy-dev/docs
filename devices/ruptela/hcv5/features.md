# Ruptela - HCV5 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Ruptela HCV5 and how its capabilities are presented and used with Plaspy for fleet management and asset tracking. It focuses on non-sensitive, operational functions that matter to fleet operators and integrators when the HCV5 is used as part of a Plaspy deployment.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and the vehicle or accessory environment. Where a capability depends on vehicle support or optional accessories, that dependency is noted and users should consult manufacturer documentation for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The HCV5 is a compact professional telematics unit designed for commercial fleet use and validated for compatibility with Plaspy. It combines continuous GNSS positioning, multi network cellular connectivity, vehicle bus access and a range of I O and expansion options so operators can collect location and vehicle telemetry for live monitoring, reporting and event driven workflows in Plaspy.

- Continuous GNSS positioning using a premium u blox receiver for reliable location tracking across urban and rural environments.
- Multi network cellular connectivity with LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT and 2G fallback to maintain telemetry delivery in variable coverage areas.
- Direct vehicle data capture via dual CAN and K line OBD for fuel and engine parameters and support for tachograph related workflows where available.
- BLE 5.1 support for short range sensors and driver ID tags that can be integrated into Plaspy reporting and alerts.
- Tamper and jamming detection plus an internal backup battery to preserve tracking and event logs during power interruptions.
- Extensive I O and serial interfaces for inputs outputs sensor integration and accessory connections.

## Core Features of Ruptela - HCV5

- Premium GNSS positioning module for accurate and continuous location reporting.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT connectivity with 2G fallback for resilient telemetry transmission.
- Dual CAN bus plus K line OBD access for rich vehicle telemetry and diagnostics.
- Bluetooth Low Energy 5.1 for external sensors and short range device pairing.
- Tamper detection and jamming detection for improved asset security.
- Multiple digital and analog inputs and configurable digital outputs for event capture and control.
- Internal backup battery to support operation during external power loss.
- Remote management features including firmware update capabilities for large deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an HCV5 device is deployed and linked to a Plaspy account, its location and vehicle telemetry are collected and presented through Plaspy dashboards, maps and reports. Plaspy ingests supported GNSS and vehicle bus data and makes event driven inputs accessible to fleet managers for monitoring and analysis.

- Live map tracking and historical routes based on continuous GNSS updates sent by the device.
- Vehicle bus parameters from CAN and OBD reflected in Plaspy dashboards for fuel monitoring, engine status indicators and reporting where the vehicle exposes those signals.
- Event inputs such as ignition, door or sensor triggers shown as events and timelines inside Plaspy for faster incident investigation.
- BLE sensor or driver tag data included in asset reports and condition monitoring when paired with the HCV5 and provided to Plaspy.
- Remote configuration and firmware management workflows supported when device management is enabled alongside Plaspy monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time fleet tracking and route visibility for commercial vehicle operations.
- Fuel and engine parameter monitoring for cost control and preventive maintenance planning.
- Remote tachograph download and driver identification workflows where supported by vehicle and regulator processes.
- Stolen vehicle recovery and anti theft monitoring using tamper alerts and backup power features.
- Cargo condition and refrigeration monitoring with BLE sensors integrated into telemetry reports.
- Mixed fleet and trailer deployments consolidating vehicle and trailer telemetry under one Plaspy account.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features depend on firmware level and may be added or refined in later releases by the manufacturer.
- Vehicle compatibility affects access to CAN and OBD parameters and any tachograph related capabilities.
- Regional hardware variants and radio band support differ by market; consult product documentation for the correct variant.
- Installation choices such as concealed mounting or harness selection influence which I O and accessory functions are available.
- BLE and certain peripheral features require compatible accessories and may need configuration in Plaspy to appear in reports.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the Ruptela HCV5 with Plaspy gives operators unified visibility into location and vehicle telemetry, enabling real time monitoring, actionable alerts and consolidated reporting across mixed fleets. The HCV5 delivers the raw vehicle data and secure connectivity while Plaspy provides the visualization, event handling and historical analysis that fleet teams use to improve operations and security.
