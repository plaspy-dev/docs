# Ruptela

Ruptela is a recognizable name in the telematics industry offering a range of vehicle and asset tracking devices. This page explains how Ruptela devices work with Plaspy, what to expect from this brand class, and how to find the right device model for your fleet or assets. If you are evaluating hardware for integration with Plaspy, this guide will help you understand compatibility and next steps.

## How Ruptela devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives tracking data from Ruptela devices using common telematics data flows and network transport methods. Once a device is configured to forward its telemetry, Plaspy can visualize location, manage alerts, and process vehicle signals alongside other fleet data. Typical setup steps like registering device IDs, selecting the correct protocol profile in the Plaspy platform, and setting reporting intervals are handled on device or in your Plaspy account configuration. For device specific instructions consult the device entry in the catalog below or the manufacturer documentation at https://ruptela.com/.

## Common features found in Ruptela devices

Ruptela devices are used in a wide range of telematics applications and tend to provide the capabilities required by modern fleet operations. Common capabilities you will encounter include:
- Accurate GPS based positioning and time stamped location reports
- Cellular data connectivity to forward telemetry to a cloud platform
- Vehicle interfaces for reading CAN or serial vehicle bus data
- Digital and analog inputs for sensors such as fuel level and door status
- Event detection features like ignition and motion reporting
