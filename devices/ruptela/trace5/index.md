# Ruptela - Trace5

![Trace5](./logo..png)

The Ruptela Trace5 is a compact GNSS-based vehicle tracker that is specifically designed for efficient vehicle tracking and fleet management. With its 4G connectivity and premium GNSS module, this tracker ensures reliable and accurate data transmission, making it ideal for a wide range of applications including logistics, delivery services, and utility services.

One of the standout features of the Trace5 is its rugged IP67-rated housing, which provides complete protection against water, dirt, and mechanical factors. This allows the device to withstand harsh environments and be installed outside the cabin, near the engine, without compromising its performance and functionality.

The Trace5 offers several key values that make it an excellent choice for fleet management. It boasts excellent performance thanks to its premium GNSS module and high-quality components. Additionally, it is easy to install and use, with its small size, ergonomic design, screwless housing, and dedicated tools such as the device center and installation assistant. The tracker also offers wireless solutions, concealed OBD installation, and tailored harnesses for added convenience.

Security is a top priority with the Trace5, as it features jamming detection, a back-up battery, and TLS v1.2 encryption \(except for the Trace5-LTE version\). This ensures that your data is secure and protected at all times. Furthermore, the Trace5 is a full solution from one supplier, combining the GPS tracking device with suitable accessories for a comprehensive fleet management solution.
