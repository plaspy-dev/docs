# Ruptela - Trace5 Trailer Tracker

![Trace5 Trailer Tracker](./logo..png)

The Ruptela Trace5 Trailer Tracker is more than just a basic tracking device. It is equipped with the newest rechargeable, long-lasting battery edition, ensuring that you can rely on it for extended periods of time. This compact GNSS-based Automatic Vehicle Location \(AVL\) tracker is designed for vehicle and trailer tracking, as well as fleet management. It utilizes GNSS signals to acquire precise location data and transfers it to a server through cellular connectivity options, including LTE Cat M1, NB-IoT, or GSM networks.

One of the key values of the Trace5 Trailer Tracker is its excellent performance. It features premium GNSS technology by U-blox and high-quality components, ensuring reliable and accurate tracking. Additionally, the housing of the tracker is IP67 rated, providing outstanding resistance to harsh environments. This means that the tracker can survive in challenging conditions, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.

Another standout feature of the Trace5 Trailer Tracker is its long-lasting internal battery. With a 5-hour reporting period, the tracker can last up to 36 days on a single charge. This ensures that you can track your vehicles and trailers without worrying about frequent battery replacements. The tracker also offers enhanced security features, including jamming detection, a back-up battery, and TLS v1.2 encryption.

With the Trace5 Trailer Tracker, you get a full solution from one supplier. It is a GPS tracking device that can be combined with suitable accessories to meet your specific tracking needs. Whether you need to track trailers, vehicles, or manage your fleet, the Trace5 Trailer Tracker offers the features and reliability you need.
