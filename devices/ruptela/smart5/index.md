# Ruptela - Smart5

![Smart5](./tracker.png)

Smart5 is a compact, hard‑wearing GPS tracker designed for robust fleet management and deep vehicle-data integration. Built with LTE Cat 1 \(4G\) and 2G fallback, Smart5 delivers reliable real-time tracking and telemetry across broad coverage areas and pairs seamlessly with Plaspy to provide live maps, alerts and fleet dashboards. Its ability to read CANbus and OBD data \(including CustomCAN\) makes Smart5 especially useful for fleets that need diagnostic insights, fuel monitoring and driver‑behavior analytics.

Available in a slim IP54 housing \(14 mm\) or a rugged waterproof IP68 body \(21 mm\), Smart5 supports Bluetooth LE 5.0 for wireless sensors and accessories, a Swiss‑made u‑blox GNSS module for multi‑constellation positioning, and a set of vehicle I/O for integrations. When used with Plaspy, Smart5 becomes a Plaspy compatible solution for anti‑theft protection, telemetry-driven maintenance and efficient fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE Cat 1 \(4G\) and 2G fallback for consistent real‑time tracking and fleet management.
- CANbus and OBD data reading, including CustomCAN support, to capture vehicle diagnostics, fuel metrics and ignition status.
- Two form factors — slim IP54 \(14 mm\) for discreet installs and rugged IP68 \(21 mm\) for harsh environments.
- Bluetooth LE 5.0 for connecting wireless sensors and beacons to monitor cargo condition and auxiliary telemetry.
- Security features such as jamming detection and power disruption alerts to support anti‑theft workflows in Plaspy.
- Low‑power operation \(\<0.5 mA in deep sleep\) and an internal 180 mAh backup battery for offline reporting and resilience.
- Broad voltage support \(9–32 V DC\) and a 3‑axis accelerometer for driver behavior and eco‑driving insights.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Smart5 streams location and vehicle telemetry into Plaspy using its cellular link and buffered internal memory. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes, CAN/OBD frames, accelerometer events, and Bluetooth sensor readings to generate live maps, geofence alerts, trip reports and telemetry dashboards. Integration is straightforward: Smart5 sends standardized tracking messages and diagnostic records that Plaspy uses for real‑time tracking, historical reporting and automated alerts.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates \(multi‑constellation GNSS: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo\).
- Vehicle diagnostics via CANbus/OBD and CustomCAN for ignition status, engine data and fault codes.
- Fuel monitoring from CAN/OBD and analog inputs to feed Plaspy fuel reports and consumption analytics.
- Remote immobilizer capability using configurable digital outputs \(DOUTs\) when deployed in immobilization workflows.
- Bluetooth sensors support for temperature, humidity or door sensors to track cargo conditions alongside location.
- Security events — jamming detection and power disruption alerts — appear in Plaspy as immediate notifications for anti‑theft response.
- Local buffering \(8 MB, ~36,000 records\) ensures data continuity and upload to Plaspy after temporary connectivity loss.
