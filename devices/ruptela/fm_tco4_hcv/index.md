# Ruptela - FM-Tco4 HCV

![FM-Tco4 HCV](./logo.jpg)

The Ruptela FM-Tco4 HCV is a highly advanced GPS tracker designed specifically for modern trucks and other transportation equipment. With its cutting-edge technology, this tracker collects data and provides valuable insights to help optimize fleet monitoring and control. It is the perfect solution for businesses looking to improve route and order optimization, fuel control and management, remote tachograph data reading and downloading, eco-drive monitoring, and temperature control.

One of the standout features of the FM-Tco4 HCV is its ability to read vehicles' on-board computer \(CANbus\) data in J1708 and FMS standards. This means that in addition to basic tracking information, users can access comprehensive data about the vehicle's status. The device is also equipped with Dual Can functionality, allowing users to obtain data from the vehicle's CANbus and digital tachographs simultaneously.

In terms of connectivity, the FM-Tco4 HCV offers RS232 and RS485 ports that can work simultaneously, as well as a 1-Wire interface for connecting a wide range of additional accessories for vehicle safety and driver control. The device also monitors ThermoKing, Optitemp, and Carrier refrigerators data, making it a versatile and comprehensive solution for fleet management.

With its advanced telematics technologies, the FM-Tco4 HCV enables businesses to implement a wide range of fleet management tasks, resulting in absolute control over the fleet, reduced operating costs, and increased efficiency. From driver behavior monitoring to remote ignition blocking, fuel monitoring, and internal geozones, this GPS tracker offers a host of features that can be accessed via SMS for added convenience.

Overall, the Ruptela FM-Tco4 HCV is a powerful and reliable GPS tracker that provides businesses with the tools they need to effectively manage their fleets and optimize their operations. With its advanced features and comprehensive data collection capabilities, it is an invaluable asset for any transportation company.
