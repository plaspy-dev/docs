# CalmAmp - LMU-1000 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the CalmAmp LMU-1000 and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet and asset tracking. The LMU-1000 is positioned as an economy class tracking unit developed for insurance and sub-prime lending markets, and this document highlights the practical functions available to Plaspy users without going into private configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Refer to CalAmp documentation for device specific implementation notes and confirm how a particular LMU-1000 is configured before planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The LMU-1000 is designed to deliver reliable, low cost track and trace functionality with simplified installation and basic power resilience. It focuses on core location reporting and continuous tracking suitability for recovery and asset management use cases.

- Economical vehicle and asset tracking for cost sensitive deployments
- Track and trace reporting suitable for vehicle recovery workflows
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas to simplify installation
- Built in backup battery to continue reporting during primary power loss
- Sleep mode to conserve power during long idle periods
- Durable plastic casing for everyday operational durability

## Core Features of CalmAmp - LMU-1000

- Economy class GPS tracking capability tailored for asset management
- Track and trace functionality useful for locating and recovering vehicles
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas that reduce installation complexity
- Backup battery allowing continued operation during main power interruptions
- Sleep mode to lower power consumption when the unit is inactive
- Plastic enclosure designed for everyday durability
- Customizable configuration options to adapt behavior to different applications
- Intended market focus on insurance and sub prime lending requirements

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and displays the LMU-1000 position and status information to provide visibility into asset movement, uptime, and basic event history. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and surfaces the device data in monitoring views so operators can act on location and availability information.

- Device location and status appear in Plaspy maps and device lists for operational awareness
- Sleep mode behavior is reflected as reduced reporting frequency or periodic check ins
- Backup battery events can be surfaced as part of device online status and uptime history
- Internal antenna design reduces installation variability, helping rapid commissioning into Plaspy
- Plaspy can accept reports from compatible LMU-1000 units and integrate them into fleet workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Economical vehicle tracking programs for insurance and sub prime lending portfolios
- Track and trace for vehicle recovery operations after theft or repossession events
- Low cost fleet oversight where practical location reporting and uptime visibility are primary needs
- Deployments that prefer simplified installation using internal antennas
- Situations that benefit from basic power resilience through a backup battery
