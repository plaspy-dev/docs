# CalmAmp - LMU-1000

![LMU-1000](./logo.jpg)

The CalAmp LMU-1000 is an economy class GPS tracking unit built to deliver cost effective asset management for insurance and sub prime lending markets. It provides track and trace functionality and vehicle recovery support in a compact, durable plastic housing. The unit includes internal GPS and GSM antennas, a backup battery to maintain location reporting during power loss, and a sleep mode that conserves power when the device is idle. It does not include voice capability and is designed for straightforward, reliable tracking use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-1000 can be used within Plaspy to provide location visibility and operational oversight for fleets and collateral management portfolios. Its economy positioning and configurable options make it suitable for organizations that need practical tracking and recovery features without complex hardware overhead. When paired with Plaspy, the LMU-1000 helps translate raw location data into actionable monitoring, alerts, and reporting for business workflows.

When used with Plaspy, the LMU-1000 becomes a reporting endpoint that feeds location and status information into a centralized fleet management environment. Plaspy can use the device feed to provide situational awareness, alerting, and historical reporting that support recovery and asset oversight.

- Conserves device power via sleep mode while still allowing Plaspy to receive updates when active
