# CalmAmp - LMU-1000

![LMU-1000](./logo.jpg)

The CalAmp LMU-1000 is an economy class tracking unit designed to provide economical asset management solutions for the insurance and sub-prime lending markets. CalAmp understands the complexities of these industries and has developed the LMU-1000 to meet their specific requirements. This GPS tracker offers a range of features and can be fully customized to suit any application.

The LMU-1000 is a reliable and efficient tracking unit that is ideal for track and trace applications as well as vehicle recovery. It does not have voice capabilities but comes equipped with a backup battery, allowing for uninterrupted tracking even in the event of a power failure. While it does not have internal memory, it does offer sleep mode functionality, conserving power when the unit is not in use.

The LMU-1000 features internal GPS and GSM antennas, eliminating the need for external antennas and simplifying installation. The casing is made of durable plastic, ensuring the tracker can withstand the rigors of daily use. Designed and manufactured in the USA, the CalAmp LMU-1000 is a reliable and cost-effective solution for asset management and tracking needs.
