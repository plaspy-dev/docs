# CalmAmp - MDT-7

![MDT-7](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp MDT-7 is a versatile GPS tracker that allows customers to develop and license user applications specific to their markets on the open Android OS environment. With its small size, rugged design, and wired and wireless connectivity options, the MDT-7 is ideal for long-haul trucking, local fleet, asset delivery, and mobile workforce management market segments. 

One of the standout features of the MDT-7 is its 7" touch display, which provides a user-friendly interface for accessing applications and information. The device also features front and rear-facing cameras, on-board memory, and micro SD support, allowing for the storage and capture of important data. 

The MDT-7 platform centralizes workflow applications, reducing driver paperwork and integrating driver dispatching and messaging. It also enables the communication of critical information from the driver and vehicle directly back to your business information systems. Messages and captured information are transported across the cellular network via a connected CalAmp LMU device. 

Additionally, the MDT-7 is designed to dramatically reduce the cost of ownership while providing an expandable platform for your business-specific mobile applications. It also offers the option to integrate CalAmp's preferred fleet navigation software from Magellan, which includes truck attributes for navigating routes based on vehicle profile and load nature. The Magellan software also includes driver safety and productivity tools such as Toll Booth and Weigh Station Alerts, Speed Limit Warnings, and Landmark Guidance. 

With its advanced features and customizable applications, the CalmAmp MDT-7 is a reliable and cost-effective solution for businesses in need of a versatile GPS tracker.

### Outstanding Features:

- Open Android OS environment for developing and licensing user applications
- Small size and rugged design
- Wired and wireless connectivity options
- 7" touch display for easy access to applications and information
- Front and rear-facing cameras for capturing important data
- On-board memory and micro SD support for storage
- Centralized workflow applications to reduce paperwork and integrate driver dispatching and messaging
- Integration with CalAmp's preferred fleet navigation software from Magellan
- Over-the-air serviceability for easy installation of new applications
