# CalmAmp - MDT-7

<div className="device-logo">
![MDT-7](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp MDT-7 is a versatile GPS terminal built on an open Android operating environment. It combines a small, rugged form factor with a 7 inch touch display, front and rear cameras, on-board memory and micro SD support. With wired and wireless connectivity options and a focus on workflow centralization, the MDT-7 is positioned for use in long haul trucking, local fleets, asset delivery, and mobile workforce management.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MDT-7 can feed location, driver messaging, and captured information into a fleet platform for operational visibility and reporting. Messages and captured data are transported via a connected CalAmp LMU device, allowing Plaspy to surface driver workflows, dispatching, and event information alongside vehicle tracking for unified fleet oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Open Android environment that supports custom user applications and licensing for market specific needs
- Rugged compact design suited to vehicle and mobile deployments
- Large 7 inch touch display for driver interaction and workflow access
- Front and rear cameras plus on-board memory and micro SD support for data capture and storage
- Centralized workflow features to reduce paperwork and streamline dispatching and messaging
- Option to integrate Magellan navigation for route guidance and operational assistance

## How It Works with Plaspy

The MDT-7 transmits operational messages and captured data via a connected CalAmp LMU, enabling Plaspy to ingest and display the device outputs alongside fleet location data. Plaspy can use those inputs to improve visibility, manage workflows, and produce operational reports for fleet managers.

- Real time location visibility and mapping of vehicles and assets within the Plaspy platform
- Consolidated driver messaging and dispatch workflows accessible to operations teams
- Capture and associate images or event data from the MDT-7 with trips, stops, or incidents
- Alerts and reporting derived from device events and workflow status for operational oversight
- Historical activity and stored data available in Plaspy for audits and performance analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Long haul trucking operations that require driver workflows and route guidance on a rugged terminal
- Local delivery fleets needing proof of delivery capture and easy driver interaction
- Mobile workforce deployments where centralized dispatching and messaging reduce paperwork
- Asset delivery and tracking where onboard cameras and storage support documentation needs
- Fleets seeking an expandable platform for business specific mobile applications

The MDT-7 is a practical choice for organizations that want a flexible in‑vehicle terminal with a large display and the ability to run tailored applications. Its design emphasizes workflow centralization and data capture, which aligns with Plaspy goals of improving fleet visibility, reducing administrative burden, and consolidating operational data.

Because the MDT-7 is built on an open Android environment and is compatible with Plaspy, fleets can combine custom or third party apps with Plaspy monitoring and reporting to create a cohesive operational solution.
