# CalmAmp - LMU-800

<div className="device-logo">
![LMU-800](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp LMU-800 is a compact, cost effective vehicle tracking device designed for straightforward installation in automobiles. Built for 12 and 24 volt mobile vehicles, it is positioned for applications such as stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance, and auto rental where reliable tracking and a backup power source are important. The LMU-800 combines a small footprint with strong GPS performance and features intended to maintain tracking continuity even when vehicle power is interrupted.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the LMU-800 can feed location updates and event information into Plaspy for fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Its internal backup battery, motion sensing capability, and programmable event engine make it a practical choice for organizations that need persistent visibility and rule driven alerts. Integrating the LMU-800 with Plaspy helps teams use device events and position data in dashboards, alerts, and reporting workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for discreet vehicle installation and space constrained deployments
- Built in 200 mAh backup battery to maintain location reporting during vehicle power loss
- Designed for use in both 12 volt and 24 volt automotive electrical systems
- 3 axis accelerometer for motion sensing to detect movement and enable event based tracking
- PEG programmable event generator for flexible, condition based alerts and exceptions
- PULS remote management capability for over the air configuration and firmware updates
- Cost effective option aimed at fleet and asset tracking use cases

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the LMU-800 supplies position and event data that Plaspy can display, analyze, and act on within platform workflows. Plaspy can incorporate device events and location history to improve fleet visibility and operational decision making.

- Real time and historical location visibility in Plaspy maps and timeline views
- Event based alerts in Plaspy triggered by device conditions such as motion or power loss
- Consolidated reporting and exportable data for fleet performance and activity analysis
- Use of device generated exception rules to reduce false positives and focus attention
- Reduced maintenance overhead when remote configuration and firmware updates are available alongside fleet monitoring
