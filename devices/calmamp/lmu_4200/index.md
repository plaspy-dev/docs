# CalmAmp - LMU-4200

![LMU-4200](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-4200 is a flagship GPS tracker that offers a wide range of wireless connectivity options and vehicle interfaces. It is designed to meet the needs of enterprise customers who require flexibility and expandability in their fleet management solutions. With its cellular, WiFi, Bluetooth, and satellite connectivity options, the LMU-4200 ensures reliable and seamless communication between the tracker and the fleet management system.

One of the standout features of the LMU-4200 is its comprehensive I/O system and expandable accessories, which make it a versatile and cost-effective solution. The tracker is equipped with a 3-axis accelerometer that detects hard braking, aggressive acceleration, and vehicle impacts, allowing fleet managers to monitor driver behavior and ensure the safety of their drivers and vehicles. Additionally, the optional jPOD™ ECU interface provides real-time engine condition and performance data, giving fleet managers a complete picture of vehicle health.

The LMU-4200 also offers flexibility in terms of customization and configuration. It features CalmAmp's advanced on-board alert engine, PEG™ \(Programmable Event Generator\), which allows customers to define exception-based rules based on various parameters such as time, date, motion, location, and input. This ensures that the tracker meets the specific requirements of each application. Furthermore, the LMU-4200 leverages CalmAmp's over-the-air device management and maintenance system, PULS™ \(Programming, Updates, and Logistic System\), which enables easy configuration updates, firmware upgrades, and monitoring of unit health status.

In summary, the CalmAmp LMU-4200 is a highly flexible and feature-rich GPS tracker that offers a wide range of wireless connectivity options, vehicle interfaces, and expandable accessories. It is an ideal solution for enterprise customers who require reliable and customizable fleet management solutions.
