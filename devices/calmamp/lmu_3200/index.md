# CalmAmp - LMU-3200

![LMU-3200](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-3200 is a compact GPS tracker designed for a variety of automotive telematics applications. It combines location tracking with access to the vehicle diagnostics interface and includes a 3-axis accelerometer for detecting events such as hard braking, cornering, and sudden acceleration. Its small size and internal antennas are intended to make installation straightforward and unobtrusive for mobile deployments.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the LMU-3200 can feed vehicle location, diagnostic indicators, and event data into fleet and asset management workflows. Plaspy can use the device data to provide live visibility, event-driven alerts, and reporting across vehicles, helping teams monitor operations and respond to exceptions more effectively.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor with a design suited to automotive telematics deployments
- Access to vehicle diagnostics via the OBD II interface for richer telemetry
- Built in 3-axis accelerometer to detect harsh braking, cornering, and rapid acceleration
- Superior internal cellular and GPS antennas to simplify installation
- Programmable Event Generator PEG for custom on device alerting and rules
- Over the air serviceability through CalAmp PULS for remote configuration and updates
- Practical option for insurance, rental, and driver behavior monitoring programs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LMU-3200 provides the platform with location and event data that can be used for fleet monitoring, alerts, and operational reporting. Plaspy ingests and visualizes the incoming telematics so teams can act on movement and exception data from vehicles.

- Real time location tracking and historical route playback in Plaspy dashboards
- Event and alert handling for accelerometer detected behaviors such as hard braking
- Surface OBD II diagnostic indicators within Plaspy reports and maintenance views
- Combine device side PEG rules with Plaspy alerts to reduce noise and focus on exceptions
- Use Plaspy reporting and analytics to monitor fleet trends and driver performance

## Typical Use Cases

- Automotive insurance programs that require location and driving behavior data
- Driver behavior management and coaching for safety and performance improvement
- Auto rental and shared mobility fleets needing quick installation and telemetry
- Fleet operations that want diagnostic insight to support maintenance planning
- Usage based services and telematics programs requiring event based alerts

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LMU-3200 is a practical choice for organizations deploying vehicle telematics where compact hardware, on device event logic, and access to vehicle diagnostics are valuable. Its programmable alert engine and OTA management capabilities make it easier to tune behavior monitoring without repeated physical intervention, while the accelerometer and OBD II data add context to location information that Plaspy can present in dashboards and reports.

Selecting the LMU-3200 for use with Plaspy can simplify fleet rollouts that need a balance of vehicle diagnostic access, event detection, and straightforward installation. Plaspy complements the device by turning its telemetry into actionable visibility, alerts, and analytics to support everyday fleet operations.
