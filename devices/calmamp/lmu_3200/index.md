# CalmAmp - LMU-3200

![LMU-3200](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-3200 is a versatile GPS tracker that is perfect for a range of automotive applications. Whether you need it for automotive insurance, driver behavior management, auto rental, or any other automotive use that requires access to the vehicle diagnostics interface \(ODB-II\), the LMU-3200 has you covered.

One of the standout features of the LMU-3200 is its small size and superior GPS design. It also comes equipped with an ODB-II interface and a 3-axis accelerometer. These features allow the LMU-3200 to not only track vehicle speed and location but also access vehicle diagnostic interface data and detect hard braking, cornering, or acceleration. Additionally, the LMU-3200 features superior internal antennas for both cellular and GPS, eliminating the need for professional installation and making it quick, easy, and inexpensive to install.

The LMU-3200 also offers flexibility and customization options. It utilizes CalAmp's advanced on-board alert engine, known as PEG \(Programmable Event Generator\), which allows you to monitor external conditions and set up customer-defined exception-based rules to meet your specific application requirements. This means you can program the LMU-3200 to respond instantaneously to pre-defined threshold combinations related to time, date, motion, location geozone, input, and other event combinations.

Furthermore, the LMU-3200 offers over-the-air serviceability through CalAmp's management and maintenance system, PULS \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\). This allows for easy configuration parameter updates, PEG rule changes, and firmware upgrades without the need for manual intervention. With PULS, you can monitor the health status of the LMU-3200 units across your fleet, identifying and addressing any issues before they become costly problems.

In summary, the CalmAmp LMU-3200 is a feature-packed GPS tracker that offers superior GPS design, access to vehicle diagnostics, and customizable alert capabilities. Its small size, easy installation, and over-the-air serviceability make it a convenient and cost-effective choice for a variety of automotive applications.
