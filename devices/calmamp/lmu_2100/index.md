# CalmAmp - LMU-2100

![LMU-2100](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-2100 is a cutting-edge insurance tracking unit that combines advanced technology with high reliability to meet the demands of fleet customers. This robust and affordable device offers a wide range of applications and features, making it an ideal choice for insurance purposes. 

One of the standout features of the LMU-2100 is its 3-axis accelerometer, which allows for the measurement of driver behavior and vehicle impacts. This accelerometer can detect hard braking, aggressive acceleration, and vehicle impacts, providing valuable data for insurance purposes. Additionally, the device incorporates GSM/GPRS, CDMA 1xRTT, or HSPA wireless communication, ensuring reliable and efficient communication.

Flexibility is another key aspect of the LMU-2100. It utilizes CalAmp's industry-leading on-board alert engine, PEG \(Programmable Event Generator\), which allows for the monitoring of external conditions and the creation of customer-defined, exception-based rules. This means that the device can be customized to meet the specific needs of your application, giving you a distinct advantage over your competition.

Furthermore, the LMU-2100 offers over-the-air serviceability through CalAmp's PULS \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\). This system allows for the easy updating of configuration parameters, PEG rules, and firmware over-the-air, eliminating the need for manual updates. It also provides hands-free configuration and automatic post-installation upgrades, making maintenance and management a breeze.

With its advanced features, reliability, and flexibility, the CalmAmp LMU-2100 is an excellent choice for insurance tracking. Whether you need to monitor driver behavior, track vehicle impacts, or customize the device to meet your specific requirements, the LMU-2100 has you covered.
