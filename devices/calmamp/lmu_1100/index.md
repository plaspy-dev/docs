# CalmAmp - LMU-1100

![LMU-1100](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-1100 is a compact, cost effective vehicle tracking device built for outdoor assets and recreational vehicles. It combines a small form factor with strong GPS performance to deliver accurate location tracking for motorcycles, snowmobiles, trailers, and other exposed equipment. The LMU-1100 is positioned for asset monitoring and recovery use cases and includes practical hardware features such as a backup battery and basic input output capabilities that support event based monitoring.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the LMU-1100 can feed location and event information into a unified fleet management platform for visibility and operational oversight. Its programmable on board alert engine and over the air management features make it a flexible choice for organizations that want remote control over device behavior and easier fleet level maintenance while using Plaspy for tracking, alerts, and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Small, unobtrusive form factor suited to recreational vehicles and exposed assets
- Designed for accurate GPS based location tracking and asset recovery
- Built in backup power to maintain reporting during unexpected power loss
- On board programmable event engine for custom rules and instant responses
- Remote serviceability for parameter updates and device health checks
- Input and output options for basic external event monitoring
- Positioned as a cost effective option for fleet and asset tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LMU-1100 provides location and event data that Plaspy displays, analyzes, and acts upon for fleet managers and operators. The device's programmable alerts and remote management capabilities complement Plaspy's tracking and reporting features to reduce manual intervention and improve situational awareness.

- Continuous and historical location visibility inside Plaspy dashboards and maps
- Event driven alerts mapped to Plaspy notifications for movement, geozone, and input events
- Fleet level reporting and exportable activity logs for operational review
- Device health monitoring and remote configuration supported alongside Plaspy oversight
- Use of programmable rules to reduce false alerts and focus on relevant incidents

## Typical Use Cases

- Recovery and monitoring of stolen motorcycles, snowmobiles, and similar recreational vehicles
- Tracking and oversight of seasonal fleet equipment and trailers
- Rental fleet management for precise location and event visibility
- Remote monitoring of dispersed outdoor assets to improve recovery times
- Simple input based monitoring for tamper, motion, or power events

The LMU-1100 is a practical choice for organizations that need a compact, reliable tracker for outdoor and recreational assets. Its emphasis on accurate GPS tracking, programmable event handling, and remote manageability aligns well with Plaspy workflows that prioritize visibility, alerting, and operational efficiency across a fleet.

Because the model combines on device rules with over the air maintenance capabilities, teams using Plaspy can reduce field visits and tune device behavior centrally.
