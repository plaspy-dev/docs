# CalmAmp - LMU-700

<div className="device-logo">
![LMU-700](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp LMU-700 is a compact, versatile vehicle tracking unit designed for automotive applications such as stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance monitoring, and auto rental management. It is intended for straightforward installation in 12 or 24 volt vehicles and offers high sensitivity GPS performance together with integrated antennas and multiple input and output options for basic external monitoring and control.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-700 can feed location, status, and event messages into the Plaspy platform so fleets and operators can monitor vehicles in real time. Its on-board programmable event engine and support for cellular messaging provide flexible data reporting patterns that Plaspy can visualize, alert on, and include in fleet reports for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for discreet installation in a wide range of vehicles
- Designed for 12 V and 24 V vehicle systems, supporting common automotive use cases
- Super sensitive GPS performance for reliable position fixes in challenging environments
- Internal cellular and GPS antennas for flexible installation without external wiring
- Enhanced messaging via cellular links using SMS or UDP style message transport
- Three inputs and three outputs for simple monitoring and control of vehicle conditions
- Programmable Event Generator for on device event detection and PULS support for remote maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LMU-700 transmits location and event data that Plaspy ingests to provide visibility, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy surfaces the device data in dashboards and reports, enabling fleet managers to act on the device events and location updates produced by the LMU-700.

- Real time vehicle location display and map tracking inside the Plaspy platform
- Event and alert handling based on device reports and Plaspy rule configuration
- Historical playback and reporting for trips, stops, and operational analysis
- Fleet monitoring dashboards that show unit status and recent communications
- Geofence and zone monitoring tied to reported location and event messages
- Exportable reports and data views for operational and finance teams

## Typical Use Cases

- Stolen vehicle recovery and vehicle security monitoring
- Vehicle finance monitoring and repossession support workflows
- Auto rental fleet tracking and utilization oversight
- General fleet operations for small to mid sized vehicle fleets
- Asset monitoring where compact, in vehicle tracking is required

The LMU-700 pairs a small physical footprint with a flexible event engine and integrated antennas, making it a practical option for organizations that need reliable location reporting without complex installation. Its I/O options and programmable alerts make it adaptable to several fleet workflows, while Plaspy provides the operational interface to visualize locations, manage alerts, and produce reports.

Because the LMU-700 can send enhanced messages and on device events, it can reduce noise and focus Plaspy alerts on the conditions that matter most to your operation.
