# CalmAmp - LMU-700

![LMU-700](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-700 is a highly versatile and cost-effective vehicle tracking device that offers a wide range of features. Designed for easy installation in automobiles, this tracking unit is perfect for applications such as stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance, auto rental, and other automotive track and trace needs.

One of the standout features of the LMU-700 is its compact size, making it easy to install in any 12 or 24 volt mobile vehicle. It boasts superior GPS performance and utilizes super-sensitive GPS technology on GSM/GPRS/CDMA 1xRTT/HSPA cellular networks. With internal antennas for both cellular and GPS, there's no need for wired antennas, allowing for flexible and inexpensive installations.

The LMU-700 also offers excellent communication capabilities, with messages being transported across the cellular network using enhanced SMS or UDP messaging. This ensures a reliable communication link between the device and your application servers. Additionally, the LMU-700 features three inputs and three outputs \(I/O\), providing flexibility for monitoring and controlling external conditions.

With CalAmp's advanced on-board alert engine, PEG \(Programmable Event Generator\), the LMU-700 allows you to monitor and respond to pre-defined threshold conditions related to time, date, motion, location, geo-zone, input, and other event combinations. This level of flexibility ensures that the device can be customized to meet your specific application requirements.

Furthermore, the LMU-700 offers over-the-air serviceability through CalAmp's management and maintenance system, PULS \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\). This allows for easy configuration parameter updates, PEG rule changes, and firmware upgrades without the need for manual intervention. With PULS, you can monitor the health status of units across your fleet, identifying and resolving issues before they become costly problems.

In summary, the CalmAmp LMU-700 is a reliable and feature-rich vehicle tracking device that offers easy installation, superior GPS performance, flexible monitoring capabilities, and over-the-air serviceability. Whether you need to track stolen vehicles, manage vehicle finance, or monitor your auto rental fleet, the LMU-700 is an ideal solution.
