# CalmAmp - LMU-5530

![LMU-5530](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-5530 is a versatile and high-speed GPS tracker designed to meet the ever-changing needs of fixed or mobile broadband applications. With its adaptable platform and advanced features, this tracker offers a wide range of capabilities to suit various requirements.

One of the standout features of the LMU-5530 is its 3G/4G broadband routing and cellular gateway functions, allowing for seamless connectivity and data transmission. It also boasts a Programmable Event Generation engine, which enables users to create and deploy their own custom applications. This, combined with the flexible Linux operating system, provides a highly customizable and adaptable solution.

In terms of connectivity, the LMU-5530 offers multiple interfaces, including two Ethernet 10/100 ports, host and device OTG USB, RS232/RS485 serial port, switched power TTL serial port, two 1-wire busses, seven inputs, seven outputs, and five A/D inputs. This wide range of interfaces allows for easy integration with various peripherals such as laptops, USB dongles, Mobile Data Terminals \(MDTs\), and RFID tags.

Furthermore, the LMU-5530 incorporates CalAmp's industry-leading on-board alert engine, PEG™ \(Programmable Event Generator\), which continuously monitors external conditions and supports customer-defined exception-based rules. This ensures that users can easily set up and manage alerts based on specific threshold conditions related to time, date, motion, location, geo-zone, input, and other event combinations.

Another notable feature of the LMU-5530 is its over-the-air device management and maintenance software, PULS™ \(Programming, Update and Logistics System\). This allows for easy updates of Linux applications, configuration parameters, PEG scripts, and firmware over-the-air. It also enables monitoring of unit health status across installations, helping to identify and resolve issues before they become costly problems.

In summary, the CalmAmp LMU-5530 is a powerful and feature-rich GPS tracker that offers exceptional versatility, speed, and expandability. With its advanced capabilities and flexible Linux operating system, it is well-suited for a wide range of broadband applications, making it a cost-effective solution for delivering, supporting, and growing various tracking needs.
