# CalmAmp - LMU-200

![LMU-200](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-200 is a highly versatile and cost-effective vehicle tracking device designed for discreet and reliable installation in automobiles. It is an ideal solution for various applications such as stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance, auto rental, and other automotive track and trace needs. With its compact size and advanced features, the LMU-200 offers exceptional performance and ease of use.

One of the standout features of the LMU-200 is its superior GPS performance, ensuring accurate and reliable tracking of vehicles. It also comes with an optional internal 200mAh back-up battery, allowing for continuous tracking even in the event of power loss. The device is equipped with ultra-low power sleep modes, maximizing battery life and reducing power consumption.

The LMU-200 incorporates a 3-axis accelerometer for motion sensing, enabling it to detect and respond to vehicle movement. It also offers up to four Inputs/Outputs \(I/O\), providing flexibility for connecting external devices and sensors. The device utilizes CDMA cellular networks for communication, ensuring reliable and efficient transmission of data between the device and application servers.

Installation of the LMU-200 is quick and easy, thanks to its internal antennas for both cellular and GPS signals. This eliminates the need for wired antennas and allows for flexible mounting options anywhere in the vehicle. The device supports enhanced SMS or UDP messaging for communication, ensuring a robust and reliable connection.

The LMU-200 is designed to reduce the cost of ownership while maintaining excellent field reliability. It features CalAmp's advanced on-board alert engine, PEG™ \(Programmable Event Generator\), which allows for monitoring of external conditions and supports customer-defined exception-based rules. This enables the device to respond instantly to pre-defined threshold conditions related to time, date, motion, location, and other event combinations.

Additionally, the LMU-200 offers over-the-air serviceability through CalAmp's management and maintenance system, PULS™ \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\). This allows for easy configuration, parameter updates, and firmware upgrades without the need for physical access to the device. It also enables monitoring of unit health status across fleets, helping to identify and address issues before they become costly problems.
