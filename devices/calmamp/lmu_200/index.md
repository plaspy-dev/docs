# CalmAmp - LMU-200

<div className="device-logo">
![LMU-200](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp LMU-200 is a compact vehicle tracking device designed for discreet and reliable installation in automobiles. It is positioned for applications such as stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance monitoring, auto rental fleets, and other automotive track and trace needs. The LMU-200 emphasizes strong GPS performance, low power operation, internal antennas for flexible mounting, and a small footprint for unobtrusive placement.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-200 can deliver vehicle location, motion events, and unit health information into the Plaspy platform for centralized fleet visibility and reporting. Its programmable event engine and over the air serviceability make it a practical choice for organizations that need ongoing remote management and exception based alerts integrated with Plaspy workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet form factor suited to hidden installation in passenger vehicles
- High quality GPS performance for reliable location tracking
- Optional internal 200 mAh backup battery to support continued tracking during power loss
- 3 axis accelerometer for detecting motion and activity events
- Up to four Inputs and Outputs for connecting external signals or accessories
- Internal cellular and GPS antennas to simplify mounting and installation
- On board PEG programmable event engine and PULS over the air serviceability for remote configuration and updates

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LMU-200 integrates with Plaspy to provide vehicle position, movement alerts, and unit status into a single fleet management view. Plaspy uses the device data to present real time and historical insights that support operations, security, and reporting needs.

- Real time location reporting and map visualization in Plaspy for operational oversight
- Exception based alerts and notifications using the device programmable event rules for geofence, motion, and schedule conditions
- Historical trip and activity reporting to analyze utilization and route history
- Remote configuration and parameter updates via the device management features supported by PULS
- Unit health monitoring to surface battery backup events and device connectivity status

## Typical Use Cases

- Stolen vehicle recovery and location assistance
- Vehicle finance and repossession monitoring
- Auto rental fleet tracking and utilization management
- Low profile fleet asset protection for service and shuttle vehicles
- Exception based monitoring where movement and location triggers matter

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CalmAmp LMU-200 suits organizations that need a compact, cost effective tracker with reliable GPS and motion sensing, combined with the ability to manage devices remotely. Its support for programmable event rules and over the air updates reduces the need for frequent physical access while keeping device behavior aligned with operational policies.

When paired with Plaspy, the LMU-200 contributes to a practical fleet tracking solution that focuses on visibility, alerts, and simple remote maintenance. Plaspy aggregates the LMU-200 data into dashboards and reports that help teams act quickly on exceptions and monitor fleet performance over time.
