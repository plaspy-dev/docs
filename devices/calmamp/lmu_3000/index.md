# CalmAmp - LMU-3000

![LMU-3000](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-3000 is a compact OBD II compatible vehicle tracking device designed for straightforward vehicle integration. It combines a durable enclosure and internal GPS and GSM antennas with support for quadband GSM connectivity and common data transport methods, providing a reliable foundation for continuous vehicle tracking and diagnostics access where the OBD II interface is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-3000 can deliver the location and event information needed for fleet monitoring and operational oversight within the Plaspy platform. Its configurable position update options and predefined inputs for ignition and event triggers make it a practical option for teams that need consistent visibility and configurable event reporting through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- OBD II compatible design for vehicles requiring access to the diagnostics interface
- Full quadband GSM connectivity for broad cellular reach
- Supports GPRS and UDP data transmission methods for flexible communication
- Configurable position updates based on time or distance to balance accuracy and data usage
- Predefined inputs for ignition and internal event triggers to support event driven reporting
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas simplify installation and reduce external hardware
- Sleep mode functionality to conserve power when the vehicle is not active

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LMU-3000 provides continuous positional and event data that Plaspy ingests for visualization, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy can use the device's configurable updates and event triggers to present operational status in dashboards and drive automated notifications for fleet managers.

- Location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps with configurable update frequency
- Event reporting for ignition and internal triggers to support status changes and incident detection
- Historical route and activity logging to support reporting and analysis within Plaspy
- Alerts based on movement, inactivity, or predefined event conditions routed through Plaspy
- Fleet-level visibility for grouping, filtering, and monitoring vehicles equipped with the LMU-3000

## Typical Use Cases

- Automotive insurance programs that need vehicle presence and basic diagnostics access
- Driver behavior monitoring and fleet safety programs using OBD II data where available
- Auto rental and vehicle hire operations requiring returning location and status checks
- Vehicle recovery and track and trace applications for stolen or misplaced assets
- General fleet management for commercial vehicles that benefit from OBD II integration and event triggers

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LMU-3000 is a sensible choice for organizations that require OBD II access combined with mainstream tracking capabilities. Its compact form, internal antennas, and configurable reporting make it straightforward to deploy across mixed vehicle fleets while providing the information Plaspy needs to maintain situational awareness and operational reporting.

For teams that prioritize compatibility with vehicle diagnostics and dependable cellular communication, the LMU-3000 pairs well with Plaspy to deliver a balanced solution for tracking, alerts, and historical analysis.
