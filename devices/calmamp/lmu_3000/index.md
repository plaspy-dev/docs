# CalmAmp - LMU-3000

![LMU-3000](./logo.jpg)

The CalAmp LMU-3000 is a full-featured OBD-II compatible vehicle tracking product designed for easy and reliable installation in vehicles. It is an ideal solution for automotive insurance, driver behavior management, auto rental, and automotive applications when access to the vehicle diagnostics interface \(OBD-II\) is required. With its compact design and advanced features, the CalAmp LMU-3000 offers a comprehensive solution for tracking and managing vehicles.

The CalAmp LMU-3000 is equipped with a full quadband GSM band for reliable communication. It supports GPRS and UDP communication methods, allowing for seamless data transmission. The tracker can be configured to provide position updates based on time or distance, ensuring accurate and up-to-date location information. Additionally, the CalAmp LMU-3000 features a sleep mode, which helps conserve power when the vehicle is not in use.

With pre-defined inputs for ignition and internal events-based triggers, the CalAmp LMU-3000 offers enhanced functionality for monitoring vehicle status and detecting specific events. The tracker also comes with internal GPS and GSM antennas, eliminating the need for external antennas and simplifying installation. It is housed in a durable plastic casing, ensuring long-lasting performance even in harsh environments.

Overall, the CalAmp LMU-3000 is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that is well-suited for track & trace, vehicle recovery, and fleet management applications. Its compatibility with the OBD-II interface makes it a convenient choice for automotive applications that require access to vehicle diagnostics. Made in the USA, the CalAmp LMU-3000 is built to meet the highest standards of quality and performance.
