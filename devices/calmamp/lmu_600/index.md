# CalmAmp - LMU-600

![LMU-600](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-600 is a cost effective and feature rich vehicle tracking device intended for automotive use. Designed with compact form and strong GPS performance, the LMU-600 series supports common vehicle tracking scenarios such as insurance telematics, stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance monitoring, and rental fleet tracking. It includes an internal backup battery and Inputs/Outputs for features like starter disable and panic button, together with internal cellular and GPS antennas to simplify installation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-600 can deliver position and event information into Plaspy for centralized visibility and fleet oversight. Its on board alert engine and support for over the air configuration make it a practical option for deployments where ongoing device management, exception based alerts, and automated updates are important to operations managed in Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Economical and full featured vehicle tracking device suitable for large scale fleets
- Compact size with strong GPS performance for reliable location reporting
- Internal backup battery to preserve basic tracking when external power is removed
- Inputs and Outputs for accessory control and event signaling such as panic and starter disable
- Internal cellular and GPS antennas for simplified installation and reduced hardware needs
- On board programmable alert engine for custom event based monitoring
- Support for over the air configuration and updates to streamline device maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LMU-600 can provide Plaspy with regular location updates and device events so fleet managers can monitor vehicle status and history in one place. Plaspy ingests the device messages and maps them to platform features for operational use.

- Real time and historical location visualization inside Plaspy for route review and asset tracking
- Event and exception alerts forwarded to Plaspy for geofence, speeding, or input triggered notifications
- Monitoring of I/O events such as panic button presses or external accessory states translated into platform alerts
- Aggregated reporting and analytics on vehicle activity to support operational decision making
- Use of device level alert rules to reduce noise and surface only relevant exceptions to Plaspy users

## Typical Use Cases

- Automotive insurance telematics and usage based monitoring
- Stolen vehicle recovery with persistent location reporting
- Vehicle finance and repossession tracking workflows
- Rental car fleet monitoring and return management
- General fleet operations for light and heavy duty vehicles where compact installs are preferred

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LMU-600 is a practical choice for organizations that need a balance of cost control and essential telematics features. Its compact design, internal antennas, and programmable alerting make it well suited to mixed fleets where straightforward installation and flexible event handling matter. When paired with Plaspy, the LMU-600 can contribute dependable location data and actionable alerts to a centralized fleet management workflow.

Plaspy provides the platform layer for visualizing device data, configuring alerts, and producing operational reports while the LMU-600 provides the in vehicle telemetry and event generation. Together they offer a solution that supports ongoing device management and exception based monitoring without adding unnecessary complexity.
