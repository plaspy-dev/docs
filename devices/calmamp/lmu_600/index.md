# CalmAmp - LMU-600

![LMU-600](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-600 series is a cost-effective and feature-rich vehicle tracking device designed for easy installation in automobiles. It is an ideal solution for various automotive tracking applications such as automotive insurance, stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance, and auto rental. The LMU-600 series comes equipped with a 200 mAh internal backup battery and Inputs/Outputs \(I/O\) for features like starter disable, panic button, and other accessories.

Featuring superior GPS performance and a compact size, the LMU-600 series offers excellent tracking capabilities. It utilizes next-generation, super-sensitive GPS technology on GSM/GPRS cellular networks, making it compatible with both 12 and 24 volt mobile vehicles. The device incorporates internal antennas for both cellular and GPS, eliminating the need for wired antennas and allowing for easy and cost-effective installations. Messages are transmitted across the GSM/GPRS network using enhanced SMS or UDP messaging, ensuring reliable communication between the device and application servers.

The LMU-600 series also offers flexibility through CalAmp's advanced on-board alert engine, PEG™ \(Programmable Event Generator\). PEG allows for the monitoring of external conditions and supports customer-defined, exception-based rules to meet specific application requirements. This behavior can be programmed by CalAmp before shipment, at the customer's facility, or over-the-air once the unit has been deployed.

Additionally, the LMU-600 series leverages CalAmp's management and maintenance system, PULS™ \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\), for over-the-air configuration parameters, PEG rules, and firmware updates. This system enables hands-free configuration and automatic post-installation upgrades, ensuring efficient fleet management and proactive issue identification.

### Key Features:

- Economical and full-featured vehicle tracking device
- Internal 200 mAh backup battery
- Inputs/Outputs \(I/O\) for starter disable, panic button, and accessories
- Superior GPS performance
- Compatible with 12 and 24 volt mobile vehicles
- Internal antennas for cellular and GPS
- Enhanced SMS or UDP messaging for reliable communication
- Advanced on-board alert engine \(PEG\) for flexible monitoring
- PULS system for over-the-air configuration and firmware updates
