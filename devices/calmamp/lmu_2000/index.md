# CalmAmp - LMU-2000

![LMU-2000](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-2000 is a compact, cost effective vehicle tracking device designed for straightforward installation in automobiles. It combines a focused GPS design with a small form factor to provide reliable location and speed data, and includes interfaces and sensors that support detection of driving events such as hard braking, cornering, and rapid acceleration. The device is intended for applications including automotive insurance, driver behavior management, auto rental, and general fleet management.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-2000 can feed vehicle location, event notifications, and device health indicators into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its programmable event engine and over the air management capabilities make it a practical option for operations that need configurable alerts, remote updates, and a simple hardware footprint that integrates into a broader fleet tracking deployment.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and cost effective vehicle tracking device suitable for fleet and automotive applications
- Accurate GPS design for dependable location and speed reporting
- OBD II interface enables detection of driver events such as hard braking cornering and acceleration
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer for motion based event detection and improved behavior monitoring
- Programmable Event Generator (PEG) for on board rules based alerts tied to time motion location and inputs
- Over the air serviceability via CalAmp PULS for remote configuration updates and device maintenance
- Uses enhanced messaging methods to transport device messages across the cellular network for consistent communication

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the LMU-2000 can send location updates and event messages into Plaspy so fleets gain visibility into vehicle position status and driver behavior. Plaspy ingests the device events and presents them in dashboards alerts and reports that support operational oversight.

- Real time location and speed tracking displayed in Plaspy maps and vehicle lists
- Driver behavior events from the OBD II interface and accelerometer appear as alerts for monitoring and coaching
- PEG configured rules translate to Plaspy notifications for geofence entries exits motion and custom conditions
- Device health and status reported back for remote monitoring and fleet maintenance oversight
- Historical trip data and event histories used in Plaspy reporting for analysis and operational reviews

## Typical Use Cases

- Automotive insurance programs requiring location and driving behavior data
- Driver behavior monitoring and coaching to reduce risk and improve safety
- Auto rental and shared vehicle programs needing easy installation and usage tracking
- Fleet management for mixed vehicle fleets focusing on visibility and operational efficiency
- Centralized device health monitoring and remote configuration for large deployments

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LMU-2000 offers a balance of compact design and practical fleet features that suit organizations seeking straightforward vehicle tracking and driver event monitoring. Its small footprint and built in antennas make it a convenient option for operations that prefer minimal installation complexity, while the OBD II interface and accelerometer provide the event data useful for safety and insurance programs.

Combined with Plaspy the LMU-2000 becomes part of a managed fleet solution that emphasizes visibility configurable alerts and remote device oversight. Plaspy can consolidate location data event notifications and device status from LMU-2000 units to help teams manage fleets more efficiently and respond to incidents or maintenance needs with better context.
