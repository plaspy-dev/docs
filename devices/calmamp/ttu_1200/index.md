# CalmAmp - TTU-1200

![TTU-1200](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp TTU-1200 is a rechargeable battery pack trailer tracking device engineered for reliable long term deployments. Designed to manage assets that may remain idle for extended periods, the TTU-1200 combines a compact form factor with superior GPS performance and an internal battery to support continuous tracking without a constant external power feed. Its on board inputs and outputs and built in antennas make it a practical choice for tracking trailers and other mobile assets commonly paired with 12 or 24 volt vehicle systems.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TTU-1200 can feed position and event data into Plaspy for fleet visibility and operational oversight. The tracker’s event engine and remote management features complement Plaspy’s monitoring, alerting, and reporting capabilities, enabling organizations to include TTU-1200 units in their existing Plaspy fleets for consolidated tracking and management.

## Key Highlights

- Rechargeable battery pack designed for long term deployments and intermittent power conditions
- Compact enclosure with integrated cellular and GPS antennas for flexible placement
- Internal 3.8 Ah battery for extended on board operation without external power
- Three inputs and outputs to support basic event monitoring and control
- Programmable event generator for customized alerts based on motion, location, time, and inputs
- Remote device management and configuration updates to streamline fleet maintenance

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TTU-1200 provides location and event information that Plaspy ingests and displays alongside other fleet assets. Communications from the unit are transported across cellular links, allowing Plaspy to receive periodic positions and event messages while providing a consolidated view of device health and activity.

- Plaspy displays live and historical location data received from TTU-1200 units for route and asset visibility
- Configurable alerts from the device appear within Plaspy to notify teams about predefined events and thresholds
- Plaspy reporting tools can include TTU-1200 data for utilization, movement history, and event analytics
- Remote management features of the device support coordinated maintenance and parameter updates tracked in Plaspy
- Fleet operators can include TTU-1200 units in grouping, geozone, and dashboard views for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking for fleets that leave assets parked or idle for long periods
- Monitoring of rental equipment or seasonal assets that are not continuously powered
- Visibility for logistics and transport operations that require discreet, compact trackers
- Asset recovery and movement alerts when trailers or mobile equipment are relocated
- Fleet consolidation where mixed device types are managed centrally through Plaspy

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TTU-1200 is a practical option for organizations that need reliable trailer and asset tracking without relying on continuous vehicle power. Its rechargeable battery and compact design make it suitable for long term deployments where occasional use or extended idle time is expected. The device’s programmable event engine allows operators to tailor alerts to operational needs, which pairs well with Plaspy’s alerting and reporting capabilities.

Choosing the TTU-1200 with Plaspy can simplify fleet operations by bringing battery powered trailer assets into the same management platform as powered vehicles. The combination of remote device management and centralized visibility in Plaspy helps reduce administrative overhead and supports consistent monitoring across mixed fleets.
