# CalmAmp

CalmAmp is a recognized name in GPS tracking hardware used across fleet, asset, and vehicle monitoring projects. This brand page explains how CalmAmp devices integrate with Plaspy for location tracking and status reporting, and helps you find compatible devices via the catalog below.

## How CalmAmp devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive telemetry and location data from CalmAmp devices when those devices are configured to report to Plaspy. The platform ingests device messages, maps device identifiers, and presents live and historical location data, alerts, and diagnostics in a unified dashboard. For device specific provisioning steps consult the device documentation or contact your CalmAmp vendor and Plaspy support for onboarding assistance.

## Common features found in CalmAmp devices

While specific capabilities vary by model, CalmAmp hardware commonly provides the functional building blocks required for effective GPS tracking and fleet monitoring, such as:
- GPS location and time stamping for continuous or periodic position reports
- Cellular or other wide area connectivity for real time updates
- Basic digital and analog I O for sensors and status inputs
- Battery or vehicle power options with low power reporting modes
- Configurable reporting intervals and movement based wake rules

These general features make CalmAmp devices suitable for many tracking scenarios. For exact feature lists consult the model entries in the catalog above or the vendor datasheet at the CalmAmp website.

## Typical use cases for CalmAmp GPS trackers

CalmAmp hardware is used across a range of operational scenarios including:
- Fleet vehicle tracking and route history
- Asset tracking for trailers containers and equipment
- Field service vehicle location and job coordination
- Security monitoring and tamper alerts for high value assets
- Rental and sharing services where location and usage data are required

Plaspy organizes device data into location maps, geofences, alerts and reports that support these use cases.

## Why choose Plaspy for CalmAmp device monitoring

Plaspy provides a device agnostic platform to consolidate CalmAmp telemetry alongside other vendors in a single interface. Key benefits for CalmAmp users include:
- Centralized device management and easy navigation between assets
- Event and alert rules tied to CalmAmp telemetry
- Historical playback and reporting for compliance and analysis
- Integrations and export options to connect tracking data with other systems

If you are evaluating how CalmAmp devices perform in your workflow, Plaspy can help you standardize monitoring and reporting across mixed fleets.
