# CalmAmp - LMU-1200

![LMU-1200](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-1200 is a versatile and reliable vehicle tracking device that is designed for easy installation in automobiles. It is an ideal solution for various applications such as fleet management, automotive insurance, stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance, and auto rental. One of the standout features of the LMU-1200 is its internal 1000 mAh back-up battery, which ensures continuous tracking even in the event of a power loss. This makes it a reliable choice for tracking vehicles in any situation.

The LMU-1200 is equipped with advanced GPS technology, allowing for accurate and precise tracking. It also features a 3-axis accelerometer for motion and tilt sensing, ensuring that you have access to detailed information about the vehicle's movements. Additionally, the device has four inputs/outputs \(I/O\), providing flexibility for connecting external devices or sensors.

With its small size and superior GPS performance, the LMU-1200 can be easily installed in any 12 or 24 volt mobile vehicle. It eliminates the need for wired antennas, thanks to its superior internal antennas for both cellular and GPS signals. This makes installation quick and cost-effective, as the device can be mounted virtually anywhere in the vehicle.

The LMU-1200 also offers advanced features such as CalAmp's Programmable Event Generator \(PEG\), which allows you to monitor external conditions and set up custom rules based on specific events or thresholds. This gives you the flexibility to tailor the device to your specific tracking needs. Additionally, the device can be remotely configured and updated using CalAmp's PULS \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\), ensuring that you can easily manage and maintain your fleet of vehicles.

In summary, the CalmAmp LMU-1200 is a high-value tracking unit that combines advanced GPS technology, reliable performance, and flexible features. Whether you need to track a fleet of vehicles or monitor the location of a single vehicle, the LMU-1200 is a reliable and efficient choice.
