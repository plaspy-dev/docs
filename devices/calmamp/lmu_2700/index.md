# CalmAmp - LMU-2700

<div className="device-logo">
![LMU-2700](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp LMU-2700 is a compact fleet tracking unit designed for Automatic Vehicle Location applications. It combines high sensitivity GPS positioning with multiple wireless communication options to deliver reliable location data and operational visibility. The device is offered as an affordable, robust solution with choices for internal or external antennas and an integrated backup battery to maintain tracking during power interruptions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-2700 brings those location and status capabilities into the Plaspy platform for fleet monitoring and reporting. Its on board programmable event engine and built in accelerometer enable event driven alerts and driver behavior insights that Plaspy can present alongside maps, reports, and fleet dashboards. Remote device management features further help teams keep units healthy and up to date while Plaspy focuses on visibility and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- High sensitivity GPS positioning for improved location accuracy in challenging environments
- Multiple wireless communication options for flexible deployment across vehicle fleets
- Integrated 3 axis accelerometer to detect harsh braking, aggressive acceleration, and impacts
- Programmable event engine for custom rules based on motion location time and inputs
- Built in backup battery for short term or last gasp tracking when main power is lost
- Over the air device management and configuration to simplify updates and maintenance
- Options for internal or external antennas to fit varied installation needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LMU-2700 feeds location and event data into Plaspy where teams can monitor live positions, configure alerts, and build reports. Plaspy uses the device data to provide centralized visibility and operational insights across a fleet.

- Real time and historical location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps and timelines
- Event and alert ingestion from the device event engine such as geo zone entries exits and exception conditions
- Driver behavior and impact events from the accelerometer surfaced for coaching and safety reporting
- Device health and status monitoring to highlight units needing attention or updates
- Reporting and analytics combining LMU-2700 telemetry with Plaspy dashboards for operational decisions

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operations monitoring for commercial vehicle fleets and service vehicles
- Driver safety and behavior programs using harsh event and impact detection
- Route compliance and geo zone enforcement with programmable alerts
- Asset security and recovery workflows supported by continuous positioning and alerts
- Remote device maintenance and fleet level health monitoring to reduce downtime

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LMU-2700 is a practical choice for organizations that need a balance of reliable positioning, behavioral insights, and remote management. Its programmable event engine and accelerometer make it useful for teams focused on safety and exception based workflows, while over the air update capabilities reduce the operational burden of fleet maintenance.

Paired with Plaspy, the LMU-2700's data becomes part of a unified operational view. Plaspy can help translate device events and positions into alerts, reports, and dashboards that support dispatch, compliance, and safety programs. For fleets that value flexibility and centralized oversight, this combination offers a straightforward path to improved visibility.
