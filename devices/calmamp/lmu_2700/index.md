# CalmAmp - LMU-2700

![LMU-2700](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-2700 is a cutting-edge fleet tracking unit that combines advanced technology with high reliability. Designed for AVL \(Automatic Vehicle Location\) applications, this robust device incorporates GSM/GPRS, CDMA 1xRTT, or HSPA wireless communication, along with extra sensitive GPS technology, all in an affordable package. With its high sensitivity GPS and options for internal or external antennas, the LMU-2700 can be easily installed virtually anywhere, making it a cost-effective choice for fleet tracking.

One standout feature of the LMU-2700 is its integrated 3-axis accelerometer, which allows for the measurement of driver behavior and vehicle impacts. This accelerometer can detect hard braking, aggressive acceleration, and vehicle collisions, providing valuable insights into driver performance and safety. Additionally, the device features a 1,000mAh backup battery, ensuring short-term or last-gasp tracking even when disconnected from the main power source.

Flexibility is another key aspect of the LMU-2700. It utilizes CalAmp's industry-leading on-board alert engine, PEG™ \(Programmable Event Generator\), which allows for the monitoring of external conditions and the creation of custom, exception-based rules. This means that the device can be programmed to respond to specific threshold conditions related to time, date, motion, location, geo-zones, inputs, and more. Whether pre-programmed by CalAmp, configured at a customer's facility, or updated over-the-air, PEG ensures that the device meets the unique requirements of each application, giving users a distinct advantage over their competition.

In addition to its advanced features, the LMU-2700 also benefits from CalAmp's over-the-air device management and maintenance system, PULS™ \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\). This system allows for the remote updating of configuration parameters, PEG rules, and firmware, making it easy to keep the device up-to-date and functioning optimally. PULS also offers hands-free configuration and automatic post-installation upgrades, simplifying the deployment and maintenance process. Furthermore, PULS enables users to monitor the health status of units across their fleets, allowing for the quick identification of issues before they become costly problems.

With its leading-edge technology, reliability, and flexibility, the CalmAmp LMU-2700 is an excellent choice for fleet tracking applications. Whether you need to monitor driver behavior, track vehicle impacts, or ensure efficient fleet management, this device has the features and capabilities to meet your needs.
