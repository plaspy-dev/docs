# CalmAmp - AUT-620

<div className="device-logo">
![AUT-620](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp AUT-620 is a compact, battery operated asset tracking device built for long term deployments where low maintenance is important. Designed with a small form factor and replaceable AA alkaline batteries, the AUT-620 provides reliable location monitoring and is intended for assets that remain in the field for extended periods. The device description highlights strong GPS performance and internal antennas, which support flexible mounting inside a wide range of mobile assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AUT-620 can feed location and event data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet visibility and asset oversight. Its built in programmable event engine and over the air serviceability make the AUT-620 a practical choice for organizations that want persistent tracking combined with remote configuration and rule driven alerts while using Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and operational workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Battery operated design suited for long term deployments without frequent maintenance
- Small and mountable form factor supports installation inside many mobile assets
- Superior GPS performance for consistent location reporting
- Internal antennas for simplified placement and reduced installation complexity
- Programmable Event Generator for custom exception based alerts and rules
- Over the air serviceability for remote configuration and updates
- Works across multiple cellular networks for broad coverage of deployed assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AUT-620 sends location updates and device events that Plaspy can display, analyze, and alert on to provide centralized fleet and asset management. Plaspy can leverage the AUT-620 data stream to give operators real time visibility and historical reporting across deployed devices.

- Centralized location visibility in Plaspy dashboards for individual assets and groups
- Exception and alert handling using PEG generated events forwarded into Plaspy
- Historical tracks and reporting to analyze deployment patterns and asset utilization
- Geo zone and movement monitoring surfaced in Plaspy for operational oversight
- Device status and configuration changes coordinated alongside CalmAmp PULS for remote management

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset tracking for equipment left in the field for months at a time
- Monitoring containers, trailers, or seasonal assets that require infrequent maintenance
- Tracking high value or critical assets needing reliable location reporting
- Rental fleets and shared equipment where simple replacement battery maintenance is preferred
- Deployments where discreet internal mounting and reduced installation work are important
