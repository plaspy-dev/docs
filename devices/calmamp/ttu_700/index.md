# CalmAmp - TTU-700

<div className="device-logo">
![TTU-700](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp TTU-700 is a battery operated non rechargeable trailer tracking device built for long term deployments where low maintenance and long life are priorities. It combines a compact form factor with an internal 57 Ah battery and integrated cellular and GPS antennas so units can be mounted flexibly on trailers and other assets without additional wired antennas.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TTU-700 can feed location and event information into Plaspy for fleet level visibility and operational oversight. Its support for standard cellular messaging and on board event rules, together with CalAmp over the air management, makes it a practical choice when you want persistent tracking of assets that are deployed for extended periods.

## Key Highlights

- Battery operated, non rechargeable design intended for long term deployments with reduced maintenance needs.
- Internal 57 Ah battery and compact size for discreet mounting and extended field life.
- Built in cellular and GPS antennas eliminate the need for external wired antennas and simplify placement.
- Supports multiple cellular network families and uses SMS or UDP messaging for reliable communications.
- On board programmable event engine PEG for configurable alerts based on time motion location and input conditions.
- Over the air device management with PULS for remote configuration updates monitoring and firmware maintenance.
- Designed to lower total cost of ownership while improving field reliability and power efficiency.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the TTU-700 delivers location and event data to the platform where it can be presented, analyzed and acted on as part of fleet or asset management workflows. Plaspy ingests the device messages and makes device status and events available for monitoring and reporting.

- Real time and periodic location visibility within Plaspy dashboards for trailers and long term assets.
- Fleet monitoring and operational alerts driven by PEG rules surfaced in Plaspy for exception handling.
- Geofence and movement based notifications to detect location changes or unauthorized moves.
- Remote device state and health indicators shown in Plaspy to support maintenance planning.
- Use of PULS for over the air updates and parameter changes coordinated with Plaspy reporting and device lists.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term trailer tracking for fleets that leave assets parked or stored for extended intervals.
- Monitoring containers and static equipment that require occasional location checks without frequent servicing.
- Rental or leased assets where low maintenance and reliable tracking extend operational visibility.
- Yard and depot inventory management where discreet mounted devices reduce tampering and cabling.
- Seasonal or intermittent assets that need long battery life between service intervals.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TTU-700 is well suited to organizations that need a low maintenance tracking solution for trailers and other assets that will remain deployed for long periods. Its internal battery and integrated antennas support flexible installation options while PEG and PULS provide the on device intelligence and remote management capabilities that pair naturally with a fleet platform like Plaspy.

Choosing the TTU-700 with Plaspy gives operations teams a reliable combination of long life hardware and a platform capable of consolidating location, alerts, and device health into fleet level workflows. For users who prioritize low cost of ownership and simple field deployment, this tracker offers a balance of durability and remote manageability.
