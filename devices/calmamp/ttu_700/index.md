# CalmAmp - TTU-700

![TTU-700](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp TTU-700 is a battery-operated non-rechargeable trailer tracking device that is designed for long-term deployments without the need for maintenance. It is the perfect solution for managing assets that will be deployed for extended periods without regular servicing. 

Featuring a small size and superior GPS performance, the TTU-700 is equipped with an internal 57 Ah battery. This complete trailer and asset tracking device utilizes next-generation GPS technology on GSM/GPRS, CDMA 1xRTT, and HSPA cellular networks. With its superior internal antennas for both cellular and GPS, there is no need for wired antennas, making installation easy and cost-effective. The TTU-700 can be mounted virtually anywhere on the trailer, ensuring convenience and flexibility. 

The TTU-700 utilizes enhanced SMS or UDP messaging to transport messages across the cellular network, providing a reliable communication link between the device and your application servers. This device is designed to reduce the cost of ownership, power consumption, and size while improving field reliability. 

One of the standout features of the TTU-700 is its flexibility. It employs CalAmp's industry-leading on-board alert engine, PEG™ \(Programmable Event Generator\), which monitors external conditions and supports customer-defined exception-based rules. This allows you to customize the device to meet the specific needs of your application. PEG continuously monitors the vehicle environment and responds instantly to pre-defined threshold conditions such as time, date, motion, location, geo-zone, input, and other event combinations. 

Additionally, the TTU-700 leverages CalAmp's over-the-air device management and maintenance system, PULS™ \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\). This system allows for easy configuration parameter updates, PEG rule modifications, and firmware upgrades over the air. With PULS, you can monitor the health status of units across your fleet, enabling you to quickly identify and address any issues before they become costly problems. 

With its reliable performance, ease of installation, and advanced features, the CalmAmp TTU-700 is an excellent choice for trailer and asset tracking applications that require long-term, low-maintenance deployments.
