# CalmAmp - LMU-300

<div className="device-logo">
![LMU-300](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The CalmAmp LMU-300 is a compact and cost effective GPS tracker designed for discreet and reliable installation in vehicles. It is positioned for common automotive applications such as stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance monitoring, and auto rental management. The LMU-300 emphasizes dependable location reporting and practical form factor to fit a variety of mobile vehicle deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LMU-300 can feed vehicle location and status information into Plaspy for fleet monitoring and operational oversight. Its on board event engine and over the air management capabilities make it a useful device for organizations that want programmable alerts, centralized device maintenance, and visibility across mixed fleets when managed through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact, discreet form factor suited to vehicle installations and fleet deployments
- Positioned for stolen vehicle recovery finance monitoring and rental fleet use
- Programmable Event Generator PEG allows custom rules based on time motion location geo zones and inputs
- Over the air management via PULS for configuration updates PEG changes and firmware updates
- Cost effective option that balances advanced features with field reliability
- Designed to deliver consistent GPS performance for routine tracking and recovery workflows
- Practical choice for fleets requiring remote maintenance and health monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LMU-300 integrates with Plaspy by supplying location updates and event notifications that Plaspy can present in dashboards alerts and reports. Plaspy can leverage the device's programmable rules and remote management signals to correlate device state with operational workflows and fleet performance metrics.

- Real time or periodic location visibility for individual vehicles and entire fleets
- Event driven alerts in Plaspy based on PEG conditions such as motion location or custom thresholds
- Geo zone monitoring and notifications to support secure parking and rental boundaries
- Fleet level device health and status tracking aided by the LMU-300 over the air management data
- Reporting and historical playback for route analysis utilization and incident review

## Typical Use Cases

- Stolen vehicle recovery and tracking to support rapid response
- Vehicle finance and asset monitoring where location visibility is required
- Rental car fleet management including check in check out and boundary enforcement
- General fleet tracking for utilization reporting route verification and dispatch coordination
- Remote monitoring of vehicle status and lifecycle maintenance indicators
