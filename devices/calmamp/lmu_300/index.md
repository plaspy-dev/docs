# CalmAmp - LMU-300

![LMU-300](./logo.jpg)

The CalmAmp LMU-300 is a cost-effective and feature-rich GPS tracker designed for discreet and reliable installation in vehicles. It is an ideal solution for various automotive tracking applications, including stolen vehicle recovery, vehicle finance, and auto rental. With its compact size and advanced features, the LMU-300 offers superior GPS performance and can be easily installed in any 12/24 volt mobile vehicle.

One of the standout features of the LMU-300 is its flexibility. It incorporates CalAmp's advanced on-board alert engine, PEG \(Programmable Event Generator\), which allows you to monitor external conditions and set up custom exception-based rules to meet your specific application requirements. Whether it's monitoring time, date, motion, location, geo-zones, or inputs, the LMU-300 can be programmed to respond instantaneously to pre-defined threshold conditions.

In addition to its flexibility, the LMU-300 also offers air serviceability through CalAmp's management and maintenance system, PULS \(Programming, Updates, and Logistics System\). This system allows for over-the-air configuration parameters, PEG rules, and firmware updates, making it easy to configure and maintain the tracker without the need for manual intervention. With PULS, you can monitor the health status of the LMU-300 across your fleet, identifying any potential issues before they become costly problems.

Overall, the CalmAmp LMU-300 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that combines affordability with advanced features. Whether you need to track vehicles for security purposes or manage a fleet of rental cars, the LMU-300 is a reliable choice that offers excellent field reliability and ease of use.
