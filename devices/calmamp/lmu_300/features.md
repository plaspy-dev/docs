# CalmAmp - LMU-300 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive overview of the CalmAmp LMU-300 features as they relate to use with the Plaspy platform. It describes the model's practical capabilities, the kinds of monitoring and event logic it supports, and how those capabilities typically appear to fleet and asset managers using Plaspy for vehicle tracking and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and the manufacturer's implementation. Where relevant, this page highlights general capabilities and how they map to Plaspy functionality, but owners should verify specifics against the device firmware release notes and manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LMU-300 is positioned as a compact, cost effective vehicle tracker with flexible event logic and over the air maintenance support. It is designed for discreet installation in 12 and 24 volt vehicles and for use cases that need reliable location reporting and customizable alerting.

- Compact form factor for discreet vehicle installation and minimal intrusion
- Compatible with standard 12 volt and 24 volt vehicle power systems
- High quality GPS tracking performance for location accuracy in automotive use
- PEG programmable event generator for custom rules based on time date motion location geo zones and inputs
- PULS remote management for over the air configuration, PEG rule updates, and firmware maintenance
- Practical suitability for stolen vehicle recovery vehicle finance and auto rental tracking

## Core Features of CalmAmp - LMU-300

- GPS location reporting designed for vehicle tracking applications
- Compact design for discreet placement in automotive environments
- Support for 12 volt and 24 volt vehicle electrical systems
- PEG Programmable Event Generator to create exception based rules tied to time date motion location geo zones and device inputs
- PULS based over the air updates for configuration distribution and firmware management
- Remote health and status reporting when used with fleet management systems and device management tools
- Intended use cases include stolen vehicle recovery vehicle finance monitoring and auto rental fleet tracking
- Flexible installation that adapts to diverse vehicle types and fleet setups

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an LMU-300 is connected to Plaspy, its core capabilities translate into visibility and event driven monitoring inside the Plaspy platform. Plaspy receives device reports and presents location and event information to operators without requiring deep device level configuration in the UI.

- Real time and historical location points become visible on Plaspy maps for route and stop analysis
- PEG generated events and exceptions can be mapped to Plaspy event logs to drive notifications and operational workflows
- Device health indicators reported by the tracker can be surfaced in Plaspy to flag units that need attention
- Over the air configuration and firmware updates managed by the manufacturer can change device behavior that Plaspy will reflect in subsequent reports
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and ingests device data so units appear in a unified fleet view

- Vehicle finance tracking to verify asset location and movement
- Auto rental fleet monitoring for location and activity oversight
- General fleet visibility for small to medium vehicle fleets
