# LK-GPS - LK900

<div className="device-logo">
![LK900](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The LK900 is a compact magnet car tracker from LK-GPS designed for flexible asset and vehicle tracking. It provides real time location updates and common monitoring features such as history trace review, geo fencing, overspeed alerts, and low battery notifications. The device is built for use on cars, rental vehicles, equipment, containers, and cargo where durable, continuous tracking is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK900 can feed location and event data into the Plaspy fleet platform for consolidated monitoring and reporting. Its combination of long standby operation, strong magnetic mounting, and IP67 durability make it a practical option for organizations that want to add persistent tracking points to their Plaspy deployment without specialized on vehicle wiring.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking for immediate location visibility
- Long standby operation with several built in battery capacity options to support extended deployments
- Strong magnetic mounting for easy attachment to metal surfaces
- IP67 waterproof rating for reliable use in challenging environments
- History trace and blind area tracking for reviewing past movements
- Alerts including geo fence, overspeed, low battery, movement, shock, and dropped device notifications

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the LK900 supplies location and event information that appears alongside other devices in the platform, enabling centralized oversight and historical review. Plaspy users can incorporate LK900 devices into existing fleets and operational views to improve situational awareness and response.

- Display live location and recent tracks on Plaspy maps for fleet level visibility
- Receive and manage alerts in Plaspy for geo fence breaches, overspeed events, and low battery notices
- Use history traces from the LK900 to generate movement reports and review past routes
- Group and organize LK900 assets within Plaspy for role based monitoring and targeted notifications
- Include LK900 devices in routine operational checks to track equipment, rentals, or temporary assets

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring private cars and company vehicles for location history and movement alerts
- Tracking rental vehicles and short term assets where removable mounting is required
- Securing equipment, containers, or cargo in transit with discreet magnetic attachment
- Covert or temporary tracking needs that benefit from easy relocation and long standby time
- Fleet operations that require basic event alerts and historical route review

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LK900 is a practical choice for organizations that need a durable, easy to deploy tracker that integrates with a centralized fleet platform like Plaspy. Its magnetic mounting and waterproof construction allow rapid deployment across many asset types, and the device provides the common visibility and alert features fleet managers rely on for day to day operations.

Because the LK900 is compatible with Plaspy, teams can add it into existing monitoring workflows to extend coverage without complex changes to their platform. Plaspy can consolidate LK900 device data with other trackers to provide unified reporting, alerts, and operational oversight across a mixed fleet.
