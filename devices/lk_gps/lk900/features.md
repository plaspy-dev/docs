# LK-GPS - LK900 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the LK-GPS LK900 GPS Car Magnet Tracker and how those capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing functions, operational value, and practical behavior that matter when the device reports location and events to the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, the specific LK900 hardware revision (A, B, or C variants), installation method, and manufacturer implementation choices. For the latest device specific details consult the official LK GPS documentation and firmware notes.

## Feature Overview

The LK900 is a magnet-mounted GPS tracker built for long standby operation and persistent location reporting. It combines GPS position reporting with cellular connectivity to deliver location, historical traces, and event alerts suitable for vehicle and asset monitoring.

- Real time tracking for immediate position updates to a tracking platform
- Long standby operation with selectable battery capacity variants for extended deployments
- History trace checking to review past routes and movement over time
- Geo fence support to receive notifications when an asset enters or leaves a defined area
- Alerts for low battery, overspeed, movement, drop, and shock events
- IP67 rated enclosure and built in magnet for secure attachment in outdoor conditions

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK900

- GPS plus GSM connectivity for position reporting and remote updates
- Multiple battery capacity options (5000 mAh, 10000 mAh, 20000 mAh) for extended standby
- Real time and auto tracking modes for continuous or scheduled reporting
- History trace logging for playback of past routes and locations
- Geo fencing with event alerts when boundaries are crossed
- Movement alarm and dropped alarm to detect unexpected device movement or detachment
- Shock alarm for impact detection and low battery alert to signal power remaining
- IP67 waterproof rating and powerful built in magnet for durable mounting on metal surfaces

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location reports and event messages from the LK900 and presents them in maps, timelines, and alert streams so teams can monitor assets and review past activity. Plaspy is designed to detect supported tracker protocols automatically and handle the common tracking events LK900 produces.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness
- Historical tracks are available for route review and playback in Plaspy
- Geo fence events generate notifications and can be included in Plaspy alert rules
- Battery level alerts and movement or shock events surface in device event logs
- Device attachments such as IP67 and magnet mounting are noted for installation context in asset records
- Plaspy accepts standard tracker reports and will integrate LK900 messages into vehicle and asset dashboards

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking private cars and rental vehicles for location and history monitoring
- Monitoring mobile equipment or portable assets where long battery life is required
- Securing containers and cargo during transit with geofence and movement alerts
- Covert or semi-permanent installations on metal vehicles or equipment using the magnet mount
- Fleet oversight where history trace and event alerts support operational audits
- Remote assets operating in outdoor environments that require waterproof protection

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some functions and alert types depend on the LK900 firmware version and feature set offered by the manufacturer for A B or C variants.
- Battery runtime will vary with reporting frequency, environmental conditions, and configuration such as auto tracking versus sleep modes.
- Regional cellular compatibility and SIM configuration can affect connectivity and report delivery in certain markets.
- Physical installation and the presence of metal surfaces influence GPS reception and magnet mount performance.
- For definitive behavior, feature lists, and firmware change logs refer to the manufacturer documentation.
