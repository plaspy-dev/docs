# LK-GPS - LK610 Features

This page provides a public, features-oriented overview of the LK-GPS LK610 and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for location monitoring and event visibility. It is intended to help prospective users and operators understand the practical functions of the device when used with Plaspy and what to expect in terms of monitoring and notifications.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation method. The information below is drawn from the device description and is intended as a general, non-sensitive summary; consult the manufacturer documentation for the most current device-specific details.

## Feature Overview

The LK610 is a wearable SIM card GPS positioning terminal designed for personal use, with a focus on real-time location, safety alerts, and configurable reporting modes. Its practical design and notification features make it suitable for caregivers and guardians who need straightforward, continuous awareness.

- Real time tracking for continuous location visibility through online platforms and apps
- SOS alarm that sends an emergency alert to the tracking platform for rapid awareness
- Electronic fence entry and exit alerts to notify when the tracker crosses a configured boundary
- Multiple locating upload modes including timed reporting, continuous real time uploads, and fixed point reporting
- Low power and displacement alerts to signal battery issues and unexpected movement
- LED indicators for power and connectivity status to check device health at a glance

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK610

- Real time GPS positioning for live location updates
- SOS alarm function that reports an emergency to the platform
- Electronic fence entry and exit alarms for geofence monitoring
- Low battery alerts to indicate when the device requires charging or battery attention
- Displacement alerts driven by the onboard G sensor to detect movement
- Multiple locating data upload modes including timing, real time, and fixed point modes
- AGPS support to improve time to first fix and positioning reliability
- Wearable form factor with a standard SIM card slot for cellular connectivity

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and displays location and event data from compatible devices so teams can monitor movement, receive alerts, and review history from a single platform. The LK610's alert and reporting modes translate into discrete events and map updates inside Plaspy for operational visibility.

- Live location updates appear on the Plaspy map when the device reports in real time
- SOS events are surfaced as alerts so operators can see the device location and event time
- Geofence entry and exit produce notifications and event logs for boundary monitoring
- Low power and displacement alerts are recorded and shown in Plaspy event feeds
- Plaspy automatically detects the device protocol and accepts connections to the platform; devices can be pointed to d.plaspy.com and configured to use UDP or TCP on the standard Plaspy port if required
- Historical position data from timed or fixed point upload modes is retained and available for review in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and caregiver monitoring where discrete alerts and live location are required
- Personal tracking for vulnerable users who need quick notification and location visibility
- Supervision during outings or school trips with geofence boundaries defining safe areas
- Remote check in and status monitoring through periodic or event-driven location reports
- Rapid response scenarios where SOS alerts provide location context to responders

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior depends on firmware version and manufacturer configuration; not all functions may be present on every unit
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect supported network bands and connectivity behavior
- Installation and how the device is worn or placed can influence displacement and motion reporting
- SIM activation, network coverage, and local telecom policies can affect real time reporting and message delivery
- Always verify the exact capabilities and firmware release notes with the manufacturer for mission critical deployments

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the LK610 with Plaspy gives organizations a simple way to consolidate location updates, alerts, and historical data into a single monitoring platform. Plaspy presents SOS, geofence, low battery, and displacement events alongside map views and event histories so teams can act quickly and maintain situational awareness.
