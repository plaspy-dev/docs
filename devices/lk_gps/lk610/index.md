# LK-GPS - LK610

![LK610](./logo..jpg)

The KJ-GPS LK610 is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker designed for kids and personal use. With real-time tracking capabilities and an SOS alarm feature, it provides peace of mind for parents and caregivers. The LK610 offers multiple ways to query location, including through a mobile app, PC webpage, and WeChat. This ensures that you can easily keep track of your loved ones no matter where you are.

One of the standout features of the LK610 is its support for electronic fence entry/exit alarms, low power alerts, and displacement alerts. This means that you will be notified if the tracker goes outside of a designated area, if the battery is running low, or if it is being moved without authorization. The LED lamp on the device indicates the power, GPS, and GSM status, making it easy to check the device's status at a glance.

The LK610 supports various locating data uploading modes, including timing, real-time, and fixation point. This allows you to choose the most suitable mode for your needs. In case of an emergency, the SOS alarm feature will send an alarming message to the platform, ensuring that help can be dispatched quickly. Additionally, the LK610 supports AGPS, which enhances the GPS accuracy and provides faster and more reliable positioning.
