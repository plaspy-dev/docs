# LK-GPS - LK880 Cat1

![LK880 Cat1](./tracker.jpg)

The LK880-4G \(LK880 Cat1\) from Shenzhen Funny Auto Technology is a purpose-built waterproof GPS tracker for cats and dogs that is Plaspy compatible out of the box. Designed for pet owners who want reliable location awareness, activity telemetry, and targeted behavior control, the LK880-4G uses continuous 4G Cat‑1 connectivity to deliver fast, frequent position updates and activity summaries to both its companion mobile app and Plaspy's cloud platform.

As a compact, rugged pet GPS tracker, the LK880-4G combines real‑time tracking, historical route playback, blind‑zone supplementary reporting, configurable geofencing and displacement/low‑battery alarms with an integrated remote training collar capability. When integrated with Plaspy, the LK880 becomes part of a centralized GPS tracker ecosystem that consolidates real‑time tracking, telemetry and alerts for easy monitoring and rapid response.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: direct integration for centralized location and activity telemetry.
- Continuous 4G Cat‑1 connectivity for faster, more reliable real‑time tracking in urban and rural environments.
- Waterproof, durable build designed for everyday outdoor use on cats and dogs.
- Integrated remote training collar with adjustable correction levels to support behavior training sessions.
- Comprehensive safety alerts: geofence notifications, displacement alarms, and low‑battery warnings.
- Historical route playback and platform‑mode positioning queries for post‑event analysis and recovery planning.
- Companion iOS and Android app for interactive maps, activity statistics, and geofence management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the LK880-4G is paired with Plaspy, location and activity data reported by the device feed directly into Plaspy’s dashboard and alerting engine. This makes the LK880 a practical Plaspy compatible option for pet owners who want centralized monitoring and historical reporting alongside other GPS tracker devices in their account.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for immediate situational awareness.
- Configurable geofence events and instant alerts when a pet leaves a safe zone.
- Historical route playback available in Plaspy for recovering lost pets or reviewing activity patterns.
- Displacement and low‑battery alarms forwarded to Plaspy so you can act quickly.
- Platform‑mode positioning queries supported for on‑demand location checks.
