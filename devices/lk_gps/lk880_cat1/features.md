# LK-GPS - LK880 Cat1 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the LK-GPS LK880 Cat1 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, explains how the tracker reports location and activity data, and outlines the practical benefits of using the LK880 Cat1 in Plaspy for centralized monitoring and alerting.

Exact feature availability and behavior may vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device specific details please consult the official manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The LK880 Cat1 (also referenced as LK880-4G) is a compact waterproof tracker designed for cats and dogs that is Plaspy compatible out of the box. It emphasizes frequent position updates, activity telemetry, and owner controls such as configurable geofences and a remote training collar, making it suitable for pet recovery and supervised behavior control.

- Continuous 4G Cat1 connectivity for faster and more frequent position updates
- Activity telemetry and periodic activity summaries for movement awareness
- Configurable geofence alerts to notify when a pet leaves a designated area
- Displacement and low battery alarms to surface urgent conditions quickly
- Historical route playback for post event review and recovery planning
- Integrated remote training collar capability for supervised behavior sessions

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK880 Cat1

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for direct integration into a centralized tracking account
- Continuous 4G Cat1 cellular reporting for timely location updates
- Waterproof, compact form factor designed for everyday outdoor use on cats and dogs
- Real time location reporting with frequent position updates suitable for pet tracking
- Activity telemetry and summaries that provide visibility into movement patterns
- Configurable geofencing with alerts when a pet enters or exits a zone
- Displacement and low battery alarms that can trigger immediate notifications
- Integrated remote training collar with adjustable correction levels for supervised control

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the LK880 Cat1 is connected to Plaspy, device reports and events are routed into Plaspy's dashboard and alerting system so owners and administrators can monitor pets alongside other tracked assets. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols for straightforward integration and centralized visibility.

- Real time location and activity updates appear in Plaspy for immediate situational awareness
- Geofence events are reflected as alerts so you can act when a pet leaves a safe zone
- Historical route playback stored from the device is available in Plaspy for review and recovery
- Displacement and low battery alarms are forwarded to Plaspy to prompt rapid response
- Remote training collar activity and related status updates are visible alongside other telemetry
- Consolidated reporting lets you monitor multiple devices and compare activity over time

## Typical Use Cases

- Rapid pet location recovery using Plaspy maps and frequent position updates
- Daily activity monitoring to review movement patterns and outdoor time
- Safe zone enforcement for roaming pets with instant geofence notifications
- Supervised behavior training using the integrated remote training collar
- Durable outdoor tracking for pets that spend time in wet or rugged conditions
- Centralized management of multiple pet trackers within a single Plaspy account
