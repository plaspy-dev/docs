# LK-GPS - LK980

![LK980](./logo..jpg)

The LK-GPS LK980 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed primarily for car tracking. Its small form factor and built in GPS and GSM antennas make it suitable for discreet placement in a vehicle. The LK980 supports mobile app tracking on both Android and Apple platforms and provides access via web based platforms, enabling convenient monitoring of vehicle location and reported remaining battery capacity.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK980 can be integrated into fleet and vehicle monitoring workflows to provide real time position updates and event alerts. Its 4G connectivity and a set of built in alarms such as low battery, vibration, removal, and overspeed make it a practical option for organizations and individual vehicle owners who need consistent visibility and historical route information on a central platform like Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact design for discreet placement in passenger vehicles and fleet cars
- Built in GPS and GSM antennas for integrated location reporting
- 4G connectivity for responsive communication with tracking platforms
- Mobile app and web based access for on the go and office monitoring
- Alarms for low battery, vibration, removal, and over speed to enhance security
- Historical route tracking and street view map functionality for context and reviews
- Long standby time and multi platform query for flexible monitoring needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the LK980 supplies location and event information to a centralized monitoring environment so teams can track vehicles, review history, and manage alerts from one place. Plaspy can surface the tracker data alongside other fleet devices to provide operational oversight and reporting.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring and dispatch
- Device alerts such as low battery, vibration, removal, and over speed can be forwarded to Plaspy alerting and notification workflows
- Historical route data from the LK980 is viewable in Plaspy for trip review and analysis
- Street view map functionality helps operators confirm vehicle positions against local context
- Multi platform access allows teams to query and view LK980 data from different devices via Plaspy
