# LK-GPS - LK710-CatM Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the LK-GPS LK710-CatM and explains how its capabilities operate when used with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing functions that matter for fleet management, vehicle security, and basic telemetry reporting into Plaspy dashboards and mobile clients.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current model specifications and firmware details consult the device manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The LK710-CatM is a compact, waterproof Cat‑M GPS tracker designed for discreet vehicle mounting and reliable position reporting into Plaspy. It combines integrated antennas, rugged enclosure, and a small footprint to deliver continuous location visibility and basic status telemetry suitable for commercial and personal vehicle deployments.

- Continuous real time tracking for fleet and vehicle monitoring visible in Plaspy
- IP68 rated enclosure and compact form factor for discreet installation on cars, motorbikes and e-bikes
- Integrated GPS and GSM antenna design to simplify installation and maintain signal reliability
- Remote engine oil cut off and resume controls to support immobilizer based anti theft workflows
- Battery capacity reporting to Plaspy for basic device health and maintenance planning
- SMS configuration options for lightweight remote setup and queries

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK710-CatM

- Cat‑M cellular connectivity for efficient mobile data reporting
- Integrated GPS positioning with onboard antenna for accurate location feeds
- IP68 waterproof enclosure for outdoor and vehicle exposed installations
- Compact, low profile form factor suitable for a variety of vehicle types
- Remote engine oil cut off and resume controls providing immobilizer functionality
- Remaining battery capacity reporting for device health monitoring
- SMS based configuration and remote queries for simple field setup
- Live access compatibility with Android, iOS and browser based interfaces

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected, the LK710-CatM sends positional and status updates that Plaspy ingests and presents for monitoring, alerting, and historical reporting. Plaspy surfaces the device location, battery status, and immobilizer events so operators can maintain situational awareness and act on security or operational triggers.

- Display of live vehicle location and recent track history on Plaspy maps and views
- Visibility of remaining battery capacity to support maintenance scheduling in Plaspy
- Immobilizer event logging such as engine oil cut off and resume visible for audit and response
- SMS based setup and queries can be used to configure devices before or during Plaspy onboarding
- Unified access to device data via Plaspy mobile apps and browser interfaces for dispatch and field teams

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route oversight for light commercial vehicles and delivery fleets
- Anti theft workflows using remote immobilizer controls to prevent unauthorized vehicle use
- Rental and shared vehicle monitoring for cars, motorbikes and micromobility assets
- Last mile delivery tracking and proof of service through live location updates
- Asset protection for vehicles stored outdoors or operating in harsh environments
