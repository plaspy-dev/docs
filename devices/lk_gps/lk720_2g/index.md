# LK-GPS - LK720-2G

![LK720-2G](./tracker.jpg)

The LK720-2G Mini Relay GPS Tracker is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for discreet vehicle security and reliable real-time tracking. Designed for cars and motorcycles, the LK720-2G pairs built-in GPS and GSM antennas with a mini relay output to deliver anti-theft immobilizer capabilities, shock-triggered alarms, and continuous telemetry—making it a practical choice for owners and fleet managers who require straightforward integration with Plaspy’s tracking platform.

The LK720-2G supports browser/server \(B/S\) platforms and native mobile apps for Android and iOS, providing live location updates, historical routes, and battery status in the Plaspy dashboard. With SMS parameter configuration and query functions as a fallback, this 2G GPS tracker keeps vehicles connected in areas where internet access may be intermittent, while giving rental operators, logistics teams, and private owners an easy path to remote control and fleet management via Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: Seamless integration for real-time tracking and reporting on Plaspy’s platform and apps.
- Compact, discreet form factor: Easy installation in cars and motorcycles for covert vehicle tracking.
- Mini relay immobilizer: Remote fuel/engine cut-off to support anti-theft and immobilization workflows.
- Shock wake-up and alarm: Power-efficient shock detection to conserve battery while remaining responsive to impacts or unauthorized movement.
- 2G GSM connectivity with SMS fallback: Keeps telemetry and alerts flowing even when data services are unavailable.
- Telemetry and diagnostics: Reports remaining battery status, historical routes, and location updates for improved fleet management.
- Simple configuration: SMS commands, web-based setup, or mobile app management for rapid deployment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the LK720-2G with Plaspy delivers reliable real-time tracking and practical remote-control features. The tracker transmits GPS coordinates and onboard telemetry over 2G GSM to Plaspy’s cloud service, allowing dispatchers and vehicle owners to monitor live location, review historical routes, and receive status alerts through the Plaspy web portal or apps. When internet connectivity is limited, SMS-based parameter configuration and status queries act as a robust fallback to maintain control and diagnostics.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live tracking and fleet management dashboards.
- Shock alarm and movement alerts pushed to Plaspy to support anti-theft notifications and incident response.
- Battery status reporting visible in Plaspy for proactive maintenance and uptime management.
- Remote immobilizer \(via mini relay\) control through Plaspy workflows or authorized commands for theft mitigation.
- SMS configuration/query enables setup and troubleshooting when internet services are unavailable.
