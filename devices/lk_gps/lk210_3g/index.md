# LK-GPS - LK210-3G

![LK210-3G](./tracker.jpg)

The LK210-3G is a compact, easy-to-install GPS tracker engineered for motorcycles, cars, and trucks and presented here as a Plaspy compatible tracking solution. With built-in GPS and GSM antennas plus an integrated vibration sensor and shock wake-up, the LK210-3G delivers reliable real-time tracking and anti-theft alerts for personal vehicles and fleet assets. Its magnetic mounting and small form factor make deployment fast and non‑intrusive, while mobile apps and a browser-based platform provide flexible monitoring and control.

Designed for both individual owners and fleet operators, the LK210-3G pairs straightforward installation with robust telemetry and long standby performance. Plaspy users get consistent location updates, tamper and vibration alerts, and battery status reporting from the tracker to support fleet management, security, and logistics workflows. SMS command support adds resilience in regions with limited data connectivity.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management integration.
- Built-in GPS and GSM antennas for reliable position fixes and cellular connectivity without external antennas.
- Integrated vibration sensor with shock wake-up for anti-theft and tamper detection.
- Powerful internal battery plus low-power modes for extended standby and reduced maintenance.
- Compact, magnetic design for quick attachment to metal surfaces—ideal for motorcycles and rental vehicles.
- Flexible access via Android/iOS apps, browser-based \(B/S\) platform, and SMS commands for remote querying and configuration.
- OEM customization options to support branded or large-scale deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LK210-3G streams location and status data into a centralized platform so you can manage assets, monitor routes, and respond to incidents in real time. Plaspy normalizes the device’s telemetry into maps, alerts, and reports that fleet managers and security teams rely on. The tracker’s built-in features are exposed to Plaspy for automated workflows and notification rules.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and route history.
- Vibration and shock alerts forwarded to Plaspy for instant anti-theft notifications and tamper detection.
- Battery and standby status reported to Plaspy to schedule maintenance and avoid unexpected downtime.
- SMS command support for remote configuration or querying where data connectivity is limited; those responses can be reflected in Plaspy when the device is online.
- Browser and mobile app integration provides redundant access to Plaspy dashboards and alarms for on-the-go fleet management.
