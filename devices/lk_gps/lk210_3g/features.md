# LK-GPS - LK210-3G Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the LK-GPS LK210-3G and how it is used with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities that Plaspy can surface for monitoring and managing vehicles and assets when the LK210-3G is part of your telemetry fleet.

Exact feature availability and behavior may vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest specifications, consult the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LK210-3G is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker designed for motorcycles, cars, and trucks, and presented here as a Plaspy compatible option. It combines location reporting with a vibration based anti theft capability and simple mounting to enable quick deployments and persistent monitoring.

- Real time GPS location reporting for live tracking and route history.
- Built in GSM connectivity with support for SMS commands for remote querying and basic configuration.
- Integrated vibration sensor and shock wake up for anti theft and tamper alerts.
- Internal battery with low power modes for extended standby and reduced maintenance frequency.
- Compact magnetic mounting for discreet and fast installation on metal surfaces.
- Access to monitoring and alerts via mobile apps and a browser platform for flexible oversight.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK210-3G

- Built in GPS receiver for precise positioning and route logging.
- Integrated GSM connectivity for cellular reporting and SMS command support.
- Vibration sensor with shock wake up to detect tampering and movement.
- Internal rechargeable battery with low power standby modes to extend operational life.
- Magnetic mounting and small form factor for quick attachment and discreet placement.
- Mobile app and browser based configuration and monitoring options.
- OEM customization available for branded or large deployments.
- Designed for vehicle applications including motorcycles, cars, and trucks.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes the tracker telemetry so teams can view location, status, and alarm events in centralized dashboards and reports. The LK210-3G features map naturally to common Plaspy workflows for monitoring, alerting, and maintenance planning.

- Live location and route history displayed on Plaspy maps for operational oversight.
- Vibration and shock alerts forwarded into Plaspy so security teams receive timely notifications.
- Battery and standby status reported to Plaspy to help schedule recharge or maintenance before devices go offline.
- SMS command resilience helps when data connectivity is limited and Plaspy reflects updated status once the device reconnects.
- Browser and mobile access provides redundant ways to review LK210-3G events and respond to alarms while on the move.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft monitoring where vibration alerts trigger rapid response workflows.
- Rental vehicle tracking and recovery using discreet magnetic mounting and remote monitoring.
- Motorcycle and light vehicle tracking for owner peace of mind and recovery support.
- Logistics visibility for pickups, deliveries, and asset movement confirmation.
- General asset tracking for field equipment and occasional use assets where long standby is required.
- Small to medium fleet management that needs simple installation and mobile first access.
