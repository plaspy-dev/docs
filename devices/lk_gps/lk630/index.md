# LK-GPS - LK630

![LK630](./tracker.jpg)

The LK630 CatM is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for cats and small dogs that prioritizes comfort, reliable location updates, and easy integration. Using Cat‑M/4G connectivity together with multi‑technology positioning \(GPS plus Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth assistance\), the LK630 delivers real‑time tracking and configurable alerts so pet owners can quickly locate a roaming pet and receive timely notifications when a boundary is crossed or battery levels are low.

Lightweight and water‑resistant, the LK630 CatM attaches comfortably to collars and harnesses without impeding movement. Its design focuses on practical, dependable monitoring: frequent location updates in outdoor and urban environments, movement/activity reporting, and a companion iOS/Android app for on‑the‑go alerts. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the LK630 brings pet‑focused telemetry into the Plaspy ecosystem for centralized monitoring and notification management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed specifically for cats and small dogs — real‑time tracking and geofence alerts through the Plaspy platform.
- Multi‑technology positioning: GPS for outdoor accuracy with Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth assistance for improved urban and last‑meter performance.
- Cat‑M/4G connectivity for reliable, low‑power cellular reporting and frequent location updates.
- Compact, lightweight and water‑resistant enclosure that fits collars and harnesses comfortably.
- Configurable geofencing and instant notifications for safe‑zone entry/exit and low‑battery warnings via companion mobile app \(iOS and Android\).
- Movement and activity monitoring to track roaming behavior and detect unexpected motion patterns.
- Rechargeable battery with a flexible power profile — continuous active tracking up to ~4 hours; power‑saving configurations can extend operation to multiple days or longer depending on reporting intervals.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LK630 integrates with Plaspy to stream location and status data into a central dashboard and notification engine. Position fixes and assistive positioning information are transmitted over Cat‑M/4G and ingested by Plaspy for live visualization, rule‑based alerts, and historical route playback. Owners use the companion mobile app for immediate notifications, while Plaspy extends that data into configurable alerts, reports, and centralized device management.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates sent via Cat‑M/4G to Plaspy for live tracking and history.
- Geofence events: instant enter and exit notifications forwarded to owners through Plaspy rules.
- Movement and activity status reported for behavior monitoring and alert conditions.
- Low‑battery and status alerts delivered to the Plaspy platform and mobile app so you can recharge before the next outing.
- Bluetooth assistance and Wi‑Fi positioning information improve accuracy in urban and indoor‑edge environments; Plaspy stores those assistive data points with the location history.

## Use Cases

- Pet recovery: quickly locate a lost cat or small dog with real‑time tracking and historical route playback.
- Geofence protection: set safe zones around home or yard and receive immediate alerts on leave/enter events.
- Activity and roaming monitoring: track movement patterns and get notified of unusual activity while away from home.
- Urban tracking with assistive fixes: Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth assistance improve positioning when GPS alone is challenged by buildings or dense environments.
