# LK-GPS - LK930 Features

This page provides a public, user-facing summary of the LK-GPS LK930 tracker and how its core capabilities are used together with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on the practical features you can expect to see in Plaspy when this model is deployed, how those features add operational value, and which capabilities are specifically described by the manufacturer.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a functional overview and confirm device specific options, firmware details, and current limitations with the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LK930 is presented as a compact vehicle tracker designed for continuous tracking and long standby operation. It combines location reporting, event alerts, and optional locating aids to support vehicle monitoring and basic asset oversight.

- Real time GPS tracking with access via web and smartphone platforms for live position visibility.
- Long standby performance enabled by a 6000 mAh battery, designed to reduce maintenance for long term deployments.
- Multiple alert types including vibration, displacement, low battery, power off, and over-speed to flag unusual activity.
- Geo fence and history route features for boundary monitoring and retrospective route review up to the period indicated by the manufacturer.
- Assisted locating options such as AGPS and optional WiFi locating to improve fix speed and position reliability.
- One-way communication capability to listen to the device surroundings when that feature is enabled by the device and configured per regional rules.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK930

- Real time location reporting suitable for vehicle tracking and asset visibility.
- 6000 mAh battery with extended standby time as published by the manufacturer.
- Alert notifications for vibration, displacement, low battery, power off, and over-speed events.
- Geo-fence support to create virtual boundaries and detect breaches.
- History route storage and playback to review past movements for the period noted by the manufacturer.
- AGPS assistance and optional WiFi locating to enhance position acquisition.
- One-way audio listening for situational awareness when enabled on the device.
- GSM and GPRS network support with quad band compatibility as documented by the manufacturer.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests standard location and status reports from compatible trackers and presents them in a unified interface. When an LK930 is connected to Plaspy, the platform surfaces position, history, and event information so teams can monitor assets and respond to alerts.

- Live location shown on Plaspy maps for real time tracking and monitoring.
- Route history and playback available in Plaspy for retrospective analysis and route verification.
- Alerts and events such as low battery, power off, over-speed, and geo-fence breaches appear as events in Plaspy for operator awareness and reporting.
- Device status indicators such as battery and connectivity are available where the tracker reports that information.
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and can ingest the LK930 reporting format so device data appears without manual protocol selection.
- One-way audio is a device level feature; whether audio streams or recordings appear in Plaspy depends on device routing and platform configuration and may require the manufacturer app or additional setup.
