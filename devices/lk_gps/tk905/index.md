# LK-GPS - TK905

![TK905](./tracker.jpg)

The TK905 is a compact GPS tracker from LK GPS designed for discreet vehicle security and reliable real time tracking. Targeted at cars and motorcycles, the TK905 reports position data to online platforms and mobile apps, and is frequently chosen for anti theft protection, fleet visibility, and personal vehicle monitoring where a small form factor and extended standby life are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK905 can forward its location updates and alarm events into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and alerting. This makes it a practical option for teams that need straightforward remote tracking, basic telemetry, and configurable notifications without complex integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy for unified location and alert management across web and mobile interfaces.
- Compact, concealable form factor suitable for cars and motorcycles.
- Long standby battery with extended deployment between charges depending on reporting intervals.
- Configurable alarm suite including overspeed and shock or vibration alerts for tamper and impact detection.
- Power saving mode to extend battery life while maintaining periodic reporting.
- Remote wake and control options via vibration, SMS, or incoming call for low power operation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the TK905 transmits GPS positions and alarm events to the Plaspy platform, where those updates are displayed on maps and used to generate notifications and movement reports. Plaspy collects the incoming data to provide live tracking, history playback, and alert delivery for operational oversight.

- Real time location updates and history playback available in Plaspy map views.
- Overspeed events forwarded to Plaspy so administrators can be notified and review incidents.
- Shock and vibration alerts delivered to Plaspy to highlight potential tampering or collisions.
- Device wake and status updates reflected in Plaspy dashboards after communications are received.
- Adjustable reporting intervals and alert settings to balance tracking granularity and battery life.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet monitoring for basic position tracking and route history.
- Vehicle anti theft and recovery with discreet installation and alarm notifications.
- Motorcycle security where a small, low profile tracker is preferred.
- Parked vehicle tamper monitoring using shock or vibration alerts.
- Personal vehicle tracking for owners who want remote visibility and event alerts.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK905 is a practical choice for organizations and vehicle owners who need a simple, discreet tracker that delivers essential location reporting and alarm notifications. Paired with Plaspy, the device provides a straightforward way to centralize tracking data, receive actionable alerts, and review movement history without complex configuration.
