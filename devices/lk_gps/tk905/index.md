# TK905 GPS Tracker

The TK905 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for discreet vehicle security and reliable real-time tracking. Designed for cars and motorcycles, the TK905 delivers continuous GPS position reporting to online platforms and mobile apps \(Google Maps supported on app, PC and tablet\), making it a practical choice for anti-theft protection, fleet management visibility, and personal vehicle monitoring where concealment and long standby life matter.

The unit’s 3.7V 5000mAh rechargeable Li-ion battery and power-saving sleep mode allow extended deployment without frequent charging. TK905 supports configurable alerts — including overspeed and shock/vibration alarms — and can be woken by vibration, SMS or an incoming call. When paired with Plaspy, this tracker provides straightforward remote monitoring, alerting, and basic telemetry for everyday fleet and security needs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for discreet vehicle installation and real-time tracking.
- Long standby battery: built-in 3.7V 5000mAh Li‑ion battery with up to ~20 days standby \(depending on reporting interval\).
- Essential alarm suite: overspeed alarm and shock/vibration \(impact\) alarm for anti-theft and tamper detection.
- Power-saving sleep mode: GPS can be disabled to conserve power while GSM remains active at low consumption.
- Wake and control by vibration, SMS, or incoming call — practical for low-power vehicle deployments.
- Remote monitoring and configurable alerts through web and mobile apps; reports positions to standard tracking platforms \(Google Maps supported\).
- Small, easy-to-hide form factor optimized for motorcycles and covert vehicle mounting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the TK905 with Plaspy provides a simple path to live location, basic telemetry and alert management. The tracker transmits GPS positions and alarm events to online platforms; Plaspy ingests these updates to display location on maps, generate alerts, and produce movement reports for fleet management or security workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for map visualization and history playback.
- Overspeed alarm events forwarded to Plaspy and administrators so managers can address risky driving.
- Shock/vibration \(impact\) alerts sent when tampering or collisions are detected, enabling rapid response.
- Device wake and status queries via SMS or call — Plaspy dashboards reflect the latest device state when communications are received.
- Configurable alerts and reporting intervals: adjust how frequently the TK905 reports to Plaspy to balance battery life and tracking granularity.

## Use Cases

- Fleet monitoring: track vehicle locations, monitor speed, and collect movement history for route auditing and basic telemetry needs.
- Vehicle anti-theft and recovery: discreet installation with shock/vibration alerts to detect tampering and provide location data for recovery.
- Motorcycle security: small, easy-to-hide form factor ideal for scooters and motorcycles that require long standby times.
- Parked-vehicle tamper monitoring: receive shock alerts when a parked car experiences impact or vandalism.
- Personal vehicle monitoring: a low-profile solution for owners who want simple remote tracking and event alerts without frequent charging.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the TK905 for use with Plaspy gives you a straightforward, cost-effective GPS tracker that emphasizes discretion and long standby life. With built-in overspeed and shock alarms, SMS/call wake functionality, and remote reporting to web and mobile apps, the TK905 covers the most common needs for anti-theft protection and basic fleet management. Plaspy makes it easy to centralize location data and alerts, so operations teams get real-time tracking and actionable telemetry without complex integration.

If your deployment requires advanced telemetry such as fuel monitoring, a dedicated ignition input, remote immobilizer control, or Bluetooth sensor integration, verify those specific capabilities before purchase — they are not listed in the TK905 description. For many use cases where discreet installation, long battery life, and reliable location/alert reporting are primary needs, the TK905 paired with Plaspy provides a dependable, easy-to-manage solution.
