# LK-GPS - TK905 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the LK GPS TK905 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is focused on practical, non sensitive information that explains what the tracker can do within Plaspy for vehicle monitoring, alerting, and basic telemetry.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and regional manufacturer implementation. Always verify the specifics for your unit and firmware version when planning deployments or integrations.

## Feature Overview

The TK905 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for discreet vehicle and motorcycle installations. It emphasizes long standby life, basic alarm reporting, and simple remote wake and control methods that make it a practical option for anti theft monitoring and basic fleet visibility.

- Real time GPS position reporting to online platforms and apps for map visualization and history playback.
- Long standby battery with power saving sleep mode to reduce charging frequency during prolonged deployments.
- Overspeed and shock vibration alarm reporting to detect risky driving or tampering events.
- Wake and control options via vibration, SMS, or incoming call to trigger updates while conserving power.
- Small form factor suited to concealed mounting on cars and motorcycles for anti theft and personal monitoring.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - TK905

- Continuous GPS position reporting for live location and historical playback.
- Built in 3.7V 5000mAh rechargeable Li ion battery with multi day standby depending on reporting interval.
- Power saving sleep mode that conserves energy by reducing GPS activity while maintaining communication readiness.
- Overspeed alarm that can be configured to generate alerts when a configured speed threshold is exceeded.
- Shock and vibration alarm to indicate impact, tampering, or sudden movement.
- Wake on vibration plus wake and control via SMS or incoming call for low power remote updates.
- Compact and concealable enclosure intended for vehicle and motorcycle mounting.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the TK905 position updates and event messages so operators can visualize location, receive alerts, and review movement history in one central platform. Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols and makes incoming updates available for map display and alerting.

- Real time positions appear on Plaspy maps and can be reviewed in location history and playback.
- Overspeed events are forwarded into Plaspy alert streams so administrators can be notified and review incidents.
- Shock and vibration alerts arrive in Plaspy as event records to support tamper or collision response workflows.
- Device wake actions and SMS or call triggered updates result in refreshed device state visible in Plaspy dashboards.
- Adjustable reporting intervals supported by the tracker translate into configurable update frequency in Plaspy for a balance of battery life and tracking granularity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet vehicle tracking for theft deterrence and recovery support.
- Motorcycle security monitoring where small form factor and long standby are important.
- Basic fleet location visibility for small fleets needing position history and speed monitoring.
- Parked vehicle tamper detection using shock and vibration alerts to notify owners or managers.
- Personal vehicle monitoring for owners who want periodic location updates without frequent charging.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Reported features depend on the tracker firmware version and manufacturer configuration options.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants can change radio bands, available commands, or accessory support.
- Installation style and placement can affect GPS reception and wake responsiveness from vibration.
- Some device behaviors such as exact standby time, alarm thresholds, and wake sensitivity are configurable and may differ from unit to unit.
- Consult the manufacturer documentation for the most current technical specifications for your exact model and firmware.

Using the TK905 together with Plaspy provides a straightforward way to centralize position updates and event notifications into a single monitoring platform. Plaspy presents location, alert history, and basic telemetry so teams can maintain situational awareness and respond to overspeed or tamper events without managing multiple vendor interfaces.
