# LK-GPS - LK960-4G

![LK960-4G](./tracker.jpg)

The LK960-4G is a wired vehicle GPS tracker built for continuous, manufacturer-direct deployment in cars and motorcycles. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this tracker delivers reliable real-time tracking and robust anti-theft control for fleets, rental operations and security-focused distributors. With 4G connectivity \(and fallback to 2G where applicable\) and multi‑GNSS reception, the LK960-4G provides the positional accuracy and telemetry needed for professional fleet management and remote immobilization.

Designed for 24/7 operation on vehicle power, the LK960-4G combines wide voltage support, LED status indicators and a full set of vehicle alarms to help operations teams reduce theft risk, monitor driver behaviour, and optimize routing and maintenance. Its platform-friendly features — SMS queries and platform-based commands — make integration with Plaspy straightforward for centralized monitoring, alerts and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and centralized fleet management.
- 4G connectivity with 2G fallback to maintain telemetry where networks vary.
- Multi‑GNSS positioning \(GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou\) for dependable location updates.
- Wide input voltage \(10–100V\) supports cars, motorcycles and commercial vehicles.
- Comprehensive anti-theft controls including remote cut/recover of fuel or electricity \(immobilizer commands\).
- Rich alarm set: geofence, tamper/dismantle, vibration, power-off, displacement and overspeed warnings.
- Fleet telematics: trip history, mileage, idling time and driving behaviour \(harsh acceleration, braking and cornering\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LK960-4G streams the core telemetry and location data required for real-time tracking, alerting and historical reporting. Plaspy receives periodic position updates and event-driven alerts from the tracker, enabling immediate visualization, rule-based notifications and archived trip analytics within the Plaspy dashboard.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live vehicle tracking and map display.
- Ignition \(ACC\) state detection — visible in Plaspy to track engine on/off events and driving sessions.
- Geofence, tamper/dismantle, vibration, power-off and overspeed alerts forwarded to Plaspy for instant notifications.
- Remote immobilizer support \(cut/recover fuel or electricity\) accessible from Plaspy where configured for rapid anti-theft response.
- Fuel monitoring or external sensor integration can be supported via platform if external fuel sensors are installed; Bluetooth sensors are not specified in the device and should be confirmed before deployment.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: continuous real-time tracking, trip history, mileage logging and driver behaviour monitoring to optimize routes and reduce fuel consumption.
- Anti-theft protection: rapid immobilization via remote cut/recover commands, tamper and dismantle alarms, and displacement alerts for stolen vehicle response.
- Car rental operations: monitor vehicle usage, enforce geofences, track idling time and receive overspeed warnings to protect assets and control costs.
- Security companies and corporate fleets: centralized telemetry and alarm forwarding to Plaspy for incident response and compliance reporting.
- Wholesale/manufacturer-direct deployments: scalable option for resellers and distributors requiring CE/RoHS certified, wired trackers for large rollouts.
