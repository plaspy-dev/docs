# LK-GPS - LK209 A/B/C Features

This page provides a public, feature-oriented overview of the LK-GPS LK209 A/B/C tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for location, event telemetry, and history. It focuses on the practical, user-facing functions you can expect when the LK209 is connected to Plaspy for fleet and asset monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where the LK209 description omits a capability, consult the manufacturer documentation and the deployed device firmware to confirm current behavior before planning integrations or large scale rollouts.

## Feature Overview

The LK209 A/B/C is a magnetic, battery powered GPS tracker designed for long standby operation and discreet mounting. It pairs GPS positioning with GSM uplink to deliver live location and event data to Plaspy for real time tracking and post trip analysis.

- Magnetic mounting for quick covert placement on ferrous surfaces and vehicle chassis.
- Three battery variants (5000 mAh, 10000 mAh, 20000 mAh) to support days or weeks of standby depending on reporting intervals.
- GPS positioning plus GSM connectivity for live position reporting, route history, and event telemetry.
- Fleet focused alerts including geo-fencing, overspeed, movement and shock detection, and low battery warnings.
- Low power design and configurable sleep modes to reduce maintenance and extend deployment cycles.
- GSM fallback for continued reporting in areas where GPS is limited.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK209 A/B/C

- Magnetic housing for non-invasive, discreet installation on compatible surfaces.
- Three selectable battery capacities to match standby and tracking interval needs.
- GPS location fixes for live tracking and route history.
- GSM cellular uplink to transmit position and event data to Plaspy and other platforms.
- Geo-fence support for boundary alerts and event markers.
- Movement and shock detection for unauthorized movement and impact notifications.
- Overspeed alerting to flag speed events in Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Low battery warnings and sleep mode behavior to conserve power between reports.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected, the LK209 reports location fixes and events into Plaspy so operators can view live positions, review history, and receive alerts. Plaspy ingests the tracker data and exposes it across maps, dashboards, and reporting tools to support operational decisions.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear in Plaspy for live tracking and fleet visibility.
- Movement and shock detection create event markers and alert notifications in Plaspy.
- Geo-fence and overspeed events feed into Plaspy alerting workflows for immediate attention.
- Low battery and dropped movement warnings are surfaced to reduce unexpected downtime.
- Route history and playback are available in Plaspy for trip review and audit trails.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols so reported data becomes visible without manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, vans, and trucks requiring live position, route playback, and overspeed monitoring.
- Logistics and container tracking where magnetic mounting enables non intrusive placement on trailers and equipment.
- Rental and leasing operations for non permanent installations and vehicle recovery workflows.
- Remote asset protection for off grid equipment with long standby life and movement alerts.
- Personal vehicle security and discreet anti theft monitoring with historical route analysis.
