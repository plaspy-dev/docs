# LK-GPS - LK880

![LK880](./logo..jpg)

The KJ-GPS LK880 is a 4G waterproof pet GPS tracker and activity remote controller that also functions as a dog shock training collar. This versatile device is designed to keep your pet safe and well-trained, providing you with real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities. With the LK880, you can track your pet's location, count their steps, measure the distance they walk, and even set up a geo-fence to receive alerts if they wander outside a designated area.

One of the standout features of the LK880 is its dog training function, which allows you to remotely control and train your dog using the shock training collar. This feature can be especially useful for teaching your pet to obey commands and stay within boundaries. Additionally, the LK880 offers a blind zone supplementary report, ensuring that you receive accurate and up-to-date information about your pet's location even in areas with poor GPS signal.

With its 4G network connectivity, the LK880 provides fast and reliable tracking capabilities. It also offers a platform mode query positioning, allowing you to easily access and view your pet's location history. The device is equipped with GPS and LBS positioning, ensuring accurate and precise tracking. It also features a displacement and low battery alarm, alerting you if your pet goes missing or if the device needs to be charged.

Overall, the KJ-GPS LK880 is a feature-packed pet GPS tracker and training collar that offers real-time tracking, training capabilities, and various other useful features. Whether you want to keep your pet safe or train them to be well-behaved, the LK880 is a reliable and effective solution.

#### Key Features:

- Dog training function
- Real-time tracking
- Real-time monitoring
- Blind Zone Supplementary Report
- Platform mode query positioning
- Historical track
- Geo-fence
- Health status
- Displacement / low battery alarm
- GPS + LBS positioning

#### Technical Specifications:

- Network: 4G
- Weight: 125g
- Band / MHz: GPS
- GPS sensitivity: -159dBm
- GPS accuracy: 5M
