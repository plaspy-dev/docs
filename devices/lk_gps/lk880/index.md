# LK-GPS - LK880

<div className="device-logo">
![LK880](./logo..jpg)
</div>

The LK880 is a compact 4G waterproof pet GPS tracker and activity remote controller that also functions as a dog training collar. Designed for pet owners who need both location visibility and basic training controls, the device offers real time tracking, step counting, distance monitoring, geo fence alerts, and historical track queries. It also reports displacement and low battery events and provides supplementary reporting to help with location gaps in areas of weak GPS reception.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK880 can be integrated into fleet and asset tracking workflows on Plaspy to provide centralized visibility of pets and collars alongside other tracked assets. Plaspy can present the LK880 location updates, alerts, and historical routes within its platform so owners and operators can monitor movements, receive notifications when geofence boundaries are crossed, and review past activity for operational or care decisions.

## Key Highlights

- 4G network connectivity for timely location updates and remote communication
- Waterproof design suitable for use on dogs and outdoor conditions
- Real time tracking and monitoring using GPS and LBS positioning
- Activity features including step count and distance walked to support wellness monitoring
- Geo fence support and displacement plus low battery alerts for prompt notifications
- Remote dog training controller function paired with blind zone supplementary reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the LK880 sends position updates and event notifications that Plaspy displays and manages through its mapping and alerting tools. This lets users view current location, replay historical tracks, and act on device alerts from a single interface.

- Show real time device locations on Plaspy maps for immediate visibility
- Trigger Plaspy alerts when the LK880 reports geofence breaches or low battery conditions
- Store and review historical tracks and platform mode query positioning inside Plaspy reports
- Aggregate activity data such as step counts and distance for summary reports
- Use supplementary reports to help interpret gaps in coverage or periods with limited GPS reception

## Typical Use Cases

- Locating lost or wandered pets quickly with real time position tracking
- Managing remote training sessions and monitoring responses to commands
- Monitoring daily activity and distance for pet health and exercise tracking
- Setting safe boundaries with geo fences and receiving instant alerts on breaches
- Coordinating care across multiple pets or between owners and pet service providers

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LK880 combines core pet tracking functions with training control and activity monitoring, making it a practical choice for owners and small operations that need both oversight and basic remote training capabilities. Paired with Plaspy, the device's location and event data become part of a broader monitoring and reporting environment that helps simplify day to day tracking and alert management.
