# LK-GPS - LK800 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the LK-GPS LK800 personal GPS tracker and how its capabilities are presented and used within Plaspy. It is written to help administrators, caregivers, and technical evaluators understand which user-facing functions of the LK800 map into Plaspy workflows for real time monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by device firmware version, hardware revision, installation style, accessory fitment, and manufacturer configuration. For implementation specifics, configuration steps, and the latest technical details consult the official LK-GPS documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The LK800 is a compact 4G personal tracker designed for frequent position updates and safety workflows. In practical terms it focuses on reliable location reporting, emergency alerting, and caregiver communications while remaining small and discreet for everyday carry.

- Frequent real time location updates with manufacturer-stated capability down to 5 second intervals for active monitoring.
- Prominent SOS button and automatic fall detection to quickly notify caregivers or administrators.
- Two-way voice and remote listening to enable direct communication and situational checks.
- Multi system GNSS with A-GPS assistance for improved fixes in weak signal conditions.
- Battery status reporting and location history retention visible in Plaspy for service and review.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK800

- 4G cellular connectivity for continuous reporting and remote telemetry.
- Real time tracking with configurable update intervals as supported by the device firmware.
- SOS alarm button that sends immediate location and alert events.
- Automatic fall detection to generate alerts when an impact or sudden fall is detected.
- Two-way voice communication and remote listening capabilities.
- Multi system GNSS navigation with A-GPS assistance for faster position fixes.
- Compact, water resistant form factor with internal antennas for discreet carry.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an LK800 is added to Plaspy, the device’s location and event messages are consumed by the platform and displayed alongside other assets for unified operational visibility. Plaspy is designed to present personal tracker telemetry in the same monitoring and reporting environment used for other compatible devices.

- Real time location and telemetry appear on Plaspy maps and monitoring dashboards for immediate situational awareness.
- SOS alerts and fall detection events are routed to authorized contacts and platform alarms so response teams can act quickly.
- Two way voice and remote listening are available as part of the device workflow for direct communication from monitoring staff or caregivers.
- Battery status and device health information are surfaced in Plaspy so low power devices can be identified and serviced.
- Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol and accepts device reports; LK800 units may be configured to report to Plaspy endpoints such as d.plaspy.com using TCP or UDP on the shared platform port.
- Historical routes and event logs can be replayed in Plaspy for incident review and reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Family safety and child location monitoring with instant SOS notifications and geofence oversight.
- Elder care and assisted living scenarios where fall detection and two way voice reduce response times.
- Student and lone worker monitoring for campus safety, fieldwork, or supervision of individuals.
- Pet tracking when used with appropriate accessories that support safe attachment.
- Personal security and guardian monitoring where compact form factor and discreet carry are priorities.
- Short term deployments for event staffing or temporary care assignments that require portable personal tracking.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Reported update intervals, fall detection sensitivity, and voice capabilities depend on the device firmware and manufacturer settings.
- Hardware revisions, accessory choices, and installation method can affect antenna performance and effective GPS accuracy.
- Regional cellular bands and local network availability influence 4G performance and real time reporting reliability.
- Some operational features shown in Plaspy require the device to be configured correctly and authorized within the platform.
- Always check the LK-GPS user manual and firmware notes for feature lists and configuration options applicable to your device model and region.
