# LK-GPS - LK900A/B/C

![LK900A/B/C](./tracker.jpg)

The LK900A/B/C is a Plaspy compatible 4G magnetic GPS and GSM tracker designed for long term vehicle and asset monitoring. Offered in three high capacity battery variants, the LK900 series provides extended standby times and continuous location updates suitable for fleet managers, rental operators, logistics providers, and asset security teams. Its magnetic housing enables discreet, non permanent mounting that supports temporary deployments and fast redeployment between assets.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the LK900A/B/C delivers real time position updates and telemetry into Plaspy’s cloud services where data is visualized, reported, and archived. Movement detection, geofence alerts, and a suite of event notifications integrate with Plaspy’s platform and mobile app to give teams timely visibility and operational oversight without complex installation work.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible delivery of live positions and historical routes to Plaspy web and mobile interfaces for quick access.
- Multiple battery options including 5000 mAh, 10000 mAh, and 20000 mAh for extended standby and long deployments.
- 4G connectivity for timely location and telemetry updates across supported networks.
- Magnetic housing for discreet, non permanent mounting on vehicles, containers, and portable assets.
- Built in event reporting for geofence alerts, overspeed warnings, drop or detachment detection, vibration and shock alerts, and movement detection.
- Power saving modes with interval tracking to balance update frequency and battery life for long term monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once registered on the Plaspy platform, the LK900A/B/C transmits GPS location and device telemetry over 4G to Plaspy’s cloud where positions are displayed in real time and archived for reporting and playback. Plaspy aggregates events and location history so teams can monitor assets, respond to incidents, and review past routes from a single interface.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear in Plaspy for live tracking and dispatch.
- Geofence alerts and route history playback are available in Plaspy for route verification and compliance checks.
- Movement, drop or detachment, vibration, and shock alerts populate Plaspy event logs to support anti theft workflows and incident response.
- Low battery and overspeed notifications are sent to Plaspy so fleet managers receive proactive alerts for maintenance or intervention.
- Interval and periodic tracking modes preserve battery while Plaspy reconciles periodic updates into coherent route views.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars, vans, and light commercial vehicles where temporary mounting and long battery life are beneficial.
- Anti theft protection and tamper detection using movement and detachment alerts tied into Plaspy notifications.
- Logistics and delivery operations that need geofence monitoring and route history to verify service and performance.
- Container and portable asset tracking when non permanent attachment and extended standby are required.
- Rental and shared vehicle programs seeking fast redeployment and consistent telemetry without permanent wiring.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LK900A/B/C combines endurance, discreet mounting, and a broad event set that aligns well with Plaspy’s fleet monitoring and asset protection capabilities. Its battery choices and magnetic form factor make it practical for organizations that need flexible, temporary, or rotating deployments while keeping continuous visibility through Plaspy’s platform.

Plaspy can take the LK900A/B/C data streams and surface them in dashboards, reports, and event workflows that help teams act quickly and analyze operational trends. For customers who require additional vehicle level inputs or integrations, Plaspy can incorporate telemetry from this tracker alongside other data sources to support broader fleet management use cases.
