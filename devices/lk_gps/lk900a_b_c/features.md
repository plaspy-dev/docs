# LK-GPS - LK900A/B/C Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the LK-GPS LK900A/B/C tracker and describes how those features work when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, user facing capabilities such as real time location, event alerts, battery options, and magnetic mounting so fleet and asset teams can understand the tracker behavior inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a feature guide rather than a definitive technical manual, and consult manufacturer documentation for device specific details and the latest firmware notes.

## Feature Overview

The LK900A/B/C is a Plaspy compatible 4G magnet GPS tracker built for long term vehicle and asset monitoring. It combines extended battery options, compact magnetic mounting, and a range of event alerts to deliver continuous location and telemetry into Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and incident response.

- Real time location updates and historical route recording delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and playback
- High capacity battery options for extended standby and long deployments without vehicle power
- Magnetic housing for quick and discreet non permanent mounting on vehicles and assets
- Event alerts including geofence, movement detection, drop or detachment, vibration or shock, low battery, and overspeed
- Power saving modes and interval tracking to balance update frequency and battery life
- History route playback for route verification and operational review inside Plaspy

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK900A/B/C

- Plaspy compatibility for seamless delivery of live positions and archived routes
- 4G LTE network connectivity for timely telemetry updates
- Multiple high capacity battery variants including 5000 mAh 10000 mAh and 20000 mAh
- Magnetic compact housing for temporary or rotating installations
- Geofence alerts for perimeter monitoring and policy enforcement
- Movement detection and drop or detachment alerts for anti theft monitoring
- Vibration or shock detection and low battery notifications to signal tampering or power issues
- Power saving modes including sleep and interval tracking to extend deployment life

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the LK900A/B/C is registered to Plaspy the device sends location and telemetry to the platform for visualization, alerting, and archive. Plaspy reconciles position updates and events so operations teams can monitor activity and respond to incidents through the web dashboard and mobile interface.

- Live position updates appear on the Plaspy map for dispatch and monitoring
- Geofence events and history playback are available for route verification and compliance checks
- Movement drop detachment vibration and shock alerts are recorded in Plaspy event logs for anti theft workflows
- Low battery and overspeed warnings are visible in Plaspy so teams can plan maintenance or interventions
- Interval and power saving tracking modes are represented in historical routes and status reports to reflect battery optimized deployments

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route oversight for medium and long haul vehicles
- Anti theft monitoring with movement and drop detachment alerts for rapid response
- Logistics and delivery tracking where geofence notifications and route history support operations
- Container and temporary asset tracking using magnetic mounting and long battery life
- Rental and shared vehicle monitoring for usage visibility without permanent installation

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets can vary by firmware release and hardware revision; manufacturer firmware updates can add or modify behavior
- Installation method and mounting location affect sensor performance and battery life for field deployments
- Regional variants and carrier support can influence 4G LTE connectivity and update frequency
- Battery capacity and expected standby times differ between the 5000 mAh 10000 mAh and 20000 mAh models
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols to simplify registration and integration

Using the LK900A/B/C with Plaspy gives organizations consolidated visibility over position and event telemetry without complex wiring or permanent installation. The combination of extended battery options magnetic mounting and a rich event set makes the tracker practical for temporary deployments, anti theft monitoring, and fleet oversight while Plaspy provides the visualization reporting and alerting needed to act on that data.
