# LK-GPS - LK120-4G Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the LK-GPS LK120-4G tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes practical capabilities you can expect when the device reports into the Plaspy platform and explains how those capabilities are surfaced for live monitoring, alerts, and history playback.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. The notes and examples on this page are grounded in the LK120-4G product description and are intended to help you understand typical behavior with Plaspy rather than to replace official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LK120-4G is a compact, waterproof tracker built for pet and personal use that pairs GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity and A-GPS assistance to deliver responsive location updates. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker provides live location, event reporting, and route history that are useful for owners who need real time situational awareness and simple management tools.

- Real time location reporting with A-GPS assisted fixes for faster position acquisition
- High update frequency suitable for responsive tracking and short term location monitoring
- Compact waterproof enclosure designed for collar mounting and outdoor use
- SOS button and two way voice support for emergency notification and situational checks
- Long term route history and playback for review of past movements and events

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK120-4G

- Out of the box compatibility with Plaspy for map display and event handling
- Built in GPS and cellular antennas with A-GPS assistance for faster fixes
- Position updates reported as frequently as every 5 seconds for responsive tracking
- Typical GNSS accuracy reported around 5 meters under normal conditions
- Compact, waterproof design intended for pet collars and personal carry
- Built in SOS button to trigger alerts and platform notifications
- Two way voice and ambient listening features for remote audio checks
- Power saving modes and shock wake up to extend usable battery life while preserving telemetry

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests telemetry and location streams from the LK120-4G so users can monitor devices on maps, receive event notifications, and review historical routes in one interface. The platform presents device events and status in ways that make it easier to act on location and safety information.

- Live map visualization of device position and movement for immediate situational awareness
- Geofence entry and exit notifications routed through Plaspy alerts to help owners respond quickly
- SOS events forwarded into the Plaspy event stream so authorized contacts are notified
- Two way voice and ambient listening events initiated through supported workflows for situational checks
- History playback and route review to analyze past activity and movement patterns
- Battery and movement related telemetry visible in Plaspy for proactive monitoring and maintenance

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet safety monitoring during outdoor activities with geofence alerts for boundary breaches
- Personal tracking for children or vulnerable individuals during outings
- Lost pet recovery scenarios using SOS alerts and two way voice to confirm status
- Route logging and behavior review to study range and movement over time
- Light portable asset tracking when compact waterproof coverage is required

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets such as two way voice, SOS workflows, and update frequency can depend on device firmware and regional variants
- History retention and available playback length may depend on Plaspy account settings and device reporting behavior
- Certain features may require carrier support or local network availability to function as expected
- Installation and mounting choices can affect GPS reception and overall tracking reliability
- Always consult the device manufacturer for the latest firmware release notes and hardware information
