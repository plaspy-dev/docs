# LK-GPS - LK720 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the LK-GPS LK720 CatM tracker and describes how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for tracking, alerts, and operational monitoring. It focuses on the user-facing functions and practical value of the LK720 when paired with Plaspy rather than detailed configuration steps.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific limits and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The LK720 is a compact CatM GPS tracker built for discreet vehicle and asset monitoring, offering continuous GNSS positioning, resilient cellular connectivity, and an integrated relay for remote cut off. Below are the most relevant practical capabilities to consider when using the LK720 with Plaspy.

- CatM cellular connectivity for reliable data delivery in marginal coverage areas and wide area deployments.
- Continuous GNSS position reporting with typical accuracy in the meter range to support real time location visibility.
- Integrated relay for remote fuel or oil cut off to support immobilization workflows and theft response.
- Shock and vibration alarm plus geofence support to detect impacts, movement, or boundary events and trigger alerts.
- SMS and mobile app configuration for device setup and local control alongside remote management through Plaspy.
- Base station positioning fallback to maintain approximate location continuity when GNSS signals are limited.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK720

- CatM LTE connectivity for low power wide area data reporting and persistent connections
- Built in GNSS receiver with meter class positioning for tracking and historical traces
- Integrated relay output for remote fuel or oil cut off and immobilization actions
- Shock and vibration alarm functionality to detect impacts or tampering
- Geofencing support for boundary monitoring and event generation
- SMS and Android/iOS app configuration options for local device management
- Base station BS positioning fallback to provide alternative location estimates when GNSS is unavailable
- Compact, waterproof design suited to vehicle and motorcycle installations

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position, state, and event information from compatible trackers like the LK720 and presents that information in dashboards, alerts, and reports. When the LK720 reports GNSS coordinates, alarms, or status updates, Plaspy maps, logs, and routes those events into operational workflows for monitoring and response.

- Real time GNSS positions from the LK720 appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and route playback.
- Shock and geofence events are forwarded to Plaspy as alerts to trigger notifications and incident workflows.
- Relay state and remote immobilizer commands can be coordinated from Plaspy to support anti theft interventions where available.
- Device voltage and basic status telemetry are visible in Plaspy to assist with power monitoring and maintenance decisions.
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and ingests LK720 reports when devices are pointed to the Plaspy server endpoint, enabling straightforward integration.
