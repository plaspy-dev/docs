# LK-GPS - LK720

<div className="device-logo">
![LK720](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The LK720 is a compact, high performance CatM GPS tracker built for discreet vehicle and asset monitoring. Designed for cars, motorcycles, rental fleets, and OEM or ODM installations, the LK720 provides continuous GNSS positioning, CatM cellular connectivity, and an integrated relay for remote fuel or oil cut off. Its feature set targets reliable location reporting, practical anti theft response, and everyday fleet oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK720 can deliver the location, status, and event data Plaspy needs to provide real time visibility and alerting. Built for challenging coverage and environmental conditions with optimized power design and waterproof protection, the unit helps ensure Plaspy users receive timely updates for tracking, incident response, and operational monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- CatM cellular connectivity for resilient real time tracking and telemetry in marginal coverage.
- Integrated relay for remote fuel or oil cut off to support immobilization and recovery workflows.
- Continuous GNSS positioning with manufacturer stated typical accuracy around 5 m for dependable location reporting.
- Shock and vibration alarm plus geofencing to trigger immediate alerts on impact or unauthorized movement.
- SMS and Android iOS app configuration for straightforward device control and status checks.
- Compact, lightweight and rugged design with waterproof protection suitable for vehicle and motorcycle deployments.
- OEM and ODM friendly options for branded implementations in commercial rollouts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the LK720 streams GNSS coordinates, status updates, and event triggers to the platform so fleet managers and vehicle owners can monitor assets in real time, receive alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy aggregates those feeds into dashboards, notifications, and reports that support operational oversight and security responses.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy dashboards for continuous visibility of vehicles and assets.
- Shock and vibration alarms, plus geofence events, are forwarded to Plaspy for instant alerting and incident handling.
- Relay state and immobilizer actions can be managed from Plaspy to support remote intervention when needed.
- Device status and input voltage reporting help Plaspy detect power issues and flag devices for maintenance.
- Base station positioning is available as a fallback to maintain location continuity in GNSS limited environments.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium vehicle fleets requiring real time tracking and route oversight.
- Anti theft protection using alarm events and remote relay immobilization to assist recovery.
- Rental vehicle monitoring to enforce boundaries, detect impacts, and reduce losses.
- Motorcycle and personal vehicle tracking where a compact and discreet device is preferred.
- OEM and commercial deployments that need a customizable tracker compatible with Plaspy aggregation.
