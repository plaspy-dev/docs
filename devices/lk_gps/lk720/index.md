# LK-GPS - LK720

![LK720](./tracker.jpg)

The LK720 CatM is a compact, high-performance Cat-M GPS tracker engineered for discreet vehicle and asset monitoring. Designed for cars, motorcycles, rental fleets, and OEM/ODM installations, the LK720 delivers continuous GNSS positioning, Cat-M cellular connectivity, and an integrated relay for remote fuel/oil cut-off—making it a dependable Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking, anti-theft response, and everyday fleet management.

Built for reliability in challenging coverage and environmental conditions, the LK720 combines accurate telemetry with straightforward controls via SMS and mobile apps \(Android and iOS\). Its optimized power design and waterproof protection mean the unit stays online when it matters most—providing Plaspy users with timely location, status, and alert data for fast decisions and improved vehicle security.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with Cat‑M cellular connectivity for reliable real-time tracking and telemetry even in marginal coverage.
- Integrated relay for remote fuel/oil cut-off and immobilization—effective anti‑theft capability for vehicles and rental fleets.
- Accurate GNSS positioning \(≈5 m\) with fast TTFF and built-in GPS/GSM antennas for discreet installation on cars and motorcycles.
- Shock and vibration alarm plus geofencing for immediate alerts on impact, unauthorized movement, or boundary breaches.
- SMS and app configuration with Android/iOS support and base station \(BS\) positioning fallback to maintain continuity when GNSS is limited.
- Durable, compact form with IPX6/IP65-level protection and wide operating temperature range suited to vehicle deployments.
- Lightweight design \(~29.3 g\) and OEM/ODM-friendly options for customized appearance and branding in commercial deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LK720 streams location and status updates over Cat‑M to Plaspy’s platform, enabling real‑time tracking, alerts, and historical reports. Plaspy ingests GNSS coordinates, telemetry \(voltage and device status\), and event triggers \(shock/vibration alarms, geofence entrances/exits\) to provide dashboards and automated notifications for fleet managers and vehicle owners.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered via Cat‑M for continuous monitoring on Plaspy dashboards.
- Shock/vibration alarm and geofence events are forwarded to Plaspy for instant alerting and incident workflows.
- Integrated relay supports remote immobilizer commands \(fuel/oil cut-off\) from Plaspy when immediate intervention is required.
- Device input voltage and basic status data are reported to Plaspy to monitor battery health and detect power tamper or loss.
- Base station \(BS\) positioning acts as a GNSS backup, helping Plaspy maintain location continuity in urban canyons or indoor coverage gaps.
