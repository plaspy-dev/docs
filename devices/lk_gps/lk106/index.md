# LK-GPS - LK106

![LK106](./tracker.jpg)

The LK106 is a compact 4G GPS tracker and Plaspy compatible device built for reliable, real-time tracking in a small, waterproof package. With built-in GPS and GSM antennas, a SIM card slot, and support for both Android/iOS apps and a browser/server \(B/S\) web platform, the LK106 delivers discreet location monitoring and emergency alerting for personal safety and asset protection.

Designed for extended outdoor use and simple integration with Plaspy, the LK106 provides up to 7 days of standby battery life \(actual runtime varies with reporting frequency and network conditions\), an SOS emergency alarm, geofencing and movement alerts, shock wake-up mode, and SMS configuration for environments without data. It is an ideal Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for users who need dependable personal tracking, anti-theft monitoring, and basic telemetry without a bulky device.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real-time tracking and location updates.
- Compact, lightweight, waterproof design suitable for wearables, pockets, bags, and discreet installation.
- Built-in GPS and GSM antennas plus a SIM card slot for live tracking over mobile networks and SMS fallback.
- Emergency SOS button sends immediate alerts with current location to preset contacts or services.
- Long standby battery life — up to 7 days on a single charge depending on settings and network conditions.
- Customizable alerts: geofencing \(enter/leave\), movement detection, low battery warnings, and shock wake-up for motion-triggered reporting.
- Works via Android/iOS mobile apps and a browser/server platform for remote position and battery checks.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LK106 streams location and event data to your Plaspy account so you can view live positions, set geofence rules, and receive alerts across mobile and web interfaces. The device uses mobile data for continuous updates and SMS as a fallback for configuration or alerts in areas with limited data service. Plaspy ingests telemetry from the tracker and presents it in user-friendly dashboards, enabling instant situational awareness and quick response to SOS events.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy dashboards and apps.
- SOS alarm with immediate location push to preset contacts and Plaspy notifications.
- Geofencing enter/leave events and movement/shock wake-up alerts forwarded to Plaspy rules and notifications.
- Battery level monitoring and low-battery alerts accessible in Plaspy for proactive management.
- SMS configuration and status queries supported as a data-backup method when mobile connectivity is limited.
