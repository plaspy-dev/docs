# LK-GPS - LK106

![LK106](./tracker.jpg)

The LK106 is a compact 4G GPS tracker designed for reliable, real time location monitoring in a small waterproof package. It includes built in GPS and GSM antennas, a SIM card slot, an SOS emergency button, and supports both mobile apps and a browser based platform. The device is intended for discreet on person or small asset use where continuous position reporting and emergency alerting are required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK106 integrates with Plaspy to deliver live tracking, event reporting, and alerting through the Plaspy web and mobile interfaces. Its support for data reporting and SMS fallback makes it a practical choice for Plaspy users who need straightforward personal safety monitoring, anti theft protection, and basic remote oversight without a large form factor.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for live position updates and event reporting
- Compact, lightweight, and waterproof design suitable for wearables and discreet placement
- Built in GPS and GSM antennas with a SIM card slot for mobile data and SMS fallback
- SOS emergency button that can trigger immediate location alerts
- Up to 7 days standby battery life depending on reporting frequency and network conditions
- Geofencing, movement alerts, and shock wake up features for situational awareness

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the LK106 sends location and event data to your Plaspy account where positions, alerts, and device status are presented in dashboards and notifications. Plaspy centralizes incoming messages from the tracker so operators can set rules, receive timely alerts, and review location history through both mobile and web interfaces.

- Real time location updates and position history visible in Plaspy dashboards
- SOS alarm notifications routed to Plaspy alerts and assigned contacts
- Geofence enter and leave events forwarded to Plaspy rules for automated notifications
- Movement and shock wake up alerts delivered to Plaspy for rapid awareness
- Battery level reporting and low battery alerts available in Plaspy for proactive device management

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for children, elderly persons, or lone workers with SOS support
- Discreet anti theft monitoring for bags, luggage, and small valuables
- Traveler safety and situational awareness during transit or remote trips
- Small asset monitoring where lightweight waterproof protection is required
- Short term workforce oversight where quick location and alerting are needed
