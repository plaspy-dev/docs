# LK-GPS - LK106 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive feature overview of the LK-GPS LK106 and how its capabilities map to use within Plaspy. It focuses on practical, user-facing functions and the typical operational value the device brings when integrated with the Plaspy platform.

Exact availability and behavior of any feature described here can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, method of installation, regional device variants, and how the manufacturer configures the unit. For device-specific technical details and the latest firmware notes consult the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LK106 is a compact 4G GPS tracker designed for discreet, real-time location monitoring and basic emergency alerting. It combines a waterproof enclosure and integrated antennas with mobile network connectivity and simple management options, delivering dependable position reporting for people and small assets.

- Real-time location reporting over mobile networks for continuous tracking and monitoring.
- SOS emergency alarm that transmits current location and triggers alerts to preset contacts or services.
- Compact, waterproof design intended for wearables, pockets, bags, and discreet placement.
- Built-in GPS and GSM antennas with a SIM card slot and SMS configuration fallback for limited-data environments.
- Geofence and movement alerts plus shock wake-up mode to detect motion and notify monitoring systems.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK106

- 4G mobile connectivity for live position updates and event reporting.
- Built-in GPS positioning for regular location fixes and tracking history.
- SOS emergency button to send immediate alarms with location information.
- Geofencing support for enter and leave alerts configured by user-defined zones.
- Movement detection and shock wake-up mode for motion-triggered reporting.
- SMS configuration and status query support as a fallback when data service is unavailable.
- Compact, waterproof enclosure suitable for on-person use or discreet mounting.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an LK106 is connected to Plaspy, the device reports location and event data to the Plaspy account where it can be monitored, recorded, and used to trigger notifications and rules. Plaspy presents the incoming telemetry in dashboards and notification streams so teams can respond quickly to alarms and operational events.

- Live position and event updates appear in Plaspy maps and recent activity feeds for situational awareness.
- SOS alarms are reflected in Plaspy alerts and can be routed to predefined contacts or notification channels.
- Geofence enter and leave events and movement/shock alerts are surfaced in Plaspy rule engines and notification settings.
- Battery status and low battery warnings reported by the device are available in Plaspy for proactive device management.
- SMS-capable configuration and status queries work as a backup when data coverage is limited and Plaspy is used for centralized monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for children, elderly relatives, or lone workers with SOS alerting.
- Discreet anti-theft monitoring for bags, luggage, and other small valuables.
- Traveler or commuter safety where compact, waterproof tracking and emergency notifications are required.
- Short-term asset monitoring or temporary deployments where low weight and simple setup are priorities.
- Small-asset recovery efforts that rely on timely location updates and motion alerts.
- Remote monitoring scenarios where SMS configuration is beneficial due to intermittent data coverage.
