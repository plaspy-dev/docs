# LK-GPS - LK680 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the LK-GPS LK680 and how its core capabilities map to Plaspy for real time tracking and fleet monitoring. It focuses on the practical, non sensitive aspects of the device that matter to fleet managers, shared micromobility operators, and individual owners using Plaspy as the platform for location visibility and event management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, local cellular configuration, and installation method. Use this page as a functional summary for Plaspy integration and consult the manufacturer documentation for device specific technical details and the latest firmware behavior.

## Feature Overview

The LK680 is a compact, tamper resistant GPS tracker designed for long term monitoring of e bikes, bicycles, motorcycles, and similar light vehicles. It combines GNSS positioning with cellular uplink and configurable reporting to deliver location, movement alerts, and battery status into the Plaspy platform for actionable oversight.

- Real time GNSS location reporting for route visibility and recovery workflows.
- Cellular connectivity for continuous uplink of position, event, and battery telemetry to Plaspy.
- Configurable upload frequency to balance responsiveness and long battery standby life.
- Movement and vibration alerts plus geofence notifications for anti theft monitoring.
- Rugged, waterproof, and discreet form factor for concealed installation on two wheel vehicles.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK680

- Proven GNSS positioning for accurate location and route history.
- 2G and 4G cellular connectivity to send telemetry to cloud platforms.
- Configurable reporting intervals to tune update frequency and power consumption.
- Movement and vibration alerts to indicate suspicious activity or motion.
- Geofence support for enter and exit zone notifications.
- Low battery notifications to help schedule recharge and maintenance.
- Rechargeable internal battery designed for extended standby life under low power reporting profiles.
- Rugged, tamper resistant, waterproof enclosure suited to outdoor use and concealed mounting.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the LK680 telemetry to provide live location, event awareness, and historical reporting through its dashboard and mobile app. Device configuration and event thresholds let you control how often the tracker sends data to Plaspy and which events should trigger alerts.

- Live map location updates and historical tracks shown in Plaspy for situational awareness.
- Movement and vibration events forwarded to Plaspy so operators receive prompt notifications.
- Geofence events recorded and displayed to monitor zone compliance and trigger workflows.
- Low battery alerts surfaced in Plaspy to schedule recharging or maintenance for distributed assets.
- Adjustable reporting intervals from the device side reflected in Plaspy to manage data volume and battery life.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and accepts device reports for seamless onboarding.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft monitoring for privately owned e bikes and motorcycles with instant movement alerts.
- Shared mobility and e bike fleet management with centralized location and battery status monitoring.
- Long term deployments where extended standby battery life reduces maintenance trips.
- Route history and activity reporting for operating analytics and service planning.
- Geofence enforcement for parking zones, pick up areas, or restricted routes.
- Asset recovery workflows using real time tracking and historical position logs.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some capabilities depend on the firmware version installed on the LK680 and may change with updates.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants can affect supported cellular bands and behavior.
- Installation quality and placement influence GPS reception and movement detection performance.
- Geofence behavior and alert timing are influenced by reporting intervals configured on the device.

Using the LK680 with Plaspy gives operators centralized visibility into vehicle location, movement events, and battery status so they can act quickly on theft incidents, schedule maintenance, and analyze fleet performance. The LK680’s configurable reporting and long standby profile make it a practical choice for distributed micromobility fleets and owners who need reliable telemetry without frequent maintenance.
