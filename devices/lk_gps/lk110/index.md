# LK-GPS - LK110

![LK110](./logo..jpg)

The LK110 is a compact 4G e bike and bicycle GPS tracker from LK GPS designed for vehicle security and location monitoring. Built for two wheeled applications, it offers positioning with roughly 10 meter accuracy, a 3000mAh battery providing up to 10 days of standby, and a lightweight 130 gram form factor. The device includes visibility features such as three flashing light modes, multiple alarm types, and configurable upload frequency, and it can be queried from mobile app, PC web page, SMS, or WeChat.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK110 can feed location updates, alarm events, and status information into Plaspy for consolidated monitoring and reporting. Its configurable reporting rate, geofence and vibration alarms, and remote arm and restart capabilities make it well suited to Plaspy workflows for bike security, fleet oversight, and operational visibility without requiring highly specific custom integration details on this page.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for e bikes and bicycles with a lightweight 130 gram design
- 4G global connectivity and GPS positioning with approximately 10 meter accuracy
- Long life 3000mAh battery offering up to 10 days standby in typical conditions
- Multiple alarm features including geofence entry and exit alerts, vibration alarm, low power alarm, and displacement alarm
- Three flashing light modes to improve visibility and deter theft
- Configurable upload frequency and working modes including power saving and real time behavior

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LK110 supplies regular location updates and alarm notifications that Plaspy can present on maps, dashboards, and reports to support fleet and asset management. Plaspy can be used to centralize device visibility, set operational alerts, and review location history for this tracker alongside other devices in your account.

- Real time and historical location tracking displayed within Plaspy maps and timelines
- Alerts and notifications for geofence events, vibration, and low battery routed through Plaspy
- Customizable upload frequency used to balance update granularity and power consumption for Plaspy reporting
- Remote arm and disarm commands and remote restart actions handled through the platform when supported by the device
- Centralized status and battery monitoring for fleet level oversight and operational planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal e bike or bicycle owners seeking theft deterrence and recovery tools
- Bike rental and shared mobility operators managing a dispersed fleet
- Delivery services using bicycles or e bikes that need location visibility and simple alerts
- Maintenance teams tracking asset location and battery status across many vehicles
