# LK-GPS - LK110

![LK110](./logo..jpg)

The LK110 4G Global E-bike/Bicycle GPS Tracker by KJ-GPS is a reliable and versatile tracking device designed specifically for e-bikes and bicycles. With its accurate positioning capabilities, you can easily keep track of your bike's location at all times. Whether you're using the mobile app, PC web page, SMS, or WeChat, you have multiple platforms to query the device's position.

One of the standout features of the LK110 is its three flashing modes of car lights. This not only enhances visibility and safety while riding, but it also serves as a deterrent to potential thieves. Additionally, the device supports a customizable upload frequency, allowing you to choose how often the device sends location updates.

The LK110 offers a range of alarm features to ensure the security of your bike. The platform supports electronic fence entry and exit alarms, vibration alarms, low power alarms, and displacement alarms. You can also remotely arm and disarm the device through the platform.

With multiple working modes, including power-saving mode and real-time mode, the LK110 provides flexibility and efficiency. The device can automatically go to sleep and conserve power when stationary, and it can be woken up by vibration or text messages. It also supports remote restart, making it convenient to manage the device.

In terms of specifications, the LK110 is equipped with a 3000mAh battery that provides up to 10 days of standby time. It weighs only 130g, ensuring it doesn't add unnecessary bulk to your bike. The device supports 4G network connectivity and has GPS accuracy of 10m.

Overall, the LK110 4G Global E-bike/Bicycle GPS Tracker is a reliable and feature-packed device that offers accurate positioning, customizable settings, and a range of alarm features. Whether you're a bike enthusiast or a bike rental business, this tracker is an excellent choice to ensure the security and safety of your bikes.
