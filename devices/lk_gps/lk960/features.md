# LK-GPS - LK960 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the KJ-GPS LK960 and how its reported capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It concentrates on observable functions and operational value you can expect when the LK960 is connected to the Plaspy platform, without attempting to replicate manufacturer configuration or private protocols.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and regional or manufacturer configuration. Use this page to learn how the LK960’s publicly described capabilities map to monitoring and control inside Plaspy, and consult the manufacturer for the most current technical specifics.

## Feature Overview

The LK960 is a compact wired GPS tracker designed for cars and motorcycles that supports both 2G and 4G networks and a wide operating voltage range. It reports location, status indicators, and a range of alarm events to a platform, and it includes remote control options when installed and configured accordingly.

- Real time location reporting via SMS or platform mode for tracking vehicles and motorcycles
- SOS alarm to signal emergency situations for rapid awareness
- Remote fuel and electricity cut off and recovery for added vehicle security and recovery workflows
- Vibration alarm and other tamper related alerts to detect unauthorized movement
- LED indicators for quick visual checks of power, GPS, and GSM connectivity
- Support for standard vehicle voltage ranges to suit a variety of installations

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK960

- Wired installation designed for cars and motorcycles with a compact form factor
- Network support for both 2G and 4G connectivity to maintain wide coverage
- SOS alarm function to send emergency alerts from the device
- Remote cut off and recovery of fuel and electricity as reported by the manufacturer
- Vibration alarm to detect movement or tampering
- Platform and SMS modes for location requests and alarm delivery
- Multiple alarm types supported by the platform mode, including geofence, tamper, displacement, power off, and overspeed reports as described by the manufacturer
- LED indicators that show power, GPS, and GSM status for onsite diagnostics

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and processes location updates and alarm messages from compatible trackers and presents them through its device interface for monitoring and reporting. When the LK960 is connected and correctly configured, Plaspy can surface the device’s reported events and allow platform-driven actions where supported.

- Display live and recent location points reported by the LK960 inside Plaspy maps and device timelines
- Surface alarm events such as SOS, vibration, displacement, power off, and geofence breaches in the device event feed
- Reflect device connectivity and basic status using the tracker’s reported GPS GSM and power indicators
- Allow remote commands from Plaspy to the tracker where the device and installation support remote arming, disarming, and oil cut or recovery
- Store historical events and location records for review and operational reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time tracking and monitoring of personal cars and motorcycles
- Theft detection and response workflows using vibration alerts and SOS signals
- Remote immobilization for recovered vehicles through fuel or electricity cut off
- Fleet oversight where regional cellular coverage supports 2G or 4G connectivity
- Rapid alerting for drivers in emergency situations via the SOS function
- Visual onsite checks aided by device LED status indicators during installation and maintenance

## Feature Availability Notes

- Function set and exact behavior depend on device firmware and manufacturer implementation; different firmware versions may expose different commands or alarms.
- Regional cellular support varies; 2G or 4G availability depends on local carrier networks and the hardware revision of the device.
- Remote cut off and recovery functions require proper wiring, installation, and configuration; availability can vary by vehicle and installer setup.
- Platform features visible in Plaspy are limited to the telemetry and commands the device is configured to send and receive.
- Always confirm regional product variants and firmware change logs with the manufacturer for the latest feature set.
