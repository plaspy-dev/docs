# LK-GPS - LK960

![LK960](./logo..jpg)

The LK960 from LK-GPS is a wired vehicle GPS tracker designed for use in cars and motorcycles. It combines a compact form factor with key security and control features such as an SOS alarm, vibration alarm for tampering detection, remote fuel and electricity cut off, and LED status indicators for power, GPS, and GSM. The device supports both 2G and 4G networks and operates across a wide voltage range, making it suitable for a variety of vehicle types.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK960 can feed its location and alarm signals into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and management. That compatibility makes the LK960 a practical option for operators who need real time visibility, alarm routing, and remote control actions from a single fleet management platform while retaining the device level functionality such as SMS fallback and platform mode tracking.

## Key Highlights

- Wired tracker suitable for cars and motorcycles with compact design
- SOS alarm for emergency notifications
- Remote fuel and electricity cut off for increased vehicle control
- Vibration alarm to detect unauthorized movement or tampering
- Supports 2G and 4G connectivity for broad network coverage
- Wide voltage operation range of 10 100V for compatibility with many vehicle systems
- LED indicators for quick status checks of power GPS and GSM

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LK960 delivers location updates and alarm events into the Plaspy interface so fleet managers and vehicle owners can monitor assets from a single dashboard. Plaspy aggregates those signals with other fleet data to enable operational oversight and actionable alerts.

- Real time location visibility and historical position playback in Plaspy
- Alarm forwarding for SOS vibration power off overspeed and geofence events to notify operators
- Centralized event reporting and logs for compliance and operational review
- Remote control actions such as arming disarming and oil cut or recovery issued through the platform where supported
- SMS mode can act as a fallback for basic tracking or alerts when platform connectivity is not available

## Typical Use Cases

- Company car tracking and centralized fleet oversight
- Motorcycle and scooter monitoring for delivery or service fleets
- Personal vehicle security with SOS and tamper alerts
- Remote immobilization for unauthorized use or recovery workflows
- Vehicles operating across varying voltages and mixed fleet environments
