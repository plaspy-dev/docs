# LK-GPS - LK960

![LK960](./logo..jpg)

The KJ-GPS LK960 is a wired car GPS tracker that offers a range of advanced features to help you keep track of your vehicles. With its compact design and easy installation, this tracker is suitable for use in cars and motorcycles. It supports both 2G and 4G networks, ensuring reliable and fast connectivity wherever you are.

One of the standout features of the LK960 is its SOS alarm function, which allows you to send an emergency alert in case of an accident or other dangerous situations. Additionally, this tracker offers the ability to cut off car fuel and electricity remotely, providing an extra layer of security and control. The vibration alarm feature alerts you if any unauthorized movement or tampering is detected, helping to prevent theft.

With the LK960, you can easily track the location of your vehicle using SMS or the platform mode. The platform supports various alarm functions, including electronic fence access alarm, demolition burglar alarm, vibration alarm, power off alarm, displacement alarm, and overspeed alarm. You can also remotely arm or disarm the tracker, as well as cut off or recover the oil supply of your vehicle. The device supports a wide voltage operation range of 10-100V, making it compatible with a variety of vehicles.

The LK960 also features LED indicators that display the power, GPS, and GSM status, allowing you to quickly check the device's connectivity and functionality. Whether you need to keep track of your personal car or manage a fleet of motorcycles, the KJ-GPS LK960 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that offers peace of mind and enhanced security.

### Key Features:

- SOS alarm for emergency situations
- Remote fuel and electricity cut-off
- Vibration alarm to prevent theft
- Supports 2G/4G networks for reliable connectivity
- Wide voltage operation range of 10-100V
- Supports SMS and platform mode for location tracking
- Platform supports various alarm functions
- Remote arming/disarming and oil cut/recovery
- LED indicators for power, GPS, and GSM status
