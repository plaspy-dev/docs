# LK-GPS - LK110 4G Global E-bike/Bicycle GPS Tracker Features

This page provides a public, feature focused overview of the LK-GPS LK110 4G Global E-bike/Bicycle GPS Tracker and how the device integrates with Plaspy for centralized tracking and monitoring. It is intended to explain the practical capabilities you can expect when using the LK110 with Plaspy, and how those capabilities support everyday tracking, anti theft workflows, and small fleet operations.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a high level guide to capabilities and to understand what can be exposed inside Plaspy. For device specific commands, firmware release notes, or hardware variants consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LK110 is a compact, rugged tracker purpose built for bicycles and e-bikes that is designed to deliver reliable location updates, configurable alerts, and simple remote controls. When used with Plaspy, the device feeds location and event telemetry into a single monitoring platform so owners and administrators can view status and act on alerts quickly.

- Real time location reporting using global 4G connectivity for coverage across regions
- Rugged waterproof and shock resistant enclosure with a fixed bracket for quick installation
- Configurable upload frequency and power saving sleep modes to balance battery life and tracking detail
- Multiple intelligent alarms including geofence entry and exit, vibration, displacement, and low battery alerts
- Remote controls for arming and disarming, restarting the device, and toggling a bicycle taillight for safety

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK110 4G Global E-bike/Bicycle GPS Tracker

- Plaspy compatible for straightforward integration into existing tracking dashboards and workflows
- Global 4G cellular connectivity for timely location updates across supported regions
- Durable, compact housing that is waterproof and shock resistant to suit outdoor bicycle use
- Fixed bracket mounting designed for tool free installation on bicycles and e-bikes
- Configurable upload intervals and sleep mode options to extend operational time between charges
- Alarm types including geofence, vibration, displacement, and low battery notifications
- Remote command support for arming/disarming, remote restart, and taillight on/off control

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the LK110 telemetry and event streams so you can monitor live position, receive alerts, and review history in a single platform. Device modes and remote commands are represented in Plaspy to support operational oversight and timely responses.

- Live map tracking and history playback showing the LK110 reported positions
- Alarm events such as geofence entry or exit, vibration, displacement, and low battery forwarded into Plaspy for notifications and logging
- Remote management actions exposed in Plaspy allowing authorized users to issue arm/disarm and restart commands or toggle supported outputs
- Configurable reporting intervals from the device reflected in Plaspy to balance update frequency and battery life
- Centralized reporting and event histories for audits, maintenance planning, and operational review

## Typical Use Cases

- Individual riders and families monitoring real time location and receiving anti theft alerts
- Small fleet management of shared e bikes with centralized tracking, history, and alerting
- Rental and shared bike services enforcing geofence rules and monitoring device status
- Nighttime safety management by remotely controlling taillight and ensuring rider visibility
- Preventive maintenance triggered by low battery alerts to avoid tracking downtime
- Owners and administrators responding to vibration or displacement alarms to investigate possible theft
