# LK-GPS - LK970A/B/C Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the LK-GPS LK970A/B/C magnetic 4G GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet and asset visibility. It is intended to describe practical, non-sensitive device functions and the ways Plaspy presents tracking, alerts, and history for operators and managers.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation method. Where the LK970A/B/C family offers optional configurations or I/O variants, confirm the specific capabilities for your device with the manufacturer or vendor.

## Feature Overview

The LK970A/B/C series is a covert, magnet-mounted GPS tracker designed for long-endurance deployments and continuous location reporting to Plaspy. Its combination of 4G GPS+GSM connectivity, selectable high-capacity batteries, and an alerting feature set makes it useful for remote assets that need low-maintenance monitoring and recovery workflows.

- 4G GPS and GSM positioning for reliable live location updates and prompt status reporting to a cloud platform.
- High-capacity rechargeable battery options (5000 mAh, 10000 mAh, 20000 mAh) for extended field autonomy.
- Rugged magnet housing for covert attachment to vehicle chassis, containers, and other metal surfaces for quick installation.
- Comprehensive alerting including overspeed, geo-fence entry and exit, movement detection, low battery, shock or shaking, and tamper notifications.
- Blind-area recovery and offline caching to store events and locations while out of coverage and upload them automatically on reconnection.
- Intelligent Sleeping Mode to reduce power consumption during long stationary periods and extend standby life.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK970A/B/C

- Native compatibility with Plaspy for centralized onboarding and unified fleet visibility.
- GPS positioning combined with 4G cellular connectivity for real-time tracking and status updates.
- Multiple battery capacity options to match deployment duration and maintenance cycles.
- Magnet-mounted, rugged enclosure for covert placement and secure attachment to metal surfaces.
- Configurable alert set including overspeed, geo-fence, movement, low battery, shock, shaking, and tamper events.
- Offline caching and blind-area recovery to preserve telemetry during temporary coverage gaps.
- Intelligent power management mode to balance reporting frequency and battery life.
- History playback support for route review and incident analysis once data is uploaded to the platform.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the LK970 streams location and event data into Plaspy, operators gain a consolidated view of device positions, alerts, and historical traces. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and ingests telemetry so managers can act on events and review activity without manual packet handling.

- Live location markers and moving traces displayed on Plaspy maps for real-time situational awareness.
- Alerts such as geo-fence entry or tamper are presented in Plaspy as configurable notifications for dispatch or operations teams.
- Cached locations and events are uploaded after reconnection and appear in Plaspy history for complete trace reconstruction.
- Historical playback and reporting tools in Plaspy allow route review, timeline inspection, and incident analysis.
- When a device variant or installation exposes additional inputs such as ignition or remote sensors, Plaspy can show those inputs alongside location data after confirming availability with the vendor.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and dispatch for vehicles that require covert mounting and long battery life between service intervals.
- Anti-theft monitoring where tamper, movement, shock, and low battery alerts support rapid response.
- Long-term asset tracking for containers, trailers, or equipment stored offsite with intermittent coverage.
- Rental and shared vehicles that benefit from a non-invasive magnet mount and extended autonomy.
- Logistics and delivery verification using history playback and geo-fence event records for audits.
