# LK-GPS - LK209C Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the LK-GPS LK209C and how it can be used with Plaspy for real time tracking, historical playback, and fleet management workflows. It summarizes the practical capabilities that matter to operators deploying the device and explains how those capabilities appear in Plaspy without reproducing manufacturer configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional network support, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, firmware change logs, or the latest technical details refer to the manufacturer documentation linked at the end of this page.

## Feature Overview

The LK209C is a rugged, magnet-mounted tracker built for long standby deployments and discreet installation on vehicles, trailers, containers, and other mobile assets. It pairs a high-capacity battery and GSM connectivity with local blind-area logging and a configurable alarm set to preserve location continuity and provide timely notifications.

- Extended runtime from a high capacity 20000 mAh battery for infrequent servicing and long covert deployments.
- Magnet mounted housing for quick, discreet attachment to vehicles and containers.
- Blind-area data logging that preserves GPS points locally when cellular coverage is lost and uploads them when connectivity resumes.
- Configurable event alerts including movement, shock, overspeed, geo-fence, drop and low battery to support operational monitoring.
- GNSS performance with high sensitivity and typical position accuracy suitable for route verification and recovery workflows.
- Auto-tracking and configurable sleep modes to balance reporting frequency with battery life.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK209C

- High capacity 20000 mAh internal battery designed for long standby and reduced maintenance intervals.
- Rugged magnet mounted form factor for covert installation on vehicles, trailers, and containers.
- GSM 2G and 3G cellular connectivity for wide area telemetry transmission.
- Blind-area logging that stores location fixes locally during signal outage and forwards them when connection returns.
- Auto-tracking modes and configurable sleep patterns to optimize battery consumption based on movement or schedule.
- Configurable alarm suite covering movement, shock/shaking, overspeed, geo-fence breaches, drop events, and low battery.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver with typical position accuracy suitable for operational tracking and historical trace playback.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LK209C's telemetry and event messages are presented in Plaspy's mapping, reporting, and alerting interfaces so teams can monitor assets and investigate incidents from a single platform. Plaspy stores and displays both live fixes and blind-area logs as they arrive to preserve continuity of the location history.

- Real time location updates and position history visible on Plaspy maps and playback timelines for route verification.
- Blind-area logs uploaded by the device are ingested and appended to historical traces inside Plaspy to reduce data gaps.
- Event and alarm routing for movement, shock, overspeed, geo-fence, drop and low battery is handled by Plaspy notification and escalation rules.
- Geo-fence events and historical trace playback available in Plaspy for incident analysis and auditing.
- Battery status and device online/offline visibility shown in Plaspy to support maintenance planning and device health monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with passive long term tracking for route verification, compliance, and historical playback.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using covert magnet mounting plus movement and shock alerts to detect unauthorized moves.
- Rental vehicle and equipment monitoring where long standby reduces maintenance visits and blind-area logging preserves traces.
- Cargo and container location tracking during transit where discreet installation and durable housing are important.
- Monitoring off grid or intermittently connected assets where blind-area logging and automatic uploads maintain continuity.
