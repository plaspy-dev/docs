# LK-GPS - LK300

<div className="device-logo">
![LK300](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The LK300, often referenced as LK300-2G in manufacturer materials, is a compact GPS tracker designed for reliable real time tracking and anti theft protection across cars, motorcycles, trucks, and outdoor equipment. It combines an integrated GPS antenna and GSM connectivity with a small form factor intended for discreet mounting, vibration based wake up for movement detection, and multi platform access for monitoring device status and location.

This model is compatible with Plaspy and can be paired to bring live location, basic telemetry, and tamper alerts into a centralized Plaspy workflow. That compatibility makes the LK300 a practical choice for operators who need straightforward vehicle and asset visibility on Plaspy for single units or mixed fleet deployments without adding unnecessary complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible integration for centralized real time tracking and reporting
- Compact and discreet design with built in GPS and GSM antennas
- Vibration shock wake up to detect movement and support anti theft alerts
- Multi platform access for viewing location and device status from web and mobile
- SMS parameter setting and query for quick remote adjustments and checks
- Rugged, waterproof construction suitable for outdoor equipment and heavy use
- Cost effective option for fleet monitoring and operational oversight

## How It Works with Plaspy

When installed and configured, the LK300 transmits location and device status to back end services that Plaspy ingests and displays within the user account. Plaspy consolidates incoming positions, tamper events, and basic device status so operators can monitor assets, respond to alerts, and review historical movement from a single dashboard or supported mobile apps.

- Real time location updates visible in Plaspy for immediate situational awareness
- Vibration and tamper alerts routed to Plaspy to trigger notifications and response
- Device status such as remaining battery capacity reported into Plaspy for maintenance planning
- SMS based parameter setting and query available for remote configuration when needed
- Integration supports fleet workflows on Plaspy including routing visibility, geofence alerts, and incident review

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle and scooter anti theft monitoring with discreet mounting and tamper alerts
- Fleet management for logistics and delivery to improve route visibility and dispatch
- Construction and heavy machinery tracking in remote or rugged environments
- Long distance transport and trucking for consistent location updates and security
- Car rental and sharing services for geofence enforcement and unauthorized movement notifications

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LK300 offers a focused set of features that align with common fleet and asset tracking requirements. Its small footprint, integrated antennas, and vibration based detection make it well suited for discreet installations on vehicles and equipment where ongoing visibility and anti theft alerts are priorities. By feeding location, tamper events, and device status into Plaspy, the tracker supports timely operational decisions and consolidated reporting without excessive configuration overhead.
