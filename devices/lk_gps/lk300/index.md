# LK-GPS - LK300

![LK300](./tracker.jpg)

The LK300-2G is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for reliable real-time tracking and anti-theft protection across cars, motorcycles, trucks and outdoor equipment. With integrated GPS and GSM antennas, vibration shock wake-up and multi-platform access, the LK300-2G delivers discreet installation and consistent location updates for owners and fleet operators who rely on Plaspy for centralized monitoring.

The LK300-2G pairs easily with Plaspy to bring live location, basic telemetry and tamper alerts into your Plaspy dashboard and mobile workflow. Whether you manage a single vehicle, a mixed fleet, or remote equipment, this small wired tracker provides cost-effective visibility and anti-theft functionality that integrates into broader fleet management processes.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible — integrates location and status data into Plaspy for centralized, real-time tracking and reporting.
- Compact, discreet form factor with built-in GPS and GSM antennas for easy installation in cars, motorcycles and equipment.
- Vibration shock wake-up conserves power while detecting tampering or unauthorized movement for effective anti-theft alerts.
- Multi-platform access — view location and device status from Plaspy via web B/S platform and Android/iOS mobile apps.
- SMS parameter setting and query for quick configuration and remote status checks when needed.
- Rugged, waterproof construction suitable for heavy machinery, construction sites and outdoor equipment tracking.
- Cost-effective vehicle GPS solution that supports fleet management workflows and improves operational oversight.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LK300-2G transmits GPS location and device status over GSM to back-end servers, where Plaspy ingests the data and displays it within your Plaspy account. Integration enables operators to view live positions, review recent tracks, receive tamper alerts and examine battery status directly from Plaspy’s dashboard or mobile app. Configuration can be performed via SMS for quick adjustments in the field, while Plaspy provides consolidated alerts and historical reporting for fleet management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates visible in Plaspy for immediate situational awareness.
- Vibration/tamper alerts — movement detection triggers notifications through Plaspy for anti-theft response.
- Device battery status — remaining battery capacity reported to Plaspy for proactive maintenance.
- SMS-based parameter setting and query for remote configuration when network access is limited.
- Integrates into fleet management workflows on Plaspy to support routing, geofence enforcement and incident response.
