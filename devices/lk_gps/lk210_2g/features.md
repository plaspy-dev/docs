# LK-GPS - LK210-2G Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the LK-GPS LK210-2G and explains how its capabilities are presented when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive details about the tracker functions that Plaspy can ingest for monitoring, alerts, and historical playback.

Exact feature availability for a specific unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where possible the content below describes confirmed functions for the LK210-2G such as GPS location reporting, vibration detection, geofence events, and battery status reporting.

## Feature Overview

The LK210-2G is a compact vehicle tracker designed for straightforward real time tracking and basic anti-theft alerts. It combines position reporting with event notifications and simple remote configuration options so owners and fleet teams can track assets and respond to incidents via Plaspy or standard SMS and mobile/web tools.

- Continuous GPS position updates for live location visibility and historical route playback.
- Vibration shock detection to trigger anti-theft or tamper alerts when movement or impact is detected.
- Geofence notifications to report entry and exit events for defined areas.
- Low battery status reporting and power optimization modes to extend operating intervals between charges or power events.
- Remote configuration and basic control via Android and iOS apps, a web B S platform, or SMS commands for on the field adjustments.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK210-2G

- Integrated GPS for reliable position and route data suitable for tracking and playback.
- 2G GSM connectivity for reporting where 2G networks are available.
- Vibration shock sensor with configurable shock wake behavior for theft and tamper detection.
- Geofence support to notify operators of area breaches or arrivals.
- Battery status reporting and low battery alerts to help with maintenance planning.
- Compact waterproof housing for discreet mounting and protection in outdoor vehicle environments.
- Remote management via mobile apps web B S platform and SMS for quick configuration.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an LK210-2G is connected to Plaspy, the device streams its confirmed telemetry into a central dashboard so teams can monitor live position and receive event alerts. Plaspy normalizes incoming messages from supported devices and presents location, event, and status information alongside historical playback and reporting tools.

- Live location markers and recent route playback visible in Plaspy maps and history views.
- Vibration or shock events appear as immediate alerts that can be routed to operators or logged for investigation.
- Geofence events are translated into entry and exit notifications for dispatch and audit trails.
- Battery level and low battery alerts are surfaced for proactive device maintenance and replacements.
- Remote parameter changes made via SMS or the device apps can be reflected in Plaspy once the tracker reports updated settings.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft monitoring using vibration alerts combined with live GPS tracking for rapid response.
- Route playback and simple performance reviews for small commercial fleets.
- Personal vehicle security for motorcycles and cars with discreet waterproof mounting.
- Remote asset tracking in outdoor or rugged environments where a compact, sealed device is required.
- Low complexity telematics deployments where SMS and app based configuration are preferred.
