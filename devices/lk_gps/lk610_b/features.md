# LK-GPS - LK610-B Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the LK-GPS LK610-B features as they relate to using the device with Plaspy. It focuses on functional capabilities that matter for deployment, monitoring, and operational use on the Plaspy platform while avoiding implementation-level configuration guidance.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and the specific installation. Always consult the device manufacturer for the most current technical details and review any device-specific documentation before commissioning a tracker in production.

## Feature Overview

The LK610-B is a compact wearable GPS tracker designed for personal safety and continuous location awareness. It combines GPS and LBS positioning with AGPS assistance, SIM based cellular connectivity, an SOS emergency button, two-way voice calling, and configurable upload modes to balance update frequency and power use.

- Real-time and timed location reporting to support continuous visibility or periodic updates.
- SOS emergency alerts that notify preset contacts with location information for rapid response.
- Two-way calling so caregivers can reach the wearer and confirm status when needed.
- Multiple locating upload modes including timing, real-time, and fixed-point to manage reporting behavior.
- Wearable, waterproof design suited to everyday use in schools, field operations, and travel.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK610-B

- GPS and LBS positioning with AGPS assistance for improved fix times and coverage.
- SOS emergency button that triggers immediate alarms and location reporting.
- Two-way voice calling for direct communication between caregiver and wearer.
- Locating upload modes: timing, real-time, and fixed-point options for flexible reporting cadence.
- G-sensor based motion and displacement detection for movement alerts.
- SIM based cellular connectivity with model variants that may include 2G or NB related support.
- Compact, waterproof wearable form factor designed for personal safety use.
- SMS based configuration options for admin number, APN, and GPRS activation where supported.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the LK610-B’s location fixes, alarms, and device events are presented through a unified monitoring interface so teams can act on safety incidents and maintain records for review.

- Live map view and history playback show reported GPS and LBS positions captured by the device.
- SOS alerts and safety notifications (geo-fence enter/exit, low battery, displacement) appear in Plaspy for routing to preconfigured contacts.
- Timed and real-time upload modes translate into different update frequencies visible in Plaspy for operational monitoring.
- Two-way call events and SOS activations can be correlated with location and timestamps in Plaspy for incident context.
- Device status and some settings can be managed by SMS commands or via the Plaspy platform where device support is available.

## Typical Use Cases

- School safety monitoring for students commuting to and from campuses with SOS and geo-fence alerts.
- Child and elderly care where two-way calling and displacement alarms provide caregiver reassurance.
- Lone worker or staff safety monitoring for field teams requiring discreet wearable tracking.
- Outdoor activities and short trips where a waterproof wearable provides continuous location awareness.
- Temporary deployments and short-term asset monitoring needing compact SIM based trackers.
