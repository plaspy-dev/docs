# LK-GPS - LK610-B

![LK610-B](./tracker.jpg)

The LK610-B \(marketed as LK610-NB in some variants\) is a compact wearable GPS tracker designed for reliable personal safety and Plaspy compatible real-time tracking. Optimized for children, students and staff, this SIM Card GPS Positioning Terminal delivers continuous location updates via GPS and LBS with AGPS support, SOS emergency alerts, two-way calling and configurable locating upload modes — all integrated into Plaspy's monitoring platform for easy deployment and oversight.

The lightweight, waterproof design and simple SIM-based connectivity make the LK610-B ideal for everyday use in schools, field operations and travel. Caregivers and administrators can use Plaspy’s mobile app or PC web platform to view live location, receive safety notifications \(geo-fence enter/exit, low battery, displacement\) and review history playback — combining the convenience of a personal GPS tracker with enterprise-grade monitoring on Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible wearable GPS tracker for real-time tracking and location monitoring of children, elderly and staff.
- SOS emergency button sends immediate alerts with current location to preset contacts for rapid response.
- Two-way calling lets caregivers communicate directly with the wearer for situational verification and reassurance.
- Multiple locating upload modes — timing, real-time and fixed-point — to balance update frequency and battery life.
- Waterproof, compact wearable form factor suitable for outdoor activities, school use and travel.
- Geo-fencing and safety notifications \(enter/exit alerts, low battery, displacement\) supported through Plaspy.
- Simple SMS configuration and platform setup: admin number, APN and GPRS activation are supported for quick commissioning.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LK610-B integrates with Plaspy to provide live location, alarms, and history through a unified interface. Location fixes obtained by GPS and LBS \(with AGPS assistance\) are uploaded to Plaspy according to the tracker’s configured upload mode. Plaspy normalizes that data and offers map views, playback, alarm routing and contact notifications so organizations can act quickly on safety events and maintain audit trails for compliance.

- Real-time tracking and timed location uploads for continuous visibility and historical playback.
- SOS emergency alerts: immediate push/SMS/email notifications to pre-set contacts with coordinates and timestamp.
- Alarm management: geo-fence enter/exit events, low battery and displacement alerts appear in Plaspy for centralized handling.
- Two-way voice capability: caregivers can call the device directly; Plaspy records status and location around call events.
- Device configuration and status via SMS commands or the Plaspy platform: set admin number, APN and GPRS to enable data reporting.
