# LK-GPS - LK610-B

![LK610-B](./tracker.jpg)

The LK610-B is a compact wearable GPS tracker marketed in some variants as the LK610-NB. Designed for personal safety and everyday wear, this SIM based positioning terminal delivers continuous location updates using GPS and LBS with AGPS assistance. The device includes an SOS emergency button, two way calling and configurable locating upload modes to support reliable monitoring of children, students and mobile staff.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK610-B integrates its location feeds and safety alerts into Plaspy’s monitoring platform for straightforward deployment and oversight. Caregivers and administrators can view live location, receive geo fence and safety notifications, and review history playback through Plaspy’s web and mobile interfaces, bringing wearable device convenience into an enterprise ready monitoring workflow.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible wearable GPS tracker for real time tracking and location monitoring of children students and staff
- SOS emergency button that sends immediate alerts with current location to preset contacts
- Two way calling for direct voice contact between caregivers and the wearer
- Multiple locating upload modes including timing real time and fixed point to balance visibility and reporting frequency
- Waterproof compact form factor suitable for school use outdoor activities and travel
- Geo fencing and safety notifications such as enter exit low battery and displacement available through Plaspy
- Simple SIM based setup with SMS configuration options for quick field commissioning

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LK610-B uploads location fixes obtained from GPS and LBS (with AGPS support) according to the tracker’s configured upload mode, and Plaspy ingests and normalizes that data for mapping reporting and alert routing. Plaspy presents live maps history playback and centralized alarm handling so organizations and caregivers can respond to incidents and maintain operational oversight.

- Real time tracking and scheduled location uploads for continuous visibility and historical playback
- SOS emergency alerts trigger immediate notifications to preset contacts with coordinates and timestamps
- Centralized alarm management for geo fence enter and exit events low battery and displacement alerts
- Two way voice capability enables caregivers to verify situations directly with the wearer while location context is available in Plaspy
- Device configuration and status monitoring via SMS commands or platform driven settings to enable data reporting and administrative control
