# LK-GPS - LK209

![LK209](./logo..jpg)

The KJ-GPS LK209 is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker designed for various applications. Whether you need to track private cars, vehicle rentals, or equipment outside, this GPS locator has got you covered. It is also suitable for tracking containers and cargo, making it an ideal choice for logistics and transportation companies.

One of the standout features of the LK209 is its real-time tracking capability. You can monitor the location of your vehicle or asset in real-time, ensuring that you always have up-to-date information. The device also offers auto-tracking, allowing you to set intervals for automatic location updates.

With the LK209, you can create geo-fences to define specific areas. If the device enters or exits these predefined zones, you will receive an alert, providing you with added security and peace of mind. Additionally, the LK209 offers a range of alerts, including overspeed, low battery, shaking sensor, and movement alerts, ensuring that you are promptly notified of any unusual activity.

Installation of the LK209 is simple and user-friendly, and the device provides accurate positioning data. It also has a high battery capacity, ensuring that it can operate for extended periods without needing frequent recharging. Furthermore, the LK209 includes features to prevent detection, making it an excellent choice for covert tracking operations.

#### Key Features:

- Real-time tracking
- Auto-tracking GPS Vehicle Tracking
- Blind area tracking GSM Tracker
- GPS tracking, GSM alert System
- History-trace checking GPS tracker
- Geo-fence
- Dropped alert
- Overspeed alert
- Low battery alert
- Shaking sensor alert
- Sleeping mode
- Shock alert
- Movement alert
