# LK-GPS - LK100 Features

This page provides a public overview of the LK-GPS LK100 Mini Pet GPS Tracker and the features you can expect to use when pairing the device with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing capabilities such as real time location, history playback, alerts, and voice monitoring and explains how those capabilities map to operational use inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information below is grounded in the LK100 product description and intended to help you understand practical uses with Plaspy; for the most current device details consult manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LK100 is a compact pet tracker designed to be attached to a collar or harness and to provide continuous location awareness and safety features for pets. It combines GPS positioning with location based service support to improve coverage in challenging environments and offers a set of user-visible functions valuable for pet monitoring and recovery.

- Real time tracking with frequent location updates for near live monitoring
- GPS plus LBS and A GPS support to improve location coverage in marginal reception areas
- History route playback so you can review past movements on the platform
- SOS button and alerting to notify authorized contacts and the platform in emergencies
- Two way voice monitoring so you can hear surroundings and communicate with your pet
- Geo fence and movement alerts for defined safe areas and breach notifications

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK100

- GPS positioning with assisted location service support for improved coverage
- Real time location updates with short reporting intervals for timely tracking
- Location accuracy reported in consumer terms for practical recovery use
- Route history playback available on the platform for retrospective review
- Two way voice capability allowing remote listening and voice communication
- SOS emergency button that notifies authorized contacts and the platform
- Geo-fence setup with breach alerts to authorized numbers and the platform
- Power saving behavior that reduces activity when no movement is detected and resumes when motion occurs

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and presents the LK100 device data so fleet or pet managers can monitor location, events, and alerts from a single platform. Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols automatically and displays device-sent events and location updates in the interface.

- Real time position and movement appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring and recovery
- Route history transmitted by the tracker can be viewed and replayed in Plaspy to analyze past movements
- SOS presses and geo-fence breaches are recorded as events and surfaced in the Plaspy event feed and notifications
- Device status such as sleep or wake behavior is visible as activity or last seen timestamps
- Two way communication features from the device are represented as device events or status; consult device documentation and Plaspy settings for details on audio handling and permissions

## Typical Use Cases

- Locating a lost or escaped pet using near real time position updates
- Defining safe zones and receiving alerts when a pet leaves a designated area
- Reviewing a pet's route history to understand roaming patterns over time
- Rapid response to SOS events triggered by the device
- Remote listening for a pet's surroundings and sending short voice messages when supported
- Extended monitoring where power saving behavior helps preserve device uptime between movements

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features depend on the tracker firmware version and may change with manufacturer updates
- Hardware revisions or regional variants of the LK100 can affect available functions and integrations
- Installation and how the tracker is mounted can influence motion detection and power saving behavior
- Integration details such as how voice or audio is exposed in Plaspy depend on both device implementation and platform configuration
- Always verify supported features against the official LK GPS documentation and release notes for your specific device unit

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the LK100 with Plaspy gives you a single view of location, history, and event alerts so that pet owners and managers can respond quickly to escapes, breaches, or emergencies. Plaspy's event feed, mapping, and history playback make it practical to combine live monitoring with retrospective review for better situational awareness and operational oversight.
