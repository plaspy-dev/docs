# LK-GPS - LK100

![LK100](./logo..jpg)

The LK100 Mini Pet GPS Tracker is a compact, lightweight device designed to help owners keep pets safe and located. Intended to attach easily to a collar or harness, the LK100 provides continuous location information using GPS and assisted location services. The tracker supports real time updates and history playback, plus practical features such as an SOS button, two-way voice monitoring, and geo fencing to help manage pet movements.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LK100 can be monitored through Plaspy for consolidated visibility alongside other assets and trackers. Plaspy can receive the LK100 location updates, alerts, and route history so owners and operators can use familiar maps, notifications, and reporting tools to monitor pets and act quickly when an alert occurs.

## Key Highlights

- Compact, lightweight design suitable for dogs and cats for collar mounting
- GPS and assisted location service support for reliable positioning
- Real time tracking with frequent updates and reported location accuracy
- One year route history playback available on the platform for movement review
- Built in SOS button to notify authorized contacts and the web platform in emergencies
- Geo fence and movement alerts to notify when a pet leaves a designated area
- Two way voice capability for remote monitoring and simple communication

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LK100 becomes part of a unified tracking view that consolidates live location, alerts, and history on Plaspy maps and dashboards. Plaspy displays incoming updates and lets users manage notifications and review past routes, making it easier to respond to incidents and maintain oversight of tracked pets.

- View live location of the LK100 on Plaspy maps alongside other tracked devices
- Receive geo fence and movement alerts within Plaspy to act on boundary breaches
- Access route history and playback on Plaspy for review of past movements
- Get notified of SOS events and see the last known location in the Plaspy interface
- Monitor device status such as active tracking or standby periods to assess activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Locating a lost or escaped pet quickly using real time tracking
- Monitoring pets in boarding, daycare, or multi pet facilities
- Setting safe boundaries for pets with geo fence alerts for home or property
- Reviewing movement history to evaluate exercise patterns or roaming behavior
- Remote monitoring and simple communication with pets via two way voice

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The LK100 pairs practical pet focused features with Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting capabilities to deliver a straightforward solution for pet owners and small operations. Its compact form factor and feature set—real time updates, geo fencing, SOS alerts, history playback, and voice monitoring—make it well suited to everyday pet safety and recovery scenarios where timely location information matters.

Plaspy adds operational value by consolidating LK100 data into a single platform for alerts, mapping, and history review.
