# LK-GPS - LK710

![LK710](./tracker.png)

The LK710 is a compact, waterproof GPS tracker engineered for reliable, discreet real-time tracking and remote vehicle control. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the LK710 combines GPS positioning \(MTK2503\) and quad-band GSM/GPRS communication into a small housing \(50 × 45 × 15 mm\), delivering accurate telemetry, battery reporting and remote immobilizer control for anti-theft and fleet management applications.

Designed for easy integration with Plaspy, the LK710 brings vehicle-level features—remote fuel cut/resume \(immobilizer\), low-power sleep mode, and SMS/config support—into a complete tracking solution. Its compact waterproof design makes it suitable for cars, rental fleets, portable assets, bikes or personal protection cases where discreet, dependable tracking and real-time alerts are essential.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management visibility.
- Compact, waterproof form factor \(50 × 45 × 15 mm\) for discreet installation on vehicles, assets or personal items.
- Quad-band GSM/GPRS \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) ensures wide cellular coverage for telemetry and location updates.
- Accurate GPS positioning with MTK2503 chipset: typical accuracy ~5 meters and GPS sensitivity to -159 dBm.
- Remote immobilizer \(fuel cut/resume\) capability for anti-theft response and remote vehicle control.
- Built-in rechargeable Li-ion battery \(3.7 V, 100 mAh\) plus wide vehicle power input \(9–120 V\) for flexible power options.
- Mobile apps \(Android/iOS\), browser access and SMS command support for straightforward configuration and monitoring.
- OEM/ODM customization and 24-hour support from the manufacturer for tailored deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LK710 delivers the GPS positions and device telemetry Plaspy needs to provide live maps, alerts and reports. When configured to report to a cloud platform like Plaspy, the unit forwards location updates and status information over the GSM/GPRS link; when cellular coverage is limited, SMS-based queries and commands provide an alternative channel for critical status checks and remote control.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GPS coordinates, signal and battery level\) for map-based tracking and history.
- Remote immobilizer \(fuel cut/resume\) control tied to Plaspy alert workflows for anti-theft and fleet security actions.
- Battery level and power status reporting so Plaspy can trigger low-battery alerts and maintenance reminders.
- SMS command support allows Plaspy or operators to query or configure the device where GPRS is unavailable.
- Note: LK710 does not include Bluetooth sensors; Plaspy can still combine LK710 data with Bluetooth sensor feeds from other compatible devices on the platform.

- Fleet management: real-time GPS tracker for route monitoring, driver oversight and position history in Plaspy dashboards.
- Anti-theft and immobilization: remote fuel cut/resume to secure stolen or unauthorized vehicles via Plaspy alerts.
- Rental vehicle and car-sharing programs: discreet installation with telemetry and battery reporting to manage assets and usage.
- Asset, equipment and personal protection: portable mounting for bikes, bags, pets or vulnerable people requiring discreet tracking.
- Field staff management: location visibility and quick check-ins for teams working across distributed sites.
