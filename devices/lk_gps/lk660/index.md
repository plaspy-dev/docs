# LK-GPS - LK660

![LK660](./tracker.jpg)

The LK660 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for reliable personal safety and simple deployment. Designed for eldercare, child safety, outdoor activities, and institutional monitoring, the LK660 combines GPS and LBS \(A‑GPS\) positioning with 2G cellular connectivity to deliver accurate real‑time tracking and easy integration into the Plaspy platform.

The LK660 is optimized for everyday use: a small, lightweight, waterproof housing, integrated antennas, long battery life through low‑power and sleep modes, and straightforward controls such as one‑touch SOS calling and two‑way voice. When paired with Plaspy you get continuous location updates, zone alerts, fall detection alarms, and up to one year of route history for playback and review — all accessible via Android/iOS apps or a browser‑based B/S platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering reliable real‑time tracking and route playback.
- Combined GPS + LBS \(A‑GPS\) positioning with typical GPS accuracy around 5 meters.
- Supports location updates as frequently as every 5 seconds for timely telemetry.
- Built‑in fall detection, geo‑fence/zone alerts, and one‑touch SOS for rapid response.
- Two‑way voice and ambient listening for situational awareness and remote check‑ins.
- Waterproof, compact form factor with integrated antenna—suitable for daily wear or carry.
- Long battery life with low‑power and sleep modes to maximize time between charges.
- Platform features include Android/iOS apps, browser access, SMS configuration, and OEM customization.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the LK660 forwards location and event data to the platform so you can monitor devices in real time, configure alerts, and review historical routes. Plaspy ingests the LK660’s telemetry to present live maps, playback, alerting and reporting alongside other compatible devices in your account.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates \(GPS + LBS\) for live monitoring and map display.
- Fall detection alarm and geo‑fence/zone alerts delivered to Plaspy for immediate notification.
- Route history retention on the web platform for up to one year, available for playback and analysis.
- One‑touch SOS and event notifications routed through Plaspy or device apps to designated contacts.
- Two‑way voice and ambient listening available on the device and companion apps to support remote checks and verification.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the LK660 with Plaspy delivers a practical, user‑friendly solution for personal safety and small‑scale telemetry needs. The combination of accurate GPS + LBS positioning, rapid update intervals \(down to 5 seconds\), and platform features like one‑year route history and geo‑fence alerts gives caregivers and administrators the visibility they need without complex installation. LK660’s long battery life, waterproof housing, and simple controls make it especially suitable for eldercare, child monitoring, and outdoor use.

While the LK660 is focused on personal tracking rather than advanced vehicle telemetry, its Plaspy compatibility makes it easy to consolidate telemetry from mixed device fleets — from wearable personal trackers to vehicle trackers and sensor nodes. If your deployment requires integration of multiple device types \(including BLE sensor‑equipped devices or vehicle trackers that support fuel monitoring, ignition status, or immobilizer control\), Plaspy can centralize those different data streams alongside LK660 endpoints for unified reporting and alerts.
