# LK-GPS - LK208

![LK208](./tracker.jpg)

**LK208** is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for fast deployment on vehicles and metal assets. With a built-in magnetic mounting system and reliable quad‑band GSM communication, the LK208 delivers real-time tracking and telemetry over the cellular network so fleet managers and security teams can monitor routes, speed and device status without permanent wiring. Its weather‑resistant enclosure and powerful internal magnet make the unit ideal for covert and temporary installations on cars, motorcycles, trucks and equipment.

Designed for straightforward cloud integration, the LK208 transmits continuous location data to web and mobile platforms such as Plaspy for immediate visibility and historical playback. The unit’s long life 3.7V 5000mAh rechargeable battery, energy‑efficient reporting modes and rugged construction combine to provide dependable short‑term and long‑term asset tracking for fleet management, anti‑theft recovery and operational telemetry.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker: seamless transmission of real-time tracking data to cloud dashboards and mobile apps.
- Magnetic mounting system for tool‑free, tamper‑resistant installation on any ferrous surface.
- Quad‑band GSM connectivity \(850/900/1800/1900 MHz\) for wide regional coverage and stable data links.
- Long‑life 3.7V 5000mAh rechargeable battery enabling days or weeks of standby depending on reporting intervals.
- Reliable GPS positioning with ~5 m accuracy and fast hot starts for responsive location updates.
- Operational alerts including geo‑fencing, movement and tamper notifications plus historical route playback.
- Rugged, water‑resistant enclosure built to withstand vibration, shocks and outdoor conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LK208 transmits GPS location and status over the GSM network to Plaspy, enabling real‑time tracking, event alerts and route history inside the Plaspy platform. Integration is focused on delivering actionable telemetry that fleet operators can use for monitoring and incident response. Plaspy receives position fixes, speed and device state so you can build geofences, trigger alerts, view historical playback and send management commands where supported.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(position, speed and device status\) delivered to Plaspy dashboards.
- Geo‑fencing and movement/tamper alerts for immediate notification of unusual activity.
- Historical route playback for post‑trip analysis and compliance reporting.
- Remote control commands \(lock/unlock or custom platform commands\) — configurable from Plaspy where the platform supports command delivery.
- Battery‑powered operation for off‑grid tracking; reporting intervals can be adjusted in Plaspy to balance reporting frequency and battery life.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management — temporary or supplemental tracking for cars, vans and light trucks without hardwiring.
- Anti‑theft recovery — covert magnetic placement on vehicle chassis, trailers or equipment for quick recovery and tracking.
- Cargo and equipment monitoring — track construction machinery, rental equipment and on‑site assets across job sites.
- Short‑term rentals and logistics — deployable asset locator for rental fleets, trailers and motorcycles that need periodic tracking.
- OEM and integration projects — battery‑powered vehicle locator option for partners requiring simple installation and Plaspy integration.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the LK208 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker provides a practical balance of portability, battery life and dependable GSM/GPS performance. Its magnetized, tool‑free installation makes it exceptionally easy to deploy across a variety of vehicles and metal assets without modification, reducing installation time and cost. When paired with Plaspy, the LK208 delivers real‑time tracking, telemetry and alerting that support effective fleet management and anti‑theft workflows. For organizations that require additional telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer control, or Bluetooth sensors, confirm platform and accessory support within Plaspy — the LK208 focuses on robust location reporting, movement/tamper alerts and remote command capability as provided by the hosting platform.
