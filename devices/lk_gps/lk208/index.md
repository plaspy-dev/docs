# LK-GPS - LK208

![LK208](./tracker.jpg)

The LK208 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for fast deployment on vehicles and metal assets. It combines a strong internal magnet and a weather resistant enclosure for tool free, temporary or covert mounting on cars, motorcycles, trucks and equipment. The unit is intended to provide continuous location reporting over the cellular network so teams can monitor routes, movement and device status without permanent wiring.

As a device that transmits location and status to cloud platforms, the LK208 works naturally with Plaspy for fleet visibility and historical playback. Its rechargeable battery and energy efficient reporting modes make it suitable for short term and longer term asset tracking scenarios where quick installation and reliable position updates are priorities.

## Key Highlights

- Compact, magnet mounted GPS tracker suitable for tool free placement on ferrous surfaces
- Plaspy compatible for cloud based visibility, alerts and historical route playback
- Quad band GSM connectivity for broad regional cellular coverage
- Long life rechargeable battery designed for extended standby and configurable reporting intervals
- Weather resistant, rugged enclosure built to withstand outdoor conditions and vibration
- Designed for rapid deployment across vehicles and mobile equipment without permanent installation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to report to Plaspy, the LK208 streams position fixes and device state to the Plaspy platform so operators gain immediate and ongoing visibility. Integration focuses on core fleet and asset tracking workflows that help monitoring teams respond to events and analyze routes over time.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight
- Geo fencing and movement or tamper alerts that trigger notifications inside Plaspy
- Historical route playback for trip review, incident investigation and reporting
- Adjustable reporting intervals to balance update frequency with battery life from the Plaspy interface
- Support for remote platform commands where Plaspy provides command delivery to the device

## Typical Use Cases

- Temporary or supplemental fleet tracking for cars, vans and light trucks without hardwiring
- Anti theft recovery using covert magnetic placement on vehicle chassis, trailers or equipment
- Cargo and equipment monitoring across job sites for rental and construction fleets
- Short term rentals and logistics for motorcycles, trailers and portable assets
- OEM integrations or partner deployments that need a battery powered, easily mounted locator

Its magnetic mounting, rugged enclosure and configurable reporting behavior make it a flexible choice for fleet managers and security teams who require rapid deployment and ongoing position visibility without modifying vehicles.
