# LK-GPS - LK208 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the LK-GPS LK208 features and how the device works with Plaspy for visibility and fleet monitoring. It is written for operators, integrators, and fleet teams who want to understand the practical capabilities of the LK208 when used with the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary with device firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page for general guidance and consult the LK-GPS manufacturer documentation and Plaspy configuration resources for device-specific setup and the latest technical details.

## Feature Overview

The LK208 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker designed for fast deployment on vehicles and metal assets. It combines a strong internal magnet for tool free mounting with quad band cellular connectivity and a large rechargeable battery to deliver real time location reporting, event alerts, and route history to cloud platforms such as Plaspy.

- Fast magnetic mounting for temporary or covert placement on cars, motorcycles, trailers and equipment.
- Continuous GPS position reporting and speed data for real time tracking and historical playback.
- Quad band GSM connectivity to transmit location and status over the cellular network.
- Long life 3.7V 5000mAh rechargeable battery with energy efficient reporting modes for extended deployments.
- Movement, tamper and geo fence alerts to notify operators of unexpected activity.
- Rugged, weather resistant enclosure suitable for outdoor and vehicle use.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK208

- Compact form factor designed for quick, tool free deployment with a powerful built in magnet.
- Quad band GSM communications for wide regional cellular coverage.
- Rechargeable 3.7V 5000mAh battery and multiple power modes to balance reporting frequency and run time.
- GPS positioning with typical accuracy suitable for vehicle and asset location tracking.
- Movement detection and tamper notification to flag unauthorized handling.
- Geo fencing support for entry and exit alerts based on configured areas.
- Historical route playback capability for post trip analysis and compliance review.
- Remote command support where the hosting platform provides command delivery for supported actions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the LK208 transmits location and device state to provide operational awareness and historical context. Plaspy surfaces those updates in dashboards and maps so teams can monitor assets, investigate events, and tune reporting for endurance or responsiveness.

- Real time position and speed updates displayed on Plaspy maps and device lists.
- Movement, tamper and geo fence events routed into Plaspy alerting and notification channels.
- Historical route playback and exportable trip data for review inside Plaspy.
- Adjustable reporting intervals configurable to balance battery life and telemetry frequency.
- Remote control and command delivery where Plaspy supports sending platform commands to the device.
- Plaspy platform detection and configuration helps simplify adding LK208 devices for immediate visibility.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for temporary or supplemental tracking on cars, vans and light trucks without hardwiring.
- Anti theft recovery by covert magnetic placement on vehicle chassis, trailers or equipment.
- Tracking of construction equipment, rental assets and on site machinery across job sites.
- Short term rentals and logistics where deployable asset locators are needed for tracking periods.
- Temporary monitoring of motorcycles and small vehicles during events or transport.
- OEM and systems integration projects that require a battery powered, easily deployed GPS locator with Plaspy compatibility.
