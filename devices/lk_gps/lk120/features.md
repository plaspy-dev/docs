# LK-GPS - LK120 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the LK-GPS LK120 with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker’s practical capabilities, how those capabilities appear inside Plaspy, and the typical scenarios where the device is useful. Content here is focused on user facing features and how Plaspy supports centralizing location, alerting, and historical tracking for this model.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, local model variant, and installation method. For device specific implementation details such as regulatory band support, firmware-controlled behaviors, or optional accessories, consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The LK120 is a compact wearable GPS tracker designed for real time monitoring of pets and people and for small scale portable asset tracking. When paired with Plaspy, the device reports location and event data for live maps, alerts, and historical route analysis so owners and operators can act quickly.

- Real time GPS tracking over cellular networks for prompt location updates and continuous monitoring.
- Configurable geofence alerts to notify when a device enters or leaves a defined area.
- Dedicated SOS button that sends immediate alerts and location information to configured contacts.
- Wearable, lightweight, and rugged design suitable for collars or personal carry with water resistant housing.
- Rechargeable battery with approximately 72 hours of typical runtime between charges.
- Movement and low battery alerts to help detect roaming or imminent power loss.

## Core Features of LK-GPS - LK120

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward integration into dashboards and alerts.
- Real time location reporting via cellular connectivity for fast position updates.
- Geofence entry and exit alerts that can trigger notifications in Plaspy.
- Dedicated SOS notification function to escalate emergencies with position context.
- Wearable and weather resistant form factor suited for everyday use outdoors.
- Rechargeable battery designed for multi day typical operation on a single charge.
- Configurable movement and low battery notifications to help maintain situational awareness.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the LK120’s location and status messages to provide unified visibility across devices, trigger event driven notifications, and build historical tracks for review. Plaspy also handles device detection and maps device messages into the platform so users see location, alerts, and status without manual protocol work.

- Live map tracking of GPS coordinates and timestamps for immediate location awareness.
- Event handling for SOS activations and geofence breaches so pre configured contacts and workflows can be notified.
- Historical route playback and reporting for review of past movements and patterns.
- Low battery and movement alerts surfaced in Plaspy to prompt proactive maintenance or follow up.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and maps incoming telemetry into its event and device models.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet recovery and anti theft monitoring where rapid location updates and geofence alerts improve recovery chances.
- Shelters and boarding facilities tracking multiple animals and receiving movement or low battery notifications.
- Outdoor recreation monitoring for pets or people during hikes, travel, or day trips.
- Personal safety use where a compact SOS enabled tracker can provide location to responders or contacts.
- Small scale portable asset monitoring for items that need periodic location oversight without vehicle integrations.
