# LK-GPS - LK970

![LK970](./logo..jpg)

The LK970 \(A/B/C\) is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker from KJ-GPS. This 4G tracker is designed for discreet tracking and monitoring of vehicles, motorcycles, and handheld devices. With its compact size and strong magnetic free installation, it can be easily placed and hidden in various locations. The LK970 offers a range of advanced features to ensure accurate and real-time tracking, making it an ideal choice for fleet management and personal use.

One of the standout features of the LK970 is its support for SMS platform mode query and positioning. This allows users to easily track and locate their vehicles or devices by sending a simple SMS command. The tracker also provides real-time tracking, giving users up-to-date information on the location and movement of their assets. Additionally, the LK970 offers inflection point reporting, electronic fence alarms, overspeed/displacement vibration alarms, and GPS data supplement functions, providing comprehensive monitoring and security.

The LK970 is equipped with a 3.7V 5000mAh polymer battery, ensuring long-lasting performance and reliable tracking. It also features a built-in battery of 6000mAh, providing backup power in case of power loss or disconnection. The tracker supports various bands, including FDD-LTE, TDD-LTE, WCDMA, and GSM, ensuring wide coverage and compatibility. With its Android and iOS compatible track view app, users can easily access and manage their tracking data on their smartphones or tablets.

With its advanced features, compact size, and reliable performance, the LK970 is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a high-quality GPS tracker for vehicles, motorcycles, or handheld devices. Whether for fleet management or personal use, the LK970 offers the functionality and reliability you need to keep track of your assets.

### Outstanding Features:

- Supports SMS platform mode query and positioning
- Real-time tracking for accurate location information
- Inflection point reporting for detailed tracking data
- Electronic fence alarm for added security
- Overspeed/displacement vibration alarm for monitoring driving behavior
- GPS data supplement function for enhanced tracking accuracy
- Built-in battery of 6000mAh for backup power
- Compact size and strong magnetic free installation
- Compatible with Android and iOS devices

### Technical Specifications:

- Size: 95mm x 60mm x 33.6mm
- CPU: SC9820E
- Band: 2G: 900/1800, TDD: B38 / B39 / B40 / B41, FDD: B1 / B3 / B5 / B7 / B8 / B20
