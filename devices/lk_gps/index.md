# LK-GPS

LK-GPS is a known manufacturer of GPS tracking hardware used in vehicle and asset monitoring. This page explains how LK-GPS devices integrate with Plaspy, what to expect from the device family in general terms, and where to find compatible models within Plaspy. Use the catalog below to browse all supported LK-GPS devices and jump to individual device pages for setup and configuration details.

## How LK-GPS devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy connects to compatible LK-GPS trackers using the device telemetry they transmit over the cellular network. Once a device is added to Plaspy, the platform ingests location updates, status signals, and common telemetry so you can monitor units in real time, review history, and create alerts. Plaspy’s device pages and documentation provide the steps to register an LK-GPS tracker and configure reporting intervals, geofences, and notifications where those capabilities are available on the device.

If you need more information from the manufacturer, visit the LK-GPS website at https://www.lk-gps.com for product specifications and manuals.

## Common features found in LK-GPS devices

While exact capabilities vary by model, LK-GPS trackers commonly offer a set of features useful for vehicle and asset management, such as:
- GPS location reporting and basic position history
- Cellular connectivity for remote telemetry
- Configurable reporting intervals and power modes
- Event notifications like movement, tamper, or low battery alerts
- Support for geofencing and overspeed alerts on many models

Plaspy reads the available telemetry from each device and exposes these capabilities through its tracking interface and alerting system. For model specific capabilities see the device page in the catalog above.

## Typical use cases for LK-GPS GPS trackers

LK-GPS trackers are used across a variety of scenarios including:
- Light vehicle tracking for mileage and route visibility
- Fleet asset monitoring to reduce theft and unauthorized use
- Equipment and trailer tracking for improved asset utilization
- Remote asset monitoring where cellular coverage is available

Plaspy helps turn raw device data into operational insights like trip reports, idle analytics, and event-based notifications.

## Why choose Plaspy for LK-GPS device monitoring

Plaspy provides a flexible platform to monitor LK-GPS devices alongside trackers from other brands. Benefits include:

Plaspy is designed to make it straightforward to add your LK-GPS devices, tune reporting behavior, and use the platform’s dashboards and reports for daily operations.
