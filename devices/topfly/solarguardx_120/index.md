# TopFly - SolarGuardX 120

<div className="device-logo">
![SolarGuardX 120](./tracker.png)
</div>

SolarGuardX 120 is a rugged, solar powered sub lock built for secure remote asset protection and synchronized multi lock operation. Designed for outdoor logistics, the unit brings low maintenance security to containers, trailers, pallets and other high value assets. Its weatherproof housing and solar assisted charging are intended to extend field life and reduce the frequency of onsite service.

As a Plaspy compatible device, SolarGuardX 120 contributes security telemetry and status events that augment GPS tracker position feeds within Plaspy. That integration enables combined anti theft monitoring, coordinated master and slave lock actions, and centralized reporting to support fleet and asset management workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatibility for delivering tamper events battery and lock state telemetry alongside GPS data.
- Solar assisted operation for extended field life and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Long range wireless control for coordinated master and slave lock operation across multiple access points.
- Rugged IP67 rated housing suitable for harsh outdoor logistics and asset protection.
- Local alarms and visible status indicators to support quick diagnostics at the asset.
- Remote management features including firmware updates and USB data access for lifecycle maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

SolarGuardX 120 provides security events and status information that Plaspy ingests and correlates with GPS tracker feeds to create a unified view of both location and asset condition. Within Plaspy this data is used to improve situational awareness, trigger alerts, and maintain audit trails for access events.

- Real time telemetry and event alerts such as tamper motion and open or close events appear alongside location data in Plaspy dashboards.
- Master and slave lock coordination is recorded for synchronized unlocking and centralized audit reporting.
- BLE telemetry can be collected through Plaspy compatible gateways or mobile devices to associate lock status with tracker positions.
- Remote firmware updates and diagnostics help maintain consistent configurations across deployments managed through Plaspy.
- Aggregated reporting and historical event logs enable analysis of access patterns and security incidents for operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer logistics where synchronized multi point locking and audit trails are required.
- High value asset protection combining tamper and movement alerts with GPS location for anti theft monitoring.
- Cold chain or sensitive cargo where local condition awareness supplements routing and compliance reporting.
- Distributed asset fleets and pallets deployed in remote locations that benefit from solar assisted low maintenance security.
- Coordinated multi lock operations in complex loading or access workflows for large vehicles and intermodal containers.

SolarGuardX 120 is well suited to organizations that need to add a dedicated security layer to GPS based fleet and asset management. Its design emphasizes long field life, environmental resilience, and coordinated access control, which complement Plaspy's strengths in real time tracking, alerting, and centralized reporting. When combined in a Plaspy deployment, the SolarGuardX 120 helps improve auditability of access events and provides operators with actionable security telemetry alongside positional data.
