# TopFly - SolarGuardX 200

![SolarGuardX 200](./tracker.png)

The SolarGuardX 200 by TopFly is a rugged, solar powered cargo GPS tracker and high security padlock designed for long term outdoor telematics. Built for container, trailer and remote asset protection, the unit combines persistent solar charging, a large 14,400 mAh Li‑Polymer battery and an IP67 rated enclosure to deliver extended autonomy, real time tracking and tamper alerts in a weatherproof padlock form factor.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, SolarGuardX 200 can feed location, security events and status telemetry into Plaspy for fleet monitoring and incident management. Its tamper reporting, lock and unlock events, buffered history and remote command capabilities make it a practical choice for organizations that want to centralize tracking and alerting in Plaspy dashboards without frequent on site maintenance.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible solar powered GPS padlock for container, trailer and remote asset protection.
- Long field autonomy from a 14,400 mAh rechargeable battery assisted by an integrated solar panel.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and padlock design with tamper detection for rope cut, SIM cover removal and lock unlock events.
- High accuracy GNSS positioning with parallel satellite systems delivering sub 2.5 m horizontal accuracy for precise location reporting.
- On device storage for buffered tracking to preserve history during coverage outages.
- Multiple remote access and management features including OTA firmware updates and remote lock unlock commands.
- Built for harsh outdoor use with weatherproof construction, status indicators and onboard environmental monitoring capabilities.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SolarGuardX 200 delivers continuous location updates and security events into your Plaspy account so you can monitor assets, trigger alerts and include device telemetry in operational workflows. Plaspy ingests the device reports and exposes them through maps, notifications and reporting tools used for fleet oversight and incident response.

- Real time location and telemetry displayed on Plaspy maps for live asset visibility and route monitoring
- Tamper, lock and unlock events forwarded to Plaspy to generate immediate alerts and notification workflows
- Buffered location history uploaded to Plaspy after outages to provide gapless tracking and audit trails
- Remote commands and configuration sent from Plaspy to control lock state and adjust device reporting behavior
- Centralized device management with FOTA and status monitoring so fleets remain up to date and visible in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Remote cargo monitoring where long autonomy and solar charging reduce maintenance visits
- Customs and asset protection deployments that rely on tamper detection and event audit trails
- Outdoor equipment and high value asset tracking in exposed environments requiring weatherproof hardware
- Fleet integration for route auditing, geofence enforcement and operational reporting in a single platform
