# TopFly - SolarGuardX 200

![SolarGuardX 200](./tracker.png)

The SolarGuardX 200 by SolarGuard is a rugged, solar-powered cargo GPS tracker and high-security padlock engineered for long-term outdoor telematics. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this GPS tracker pairs persistent solar charging, a large 14,400 mAh Li‑Polymer battery and an IP67-rated enclosure to deliver extended autonomy for container, trailer and remote-asset protection with real-time tracking and tamper alerts.

Designed for fleet management and anti-theft deployments, SolarGuardX 200 reports lock/unlock events, rope-cut and SIM-cover removal alarms in real time and supports remote and local opening via RFID, Bluetooth 5.1, OTA commands or SMS. With parallel GNSS \(GPS + GLONASS + Galileo + Beidou\) and a Qualcomm Gen 8C receiver, the unit gives accuracy better than 2.5 m CEP and frequent position updates for accurate telemetry and integration with Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for reliable real-time tracking and fleet management of containers, trailers and outdoor assets.
- Long autonomy from a 14,400 mAh Li‑Polymer battery plus integrated solar panel for extended field operations.
- Rugged IP67 padlock form factor with tamper detection: rope-cut, SIM-cover removal and lock/unlock event reporting.
- High-accuracy GNSS: Qualcomm Gen 8C with GPS + GLONASS + Galileo + Beidou and \<2.5 m horizontal accuracy \(CEP\).
- Multiple connectivity options \(LTE Cat 4 / Cat 1 by SKU, WCDMA, GSM/EDGE\) with TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS reporting and optional MD5/AES256 encryption.
- Local and remote access methods \(RFID, BLE 5.1, OTA, SMS\) plus FOTA-enabled firmware management for fleet-wide updates.
- Compact, weatherproof design with USB‑C and 2‑pin 5V charging ports, onboard temperature sensor and status LEDs.

## How It Works with Plaspy

SolarGuardX 200 integrates with Plaspy to deliver continuous GPS-based location, event telemetry and tamper alerts into your Plaspy account. The device pushes position updates, security events and sensor data using standard protocols \(TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS\), enabling Plaspy to display real-time location, generate geofence notifications, and feed telemetry into fleet management workflows and reporting tools.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(position reports as frequent as every 3 seconds\)
- Lock/unlock events and tamper alarms \(rope cut, SIM cover removal, locking/unlocking\) forwarded to Plaspy for immediate alerts
- Buffered tracking: on-device storage of up to 49,000 location points for gapless history during coverage outages
- Remote control and commands via OTA or SMS for lock/unlock and device configuration from Plaspy
- BLE 5.1 accessory support \(compatible with TOPFLYtech TSTH1-B, TSDT1-B and TSR1-B\) to add temperature, motion or other sensor telemetry into Plaspy
