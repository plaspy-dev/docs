# TopFly - Pioneer X100

![Pioneer X100](./Logo.png)

The **PioneerX 100 \(4G CAT-1\)** is a wired GNSS tracker designed for versatile applications in fleet management and vehicle security. This compact and robust device supports global LTE Cat-1 bands with 2G fallback, ensuring reliable connectivity even in areas with limited network coverage. Its design includes multiple digital inputs and outputs, making it ideal for monitoring conditions like engine ignition, relay activation, and accessories such as SOS buttons. Additionally, its compatibility with Bluetooth \(BLE 4.2\) sensors enables integration with accessories for monitoring temperature, humidity, and door status.

Equipped with driver behavior detection and aggressive driving alerts, this tracker enhances safety while offering advanced functionalities such as crash detection and incident data analysis for reconstruction. The firmware can be updated remotely \(FOTA\), ensuring the device remains up-to-date without physical intervention. It is suitable for commercial fleets, personal vehicles, and any asset with a constant power source.

### Key Features

- **Global Connectivity:** Supports LTE Cat-1 bands with 2G fallback for reliable usage anywhere.
- **Real-Time Monitoring:** Scheduled reports based on time, angle, or distance.
- **Integration with BLE Sensors:** Monitors temperature, humidity, and door status via additional accessories.
- **Alerts and Notifications:** Includes alerts for events like engine on/off, power disconnection, and more.
- **Compact and Versatile Design:** Perfect for hidden installations in various vehicle types.

This tracker combines advanced functionality with practical design and simple implementation, ensuring efficient and secure monitoring for any vehicular application.
