# TopFly - TLW2-12B Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the TopFly TLW2-12B with Plaspy. It focuses on the model's capabilities relevant to fleet and asset tracking, how those capabilities map into Plaspy workflows, and practical operational value for trailer and long-dwell asset monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device setup, firmware updates, and the most current technical details consult official TopFly documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The TLW2-12B is a hardwired trailer tracker designed for rugged, long-dwell asset tracking with native compatibility for Plaspy. It combines frequent real-time updates, local buffering for offline periods, BLE sensor support, and configurable inputs and outputs to support a range of trailer and cold-chain workflows.

- Real-time position updates configurable down to 3 second intervals for live fleet visibility.
- Large internal rechargeable battery to maintain tracking and telemetry when external power is not available.
- Buffered local storage that preserves location history during coverage gaps and uploads when connectivity returns.
- BLE 5.0 support for pairing environmental sensors and wireless relays to extend telemetry.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and mounting options intended for trailers and outdoor assets.
- Multi-band cellular connectivity and standard data protocols to report device and sensor data into Plaspy.

## Core Features of TopFly - TLW2-12B

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward integration into real-time tracking and alerting workflows.
- Frequent real-time tracking with configurable reporting intervals as short as 3 seconds.
- High-capacity internal rechargeable battery for extended off‑power operation and long standby.
- Large buffered storage to retain tens of thousands of location points while offline.
- BLE 5.0 radio for connecting temperature and humidity sensors and wireless relays.
- Rugged IP67 rated housing designed for trailer mounting and outdoor exposure.
- Configurable digital and analog inputs and outputs for alarms, door sensors, and remote output control.
- Multi GNSS support and high position accuracy enabled by the onboard GNSS receiver.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TLW2-12B is connected to Plaspy, its location and sensor streams feed Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and historical reports so operators can monitor trailers in real time and investigate events after the fact.

- Live location and movement updates appear on Plaspy maps for tracking and dispatch visibility.
- Buffered points collected during coverage loss are uploaded to Plaspy on reconnection, preserving trip history.
- BLE sensor readings forwarded to Plaspy enable environmental monitoring and threshold alerts for cold-chain use.
- Digital input and output events show as status changes or triggers inside Plaspy for automated notifications and workflows.
- Remote output control and alarm commands issued from Plaspy can be relayed back to the device to activate accessories or alert circuits.

## Typical Use Cases

- Persistent trailer tracking across long yard dwell times and between truck hookups.
- Anti-theft monitoring and response with movement and disconnection alerts plus remote output actions.
- Cold-chain monitoring with BLE temperature and humidity sensors reporting to Plaspy for threshold alerts.
- Off-grid or parked asset tracking where battery endurance and buffered reporting preserve historical data.
- Remote status monitoring for doors, alarms, and auxiliary sensors integrated into fleet operations.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the TLW2-12B with Plaspy gives organizations centralized visibility into trailer location, environmental telemetry, and device events. Plaspy consumes the tracker’s frequent updates and buffered history to provide continuous maps, alerts, and reports that help reduce recovery time, protect temperature sensitive cargo, and streamline yard operations.

Plaspy’s platform is designed to accept telemetry and events from compatible trackers like the TLW2-12B so fleets can combine position data, sensor readings, and input/output events into operational dashboards and automated notifications.
