# TopFly - WarriorX 300

![WarriorX 300](./tracker.png)

The TOPFLYtech WarriorX 300 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for long-term outdoor asset monitoring. As a standalone, battery-powered 4G CAT‑M1 asset tracker with replaceable non-rechargeable batteries, it delivers maintenance-free, multi-year reporting for trailers, containers, high-value assets and distributed equipment. Plaspy users gain reliable real-time tracking and condition telemetry from a device engineered for harsh environments.

Designed for field durability and flexible reporting, the WarriorX 300 combines IP67 waterproofing, an all-in-one GNSS receiver and low-power LTE/NB cellular connectivity to provide consistent position updates and event alerts. Its configurable reporting intervals and SVR mode let Plaspy managers balance battery life with high-frequency updates when an asset requires closer monitoring.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for multi-year, low-maintenance deployments on outdoor assets.
- Ultra-long battery life with replaceable 4000 mAh or optional 8000 mAh non-rechargeable cells — ideal for remote fleet management and unattended assets.
- Rugged IP67 housing and wide operating temperature range for reliable field operation in logistics and cold‑chain scenarios.
- Flexible reporting profiles and SVR mode for adaptive real-time tracking when an event or movement is detected.
- Removal alerts and multiple mounting options \(bracket, magnet, screws, polyurethane straps, zip ties\) to support anti-theft and tamper detection workflows.
- Integrated multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou/Galileo/QZSS\) with autonomous horizontal accuracy \< 1.5 m CEP.
- BLE ecosystem support for Bluetooth sensors \(temperature & humidity\), enabling telemetry and condition sensing for cold‑chain monitoring.
- Secure transports \(TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS\) and optional MD5/AES encryption to protect telemetry to Plaspy servers.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the WarriorX 300 streams location and status data over cellular networks to Plaspy’s platform using supported protocols \(TCP, UDP, MQTT or SMS\). Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes, device telemetry and alert events so operators can access real-time tracking, historical playback and automated notifications across fleets and asset groups.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates from multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS + GLONASS + BeiDou + Galileo + QZSS\).
- Removal and tamper alerts delivered to Plaspy for instant anti-theft notifications and geofence-triggered workflows.
- Low-battery alarms and status LED indicators reported to Plaspy to simplify maintenance planning.
- Bluetooth sensors for temperature and humidity can forward condition telemetry to Plaspy for cold‑chain visibility.
- Remote firmware updates \(FOTA\) and configuration via Plaspy-compatible provisioning tools or Type‑C USB for on-site maintenance.

## Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking: long-life battery and rugged enclosure make it ideal for extended deployments without frequent maintenance.
- High-value asset protection: removal alerts and geofencing integrated with Plaspy enable rapid anti-theft responses.
- Logistics and equipment visibility: periodic reporting and SVR mode provide low-duty power consumption with on-demand real-time updates for fleet management.
- Cold‑chain condition monitoring: pair with Bluetooth temperature & humidity sensors to feed Plaspy with environmental telemetry alongside location.
- Remote site and asset telemetry: durable IP67 housing and long battery life support monitoring of pumps, generators and storage units in remote locations.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The WarriorX 300 is purpose-built for asset owners and integrators who need trustworthy, long-life GPS trackers that integrate smoothly with Plaspy. Its low-power architecture, flexible reporting and strong GNSS performance deliver accurate real-time tracking and historical telemetry while minimizing maintenance cycles. Removal alerts, robust waterproofing and a range of mounting options make deployment straightforward for field technicians, and BLE accessory support extends Plaspy’s capability into temperature and condition sensing for cold‑chain and logistics applications.

For organizations that require scalable fleet management, anti-theft alerts and consolidated telemetry, the WarriorX 300 paired with Plaspy provides a durable, secure and easy-to-manage solution. Note that WarriorX 300 focuses on asset tracking and telemetry; for vehicle-specific ignition or immobilizer control, Plaspy can integrate those functions using compatible vehicle-grade trackers alongside the WarriorX line.
