# TopFly - WarriorX 300

<div className="device-logo">
![WarriorX 300](./tracker.png)
</div>

The TopFly WarriorX 300 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for long term outdoor asset monitoring. As a standalone, battery powered device with replaceable non rechargeable batteries, it is built to deliver multi year reporting for trailers, containers, high value assets and distributed equipment. The product emphasizes durability and low maintenance for deployments where ongoing access is limited.

Because the WarriorX 300 is engineered for field durability and configurable reporting, it pairs well with Plaspy for fleet and asset management. Plaspy users can ingest position updates, alerts and condition telemetry from the device to maintain visibility, automate notifications and reduce maintenance overhead for widely distributed assets.

## Key Highlights

- Built for long term outdoor deployments with replaceable non rechargeable batteries that support multi year reporting.
- Rugged IP67 housing for reliable operation in harsh environments and outdoor logistics.
- Configurable reporting profiles and SVR mode to balance battery life with higher frequency updates when needed.
- Removal and tamper alerts plus multiple mounting options to support anti theft and tamper detection workflows.
- Multi constellation GNSS for consistent positioning and accurate location reporting.
- BLE accessory support for optional temperature and humidity sensors to extend environmental visibility.
- Designed to integrate with fleet platforms such as Plaspy for centralized monitoring and alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected with Plaspy, the WarriorX 300 sends location fixes, device status and event notifications over cellular networks so operators can monitor assets in real time and review historical movement. Plaspy collects those inputs to present live maps, automated alerts and fleet level reporting for operational decision making.

- Real time location updates and status reporting visible inside Plaspy dashboards and maps.
- Tamper and removal alerts fed into Plaspy to trigger notifications and geofence workflows.
- Battery and device health indicators available in Plaspy to support maintenance planning and replacement cycles.
- Condition telemetry from Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors forwarded to Plaspy for cold chain and environmental monitoring.
- Remote configuration and provisioning compatible with Plaspy enabled workflows to adjust reporting profiles as operational needs change.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking for long term unattended asset visibility.
- High value asset protection using removal alerts and geofencing integrated with Plaspy notifications.
- Logistics and equipment visibility that balances low power reporting with event driven high frequency updates.
- Cold chain monitoring when paired with Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors for combined location and condition telemetry.
- Remote site asset telemetry for pumps, generators and storage units where rugged enclosure and long battery life reduce visits.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The WarriorX 300 is a practical choice for organizations that need durable, low maintenance trackers that feed a centralized fleet or asset management platform. Its combination of rugged construction, configurable reporting and accessory support helps Plaspy users maintain accurate location visibility while minimizing field service requirements.
