# TopFly - WarriorX 300 Features

This page provides a public, feature oriented summary of the TopFly WarriorX 300 and how it is used with Plaspy for asset tracking and condition monitoring. It focuses on practical capabilities and describes how the device’s capabilities map to visibility and operational workflows inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant this page notes optional accessories and common deployment considerations, and it encourages review of the manufacturer documentation for the most current device specifics.

## Feature Overview

The WarriorX 300 is a rugged, battery powered asset tracker built for long term outdoor deployments and integrated telemetry. It is designed to minimize maintenance while providing configurable reporting and event alerts that Plaspy can ingest for fleet and asset management.

- Long life replaceable non rechargeable batteries designed for multi year reporting cycles
- Rugged IP67 rating for outdoor, unattended installations
- Multi constellation GNSS for precise position updates and reliable location fixes
- Configurable reporting intervals and SVR mode for adaptive tracking when movement or events occur
- BLE accessory support for temperature and humidity sensors to add condition telemetry
- Removal and tamper alert capability with multiple mounting options for anti theft workflows

## Core Features of TopFly - WarriorX 300

- Cellular connectivity optimized for low power wide area networks including LTE Cat M1 and NB2 with fallback cellular support
- Replaceable non rechargeable battery options with multi year operational profiles for low maintenance asset monitoring
- IP67 waterproof housing and wide operating temperature range for harsh environment deployments
- Integrated multi constellation GNSS receiver with high horizontal accuracy for dependable location data
- Configurable reporting profiles and SVR mode to balance update frequency and battery life
- BLE support for TOPFLYtech sensors such as temperature and humidity for condition monitoring
- Removal and tamper alerts combined with multiple mounting options including brackets and magnetic mounts
- Secure telemetry transports and optional data protection with supported encryption methods plus remote firmware update support and Type C configuration

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the WarriorX 300’s position fixes, telemetry and alert events and presents them alongside other fleet and asset data. The device’s long life design and optional sensors extend Plaspy visibility without frequent onsite maintenance.

- Real time location updates and historical playback in Plaspy from the device’s GNSS fixes
- Condition telemetry from BLE temperature and humidity sensors shown alongside location for cold chain visibility
- Removal and tamper alerts routed to Plaspy notifications and workflows to support anti theft response
- Battery and status reporting surfaced in Plaspy to help schedule maintenance and battery replacement
- Configurable reporting modes reflected in Plaspy as lower or higher frequency position streams depending on device state
- Remote firmware update status and provisioning notes available when managed alongside Plaspy compatible provisioning tools

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking for long unattended deployments requiring minimal maintenance
- High value asset protection using removal alerts and geofencing driven workflows
- Logistics fleet visibility with low duty periodic reporting and on demand higher frequency updates
- Cold chain monitoring by pairing BLE temperature and humidity sensors to the tracker
- Remote site asset monitoring for equipment stored in the field or off grid locations
- Distributed equipment tracking where rugged enclosure and battery lifetime are primary concerns
