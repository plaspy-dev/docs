# TopFly - SolarGuardX 100 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TopFly SolarGuardX 100 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It summarizes the model's practical functions for fleet and cargo security, explains the types of telemetry and events the device can provide, and describes how those signals appear in Plaspy for monitoring and operational workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware revision, hardware variant, installation method, regional product version, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a general guide to functionality when planning Plaspy deployments and consult the manufacturer and device documentation for the most current, device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The SolarGuardX 100 is a rugged, solar assisted GPS tracker and padlock designed for outdoor asset protection and long duration deployments. It combines a high capacity battery with integrated solar charging, industrial waterproofing, and multiple wireless interfaces so that location, alarms, and auxiliary sensor data can be delivered into Plaspy for near real time visibility and resiliency during connectivity interruptions.

- Solar assisted power with large rechargeable battery and USB Type C charging for extended field endurance and low maintenance operations.
- Industrial IP67 enclosure, built in magnet and supplied rope for secure mounting on containers, trailers, and trucks.
- High accuracy GNSS positioning with multi constellation support to improve location reliability for cargo and fleet tracking.
- Wide cellular connectivity including LTE Cat M1 and NB2 with GSM fallback for broad network coverage and IoT optimized links.
- BLE accessory support for temperature, humidity and door sensors so environmental telemetry can be paired with location data.
- Large local buffering and configurable geofence and security alarms to preserve track history and trigger alerts during incidents.

## Core Features of TopFly - SolarGuardX 100

- Solar assisted battery system with onboard rechargeable pack and USB Type C charging to reduce manual recharge cycles.
- Rugged padlock form factor with IP67 ingress protection, integrated magnet and supplied rope for secure outdoor mounting.
- GNSS performance with multi constellation operation for submeter to low meter class horizontal accuracy in typical conditions.
- Cellular connectivity that includes LTE Cat M1 and NB2 plus GSM fallback to support a wide range of network environments.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support to pair with TOPFLYtech and third party BLE accessories for environmental and door sensor telemetry.
- Large local storage buffer to retain thousands of location points when coverage is unavailable and forward them when connectivity resumes.
- Configurable security events such as rope cut and lock or unlock alarms, with event reporting to backend platforms.
- Remote management features including over the air updates and network command configuration to adjust behavior in the field.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, SolarGuardX 100 devices report position, events, and sensor telemetry so operations and security teams can track assets and respond to alarms. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and ingests location and event messages for visualization and rule based workflows.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks appear in Plaspy so dispatchers can see asset movement and recent position history.
- Security alarms and geofence events such as rope cut and lock or unlock are delivered to Plaspy for notifications and escalation.
- Buffered location and event uploads are reconciled in Plaspy after connectivity is restored to maintain a continuous activity record.
- BLE accessory telemetry from paired sensors can be forwarded to Plaspy as auxiliary telemetry for environmental monitoring.
- Remote configuration and firmware updates are reflected in device state within Plaspy so administrators can manage fleet settings and view device health.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer anti theft monitoring with rope cut and lock state alerts reported to operations teams.
- Fleet asset tracking for trailers, portable equipment and intermodal containers to improve visibility and utilization.
- Cold chain and temperature sensitive cargo monitoring by forwarding BLE sensor telemetry alongside location.
- Port, yard and staging area monitoring where rugged weatherproof devices reduce maintenance needs.
- Remote deployments that rely on solar charging and large buffers to survive intermittent connectivity.
- Last mile or access control workflows that use RFID or BLE unlocking in coordinated operational procedures.
