# TopFly - SolarGuardX 100

<div className="device-logo">
![SolarGuardX 100](./tracker.png)
</div>

SolarGuardX 100 is a rugged, solar assisted cargo GPS tracker and padlock designed for outdoor asset protection. Built for containers, trailers and trucks, it combines extended battery capacity with integrated solar charging and an industrial waterproof enclosure to provide persistent, low maintenance tracking and security. The unit includes physical mounting aids such as a strong embedded magnet and supplied rope to suit demanding transport and storage environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, SolarGuardX 100 feeds location, alarm and auxiliary telemetry into Plaspy for real time visibility and operational workflows. Its design is aligned to fleet management and anti theft scenarios, making it a practical choice for operators who want frequent updates, buffered data resilience and the ability to surface geofence and security events inside Plaspy dashboards and alerting systems.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for outdoor cargo security with a rugged IP67 rated enclosure and physical mounting features for containers and trailers.
- Solar assisted power and substantial onboard capacity to reduce maintenance cycles and extend field endurance.
- Integrates GNSS performance and buffering to maintain tracking through coverage gaps and ensure historical data is preserved.
- Supports wireless accessory data that can augment cargo monitoring, including temperature and door status from compatible sensors.
- Delivers configurable security events such as rope cut and lock or unlock notifications to support anti theft workflows.
- Wide cellular coverage options and fallback behavior to keep devices connected across different network conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once registered in Plaspy, SolarGuardX 100 streams position, event and sensor data so operations and security teams can monitor assets in near real time and automate responses to alarms. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it available for live tracking, alerts and reporting.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for dispatch and monitoring, configurable to match operational needs.
- Geofence events, motion and security alarms routed into Plaspy for automated notifications and workflow triggers.
- Local buffering of location points to prevent data loss during connectivity outages, with automatic upload when coverage is restored.
- Forwarding of compatible accessory telemetry such as temperature or door sensor readings to Plaspy for consolidated visibility.
- Secure network transports and payload protection options supported to align device traffic with enterprise data policies.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer security with alarmed rope cut and lock unlock events reported into Plaspy.
- Fleet asset tracking for trailers and portable equipment to improve routing, utilization and oversight.
- Cold chain or temperature sensitive cargo monitoring by pairing BLE sensors and forwarding readings to Plaspy.
- Remote access and control workflows that combine local unlocking methods with remote command options managed via Plaspy.
- Resilient tracking in areas with intermittent coverage using extensive local buffering and delayed upload.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

SolarGuardX 100 is focused on long duration outdoor deployments where low maintenance and reliable event reporting matter. Its combination of solar charging, strong ingress protection and GNSS backed position reporting makes it a practical option for operators who need continuous cargo visibility and alarmed security events delivered into Plaspy. Support for sensor accessories and multiple remote control methods extends its usefulness for telemetry enriched workflows.
