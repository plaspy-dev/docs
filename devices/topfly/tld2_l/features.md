# TopFly - TLD2-L Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TopFly TLD2-L GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive details about what the device can do when paired with the Plaspy platform and what fleet managers and integrators can expect to see in the platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware revision, hardware variant, regional configuration and installation type. For critical or security sensitive details consult the official manufacturer documentation and release notes to confirm behavior for your specific device and firmware.

## Feature Overview

The TLD2-L is a compact plug-and-play OBDII tracker designed for rapid deployment and continuous vehicle telemetry. It combines frequent GNSS fixes, cellular connectivity, BLE accessory support and event detection to deliver location and behavior data into Plaspy for monitoring and operational workflows.

- Plug-and-play OBDII installation for quick field deployment without wiring.
- Frequent real-time tracking with configurable reporting intervals down to seconds and large offline buffering for coverage gaps.
- LTE Cat M1 and Cat NB2 cellular connectivity with GSM fallback to maintain wide network reach.
- Built-in 6-axis accelerometer for harsh driving and collision detection to support driver behavior analytics.
- BLE 4.0/5.0 compatibility for pairing temperature, humidity, contact sensors and wireless relay accessories.
- Remote firmware updates via FOTA and support for common transport protocols to maintain devices at scale.

## Core Features of TopFly - TLD2-L

- OBDII form factor for tool free installation and easy redeployment between vehicles.
- High-frequency reporting and on-device buffer storage to preserve telemetry during network outages.
- LTE-M and NB‑IoT connectivity with legacy GSM fallback for broad carrier compatibility.
- 6-axis accelerometer enabled events such as harsh acceleration, harsh braking, sharp turns and collision detection.
- BLE sensor support to extend telemetry with external temperature, humidity or contact sensors and to drive wireless relays.
- Ignition on and off alerts plus disconnection detection with internal backup battery for anti-tamper awareness.
- Firmware over the air (FOTA) and support for TCP, UDP, MQTT and SMS transport protocols for remote management and data delivery.
- GNSS positioning with fast time to first fix and accuracy suitable for vehicle tracking and fleet operations.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the TLD2-L telemetry so teams can visualize location, receive event alerts and analyze driver behavior in a single fleet management view. The device's reporting options and sensor inputs translate into actionable items inside Plaspy for dispatch, safety and condition monitoring.

- Live location traces and historical routes visible on Plaspy maps using the device reports.
- Driving behavior events from the accelerometer presented as alerts and analytics for safety programs.
- Ignition, disconnection and low battery events flagged in Plaspy for anti-theft and operational notifications.
- BLE sensor telemetry surfaced alongside location to correlate environmental conditions with route and time.
- Scheduled reporting and buffered uploads help ensure data continuity in Plaspy even through temporary network interruptions.
- FOTA and transport protocol support enable remote maintenance and reliable data delivery into the platform.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and route visibility for dispatch teams and operational oversight.
- Driver behavior monitoring and safety programs using accelerometer derived events.
- Cargo and condition monitoring by pairing BLE temperature and humidity sensors to the tracker.
- Anti-theft and tamper detection using ignition status and disconnection alerts with remote response options.
- Shared or rental vehicle programs that benefit from quick OBDII installation and minimal downtime.
- Connected asset initiatives for light commercial vehicles where integrated telemetry and sensor data matter.
