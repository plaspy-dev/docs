# TopFly - TorchX 110

![TorchX 110](./tracker.jpg)

The TopFly TorchX 110 is a plug-and-play OBDII GPS tracker designed for fleet telematics, usage based insurance programs, and vehicle diagnostics. It combines high resolution real time tracking with vehicle level telemetry and accessory support in an OBDII form factor, enabling rapid deployment across mixed fleets. The TorchX 110 is engineered to provide frequent position updates, CAN bus vehicle parameters, and Bluetooth sensor connectivity to support broad telematics needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TorchX 110 streams location and vehicle telemetry into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring, alerts, and reporting. Its combination of GNSS and supplemental positioning, onboard buffering, and vehicle data access makes it a practical choice for organizations that want to capture both precise location and validated vehicle signals within Plaspy dashboards and workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible plug and play OBDII connection for fast integration and deployment.
- High resolution real time tracking with position updates as frequent as every 3 seconds for detailed route and driver analysis.
- CAN bus telemetry providing ignition status, VIN, true odometer, fuel level and Diagnostic Trouble Codes for vehicle insights.
- Hybrid positioning using GNSS plus Wi Fi positioning and Bluetooth to improve fixes in urban and challenging environments.
- Large offline buffer to store extended location history when the device is out of network coverage.
- BLE 5.0 sensor support for temperature, humidity, door sensors and other accessory telemetry.
- Crash detection and remote maintenance features including over the air firmware updates to support device lifecycle management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, TorchX 110 delivers continuous location updates and parsed vehicle telemetry into Plaspy for visualization, alerting and historical analysis. Plaspy uses these inputs to populate live dashboards, trigger rules, and generate reports that support operations, safety, and maintenance decision making.

- Real time location streaming and frequent updates to Plaspy for live maps and route monitoring.
- Immediate ignition and power alerts forwarded to Plaspy for anti theft and operational notifications.
- Vehicle diagnostics and DTC forwarding to support maintenance workflows and mileage verification inside Plaspy reports.
- Crash detection and incident data delivered to Plaspy for reconstruction and incident response workflows.
- Bluetooth sensor data integrated into Plaspy rules and alerts for cold chain and asset condition monitoring.
- Offline buffering ensures Plaspy receives consistent historical data and reduces gaps during network outages.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for real time tracking, route replay and driver performance monitoring.
- Usage based insurance programs needing accurate odometer, VIN and driving event telemetry.
- Anti theft and security with ignition alerts, power disconnection notifications and crash detection.
- Cold chain and asset monitoring using BLE temperature and door sensors tied into alerts and reporting.
- Vehicle diagnostics and preventive maintenance driven by DTCs, fuel level and validated odometer data.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TorchX 110 is a practical choice for organizations seeking a compact OBDII device that balances frequent location updates with validated vehicle telemetry. Its plug and play design reduces deployment complexity across large fleets, while onboard buffering and hybrid positioning help maintain reliable tracking in varied operating environments. CAN level data gives Plaspy the verified vehicle signals needed for maintenance, compliance, and usage based analytics.
