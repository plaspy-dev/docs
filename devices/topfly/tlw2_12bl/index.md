# TopFly - TLW2-12BL

<div className="device-logo">
![TLW2-12BL](./tracker.png)
</div>

The TLW2-12BL is a hardwired 4G LTE vehicle GPS tracker designed for fleet management, anti theft protection, and advanced telemetry. It provides high frequency position updates, robust GNSS performance, and on device support for external sensors, making it suitable for operators who need dependable location intelligence and vehicle oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the TLW2-12BL connects into Plaspy to deliver live location, telemetry and event data to dashboards, maps and reports. Its feature set—including global cellular coverage with fallback, extensive I O and integrated sensor support—makes it a practical choice for organizations that want to add precise tracking and remote vehicle control to their Plaspy installation.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker offering high frequency location updates for precise fleet visibility.
- Global 4G connectivity with fallback for resilient tracking across wide deployment areas.
- Integrated Bluetooth Low Energy support for external temperature, humidity and door sensors.
- Extensive I O for ignition detection and relay controlled immobilization to support anti theft workflows.
- On device buffering of location points to preserve history during temporary network interruptions.
- Vehicle safety features such as crash and towing detection and driving behavior monitoring.
- Compact, vehicle grade housing and wide operating voltage range for diverse fleet installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TLW2-12BL streams position and telemetry to the platform where data is presented in live maps, event feeds and reports. Plaspy ingests vehicle location, input state changes and sensor telemetry and makes them available for monitoring, alerting and historical analysis while device side buffering helps maintain continuity across intermittent connectivity.

- Real time location and telemetry visible in Plaspy live views and mapping, with configurable update frequency.
- Ignition and digital input states used for trip detection, door alarms and PTO events integrated into Plaspy alerts.
- Analog sensor inputs relayed to Plaspy for fuel level monitoring and consumption reporting.
- Remote relay and immobilizer control executed via Plaspy commands to support anti theft and access workflows.
- Bluetooth sensor telemetry such as temperature and humidity delivered to Plaspy for cold chain and condition monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for live vehicle tracking, route replay and driver behavior oversight.
- Anti theft and immobilization workflows using ignition detection and remote relay control.
- Fuel monitoring and consumption analytics through analog inputs and event reporting.
- Cold chain logistics and refrigerated transport using BLE sensor telemetry for environmental visibility.
- Mixed fleet deployments where wide voltage range and robust I O are required across vehicle types.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TLW2-12BL combines frequent position updates, resilient cellular coverage and broad sensor support in a form factor suited to vehicle use. Its compatibility with Plaspy enables operators to centralize vehicle location, safety events and environmental telemetry in a single platform, simplifying operational oversight and incident response.
