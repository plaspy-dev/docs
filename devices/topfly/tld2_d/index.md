# TopFly - TLD2-D

![TLD2-D](./tracker.png)

The TLD2-D \(4G LTE\) is a plug-and-play OBDII GPS tracker engineered for fast deployment in fleets, company cars and rental vehicles. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this unit delivers high-frequency, real-time tracking and rich CAN BUS telemetry without installation — simply plug into the vehicle OBDII port to unlock VIN, odometer, fuel level, RPM and DTC data alongside precise GNSS location.

Designed for fleet management and vehicle monitoring programs that require immediate telemetry, the TLD2-D pairs with Bluetooth Low Energy sensors for temperature, humidity and door monitoring, and uses an internal 6-axis accelerometer plus a driver buzzer to detect harsh events and warn drivers. With LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT connectivity, GSM/EDGE fallback and multiple data transports \(TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS\), this GPS tracker integrates seamlessly with Plaspy for scalable telematics, anti-theft alerts and operational visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBDII installation for rapid deployment across fleets — no hard wiring required.
- Real-time tracking with update intervals as frequent as every 3 seconds for tight location accuracy.
- Deep CAN BUS telemetry \(VIN, odometer, fuel level, RPM, DTC\) to support fuel monitoring and vehicle diagnostics.
- BLE 5.0 support for Bluetooth sensors \(temperature, humidity, door sensors\) to extend environmental monitoring.
- 6-axis accelerometer with harsh acceleration/braking, sharp turn and crash detection plus internal buzzer for driver warnings.
- Cellular resilience: LTE FDD Cat M1 and NB‑IoT with GSM/EDGE fallback and data transports including MQTT for reliable telemetry delivery to Plaspy.
- Built-in 200 mAh backup battery and network jamming detection to aid anti-theft response and disconnection alerts.
- FOTA support, diagnostic LEDs and broad temperature range for dependable fleet operation in varied environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TLD2-D streams GPS position, CAN BUS telemetry and sensor inputs directly into the Plaspy platform using standard transports \(TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS\). Plaspy ingests the device’s data for live mapping, geofencing, alerts and historical reporting. Scheduled reporting and configurable update intervals let fleet managers balance data freshness and network usage. BLE sensor data and driving events are delivered alongside location to enrich safety and cargo monitoring workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(configurable intervals, down to ~3 seconds\).
- Vehicle CAN BUS data: VIN, odometer, fuel level, RPM and diagnostic trouble codes \(DTC\).
- Ignition on/off alerts and disconnection notifications via the backup battery for anti-theft response.
- Crash detection and driving behavior events \(harsh braking/acceleration, sharp turns\) with driver buzzer warnings and crash data collection for analysis.
- Bluetooth sensors: temperature, humidity and door status delivered to Plaspy when paired \(TOPFLYtech accessories supported\).

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: track vehicle location, mileage, RPM and fuel level to optimize routes, maintenance and fuel monitoring.
- Anti-theft and security: receive disconnection alerts, network jamming detection and ignition status to accelerate recovery and response.
- Driver safety and coaching: monitor harsh acceleration, braking and turning events with buzzer warnings and event reporting for behavior coaching.
- Temperature-sensitive cargo: combine BLE temperature & humidity sensors \(e.g., TSTH1-B\) with GPS data to audit environmental conditions during transport.
- Door and access monitoring: use BLE door sensors \(e.g., TSDT1-B\) to detect unauthorized door openings and trigger alerts in Plaspy.

For operators seeking richer telemetry, faster location updates and straightforward integration into Plaspy dashboards and alerting workflows, the TLD2-D is a dependable choice backed by industry certifications and accessory compatibility for extended control and monitoring.
