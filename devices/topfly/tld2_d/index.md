# TopFly - TLD2-D

<div className="device-logo">
![TLD2-D](./tracker.png)
</div>

The TopFly TLD2-D is a 4G LTE plug-and-play OBDII GPS tracker designed for fast deployment in fleets, company cars and rental vehicles. It delivers high frequency real-time tracking along with CAN BUS telemetry such as VIN, odometer, fuel level, RPM and diagnostic trouble codes, all accessible without hard wiring by plugging into the vehicle OBDII port. The unit also supports Bluetooth sensor pairing, an internal accelerometer with driver buzzer for harsh event detection, and on device features to support anti-theft monitoring.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the TLD2-D can stream location, vehicle telemetry and sensor inputs directly into Plaspy for live mapping, alerts and reporting. That integration makes it a practical choice for operators who need immediate visibility into vehicle location and condition, and who want to centralize events, geofences and historical data within the Plaspy fleet management environment.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBDII form factor for rapid deployment across large fleets with no hard wiring.
- High frequency real time tracking with configurable update intervals down to approximately 3 seconds.
- Deep CAN BUS telemetry including VIN, odometer, fuel level, RPM and DTC for vehicle diagnostics and fleet reporting.
- BLE sensor support for temperature, humidity and door monitoring to extend cargo and access visibility.
- Built in accelerometer and internal buzzer for harsh event detection and driver warnings to support safety programs.
- Network resilience, remote management with firmware update support, and backup battery plus jamming detection for better anti theft awareness.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the TLD2-D streams GPS position, CAN BUS telemetry and paired sensor inputs into the platform so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy ingests events and measurements to drive mapping, alerts, scheduled reporting and operational dashboards.

- Real time location and telemetry updates with configurable intervals for fine grained visibility.
- Vehicle CAN BUS data such as VIN, odometer, fuel level, RPM and diagnostic trouble codes available inside Plaspy.
- Ignition and disconnection notifications plus backup battery alerts to support anti theft workflows.
- Driving behavior and crash related events including harsh braking, acceleration and sharp turn detections for safety analysis.
- BLE sensor data for temperature, humidity and door status delivered alongside location to support cargo monitoring and access control.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operations tracking to optimize routing, maintenance scheduling and fuel management.
- Rental car monitoring to capture mileage, diagnostics and location for asset oversight.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows that rely on disconnection alerts, jamming detection and ignition status.
- Driver safety programs that use harsh event reporting and buzzer warnings for coaching and incident review.
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring by pairing BLE environmental sensors with GPS tracking.
