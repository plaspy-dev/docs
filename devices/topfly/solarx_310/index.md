# SolarX 310

The SolarX 310 is a rugged, solar-powered GPS tracker designed for long-term outdoor deployments and is Plaspy compatible out of the box. Built for containers, trailers, trucks and high-value mobile assets, the unit combines real-time tracking with robust buffering and environmental resistance so your fleet management and asset-protection workflows stay live even in remote areas.

The SolarX 310 delivers configurable real-time tracking \(updates as frequent as 3 seconds\), multi-constellation GNSS accuracy under 2 m CEP, and BLE 5.3 support for temperature and door sensors. With integrated solar charging, a 2500 mAh Li‑Polymer battery and IP67 protection, it’s optimized for telemetry, anti-theft alerts, and long-duration deployments that integrate seamlessly into Plaspy for reporting, alerts and historical analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with real-time tracking updates as fast as 3 seconds for responsive fleet management and asset visibility.
- Solar-assisted power: 2500 mAh Li‑Polymer battery plus integrated solar panel to extend operational life for months under typical reporting schedules.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and thick casing for outdoor use on containers, trailers, trucks and other exposed assets.
- Large offline buffer: stores up to 60,000 location points when outside cellular coverage and forwards when connectivity restores.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou/Galileo/QZSS\) with horizontal accuracy under 2 m CEP and fast TTFF performance.
- BLE 5.3 for Bluetooth sensors — supports TOPFLYtech accessories \(T‑Sense, TSTH1‑B\) for temperature, humidity and door monitoring.
- Removal and tamper detection via integrated hall switch, plus configurable geo-fences and 31 alarm types for comprehensive anti-theft telemetry.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrated with Plaspy, the SolarX 310 provides continuous position and telemetry feeds for dashboards, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the device’s TCP/UDP/MQTT or SMS messages and uses the buffered location points to preserve continuity if a tracker goes temporarily offline. Device events — such as removal alerts, temperature thresholds and geofence breaches — are forwarded to Plaspy in real time for instant notification and downstream automation.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(configurable intervals, real-time as frequent as 3 seconds\).
- Buffered historical playback: up to 60,000 stored points for reliable track reconstruction after connectivity loss.
- Removal/tamper alerts from the built-in hall switch for anti-theft workflows and immediate notifications in Plaspy.
- Built-in temperature sensor and Bluetooth sensors support \(T‑Sense, TSTH1‑B\) for environmental telemetry and door/open status reporting.
- Communications via TCP, UDP, MQTT or SMS to integrate with Plaspy’s ingestion endpoints; secure options include MD5 and AES128.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management for trailers and trucks — continuous GPS tracker reporting for route visibility and operational telemetry.
- Container and asset tracking — solar power and IP67 durability keep devices online on remote containers and freight.
- Anti-theft and removal detection — hall switch and instant Plaspy alerts for unauthorized removal or tampering.
