# TopFly - SolarX 310

![SolarX 310](./tracker.png)

The SolarX 310 is a rugged, solar powered GPS tracker designed for long term outdoor deployments. Built for containers, trailers, trucks and other high value mobile assets, it combines real time tracking, a large offline buffer and environmental resistance to keep location and telemetry alive even in remote areas. Its design centers on long duration autonomy and durable protection for exposed installations.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the SolarX 310 feeds position and event data directly into Plaspy for dashboards, alerts and historical reporting. That native compatibility makes the model relevant for teams that need continuous visibility, anti theft notifications and sensor aware telemetry inside a fleet management platform like Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible with configurable real time updates for responsive fleet visibility and asset tracking.
- Solar assisted power with an integrated panel and an internal battery to extend operational life between maintenance intervals.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and reinforced casing for outdoor use on containers, trailers and trucks.
- Large offline buffer storing tens of thousands of points to preserve tracks when cellular coverage is unavailable.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning with high horizontal accuracy to improve location precision for asset workflows.
- Bluetooth sensor support for environmental monitoring and door or status sensors to combine telemetry with location.
- Tamper and removal detection plus configurable geofences and alarm types for anti theft and security monitoring.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SolarX 310 delivers continuous position and event feeds that power live dashboards, alerting rules and historical playback. Plaspy ingests the device messages and uses buffered location points to preserve continuity if a tracker goes temporarily offline, then surfaces events and sensor readings for immediate action.

- Real time location and telemetry updates available to Plaspy dashboards for live monitoring and operational oversight.
- Buffered historical playback from stored points to reconstruct routes and timelines after connectivity interruptions.
- Instant alerts in Plaspy for removal, tamper or geofence breaches to support anti theft workflows.
- Environmental and status telemetry from onboard and Bluetooth sensors forwarded into Plaspy for condition based alerts.
- Reporting and analytics in Plaspy using device events and historical data to support service optimization and compliance reviews.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trailers and trucks requiring continuous GPS tracking and operational visibility.
- Container and freight asset tracking where solar power and rugged housing reduce maintenance needs.
- Anti theft and removal detection workflows using tamper alerts and geofence notifications.
- Environmental telemetry for sensitive cargo using sensor data paired with location context.
- Long duration asset monitoring that relies on buffered logging and extended deployment life.
