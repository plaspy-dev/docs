# TopFly - SolarX 110

![SolarX 110](./tracker.png)

The SolarX 110 is a durable, solar powered GPS tracker built for long term outdoor asset monitoring. Designed for containers, trailers, trucks and other high value assets, it combines rugged IP67 protection with solar harvesting and an internal rechargeable battery to extend deployment life in remote and exposed environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SolarX 110 delivers persistent location visibility and telemetry to the Plaspy fleet management platform. Its field oriented feature set, including multi constellation GNSS and accessory sensor support, makes it suitable for operations that need reliable tracking, anti theft alerts and historical route analysis inside Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for outdoor solar powered deployments
- Real time tracking with reporting intervals down to 3 seconds for precise route visibility
- Solar harvesting combined with a rechargeable 2500 mAh battery for extended field uptime
- Multi constellation GNSS with sub 1.5 meter autonomous horizontal accuracy for improved position reliability
- LTE Cat 1 connectivity with 2G fallback to support broad area fleet coverage
- BLE 5.3 support for accessory sensors and IP67 rated enclosure with anti tamper features
- Large offline buffer of up to 60,000 location points to preserve historical data during connectivity gaps

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SolarX 110 transmits positions, telemetry and accessory sensor data to Plaspy using common transport methods, allowing Plaspy to visualize live location, trigger alerts and archive historical tracks for reporting and analysis.

- Real time location updates configurable to short reporting intervals for live asset monitoring and route replay
- Buffered storage that preserves location history when connectivity is lost and synchronizes to Plaspy when the device regains network access
- Removal and tamper alerts integrated into Plaspy alarm rules so operations teams receive timely notifications
- Accessory sensor telemetry gathered over Bluetooth shown alongside GPS data in Plaspy dashboards for combined situational awareness
- Secure transmission and remote device update options help maintain consistent behavior across a deployed fleet

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking where solar power and high accuracy GNSS maintain continuous position history for intermodal logistics
- Anti theft and tamper detection using removal alerts and configurable Plaspy notifications for fast response
- Cold chain adjunct monitoring by pairing external temperature sensors and seeing environmental telemetry together with location in Plaspy
- Fleet asset visibility for non powered equipment such as trailers and yard assets using scheduled reports and buffered history
- Remote site monitoring where long term power autonomy and large onboard storage preserve data until connectivity returns

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SolarX 110 is a practical choice for organizations that need resilient, solar assisted tracking for outdoor assets while integrating with a scalable fleet platform. Its combination of multi constellation GNSS accuracy, extended uptime through solar harvesting, sensor support and substantial offline buffering helps reduce operational blind spots and data loss for asset intensive workflows.

Paired with Plaspy, the SolarX 110 provides operations teams with consolidated location views, configurable alarms and historical analysis to support anti theft, environmental sensing and mixed sensor fleet use cases.
