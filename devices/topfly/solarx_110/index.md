# TopFly - SolarX 110

![SolarX 110](./tracker.png)

The SolarX 110 is a durable, solar-powered GPS tracker designed for long-term outdoor asset monitoring and is fully Plaspy compatible for seamless fleet management and asset visibility. Built for containers, trailers, trucks and other high-value assets, the SolarX 110 combines IP67 ruggedization with an efficient solar harvesting system and a rechargeable 2500 mAh Li‑Polymer battery to deliver extended uptime in remote deployments.

With high-frequency real-time tracking \(reports as fast as every 3 seconds\), multi-constellation GNSS, BLE 5.3 support for accessory sensors, and robust data buffering, the SolarX 110 gives operations teams reliable telemetry, anti-theft alerts, and detailed historical tracks through the Plaspy platform. Its field-oriented feature set and flexible data transport options make it a practical choice for telemetry-driven workflows and mixed-sensor fleet solutions.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for outdoor, solar-powered deployments.
- Real-time tracking with reporting intervals down to 3 seconds for precise route visibility.
- Solar harvesting plus a rechargeable 2500 mAh Li‑Polymer battery for extended field life.
- All‑in‑one GNSS \(GPS, BeiDou, Galileo, QZSS\) with \<1.5 m CEP autonomous horizontal accuracy.
- LTE Cat 1 with GSM 2G fallback across wide global bands to support global fleet management.
- BLE 5.3 for Bluetooth sensors \(temperature, door\) and built-in temperature sensor for cold-chain adjunct monitoring.
- Robust anti‑tamper features: hall switch for removal alerts, LED indicators and IP67 enclosure.
- Large offline buffer \(up to 60,000 location points\) to preserve historical data when out of network coverage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SolarX 110 transmits GNSS positions, telemetry and sensor data to Plaspy using standard transport methods, enabling real-time tracking, configurable alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests location and telemetry via TCP/UDP/MQTT or SMS, and then visualizes routes, triggers geofence alarms and forwards event notifications to operations teams.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(configurable intervals down to 3 seconds\) for live asset visibility and route replay.
- Buffered location storage \(up to 60,000 points\) ensures continuous historical tracking even during network outages; Plaspy syncs when connectivity resumes.
- Removal/anti-theft alerts via hall switch and tamper notifications integrated into Plaspy alarm rules.
- BLE 5.3 support connects external sensors \(temperature, door\) — Plaspy displays accessory sensor telemetry alongside GPS data.
- Secure transmission options \(MD5/AES128\) and FOTA-capable firmware updates to keep device behavior consistent with Plaspy integrations.

## Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking — long-term solar operation and high-accuracy GNSS give continuous position history for intermodal logistics.
- Anti-theft and tamper detection — hall switch removal alerts and configurable Plaspy alarms let fleets respond quickly to unauthorized moves.
- Cold-chain adjunct monitoring — BLE temperature sensors paired to the SolarX 110 feed Plaspy with environmental telemetry alongside location.
- Fleet asset visibility — track non-powered assets \(trailers, equipment\) with scheduled reports and buffered history for operational planning.
- Remote sites and out-of-coverage scenarios — large on‑board buffer and robust power management preserve data until connectivity returns.
