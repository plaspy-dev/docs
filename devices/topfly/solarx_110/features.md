# TopFly - SolarX 110 Features

This page covers the public feature context for using the TopFly SolarX 110 with the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the SolarX 110 capabilities that matter for fleet managers and asset teams when pairing the device with Plaspy for location visibility, sensor telemetry, and operational monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and manufacturer configuration. For device specific installation steps and the most current technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and firmware notes from TopFly. Plaspy compatibility described here reflects typical integrations and reporting behavior rather than device specific guarantees.

## Feature Overview

The SolarX 110 is a rugged, solar powered tracker intended for long term outdoor asset monitoring with built in features that support high frequency reporting, sensor accessories, and robust offline data retention. Its design focuses on reliable position reporting and environmental accessory telemetry for assets such as trailers, containers, and other high value equipment.

- Solar assisted power system with a rechargeable Li polymer battery to extend field uptime for remote deployments.
- High frequency real time tracking capable of reporting as fast as every 3 seconds to support detailed route visibility.
- Multi constellation GNSS for improved position accuracy and consistent location fixes in challenging environments.
- BLE 5.3 support for accessory sensors so environmental and door status telemetry can be paired with location.
- Large onboard buffer to store tens of thousands of location points while out of network coverage for later synchronization.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and anti tamper hall switch to support outdoor mounting and removal alerts.

## Core Features of TopFly - SolarX 110

- Solar powered operation with an integrated rechargeable 2500 mAh Li polymer battery for extended deployments.
- Real time reporting with configurable intervals down to 3 seconds for precise tracking and route replay.
- Multi constellation GNSS including GPS, BeiDou, Galileo and QZSS for improved horizontal accuracy.
- BLE 5.3 connectivity to attach external sensors such as temperature and door sensors for combined telemetry.
- Large offline buffer capable of preserving an extended history of location points when cellular connectivity is unavailable.
- IP67 rated enclosure with hall switch based removal detection and LED status indicators for quick site checks.
- Cellular connectivity with LTE Cat 1 and GSM fallback to provide global coverage options and reliable data transport.
- Remote management features including firmware over the air updates and configurable encryption options for data transmission.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location, telemetry and sensor events transmitted by the SolarX 110 and presents them in a unified fleet view for monitoring, alerts and historical analysis. The platform is designed to accept common transport methods and automatically detect compatible tracker protocols so teams can focus on operations rather than protocol setup.

- Live location updates and high frequency telemetry appear in Plaspy for route visibility, live tracking and replay.
- Buffered data stored on the device is synced to Plaspy once connectivity resumes, preserving historical continuity.
- Removal and tamper alerts triggered by the hall switch are visible as events in Plaspy and can drive alarm rules and notifications.
- BLE accessory sensor readings such as temperature or door status are shown alongside GPS positions for combined situational awareness.
- Remote firmware updates and telemetry configuration can be coordinated so device behavior aligns with Plaspy reporting and alarm requirements.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term container and trailer tracking where solar power and large buffers reduce maintenance and data loss.
- Anti theft monitoring for removable assets using the hall switch and tamper event visibility in Plaspy.
- Cold chain adjunct monitoring by pairing BLE temperature sensors and viewing environmental telemetry with location.
- Asset visibility for non powered equipment such as mobile machinery and parked trailers with scheduled reporting.
- Remote site tracking where intermittent cellular coverage requires robust local storage and later syncing.
- Fleet route analysis and historical playback for operational planning and incident investigation.
