# TopFly - SolarX 130 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the TopFly SolarX 130 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for tracking, monitoring, and event reporting. It summarizes practical device functions that Plaspy can consume for location intelligence, buffered telemetry, and sensor-backed condition monitoring for containers, trailers, and outdoor assets.

Exact behavior and feature availability depend on firmware, hardware revision, installation details, and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, configuration, sensor use, or accessory compatibility can vary by build or regional variant, so consult manufacturer documentation for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The SolarX 130 is a purpose built Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for outdoor and maritime asset visibility. It emphasizes long field life, resilient GNSS performance, and the ability to retain and upload history after connectivity interruptions, while also supporting wireless sensor integration for condition monitoring.

- Solar assisted power with rechargeable Li‑Polymer battery options for extended field deployments and reduced maintenance visits.
- Rugged IP67 rated housing and multiple mounting options for secure attachment to containers, trailers, and outdoor equipment.
- High frequency location reporting and a large offline buffer to preserve route history when cellular connectivity is intermittent.
- Native Bluetooth sensor support for temperature, humidity, and door status reporting to help monitor cargo condition.
- Motion and removal detection plus configurable alarms to support anti theft monitoring and operational alerts.

## Core Features of TopFly - SolarX 130

- Solar charging combined with Li‑Polymer battery options (PN01 15000 mAh and PN02 7500 mAh) to extend time between service cycles.
- Plaspy compatible telemetry with support for common transport protocols and the ability to report real time location and events.
- High frequency position updates configurable down to every 3 seconds for detailed route and movement visibility.
- Large offline buffer capable of storing up to 49,000 GPS points and uploading stored data when connectivity returns.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and multiple mounting methods including magnets, screws, cable ties, and heavy duty tape.
- Native BLE 5.3 support for external sensors such as temperature, humidity, and door status accessories.
- Motion detection via a 3 axis accelerometer and removal detection using an ambient light sensor for tamper awareness.
- Multi constellation GNSS receiver designed for reliable positioning in challenging environments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the SolarX 130 telemetry so fleets and asset managers can visualize location, review event history, and configure automated alerts. The combination of buffered storage, high frequency updates, and BLE sensor data makes the device useful for continuous monitoring and post transit verification inside Plaspy.

- Real time location and movement events appear in Plaspy dashboards and map views for operational oversight.
- Offline buffered GPS points are uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity resumes, preserving complete route history.
- Movement, removal, and low battery alarms sent by the device are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and incident workflows.
- BLE sensor readings for temperature, humidity, and door status are reported to Plaspy to support condition monitoring and compliance tracking.
- Device status indicators such as battery or GNSS health can be reflected in Plaspy to help prioritize maintenance or investigations.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking across ports and long haul routes where intermittent connectivity is expected.
- Cold chain monitoring with BLE temperature and humidity sensors reporting condition to Plaspy.
- Anti theft and tamper detection using movement and removal alarms to trigger timely alerts.
- Outdoor fleet management in harsh or maritime environments where rugged hardware and solar charging reduce service visits.
- Remote asset monitoring where long offline history is critical to reconstruct routes and verify presence.
- Situations requiring detailed route analysis provided by high frequency reporting and multi constellation GNSS accuracy.
