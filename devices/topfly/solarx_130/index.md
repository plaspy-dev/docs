# SolarX 130

SolarX 130 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker purpose-built for container, trailer and outdoor asset visibility. Rugged and IP67-rated, SolarX 130 combines solar charging, long-life Li‑Polymer batteries and Bluetooth sensor support to deliver reliable real-time tracking and buffered telemetry for long deployments in maritime and harsh-environment use.

Designed for fleet management and anti-theft monitoring, the SolarX 130 provides high-frequency real-time tracking, removal and movement alarms, and integration with Bluetooth sensors for temperature, humidity and door-status monitoring. Its large offline buffer and proven GNSS performance make it an ideal GPS tracker to pair with Plaspy for continuous location intelligence and operational alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Solar-powered design with rechargeable Li‑Polymer batteries \(PN01 15000 mAh; PN02 7500 mAh\) extends field life and reduces maintenance.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and telemetry, with support for TCP, UDP, MQTT and SMS transport protocols.
- High-frequency location updates — configurable as fast as every 3 seconds — for precise movement and route visibility.
- Large offline buffer stores up to 49,000 GPS points when connectivity is unavailable, ensuring no data loss during transit or remote storage.
- Rugged IP67-rated housing and thick casing with multiple mounting options \(magnets, screws, cable ties, heavy-duty tape\) for outdoor and maritime deployments.
- Native BLE 5.3 support for external temperature, humidity and door sensors; ideal for cold-chain and condition monitoring.
- Built-in motion detection \(3-axis accelerometer\), removal detection via light sensor, and configurable low-battery alerts for anti-theft and asset protection.

## How It Works with Plaspy

SolarX 130 integrates with Plaspy to deliver continuous GPS tracker telemetry, alerting and reporting. Location, movement events and sensor readings are transmitted to Plaspy using standard network protocols and can be visualized, archived and used to trigger automated alerts or workflows in your Plaspy instance.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(as fast as every 3 seconds\) via TCP/UDP/MQTT or SMS to Plaspy servers.
- Buffered offline location storage \(up to 49,000 points\) uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity resumes, preserving route history.
- Movement, removal and parking alarms forwarded to Plaspy for immediate anti-theft notifications and incident tracking.
- Bluetooth sensors: temperature, humidity and door-status data from BLE accessories are reported to Plaspy for cold-chain and access monitoring.
- Integration with wireless relays and external I/O hubs allows Plaspy to receive or control ignition or immobilizer signals where those accessories are present.

## Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking across ports and long-haul routes — continuous GPS tracker data with buffered upload for intermittent connectivity.
- Cold‑chain monitoring using Bluetooth sensors for temperature and humidity, with readings reported to Plaspy for compliance and alerts.
- Anti-theft and asset protection — movement, removal and low-battery alarms routed to Plaspy for rapid response.
- Outdoor fleet management in harsh or maritime environments where rugged, solar-powered telemetry reduces maintenance visits.
- Remote asset monitoring where long offline history and GNSS accuracy are critical to reconstruct routes and verify presence.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

When you need a GPS tracker that stands up to the elements and delivers reliable, high-frequency location and telemetry, SolarX 130 is a compelling Plaspy compatible option. Its solar-assisted power system and large battery variants minimize downtime and service visits, while BLE 5.3 support enables temperature, humidity and door-status monitoring for cold-chain and asset condition tracking. The device’s extensive GNSS support and under-2 m CEP accuracy provide precise positioning for detailed route analysis.

For fleet management and anti-theft applications, SolarX 130 supplies timely movement and removal alerts and a durable form factor that mounts securely to containers, trailers and equipment. Plaspy can ingest the device’s TCP/UDP/MQTT or SMS telemetry, present real-time tracking dashboards, and trigger workflows on critical events. Where ignition control or an immobilizer is required, SolarX 130’s compatibility with external I/O hubs and wireless relays enables Plaspy to integrate control or input signals without assuming those functions are built into the tracker itself. Overall, SolarX 130 delivers the rugged telemetry, Bluetooth sensors and buffered tracking that make it easy to scale Plaspy-based tracking solutions for outdoor and maritime asset fleets.
