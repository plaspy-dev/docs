# TopFly - CyberLabelX 100 Features

This page provides a public, non sensitive feature overview for the TopFly CyberLabelX 100 and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for tracking, telemetry, and operational visibility. It describes the practical functions you can expect when using this slim rechargeable parcel tracker with Plaspy, without replacing manufacturer documentation.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, this page highlights optional hardware or configurable features and encourages verification of device specifics against TopFly documentation and firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The CyberLabelX 100 is designed for parcel level and high value shipment visibility. It delivers precise GNSS positioning, configurable reporting intervals, buffered logging for intermittent connectivity, and integrated environmental and tamper awareness in a compact, adhesive mountable form factor.

- Multi constellation GNSS positioning for precise parcel and container location awareness.
- Configurable reporting intervals down to every 3 seconds for high frequency tracking when required.
- Buffered logging to retain location history during cellular outages and upload when connectivity returns.
- Internal ambient light, motion, and temperature sensors for tamper detection and environmental monitoring.
- Optional external temperature or temperature and humidity probe for cold chain monitoring.
- Slim rechargeable design with wireless charging and simple adhesive or zip tie installation for discreet attachment.

## Core Features of TopFly - CyberLabelX 100

- Plaspy compatibility out of the box for cloud integration and real-time visibility.
- Multi constellation GNSS support for accurate positioning of parcels and assets.
- Configurable reporting intervals with high frequency reporting capability.
- Buffered logging to maintain continuous location history when network coverage is intermittent.
- Internal ambient light sensor and motion sensor to support tamper and handling alerts.
- Internal temperature sensor plus support for an optional external temperature or temperature and humidity probe.
- Rechargeable battery and wireless charging for repeated deployment on non powered assets.
- Compact, lightweight enclosure with adhesive and zip tie mounting options for parcel and carton attachment.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and surface the CyberLabelX 100 location fixes, telemetry, and event alerts so logistics teams and asset managers can monitor shipments and respond to incidents. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and maps incoming events into dashboards, reporting, and historical playback.

- Real time location updates and historical playback inside Plaspy for shipment tracking and audit trails.
- Alarm and event visibility for tamper, motion, and temperature thresholds sent from the device to Plaspy.
- Buffered data reconciliation so points stored during an outage are uploaded and visible in Plaspy when the tracker reconnects.
- Configurable reporting from the device reflected as update frequency and point density in Plaspy views.
- Environmental telemetry from internal and optional external probes available as time series data for compliance and alerts.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parcel and carton tracking for last mile delivery visibility and proof of movement.
- High value asset transit monitoring where discreet, rechargeable trackers reduce loss risk.
- Cold chain shipment monitoring using the optional external temperature or temperature and humidity probe.
- Returnable asset tracking for pallets, crates, and roll cages with low profile mounting.
- Inventory reconciliation and shipment condition audits that combine location and temperature history.
