# TopFly - CyberLabelX 100

![CyberLabelX 100](./tracker.png)

The CyberLabelX 100 is a slim, rechargeable asset GPS tracker built for parcel-level and high-value shipment visibility. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this compact tracker combines multi-constellation GNSS positioning, configurable reporting intervals down to every 3 seconds, and buffered logging to ensure continuous location history even when cellular coverage is intermittent.

Designed for logistics teams and asset managers who need reliable real-time tracking and environmental telemetry, the CyberLabelX 100 adds tamper and temperature awareness through an internal light sensor, motion sensor and temperature sensor, plus support for an optional external temperature or temperature-and-humidity probe for cold‑chain shipments. Its thin profile, wireless charging and simple 3M adhesive or zip‑tie installation make it ideal for cartons, parcels and returnable asset flows where discreet, long‑life tracking is required.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and cloud integration via TCP, UDP, MQTT or SMS transport.
- Multi‑constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou/Galileo/QZSS\) with autonomous horizontal accuracy &lt; 1.5 m CEP for precise parcel and container positioning.
- Long‑life rechargeable 1900 mAh battery with wireless charging and multi‑day operational profiles \(examples: 57 days at 1‑hour reporting; 194 days at 4‑hour reporting\).
- Buffered logging stores location points during network outages to avoid data loss and syncs when coverage resumes.
- Environmental sensing: internal temperature sensor plus optional external temperature or temp & humidity probe for cold‑chain visibility.
- Tamper and security alerts via ambient light and motion sensors, plus up to 25 customizable alarm types for anti‑theft and exposure notifications.
- Compact, lightweight form factor with IP54 rating and easy mounting using 3M adhesive or cable zip tie — purpose‑built for parcel and carton attachment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the CyberLabelX 100 with Plaspy enables real‑time tracking, event alerts and historical playback within your Plaspy dashboards and APIs. The device communicates over standard transports supported by Plaspy \(TCP, UDP, MQTT, SMS\), delivering location fixes, telemetry and alarm events. When signal is lost, buffered points are retained on the device and automatically uploaded when connectivity returns, preserving continuity for logistics reporting.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(configurable reporting down to every 3 seconds\).
- Tamper and visibility alerts via ambient light sensor \(useful for anti-theft notifications\).
- Motion detection events for shipment handling and unauthorized movement.
- Temperature monitoring: internal sensor plus support for an optional external temperature or temperature-and-humidity probe for cold‑chain compliance.
- Buffered logging to prevent data loss during out‑of‑coverage periods; automatic upload to Plaspy when online.

## Use Cases

- Parcel and carton tracking for last‑mile visibility and delivery confirmation in e‑commerce operations.
- High‑value asset transit monitoring where discreet, rechargeable GPS trackers reduce theft and loss risk.
- Cold‑chain shipments using the optional external temperature or temperature & humidity probe to monitor compliance during transit.
- Returnable asset visibility for pallets, crates and roll cages where a slim, adhesive mount is required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

CyberLabelX 100 delivers focused parcel‑level telemetry and robust location fidelity in a package designed to work smoothly with Plaspy. Its multi‑constellation GNSS, reliable LTE/GSM connectivity and buffered logging give logistics teams accurate, continuous insight into shipments and high‑value assets. Built‑in tamper \(light\) and motion detection provide practical anti‑theft alerts, while the optional external probe extends capability into cold‑chain use cases.

For organizations using Plaspy for fleet management, the CyberLabelX 100 is an excellent complement when you need compact, long‑life GPS trackers for non‑powered assets and shipments. Note that vehicle‑specific telematics features such as ignition inputs, immobilizer control or on‑board fuel monitoring are not part of this device profile; if those capabilities are required, combine CyberLabelX 100 parcel tracking with vehicle telematics modules or other sensors in your Plaspy deployment. Similarly, while this model focuses on integrated environmental sensors rather than Bluetooth sensors, Plaspy can correlate data from Bluetooth devices and gateways in multi‑sensor solutions to provide a complete telemetry picture.
