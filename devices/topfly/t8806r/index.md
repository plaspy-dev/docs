# TopFly - T8806+R

![T8806+R](./logo..png)

The TopFly T8806+R \(2G\) is a highly advanced hardwired tracker designed for tracking powered assets and vehicles. It comes equipped with a range of features that make it a versatile and reliable tracking solution. With ignition detection and output for relay/buzzer/siren, this tracker offers enhanced security and control. 

One of the standout features of the T8806+R is its real-time and buffer location capabilities. It can provide real-time location updates as frequent as every 3 seconds, ensuring that you have up-to-date information on the whereabouts of your assets or vehicles. Additionally, it has a buffer that can store up to 60,000 location points in case of network coverage issues.

The T8806+R also features BLE 4.0 technology, which allows it to be compatible with TOPFLYtech's range of BLE sensors. This includes temperature and humidity sensors, door sensors, wireless relays, and more. This opens up a world of possibilities for monitoring and controlling various aspects of your assets or vehicles.

In terms of safety and security, the T8806+R offers a range of alerts and detection features. It can detect reckless driving events such as harsh acceleration, turning, braking, crashes, and speeding. It also provides alerts for device disconnection from power and ignition on/off events. Additionally, it has a backup battery that ensures disconnection alerts can still be sent even when the power is disconnected.

With its analog input and RS232 input, the T8806+R is compatible with TOPFLYtech's ultrasonic fuel sensor, allowing for accurate fuel monitoring. This can be particularly useful for fleet management and monitoring fuel consumption.

In terms of technical specifications, the T8806+R operates on a 2G quad-band frequency and is compliant with GSM phase 2/2+. It has a built-in GNSS chipset for accurate positioning and supports various GNSS systems including GPS, Glonass, Galileo, and Beidou. The tracker also features multiple digital inputs and outputs, as well as an RS232 connector for additional connectivity options.

With its compact dimensions and wide operating temperature range, the T8806+R is suitable for use in various environments and applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, equipment, or other assets, this tracker offers the features and reliability you need.
