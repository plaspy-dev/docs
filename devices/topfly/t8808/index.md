# TopFly - T8808

![T8808](./logo.jpg)

The TopFly T8808 is a compact GPS tracker designed to provide reliable real time monitoring and location tracking for vehicles and mobile assets. The device emphasizes low energy consumption and reduced GPRS data use so it can operate for extended periods without imposing heavy load on a vehicle electrical system. Built with internal GSM and GPS antennas and a simple SIM card insertion method, the T8808 aims to deliver straightforward installation and dependable positioning for both personal and commercial use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T8808 can be integrated into a fleet management environment to provide continuous visibility and event reporting within the Plaspy platform. That compatibility makes it a practical choice for organizations that need to combine a proven hardware tracker with Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and reporting tools to centralize monitoring and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location monitoring for continuous visibility of vehicles and assets
- Low energy use and reduced GPRS consumption to support longer operational periods
- Smart power on and off control to manage device activity and conserve power
- Internal GSM and GPS antennas for reliable signal reception in typical vehicle environments
- Easy SIM card insertion for quick activation and deployment
- Built in anti theft features such as immobilization and overspeed alerts
- Geofence and vibration alarms to detect unauthorized movement or tampering

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TopFly T8808 provides location and event data that Plaspy ingests for mapping, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy acts as the operational layer that organizes the tracker data into actionable insights for fleet managers and operators.

- See live vehicle positions on Plaspy maps and follow movement in real time
- Receive configurable alerts for geofence entry or exit, overspeed events, vibration alarms, and other tracker notifications
- Review trip histories and playback routes to analyze past movements and operational patterns
- Consolidate immobilization events and theft related alerts for centralized incident handling
- Generate summaries and reports in Plaspy to support compliance, billing, or performance reviews

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet monitoring for light commercial vehicles and service fleets
- Vehicle security and anti theft tracking for personal cars or company vehicles
- Delivery and logistics oversight to verify routes and stops
- Asset protection during transport with geofence and vibration alerts
- Operational visibility for small to medium sized fleets requiring simple hardware integration
