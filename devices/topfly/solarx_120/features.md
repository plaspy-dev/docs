# TopFly - SolarX 120 Features

This page provides a public, feature focused summary of the TopFly SolarX 120 and how its capabilities map to the Plaspy platform. It is intended to help fleet managers, asset operators, and technical evaluators understand the practical functions you can expect when using this heavy duty, solar assisted tracker with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional variant, and manufacturer implementation. Refer to manufacturer documentation and release notes for the most current device specific details when planning deployments or integrating with Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The SolarX 120 is designed for long term outdoor asset monitoring where durability, extended unattended operation, and environmental sensor support are important. With solar assisted charging, rugged IP67 protection, multi constellation GNSS, cellular connectivity, and BLE sensor support, the device is optimized for containers, trailers, trucks, and other heavy assets.

- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure for outdoor deployments and heavy asset mounting.
- Solar assisted charging plus a 7,500 mAh rechargeable Li Polymer battery for extended field life.
- Configurable reporting intervals down to 3 seconds for high frequency location updates.
- BLE 5.3 support for external sensors including TOPFLYtech TSTH1-B and TSDT1-B for temperature humidity and door status telemetry.
- Movement, parking, and removal alerts driven by the internal accelerometer and light sensor.
- Buffered storage of location points for later upload to preserve historical tracks when coverage is intermittent.

## Core Features of TopFly - SolarX 120

- IP67 rugged enclosure and multiple mounting options for magnets, screws, tape, cable ties, or polyurethane mounting.
- Solar assisted charging with an internal 7,500 mAh Li Polymer battery for long unattended operation.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning for reliable location fixes in outdoor environments.
- Cellular connectivity across LTE and 2G networks with support for common transport methods for telemetry.
- BLE 5.3 radio for pairing and collecting telemetry from compatible sensors such as TSTH1-B and TSDT1-B.
- Internal sensors including a 3 axis accelerometer, light sensor for removal detection, and an internal temperature sensor.
- Local buffered storage up to 49,000 location points to support store and forward workflows.
- Remote management features including configurable reporting intervals and firmware updates over the air.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, SolarX 120 streams location and sensor data into the platform and supports both live and store and forward operation. Plaspy receives telemetry and events from the device so teams can monitor assets, act on alerts, and analyze historical tracks.

- Live location updates and high frequency tracking appear in Plaspy for route visibility and monitoring.
- Buffered data uploads restore historical tracks to Plaspy after connectivity is restored, preserving audit trails.
- BLE sensor telemetry for temperature humidity and door status is integrated into Plaspy dashboards and reports when paired to the device.
- Movement, parking, and removal alerts feed into Plaspy rule engines and notification workflows for fleet and anti theft responses.
- Remote configuration and firmware updates help keep device behavior aligned with Plaspy deployment policies and reporting needs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long haul fleet tracking for trucks and trailers requiring extended unattended operation and detailed trip history.
- Shipping container monitoring where solar charging and rugged sealing enable prolonged deployments in the field.
- Trailer and heavy equipment anti theft workflows using movement and removal alerts to trigger recovery processes.
- Environmental monitoring for payloads using BLE temperature and humidity sensors reported into Plaspy dashboards.
- Remote asset auditing where buffered storage preserves location history during periods of poor cellular coverage.
- Mobile or temporary site assets that need simple mounting and long battery life between service visits.
