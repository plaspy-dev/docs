# TopFly - SolarX 120

<div className="device-logo">
![SolarX 120](./tracker.png)
</div>

The SolarX 120 from TopFly is a heavy duty, solar assisted GPS tracker designed for long term outdoor asset monitoring. It features a rugged IP67 enclosure, a large rechargeable battery and solar charging to extend deployments without frequent maintenance. The device is specified for use on containers, trailers, trucks and other heavy assets where durable weatherproof hardware and reliable location reporting are essential.

As a Plaspy compatible device, SolarX 120 can stream location and sensor telemetry into Plaspy for live visibility, event alerts and historical reporting. Its ability to buffer thousands of points during coverage loss and later upload them makes it suitable for remote or intermittent connectivity scenarios, while sensor support enables environmental and door status data to appear alongside location traces in Plaspy dashboards.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure built for long term outdoor deployments and resistant to harsh conditions.
- Solar assisted charging with a large rechargeable battery to reduce maintenance visits and extend unattended operation.
- Configurable reporting with options for frequent updates down to three second intervals for near real time tracking needs.
- Support for external BLE sensors to capture temperature, humidity and door status alongside location data.
- Movement, parking and removal alerts that support anti theft workflows and operational notifications.
- Buffered storage of historical track points to preserve data when cellular coverage is unavailable.
- Multiple cellular connectivity options to maintain telemetry delivery across different networks.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, SolarX 120 provides continuous location streams and sensor telemetry for monitoring and operational workflows. In normal coverage it transmits frequent GNSS fixes and external sensor readings to Plaspy for immediate use in maps, geofences and rules. If network connectivity is lost, the device stores data locally and forwards it to Plaspy once a connection is restored, ensuring track continuity and auditability.

- Real time location updates and sensor telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for fleet visibility and live monitoring.
- Event and alarm delivery such as movement, parking and removal alerts can trigger Plaspy rules and notifications.
- Buffered data upload preserves historical tracks and syncs to Plaspy for reporting, compliance and post event analysis.
- BLE sensor readings for temperature, humidity and door status are integrated into Plaspy reports and can be combined with location context.
- Data collected by SolarX 120 supports geofencing, trip history analysis and operational dashboards inside Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long haul fleet tracking for trucks and trailers requiring extended unattended operation and reliable location history.
- Shipping container monitoring where solar power and rugged housing support prolonged deployments and tamper detection.
- Trailer and heavy equipment anti theft use cases using movement and removal alerts to prompt recovery workflows.
- Condition monitoring for payloads using paired sensors to report temperature and humidity alongside location.
- Remote asset auditing and compliance where buffered storage ensures no loss of track history when coverage is intermittent.

SolarX 120 is a practical choice for organizations that need resilient hardware for outdoor asset tracking while integrating with a modern fleet management platform like Plaspy. Its combination of solar assisted power, rugged enclosure and extensive buffering makes it suited to long term, low maintenance deployments.
