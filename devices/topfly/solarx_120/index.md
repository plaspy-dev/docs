# TopFly - SolarX 120

![SolarX 120](./tracker.png)

The SolarX 120 from SolarX \(model SolarX 120\) is a heavy-duty, solar-assisted GPS tracker engineered for long-term outdoor asset monitoring. Built to withstand harsh environments with an IP67-rated rugged casing and a 7,500 mAh rechargeable Li-Polymer battery, this Plaspy compatible tracker extends field life with continuous solar charging and configurable reporting intervals down to every 3 seconds for real-time tracking.

Designed for containers, trailers, trucks and other heavy assets, SolarX 120 combines multi‑constellation GNSS positioning, LTE/2G cellular connectivity and BLE 5.3 sensor support to deliver robust telemetry, movement and removal alerts. When integrated with Plaspy, the device provides reliable location streams, buffered reporting when coverage drops, and sensor telemetry for temperature, humidity and door status using TOPFLYtech BLE sensors \(TSTH1-B, TSDT1-B\) or other compatible BLE devices.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for outdoor asset tracking with IP67 waterproofing and a rugged, thick casing.
- Solar-assisted charging plus a 7,500 mAh Li‑Polymer battery for extended unattended operation.
- Real-time tracking with configurable updates as frequent as every 3 seconds and buffered storage up to 49,000 points.
- BLE 5.3 support and compatible with TOPFLYtech sensors \(TSTH1-B, TSDT1-B\) for temperature, humidity and door monitoring.
- Movement, parking and removal alarms \(triggered by internal accelerometer and light sensor\) for anti-theft workflows and fleet management alerts.
- Multi-network cellular connectivity \(LTE FDD/TDD Cat 1 and 2G GSM\) with TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS transport for flexible telemetry delivery to Plaspy.
- Rugged mounting options — magnets, screws, tape, cable ties or polyurethane — for fast installation on heavy assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

SolarX 120 streams location and sensor telemetry to Plaspy using standard transport protocols and can operate in live or store-and-forward modes. In normal coverage it sends frequent GNSS fixes and BLE sensor data to Plaspy for immediate display, geofencing, and rule-based alerts. If coverage is interrupted, the device buffers up to 49,000 points and uploads them when a connection is restored, ensuring no loss of historical track data.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy using TCP, UDP, MQTT or SMS.
- Movement alerts \(start moving and stop moving\) and parking events usable in Plaspy workflows for fleet management and anti-theft detection.
- Removal alerts via the built-in light sensor to notify Plaspy of potential tampering or detachment.
- BLE sensor telemetry \(temperature, humidity, door status\) from compatible TOPFLYtech sensors and other BLE devices, integrated into Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Buffered data storage for later transmission when the device regains network connectivity, preserving historical tracks for audits and analytics.

- Fleet management for long-haul trucks and trailers: continuous GPS tracker telemetry for routing, idle detection and trip history.
- Shipping container monitoring: solar power and rugged housing enable prolonged deployments with removal and tamper alerts.
- Trailer and heavy-equipment anti-theft: movement and removal alarms feed Plaspy rules to trigger alerts and recovery workflows.

- Remote asset auditing: buffered location storage preserves track history when devices operate in low-coverage areas and later syncs to Plaspy for compliance reports.
