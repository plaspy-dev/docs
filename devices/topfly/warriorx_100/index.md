# TopFly - WarriorX 100

<div className="device-logo">
![WarriorX 100](./tracker.png)
</div>

The WarriorX 100 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for long life outdoor asset monitoring. As a standalone battery powered device with a rugged IP67 enclosure and multi year operating life, it is aimed at tracking containers, trailers, heavy equipment, and other high value assets where low maintenance and durability are essential. The unit combines parallel GNSS positioning with reporting modes designed to deliver reliable location and basic telemetry over extended deployments.

Because the WarriorX 100 is built to integrate with fleet platforms, it is relevant as a Plaspy compatible device for organizations that want centralized location, sensor, and alert management. Its flexible reporting strategy, support for remote firmware updates, and event notifications such as removal alerts make it suitable for Plaspy workflows that emphasize operational visibility, anti theft response, and long term asset oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker designed for real time tracking and centralized telemetry ingestion.
- Multi year replaceable battery options to minimize field maintenance and maximize uptime.
- Rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure suitable for outdoor fleet and asset deployments.
- Parallel GNSS positioning for improved location confidence and timely fixes.
- Support for standard transport options and over the air firmware updates for remote device management.
- Built in temperature sensing and removal alerts to support basic cold chain and anti theft workflows.
- Configurable geo fencing and low battery notifications to help automate operational alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the WarriorX 100 streams location fixes, sensor readings, and event notifications so Plaspy can present live maps, history, alerts, and reports. The device's reporting profiles let you balance update frequency and battery life, and remote management features permit firmware updates and configuration changes from the platform.

- Live location and telemetry updates forwarded to Plaspy for dashboard visibility and map playback.
- Removal and tamper alerts delivered to Plaspy to trigger anti theft workflows and operator notifications.
- Temperature readings and compatible sensor data ingested for basic ambient monitoring and reporting.
- Geo fence entry and exit events plus configurable low battery alerts used by Plaspy to automate notifications and reports.
- Support for frequent reporting modes such as SVR for periods that require near real time situational awareness.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term container and trailer monitoring where waterproofing and extended battery life reduce onsite servicing.
- Heavy equipment and off grid assets that need reliable location reporting and removal alerts with minimal maintenance.
- Logistics and high value shipments where temperature sensing and event alerts support loss prevention and cold chain oversight.
- Distributed remote asset portfolios that benefit from geo fencing, automated low battery notifications, and centralized reporting through Plaspy.
- Fleet oversight during intensive operations where configurable reporting windows provide the needed balance of update cadence and battery conservation.

The WarriorX 100 pairs durability and battery longevity with the transport and management features that make integration with Plaspy straightforward. Its design focuses on delivering reliable location and essential telemetry over long deployments, reducing the frequency of field visits while providing the situational awareness fleet managers need.
