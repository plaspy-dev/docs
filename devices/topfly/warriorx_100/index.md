# TopFly - WarriorX 100

![WarriorX 100](./tracker.png)

The WarriorX 100 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for long-life outdoor asset monitoring. This standalone battery-powered tracker pairs rugged IP67 protection with multi-year battery life and high-precision GNSS positioning to deliver reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for containers, trailers, heavy equipment, and other high-value assets. Designed for low-power operation, the WarriorX 100 minimizes maintenance while maximizing uptime for fleet management and anti-theft use cases.

Built for simple integration with Plaspy, the WarriorX 100 supports standard transport protocols and remote firmware updates so fleets and asset managers can centralize location, sensor, and alert data. With parallel GNSS \(GPS + GLONASS + Beidou + Galileo + QZSS\), removal alerts, temperature sensing, and a flexible reporting strategy including an SVR mode for frequent updates, this GPS tracker delivers the telemetry and situational awareness modern asset programs require.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and centralized telemetry ingestion.
- Long-life replaceable non-rechargeable battery \(standard 4000 mAh; optional 8000 mAh\) with multi-year operating life depending on reporting profile.
- Rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure and multiple mounting options \(magnets, screws, adhesive, zip ties\) for outdoor fleet and asset deployments.
- High-accuracy parallel GNSS \(GPS + GLONASS + Beidou + Galileo + QZSS\) with sub-1.5 m CEP autonomous horizontal accuracy and fast TTFF.
- Supports TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS transports and FOTA for secure, scalable telemetry and remote management via Plaspy.
- Built-in temperature sensor and removal alerts to support anti-theft workflows and cold-chain monitoring with compatible BLE sensors.
- Geo-fencing support for up to 64 internal regions and configurable low-battery alarms to reduce operational risk.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the WarriorX 100 streams location and telemetry data using secure transport options \(TCP/UDP/MQTT/SMS\). Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes, sensor readings, and event notifications to power live maps, historical playback, alerts and reporting. The device’s flexible reporting modes allow you to balance battery life against update frequency — from daily low-power reports to SVR \(super-verbose\) mode for near real-time tracking when needed.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via TCP/UDP/MQTT \(and SMS where available\) for fleet management dashboards.
- Removal alerts and tamper events forwarded to Plaspy for anti-theft workflows and rapid incident response.
- Temperature sensor readings transmitted for basic cold-chain monitoring and ambient telemetry.
- Support for compatible Bluetooth sensors and peripherals \(temperature/humidity sensors, relays, key fobs\) to extend capabilities through Plaspy.
- Geo-fence entry/exit events, low-battery alerts and custom event triggers sent directly to Plaspy for automated alerts and reporting.

The WarriorX 100 pairs the durability and battery longevity required for outdoor asset programs with the interoperable data transport and device management features Plaspy expects. For fleet management teams and asset owners seeking a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker that minimizes field visits and maximizes situational awareness, the WarriorX 100 offers a balanced solution: long battery life, flexible reporting profiles \(including SVR for intensive tracking windows\), and a rugged IP67 design to withstand harsh outdoor conditions.
