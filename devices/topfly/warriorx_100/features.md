# TopFly - WarriorX 100 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the TopFly WarriorX 100 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy when the device is deployed, and it highlights the WarriorX 100 design points such as rugged IP67 protection, parallel GNSS positioning, long life battery options, and compatibility with Plaspy for centralized telemetry and device management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a practical overview and consult the device manufacturer documentation for precise, model specific details before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The WarriorX 100 is a standalone battery powered asset tracker built for long life outdoor deployments and simple integration with Plaspy. It balances low power operation, rugged packaging, and flexible reporting to support multi year deployments for containers, trailers, heavy equipment, and other high value assets.

- Long life replaceable non rechargeable battery options with multi year operating life depending on reporting profile and configuration
- Rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure and multiple mounting options for outdoor assets
- Parallel GNSS positioning combining GPS GLONASS Beidou Galileo and QZSS for high accuracy location fixes
- Flexible transport support including TCP UDP MQTT and SMS plus remote firmware updates for centralized management
- Built in temperature sensing and removal alerts to support basic cold chain and anti theft monitoring
- Configurable reporting strategies including high frequency SVR mode and geo fencing for operational visibility

## Core Features of TopFly - WarriorX 100

- Plaspy compatible tracker with standard transport options for centralized telemetry ingestion
- Long life replaceable battery options commonly offered as 4000 mAh standard and 8000 mAh optional
- IP67 rated waterproof enclosure and multiple mounting choices including magnets screws adhesive and zip ties
- Parallel GNSS receiver providing GPS GLONASS Beidou Galileo and QZSS positioning with high horizontal accuracy
- Support for TCP UDP MQTT and SMS transports and firmware over the air updates for remote maintenance
- Built in temperature sensor and removal or tamper alert capability for event based monitoring
- Compatibility with BLE sensors and manufacturer accessories to extend telemetry and event inputs
- Geo fencing support with multiple configurable regions and low battery alerts for operational safety

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location fixes sensor readings and event notifications from the WarriorX 100 so fleets and asset managers can view live location data play back history and configure alerts and reports. The platform centralizes telemetry while allowing you to tune reporting frequency and alert thresholds to balance battery life and update needs.

- Live GNSS fixes and historical playback for route reconstruction and location oversight
- Event forwarding for removal alerts tamper notifications and low battery alarms to trigger notifications or workflows
- Temperature telemetry and BLE sensor readings available in Plaspy for basic ambient monitoring and reporting
- Flexible reporting modes reflected in Plaspy so you can balance multi year battery life against periods of increased update frequency
- Remote firmware update status and transport configuration visible through device management features in Plaspy
