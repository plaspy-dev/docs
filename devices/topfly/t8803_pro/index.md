# TopFly - T8803 PRO

![T8803 PRO](./logo.jpg)

The TFT T8803 PRO is a highly useful GPS tracker designed specifically for vehicle tracking. With its compact design and no external antennas, installation is quick and easy. This PRO model is an upgraded version of the T8803A, offering additional sensor alarms such as towing and vibration alarms, as well as an integrated G-sensor, SOS, and Mic cable. It also features a smart sleep mode and a newer protocol that optimizes GPRS consumption. With its IP66 certification, the T8803 PRO is also waterproof, ensuring durability and reliability in various weather conditions.

The T8803 PRO comes with a package that includes the GPS tracker itself, a digital input extension line, sponge double-side adhesive tape, and a power supply extension line. Additionally, the manufacturer offers optional accessories such as an engine block relay, microphone, and SOS line. This tracker is equipped with GPS for location and tracking purposes, and in the absence of satellite signals, it utilizes Cell ID location \(LBS-tracking\) as a backup. It supports various tracking modes, including time/interval tracking, direction variation tracks, distance interval tracks, and SMS reports on a time interval.

With its quad-band GSM 2G module, the T8803 PRO can be operated worldwide. It has a wide range of power supply compatibility, from 6V to 36V, making it suitable for installation in both cars and vans. The tracker also features a built-in rechargeable backup battery that provides approximately 3-4 hours of device operation in normal conditions when fully charged. Remote management of the T8803 PRO is possible through GPRS, and it supports remote APN settings configuration via SMS. The tracker is equipped with two LEDs - a red LED indicating GSM/GPRS mode and a blue LED indicating GPS reception. These LEDs automatically turn off after five minutes of device activation.
