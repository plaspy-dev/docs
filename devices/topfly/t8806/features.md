# TopFly - T8806 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the TopFly T8806 GPS tracker and how those features are commonly used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities, operational value, and how T8806-reported events and status are reflected when the device is paired with Plaspy for tracking and monitoring.

Exact feature availability for any individual T8806 unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and how the unit is installed and configured. Always check the manufacturer documentation and your installed firmware to confirm which features are active on a particular device.

## Feature Overview

The TopFly T8806 is positioned as a vehicle tracker with real time location, historical route recording, and a range of security and monitoring functions. When integrated with Plaspy, the device can provide location awareness, event reporting, and remote control actions that help fleets and vehicle owners monitor and respond to incidents.

- Real time tracking to monitor current vehicle location and movement
- Historical waypoint record for route playback and activity review
- Remote immobilization control to stop engine operation remotely when supported by installation
- Multiple alarm types including overspeed, geo fence, tow, and theft alerts
- Two way audio and SOS call capability for in vehicle communication and emergency signaling

## Core Features of TopFly - T8806

- Real time GPS tracking for live position updates
- Historical waypoint storage for viewing past routes and stops
- Remote vehicle immobilization functionality for recovery and theft response
- Overspeed alarm to report speed threshold violations
- Geo fence alarm to notify when a vehicle enters or leaves defined areas
- Tow alarm and theft alarm based on unauthorized movement or ACC disconnect
- Two way audio and SOS call for communication and emergency alerts
- Fuel detection, fuel leakage alarm, temperature detection, car door motion detection, and air conditioner detection as reported by the device

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives status, location, and alarm messages from compatible trackers like the T8806 and presents those events and history in the platform for monitoring and analysis. Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocol and can accept reports from devices configured to use TCP or UDP; when configured correctly, the platform surfaces device-reported data for operators.

- Live position and map tracking appear in Plaspy for situational awareness
- Historical waypoints reported by the device are available for route playback and audits
- Alarms such as overspeed, geo fence, tow, and theft appear as events that can trigger notifications or follow up actions
- Remote immobilization commands reported by the device can be managed through Plaspy where the device and installation support that control
- Two way audio capable devices and SOS events are represented as device features or events when supported and configured by the installer and firmware
- Sensor-based reports such as fuel, temperature, and door motion are shown as status updates and event records when the device provides those telemetry types

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet location monitoring with live tracking and historical route review
- Theft prevention and recovery workflows using theft alarm and remote immobilization where available
- Safety and emergency response using SOS call and two way audio features
- Geofencing to enforce operational boundaries and receive alerts on unauthorized movements
- Asset health and operational monitoring through fuel, temperature, and door motion alerts
- Tow detection for stationary vehicle protection and incident notification

## Feature Availability Notes

- Available features depend on the device firmware and hardware revision installed in each unit
- Some functions require specific wiring or installation steps to expose sensors and controls to the tracker
- Regional variants or carrier-specific firmware may enable or disable particular alarms or telemetry
- Plaspy surfaces the events and controls that the device reports; behavior can vary by firmware and configuration
- For precise specifications, supported interfaces, and installation guidance consult the official TopFly documentation

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the TopFly T8806 centralizes vehicle location, event reporting, and historical data in a single platform. This combination helps operations teams and vehicle owners maintain visibility, respond faster to incidents, and review historical activity for compliance or optimization.

Plaspy is designed to accept and present the events and telemetry that compatible devices like the T8806 report, making it easier to manage alarms, view route history, and issue remote controls when supported by the device and installation.
