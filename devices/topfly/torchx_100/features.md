# TopFly - TorchX 100 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the TopFly TorchX 100 and explains how its capabilities map into Plaspy for fleet management, compliance, and operational monitoring. The content focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about what the device reports and how those reports are visible and actionable inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, regional variant, and manufacturer implementation. Review manufacturer documentation and release notes for the latest device-specific details before planning deployments or compliance workflows.

## Feature Overview

The TorchX 100 is a plug-and-play OBDII GPS tracker designed to deliver frequent location updates and deep vehicle telemetry into Plaspy. It combines vehicle CAN BUS reads with connectivity and buffering to provide continuous visibility for fleets and passenger transport operations.

- Plug-and-play OBDII installation for fast deployment and straightforward fitment in most vehicles
- Full CAN BUS telemetry including VIN, true odometer, fuel level, ignition status, and diagnostic trouble codes
- ELD support for driving hours reporting and regulatory workflows visible inside Plaspy
- Global cellular connectivity with fallback to legacy networks and local buffering to preserve data when coverage is lost
- Built-in dual-band WiFi hotspot and BLE sensor support to expand passenger services and sensor integration
- Driving behavior detection and network jamming alerts to improve safety monitoring and device health visibility

## Core Features of TopFly - TorchX 100

- OBDII plug-and-play form factor for rapid installation in compatible vehicles
- Full CAN BUS data reading with VIN, true odometer, fuel level, ignition status, and DTC reporting
- ELD functionality for RODS and hours of service tracking
- Global 4G LTE Cat 4 connectivity with fallback to 3G and 2G to maintain reporting in varied coverage areas
- High accuracy GNSS positioning to support frequent and precise location updates
- Large local data buffer to store location points when the device is offline
- Built-in dual-band WiFi hotspot to provide passenger internet access and on-board services
- BLE 4.2 support for external Bluetooth sensors and beacons plus device safety features such as driving behavior detection and jamming alerts

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the TorchX 100 telemetry and presents it in dashboards, reports, and alerting workflows so fleet managers and compliance teams can act on location and vehicle data. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and maps reported fields into the platform for visibility and event-driven workflows.

- Real-time location and CAN BUS telemetry shown on Plaspy maps and asset timelines, with configurable reporting intervals
- ELD logs and driver hours integrated into Plaspy reporting for regulatory oversight and historical review
- Diagnostic trouble codes, VIN, and true odometer values recorded in Plaspy to support maintenance workflows and asset identification
- Offline buffering continuity surfaced as delayed uploads or gap-filling data so historical traces remain accurate after connectivity resumes
- BLE and WiFi state and device health events available as operational indicators inside Plaspy for passenger services and sensor monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for route monitoring, odometer based workflows, and fuel-informed operational planning
- Passenger transport and shuttle services combining frequent location updates with onboard WiFi to improve rider experience and scheduling
- Car rental and shared mobility operations using VIN and true odometer reporting to simplify check in and check out and support anti-theft monitoring
- Regulatory compliance for commercial drivers using built-in ELD functionality and Plaspy reporting for audits
- Vehicle diagnostics and maintenance planning based on DTC reporting and CAN BUS telemetry
- Anti-theft detection and recovery efforts supported by frequent position updates and ignition state reporting
