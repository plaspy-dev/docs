# TopFly - TorchX 310

<div className="device-logo">
![TorchX 310](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The TorchX 310 is a compact plug and play OBD-II GPS tracker designed for straightforward deployment in commercial fleets and individual vehicles. It combines onboard GNSS location with CAN-bus derived vehicle data to deliver VIN, true odometer, fuel level, diagnostic trouble codes, ignition status and other vehicle parameters without extensive installation work. The device also supports BLE accessory pairing and built-in driver alerts to capture behavioral and environmental context alongside location.

Because the TorchX 310 is Plaspy compatible out of the box, it integrates into Plaspy-based fleet management workflows to provide continuous location visibility and vehicle diagnostics. Its combination of real-time tracking, buffered offline logging and accessory support makes the device useful for live monitoring, automated reporting and alerting within Plaspy, helping operators reduce downtime and improve operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD-II installation for fast rollouts and minimal vehicle downtime.
- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking, reporting and dashboard integration.
- Direct CAN-bus reads of VIN, true odometer, fuel level and diagnostic trouble codes for accurate vehicle telemetry.
- BLE 5.0 support for pairing temperature sensors, door sensors, wireless relays and key fobs to extend telemetry.
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity with NB2 and 2G fallback where applicable for broad cellular coverage.
- Large offline buffer and configurable reporting intervals for reliable logging during connectivity gaps.
- Driver safety features including crash detection, harsh event monitoring and an internal buzzer for in-cab alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TorchX 310 streams GNSS location and CAN-derived vehicle telemetry into the Plaspy platform so fleet managers can visualize position, monitor vehicle health and trigger alerts. Plaspy ingests the incoming data to populate live maps, historical tracks, diagnostic logs and rule based notifications that support operational decision making.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates with configurable reporting intervals for live visibility.
- Vehicle state reporting including VIN, true odometer, fuel level and diagnostic trouble codes for maintenance and compliance workflows.
- Event and behavior detection such as ignition state, harsh acceleration, harsh braking, sharp turning and crash events to support driver coaching and safety programs.
- Buffered data forwarding so stored points are transmitted to Plaspy when connectivity is restored, preserving continuity of records.
- BLE accessory integration to surface temperature, door status or other sensor readings alongside vehicle data.
- Configurable alerts and automated reports to support anti-theft monitoring, maintenance scheduling and operational KPIs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for location tracking, fuel monitoring and CAN-based diagnostics across light and heavy vehicles.
- Usage based insurance and driver behavior programs that require harsh event capture and crash detection data.
- Anti-theft monitoring and tamper alerts using backup data reporting and in-cab notifications.
- Cold chain and refrigerated transport monitoring by pairing BLE temperature sensors with vehicle telemetry.
- Remote control and immobilizer workflows using paired wireless relays or key fobs for access and security.
