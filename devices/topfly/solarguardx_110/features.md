# TopFly - SolarGuardX 110 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the TopFly SolarGuardX 110 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that matter for fleet management and outdoor asset protection when reporting into the Plaspy platform, and explains how those capabilities translate into operational value for monitoring, alerting, and remote management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, regional model, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical limits, configuration instructions, and the latest firmware details consult the official manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The SolarGuardX 110 is a rugged, solar assisted GPS padlock designed for long term outdoor deployments and anti theft protection. It focuses on keeping assets visible and reducing on site maintenance through a large rechargeable battery, solar charging, reliable cellular connectivity, and robust offline logging.

- Solar augmented long life battery to minimize field visits and extend unattended operation.
- Multi constellation GNSS for higher satellite visibility and improved position accuracy for outdoor assets.
- High frequency reporting and large on device buffer to preserve tracking history through coverage gaps.
- Rugged IP67 padlock form factor with physical attachment options for containers and trailers.
- Remote management features including network commands, BLE and RFID support plus firmware updates for operational control.
- Secure communications options and carrier approvals to ease large scale deployment and integration.

## Core Features of TopFly - SolarGuardX 110

- Solar assisted rechargeable battery with a high capacity pack for extended field operation.
- LTE Cat 1 cellular connectivity for reliable wide area reporting and real time telemetry.
- Multi constellation GNSS support with high precision autonomous horizontal accuracy as specified by the manufacturer.
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure with integrated magnet and tether for secure attachment in harsh environments.
- Configurable reporting intervals including high frequency reporting for near real time position updates.
- Large on device buffered logging to store points during offline periods and upload when connectivity is restored.
- Remote management capabilities such as network commands, BLE pairing, RFID access, SMS control, and FOTA firmware updates.
- Event and alarm support for common anti theft conditions such as rope cut, lock and unlock events, movement and stop alerts.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position and event data from compatible devices to provide continuous situational awareness, historical playback, and configurable alerts. When the SolarGuardX 110 transmits location and telemetry, Plaspy displays that data and makes it actionable for operators and workflows.

- Real time location and telemetry updates in Plaspy for live monitoring and map visualization.
- Device alarms and events such as rope cut, lock or unlock, movement and stop are surfaced as alerts and viewable in event logs.
- Buffered logging points are uploaded after connectivity returns and shown in Plaspy to preserve complete movement history.
- Remote actions and maintenance tasks initiated from Plaspy or network commands support device management and FOTA updates.
- BLE accessory and sensor data reported by the device can be associated with asset records in Plaspy to enrich location data.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer security with remote lock state monitoring and rope cut alerts.
- Long term fleet asset tracking for intermittently used trailers, chassis and intermodal equipment.
- Remote equipment deployments where solar charging and large buffers reduce service frequency.
- Environmental telemetry augmentation by pairing BLE sensors to combine location and sensor data in Plaspy.
- Short term rentals and shared asset programs requiring movement alerts, geofence monitoring and access control.
- Logistics and yard management where rugged attachment and precise positioning improve operational oversight.
