# AutoFon - DX Маяк

<div className="device-logo">
![DX Маяк](./logo..png)
</div>

The AutoFon DX Маяк is a compact multipurpose GPS tracker designed to determine the location of a protected object using GLONASS and GPS satellite systems, with fallback location estimation via cellular network base stations. It packages a GNSS receiver, GSM/GPRS communications, an accelerometer for movement and shock detection, and a rechargeable power source in a small enclosure that is suitable for discreet placement and long term monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the DX Маяк can feed position updates and recorded events into a monitoring platform for visibility and oversight. Its ability to transmit coordinates and events over 2G networks by GPRS or SMS, plus features like movement sensing and Bluetooth presence, make it a practical option for organizations that want to include this model in Plaspy for fleet, asset, or personal tracking workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Accurate position fixes using GLONASS and GPS satellites with cellular base station fallback for approximate location
- Transmits coordinates and events via 2G GSM networks using GPRS or SMS messaging
- Built in accelerometer for movement detection and shock alerts suitable for anti theft monitoring
- Bluetooth BLE functionality for smartphone based presence detection and direction finding
- Compact form factor and long battery life with selectable continuous online or interval sleep modes
- Non volatile black box memory for storing unsent data and an energy consumption meter for battery monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

The DX Маяк delivers periodic and event driven location updates and status messages that Plaspy can ingest for map visualization, alerts, and reporting. When configured to send data to a monitoring server, the device provides the basic position and event information Plaspy uses to present device state and history.

- Live and historical location visibility on Plaspy maps to track assets and vehicles
- Event alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on movement, shock detection, low battery, or loss of connection
- Inclusion in fleet groups and status dashboards for operational oversight and dispatch coordination
- Reporting and export of route history and event logs for audits and analysis
- Monitoring of device health indicators such as battery level and signal strength within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft protection and real time tracking of passenger vehicles and commercial fleets
- Monitoring of valuable cargo and trailers during transit
- Tracking of motorcycles, ATVs, bicycles, and other small vehicles where discreet installation is required
- Personal safety tracking for children, elderly family members, or domestic animals
- Long term periodic monitoring of assets stored off site using interval sleep mode to preserve battery life

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AutoFon DX Маяк is a versatile choice where compact size and flexible power management are important. Its combination of satellite based positioning, cellular fallback, and accelerometer based event detection makes it suitable for organizations that need reliable location reporting with configurable alerting. The added Bluetooth presence and direction finding features provide useful options for close range recovery or presence dependent alarms.

Paired with Plaspy, the DX Маяк can be included in map based monitoring, alert workflows, and fleet reports to give operations teams a clear view of asset location and status.
