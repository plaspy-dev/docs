# AutoFon - DX Mayak 8.1

![DX Mayak 8.1](./tracker.png)

The AutoFon DX Mayak 8.1 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker built for discreet anti theft protection and long term asset monitoring. Designed to be small and power efficient, the device supports dual GNSS positioning and event driven alerts such as movement, crash and SOS to keep vehicles, cargo and portable equipment visible and secure over extended deployments. Its enclosure options and low duty tracking modes make it suitable for concealed mounting and multi year installations where regular maintenance is impractical.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the DX Mayak 8.1 can feed location, telemetry and alarm events into Plaspy monitoring workflows. The tracker uses standard reporting channels with SMS fallback and includes rich telemetry like battery level, temperature, satellite count and GSM signal with each message. Those capabilities make it a practical choice for organizations that want reliable position updates, alerting and long term asset oversight inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible with primary server reporting and SMS fallback for redundancy
- Long battery life suitable for multi year deployments in low duty interval mode
- Dual GNSS positioning with assistance features for improved fix reliability
- Built in accelerometer and SOS button for movement, tilt and crash alerts
- BLE based owner presence and close range locating via smartphone app
- Robust offline behavior with non volatile packet storage and retransmission
- Compact form factor with IP54 and optional IP67 enclosure choices

## How It Works with Plaspy

The DX Mayak 8.1 integrates with Plaspy by delivering structured location and telemetry messages to monitoring servers, while using SMS as a secondary channel when needed. Plaspy ingests those updates and exposes them through maps, dashboards and notification workflows so operators can track assets, receive alarms and review historical events. Built in message buffering ensures that events recorded during connectivity outages are forwarded to Plaspy once the device is back online.

- Real time location and telemetry updates visible on Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Movement, tilt, crash and SOS event alerts routed to Plaspy notification channels
- Battery level, internal temperature, satellite count and signal quality included with each update
- BLE owner presence and close range locating available to augment Plaspy visibility
- GSM base station fallback for limited satellite reception scenarios such as indoor parking
- Automatic retransmission of stored messages to preserve a continuous event history in Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset monitoring of containers, toolboxes and equipment with infrequent check ins
- Personal safety and vulnerable person supervision when discreet tracking and emergency alerts are required
- Two wheel and off road vehicle recovery where compact design and concealed mounting are important
