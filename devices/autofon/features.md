# AutoFon - Омега-Маяк+ Features

This page provides a public, features-focused overview of the AutoFon Омега-Маяк+ tracker and how its capabilities map to monitoring and fleet management workflows in Plaspy. It explains the device functions that are relevant to Plaspy users and highlights practical operational value without exposing private configuration details.

Exact feature availability and behavior for any specific Омега-Маяк+ unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, SIM and network conditions, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware behavior please consult the official AutoFon documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Омега-Маяк+ is designed to provide reliable location reporting and event notifications for a wide range of mobile and stationary objects. It combines satellite positioning, cellular fallback, short range presence detection, and motion sensing to deliver continuous situational awareness to monitoring platforms such as Plaspy.

- Accurate position from GLONASS and GPS satellite systems for primary location fixes
- Approximate location based on cellular network base stations when satellite reception is limited
- Reporting of coordinates and events over 2G GSM networks using GPRS to a monitoring server or via SMS to selected phone numbers
- Built in accelerometer for movement detection, shock and impact monitoring, and crash mode alerts
- Bluetooth BLE for detecting the presence of an owner smartphone and improving ground level location workflows
- Micro USB connector for device configuration from a computer and charging the internal lithium polymer battery

## Core Features of AutoFon - Омега-Маяк+

- GLONASS and GPS satellite positioning for accurate outdoor fixes
- Cellular base station location as a fallback for limited-signal environments
- Data transfer over 2G GSM networks via GPRS reporting to monitoring servers
- SMS message transmission of coordinates and events to configured phone numbers
- Digital accelerometer for movement detection, shock sensing, and crash sensor mode
- Bluetooth BLE module to check smartphone proximity and assist in ground searches
- Micro USB port for configuration and battery charging
- Built in microphone for remote listening where permitted by local law

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When configured to report via GPRS to Plaspy, the Омега-Маяк+ can deliver position updates and event notifications into the Plaspy monitoring environment for centralized visibility and analysis. Plaspy automatically detects many common tracker protocols and accepts device reports for mapping, alerts, and history playback.

- Real time and historical position display on Plaspy maps when the device reports to the Plaspy server
- Movement, shock, and crash events become visible as time stamped events in Plaspy for operational follow up
- BLE proximity information can be used offline by installers and operators to verify presence near the device; BLE presence itself is typically reported locally and may be reflected in Plaspy only if integrated via supported reporting paths
- SMS notifications are delivered to configured phone numbers and are independent of Plaspy unless forwarded to the monitoring server
- Plaspy supports centralized logging of GPRS reports and event sequences for auditing and operational review
- Plaspy will automatically detect the tracker protocol in most cases when the device is pointed at the Plaspy monitoring server

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft protection for cars, motorcycles, and other vehicles with remote event alerts
- Monitoring and tracking of valuable cargo, containers, and transport assets
- Location tracking for personal and company vehicles to support fleet oversight
- Locating stolen or lost small vehicles and movable equipment
- Monitoring children, elderly individuals, and other persons where compact tracking is required
- Tracking domestic animals and livestock for location recovery and supervision
- Protecting remote stationary objects such as garages, cottages, and retail pavilions with listening and movement alerts

## Feature Availability Notes

- Reported behavior depends on device firmware and hardware revision; features may change across production batches
- Network reporting uses 2G GSM and GPRS; regional cellular network availability and SIM configuration will affect reporting reliability
- BLE proximity detection and remote listening behavior depend on smartphone compatibility and local privacy regulations
- SMS reporting delivers messages to phone numbers and is not automatically captured by Plaspy unless those messages are routed to the monitoring server
- Proper installation and power management influence battery life and the reliability of motion detection events
- Always verify the specific unit configuration and available firmware features with the manufacturer documentation

Using the AutoFon Омега-Маяк+ with Plaspy gives organizations a unified view of location, movement, and event history across many devices. Plaspy also simplifies device onboarding by detecting many tracker protocols automatically and accepting reports sent to the monitoring server.
