# AutoFon - Альфа-Маяк XL

![Альфа-Маяк XL](./tracker.jpg)

The AutoFon Альфа-Маяк XL is a compact, low-maintenance GPS tracker designed for long-term, autonomous deployments. Built for covert vehicle and asset protection, the device transmits precise locations over SMS or GPRS and is factory configured with an embedded prepaid SIM and extended runtime. Its sealed, weatherproof enclosure and compact footprint make it suitable for discreet placement on vehicles, trailers, equipment or remote assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Альфа-Маяк XL integrates into Plaspy’s fleet management and telemetry tools to deliver continuous visibility without frequent servicing. Plaspy receives the tracker’s periodic position and event messages, aggregates them into maps, alerts and historical routes, and helps teams act on theft events or operational exceptions with centralized monitoring and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device sending location and status via SMS and GPRS for platform integration and alerts
- Ultra long autonomous runtime of up to 5 years with typical once per day reporting
- Covert and rugged hermetic plastic housing approximately 77×52×24 mm with IP67 level protection
- Combined GLONASS and GPS positioning for reliable location fixes in varied environments
- Factory configured operation with an embedded prepaid SIM for immediate deployment
- Built in SOS button and micro control with optional audio monitoring for investigative support
- Local black box buffer to preserve unsent packets and ensure data persistence during connectivity gaps

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Альфа-Маяк XL forwards periodic position and event messages over GSM channels so Plaspy can parse incoming reports into real time tracking, history and notifications. The platform consolidates those updates into fleet views and reports to improve operational oversight and incident response.

- Real time location and status updates delivered via SMS and GPRS are consumed by Plaspy for map visualization
- SOS and emergency triggers are forwarded to Plaspy to generate immediate alerts for operators
- Local black box buffering maintains recent unsent data and allows Plaspy to receive complete updates when connectivity resumes
- Remote changes to reporting schedules and device modes are reflected in Plaspy reporting and history
- Audio monitoring and full access command modes, when enabled on the device, can be used alongside Plaspy location feeds for incident investigations

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle anti theft monitoring and recovery for cars, motorcycles and scooters
- Long term asset tracking for trailers, leased machinery and equipment with minimal maintenance needs
- Remote property and structure security for off site storage, garages and seasonal sites
- Periodic tracking of valuable cargo and logistics items that benefit from long autonomous runtime
- Monitoring rental equipment and other assets where discreet and weatherproof tracking is required

Pairing the AutoFon Альфа-Маяк XL with Plaspy provides a practical option for organizations that need discreet, long duration tracking with low upkeep.
