# AutoFon - Альфа-Маяк

<div className="device-logo">
![Альфа-Маяк](./tracker.png)
</div>

The AutoFon Альфа‑Маяк (Alfa Beacon) is a matchbox‑sized GPS/GLONASS tracker designed for long‑term autonomous operation and discreet installation. It reports position and basic status over GSM using SMS and GPRS, and is built for scenarios where compact size and extended battery life are priorities. The device is described as having robust location capability, simple alert functions such as an SOS button and microphone, and buffered messaging to preserve critical events when connectivity is intermittent.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the Alfa Beacon can deliver its location and event messages to Plaspy using standard SMS and GPRS channels. That compatibility makes the tracker a practical option for operators who need straightforward integration into Plaspy for real‑time location visibility, alerting, and simple fleet or asset oversight without requiring complex hardware adaptations.

## Key Highlights

- Extremely compact form factor suitable for discreet installation in vehicles, bikes, boats, and portable assets.
- Very long autonomous operation potential, advertised up to three years under typical low‑frequency reporting settings.
- Dual GNSS positioning with both GPS and GLONASS to improve location fixes in mixed reception environments.
- Factory provisioned M2M SIM with preloaded credit for simplified connectivity and initial deployment.
- SOS micro button and integrated microphone for manual alerts and optional remote audio monitoring in permitted configurations.
- Message buffering and retry logic to preserve and forward missed reports after connectivity is restored.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Alfa Beacon reports position and status via SMS and GPRS to a monitoring endpoint. Plaspy can ingest those standard messages and present them in its platform for tracking, alerts, and reporting. Integration leverages the device's existing reporting behavior so devices can be registered in Plaspy and begin sending location updates and events with minimal additional setup.

- Real‑time location visibility through periodic GPRS reports and on‑demand SMS location links for immediate map access.
- Centralized alert handling in Plaspy for SOS activations and other status notifications transmitted by the tracker.
- Remote battery and device health visibility in Plaspy to help plan maintenance and replacement cycles.
- Buffered message delivery ensures Plaspy receives stored events after temporary network outages.
- Event and position history available in Plaspy for retrospective analysis and reporting of device activity.
- Remote configuration and firmware update capabilities over GPRS allow administrators to adjust reporting intervals and settings without physical access when supported.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert anti‑theft tracking and recovery for cars, motorcycles, scooters, and small boats where discreet installation is required.
- Lightweight fleet monitoring for service vehicles and small commercial fleets that prioritize low maintenance and periodic tracking.
- Personal safety tracking for individuals who need discreet location reporting and an SOS alert option.
- Asset protection for trailers, containers, and portable equipment that require long autonomous operation and intermittent position updates.
- Simple pet or livestock location monitoring in areas with GNSS and GSM coverage where occasional position reports are sufficient.
