# AutoFon - α-Маяк Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the AutoFon α-Маяк tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities that matter for location awareness, monitoring, and operational oversight when the device reports into Plaspy. The intent is to provide a clear, non sensitive summary of what the tracker can do and how those capabilities are reflected in the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and specific manufacturer configuration. Where possible the content below relies on the device description published by the manufacturer, but users should consult official AutoFon documentation and firmware release notes for device specific behavior and limitations.

## Feature Overview

The AutoFon α-Маяк is a compact, battery powered tracker that determines location with GLONASS and GPS and transmits coordinates via the GSM network. It is designed for long unattended operation and simple setup, offering an out of the box tracking experience with options for configuration through the device Personal Cabinet.

- Very small footprint suited to discreet placement and lightweight assets
- Combined GLONASS and GPS positioning for improved satellite coverage
- Long battery life designed for multi year operation on a single battery set
- Built in SOS button for manual activation and mode switching
- Pre configured SIM and personal configuration tools for remote adjustments
- Durable performance across a wide temperature range and a leather holster for mounting

## Core Features of AutoFon - α-Маяк

- Compact matchbox size for discreet attachment to small assets or personal items
- GLONASS plus GPS navigation module with high sensitivity for reliable positioning
- Up to three years of automatic operation on a single set of batteries under typical settings
- Built in SOS button to force activation and change operation modes
- Pre configured built in SIM chip for device life to simplify initial setup
- Remote firmware upgrade and factory reset capability as provided by the manufacturer
- Configurable reporting frequency and communication timing via the Personal Cabinet
- Leather holster included for belt or collar mounting and convenient placement

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the α-Маяк is connected to Plaspy, the device's location and event information become visible inside Plaspy for monitoring and historical review. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and presents standard reporting without requiring advanced manual protocol configuration.

- Location fixes received from the device appear on Plaspy maps for real time and historical traces
- SOS button events sent by the tracker are rendered in Plaspy as an alert or event for operator attention
- Changes to reporting frequency and timing on the device affect how often Plaspy receives updates and updates the object timeline
- Remote firmware updates and factory resets performed by the device manufacturer are reflected in subsequent device behavior visible in Plaspy
- Plaspy detects the tracker protocol automatically and accepts device reports to the platform for ease of onboarding

Note: Plaspy presents received telemetry and events as reported by the device. Exact fields and event names depend on the device firmware and manufacturer settings.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of small portable assets where low maintenance and long battery life are priorities
- Monitoring personal belongings or equipment in storage with occasional position checks
- Discreet location awareness for equipment rental or loaned items
- Periodic status and location history review for inventory and asset audits
- Simple emergency alerting via the built in SOS function when activated

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and behavior can change with firmware updates; verify which firmware the device runs for exact behavior
- Hardware revision differences such as the platform v.7 noted by the manufacturer can affect size, battery life, and setup steps
- Some capabilities are exposed through the manufacturer's Personal Cabinet and may require that account or service access
- Regional GSM network differences and SIM provisioning can affect connectivity and reporting frequency
- Manufacturer services such as the AvtoFon response program are separate from Plaspy and may have their own terms and procedures

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the AutoFon α-Маяк gives organizations a straightforward way to turn compact, long life trackers into visible assets on a centralized monitoring platform. Plaspy focuses on presenting location, event and historical traces so teams can maintain operational awareness without complex protocol setup. The platform's automatic protocol detection and unified device handling simplify onboarding and let you focus on fleet or asset management tasks.
