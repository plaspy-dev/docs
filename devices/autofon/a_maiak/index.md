# AutoFon - α-Маяк

![α-Маяк](./logo.jpg)

The AutoFon α-Маяк is a compact independent GPS tracker designed to determine the location of an object and transmit coordinates via the GSM network. Built on a newer hardware platform, the device emphasizes a reduced size and long operational life on a single set of batteries. It includes a combined GLONASS and GPS navigation module for reliable position fixing, a built in SOS button for forced activation, and comes pre configured for automatic operation to minimize day to day management.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the α-Маяк can feed position data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and management. Its pre configured operation and adjustable reporting behavior make it a practical option for users who want a low maintenance tracker that integrates into a larger telematics workflow. Plaspy can use the device data to display location history, generate alerts, and support routine monitoring across assets and personnel.

## Key Highlights

- Miniature form factor similar in size to a matchbox for discreet placement and ease of carrying
- Extended autonomous operation up to approximately three years on a single set of batteries for reduced maintenance
- Combined GLONASS and GPS navigation module with high sensitivity to support accurate location determination
- Built in SOS button for manual activation and quick mode switching when needed
- Pre configured device behavior with a built in SIM setup to simplify initial operation
- Configurable reporting frequency and access to location history via the manufacturer Personal Area

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the AutoFon α-Маяк supplies position updates that Plaspy can ingest and present alongside other fleet and asset data. Plaspy leverages those location reports to provide operational oversight and historical records without requiring constant device supervision.

- Live and periodic location visibility of tracked objects on Plaspy maps
- Historical route and position reporting for review and auditing
- Event handling such as SOS activations routed into Plaspy for alerting and response
- Customizable alerting and notifications based on movement or location changes
- Reduced maintenance signals such as infrequent reporting intervals that help extend battery life while still providing necessary oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking small valuable assets where compact size and long battery life are priorities
- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring using the built in SOS function
- Temporary or covert placement for recovery of mobile property or equipment
- Small fleet or mixed asset monitoring where low maintenance tracking is desired
- Situations requiring long unattended operation with periodic remote position checks
