# AutoFon - Маяк 4.4

The AutoFon Маяк 4.4 is a compact GPS tracker designed for long term, low maintenance covert tracking. It provides dependable GPS positioning and GSM based reporting through SMS and periodic GPRS packets, plus an integrated microphone and basic remote control via SMS. The device is engineered for discreet installation and extended autonomous operation, making it suitable for vehicles, small craft, containers, kiosks, and other assets where stealth and battery life are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, Маяк 4.4 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for map display, alerting, and reporting workflows. Its intermittent reporting approach and command based SMS control align well with Plaspy use cases that emphasize low maintenance endpoints, periodic life signals, and on demand control or monitoring from a centralized fleet management platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with SMS and interval GPRS reporting for location updates and alerts.
- Ultra compact form factor for discreet placement in vehicles and assets.
- Extended autonomous operation on internal batteries for long standby between maintenance cycles.
- Integrated microphone for remote audio monitoring when activated by command.
- Configurable alarm input and an auxiliary channel for basic external device control such as immobilizer or siren.
- PIN protected SMS control, authorized number filtering, SIM balance monitoring, and periodic life signal reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Маяк 4.4 interacts with Plaspy by delivering location fixes and status messages through SMS and scheduled GPRS packets that Plaspy ingests and displays on maps. Plaspy can consolidate those intermittent updates into live views, alerts, and historical reports while also sending command SMS back to the device when remote intervention or configuration is needed.

- Location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via SMS and periodic GPRS packets, adjustable within the device power budget.
- Alarm input events forwarded to Plaspy as alert messages for rapid notification and response.
- Auxiliary channel control executed by sending authorized SMS commands from Plaspy to activate external devices such as immobilizers or sirens.
- Remote audio monitoring initiated by command so Plaspy users can request on demand environmental audio when required.
- Periodic life signals and SIM health messages tracked in Plaspy for device monitoring and maintenance planning.

- Small boat and craft monitoring where compact size and long battery life are advantageous.
- Cargo and container security for concealed tracking of high value shipments.
- Remote site protection for kiosks, storage units, or equipment in locations with limited mains power.
- Personal safety and lone worker monitoring using alarm input and on demand audio monitoring.
