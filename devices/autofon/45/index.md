# AutoFon - Маяк 4.5

![Маяк 4.5](./tracker.png)

The AutoFon Маяк 4.5 is a compact GPS tracker intended for long term, low maintenance location monitoring. Designed for discreet placement in vehicles, trailers, boats, cargo and on people or animals, the Mayak 4.5 pairs a LEADTEK 9101 GPS module with GSM connectivity via a QUECTEL M10 modem to report position by SMS and to send interval GPRS packets to a selected monitoring server. Its combination of long battery life, motion detection and remote audio capability makes it suited to anti theft, covert tracking and safety applications where minimal maintenance is important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Mayak 4.5 can push location and status data to a monitoring server or provide SMS based reports to authorized numbers, allowing it to feed Plaspy dashboards, alarms and reports. That compatibility makes the device a practical option for adding low maintenance assets to Plaspy real time tracking, event alerting and history workflows for small fleets, personal security or asset recovery projects.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatibility via GPRS interval packets and SMS for server based tracking and alerts
- Very long autonomous operation under typical settings with low maintenance battery life
- Compact, discreet form factor suitable for covert placement in vehicles, cargo or personal items
- Built in microphone for remote audio monitoring to aid situational verification
- Motion detection with an internal accelerometer for movement alerts and power saving standby
- Reports external power disconnect and supports one alarm input plus one auxiliary output
- Simple SMS control with PIN protection and authorized number management for secure commands

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the Mayak 4.5 with Plaspy relies on the device sending coordinate and status messages either by SMS or by GPRS packet mode to a configured monitoring server. Once Plaspy receives those updates, the platform translates them into map markers, event alerts and history for operational oversight.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy via SMS or periodic GPRS packets
- Movement and tamper notifications surfaced as immediate alerts inside Plaspy
- Power state reporting including external power disconnects to flag potential tampering
- Remote audio activation available for situational verification where permitted by law
- Historical trails and event logs generated from the device packets for reporting and review

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle tracking and recovery for cars, motorcycles and boats
- Asset and cargo monitoring where periodic telemetry and long battery life are priorities
- Personal safety tracking for people, children or animals with movement alerts and audio check
- Remote security monitoring of outbuildings, kiosks or unattended sites to detect tampering
- Panic or auxiliary control scenarios using the alarm input and auxiliary output with external hardware
