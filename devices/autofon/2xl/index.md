# AutoFon - Альфа-Маяк 2XL

![Альфа-Маяк 2XL](./tracker.jpg)

The AutoFon Альфа-Маяк 2XL is a compact, long life GPS and GLONASS tracker intended for discreet, long term asset monitoring. Designed for autonomous operation with a sealed IP67 plastic enclosure and an integrated high capacity battery block, the 2XL is aimed at deployments where extended runtime, low maintenance and reliable reporting are priorities. It supports position reporting, SOS alerts and remote audio monitoring and ships with a prepaid integrated SIM for uninterrupted service.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the 2XL can forward location and telemetry to Plaspy based fleet management and monitoring platforms using SMS or GPRS reporting. Its preconfigured operation and buffering for unsent packets make it well suited to feeding Plaspy dashboards with regular location updates and incident alerts while minimizing hands on maintenance for large deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device that reports position and status via SMS and GPRS for easy integration.
- Compact IP67 sealed housing for discreet installation in vehicles, trailers, boats or portable assets.
- Designed for extended autonomous operation to reduce maintenance visits and service overhead.
- Built in SOS button and remote audio monitoring to support safety and anti theft workflows.
- Internal buffer for unsent packets and retry logic to improve data continuity in intermittent coverage.
- Preinstalled integrated SIM and preconfigured settings for simplified rollout and low maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to send its SMS messages or GPRS packets to Plaspy endpoints, the Альфа-Маяк 2XL delivers coordinate reports, status updates and alerts into Plaspy for real time tracking, logging and notification. Plaspy ingests these messages to provide visibility, event handling and historical reports across fleets or distributed asset groups.

- Real time location updates and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight.
- SOS alerts and on demand audio monitoring can be routed into Plaspy incident workflows and notifications.
- Built in buffering and retry behavior reduces gaps in Plaspy logs when cellular coverage is intermittent.
- Device reports support automated logging and historical playback for audits and route review.
- SMS reporting can act as a fallback channel to maintain basic location and alerting when packet data is unavailable.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert anti theft protection and stolen asset recovery with SOS reporting and incident tracking.
- Long term monitoring of rented equipment or seasonal assets where battery autonomy matters.
- Tracking small vehicles and watercraft where compact form factor and weather resistance are required.
- Monitoring stationary remote objects such as kiosks or storage units with low maintenance telemetry.
- Asset visibility for logistics and shipment tracking where periodic reporting and alerts are useful.
