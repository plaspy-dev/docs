# AutoFon - D-Маяк МОТО Features

This page provides a public, feature oriented overview of the AutoFon D‑Маяк МОТО and how its capabilities are used with the Plaspy tracking and monitoring platform. It focuses on practical, user facing functions and the kinds of data and events the device can supply to Plaspy for real time tracking, alerts, and historical reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, regional variants, and the most current technical details consult the official AutoFon documentation.

## Feature Overview

The D‑Маяк МОТО is a compact motorcycle focused tracker that emphasizes long autonomous operation, rugged sealing for exposed installs, and event driven telemetry. It supports both GPRS monitoring and SMS control for flexible connectivity and includes onboard sensors and local storage to improve data reliability.

- Dual GNSS positioning support for accurate location reporting under typical road conditions.
- GPRS data reporting with SMS fallback for command and alert delivery when packet connectivity is limited.
- Long life battery operation designed for extended deployments with configurable reporting intervals to conserve power.
- Sealed, compact housing suited for covert or exposed motorcycle installations and outdoor assets.
- Built in motion and impact detection with configurable sensitivity for event generation and theft detection.
- Internal packet storage with resend logic to reduce data loss during temporary network outages.

## Core Features of AutoFon - D-Маяк МОТО

- GLONASS and GPS positioning to provide accurate coordinates for live tracking and history.
- GSM/GPRS communications for real time telemetry with SMS command fallback as an alternative channel.
- Motion and impact detection driven by an internal accelerometer for start, impact, tilt and fall events.
- Internal black box storage that buffers GPRS packets and retries transmission after connectivity restoration.
- Compact waterproof housing designed for motorcycle and exposed vehicle installations.
- Battery powered operation optimized for long autonomous life with battery status reporting.
- Alarm input and auxiliary output channels for integration with external security or control circuits.
- Remote maintenance features including firmware updates over GPRS and configurable life sign messages.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the D‑Маяк МОТО reports location, events and diagnostic telemetry so operators can monitor assets and respond to alerts. Plaspy ingests the device packets to present location on maps, trigger notifications, and retain historical records for analysis.

- Real time location and trajectory plotting in Plaspy dashboards when GPRS reporting is active.
- Event alerts (motion start, impact, tilt, fall and alarm input) forwarded into Plaspy event streams for notifications and automated rules.
- Battery and external power status visible in device telemetry to aid preventive maintenance and uptime monitoring.
- Buffered packet delivery improves continuity of tracking inside Plaspy after short network interruptions.
- Remote firmware update indicators and configurable heartbeat reports assist administrators in device lifecycle management.

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle anti theft monitoring with discreet installation, motion and impact alerts, and remote control channels for security responses.
- Long term asset surveillance for trailers, equipment, and exposed assets that need low maintenance tracking.
- Small fleet monitoring for motorcycles and scooters requiring position, event notifications and simple diagnostics.
- Remote security and safety monitoring where waterproofing and compact form factor are necessary.
- Covert installations where a sealed housing and long battery life reduce service visits.
