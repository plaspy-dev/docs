# AutoFon - Альфа-Маяк XL

The AutoFon Альфа-Маяк XL is a compact, autonomous GPS beacon designed for long term, low maintenance deployments. Built for covert vehicle and asset protection, it delivers position reports over SMS or GPRS and is factory configured with an embedded prepaid SIM and extended runtime for discreet, continuous operation in the field.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Альфа-Маяк XL integrates into fleet monitoring workflows to provide real time locations, event alerts and historical tracks. Its combination of rugged enclosure, long autonomous runtime and autonomous reporting makes it a practical option for operators who want to aggregate location updates and anti theft notifications within Plaspy’s fleet management tools.

## Key Highlights

- Compatible with Plaspy: sends location and status updates via SMS and GPRS for integration into Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- Ultra long autonomous runtime with multi year battery life under typical low frequency reporting.
- Covert and rugged design with hermetic housing and IP67 protection for outdoor and concealed installations.
- Combined GLONASS and GPS positioning for reliable location in varied environments.
- Factory provisioned with an embedded prepaid SIM and default configuration for immediate deployment.
- SOS button and optional audio monitoring together with local event buffering to improve data persistence.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Альфа-Маяк XL transmits periodic position and status messages over cellular links that Plaspy receives and normalizes into maps, alerts and reports. Plaspy consolidates incoming coordinates and device events so operators can monitor assets, investigate alarms and review movement history from a single platform.

- Real time location and telemetry updates are shown on Plaspy maps and vehicle lists as messages arrive.
- SOS and life signal events are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification routing and escalation.
- Local black box buffering preserves recent unsent data so Plaspy receives complete updates once connectivity is restored.
- Reporting frequency and schedule changes are reflected in Plaspy reporting when the device is configured accordingly.
- Audio monitoring and full access command reports can be used as complementary investigative inputs alongside Plaspy location data.

- Long term tracking of trailers, leased machinery and equipment where minimal maintenance is required.
- Remote property and structure security for garages, cabins and off site assets.
- Rental fleet monitoring to keep periodic visibility without frequent servicing.
- Periodic cargo and logistics checks where durability and extended runtime are priorities.
