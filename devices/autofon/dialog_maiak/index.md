# AutoFon - Диалог-Маяк

![Диалог-Маяк](./tracker.jpg)

The AutoFon Диалог-Маяк is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for discreet long term location and status monitoring. Built on a v.6.x hardware platform, it combines GPS and GLONASS positioning with GPRS and SMS reporting and a large internal black box buffer. The device offers remote audio monitoring and configurable alarm handling, making it suitable for vehicles, cargo, watercraft and portable assets where stealth and reliability matter.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Диалог-Маяк can transmit coordinates and telemetry to Plaspy over GPRS or via SMS messages, enabling live tracking, alerting and historical playback without extensive wiring or frequent maintenance. Its power optimized operating modes and buffering behavior help Plaspy maintain visibility of assets while preserving battery life for long term deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet form factor suitable for covert installation in vehicles, containers and portable assets.
- GPS and GLONASS positioning for reliable location tracking across regions.
- Flexible reporting over GPRS or SMS to suit different coverage and deployment scenarios.
- Large black box buffer that stores and retries unsent telemetry for resilient data delivery.
- Long autonomous operation optimized for up to two years on internal battery supply for low maintenance.
- Built in microphone and SOS button plus accelerometer based event detection for enhanced situational awareness and alarm handling.
- External control channel and auxiliary interfaces to support remote control actions and integration with external systems.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Диалог-Маяк sends location and status updates to Plaspy using GPRS reporting or SMS messages, and Plaspy ingests that telemetry for visualization, alerting and reporting. The device’s configurable operating cycle balances update responsiveness and power use so Plaspy receives timely information while battery life is preserved.

- Live location and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards and on real time maps for operational visibility.
- Event driven alerts such as SOS presses, alarm inputs and accelerometer events are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification.
- Historical playback and reporting in Plaspy use buffered and received data to reconstruct routes and event timelines.
- Remote audio monitoring data can be associated with events in Plaspy where local rules and regulations permit such use.
- Remote control channel events can be coordinated through Plaspy workflows to support immobilization or other operational responses.
- Robust black box buffering reduces data gaps by retrying delivery of unsent packets so Plaspy retains a continuous event history.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle anti theft and recovery with movement and impact alerts plus remote control workflows.
- Portable asset and cargo tracking where long battery life and compact size reduce maintenance and visibility.
- Remote site protection and personnel safety using SOS and audio monitoring together with alarm inputs.
- Fleet management and route playback for mixed asset types that require occasional telemetry without constant power.
- Unattended equipment monitoring where buffered storage preserves event history across temporary coverage gaps.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AutoFon Диалог-Маяк pairs long life hardware with resilient telemetry strategies that fit Plaspy use cases for low maintenance, discreet tracking. Its buffering and retry logic reduce the risk of lost events during coverage gaps, and the combination of location, event detection and remote control options provides useful inputs for Plaspy rules, alerts and reporting workflows.

For organizations seeking a compact tracker that minimizes on site service while still delivering event rich telemetry, the Диалог-Маяк provides a practical option to integrate into Plaspy. The device’s mix of reporting modes and auxiliary channels makes it adaptable to a range of monitoring scenarios without requiring constant rewiring or high subscription overhead.
