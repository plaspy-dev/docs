# AutoFon - Омега-Маяк XL

![Омега-Маяк XL](./logo..png)

The AutoFon Омега-Маяк XL is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed to determine and report the position of a protected object using combined GLONASS and GPS satellite systems with A GPS support. Built on the v8 hardware platform, the device supports transmission of coordinates via GSM using SMS and uploads to monitoring servers over GPRS. Its feature set includes movement and impact sensors, dual SIM support, significant local storage for tracking packets, a built in microphone, and an IP67 sealed housing suitable for a variety of environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Омега-Маяк XL can feed location and event data into fleet management and monitoring workflows. Its ability to buffer a large number of tracking packets and to work across multiple carrier SIMs helps maintain continuity of position history and alerts, making it a practical option where Plaspy is used for live tracking, historical playback, and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Combined GLONASS and GPS navigation with A GPS support for improved positioning sensitivity
- Multiple sensors for movement, impacts, accidents, overturns, and falls for event based detection
- Dual SIM capability to improve communication reliability across different operators
- Local black box storage for up to 15,000 GPRS packets to preserve history during connectivity gaps
- Rugged IP67 sealed housing for use in exposed or outdoor deployments
- Built in microphone and SOS button for remote audio monitoring and emergency alerts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Омега-Маяк XL provides continuous location updates and event notifications that Plaspy can ingest, display, and archive. Plaspy uses the telemetry and buffered packet uploads to present a coherent timeline of movement and incidents and to support operational reporting.

- Real time location visibility on Plaspy maps using coordinates transmitted from the device
- Historical playback and route reconstruction leveraging stored packets uploaded after connectivity is restored
- Event driven alerts for movement, impacts, and SOS triggers routed through Plaspy notification channels
- Supplemental Wi Fi and BLE signals can provide additional location context where the device reports them
- Dual SIM behavior helps reduce data gaps so Plaspy receives more consistent tracking information
- Configurable reports and dashboards in Plaspy that use device events and location history for fleet oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle fleet monitoring for cars, trucks, and small commercial vehicles
- Asset tracking for valuable cargo, containers, and portable equipment
- Portable unit tracking for motorcycles, boats, and other mobile assets
- Protection and monitoring of stationary remote objects such as garages and temporary sites
- Discreet tracking and presence monitoring for people, children, or animals where permitted

The included motion and impact sensors add event context that Plaspy can surface as alerts, helping operations teams respond more effectively.
