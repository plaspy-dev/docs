## Feature Overview

The AutoFon Микро‑Маяк + is a compact autonomous tracker built for asset visibility and fast recovery. It combines satellite positioning, cellular reporting, and a short range radio mode to keep small vehicles and portable assets locatable in a variety of conditions.

- Dual GNSS positioning with GLONASS and GPS and AGPS assistance for faster fixes and improved location accuracy.
- GSM GPRS telemetry reporting plus SMS command support for live location delivery and remote queries.
- 868 MHz short range radio direction finding to assist recovery in indoor or jammed locations where satellite signals are limited.
- Robust offline buffering with black box memory to preserve telemetry during temporary network outages.
- Built in motion detection and temperature telemetry to support alerts and condition monitoring.
- Compact, rugged form factor and long standby autonomy for covert installations and long duration protection.

## Core Features of AutoFon - Микро-Маяк +

- GLONASS and GPS satellite positioning with AGPS support for quicker satellite acquisition.
- GSM GPRS reporting for continuous telemetry and compatibility with monitoring servers.
- 868 MHz short range radio direction finding mode for close range recovery assistance.
- Internal 3D accelerometer for motion alerts and basic anti evacuation detection.
- Temperature telemetry to record device environmental conditions.
- Black box memory capable of storing large numbers of GPRS packets to prevent data loss.
- One alarm input and one controllable output for simple remote control and event wiring.
- Compact IP64 rated enclosure suitable for vehicle and portable asset installations.
