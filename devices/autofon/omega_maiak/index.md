# AutoFon - Омега-Маяк+

![Омега-Маяк+](./logo..png)

The AutoFon Омега-Маяк+ is a compact GPS tracker designed to determine the location of a protected object using GLONASS and GPS satellite systems, with fallback positioning through cellular network base stations. According to the product description, the device transmits coordinates and recorded events over 2G GSM networks using either data transmission or SMS messages. Built in movement detection and shock monitoring help the Омега-Маяк+ provide situational awareness for secured assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Омега-Маяк+ can feed positional and event data into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can ingest the location updates and notifications produced by the tracker so operators gain continuous visibility, configured alerts, and historical records for fleet or asset management without relying on device specific vendor portals.

## Key Highlights

- Satellite positioning with GLONASS and GPS for accurate outdoor location
- Cellular base station fallback for approximate location when satellites are limited
- Multiple reporting options via 2G GSM including data transmission and SMS
- Built in accelerometer for movement detection and shock event reporting
- Bluetooth module for detecting nearby smartphone presence and proximity checks
- Micro USB connector for configuration and battery charging, supporting discreet installation
- Onboard microphone for remote listening and additional situational awareness

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Омега-Маяк+ supplies location and event messages that Plaspy presents in a unified interface for monitoring and analysis. Plaspy uses incoming coordinates and alerts to provide operational oversight and record keeping across devices and vehicles.

- Real time location display and tracking of individual devices on the Plaspy map
- Event processing for movement, shocks, and other device reports to trigger notifications
- Historical track playback and reporting for route analysis and audits
- Presence information from the Bluetooth module available as an added context data point
- Consolidated alerts and notification delivery to assigned users or teams

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft protection and recovery support for cars, motorcycles, and boats
- Monitoring valuable cargo, containers, and movable equipment during transit
- Tracking personal or official vehicles for fleet oversight and driver accountability
- Location monitoring for children, elderly individuals, or personnel with safety needs
- Protection of stationary remote objects such as garages, cottages, and retail pavilions
- Wearable panic button or personal safety device scenarios where compact tracking is required
