# AutoFon - Альфа-Маяк 2XL

The AutoFon Альфа-Маяк 2XL is a compact long-life GPS and GLONASS tracker intended for discreet, extended asset monitoring. It is designed for autonomous operation with a high-capacity 3500 mAh battery block and an IP67 plastic enclosure, making it suitable for long-term deployments on vehicles, trailers, small craft and other portable or stationary assets. The device is preconfigured for immediate use and includes features such as SOS alerts, remote audio monitoring in full-access mode, and an internal buffer to preserve telemetry when connectivity is intermittent.

This model is Plaspy compatible and can forward position and status information to Plaspy-based fleet management and monitoring platforms using SMS or GPRS reporting. Its integrated prepaid SIM and default configuration simplify roll-out for Plaspy users who need reliable, low-maintenance tracking. Built-in buffering, retry logic and fallback location methods help maintain continuity of location logs and alerts within Plaspy dashboards and incident workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured to send GPRS packets or SMS messages to Plaspy, the Альфа-Маяк 2XL delivers coordinate messages, status updates and alerts that Plaspy ingests for real-time tracking, historical logging and automated notifications. The device features buffering and fallback reporting behavior that improve data continuity for Plaspy users operating in areas with variable signal quality.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates routed into Plaspy dashboards, with SMS as a fallback reporting channel
- SOS alerts and on-demand audio monitoring sent to Plaspy incident handling workflows
- GSM location fallback to preserve coarse tracking when GNSS signals are weak, maintaining visibility in Plaspy
- Internal black-box buffer for unsent packets to reduce gaps in Plaspy logs after temporary connectivity loss
- Remote firmware update support to help keep devices manageable and compatible with Plaspy integrations
