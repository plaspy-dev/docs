# AutoFon - Мега-Маяк+

<div className="device-logo">
![Мега-Маяк+](./logo..png)
</div>

The AutoFon Мега-Маяк+ is a compact GPS tracker built to determine and transmit the precise position of a protected object using GLONASS and GPS satellite systems. It supports sending coordinates via GSM network SMS and can forward data to a monitoring server using GPRS, while on-board features such as A GPS assisted positioning, multiple motion and impact sensors, and a durable IP67 enclosure make it suitable for a wide range of mobile and stationary tracking tasks.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Мега-Маяк+ can deliver the location updates and event notifications that fleet and asset managers expect from a monitoring platform. Plaspy can ingest the device's position reports and status events to provide map visibility, alerts, and historical reporting, making the tracker a practical choice for organizations that want to combine AutoFon hardware with Plaspy monitoring and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Combined GLONASS and GPS navigation module with A GPS support for improved location sensitivity
- Motion and impact sensors with configurable detection for movement start, impacts, accidents, overturns, and falls
- Dual SIM support for redundancy and a black box capable of storing up to 15,000 GPRS packets for offline buffering
- IP67 sealed housing for protection against dust and water and a compact form factor for discreet placement
- Built in microphone and SOS button to support remote listening and emergency notifications
- Wi Fi location assistance and BLE features for enhanced local positioning and presence detection
- Long life built in battery and remote firmware update capability via GPRS for maintenance flexibility

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Мега-Маяк+ sends periodic position updates and event messages to a chosen monitoring endpoint, which Plaspy can receive and process. Once data from the device reaches Plaspy, the platform can display live locations, surface events, and incorporate stored telemetry into reports and operational workflows.

- Live location tracking displayed on Plaspy maps for vehicle and asset visibility
- Event and alert handling for motion starts, impacts, SOS presses, and tamper notifications
- Historical location and black box data available for route review and incident analysis
- Configurable geofence and status alerts to support operational notifications and automated workflows
- Centralized device management and reporting to monitor battery and connectivity trends over time

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and oversight for commercial vehicle operators
- Monitoring of valuable mobile assets such as motorcycles, boats, and trailers
- Protection of remote or stationary property like garages, cottages, and kiosks
- Tracking valuable cargo and containers during transport and storage
- Personal safety monitoring for selected personnel, children, or companion animals

The AutoFon Мега-Маяк+ pairs a versatile set of sensors and positioning capabilities with resilience features such as dual SIM support and a substantial on device data buffer.
