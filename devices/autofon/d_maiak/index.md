# AutoFon - D-Маяк

<div className="device-logo">
![D-Маяк](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AutoFon D‑Маяк (D‑Beacon) is a compact autonomous GSM/GLONASS+GPS beacon built for covert tracker deployments and reliable anti theft recovery. Based on a modern v.6.x hardware platform, the unit is designed for long autonomous operation with configurable SMS and GPRS reporting, multi event detection via a digital accelerometer, and a suite of inputs and outputs for alarm and remote control functions. The D‑Маяк combines discreet form factor with features aimed at persistent tracking and recovery scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D‑Маяк feeds location updates, event notifications, and historical telemetry into the Plaspy platform for real time monitoring and post event review. Its reporting modes, onboard buffering, and alarm inputs make it suitable for teams using Plaspy to maintain visibility over vehicles and remote assets, receive timely alerts, and coordinate recovery or response actions from a central monitoring dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- Long autonomous operation with extended battery life suitable for covert deployments and remote guarding.
- Combined GLONASS and GPS navigation for improved positioning compared with single constellation devices.
- GPRS monitoring with SMS reporting fallback to maintain connectivity and deliver alerts to Plaspy.
- Digital accelerometer with multiple event detections for movement, impacts and other activity driven alarms.
- Integrated SOS microbutton, external alarm input, and universal auxiliary channel for remote control actions.
- Large black box buffer to store unsent GPRS packets and preserve telemetry during network outages.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AutoFon D‑Маяк sends its location and event messages to the platform where they appear on maps, timelines, and in configurable alert channels. Plaspy ingests incoming messages from the device, reconciles stored packets after connectivity lapses, and offers operators tools to monitor, report and act on the D‑Маяк telemetry alongside other fleet assets.

- Real time location updates and configurable reporting intervals visible on Plaspy maps and dashboards.
- Immediate forwarding of alarm and SOS events to operators and notification channels configured in Plaspy.
- Delivery of buffered telemetry after connection restores to preserve continuity in historical records.
- Remote control commands routed from Plaspy to the device to trigger auxiliary channel actions where configured.
- Audio monitoring and owner presence tag status presented in Plaspy as supplemental situational information.
- Flexible reporting failover modes help maintain persistent tracking behavior for recovery and oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle anti theft protection and rapid recovery with hidden installation and SOS support.
- Valuable cargo and container monitoring where long battery life and buffered telemetry are important.
- Remote asset guarding for locations without reliable external power such as kiosks, outbuildings, and seasonal sites.
- Personal or animal tracking when a discreet, long endurance beacon is required for safety monitoring.
- Supplementary discreet tracking for fleet operations where a compact beacon complements primary telematics devices.
