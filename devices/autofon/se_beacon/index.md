# AutoFon - SE+ Beacon

![SE+ Beacon](./tracker.png)

The AutoFon SE+ Beacon is a compact GPS tracker designed for discreet, long term monitoring of vehicles and mobile or stationary assets. It combines GPS and GLONASS positioning with GSM based GPRS reporting and SMS fallback to deliver location telemetry and event alerts while optimizing for low maintenance and extended battery life. Its small form factor and focus on silent operation make it suitable where low detectability and minimal servicing are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SE+ Beacon can feed position and event data into Plaspy for live monitoring, alerting and historical playback. The device's dual reporting paths, configurable heartbeat messages and a large offline buffer help maintain visibility in Plaspy even when network coverage is intermittent, while accelerometer driven events and an auxiliary channel provide actionable signals for fleet or asset workflows managed through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and covert form factor suited to long term deployments in vehicles or assets.
- GPS plus GLONASS positioning for dependable location data.
- Dual reporting modes with GPRS for real time tracking and SMS as a fallback channel.
- Motion, tilt and impact detection plus an SOS micro button for event driven alerts.
- Auxiliary channel and alarm input for remote control actions such as immobilizer or relay control.
- Large offline buffer for storing unsent packets and support for remote firmware updates to simplify maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the SE+ Beacon delivers GNSS positions and event telemetry to Plaspy servers over GPRS with SMS used as a backup notification method. Plaspy ingests those messages to present locations on live maps, trigger configurable alerts and keep historical records for playback and reporting, while device heartbeat and buffer behavior help ensure continuity of data.

- Real time location and telemetry visible on Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight.
- Event forwarding for motion start and stop, tilt and impact events, and SOS presses as configurable alerts.
- Alarm input reporting and auxiliary channel control can be used for authorized remote actions under Plaspy managed workflows.
- SMS notifications and map link options provide alternative alerting when needed.
- Offline resilience via the device buffer ensures stored packets are delivered to Plaspy once connectivity is restored.
- Configurable heartbeat messages help Plaspy detect device health and reporting frequency.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert long term vehicle monitoring for cars, motorcycles and small boats.
- Asset tracking for trailers, containers and high value cargo with motion and impact alerts.
- Remote stationary monitoring of sites such as cottages, utility boxes or equipment storage where infrequent check ins extend battery life.
- Discreet personal or pet tracking with SOS alerting for immediate attention.
- Small equipment and trailer fleet oversight that relies on location and event alerts rather than full vehicle telematics.

The SE+ Beacon is a practical option for organizations using Plaspy that need a low profile, low maintenance tracker with reliable location reporting and event driven alerts.
