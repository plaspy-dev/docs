# AutoFon - SE+ Beacon

![SE+ Beacon](./tracker.png)

The AutoFon SE+ Beacon is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for discreet long term monitoring of vehicles and other mobile or stationary assets. It combines GPS and GLONASS positioning with GSM based reporting channels to deliver reliable location telemetry and event alerts while optimizing battery life. Its small 69 × 51 × 22 mm form factor and long service interval orientation make it suitable for deployments where low detectability and minimal maintenance are priorities.

As a device that can report positions and event data to third party platforms, the SE+ Beacon is relevant to Plaspy users who need lightweight, resilient tracking hardware. The tracker supports GPRS reporting with SMS as a backup and provides motion, tilt and impact alerts as well as an SOS input and an auxiliary control channel. These capabilities map directly into Plaspy workflows for live maps, alerting, and historical playback, helping teams maintain oversight with a low profile tracker.

## Key Highlights

- Compact, low profile form factor ideal for covert or space constrained installations.
- GPS plus GLONASS positioning for dependable location data.
- Dual reporting paths with GPRS for regular telemetry and SMS for fallback alerts.
- Long battery oriented design suitable for extended deployments with infrequent maintenance.
- Motion, tilt, impact detection and SOS alerting for event driven notifications.
- Auxiliary channel and alarm input for remote control actions and external device control.
- Large offline buffer to store unsent location packets and support data resilience.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the SE+ Beacon delivers GNSS positions and event messages to Plaspy servers so locations, alerts, and history are available in Plaspy dashboards. Plaspy ingests the device data to present live tracking, generate alerts, and build playback trails while accommodating the device's heartbeat behavior and offline buffering.

- Real time location updates and telemetry displayed on Plaspy maps for operational visibility.
- Event forwarding for motion start and stop, tilt and impact alerts, and SOS triggers so teams receive actionable notifications.
- Auxiliary channel and alarm input signals can be reflected in Plaspy workflows for authorized remote actions or response procedures.
- SMS fallback and SMS based notification modes provide alternative alert delivery where data coverage is limited.
- Offline buffer behavior ensures Plaspy can recover historic telemetry after temporary connectivity interruptions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert long term vehicle monitoring for cars, motorcycles and small boats.
- Asset tracking for containers, trailers and valuable cargo with motion and impact alerts.
- Remote stationary monitoring of sites such as cottages, garages or equipment enclosures with infrequent check ins.
- Personal or pet location tracking where discreet form factor and SOS alerting are important.
- Small equipment and trailer fleet oversight where compact devices and event alerts meet operational needs.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AutoFon SE+ Beacon is a practical option for organizations using Plaspy when the priorities are discreet installation, long service intervals, and reliable event driven telemetry rather than full vehicle telematics. Its combination of GNSS positioning, dual reporting paths, event detection and a sizeable offline buffer helps reduce data gaps and supports anti theft and safety oriented workflows managed through Plaspy.

For operations that require additional signals such as detailed engine metrics or fuel consumption, the SE+ Beacon can be incorporated into a broader Plaspy deployment alongside other sensors or integrations to fill those needs. Remote configuration and firmware management over the air keep devices manageable at scale while Plaspy provides the visibility, alerting, and reporting needed to turn device events into operational insight.
