# AutoFon - GL-Маяк

![GL-Маяк](./tracker.png)

The AutoFon GL-Маяк, also known as the GL-Beacon, is a compact GPS and GLONASS tracker designed for long autonomous operation and dependable location reporting. Built on the v.5.x platform, the device emphasizes battery life, precise positioning and event-driven alarm reporting. Its small form factor and buffered offline storage make it suitable for discreet asset protection and remote tracking tasks where sustained telemetry is important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GL-Маяк can feed location, event and alarm data into Plaspy monitoring and fleet management workflows using GPRS and SMS reporting. Plaspy can use the tracker signals to provide real-time visibility, alerts, historical playback and operational oversight, while the device's buffered packet storage helps preserve records during temporary connectivity loss.

## Key Highlights

- Long autonomous operation suitable for long-term asset deployments and discreet monitoring.
- Combined GPS and GLONASS positioning for more consistent location fixes in mixed environments.
- Large offline buffer that preserves telemetry when the connection is interrupted and forwards data when connectivity is restored.
- Built-in event sensing for motion impact rollover and configurable sensitivity to support alarm workflows.
- Integrated SOS button and microphone for immediate alarms and remote audio checks.
- Auxiliary output for remote control actions paired with a compact form factor for covert installation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the GL-Маяк transmits positional data and events to Plaspy servers over GPRS and can report critical alerts via SMS. Plaspy consumes these inputs to deliver live tracking maps, alarm notifications, timeline playback and reporting dashboards. The tracker’s buffering and retry behavior helps ensure continuity of records that Plaspy can display and act on once connectivity returns.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates to Plaspy with SMS fallback for urgent alerts.
- Immediate reporting of alarm events including SOS activations and motion impact or rollover detections.
- Remote audio monitoring where supported by operational workflows to add context to incidents.
- Remote actuator control using the auxiliary channel for authorized interventions coordinated through Plaspy.
- Offline resilience with stored packets forwarded to Plaspy when coverage is restored and coarse positioning fallback when GNSS is unavailable.

## Typical Use Cases

- Covert vehicle tracking for cars motorcycles and small vessels where discreet installation and long battery life matter.
- Protection of valuable cargo and containers with alarm reporting and buffered telemetry for shipment auditing.
- Personal or pet monitoring where compact autonomous tracking and an SOS function are beneficial.
- Security for remote stationary facilities such as kiosks cabins or seasonal sites that need periodic supervision.
- Small fleet and asset telemetry integrated into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and incident response.
