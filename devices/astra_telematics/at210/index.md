# Astra Telematics - AT210

![AT210](./logo.jpg)

The Astra Telematics AT210 is a highly advanced GPS tracker that has been designed using the latest technology to provide accurate and reliable tracking capabilities. Equipped with a Cortex M3 Processor and SiRFStar IV GPS, this tracker ensures precise location tracking and efficient performance. With its automotive looks and IP67 waterproof sealing, the AT210 is not only functional but also durable, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.

Installation of the Astra Telematics AT210 is simple and hassle-free, and its inconspicuous appearance allows for discreet tracking. Additionally, this tracker offers the option of using an external GPS antenna, which is particularly useful in situations where mounting the device with good GPS visibility is not practical. This flexibility ensures that the AT210 can be customized to meet the specific needs of different tracking scenarios.

Key Features of the Astra Telematics AT210:

- Full quadband GSM band for reliable communication
- Back-up battery for uninterrupted tracking
- Internal memory for storing data
- Supports communication methods such as GPRS, TCP, and UDP
- Position tracking based on time and distance
- Sleep mode for power-saving functionality
- Two digital inputs for additional functionality
- Internal events-based tracking
- Integrated GPS and GSM antennas
- Plastic casing for durability
- Extra connectivity options including Driver ID and Serial Port
- Made in Europe for quality assurance
