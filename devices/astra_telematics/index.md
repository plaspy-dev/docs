# Astra Telematics

Astra Telematics develops GPS tracking and telematics devices commonly used for vehicle, asset, and fleet monitoring. This page explains how Astra Telematics hardware can work with Plaspy, what to expect from devices in this family, and how to find the right tracker for your use case using the catalog below.

## How Astra Telematics devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy integrates with Astra Telematics devices by receiving the telemetry they transmit and translating that data into map views, alerts, and reports inside the Plaspy platform. Integration covers core GPS positioning, movement status, and standard telematics signals. For each compatible Astra Telematics device, Plaspy provides the connectivity settings and data mapping required to stream device messages into your Plaspy account.

Device configuration steps and protocol details are provided on each device page within Plaspy. Use the BrandCatalog below to select a device and open its dedicated guide when you need step by step setup, server parameters, or message format information.

## Common features found in Astra Telematics devices

While models vary, Astra Telematics trackers typically include a range of features that align well with fleet and asset tracking needs. Common capabilities you can expect to leverage with Plaspy include:
- GPS positioning and configurable reporting intervals
- Movement and ignition detection for vehicles
- Basic IO monitoring for external sensors
- Battery and power source reporting for asset and portable trackers
- Support for standard telematics reporting formats compatible with cloud platforms

Plaspy uses these signals to generate location history, geofence events, and telemetry dashboards without requiring model specific changes on the platform side.

## Typical use cases for Astra Telematics GPS trackers

Astra Telematics devices are used across a variety of scenarios where reliable position and event reporting is required. Typical use cases include:
- Small and medium fleet vehicle tracking for route oversight and duty logs
- Trailer and asset tracking to monitor location and movement
- Service vehicle monitoring to track job times and routes
- Equipment theft deterrence and recovery with movement alerts

Plaspy adapts reporting and rules to match these use cases so teams can focus on operations rather than device integration.
