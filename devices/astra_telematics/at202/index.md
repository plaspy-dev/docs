# Astra Telematics - AT202

![AT202](./tracker.jpg)

The AT202 is a compact, high-capability GPS tracker engineered for modern IoT and fleet applications and fully compatible with Plaspy. Combining multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, Galileo, GLONASS and BeiDou\) with multi-network cellular communications — GPRS \(2G\), LTE‑M \(4G\) and NB‑IoT — the AT202 delivers reliable positioning and broad coverage for real-time tracking and fleet management. Its rugged electrical design supports vehicle systems up to 65V, making it practical for a wide range of cars, trucks and many electric vehicles.

Designed for integrators and fleet operators, the AT202 offers rich I/O and telematics interfaces — including CANBus, digital inputs/outputs, ADC, RS232 and 1‑Wire — to capture vehicle telemetry, driver ID and external sensor data. With a 900mAh internal backup battery, internal antennas and MEMS accelerometer, the AT202 keeps reporting in low‑power or disconnected scenarios and plugs directly into Plaspy for streamlined ingestion of location, CANBus and event data.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with multi‑constellation GNSS for accurate positioning and dependable real‑time tracking.
- Multi‑network cellular connectivity \(GPRS, LTE‑M, NB‑IoT\) for wide coverage and future‑proof communications.
- Rich I/O set \(CANBus, 2 digital inputs, 2 digital outputs, 1 ADC, RS232, 1‑Wire\) to capture vehicle telemetry and driver ID.
- 900mAh internal backup battery and up to 30 days low‑power reporting to maintain telemetry in power‑loss scenarios.
- Vehicle electrical tolerance to 65V and internal MEMS accelerometer for motion and tamper detection — ideal for fleet management and anti‑theft monitoring.
- Supplied documentation, multiple kit variants and accessories for fast deployment and tailored installations.
- Five‑year warranty with lifetime system updates and included hardware/reporting customisation options.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT202 integrates with Plaspy to deliver continuous location and telemetry to your fleet dashboard. The device streams GNSS fixes and vehicle data over cellular links to Plaspy where it is processed for real‑time tracking, alerts and historical reporting. Plaspy parses CANBus channels, digital events, ADC values and serial messages to convert raw inputs into meaningful fleet KPIs and triggers.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS position, speed, course\).
- CANBus vehicle data integration for engine, odometer and other OEM telemetry channels.
- Digital input events \(door, ignition\) and digital outputs usable for remote control or immobilizer workflows.
- ADC readings for analog sensors such as fuel level where a sensor is connected, enabling fuel monitoring when configured.
- RS232 and 1‑Wire \(Dallas\) support for external sensors and driver ID \(iButton\) integration.
- Accelerometer‑based movement and impact events for tamper and anti‑theft alerting.
- Backup battery and connectivity status reporting to Plaspy for offline/low‑power alerts and recovery workflows.
