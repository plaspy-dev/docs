# Astra Telematics - AT202

<div className="device-logo">
![AT202](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AT202 is a compact, high capability GPS tracker designed for modern IoT and fleet applications. It combines multi constellation GNSS support with multi network cellular communications to provide reliable positioning and broad coverage for real time tracking. The unit is built with a rugged electrical tolerance suitable for vehicle systems up to 65V and includes a modest internal backup battery, internal antennas and an integrated accelerometer to maintain reporting in low power or disconnected conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT202 can stream location and vehicle telemetry into Plaspy for unified fleet monitoring and reporting. Its rich I/O and telematics interfaces let integrators and fleet operators send CANBus channels, digital events, ADC values and serial messages into Plaspy where data is parsed, alerted on and stored for historical analysis and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Multi constellation GNSS support for dependable positioning across diverse regions.
- Multi network cellular connectivity including legacy and LPWA options for wide coverage.
- Comprehensive I/O suite including CANBus, digital inputs and outputs, ADC, RS232 and 1 Wire for telemetry and driver ID.
- Internal 900mAh backup battery and onboard accelerometer to support low power reporting and movement detection.
- Electrical tolerance up to 65V making the device suitable for a range of cars, trucks and many electric vehicle platforms.
- Available in multiple kit variants with documentation and accessory options to streamline deployment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT202 sends GNSS fixes and vehicle telemetry over cellular links to Plaspy where that data is processed for live tracking, events and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests raw inputs from the device and converts them into actionable fleet insights, alerts and standard reports that operators can use for monitoring and decision making.

- Real time location updates and movement data visible in the Plaspy dashboard.
- CANBus channel parsing for vehicle telemetry such as odometer and engine related metrics when available.
- Digital input events for door, ignition and other binary signals used in alerts and workflows.
- ADC readings for analog sensors including fuel level when a sensor is connected and configured.
- RS232 and 1 Wire support for external sensors and driver ID integration to attribute events to personnel.
- Accelerometer based movement and impact events for tamper and anti theft alerting, plus backup battery and connectivity status reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with continuous vehicle location, route replay and dispatch oversight.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using accelerometer alerts, event rules and remote output controls.
- Vehicle telemetry and fuel monitoring via CANBus channels and ADC sensor inputs for maintenance planning.
- Driver identification and access control using 1 Wire or serial connected ID devices.
- Mixed fleet asset monitoring where compact form factor and higher voltage tolerance are required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT202 is a practical option for organizations that need a compact, Plaspy compatible tracker with broad telemetry capabilities. Its combination of multi constellation GNSS, multi network cellular communications and a comprehensive interface set enables integrators to capture and forward a wide range of vehicle signals into Plaspy without extensive additional hardware. Built in resilience features such as the internal backup battery and accelerometer help maintain visibility in low power or disconnected scenarios, and the device electrical tolerance supports deployments across conventional and many electric vehicles.
