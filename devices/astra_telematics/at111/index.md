# Astra Telematics - AT111

<div className="device-logo">
![AT111](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Astra Telematics AT111 is a compact GPS tracker designed for professional telematics installations that require external antenna placement. Based on the proven AT241 platform, the AT111 provides multi network cellular connectivity and multi constellation GNSS positioning in a rugged aluminium alloy enclosure. Its feature set and form factor make it suitable for real time tracking, telemetry and asset protection across vehicles and heavy equipment.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT111 integrates into fleet management and asset monitoring workflows to deliver location, telemetry and event data into Plaspy dashboards and reports. External GNSS and GSM antenna options, accessible SIM handling and a backup battery make the unit practical for installations where antenna placement or concealed mounting are important, while lifetime system updates and a multi year warranty help keep integrations current.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for live location, telemetry and alerts integration.
- Multi network cellular connectivity with LTE M and 2G support for broad coverage.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning with external antenna options for improved reception.
- Extensive I O and vehicle interfaces including CANBus, RS232 and 1 Wire for telemetry capture.
- Built in backup battery for continued reporting during primary power loss.
- Compact, rugged aluminium alloy housing suited to professional telematics installations.
- Manufacturer warranty and ongoing system updates to support long term deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT111 transmits position, sensor and vehicle telemetry over cellular networks to Plaspy, where that data is ingested into maps, history logs, alerts and reports. Plaspy uses the telemetry and event streams from the device to provide situational awareness, operational oversight and configurable notifications for fleet and asset teams. External antenna connections let integrators place antennas for optimal reception while locating the tracker in discreet or protected locations.

- Real time location and telemetry updates streamed to Plaspy for mapping and route history.
- Event and status reporting such as ignition, door and alarm signals captured via digital inputs and CANBus.
- Vehicle telemetry and fuel related data fed into Plaspy reporting from CANBus and analog inputs.
- Remote control workflows like immobilizer commands executed through digital outputs and Plaspy command channels.
- Support for combining external sensor or gateway data so BLE or other sensor streams can be correlated with AT111 location and telemetry in Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for delivery, service and transport operations.
- Anti theft and remote immobilization for vehicles and high value assets.
- Plant and machinery tracking where external antenna placement is required for GNSS visibility.
- Fuel monitoring and engine telemetry reporting for maintenance and cost control.
- Rapid evaluation and deployment for pilots using bundled cabling and antenna options.
