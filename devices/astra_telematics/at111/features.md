# Astra Telematics - AT111 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Astra Telematics AT111 and how the device is used with Plaspy for location, telemetry and fleet management. It focuses on practical capabilities, installation considerations and the ways the AT111 can deliver value inside Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reports.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware build, hardware revision, regional model and installation method. Manufacturer implementation details and optional accessories can affect which features are present on a specific unit, so installers and integrators should confirm capabilities for each deployment.

## Feature Overview

The AT111 is a compact professional tracker built on a proven platform and designed for installations that require external antennas and rich vehicle interfaces. It combines multi network cellular connectivity, multi constellation GNSS positioning and extensive I O to support reliable real time tracking and telemetry reporting into Plaspy.

- Designed for external GNSS and GSM antenna placement to enable concealed or protected installations without sacrificing signal performance.
- Cellular connectivity with LTE M and 2G support plus NB IoT capability for broad coverage and low power options.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning to improve fix reliability in challenging environments.
- Rich I O and vehicle interfaces including CANBus, serial ports and 1 Wire for sensor and vehicle data capture.
- Internal backup battery to maintain reporting and anti theft monitoring during primary power loss.
- Rugged aluminium alloy case and professional connectors for durable field installations.

## Core Features of Astra Telematics - AT111

- 4G LTE M cellular modem with 2G fallback and NB IoT capable modes for wide area connectivity.
- Multi constellation GNSS support for GPS, Galileo, GLONASS and Beidou positioning.
- External GNSS and GSM antenna connectors to accommodate roof, glass or magnetic antenna options.
- Multiple vehicle and sensor interfaces including CANBus, two RS232 ports and 1 Wire.
- Comprehensive digital and analog I O such as digital inputs, digital outputs and ADC channels for telemetry and alarm signals.
- Built in backup battery for continued reporting during power interruptions.
- Compact, rugged aluminium alloy enclosure with external LEDs and easy Micro SIM access.
- Manufacturer backed updates and long term support to maintain integration compatibility.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AT111 forwards position fixes and available telemetry so operators can monitor vehicles and assets in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to present location, status and event information in a unified platform for operational oversight.

- Live location reporting and route history based on multi constellation GNSS fixes.
- Vehicle status and event visibility using digital inputs and CANBus telemetry for ignition, doors and alarm indicators.
- Analog and ADC readings available in Plaspy for sensor values and custom telemetry displays.
- Continued alerting and location updates during power loss thanks to the device backup battery.
- Flexible installation visibility by using external antenna placement while keeping the tracker concealed.
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols to simplify integration and reporting setup.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring where reliable GNSS reception and vehicle telemetry are required.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using discreet installation, backup battery reporting and remote outputs for immobilizer control.
- Plant and machinery tracking where antenna placement options improve satellite reception on equipment.
- Fuel and engine telemetry reporting when CANBus and ADC inputs are available in the vehicle.
- Professional telematics installations requiring durable hardware and multiple serial and sensor interfaces.
- Rapid evaluation and proof of concept deployments using available integration guides and accessory kits.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Features such as CANBus frames, serial port telemetry and ADC inputs depend on hardware revision and regional model variants.
- Firmware version can affect behaviour, available commands and reporting intervals; confirm firmware details for advanced integrations.
- Installation choices including antenna type and placement influence GNSS and GSM performance in the field.
- Some optional functions or custom reporting may require configuration changes or additional accessories from the manufacturer.
- Review manufacturer documentation for model specific limits, supported bands and optional accessories before deployment.

Using the AT111 with Plaspy provides organizations a practical path to combine rugged, professional telematics hardware with a cloud platform for visibility, monitoring and operational oversight. The device I O and vehicle interfaces enable integrators to collect location and vehicle signals that Plaspy can present as maps, alerts and historical reports for dispatch, compliance and asset protection workflows.
