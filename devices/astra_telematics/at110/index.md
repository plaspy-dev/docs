# Astra Telematics - AT110

![AT110](./logo.jpg)

The Astra Telematics AT110 GPS Tracker is a high-tech device that offers advanced features and reliable performance. It has been designed using the latest technology, including a Cortex M3 Processor, SiRFStar IV GPS, Bluetooth Low Energy, CANBus, and intelligent power/battery management. With its compact 'matchbox' dimensions and external antennas, the AT110 is easy to install and inconspicuous in appearance.

This GPS tracker is ideal for various applications, including track & trace, vehicle recovery, fleet management, and telematics. It operates on a full quadband GSM band and supports communication methods such as GPRS, TCP, and UDP. The AT110 also features a backup battery and internal memory for enhanced functionality. It can provide position updates based on time or distance and has a sleep mode for power-saving purposes.

With its durable aluminum casing and additional connectivity options like fixed antennas, driver ID, and a serial port, the Astra Telematics AT110 is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker. It is designed and manufactured in Europe, ensuring high-quality standards and performance.
