# Astra Telematics - AT110

<div className="device-logo">
![AT110](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Astra Telematics AT110 is a compact, matchbox sized GPS tracker designed for vehicle and fleet telematics. Built with modern components such as a Cortex M3 processor and SiRFStar IV GPS positioning, the AT110 combines accurate location reporting with a durable aluminum casing and external antenna options. The device supports onboard storage and a backup battery to preserve data during interruptions, and it offers communication over quadband cellular links to transmit position updates by time or distance while providing a sleep mode for power management.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT110 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet management platform to provide location visibility and operational oversight. Its support for wireless connectivity options and vehicle bus compatibility makes it suitable for organizations that need reliable tracking, historical playback from internal memory, and continuity of reporting when cellular connectivity fluctuates. Plaspy can use the AT110's reporting capabilities to build alerts, reports, and dashboards that help manage vehicles and assets in daily operations.

## Key Highlights

- Compact matchbox form factor that is easy to place discreetly in vehicles
- SiRFStar IV GPS module for reliable position fixes and consistent tracking
- Onboard backup battery and internal memory to maintain data continuity during outages
- Quadband cellular connectivity with common data modes for remote transmission of position updates
- CANBus compatibility and additional connectivity options to support vehicle monitoring needs
- Durable aluminum enclosure and external antenna options for improved reception and longevity

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the AT110 delivers location and telemetry to the platform for real time monitoring and historical review. Plaspy ingests the device's position updates and stores them alongside other fleet data to enable operational reporting and alerting.

- Live map view and position history for each AT110 equipped vehicle
- Alerts and notifications based on movement, geofencing, or configurable reporting intervals
- Historical trip reporting and playback using stored data when needed
- Visibility into vehicle bus data and driver identification where configured for richer telematics
- Reporting and export options for fleet performance and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and daily vehicle tracking for companies operating multiple vehicles
- Track and trace for high value or mobile assets requiring discreet installation
- Vehicle recovery and location reporting when an asset is lost or stolen
- Telematics projects that combine position data with vehicle bus information for maintenance planning
- Rental and leasing operations that need reliable position and usage history
