# Astra Telematics - AT502 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Astra Telematics AT502 and how the device works with Plaspy for asset tracking. It focuses on practical capabilities and operational value that matter when the AT502 is used to provide location and telemetry into Plaspy-managed fleets and inventories.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and how the device is installed. Use this page as a high level guide and refer to manufacturer documentation for the most current device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The AT502 is a compact, battery powered asset tracker designed for discreet long term deployments. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning, low power cellular connectivity, and local BLE configuration to deliver reliable location reports with minimal maintenance overhead.

- Long endurance battery options including an internal 7800 mAh pack and a user replaceable 3 x AA cell design for extended field life.
- Multi constellation GNSS support for consistent positioning across regions.
- Low power cellular connectivity with LTE‑M and NB‑IoT plus GSM fallback for broad network coverage.
- Bluetooth Low Energy for on site configuration and diagnostics using a smartphone.
- IP68 rated enclosure and multiple mounting options including magnetic mount and bolt fixings for secure, discreet installation.
- Purpose built for passive asset tracking where small size, battery life and environmental protection are primary requirements.

## Core Features of Astra Telematics - AT502

- Internal long life battery pack plus user replaceable AA cell option for flexible maintenance.
- Multi constellation GNSS support including GPS, Galileo, GLONASS and BeiDou for reliable location fixes.
- Cellular modes including LTE‑M and NB‑IoT with GSM fallback to maximize coverage across networks.
- Bluetooth Low Energy interface for local configuration and diagnostics via smartphone tools.
- IP68 ingress protection for harsh environmental conditions and rugged installations.
- Integrated mounting choices such as built in magnetic mount and M4 bolt fixings for discreet attachment.
- Minimal external I O with a design focused on long battery life and small form factor.
- Motion detection and movement reporting to support activity alerts and theft recovery workflows.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AT502 reports location and telemetry, Plaspy ingests that data to provide visibility, history and operational alerts. Plaspy presents location fixes and device health information so teams can monitor asset status and respond to movement or maintenance needs.

- Real time and periodic location updates appear in Plaspy maps and history views for playback and reporting.
- Battery level and connectivity health are reported to help schedule maintenance and replacement cycles.
- Motion events produce movement alerts in Plaspy that support anti theft and recovery workflows.
- BLE is used for on site setup and diagnostics prior to or after installation while cellular telemetry is used for cloud reporting.
- Plaspy displays device state and telemetry so operators can combine AT502 data with other assets for consolidated oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of small unpowered assets such as tools, cases and portable equipment where discreet installation is required.
- Container and pallet monitoring where periodic location checks and long battery life are priorities.
- Inventory management and rental equipment oversight with reduced maintenance trips.
- Anti theft and recovery operations leveraging motion alerts and cloud notifications.
- Large scale deployments of many small assets where cost and service intervals are important operational factors.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior can depend on firmware version and hardware revision; capabilities may change with updates.
- Regional cellular band support and variant selection affect network compatibility and should be confirmed for each deployment region.
- Installation choices influence performance; antenna placement and mounting orientation matter for GNSS and cellular reception.
- The AT502 has limited external I O and does not provide vehicle grade interfaces such as CAN or dedicated immobilizer outputs.
- Verify warranty terms, accessory options and customization possibilities with the manufacturer before procurement.

Pairing the AT502 with Plaspy gives organizations a straightforward way to turn long life, low maintenance trackers into operational visibility. Plaspy centralizes location fixes, movement alerts and device health so teams can monitor distributed assets, generate reports and respond to events without manual polling.
