# Astra Telematics - AT503 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Astra Telematics AT503 and explains how its capabilities map to Plaspy for real world asset tracking and telemetry. It is intended to help fleet managers, integrators, and technical users understand what the AT503 offers when used with Plaspy’s tracking platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes, consult Astra Telematics documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The AT503 Mini Asset Tracker is built for long life, low maintenance monitoring of unpowered assets. Its rugged, epoxy encapsulated design and internal antennas are aimed at outdoor deployments where periodic servicing is infrequent. The device emphasizes battery life, resilient connectivity, and simple field provisioning.

- Long life, low power design intended for infrequent reporting profiles and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Rugged, maintenance free construction with epoxy encapsulation and IP68 ingress protection.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning to improve fix reliability in challenging environments.
- Cellular connectivity that includes LTE‑M and NB‑IoT with 2G fallback and integrated eSIM for wide area coverage.
- Bluetooth Low Energy provisioning for on-site setup and diagnostics using a smartphone.
- Integrated movement detection to enable intelligent reporting and extend battery life.

## Core Features of Astra Telematics - AT503

- Epoxy encapsulated enclosure with IP68 rating for dust and water resistance and internal antennas to minimize ingress points.
- Ultra‑low‑power electronics and a large internal LiFeS2 battery designed to provide multi‑year operation in typical low frequency reporting modes.
- Multi constellation GNSS support including GPS, Galileo, GLONASS and Beidou for reliable positioning.
- LTE‑M and NB‑IoT cellular connectivity with GSM/GPRS 2G fallback and integrated eSIM for remote provisioning.
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for field provisioning, configuration, and diagnostics via a smartphone.
- Integrated MEMS accelerometer for movement detection and intelligent wake/report triggers to conserve battery.
- Flexible mounting options including integrated magnetic mount and mechanical bolt provisions for secure attachment.
- Designed without external ports or LEDs to maximize durability and minimize maintenance needs.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AT503 is connected to Plaspy, its location and health telemetry feed into Plaspy dashboards and maps for operational oversight. The device’s low power modes and movement driven reporting help balance timely updates with long battery life, and BLE provisioning simplifies field commissioning tied to Plaspy asset records.

- Real time location updates and telemetry are visible on Plaspy maps and location history views for each asset.
- Movement detection triggers and wake logic translate into event visibility inside Plaspy so managers can act on motion or inactivity.
- Battery and device health reporting in Plaspy help schedule maintenance or replacement well before service interruption.
- BLE provisioning streamlines on‑site setup and links the physical device to Plaspy asset records during commissioning.
- Multi constellation GNSS fixes improve positional accuracy shown in Plaspy’s tracking layers and location reports.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container monitoring where long battery life and rugged encapsulation reduce field visits.
- Construction and rental equipment tracking to monitor location and movement for utilization and recovery.
- Remote asset fleets and large scale rollouts that require simple provisioning and minimal ongoing maintenance.
- Anti‑theft and recovery workflows relying on movement triggered reporting to provide timely location telemetry.
- Long term unattended asset tracking where ignition or engine interfaces are not required.
