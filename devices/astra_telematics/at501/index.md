# Astra Telematics - AT501

<div className="device-logo">
![AT501](./tracker.png)
</div>

The AT501 Mini Asset Tracker is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker engineered for long term monitoring of small, unpowered assets. It is designed for extended deployment on tools, trailers, containers and other remote equipment, combining multi network cellular connectivity, multi constellation GNSS and ultra low power operation to provide reliable position reporting over months or years.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the AT501 delivers location and event telemetry directly into Plaspy for centralized fleet visibility. Its long life replaceable battery, rugged IP68 housing, magnetic mounting option and local configuration via Bluetooth make it a practical choice for organizations that want low maintenance asset tracking integrated with Plaspy reporting and alerting workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Long duration operation using a replaceable 13,000 mAh LTC battery pack with up to eight years in low power reporting modes.
- Plaspy compatible for direct integration of position and telemetry into fleet management, reporting and alerting.
- Multi network cellular connectivity via e SIM with LTE M and NB IoT plus 2G fallback for broad coverage.
- Multi constellation GNSS support to improve positioning reliability across environments.
- Rugged IP68 enclosure with magnetic mount and optional M4 bolt attachment for secure field installation.
- Built in accelerometer for movement based reporting and Bluetooth Low Energy for local configuration and diagnostics.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AT501 transmits position and selected telemetry to Plaspy using its multi network cellular uplink. It is optimized for low power scheduled reporting and event based uplinks triggered by movement detection, while Bluetooth provides a local channel for commissioning and diagnostics before or during registration in Plaspy.

- Real time location and telemetry when reporting intervals are set for frequent updates, allowing Plaspy to display current asset positions.
- Scheduled and event based reports preserve battery life by reducing uplinks while still providing useful history in Plaspy.
- Movement detection and event alerts from the internal accelerometer feed into Plaspy for anti theft and relocation notifications.
- Multi network uplink helps ensure telemetry reaches Plaspy across varied coverage areas where different cellular technologies are available.
- Bluetooth Low Energy enables on site device setup and diagnostics that align device behavior with Plaspy device profiles.
- Vendor provided reporting customization can be used to match AT501 telemetry to Plaspy workflows and reporting needs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of unpowered assets such as trailers, containers and attachments across large fleets.
- Monitoring high value portable tools and outdoor equipment with strong environmental protection.
- Seasonal or infrequently moved assets where extended battery life reduces maintenance cycles.
- Remote or stationary asset telemetry with movement triggered reporting to detect relocation or theft.
- Rapid deployments that use magnetic mounting or a single bolt for secure, simple installation.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT501 is a solid option for organizations that need reliable, low maintenance visibility of non powered assets. Its combination of long life battery, rugged enclosure and movement based reporting reduces field service and helps keep telemetry flowing into Plaspy for consolidation and analysis. For fleet managers focused on asset location, anti theft alerts and long term history, the AT501 aligns well with those operational goals.

This model is targeted at asset level monitoring rather than engine or vehicle diagnostics and does not provide vehicle bus interfaces or dedicated analog inputs. Plaspy integration focuses on bringing position, movement events and device health into your fleet view, and vendor customization can help ensure reporting formats and intervals match your operational requirements.
