# Astra Telematics - AT241

![AT241](./tracker.jpg)

The AT241 is a rugged GPS tracker engineered for continuous vehicle and asset monitoring. Designed for exposed installations with an IP67 sealed enclosure and a compact footprint, the AT241 provides high sensitivity GNSS positioning, multi generation cellular connectivity and a broad set of inputs and outputs to support real time tracking, telemetry and anti theft workflows in demanding fleet and security environments.

This model is fully Plaspy compatible out of the box, making it suitable for organizations that want direct integration into a fleet management platform. Plaspy can ingest GNSS coordinates, motion events, I O signals and telemetry from the AT241 to enable live maps, alerts and reporting without extensive custom integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Manufacturer stated Plaspy compatibility for straightforward onboarding into fleet and asset management workflows.
- IP67 rated rugged housing suitable for exposed vehicle and outdoor asset installations.
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning with support for multiple satellite systems for reliable location in varied environments.
- Multi generation cellular connectivity and NB IoT readiness to support wide area telemetry and future connectivity options.
- Rich I O set including digital inputs, digital outputs, ADC inputs and vehicle bus connectivity for flexible sensor and accessory integration.
- Built in motion sensing and tamper detection to support anti theft alerts and motion based reporting.
- Internal backup power, lifetime system updates and a five year warranty for long term deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the AT241 supplies continuous location, telemetry and event data that feed Plaspy dashboards, alerts and historical playback. Plaspy maps device signals to platform events so operators can monitor vehicle state, respond to incidents and generate operational reports from a single interface.

- Real time location updates and historical route playback displayed on Plaspy live maps.
- Digital inputs mapped to Plaspy events for ignition, door or alarm state reporting where those signals are present.
- Motion and tamper alerts from the device routed into Plaspy alarm workflows for immediate notification and follow up.
- Telemetry such as analog sensor readings forwarded to Plaspy for inclusion in scheduled reports and dashboards.
- Remote output control and alarm actions tied to Plaspy rules to support immobilizer or auxiliary functions depending on installation.
- Local sensor and beacon information available via optional local interfaces and represented inside Plaspy for richer situational awareness.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live GPS data, driver identification events and telemetry for route oversight and compliance monitoring.
- Vehicle sharing platforms requiring tamper detection, remote locking or driver tag detection to automate rental workflows.
- Heavy equipment monitoring where waterproof construction and wide operating ranges are required for construction or agricultural machinery.
- Security and asset protection using motion based alerts, tamper notifications and remote output control integrated into Plaspy alarms.
- Sensorized assets that benefit from ADC inputs and local sensor integrations for fuel level, temperature or other analog telemetry.
