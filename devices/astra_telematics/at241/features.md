# Astra Telematics - AT241 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the Astra Telematics AT241 when used with Plaspy. It highlights the tracker capabilities that are most relevant to vehicle and asset monitoring, and how those capabilities map into Plaspy for real time tracking, event visibility and operational workflows.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and installation method. For device specific configuration, supported variants, and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from Astra Telematics.

## Feature Overview

The AT241 is a rugged, compact GPS tracker designed for continuous vehicle and asset monitoring and is shipped Plaspy compatible out of the box. It combines high sensitivity GNSS positioning, multi generation cellular connectivity and a broad set of inputs and outputs to support tracking, telemetry and anti theft workflows in demanding environments.

- IP67 rated housing for exposed installations where water and dust resistance matter.
- Multi generation cellular support with a focus on long term telemetry connectivity and future ready narrowband options.
- Multi constellation GNSS reception for improved location reliability in challenging environments.
- Extensive I O and serial interfaces to connect ignition signals, sensors and accessories for mapped events.
- Tamper detection and motion sensing for anti theft alerts and impact or movement based reporting.

## Core Features of Astra Telematics - AT241

- Plaspy compatible out of the box for straightforward onboarding into real time tracking and fleet management.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure suitable for exposed vehicle and asset installations.
- High sensitivity GNSS with support for GPS, Galileo, GLONASS and BeiDou for reliable positioning.
- Multi generation cellular connectivity including LTE M and 2G support with NB IoT readiness for future deployments.
- Comprehensive I O set including digital inputs and outputs, ADC inputs, RS232 ports, 1 Wire support and CANBus for accessory integration.
- MEMS accelerometer and tamper detection for motion based alerts and anti theft workflows.
- Optional Bluetooth Low Energy interface for local sensor or beacon integrations and driver tag detection.
- Internal backup power with extended standby and manufacturer provided lifetime system updates and a five year warranty.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the AT241 reports location, motion and I O events that Plaspy uses to populate live maps, event timelines and scheduled reports. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding and lets you map hardware signals to platform events and alarms.

- Real time GNSS coordinates and historical playback for route review and location verification in Plaspy.
- Motion and tamper events reflected as alerts or timeline entries to support anti theft and incident review.
- Digital inputs and ADC readings mapped to Plaspy events for ignition, door status or sensor telemetry where those signals are present.
- Driver ID and BLE based proximity events usable in Plaspy workflows for driver attribution and access control scenarios.
- Serial and CANBus telemetry forwarded to Plaspy where supported by the installation to enrich vehicle or equipment telemetry.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for live vehicle tracking, route analysis and driver event monitoring.
- Vehicle sharing and rental platforms using tamper detection and driver tag workflows for secure access.
- Heavy equipment telematics where rugged, waterproof hardware and wide voltage support are needed.
- Security and anti theft applications leveraging motion detection, tamper alerts and remote event monitoring.
- Sensorized asset tracking that uses ADC inputs and optional BLE sensors for fuel, temperature or proximity monitoring.
- Long term deployments that benefit from lifetime system updates and a durable enclosure.
