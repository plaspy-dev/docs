# Astra Telematics - AT402

![AT402](./tracker.jpg)

The Astra Telematics AT402 is a compact, automotive ready GPS tracker designed for moto sharing and broader connected vehicle deployments. Built on the AT400 series, the AT402 combines LTE M primary connectivity with GSM GPRS 2G fallback to provide continuous position reporting and vehicle telemetry. The unit is IP65 rated, includes internal GNSS and cellular antennas, and uses an automotive style wiring connector to simplify production or aftermarket fitment.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AT402 delivers the kinds of location and vehicle data that Plaspy uses for real time tracking, fleet oversight, and anti theft monitoring. Its support for vehicle interfaces, local wireless pairing, digital inputs and an internal backup battery make it suitable for shared mobility services and light commercial fleets that need resilient tracking and integration flexibility with the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker with LTE M primary connectivity and GSM GPRS 2G fallback for reliable reporting.
- Rugged IP65 automotive ready housing with internal GNSS and cellular antennas for simplified installation.
- Broad vehicle interface support including CANBus, RS232 and 1 Wire plus driver ID and digital I O for telemetry and event capture.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support for local sensor pairing and short range interactions.
- Internal backup battery and wide operating voltage range for resilience in anti theft and shared mobility scenarios.
- Designed for both production and aftermarket fitment with automotive style connector and compact form factor.
- Astra offered warranty and ongoing system updates to support deployment scale and long term operation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the AT402 forwards GNSS positions and vehicle telemetry to the Plaspy cloud where that data is processed for live tracking, alerts, geofencing and reporting. Plaspy combines the device streams with platform rules and dashboards so operators can monitor fleet status, receive theft alarms, and generate operational analytics.

- Real time location and position updates for live fleet visibility.
- Ignition and digital input monitoring to track start stop events and trigger alarms.
- Vehicle telemetry via CANBus and serial interfaces for fleet health and performance reporting when vehicle data is available.
- Remote actions and control through configurable digital outputs coordinated by Plaspy rules.
- BLE pairing for local sensor data and proximity based interactions to support shared mobility workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Moto sharing and micromobility fleets that need compact, weather resistant devices with local pairing capability.
- Light commercial and service vehicle fleets requiring reliable real time tracking and vehicle telemetry.
- Anti theft protection and remote immobilization managed through ignition inputs and digital outputs.
- Fuel monitoring and vehicle health reporting by forwarding available vehicle bus parameters to Plaspy.
- Aftermarket installations and scaled production rollouts where simplified wiring and evaluation kits speed integration.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AT402 is a practical choice for organizations that need a durable, integration friendly tracker for shared mobility or fleet operations. Its combination of low power cellular connectivity with 2G fallback, vehicle interface options and local wireless pairing gives Plaspy customers the flexibility to capture location, driver events and vehicle data in a single device. The compact IP65 design and automotive connector make it suitable for both retrofit and production environments, while the internal backup battery and wide voltage range improve resilience for anti theft and service continuity.
