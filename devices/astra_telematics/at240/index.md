# Astra Telematics - AT240

![AT240](./logo.jpg)

The Astra Telematics AT240 is a highly advanced GPS tracker that incorporates the latest technology to provide accurate and reliable tracking capabilities. Powered by a Cortex M3 Processor and equipped with a SiRFStar IV GPS chip, this tracker ensures precise location tracking at all times. Additionally, it features Bluetooth Low Energy and CANBus technology, making it compatible with a wide range of devices and vehicles.

Designed with both functionality and aesthetics in mind, the AT240 boasts automotive looks and an IP67 waterproof sealing, making it suitable for installation in various environments. Its integrated antennas further enhance its inconspicuous appearance, ensuring that it remains discreet while providing exceptional tracking performance.

With its versatile features and robust construction, the Astra Telematics AT240 is ideal for a range of applications, including track and trace, vehicle recovery, fleet management, and telematics. Whether you need to monitor a single vehicle or an entire fleet, this GPS tracker offers the reliability and functionality you need to effectively manage your assets.

### Outstanding Features:

- Cortex M3 Processor for efficient performance
- SiRFStar IV GPS chip for accurate location tracking
- Bluetooth Low Energy for compatibility with various devices
- CANBus technology for seamless integration with vehicles
- IP67 waterproof sealing for durability in harsh environments
- Integrated antennas for inconspicuous installation
