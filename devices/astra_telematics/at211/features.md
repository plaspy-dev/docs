# Astra Telematics - AT211 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Astra Telematics AT211 and describes how the device can be used with Plaspy for fleet, plant, and asset monitoring. It focuses on the practical capabilities Plaspy users will encounter when integrating the AT211 into their monitoring stack and how those capabilities translate into visibility and event handling in Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where a capability is optional or variant dependent, verify the specific device configuration and firmware release with the manufacturer before purchase or deployment.

## Feature Overview

The AT211 is a rugged IP67 GPS tracker built for plant, machinery, and other outdoor assets. It combines multi-network cellular connectivity, GNSS location, compact rugged packaging, and a small internal backup battery to support reliable location and event reporting in harsh environments.

- IP67 rated enclosure for water and dust resistance suitable for outdoor plant and machinery.
- Multi-network cellular connectivity including GSM/GPRS and low power wide area networks LTE Cat‑M1 and NB‑IoT for broad coverage.
- Internal GNSS with optional external 25 mm GNSS patch antenna for improved reception on metal-bodied equipment.
- 510 mAh internal backup battery to support reporting during power loss or tamper scenarios.
- Flexible I/O and interfaces including 2 digital inputs, 2 digital outputs, RS232 via DB9 connector, and a 1‑Wire interface for compatible peripherals.
- Optional BLE for immobiliser and driver identification workflows plus an integrated MEMS accelerometer and status LEDs for movement detection and diagnostics.

## Core Features of Astra Telematics - AT211

- Rugged IP67 ingress protection for installation on construction, agricultural, and heavy plant equipment.
- Cellular connectivity across GSM/GPRS, LTE Cat‑M1, and NB‑IoT networks to balance coverage and low power operation.
- Multi‑GNSS positioning support via internal antenna with an option for an external GNSS patch antenna for improved fixes on metal surfaces.
- 510 mAh internal backup battery for continued periodic reporting during power interruptions.
- MEMS accelerometer for movement and impact detection and internal LEDs for installation diagnostics.
- Flexible physical interfaces: 2 digital inputs, 2 digital outputs, RS232 DB9 connector, and 1‑Wire support for external peripherals.
- Optional Bluetooth Low Energy to enable immobiliser and driver identification features when paired with Astra mobile solutions.
- Wide operating voltage range to accommodate diverse vehicle and machinery power systems.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AT211 reports position, movement events, and input/output status so fleet managers and operators can monitor assets and respond to incidents. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and accepts device reports, which helps simplify onboarding and reduces configuration time.

- Real‑time GNSS position updates and historical location traces visible in Plaspy dashboards and maps.
- Movement and impact events derived from the onboard accelerometer available as alerts and events in Plaspy.
- Digital input status and digital output control reflected in Plaspy for monitoring switches, ignition, or alarm circuits.
- BLE related events such as immobiliser state or driver identification can be surfaced in Plaspy when configured and supported by the device firmware.
- Backup battery and low‑power reporting help maintain event visibility in Plaspy during power loss or tamper conditions.

## Typical Use Cases

- Security and recovery of plant and heavy equipment on remote job sites using movement alerts and BLE immobiliser workflows.
- Tow and movement detection for stolen vehicle response and operator notifications.
- Fleet and equipment management for construction and agriculture with location awareness and basic telemetry for utilisation planning.
- Weather resistant tracking for caravans, motorcycles, and other exposed vehicles.
- Remote asset monitoring in harsh environments where ingress protection and wide voltage tolerance are required.
