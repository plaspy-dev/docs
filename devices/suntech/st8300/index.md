# Suntech - ST8300

![ST8300](./tracker.jpg)

The Suntech ST8300 Series is a professional grade GPS tracker designed for vehicle and industrial monitoring where durable hardware and continuous positioning matter. The family includes multiple variants and combines persistent GNSS positioning with resilient cellular connectivity, over the air configuration and rugged construction intended for fleet and asset deployments. Its feature set is aimed at operational visibility, theft recovery and telemetry collection across mixed vehicle fleets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST8300 Series can feed real time location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring, alerting and reporting. Plaspy can ingest the tracker’s position fixes, motion derived events and wired I O statuses to provide dispatchers and managers with live situational awareness and historical analysis, while remote configuration capabilities simplify device lifecycle management across large deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time tracking and fleet monitoring into Plaspy dashboards and reports
- Resilient cellular connectivity with fallback to maintain continuous reporting across coverage variations
- Robust GNSS performance with GPS plus GLONASS and SBAS support for precise positioning
- OTA configuration and firmware updates to streamline remote device provisioning and maintenance
- Flexible I O and variant options to integrate vehicle signals and external sensors where required
- Integrated motion analysis and jamming detection to support driver behavior review and security workflows
- Rugged design and wide input range suited to vehicle and industrial environments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the ST8300 Series supplies location fixes, event notifications and telemetry that integrate into Plaspy’s real time monitoring and historical reporting workflows. Plaspy uses the device data to power maps, alerts and operational insights so teams can track assets, respond to incidents and review fleet performance without manual data collection.

- Real time location updates and location history for routing, ETA estimates and replay
- Wired I O and status events reported into Plaspy for ignition, door, alarm or custom signals
- Alerts and events such as geofence breaches and jamming detection delivered to Plaspy for immediate notification
- Motion and accelerometer derived insights used for driver behavior analysis and incident review
- Remote device configuration and firmware synchronization to maintain consistent behavior across a fleet

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and live dispatch for buses vans and service vehicles
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery with geofence and tamper detection support
- Driver safety and coaching programs using motion and DPA insights
- OEM and telemetry integrations where multiple wired inputs and serial options are required
- Cargo and asset monitoring leveraging optional proximity and sensor inputs
