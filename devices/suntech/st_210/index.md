# Suntech - ST 210

![ST 210](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST 210 is a vehicle GPS tracker that combines satellite positioning with cellular data to provide real time location monitoring. Designed for track and trace, fleet management, vehicle recovery and in car navigation, the ST 210 includes features commonly expected in fleet devices such as geo fencing, two way voice capability, internal memory and a backup battery to preserve data during power interruptions. The unit is supplied in a durable plastic casing with built in antennas and offers additional connectivity options for external microphones, speakers, and fixed antennas.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 210 can feed live location and event information into the Plaspy platform to support visibility and operational oversight. Its geo fencing and alerting features map directly to common Plaspy workflows for zone monitoring and instant notifications. The device characteristics that preserve data continuity, such as internal memory and backup power, help maintain reliable reporting and historical playback within Plaspy even when vehicle power is disrupted.

## Key Highlights

- Real time vehicle tracking using GPS position and cellular data transmission for continuous visibility.
- Geo fencing capability with instant alerts when vehicles enter or exit predefined zones.
- Two way voice support that can aid driver communication and operational coordination.
- Internal memory and backup battery to retain location data during power interruptions.
- Multiple digital inputs including ignition detection to support basic event monitoring.
- Durable plastic enclosure with internal antennas and optional external connectivity for flexible fitment.

When paired with Plaspy, the ST 210 supplies location and event information to the platform so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it alongside other fleet telematics for consolidated oversight.
