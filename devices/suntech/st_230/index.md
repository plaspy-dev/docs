# Suntech - ST 230

![ST 230](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST230 is a standalone GPS tracker designed for tracking and monitoring high-value mobile assets such as trailers, containers, trains, or yachts. With its plastic cover and IP67 waterproof rating, the ST230 is built to withstand harsh environments and ensure reliable performance even in challenging conditions. It features a 5200mAh internal battery, providing long-lasting power for extended tracking periods.

One of the standout features of the Suntech ST230 is its advanced functionality. It comes equipped with 4 digital inputs, allowing you to control various aspects such as panic buttons, ignition, or external sensors. This gives you greater control and flexibility in managing your assets. Additionally, the ST230 supports communication methods such as GPRS, TCP, and UDP, ensuring seamless and efficient data transmission.

With its comprehensive range of features and durable construction, the Suntech ST230 is an ideal choice for track and trace applications, vehicle recovery, and fleet management. Whether you need to monitor the location of your assets or ensure their security, the ST230 offers the reliability and performance you need.

### Key Features:

- Plastic cover with IP67 waterproof rating
- 5200mAh internal battery for extended tracking periods
- 4 digital inputs for controlling panic buttons, ignition, or external sensors
- Supports communication methods such as GPRS, TCP, and UDP
- Ideal for track and trace, vehicle recovery, and fleet management
