# Suntech - ST 230

<div className="device-logo">
![ST 230](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Suntech ST 230 is a standalone GPS tracker built for tracking and monitoring high value mobile assets such as trailers, containers, trains, and yachts. It combines a rugged plastic cover with an IP67 waterproof rating and a 5200mAh internal battery to provide extended operation in challenging outdoor environments. The device also includes four digital inputs for event or status monitoring and supports common data transport methods to transmit position and activity information.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 230 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet and asset management workflows to provide persistent visibility and operational oversight. Its durable enclosure and long lasting power pack make it appropriate for unattended or hard to access assets, while the digital inputs let Plaspy users receive event driven signals alongside location updates for improved situational awareness.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure suitable for outdoor and marine environments
- Large internal battery designed for extended tracking intervals
- Four digital inputs for external event monitoring such as panic or status signals
- Standalone design ideal for non powered or mobile assets like trailers and containers
- Supports common data transport methods to relay tracking data to platforms
- Suited for long duration deployments where maintenance access is limited

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the Suntech ST 230 sends location and event information to the platform so teams can monitor assets, review history, and act on alerts. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it alongside other fleet information to support operational decision making.

- Live and historical position visibility for tracked assets within Plaspy dashboards
- Event based alerts using the ST 230 digital inputs for panic notifications or external triggers
- Reduced maintenance overhead thanks to the device's long battery life and reliable uptime
- Improved asset protection for trailers containers trains and yachts through continuous monitoring
- Integration into Plaspy reporting and operational workflows for oversight and audits

## Typical Use Cases

- Track trailers and container movements during intermodal transport
- Monitor rail rolling stock and transport wagons for location and status
- Provide location awareness and theft recovery support for yachts and marine assets
- Manage dispersed or long term unattended equipment where battery life is critical
- Support vehicle recovery and high value asset protection programs
