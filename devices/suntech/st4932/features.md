# Suntech - ST4932 Features

This page provides a public, features-focused overview of the Suntech ST4932 and how its capabilities are presented when used with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical, non-sensitive functions that matter to operators, managers, and integrators who plan to monitor vehicles and assets with Plaspy’s platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional SKU, and installation style. For deployment planning please treat the content here as a high-level feature guide and verify device-specific details with manufacturer documentation and your Plaspy configuration team.

## Feature Overview

The ST4932 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible tracker designed for long-term asset monitoring and professional fleet use. It combines low-power wide area cellular connectivity, multi-constellation GNSS positioning, and local BLE gateway capabilities to deliver location intelligence and event telemetry for diverse operational workflows.

- Reliable cellular connectivity with LTE Cat M1 and NB2 plus 2G fallback for broad coverage in mixed network environments.
- Concurrent multi-constellation GNSS positioning (GPS + GLONASS with optional additional constellations) for high-accuracy location reporting.
- Integrated BLE gateway to extend telemetry with short-range sensors and proximity detection.
- Motion and tamper awareness through an integrated accelerometer and light-sensor based tamper detection.
- Long-life rechargeable backup battery and power saving modes for infrequent reporting and extended deployments.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and industrial operating range suitable for vehicle and asset mounting.

## Core Features of Suntech - ST4932

- LTE Cat M1 / NB2 cellular modem with EGPRS 2G fallback to maintain connectivity where newer networks are unavailable.
- Concurrent GNSS reception including GPS and GLONASS with optional support for additional constellations for improved positioning accuracy.
- BLE gateway functionality to capture proximity events and aggregate data from Bluetooth sensors and tags.
- Long-life rechargeable backup battery combined with ultra-low power operating modes for multi-year reporting in low-frequency scenarios.
- 6-axis accelerometer for motion detection and collision-related event reporting.
- Light-sensor tamper detection to notify of potential enclosure opening or interference.
- Configurable digital I/O for integration with ignition, door, and other vehicle or asset signals to provide event-based telemetry.
- Industrial design with IP67 rating and a wide operating temperature range for harsh environments; device supports remote configuration and provisioning.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the ST4932’s location fixes and event telemetry to provide continuous visibility, historical playback, and actionable alerts. Plaspy automatically detects the device protocol and consolidates reported GNSS, motion, BLE, and I/O events so operators can act on a single operational view.

- GNSS fixes appear on Plaspy maps and history for real-time tracking and route analysis.
- Motion and collision events from the accelerometer can be surfaced as alerts and used in incident workflows.
- Tamper signals and light-sensor events are presented as notifications to help protect assets and trigger recovery procedures.
- BLE proximity events and sensor data are available in telemetry streams for short-range asset association or environmental monitoring.
- Configurable I/O events such as ignition or door state are recorded and searchable within Plaspy for route-based reporting and rule triggers.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and dispatch with real-time tracking, route history, and event-driven reporting for vehicles.
- Container and trailer monitoring where rugged enclosure and long battery life support long deployments.
- Asset recovery and anti-theft workflows using motion, tamper alerts, and proximity data to accelerate location and retrieval.
- Remote equipment telemetry that leverages BLE sensors and configurable I/O for environmental or status monitoring.
- Low-reporting, long-term tracking for seasonal or infrastructure assets requiring multi-year battery autonomy.
