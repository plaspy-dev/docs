# Suntech - ST25T

![ST25T](./tracker.jpg)

The ST25T is a compact vehicle telematics interface module designed to extend Plaspy compatible fleet tracking and telemetry solutions. It forwards vehicle bus and sensor data to gateway devices such as the ST4305 and ST8300, enabling integration of legacy serial equipment, fuel sensors and standard vehicle networks like J1939 and J1708 into a unified fleet workflow. Its small form factor, wide operating temperature range and low power draw make it suitable for heavy trucks, buses and other commercial vehicles operating in demanding environments.

As a Plaspy compatible interface, the ST25T is intended to enrich gateway telemetry rather than function as a standalone GPS tracker. When paired with a Plaspy enabled gateway, it delivers the vehicle and sensor signals required for real time tracking, fuel monitoring and anti theft alerting across Plaspy dashboards. This role makes the ST25T relevant for fleets that need robust telemetry forwarding, legacy device integration and reliable sensor inputs within a Plaspy managed deployment.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible telemetry interface that forwards vehicle bus and sensor data to gateway devices for real time fleet management.
- Multiple serial interfaces to connect legacy equipment and third party subsystems without redesigning gateway hardware.
- Flexible I O with ADC or digital inputs for ignition detection, door or alarm events and other telemetry signals.
- Optional Bluetooth 4.1 support for local configuration and accessory pairing during installation and maintenance.
- Low power operation and compact packaging suited to continuous vehicle deployments.
- Rugged design with a wide operating temperature range for heavy trucks buses and commercial vehicles.
- Focused on telemetry aggregation rather than acting as a standalone GNSS tracker.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ST25T operates as a telemetry aggregator that supplies vehicle bus messages, fuel readings and auxiliary sensor inputs to a Plaspy enabled gateway. Plaspy ingests the combined stream from the gateway and the ST25T to provide unified visibility, alerts and reporting across a fleet in real time.

- Real time telemetry forwarding — vehicle bus messages and serial data are relayed to the gateway for live monitoring and historical reporting in Plaspy.
- Fuel monitoring support — fuel sensor data can be forwarded via the ST25T to Plaspy for consumption analysis and anomaly detection.
- Event and status inputs — ADC or digital inputs capture ignition status, door events and alarm triggers that feed trip, idle and anti theft workflows in Plaspy.
- Local configuration and accessory pairing — optional Bluetooth allows technicians to pair configuration tools or local BLE sensors during service.
- Legacy equipment integration — multiple serial channels let fleets retain onboard devices such as ticket printers or other subsystems while providing data to Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Enhancing fleet telemetry by forwarding engine and vehicle bus data into Plaspy for operational reporting.
- Fuel level monitoring and discrepancy detection using dedicated fuel sensor interfaces.
- Integrating legacy serial devices and onboard accessories without replacing existing hardware.
- Capturing ignition, door and alarm events for trip tagging, idle analysis and security alerts.
- Local configuration and BLE sensor pairing during installation or routine maintenance.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the ST25T as part of a Plaspy compatible solution makes sense when the primary need is telemetry aggregation rather than standalone GNSS tracking. The unit is built to forward vehicle bus and sensor signals into a gateway that provides GNSS and connectivity, enabling Plaspy to deliver comprehensive situational awareness, alerting and reporting across mixed vehicle fleets.

For organizations that require fuel monitoring, diagnostic forwarding or support for multiple serial devices, the ST25T simplifies integration and preserves investments in legacy equipment while fitting naturally into Plaspy managed workflows. Its rugged design and low power characteristics support deployment in heavy duty vehicles operating in challenging conditions.
