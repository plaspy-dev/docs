# ST4410G

The ST4410G is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for carton- and asset-level tracking where long battery life, robust wireless connectivity and integrated RF sensing matter. Combining LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT with 2G fallback, a multi‑constellation GNSS receiver and a 433–435 MHz RF receiver, the ST4410G delivers reliable real-time tracking, movement detection and telemetry in demanding logistics and supply chain environments.

Engineered for easy integration with Plaspy, the ST4410G is IP67-rated, lightweight and optimized for low-power operation. Its internal antenna and rechargeable 3.7V 2750 mAh battery simplify installation in cartons or portable assets while providing configurable reporting modes to maximize autonomy without sacrificing position accuracy or event-based alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and telemetry via TCP/UDP to your backend.
- Multi-mode cellular connectivity: LTE Cat M1, NB‑IoT and 2G fallback for broad global coverage.
- Integrated RF receiver \(433–435 MHz\) to support tag reading and RF sensor network integration.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/BeiDou\) with SBAS support and typical ±2 m CEP accuracy.
- Long battery life: internal 3.7V, 2750 mAh Li‑ion backup battery with configurable power profiles \(up to 60 days in stock mode\).
- Rugged IP67 enclosure \(100 × 49.5 × 18.5 mm, 78.8 g\) for carton and asset mounting in logistics workflows.
- Motion sensing \(Bosch SMA131\) for reliable movement detection and event-driven reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the ST4410G feeds GNSS position, RF tag events and motion telemetry into Plaspy’s platform for real-time tracking, alerts and analytics. The tracker transmits data over TCP/UDP using LTE Cat M1, NB‑IoT or 2G networks. Plaspy ingests these messages, decodes the payload, and makes location, battery and event states available in dashboards, maps and integrations.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT / 2G and TCP/UDP transmission.
- RF event reporting from the 433–435 MHz receiver for carton- or tag-level detection and inventory correlation.
- Motion-based alerts using the integrated Bosch SMA131 sensor for movement, tamper or activity detection.
- Battery and charge-state telemetry to track remaining autonomy and trigger maintenance workflows.
- Configurable reporting intervals and power profiles to balance tracking fidelity and battery life for Plaspy dashboards and APIs.

## Use Cases

- Carton-level shipment tracking in multimodal logistics where long battery life and waterproofing are required.
- Asset monitoring for portable equipment or inventory that benefits from RF tag integration and movement detection.
- Supply chain and warehouse operations requiring carton-to-pallet correlation and inventory reconciliation via RF events.
- Temporary deployments for rental equipment or field assets that need easy mounting, internal antenna and long standby autonomy.
- Complementary telemetry for fleet management programs where carton- or asset-level visibility augments vehicle trackers and reporting.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the ST4410G with Plaspy gives teams an efficient way to add carton- and asset-level visibility to their tracking strategy. The device’s multi-mode cellular connectivity and TCP/UDP transport ensure data reaches Plaspy reliably across different network environments. Integrated RF sensing and a sensitive motion sensor let you correlate RF tag reads with movement events, improving inventory accuracy and anti-theft workflows without installing extra gateways.

Operational value is clear for telemetry-driven deployments: configurable reporting intervals, tailored power profiles and clear battery-state telemetry let you optimize for cost and autonomy. The IP67-rated enclosure and compact form factor simplify installation in cartons or on portable assets, while Plaspy’s platform converts raw GNSS, RF and motion data into real-time maps, alerts and reports for supply chain managers, logistics operators and asset owners.

Note: The ST4410G focuses on GNSS, RF sensing and motion telemetry. If your solution requires ignition inputs, immobilizer control, fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensors, Plaspy can integrate those data streams when your deployment includes devices or interfaces that provide them—combining multiple device types into a single operational dashboard.
