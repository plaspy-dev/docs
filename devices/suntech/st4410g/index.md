# Suntech - ST4410G

<div className="device-logo">
![ST4410G](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The ST4410G is a compact GPS tracker designed for carton and asset level tracking in logistics and supply chain environments. It combines multi mode cellular connectivity with a multi constellation GNSS receiver and an integrated RF receiver to provide location reporting, tag event detection and motion based telemetry for portable assets and packaged shipments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST4410G is engineered for easy integration with cloud tracking platforms. Its rugged IP67 enclosure, internal antenna and configurable power profiles make it a practical choice for deployments where long standby life, reliable wireless links and event driven reporting are important for operational visibility within Plaspy dashboards and reports.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless telemetry and position reporting into Plaspy workflows and dashboards.
- Multi mode cellular connectivity for broad coverage across modern cellular networks with fallback options.
- Integrated 433–435 MHz RF receiver to capture tag events and support carton level correlation.
- Multi constellation GNSS with SBAS support and typical position accuracy suitable for asset tracking.
- Long battery life with a rechargeable internal battery and configurable power profiles to extend autonomy.
- Rugged IP67 rated enclosure and compact form factor suitable for carton and portable asset mounting.
- Integrated motion sensing for reliable movement detection and event driven updates.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the ST4410G transmits GNSS positions, RF tag events and motion telemetry so Plaspy can present real time location, alerts and historical activity. Plaspy ingests and decodes device messages and makes state, battery and event information available in maps, reports and integrations for operational teams.

- Real time location and telemetry updates visible in Plaspy maps and live tracking views.
- RF event reporting to correlate tag reads with cartons or inventory items inside the Plaspy platform.
- Motion triggered alerts and activity detection to generate notifications for movement or tamper events.
- Battery and charge state monitoring to support maintenance and replacement workflows in Plaspy.
- Configurable reporting intervals and power profiles to balance tracking fidelity and battery life for reporting and analytics.

## Typical Use Cases

- Carton level shipment tracking in multimodal logistics where waterproofing and long autonomy are required.
- Asset monitoring for portable equipment or inventory that benefits from RF tag integration and movement detection.
- Warehouse and supply chain operations needing carton to pallet correlation and inventory reconciliation.
- Temporary or rental equipment deployments that require easy mounting, internal antenna and long standby life.
- Supplemental visibility where carton or asset level data augments vehicle tracking and fleet reports.

The ST4410G is a practical option for teams looking to add carton and small asset visibility to a Plaspy deployment. Its combination of GNSS positioning, RF sensing and motion telemetry helps reduce manual inventory checks and supports event driven workflows without adding complex infrastructure. Configurable reporting modes and clear battery telemetry let operators tune the device for longer autonomy or higher reporting fidelity depending on operational priorities.
