# Suntech - ST6560 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Suntech ST6560 and how its capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on the practical, non sensitive aspects of the device that fleet operators and integrators commonly ask about when pairing an OBD II tracker with Plaspy for location, diagnostics, and telemetry visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on the device firmware, hardware revision, vehicle compatibility, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a high level guide to what the ST6560 can offer with Plaspy and consult official Suntech documentation for the definitive device details.

## Feature Overview

The ST6560 is a plug and play OBD II tracker designed to deliver vehicle location and engine telemetry to cloud platforms such as Plaspy. Its combination of multi network cellular connectivity, multi constellation GNSS and OBD II / J1939 data access is aimed at fleets that need continuous position reporting and actionable vehicle diagnostics.

- Simple OBD II plug in installation for fast deployment and access to vehicle signals and identification.
- Multi network cellular connectivity for resilient reporting across varying coverage areas.
- Multi constellation GNSS plus Dead Reckoning to maintain position continuity in tunnels and urban canyons.
- Deep OBD II diagnostics and DTC reporting to surface engine faults and vehicle telemetry.
- J1939 and FMS support for heavy duty and commercial vehicle telemetry integration.
- BLE 5.3 support for pairing local sensors that extend on board telemetry such as temperature or cargo sensors.

## Core Features of Suntech - ST6560

- OBD II J1962 plug and play connection providing vehicle identification, PID polling and diagnostic trouble code access.
- Multi network cellular design including LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT with 2G fallback for broad connectivity resilience.
- Multi constellation GNSS (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou) combined with Dead Reckoning for improved tracking continuity.
- J1939 ELD and FMS protocol support for heavy vehicles and commercial fleet telemetry.
- BLE 5.3 radio for local wireless sensors and accessory integration.
- Motion sensing and orientation awareness via integrated accelerometer and gyroscope for movement detection.
- Geofence support including circular and polygonal zones for automated entry and exit events.
- Compact form factor with a small backup battery to preserve basic reporting during brief power interruptions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and telemetry from compatible trackers like the ST6560 to provide live maps, historical playback, alerts, and reporting. When an ST6560 is installed and reporting, Plaspy surfaces vehicle position and the available diagnostics so fleet managers can monitor status and trends from a single platform.

- Real time GPS positions and historical tracks appear in Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight and playback.
- OBD II engine data and DTCs can be displayed as vehicle status indicators and incorporated into maintenance workflows.
- J1939 and FMS message support allows Plaspy to show heavy vehicle telemetry where that data is available from the vehicle network.
- Geofence events generated by the tracker feed into Plaspy alerts for route compliance and site entry/exit notifications.
- BLE sensor data paired to the tracker is relayed to Plaspy as supplemental telemetry when supported by the device.
- Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols automatically and presents incoming telemetry without requiring users to manage low level protocol details.

## Typical Use Cases

- Centralized fleet tracking for route visibility, dispatching, and historical trip analysis.
- Preventive maintenance programs using OBD II data and DTC alerts to reduce downtime.
- Heavy vehicle telematics for long haul fleets leveraging J1939 and FMS telemetry in Plaspy reports.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using discreet OBD II installation, live location and geofence alerts.
- Sensor augmented cargo monitoring by pairing BLE sensors to the tracker and viewing sensor data in Plaspy.
- Telematics pilot projects that require compact, deployable OBD II devices with rich telemetry for evaluation.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and software revisions can change which PID sets, J1939 messages, or sensor types are supported; verify the feature list for your device revision.
- Vehicle make, model and model year affect which OBD II PIDs and J1939 messages are exposed by the vehicle ECU.
- Regional device variants may differ by cellular bands and certified features; confirm the hardware variant intended for your deployment.
- BLE sensor compatibility depends on the sensor profile and firmware support on the tracker.
- Geofence precision and Dead Reckoning performance depend on installation location, antenna mounting and GNSS signal conditions.
