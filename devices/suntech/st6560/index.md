# Suntech - ST6560

<div className="device-logo">
![ST6560](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The ST6560 is a compact OBD II GPS tracker designed to provide vehicle level diagnostics and continuous location reporting. Built for plug and play installation into the vehicle 16 pin J1962 connector, the device is positioned for fleet management, anti theft recovery and telematics projects that need an integrated diagnostics and tracking solution. The model description highlights multi network resilience and multi constellation GNSS with Dead Reckoning to maintain position reporting in challenging environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST6560 delivers standardized telemetry and engine information that integrates into Plaspy dashboards and reporting workflows. Its ability to expose OBD II data, J1939 and FMS signals, plus local BLE sensor input, makes the unit relevant for fleets and operators who want diagnostic visibility, geofence alerts and continuous tracking inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD II form factor that provides vehicle diagnostics together with position reporting.
- Multi network cellular design for wider coverage and resilient connectivity across regions.
- Multi constellation GNSS plus Dead Reckoning for improved continuity of location in tunnels and urban canyons.
- Deep access to engine diagnostics and DTC information for maintenance and fault detection.
- Support for J1939 ELD and FMS messages to accommodate heavy duty vehicle telemetry needs.
- Built in BLE support for local sensor pairing to extend telemetry such as temperature or door status.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected and activated, the ST6560 sends location, diagnostic and telemetry data to Plaspy so fleet managers can monitor vehicles in real time and investigate historical activity. The device standardizes vehicle signals and telematics into a format Plaspy can use for visualization, alerts and reporting.

- Real time location updates and historical playback available in Plaspy dashboards.
- Engine diagnostics and DTC reporting surfaced as alerts and maintenance triggers.
- Geofence events including circular and polygonal boundaries for entrance and exit notifications.
- J1939 and FMS telemetry visible for heavy duty vehicles where those signals are available.
- BLE sensor data synced through the device so environmental and cargo telemetry appear in Plaspy.
- Continuous tracking support from GNSS and Dead Reckoning to reduce gaps in position reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and recovery with discreet OBD II installation and geofence based alerts.
