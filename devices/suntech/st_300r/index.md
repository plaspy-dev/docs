# Suntech - ST 300R

![ST 300R](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST 300R is a vehicle GPS tracker designed to support fleet management and security use cases. It offers real time reporting of key vehicle states such as speed, ignition status, and idle times, and includes a serial communication interface RS232 for connection to external sensors. When paired with sensors, the ST 300R can supply additional data such as fuel levels, harsh braking events, and passenger presence to help operators better understand vehicle usage.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 300R can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet monitoring environment to consolidate location and vehicle state information alongside other assets. This compatibility makes the ST 300R a practical option for organisations that want to combine sensor driven vehicle insights with Plaspy reporting, oversight, and operational tools.

## Key Highlights

- Real time reporting of speed, ignition status, and idle time for continuous visibility
- Serial RS232 interface for attaching external sensors and data sources
- Ability to detect fuel related readings and driving events when connected to sensors
- Enhances fleet visibility to support route optimisation and operational control
- Helps improve security and monitoring of vehicles and on board activity
- Useful for businesses seeking to reduce costs through better vehicle oversight

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ST 300R can feed positional and vehicle state data into Plaspy, enabling combined visibility of location and sensor driven telemetry within the same platform. Plaspy can then use that information to support monitoring, alerts, and reporting across a mixed fleet.

- Consolidate real time location and vehicle status on the Plaspy dashboard for operational oversight
- Use historical data in Plaspy for route analysis, idle time reports, and efficiency reviews
- Configure alerts in Plaspy to notify teams about ignition events, excessive idling, or notable driving events
- Combine sensor inputs from the ST 300R with Plaspy reporting to spot fuel trends and maintenance indicators
- Include ST 300R equipped vehicles in grouped fleet views for dispatch and logistics planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial delivery fleets tracking routes and stop times for efficiency improvements
- Logistics operations monitoring vehicle idle and driving patterns to reduce fuel costs
- Passenger transport services using occupancy and event data to improve service planning
- Security conscious fleets wanting continuous vehicle visibility and event reporting
- Field service operators coordinating vehicle locations and job schedules
