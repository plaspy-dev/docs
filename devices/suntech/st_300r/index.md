# Suntech - ST 300R

![ST 300R](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST 300R is a GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features to enhance fleet management and increase security. With its serial communication interface RS232, it can connect to various sensors, allowing you to gather valuable data on driving habits, vehicle performance, and management and delivery routes. This information can help you optimize logistics, reduce costs, and improve overall efficiency.

One of the standout features of the ST 300R is its ability to provide real-time information on speed, ignition status, and idle times. This allows you to monitor your vehicles closely and implement new surveillance strategies to ensure the safety of your fleet. Additionally, when connected to external devices or sensors, the ST 300R becomes highly versatile. It can detect fuel levels, driving speed in rain, heavy braking, and even the presence of a greater number of passengers in the cabin, among other things. This level of detail can help you make informed decisions and provide added value to your customers.

With the Suntech ST 300R, you can expand your business opportunities and take your fleet management to the next level. Its advanced features and reliable performance make it an ideal choice for businesses looking to improve security, reduce costs, and enhance their overall operations.

### Key Features:

- Serial communication interface RS232 for connecting to various sensors
- Real-time monitoring of speed, ignition status, and idle times
- Ability to detect fuel levels, driving speed in rain, heavy braking, and more
- Enhances fleet management and improves logistics
- Increases security and reduces costs
