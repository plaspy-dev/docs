# Suntech - ST 300K

<div className="device-logo">
![ST 300K](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Suntech ST 300K is a high end GPS tracker designed for fleet management and operational oversight. It includes multiple vehicle interfaces such as RS232 and CANbus along with a 1-Wire interface that supports up to three temperature sensors or an i-Button for driver identification. The device also offers configurable analog and digital inputs and outputs and built in event detection that can generate alerts for situations like collisions, bumps, and crane dragging, plus features such as engine immobilization and panic button detection.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 300K can feed vehicle location, event signals, and reporting data into the Plaspy platform to improve visibility and decision making across fleets. Plaspy can use the tracker’s reporting and input/output capabilities to produce driving profiles, maintenance reports, and work schedule summaries, helping teams track activity, respond to alerts, and manage preventive maintenance within a single fleet management environment.

## Key Highlights

- Multi interface support including RS232 and CANbus for vehicle system integration
- 1-Wire interface supports up to three temperature sensors or i-Button driver identification
- Advanced reporting features for driving profiles, work schedules, and maintenance summaries
- Configurable analog and digital inputs and outputs for events like immobilization and panic signals
- Built in event detection for collisions, bumps, and crane dragging with alert generation
- Designed to support fleet level monitoring and operational reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the ST 300K provides a stream of location and event information that Plaspy presents through dashboards, alerts, and reports. Plaspy translates the device outputs into operational insights and historical records useful for fleet managers and dispatch teams.

- Live vehicle location and basic status visibility inside Plaspy dashboards
- Event alerts and notifications for collisions, bumps, crane dragging, and panic button activations
- Temperature sensor readings and driver identification events surfaced for monitoring and compliance
- Aggregated driving profile and maintenance reports for trend analysis and preventive planning
- Use of digital and analog input events to trigger workflows and operational alerts within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet route monitoring and activity reporting for medium to large vehicle fleets
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring using the 1-Wire temperature sensor inputs
- Driver identification and shift tracking with i-Button support for operator accountability
- Heavy equipment and crane monitoring where collision and dragging alerts are important
- Scheduled maintenance planning using advanced maintenance and driving profile reports

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ST 300K combines multiple integration interfaces and event reporting with flexible inputs and outputs, making it a practical option for fleets that need more than simple position tracking. Its reporting capabilities align well with Plaspy’s focus on operational oversight, offering a clear path from raw device events to actionable reports and alerts.

For organizations that require temperature monitoring, driver identification, and configurable event handling alongside location tracking, the ST 300K paired with Plaspy can provide a consolidated view of vehicle health, driver activity, and exception events.
