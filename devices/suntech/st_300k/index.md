# Suntech - ST 300K

![ST 300K](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST300K is a high-end GPS tracker that offers a range of advanced features for fleet management. With its RS232 and CANbus interfaces, it allows for seamless integration with various vehicle systems. Additionally, it features a 1-Wire technology interface, which enables the connection of up to 3 temperature sensors or the use of an i-Button for driver identification.

One of the standout features of the ST300K is its ability to generate advanced reports, such as driving profiles, work schedules, and maintenance reports. These reports can help reduce wear and tear on your fleet, optimize routes, and improve overall efficiency. Furthermore, the ST300K comes equipped with a configurable analog input and digital inputs and outputs, allowing for engine immobilization, panic button detection, and the ability to receive alerts for events such as crane dragging, collisions, and bumps.

With its robust set of features and advanced reporting capabilities, the Suntech ST300K is an ideal choice for businesses looking to optimize their fleet management operations and improve overall efficiency.

#### Key Features:

- RS232 and CANbus interfaces for seamless integration
- 1-Wire technology interface for temperature sensors or driver identification
- Advanced reporting capabilities for driving profiles, work schedules, and maintenance reports
- Configurable analog input and digital inputs and outputs
- Engine immobilization and panic button detection
- Alerts for crane dragging, collisions, and bumps
