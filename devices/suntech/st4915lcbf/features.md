# Suntech - ST4915LCBF Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Suntech ST4915LCBF and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for fleet and asset monitoring. It summarizes the device functions that are relevant to Plaspy users and describes the practical value this tracker can add to visibility, telemetry, and operational workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and regional manufacturer implementation. Always confirm device specifics and the latest firmware behavior with the official Suntech documentation when planning deployments or integrations.

## Feature Overview

The ST4915LCBF is a rugged, long life GPS tracker designed for low maintenance deployments. It combines multi radio connectivity, high sensitivity GNSS, BLE and Wi Fi location augmentation, and an IP67 enclosure to support reliable tracking and sensor aggregation in harsh environments.

- Multi standard cellular connectivity with LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT plus 2G fallback to maximize coverage options.
- High sensitivity GNSS for reliable positional fixes and Wi Fi geolocation to improve accuracy where satellite signals are limited.
- BLE 5.x support and gateway functionality to aggregate nearby Bluetooth sensors and beacons.
- Long primary battery autonomy and low deep sleep current for multi year field operation with minimal maintenance.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and shock vibration compliance for use on trailers, containers, and remote equipment.

## Core Features of Suntech - ST4915LCBF

- LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT connectivity with 2G fallback for resilient cellular links across network conditions.
- High sensitivity GNSS receiver for frequent and accurate position reporting.
- BLE 5.x support with BLE gateway mode to collect data from nearby beacons and sensors.
- Wi Fi assisted geolocation to supplement GNSS in urban canyons and transition zones.
- Primary long duration battery design optimized for multi year operation and ultra low deep sleep currents.
- Rugged environmental protection with IP67 enclosure and compliance for shock and vibration.
- Onboard motion sensing and status reporting to support movement detection and event driven reporting.
- Local user interfaces including status LEDs and a magnetic on off control for secure field operation.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position, sensor, and power telemetry from compatible trackers to provide maps, alerts, and historical reports for operations teams. The ST4915LCBF’s radios and sensors translate into actionable data streams that Plaspy surface in dashboards and reporting workflows.

- GNSS fixes and Wi Fi location hints are displayed on Plaspy maps and used for location history and route playback.
- BLE gateway data can be surfaced in Plaspy as aggregated sensor inputs or presence events for nearby beacons.
- Motion and tamper events generated by the device produce alerts and event markers inside Plaspy timelines.
- Battery and power state telemetry appear in device health views to support long term maintenance planning.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and accepts connections over standard TCP or UDP reporting channels for simple integration.
