# Suntech - ST4915LCBF

<div className="device-logo">
![ST4915LCBF](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The ST4915LCBF is a rugged, long life GPS tracker designed for extended field deployments and reliable asset and fleet monitoring. It is housed in an IP67 enclosure and combines high sensitivity GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity and complementary location aids to provide continuous tracking in demanding environments. The device emphasizes durability and low maintenance for long term installations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST4915LCBF can stream location and telemetry into Plaspy to support scalable fleet management and asset visibility. Its mix of cellular radios, fallback connectivity, and local aggregation features make it a practical choice for operators who need real time updates, event driven alerts, and consolidated sensor data inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker optimized for long duration deployments and remote asset visibility.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and design suited for harsh outdoor conditions and vibration prone installations.
- Multi radio connectivity including LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT with 2G fallback to maximize coverage.
- High sensitivity GNSS with Wi Fi assisted geolocation to improve accuracy in marginal signal areas.
- BLE 5.x gateway capability to aggregate Bluetooth sensors and beacons for extended telemetry.
- Very long battery autonomy allowing multi year operation at low reporting rates to reduce maintenance visits.
- Low deep sleep current and onboard motion sensing for tamper detection and event driven reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the ST4915LCBF delivers positional fixes, motion events, and aggregated sensor hints into Plaspy dashboards and reporting engines so operators can monitor assets and fleets from a single platform. Plaspy ingests the device data to create map views, alerts, historical traces, and health reports that support operational decision making.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks visible in Plaspy map and reporting interfaces.
- Motion and tamper alerts routed to Plaspy notifications for anti theft and security workflows.
- BLE sensor aggregation presented in Plaspy dashboards to support temperature, presence, or proximity monitoring.
- Wi Fi and GNSS combined location hints used by Plaspy to improve positioning in yards, urban canyons, and transition zones.
- Battery and power telemetry monitored in Plaspy for lifecycle planning and maintenance scheduling.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset tracking for containers, trailers, and remote equipment where infrequent maintenance is needed.
- Fleet management for mixed vehicle fleets requiring reliable tracking and event logging across large regions.
- Remote equipment monitoring combining location, motion events, and aggregated sensor data to detect movement or tampering.
- BLE beacon aggregation for inventory checks, temperature sensitive loads, or local sensor networks using the tracker as a gateway.
- Geolocation assisted recovery and yard management where Wi Fi and GNSS hints improve operational accuracy.
