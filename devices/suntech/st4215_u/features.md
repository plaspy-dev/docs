# Suntech - ST4215/U Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Suntech ST4215/U as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker. It focuses on practical capabilities and how the device can be used with Plaspy for vehicle and asset monitoring, telemetry, and event reporting without delving into private configuration details.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and how the unit is installed or configured by the integrator. For device specific behavior and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The ST4215/U is a compact, rugged tracker built for continuous connectivity and precise GNSS positioning in challenging environments. It combines low power design, multi band cellular support and multi GNSS positioning to support real time tracking and event reporting that Plaspy ingests for live maps, alerts and historical analysis.

- Robust connectivity using LTE Cat M1 and NB2 with 2G fallback for broad carrier reach and resilient data transport.  
- High accuracy multi GNSS positioning with GPS GLONASS Galileo and Beidou for precise location fixes and up to 10 Hz update rate.  
- Rugged IP67 rated housing and internal antennas for outdoor and vehicle installations needing environmental protection.  
- Motion and event reporting via an integrated 3 axis accelerometer and configurable input logic for ignition and accessory events.  
- Backup battery and low power modes to report power loss and maintain essential reporting during wired power interruptions.

## Core Features of Suntech - ST4215/U

- Plaspy compatible data streaming over standard TCP and UDP transport for real time ingestion.  
- Multi GNSS engine supporting GPS GLONASS Galileo and Beidou with high accuracy positioning (~±2 m under good conditions).  
- Up to 10 Hz position updates for fine telemetry and detailed route reconstruction.  
- LTE Cat M1 and NB2 cellular radio with 2G fallback for extended regional coverage.  
- IP67 waterproof enclosure and compact footprint suitable for vehicle and asset mounting.  
- Integrated 3 axis accelerometer for motion detection and higher resolution movement reporting.  
- Rechargeable backup battery and low power modes to preserve reporting during external power loss.  
- Wiring variants for installation flexibility including 6 pin and 5 pin harness options and ignition/power input logic.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the ST4215/U streams periodic position reports and event messages so Plaspy can present live location, alerts and historical telemetry. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and uses standard TCP or UDP ingestion to receive the device data for mapping and reporting.

- Real time location updates and route replay in Plaspy from GNSS position reports.  
- Motion and accelerometer based events appear as movement or tamper indicators in Plaspy dashboards.  
- Power loss and backup battery notifications provide status updates and can trigger alerts in Plaspy.  
- Ignition and auxiliary input events support trip segmentation and event driven reporting in Plaspy.  
- Geofence and perimeter events sent by the device enable Plaspy to generate alerts and compliance reports.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for cars trucks and service vehicles requiring continuous position and trip telemetry.  
- Theft detection and recovery workflows using motion events high resolution tracking and backup battery reporting.  
- Outdoor equipment and asset tracking where IP67 protection is required.  
- High resolution telemetry tasks such as detailed route analysis and incident reconstruction with 10 Hz updates.  
- Installations needing reliable cellular connectivity across regions using LTE Cat M1 NB2 and 2G fallback.
