# Suntech - ST4215/U

![ST4215/U](./tracker.jpg)

The ST4215/U is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed to be Plaspy compatible for vehicle and asset monitoring in the toughest environments. Built around LTE Cat M1 and NB2 cellular technology with 2G fallback and TCP/UDP data transport, this unit brings dependable real-time tracking, telemetry and event reporting to fleet management and recovery workflows. Its IP67 waterproof housing, internal antennas and wide input voltage range make the ST4215/U an ideal GPS tracker for installations that demand durability and uninterrupted connectivity.

Engineered for precision and low power consumption, the ST4215/U delivers high‑accuracy GNSS positioning \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/Beidou\) with an approximate +/-2 m \(50% 2D RMS\) accuracy under good signal conditions and up to 10 Hz position updates for fine telemetry. Plaspy-compatible by design, the ST4215/U streams location, motion and input/output events into Plaspy for actionable insights, anti-theft alerts, geofence enforcement and scalable fleet management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible via standard TCP/UDP transport—integrate real-time tracking and telemetry quickly into your platform.
- Rugged IP67 waterproof enclosure and compact 61 x 61 x 20.7 mm form factor for vehicle and asset installations.
- Global cellular support: LTE Cat M1 and NB2 with 2G fallback for broad carrier coverage across regions.
- High-accuracy multi-GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/Galileo/Beidou\) with ~±2 m positioning and 10 Hz update rate for precise telemetry.
- Low-power modes and a rechargeable 3.7V 220 mAh backup battery for extended field deployment and power-loss reporting.
- Integrated 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection, fine tracking and up to 10 locations per report.
- Wiring variants \(ST4215 6-pin, ST4215U 5-pin\) with ignition/power/motion logic and accessory compatibility for event and i-button cables.
- Optional jamming detection and approvals for major carriers and international certifications \(FCC, IC, PTCRB, CE\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

Connecting the ST4215/U to Plaspy is straightforward: the device communicates over LTE Cat M1 / NB2 \(with 2G fallback\) using TCP or UDP, allowing Plaspy to ingest GNSS position fixes, telemetry and event inputs in real time. Plaspy reads the device’s periodic position reports and event-triggered messages to provide live maps, alerts and historical reports for fleet management and anti-theft workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via TCP/UDP for live tracking dashboards and route replay.
- Ignition, power and motion detection events are reported to Plaspy for automated alerts and trip segmentation.
- Geofence events \(circular and polygonal\) transmitted to Plaspy for perimeter alerts and compliance monitoring.
- Accelerometer-based motion data and fine tracking \(up to 10 locations per report\) for theft detection and high-resolution telemetry.
- Backup battery and power-loss reporting ensure Plaspy receives status changes during wired power interruptions.
