# Suntech - ST 240

<div className="device-logo">
![ST 240](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Suntech ST 240 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker engineered for reliable position reporting and continuous operation. Built with a plastic cover and an IP67 waterproof rating, the ST 240 is suited to installations where space is limited and exposure to moisture or dust is a concern. It reports vehicle position based on configurable changes in angle, distance, or time, and includes an internal rechargeable backup battery to maintain tracking during power interruption.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 240 can deliver its tracking and event data into the Plaspy platform for visualization, monitoring, and reporting. Plaspy accepts the location and event information produced by the tracker so fleet managers can combine device feeds with Plaspy tools for oversight, historical playback, and alerting. This compatibility makes the ST 240 a practical option for operators who want dependable tracking hardware paired with Plaspy’s fleet management features.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged IP67 rated plastic enclosure suitable for compact vehicle installations
- Position reporting on changes in angle, distance, or time for configurable tracking
- Internal rechargeable backup battery with 450mAh capacity to sustain tracking during power loss
- Wide cellular coverage with full quadband support and multiple communication methods
- Onboard memory plus sleep mode for power management and offline data storage
- Multiple digital inputs and pre defined inputs such as ignition and panic for event tracking
- Internal event generation based on user defined parameters for tailored alerts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the ST 240 streams its location and event messages into the platform where they are translated into meaningful fleet intelligence. Plaspy consolidates device updates and presents them within maps, dashboards, and reports so teams can monitor vehicles in real time and review historical activity.

- Real time location visibility of ST 240 equipped vehicles on Plaspy maps
- Configurable alerting in Plaspy based on digital inputs and internal events such as panic or ignition changes
- Historical route playback and reporting using the device’s stored and transmitted data
- Fleet monitoring dashboards to track vehicle status, movement, and offline events
- Operational oversight tools for grouping assets, assigning vehicles, and reviewing activity trends

## Typical Use Cases

- Track and trace operations for small and medium vehicle fleets
- Vehicle recovery and theft deterrence through continuous position reporting
- Fleet management where compact, waterproof hardware is needed
- Installations that benefit from backup battery tracking during power loss
- Environments requiring basic digital input monitoring for ignition or emergency events

The ST 240 pairs practical durability with useful tracking features that align well with Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting capabilities. Its waterproof enclosure and compact form factor make it a suitable choice for vehicles with limited installation space, while the backup battery and onboard memory help ensure continuity of data during temporary power interruptions. The available digital inputs and internal event logic allow organizations to surface relevant alerts and integrate them into Plaspy workflows.

For teams evaluating hardware for integration into a cloud based fleet management system, the ST 240 represents a balanced option when durability, basic event handling, and reliable position reporting are priorities. Plaspy can ingest the ST 240 data feed to provide mapping, alerts, and fleet level reports without requiring proprietary platform changes.
