# Suntech - ST 300H Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Suntech ST 300H tracker with Plaspy. It highlights the ST 300H capabilities that are relevant to fleet telemetry and how those capabilities surface inside Plaspy for monitoring and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability for the ST 300H can vary depending on firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where a capability is optional or conditional the text will note that it can require additional configuration or equipment per Suntech documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Suntech ST 300H is positioned as a telemetry focused GPS tracker for fleet management. It delivers continuous vehicle location and operational metrics that help fleets monitor movement, driver activity, and route performance.

- Real time tracking of vehicle location and movement to support live monitoring
- Reporting of speed, ignition status, and idle times without requiring external sensors
- Driver identification capability that can associate driving activity with specific drivers when configured
- Aggregated information on driving habits and vehicle performance to support operational analysis
- Support for route and delivery monitoring to help with logistics and dispatch workflows

## Core Features of Suntech - ST 300H

- Real time location reporting suitable for live fleet monitoring and tracking
- Speed reporting that assists in trip analysis and operational review
- Ignition status detection to identify vehicle on and off states
- Idle time reporting to help measure productivity and fuel related behaviors
- Driver identification function to tie journeys to specific drivers when enabled
- Telemetry oriented design focused on fleet and route management insights
- Compatibility with fleet management platforms such as Plaspy for centralized data collection

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests telemetry from compatible trackers like the ST 300H and presents location and operational data in a centralized interface for monitoring, reporting, and operational decision making.

- Real time locations appear on Plaspy maps to support live tracking and dispatch decisions
- Speed, ignition state, and idle time metrics are available in trip summaries and event logs
- Driver identification data can be shown alongside trip records to support driver performance analysis
- Route and trip history are stored for review and operational reporting in Plaspy
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and processes incoming telemetry for visibility and reporting

## Typical Use Cases

- Live fleet monitoring for dispatch and route adjustments
- Monitoring driver activity and correlating trips with individual drivers
- Reviewing speed and idle behavior to identify efficiency improvements
- Tracking delivery routes and verifying completed runs
- Aggregating trip history for operational reporting and planning
