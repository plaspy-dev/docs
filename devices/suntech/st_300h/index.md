# Suntech - ST 300H

![ST 300H](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST300H is a highly advanced GPS tracker designed specifically for telemetry fleet management. With its wide range of features and capabilities, it is the ideal solution for businesses looking to optimize their fleet operations and reduce costs.

One of the standout features of the ST300H is its ability to provide detailed information about driving habits, vehicle performance, and management and delivery routes. This allows fleet managers to gain valuable insights into their operations and make informed decisions to improve efficiency and productivity.

The ST300H offers real-time tracking, providing up-to-date information on speed, ignition status, and idle times without the need for external sensors. This ensures that fleet managers have accurate and reliable data at their fingertips, allowing them to monitor and analyze vehicle performance in real-time.

In addition, the ST300H can be equipped with a driver identification function, allowing fleet managers to associate driving profiles with specific drivers. This feature is particularly useful for businesses that have multiple drivers operating their vehicles, as it allows for better monitoring of individual driver performance and behavior.

Overall, the Suntech ST300H is a powerful and versatile GPS tracker that can significantly enhance fleet productivity and reduce costs. Its advanced features, real-time tracking capabilities, and driver identification function make it an invaluable tool for businesses in the telemetry fleet industry.

### Key Features:

- Real-time tracking of speed, ignition status, and idle times
- Driver identification function for monitoring individual driver performance
- Detailed information on driving habits, vehicle performance, and management and delivery routes
- Enhanced fleet productivity and cost reduction
