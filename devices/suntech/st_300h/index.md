# Suntech - ST 300H

<div className="device-logo">
![ST 300H](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Suntech ST 300H is a GPS tracker built for telemetry fleet management. According to the model description, it delivers real time tracking and provides detailed information on driving habits, vehicle performance, and management and delivery routes. The device is positioned for businesses that need continuous visibility over vehicle movement and operational patterns to improve efficiency.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 300H can feed location and status information into Plaspy to support centralized fleet monitoring. Its real time reporting features and driver identification capabilities make it a natural fit for organizations that use Plaspy to consolidate tracking data, evaluate driver performance, and generate operational reports.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking of vehicle location and status for active fleet visibility
- Reports on driving habits and vehicle performance to support operational decisions
- Monitoring of speed related status, ignition status, and idle times from the device
- Optional driver identification to link driving profiles with individual operators
- Designed for telemetry fleet management and route oversight
- Helps organizations reduce costs through improved tracking and operational insight

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Suntech ST 300H supplies position and status information that Plaspy displays and stores for monitoring and analysis. Plaspy provides tools to visualize device data, run reports, and set operational rules that use the incoming information from trackers such as the ST 300H.

- Live location and status on the Plaspy dashboard for real time operational awareness
- Driver identification mapping so trips and events can be associated with individual drivers
- Trip and route playback to review delivery runs and route efficiency
- Idle and ignition monitoring visible in Plaspy to help identify efficiency opportunities
- Alerts and notifications configured in Plaspy based on tracker status and events
- Fleet level reporting and summaries for performance analysis and cost control

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial vehicle fleets looking to track delivery routes and schedules
- Fleet managers monitoring driver behavior and assigning accountability
- Logistics operations seeking route playback and trip reporting for audits
- Companies aiming to reduce fuel and operational costs through idle and status monitoring
- Multi vehicle operations needing centralized visibility and basic telemetry insights
