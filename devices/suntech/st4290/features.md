# Suntech - ST4290 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Suntech ST4290 tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant for real time tracking, telemetry and anti theft workflows when integrated with the Plaspy platform. Information here is intended to help operations and technical teams understand how the ST4290 can be used with Plaspy for mapping, alerts and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type and manufacturer implementation. Always verify device capabilities for a specific unit and firmware revision before relying on a feature for production workflows.

## Feature Overview

The ST4290 is a rugged, battery powered GPS tracker built for long term asset monitoring and recovery. It combines low power design, integrated GNSS and vehicle oriented inputs and outputs to support unattended assets and vehicle focused deployments where Plaspy delivers tracking, alerting and analytics.

- Long life primary battery options to reduce maintenance and support multi week or multi month deployments.
- Integrated GNSS with GPS and GLONASS plus SBAS for improved positioning accuracy in open sky conditions.
- LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT connectivity with regional 2G fallback where supported for broad cellular coverage.
- Vehicle focused I O including ignition input and panic and door inputs together with outputs for vehicle blocking and siren use cases.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and extended operating temperature range for deployments in harsh environments.

## Core Features of Suntech - ST4290

- Battery powered design with primary Li SOCl2 battery options including an 8Ah standard version and a 19Ah extended capacity variant.
- Integrated GNSS receiver supporting GPS and GLONASS with SBAS assistance and manufacturer stated accuracy around ±2.5 m CEP.
- Cellular connectivity using LTE Cat M1 and NB IoT with 2G fallback where supported, and support for OTA firmware updates.
- Vehicle oriented digital I O set including ignition input, panic and door inputs, and dedicated outputs for vehicle blocking and siren control.
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer for motion detection and movement events.
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure and an operating temperature range designed for outdoor and vehicle mounted use.
- Fast GNSS acquisition performance characteristics as provided by the vendor for hot and warm starts and high sensitivity reception.
- Remote configuration and OTA management capabilities for field updates and settings adjustment.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position and event telemetry from compatible devices like the ST4290 to provide real time location, event notifications and historical reports. When the ST4290 is configured to report to Plaspy, its position fixes, motion events and I O state changes become actionable within the platform.

- Real time location updates and history available in Plaspy mapping and timeline views for asset oversight.
- Motion events from the accelerometer and digital input changes such as ignition, door or panic button are surfaced as alerts and timeline events.
- Outputs for immobilizer or siren can be coordinated through Plaspy workflows to support anti theft and recovery procedures when the device and installation support remote control.
- OTA firmware updates and remote configuration reduce site visits and let Plaspy administrators maintain device settings centrally.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and presents incoming telemetry consistently for monitoring and reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long duration cargo and shipment tracking where battery life and GNSS accuracy are critical.
- Fleet asset recovery and anti theft workflows using ignition sensing and vehicle blocking outputs integrated into Plaspy alerts.
- Remote equipment monitoring and tamper detection for unattended assets in harsh or remote locations.
- Long term field deployments that require IP67 protection and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Consolidated telemetry and event reporting for mixed asset fleets managed through Plaspy.
