# Suntech - ST 340

![ST 340](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST 340 is a compact GPS tracker designed for a wide range of automotive and marine applications. Its small form factor and low power consumption make it a practical choice for motorcycles, heavy machinery, boats, jet skis, and mixed flotillas. Built to operate in harsh environments, the ST 340 carries an IP67 rating for resistance to dust and water, enabling external installations with reliable exposure protection.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the ST 340 can be integrated into centralized fleet and asset monitoring workflows to provide real time location visibility, geofencing, and remote immobilization features. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker helps organizations maintain operational awareness across diverse vehicle types and environments while presenting data through Plaspy's platform for monitoring, alerts, and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Compact design suitable for confined or portable installations
- Low power consumption for extended operation in remote assets
- IP67 rated for resistance to dust and water for outdoor and marine use
- Real time location tracking to support operational visibility
- Geofencing capability for virtual boundary alerts
- Remote immobilization option for theft response and security
- Well suited for mixed fleets including motorcycles, heavy equipment, and watercraft

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ST 340 integrates with Plaspy to deliver location and event information into a single fleet management environment. Plaspy collects and displays position updates, triggers alerts for configured rules, and provides reporting tools that help teams act on the tracker data.

- Live location display and map tracking within Plaspy for fleet oversight
- Geofence creation and alerting to notify teams when assets enter or exit defined areas
- Immobilization control available through the platform to support security workflows
- Historical tracking and trip playback for route review and incident analysis
- Notifications and alert routing to keep operations teams informed of exceptions

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking motorcycles used for deliveries or couriers
- Monitoring heavy machinery and construction equipment on site
- Managing boats and jet skis for rental fleets and marine operations
- Overseeing mixed vehicle flotillas that include land and marine assets
- Enhancing theft response and recovery through immobilization and alerts
