# Suntech - ST 340

![ST 340](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST340 GPS tracker is the perfect solution for a wide range of automotive applications. Its compact size and low battery consumption make it an ideal choice for installation in motorcycles, heavy machinery, boats, jet skis, and various flotillas. The ST340 is designed to withstand the harshest environmental conditions, with an IP67 rating that ensures resistance to dust and water. This means that it can be installed externally without worrying about damage from the elements.

With the Suntech ST340, you can track the location of your vehicles in real-time, ensuring that you always know where they are. This can be especially useful for fleet management, allowing you to optimize routes, monitor driver behavior, and improve overall efficiency. The ST340 also offers a range of advanced features, including geofencing, which allows you to set up virtual boundaries and receive alerts when a vehicle enters or exits a designated area. Additionally, the ST340 supports remote immobilization, giving you the ability to disable a vehicle's engine in case of theft or unauthorized use.

Key Features:

- Compact size and low battery consumption
- IP67 rating for resistance to dust and water
- Ideal for motorcycles, heavy machinery, boats, jet skis, and flotillas
- Real-time tracking for improved fleet management
- Geofencing for setting up virtual boundaries and receiving alerts
- Remote immobilization for added security
