# Suntech - ST 340 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Suntech ST 340 GPS tracker and how those features are used with Plaspy. It is intended to describe user facing capabilities and practical value when the ST 340 is paired with Plaspy for vehicle and asset monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Refer to the device documentation and your installer for details specific to a unit, and consider manufacturer guidance when planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The ST 340 is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for a wide range of automotive and marine installations. It combines low power consumption and an IP67 environmental rating with location reporting and security-oriented features that are useful for fleet and asset management.

- Real time location tracking for continuous visibility of vehicles and assets
- Geofencing support to create virtual boundaries and receive entry or exit alerts
- Remote immobilization capability to disable a vehicle engine when supported and properly configured
- Compact form factor and low battery consumption suitable for motorcycles and small assets
- IP67 rated enclosure for resistance to dust and water, enabling external or exposed installations
- Broad suitability for motorcycles, heavy machinery, boats, jet skis, and flotillas

## Core Features of Suntech - ST 340

- Real time GPS position reporting for operational tracking
- Geofence configuration and event generation for perimeter monitoring
- Remote immobilization support where implemented by the manufacturer and installer
- Compact size tailored for limited-space installations
- Low battery consumption characteristics to extend operational life on battery powered installs
- IP67 environmental protection for dust and water resistance
- Multi asset suitability including land and marine vehicles

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy displays and manages the tracker data to give teams actionable location awareness and event visibility. When an ST 340 is connected to Plaspy, its location and supported events are presented alongside other fleet assets for centralized oversight.

- Real time positions appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring and historical playback
- Geofence events reported by the device create alerts and records within Plaspy for compliance and workflow triggers
- Remote immobilization commands can be issued or tracked in Plaspy when the device and installation support that function
- Asset type and installation notes (for example marine or motorcycle) help operators interpret telemetry and alerts in Plaspy
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring motorcycles and scooters for delivery or single rider fleets
- Tracking boats, jet skis, and marine assets requiring external rated hardware
- Securing construction and heavy machinery across dispersed sites
- Managing small flotillas where compact, low power trackers are preferred
- Improving fleet route oversight and vehicle location visibility for dispatch
- Theft response workflows that use immobilization and location reporting

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version and manufacturer configuration determine which features are active on a specific unit
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may change physical connectors, enclosure details, or supported functions
- Remote immobilization requires proper installation, wiring, and compliance with local regulations
- Installation type and placement can affect GPS reception and reporting frequency
- Confirm supported features and configuration steps with the device manufacturer and your installer
