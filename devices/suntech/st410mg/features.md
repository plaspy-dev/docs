# Suntech - ST410MG Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Suntech ST410MG and how the device integrates with Plaspy for asset and cargo monitoring. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, including GNSS positioning, dual communication paths, motion and jamming detection, and battery endurance for long deployments.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by factory firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration and the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and verify firmware behavior before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The ST410MG is designed for discreet long-endurance asset and cargo monitoring with a focus on low-power operation and robust position reporting. It pairs GNSS positioning and SBAS correction support with both wide area cellular and specialized RF reception to provide continuous tracking and recovery options where battery life and extended RF range matter.

- Real-time GNSS position reporting with GPS and GLONASS plus SBAS support for improved positional accuracy.
- Dual communication architecture combining GSM GPRS for wide area reporting and RF 433–435 MHz for extended range recovery workflows.
- Long endurance rechargeable backup battery with configurable operating modes to extend time between charges.
- Motion detection via a built-in 3-axis accelerometer and jamming detection to surface potential interference or tampering events.
- Compact and lightweight form factor for discreet mounting on trailers cargo or portable assets.

## Core Features of Suntech - ST410MG

- GNSS support including GPS and GLONASS with SBAS assistance for improved accuracy and faster fixes.
- Dual communications: GSM GPRS for cellular reporting and RF 433–435 MHz for complementary reception or recovery scenarios.
- Built-in 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection and movement logging.
- Jamming detection that can flag potential GPS interference or tampering events.
- Rechargeable 2,750 mAh Li ion backup battery with configurable operating modes to prioritize endurance or responsiveness.
- Ultra low deep sleep current and optimized operating consumption to maximize deployment life between charges.
- Multiple LED status indicators to help installers verify GSM GPS and RF activity during setup.
- Compact enclosure and light weight for discreet attachment to cargo and assets.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the ST410MG feeds location and telemetry into the Plaspy platform so teams can monitor assets, receive event alerts, and review historical routes. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and ingests the position and status messages the device sends, which enables dashboard visibility and alerting based on the tracker’s telemetry.

- Continuous location updates and historical route playback in Plaspy for operational awareness.
- Motion event and tamper alerts derived from the accelerometer visible in the Plaspy alert stream.
- Jamming or interference notifications surfaced as events so operators can investigate potential GPS disruption.
- Battery level and power mode telemetry available in Plaspy to inform maintenance and charging schedules.
- RF 433–435 MHz support provides an additional recovery channel that can be used in Plaspy workflows alongside cellular reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term cargo and merchandise monitoring where battery endurance and discreet mounting are important.

- Periodic telemetry deployments such as seasonal or intermittent monitoring where low power reporting stretches operating life.
- High value pallet or equipment tracking that requires compact hardware and continuous position reporting.
