# Suntech - ST 940

![ST 940](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST 940 is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for straightforward localization tasks. Its small and discreet form factor, combined with a magnetic harness, makes it easy to attach to equipment, packages, vehicles, or personal items. The ST 940 emphasizes long field life and durability, offering extended operational periods on a single charge and a robust waterproof enclosure for use in exposed environments.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the ST 940 can be integrated into fleet and asset monitoring workflows to provide persistent location awareness. Plaspy customers can use the ST 940 to increase visibility across assets and personnel without needing complex hardware changes. Its portability and long battery life make it a practical option for long-term or intermittent tracking scenarios within the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and portable form factor suitable for discreet placement and temporary installs
- Very long battery life when configured for infrequent transmissions, reducing maintenance needs
- Waterproof enclosure rated for outdoor conditions, improving reliability in harsh environments
- Magnetic harness for quick and secure attachment to a wide range of surfaces
- Versatile across multiple tracking roles including assets, parcels, and personnel monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the ST 940 provides location updates that can be incorporated into dashboards, maps, and operational reports. Integration enables centralized monitoring and ongoing oversight of tracked items, helping teams make informed decisions based on movement and presence.

- View and track ST 940 locations on Plaspy maps for real time or periodic visibility
- Set geofences and receive alerts in Plaspy when a tracker enters or leaves defined areas
- Aggregate historical location data for reporting and operational analysis inside Plaspy
- Monitor device status and battery trends through Plaspy to plan maintenance and recharging
- Use Plaspy notification tools to escalate events related to tracked assets or personnel

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking high value or frequently moved assets during transit or storage
- Monitoring personnel or lone workers where compact, portable tracking is preferred
- Temporarily tracking parcels, shipments, or consignments that need intermittent visibility
- Attaching to trailers, equipment, or other movable fleet items for occasional location checks

The ST 940 is a straightforward option for organizations that need a dependable, low-maintenance tracker that pairs well with Plaspy's monitoring and reporting capabilities. Its combination of long battery life, weather resistance, and a magnetic mounting option make it suitable for deployments where periodic location updates and physical robustness matter more than continuous high-frequency telemetry.
