# Suntech - ST 940

![ST 940](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST940 GPS tracker is the perfect solution for all your localization needs. With its portable design and easy installation, this tracker is ideal for monitoring personnel, keeping track of loved ones, pets, or even monitoring the movement of packages or merchandise. Its compact size and discreet appearance make it a versatile and practical choice for any situation.

One of the standout features of the ST940 is its impressive battery life. With a daily transmission configuration, this tracker can last up to 400 days on a single charge. This means you can rely on it for long-term monitoring without the need for frequent recharging. Additionally, the ST940 boasts a robust and waterproof design \(IP66\), ensuring its durability and reliability even in harsh environments.

Equipped with a magnetic harness, the ST940 offers easy and secure attachment to any surface. This feature adds to its versatility and makes it suitable for a wide range of applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or individuals, the ST940 is a reliable and efficient choice.

### Key Features:

- Portable and easy to install
- Long battery life - up to 400 days on a single charge
- Robust and waterproof design \(IP66\)
- Equipped with a magnetic harness for secure attachment
- Versatile and suitable for various tracking applications
