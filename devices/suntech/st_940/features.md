# Suntech - ST 940 Features

This page provides a public features overview for the Suntech ST 940 GPS tracker and how those features are typically used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that matter for location monitoring, long term deployments, and common operational scenarios when the ST 940 is connected to Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The summary here is based on the publicly available description of the model and is intended to help you understand practical capabilities rather than replace official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Suntech ST 940 is a compact, portable GPS tracker designed for discreet long term monitoring. It is built for applications that require easy attachment and reliable operation in outdoor or mobile environments, and it is compatible with Plaspy for centralized tracking and operational oversight.

- Portable and easy to install for temporary or semi permanent placements
- Long battery life with up to 400 days on a daily transmission configuration
- Robust waterproof construction certified to IP66 for outdoor use
- Magnetic harness for secure, quick attachment to metal surfaces
- Compact and discreet design suitable for personnel, pets, packages, or assets

## Core Features of Suntech - ST 940

- Portable form factor optimized for quick deployment and unobtrusive placement
- Up to 400 days of battery life under a daily reporting configuration as reported by the manufacturer
- IP66 rated enclosure providing dust protection and resistance to strong jets of water
- Magnetic harness included for easy mounting and repositioning
- Suitable for a range of tracking applications including personnel, pets, parcels, and general asset monitoring
- Discreet appearance that helps reduce visibility in sensitive installations
- Simple installation workflow that supports rapid field deployment

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the ST 940’s practical strengths translate into easier fleet and asset oversight and lower maintenance overhead. Plaspy can ingest location updates from the device and present them with maps, history, and device status to help teams stay informed.

- Plaspy receives and displays location reports from the ST 940 so operators can follow movement and history on a map
- Long battery life reduces the frequency of field visits and helps maintain continuous monitoring through Plaspy
- Robust IP66 design means devices are more likely to remain operational in outdoor conditions reported into Plaspy
- Magnetic harness support simplifies temporary mounting and reassignment of devices while remaining visible in Plaspy device lists
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol to simplify onboarding and avoid manual protocol selection
- Devices can be configured to report into Plaspy so location and operational data appear in the same management interface
