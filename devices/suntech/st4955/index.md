# Suntech - ST4955

![ST4955](./tracker.jpg)

The ST4955 is a rugged, solar powered GPS tracker engineered for long term outdoor monitoring of vehicles, non powered assets and high value equipment. Built for extended field deployments, the device combines a high sensitivity GNSS receiver with energy harvesting and large battery capacity options to support multi month autonomous operation in harsh environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST4955 brings location fixes, accelerometer events and environmental sensor streams into Plaspy for consolidated fleet and asset oversight. Its low power design and remote management features make it a practical choice where persistent tracking, condition monitoring and remote visibility are required across widely distributed or hard to reach assets.

## Key Highlights

- Solar powered design and multiple internal battery options to extend autonomous field life for long term deployments.
- Multi network cellular connectivity with fallback to maintain telemetry from remote sites and moving assets.
- High sensitivity 56 channel GNSS receiver for accurate positioning and reliable location fixes in challenging conditions.
- Ultra low power operating modes to enable multi month reporting intervals and reduced maintenance visits.
- Built in accelerometer and support for environmental inputs to capture movement events and condition telemetry beyond location.
- Rugged enclosure and wide operating temperature range for outdoor and industrial applications.
- Remote firmware updates and device health reporting to simplify fleet maintenance and lifecycle management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the ST4955 transmits location and telemetry back to the platform where Plaspy decodes and presents the data for operational use. Plaspy consolidates GNSS fixes, movement events and sensor streams into maps, alerts and historical reports so teams can monitor assets, react to incidents and analyze performance over time.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for mapping, live tracking and geofence alerting.
- Movement and impact detection from the integrated accelerometer to support anti theft workflows and recovery actions.
- Environmental sensor streams and probe data available within Plaspy for condition monitoring of temperature sensitive or climate exposed assets.
- Optional Bluetooth sensor data aggregated into Plaspy to extend local telemetry coverage when required.
- Remote firmware management and device health metrics surfaced in Plaspy to support scalable fleet maintenance and updates.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term asset tracking for non powered equipment where solar charging and low standby draw reduce maintenance needs.
- Fleet monitoring of trailers, rental equipment and off road machinery requiring persistent cellular telemetry.
- Anti theft and recovery operations using movement detection plus continuous location reporting.
- Remote site monitoring for construction, mining and other outdoor deployments that need rugged hardware and wide temperature tolerance.
- Environmental condition monitoring of sensitive assets using onboard and optional sensor inputs.
