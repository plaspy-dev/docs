# Suntech - ST 180 Wi-FI

![ST 180 Wi-FI](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST180 is a GPS vehicle tracker that offers advanced features and reliable performance. With its internal memory, it can store up to 2000 GPS positions, which can be downloaded when connected to a Wi-Fi network. This feature allows for easy access to the tracker's data without the need for a GSM network, reducing operating costs in fleet management from the very beginning of installation.

One of the standout features of the ST180 is its support for connection to the Can Bus of vehicles with standard J1939, FMS, and OBDII protocols. This enables the tracker to gather valuable data from the vehicle's systems, providing insights into fuel consumption, engine performance, and more. This information is crucial for effective fleet management and can help optimize operations and reduce costs.

In addition to its advanced capabilities, the Suntech ST180 offers a range of other features that make it a versatile and reliable choice for various applications. It supports communication methods such as TCP, UDP, and Wi-Fi, ensuring seamless connectivity and data transmission. The tracker also has a sleep mode, which helps conserve power when the vehicle is not in use. With its multiple digital inputs, internal events-based triggers, and backup battery, the ST180 provides flexibility and reliability in tracking and monitoring vehicles.

Overall, the Suntech ST180 is a high-quality GPS vehicle tracker that offers advanced features, reliable performance, and cost-saving benefits. Whether it's for track and trace, vehicle recovery, fleet management, or telematics applications, the ST180 is a reliable choice that can meet the needs of various industries and businesses.
