# Suntech - ST4955LCBW

![ST4955LCBW](./tracker.jpg)

The Suntech ST4955LCBW is a rugged, solar powered GPS tracker built for long term outdoor asset monitoring. Designed around low power cellular modules with fallback options and optimized energy harvesting, it provides extended autonomous operation and simplified maintenance for distributed equipment. The device includes local sensor interfaces and motion sensing to expand telemetry for environmental and security use cases.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST4955LCBW can be integrated quickly into tracking and fleet management workflows. Its telemetry and location data can be forwarded to Plaspy for real time visibility, alerting, and historical reporting, while on site sensor inputs such as temperature and local wireless peripherals extend the kinds of data Plaspy can consume for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Solar assisted power and energy harvesting for reduced maintenance visits and longer deployments.
- Multi mode cellular connectivity for broad geographic coverage and efficient data use.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure and compact form factor suited for outdoor assets and harsh environments.
- Expanded telemetry options including local wireless peripherals and a 1 wire temperature input.
- Built in motion sensing for activity based alerts and anti theft detection.
- Multiple internal battery options to match different mission endurance requirements.
- Direct compatibility with Plaspy for streamlined integration into fleet and asset monitoring dashboards.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the ST4955LCBW with Plaspy is straightforward: the tracker transmits GNSS fixes, motion status and sensor telemetry over standard IP transport so Plaspy can ingest the data and present it in dashboards, alerts and reports. Local sensor inputs and wireless peripherals can be relayed as part of the same data stream so Plaspy provides a unified view of asset location and condition.

- Real time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for live monitoring and situational awareness.
- Motion and tamper events forwarded to Plaspy to trigger notifications and activity based workflows.
- External temperature sensor readings relayed into Plaspy for environmental monitoring and cold chain alerts.
- Battery and solar charge status available as telemetry for maintenance scheduling and low power warnings.
- BLE sourced data and local sensor inputs can be included in Plaspy records to enrich reporting and analytics.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term outdoor asset tracking where solar charging minimizes service calls.
- Trailer and container monitoring with motion based anti theft alerts and position history.
- Cold chain and environmental telemetry using an external temperature sensor for condition monitoring.
- Distributed sensor enabled assets that need periodic reporting and centralized oversight.
- Fleet asset recovery and management for non powered equipment that requires intermittent tracking.
