# Suntech - ST 215C

![ST 215C](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST215C is a GPS vehicle tracker designed for fleet management and track & trace applications. It offers direct connection to Can Bus standards J1939 and OBDII, allowing for advanced vehicle monitoring and reporting. This improved version of the ST215 model also includes an internal GPS antenna, as well as the option for an external antenna for enhanced signal reception.

With its full quadband GSM band support, the ST215C ensures reliable communication and coverage. It supports GPRS, TCP, and UDP communication methods, providing flexibility in data transmission. The tracker offers various positioning options, including time, distance, and angle change, allowing for accurate and customizable tracking. Additionally, the ST215C features a sleep mode, conserving power when the vehicle is not in use.

The ST215C is equipped with multiple digital inputs, including ignition, panic, and doors, as well as an analogue input. It has internal memory for storing data and supports internal events-based reporting. The tracker comes with GPS and GSM internal antennas, and it also offers the option for fixed antennas and a serial port for additional connectivity. The ST215C is made in South Korea, ensuring high-quality craftsmanship and reliability.

### Key Features:

- Direct connection to Can Bus standards J1939 and OBDII
- Internal GPS antenna with the option for an external antenna
- Full quadband GSM band support
- GPRS, TCP, and UDP communication methods
- Positioning by time, distance, and angle change
- Sleep mode for power conservation
- Multiple digital inputs and an analogue input
- Internal memory for data storage
- Support for internal events-based reporting
- GPS and GSM internal antennas
- Option for fixed antennas and a serial port
