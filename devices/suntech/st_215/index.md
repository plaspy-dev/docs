# Suntech - ST 215

<div className="device-logo">
![ST 215](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Suntech ST 215 is a GPS vehicle tracker designed for track and trace, vehicle recovery, and fleet management. It combines internal GPS and GSM antennas with a quad band GSM GPRS modem to provide reliable location reporting. The device supports flexible positioning options that can report based on time intervals, distance traveled, or angle change, and it is offered in multiple versions to suit different deployment needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 215 can be integrated into centralized fleet monitoring workflows to deliver location visibility and event reporting. Its internal memory and backup battery help preserve data continuity during power interruptions, while support for common communication methods enables efficient data transmission to Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Quad band GSM GPRS modem for wide area communication
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas for consolidated fitting and accurate reporting
- Flexible positioning options based on time distance or angle change
- Multiple versions available to match different input output and monitoring requirements
- Backup battery and internal memory to maintain data integrity during power loss
- Supports GPRS TCP and UDP communication methods for data delivery
- Pre defined inputs such as ignition panic and door signals plus optional Can Bus connectivity

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the ST 215 forwards location and event data to the platform where it can be visualized and analyzed. Plaspy ingests the tracker messages and converts them into map locations alerts and reports that fleet managers use for operational oversight. The device features that store and buffer data help ensure continuous tracking even when external power is interrupted.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for live fleet visibility
- Configurable reporting driven by the tracker’s time distance or angle based positioning
- Event monitoring for inputs such as ignition door and panic to trigger alerts in Plaspy
- Historical route playback and reporting based on stored device data for post event review
- Alerting and notification workflows in Plaspy to surface exceptions and recovery events
- Resilience to temporary connectivity loss thanks to internal memory and backup battery

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for delivery and service vehicles requiring regular location updates
- Vehicle recovery and theft response where precise tracking and event alerts are important
- Rental and leasing operations that need monitoring of vehicle use and movement
- Long distance transport and logistics where continuous location reporting is beneficial
- Municipal or commercial vehicle oversight for route adherence and operational reporting

The ST 215 is a pragmatic choice for organizations seeking a proven tracker that balances core tracking features with flexible configuration options. Its multiple variants allow teams to select a model matched to the inputs and connectivity they need while the device’s internal memory and backup power provide operational continuity that complements Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting capabilities.
