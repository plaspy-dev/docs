# Suntech - ST4345 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Suntech ST4345 series and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for real-time tracking and fleet management. It focuses on the practical, non-sensitive features that are relevant to deploying the ST4345 with Plaspy and explains how common functions are reflected in the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision or variant, installation wiring and manufacturer implementation. Where features are optional or variant-specific this page notes those distinctions, and readers should consult manufacturer documentation for device-specific configuration and the latest technical details.

## Feature Overview

The ST4345 is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for concealed and low-power telematics. It supports multiple cellular modes, GNSS positioning, flexible wired interfaces and optional Bluetooth, making it suitable for vehicle, trailer, motorcycle and high-value asset tracking while reporting events and telemetry to Plaspy.

- Multi-mode cellular connectivity with LTE Cat M1, NB‑IoT and 2G fallback for broad regional coverage.
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure and wide temperature tolerance for outdoor and concealed installations.
- Internal GNSS antenna and position reporting for live location, historical playback and geofence triggers in Plaspy.
- Flexible wired I/O and optional RS232 for ignition detection, external sensors and integration with immobilizer or alarm circuits.
- Low-power design with an internal rechargeable backup battery for long-term or low-activity deployments.
- Optional BLE on select LB and LC variants to connect external Bluetooth sensors and beacons.

## Core Features of Suntech - ST4345

- Multi-radio cellular support including LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT with EGPRS fallback for continued connectivity.
- GNSS positioning with GPS and GLONASS support and SBAS assistance for improved location accuracy.
- IP67 rated compact enclosure designed for concealed mounting and harsh environmental conditions.
- Variant-dependent wired I/O (5 or 8 inputs/outputs) supporting ignition detection, sensor inputs and alarm or immobilizer interfacing.
- Internal rechargeable backup battery to preserve tracking and low-power operation during primary power loss.
- Optional BLE functionality on ST4345LB and ST4345LC variants for Bluetooth sensor and beacon integration.
- Remote maintenance and diagnostics support via standard reporting to a maintenance server.
- Advanced telematics features available in device firmware such as Driving Pattern Analysis, Crash Reconstruction and jamming detection as reported into Plaspy.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The ST4345 exchanges position, telemetry and event messages with Plaspy so that operators see live location, status and alerts in the platform. Plaspy ingests device messages and makes location history, events and diagnostic information available in dashboards and reports for operational oversight.

- Live and periodic GNSS position updates appear on Plaspy maps and historical playback.
- Ignition and motion related events are reflected as engine-on and engine-off or movement events in Plaspy reporting.
- Configurable geofence events and driving‑mode reports generated by the device are available as alerts and logs inside Plaspy.
- Wired sensor inputs and external alarm or immobilizer signals are reported as status flags and events for automated workflows.
- Remote diagnostics and maintenance reports from the device are accessible through Plaspy to support troubleshooting and firmware management.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking and management for light and heavy vehicles with driving behavior and route history.
- Concealed anti‑theft installations where small form factor and IP67 protection reduce tampering risk.
- Trailer and container monitoring where low-power cellular modes and rugged housing extend deployment life.
- Motorcycle and off‑road vehicle tracking where compact size and wide temperature tolerance matter.
- Sensorized asset tracking using BLE capable variants for cargo, temperature or proximity monitoring.
- Remote diagnostics and maintenance workflows for distributed fleets using Plaspy dashboards.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Several capabilities are variant dependent; optional features like BLE and RS232 are available only on specified models.
- Firmware and hardware revisions can change available telematics features, event behavior and parameter names.
- Installation wiring affects which inputs and outputs are present and functional; ignition detection and immobilizer control depend on proper integration.
- Regional cellular band support and certifications vary by model; check the manufacturer model number for regional compatibility.
- Consult the official Suntech documentation for the definitive device specification and the latest firmware release notes.
