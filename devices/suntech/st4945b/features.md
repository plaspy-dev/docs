# Suntech - ST4945B Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Suntech ST4945B and how those capabilities map to use with the Plaspy platform. It summarizes the tracker functions you can expect to see in Plaspy dashboards and reports and explains the practical value of the device for fleet and asset monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration details, firmware release notes and variant options consult the official Suntech documentation and your device supplier.

## Feature Overview

The ST4945B is a rugged LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT asset tracker with EGPRS 2G fallback engineered for long term, low power monitoring in outdoor and industrial deployments. It combines long battery autonomy, robust connectivity options and anti tamper capabilities to keep assets visible and manageable through Plaspy.

- Cellular connectivity via LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT with 2G fallback for broad coverage and power efficient reporting
- Large rechargeable backup battery designed for long service intervals and ultra low deep sleep current
- IP67 rated enclosure for dust and water resistance suitable for containers, trailers and outdoor assets
- GNSS positioning with GPS and GLONASS plus SBAS support for reliable location reporting
- Jamming detection and low battery alerts to notify operators of potential tampering or power loss
- Optional Bluetooth Low Energy 4.2 for local sensor pairing and on site maintenance when required

## Core Features of Suntech - ST4945B

- Real time GNSS location reporting using GPS plus GLONASS with SBAS assistance and typical position accuracy under good conditions
- Cellular transport over LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT with EGPRS 2G fallback for resilient connectivity
- Large 3.7 V rechargeable Li ion backup battery designed for extended operation with sleep and deep sleep modes
- IP67 rated rugged housing for outdoor and industrial asset protection
- Motion based telemetry and event reporting for movement detection and operational alerts
- Jamming detection and low battery notifications to support anti theft and security workflows
- Over the air firmware upgrades for remotely managed device maintenance
- Optional BLE 4.2 interface for local sensor integration and maintenance tools

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The ST4945B streams GNSS positions, motion events and device status messages to Plaspy over standard TCP or UDP data streams. Plaspy ingests those messages to provide live maps, history playback and rule based alerts for operational oversight.

- Real time and historical location data shown on Plaspy maps and timelines for situational awareness
- Motion and event telemetry surfaced as alerts and status markers to indicate towing, movement or unexpected activity
- Low battery and jamming notifications forwarded to Plaspy so operators can prioritize maintenance and security responses
- BLE paired sensors and local telemetry reported to Plaspy when the device is configured to relay those readings
- OTA firmware status and device health visible in Plaspy to support lifecycle and provisioning workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer monitoring for long haul shipments and depot storage with tamper and jamming alerts
- Fleet and equipment tracking where extended battery life and rugged housing reduce field maintenance visits
- Asset recovery and anti theft workflows using motion alerts and continuous location reporting
- Remote infrastructure monitoring of seasonal or off grid equipment requiring long standby times
- On site sensor aggregation using optional BLE to enhance telemetry context in Plaspy

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence can vary by firmware build and hardware revision; some options are model dependent
- Optional interfaces such as BLE and additional I O capabilities may not be available on every unit or regional variant
- Installation and wiring choices affect which I O and sensor functions are accessible for reporting
- Manufacturer firmware updates can change behavior or add features; always verify with official release notes
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols but check device configuration and provisioning for best results

Using the Suntech ST4945B with Plaspy gives organizations a practical way to combine rugged device hardware with a centralized monitoring platform.
