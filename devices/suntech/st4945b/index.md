# Suntech - ST4945B

![ST4945B](./tracker.jpg)

The ST4945B is a rugged LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT asset GPS tracker engineered for long-term, low-power monitoring and seamless integration with Plaspy. Designed for demanding outdoor deployments, the ST4945B brings reliable real-time tracking, robust connectivity fallbacks and extended battery autonomy to fleet management, container and trailer monitoring, and remote asset recovery projects.

Plaspy compatible out of the box, the ST4945B delivers real-time location, motion-based telemetry and event alerts to the Plaspy platform using TCP/UDP data streams. With IP67 protection, jamming detection, OTA firmware updates and an optional BLE 4.2 interface, this tracker is built to support anti-theft workflows, telemetry use cases and scalable fleet management deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker providing real-time tracking and telemetry over LTE Cat M1 / NB‑IoT with 2G fallback.
- Large 3.7V, 18,200 mAh rechargeable Li‑ion backup battery for extended autonomy in sleep and deep‑sleep modes.
- IP67-rated rugged housing for dust and water resistance—ideal for containers, trailers and outdoor assets.
- Built-in GPS/GLONASS with SBAS support and typical accuracy of approximately ±3 m CEP under good signal conditions.
- Advanced power management with ultra-low deep-sleep current \(\<10 μA\) to maximize service intervals.
- Jamming detection, low-battery alerts and OTA firmware upgrades for secure, remotely managed deployments.
- Optional Bluetooth Low Energy 4.2 for local sensor pairing and maintenance, plus an optional Maintenance Server for device management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ST4945B transmits GNSS positions and telemetry to Plaspy via TCP/UDP over LTE Cat M1, NB‑IoT or EGPRS \(2G\) when required. Plaspy ingests location, motion events and device status messages to provide continuous situational awareness, geofencing, historical playback and automated alerts. Integration is straightforward: the device streams standard telemetry packets that Plaspy can parse for real-time dashboards, reports and rule-based notifications.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and history playback.
- Motion and accelerometer-based movement detection used for tamper, towing or cargo-shift alerts.
- Low-battery and jamming detection notifications forwarded to Plaspy for rapid operator response.
- Digital I/O and interfaces vary by model—when available they can report ignition, door/alarm events or drive external immobilizer controls \(model dependent\).
- Optional BLE 4.2 support for local sensor integration and Bluetooth sensors/beacons used by Plaspy for enhanced on-site telemetry.
