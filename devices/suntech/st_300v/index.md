# Suntech - ST 300V

![ST 300V](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST300V is a GPS vehicle tracker and driving control device designed for fleet management purposes. It is ideal for businesses that require direct communication with drivers or the ability to monitor activities inside the cabin. With the ST300V, you can extract reports from the vehicle's Can Bus system, which is compatible with standard J1939/OBDII protocols. Additionally, it features a 1-Wire interface that allows for driver identification.

Tracking the location of your vehicles with the Suntech ST300V is easy and convenient. You can do it through a tracking management platform via the web or by sending an SMS to the device to receive its current location, along with a link to Google Maps for visual representation.

The Suntech ST300V offers a range of outstanding features that make it a reliable and efficient GPS tracker for fleet management. Some of its key features include:

- Full quadband GSM band support
- Two-way voice communication
- Backup battery for uninterrupted tracking
- Internal memory for storing data
- Multiple communication methods including GPRS, TCP, and UDP
- Flexible positioning options based on time, distance, or angle change
- Sleep mode for power-saving
- Three digital inputs and two analog inputs
- Support for various external connectivity options such as fix antennas, microphone/speaker, fuel sensors, temperature sensors, driver ID, 1-wire, and serial port
- Can Bus compatibility with J1939 and OBDII protocols
- Durable plastic casing
- Made in South Korea for quality assurance

The Suntech ST300V is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that is well-suited for fleet management applications. Its advanced capabilities and compatibility with various systems make it an excellent choice for businesses looking to optimize their operations and improve driver communication and monitoring.
