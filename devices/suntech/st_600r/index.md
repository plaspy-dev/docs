# Suntech - ST 600R

![ST 600R](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST600R is a high-performance GPS tracker that is perfect for transmitting and receiving data at 3G speeds. It is equipped with an enhanced cellular modem that provides support for HSDPA, UMTS, EDGE, and GSM/GPRS cellular networks. This means that even in areas where 3G is not available, the ST600R can still use the 2G network to ensure a reliable and continuous flow of information.

One of the standout features of the ST600R is its multiple lines of inputs and outputs, which allow for the integration of different peripheral devices. This means that you can easily connect additional sensors, cameras, or other devices to enhance the functionality of the tracker. Whether you need to monitor temperature, fuel levels, or any other variable, the ST600R can accommodate your needs.

With its robust and reliable design, the Suntech ST600R is an excellent choice for a wide range of applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or even people, this GPS tracker offers the performance and features you need. Its 3G capabilities, multiple inputs and outputs, and compatibility with various cellular networks make it a versatile and reliable solution for any tracking needs.
