# Suntech - ST20U

![ST20U](./tracker.jpg)

The ST20U from ST SUNLAB is a compact vehicle telematics interface module designed to capture vehicle-level data in heavy duty and commercial fleet environments. It is intended to operate as a lightweight bridge between onboard vehicle networks and a GNSS-enabled host or gateway, collecting key telemetry such as VIN, road speed, odometer, engine hours, RPM, and fuel consumption for delivery to a host device.

As a Plaspy compatible device when paired with GNSS enabled hosts or Plaspy capable gateways, the ST20U augments location hardware by supplying rich vehicle telemetry that Plaspy can ingest for monitoring, reporting, and alerting. That combination turns basic position tracking into actionable fleet intelligence for route management, maintenance planning, and operational oversight inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible data stream when integrated with GNSS enabled hosts and gateways for combined location and vehicle telemetry.
- Captures vehicle identity and parameters including VIN, road speed, odometer, engine hours, RPM, and fuel consumption for richer fleet insight.
- Designed for heavy duty and commercial fleets with a compact, rugged form factor and wide operating temperature range.
- Low power footprint to minimize impact on host power systems while operating continuously in vehicle environments.
- Flexible connector options and a simple status LED to ease integration and field verification with host devices.
- Acts as a telemetry bridge rather than a standalone GNSS device, pairing with GNSS hosts or trackers to provide location plus vehicle data.
- Intended to work reliably with established vehicle bus interfaces and RS232 host connections for telemetry forwarding.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ST20U reads vehicle bus telemetry and forwards that data to a GNSS enabled host or Plaspy capable gateway. Once the host combines location from its GNSS receiver with the ST20U telemetry, that combined stream can be uploaded into Plaspy for live monitoring and historical analysis. Plaspy then uses the telemetry to enhance dashboards, reporting, and operational alerts.

- Provide synchronized position and vehicle parameter updates to Plaspy when used with a compatible GNSS host.
- Maintain asset records in Plaspy using VIN and identity data supplied by the ST20U.
- Feed odometer, engine hours, RPM and speed into Plaspy for maintenance scheduling and driver performance review.
- Supply fuel consumption data to Plaspy dashboards for fuel monitoring and cost analysis.
- Forward events and status through the host to trigger alerts, workflows, and reporting in Plaspy for operational oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for heavy duty trucks and commercial vehicles where combined location and vehicle telemetry is required.
- Fuel monitoring and efficiency programs that rely on consumption data integrated into Plaspy reports.
- Maintenance planning using odometer and engine hours telemetry to schedule service and reduce unplanned downtime.
- Asset identification and recordkeeping using VIN and bus data to keep fleet inventories accurate.
- Security and anti-theft workflows when paired with hosts that support immobilizer or remote control features.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ST20U is well suited for organizations that need reliable vehicle parameter acquisition to complement GNSS tracking. Its role as a telemetry bridge means it can extend the value of existing or new GNSS enabled hosts by supplying vehicle-level data that Plaspy can use for analytics, alerts, and operational decision making. For fleets operating heavy duty vehicles, the ST20U provides a practical path to richer telematics without replacing GNSS hardware.

Choosing the ST20U with Plaspy makes sense when you want to turn location data into actionable insights across fuel management, driver behavior, maintenance, and asset tracking. Its compact design and low power profile help it fit into vehicle telematics stacks while enabling Plaspy to present a unified view of position and vehicle health.
