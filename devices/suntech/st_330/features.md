# Suntech - ST 330 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Suntech ST 330 and explains how its container and dry box tracking capabilities are used within Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non sensitive feature context so you can understand what the device is designed to do and how it appears in Plaspy-managed tracking workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior for any deployed ST 330 can vary by firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer configuration. Use the details below as a public guide and confirm device specific behavior with the manufacturer documentation and your firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The ST 330 is a battery powered tracker made for long duration monitoring of containers and dry boxes. It combines periodic position reporting, a rugged enclosure, and strong magnetic mounting to support deployments where access and power are limited.

- Designed for container and dry box solutions to provide location visibility over extended periods
- Long lasting battery performance capable of multiweek operation with intermittent position reporting
- Configurable periodic position reports to balance update frequency and battery life
- Strong magnets for quick attachment to metal surfaces and fast field deployment
- IP67 rated enclosure to resist water and dust for outdoor and transport conditions

## Core Features of Suntech - ST 330

- Long duration battery operation suitable for extended monitoring cycles
- Periodic position reporting, commonly used at 15 minute intervals for ongoing tracking
- Strong magnetic mounting for easy placement on metal containers and dry boxes
- IP67 water and dust resistance for reliable operation in harsh environments
- Purpose built for container and dry box asset tracking and route or parking monitoring
- Standalone battery powered design that minimizes wiring for simpler installations

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and displays device location reports and status so teams can monitor assets, analyze routes, and review historical movements. The ST 330's periodic reports and rugged design make it suitable for Plaspy use where long term visibility and low maintenance are priorities.

- Periodic location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live and historical location awareness
- Long battery life enables lower frequency reporting in Plaspy while preserving multiweek monitoring
- Magnetic mounting and easy deployment reduce setup time before devices are visible in Plaspy
- IP67 protection helps maintain consistent reporting in outdoor and transport conditions
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols so ST 330 reports can be ingested with minimal configuration

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking shipping containers in intermodal transport and yards
- Monitoring dry box locations during transit and storage
- Long term parked asset surveillance where periodic updates preserve battery life
- Fleet or asset inventory checks for equipment that is rarely accessed
- Temporary deployments that require fast magnetic mounting and later removal

## Feature Availability Notes

- Battery life estimates depend on reporting interval, network coverage, and environmental conditions
- Firmware version and manufacturer settings can change reporting options and behavior
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may affect enclosure, magnets, or configuration options
- Installation location and metal mounting surfaces can influence GPS reception and report accuracy
- Refer to device configuration guides from the manufacturer for exact setup and supported modes

Using the Suntech ST 330 with Plaspy gives teams a centralized place to view container and dry box locations, review recent movements, and manage devices alongside other fleet or asset trackers. Plaspy's mapping, history playback, and device management features make it straightforward to incorporate periodic position reports into operational workflows while preserving battery life for long deployments.
