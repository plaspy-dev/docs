# Suntech - ST 330

![ST 330](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST330 is a powerful GPS tracker designed specifically for container and dry box solutions. With its long-lasting battery, this tracker is perfect for monitoring routes or parking for extended periods of time. The ST330 is equipped with a battery that can provide a position report every 15 minutes for up to 5 weeks, ensuring that you have continuous and reliable tracking information.

One of the standout features of the ST330 is its powerful magnets, which allow for easy placement on any metal surface, such as containers or dry boxes. This makes installation quick and hassle-free. Additionally, the ST330 is built to withstand tough conditions, with its water and dust resistance capabilities. It complies with the IP67 standard, guaranteeing impermeability and ensuring that it can operate effectively even in challenging environments.

With the Suntech ST330, you can have peace of mind knowing that your containers and dry boxes are being monitored accurately and reliably. Whether you need to track the location of your assets or ensure their security, the ST330 is the ideal solution.

### Key Features:

- Long-lasting battery for up to 5 weeks of continuous tracking
- Position report every 15 minutes for accurate and up-to-date information
- Powerful magnets for easy placement on metal surfaces
- Water and dust resistance \(IP67 standard\) for reliable operation in tough conditions
