# Suntech - ST 330

<div className="device-logo">
![ST 330](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Suntech ST 330 is a compact GPS tracker purpose built for container and dry box monitoring. It offers extended battery operation suitable for long routes or extended parking periods and is designed to remain attached to metal surfaces using strong integrated magnets. The ST 330 is rated for water and dust resistance to IP67 standards, helping it withstand the environmental demands common in freight and storage use.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the ST 330 can feed location updates and status data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet oversight and asset tracking workflows. Its long reporting interval capability and rugged design make it a practical option for organizations that need reliable, low maintenance tracking for containers and dry boxes within Plaspy dashboards and reports.

## Key Highlights

- Designed specifically for container and dry box applications
- Long battery life suitable for multiweek deployments
- Position reporting cadence oriented toward extended monitoring needs
- Strong magnets for quick and secure attachment to metal surfaces
- IP67 water and dust resistance for harsh environments
- Practical choice for assets that require low maintenance tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

The ST 330 integrates with Plaspy to provide consistent location visibility for containerized and boxed assets, enabling centralized monitoring alongside other fleet devices. Plaspy can ingest the ST 330 reporting and present that information within maps, alerts, and historical reports to support operational decision making.

- Visualize container and dry box locations on Plaspy maps
- Monitor routes and extended parking sessions with periodic position reports
- Configure alerts and notifications in Plaspy for movement or status changes
- Include ST 330 assets in fleet reports and operational summaries
- Use historical location data in Plaspy for route analysis and audits

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking shipping containers during intermodal transport
- Monitoring dry boxes and secured cargo during long term storage
- Keeping visibility on trailers or chassis while parked for extended periods
- Auditing asset movement and location history for logistics operations
- Low maintenance tracking for remote or infrequently serviced assets

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The ST 330 is a sensible match for organizations that need durable, low intervention tracking for metal mounted assets. Its magnetic mounting and IP67 protection reduce the need for specialized installation and enable deployment in challenging environments, while the extended battery life supports long duration monitoring without frequent servicing.

When paired with Plaspy, the ST 330 becomes part of a broader fleet and asset management workflow, bringing asset location and activity into centralized visibility. This combination is particularly useful for logistics teams and asset managers who prioritize reliability and straightforward integration for container and dry box tracking.
