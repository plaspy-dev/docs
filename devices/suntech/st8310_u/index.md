# Suntech - ST8310/U

<div className="device-logo">
![ST8310/U](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Suntech ST8310/U is a rugged GPS tracker designed for essential vehicle telematics and fleet management. Offered in two compact variants, the ST8310 and the ST8310U, the unit is built for outdoor and exposed vehicle installations with a waterproof IP67 lugged enclosure. It delivers wide area connectivity, high sensitivity GNSS positioning and a compact form factor intended for tracking, anti theft and continuous operational monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST8310/U is suitable for fleets and integrators who need reliable live location, event reporting and remote device management. Its support for over the air configuration and firmware updates, configurable inputs and outputs, and optional expanded telemetry make it a practical fit for Plaspy deployments that require real time tracking, geofence alerts and remote immobilization workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged IP67 waterproof housing and compact dimensions suitable for vehicle and exposed installations.
- LTE Cat 1 connectivity with fallback to legacy 2G for broad coverage and reliable reporting.
- High sensitivity GNSS positioning with GPS and GLONASS support plus SBAS for improved accuracy.
- Configurable inputs and outputs across variants to support ignition, panic or door events and remote immobilization control.
- Built in anti theft features including jamming awareness, geofencing and motion detection for immediate alerts.
- OTA configuration and firmware upgrade support to simplify remote device maintenance and large scale rollouts.
- Optional Bluetooth and advanced analytics modules available to extend telemetry and driver behavior insights.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the ST8310/U provides location and device event streams that Plaspy uses to deliver live visibility, alerting and operational reporting for fleets. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes and device events, enables remote command workflows, and allows centralized monitoring and historical reporting across an entire fleet of ST8310/U units.

- Real time location tracking and route history for operational oversight and dispatching.
- Event and input reporting such as ignition, panic or door changes for precise activity logs.
- Remote output control through Plaspy to support immobilization and alert actions.
- Geofence creation and breach alerts for perimeter monitoring and automated notifications.
- Security alerts from jamming detection and motion sensor events forwarded to Plaspy dashboards.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with continuous tracking, trip logging and operational reporting for mixed vehicle fleets.
- Vehicle recovery and anti theft deployments using geofences, motion alerts and remote immobilizer control.
- Driver behavior and incident analysis with optional modules for richer telemetry and post event reconstruction.
- Last mile and emergency reporting where low power modes and backup reporting help keep devices connected during power loss.
- Cargo and asset monitoring by pairing optional sensors to track proximity or environmental conditions.
