# Suntech - ST 910

![ST 910](./logo.jpg)

The Suntech ST910 is a standalone GPS asset tracker designed to provide reliable and accurate tracking for mobile assets. With its Li-Polymer 800mAh rechargeable battery, this tracker ensures long-lasting performance. It also features an internal memory that can store up to 1000 events, allowing you to keep track of your assets even when they are out of range.

One of the standout features of the ST910 is its panic button, which allows users to send alerts with the current GPS location in case of emergencies. These alerts can be sent via SMS or through the GPS location service via the web, ensuring that help can be dispatched quickly and efficiently.

The ST910 is equipped with full quadband GSM connectivity, making it compatible with networks worldwide. It supports communication methods such as GPRS, TCP, and UDP, ensuring seamless and reliable data transmission. Additionally, the tracker offers sleep mode functionality, conserving battery life when the asset is not in use.

With its compact and durable plastic casing, the ST910 is built to withstand harsh environments and provide reliable tracking in any situation. It also features a mini USB port for extra connectivity options.

Overall, the Suntech ST910 is an excellent choice for track and trace applications, as well as vehicle recovery. Its advanced features, reliable performance, and user-friendly design make it a top choice for asset tracking needs.
