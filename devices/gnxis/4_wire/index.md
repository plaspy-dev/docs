# Gnxis - 4-wire

![4-wire](./Logo.jpg)

The Gnxis 4-wire is a compact GPS tracker designed for real time vehicle location monitoring and security alerts. It is positioned for use in private cars, commercial fleets, and motorcycles, delivering precise positioning and a range of alarm options to help protect and monitor assets. The device is built to maintain connectivity across common cellular networks, and it includes features aimed at conserving power while parked.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Gnxis 4-wire can feed live location data and status alerts into the Plaspy platform for centralized fleet oversight. This compatibility makes it straightforward to include Gnxis 4-wire units in a Plaspy deployment for monitoring, alerting, and basic reporting, helping operations teams maintain situational awareness across diverse vehicle types.

## Key Highlights

- Accurate real time tracking suitable for private vehicles motorcycles and commercial fleets  
- Built in antennas for simplified device form factor and installation flexibility  
- Ignition and movement alerts to inform when vehicles start or stop  
- Speeding and ignition status alerts to support basic safety monitoring  
- Angle based location updates to refine position reporting during turns and maneuvers  
- Power off alarm to notify when external power is disconnected  
- Energy saving mode that reduces reporting when the vehicle is parked

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the Gnxis 4-wire provides continuous position updates and event notifications that appear in the Plaspy dashboard and reporting tools. Plaspy ingests the device data to provide visualization and operational context, enabling teams to use the tracker for day to day fleet management and security workflows.

- Live map view of vehicle positions and recent movement history  
- Event and alarm delivery into Plaspy for ignition movement power disconnect and speed related alerts  
- Centralized fleet monitoring for mixed vehicle types including cars and motorcycles  
- Historical reporting for trip review and basic operational analysis  
- Alert routing to operations teams for timely response and oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking and securing private vehicles with real time location and power disconnect alerts  
- Commercial fleet monitoring for route oversight and basic safety notifications  
- Motorcycle tracking where compact hardware and reliable location updates are needed  
- Asset security and theft deterrence using alarm and movement notifications  
- Day to day operational visibility for small to medium sized fleets
