# STL

STL produces a range of GPS tracking devices used in vehicle and asset tracking workflows. This page explains how STL devices can work with Plaspy, how to find compatible models, and what to expect when you integrate STL hardware into a Plaspy deployment. Use the device catalog below to navigate available STL models and view the units that are compatible with Plaspy.

## How STL devices work with Plaspy

STL devices send location and telemetry data from the field to a tracking platform. When an STL device is configured to report to Plaspy, the device transmits position updates and status messages which Plaspy ingests, parses, and displays in the platform interface. Integration typically requires configuring the device with the correct server address and credentials so that Plaspy can receive updates from the device.

Detailed configuration steps such as protocol selection, APN settings, and device specific parameters are available on each device information page and in the device manual. For device level setup and troubleshooting consult the catalog entry for the model you are using or contact your hardware provider.

## Common features found in STL devices

While specific feature sets vary by model, STL devices commonly provide the following capabilities that are relevant when used with Plaspy:

- Real time location tracking and periodic position reporting
- Movement and tamper alerts
- Geofence support and location based triggers
- Battery level and signal status reporting
- Historical route playback and basic telemetry

Plaspy displays and organizes this data in maps, timelines, and reports once the device is configured to send data to the platform.

## Typical use cases for STL GPS trackers

STL devices are suitable for a variety of tracking scenarios, for example:

- Fleet vehicle monitoring for operational visibility
- Asset tracking for trailers, equipment, and cargo
- Driver and route performance review with historical playback
- Theft deterrence and recovery through alerts and live tracking
- Service and rental fleet management with location based scheduling

Plaspy adds visualization, alerting, and reporting capabilities to turn device data into actionable insights.

## Why choose Plaspy for STL device monitoring

Plaspy is designed to work with a wide range of tracking devices and to present device data in a unified, searchable interface. Key reasons teams choose Plaspy with STL hardware include:

- Centralized map and timeline view for devices from multiple manufacturers
- Alerting and notification rules that act on device events and geofences
- Reporting and export options to analyze fleet performance
- APIs and integrations to connect tracking data with other systems

If you are evaluating a monitoring platform, review Plaspy capabilities and compare how the data you need will appear in the platform. Learn more at https://www.plaspy.com.

## Help finding the right STL device

To pick the correct STL tracker for your project:

1. Use the catalog at the top of this page to see models that are known to work with Plaspy.
2. Review device specs on the manufacturer site at http://siliconwireless.in to confirm physical form factor, power options, and network compatibility.
3. Consider telemetry and I O needs such as input sensors, battery life, and mounting requirements.
4. If you have questions about configuration or compatibility, check the device specific documentation linked from the catalog entry or contact Plaspy support for guidance on how to integrate the device with the platform.

Device configuration, APN setup, and protocol specifics are documented at the device level; this brand page focuses on compatibility and integration guidance.

Q: Does Plaspy support fleet monitoring with STL devices?
