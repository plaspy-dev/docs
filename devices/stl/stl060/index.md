# STL - STL060

![STL060](./logo.jpg)

The STL STL060 GPS tracking device is a reliable and user-friendly solution for tracking vehicles, people, and other assets. Using the Global Positioning System \(GPS\), this device accurately determines the precise location of the attached asset and records its position at regular intervals. Whether you need to keep an eye on your fleet of vehicles or ensure the safety of a loved one, the STL060 is designed to meet your tracking needs.

One of the standout features of the STL060 is its ease of use and user-friendly interface. You don't need to be a tech expert to operate this device. It provides precise coordinates by utilizing GPS satellites, ensuring accurate and reliable tracking information. Even in the absence of GPRS, the device stores the position data in its memory, so you can access it later. Additionally, the STL060 can send location data on SMS request, allowing you to retrieve real-time information on the go. With its accurate data export and SMS-based settings, this GPS tracker offers convenience and flexibility.

Key Features:

- Easy to use and user-friendly interface
- Precise coordinates from GPS satellites
- Stores position data in memory in the absence of GPRS
- Location data can be sent on SMS request
- Accurate data export and SMS-based settings
