# Orion - ET-100

<div className="device-logo">
![ET-100](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Orion ET-100 is a compact GPS tracker designed for asset management and security. It is suitable for a wide range of scenarios, from tracking personal valuables to managing vehicle fleets and monitoring the safety of family members. The ET-100 emphasizes discreet placement thanks to its lightweight form factor and provides continuous location visibility to help users know where their assets are at all times.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the ET-100 brings its core tracking features into a centralized fleet and asset management platform. Real time location updates, geo fencing events, and SOS alerts from the ET-100 can be surfaced inside Plaspy to support monitoring, alerting, and reporting workflows. That compatibility makes the ET-100 a practical option when you want tracker hardware combined with Plaspy for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design for discreet placement on assets
- Real time live location updates to maintain constant visibility
- Geo fencing functionality to trigger alerts when boundaries are crossed
- SOS button for immediate emergency alerting
- High GPS sensitivity for accurate positioning in challenging environments
- Robust construction with compliance to international safety and efficiency standards

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, data from the ET-100 can be integrated into a single monitoring interface so teams can view locations and respond to events quickly. Plaspy translates the tracker signals into actionable information for operations, security, and reporting.

- Live location visualization on Plaspy maps for ongoing monitoring
- Geo fence creation and automated alerts when assets enter or leave set areas
- SOS event handling with notifications routed through Plaspy for fast response
- Historical movement and route logs for incident review and analysis
- Asset grouping and status views to manage fleets or portfolios of tracked items
- Customizable alerts and reporting to match operational needs

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle monitoring for routing awareness and oversight
- Management of high value assets and equipment tracking
- Personal item tracking for valuables and luggage
- Monitoring the safety and location of family members or caregivers
- Security and loss prevention for commercial operations
