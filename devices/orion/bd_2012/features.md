# Orion - BD-2012 Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the Orion BD-2012 GPS tracker and how its user-facing capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical feature context for monitoring and tracking, explains how those features map into Plaspy visibility and workflows, and helps readers understand typical operational uses.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The content below summarizes the BD-2012 features described by the manufacturer and how those capabilities are useful when the device reports into Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The Orion BD-2012 is positioned as a compact, versatile tracker for asset management and personal or fleet security. It combines continuous location reporting with perimeter alerting and a manual emergency alert function to support a range of monitoring needs.

- Real time tracking with continuous live location updates for ongoing visibility.
- Geo-fencing functionality to define virtual perimeters and receive entry or exit alerts.
- Emergency SOS button that lets users send immediate alerts in urgent situations.
- Compact, lightweight and discreet design for unobtrusive placement on assets.
- Adaptable power input options intended for global application and flexible installations.
- Designed for reliable GPS sensitivity and operation in a range of environmental conditions.

## Core Features of Orion - BD-2012

- Real time location reporting for continuous monitoring of assets and vehicles.
- Geo-fence support allowing creation of virtual perimeters and event notifications.
- Emergency SOS button to trigger immediate alerts for urgent situations.
- Compact and lightweight form factor suited to discreet placement on a variety of assets.
- Compatibility with a variety of installation types for personal and professional tracking.
- Design intended to operate across a broad temperature range and environmental settings.
- Manufacturer compliance with applicable safety and operational standards.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the BD-2012’s location and event capabilities become part of a centralized tracking workflow. Plaspy receives the device’s reports and presents location, perimeter events, and emergency signals alongside other devices in a unified interface.

- Live location displayed on Plaspy maps for ongoing situational awareness and monitoring.
- Geo-fence events logged and surfaced as alerts so administrators can review entries and exits.
- SOS events highlighted in Plaspy to draw attention to urgent notifications from the device.
- Historical location data and event timelines stored in Plaspy for post event review and auditing.
- Plaspy detects common tracker protocols automatically and integrates BD-2012 reports into fleet and asset views.

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking company vehicles and monitoring location for routine operations.
- Monitoring portable equipment or high value assets that benefit from discreet placement.
- Providing location visibility for personal items or family member safety.
- Asset security and theft response where geo-fence alerts and SOS signals support incident handling.
- Short term rentals or shared equipment where portability and simple installation are important.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior such as reporting cadence, alert formats, and SOS handling can depend on device firmware version.
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may affect available power input options or environmental ratings.
- Installation type and placement can influence GPS reception and the effectiveness of real time tracking.
- Manufacturer feature lists and compliance statements may be updated over time; verify with official documentation.
- Plaspy visibility of features depends on the device sending compatible events and on correct configuration during setup.
