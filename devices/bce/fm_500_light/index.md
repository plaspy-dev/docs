# BCE - FM-500 Light+

![FM-500 Light+](./logo.jpg)

The BCE FM-500 Light+ is a versatile GPS tracker designed for object tracking. With GPS/GLONASS and GSM connectivity, this device is capable of acquiring information on object location, speed, direction, and more, and transferring the data via the GSM network. It features digital and analog inputs that can be used to connect various external sensors and devices, as well as outputs that allow for remote control of external equipment. The FM-500 Light+ offers flexible configuration options, allowing users and dealers to adjust the device to meet their specific requirements.

One of the standout features of the FM-500 Light+ is its range of interfaces, including 1-Wire \(iButton\), RS-232, and EIA-485. These interfaces provide compatibility with a wide range of devices and sensors, expanding the capabilities of the tracker. Additionally, the device offers a variety of inputs and outputs, including digital and analog inputs and digital outputs, further enhancing its versatility.

With a compact design measuring 26x80x55 mm and weighing just 64 g, the FM-500 Light+ is highly portable and can be easily installed in various objects or vehicles. It operates in a wide temperature range from -40 to +85 C°, making it suitable for use in various environments. The device also features an internal battery with a capacity of 700 mAh, providing backup power in case of a power cut-off.

Overall, the BCE FM-500 Light+ is a reliable and feature-rich GPS tracker that offers flexibility, compatibility, and ease of use. Whether you need to track vehicles, assets, or any other objects, this tracker is a great choice.

### Key Features:

- GPS/GLONASS and GSM connectivity
- Flexible configuration options
- 1-Wire \(iButton\), RS-232, and EIA-485 interfaces
- Digital and analog inputs
- Digital outputs for remote control
- Compact design and lightweight
- Wide operational temperature range
- Internal battery for backup power
