# BCE - FM-500 Light

![FM-500 Light](./logo.jpg)

The BCE FM-500 Light is a compact object tracker designed to provide reliable location and movement information for a wide range of assets. It uses GPS and GLONASS positioning together with GSM data transfer to report location, speed, direction, and event information. The device includes digital and analog inputs, a 1-Wire interface for external sensors, and outputs for remote control of connected equipment, offering flexible configuration to suit different tracking needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FM-500 Light can feed its location and status data into Plaspy for consolidated monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can ingest the device's position updates, event logs, and input/output states to present that information in dashboards, alerts, and historical reports. This compatibility makes the FM-500 Light a practical option for businesses that want to combine proven hardware with Plaspy's fleet and asset management features.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight form factor suitable for a variety of object tracking applications
- GPS and GLONASS positioning with assisted acquisition options for faster fixes
- GSM data transfer to send location and event information to a tracking platform
- Multiple inputs and a 1-Wire interface for connecting external sensors and devices
- Outputs available for remote control or signalling of external equipment
- Internal memory and optional backup battery to retain events when connectivity is interrupted

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the FM-500 Light becomes part of a unified tracking environment where location, motion, and event data are visible alongside other devices in your fleet. Plaspy receives the device's reported positions and status messages and allows teams to use that data for operational oversight and reporting.

- Real time and historical location display within Plaspy maps and timelines
- Alerts and notifications based on movement, inputs, or custom event conditions
- Aggregated reporting on trips, distance, and event counts for operational review
- Visual status indicators for digital and analog inputs as captured by the tracker
- Remote control actions and output state monitoring presented in Plaspy when supported

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking portable assets and high value objects that require compact hardware
- Monitoring small vehicles or equipment where space and weight are limited
- Remote status monitoring using connected sensors for tamper, door, or utility signals
- Rental or short term deployments where flexible configuration and portability matter
- Situations that benefit from event logging and post event analysis
