# Falcom - FOX3-3G

![FOX3-3G](./logo.jpg)

The FOX3-3G Series by Falcom is a compact and versatile vehicle telematics and gateway device that combines high-speed data connection with advanced GNSS technology. This all-in-one device is designed to meet the diverse needs of customers and offers a wide range of inputs and outputs for maximum flexibility. Whether you need to track vehicles, monitor driver behavior, or collect data for logistics purposes, the FOX3-3G Series has you covered.

One of the standout features of the FOX3-3G Series is its compatibility with various external antennas, allowing for optimal signal reception. The device also supports the use of additional boxes \(IOBOX-MINI, IOBOX-CAN, or IOBOX-WLAN\) to further expand its functionality, making it one of the most versatile options in the industry.

With the FOX3-3G Series, you can simplify your logistics processes and streamline installation and post-installation support. The device comes equipped with FALCOM's innovative "Premium-Features," which offer advanced capabilities for mobile data logging, storing, processing, and sending. These features include data encryption, multiple history modes, eco-drive/behavior monitoring, and GSM/GPS jamming detection. Additionally, the device offers standard functionalities such as status reports, alert messaging, audio calls, spy calls, and geofencing.

Please note that while the FOX3-3G Series supports audio interfaces in the FOX3-3G-AU model, the other models in this series do not have this feature.
