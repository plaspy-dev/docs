# Falcom - FOX3-3G

![FOX3-3G](./logo.jpg)

The FOX3-3G Series by Falcom is a compact and versatile vehicle telematics and gateway device that pairs high speed data connectivity with advanced GNSS positioning. Designed as an all in one unit, it provides a broad set of inputs and outputs and supports external antenna options and add on IO boxes to extend capability. The series targets common telematics needs such as vehicle location, behavior monitoring, and logistics data collection while including a suite of built in features like data encryption, multiple history modes, eco drive and behavior monitoring, jamming detection, status reporting, alert messaging, audio call support for specific models, and geofencing.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FOX3-3G can feed location and operational data into the Plaspy platform to support fleet visibility and operational oversight. Its flexible I O options and expanders make it useful for mixed fleets and deployments that require modest customization. When paired with Plaspy, the FOX3-3G can be used to centralize tracking, alerts, and reporting so operations teams can monitor assets and make data driven decisions without needing specialized integration work for basic telematics workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact all in one telematics gateway suitable for vehicle installations
- High speed data connection combined with advanced GNSS positioning
- Wide range of inputs and outputs for flexible integration with vehicle systems
- Support for external antennas and optional IOBOX expansions for added functionality
- Built in Premium Features such as data encryption, multiple history modes, eco drive behavior monitoring, and jamming detection
- Standard capabilities including status reports, alert messaging, geofencing, and audio call functions in selected models

## How It Works with Plaspy

The FOX3-3G sends positional and status information that Plaspy ingests to provide real time visibility and historical tracking for fleets. Plaspy organizes incoming data into maps, timelines, and reports so fleet managers can monitor assets, receive alerts, and analyze operational patterns.

- Live location and history playback for vehicle movements and routes
- Configurable alerts and notifications for geofence events status changes and detected anomalies
- Aggregated reporting for driver behavior and eco driving trends based on device events
- Device health and connectivity monitoring to track online status and basic telemetry availability
- Operational dashboards and exportable reports to support logistics planning and compliance

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for commercial delivery and transport operations
- Monitoring driver behavior and eco driving programs across mixed fleets
- Logistics and route oversight for distribution and field service teams
- Asset location and status reporting for rental and leasing operations
- Expanding telematics capability using external antennas and IOBOX modules for specialized needs

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The FOX3-3G Series is a practical choice for organizations that need a flexible telematics gateway with built in features that support common fleet workflows. Its expandability with optional IOBOX modules and external antenna support makes it adaptable to a range of vehicle types and operational requirements. When integrated with Plaspy, the device can contribute rich location and event data that helps reduce manual tracking work and improves situational awareness across operations.

Because device capabilities and model variants can differ, consider the FOX3-3G family as a versatile platform rather than a one size fits all solution. For example audio interfaces are available in the FOX3-3G-AU model while other models in the series do not include that feature. Plaspy can help you evaluate how this device fits within your tracking and reporting needs and turn device data into actionable fleet insights.
