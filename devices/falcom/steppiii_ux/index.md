# Falcom - STEPPIII-UX

![STEPPIII-UX](./logo.jpg)

The Falcom STEPP III-UX is a highly versatile and customizable smart tracking device that can be tailored to meet the specific needs of users. It is designed to function as a mobile client for various system solutions such as AVL, fleet management, vehicle security, and recovery. With its ability to operate autonomously and interact with sensors and actors, this device offers a wide range of functionalities.

One of the key advantages of the STEPP III-UX is its adaptability to existing tracking solutions. It can be easily configured to gather or exchange relevant information with servers or users directly. For instance, it can send status reports or alert messages via SMS to users or via TCP to tracking servers. This flexibility makes it an ideal choice for businesses looking to integrate the device into their existing tracking systems.

The STEPP III-UX also offers a combination of comfort and security features. Users can enjoy regular voice calls as well as emergency spy calls in critical situations. Additionally, the device includes a driver's logbook and data logging functionalities, which are integrated into the Falcom history feature. This allows for efficient tracking and monitoring of vehicle activities.

Furthermore, the STEPP III-UX supports geofencing, which enables users to define specific routes or areas and receive notifications if a vehicle enters or leaves these predefined zones. This feature is particularly useful for monitoring and reporting any violations or unauthorized movements.

In summary, the Falcom STEPP III-UX is a highly adaptable and cost-effective tracking device that offers a wide range of features for AVL, fleet management, vehicle security, and recovery. Its customizable nature, combined with its integration capabilities, makes it an excellent choice for businesses seeking efficient and reliable tracking solutions.
