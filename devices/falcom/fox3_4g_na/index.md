# Falcom - FOX3-4G-NA

<div className="device-logo">
![FOX3-4G-NA](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Falcom FOX3-4G-NA is a high performance GPS tracker and vehicle telematics gateway built for industrial IoT and fleet environments that require robust data connectivity. Designed around LTE Cat 4 with 3G and 2G fallback, it delivers continuous network reach while using modern GNSS positioning for accurate location data. The device supports a range of inputs and outputs and can be extended with Falcom expansion boxes such as IOBOX-MINI, IOBOX-CAN, or IOBOX-WLAN to adapt to varied vehicle and equipment integration needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the FOX3-4G-NA can feed location, status, and event data into Plaspy's fleet management platform to support real time monitoring, alerts, and historical reporting. Its combination of connectivity options, flexible I O support, and premium features like data encryption, multiple history modes, eco drive behavior monitoring, data logging, and jamming detection make it a practical choice for organizations that want to pair advanced hardware with Plaspy for operational oversight and analytics.

## Key Highlights

- LTE Cat 4 connectivity with 3G and 2G fallback for resilient mobile data coverage
- Latest GNSS technology for reliable and precise positioning
- Flexible inputs and outputs with support for IOBOX expansion for additional capabilities
- Internal and external antenna support with automatic switching for installation flexibility
- Premium features including data encryption, multiple history modes, eco drive monitoring, data logging, and jamming detection
- Standard telematics functions such as status reports, alert messaging, and geofencing

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the FOX3-4G-NA supplies vehicle location and event information that Plaspy uses to provide operational visibility and fleet management tools. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it in dashboards, alert rules, and reports so teams can act on live and historical information.

- Real time location tracking and status display in Plaspy dashboards
- Geofence and alert messaging pushed into Plaspy for timely notifications
- Historical route playback and reporting using the device multiple history modes
- Behavior and eco drive data available for monitoring and operational analysis
- I O events and expansion box signals mapped into Plaspy for custom monitoring and workflows

- Industrial IoT and equipment tracking where expanded I O and gateway features are needed
