# Falcom - FOX-IN

![FOX-IN](./logo.jpg)

The Falcom FOX-IN is a highly versatile and configurable smart tracking device that can be customized to meet the specific requirements of users. Its primary function is to serve as a mobile client for various system solutions such as AVL \(Automatic Vehicle Location\), fleet management, vehicle security, and recovery. With its advanced capabilities, the FOX-IN can operate autonomously and interact with sensors and actuators, making it a powerful tool for gathering and exchanging relevant information with servers and users.

One of the key features of the FOX-IN is its ability to send status reports and alert messages directly to users via SMS or TCP to tracking servers. This ensures that users stay informed about the status of their vehicles or assets in real-time. Additionally, the device offers a range of comfort and security features, including regular voice calls and emergency spy calls. The FOX-IN also combines the functionalities of a driver's logbook and data logging, providing a comprehensive history feature.

Another notable feature of the FOX-IN is its geofencing capability, which allows users to define specific routes or areas and receive notifications if a vehicle enters or leaves these predefined zones. This is particularly useful for monitoring and reporting any violations or unauthorized movements. With its seamless integration of various features and its cost-effective tracking and security solutions, the FOX-IN is an excellent choice for businesses and individuals looking for a reliable and customizable tracking device.

#### Key Features:

- Configurable and adaptable to user requirements
- Mobile client for AVL, fleet management, vehicle security, and recovery
- Autonomous operation with sensor and actuator interaction
- Direct communication via SMS and TCP
- Comfort and security features including voice calls and emergency spy calls
- Driver's logbook and data logging functionalities
- Geofencing for monitoring predefined routes or areas
- Low cost tracking and security solutions
