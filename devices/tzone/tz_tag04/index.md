# TZone - TZ-Tag04

![TZ-Tag04](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-Tag04 is a compact tracking tag designed for monitoring the location and condition of small assets. It combines GPS tracking with RFID 2.4 G wireless communication and an integrated precision sensor to deliver both position awareness and environmental readings such as temperature and humidity. Built for durability, the TZ-Tag04 has an IP65 rating and a lightweight enclosure that makes it easy to attach to a wide range of items.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-Tag04 brings location and environmental data into Plaspy's fleet and asset management environment. Its adjustable transmission settings and long operating life make it suitable for persistent monitoring tasks, while features like temperature and humidity detection and a low power alarm add operational value for use cases that require condition awareness alongside location tracking.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated GPS tracking combined with RFID 2.4 G wireless communication for flexible asset monitoring
- Built in precision sensor with temperature and humidity detection for environmental awareness
- Durable IP65 rated enclosure suitable for use in dusty or wet environments
- Compact and lightweight form factor at 67mm x 51mm x 15mm and 25 g for easy attachment
- Long operating life up to 3 years and a built in battery with low power alarm for maintenance visibility
- Adjustable sending intervals and TX power plus a maximum transmission distance of 30 meters for configurable reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TZ-Tag04 feeds location and condition data into Plaspy so that teams can view, analyze, and act on asset status from a single platform. Plaspy ingests the tag's updates and presents them alongside other fleet telemetry to support operational oversight and reporting.

- Real time location updates and presence tracking within Plaspy's monitoring interface
- Environmental telemetry display for temperature and humidity alongside location history
- Low battery alerts routed through Plaspy notifications to help schedule maintenance
- Historical logs and reporting to review movement and condition trends over time
- Asset grouping and tagging to manage collections of TZ-Tag04 units within Plaspy
- Configurable alerts and reports to match business rules and operational needs

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking small high value assets and equipment across facilities or job sites
- Monitoring temperature sensitive items during storage or transport
- Inventory visibility for warehouses and distribution centers
- Condition monitoring of packaged goods where humidity and temperature matter
- Long term asset deployment where low maintenance and battery life are important

The TZ-Tag04 is a practical choice when you need a small, durable tag that provides both location and environmental context. Its compact size and IP65 protection make it suitable for many asset tracking scenarios, while its configurable transmission behavior and long operating life reduce the need for frequent maintenance.
