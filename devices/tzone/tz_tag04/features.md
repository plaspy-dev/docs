# TZone - TZ-Tag04 Features

This page provides a public overview of the TZone TZ-Tag04 features as they relate to use with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to monitoring, location awareness, and environmental sensing when the device is connected to Plaspy, and explains how those capabilities can appear inside the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on the tracker firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and how the manufacturer implements each function. Where applicable, this page calls out the capabilities described in the public device description while advising verification against manufacturer documentation for device specific or firmware dependent details.

## Feature Overview

The TZ-Tag04 is described as a compact, durable tracker designed to provide location and environmental visibility for belongings and assets. Its practical value comes from a combination of short range wireless reporting, precise sensing for temperature and humidity, adjustable transmission settings for battery life optimization, and a robust enclosure for use in varied conditions.

- Short range wireless location and presence reporting using RFID 2.4 G communication for item awareness
- Built in precision sensor plus temperature and humidity detection for environmental monitoring
- Long lasting 3V built in battery and low power alarm to support extended deployments
- IP65 rated housing for water and dust resistance suited to many field installations
- Adjustable reporting intervals and transmit power to balance update frequency and battery life
- Compact lightweight form factor for easy attachment to valuables and small assets

## Core Features of TZone - TZ-Tag04

- RFID 2.4 G wireless communication for item level location or proximity reporting
- Location and presence monitoring suitable for tracking belongings within the device transmission range
- Built in precision sensor used along with environmental sensing functions
- Temperature detection with stated accuracy of ±0.3°C
- Humidity detection with stated accuracy of ±3.0%RH
- Adjustable sending time intervals to control how often the device reports
- Configurable transmission power to tune range versus battery consumption
- Low power alarm to notify when battery level requires attention
- Durable IP65 enclosure for resistance to dust and water ingress
- Compact dimensions and light weight for unobtrusive attachment

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a TZ-Tag04 is registered and reporting into Plaspy, the platform can surface the core device signals as monitored items and event entries for operational oversight. Plaspy's device handling focuses on turning device reports into location and condition visibility without exposing low level protocol details.

- Location or presence updates appear in device lists and on maps to help locate tracked items
- Temperature and humidity readings are shown in device telemetry so environmental trends can be monitored
- Low power events generate alerts or status flags in Plaspy to prompt battery replacement or inspection
- Adjustable reporting intervals on the tracker translate to configurable update cadence in Plaspy for history and live views
- Plaspy automatically recognizes many tracker protocols and will incorporate the device data into the platform interface for immediate visibility
- Device metadata such as model name, battery state, and recent communication timestamps are recorded for operational tracking

## Typical Use Cases

- Asset tracking for tools, equipment, and personal items within facilities or defined short range areas
- Monitoring temperature and humidity for boxed goods, packages, or small storage compartments
- Inventory presence checks in warehouses or retail back rooms where short range reporting is sufficient
- Long term passive tagging of items where low maintenance and long battery life are priorities
- Field operations where a dust and water resistant enclosure is required
- Situations requiring compact lightweight tags that do not add bulk to the asset

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and exact reporting fields can vary by firmware version and hardware revision
- Battery life and operating life estimates depend on configured transmission intervals and transmit power
- Regional variants or different manufacturing runs may change radio characteristics or certified operating bands
- Installation and attachment method can affect sensing accuracy and wireless transmission performance
- Always consult manufacturer documentation for the most current specification and configuration details

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the TZ-Tag04 centralizes visibility of both location presence and environmental measurements so teams can monitor assets and conditions from one platform. Plaspy collects and presents status, history, and events generated by the tag to support operational workflows such as inventory checks, environmental compliance monitoring, and battery maintenance scheduling.
