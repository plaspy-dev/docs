# TZone - TZ-BT04

![TZ-BT04](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-BT04 is a Bluetooth Low Energy temperature and humidity data logger that utilizes the latest Bluetooth 4.0 technology and Nordic N51822 chipset. This compact device is designed to collect temperature and humidity data from the surrounding environment and transfer it to mobile devices such as smartphones via Bluetooth 4.0. The collected data can be recorded as history data, allowing for comprehensive monitoring and analysis.

With a storage capacity of up to 15,000 pieces of temperature and humidity data, the TZ-BT04 is ideal for use in various applications such as cold chain logistics, archives, labs, and refrigerators. It is small-sized, lightweight, and easily portable, making it convenient for on-the-go monitoring. The device is highly accurate, with a temperature detection range of -40℃ to +125℃ and a humidity detection range of 0% to 100%RH. It boasts a temperature detection accuracy of ±0.5℃ and a humidity detection accuracy of ±4%RH, ensuring reliable and precise measurements.

The TZ-BT04 operates on a built-in 600mAh/3V battery, providing a service life of approximately 1 year depending on the mode of operation. It supports a signal transmission frequency of 2.400 - 2.4835GHz and follows the Bluetooth 4.0 protocol standard. The device features adjustable send intervals, output power, and maximum transmission distance, allowing for customization based on specific monitoring requirements. To further enhance its versatility, the TZ-BT04 can also upload temperature and humidity data to a server through a GPRS network wirelessly.

### Key Features:

- Bluetooth Low Energy temperature and humidity data logger
- Bluetooth 4.0 technology and Nordic N51822 chipset
- Collects and transfers data to mobile devices via Bluetooth 4.0
- Stores up to 15,000 pieces of temperature and humidity data
- Small-sized, lightweight, and easily portable
- Highly accurate temperature and humidity detection
- Built-in 600mAh/3V battery with a service life of 1 year
- Supports adjustable send intervals, output power, and maximum transmission distance
- Uploads data to a server through a GPRS network wirelessly
