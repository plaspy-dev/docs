# TZone - TZ-BT04

<div className="device-logo">
![TZ-BT04](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The TZone TZ-BT04 is a compact Bluetooth Low Energy temperature and humidity data logger built around the Nordic N51822 chipset and Bluetooth 4.0 technology. It collects ambient temperature and relative humidity readings, records up to 15,000 data points, and can transfer stored or live data to mobile devices. The unit is designed for portable, on-the-go monitoring and supports adjustable send intervals and transmission settings to suit different monitoring scenarios.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-BT04 can serve as an environmental telemetry source within Plaspy workflows. Data collected by the logger can be routed into Plaspy's monitoring and reporting tools via supported upload paths, enabling centralized visibility of temperature and humidity history, threshold alerts, and operational reporting. This makes the TZ-BT04 a practical option for organizations that need environmental oversight alongside their asset and fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Bluetooth Low Energy temperature and humidity data logger using Nordic N51822 chipset
- Stores up to 15,000 temperature and humidity records for historical analysis
- Wide measurement ranges: temperature from -40℃ to +125℃ and humidity from 0% to 100%RH
- Reported measurement accuracies of ±0.5℃ for temperature and ±4%RH for humidity
- Small sized, lightweight, and easily portable for mobile or spot monitoring
- Built-in 600mAh/3V power source with an expected service life around one year depending on use
- Configurable send intervals and transmission settings, with option to upload data to a server via GPRS

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TZ-BT04 provides environmental measurements that Plaspy can store, visualize, and alert on alongside other asset data. Plaspy ingests the logger's records through supported upload methods and presents the information within a centralized monitoring environment to support operational decisions and compliance reporting.

- Record and forward temperature and humidity measurements into Plaspy for centralized visibility
- Visualize historical trends and time series of environment data collected by the logger
- Configure threshold alerts in Plaspy to notify teams when readings move outside acceptable ranges
- Generate reports and exports from Plaspy for audits, quality control, and performance reviews
- Correlate environmental data with other tracked assets in Plaspy to add context to transport and storage events

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain logistics monitoring during shipment and storage of temperature sensitive goods
- Continuous monitoring inside refrigerators, freezers, and cold storage units
- Environmental oversight for laboratory samples and research storage
- Archive and museum climate monitoring to protect sensitive collections
- Portable spot checks during transport and facility inspections
