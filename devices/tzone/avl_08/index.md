# TZone - AVL-08

![AVL-08](./logo.jpg)

The TZone AVL-08 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for vehicle security, asset tracking, and fleet management. It supports both single location queries and continual tracking, and includes a range of alarm and monitoring functions such as over speed, low power, geo-fence, tremble, parking, and SOS alerts. The device also offers features for vehicle control and status detection, optional external device connections, recording capability, mileage calculation, and additional sensor support that help address common fleet and asset monitoring needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AVL-08 can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet management environment to provide live location visibility, event alerts, and historical tracking data. Compatibility with Plaspy makes it straightforward to use the AVL-08 within a broader operational workflow, enabling fleet managers and operators to consolidate tracking, alerts, and reporting in one platform while retaining the AVL-08's practical features.

## Key Highlights

- Supports single point and continuous tracking modes for flexible monitoring.
- Multiple alarm types including over speed, geo fence, parking, tremble, low power, and SOS.
- Vehicle control and status features such as door control and gradual engine cut off for enhanced security.
- External device integration options including SD card recording and connectors for RFID readers, cameras, and printers.
- Onboard capabilities for mileage calculation, fuel and oil level detection, optional temperature sensing, and 3D accelerometer based events.
- Two way voice capability and mobile map integration to support operational communication and visualization.
- Built in flash memory for local data storage and support for sending location and event data to remote services.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AVL-08 delivers location updates and alarm events into the Plaspy platform so teams can monitor assets in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy can use the tracker data to generate alerts, visualize routes, and support operational decisions across vehicles and mobile assets.

- Real time location display and continual tracking visibility in Plaspy dashboards and maps.
- Forwarding of alarm events such as geo fence breaches, overspeed, low power, and SOS for immediate notification handling.
- Historical route playback and mileage records to support reporting and operational reviews.
- Integration of external device events and recordings into Plaspy workflows for consolidated incident context.
- Use of recorded data from onboard storage to supplement cloud records and support audits or investigations.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium vehicle fleets requiring tracking, alerts, and mileage reporting.
- Vehicle security and theft prevention with real time alerts and engine control features.
- School bus and passenger transport monitoring to improve safety oversight and route visibility.
- Logistics and delivery operations that need location tracking, route history, and alarm notifications.
- Asset tracking for mobile equipment where external sensors or RFID integration provide additional context.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AVL-08 combines an extensive set of practical features with the ability to integrate into Plaspy, making it a good option for organizations looking to centralize tracking and alerting. Its mixture of continuous tracking, event alarms, external device support, and local recording provides flexibility for a range of operational scenarios without requiring highly specialized hardware changes.

Because Plaspy focuses on visibility, alerts, and reporting, pairing the AVL-08 with the Plaspy platform helps translate the device's events and location streams into actionable operational information.
