# TZone - TZ-AVL301

![TZ-AVL301](./logo.jpg)

Introducing the new TZ-AVL301 3G GPS Tracker from TZone. This advanced tracker offers a range of features and capabilities to help you keep track of your vehicles or assets with ease. With support for both GSM and WCDMA frequencies, you can rely on this tracker to provide accurate and real-time location information. The TZ-AVL301 also supports iButton for driver identification, allowing you to monitor and manage driver activities effectively.

Equipped with a waterproof design \(IP67\), this GPS tracker is built to withstand harsh weather conditions and ensure reliable performance in any environment. It features a flash memory for logging, allowing you to store and retrieve data as needed. The built-in GSM and GPS antennas ensure strong signal reception, while the support for TCP/UDP protocol enables seamless communication with your tracking platform.

One of the standout features of the TZ-AVL301 is its ability to detect ignition and calculate mileage. This information can be invaluable for fleet management and monitoring driver behavior. Additionally, the tracker can send Google map links of the location via SMS, making it easy to track and locate your vehicles or assets on the go. With engine immobilization capability, you have the power to remotely disable the engine in case of theft or unauthorized use.

Technical Specifications:

- Charging Voltage: DC 9-36V
- Dimension: 87mm\*67mm\*21mm
- 3G module: GSM 850/900/1800/1900Mhz \(Custom\), WCDMA 900/2100Mhz
- Flash Memory: 32MBit
- GPS Sensitivity: -165dBm
- GPS Frequency: L1, 1575.42 MHz
- C/A Code: 1.023 MHz chip rate
- Channels: 66 channel all-in-view tracking
- Position Accuracy: 3 meters, 2D RMS
- Velocity Accuracy: 0.1 m/s
- Time Accuracy: 1 us synchronized to GPS time
- Default datum: WGS-84
- Operating temperature: -20°C to 60°C
- Humidity: 5% to 95% Non-condensing
- Voltage: Rechargeable 320mAh battery \(3.7V\)
- Work time: 7 hours in normal mode

With its compact size, reliable performance, and advanced features, the TZ-AVL301 3G GPS Tracker is an excellent choice for fleet management, asset tracking, and personal vehicle monitoring. Stay connected and in control with this powerful GPS tracker from TZone.
