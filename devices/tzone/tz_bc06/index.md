# TZone - TZ-BC06

![TZ-BC06](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-BC06 is a compact and lightweight GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable location tracking. With its creamy white color and sleek design, it is not only functional but also aesthetically pleasing. Measuring just 60\*24\*9 mm and weighing only 15 grams, it is small enough to be easily carried or attached to various objects.

One of the standout features of the TZ-BC06 is its use of the iPhone iBeacon protocol, which utilizes Bluetooth 4.0 technology. This allows for seamless and efficient communication between the tracker and compatible devices. The tracker operates on a 5V DC voltage and has an adjustable broadcasting interval ranging from 0.1s to 3s. The transmitted power can also be adjusted from -30dBm to 4dBm, giving users flexibility in optimizing the tracker's performance.

The TZ-BC06 is compatible with iOS 7.0 and above as well as Android 4.3 and above, making it suitable for a wide range of devices. It also offers added security with support for password-protected connections. In terms of transmission distance, the tracker can reach up to 50-80 meters in an open field, ensuring that you can track your belongings from a reasonable distance.

Overall, the TZone TZ-BC06 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that offers accurate location tracking in a compact and stylish package. Whether you need to keep an eye on your personal belongings or track the whereabouts of your loved ones, this tracker is a great choice.

### Outstanding Features:

- Compact and lightweight design
- Utilizes iPhone iBeacon protocol
- Bluetooth 4.0 technology
- Adjustable broadcasting interval
- Adjustable transmitted power
- Compatible with iOS 7.0 and above, Android 4.3 and above
- Supports password-protected connections
- Transmission distance of 50-80 meters in an open field
