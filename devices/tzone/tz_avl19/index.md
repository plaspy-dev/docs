# TZone - TZ-AVL19

![TZ-AVL19](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-AVL19 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a wide range of features to help you keep track of your vehicles. With support for single location and continual tracking, you can always know the exact whereabouts of your vehicles in real-time. The tracker also comes with various alarm features, including over-speed alarm, low power alarm, geo-fence alarm, tremble alarm, parking alarm, and SOS alarm, ensuring that you are promptly notified of any potential issues or emergencies.

One of the standout features of the TZ-AVL19 is its ability to control car doors close/open and detect the status of the engine on/off. This allows you to remotely manage your vehicles and ensure their security. In case of an emergency, the tracker can also cut off the engine power gradually and safely, providing an added layer of safety and control.

The TZ-AVL19 supports both GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) and SMS communication, giving you flexibility in how you receive and send data. Additionally, the device has a sleep mode that activates when there is no tremble, helping to conserve power and extend battery life.

Some of the outstanding features of the TZone TZ-AVL19 GPS tracker include:

- SD card record \(optional\)
- Ability to connect external RFID Reader, Cameras, Printer \(optional\)
- 3D accelerometer
- Support for sending GPRS data to IP or DNS
- Mileage calculation
- 32Mb flash memory
- Fuel/oil level detection
- Temperature sensors \(optional\)
- Two-way conversation
- Mobile Google map
- Support for ibutton

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the TZone TZ-AVL19 GPS tracker is an excellent choice for fleet management, vehicle tracking, and ensuring the safety and security of your vehicles.
