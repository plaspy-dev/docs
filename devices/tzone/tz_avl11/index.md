# TZone - TZ-AVL11

![TZ-AVL11](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-AVL11 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for vehicle and asset monitoring. It combines built-in GSM and GPS antennas, an integrated 2.4G RFID receiver, and a range of onboard sensors to provide continuous location reporting and basic telemetry. The unit includes a built-in battery and a mini USB port for charging and firmware updates, and it supports multiple reporting methods such as GPRS, SMS, and software based monitoring to fit a variety of deployment preferences.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-AVL11 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized fleet oversight. Its low power consumption, on device memory for stored history, and configurable alarm functions make it useful for long term deployments and routine fleet operations. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker can help teams consolidate tracking, alerts, and history in a single platform for operational visibility.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated 2.4G RFID receiver for inventory or access related workflows
- Built-in GPS and GSM antennas with position accuracy reported around 10 to 15 meters
- Onboard battery with reported 4500 mAh capacity for backup power and extended tracking
- Multiple reporting options including GPRS and SMS and support for computer or smartphone based monitoring
- Extensive alarm set including over speed, geo fence, low battery, power cut, motion, and SOS
- On device memory for recording historical data and a mini USB port for firmware updates

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TZ-AVL11 can transmit its location and status data to Plaspy where it becomes part of a centralized fleet management view. Plaspy ingests the tracker events and presents them in dashboards, maps, and reports so operators can act on live and historical information.

- Real time location display and history playback inside Plaspy maps
- Alerts and notifications forwarded to Plaspy for over speed, geo fence breaches, low battery, and SOS events
- Device status and event logs retained for reporting and audit within Plaspy
- Use of RFID read events and external sensor states to enrich asset context in the Plaspy platform where supported
- Microphone and listen in capability noted on the device can be surfaced in Plaspy if enabled and configured

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for small to medium sized commercial operations
- Asset monitoring where RFID identification adds inventory or driver association value
- Security and anti theft workflows using motion alerts, power cut detection, and SOS signaling
- Long term deployments that benefit from low power consumption and onboard data storage
- Temperature sensitive cargo monitoring where a temperature sensor can provide additional telemetry

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TZ-AVL11 is a practical choice for organizations that need a combined location tracker and RFID capable device with a balanced feature set. Its reported long battery standby, onboard memory, and multiple alarm types make it suitable for mixed use across vehicles and fixed assets. When integrated with Plaspy, the device contributes actionable location and event data to a single operational view, simplifying monitoring and reporting for dispatchers and fleet managers.

Because the TZ-AVL11 supports several common reporting methods and has built in features for audio monitoring, RFID reads, and temperature sensing, it can cover diverse operational needs without requiring multiple specialized devices. Pairing this hardware with Plaspy allows teams to centralize tracking, set up notifications, and review historical movement in context.
