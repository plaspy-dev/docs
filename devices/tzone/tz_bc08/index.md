# TZone - TZ-BC08

![TZ-BC08](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-BC08 is a compact and lightweight GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable tracking capabilities. With its creamy white color and sleek design, it is not only functional but also aesthetically pleasing. Measuring just 65\*50\*10 mm and weighing only 15 grams, it is easy to carry and can be discreetly placed in various objects or attached to personal belongings.

One of the standout features of the TZ-BC08 is its use of the iPhone iBeacon protocol, which utilizes Bluetooth 4.0 technology. This allows for seamless and efficient communication with compatible devices, ensuring a stable and reliable connection. The tracker is compatible with iOS 7.0 and above, as well as Android 4.3 and above, making it suitable for a wide range of users.

The TZ-BC08 is powered by a CR 2450 battery, providing a voltage of 3V DC. With a broadcasting interval of 0.1 to 3 seconds and a transmitted power range of -30 to 4 dBm \(adjustable\), it offers flexibility in terms of customization and power consumption. The tracker has a transmission distance of 50-90 meters in an open field, allowing for accurate tracking even over long distances.

In terms of security, the TZ-BC08 supports password protection for connection, ensuring that only authorized users can access the tracker's data. With a working time of 1 to 1.5 years, it offers long-lasting performance without the need for frequent battery replacements. Whether you need to track personal belongings, vehicles, or even pets, the TZone TZ-BC08 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that delivers on both functionality and style.

### Outstanding Features:

- Compact and lightweight design
- Utilizes iPhone iBeacon protocol
- Bluetooth 4.0 technology
- Compatible with iOS 7.0 and above, Android 4.3 and above
- CR 2450 battery with 3V DC voltage
- Broadcasting interval of 0.1 to 3 seconds
- Transmitted power range of -30 to 4 dBm \(adjustable\)
- Transmission distance of 50-90 meters in an open field
- Supports password protection for connection
- Working time of 1 to 1.5 years
