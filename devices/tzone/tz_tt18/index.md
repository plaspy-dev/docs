# TZone - TZ-TT18

![TZ-TT18](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-TT18 is a single use temperature and humidity transmitter designed to collect high precision environmental data and send it to a server via mobile data. It integrates a temperature and humidity sensing element together with a GSM module and requires insertion of a SIM card to operate. The device is intended for intermittent reporting, remaining in a low power sleep state until scheduled transmissions or user activation, which helps extend operational lifetime for shipment and storage monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-TT18 can forward its measured temperature and humidity readings to the Plaspy platform for monitoring and reporting. Its compact form factor and timed wake up behavior make it suitable for discreet placement in shipment packaging, cold chain containers, and storage environments where periodic, reliable environmental snapshots are needed. When paired with Plaspy, data collected by the TZ-TT18 can be viewed alongside other fleet and asset information to support operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Single use temperature and humidity transmitter with integrated GSM communications
- Designed for low power operation with sleep and scheduled wake behavior for longer life
- High precision temperature measurement with ±0.3℃ accuracy and wide measurement range
- Humidity accuracy of ±3% across a broad humidity range
- Can store a large number of GPRS data entries for later transmission
- Compact dimensions 80mm x 64mm x 25mm for discreet placement
- LED indicators for temperature and GSM status to aid basic on device checks

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TZ-TT18 transmits its recorded temperature and humidity readings to the Plaspy platform where data is consolidated, visualized, and made available for operational use. Plaspy can ingest the device's periodic GPRS uploads and present them alongside other monitored assets to give teams a unified view of environmental conditions and shipment status.

- Centralized visualization of temperature and humidity readings received from the TZ-TT18
- Scheduled or event driven data points appear in Plaspy reports for historical analysis
- Alerting and notifications in Plaspy based on thresholds or gaps in expected transmissions
- Assignment of individual TZ-TT18 units to shipments, storage locations, or asset records
- Exportable data and logs for compliance reporting and audit trails

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain logistics monitoring for temperature sensitive shipments
- Medicine and vaccine storage checks during transport and in temporary storage
- Refrigeration spot checks in distribution and retail environments
- Single use environmental monitoring for one way shipments or short term storage
- Remote or temporary storage locations where periodic checks are sufficient

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TZ-TT18 is a practical option for organizations that need a compact, single use transmitter to capture precise temperature and humidity readings and deliver them to a central platform. Its built in communications and sleep wake design reduce operational overhead, and when combined with Plaspy the device becomes part of a broader monitoring and reporting workflow that supports cold chain integrity and asset oversight.

Because the TZ-TT18 is engineered for intermittent reporting and discrete placement, it is especially well suited to scenarios where continuous real time tracking is not required but documented environmental records are important. Plaspy provides the visibility, alerts, and reporting that let those records be acted on and archived alongside other fleet and asset data.
