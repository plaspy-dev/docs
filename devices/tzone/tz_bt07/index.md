# TZone - TZ-BT07

<div className="device-logo">
![TZ-BT07](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The TZone TZ-BT07 is a compact, lightweight tracker designed to deliver reliable location and proximity reporting for personal items and small assets. In a creamy white housing measuring 86 x 55 x 55 mm and weighing approximately 40 grams, the device is easy to carry and conceal. The TZ-BT07 uses the iPhone iBeacon protocol with Bluetooth 4.0 connectivity, supports common smartphone platforms, and offers configurable broadcast intervals and transmit power to balance visibility and battery life.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-BT07 can be incorporated into Plaspy tracking workflows to extend visibility to small or portable assets. Its portable form factor and long working life make it practical for deployments where discreet monitoring and proximity alerts are useful. Plaspy can use the TZ-BT07 to provide consolidated location views, event notifications, and reporting alongside other fleet and asset devices on the platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design suitable for discreet placement on personal items or small assets
- Uses iBeacon Bluetooth 4.0 technology for proximity aware connectivity with supported devices
- Compatible with iOS 7.0 and above and Android 4.3 and above for broad device support
- Long working life advertised at around three years on its included CR3032 battery
- Adjustable broadcasting interval from 0.1 to 3 seconds to tune update frequency
- Configurable transmit power for flexibility in effective range and signal strength

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TZ-BT07 integrates with Plaspy as a compact proximity and tracking device that enhances asset visibility within the platform. Plaspy can receive and interpret the presence and reporting from TZ-BT07 units to provide operational awareness and historical records.

- Centralized visibility of TZ-BT07 devices within Plaspy dashboards and maps for easy monitoring
- Proximity based alerts and notifications when a tracked item is detected or goes out of range
- Event logging to capture broadcast intervals and presence events for reporting and audit
- Grouping and tagging of TZ-BT07 units inside Plaspy to organize items by site, user, or purpose
- Historical reports to analyze movement patterns and presence timelines over time

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking small personal belongings such as bags, keys, or luggage for recovery and monitoring
- Asset tagging of portable equipment where low weight and concealability are important
- Proximity alerts for controlled areas or checkpoints where presence needs to be detected
- Temporary deployments for inventory checks or short term asset oversight
- Combining with other Plaspy devices to provide comprehensive coverage across vehicles and portable items
