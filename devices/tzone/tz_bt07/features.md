# TZone - TZ-BT07 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the TZone TZ-BT07 and how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It summarizes the device description and explains practical functions and integration context users can expect when adding the TZ-BT07 into Plaspy for asset and proximity visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior depend on the device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. The information below is based on the supplied model description and is intended as a practical, non-sensitive summary rather than a replacement for the manufacturer's technical documentation.

## Feature Overview

The TZ-BT07 is described as a compact, lightweight tracking beacon that uses the iBeacon Bluetooth 4.0 protocol to provide proximity and presence information. The tracker emphasizes portability, long nominal battery life, and adjustable broadcast settings, making it suitable for tagging personal items and assets that need regular location or proximity checks.

- Compact and lightweight form factor for easy placement or concealment
- iBeacon Bluetooth 4.0 based broadcasting for proximity detection and integration with compatible platforms
- Broad device compatibility with iOS 7.0 and above and Android 4.3 and above
- Long nominal working time from a CR3032 battery for low maintenance operation
- Adjustable broadcast interval and transmit power to balance update rate and range
- Password protection to restrict unauthorized access to the device settings

## Core Features of TZone - TZ-BT07

- iBeacon protocol support using Bluetooth 4.0 for beacon style proximity signaling
- Compatibility with iOS 7.0 and higher and Android 4.3 and higher
- Adjustable broadcasting interval from very frequent to infrequent updates
- Configurable transmit power to tailor effective broadcast range
- Long life CR3032 battery design described with multi year working time
- Lightweight and compact dimensions for discreet placement or carrying
- Password protection for basic access control to device settings

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can be used as the platform to collect and present presence, last seen, and proximity related information reported by devices like the TZ-BT07. When the tracker is used in conjunction with Plaspy, the beacon broadcasts and device status can be surfaced as location or presence events within the platform, subject to how the device is installed and how its messages are forwarded to Plaspy.

- Presence and last seen indicators in Plaspy reflect the tracker broadcast and update cadence
- Adjustable broadcast interval translates into more or less frequent visibility updates within Plaspy
- Transmit power and effective range affect how and where the tracker is detected in Plaspy maps and lists
- Password protected configuration may limit who can change device settings before the device is connected to Plaspy
- Compatibility with mobile platforms facilitates on site verification and pairing workflows when needed
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and surfaces the device without manual protocol selection

## Typical Use Cases

- Tagging personal items for proximity alerts and last seen history
- Asset presence checks in offices, storage areas, or mixed inventory environments
- Short range location and proximity monitoring for events or temporary deployments
- Mobile device assisted discovery when staff use compatible iOS or Android devices
- Low maintenance tracking where multi year battery life reduces service visits
- Situations that require discrete and lightweight tracking beacons
