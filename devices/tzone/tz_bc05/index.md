# TZone - TZ-BC05

![TZ-BC05](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-BC05 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable location tracking. With its creamy white color and sleek design, it is both stylish and discreet. Measuring just 45\*45\*21 mm and weighing only 40 grams, it is lightweight and easy to carry or attach to any object.

One of the standout features of the TZ-BC05 is its use of the iPhone iBeacon protocol, which utilizes Bluetooth 4.0 technology. This allows for seamless and efficient communication with compatible devices, ensuring a stable and reliable connection. The tracker is compatible with iOS 7.0 and above, as well as Android 4.3 and above, making it suitable for a wide range of smartphones and tablets.

The TZ-BC05 is powered by a CR 2477 battery, providing a long-lasting and reliable power source. With a transmission distance of 50-143 meters in an open field, it offers a wide coverage area for tracking purposes. The broadcasting interval can be adjusted between 0.1 and 3 seconds, allowing for customization based on specific tracking needs. The transmitted power is also adjustable, ranging from -30 to 4 dBm.

In terms of security, the TZ-BC05 supports password protection for connection, ensuring that only authorized users can access the tracker's data. With a working time of 1.5 to 2.5 years, it offers long-term tracking capabilities without the need for frequent battery replacements. Whether you need to track personal belongings, vehicles, or even pets, the TZone TZ-BC05 is a reliable and efficient choice.

### Key Features:

- Compact and lightweight design
- iPhone iBeacon protocol with Bluetooth 4.0 technology
- Compatible with iOS 7.0 and above, Android 4.3 and above
- CR 2477 battery for long-lasting power
- Adjustable broadcasting interval and transmitted power
- Supports password protection for connection
- Transmission distance of 50-143 meters in an open field
- Working time of 1.5 to 2.5 years
