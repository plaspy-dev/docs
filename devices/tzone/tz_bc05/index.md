# TZone - TZ-BC05

![TZ-BC05](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-BC05 is a compact and lightweight tracking device designed to provide reliable location and proximity monitoring. It combines a discreet creamy white enclosure with a small footprint of 45 x 45 x 21 mm and a weight of approximately 40 grams. The TZ-BC05 uses the iPhone iBeacon protocol and Bluetooth 4.0 technology for broadcasted signals, supports common mobile platforms, and offers adjustable broadcast parameters and a long operational life from a CR 2477 battery.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-BC05 is relevant for users who need a small, low profile beacon that integrates into a centralized tracking and fleet management workflow. Plaspy can surface the presence, approximate location, and operational status of compatible beacons like the TZ-BC05 alongside other devices, helping teams monitor assets and respond to location based events from a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and discreet form factor at 45 x 45 x 21 mm and about 40 grams for easy placement on assets
- Uses iPhone iBeacon protocol with Bluetooth 4.0 for consistent proximity broadcasts
- Compatible with iOS 7.0 and above and Android 4.3 and above for broad smartphone support
- Long life CR 2477 battery with working time around 1.5 to 2.5 years depending on settings
- Adjustable broadcasting interval from 0.1 to 3 seconds and transmitted power from -30 to 4 dBm for flexible coverage
- Password protected connection to help limit access to authorized devices
- Transmission range of 50 to 143 meters in open field conditions for extended coverage

## How It Works with Plaspy

Plaspy can present information from compatible TZ-BC05 devices to provide operational visibility and simple proximity based insights. The TZ-BC05's broadcasted signals and configurable intervals allow Plaspy to detect presence and report status where supported, and to include the device in monitoring, alerting, and reporting workflows.

- Show device presence and recent signal activity on Plaspy maps and device lists
- Include TZ-BC05 units in asset inventories for easier tracking of small items and equipment
- Trigger proximity or presence alerts based on detected broadcasts and user defined rules
- Use reporting and history in Plaspy to review past presence events and activity windows
- Manage device metadata and identifiers within Plaspy for operational oversight
- Surface operational hints such as last seen time and basic status where the device and platform provide that information

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking personal items and small valuable assets that benefit from a low profile beacon
- Locating luggage, backpacks, or equipment in shared facilities or transit situations
- Pet tracking for short to medium range presence detection and recovery assistance
- Inventory and proximity monitoring in warehouses or retail environments
- Supplementing vehicle or fleet workflows where short range beaconing is useful for specific items
