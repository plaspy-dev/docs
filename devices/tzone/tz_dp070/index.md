# TZone - TZ-DP070

<div className="device-logo">
![TZ-DP070](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The TZone TZ-DP070 is a purpose built temperature monitor intended for secondary development on the Windows CE 6.0 operating system. It provides a visual interface that receives temperature data via RS485 and is designed to pair with an RFID reader and temperature tag system. The device transmits collected measurements using 2.4G RF wireless technology and includes a 7 inch display, SD and MMC card support, Ethernet and USB connectivity, and a resistive touch screen for local operation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-DP070 can be used to bring temperature readings and tag identification into a centralized fleet or asset monitoring workflow. Plaspy can display and archive the monitor's data, provide operational oversight, and include the TZ-DP070 in alerts and reports alongside other devices. While the TZ-DP070 is primarily a temperature monitor rather than a GPS tracker, its compatibility with Plaspy makes it useful where environmental monitoring and tag based identification are part of a broader tracking or fleet management strategy.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for secondary development on Windows CE 6.0 for customization and integration.
- Visual 7 inch 800x480 display with resistive touch for on device interaction.
- Works with RFID readers and temperature tags and transmits data via 2.4G RF wireless.
- RS485 input for receiving sensor temperature data and local display of readings.
- Supports SD and MMC storage plus USB and 100M Ethernet for connectivity and logging.
- IP65 rated front panel for dust and water resistance in many operational environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TZ-DP070 can feed temperature and tag identification data into Plaspy so teams can monitor conditions and assets from the Plaspy platform. Plaspy aggregates incoming data and makes it available for visualization, alerting, and reporting across a fleet or asset inventory.

- Centralized visibility of temperature readings and tag associations within Plaspy.
- Configurable alerts and notifications in Plaspy for threshold breaches and status changes.
- Historical logging and reporting using data received from the device and local card storage.
- Device status and recent activity displayed alongside other Plaspy managed devices for operational oversight.
- Use Plaspy dashboards to correlate temperature data with asset records and RFID tag identities.

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring refrigerated goods or compartments where temperature logging is required.
- Cold chain points such as depots, storage rooms, and transfer stations.
- Asset identification combined with environmental monitoring using RFID temperature tags.
- Industrial or commercial environments that need a rugged front display and remote data collection.
