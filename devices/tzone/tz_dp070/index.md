# TZone - TZ-DP070

![TZ-DP070](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-DP070 is a temperature monitor that is designed for secondary development based on the WinCE6.0 operating system. It features a visual display through RS485 to receive temperature data from a sensor. This temperature monitor is specifically designed to work with an RFID reader and temperature tag, and it sends data using 2.4G RF wireless transmission technology.

The TZ-DP070 temperature monitor offers a range of impressive features. It has a four-wire resistive touch screen for easy navigation and operation. It also supports SD and MMC cards, allowing for convenient storage and retrieval of data. With its 100M Ethernet interface, it provides fast and reliable connectivity. The monitor operates on a voltage range of 12-24V, with 12V being the recommended voltage. It also has a USB interface for additional connectivity options.

In terms of display, the TZ-DP070 features a 7-inch screen with a resolution of 800x480 pixels. It supports either 64K colors or 260K colors, providing vibrant and clear visuals. The monitor is designed with an IP65 rating on the front, making it resistant to dust and water. With a net weight of 0.74KG and dimensions of 206x142x45mm, it is compact and lightweight, making it easy to install and transport.

The TZ-DP070 temperature monitor operates in a temperature range of -10℃ to +60℃, allowing it to function effectively in various environments. It also has a humidity working range of 10% to 90% \(non-condensing\). The monitor runs on the Windows CE 6.0 R3 operating system, providing a stable and user-friendly interface. Additionally, it features LED backlighting with a working life of over 25,000 hours, ensuring long-lasting performance.

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the TZone TZ-DP070 temperature monitor is an excellent choice for applications that require accurate temperature monitoring and data transmission. Whether it's for industrial, commercial, or personal use, this temperature monitor offers the functionality and convenience needed to meet your temperature monitoring needs.
