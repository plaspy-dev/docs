# TZone - TZ-BR04

![TZ-BR04](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-BR04 is an industrial GPS tracker designed for robust location sensing and monitoring in large outdoor and harsh environments. Operating in the ISM microwave frequency band, it provides reliable signal reception over distances up to 50 meters. The device is built with an industrial-grade enclosure, an external antenna for improved reception, and a wide operating temperature range from -40℃ to +60℃, making it suitable for demanding conditions. Its compact dimensions (L78mm x W67mm x H27mm) and omni-directional identification angle allow flexible placement across a variety of assets.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the TZ-BR04 can be integrated into fleet and asset management workflows to provide consistent location visibility and operational oversight. Its Bluetooth 4.0 support and RS232/RS485 output modes offer flexible connectivity options for installers and system integrators, while the model’s sensitivity rating supports steady reception in challenging environments. Plaspy users can add TZ-BR04 devices to monitoring groups to leverage the platform’s mapping, alerting, and reporting features for day to day fleet operations.

## Key Highlights

- Industrial grade design built for harsh environmental conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TZ-BR04 supplies location and device status information that Plaspy presents on maps and in operational dashboards. Plaspy ingests the tracker data to provide ongoing visibility and historical records for fleet and asset managers.

- Real-time location visibility on Plaspy maps for monitoring moving assets
- Fleet monitoring and grouping to manage multiple TZ-BR04 units across sites
- Alerting and notifications for events such as entry or exit of defined areas
- Historical reporting and playback to analyze past movements and operational trends
- Operational oversight via device lists and summary views for status checks
