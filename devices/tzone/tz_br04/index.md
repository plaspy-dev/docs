# TZone - TZ-BR04

![TZ-BR04](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-BR04 is a GPS tracker designed for industrial use. It operates in the ISM microwave frequency band, providing a reliable and secure signal reception over a long distance of up to 50 meters. This makes it ideal for applications that require tracking and monitoring in large areas or outdoor environments.

With its industrial-grade product design, the TZ-BR04 is built to withstand harsh conditions and deliver consistent performance. It features a Bluetooth 4.0 protocol standard, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of devices and systems. The tracker supports both RS232 and RS485 output modes, allowing for flexible connectivity options.

One of the standout features of the TZ-BR04 is its impressive receiving sensitivity of -85dBm at 1Mbps, ensuring reliable and accurate data transmission. The external antenna type further enhances signal reception, ensuring a strong and stable connection. The tracker also boasts a wide working temperature range of -40℃ to +60℃, making it suitable for use in extreme weather conditions.

With its compact dimensions of L78mm x W67mm x H27mm, the TZ-BR04 is easy to install and can be discreetly placed in various locations. It offers an omni-directional identification angle, allowing for efficient tracking from any direction. Whether you need to monitor assets, vehicles, or personnel, the TZone TZ-BR04 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that delivers exceptional performance.
