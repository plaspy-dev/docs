# TZone - TZ-AVL10

<div className="device-logo">
![TZ-AVL10](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The TZone TZ-AVL10 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for flexible tracking and monitoring of mobile assets. It offers extended standby operation exceeding three months, supports tracking via software or cell phone, and provides a range of alarm functions including over-speed, low power, and geo-fence alerts. The device also includes features for roaming cost management, status detection for car doors and engine on/off, and the ability to perform a controlled engine power cut-off in emergency situations. Communication options listed for this model include GPRS and SMS, and firmware updates can be applied over the air.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-AVL10 can feed location updates and alarm events into Plaspy's fleet monitoring platform to give operators consolidated visibility and reporting. Plaspy can present the device's position history, alert activity, and status events alongside other fleet devices, helping teams manage operations, track exceptions, and respond to incidents from a single platform. Compatibility makes the TZ-AVL10 a practical option for organizations that want to combine the device's long standby and alarm features with Plaspy's operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Very long standby time of more than three months for reduced maintenance and fewer recharges
- Flexible tracking options by software or cell phone for on demand access to location data
- Built in alarm suite including over speed, low power, and geo fence alerts for event driven monitoring
- Roaming feature to help manage cross region communication costs
- Remote detection and control capabilities for car doors and engine on off status
- Gradual and safe engine power cut off capability intended for emergency immobilization
- GPRS and SMS communication with over the air firmware upgrade support

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TZ-AVL10 transmits its location and alarm events so they appear in the Plaspy dashboard alongside other tracked assets. Plaspy ingests position reports and alerts from compatible devices and makes that information available for live monitoring, historical review, and operational reporting.

- Real time location visibility and breadcrumb history for route review and auditing
- Alarm and event notifications forwarded to Plaspy for immediate operator attention
- Geofence events and over speed alerts surfaced in platform dashboards and reports
- Historical reporting and exportable logs to support analysis of vehicle utilization and incidents
- Status and control events from the tracker logged in Plaspy so teams can see door and engine on off activity
- Use of SMS or GPRS communication channels ensures the device can report even where one method is preferred

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle monitoring for delivery, service, and sales teams that need long standby tracking
- Asset tracking for periodically used vehicles or trailers where extended battery life is important
- Security and recovery scenarios leveraging geo fence alerts and remote detection of door or engine state
- Cost conscious cross region tracking where roaming features help reduce communications expense
- Emergency response workflows that benefit from a controlled engine immobilization option
