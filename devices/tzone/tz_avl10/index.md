# TZone - TZ-AVL10

![TZ-AVL10](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-AVL10 GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device that offers a range of features to help you track and monitor your vehicles. With its long standby time of over three months, you can rest assured that your tracker will keep working for extended periods without needing frequent recharging. Whether you want to track your vehicles using software or your cell phone, the TZ-AVL10 has you covered. It supports both options, giving you flexibility and convenience in monitoring your assets.

One of the standout features of the TZ-AVL10 is its ability to send various alarms, including over-speed alarm, low power alarm, and geo-fence alarm. These alarms help you stay informed about any unusual activities or events related to your vehicles. Additionally, the tracker offers a roaming feature to save costs, making it an economical choice for tracking your assets.

The TZ-AVL10 also provides control and detection capabilities for your vehicles. You can remotely control or detect the car doors and engine on/off status, giving you an added layer of security and control. In case of emergencies, the tracker can gradually and safely cut off the engine power, ensuring the safety of your vehicles and personnel.

With its GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) and SMS support, the TZ-AVL10 allows for seamless communication and data transmission. You can easily upgrade the firmware via GPRS, ensuring that your tracker is always up to date with the latest features and improvements.

Some of the outstanding features of the TZone TZ-AVL10 GPS tracker include:

- Long standby time of over three months
- Track by software or cell phone
- Over-speed alarm, low power alarm, and geo-fence alarm
- Roaming feature to save costs
- Control or detect car doors/engine on/off
- Gradual and safe engine power cut-off in emergencies
- GPRS \(TCP/UDP\) and SMS support
- OTA firmware upgrade via GPRS

The TZone TZ-AVL10 GPS tracker is a reliable and feature-packed device that offers a range of capabilities to help you track and monitor your vehicles. With its long standby time, versatile tracking options, and advanced alarm features, it is an excellent choice for fleet management, asset tracking, and personal vehicle security.
