# TZone - TZ-RD05

![TZ-RD05](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-RD05 is a 2.4G RFID reader designed to manage multiple 2.4G RFID tags including TZ-Tag01, TZ-Tag02, TZ-Tag03, TZ-Tag04, and TZ-Tag201. It provides omnidirectional tag detection with a reading distance up to 50 m, and offers RS485 and LAN communication options for integration with upper management systems. Built for demanding environments, the TZ-RD05 has an IP55 protection level and a wide operating temperature range, and its compact cylindrical form is intended for ceiling mounted installation.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-RD05 can feed tag read events and location context into the Plaspy fleet and asset management platform to increase operational visibility. Organizations using Plaspy can combine reads from TZ-RD05 units with other telemetry and data sources to support inventory control, asset location, access oversight, and reporting within the Plaspy environment.

## Key Highlights

- Built for 2.4 GHz tag management supporting multiple TZ tag models
- Omnidirectional identification for consistent reads from any direction
- Long read distance up to 50 m for broader coverage areas
- RS485 and LAN communication interfaces for flexible system integration
- IP55 protection and wide operating temperature range for harsh environments
- Firmware upgrade support via serial connection to maintain device longevity
- Compact cylindrical design suitable for ceiling mounted deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TZ-RD05 delivers tag read events and device status that Plaspy can ingest to improve real time visibility and historical tracking of tagged assets. Once connected, the reader becomes a source of location and presence data that Plaspy can display, filter, and report on alongside other fleet or site information.

- Send tag detection events into Plaspy to show current asset presence and last known location
- Use Plaspy alerts to notify teams when tagged items enter or leave designated zones
- Consolidate tag reads for inventory reconciliation and operational audits in Plaspy reports
- Visualize reader coverage and tag interactions on Plaspy dashboards and maps
- Combine TZ-RD05 data with other Plaspy monitored devices for unified oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Asset tracking in warehouses and production facilities where broad read range is needed
- Inventory management and automated stock monitoring using ceiling mounted readers
- Access control and personnel or equipment presence detection in controlled areas
- Long range identification of tagged containers, pallets, or high value items
- Site level monitoring where environmental durability and wide temperature tolerance are required
