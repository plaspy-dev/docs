# TZone - TZ-AVL05 3G

<div className="device-logo">
![TZ-AVL05 3G](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The TZone TZ-AVL05 3G is a vehicle GPS tracker designed for real time location monitoring and vehicle security. It combines GPS and GSM tracking modules to provide periodic position reports, and includes a suite of alarms such as distance change, speed alerts, SOS, GPS antenna cut, power supply cut, and GPS no signal alarms. The device also supports features commonly used in vehicle oversight including geo fence alerts, mileage reporting, fuel level monitoring, engine and door check alarms, and a G sensor for sudden movement detection.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TZ-AVL05 3G can integrate into a fleet management workflow to deliver location feeds and event alerts to the Plaspy platform. Its ability to store data when signal is lost and to report periodically helps maintain continuity of tracking records, while optional inputs and storage expand its usefulness across different vehicle and fleet scenarios. Plaspy users can take advantage of those capabilities to improve visibility, event awareness, and reporting across a mixed device fleet.

## Key Highlights

- Real time GPS plus GSM based positioning with periodic location reporting for continuous visibility
- Comprehensive anti theft alerts including SOS alarm, antenna cut and power cut notifications
- Event driven alarms such as speed and distance change alerts for operational oversight
- Data storage when offline to prevent gaps in tracking history during signal loss
- Fleet oriented outputs including mileage reports and fuel monitoring for operational metrics
- Optional extensions available such as iButton driver ID, temperature sensor, RS232 interface and TF card storage

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TZ-AVL05 3G supplies location updates and alarm events so Plaspy can present live positions, history, and alerts in a unified fleet view. The device sends periodic positioning and event data which Plaspy uses to power monitoring, alerting, and reporting workflows for fleet operators.

- Live location display and historical playback for route review and analysis
- Forwarding of critical alarms to Plaspy so operators can act on SOS, power or antenna cut, and geo fence breaches
- Aggregated mileage and activity reporting to support maintenance schedules and operational KPIs
- Handling of stored data after offline periods so Plaspy can reconcile missed telemetry when connectivity is restored
- Use of optional inputs and accessories to enrich device data available inside the Plaspy platform

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for light and commercial vehicles requiring real time tracking and periodic reporting
- Vehicle security and anti theft monitoring with immediate alerts for tampering or unexpected movement
- Rental and lease vehicle oversight including mileage and usage tracking
- Delivery and logistics operations that need location visibility and route history
- Asset monitoring where optional sensors and external interfaces are used to capture additional status information
