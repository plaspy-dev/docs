# TZone - TZ-AVL05 3G

![TZ-AVL05 3G](./logo.jpg)

The TZone TZ-AVL05 3G GPS tracker is a versatile and reliable device designed to provide real-time tracking and anti-theft features for vehicles. With its GPS+GSM double module tracking, you can easily monitor the location of your vehicle at any time. The tracker sends positioning data periodically, allowing you to keep track of the vehicle's movements. It also features distance change and speed alarms, alerting you to any unexpected changes in the vehicle's location or speed.

One of the standout features of the TZ-AVL05 3G is its anti-theft and alarm functions. It includes an SOS alarm, GPS antenna cut alarm, power supply cut alarm, and GPS no signal alarm, ensuring that you are immediately notified in case of any suspicious activity or tampering with the tracker. Additionally, the tracker supports remote fuel and power cut, engine and door check alarms, geo-fence alarm, and fuel level monitoring, providing comprehensive security for your vehicle.

In addition to its tracking and anti-theft features, the TZ-AVL05 3G offers a range of other useful functions. It supports TZONE standard protocol and can store data when there is no signal, ensuring that no tracking data is lost. The tracker also provides mileage reports, low voltage alarms, G-sensor function for detecting sudden movements or impacts, and anti-shifting of static GPS. With OTA \(Over-The-Air\) capabilities, you can easily update the tracker's firmware remotely.

The TZ-AVL05 3G offers several optional features to enhance its functionality. These include an iButton for driver identification, a temperature sensor \(18B20\), and an RS232 interface to connect a camera, RFID reader, or other devices. The tracker also supports TF card storage for data backup.

With its compact dimensions of 118mm\*93mm\*31mm and a weight of just 0.26KG, the TZ-AVL05 3G is easy to install and conceal in a vehicle. It operates on an external voltage range of 9V to 36V and includes a backup battery of 850mAh/3.7V. The tracker has excellent GPS sensitivity \(-165dBm\) and comes with external antennas for better reception of GPS, GSM, and RF signals. It has a power consumption of less than 100mA in active mode and can operate in temperatures ranging from -20℃ to +60℃.

With its advanced features and reliable performance, the TZone TZ-AVL05 3G GPS tracker is an excellent choice for fleet management, vehicle security, and personal tracking applications.
