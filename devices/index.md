## What Is Plaspy?

Plaspy is a software platform for GPS tracking, fleet management, and telemetry monitoring. Businesses and GPS service providers use Plaspy to monitor vehicles, assets, and connected sensors from a single platform.

With Plaspy, you can:

- Track vehicles and assets in real time
- Create geofences and alerts
- Monitor ignition, temperature, and sensor data
- Manage fleets from a centralized platform
- Analyze activity through reports and telemetry data

## Why Hardware Compatibility Matters

Choosing the right GPS tracker is an important part of a successful deployment. Different devices offer different capabilities depending on the project requirements, such as:

- Real-time GPS tracking
- Ignition detection
- Geofence alerts
- Temperature monitoring
- Driver behavior analysis
- Fuel and CAN data reading
- Bluetooth sensor support
- Asset and trailer tracking
- OBD-II plug-and-play installation
- Remote fleet supervision

Plaspy is designed to support a broad ecosystem of tracking hardware so that companies, installers, and fleet operators can centralize monitoring in one platform.

## Need Help Choosing a GPS Tracker?

If you are not sure which GPS device is the best fit for your project, it is usually better to evaluate the device based on:

- Installation type
- Network availability
- Required sensors
- Vehicle type
- Reporting needs
- Regional hardware availability

Some businesses need a compact plug-and-play tracker, while others need advanced telemetry, Bluetooth accessories, or temperature monitoring for refrigerated transport. Plaspy can support both simple and advanced deployments depending on the selected hardware.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What GPS devices are compatible with Plaspy?
Plaspy supports multiple GPS tracker brands and models used for vehicle tracking, fleet management, asset monitoring, and sensor-based telemetry.

### Can I use my existing GPS tracker with Plaspy?
In many cases, yes. Compatibility depends on the device protocol, firmware, and available communication settings.

### Does Plaspy support fleet tracking hardware with sensors?
Yes. Many compatible devices support features such as ignition status, geofences, temperature sensors, Bluetooth accessories, and telemetry data.

### Is this catalog only for vehicle trackers?
No. While many devices are designed for vehicles, some models are also used for asset tracking, cargo monitoring, trailers, and IoT telemetry use cases.
