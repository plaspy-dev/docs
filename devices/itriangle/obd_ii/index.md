# iTriangle - OBD II

![OBD II](./tracker.png)

The OBD II is a professional-grade OBD-II GNSS-cellular GPS tracker built for fast, plug-and-play installation and deep vehicle telemetry. Plaspy compatible out of the box, this OBD-II tracker combines onboard GNSS \(GPS + GLONASS\), internal cellular and Bluetooth antennas, CAN-bus access and a built-in accelerometer to deliver reliable real-time tracking, remote diagnostics and robust fleet management data without external antennas or complex wiring.

Designed and manufactured in India for commercial fleets, rental operators and vehicle owners, the OBD II provides compact, integrated telemetry that pairs seamlessly with Plaspy for live location, diagnostics, alerts and reports. Its support for OTA/FOTA updates, low sleep current and wide operating temperature range make it suitable for 24/7 real-time tracking deployments in challenging conditions.

## Key Highlights

- True plug-and-play OBD-II installation: get a GPS tracker operational in minutes without splicing wires.
- Plaspy compatible for immediate integration into real-time tracking and fleet management workflows.
- GNSS + cellular + BLE all with internal antennas for a clean install and reliable connectivity.
- Access vehicle telemetry and diagnostics via OBD/CAN for RPM, engine status and common OBD-II PIDs where supported.
- OTA/FOTA remote firmware updates and remote configuration \(SMS / TCP/IP / Bluetooth\) reduce maintenance visits.
- Low-power design with \<10 mA sleep current and a 300 mAh backup battery to preserve data and basic reporting during power loss.
- Rugged operating range \(-25°C to +85°C\) and tamper alert support for anti-theft workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the OBD II streams location and vehicle telemetry to the cloud so operators can monitor assets in real time, analyze diagnostics and trigger automated alerts. Plaspy ingests OBD/CAN records, GNSS fixes and sensor events from the device, making it easy to build geofences, trip reports, maintenance reminders and incident workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GNSS fixes \(GPS + GLONASS\) are sent to Plaspy for live tracking maps and historical trails.
- Vehicle diagnostics and engine status: OBD-II/CAN data such as fault codes \(DTCs\), speed and RPM are available in Plaspy dashboards where supported by the vehicle.
- Power loss and tamper alerts: internal tamper detection and backup battery ensure the device reports critical events to Plaspy even during interruptions.
- Bluetooth sensors: BLE support lets you pair compatible Bluetooth sensors and transmit additional telemetry to Plaspy \(e.g., asset IDs, accessory sensors\).
- Remote configuration and updates: change reporting intervals, thresholds or apply firmware updates through OTA/FOTA workflows managed alongside Plaspy.
