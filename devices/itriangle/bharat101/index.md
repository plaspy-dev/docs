# iTriangle - Bharat101

<div className="device-logo">
![Bharat101](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Bharat101 is an AIS 140 compliant GPS tracker designed and manufactured in India for demanding vehicle environments. It combines GNSS positioning with NavIC support, an embedded eSIM, onboard motion sensing, substantial I O options, and a rugged IP65 enclosure to deliver continuous tracking and event telemetry for public transport, commercial fleets, and other vehicle applications.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible out of the box, the Bharat101 is suited to integrate directly with Plaspy fleet workflows. It can forward location, motion, and event data to Plaspy services while retaining local records during connectivity interruptions, making it a practical choice for operators who need indigenous navigation support, reliable cellular connectivity, and scalable telemetry when using Plaspy as their tracking platform.

## Key Highlights

- AIS 140 compliant tracker designed for public and commercial vehicle environments.
- Integrated GPS with NavIC reception for improved indigenous satellite positioning.
- Embedded eSIM for resilient cellular connectivity and multi server data transmission.
- Onboard accelerometer and gyroscope for motion, impact, and driving behavior events.
- Multiple analog and digital inputs plus digital outputs and RS232 for peripheral integration.
- OTA FOTA support and flexible configuration methods for remote management.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and onboard storage with backup battery for continued operation.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Bharat101 delivers positional and event data to Plaspy servers and can operate as a persistent telemetry source for fleet monitoring and reporting. Its built in connectivity and multi server transmission enable continuous feeds of location and event information, while local buffering supports data continuity during temporary network loss.

- Real time location and NavIC enhanced positioning for mapping and route visibility in Plaspy.
- Motion and impact events from onboard sensors for safety alerts and driver behavior analysis.
- Digital and analog input events for ignition, doors, alarms, and analog sensor telemetry in Plaspy dashboards.
- Multi server transmission and local record storage to ensure data reachability and continuity.
- Remote device maintenance and updates via OTA FOTA to keep devices aligned with Plaspy deployments.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and real time tracking for buses, trucks, and light commercial vehicles.
- Public transport deployments requiring AIS 140 compliance and durable hardware.
- Anti theft workflows and remote immobilization using digital I O events and outputs.
- Driving behavior analysis and safety reporting using accelerometer and gyroscope data.
- Remote telemetry including fuel level and other analog sensor monitoring for operational oversight.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Bharat101 pairs certified vehicle compliance and indigenous NavIC positioning with flexible connectivity and I O, making it a practical option for organizations that operate mixed fleets and require dependable telemetry in Plaspy. Its embedded eSIM and multi server transmission model help maintain continuous reporting, while onboard storage and a backup battery provide resilience during power or connectivity interruptions.
