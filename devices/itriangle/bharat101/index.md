# iTriangle - Bharat101

![Bharat101](./tracker.png)

The Bharat101 is an AIS 140 compliant GPS tracker designed and manufactured in India, built for demanding vehicle environments and seamless integration with Plaspy. With integrated NavIC \(IRNSS\) alongside GPS reception, an embedded eSIM \(MFF2\), BLE 4 for short-range sensor connectivity, and rugged IP65 protection, the Bharat101 delivers reliable real-time tracking and telemetry for fleet management, public transport, and commercial vehicle applications.

Plaspy compatible out of the box, the Bharat101 sends location, motion, and event data to multiple IP servers simultaneously and supports OTA/FOTA updates plus USB/SMS/TCP/IP/Bluetooth configuration. This combination of native AIS 140 compliance, onboard accelerometer and gyroscope, and extensive I/O makes the device a strong choice for operators who need accurate indigenous navigation \(NavIC\), anti-theft alerts, driving behavior analysis, and scalable fleet telemetry with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with integrated NavIC \(IRNSS\) for improved indigenous satellite positioning.
- Embedded eSIM \(MFF2\) for tamper-resistant, reliable cellular connectivity and multi-server data transmission.
- Built-in accelerometer and gyroscope for driving behavior analysis, impact detection, and motion events.
- 2 Analog and 4 Digital inputs plus 2 Digital outputs and RS232 for ignition, sensors, and peripheral integration.
- Supports OTA/FOTA and USB/SMS/TCP/IP/Bluetooth configuration for remote management and easy deployment.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure with AIS 140 certification—engineered for harsh vehicle environments and public/commercial transport compliance.
- Onboard memory to store up to 40,000 records and an 850 mAh backup battery for continued operation during power loss.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Bharat101 integrates with Plaspy via standard TCP/IP and AIS 140-compatible protocols, delivering continuous real-time tracking and event telemetry. Its embedded eSIM and multi-server transmission capability allow the device to forward GNSS + NavIC positions, sensor readings, and digital I/O events directly to Plaspy servers while retaining local records in case of temporary connectivity loss.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GPS + NavIC\) for precise mapping in Plaspy dashboards.
- Motion, impact, and driving behavior events from the internal accelerometer and gyroscope for safety and compliance reporting.
- Ignition, door, alarm and other digital events via 4 digital inputs; control or immobilizer actions can be implemented through 2 digital outputs.
- Fuel monitoring and analog sensor telemetry via 2 analog inputs for fuel-level or analog sensor integration.
- BLE 4 support for Bluetooth sensors or beacons to extend telematics with temperature, cargo, or proximity sensors.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and real-time tracking for buses, trucks, and light commercial vehicles, using GPS + NavIC for reliable positioning.
- Anti-theft and remote immobilization workflows—monitor main battery removal and actuate outputs for immobilizer control through digital I/O.
- Driving behavior and safety analytics—use accelerometer and gyroscope data to detect harsh braking, cornering, or impacts.
- Fuel monitoring and remote telemetry—integrate analog fuel sensors to track consumption and support fuel-management reports in Plaspy.
- Public transport and compliance deployments where AIS 140 certification and environmental robustness \(IP65\) are required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Choosing the Bharat101 for Plaspy-enabled deployments provides a combination of certified compliance, indigenous NavIC positioning, and flexible I/O that supports a wide range of telemetry and anti-theft workflows. The embedded eSIM \(MFF2\) ensures resilient cellular connectivity and multi-server reporting while OTA/FOTA keeps firmware and protocols current without returning devices to workshops. With BLE 4 for Bluetooth sensors, RS232 for peripherals, and substantial onboard storage for up to 40,000 records, operators gain dependable real-time tracking, diagnostics, and event-driven alerts—ideal for modern fleet management, anti-theft strategies, and vehicle telemetry integration in Plaspy.
