# iTriangle

iTriangle produces a range of GPS tracking hardware commonly used for vehicle, asset, and personal tracking. This brand page explains how iTriangle devices can work with Plaspy, what to expect from typical iTriangle hardware, and how to find the right tracker for your needs. Use this page to learn how Plaspy integrates with iTriangle devices and to navigate the available models via the catalog below.

## How iTriangle devices work with Plaspy

iTriangle trackers typically collect GNSS position data and transmit telemetry over cellular networks. Plaspy receives that data, stores historical locations, and presents real time views, alerts, and reports through its fleet management platform. Integration steps generally include registering the device identifier in Plaspy and configuring the device to send data to Plaspy servers. Exact connection details and configuration parameters are provided on individual device pages and in device manuals.

This architecture allows Plaspy to:
- Display live location and movement history
- Trigger alerts for geofence entry, overspeed, and other events
- Produce utilization and trip reports for fleet operations

Plaspy can serve as a single pane of glass for monitoring iTriangle devices alongside other brands, so mixed fleets are easier to manage.

## Common features found in iTriangle devices

While specific features vary by model, iTriangle trackers commonly include these capabilities:
- GNSS positioning for latitude and longitude fixes
- Cellular telemetry for remote reporting
- Power and battery monitoring for both wired and portable units
- Motion detection or accelerometer based activity reporting
- Basic I O for vehicle status inputs and outputs
- SOS or emergency alert functionality on some form factors
- Support for geofencing and motion based alerts

For model specific capabilities consult the model details shown in the brand catalog below or the device documentation.

## Typical use cases for iTriangle GPS trackers

iTriangle hardware is often used for:
- Fleet vehicle tracking and route monitoring
- Asset tracking for trailers, equipment, and high value goods
- Rental management to monitor usage and location
- Remote workforce safety when paired with panic or SOS features
- Theft recovery and location verification for stored assets

Choosing the right device depends on power availability, reporting frequency, installation environment, and desired telematics features.

## Why choose Plaspy for iTriangle device monitoring

Plaspy provides a platform designed to ingest, normalize, and present data from a wide range of GPS trackers. When using Plaspy with iTriangle devices you get:
- Centralized live tracking and historical playback
- Configurable alerts and notification channels
- Reporting and analytics tailored for fleet operations
- Device management tools to organize trackers by vehicle, asset, or site
- APIs and integrations to connect tracking data with third party systems

Plaspy focuses on operational clarity and flexibility so teams can adopt iTriangle devices without replacing existing tools or workflows.

## Help finding the right iTriangle device

Select an iTriangle device based on these practical considerations:
- Power source and installation method required for the vehicle or asset
- Expected reporting interval and battery life for portable units
- Connectivity options and cellular bands for your region
- Required I O such as ignition sensing, door sensors, or relay control
- Environmental rating if the device will be exposed to harsh conditions

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What iTriangle devices are compatible with Plaspy?
A: Plaspy supports iTriangle trackers that can send location and telemetry over cellular networks. The BrandCatalog above lists the compatible iTriangle models and links to their individual device pages for configuration and capabilities.

Q: Can I use iTriangle GPS trackers with Plaspy?
A: Yes. iTriangle trackers that are configured to transmit their telemetry to Plaspy can be monitored through the Plaspy platform. Configuration typically involves registering the device in Plaspy and setting the device to report to the Plaspy server address described on the device page.

Q: Does Plaspy support fleet monitoring with iTriangle devices?
A: Plaspy is designed for fleet monitoring and can display live positions, trips, fuel or battery status indicators, alerts, and reports for iTriangle devices alongside other brands within the same account.

Q: Where can I find iTriangle device documentation in Plaspy?
A: Each iTriangle model shown in the catalog links to a device page with documentation, configuration details, and setup steps specific to Plaspy. If you need additional help, Plaspy support resources and guides are available on the main site.

Q: Do I need a special SIM or data plan to use iTriangle devices with Plaspy?
A: iTriangle devices require cellular connectivity to report to Plaspy. The exact SIM type, APN, and data plan depend on the device and your region. Consult the device page for recommended settings and your cellular provider for plan options.
