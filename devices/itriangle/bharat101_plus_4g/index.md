# iTriangle - Bharat101 Plus 4G

![Bharat101 Plus 4G](./tracker.png)

The Bharat101 Plus 4G is a compact, AIS 140 certified GPS tracker built for reliable, Plaspy compatible deployment across commercial fleets, public transport and heavy equipment. With 4G LTE Cat 1 connectivity, multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, BeiDou, NavIC, Galileo\), rugged IP65 housing and secure eSIM support, this device delivers real-time tracking and robust telemetry to meet regulatory and operational needs. Plaspy-compatible integration enables fleet managers to monitor location, driver behavior and vehicle health in a single dashboard.

Designed for fleet management, logistics and security-sensitive operations, the Bharat101 Plus 4G supports ignition monitoring, panic input, tamper alerts and remote immobilization via external relay. It also includes BLE 5.0 for Bluetooth sensors, on-device offline storage and OTA/FOTA updates for low-touch remote maintenance. When paired with Plaspy, this GPS tracker becomes a dependable endpoint for anti-theft measures, fuel monitoring integrations and comprehensive vehicle telemetry.

## Key Highlights

- AIS 140 certified GPS tracker tailored for public transport and commercial fleet compliance.
- Plaspy compatible real-time tracking over 4G LTE Cat 1 with 2G fallback for wide-area connectivity.
- Multi-constellation GNSS \(GPS, NavIC, Galileo, BeiDou\) for resilient positioning in urban and rural environments.
- Internal accelerometer and gyroscope for driving behavior telemetry: harsh braking, cornering and shock detection.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure protects against dust and water for reliable field operation.
- Flexible I/O and serial interfaces \(RS232/RS485, analog/digital inputs/outputs\) for external sensors and telemetry expansion.
- Security features including instant tamper and power-disconnect alerts plus remote immobilizer support for anti-theft workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Bharat101 Plus 4G streams GNSS location and vehicle telemetry to Plaspy over cellular data, providing near real-time tracking and event reporting. On-board logging stores records when connectivity is lost and automatically synchronizes data with Plaspy when the device reconnects. Plaspy ingests the device’s telemetry to drive live dashboards, automated alerts, route replay and compliance reports.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via 4G/2G data links.
- Ignition status and panic button events for immediate operator and dispatch notifications.
- Fuel monitoring and external sensor data when paired through RS232/RS485 or analog inputs \(requires compatible fuel sensors\).
- Remote immobilizer control \(via external relay\) for anti-theft intervention managed from Plaspy.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons supported through BLE 5.0 for temperature, cargo or proximity monitoring.
- Offline storage \(128MB\) preserves up to approximately 40,000 records and syncs with Plaspy after reconnection.
