# iTriangle - Bharat101 Plus 4G

<div className="device-logo">
![Bharat101 Plus 4G](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Bharat101 Plus 4G is a compact AIS 140 certified GPS tracker from iTriangle designed for reliable deployment across commercial fleets, public transport and heavy equipment. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning, 4G connectivity with fallback options, a rugged IP65 enclosure and secure eSIM support to deliver continuous location and telemetry suited to regulatory and operational requirements.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Bharat101 Plus 4G can serve as a dependable endpoint for fleet monitoring and compliance workflows within the Plaspy platform. Its on device logging, remote management features and security inputs make it practical for organizations that need real time visibility, tamper and panic alerts, and integration of external sensor data into a single Plaspy dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- AIS 140 certification for public transport and regulated fleet deployments
- 4G connectivity for real time tracking with fallback for broader coverage
- Multi constellation GNSS support for resilient positioning in varied environments
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and wide voltage support for harsh field conditions
- Security focused inputs including tamper alerts, panic input and remote immobilization support
- Remote management capabilities with secure eSIM, on device storage and OTA updates

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Bharat101 Plus 4G streams GNSS location and vehicle telemetry to the platform over cellular data while preserving logged records when connectivity is interrupted. Plaspy consolidates that data to drive live maps, events and consolidated reports so operators can monitor fleet status and react to operational issues.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live monitoring
- Ignition status and panic button events forwarded as alerts to operators and dispatch
- External sensor and fuel data supported via the device inputs for consolidated reporting in Plaspy
- Remote immobilizer control managed from Plaspy for anti theft workflows
- Bluetooth sensor support for temperature proximity or cargo monitoring integrated into Plaspy views
- On device storage preserves records during outages and synchronizes with Plaspy when connectivity resumes

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization workflows coordinated from a central platform
- Driver behavior and safety programs using motion based telemetry for coaching and safety audits
- Public transport compliance and regulatory reporting enabled by AIS 140 certification
- Logistics operations requiring fuel monitoring and external sensor integration for consumption analysis
- Asset tracking on construction and heavy equipment where environmental robustness is required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Pairing the Bharat101 Plus 4G with Plaspy gives fleet operators a compliant and operationally focused tracker that emphasizes continuous visibility and security. The combination of multi constellation positioning, robust cellular connectivity and built in tamper and panic features helps organizations meet both regulatory and operational needs while keeping telemetry centralized in Plaspy.

Management overhead is reduced through secure eSIM support, remote configuration and over the air updates, while on device storage and rugged design help maintain data continuity in challenging field conditions. For fleets, public transport operators and heavy equipment managers looking to consolidate tracking, alerts and reporting in Plaspy, the Bharat101 Plus 4G provides a practical, dependable endpoint.
