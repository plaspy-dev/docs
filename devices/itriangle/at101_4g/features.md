# iTriangle - AT101 4G Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the iTriangle AT101 4G and how its capabilities are presented when used with the Plaspy platform. The content focuses on user facing features and practical workflows such as long term cordless asset tracking, motion and tamper alerts, geofencing, BLE integration, and over the air management for deployments managed in Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. Where helpful, this page highlights typical capabilities of the AT101 4G while encouraging verification against the manufacturer documentation for device specific limits and the latest firmware behavior.

## Feature Overview

The AT101 4G is designed for discreet, long term asset tracking with an emphasis on endurance and remote manageability. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning, cellular connectivity, motion sensing, and BLE to provide a versatile cordless tracker suitable for a range of assets that need reliable reporting into Plaspy.

- Long runtime cordless operation enabled by a high capacity internal battery for extended deployments between charges.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning for reliable location fix across varied environments.
- Cellular 4G connectivity with fallback for broad coverage and live location updates to Plaspy.
- Motion detection and tamper alerts to support anti theft workflows and automated notifications.
- BLE 4.0 support for short range sensor and beacon integration close to the device.
- Onboard local storage and OTA management to preserve data during outages and simplify remote updates.

## Core Features of iTriangle - AT101 4G

- High capacity internal battery designed for months of deployment without external wiring.
- Multi constellation GNSS support including GPS, Galileo, and BeiDou for accurate positioning.
- 4G cellular connectivity with fallback for extended coverage in varied regions.
- Built in motion sensing using accelerometer and gyroscope for movement detection.
- Tamper detection for case open events to alert on potential interference.
- BLE 4.0 for short range integrations with sensors and beacons.
- Substantial local storage for logged records to maintain data continuity during temporary connectivity loss.
- OTA and FOTA support for remote configuration and firmware updates.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and event telemetry from the AT101 4G and surfaces that data in centralized dashboards, maps, and alerting workflows. The platform helps turn the device telemetry into operational insight for monitoring and historical analysis while enabling remote device management where supported by the tracker.

- Real time location updates and historical track playback in Plaspy for asset visibility.
- Motion and tamper events appear as discrete alerts that can trigger notifications and escalation rules.
- Geofence triggers reported by the device can be used for zone based monitoring and automated reporting in Plaspy.
- BLE related short range sensor data can be integrated into Plaspy views when the device reports those events.
- Local buffered records sync into Plaspy after temporary outages to reduce gaps in historical telemetry.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term cordless monitoring of rental equipment and tools where quick, non invasive installation is preferred.
- Asset security and anti theft monitoring for trailers, containers, and other movable high value items.
- Logistics tracking for non powered assets that need periodic live updates and historical route data.
- Seasonal and off road machinery monitoring where rugged enclosure and wide temperature range are required.
- High value shipment protection where BLE sensor extensions and buffered logging improve continuity of tracking.
- Remote deployments that benefit from OTA management to reduce on site maintenance visits.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific features and limits such as battery life, storage capacity, and supported cellular bands may vary by firmware version and hardware revision.
- BLE behavior, sensor reporting rates, and geofence threshold settings can be affected by device firmware and regional variants.
