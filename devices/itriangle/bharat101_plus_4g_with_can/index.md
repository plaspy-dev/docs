# iTriangle - Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN

![Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN](./tracker.png)

\<meta itemprop="name" content="Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN">
  
The Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN is an AIS-140 certified GPS tracker designed for professional fleets, public transport, and heavy-duty vehicle applications. Built for tough environments with IP65 protection and a wide 9–90V DC input range, this Plaspy compatible tracker brings LTE Cat-1 connectivity, CAN bus diagnostics, and multi-constellation GNSS to deliver reliable real-time tracking and vehicle telemetry.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN integrates vehicle location, CAN-sourced diagnostic parameters, and on-board sensor data into Plaspy’s fleet management and reporting workflows. The device supports remote immobilization, fuel monitoring, ignition detection, tamper alerts, and OTA firmware updates, making it a complete telematics endpoint for anti-theft, compliance, and operational efficiency use cases.

## Key Highlights

- AIS-140 certified GPS tracker purpose-built for commercial fleets and public transport compliance when used with Plaspy.
- LTE Cat-1 with 2G fallback for broad cellular coverage and resilient real-time tracking across regions.
- Integrated CAN bus access to read engine and vehicle diagnostic data for advanced telemetry and reporting.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and wide operating temperature range for reliable operation in harsh environments.
- Multiple inputs/outputs and sensor support for fuel monitoring, ignition status, panic/tamper alerts, and remote immobilization.
- Internal GNSS supporting GPS, Galileo, NavIC and BeiDou for high-precision location and route replay in Plaspy.
- Onboard accelerometer and gyroscope for event-driven alerts like harsh braking, towing, and crash detection.
- eSIM, internal storage for offline logging, and FOTA support to simplify deployment and remote maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN streams real-time GPS coordinates and CAN-derived telemetry to Plaspy’s platform using secure LTE Cat-1 connectivity with automatic 2G fallback. Plaspy ingests location, sensor events, and vehicle state to provide live tracking, geo-fencing, trip reports, and exception alerts. Field teams and administrators can rely on continuous position updates and detailed vehicle diagnostics for smarter fleet management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via LTE Cat-1 \(with 2G fallback\) for continuous tracking.
- CAN bus data integration for engine parameters, speed, fuel level readings \(where available from vehicle CAN\), and diagnostic signals.
- Ignition status detection and digital inputs for door/door-alarm events and panic button monitoring.
- Remote immobilizer support through configurable digital outputs to enable anti-theft responses.
- BLE available for configuration and compatible Bluetooth sensors or beacons \(device includes BLE antenna and configuration via BLE\).
