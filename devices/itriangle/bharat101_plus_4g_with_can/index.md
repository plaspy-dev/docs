# iTriangle - Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN

![Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN](./tracker.png)

The Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN is an AIS-140 certified GPS tracker designed for professional fleets, public transport, and heavy duty vehicle applications. It is built to operate in demanding environments with a rugged IP65 enclosure and a wide voltage input range. The device supports LTE Cat 1 connectivity, multi constellation GNSS, CAN bus diagnostics, and on board sensors to provide continuous location and vehicle telemetry for operational monitoring and compliance workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN can feed vehicle location, CAN sourced diagnostic parameters, and sensor events into Plaspy’s fleet management environment. That integration enables fleet operators to combine real time tracking, telemetry driven alerts, and reporting within Plaspy to support anti theft, compliance reporting, fuel monitoring, and overall operational efficiency without requiring extensive custom integration work.

## Key Highlights

- AIS-140 certified device suitable for commercial fleets and public transport compliance.
- LTE Cat 1 connectivity with broad cellular coverage for resilient real time tracking.
- Integrated CAN bus access to surface vehicle diagnostic data and telemetry.
- Rugged IP65 enclosure and wide input voltage range for reliable field operation.
- Multi constellation GNSS for improved position accuracy and route replay.
- Multiple inputs and outputs plus on board motion sensors for event detection and control.
- OTA firmware update capability and internal storage for offline logging.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Bharat101 Plus 4G with CAN transmits location data and vehicle telemetry so Plaspy can provide live visibility, geo fencing, trip analysis, and automated alerts. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it available to administrators and field teams for operational oversight, reporting, and exception handling.

- Live location tracking and route history visible in Plaspy for fleet monitoring.
- CAN derived parameters and sensor events included in Plaspy reporting and dashboards.
- Configurable alerts for ignition changes, tamper events, fuel exceptions, and harsh driving detected by on board sensors.
- Remote control and outputs can be coordinated through Plaspy workflows for immobilization or recovery actions.
- Stored device logs and OTA update status available for device management and troubleshooting.
