# ATrack - AX7

![AX7](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AX7 is a GPS vehicle tracker designed for fleet control, vehicle tracking, and car rental applications. It features an OBDII port connection, which allows for easy installation and reduces installation costs. By connecting directly to the vehicle's OBDII port, there is no need for additional wiring or complicated installation procedures. Simply plug in the AX7 and you're ready to go.

With its compact design and robust features, the ATrack AX7 is an ideal solution for businesses looking to track and manage their fleet of vehicles. It offers real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities, allowing you to keep an eye on your vehicles' location, speed, and other important data. The AX7 supports TCP and UDP communication methods, ensuring reliable and secure data transmission.

Some of the outstanding features of the ATrack AX7 include a backup battery for uninterrupted tracking, internal memory for storing data, sleep mode for power-saving, and support for internal events-based tracking. It also has GPS and GSM internal antennas, a USB port for additional connectivity, and an OBDII port for easy installation. The AX7 is made in Taiwan, ensuring high-quality craftsmanship and reliability.
