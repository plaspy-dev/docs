# ATrack - AL300

<div className="device-logo">
![AL300](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AL300 is a rugged GPS tracker from ATrack designed for demanding fleet management and heavy equipment telematics. Built with IP67 protection and vibration compliance to common industry standards, the AL300 aims to deliver dependable positioning and telemetry over regional cellular networks. It is available in regional variants to match coverage needs and offers optional vehicle and sensor interfaces to extend observable data beyond location.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AL300 can feed location, event alerts and vehicle data into the Plaspy platform for real time monitoring and historical reporting. Its flexible transport options and optional telemetry interfaces make it a practical choice for organizations that need durable hardware paired with a fleet focused software platform like Plaspy to improve visibility, alerting and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged design with IP67 rating and vibration compliance suited for harsh environments and heavy machinery.
- Regional cellular variants to maintain coverage in diverse deployment areas.
- Flexible data transport options for integration with fleet platforms such as Plaspy.
- Optional vehicle and sensor interfaces expand telemetry for mileage, fuel and peripheral monitoring.
- Onboard logging and message queuing help preserve data during temporary coverage gaps.
- Built for vehicle and asset deployments where durability and continuous tracking are required.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the AL300 transmits position fixes, event data and available telemetry so Plaspy can present live location, alerts and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the device data and surfaces it in dashboards, geofence rules and fleet reports to support operational decisions and maintenance planning.

- Live location updates and route history for fleet visibility and dispatching.
- Event and alert forwarding to Plaspy for geofence breaches, movement or predefined conditions.
- Vehicle telemetry and sensor feeds where available to populate reports and support preventive maintenance.
- Onboard buffering and queued uploads to ensure data continuity during intermittent connectivity.
- Remote control triggers and digital output workflows for immobilization or remote actions when configured.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for delivery vans, service vehicles and mixed commercial fleets requiring live tracking and reporting.
- Heavy equipment and construction assets operating in harsh environments that need rugged tracking hardware.
- Anti theft monitoring and remote immobilization workflows integrated into Plaspy alerting rules.
- Fuel monitoring and operational cost tracking when vehicle telemetry is available.
- Sensorized trailers or assets combining location with temperature or door status for cargo oversight.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AL300 pairs rugged hardware with flexible integration choices, making it a sensible option for organizations that depend on continuous fleet visibility and resilient telematics. Its regional variants and transport flexibility help maintain connectivity across different markets, while optional vehicle and sensor interfaces extend the usefulness of data collected and surfaced in Plaspy.

For teams that need a durable device feeding a full featured fleet platform, the AL300 offers a balanced combination of environmental robustness and telemetry expandability that complements Plaspy dashboards, alerting and reporting.
