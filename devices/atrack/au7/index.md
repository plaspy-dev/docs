# ATrack - AU7

![AU7](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AU7 is a GPS and GLONASS enabled vehicle tracking device designed for both basic and advanced fleet and asset tracking needs. It supports multiple mobile communication technologies and provides configurable reporting options, event control, and a suite of features such as external accessory support, data encryption, and a built in motion sensor. These capabilities make it suitable for applications that require flexible reporting, location logging, and a range of event driven alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible model, the AU7 can feed its location, event, and reporting data into Plaspy to provide unified visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy can use the AU7 data to present real time positions, geo fence events, and custom reports alongside other devices in your fleet, letting organizations consolidate tracking data and act on vehicle events through the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Dual GNSS tracking with support for GPS and GLONASS positioning for reliable location data.
- Custom reporting mechanism to tailor data output and optimize communication needs.
- Support for multiple wireless communication methods and message transport options.
- Built in 3 axis G sensor and configurable event engine for motion and harsh driving detection.
- External accessory compatibility including RS 232, CAN Bus interpreter, and 1 Wire devices for expanded telemetry.
- AES 128 encryption for secure transmission of device data.
- Configurable geo fences with multiple shape options and support for numerous user defined geozones.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated into Plaspy, the AU7 provides structured location and event information that Plaspy can display, analyze, and include in alerts and reports. Plaspy ingests the AU7 feed and maps device events into fleet dashboards and historical logs for operational insight.

- Real time location visualization and historical route playback in Plaspy dashboards.
- Geo fence monitoring and alerting based on the AU7 user defined zones and shapes.
- Event driven alerts for motion, harsh driving, tamper, and other device detected conditions.
- Custom reports and logs using the AU7 configurable reporting to match organizational reporting needs.
- Consolidated fleet monitoring combining AU7 devices with other compatible trackers in Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet location tracking and route history for medium to large vehicle operations.
- Driver identification and behavior monitoring using supported accessory inputs for driver or sensor data.
- Asset security and tamper detection for vehicles and valuable mobile equipment.
- Temperature or ancillary sensor monitoring where 1 Wire sensors are used for environmental or driver inputs.
- Route compliance and geozone enforcement with configurable geo fence shapes and alerts.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AU7 is a flexible option for organizations that need robust location tracking combined with adaptable reporting and accessory support. Its event control engine and configurable outputs make it a good match for fleets that require tailored reporting intervals, multiple alert types, and integration of external sensor or identification inputs.

Pairing the AU7 with Plaspy helps bring that device level flexibility into a centralized management platform. Plaspy can use the AU7 data to simplify operational oversight, reduce the time to act on events, and incorporate AU7 telemetry into standardized reports across a mixed device fleet.
