# ATrack - AK300

![AK300](./tracker.jpg)

The AK300 is a professional LTE vehicle GPS tracker designed for fleet management and safety monitoring, and it is fully Plaspy compatible for seamless integration. Built to capture real-time tracking and telemetry, the AK300 reports precise GPS/GLONASS positioning, mileage, speed, fuel consumption, and engine status to Plaspy for immediate operational insights and actionable alerts.

Ideal for mixed fleets and demanding vehicle environments, the AK300 combines LTE Cat.1 connectivity with 2G fallback, robust vehicle I/O, optional Bluetooth sensors, and local logging. Integrating the AK300 with Plaspy gives fleet operators reliable real-time tracking, anti-theft readiness, and configurable alerts that support preventive maintenance, driver safety programs, and efficient fleet management workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker delivering accurate real-time tracking and telemetry for fleets.
- LTE Cat.1 with 2G fallback for broad coverage and reliable data transport \(UDP/TCP/IP and SMS\).
- Comprehensive vehicle I/O: ignition input, multiple digital/analog inputs, and configurable digital outputs suitable for immobilizer or cut-off relay integration.
- Onboard GPS/GLONASS with SBAS support and approximately 2.5 m CEP position accuracy for precise location reporting.
- Optional Bluetooth Low Energy sensors \(BLE v5.1\) and accessory support for TPMS, temperature monitoring, and RFID driver identification.
- Low-power modes and a backup battery for persistent presence reporting and event logging.
- Rugged design, MIL-STD-810G / SAE J1455 vibration compliance, and regional certifications for commercial deployment.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AK300 becomes a gateway for vehicle data, delivering location, telemetry, and event-driven alerts in real time. The device transmits GPS/GLONASS fixes, fuel and mileage readings, speed, and digital I/O states over secure UDP/TCP/IP or SMS channels to Plaspy. Fleet managers can configure thresholds and alerts in Plaspy to detect speeding, excessive idling, abnormal fuel use, and other unsafe driving behaviors.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent to Plaspy for dashboarding and route monitoring.
- Ignition and configurable digital input status reporting for trip start/stop and door/alarm events.
- Fuel monitoring and mileage reporting for fuel consumption analysis and cost control.
- Configurable digital outputs that can be used to implement immobilizer or remote cut-off controls where supported by vehicle wiring and policy.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons \(optional\) for TPMS, temperature monitoring, and proximity-based events.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and rapid response: real-time tracking plus configurable outputs for integration with immobilizer relays and remote cut-off procedures.
- Driver safety and behavior management: monitor speeding, harsh acceleration/braking \(3-axis accelerometer\), and excessive idling to reduce risk and costs.
- Fuel monitoring and telematics: capture fuel consumption and mileage for route optimization and fuel cost control.
- TPMS and environmental monitoring: use optional Bluetooth sensors for tire pressure/temperature and 1-Wire or BLE temperature sensors for cold-chain or sensitive cargo.
- Mixed-vehicle deployments: compact, rugged design with broad regional variants makes the AK300 suitable for cars, vans, and heavy vehicles in multi-region fleets.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AK300 delivers a practical balance of precision GPS tracking, reliable LTE connectivity, and extensive vehicle I/O to support modern fleet management on Plaspy. Its telemetry capabilities — including fuel monitoring, ignition and digital input reporting, and accelerometer-based event detection — feed Plaspy with the data operators need to reduce downtime, prevent theft, and improve driver safety. Optional Bluetooth sensor support and local logging increase flexibility for specialized applications like TPMS and temperature-sensitive cargo.

For fleet operators evaluating Plaspy compatible devices, the AK300 stands out for its multiple regional variants, robust environmental ratings, and ATrack Device Management support for remote firmware and configuration. That combination makes it straightforward to deploy, scale, and maintain a secure real-time tracking solution that strengthens anti-theft measures, enhances telemetry-driven decision making, and keeps your vehicles connected and monitored around the clock.
