# ATrack - AX5

![AX5](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AX5 is a GPS vehicle tracker that offers direct vehicle OBD II port connection. With this tracker, installation costs are significantly reduced as it can be connected to the vehicle's OBD II port in a matter of seconds, allowing you to start tracking the location of your fleet or any type of vehicle. The ATrack AX5 also supports Can Bus and can update its firmware using GPRS.

Designed for track & trace, vehicle recovery, fleet management, and telematics applications, the ATrack AX5 is a reliable and efficient solution for monitoring and managing your vehicles. It operates on a full quadband GSM band and supports communication methods such as GPRS, TCP, and UDP. The tracker also features a backup battery and internal memory for storing data.

With its sleep mode functionality, the ATrack AX5 can conserve power when the vehicle is not in use, extending the battery life. It also offers pre-defined inputs for panic situations and supports internal events-based tracking. The tracker comes with internal GPS and GSM antennas and is housed in a durable plastic casing. Additionally, it offers extra connectivity options with a USB port and an OBDII port.

Overall, the ATrack AX5 is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker that offers easy installation, advanced features, and seamless integration with your vehicle. Whether you need to track your fleet, recover stolen vehicles, or manage your vehicles efficiently, the ATrack AX5 is an excellent choice.

### Outstanding Features:

- Direct vehicle OBD II port connection for easy installation
- Supports Can Bus and firmware updates via GPRS
- Full quadband GSM band for reliable communication
- Backup battery and internal memory for data storage
- Sleep mode for power conservation
- Pre-defined inputs for panic situations
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas
- Durable plastic casing
- Extra connectivity with USB and OBDII ports
