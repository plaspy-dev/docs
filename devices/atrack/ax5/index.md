# ATrack - AX5

<div className="device-logo">
![AX5](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The ATrack AX5 is a GPS vehicle tracker designed for quick connection to a vehicle through its OBD II port. It is intended for track and trace, vehicle recovery, fleet management, and general telematics tasks. The device includes internal GPS and GSM antennas, internal memory for temporary data storage, a backup battery, and a durable plastic enclosure, providing a balance of convenience and reliability for vehicle monitoring deployments.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the AX5 can feed location and event data into Plaspy's fleet management platform to enable live tracking, alerts, and reporting. Its OBD II form factor reduces installation time and cost, while built-in features such as sleep mode, panic inputs, and remote firmware update capability via cellular communication make it a practical choice for organizations looking to integrate vehicle-level tracking with Plaspy's operational tools.

## Key Highlights

- Direct OBD II connection for fast, low-cost installation and deployment
- Supports CAN bus and remote firmware updates via cellular communication
- Full quadband cellular connectivity and multiple communication methods for reliable data transfer
- Internal memory and a backup battery to preserve data during temporary outages
- Sleep mode to conserve power when vehicles are not in use
- Predefined inputs for panic or emergency signaling and event-based tracking
- Internal GPS and GSM antennas housed in a rugged plastic case

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the ATrack AX5 transmits vehicle location and event messages over cellular links so Plaspy can present live positions, event timelines, and historical tracks. Plaspy ingests the device data and makes it available for monitoring, alerting, and reporting across a fleet, while device-side buffering and internal memory help ensure continuity during brief communication gaps.

- Live location and historical trip visualization in Plaspy dashboards
- Event and panic alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and escalation
- Aggregated reporting and analytics on vehicle activity and uptime for operational oversight
- Offline buffering in the tracker with later upload to Plaspy when connectivity is restored
- Device management workflows in Plaspy can account for remote firmware update capability to reduce on-vehicle servicing

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for commercial vehicle fleets to monitor assets and routes
- Vehicle recovery and theft response leveraging real-time location reporting
- Rental and car sharing operations that need easy device installation and quick turnover
- Delivery and logistics operations requiring position visibility and event alerts
- Telematics deployments where event-based tracking and local data storage are important

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AX5 is well suited for organizations that prefer an OBD II style tracker to minimize installation time and cost while retaining key telematics features. Its combination of local data buffering, backup power, and event inputs helps maintain visibility even when vehicles experience brief connectivity gaps, and these characteristics pair naturally with Plaspy's monitoring and reporting capabilities.

Choosing the AX5 with Plaspy can simplify rollout across mixed fleets, providing fast installation and a compact footprint without sacrificing the basic device-level reliability expected in fleet operations. For teams seeking a practical, OBD II based tracking option, the AX5 offers a balanced feature set that complements Plaspy's tracking, alerting, and operational oversight tools.
