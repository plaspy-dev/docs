# ATrack - AS1

![AS1](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AS1 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for monitoring mobile assets. It combines GPS for location tracking and GPRS/HSPA/CDMA for data communication, ensuring reliable and accurate tracking of your assets. With its optimal design and IP67 rating, the AS1 provides total protection against temperature, dust, and water spray, making it suitable for various operating environments.

One of the standout features of the AS1 is its long battery life, with a primary battery that can last up to 3 years. This makes it an ideal solution for long-term deployments where continuous tracking is required without the need for frequent battery replacements. Additionally, the AS1 offers flexible data communication network options, allowing you to choose the most suitable network for your tracking needs.

The AS1 is designed for easy installation and use, with no external wires or antennas required. It supports data communication through SMS, TCP, and UDP, providing multiple options for transmitting tracking data. The tracker also features a built-in 3-axis G-sensor for motion detection, ensuring that you receive accurate and timely updates on the movement of your assets.

With the AS1, you can enjoy the convenience of FOTA \(Firmware Over-The-Air\) firmware upgrades using FTP, eliminating the need for manual updates. The tracker also offers configurable real-time tracking and logging, allowing you to customize the tracking parameters to suit your specific requirements. Additionally, you can set up to 5 user-defined geo-fences, enabling you to receive alerts when your assets enter or exit specific areas.

Overall, the ATrack AS1 is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that is perfect for monitoring mobile assets. Its long battery life, flexible data communication options, and durable design make it an excellent choice for various tracking applications.
