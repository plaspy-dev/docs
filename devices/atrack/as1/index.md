# ATrack - AS1

![AS1](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AS1 is a compact, battery powered GPS tracker designed for monitoring mobile assets. It combines GPS location tracking with flexible cellular data communication options, and its IP67 rated enclosure protects against dust and water spray for use in a range of operating environments. The AS1 is built for long term deployments, offering a primary battery life of up to three years and a no external wiring design for straightforward placement on vehicles, trailers, equipment, or other assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AS1 provides the kinds of steady location updates and status signals that fleet managers and asset owners rely on for operational visibility. Its support for multiple communication methods and onboard motion detection translates into actionable data in Plaspy for monitoring, alerting, and reporting. The AS1 is therefore a practical choice when you need durable hardware that can feed consistent tracking information into the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- IP67 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water spray suitable for outdoor use  
- Long primary battery life up to 3 years for extended field deployment and reduced maintenance  
- Multiple cellular communication options to suit different coverage needs  
- Built in 3 axis G sensor for motion detection and activity reporting  
- Supports SMS, TCP, and UDP data transmission for flexible connectivity  
- FOTA firmware upgrade capability using FTP to simplify remote updates  
- No external wires or antennas required for simpler installation and concealment

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AS1 transmits location and status data over cellular networks, which Plaspy receives and displays to provide real time visibility and historical logs. Plaspy ingests the device data to present location history, activity events, and configurable alerts so teams can monitor assets efficiently across large fleets or dispersed sites.

- Real time location tracking and map visualization inside the Plaspy platform  
- Historical logging and exportable reports for operational review and audits  
- Configurable geofence alerts using up to five user defined zones supported by the device  
- Motion and activity alerts based on the built in 3 axis G sensor for movement detection  
- Flexible communication handling so Plaspy can receive updates using the device supported channels

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term monitoring of trailers, containers, and other non powered assets  
- Remote equipment tracking where frequent battery replacement is impractical  
- Fleet oversight for businesses needing reliable, low maintenance asset visibility  
- Rental equipment tracking to monitor usage and location between customers  
- Site and field asset monitoring in environments where dust and water resistance is required

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AS1 is a solid fit for organizations that need a durable, low maintenance tracker to integrate with a fleet management platform like Plaspy. Its extended battery life and sealed enclosure reduce the frequency of onsite service visits, while its flexible data transmission options help ensure Plaspy receives timely updates across different coverage scenarios. Built in motion detection and configurable geofences provide practical eventing that translates into useful alerts and operational insight within Plaspy.
