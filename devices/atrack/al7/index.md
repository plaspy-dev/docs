# ATrack - AL7

<div className="device-logo">
![AL7](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The ATrack AL7 is an economical fleet tracking unit designed for reliable real time track and trace in challenging environments. Housed in a rugged IP66 waterproof casing with an internal antenna, the AL7 is built to withstand outdoor and automotive conditions while offering features such as high GPS sensitivity, a built in G sensor for motion detection, configurable real time tracking and logging, and a broad operating voltage range that makes it suitable for many vehicle types.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AL7 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. Its flexible wireless data methods and buffered logging capability allow Plaspy to present continuous tracking, store history for reporting, and generate alerts and geofence events useful to fleet managers, rental operators, and small to medium sized enterprises.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged IP66 waterproof enclosure for reliable outdoor use
- Wide operating voltage range from 6V to 30V for compatibility with diverse vehicles
- High GPS sensitivity and built in G sensor for improved location accuracy and motion detection
- Flexible data communication methods including SMS and packet based transmission for integration with platforms like Plaspy
- Configurable real time tracking and logging with support for user defined events and reports
- Supports multiple geofences and extensive buffered event storage to preserve tracking during temporary connectivity loss
- Designed for ease of deployment and economical fleet scale rollouts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AL7 becomes a source of live location, event, and historical data that Plaspy uses to provide fleet visibility, reporting, and alerts. Plaspy ingests the device output and presents it alongside other assets for centralized monitoring and decision making.

- Display live vehicle positions on Plaspy maps and view recent movement history
- Configure geofence alerts and notifications based on the AL7 user defined geofences
- Use buffered event messages from the AL7 to fill gaps and ensure continuity of historical reports
- Monitor behavior events such as harsh driving or overspeeding through Plaspy alerts and summaries
- Generate trip and downtime reports inside Plaspy using AL7 logging and event data

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle tracking for theft recovery and route monitoring
- Small and medium sized enterprise fleet management for delivery and service vehicles
- Car rental fleet monitoring and usage reporting
- Low cost fleet deployments where rugged hardware and economical operation are priorities
- Vehicles operating in harsh or outdoor conditions that benefit from waterproof housing and internal antenna

The AL7 is a practical choice when organizations need a balance of ruggedness, basic event intelligence, and affordable operation. Its wide voltage tolerance and waterproof casing make it versatile across vehicle types, while configurable tracking and sizeable buffered storage help maintain a reliable historical record that Plaspy can use for reporting and alerts.

For teams evaluating hardware for Plaspy, the AL7 offers a straightforward, cost conscious option that covers common fleet needs without unnecessary complexity.
