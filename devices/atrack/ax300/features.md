# ATrack - AX300 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the ATrack AX300 as used with Plaspy. It summarizes the user facing capabilities and practical functions that operators can expect when integrating the AX300 into a Plaspy deployment for real time location, engine telemetry and vehicle diagnostics.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and how the unit is installed in a vehicle. This page uses the AX300 description as its factual basis and is intended to give clear, general guidance rather than replace manufacturer documentation or device specific configuration guides.

## Feature Overview

The AX300 is an OBD II plugged vehicle tracker built for professional fleet telematics and remote monitoring. When paired with Plaspy, it provides continuous position reporting and vehicle diagnostic feeds that support live maps, event reporting and historical analysis.

- Real time GNSS tracking with GPS and GLONASS for precise vehicle location and route history.
- OBD II access for engine and vehicle diagnostics to surface status and telemetry to Plaspy dashboards.
- LTE M cellular connectivity for low power wide area reporting suitable for continuous fleet monitoring.
- Dual CAN support and OEM CAN data compatibility for richer vehicle parameter access where supported by the vehicle.
- Optional Bluetooth sensor support to extend situational awareness with wireless sensor or companion app data.

## Core Features of ATrack - AX300

- OBD II plug in design for direct vehicle interface and diagnostics collection.
- LTE M based cellular transport for persistent telemetry connectivity.
- GPS plus GLONASS GNSS receivers to improve fix reliability and tracking accuracy.
- Dual CAN bus support and OEM CAN data access for modern vehicle telemetry.
- Optional Bluetooth v5 point for wireless sensors and companion device integration.
- Remote device management capabilities including firmware updates over the air.
- Rugged vehicle grade design for vibration and temperature tolerant deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The AX300 reports location and diagnostic data into Plaspy so operators can monitor fleets from a single platform. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and surfaces incoming telemetry in maps, reports and alerting tools.

- Live location updates and historical traces visible in Plaspy maps and trip replay.
- Engine and ignition related telemetry from OBD II and CAN channels shown in device details and event logs.
- Diagnostic and vehicle parameters available for threshold alerts, maintenance workflows and reporting.
- Optional Bluetooth sensor or companion app data relayed into Plaspy to augment vehicle context.
- Remote management and firmware updates coordinated through supported device management workflows accessible from Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Centralized fleet tracking and route replay for logistics and delivery operations.
- School bus and passenger fleet monitoring with ignition and location visibility for operators.
- Car sharing and rental fleets requiring OBD based diagnostics and location for usage tracking.
- Construction and heavy vehicle deployments where rugged telemetry and engine data aid maintenance.
- Taxis and on demand services that need live dispatching and vehicle status monitoring.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and software revisions can change which OBD II or CAN parameters are exposed by the AX300.
- Hardware variants and regional LTE M models may differ in supported cellular bands and certifications.
- Optional features such as Bluetooth sensors or specific adapter types depend on the purchased model and configuration.
- Some vehicle parameters depend on the vehicle make, model and ECU implementation and may not be available on all vehicles.
- For installation dependent features such as adapters or actuator workflows, consult a professional installer and the manufacturer documentation.

Pairing the AX300 with Plaspy gives operators a unified view of location, vehicle diagnostics and event activity. Plaspy turns the AX300 feed into actionable insights through maps, alerts, historical reporting and device management, helping teams improve fleet utilization, maintenance planning and operational oversight.
