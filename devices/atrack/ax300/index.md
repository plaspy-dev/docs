# AX300 LTE-M OBD Vehicle Tracker

The AX300 from ATrack is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for professional fleet management and vehicle telematics. Designed to plug into the OBD-II port, the AX300 delivers real-time tracking, engine telemetry and diagnostic data over LTE-M so fleet operators can monitor vehicle location, speed and health through Plaspy’s platform.

Built for demanding vehicle deployments, the AX300 supports OEM CAN data, dual-CAN channels, RP1226 adapter compatibility and optional Bluetooth sensors — making it ideal for logistics, school buses, car rental and construction fleets that need reliable telemetry, anti-theft workflows and actionable insights via Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: Native integration ready to feed real-time location and diagnostic telemetry into Plaspy for dashboards, alerts and reporting.
- LTE-M connectivity: Regional Cat.M1 variants \(AX300‑MX, AX300‑MT, AX300‑MG\) provide low-power, wide-area cellular transport for continuous tracking.
- Direct OBD-II access: Retrieves engine and vehicle diagnostics \(ISO 15765-4, Dual SAE J1939, SAE J1708\) for richer fleet insights including fuel-related telemetry when available from the vehicle.
- Dual CAN & auto-detect: Supports dual CAN bus and OEM CAN data with automatic protocol detection for seamless integration with modern vehicles.
- Robust GNSS performance: GPS + GLONASS with excellent sensitivity and typical 2.5 m CEP accuracy for precise real-time tracking.
- Vehicle-grade durability: Wide operating temperature range, vibration/shock resistance to MIL-STD-810G and SAE J1455 for harsh environments.
- Optional Bluetooth sensors: BLE v5.0 support enables wireless sensors or a companion mobile app for added telemetry or driver workflows.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AX300 streams GNSS position and vehicle diagnostics to Plaspy over LTE-M using UDP/IP, TCP/IP or SMS transport. Once configured in Plaspy, the tracker supplies continuous location updates, CAN-sourced parameters and event-driven alerts so you can run efficient fleet management, anti-theft monitoring and maintenance programs from a single pane of glass.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GNSS fixes and OBD-derived vehicle data flow into Plaspy for live maps and historical traces.
- Engine and ignition status — OBD-II and CAN channels report engine state, RPM and ignition status to support driver behavior and route analysis.
- Fuel monitoring and diagnostics — where vehicle OBD-II exposes fuel or consumption parameters, AX300 passes this telemetry to Plaspy for fuel monitoring and cost control.
- Anti-theft and immobilizer workflows — Plaspy can use reported ignition or diagnostics to trigger alerts; immobilizer or cut-off workflows can be implemented when paired with compatible actuators and installation.
- Bluetooth sensors and beacons — optional BLE data \(temperature, door sensors or mobile app presence\) is relayed to Plaspy to enhance situational awareness.

- Fleet management and logistics — centralized real-time tracking, route replay and engine telemetry for operational efficiency.
- School buses and passenger fleets — safety monitoring, ignition status and location visibility through Plaspy for parent and operator alerts.
- Car sharing and rental — OBD-sourced diagnostics and location for usage tracking, billing and anti-theft measures.

- Taxis and on-demand services — real-time dispatching, driver behavior telemetry and optional BLE for driver app pairing.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

When you pair the AX300 with Plaspy, you get a tightly integrated solution for GPS tracker-based fleet management that combines precise GNSS positioning with deep vehicle diagnostics. The OBD-II connection and dual-CAN support deliver high-value telemetry — from speed and engine parameters to fuel-related data where available — enabling smarter maintenance scheduling, reduced downtime and better fuel control.

AX300’s LTE-M connectivity and low-power design are optimized for scalable deployments, while ADM and FOTA support simplify device lifecycle management. For operators focused on anti-theft and remote control workflows, Plaspy can act on ignition and diagnostic signals from the AX300 and implement immobilizer or cut-off procedures when paired with compatible hardware. Optional Bluetooth sensors further extend capabilities for temperature monitoring, door sensing or driver app integration.

Overall, the AX300 delivers a reliable, Plaspy compatible platform for real-time tracking, telemetry and vehicle diagnostics — helping fleets improve utilization, safety and operational efficiency without sacrificing ruggedness or manageability.
