# ATrack - AL1

<div className="device-logo">
![AL1](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The ATrack AL1 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for real time monitoring and flexible remote control. It combines precise GPS positioning with GSM GPRS communication and a built in 3 axis G sensor to support tracking, driver behavior detection, and configurable event actions. The device also provides multiple data transmission options and supports firmware upgrades over the air, making it suitable for a wide range of vehicle and asset tracking applications.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AL1 can feed location and event data into Plaspy's fleet management platform for visibility, monitoring, and reporting. Its intelligent event control engine and configurable tracking make it a practical choice for organizations that want to use Plaspy to centralize alerts, review trip history, and maintain operational oversight across mixed fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor suitable for discreet vehicle mounting and simple deployment
- High accuracy GPS positioning for dependable location tracking
- Built in 3 axis G sensor to detect harsh driving events and support driver behavior monitoring
- Intelligent event control engine for customizable actions based on vehicle conditions
- Multiple data communication options including SMS USSD TCP and UDP for flexible connectivity
- Support for FOTA firmware upgrades to keep devices updated over the air
- Features such as GSM jamming detection configurable GPIOs and external fuel sensor support for expanded monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the AL1 becomes a source of continuous location and event information that Plaspy can display and analyze. Plaspy ingests the tracker data so fleet managers can use a single interface to monitor vehicles, set alerts, and generate operational reports.

- Real time location visibility on Plaspy dashboards for active fleet monitoring
- Event based alerts forwarded to Plaspy for harsh driving detection geofence breaches and jamming notifications
- Historical tracking and configurable logging available in Plaspy for trip reconstruction and reporting
- Device status information surfaced in Plaspy to assist with operational oversight and maintenance planning
- Mapping of AL1 configured geofences and user defined zones into Plaspy for automated notifications

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking and monitoring of driver behavior for safety programs
- Field service and delivery vehicle oversight with real time route visibility
- Rental car and shared vehicle management where compact installation is preferred
- Asset tracking for small vehicles or equipment that require periodic reporting
- Fuel monitoring when paired with external fuel level sensors for consumption insights

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AL1's combination of accurate positioning, a built in G sensor, and an intelligent event engine makes it a practical fit for organizations that need configurable tracking paired with a central management platform. Its small size and flexible communication methods help ensure consistent data flow to Plaspy, while features like geofences and configurable IO extend its usefulness across diverse operational scenarios.

Plaspy provides the visualization and reporting layer that turns AL1 telemetry into actionable insight. For teams managing vehicles and assets, the AL1 plus Plaspy offers a balanced approach between device level configurability and platform level oversight without requiring complex customization.
