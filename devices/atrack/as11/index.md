# ATrack - AS11

<div className="device-logo">
![AS11](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AS11 is a rugged LTE GPS tracker built for long term asset management in harsh environments. Designed for containers, heavy equipment, trailers and other high value assets, the device pairs industrial grade protection with extended battery endurance and flexible cellular and GNSS options. Its enclosure meets IP68 and MIL STD 810H standards and the hardware is engineered for deployments where maintenance access is limited.

AS11 is Plaspy compatible out of the box, making it a practical choice for organizations that need reliable location and telemetry feeding directly into a fleet management platform. The tracker provides GNSS positioning, sensor and I/O event reporting, and queued logging for intermittent networks so Plaspy can offer continuous visibility, alerts and reporting even for remote or intermittently connected assets.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible asset tracking for direct integration with dashboards, alerts and reporting.
- Rugged IP68 enclosure and MIL STD 810H resilience for water dust vibration and shock.
- Long life rechargeable battery and power management designed for extended unattended deployments.
- Dual LTE variants including Cat.1 and Cat.M1 for broad carrier options and wide area coverage.
- Embedded GNSS with optional external antenna for improved reception in challenging locations.
- Extensive I/O and local sensor support plus queued logging to retain data during network outages.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the AS11 streams the core telemetry and event data needed for real time tracking, fleet oversight and anti theft workflows. It can transmit location fixes, I/O and sensor readings to Plaspy endpoints and holds logged records locally when connectivity is lost, uploading them once the network is available. This behavior supports reliable operational visibility for assets that operate in remote or harsh conditions.

- Continuous location updates and position history for live tracking and playback.
- I/O and sensor event forwarding to drive geofences alerts and state change notifications.
- Offline logging and queued uploads to preserve data continuity during connectivity gaps.
- BLE and local sensor integration to enrich telemetry with temperature proximity or accessory readings.
- Data feeding into Plaspy reporting and dashboard views for operational analysis and compliance.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for non powered trailers and heavy equipment to track utilization and movement.
- Logistics containers and remote assets requiring long duration deployments with minimal maintenance.
- Anti theft and recovery workflows using real time tracking and alarm events to support response.
- Telemetry and analogue sensor monitoring for fuel equipment or environmental parameters.
- Condition sensitive shipments augmented by local sensor readings for basic cold chain visibility.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AS11 balances rugged construction, extended battery life and flexible I/O to suit demanding asset tracking projects. Its ability to continue logging while offline and to transmit a mix of location and event data makes it a reliable data source for Plaspy, reducing data gaps and simplifying remote monitoring across distributed fleets.

Because the AS11 is compatible with Plaspy out of the box, organizations can reduce integration time and get telemetry flowing into Plaspy dashboards for alerts, reporting and operational oversight.
