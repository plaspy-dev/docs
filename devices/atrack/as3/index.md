# ATrack - AS3

![AS3](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AS3 is a versatile GPS tracker designed for monitoring vehicles or trailers. It combines GPS for location tracking and GPRS/HSPA/CDMA for data communication, ensuring reliable and accurate information. With its internal rechargeable battery, the AS3 is an ideal solution for managing assets that are normally connected to permanent power but may be disconnected for periods of time.

One of the standout features of the AS3 is its optimal design for operating in various environments. It has an IP67 rating, providing total protection against temperature, dust, and water spray. This means that the tracker can withstand harsh conditions and continue to function effectively.

The AS3 also offers flexibility in terms of data communication network options. It supports communication via SMS, TCP, and UDP, allowing users to choose the most suitable method for their needs. Additionally, the AS3 has a large capacity backup battery, ensuring continuous operation even when the main power source is disconnected.

Other notable features of the AS3 include an optional GPS+GLONASS engine for enhanced positioning accuracy, a built-in 3-axis G-sensor for detecting motion and impact, and FOTA firmware upgrade capability using FTP. The tracker also offers configurable real-time tracking and logging, as well as an intelligent event control engine for customized monitoring and alerts.
