# ATrack - AS3

![AS3](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AS3 is a compact, versatile GPS tracker built for monitoring vehicles and trailers. It combines satellite positioning with cellular data communication to deliver continuous location information and configurable tracking modes. Designed for assets that are normally connected to power but may be disconnected for periods of time, the AS3 includes an internal rechargeable battery and a large capacity backup battery to maintain operation when main power is unavailable.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AS3 can be used to bring reliable location and event data into the Plaspy platform for fleet and asset oversight. Its rugged IP67 enclosure and flexible communication options make it suitable for deployments where environmental resilience and dependable reporting matter. Plaspy users can take advantage of the AS3 feature set to improve visibility, alerts, and historical reporting across vehicle and trailer fleets.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for vehicle and trailer monitoring with configurable real time tracking and logging
- Multiple data communication options for flexible connectivity choices
- Internal rechargeable power with a large backup battery for continued operation when main power is removed
- IP67 rated enclosure for dust and water resistance in demanding environments
- Optional GPS plus GLONASS positioning for improved location reliability
- Built in 3 axis G sensor to support motion and impact event detection
- FOTA capable firmware updates for remote maintenance and feature updates

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AS3 transmits location and event information which Plaspy ingests to provide live visibility, alerts, and historical playback. Plaspy translates the AS3 data into maps, reports, and operational notifications so fleets can monitor movement, detect incidents, and analyze performance.

- Live location tracking and map visualization within the Plaspy dashboard
- Event based alerts and notifications driven by the AS3 intelligent event control engine
- Historical route logging and reporting for trip review and operational analysis
- Continued data capture and upload when power is restored thanks to the backup battery
- Operational oversight for assets in harsh environments supported by the AS3 IP67 rating

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and vehicle tracking for logistics and transport operators
- Asset monitoring where units are normally powered but may be disconnected periodically
- Fleets operating in outdoor or harsh environments requiring rugged hardware
- Rental or shared equipment tracking to record movement and usage history
- Long term route logging and post trip analysis for operational improvement

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AS3 is a practical option for organizations that need a rugged tracker capable of handling occasional power loss without losing critical data. Its combination of GNSS positioning options, event detection, and backup power make it well suited for trailers and vehicles that move between powered and unpowered states. When integrated with Plaspy, the device provides the core telemetry and events that fleet managers use to maintain visibility, automate alerts, and produce meaningful reports.

Plaspy can ingest the AS3 data stream to deliver consolidated monitoring across mixed fleets, helping teams respond faster to incidents and make better informed operational decisions.
