# ATrack - AS300

<div className="device-logo">
![AS300](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AS300 Compact Asset Tracker is a compact, rugged GPS tracker engineered for short term shipping and movable asset monitoring. It combines waterproof IP67 construction and a lightweight form factor with accurate GNSS positioning and onboard motion detection to provide dependable location and movement data for assets in transit or storage.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AS300 is designed to integrate its position fixes, telemetry and event-driven readings into Plaspy for mapping, alerts and reporting. Its BLE sensor support and internal buffering make it a practical choice when intermittent reporting, cold chain telemetry or portable asset protection must be visible inside a unified fleet management platform.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible asset tracker optimized for short term shipping and movable assets.
- Accurate GNSS positioning with SBAS support for improved location reliability.
- Rugged IP67 waterproof housing and shock resistant construction for demanding environments.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support to pair external sensors for temperature, humidity and door or angle events.
- Long battery standby behavior suited for intermittent reporting schedules.
- Onboard accelerometer and Wi Fi scanning to assist motion detection and hybrid location assistance.
- Internal flash buffering and remote device management support to maintain continuity across connectivity interruptions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the AS300 forwards location fixes, motion events and BLE sensor readings into Plaspy’s mapping and alerting workflows so teams can monitor assets, trigger notifications and generate operational reports. Plaspy correlates device telemetry with geofences, movement history and fleet dashboards to give operators practical oversight of short term shipments and portable equipment.

- Real time location and telemetry delivered into Plaspy for live maps and history playback.
- Motion and tamper events from the accelerometer create anti theft alerts and movement summaries.
- BLE sensor data such as temperature and humidity integrated into Plaspy telemetry for cold chain visibility.
- Wi Fi scanning and SBAS assisted GNSS improve positioning in constrained environments.
- Internal flash buffering and remote management help keep data continuity and device settings synchronized within Plaspy managed fleets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Cold chain trailer and temperature sensitive shipment monitoring with BLE temperature and humidity sensors.
- Short term rental asset tracking for construction equipment and portable machinery with location and tamper alerts.
- High value package and cargo tracking during transit where waterproof and impact resistant protection is required.
- Personal item or gear protection for backpacks and portable kits with discreet mounting and sensor integration.
- Logistics and last mile visibility combined with Plaspy geofences and route reporting to reduce shrinkage and improve delivery reliability.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AS300 offers a balanced mix of ruggedness, accurate GNSS positioning and low power cellular efficiency that aligns with many Plaspy use cases, from fleet oversight to anti theft workflows. Its support for external BLE sensors and onboard motion detection lets teams extend telemetry beyond location to include environmental and tamper signals, all visible inside Plaspy dashboards and reports.
