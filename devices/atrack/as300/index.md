# ATrack - AS300

![AS300](./tracker.jpg)

The AS300 Compact Asset Tracker is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for short-term shipping and movable asset monitoring. Rugged, waterproof \(IP67\) and lightweight, the AS300 delivers accurate GNSS positioning, long battery life for intermittent reporting, and Bluetooth Low Energy integration for external sensors — all designed to plug into Plaspy for real-time tracking, telemetry and event-driven alerts.

Optimized for logistics, cold chain trailers, rental equipment and high-value portable assets, the AS300 combines LTE FDD Cat M cellular efficiency, GPS & GLONASS positioning, SBAS corrections and a 3-axis accelerometer to provide dependable location, motion and tamper data that Plaspy can ingest for fleet management, anti-theft workflows and operational reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with LTE FDD Cat M connectivity for efficient, wide-area real-time tracking and low data costs.
- Accurate GNSS: 99-channel GPS & GLONASS receiver with ~2.5 m CEP \(50%\) and SBAS support for improved positioning.
- IP67-rated, MIL‑STD‑810H vibration/shock construction—built for rugged logistics, cold chain and outdoor asset use.
- Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 \(long range\) to pair optional BL1 sensors for temperature, humidity, angle and door-open events.
- Long-life 920 mAh rechargeable battery with USB Type‑C charging — battery life scales with reporting interval \(up to 2 years at daily reporting\).
- Wi‑Fi scanning and onboard accelerometer \(±16g\) enable hybrid location assistance and tamper/motion detection for anti-theft alerts.
- 16 Mbit internal flash and support for both nano‑SIM and eSIM; UDP/IP and TCP/IP data transport to integrate seamlessly with Plaspy.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy, the AS300 streams position and telemetry data over LTE Cat M to deliver actionable insights in near real time. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes, sensor readings from Bluetooth devices and motion/tamper events, then correlates them into maps, geofences, alerts and fleet reports. Communication is resilient — with internal flash for buffering and Wi‑Fi scanning to assist location when GNSS is limited.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via UDP/IP or TCP/IP over LTE Cat M.
- Motion and tamper detection from the 3-axis accelerometer for anti-theft alerts and movement history.
- Bluetooth sensors \(optional BL1 series\) for temperature, humidity, angle and door-open events integrated into Plaspy telemetry.
- Wi‑Fi scanning and SBAS \(WAAS/EGNOS/GAGAN/MSAS\) corrections to enhance positioning accuracy in challenging environments.
- ADM \(ATrack Device Management\) support enables remote firmware/configuration updates to keep AS300 devices current within Plaspy-managed fleets.

## Use Cases

- Cold chain trailer and temperature-sensitive shipment monitoring using BLE temperature/humidity sensors paired to the AS300 and tracked through Plaspy.
- Short-term rental asset monitoring for construction equipment, toolboxes and portable machinery — location, motion and tamper alerts feed Plaspy fleet dashboards.
- High-value package and cargo tracking during transit where waterproof, impact-resistant enclosure and long battery standby are required.
- Personal item protection for backpacks or gear with discreet magnet or zip‑tie mounting and BLE sensor integration for door/open or angle events.
- Logistics and last-mile visibility when combined with Plaspy geofences and route reports to improve delivery reliability and reduce shrinkage.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AS300 delivers a practical balance of rugged design, accurate GNSS positioning and low-power cellular connectivity that fits Plaspy use cases from fleet management to anti-theft monitoring. Its BLE 5.0 support enables easy expansion with temperature, humidity and door sensors for cold chain telemetry, while onboard accelerometry and Wi‑Fi scanning add layers of situational awareness. ADM-based remote management and internal buffering help maintain continuity of service across fleets of devices.

For organizations seeking Plaspy compatible GPS trackers that emphasize reliability, compact form factor and modular sensor integration, the AS300 offers a proven asset-tracking platform. For vehicle-specific telemetry such as direct fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer control, AS300 pairs well with Plaspy when used alongside appropriate vehicle interfaces or gateways to capture those signals and translate them into Plaspy telemetry and automation workflows.
