# ATrack - AS700

<div className="device-logo">
![AS700](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The AS700 is a solar powered GPS tracker engineered for long term outdoor deployment and industrial asset monitoring. Designed for containers, generators, trailers and heavy equipment, it pairs a rugged IP68/IP69K and MIL STD 810H rated enclosure with GNSS positioning, cellular connectivity and Bluetooth sensor support to deliver reliable location and condition telemetry with minimal maintenance.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AS700 provides unified asset visibility inside the Plaspy platform. Plaspy users can access positional fixes, hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning aids, and battery and solar charge status to support fleet workflows, anti theft monitoring and wide area asset telemetry. This makes the AS700 suitable for operations that need durable, long life tracking hardware integrated into centralized fleet management.

## Key Highlights

- Solar assisted power and large internal capacity for extended remote runtime and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Certified rugged enclosure for IP68 and IP69K protection plus MIL STD mechanical resilience for harsh environments.
- Plaspy compatibility for real time tracking, alerting and historical reporting within fleet dashboards.
- Hybrid positioning that combines GNSS with Wi Fi scanning and Bluetooth beacon support to improve accuracy in mixed environments.
- Low power design that maximizes unattended field life between charge cycles.
- Bluetooth sensor support to capture door, temperature, humidity and angle status for asset condition monitoring.
- Global cellular connectivity available in LTE Cat.1 variants to provide wide area coverage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AS700 streams position and sensor data into Plaspy where it is processed for visualization, rules and reporting. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes alongside Wi Fi and Bluetooth witness data, and maps battery and solar telemetry to help teams maintain long term deployments.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered over cellular connectivity for live fleet visibility.
- Battery and solar charge reporting surfaced in Plaspy to support maintenance scheduling and remote health checks.
- Motion and tamper event reporting used by Plaspy to trigger anti theft alerts and automated workflows.
- Bluetooth sensor telemetry for door, temperature, humidity and angle sent to Plaspy for condition monitoring dashboards.
- Wi Fi scanning and Bluetooth beacon data used by Plaspy to improve hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning when GNSS signals are limited.
- Device configuration and firmware management integrated into operational processes via the manufacturer management tools and supported paths.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container tracking across ports and storage yards where robust protection and solar charging cut down on service visits.
- Trailer and generator monitoring to preserve uptime and enable geofenced fleet management.
- Heavy equipment logistics in environments where vibration, shock and salt fog resistance are required.
- Remote asset condition monitoring using Bluetooth sensors for temperature, humidity and door or angle state.
- Long term deployments that require hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning and reduced maintenance overhead.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Selecting the AS700 for use with Plaspy provides a balance of environmental resilience and long term autonomy for industrial fleets and remote assets. Its solar assisted design and low power behavior reduce the need for frequent servicing, while the rugged enclosure and certifications make it suitable for demanding outdoor applications.

Integrated into Plaspy, the AS700’s telemetry and event reporting are turned into actionable insight through dashboards, geofencing, and alerting. For organizations that manage dispersed assets such as containers, trailers, generators or heavy equipment, this combination helps centralize visibility and simplify operational oversight without sacrificing device durability.
