# ATrack - AS700

![AS700](./tracker.jpg)

The AS700 is a solar-powered GPS tracker engineered for long-term outdoor deployment and is Plaspy compatible for unified asset visibility. Built for containers, generators, trailers and heavy equipment, the AS700 combines ruggedized IP68/IP69K- and MIL-STD-810H-rated hardware with GNSS positioning, LTE Cat.1 connectivity and Bluetooth sensor support to deliver reliable real-time tracking and extended field life with minimal maintenance.

Plaspy users gain immediate access to telemetry, hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning and battery/solar status from the AS700. Whether you manage fleet management workflows, anti-theft monitoring or wide-area asset telemetry, this tracker offers the durability and mixed-signal inputs needed for industrial deployments in harsh environments.

## Key Highlights

- Long-term outdoor operation: 1.7W solar cell plus 8,000 mAh internal battery for extended runtime and reduced maintenance cycles.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and fleet management dashboards, alerts and historical reporting.
- Rugged, certified enclosure: IP68 / IP69K and MIL-STD-810H for vibration, shock, salt fog and drop resilience.
- Hybrid positioning: 99-channel GNSS \(GPS & GLONASS\) with Wi‑Fi scanning and Bluetooth beacons to improve indoor/outdoor accuracy.
- Low power design: deep-sleep current \<22 µA for maximized battery life when reporting intervals are infrequent.
- Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 \(Long Range\) to connect external BL1 sensors for door, temperature, humidity and angle sensing.
- Global cellular connectivity via LTE FDD Cat.1 model \(AS700-LG\) and flexible data transport over UDP/IP or TCP/IP.
- Onboard motion/tamper detection with a ±16g 3-axis accelerometer for anti-theft and event-driven reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The AS700 feeds Plaspy with positional and sensor data over LTE. Plaspy ingests GNSS fixes, Wi‑Fi scan/witness data and Bluetooth sensor telemetry to provide real-time tracking, geofencing alerts and telemetry dashboards. The device’s low-power behavior and solar charge reporting enable long-term monitoring of remote assets with automatic status updates to Plaspy.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates sent over LTE Cat.1 using UDP/IP or TCP/IP.
- Battery and solar charge status for maintenance scheduling and remote health checks.
- Motion and tamper events via the 3-axis accelerometer for immediate anti-theft alerts.
- Bluetooth sensor data \(BL1 series\): door open, temperature, humidity and angle reporting for asset condition monitoring.
- Wi‑Fi scanning and Bluetooth beacons to assist Plaspy with hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning when GNSS is unavailable.
- Local configuration and firmware management through ATrack Device Management \(ADM\) over Bluetooth or USB Type-C.

The AS700’s solar-assisted power system and extremely low deep-sleep consumption lower lifecycle costs and extend unattended deployment intervals. Plaspy-compatible integration brings those device capabilities into your operational workflows: real-time tracking, event-driven anti-theft alerts and telemetry dashboards for condition monitoring.
