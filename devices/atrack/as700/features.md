# ATrack - AS700 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the ATrack AS700 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about what the device can report into Plaspy and how those capabilities are typically used for long term outdoor asset visibility and condition monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional product variant, and manufacturer implementation. For critical deployments, verify the current device specifications and firmware notes on the manufacturer site and in product documentation.

## Feature Overview

The AS700 is a solar assisted, rugged GPS tracker designed for extended outdoor deployments and mixed indoor/outdoor positioning. When connected with Plaspy, the AS700 provides ongoing location updates, condition telemetry, and event reporting intended to reduce maintenance visits and increase visibility for widely distributed assets.

- Solar charging and a high capacity internal battery to support long unattended deployments and reduce maintenance cycles.
- GNSS positioning combined with Wi‑Fi scanning and Bluetooth witnesses to improve location awareness in hybrid environments.
- LTE Cat.1 cellular connectivity for real time telemetry and Plaspy dashboard integration.
- Bluetooth Low Energy sensor support for door, temperature, humidity, and angle data to monitor asset condition.
- Rugged IP68/IP69K and MIL STD 810H rated enclosure for reliable operation in harsh environments.

## Core Features of ATrack - AS700

- Solar assisted power system with internal high capacity battery for extended runtime.
- 99-channel GNSS receiver supporting GPS and GLONASS for reliable positioning.
- LTE FDD Cat.1 cellular connectivity for global data reporting in supported variants.
- Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 with Long Range support for external BL1 series sensors.
- Wi‑Fi scanning and Bluetooth beacon witness support to aid hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning.
- Very low deep sleep current to maximize battery life during infrequent reporting intervals.
- Onboard 3‑axis accelerometer for motion and tamper detection and event driven reporting.
- Rugged enclosure certified to IP68, IP69K and MIL STD 810H for harsh environment resilience.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests positional fixes, sensor telemetry and event reports from the AS700 so teams can monitor assets and respond to exceptions. The platform translates device reports into map positions, dashboards and alerts that support operational workflows and historical analysis.

- Real time location updates and historical tracks visible in Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Battery and solar charge status reported to Plaspy for maintenance planning and health checks.
- Motion and tamper events delivered as alerts to support anti theft monitoring and immediate investigation.
- Bluetooth sensor telemetry such as door status, temperature, humidity and angle surfaced in Plaspy for condition monitoring.
- Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth witness data used by Plaspy to improve positioning when GNSS is limited.
- Local device configuration and firmware management are handled via the manufacturer tools (ADM) while Plaspy focuses on telemetry, visualization and rule based alerts.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container tracking across ports and storage yards where rugged sealing and solar charging reduce onsite maintenance.
- Trailer and generator monitoring to maintain uptime and feed fleet management dashboards.
- Heavy equipment logistics requiring vibration and shock tolerant tracking hardware.
- Remote asset condition monitoring using BLE sensors for temperature, humidity and door status.
- Long term deployments that need hybrid indoor/outdoor positioning and predictable battery autonomy.
- Wide area asset telemetry for operations that require consolidated visibility across sites.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some capabilities depend on the installed firmware version and may be added or refined in later firmware releases.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants (for example cellular banding) can affect which connectivity features are available.
- Installation type and antenna placement impact GNSS reception, hybrid positioning performance, and reported battery behavior.
- Bluetooth sensor support requires compatible BL1 series sensors and appropriate pairing and configuration.
- Confirm regulatory and certification status for your target region via the manufacturer before deployment.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the AS700 with Plaspy provides a practical way to centralize location and condition telemetry for industrial assets deployed outdoors or moving across sites. Plaspy turns device-originated GNSS fixes, witness data and sensor telemetry into actionable views, alerts and historical reports so operations teams can reduce downtime and plan maintenance more effectively.
