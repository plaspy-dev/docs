# ATrack - AK7

![AK7](./logo.jpg)

The ATrack AK7 is a versatile and reliable vehicle telematics device and GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features to monitor and control your vehicles. With its GPS and UMTS/HSPA/CDMA communication capabilities, the AK7 can provide real-time vehicle location tracking and remote control functionality over the UMTS/HSPA/CDMA network.

One of the standout features of the AK7 is its intelligent event control engine, which allows users to define various combinations of vehicle conditions and generate customized actions to meet their unique requirements. This means you can set up specific triggers and actions based on factors such as speed, location, or other vehicle parameters, giving you greater control and flexibility in managing your fleet.

The AK7 also offers high accuracy GPS positioning, ensuring that you always have precise and reliable location data for your vehicles. It features a built-in 3-axis G-sensor for detecting and reporting harsh driving behavior events, allowing you to monitor and address unsafe driving practices. Additionally, the AK7 supports the 1-Wire® protocol, allowing for easy integration with external sensors and accessories.

With its small and easy installation, the AK7 can be quickly and conveniently installed in your vehicles without any hassle. It supports data communication through SMS, USSD, TCP, and UDP, giving you multiple options for accessing and retrieving data from the device. The AK7 also supports FOTA \(Firmware Over-The-Air\) upgrades using FTP, ensuring that you can easily keep the device up to date with the latest firmware and features.

Overall, the ATrack AK7 is a powerful and feature-rich GPS tracker that offers advanced telematics capabilities for monitoring and managing your vehicles. Whether you need real-time tracking, remote control functionality, or customized event triggers, the AK7 has you covered.
