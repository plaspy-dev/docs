# ATrack - AL100 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the ATrack AL100 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the user-facing capabilities that fleet operators and owners can expect when monitoring e-bikes through Plaspy, and how those capabilities map into operational workflows and reports.

Exact feature availability for any AL100 device can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional accessories and how the unit is installed. For the most current device-specific details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes before planning deployments.

## Feature Overview

The AL100 is a rugged e-mobility tracker designed to deliver reliable location and telemetry for rental fleets and personal e-bikes. Built to operate outdoors, the AL100 streams vehicle and motor-related metrics that help operators track usage, plan maintenance, and respond to events in Plaspy.

- Real-time location tracking suitable for fleet and theft recovery scenarios.
- LTE Cat.1 cellular connectivity for continuous telemetry uplinks in urban and rural environments.
- IP67-rated rugged enclosure to protect against dust and water during outdoor use.
- Monitors e-bike metrics such as mileage, speed, cadence, torque, and battery level plus battery and motor temperatures.
- Optional CAN Bus integration to access motor and vehicle data via a customized protocol.
- Optional Bluetooth to pair with sensors and mobile apps for extended telemetry.

## Core Features of ATrack - AL100

- Rugged IP67 enclosure for reliable outdoor deployment.
- LTE Cat.1 cellular link for continuous location and telemetry reporting.
- E-bike telemetry including mileage, speed, cadence, torque, battery level, and temperature readings for battery and motor.
- Optional CAN Bus integration that can surface detailed motor and vehicle data when available.
- Optional Bluetooth support to expand telemetry with paired sensors and companion apps.
- Driving behavior monitoring to help identify rider safety and performance patterns.
- Designed for fleet use cases such as rental fleets and maintenance-focused deployments.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, AL100 device data becomes part of a unified fleet view that combines live maps, event visibility and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests the tracker telemetry and makes it available for monitoring, alerts, and operational workflows tailored to e-mobility fleets.

- Location and continuous telemetry are visible on Plaspy maps for real-time tracking.
- Mileage, speed, cadence, torque and battery metrics appear in device dashboards and reports.
- Alerts and rules in Plaspy can be configured for low battery, over-temperature conditions and movement events.
- Driving behavior events are available for fleet scoring, incident review and safety programs.
- Optional CAN Bus and Bluetooth telemetry reported by the AL100 is presented in Plaspy when those inputs are enabled on the device.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for e-bike rental operations with live tracking and usage analytics.
- Theft recovery and unauthorized movement detection using continuous location updates.
- Maintenance planning driven by mileage, battery level and temperature telemetry.
- Rider safety and behavior monitoring programs to reduce incidents and improve training.
- Extending IoT deployments by pairing Bluetooth sensors for lock state or environmental data.
- Personal ownership tracking for improved visibility into range and device health.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Optional features such as CAN Bus and Bluetooth depend on the specific AL100 variant and installed configuration.
- Firmware versions and hardware revisions can change available telemetry, event behavior and reporting cadence.
- Installation choices and vehicle interfaces affect which motor and vehicle signals are accessible to the tracker.
- Regional device variants and carrier requirements may influence connectivity behavior and supported bands.
- Always review the manufacturer documentation for the specific AL100 unit to confirm supported features.

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the ATrack AL100 with Plaspy gives organizations a single pane of glass to translate e-bike telemetry into actionable operations data. Plaspy aggregates location, battery and motor metrics alongside event histories so teams can prioritize maintenance, coordinate recovery, and optimize fleet availability without switching systems.
