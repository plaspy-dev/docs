# ATrack - AL100

![AL100](./tracker.jpg)

The AL100 is a rugged, Plaspy compatible E‑Mobility tracker built for reliable real‑time tracking of e‑bikes in rental fleets and personal ownership. With LTE Cat.1 connectivity and an IP67‑rated enclosure, the AL100 is designed to deliver continuous location and telemetry where bikes are used outdoors and in harsh conditions. Integrating with Plaspy lets fleet operators and owners monitor mileage, speed, cadence, torque and battery health through a single dashboard for fast decision making and improved uptime.

Engineered for fleet management, theft recovery and maintenance planning, the AL100 supports optional CAN Bus integration to retrieve detailed motor and vehicle data through a customized protocol. Optional Bluetooth expands the device’s telemetry ecosystem by pairing wireless sensors and mobile apps. When used with Plaspy, the AL100 provides the data, alerts and reports operators need to optimize operations, enhance rider safety and reduce downtime.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for consolidated fleet management and real‑time tracking across e‑bike deployments.
- LTE Cat.1 connectivity for reliable cellular telemetry and location updates in urban and remote areas.
- IP67‑rated rugged enclosure protects against dust and water for durable outdoor operation.
- Optional CAN Bus integration captures motor and vehicle data \(custom protocol\) including torque and motor temperature.
- Monitors critical e‑bike metrics: mileage, speed, cadence, torque, battery level, and battery & motor temperatures.
- Optional Bluetooth enables pairing with BLE sensors and mobile apps to extend telemetry and sensor coverage.
- Driving behavior monitoring helps manage rider safety and improve fleet performance through event analytics.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the AL100 with Plaspy is designed to turn raw device data into actionable fleet intelligence. The device streams location and telemetry to Plaspy over LTE Cat.1; Plaspy then processes those feeds for live maps, alerts, historical reports and automated workflows. Fleet managers can use Plaspy to trigger alerts on abnormal battery levels, over‑temperature conditions, unauthorized movement and driving‑behavior events.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates via LTE Cat.1 for continuous tracking on the Plaspy map.
- Motor and vehicle status from optional CAN Bus integration — mileage, speed, cadence and torque reported through Plaspy.
- Battery level and temperature telemetry to support maintenance planning and range estimates.
- Bluetooth sensor data \(when enabled\) for additional telemetry such as proximity or environmental sensors.
- Driving behavior monitoring events are available in Plaspy for safety scoring and operational insights.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management for e‑bike rental services — live tracking, usage metrics and scheduled maintenance based on battery and motor telemetry.
- Theft recovery and anti‑theft monitoring — location tracking and movement alerts to support fast recovery.
- Maintenance planning — battery level, motor temperature and mileage data routed to Plaspy for predictive servicing.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The AL100 delivers focused e‑mobility telemetry in a hardened package and integrates with Plaspy to provide a single pane of glass for operations teams. Its LTE Cat.1 connection ensures dependable data uplinks for real‑time tracking, while optional CAN Bus access unlocks deep motor and battery insights not typically available from generic GPS tracker hardware. The IP67 enclosure reduces field failures and maintenance related to environmental exposure, helping fleets stay on the move.

For organizations managing aggregated telemetry across mixed fleets, the AL100 paired with Plaspy offers scalable visibility into usage, battery health and rider behavior. Although conventional vehicle features like ignition and immobilizer are less common on e‑bikes, Plaspy can map motor on/off events and movement alerts similarly to ignition events to support anti‑theft workflows. And while the AL100 focuses on electric vehicle telemetry \(battery monitoring rather than fuel monitoring\), operators benefit from unified reporting and alerting that reduces manual checks and improves response times.
