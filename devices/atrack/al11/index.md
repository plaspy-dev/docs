# ATrack - AL11

![AL11](./logo..jpg)

The ATrack AL11 is a compact, rugged GPS tracking device designed for monitoring a broad range of mobile assets. According to the manufacturer description, it supports 4G Cat.1 and 3G cellular connectivity, offers an IP67 rated enclosure for water and dust protection, includes a built in 3 axis accelerometer for impact detection, and uses a simple 3 wire connection for installation. Those characteristics make the AL11 suitable where robust environmental protection and reliable data transmission are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AL11 can be used to feed location and event data into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. Plaspy can present position updates, activity events, and historical tracks from the AL11 alongside other devices, allowing fleet managers and asset owners to maintain visibility and respond to incidents from a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- 4G Cat.1 and 3G cellular connectivity for reliable data transmission across wide areas
- IP67 rated housing for dust and water resistance, suitable for harsh environments
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer to detect impact and movement events
- Simple 3 wire connection designed for quick and discreet installation
- Compact form factor appropriate for a variety of vehicle and asset installations
- Suited for real time tracking and historical route review when paired with a management platform

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the AL11 supplies position and event information that Plaspy aggregates and displays for operational oversight. Integration focuses on converting the device feed into actionable visibility, alerts, and reports that support fleet and asset management workflows.

- Live and near real time location updates displayed in Plaspy dashboards for immediate situational awareness
- Movement and impact events derived from the accelerometer can trigger alerts and workflows inside Plaspy
- Historical tracking and trip reports available for analysis, compliance, and operational review
- Fleet level views and device grouping simplify monitoring of many AL11 units at once
- Geofencing and zone based alerts in Plaspy can be used to notify teams when assets enter or leave predefined areas

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for logistics, delivery, and service operations
- Monitoring of rental or leased equipment that requires discreet installation
- Protection and oversight of high value assets operating in outdoor or industrial environments
- Security monitoring where impact or tamper alerts add situational context
- Long distance asset tracking where cellular coverage and rugged housing are important
