# Aquila - UX101

![UX101](./logo.jpg)

The Aquila UX101 is an advanced tracking device designed for vehicle tracking and fleet management. With its compact and rugged IP67 casing, it is built to withstand harsh environments. The UX101 offers a range of features that make it a versatile and reliable tracking solution.

One of the standout features of the UX101 is its automotive ECU type connector, which provides additional stability and ensures a secure connection. This makes installation and configuration a breeze, allowing for easy integration with your existing systems. The device also comes with both GPS and GSM internal antennas, making it suitable for hidden or stealth installations.

With the UX101, you can track various parameters such as location, time, speed, distance, and ignition status. It offers multiple connectivity options, including 1 RS232 and 1 CAN or 2 RS232 ports, as well as 1 USB Type port. The device also features 2 analog inputs, 5 digital inputs, and 3 digital outputs, providing flexibility for different tracking and monitoring needs.

In terms of power consumption, the UX101 is designed to optimize battery usage and save vehicle battery life. It comes with a built-in 700mAh Lion battery, providing up to 8 hours of tracking. The device also includes a motion sensor-accelerometer and a mains battery removal alert for added security.

Overall, the Aquila UX101 is a reliable and feature-rich tracking device that is suitable for a range of applications, including fleet management, logistics, security tracking, and asset tracking. Its compact size, easy installation, and advanced features make it a top choice for businesses looking to improve their tracking and monitoring capabilities.

### Key Features:

- Compact and rugged IP67 casing
- Automotive ECU type connector for additional stability
- GPS and GSM internal antennas for hidden/stealth installations
- Multiple connectivity options: 1 RS232 and 1 CAN or 2 RS232 ports, 1 USB Type port
- 2 analog inputs, 5 digital inputs, and 3 digital outputs
- Voice communication enabled
- Low power consumption and power optimization
- Long battery backup of up to 8 hours
- Motion sensor-accelerometer and mains battery removal alert
- Easy software integration with SDK
- Configurable tracking interval
- Data storage and forward architecture with 16MB storage capacity
