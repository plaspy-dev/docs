# Aquila - TS101-Basic

![TS101-Basic](./logo.jpg)

The Aquila TS101-Basic is a versatile tracking device designed for vehicle tracking and fleet management. With its tamper-proof switch and rugged IP65 casing, it ensures the security and durability of your tracking system. The device features online real-time tracking, allowing you to monitor your vehicles or assets in real-time from anywhere. It also offers a solid-state storage with 10,000 tracking records, ensuring that you have access to historical data whenever you need it.

One of the standout features of the TS101-Basic is its compatibility with various applications. It can be used in logistics, taxi operations, rent-a-car services, security tracking, insurance telematics, and asset tracking, among others. Its compact size and internal antennas make it suitable for hidden or stealth installations, ensuring that it remains discreet while providing accurate tracking information. Additionally, the device has a large battery backup of 700mAH, ensuring that it remains operational even in the event of power loss.

Overall, the Aquila TS101-Basic is a reliable and efficient tracking device that offers a range of features for vehicle tracking and fleet management. Its compact size, tamper-proof design, and compatibility with various applications make it a versatile choice for businesses and individuals looking to enhance their tracking capabilities.
