# Aquila - TS101-Basic

![TS101-Basic](./logo.jpg)

The Aquila TS101-Basic is a compact vehicle tracking device designed for fleet management and asset monitoring. It features a tamper proof switch and a rugged IP65 casing to protect electronics from dust and water exposure in typical mobile environments. The TS101-Basic provides online real time tracking and maintains solid state storage for up to 10,000 tracking records, ensuring both live visibility and a reliable history of past movements.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TS101-Basic is a practical option for organizations that want to combine durable hardware with a fleet management platform. Its compact form factor and internal antennas make it suitable for discreet installations, while battery backup and onboard record storage help maintain continuity of data that Plaspy can present for monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged IP65 enclosure for protection in vehicle environments
- Tamper proof switch for enhanced security and event awareness
- Real time tracking capability for live location visibility
- Solid state storage holding up to 10,000 tracking records for historical playback
- Compact design with internal antennas suitable for discreet installations
- Built in battery backup to preserve operation during power interruptions

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TS101-Basic delivers location and event information that the platform can display, archive, and analyze to support fleet operations. Plaspy can use the device data to provide a single pane of glass for tracking, alerts, and reporting across a mixed fleet.

- Live location visualization in Plaspy for route monitoring and dispatch support
- Historical playback using stored records to review past trips and events
- Alerting and notification in Plaspy for tamper events and other status changes
- Reporting and exportable history for operational analysis and compliance
- Continuity of data display when battery backup or onboard storage covers temporary connectivity gaps

## Typical Use Cases

- Taxi and ride services where discreet mounting is preferred
- Rent a car and vehicle rental fleets for location oversight and history
