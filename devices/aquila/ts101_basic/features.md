# Aquila - TS101-Basic Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Aquila TS101-Basic and explains how those features are used with Plaspy for tracking, monitoring, and fleet oversight. It is written for customers and integrators who want a clear, non sensitive view of what the device does and how it maps to visibility inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary depending on device firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current and device specific technical details consult the official manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Aquila TS101-Basic is positioned as a compact vehicle and asset tracker designed for reliable operation in everyday fleet and security scenarios. Its core strengths are durability, continuous online tracking, and onboard record keeping that help maintain visibility even when telemetry is temporarily interrupted.

- Real time online tracking for continuous location visibility and operational monitoring.
- Tamper proof switch for improved security and detection of unauthorized access.
- Rugged IP65 rated casing suitable for exposed vehicle environments.
- Solid state storage with capacity for up to 10,000 tracking records for offline history.
- Compact form factor with internal antennas for discreet and hidden installations.
- Built in battery backup of 700mAH to keep the device reporting after power loss.

## Core Features of Aquila - TS101-Basic

- Online real time location reporting suitable for live fleet tracking and dispatch.
- Tamper proof switch to signal cover removal or tampering events.
- IP65 rated enclosure for dust and water resistance in common vehicle use.
- Onboard solid state storage capable of retaining a long sequence of position records.
- Compact design with internal antennas for low profile or concealed installations.
- Internal battery backup to sustain reporting during main power interruptions.
- Designed for a range of applications including logistics, taxi and rent a car, security tracking, insurance telematics, and general asset tracking.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the TS101-Basic is connected to Plaspy, its primary capabilities translate into visibility and record continuity inside the platform. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and surfaces device events alongside location data so operators can act on both live and historical information.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live monitoring and routing oversight.
- Historical records stored on the device can be synchronized and replayed within Plaspy for investigation and reporting.
- Tamper events and status changes are represented as events in Plaspy logs and timelines for audit and security workflows.
- Battery backup status and power loss events help trace outages and confirm device resilience.
- Devices can be assigned to vehicles or assets in Plaspy for group management, reporting, and operational context.
