# Aquila - BHARAT 101

![BHARAT 101](./logo.jpg)

The BHARAT 101 is an AIS140 certified vehicle tracking and monitoring black box from Aquila. Designed as a compact, rugged telematics unit, it combines GPS/GLONASS positioning, solid state storage for historical records, a variety of input and output options, and motion sensing to support reliable vehicle tracking and operational monitoring. The device includes an IP67 rated ABS casing and certifications from ICAT and AIS140, reflecting its suitability for regulated fleet deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the BHARAT 101 can feed location and status information into Plaspy for fleet visibility, reporting, and operational oversight. Its ample storage capacity and motion sensing help maintain useful data continuity, while the rugged build supports vehicles in demanding conditions. Plaspy can use the BHARAT 101 data to support compliance reporting, location history, and real time monitoring workflows common to fleet operators.

## Key Highlights

- AIS140 and ICAT certified unit suitable for regulated fleet deployments
- Real time location using GPS and GLONASS positioning
- Solid state storage with capacity for 40,000 tracking records for extended history
- Multiple I/O options including 4 digital inputs, 2 digital outputs, 2 analog inputs, and a serial port (RS232)
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer and 3 axis gyrometer for motion detection
- Rugged IP67 ABS enclosure designed for harsh environments and stealth installations
- Supports both normal and embedded SIM cards and has provision for IRNSS support

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the BHARAT 101 provides vehicle location and status information that Plaspy ingests for visualization, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy maps live positions, stores historical traces, and enables operators to configure monitoring tailored to their fleet needs.

- Display real time vehicle location and recent movement history on Plaspy maps
- Retain and access historical records for route review and operational analysis
- Configure alerts in Plaspy for events such as motion detection, input changes, or offline units
- Use Plaspy reporting to generate trip summaries, uptime reports, and usage logs based on BHARAT 101 data
- Monitor digital and analog input states within Plaspy for basic telematics and oversight
