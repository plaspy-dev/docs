# Aquila - TS101 Advanced

![TS101 Advanced](./logo.jpg)

The Aquila TS101 Advanced is a versatile tracking device designed for basic and advanced tracking applications. With its compact size and rugged IP67 casing, it is suitable for use in various domains such as logistics, taxi operations, rent-a-car services, security tracking, insurance telematics, and asset tracking. 

One of the standout features of the TS101 Advanced is its real-time tracking capability, allowing you to monitor the location of your vehicles or assets in real-time. It also comes with a solid-state storage that can store up to 16,000 tracking records, ensuring that you have access to historical data whenever you need it. 

In addition to real-time tracking, the TS101 Advanced offers a range of other features to enhance your tracking experience. It includes a vehicle immobilizer, hooter interface, and fuel sensor interface, allowing you to remotely control your vehicles and monitor fuel levels. The device is also equipped with a 3-axis accelerometer, providing you with accurate motion detection and impact sensing capabilities. 

With its rugged and environment-proof ABS casing, the TS101 Advanced is built to withstand harsh conditions and ensure reliable performance. It also has a large battery backup of 700mAH, ensuring that the device remains operational even in the event of a power outage. 

Overall, the Aquila TS101 Advanced is a reliable and feature-packed tracking device that offers real-time tracking, advanced I/O capabilities, and rugged construction. Whether you need to track your fleet of vehicles or monitor the location of valuable assets, the TS101 Advanced is a versatile solution that can meet your tracking needs.
