# Parallel track

Parallel track produces GPS tracking devices used across vehicle and asset monitoring markets. If you are evaluating hardware for location tracking, fleet management, or remote asset oversight, Parallel track devices are commonly chosen for their practical feature sets and straightforward installation. This page explains how Parallel track devices work with Plaspy, what to expect from the brand, and how to find the right device model for your deployment.

## How Parallel track devices work with Plaspy

Parallel track devices collect position and sensor data from GPS and onboard inputs and transmit that information over cellular networks. When configured to report to Plaspy, those transmissions appear in the Plaspy platform where you can view live locations, historical tracks, and telemetry.

Plaspy provides a single interface to receive data from Parallel track devices, process events and alerts, and present actionable insights through maps, reports, and notifications. For device specific connection details and setup steps refer to the device entry in the catalog and the device guide pages inside Plaspy.
