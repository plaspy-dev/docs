# AoYa - A516 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the AoYa A516 GPS Tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities relevant to location monitoring and operational visibility when paired with the Plaspy platform, and explains how those capabilities can be used in common tracking scenarios.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation type. For device specific settings, detailed operational limits, or latest technical updates refer to the manufacturer's documentation.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa A516 is a compact automotive tracker designed for basic real time location tracking using GSM and GPRS networks. Its small size and replaceable battery make it flexible for vehicle installations and light personal tracking use.

- Compact form factor at approximately 70mm x 35mm x 20mm for discreet installation
- GSM GPRS connectivity for real time location reporting over cellular networks
- High quality UBLOX GPS module providing sensitivity around -159dBm and typical accuracy of 5 to 10 meters
- Changeable 3.7V 1000mAh Li ion battery for extended operation and easy replacement
- Suited for both automotive tracking and light personal or pet tracking scenarios

## Core Features of AoYa - A516

- Compact and lightweight enclosure suitable for hidden installation in vehicles or attachment to personal items
- GSM GPRS network support for sending location updates to a tracking server
- UBLOX GPS chip delivering stated sensitivity and positional accuracy in normal conditions
- Replaceable 3.7V 1000mAh Li ion battery for extended runtime and simple maintenance
- Designed without an integrated display to keep the device small and unobtrusive
- Marketed for automotive use with additional suitability for personal or pet tracking
- One year manufacturer warranty as noted in product information

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy accepts location and status reports from compatible trackers and presents them through its platform for monitoring and analysis. The A516's core capabilities translate into practical visibility and oversight inside Plaspy.

- Real time location updates from the tracker appear on Plaspy maps to support live monitoring
- Historical position data can be reviewed in Plaspy to see recent routes and last known locations
- Device presence and basic operational visibility are available through Plaspy when the tracker is reporting
- Plaspy automatically detects tracker protocols and accepts common tracker configurations so the A516 can be added without extensive protocol setup
- Platform tools can be used to group and manage A516 devices alongside other trackers for operational oversight
