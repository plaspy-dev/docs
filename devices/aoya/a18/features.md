# AoYa - A18 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the AoYa A18 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It describes the A18's principal capabilities, what those capabilities mean in real operational terms, and how those functions appear when the device is used with Plaspy for vehicle location and monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, this page highlights practical considerations and encourages verification against the manufacturer's current documentation.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa A18 is a compact automotive tracker designed to provide reliable location reporting and long standby life. Its combination of GPS, LBS, and AGPS positioning modes and a small form factor make it suitable for discreet or fixed installations where ongoing location visibility is required.

- Compact dimensions and light weight for unobtrusive vehicle installation
- Multiple positioning methods including GPS, LBS, and AGPS for broader coverage
- Industry grade GPS receiver components for improved sensitivity and location accuracy
- Extended standby time suitable for occasional reporting or long dormancy periods

## Core Features of AoYa - A18

- GPS position reporting using GPS and AGPS capabilities for faster fixes and improved accuracy
- LBS based positioning to supplement GPS in areas with limited satellite visibility
- High sensitivity GPS receiver implemented with a UBLOX component as supplied by the manufacturer
- Typical location accuracy reported by the manufacturer in the range of about 5 to 10 meters under good conditions
- Compact enclosure and light weight to support discreet mounting options in vehicles
- Embedded Li ion battery as specified by the manufacturer with extended standby characteristics
- No integrated display on the device requiring remote monitoring via an online platform

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and processes location reports and status messages from compatible trackers like the AoYa A18, presenting them in a centralized interface for easy monitoring and historical review.

- Real time location display and map tracking for reports received from the A18
- Historical route playback using the device's past position records
- Device listing and basic status indicators reflecting recent check ins from the tracker
- Automatic protocol detection by Plaspy to simplify initial device setup and connectivity
- Alerts and notifications within Plaspy can be configured around location changes and connectivity status where supported by the tracker and its server reports

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet vehicle location monitoring for asset oversight
- Periodic check ins for vehicles that are stored or rarely in use leveraging long standby life
- Centralized fleet visibility for small fleets where discreet installation is desirable
- Long term tracking of vehicles where maintenance of battery powered operation is important
- Supplementing primary vehicle tracking with LBS when operating in areas with variable satellite reception

## Feature Availability Notes

- Manufacturer firmware and hardware revisions can change available features or behaviour over time
- Battery standby and operating life depend on reporting frequency, environmental factors, and configuration
- LBS and AGPS performance depend on local cellular coverage and network support in the operating region
- Device functions such as remote reporting intervals are subject to manufacturer defaults and may be configurable
- Always confirm the exact feature set and firmware behavior for your unit with the official manufacturer documentation

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Plaspy with the AoYa A18 brings device location reports into a single platform that is designed for visibility, monitoring, and operational oversight. Plaspy consolidates live position updates, stores historical tracks for review, and provides configurable notifications so teams can maintain awareness of vehicle location and status without needing to access the device directly.
