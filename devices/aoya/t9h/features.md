# AoYa - T9H Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the AoYa T9H mini GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It highlights the main, non sensitive aspects of the device that matter for tracking, monitoring, and operational oversight when the T9H is paired with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information below is based on the device description provided by the manufacturer and is intended as a practical, non exhaustive guide for Plaspy users.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa T9H is a compact, lightweight mini tracker built to deliver accurate position reporting and extended runtime for personal and asset tracking. Its design and radio capabilities make it suitable for scenarios that need reliable location visibility over cellular networks.

- Compact and lightweight form factor that is easy to carry or conceal for personal and asset tracking.
- High performance GPS positioning with stated sensitivity and 5 meter accuracy for dependable location fixes.
- Broad GSM band support to connect over common cellular networks for data transmission.
- Built in GPRS with TCP IP support to send location reports and status updates for real time tracking.
- Large capacity battery intended to support extended periods of continuous operation between charges.

## Core Features of AoYa - T9H

- Compact dimensions and low weight for discreet placement and flexible mounting.
- High performance GPS receiver compatible with u blox and BDS positioning systems.
- Reported GPS sensitivity at the specified level and position accuracy around 5 meters.
- Quad band GSM support covering 850 900 1800 and 1900 MHz bands.
- Built in GPRS Class 12 data connection for sending position and status data.
- TCP IP support for standard network based reporting to tracking servers.
- Large 6000 mAh Li ion battery intended for long duration tracking sessions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the T9H's positioning and cellular reporting capabilities provide clear operational value for location awareness and historical visibility. Plaspy can take incoming position reports and present them on maps, timelines, and device histories for monitoring and review.

- Position reports sent over the device data connection are available in Plaspy for live location and history playback.
- Long battery capacity supports extended monitoring intervals that Plaspy can display as continuous tracks.
- Broad GSM band support helps maintain connectivity that Plaspy relies on to receive timely updates.
- GPS accuracy and sensitivity improve positional confidence for mapping and route reconstruction in Plaspy.
- Plaspy can automatically detect supported tracker protocols and integrate the device into a fleet or asset view without manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal location tracking where compact size and concealment are important.
- Asset tracking for valuables, equipment, or portable inventory items.
- Fleet monitoring for vehicles that require simple tracking and route history.
- Long distance trips or multi day monitoring where extended battery life is beneficial.
- Temporary installations where a small form factor and cellular reporting are required.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware version can change how features operate and which reporting options are available.
- Hardware revisions and model variants may alter battery capacity, radio performance, or supported bands.
- Installation location and mounting can affect GPS reception and observed accuracy.
- Cellular network coverage and local operator support determine connectivity and reporting frequency.
- Always consult manufacturer materials for the most precise, model specific details before deployment.
