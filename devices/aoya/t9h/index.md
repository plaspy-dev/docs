# AoYa - T9H

![T9H](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa T9H is a compact and versatile mini GPS tracker designed to provide accurate and reliable tracking capabilities. With its small dimensions of 49mm x 66mm x 15mm and lightweight design of 141g, it is easy to carry and conceal, making it ideal for personal or asset tracking applications. Whether you need to keep an eye on your loved ones, monitor your fleet vehicles, or track valuable assets, the T9H is a reliable solution that offers peace of mind.

Equipped with a high-performance GPS chip that is compatible with u-blox/BDS, the T9H provides precise location information with a sensitivity of -159dBm and an accuracy of 5m. It supports GSM frequencies of 850MHz, 900MHz, 1800MHz, and 1900MHz, ensuring wide coverage and reliable communication. The built-in GPRS with Class 12 and TCP/IP support allows for seamless data transmission and real-time tracking.

One of the standout features of the AoYa T9H is its long-lasting battery life. With a powerful 6000mAh Li-ion battery, it can provide continuous tracking for extended periods, ensuring that you never lose sight of your target. Whether you need to track a vehicle for long-distance trips or monitor an asset for an extended period, the T9H has you covered.

### Key Features:

- Compact and lightweight design for easy portability
- High-performance GPS chip for accurate and reliable tracking
- Wide GSM frequency support for global coverage
- Built-in GPRS with Class 12 and TCP/IP for seamless data transmission
- Long-lasting 6000mAh Li-ion battery for extended tracking
