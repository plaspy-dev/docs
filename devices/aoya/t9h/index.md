# AoYa - T9H

<div className="device-logo">
![T9H](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The AoYa T9H is a compact and versatile mini GPS tracker designed for accurate and reliable location monitoring. With small dimensions of 49mm x 66mm x 15mm and a lightweight build of 141g, the T9H is easy to carry and conceal, making it suitable for personal tracking and a variety of asset tracking applications. The device uses a high performance GPS chip compatible with u blox and BDS signals and reports typical position accuracy around 5 meters, enabling precise tracking in many operational environments.

This model is compatible with Plaspy, making it a practical choice for users who want to integrate a compact tracker with a modern fleet and asset management platform. The T9H supports wide GSM frequency bands and built in GPRS with TCP IP for data transmission, and its large 6000mAh battery supports extended operation between charges. Those factors combine to provide continuous visibility and simplified remote monitoring when the device is paired with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight form factor at 49mm x 66mm x 15mm and 141g for flexible placement
- High performance GPS chip compatible with u blox and BDS for reliable position fixes
- Reported location accuracy around 5 meters for precise tracking
- Wide GSM frequency support covering common bands for broad coverage
- Built in GPRS with TCP IP for real time data transmission to cloud platforms
- Large 6000mAh battery designed for extended tracking intervals

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the AoYa T9H can feed location data into the platform to provide ongoing visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy ingests the device’s position updates and presents them alongside other assets so teams can monitor movement and status from a single interface.

- Real time location display on Plaspy maps for live tracking and fleet visibility
- Historical track and position history for post trip review and reporting
- Configurable alerts and notifications within Plaspy based on movement or location events
- Aggregated reporting and exportable data to support operational analysis
- Centralized device listing and status overview to manage multiple T9H units

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and mobile asset tracking where a small concealed device is needed
- Fleet vehicle monitoring for small vehicles or auxiliary assets
- Tracking valuable equipment or cargo during transport or storage
- Long duration monitoring tasks that benefit from extended battery life
- Temporary or seasonal deployments where portability is important
