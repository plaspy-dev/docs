# AoYa - A8

![A8](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa A8 is a compact and versatile personal GPS tracker designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities. With its small dimensions of 40X38X15mm and lightweight design of only 29g, it is highly portable and can be easily carried or attached to various objects or individuals. 

Equipped with a GSM band that supports frequencies of 850/900/1800/1900MHz, the A8 ensures reliable and accurate tracking coverage across different regions. It features an SOS button, allowing users to send distress signals in emergency situations, ensuring their safety and well-being. Additionally, the A8 offers voice monitoring functionality, enabling users to remotely listen to the surrounding environment for added security and peace of mind.

One of the standout features of the AoYa A8 is its live tracking capability. This means that users can monitor the real-time location of the tracker through a web-based platform or a mobile app. This feature is particularly useful for keeping track of loved ones, such as children or elderly family members, or for monitoring the whereabouts of valuable assets.

The A8 is powered by a rechargeable Li-ion battery with a capacity of 450mAh, providing a work time of 3-5 days on a single charge. This ensures that the tracker can be used for extended periods without the need for frequent recharging. The A8 also includes a built-in microphone, allowing for two-way communication and facilitating easy and convenient interaction with the tracker.

Overall, the AoYa A8 is a reliable and feature-packed personal GPS tracker that offers peace of mind and enhanced security. Its compact size, live tracking capability, and long battery life make it an ideal choice for a wide range of applications, including personal safety, asset tracking, and more.
