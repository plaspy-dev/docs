# AoYa - T8GPS Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the AoYa T8GPS and explains how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is written to help fleet managers, installers, and technical evaluators understand the tracker’s practical functions and how those functions appear when the device reports into Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary with firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and regional manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes refer to the official AoYa documentation.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa T8GPS is a compact automotive tracker designed to deliver reliable location information across a range of vehicle types and operating conditions. Its small footprint and robust enclosure make it suitable for discrete installation on cars, trucks, and boats, while its network and positioning features support continuous tracking and recovery workflows.

- Compact and lightweight form factor suited to tight installations and discreet placement
- IP67 rated enclosure for protection against dust and temporary water immersion
- GPS, LBS, and AGPS positioning for improved location fixes in varied environments
- Wide input voltage compatibility from 9V to 100V to support many vehicle power systems
- GSM GPRS connectivity for on‑network reporting and remote visibility
- Small emergency battery to preserve reporting for short intervals during main power loss

## Core Features of AoYa - T8GPS

- Compact dimensions and low weight for easy installation in automotive environments
- IP67 waterproof rating for weather resistant use on vehicles and small watercraft
- GPS with AGPS and LBS assistance to improve time to first fix and coverage in urban areas
- Wide voltage support across typical vehicle electrical systems for fleet flexibility
- GSM GPRS network compatibility for cellular reporting
- SIMTK6260 GSM module as the cellular interface component
- UBLOX GPS module used for satellite positioning
- Emergency backup battery with a small reserve capacity to maintain limited operation during power interruptions
- GPS sensitivity and expected accuracy guidance as specified by the manufacturer

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the T8GPS is connected and reporting, Plaspy collects and presents the device location and status so teams can monitor assets and review historical movement. Plaspy’s platform is designed to detect supported tracker protocols automatically and integrate reported GPS and network information into maps and reports.

- Real time location points appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and route visibility
- Positioning modes such as GPS and assisted fixes improve tracking continuity in varied reception conditions
- Short term emergency battery reporting helps preserve the last known location during power loss events
- Centralized device list and status indicators in Plaspy show device connectivity and recent report times
- Plaspy automatically recognizes many tracker protocols to simplify onboarding and reduce manual configuration
- Historical tracks and location timestamps facilitate playback and operational review

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle location tracking for passenger cars, delivery vans, and light trucks
- Fleet management where a compact, weather resistant tracker is required
- Marine tracking for small boats and watercraft where IP67 protection is beneficial
- Asset recovery and theft deterrence through continuous GPS and cellular reporting
- Mixed fleet deployments that benefit from wide voltage support across different vehicle types

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware revisions can change behavior for AGPS performance, reporting interval options, and power management
- Hardware revisions or regional variants may alter supported cellular bands or component details
- Installation affects GPS reception and waterproofing effectiveness; follow mounting guidelines in manufacturer docs
- Emergency battery duration is limited; confirm expected backup time with the manufacturer for your revision
- Confirm network compatibility and SIM provisioning for the regions where devices will operate

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the AoYa T8GPS with Plaspy gives organizations a straightforward way to collect location reports from a compact and weather resistant tracker and present that information in a single operational view. Plaspy’s device detection and mapping capabilities make it easier to include the T8GPS in mixed fleets, produce location histories, and monitor device connectivity across deployments.
