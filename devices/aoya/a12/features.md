# AoYa - A12 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the AoYa A12 GPS tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities the device provides, how those capabilities map to common monitoring and fleet workflows, and what to expect when using the A12 as a location source inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The details below reflect the publicly described capabilities of the A12, including its tracking technologies, compact form factor, and built in battery backup, but customers should verify device specifics for their unit and installation.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa A12 is a compact automotive tracker designed to provide reliable location reporting using GPS, LBS, and AGPS technologies. Its small size and lightweight design make it easy to install discreetly in vehicles, while the built in backup battery helps maintain basic operation during power interruptions.

- Real time location reporting using GPS with AGPS and LBS assistance for faster fixes in varied conditions
- Compact dimensions and light weight for flexible installation in cars and light vehicles
- Reported GPS accuracy in the published range of approximately 5 to 10 meters for most conditions
- Backup Li ion battery to maintain device operation during vehicle power loss
- Uses a GSM communications module for network reporting and position transmission

## Core Features of AoYa - A12

- GPS, LBS, and AGPS based positioning for improved fix times and coverage
- Compact automotive form factor suitable for discreet installation
- GPS sensitivity and accuracy characteristics as published by the manufacturer
- Built in Li ion battery 3.7V 250mAh for emergency power backup
- Integrated GSM communications using the device GSM module
- Designed primarily for vehicle tracking and monitoring scenarios
- Hardware and performance attributes are defined by the manufacturer specifications

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When an A12 is used with Plaspy, the device's location and basic operational characteristics become part of your monitored fleet view. Plaspy integrates position reports and presents them in maps, timelines, and device lists so teams can maintain situational awareness.

- Plaspy displays the A12 position updates and location history for monitoring and review
- Location accuracy information helps operators assess confidence in a reported position
- Small device size simplifies installation choices that affect how and where Plaspy receives updates
- The A12 backup battery can allow continued reporting during vehicle power loss and Plaspy will reflect ongoing or last known positions accordingly
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols for devices that report into the platform

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time vehicle location tracking for small fleets or single vehicles
- Monitoring rental cars, company cars, or service vehicles for operational oversight
- Delivery route visibility where compact, unobtrusive placement is important
- Basic theft deterrence and recovery support through continuous location reporting
- Situations where a battery backup can preserve tracking during temporary power interruptions

## Feature Availability Notes

- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect supported cellular bands and regulatory compliance
- Installation quality and placement influence GPS performance and reported accuracy
