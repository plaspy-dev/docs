# AoYa - A206

![A206](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa A206 is an automotive GPS tracker designed for vehicle tracking and security. It combines compact dimensions and a lightweight housing to make it simple to place and conceal in a car. The A206 uses dual positioning with GPS and AGPS and is built around a UBLOX GPS chip, offering high sensitivity and reported accuracy suitable for routine vehicle location tasks.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the A206 can feed location updates into Plaspy's fleet management and tracking platform. That compatibility makes the A206 a practical choice for organizations and individuals who want precise location visibility, longer operational time between charges thanks to a 7800mAh battery, and straightforward integration into Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and alerting.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor at 96mm x 42mm x 24mm and lightweight at 100g for easy placement in vehicles
- Dual positioning with GPS and AGPS for reliable location fixes in typical driving environments
- UBLOX GPS chip with high sensitivity and position accuracy around 5 meters for dependable tracking
- Long runtime from a high capacity 7800mAh Li ion battery suitable for extended use
- Designed for automotive use with practical applications in fleet management and anti theft monitoring
- Simple device profile that fits Plaspy workflows for vehicle visibility and operational oversight

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the AoYa A206 becomes a source of vehicle location data that Plaspy presents on maps, in reports, and through configurable alerts. Plaspy ingests the A206 location updates and makes them available alongside other devices for consolidated fleet monitoring and analysis.

- Live and historical location visualization for individual vehicles and whole fleets
- Geofence and movement alerts to notify managers when vehicles enter or leave defined areas
- Trip playback and basic route history to review journeys and stopping points
- Aggregated reports and dashboards for operational oversight and usage trends
- Notification delivery for events such as movement, extended stops, or out of area activity

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for small to medium enterprise vehicle pools
- Rental vehicle monitoring and basic usage oversight
- Personal vehicle security and anti theft monitoring
- Logistics and delivery visibility where location accuracy and battery endurance matter
- Long distance or irregular use vehicles that benefit from extended battery life
