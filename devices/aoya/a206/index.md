# AoYa - A206

![A206](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa A206 GPS Tracker is a reliable and efficient device designed specifically for automotive use. With its compact dimensions of 96mm x 42mm x 24mm and lightweight design weighing only 100g, it is easy to install and conceal in your vehicle. This GPS tracker is perfect for fleet management, personal vehicle tracking, and anti-theft purposes.

The AoYa A206 GPS Tracker utilizes both GPS and AGPS dual positioning and tracking technology, ensuring accurate and real-time location information. It features a powerful GPS chip from UBLOX, providing a high level of sensitivity with a rating of -159dBm and an accuracy of 5m. This means you can trust that the location data provided by the tracker is precise and reliable.

One of the standout features of the AoYa A206 GPS Tracker is its long battery life. Equipped with a Li-ion battery with a capacity of 7800mAh, this tracker can operate for an extended period without the need for frequent recharging. This is especially useful for long trips or situations where access to power sources may be limited.

In summary, the AoYa A206 GPS Tracker is a versatile and dependable device that offers accurate tracking and long battery life. Whether you need to monitor your fleet, keep an eye on your personal vehicle, or enhance its security, this GPS tracker is an excellent choice.
