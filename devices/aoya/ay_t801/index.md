# AoYa - AY-T801

![AY-T801](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa AY-T801 is a powerful and reliable vehicle tracker that combines Beidou and GPS technology for precise positioning and tracking. With its compact and discreet design, it can be easily hidden in any car. This tracker offers a wide range of features including positioning tracking, track playback, remote oil/engine cut, ACC detection, electronic fence, track compensation, and more. It also provides various alarm methods such as power alarm, vibration alarm, low alarm, speed alarm, fence alarm, and light alarm, ensuring comprehensive vehicle security.

The AY-T801 features an ultra-small body design that is compatible with a variety of car models. It utilizes the Big Dipper, GPS, and LBS binary multi-mode precision positioning system for accurate and reliable tracking. This tracker supports remote oil off and ACC detection, allowing you to remotely control your vehicle's engine. Additionally, it offers a unique light alarm feature along with five intelligent alarms, providing all-round protection for your vehicle's safety. The AY-T801 also supports monitoring multiple cars with the main account and can be easily accessed and controlled through the web platform or synchronized mobile app, allowing you to monitor your target vehicle at any time with ease.

#### Key Features:

- Precise positioning and tracking with Beidou and GPS technology
- Compact and discreet design for easy hiding in any car
- Remote oil/engine cut and ACC detection
- Electronic fence for added security
- Track playback and compensation
- Multiple alarm methods for comprehensive vehicle security
- Supports monitoring multiple cars with the main account
- Web platform and mobile app for easy access and control
