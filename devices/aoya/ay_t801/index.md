# AoYa - AY-T801

<div className="device-logo">
![AY-T801](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The AoYa AY-T801 is a compact vehicle tracker that combines Beidou and GPS positioning to provide accurate location tracking for cars. Designed with a small and discreet form factor, the AY-T801 is intended to be installed unobtrusively in a wide range of passenger vehicles. Core features described by the manufacturer include positioning and track playback, electronic fencing, remote engine cut and ACC detection, plus multiple alarm types such as vibration, power, low battery, speed, and fence alarms.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the AY-T801 can be used within Plaspy to add real time visibility and historical tracking to your fleet or vehicle monitoring workflows. Its multi mode positioning and suite of security alarms make it a practical option for organizations and individuals who want centralized monitoring, alerting, and basic remote control functions through Plaspy web and mobile interfaces.

## Key Highlights

- Dual Beidou and GPS positioning for improved location accuracy
- Ultra small and discreet design suitable for many car models
- Remote oil and engine cut support plus ACC detection for operational control
- Multiple alarm types including vibration, speed, fence, and power alarms
- Electronic fence and track playback for historical route review
- Support for monitoring multiple vehicles from a single main account with web and mobile access

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the AY-T801 transmits location and status information that Plaspy processes for visibility, alerts, and reporting. Plaspy presents live position updates and historical tracks, and can surface the device alarms and state changes so fleet managers and vehicle owners can act quickly.

- Real time location display and map visualization for ongoing vehicle monitoring
- Geofence setup and alarm forwarding based on the tracker fence events
- Track playback and route history to review trips and verify activities
- Alert consolidation for vibration, speed, power, and other supported alarms
- Fleet level oversight with multi vehicle monitoring under a single account
- Where configured, Plaspy can expose remote control options and ACC state for operational handling
