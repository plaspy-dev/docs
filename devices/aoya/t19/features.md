# AoYa - T19 Features

This page provides a public, practical summary of the AoYa T19 features and how the device is used with Plaspy for asset tracking and monitoring. It focuses on user facing capabilities relevant to Plaspy users and operators, describing what the tracker is designed to do and how those capabilities map to monitoring and operational workflows.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where useful, this page highlights the T19 capabilities described by the manufacturer, but users should verify device specific behavior against the official AoYa documentation and the installed device configuration.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa T19 is a compact magnetic GPS tracker built for discreet, long term tracking of vehicles, luggage, containers, and other valuable assets. It pairs a small form factor and a strong magnetic base with manufacturer stated long battery life and real time location reporting to provide a low maintenance tracking option.

- Magnetic base for easy attachment to metal surfaces for discreet placement
- Compact mini tracker form factor suitable for vehicles luggage and containers
- Manufacturer stated long battery life of up to three years on a single charge for extended monitoring
- Real time tracking capability visible through mobile app or web platform
- Geofence support to receive entry and exit notifications for defined areas

## Core Features of AoYa - T19

- Magnetic mounting for secure, concealed placement on metal surfaces
- Miniature, portable form factor designed for covert or unobtrusive installations
- Rechargeable battery with manufacturer stated capacity and long standby operating life
- Real time location reporting through the device vendor app and compatible tracking platforms
- Geofence function for area entry and exit notifications
- Intended suitability for vehicles luggage containers and general asset tracking
- Designed for long term monitoring scenarios with reduced maintenance intervals

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy is compatible with the AoYa T19 and can present the device capabilities alongside other fleet and asset tracking devices in a single operational view. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols to simplify bringing devices online and adding them to your account.

- Real time location updates from the T19 are displayed on Plaspy maps for live asset visibility
- Geofence entry and exit events from the device can generate notifications and event records inside Plaspy
- Long battery life supports extended monitoring in Plaspy without frequent battery maintenance
- Compact magnetic mounting helps you position the tracker and then track that asset in Plaspy asset lists
- Plaspy automatic protocol detection reduces manual configuration during device onboarding

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term vehicle tracking where discreet placement is required
- Monitoring high value or checked luggage during travel
- Tracking the movement of shipping containers and portable cargo
- Periodic or continuous location monitoring of trailers and non powered assets
- Securing assets in storage yards where concealed tracking is desired

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior such as reporting interval and battery life depends on firmware version and the device configuration chosen at installation
- Hardware revisions or regional variants can affect available functions and battery performance
- Installation method and placement on the asset will influence GPS reception and tracking reliability
- Geofence notification behavior is a combination of device reporting and Plaspy platform settings
- Always check the device serial, firmware revision, and manufacturer documentation to confirm capabilities for your unit
