# AoYa - T19

![T19](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa T19 is a magnetic mini GPS tracker designed for discreet and efficient tracking of vehicles, luggage, containers, and other valuable assets. With its compact size and powerful magnetic base, this tracker can be easily attached to any metal surface, making it ideal for covert tracking operations. Whether you need to keep an eye on your car, monitor the whereabouts of your luggage during travel, or track the movement of containers, the T19 is a reliable and versatile solution.

One of the standout features of the AoYa T19 is its impressive battery life. With a built-in 5000mAh rechargeable battery, this tracker can provide up to 3 years of continuous tracking on a single charge. This long battery life ensures that you can track your assets without worrying about frequent recharging, making it perfect for long-term monitoring.

The T19 also offers real-time tracking capabilities, allowing you to monitor the location of your assets in real-time through a user-friendly mobile app or web platform. You can set up geofences to receive instant notifications when your assets enter or exit specific areas, ensuring that you stay informed and in control at all times.

With its magnetic base, long battery life, and real-time tracking capabilities, the AoYa T19 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that can be used for a wide range of applications. Whether you need to track your car, monitor your luggage, or keep an eye on containers, this tracker has you covered.

#### Key Features:

- Magnetic base for easy attachment to metal surfaces
- Long battery life of up to 3 years on a single charge
- Real-time tracking through mobile app or web platform
- Geofence feature for instant notifications
