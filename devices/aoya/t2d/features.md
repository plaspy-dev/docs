# AoYa - T2D Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the AoYa T2D GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities map into Plaspy for vehicle tracking and monitoring. It summarizes the practical, user-facing features you can expect when using the T2D with Plaspy while keeping the technical detail appropriate for a general audience.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a guide to common capabilities and consult the device manufacturer for device specific and up to date technical details.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa T2D is a compact automotive GPS tracker designed for straightforward installation and reliable location reporting. Its core strengths are location accuracy across multiple positioning methods, broad vehicle voltage compatibility, and a small form factor suitable for hidden or space constrained installations.

- Compact and lightweight form factor for flexible installation in cars, trucks, or motorcycles
- Multi-mode location using GPS, AGPS, and LBS to improve position availability
- 2G GSM/GPRS cellular connectivity for regular position reporting
- Wide operating voltage range that supports many vehicle electrical systems
- Built-in emergency battery to preserve tracking during power interruptions
- Positioning accuracy suitable for routine vehicle monitoring and fleet oversight

## Core Features of AoYa - T2D

- Compact dimensions and low weight aimed at discreet mounting in vehicles
- GPS, AGPS, and LBS hybrid location methods for improved fix rates
- 2G GSM/GPRS connectivity for sending location updates and status
- Wide voltage support from low to high vehicle system voltages for broad vehicle compatibility
- 180 mAh emergency battery to maintain basic operation if external power is lost
- Published GPS sensitivity and typical positional accuracy that support routine tracking needs
- Onboard RF and GNSS components noted by the manufacturer to support the device performance

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy displays and stores device-reported locations and events so you can monitor vehicles centrally. The T2D's multi-mode positioning and power resilience translate into more consistent visibility inside the Plaspy platform.

- Real time and historical location points appear on the Plaspy map when the device reports position data
- AGPS and LBS assist modes help maintain location updates when a GPS fix is delayed or unavailable
- Emergency battery support helps devices continue to report into Plaspy during short power outages
- 2G GPRS connectivity provides the transport for the T2D to send updates into the Plaspy service
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and accepts connections from compatible devices for streamlined setup

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location monitoring for light commercial fleets and service vehicles
- Vehicle security and recovery where persistent location reporting is required
- Mixed vehicle deployments that need a wide input voltage range for diverse electrical systems
- Temporary or discreet installations that benefit from a small physical footprint
- Basic route and movement awareness for operational oversight

## Feature Availability Notes

- Actual availability of each function depends on the device firmware and the specific hardware revision shipped by the manufacturer
- 2G cellular service availability varies by region and carrier; check local network support before deployment
- Installation choices and antenna placement can affect GPS performance and reported accuracy
- Some features or behaviors may be configurable by the installer or require manufacturer configuration

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the AoYa T2D with Plaspy gives organizations a simple path to centralize vehicle location data and operational visibility. Plaspy consolidates position reports, presents them on a map, and makes it easier to monitor vehicle movement, uptime, and presence across a mixed fleet.
