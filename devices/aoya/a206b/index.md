# AoYa - A206b

![A206b](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa A206B is a compact and powerful GPS tracker designed specifically for automotive use. With its small dimensions of 96mm x 42mm x 24mm and lightweight design weighing only 100g, it can easily be installed in any vehicle without taking up much space. This tracker is perfect for fleet management, personal vehicle tracking, and anti-theft purposes.

The AoYa A206B utilizes GSM/GPRS network technology to provide accurate and real-time GPS tracking information. It features a high-quality GPS chip from UBLOX, ensuring reliable and precise location data. With a GPS sensitivity of -159dBm and an accuracy of 5m, you can trust that the A206B will provide accurate and up-to-date information about the location of your vehicle.

One of the standout features of the AoYa A206B is its long battery life. Equipped with a powerful 5000mAh Li-ion battery, this tracker can operate for up to 720 hours on a single charge. This means you can track your vehicle for extended periods without worrying about the battery running out.

The AoYa A206B is a magnetic wireless vehicle GPS tracker, making it easy to install and remove as needed. Its compact size and lightweight design make it discreet and unobtrusive, ensuring that it won't interfere with the normal operation of your vehicle.

Overall, the AoYa A206B is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that is perfect for automotive use. Whether you need to track your personal vehicle, manage a fleet of vehicles, or protect against theft, the A206B has you covered. With its accurate GPS tracking, long battery life, and easy installation, it is a top choice for anyone in need of a high-quality GPS tracker.

### Key Features:

- Compact and lightweight design
- GSM/GPRS network technology
- High-quality GPS chip from UBLOX
- GPS sensitivity of -159dBm
- GPS accuracy of 5m
- 5000mAh Li-ion battery for long battery life
- Magnetic and wireless installation
