# AoYa - A206b

<div className="device-logo">
![A206b](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The AoYa A206b is a compact automotive GPS tracker built for discreet vehicle monitoring. At 96mm x 42mm x 24mm and about 100g, the unit is small and lightweight for unobtrusive placement. It uses GSM GPRS network connectivity together with a UBLOX GPS module to provide location data, and the manufacturer lists positional accuracy around 5 meters and high GPS sensitivity for reliable fixes. The A206b is designed for use cases where size, endurance, and accurate positioning are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the A206b can be integrated into fleet management and vehicle monitoring workflows on the Plaspy platform. Plaspy can consume the tracker's location updates to provide visual tracking, historical playback, and operational oversight across a mixed fleet. This makes the A206b a practical option for organizations that need a compact tracker that works with a full featured tracking platform.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design suitable for discreet automotive placement
- UBLOX GPS module delivering reliable position data with about 5 meter accuracy
- Operates on GSM GPRS network technology for remote reporting
- Long runtime supported by a 5000mAh Li ion battery with extended standby duration
- Magnetic wireless form factor for easy attachment and removal
- Small footprint and low weight reduce impact on vehicle use

## How It Works with Plaspy

The A206b transmits location information that Plaspy ingests and presents through its fleet management tools. Once paired with Plaspy, the tracker’s updates become part of a centralized view that helps teams monitor assets and respond to events.

- Real time location visibility on the Plaspy map for individual vehicles
- Historical route playback and location history for auditing and review
- Alerts and notifications configured in Plaspy for movement or geofence events
- Aggregated reporting to support fleet oversight and operational analysis
- Centralized device status and last seen information to manage deployments
