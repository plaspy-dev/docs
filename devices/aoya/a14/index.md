# AoYa - A14

![A14](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa A14 GPS Tracker is a compact and reliable device designed for automotive use. With its small size of 88mm x 46mm x 16mm and lightweight design of 80g, it can easily be installed in any vehicle without taking up much space. This GPS tracker offers GPS/LBS/AGPS tracking functionality, allowing you to accurately track the location of your vehicle in real-time.

The AoYa A14 GPS Tracker utilizes a GPS chip from UBLOX, ensuring high sensitivity and accuracy with a GPS sensitivity of -159dBm and a GPS accuracy of 5-10m. It also features a GSM chip from SIMTK6260, providing reliable communication through GPS/AGPS/GSM/GPRS networks. This tracker is compatible with both Android and iOS devices, allowing you to easily track your vehicle using the free tracking system.

In case of emergencies or power outages, the AoYa A14 GPS Tracker is equipped with an emergency battery. The Li-ion battery has a capacity of 3.7V and 250mAh, ensuring that the tracker remains operational even when the main power source is unavailable. With a warranty of 1 year, you can have peace of mind knowing that your investment is protected.

Outstanding Features:
- Compact and lightweight design
- GPS/LBS/AGPS tracking functionality
- High GPS sensitivity and accuracy
- Compatible with Android and iOS devices
- Emergency battery for uninterrupted tracking
- 1-year warranty for added assurance

The AoYa A14 GPS Tracker is an excellent choice for anyone looking to enhance the security and tracking capabilities of their vehicle. Whether you're a fleet manager or a concerned vehicle owner, this tracker offers reliable and accurate tracking to ensure the safety of your assets.
