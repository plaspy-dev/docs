# AoYa - A14 Features

This page provides a public summary of the AoYa A14 GPS tracker features and how those capabilities are used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about location tracking, device behavior, and the ways the device can integrate with Plaspy for vehicle monitoring.

Exact feature availability and telemetry can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. The information here is drawn from the device description and is intended to help you understand everyday capabilities and how the tracker can be used with Plaspy rather than to replace official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa A14 is a compact automotive GPS tracker designed for straightforward vehicle tracking. It combines GPS and cellular positioning features with a small form factor and a built-in emergency battery to maintain reporting when external power is interrupted.

- Compact and lightweight design that simplifies discreet installation in a wide range of vehicles
- GPS / LBS / AGPS tracking to provide position updates and improve location acquisition
- High sensitivity UBLOX GPS performance with typical accuracy in the 5 to 10 meter range for position fixes
- GSM/GPRS communication via the SIMTK6260 cellular module for sending location reports to tracking platforms
- Built-in Li-ion emergency battery (3.7V 250mAh) to preserve basic tracking capability during power loss

## Core Features of AoYa - A14

- Compact dimensions (88mm x 46mm x 16mm) and lightweight construction (approximately 80g)
- GPS tracking with AGPS and LBS assistance for faster fixes in challenging environments
- UBLOX GPS receiver performance with high sensitivity reported in the device description
- GSM communication handled by the SIMTK6260 module for data uplink over cellular networks
- Compatibility with Android and iOS user interfaces via the device's tracking system
- Emergency Li-ion backup battery at 3.7V 250mAh to keep the device operational when vehicle power is lost
- Manufacturer backed 1 year warranty for hardware assurance

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the A14's location and status information can be integrated into a single monitoring system to support visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy supports receiving reports from compatible trackers and will reflect position and basic device status for management and review.

- Real-time location display within Plaspy so you can view current position on maps and dashboards
- Historical position and movement logs in Plaspy help reconstruct routes and review recent activity
- Device online/offline and last-known status visible in Plaspy; emergency battery activity can support last-known reporting during power loss
- Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and accepts incoming reports to simplify integration; the A14 can be pointed at Plaspy server domain d.plaspy.com and configured to use TCP or UDP transport
- Centralized device management in Plaspy consolidates tracking data from the A14 alongside other fleet devices for consistent oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location monitoring for improved route visibility and scheduling
- Recovery and tracking of a single vehicle or high-value asset
- Managing service or delivery vehicles where compact installation is required
- Personal vehicle monitoring for family or shared vehicles
- Temporary tracking installations where an emergency battery can provide minimum reporting during power interruptions

## Feature Availability Notes

- Exact feature behavior depends on the installed firmware version and any manufacturer firmware updates
- Hardware revisions and regional variants may alter supported cellular bands or LBS behavior
- Installation placement and vehicle wiring affect GPS reception and AGPS performance
- Network coverage and SIM service quality determine uplink reliability for GSM/GPRS reports
- Always consult the official manufacturer documentation for the most current and model-specific technical details
