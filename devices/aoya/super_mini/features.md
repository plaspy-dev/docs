# AoYa - super mini Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the AoYa super mini GPS tracker and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is written to help fleet managers, rental operators, and technical evaluators understand the tracker’s practical functions and how those functions map into Plaspy for monitoring and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation choices. For device-specific configuration, thresholds, and the latest behavior details consult the manufacturer documentation and test the device in your environment before applying operational policies.

## Feature Overview

The AoYa super mini is a compact tracking unit combining multiple location technologies and a set of vehicle-oriented alerts. It is designed for discreet installation and continuous location reporting, with a focus on providing timely events and history playback for operational decision making.

- Compact form factor suited for discreet vehicle installation and small fleet use
- Multi-mode location reporting using GPS plus LBS and WiFi assistance for improved availability
- Real time position updates to support live monitoring of vehicles
- Multiple alarm types including light alarm, power cut alarm, over speed alarm, and vibration alarm
- Support for remote vehicle cut off of oil or engine and geo-fence with history route playback

## Core Features of AoYa - super mini

- GPS based location reporting for continuous position tracking
- LBS and WiFi assisted location to improve fix availability in urban or indoor edge cases
- Real time tracking capability for live location visibility
- Built-in light sensor enabling light alarm detection
- Power cut alarm to notify when external power is lost
- Over speed alarm to flag potential speeding events
- Vibration alarm for movement or tamper indication
- Remote cut off capability to interrupt oil or engine circuits when supported and correctly installed

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives device reports and presents position, event, and history data in a unified view so teams can act on alerts and review movements. When a super mini device is connected, Plaspy treats its location updates and alarms as events and exposes them for monitoring, playback, and alerting workflows.

- Real time positions appear on the Plaspy map for live tracking and location awareness
- Alarm events such as light alarm, power cut, over speed, and vibration are shown as alerts and can be routed to notifications or reports
- Geo-fence entering and leaving events are captured and visible in Plaspy to support boundary monitoring
- History route playback in Plaspy allows review of past movements reported by the device for auditing and analysis
- Remote immobilization or engine cut commands, when enabled by the device and installation, can be coordinated with Plaspy workflows and command interfaces

## Typical Use Cases

- Car rental operations that need discreet tracking and event alerts for rented vehicles
- Small fleet management for delivery or service vehicles requiring live position updates and history playback
- Theft response workflows that rely on remote engine or oil cut capabilities and location visibility
- Boundary monitoring with geo-fence alerts for depot entry and exit tracking
- Monitoring for power disconnection events to detect tampering or unauthorized battery removal
- Over speed and vibration alerting to highlight potential misuse or incidents in near real time

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and behavior depend on the specific firmware version and hardware revision of each unit
- Remote cut off of oil or engine requires compatible wiring and correct installation by a qualified technician
- Alarm thresholds and geo-fence behaviors may be configurable and can vary by manufacturer firmware
- Regional product variants or distributor configurations may alter available functions; always verify against the device label and documentation

Using the AoYa super mini with Plaspy gives organizations a cohesive platform for turning device signals into operational insight. Plaspy consolidates real time positions, alarms, and historical route data so teams can monitor fleets, respond to alerts, and review activity from a single interface.
