# AoYa - A202

![A202](./logo.jpg)

The AoYa A202 GPS Tracker is a reliable and versatile device designed for automotive use. With its compact size of 130mm x 72mm x 20mm and lightweight design weighing only 240g, it is easy to install and conceal in your vehicle. This GPS tracker is perfect for fleet management, personal vehicle tracking, and asset tracking.

The AoYa A202 GPS Tracker utilizes GSM/GPRS network technology, ensuring a stable and reliable connection for real-time tracking. It features a high-quality GPS chip from UBLOX, providing accurate positioning with a sensitivity of -159dBm and an accuracy of 5m. Whether you need to track your vehicle's location, monitor its speed, or set up geofence alerts, this tracker has got you covered.

One of the standout features of the AoYa A202 GPS Tracker is its long battery life. Equipped with a powerful 10000mAh Li-ion battery, it can provide continuous tracking for an extended period without the need for frequent recharging. This makes it an ideal choice for long-term tracking applications where power sources may not be readily available.

In addition to its impressive battery life, the AoYa A202 GPS Tracker is also designed to withstand harsh environments. It is waterproof and dustproof, ensuring reliable performance even in challenging weather conditions. With its durable construction, this tracker can withstand the rigors of daily use and provide reliable tracking data in any situation.

Overall, the AoYa A202 GPS Tracker is a reliable and feature-packed device that offers accurate tracking, long battery life, and durability. Whether you need to track your personal vehicle or manage a fleet of vehicles, this tracker is a great choice. With its compact size and easy installation, it provides a hassle-free tracking solution for all your needs.
