# Maxtrack - MXT-160

![MXT-160](./logo.jpg)

The Maxtrack MXT-160 is a compact, durable GPS tracker designed for demanding mobile asset monitoring. It is offered in two variants that differ only by the number of inputs and outputs, and its construction emphasizes robustness against interference, voltage variations, and harsh environmental conditions. The device supports remote position reporting over GPRS and combines positioning, connectivity, and embedded application logic in a single internal processor for simplified operation in high volume deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the MXT-160 can feed location and event data into fleet management workflows to provide visibility and operational control. Its small size and waterproof mechanical design make it suitable for motorcycle installations and other mobile assets where exposure and space constraints are common. Plaspy can ingest the MXT-160 data to enable centralized monitoring, alerts, and reporting for organizations managing large numbers of tracked units.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and rugged design suited to motorcycle and tight installations
- Waterproof mechanical construction for use in exposed environments
- Two variant options differing by inputs and outputs to suit basic I O needs
- Single processor architecture combining positioning and connectivity
- GPRS based remote monitoring and control for continuous operations
- ANATEL certified and compliant with relevant informatics legislation
- Features such as accelerometer detection, emergency trigger input, and output control for vehicle locking

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the MXT-160 supplies location and status events that Plaspy uses to present real time visibility and historical tracking across a fleet. Plaspy organizes incoming data to support operational oversight without requiring complex device side configuration.

- Real time location display and historical route playback within Plaspy
- Event handling for inputs such as panic triggers or movement detected by the accelerometer
- Monitoring of virtual ignition state and other input derived statuses for operational alerts
- Remote output control via Plaspy to support actions such as vehicle locking where the tracker hardware provides an output
- Fleet level reporting and analytics based on location, events, and uptime

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle fleets and delivery services requiring compact and weather resistant trackers
- Rental car fleets and vehicle rental operations needing location and remote control features
- Insurance and asset recovery projects that use movement and emergency triggers for risk management
- Financial institutions and high volume telemetry programs where low maintenance devices are preferred
- Operations that require simplified installations and straightforward event reporting

The MXT-160 is a practical choice when organizations need a small, robust tracker that minimizes field maintenance while providing essential tracking and event capabilities. Its focus on durable construction, interference resistance, and basic input/output flexibility makes it a fit for large scale deployments where operational continuity is a priority.
