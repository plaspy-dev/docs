# Maxtrack - MXT-140 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Maxtrack MXT-140 and explains how its capabilities are typically used with Plaspy. It focuses on user-facing functions and operational value without diving into confidential configuration details. Where relevant, the content highlights functions that Plaspy can receive and present for fleet visibility and monitoring.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation choices. MXT-140 variants and installation wiring influence which inputs and outputs are present and how features such as virtual ignition, panic button input, or lock output behave in practice.

## Feature Overview

The MXT-140 is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for high volume operations and low field maintenance. It pairs positioning and GPRS communications in a single optimized unit and is commonly used where a small form factor, waterproofing, and electrical robustness are priorities.

- Compact, low profile form factor suited for installations with limited space such as motorcycles
- GPRS based remote reporting and monitoring for regions with cellular coverage
- Robust electrical protections and design tolerant of voltage variations and interference
- Waterproof mechanical design for exposure to outdoor and automotive environments
- Two model variants A and B that differ in the number of available inputs and outputs for flexible installations

## Core Features of Maxtrack - MXT-140

- Real time positioning capability suitable for continuous monitoring and fleet tracking
- Integrated GSM/GPRS connectivity managed by a single internal processor for positioning and embedded applications
- Electrical protection and design that helps tolerate voltage peaks and interference in automotive environments
- Waterproof mechanical construction for improved reliability in wet or exposed installations
- Model variants MXT-140 A and MXT-140 B with differing counts of inputs and outputs for installation flexibility
- Panic button input for emergency event triggering when wired in an installation
- Output for vehicle locking or immobilization controlled by external wiring
- Accelerometer based movement detection to report motion when the vehicle is off where the feature is enabled

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location and event data from compatible trackers like the MXT-140 and presents that information in maps, event timelines, and device status views. Plaspy automatically detects many tracker protocols and surfaces the most relevant data for monitoring and operational use.

- Location updates and movement events appear on Plaspy maps for live and historical tracking
- Digital input events such as panic button presses are logged and shown as alerts or timeline events
- Output state changes or immobilizer actions can be recorded and displayed where the hardware and installation support remote control
- Motion detection events derived from the tracker accelerometer are available for activity alerts and theft indication workflows
- Device health and connectivity status reported by the unit are visible in Plaspy for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Motorcycle fleet tracking where small size, impermeability, and electrical robustness are required
- High volume rental or shared vehicle fleets that need low maintenance hardware and reliable positioning
- Insurance and telematics projects that require movement detection and emergency input events
- Financial service vehicle monitoring where simplified installation and continuous operation are priorities
- Remote monitoring of mobile assets in areas with GPRS coverage
- Projects that require a choice between different input output configurations using the A and B variants
