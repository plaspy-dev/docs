# Maxtrack - MXT-140

![MXT-140](./logo.jpg)

The Maxtrack MXT-140 is a highly efficient GPS tracker that has gained popularity in the market. It is specifically designed for high volume operations that require minimal field maintenance. One of its standout features is its robustness in terms of electrical protections and impermeability. The MXT-140 is available in two models: MXT-140 A and MXT-140B, with the only difference being the number of inputs and outputs. This tracker's small size and versatile configuration options make it suitable for installation on motorcycles, where issues such as consumption, interference, and impermeability are common. It can be used for remote monitoring and control of various mobile assets in regions with GPRS coverage.

The MXT-140 is equipped with a single internal processor that handles positioning, GSM/GPRS connection, and embedded applications. This optimized architecture ensures that the product offers the best consumer ratio available in the market. The electronic design of the tracker enables it to operate in the most challenging automotive situations, dealing with interference and voltage peaks. Additionally, the mechanical design ensures that the MXT-140 is waterproof and can withstand diverse environmental conditions.

The MXT-140 is certified by ANATEL and complies with the Informatics Legislation. It is an ideal solution for high volume operations that prioritize positioning. Its robustness, resistance to interference, mechanical durability, impermeability, and ability to handle voltage variations make it highly efficient in continuous operations without the need for frequent interventions. This tracker is particularly suitable for projects in the insurance, financial, and rental car fleet industries, offering simplified installation and low complexity of information. It provides essential features such as positioning information, emergency triggering through a panic button input, and the ability to lock the vehicle through an output. The MXT-140 can also utilize accelerometer information to detect movement when the vehicle is off, potentially indicating theft. Another noteworthy feature is the virtual ignition, which simplifies installation and provides flexibility in choosing the installation location while generating information about the vehicle's electrical system for proactive actions.
