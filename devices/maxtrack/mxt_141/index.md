# Maxtrack - MXT-141

![MXT-141](./logo.jpg)

The Maxtrack MXT-141 is a unique GPS tracker in the market, developed with the latest technology to provide advanced tracking capabilities. It features a circuit for wireless communication with embedded accessories, making it stand out from other trackers in the industry.

Designed with the same platform as the MXT-151, the MXT-141 maintains the same technical and constructive characteristics. However, it offers additional features such as a secondary communication circuit operating at 2.4GHz, extra inputs for RPM/odometer evaluation, and an accelerometer with the ability to detect movement and stoppage.

The MXT-141 is equipped with a range of features that enable flexible and customized solutions to meet the specific needs of each operation. Its wireless communication capability allows for the use of devices developed by Maxtrack, creating a seamless and integrated tracking system.

With the MXT-141, you have the option to utilize an embedded communication circuit for short-distance communication, opening up a wide range of architectural alternatives. This includes the construction of a secondary 2.4GHz communication network, providing practical and efficient solutions for various tracking applications.

### Outstanding Features:

- Advanced wireless communication circuit for embedded accessories
- Secondary communication circuit operating at 2.4GHz
- Additional inputs for RPM/odometer evaluation
- Accelerometer with movement and stoppage detection
- Flexible and customizable architecture solutions
