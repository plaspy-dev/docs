# Maxtrack - MXT-141

<div className="device-logo">
![MXT-141](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Maxtrack MXT-141 is a compact GPS tracker built on the same platform as the MXT-151 and designed to provide advanced tracking capabilities for vehicle and asset monitoring. It includes an embedded wireless communication circuit for accessories, a secondary 2.4GHz communication link, additional inputs for RPM and odometer evaluation, and an accelerometer capable of detecting movement and stoppage. These features make the MXT-141 suitable for operations that need flexible sensor integration and short range accessory communication.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the MXT-141 can be integrated into fleet monitoring and tracking workflows to provide visibility and operational data within the Plaspy platform. Its accessory communication options and movement detection complement Plaspy’s tracking, alerting, and reporting features, helping teams centralize monitoring and gain actionable insight without requiring bespoke hardware changes.

## Key Highlights

- Embedded wireless communication circuit for Maxtrack accessories enabling short range integrations
- Secondary 2.4GHz communication link for additional local connectivity options
- Extra inputs for RPM and odometer evaluation to support operational reporting
- Built in accelerometer for movement and stoppage detection to assist in event generation
- Shares platform and construction characteristics with the MXT-151 for deployment consistency
- Flexible architecture that supports customized tracking solutions and accessory layouts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the MXT-141 supplies positional and event information to the platform so operators can view, analyze, and act on vehicle and asset activity. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it alongside other fleet information for consolidated oversight.

- Real time location visibility and historical route playback inside Plaspy dashboards
- Movement and stoppage events from the accelerometer mapped to alerts and timelines
- RPM and odometer inputs used for operational reports such as utilization and service scheduling
- Wireless accessory events integrated into Plaspy event logs to track auxiliary devices
- Use of the secondary 2.4GHz link to support short range architectures that feed into Plaspy monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking with added movement detection for stop and start monitoring
- Integration of Maxtrack accessories for localized sensor reporting and accessory control
- Reporting on RPM and odometer data to support maintenance planning and utilization analysis
- Site or yard monitoring using short range 2.4GHz links for dense deployment scenarios
- Asset protection and movement alerts where accelerometer detection improves situational awareness

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The MXT-141 is a pragmatic choice for organizations that need a tracker capable of supporting on-device accessory communication while feeding core tracking and event data into a centralized platform. Its shared platform with the MXT-151 can simplify procurement and deployment decisions, and the additional inputs and accelerometer broaden the types of operational data that Plaspy can display and report on.

Paired with Plaspy, the MXT-141 supports a range of monitoring workflows without requiring extensive modification to your fleet software environment. The device’s flexible architecture makes it a good fit where short range accessory links and movement-based events are valuable to daily operations.
