# NP3

NP3 is a brand of GPS tracking devices commonly used for vehicle and asset tracking. This page explains how NP3 devices work with Plaspy, what common capabilities you can expect from NP3 hardware, and how to find the right NP3 device for your monitoring needs. Use the device catalog below to view compatible models and navigate to detailed device pages in Plaspy.

## How NP3 devices work with Plaspy

Plaspy connects to NP3 devices through standard GPS telemetry and cellular data links. When an NP3 tracker is configured to send location and status packets, Plaspy ingests that data, decodes the fields, and visualizes them in maps, reports, and alerts. Integration typically involves:

- Adding the device identifier and IMEI to your Plaspy account
- Ensuring the device is configured to send reports in a format compatible with Plaspy
- Verifying data transmission over the device SIM or network link

For most NP3 trackers, Plaspy provides the ability to process GPS positions, speed, ignition status, and basic input/output signals. Detailed setup steps and parameter values belong on each device model page where model specific instructions and example configurations are provided.
