# NP3 - ST 210

![ST 210](./logo.jpg)

The NP3 ST 210 is an onboard GPS tracker built to provide comprehensive control over vehicle parameters. Designed with time tested components and passed through individual quality checks and climatic testing, the ST 210 is intended for reliable daily operation across a wide range of transport types. Its features focus on continuous location tracking, event detection, and a suite of vehicle metrics that help managers keep close oversight of assets in motion.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the ST 210 can feed its tracking and event data into the Plaspy fleet management platform to support real time visibility and historical reporting. Plaspy users can leverage the device's telemetry and event reporting for monitoring routes, fuel and sensor inputs, alarm conditions, and offline uploads, taking advantage of the device capabilities while managing fleets from a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- Onboard GPS tracking with Galileo Lite positioning for consistent vehicle location reporting
- Comprehensive telemetry including coordinates speed direction and recorded events
- Analog sensor support for fuel monitoring and detection of drains and refills
- Built in memory for offline storage and later upload of recorded points
- Event recording support including connection for cameras and driver manager voice communication
- Robust design with individual quality control and testing in varied climatic conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

The NP3 ST 210 streams vehicle data that Plaspy can display, analyze and act on to give fleet managers operational control. Plaspy ingests device messages to provide a unified view of location history current status and alerts tied to vehicle behavior.

- Real time and near real time location tracking visible on Plaspy maps for fleet oversight
- Alerts and event notifications for speeding sensor triggers and other violations routed into Plaspy workflows
- Fuel and analog sensor readings integrated into reports and dashboards for consumption monitoring
- Offline data upload capability lets Plaspy reconstruct trips and events when a device reconnects
- Support for camera event flags and recorded files to associate visual evidence with tracked events
- Historical playback and route reconstruction using the device memory of recorded points

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet route monitoring for delivery and logistics operations
- Fuel consumption tracking and detection of unauthorized refills or drains
- Vehicle event recording and evidence collection for incidents using connected cameras
- Remote supervision of mixed fleets operating in challenging climates or remote areas
- Driver communication and simple voice interactions between driver and manager during operations

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The NP3 ST 210 pairs a broad set of vehicle monitoring features with a focus on durability and reliable data capture, making it a practical choice for organizations that need consistent telemetry across varied transport types. When used with Plaspy the tracker helps turn raw device data into actionable insights for route optimization, asset protection, and operational reporting.

Because the ST 210 supports both continuous online monitoring and offline data storage and upload, it is well suited to operations that require both live visibility and accurate historical records. Combined with Plaspy the device can contribute to a clearer operational picture without adding unnecessary complexity.
