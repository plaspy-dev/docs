# Telic - SafeDrive

![SafeDrive](./logo.jpg)

The Telic SafeDrive is a versatile GPS tracker designed specifically for telematics applications. Its plug and play functionality makes it incredibly easy to use, allowing for quick installation and removal from the 12V socket of a vehicle. This feature is particularly useful in delivery logistics, where subcontractors can be easily located for the duration of a delivery by simply plugging in the device. 

But the SafeDrive is not limited to just delivery logistics. It can also be used for a variety of other applications such as track and trace, theft protection, and monitoring business travel with a car. Its wide range of features and setting options make it a flexible solution for various tracking needs. Additionally, the SafeDrive comes equipped with an integrated USB socket, allowing for the charging of external devices such as smartphones.

With its compact size and easy installation, the Telic SafeDrive is a reliable and convenient GPS tracker for telematics applications. Whether you need to track vehicles for delivery purposes or ensure the safety and security of your assets, the SafeDrive offers a user-friendly solution with advanced features. Stay connected and in control with the Telic SafeDrive.

### Outstanding Features:

- Plug and play functionality for easy installation and removal
- Wide range of applications including delivery logistics, track and trace, theft protection, and business travel
- Integrated USB socket for charging external devices
- Compact size for discreet tracking
- Advanced features and setting options for customization
