# Telic - SafeDrive Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Telic SafeDrive tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes practical capabilities and common uses, and explains how those capabilities map into Plaspy for visibility and operation. The content here is intended as a features-oriented overview rather than an installation or configuration guide.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, firmware details, and the latest technical specifications please consult Telic documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Telic SafeDrive is a compact plug and play GPS tracker built for telematics applications where quick installation and mobility are important. Its ease of use and integrated convenience features make it practical for short term installs and fleet workflows that need simple, reliable location reporting.

- Plug and play design that connects to a vehicle 12V socket for quick installation and removal
- Practical for delivery logistics and subcontractor tracking where devices are added temporarily
- Track and trace capability for monitoring vehicle location and movement
- Useful for theft protection and locating vehicles in case of unauthorized movement
- Integrated USB socket to charge external devices such as smartphones
- Compact, discreet form factor for unobtrusive installation

## Core Features of Telic - SafeDrive

- Plug and play operation making deployment and transfer between vehicles fast
- Designed for use from a vehicle power socket which simplifies temporary installs
- GPS tracking and location reporting suitable for telematics and track and trace
- Practical support for delivery logistics and short term driver assignments
- Features and setting options that allow customization according to use case
- Integrated USB socket for charging an external device during operation
- Compact size for discreet placement inside the vehicle
- Suitable for monitoring business travel and fleet activity

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and displays SafeDrive location and status information so fleet managers and operators can monitor vehicles and assignments from a central platform. The combination of SafeDrive hardware and Plaspy software provides operational visibility without complex installation work.

- Live location and position updates visible in Plaspy maps and device lists
- Historical tracks and trip visibility to review delivery routes and driver movement
- Device status indicators to show when a SafeDrive unit is connected or offline
- Settings exposed by the device can be reflected in Plaspy as configurable reporting behavior
- Operational overlays such as vehicle assignment and tracking periods support delivery workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Short term tracking for subcontractors in delivery operations where devices are shared
- Track and trace of vehicles for logistics and last mile visibility
- Monitoring business travel and mileage oversight for company cars
- Theft protection support by providing location data to aid recovery
- Temporary tracking for events, rentals, or loaned vehicles
- Compact asset tracking where discreet installation is preferred

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions and device settings determine the exact set of available functions and how they behave
- Hardware revisions or regional variants might change connectors, power requirements, or supported options
- Installation type matters: plug and play use from a 12V socket differs from hardwired installations in behavior and permanence
- Manufacturer configuration and provisioning affect what settings are available for Plaspy integration
- Always test a device after installation to confirm expected reporting and visibility in Plaspy

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using Telic SafeDrive with Plaspy gives organizations a streamlined way to turn small, portable trackers into operational fleet data. The plug and play nature of the SafeDrive reduces installation overhead for temporary assignments, while Plaspy provides centralized visibility, history, and device management so teams can monitor deliveries, business travel, and recover assets more efficiently.

Learn more about how Plaspy supports trackers like the Telic SafeDrive at https://www.plaspy.com and verify the most current device feature details, firmware behavior, and manufacturer guidance on the official Telic website https://www.telic.de.
