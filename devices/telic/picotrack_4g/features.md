# Telic - Picotrack 4G Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Telic Picotrack 4G with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities that matter when integrating the Picotrack 4G into Plaspy powered tracking, telemetry, and asset management workflows, and explains how those capabilities map into Plaspy visibility and operations.

Exact feature availability for any Picotrack 4G unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the specific manufacturer implementation or variant. For device level details and the latest specifications consult the manufacturer documentation before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The Picotrack 4G is a compact LTE Cat M1 device designed for long life, reliable telemetry, and flexible field installation. In Plaspy deployments it provides location and telemetry reporting suitable for long term unattended assets, fleet units, and regulated environments where a government protocol variant is required.

- LTE Cat M1 connectivity for efficient cellular telemetry and long range coverage where LTE M is supported
- Configurable reporting intervals and power efficient operation that can extend deployments to weeks or months
- Rugged IP69K housing option for demanding outdoor and environmental conditions
- Multiple mounting options including magnets, screws, and cable ties for flexible field installation
- Compact form factor suited to space constrained or discreet installations
- PAIP UKSP protocol variant available for government environment integrations

## Core Features of Telic - Picotrack 4G

- LTE Cat M1 wireless connectivity for low power wide area telemetry
- Configurable reporting cadence to balance update frequency and battery life
- Rugged IP69K rated housing option for harsh conditions
- Multiple mounting choices for rapid field installation and repositioning
- Compact telematics form factor for discreet asset and vehicle installs
- Government environment protocol variant PAIP UKSP for compliant deployments
- Designed for long term unattended operation with power efficient firmware

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When you register and operate a Picotrack 4G with Plaspy, device messages and location updates are ingested and presented through Plaspy monitoring, mapping, and reporting tools. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and accepts telemetry over standard transport configurations so device data becomes visible in dashboards and historical records.

- Location and periodic telemetry appear on Plaspy real time maps and device lists for operational awareness
- Configurable report intervals on the device translate into heartbeat and history entries inside Plaspy so you can track device activity and uptime
- Rugged housing and flexible mounting options reduce field maintenance and improve data continuity for Plaspy reporting
- The PAIP UKSP variant is accepted when the manufacturer provides that protocol, enabling regulated or government workflows to deliver messages into Plaspy
- Plaspy can receive device traffic using common transport options and automatically detect the device protocol so onboarding is straightforward

Note: Plaspy accepts trackers configured to report to the platform endpoints and can auto detect supported tracker protocols during ingestion.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for vehicles where compact units and long life reporting are required
- Remote asset monitoring for equipment deployed in harsh or exposed environments
- Anti theft and recovery workflows where discreet mounting and real time location help recovery
- Government or regulated deployments that require PAIP UKSP protocol compatibility
- Seasonal or temporary installations using magnets or ties for quick deployment
- Long term telemetry projects where low power LTE M reporting reduces maintenance visits

## Feature Availability Notes

- Exact behavior depends on firmware version and configuration; reporting intervals and power use vary by settings
- Hardware revisions and regional cellular band support affect LTE M availability and carrier compatibility
- The government protocol variant PAIP UKSP is a specific firmware or communication option and may not be present on all units
- Installation method and environment influence signal quality and battery life for remote monitoring
- Always verify the variant and firmware that will be shipped for your order when planning regulated or critical deployments

Pairing the Picotrack 4G with Plaspy provides a straightforward path from rugged LTE M telematics to operational visibility.
