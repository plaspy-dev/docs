# Telic - SBC3 CAN

<div className="device-logo">
![SBC3 CAN](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Telic SBC3 CAN is a rugged and versatile GPS tracker designed for demanding telematics applications. It provides access to a range of vehicle interfaces including RS232, 1-wire, and CAN bus, and is built in a durable housing intended for harsh environments. Available antenna configurations and an optional 4 Ah battery variant give operators flexibility in how they deploy the device.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SBC3 CAN can be integrated into Plaspy's fleet monitoring and tracking workflows to provide location visibility and operational oversight. Its combination of interface access, form factor options, and robust design makes it a practical choice for fleets and assets where reliable telematics data and environmental resilience are important.

## Key Highlights

- Access to multiple vehicle interfaces including RS232, 1-wire, and CAN bus for broad telematics integration
- Durable housing suited to harsh environmental conditions common in transportation and logistics
- Choice of internal or external antenna configurations to match installation and reception needs
- Optional 4 Ah battery variant for extended operation where external power is limited
- Designed for demanding fleet and industrial telematics use cases
- Flexibility of configuration to suit different vehicle and asset types

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SBC3 CAN can feed vehicle location and telematics information into Plaspy so teams can monitor assets, analyze trips, and respond to events. Plaspy ingests the device data and presents it through dashboards, reports, and alerting tools tailored to fleet operations.

- Live location tracking and map visualization within the Plaspy platform for operational visibility
- Delivery of vehicle interface data to Plaspy for correlation with location and status monitoring
- Configurable alerts and notifications in Plaspy based on movement, location, or device status
- Historical reporting and trip playback to support route analysis and compliance review
- Centralized device status and fleet overview to simplify operational management

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet tracking for transportation and logistics companies needing robust device hardware
- Monitoring heavy vehicles and equipment operating in harsh environments
- Remote asset tracking where antenna and power configuration flexibility is required
- Telematics integration projects that require access to vehicle interface data
- Operations where extended battery options help bridge gaps in external power availability

The SBC3 CAN is a practical choice for organizations that need a dependable tracker able to handle tough operating conditions while providing access to vehicle interface data. When paired with Plaspy, it becomes part of a managed telematics solution that emphasizes visibility, reporting, and operational oversight across a mixed fleet.

Plaspy users benefit from the SBC3 CAN's configuration options and rugged design without needing to manage separate visualization and reporting tools. The combination is well suited to transportation, logistics, and other fleet-focused operations that value flexibility and resilience.
