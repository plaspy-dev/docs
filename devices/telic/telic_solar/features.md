# Telic - Telic Solar Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Telic Solar tracker with Plaspy. It summarizes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to Plaspy users, explains how Telic Solar can contribute to persistent asset visibility, and highlights the kinds of telemetry and operational value Plaspy can present when the device is connected to the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For deployment planning and technical details always refer to the official Telic documentation and the device datasheet to confirm the options and configurations available on the units you intend to use.

## Feature Overview

Telic Solar is a compact, high ingress protection tracker designed for long term tracking of mobile capital goods. Its small flat form factor, integrated solar energy management, and the ability to receive interior sensor telemetry via a directional Bluetooth antenna are the key user-facing aspects that make it well suited for container, swap body, and wagon deployments.

- Compact flat design built to fit into common container beads for unobtrusive installation.
- IP69 rated enclosure for protection in harsh logistics and intermodal environments.
- Integrated solar panel and energy management to support extended autonomous operation.
- Directional Bluetooth antenna to collect interior sensor telemetry from inside containers.
- Includes Telic tracking application software with configurable data forwarding for Plaspy.
- Cost optimized construction focused on long duration, low maintenance tracking campaigns.

## Core Features of Telic - Telic Solar

- Small, flat physical form factor intended for tight mounting locations in containers and trailers.
- High ingress protection rating designed for exposure to dust, water spray, and heavy handling.
- Onboard solar energy harvesting and proven energy management for extended deployments.
- Directional Bluetooth antenna for receiving interior sensor data such as environmental telemetry.
- Telic tracking application software that can be customized to map and forward telemetry.
- Rugged mechanical construction suitable for intermodal transport and freight environments.
- Designed specifically for long term visibility of mobile capital assets like containers and wagons.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When Telic Solar is deployed and configured to forward data, Plaspy ingests location and telemetry so fleet and operations teams can see asset position and interior sensor readings in a single view. Plaspy maps the device data into dashboards, histories, and monitoring rules so teams can manage large numbers of assets with transparency.

- Location and tracking updates appear in Plaspy for real time and historical position awareness.
- Interior telemetry collected by the directional Bluetooth antenna is forwarded and displayed alongside location data.
- Energy and solar status information can be used by Plaspy to inform expected reporting cadence and maintenance planning.
- Customizable data fields from the Telic application can be mapped in Plaspy for tailored reporting and alerting.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify initial device onboarding and data ingestion.

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term fleet management of intermodal containers with persistent position visibility.
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery workflows supported by continuous tracking and telemetry.
- Environmental monitoring inside cargo using Bluetooth sensors relayed by the tracker.
- Rail wagon and port handling visibility where compact form factor and protection are required.
- Extended duration tracking campaigns that reduce maintenance through solar energy harvesting.
