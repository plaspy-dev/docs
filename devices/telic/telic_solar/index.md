# Telic - Telic Solar

![Telic Solar](./tracker.png)

Telic Solar is a compact, IP69-rated GPS tracker-class device engineered for reliable long-term location of mobile capital goods such as containers, swap bodies and freight wagons. Designed to be exceptionally small and flat, Telic Solar fits into common container beads without adding bulk. Its robust housing and powerful solar panel deliver extended autonomous operation, making it an ideal Plaspy compatible choice for customers who need persistent asset visibility with minimal maintenance.

Built for cost-optimised performance, Telic Solar includes Telic’s tracking application software and can be customised to match operational needs. A directional Bluetooth antenna relays interior sensor data from inside the container to the tracker, allowing Plaspy to display telemetry, environmental sensor readings, and location information together—supporting fleet management, anti-theft workflows, and long-duration tracking campaigns.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Telic Solar forwards asset location and sensor telemetry into Plaspy’s platform via the Telic tracking application and configurable back-end settings, enabling unified dashboards, alerts, and historical reports. Plaspy ingests the device’s transmitted data to provide fleet managers and logistics teams with actionable insights—combining location, interior sensor readings and energy status into a single view for efficient fleet management and anti-theft monitoring.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(location and reporting cadence depend on configuration and connectivity\).
- Interior environmental telemetry received via the tracker’s directional Bluetooth antenna and passed to Plaspy.
- Energy and solar-panel status telemetry to help Plaspy predict reporting availability and schedule checks.
- Customizable data fields via Telic’s tracking application so Plaspy can map device telemetry to fleet management, reporting and alert rules.
- Long-term tracking for mobile capital goods—optimised for extended deployments with minimal maintenance.
