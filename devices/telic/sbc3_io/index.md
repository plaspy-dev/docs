# Telic - SBC3 IO

![SBC3 IO](./logo.jpg)

The Telic SBC3 IO is a robust telematics unit designed for outdoor location applications. Its rugged and water-resistant housing makes it ideal for track and trace applications that require outdoor installation. The device features a high-sensitivity GPS receiver and integrated GSM/GPS antennas, allowing for fast and uncomplicated installation. 

One of the standout features of the SBC3 IO is its built-in rechargeable battery, which ensures the transmission of position data even when the device is disconnected from the main power source. This is particularly useful in situations where a continuous power supply may not be available. 

Additionally, the SBC3 IO offers digital/analog inputs and outputs, enabling the implementation of telematics applications such as the recording of the service life of vehicles. This allows for greater flexibility and customization in tracking and monitoring operations. 

With its durable construction and versatile features, the Telic SBC3 IO is a reliable choice for outdoor location applications that require robust and accurate tracking capabilities. Whether it's for fleet management, asset tracking, or other outdoor tracking needs, the SBC3 IO delivers reliable performance in demanding environments.
