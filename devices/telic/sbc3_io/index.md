# Telic - SBC3 IO

<div className="device-logo">
![SBC3 IO](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Telic SBC3 IO is a rugged telematics unit built for outdoor location applications. Designed with a water resistant housing and a high sensitivity GPS receiver, the SBC3 IO is intended for track and trace scenarios where devices are exposed to the elements. Integrated GSM and GPS antennas simplify deployment for sites that require reliable position reporting in open environments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the SBC3 IO brings durable outdoor tracking into the Plaspy platform for fleet and asset visibility. Its built in rechargeable battery provides continuity of position transmissions if main power is lost, and the available digital and analog inputs and outputs support common telematics tasks such as recording operational counters or service intervals. These characteristics make the unit a practical option for organizations that need resilient outdoor tracking combined with Plaspy monitoring and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Rugged water resistant housing designed for outdoor deployments and demanding environments
- High sensitivity GPS receiver for improved location acquisition in open areas
- Integrated GSM and GPS antennas to streamline unit placement and reduce external cabling needs
- Built in rechargeable battery to transmit position data when external power is unavailable
- Digital and analog inputs and outputs for custom telematics signals and event recording
- Durable construction suited for long term outdoor asset and fleet tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

The SBC3 IO can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous location visibility and event driven monitoring for outdoor assets. Once connected, Plaspy can aggregate position updates and input driven events from the device to give operators a unified operational picture.

- Real time location tracking and map visualization within the Plaspy platform
- Event and input monitoring using the device IO to flag key operational changes
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy for power loss, movement, or configured input conditions
- Historical route and position reporting to support audits and performance analysis
- Centralized oversight for dispersed outdoor assets and mixed fleets

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicles and trailers that operate in outdoor or rugged conditions
- Asset tracking for equipment stored or used outdoors at construction or worksites
- Remote units that require battery backup to maintain position reporting during power interruptions
- Service life or usage recording where IO signals track operational hours or events
- Monitoring of stand alone assets that need durable, weather resistant telematics

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The SBC3 IO is a sensible choice for organizations that need a robust outdoor tracker combined with flexible event inputs. Its durable housing and battery backup reduce the risk of data gaps in environments where power or shelter is limited, while IO capability supports tailored telematics workflows that Plaspy can monitor and report on.

Paired with Plaspy, the SBC3 IO helps bring location, event data, and operational oversight together in one platform. This combination is useful for teams looking to extend fleet and asset visibility to outdoor equipment without sacrificing continuity or basic customization options.
