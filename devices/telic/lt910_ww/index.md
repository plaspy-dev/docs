# Telic - LT910-WW

<div className="device-logo">
![LT910-WW](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The LT910-WW is a compact industrial IoT terminal designed for global cellular deployments. Built around the Telit LE910C1-WWX module, it provides LTE primary connectivity with automatic fallback to 3G and 2G, a rugged operating temperature range, a wide input voltage capability, and industrial interfaces for attaching external GNSS units or telemetry equipment. Its lockable SIM holder and robust housing make it suitable for vehicle, cabinet, and field asset installations where reliable cellular transport is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the LT910-WW serves as a reliable communications gateway for fleet management and remote telemetry workflows. When combined with external GNSS receivers, sensors, or vehicle control interfaces via the available serial and USB connections, the terminal can forward location and status data to Plaspy for real time visibility, alerts, and historical reporting. This combination makes the LT910-WW a practical choice for organisations that need modular, field hardened connectivity paired with Plaspy analytics and operations tools.

## Key Highlights

- LTE primary connectivity with automatic fallback to 3G and 2G for persistent cloud reporting.
- Based on the Telit LE910C1-WWX wideband module for broad global cellular support.
- Industrial interfaces including RS232 and USB for integration with GNSS units and external telemetry devices.
- Wide supply range and rugged operating temperature for demanding vehicle and field environments.
- Lockable SIM holder and external antenna connector for secure and reliable field use.
- Low power mode and compact industrial housing suited to portable or constrained deployments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LT910-WW transmits telemetry and status data over cellular links to Plaspy, where that data is processed into dashboards, reports, geofences and alerting rules. Plaspy ingests positional and telemetry streams forwarded by the terminal so fleet operators and technicians can monitor assets in near real time and run operational reports over historical data.

- Real time location and movement updates when the terminal is paired with an external GNSS receiver or NMEA feed.
- Status and event forwarding such as ignition, door, or alarm conditions when those signals are provided to the terminal interfaces.
- Alerting and geofence triggers in Plaspy based on location, time, or telemetry thresholds reported by the device.
- Historical reporting, route analysis and uptime monitoring from the telemetry Plaspy stores.
- Remote action workflows such as immobilizer commands or remote controls when Plaspy triggers outputs through connected vehicle interfaces.

## Typical Use Cases

- Global fleet connectivity and monitoring across international deployments where multi band cellular coverage is required.
- Remote asset telemetry for field equipment and generators using external sensors and GNSS receivers.
- Vehicle anti theft and immobilization workflows when combined with external control interfaces and Plaspy actions.
- Temporary or portable site monitoring where wide input voltage tolerance and low power modes are beneficial.
- Industrial machine monitoring for centralized visibility of status and alerts through Plaspy.
