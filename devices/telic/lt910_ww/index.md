# LT910-WW IoT Terminal — Plaspy Compatible

The LT910-WW is a compact, industrial IoT terminal built for global LTE deployments with automatic fallback to 3G and 2G. Based on the Telit LE910C1-WWX module, the LT910-WW delivers broad band support and rugged design for reliable cellular connectivity in demanding environments. Its industrial interfaces, wide supply range, lockable SIM holder and robust operating temperature make it a strong choice for Plaspy-compatible integrations across fleet management, remote telemetry and anti-theft workflows.

The LT910-WW pairs easily with Plaspy to provide secure data transport and flexible telemetry forwarding. While the terminal focuses on robust cellular connectivity and industrial I/O, it can be integrated with external GNSS receivers, sensors or vehicle systems via RS232 or USB to act as a GPS tracker or telemetry gateway. This makes it an ideal Plaspy compatible device for customers who need real-time tracking, fleet management insights, or integration with ignition, immobilizer and fuel monitoring systems when additional peripherals are attached.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible connectivity: LTE primary with 3G/2G fallback for continuous cloud reporting and real-time tracking.
- Wideband global support via the Telit LE910C1-WWX module — designed for international IoT deployments.
- Industrial-grade power and thermal range \(approx. 5–32 V DC; -30 °C to +80 °C\) for harsh environments.
- Standard industrial interfaces \(RS232 V.24/V.28 on Sub-D, USB\) for integrating GNSS receivers, vehicle telemetry, or external sensors.
- Secure, lockable SIM holder and FME antenna connector for reliable field installations and anti-theft resilience.
- Low-power mode for battery-backed or energy-constrained applications and remote asset tracking scenarios.
- Compact industrial housing simplifies installation in vehicles, cabinets, or field assets for fleet management and asset tracking.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The LT910-WW sends telemetry and status data over LTE \(with 3G/2G fallback\) to Plaspy’s cloud endpoints using standard TCP/UDP or HTTP\(S\) protocols supported by your Plaspy instance. Plaspy ingests that data to provide dashboards, geofences, alerts and historical reports. Because the terminal exposes RS232 and USB interfaces, you can attach GNSS receivers, CAN/OBD adapters or other sensor modules to provide the positional and analog/digital telemetry Plaspy expects for GPS tracker and fleet management use cases.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — when paired with an external GNSS receiver or NMEA feed via RS232/USB.
- Ignition, door or alarm status — achievable by integrating vehicle interfaces or external I/O converters that feed Plaspy through the LT910-WW.
- Fuel monitoring and analog telemetry — supported when external fuel sensors or ADC gateways are connected to the terminal’s interfaces.
- Remote immobilizer workflows — possible when the LT910-WW is combined with vehicle control interfaces and Plaspy rule actions.
- Bluetooth sensors — while the LT910-WW does not list built-in BLE, Plaspy can ingest data from BLE gateways attached via USB or serial.
