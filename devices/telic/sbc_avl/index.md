# Telic - SBC AVL

![SBC AVL](./logo.jpg)

The Telic SBC-AVL is a compact telematics unit designed for fleet management applications. With its integrated GSM/GPS antennas and support for 1-wire and CAN-bus interfaces, this cost-effective locating unit offers a wide range of features and capabilities.

One of the key advantages of the SBC-AVL is its versatility. It can be used for various telematics applications, including fleet management and driver detection. The integrated GSM and GPS antennas ensure reliable and accurate tracking, while the 1-wire and CAN-bus interfaces allow for seamless integration with other systems and devices.

With the Telic SBC-AVL, you can effectively manage your fleet and optimize your operations. It helps minimize the costs of commissioning and provides valuable insights into your fleet's performance and efficiency. Whether you need real-time tracking, driver behavior monitoring, or vehicle diagnostics, the SBC-AVL is a reliable and cost-effective solution.

Key Features:

- Compact and cost-effective telematics unit
- Integrated GSM/GPS antennas for reliable tracking
- Supports 1-wire and CAN-bus interfaces for seamless integration
- Ideal for fleet management and driver detection
- Helps minimize commissioning costs
