# Telic - SBC product family

![SBC product family](./tracker.jpg)

The SBC product family \(including SBC3 CAN 4G and SBC AVL 4G\) delivers a rugged telematics platform designed for fleet management and vehicle tracking and is Plaspy compatible out of the box. Built for quick installation and reliable long-term operation, SBC devices combine LTE Cat M1 connectivity and multiple wired interfaces so fleets and asset managers can enable real-time tracking, telemetry, and integration with Plaspy dashboards and automation rules.

Whether you deploy SBC units for trailer telematics, automatic production data acquisition, or mixed-vehicle fleets, the family gives you a stable hardware base for anti-theft workflows, fuel monitoring analytics, and broader fleet telemetry use cases. The SBC family’s robust housing and integrated antenna simplify mounting in varied positions while preserving consistent uplink for Plaspy real-time tracking and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible — designed to stream telematics over LTE Cat M1 for real-time tracking and centralized fleet management.
- LTE Cat M1 connectivity to balance coverage and low-power IoT communication across wide-area networks.
- Multiple wired interfaces \(CAN bus, 1-Wire, RS232\) for deep telemetry capture from vehicle systems and sensors.
- Robust housing and integrated antenna to support quick installation and dependable operation in vehicle and trailer environments.
- Optimized for fleet management workflows including route visibility, maintenance telemetry, and anti-theft monitoring.
- Suitable for automated production data acquisition — ideal where vehicle or asset signals need to be recorded and acted upon.
- Scalable platform: deploy single units or roll out across an entire fleet while maintaining Plaspy compatibility for reporting and alerts.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating SBC devices with Plaspy connects vehicle and asset telemetry to a cloud-based control plane for real-time tracking, alerts, and historical reporting. SBC units transmit data over LTE Cat M1; Plaspy ingests that stream and maps CAN/serial/1-Wire signals to dashboards, geofences, and operational rules. This enables live position updates and telemetry-driven automation within your Plaspy account.

- Real-time tracking: location updates sent over LTE Cat M1 feed Plaspy for live map views and route replay.
- Vehicle telemetry via CAN bus: SBC devices expose engine and vehicle bus data so Plaspy can surface fuel monitoring, RPM, and other CAN-provided metrics when available from the vehicle network.
- Sensor integration: 1-Wire and RS232 interfaces allow Plaspy to record ancillary sensors and production data from connected devices.
- Anti-theft workflows: combine real-time position with CAN-derived status to create alerts and immobilizer-like actions through Plaspy when integrations or external hardware support control functions.
- Correlated sensor data: Plaspy can combine SBC telemetry with external Bluetooth sensor feeds or other sources in your platform to support temperature monitoring, asset movement detection, and compliance reports.
