# ThinkRace - VT200

![VT200](./logo.jpg)

The ThinkRace VT200 is a professional OBD car tracker designed to deliver real time GPS tracking and vehicle monitoring for a wide range of vehicle types. Described as suitable for taxis, ambulances, and engineering vehicles, the VT200 provides location updates, mileage tracking, moving tests, abnormal alarms, and motion detection using an internal 3 axis accelerometer. Remote upgrade capability is also part of the device feature set.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT200 can feed vehicle location and status into the Plaspy platform so fleet managers and operators can view position, mileage trends, and abnormal activity from a single dashboard. Plaspy can use the VT200 data to improve fleet visibility, operational oversight, and alerting without requiring users to change their existing tracker hardware.

## Key Highlights

- Professional OBD car tracker built for vehicle fleets and professional use
- Real time GPS tracking for continuous location visibility
- Mileage tracking to help monitor distance and support maintenance planning
- Moving test and abnormal alarm features to detect unexpected activity
- Internal 3 axis accelerometer for motion detection and driving behavior insights
- Supports remote upgrades to keep the device up to date

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT200 provides vehicle position and event data that Plaspy ingests for mapping, monitoring, and reporting. Plaspy displays the tracker feeds alongside other devices to give a unified view of fleet status and historical activity.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for real time fleet visibility
- Mileage and movement records available for reporting and usage analysis
- Alerts for abnormal activity delivered through Plaspy notification settings
- Driving behavior indicators available for oversight and operational review
- Historical playback and timeline views to investigate past trips and events

## Typical Use Cases

- Taxi fleet location tracking and dispatch coordination
- Ambulance and emergency vehicle monitoring for response oversight
- Engineering and service vehicles tracking for jobsite coordination
- Company car mileage monitoring for maintenance planning and cost control
- Mixed vehicle fleets that require an OBD based solution for fleet management

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT200 is a practical choice for organizations that need an OBD style tracker with straightforward vehicle monitoring capabilities. Its combination of real time GPS, mileage logging, motion detection, and alarm features makes it suitable for business fleets where location accuracy and operational alerts matter. Using the VT200 with Plaspy lets teams centralize data from multiple vehicles and use Plaspy reporting and alerting tools to support day to day fleet decisions.
