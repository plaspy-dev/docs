# ThinkRace - VT200

![VT200](./logo.jpg)

The ThinkRace VT200 is a professional OBD Car Tracker designed to provide real-time GPS tracking for vehicles. Whether you own a taxi, ambulance, or engineering vehicle, this OBD2 Tracker is the perfect solution for keeping track of your vehicle's location and monitoring its performance. With its advanced features and reliable performance, the VT200 stands out as one of the best OBD Vehicle Tracking devices in its range.

One of the key features of the VT200 is its ability to perform real-time GPS tracking. This means that you can easily monitor the location of your vehicle at any given time. Whether you want to keep an eye on your fleet of taxis or ensure the safety of your ambulance, the VT200 provides accurate and up-to-date location information.

In addition to GPS tracking, the VT200 also offers a range of other useful functions. It includes a moving test feature, which allows you to test the movement of your vehicle and ensure that it is functioning properly. The mileage tracker feature helps you keep track of the distance traveled by your vehicle, making it easier to manage maintenance and fuel costs. The VT200 also includes an abnormal alarm feature, which alerts you in case of any unusual activity or behavior.

With its internal 3-axis accelerometer, the VT200 is capable of motion detection and driving behavior analysis. This allows you to monitor how your vehicle is being driven, helping you identify any unsafe or inefficient driving habits. The VT200 also supports remote upgrades, ensuring that you always have access to the latest features and improvements.

Overall, the ThinkRace VT200 is a reliable and feature-packed OBD Car Tracker that is suitable for a variety of vehicles. Whether you need to track your taxi fleet, monitor the performance of your ambulance, or keep an eye on your engineering vehicles, the VT200 is a top choice. With its real-time GPS tracking, mileage tracking, abnormal alarm, and motion detection capabilities, the VT200 offers everything you need to ensure the safety and efficiency of your vehicles.
