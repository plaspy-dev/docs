# ThinkRace - VT06

![VT06](./logo.jpg)

The ThinkRace VT06 is a compact vehicle tracking device built for continuous and accurate positioning. It provides real time tracking, track playback, electric fence functionality, and data statistics, together with an alarm suite covering power failure, low power, vibration, displacement, speedup, and fence breaches. These features make the VT06 a practical choice for monitoring vehicle location and activity across a range of vehicle types.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT06 can feed location and event data into Plaspy for unified monitoring and reporting. Plaspy users can incorporate VT06 units into fleet dashboards to view position history, generate usage reports, and receive configurable alerts, enabling better operational oversight without changing the core tracking capabilities the device provides.

## Key Highlights

- Continuous and accurate positioning for ongoing vehicle visibility
- Real time tracking with track playback for historical route review
- Electric fence support to detect and notify on fence breaches
- Comprehensive alarm system including power and motion related alerts
- Built in data statistics to support reporting and operational analysis
- Designed for integration into vehicle command and dispatch systems

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT06 streams its positional and event data into the platform so operators can manage devices and fleets from a single interface. Plaspy consolidates incoming data to provide situational awareness and operational controls suitable for both small fleets and larger deployments.

- Centralized live map view showing VT06 locations and recent movements
- Event and alarm forwarding so Plaspy can surface power, vibration, and fence alerts
- Track playback inside Plaspy for reviewing historical routes and activity
- Aggregated data statistics for reporting on usage patterns and vehicle behavior
- Notification rules and alerting to keep fleet managers informed of critical events

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for delivery and service operations
- Real time monitoring for company cars and light commercial vehicles
- Route review and driver behavior analysis using playback and statistics
- Asset security through electric fence and motion based alarms
- Integration into dispatch workflows for status and location queries

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The VT06 offers a balance of core tracking features and event alerts that align well with Plaspy's fleet visibility and monitoring capabilities. Its feature set supports common operational needs such as live tracking, geofencing alerts, and historical playback, allowing organizations to use Plaspy for day to day fleet oversight and reporting while relying on the VT06 for reliable position and event data.
