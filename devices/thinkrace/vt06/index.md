# ThinkRace - VT06

![VT06](./logo.jpg)

The ThinkRace VT06 is a highly reliable and feature-packed vehicle tracking device. Designed to provide continuous and accurate positioning, it offers a wide range of functions including real-time tracking, track playback, electric fence, and data statistics. With its advanced alarm system, it ensures the safety and security of your vehicle by providing alerts for power failure, low power, vibration, displacement, speedup, and fence breaches.

Whether you need GPS tracking for fleet vehicles or a global satellite positioning system for vehicle location queries and real-time monitoring, the VT06 is the perfect solution. It also offers time tracking and can be integrated into various vehicle command and dispatch systems. With its stable performance and powerful features, the VT06 is an ideal choice for businesses and individuals looking to enhance the security and efficiency of their vehicle operations.

#### Key Features:

- Continuous and accurate positioning
- Real-time tracking
- Track playback
- Electric fence
- Data statistics
- Power failure alarm
- Low power alarm
- Vibration alarm
- Displacement alarm
- Speedup alarm
- Fence alarm
