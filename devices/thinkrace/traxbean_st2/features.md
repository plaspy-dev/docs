# ThinkRace - Traxbean ST2 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the ThinkRace Traxbean ST2 and how its capabilities map into Plaspy for unified monitoring and incident response. It summarizes the tracker functions that are relevant to operators and integrators who want to bring the ST2 into a Plaspy powered monitoring environment without delving into private implementation details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, regional model variant, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific configuration, firmware updates, and the latest technical details consult the official ThinkRace documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Traxbean ST2 is a wrist worn tracker oriented to eldercare and supervised environments. It combines multi mode positioning, continuous physiological telemetry, and direct caregiver communication to support safety and situational awareness for individuals in home care or institutional settings.

- Four fold positioning using GPS, LBS, Wi Fi and Bluetooth to improve location accuracy across indoor and outdoor environments.
- Continuous health telemetry including heart rate, blood oxygen SpO2, blood pressure and step or activity counts for ongoing monitoring.
- Safety alerts such as automatic fall detection, immobility notifications and a user accessible SOS alarm for rapid incident reporting.
- Multimodal communications with two way voice calling plus text and voice message dispatch for caregiver interaction.
- Integration options including RESTful API, direct device to server forwarding, SDKs and ThinkRace white label SaaS channel for flexible deployment with Plaspy.

## Core Features of ThinkRace - Traxbean ST2

- Multi mode positioning: GPS combined with LBS Wi Fi and Bluetooth for enhanced indoor/outdoor location awareness.
- Physiological telemetry: continuous measurement streams for heart rate SpO2 and blood pressure plus activity and step counts.
- Safety and incident detection: fall detection algorithms, immobility alerts and a dedicated SOS button for wearer initiated alarms.
- Two way voice and messaging: built in speaker and microphone to enable direct calls and voice messages between wearer and caregivers.
- Bluetooth Low Energy support to enable proximity based location refinement and interaction with compatible beacons or accessories.
- Flexible data forwarding: support for direct device to server push, RESTful API ingestion and ThinkRace SaaS channel for different integration models.
- Mobile and web management tools: Android and iOS client apps and a web monitoring platform for device administration and oversight.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the Traxbean ST2 sends location and health telemetry into a centralized monitoring platform so teams can visualize status and respond to incidents from a single interface. Plaspy ingests device events and exposes them in maps dashboards and alerting workflows suited to care operations.

- Real time location plotting on Plaspy maps using combined GPS LBS Wi Fi and Bluetooth inputs to improve situational awareness.
- Health telemetry displayed in dashboards and available for alert rules so caregivers can be notified when heart rate SpO2 or blood pressure cross configured thresholds.
- SOS fall detection and immobility events generate clear alerts inside Plaspy to drive immediate response workflows.
- Two way communications and message events appear as activity items so caregiver interactions are visible alongside location and telemetry history.
- Historical reporting and exportable data can support auditing analytics and operational review for care programs.
- Central device management through Plaspy for mixed deployments that include wearables alongside other tracked assets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Home care monitoring for independent seniors with continuous vitals and SOS support.
- Assisted living and nursing home supervision with fall detection routed to staff dashboards.
- Hospital and facility tracking where indoor and outdoor positioning help locate patients or residents.
- Rapid caregiver coordination using two way voice and messaging tied to incident alerts.
- Integrated telemetry programs that combine physiological and location data for reporting and compliance.
- Mixed device deployments where wearable trackers are managed alongside other asset trackers within Plaspy.
