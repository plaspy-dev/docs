# ThinkRace - AT15 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the ThinkRace AT15 and how its capabilities relate to monitoring and reporting within the Plaspy platform. It focuses on user-facing functions such as live location visibility and stored location history that matter when you use the AT15 as part of a Plaspy deployment.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device-specific specifications and firmware notes, consult the official ThinkRace documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The ThinkRace AT15 is a compact luggage tracker designed to provide continuous location awareness and a record of where an item has traveled. When used with Plaspy, the AT15 contributes device-level position updates and history records that are useful for monitoring bags, briefcases, and other portable items.

- Real-time location reporting to enable immediate visibility of a tracked bag
- Stored location history to review past movements and routes
- Compact and portable form factor suitable for placement inside luggage or personal bags
- Integrated mobile app access for direct manufacturer app tracking on iOS and Android
- Web platform access for remote review of current position and history
- Compatibility with Plaspy for centralized tracking and device management

## Core Features of ThinkRace - AT15

- Live position updates that support near real-time location awareness
- Location history logging so past positions can be reviewed
- Small, travel-focused design intended for luggage and personal items
- Native mobile app integration for on-device setup and monitoring via iOS and Android
- Web platform access provided by the manufacturer for remote tracking
- Interoperability with third-party tracking platforms such as Plaspy
- Simple user-oriented setup intended for consumer and travel scenarios

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the AT15 is connected into Plaspy, its core tracking functions are reflected in the platform to provide centralized visibility and operational oversight. Plaspy presents location updates and history alongside other devices for easier monitoring of distributed assets.

- Current position for each AT15 shown on Plaspy maps and device lists
- Recorded location history accessible in Plaspy to review prior movements
- Device status and last reported time visible in the Plaspy dashboard
- Grouping and inventory management of AT15 units within Plaspy for fleets of luggage or tagged items
- Use of Plaspy reporting and export features to include AT15 location history in operational workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Tracking checked bags and carry-on luggage during travel to monitor location and route
- Monitoring personal bags and briefcases for peace of mind while commuting or on business trips
- Managing fleets of tagged luggage for rental or concierge services
- Reviewing the travel history of a bag after a trip to resolve loss or transfer questions
- Consolidating multiple trackers into a single Plaspy view for operational oversight

## Feature Availability Notes

- Regional hardware variants or carrier configurations may change connectivity characteristics or supported services
- The manufacturer mobile app and web platform may provide features that differ from what is available through Plaspy

Using the ThinkRace AT15 together with Plaspy provides a centralized way to monitor multiple trackers alongside other asset types. Plaspy aggregates location updates and history so operators and travelers can maintain situational awareness, review journeys, and include tracked items in broader reporting and operational views.
