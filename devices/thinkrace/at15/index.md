# ThinkRace - AT15

![AT15](./logo.jpg)

ThinkRace Technology presents the AT15, a compact and versatile smart luggage tracker designed to keep your belongings safe and secure. Whether you're traveling for business or pleasure, the AT15 is the perfect companion for all types of luggage, including backpacks, suitcases, and briefcases. With real-time tracking and location history, you can have peace of mind knowing that your belongings are always within reach.

With the AT15, you can easily access your bag's location through our integrated mobile app, available for both iOS and Android devices. Simply download the app and connect to your tracker to start tracking your luggage in real-time. You can also access the location information through our web platform, making it convenient to keep an eye on your belongings from anywhere, at any time.

Key Features:

- Real-time tracking: Keep track of your luggage's location in real-time.
- Location history: Access the history of your bag's locations to track its journey.
- Compact and portable: The AT15 is designed to fit seamlessly into any type of luggage.
- Integrated mobile app: Use our user-friendly mobile app to easily track your luggage.
- Web platform access: Access your bag's location information through our web platform.
