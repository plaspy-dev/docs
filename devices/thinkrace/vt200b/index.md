# ThinkRace - VT200B

![VT200B](./logo.jpg)

The ThinkRace VT200B is an OBD2 GPS tracker built to deliver vehicle diagnostics together with continuous location tracking. Designed for plug and play use via the vehicle OBD2 port, the VT200B provides diagnostic reporting such as fuel consumption and engine condition while also supporting real time tracking and over speed alerts. Its feature set makes it useful for both individual vehicle owners and operators who need frequent visibility into vehicle health and movement.

As a model compatible with Plaspy, the VT200B can feed diagnostic and tracking information into Plaspy's fleet monitoring environment to improve visibility and operational oversight. Integrating VT200B data into Plaspy helps combine vehicle status reporting with mapping, alerts, and consolidated reporting so fleets and managers can monitor efficiency and safety from a single platform.

## Key Highlights

- OBD2 based plug and play tracker that connects via the vehicle OBD2 port
- Provides vehicle diagnostics including fuel consumption and engine health reporting
- Real time location tracking for continuous visibility of vehicle position
- Over speed alerting to support driver safety and policy enforcement
- Simple compatibility with vehicles manufactured after 1996
- Useful for both single vehicle monitoring and fleet oversight
- Designed for straightforward user experience and routine diagnostics review

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT200B supplies vehicle diagnostics and location updates into Plaspy's monitoring and reporting environment so operators can view and act on both status and movement data in one place.

- Display real time vehicle location on Plaspy maps for tracking and dispatch
- Surface diagnostics summaries such as fuel consumption and engine condition within Plaspy reports
- Configure alerts in Plaspy for events like over speed notifications to support safety workflows
- Use Plaspy reporting to analyze historical data for maintenance planning and operational trends
- Combine VT200B data with other assets in Plaspy for centralized fleet performance oversight
