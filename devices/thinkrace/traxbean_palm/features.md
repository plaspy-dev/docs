# ThinkRace - Traxbean Palm Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused summary of the ThinkRace Traxbean Palm and how its capabilities map to monitoring and incident workflows when used with Plaspy. It is meant to explain practical functions and operational value for teams using Plaspy for personnel safety and situational awareness.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation choices. Where details are implementation dependent, this page stays at a product level and encourages verification against manufacturer documentation and the deployed device firmware.

## Feature Overview

The Traxbean Palm is a compact handheld tracker that combines multiple positioning methods, voice and visual tools, and connectivity to support rapid response and ongoing monitoring. When paired with Plaspy, the Palm provides location and event context to operations teams and designated responders.

- Multi mode positioning with GPS plus assisted locating using Wi Fi and cellular triangulation to improve fix times and availability in dense or urban areas.
- RF based indoor positioning to help with locating users where GPS reception is poor.
- One press SOS that initiates a two way hands free voice call and can trigger automatic camera capture for visual context.
- Integrated camera capable of remote photo and video capture or streaming on demand and during emergency events.
- 4G LTE connectivity with audible siren, audio alerts and vibration to support user safety and attention signaling.
- SDK and Open API options plus ThinkRace platform support for integration and device management.

## Core Features of ThinkRace - Traxbean Palm

- Compact handheld form factor intended for personnel safety and situational awareness.
- GPS positioning combined with assisted Wi Fi and cellular triangulation for improved location availability.
- RF based indoor positioning to extend locating capability inside buildings or covered areas.
- One press SOS button with immediate two way hands free voice call for emergency communication.
- High resolution camera with remote photo and video capture and streaming capability.
- Audible siren, audio alerts and vibration for local alarms and operator notification.
- SDK and Open API availability and support from ThinkRace for integration and remote management.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location fixes, assisted positioning signals, and SOS events from the Traxbean Palm so control centers and responders can see near real time status and incident context. Plaspy can surface visual and audio event data alongside location history to improve situational decisions and escalation.

- Real time location display in Plaspy using GPS fixes and assisted Wi Fi and cellular positions to improve availability in urban and indoor settings.
- SOS events and two way voice indicators appear in incident streams so operators can prioritize and action alarms.
- Remote camera captures and streaming flagged in Plaspy incident records for visual confirmation during emergencies.
- Alerts and notifications based on device events can be routed through Plaspy workflows to designated contacts or response teams.
- Data from the Palm can be combined with other device types in Plaspy to provide unified operational views across personnel and vehicle assets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Lone worker protection where instant SOS, voice and visual context improve responder decisions.
- Field staff location and oversight for teams operating in urban, indoor, or mixed coverage environments.
- Security patrol monitoring with photo verification of incidents and coordinated response through a central dashboard.
- Caregiver and family safety monitoring for vulnerable individuals who need rapid escalation and visual confirmation.
- Incident response and verification where audio and video from the device reduce uncertainty during alarms.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some functions depend on device firmware and may change with firmware updates or manufacturer revisions.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect radio bands, indoor locating performance, and camera capabilities.
- Certain vehicle focused telemetry functions such as ignition or fuel monitoring are not native to this handheld device; Plaspy can still present Palm data alongside vehicle trackers that provide those signals.
- Integration features such as SDK and Open API availability may require credentials or licensing from ThinkRace and appropriate configuration.
- Installation environment and mounting or carrying method will influence RF and GPS performance for assisted locating and indoor positioning.
