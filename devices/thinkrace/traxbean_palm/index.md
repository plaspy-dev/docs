# ThinkRace - Traxbean Palm

![Traxbean Palm](./tracker.png)

The Traxbean Palm is a compact handheld GPS tracker designed for personnel safety and situational awareness. It combines precise GPS positioning with assisted locating via Wi‑Fi and cellular triangulation and adds RF based indoor positioning to improve location availability where GPS is limited. The Palm also includes a one press SOS button that initiates two way hands free voice communication and a high resolution camera capable of streaming or capturing photos and video for situational context.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the Traxbean Palm provides the kinds of real time location updates and event signals that operations teams rely on. Its SOS, audio and visual features, plus SDK and Open API options and ThinkRace platform support, make it a practical choice for organizations that want to integrate people centric tracking and incident context into Plaspy monitoring workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact handheld design focused on personnel safety and situational awareness.
- Multi mode positioning with GPS plus Wi‑Fi and cellular assisted locating and RF based indoor positioning for improved availability.
- One press SOS with immediate two way hands free voice to provide rapid incident notification.
- High resolution camera for remote streaming or on demand photo and video capture to support visual verification.
- 4G/LTE connectivity for continuous updates, push alerts and low latency reporting.
- Integrated audible siren, audio alerts and vibration to support rapid response and user safety.
- SDK and Open API plus ThinkRace platform support to facilitate integration and management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy the Traxbean Palm streams positional fixes and event data over cellular connectivity so control centers and responders receive near real time tracking and incident updates. Plaspy ingests location data, SOS events and media from the device, and then applies rules and notification workflows to route alerts and visual context to the right teams.

- Real time location and telemetry updates combining GPS with assisted Wi‑Fi and cellular positions for better coverage in urban and indoor areas.
- SOS and two way voice handling so emergency calls and event flags appear in Plaspy incident streams for immediate attention.
- Visual verification through remote photo and video capture that helps responders assess incidents faster and with more context.
- Geofence alerts and zone based notifications to trigger automated workflows and entry or exit reporting.
- Integration and automation using the Palm’s SDK and Open API to build custom dashboards, incident workflows and combined views with vehicle trackers or other assets.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elderly emergency tracking and caregiver monitoring with one press SOS and live audio visual context.
- Lone worker protection for field staff who need continuous monitoring and fast incident escalation.
- Security patrol verification where photos and video confirm incidents and patrol routes are monitored centrally.
- Field service operations that benefit from location visibility and rapid remote assessment during incidents.
- Public safety or rapid response teams that require visual confirmation and two way communication alongside location updates.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Traxbean Palm is well suited to organizations that need people centric monitoring rather than vehicle focused telemetry. Its combination of real time positioning, immediate SOS voice, and camera based verification makes it useful for teams that must act quickly with strong situational context. Because the Palm is Plaspy compatible and provides SDK and Open API options, it can be integrated into existing Plaspy workflows to deliver alerts, historical telemetry and media into operational dashboards.

If your priority is dependable field communications, rapid emergency escalation and straightforward integration into a monitoring platform, the Traxbean Palm is a practical option to consider for use with Plaspy.
