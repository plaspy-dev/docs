# ThinkRace - Traxbean Palm

![Traxbean Palm](./tracker.png)

The Traxbean Palm is a compact, handheld GPS tracker designed for personnel safety and situational awareness. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the Palm brings precise GPS positioning together with assisted locating via Wi‑Fi and cellular triangulation and RF-based indoor positioning to deliver reliable real-time tracking and clear incident context to family members, control centers, or operations teams.

The Palm is built for fast, informed response: a one-press SOS button establishes an immediate two-way hands-free voice call, and a high-resolution camera can stream or capture photos and video on demand or automatically when an SOS is triggered. With 4G/LTE connectivity, audio alerts, siren and vibration alarms, plus SDK/Open API options and ThinkRace platform support, the Traxbean Palm is a practical, lower-cost monitoring device for services that require rapid, visual confirmation and continuous telemetry.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible handheld GPS tracker for clear, reliable real-time tracking of people and field staff.
- Multi-mode positioning: GPS positioning plus Wi‑Fi and cellular triangulation for faster fixes and RF indoor locating where GPS is weak.
- Instant SOS with two‑way hands-free voice and automatic camera capture to deliver audio and visual context to emergency contacts and control centers.
- 4G/LTE connectivity for continuous updates, push alerts, and low-latency telemetry in the field.
- Integrated audible siren, audio alerts and vibration to increase user safety and support rapid intervention.
- SDK and Open API for easy integration into Plaspy workflows, enterprise dashboards and custom apps.
- Supported by ThinkRace web platform and native Android/iOS apps for monitoring, configuration and device management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When registered in Plaspy, the Traxbean Palm streams positional and event data over 4G/LTE so operations teams get near real-time tracking and incident updates. Plaspy ingests GPS fixes, assisted location signals and SOS events, then uses rules, geofences and notification workflows to route alerts and visual data to designated responders. Integration is streamlined via the Palm’s SDK and Open API support, enabling custom telemetry dashboards, automated incident workflows and secure remote management.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates — GPS fixes plus Wi‑Fi and cellular-assisted positions for improved availability in urban and indoor environments.
- Two-way voice and SOS event handling — immediate hands-free calls and event flags routed into Plaspy incident streams.
- Visual verification — remote photo/video capture from the device camera for faster, better-informed decisions during emergencies.
- Geofencing inclusion/exclusion — entry/exit alerts sent to Plaspy for zone-based monitoring and automated notifications.
- Ignition/immobilizer/fuel monitoring and Bluetooth sensors — these vehicle-focused telemetry features are not native to this handheld device; however, Plaspy can combine Palm data with vehicle trackers that supply ignition, immobilizer and fuel monitoring to provide a unified operations view.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Traxbean Palm is a purpose-built GPS tracker that pairs well with Plaspy when organizations need people-centric monitoring rather than vehicle-focused telemetry. It brings real-time tracking, visual verification and voice communications into a single handheld unit that’s easy to deploy and manage through Plaspy’s platform. For operations that already use Plaspy for fleet management or vehicle telemetry \(ignition, immobilizer, fuel monitoring\), the Palm complements those capabilities by adding reliable personnel safety data and visual context without replacing vehicle trackers.

Choose the Palm with Plaspy when your priority is dependable field communications, rapid emergency escalation and straightforward integration. The bundled SDK/Open API and ThinkRace platform support reduce time-to-deploy, while Plaspy’s workflows turn raw GPS tracker data into actionable alerts, historical telemetry and scalable monitoring across mixed fleets of vehicles and handheld devices.
