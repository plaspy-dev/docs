# ThinkRace - PT880 Features

This page provides a public overview of the ThinkRace Traxbean PT880 wrist tracker and the practical feature context for using this device with Plaspy. It describes the tracker capabilities that are relevant to real time supervision, location visibility, alerting, and integration into Plaspy workflows without reproducing sensitive configuration or proprietary implementation details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Planners and integrators should treat the descriptions below as high level guidance and verify device behavior against current manufacturer documentation and test devices before deploying into production.

## Feature Overview

The PT880 is a purpose built wrist wearable designed for continuous supervision programs that need persistent location awareness, wearer alerts, and tamper protection. Its positioning suite and event reporting are intended to improve location availability and escalate incidents into monitoring platforms such as Plaspy for operational oversight.

- Wrist worn form factor with durable, tamper resistant strap and water resistant construction for everyday wear.
- Multi mode positioning combining GPS with assisted Wi Fi, cellular triangulation, and RF based indoor fixes to improve availability in challenging environments.
- Tamper detection and removal alarms that notify supervisors and generate event records for audit and response.
- One press SOS panic activation combined with two way voice connectivity and multiple local alerts such as siren and vibration.
- Portable recharging and field ready design to reduce downtime and support continuous monitoring.

## Core Features of ThinkRace - PT880

- Continuous GPS positioning augmented by assisted Wi Fi and cellular triangulation for improved outdoor and near building coverage.
- RF based indoor positioning to increase location availability inside structures where GNSS alone may be limited.
- Tamper proof and cut resistant strap design plus tamper alarm events that signal potential removal or compromise.
- One press SOS panic button that initiates a prioritized emergency event and offers two way voice connectivity.
- Audible siren and vibration alerts on the device for local incident signaling and wearer notification.
- Portable recharging capability to support regular field operation and reduce supervision gaps.
- SDK and open API support for integration into back office systems and custom workflows.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the PT880 streams location and event information into supervisory dashboards, alerts, and reporting tools so teams can monitor devices in real time and review historical activity. Plaspy ingests position fixes, tamper events, SOS activations, and other supported signals to present actionable telemetry to supervisors.

- Real time location traces and periodic updates displayed in Plaspy maps and playback tools for operational awareness.
- Tamper and removal alarms forwarded to Plaspy so supervisors receive immediate notifications and incident logs.
- SOS or panic activations routed as prioritized events with associated two way voice records or links where supported.
- Indoor assisted fixes and assisted Wi Fi or cellular based updates improve location continuity shown in the Plaspy interface.
- Event logs and audit records stored for later review and reporting to support case management or compliance needs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Community corrections electronic monitoring with continuous position reporting, tamper alerts, and geo event oversight.
- Offender supervision with SOS panic monitoring and two way voice for rapid incident response and welfare checks.
- Workforce protection in distributed or remote teams where discreet wearable alerts and location awareness are required.
- Case management programs that require auditable event logs, tamper history, and location traces for review.
- Situations where indoor positioning support is important to maintain coverage inside buildings or covered areas.
