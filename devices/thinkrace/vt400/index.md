# ThinkRace - VT400

<div className="device-logo">
![VT400](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The ThinkRace VT400 is a compact OBD WiFi GPS tracker designed for cars. Intended to be used with a vehicle OBD II port, the VT400 provides continuous location tracking, history playback, geofence alerts, and trip reporting. Its 4G connectivity and built in WiFi support enable frequent position updates and route review capabilities suitable for individual vehicle monitoring or fleet operations.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the VT400 can feed location and vehicle data into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and reporting. That compatibility means fleet managers and vehicle owners can view VT400 tracked vehicles alongside other devices in Plaspy, receive configurable alerts, and include VT400 data in operational reports without requiring separate systems.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for car use with OBD II connectivity for straightforward power and vehicle data access.
- 4G network support for reliable real time location updates and responsive tracking.
- History playback to review past routes and examine trip details over time.
- Geo fence functionality to set virtual boundaries and receive notifications on crossing events.
- Trip reports that summarize distance traveled, average speed, and trip duration for operational insight.
- Multi alarm features for events such as overspeeding, harsh braking, and ignition on or off.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the VT400 delivers vehicle location and trip information into a single fleet management interface so teams can monitor assets, set alerts, and generate reports. Integration focuses on visibility and operational oversight rather than low level configuration details.

- Centralized live location display within Plaspy for fleet wide situational awareness.
- Configurable geo fence alerts and alarm notifications routed through Plaspy to relevant users.
- Trip and historical playback accessible from Plaspy reports to review routes and driver behavior.
- Aggregated trip reports and summaries to support mileage tracking and operational analysis.
- Vehicle health and OBD II data available alongside location records for combined monitoring.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet managers tracking cars for route optimization and driver oversight.
- Car owners and small fleet operators monitoring vehicle location and security.
- Operations teams using trip reports to reconcile mileage and trip duration.
- Managers setting geo fences to protect assets or control vehicle movement.
- Maintenance planners reviewing OBD II data trends in conjunction with vehicle usage.
