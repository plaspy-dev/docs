# ThinkRace - VT400

![VT400](./logo.jpg)

The ThinkRace VT400 is a cutting-edge OBD WiFi GPS tracker designed specifically for cars. With its sleek and compact design, this tracker can easily be plugged into the OBD II port of your vehicle, providing you with real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities. 

One of the standout features of the VT400 is its support for 4G network connectivity. This ensures that you have access to high accuracy location tracking, allowing you to keep a close eye on the whereabouts of your vehicle at all times. Additionally, the tracker offers history playback, allowing you to review past routes and locations.

The VT400 also comes equipped with a Geo-fence feature, which allows you to set up virtual boundaries for your vehicle. If the vehicle crosses these boundaries, you will receive an instant notification, ensuring that you are always aware of its movements. 

Another useful feature of the VT400 is its ability to provide various trip reports. This includes information such as distance traveled, average speed, and duration of the trip. This can be particularly useful for fleet management or for keeping track of personal trips.

In addition to tracking and monitoring, the VT400 also provides OBD II real-time data. This means that you can access information about the health and performance of your vehicle, such as engine RPM, fuel consumption, and battery voltage. This can help you identify any potential issues with your vehicle and take appropriate action.

With its multi-alarm functionality, the VT400 ensures that you are alerted to any unusual or suspicious activities involving your vehicle. This includes alarms for overspeeding, harsh braking, and unauthorized ignition on/off. These alarms can help you protect your vehicle from theft or misuse.

Overall, the ThinkRace VT400 OBD WiFi GPS tracker is a powerful and versatile device that offers a range of features to enhance your driving experience. Whether you are a fleet manager looking to optimize your operations or a car owner wanting to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle, the VT400 is an excellent choice.
