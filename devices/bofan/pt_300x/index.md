# Bofan - PT-300X

![PT-300X](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-300X is a compact GPS vehicle tracker designed to provide reliable location monitoring and core alerting features for vehicles. It supports tracking via SMS and GPRS, offers configurable time interval reporting for movement history, and includes practical alerts such as low battery, overspeed, and geo-fence notifications. The device also provides an SOS button for emergency signalling and supports stop car functionality through an external relay for vehicle immobilization when required.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the PT-300X can feed its location updates and alerts into the Plaspy platform for consolidated fleet visibility and reporting. That compatibility lets fleet managers and administrators view live locations, review historical routes, receive alarm notifications, and combine device reports with Plaspy’s monitoring features to improve operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Real time location reporting via SMS or GPRS for continuous tracking
- Configurable time interval reporting to build detailed movement history
- Overspeed alerting to notify when a preset speed threshold is exceeded
- Geo-fencing controls to detect entering or exiting predefined areas
- Low battery alert to help maintain device uptime and availability
- SOS button for immediate alerting to predefined contacts
- Support for stop car functionality via an external relay for remote immobilization

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PT-300X sends periodic location updates and event alerts that Plaspy ingests and presents on its platform. Plaspy organizes those messages into map views, event logs, and reports so teams can monitor vehicles and respond to incidents from a single interface.

- Live location and recent position updates displayed on Plaspy maps
- Alerts such as overspeed, geofence breaches, low battery, and SOS surfaced as notifications and event logs
- Historical route reconstruction based on the tracker’s time interval reports for trip review and auditing
- Fleet monitoring dashboards to track vehicle availability and recent activity
- Reporting tools that consolidate device events and positions for compliance or operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Commercial fleet monitoring for delivery vans, service vehicles, and field crews
- Rental or leased vehicle tracking to maintain location history and enforce boundaries
- Security oversight for high value or sensitive assets requiring geofence alerts and SOS signalling
- Driver oversight where overspeed alerts help enforce safe driving policies
- Remote immobilization scenarios where stop car functionality adds an extra layer of theft response

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT-300X pairs a practical set of vehicle tracking features with straightforward reporting options, making it a sensible choice for organizations that need dependable location data and basic alerting. When integrated with Plaspy, those device capabilities become part of a broader monitoring environment where managers can correlate device events, visualize routes, and maintain operational records.

While the PT-300X covers essential fleet tracking needs, Plaspy enhances its value by centralizing alerts and historical data for teams that require consolidated oversight. For organizations seeking a compact tracker that can be monitored and reported on through Plaspy, the PT-300X presents a balanced option between simplicity and functional control.
