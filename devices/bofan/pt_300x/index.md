# Bofan - PT-300X

![PT-300X](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-300X GPS Vehicle Tracker is a reliable and versatile device that offers a range of features to help you keep track of your vehicles. With the ability to track via SMS or GPRS \(TCP/UDP\), you can easily monitor the location of your vehicle in real-time. The tracker provides accurate and up-to-date location reports, allowing you to stay informed about the whereabouts of your vehicle at all times.

One of the standout features of the PT-300X is its ability to track by time interval. This means that you can set the tracker to automatically send location updates at specific time intervals, giving you a detailed history of your vehicle's movements. Additionally, the tracker offers a low battery alert, ensuring that you are notified when the battery is running low and needs to be recharged.

Another useful feature of the PT-300X is its overspeed alert. You can set a specific speed limit, and if your vehicle exceeds this limit, you will receive an alert, allowing you to take immediate action. The tracker also offers geo-fencing control, which allows you to set up virtual boundaries for your vehicle. If the vehicle enters or exits these boundaries, you will be notified, providing an extra layer of security and control.

The PT-300X also includes an SOS button, which can be used in case of emergencies. With just a press of a button, you can send an alert to a pre-defined contact, ensuring that help is on the way when you need it most. Additionally, the tracker supports stop car functionality via an extra relay, giving you the ability to remotely immobilize your vehicle if necessary.

With its comprehensive range of features and reliable performance, the Bofan PT-300X GPS Vehicle Tracker is an excellent choice for anyone looking to enhance the security and monitoring of their vehicles. Whether you need to keep track of a single vehicle or manage a fleet, this tracker offers the functionality and convenience you need.
