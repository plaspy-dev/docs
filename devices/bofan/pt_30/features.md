# Bofan - PT-30 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Bofan PT-30 and explains how its capabilities are relevant when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on the PT-30's user-facing functions such as location reporting via SMS or GPRS, SOS alerts, geo-fencing, and how those functions are typically represented within a fleet and asset visibility platform like Plaspy.

Exact feature availability for any individual PT-30 unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's configuration. The descriptions on this page are based on public device information and are intended as general guidance for use with Plaspy rather than an exhaustive technical specification.

## Feature Overview

The Bofan PT-30 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for discreet tracking of people and assets. It combines GPS positioning with GSM/GPRS communications to provide location updates and alarms that can be delivered via SMS or GPRS, enabling both direct phone-based interactions and online tracking through platforms such as Plaspy.

- Compact, easy to conceal, suitable for personal or small asset tracking.
- GPS positioning with high sensitivity for improved fixes in challenging reception areas.
- Dual reporting options: position and alerts sent via SMS or over GPRS for online monitoring.
- SOS button and preset emergency contact support for rapid alerting.
- Geo-fence setup and boundary crossing alerts configurable via SMS commands.
- Real-time online tracking supported when the device is connected via GPRS.

## Core Features of Bofan - PT-30

- Compact form factor for discreet placement and transport.
- GPS positioning with high sensitivity thanks to the SiRF-Star III chipset included in public device descriptions.
- GSM/GPRS cellular communications supporting wide frequency coverage across common bands.
- Position reporting available via SMS commands or GPRS-based uploads.
- SOS emergency button that can trigger alerts to pre-set contacts.
- Geo-fence capability allowing preset or customized boundary monitoring and alerts.
- Two-way communication via SMS with preconfigured numbers for emergency or command responses.
- Low power alarm reported by SMS or GPRS when supported by the device firmware.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a PT-30 is used with Plaspy, the platform receives and interprets the device's GPRS position reports and event messages so organizations can monitor location and events in one place. SMS-based commands and direct phone calls remain available for phone-to-device interactions, while Plaspy provides online visibility when the tracker is configured to send data over GPRS.

- Real-time locations and movement history appear on Plaspy maps when the PT-30 sends GPRS updates.
- SOS events and boundary crossing messages can be displayed as alerts or events within Plaspy when those reports are sent to the platform.
- Geo-fence configurations set on the device produce boundary alerts that help track compliance or unauthorized movement when reported.
- Low battery or low power alerts sent by the tracker can be surfaced in Plaspy as notifications if the device forwards those reports.
- SMS-only interactions remain a device-level function; Plaspy reflects online reports received through GPRS while phone-based SMS usage continues outside the platform.

## Typical Use Cases

- Discreet personal tracking for lone workers or family members who require location awareness.
- Small high-value asset monitoring where a compact device is needed.
- Emergency response scenarios using the SOS button to notify preconfigured contacts and generate platform alerts.
- Short-term or temporary deployments where easy concealment and simple SMS controls are beneficial.
- Urban or dense environment tracking where high GPS sensitivity improves position fixes.
