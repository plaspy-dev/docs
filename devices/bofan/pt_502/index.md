# Bofan - PT-502

<div className="device-logo">
![PT-502](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Bofan PT-502 is a GPS car tracker designed for real time vehicle tracking and monitoring. It combines satellite positioning with cellular reporting to provide location updates, historical position logging, and a suite of alarm features. Notable functions described for the PT-502 include remote engine cut capability, support for tracking via SMS and GPRS, large onboard position logging capacity, and multiple alarm types such as SOS, geofence, low power, and speed alerts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT-502 can feed vehicle location and alert data into Plaspy for centralised visibility and operational oversight. That compatibility makes the PT-502 relevant for organisations and fleet operators who want to combine the tracker hardware features with Plaspy’s mapping, alerting, and reporting tools to manage vehicles, respond to incidents, and review historical movement data.

## Key Highlights

- Real time tracking available by SMS or GPRS for flexible reporting
- Remote engine cut function for vehicle immobilisation when needed
- Large position log capacity of 7000 plus waypoints for extended history
- SOS alarm with up to three preset emergency contacts for rapid alerts
- Geofence support plus configurable speed and low power alerts
- Glonass and GPS positioning with the ability to request street addresses and Google links by SMS

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the PT-502 delivers location updates and alarm events that Plaspy displays on maps and in operational dashboards, enabling centralized monitoring and decision making. Integration lets teams combine the tracker’s on device features with Plaspy’s visibility, notification, and reporting capabilities to run vehicle operations more effectively.

- Live location and movement tracking shown on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility
- Alarm events such as SOS triggers, geofence breaches, speed alerts, and low power notices routed into Plaspy for notification and response
- Historical position logs and trip data available in Plaspy for replay and operational review
- Remote immobilisation (engine cut) can be managed through platform controls where supported to assist in theft response
- Custom reporting and mileage summaries produced in Plaspy using data from the device

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for small to medium commercial vehicle operations
- Anti theft monitoring and rapid response for private cars and company vehicles
- Rental vehicle oversight including mileage and movement history
- Delivery and service vehicle tracking for route visibility and proof of movement
- Field workforce monitoring where SOS and remote alerting are valuable

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT-502 is a practical choice when paired with Plaspy because it offers a mix of real time reporting, extensive position logging, and alarm features that align with common fleet and vehicle security needs. Organisations that value historical route data, remote immobilisation capability, and configurable alerts can combine those device features with Plaspy’s visualization and reporting tools to improve oversight and response.
