# Bofan - PT-502

![PT-502](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-502 GPS Car Tracker is a reliable and versatile device designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring for vehicles. With its advanced features and capabilities, it offers enhanced security and control over your vehicle's whereabouts. 

One of the standout features of the PT-502 is its ability to cut the engine remotely. This means that in case of theft or unauthorized use, you can disable the engine with just a few clicks, ensuring that your vehicle remains safe and secure. 

The tracker supports tracking via SMS and GPRS, allowing you to monitor your vehicle's location and movements in real-time. You can receive current location reports, track by time intervals, and even log positions with a capacity of up to 7000+ waypoints. 

In addition to its tracking capabilities, the PT-502 also offers various alarm features for added security. It has an SOS alarm function that allows you to preset three emergency numbers, ensuring that help is just a button press away. It also supports geo-fence control, low power alerts, and speed alarms to keep you informed and in control at all times. 

With its Glonass + GPS technology, the PT-502 provides accurate and reliable positioning, ensuring that you always have the most up-to-date information about your vehicle's location. It also has the ability to retrieve street addresses and Google links for the current location via SMS requests. 

Overall, the Bofan PT-502 GPS Car Tracker is a powerful and feature-packed device that offers comprehensive tracking and monitoring capabilities for your vehicle. Whether you're concerned about security, fleet management, or simply want to keep an eye on your vehicle's whereabouts, the PT-502 is an excellent choice.

### Key Features:

- Tracking via SMS or live tracking by GPRS
- Current location report
- Tracking by time interval
- Position logging capacity up to 7000+ waypoints
- SOS alarm \(available to preset 3 SOS numbers\)
- Geo-fence control
- Low power alert
- Speed alarm
- Engine cut \(optional\)
- Get street address by SMS request
- Get Google link for current location by SMS request
- Save 50% GPRS cost above in different methods when working on FMS \(BOFAN Smart Report Tech, ACC, Mileage report\)
- Available to set different alarm notification methods when alarm \(sending alarm SMS, calling or platform\)
- Mileage and GPRS report by distance
- I/O: 2 digital inputs, 1 negative and 1 positive triggering; 1 output
- Analog Input: 1 10 bits resolution analog input
