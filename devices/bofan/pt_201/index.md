# Bofan - PT-201

![PT-201](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-201 is a GPS/GSM/GPRS tracking device specifically designed for motorcycle real-time tracking and security. With its superior GPS and GPRS modules, the PT-201 offers excellent sensitivity and stable performance. It is especially designed to work seamlessly with LiveGTS, a web-based tracking system that allows companies to establish their own tracking platform and provide real-time tracking services to their customers or manage their fleet.

The PT-201 offers a range of features to ensure accurate and reliable tracking. Users can track their motorcycles via SMS or live tracking through GPRS. The device provides current location reports and supports tracking by time interval. With a position logging capacity of up to 7000+ waypoints, users can easily review the travel history of their motorcycles. The PT-201 also includes an SOS panic feature, allowing users to preset three SOS numbers for emergency situations. Additionally, the device is waterproof with an IP66 rating, ensuring its durability in various weather conditions.

Some other notable features of the Bofan PT-201 include geo-fence control, low power alert, speed alarm, engine cut \(optional\), and the ability to request street addresses and Google links for the current location via SMS. The device also incorporates BOFAN Smart Report Tech, which helps save up to 50% of GPRS costs through various methods such as ACC, mileage report, and motion sensor. It also offers different alarm notification methods and provides vehicle maintenance reminders based on mileage reports. With 2 digital inputs, 1 negative and 1 positive triggering, and 1 output, as well as 1 10-bit resolution analog input, the PT-201 offers versatile connectivity options.
