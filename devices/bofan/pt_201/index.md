# Bofan - PT-201

![PT-201](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-201 is a compact GPS GSM GPRS tracker designed for motorcycle real time tracking and security. Built around higher sensitivity GPS and GPRS modules, the PT-201 is intended to provide stable location reporting, support live tracking and SMS queries, and store long term travel history with capacity for thousands of waypoints. It also includes practical features for two wheeled vehicles such as an SOS panic function, geo fence control, speed alarm, and an IP66 waterproof rating for weather resistance.

This model is relevant as a Plaspy compatible device because its live tracking, SMS reporting, and waypoint logging align with the kinds of data Plaspy consumes for fleet visibility and operational oversight. When integrated with Plaspy, the PT-201 can feed real time locations, alerts, and historical tracks into a centralized platform so fleet managers, service providers, and operators can monitor motorcycles and respond to events from a single interface.

## Key Highlights

- Designed specifically for motorcycle real time tracking and security
- High sensitivity GPS and GPRS modules for stable location reporting
- Live tracking via GPRS plus SMS query support for immediate updates
- Large position logging capacity of over 7000 waypoints for history review
- Built in SOS panic functionality with three preset emergency numbers
- IP66 waterproof rating for reliable outdoor use in varied weather
- Geo fence, speed alarm, low power alert, and optional engine cut feature

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PT-201 can be integrated into Plaspy to provide continuous visibility of motorcycle assets and to trigger alerts and reports that support operational decision making. Plaspy can ingest position updates and alarms from the device and present them alongside other fleet data for unified monitoring.

- Display live positions and movement on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring
- Store and review travel history using the device waypoint logs within Plaspy
- Configure geofence alerts and speed notifications in Plaspy to match device events
- Surface SOS and other alarm notifications to operations teams through Plaspy
- Use Plaspy reporting tools to analyze mileage and activity patterns reported by the tracker

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring motorcycle delivery fleets for route visibility and punctuality
- Security and recovery tracking for individual motorcycles or small fleets
- Management of ride services and motorcycle rental fleets with location history
- Tracking for field technicians or couriers who use motorcycles for local service
- Oversight of asset movement and automated alerts for incidents or boundary breaches
