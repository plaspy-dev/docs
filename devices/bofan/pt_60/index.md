# Bofan - PT-60

![PT-60](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-60 is a compact and versatile personal GPS/GSM/GPRS tracker that offers a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your loved ones and valuable assets. With its built-in GPS and GSM/GPRS technology, this tracker provides real-time locating and tracking capabilities, allowing you to easily monitor the location of the device. Whether you want to protect your vehicle, heavy equipment, child, elderly, disabled, or pet, or manage your personnel, the PT-60 is an ideal solution.

One of the standout features of the PT-60 is its geo-fence function, which allows you to set up restricted areas. If the tracker breaches these areas, it will send an alert to the authorized cell phone numbers, ensuring that you are immediately notified of any unauthorized movement. Additionally, the PT-60 features an SOS function, which can be activated by pressing the SOS button. This will send a help alert along with the exact location to the authorized cell phone numbers, enabling quick and efficient rescue.

The PT-60 also offers other useful features such as excess speed alarm, movement alarm, and low-battery alarm. The excess speed alarm will notify you if the tracker exceeds a certain speed limit, while the movement alarm will alert you if the device is moved. The low-battery alarm ensures that you are aware when the tracker's battery is running low, so you can recharge it in a timely manner. Additionally, the PT-60 supports two-way talking communication, allowing you to communicate with the tracking target just like a cell phone, and even spy on the sound around the GPS tracker in a 3-meter radius.

With its compact size and advanced features, the Bofan PT-60 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that offers peace of mind and enhanced security. Whether you need to track vehicles, protect loved ones, or manage personnel, the PT-60 is a powerful tool that can meet your needs.

- Excess speed alarm
