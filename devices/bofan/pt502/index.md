# PT502 GPS Tracker

The PT502 is a 4G GPS tracker built for professional fleet management and vehicle security. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the PT502 delivers reliable real-time tracking, comprehensive telemetry and robust alarm handling so fleet operators can monitor vehicles, drivers and critical signals through Plaspy's online platform, mobile app or SMS.

Designed for demanding vehicle environments, the PT502 supports advanced features such as driver identification via RFID, ultrasonic fuel sensing for precise fuel monitoring, multi-camera photo capture and remote immobilizer controls. These capabilities make it a strong choice for fleets focused on anti-theft protection, trip replay, maintenance reminders and driver behaviour analysis when integrated with Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible 4G GPS tracker for real-time tracking and fleet management.
- Supports up to four external cameras for automatic photo capture on alarms and events.
- Driver identification with RFID for secure driver-to-vehicle association and reporting.
- Ultrasonic fuel sensing support for accurate fuel monitoring and discrepancy alerts.
- Remote engine shutdown and speed limitation via external relays \(remote immobilizer functionality\).
- Flexible alarm suite: SOS, geo-fence, speed, ACC on/off, power cut and hard driving event alarms.
- 2 digital inputs \(one negative, one positive-triggering\) and 2 outputs for relays, buzzers or sirens.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PT502 sends position updates and telemetry to Plaspy using configurable triggers so you get the right data at the right moment. Plaspy aggregates location, sensor and event data into live maps, alerting rules and historical reports for effective fleet operations, anti-theft response and compliance monitoring.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy \(via 4G/SMS/app/FMS integration\).
- ACC/ignition status reporting for accurate engine-on/off tracking and trip segmentation.
- Ultrasonic fuel monitoring data for fuel-level reports and fuel-sensor-triggered alerts.
- Remote immobilizer and speed-limiting control through Plaspy command functions using the device's outputs and relays.
- Automatic camera photo capture and event images sent with alarm events for visual verification.

## Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and immobilization — remotely disable or limit a vehicle via Plaspy when theft or unauthorized use is detected.
- Driver identification and behaviour monitoring — use RFID plus Plaspy reporting to track who is driving and generate fatigue or harsh driving alerts.
- Fuel management optimization — integrate ultrasonic fuel sensors to detect fuel theft, measure consumption and reconcile telemetry with trip data.
- Event-driven photo capture and evidence collection — automatic camera photos on alarms \(e.g., collision, ignition events or geo-fence breach\) for incident verification.
- Maintenance and compliance — automated mileage logging and maintenance reminders to keep fleets service-ready and compliant.

Choosing the PT502 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker gives fleet operators a practical combination of real-time tracking, rich telemetry and actionable alarms.
