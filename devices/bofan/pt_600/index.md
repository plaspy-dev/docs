# Bofan - PT-600

![PT-600](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-600 is an advanced camera GPS vehicle tracker that offers a range of features to enhance your tracking and monitoring capabilities. With the ability to take photos and record videos, this tracker provides visual evidence of vehicle activities. It also offers driver identification via RFID, allowing you to track and manage specific drivers or students taking school buses. 

One of the standout features of the PT-600 is its voice monitoring and two-way talking capabilities. This allows you to listen in on the vehicle's surroundings and communicate with the driver when necessary. Additionally, the tracker offers fuel and temperature monitoring, providing valuable insights into the vehicle's performance and ensuring optimal conditions for transportation.

The PT-600 utilizes both Glonass and GPS technology for accurate and reliable tracking. It supports tracking via SMS/GPRS and provides real-time online tracking in the FMS platform via GPRS. You can also request the current location of the vehicle and receive a Google Map link via SMS. 

With a wide range of alarm features, including SOS alarm, speed alarm, and geo-fence alarm, you can set up notifications for various events and ensure the safety and security of your vehicles. The tracker also allows you to remotely stop the vehicle via relay, providing an added layer of control and security.

In terms of storage capacity, the PT-600 can log up to 30,000+ waypoints if there is no GPRS signal, ensuring that you have access to historical data even in areas with limited connectivity. It also supports taking photos automatically at preset time intervals or when specific alarms are triggered.

With its versatile I/O capabilities, including digital inputs and outputs, as well as analog inputs, the PT-600 can be customized to suit your specific tracking and monitoring needs. Whether you need to monitor vehicle mileage, control vehicle doors, or integrate additional cameras, this tracker offers the flexibility to adapt to your requirements.

Overall, the Bofan PT-600 is a feature-rich GPS vehicle tracker that combines advanced tracking capabilities with camera functionality, driver identification, and various alarm features. It is an ideal solution for fleet management, vehicle security, and transportation monitoring.
