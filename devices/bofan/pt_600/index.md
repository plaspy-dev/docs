# Bofan - PT-600

<div className="device-logo">
![PT-600](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Bofan PT-600 is a camera equipped GPS vehicle tracker designed for fleets and transport operations that need visual evidence alongside location data. It combines photo and video capture with driver identification via RFID, voice monitoring and two way communication, plus vehicle condition monitoring such as fuel and temperature. The device stores large numbers of waypoints locally and supports real time updates via GPRS as well as location requests by SMS, making it suitable for environments with varying connectivity.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT-600 can bring its multimedia and identification features into a centralized fleet platform. When integrated with Plaspy, PT-600 devices can provide location visibility, event alerts, and stored route playback together with images and driver assignment information. This makes the model a practical option for organizations that want combined location, visual and operational oversight within the Plaspy system.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated camera functionality for photos and video to support visual verification of events
- Driver identification through RFID for mapping trips to individual operators
- Voice monitoring and two way talking to improve situational awareness and driver communication
- Dual satellite positioning with real time tracking via GPRS and SMS location request support
- Wide set of alarm features including SOS speed and geo fence alerts for safety and security
- Large local waypoint storage to preserve historical route data when connectivity is limited
- Flexible I O options and analog inputs to adapt to fleet monitoring and control needs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the PT-600 feeds location, alarms, and multimedia evidence into the platform so fleet managers can monitor vehicles and respond to events from a single interface.

- View real time vehicle location and status on Plaspy maps for operational oversight
- Receive and manage alarms such as SOS speed and geo fence notifications through Plaspy alerts
- Access photos and recorded video tied to events or time intervals for incident review
- Associate RFID driver IDs with user profiles in Plaspy for driver level reporting and accountability
- Replay historical routes and review stored waypoints for trip analysis and reporting
- Send authorized remote commands such as relay stop where supported to assist in vehicle control

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet operators needing visual evidence of deliveries or incidents alongside GPS tracking
- School bus or passenger transport fleets using RFID to verify driver assignment and rider safety
- Transport and logistics operations monitoring temperature sensitive cargo and fuel usage
- Security focused vehicle fleets requiring SOS alerts remote immobilization and media capture
- Organizations that need historical route playback and integrated reporting for compliance
