# Bofan - PT-600 Features

This page provides a public, feature-oriented overview of the Bofan PT-600 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy for vehicle tracking and monitoring. It focuses on user-facing functions and practical value, and is intended to help fleet managers, transport operators, and integrators understand what to expect when the PT-600 is connected to Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. For device-specific configuration, firmware notes, and the latest technical details, consult official Bofan documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The PT-600 is a camera-enabled vehicle tracker designed to combine visual evidence with location and event reporting. It supports driver identification, two-way voice, environmental monitoring, and a range of alarm and control functions suitable for fleet and transport oversight.

- Integrated camera functions for capturing photos and recording video tied to events or timed intervals
- Driver identification via RFID to associate trips with specific drivers or passengers
- Voice monitoring and two-way talk for situational awareness and in-cab communication
- Fuel and temperature monitoring to track key vehicle metrics relevant to operations
- Real-time tracking over GPRS with SMS location queries and Google Map link responses

## Core Features of Bofan - PT-600

- Camera capture and video recording with automatic photo capture on schedule or triggered by alarms
- Driver identification using RFID to log who is operating the vehicle
- Voice monitoring plus two-way talk capability to listen and communicate with the vehicle cabin
- Fuel level monitoring to provide alerts or logs related to fuel status
- Temperature monitoring for environments where thermal data is relevant
- GPS and GLONASS positioning to improve location accuracy and reliability
- Event alarms including SOS, speed alarm, and geo-fence alarm for safety and compliance
- Remote stop functionality via relay to enable authorized immobilization when needed

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the PT-600 reports to Plaspy, its visual, positional, and event data become part of a single operational view for monitoring and review. Plaspy detects compatible tracker protocols so the PT-600 can send location and event information into the platform for real-time visibility and historical analysis.

- Camera captures and alarm-triggered photos are available as attachments or events in Plaspy for incident review
- Driver ID events recorded by RFID appear in trip logs and driver attribution reports
- Voice monitoring and two-way talk sessions are surfaced as event entries or session indicators where supported by platform settings
- Fuel and temperature readings can be used to generate alerts, trends, and reports for vehicle performance monitoring
- Geofence, SOS, and speed alarms are delivered to Plaspy as events for immediate notification and auditing

## Typical Use Cases

- School bus and passenger transport where driver ID and camera evidence are required
- Fleet safety programs using alarms, speed monitoring, and in-cab voice communication
- Logistics and delivery operations needing visual proof of loading, unloading, or incidents
- Remote monitoring of fuel consumption and vehicle temperature for route management
- Security use cases that combine location tracking, SOS alerts, and remote stop capability

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature availability may differ by PT-600 firmware version and hardware revision; not every unit will include all capabilities
- Some functions depend on installation choices and connected sensors or accessories such as external cameras or RFID readers
- Regional product variants or regulatory limitations can affect features like voice monitoring or wireless behavior
- For reliable operation in Plaspy, ensure the device firmware is compatible with platform reporting methods and that device settings are properly configured
- Refer to the manufacturer for supported accessories and any compatibility lists for specific PT-600 builds

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the PT-600 with Plaspy brings camera evidence, driver identification, environmental metrics, and alarm visibility into a consolidated fleet management view. This combination helps fleets reduce incident investigation time, assign events to specific drivers, and respond more quickly to safety alarms or operational exceptions.
