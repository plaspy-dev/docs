# Bofan - B5

![B5](./tracker.png)

The Bofan B5 is a compact vehicle mounted GPS tracker designed for continuous 24 7 fleet monitoring and security. It provides persistent GNSS positioning, configurable geo fence alerts, remote engine control and engine runtime analytics, together with an internal accelerometer and internal high gain antennas for reliable reception in typical vehicle deployments. The device also includes onboard flash for temporary trip logging and data saving modes to help manage cellular usage.

As a Plaspy compatible device out of the box, the B5 delivers location, event and telemetry data directly into Plaspy so managers can view live positions and historical activity from a single platform. That native compatibility makes the B5 a practical option for fleets and owners who want centralized visibility, anti theft controls and configurable alerts without complex third party integration work.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for centralized real time fleet tracking and event monitoring
- Remote engine control and one touch SOS for rapid anti theft response and emergency alerts
- Configurable geo fence and scenario alerts including overspeed towing crash jamming and unplug detection
- Built in accelerometer for movement detection trip logging and basic driver event capture
- GPRS data saving modes with onboard flash for temporary offline logging and later upload
- Internal high gain GNSS and GSM antennas and multiple digital inputs and a digital output for richer telemetry

## How It Works with Plaspy

The B5 streams location and event information to Plaspy so fleet operators can combine position data, alerts and runtime analytics in dashboards and reports. Plaspy ingests the B5 telemetry and maps device events to alarms and workflows that support monitoring and response.

- Real time position updates and history replay visible on Plaspy live maps
- Geo fence and scenario alerts routed into Plaspy alarms for rapid notification and follow up
- Engine runtime and ignition sensing reported to Plaspy for utilization and maintenance insight
- Remote immobilizer or engine control actions initiated from Plaspy when configured
- Trip logs stored locally on the device are uploaded to Plaspy when connectivity returns

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and immobilization for rapid response to unauthorized movement
- Driver behavior monitoring and preventive maintenance using engine runtime and trip analytics
- Emergency response with one touch SOS and remote monitoring to verify incidents
- Asset protection and tamper detection leveraging unplug jamming and crash alerts
- Operational oversight and routing enforcement using geo fence and overspeed events

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The B5 pairs practical vehicle oriented hardware with Plaspy platform features to give operations a balanced solution for tracking security and cost control. Its combination of internal antennas, accelerometer based event detection, data saving modes and onboard logging helps preserve critical trip and event data for Plaspy reporting while reducing unnecessary cellular usage.
