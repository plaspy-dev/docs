# Bofan - PT-100 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for the Bofan PT-100 and how those capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, non-sensitive information about what the PT-100 can report and how those reports are reflected in fleet and vehicle monitoring workflows on Plaspy.

Exact feature availability for any PT-100 unit can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation wiring, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a high level reference and consult the manufacturer for the most current device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The PT-100 is a low cost GPS car tracker designed to deliver basic real time and historical location visibility together with a set of configurable alerts and I O options. Its mixed SMS and GPRS reporting plus onboard logging provide flexibility for single vehicles or small fleets where budget and straightforward monitoring are priorities.

- Real time location reporting using GPRS or SMS to provide live tracking and remote queries
- Time interval reporting for scheduled location updates
- Onboard position logging capacity of 3,000 plus waypoints for historical playback
- SOS button for sending emergency alerts to designated contacts
- Configurable alerts including geo fence, low power, cut power, and overspeed notifications
- I O options with two digital inputs and one output plus remote engine cut functionality for integration with vehicle systems

## Core Features of Bofan - PT-100

- GPS vehicle location reporting via GPRS and SMS communication
- Configurable periodic reporting by time interval
- Onboard history storage with capacity for more than 3,000 waypoints
- Dedicated SOS button to trigger emergency alerts
- Geo fence alerts to notify when a vehicle enters or leaves a defined area
- Power related alerts including low battery and external power cut notifications
- Over speed alerts to flag when a preset speed threshold is exceeded
- Two digital inputs and one output available for basic external sensing and control plus engine cut support when installed and configured

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives and normalizes the PT-100 reports so location, alerts, and event history become visible in a single monitoring environment. Plaspy’s platform detects compatible tracker protocols and maps incoming messages into device position records and alert events for operational use.

- Live and periodic location updates appear on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Stored waypoints uploaded by the device enable route history and playback inside Plaspy
- SOS and alert events such as geo fence, overspeed, low power, and power cut are shown as notifications and event log entries
- I O status and output activations can be presented as device inputs and control events where supported by the platform
- Reporting intervals and alert thresholds configured on the device are reflected in Plaspy as the cadence and types of events you will see

## Typical Use Cases

- Basic vehicle tracking for personal cars or single assets
- Small fleet monitoring with scheduled position updates and route history
- Emergency alerting using the SOS button for driver safety
- Geofencing to detect unauthorized movements or to monitor area based activity
- Overspeed monitoring to support safe driving policies
- Remote immobilization for stolen or unauthorized vehicles where engine cut is configured

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features depend on the device firmware version and may vary across hardware revisions
- Engine cut and output control require correct installation and wiring and may be restricted by local laws
- SMS versus GPRS behavior can differ by carrier and regional cellular support
- Certain alert behaviors such as geo fence or overspeed thresholds may require configuration after installation
- Always confirm feature presence and exact behavior for your unit with the manufacturer documentation

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the PT-100 with Plaspy provides a simple path to centralize vehicle location, alerts, and history. Plaspy consolidates incoming reports into a unified view so operations teams can monitor live positions, review historical routes, and receive event notifications from the tracker without dealing with raw device messages.
