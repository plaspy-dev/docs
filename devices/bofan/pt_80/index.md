# Bofan - PT-80

![PT-80](./logo.jpg)

The Bofan PT-80 GPS Personal Tracker is a compact and portable device designed to provide real-time tracking and monitoring for personal use. With its small size of 65 x 42 x 20 mm and lightweight design of only 50g, it is easy to carry and can be discreetly placed in a bag or pocket.

The PT-80 features built-in GSM and GPS antennas, ensuring reliable and accurate tracking. It also has quick dialing buttons for three preset phone numbers, allowing for easy communication in case of emergencies or urgent situations. Additionally, the device offers voice monitoring, enabling users to listen in on the surroundings of the tracker.

One of the standout features of the PT-80 is its panic button, which can be pressed in emergency situations to send an immediate alert to designated contacts. This feature provides peace of mind and ensures that help can be quickly summoned when needed.

The device also offers geo-fence management, allowing users to set virtual boundaries and receive notifications when the tracker enters or exits these predefined areas. This is particularly useful for keeping track of loved ones or monitoring the movement of valuable assets.

Other notable features of the PT-80 include low power alert, over speed alert, tracking via SMS/GPRS, and the ability to request street addresses and Google links for the current location via SMS. It also comes with a web-based real-time tracking service, allowing users to monitor the tracker's location and movement remotely.

With its internal motion sensor, the PT-80 can save power and send movement alarms when it detects any activity. It is equipped with an internal battery that is rechargeable and offers long standby time. The device also has a position logging capacity of up to 3,000+ waypoints when there is no GPRS signal, ensuring that no tracking data is lost.

Overall, the Bofan PT-80 GPS Personal Tracker is a reliable and feature-packed device that provides peace of mind and enhanced security for personal tracking needs. Whether it's for keeping track of loved ones, monitoring valuable assets, or ensuring personal safety, the PT-80 offers a range of functions and features to meet these requirements.
