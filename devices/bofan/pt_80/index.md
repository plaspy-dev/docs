# Bofan - PT-80

<div className="device-logo">
![PT-80](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Bofan PT-80 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for discreet, portable location monitoring. At a small size and lightweight form factor, the PT-80 is intended for personal safety and asset tracking use. It includes built in GSM and GPS antennas for location reporting, quick dial buttons for preset contacts, voice monitoring capability, a panic button for emergency alerts, geo fence management, and a rechargeable internal battery with long standby and onboard position logging capacity.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT-80 can be integrated into a centralized tracking workflow so location, alerts, and history are visible alongside other units. Plaspy can ingest the PT-80 reported positions and events to provide map visibility, consolidated notification handling, historical playback, and operational reporting. That combination makes the PT-80 a suitable choice for users who need a portable tracker paired with a fleet management or monitoring platform like Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight design for discreet personal or portable asset tracking
- Real time tracking available through web based service and device reporting
- Panic button and quick dial keys for rapid alerting to preset contacts
- Geo fence management with entry and exit notifications for virtual boundaries
- Voice monitoring feature for remote listening where supported by the device
- Internal motion sensor and onboard logging of 3,000+ waypoints when live data is unavailable

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the PT-80's location updates and alert events become part of a unified monitoring environment. Plaspy consolidates device reports to offer continuous situational awareness and event history for operators and administrators.

- See live PT-80 positions on the Plaspy map alongside other tracked assets
- Receive and manage alerts from the device within Plaspy, including panic, geofence, low power, and overspeed notifications
- Access historical tracks and playback logged waypoints to review movement when GPRS or data was intermittent
- Use Plaspy reporting to aggregate events and generate operational summaries for oversight
- Combine SMS/GPRS reporting behavior of the device with Plaspy monitoring to maintain visibility in varied connectivity conditions

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children, seniors, or lone workers
- Tracking portable valuables and equipment that move between locations
- Temporary deployment for event staff, security teams, or field workers
- Rental items or shared assets that require basic location and entry/exit alerts
- Short term asset tracking when discreet form factor and long standby are important
