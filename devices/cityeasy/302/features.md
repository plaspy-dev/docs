# Cityeasy - 302 Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Cityeasy 302 car GPS tracker and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for fleet and vehicle monitoring. It explains the main, user facing functions you can expect when using the 302 with Plaspy while keeping the description at a practical, non sensitive level.

Exact feature availability and behaviour can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and how the manufacturer has implemented particular functions. For device specific setup, firmware notes, or the latest capability details consult the official manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Cityeasy 302 is a vehicle tracking unit focused on location awareness and event alerts. It is designed to provide continuous position updates, a set of movement and tamper alerts, and historical route playback for review and analysis.

- Real time LBS and GPS position tracking to follow vehicle location
- Tamper detection with a cut electric alert to notify of power interruption
- Movement related alerts including over speed, vibration, and displacement
- Geofence support to generate alerts when a vehicle enters or leaves defined areas
- Historical route relay for reviewing past trips and route history

## Core Features of Cityeasy - 302

- LBS and GPS based real time position reporting for continuous tracking
- Cut electric alert to indicate power supply tampering or removal
- Over speed alert to signal when configured speed thresholds are exceeded
- Vibration alert to detect unusual motion or impacts
- Geofence alerting to support perimeter monitoring of vehicles
- Displacement alert to flag unexpected or unauthorized movement
- Historical route relay for playback and analysis of past movements

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Cityeasy 302’s reporting and alerts become visible within the platform for monitoring and operational oversight. Plaspy handles incoming device reports and presents location and event data in a consistent interface.

- Real time location appears on Plaspy maps allowing live tracking of the vehicle
- Alerts such as cut electric, over speed, vibration, geofence, and displacement are logged and shown in event timelines
- Historical route relay data can be used for route playback and review inside Plaspy
- Plaspy detects common tracker protocols automatically and matches reported events to platform features
- Devices compatible with Plaspy can be pointed to the Plaspy server domain so reports appear in the account interface

## Typical Use Cases

- Monitoring a company car fleet for location and route history
- Notifying fleet managers of power tampering or cut electric events
- Tracking vehicle movements and receiving displacement alerts for security
- Using geofence alerts to enforce area based operational rules
- Reviewing historical routes to analyze usage patterns or trip compliance
- Receiving over speed and vibration alerts to investigate potentially unsafe events
