# Cityeasy - 302

![302](./logo.jpg)

The Cityeasy 302 is a car GPS tracker that offers a range of features to help you keep track of your vehicle's location and ensure its safety. With its free installation and power-on capabilities, you can easily set up the tracker without any hassle. The device comes with a cut electric alert, which notifies you if someone tries to tamper with the power supply, providing an added layer of security.

One of the standout features of the Cityeasy 302 is its LBS/GPS real-time position tracking. This allows you to accurately monitor the location of your vehicle at any given time. Additionally, the tracker offers various alerts such as over-speed alert, vibration alert, geofence, and displacement alert. These alerts help you stay informed about any unusual activities or movements of your vehicle.

The Cityeasy 302 also provides a historical route relay, which allows you to view the past routes taken by your vehicle. This can be useful for tracking purposes or analyzing the usage patterns of your vehicle. Furthermore, the tracker is IP67 waterproof, ensuring its durability and functionality even in harsh weather conditions.

Overall, the Cityeasy 302 is a reliable and feature-packed car GPS tracker that offers easy installation, real-time tracking, and a range of alerts to keep your vehicle safe and secure.

### Key Features:

- Car GPS tracker with free installation and power-on capabilities
- Cut electric alert for added security
- LBS/GPS real-time position tracking
- Over-speed alert, vibration alert, geofence, and displacement alert
- Historical route relay for tracking and analysis
- IP67 waterproof for durability
