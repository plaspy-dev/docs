# Cityeasy - 302

<div className="device-logo">
![302](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Cityeasy 302 is a car GPS tracker designed to provide continuous vehicle location monitoring and basic security alerts. It supports both LBS and GPS position reporting for real time tracking, offers a set of motion and tamper alerts including over speed, vibration, displacement, and a cut electric alert, and records historical routes for later review. The device is built to withstand wet conditions thanks to its IP67 rating, and it is described as offering free installation and easy power on capabilities.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the Cityeasy 302 can feed location and alert information into the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility and fleet oversight. Plaspy users can view live positions, review historical routes, and receive the tracker alerts within the Plaspy environment to support monitoring, incident response, and reporting workflows. Compatibility makes the 302 a practical option for teams that want to combine a compact tracker with Plaspy monitoring and management features.

## Key Highlights

- Car focused GPS tracker with LBS and GPS real time position tracking
- Tamper detection through a cut electric alert to notify of power interruption
- Common vehicle alerts such as over speed, vibration, geofence, and displacement
- Historical route relay for route review and usage analysis
- IP67 waterproof rating for reliable operation in wet conditions
- Described free installation and power on to simplify initial deployment

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy the Cityeasy 302 supplies location points and alert events so operators can monitor vehicles from a single platform. Plaspy collects the tracker data and presents it alongside other assets to support operational decisions and oversight.

- Live location display for individual vehicles to support real time tracking
- Event and alert forwarding so over speed, tamper, and movement alerts appear in Plaspy
- Historical route playback inside Plaspy for trip review and operational analysis
- Geofence event handling for area based notifications and policy checks
- Consolidated reporting and logs that include tracker events and positional history

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle location monitoring for small to medium sized fleets
- Theft or tamper detection using power cut and vibration alerts
- Route analysis for delivery and service vehicles via historical replay
- Safety and compliance monitoring with over speed alerts and geofence checks
- Asset protection for vehicles that operate in varied weather conditions
