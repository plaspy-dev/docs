## Feature Overview

The Cityeasy 007 is a vehicle tracker built for real time location monitoring and extended field use. It combines LBS and GPS position reporting with durable construction and a removable battery to support longer deployments and basic environmental protection.

- LBS and GPS based real time position tracking for continuous location awareness
- Long distance monitoring for remote oversight of vehicles and assets
- Removable 5000mAh battery to extend operation between charges
- IP67 rated enclosure for protection against dust and water ingress
- Designed for both personal vehicles and light commercial fleet use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Cityeasy 007 devices can deliver position reports and status data into Plaspy where managers can view location, history, and device presence. Plaspy’s platform is designed to accept common tracker protocols and display device activity on maps and dashboards.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and monitoring
- Historical routes and recent position history are available for operational review
- Device presence and connectivity status are indicated in Plaspy to help identify offline devices
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and simplifies onboarding
- The tracker can be configured to report into Plaspy servers; Plaspy accepts UDP or TCP reporting and uses a common device port for incoming reports
