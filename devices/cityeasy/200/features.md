# Cityeasy - 200 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Cityeasy 200 GPS tracker and explains how its capabilities can be used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, user facing information about location tracking, alerts, and historical route review that are relevant when integrating the tracker with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability for the Cityeasy 200 can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. The content below is based on the general device description and is intended for planning and documentation purposes rather than as a definitive technical specification.

## Feature Overview

The Cityeasy 200 is a compact car GPS tracker designed to deliver continuous position awareness and event alerts for vehicle security and operational monitoring. It emphasizes location reporting, movement alerts, and durable environmental protection for use in a range of vehicle deployments.

- Real time position tracking using GPS and LBS for continuous location awareness
- Vibration alert to notify on tampering or unexpected movement
- Geofence support to trigger alerts when a vehicle enters or leaves a defined area
- Displacement alert to detect unauthorized relocation of the vehicle
- Historical route relay so past journeys can be reviewed in timeline or map form
- IP67 rated enclosure for dust and water resistance during normal outdoor use

## Core Features of Cityeasy - 200

- LBS and GPS position reporting for location tracking
- Real time location updates to support live monitoring
- Vibration alert for tamper and movement detection
- Geofence event notification for boundary monitoring
- Displacement alert for unauthorized movement detection
- Historical route relay for route playback and review
- IP67 waterproof rating for weather resistant installations

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Cityeasy 200 reports location and event data that the platform uses to provide visibility and operational insights. Plaspy ingests supported events and presents them in dashboards and maps to simplify monitoring and response.

- Location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and position history
- Vibration and displacement alerts show up as events so teams can be notified and investigate
- Geofence triggers are translated into alerts or notifications inside the Plaspy platform
- Historical route relay data is available for playback in Plaspy to review trips and routes
- Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol to streamline setup and device onboarding

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft and tamper monitoring for private cars and company vehicles
- Rental fleet monitoring to track vehicle locations and route history
- Operational oversight for small fleets that require simple live tracking
- Route verification and trip review for mileage or activity reconciliation
- Parking zone enforcement and location based alerts for managed vehicles
