# Cityeasy - 200

![200](./logo.jpg)

The Cityeasy 200 is a reliable and efficient car GPS tracker that offers a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle. With its LBS/GPS real-time position tracking, you can easily monitor the location of your car at any time. Whether you're concerned about theft or simply want to keep tabs on your vehicle's whereabouts, this tracker has got you covered.

One of the standout features of the Cityeasy 200 is its vibration alert, which notifies you if any unauthorized movement or tampering is detected. This is especially useful in preventing theft or unauthorized use of your vehicle. Additionally, the geofence feature allows you to set up virtual boundaries for your car, and you will receive an alert if the vehicle crosses those boundaries. This is perfect for keeping track of your car's movements and ensuring it stays within designated areas.

The Cityeasy 200 also offers a displacement alert, which notifies you if your vehicle is moved without your authorization. This is particularly useful in situations where you have entrusted your car to someone else or if you suspect any unauthorized use. Furthermore, the tracker provides a historical route relay, allowing you to review the past routes taken by your vehicle. This can be helpful for tracking mileage, monitoring driver behavior, or even providing evidence in case of accidents or disputes.

With its IP67 waterproof rating, the Cityeasy 200 is designed to withstand various weather conditions, ensuring its durability and reliability. Whether it's rain, snow, or dust, this tracker will continue to function optimally, providing you with peace of mind.

Overall, the Cityeasy 200 is a versatile and feature-packed car GPS tracker that offers real-time tracking, anti-theft features, and historical route relay. Its waterproof design adds an extra layer of durability, making it suitable for a wide range of applications. Whether you're a concerned car owner, a fleet manager, or a rental car company, the Cityeasy 200 is an excellent choice to enhance the security and monitoring of your vehicles.
