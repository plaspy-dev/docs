# Cityeasy - 200

<div className="device-logo">
![200](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Cityeasy 200 is a reliable and efficient car GPS tracker designed to provide continuous location monitoring and anti theft alerts for vehicles. It supports LBS and GPS real time position tracking and includes features such as vibration alerts, displacement alerts, geofence notifications, and historical route relay. The device is built to withstand outdoor conditions with an IP67 waterproof rating, making it suitable for everyday vehicle protection and monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Cityeasy 200 can be integrated into Plaspy's tracking platform to provide centralized visibility and management of vehicle assets. When used with Plaspy, the tracker delivers location updates and event notifications into the platform so fleet managers and vehicle owners can view live position, review historical routes, and receive alerts that support security and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Real time LBS and GPS position tracking for continuous location visibility
- Vibration alert for detecting potential tampering or unauthorized movement
- Geofence support to trigger alerts when a vehicle enters or leaves defined areas
- Displacement alert to notify when a vehicle is moved without authorization
- Historical route relay to review past journeys and verify routes
- IP67 waterproof rating for reliable performance in varied weather conditions

## How It Works with Plaspy

Cityeasy 200 devices send location and event data that Plaspy can surface in its tracking interface, allowing users to monitor vehicles from a single platform. Plaspy aggregates those updates and presents them alongside alerts and historical data to support operational and security workflows.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for immediate visibility of vehicle positions
- Geofence events routed into Plaspy so teams receive boundary crossing notifications
- Vibration and displacement alerts shown within Plaspy alerting dashboards for rapid response
- Historical route replay available in Plaspy for reviewing trips and verifying mileage
- Fleet level views and grouping in Plaspy to manage multiple Cityeasy 200 units efficiently

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal vehicle security and real time location monitoring
- Small to medium sized fleet tracking for daily operations oversight
- Rental or shared vehicle monitoring to enforce designated area rules
- Route verification and historical review for mileage or incident analysis
- Vehicle protection in outdoor or harsh conditions thanks to waterproof design

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Cityeasy 200 provides a compact set of features that address common needs for vehicle security and operational monitoring. Its combination of real time tracking, geofence control, movement alerts, and route history makes it a practical option for owners and managers who require clear location visibility and basic anti theft notifications.

Paired with Plaspy, the Cityeasy 200 becomes part of a managed tracking environment where alerts, live positions, and historical data are presented together for easier decision making. For organizations that want a straightforward tracker with weather resistant construction and the ability to integrate into a broader fleet platform, this model is a compatible choice to consider.
