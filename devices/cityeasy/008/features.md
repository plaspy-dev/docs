# Cityeasy - 008 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Cityeasy 008 GPS tracker with the Plaspy platform. It explains the tracker capabilities that are relevant for monitoring and managing vehicles inside Plaspy, focusing on practical functions such as real time position reporting, tamper alerts, and route history.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and the specifics of an installation. For definitive device details and the latest operational notes, consult the manufacturer documentation in addition to Plaspy resources.

## Feature Overview

The Cityeasy 008 is a compact car GPS tracker designed to deliver location visibility and basic movement alerts for personal vehicles and small fleets. Its features are oriented toward continuous position reporting, simple tamper notification, and route history access, while the unit is built to withstand common outdoor conditions.

- LBS and GPS based real time position tracking for ongoing location visibility
- Vibration alert notifications to signal potential unauthorized movement or tampering
- Historical route relay and playback to review past vehicle movements
- IP67 rated waterproofing for reliable operation in wet environments
- Compatible with Plaspy for integration into maps, alerts, and reporting workflows

## Core Features of Cityeasy - 008

- Real time LBS and GPS position reporting for live location tracking
- Vibration based alarm to indicate movement or potential tampering
- Historical route relay that records past routes for later review and playback
- IP67 waterproof rating to help ensure device durability in wet conditions
- Compatibility with the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring
- Practical form factor suited to vehicle installation and discreet placement

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Cityeasy 008's core capabilities become part of a centralized view for fleet and vehicle monitoring. Plaspy presents location and event data so operations teams and individual owners can act on relevant signals.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps for ongoing vehicle tracking
- Vibration alerts can be surfaced as events or notifications inside Plaspy
- Historical route data is available for playback and route auditing within Plaspy
- Device status and recent reports are consolidated on the Plaspy device dashboard
- Compatibility is handled by Plaspy so the tracker can be added and discovered for normal monitoring workflows

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal vehicle security and remote monitoring for peace of mind
- Small fleet tracking to maintain location awareness and manage assets
- Tamper and movement detection while vehicles are parked or unattended
- Route review and driver behavior observation using historical playback
- Incident and dispute support by providing recorded route history
