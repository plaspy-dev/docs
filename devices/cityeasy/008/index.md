# Cityeasy - 008

![008](./logo.jpg)

The Cityeasy 008 is a reliable and efficient car GPS tracker that offers a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle. With its LBS/GPS real-time position tracking, you can easily monitor the location of your car at any time. Whether you want to keep an eye on your personal vehicle or manage a fleet of cars for your business, the Cityeasy 008 is a versatile and practical solution.

One of the standout features of the Cityeasy 008 is its vibration alert system. This feature allows you to receive instant notifications on your mobile device whenever there is any unauthorized movement or tampering with your vehicle. This can be particularly useful in preventing theft or ensuring the safety of your car in parking lots or public spaces.

In addition to real-time tracking and vibration alerts, the Cityeasy 008 also offers historical route relay. This means that you can access detailed information about the routes taken by your vehicle in the past. This can be helpful for monitoring driver behavior, optimizing routes, or even providing evidence in case of accidents or disputes.

With its IP67 waterproof rating, the Cityeasy 008 is designed to withstand various weather conditions and environments. This ensures that the tracker remains functional and reliable even in challenging situations.

Overall, the Cityeasy 008 is a feature-packed car GPS tracker that offers real-time tracking, vibration alerts, historical route relay, and waterproof capabilities. Whether you are a concerned car owner or a fleet manager, this tracker provides the necessary tools to keep your vehicles safe and secure.
