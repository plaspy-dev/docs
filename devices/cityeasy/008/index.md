# Cityeasy - 008

![008](./logo.jpg)

The Cityeasy 008 is a reliable and efficient car GPS tracker designed for vehicle security and location monitoring. It provides LBS and GPS real time position tracking, vibration alerts to notify you of unauthorized movement, and historical route relay so past trips can be reviewed. Its IP67 rating indicates the unit is built to withstand exposure to harsh weather and wet conditions, making it suitable for everyday vehicle use.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the Cityeasy 008 can be integrated into a centralized tracking platform to give fleet managers and vehicle owners continuous visibility. Plaspy can surface the tracker data for live monitoring, alerting, and route history review, allowing organizations to use the 008 within broader operational workflows without needing separate monitoring tools.

## Key Highlights

- Real time LBS and GPS position tracking for continuous location visibility
- Vibration alert system that notifies on unauthorized movement or tampering
- Historical route relay for trip playback and review of past movements
- IP67 water resistance for reliable performance in wet or dusty environments
- Suitable for both individual car owners and light fleet applications
- Practical and efficient design aimed at vehicle security and oversight

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Cityeasy 008 feeds its position and event data into the Plaspy platform so that location, alerts, and history are available alongside other fleet information. Plaspy organizes incoming data into maps, timelines, and reports so teams can act on real world events quickly and consistently.

- View real time location of each 008 unit on Plaspy maps for live tracking
- Receive vibration alerts and other event notifications inside Plaspy dashboards
- Access historical route relay as trip history for review and evidence
- Monitor multiple vehicles from a single Plaspy fleet dashboard for operational oversight
- Generate reports and summaries from tracked data to support routing and management decisions

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal vehicle security and remote monitoring of a single car
- Parking lot surveillance and quick detection of unauthorized movement
- Fleet tracking for small commercial vehicle groups or service fleets
- Route history review for driver accountability and operational analysis
- Incident reconstruction when a detailed record of past movements is needed
