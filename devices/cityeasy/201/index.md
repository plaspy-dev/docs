# Cityeasy - 201

![201](./logo.jpg)

The Cityeasy 201 is a reliable and efficient car GPS tracker that offers a range of features to ensure the safety and security of your vehicle. With its LBS/GPS real-time position tracking, you can easily monitor the location of your car at any time. Whether you want to keep an eye on your personal vehicle or manage a fleet of cars for your business, this tracker provides accurate and up-to-date information.

One of the standout features of the Cityeasy 201 is its vibration alert system. This feature notifies you immediately if any unauthorized movement or tampering is detected, allowing you to take prompt action. Additionally, the tracker offers historical route relay, which means you can access detailed information about the routes your vehicle has taken in the past. This can be useful for tracking purposes or for analyzing driving patterns.

The Cityeasy 201 is designed to withstand various weather conditions, thanks to its IP67 waterproof rating. This means you don't have to worry about the tracker getting damaged by rain or other environmental factors. Furthermore, the tracker is equipped with a powerful 5000mAh removable battery, ensuring long-lasting performance and reducing the need for frequent recharging.

Overall, the Cityeasy 201 is a reliable and feature-packed car GPS tracker that offers real-time tracking, vibration alerts, historical route relay, and a waterproof design. Whether you're a concerned car owner or a fleet manager, this tracker provides the necessary tools to keep your vehicles safe and secure.

### Key Features:

- LBS/GPS real-time position tracking
- Vibration alert system
- Historical route relay
- IP67 waterproof rating
- 5000mAh removable battery
