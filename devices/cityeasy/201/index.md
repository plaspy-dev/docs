# Cityeasy - 201

![201](./logo.jpg)

The Cityeasy 201 is a compact car GPS tracker designed to provide reliable location monitoring and basic security features for personal vehicles and small fleets. It combines LBS and GPS real time position tracking with route history capabilities to give operators visibility into vehicle movements and past journeys.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Cityeasy 201 can feed location and alert information into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. Its vibration alerting, route relay, waterproof design, and removable battery make it a practical option to pair with Plaspy for continuous vehicle tracking and incident awareness.

## Key Highlights

- Real time LBS and GPS position tracking for up to date location visibility
- Vibration alert system to notify of movement or potential tampering
- Historical route relay for reviewing past routes and trip history
- IP67 rated waterproofing for reliable outdoor performance
- Large removable 5000mAh battery for extended on device operation
- Suitable for both individual vehicles and small fleet deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated into Plaspy, the Cityeasy 201 supplies location updates and alerts that Plaspy presents through its dashboard for easy monitoring, reporting, and operational use. Plaspy ingests the device data so fleet managers and vehicle owners can see position, access route history, and receive activity notifications from a single platform.

- Centralized live location display for each Cityeasy 201 device within Plaspy
- Route history visualization and export to support operational review and auditing
- Alert routing so vibration notifications are surfaced to operators in Plaspy
- Fleet level overviews to monitor multiple Cityeasy 201 units together
- Reporting and logs that combine device events and movement history for analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time vehicle tracking for personal cars and company cars
- Fleet monitoring for small commercial vehicle groups
- Security and anti tamper monitoring using vibration alerts
- Reviewing driver routes for route planning and operational insight
- Temporary tracking deployments using the removable battery for flexible placement

The Cityeasy 201 offers a balanced set of features that map well to common tracking needs: consistent location updates, basic security alerts, and route history for post trip review.
