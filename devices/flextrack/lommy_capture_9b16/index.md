# Flextrack - Lommy Capture 9B16

![Lommy Capture 9B16](./tracker.jpg)

Lommy Capture 9B16 is a compact, batteryless GPS tracker designed for discreet, real time tracking of trailers, caravans and other towed assets. Purpose built to fit inside light housings, it harvests energy from vehicle lighting circuits to maintain GNSS position reporting and motion telemetry without a dedicated battery. The small IP65 enclosure and tamper resistant packaging make it suitable for long term fleet deployments where concealment and low maintenance are priorities.

As a Plaspy compatible device, Lommy Capture 9B16 transmits location and activity data in formats and channels that integrate with monitoring platforms and APIs used by Plaspy. Its adaptive reporting behavior, multi constellation GNSS and local buffering mean fleet managers can add trailer visibility, motion based alerts and historical tracking into Plaspy powered workflows while minimizing wiring and upkeep.

## Key Highlights

- Batteryless energy harvesting from tail brake and indicator circuits to reduce maintenance needs.
- Plaspy compatible real time tracking and telemetry using cellular uplink with 2G fallback and data or SMS transport.
- Multi constellation GNSS with SBAS support for improved positioning performance in varied environments.
- Adaptive reporting that increases frequency while charging from light circuits and conserves energy when power is limited.
- Compact tamper resistant IP65 housing sized for in lamp installation for discreet placement.
- Built in 3 axis motion sensor to detect movement and trigger activity reporting for anti theft responsiveness.
- Local flash buffer to retain position reports when connectivity is unavailable and forward them later.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, Lommy Capture 9B16 delivers GNSS fixes, motion activity signals and buffered telemetry into Plaspy workflows for real time visibility and post event analysis. Plaspy can consume the device data via common integration methods so you can monitor trailers alongside other assets in a unified fleet view.

- Real time location and activity updates flow into Plaspy compatible endpoints using data or SMS transport.
- Motion detection events trigger adaptive reporting to improve responsiveness to tampering or movement.
- Reporting intervals adjust based on harvested charge so Plaspy receives frequent updates when powered and periodic reports when idle.
- Local buffering ensures position and event data are retained and forwarded to Plaspy when connectivity resumes.
- Data from the tracker can be used within Plaspy for alerts, route history, utilization reports and asset status dashboards.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for trailers and caravans seeking low maintenance GPS visibility with discreet installation.
- Anti theft deployments where concealed placement inside light housings reduces risk of removal.
- Historical route logging and utilization tracking for maintenance planning and asset optimization.
- Geofencing and movement based alarms to notify operators of unexpected trailer activity.
- Low access installations where avoiding battery replacements reduces operational overhead.

Lommy Capture 9B16 is well suited to organizations that need reliable, low profile trailer tracking without the recurring maintenance of battery replacements. Its energy harvesting approach, combined with adaptive reporting and local buffering, provides a practical balance of continuous visibility and long term operation, which complements Plaspy managed monitoring and alerting.
