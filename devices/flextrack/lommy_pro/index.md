# Flextrack - Lommy Pro

![Lommy Pro](./tracker.jpg)

Lommy Pro is a compact, power efficient GPS tracker designed for reliable real time tracking of vehicles, machinery, boats and other mobile assets. The device combines multi constellation GNSS positioning with cellular connectivity to deliver continuous location, movement and telemetry updates, and is intended for fleet management and anti theft applications where persistent visibility and sensible power management are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, Lommy Pro can feed location and telemetry into Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reporting workflows. Its configurable sleep modes, onboard movement sensing and rechargeable backup battery make it suitable for mixed installations from wired vehicle fits to autonomous battery backed deployments, while open API and alert channel support enables integration with Plaspy for operational oversight and automated responses.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker offering reliable positioning using multi constellation GNSS for consistent location fixes.
- Cellular connectivity designed for broad coverage and continuous telemetry reporting to Plaspy.
- Compact, low power design with an internal rechargeable backup battery to support battery backed deployments.
- Onboard movement sensing and configurable sleep modes to extend field life when primary power is removed.
- Supports trip and odometer logging, overspeed and idling detection, and immobilizer control for fleet operations.
- Temperature monitoring and external sensor support for condition monitoring of refrigerated trailers and similar assets.
- Expandable accessory support for localized tagging and enhanced asset identification in mixed environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Lommy Pro transmits GNSS fixes and telemetry to Plaspy where data is normalized for live tracking, historical analysis and automated alerts. Plaspy ingests position and event data, applies configured rules, and presents that information on dashboards and in reports so teams can monitor movement, condition and compliance.

- Real time location updates and trip replay for live fleet visibility and route review.
- Alerts and automated notifications for events such as overspeed, idling, immobilizer triggers and geofence breaches.
- Historical reporting for odometer and trip summaries to support operational analysis and scheduling.
- Sensor and condition monitoring for temperature, door events and other external inputs to support cold chain and asset health workflows.
- Remote device management and configuration through the platform to keep telemetry flowing and to apply policy changes at scale.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with continuous vehicle tracking, trip logging and operational reporting.
- Anti theft and recoverability with immobilizer control, start stop detection and geofence alerts.
- Fuel and engine telemetry monitoring to support consumption analysis and maintenance planning.
- Temperature surveillance for refrigerated trailers and cold chain asset condition monitoring.
- Asset tracking in mixed environments including trailers, equipment and marine applications using accessory pairing.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Lommy Pro is a practical choice for organizations that need a balance of accurate positioning, sensible power management and vehicle grade telemetry options. When paired with Plaspy, the device can deliver the basic building blocks for real time fleet oversight, event driven alerts and historical reporting without requiring complex customization.

Plaspy’s platform accepts position and telemetry from Lommy Pro and presents it in dashboards and reports that operations and security teams use to make decisions. For businesses that need scalable tracking, anti theft features and environmental monitoring, Lommy Pro combined with Plaspy provides a straightforward path to consolidated visibility and automated alerting.
