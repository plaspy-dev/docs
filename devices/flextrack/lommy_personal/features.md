# Flextrack - Lommy Personal Features

This page describes the public feature context for the Flextrack Lommy Personal when used with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities that are relevant for visibility, monitoring, and operational use in Plaspy without covering private configuration steps or internal protocols. Where useful, the page explains how Lommy Personal features are reflected inside Plaspy for general planning and deployment.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, optional accessories, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current and device specific technical details consult Flextrack documentation while using this page as a compatibility and feature summary for Plaspy users.

## Feature Overview

The Lommy Personal is built for personal safety and everyday tracking scenarios. It emphasizes reliable location reporting with a focus on long operating times and adaptability to individual needs. Below are the most relevant practical capabilities that matter for operations and monitoring in a fleet or personal device context.

- Designed specifically for personal safety use across professional and private settings
- Customizable and easy to modify to match individual user requirements and deployments
- Powerful battery that delivers extended operating time between charges for longer coverage
- Highly sensitive GPS receiver for improved location accuracy in challenging conditions
- Available option for a high capacity extended battery to lengthen usage even further

## Core Features of Flextrack - Lommy Personal

- Personal safety oriented design suitable for lone workers, caregivers, elderly monitoring, and family use
- Device configuration flexibility to adapt to different user needs and installation methods
- Long operating time from the primary battery to reduce charging frequency and downtime
- Highly sensitive GPS reception to maintain location accuracy in difficult or obstructed environments
- Optional extended high capacity battery for longer continuous operation
- Compatibility with Plaspy for centralized device visibility and management

Plaspy provides a central platform to receive and present location and status information from compatible trackers like the Lommy Personal. - Real time and historical location visibility to support tracking and movement review
- Device labelling and grouping inside Plaspy to reflect personal use cases and organizational roles

- Manufacturer options such as extended batteries are reflected as longer online availability for devices within Plaspy
- Plaspy consolidates device reporting and presents data in a way that supports operational oversight and simple device management
