# Flextrack - Lommy Personal

![Lommy Personal](./logo.jpg)

The Flextrack Lommy Personal is a unique tracking device designed to ensure personal safety in a variety of settings. Whether you're a lone worker, a social worker, an elderly individual, or a parent concerned about your child's safety, the Lommy Personal is the perfect solution for you.

This device can be easily modified to meet your individual needs, providing you with the peace of mind you deserve. It boasts a powerful battery that ensures a long operating time between charges, so you can rely on it to keep you safe throughout the day. Even in difficult conditions, the Lommy Personal excels thanks to its highly sensitive GPS receiver, which allows for accurate tracking even in challenging environments.

For those who require an extended battery life, Flextrack offers a version of the Lommy Personal with a high-capacity extended battery. This ensures that you can stay connected and protected for even longer periods of time, without worrying about running out of power.

### Outstanding Features:

- Designed for personal safety in professional and private settings
- Easy to use and modify to match individual needs
- Powerful battery for long operating time between charges
- Highly sensitive GPS receiver for accurate tracking in difficult conditions
- Option for high-capacity extended battery for extended usage
