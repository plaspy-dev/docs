# Flextrack - Lommy Pro T

<div className="device-logo">
![Lommy Pro T](./tracker.png)
</div>

Lommy Pro T is a temperature monitoring and GPS tracker designed for high value, temperature sensitive shipments that require continuous cold chain oversight. It combines high precision temperature telemetry with reliable positioning and GSM triangulation to help operators verify storage conditions and locate assets while goods are in transit. The device's broad measurement range and continuous logging make it suitable for medicines, vaccines, and other regulated goods where documented handling is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, Lommy Pro T streams real time tracking and environmental telemetry into Plaspy via open APIs, enabling centralized monitoring and action. That compatibility makes it straightforward for fleet and cold chain teams to receive alarms, retain continuous documentation, and view location context alongside temperature history in Plaspy dashboards and reports.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for integration via open API to send temperature and location telemetry into monitoring platforms.
- Wide temperature measurement range for extreme cold chain environments suitable for regulated goods.
- Continuous data logging to maintain timestamped records for compliance and audit purposes.
- Dual location capabilities combining GPS positioning with GSM triangulation for improved asset visibility.
- Flexible power approach with internal battery and options to leverage external power sources for uninterrupted operation.
- Immediate alarm forwarding when configured temperature thresholds are exceeded to support rapid corrective action.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, Lommy Pro T pushes periodic temperature readings, event driven alarms, and location updates to Plaspy over the cellular network. Plaspy ingests and visualizes those streams so operators can monitor live conditions, investigate excursions, and archive certified records for compliance.

- Real time location and telemetry visibility in Plaspy map views and live status panels.
- Automated temperature alarms and excursion reports delivered to configured recipients and workflows.
- Historical trails and timestamped temperature logs available for reporting and forensic review.
- Centralized documentation for regulatory compliance with downloadable records from the Plaspy platform.
- Remote configuration and management through supported device APIs to adjust reporting intervals and alert thresholds.

## Typical Use Cases

- Vaccine and pharmaceutical transport requiring certified temperature documentation and location tracking.
- Refrigerated container and trailer shipments where continuous monitoring and alerts reduce spoilage risk.
- Cold storage rooms and warehouse oversight to detect excursions and speed corrective actions.
- Portable vaccine carriers and outreach distribution where both temperature control and location context matter.
- Asset protection and recovery for high value cold chain units using combined GPS and GSM positioning.

Lommy Pro T pairs environmental telemetry with location context to address both compliance and operational visibility for cold chain operations.
