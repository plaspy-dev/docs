# HunterPro - CP60G

![CP60G](./logo.jpg)

The HunterPro CP60G is a low cost GPS tracking device designed primarily for vehicle tracking and recovery. It uses GPS positioning together with GSM GPRS communication to provide location updates and event reporting. The device is offered with a durable plastic enclosure, GPS and GSM antennas, a backup battery for continued tracking, and inputs for common vehicle events such as ignition, panic, and door status. Optional connectivity options are available for fixed antennas and external sensors like fuel and temperature if projects require additional telemetry.

As a model compatible with Plaspy, the CP60G can send location and event data to a centralized fleet platform for monitoring and reporting. Its combination of time based position reporting, sleep mode to conserve power, and digital inputs for vehicle events makes it a practical choice for organizations that want tracked visibility without a high hardware cost. Plaspy can ingest the device data and present it alongside other fleet information to support operations, recovery, and oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Cost effective tracker suited for large scale deployments and budget conscious projects
- GPS positioning combined with GSM GPRS communication for wide area coverage
- Full quadband GSM support for broader regional connectivity
- Backup battery included to maintain tracking during power interruptions
- Multiple digital inputs with predefined functions for ignition panic and doors
- Time based position reporting and sleep mode to balance reporting and power use
- Durable plastic casing with supplied GPS and GSM antennas and options for additional sensors

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the CP60G streams location fixes and event messages to the platform where data is visualized, logged, and analyzed. Plaspy collects the information transmitted by the tracker and makes it available through maps, timelines, and reports to help teams manage vehicles and respond to incidents.

- Live and near real time location display on Plaspy maps for fleet visibility
- Event driven alerts and notifications based on inputs such as ignition panic and door activity
- Historical route playback and reporting for recovery investigations and compliance
- Fleet level overview and status monitoring to improve operational decision making
- Support for external sensor data such as fuel and temperature when those options are connected
