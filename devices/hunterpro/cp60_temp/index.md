# HunterPro - CP60-TEMP

![CP60-TEMP](./logo.jpg)

The HunterPro CP60-TEMP is a cutting-edge GPS tracker that is specifically designed for monitoring temperature in refrigerated trucks, containers, or storage facilities. With its advanced temperature sensors and remote data reading capabilities, this tracker allows you to effectively manage the cooling chain and ensure the optimal temperature of your cargo.

With the HunterPro CP60-TEMP, you can easily monitor and track the temperature of your goods in real-time from a remote monitoring station. This means that you can have complete visibility and control over the temperature conditions of your refrigerated assets, even when you are not physically present. This is especially crucial for industries such as food and pharmaceuticals, where maintaining the right temperature is essential for preserving the quality and safety of the products.

Key Features:

- Real-time temperature monitoring
- Remote data reading from a monitoring station
- Designed for refrigerated trucks, containers, and storage facilities
- Ensures optimal temperature of cargo
- Ideal for managing the cooling chain
