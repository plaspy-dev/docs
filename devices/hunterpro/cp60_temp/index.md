# HunterPro - CP60-TEMP

<div className="device-logo">
![CP60-TEMP](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The HunterPro CP60-TEMP is a GPS tracker engineered for monitoring temperature in refrigerated trucks, containers, and storage facilities. It combines GPS positioning with temperature sensing and remote data reading to help operators observe and manage thermal conditions along the cold chain. This device is intended to provide continuous visibility of cargo temperature alongside location information, supporting quality control for perishable goods.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CP60-TEMP can feed temperature and location information into the Plaspy platform for consolidated fleet and asset oversight. Integrating this tracker with Plaspy lets operations teams view temperature trends, receive condition alerts, and include temperature status in routing and reporting workflows, all from a centralized monitoring environment.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for temperature monitoring in refrigerated trucks, containers, and storage facilities
- Real time temperature monitoring and remote data reading for continuous visibility
- Combines location tracking with temperature data for integrated cold chain oversight
- Designed to help maintain optimal cargo conditions for food, pharmaceuticals, and other perishables
- Supports remote monitoring from a centralized station to reduce manual checks
- Useful for documenting temperature conditions for operational and quality reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the CP60-TEMP provides temperature and location updates that Plaspy translates into actionable views for fleet managers and logistics teams. Plaspy displays the device data in dashboards and timelines so teams can monitor cargo conditions alongside vehicle movements.

- Centralized visualization of temperature readings and asset location within Plaspy dashboards
- Configurable alerts and notifications based on temperature thresholds and condition changes
- Historical reporting for cold chain audits and compliance reviews
- Combined operational views for routing, status monitoring, and exception handling
- Aggregated fleet-level monitoring to identify recurring temperature issues or route risks
