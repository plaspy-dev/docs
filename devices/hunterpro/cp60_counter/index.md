# HunterPro - CP60-COUNTER

With advanced location and real-time data transmission technology, this unit allows you to easily control and manage passenger information from your computer. The information is securely sent through GPRS, ensuring reliable and accurate data transmission.

- Real-Time Data Transmission: With GPRS connectivity, the tracker enables seamless and instant transmission of passenger information to your computer.
- Efficient Passenger Management: The CP60-COUNTER allows you to easily monitor and control the entry and exit of passengers, ensuring accurate passenger counts and efficient operations.
- User-Friendly Interface: The tracker comes with a user-friendly interface that makes it easy to navigate and access important information.
- Reliable Performance: Built with high-quality materials, the CP60-COUNTER is designed to withstand harsh environments and deliver reliable performance.

With the HunterPro CP60-COUNTER GPS tracker, you can effectively manage passenger information, improve operational efficiency, and ensure a smooth and hassle-free passenger transport experience.
