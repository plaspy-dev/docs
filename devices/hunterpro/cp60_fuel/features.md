# HunterPro - CP60-FUEL Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the HunterPro CP60-FUEL and how its fuel measurement capabilities can be used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about what the device reports and how those reports can support fuel monitoring and operational visibility inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and the way readings appear in Plaspy can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For precise integration details and the latest device behavior, consult the official HunterPro documentation and your device firmware release notes.

## Feature Overview

The CP60-FUEL is engineered to measure fuel level in transport unit tanks using a coaxial capacitor probe and to convert that measurement into digital pulses that represent tank capacity. The device is intended to provide reliable level data that can be translated into calibrated fuel volumes for monitoring and reporting.

- Accurate fuel level measurement using a coaxial capacitor probe
- Probe output converted into digital pulses for clear numeric readings
- Linear relationship between probe capacity and fuel level for predictable measurements
- Tank calibration procedure to map sensor capacity to actual fuel volume
- Designed for easy installation and use in transport unit tanks
- Compatible with Plaspy for centralized reporting and monitoring

## Core Features of HunterPro - CP60-FUEL

- Coaxial capacitor probe design where the outer tube and inner electrode form the sensing capacitor
- Measurement principle where capacitance changes proportionally with fuel level
- Electronic circuitry that converts capacitance into digital pulse output
- Support for tank calibration to align sensor readings with actual tank geometry and volume
- Intended use for measuring fuel level in transport unit tanks
- User oriented design to simplify installation and calibration procedures

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

The CP60-FUEL provides calibrated fuel level information that Plaspy can display and archive for operational use. When the device reports fuel readings into Plaspy, the platform can present those values alongside vehicle location and operational context to help teams understand fuel status over time.

- Display of current fuel level and calibrated fuel volume as reported by the device
- Historical fuel level graphs and trend visualization for consumption and filling events
- Correlation of fuel readings with vehicle location and activity records in Plaspy
- Storage of calibration parameters and reported pulses as part of device telemetry
- Exportable fuel data for offline analysis and reporting from Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet managers tracking tank fuel levels across transport vehicles
- Verifying fuel deliveries and tank fills through calibrated volume readings
- Monitoring fuel on dedicated tankers or bulk transport units
- Keeping consolidated fuel records in Plaspy for accounting and logistics
- Using calibrated data to reconcile fuel inventories and operational reports

## Feature Availability Notes

- Tank calibration is required to convert measured capacitance into accurate fuel volume for each tank geometry
- Firmware revisions and hardware variants can change how the probe output is processed and reported
- Installation method and tank construction influence reading accuracy and may require professional setup
- Regional product variants and manufacturer configuration options can affect available features
- Always check HunterPro documentation for model specific instructions and the latest firmware behavior

Using the HunterPro CP60-FUEL with Plaspy gives organizations a single platform to collect, visualize, and analyze fuel level data from transport unit tanks. Plaspy can help surface calibrated fuel volumes, historical trends, and location context alongside other fleet telemetry to support operational decision making.
