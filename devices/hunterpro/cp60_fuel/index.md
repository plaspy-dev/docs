# HunterPro - CP60-FUEL

![CP60-FUEL](./logo.jpg)

The HunterPro CP60-FUEL is a GPS tracker specially designed for measuring the fuel level in tanks of transport units. With its innovative probe technology, this tracker provides accurate and reliable fuel level measurements. The probe is designed as a coaxial capacitor with a metallic tube serving as the external electrode and a central electrode as the inner electrode. When immersed in the fuel tank, the sensor probe operates as a capacitor, and its capacity linearly depends on the fuel level in the tank.

The CP60-FUEL also includes electronic circuitry that transforms the capacity of the sensor into digital pulses, allowing for easy and precise fuel volume determination. To ensure accurate readings, the tank calibration procedure should be performed to establish the correspondence between fuel volume and the measured sensor capacity. With its advanced technology and user-friendly design, the HunterPro CP60-FUEL is the ideal solution for monitoring fuel levels in transport units.

#### Key Features:

- Accurate fuel level measurement
- Coaxial capacitor probe technology
- Linear transformation of capacity into digital pulses
- Tank calibration procedure for precise fuel volume determination
- User-friendly design
