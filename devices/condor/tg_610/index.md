# Condor - TG-610

![TG-610](./tracker.png)

The TG-610 is a compact GPS tracker engineered for motorcycles and small vehicles, now listed as Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your tracking and fleet workflows. Its low-power design, integrated high-sensitivity GPS and GPRS antennas, and simple I/O make the TG-610 a practical choice for owners and fleet managers who need reliable real-time tracking, anti-theft control, and straightforward telemetry over cellular networks.

Designed for easy installation and everyday use, the TG-610 pairs with Plaspy to turn location and status data into actionable insights. Whether your priority is theft prevention with an immobilizer, quick emergency response via a panic button, or basic vehicle telemetry for a small motorcycle fleet, TG-610 provides the essential features to get you connected to Plaspy’s platform fast.

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for motorcycles and small vehicles, enabling real-time tracking and centralized fleet management.

- Dedicated inputs/outputs for engine blocking \(immobilizer\) and a panic button to support anti-theft and emergency workflows.

- Delivers telemetry and status signals over GPRS to Plaspy for timely alerts and reporting.
- Robust for everyday use: built to operate reliably in motorcycle and small vehicle installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TG-610 transmits location and status information over the cellular GPRS network to Plaspy, enabling real-time tracking and integration into fleet dashboards and alerting systems. Plaspy ingests the device’s telemetry and I/O events to provide geofencing, live map updates, and event-driven notifications such as panic alerts or immobilizer activations.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates are sent over GPRS for continuous tracking on Plaspy.
- Immobilizer/engine-block control and panic button events are reported to Plaspy to trigger anti-theft workflows or emergency notifications.
- Telemetry data \(movement, stop/start events, and status inputs\) is available in Plaspy reports and alerts.
- Integrates into fleet management workflows for small fleets of motorcycles and compact vehicles using Plaspy’s dashboards and reporting tools.
- Can complement fuel monitoring or Bluetooth sensor data within Plaspy when those external systems are present and integrated into the platform.
