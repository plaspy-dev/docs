# Condor - TG-610

![TG-610](./tracker.png)

The TG-610 is a compact GPS tracker engineered for motorcycles and small vehicles. It combines a high sensitivity GPS antenna and GPRS connectivity in a small form factor with low power design and simple I/O. The device is intended to provide reliable real time location reporting, basic telemetry and anti theft control for owners and fleet managers who need a discreet tracking solution for two wheel and compact vehicle applications.

As a device listed as Plaspy compatible, the TG-610 can be integrated into Plaspy’s monitoring and fleet workflows to turn location and status signals into actionable insights. Its support for engine blocking inputs and a panic button, along with routine telemetry over cellular networks, makes the TG-610 a practical option when pairing hardware with Plaspy for live tracking, alerts, and straightforward fleet oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker optimized for motorcycles and small vehicles for real time tracking and centralized monitoring.
- Integrated high sensitivity GPS and GPRS antennas for consistent location updates over cellular networks.
- Low power design suitable for use on two wheel vehicles where power management is important.
- Dedicated inputs and outputs for engine blocking and a panic button to support anti theft and emergency workflows.
- Compact form factor that reduces visual impact and simplifies discreet installation on motorcycles.
- Provides telemetry and status signals over GPRS so Plaspy can generate timely alerts and reports.
- Built for everyday use in motorcycle and small vehicle environments.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the TG-610 sends location and status information over the cellular GPRS network so Plaspy can display live positions, log events, and trigger notifications. Plaspy ingests the tracker’s telemetry and I/O events to support geofencing, alerting, and fleet reporting that help operators respond quickly to incidents and monitor ongoing activity.

- Live location updates and movement status appear on Plaspy maps for real time visibility.
- Panic button activations and immobilizer events are reported to Plaspy to trigger alerts and incident workflows.
- Telemetry and input state changes are available in Plaspy reports for operational analysis and audit trails.
- Geofence and event rules in Plaspy can be applied to TG-610 data to automate notifications and escalation.
- Integration supports small fleet dashboards and routine oversight through Plaspy reporting and history features.
