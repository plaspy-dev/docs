# Condor - TA-913

![TA-913](./tracker.png)

The Condor TA-913 is a compact aviation communications and tracking device designed for fixed wing and rotary wing operations. Built to support in flight safety and ground station integration, the TA-913 provides pilot friendly controls, dedicated event buttons, satellite text messaging capability, and a Bluetooth gateway for pairing with mobile devices. Its design targets straightforward crew use and resilient messaging when conventional voice links are limited.

As a device that is Plaspy compatible, the TA-913 can feed messages and event signals into Plaspy for unified visibility across operations. When paired with Plaspy, TA-913 status messages, emergency inputs, and satellite text communications can be displayed, logged, and used in fleet workflows to improve situational awareness and operational oversight for aircraft operators.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for aviation operations including fixed wing and rotary wing aircraft.
- Pilot friendly buttons for quick actions such as panic alerts and point of interest marking.
- Ground call notification capability to reestablish crew to ground contact when radio links are disrupted.
- Satellite text messaging for short crew to ground exchanges when other communication methods are unavailable.
- Bluetooth gateway to pair a smartphone or tablet as a user interface and messaging conduit.
- Intuitive controls and compact form factor designed to reduce crew training time and improve readiness.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TA-913 becomes part of a single operational picture for aircraft fleets. Plaspy ingests the TA-913’s status messages, event inputs, and satellite text communications, correlates them with other operational data, and surfaces them in dashboards and logs for dispatchers and operations teams.

- Real time alerts and message relay so operators see safety and status messages as they arrive.
- Panic and point of interest events forwarded to Plaspy to support rapid dispatcher response and coordination.
- Satellite text messaging archived in Plaspy for traceability and operational review.
- Bluetooth paired devices act as the in flight interface while Plaspy captures the resulting communications and events.
- Event and message capture enables reporting, auditing, and integration with fleet management workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Emergency signaling and rapid response coordination between crew and ground control.
- Loss of primary radio contingency to maintain crew to ground communications via satellite text.
- Marking and transmitting critical locations during missions for mapping and debrief.
- Short crew to ground messaging when line of sight communications are compromised.
- Centralized fleet operational awareness by bringing device events into Plaspy dashboards.
