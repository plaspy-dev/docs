# Condor

Condor produces GPS tracking devices used across vehicle, asset, and equipment tracking applications. Plaspy integrates with Condor devices so customers can view location, status, and alerts from these trackers inside a single fleet management platform. This page explains how Condor devices work with Plaspy, common capabilities you can expect, and how to navigate to compatible models.

<BrandCatalog brand={brand} />

## How Condor devices work with Plaspy

Condor devices transmit location and telematics messages that Plaspy ingests, decodes, and presents in the Plaspy dashboard. Once a device is registered in Plaspy, the platform receives periodic updates and event messages and converts them into map positions, trip histories, and configurable alerts. Plaspy normalizes incoming data so you can manage Condor devices alongside other brands without switching tools.

If you need setup help, individual device pages linked from the catalog provide model specific instructions such as server settings or data forwarding requirements.

## Common features found in Condor devices

While features vary by model, Condor devices commonly offer:
- Real time GPS position reporting and location history
- Cellular connectivity for continuous data transmission
- Battery backup or internal power management for asset tracking
- Motion and vibration sensing for movement detection
- Configurable reporting intervals to balance precision and battery life
- Inputs and outputs for ignition, door or accessory monitoring
- Basic alerting for geofence, overspeed, low battery, or tampering

These common capabilities make Condor hardware suitable for a wide range of tracking tasks and allow Plaspy to provide rich monitoring and automation.

## Typical use cases for Condor GPS trackers

Condor devices are typically used for:
- Vehicle fleet tracking for route visibility and driver monitoring
- Heavy equipment and asset tracking to prevent loss and optimize utilization
- Rental or leased vehicle tracking with location history and status checks
- Logistics visibility for cargo and trailer monitoring
- Remote asset monitoring where cellular coverage is available

Plaspy adds reporting, alerting, and operational tools that help turn raw device data into actionable insights.
