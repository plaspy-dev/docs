# Condor - TG-512

![TG-512](./tracker.png)

The TG-512 is a compact GPS tracker designed for vehicles and industrial machinery. It combines high-sensitivity integrated GPS and GPRS antennas with flexible inputs and outputs to transmit location and telemetry over the cellular network. The unit includes interfaces commonly used to connect sensors and on‑board equipment, making it suitable for a range of telematics tasks from routine fleet oversight to equipment monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TG-512 can act as a reliable data source for the Plaspy platform. Its telemetry and event signals can be ingested into Plaspy to provide live location, historical playback, alerts, and operational reporting. The TG-512’s adaptable I/O options make it practical for users who need to bring vehicle events and external sensor data into Plaspy workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Compact vehicle and machinery form factor suited to fleet and asset deployments.
- Integrated high sensitivity GPS and GPRS antennas for dependable position reporting.
- Multiple general purpose inputs and outputs to capture event status and control external equipment.
- RS-232 serial interface for connection to third party telematics modules or sensors.
- 1-wire support for compatible sensors and identification devices.
- Microphone and speaker outputs for local audio or two-way voice scenarios.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TG-512 supplies position fixes and telemetry to the platform where managers can monitor assets, respond to alerts, and analyze historical behavior. Plaspy displays incoming device data alongside fleet management tools so teams can turn raw device signals into operational insight.

- Live location updates and historical playback available in Plaspy for route review and audits.
- Event monitoring from general purpose inputs and outputs enables status alerts such as ignition, door, or alarm conditions.
- Sensor telemetry from compatible devices connected via RS-232 or 1-wire can be presented in Plaspy dashboards and reports.
- Alerts and rules in Plaspy can be triggered by device events to support operational workflows.
- Outputs on the TG-512 can be used to implement remote control actions managed through Plaspy when configured by a qualified technician.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for vehicles that require continuous location visibility and historical tracking.
- Anti-theft monitoring and immobilization workflows leveraging device outputs and Plaspy alerts.
- Monitoring of heavy machinery and construction equipment to reduce downtime and track assets on site.
- Telemetry aggregation such as fuel or temperature data through compatible external sensors.
- Driver communication or local audio monitoring tied into Plaspy event workflows.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TG-512 offers a practical balance of compact hardware and flexible connectivity that fits many Plaspy deployments. Its integrated antennas reduce external complexity while the RS-232 and 1-wire interfaces provide options to expand telemetry without replacing core hardware. Multiple inputs and outputs let organizations capture event-driven signals and implement control logic through Plaspy when professionally integrated.

For fleets and asset managers seeking a straightforward device to feed location, event status, and external sensor data into Plaspy, the TG-512 is a sensible option. Its adaptability to vehicle and machinery use cases makes it a useful component of a broader telematics solution managed through Plaspy.
