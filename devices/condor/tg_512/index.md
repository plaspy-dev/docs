# TG-512 GPS Tracker

The TG-512 is a compact, vehicle- and machinery-focused GPS tracker designed to integrate with Plaspy for reliable, Plaspy compatible fleet monitoring. Built around high-sensitivity integrated GPS and GPRS antennas, the TG-512 transmits location and telemetry over the cellular network and offers flexible interfaces for sensors and vehicle inputs/outputs.

Whether you need real-time tracking for fleet management, anti-theft monitoring, or telemetry collection from specialized equipment, the TG-512 provides the connectivity and I/O options to feed Plaspy with the data your operations depend on. Its RS-232 and 1-wire ports plus multiple general-purpose I/O make it adaptable to a wide range of telematics and sensor use cases.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with high-sensitivity integrated GPS and GPRS antennas for dependable location updates.
- Real-time tracking and telemetry transmission over the cellular network for fleet management and asset oversight.
- Multiple general-purpose inputs/outputs enable vehicle event monitoring and control when configured through Plaspy.
- RS-232 serial port for connection to external telematics modules, third-party sensors, or on-board equipment.
- 1-wire interface supports compatible one-wire sensors and identification devices \(for driver ID or simple sensor integration\).
- Audio outputs \(microphone and speaker\) enable two-way voice or local audio monitoring where required.
- Compact form factor suited to vehicles and heavy machinery installations, minimizing installation complexity.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the TG-512 becomes a data source for location, event status, and sensor telemetry. The device transmits GNSS fixes and telemetry via GPRS to Plaspy's platform, where managers can view live position, configure alerts, and run reports for operational insight. Plaspy ingests device data over standard telematics channels and displays it alongside fleet management tools.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and historical playback.
- Vehicle event and status monitoring via general-purpose inputs/outputs — usable for ignition, door, or alarm status when wired to those circuits.
- Sensor telemetry \(fuel, temperature, etc.\) available to Plaspy through devices or sensors connected over RS-232 or 1-wire when compatible sensors are used.
- Remote control capability \(such as immobilizer or ignition cut\) can be implemented through the TG-512’s outputs and managed from Plaspy when professionally wired and configured.
- Audio channel support via microphone and speaker outputs can be used for driver communication or local monitoring alongside Plaspy event workflows.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management: real-time GPS tracker data in Plaspy for route oversight, dispatch, and utilization tracking.
- Anti-theft and immobilization: monitor unauthorized movement and implement immobilizer strategies via outputs when professionally integrated.
- Heavy machinery and construction equipment monitoring: collect location and operational events to reduce downtime and theft.
- Telemetry and fuel monitoring: feed external sensor data into Plaspy via RS-232 or 1-wire interfaces for fuel monitoring and engine telemetry.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TG-512 pairs practical hardware flexibility with Plaspy’s platform to deliver a cost-effective telematics solution. Its integrated GPS and GPRS antennas simplify installations while the RS-232 and 1-wire ports give you options to connect third-party sensors and telematics modules for expanded telemetry. Multiple general-purpose inputs and outputs enable event-driven alerts and control, supporting operational needs from ignition monitoring to remote immobilization when installed and configured by a qualified technician.

Choosing the TG-512 for Plaspy-compatible deployments helps fleets and asset managers achieve reliable real-time tracking, better telemetry visibility, and anti-theft capabilities without unnecessary complexity. For organizations that require additional sensor types — including Bluetooth sensors — the TG-512 can be part of a broader solution architecture \(e.g., using gateways or adapters\) to bring diverse data streams into Plaspy and centralize fleet management, fuel monitoring, and vehicle health reporting.
