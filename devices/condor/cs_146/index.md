# Condor - CS-146

<div className="device-logo">
![CS-146](./tracker.png)
</div>

The CS-146 is a purpose built GPS tracker for container control and cargo security. Designed to monitor container position and status, the CS-146 transmits location and operational messages over cellular or satellite networks to provide reliable tracking even across mixed transport legs. Its form factor and feature set are focused on protecting cargo and supporting container access workflows.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CS-146 can feed its position and event information into Plaspy for centralized visibility and operational oversight. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s updates appear in live maps, alert systems, and reporting tools so logistics teams can monitor containers, respond to access events, and maintain an audit trail for shipments.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for container monitoring and cargo security with a compact design for container installation
- Transmits position and status using cellular or satellite paths to support wide area coverage
- Remote opening capability enables controlled access from a central control room
- Helps reduce theft risk by centralizing access control and delivering timely status updates
- Integrates with Plaspy to provide live tracking, alerts, and historical location playback
- Suitable for intermodal operations where transport modes and coverage vary
- Supports operational auditing by reporting open and close events for chain of custody

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the CS-146 is paired with Plaspy, the device sends periodic and event driven position and status updates to the platform. Plaspy ingests those messages to populate live maps, power alerts and workflows, and produce reports that operations teams use to manage containers in real time.

- Real time tracking and history playback so teams can view current location and movement over time
- Remote open and close events reported into Plaspy for audit logs and triggerable alerts
- Status messages used as telemetry inputs to drive notifications and operational rules
- Anti theft alerting for unexpected access events routed to operators through Plaspy workflows
- Integration friendly design so CS-146 data can be combined with other telemetry sources in Plaspy dashboards

## Typical Use Cases

- Intermodal fleet management for consolidated visibility across truck rail and sea legs
- Anti theft and access control to verify and manage container door activity from a control room
- Supply chain visibility for high value or time sensitive shipments requiring continuous oversight
- Extended coverage scenarios using satellite fallback where cellular service is intermittent
- Operational audits and chain of custody reporting using event logs and status history

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CS-146 aligns with fleet and cargo security programs that need focused container level visibility. Paired with Plaspy, it provides a straightforward way to bring container position and access events into centralized dashboards, alerting, and reporting workflows used by operations teams. That combination helps streamline monitoring and response without adding unnecessary integration complexity.
