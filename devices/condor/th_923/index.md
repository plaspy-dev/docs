# Condor - TH-923

![TH-923](./tracker.png)

The TH-923 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for continuous monitoring of heavy machinery, maritime vessels, containers and mobile units that transport high-value cargo. Designed to keep assets visible in both urban and remote environments, the TH-923 combines cellular and Iridium satellite transmission to provide uninterrupted location and status updates, making it ideal for fleet management, anti-theft protection and long-haul logistics.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TH-923 integrates with Plaspy's real-time tracking platform so fleet managers and security teams receive timely telemetry, alerts and location history. Its automatic failover from cellular to the Iridium satellite network ensures that position and event data continue to flow to Plaspy even when cellular coverage is unavailable — a crucial capability for maritime operations, rural worksites and cross-border transport.

## Key Highlights

- Dual-link communications: normal operation over cellular networks with automatic failover to Iridium satellite for continuous coverage.
- Designed for remote and maritime environments where persistent connectivity matters for fleet management and theft prevention.
- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking, geofencing, alerts and reporting — keeps telemetry flowing into the platform.
- Optimized for high-value cargo, containers and heavy equipment that require reliable anti-theft monitoring and traceability.
- Supports continuous position updates to maintain situational awareness during long transits or operations in coverage gaps.
- Deployable across mixed fleets — suitable for trucks, worksite machinery, vessels and portable asset installations.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TH-923 transmits location and status data to Plaspy using cellular networks under normal conditions. When the cellular link is lost or signal levels fall below operational thresholds, the device automatically switches to the Iridium satellite network so Plaspy continues to receive position fixes and event telemetry. This hybrid connectivity model enables continuous real-time tracking and reliable alert delivery for remote and maritime deployments.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for mapping, history and reports.
- Automatic cellular-to-Iridium failover preserves tracking continuity in coverage gaps and at sea.
- Continuous position and event data support fleet management dashboards, route replay and compliance logs.
- Geofence and motion alerts from the device are routed into Plaspy for immediate notification and response.
- Plaspy can ingest and display ignition, fuel or immobilizer telemetry and Bluetooth sensor data when those signals are provided by the installed tracker or vehicle wiring — the TH-923’s persistent link helps ensure those fields arrive in remote areas.
