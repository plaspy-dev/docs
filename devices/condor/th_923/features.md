# Condor - TH-923 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Condor TH-923 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities relevant to Plaspy users, including how the TH-923 delivers location and status updates into the Plaspy platform for mapping, alerts, and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. Where the device supports optional telemetry or wiring-dependent signals, those functions will depend on the specific model variant, the installation performed, and any accessory options chosen.

## Feature Overview

The TH-923 is designed to provide persistent tracking for high-value and distributed assets by combining cellular connectivity with Iridium satellite fallback. In practice, this means reduced blind spots and continued telemetry when cellular service is unavailable, helping maintain situational awareness for fleets, maritime operations, and remote worksites.

- Dual-link communications with cellular operation and automatic Iridium satellite failover for continuous tracking
- Real-time location updates and status reporting into Plaspy for mapping and history
- Optimized for heavy machinery, containers, vessels, and mobile units carrying high-value cargo
- Designed to support anti-theft monitoring, route visibility, and long-transit traceability
- Deployable across mixed fleets to provide a unified tracking feed into Plaspy

## Core Features of Condor - TH-923

- Hybrid connectivity combining cellular transmission with automatic Iridium satellite fallback
- Continuous GNSS-based positioning for location reporting and route history
- Automatic failover behavior to preserve location and event telemetry in coverage gaps
- Plaspy compatibility for real-time tracking, geofencing, alerts, and reporting
- Designed form factor and ruggedization for vehicle, container, and maritime installations
- Support for wiring-dependent telemetry fields such as ignition, fuel related signals, immobilizer events, and Bluetooth sensor data when those inputs are provided by the installation
- Suitable configuration and deployment for mixed fleet and long-haul logistics scenarios

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When installed and commissioned, the TH-923 transmits location and event data to Plaspy so operators can view live positions, replay history, and receive alerts. The device’s hybrid connectivity helps ensure that Plaspy continues to receive updates when cellular coverage is lost by switching to satellite transmission.

- Real-time location and telemetry appear in Plaspy for live mapping and historical replay
- Automatic cellular to Iridium failover maintains continuity of position and event data into Plaspy
- Telemetry and events support dashboard visibility, route replay, and reporting in Plaspy
- Geofence and motion alerts from the device are routed into Plaspy for notifications and workflows
- Plaspy can ingest wiring-dependent signals such as ignition, fuel related fields, immobilizer events, and Bluetooth sensor inputs when those signals are provided by the installed tracker

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti-theft monitoring and response workflows for long-haul trucks and trailers operating in remote regions
- Maritime vessel tracking where cellular coverage is intermittent and satellite continuity is required
- Container and cargo monitoring during ocean or cross-border transits to maintain chain of custody
- Heavy machinery and mining equipment oversight at rural worksites to keep location history and status logs
- High-value mobile units that require resilient telemetry across mixed coverage environments

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature sets may vary by firmware version; behaviors such as failover timing and data throttling can be firmware dependent
- Hardware revisions and optional accessory packages affect available I/O, backup power options, and mounting choices
- Installation type determines which wiring-dependent signals are available to Plaspy, for example ignition or immobilizer inputs
- Regional cellular support depends on operator coverage and the specific cellular variant supplied for a given market
- Confirm Bluetooth sensor support, remote management capabilities, and any variant-specific details with the manufacturer or your reseller
