# Condor - GE-810

<div className="device-logo">
![GE-810](./tracker.png)
</div>

The GE-810 is a Plaspy compatible personal monitoring device designed for controlled supervision environments. It combines modern 4G LTE cellular connectivity with a rugged IP67 housing and a wearable shackle style enclosure. On-device features include secure two way voice, vibration alerts, and tamper or removal notifications, making the GE-810 suitable for programs that require continuous oversight and timely event reporting.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GE-810 is intended to surface critical telemetry, status alerts, and communications to monitoring teams through the Plaspy platform. Its wearable form factor and event driven alerts align with Plaspy workflows for on-person monitoring, incident response, and centralized reporting so supervisors can maintain visibility into device state and wearer communications.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible device for centralized monitoring and consolidated reporting
- 4G LTE cellular connectivity for reliable voice and data transmission
- Two way voice to enable direct communication between wearer and supervisors
- Vibration alert for discreet notifications and acknowledgement processes
- Tamper and removal detection that generates immediate alerts for supervisors
- IP67 rated waterproof enclosure for durability in wet and outdoor conditions
- Wearable shackle form factor tailored to supervised release and secure monitoring programs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the GE-810 is used with Plaspy, device events and status updates are sent to the Plaspy platform so monitoring teams receive timely information and can maintain operational oversight. Plaspy records alerts and event logs, presents device state to supervisors, and enables coordinated responses based on the GE-810s notifications and communications. Where location data is available from the device or paired systems, Plaspy can combine that information with GE-810 events for broader situational awareness.

- Real time alerts and event updates delivered to Plaspy for active monitoring and audit trails
- Two way voice event logs integrated into incident handling and supervisor workflows
- Tamper and removal alerts pushed to teams to trigger established response procedures
- Vibration alerts used for discreet warnings, compliance prompts, or acknowledgement flows
- Device events correlated with other tracker feeds and telemetry inside Plaspy for unified oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Supervised release and court mandated monitoring programs that need tamper alerts and direct communications
- Community corrections and case management where lightweight wearable monitoring is required
- Welfare and vulnerable person oversight using discreet vibration alerts and voice contact for checks
- Temporary custody or transport monitoring where durability and reliable communications are needed
