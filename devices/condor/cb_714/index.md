# Condor - CB-714

<div className="device-logo">
![CB-714](./tracker.png)
</div>

The CB-714 is a compact GPS tracker designed specifically for bicycle control and rider telemetry. Tailored to casual cyclists, commuters, competitive riders and bike rental fleets, the model emphasizes route visualization, precise distance measurement and accurate trip duration so rides can be monitored, usage verified and performance analyzed with confidence.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the CB-714 integrates its cycling telemetry into the Plaspy platform to provide consolidated route maps, trip logs and duration summaries. Its focused feature set makes it a practical choice for operators and riders who want straightforward cycling data presented inside Plaspy for reporting, alerts and historical analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built for bicycles with emphasis on route visualization distance and trip duration
- Plaspy compatible for consolidated tracking reports and map visualization
- Precise route mapping to support post ride review and replay
- Accurate distance and duration metrics useful for analytics and usage billing
- Well suited for bike fleets and rental operations to monitor activity
- Enables anti theft monitoring with location history and movement alerts
- Simple telemetry focused design without unnecessary vehicle specific inputs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the CB-714 delivers recorded cycling telemetry to the platform where route maps distance totals and ride durations are displayed and stored. Operators and riders can access historical rides produce reports and configure alerts inside Plaspy, enabling both real time visibility and detailed post ride analysis depending on how the device is set up to transmit data.

- Real time position updates and current ride visibility when configured to report live location
- Route history and trip replay to review full paths taken during each ride
- Distance and duration metrics appear in Plaspy dashboards reports and exportable logs
- Alerting for movement or unauthorized use to support anti theft monitoring and response
- Fleet level summaries and activity reports to track utilization and support billing or maintenance planning

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for rental and shared bike programs to track usage and aggregate activity
- Anti theft monitoring and recovery by monitoring last known locations and movement patterns
- Rider training and route analytics for coaching and performance review
- Event and tour support to provide route logs and participant tracking for organizers
- Operational maintenance planning using distance totals to schedule servicing for fleets

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CB-714 offers a focused set of cycling telemetry that pairs naturally with Plaspy for ride centric visibility and fleet oversight. Its emphasis on clear route maps distance accuracy and trip duration makes it useful for operators who need straightforward data for reporting billing and operational decision making without vehicle specific complexity.
