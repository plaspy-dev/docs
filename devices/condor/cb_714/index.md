# Condor - CB-714

![CB-714](./tracker.png)

The CB-714 is a GPS tracker designed specifically for bicycle control and rider telemetry, and is Plaspy compatible for seamless integration into your tracking dashboard. Tailored to meet the needs of casual cyclists, commuters, competitive riders and bike-rental fleets, the CB-714 focuses on route visualization, exact distance measurement and accurate trip duration so you can monitor rides, verify usage and analyze performance with confidence.

When paired with Plaspy, the CB-714 becomes a compact solution for bicycle fleet management and anti-theft monitoring—delivering clear route maps, trip logs and duration summaries into the Plaspy platform. Its purpose-built design emphasizes rider-focused tracking and straightforward data that works with Plaspy’s tools for reporting, alerts and historical analysis.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The CB-714 sends the cycling telemetry it records to Plaspy, where route maps, distance totals and ride duration are displayed and stored. Plaspy-compatible configuration lets operators and riders access historical rides, generate reports and receive alerts through the standard Plaspy interface. Depending on how the device is configured to transmit location data, Plaspy can present near real-time position updates or detailed trip history for post-ride analysis.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(when the device is configured to transmit location data to Plaspy\).
- Route history and trip replay — full route visualization for each recorded ride in Plaspy.
- Distance and duration telemetry — exact distance and total ride time appear in Plaspy reports and dashboards.
