# Sentar - Q60 Tracker

![Q60 Tracker](./tracker.png)

The Q60 is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker built for reliable personal and asset location monitoring. With a small form factor and multi-mode positioning \(GPS, BDS, LBS and WiFi\), the Q60 provides consistent real-time tracking that fits child safety, elderly care, personal security, and movable asset monitoring needs. Its MTK2503 chipset and GSM/GPRS connectivity make it an efficient GPS tracker for daily use.

Designed to integrate with Plaspy out of the box, the Q60 streams location updates to Plaspy for mapping, alerts, and historical reports. The device balances accurate GPS positioning \(typically 5–15 meters\) with network-based fallbacks \(LBS and WiFi\) to keep telemetry flowing even when satellite signals are limited. If you’re adding Plaspy-compatible hardware to a monitoring program, the Q60 is an entry-level option that emphasizes compact size, battery endurance, and dependable location data.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless integration with real-time tracking dashboards and alerts.
- Hybrid positioning: GPS + BDS + LBS + WiFi for consistent location fixes in varied conditions.
- Compact, unobtrusive form factor ideal for personal carry, children, seniors, and small assets.
- GSM/GPRS connectivity across 850/900/1800/1900 MHz ensures wide network support.
- Built-in 630mAh Li‑Po battery for extended standby operation and long unattended use.
- MTK2503 chipset delivering reliable GNSS performance and efficient power use.
- Simple installation using a Nano SIM slot and modest memory footprint \(4MB RAM + 4MB flash\).

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Q60 transmits position reports and basic telemetry to the Plaspy platform where you can monitor devices in real time, configure geofences, and generate location histories. Plaspy ingests the Q60’s hybrid positioning data so you get continuous visibility whether the unit relies on GPS satellites, local base station triangulation \(LBS\), or WiFi positioning.

- Real-time tracking: Frequent location updates visible on Plaspy maps and mobile apps.
- Hybrid positioning: GPS/BDS for GNSS fixes, LBS and WiFi for fallback location when satellite signals are weak.
- Telemetry support: Plaspy receives device status such as power/session indicators and can surface low-battery notifications.
- Alerts & geofencing: Configure entry/exit alerts, movement alarms, and location-based notifications via Plaspy.
- Reports & history: Exportable travel history and activity logs for compliance or analysis in Plaspy.

The Q60 offers a pragmatic balance of accuracy, portability, and battery life for anyone adopting Plaspy as their tracking platform. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, it delivers dependable real-time tracking and hybrid positioning that keeps telemetry flowing in urban, suburban, and rural environments.
