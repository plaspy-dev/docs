# Sentar - L80

![L80](./logo.jpg)

The Sentar L80 3G Kids GPS Watch is a reliable and feature-packed device designed to keep your child safe and give you peace of mind. With its advanced technology and user-friendly design, this GPS watch is the perfect companion for your little one.

The L80 is equipped with a powerful MT6572A chipset, ensuring fast and accurate location tracking. It supports multiple location modes, including GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi, allowing you to track your child's whereabouts with precision. Whether they are at school, the park, or a friend's house, you can easily monitor their location in real-time.

Available in blue and pink, the Sentar L80 3G Kids GPS Watch is not only functional but also stylish. It features a comfortable and adjustable strap, making it suitable for children of all ages. The watch is also equipped with a variety of useful features, including a built-in microphone and speaker for two-way communication, an SOS button for emergencies, and a geo-fence function to set safe zones for your child.

With its reliable performance, advanced features, and stylish design, the Sentar L80 3G Kids GPS Watch is the perfect choice for parents who want to ensure their child's safety and stay connected at all times.

### Outstanding Features:

- MT6572A chipset for fast and accurate location tracking
- Supports GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi location modes
- Available in blue and pink
- Built-in microphone and speaker for two-way communication
- SOS button for emergencies
- Geo-fence function to set safe zones
