# Sentar - D38 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Sentar D38 and how its capabilities map into the Plaspy platform. It focuses on high level, non sensitive descriptions of what the D38 offers and how those features are typically used when the device is connected to Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and historical playback.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and how the device is installed or provisioned. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes consult the official manufacturer resources referenced at the end of this page.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar D38 is a compact Android based kids smartwatch designed for child safety and caregiver visibility. It combines multiple positioning sources, cellular connectivity, an SOS button, two way communication capability and an on device camera to offer a practical wearable endpoint that reports location and status into Plaspy.

- Multi mode positioning with GPS AGPS LBS and WiFi to improve coverage indoors and outdoors
- Real time tracking and location history reporting when paired with Plaspy
- Dedicated SOS button for immediate alerting of caregivers and rapid location sharing
- On device camera and two way communication capability for visual confirmation and contact
- IPX7 rating plus magnetic charging and Nano SIM support for daily wearability and simple setup

## Core Features of Sentar - D38

- GPS AGPS LBS and WiFi positioning for complementary location signals
- 4G 3G 2G cellular connectivity with Nano SIM support for broad network availability
- Dedicated physical SOS button for emergency notification
- Two way communication capability for direct voice or call style interactions
- On device camera for snapshots and visual confirmation
- Android 4.4 based platform allowing familiar wearable interactions
- IPX7 waterproof rating for protection against splashes and short immersion
- 710mAh battery with magnetic 4PIN charging for extended day to day use

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the D38 transmits location and status information so caregivers and administrators can view positions on maps, receive alerts, and review device history. Plaspy normalizes the D38 inputs into consistent events and telemetry for monitoring and reporting.

- Live location updates and historical playback based on GPS AGPS LBS and WiFi fixes
- SOS events reported to Plaspy so assigned users can receive immediate notifications
- Connectivity and battery status visible in Plaspy for device health monitoring
- Camera snapshots and communication events can be associated with device activity logs
- Indoor positioning signals from WiFi and LBS help improve accuracy in buildings and urban areas

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety monitoring with live location and emergency alerts for caregivers
- School and daycare check ins where arrival and departure events are tracked
- Outdoor supervision during walks sports and short trips with waterproof protection
- Family travel oversight for quick location checks and incident response
- Remote caregiver check ins using two way communication and camera verification

Using the Sentar D38 with Plaspy gives caregivers and organizations a centralized view of wearable endpoints alongside other tracked assets.
