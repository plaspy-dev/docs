# Sentar - D50 Features

This page provides a public feature summary for the Sentar D50 4G Kids Smart Watch and explains its practical capabilities when used with Plaspy. It focuses on the device features that matter to families and administrators and how those features are reflected in Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and basic device oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Use this page as a practical guide to what the D50 commonly offers and verify any device specific or time sensitive details with the official Sentar documentation.

## Feature Overview

The D50 is a compact, wearable GPS tracker designed for child safety and family communication. It combines multi source positioning, cellular connectivity, an SOS function, and a front camera to provide location awareness and contextual status updates through Plaspy.

- Real time location using GPS with LBS and WiFi fallback to improve indoor and mixed environment coverage.
- 4G cellular connectivity and nano SIM support for consistent data upload and two way voice and video calls.
- Dedicated physical SOS button to transmit immediate alerts and location data to Plaspy.
- Front camera that enables photo capture and video calling to provide visual context during events.
- IPX7 water protection and magnetic charging for everyday durability and convenient recharging.

## Core Features of Sentar - D50

- Plaspy compatibility for integrating location, alerts, and device status into a central monitoring platform.
- Multi source positioning: GPS combined with LBS and WiFi positioning to support better location results in varied conditions.
- 4G LTE and GSM cellular connectivity for near real time telemetry and two way communication.
- Physical SOS button that triggers an alert event and location transmission.
- Front facing camera for photos and video calls to help verify status during incidents.
- Onboard RTOS and battery management designed for consistent daily operation and periodic telemetry.
- IPX7 rated water resistance and a wearable form factor intended for children.
- Nano SIM slot and magnetic 4 pin charging interface for practical deployment and charging.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a D50 is registered and active in Plaspy, device events and periodic updates feed into the platform so caregivers and administrators can maintain situational awareness. Plaspy receives location and status updates from the watch and displays them in maps, timelines, and notifications.

- Real time location display on maps using GPS coordinates with LBS and WiFi fallback to improve coverage in challenging environments.
- SOS alert events appear in Plaspy with associated timestamp and reported location so caregivers can respond quickly.
- Battery and online status telemetry are available for device management and proactive maintenance.
- Camera initiated events and two way call records can be logged as contextual events in Plaspy timelines.
- Periodic position updates and event driven messages are presented in Plaspy for audit trails and visibility across devices.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety monitoring where parents want continuous location awareness and emergency alerting.

- Short range family communication with two way voice and video calls to check on a child visually and verbally.
