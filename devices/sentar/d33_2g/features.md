# Sentar - D33-2G Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Sentar D33-2G and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It focuses on the device features that are visible to users and administrators when the tracker is integrated with Plaspy, helping you understand practical behavior and expected outcomes on the platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary based on device firmware, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation. This page summarizes the common, user-facing capabilities of the D33-2G as reported by the manufacturer and how those features appear in Plaspy, but you should verify device-specific details with the official Sentar documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar D33-2G is a child-focused GPS smartwatch designed to provide location awareness, direct communication, and emergency alerting while remaining wearable for everyday use. When paired with Plaspy, the D33-2G reports location and events so families and supervisors can monitor movement, respond to alerts, and review history from the same platform used for other compatible devices.

- Real-time location reporting using GPS plus LBS positioning for better coverage in mixed indoor and outdoor environments.
- Built-in front-facing camera with remote capture capability to verify surroundings or record moments when permitted.
- Two-way voice calling and voice message support with a phonebook of approved contacts for direct communication.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button that sends immediate alerts to caregivers and can trigger fast notifications in Plaspy.
- Safe zone geo-fence alerts and historical route playback for monitoring routes and receiving notifications when boundaries are crossed.
- Waterproof, durable smartwatch form factor with a color TFT display designed for everyday wear by children.

## Core Features of Sentar - D33-2G

- GPS positioning combined with LBS cell tower assistance to improve fixes where GPS alone is limited.
- Two-way voice calling and phonebook control to allow communication between the device wearer and approved contacts.
- Built-in front-facing camera with remote camera control available through the companion app.
- SOS emergency button to provide immediate alerting and direct calls to caregivers.
- Safe zone geo-fence alerts to notify caregivers when the device enters or exits defined areas.
- Historical route playback to review past movements and verify travel patterns.
- Water-resistant construction and an eye-protecting TFT color display suitable for daily use.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the D33-2G's position updates, alerts, and activity events so caregivers and administrators can view live location, receive time-sensitive notifications, and access movement history alongside other devices on the platform. Plaspy automatically recognizes compatible tracker reports and presents events in a consistent interface.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps using the device's GPS and LBS reports for situational awareness.
- SOS and emergency events shown as high-priority notifications so caregivers can act quickly.
- Geo-fence events and safe zone notifications available in Plaspy to track boundary crossings and receive alerts.
- Route history and playback accessible in Plaspy to review previous movements and timelines.
- Voice call and message events associated with the device’s activity logs where supported by the integration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parental monitoring for children to receive live location updates and respond to SOS alerts.
- Confirming school pickups and drop offs with geo-fence alerts and route playback.
- Allowing supervised independent play with quick two-way contact and remote camera verification.
- Daycare or group supervision where administrators monitor multiple devices and get notifications when a child leaves a safe area.
- Verifying location and surroundings remotely via camera capture when checking on a child’s environment.
- Keeping an organized contact list on the device so only approved individuals can call the wearer.
