# D33-2G Children's GPS Smartwatch — Plaspy Compatible

The D33-2G is a compact, child-focused GPS tracker in smartwatch form that provides parents reliable, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and peace of mind. Designed for everyday wear, the D33-2G blends GPS + LBS positioning, two-way voice calling and an SOS emergency button with a water-resistant construction and an eye-protection TFT display — all controllable from a companion mobile app.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D33-2G integrates location, alerts and activity data into the Plaspy platform so families and administrators can monitor routes, respond to emergencies, and keep children safe without losing the simple usability that kids need. Its camera, voice features and geo-fence alerts make this GPS tracker well-suited for family safety and supervised independence.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker in a wearable smartwatch form factor — ideal for child safety and family monitoring.
- Real-time tracking using GPS plus LBS positioning for improved location accuracy indoors and outdoors.
- Built-in front-facing camera with remote camera control via the app to capture moments and verify surroundings.
- Two-way voice calling, phonebook control and voice messages to keep approved contacts connected.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button for fast alerts and direct calls to caregivers.
- Safe zone \(geo-fence\) alerts and historical route playback to review movements and receive instant notifications.
- Waterproof, durable design and an eye-protecting TFT color display for comfortable, everyday use.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the D33-2G becomes a child-focused telemetry and location node on the platform. Plaspy ingests the device’s position updates, status events and alerts so you can view live location, receive time-sensitive notifications and access historical routes from the same interface used for other Plaspy compatible trackers.

- Real-time location updates: GPS + LBS positions are reported to Plaspy for live tracking on maps and fast location fixes.
- SOS and emergency alerts: SOS button presses generate immediate alerts and can trigger predefined Plaspy notifications to caregivers.
- Remote camera snapshots: Parents can trigger the front-facing camera remotely through the app and see images in Plaspy records where supported.
- Activity and route playback: Movement history and route playback are available in Plaspy for timeline review and verification.
- Call and voice logs: Two-way calls and voice messages are logged and can be associated with device events inside Plaspy.

The D33-2G offers a purpose-built combination of features for family safety while integrating smoothly with Plaspy for platform-wide visibility. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, it feeds real-time location updates, event alerts and activity logs into a centralized interface so caregivers can manage multiple devices and accounts from one place.
