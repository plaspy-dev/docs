# Sentar - D33-2G

<div className="device-logo">
![D33-2G](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Sentar D33-2G is a compact, child-focused GPS tracker presented as a wearable smartwatch. It combines satellite positioning with cell tower assisted location (GPS + LBS), two-way voice calling, an SOS emergency button, a front-facing camera, and a water-resistant build with an eye-protection TFT display. The device is designed for everyday wear by children and provides caregivers with location and status updates via a companion mobile app.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D33-2G can integrate its location reports, alerts and activity events into the Plaspy platform so families and administrators can monitor routes, receive time-sensitive notifications, and manage devices from a single interface. Its wearable form factor and child safety features make it a practical choice when added to Plaspy for visibility and basic supervision use cases.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable smartwatch form factor tailored for children and family monitoring.
- Real-time tracking using GPS with LBS assistance for improved indoor and urban coverage.
- Two-way voice calling and phonebook control to keep approved contacts reachable.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button for fast alerts to caregivers.
- Built-in front-facing camera with remote capture capability through the companion app.
- Geo-fence safe zone alerts and historical route playback for movement review.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the D33-2G acts as a location and event source within the platform, sending position fixes and alert events to Plaspy so that caregivers can view live locations, receive notifications, and review device history alongside other compatible trackers.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps using the device position reports.
- SOS and emergency notifications forwarded to configured Plaspy alert channels for fast awareness.
- Route history and timeline playback available in Plaspy for reviewing past movements.
- Remote camera snapshots and device events associated with the device record where supported.
- Call and voice event records can be linked to device activity and timelines in Plaspy.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parental monitoring and rapid response when a child triggers an SOS or needs help.
- Confirming school commutes and pickups using geo-fence alerts and route playback.
- Supervised outdoor play with reachable two-way calling and periodic location checks.
- Daycare or group supervision where administrators need basic location awareness and alerts.
- Verifying surroundings or moments remotely with camera capture and voice messages.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The D33-2G offers a focused set of features for family safety and supervised independence, and its compatibility with Plaspy brings those device events into a centralized tracking environment. For caregivers and small organizations seeking straightforward visibility, the combination of GPS plus LBS positioning, SOS alerts, calling, and camera control provides practical situational awareness while keeping the experience simple for children.
