# Sentar - V80

![V80](./logo.jpg)

The Sentar V80-1.22 Niños Reloj GPS is a versatile and reliable GPS tracker designed specifically for children. With its compact and stylish design, this tracker is available in three vibrant colors - blue, pink, and orange - making it appealing to kids of all ages. The V80-1.22 is equipped with a powerful MTK2503 chipset, ensuring accurate and real-time tracking of your child's location.

One of the standout features of the Sentar V80-1.22 is its multiple location modes. It combines GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi technologies to provide precise and reliable location information. This means that no matter where your child is, you can easily track their whereabouts and ensure their safety. Whether they are at school, the park, or a friend's house, you can have peace of mind knowing their location is just a few clicks away.

In addition to its exceptional tracking capabilities, the Sentar V80-1.22 offers a range of other useful features. It has a built-in SOS button that allows your child to send an emergency alert with their exact location to predefined contacts. The V80-1.22 also supports two-way communication, allowing you to call your child or send voice messages directly to the tracker. Furthermore, it has a geo-fence feature that enables you to set virtual boundaries and receive notifications when your child enters or exits these areas.

The Sentar V80-1.22 Niños Reloj GPS is a reliable and feature-packed GPS tracker that provides peace of mind for parents and ensures the safety of their children. With its accurate tracking, multiple location modes, and additional features like SOS alerts and two-way communication, this tracker is an excellent choice for parents who want to stay connected with their kids at all times.
