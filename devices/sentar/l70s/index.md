# Sentar - L70S

![L70S](./logo.jpg)

The Sentar L70S Kids GPS Tracker Watch is a reliable and stylish device designed to keep your child safe and give you peace of mind. With its advanced features and accurate tracking capabilities, you can easily monitor your child's whereabouts at all times. The L70S is equipped with a powerful MTK2503 chipset, ensuring fast and efficient performance.

One of the standout features of the L70S is its multiple location modes. It combines GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi technologies to provide precise and real-time location information. Whether your child is indoors or outdoors, you can always track their location with confidence. The L70S is available in four vibrant colors - blue, pink, black, and white - allowing your child to choose their favorite.

Key Features:

- MTK2503 chipset for reliable performance
- GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi location modes for accurate tracking
- Available in blue, pink, black, and white colors
