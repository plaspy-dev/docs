# Sentar - L70S

![L70S](./logo.jpg)

The Sentar L70S is a compact kids GPS tracker watch designed to provide parents and caregivers with continuous location visibility. Marketed as a child safety device, the L70S combines multiple positioning methods to deliver timely location updates and uses an MTK2503 chipset for core tracking functions. It is available in several colors and is presented as a wearable that balances everyday usability with location monitoring.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the L70S can be integrated into Plaspy's tracking platform to centralize location data alongside other devices. That compatibility makes it a practical choice for families, small care providers, and organizations that want to view and manage device locations through Plaspy's monitoring and reporting tools. Plaspy can surface L70S location information within broader visibility dashboards and historical reports.

## Key Highlights

- Designed as a kids GPS tracker watch for continuous location visibility
- Multiple location modes including GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi for improved accuracy
- MTK2503 chipset cited for core tracking performance
- Real time location reporting suitable for parent and caregiver monitoring
- Available in blue, pink, black, and white to suit personal preference
- Compatible with Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the L70S delivers position data that Plaspy normalizes and displays alongside other tracked assets. Plaspy receives location updates and makes them available through its monitoring interfaces so users can follow movement, review history, and manage multiple devices in one place.

- View live location updates from the L70S on Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Review historical location trails and movement summaries for oversight
- Configure location based alerts and notifications within Plaspy to be informed of important events
- Group and organize L70S units with other devices for consolidated reporting
- Use Plaspy reporting tools to export activity summaries and location logs
