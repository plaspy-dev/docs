# Sentar - D37 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Sentar D37 and how its capabilities are surfaced when used with Plaspy. It focuses on user facing functions such as location tracking, emergency alerts, and basic on-device interactions that Plaspy can ingest for monitoring and reporting.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and installation or provisioning choices made by the manufacturer or reseller. For device specific technical details and the latest firmware notes, review the manufacturer documentation linked at the end of this page.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar D37 is a compact 4G kids smartwatch designed for reliable, Plaspy compatible real time tracking and basic two-way communication. It combines several positioning modes and on-device controls to provide parents and caretakers with timely location and status information.

- Multi-mode positioning with GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi to improve location accuracy in varied environments  
- Real-time tracking and historical routes visible in Plaspy dashboards for ongoing monitoring  
- Dedicated SOS/power button for immediate emergency signaling to Plaspy and configured contacts  
- Two-way voice and data capability via a Nano SIM on supported 4G and WCDMA networks  
- Compact 1.47 inch touchscreen for simple device status checks and local control  
- IPX7 waterproof rating and wearable form factor suitable for daily child use

## Core Features of Sentar - D37

- Plaspy compatibility for automatic integration into monitoring dashboards and alerting flows  
- Multi-mode GNSS and positioning: GPS plus AGPS with supplemental LBS and WiFi positioning  
- 4G FDD/TDD and WCDMA cellular support with Nano SIM for voice and data connectivity  
- Dedicated SOS and power button for emergency alerts and immediate event signaling  
- 1.47 inch 2.5D touchscreen for basic on-device interaction and status display  
- Integrated camera for basic photo or video capture to provide situational context  
- RTOS based Android 8.1 operating environment and ASR3603S chipset as reported by the manufacturer  
- IPX7 waterproof rating and compact watch form factor for everyday wear

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives periodic position updates and device status reports from the D37 so that location, event, and health information appears in dashboards and reporting tools. The platform maps the device's telemetry to common monitoring constructs such as routes, alerts, and device status.

- Live location plotting and position history based on GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi fixes reported by the device  
- SOS events triggered by the dedicated button appear as high priority alerts in Plaspy for rapid notification  
- Two-way communication indicators and basic messaging or call events can be reflected in device activity logs  
- Battery and network signal telemetry reported by the watch help Plaspy surface device health and uptime information  
- Location tagged photos or basic media capture can be associated with events to aid situational verification

## Typical Use Cases
