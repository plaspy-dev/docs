# Sentar - Q60

<div className="device-logo">
![Q60](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Sentar Q60 is a compact GPS tracker designed for reliable location tracking across a range of everyday use cases. Powered by an MTK2503 chipset, the device combines GPS, AGPS, and LBS location modes to provide fast position fixes and continuity when GPS reception is limited. Its compact, low profile design and availability in black, white, and blue make it easy to position discreetly for vehicle, asset, or personal tracking needs.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Q60 can deliver its location data into the Plaspy platform for visualization, monitoring, and operational use. The combination of multi mode positioning and a compact form factor makes the Q60 suitable for organizations and individuals who want straightforward location visibility through Plaspy without adding unnecessary complexity.

## Key Highlights

- MTK2503 chipset for responsive and efficient position processing
- GPS AGPS LBS location modes to balance accuracy and continuity
- Designed for accurate and near real time location reporting
- Compact and discreet design available in black white and blue
- Suitable for tracking vehicles assets and people in common scenarios
- Simple hardware profile that integrates with standard tracking platforms

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Sentar Q60 provides location updates that Plaspy can display and manage alongside other devices in your account. Plaspy ingests the Q60 location data and makes it accessible for map visualization, history, alerts, and reporting so teams can monitor assets and movements from a single dashboard.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for immediate situational awareness
- Historical route and position history for playback and review
- Configurable alerts for movement events and zone transitions to support operational oversight
- Device grouping and fleet views to monitor multiple Q60 units together
- Reporting and export options to support operational analysis and audits
- LBS fallback helps maintain location visibility in areas with limited GPS reception

## Typical Use Cases

- Asset monitoring for equipment and portable inventory
- Personal location tracking for relatives or lone workers
- Short term rental or loaner equipment visibility
