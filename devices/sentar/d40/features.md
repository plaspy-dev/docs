# Sentar - D40 Features

This page describes the public feature context for using the Sentar D40 kids smartwatch with Plaspy. It highlights the device capabilities that can be surfaced inside the Plaspy platform for guardian monitoring, real-time alerts, and centralized device visibility. The focus is on practical, user-facing functions and how those functions appear when the D40 is integrated with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where helpful, this page points to the kinds of signals Plaspy can accept from the D40, but users should confirm device-specific details with the manufacturer and review the device firmware notes for the most current information.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar D40 is a family-focused wearable designed to provide child-oriented location awareness, simple communication, and emergency alerting. Its combination of LBS and Wi‑Fi positioning with on-device SOS and media features is intended to support indoor-aware tracking and guardian response workflows.

- LBS and Wi‑Fi positioning for practical indoor-oriented location reporting where GPS may be limited
- Physical SOS button for immediate emergency alerts routed into Plaspy
- Voice and data support via Nano SIM and Android 8.1 for basic calls and messaging
- Battery telemetry reporting to monitor device charge state over time
- Integrated camera for basic photo capture tied to location events and situational context
- Splash resistant IPX7 design and a clear 1.38 inch 2.5D curved HD display for everyday wear

## Core Features of Sentar - D40

- LBS positioning and Wi‑Fi based location reporting suitable for indoor and urban environments
- Physical SOS button that generates an emergency notification from the device
- Nano SIM support with cellular voice and data capability under Android 8.1
- 1.38-inch 2.5D curved HD round display (240×240) for on-wrist interaction
- 800mAh battery with on-device telemetry that Plaspy can display
- 8W camera for simple photo capture tied to events or manual use
- IPX7 splash resistance designed for daily wear and light water exposure

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests position updates, device state, and alert events from compatible devices like the D40 and presents them in a centralized monitoring interface. Integration focuses on delivering timely location, alert, and health information to guardians and administrators.

- Location updates derived from LBS and Wi‑Fi appear in Plaspy as position reports for tracking and history
- SOS button activations are forwarded to Plaspy as high-priority alerts for immediate notification
- Battery level and basic connectivity telemetry are visible in device detail views to help monitor device health
- Photos captured on the device can be associated with location events to provide situational context
- Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and maps incoming data for display and alerting
- Alert rules and notifications within Plaspy can be configured to respond to SOS, low battery, or connectivity events

## Typical Use Cases

- Guardian monitoring for child safety with immediate SOS notification and approximate location visibility
- Indoor-aware tracking at schools, malls, or other environments where GPS is limited and LBS or Wi‑Fi is more practical
- Routine check-ins and voice communication between guardians and children using the device’s cellular capability
- Situational verification by linking basic photos to location events for added context
- Device health oversight by monitoring battery telemetry and connectivity status
- Everyday wearable use where splash resistance and a durable display are useful
