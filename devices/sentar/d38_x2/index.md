# Sentar - D38-X2

![D38-X2](./tracker.jpg)

The D38-X2 is a Plaspy compatible kids' GPS tracker in the form of a smart watch designed for safety, communication, and everyday convenience. Running Android 8.1 with 1GB RAM and 8GB of internal storage, the D38-X2 supports messaging apps such as WhatsApp, two-way voice calling, and standalone app operation — all while delivering accurate location via GPS, A-GPS, LBS, and Wi‑Fi positioning. Its removable watch case and child-friendly design make it a comfortable, customizable companion for school, family life, and outdoor play.

Integrated with Plaspy, the D38-X2 becomes a reliable endpoint for real-time tracking and parental monitoring. Plaspy-compatible integration lets caregivers view live location fixes, receive device-status updates, and manage alerts across a single dashboard. The result is a purpose-built GPS tracker experience for child safety that fits into a wider telematics platform without sacrificing simplicity or privacy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates location and status data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and alerts.
- Real-time tracking: GPS and A-GPS with LBS and Wi‑Fi positioning provide timely location updates for child safety.
- Two-way communication: voice calling and messaging apps \(including WhatsApp support\) for direct contact with caregivers.
- Standalone smart device: Android 8.1 platform with 1GB RAM and 8GB internal storage supports apps and local data storage.
- Parental controls: built-in monitoring and management features designed to simplify supervision and device configuration.
- Customizable and comfortable: removable watch case and interchangeable faces make the device kid-friendly and personalizable.
- Suitable for daily routines: useful for school check-ins, family monitoring, and outdoor activities where reliable location and communication matter most.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the D38-X2 streams device data and location information into Plaspy’s interface so you can monitor activity, set geofences, and receive alerts in real time. Integration focuses on the device’s primary outputs — location fixes, connectivity state, and communication status — allowing parents and administrators to act quickly if a child needs help.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: GPS/A-GPS coordinates plus LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance feed into Plaspy for near real-time tracking.
- Voice call and messaging status: two-way communication events and messaging availability are trackable through the platform.
- Wi‑Fi and LBS-assisted positioning: complementary fixes improve location accuracy in urban or indoor environments.
- Parental-control events and device health: device connection state, app availability, and basic status notifications are visible in Plaspy.
- Centralized alerts and reports: set notifications for check-ins, prolonged inactivity, or out-of-zone events via Plaspy’s dashboard.
