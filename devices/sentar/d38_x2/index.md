# Sentar - D38-X2

![D38-X2](./tracker.jpg)

The D38-X2 is a kids oriented GPS tracker in the form of a smart watch that focuses on safety, communication, and everyday convenience. It runs an Android 8.1 platform with modest memory and storage, supports popular messaging apps such as WhatsApp, enables two-way voice calling, and provides location using GPS, A-GPS, LBS, and Wi‑Fi positioning. The removable watch case and child friendly design make it comfortable and adaptable for school, family life, and outdoor play.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D38-X2 acts as a straightforward endpoint for real time location and device status within a centralized monitoring platform. When integrated with Plaspy, caregivers and administrators can view live location fixes, monitor connectivity and basic device health, and receive configurable alerts so the watch fits into broader oversight workflows without adding complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for centralized monitoring and alerting across devices
- Real time location using GPS and A-GPS with LBS and Wi‑Fi assistance
- Two way voice calling and support for messaging apps including WhatsApp
- Standalone smart watch running Android 8.1 with on device storage for apps and data
- Child friendly physical design with a removable watch case and interchangeable faces
- Built in parental monitoring features to simplify supervision and configuration

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the D38-X2 streams its core outputs into the Plaspy dashboard so caregivers can monitor movement, check device status, and configure alerts from a single interface. Integration emphasizes the device's location and communication signals to provide timely insights for safety and coordination.

- Live location updates and position history visible within Plaspy
- Device connectivity and basic health indicators available for status monitoring
- Notifications and alerts for check ins, out of zone events, or prolonged inactivity
- Visibility of communication events such as incoming or outgoing calls and messaging availability
- Centralized reporting for routine check ins and activity summaries

## Typical Use Cases

- Family monitoring for day to day location checks and caregiver coordination
- School check ins and supervised routines where quick status updates are helpful
- Outdoor play and local travel where real time positioning and contact are priorities
- Short trips and errands to maintain communication and location visibility
- Care settings where a simple wearable provides both safety and messaging options
