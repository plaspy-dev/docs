# Sentar - Q50

![Q50](./logo.jpg)

The Sentar Q50 Kids GPS Watch is a reliable and stylish device designed to keep track of your child's whereabouts. With its advanced features and user-friendly interface, this GPS watch provides peace of mind for parents and a sense of independence for kids. The Q50 model is available in four vibrant colors - Blue, Pink, Green, Black, and Dark blue - allowing your child to choose their favorite.

Equipped with the MTK2503 chipset, the Sentar Q50 offers accurate and real-time location tracking. It combines GPS, AGPS, and LBS \(Location-Based Service\) technologies to ensure precise positioning, both indoors and outdoors. Whether your child is at school, the park, or a friend's house, you can easily monitor their location through the accompanying mobile app.

Outstanding Features:

- Real-time location tracking using GPS, AGPS, and LBS technologies
- Available in four vibrant colors - Blue, Pink, Green, Black, and Dark blue
- User-friendly interface for easy operation
- Two-way communication with voice calls and voice messages
- Geo-fencing feature to set safe zones and receive alerts when your child enters or exits them
- SOS button for emergency situations
- Remote monitoring to listen to your child's surroundings
- Waterproof design for added durability
- Long battery life for extended usage
