# Sentar - Elderly

![Elderly](./tracker.jpg)

The Sentar Elderly is a 4G wearable GPS watch designed for seniors and other vulnerable users who need straightforward, reliable location and safety services. The device combines multi mode positioning with a 1.4 inch IPS touchscreen, a dedicated SOS button, a built in camera and an IPX7 water resistance rating to provide practical day to day monitoring in a wrist form factor. Performance is delivered on an Android 4.4 platform with modest memory and storage that keep the user experience simple and focused on safety features.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker, the Elderly watch can be registered into Plaspy for centralized visibility and event handling. When connected, the device provides live location updates, position history and emergency alerts that Plaspy surfaces to caregivers, family members and monitoring teams. This compatibility lets organizations and households manage personal wearable tracking alongside other tracked assets within the same Plaspy environment.

## Key Highlights

- Purpose built wearable for seniors with an easy to read 1.4 inch IPS touchscreen and simplified controls.
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi to improve location accuracy across environments.
- 4G cellular connectivity for wide area coverage and timely data uplink to monitoring platforms.
- Dedicated SOS button and on device camera to support rapid alerts and situational checks.
- IPX7 water resistance and a rechargeable 700mAh battery for practical daily use.
- Lightweight form factor available in Blue, Pink and Black to suit personal preference.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Sentar Elderly watch sends location points and key status events to the Plaspy platform where they are presented on maps, retained as history and included in alerts and reports. Plaspy aggregates device data so caregivers and administrators can maintain situational awareness across personal wearables and other tracked assets.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps for real time monitoring.
- SOS button events are forwarded to Plaspy to trigger notifications with location context.
- Battery level and basic device status are reported so monitoring teams can act on low power alerts.
- Photo capture from the built in camera can provide situational awareness where device permissions and configuration allow.
- Devices like the Elderly watch can be managed alongside vehicle and asset trackers from the same Plaspy dashboard for unified oversight.

## Typical Use Cases

- Elder care monitoring for seniors who live independently and need an easy way to call for help.
- Family and caregiver coordination to centralize alerts and location history for shared oversight.
- Short term supervision during travel or day trips where cellular real time tracking is required.
- Daily safety checks and simple activity awareness for vulnerable users and their support teams.
- Mixed device monitoring where personal wearables are managed together with fleet or asset trackers.
