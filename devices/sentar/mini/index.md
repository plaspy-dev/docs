# Sentar - Mini

![Mini](./tracker.png)

The Mini 4G GPS Tracker is a compact, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker engineered for reliable personal and small-asset tracking. With multi-mode positioning \(GPS, AGPS, LBS and WiFi\) and worldwide cellular support across 4G/3G/2G, the Mini delivers accurate real-time tracking and status telemetry to Plaspy for situational awareness and location history. Its IPX7 waterproof rating and 700mAh rechargeable battery make it a practical choice for everyday use with children, pets, luggage, or vulnerable adults.

Designed to integrate smoothly with Plaspy's device onboarding and tracking dashboard, the Mini provides continuous location updates and device health data over cellular networks. The compact form factor and selectable colors \(Blue, Pink, Black\) let organizations and families deploy discreet GPS trackers that appear less intrusive while still feeding essential telemetry to Plaspy for alerts, geofencing, and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real-time tracking and location history via cellular uplink and multi-mode positioning.
- Multi-mode positioning \(GPS + AGPS + LBS + WiFi\) improves accuracy indoors and outdoors for dependable location fixes.
- Global cellular coverage with 4G/3G/2G support and broad LTE FDD bands for roaming and international use.
- IPX7 waterproof rating suitable for wet environments and accidental immersion—ideal for active lifestyles and outdoor use.
- 700mAh rechargeable battery for extended portable operation and straightforward recharging routines.
- Compact, user-friendly design available in Blue, Pink, and Black to suit personal and family use cases.
- Powered by the ASR3603S chipset with 128MB RAM + 128MB ROM for responsive device operation and reliable telemetry reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Mini 4G GPS Tracker transmits location and telemetry data over the cellular network to Plaspy’s ingestion endpoints, enabling real-time tracking, configurable alerts, and historical route playback. The device’s multi-mode positioning ensures Plaspy receives the best available fix—GPS outdoors, and a combination of AGPS, WiFi and LBS for improved indoor accuracy. Plaspy uses this incoming data to power dashboards, geofences, and notification rules for timely situational awareness.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GPS + AGPS + LBS + WiFi\) delivered to Plaspy for live tracking.
- Battery level and device status reporting to Plaspy so administrators and caregivers can monitor uptime and charge state.
- Cellular network and band information available to Plaspy to help diagnose connectivity and roaming behavior.
- Location history and route playback in Plaspy dashboards for post-event review and auditing.
- Configurable alerts in Plaspy based on geofencing, movement, or low-battery conditions triggered by device telemetry.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Mini 4G GPS Tracker pairs practical hardware with Plaspy’s tracking platform to deliver cost-effective, real-time location and telemetry for personal and small-asset deployments. Its multi-mode positioning and broad cellular band support ensure robust connectivity and improved accuracy in mixed environments, while IPX7 protection and a 700mAh battery make it resilient for daily use. For organizations or families seeking Plaspy compatible solutions without vehicle-specific I/O, the Mini is a compact option that focuses on accurate location updates, device health telemetry, and simple integration.

Note on advanced vehicle features: if your fleet management needs include ignition sensing, immobilizer control, fuel monitoring, or Bluetooth sensors integration, verify the specific I/O and sensor support for your deployment. The Mini’s specification emphasizes reliable GPS/WiFi/LBS positioning and cellular telemetry; consult the supplier if you require additional vehicle telematics capabilities \(ignition, immobilizer, fuel monitoring\) or Bluetooth sensor support for temperature or other telemetry.
