# Sentar - Mini Features

This page provides a public overview of the Sentar Mini GPS tracker and the features it exposes when used with Plaspy. It describes practical, user facing capabilities and explains how those capabilities map to visibility and monitoring inside the Plaspy platform without presenting private configuration or manufacturer internal details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, and installation method. For device specific parameters, advanced configuration, or the latest technical details consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes from Sentar.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar Mini is a compact 4G capable personal tracker designed for reliable location reporting and basic device health telemetry. Its multi mode positioning improves fix reliability across environments and it is built for portable, everyday use with splash and immersion protection.

- Multi mode positioning with GPS, AGPS, WiFi, and LBS for improved indoor and outdoor location fixes.
- Worldwide cellular support across 4G 3G and 2G for roaming and international coverage.
- IPX7 waterproof rating suitable for wet conditions and accidental immersion.
- Rechargeable battery for portable operation and straightforward recharging.
- Compact discreet form factor available in Blue Pink and Black for personal and family deployments.

## Core Features of Sentar - Mini

- Multi mode positioning: GPS plus AGPS with supplemental WiFi and LBS positioning to improve fix availability.
- Cellular connectivity across 4G 3G and 2G bands for global coverage and roaming.
- IPX7 waterproof construction to tolerate wet environments and brief immersion.
- Rechargeable battery to support extended portable use and repeated charge cycles.
- Compact, wearable oriented design with color options for discreet personal tracking.
- Onboard memory and chipset details as provided by the manufacturer for responsive device operation.
- Continuous location and telemetry reporting designed to integrate with cloud tracking platforms.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location and telemetry from compatible trackers to present live position, history, and device health in a unified dashboard. The Mini’s multi mode positioning and cellular uplink allow Plaspy to receive the best available fixes and status updates for situational awareness and administrative workflows.

- Real time location updates delivered into Plaspy for live map tracking and monitoring.
- Location history and route playback in Plaspy dashboards for review and auditing.
- Battery level and device status reporting shown in Plaspy to help manage uptime and charging needs.
- Geofence and notification rules in Plaspy triggered by incoming device telemetry for alerts.
- Plaspy device onboarding and dashboard tools used to register and visualize the Mini without requiring bespoke integration work.
- Plaspy will automatically detect commonly used tracker protocols to simplify connection for supported devices.

- Child safety tracking with discreet wearable placement and caregiver monitoring via Plaspy.

- Luggage and personal item tracking while traveling where global cellular coverage is needed.
- Small asset and portable equipment tracking that requires compact form factor and telemetry.
- Personal security use for location history and incident follow up.
