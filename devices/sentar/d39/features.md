# Sentar - D39 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Sentar D39 smartwatch with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities that matter to parents, administrators, and deployments where wearable tracking devices are useful alongside other assets managed on Plaspy. Where relevant, the page explains how the D39 reports position and status into Plaspy for real time monitoring and historical review.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional product variant, installation method, and how the manufacturer configures companion app features. The description on this page is grounded in the public model details for the D39 and is intended for general guidance rather than exhaustive device specifications.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar D39 is a Plaspy compatible 4G Android style GPS smartwatch designed primarily for child safety and parental monitoring. It combines multi mode positioning with voice communication and an SOS alert to provide clear, timely location and status information to caregivers and Plaspy dashboards.

- Real time tracking using multi mode positioning: GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi to improve outdoor and indoor accuracy.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button and two way voice capability for rapid communication with caregivers.
- 4G cellular connectivity on common LTE, WCDMA, and GSM bands for continuous position updates and calls.
- Compact, wearable design with IPX7 water resistance and a bright 1.85 inch IPS touchscreen for daily use.
- Companion mobile app features for parental controls, location history, and remote management integrated into Plaspy monitoring.

## Core Features of Sentar - D39

- Plaspy compatibility for real time ingestion of location and status reports into the Plaspy platform.
- Multi mode positioning stack including GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi derived fixes for better coverage indoors and outdoors.
- SOS and power button that supports immediate alerting and two way communication to registered contacts.
- 4G voice and data support across common FDD/TDD LTE, WCDMA and GSM bands for reliable connectivity.
- 1.85 inch IPS touchscreen for interaction and basic on device configuration.
- IPX7 level water resistance suitable for everyday wear and light exposure to water.
- 710 mAh battery capacity with magnetic charging for convenient recharges.
- Nano SIM slot and basic on device interfaces for cellular connectivity and management.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests positional and status data from the D39 and normalizes those reports for display, alerts, and history on the Plaspy dashboard. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and presents the device as a managed endpoint alongside other assets, so caregivers and administrators can monitor location and events from one platform.

- Map display of real time and historical positions derived from GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi fixes.
- SOS event visibility and incident alerts routed into Plaspy so watchers can respond quickly.
- Battery and charging state reporting to track device uptime and schedule recharges from the dashboard.
- Location history and activity reports for review and administrative oversight.
- Parental control and companion app integration represented in Plaspy through device status and configuration summaries.
- Devices can be configured to report to Plaspy servers and Plaspy will normalize incoming fixes for consistent display.
