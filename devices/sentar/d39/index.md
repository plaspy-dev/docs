# Sentar - D39

![D39](./tracker.png)

The Sentar D39 is a compact 4G Android GPS smartwatch designed for children. It combines continuous location reporting with two way voice communication, an SOS emergency button, and multi mode positioning that leverages GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi. Built with a 1.85 inch IPS touchscreen and a water resistant IPX7 housing, the D39 is intended for daily wear and activity while keeping caregivers informed through a companion app.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the D39 feeds its positional and status information into Plaspy’s tracking environment so parents and administrators can monitor location, receive alerts, and review history alongside other assets. Its wearable form factor complements vehicle and fixed trackers in mixed deployments and provides a child focused option for organizations and families that need real time visibility and straightforward communication.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible kids smartwatch offering continuous real time tracking and reporting.
- Multi mode positioning with GPS AGPS LBS and WiFi to improve outdoor and indoor location accuracy.
- 4G voice and data connectivity for two way calls and rapid position updates.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button for immediate alerting and fast caregiver response.
- Water resistant IPX7 housing and a bright 1.85 inch IPS touchscreen built for everyday use.
- Compact wearable design with onboard battery and magnetic charging for convenient recharges.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the D39 streams location fixes and device status into Plaspy dashboards so caregivers and administrators gain consolidated visibility. Plaspy normalizes position inputs from assisted positioning methods for map display and alerting, and it presents device events and history alongside other tracked assets for simpler oversight.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for live monitoring.
- SOS and panic alert reporting routed into Plaspy notifications for rapid response.
- Indoor assistance using WiFi and LBS derived hints where available to improve situational awareness.
- Battery and charging state reporting to help manage device uptime and maintenance.
- Parental controls location history and permissions visible through companion app and integrated into Plaspy monitoring.
