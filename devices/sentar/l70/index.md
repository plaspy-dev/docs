# Sentar - L70

<div className="device-logo">
![L70](./logo.jpg)
</div>

The Sentar L70 is a compact, multi mode GPS tracker designed for reliable location tracking across a range of scenarios. Built around an MTK2503 chipset, the L70 combines GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi positioning to provide usable location information even where a single method alone might be limited. The device is offered in black and white and is intended for discreet tracking of vehicles, assets, or people.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the L70 can feed location data into Plaspy's fleet management platform to help organizations maintain visibility and operational control. Plaspy can use the L70's location updates to display real time positions, support reporting, and trigger alerts, making the tracker a practical option for users who want centralized oversight through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Multi mode positioning using GPS AGPS LBS and WiFi for improved location availability
- MTK2503 chipset for accurate and timely location fixes
- Compact and discreet design available in black or white for flexible deployment
- Suitable for tracking vehicles assets and people in a variety of environments
- Real time location capability that supports operational monitoring and reporting
- Designed to provide usable location in urban or signal challenged areas

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the Sentar L70 sends its location updates to the platform so positions can be visualized and acted upon. Plaspy ingests the incoming data and makes it available for monitoring dashboards reporting and notifications to support daily operations.

- Live position display on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Geofencing and boundary alerts configured within Plaspy to notify on entry or exit
- Trip history and activity reports for operational review and auditing
- Grouping and tagging of devices to organize fleets and assets in Plaspy
- Configurable notifications and event summaries for operational oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for route oversight and asset utilization
- Monitoring high value or mobile equipment to reduce loss and improve recovery
- Personal safety tracking for lone workers or vulnerable individuals
- Coordinating field teams and mobile workforce assignments
- Rental asset tracking and accountability between uses

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Sentar L70 is a practical choice when you need a compact tracker that uses multiple location sources to maintain visibility in mixed environments. Its combination of positioning modes helps Plaspy present a more consistent view of device location across urban areas indoors and open roads, which supports operational decision making.

Pairing the L70 with Plaspy provides a straightforward path to centralized monitoring without requiring extensive customization. Organizations that prioritize reliable location updates and simple device management can use the L70 within Plaspy to consolidate tracking data and generate useful insights for fleet and asset operations.
