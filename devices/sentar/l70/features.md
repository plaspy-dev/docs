# Sentar - L70 Features

This page provides a public, feature-focused overview of the Sentar L70 GPS tracker and how its capabilities are presented when used with Plaspy. It is intended to help customers, integrators, and fleet operators understand the practical functions the L70 can deliver through Plaspy without listing sensitive implementation details.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. For device-specific setup, firmware updates, and the most current technical specifications consult the official Sentar documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar L70 is positioned as a compact, multi-mode location tracker that combines satellite and network-based positioning methods for broader coverage and improved positioning in challenging environments. When integrated with Plaspy, the L70's reported location and status information support real-time visibility and operational monitoring.

- Multi-mode positioning using GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi to improve location availability in urban or indoor areas
- MTK2503 chipset for reliable location processing and real-time updates
- Compact, discreet form factor available in black or white for flexible installation and discreet tracking
- Compatibility with Plaspy, where the platform detects the device protocol and presents location data on maps and timelines
- Suitable for tracking vehicles, portable assets, and people where multi-source positioning is beneficial

## Core Features of Sentar - L70

- GPS positioning for satellite-based location fixes
- AGPS support to accelerate initial position acquisition and improve fix times
- LBS positioning for coarse location using cellular base stations when GPS is limited
- WiFi positioning to assist location estimation indoors or in dense urban areas
- MTK2503 chipset as the device's core positioning engine
- Small and discreet enclosure available in two color options
- Designed for general-purpose tracking of vehicles, assets, and individuals

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy receives location reports from the L70 and surfaces those reports in maps, history views, and device lists to provide operational context and ongoing visibility. Plaspy's device detection helps get the L70 reporting with minimal configuration in most deployments.

- Location points from GPS, AGPS, LBS, and WiFi are shown on Plaspy maps to reflect the device's best available position
- Plaspy presents device identity and recent update times so operators can confirm active reporting
- Historical tracks and timeline views allow review of past movements reported by the L70
- Plaspy's automatic protocol detection simplifies bringing the L70 online without manual protocol selection
- Device status and recent activity are visible in Plaspy dashboards for monitoring and oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Vehicle tracking for route visibility and basic fleet oversight
- Portable asset tracking where discreet form factor is important
- Personal tracking for location awareness of personnel in urban environments
- Short-term rental or equipment monitoring to confirm general location and movement
- Situations that need improved location coverage where GPS alone may be insufficient
