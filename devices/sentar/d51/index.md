# Sentar - D51

![D51](./tracker.png)

The Sentar D51 is a compact 4G kids smart watch designed for child safety and family communication. As a wearable GPS tracker, it combines multi mode positioning with cellular connectivity to provide caregivers with regular location updates and a simple SOS pathway for emergencies. The device is built for everyday wear and basic interaction, offering a touchscreen, a small camera, and core communication features oriented toward supervised use.

The D51 is Plaspy compatible out of the box, so location points, SOS events, and device telemetry can be relayed into Plaspy for centralized monitoring. This compatibility makes the D51 a practical choice for families and organizations that want to integrate a child wearable into Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and historical reports without requiring complex custom integration.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable 4G tracker tailored for child safety and family communications.
- Multi mode positioning combining GPS, LBS, and WiFi for improved location visibility.
- Built in SOS emergency function and remote communication to support fast caregiver response.
- Compact touchscreen form factor with basic camera for simple interaction and photo capture.
- 640mAh battery designed to support extended daily use in normal wear scenarios.
- Broad LTE band support for wider area coverage across common frequency bands.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected, the D51 streams location updates and event data over its cellular link to Plaspy, where those feeds become actionable on maps and in alerts. Plaspy turns the watch's position fixes, SOS signals, and device status into consolidated views for caregivers and administrators, helping teams maintain visibility and respond to incidents efficiently.

- Live location updates displayed as points on Plaspy maps and location timelines.
- SOS alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and escalation workflows.
- Device status and battery reporting visible in Plaspy dashboards to track health and readiness.
- Geofence monitoring and location history for retrospective review and arrival departure checks.
- Two way remote communication entries reflected in Plaspy logs where supported by the watch.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time child safety monitoring for parents and guardians during daily activities.
- Family check ins and voice or message based communication for routine coordination.
- School and childcare arrival and departure confirmation using geofence alerts and location history.
- Supervised outings and local excursions where prompt location and SOS reporting matter.
- Care programs and elder or vulnerable person monitoring where a discreet wearable is preferred.
