# Sentar - D41 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Sentar D41 and how its capabilities map to Plaspy for tracking, monitoring, and alerts. It focuses on user-facing capabilities and operational usefulness when the D41 is used with Plaspy and does not replace manufacturer documentation.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method and the manufacturer implementation on a per-unit basis. Where the D41 description lists capabilities such as GPS, LBS, WiFi positioning, an SOS button, or multimedia features, those functions may require specific firmware support or configuration to be visible inside Plaspy.

## Feature Overview

The D41 is a wearable 4G kids smartwatch built to provide location awareness, emergency alerts and basic multimedia features while remaining durable for daily use. In Plaspy, the device is used primarily for position reporting, event alerts such as SOS, device status monitoring, and historical location review.

- Real-time and historical location reporting using GPS, LBS and WiFi positioning for improved coverage and fallback options
- Physical SOS and power button events that can generate immediate alerts and status notifications in Plaspy
- Long standby battery behavior optimized for wearable use to reduce frequent charging in day to day monitoring
- IPX7 rated enclosure and a compact wearable form factor suitable for children and active use
- Android 8.1 based platform with local apps and a front camera that can provide multimedia where firmware allows uploads to Plaspy

## Core Features of Sentar - D41

- Multi-source positioning: GPS combined with LBS and WiFi positioning for better location continuity
- Cellular connectivity across 4G, WCDMA and GSM networks to maintain broad mobile coverage
- Physical SOS and power buttons for straightforward emergency signaling and device control
- Wearable design with a 1.91 inch touch display and a rotatable control button for local interaction
- Front camera capability available on the device where firmware supports multimedia transfers
- IPX7 water resistance for protection against splashes and short immersion
- Android 8.1 platform with onboard storage for device-side features and local apps
- 800 mAh battery optimized for longer standby duration in wearable scenarios

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the D41 is paired with Plaspy, positioning and status events from the device are presented as location points, alerts and device health indicators. Plaspy ingests available device data and surfaces it in dashboards, maps and reports for real time monitoring and historical analysis.

- Continuous position updates from GPS, LBS and WiFi are shown on Plaspy maps and in location history
- SOS and power button activations are mapped to alerts and event logs so guardians and operators can respond quickly
- Device battery level and connectivity status provide visibility into device health and availability in Plaspy
- Multimedia captures such as images may be available in Plaspy when device firmware supports uploading camera data
- Plaspy exposes event timelines and location trails that help reconstruct movements and actions reported by the D41

## Typical Use Cases

- Parental monitoring and child safety with live tracking and SOS alerts
- School and camp oversight for monitoring student device status and location during activities
- After-school pickup coordination using precise location updates to streamline handoffs
- Outdoor play and excursions where waterproofing and multi-source positioning improve reliability
- Low intensity wearable tracking deployments where compact form factor and long standby are priorities
