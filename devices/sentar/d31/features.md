# Sentar - D31 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Sentar D31 with Plaspy. It focuses on the device capabilities that are relevant for integrating the D31 as a wearable GPS tracker in Plaspy for real time location visibility, emergency alerts, and basic device telemetry.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, installation, and how the manufacturer implements specific functions. Verify the device model and firmware details with the manufacturer and follow Plaspy setup guidance for best results.

## Feature Overview

The Sentar D31 is a compact 4G kids GPS smart watch designed to provide caregivers with continuous location awareness and emergency calling while feeding location and status data into Plaspy. Its hybrid positioning and cellular connectivity aim to maintain useful tracking in outdoor and urban settings while the wearable form factor supports everyday use.

- Hybrid positioning using GPS, A GPS, LBS, and WiFi to improve location results across environments.
- 4G cellular connectivity with fallback to 3G and 2G for ongoing uplink of location and telemetry.
- Dedicated SOS emergency button to place priority calls and generate alerts for caregivers.
- Device health reporting such as battery level and connectivity status for proactive monitoring.
- Child friendly hardware including a 1.4 inch IPS touchscreen and IPX7 waterproof rating.
- Plaspy compatibility for real time tracking, historical route review, and event visibility.

## Core Features of Sentar - D31

- Plaspy compatible tracker that streams positioning and status updates into a Plaspy account.
- Multi mode positioning: GPS, A GPS, location by cell tower LBS, and WiFi assisted fixes.
- Cellular support across 4G FDD with fallback to 3G and 2G to maintain connectivity.
- Integrated SOS power button for fast emergency calling and alert generation.
- On device platform details included in manufacturer specs such as SL8521E chipset and Android 4.4.
- 1.4 inch IPS touchscreen for on device interaction and status displays.
- 700mAh battery with magnetic 4 pin charging for convenient recharge cycles.
- IPX7 waterproof rating and a compact wearable form factor suitable for children.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the D31 is connected to Plaspy, the device acts as a wearable positioning source that provides ongoing location and status information to the platform. Plaspy ingests the device positions and events so caregivers and administrators can monitor location, review history, and receive priority alerts from a single dashboard.

- Real time location updates are presented on Plaspy maps for live tracking and situational awareness.
- SOS emergency events from the watch are forwarded as high priority alerts with timestamp and last known location.
- Hybrid positioning improves location coverage in urban and indoor edge cases and is reflected in Plaspy positions.
- Device telemetry such as battery level and connectivity status is available in Plaspy to help manage uptime.
- Event and communication logs from the device can be surfaced in Plaspy for review and audit.
