# Sentar - Q60 Pet

<div className="device-logo">
![Q60 Pet](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Q60 Pet is a compact GPS tracker engineered for pet owners who want continuous, Plaspy compatible real-time tracking and fast location visibility. It combines multi-mode positioning with GSM GPRS communications to provide location updates both outdoors and in challenging urban or indoor environments. The device is built around the MTK2503 chipset and supports GPS, BDS, LBS and WiFi assisted positioning, with Micro SIM connectivity and a built in 500 mAh polymer battery for everyday use.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Q60 Pet sends position and basic telemetry into the Plaspy platform so you can monitor movement, view location history, set safe zones, and receive timely alerts. Its small form factor and practical battery life make it suitable for collar or harness mounting while Plaspy handles the mapping, alerting, and device visibility from a single dashboard.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real time tracking and visibility on the Plaspy platform.
- Multi mode positioning combining GPS and BDS with LBS and WiFi assisted fixes for improved coverage.
- Compact, pet friendly design that is suitable for attachment to collars and harnesses.
- GSM GPRS connectivity via Micro SIM for wide cellular network compatibility.
- Built in 500 mAh polymer battery offering a balance of runtime and lightweight form factor.
- Outdoor positioning accuracy typically in the single digit to low double digit meters range.
- Fallback LBS and WiFi positioning to provide location when GPS is unavailable.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Q60 Pet transmits location and basic telemetry over the cellular network to the Plaspy platform, which displays updates on maps, timelines, and alert feeds. Plaspy aggregates the device's multi mode reports so you can maintain visibility of a pet's location both outdoors and in more difficult signal environments, and it surfaces battery status so you can manage device uptime.

- Real time location updates displayed on Plaspy maps for live pet tracking.
- Multi mode position reports available in Plaspy to improve indoor and urban visibility.
- Battery level telemetry shown in Plaspy to help plan recharging and avoid downtime.
- Safe zone and geofencing alerts configurable in Plaspy to notify departures from allowed areas.
- Timeline and historical position views for reviewing movement patterns and events.

## Typical Use Cases

- Pet safety and recovery for dogs and cats during walks, roaming, or if lost.
- Improved indoor and urban location awareness using WiFi and LBS fallbacks.
- Active monitoring of pet movement patterns for routine checks and caregiver peace of mind.
- Day trips and outings where a lightweight collar mounted tracker is preferred.
- Managing multiple household pets from a single Plaspy account for centralized oversight.
