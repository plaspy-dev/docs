# Sentar - D32 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Sentar D32 and how the device integrates with Plaspy for real time location, two way communication, and event reporting. It is written to help fleet managers, safety teams, and end users understand the practical capabilities of the D32 when used with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model, and how the device is installed or configured by the manufacturer. Users should consider those variables when planning deployments and consult the manufacturer for device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The D32 is a wrist worn 4G Android GPS smartwatch that combines multi mode positioning with cellular voice and data for continuous location updates, direct communication, and emergency alerting. When connected to Plaspy, these capabilities provide real time situational awareness and incident context for personnel tracking and safety workflows.

- Continuous, real time tracking from a compact wearable suitable for on foot personnel
- Multi mode positioning using GPS, AGPS, LBS, and Wi Fi to improve location reliability
- 4G voice and data for two way calls and video that can be associated with events
- Configurable SOS power button to trigger immediate alerts and two way contact
- Integrated camera for image capture and short video clips to support incident verification
- IPX7 water resistance and rechargeable battery for daily use in outdoor conditions

## Core Features of Sentar - D32

- Plaspy compatible wearable tracker for live location and event streaming
- Multi mode location technologies: GPS plus AGPS, LBS, and Wi Fi assisted positioning
- Cellular connectivity across 4G/3G/2G networks with a Nano SIM slot for voice and data
- Two way voice calling and video calling capability supported by the onboard camera
- SOS configurable physical button for immediate alert generation and response
- 1.4 inch IPS touchscreen for local device interaction and status visibility
- IPX7 rated water resistance for splash and short immersion protection
- 700mAh rechargeable battery with magnetic charging for routine charging workflows

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests location, status, and event data from compatible devices like the D32 and surfaces that information in maps, timelines, and alerting tools to improve operational visibility. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and routes device events into dashboards and incident workflows so teams can monitor people alongside other assets.

- Live positions and assisted location inputs displayed on Plaspy maps for situational awareness
- SOS alerts and related event data routed into Plaspy incident timelines for rapid response
- Two way voice and video activity referenced in Plaspy incident records to aid verification
- Battery and device status reported into Plaspy for health monitoring and uptime oversight
- Camera images and short videos associated with location events to support documentation

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and school commute monitoring with SOS alerting
- Elderly or vulnerable person location tracking and rapid help requests
- Lone worker protection and remote verification for field staff
- On foot personnel and last mile courier tracking to complement vehicle fleets
- Event and excursion oversight for groups of staff or volunteers

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior and menu options can differ by firmware version and software build
- Regional hardware variants and network banding may affect cellular performance and calling
- Some features such as video streaming and media uploads depend on mobile data availability

Pairing the Sentar D32 with Plaspy gives organizations a wearable option for continuous personal tracking, immediate alerting, and direct communication. The combination of multi mode positioning, cellular voice and video, and SOS alerting helps teams maintain awareness of personnel movements, verify incidents quickly, and coordinate responses from a unified platform.
