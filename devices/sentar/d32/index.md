# Sentar - D32

![D32](./tracker.png)

The D32 is a Plaspy compatible 4G Android GPS smartwatch designed for reliable, real-time tracking, two-way communication, and personal safety monitoring. As a compact wearable, the D32 combines multi-mode positioning \(GPS, AGPS, LBS, Wi‑Fi\) with cellular voice and data to deliver continuous location updates and emergency alerts that integrate directly into Plaspy for live situational awareness.

The D32 smartwatch balances everyday usability and safety features: a 1.4‑inch IPS touchscreen, a 30W camera for video calling and image capture, an SOS-configurable power button, and IPX7 water resistance. With 4G connectivity and a Nano SIM slot, the D32 works with Plaspy to surface real-time location, telemetry, and event-driven alerts for personnel tracking, lone worker protection, childcare or eldercare monitoring, and on-foot fleet personnel management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker in a wearable form factor — continuous, real-time tracking from a wrist-worn device.
- Multi-mode positioning \(GPS + AGPS + LBS + Wi‑Fi\) for stronger location fidelity in urban and indoor edge cases.
- 4G/3G/2G cellular connectivity \(Nano SIM\) enabling HD voice, video calling, and data streaming to Plaspy dashboards.
- SOS emergency button that can be configured for immediate alerting and two-way voice contact through Plaspy workflows.
- 30W camera for basic image capture and live video that enhances incident verification and remote assistance.
- 700mAh battery with magnetic 4‑pin charging for easy daily use and reliable uptime for telemetry and alerts.
- IPX7 splash and short immersion protection — suitable for everyday wear and outdoor conditions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the D32 becomes a cloud-connected GPS tracker that streams location, status, and event data over cellular networks. Plaspy ingests GNSS and assisted location inputs, maps live positions, and triggers alerts or reports based on SOS events, geofence crossings, or predefined schedules. Video/photo captures and voice calls generated by the D32 can be referenced in Plaspy incident timelines to accelerate verification and response.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates via GPS + AGPS + LBS + Wi‑Fi
- Configurable SOS emergency alerts through the physical power/SOS button
- Two-way voice and HD video calling over 4G for direct communication linked to Plaspy incident records
- Battery and device status telemetry relayed to Plaspy for uptime and health monitoring
- Camera images and short video clips associated with location events inside Plaspy reports

## Use Cases

- Child safety and school commuting — parents and administrators use Plaspy to monitor routes and receive SOS notifications.
- Elderly or vulnerable person monitoring — location, fall-prone situations, and immediate help requests routed through Plaspy.
- Lone worker and field staff tracking — on-foot personnel tracked in real time; video/voice improves remote support and incident documentation.
- On-foot fleet personnel management — complements vehicle GPS trackers by monitoring drivers, couriers, or inspectors while they are away from vehicles.
- Event and excursion oversight — organizers maintain position awareness and rapid incident response for groups in outdoor or urban environments.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The D32 offers a wearable GPS tracker option that is Plaspy compatible for organizations and families that need continuous personal tracking and direct communication. Its multi-mode positioning and cellular 4G connectivity deliver dependable real-time tracking and voice/video capabilities that enrich Plaspy incident workflows. The SOS button and camera add fast verification and response options, while IPX7 protection and a 700mAh battery keep the device practical for daily use.

For fleet management scenarios that require vehicle-specific telemetry — such as ignition status, immobilizer control, or fuel monitoring — vehicle-mounted trackers with dedicated I/O and sensor inputs remain the appropriate choice. The D32, however, fills a different but complementary role: it provides telemetric insight and safety features for people on the move, and it integrates into Plaspy to give operators unified situational awareness across personnel and assets.
