# Sentar - D32

![D32](./tracker.png)

The Sentar D32 is a wrist worn 4G Android GPS smartwatch designed for continuous location monitoring, two way communication, and personal safety. As a compact wearable it combines multi mode positioning with cellular voice and data, an SOS configurable power button, a 1.4 inch IPS touchscreen, and a camera to support live situational awareness and basic image capture.

Because the D32 streams real time location and event data over cellular networks and supports emergency alerts and voice or video contact, it is a practical Plaspy compatible device for organizations that need personnel visibility. Plaspy can ingest the D32 location and event signals to present live positions, alerts, and incident context alongside other assets in a unified fleet management view.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable 4G Android GPS tracker suitable for continuous personal tracking from the wrist.
- Multi mode positioning including GPS, assisted location, cellular based location, and Wi Fi to improve location coverage in urban or indoor edge cases.
- Built in camera and voice capability to provide visual and audio context for incidents and remote assistance.
- Configurable SOS button for immediate alerting and two way contact tied into Plaspy workflows.
- IPX7 water resistance and a compact touchscreen for everyday use and quick status checks.
- On device battery and charging design intended for regular daily operation and telemetry continuity.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the D32 becomes a cloud connected wearable that provides live location, status updates, and event driven alerts to operators. Plaspy collects position and event data from the device, maps movements, and ties alerts and media into incident timelines for faster response and reporting.

- Live location and positioning updates displayed on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- SOS emergency alerts routed into Plaspy incident workflows for rapid notification
- Two way voice and video interactions associated with device events to support verification and remote assistance
- Battery and device status shared with Plaspy for device health monitoring and uptime awareness
- Camera images and short video references attached to location events inside Plaspy reports

## Typical Use Cases

- Child monitoring and school commuting programs where guardians and administrators need location and emergency alerts
- Elder care and vulnerable person oversight with quick alerting and two way contact options
- Lone worker or field staff tracking where on foot personnel require continuous visibility and incident verification
- On foot fleet personnel management to complement vehicle trackers by monitoring couriers, inspectors, and drivers when away from vehicles
- Event and group oversight to maintain position awareness and coordinate responses during outdoor or urban activities

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The D32 is a sensible choice for teams and families that require a wearable form factor with integrated communication and emergency features. Its multi mode positioning and cellular connectivity provide Plaspy with consistent location feeds and event signals that improve operational awareness for personnel tracking and safety monitoring.

While vehicle specific telemetry is best served by dedicated vehicle trackers, the D32 fills a complementary role by extending monitoring and rapid communication to people on the move. Combined with Plaspy, the device helps unify situational awareness across personnel and assets in a single operational view.
