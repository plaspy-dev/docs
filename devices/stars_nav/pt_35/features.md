# Stars Nav - PT-35 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the Stars Nav PT-35 tracker with Plaspy. It focuses on the practical capabilities you can expect to see reported into Plaspy and explains how the device is typically used in real time tracking, alerting, and historical reporting scenarios.

Exact feature availability for any given unit can vary depending on firmware version, hardware revision, how the device is installed and wired, and manufacturer implementation choices. For device specific limits, optional accessories, and the latest firmware behavior consult the official Stars Nav documentation linked at the end of the page.

## Feature Overview

The PT-35 is a compact, purpose built personal tracker that delivers position and event telemetry to Plaspy using GSM data and messaging. It is optimized for low power schedule driven operation and offers flexible I O for panic, alarms, and sensor integration, making it suitable for discreet personal safety and asset monitoring deployments.

- GPRS and SMS reporting to forward location and alert data into Plaspy for live maps and history.
- Ultra low power operation with configurable timer and deep sleep modes for extended standby life.
- Compact rugged housing with an optional waterproof magnetic case for covert or harsh environment installs.
- Rich digital and analog inputs plus multi I O support for panic inputs, alarms, and external sensor telemetry.
- Built in voice and panic support to pair location updates with hands free audio and immediate alerts.
- Onboard storage for thousands of position and activation messages to preserve history during offline periods.

## Core Features of Stars Nav - PT-35

- u‑blox GNSS positioning for accurate location reporting and reliable satellite fixes.
- Quad band GSM modem supporting GPRS TCP IP and SMS for flexible reporting into Plaspy.
- Ultra low power modes including schedule driven wake, deep sleep timer, and long standby options.
- Comprehensive I O set including digital input, analog input, external panic input, and multi I O connector options.
- Panic button support plus built in microphone and speaker for hands free voice calls and alerting.
- Onboard message buffering with capacity for thousands of stored position and activation records.
- USB power and charging support for a 3.7V Li ion battery plus external battery input options.
- Optional waterproof magnetic housing for covert mounting and improved environmental protection.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the PT-35 forwards its location and event telemetry using GSM data or SMS so that Plaspy can display live positions, raise alerts, and retain historical records. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols so incoming reports appear in the dashboard without manual protocol selection.

- Real time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via GPRS or SMS for live map visibility.
- Panic alerts and hands free voice events appear alongside location so response teams can see status and context in Plaspy.
- Buffered message upload ensures stored positions and activations are forwarded to Plaspy after reconnection for complete history.
- Digital and analog input states reported into Plaspy provide visibility into external sensor events such as door, ignition, or other wired signals.
- Schedule wake, motion or panic triggered wakes translate into event driven reporting visible in Plaspy activity logs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking with immediate panic alerts and optional voice communication for rapid response.
- Asset and equipment tracking where covert installation and long standby are required.
- Motorcycle, watercraft, and small vehicle monitoring when a compact rugged tracker is preferred.
- Field staff and security operations that need real time location plus voice support for coordination.
- Telemetry collection for simple external sensors using analog and digital inputs routed into Plaspy dashboards.
- Recovery and incident review using buffered message upload after temporary network loss.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions can enable or disable specific behaviors and reporting intervals; verify which firmware your unit is running.
- Hardware revisions and optional accessories such as the waterproof magnetic housing may change mountability and environmental ratings.
- I O and sensor availability depend on how the device is wired during installation; installers control which inputs are used.
- Voice, SMS and GPRS functionality rely on cellular network coverage and the operator service plan in the deployment region.
- Stored message capacity and battery life figures are influenced by reporting frequency, GNSS fix time, and operating conditions.

Pairing the PT-35 with Plaspy gives organizations and individuals a practical way to centralize location, alerting, and simple telemetry from a compact, low power tracker. The combination is well suited to scenarios that require discreet installation, long standby intervals, and a mix of immediate alerts plus historical reporting for investigations or audits.
