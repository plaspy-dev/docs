# Stars Nav - PT-35

![PT-35](./tracker.jpg)

The PT-35 from Stars Nav is a compact, rugged personal GPS tracker designed for reliable deployments where discretion and long battery life are priorities. Based on the PT series description, the device combines a u‑blox GNSS receiver with a quad band GSM modem to provide accurate location and event telemetry suitable for personal safety, asset security and light vehicle monitoring. Its small form factor and optional waterproof magnetic housing make it suitable for covert or harsh environment use.

This model is designed to work with Plaspy as a compatible tracker by delivering location reports, panic alerts and stored messages over GPRS TCP IP and SMS channels. That capability allows the PT-35 to feed live position and event data into Plaspy for real time maps, centralized alerts and historical reporting. The unit’s low power modes, buffered message storage and flexible I/O make it a practical choice for scenarios where continuous connectivity cannot be assumed.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for GPRS and SMS delivery of location and event data
- Long life operation with configurable deep sleep and timer driven modes
- Compact rugged design with optional waterproof magnetic housing for covert placement
- Rich telemetry I O for alarms, motion detection and external sensor inputs
- Panic and voice support for immediate alerting and hands free calls
- Onboard storage for thousands of position and activation messages for later upload
- Flexible power options including USB charging and support for external battery inputs

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the PT-35 sends periodic or event driven updates to your Plaspy account so operators can monitor position, receive alerts and review history. The tracker can be set to wake on a schedule, on motion, or on a panic input and then forward GPS coordinates and status messages to Plaspy by GPRS or SMS for immediate processing.

- Real time location updates and telemetry delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and monitoring
- Panic button and hands free voice events forwarded to Plaspy to support rapid response workflows
- Motion and tamper alerts generate immediate notifications in Plaspy for operational awareness
- Buffered message upload restores historical data to Plaspy after network reconnection
- Digital and analog I O readings can be surfaced in Plaspy dashboards and reports as status indicators

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety and lone worker protection with panic alerts routed to Plaspy
- Asset and equipment tracking for tools, containers and rental gear using covert mounting
- Monitoring small vehicles and special purpose craft where compact size and standby life matter
- Security and field staff coordination with live position and event alerts
- Simple telemetry collection such as sensor states or alarm inputs for centralized reporting

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT-35 pairs a small rugged package with flexible telemetry options and long standby capability, making it a sensible choice for organizations and individuals who need discreet, reliable tracking integrated with a centralized platform. Its support for both data and messaging transport, plus onboard message buffering, helps maintain continuity of location history in environments with intermittent coverage.
