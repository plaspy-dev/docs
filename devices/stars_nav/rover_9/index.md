# Stars Nav - Rover 9

<div className="device-logo">
![Rover 9](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Rover 9 is a compact, professional grade vehicle tracker from Stars Nav designed for fleets, security providers, and businesses that need dependable real time tracking. It combines a SiRFstarIII GNSS module with GPRS communication and over the air programming to deliver consistent positioning, telemetry reporting, and remote configuration for vehicles and portable assets.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Rover 9 can feed continuous location and event data into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring, alerting, and reporting. Its wide input voltage range and built in backup battery plus multiple alarm and analog inputs make it well suited to the operational visibility and security workflows that fleet managers build on Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible vehicle tracker offering consistent satellite positioning with a SiRFstarIII GNSS module.
- Wide 6 to 37 V input range and a built in backup battery providing several hours of portable operation when vehicle power is lost.
- GPRS communication with power saving capabilities for continuous reporting while controlling data use.
- Multiple alarm inputs, immobilizer control, and panic input to support anti theft and security response procedures.
- Analog inputs for fuel level and temperature monitoring to supply telemetry useful for logistics and cost control.
- Over the air programming and updates for remote configuration and scalable fleet maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the Rover 9 supplies location, event, and analog telemetry data that Plaspy uses to populate dashboards, trigger alerts, and build historical reports. This enables operational teams to maintain visibility and act on vehicle events from a single platform.

- Live location feeds and history for vehicle tracking and route reconstruction.
- Security event reporting from door, alarm, immobilizer, and panic inputs to support rapid response.
- Analog telemetry such as fuel and temperature available to Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and compliance workflows.
- Driver identification and communication events logged for accountability and operational context.
- Remote configuration and over the air updates reduce the need for physical device access while maintaining Plaspy integration.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and utilization tracking across cars, trucks, and motorcycles.
- Anti theft protection and recovery with immobilizer control and backup battery reporting.
- Insurance telematics and driver behavior programs using event logs and telemetry.
- Cold chain and temperature sensitive logistics supported by analog temperature inputs.
- Security patrols and asset protection where reliable remote reporting is required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Rover 9 is a practical choice for organizations that need a durable, feature rich tracker that integrates with Plaspy for centralized monitoring and reporting. Its combination of reliable GNSS positioning, multiple alarm and analog inputs, and remote update capability helps maintain continuous visibility and reduces operational overhead for fleet and security teams.
