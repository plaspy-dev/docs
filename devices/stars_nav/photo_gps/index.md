# Stars Nav - Photo GPS

![Photo GPS](./logo.jpg)

The Stars Nav Photo GPS is a portable GPS logger designed to simplify photo geotagging. It appears to a computer as removable storage, uses micro SD cards for expandable capacity, and stores location data in a plain text log that pairs with image files. The device is compact and lightweight, with multiple LED indicators for status and a rechargeable battery offering extended operation, making it convenient for field use and travel photography.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Photo GPS can be used to bring photographic location data into Plaspy for mapping, review, and reporting. Its simple file format and plug and play workflow make it straightforward to incorporate into Plaspy workflows for asset documentation, route reconstruction, and photo location verification without requiring complex configuration.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play operation via PC with the device recognized as removable storage for easy file access.
- Micro SD card based storage allowing expandable capacity to hold large photo collections.
- Plain text log files that include longitude latitude altitude time speed and direction for straightforward import.
- Compact lightweight design with four LED indicators for GPS memory charging and status feedback.
- Rechargeable battery providing long operation time suitable for day long use and extended outings.
- Compatibility with common geo tagging and web album services such as Locr Picasa and Google Panoramio.
- High performance GPS chipset for reliable positional data collection during photo shoots.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the Photo GPS can provide historical location traces and photo timestamps that enhance mapping and reporting workflows. Plaspy can ingest the device's exported log files or read associated photo timestamps to place images on a timeline and map, improving situational awareness and documentation.

- Import text log files from the device into Plaspy to visualize tracks and photo points.
- Match photo timestamps to logged locations to geotag images and build an organized photo timeline.
- Use mapped photo locations within Plaspy for route review photographic evidence and operational oversight.
- Generate reports and exportable summaries that include photographed locations for audits or client delivery.
- Combine Photo GPS data with other tracked assets in Plaspy to create consolidated views of field activity.

## Typical Use Cases

- Travel and landscape photographers who want an easy way to geotag large photo collections.
- Field documentation for inspections site surveys and progress photography.
- Real estate and property photographers needing reliable location records for image libraries.
- Research and expedition teams collecting visual documentation with location context.
- Media teams assembling photo-based storylines with mapped imagery for editorial work.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Stars Nav Photo GPS is a practical option for teams and individuals who prioritize a simple workflow for capturing and organizing photo location data. Its removable storage approach and plain text logging make the device interoperable with a variety of tools, and that simplicity translates into an easy path to bring data into Plaspy for visualization and reporting.

Because the device focuses on straightforward geotagging and expandable storage rather than complex telemetry, it fits well where photographic documentation and location accuracy are the primary needs. If your use case emphasizes photo-centric location records and you prefer a plug and play device, the Photo GPS is a compatible choice to integrate with Plaspy for mapping and operational insights.
