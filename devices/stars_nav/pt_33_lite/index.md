# Stars Nav - PT-33 Lite

<div className="device-logo">
![PT-33 Lite](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The PT-33 Lite is a compact, SMS based GPS tracker designed for reliable personal safety and low bandwidth real time tracking. It supports on demand location requests by call or SMS and issues event alerts such as panic button activations, motion or immobility notifications, geo fence triggers, and speed alarms. The device emphasizes straightforward operation and quick actionable position reporting where continuous data connections are limited.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the PT-33 Lite can feed its SMS and call based location reports and alerts into Plaspy dashboards and workflows. That compatibility lets organizations and families centralize monitoring, receive safety alerts, and log position updates within Plaspy without relying on continuous mobile data, making it a practical choice for simple safety monitoring and low bandwidth tracking scenarios.

## Key Highlights

- SMS first design delivers on demand GPS coordinates and event alerts without continuous data usage.
- Immediate location requests by call or text for fast, actionable position reporting.
- Dedicated panic button that sends emergency alerts when triggered for a short press.
- Motion detection and immobility alerts with audible feedback and subsequent SMS notification.
- Configurable geo fence and speed alarms, including a configurable overspeed threshold up to 120 km/h.
- Compact form factor suitable for personal wear or small asset attachment and simple maintenance.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The PT-33 Lite communicates location and events via SMS or call triggered responses. When integrated with Plaspy, those messages can be forwarded into Plaspy intake channels so that location points and event notifications appear on maps, in alert workflows, and within reports. This model suits deployments that need dependable, low bandwidth position updates and clear safety notifications rather than continuous telemetry streams.

- Real time location updates delivered by SMS in response to calls or text requests and shown on Plaspy maps.
- Panic and emergency alerts forwarded into Plaspy to trigger notifications and incident workflows.
- Motion and immobility alerts converted into Plaspy events for monitoring worker or asset movement patterns.
- Geo fence and speed alarm messages synchronized to Plaspy event logs for rule based oversight.
- Simple SMS and phone command interface that complements Plaspy monitoring where minimal configuration is preferred.

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety monitoring for children or elderly relatives using easy location requests and a panic button.
- Lone worker tracking where emergency alerts and intermittent position reports are essential but data is limited.
- Basic asset tracking for small equipment or personal property that requires periodic location checks.
- Light fleet scenarios focused on specific personnel or portable assets needing low bandwidth tracking.
- Short term rentals and shared equipment oversight using geo fence and speed alerts to enforce usage rules.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The PT-33 Lite offers a pragmatic combination of simple operation and essential safety features that align well with Plaspy monitoring and reporting. Its SMS based approach reduces ongoing data requirements and lowers operational complexity while still providing immediate location reports and event alerts that Plaspy can surface in maps, logs, and notifications.

For organizations and families looking for Plaspy compatible tracking that prioritizes safety, low bandwidth operation, and straightforward alerting, the PT-33 Lite is a useful option. It is particularly suited to scenarios where core location and emergency reporting matter more than continuous telemetry or advanced vehicle integrations.
