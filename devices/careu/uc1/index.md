# CAREU - UC1

![UC1](./tracker.png)

The CAREU UC1 is a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and smart electronic cargo lock engineered for secure container and asset sealing. Manufactured in Taiwan, the UC1 pairs robust mechanical protection—a stainless steel chain and strong magnet mounting—with global real-time tracking and tamper detection to deliver continuous visibility for cargo, trailers, vans and intermodal containers. Plaspy users can add the UC1 to their fleet management mix to receive immediate alerts and location updates that improve anti-theft response and operational control.

The UC1 is purpose-built for logistics and cargo security workflows. It supports multiple unlocking methods \(RFID cards/tags, Bluetooth/mobile phone, and remote commands from fleet platforms\), global 4G \(Cat 1\) connectivity with eSIM option, and a large rechargeable battery for long deployments. Rugged IP68 and IK07 ratings keep telemetry and alerting reliable in harsh conditions, making the UC1 a versatile GPS tracker for Plaspy-compatible real-time tracking and asset management deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and electronic cargo lock for integrated fleet management and anti-theft workflows.
- Global real-time tracking over 4G Cat 1 with 3G/2G fallback and optional eSIM for wide-area coverage.
- Multi-method unlocking: RFID whitelist \(up to 10 groups\), Bluetooth/mobile unlocking, and remote platform commands.
- Rugged design with IP68 dust/water resistance and IK07 impact rating to withstand demanding logistics environments.
- Advanced tamper detection: chain-cut \(shear-proof cable\) detection, vibration/tamper alarms and low-battery alerts.
- Large internal 6600 mAh rechargeable battery with Type-C charging for extended field operation and flexible reporting intervals.
- Compact, lightweight enclosure and magnetic mounting enable reusable, easy installation across assets.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the CAREU UC1 delivers location, security events and device telemetry to the Plaspy platform in near real time. The UC1's GNSS and cellular uplink feed Plaspy with position fixes, while built-in sensors notify the platform of door openings, tamper events and chain-cut incidents. Plaspy users can configure alerts, automated workflows and reports that combine UC1 inputs with other fleet telemetry to improve situational awareness and theft prevention.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(GNSS fixes forwarded to Plaspy over 4G/3G/2G\).
- Door opening, tamper, vibration and chain-cut alerts for immediate anti-theft notification.
- RFID and Bluetooth unlocking events synchronized with Plaspy logs for access auditing.
- Remote commands from fleet management platforms \(e.g., lock/unlock notifications and state changes\) via the device’s cellular connection.
- Device health and battery status reporting to support maintenance scheduling and long-term deployments.

## Use Cases

- Container and trailer security: continuous monitoring of door status, tamper events and chain integrity to prevent theft during transit or storage.
- Intermodal cargo tracking: reusable, easy-to-install lock and GPS tracker for containers moved between ships, rail and road.
- High-value cargo protection: immediate chain-cut and tamper alerts routed to Plaspy for rapid incident response and recovery.
- Access control logging: RFID and Bluetooth unlock events integrated with Plaspy to audit who opened a container and when.
- Fleet management augmentation: correlate UC1 security events and location telemetry with other fleet data \(telemetry, fuel monitoring systems, route analytics\) in Plaspy to improve operational oversight.
