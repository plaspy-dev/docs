# CAREU - UC1 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the CAREU UC1 when used with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical capabilities and common operational uses of the UC1 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker and electronic cargo lock, and explains how its events and telemetry appear in the Plaspy platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, regional variant, and how the manufacturer implements specific functions. For device-specific technical details and the latest firmware information consult the manufacturer documentation linked at the end of this page.

## Feature Overview

The CAREU UC1 combines a mechanical cargo lock with integrated GNSS tracking and cellular connectivity to deliver continuous location visibility plus security events useful for logistics and asset protection. It is designed for reuse across trailers, containers, and other transported assets where both seal integrity and position tracking matter.

- Real-time GNSS location reporting for continuous position visibility during transit and storage.
- Electronic cargo lock with multiple unlocking methods including RFID and Bluetooth mobile unlocking.
- Tamper and chain-cut detection that generates immediate alerts for potential security incidents.
- Rugged construction with IP68 water resistance and IK07 impact protection for harsh environments.
- Long-life rechargeable battery with USB Type-C charging to support extended deployments.

## Core Features of CAREU - UC1

- GNSS positioning using multi-constellation satellites for reliable location fixes across regions.
- Global cellular connectivity with 4G Cat 1 and fallback to 3G/2G plus optional eSIM provisioning for broad coverage.
- Multi-method unlocking: RFID whitelist support, Bluetooth mobile phone unlocking, and remote platform commands.
- Tamper detection including chain-cut detection and vibration/tamper alarms for anti-theft monitoring.
- Large internal rechargeable battery with Type-C charging to support long field deployments and scheduled reporting.
- Mechanical security elements including a stainless steel chain and strong magnetic mounting for reusable installations.
- Rugged environmental ratings (IP68 and IK07) to maintain telemetry and alerts in demanding logistics conditions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the UC1 is integrated into Plaspy, its location fixes and security events are forwarded to the platform so fleet and operations teams can monitor assets in near real time. Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and ingests device reports for visualization, alerting, and historical logging.

- Real-time location updates appear on Plaspy maps and timelines for route tracking and asset visibility.
- Tamper, chain-cut, and vibration alerts are delivered as events in Plaspy for rapid notification and incident handling.
- Unlocking events (RFID and Bluetooth) are synchronized as audit entries to record access activity.
- Device health and battery status reports are available to schedule maintenance and replace units proactively.
- Remote lock state changes and platform-initiated commands are reflected in Plaspy logs to support operational workflows.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer security monitoring to detect unauthorized openings and chain integrity breaches.
- Intermodal cargo tracking where a reusable mechanical lock with telemetry is needed across ship, rail, and road.
- High-value shipment protection with immediate alerts routed to operations for faster incident response.
- Access logging and audit trails by recording RFID and Bluetooth unlock events alongside location history.
- Long-term deployments where rugged hardware and a large rechargeable battery reduce maintenance cycles.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some functions may vary by firmware level; capabilities such as unlocking options and alert thresholds are often firmware dependent.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect supported cellular bands, eSIM availability, and GNSS performance.
- Installation method and mounting location influence GNSS reception and tamper detection performance.
- Manufacturer configuration choices at provisioning time can enable or disable specific features such as RFID group limits or remote command behavior.
- Always confirm the exact feature set for a specific serial number or device batch with the official manufacturer documentation.

Pairing the CAREU UC1 with Plaspy gives operations a combined view of location and security events so teams can act on potential theft or tamper incidents quickly while maintaining a detailed audit trail.
