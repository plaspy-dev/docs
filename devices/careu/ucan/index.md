# CAREU - UCAN

<div className="device-logo">
![UCAN](./tracker.png)
</div>

The CAREU UCAN is a plug and play OBD-II GPS tracker engineered for rapid deployment across vehicle fleets. It provides continuous location reporting and vehicle telemetry by connecting directly to a standard OBD-II port, delivering position, engine and ignition status, and OBD diagnostics to a central tracking platform. The UCAN is designed with fleet operations in mind, combining onboard logging, accelerometer-driven event detection, and cellular connectivity to keep vehicles visible and monitored.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the UCAN feeds its location and telemetry streams into Plaspy for consolidated fleet oversight. Its out-of-the-box OBD-II integration and built-in sensors make it suitable for organizations that want fast rollout and immediate visibility in Plaspy dashboards for tracking, alerts, and reporting without complex hardware changes.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD-II installation for fast fleet rollout and minimal downtime, compatible with Plaspy.
- Cellular connectivity with modern 4G LTE variants plus fallback options and support for optional eSIM provisioning.
- Access to OBD-II vehicle data such as mileage, RPM, speed, fuel level, working hours, and DTC diagnostic codes for maintenance insight.
- Built-in 6-axis accelerometer to detect harsh acceleration, harsh braking, impacts, and aggressive cornering for safety monitoring.
- Large onboard log memory to preserve position history during network interruptions, supporting continuity of records.
- Optional Bluetooth 4.0 (4G variant) and remote FOTA management for sensor integration and centralized device updates.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed in a vehicle, the UCAN collects GNSS position and OBD telemetry and transmits that data over cellular networks to Plaspy. Fleet managers and operations teams can then access that information from the Plaspy platform for real-time monitoring, historical analysis, and configured alerts. Integration is focused on delivering continuous visibility and actionable events rather than requiring additional hardware or complex integrations.

- Real-time location and telemetry pushed to Plaspy for live fleet tracking and route monitoring.
- Engine and ignition status plus OBD diagnostics available in Plaspy for maintenance workflows and fault awareness.
- Fuel and odometer readings feed fuel monitoring and cost analysis reports within Plaspy.
- Accelerometer events reported to Plaspy for driver scorecards, safety alerts, and incident review.
- Bluetooth sensor data (where available) surfaced in Plaspy dashboards to enrich telemetry with accessory inputs.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for logistics, courier, and delivery operations using live tracking and telemetry.
- Driver behavior and safety programs leveraging accelerometer events for coaching and incident analysis.
- Fuel monitoring and maintenance scheduling using OBD-derived fuel level, odometer, and diagnostic codes.
- Anti-theft and security monitoring with remote visibility, event logging, and optional accessory control for immobilization workflows.
- Cold chain or time sensitive deliveries where location history and timely alerts enhance operational reliability when combined with Plaspy notifications.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CAREU UCAN combines easy installation with the depth of vehicle-level telemetry, making it a practical option for fleets that need immediate data in Plaspy without complex wiring or lengthy setup. Its ability to report location, OBD diagnostics, and accelerometer events gives operators the core inputs needed for tracking, maintenance planning, and driver safety programs.
