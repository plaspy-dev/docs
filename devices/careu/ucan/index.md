# CAREU - UCAN

![UCAN](./tracker.png)

The CAREU UCAN GPS Tracker is a Plaspy compatible, plug-and-play OBD-II tracker built for fast deployment across vehicle fleets. Designed to deliver reliable real-time tracking and vehicle telemetry, the UCAN connects directly to any standard OBD-II port to begin supplying location, engine/ignition status, and OBD diagnostics to Plaspy for immediate visibility into fleet operations and security.

With 4G LTE \(Cat.1 / Cat.M\) connectivity and fallback to 3G/2G where required, optional eSIM support, a built-in GPS/GSM antenna, and a 6-axis accelerometer, the UCAN is engineered for fleet management, anti-theft monitoring, driver behavior analysis, and fuel monitoring — all accessible through your Plaspy dashboard for consolidated reporting and alerts.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible plug-and-play OBD-II installation for fast fleet rollout and minimal downtime.
- 4G LTE Cat.1 / Cat.M connectivity with 3G/2G fallback \(variant-dependent\) and optional eSIM for flexible roaming and provisioning.
- Reads OBD-II vehicle data — mileage/odometer, RPM, vehicle speed, fuel level, working hours and DTC error codes — enabling robust telemetry and maintenance insight.
- Built-in 6-axis accelerometer detects harsh acceleration, harsh braking, impact events, harsh cornering and flip-over conditions for driver behavior and safety monitoring.
- Large onboard log memory: up to 200,000 position logs \(4G version\) and 150,000 logs \(3/2G version\) to preserve history during network interruptions.
- Bluetooth 4.0 \(4G variant\) for integration with Bluetooth sensors and accessories where sensor telemetry is required.
- Supports remote configuration and FOTA \(firmware over-the-air\) updates via FTP for centralized management and reduced maintenance trips.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The UCAN integrates directly with Plaspy to provide continuous, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker data streams. Once plugged into the vehicle OBD-II port, the device collects GNSS position and OBD telemetry, then transmits secure packets to Plaspy over cellular networks. Fleet managers can view real-time tracking, configure alerts, and generate reports from Plaspy without additional hardware changes.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates pushed to Plaspy for live fleet tracking and route monitoring.
- Engine/ignition status detection and OBD-II diagnostics \(DTC codes, RPM, speed\) for maintenance and compliance workflows.
- Fuel monitoring via OBD-level fuel level and odometer readings to support fuel management and cost analysis.
- Harsh driving and impact events captured by the 6-axis accelerometer and reported to Plaspy for driver scorecards and safety alerts.
- Bluetooth 4.0 support \(4G variant\) to connect Bluetooth sensors or beacons and surface sensor data inside Plaspy dashboards.

## Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring for logistics, couriers, and delivery services using real-time tracking and telemetry.
- Vaccine and temperature-sensitive distribution where location history and delivery timing are critical \(combine with Plaspy alerts\).
- Driver behavior and safety programs using accelerometer-driven event detection for harsh braking, acceleration and cornering.
- Fuel monitoring and maintenance scheduling by leveraging OBD-II fuel level, odometer and DTC data for predictive servicing.
- Anti-theft and security monitoring: power loss/low battery alarms and optional accessory relay/digital I/O for integration with immobilizer-style controls when configured.

The CAREU UCAN offers a balanced mix of plug-and-play convenience and telematics depth, making it a strong choice for fleets that require quick installation, reliable real-time tracking, and detailed vehicle telemetry. As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, UCAN feeds position, OBD diagnostics and event data directly into Plaspy, enabling unified fleet management, driver behavior analysis, and fuel monitoring from a single platform. Remote configuration and FOTA support reduce onsite maintenance, while optional eSIM and Bluetooth expand connectivity and sensor integration options. For fleet operators looking for scalable, easy-to-deploy vehicle trackers that support telemetry, anti-theft workflows and comprehensive reporting, UCAN delivers practical value without complex wiring or lengthy install processes.
