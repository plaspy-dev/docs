# CAREU - U1 Lite+ LTE

<div className="device-logo">
![U1 Lite+ LTE](./tracker.png)
</div>

The CAREU U1 Lite+ LTE is a compact, cost effective vehicle telematics device designed for fleet management and video enabled telematics. It combines multi network cellular connectivity with GNSS position reception and integrated vehicle diagnostics to deliver real time tracking and telemetry for commercial vehicles, heavy trucks and refrigerated fleets. The U1 Lite+ family is built to provide continuous positional data, engine and fuel information, and an option for video transmission where required.

As a Plaspy compatible tracker out of the box, the U1 Lite+ is relevant for operators who need minimal integration effort to get telematics data into a managed platform. Plaspy can ingest the U1 Lite+ telemetry and present location, diagnostics and sensor inputs in dashboards, geofences and reports so mixed fleets can be monitored and controlled from a single pane of glass.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible tracker delivering continuous location and fleet management data.
- Multi network cellular support including LTE Cat 4 and Cat 1 with 3G and 2G fallback for broad coverage.
- GPS GLONASS QZSS GNSS reception plus a 3 axis accelerometer for impact and harsh event detection.
- Integrated OBD II and CAN interpretation for engine telemetry such as fuel level RPM odometer and engine temperature.
- Advanced fuel monitoring with support for ultrasonic and external sensors for improved fuel reporting.
- Video telematics ready on Cat.4 models with an optional internal Wi Fi hotspot for dash cam connectivity.
- Remote management features including remote configuration and FOTA updates along with anti theft functions.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the U1 Lite+ streams positional fixes, diagnostic messages and sensor inputs to the platform for live monitoring and historical analysis. Plaspy reads the device messages and makes location and telemetry available to operators through configurable alerts, geofences and reporting tools.

- Real time location and telemetry updates visible in Plaspy dashboards for operational oversight.
- Engine and vehicle data from OBD II and CAN surfaced in Plaspy for maintenance planning and diagnostics.
- Geofence reporting with configurable alerts for arrivals departures and unexpected route deviations.
- Fuel monitoring integration using ultrasonic and external sensor readings to support consumption reports.
- Remote immobilizer and anti theft triggers available for integration into Plaspy workflows and response procedures.
- Video ready connectivity on Cat.4 models to support dash cam transmission and incident review through Plaspy compatible ingest or linked services.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and asset protection using remote immobilization and jamming detection workflows.
- Video enabled security and incident review for vehicles equipped with dash cams.
- Fuel management and consumption analysis for fleets using ultrasonic or CAN based fuel data.
- Cold chain and refrigerated transport monitoring using external temperature sensor inputs.
- Mixed fleet telematics for commercial vehicles trucks and specialized transport where centralized oversight is required.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CAREU U1 Lite+ LTE offers a practical mix of capabilities for organizations that need more than basic GPS tracking. Its support for OBD II and CAN diagnostics brings engine telemetry into Plaspy workflows, enabling better maintenance scheduling and fuel analysis.
