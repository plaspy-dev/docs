# CAREU - Ugo

![Ugo](./tracker.png)

The CAREU Ugo GPS Tracker is a plug-and-use OBD-II tracker engineered for fast deployment and reliable vehicle telemetry. Plaspy compatible out of the box, Ugo brings real-time tracking and fleet management data to your Plaspy dashboard via 4G LTE \(Cat.1 / Cat.M\) with fallback to 3G/2G. With a built-in GPS/GSM antenna set, internal battery, and a 6-axis accelerometer, Ugo delivers continuous location, driving-behavior detection, and detailed OBD-II vehicle data without complex installation.

Designed for commercial fleets and end-user vehicles alike, CAREU Ugo simplifies anti-theft monitoring, ignition and engine status reporting, and driver behavior analytics. Its plug-and-play OBD-II form factor and support for either a standard SIM or integrated eSIM make it an ideal Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for organizations that need scalable, cost-effective real-time tracking and telemetry with robust data logging and remote management.

## Key Highlights

- Plug-and-play OBD-II installation for immediate real-time tracking with Plaspy.
- 4G LTE \(Cat.1 / Cat.M\) connectivity with automatic fallback to 3G/2G for wider coverage.
- Built-in GPS and GSM antennas plus a backup battery for continued reporting and power-loss alerts.
- 6-axis accelerometer to detect harsh acceleration, braking, cornering, impact and flip-over events.
- Onboard OBD-II data reading \(including odometer\) to enrich telemetry and fuel monitoring where the vehicle provides it.
- Large local log capacity: up to 200,000 position logs on 4G variants \(150,000 on 3/2G variants\).
- Remote configuration and FOTA firmware updates via FTP for simplified device management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the CAREU Ugo forwards GNSS positions and vehicle telematics to your Plaspy instance for live views, alerts, and historical reports. Ugo’s OBD-II data and accelerometer events feed Plaspy’s fleet management workflows to enable driver scoring, geofence alerts, and anti-theft notifications. Remote configuration and FOTA support keep fleet firmware and reporting aligned with Plaspy policies without returning units to a workshop.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates streamed to Plaspy for mapping and dispatch.
- Engine/ignition status reporting for on/off state and trip segmentation.
- OBD-II data reading \(including odometer\); fuel monitoring supported when the vehicle exposes fuel data via OBD-II.
- Harsh driving and impact events \(harsh acceleration, braking, cornering, flip-over\) pushed as alerts for driver coaching.
- Optional immobilizer or remote control via the wireless relay accessory \(configurable within Plaspy workflows\).
- Bluetooth 4.0 \(4G variant\) support for connecting Bluetooth sensors and beacons to extend telemetry and asset context.
