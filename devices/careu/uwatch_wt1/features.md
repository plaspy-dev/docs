# CAREU - uWatch WT1 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the CAREU uWatch WT1 and how its location and health telemetry integrate with Plaspy. It describes practical capabilities users can expect when the device is used alongside Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and historical review.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation type, and manufacturer implementation. For the most current device specific behavior, consult the official CAREU documentation as firmware and configuration options change over time.

## Feature Overview

The uWatch WT1 is a compact smartwatch style GPS tracker designed to combine continuous location updates with basic health telemetry for caregiving and personnel monitoring. When paired with Plaspy, the device feeds location and vital sign data into a shared operational view for situational awareness and incident response.

- Real time GPS location updates with historical route playback for review and investigation.
- SOS emergency signaling that triggers alerts for rapid caregiver or operator response.
- Vital signs telemetry including heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, and body temperature for remote health oversight.
- Geofencing and perimeter notifications to detect exits from configured safe areas.
- Daily wellness features such as exercise monitoring, sleep tracking, pedometer, and sedentary reminders to support proactive care.
- Practical wearable utilities like alarm clock, watch finder, and SMS message display to improve everyday usability.

## Core Features of CAREU - uWatch WT1

- GPS enabled tracking with continuous position updates and historical route playback.
- SOS emergency button for immediate alerting to caregivers or monitoring teams.
- Health telemetry reporting of heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, and body temperature.
- Geofence support for configurable safe areas and exit notifications.
- Multi user sharing so multiple caregivers or operators can view the wearer data.
- Wellness features including sleep tracking, exercise monitoring, pedometer, and sedentary reminders.
- On device utilities such as alarm clock, watch finder, and SMS message display.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the uWatch WT1 telemetry and presents it alongside other tracked assets to create a unified operational picture. Plaspy automatically detects the tracker protocol and organizes incoming location and vital sign updates into maps, alerts, and historical reports to support monitoring and response workflows.

- Device location and historical routes appear on Plaspy maps for situational awareness and event review.
- SOS events generate alerts and notifications so responders can be informed quickly.
- Geofence events are delivered into Plaspy so safe area exits can be tracked and escalated when needed.
- Vital signs telemetry is available for monitoring and trend review within Plaspy where account permissions and data flows permit.
- Multi user sharing and alerting enable teams and family caregivers to receive the same incident and health updates.
- Routine device events such as alarms and SMS display status can be surfaced in monitoring workflows where supported.

## Typical Use Cases

- Assisted living and care centers monitoring resident location and basic vitals to reduce elopement risk and streamline incident response.
- Family caregivers keeping remote visibility on seniors or vulnerable people, with SOS alerts and health summaries available to multiple family members.
- Organizations tracking lone workers or staff in the field where location plus physiological context improves safety oversight.
- Wellness programs that combine activity, sleep, and vital sign summaries for preventive care and remote check ins.
- Incident review and compliance where historical routes and recorded telemetry help verify timelines and actions.
