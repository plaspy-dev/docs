# CAREU - uWatch WT1 Lite Features

This page outlines the public feature context for using the CAREU uWatch WT1 Lite with Plaspy. It summarizes the device capabilities described by the manufacturer and explains how those capabilities map to monitoring, alerting, and historical reporting workflows when the tracker is used with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional variant, installation type, and the manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical limits, firmware change notes, and the most current feature list, consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The uWatch WT1 Lite is a wearable GPS tracker and health watch designed for personal safety and continuous wellness monitoring. It pairs location services with health telemetry and caregiver alerts to provide both real time visibility and historical playback when integrated into a monitoring platform such as Plaspy.

- Real time GPS positioning with historical GPS playback for incident review and route history
- SOS emergency button that can generate immediate alerts for caregivers and monitoring services
- Geofence notifications to warn when the wearer leaves predefined safe areas
- Continuous health telemetry including heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation
- Activity and wellness tracking features such as a pedometer, sleep monitoring, and sedentary reminders

## Core Features of CAREU - uWatch WT1 Lite

- Wearable smartwatch form factor for unobtrusive continuous monitoring
- Real time GPS location updates and the ability to view historical location playback
- Dedicated SOS emergency button for rapid alerts to authorized caregivers
- Geofence or electronic fence notifications for safe zone management
- Heart rate monitoring reported as part of health telemetry
- Blood pressure measurements available as part of continuous health data
- Oxygen saturation SpO2 reported for basic oxygen level awareness
- Activity features including step counting, sleep tracking, and sedentary reminders

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the uWatch WT1 Lite is made Plaspy compatible, device events and telemetry can be consolidated into the Plaspy monitoring environment so teams and authorized users can access and act on location and health information from a single platform.

- Real time location and historical GPS tracks appear in Plaspy for monitoring and playback
- SOS button events are forwarded to Plaspy so alerts can be routed to designated responders
- Geofence events are available in Plaspy to trigger notifications and operational workflows
- Health telemetry such as heart rate, blood pressure, and SpO2 is viewable by authorized users for caregiving context
- Activity and sleep summaries can be retained in Plaspy to support ongoing wellness monitoring and correlation with location events

## Typical Use Cases

- Elderly care monitoring where caregivers need location awareness plus basic vital sign telemetry
- Child safety tracking with geofence alerts for school or home boundaries and SOS alerts for emergencies
- Lone person or lone worker oversight where both position and health indicators add situational awareness
- Daily wellness and activity tracking to support preventive care and lifestyle monitoring
- Shared caregiving scenarios where multiple family members or professionals receive alerts and review history

Integrating the CAREU uWatch WT1 Lite into Plaspy centralizes location, alerting, and health telemetry into a single monitoring platform. This can simplify multi user access, provide consistent historical playback for incident review, and enable operational workflows that route SOS and geofence alerts to the right responders.
