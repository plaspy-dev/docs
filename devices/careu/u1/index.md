# CAREU - U1

![U1](./logo..png)

The CAREU U1 PLUS LTE is a highly advanced GPS tracker that offers a wide range of features and capabilities. It supports 4G \(LTE Cat 4/1/M1/NB1\) network coverage, with fallback compatibility to 3G \(UMTS\) and 2G \(GSM\). Equipped with GNSS and LTE modules, as well as internal antennas, this tracker ensures reliable and accurate positioning. It also has the option for Bluetooth connectivity for easy configuration.

One of the standout features of the CAREU U1 PLUS LTE is its ability to communicate with various third-party accessories, such as fatigue sensors, dash cams, and RFID readers, through RS-232 or RS-485 interfaces. It also has OBDII and CAN bus data reading capability, allowing it to retrieve information from the vehicle's on-board computer. This makes it an excellent choice for fleet management and monitoring.

In addition, the CAREU U1 PLUS LTE is well-suited for refrigerated transport, thanks to its extended input/output set and 1-Wire® interface for temperature monitoring. It also offers optional WiFi for video transmission through compatible cameras, as well as the ability to function as a hotspot for internet access.

With its advanced features and capabilities, the CAREU U1 PLUS LTE is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that can meet the needs of various industries and applications. Whether you need to track vehicles, monitor temperature-sensitive cargo, or integrate with other accessories, this tracker offers a comprehensive solution.

### Key Features:

- Built-in OBD II / CAN \(J1939 or J1708\) Interpreter
- Supports harsh acceleration, harsh braking, impact detection, harsh cornering, and flip over detection with its 6-axis accelerometer
- WiFi version available for video transmission and internet hotspot
- GSM jamming detection \(Only for 2G/3G\)
- RS232 interface for accessories such as fatigue sensors, dash cams, fuel level sensors, RFID readers, and barcodes
- Data transmission through LTE / UMTS / HSPA / GPRS / EDGE, SMS, FTP, USSD
- Supports 1-Wire® for temperature control and driver identification through i-Button
- Assisted-GPS \(Ephemeris\) for improved positioning
- Records up to 200,000 position logs
- Supports Garmin FMI 2.0 operation
- FOTA firmware via FTP for easy updates
- Geofence report in circular and polygonal types
- GPS Odometer data reading and flexible user-defined reports
- Power low/lost alarm for added security
- GPS antenna tamper detect alarm

With its optional features such as Bluetooth interface, WiFi communication, AES-256 data encryption, built-in OBD/CAN interpreter, DSC camera support, two-way voice communication, and support for multiple satellite systems \(GPS/Glonass/BDS/Galileo\), the CAREU U1 PLUS LTE offers even more flexibility and functionality.

Please note that the specifications of the CAREU U1 PLUS LTE may be subject to change without prior notice. For the most up-to-date information, please refer to the manufacturer's documentation.
