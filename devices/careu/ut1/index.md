# CAREU - UT1

<div className="device-logo">
![UT1](./tracker.png)
</div>

The CAREU UT1 is a powered asset GPS tracker designed for heavy duty environments. Built with an IP67 rated enclosure and rugged construction, the UT1 is intended for reliable real time tracking and telemetry of construction machinery, trailers, safes and other industrial assets. Its specifications emphasize durable performance in dusty, wet and vibration prone sites and include onboard logging and backup power options to support extended field deployments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the UT1 streams location and event data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. That compatibility makes the UT1 a practical choice for organizations that want to combine rugged hardware with Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reporting to manage fleets, protect high value assets and support anti theft workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker providing continuous real time tracking over LTE Cat 1 with fallback to 3G and 2G for broad cellular coverage.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure designed to resist dust water ingress and heavy vibration for industrial and field use.
- Internal backup battery options of approximately 5.2 Ah or 10.4 Ah plus super deep sleep modes to extend offline autonomy.
- Anti theft capabilities including remote engine immobilization and tamper or power loss alarms for security workflows.
- Large on board position log capacity up to 200,000 records to preserve historical tracks and events until sync.
- Flexible sensor and interface support including 1 Wire and RS 232 with optional CAN for extended telemetry and integrations.
- Remote configuration and firmware update support via FOTA over FTP to simplify device management at scale.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed with Plaspy the CAREU UT1 delivers location fixes and telemetry into the platform so teams can monitor assets in real time and act on alerts. Plaspy ingests event and sensor data from the UT1 to power location awareness reporting and operational dashboards for dispatchers fleet managers and security personnel.

- Real time location updates and telemetry appear in Plaspy dashboards to support live tracking and route visibility.
- Geofence breaches tamper events and power or low battery alerts are forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and escalation.
- Immobilizer and ignition related events can be reflected in Plaspy workflows to support anti theft responses.
- Onboard event logging is synchronized to Plaspy for historical analysis and auditing when connectivity is restored.
- Sensor data such as temperature or driver identification inputs can be surfaced in Plaspy reports and compliance views.
- Remote configuration and firmware updates can be coordinated alongside Plaspy provisioning to reduce on site maintenance.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti theft and remote immobilization for vehicles and mobile equipment in mixed site environments.
- Tracking and telemetry for heavy machinery on construction or mining sites where ruggedization is essential.
- Trailer and non powered asset monitoring where long autonomy and deep sleep modes extend field life.
- Protection of cash handling equipment such as ATMs and safes where vibration resistance and sealing are required.
- Fuel and vehicle telemetry when paired with optional CAN interfaces for operational monitoring in industrial fleets.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CAREU UT1 is a strong match for organizations that need a rugged telemetry capable tracker integrated into a centralized fleet management platform. Its combination of durable hardware internal backup power extensive logging and flexible I O options supports a wide range of fleet and asset protection workflows. Using the UT1 with Plaspy enables visible tracking of assets across challenging sites while allowing administrators to manage alerts reporting and remote configuration from a single platform.
