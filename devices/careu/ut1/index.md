# CAREU - UT1

![UT1](./tracker.png)

The CAREU UT1 GPS tracker is a Plaspy compatible powered asset tracker engineered for heavy-duty environments. Built to withstand vibration, dust and water ingress with an IP67 rating, the UT1 delivers reliable real-time tracking and rich telemetry for construction machinery, trailers, safes/ATMs and other industrial assets. With LTE Cat‑1 connectivity and fallback to 3G/2G, the UT1 maintains broad cellular coverage to keep Plaspy dashboards and alerts up to date even in challenging sites.

Designed for fleet management and anti-theft use cases, the UT1 combines long autonomy \(internal backup batteries of approximately 5.2 Ah or 10.4 Ah\), extensive logging \(up to 200,000 positions\), and interfaces for external sensors \(RS‑232, 1‑Wire, optional CAN and RS‑232\) so telemetry, fuel monitoring and immobilizer commands are fully integrated into Plaspy workflows. Remote configuration and FOTA via FTP simplify deployments and ongoing device management.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker providing continuous real-time tracking over LTE Cat‑1 with 3G/2G fallback.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure rated for dust, water and heavy vibration—ideal for construction, mining and industrial fleets.
- Internal battery backup options \(~5.2 Ah or ~10.4 Ah\) and super deep‑sleep mode for extended offline autonomy.
- Anti-theft functionality including remote engine immobilization and fleet-control alarms.
- Comprehensive telemetry: GPS/GLONASS/QZSS positioning, 3‑axis accelerometer \(optional 6‑axis\), geofence reporting and 200,000 position log capacity.
- Flexible I/O and sensor support: 1‑Wire \(temperature, i‑Button\), RS‑232 for RFID/fatigue sensors, optional CAN for fuel monitoring and vehicle data.
- Communications via SMS, FTP and USSD plus GSM jamming detection and remote configuration/FOTA via FTP.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the CAREU UT1 streams location and telemetry over cellular networks into the Plaspy platform for live monitoring, alerts and reporting. Plaspy ingests GPS/GLONASS/QZSS position fixes, accelerometer events, geofence breaches, power and tamper alarms and sensor data to provide drivers, dispatchers and security teams with actionable insights.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy via LTE/UMTS/GPRS with fallback for resilient coverage.
- Ignition and immobilizer control: remote engine immobilization commands and ignition state monitoring for anti-theft response.
- Fuel monitoring and vehicle diagnostics when paired with optional CAN interfaces—data visible in Plaspy fleet management views.
- Power, low-battery and power-lost alarms forwarded to Plaspy so administrators can respond to potential theft or power issues.
- Sensor integration: 1‑Wire temperature sensors and i‑Button driver identification can be processed by Plaspy for compliance and driver logins.
- Event logging: on-board storage of up to 200,000 records ensures historical positions and events can be synced to Plaspy for analysis.
- Remote commands, configuration and firmware updates \(FOTA via FTP\) executed from Plaspy or backend systems for scalable management.
