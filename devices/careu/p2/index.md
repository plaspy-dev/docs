# CAREU - P2

![P2](./logo..png)

The CAREU P2 GPS Personal Tracker is a compact and easy-to-use device that offers reliable tracking and communication capabilities. With 3G/2G connectivity, this tracker is suitable for a variety of applications, including elder care, child safety, security services, and lone workers. It is designed for both business and end-users, providing accurate position data every second for precise tracking.

One of the standout features of the CAREU P2 is its two-way communication capability, allowing users to make and receive calls. It can store up to four phone numbers to call in case of an emergency, making it an ideal choice for personal safety. The tracker also features an adjustable sensitivity level, ensuring that it can be customized to meet individual needs.

In addition to its impressive communication features, the CAREU P2 offers an optional Charging Station. This station not only charges the tracker's battery but also allows for wireless battery charging. It can connect with up to four trackers via Bluetooth 4.1, conserving battery life and providing a convenient charging solution. The Charging Station also includes an automatic geofence feature, alerting users if a paired tracker leaves a predefined boundary.

With its reliable network connectivity, SOS emergency alert, two-way communication, and optional Charging Station, the CAREU P2 GPS Personal Tracker is a versatile and practical device for personal tracking and safety. Whether you need to keep an eye on your loved ones or ensure the safety of your employees, this tracker offers the features and functionality you need.

### Key Features:

- 3G and 2G Network
- SOS Emergency alert
- Call and be called
- Memory stores up to 4 different phone numbers to call
- Man down alert
- 3-axis accelerometer for high sensitivity
- Cancel manually pre-alerts and alerts
- Low battery alert
- Configurable for reports every 1 second
- Predefined geofence boundary \(Circle\)
- Dynamic geofence boundary \(Circle\)
- Assisted GPS
- Smart GSM multi-cell positioning
- Wireless battery charging through Charging Station
- BLE Signal Synching
- Smart Safe-zone - A smart BLE synch area, to be aware device inside and outside particular safe area

### Optional Features:

- Optional Charging Station with Bluetooth connectivity
- Sticker for wireless charging
- Jelly pouch for belt use
- AC charger
