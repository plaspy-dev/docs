# CAREU - P2

<div className="device-logo">
![P2](./logo..png)
</div>

The CAREU P2 is a compact personal GPS tracker designed for straightforward location monitoring and two way communication. It provides frequent position updates and core safety features such as an SOS alert, emergency call capability with stored phone numbers, and configurable sensitivity for movement detection. The device is intended for both consumer and business users who need reliable, on‑person tracking for people and small asset protection.

This model is compatible with Plaspy, making it suitable for organizations that want to integrate P2 devices into a centralized tracking platform. When connected to Plaspy, the P2 can contribute live location data, alerting events, and call‑related status into fleet and safety workflows, helping operators maintain visibility and respond quickly to incidents.

## Key Highlights

- Compact personal tracker with frequent position reporting for precise location updates
- Two way communication and SOS emergency alert with up to four stored emergency contacts
- Adjustable sensitivity and man down style awareness for enhanced personal safety
- Optional Charging Station for wireless charging and multi‑device convenience
- Configurable geofence alerts to monitor boundary exits and entries
- Designed for both consumer and business use cases such as elder care and lone workers

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the CAREU P2 feeds its location and alert signals into the Plaspy platform so operators can monitor devices in real time and review historical movements. Plaspy users can incorporate P2 data into dashboards, notifications, and reports alongside other devices for unified operational oversight.

- Real time location visibility of P2 units on Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Alert forwarding for SOS events and geofence breaches to Plaspy notification rules
- Periodic position reports available within Plaspy for tracking accuracy and replay
- Centralized monitoring of multiple P2 trackers for fleet or personnel oversight
- Reporting and logs in Plaspy to support incident review and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Elder care monitoring to ensure timely assistance and location awareness
- Child safety tracking for parents and guardians seeking regular position updates
- Lone worker safety to combine SOS alerts with centralized oversight
- Security and patrol personnel tracking for shift and route monitoring
- Small scale asset or equipment tracking where compact personal devices are suitable
