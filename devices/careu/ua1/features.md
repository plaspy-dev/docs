# CAREU - UA1 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the CAREU UA1 GPS tracker and how its capabilities map into Plaspy for asset visibility, telemetry reporting and operational monitoring. It is intended to describe practical, non sensitive device capabilities and how those capabilities can be used inside Plaspy workflows.

Exact feature availability for a specific UA1 device can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, chosen battery and accessory options, installation method and manufacturer configuration. For deployment planning and precise technical details always review the manufacturer documentation and firmware release notes in addition to Plaspy setup guidance.

## Feature Overview

The CAREU UA1 is a compact, IP67 rated tracker built for long term monitoring of non powered and hidden assets. It focuses on low power GNSS reporting, multi year battery options and flexible connectivity methods to deliver location and event telemetry suitable for Plaspy dashboards and alerts.

- GNSS positioning using GPS, GLONASS and QZSS to provide reliable location data for static and in transit assets.
- Cellular connectivity optimized for low power telemetry over LTE Cat M1 and NB1 with 2G fallback plus SMS, FTP and USSD reporting methods.
- Multiple battery configurations including rechargeable and long life non rechargeable packs to match multi year deployments.
- Onboard 3 axis accelerometer and a bottom tamper sensor to report movement and intrusion events relevant to anti theft monitoring.
- Wireless Bluetooth commissioning and optional BLE sensor support for easy field setup and environmental telemetry from add on sensors.
- Rugged IP67 enclosure with multiple mounting options for concealed installation on containers, trailers and heavy equipment.

## Core Features of CAREU - UA1

- GNSS support: GPS, GLONASS and QZSS for position fixes suited to asset tracking.
- Low power cellular communication: LTE Cat M1 and NB1 with fallback to 2G and support for SMS, FTP and USSD delivery paths.
- Multi year battery options: internal rechargeable 6,700 mAh or non rechargeable packs such as 18,000 mAh and 27,000 mAh for long unattended use.
- IP67 rated compact enclosure with magnet and hardware mounting choices for concealed or exposed installations.
- Built in 3 axis accelerometer for movement and impact detection.
- Bottom tamper sensor for intrusion and mounting disturbance detection.
- Bluetooth configuration and optional BLE 4.0 support for commissioning and external sensor integration.
- FOTA updates via FTP and support for attachable external battery options and an optional USB C I O cable for basic digital input and output functions.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy the UA1's position and telemetry become visible in dashboards and reporting tools, enabling both long term beaconing and higher frequency tracking when required. Plaspy ingests location reports, motion and tamper events and optional sensor data so teams can monitor assets and respond to events from a central interface.

- Location reporting visible in Plaspy maps and history when the device sends GNSS fixes at configured intervals.
- Periodic low power beaconing and higher frequency reporting modes support trade offs between battery life and tracking granularity inside Plaspy.
- Movement and tamper events from the accelerometer and bottom sensor surface as alerts or event records for anti theft workflows.
- Optional BLE sensor and ambient telemetry can be forwarded into Plaspy as environmental data for refrigerated or sensitive cargo monitoring.
- FOTA and Bluetooth commissioning simplify remote device maintenance and configuration workflows that tie into Plaspy device management processes.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and intermodal cargo tracking that requires long battery life for multimonth or multiyear transit.
- Static asset monitoring for generators, trailers and site equipment where concealed mounting and tamper detection are important.
- Anti theft monitoring with motion and tamper alerts feeding Plaspy alarm rules and notification channels.
- Refrigerated or sensitive cargo oversight when paired with BLE environmental sensors to provide temperature or door status telemetry.
- Supplementary or backup asset tracking where a compact, rugged tracker is needed for hidden installations.
- Low maintenance fleet augmentation where devices must run for long periods on primary battery with occasional high frequency reporting.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware versions and manufacturer configuration can change how features behave and which reporting modes are available.
- Hardware variants and regional cellular band selections may affect connectivity performance and fallback behavior.
- Battery configuration and whether the unit includes an internal battery or external attachable packs will determine operating lifetime between service intervals.
- Optional accessories such as the USB C I O cable and BLE sensors are sold separately and must be specified during procurement.
- For the most current technical specifications, connector pinouts and accessory compatibility consult the manufacturer documentation.
