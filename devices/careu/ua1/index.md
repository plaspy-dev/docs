# CAREU - UA1

![UA1](./tracker.png)

The CAREU UA1 is a compact, IP67-rated GPS tracker built for long-term monitoring of non-powered and hidden assets. Designed for container, cargo and static-asset deployments, the UA1 provides Plaspy compatible location and telemetry reporting over 4G LTE Cat M1 / NB1 with 2G fallback, plus SMS, FTP and USSD, so you can integrate battery‑friendly tracking into Plaspy-powered fleet management and asset oversight workflows.

The UA1 combines multi‑year battery options, GNSS positioning \(GPS/GLONASS/QZSS\) and an onboard 3‑axis accelerometer with tamper detection and wireless Bluetooth configuration. Whether you need periodic beaconing for long-term logistics or higher-frequency, near‑real‑time tracking when assets are active, the CAREU UA1 is optimized for secure, low-power telemetry and simple Plaspy integration.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with GNSS \(GPS/GLONASS/QZSS\) for reliable location data.
- Low‑power designs with multiple battery configurations: rechargeable 6,700 mAh or long‑life non‑rechargeable packs \(18,000 mAh / 27,000 mAh\) to match deployment needs.
- 4G LTE Cat.M1 / NB1 connectivity with 2G fallback \(GPRS/EDGE\), plus FTP, SMS and USSD for robust data delivery even in variable networks.
- Durable IP67 enclosure and flexible mounting \(magnets, hardware\) for containers, heavy machinery and hidden assets.
- Onboard 3‑axis accelerometer and bottom tamper sensor for movement, impact and intrusion detection — core anti‑theft telemetry for Plaspy alerts.
- Wireless configuration via Bluetooth \(optional BLE 4.0\) for easy commissioning and BLE sensors support for temperature/door/ambient data.
- FOTA firmware updates via FTP and attachable external battery options for scalable, low‑touch fleet management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The CAREU UA1 sends GNSS positions and device telemetry to Plaspy using standard cellular and messaging channels supported by the device. Plaspy ingests location reports, motion and tamper events, and optional sensor data to provide dashboards, alerts and reports suitable for both fleet management and long‑term asset monitoring.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates when configured for frequent reporting, and deep battery‑saving periodic reports for long-term deployments.
- Movement and impact alerts from the 3‑axis accelerometer and tamper notifications from the bottom sensor for anti‑theft workflows.
- Optional ambient light and pressure sensors or BLE sensors can provide environmental telemetry to Plaspy for refrigerated or high‑value cargo monitoring.
- Digital input/output via the optional USB‑C I/O cable enables ignition detection or basic remote immobilizer workflows when integrated in Plaspy alarms and commands.
- FOTA \(firmware over‑the‑air\) via FTP and Bluetooth configuration streamline field updates and remote device management through Plaspy operational processes.
