# CAREU - UA1

<div className="device-logo">
![UA1](./tracker.png)
</div>

The CAREU UA1 is a compact, IP67 rated GPS tracker built for long term monitoring of non powered and hidden assets. It is designed for container, cargo and static asset deployments and provides GNSS positioning with an emphasis on low power consumption and tamper resistant operation. The device supports multi year battery options and includes an onboard 3 axis accelerometer plus Bluetooth configuration for field commissioning.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the UA1 can deliver location and essential telemetry into Plaspy powered fleet management and asset oversight workflows. Its support for cellular reporting with fallback messaging channels and low power periodic reporting modes makes it suitable for integrating long duration asset tracking, tamper alerts and optional sensor telemetry within Plaspy dashboards, alerts, and reporting pipelines.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker with GNSS positioning for reliable location updates.
- IP67 rated enclosure suited to container, cargo and outdoor static asset use.
- Low power design with multiple battery configurations for long unattended operation.
- Cellular reporting with fallback messaging channels to maintain data delivery in varied coverage.
- Onboard 3 axis accelerometer and bottom tamper sensor for movement and intrusion alerts.
- Wireless Bluetooth configuration for field setup and support for optional external sensors.
- Support for remote firmware updates and attachable external battery options to simplify lifecycle management.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The CAREU UA1 sends GNSS positions, motion and tamper events, and optional sensor telemetry to Plaspy using the device's supported data channels. Plaspy ingests these reports to present location visibility, trigger alerts, and create reports that support both fleet operations and long term asset monitoring.

- Configure frequent reporting for near real time location updates or periodic beaconing for battery friendly long duration tracking.
- Receive movement, impact and tamper notifications in Plaspy to power anti theft workflows and immediate alerts.
- Include optional environmental or BLE sensor data so Plaspy can monitor cargo conditions and raise threshold based alerts.
- Use digital input/output options when present to integrate ignition status or basic immobilizer actions into Plaspy rules.
- Manage devices at scale with over the air updates and Bluetooth commissioning workflows that reduce field service time.

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and intermodal logistics where long battery life and concealed mounting are required.
- Refrigerated or sensitive cargo monitoring when combined with external temperature or ambient sensors.
- Cash, parcel and ATM protection relying on tamper and motion alerts to trigger Plaspy security workflows.
- Static and slow moving asset tracking such as generators, trailers and parked equipment that need long unattended operation.
- Supplemental or backup tracking for vehicles and high value assets when ignition or I/O based signals are desired.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The CAREU UA1 is a practical option for organizations that need durable, low maintenance tracking integrated into Plaspy. Its combination of long life battery options, IP67 durability and movement/tamper detection makes it well suited to mixed deployments spanning containers, static assets and occasional active tracking needs. Wireless configuration and remote update capabilities help reduce ongoing operational overhead when managing large fleets of devices.

When paired with Plaspy, the UA1 provides a straightforward path to translate low power telemetry into searchable location history, alerting and operational reporting. That integration supports both routine fleet oversight and focused anti theft or environmental monitoring workflows without requiring intensive field intervention.
