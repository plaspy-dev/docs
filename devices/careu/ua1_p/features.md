# CAREU - UA1-P Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the CAREU UA1-P and explains how its capabilities are used when the device is paired with Plaspy. The information here focuses on practical, user facing features such as location tracking, environmental telemetry, local configuration, and remote device management that are relevant when integrating UA1-P devices into Plaspy for logistics and asset monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where relevant, this page notes those variation points and encourages verification against the manufacturer documentation for the most current device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The CAREU UA1-P is a compact, rugged tracker designed for parcel and valuable asset tracking with Plaspy compatibility. It combines multi constellation GNSS positioning, environmental sensors, Bluetooth based local setup, and rechargeable operation to deliver reliable tracking and condition awareness in outdoor logistics environments.

- Real time GNSS location reporting for continuous tracking of parcels and small assets.
- Environmental telemetry channels including temperature, ambient light, and barometric pressure for condition monitoring.
- Compact IP67 rated enclosure for dust and water resistance suitable for outdoor transit.
- Global 4G and 2G cellular connectivity for wide area coverage where cellular service is available.
- Bluetooth 4.0 local configuration to make field parameter changes without opening the device.

## Core Features of CAREU - UA1-P

- Multi constellation GNSS positioning for consistent location fixes across varied environments.
- Environmental sensors for ambient temperature, ambient light, and barometric pressure monitoring.
- Global cellular communications with 4G and 2G support for reporting and remote telemetry.
- IP67 rated rugged enclosure designed for parcels and exposed asset mounting.
- Rechargeable battery operation with configurable reporting intervals to balance updates and battery life.
- Bluetooth 4.0 local setup for onsite configuration and parameter tuning.
- Remote parameter configuration and FTP based firmware update support for scalable maintenance.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated into Plaspy, the UA1-P streams its location and sensor telemetry into the Plaspy platform so teams can monitor assets, set alerts, and review historical behavior. Plaspy ingests device messages and presents them as location points and telemetry channels that can be used for live operations and post trip analysis.

- Device GNSS fixes appear in Plaspy for live location, route playback, and historical reporting.
- Environmental sensor readings are available as telemetry channels for condition based alerts and reporting.
- Configurable reporting profiles from the UA1-P map to Plaspy update policies so you can balance frequency with battery life.
- Remote parameter changes and firmware updates supported by the device enable simplified management workflows that integrate with Plaspy administration processes.
- Bluetooth based local configuration remains useful for field technicians to perform on site adjustments that will then be reflected in Plaspy data once the device reconnects.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parcel tracking and delivery visibility with environmental context for fragile or sensitive shipments.
- Valuable asset protection with continuous location and condition telemetry to support recovery workflows.
- Logistics and supply chain monitoring where small form factor trackers are needed for consolidated dashboards.
- Cold chain and condition sensitive shipment oversight using onboard temperature and pressure data for alerts.
- Fixed asset visibility for inventory, containers, and equipment that need periodic location reporting.
- Exception monitoring during transit to detect deviations in route or environmental conditions.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Hardware revisions and regional variants may affect cellular band support and specific connectivity behavior.
- Installation method and mounting location influence GNSS reception and sensor readings, so placement matters for reliable data.
- Bluetooth local setup requires physical proximity to the device and is intended for secure onsite configuration.
- Verify FTP update workflows and remote management features against your device fleet procedures before large scale deployment.
