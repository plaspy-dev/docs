# CAREU - UA1-P

<div className="device-logo">
![UA1-P](./tracker.png)
</div>

The CAREU UA1-P is a compact, rugged GPS tracker designed for parcel and valuable asset tracking. Built with an IP67 rated enclosure, the UA1-P is intended to withstand outdoor conditions while providing real time location updates and environmental telemetry. Its combination of multi constellation GNSS positioning, global cellular connectivity, onboard sensors, Bluetooth 4.0 local setup, and a rechargeable power option make it suitable for logistics, supply chain, and condition sensitive shipments.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the UA1-P can stream location and sensor data into Plaspy for centralized monitoring and operational oversight. Integration with Plaspy enables configurable reporting profiles to help balance update frequency with battery life and connectivity cost, while remote parameter management and firmware update paths support scalable device administration across large deployments.

## Key Highlights

- Designed for parcel and valuable asset tracking with a compact, mountable form factor.
- IP67 rated enclosure for dust and water resistance suitable for outdoor logistics.
- Global cellular connectivity for wide area reporting across diverse geographies.
- Multi constellation GNSS positioning for consistent location fixes in varied environments.
- Onboard environmental sensors including temperature, ambient light, and barometric pressure.
- Bluetooth 4.0 local configuration for quick field setup and parameter changes.
- Rechargeable battery and configurable reporting intervals to optimize battery usage.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the UA1-P transmits location fixes and environmental telemetry into the Plaspy platform so teams can monitor assets in real time, set alerts, and review historical movements. Plaspy ingests the device messages to present location and condition context alongside other fleet and asset data.

- Real time location updates in Plaspy for live tracking and operational visibility.
- Environmental sensor channels available in Plaspy for condition based alerts and reporting.
- Configurable reporting profiles to balance update frequency with battery and connectivity considerations.
- Remote parameter configuration workflows to adjust device behavior without field visits.
- Support for firmware updates and centralized maintenance workflows to keep device fleets current.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parcel tracking and delivery visibility with real time location and condition monitoring.
- Valuable asset protection and recovery support through continuous location telemetry.
- Logistics and supply chain monitoring with consolidated dashboards and historical reporting.
- Cold chain and condition sensitive shipments using onboard temperature and pressure telemetry.
- Fixed asset visibility for inventory, containers, and equipment requiring periodic reporting.
