# Winrich - TK206 OBD

![TK206 OBD](./logo.jpg)

The Winrich TK206 OBD GPS tracker is a versatile and easy-to-use device that allows you to remotely locate and manage your vehicle using the GSM/GPRS network and GPS satellite positioning system. With its built-in GSM and GPS antennas, this tracker can be conveniently inserted into the car's OBDII interface, eliminating the need for any wiring harness. This plug-and-play design makes installation a breeze, saving you time and effort.

One of the standout features of the TK206 is its ability to read OBD parameters from the car's ECU \(Engine Control Unit\). This means that in addition to GPS tracking for car location, you can also access valuable diagnostic data remotely. All the data collected by the tracker is transmitted to a backend server, allowing for comprehensive remote diagnostics and tracking.

Designed for use with 12-24V cars, vehicles, taxis, and fleet management, the TK206 offers a range of useful functions. These include location-based service \(LBS\) and GPS double real-time tracking, geofence and movement alarms, overspeed alarms, low battery and external power off alarms, as well as shock and ACC alarms when the vehicle is armed. With its wide voltage range of 9V-50V, this tracker is compatible with a variety of vehicles.

With the Winrich TK206 OBD GPS tracker, you can have peace of mind knowing that you can easily monitor and manage your vehicle remotely. Whether you're a fleet manager looking to optimize operations or a car owner wanting to keep tabs on your vehicle's location and performance, this tracker offers a reliable and user-friendly solution.

### Key Features:

- Vehicle OBD II tracker \(9V-50V\)
- Location-based service \(LBS\) + GPS double real-time tracking
- Geofence, movement, overspeed alarms
- Low battery and external power off alarms
- Alarms under ARM: Shock/ACC alarms
