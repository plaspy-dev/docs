# Winrich - TK206 OBD

![TK206 OBD](./logo.jpg)

The Winrich TK206 OBD is a plug and play GPS tracker designed for easy vehicle monitoring. It combines GPS satellite positioning with GSM GPRS connectivity and integrates into a vehicle through the OBD interface. The built in antennas and plug in design remove the need for an external wiring harness, making it straightforward to deploy across cars, taxis, and many fleet vehicles. The device also reads OBD parameters from the vehicle ECU and transmits location and vehicle data to a backend server for remote access.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK206 OBD can be used to bring both location visibility and remote diagnostics into a unified fleet platform. Plaspy can ingest the tracking and vehicle data the device sends, enabling centralized monitoring, alerts, and reporting. This compatibility makes the TK206 a practical option for organizations that want a simple OBD based tracker that feeds into Plaspy for operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play OBD interface for fast installation without added wiring
- Built in GPS and GSM connectivity for location reporting and remote transmission
- Ability to read OBD parameters from the vehicle ECU for basic diagnostics
- Supports a wide vehicle voltage range suitable for cars taxis and mixed fleets
- Real time location with LBS plus GPS reporting for improved coverage
- Built in alarm features including geofence movement overspeed and power alerts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy the TK206 OBD sends its location and vehicle data to the platform where fleet managers can view and act on that information. Plaspy receives the backend data and presents it alongside other fleet devices so teams can monitor operations from one place.

- Real time and near real time vehicle location display in Plaspy maps and dashboards
- Vehicle parameter monitoring from OBD readings shown in Plaspy for remote diagnostics
- Geofence and movement alerts forwarded into Plaspy for immediate notification
- Historical route playback and trip reports for operational analysis and compliance
- Fleet level alerts and summaries for overspeed power loss and other alarm conditions
- Use data from the tracker in Plaspy reports to support maintenance planning and utilization tracking

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for mixed vehicle fleets and taxi operations
- Remote diagnostics and vehicle health monitoring for service teams
- Location based monitoring for asset security and recovery
- Overspeed and movement monitoring to support driver oversight programs
- Rental car tracking and basic operational telemetry for operations teams

The TK206 OBD is a pragmatic choice for teams that need a low friction deployment path and want OBD based vehicle data without complex installation. Its plug and play nature reduces installation time, while built in antennas keep the device compact and easy to reposition between compatible vehicles. For organizations using Plaspy, the tracker provides both location and vehicle parameter streams that enhance visibility into daily operations.
