# Winrich - Q50 Features

This page provides a public feature overview for the Winrich Q50 GPS watch and explains how those features map to the Plaspy platform for real time monitoring and alerts. It focuses on practical, non sensitive information about the device that is useful when using the tracker with Plaspy.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant and the way the device is installed and configured by the manufacturer or installer. Users should verify specific capabilities for a particular unit with official Winrich documentation and through their Plaspy account settings.

## Feature Overview

The Winrich Q50 is a compact wearable designed for continuous personal location monitoring and safety. Its primary user value is reliable position reporting combined with immediate emergency communication and event notifications that Plaspy presents on map views and reports.

- Continuous location reporting using GPS and LBS hybrid positioning to improve coverage in weak signal areas.
- SOS emergency button and two way voice for immediate contact with caregivers or response teams.
- GEO-fence support to trigger entry and exit alerts for predefined safe or restricted zones.
- Tamper and anti drop alarms plus low battery notifications to maintain device reliability and awareness.
- Compact wristwatch form factor intended for child safety, elder care and lone worker monitoring.
- SIM based cellular operation for live tracking without additional gateways or pairing.

## Core Features of Winrich - Q50

- Dual mode positioning with GPS and LBS to provide the best available location fix.
- SOS emergency button that generates high priority alerts when pressed.
- Two way voice support for direct calls between the watch and designated responders.
- GEO-fence functionality to define safe areas and receive enter or leave events.
- Tamper and anti drop alarm reporting to indicate potential removal or impact.
- Low battery notifications to prompt timely charging and uninterrupted monitoring.
- Wearable wristwatch design for discreet continuous use and comfortable wear.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy ingests the Q50's location updates and event messages so operators can monitor people on a map, receive alerts, and review historical activity. Plaspy presents the incoming telemetry and flags events to support timely responses.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps showing the latest GPS or LBS fix and timestamp.
- SOS events are surfaced as high priority alerts in Plaspy so dispatchers and caregivers can respond quickly.
- GEO-fence enter and exit messages generate configurable notifications inside Plaspy for perimeter monitoring.
- Tamper and low battery alerts feed into Plaspy notification rules and history for device health monitoring.
- Two way voice events are indicated in Plaspy workflows as part of the incident context so responders see the communications timeline.

## Typical Use Cases

- Parental monitoring of young children with instant alerts and live location checks.
- Caregiver oversight for elderly users in assisted living or home care scenarios.
- Lone worker protection where discreet wearable alerts and voice help maintain safety.
- Personal anti theft and tamper awareness for individuals who need continuous monitoring.
- School or group supervision where staff track approved movement and respond to SOS incidents.

- Installation and SIM provisioning affect network connectivity and voice or data service availability.
