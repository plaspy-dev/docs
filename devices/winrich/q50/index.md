# Winrich - Q50

![Q50](./tracker.jpg)

The GPS Watch Q50 is a compact wearable GPS tracker designed for personal safety and continuous location monitoring. Plaspy compatible out of the box, the Q50 combines an MTK3337 GPS chipset with MTK6261 GSM connectivity to deliver dual-mode GPS + LBS positioning, live alarm reporting and two-way voice so caregivers and security managers can monitor people in real time from the Plaspy platform.

The Q50 is ideal for parents, caregivers and lone workers who need discreet, reliable real-time tracking and emergency response. With configurable GEO-fence alerts, SOS emergency calling, tamper and anti-drop alarms, and low-battery notifications, the device supplies the core telemetry that Plaspy uses to provide map-based tracking, notifications and historical reports.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker in wristwatch form — continuous location and status reporting to Plaspy’s dashboard.
- Dual-mode positioning \(GPS + LBS\) for improved accuracy outdoors and more locations indoors or in weak-signal areas.
- Two-way voice communication and SOS emergency button for immediate contact and assistance.
- GEO-fence support with configurable safe/alert areas to trigger real-time notifications in Plaspy.
- Tamper/anti-drop alarm and low-battery alerts to maintain reliable telemetry and anti-theft awareness.
- Compact, wearable form factor designed for child safety, elder care and lone-worker protection.
- Simple cellular operation using a standard SIM — live tracking without additional gateways or pairing.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Q50 sends location updates and event messages over the cellular network using its GSM modem; Plaspy ingests those messages to provide real-time tracking, alerts and history. Positioning is reported using GPS when available, and LBS \(base station positioning\) when GPS signal is weak — Plaspy displays the best available fix and flags LBS-based fixes so operators can assess accuracy.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates \(position, SOS events, tamper and low-battery alarms\).
- GEO-fence alerts sent to Plaspy when the watch enters or leaves configured safe zones.
- SOS and two-way voice events appear as high-priority alerts in Plaspy for immediate response.
- Tamper / anti-drop alarms and battery status feed into Plaspy for continuous personal anti-theft and safety monitoring.
- Bluetooth sensors/beacons: not specified in the provided description.
- Vehicle-specific telemetry such as fuel monitoring, ignition or immobilizer control are not features of this personal tracker.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GPS Watch Q50 provides a focused personal-tracking feature set that maps directly onto Plaspy’s real-time tracking and alerting capabilities. As a GPS tracker designed for people rather than vehicles, it supplies the essential telemetry \(location, SOS, tamper and battery status\) that operators need to maintain safety and situational awareness. While vehicle features such as ignition, immobilizer or fuel monitoring are outside its scope, the Q50 excels at what personal trackers do best: discreet, continuous monitoring with immediate emergency communication.

For organizations and families using Plaspy, the Q50 is a reliable option to extend monitoring beyond vehicles and fixed assets into wearable safety. Its GSM-based operation, GEO-fence support and direct SOS/voice functions make it easy to deploy, keep monitored and respond quickly when an alert appears on Plaspy’s map and reports.
