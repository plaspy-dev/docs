# Winrich - Q50

<div className="device-logo">
![Q50](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The Winrich Q50 is a compact GPS watch designed for personal safety and continuous location monitoring. It combines GPS position capability with GSM cellular connectivity to provide live location updates, SOS emergency reporting and two way voice for direct contact. The form factor and core features make it suited to people monitoring scenarios where discreet, continuous tracking and fast response matter.

As a device described as Plaspy compatible out of the box, the Q50 feeds its position and event data into the Plaspy platform for map based monitoring, alerting and historical reporting. Plaspy can display the Q50's location, surface SOS and tamper alerts, and include the watch in GEO fence and notification workflows so caregivers and security teams maintain situational awareness.

## Key Highlights

- Wearable wristwatch tracker built for personal safety and continuous monitoring.
- Plaspy compatible by default for immediate integration with live tracking and alerts.
- Dual mode positioning using GPS plus base station location to improve coverage in mixed signal conditions.
- SOS emergency button and two way voice enable rapid contact and assistance.
- GEO fence support with configurable safe and alert areas for on demand notifications.
- Tamper and anti drop alarms plus low battery notifications to maintain dependable telemetry.
- Cellular SIM operation allows live reporting without additional gateways or complex pairing.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy the Q50 sends location updates and event messages over the cellular link so operators see live positions and status on Plaspy maps and dashboards. Plaspy ingests position fixes and incoming events to trigger alerts, populate reports and support operational response.

- Real time location updates and historical position history visible in Plaspy.
- SOS events and two way voice incidents surface as high priority alerts for rapid response.
- GEO fence entry and exit notifications routed to Plaspy workflows and alert channels.
- Tamper, anti drop and low battery alerts included in Plaspy notifications and device health views.
- Event and position reports available for review and export through Plaspy reporting tools.

## Typical Use Cases

- Child safety and parental monitoring with SOS and GEO fence alerts for quick intervention.
- Elder care and assisted living monitoring where discrete continuous location and tamper alerts help caregivers.
- Lone worker protection using live tracking and emergency reporting integrated into incident workflows.
- Personal anti theft and loss prevention by tracking tamper and low battery events alongside location history.

The Q50 focuses on the essential telemetry that Plaspy needs to offer effective people monitoring: position, alert events and two way voice in a wearable form factor. For organizations and families that rely on Plaspy for situational awareness, the Q50 provides a direct way to extend monitoring from vehicles and assets to individuals who need discreet, persistent protection.

Because the device is described as Plaspy compatible out of the box, integration is straightforward and the watch's live alerts and GEO fence events become part of the same monitoring and reporting environment teams already use. If your requirements center on personal safety, emergency response and continuous location visibility, the Q50 is a practical option to consider.
