# Winrich - TK102

![TK102](./logo.jpg)

The Winrich TK102B is a GPS tracker that offers GPS and GSM dual positioning, making it a reliable and versatile device for tracking. It supports GSM frequencies of 850/900/1800/1900MHz, making it globally applicable in most countries around the world. In situations where GPS positioning is not available, the TK102B seamlessly switches to GSM positioning, ensuring continuous tracking capabilities. One of the standout features of this tracker is its ability to reply with the real address in the local language, supporting languages such as Arabic, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, and English.

The TK102B also comes equipped with a built-in shock sensor and supports the use of a TF card. In case of poor GSM signal, the tracker automatically stores GPS location information, ensuring a seamless route trajectory with no breakpoints. Additionally, it features an auto-set APN function for easy setup. With its compact size of 46\*64\*17mm and weight of 40g, the TK102B is portable and easy to carry.

With its wide range of applications, the Winrich TK102B is suitable for various tracking needs. It can be used for preventing vehicle theft, ensuring the safety of kids, elderly individuals, and pets, managing personnel, and even for discreet tracking purposes. Whether you need to track your assets or loved ones, the TK102B provides an easy and efficient solution.

Outstanding features of the Winrich TK102B:

- GPS and GSM dual positioning
- Globally applicable with support for GSM frequencies
- Local language support for real address replies
- TF card support for seamless route trajectory
- Auto-set APN function for easy setup
- Built-in shock sensor for added security
