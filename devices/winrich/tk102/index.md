# Winrich - TK102

![TK102](./logo.jpg)

The Winrich TK102B is a compact GPS tracker that combines satellite positioning with GSM based location fallback. Designed for continuous tracking in a variety of environments, the TK102B supports global GSM bands and can switch to GSM positioning when GPS signals are unavailable. It also includes features such as local language real address replies, a built in shock sensor, TF card support for route storage, and an auto set APN function to simplify network configuration.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK102B can feed location and status information into the Plaspy platform for live visibility and historical playback. Its dual positioning and offline route storage make it a practical choice for organizations that need resilient tracking across areas with variable satellite coverage. Plaspy can ingest the TK102B reporting to provide unified fleet monitoring, alerts, and record keeping alongside other devices.

## Key Highlights

- GPS and GSM dual positioning for continuous location reporting in varied environments
- Supports global GSM frequency coverage for widespread applicability
- Replies with real addresses in multiple local languages to improve readability of location data
- Built in shock sensor for movement or impact detection
- TF card support enables local route storage when network coverage is poor
- Compact size and lightweight design for portable tracking needs
- Auto set APN function to streamline initial network setup

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TK102B can be integrated into Plaspy to provide persistent location updates and event information that help operators track assets and people in real time or review historical routes. Plaspy aggregates incoming position reports and events from compatible devices like the TK102B to present them in maps, dashboards, and reports for operational teams.

- Continuous location feed to Plaspy for live map visualization and last known position
- Route history and stored trajectories available for playback and auditing in Plaspy
- Event driven notices such as shock detection can be surfaced as alerts or notifications
- Local language address replies improve location clarity in operator interfaces and logs
- Use of GSM fallback ensures fewer gaps in tracking data when satellite signals are limited

## Typical Use Cases

- Preventing and investigating vehicle theft with real time location tracking
- Monitoring the safety and whereabouts of children elderly individuals and pets
- Managing mobile personnel and confirming route history for field teams
- Tracking portable assets that are moved between locations
- Discreet tracking where a compact and lightweight device is required

Its GSM fallback and TF card storage help maintain a continuous route history, while local language address replies aid teams operating across different regions and languages. These characteristics make the TK102B a useful complement to Plaspy for businesses that require straightforward location visibility without complex device profiles.

Because device capabilities and deployment needs can vary, pairing the TK102B with Plaspy lets operators centralize monitoring and alerts alongside other device types.
