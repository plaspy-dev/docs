# Winrich - TK207 OBD

![TK207 OBD](./logo.jpg)

The Winrich TK207 OBD is a plug and play vehicle tracker designed to be inserted into a car OBDII interface. It combines GPS satellite positioning with GSM GPRS data transmission to provide remote location monitoring and basic vehicle diagnostic data. The device transmits collected information to a backend server and supports common alarms such as geofence, movement, overspeed, low battery, external power off, shock, and ACC alarms under ARM mode.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK207 OBD can forward its location and diagnostic information into the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility and management. This makes it a practical option for car owners and fleet operators who want fast deployment without a wiring harness, plus the ability to surface vehicle status and alerts inside Plaspy for monitoring, reporting, and operational oversight.

## Key Highlights

- Plug and play design that connects directly to a vehicle OBDII port for fast installation
- Real time GPS location tracking combined with GSM GPRS data uplink to a backend server
- Reads OBDII parameters from the vehicle ECU to support remote diagnostic insight
- Compatibility with 12 to 24 V vehicles suitable for cars and light commercial fleets
- Built in alarms including geofence, movement, overspeed, low battery, external power off, shock, and ACC in ARM mode
- Designed for straightforward vehicle management use cases without permanent wiring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the TK207 OBD sends its location and diagnostic feeds to Plaspy's device management backend so operators can view live positions, historical tracks, and alarm events in one place. Plaspy can use the incoming data to generate alerts, produce reports, and provide a unified operational view across a mixed fleet of devices.

- Live location display and trip history for individual vehicles on the Plaspy map
- Diagnostic readings and status indicators available alongside location data to aid maintenance decisions
- Configurable geofence and movement alerts delivered through Plaspy to notify operators of unauthorized events
- Overspeed and other alarm events routed to Plaspy for immediate operator awareness
- Aggregated reporting and simple export options to support fleet performance reviews and operational oversight
