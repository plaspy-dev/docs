# Winrich - TK168

![TK168](./logo.jpg)

The Winrich TK168 is a compact and versatile GPS tracker designed for personal and vehicle positioning as well as fleet management. It combines a high sensitivity GPS chipset with support for SMS and GPRS tracking modes, offering precise location reporting even in areas with weaker signal coverage. The TK168 also includes remote control features such as the ability to cut off oil and power, and optional alarm and SOS functions for added security.

Because the TK168 offers online real time tracking and flexible reporting options, it can be integrated into vehicle monitoring platforms like Plaspy to provide continuous visibility and operational oversight. Its compact size and worldwide frequency support make it a practical choice for organizations and individuals that need discreet, reliable tracking tied into a fleet management workflow.

## Key Highlights

- Supports personal and vehicle positioning plus fleet management workflows
- High sensitivity GPS chipset for improved positioning in challenging areas
- Remote cut off of oil and power for enhanced asset control
- Online real time tracking platform available for continuous monitoring
- Supports SMS and GPRS modes for flexible connectivity
- Compact form factor suitable for discreet installation and covert tracking

## How It Works with Plaspy

The TK168 can be used with Plaspy to bring device location and status into a centralized fleet management interface, enabling operators to monitor assets and respond to events from one platform. Plaspy consolidates positional data and device alerts so teams can keep track of vehicles and personnel across regions.

- Display live locations and historical routes within Plaspy dashboards
- Configure alerts for events such as movement, geofence entry or exit, and SOS signals
- Use Plaspy reporting to analyze fleet activity and operational patterns
- Combine TK168 status and control functions with Plaspy workflows for oversight and response
- Manage a mixed fleet of devices and review device connectivity and tracking performance in one place
