# Winrich

Winrich is a manufacturer of GPS tracking hardware commonly used for vehicle and asset tracking. Plaspy can work with Winrich devices to provide centralized location monitoring, historical playback, alerts, and fleet insights. This brand page explains how Winrich trackers integrate with Plaspy, what to expect from compatible devices, and how to choose the right unit for your needs.

## How Winrich devices work with Plaspy

Winrich trackers typically transmit location and telemetry over cellular networks to a backend server. Plaspy receives that data, decodes the incoming messages where needed, and presents live and historical information through maps, reports, and notifications. If your Winrich device is listed in the Plaspy catalog it has been tested for compatibility and can be added to Plaspy using the device setup workflow.

Key integration points include:
- Device connectivity and unique identifier registration in Plaspy.
- Periodic GPS updates and status messages displayed in the Plaspy interface.
- Alerting and geofence events generated by Plaspy based on incoming device data.

For device specific configuration steps such as APN settings or message formats consult the device documentation available in the Plaspy device pages and the Winrich vendor resources at the manufacturer website.

## Common features found in Winrich devices

While individual models vary, many Winrich trackers include common capabilities that make them useful for Plaspy monitoring:
- GPS location and timestamped position updates
- Cellular data transmission for remote reporting
- Motion or ignition detection for activity based tracking
- Configurable reporting intervals to balance battery life and timeliness
- Geofence and movement alerts that trigger notifications in Plaspy

These general features allow Plaspy to offer location history, status reporting, and alerting without additional hardware changes. Exact feature availability depends on the device model listed in the BrandCatalog.
