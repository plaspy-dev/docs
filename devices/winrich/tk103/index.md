# Winrich - TK103

![TK103](./tracker.jpg)

The TK103 is a compact vehicle GPS tracker designed for continuous, real time tracking and monitoring. Combining GPS satellite positioning with GSM GPRS communications, the device reports location over GPRS Internet or SMS. Its small form factor and straightforward connectivity make it suitable for cars, vans, and other mobile assets where discreet, ongoing location reporting is required.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the TK103 integrates into Plaspy's online platform to provide live maps, alerts, and historical playback. Features such as emergency alarm reporting, multiple monitoring modes, automatic GPRS reconnection, and remote configuration align with common fleet and security workflows, making the TK103 a practical choice for organizations that want to add tracked vehicles to a Plaspy deployment.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for reliable real time tracking over GPRS and SMS
- Dual mode data transmission with automatic GPRS reconnection to help maintain continuous telemetry
- Emergency alarm reporting and multiple monitoring modes for security and anti theft workflows
- Remote configuration capability to reduce on site maintenance and simplify fleet administration
- Support for point to point, point to group, and group to group monitoring for coordinated operations
- Compact vehicle focused form factor suitable for discreet installation in cars, vans, and mobile assets

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the TK103 delivers position fixes and alarm messages to the platform so managers can monitor assets on live maps, receive notifications, and review historical routes. GPRS Internet is used as the primary data channel while SMS can serve as a fallback to ensure critical events are still communicated. This combination supports continuous visibility and centralized oversight inside Plaspy.

- Real time location updates visible on Plaspy live maps for immediate operational awareness
- Alarm and emergency reporting routed to Plaspy notification channels for rapid response
- SMS fallback preserves delivery of essential messages when data connectivity is interrupted
- Grouped monitoring support lets Plaspy display point to point, point to group, and group to group relationships
- Remote configuration options permit administrators to adjust reporting behavior from the platform where terminal support is available
- Historical route playback and reporting within Plaspy enable review of past movements and simple operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live tracking, route history, and centralized monitoring across vehicles
- Anti theft and vehicle protection using emergency alarm reporting and fallback notification
- Asset surveillance for trailers, rental vehicles, or other mobile equipment requiring continuous location reporting
- Coordinated group operations such as convoys or multi vehicle job sites where grouped views improve oversight
- Operational dispatch and utilization monitoring with regular position updates to support scheduling and routing

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The TK103 is a practical choice for organizations looking to add straightforward, security oriented vehicle tracking to a Plaspy deployment. Its dual mode communications and automatic reconnection behavior are aimed at maintaining continuity of location reporting, and remote configuration reduces the need for frequent onsite adjustments. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker becomes part of a centralized solution for maps, alerts, and historical playback that supports day to day fleet and security operations.
