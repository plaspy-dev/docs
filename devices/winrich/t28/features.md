# Winrich - T28 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Winrich T28 as it relates to using the device with Plaspy. It summarizes the practical capabilities the T28 is marketed for, how those features appear in fleet and asset workflows, and what organizations can expect when adding the T28 to Plaspy for long term location visibility.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Always confirm device capabilities and configuration details against the official Winrich documentation and the T28 configuration guide before deployment.

## Feature Overview

The T28 is positioned as a long life vehicle GPS tracker optimized for extended unattended deployments and discreet installation. Its design emphasizes ultra low power operation and configurable reporting so it can deliver periodic location and status updates while minimizing maintenance.

- Very long advertised operating life in low power configurations for reduced battery service cycles
- Compact form and discreet installation suited to vehicles, trailers, and mobile assets
- Ultra low power modes and sleep wake cycle behavior to preserve battery between reports
- Configurable reporting intervals so you can trade tracking frequency for battery endurance
- Tamper and low battery alerts to support basic anti theft monitoring and device health checks

## Core Features of Winrich - T28

- Extended battery endurance designed for deployments measured in months to years under low power settings
- Configurable reporting intervals to control how often location updates are sent
- Sleep and wake modes to minimize power consumption during long idle periods
- Tamper alert support to notify operators of potential interference or enclosure opening
- Low battery alerting to signal when service or replacement is required
- Compact form factor intended for discreet, hidden installation in vehicles and assets
- Optimized for periodic telemetry rather than continuous high frequency streams

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the T28 reports position and status on the schedule set on the device, and Plaspy ingests those reports into maps, history, and operational tools. Because the T28 is optimized for long standby times, Plaspy treats its telemetry as periodic updates that maintain situational awareness without unnecessary battery drain.

- Periodic location updates are shown on Plaspy maps and archived in history for later review
- Tamper and low battery alerts forwarded to Plaspy help operators prioritize maintenance and security checks
- Configurable reporting intervals let Plaspy balance update frequency and battery conservation for each asset
- Sleep and wake patterns are represented in Plaspy device health indicators to reduce false offline alarms
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols to simplify device onboarding and visibility

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management of intermittently used vehicles where low maintenance overhead is a priority
- Trailer and cargo container tracking for assets that spend long periods idle between movements
- Seasonal equipment monitoring for machinery stored off season that requires occasional checks
- Unattended asset protection and anti theft monitoring using tamper and low battery notifications
- Periodic asset checks for remote or dispersed inventory that do not require continuous tracking

## Feature Availability Notes

- Advertised operating life depends on reporting frequency, configured sleep cycles, and deployment conditions
- Firmware revisions and hardware variants can enable or limit specific behaviors and alerts
- Installation method and mounting location affect signal performance and reported battery life
- Regional variants may differ in cellular support and should be confirmed in the technical specification
- Consult the T28 configuration guide for required network, APN, and device settings before onboarding to Plaspy

Using the Winrich T28 with Plaspy gives organizations a practical way to add long term, low maintenance location awareness to fleet and asset operations. The combination of configurable reporting, ultra low power modes, and alerting for tamper and low battery makes the T28 a fit for scenarios where intermittent but reliable telemetry is more valuable than constant high frequency streams.
