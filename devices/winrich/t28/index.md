# Winrich - T28

![T28](./tracker.jpg)

The T28 is a long-life vehicle GPS tracker engineered for extended unattended deployments and discreet installation. Marketed with an operating life of up to 2 years under low-power configurations, the T28 is positioned as a Plaspy compatible option for teams that need dependable location visibility and low maintenance overhead across vehicles, trailers, and mobile assets.

The device is designed around ultra-low power operation, configurable reporting intervals and sleep/wake cycles to maximize battery endurance. When paired with Plaspy, the T28 provides an efficient way to add long-term telemetry and location data to fleet management workflows, anti-theft monitoring, and periodic asset checks without frequent battery service.

## Key Highlights

- Exceptional battery endurance — advertised operating life up to 2 years under low-power configuration for minimal maintenance cycles.
- Designed for discreet, hidden installation in vehicles and mobile assets where compact form and concealment are needed.
- Ultra-low power modes and sleep/wake cycles reduce energy use while preserving essential telemetry and location reporting.
- Configurable reporting intervals let you balance tracking frequency and battery life for each deployment scenario.
- Tamper and low-battery alerts provide basic anti-theft and health notifications to help protect assets between service intervals.
- Optimized for long-term asset tracking: ideal for trailers, seasonal equipment, cargo containers, and fleet vehicles that are monitored intermittently.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the T28 sends periodic position and status updates according to its configured reporting schedule. Plaspy ingests these reports and converts them into live maps, geofencing alerts, scheduled reports and fleet management dashboards. Because the T28 is optimized for long standby times, expect telemetry to be delivered on a configured cadence rather than continuous high-frequency streams; this preserves battery life while maintaining meaningful situational awareness.

- Real-time tracking \(near real-time based on configured intervals\): location updates and basic telemetry are relayed to Plaspy for map visualization and history.
- Tamper and low-battery alerts are forwarded to Plaspy so operators can respond to potential theft or maintenance needs.
- Configurable reporting intervals let Plaspy balance update frequency with battery conservation for each asset profile.
- Sleep/wake cycles are recognized by Plaspy as part of device health and activity reporting to avoid false offline alarms.
- Compatibility note: confirm required network, APN and reporting protocol settings in the T28 configuration guide to enable straightforward Plaspy onboarding.

- Fleet management for intermittently used vehicles — reduce maintenance visits while retaining positional history and periodic telemetry for planning.
- Trailer and cargo container tracking — long battery life supports long dwell times between check-ins and seasonal movements.
- Seasonal equipment monitoring — ideal for machinery stored off-season that needs periodic location verification and tamper alerts.

Choosing the T28 for Plaspy integration brings a clear operational advantage when long-term, low-maintenance tracking is a priority.
