# Winrich - T18 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the Winrich T18 GPS tracking watch and how it functions when used with the Plaspy platform. It focuses on practical, non sensitive details about what the device can do and how those capabilities appear to caregivers and administrators inside Plaspy.

Exact feature availability can vary by device firmware, hardware revision, regional model variant, and installation choices. Manufacturer implementation and firmware updates may enable or change behaviors, so consider this a high level guide rather than exhaustive technical documentation.

## Feature Overview

The Winrich T18 is a compact wearable tracker designed for personal safety and caregiver monitoring. It combines GPS positioning with LBS support and GSM connectivity to provide persistent location updates, emergency alerts, and voice communication while integrating with Plaspy for visibility and event handling.

- Real time GPS plus LBS positioning for indoor and outdoor location awareness
- SOS emergency alert function that notifies authorized contacts through Plaspy
- Two way voice calling and optional voice monitoring to maintain contact with the wearer
- Geo fence support to receive entry and exit notifications for predefined safe zones
- Low battery warnings and status updates sent to Plaspy to reduce unexpected downtime

## Core Features of Winrich - T18

- GPS positioning enhanced by LBS for improved coverage in urban or indoor areas
- GSM connectivity for voice and data transmission using a standard SIM card slot
- SOS emergency button that sends an alert with location information
- Two way voice calling capability and voice monitoring for caregiver communication
- Low battery reporting to the management platform to support continuous operation
- Route history playback for reviewing past movements through the management interface
- Compact watch form factor with a user oriented LED digital display

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the T18 is paired with Plaspy, its location and event data are collected and presented through the Plaspy management interface so guardians and administrators can act on alerts and review history. Plaspy aggregates incoming device reports into dashboards, alerts, and playback tools for oversight.

- Live location points and position updates displayed on Plaspy maps
- SOS alerts delivered to authorized contacts with time and location context
- Geo fence entry and exit events presented as configurable notifications
- Battery level updates and low battery warnings routed into Plaspy alerts
- Historical route playback accessible for incident review and context
- Voice call events visible in the device activity log to track communications

## Typical Use Cases

- School supervision for student safety during school hours and field trips
- Family monitoring to provide parents real time location and direct voice contact
- Elderly care where caregivers need periodic location updates and emergency alerts
- Outdoor supervision for activities where route review and safe zone alerts are helpful
- Small facility monitoring to consolidate wearable locations and alerts in one portal

## Feature Availability Notes

- Specific features and menu options depend on the T18 firmware version and hardware revision
- Regional variants and local mobile network compatibility can affect GSM behavior and voice services
- Installation and SIM provisioning choices influence reporting frequency and connectivity
- Plaspy presents the information reported by the device and may display different fields depending on the device firmware
- Users should consult manufacturer documentation for model specific configuration and behavior; Plaspy automatically manages common tracker protocols to simplify integration

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Pairing the Winrich T18 with Plaspy provides a central view of location, alerts, and communication events so caregivers and managers can maintain situational awareness without juggling multiple systems. Plaspy consolidates SOS notifications, geo fence triggers, battery warnings, and historical routes into a single monitoring environment suitable for families, schools, and care organizations.
