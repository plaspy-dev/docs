# Winrich - T18

![T18](./tracker.jpg)

**T18 GPS Tracking Watch** — The T18 is a compact, easy-to-use GPS tracker watch designed for children and elderly users that is Plaspy compatible for seamless monitoring and alerts. Combining GPS and LBS positioning with GSM connectivity, the T18 delivers reliable real-time tracking and emergency communication so caregivers and guardians can stay informed and react quickly when it matters most.

The T18 pairs with the Plaspy platform to provide persistent location updates, SOS alerts, and route history playback through a management portal or mobile app. Built for safety and simplicity, the T18 is ideal for schools, families, nursing care, and outdoor supervision where fast, accurate information and clear two-way voice communication are critical.

## Key Highlights

- Real-time GPS + LBS location tracking for reliable indoor and outdoor positioning.
- SOS emergency button sends instant alerts and coordinates to authorized contacts via Plaspy.
- Two-way voice calling and voice monitoring to keep communication open between caregiver and wearer.
- Geo-fence alerts notify caregivers when the wearer enters or leaves predefined safe zones.
- Low battery warnings delivered through Plaspy to avoid unexpected downtime.
- History route playback via the management platform or mobile app for incident review and context.
- Secure SIM card slot for standard GSM communication and dependable voice/data links.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When integrated with Plaspy, the T18 becomes part of a secure monitoring ecosystem. The device transmits GPS and LBS coordinates over GSM to Plaspy, which processes location points, SOS events, battery telemetry, and geo-fence triggers. Plaspy presents this information in an easy-to-use dashboard and mobile app so guardians can view live positions, receive immediate alerts, play back historical routes, and initiate voice contact when needed.

- Real-time location and position updates \(GPS + LBS\) visible on Plaspy maps.
- SOS emergency alerts with coordinates and timestamp pushed to authorized contacts.
- Two-way voice calling and optional voice monitoring routed through the device’s GSM connection.
- Geo-fence entry/exit notifications configured and delivered via Plaspy.
- Battery level telemetry and low battery warnings to ensure continuous operation.
- History route playback for post-event review in the management platform or mobile app.

Choosing the T18 as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker gives you a focused, safety-first solution that pairs wearable convenience with a powerful monitoring platform. The T18’s SOS button, reliable GPS + LBS positioning, and two-way voice make it straightforward to protect vulnerable users while Plaspy delivers consolidated real-time tracking, alerting, and historical telemetry.
