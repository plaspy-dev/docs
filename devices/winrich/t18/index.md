# Winrich - T18

<div className="device-logo">
![T18](./tracker.jpg)
</div>

The T18 is a compact GPS tracking watch from Winrich designed primarily for children and elderly users. It combines GPS and LBS positioning with GSM connectivity to provide continuous location updates, an SOS emergency button, two-way voice communication, and history route playback. The form factor and feature set emphasize safety, simplicity, and constant communication between the wearer and their caregivers or guardians.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the T18 can be integrated into the Plaspy platform to centralize monitoring and alerts. When connected to Plaspy, the watch provides persistent location visibility, SOS notifications, low battery warnings, and route history that caregivers can access through Plaspy management tools. This pairing helps organizations and families maintain situational awareness and respond quickly when incidents occur.

## Key Highlights

- Compact wearable design intended for children and elderly users
- Combined GPS and LBS positioning for improved indoor and outdoor coverage
- SOS emergency button that sends immediate alerts to authorized contacts
- Two-way voice calling to enable direct communication between caregiver and wearer
- Geo-fence alerts to notify caregivers when a wearer enters or leaves predefined safe areas
- Route history playback and low battery notifications available through a monitoring platform

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the T18 is paired with Plaspy, location and event data from the watch are sent to the platform where they are processed and displayed for caregivers and managers. Plaspy consolidates real-time positions, SOS events, geo-fence triggers, and battery status into an accessible interface so authorized users can monitor devices, receive alerts, and review past activity.

- Real-time location updates visible on Plaspy maps for quick situational awareness
- SOS alerts with coordinates and timestamps routed to designated contacts through Plaspy
- Two-way voice calling initiated by the device and coordinated with Plaspy contact lists
- Geo-fence entry and exit notifications configurable and delivered by Plaspy
- Low battery warnings and status updates shown in Plaspy to help maintain continuous operation
- Route history playback for post event review and incident context within the platform

## Typical Use Cases

- School safety programs monitoring student movements during pick up and drop off
- Family use for parental reassurance and direct communication with children
- Elderly care where caregivers need location updates and emergency call capability
- Outdoor supervision for group outings and activities with geo-fence monitoring
- Facility or program oversight where multiple wearable devices are managed centrally

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The T18 offers a focused solution for personal safety monitoring that aligns well with Plaspy’s strengths in real-time tracking and alert management. Its wearable format and safety features make it easy to deploy for caregiving scenarios, while Plaspy provides the centralized dashboard, notifications, and history tools required for effective oversight. Together they deliver a straightforward way to improve response times and maintain clear records of events.
