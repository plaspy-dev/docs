# DCT - Syrus Satcom Lite

![Syrus Satcom Lite](./tracker.png)

The Syrus Satcom Lite \(Item #1204-10\) is a battery-powered, satellite-enabled GPS tracker built to extend Plaspy compatible real-time tracking to assets operating beyond cellular coverage. Designed for harsh and remote environments, the device combines motion sensing, comparative GPS position reporting and configurable auxiliary sensor inputs to give operations teams continuous visibility and actionable telemetry where traditional trackers cannot reach.

As a Plaspy compatible asset tracker, Syrus Satcom Lite delivers remote visibility, configurable alerts and emergency notifications that integrate with cloud platforms. Management through the Pegasus IoT Cloud and the ability to forward status updates and urgent alerts by email or text makes this GPS tracker a reliable choice for fleet management, anti-theft protection and remote asset monitoring across industries such as agriculture, maritime, construction, mining, forestry, government and public safety.

## Key Highlights

- Satellite-enabled connectivity for reliable tracking outside cellular coverage — ideal for remote fleet management and field operations.
- Battery-powered, rugged design for long-term deployments in harsh environments.
- Built-in motion sensors and comparative GPS positions detect movement and location changes for anti-theft and recovery workflows.
- Configurable auxiliary sensor inputs allow custom telemetry and status monitoring tailored to your assets.
- Configurable intermediate reports and urgent emergency alerts delivered via email or text for fast response.
- Managed through Pegasus IoT Cloud with real-time visualization and remote device management; integrable with Plaspy for consolidated tracking and reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When deployed as a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the Syrus Satcom Lite streams satellite and GPS telemetry into Plaspy \(directly or via integration\), enabling real-time tracking, rules-based alerts and centralized reporting. Plaspy ingests location updates, motion events and auxiliary sensor data so operations teams can monitor assets, trigger workflows and view historical movement on maps and dashboards.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates routed into Plaspy for continuous situational awareness.
- Motion detection and comparative GPS position changes for anti-theft alerts and recovery notifications.
- Configurable auxiliary sensor telemetry \(custom inputs\) available to Plaspy for condition monitoring.
- Emergency and exception alerts forwarded by text or email to contact lists for immediate action.
- Plaspy can accept and display ignition, fuel monitoring, immobilizer or Bluetooth sensor data when those inputs or accessory modules are provided by the device or integrations.
