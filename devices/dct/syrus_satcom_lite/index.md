# DCT - Syrus Satcom Lite

<div className="device-logo">
![Syrus Satcom Lite](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Syrus Satcom Lite (Item #1204-10) is a battery powered, satellite enabled GPS tracker designed to extend real time tracking to assets operating beyond cellular coverage. Built for harsh and remote environments, the device combines motion sensing, comparative GPS position reporting and configurable auxiliary sensor inputs to provide continuous visibility and actionable telemetry where traditional trackers may lose connectivity.

As a Plaspy compatible asset tracker, the Syrus Satcom Lite brings satellite reach and event driven reporting into Plaspy for consolidated monitoring and incident response. Its integration into cloud based workflows and the ability to forward status updates and urgent alerts by email or text make it a suitable option for teams that need reliable remote visibility, configurable alerts and centralized reporting through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Satellite enabled connectivity for tracking assets outside cellular coverage
- Battery powered and rugged design for long term deployments in remote locations
- Built in motion sensing and comparative GPS position reporting for movement detection
- Configurable auxiliary sensor inputs to capture custom telemetry and status
- Emergency and exception alerts forwarded by email or text for rapid notification
- Managed through Pegasus IoT Cloud with options to integrate telemetry into Plaspy

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used as a Plaspy compatible device, the Syrus Satcom Lite feeds satellite and GPS telemetry into Plaspy so operations teams can maintain situational awareness, trigger alerts and review historical movement. Plaspy ingests location updates, motion events and auxiliary sensor data to support monitoring, reporting and workflow automation.

- Real time location and telemetry updates routed into Plaspy for continuous visibility
- Motion detection and comparative position events used to trigger anti theft and movement alerts
- Auxiliary sensor inputs available in Plaspy for condition monitoring and customized rules
- Emergency and exception alerts forwarded to contact lists for immediate attention
- Historical playback and reporting in Plaspy for incident review and operational analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet and heavy equipment anti theft and recovery when assets operate beyond cellular coverage
- Remote site and asset monitoring in construction, mining and forestry deployments
- Maritime and agricultural asset tracking where offshore or field operations lack cellular service
- Public safety and government asset monitoring with remote emergency notification needs
- Long duration deployments of trailers, containers and field equipment requiring low maintenance visibility

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Syrus Satcom Lite brings satellite connectivity and configurable telemetry into a Plaspy compatible tracking strategy, helping close coverage gaps that cellular only devices cannot address. Its combination of motion sensing, comparative GPS reporting and auxiliary inputs provides flexible data points that integrate with Plaspy dashboards, alerts and reports to improve operational oversight and incident response.

If your operations require dependable remote visibility across distributed assets in tough environments, pairing the Syrus Satcom Lite with Plaspy offers a practical path to centralized monitoring and configurable alerting.
