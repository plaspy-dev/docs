# DCT - Solar Asset Tracker Features

This page provides a public summary of the Solar Asset Tracker from DCT and how its capabilities relate to monitoring and fleet workflows in Plaspy. It focuses on user facing functions and operational value that Plaspy users can expect when deploying this model for remote asset tracking and anti theft monitoring.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation details, and manufacturer implementation. For device specific technical limits, radio bands, and the latest firmware behavior consult the official manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Solar Asset Tracker is designed for long duration outdoor deployments where mains power is unavailable. It combines solar charging with an internal battery, GNSS positioning with fallback options, cellular telemetry, and event-driven alerts to keep asset location and device health visible to monitoring platforms such as Plaspy.

- Self charging with an integrated solar panel plus a rechargeable battery to extend unattended operation.
- GNSS positioning using GPS and BDS with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to improve fix rates in difficult environments.
- Global 4G cellular telemetry for continuous location and event reporting to cloud platforms.
- Rugged IP67 polycarbonate enclosure suitable for outdoor, maritime, and industrial asset installations.
- Event notifications for tamper or vibration, movement, geofence breaches, and low battery to support anti‑theft and maintenance workflows.

## Core Features of DCT - Solar Asset Tracker

- Integrated solar panel and rechargeable battery for extended off grid autonomy.
- GNSS positioning with support for GPS and BDS satellites.
- Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to supplement GNSS where satellite reception is limited.
- Global 4G cellular connectivity for live telemetry and alerts.
- Rugged IP67 rated polycarbonate housing for dust and water protection.
- Tamper and vibration detection for anti‑theft alerts.
- Configurable geofence and movement alerts for perimeter and unauthorized movement detection.
- Multiple mounting options including magnet or bolt mounts for flexible deployment.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When configured for Plaspy compatibility, the tracker forwards location, event flags, and device health so you can manage assets alongside other fleet data in a single system. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry and presents it in maps, alerts, and historical reports to support operational decisions.

- Real time location updates and live map tracking for remote assets inside Plaspy.
- Tamper, movement, geofence, and low battery events delivered as notifications and visible in event logs.
- Device health indicators such as battery status and connectivity shown for maintenance planning.
- Historical route playback and timestamped telemetry for post event analysis and reporting.
- Integration with Plaspy alerts and workflows to trigger notifications, assignments, or escalation processes.

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container location tracking to improve cargo security and logistics visibility.
- Anti theft monitoring for remote or parked assets using tamper and movement alerts.
- Heavy equipment oversight on construction and mining sites to detect unauthorized moves.
- Off grid asset monitoring in agriculture and forestry where solar autonomy reduces maintenance.
- Maritime and near shore equipment tracking that benefits from rugged enclosures and GNSS fallback.
