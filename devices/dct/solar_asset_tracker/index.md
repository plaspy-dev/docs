# DCT - Solar Asset Tracker

<div className="device-logo">
![Solar Asset Tracker](./tracker.png)
</div>

The DCT Solar Asset Tracker (SPA4G-1204-16) is a rugged, self powered GPS tracker built for long term outdoor deployments. With an integrated solar panel and a 10Ah rechargeable battery, the unit is intended to remain operational on remote assets without mains power. It combines GNSS positioning with Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback and uses global 4G connectivity to deliver continuous location updates, device health, and event flags for fleet monitoring and anti theft scenarios.

This tracker is listed as compatible with Plaspy and can forward its telemetry into the Plaspy platform for centralized monitoring and management. That compatibility makes the Solar Asset Tracker a practical option for organizations that want to incorporate long duration, off grid assets into Plaspy workflows for live tracking, alerting, and historical reporting alongside other fleet devices.

## Key Highlights

- Integrated solar panel with 10Ah rechargeable battery for extended off grid operation
- Designed for Plaspy compatibility to stream real time location and telemetry into your fleet platform
- Multiple positioning methods with GNSS plus Wi‑Fi and LBS fallback to improve fixes in challenging areas
- Global 4G connectivity for continuous telemetry, event delivery, and remote cloud management
- Rugged IP67 polycarbonate enclosure suitable for outdoor, maritime, and industrial environments
- Event driven alerts including tamper or vibration detection, configurable geofences, movement and low battery notifications
- Flexible mounting options such as magnet or bolt mounts for fast deployment on trailers, containers, and equipment

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured for Plaspy, the Solar Asset Tracker sends location, status, and event data to the Plaspy platform where it becomes part of fleet dashboards, alerts, and reports. Plaspy can then present live positions on maps, trigger notifications, and retain historical data for auditing and analytics.

- Real time location updates over global 4G for live map tracking in Plaspy
- Immediate tamper and vibration alerts routed to Plaspy notifications for rapid response
- Device health and low battery reporting to help schedule maintenance and reduce downtime
- Configurable geofence and movement alerts to detect perimeter breaches and unauthorized moves
- Data available for historic route playback, fleet dashboards, and exportable reports within Plaspy

## Typical Use Cases

- Trailer and container tracking for cargo security and logistics visibility
- Heavy equipment monitoring on construction and mining sites to detect unauthorized moves
- Anti theft protection for remote assets using tamper and geofence alerts
- Off grid fleet monitoring in agriculture and forestry where solar autonomy reduces maintenance
- Commercial maritime and field equipment tracking where weather resistance and fallback positioning improve reliability
