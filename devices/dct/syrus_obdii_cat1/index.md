# DCT - SYRUS OBDII-CAT1

![SYRUS OBDII-CAT1](./tracker.png)

The SYRUS OBDII-CAT1 \(Item # SO4G-CAT1-1809\) is a plug-and-play OBDII telematics dongle from Syrus designed for passenger and light-duty vehicles. Plaspy-compatible and engineered for rapid deployments, this OBDII tracker delivers real-time tracking and vehicle diagnostics directly from the vehicle port, enabling fleet managers to start collecting location, telemetry, and driving-event data within minutes.

Built for global use, the OBDII-CAT1 combines a high-sensitivity GPS engine \(with SBAS support and up to 2 m performance\) and a 3-axis accelerometer with pentaband cellular connectivity to provide reliable telematics for fleet management, anti-theft awareness, fuel monitoring and preventive maintenance. Its self-configuring installation and cloud-ready design make it a practical choice for scale deployments and integration with Plaspy for centralized visualization, alerts, and API-based workflows.

## Key Highlights

- Instant plug-and-play OBDII installation — no wiring required for fast fleet rollouts.
- High-sensitivity GPS with SBAS \(WAAS/EGNOS/MSAS/GAGAN\) and performance up to 2 meters for precise real-time tracking.
- Pentaband cellular support \(GSM/GPRS/EDGE/UMTS/HSPA+\) for wide regional coverage and reliable telemetry delivery.
- Rich engine diagnostics from OBDII: engine RPM, fuel level, oil and coolant temperature, throttle position and other OBD parameters.
- Integrated 3-axis accelerometer for detection of harsh braking, impacts and driving events to support driver behavior monitoring.
- Cloud-ready device management — self-configures with gateway platforms and can be deployed as Plaspy compatible for dashboards and API access.
- Optimized for passenger and light commercial vehicles, enabling fleet management, trip analysis and preventive maintenance at scale.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When configured as Plaspy compatible, the SYRUS OBDII-CAT1 streams OBD telemetry, GNSS location, and accelerometer events to the cloud where Plaspy ingests the data for real-time tracking, alerts, and historical reporting. The device reads standard OBD PIDs from the vehicle, acquires GPS fixes with high sensitivity and forwards telemetry over cellular networks to your telematics platform. Plaspy can then use that feed for fleet monitoring, maintenance scheduling, anti-theft notifications, and API integrations into back-office systems.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates for continuous visibility and route monitoring.
- Engine diagnostics: engine RPM, fuel level, oil & coolant temperature, throttle position and other OBD parameters for fuel monitoring and health checks.
- Driving-event detection and impact alerts via the integrated 3-axis accelerometer to support driver behavior programs.
- Trip logs, route playback, and aggregated reports for fleet management and route optimization.
- Remote provisioning and device diagnostics via cloud device management; integrates with Plaspy dashboards and APIs for alerting and data export.
