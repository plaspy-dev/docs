# DCT - Titan Tracker

<div className="device-logo">
![Titan Tracker](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Titan Tracker is a portable GPS tracker designed for personal safety and asset monitoring. It is offered in two SKUs to match regional cellular availability and combines an SOS panic button, a 3-axis accelerometer for motion and impact detection, vibration feedback for panic confirmation, and enhanced GNSS positioning in an IPX6 waterproof housing suited to demanding field conditions.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Titan streams location, alert and event data into Plaspy for unified monitoring and reporting. Its remote management capabilities and cloud device management support make it a practical choice to integrate with Plaspy dashboards, alerting rules and fleet workflows for real time oversight and historical analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for real time location and alert ingestion into unified monitoring and reporting.
- Dual SKUs for regional cellular needs with a 3G option and a 4G option that extends coverage capacity.
- SOS panic button with vibration feedback to confirm distress activations in the field.
- 3-axis accelerometer for motion and impact detection to generate timely events and improve situational awareness.
- IPX6 rated waterproof housing and rugged design for use in wet and harsh environments.
- Assisted Wi Fi tracking to complement GNSS and improve position reliability in obstructed or urban areas.
- Cloud based device management via the manufacturer's gateway to simplify deployment and remote configuration.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Titan Tracker feeds positional updates, alerts and event information into Plaspy’s platform so operators can monitor devices, respond to incidents and analyze historical trends. Plaspy can receive SOS activations, motion and impact events, device health indicators and location fixes to support operational workflows.

- Real time location updates delivered to Plaspy dashboards for live monitoring and route playback.
- SOS and panic alerts integrated into Plaspy notification and escalation workflows with clear confirmation signals.
- Motion and impact events from the accelerometer used to trigger immediate alerts and logging for incident review.
- Device connectivity and health status surfaced for maintenance planning and uptime monitoring.
- Assisted Wi Fi and GNSS data combined in Plaspy to improve tracking accuracy in challenging signal environments.
- Historical reporting and analytics within Plaspy for trend analysis and operational optimization.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet worker safety monitoring for mobile staff and field teams requiring position and alerting.
- Lone worker protection using a discrete panic button with vibration confirmation.
- Portable asset tracking for rented equipment, tools, and temporary assets in transit or storage.
- Logistics and last mile operations where real time location and incident reporting improve dispatch and customer service.
- Short term or seasonal deployments that benefit from remote configuration and low power modes.

The Titan Tracker is a practical fit for organizations using Plaspy when a compact, rugged device is needed to deliver location, safety alerts and event telemetry into a single monitoring platform. Its SKU options help align connectivity choices to regional coverage, while assisted positioning and impact detection provide useful context for response and reporting workflows inside Plaspy.

Paired with Plaspy, the Titan reduces the need for frequent field visits through cloud based management and supports common fleet and safety workflows without adding complexity.
