# DCT - Titan Tracker Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the DCT Titan Tracker and the primary features you can expect when integrating the device with Plaspy. It focuses on practical capabilities, how those capabilities map into Plaspy workflows, and the types of operational value the tracker can deliver for safety, asset monitoring, and field deployments.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, SKU, installation type, and the manufacturer's implementation choices. Use this page for general guidance about capabilities and Plaspy compatibility, and consult the manufacturer documentation for the latest device specific details.

## Feature Overview

The Titan Tracker is a portable, rugged GPS tracker built for personal safety and asset monitoring with explicit compatibility for integration into Plaspy. It is offered in two SKUs so organizations can choose the cellular technology that best fits their regional coverage and operational needs.

- Two SKUs to match regional networks: a 3G model ST-1734G and a 4G model ST-4GM1 with LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT support.
- Dedicated SOS panic button with vibration feedback so users receive tactile confirmation when an alert is sent.
- 3-axis accelerometer for motion and impact detection to generate event-based alerts and incident records.
- Assisted Wi‑Fi and sensitive GNSS performance to improve location reliability in urban or obstructed environments.
- Rugged IPX6 waterproof housing and a portable form factor suited to harsh field conditions.

## Core Features of DCT - Titan Tracker

- SOS / panic button with vibration confirmation for user-initiated emergency alerts.
- 3-axis accelerometer for motion detection and impact sensing to create automated events.
- Dual SKU cellular options: ST-1734G (3G) and ST-4GM1 (4G) to support regional connectivity needs.
- 4G variant supports LTE Cat M1 and NB‑IoT with fallback to GPRS/EDGE for broader coverage.
- Assisted Wi‑Fi positioning to supplement GNSS where satellite signals are weak.
- IPX6 rated enclosure designed for wet and harsh environmental use.
- Ultra-low-power sleep mode intended to extend deployment duration for portable uses.
- Cloud device management via the Pegasus Gateway IoT Platform for remote configuration and OTA updates.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the Titan Tracker supplies location, event, and status information that Plaspy can show on dashboards, include in alerts, and store for reporting and playback. Plaspy automatically detects supported tracker protocols and ingests the tracker data into monitoring workflows.

- Real-time location updates displayed in Plaspy dashboards and route playback for operational visibility.
- SOS/panic events appear in Plaspy alerting streams and can be routed to escalation policies or dispatch procedures.
- Motion and impact events from the accelerometer are logged and can trigger notifications or incident workflows in Plaspy.
- Device connectivity and health indicators reported to Plaspy to support maintenance and uptime monitoring.
- Assisted Wi‑Fi and GNSS improvements help Plaspy provide more reliable positioning in dense or obstructed areas.

## Typical Use Cases

- Lone worker protection where a panic button and event detection provide immediate alerting and location context.
- Portable asset tracking for tools, rental equipment, and valuables that require rugged, weatherproof devices.
- Field workforce safety and monitoring for mobile staff and service crews in challenging environments.
- Logistics and last mile oversight to monitor location, incidents, and connectivity during urban routes.
- Temporary or project-based deployments that benefit from low-power modes and OTA device management.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature behavior can differ between the ST-1734G (3G) and ST-4GM1 (4G) SKUs; choose the SKU that matches regional network availability.
- Firmware version and hardware revision may enable, refine, or change how specific features behave; consult release notes for details.
- Installation and mounting method can affect sensor performance such as accelerometer-triggered event sensitivity.
- Some connectivity behaviors, including fallback strategies and network band support, are dependent on carrier and regional variants.
- Manufacturer documentation and technical datasheets remain the authoritative source for precise device specifications.
