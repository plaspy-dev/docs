# DCT - Syrus 4G

![Syrus 4G](./tracker.png)

The Syrus 4G from DCT is an enterprise-grade telematics gateway built for demanding fleet and industrial IoT deployments. Designed as a rugged vehicle gateway, it combines multi-constellation GNSS positioning, redundant cellular connectivity, and a range of vehicle and sensor interfaces to deliver dependable real-time tracking and telemetry in harsh environments and at scale.

As a Plaspy compatible device, Syrus 4G can forward preprocessed location and telemetry to Plaspy for consolidated fleet visibility. Its support for local data preprocessing, sensor connectivity, and remote management makes it a practical option for organizations that want reliable tracking, diagnostic forwarding, and integrated monitoring inside the Plaspy platform.

## Key Highlights

- Enterprise-grade telematics gateway engineered for vehicle and industrial environments
- Multi-constellation GNSS for improved positioning across regions
- Dual cellular modems with optional satellite backup and eSIM support for resilient connectivity
- Rich vehicle and sensor interfaces for collecting telemetry from buses and external devices
- Onboard edge processing to preprocess or aggregate data before forwarding
- Bluetooth sensor support for temperature, driver devices, and auxiliary sensors
- Remote device management and OTA tooling to help maintain fleet integrations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, Syrus 4G acts as a local collector and forwarder of location and telemetry, enabling live tracking and historical analysis while reducing unnecessary cloud bandwidth through edge preprocessing. Integration with Plaspy allows operators to centralize monitoring, alerts, and reporting across fleets using the data forwarded by the gateway.

- Real-time location updates pushed to Plaspy for live fleet tracking and map visualization
- Vehicle bus telemetry mapped into Plaspy for dashboarding and operational insight
- Diagnostic trouble codes and maintenance events forwarded to Plaspy for alerting and workflows
- Bluetooth sensor and driver device inputs relayed through the gateway into Plaspy records
- Remote management and OTA practices help keep Plaspy integrations updated and secure

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route monitoring with consolidated location and telemetry in Plaspy
- Anti-theft monitoring and tamper detection using secure hardware and live tracking
- Remote diagnostics and preventive maintenance workflows driven by forwarded error codes
- Fuel and engine telemetry analytics for efficiency programs and reporting
- Sensorized asset monitoring and driver behavior detection for safety and compliance

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

Syrus 4G is suited to organizations that need a robust gateway rather than a basic tracker. Its combination of resilient connectivity options, broad telemetry interfaces, and edge processing capabilities make it appropriate for large fleets and industrial applications where uptime and data quality matter. When paired with Plaspy, the device can provide consistent location visibility and actionable telemetry for operations, maintenance, and security teams.
