# DCT - Syrus Lynx 4G

<div className="device-logo">
![Syrus Lynx 4G](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Syrus Lynx 4G is a compact vehicle mounted GPS GNSS tracker designed for robust fleet management and large scale vehicle tracking. Its small form factor supports discreet installation while maintaining continuous reporting and reliable telemetry suitable for rental fleets, insurance telematics, and high volume rollouts.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Lynx 4G provides a straightforward path to integrate hardware telemetry with Plaspy’s monitoring and reporting tools. When paired with Plaspy, the tracker’s position, status, and alerts feed into a centralized platform for real time visibility, rule based notifications, and remote response options such as immobilization.

## Key Highlights

- Confirmed Plaspy compatibility for real time tracking, alerts, and reporting integration
- LTE Cat 1 4G connectivity with 3G and 2G fallback for broad coverage
- Internal backup battery preserves location reporting during power interruptions
- Built in telemetry including time based and heading reports, tow alarm detection, and driving behavior monitoring
- Remote immobilizer support to enable anti theft response from the platform
- Compact concealed form factor suitable for rental and fleet installations
- Provisioned multi carrier M2M SIM to simplify large deployment logistics and reduce regional dead zones

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Syrus Lynx 4G transmits GNSS position and telemetry over its cellular connection to cloud endpoints that Plaspy ingests. Once connected as a Plaspy compatible tracker, the device supplies live location and event streams that Plaspy uses for mapping, alerts, and historical analysis, enabling operators to monitor vehicles and act on incidents from a central dashboard.

- Real time location and telemetry visible in Plaspy for mapping and live monitoring
- Alarm and tow detection events generate immediate alerts and notifications within Plaspy
- Driving behavior telemetry feeds into scoring and safety reports for fleet oversight
- Remote immobilizer commands issued from Plaspy are forwarded to the device for asset protection
- Aggregation of external sensor or fuel data in Plaspy when such integrations are present and configured

## Typical Use Cases

- Rental fleet management requiring discreet tracking, utilization monitoring, and theft prevention
- Insurance telematics programs collecting driving behavior for usage based policies
- Large scale rollouts where multi carrier SIM provisioning simplifies logistics
- Fleet operations seeking continuous location, alerts, and historical reporting for efficiency
- Anti theft protection and recovery workflows using tow alarms and remote immobilization
