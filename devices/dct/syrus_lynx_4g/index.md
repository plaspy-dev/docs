# Syrus Lynx 4G

The Syrus Lynx 4G is a compact, vehicle-mounted GPS/GNSS tracker engineered for robust fleet management and large-scale vehicle tracking deployments. Built for discreet installation and continuous reporting, the Lynx 4G pairs reliable telemetry with a small form factor — now presented as Plaspy compatible to give fleet operators a simple path to real-time tracking, centralized alerts, and remote vehicle control.

Purpose-built for rental fleets, insurance telematics, and high-volume rollouts, the Lynx 4G delivers LTE Cat-1 4G connectivity with fallback to 3G and 2G, an internal backup battery to preserve location reporting during power interruptions, and operational features such as time-based and heading reports, tow alarm detection, driving behavior monitoring, and remote vehicle immobilization.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible: integrates with Plaspy platforms for real-time tracking, alerts, and reporting.
- Multi-network cellular connectivity: LTE Cat-1 4G with 3G/2G fallback for wide coverage and reliability.
- Internal backup battery preserves location and alerting during power loss or tampering.
- Built-in telemetry: time-based and heading reports, tow alarm detection, and driving behavior monitoring for safer fleets.
- Remote immobilizer support enables anti-theft response from the Plaspy interface.
- Concealed, compact vehicle form factor ideal for rental operations and discreet installations.
- Provisioned with multi-carrier M2M Cloud Connect SIM to reduce regional dead zones and simplify global logistics.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The Syrus Lynx 4G sends GNSS position and telemetry over its multi-carrier cellular link to a cloud backend that Plaspy can ingest. When deployed as a Plaspy compatible tracker, location, status, and alert streams are available in Plaspy’s dashboard for live monitoring, rule-based alerts, and historical reporting. Remote commands such as immobilization are executed through secure cloud APIs so operators can respond quickly to theft, misuse, or critical events.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates transmitted via LTE Cat-1 \(with 3G/2G fallback\) and received by Plaspy for mapping and monitoring.
- Tow alarm and alarm/event status—Plaspy receives alarm triggers to generate immediate alerts.
- Driving behavior telemetry \(speeding, harsh braking\) is relayed as telematics data Plaspy can use for scoring and reports.
- Remote immobilizer commands issued from Plaspy are forwarded to the device to help secure assets.
- Fuel monitoring and Bluetooth sensors: Plaspy can aggregate fuel or sensor data if external sensors are integrated and supported in the installation and backend configuration.
