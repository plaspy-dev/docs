# DCT - Syrus Asset Tracker Features

This page provides a public summary of the Syrus Asset Tracker features and how the device is used with Plaspy for location monitoring and event reporting. It focuses on observable, non sensitive capabilities and practical value when the Syrus Asset Tracker is paired with Plaspy for fleet and asset management.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional cellular variant, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. For precise, device specific technical details and the latest specifications consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The Syrus Asset Tracker is a rugged, waterproof GPS tracker designed for long term monitoring of non powered and exposed assets. When integrated with Plaspy it provides dependable location reporting, motion based alerts, and remote mode control to balance battery life and tracking granularity.

- Long autonomy optimized reporting modes for minimal maintenance on assets without vehicle power
- Rugged, weatherproof enclosure and flexible mounting for exposed outdoor deployments
- Motion sensing with accelerometer based Unauthorized Movement alerts and event driven reporting
- Over the air mode switching to toggle between low power locate cadence and higher frequency tracking
- Dedicated 4G variants including a temperature capable model for condition monitoring in transit or storage

## Core Features of DCT - Syrus Asset Tracker

- Rugged waterproof form factor intended for long term outdoor asset use
- Extremely long battery life through configurable reporting and low power modes
- 3G and 4G model variants noted in product line with 3G discontinued and 4G models current
- Built in accelerometer for motion detection and Unauthorized Movement alerts
- Over the air remote management to switch reporting modes and adjust profiles
- Place ’n Trace rapid provisioning for fast deployment and immediate reporting
- 4G Temp Tracker variant that provides temperature telemetry for inclusion in asset condition tracking

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the Syrus Asset Tracker reports location and motion events so operators can monitor assets in real time, review history, and configure alerts. Plaspy exposes the device state, notifications, and remote mode controls that help preserve battery life while keeping assets visible when needed.

- Live location updates displayed on Plaspy maps and included in historical reports
- Motion and Unauthorized Movement alerts forwarded to Plaspy for immediate notification and incident review
- Remote switching between low frequency locate mode and high frequency track mode from the Plaspy interface
- Place ’n Trace provisioning speeds up onboarding so assets appear in Plaspy shortly after installation
- Temperature readings from the 4G Temp Tracker variant can be captured and shown alongside location history where applicable

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers, shipping containers, and other unpowered assets
- Construction and rental fleet management to locate and recover equipment with minimal maintenance
- Yard and intermodal container management for outdoor cargo visibility
- Temperature sensitive shipments or stored equipment using the 4G Temp Tracker variant
- Industrial IoT deployments that require battery powered, event driven location telemetry
- Low touch asset monitoring programs where extended autonomy is a priority

## Feature Availability Notes

- The 3G model in the product line has been discontinued while 4G variants are current; confirm the specific part number before purchase
- Firmware version and configuration profiles affect available reporting cadences and alert behavior
- Hardware revisions and regional cellular variants can change supported bands and network availability
- Installation method and mounting location influence GPS reception and motion sensing sensitivity
- Verify temperature telemetry capabilities and limits for the Temp Tracker variant in the manufacturer documentation

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the Syrus Asset Tracker with Plaspy gives teams a low maintenance way to keep off grid and exposed assets visible. Plaspy consolidates location data, event alerts, and remote device mode controls so operators can maintain awareness and respond to unauthorized movement without frequent physical visits. The combination is well suited to deployments that prioritize long battery life, robust enclosure design, and straightforward provisioning.
