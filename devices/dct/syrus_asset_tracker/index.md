# DCT - Syrus Asset Tracker

<div className="device-logo">
![Syrus Asset Tracker](./tracker.png)
</div>

The Syrus Asset Tracker is a rugged, waterproof GPS tracker designed for long term monitoring of non powered and exposed assets. It is built for harsh outdoor environments and delivers dependable, low maintenance location reporting for containers, trailers, construction equipment, and other off grid assets where long autonomy and reliable cellular reporting are important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the Syrus Asset Tracker integrates with Plaspy to provide real time location visibility, configurable telemetry cadence, and motion based unauthorized movement alerts. Its compact weatherproof enclosure and flexible mounting options make it a practical choice for fleet management and industrial IoT deployments that require minimal intervention and steady reporting over extended periods.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker optimized for long term asset monitoring and fleet management integration
- Rugged, waterproof enclosure and versatile mounting options for exposed outdoor use
- Multiple cellular variants including legacy 3G option and 4G models to match deployment needs
- Extremely long battery life supported by reporting mode switching to balance autonomy and tracking granularity
- Built in motion sensing for unauthorized movement alerts and event driven reporting
- Rapid Place 'n Trace provisioning for fast onboarding of assets into Plaspy
- Optional temperature capable 4G Temp Tracker variant for asset condition monitoring

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the Syrus Asset Tracker provides low maintenance, persistent location and motion telemetry. The device reports positions and motion events over cellular links to Plaspy where operators can view live locations, set alerts, and analyze historical movements. Remote configuration lets teams prioritize battery life or tracking frequency as operational needs change.

- Real time location and motion telemetry available in Plaspy mapping and dashboards
- Unauthorized movement alerts trigger notifications and provide immediate location snapshots
- Over the air mode switching from low power locate to higher frequency track modes via Plaspy
- Fast provisioning with Place 'n Trace so assets appear in Plaspy shortly after installation
- Temperature readings from the 4G Temp Tracker variant can be included in Plaspy condition monitoring

## Typical Use Cases

- Long term tracking of trailers, containers, and mobile equipment without vehicle power
- Construction and rental fleet asset management where low maintenance tracking is required
- Intermodal and yard management for outdoor cargo and shipping containers
- Temperature sensitive asset monitoring using the 4G Temp Tracker variant
- Industrial IoT deployments that need reliable battery powered telemetry and event driven alarms

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The Syrus Asset Tracker is a purpose built option for organizations that need dependable, long life monitoring of non powered assets. Its durable, waterproof design and flexible mounting make it well suited to outdoor fleet and industrial use cases where maintenance visits should be minimized and reporting must remain reliable.

Integrating the Syrus Asset Tracker with Plaspy brings real time visibility, configurable reporting cadence, and event based alerts into a unified fleet or asset management workflow. For broader telemetry needs, this tracker pairs within Plaspy deployments alongside vehicle installed trackers and compatible sensor gateways to extend monitoring where required.
