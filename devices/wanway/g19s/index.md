# WanWay - G19S

![G19S](./Logo.jpg)

The WanWay G19S GPS tracker is a smart and versatile device that combines GSM and GPS technologies with built-in antennas and sensors. With its compact dimensions of 83mm\(L\) x 40mm\(W\) x 14mm\(H\) and lightweight design of just 54g, it is easy to install and conceal in any vehicle. This tracker offers a range of features including ACC detection, vibration alarm, SOS emergency call, and the ability to remotely cut off petrol or electricity. Additionally, when connected to a microphone, users can even listen to what is happening around the vehicle.

One of the key advantages of the G19S is its real-time tracking capability, allowing vehicle owners to monitor the location of their assets directly from their smartphones. Whether you need to track a single vehicle or an entire fleet, this GPS tracker provides accurate positioning with a GPS accuracy of \<=5m. It operates on quad-band frequencies \(850/1900/900/1800MHz\) and has a voltage input range of 9-90v, making it compatible with a wide range of vehicles.

The WanWay G19S GPS tracker also offers additional functions such as waterproof protection, trace playback, SMS notifications, and multiple alert protections. With its reliable performance, compact size, and comprehensive features, the G19S is an ideal choice for individuals and businesses looking to enhance the security and tracking capabilities of their vehicles.

### Key Features:

- Real-time tracking for individual vehicles or entire fleets
- Compact and lightweight design for easy installation and concealment
- ACC detection, vibration alarm, and SOS emergency call
- Remote cut-off of petrol or electricity
- Ability to listen to what is happening around the vehicle with a connected microphone
- Waterproof protection for reliable performance in various conditions
- Trace playback for reviewing past routes and movements
- SMS notifications for important alerts and updates
- Multiple alert protections for enhanced security
