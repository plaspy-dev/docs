# WanWay - G19S Features

This page provides a public, non-sensitive overview of the WanWay G19S features and how those capabilities are presented when the device is used with Plaspy. It focuses on practical, user-facing functions you can expect to see in the platform and describes how the tracker reports location and events into Plaspy for monitoring and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and by how the device is installed or configured. For device specific setup, wiring details, or the latest technical specifications consult the official manufacturer documentation and your installer.

## Feature Overview

The WanWay G19S is a compact GSM GPS tracker built for vehicle tracking and security monitoring. It combines real-time location reporting with a set of alarms and auxiliary functions intended to support both single vehicle use and small to medium fleet oversight.

- Real time GPS tracking with reported position accuracy up to 5 meters for location monitoring.
- ACC detection and vibration alarm to surface ignition state changes and movement alerts.
- SOS emergency call capability for immediate alerting of an emergency event.
- Remote cut off function to interrupt petrol or electrical supply when configured and wired.
- Optional listen in support when an external microphone is connected for audio monitoring.
- Trace playback and SMS alerting to review historical routes and receive important notifications.

## Core Features of WanWay - G19S

- Real time GPS location reporting suitable for live tracking and map visualization.
- ACC detection to indicate vehicle ignition state changes.
- Vibration alarm to detect unexpected motion or tampering.
- SOS emergency call to notify predefined contacts or services.
- Remote petrol or electricity cut off capability when the relay output is installed and configured.
- Microphone input support for audio monitoring when a microphone is connected.
- Trace playback for route history review within a tracking platform.
- SMS notifications and multiple alert protections for key events.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

Plaspy can receive and display the tracking and alert data the G19S reports, giving operators a centralized view of location, events, and route history. The platform surfaces device events and route data while leaving device-level configuration and firmware behavior to the tracker and its installer.

- Live location updates appear on Plaspy maps for situational awareness and fleet monitoring.
- Historical routes reported by the device are available for trace playback and review inside Plaspy.
- Event alerts such as ACC changes, vibration alarms, and SOS notifications are captured and shown in the platform for operator attention.
- SMS alerts and other text based notifications generated by the tracker can be reflected as device events depending on configuration.
- Actions like remote cut off or microphone listen in require correct wiring and device configuration; Plaspy will present related events and statuses while remote command capability depends on the device firmware and setup.
- Plaspy automatically detects compatible tracker protocols and will surface the G19S device data for monitoring once the device is configured to report to the platform.

## Typical Use Cases

- Real time tracking of individual vehicles for personal or business use.
- Fleet location monitoring and route oversight for small commercial fleets.
- Vehicle security and anti tamper monitoring using vibration and SOS alerts.
- Remote incident response informed by live location and emergency notification.
- Historical route analysis for logistics or driver behavior review.
- Situations requiring interruption of fuel or electrical supply under controlled and authorized conditions.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Feature presence and exact behavior depend on the G19S firmware version and hardware revision supplied by the manufacturer.
- Remote cut off and microphone listen in require physical wiring and may need professional installation and local legal compliance.
- Regional cellular band support and SIM provisioning affect connectivity and real time reporting performance.
- SMS alert behavior and text content may vary by firmware and by how alert recipients are configured.
- Always verify current capabilities, firmware notes, and installation instructions with the official WanWay documentation and your device supplier.
