# WanWay - GS21 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the WanWay GS21 and how its capabilities relate to use with Plaspy. It is focused on user-facing, non-sensitive details about what the GS21 offers and how those features appear in Plaspy for vehicle monitoring, route playback, alerts, and operational oversight.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and manufacturer implementation. Where appropriate this page notes practical considerations, but you should confirm device-specific behavior and firmware details with WanWay documentation for the most current information.

## Feature Overview

The WanWay GS21 is an in-vehicle OBD II positioning device designed for vehicle monitoring scenarios such as car-hailing, car rental, and general fleet or personal vehicle oversight. It combines compact in-line installation with persistent position reporting and basic tamper protection to support tracking and operational workflows.

- Real-time tracking for live location visibility while the vehicle is in use.
- Trace playback of historical routes to review previous trips and movement history.
- User-defined geo-fence support enabling virtual perimeter monitoring and alerts.
- Built-in rechargeable battery (60 mAh) to continue sending positioning data after primary power is removed.
- Anti-disassembly alarm that can provide an alert if the device is tampered with or removed.
- Malfunction diagnostics to report device or vehicle-related fault information where available.

## Core Features of WanWay - GS21

- OBD II form factor intended for in-vehicle installation that simplifies mounting and power connection.
- Real-time position reporting suitable for live monitoring in Plaspy.
- Historical trace playback to review routes and trip history.
- User-configurable geo-fence capability for perimeter alerts and automated event recording.
- Built-in rechargeable battery (60 mAh) to provide limited operation and reporting after vehicle power loss.
- Anti-disassembly alarm for additional tamper detection and security notification.
- High-sensitivity GPS receiver with quoted position accuracy up to 5 meters and quick start performance under open sky conditions.
- Malfunction diagnostics features for basic fault detection and reporting to a monitoring platform.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When used with Plaspy, the GS21's core capabilities become part of a centralized monitoring and reporting workflow that supports fleet visibility and operational oversight.

- Live GPS positions from the GS21 appear in Plaspy's device list and map view for real-time location tracking.
- Historical trace playback recorded by the GS21 can be reviewed in Plaspy to analyze past trips and routes.
- Geo-fence events generated by the device or configured via the platform result in alerts and event logs inside Plaspy.
- Tamper or anti-disassembly alarms from the GS21 are surfaced in Plaspy as events so operators can investigate potential device removal.
- Malfunction diagnostics reported by the device can be included in Plaspy event streams and device health summaries to support maintenance workflows.
- Plaspy automatically detects common tracker protocols and integrates incoming device reports so that the GS21 can be added without manual protocol selection.

## Typical Use Cases

- Car-hailing and ride services that need continuous location visibility and route history.
- Car rental operators tracking vehicle location, route playback, and tamper alerts.
- Fleet managers monitoring vehicle movement, geofence compliance, and basic diagnostics.
- Individual vehicle owners who want real-time tracking and historical trip review.
- Operations teams that require quick detection of vehicle disconnection or device removal via anti-disassembly alerts.
- Situations where continued reporting for a short period after power removal is valuable for recovery or investigation.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Some features depend on device firmware and manufacturer configuration; functionality such as diagnostics and alarm behavior may change with firmware updates.
- OBD II installation and wiring affect which vehicle signals are available; GS21 behavior can vary by vehicle OBD implementation and how the unit is connected.
- Regional variants and cellular bands may differ by market; check manufacturer specifications for the version purchased.
- Battery backup provides limited operation time and is intended for short-term reporting after power loss; available runtime will vary with reporting frequency and signal conditions.
- Always consult the official WanWay documentation for the precise feature list and deployment recommendations for your firmware and hardware revision.
