# WanWay - GS21

<div className="device-logo">
![GS21](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The WanWay GS21 is an in vehicle OBD II positioning device designed for car monitoring, online car hailing, and car rental scenarios. It is compact and easy to deploy, with a small footprint that fits most passenger vehicles. The GS21 includes a built in rechargeable battery to continue sending positioning data after main power is removed and provides an anti disassembly alarm to detect tampering.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GS21 can feed location, event, and diagnostic information into the Plaspy platform for centralized visibility and management. Its features such as real time tracking, trace playback, user defined geo fence, malfunction diagnostics, and multiple alert protections align with common fleet and vehicle monitoring workflows supported by Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- Compact OBD II form factor suited to passenger vehicles and rapid deployment
- Built in rechargeable backup battery to continue reporting after power off
- Anti disassembly alarm for added theft and tamper protection
- GPS positioning accuracy reported up to 5 meters with fast start performance
- Real time tracking and historical trace playback for route analysis
- User defined geo fence and multiple alert protections including diagnostic alerts

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected and configured, the GS21 transmits positioning and event data that Plaspy ingests and presents through its monitoring tools. Plaspy aggregates the device data so fleets and administrators can maintain situational awareness and generate operational reports.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for real time vehicle visibility
- Historical route playback for incident review and trip auditing
- Custom geo fence creation with alerting for arrivals, departures, and boundary events
- Recording of diagnostic events and status changes to support maintenance workflows
- Tamper and power loss alerts forwarded to Plaspy for prompt notification
- Fleet level reporting and overview to support operational decisions

## Typical Use Cases

- Car rental monitoring for vehicle location, usage tracking, and return verification
- Ride hailing fleet management to maintain live visibility and route history
- General fleet vehicle oversight for small to medium sized fleets
- Security and anti theft monitoring using tamper alerts and backup battery reporting
- Troubleshooting and maintenance planning guided by diagnostic event logging

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GS21 is a practical choice for organizations that need a compact, vehicle focused tracker with backup reporting and tamper protection. Its OBD II oriented form factor and onboard battery make it suitable for car centric deployments where continuous monitoring and quick installation are priorities. Paired with Plaspy, the GS21 provides a reliable source of position and event data that Plaspy can visualize and act upon.

Plaspy users gain the advantage of centralized oversight, configurable alerts, and historical reporting while relying on the GS21 to supply the core positional and event information. For teams that manage rental fleets, ride hailing vehicles, or mixed car fleets, this combination delivers straightforward tracking and operational insights without excessive complexity.
