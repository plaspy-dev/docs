# WanWay - S20

![S20](./tracker.webp)

The S20 Asset GPS Tracker is a self-contained, battery-powered GPS tracker built for flexible, Plaspy compatible deployment across vehicles, trailers, containers and portable assets. Designed for transport and logistics, refrigerated cargo, construction sites and other high-value goods, the S20 delivers reliable real-time tracking and long-term monitoring without permanent wiring, making it ideal for moving trackers between assets as operational needs change.

The S20 combines extended battery life, a light sensor disassemble alarm and a strong magnetic mounting option to provide persistent visibility into location and status. When integrated with Plaspy, the S20 becomes part of a single fleet management view that supports rapid incident response, anti-theft monitoring and consolidated telemetry for operational efficiency.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time tracking and historical playback across assets.
- Large battery capacity for extended standby and long-term remote monitoring without frequent recharging.
- Light sensor and disassemble alarm provide tamper and unauthorized removal alerts for anti-theft protection.
- Strong magnetic mounting enables quick installation and relocation between vehicles, containers or equipment.
- Multiple alert protections to notify you of motion, tampering and other configurable events.
- Compact, self-contained form factor designed for asset mobility and covert installation.
- Suited for logistics operations such as refrigerated cargo, container management and construction asset tracking.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The S20 transmits location and status updates that Plaspy ingests to provide dashboard visibility, geofencing alerts, route playback and reporting. Because the S20 is designed for long-term battery operation, Plaspy can display both high-frequency real-time tracking when needed and periodic position reports for long-term asset monitoring to conserve power.

- Real-time location and status updates streamed to Plaspy for live tracking and mapping.
- Disassemble/tamper alerts generated by the light sensor feed into Plaspy alarm rules and notifications.
- Battery level and power state telemetry enable fuel for operational decisions around redeployment and maintenance.
- Plaspy can correlate S20 location and tamper telemetry with other fleet inputs \(fuel monitoring, ignition and immobilizer status, Bluetooth sensors\) when those data sources are present, for a unified fleet management view.
- Historical tracking and long-term monitoring reports help optimize asset utilization and investigate incidents.

- Container and trailer tracking — monitor location, tamper events and battery status for shipping containers and detached trailers during multi-leg transport.
- Refrigerated cargo logistics — deploy S20 units on refrigerated trailers or pallets to maintain location visibility across cold-chain shipments.
- Construction equipment and site assets — keep track of valuable machinery, tools and portable equipment that move between sites.
- Temporary or pooled fleet vehicles — quickly redeploy the S20 between rented vehicles, subcontractor vans or pooled assets without hardwiring.
- High-value asset protection — use magnetic mounting and tamper alarms to detect unauthorized access and support rapid anti-theft response.
