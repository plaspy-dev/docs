# WanWay - S20 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the WanWay S20 and how its capabilities are used with Plaspy. It is intended to describe practical, non-sensitive feature context for operations teams, integrators and fleet managers who want to understand what the S20 can provide when paired with the Plaspy fleet management platform.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional model or installation type. The content below is grounded in the S20 product description and its stated compatibility with Plaspy; for device-specific technical details and the latest specifications consult the manufacturer documentation.

## Feature Overview

The S20 is a self-contained, battery-powered asset GPS tracker built for flexible deployment across vehicles, trailers, containers and portable assets. It is designed to provide both reliable real-time tracking when needed and long-term periodic reporting to conserve battery life, with physical mounting and tamper detection features to support anti-theft monitoring.

- Self-contained battery powered design for installation without permanent wiring
- Real-time location updates and historical position playback for asset visibility
- Light sensor driven disassemble and tamper alarm for anti-theft awareness
- Strong magnetic mounting for quick relocation between assets or covert placement
- Motion and other configurable alert protections to support incident response

## Core Features of WanWay - S20

- Battery powered operation with extended standby capability for long-term monitoring
- Self-contained compact form factor suitable for portable asset deployment
- GPS location reporting for live tracking and historical route playback
- Light sensor disassemble alarm to detect unauthorized removal or tampering
- Strong magnetic mounting option for easy attachment to vehicles, containers and equipment
- Motion detection and configurable alert protections for event-driven reporting
- Designed for logistics and asset mobility use cases such as trailers, containers and equipment

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the S20 is integrated with Plaspy, its location and status updates are received by the platform and presented alongside other fleet data to support monitoring and response workflows. Plaspy automatically recognizes supported tracker protocols and surfaces device events, enabling centralized oversight without manual protocol handling.

- Live location and mapping in Plaspy for situational awareness and dispatching
- Tamper and disassemble alerts from the S20 feed Plaspy alarm rules and notification channels
- Historical tracking and playback for incident investigation and route analysis
- Battery and power state visibility in Plaspy to inform redeployment and maintenance planning
- Correlation of S20 telemetry with other fleet inputs in Plaspy when those data sources are available

## Typical Use Cases

- Container and trailer tracking during multi-leg shipping to monitor location and tamper events
- Refrigerated cargo and cold chain logistics where portable trackers are moved between trailers or pallets
- Construction site asset tracking for portable machinery, tools and equipment that change location frequently
- Temporary or pooled fleet vehicles that require rapid redeployment without hardwiring
- High-value asset protection and anti-theft monitoring using magnetic mounting and tamper alerts
- Long-term asset monitoring where periodic reporting preserves battery life while keeping visibility

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and hardware revisions can affect feature behavior, reporting intervals and alarm logic
- Regional product variants may differ in cellular bands and connectivity details not covered here
- Bluetooth or other accessory integrations are not specified for the S20 in this description

## Why Use Plaspy with These Features

Using the WanWay S20 with Plaspy gives organizations a way to combine portable, long-life asset tracking hardware with a centralized fleet management platform. The S20's tamper detection and magnetic mounting make it practical for anti-theft monitoring and flexible redeployment, while Plaspy captures location and event data for live visibility, alerts and historical reporting.
