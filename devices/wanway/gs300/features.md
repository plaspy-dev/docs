# WanWay - GS300 Features

This page summarizes the public feature context for using the WanWay GS300 tracker with Plaspy. It covers the practical capabilities you can expect to see in Plaspy when the device is reporting, and explains how those capabilities support real time tracking, telemetry, and alerting workflows in the platform.

Exact feature availability can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, installation method, and how the manufacturer implements individual functions. Before deployment, confirm device specifics with manufacturer documentation and test a sample device to validate the behaviors you rely on in production.

## Feature Overview

The GS300 is a durable, long life GPS tracker designed for continuous location reporting and event detection for assets and vehicles. It combines cellular connectivity and multi constellation GNSS positioning with a high capacity battery and tamper aware construction to support long deployments where reliable telemetry is required.

- Real time location updates using 4G full Netcom connectivity for responsive tracking and map visualization
- GPS and BDS satellite navigation to improve position availability in varied environments
- Large 7800mAh battery and low power consumption for extended operation without frequent maintenance
- On device alarms including ACC ignition detection, overspeed alarm, and disassemble alarm for anti theft and safety monitoring
- Mileage statistics and trace playback for route review, reporting, and maintenance planning

## Core Features of WanWay - GS300

- 4G full Netcom cellular connectivity for telemetry and location reporting
- Dual constellation GNSS using GPS and BDS for positioning
- High capacity 7800mAh battery and low power design for long runtime
- ACC ignition detection to report power on and off states relevant to vehicle use
- Overspeed alarm to notify of potential unsafe driving events
- Disassemble alarm and built in light sensor to detect tamper or unauthorized access
- Mileage statistics and historical trace playback for route analysis and reporting

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When the GS300 is connected and streaming to Plaspy, its location and event stream become visible in the platform so teams can monitor assets, respond to alerts, and analyze historical movement. Plaspy automatically detects compatible device protocols and surfaces supported telemetry without heavy manual configuration.

- Live position updates and map tracking for immediate location awareness and operational oversight
- Event reporting such as ACC status changes and tamper alarms displayed in Plaspy event logs and alert feeds
- Overspeed events and other on device alarms routed to Plaspy notifications and incident workflows
- Mileage and trace playback available in reports and history views for maintenance planning and route review
- Device health and runtime benefits from long battery life reflected in reduced service intervals and longer unattended monitoring windows

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet vehicle tracking for dispatch, routing, and route verification
- High value asset protection where tamper alerts and disassemble detection are needed
- Long term deployments on trailers or equipment that require extended battery life
- Driver behavior monitoring using overspeed alerts and ignition reporting
- Route compliance and trace playback for audits and proof of service
- Remote assets where cellular connectivity and reliable GNSS positioning are required

## Feature Availability Notes

- Firmware and software revisions can enable or change specific alarms and reporting intervals; verify the firmware build on devices before relying on a given behavior
- Hardware revisions and regional cellular band variants may affect connectivity performance or supported bands; consult the device datasheet for regional availability
- Installation type and wiring for ACC ignition detection may change how ACC events are reported compared to factory examples
- Some features may require specific device configuration or permissions enabled by the installer or manufacturer
- Always confirm the exact feature set and limits with WanWay documentation and by testing the device in your intended deployment environment
