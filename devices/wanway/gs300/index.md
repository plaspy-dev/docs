# GS300 Wireless Asset GPS Tracker — Plaspy Compatible

The GS300 is a durable, long‑life GPS tracker designed for reliable asset tracking and fleet management. Built for real‑time positioning with 4G full Netcom connectivity and GPS/BDS satellite navigation, the GS300 delivers continuous location updates, trace playback, and on‑device alarms to protect assets in motion or at rest. Its large 7800mAh battery, waterproof housing, and low power consumption make it ideal for deployments that require extended uptime and minimal maintenance.

As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the GS300 integrates with Plaspy to provide real‑time tracking, telemetry, and alerting across fleets and high‑value assets. Plaspy users can ingest the GS300’s location stream, ACC/ignition status, disassemble alarms, overspeed alerts, and mileage statistics for monitoring, reporting, and operational workflows that improve security and efficiency.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for seamless integration into your real‑time tracking dashboard and reporting.
- 4G full Netcom wireless communication for reliable telemetry and faster location updates across cellular networks.
- GPS + BDS satellite navigation to improve positioning accuracy in diverse operating environments.
- Long‑running 7800mAh battery with low power consumption for extended deployments and reduced maintenance visits.
- Built‑in ACC \(ignition\) detection, overspeed alarm, and disassemble alarm for anti‑theft monitoring and driver safety alerts.

- Waterproof construction and built‑in light sensor for durable outdoor use and tamper detection.

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GS300 streams location and event data in real time so you can monitor assets, receive alerts, and analyze historical movement. Plaspy consumes the device’s telemetry and converts it into actionable insights — live maps, geofence alerts, trace playback, and fleet reports — without extra configuration on your end.

- Real‑time location and telemetry updates: continuous position data via 4G for live monitoring and map visualization.
- ACC/ignition status: ACC detection reports ignition on/off events to Plaspy for usage and driver behavior analysis.
- Alarm and tamper notifications: disassemble alarm and overspeed alarm generate immediate alerts through Plaspy’s alerting engine.
- Mileage statistics and trace playback: distance tracking and historical route review feed into Plaspy’s reporting and maintenance workflows.
- Low power and long battery life: extends uptime between service intervals, enabling long‑term telemetry for assets where permanent power is unavailable.
