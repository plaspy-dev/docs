# WanWay - GS300

![GS300](./tracker.webp)

The WanWay GS300 is a rugged GPS tracker built for reliable asset tracking and fleet management. Designed for continuous positioning, the GS300 combines GPS and BDS satellite navigation with 4G full Netcom wireless connectivity to deliver frequent location updates, trace playback, and on device alarms. Its waterproof housing, light sensor, and large 7800mAh battery support extended deployments where uninterrupted monitoring and reduced maintenance are priorities.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the GS300 can feed real time location and event data into the Plaspy platform for consolidated monitoring, alerting, and reporting. Plaspy ingests the GS300 telemetry including ignition state, disassemble alarm, overspeed alerts, and mileage statistics so teams can use those signals in map views, alerts, historical playback, and operational reports.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for straightforward integration into live maps and reporting dashboards
- 4G full Netcom connectivity for reliable telemetry and faster position updates
- GPS and BDS satellite navigation to support positioning in varied environments
- Large 7800mAh battery and low power consumption for extended unattended operation
- Built in ACC ignition detection, overspeed alarm, and disassemble alarm for security and safety monitoring
- Mileage statistics and trace playback to help with route analysis and maintenance planning
- Waterproof enclosure and light sensor for durable outdoor deployments and basic tamper indication

## How It Works with Plaspy

When the GS300 is connected to Plaspy it streams location and event data that Plaspy translates into visual maps, alerts, and reports. This allows operations teams to consolidate fleet and asset telemetry alongside other devices on the Plaspy platform, improving situational awareness and operational workflows.

- Live location and telemetry display on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Event driven alerts such as disassemble alarm and overspeed notifications routed through Plaspy alerting
- ACC ignition status integrated into usage reports and driver behavior analysis
- Mileage statistics and trace playback available for maintenance scheduling and route review
- Historical data retention in Plaspy for audit and performance analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management for vehicles requiring regular location visibility and route playback
- High value asset protection where disassemble alarm and tamper indication are needed
- Long term deployments for trailers or equipment that need extended battery life between service visits
- Driver safety and compliance programs that use overspeed alerts and ignition event logs
- Route verification and proof of service through historical trace playback

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GS300 pairs durable hardware characteristics with telemetry that is useful for fleet and asset monitoring. Its long battery life, waterproof design, and built in alarm features make it a practical option for teams that need reliable, low maintenance tracking on a range of mobile and stationary assets. Because the device is Plaspy compatible, onboarding it into existing Plaspy monitoring and reporting workflows is straightforward, helping reduce time to value.
