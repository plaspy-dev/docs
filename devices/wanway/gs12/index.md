# WanWay - GS12

<div className="device-logo">
![GS12](./tracker.webp)
</div>

The WanWay GS12 is an intelligent vehicle GPS tracker designed for reliable, real time tracking and vehicle security. Built around 4G full netcom wireless communication and GPS BDS satellite navigation, the GS12 provides position reporting and vehicle event telemetry such as ACC detection, disassembly alarm, overspeed alerts, mileage statistics, and remote petrol or electricity cut off. Its feature set targets vehicle focused deployments that need consistent location data and practical anti theft controls.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GS12 integrates with Plaspy to deliver location streams, event driven alerts, and operational reports for fleet and asset managers. When connected to Plaspy, the tracker’s position updates and status events become part of centralized monitoring, helping teams handle dispatch, theft response, and reporting workflows without added complexity.

## Key Highlights

- 4G full netcom connectivity for consistent data transmission across broad cellular coverage areas
- GPS and BDS satellite navigation for reliable position fixes across regions
- Built in security features including disassembly alarm and remote petrol or electricity cut off for anti theft interventions
- Vehicle telemetry like ACC detection, overspeed alerts, and mileage statistics to support reporting and operational oversight
- Designed for fleets, rental car operations, insurers, taxis, and individual vehicle owners
- Plaspy compatible to enable real time tracking, alerting, and centralized device management

## How It Works with Plaspy

Once provisioned on Plaspy, the GS12 sends GNSS location and vehicle event telemetry over cellular links so Plaspy can display live positions and surface events to operations staff. Plaspy ingests the device data to populate maps, trigger rule based notifications, and generate reports that support fleet workflows and security responses.

- Real time location and telemetry updates for map tracking and operational visibility
- Event alerts for ignition status, disassembly alarms, and overspeed conditions to prompt notifications and response actions
- Mileage statistics support reporting and fuel monitoring workflows inside Plaspy
- Remote immobilizer control via petrol or electricity cut off commands available from the platform for theft mitigation
- Centralized device management and reporting to simplify fleet scale monitoring and historical analysis

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management and route oversight for commercial vehicle operators
- Insurance telematics for event logging and location history to support risk assessment
- Anti theft and vehicle recovery workflows using disassembly alarms and remote cut off controls
- Rental and taxi operations that need usage monitoring and remote intervention options
- Support for electric and new energy vehicle monitoring where mileage and remote control functions are needed

Pairing the GS12 with Plaspy offers a practical solution for organizations that need dependable vehicle tracking and event driven controls without excessive complexity. The GS12 delivers the essential position and telemetry streams that Plaspy uses to create alerts, dashboards, and reports, while remote cut off and disassembly alarm features provide direct tools for theft response and immobilization workflows.
