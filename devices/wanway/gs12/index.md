# WanWay - GS12

![GS12](./tracker.webp)

GS12

The GS12 is an intelligent vehicle GPS tracker designed for Plaspy-compatible deployments that demand reliable, real-time tracking and robust anti-theft controls. Built around 4G full-netcom wireless communication and GPS/BDS satellite navigation, the GS12 provides fleet management and telemetry capabilities such as ACC detection, disassembly alarm, overspeed alerts, mileage statistics, and remote cut-off of petrol/electricity. Its feature set makes it a practical choice for insurers, fleet operators, rental car companies, and individual vehicle owners who need dependable location data and remote control functions.

As a Plaspy compatible GPS tracker, the GS12 integrates with Plaspy’s platform to deliver real-time tracking, event-driven alerts, and actionable reports for operational teams. Whether you are optimizing routes, reducing theft risk, or monitoring vehicle health indicators through mileage and telemetry, the GS12 supplies the essential data streams that Plaspy ingests and displays for scalable fleet management and monitoring workflows.

## Key Highlights

- 4G full-netcom connectivity for reliable data transmission and broad cellular coverage.
- GPS and BDS satellite navigation for consistent and accurate position fixes across regions.
- Built-in security features: disassembly alarm and remote petrol/electricity cut-off for anti-theft and immobilizer control.
- Vehicle telemetry: ACC detection, overspeed alarm, and mileage statistics to support fleet reporting and fuel monitoring workflows.
- Designed for enterprise fleet management, insurance telematics, rental vehicles, taxis, and new energy/electric vehicle use cases.
- Plaspy compatible — integrates with Plaspy for real-time tracking, alerting, and centralized device management.
- Compact vehicle-focused form factor for discreet installation in cars, taxis, and commercial vehicles.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GS12 sends GNSS location and vehicle event telemetry over 4G to Plaspy’s platform, enabling real-time tracking and actionable alerts. Plaspy processes position updates and status messages from the device to populate maps, trigger rule-based notifications, and produce fleet reports. Integration is designed to be straightforward: once the GS12 is provisioned on Plaspy, location streams and event channels become available to operations staff for monitoring, dispatch, or automated workflows.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates: frequent GPS/BDS fixes transmitted via 4G for map tracking and geofencing.
- Ignition and alarm status: ACC detection and disassembly alarm events are reported to Plaspy for instant alerting.
- Fuel monitoring workflows: mileage statistics support fuel consumption analysis and reporting in Plaspy \(fuel sensor integration can be handled through platform workflows\).
- Remote immobilizer/control: remotely cut off petrol/electricity command is supported for anti-theft intervention from Plaspy.
- External sensors and BLE: while GS12 focuses on GNSS and cellular telemetry, Plaspy’s platform can accept data from Bluetooth sensors or other integrations to extend temperature, load, or movement monitoring across the fleet.
