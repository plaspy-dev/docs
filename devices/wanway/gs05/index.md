# WanWay - GS05

![GS05](./tracker.webp)

The GS05 from GS \(model GS05\) is a compact, Plaspy compatible 3G GPS tracker designed for straightforward vehicle tracking and fleet management. Combining 3G GSM connectivity, GPS positioning, a built-in antenna, and multiple onboard sensors, the GS05 provides reliable real-time tracking for individual cars or entire fleets. Its small footprint and 11PIN connector make installation flexible, while a built-in microphone option enables remote listening when privacy and legal allowances permit.

Optimized for integration with Plaspy, the GS05 enables vehicle owners and fleet managers to view location, telemetry, and event data through smartphone apps and web dashboards. Whether you need anti-theft awareness, live location feeds, or basic telemetry for routing and operations, the GS05 is positioned as a practical device for everyday vehicle tracking needs.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for seamless real-time tracking and centralized fleet management.
- 3G GSM connectivity with GPS positioning and built-in antenna for consistent signal reception.
- Compact design with 11PIN connector for flexible vehicle installations and sensor connections.
- Integrated microphone support — listen-in capability when a microphone is connected for situational awareness.
- Multiple onboard sensors providing telemetry that integrates with Plaspy dashboards and alerts.
- Internal 180mAh backup battery to maintain basic reporting during temporary power loss.
- Suitable for both single-vehicle monitoring and scaled fleet deployments requiring reliable location services.

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GS05 sends GPS location and sensor telemetry over the 3G GSM network to Plaspy’s platform for real-time visualization, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy ingests standard position updates and sensor events, consolidating them into live maps, route playback, and telemetry reports accessible on smartphones and desktops.

- Real-time location and telemetry updates delivered to Plaspy for live tracking and route history.
- Microphone input support — audio monitoring available when a microphone is connected and permitted by law.
- Sensor and event reporting for movement, ignition status \(where available from vehicle wiring\), and other telemetry aggregated in Plaspy.
- Plaspy dashboards and alerts can be configured for anti-theft notifications, geofence breaches, and activity summaries.
- Integrates with Plaspy’s fleet management tools to combine GPS tracking with operational reports and driver routing.
