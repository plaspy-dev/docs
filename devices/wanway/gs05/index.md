# WanWay - GS05

![GS05](./tracker.webp)

The GS05 from WanWay is a compact 3G GPS tracker designed for straightforward vehicle tracking and fleet management. It combines GPS positioning, 3G GSM connectivity, a built in antenna, multiple onboard sensors, and an 11PIN connector in a small footprint. An optional built in microphone is available for remote listening when local laws and privacy considerations allow.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GS05 is positioned for easy integration into Plaspy dashboards and mobile views. It enables vehicle owners and fleet managers to receive location, telemetry, and event data through Plaspy for real time visibility, basic telemetry reporting, and common fleet workflows such as anti theft awareness and route review.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible for centralized real time tracking and fleet oversight
- 3G GSM connectivity with GPS positioning and built in antenna for reliable reception
- Compact form factor with 11PIN connector for flexible vehicle installation
- Optional microphone support for remote audio monitoring where permitted
- Multiple onboard sensors to provide telemetry that integrates with Plaspy
- Internal 180mAh backup battery to maintain basic reporting during temporary power loss
- Suitable for single vehicle monitoring and scaled fleet deployments

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GS05 transmits location and sensor telemetry over the cellular network so Plaspy can ingest position updates and events for visualization, alerts, and historical reporting. Plaspy consolidates incoming data into live maps, route playback, and configurable reports to support operational oversight.

- Real time location and telemetry updates appear in Plaspy for live tracking and route history
- Microphone input can be used for situational audio monitoring when a microphone is connected and legal permissions are in place
- Sensor and event reporting such as movement and ignition status where available from wiring are aggregated into Plaspy feeds
- Configurable Plaspy dashboards and alerts support anti theft notifications geofence events and activity summaries
- Integration with Plaspy fleet tools allows combining GPS tracking with operational reports and routing oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet management with live location feeds and consolidated telemetry for dispatching
- Anti theft monitoring with alerts and rapid location awareness for recovery
- Driver oversight and trip logging with route playback for incident review
- Situational awareness using optional microphone input for security or investigation when allowed
- Asset monitoring for light vehicles or equipment where a compact tracker is preferred

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The GS05 offers a practical mix of connectivity, basic telemetry, and flexible installation features that fit common vehicle tracking needs. Its compact design, built in antenna, and 11PIN connector make it adaptable to many installations while the backup battery helps maintain reporting during brief power interruptions. Optional microphone support adds another layer of situational capability when used in line with applicable laws.

If you want a straightforward, Plaspy compatible device for real time vehicle and fleet tracking, the GS05 is a sensible choice for organizations seeking reliable location services without unnecessary complexity.
