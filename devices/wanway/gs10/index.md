# WanWay - GS10

![GS10](./Logo.jpg)

The WanWay GS10 is an intelligent vehicle GPS tracker that combines 4G full netcom wireless communication with GPS and BDS satellite navigation. Designed as a compact and lightweight unit, the GS10 is described as suitable for a wide range of vehicle types and industries including insurance, enterprise fleets, auto manufacturers and 4S stores, individual owners, electric vehicle fleets, passenger vehicles, taxis, and rental fleets. It supports real time tracking and vehicle monitoring features commonly required for security and operational oversight.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GS10 integrates its location and status capabilities into Plaspy's fleet and tracking platform to provide centralized visibility and management. Key functions such as real time position reporting, ACC detection events, and remote cut off for petrol or electricity translate into actionable items within Plaspy for monitoring, alerts, and operational workflows. The GS10's compact footprint and wide input voltage range make it a practical option for mixed vehicle deployments managed through Plaspy.

## Key Highlights

- 4G full netcom wireless communication for reliable data connectivity
- GPS and BDS satellite navigation for position information
- Real time tracking capability for up to date location visibility
- Remote cut off function to immobilize petrol or electricity supply when needed
- ACC detection to log ignition on and off events
- Compact and lightweight form factor suitable for discreet deployment
- Wide voltage input range and a built in backup battery for continued operation

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the GS10 streams its location and event data to the platform so fleet managers and operators can monitor vehicles from a single interface. Plaspy uses those device signals to present maps, alerts, and historical records useful for day to day operations and incident response.

- Live location display on Plaspy maps for real time fleet visibility
- Event logging such as ACC on and off to track vehicle usage patterns
- Alerts and notifications in Plaspy when predefined rules are triggered
- Remote immobilization control made available through Plaspy when supported by the device
- Historical route playback and reporting for operational review and audits

## Typical Use Cases

- Enterprise fleet tracking and operational oversight
- Rental vehicle security and asset control
- Taxi and passenger vehicle monitoring for dispatch and safety
- Electric vehicle fleet management where compact tracking devices are preferred
