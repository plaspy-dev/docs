# WanWay - GS10

![GS10](./Logo.jpg)

The WanWay GS10 is an intelligent vehicle GPS tracker that combines 4G full netcom wireless communication technology with GPS/BDS satellite navigation. With its compact design and multiple functions, this tracker is perfect for various applications in the insurance industry, enterprise fleet industry, auto manufacturers or 4S stores, individuals, electric new energy fields, passenger vehicles, taxis, rental vehicles, and more.

The GS10 offers a range of features to enhance vehicle security and monitoring. It allows for real-time tracking, providing accurate and up-to-date location information. Additionally, it has the capability to remotely cut-off petrol or electricity, ensuring that the vehicle can be immobilized if necessary. The tracker also includes an ACC detection feature, which can detect when the vehicle's ignition is turned on or off. This can be useful for monitoring driver behavior or unauthorized use of the vehicle.

With its compact dimensions of 80mm\(L\) x 39mm\(W\) x 17mm\(H\) and lightweight design of 55g, the GS10 can be easily installed in a vehicle without taking up much space. It operates on a wide voltage input range of 9-90v, making it compatible with a variety of vehicles. The device also features a built-in backup battery of 140mAh, ensuring that it can continue to function even if the main power source is disconnected.

Overall, the WanWay GS10 is a reliable and versatile GPS tracker that offers a range of features to enhance vehicle security and monitoring. Whether you are an individual looking to track your personal vehicle or a business in need of fleet management solutions, the GS10 is a great choice.

### Key Features:

- 4G full netcom wireless communication technology
- GPS/BDS satellite navigation
- Real-time tracking
- Remotely cut-off petrol/electricity
- ACC detection
- Compact dimensions and lightweight design
- Wide voltage input range
- Built-in backup battery
