# WanWay - GS22

![GS22](./Logo.jpg)

The WanWay GS22 is a 4G vehicle OBD II positioning device that offers a range of features including electronic fences, remote tracking, and vehicle diagnostics. This compact device measures 60x46x22mm, making it easy to install and fix in your vehicle. It is designed to meet the needs of various scenarios such as car monitoring, online car rental, and car rental.

The GS22 is equipped with a built-in 140mAh rechargeable battery, ensuring that it can continue to send positioning data to the platform terminal even after power is disconnected. Additionally, the device features a disassembly alarm, providing an extra layer of security. With its small size and versatile functionality, the GS22 is an ideal choice for those looking for a reliable and efficient GPS tracker for their vehicles.

Key Features:

- 4G vehicle tracking
- Compact size \(60x46x22mm\)
- Built-in 140mAh rechargeable battery
- Disassembly alarm
- Real-time tracking
- Malfunction diagnostics
- Trace playback
- User-defined geo-fence
- Wifi hotspot supported
