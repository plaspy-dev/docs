# WanWay - G18 Features

This page provides a public feature overview of the WanWay G18 GPS tracker and how its capabilities map into Plaspy for fleet tracking, safety monitoring, and anti theft workflows. It summarizes user-facing functions and practical uses when the G18 is connected to Plaspy, and explains what operators can expect at a high level.

Exact feature availability and behavior can vary by firmware version, hardware revision, regional variant, and the details of installation and wiring. For device specific configuration, firmware behaviour, or the latest technical details please consult the manufacturer documentation and release notes.

## Feature Overview

The WanWay G18 is a slim vehicle tracker designed for compact installation while delivering core telematics useful to fleet and usage based insurance programs. It focuses on real-time location, event alerts, and anti theft protections that feed into Plaspy for monitoring and operational response.

- Real time GPS positioning with a built in antenna to provide continuous location updates for mapping and playback.
- Driver behavior alerts including rapid acceleration, harsh braking, sharp turns, and collision reports for safety programs and UBI scoring.
- Tamper and vibration alarms to detect disassembly attempts or impacts and notify operators promptly.
- Remote petrol or electricity cutoff support to assist anti theft procedures where wiring and policy permit.
- Compact form factor with light sensor and onboard backup battery to support discreet installation and basic power loss reporting.

## Core Features of WanWay - G18

- GPS positioning with built in antenna for vehicle location tracking.
- GSM 2G cellular connectivity for broad deployment in areas with 2G coverage.
- Driver behavior detection and event reporting for rapid acceleration, harsh braking, sharp turns, and collisions.
- Tamper or disassemble alarm and vibration alarm for unauthorized access and impact detection.
- Remote petrol or electricity cutoff (remote immobilizer) capability to support theft mitigation.
- Slim hardware design with a 4 pin connector for vehicle installations.
- Onboard backup battery (55 mAh) to provide limited reporting during power loss.

## How These Features Work with Plaspy

When a G18 is integrated into Plaspy, its location fixes and event packets become part of a unified fleet view. Plaspy ingests the device telemetry, normalizes events, and surfaces alerts and history for fleet operators and safety teams.

- Real time location updates appear on Plaspy maps for live tracking and route playback.
- Driver behavior events and collision reports are logged and available in dashboards and reports for coaching or UBI workflows.
- Tamper and vibration alarms generate immediate notifications to help rapid response.
- Remote immobilizer commands can be managed through Plaspy workflows where device wiring and operational policy allow such actions.
- Power loss and backup battery events are reflected so operators can see device health and recent reporting.

## Typical Use Cases

- Anti theft response and recovery workflows for fleet vehicles and high value assets.
- Usage Based Insurance and driver safety programs that require event logging for harsh driving and collisions.
- Discreet vehicle tracking for rental fleets, valet services, or shared vehicle programs.
- Small and medium fleet operational visibility for routing, utilization, and incident follow up.
- Rapid incident handling when collision or vibration alarms indicate an impact.

## Feature Availability Notes

- Functionality depends on the G18 firmware version and manufacturer implementation; features may be added, changed, or limited in different firmware releases.
- Hardware revisions and regional variants can affect supported bands, connectors, and installed sensors.
- Installation wiring and vehicle integration determine whether remote immobilizer and some event detections are available and safe to use.
- 2G cellular coverage availability varies by country and may affect connectivity and reporting frequency.
- Always verify the exact feature set and installation requirements with WanWay documentation for your specific unit.
