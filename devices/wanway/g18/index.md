# WanWay - G18

<div className="device-logo">
![G18](./tracker.webp)
</div>

The WanWay G18 is a slim, Plaspy compatible GPS tracker designed for vehicle and fleet management teams that need compact hardware with practical telematics features. It combines GPS positioning, GSM 2G connectivity, a built-in antenna and multiple onboard sensors to deliver real-time tracking, driver-behavior alerts, tamper detection and collision reporting in a low-profile enclosure. The G18 targets everyday fleet operations and Usage Based Insurance (UBI) programs where discreet installation and essential event reporting are priorities.

As a device compatible with Plaspy, the G18 feeds location fixes and event packets into the Plaspy fleet platform for mapping, alerts and reporting. When integrated with Plaspy, the tracker’s tamper and vibration alarms, collision reports and remote immobilizer functions are available as events and controls inside the Plaspy workflow, helping teams improve routing, compliance and operational visibility without adding unnecessary complexity.

## Key Highlights

- Plaspy compatible GPS tracker for real-time fleet monitoring and dashboard visibility.
- Driver behavior monitoring with alerts for rapid acceleration, harsh braking, sharp turns and collision reports useful for safety programs and UBI.
- Tamper and vibration alarms to detect unauthorized access or significant impacts.
- Remote petrol or electricity cutoff capability (remote immobilizer) to support anti-theft workflows.
- Slim, compact form factor with built-in antenna and light sensor for discreet vehicle installation.
- Cost effective 2G GSM connectivity and a small onboard backup battery for power loss reporting.

## How It Works with Plaspy

Integrating the G18 with Plaspy brings the device’s position updates and event alerts into a single fleet management environment where data is normalized, displayed on maps, and routed into rules, notifications and reports.

- Real-time location feeds and historical playback for route review and operational analysis.
- Event forwarding for tamper, vibration and collision alerts so operators receive immediate notifications.
- Driver behavior events logged and available for scoring, coaching and UBI workflows.
- Remote immobilizer commands initiated from Plaspy where operational policy and the vehicle wiring permit.
- Consolidated reporting and rule-based alerts to support compliance, maintenance planning and incident follow up.

## Typical Use Cases

- Fleet anti-theft and asset protection with tamper detection and remote immobilization.
- Driver safety programs and UBI deployments that rely on behavior events and collision reporting.
- Incident response workflows where vibration or collision alerts accelerate follow up.
- Discreet tracking for rental fleets, valet services and high value vehicles.
- Small and medium fleet operational oversight including routing, utilization and basic telemetry.

## Why Choose This Tracker with Plaspy

The G18 is a pragmatic option for organizations that want a compact, Plaspy compatible tracker focused on core telematics and anti-theft features. Its design prioritizes discreet installation and the essential event reporting vehicle fleets commonly need, while keeping the device footprint small and unobtrusive. For fleets operating where 2G coverage is available, the G18 offers consistent location and event delivery that plugs into Plaspy’s dashboards and alerting rules.

If your operation values straightforward telemetry, tamper and collision alerts, and the ability to combine device events with Plaspy workflows, the G18 is a sensible fit to evaluate.
