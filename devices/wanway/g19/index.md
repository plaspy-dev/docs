# WanWay - G19

<div className="device-logo">
![G19](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The WanWay G19 is a small, compact GPS tracker that combines GSM and GPS technology into a lightweight enclosure. It includes a built in antenna and sensors and delivers real time tracking, trace playback, SMS notification and multiple alert protections such as over speed alarm. The device is rated IP67 for waterproof protection, measures 74mm L x 35mm W x 13mm H and weighs about 29g, making it easy to conceal and deploy on a wide range of vehicles and assets. The G19 operates on quad bands and reports location with a stated GPS accuracy of around 5 meters while accepting a wide input voltage range.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the G19 provides the core functions most fleet and asset managers expect to see in a connected tracker. Its compact form factor and waterproof design make it suitable for mixed fleet environments and outdoor use, while standard tracking features such as live location, trace playback and alerting map well into Plaspy's monitoring and reporting capabilities. Plaspy can ingest the G19 data to provide centralized visibility, operational oversight and historical playback for monitoring and analysis.

## Key Highlights

- Compact and lightweight profile at roughly 74 x 35 x 13 mm and 29 g for easy concealment
- IP67 waterproof rating suitable for wet conditions and vehicle wading scenarios
- Real time tracking and trace playback for historical route review
- Built in antenna and sensors with MTK GPS chip and GPS accuracy around 5 m
- Multiple alert protections including over speed alarm and SMS notification
- Wide operating voltage input that supports typical vehicle installations

## How It Works with Plaspy

When connected to Plaspy, the G19 delivers location and alert information that Plaspy displays, stores and uses for operational workflows. Plaspy centralizes device feeds so teams can monitor assets from a single interface and combine G19 events with other data across the fleet.

- Live location plotting of G19 units on Plaspy maps for real time visibility
- Historical trace playback in Plaspy to review routes and timelines
- Alert forwarding and notification handling so over speed and other alarms surface in Plaspy dashboards
- Fleet level oversight and grouping to manage multiple G19 devices together
- Reporting and export options in Plaspy that incorporate G19 position and event history
