# WanWay - G19

![G19](./Logo.jpg)

The WanWay G19 GPS tracker is a small and compact device that integrates GSM and GPS technology. With its built-in antenna and sensors, this tracker offers real-time tracking and various functions such as over-speed alarm. One of the standout features of the G19 is its waterproof performance, reaching an IP67 level. This means that it can withstand vehicle wading situations or rainy days without any issues.

The G19 GPS tracker has a dimension of 74mm\(L\) x 35mm\(W\) x 13mm\(H\) and weighs only 29g, making it easy to install and hide. It operates on quad bands \(850/1900/900/1800MHz\) and has a GPS accuracy of \<=5m. The device can be powered by a voltage input ranging from 9-90v. With its MTK GPS chip and built-in GSM/GPS antenna, the G19 provides reliable and accurate tracking.

Some of the key functions of the WanWay G19 GPS tracker include real-time tracking, waterproof protection, trace playback, mini size for easy hiding, SMS notification, and multiple alert protections. Whether you need to track your vehicle's location, monitor its speed, or receive alerts for specific events, the G19 has you covered. Its compact size and waterproof design make it suitable for a wide range of applications, from personal vehicles to fleet management.
