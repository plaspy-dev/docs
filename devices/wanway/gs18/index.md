# WanWay - GS18

![GS18](./Logo.jpg)

The WanWay GS18 is a wireless real-time positioning device designed for various tracking needs. With its large 4200mAh battery capacity, it can provide long-term positioning and tracking capabilities. This makes it an ideal choice for industries such as transport and logistics, refrigerated cargo, container tracking, construction, valuable assets, and criminal investigation.

The GS18 GPS tracker is equipped with a quad-band frequency \(850/1900/900/1800MHz\) and has a GPS accuracy of \<=5m. It features a hot start time of less than 2 seconds in an open sky environment, ensuring quick and accurate positioning. The device also has a backup battery, allowing it to continue functioning even when the main battery is depleted.

One of the standout features of the GS18 is its long battery life, which ensures continuous tracking without the need for frequent recharging. Additionally, it offers functions such as real-time tracking, light sensor disassemble alarm, strong magnetic attachment, user-defined geo-fencing, trace playback, and more. With its compact dimensions of 77mm\(L\) x 41mm\(W\) x 27mm\(H\) and lightweight design of 106g, the GS18 is portable and easy to use.
