# WanWay - GS18

<div className="device-logo">
![GS18](./Logo.jpg)
</div>

The WanWay GS18 is a wireless real time positioning device designed for a range of tracking needs. It combines compact portability with a large 4200mAh battery capacity and a backup battery to support extended periods of location monitoring. The GS18 provides quick positioning with a fast hot start in open sky conditions and a typical GPS accuracy of five meters or better, making it suitable for moving assets and equipment where reliable location data is important.

As a Plaspy compatible device, the GS18 can be used to bring that positioning capability into a centralized fleet and asset management workflow. Core features such as real time tracking, user defined geo fencing, trace playback, and a tamper alarm align well with Plaspy functionality for visibility, alerts, and historical route review. Organizations using Plaspy can include the GS18 in monitoring setups to improve operational oversight without requiring overly specific device level changes.

## Key Highlights

- Large 4200mAh battery capacity for extended tracking intervals and reduced recharge frequency
- Backup battery support to maintain basic tracking when the main power is depleted
- Real time positioning and trace playback for route history and review
- Built in light sensor disassemble alarm to detect tampering
- Strong magnetic attachment for straightforward mounting on metal surfaces
- Compact and lightweight form factor suitable for a variety of mobile and asset tracking deployments
- Quad band frequency coverage and GPS accuracy typically within five meters for reliable location data

## How It Works with Plaspy

When paired with Plaspy, the GS18 provides location and event information that Plaspy can present on maps, dashboards, and reports to support fleet operations and asset monitoring. Plaspy aggregates device updates and makes them actionable through alerts, playback, and reporting features.

- Live map visibility for individual units and fleet level monitoring to track current positions
- User defined geo fence alerts delivered through Plaspy when the device enters or leaves configured areas
- Trace playback and historical route export for operational analysis and incident review
- Tamper or disassemble alerts surfaced inside Plaspy when the device triggers its light sensor alarm
- Battery status and long life characteristics used to plan maintenance intervals and reduce downtime when device reported status is available

## Typical Use Cases

- Transport and logistics monitoring for visibility of shipments and vehicles
- Refrigerated cargo and sensitive goods tracking where long runtime is beneficial
- Container tracking and asset location for yards and intermodal operations
- Construction site equipment monitoring for portable tools and machinery
- Protection of high value assets and evidence tracking in investigative scenarios
