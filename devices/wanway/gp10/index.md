# WanWay - GP10

![GP10](./Logo.jpg)

The WanWay GP10 is a compact personal tracking and positioning device designed for everyday tracking needs. With dimensions of 48x33x17mm it is small enough for personal use and portable enough to attach to belongings, carry for personal safety, or keep with pets. Core capabilities described for this model include real time location updates, user defined geo fences, SMS notifications for key events, and remote voice monitoring.

This model is listed as compatible with Plaspy which makes it a practical choice for users who want to centralize tracking and alerts on a single fleet management platform. Through Plaspy you can view location updates on maps, manage geo fence settings, receive event notifications, and keep a history of device activity, making the GP10 useful for both individual and light fleet scenarios where visibility and timely alerts matter.

## Key Highlights

- Compact form factor ideal for personal carry or attachment to small assets
- Real time tracking for up to date location visibility
- User definable geo fences with instant notifications on entry and exit
- SMS alerts for low battery, geo fence events, and SOS signals
- Remote voice monitoring for additional situational awareness
- Built in 800mAh battery for extended tracking and positioning use

## How It Works with Plaspy

The GP10 transmits location and event information that Plaspy can surface in its user interface, enabling centralized monitoring and alerting across devices. Plaspy acts as the platform to visualize location, manage geofences, and route notifications so teams can respond to events efficiently.

- View real time device location on Plaspy maps and monitor movement history
- Configure and manage geo fences in Plaspy to receive entry and exit alerts
- Route SMS and device event notifications through Plaspy notification settings for timely awareness
- Keep an event log and location history in Plaspy for reporting and operational review
- Monitor device status and battery alerts from the Plaspy dashboard for proactive oversight

## Typical Use Cases

- Personal safety tracking for children, seniors, or lone workers
- Tracking of small valuable items and portable assets
- Pet tracking when a compact device is required
- Short term rental item monitoring and oversight
- Field personnel location visibility in light duty operations
