# Intellitrack - Intellitrac P1

![Intellitrac P1](./logo.gif)

The IntelliTrac P1 Series personal GPS device from STC is a compact and mobile tracker designed for tracking individuals. Its sleek and portable design makes it easy to carry and conceal, ensuring discreet tracking. Whether you need to keep an eye on a loved one, monitor the whereabouts of employees, or track the movements of valuable assets, the IntelliTrac P1 is a reliable solution.

One of the standout features of the IntelliTrac P1 is its waterproof enclosure. This ensures that the device remains protected from water damage, making it suitable for use in various environments and weather conditions. Whether it's rain, splashes, or accidental submersion, you can trust that the IntelliTrac P1 will continue to function without any issues.

In addition to its durability, the IntelliTrac P1 is equipped with an internal backup battery. This allows the device to maintain a constant connection for several days, ensuring uninterrupted tracking. The device also tracks motion and height changes, providing you with comprehensive information about the movements of the tracked individual or asset.

With its compact design, waterproof enclosure, and reliable tracking capabilities, the IntelliTrac P1 Series personal GPS device is an excellent choice for anyone in need of a reliable and discreet tracking solution.

### Key Features:

- Compact, mobile design
- Waterproof enclosure
- Internal antenna for GPS and GSM connections
- Designed for personal tracking
- Accelerometer & barometer sensors built-in
