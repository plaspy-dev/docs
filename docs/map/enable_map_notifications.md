# Enable map notifications
To receive Plaspy alert notifications on your desktop, you need to enable notifications in your browser. By default, browsers usually block all sounds and notifications automatically. Below are the steps to enable notifications in the most common browsers: Google Chrome and Firefox.

## Google Chrome

1. **Access Notification Settings:**

    - Click on the lock icon🔒 \(1\) next to the address bar.
    - A dropdown menu with several options will appear.
2. **Check Notification Status:**

    - Verify if "Sound" and "Notifications" \(2\) are allowed.
    - If they are not, select "Allow" to enable these options.
3. **Save Changes:**

    - Close the menu to save the changes. You should now receive Plaspy notifications on your desktop.
4. **Additional Recommendation:**

    - Ensure the browser is not in incognito mode, as this mode can block notifications.

![](./1-notifications-google.mp4)

## Firefox

1. **Access Notification Settings:**

    - Click on the information icon \(1\) next to the address bar.
    - A dropdown menu with several options will appear.
2. **Check Notification Status:**

    - Verify if "Autoplay Sound" and "Send Notifications" \(2\) are allowed.
    - If they are not, select "Allow" to enable these options.
3. **Save Changes:**

    - Close the menu to save the changes. You should now receive Plaspy notifications on your desktop.
4. **Additional Recommendation:**

    - Ensure the browser is not in private mode, as this mode can block notifications.

![](./2-notifications-firefox.mp4)

## Final Considerations

Enabling notifications in your browser is crucial for receiving important Plaspy alerts in real-time. Follow the steps mentioned to ensure you do not miss any critical notifications. Additionally, regularly check your browser settings to keep notifications enabled and avoid interruptions.

For more information on autoplay policy changes, refer to the following link: [Autoplay Policy Changes](https://developers.google.com/web/updates/2017/09/autoplay-policy-changes).

By following these simple steps, you can stay informed and up-to-date with all Plaspy alerts directly on your desktop.
