# Subscription Plans
We offer different **subscription plans** so you can choose the one that best fits your workflow. Each plan includes a set of features designed for different needs: from basic daily tracking to advanced tools for larger operations with special requirements.

In this section of the help center, you will learn about the main features of each plan, the type of use they cover, and how to decide which one is right for you.

## Subscriptions

### Standard Plan

It is ideal if you want to start with clear and complete control of your vehicles, featuring essential monitoring and reporting functions.

- **Real-time tracking** of the device.
- **Trip history** to check previous routes for up to one year.
- **Configurable alerts**, such as:

    - Speeding.
    - Geofences \(Zones\).
    - Sensors \(if supported by your device\).
    - And other basic alerts.
- **Sub-accounts and shared access**, so more people can view the information based on specific permissions.
- **Exportable reports** to download information in Excel or KML formats to work outside the platform.
- **Digital support channels** to help you whenever you need it.

### Professional Plan

Designed for those who need more control, more automation, and advanced tools to better analyze their operation. It includes everything from the **Standard** plan, plus:

- **Integrated maps** with advanced visualization options.
- **Advanced reports and statistics** to understand the behavior of the operation in detail.
- **Expanded notifications** with more delivery options \(WhatsApp and Telegram\).
- **More frequent data updates** to see changes more quickly.
- **Priority support** for faster assistance in important cases.

### Enterprise Plan

A customized solution for companies with complex needs, such as integrations, special configurations, or large-scale operations. This plan may include:

- **Advanced integration** via API and Webhooks.
- **Platform customization**, such as branding and tailor-made adjustments \(based on the agreement\).
- **Dedicated support** with technical accompaniment and guided configuration.
- **Advanced technical assistance**, including technical sessions when required.
- **Scalability**, designed for operations with many vehicles, users, and processes.

## Frequently Asked Questions \(FAQ\)

- **What is the main difference between Standard and Professional?**
    - The **Standard** plan covers the essentials: seeing vehicles on the map, reviewing trips, setting up alerts, and downloading reports. The **Professional** plan goes a step further: it is designed for those who need **more control and deeper analysis**, especially when managing several vehicles and making decisions based on data.
- **Why is the Professional plan usually the most useful for fleet managers?**
    - Because a fleet manager usually doesn't just need to "see where the vehicle is," but also to **understand what is happening and improve the operation**. With the Professional plan, you can:
        - Review **advanced reports and statistics** to detect patterns \(speeding, frequent stops, inefficient routes, etc.\).
        - Have **more frequent updates**, which helps when closer monitoring is needed.
        - Receive **more complete notifications** to act quickly during important events.
        - Count on **priority support**, useful for a daily operation that cannot stop.
    - *In short: it makes the daily work of controlling, reporting, and optimizing a fleet much easier.*
- **If I only have a few vehicles, should I choose Professional or Standard?**
    - If your goal is basic monitoring and occasional reports, **Standard** is usually enough. If you are at a point where you want to **measure better**, compare routes, monitor driving habits, or perform more constant tracking, **Professional** will give you more tools.
- **What is Enterprise and who is it for?**
    - **Enterprise** is a more flexible option, designed for companies that need something "custom-made." For example: integrations with internal systems, automation with API/Webhooks, special configurations, custom branding, or a large operation with several teams working at the same time.
- **How is the Enterprise plan activated?**
    - It’s not just a matter of "activating and that's it," because it usually requires adjusting details based on your specific case. Therefore, to use it, you just need to **fill out a form**. With that information, our team can review your needs and help you get everything set up correctly, without any guesswork.
- **Can I change my plan later?**
    - Yes. As your operation grows, you can start with one plan and then switch to another when you need more features or a more advanced level of configuration.
